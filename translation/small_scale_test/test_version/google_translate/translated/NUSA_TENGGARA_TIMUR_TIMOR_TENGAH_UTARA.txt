GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
article 1
In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is North Central Timor Regency.
2. Regional Government is the North Central Timor Regency Government.
2
3. The Regent is the Regent of North Central Timor.
4. The Regional People's Representative Council, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the Regional People's Representative Council of North Central Timor Regency.
5. The regional secretary is the Regional Secretary of North Central Timor Regency.
6. Department is a Regional Apparatus that carries out government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and Child Protection.
7. Head of department is the Head of Regional Apparatus who carries out government affairs in the field of women's empowerment.
8. Regional Apparatus, hereinafter abbreviated as PD, is the Regional Apparatus in North Central Timor Regency. 9. Head of Regional Apparatus is the Head of Regional Apparatus in North Central Timor Regency.
10. Agency is a Regional Apparatus that carries out Government affairs in the field of development planning and regional research.
11. The Head of the Agency is a Regional Apparatus who carries out Government affairs in the planning sector.
12. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social, knowledge and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.
13. Gender Mainstreaming, hereinafter abbreviated to PUG, is a strategy developed to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of regional development policies and programs.
14.Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women which occur as a result of the formation of society and can change by the social and cultural conditions of society.
15. Gender equality is equal conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings to be able to play a role and participate in the development process.
16. Gender Justice is a process of being fair to men and women.
17. Gender Mainstreaming Working Group, hereinafter referred to as Pokja PUG, is a consultation forum for implementers and mobilizers of gender mainstreaming from various agencies/institutions in the regions.
18. Gender Mainstreaming Focal Point, hereinafter referred to as PUG Focal Point, is a PD apparatus that has the ability to carry out gender mainstreaming in their respective work units.
19. The Regional Medium Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMD, is a description of the Regent's vision, mission and program, the preparation of which is guided by the Regional Long Term Development Plan, which contains regional development strategies, regional general policies, 3
regional programs and a macroeconomic framework that includes a comprehensive picture of the economy, including the direction of fiscal policy in a work plan in the form of a regulatory framework and an indicative funding framework.
20.Gender Analysis is a process that is developed systematically to identify and understand the division of labor or roles of men and women, access and control over development resources, participation in development and the benefits they enjoy.
21. Gender Responsive Budget is a performance-based budget allocation for certain programs or activities whose input, output and outcome indicators can ensure that the aspirations and needs of women and men are met fairly.
22. Communication, Information and Education, hereinafter abbreviated as KIE, is the delivery of messages in the form of information, ideas and facts related to gender mainstreaming that the public needs to know directly or indirectly through communication channels to message recipients to get an effect, as a process of changing behavior towards gender responsiveness.
23. The sub-district/village development planning meeting, hereinafter referred to as Musrembangkel, is an annual sub-district/village development planning deliberation forum involving development actors in the sub-district/village.
24. Sub-District Development Planning Deliberation, hereinafter referred to as Musrembangcam, is a deliberation forum between development actors to discuss and agree on steps to handle priority activity programs listed in the list of proposed sub-district development activity plans that are integrated with the regional development priorities of North Central Timor Regency in the sub-district area coordinated by the Development Planning Agency
Regional and implemented by the sub-district head.
25. Development planning deliberation for the preparation of the Regional Medium Term Development Plan, hereinafter referred to as Musrembang RPJMD, is a deliberation forum between stakeholders to discuss, sharpen and agree on the draft Regional Medium Term Development Plan.
26. The Strategic Plan, hereinafter referred to as the Strategic Plan, is a five-year planning document containing the vision, mission, goals, strategies, policies, programs and development activities in accordance with the tasks and functions of the organization as an elaboration of the RPJMD.
4
27. Work Plan, hereinafter referred to as Renja, is an annual planning document containing policies, programs and development activities, whether planned directly by Regional Apparatus or implemented by encouraging community participation as an elaboration of the Strategic Plan.
28. Regional Government Work Plan, hereinafter abbreviated to RKPD, is an annual planning document containing policies, programs and development activities, whether planned directly by the Regional Government or
achieved by encouraging community participation as an elaboration of the Strategic Plan.
29.Regional Action Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RAD, is an activity planning document prepared by a technical team that will be implemented in a certain period/time.
30. Gender Analysis Pathway, hereinafter abbreviated to GAP, is a gender analysis tool that can be used to assist planners in mainstreaming gender in planning development policies/programs/activities with the aim of identifying gender gaps.
and gender issues and at the same time formulate plans for policies/programs/development activities aimed at reducing or eliminating the gender gap.
31. The Technical Team is a team formed by the PUG working group with the task of carrying out budget analysis with a gender perspective and preparing the PUG RAD.

EXPLANATION OF THE TOP

ABOUT
IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT
I. GENERAL
The presence of Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government as amended several times, most recently with Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014
concerning Regional Government and Law Number 33 of 2004 concerning Financial Balance between the Central Government and Regional Government is the entry point for Women's Empowerment in regional development. Women's empowerment is the right development strategy to realize gender equality and justice through development policies and programs that are gender sensitive and responsive in the future, especially in North Central Timor Regency.
In North Central Timor Regency there are several conditions that need to be intervened with by establishing Regional Regulations on Women's Empowerment. This happens because there is a lot of discrimination against women in developing themselves and opportunities to fulfill their rights. Women were only considered second class. Her job is to take care of the household and does not need to work. This needs to be fought, this stigma is a problem that must be resolved with an empowerment program.
The above paradigm is reflected in the data on the presence of women working in Government, the data shows that women are not in a strategic position to take part in making decisions or policies, especially in Government affairs through ASN data based on gender available in the field.
