CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:
The Region is Lampung Timur Regency.
The Regional Government is the Regent and the Regional Apparatus as the elements administering regional government.
The Regent is the Regent of Lampung Timur.
The Regional People's Representative Council, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of Lampung Timur Regency as an element administering regional government.
The Regional Government Working Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the Regional Government Working Unit of Lampung Timur Regency.
The Regional Government Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as UKPD, is the Regional Government Work Unit of Lampung Timur Regency.
A Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 years, including a child still in the womb.
A Woman is an adult human being of the female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
Discrimination is any distinction, harassment, or exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on a distinction among human beings on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social class, economic status, sex, language, or political belief, which results in the diminishment, deviation, or elimination of the recognition, implementation, or enjoyment of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life, whether individual or collective, in the fields of politics, economics, law, social affairs, culture, and other aspects of life.
Violence is any act that results in or causes suffering and distress, whether physical, sexual, or psychological, including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion, or the arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
Protection of women is every effort directed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by giving consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
Protection of children is every activity to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they may live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
Gender Mainstreaming is a strategy built to integrate gender as an integral dimension in the planning, implementation, budgeting, monitoring, and evaluation of policies/programs/activities in various fields of life and development.
Mainstreaming of Children's Rights, hereinafter referred to as PUHA, is a child protection strategy that integrates children's rights into every development activity, from the preparation of planning, budgeting, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of various laws and regulations, policies, programs, and activities, by applying the principle of the best interests of the child in welfare and child protection programs covering four (4) areas of work, namely child health, child education, child protection, and HIV/AIDS prevention.
A Child-Friendly Regency, hereinafter referred to as KLA, is a development model that integrates the commitments and resources of the government, society, and the business world comprehensively and sustainably through a strategy of mainstreaming children's rights.
The Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the regional government to provide services for the empowerment of women, the protection of women, and the protection of children from various forms of discrimination and violence, including trafficking in persons.
A Safe House is temporary accommodation provided to give protection to victims in accordance with established standards.
The Children's Friend Telephone 129, hereinafter referred to as TESA 129, is a form of child protection service in the form of a local toll-free telephone access (home/office telephone) for children who need protection or are in an emergency situation, as well as children who need counseling services.
A Victim is an individual or group of persons who has suffered physical, mental, or emotional harm, economic loss, or has experienced the neglect, diminishment, or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of a violation of human rights, or a criminal act or act of violence committed by state officials, the state, regional government officials, or by individuals.
Implementing officers or functional officers are health workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, and legal aid personnel who are civil servants within their respective agencies or special personnel employed at the P2TP2A.
Service is an activity and immediate action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions, in the form of counseling, therapy, and advocacy aimed at strengthening and recovering victims of violence.
Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disturbance and the restoration of normal social functioning, both within the family and in society.
Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with their family, community, institution, or other social environment that can provide protection.
A community institution is an institution formed voluntarily by members of the Indonesian public on the basis of a shared vision, mission, profession, function, and activity, in order to participate in development to achieve national goals within the framework of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia based on Pancasila, consisting of religious organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, private organizations, social organizations, political organizations, mass media, and other forms of organization.

CHAPTER II
INTENT AND PURPOSE

Article 2

The Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children is intended to provide guidance to the Government of Lampung Timur Regency and various related parties (law enforcement, community institutions, universities, and civil society) to optimize protection efforts for women and children against discrimination and violence.

Article 3

The Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children aims to:
ensure the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children by the Regional Government in a systematic, comprehensive, sustainable, and integrated manner;
enhance the commitment of the Regional Government in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children;
realize regional planning and development that is gender-responsive and child-friendly;
encourage institutions that handle the duties and functions of women's empowerment, women's protection, and child protection, both within the Regional Government and within community institutions, universities, and civil society.

CHAPTER III
PLANNING

Article 4

The Regional Government shall integrate development policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the region into the Long-Term Regional Development Plan (RPJPD), the Medium-Term Regional Development Plan (RPJMD), the Strategic Plan of the Regional Government Working Unit (Renstra-SKPD), the Regional Development Work Plan (RKPD), and the Work Plan and Budget of the Regional Government Working Unit (RKA-SKPD), with reference to national policy on the Protection of Women and Children.

The working unit whose duties and functions relate to the protection of women and children shall facilitate the integration of development policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children into the preparation of planning and budgeting documents as referred to in paragraph (1).
In carrying out the matters referred to in paragraph (2), the working unit whose duties and functions relate to the protection of women and children in the region shall coordinate with the Regional Development Planning Agency.

CHAPTER IV
IMPLEMENTATION

Part One
Duties and Authority of the Regional Government

Article 5

The Regent is obliged to implement policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in order to eliminate all forms of discrimination and violence against women and children in the region.
The implementation referred to in paragraph (1) shall be coordinated by the Deputy Regent.

Article 6

The working unit whose duties and functions relate to the empowerment of women and children shall implement the established policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, and in doing so shall cooperate with regional government working units (SKPD), the business sector, law enforcement, community institutions, universities, and civil society.
The implementation referred to in paragraph (1) shall take the form of: policy analysis, coordination, advocacy, socialization, communication, information, and education (KIE), training, service facilitation, service provision, the development of models for the protection of women and children, and other forms.

Article 7

To promote the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the region, this may be done by establishing, developing, strengthening, or utilizing task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions at the provincial, regency, and municipal levels.
Membership of the task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of relevant SKPDs, community institutions, universities, and civil society.
The establishment of task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by a Decree of the Regent.

Article 8

In implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, the Government of Lampung Timur Regency may cooperate with international institutions.
Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 9

The Regent, in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, shall undertake efforts to:
coordinate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children among SKPDs within the region;
cooperate with law enforcement, community institutions, universities, the business sector, and civil society in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children;
strengthen the institutional capacity of gender mainstreaming and PUHA;
facilitate the services required in implementing the protection of women and children;
provide services for the protection of women and children;
formulate affirmative action for the protection of women and children;
establish and develop a Child-Friendly Regency (KLA); and
develop a data collection system for the protection of women and children, including a data collection system on violence against women and children.

Article 10

The provision of protection services for women and children as referred to in Article 9 letter e shall cover efforts to fulfill rights and improve the quality of life of women and children, particularly in the fields of education, health, economy, and legal protection.
For women and children who are victims of violence and trafficking in persons, services shall include: complaint/victim identification services, legal assistance, health rehabilitation, psychosocial rehabilitation, social reintegration, repatriation assistance, or other forms in accordance with the needs and best interests of the victim.
The provision of protective services referred to in paragraph (2) shall also be intended for women and children in areas prone to conflict and disaster, women and children with disabilities, women and children who are victims of sexual exploitation, women and children affected by HIV/AIDS, women and children in other vulnerable groups, and children requiring special protection.
The provisions referred to in paragraphs (1), (2), and (3) shall be further regulated by a Regent's Regulation.

Article 11

The provision of protection services for women and children shall be carried out through the P2TP2A and other institutions in accordance with the needs and best interests of women and children.

Part Two
Implementation of Protection

Article 12

In order to provide protection services for women and children, the regional government shall establish a P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children.

The establishment and development of the P2TP2A shall be adapted to the development of priority needs, the region's financial capacity, and the existing institutional and personnel capacity in the region.

Article 13

The P2TP2A shall have the principal duties and functions of an institution for the empowerment of women in various fields of development, as well as for the protection of women and children from various forms of discrimination and violence, including trafficking in persons, whether established by the government or community-based.
In carrying out the principal duties and functions referred to in paragraph (1), the P2TP2A may coordinate with sub-districts and various competent parties in undertaking efforts to protect women and children from various forms of discrimination and violence, including trafficking in persons.
The provision of integrated services must be supported by implementing officers or functional officers, including health workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, and legal aid personnel provided by relevant agencies or institutions.

Article 14

The P2TP2A shall consist of various elements, including: working units responsible for women's empowerment, family planning, health, social affairs, religion, manpower, education, population, community empowerment, civil service police, the police, the public prosecutor's office, the courts, hospitals, community institutions, and universities.
The membership of the P2TP2A referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by a Decree of the Regent.

Article 15

The P2TP2A, as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children in Lampung Timur Regency, shall gradually be able to function as: a referral center, a reproductive health consultation center, a legal consultation center, an integrated crisis center (PKT), a center for handling acts of violence, a trauma recovery center (trauma center), a women's crisis center, a training center, a science and technology information center (PIPTEK), a business consultation center, a safe house (shelter), a transit house, a children's friend telephone (TESSA) 129, or other forms that support the improvement of protection services for women and children.
Further provisions concerning the organization and working procedures of the P2TP2A referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by a Regent's Regulation.

CHAPTER V
MONITORING AND EVALUATION

Article 16

To ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, the Regional Government shall carry out monitoring.

The monitoring referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to identify the progress and obstacles in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in Lampung Timur Regency.
Monitoring shall be carried out periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of the SKPDs implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children.
Monitoring shall be carried out from the planning stage through to the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children for the current year.

Article 17

Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children shall be conducted at the end of every budget year, or as needed.
The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children shall be used as input for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children for the following year.
The evaluation referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI
REPORTING

Article 18

Every working unit and work unit implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children must submit a report at the end of every budget year.
The reporting of implementation referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out annually and/or as needed.
The form of reporting referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VII
FUNDING

Article 19

Funding for the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the regency shall be sourced from the Regency Regional Budget (APBD) and other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
The Regional Government may provide funding assistance for the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VIII
GUIDANCE

Article 20

The Regional Government is obliged to provide guidance for the implementation of the protection of women and children.
The guidance referred to in paragraph (1) shall include:
establishing guidelines and standards of fulfillment;
facilitating technical guidance and training;
increasing the provision of facilities;
strengthening institutional capacity periodically through training, consultation, advocacy, and coordination to strengthen the role of the P2TP2A;
strengthening institutions or organizational units handling the protection of women and children;
carrying out monitoring and evaluation.

CHAPTER IX
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 21

The community may participate in efforts to implement the protection of women and children against discrimination and violence, either individually and/or through organizations.
The community may participate in efforts to implement the protection of women and children against discrimination and violence, either through organizations and/or individually.

CHAPTER X
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 22

This Regulation constitutes one of the measures of performance in the implementation of development for the protection of women and children within the Regional Government of Lampung Timur Regency.

Article 23

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of its promulgation.

So that everyone may be aware of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation shall be ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Lampung Timur Regency.

I. GENERAL

The right to receive protection for every citizen has in fact been guaranteed by the constitution. This can be seen in the purpose of establishing the state as referred to in the fourth paragraph of the Preamble to the 1945 Constitution, which states that the State shall protect the entire Indonesian nation and the entire homeland of Indonesia, and shall promote the general welfare, advance the intellectual life of the nation, and participate in maintaining world order based on freedom, lasting peace, and social justice. The notion of protection here includes protecting every citizen from all forms of discrimination and violence. In addition, Article 28G paragraph 2 of the 1945 Constitution states that every person has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity.

However, in reality, problems of discrimination, exploitation, violence, neglect, and trafficking in persons still often occur, particularly affecting women and children. This shows how vulnerable these two groups are. Yet, upon closer examination, women and children have a very strategic role in the dynamics of national progress. Women, as part of the potential human resources, contribute to the success of a family, society, and nation. Success can only be achieved when there is good cooperation between the resources of women and men. Meanwhile, children are the shoots, potential, and the younger generation who carry on the ideals of the nation's struggle. This responsibility can only be properly borne by children who are given broad opportunities to grow and develop optimally, both physically, mentally, and socially.

The existence of the Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children in Lampung Timur Regency is part of the effort to enable women and children to carry out their strategic role properly. With this Regional Regulation, there is a guarantee that their rights will be fulfilled and that they will be protected from various forms of discrimination and treatment that degrade the dignity of women and children.
