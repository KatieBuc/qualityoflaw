CHAPTER I
GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

article 1






In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
The area is East Lampung Regency.
Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
The Regent is the Regent of East Lampung.
The Regional People's Representative Council, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of East Lampung Regency as an element of regional government administration.
The Regional Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the East Lampung Regency Regional Work Unit.
The Regional Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as UKPD, is the East Lampung Regency Regional Work Unit.
A child is someone who is not yet 18 years old, including an unborn child.
A woman is an adult human being of the female sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.
Discrimination is any differentiation, harassment or exclusion that is direct or indirect based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs which results in reducing deviations or eliminating recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life, both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
Violence is any act that results in or results in misery and suffering, whether physical, sexual or psychological, including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
Women's protection is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in fulfilling their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
Gender Mainstreaming is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension in planning, implementation, budgeting, monitoring and evaluation of policies/programs/activities in various areas of life and development.
Mainstreaming Children's Rights, hereinafter referred to as PUHA, is a child protection strategy by integrating children's rights into every development activity starting from the preparation of planning, budgeting, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of various laws and regulations, policies, programs and activities by applying the principle of the best interests of children in child welfare and protection programs which cover four (4) areas of work, namely the field of child health, the field of child education, the field of child protection and the field of HIV/AIDS control.
Child Friendly Regency, hereinafter referred to as KLA, is a development model that integrates the commitment and resources of government, society and the business world in a comprehensive and sustainable manner through a strategy for mainstreaming children's rights.
The Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit formed by the regional government to provide services in the context of empowering women, protecting women, and protecting children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including human trafficking.
A Safe House is a temporary residence, which is provided to provide protection for victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
Children's Friend Telephone 129, hereinafter referred to as TESA 129, is a form of child protection service in the form of local toll-free telephone access (home/office telephone) for children who need protection or in emergency situations or children who need counseling services.
Victims are individuals or groups of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of violations of human rights (HAM), or criminal acts and acts of violence committed either by state officials or by state or regional government officials or by individuals.
Implementing officers or functional officers are health personnel, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, legal aid personnel who come from civil servants in their respective service environments or special personnel employed at P2TP2A
Services are activities and immediate actions carried out by professional staff in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy to strengthen and restore victims of violence.
Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the return to normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions or other social environments that can provide protection.
Community institutions are institutions formed by members of the Indonesian citizen community voluntarily on the basis of a common vision, mission, profession, function and activities to participate in development in order to achieve national goals within the framework of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia based on Pancasila, which consists of religious organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, private organizations, social organizations, political organizations, mass media and other forms of organization.







CHAPTER II
PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2







The Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children is intended to provide a reference for the East Lampung Regency Government and various related parties (law enforcement, community institutions, universities and civil society) to optimize efforts to protect women and children from discrimination and violence.






Article 3

The District Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children aims to:
guarantee the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children by regional governments in a systematic, comprehensive, sustainable and integrated manner;
increase the commitment of Regional Governments in implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children;
realizing regional planning and development that is gender responsive and cares for children;
encourage institutions that handle the tasks and functions of women's empowerment, women's protection and child protection both in Regional Government and community institutions, universities and civil society.






BAB III
PLANNING

Article 4






The Regional Government integrates policies, programs and development activities for the protection of women and children in the region which are outlined in the Regional Long Term Development Plan (RPJPD), Regional Medium Term Development Plan (RPJMD), Strategic Plan for Regional Work Units (Renstra-SKPD), Regional Development Work Plan (RKPD), and Work Plan and Budget for Regional Work Units (RKA-SKPD), with reference to the national policy for the Protection of Women and Children.

The work unit whose duties and functions deal with the protection of women and children facilitates the integration of policies, programs and development activities for the protection of women and children into the preparation of planning and budget documents as stated in paragraph (1).
In implementing as intended in paragraph (2), the work unit whose duties and functions handle the protection of women and children in the region coordinates with the Regional Development Planning Agency.






CHAPTER IV
IMPLEMENTATION

Part One
Duties and Authorities of Regional Government

Article 5






The Regent is obliged to implement policies, programs and activities to protect women and children, to eliminate all forms of discrimination and acts of violence against women and children in his area.
The implementation as intended in paragraph (1) is coordinated by the Deputy Regent.






Article 6






The work unit whose duties and functions deal with the empowerment of women and children implements policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children that have been determined, and in its implementation collaborates with regional work units (SKPD), the business world, law enforcement, community institutions, universities and civil society.
Implementation as intended in paragraph (1) takes the form of: policy analysis, coordination, advocacy, socialization, communication, information and education (KIE), training, service facilitation, service provision, development of women and children protection models, and other forms.






Article 7






To encourage the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children in the regions, this can be done by establishing, developing, strengthening or utilizing task forces, forums, working groups or other institutions at the provincial, district and city levels.
Membership of the task force, forum, working group, or other institution as intended in paragraph (1) consists of related SKPD, community institutions, universities, and civil society.
The formation of a task force, forum, working group or other institution as intended in paragraph (1) is determined by a Regent's Decree.






Article 8






In implementing policies, programs and activities to protect women and children, the Regional Government of East Lampung Regency can collaborate with international institutions.
The cooperation as intended in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of statutory regulations.






Article 9






The Regent, in implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children, makes efforts to:
coordinating the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children between SKPDs in their regions;
cooperation with law enforcement, community institutions, universities, the business world and civil society in implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children;
strengthening the institutional capacity of PUG and PUHA;
facilitation of services needed to implement the protection of women and children;
provision of women and child protection services;
preparation of affirmative action for the protection of women and children;
formation and development of KLA; And
preparation of a data collection system for the protection of women and children, including a data collection system for violence against women and children.





Article 10






Provision of protection services for women and children as intended in Article 9 letter e includes efforts to fulfill the rights and improve the quality of women and children, especially in the fields of education, health, economics and legal protection.
For women and children who are victims of violence and human trafficking, services include: victim complaint/identification services, legal assistance, health rehabilitation, psychosocial rehabilitation, social reintegration, repatriation assistance or other forms according to the needs of the victim's best interests.
The provision of protection services as intended in paragraph (2) is also intended for women and children in areas prone to conflict and disasters, women and children with disabilities, women and children victims of sexual exploitation, women and children affected by HIV/AIDS, women and children who are in other vulnerable groups, and children who need special protection.
The provisions as intended in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) are regulated through Regent Regulations.






Article 11






The provision of women and child protection services is carried out through P2TP2A, and other institutions in accordance with the needs and best interests of women and children.






Part Two
Implementation of Protection

Article 12






In order to provide services for the protection of women and children, the regional government established P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children.

The formation and development of P2TP2A is adjusted to developments in priority needs and regional financial capacity as well as institutional and personnel capabilities in the region.






Article 13






P2TP2A has the main duties and functions as an institution for empowering women in various fields of development, as well as protecting women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including human trafficking, which are established by the government or community-based
In carrying out the main tasks and functions as intended in paragraph (1), P2TP2A can coordinate with sub-districts, and various competent parties in carrying out efforts to protect women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including human trafficking.
The implementation of integrated services must be supported by implementing officers or functional officers who include health workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, legal aid workers provided by the relevant agencies or institutions.






Article 14






P2TP2A consists of various elements, including: work units dealing with women's empowerment, family planning, health, social, religion, employment, education, population, community empowerment, civil service, police, prosecutor's office, courts, hospitals and community institutions, as well as universities.
P2TP2A membership as referred to in paragraph (1), is determined by a Regent's Decree.






Article 15






P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children in East Lampung Regency, must gradually be able to function as: referral center, reproductive health consultation center, legal consultation center, integrated crisis center (PKT), violence handling center, trauma recovery center, women's crisis center, training center, science and technology information center (PIPTEK), business consultation center, safe house (shelter), halfway house, children's friend telephone (TESSA) 129 or other forms that support improving women's protection services and child.
Further provisions regarding the organization and work procedures of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in a Regent's Regulation.






BAB V
MONITORING AND EVALUATION

Article 16






To ensure synergy, continuity and effectiveness of integrated steps in implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children, the Regional Government carries out monitoring.

Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine developments and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children in East Lampung Regency.
Monitoring is carried out regularly through direct coordination and monitoring of SKPD implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children.
Monitoring is carried out from planning to implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children for the current year.
.




Article 17






Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children is carried out at the end of each fiscal year or if necessary as needed.
The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children are used as input for the preparation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children for the following year.
The evaluation as intended in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of statutory regulations.






BAB VI
REPORTING

Article 18






Every work unit and work unit that implements policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children must report at the end of each fiscal year
Implementation reporting as intended in paragraph (1) is carried out every year and/or if necessary.
The form of reporting as intended in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of statutory regulations.






BAB VII
FUNDING

Article 19








Funding for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children in the district comes from the district APBD and other legitimate sources in accordance with statutory provisions.
Regional Governments can provide funding assistance for implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children in accordance with statutory provisions.






CHAPTER VIII
CONSTRUCTION

Article 20






Regional governments are obliged to provide guidance on the implementation of protection for women and children.
Guidance as intended in paragraph (1), includes:
establish compliance guidelines and standards;
facilitating technical guidance and training;
increasing the provision of facilities;
strengthen institutional capacity periodically through training, consultation, advocacy and coordination to strengthen the role of P2TP2A;
strengthening institutions or organizational units that handle the protection of women and children;
carry out monitoring and evaluation;






BAB IX
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION
Article 21






The community can participate in efforts to protect women and children from discrimination and violence, both as individuals and/or organizations.
The community can participate in efforts to protect women and children from discrimination and violence, both organizationally and/or individually






CHAPTER X
CLOSURE

Article 22






This regulation is one measure of performance in implementing the development of women's and children's protection in the Regional Government of East Lampung Regency.






Article 23






This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

So that everyone is aware, this Regional Regulation is ordered to be promulgated by placing it in the Regional Gazette of East Lampung Regency.

I. GENERAL

The right to obtain protection for every citizen is actually guaranteed by the constitution. This can be seen in the aim of establishing the state as referred to in paragraph IV of the Preamble to the 1945 Constitution which states that "The state protects the entire Indonesian nation and all of Indonesia's blood and to promote general welfare, educate the life of the nation and participate in implementing world order based on independence, eternal peace and social justice". The meaning of protect here includes protecting every citizen from all forms of discrimination and violence. Apart from that, Article 28 G paragraph 2 of the 1945 Constitution states that everyone has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity.
However, in reality, problems of discrimination, exploitation, violence, neglect and human trafficking still often occur, especially among women and children. This shows how vulnerable these two groups are. In fact, if you look closely, women and children have a very strategic role in the dynamics of the country's progress. Women are part of the human resource potential that also determines the success of a family, society and country. Success can only be achieved when there is good cooperation between women and men. Meanwhile, children are the shoots, potential, and successors of the young generation to the ideals of the nation's struggle. This responsibility can only be carried out properly by children who are given ample opportunities to grow and develop optimally, both physically, mentally and socially.
The existence of Regional Regulations on the Protection of Women and Children in East Lampung Regency is part of efforts so that women and children can carry out their strategic roles well. With the existence of this Regional Regulation, it means that there is a guarantee that their rights will be fulfilled and protection from various forms of discrimination and treatment that degrades the dignity of women and children.
