CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following shall mean:
Region is East Lampung Regency.
Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as an element of regional government administration.
Regent is the Regent of East Lampung.
Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional representative institution of East Lampung Regency as an element of regional government administration.
Regional Apparatus Working Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the Regional Apparatus Working Unit of East Lampung Regency.
Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as UKPD, is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit of East Lampung Regency.
Child is someone who is not yet 18 years old, including a child in the womb.
Woman is an adult human of the female gender and a person recognized by law as a woman.
Discrimination is any distinction, harassment, or exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on distinctions of humans on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, group, social status, economic status, gender, language, or political beliefs, which results in the reduction, distortion, or elimination of the recognition, implementation, or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life, whether individual or collective, in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural, and other aspects of life.
Violence is any act that results in or is likely to result in physical, sexual, or psychological misery or suffering, including neglect, threats of certain acts, coercion, or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
Gender Mainstreaming is a strategy built to integrate gender as an integral dimension in planning, implementation, budgeting, monitoring, and evaluation of policies/programs/activities in various fields of life and development.
Child Rights Mainstreaming, hereinafter referred to as PUHA, is a child protection strategy by integrating child rights into every development activity, starting from planning, budgeting, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of various laws, regulations, policies, programs, and activities by applying the principle of the best interests of the child in child welfare and protection programs, which includes four (4) areas of work: child health, child education, child protection, and HIV/AIDS management.
Child-Friendly Regency, hereinafter referred to as KLA, is a development model that integrates the commitment and resources of the government, society, and the business world comprehensively and sustainably through the Child Rights Mainstreaming Strategy.
Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the regional government to provide services in the context of women's empowerment, protection of women, and protection of children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including human trafficking.
Safe House is a temporary residence provided to provide protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
Child Helpline 129, hereinafter referred to as TESA 129, is a form of child protection service in the form of local toll-free telephone access (home/office telephone) for children who need protection or are in emergency situations, as well as children who need counseling services.
Victim is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental, or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction, or deprivation of their basic rights as a result of human rights violations, or criminal acts and acts of violence committed by state apparatus or the state, or regional government apparatus, or by individuals.
Implementing officer or functional officer is a health worker, psychologist, psychiatrist, social worker, or legal aid worker who comes from civil servants within their respective departments or special personnel employed at P2TP2A.
Service is an immediate activity and action carried out by professionals in accordance with their respective professions, in the form of counseling, therapy, and advocacy for the purpose of strengthening and recovering victims of violence.
Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both within the family and in the community.
Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with their families, communities, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
Community institution is an institution established by Indonesian citizens voluntarily on the basis of a similarity in vision, mission, profession, function, and activity to participate in development in order to achieve national goals within the framework of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia based on Pancasila, consisting of religious organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, private organizations, social organizations, political organizations, mass media, and other forms of organization.

CHAPTER II
INTENTION AND OBJECTIVE

Article 2

The Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children is intended to provide a reference for the East Lampung Regency Government and various related parties (law enforcement, community institutions, universities, and civil society) to optimize efforts to protect women and children from discrimination and violence.

Article 3

The Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children aims to:
ensure the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children by the Regional Government systematically, comprehensively, sustainably, and in an integrated manner;
increase the Regional Government's commitment to implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children;
realize regional planning and development that is gender-responsive and child-sensitive;
encourage institutions that handle the duties and functions of women's empowerment, women's protection, and child protection, both in the Regional Government and in community institutions, universities, and civil society.

CHAPTER III
PLANNING

Article 4

The Regional Government integrates policies, programs, and development activities for the protection of women and children in the region, which are set forth in the Regional Long-Term Development Plan (RPJPD), Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD), Strategic Plan of the Regional Apparatus Working Unit (Renstra-SKPD), Regional Development Work Plan (RKPD), and Work Plan and Budget of the Regional Apparatus Working Unit (RKA-SKPD), by referring to the national policy on the Protection of Women and Children.
The work unit whose duties and functions handle the protection of women and children facilitates the integration of policies, programs, and development activities for the protection of women and children into the preparation of planning and budget documents as stated in paragraph (1).
In carrying out the implementation as referred to in paragraph (2), the work unit whose duties and functions handle the protection of women and children in the region coordinates with the Regional Development Planning Agency.

CHAPTER IV
IMPLEMENTATION

Part One
Duties and Authorities of the Regional Government

Article 5

The Regent is obligated to implement policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, to eliminate all forms of discrimination and acts of violence against women and children in their region.
The implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) is coordinated by the Vice Regent.

Article 6

The work unit whose duties and functions handle the empowerment of women and children implements policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children that have been established, and in their implementation, cooperates with regional apparatus working units (SKPD), the business world, law enforcement, community institutions, universities, and civil society.
The implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of: policy analysis, coordination, advocacy, socialization, communication, information, and education (KIE), training, service facilitation, provision of services, development of women and child protection models, and other forms.

Article 7

To encourage the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the region, it can be done by establishing, developing, strengthening, or utilizing task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions at the provincial, regency, and city levels.
Membership of the task force, forum, working group, or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of related SKPDs, community institutions, universities, and civil society.
The establishment of the task force, forum, working group, or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) is determined by a Regent's Decision.

Article 8

In implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, the Regional Government of East Lampung Regency may cooperate with international institutions.
The cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 9

The Regent, in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, carries out efforts:
coordination of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children between SKPDs in their territory;
cooperation with law enforcement, community institutions, universities, the business world, and civil society in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children;
strengthening the capacity of Gender Mainstreaming (PUG) and Child Rights Mainstreaming (PUHA) institutions;
facilitation of services needed in the implementation of the protection of women and children;
provision of protection services for women and children;
preparation of affirmative action for the protection of women and children;
establishment and development of Child-Friendly Regency (KLA); and
preparation of a data collection system for the protection of women and children, including a data collection system for violence against women and children.

Article 10

The provision of protection services for women and children as referred to in Article 9 letter e includes efforts to fulfill rights and improve the quality of women and children, especially in the fields of education, health, economy, and legal protection.
For women and children who are victims of violence and human trafficking, the services include: complaint/victim identification services, legal aid, health rehabilitation, psychosocial rehabilitation, social reintegration, repatriation assistance, or other forms according to the needs of the victim's best interests.
The provision of protection services as referred to in paragraph (2) is also intended for women and children in conflict-prone and disaster-affected areas, women and children with disabilities, women and children victims of sexual exploitation, women and children affected by HIV/AIDS, women and children in other vulnerable groups, and children in need of special protection.
The provisions as referred to in paragraphs (1), (2), and (3) are regulated through a Regent's Regulation.

Article 11

The provision of protection services for women and children is carried out through P2TP2A and other institutions in accordance with the needs and best interests of women and children.

Part Two
Implementation of Protection

Article 12

In order to provide protection services for women and children, the regional government establishes P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children.
The establishment and development of P2TP2A are adjusted to the development of priority needs and regional financial capacity, as well as the institutional and personnel capabilities available in the region.

Article 13

P2TP2A has the main task and function as an institution for the empowerment of women in various fields of development, as well as the protection of women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including human trafficking, which is established by the government or based on society.
In carrying out the main tasks and functions as referred to in paragraph (1), P2TP2A may coordinate with sub-districts and various competent parties in carrying out protection efforts for women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including human trafficking.
The implementation of integrated services must be supported by implementing officers or functional officers, including health workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, and legal aid workers provided by related agencies or institutions.

Article 14

P2TP2A consists of various elements, including: work units in charge of women's empowerment, family planning, health, social, religious, manpower, education, population, community empowerment, civil service, police, prosecutor's office, court, hospitals, and community institutions, as well as universities.
The membership of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is determined by a Regent's Decision.

Article 15

P2TP2A, as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children in East Lampung Regency, must gradually be able to function as: a referral center, reproductive health consultation center, legal consultation center, integrated crisis center (PKT), violence handling center, trauma recovery center (trauma center), women's crisis center, training center, science and technology information center (PIPTEK), business consultation center, safe house (shelter), halfway house, child helpline (TESSA) 129, or other forms that support the improvement of protection services for women and children.
Further provisions regarding the organization and work procedures of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by a Regent's Regulation.

CHAPTER V
MONITORING AND EVALUATION

Article 16

To ensure synergy, sustainability, and effectiveness of steps in an integrated manner in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, the Regional Government conducts monitoring.
The monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in East Lampung Regency.
Monitoring is carried out periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of SKPDs that implement policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children.
Monitoring is carried out from planning to the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children for the current year.

Article 17

Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children is carried out at the end of each fiscal year or as needed.
The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children are used as input for the preparation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children for the following year.
The evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI
REPORTING

Article 18

Every work unit and unit that implements policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children must report at the end of each fiscal year.
Reporting on implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out annually and/or as needed.
The form of reporting as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VII
FUNDING

Article 19

Funding for the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the regency is sourced from the regency APBD and other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
The Regional Government may provide funding assistance for the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VIII
GUIDANCE

Article 20

The Regional Government is obligated to conduct guidance on the implementation of protection for women and children.
Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
establishing guidelines and fulfillment standards;
facilitating technical guidance and training;
increasing the provision of facilities;
periodically strengthening institutional capacity through training, consultation, advocacy, and coordination to strengthen the role of P2TP2A;
strengthening institutions or organizational units that handle the protection of women and children;
conducting monitoring and evaluation;

CHAPTER IX
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 21

The community may participate in efforts to implement protection for women and children from discrimination and violence, either individually and/or as an organization.
The community may participate in efforts to implement protection for women and children from discrimination and violence, either as an organization and/or individually.

CHAPTER X
CLOSING

Article 22

This regulation is one of the performance measures for the implementation of development for the protection of women and children in the East Lampung Regency Regional Government.

Article 23

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of its enactment.
In order for everyone to know, it is ordered that this Regional Regulation be enacted by placing it in the Regional Gazette of East Lampung Regency.

I. GENERAL

The right to protection for every citizen has actually been guaranteed by the constitution. This can be observed in the purpose of forming the state as intended in the 4th paragraph of the Preamble to the 1945 Constitution, which states that "The State protects the entire Indonesian nation and the entire homeland of Indonesia and to advance general welfare, educate the nation's life, and participate in implementing world order based on independence, eternal peace, and social justice." The meaning of protecting here includes protecting every citizen from all forms of discrimination and violence. Furthermore, Article 28 G paragraph (2) of the 1945 Constitution states that everyone has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity.
However, in reality, the problems of discrimination, exploitation, violence, neglect, and human trafficking still often occur, especially among women and children groups. This shows how vulnerable both of these groups are. In fact, if examined further, women and children have a very strategic role in the dynamics of a nation's progress. Women, as part of the human resource potential, also determine the success of a family, community, and country. Success can only be achieved when there is good cooperation between female and male resources. Meanwhile, children are the shoots, potential, and young generation who will continue the nation's struggle. This responsibility can only be carried out well by children who are given broad opportunities to grow and develop optimally, both physically, mentally, and socially.
The existence of a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children in East Lampung Regency is part of the effort so that women and children can carry out their strategic roles well. With the existence of this Regional Regulation, it means there is a guarantee for the fulfillment of their rights and protection from various forms of discrimination and treatment that degrades the dignity of women and children.
