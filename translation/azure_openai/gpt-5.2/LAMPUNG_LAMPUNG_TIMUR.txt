CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
Region means East Lampung Regency.
Regional Government means the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements administering regional government.
Regent means the Regent of East Lampung.
The Regional People’s Representative Council, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people’s representative institution of East Lampung Regency as an element administering regional government.
Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, means the Regional Apparatus Work Unit of East Lampung Regency.
Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as UKPD, means the Regional Apparatus Work Unit of East Lampung Regency.
Child means a person who is not yet 18 years old, including a child in the womb.
Woman means an adult human of the female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
Discrimination means any distinction, harassment, or exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on distinctions among humans on the basis of religion, tribe, race, ethnicity, group/class, social status, economic status, sex, language, political belief, resulting in the reduction, deviation, or elimination of recognition, implementation, or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life, whether individual or collective, in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural fields and other aspects of life.
Violence means any act that results in or causes misery and suffering, whether physical, sexual, or psychological, including neglect, threats of certain acts, coercion, or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
Protection of women means all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
Child protection means all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
Gender Mainstreaming is a strategy built to integrate gender as an integral dimension in planning, implementation, budgeting, monitoring, and evaluation of policies/programs/activities in various fields of life and development.
Mainstreaming of Children’s Rights, hereinafter referred to as PUHA, is a child protection strategy by integrating children’s rights into every development activity from the preparation of planning, budgeting, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of various laws and regulations, policies, programs, and activities by applying the principle of the best interests of the child in child welfare and protection programs covering four (4) areas of work, namely child health, child education, child protection, and HIV/AIDS prevention and control.
Child-Friendly Regency, hereinafter referred to as KLA, is a development model that integrates the commitment and resources of government, society, and the business sector comprehensively and sustainably through the Children’s Rights Mainstreaming Strategy.
Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the regional government to provide services in the context of women’s empowerment, protection of women, and protection of children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons.
Safe House is a temporary residence provided to provide protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
Child Friend Telephone 129, hereinafter referred to as TESA 129, is a form of child protection service in the form of access to a local toll-free telephone (home/office telephone) for children who need protection or are in an emergency situation or children who need counseling services.
Victim means an individual or group of people who experience suffering, whether physical, mental, or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction, or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights violations (HAM), or criminal acts and acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
Implementing officers or functional officers are health workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, legal aid personnel who come from civil servants within their respective agencies or special personnel employed at P2TP2A.
Service means activities and immediate actions carried out by professional personnel according to their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy, and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
Rehabilitation means the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both within the family and in society.
Social Reintegration means efforts to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
Community institutions are institutions formed voluntarily by members of the Indonesian citizenry on the basis of shared vision, mission, profession, function, and activities to participate in development in order to achieve national goals within the framework of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia based on Pancasila, consisting of religious organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, private organizations, social organizations, political organizations, mass media, and other forms of organizations.



CHAPTER II
INTENT AND PURPOSE

Article 2

This Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children is intended to provide guidance to the Government of East Lampung Regency and various related parties (law enforcement, community institutions, higher education institutions, and civil society) to optimize efforts to protect women and children from discrimination and violence.

Article 3

This Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children aims to:
ensure the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children by the Regional Government in a systematic, comprehensive, continuous, and integrated manner;
increase the commitment of the Regional Government in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children;
realize regional planning and development that is gender-responsive and child-sensitive;
encourage institutions that handle the duties and functions of women’s empowerment, protection of women, and child protection both within the Regional Government and in community institutions, higher education institutions, and civil society.



CHAPTER III
PLANNING

Article 4

The Regional Government integrates policies, programs, and development activities for the protection of women and children in the region as set forth in the Regional Long-Term Development Plan (RPJPD), Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD), Strategic Plan of Regional Apparatus Work Units (Renstra-SKPD), Regional Government Work Plan (RKPD), and Work Plan and Budget of Regional Apparatus Work Units (RKA-SKPD), with reference to national policies on the Protection of Women and Children.

The work unit whose duties and functions handle the protection of women and children facilitates the integration of policies, programs, and development activities for the protection of women and children into the preparation of planning and budgeting documents as referred to in paragraph (1).
In carrying out as referred to in paragraph (2), the work unit whose duties and functions handle the protection of women and children in the region coordinates with the Regional Development Planning Agency.



CHAPTER IV
IMPLEMENTATION

Part One
Duties and Authorities of the Regional Government

Article 5

The Regent is obliged to implement policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, to eliminate all forms of discrimination and acts of violence against women and children in his/her region.
The implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) is coordinated by the Deputy Regent.

Article 6

The work unit whose duties and functions handle the empowerment of women and children implements the established policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, and in its implementation cooperates with regional apparatus work units (SKPD), the business sector, law enforcement, community institutions, higher education institutions, and civil society.
The implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) takes the form of: policy analysis, coordination, advocacy, socialization, communication, information, and education (KIE), training, facilitation of services, provision of services, development of models for the protection of women and children, and other forms.

Article 7

To encourage the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the region, it may be carried out by establishing, developing, strengthening, or utilizing task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions at the provincial, regency, and municipal levels.
The membership of the task force, forum, working group, or other institution as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of relevant SKPD, community institutions, higher education institutions, and civil society.
The establishment of the task force, forum, working group, or other institution as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by a Regent’s Decree.

Article 8

In implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, the Regional Government of East Lampung Regency may cooperate with international institutions.
The cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 9

In implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, the Regent undertakes efforts to:
coordinate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children among SKPD within his/her territory;
cooperate with law enforcement, community institutions, higher education institutions, the business sector, and civil society in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children;
strengthen the institutional capacity of PUG and PUHA;
facilitate the services needed in implementing the protection of women and children;
provide protection services for women and children;
prepare affirmative action for the protection of women and children;
establish and develop KLA; and
prepare a data collection system for the protection of women and children, including a data collection system on violence against women and children.

Article 10

The provision of protection services for women and children as referred to in Article 9 letter e includes efforts to fulfill rights and improve the quality of women and children, especially in the fields of education, health, economy, and legal protection.
For women and children who are victims of violence and trafficking in persons, services include: complaint/identification services for victims, legal aid, health rehabilitation, psychosocial rehabilitation, social reintegration, repatriation assistance, or other forms according to the needs and best interests of the victim.
The provision of protection services as referred to in paragraph (2) is also intended for women and children in conflict- and disaster-prone areas, women and children with disabilities, women and children victims of sexual exploitation, women and children affected by HIV/AIDS, women and children in other vulnerable groups, and children who need special protection.
The provisions as referred to in paragraphs (1), (2), and (3) are regulated through a Regent Regulation.

Article 11

The provision of protection services for women and children is carried out through P2TP2A, and other institutions in accordance with the needs and best interests of women and children.

Part Two
Implementation of Protection

Article 12

In order to provide protection services for women and children, the regional government establishes P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children.

The establishment and development of P2TP2A is adjusted to the development of priority needs and the region’s financial capacity as well as the capacity of existing institutions and personnel in the region.

Article 13

P2TP2A has the main duties and functions as an institution for women’s empowerment in various fields of development, as well as the protection of women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons, established by the government or community-based.
In carrying out the main duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (1), P2TP2A may coordinate with sub-districts and various competent parties in undertaking efforts to protect women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons.
The implementation of integrated services must be supported by implementing officers or functional officers including health workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, and legal aid personnel provided by the relevant agencies or institutions.

Article 14

P2TP2A consists of various elements, including: work units in charge of women’s empowerment, family planning, health, social affairs, religion, manpower, education, population, community empowerment, public order officers, police, prosecutor’s office, courts, hospitals, and community institutions, as well as higher education institutions.
The membership of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by a Regent’s Decree.

Article 15

P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children in East Lampung Regency, gradually must be able to function as: a referral center, a reproductive health consultation center, a legal consultation center, an integrated crisis center (PKT), a center for handling acts of violence, a trauma recovery center (trauma center), a women’s crisis center, a training center, a science and technology information center (PIPTEK), a business consultation center, a safe house (shelter), a halfway house, the child friend telephone (TESSA) 129, or other forms that support the improvement of protection services for women and children.
Further provisions regarding the organization and work procedures of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by a Regent Regulation.



CHAPTER V
MONITORING AND EVALUATION

Article 16

To ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated steps in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, the Regional Government conducts monitoring.

Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine developments and obstacles in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in East Lampung Regency.
Monitoring is carried out periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of SKPD that implement policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children.
Monitoring is carried out from planning through implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children for the current year.
.

Article 17

Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children is carried out at the end of each fiscal year or if necessary as needed.
The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children are used as input material for the preparation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children for the following year.
The evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.



CHAPTER VI
REPORTING

Article 18

Each work unit and work unit that implements policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children must report at the end of each fiscal year.
Reporting on implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out annually and/or if necessary.
The form of reporting as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.



CHAPTER VII
FUNDING

Article 19

Funding for the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the regency is sourced from the regency APBD and other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
The Regional Government may provide funding assistance for the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.



CHAPTER VIII
GUIDANCE

Article 20

The Regional Government is obliged to provide guidance for the implementation of protection for women and children.
Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
establishing guidelines and standards for fulfillment;
facilitating technical guidance and training;
increasing the provision of facilities;
strengthening institutional capacity periodically through training, consultation, advocacy, and coordination to strengthen the role of P2TP2A;
strengthening institutions or organizational units that handle the protection of women and children;
conducting monitoring and evaluation;



CHAPTER IX
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION
Article 21

The community may participate in efforts to implement protection for women and children from discrimination and violence, either individually and/or through organizations.
The community may participate in efforts to implement protection for women and children from discrimination and violence either through organizations and/or individually



CHAPTER X
CLOSING

Article 22

This Regulation is one measure of performance for the implementation of development for the protection of women and children in the Regional Government of East Lampung Regency.

Article 23

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of its promulgation.

So that everyone may know it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of East Lampung Regency.

IN EAST LAMPUNG REGENCY


I. GENERAL

The right to obtain protection for every citizen has in fact been guaranteed by the constitution. This can be observed in the purpose of establishing the state as referred to in the fourth paragraph of the Preamble to the 1945 Constitution, which states that “The State protects the entire Indonesian nation and the entire homeland of Indonesia and to promote the general welfare, educate the life of the nation and participate in implementing world order based on freedom, lasting peace and social justice.” The meaning of protection here includes protecting every citizen from all forms of discrimination and violence. In addition, Article 28G paragraph (2) of the 1945 Constitution states that every person has the right to be free from torture or treatment that demeans human dignity.
However, in reality, problems of discrimination, exploitation, violence, neglect, and trafficking in persons still often occur, especially among women and children. This shows how vulnerable these two groups are. Whereas, upon closer examination, women and children have a very strategic role in the dynamics of a nation’s progress. Women, as part of the potential of human resources, help determine the success of a family, society, and the state. Success can only be achieved when good cooperation is established between female and male human resources. Meanwhile, children are buds, potential, and the young generation who will continue the ideals of the nation’s struggle. This responsibility can only be borne well by children who are given broad opportunities to grow and develop optimally, physically, mentally, and socially.
The existence of a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children in East Lampung Regency is part of efforts so that women and children can carry out their strategic roles properly. With the existence of this Regional Regulation, it means there is a guarantee of the fulfillment of their rights and protection from various forms of discrimination and treatment that demeans the dignity of women and children.
