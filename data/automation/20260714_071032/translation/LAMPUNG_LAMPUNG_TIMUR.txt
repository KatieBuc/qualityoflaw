that women and children are vulnerable groups who often experience discrimination and violence and therefore need to receive protection;
that in order to guarantee the availability of protection services for women and children in East Lampung Regency, a regional regulation on the protection of women and children is necessary;
that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b, and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children in East Lampung Regency;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by: Region is East Lampung Regency.
Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
Regent is the Regent of East Lampung.
Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of East Lampung Regency as an element of regional government administration.
Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit of East Lampung Regency.
Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UKPD, is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit of East Lampung Regency.
Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including a child in the womb.
Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.
Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion that is direct or indirect based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that results in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individual and collective in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
Gender Mainstreaming is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension in the planning, implementation, budgeting, monitoring and evaluation of policies/programs/activities in various fields of life and development.
Mainstreaming of Children's Rights hereinafter referred to as PUHA is a child protection strategy by integrating children's rights into every development activity from the preparation of planning, budgeting, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of various laws and regulations, policies, programs, and activities by applying the principle of the best interests of the child in child welfare and protection programs covering four (4) areas of work, namely the field of child health, the field of child education, the field of child protection and the field of HIV/AIDS prevention.
Child-Friendly Regency hereinafter referred to as KLA is a development model that integrates the commitment and resources of government, society and the business world comprehensively and sustainably through the Strategy of Mainstreaming children's rights.
Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service unit established by the regional government to provide services in the context of women's empowerment, protection of women, and protection of children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons.
Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
Child Friend Telephone 129, hereinafter referred to as TESA 129, is a form of child protection service in the form of local toll-free telephone access (home/office telephone) for children who need protection or in emergency situations as well as children who need counseling services.
Victim is an individual or group of people who experience suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
Implementing officer or functional officer is a health worker, psychologist, psychiatrist, social worker, legal aid worker who comes from civil servants within their respective agencies or special personnel employed at P2TP2A Service is an activity and immediate action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
Community institution is an institution formed by members of the Indonesian citizen community voluntarily on the basis of shared vision, mission, profession, function and activities to participate in development in order to achieve national goals within the framework of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia based on Pancasila, consisting of religious organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, private organizations, social organizations, political organizations, mass media, and other forms of organizations.

CHAPTER II
PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVE
Article 2
The Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children is intended to provide guidance to the East Lampung Regency Government and various related parties (law enforcement, community institutions, universities and civil society) to optimize efforts to protect women and children from discrimination and violence.

Article 3
The Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children aims to: guarantee the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children by the Regional Government in a systematic, comprehensive, continuous and integrated manner;
increase the commitment of the Regional Government in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children;
realize regional planning and development that is gender responsive and child sensitive;
encourage institutions that handle the duties and functions of women's empowerment, women's protection, and child protection both in the Regional Government as well as community institutions, universities, and civil society.

CHAPTER III
PLANNING
Article 4
The Regional Government shall integrate policies, programs and activities for the development of protection of women and children in the region as set forth in the Regional Long-Term Development Plan (RPJPD), Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD), Strategic Plan of Regional Work Units (Renstra-SKPD), Regional Development Work Plan (RKPD), and Work Plan and Budget of Regional Work Units (RKA-SKPD), with reference to the national policy on Protection of Women and Children.
The work unit whose duties and functions handle the protection of women and children shall facilitate the integration of policies, programs, and activities for the development of protection of women and children into the preparation of planning and budget documents as set forth in paragraph (1).
In implementing as referred to in paragraph (2), the work unit whose duties and functions handle the protection of women and children in the region shall coordinate with the Regional Development Planning Agency.

# BAB IV
IMPLEMENTATION
First Part
Duties and Authorities of Regional Government

Article 5
The Regent is obligated to implement policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, to eliminate all forms of discrimination and acts of violence against women and children in their region.
The implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be coordinated by the Vice Regent.

Article 6
Work units whose duties and functions handle the empowerment of women and children shall implement policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children that have been established, and in their implementation cooperate with regional apparatus work units (SKPD), the business sector, law enforcement, community institutions, universities, and civil society.
The implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of: policy analysis, coordination, advocacy, socialization, communication, information, and education (KIE), training, service facilitation, service provision, development of models for the protection of women and children, and other forms.

Article 7
To encourage the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the regions, this may be carried out by establishing, developing, strengthening, or utilizing task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions at the provincial, regency and municipal levels.
The membership of task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of relevant SKPD, community institutions, universities, and civil society.
The establishment of task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree.

Article 8
In implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, the Regional Government of East Lampung Regency may conduct cooperation with international institutions.
The cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 9
The Regent in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, shall undertake efforts to: coordinate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children among SKPDs in their region;
cooperate with law enforcement, community institutions, universities, business sector, and civil society in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children;
strengthen the institutional capacity of PUG and PUHA;
facilitate services required in the implementation of protection of women and children;
provide protection services for women and children;
formulate affirmative action for the protection of women and children;
establish and develop KLA; and formulate a data system for the protection of women and children, including a data system on violence against women and children.

Article 10
The provision of protection services for women and children as referred to in Article 9 letter e includes efforts to fulfill the rights and improve the quality of women and children, especially in the fields of education, health, economy and legal protection.
For women and children who are victims of violence and human trafficking, services include: complaint services/victim identification, legal assistance, health rehabilitation, psychosocial rehabilitation, social reintegration, repatriation assistance or other forms according to the needs and best interests of the victim.
The provision of protection services as referred to in paragraph (2) is also intended for women and children in conflict-prone and disaster areas, women and children with disabilities, women and children victims of sexual exploitation, women and children affected by HIV/AIDS, women and children in other vulnerable groups, and children who require special protection.
The provisions as referred to in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) shall be regulated through a Regent Regulation.

Article 11
The provision of protection services for women and children is implemented through P2TP2A, and other institutions in accordance with the needs and best interests of women and children.

Part Two
Implementation of Protection

Article 12
In order to provide protection services for women and children, the regional government establishes P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children.
The establishment and development of P2TP2A is adjusted to the development of priority needs, and the regional financial capacity as well as the institutional and personnel capacity available in the region.

Article 13
P2TP2A has the principal duties and functions as an institution for women's empowerment in various fields of development, as well as protection of women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons, which is established by the government or community-based In carrying out the principal duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (1), P2TP2A may coordinate with sub-districts, and various competent parties in making efforts to protect women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons.
The implementation of integrated services must be supported by implementing officers or functional officers which include health workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, legal aid workers provided by relevant agencies or institutions.

Article 14
P2TP2A consists of various elements, among others: work units that handle women's empowerment, family planning, health, social affairs, religion, manpower, education, population, community empowerment, civil service police, police, prosecutor's office, courts, hospitals, and community institutions, as well as universities.
The membership of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be determined by Regent's Decree.

Article 15
P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children in East Lampung Regency, must gradually be able to function as: a referral center, reproductive health consultation center, legal consultation center, integrated crisis center (PKT), violence handling center, trauma recovery center (trauma center), women's crisis center (women crisis center), training center, science and technology information center (PIPTEK), business consultation center, safe house (shelter), transit house, children's friend telephone (TESSA) 129 or other forms that support the improvement of protection services for women and children.
Further provisions regarding the organization and working procedures of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER V
MONITORING AND EVALUATION
Article 16
To guarantee synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, the Regional Government shall conduct monitoring.
The monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in East Lampung Regency.
Monitoring shall be conducted periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of SKPD implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children.
Monitoring shall be conducted from the planning stage through to the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children for the current year.

Article 17
Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children shall be conducted at the end of each fiscal year or when necessary according to needs.
The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children shall be used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children for the following year.
The evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI
REPORTING
Article 18
Every work unit and work division that implements policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children must conduct reporting at the end of each fiscal year. Reporting of implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted annually and/or when necessary.
The form of reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VII
FUNDING
Article 19
Funding for the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the regency is sourced from the regency APBD and other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
The Regional Government may provide funding assistance for the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VIII
GUIDANCE
Article 20
The Regional Government is obligated to conduct guidance on the implementation of protection for women and children.
The guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes: establishing guidelines and fulfillment standards;
facilitating technical guidance and training;
improving the provision of facilities;
conducting institutional capacity strengthening periodically through training, consultation, advocacy and coordination to strengthen the role of P2TP2A;
strengthening institutions or organizational units that handle the protection of women and children;
conducting monitoring and evaluation;

CHAPTER IX
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION
Article 21
The community may participate in efforts to implement protection for women and children from discrimination and violence, either individually and/or organizationally.
The community may participate in efforts to implement protection for women and children from discrimination and violence either organizationally and/or individually

BAB X
CLOSING PROVISIONS
Article 22
This Regulation constitutes one of the performance measures for the implementation of development of women and children protection in the Regional Government of East Lampung Regency.

Article 23
This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.
In order that every person shall know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placement in the Regional Gazette of East Lampung Regency.

I. GENERAL
The right to obtain protection for every citizen has actually been guaranteed by the constitution. This can be observed in the objectives of state formation as referred to in the fourth paragraph of the Preamble of the 1945 Constitution which states that "The State protects all Indonesian people and all Indonesian territory and to promote general welfare, educate the life of the nation and participate in implementing world order based on freedom, lasting peace and social justice". The meaning of protecting here includes protecting every citizen from all forms of discrimination and violence. In addition, in Article 28 G paragraph 2 of the 1945 Constitution which states that every person has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity.
However, in reality the problems of discrimination, exploitation, violence, neglect, and human trafficking still frequently occur, especially among women and children groups. This shows how vulnerable these two groups are. Whereas if examined further, women and children have a very strategic role in the dynamics of national progress. Women as part of human resource potential who contribute to determining the success of a family, society, and state. Success can only be achieved when good cooperation is established between women and men resources. Meanwhile, children are the buds, potential, and young generation successors of the nation's ideals and struggle. This responsibility can only be carried out well by children who are given broad opportunities to grow and develop optimally, both physically, mentally and socially.
The existence of the Regional Regulation on Protection of Women and Children in East Lampung Regency is part of the effort so that women and children can carry out their strategic roles properly. With the existence of the Regional Regulation, it means there is a guarantee of the fulfillment of their rights and protection from various forms of discrimination and treatment that degrades the dignity of women and children.
