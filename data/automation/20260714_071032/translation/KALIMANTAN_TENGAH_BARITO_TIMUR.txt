Considering : a. that women and children with their dignity have the right to be protected from various acts of violence, exploitation, and discrimination that marginalize and degrade their status as human beings;

b. that negative behavior and actions that may result in women and children being in a position of oppression or powerlessness in the scope of social community, or in the scope of legal processes should be given special treatment that maintains the stability of their soul and spirit to remain capable of carrying out their lives in social interaction;

c. that the Regional Government is obligated to provide protection for women and children;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation regarding the Protection of Women and Children;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is the Region of East Barito Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of Regional Government Administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of East Barito.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives Institution which is positioned as an element of Government administration.
5. Regional Apparatus of East Barito Regency is an auxiliary element of the Regent and Regional House of Representatives of East Barito Regency in the Administration of government affairs which are the authority of East Barito Regency.
6. Dinas is a PD that handles government affairs in the field of women and children.
7. Technical Service Unit is a Regional Technical Service Unit that handles the affairs of protection of Women and children.
8. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child who is still in the womb.
9. Woman is an adult human of female sex and a person who is recognized by law as a Woman.
10. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
11. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.
12. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.
13. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or fainting and/or causes death.
14. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness, and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
15. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
16. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
17. Victim of acts of violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
18. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
19. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.
20. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution that has the expertise to provide assistance.
21. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of acts of violence to the family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.
22. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
23. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide Protection.
24. Institution is an agency/dinas/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organizations (NGOs) that provide Assistance.
25. Regional Action Plan hereinafter abbreviated as RAD is a foundation and guideline for relevant dinas, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.
26. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
27. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
28. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral lines up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is the dependent of a woman and/or child.
29. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
30. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the annual financial plan of the Regional Government which is discussed and approved jointly by the Regional Government and DPRD and established by Regional Regulation.
31. Gender Equality is the view that all people should receive equal treatment and not be discriminated against based on their gender identity.
32. Medicolegal Service is a form of health service carried out by medical personnel using medical science and technology based on the authority possessed for legal purposes and to implement applicable regulations.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES
Article 2 (1) The implementation of protection for women and children shall be carried out based on the principles of:
a. respect for the rights of women and children;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination in the empowerment of women and children;
d. the best interests of women and children; and
e. restoration of social and economic rights for women and children who experience acts of violence or neglect. (2) The objectives of implementing protection for women and children include:
a. preventing acts of violence or neglect against women and children including trafficking in persons;
b. eliminating all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
c. protecting and providing a sense of security for women and children;
d. providing services to women and children who are victims of acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses;
e. facilitating and conducting mediation of domestic disputes to realize the integrity of harmonious and prosperous households; and
f. seeking compensation/restitution for losses suffered by victims from perpetrators of violence.

CHAPTER III
SCOPE
Article 3 The protection of women and children in the region includes prevention, services, and empowerment for victims of physical violence, psychological violence, sexual violence, exploitation, human trafficking, and domestic neglect including child deviant behavior.
The implementation of protection for women (2) and children from acts of violence is a joint obligation and responsibility among the Regional Government, community, family, and parents.

CHAPTER XIII
SANCTION PROVISIONS
Article 24 (1) Every person who intentionally does not provide protection to women and children resulting in the occurrence of acts of violence, allows acts of violence to occur, and/or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as regulated in the Laws and Regulations.
(2) If officials appointed to provide protection do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities, they shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the Laws and Regulations.
(3) Officials who carry out service and protection duties for Children and Women from acts of violence, who violate service principles, may be subject to sanctions in accordance with the internal mechanisms of the organization.

Article 27

ABOUT
THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
The role of women is very important because they are the mothers of the nation who serve as the pillars of the family to build the future of human resources in various fields including family economic empowerment, likewise with children who are the generation of the nation who will play a role in filling development in the future.
Children and Women must receive protection from acts of violence, exploitation, human trafficking and neglect by responsible parties. Protection is intended to create a peaceful and prosperous atmosphere for all levels of society in fostering life and togetherness within the regional area. Violence cannot be avoided just like that, but must be prevented as early as possible from spreading to a level that is detrimental to all parties. Protection efforts are prioritized on prevention efforts and minimizing incidents.
Acts of violence against Children and Women constitute violations of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination. So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of Children and Women victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts in the Region so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their protection. Regional Regulations regulate protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of Children and Women victims of violence in Kotabaru Regency. As one of the efforts to protect Children and Women, it is carried out through guidance, training and assistance to victims and families of violence victims. Economic empowerment is also carried out for the prevention of women as victims, which can be done by growing and developing micro and small enterprises in accordance with local potential. To grow micro and small enterprises, it is necessary to increase the capacity and capability of women through assistance and guidance so that women can develop their businesses.

Article 2

Letter d What is meant by "the best interests of victims" is all best actions concerning victims carried out by the Government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, therefore the best interests of victims must be the primary consideration.
Letter e What is meant by restoration of socio-economic rights is increasing understanding, awareness, responsibility, commitment, participation, capability and independence for the purpose of economic improvement and quality of women or target groups so as to avoid problems.

What is meant by "trafficking in persons" is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether conducted within a country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in a person being exploited.
What is meant by "Household Neglect" is: an act that results in the non-fulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
b. an act of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate;
c. an act that neglects a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person;
d. an act that results in economic dependence by means of limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.
