# PROTECTION AND EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN

REGENT OF BOYOLALI

CENTRAL JAVA PROVINCE

# REGIONAL REGULATION OF BOYOLALI REGENCY

NUMBER 9 OF 2021

CONCERNING

PROTECTION AND EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF BOYOLALI,

Considering:

a. that women have an important position in development, have the right to obtain a sense of security, freedom from threats of fear, and freedom from torture or treatment that degrades dignity as mandated and bestowed by Almighty God;
b. that various forms of violence, mistreatment, exploitation, and neglect of women occurring in the region require strategic efforts from the regional Government to provide protection for women;
c. that in order to realize regional policies to administer governmental affairs that are regional authority in the field of women's protection based on Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government as amended several times most recently by Law Number 11 of 2020 concerning Job Creation, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection and Empowerment of Women;

In View of:

1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 13 of 1950 concerning Formation of Regency Regions Within the Province of Central Java (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1950 Number 42);
3. Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419;
4. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5512) as amended several times most recently by Law Number 11 of 2020 concerning Job Creation (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2020 Number 245, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6573;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Part One

Definitions

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

1. Region is Boyolali Regency.
2. Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the regional government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
4. Regent is the Regent of Boyolali.
5. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution that is positioned as an element of the Regional Government administrator.
6. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and DPRD in administering governmental affairs that are the authority of the Region.
7. Regional Technical Implementation Unit is a technical implementation unit established by the Regional Government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, exploitation, discrimination, who require special protection, and other problems.
8. Integrated Service Center is a non-structural institution that coordinates the implementation of an integrated service system for women victims.
9. Human Rights are a set of rights inherent in the nature and existence of human beings as creatures of God Almighty and are His gift that must be respected, upheld and protected by the state, law, government, and every person for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity and worth.
10. Women's Empowerment is all efforts to improve physical, mental spiritual, social capacity, knowledge and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.
11. Women's Protection is all efforts aimed at protecting and providing a sense of security to women as well as fulfilling their rights through consistent, structured, and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
12. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs, which results in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of Human Rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
13. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural circumstances of society.
14. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.
15. Violence Against Women is any act based on gender differentiation that results in, or could result in physical, psychological and/or sexual suffering to women, including threats, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty whether occurring in the public sphere or in the sphere of private life.
16. Gender-Based Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as KBG, is a violation of Human Rights caused by gender-based role labeling, which denies human dignity and the right to oneself that has an impact on, or has an impact resembling physical, psychological, and sexual or brings suffering to women and children including all forms of actions, coercion, arbitrariness and deprivation of freedom, carried out in the public sphere or private life.
17. Disaster is an event or series of events that threaten and disrupt the life and livelihood of the community caused by natural and/or non-natural factors as well as human factors resulting in human casualties, environmental damage, property losses, and psychological impacts.
18. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman.
19. Elderly is a person who has reached the age of 60 (sixty) years and above.
20. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.
21. Person with Disability is every person who experiences physical, intellectual, mental, and/or sensory limitations in the long term who in interacting with the environment may experience obstacles and difficulties to participate fully and effectively with other citizens based on equal rights.
22. Labor Force is every person who is capable of performing work to produce goods and/or services either to meet their own needs or for the Community.
23. Worker/Laborer is every person who works by receiving wages or remuneration in other forms.
24. Data and Information System is the collection, management, and utilization of Women's data needed in the Implementation of Women's Protection and Empowerment.

## Part Two

Principles, Objectives, and Scope

#### Article 2

Women's Protection and Empowerment shall be implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect for Human Rights;
b. justice and Gender Equality;
c. non-discrimination; and
d. victim protection.

#### Article 3

Women's Protection and Empowerment aims to:
a. increase women's participation in the development process;
b. improve the quality of life of women;
c. provide guarantees to women in the fulfillment of rights as human beings; and
d. provide a sense of security by increasing protection for women from various acts of violence.

#### Article 4

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:
a. women's rights;
b. Women's Protection;
c. Women's Empowerment;
d. obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government;
e. women's protection and Empowerment strategy;
f. mechanism for implementing women's protection and Empowerment;
g. institutions;
h. coordination and cooperation;
i. development of Data and Information Systems;
j. Community participation;
k. funding; and
l. guidance and supervision.

# CHAPTER II

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 5

1. Every woman has the right to:  
a. live and maintain her life and improve her standard;   
b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family, and continue her lineage;  
c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life to become a faithful, pious, responsible, noble, happy, and prosperous human being in accordance with Human Rights;  
d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without Discrimination;  
e. be involved in every stage of the development process;  
f. be free from slavery or enslavement and threats;  
g. obtain protection of her person, Family, honor, dignity and property rights;  
h. obtain welfare and a decent life;  
i. participate in politics;  
j. perform legal acts;  
k. freely choose a partner in her marriage; and  
l. obtain special protection in the performance of her work or profession against matters that may threaten her safety and or health in relation to women's reproductive function.  
2. Every wife during the marriage bond has equal rights and responsibilities with her husband over all matters relating to their married life, relations with their children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
3. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to children with due regard to the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER III

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 6

Implementation of Protection of Women is provided to:  
a. women Workers/Laborers;  
b. Elderly women;  
c. women with Disabilities;  
d. homeless women;  
e. domestic worker women;  
f. women as Family economic pillars;  
g. women former residents of correctional institutions;  
h. women victims of Disasters; and  
i. women victims of violence.

## Part Two

Women Workers/Laborers

#### Article 7

1. Implementation of Protection for women Workers/Laborers is realized by preventing acts of economic exploitation and cruel and inhumane treatment and seeking proper appreciation for work performed by women.  
2. Women Workers/Laborers as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection over:  
a. recognition of rights, wages, and decent working conditions;  
b. health insurance and employment social security;  
c. access to information and legal consultation services;  
d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve status; and  
e. violence in the workplace.

## Part Three

Elderly Women

#### Article 8

1. Implementation of protection for Elderly women in order to extend life expectancy so they can carry out their social functions and play an active role naturally in their social life.  
2. Elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to Protection over:  
a. guarantee of continuity and personal development;  
b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, arts and culture as well as utilization of information and communication technology;  
c. accessibility of information, legal assistance, mobility, social services, public facilities, and buildings; and  
d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

## Part Four

Women with Disabilities

#### Article 9

1. Implementation of protection for women with Disabilities in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of Discrimination including freedom from cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity and freedom from exploitation.  
2. Women with Disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection over:  
a. guarantee of continuity and personal development;  
b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, sports, arts and culture as well as utilization of information and communication technology;  
c. accessibility of information, legal assistance, mobility, social services; and  
d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

## Part Five

Homeless Women

#### Article 10

1. Implementation of protection for homeless women in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.  
2. Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection over:  
a. decent housing;  
b. health and social security;  
c. population administration registration;  
d. opportunities to obtain skills to improve economic status; and  
e. access to information and legal consultation services.

## Part Six

Domestic Worker Women

#### Article 11

1. Implementation of protection for domestic worker women in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of Discrimination, cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation in employment relations.  
2. Domestic worker women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection over:  
a. recognition of rights, wages, and decent working conditions;  
b. health insurance and employment social security;  
c. access to information and legal consultation services; and  
d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Seven

Women as Family Economic Pillars

#### Article 12

1. Implementation of protection for women as Family economic pillars in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of Discrimination, negative stigma and economic empowerment for women as Family economic pillars.  
2. Women as Family economic pillars as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection over:  
a. acts of Discrimination and efforts to weaken the function of women as Family economic pillars;  
b. strengthening capacity and recognition as Family economic pillars in their environment; and  
c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.

## Part Eight

Women Former Residents of Correctional Institutions

#### Article 13

1. Implementation of protection for women former residents of correctional institutions in order to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting in community life, so they can carry out life normally.  
2. Women former residents of correctional institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection over:  
a. social rehabilitation and social reintegration; and  
b. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Nine

Women Victims of Disasters

#### Article 14

1. Implementation of protection for women victims of Disasters in order to provide protection during Disasters and post-Disaster.  
2. Protection for women victims of Disasters as referred to in paragraph (1) includes women from vulnerable groups in Disasters.  
3. Women victims of Disasters as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:  
a. access to basic needs;  
b. access to adequate temporary shelter;  
c. Health services;  
d. guarantee of security;  
e. specific needs of women;  
f. psychosocial service needs; and  
g. post-Disaster recovery.  
4. The Regent coordinates the Protection of women victims of Disasters at the Regional level as referred to in paragraph (2) by:  
a. providing information and disaggregated data based on gender, age, and type of vulnerability of vulnerable groups;  
b. building stakeholder commitment to prevention, handling, and protection of vulnerable groups in Disasters;  
c. providing women-friendly space facilities, as well as Gender-responsive facilities and infrastructure in emergency response and rehabilitation conditions that accommodate local resources;  
d. providing complaint, handling, and referral services needed by women victims of violence that accommodate local wisdom; and  
e. ensuring victims or survivors receive empowerment services according to their potential, abilities, and needs so they can recover quickly, become independent, and productive.  
5. Implementation of Protection of Women from Disaster victims refers to guidelines and standards established based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Ten

Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 15

1. Implementation of protection for women victims of violence in order to provide legal protection and/or health recovery.  
2. Protection for women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. provision of safe houses or alternative shelter;  
b. legal aid;  
c. assistance after the occurrence of acts of violence;  
d. rehabilitation and social reintegration in accordance with Regional authority; and  
e. health services.

# CHAPTER IV

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

## Part One

General

#### Article 16

Women's Empowerment is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:  
a. economy;  
b. social culture;  
c. politics and government;  
d. law;  
e. education;  
f. health;  
g. employment; and  
h. social security.

## Part Two

Economic Field

#### Article 17

Women's Empowerment in the economic field is implemented through:  
a. provision of skills and job training;  
b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;  
c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;  
d. facilitation and capital assistance; and  
e. facilitation of marketing network development.

## Part Three

Social and Cultural Field

#### Article 18

Women's Empowerment in the social and cultural field is implemented through:  
a. improvement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education according to potential to improve social status;  
b. improvement of awareness, willingness, and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health;  
c. improvement of awareness and knowledge about planning independent, healthy and prosperous Families including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and  
d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

## Part Four

Political and Governmental Field

#### Article 19

1. Women's Empowerment in the political and governmental field includes:  
a. involvement of women in decision-making in the field of development and life at various levels;  
b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;  
c. participation in general elections; and  
d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble, and express opinions.  
2. Women's Empowerment in the political and governmental field (2) as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
3. The Regional Government provides guarantees to women to (3) have the right to vote and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting according to the provisions of laws and regulations.  
4. The Regional Government provides opportunities for women to be appointed as Regional Government officials and occupy strategic positions in Regional Government.  
5. The Regional Government and/or political parties may provide political education for women.

## Part Five

Legal Field

#### Article 20

1. Women's Empowerment in the legal field includes:  
a. improvement of awareness and knowledge in the legal field through communication, information, and education services; and  
b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.  
3. Women's Empowerment in the legal field as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Six

Education Field

#### Article 21

1. Women's Empowerment in the education field through the provision of formal and non-formal education for women in the Region.  
2. Women's Empowerment in the education field as referred to in paragraph (1) aims to improve women's capabilities.  
3. Non-formal education as referred to in paragraph (1) includes Women's Empowerment education, skills education, and job training.  
4. Women's Empowerment in the education field is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Seven

Health Field

#### Article 22

1. Women have the right to obtain health services in order to undergo reproductive life and sexual life that is healthy, safe, and free from coercion and/or violence.  
2. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. health services during the period before pregnancy, pregnancy, childbirth, and after giving birth;  
b. pregnancy regulation, contraceptive services, and sexual health; and  
c. reproductive health services.  
3. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented (3) through promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative approaches.

## Part Eight

Employment Field

#### Article 23

1. Women have equal opportunities without Discrimination to obtain employment.  
2. Employers who employ female Workers must provide protection that includes welfare, safety, and health.

## Part Nine

Social Security Field

#### Article 24

1. The Regional Government is responsible for providing guidance facilities for women with social problems.  
2. The Regional Government provides rights to social security for women with social problems.  
3. The responsibility for providing guidance facilities for women with social problems is under the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 25

1. Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility for the protection and Empowerment of Women.  
2. The protection and Empowerment of Women efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), include:  
a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;  
b. determination of implementation guidelines;  
c. provision of services; and  
d. coordination of policies, programs, and activities.  
3. The protection and Empowerment of Women efforts as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## STRATEGY FOR PROTECTION AND EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN

#### Article 26

1. Protection and Empowerment of Women shall be implemented by integrating Gender mainstreaming and Gender-disaggregated data in an integrated manner based on the provisions of laws and regulations.  
2. Protection and Empowerment of Women shall be coordinated by the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

# CHAPTER VII

## MECHANISM FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION AND

## EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN

#### Article 27

The mechanism for the implementation of Protection and Empowerment of Women shall be carried out through the following stages:  
a. formulation of policy planning for programs and activities;  
b. budgeting of programs and activities;  
c. implementation of programs and activities; and  
d. monitoring, evaluation, and reporting.

#### Article 28

1. The formulation of policy planning for programs and activities as referred to in Article 27 letter a, shall be implemented consisting of:  
a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of data;  
b. coordination and synchronization of the preparation of planning for programs and activities with related Regional Apparatus; and  
c. determination of programs and activities in planning documents.  
2. The formulation of policy planning for programs and activities shall be further regulated in minimum service standards and standard operating procedures.  
3. The formulation of policy planning for programs and activities for the implementation of protection and Empowerment of Women, shall be coordinated by the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of planning.  
4. The budgeting of programs and activities as referred to in Article 27 letter b, shall be implemented through general budget policies set forth in the Regional revenue and expenditure budget.  
5. The implementation of programs and activities as referred to in Article 27 letter c, shall be implemented consisting of:  
a. implementation of programs and activities set forth in the budget documents of the Regional Apparatus work unit;  
b. coordination and synchronization of the implementation of activity programs with the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and child protection.  
6. The implementation of monitoring, evaluation, and reporting as referred to in Article 27 letter d, shall be implemented consisting of:  
a. conducting monitoring and evaluation periodically on the implementation of programs and activities that have been carried out;  
b. preparing reports periodically; and  
c. evaluation results as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the following year.

# CHAPTER VIII

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 29

1. In the context of implementing protection and Empowerment of Women, the Regional Government shall establish:  
a. Integrated Service Center; and  
b. Regional Technical Implementation Unit.  
2. In the implementation of services for women victims, the Regional Government shall establish an Integrated Service Center as a victim service network.  
3. The establishment of the Integrated Service Center as referred to in paragraph (2) is so that women victims obtain comprehensive, integrated, quality, inclusive and sustainable services.  
4. In the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection, a Regional Technical Implementation Unit shall be established to carry out operational technical activities and/or certain supporting technical activities for the implementation of women's empowerment and child protection affairs.  
5. Further provisions regarding the Integrated Service Center and Regional Technical Implementation Unit shall be regulated by Regent Regulation in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 30

1. In addition to establishing the Integrated Service Center and Regional Technical Implementation Unit as referred to in Article 29, the Regional Government may establish a task force and/or Regional action committee for Women's Protection.  
2. The task force and/or Regional action committee for Women's Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be established to support the implementation of protection for Women from acts of violence.

#### Article 31

1. The task force and/or Regional action committee as referred to in Article 30 is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating Women's Protection, prevention and handling of trafficking in persons.  
2. Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties, and functions of the task force and/or Regional action committee as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

COORDINATION AND COOPERATION

## Part One

Coordination

#### Article 32

In the context of implementing protection and Empowerment of Women, the Regional Government shall carry out coordination with the central government, provincial government, other district/city governments, related agencies, law enforcement institutions, service institutions, educational institutions, religious institutions, community social institutions, Islamic boarding schools, professional organizations, legal aid organizations, youth organizations, broadcasting supervisory institutions, media, communities, professional institutions, business sector and other related stakeholders.

## Part Two

Cooperation

#### Article 33

1. In the context of implementing protection and empowerment of women, the Regional Government may conduct cooperation.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## DEVELOPMENT OF DATA AND INFORMATION SYSTEM

#### Article 34

1. The Regional Government shall establish an integrated and integrated Data and Information System for protection and Empowerment of Women.  
2. The Data and Information System as referred to in paragraph (1) shall involve the government, provincial government and vertical agencies as well as stakeholders.  
3. Data and information on protection and Empowerment of Women shall be published to the Community and submitted to Regional Apparatus and related agencies for the development of programs and policies for Women's Protection.  
4. The development of the Data and Information System for protection and Empowerment of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations and shall be the responsibility of the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and child protection and the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of communication and informatics.

# CHAPTER XI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 35

1. The Community may participate in the implementation of protection and Empowerment of Women.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be realized by:  
a. actions to provide information and/or report Violence Against Women to law enforcement or authorized parties, or participate in handling acts of Violence Against Women; and/or  
b. provision of communication, information, and education media regarding empowerment and prevention of Violence Against Women in the Family, Community, educational institutions, and public spaces.

# CHAPTER XII

## FUNDING

#### Article 36

Funding, implementation of policies, and activity programs for protection and Empowerment of Women shall be sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget and other legitimate and non-binding sources of funds.

# CHAPTER XIII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 37

1. The Regent has the authority to conduct guidance and supervision over the implementation of the protection and Empowerment of Women.  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 38

Guidance as referred to in Article 37 paragraph (2) includes:  
a. providing socialization to the Community and business sector regarding policies on the protection and Empowerment of Women;  
b. providing books, leaflets, brochures regarding the protection and Empowerment of Women, and other related issues and disseminating them to the Community;  
c. facilitating capacity building of institutions organizing the protection and Empowerment of Women;  
d. facilitating forums for women's participation and women's organizations;  
e. facilitating rehabilitation and reintegration services for women victims of Disasters and victims of violence;  
f. coordinating the participation of the business sector and other institutions, including international assistance for the protection and Empowerment of Women; and  
g. providing awards to the Community and business sector both individually and institutionally who have made efforts in the protection and Empowerment of Women.

#### Article 39

1. Supervision as referred to in Article 37 paragraph (2) shall be in the form of monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of the protection and Empowerment of Women in the Region.  
2. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted with professional, transparent, and accountable principles.  
3. The Community may conduct supervision of the implementation of the protection and Empowerment of Women organized in the Region.  
4. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be conducted through a mechanism of conveying aspirations to the Regional Government and/or DPRD.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF BOYOLALI REGENCY

NUMBER YEAR 2021 CONCERNING PROTECTION AND EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN

##### I. GENERAL

The State through the government and Regional Government according to their authority is responsible for respecting, protecting, and guaranteeing the human rights of every citizen including women and children without discrimination, because women and children as part of Indonesian citizens have the same rights as others, and the rights of women and children are human rights that must be guaranteed, protected not only by the government but also by Regional Governments.  

Although there are guarantees in laws and regulations that protect citizens, especially women, cases involving women continue to increase, not decrease, because cases of violence against women are like the tip of an iceberg, namely cases reported to women's protection institutions are only a small part of the actual cases. Women victims of violence often feel hesitant or afraid to report the violence they experience, or there are other obstacles such as difficulty in accessing services and lack of information about their rights because most women victims come from poor and underprivileged families, so they need assistance, assistance costs, and legal consultation is expensive. On the other hand, institutions that handle women's protection are still lacking, especially in areas far from the city center.  

Various forms of violence, mistreatment, exploitation, and neglect of Women that occur in the Region require strategic efforts from the Regional Government to provide protection for women.  

Furthermore, in order to realize Regional policies to administer governmental affairs that are the authority of the Region in the field of women's protection based on Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government as amended several times most recently by Law Number 11 of 2020 concerning Job Creation, it is necessary to compile it in a Regional Regulation.  

This Regional Regulation is a concrete manifestation of the Boyolali Regency Government in providing protection and guaranteeing the fulfillment of Women's human rights in accordance with its duties and responsibilities.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a  
What is meant by "the principle of respect for Human Rights" is that the protection and empowerment of women is carried out as a manifestation of respect for women's rights as part of Human Rights.  

Letter b  
What is meant by "the principle of justice and Gender Equality" is that in organizing the protection and empowerment of women it is based on justice and equality between men and women.  

Letter c  
What is meant by "the principle of non-discrimination" is that in the protection and empowerment of women it is carried out without regard to differences in regional dimensions (disaster areas, conflict areas, border areas, disadvantaged areas, remote areas, and others), age dimensions (productive age, elderly, and others), and special dimensions (persons with disabilities, workers, and others).  

Letter d
