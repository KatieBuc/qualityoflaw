BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF HULU SUNGAI UTARA,

a. that in the framework of protecting the rights of women and children as mandated by the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, improving the quality of life of women and children and addressing all forms of violence against women and children, there is a need for concern from all parties, both the community and related government institutions and law enforcement officials;
b. that there is a tendency of increasing victims and cases of violence against women and children, which have not been handled optimally so that integrated services are needed by relevant agencies and community institutions/organizations to provide protection for women and children victims of violence;
c. that in an effort to facilitate women and children to improve their skills and independence and obtain consultation services and problem solving experienced by women and children, there is a need for an integrated service center for the empowerment of women and children;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:  
1. Region is the Regency of Hulu Sungai Utara.  
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the regional government administration of Hulu Sungai Utara Regency.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Hulu Sungai Utara.  
4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of Hulu Sungai Utara Regency.  
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb.  
6. Woman is a person of female sex and a person recognized by law as a woman.  
7. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.  
8. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.  
9. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, or psychological to the victim.  
10. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes death.  
11. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.  
12. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  
13. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.  
14. Victim of acts of violence is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.  
15. Service is an action that must be immediately taken to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is, or has occurred.  
16. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.  
17. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to assist victims in conducting counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.  
18. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of acts of violence to their family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.  
19. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.  
20. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions or other social environments that can provide protection.  
21. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of the Regional Government, and/or Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) that provides assistance.  
22. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is a functional work unit located in sub-districts and regencies to provide integrated services for witnesses and/or victims of acts of violence in the context of empowerment of women and children victims of acts of violence.  
23. Family Information and Consultation Center (PIK) is an institution located in villages/sub-districts that provides consultation services and social guidance to individuals, family members, and communities facing disruptions to their social functions.  
24. Implementing officers or functional officers are health workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, legal aid workers who come from civil servants within their respective offices or special personnel employed at P2TP2A and PIK Family.  
25. Special Service Room, hereinafter abbreviated as RPK, is a safe and comfortable room specifically designated for witnesses and/or victims of criminal acts including suspects of criminal acts consisting of women and children who should be treated or require special treatment, and whose cases are being handled at the Police office.  
26. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for relevant offices, vertical agencies, and the community in carrying out activities for the prevention and protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.  
27. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with established operational standards.  
28. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.  
29. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, and mother and her children.  
30. Household is family members and relatives (grandchildren, nephews/nieces, older siblings, younger siblings, grandfathers, grandmothers, cousins, etc.) and non-relatives (servants, drivers, etc.), who live and eat from one kitchen and reside in one house.  
31. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or adoptive mother.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The principles of organizing protection for women and children are:  
a. respect for the rights of women and children;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination;  
d. best interests of women and children.

#### Article 3

1. The objectives of organizing protection for women and children are to:  
a. prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;  
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;  
c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;  
d. provide services to women and children, reporters, and witnesses;  
e. empower women for the fulfillment of the rights of women and children; and  
f. facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize the integrity of harmonious and prosperous households.  
2. The objectives of organizing protection for women and children as referred to in paragraph (1), include aspects of:  
a. prevention;  
b. service and assistance;  
c. reunification;  
d. empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

Every victim has the right to receive:  
a. protection;  
b. information;  
c. optimal service;  
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;  
e. confidential handling;  
f. psychological and legal assistance; and  
g. guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member.

#### Article 5

Children, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 4, also receive special rights, as follows:  
a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth, development and participation;  
b. the right to basic services;  
c. the right to equal protection;  
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and  
e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 6

Obligations and responsibilities in organizing protection for women and children are the shared responsibility of:  
a. regional government;  
b. community;  
c. family; and  
d. parents.

#### Article 7

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 6 letter a, include:  
a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children established by the government;  
b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children;  
c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection for women and children;  
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection for women and children;  
e. allocating budget for the implementation of protection for women and children in accordance with regional financial capacity;  
f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection for women and children.  
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government establishes action programs and activities for the protection of women and children in the Regional Action Plan as a basis for implementing protection for women and children.  
3. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), constitutes part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.  
4. Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 8

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letter b, shall be implemented in the form of community participation.  
2. The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:  
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;  
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and  
c. participating in providing assistance and/or handling of victims of acts of violence.  
3. The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 9

The obligations of the family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER V

## DUTIES AND AUTHORITIES OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 10

1. The Regional Government has the duty to carry out efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children, in the form of:  
a. collecting data and information about women and children victims of acts of violence, and laws and regulations;  
b. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children;  
c. conducting education on anti-violence values toward women and children; and  
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children.  
2. The Regional Government in anticipating the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, has the authority to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:  
a. providing and facilitating the establishment of integrated services for victims of acts of violence by involving institutions and community elements;  
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims; and  
c. conducting community empowerment for the prevention of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER VI

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional Framework

#### Article 11

1. In order to provide services and protection to women and children, the Regional Government establishes P2TP2A as an Integrated Service Center for Women Empowerment and Child Protection located in the Regency and Sub-district, and Family Information and Consultation Center (PIK) located in the Village/Sub-district.  
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with: Health Office, Education Office, Youth Sports Culture and Tourism Office, Social Manpower and Transmigration Office, Cooperatives SMEs Industry and Trade Office, Population and Civil Registration Office, Women Empowerment and Child Protection Agency, Community Empowerment and Village Government Agency, Civil Service Police Unit, Pambalah Batung Amuntai Regional General Hospital, Hulu Sungai Utara Resort Police, Non-Governmental Organizations, and Higher Education Institutions.  
3. P2TP2A in its management shall be established by Regent Regulation.  
4. Regency P2TP2A may receive case referrals from Sub-district P2TP2A and Village/Sub-district Family PIK as well as Community Health Centers.  
5. Further provisions regarding P2TP2A and Family PIK as referred to in paragraph (4), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Forms and Service Mechanisms

#### Article 12

Forms of prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children carried out by P2TP2A and Family PIK, may be implemented through:  
a. socialization activities of laws and regulations to the community related to the protection of the rights of women and children; and  
b. training of P2TP2A and Family PIK members related to the implementation of their duties in preventing acts of violence against women and children.

#### Article 13

1. Regency P2TP2A in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with Sub-district P2TP2A, and competent parties in making efforts to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.  
2. Further provisions regarding the mechanism for implementing prevention duties as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 14

Forms of services for victims organized by P2TP2A include:  
a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;  
b. medicolegal services constitute a form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;  
c. psychosocial services constitute services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in resolving their problems;  
d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process;  
e. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and may provide economic access so that victims can be independent.

#### Article 15

1. In carrying out the task of serving victims of acts of violence against women and children, Regency P2TP2A coordinates with Sub-district P2TP2A.  
2. The service mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be organized according to standard operating procedures which shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 16

1. To prevent acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government shall conduct empowerment and awareness-raising to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.  
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness-raising as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall undertake efforts as follows:  
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education, both formal and non-formal and informal;  
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;  
c. opening employment opportunities for women;  
d. building community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;  
f. imposing curfew restrictions for children (under 18 years of age) leaving the house at night no later than 11.00 p.m., if beyond that time they must be accompanied by parents or family;  
g. restricting children's access to the internet at internet cafes (warnet) during school hours;  
h. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women and children; and  
i. opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

#### Article 17

1. Prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 16, shall be implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:  
a. social affairs;  
b. health;  
c. education;  
d. manpower;  
e. women's empowerment and child protection;  
f. mental and spiritual; and  
g. public order and security.  
2. Prevention of acts of violence by SKPD as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in an integrated and continuous manner based on the Regional Action Plan.

## Part Four

Assistance Mechanism

#### Article 18

1. Assistance shall be implemented by persons and/or institutions that cooperate with P2TP2A and PIK Keluarga.  
2. The assistance mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be organized according to standard operating procedures that are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Five

Principles of Service and Assistance

#### Article 19

The provision of services and assistance to victims shall be conducted with the principles of:  
a. fast, safe, and empathetic;  
b. guarantee of confidentiality;  
c. easily accessible; and  
d. free of charge.

## Part Six

Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 20

1. Forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence, are as follows:  
a. complaint services;  
b. health services;  
c. legal aid;  
d. repatriation;  
e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medicolegal;  
f. identification services; and  
g. psychological services.  
2. Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in accordance with minimum service standards established by the Government and implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:  
a. social affairs;  
b. health;  
c. women's empowerment and child protection; and  
d. mental and spiritual.  
3. In carrying out duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government shall cooperate with Government agencies, Provincial Government, other Regency/City Governments, and the community.  
4. Further provisions regarding procedures for services and handling of women and children victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 21

1. The Regional Government is obliged to provide complaint services and provision of special service rooms at police stations, in order to provide protection to women and children from acts of violence.  
2. The Regional Government and the community or social service institutions may establish recovery houses or safe houses.  
3. Further provisions regarding complaint services and social services as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Seven

Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 22

1. The Regional Government is obliged to conduct empowerment of women victims of acts of violence.  
2. Empowerment of victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented by P2TP2A.  
3. In conducting empowerment of women victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall prepare empowerment programs in the economic field, which include:  
a. procuring the needs required for entrepreneurship training for women, in order to increase business knowledge, attitude, and skills;  
b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;  
c. providing assistance in developing productive economic enterprises;  
d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large enterprises, as well as financial institutions to develop women's businesses;  
e. endeavoring to provide capital for women victims of acts of violence;  
f. expanding access to information and promoting women's products.  
4. In conducting empowerment of women victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may establish cooperation with job training institutions organized by the private sector.

#### Article 23

1. Empowerment of women victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 22, shall be conducted by providing training for capacity building, skills, and independence.  
2. Training as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be conducted through:  
a. on the job training;  
b. pre employment training; and  
c. understudy training.

# CHAPTER VII

## COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 24

1. In order to achieve the objectives of providing protection to women and children from acts of violence as referred to in Article 3, the Regional Government shall cooperate with:  
a. the government;  
b. the provincial government;  
c. other regency/city governments;  
d. universities; and  
e. non-governmental institutions.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1), shall include:  
a. exchange of data and information;  
b. rehabilitation of victims of acts of violence;  
c. repatriation and social reintegration; and  
d. provision of evidence and witnesses, and followed up in accordance with applicable law.  
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be set forth in the form of a joint agreement.

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 25

1. The Regional Government shall establish partnerships with the business sector in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.  
2. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be conducted through:  
a. notification of information on employment opportunities for women victims of acts of violence;  
b. education and training for women victims of acts of violence;  
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of acts of violence whose education has been interrupted; and  
d. fostering and increasing the economic independence of women victims of acts of violence.  
3. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be set forth in the form of an agreement.

# CHAPTER VIII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 26

1. The Regional Government is obliged to conduct guidance and supervision of the provision of protection to women and children.  
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), shall include:  
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;  
b. technical guidance and training;  
c. provision of facilities;  
d. monitoring; and  
e. evaluation.  
3. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be conducted with the principles of:  
a. professional;  
b. transparent; and  
c. accountable.

#### Article 27

The guidance and supervision as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), in order to realize the objectives of organizing protection for women and children in accordance with minimum service standards implemented by SKPD and the community.

#### Article 28

The community may conduct supervision of the organization of protection for women and children organized by the Regional Government, with a mechanism for conveying aspirations to the Regent or DPRD.

# CHAPTER IX

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 29

1. The community may participate in efforts to organize protection for women and children under the Regional Regulation.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), may be carried out by individuals or organizations.
3. Forms of community participation carried out by individuals or organizations as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2).

# CHAPTER X

## REPORTING

#### Article 30

1. P2TP2A Regency is obliged to report the implementation of the organization of protection for women and children to the Regent.
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be submitted in writing, covering:
a. administration;
b. finance;
c. services.
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be carried out at least once every 6 (six) months.

# CHAPTER XI

## SOURCES OF FUNDS

#### Article 31

Funds for organizing protection for women and children, are sourced from:
a. state revenue and expenditure budget;
b. provincial regional revenue and expenditure budget;
c. regency regional revenue and expenditure budget;
d. village revenue and expenditure budget; and
e. other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 32

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or non-governmental organizations that implement protection for women and children.
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be in accordance with the regional capacity in the financial sector and implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XII

## INVESTIGATION

#### Article 33

1. In addition to general investigation officials, investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children in this Regional Regulation, Regional Regulation of HSU Regency Year 2016 Number 3 concerning Protection of Women and Children may also be carried out by Civil Servant Investigators (PPNS) as referred to in the Criminal Procedure Law.
2. PPNS as referred to in paragraph (1), are certain civil servant officials within the Regional Government appointed by authorized officials in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
3. In carrying out investigation duties, PPNS as referred to in paragraph (2), is authorized to:
a. receive reports or complaints from a person regarding the existence of criminal acts of violence against women and children;
b. take first action at that time at the scene and conduct an examination;
c. interrogate a suspect and examine the suspect's identity;
d. seize objects and/or documents;
e. take fingerprints and photograph a person;
f. summon persons to be heard and examined as suspects or witnesses;
g. bring in expert witnesses needed in connection with the examination of the case;
h. terminate the investigation after receiving instructions from the General Investigator that there is insufficient evidence, or the incident does not constitute a criminal act and subsequently through the General Investigator notify this matter to the Public Prosecutor, the suspect or their family; and/or
i. take other actions necessary for the smooth investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children, which according to law can be accounted for.
4. PPNS as referred to in paragraph (2), shall notify the commencement of the investigation and submit the results of the investigation to the Public Prosecutor through the Investigation Official of the Police of the Republic of Indonesia, in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Criminal Procedure Law.

# CHAPTER XIII

## SANCTION PROVISIONS

#### Article 34

1. Any person who intentionally does not provide protection for women and children resulting in the occurrence of violence, allows violence to occur and/or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as stipulated in the applicable laws and regulations.
2. If Officials appointed to organize protection do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities, they shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.
3. Managers of P2TP2A Regency/District and PIK Family Village/Sub-district who carry out service duties that violate service principles, may be subject to sanctions in accordance with the internal mechanisms of P2TP2A and PIK Family.

# CHAPTER XIV

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 35

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF HULU SUNGAI UTARA REGENCY

NUMBER 3 YEAR 2016 CONCERNING ORGANIZATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

### I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide a sense of security to its citizens from threats and actions that can disturb and damage psychological, physical, sexual and economic security. This is philosophically stated in the preamble of the 1945 Constitution, that the purpose of establishing the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia is to protect the entire Indonesian nation and the Indonesian homeland.

Since the Government of Indonesia has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women), it is obliged to fulfill these provisions. Human Rights state that the enforcement and fulfillment of Human Rights is the responsibility of the State, especially the Government.

Through Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014, the Government recognizes the existence of children's rights. The inherent human rights of children include basic rights as human beings, namely the Right to Life, the Right to Growth and Development, the Right to Protection and the Right to Participation.

To fulfill the Right to life, children need food in sufficient quantities, healthy and nutritious, as well as optimal access to health. Protection from violence that threatens their safety and health must be provided from an early age. Protection is needed to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. To fulfill the right to growth and development, children need space to play, exercise, and education that is appropriate to their physical and psychological development. In fulfilling children's rights, every government administrator, community and parents are obliged to understand and care about children's rights. The existence of legal instruments and law enforcement officials who defend the interests of children is necessary for this protection effort. Legislation that is principled on defending the best interest of the child is needed to realize legal protection.

Furthermore, it is not uncommon to find cases of women and children who experience problems of physical, psychological, sexual and social violence. For example, women and children who are victims of persecution, neglect, eviction and abuse by parents or others. For women and children who experience such problems, the government must provide a Safe House (children protection home) or Integrated Service Center (PPT) for women and children victims of violence. A Safe House or PPT is a safe and friendly place for women and children. The PPT is equipped with companions from educators, doctors, psychologists, religious leaders and networked with lawyers for women and children as well as the RPK Unit at the Police.

The fate of women and children victims of violence must be given attention by the government. The large number of cases of violence against women and children are often caused by factors that develop in society, such as low economic levels, education, and environments in the industrial sector. Therefore, victims of violence like this need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights.

For the empowerment of victims of violence, the Regional Government can establish an Integrated Service Center as a service center for the empowerment of women victims of violence which specifically has the main duties and functions for the empowerment of women and children victims of violence.

From the above framework, the Regional Government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, economically and socially to prevent, suppress, reduce, and eliminate all forms of violence against women and children. From these various efforts, a legal framework will be realized in the implementation of protection for women and children.

### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently Clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a  
What is meant by "respect for the rights of victims" is a series of actions to respect, appreciate and ensure the fulfillment of the rights of victims.

Letter b  
What is meant by "justice and gender equality" is a process to be fair to men and women and equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, defense and National security activities and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

Letter c  
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims by not making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d  
What is meant by "The best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, must be the primary consideration.

#### Article 3

Paragraph (1)  
Sufficiently clear.

Paragraph (2)  
Letter a  
What is meant by the "prevention" aspect is a protection strategy effort through:  
a. primary prevention, all people, families, communities and the state in efforts to increase the ability of knowledge, understanding and ensure that violence against women and children does not occur, including policy socialization, adequate services, supportive workplace policies, and life skill training for women and children. What is meant by life skill training includes conflict resolution without violence, stress management skills, resource management, making effective decisions, effective interpersonal communication, guidance on the psychosocial development of women and children.  
b. secondary prevention, aimed at community groups with high risk in efforts to increase skills, including training and victim services to ensure that violence against women and children does not occur in the next generation. Activities carried out here include conducting home visits for parents who have just had children to conduct self-assessment of whether they are at risk of committing violence against children in the future.  
c. tertiary prevention, intended to increase parenting abilities that ensure that violence against women and children is not repeated, what is done here is integrated services for women and children who are victims of violence, through counseling, stress management training.

Letter b  
What is meant by the "service and assistance" aspect is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals and assistants in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

Letter c  
What is meant by the "reunification" aspect is an effort to restore and recover the physical and psychological condition of victims which then reunites them with the victim's family and community.

Letter d  
What is meant by the "empowerment" aspect is a process of increasing the role of the community to participate in the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence which aims to obtain access and participation in the process of protection for women and children broadly.

#### Article 4

Point a  
What is meant by "obtaining protection" is obtaining protection from individuals, groups and institutions both governmental and non-governmental.  

Point b  
What is meant by "obtaining information" is access and information about the existence of complaint centers, P2TP2A, and all matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and involvement in the decision-making process related to assistance and case development.  

Point c  
What is meant by "optimal services" is services that include medical, extensive medicolegal, psychosocial and legal.  

Point d  
What is meant by "continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage" is handling that does not stop at physical and psychological healing, but until the victim can resume life in society including the restoration of their good name.  

Point e  
What is meant by "confidential handling" is an effort to guarantee certainty for the victim that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disseminated.  

Point f  
What is meant by "obtaining psychological assistance" is assistance provided by psychologists to victims suffering from trauma/other mental problems to restore the victim's mental condition. Meanwhile, legal assistance is assistance provided by persons and/or legal aid institutions to victims at every level of examination and throughout the legal process.  

Point g  
What is meant by "guarantee of rights related to the victim's status" is an effort to provide certainty and protection for the victim as a family member and member of society.

#### Article 19

Point a  
What is meant by "fast" is immediate action taken without complications or simplified procedures. What is meant by "safe" is a guarantee of service protection that feels comfortable, undisturbed, and served in a friendly, respectful and appreciative manner.  

What is meant by "empathy" is actions of appreciating, respecting, caring, being friendly, and making happy that aim to please and calm the victim's heart.  

Point b  
What is meant by "guarantee of confidentiality" is an effort to guarantee certainty for the victim that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disseminated.  

Point c  
What is meant by "easily accessible" is the provision of services and assistance for all people regardless of their social status, so that such services are affordable for the underprivileged or relatively sufficient for the privileged.  

Point d  
What is meant by "not charged" is that service and assistance activities carried out by P2TP2A are not charged to the victim.
