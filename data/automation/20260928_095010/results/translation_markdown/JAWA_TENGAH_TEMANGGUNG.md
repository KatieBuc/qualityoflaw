# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

1. Region is Temanggung Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of Regional Government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Temanggung.
4. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of Temanggung.
5. Regional Apparatus is the supporting element of the Regional Head and Regional House of Representatives in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling rights carried out by families, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
7. Protection of Women is all activities to guarantee and protect women and their rights, so that they can live with justice, in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
8. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or distress to women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.
10. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
11. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes or certain purposes.
12. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that results in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
13. Victim is a woman including Female Workers working abroad and children who experience suffering and/or distress either directly or indirectly as a result of such violence.
14. Women's Empowerment is any effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social, knowledge and skills capabilities so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.
15. Gender is the difference between men and women in roles, functions, rights, responsibilities, and behavior shaped by social, cultural and customary values of community groups that can change according to time and local conditions. Responsibilities and behavior shaped by social, cultural and customary values of community groups that can change according to time and local conditions.
16. Gender equality and justice is equal opportunity between men and women to participate in the development process, equal access to all forms of services, and have balanced social and economic status.
17. Gender Mainstreaming hereinafter abbreviated as PUG is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, budgeting, monitoring and evaluation of policies, programs is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, budgeting, monitoring and evaluation of policies, programs and development activities in the region
18. Gender Mainstreaming Working Group hereinafter abbreviated as Pokja PUG is a consultation forum for implementers and movers of Gender Mainstreaming from various agencies/institutions in the region.
19. Integrated service is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of violence including criminal acts of trafficking in persons carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as a unified implementation of health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
20. Protection of Women is any act aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
21. legal act is any act or action of a legal subject that has legal consequences and such legal consequences are indeed desired by the legal subject.
22. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.
23. Companion is a person who has the expertise to conduct counseling, therapy, and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
24. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a Complaint Center that provides integrated services for women and children victims of violence
25. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

#### Article 2

The principles in Women's Empowerment and Protection are:
a. respect for Human Rights;
b. gender equality;
c. gender justice; and
d. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

Women's Empowerment and Protection aims to:
a. guarantee the fulfillment of women's rights so that they can live and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth;
b. provide Gender Justice and Equality, improve the quality of life of women through increasing the institutional capacity of Women's Empowerment and Protection in the region, including the development of gender and child data systems; and
c. provide protection of women's rights from various forms of violence and other discriminatory treatment.

#### Article 4

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:
a. rights and obligations of women;
b. obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government;
c. protection of women;
d. empowerment of women;
e. protection of women victims of violence;
f. community participation and involvement;
g. monitoring, evaluation and reporting; and
h. funding.

#### Article 5

In order to realize the objectives as referred to in Article 3 letter a, efforts are made through:
a. providing access for women to education, health, and other strategic services;
b. encouraging women's involvement in the development process;
c. providing knowledge, skills, character values, morals and family resilience; and
d. encouraging programs that can increase women's independence in the economic, political, legal, social, cultural and other strategic fields.

#### Article 6

In order to realize the objectives as referred to in Article 3 letter b, efforts are made through:  
a. establishment, development, and capacity strengthening of institutions for the protection of women and children including complaint service units for violence against women and children, as well as legal aid services;  
b. improvement of the quality of human resources management;  
c. capacity strengthening of PUG and child institutions; and  
d. strengthening and development of gender and child data systems.

#### Article 7

In order to realize the objectives as referred to in Article 3 letter c, the Regional Government makes promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative efforts in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 8

Promotive efforts as referred to in Article 7 are as follows:  
a. strengthening coordination mechanisms and service unit networks in efforts to handle cases of violence;  
b. providing Communication, Information and Education materials related to the prevention and handling of violence; and  
c. conducting socialization, advocacy and social campaigns in the context of preventing and handling violence.

#### Article 9

Preventive efforts as referred to in Article 7 are as follows:  
a. conducting legal awareness counseling for the community, especially women and children;  
b. conducting massive and sustainable movements involving the community in actions to prevent and handle violence;  
c. instilling character values, morals, and family resilience; and  
d. involving the role and participation of the community in women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 10

Curative efforts as referred to in Article 7 are as follows:  
a. optimizing technical service units related to complaints of violence against women and children;  
b. providing adequate facilities and infrastructure for handling health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal aid; and  
c. handling victims of crime and violence quickly, precisely and accurately by law enforcement officials.

#### Article 11

Rehabilitation efforts as referred to in Article 7 are as follows:  
a. providing companion personnel for victims of crime and violence which include among others psychologists, psychiatrists, clergy/spiritual companions, lawyers, and medical personnel;  
b. strengthening networks and coordination in the reintegration process and repatriation of victims to their families and/or social environment.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 12

1. Every woman has the right to:  
a. live and maintain life and improve her standard of living;  
b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue offspring;  
c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life to become a faithful, pious, responsible, noble, happy, and prosperous human being in accordance with human rights;  
d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;  
e. be free from slavery or enslavement and threats;  
f. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;  
g. obtain welfare and a decent life;  
h. participate in politics;  
i. perform legal acts;  
j. freely choose a partner in her marriage;  
k. choose the work she likes and is also entitled to fair employment conditions; and  
l. receive fair wages in accordance with her work that is comparable and commensurate with her human dignity.  
2. The right to participate in politics as referred to in paragraph (1) letter h is, women have the right to:  
a. vote and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret voting; and  
b. be appointed to every government position.

## Part Two

Rights of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 13

Every woman victim of violence has:  
a. the right to be respected for her dignity and worth as a human being;  
b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;  
c. the right to determine her own decisions;  
d. the right to obtain information;  
e. the right to confidentiality;  
f. the right to compensation;  
g. the right to social rehabilitation;  
h. the right to complaint handling;  
i. the right of the victim and her family to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and  
j. the right to assistance.

## Part Three

Obligations of Women

#### Article 14

Every woman is obliged to:  
a. uphold her dignity and worth as a woman;  
b. carry out her duties and responsibilities as a wife, mother, child and member of society well and responsibly; and  
c. comply with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 15

1. The Regional Government is obliged and responsible for the implementation of women's empowerment and protection.  
2. Women's empowerment and protection efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;  
b. determination of implementation guidelines;  
c. provision of services; and  
d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.  
3. Women's empowerment and protection efforts as referred to in paragraph (2) are carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 16

1. In implementing policies, programs, and activities for women's protection and women's empowerment, the Regional Government may cooperate with the community/community institutions.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 17

The Regent in implementing policies, programs, and activities for women's protection by making efforts to:  
a. coordinate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities;  
b. protect women among Regional Apparatus;  
c. cooperate with Regencies/Cities within one Province, and cooperation between Regencies/Cities in other Provinces in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for women's protection in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
d. strengthen the institutional capacity of PUG for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for women's protection;  
e. facilitate women's protection services;  
f. provide women's protection services;  
g. implement affirmative action for women's protection; and  
h. prepare a women's protection data system.

#### Article 18

1. Strengthening the institutional capacity of PUG as referred to in Article 17 letter d and in efforts to accelerate the institutionalization of PUG in all Regional Apparatus is implemented by forming a PUG Working Group.  
2. The coordinator of PUG implementation is the Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment affairs.  
3. In its implementation, the PUG Working Group forms a Technical Team and Focal Point. (4) The implementation of PUG institutional strengthening and the implementation of policies, programs and activities for women's protection is further stipulated in a Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

## WOMEN'S PROTECTION

## Part One

Health Protection

#### Article 19

1. Women have the right to obtain special protection from things that threaten their safety and/or health.  
2. Special protection is given to women because their reproductive function is guaranteed and protected by law.

## Part Two

Employment Protection

#### Article 20

1. Women have equal opportunities without Discrimination to obtain employment.  
2. Equal opportunities as referred to in paragraph (1) are realized through the fulfillment of women's rights to receive equal treatment from Employers.

#### Article 21

Employers who employ female workers are obligated to provide protection in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN

#### Article 22

In order to improve the position, role, and quality of women, as well as efforts to realize equality and Gender Justice in family, community, national, and state life, women must be empowered.

#### Article 23

Empowerment of Women is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings so as to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:  
a. economy;  
b. social culture;  
c. politics; and  
d. law.

#### Article 24

Implementation of Empowerment of Women in the economic field as referred to in Article 23 letter a, is carried out through:  
a. provision of skills and job training;  
b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;  
c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;  
d. facilitation and capital assistance; and  
e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 25

Empowerment of women in the social cultural field as referred to in Article 23 letter b, is carried out through:  
a. improvement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;  
b. improvement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotional, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health;  
c. improvement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and  
d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 26

1. Implementation of Empowerment of Women in the political field as referred to in Article 23 letter c, includes:  
a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;  
b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;  
c. participation in general elections; and  
d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.  
2. Implementation of Empowerment of Women in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 27

1. Implementation of Empowerment of Women in the legal field as referred to in Article 22 letter d, includes:  
a. improvement of awareness and knowledge in the legal field through communication, information and education services; and  
b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.  
2. Implementation of Empowerment of Women in the legal field as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 28

1. Every Victim has the right to obtain Protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage and confidential handling from individuals, groups or institutions, both Regional Government and the community.  
2. In the event of violence, every Victim has the right to obtain health services and assistance, both psychologically and legally and to obtain guarantees of their rights related to their status as women.

## Part Two

Obligations and Efforts of Regional Government

#### Article 29

1. The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to carry out efforts to prevent the occurrence of Violence against Women in the form of:  
a. collecting data and information about Victims of violence and Laws and Regulations;  
b. conducting education on anti-Violence against Women values;  
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of Protection for Victims of violence;  
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of Protection of Victims of violence; and  
e. preparing reports on the implementation of Protection of women Victims of violence.  
2. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women, the Regional Government is obligated to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:  
a. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for Victims by involving community elements; and  
b. encouraging public awareness of the importance of Protection for Victims.  
3. The Regional Government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.  
4. To prevent the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women, the Regional Government makes efforts as follows:  
a. conducting counseling and socialization regarding matters relating to Violence against Women;  
b. conducting socialization of women's rights;  
c. seeking to improve education for women;  
d. opening skills courses for women to support the family's economic needs;  
e. creating employment opportunities for women;  
f. providing counseling to immediately report to local authorities if Violence against Women occurs;  
g. empowering Social Community Organizations and women's organizations to increase women's knowledge and insight;  
h. establishing Women's Protection Complaint Posts; and  
i. forming networks in violence prevention efforts.

## Part Three

Implementation of Protection

#### Article 30

1. The Regional Government facilitates the formation of Women's Protection institutions.  
2. Women's Protection institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by forming P2TP2A.  
3. Further provisions regarding P2TP2A are regulated by Regent Regulation. Article 31 The implementation of the duties and functions of P2TP2A in the Region is coordinated by the Regional Apparatus that handles women's empowerment affairs.

#### Article 32

Implementation of services to Victims is carried out free of charge, quickly, safely, empathetically, non-Discriminatory, easily accessible, and with a guarantee of confidentiality.

#### Article 33

1. P2TP2A may consult and coordinate with the Ministry that administers women's empowerment and child protection affairs by first coordinating with Regional Apparatus or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection at the Provincial level.  
2. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out in the form of:  
a. coordination meetings with work networks;  
b. consultation;  
c. submission of data and information; and/ord. follow-up on case handling.

## Part Four

Forms of Violence and Protection

#### Article 34

1. Forms of Violence against Women include:  
a. physical violence;  
b. sexual violence;  
c. economic violence; and  
d. psychological violence.  
2. The form of physical violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, unconsciousness and or causes death.  
3. The form of sexual violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, is sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  
4. The form of economic violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, is the neglect of women in the form of not providing proper livelihood, care or maintenance.  
5. The form of psychological violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 35

1. Every Victim of violence must receive Protection in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.  
2. Protection of victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is provided to Regional Residents who experience violence in the Region or outside the Region.  
3. Protection of victims of violence in addition to being provided to Regional residents as referred to in paragraph (2) is also provided to non-Regional residents that occurs in the Region.

#### Article 36

1. Forms of Protection against Violence against Women include:  
a. Medical protection;  
b. Legal protection;  
c. Medico-legal protection (forensic medicine);  
d. Economic protection; and  
e. Psychological protection.  
2. The form of medical protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, is treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of Victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.  
3. The legal form as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, is services and assistance to help Victims in undergoing legal and judicial processes.  
4. The form of medico-legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, is medical services for the purposes of evidence in the field of law.  
5. The form of economic protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, is services for skills and to provide economic access so that victims of violence can be independent.  
6. The form of psychological protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e is psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving, assistance in order to restore the traumatic condition of Victims including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide.

#### Article 37

To provide Protection as referred to in Article 35, the Regional Government may undertake efforts to:  
a. provide services and handling as quickly as possible to witnesses and/or Victims;  
b. provide convenience, comfort, safety, and free of charge for witnesses and/or Victims;  
c. maintain the confidentiality of witnesses and/or Victims;  
d. guarantee justice and legal certainty for witnesses and/or Victims;  
e. provision of special service rooms at Police Offices;  
f. provision of apparatus, health workers, social workers, and spiritual counselors;  
g. creation and development of systems and mechanisms for cooperation of service programs involving parties that are easily accessible to Victims;  
h. provide Protection for companions, witnesses, families, and friends of Victims;  
i. provide Protection for female workers/laborers in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF TEMANGGUNG REGENCY

NUMBER 14 OF 2020 CONCERNING WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION

##### I. GENERAL

The Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia mandates Regional Governments to regulate and manage their own regions for the realization of community welfare through improved services, empowerment and community participation. One of the authorities of Regional Governments is to make regional policies with the aim of improving the welfare of the people in their regions.  

In addition, the constitution of the Republic of Indonesia has also mandated in Article 28D Paragraph (1) of the 1945 Constitution that everyone has the right to recognition, guarantees, protection, and fair legal certainty as well as equal treatment before the law. Article 28B Paragraph (2) of the 1945 Constitution also states that every child has the right to survival, growth and development and has the right to protection from violence and discrimination. This shows that women should be protected by the state, especially against violence because their rights are already regulated in the constitution.  

Protection carried out by the Government for women is concretely implemented, namely by the issuance of Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women, hereinafter abbreviated as CEDAW.  

The government's efforts to protect its citizens, especially women, are also clearly implied in the provisions of Article 3 paragraph (2) of Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights which states that: "everyone has the right to recognition, guarantees, protection and fair legal treatment as well as legal certainty and equal treatment before the law".  

Violence and trafficking against women are already regulated in Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, and Law Number 21 of 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Crime of Trafficking in Persons.  

Therefore, efforts need to be made to prevent and eradicate all forms of discrimination and violence against women, especially for the Temanggung Regency area. The Temanggung Regency Government is one of the parties most authorized to empower women in order to provide guarantees for justice and gender equality, through efforts to build commitment to integrate gender-responsive budgets in programs and activities.  

This Regional Regulation is expected to be able to encourage improvement efforts regarding access, control, participation and benefits for justice and gender equality as well as the physical and mental conditions of women victims of violence.
