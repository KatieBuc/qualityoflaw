# PROTECTION OF WOMEN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

REGENT OF KUBU RAYA

PROVINCE OF WEST KALIMANTAN

# REGIONAL REGULATION OF KUBU RAYA REGENCY

NUMBER 9 OF 2016

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF KUBU RAYA,

Considering:


a. that violence against women constitutes a violation of human rights so that women need to be protected in their dignity and honor and guaranteed their right to life in accordance with their nature and destiny;
b. that to overcome violence against women in Kubu Raya Regency, prevention efforts and optimization of overcoming violence against women are required through policies to guarantee legal protection for women;
c. that to provide legal protection for women, the regulation of protection of women from acts of violence needs to be regulated in a Regional Regulation;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on Protection of Women From Acts of Violence;

In view of:

1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 35 of 2007 concerning the Establishment of Kubu Raya Regency in West Kalimantan Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2007 Number 101, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4751);
3. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);

With Mutual Approval of

THE REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF KUBU RAYA REGENCY

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Kubu Raya Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government Administration who leads the Implementation of Government Affairs which are the Authority of the Autonomous Region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Kubu Raya.
4. Regional Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is an organization or institution in the regional government that is responsible to the Regent.
5. Women are human beings of female sex starting from infants, adolescents, adults up to the elderly.
6. Women with Disabilities are women who have self-limitations, which can be physical, cognitive, mental, sensory, emotional, developmental or some combination of these.
7. Elderly Women are women who are 60 years of age and above.
8. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.
9. Violence against women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering to women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including certain threats, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.
10. Victim is a woman who experiences violence and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence and life vulnerability.
11. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the regional government, community, and/or other parties who know or hear that violence against victims will or has occurred.
12. Service/handling is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and/or knowing that violence against women will, is or has occurred.
13. Assistance is a person or marriage from an institution that has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and/or advocacy.
14. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is an institution or functional work unit that organizes integrated services for victims of violence.
15. Safe House is a residence for victims during the assistance process for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers.
16. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing 5 (five) types of services for women and children victims of violence, namely (1) Complaint Handling, (2) Health Services, (3) Social Rehabilitation, (4) Law Enforcement and Legal Aid, and (5) Repatriation and Social Reintegration.
17. Complaint Handling is a series of actions carried out by integrated service providers to follow up on reports of acts of violence against women submitted by victims, families and the community.
18. Health Services are efforts that include promotive, curative and rehabilitative aspects.
19. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction to be able to carry out social functions properly.
20. Law Enforcement is action by officials authorized by the State to implement laws and regulations.
21. Legal Aid is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocacy to conduct the process of assisting witnesses and/or victims of violence against women that is gender sensitive.
22. Repatriation is an effort to return women victims of violence from abroad to the debarkation point/entry point, or from the receiving area to the area of origin.
23. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family or community that can provide protection and fulfillment for victims.
24. Integrated Service Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is a unified unit that organizes integrated service functions for women victims of violence.
25. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in one straight line upward or downward.
26. Household is husband, wife and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care and guardianship and/or domestic workers who reside in that household.

#### Article 2

Protection of Women from acts of violence is based on:
a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of victims;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination; and
d. the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

Protection of women from acts of violence aims to:
a. prevent violence against women;
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women;
c. protect and provide a sense of security for women;
d. facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household;
e. provide services and/or handling to victims; and
f. empower women victims of violence and vulnerable groups.

# CHAPTER II

## SCOPE OF PROTECTION

#### Article 4

The scope of protection of women from acts of violence includes:
a. prevention;
b. service and/or handling; and
c. empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

## PREVENTION, SERVICE AND/OR HANDLING AND EMPOWERMENT

## Part One

Prevention

#### Article 5

1. Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 4 letter a shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the SKPD responsible for Women's Empowerment.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by:
a. establishing a working network;
b. conducting coordination, integration, and synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
c. establishing a violence prevention system;
d. disseminating laws and regulations related to the protection of women victims of violence, including raising awareness among families and communities;
e. providing education for women victims of violence including access to employment opportunities; and
f. through complete and easily accessible communication, information and education media.
3. In addition to prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), prevention efforts shall also be carried out by family and/or closest relatives, community, educational institutions and customary institutions.

## Part Two

Services and/or Handling

#### Article 6

1. Services and/or handling as referred to in Article 4 letter b shall be carried out in an integrated manner by PPT.
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from and/or to other service units in a networked manner.
3. Further provisions regarding the development, mechanism, and working relationship of integrated PPT networks as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 7

1. Forms of services and/or handling for victims include:
a. complaint handling including identification;
b. health/medical rehabilitation services;
c. social rehabilitation services;
d. law enforcement and legal aid services;
e. repatriation and social reintegration services; and
f. psychological services.
2. Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with Minimum Service Standards stipulated by laws and regulations implemented by regional apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.

#### Article 8

Services and/or handling for victims shall be implemented with:
a. speed and accuracy;
b. safety and comfort;
c. sense of empathy;
d. non-discrimination;
e. easy accessibility;
f. no charges; and
g. guaranteed confidentiality.

## Part Three

Empowerment

#### Article 9

Empowerment as referred to in Article 4 letter c includes:
a. establishing communities of women victims of violence;
b. job training;
c. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
d. capital assistance.

#### Article 10

1. Job training as referred to in Article 9 letter b includes:
a. skills training;
b. field work practice; and
c. apprenticeship.
2. Implementation of job training as referred to in paragraph (1) may be facilitated by the SKPD handling Women's Empowerment and technical agencies in accordance with their main duties and functions.

#### Article 11

Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups as referred to in Article 9 letter c include:
a. entrepreneurial skills training; and
b. business implementation assistance.

•  •

#### Article 12

1. Capital assistance as referred to in Article 9 letter d includes:
a. facilities and infrastructure assistance; and
b. working/business capital assistance facilities.
2. Capital assistance as referred to in paragraph (1) may be obtained through the budget of SKPD or assistance from other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER III

## VIOLENCE

#### Article 13

Violence against women consists of:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. exploitation; and/or
f. other violence.

#### Article 14

Physical violence as referred to in Article 13 letter a includes acts that result in pain, wounds, injury or disability to the body, miscarriage, fainting, and/or death.

#### Article 15

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 13 letter b includes acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, sense of helplessness, and/or severe psychological suffering.

#### Article 16

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 13 letter c includes:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
c. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

#### Article 17

Neglect as referred to in Article 13 letter d includes:
a. acts that neglect women within the scope of their household; and/or
b. acts that result in economic dependence.

#### Article 18

Exploitation as referred to in Article 13 letter e includes:
a. acts that exploit women economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others.
b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits.
c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

#### Article 19

Other violence as referred to in Article 13 letter f includes:
a. any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom.
b. a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

##### CHAPTER IV

##### RIGHTS OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 20

Every woman victim of violence has the right to:
a. have her dignity respected as a human being;
b. obtain physical and psychological health recovery from the suffering experienced;
c. determine her own decisions;
d. obtain information;
e. have her identity kept confidential;
f. obtain compensation;
g. obtain social rehabilitation;
h. obtain spiritual guidance;
i. obtain complaint handling;
j. obtain facilitation in the judicial process;
k. obtain assistance; and
l. guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member.

# CHAPTER VI

## GOVERNMENT RESPONSIBILITY AND INSTITUTIONS

## Part One

Regional Government

#### Article 21

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement victim protection efforts by:
a. establishing PPT;
b. formulating policies;
c. preparing plans and implementing programs and activities;
d. providing facilities and infrastructure support; and
e. supervising the implementation of services for victims with minimum service standards.
2. In implementing the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall prepare a Regional Action Plan for Women's Protection.
3. Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 22

The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (1) shall be coordinated by the SKPD responsible for women's empowerment issues.

## Part Two

Community and Family

#### Article 23

The community and family have the obligation and responsibility to:  
a. prevent the occurrence of violence against women;  
b. report if violence occurs;  
c. protect victims; and  
d. provide emergency assistance.

## Part Three

Coordination of Women's Protection

#### Article 24

1. Services and/or handling for victims are carried out in a networked manner.  
2. The network as referred to in paragraph (1) aims to:  
a. coordinate and synchronize the handling of PPT services;  
b. develop networks and referral systems; and  
c. collect, compile and present reports on violence.  
3. The network as referred to in paragraph (1) carries out its duties and functions in accordance with the form of service as referred to in Article 7.

# CHAPTER VI

## INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER

#### Article 25

1. In providing services and temporary protection in the form of a safe house (shelter) for victims, a PPT needs to be established.  
2. PPT may be established by vertical agencies in the Region, Regional Government, and/or community institutions.  
3. PPT established by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of elements of the Regional Police, SKPD in charge of Health, Education, Social Affairs, Community Empowerment, Women's Empowerment and Child Protection as well as Community Social Institutions that have the function of providing protection to women from acts of violence carried out in a networked manner.  
4. In the event that the PPT does not have a safe house (shelter) as referred to in paragraph (1), then the victim is referred to a PPT that has a safe house.  
5. PPT as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of several counseling fields including:  
a. legal field;  
b. health field;  
c. spiritual field; and  
d. psychology field.  
6. PPT as referred to in paragraph (2) is stipulated by Regent's Decree.

# CHAPTER VII

## INFORMATION AND REPORTING SYSTEM

#### Article 26

1. SKPD in charge of Women's Empowerment implements the information system and submits reports on the implementation of women's protection to the Regent.  
2. Further provisions regarding the information and reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent's Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

## FUNDING

#### Article 27

Funding for women's protection activities carried out by the Regional Government is sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget and/or other lawful sources in accordance with statutory regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 28

1. The community participates in the implementation of women's protection.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:  
a. forming family partners at the sub-district/village level by the community;  
b. forming women's protection units in community organizations;  
c. conducting socialization of women's rights independently;  
d. making prevention efforts if finding indications of violence against women in the community;  
e. providing first aid to victims; and  
f. reporting to the competent authority if violence against women occurs in their environment.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, community social institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and/or mass media.

##### CHAPTER X

##### GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 29

1. The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of women's protection.  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. coordination;  
b. guidance;  
c. education and training; and  
d. monitoring and evaluation.  
3. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a covers planning and implementation.  
4. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b covers planning, implementation, procedures, funding, quality, control, and supervision.  
5. Education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is carried out in accordance with the provisions of statutory regulations.  
6. Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d is carried out based on statutory regulations periodically every 6 (six) months.

# CHAPTER XI

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 30

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Kubu Raya Regency.  

Enacted in Sungai Raya on 2 December 2016

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF KUBU RAYA REGENCY

NUMBER 9 OF 2016 CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL.  

Acts of violence against women constitute a violation of human rights, therefore, their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.  

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women in Kubu Raya Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to establish a regional regulation on the protection of women.  

Women's protection is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention.  

Existing statutory regulations or those related to the protection of women victims of violence have not yet regulated efforts to protect women in the regions, so institutional and regulatory support at the regional level is needed to ensure its implementation.  

This Regional Regulation regulates efforts to protect women, particularly in the form of prevention, service/handling, and empowerment of women in Kubu Raya Regency.

#### Article 2

Letter a  
What is meant by "respect and fulfillment of victims' rights" is the guarantee of the fulfillment of victims' basic rights.  

Letter b  
What is meant by "justice" is a state where every person, both men and women, are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to gain access and welfare.  

What is meant by "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.  

Letter c  
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims by not making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.  

Letter d  
What is meant by "the best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, regional government, community, legislative body, and judicial body, must prioritize the best interests of the victim.
