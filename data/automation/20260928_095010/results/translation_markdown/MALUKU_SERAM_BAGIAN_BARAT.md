# REGIONAL REGULATION OF WEST SERAM REGENCY

NUMBER 08 OF 2019

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF WEST SERAM,

Considering:
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
a. that every citizen has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and has the right to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence;
b. that women and children as citizens, including vulnerable groups who tend to experience violence which constitutes acts that violate human rights, need to receive protection;
c. that the number of violence against women and children in West Seram Regency continues to increase and expand while protection for women and children has not been carried out optimally;
d. that laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts in the regions so that institutional support and regulations are needed to ensure their implementation;
e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, letter c, and letter d, it is necessary to establish a regional regulation on the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.

In View of:
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 40 of 2003 concerning the Establishment of East Seram Regency, West Seram Regency and Aru Islands Regency in Maluku Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2003 Number 155, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4350);
3. Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 109, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4235) as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 297, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5606);
4. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);
5. Presidential Regulation Number 61 of 2016 concerning the Child Protection Commission.

With Mutual Approval

REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF WEST SERAM REGENCY

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  
1. Region is West Seram Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Administration of Government Affairs by the regional government and the Regional House of Representatives.  
3. Regent is the Regent of West Seram.  
4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of West Seram Regency as an element of Regional Government administration.  
5. Administration is the form of implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence.  
6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to Victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  
7. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that Violence against Women will occur or has occurred.  
8. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow and develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive Protection from Violence and discrimination.  
9. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.  
10. Violence against Women is any action based on gender distinction that results in or may result in misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually or psychologically, economic, social exploitation, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.  
11. Violence against Children is any act against Children that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, economic, social exploitation, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of Children.  
12. Physical Violence is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.  
13. Sexual Violence is any act that violates a person's human dignity based on gender discrimination, which targets a person's body and sexuality that results in or may result in physical, psychological, economic, sexual, political, and/or social harm or suffering to the victim.  
14. Economic violence is any act that neglects family members in the form of not providing proper livelihood care or maintenance.  
15. Psychological Violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.  
16. Victim is a Woman and Child, as well as vulnerable groups, such as persons with disabilities, who experience acts of Violence.  
17. Victim of acts of Violence is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of Violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.  
18. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by companions/paralegals, Professional personnel in accordance with their profession in the form of psychologists and social workers, including advocates for the empowerment and recovery of Violence Victims.  
19. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by companions during the service process.  
20. Companion is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.  
21. Repatriation is an effort to return Victims of acts of Violence to the family, substitute family, or community or other social institutions that can provide social support and prevention efforts.  
22. Rehabilitation is the recovery of Victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of proper social functioning, both in the family and in society.  
23. Recovery in a broad sense, hereinafter abbreviated as PDML, is the process of supporting women and children victims of violence to become empowered to make decisions and pursue a just, dignified, and prosperous life, through a victim-centered, rights-based, multidimensional, community-based, and sustainable, continuous, and participatory approach, carried out by the regional government involving the role of family and community.  
24. Women and children protection service institution (hereinafter abbreviated as LPPA) is an integrated service unit established by the regional government to provide services to women and children victims of violence comprehensively, with quality, including complaint and information services, legal assistance and support, PDML (recovery in a broad sense) services, consisting of counseling, psychosocial, fulfillment of economic, social and cultural rights, medical services, and safe houses through a referral mechanism;  
25. Counseling is an effort to help and or assist victims in interpreting facts related to choices, plans, or adjustments that need to be made in the psychological development of victims carried out by psychologists or psychiatrists.  
26. Psychosocial is a victim recovery mechanism that integrates social support in the community and fulfillment of victims' rights by the government including opening access and information for victims.  
27. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide Protection.  
28. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions or other social environments that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.  
29. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as LPPA, is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional government to provide services to Women and Children victims of violence comprehensively and with quality including information services related to victims' rights, complaint handling services for victims of violence against women and children, Health services for women and children victims of violence, Psychosocial services for women and children victims of violence, legal assistance services and repatriation and Social Reintegration services as well as safe house services through a referral mechanism.  
30. Regional Action Plan is the foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out Prevention and Protection activities for Women and Children Victims of acts of Violence.  
31. Safe House is temporary housing provided to provide Protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.  
32. Child is a person who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old, including a Child who is still in the womb.  
33. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person who is recognized by law as a Woman.  
34. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.  
35. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a Woman and/or Child.  
36. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.  
37. The Administration of Protection of Women and Children is carried out based on the principles of:  
a. respect for human rights;  
b. non-discrimination;  
c. justice and gender equality;  
d. the best interests of the Child;  
e. respect for the opinions of Women and Children; and. 
f. justice and protection for women and children Victims of Violence.  
g. The right of children to life, survival, and development.  
38. The Administration of Protection of Women and Children aims to:  
a. provide Services and Protection to Women and children victims of violence from gender-based violence and the best interests of children that occur in the home and community (public);  
b. prevent all forms of Violence against Women and Children; and  
c. empower Women and Children Victims of Violence.  
39. The scope of Protection for Women and Children includes:  
a. institutions;  
b. forms and mechanisms of handling;  
c. prevention of acts of violence against women and children;  
d. assistance mechanisms;  
e. services for Victims of acts of Violence;  
f. Recovery of victims of acts of violence; and  
g. empowerment of Victims and their families.

# CHAPTER II

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 2

Every Woman and Child has the right:  
a. to be respected for their dignity and worth as human beings;  
b. to obtain Protection from family, community, Regional Government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on a court Protection order;  
c. to recovery of physical, psychological and sexual health in accordance with the suffering experienced by Victims of Violence;  
d. to special handling related to the confidentiality of Victims;  
e. to assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
f. to spiritual guidance services; and  
g. to determine their own decisions.

# CHAPTER III

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 3

Obligations and responsibilities in providing Protection to Women and Children are the responsibility of:  
a. the Regional government;  
b. the community;  
c. the family; and or parents

#### Article 4

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 3 letter a, include:  
a. implementing policies for the Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence established by the government;  
b. establishing policies, programs and activities for the Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;  
c. conducting cooperation in the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;  
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;  
e. allocating budget for the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence in accordance with the financial capacity of the Region; and  
f. fostering and supervising the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.  
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1), the regent establishes programs and action activities for the Protection of Women and Children in a Regional action plan.  
3. The Regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (2) is part of the Regional medium-term development plan. (4) Further provisions regarding the Regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 5

The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 3 letter b, include:  
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women and Children;  
b. providing Protection to Victims;  
c. providing emergency assistance;  
d. providing information and/or reporting acts of Violence against Women and Children to the competent authorities; and  
e. participating in the handling of Victims of Violence.

#### Article 6

The obligations and responsibilities of the family and/or parents as referred to in Article 3 letter c include:  
a. preventing all forms of Violence and protecting Women and Children as family members.  
b. Providing support for women and children victims of violence who are part of their family members

# CHAPTER IV

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional

#### Article 7

1. The Implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out in an integrated manner through P2TP2A.  
2. The establishment of P2TP2A. as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent Decree.  
3. Further provisions regarding the position, duties, functions and working procedures of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Forms and Mechanisms of Prevention

#### Article 8

Forms of prevention of acts of Violence against Women and Children carried out by P2TP2A. can be implemented through:  
a. socialization activities of laws and regulations to the community related to the Protection of the rights of Women and Children; and  
b. training of P2TP2A. members and OPD, vertical institutions and other related service institutions regarding the implementation of their duties in preventing acts of Violence against Women and Children.  
c. Implementation of coordination mechanisms between institutions that have main duties and functions related to the prevention and handling of violence against women and children
d. Implementation of referral mechanisms in the handling and recovery of victims of violence against women and children

#### Article 9

1. LPPA in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with P2TP2A. Sub-district, Village and competent parties in making efforts to prevent and handle acts of Violence against Women and Children.  
2. Further provisions regarding the mechanism for implementing prevention duties as referred to in paragraph (1), in accordance with standard operating procedures.  
3. Further provisions regarding the mechanism for implementing duties as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by regent regulation.

#### Article 10

Service standards for Victims organized by P2TP2A. in cooperation with related institutions, include:  
a. medical services;  
b. medicolegal services;  
c. psychosocial services;  
d. legal services; and  
e. support services for economic independence.

#### Article 11

1. P2TP2A. coordinates with P2TP2A. Sub-district and Village which is conducted every 3 (three) months in carrying out the task of serving Victims of Violence against Women and Children.  
2. The implementation of service duties as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with standard operating procedures.

## Part Three

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 12

1. The Regional Government conducts empowerment and awareness to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling to prevent acts of Violence against Women and Children (2) In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government makes efforts as follows:  
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;  
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;  
c. opening employment opportunities for Women;  
d. building community participation and concern to implement prevention and against Women and Children from acts of Violence;  
e. building and providing a complete information system that is easily accessible to women and victims of violence including poor women;  
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement officials, government officials, universities and various non-governmental organizations that are engaged and/or concerned and focused on Women and Children victims of violence; and  
g. opening community-based complaint posts at the village/Negeri level for the Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

#### Article 13

1. Prevention of acts of Violence against Women and Children as referred to in Article 12, shall be implemented by the work of regional apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:  
a. social;  
b. health;  
c. education;  
d. manpower;  
e. Women's empowerment and Child Protection;  
f. mental and spiritual; and  
g. peace and order.  
2. Prevention of acts of Violence by regional apparatus work units as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented based on the Regional Action Plan.

## Part Four

Assistance Mechanism

#### Article 14

1. Assistance is carried out by individuals or persons and/or institutions that cooperate with P2TP2A and build case referral mechanisms.  
2. The assistance mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures (SOP).  
3. Further provisions regarding the assistance mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Five

Service Standards for Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 15

1. The Regent establishes minimum service standards for services provided to Women and Children Victims of acts of Violence.
2. Forms of services provided to Women and Children Victims of acts of Violence, are as follows:
a. complaint services;
b. health services;
c. legal assistance services;
d. repatriation services;
e. social reintegration;
f. identification services; and g. Rehabilitation, Psychosocial and psychological services.
3. The Regional Government cooperates with government agencies, Provincial Government, other regency/Regency governments and the community in carrying out duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (2).
4. Further provisions regarding procedures for services and handling of Women and Children Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2) and paragraph (3) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 16

1. The Regional Government is obliged to provide complaint services, in order to provide Protection to Women and Children from acts of Violence.
2. The Regional Government is obliged to provide recovery houses or safe houses.
3. Further provisions regarding complaint services and social services as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Six

Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 17

1. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out empowerment of Women Victims of acts of Violence.
2. Empowerment of Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented by P2TP2A by involving other service institutions (3) In carrying out empowerment of Women Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government prepares empowerment programs in the economic field, which include:
a. Providing the needs required for entrepreneurship training for Women Victims of acts of Violence, in order to increase knowledge, attitudes and business skills;
b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;
c. providing assistance in developing productive economic businesses;
d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large enterprises, as well as financial institutions to develop businesses of Women Victims of acts of Violence;
e. seeking provision of capital for Women Victims of acts of Violence; and
f. expanding access to information and promoting products of Women Victims of acts of Violence.
3. In carrying out empowerment of Women Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may establish cooperation with job training institutions organized by the private sector.

#### Article 18

1. Empowerment of Women Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 17, is carried out by providing training to improve capability, skills and independence.
2. Training as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out through:
a. on-the-job training;
b. pre-placement training; and
c. job-ready training.

# CHAPTER V

## COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 19

1. The Regional Government may conduct cooperation in order to achieve the objectives of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children. (2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted with:
a. the government;
b. provincial government;
c. other regency/Regency government; and d. non-governmental institutions.
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. exchange of data and information;
b. rehabilitation of Victims of acts of Violence;
c. repatriation and social reintegration; and d. provision of evidence and witnesses and followed up in accordance with laws and regulations.
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), is conducted in the form of joint agreement.

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 20

1. The Regional Government establishes partnerships with the business world in the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children.
2. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1), is conducted through:
a. Notification of information on employment opportunities for Women Victims of Violence;
b. education and training for Women Victims of Violence;
c. educational assistance for Women and Children Victims of Violence who are withdrawn from education; and
d. fostering and increasing economic independence of Women Victims of Violence.
3. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) is implemented in the form of an agreement.

# CHAPTER VI

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

## Part One

Guidance

#### Article 21

1. Regional Apparatus is obliged to provide guidance on the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence.
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. provision of facilities;
d. monitoring; and
e. evaluation.

## Part Two

Supervision

#### Article 22

1. Regional Apparatus is obliged to conduct supervision of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.
2. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), is conducted with the principles of:
a. professional;
b. transparent; and
c. accountable.
3. In addition to supervision conducted by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1), the community may conduct supervision of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.
4. Supervision conducted by the community as referred to in paragraph (3) by conveying aspirations to the Regent or DPRD.

# CHAPTER VII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 23

1. The community participates in the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. forming family partners at the sub-district and village level by the community;
b. forming women and children protection units within community organizations;
c. provision of safe houses or shelters;
d. provision and management of children's orphanages e. establishing rehabilitation centers for women and children victims of violence;
f. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
g. providing first aid to victims; and
h. reporting to the competent authority if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

# CHAPTER VIII

## REPORTING

#### Article 24

1. LPPA is obliged to report the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence to the Regent.
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) is submitted in writing, including:
a. administration;
b. finance;
c. services; and
d. performance.
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2), is implemented at least every 3 (three) months.

# CHAPTER IX

## FUNDING SOURCES

#### Article 25

Funds for the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, are sourced from:
a. regional revenue and expenditure budget; and
b. other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 26

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or non-governmental organizations that implement Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with the financial capacity of the Region and is implemented in accordance with laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

# CHAPTER X

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 27

LPPA that already exists at the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, continues to carry out its duties until the formation of P2TP2A based on the provisions in this Regional Regulation.

#### Article 28

Implementing regulations of this Regional Regulation must be stipulated no later than 1 (one) year from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

#### Article 29

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by its placement in the Regional Gazette of West Seram Regency.

#### I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide Protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. That Women and Children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience Violence and therefore need to receive Protection. Violence against Women and Children constitutes a violation of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed without discrimination.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.

Furthermore, Violence against Women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or hardship to Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child. The existence of Women and Children Victims of Violence has not yet received adequate services so that comprehensive implementation of Protection for Women and Children is required.

In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in SBB Regency so as to avoid Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children, particularly with regard to institutions, forms and mechanisms of prevention, prevention of acts of Violence, assistance mechanisms, services for Victims of acts of Violence and empowerment of Victims of acts of Violence.
