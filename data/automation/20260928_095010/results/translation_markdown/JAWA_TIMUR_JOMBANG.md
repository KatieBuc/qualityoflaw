# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is Jombang Regency;
2. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration;
3. Regent is the Regent of Jombang;
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter referred to as DPRD is the regional people's representative institution as an element of regional government administration;
5. Regional Government Administration is the implementation of government affairs by the Regional Government and DPRD according to the principles of Autonomy and co-administration with the principle of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
6. Agency is a Regional Work Unit whose main tasks and functions include the fields of social affairs, women's empowerment and child protection;
7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb;
8. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering to the victim;
9. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes death;
10. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person:
11. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, coercion of sexual relations in an unnatural or unwanted manner against another person whether for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes;
12. Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences misery and or suffering as a result of violence;
13. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims either temporarily or based on a Court decision;
14. Service is an action that must be immediately taken for victims when seeing, hearing and reasonably suspected that violence against victims will, is or has occurred;
15. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence;
16. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter referred to as P2T-P2A is a service provider institution based in a Hospital for victims of violence especially women and children which is managed jointly by the regional government and the community incorporated in a Team conducted in a cross-functional and cross-sectoral manner in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psycho-social and legal services;
17. Safe house is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims;
18. Community is individuals, individuals, families, groups, communities and social organizations;
19. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, and mother and her children;
20. Household is family members and relatives (a person who has blood relations with family members) and non-relatives, who live and eat from one kitchen and reside in one house.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The principles of implementing protection for women and children victims of violence are:
1. Respect for the rights of victims;
2. Justice and gender equality;
3. Non-discrimination;
4. Best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

The purpose of implementing protection for women and children victims of violence is to provide protection and services to women and children victims of violence that are gender-based and in the best interests of women and children.

##### CHAPTER III VICTIMS' RIGHTS

#### Article 4

Every victim has the right to obtain protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage and confidential handling from individuals, groups or Government and non-Government institutions.

#### Article 5

Every victim has the right to report and obtain assistance both psychologically and legally and to obtain guarantees of their rights related to their status as a wife, mother, child, family member or as a member of society.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to carry out efforts to prevent violence against women and children in the form of:  
a. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence as well as anti-violence values against women and children;  
b. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of socialization and understanding of anti-violence values.  
2. The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to carry out protection efforts for victims in the form of:  
a. collecting data and information about victims;  
b. establishing and facilitating integrated service institutions and safe houses for victims;  
c. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for victims;  
3. The implementation of obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), is technically carried out in an integrated manner by the relevant Regional Work Units.

# CHAPTER V

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One Institutions

#### Article 7

The Implementation of Protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children (P2T-P2A).

## Part Two

Forms and mechanisms of Services

#### Article 8

1. Forms of services for victims organized by P2T-P2A include:  
a. Medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel;  
b. Medicolegal services are a form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;  
c. Psychosocial services are services provided by companions to help restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses that protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength, and independence in resolving their problems or undergoing legal processes;  
d. Legal assistance services to victims during the legal process;  
e. Socio-political services in the form of policy advocacy that is preventive and curative in nature for victims.  
2. The service mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) is organized according to Standard Operational Procedures (SOP) which will be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

Service Principles

#### Article 9

1. The implementation of services for victims is carried out quickly, safely, empathetically, non-discriminatory, easily accessible, and with a guarantee of confidentiality;  
2. The implementation of services as referred to in paragraph (1) insofar as it concerns visum et repertum examinations is not subject to fees.

# CHAPTER VI ASSISTANCE

#### Article 10

Assistance is carried out by a person or institution that has the expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims who are members of the P2T-P2A Management Team.

# CHAPTER VII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 11

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF JOMBANG REGENCY

NUMBER 14 YEAR 2008 CONCERNING IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL ELUCIDATION

The State has an obligation to provide a sense of security for its citizens from threats and actions that can disturb or damage mental, physical, sexual or economic security, this is philosophically stated in the Preamble to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, that the purpose of establishing the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia, the first is to protect the entire nation and all of Indonesia's territory.  

Because the Government of Indonesia has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women), it is obliged to comply with these provisions.  

Likewise in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia fourth amendment, Chapter X.A on Human Rights, states that the enforcement and fulfillment of Human Rights is the responsibility of the State, especially the Government.  

Furthermore, in the National Action Plan for the Elimination of Violence Against Women of 1999 it is stated that women as mothers of the nation and children as successors of the nation are creatures of God Almighty, so they must be protected and their honor, dignity and self-esteem must be safeguarded in a reasonable and proportional manner both legally, politically, socially and culturally, without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race and class.  

From the series of provisions above, the Government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, and socially to prevent, suppress, reduce and eliminate all forms of violence against women and children because this is a crime against human existence.

##### II. ELUCIDATION ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

Number 1  
Respect for the rights of victims the fulfillment of the rights of victims.  

Number 2  
Gender justice is a process to be fair to men and women;  

Gender equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, national defense and security activities and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

Number 3  
Non-discrimination is an attitude and treatment towards victims by not making distinctions based on age, sex, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.  

Number 4  
The best interests of victims are all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, so the best interests of victims must be a primary consideration.

#### Article 3

What is meant by gender-based violence is actions based on gender relations that place women in a subordinate position to men and all actions that deprive children of their rights and freedoms.

#### Article 4

What is meant by obtaining protection is obtaining protection from family, the Police, the Prosecutor's Office, the Court, Advocates, Social Institutions or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court, husband, wife and children.

What is meant by obtaining information is knowing about the existence of complaint centers, P2T-P2A and or other competent supporting institutions regarding matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and being involved in every decision-making process related to assistance and the development of case handling.

What is meant by integrated services are services that include medical, medico-legal, psychosocial, legal and socio-political services.

What is meant by continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage is handling that does not only stop at physical and psychological healing, but until the victim can resume their life in society including restoration of good name and the victim has the right to obtain socio-economic empowerment.

#### Article 5

What is meant by the victim having the right to report is that the victim can report acts of violence to the Secretariat of P2T-P2A Jombang Regency or Jombang Police Resort (UPPA), or Women's Crisis Center (WCC) Jombang. What is meant by obtaining psychological and legal assistance is obtaining psychological and legal assistance at every level of examination and throughout the judicial process.
