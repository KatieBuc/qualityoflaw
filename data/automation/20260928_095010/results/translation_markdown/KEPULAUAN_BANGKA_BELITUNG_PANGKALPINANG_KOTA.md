# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  
1. City is the City of Pangkalpinang.  
2. City Government is the mayor as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Pangkalpinang
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of the City of Pangkalpinang.  
5. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  
6. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social, knowledge, and skills capabilities so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.  
7. Discrimination against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex, which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women.  
8. All Forms of Discrimination against Women are all forms of Discrimination that include regional dimensions (disaster areas, conflict areas, border areas, underdeveloped areas, remote areas, and others), age dimensions (productive age, elderly, and others), and special dimensions (persons with disabilities, workers, and others).  
9. Violence against Women is any act that violates, obstructs, eliminates enjoyment and disregards women's human rights.  
10. Domestic Violence is any act against a woman that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically, and/or household neglect including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty within the scope of the household.  
11. Elimination of Violence against Women is a guarantee provided by the Regional Government to prevent the occurrence of violence, prosecute perpetrators of violence and protect victims.  
12. Elimination of Domestic Violence is a guarantee provided by the state to prevent the occurrence of domestic violence, prosecute perpetrators of domestic violence, and protect victims of domestic violence.  
13. Victim is a woman who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.  
14. Companion is a parent or representative from an institution that has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.  
15. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.  
16. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.  
17. Gender Justice is a process to be fair to men and women.

#### Article 2

1. The Principles of Women's Empowerment are:  
a. respect for human rights;  
b. gender equality and justice;  
c. protection of victims' rights; and  
d. non-discriminatory.  
2. The Objectives of Women's Empowerment and Protection are to:  
a. ensure the fulfillment of women's rights so that they can live and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity;  
b. provide gender justice and equality;  
c. provide protection and security for women victims of violence and discrimination, as well as criminal acts of trafficking in persons;  
d. eliminate all forms of violence and discrimination against women;  
e. provide services to women Victims of violence, and witnesses; and  
f. carry out recovery of women Victims of violence to increase the participation of women both individually and in groups as potential and resources in the implementation of social community life.  
3. The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:  
a. women's rights;  
b. obligations and responsibilities of regional government;  
c. women's empowerment;  
d. women's protection;  
e. protection of women victims of violence;  
f. prohibitions and sanctions;  
g. community participation;  
h. monitoring and evaluation; and  
i . funding.

# CHAPTER II

## RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 3

1. Every woman has the right to:  
a. live and maintain life and improve her standard of living;  
b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue offspring;  
c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life to become a faithful, pious, responsible, noble, happy, and prosperous human being in accordance with human rights;  
d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;  
e. be involved in every stage of the development process;  
f. be free from slavery or enslavement and threats;  
g. obtain protection of personal self, family, honor, dignity and property rights;  
h. obtain welfare and a decent life;  
i. participate in politics;  
j. perform legal acts;  
k. freely choose a partner in marriage; and  
l. receive fair wages in accordance with labor regulations.  
2. The right to participate in politics as referred to in paragraph (1) letter i is, women have the right to:  
a. vote and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, or elections for political positions and other positions based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret voting; and  
b. be appointed to every government position.  
3. Every woman who is in a condition of disability, elderly, and/or pregnant, has the right to obtain facilities and special treatment.  
4. Every elderly woman, person with physical disability and/or mental disability, has the right to obtain care, non-formal education.

## Part Two

Rights of Women with Disabilities

#### Article 4

Every woman with disabilities has the right to obtain:  
a. education in all units, paths, types, and levels of education;  
b. work and a decent livelihood in accordance with the type and degree of disability, education, and ability;  
c. equal treatment to play a role in development and enjoy its results;  
d. accessibility in the context of independence;  
e. rehabilitation, social assistance, and maintenance of social welfare standards; and  
f. equal rights to develop talents, abilities, and social life.

## Part Three

Rights of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 5

Every woman victim of violence has:  
a. the right to be respected for her dignity and dignity as a human being;  
b. the right to recovery of health and psychological from the suffering experienced by the Victim;  
c. the right to determine her own decisions;  
d. the right to obtain information;  
e. the right to confidentiality;  
f. the right to compensation;  
g. the right to social rehabilitation;  
h. the right to complaint handling;  
i. the right of the Victim and her family to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and  
j. the right to assistance.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 6

1. The City Government has the obligation and responsibility for the implementation of empowerment and protection of women.
2. The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;
b. determination of implementation guidelines;
c. provision of services; and
d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.
3. The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in an integrated manner.

#### Article 7

In implementing policies, programs and activities for empowerment and protection of women, the City Government may cooperate with the community/community institutions.

# CHAPTER V

## EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN

#### Article 8

Empowerment of women is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:
a. economy;
b. social culture;
c. politics and government;
d. law;
e. education;
f. health;
g. employment; and
h. social security.

#### Article 9

The implementation of empowerment of women in the economic field as referred to in Article 8 letter a shall be carried out through:
a. provision of skills and job training;
b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;
c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;
d. facilitation and capital assistance; and
e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 10

Empowerment of women in the social cultural field as referred to in Article 8 letter b shall be carried out through:
a. improvement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;
b. improvement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotional, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health;
c. improvement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and
d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 11

The implementation of empowerment of women in the field of politics and government as referred to in Article 8 letter c includes:
a. involvement of women in decision making;
b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public positions;
c. participation in general elections; and
d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.

#### Article 12

The implementation of empowerment of women in the field of law as referred to in Article 8 letter d includes:
a. improvement of awareness and knowledge in the field of law through communication, information and education services; and
b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.

#### Article 13

The implementation of empowerment of women in the field of education as referred to in Article 8 letter e through:
a. provision of formal and non-formal education;
b. formal education as referred to in letter a is as regulated by the government; and
c. non-formal education as referred to in letter a includes women's empowerment education, skills education and job training.

#### Article 14

1. The implementation of women in the field of health as referred to in Article 8 letter f health services in order to undergo reproductive life and sexual life that is healthy, safe and free from coercion and/or violence.
2. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. health services during the period before pregnancy, during pregnancy, during childbirth and after giving birth;
b. pregnancy regulation, contraceptive services and sexual health; and
c. reproductive health services.
3. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out through promotional, preventive, curative and rehabilitative approaches.

#### Article 15

1. The implementation of women in the field of employment as referred to in Article 8 letter g have equal opportunities without gaps and differences with men.
2. Employers provide protection to female workers which includes welfare, safety and health.

#### Article 16

The implementation of women in the field of social security as referred to in Article 8 letter h, is the responsibility of the City Government and must provide guidance facilities and provide social security rights.

# CHAPTER VI

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 17

The Mayor in implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women shall undertake efforts to:
a. coordinate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women among regional apparatus;
b. cooperate with regencies/cities within one province and cooperation between regencies/cities in other provinces in the implementation of policy programs, and activities for the protection of women in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
c. strengthen the institutional capacity of gender mainstreaming for the implementation of policy programs and activities for the protection of women;
d. facilitate services for the protection of women;
e. provide services for the protection of women;
f. implement affirmative action for the protection of women; and
g. prepare a data system for the protection of women.

#### Article 18

The implementation of protection of women is provided to:
a. female workers/laborers;
b. elderly women;
c. women with disabilities;
d. homeless women;
e. domestic workers;
f. women as family economic supporters; and
g. women inmates of correctional institutions.

## Part Two

Health Protection

#### Article 19

1. Women have the right to obtain special protection from things that threaten their safety and/or health.
2. Special protection is given to women because their reproductive function is guaranteed and protected by law.
3. The City Regional Government facilitates health protection in accordance with regional financial capacity.
4. The form of health protection for women as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be further regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

## Part Three

Female workers/laborers

#### Article 20

1. Women have equal opportunities without discrimination to obtain employment and seek proper appreciation for their work.
2. Equal opportunities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be realized through the fulfillment of protection of the rights of female workers to receive equal treatment from employers.
3. Further provisions regarding the fulfillment of protection of the rights of female workers as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

## Part Four

Elderly Women

#### Article 21

1. The City Government provides protection to elderly women who are not independent, sick and economically incapable so that they can realize and enjoy a decent standard of living.
2. Further provisions regarding the form of protection as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

## Part Five

Women with Disabilities

#### Article 22

1. Implementation of protection for women with disabilities in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination including freedom from cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity and freedom from exploitation.
2. Women with disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection rights over:
a. guarantee of continuity and personal self-development;
b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, sports, arts and culture as well as utilization of information and communication technology;
c. accessibility of information, legal aid, mobility, social services; and
d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

## Part Six

Homeless Women

#### Article 23

1. Implementation of protection for homeless women in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.
2. Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection rights over:
a. decent housing;
b. health and social security;
c. population administration registration;
d. opportunity to obtain skills to improve economic status; and
e. access to information and legal consultation services.

## Part Seven

Domestic Workers

#### Article 24

1. Implementation of protection for domestic workers in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity and exploitation in employment relations.
2. Domestic workers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection over:
a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;
b. health and social security of employment;
c. access to information and legal consultation services; and
d. opportunity to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Eight

Women as Family Economic Pillars

#### Article 25

1. Implementation of protection for women as family economic pillars in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, negative stigma and economic empowerment for women as family economic pillars.
2. Women as family economic pillars as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection rights over:
a. discriminatory actions and efforts to weaken the function of women as family economic pillars;
b. strengthening of capacity and recognition as family economic pillars in their environment; and
c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.

## Part Nine

Women Former Inmates of Correctional Institutions

#### Article 26

1. Implementation of protection for women former inmates of Correctional Institutions in order to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting in community life and being able to carry out life normally.
2. Women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection over:
a. social rehabilitation and reintegration; and
b. opportunity to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Ten

Women Disaster Victims

#### Article 27

1. Implementation of protection for women disaster victims in order to provide protection during disasters and post-disaster.
2. Women disaster victims as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection over:
a. access to decent housing;
b. health services;
c. access to information services and legal consultation; and
d. post-disaster recovery

# CHAPTER VII

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 28

1. Every woman victim of violence has the right to receive protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage and confidential handling from individuals, groups or institutions, the City Government and the community.
2. In the event of violence, every victim has the right to receive health services and assistance, both psychologically and legally and to receive guarantees of their rights related to their status as women.

## Part Two

Obligations and Efforts of the Regional Government

#### Article 29

1. The City Government is obliged and responsible to carry out efforts to prevent violence against women in the form of:
a. collecting data and information about victims of violence and laws and regulations;
b. conducting education on anti-violence values against women;
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for victims of violence;
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for victims of violence; and
e. preparing reports on the implementation of protection for women victims of violence.
2. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence against women, the City Government is obliged to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
a. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving community elements; and
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims.
3. The City Government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

## Part Three

Forms of Violence and Protection

#### Article 30

1. Forms of violence against women include:
a. physical violence;
b. sexual violence;
c. economic violence; and
d. psychological violence.
2. The form of physical violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, unconsciousness and or causes death.
3. The form of sexual violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
4. The form of economic violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c is the neglect of women in the form of not providing a decent livelihood, care or maintenance.
5. The form of psychological violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
6. Every woman victim of violence must receive protection in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 31

Every victim of violence must receive protection in accordance with applicable provisions.

#### Article 32

1. Forms of protection against violence toward women include:  
a. medical protection;  
b. legal protection;  
c. medico-legal protection (forensic medicine);  
d. economic protection; and  
e. psychological protection.  
2. The form of medical protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of the victim carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.  
3. The form of legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is in the form of services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.  
4. The form of medico-legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c is in the form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field.  
5. The form of economic protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d is in the form of services for skills and to provide economic access so that victims of violence can be independent.  
6. The form of psychological protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e is in the form of psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving assistance in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims.

#### Article 33

To provide Protection as referred to in Article 27, the City Regional Government may undertake efforts to:  
a. provide services and handling as quickly as possible to witnesses and/or victims;  
b. provide ease, comfort, safety and free of charge for witnesses and/or victims;  
c. maintain the confidentiality of witnesses and/or victims;  
d. guarantee justice and legal certainty for witnesses and/or victims;  
e. provide special service rooms at Police Offices;  
f. provide apparatus, health workers, social workers, and spiritual counselors;  
g. create and develop a system and mechanism for cooperation of service programs involving parties that are easily accessible to victims;  
h. provide protection for companions, witnesses, family and friends of victims;  
i. provide protection for female workers/laborers in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 36

1. The community may participate in the implementation of empowerment and protection of women.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, groups or social community organizations.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of:  
a. cooperation;  
b. active role in policy formulation; and c. complaints/reports.  
4. The obligations and responsibilities of the community in the protection of women are implemented through activities:  
a. preventing the occurrence of criminal acts;  
b. providing protection to victims;  
c. providing emergency assistance;  
d. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection orders; and  
e. providing information or reporting the occurrence of criminal acts of violence to law enforcement officials.  
5. Further provisions regarding community participation as referred to in paragraph (3) are regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 37

1. The City Government implements monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of policies on empowerment and protection of women.  
2. The implementation of monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out periodically to ensure synergy, continuity and effectiveness of policies.  
3. The results of monitoring and evaluation are used as input for the formulation of policies, programs and activities for the following year.

# CHAPTER X

## FUNDING

#### Article 38

Funding for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for empowerment and protection of women is sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget, environmental social responsibility and other funds that are legal and non-binding.

# CHAPTER XI
