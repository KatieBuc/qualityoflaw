# CHAPTER II

## SCOPE

#### Article 2

The scope of social protection includes: women and children with social problems.

#### Article 3

Women and children with social problems as referred to in Article 2, include:  
a. Neglected children;  
b. Children who are victims of gender-based violence and/or mistreated;  
c. Delinquent children;  
d. Street children;  
e. Children with disabilities;  
f. Women with socio-economic vulnerability;  
g. Women who are victims of gender-based violence and/or mistreated;  
h. women and children victims of human trafficking;  
i. children with special needs;  
j. children in emergency situations.

#### Article 4

Social protection for women and children in the form of:  
a. Social assistance;  
b. Social advocacy; and/or  
c. legal aid.

# CHAPTER III

## PRINCIPLES, FUNCTIONS AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 5

Social protection for women and children is based on:  
a. humanity;  
b. Justice and gender equality;  
c. protection;  
d. best interests of women and children;  
e. non-discrimination;  
f. participation;  
g. professional; and  
h. partnership.

#### Article 6

Social protection for women and children functions to:  
a. prevent the emergence, growth and development of social welfare problems.  
b. restore social functions in achieving independence.  
c. increase community social resilience in preventing and handling social welfare problems.  
d. develop social potential.  
e. empower social resources.  
f. prevent social vulnerability.

#### Article 7

Social protection for women and children aims to:  
a. eliminate all forms of violence against women and children.  
b. protect, provide a sense of security for women and children.  
c. improve the level of welfare, quality, and survival.  
d. restore social functions in order to achieve independence.  
e. increase community social resilience in preventing and handling social welfare problems.  
f. increase awareness, capability, concern and social responsibility of the business world in the administration of social welfare in an institutionalized and sustainable manner.  
g. increase awareness, capability, concern and social responsibility of the business world and society.

# CHAPTER IV

## ADMINISTRATION

## Part One

General

#### Article 8

1. Social protection for women and children is a joint obligation among the Regional Government, community, social organizations and business sector which is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner.  
2. Social protection for women and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is prioritized for women and children who have lives that are not humanely decent and have social problem criteria:  
a. poverty;  
b. abandonment;  
c. disability;  
d. social disability and behavioral deviation; and/or  
e. victims of gender-based violence and children and/or mistreatment, as well as victims of human trafficking;  
f. victims of social conflict.  
3. Social protection for women and children, as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented through:  
a. prevention efforts;  
b. handling efforts;  
c. recovery efforts;  
d. protection and support efforts.

#### Article 9

Prevention Efforts as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (3) letter a, may be carried out among others through efforts of:  
a. social counseling and guidance;  
b. Social assistance;  
c. improvement of health and education levels;  
d. improvement of access to resources;  
e. empowerment.

#### Article 10

1. Handling Efforts as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (3) letter b, may be carried out among others through efforts of:  
a. outreach (raid operations);  
b. identification;  
c. selection;  
d. social motivation;  
e. social guidance and assistance.  
2. In the context of handling efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), further actions may be taken consisting of:  
a. referral to Temporary Shelter House;  
b. referral to Social Rehabilitation Institution/Center;  
c. return to parents/guardian/family/place of origin;  
d. provision of health services.

#### Article 11

1. Recovery Efforts as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (3) letter c, may be carried out among others through efforts of:  
a. Initial motivation from outreach results (raid operations);  
b. Identification;  
c. Selection;  
d. Social motivation;  
e. Distribution/referral to rehabilitation centers;  
f. Return to place of origin;  
g. Social guidance and skills training;  
h. Stimulus assistance;  
i. Supervision.  
2. Handling of rehabilitative efforts may be carried out in centers within the region and outside the region.  
3. In the implementation of rehabilitative efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), may be provided through guidance, education, training both physical, mental, social, medical rehabilitation, work skills according to their talents and abilities, social assistance, distribution and follow-up guidance.

#### Article 12

Protection and support efforts as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (3) letter d, may be carried out through efforts to provide facilities which include:  
a. Child Care Social Institution that can receive referrals from private institutions and equipped with workshop facilities;  
b. Women's Work Center;  
c. Provision of facilities/accessibility for persons with disabilities in buildings and public facilities.

#### Article 13

Technical implementation of social protection and social welfare administration for women and children as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2) is further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Social Protection and Social Welfare Administration for Women

#### Article 14

Every Woman has the right to obtain social protection in the form of equal treatment and opportunity in education and training, work protection for safety and/or health relating to women's reproductive function in accordance with decent norms of life and livelihood.

#### Article 15

Every woman is obliged to maintain her dignity and that of her family as well as her nature as a woman by taking into account her function and role in family and/or community life in accordance with her abilities.

#### Article 16

1. The Regional Government and/or community provides social protection and social welfare administration to women who are unable, abandoned, exploited, trafficked and prostitutes.  
2. Social protection and social welfare administration for women as referred to in paragraph (1), through:  
a. education and training services;  
b. opportunities to work and do business;  
c. physical, religious, mental, and social guidance;  
d. health services;  
e. legal assistance;  
f. other special social protection.  
3. Social protection and social welfare administration for women as referred to in paragraph (2) is the responsibility of SKPD/UKPD in accordance with their duties and functions.  
4. Social protection and social welfare administration for women as referred to in paragraph (2), may be organized by the community, social organizations, and business sector.

#### Article 17

Every institution both government and private that organizes public services or employs women must provide adequate facilities for the interests of women.

## Part Three

Social Protection and Social Welfare Administration for Children

#### Article 18

1. Every child has the right to receive social protection and social welfare administration from parents, family, community, and regional government to grow and develop normally.  
2. Every child has the right to receive social protection and social welfare administration to develop their abilities and social life in accordance with the culture and personality of the nation to become good and useful members of society.  
3. Every child has the right to receive maintenance of child welfare standards and protection from environments that endanger and/or hinder their growth and development normally.  
4. Every child has the right to receive first aid, assistance and protection in dangerous situations. every child has the right to receive protection from parents against all forms of physical and mental violence, neglect, mistreatment, exploitation and sexual abuse, as well as the right to care, religious guidance, and mental social guidance.

#### Article 19

1. Everyone is prohibited from abandoning, committing acts of violence and/or exploitation against children.  
2. Everyone is prohibited from employing children under working age as regulated in the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 20

The regional government and/or community may carry out social protection and social welfare administration for children for the purpose of improving child welfare carried out in accordance with statutory provisions.

#### Article 21

1. Social protection and social welfare administration for children as referred to in Article 18, in the form of:  
a. Care and upbringing;  
b. Health services and nutritional improvement;  
c. Education and recreation services;  
d. Religious, mental and social guidance;  
e. Social rehabilitation;  
f. Social assistance;  
g. Family reunification;  
h. Population administration and civil registration services;  
i. Funeral servicesj. Legal assistance services;  
k. Maintenance of social welfare standards;   
l. Provision of child care facilities at workplaces;  
m. Other special social protection.  
2. For children with disabilities in addition to receiving social protection and social welfare administration for children as referred to in paragraph (1), also receive accessibility services.  
3. Social protection and social welfare administration for children as referred to in paragraph (1), is the responsibility of SKPD/UKPD in accordance with their duties and functions.  
4. Social protection and social welfare administration for children as referred to in paragraph (1) may be organized by the community, social organizations and business sector.  
5. Further provisions regarding forms of social protection and social welfare administration for children as referred to in paragraph (1), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

## RESPONSIBILITY OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

## AND COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Responsibility of Regional Government

#### Article 22

a. planning, implementation, development, guidance and supervision of social protection and implementation of social welfare for women and children;  
b. application of service standards for social protection and implementation of social welfare for women and children;  
c. provision and/or granting of facilities as well as infrastructure and facilities;  
d. granting of facilities for social protection and implementation of social welfare for women and children;  
e. development of institutional capacity and social resources in accordance with the development of science and technology;  
f. facilitating participation from the community, social organizations and/or the business sector in social protection and implementation of social welfare for women and children.

Community Participation

#### Article 24

a. Individuals;  
b. Families;  
c. Religious organizations;  
d. Social community organizations;  
e. Non-governmental organizations;  
f. Professional organizations;  
g. Business entities;  
h. Social welfare institutions; and  
g. Foreign social welfare institutions.

#### Article 25

a. professional social worker associations;  
b. social work educational institutions; and

#### Article 26

#### Article 27

The regional government may grant awards to the community, social organizations and business entities that play a role in social protection and implementation of social welfare for women and children.

#### Article 28

1. The community may establish a social organization to optimize participation in coordinating social protection and implementation of social welfare for women and children (2) The regional government shall facilitate the establishment of social organizations as referred to in paragraph (1) according to applicable laws and regulations.

a. not seeking profit/non-profit;  
b. in the form of an institution;  
c. oriented to the public interest;  
d. managed professionally;  
6. Social organizations as referred to in paragraph (1) must obtain a permit from the Regent through the head of SKPD/UKPD responsible in the field of social protection and implementation of social welfare for women and children.  
7. Further provisions regarding the licensing procedures as referred to in paragraph (6) shall refer to applicable laws and regulations.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF WONOSOBO REGENCY

NUMBER 2 OF 2012 CONCERNING SOCIAL PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

##### I. GENERAL ELUCIDATION

The development targets in the field of social welfare regulated in this Regional Regulation are directed at Women and Children. Especially programs to improve the quality of life and protection of women and children. This is adjusted to the purpose and function of a Regional Regulation to regulate certain matters in accordance with the conditions and needs of a region.  

This Regional Regulation on Social Protection for Women and Children aims to provide a legal basis for Social Protection efforts for Women and Children as well as to provide a study within the philosophical, sociological and juridical framework regarding the need for a Regional Regulation that becomes the legal foundation for handling social problems in Wonosobo Regency especially related to Women and Children.  

Substantively, that in an effort to overcome the problems of Women and Children in Wonosobo Regency, it is necessary to formulate a Regional Regulation on Social Protection for Women and Children which essentially constitutes an action that is rooted or has a strong foundation, namely in good governance, and so that it is formulated with correct principles.  

The regulation of the field of Social Protection for Women and Children must pay attention to environmental and social aspects, so that the social conditions of the community are controlled. The regulation of Social Protection for Women and Children through Regional Regulations can be viewed as a solution as well as an effort to prevent negative impacts that have the potential to harm the interests of the community.  

The absence of a Regional Regulation that specifically regulates Social Protection for Women and Children in Wonosobo Regency, makes the urgency of this Regional Regulation indeed needed as a legal umbrella for the creation of legal certainty in the matter of Social Protection for Women and Children, which is an obligation of the Regional Government, especially the Wonosobo Regional Government to provide Social Protection for Women and Children of Wonosobo Regency.

#### Article 3
a. A neglected child is a child aged 0-18 years who, due to certain reasons (poor/unable, one or both parents/guardians are sick or deceased, family is not harmonious), cannot have their basic needs met properly, whether physically, spiritually or socially.  
b. A child who is a victim of gender-based violence or is mistreated is a child aged 0-18 years who is threatened physically and non-physically due to violence, is mistreated or treated improperly in the family environment or closest social environment, so that their basic needs cannot be met properly, whether physically, spiritually or socially.  
c. A delinquent child is a child aged 0-18 years whose behavior deviates from the norms and customs prevailing in society and their environment, thereby harming themselves, their family and others, will disturb public order, but due to age cannot yet be prosecuted legally.  
d. A street child is a child aged 0-18 years who spends most of their time seeking a livelihood and/or wandering on the streets or in public places.  
e. A child with disabilities is a child aged 0-18 years who has physical and/or mental abnormalities, which can interfere with or constitute obstacles and barriers for them to carry out activities properly, consisting of persons with physical disabilities, persons with mental disabilities, persons with physical and mental disabilities.  
f. A woman with socio-economic vulnerability is an adult woman aged 18-59 years, unmarried or widowed, who does not have sufficient income to meet daily basic needs.  
g. A woman who is a victim of gender-based violence and/or is mistreated is a woman aged 18-59 years who is threatened physically or non-physically (psychologically) due to violence, is mistreated or treated improperly in the family environment or closest social environment.  
h. Trafficking in women and children is the transfer, transportation, mobilization, sending of children and women within a country or abroad illegally and unlawfully, with or without the consent of the person concerned, to then be employed exploitatively, especially in the prostitution environment

#### Article 5
a. What is meant by "the principle of solidarity" is that in the implementation of social welfare it must be based on social concern to help people who need help with empathy and compassion.  
b. What is meant by "the principle of justice" is that in the implementation of social welfare it must emphasize aspects of equity, non-discrimination and balance between rights and obligations.  
c. What is meant by "the principle of benefit" is that in the implementation of social welfare it must provide benefits for improving the quality of life of citizens.  
d. What is meant by "the principle of integration" is that in the implementation of social welfare it must integrate various related components so that they can run in a coordinated and synergistic manner.  
e. What is meant by "the principle of partnership" is that in addressing social welfare issues, partnership is required between the Government and the community, the Government as the responsible party and the community as the Government's partner in addressing social welfare issues and improving social welfare.  
f. What is meant by "the principle of openness" is to provide the widest possible access to the community to obtain information related to the implementation of social welfare.  
g. What is meant by "the principle of accountability" is that in every implementation of social welfare it must be accountable in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
h. What is meant by "the principle of participation" is that in every implementation of social welfare it must involve all components of society.  
i. What is meant by "the principle of professionalism" is that in every implementation of social welfare to the community it should be based on professionalism in accordance with the scope of their duties and carried out as optimally as possible.  
j. What is meant by "the principle of sustainability" is that in implementing social welfare it is carried out continuously, so that independence is achieved.  
k. What is meant by "the principle of non-discrimination" is treatment that does not distinguish between ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status of children, birth order of children, and physical and mental conditions.  
l. What is meant by "gender equality" is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, legal, economic, socio-cultural, educational and national defense and security (hankamnas) activities, as well as equality in enjoying the results of such development.
