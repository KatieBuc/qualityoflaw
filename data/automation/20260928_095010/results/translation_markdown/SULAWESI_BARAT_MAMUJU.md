NUMBER 9 OF 2023

CONCERNING

EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION OF WOMEN

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF MAMUJU,

Considering:
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
a. that the fulfillment of constitutional rights and protection of women's human rights regarding empowerment and protection of women is one of the values contained in Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution;
b. that women play a very important role in the process of continuation and creation of quality generations, thus requiring a sense of security, obtaining guarantees of protection from acts of violence and discrimination, and need to be empowered in order to actualize their potential optimally;
c. that based on Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government as stated in the Appendix that the division of government affairs in the field of women's empowerment with one of the sub-affairs is the protection of women;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Empowerment and Protection of Women;

In View of:
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 29 of 1959 concerning the Establishment of Level II Regions in Sulawesi (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1959 Number 74, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 1822);
3. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended several times most recently by Law Number 6 of 2023 concerning the Stipulation of Government Regulation in Lieu of Law Number 2 of 2022 concerning Job Creation into Law (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2023 Number 41, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6856);
4. Law Number 12 of 2022 concerning the Criminal Act of Sexual Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2022 Number 120, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6792);

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this regional regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Mamuju Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Government of Mamuju Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Mamuju.
4. Regional Apparatus hereinafter abbreviated as PD is an auxiliary element of the regional head and DPRD in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the region.
5. Women's empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social, knowledge and skills capabilities so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.
6. Protection of Women is all efforts made by families, government and others aimed at women to ensure the fulfillment of women's rights as a form of respect for human rights.
7. Discrimination against women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex, which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women.
8. Violence Against Women is any action that violates, hinders, eliminates enjoyment, and neglects women's human rights.
9. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service center in the effort to Implement women in various fields of development, as well as protection of women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons, established by the government or community-based.
10. Integrated Service Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is technical service units in the region that carry out integrated service functions for women and children victims of crime and/or violence, including as an information center for the Implementation of women and child protection.
11. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles, functions and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural circumstances of society.
12. Gender Mainstreaming, hereinafter abbreviated as PUG, is a strategy built to integrate gender into men and women.
13. Regional Action Plan for Gender Mainstreaming hereinafter referred to as RANDA PUG is a planning document as a guide in efforts to implement development strategies by integrating gender into a unified integral dimension of planning, preparation, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of policies, programs, and development activities in the region.
14. PUG Institutions are institutions that meet the elements of PUG prerequisites, which function effectively in a sustainable system with agreed norms in fulfilling the human rights of women and men fairly to achieve equality between women and men in all fields of development and levels of government.
15. Community institutions are institutions formed by members of Indonesian citizen society voluntarily on the basis of shared vision, mission, profession, function, and activities to participate in development in order to achieve national goals within the framework of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia based on Pancasila, consisting of religious organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, private organizations, social organizations, political organizations, mass media, and other forms of organizations.
16. Victim is a person who experiences violence and/or threat of violence within the scope of the household.
17. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.
18. Task force is a coordinative institution formed by the Government of Mamuju Regency that coordinates policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children in Mamuju Regency.

# CHAPTER II

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 2

1. The Regional Government is obligated and responsible for the empowerment and Protection of Women.
2. Efforts for Empowerment and Protection of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;
b. determination of implementation guidelines;
c. provision of services; and
b. coordination of policies, programs and activities.
3. Efforts for Empowerment and Protection of Women as referred to in paragraph (2) are carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER III

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 3

1. Every woman has the right to:  
a. live and sustain life and improve her standard of living;  
b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, form a family and continue her lineage;  
c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life in order to become a faithful, pious, responsible, noble, happy, and prosperous human being in accordance with human rights;  
d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;  
e. be involved in every stage of the development process;  
f. be free from slavery or servitude;  
g. be free from threats;  
h. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;  
i. obtain welfare and a decent life;  
j. participate in the political, economic, socio-cultural fields;  
k. perform legal acts; and  
l. freely choose a partner in her marriage.  
2. Every wife during the marriage bond has equal rights and responsibilities with her husband over all matters relating to their married life, relations with their children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
3. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to children with due regard to the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER IV

## QUALITY OF LIFE OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 4

1. To improve the quality of life of women, the Regional Government is responsible for:  
a. respecting and guaranteeing women's rights without discrimination in accordance with human dignity;  
b. institutionalization of Gender Mainstreaming in Regional Government institutions;  
c. Women's Empowerment;  
d. strengthening and developing Women's Empowerment service provider institutions in the Region.  
2. Efforts to improve the quality of life of women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus that administer government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

## Part Two

Institutionalization of Gender Mainstreaming in Regional Government Institutions

#### Article 5

1. The Regent is responsible for the implementation of Gender Mainstreaming.  
2. The responsibility of the Regent as referred to in paragraph (1) may be delegated to the Vice Regent.

#### Article 6

The coordinator of Gender Mainstreaming implementation in the region is the Regional Apparatus that handles government affairs in the field of women's and children's empowerment.

#### Article 7

1. In an effort to accelerate the institutionalization of Gender Mainstreaming as referred to in Article 4 paragraph (1) letter b, a Gender Mainstreaming working group shall be formed.  
2. Members of the Gender Mainstreaming working group are all heads/leaders of Regional Apparatus.  
3. The Regent shall appoint:  
a. the head of the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of regional development planning as chairman of the Gender Mainstreaming working group; and  
b. the head of the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection as head of the secretariat of the Gender Mainstreaming working group.  
4. The formation of the Gender Mainstreaming working group as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 8

Provisions regarding Gender Mainstreaming as referred to in Article 6 through Article 8 shall be regulated by Regional Regulation.

## Part Three

Women's Empowerment

#### Article 9

Women's Empowerment as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter c is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:  
a. politics;  
b. law;  
c. socio-culture; and  
d. economy.

### Paragraph 1

Political Field

#### Article 10

1. Women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in Article 9 letter a includes:  
a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;  
b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;  
c. participation in general elections; and  
d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.  
2. Women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
3. The Regional Government provides guarantees to women to vote and/or be elected in:  
a. general elections;  
b. regional head elections;  
c. village head elections; and/or  
d. other political office elections.  
4. Equal rights as referred to in Paragraph (3) shall be implemented through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting according to applicable laws and regulations.  
5. The Regional Government provides opportunities for women to be appointed as regional government officials and occupy strategic positions in regional government; and  
6.  The Regional Government and/or Political Parties are responsible for providing political education for women.

### Paragraph 2

Legal Field

#### Article 11

1. Women's empowerment in the legal field as referred to in Article 9 letter b includes:  
a. increasing awareness and knowledge in the legal field through communication, information and education services;  
b. mapping and review of gender-biased policies; and  
c. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.  
2. Women's empowerment in the legal field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

### Paragraph 3

Social Field

#### Article 12

Women's empowerment in the social field shall be implemented through;  
a. increasing knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education according to potential to improve social status;  
b. increasing awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health;  
c. increasing awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and  
d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and develop science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

### Paragraph 4

Economic Field

#### Article 13

Women's empowerment in the economic field shall be implemented through:  
a. provision of skills and job training;  
b. ease in obtaining employment;  
c. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;  
d. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;  
e. facilitation and capital assistance;  
f. ease of access to information and technology; and  
g. facilitation of marketing network development.

## Part Four

Strengthening and Development of Women's Empowerment Service Provider Institutions

#### Article 14

1. Strengthening and development of women's empowerment service provider institutions shall be implemented by the Regional Apparatus that handles government affairs in the field of women's empowerment.  
2. Strengthening and development of women's empowerment service provider institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. socialization;  
b. coordination;  
c. communication, information and education;  
d. training; and  
e. increasing participation of community members.

# CHAPTER V

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

Prevention of Violence against Women

#### Article 15

Prevention of violence against women is the responsibility of:  
a. Regional Government;  
b. educational institutions;  
c. religious institutions;  
d. health service provider institutions;  
e. professional institutions;  
f. business sector;  
g. service institutions;  
h. Islamic boarding schools;  
i. youth organizations;  
j. legal aid organizations;  
k. communities; and  
l. media.

#### Article 16

1. Prevention of violence against women is intended to:  
a. create a safe environment for women; and  
b. reduce vulnerability to violence, exploitation and discrimination against vulnerable groups of women.  
2. Vulnerable groups of women as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b are as follows:  
a. women in disaster and social conflict situations;  
b. women with disabilities;  
c. women with HIV/AIDS;  
d. women migrant workers;  
e. girls;  
f. women heads of household;  
g. domestic workers;  
h. home-based workers;  
i. elderly women;  
j. informal sector workers;  
k. women in situations of intolerance; and  
l. other vulnerable groups of women.

#### Article 17

1. Prevention of violence against women is carried out in the following sectors:  
a. education;  
b. health;  
c. religion;  
d. security;  
e. justice;  
f. public works and spatial planning;  
g. public housing and settlement areas;  
h. social affairs;  
i. manpower;  
j. women's empowerment and child protection;  
k. land;  
l. environment;  
m. population administration and civil registration;  
n. population control and family planning;  
o. transportation;  
p. communication and informatics;  
q. cooperatives, small and medium enterprises;  
r. youth and sports;  
s. culture;  
t. tourism;  
u. trade and industry; and  
v. other related sectors.  
2. Prevention of violence against women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in the following forms:  
a. developing media for communication, information, education and public campaigns through media;  
b. developing educational materials and curricula;  
c. developing safe transportation systems and public spaces;  
d. establishing early detection systems, security and integrated complaint services in industrial areas, companies, residential areas, educational environments, Islamic boarding schools and other public spaces;  
e. providing consultation services for families, prospective brides and grooms, students, Islamic boarding school students, youth, university students and vulnerable groups;  
f. encouraging behavioral change of parents and the community;  
g. developing policies related to regencies/cities, villages, schools, Islamic boarding schools, and universities that are safe and friendly for women and integrated with regional spatial planning policies;  
h. establishing and developing cadres, communities, and groups from among youth, students, Islamic boarding school students, university students, journalists, social media influencers, educators, and religious leaders for prevention;  
i. conducting education and advocacy to owners, managers and/or users of social media;  
j. developing protection systems and special support for vulnerable groups of women;  
k. conducting awareness-raising for perpetrators;  
l. conducting cooperation between those responsible for prevention of violence against women; and  
m. conducting socialization of laws and regulations.  
3. The sectors and forms of prevention of violence against women as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) are the responsibility of Regional Apparatus that oversee related affairs.

#### Article 18

Prevention of violence against women is carried out in an integrated and coordinated manner between those responsible for prevention of violence against women and Regional Apparatus that oversee women's empowerment and child protection affairs.

#### Article 19

Further provisions regarding prevention of violence against women as referred to in Article 16, Article 17 and Article 18 shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Provision of Services for Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 20

1. In implementing protection services for women, the Regional Government establishes P2TP2A as a service network for women victims of violence.  
2. The establishment of P2TP2A is intended so that women victims of violence obtain comprehensive, integrated, quality, inclusive and sustainable services.  
3. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) has the following duties:  
a. providing services to women victims of violence in accordance with standard operating procedures;  
b. coordinating prevention, services and referrals between government-owned service institutions, community-owned service institutions, professional organizations, law enforcement agencies and other related institutions;  
c. conducting data collection on case services;  
d. providing various information needed by victims, victims' families and the community;  
e. preventing recurrence of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women;  
f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of victim services; and  
g. providing capacity strengthening, guidance and assistance related to victim services.

#### Article 21

P2TP2A as referred to in Article 20 consists of the following elements:  
a. related regional apparatus;  
b. government and/or private hospitals;  
c. police;  
d. prosecutor's office;  
e. courts;  
f. regional office of the ministry of law and human rights;  
g. regional office of the ministry of religious affairs;  
h. professional organizations;  
i. religious organizations;  
j. community social organizations;  
k. government-owned service institutions and community-owned service institutions;  
l. legal aid organizations;  
m. women's organizations;  
n. universities; and  
o. broadcasting media and/or mass media.

#### Article 22

Further provisions regarding the establishment and structure of P2TP2A as referred to in Article 20 and Article 21 shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 23

1. Services are implemented to fulfill the rights of women victims of violence and are carried out with the following principles:  
a. respect for human rights;  
b. non-discrimination;  
c. confidentiality;  
d. empathy;  
e. inclusion;  
f. empowerment;  
g. victim-based decisions;  
h. honesty;  
i. professionalism;  
j. integration; and  
k. sustainability.  
2. Services to women victims of violence must consider special conditions and needs based on the vulnerability experienced by women as referred to in Article 20.

#### Article 24

1. Forms of services as referred to in Article 23 include:  
a. complaint services;  
b. health services;  
c. social rehabilitation services;  
d. legal aid and enforcement services; and  
e. repatriation and social reintegration services.  
2. Services for women victims of violence are implemented quickly, free of charge, comprehensively, with quality and integrated with services provided by government agencies, the community, educational institutions, religious institutions, professional institutions, Islamic boarding schools, the business sector through an integrated service system.

#### Article 25

1. Complaint services as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter a consist of:  
a. receiving complaints;  
b. special complaints;  
c. providing information;  
d. outreach;  
e. crisis or emergency services;  
f. assessment; and  
g. other forms of complaint services needed by victims.  
2. Complaint services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by trained officers in special rooms that are safe and comfortable.  
3. Further provisions regarding complaint services shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 26

1. Health services as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter b shall be implemented with the following activities:
a. physical and mental health examination and care;
b. psychological examination and recovery;
c. pregnancy, childbirth, and reproductive health services;
d. medical resume and psychological examination results;
e. visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum;
f. Deoxyribo Nucleic Acid test; and
g. other forms of health services needed by victims.
2. Health services for female victims shall be carried out by trained officers in a special room that is safe and comfortable.
3. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out through a special mechanism of integrated services based on hospitals and shall be the responsibility of the Regional Apparatus in charge of health affairs.
4. Further provisions regarding health services shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 27

1. Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter c shall be implemented with the following activities:
a. counseling;
b. assistance;
c. linguist and/or translator;
d. spiritual guidance;
e. shelter and/or safe house;
f. temporary shelter;
g. strengthening based on family and/or group support;
h. mobility support for victims with disabilities;
i. provision of special social assistance for victims; and
j. other forms of social rehabilitation services needed by victims.
2. Social rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, letter b, letter c, letter d, letter f, letter g, letter h, and letter i shall be the responsibility of the Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment and child protection affairs.
3. Social rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e and letter j shall be the responsibility of the Regional Apparatus in charge of social affairs and women's empowerment and child protection affairs.
4. Further provisions regarding social rehabilitation services shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 28

1. Legal assistance and law enforcement services as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter d shall be implemented in an integrated manner with related parties through the following activities:
a. legal consultation;
b. legal empowerment;
c. mediation;
d. restorative justice;
e. legal aid;
f. expert witnesses;
g. restitution;
h. security protection; and
i. other forms of legal assistance and law enforcement services needed by victims.
2. In implementing the integration of legal assistance and law enforcement services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent shall be responsible for coordinating cooperation between:
a. regional apparatus;
b. service institutions;
c. legal aid organizations;
d.advocate professional organizations with law enforcement agencies; and
e. other related vertical agencies through the integration of the recovery system into the criminal justice system.
3. Further provisions regarding legal assistance and law enforcement services shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 29

1. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter e shall be implemented with the following activities:
a. repatriation and/or pick-up of victims;
b. provision of population documents or personal identity;
c. alternative family;
d. scholarships and educational support facilities;
e. non-formal education;
f. skills training and economic business;
g. financial assistance and business capital;
h. economic business assistance;
i. housing assistance;
j. food and/or nutrition assistance for victims and their families;
k. provision of social protection programs and poverty alleviation programs;
l. other forms of repatriation and social reintegration services needed by victims.
2. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided to victims, families and persons whose lives depend on victims with the aim that victims have the readiness and ability to live life in society.
3. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in an integrated manner with the Integrated Referral Service System for social protection and poverty alleviation and the Child Social Welfare Center.
4. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be the responsibility of the Regional Apparatus in charge of:
a. social affairs;
b. population administration and civil registration affairs;
c. labor affairs;
d. small and medium enterprise cooperative affairs;
e. education affairs;
f. health affairs;
g. public works and spatial planning affairs;
h. land affairs, women's empowerment and child protection affairs; and
i. other related affairs.
5. Further provisions regarding repatriation and social reintegration services shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF MAMUJU REGENCY

NUMBER 9 YEAR 2023 CONCERNING WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION

##### I. GENERAL ELUCIDATION

Empowerment and Protection of women currently needs to receive serious attention from all parties, including attention from the Regional Government, related to the existence of phenomena in social community life that often make them victims of violence and exploitation, they are often positioned as economic objects and are often marginalized and/or sidelined in social interaction.  

Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence (UU KDRT) stipulates that the state guarantees to prevent the occurrence of domestic violence, prosecute perpetrators of domestic violence, and protect victims of domestic violence. Victims of domestic violence, who are mostly women and children, must receive protection in order to avoid and be free from violence or threats of violence, torture, or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth. Therefore, the Regional Government makes regulations related to Women's Empowerment and Protection.
