BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF PULANG PISAU,

Considering:

a. that protection of women and children is an integral part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and respected by the state;
b. that violence against women and children in the Region tends to increase, therefore the Regional Government and/or community need to play an active role optimally to provide protection, so that women and children are prevented and freed from violence or threats of violence, torture, or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor;
c. that protection of women and children is a mandatory concurrent affair that is the authority, obligation, and responsibility of the Regional Government, therefore regulation is needed as a basis for implementing comprehensive and integrated protection for women and children;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Pulang Pisau Regency.
2. Regional Government is the regional head as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Pulang Pisau.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution that is positioned as an element of regional government administration.
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regional head and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
6. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
7. Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as PPA is a handling effort to protect and fulfill the rights of women and children from all forms of violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.
8. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.
9. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, economic exploitation, social, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
10. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, economic exploitation, social, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.
11. Domestic violence is any act against a person, especially women, that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically and or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts of coercion or unlawful deprivation of freedom within the scope of the household.
12. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.
13. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
14. Economic violence is any act that deliberately neglects family members in the form of not providing a decent living care or maintenance.
15. Psychological violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
16. Trafficking in persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, fraud, deception, abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payments or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in a person being exploited.
17. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion that is direct or indirect based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that results in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural fields and other aspects of life.
18. Maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity is an action or attitude that is not in accordance with applicable moral, ethical, or legal norms covering various actions that harm others, whether physically, emotionally, or psychologically.
19. Exploitation is an action with or without the victim's consent that includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.
20. Sexual Exploitation is any form of utilization of sexual body organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution and obscenity.
21. Threat of violence is any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without the use of means that cause fear or restrict the fundamental freedom of a person.
22. Victim is a woman and child who experiences and/or suffers either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
23. Rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological, and social conditions in order to be able to carry out their role again properly both in the family and in society.
24. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children, abbreviated as UPTD-PPA is an Integrated Service Center established by the Regional Government to provide services to women and children victims of violence comprehensively including information services, assistance and legal aid, counseling services, medical services and safe houses through referrals.
25. Task Force for the Prevention and Handling of Violence Against Women and Children, hereinafter referred to as the Task Force is a coordinative institution tasked with coordinating prevention and handling efforts of criminal acts of trafficking in persons in Pulang Pisau Regency.
26. Companion is a person who has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
27. Optimal Service is a dynamic effort consisting of various parts that are regularly related, followed by performance offered by one party to another party by providing benefits, in order to achieve the best goal.

Article 2

The implementation of PPA is carried out based on the principles of:
a. humanity;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. protection;
d. best interests of women and children; and
e. non-discrimination.

Article 3

The regulation of the principles of PPA implementation aims to:
a. prevent acts of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity of women and children;
b. eliminate all forms of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity of women and children;
c. protect, provide a sense of security for women and children;
d. provide services to women and children victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses; and
e. facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household.

CHAPTER II

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Article 4

Women and children who are victims of acts of violence, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor shall receive the following rights:
a. Right to guarantee space for community participation in government, including in decision-making/policy-making;
b. Right to population documents and civil registration;
c. Right to Legal Aid;
d. Right to be respected for dignity and honor as human beings;
e. Right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by victims;
f. Right to determine their own decisions;
g. Right to obtain information;
h. Right to confidentiality;
i. Right to compensation;
j. Right to social rehabilitation;
k. Right to complaint handling;
l. Right of victims and their families to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and
m. Right to assistance.

Article 5

Child victims of acts of violence, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 4, shall also receive special rights, as follows:
a. Right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;
b. Right to basic services;
c. Right to equal protection;
d. Right to be free from various stigma;
e. Right to employment;
f. Right to decent housing and environment;
g. Right to Women's empowerment; and
h. Right to violence prevention.

CHAPTER III

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 6

Obligations and responsibilities in the implementation of protection for women and children are the joint responsibility of:
a. regional government;
b. community;
c. family;
d. parents; and
e. non-governmental institutions including Customary Institutions and Business Entities.

Article 7

The obligations and responsibilities of regional government as referred to in Article 6 letter a, include:
a. implementing protection policies for women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor established by the government;
b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor;
c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor;
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor;
e. allocating budget for the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor for women and children according to regional financial capacity; and
f. fostering and supervising the implementation and protection of women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor.

Article 8

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letter b shall be implemented in the form of community participation. (2) The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor against women and children;
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
c. participating in providing assistance and/or handling of victims of acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor against women and children.
2. The form of participation shall be implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 9

The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

Article 10

1. The obligations and responsibilities of Customary Institutions in the implementation of protection for women and children as referred to in Article 6 letter e, include:
a. customary institutions have the responsibility to safeguard the dignity and human rights of women and children in accordance with highly upheld humanitarian values;
b. being responsible for preventing all forms of violence, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and honor of women and children within the customary environment;
c. ensuring gender equality and children's rights are respected, without discrimination based on gender or age;
d. being responsible for raising awareness among customary communities regarding the rights of women and children, including awareness of the importance of protection and respect for their rights;
e. organizing counseling and training programs for members of customary communities, especially customary leaders, on the rights of women and children and effective protection methods;
f. providing psychosocial and legal support to victims of violence, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and honor of women and children, including facilitating victims' access to protection and legal institutions;
g. playing an active role in the reporting process and law enforcement regarding cases of violence, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and honor of women and children in customary communities;
h. facilitating the establishment of PPA institutions at the customary level tasked with protecting their rights and providing assistance and support to victims;
i. integrating customary values that support equality and PPA into community counseling and education programs; and
j. building partnerships with non-governmental organizations, protection institutions, and educational institutions to strengthen PPA efforts.
2. The obligations and responsibilities of Business Entities in the implementation of protection for women and children as referred to in Article 6 letter e, include:
a. being obligated to comply with all laws, regulations, and rules related to PPA at the national and regional levels;
b. providing a safe and supportive work environment, free from harassment, discrimination, violence, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and honor of women and children;
c. providing shelters for female workers at the workplace;
d. forming partnerships with regional governments in PPA;
e. providing empowerment for women and children as a shared responsibility with the regional government;
f. conducting counseling programs in communities around the company to raise awareness about PPA;
g. creating policies, programs, and PPA activities;
h. supporting social programs that enhance women's economic independence and support children's education;
i. supporting the establishment of protection centers and services for victims of violence against women and children; and
j. supporting PPA programs in Pulang Pisau Regency through Corporate Social Responsibility instruments and/or assistance in other forms in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

CHAPTER IV

DUTIES OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

Article 11

The Regional Government is tasked with carrying out prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against Women and Children, by:
a. collecting data and information about Women and Children Victims of acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor;
b. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to PPA;
c. providing education on anti-violence values in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor against women and children;
d. preventing child marriage;
e. providing rehabilitation facilities; and
f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of PPA against acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor.

CHAPTER V

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Article 12

1. The implementation of protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the UPTD-PPA framework.
2. Provisions regarding UPTD-PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be further regulated in a Regent Regulation.
3. In addition to establishing UPTD-PPA, to support the implementation of protection for women and children, the Regent establishes:
a. a task force on violence against women and children;
b. a regional action committee for the elimination of the worst forms of child labor.

Article 13

1. The task force as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (3) letter a, consists of representatives from the Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, and researchers/academics.
2. The task force as referred to in Article 11 paragraph (3) letter a, is a coordinative institution tasked with coordinating the prevention and handling of acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor against women and children.

Article 14

1. The committee as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (3) letter b, consists of representatives from the Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, and researchers/academics.
2. Further provisions regarding the duties and functions of the committee shall be stipulated by Regent's decree.

Article 15

The implementation of services for victims is carried out with the principles of:
a. no fees charged;
b. fast;
c. safe;
d. empathy;
e. non-discrimination;
f. easily accessible; and
g. confidentiality.

CHAPTER VI

COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

Article 16

1. In order to achieve the objectives of implementing PPA as referred to in Article 3, the Regional Government cooperates with:
a. the Provincial government;
b. other Regency/City governments;
c. the Police;
d. the Prosecutor's Office;
e. the Court; and/orf. non-governmental institutions.
2. The cooperation referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. exchange of data and information;
b. rehabilitation of victims of violence;
c. repatriation and social reintegration;
d. provision of evidence and witnesses;
e. follow-up in accordance with applicable law; and
f. Prevention efforts to prevent the occurrence of criminal acts against Women and children.
3. The cooperation referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be set forth in the form of a joint agreement.

Article 17

1. The regional government establishes partnerships with the business sector in the implementation of PPA.
2. The partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through:
a. notification of employment opportunity information for women victims of violence;
b. education and training for women victims of violence;
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of violence who have been withdrawn from their education; and
d. fostering and enhancing the economic independence of women victims of violence.
3. The partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be set forth in the form of an agreement.

CHAPTER VII

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 18

1. The Regional Government is obligated to provide guidance and supervision for PPA from all forms of violence, discrimination, special protection and other issues.
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. guidelines and standards for fulfilling needs;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. provision of facilities;
d. monitoring; and
e. evaluation.
3. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2), is in order to realize the objectives of PPA in accordance with minimum service standards.

Article 19

The community may conduct supervision of the implementation of PPA from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor organized by the Regional Government through a mechanism of conveying aspirations to the Regent or to the DPRD.

CHAPTER VIII

ASSISTANCE

Article 20

Assistance is carried out by persons or institutions that have expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and have cooperated with UPTD-PPA.

CHAPTER IX

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 21

1. The community participates in PPA both individually and in groups.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in the form of:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against Women and Children;
b. submitting reports to the authorities if acts of violence against Women and Children occur; and
c. providing assistance to victims.

Article 22

1. Social community organizations that participate in the implementation of protection for victims as referred to in article 21 paragraph 2, are provided guidance by the Regional Government.
2. Guidance may be in the form of social guidance, skills guidance and operational technical guidance.

CHAPTER X

FUNDING

Article 23

Funding for the implementation of PPA is sourced from:

1. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget;
2. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
3. other lawful and non-binding sources of funds in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 24

1. The Regional Government may provide funding assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations that implement PPA from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor.
2. Funding assistance, in accordance with regional financial capacity, and is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

CHAPTER XI

REPORTING

Article 25

1. UPTD-PPA is obligated to report in writing the implementation of PPA Implementation to the Regent.
2. The report as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. administration;
b. finance;
c. services; and
d. performance.
3. Submission of reports as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) is carried out at least once every 3 (three) months.

CHAPTER XII

SANCTION PROVISIONS

Article 26

1. Any person who intentionally does not provide protection to women and children resulting in the occurrence of violence, allows violence to occur, and/or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as regulated in the applicable laws and regulations.
2. Officials appointed to organize protection who do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities shall be subject to administrative action or sanctions.
3. UPTD-PPA managers who carry out service duties, if they violate service principles, may be subject to sanctions in accordance with the internal mechanisms of UPTD-PPA.

CHAPTER XIII

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 27

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Pulang Pisau Regency.

NUMBER 6 OF 2024 CONCERNING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Pulang Pisau Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children in the form of regional regulations.

So far, laws and regulations governing PPA from acts of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, so institutional and regulatory support is needed that can guarantee its implementation.

The Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for women and children, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children from acts of violence in Pulang Pisau Regency.
