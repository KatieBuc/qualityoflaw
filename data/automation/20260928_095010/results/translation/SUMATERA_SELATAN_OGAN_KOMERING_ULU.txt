BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

THE REGENT OF OGAN KOMERING ULU

Considering:

a. that all forms of violence, especially gender-based violence and violence against children are violations of human rights and crimes against human dignity as well as forms of discrimination;
b. that victims of gender-based violence and violence against children must receive protection, both from the regional government and/or the community in order to be protected and freed from violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community;
c. that based on Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection and Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, the Regional Government together with the community is obligated to undertake prevention, protection, and recovery efforts for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters b and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation of Ogan Komering Ulu Regency concerning the Implementation of Protection for Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children.

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Central Government hereinafter referred to as Government is the President of the Republic of Indonesia who holds the governmental power of the State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
2. Region is the Regency of Ogan Komering Ulu.
3. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
4. Regent is the Regent of Ogan Komering Ulu.
5. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb as referred to in Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.
6. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
7. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
8. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
9. Implementation of protection is a series of activities aimed at preventing the occurrence of violence, providing protection and recovery services and social reintegration, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation carried out by the Commission for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children, and the Integrated Service Center.
10. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
11. Violence Against Children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
12. Victim of Gender-Based Violence is a person who due to their gender experiences physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
13. Child Victim of Violence is a child who receives harsh treatment either physically, psychologically, economically, socially, sexually, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
14. Victim Recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, whether physically, psychologically, socially, economically or sexually.
15. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the purpose of self-strengthening of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems they face.
16. Commission for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children hereinafter referred to as KPK2BGA is the Commission for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children of Ogan Komering Ulu Regency established by the Regent which is a non-structural commission.
17. Integrated Service is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unit of implementation, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
18. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes who can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.
19. Safe House (shelter) is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:
a. non-discrimination;
b. best interests of victims;
c. justice and gender equality;
d. victim protection;
e. human survival;
f. survival, growth, and development of children;
g. respect for the opinion of children;
h. openness;
i. integration;
j. not blaming victims;
k. empowering;
l. victim confidentiality; and
m. decision-making in the hands of victims.

Article 3

The objectives of protection of victims of gender-based violence and children are:
a. to prevent all forms of gender-based violence and violence against children, occurring within the scope of the household and/or community;
b. to provide protection;
c. to provide legal assistance;
d. to seek recovery and social reintegration; and
e. to increase community participation.

CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 4

1. Every victim of gender-based violence and child victim of violence has the right to:
a. obtain protection for the security of themselves, their family, and their property, and be free from threats related to information that will be, is being, or has been given;
b. participate in the process of selecting and determining the form of protection and security support;
c. be free from ensnaring questions;
d. obtain information regarding case developments and court decisions;
e. receive services that are fast, appropriate, comfortable, and according to needs;
f. recovery and social reintegration;
g. obtain legal, psychological, spiritual, economic, social assistance and interpreters.
2. The rights of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

CHAPTER IV

OBLIGATIONS OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

Article 5

1. The obligations of the Regional Government include:
a. preventing the occurrence of violence;
b. providing protection for victims of violence;
c. providing recovery services and social reintegration;
d. encouraging and increasing community participation;
e. conducting cooperation with service providers in efforts to prevent, protect and recover victims of violence.
2. To carry out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government has the authority to:
a. formulate policies and programs on the elimination of gender-based violence and children;
b. establish integrated services and KPK2BGA;
c. facilitate the implementation of integrated services and KPK2BGA activities;
d. provide facilities and infrastructure;
e. increase the capacity of service provider institutions;
f. conduct coordination and cooperation in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children;
g. encourage community participation;
h. conduct monitoring and evaluation.
3. The regional government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives or other persons who are legally responsible for victims.

CHAPTER V

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Part One

Institutions

Article 6

In providing protection to victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence, the Regional Government is assisted by:
a. Integrated Services; and
b. KPK2BGA.

Part Two

Integrated Services

Article 7

1. Integrated services as referred to in Article 6 letter a are established by the Regional Government in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
2. The establishment of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of members from regional government elements, non-governmental organizations, health service institutions, law enforcement apparatus, professional personnel, volunteer companions, social workers, religious leaders, safe houses (shelters), and social rehabilitation centers.

Article 8

The duties of integrated services as referred to in Article 7 are to pursue prevention, recovery and social reintegration, provide legal protection, conduct coordination and, pursue increased community participation, as well as monitoring and reporting.

Paragraph 1

Prevention Efforts

Article 9

Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 8 include:
a. conducting communication, information, and education about gender-based violence and children;
b. socialization of laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and children.

Paragraph 2

Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts

Article 10

Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts as referred to in Article 8, include;
a. providing physical recovery at health service institutions;
b. providing medicolegal services;
c. assisting victim repatriation;
d. providing temporary protection in safe houses (shelters);
e. providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;
f. providing spiritual guidance services;
g. conducting family and community preparation, economic empowerment, and return to school and or other educational institutions.

Paragraph 3

Legal Protection

Article 11

Legal protection as referred to in Article 8 includes:
a. providing protection in safe houses (shelters);
b. providing assistance in legal proceedings at the high court level; and
c. providing special legal protection for child victims of violence which may be carried out through the appointment of guardianship in accordance with applicable regulations.

Paragraph 4

Coordination and Cooperation

Article 12

Coordination and cooperation as referred to in Article 8, include:
a. conducting coordination and cooperation in handling violence cases with district/city integrated services;
b. conducting coordination and cooperation with service provider institutions for victims of gender-based violence and children;
c. conducting coordination and consultation with the Regional Government.

Paragraph 5

Efforts to Increase Community Participation

Article 13

Increasing community participation as referred to in Article 8 is carried out by:
a. gender and children;
b. encouraging the community to actively participate in providing information and reporting gender-based violence and children;
c. fostering local wisdom in handling gender-based violence and children;
d. conducting strengthening of community groups in handling gender-based violence and children; and
e. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and children.

Paragarf 6 Monitoring and Reporting

Article 14

Monitoring and reporting as referred to in Article 8 include monitoring, documentation and reporting of cases of gender-based violence and children.

Article 15

The implementation of integrated services is coordinated by the Regional Work Unit responsible for women's empowerment and child protection.

Part Three

KPK2BGA

Article 16

1. KPK2BGA as referred to in Article 6 letter b, is established by the Regional Government in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
2. The establishment of KPK2BGA as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of members from SKPD responsible for Health affairs, SKPD responsible for Social and Labor affairs, Police, community leaders, religious leaders, non-governmental organization activists, academics, and practitioners.

Article 17

The duties of KPK2BGA as referred to in Article 16 are:
a. conducting mediation of disputes between integrated service provider institutions for gender-based violence and children;
b. conducting advocacy for policies and programs for the protection of victims of gender-based violence and children;
c. conducting supervision of ongoing case handling processes; and
d. conducting monitoring and supervision of the implementation of integrated services for victims of gender-based violence and children.

Article 18

To carry out the duties as referred to in Article 17, KPK2BGA has the following functions:
a. preparing materials for policy formulation and programs for the protection of victims of gender-based violence and children;
b. coordinating disputes between integrated service provider institutions for victims of gender-based violence and children;
c. developing protection systems for victims of gender-based violence and children; and
d. monitoring, supervising and reporting on the protection of victims of gender-based violence and children.

Article 19

The implementation of KPK2BGA is facilitated by the Regional Work Unit responsible for women's empowerment and child protection.

Article 20

The procedures, requirements and establishment of Integrated Services and KPK2BGA are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Part Four

Cooperation

Article 21

1. In providing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children, the regional government may cooperate with the Government, other Regional Governments.
2. Cooperation between the Regional Government and the Government includes consultation, coordination and reporting.
3. Cooperation between the Regional Government and other Regional Governments includes coordination, advocacy, referral, repatriation, social reintegration and development of integrated service systems.

Article 22

The implementation of cooperation as referred to in Article 21 is in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 23

1. The community may participate in assisting prevention efforts and handling of victims of gender-based violence and children.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is realized by:
a. providing information and or reporting any violence that is known;
b. providing protection for victims;
c. providing emergency assistance;
d. providing advocacy to victims and or the community regarding the handling of cases of gender-based violence and children;
e. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection orders; and
f. assisting in the process of repatriation and social reintegration.

CHAPTER VII

PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 24

1. To prevent acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government conducts socialization, empowerment, and awareness to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government undertakes the following efforts:
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
b. opening employment opportunities for women;
c. building community participation and concern for the prevention of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
d. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children.

CHAPTER VIII

CONTROL, GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 25

1. Control, guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall be carried out by the Regent, the implementation of which shall be carried out by the Vice Regent.
2. The implementation of control, guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out on a daily basis by the SKPD responsible for women's empowerment and child protection affairs.

CHAPTER IX

FINANCING

Article 26

All activities related to the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall be charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of the Regency.

CHAPTER X

REPORTING

Article 27

1. The Head of the SKPD responsible for women's empowerment and child protection affairs shall report periodically to the Regent regarding the implementation of this Regional Regulation.
2. The reporting procedure as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER XI

TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Article 28

1. At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all implementing regulations related to the protection of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to and/or have not been replaced by new regulations.
2. The Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) of Ogan Komering Ulu Regency that has been established prior to the enactment of this Regional Regulation shall remain recognized for its existence.

CHAPTER XII

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 29

Matters not yet regulated in this Regional Regulation insofar as concerning its implementation shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 30

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Ogan Komering Ulu Regency.

I. GENERAL

Gender-based violence and violence against children is a social phenomenon that has existed since ancient times and has become increasingly prevalent recently. In fact, gender-based violence and violence against children continues to increase, both in number and in the forms and modus operandi which are increasingly diverse. Rape, sexual harassment, trafficking of women and children, domestic violence, sexual exploitation, violence against domestic workers, pornography, exploitation of migrant workers, and neglect, will apparently continue to be encountered in daily life, both in the domestic (household) and public spheres.

The causal factors of gender-based violence and violence against children are very complex and interrelated with one another. These factors include, among others, legal instruments that have not been able to provide protection to victims, the concept that women and children are family property (assets), media that do not support reporting on violence against women and children, public services that are not yet optimal, customs that sometimes legalize violence, poverty issues, erroneous interpretations of religious teachings, all of which are wrapped in a patriarchal culture.

The handling of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children occurring in Ogan Komering Ulu Regency, in accordance with the provisions of Article 13 of Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, states that Regional Governments are obligated to establish and develop a system and mechanism of cooperation for handling violence. Furthermore, based on the provisions of Article 26 paragraph (1) letter b of Law Number 32 of 2004 concerning Regional Government, it states that the Deputy Regional Head has the duty to assist the Regional Head in coordinating the activities of vertical agencies in the region, following up on reports and/or findings from supervisory apparatus oversight, implementing women's and youth empowerment, as well as seeking the development and preservation of social culture and the environment. Therefore, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children.

II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

Article 2

Point a "Non-discrimination" means protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinction as to ethnicity, religion, race, group, sex, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.

Point b "Best interests of the victim" means that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Point c "Gender justice" means fair treatment given to both women and men.

"Gender equality" means conditions and positions that describe harmonious, appropriate and balanced relations between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in access, participation, control and benefits in the implementation of development as well as enjoying the results of development in family life, as well as in community, national and state life.

Point d "Victim protection" means providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

Point e "Maternal survival" means ensuring that a mother does not experience death that occurs during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum, whether caused by physical or non-physical conditions.

Point f "Growth and development" of children means as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.

"Survival" of children means as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity and the right to enjoy the highest attainable health status.

Point g "Respect for the child's opinion" means respect for children's rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.

Point h "Transparency" means that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is transparent among integrated service providers and KPK2BGA.

Point i "Integration" means that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is carried out by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal advocates, psychologists, religious leaders, social workers, police.

Point j "Not blaming the victim" means an attitude and treatment of not blaming the victim for the occurrence of violence experienced.

Point k "Empowering" means every effort given must be able to strengthen the victim, both physically, psychologically, socially and economically.

Point l "Victim confidentiality" means every action taken to ensure the victim is in a safe condition from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychology of the victim.

Point m "Decision-making in the hands of the victim" means the victim's right to determine the best choice in resolving their problem.
