a. that every citizen has equal rights to live a dignified life in accordance with the principles of humanity, equality, and justice;
b. that women's empowerment is carried out so that women can actualize their potential optimally to participate in development according to their capacity;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Sampang Regency.
2. Regional Government is the administration of government affairs by the Regional Government and DPRD according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
3. Regent is the Regent of Sampang.
4. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of Sampang Regency.
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regional Head and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of Government Affairs which are the authority of the Region.
6. Family Planning Office for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection hereinafter referred to as DKBP3A is a Regional Apparatus Organization that has the main duties and functions in the field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in Sampang Regency.
7. Child is a person who is not yet 18 years old, including a child who is still in the womb.
8. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court decisions;
9. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights, so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination;
10. Violence is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.
11. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.
12. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, mentally, socially, psychosocially, and sexually.
13. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or defects to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.
14. Psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
15. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
16. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment, or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
17. Victim is a woman including Female Workers who work abroad and children who experience misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of such violence.
18. Women's Empowerment is any effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social capacity, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.
19. Gender equality and justice is equal opportunity between men and women to participate in the development process, equal access to all forms of services, and have a balanced social and economic status.
20. Integrated service is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of violence including criminal acts of trafficking in persons carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as a unified implementation of health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance;
21. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service institution for victims of violence against women and children;
22. Protection of women is any act aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will, is or has occurred.
23. Women's empowerment and child protection system is the entire process of organizing women's empowerment and child protection carried out comprehensively, inclusively, integratively starting from the stage of report/complaint handling services, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance up to repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of crime and violence to their social environment;
24. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
25. Legal act is any act or action of a legal subject that has legal consequences and the legal consequences are indeed desired by the legal subject.
26. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred;
27. Companion is a person who has the expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
28. Social worker is a person who has the expertise to listen empathically and explore problems to provide counseling in an effort to strengthen the victim psychologically.
29. Medicolegal is an effort to collect evidence for the purposes of proof in the judicial process.
30. Women's Crisis Center is a service center for women and children victims of violence.
31. Safe House is a temporary residence for victims, in order to obtain a sense of security and not be reached by the perpetrator or the perpetrator's messenger, while the case has not been resolved.
32. Standard Operational Procedure hereinafter referred to as SOP is a procedure that serves as a reference for service actions established by Regent's Decree.
33. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

The principles in Women's empowerment and child protection are:
a. Non-discrimination;
b. Best interests of the child;
c. Right to life, survival and development;
d. Respect for the views of the child;
e. Gender equality;
f. Gender justice;
g. Legal certainty.

Article 3

The Women's Empowerment and Child Protection System aims to:
a. improve the quality of life of women, children, and the quality of families;
b. enhance the institutional capacity of women's empowerment and child protection in the regions, including the development of gender and child data systems; and
c. provide protection of women's rights and fulfillment of children's rights including special protection for children from various forms of violence and other discriminatory treatment.

Article 4

In order to realize the objectives as referred to in Article 3 letter a, efforts are made through:
a. providing access for women and children to education, health and other strategic services;
b. encouraging the involvement of women and children in the development process;
c. providing knowledge, skills, character values, morals and family resilience; and
d. encouraging programs that can increase women's independence in the economic, political, legal, social, cultural and other strategic fields.

Article 5

In order to realize the objectives as referred to in Article 3 letter b, efforts are made through:
a. establishment, development, and strengthening the capacity of women and child protection institutions including complaint service units for violence against women and children, as well as legal aid services;
b. improving the quality of human resources of managers;
c. strengthening the institutional capacity of Gender Mainstreaming (PUG) and children;
d. strengthening and developing gender and child data systems.

Article 6

In order to implement the objectives as referred to in Article 3 letter c, the Regional Government carries out promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts in accordance with statutory provisions.

Article 7

Promotive efforts as referred to in Article 6 are as follows:
a. strengthening coordination mechanisms and networks between service units in efforts to handle cases of violence;
b. providing Communication, Information, and Education (KIE) materials related to the prevention and handling of violence;
c. conducting socialization, advocacy and social campaigns in the context of preventing and handling violence.

Article 8

Preventive efforts as referred to in Article 6 are as follows:
a. conducting legal awareness counseling for the community, especially for women and children;
b. conducting massive and sustainable movements involving the community in actions to prevent and handle violence;
c. instilling character values, morals, and family resilience;
d. involving the role and participation of the community in women's empowerment and child protection.

Article 9

Curative efforts as referred to in Article 6 are as follows:
a. optimizing technical services related to complaints of violence against women and children;
b. providing adequate facilities and infrastructure for handling health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal aid;
c. handling victims of crime and violence quickly, precisely, and accurately by law enforcement officers.

Article 10

Rehabilitative efforts as referred to in Article 6 are as follows:
a. providing companion personnel for victims of crime and violence which include among others psychologists, psychiatrists, clergy/spiritual companions, lawyers, medical personnel;
b. strengthening work networks and coordination in the reintegration process and repatriation of victims to their families and/or social environment.

CHAPTER III

WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND CHILD PROTECTION SYSTEM

Article 11

The Regional Government assisted by regional apparatus must have a strong commitment to build a comprehensive, inclusive and integrative women's empowerment and child protection system.

Article 12

1. The Regional Government, community, non-governmental organizations, or other institutions establish technical service units in realizing an integrated, one-stop and networked women's empowerment and child protection system;
2. The technical service unit as referred to in paragraph (1) is P2TP2A.

CHAPTER IV

P2TP2A

Article 13

1. P2TP2A is located in the Region and sub-district;
2. Provisions regarding the Establishment of P2TP2A are further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

Article 14

P2TP2A is under the coordination of DKBP3A or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection.

Article 15

P2TP2A is one form of integrated service unit that functions as:
a. an information center for women and children;
b. a service center for women and children victims of violence; and
c. an empowerment center for women and children.

Article 16

P2TP2A institutional structure:
a. is established based on a Regent/Sub-district Head decree;
b. its membership comes from structural and non-structural elements originating from professionals, academics, community leaders; and
c. the source of funding for the establishment, development, and strengthening of P2TP2A is sourced from the APBD and other legitimate and non-binding sources based on statutory provisions.

Article 17

Provisions regarding P2TP2A are further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER V

COORDINATION MECHANISM

Article 18

In carrying out the functions as referred to in Article 15, coordination is carried out with Regional Apparatus or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection.

Article 19

P2TP2A may consult and coordinate with the Ministry that administers women's empowerment and child protection affairs by first coordinating with Regional Apparatus or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection.

Article 20

Coordination can be carried out in the form of:
a. coordination meetings with work networks;
b. consultation;
c. submission of data and information; and/or
d. follow-up on case handling.

CHAPTER VI

PARTICIPATION

Article 21

The community, business world, academics, and other professional groups may participate in the implementation of the women's empowerment and child protection system.

CHAPTER VII

MONITORING, EVALUATION, AND REPORTING

Article 22

Reporting on women's empowerment and child protection is carried out 1 (one) time per year and is coordinated by DKBP3A with the province to the Minister who administers government affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection through the Ministry Secretary.

CHAPTER VIII

FUNDING

Article 23

Funding for the implementation of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection is sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBD);
b. other legitimate and non-binding sources based on statutory provisions.

CHAPTER IX

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 24

Implementing Regulations of this Regional Regulation shall be established no later than 6 (six) months from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

Article 25

This Regional Regulation comes into force from the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Sampang Regency.

1. GENERAL

The Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia mandates local governments to regulate and manage their own regions in order to realize the welfare of the community through improved services, empowerment and community participation. One of the authorities of local government is to make regional policies with the aim of improving the welfare of the people in their regions.

In addition, the constitution of the Republic of Indonesia has also mandated in Article 28D Paragraph 1 of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia that every person has the right to recognition, guarantee, protection, and fair legal certainty as well as equal treatment before the law. Article 28B Paragraph 2 also states that every child has the right to survival, growth, and development and has the right to protection from violence and discrimination. This shows that children and women should be protected by the state, especially against violence because their rights are already regulated in the constitution.

Therefore, it is necessary to make efforts to prevent and eradicate all forms of discrimination and violence against women and children, especially for the Sampang Regency area. The Government of Sampang Regency is one of the most authorized parties in carrying out women's empowerment and child protection by implementing and formulating regulations in order to provide guarantees for women's empowerment and child protection.

One form of such regulation is the Regional Regulation of Sampang Regency. The regulation is expected to be able to encourage efforts to improve the physical and mental condition of women and children victims of violence.
