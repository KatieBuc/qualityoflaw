PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF PASANGKAYU,

Considering:
a. that women and children are creations of God Almighty who constitute important elements of Indonesian society and Nation;
b. that in social life women and children often experience violence or unpleasant treatment;
c. that all forms of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children.

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Pasangkayu Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Pasangkayu.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and DPRD in the administration of Governmental Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person who is recognized by law as a Woman.
6. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including a Child in the womb.
7. Protection is all service actions to fulfill rights, as well as guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence.

LEGAL DOCUMENTATION AND INFORMATION NETWORK OF PASANGKAYU REGENCY

8. Protection of Women is an effort aimed at protecting Women and their rights in order to obtain a sense of security from acts of violence.
9. Protection of Children is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and honor, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
10. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.
11. Victim of acts of violence hereinafter referred to as victim is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.
12. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
13. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related agencies and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of protection of Women and Children Victims of acts of Violence.
14. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

CHAPTER II

OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

Article 2

The objectives of protection of Women and Children are to:

b. help prevent acts of Violence against Women and Children;
c. help prevent exploitation activities against Women and Children. d. provide Services to Women and Children in facing legal problems; ande. help improve the quality of life of Women and Children.

CHAPTER III

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 4

Forms of violence against Women and Children are:

a. Physical violence;
b. Psychological violence;
c. Sexual violence;
d. neglect; and/or
e. exploitation.

LEGAL DOCUMENTATION AND INFORMATION NETWORK OF PASANGKAYU REGENCY

Article 5

Physical violence as referred to in Article 4 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Article 6

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 4 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 7

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 4 letter c is in the form of:

a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse; and/or
c. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Article 8

Neglect as referred to in Article 4 letter d is caused by:

a. acts that result in the non-fulfillment of the needs of Children in a proper manner, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of Children as they should be, committed by parents, guardians or other parties responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the Victim is under the control of that person.

LEGAL DOCUMENTATION AND INFORMATION NETWORK OF PASANGKAYU REGENCY

Article 9

Exploitation as referred to in Article 4 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the consent of the Victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or Services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by other parties to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/or
c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other organs of the Victim to obtain benefits not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

CHAPTER IV

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Part One

Rights of Women

Article 10

1. The Regional Government is obliged to protect the rights of Women in the Region.
2. The rights of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of the right to:
a. basic needs;
b. comfort and security;
c. maximum health Services in facing acts of Violence;
d. assistance services, legal aid and spiritual guidance in facing acts of Violence; and
e. confidentiality regarding the problems faced.

Part Two

Rights of Children

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU

Article 11

1. The Regional Government is obliged to protect the rights of Children in the Region.
2. The rights of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of the right to:
a. live, grow, develop and participate reasonably in accordance with human dignity and receive guidance and Protection from Violence and discrimination;
b. worship according to their religion, think and express themselves in accordance with their level of intelligence and age under parental guidance;
c. a birth certificate obtained and/or procured by their parents in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations;
d. obtain protection from family, community and Regional Government against discriminatory treatment and exploitation both economically and socially, neglect, cruelty, violence, abuse, injustice and other mistreatment;
e. regain formal or non-formal education for Children who drop out of school;
f. obtain health Services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual and social needs;
g. obtain education and teaching according to their abilities, interests and talents; and
h. obtain other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

DUTIES AND AUTHORITIES

Part One

Duties

Paragraph 1

Duties of Regional Government in the Protection of Women

Article 12

1. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out the Protection of Women in the Region.
2. Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented through the preparation of a strategic plan

for the Protection of Women for the long term, medium term, and short term.

3. The preparation of the strategy as referred to in paragraph (2) must be integrated with the Regional development planning document.
4. The Regional Government in preparing the strategic plan as referred to in paragraph (3) may involve the community, social institutions and/or other institutions that carry out activities related to the Protection of Women.

Article 13

1. The Regional Government is obliged to prevent acts of Violence against Women.
2. Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in the form of activities:
a. Regional Action Plan;
c. preparation of an effective information system; and
d. cooperation with institutions that have activities in preventing acts of Violence against Women.

Article 14

The Regional Government is obliged to assist in handling acts of Violence against Women.

Paragraph 2

Duties of Regional Government in the Protection of Children

Article 15

1. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out the Protection of Children in the Region.
2. Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented through the preparation of a strategic plan for the Protection of Children for the long term, medium term, and short term.
3. The preparation of the strategy as referred to in paragraph (2) must be integrated with the Regional development planning document.
4. The Regional Government in preparing the strategic plan

as referred to in paragraph (3) may involve the community, social institutions and/or other institutions that carry out activities related to the Protection of Children.

Article 16

The Regional Government provides guidance and direction for Children to embrace religion in accordance with the religion and beliefs of their parents.

Article 17

1. The Regional Government is obliged to provide comprehensive health facilities for Children.
2. Comprehensive health facilities as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of community health centers and hospitals.
3. Comprehensive health as referred to in paragraph (1) includes promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, both for basic health Services and referrals.
4. Comprehensive health as referred to in paragraph (3) is provided free of charge for families who are unable or poor families.
5. The Regional Government carries out activities to support the provision of breast milk to infants.

Article 18

1. The Regional Government is obliged to provide primary and secondary education for Children.
2. In providing education as referred to in paragraph (1) the Regional Government provides education cost assistance or free education for students who come from poor families and/or neglected Children.

Article 19

1. The Regional Government is obliged to accommodate and/or care for Children who are neglected by their parents and/or family.
2. The accommodation and/or care of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out through cooperation with institutions, social foundations, or Orphanages.

Article 20

1. The Regional Government is obliged to provide special Protection for Children who are Victims of unrest or Victims of natural disasters.
2. Special protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of fulfillment of:
a. basic needs consisting of food, clothing, housing, education, health, learning and recreation, security guarantees, and equal treatment; and
b. special needs for Children with disabilities and Children who experience psychosocial disorders.

Article 21

1. The Regional Government is obliged to prevent efforts and acts of violence against Children.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out in the form of activities that include:
a. early detection and intervention;
b. socialization, seminars or workshops;
c. preparation of an effective information system; and
d. cooperation with institutions that have activities in preventing acts of violence against Children.

Article 22

The Regional Government is obliged to assist in handling acts of Violence against Children.

Part Two

Authorities

Article 23

1. The Regional Government has the authority to:
a. accommodate Victims of acts of Violence against Women and Children; and
b. accommodate and/or care for neglected Children.
2. The Regional Government in exercising the authority as referred to in paragraph (1) may cooperate with related parties.

Article 24

1. Related parties as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) are:
a. social community organizations, institutions or orphanages that have activities related to the protection, care, accommodation, guidance, empowerment of Children and/or Women;
b. social workers or volunteers;
c. advocates; and
d. religious leaders.
2. Cooperation as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) is carried out in the form of a cooperation agreement.

CHAPTER VI

HANDLING OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Article 25

1. The Regional Government shall prepare medical personnel at community health centers or at hospitals specifically handling Victims of Violence against Women and Children.
2. Medical personnel as referred to in paragraph (1) shall provide complete health services to Victims of Violence against Women and Children.

Article 26

1. The Regional Government shall assist in providing special rooms for Victims of Violence against Women and Children.
2. Special rooms as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be located at the Resort Police and at every Sector Police in the Region.

Article 27

1. Social workers or volunteer personnel as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter b shall have the duty to accompany Victims in facing the process of resolving acts of violence.
2. In carrying out duties as referred to in paragraph (1), social workers or volunteer personnel shall conduct activities:
a. counseling or guidance to strengthen and provide a sense of security for Victims;

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU
b. providing information regarding the rights of Victims to obtain Protection from the police and the determination of Protection orders from the court;
c. escorting Victims to Safe Houses or alternative residences; and
d. conducting integrated coordination in providing services to Victims with agencies or related parties needed by Victims.

Article 28

1. The Regional Government shall prepare Safe Houses.
2. Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be used for temporary handling of Victims of Violence against Women and Children.
3. Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be provided in every Sub-district.

Article 29

1. Advocates as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter c shall assist in providing legal services for Victims or provide legal consultation in the form of information regarding the rights of Victims and the judicial process;
2. Advocates shall accompany and/or represent Victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings.
3. Information on violence from Victims that is personal in nature shall be kept confidential by advocates.
4. Advocates may coordinate with fellow law enforcers, companion volunteers, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

Article 30

Religious leaders as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter d shall have the duty to:

a. provide spiritual guidance or strengthening of faith and piety to Victims; and
b. provide explanations regarding the rights and obligations of Victims.

Guidance

Article 31

2. Guidance for Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted through:

2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted in the form of:
a. providing data or information regarding Women and Children who have not yet received fulfillment of rights from the Regional Government;
b. providing information on every condition that tends to lead to acts of violence against Women and Children to the competent authorities and/or the Regional Government; and/or

I. GENERAL

Protection from violence that threatens the safety of Women and Children must be provided from an early stage. Protection is necessary to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. For example, Women and Children become Victims of abuse, neglect, eviction and ill-treatment by parents or other people. For Women and Children experiencing such problems, the Regional Government must provide Safe Houses and adequate budget.

Likewise, the role of various parties to help address violence issues faced by Women and Children must be in synergy with Regional Government programs and activities. Social community organizations, volunteers, social workers, medical personnel, advocates and religious leaders must also be mobilized and able to participate in providing and enhancing a sense of security for Women and Children.

For the Regional Government of Pasangkayu Regency, a concrete step to create a legal umbrella to provide and enhance a sense of security and prevent acts of violence against Women and Children is by establishing a Regional Regulation on Protection for Children and Women. Furthermore, the implementation of protection of Women and Children shall be carried out based on the principles of: justice; respect for the rights of Victims; gender justice and equality; non-discrimination; and the best interests of Victims.
