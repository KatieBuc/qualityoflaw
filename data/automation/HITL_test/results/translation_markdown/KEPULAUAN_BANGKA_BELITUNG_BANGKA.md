# REGENT OF BANGKA,

a. that women's empowerment is carried out so that women can actualize their potential optimally to participate in development in accordance with their nature and capacity;  
b. that women as national assets who play a role in the process of continuation and preparation of quality generations, need to receive guarantees of their rights and protection from acts of violence and discrimination in order to build society, nation and state;  
c. that based on the provisions of Article 12 paragraph (2) letter b of Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government, mandates that women's empowerment is a mandatory affair organized by the Regency Regional Government;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Regency is Bangka Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of Government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Bangka.  
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Bangka Regency.  
5. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of Bangka Regency.  
6. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of Government affairs that are the authority of the Region.  
7. Human Rights are a set of rights inherent in the nature and existence of human beings as creatures of God Almighty and are His gift that must be respected, upheld and protected by the state, law, Government, and every person for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity and worth.  
8. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social, knowledge, and skills capacity so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.  
9. Women's Protection is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.  
10. Discrimination Against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex which has the effect or purpose of impairing or nullifying the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status on the basis of equality between men and women.  
11. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.  
12. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, defense and security activities and equality in enjoying the results of development.  
13. Society is individuals, families, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.  
14. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children.  
15. Labor Force is every person who is capable of doing work to produce goods and/or services either to meet their own needs or for society.  
16. Worker/Laborer is every person who works by receiving wages or remuneration in other forms.  
17. Elderly Women are persons who have reached 60 years of age and above.  
18. Women with Disabilities are women who experience physical and/or mental barriers that interfere with their activities and social interaction normally.  
19. Homeless Women are women who do not have a permanent place of residence and for various reasons must live under bridges, public parks, roadsides or various other public facilities to sleep and carry out daily life.  
20. Domestic Workers are people who work for a person or several people in a household to do household work for wages.  
21. Women Heads of Households are women who for various reasons are left by their husbands, and ultimately act or contribute and function to carry out the role and responsibilities as head of household.  
22. Former Female Inmates of Correctional Institutions are women who have served sentences in correctional institutions.  
23. Women Victims of Disasters are women who suffer or die as a result of natural and social disasters.  
24. Female Commercial Sex Workers are women who work as sex workers of their own volition and/or are prostituted.  
25. Women Victims of Violence are women who experience physical, mental suffering and/or economic loss caused by a criminal act.

#### Article 2

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:

a. women's rights;  
b. obligations and responsibilities of the Regency Regional Government;  
c. women's empowerment;  
d. women's protection;  
e. women's empowerment and protection strategy;  
f. implementation of women's protection;  
g. mechanism for organizing women's empowerment and protection;  
h. financing; and  
i. guidance and supervision.

#### Article 3

Women's Empowerment and Protection is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect for human rights;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination; and  
d. the best interests of women.

#### Article 4

The purpose of this Regional Regulation is:

a. to empower women both individually and in groups and society at large so that they are able to live more independently; and  
b. to prevent and address the risks of violence and vulnerability of women so that their survival can be fulfilled according to the principles of equality and justice.

# CHAPTER II

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 5

1. Every woman has the right to:
   a. live and maintain life and improve her standard of living;  
   b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue offspring;  
   c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life to become a faithful, pious, responsible, noble, happy, and prosperous human being in accordance with human rights;  
   d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;  
   e. be involved in every stage of the development process;  
   f. be free from slavery/servitude and threats;  
   g. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;  
   h. obtain welfare and a decent life;  
   i. participate in politics;  
   j. perform legal acts; and  
   k. freely choose a partner in her marriage.  
2. Every woman during marriage has the same rights and responsibilities as her husband over all matters relating to her married life, relationship with her children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with laws and regulations.  
3. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to children with regard to the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER III

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE REGENCY REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 6

1. The Regency Regional Government is obligated and responsible for the empowerment and protection of women.  
2. The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;  
   b. determination of implementation guidelines;  
   c. provision of services; and  
   d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.  
3. The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (2) are carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IV

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 7

Women's empowerment is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings so that they are able to play a role and participate in the fields of:

a. economy;  
b. social culture;  
c. politics; and  
d. law.

#### Article 8

Women's empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 7 letter a shall be implemented through:
a. provision of skills and job training;  
b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;  
c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;  
d. facilitation and capital assistance; and  
e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 9

Women's empowerment in the socio-cultural field as referred to in Article 7 letter b shall be implemented through:
a. enhancement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;  
b. enhancement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome;  
c. health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health;  
d. enhancement of awareness and knowledge about family planning;  
e. being independent, healthy and prosperous, including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and  
f. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and the development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 10

1. Women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in Article 7 letter c includes:
   a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;  
   b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;  
   c. participation in general elections; and  
   d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.  
2. Women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 11

1. Women's empowerment in the legal field as referred to in Article 7 letter d includes:
   a. enhancement of awareness and knowledge in the legal field through communication, information and education services; and  
   b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.  
2. Women's empowerment in the legal field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER V

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

#### Article 12

1. Protection of women includes:
   a. social protection;  
   b. economic protection; and  
   c. legal protection.  
2. Social protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is to ensure the survival of women in the form of social protection in order to ensure the fulfillment of a decent standard of living.  
3. Economic protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is to prevent and address the risk of vulnerability due to shocks and social vulnerability of a person, family, group and/or community for the survival of women.  
4. Legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c shall be implemented in terms of providing protection to women who become witnesses and victims of violence and discrimination.

# CHAPTER VI

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 13

Implementation of protection of women shall be provided to:
a. women workers/laborers;  
b. elderly women;  
c. women with disabilities;  
d. homeless women;  
e. female domestic workers;  
f. women heads of household;  
g. women former inmates of correctional institutions;  
h. women victims of disaster;  
i. commercial sex workers; and  
j. women victims of violence.

## Part Two

Women Workers/Laborers

#### Article 14

1. Implementation of protection for women workers/laborers as referred to in Article 13 letter a is to provide protection from acts of economic exploitation and cruel, inhumane treatment and to seek proper appreciation for their work.  
2. Women workers/laborers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:
   a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;  
   b. health and social security;  
   c. access to information and legal consultation services;  
   d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve status; and  
   e. witnesses and victims.

## Part Three

Elderly Women

#### Article 15

1. Implementation of protection for elderly women as referred to in Article 13 letter b is in order to extend life expectancy so that they can carry out their social functions and play an active role naturally in their social life.  
2. Elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:
   a. basic health services to maintain and improve health status including psychological services and diseases related to the elderly (gerontologic/geriatric);;  
   b. use of facilities and accessibility of public facilities and infrastructure; and  
   c. government administration services.

## Part Four

Women with Disabilities

#### Article 16

1. Implementation of protection for women with disabilities as referred to in Article 13 letter c is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination including freedom from cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and freedom from exploitation.  
2. Women with disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:
   a. guarantee of continuity and personal self-development;  
   b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, sports, arts and culture as well as utilization of information and communication technology;  
   c. accessibility of information, legal aid, mobility, social services; and  
   d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

## Part Five

Homeless Women

#### Article 17

1. Implementation of protection for homeless women as referred to in Article 13 letter d is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.  
2. Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:
   a. decent housing;  
   b. health and social security;  
   c. population administration registration;  
   d. opportunities to obtain skills to improve economic status; and  
   e. access to information and legal consultation services.

## Part Six

Female Domestic Workers

#### Article 18

1. Implementation of Protection for female domestic workers as referred to in Article 13 letter e is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation in employment relations.  
2. Female domestic workers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:
   a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;  
   b. health and social security;  
   c. access to information and legal consultation services; and  
   d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Seven

Women Heads of Household

#### Article 19

1. Implementation of Protection for women heads of household as referred to in Article 13 letter f is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, negative stigma and economic empowerment for women heads of household.  
2. Women heads of household as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:
   a. discriminatory actions and efforts to weaken the function of women as the economic support of the family;  
   b. strengthening of capacity and recognition as head of household in their environment; and  
   c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.

## Bagian Kedelapan

Women Former Inmates of Correctional Institutions

#### Article 20

1. Implementation of Protection for women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in Article 13 letter g in order to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting to life in society, so that they can carry out life normally.
2. Women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:
   a. social rehabilitation and reintegration; and
   b. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Nine

Women Disaster Victims

#### Article 21

1. Implementation of Protection for women disaster victims as referred to in Article 13 letter h in order to provide protection during disasters and post-disaster.
2. Women disaster victims as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:
   a. access to decent housing;
   b. health services;
   c. information access services and legal consultation; and
   d. post-disaster recovery.

## Part Ten

Women Commercial Sex Workers

#### Article 22

1. Implementation of Protection for women commercial sex workers as referred to in Article 13 letter i in order to provide health protection and improvement of life skills as capital to improve social status.
2. Protection for women commercial sex workers as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   a. providing life skills;
   b. facilitating reproductive health services; and
   c. preventing human trafficking.

## Part Eleven

Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 23

1. Implementation of Protection for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 13 letter j in order to provide legal protection and/or trauma and health recovery.
2. Protection for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   a. legal aid;
   b. assistance after the occurrence of acts of violence;
   c. social rehabilitation and reintegration; and
   d. trauma and health services.

# CHAPTER VII

## WOMEN EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION STRATEGY

#### Article 24

1. Women empowerment and protection is implemented by integrating gender mainstreaming and gender-disaggregated data in an integrated manner based on applicable laws and regulations.
2. In women empowerment and protection, a Working Group and Integrated Services are formed as stipulated by Regent's Decree.
3. Women empowerment and protection is coordinated by the Agency responsible for Women's Empowerment.

# CHAPTER VIII

## MECHANISM FOR IMPLEMENTING WOMEN EMPOWERMENT AND

## PROTECTION

#### Article 25

The mechanism for implementing women empowerment and protection is carried out through stages:

a. formulation of program and activity planning policies;
b. budgeting of programs and activities;
c. implementation of programs and activities; and
d. monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

#### Article 26

1. Formulation of program and activity planning policies as referred to in Article 25 letter a is implemented through:
   a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of data;
   b. coordination and synchronization of program and activity planning preparation with relevant Regional Apparatus; and
   c. determination of programs and activities in planning documents.
2. Formulation of program and activity planning policies is further regulated in minimum service standards and standard operating procedures.
3. Formulation of program and activity planning policies for the implementation of women empowerment and protection is coordinated by the Regional Apparatus responsible for Regency development planning.
4. Budgeting of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter b is implemented through general budget policies set forth in the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget.
5. Implementation of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter c is implemented through:
   a. implementation of programs and activities set forth in the Regional Apparatus budget document; and
   b. coordination and synchronization of program and activity implementation with relevant Regional Apparatus.
6. Implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in Article 25 letter d is implemented through:
   a. conducting periodic monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of programs and activities that have been carried out; and
   b. making periodic reports.

# CHAPTER IX

## FINANCING

#### Article 27

All costs for implementing program and activity planning policies related to Women Empowerment and Protection are sourced from:

a. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget;
b. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
c. Other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 28

1. The Regent conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of women empowerment and protection.
2. The form of guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) is as follows:
   a. providing socialization to the community and business world regarding women empowerment and protection policies;
   b. providing books, leaflets, brochures regarding women empowerment and protection, and other related issues and disseminating them to the community;
   c. facilitating capacity building of women empowerment and protection implementing institutions;
   d. facilitating women's participation forums and women's organizations;
   e. facilitating rehabilitation and reintegration services for women disaster victims and victims of violence;
   f. coordinating the participation of the business world and other institutions, including international assistance for women empowerment and protection; and
   g. providing awards to the community and business world both individually and institutionally who have made efforts to empower and protect women.
3. The form of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of monitoring and evaluation activities on women empowerment and protection.

## CLOSING PROVISIONS
