# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Semarang City.  
2. Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Semarang.  
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of governmental affairs that are the authority of the Region.  
5. Human Rights are a set of rights inherent in the nature and existence of human beings as creatures of Almighty God and are His gift that must be respected, upheld and protected by the state, law, government, and every person for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity and worth.  
6. Women's Empowerment is an effort to obtain access and control over resources, economic, political, social, cultural, so that women can manage themselves and increase self-confidence to be able to play a role and participate actively in solving problems, so as to be able to build capacity and self-concept.  
3-

7. Protection of Women is all efforts to protect and provide a sense of security to women and the fulfillment of their rights through consistent, structured, and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.  
8. Violence Against Women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, and psychologically, including threats of certain actions, arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the private or public sphere.  
9. Gender is the values, roles, and responsibilities of women and men that are socially and culturally constructed by society.  
10. Gender Equality is an equal condition and position between men and women to obtain their rights as human beings, in accessing and controlling resources, participating in all development processes and decision-making, and obtaining benefits from development.

#### Article 2

Empowerment and Protection of Women is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect for human rights;  
b. non-discrimination;  
c. gender equality and gender justice;  
d. justice and legal certainty;  
e. benefit;  
f. participatory;  
g. accountability; and  
h. inclusion.

#### Article 3

Empowerment and Protection of Women aims to:

a. improve the quality of life of women;  
b. increase the participation of women in the development process;  
c. encourage women's leadership and women's bargaining position in decision-making in various development processes, both planning, implementation and monitoring and evaluation;  
d. increase the ability of women to manage the economy, both in meeting household needs and in opening productive and independent employment opportunities;  
e. provide protection of women's rights from various forms of violence and other discriminatory treatment;  
f. prevent all forms of Violence Against Women;  
4-

g. provide services and recovery for women victims of violence.  
h. increase community participation in the Protection of Women;  
i. realize a safe social life free from all forms of Violence Against Women; and  
j. increase the strengthening and development of institutions providing Women's Empowerment services.

#### Article 4

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:

a. authority and responsibility of regional government;  
b. women's rights;  
c. Women's Empowerment;  
d. Protection of Women;  
e. strategy for Empowerment and Protection of Women;  
f. mechanism for organizing Empowerment and Protection of Women;  
g. institutions;  
h. participation;  
i. guidance and supervision; and  
j. funding.

# CHAPTER III

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 6

Every Woman has the right to:

a. live and sustain life and improve her standard of living;  
b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue her lineage;  
c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life in order to become a faithful, pious, responsible, noble, happy, and prosperous human being in accordance with human rights;  
d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;  
e. be involved in every stage of the development process;  
f. be free from slavery or servitude and threats;  
g. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;  
h. obtain welfare and a decent life;  
i. participate in politics;  
j. perform legal acts; and  
k. freely choose a partner in marriage in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

## Part One

General

#### Article 7

Women's Empowerment is directed to play a role and participate in the fields of:

a. health;  
b. education;  
c. economy;  
d. social culture;  
e. politics and government;  
f. law;  
g. employment;  
h. social security; and  
i. environmental preservation.

## Part Two

Health Sector

#### Article 8

1. Women's Empowerment in the Health Sector is implemented to increase access to health services for women in all aspects of health.  
2. Women's Empowerment in the Health Sector as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented through promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative approaches.

## Part Three

Education Sector

#### Article 9

Women's Empowerment in the Education Sector through:

a. increasing awareness of average length of schooling, school participation rate, and completed education.  
b. organizing formal, non-formal, and informal education for women in the Region.

## Part Three

Economic Sector

#### Article 10

Women's Empowerment in the Economic Sector is implemented through:

a. providing skills and job training;  
b. facilitating the formation, strengthening, and development of productive economic and creative economy business groups;  
c. facilitating capital; and  
d. facilitating the development of marketing networks.

## Part Four

Social and Cultural Sector

#### Article 11

Women's Empowerment in the social and cultural sector is implemented through:

a. increasing knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education according to potential to improve social status;  
b. increasing awareness of various social problems in society through communication, information and education services;  
c. increasing awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and  
d. facilitating and efforts to preserve customs and develop science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

## Part Five

Politics and Government Sector

#### Article 12

Women's Empowerment in the politics and government sector is implemented through:

a. involving women in decision-making at various levels;  
b. involving women in development planning at various levels;  
c. encouraging increased involvement of women in organizations and providing opportunities for women to hold public positions;  
d. facilitating women's participation in general elections;  
e. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions;  
f. facilitating human resource improvement for prospective legislative members, political party members and/or women's community organizations in order to fulfill women's representation in legislative institutions of at least 30% (thirty percent);  
g. facilitating increased women's political participation in legislative institutions;  
h. facilitating facilities and infrastructure for women's community organizations or community organizations whose vision and mission is to fight for the fulfillment of women's rights in the Region;  
i. conducting socialization in order to increase awareness of women's rights and women's participation in politics; and  
j. encouraging the formation of women's organizations and/or forums.

## Part Six

Legal Sector

#### Article 13

1. Women's Empowerment in the legal sector through:
   a. increasing awareness and knowledge in the legal field with communication, information and education services; and
   b. facilitating access and legal consultation services as well as Gender-responsive legal aid.
2. Women's Empowerment in the legal sector as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with laws and regulations.

## Part Seven

Employment Sector

#### Article 14

Women's Empowerment in the employment sector is implemented through:

a. providing access to enter the labor market;  
b. freedom for women to become members of labor unions and lead labor unions; and  
c. access for women to be involved in policy-making related to wages, basic rights of workers and other aspects closely related to employment.

## Part Eight

Social Security Sector

#### Article 15

Women's Empowerment in the social security sector is implemented through:

a. facilitating social rehabilitation guidance for women with social welfare problems; and  
b. encouraging increased involvement of women in overcoming social problems.

## Part Nine

Environmental Preservation Sector

#### Article 16

Women's Empowerment in the environmental preservation sector is implemented through:

a. increasing the capacity of women, especially related to increasing environmental knowledge so that it can be highly beneficial for environmentally friendly development;  
b. increasing environmental preservation education and environmental management in households;  
c. increasing waste management education in the surrounding environment; and  
d. increasing access for women to participate in environmental development.

#### Article 17

Further provisions regarding the implementation of Women's Empowerment as referred to in Article 8 through Article 16 are regulated in the Mayor's Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

## WOMEN'S PROTECTION

## Part One

General

#### Article 18

1. Implementation of Women's Protection through:
   a. prevention of violence against women;
   b. services for women victims of violence; and
   c. strengthening of Women's Protection institutions.

3. Vulnerable women groups as referred to in paragraph (2) include:
   a. poor women;
   b. women with disabilities;
   c. women with HIV/AIDS;
   d. migrant women workers;
   e. elderly women;
   f. indigenous women;
   g. women in disaster and social conflict situations;
   h. women heads of households;
   i. informal women workers;
   j. women in situations of intolerance;
   k. women victims/exposed to NAPZA (narcotics, psychotropics, and addictive substances);
   l. women victims/exposed to terrorism, radicalism and extremism; and/or
   m. other vulnerable women groups.

i. developing protection systems and special support for vulnerable women groups;  
j. conducting awareness-raising for perpetrators;  
10-

## Part Three

Services for Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 20

Women victims of violence receive the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as human beings;  
b. the right to recovery;  
c. the right to determine their own decisions;  
d. the right to obtain easy access to information;  
e. the right to confidentiality;  
f. the right to social rehabilitation;  
g. the right to complaint handling that is fast, appropriate, comfortable and according to needs;  
h. the right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process;  
i. the right to assistance; andj. the right to security.

#### Article 21

Services for women victims of violence may be carried out through:
a. receiving reports and/or outreach to victims of violence;  
b. providing information about the rights of victims of violence;

i. facilitating the needs of victims of violence with disabilities;  
11-
j. coordinating and cooperating on the fulfillment of the rights of victims of violence with other institutions; dank. monitoring the fulfillment of the rights of victims of violence by law enforcement officials during the judicial process.

##### I. GENERAL

The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia mandates the authority of regional governments to regulate and manage their own governmental affairs directed towards accelerating the realization of community welfare through improved services, Empowerment and community participation. The authority of Regional Governments is to make regional policies to provide services, increase participation, initiative, and community Empowerment aimed at improving people's welfare.

The implementation of regional autonomy must always be oriented towards improving community welfare while always paying attention to the interests and aspirations that grow in society. Therefore, governmental affairs related to basic services such as basic education, health, fulfillment of minimum living needs, basic environmental infrastructure are mandatory to be fulfilled.

Human rights are basic rights that are inherently attached to human beings by nature, are universal and lasting, so they must be protected, respected, maintained, and may not be ignored, reduced, or deprived by anyone. The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, particularly Article 27 paragraph (1) which states that "All citizens shall be equal before the law and government and shall uphold such law and government without any exception". Although the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia guarantees equality of status for every citizen, until now women still experience various obstacles in obtaining their rights in almost all areas of life.

This has an impact, women as part of the national development process, namely as actors as well as beneficiaries of development results, have not been able to obtain optimal benefits in enjoying development results equal to men, especially in the policy formulation and decision-making process, as well as in the implementation of development in all fields and at all levels. On the other hand, women have special needs compared to men so that in accordance with their nature, under certain conditions, the Regional Government must be able to guarantee that their rights are fulfilled according to those needs.  
17-  
Based on this, a regulation is needed that serves as a guideline for all parties, especially the Regional Government in ensuring the implementation of Women's Empowerment and Protection in Semarang City. This Regional Regulation is expected to be able to encourage efforts to improve the physical and mental conditions of women in fulfilling their rights and life needs as part of human rights from various fields of development, increase the commitment and effectiveness of regional governments in implementing policies, programs and activities and encourage the institutional capacity of government and society that improves the quality of life of women.

#### Article 2

Point a What is meant by "Principle of respect for human rights" contains the understanding that the Republic of Indonesia recognizes and upholds human rights and fundamental human freedoms as rights that are naturally inherent in and inseparable from human beings, which must be protected, respected, and upheld for the enhancement of human dignity, welfare, happiness, and intelligence as well as justice.

Point b What is meant by "Principle of non-discrimination" is that in providing protection to women and children victims of violence, there shall be no differentiation on the basis of age, sex, race, ethnicity, tribe, religion and between groups.

Point c What is meant by "Principle of gender equality and justice" is that gender justice is a process to be fair to men and women. Gender equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so as to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, governmental activities and equality in enjoying the results of development.

Point d What is meant by "Principle of justice and legal certainty" contains the understanding that every person has the right to recognition, guarantee, protection and fair legal treatment as well as to obtain legal certainty and equal treatment before the law.
18- Point e What is meant by "Principle of benefit" contains the understanding that the regulation of women's empowerment policies must provide the greatest benefit for the welfare of society.

Point f What is meant by "Principle of participation" contains the understanding that the entire process of policy formulation and implementation must take into account the active participation of all components of society so as to obtain equal benefits in all fields of development and life.

Point g What is meant by "Principle of accountability" concerns that the implementation of women's empowerment must be accountable and constitutes part of the accountability for the implementation of government.

Point h What is meant by "Principle of inclusion" is that the implementation of women's empowerment and protection is carried out by inviting and involving all people with various diversities of abilities, backgrounds, characteristics, status, conditions, ethnicity, culture and others as well as facilitating special needs based on such diversity and eliminating barriers that may hinder the equality of every woman in obtaining her rights.
