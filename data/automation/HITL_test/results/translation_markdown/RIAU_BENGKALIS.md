Article Physical violence as referred to in Article letter constitutes an act that results in pain accompanied by injury, wounds or disability to the body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes death of women and/or children.  

Article Psychological violence as referred to in Article letter constitutes an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or psychological suffering to women and/or children. a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;

#### Article 10

Neglect as referred to in Article letter includes: a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care; b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after children as they should be, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
c. acts of deliberately neglecting women and children within their household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to such women; and/or d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

# CHAPTER III PROVISION OF SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE Part One General

#### Article 17

Protection of women and children is the shared responsibility of:

a. Regional Government;  
b. Parents, Guardians and/or Family;  
c. Community; andd. Business/private sector.

### Paragraph Regional Government

#### Article 18

The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government (1) as referred to in Article 17 letter a, include:

a. preparing and implementing programs and activities related to the protection of women and children;  
b. implementing the protection of women and children;  
c. supervising services to victims of violence based on minimum service standards in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations; and  
d. allocating budget for the protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity.  

In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities (2) as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government prepares programs and activities for the protection of women and children in a regional action plan which is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government (3) as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their main duties and functions. Further provisions regarding the regional action plan (4) as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be further stipulated by Regent Regulation.  

Paragraph Parents, Guardians and/or Family

#### Article 19

Parents, guardians and/or family legally have (1) full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence, report when violence occurs and protect victims. Parents and/or family must carry out prevention (2) of all forms of violence by providing guidance and example in accordance with religious teachings and prevailing norms. In the event that parents, guardians and/or family as referred to in paragraph (1) do not exist or their whereabouts are unknown or due to certain reasons cannot carry out their obligations and responsibilities, then (3) the obligations and responsibilities may be transferred to the Regional Government and other family members implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  

Paragraph Community

#### Article 20

1. The community has the obligation and responsibility for the protection of women and children victims of violence through community participation activities in the protection of women and children.  
2. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) include:

a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;  
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and  
c. participating in handling victims of acts of violence.

### Paragraph Business World/Private Sector

#### Article 21

The obligations and responsibilities of the business world/private sector as referred to in Article 17 letter include:

a. protecting the rights of women;  
b. participating in allocating/providing budget for the protection and empowerment of women and children; and/or  
c. providing wider space for women according to competence.

#### Article 44

In addition to Investigating Officers of the State Police of the Republic of Indonesia, investigations of criminal acts as referred to in paragraph (1) of this Regional Regulation may be carried out by Civil Servants within the Bengkalis Regency Government whose appointment is determined in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  

Civil Servant Investigators as referred to in paragraph (2) of paragraph (1) are authorized to:

a. examine the truth of reports or information concerning criminal acts related to violence against women and children;  
b. examine any person and/or business entity suspected of participating in criminal acts related to violence against women and children in the business environment;  
c. request information from any person and/or business entity in connection with criminal acts related to violence against women and children;  
d. examine evidence materials, records, or other documents concerning criminal acts related to violence against women and children;  
e. conduct examinations at certain places suspected of containing evidence materials;  
f. carry out seizures of goods and materials resulting from violations that can be used as evidence in criminal cases related to violence against women and children;  
g. request assistance from experts in the framework of carrying out investigative duties concerning criminal acts related to violence against women and children; and  
h. terminate the investigation if there is insufficient evidence of the existence of criminal acts related to violence against women and children.  

In carrying out their authority, Civil Servant Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) shall coordinate with the Indonesian National Police in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (3) of laws and regulations.

Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) shall notify the commencement of the investigation and submit the results of their investigation to the Public Prosecutor through the Investigator Officer of the Police of the Republic of Indonesia in accordance with the provisions regulated in the Criminal Procedure Code.

# CHAPTER VII CRIMINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 45

Any person who intentionally commits violence against women and children as referred to in Article up to Article 12 shall be subject to criminal sanctions as regulated in laws and regulations.
