# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

Region is Bangli Regency.

Regional Government is the Government of Bangli Regency.

Regent is the Regent of Bangli.

Regional House of Representatives hereinafter referred to as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Bangli Regency.

Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on a court determination.

Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person recognized by law as a Woman.

Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a Child in the womb.

Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.

Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child committed by parties who should be responsible for the Child or those who have power over the Child.

Violence against Women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.

Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences misery and/or suffering as a result of violence and/or threat of violence.

Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction to be able to carry out social functions normally.

Repatriation is an effort to return victims of Violence from the Regional territory to the region of origin.

Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims Legal Aid is legal services provided by companions, law enforcement officials which include providing legal consultation, exercising power, representing, accompanying, defending and taking other legal actions for victims of violence.

Integrated Service Center hereinafter referred to as PPT is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for victims of violence.

Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) is an integrated service unit established by the Regency government to provide services to Women and Children victims of violence comprehensively including complaint services, health rehabilitation; social rehabilitation; repatriation; social reintegration, and legal aid.

Minimum Service Standards hereinafter referred to as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing services for handling reports/complaints, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal aid, as well as repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Children victims of violence.

Pakraman Village is a customary law community unit in Bali Province that has a unified tradition and code of conduct for the social life of the Hindu community passed down through generations within the bond of Kahyangan Tiga or Kahyangan Desa which has a certain territory and its own assets and has the right to manage its own household;

Social Protection House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE OF REGULATION

#### Article 2

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out based on the principles of: respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;

justice and gender equality;

non-discrimination;

best interests of Victims; and legal certainty.

#### Article 3

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to: prevent all forms of violence against Women and Children;

protect Women and Children and provide services to Women and Children Victims of Violence; and empowerment of Children Victims of Violence.

#### Article 4

The Scope of Regulation for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence includes: victims' rights;

obligations and responsibilities;

institutions;

minimum service standards;

social protection house;

monitoring and evaluation;

reporting;

guidance and supervision;

community participation; and funding.

# CHAPTER III

## VICTIMS' RIGHTS

#### Article 5

Every Woman and Child Victim of Violence has the right: to have their dignity respected as a human being;

to receive protection from family, community, Regional Government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on the determination of a Protection order from the Court;

to Recovery of physical, psychological and sexual health services according to the suffering experienced by victims of violence;

special handling related to victim confidentiality;

assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;

spiritual guidance services; and to determine their own decisions.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 6

The Regional Government has the authority and responsibility for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) include: implementing policies for the protection of Women and Children victims of violence based on laws and regulations;

establishing policies, programs and activities for the protection of Women and Children victims of violence;

providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of violence;

allocating budget in the implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of violence;

fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of violence.

#### Article 7

The community has obligations and responsibilities in providing Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence.

The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out in the form of: preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against Women and Children;

providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against Women and Children to the competent authorities;

providing emergency assistance; and providing protection to victims of violence.

# CHAPTER V

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 8

In providing protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region, the Regent may establish a PPT.

The establishment and development of the PPT shall be adjusted to the development of priority needs, and the financial capacity of the Region as well as the institutional and personnel capacity available in the region.

The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may accommodate institutions/work units that provide services for victims of violence such as P2TP2A, safe houses, social protection houses for Children, trauma center protection houses, women's social protection houses, shelters and other service activities in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

The forms of services implemented by the PPT include: complaints;

health rehabilitation;

social rehabilitation;

legal aid;

return; and social reintegration.

In providing protection for Women and Children victims of violence, the PPT is obliged to: provide services as quickly as possible and free of charge to victims;

provide protection and fulfillment of victims' rights to health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, return, social reintegration and legal aid;

cooperate with certain institutions in providing translators and volunteer assistants required for victims;

network with government or private hospitals for treatment and health recovery of victims and cooperate with witness and victim protection institutions, social protection houses or trauma centers owned by the government, community, or other institutions for health recovery of victims;

provide convenience, comfort, and safety for victims;

maintain confidentiality of victims;

provide fulfillment of legal aid for victims; and in the case where integrated service delivery is carried out in a networked manner, the PPT remains responsible for the entire referral process of services required by victims.

The provision of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (6) shall be implemented in accordance with the Minimum Service Standards (SPM).

#### Article 9

Community institutions may establish a PPT.

In establishing a PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), it shall be guided by the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

Provisions regarding the composition of membership, duties and responsibilities of the PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated in a Regent's Decree.

# CHAPTER VI

## MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

#### Article 10

The Minimum Service Standards (SPM) for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to ensure that Women and Children victims of violence receive the minimum services needed.

The Minimum Service Standards (SPM) for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence includes services for: handling complaints/reports of victims of violence against Women and Children;

health services for Women and Children victims of violence;

social rehabilitation for Women and Children victims of violence;

law enforcement and legal aid for Women and Children victims of violence; and return and social reintegration for Women and Children victims of violence.

#### Article 11

The SPM as referred to in Article 10 has performance indicators including: coverage of Women and Children victims of violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers in integrated service units;

coverage of Women and Children victims of violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at Community Health Centers and Hospitals; coverage of social rehabilitation services provided by trained social rehabilitation officers for Women and Children victims of violence in integrated service units;

coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for Women and Children victims of violence in integrated service units;

coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of violence against Women and Children;

coverage of Women and Children victims of violence who receive legal aid services;

coverage of return services for Women and Children victims of violence; and coverage of social reintegration services for Women and Children victims of violence.

The determination of SPM performance indicators for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes minimum targets that must be achieved by integrated service units in stages.

# CHAPTER VII

## SOCIAL PROTECTION HOUSES

#### Article 12

The Regional Government is obliged to establish Social Protection Houses and/or Safe Houses.

Social Protection Houses and/or Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (1) shall guarantee, in the provision of services, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, return, and social reintegration.

In the event that victims experience trauma or diseases that endanger themselves as a result of acts of violence requiring immediate assistance, the Regional Government is obliged to provide first aid no later than 7 (seven) days after the complaint is submitted.

For the provision of services as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government may utilize social protection houses and/or Safe Houses owned by the community or other social service institutions.

# CHAPTER VIII

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 13

The Regional Government conducts monitoring to ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children victims of violence.

Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children victims of violence in the Region.

#### Article 14

Monitoring is carried out through coordination and direct monitoring of relevant Regional Apparatus on a regular basis.

Monitoring is carried out from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children victims of violence for the current year.

#### Article 15

Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children is carried out at the end of each fiscal year or if necessary according to needs.

The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children are used as input for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children for the following year.

Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## REPORTING

#### Article 16

The Regent is responsible for preparing reports on the implementation of the SPM for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence and submitting them to the Governor with copies submitted to the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection and the Minister of Home Affairs.

Reporting of implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out annually.

The form of reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 17

The Regent provides guidance for the development of the PPT.

Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: provision of implementation instructions;

guidance;

supervision; and monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of services for Women and Children victims of violence, both apparatus and community.

#### Article 18

The Regent provides guidance and supervision over the implementation of the SPM for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Regency area.

# CHAPTER XI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 19

In providing protection to Women and Children victims of violence, the community may:

a. establish family partners at the kelurahan/village level in coordination with Desa Pakraman;

b. establish Women and Children protection units within community organizations;

c. carry out independent dissemination/awareness-raising of the rights of Women and Children;

d. provide first aid to victims; and

e. report to the competent agencies if violence against victims occurs in their environment.

Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, Desa Pakraman, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

# CHAPTER XII

## FUNDING

#### Article 20

Funds for the implementation of protection for Women and Children Victims of violence shall be sourced from: regional revenue and expenditure budget; and other lawful and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XIII

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 21

In the event that PPT as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (1) has not been established, the existing P2TP2A shall continue to carry out the duties and functions of integrated services for Women and Children victims of violence in the Region.

# CHAPTER XIV

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 22

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Bangli Regency.

##### GENERAL

Violence against Women and Children is one form of act that violates human rights. As is known, the human rights of Women and Children have been guaranteed and regulated in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia and the Law on Human Rights. Therefore, the protection of the rights of Women and Children from violence should be respected, upheld and human dignity enhanced without discrimination, welfare and justice for Women and Children.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, violence against Women is any act based on gender difference that results in or is likely to result in misery or suffering to Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the Child. The existence of Women and Children victims of violence has not received adequate services so that minimum services are needed for victims of violence.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against Women and Children in Bangli Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, it is necessary to provide protection for Women and Children victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children victims of violence, particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of Women and Children victims of violence in Bangli Regency.
