# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Central Bangka Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Central Bangka.  
4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the Regional House of Representatives of Central Bangka Regency.  
5. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities as women and as men that are created and internalized in the family, society and community culture where we live, including expectations, attitudes, characteristics, behavior of how to be a man and how to be a woman.  
6. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political belief, which results in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.  
7. Empowerment of women is any effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, and social capacity, knowledge and skills so that women are ready to be utilized/assigned according to their respective abilities.  
8. Protection is any effort aimed at providing a sense of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  
9. Violence is any act unlawfully, with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.  
10. Disability is an individual who has physical or mental limitations.  
11. Regional cooperation is an agreement between governor and governor or governor and regent/mayor or between regent/mayor and other regent/mayor, and or governor, regent/mayor with third parties, which is made in writing and gives rise to rights and obligations.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Empowerment and Protection of Women is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect for human rights;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination; and  
d. protection of victims.

#### Article 3

The implementation of Empowerment and Protection of Women is carried out with the aim of:

a. increasing the participation of women both individually and in groups as potential and resources in the implementation of social community life;  
b. preventing all forms of discrimination;  
c. empowering women both individually and in groups and communities experiencing gender issues so that they are able to meet their needs independently;  
d. preventing and addressing risks from the vulnerability of women so that their survival can be fulfilled in accordance with minimum basic needs;  
e. reducing the gap in opportunities between women and men in achieving development; and  
f. reducing acts of violence against women.

# CHAPTER III

## HUMAN RIGHTS AND BASIC HUMAN FREEDOMS

#### Article 4

Human Rights and basic human freedoms consist of:

a. Right to life;  
b. Right to have a family and continue offspring;  
c. Right to self-development;  
d. Right to obtain justice;  
e. Right to personal freedom;  
f. Right to security;  
g. Right to welfare; and

h. Right to participate in government.

#### Article 5

1. Every woman has the right to:
   a. live peacefully, safely, in peace, happily, prosperous physically and spiritually;  
   b. live in a family within the bond of a legal marriage;  
   c. maintain life and improve her standard of living;  
   d. have work that she likes and is also entitled to fair employment conditions;  
   e. obtain education and teaching in all types, levels and pathways of education in accordance with the specified requirements;  
   f. a healthy and clean environment;  
   g. receive fair wages in accordance with her work that is comparable and commensurate with human dignity;  
   h. elect and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting in accordance with applicable laws and regulations;  
   i. receive special protection in the implementation of work or profession against matters that may threaten safety and/or health related to women's reproductive function; and  
   j. special rights inherent to women due to their reproductive function are guaranteed and protected by law.  
2. Women who are adults and or are married have the right to perform legal acts independently unless otherwise determined by their religious law.  
3. A legal marriage can only take place based on the free will of the woman and the man in accordance with laws and regulations.  
4. A wife during the marriage bond has equal rights and responsibilities with her husband over all matters relating to their married life, relations with their children and ownership rights and management of joint property.  
5. After the dissolution of her marriage, a woman has equal rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to their children, taking into account the best interests of the children.  
6. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has equal rights with her former husband over all matters relating to joint property without reducing the rights of children, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
7. Every woman who is in a condition of disability, elderly, pregnant is entitled to receive facilities and special treatment.  
8. Every elderly woman, with physical and/or mental disability, is entitled to receive care, education, training and special assistance at the expense of the regional government to ensure a decent life in accordance with human dignity.

# CHAPTER IV

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government is obligated and responsible for women's empowerment efforts.  
2. Women's empowerment efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
3. In order to improve the position, role and quality of women as well as efforts to realize gender equality and justice in family life, society, nation and state, women must be empowered.  
4. Women's empowerment as referred to in paragraph (3) is directed to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, order activities and equality in enjoying the results of development.

# CHAPTER V

## WOMEN'S PROTECTION

#### Article 7

1. Women have the right to receive special protection from matters that threaten their safety and/or health.  
2. Special protection is given to women because their reproductive function is guaranteed and protected by law.  
3. Special protection as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of health services related to:
   a. menstruation;  
   b. pregnancy;  
   c. childbirth; and  
   d. breastfeeding.

#### Article 8

Women victims of violence have the right to receive protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage and confidential handling either from individuals, groups or institutions, both government and non-government.

#### Article 9

1. Women have equal opportunities without discrimination to obtain employment.  
2. Equal opportunities as referred to in paragraph (1) are realized through the fulfillment of women's rights to receive equal treatment from employers.  
3. Employers who employ female workers are obligated to provide protection in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
4. Protection that must be provided by employers to women as referred to in paragraph (3) will be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 10

1. The Regional Government is obligated to provide protection to women so that they can realize and enjoy a decent standard of living.  
2. Provisions regarding Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 11

1. The Regional Government facilitates the formation of women's protection institutions.  
2. The Regent establishes policies, programs and activities for women's protection to eliminate all forms of discrimination and violence against women in the Region.  
3. Implementation of policies, programs and activities for women's protection as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out by forming, developing, strengthening or utilizing task forces, forums, working groups or other institutions.  
4. Membership of task forces, forums, working groups or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) consists of relevant Regional Work Units, community leaders, religious leaders, and universities.  
5. Further provisions regarding the Formation of task forces, forums, working groups or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 12

Implementation of policies, programs and activities for women's protection can be carried out with efforts to:
   a. cooperate with the business world, non-governmental institutions, private companies, Foundations, and other domestic institutions as well as international institutions, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
   b. conduct regional cooperation, in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations;  
   c. provide and facilitate women's protection services;  
   d. compile a data system for women's protection; and  
   e. carry out coordination of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for women's protection among Regional Work Units.

#### Article 13

1. Provision and facilitation of women's protection services as referred to in Article 12 letter c in the form of:
   a. victim identification;  
   b. legal assistance;  
   c. medical rehabilitation;  
   d. psychosocial rehabilitation;  
   e. social reintegration;  
   f. repatriation assistance; or  
   g. other forms.  
2. Provision and facilitation of women's protection services as referred to in paragraph (1) are provided to women:
   a. victims of violence;  
   b. victims of human trafficking;  
   c. elderly who are not independent;  
   d. with disabilities;  
   e. victims of sexual exploitation;  
   f. heads of households;  
   g. in conflict-prone and disaster areas;  
   h. workers; and  
   i. other vulnerable groups.

# CHAPTER VI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 14

1. The community can participate in the implementation of women's empowerment and protection.  
2. Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, groups or social community organizations.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:
   a. active role in policy formulation;  
   b. cooperation; and/or  
   c. complaints/reports.  
4. Procedures for community participation as referred to in paragraph (3) are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## FUNDING

#### Article 15

1. Funding for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women in the Region is sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of Central Bangka Regency.  
2. In the event that the Region receives funding assistance for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women from the Government, it must be implemented in accordance with the laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## MONITORING, EVALUATION AND REPORTING

#### Article 16

1. To ensure synergy, continuity and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women, the Regional Government conducts monitoring.  
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women in the Region.  
3. Monitoring is conducted periodically starting from planning up to the policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women for the current year.  
4. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women is conducted at the end of each fiscal year.  
5. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women are used as input for the formulation of policies, programs and activities for the following year.  
6. The Regent submits a report on the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women to the Governor.  
7. The implementation of reporting as referred to in paragraph (6) is conducted annually and/or when required.

# CHAPTER IX

## SANCTIONS

#### Article 17

Any person who intentionally commits violence against women shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 18

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered, with its placement in the Regional Gazette of Central Bangka Regency.
