# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. The Central Government hereinafter referred to as the Government is the President of the Republic of Indonesia who holds the governmental power of the State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
2. Region is Kudus Regency.
3. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
4. Regent is the Regent of Kudus.
5. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter referred to as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Kudus Regency.
6. Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit in charge of women's empowerment and child protection.
7. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
8. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
9. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by the family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
10. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
11. Implementation of protection is a series of activities aimed at preventing the occurrence of violence, providing protection and recovery services and social reintegration, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation carried out by the SKPD in charge.
12. Violence is any form of unlawful act that may result in or cause danger to a person whether physical, sexual, psychological or economic in nature.
13. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which are and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
14. Violence Against Children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which are and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
15. Physical Violence is any act that results in pain, illness, or serious injury.
16. Sexual Violence is any act in the form of forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural and/or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
17. Psychological Violence or psychic violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
18. Economic Violence is any act that may include restricting a person's right to work decently inside or outside the home and also acts of neglecting family members in the form of not providing a decent living, care or maintenance.
19. Victim of Gender-Based Violence is a person who because of their gender experiences physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
20. Child Victim of Violence is a child who receives harsh treatment whether physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
21. Victim Recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, whether physically, psychologically, socially, economically or sexually.
22. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the purpose of strengthening victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems they face.
23. Integrated Services is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as a unified implementation, prevention efforts, health services, medicolegal services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
24. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes who can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.
25. Safe House (shelter) is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with predetermined operational standards.
26. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations, and/or community organizations.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:

a. non-discrimination;
b. best interests of victims;
c. justice and gender equality;
d. victim protection;
e. survival, and growth and development of children;
f. respect for the views of children;
g. openness;
h. integration;
i. empowerment; and
j. confidentiality of victims.

#### Article 3

The objectives of this Regional Regulation are:

a. to prevent all forms of gender-based violence in the home, and violence against children that occurs in the household and/or community;
b. to provide services and protection;
c. to provide legal assistance;
d. to seek recovery and social reintegration; and
e. to provide a sense of security to victims.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

1. Every victim of gender-based violence and child victims has the right to:
   a. obtain protection for the security of themselves, their families, and their property, and be free from threats related to information that will be, is being, or has been given;
   b. participate in the process of selecting and determining the form of protection and security support;
   c. obtain information regarding case developments and court decisions;
   d. obtain integrated services that are fast, appropriate, comfortable, and in accordance with needs;
   e. recovery and social reintegration; and
   f. obtain legal, psychological, spiritual, economic, social assistance and interpreters.
2. The rights of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND AUTHORITIES OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 5

1. In providing protection to victims of gender-based violence and children, the Regional Government is obligated to:a. prevent the occurrence of violence;  
b. provide protection for victims of violence;  
c. provide recovery services and social reintegration for victims;  
d. encourage and increase community participation;  
e. cooperate with service providers in efforts to prevent, protect and recover victims of violence;  
f. supervise the provision of services to victims involving the community; dang. provide safe houses (shelters).  
2. To carry out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government has the authority to:a. formulate policies and programs on the elimination of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
b. facilitate the implementation of integrated services;  
c. provide facilities and infrastructure;  
d. increase the capacity of service provider institutions;  
e. coordinate and cooperate in the provision of protection to victims of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
f. encourage community participation; and  g. conduct monitoring and evaluation.  
3. The Regional Government in carrying out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must take into account the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives, or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

# CHAPTER V

## PROVISION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Integrated Services

#### Article 6

In providing protection to victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence, the Regional Government is assisted by Integrated Services.

#### Article 7

1. The Integrated Services as referred to in Article 6 shall be established by the Regent in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
2. The Integrated Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of members from the following elements:  a. regional government;  
b. non-governmental institutions;  
c. health service institutions;  
d. law enforcement apparatus;  
e. professional personnel;  
f. volunteer companions;  
g. social workers; dan/atauh. religious leaders.  
3. The Integrated Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall have the duties to:a. undertake prevention;  
b. recovery and social reintegration;  
c. provide legal protection;  
d. conduct coordination and cooperation;  
e. undertake efforts to increase community participation; andf. monitoring and reporting.

#### Article 8

Further provisions regarding the establishment of Integrated Services shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Prevention Efforts

#### Article 9

Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (3) letter a include:
a. conducting communication, information, and education about gender-based violence and violence against children; andb. socializing laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and violence against children.

## Part Three

Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts

#### Article 10

Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (3) letter b include:
a. providing physical recovery at health service institutions;  
b. medicolegal services;  
c. assisting the repatriation of victims;  
d. providing protection in Safe Houses (shelters), social rehabilitation centers, or other places desired by the victim;  
e. providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;  
f. providing spiritual guidance services; dang. preparing families and communities, economic empowerment, and return to school and/or other educational institutions.

## Part Four

Legal Protection

#### Article 11

Legal protection as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (3) letter c includes:
a. providing assistance during the legal process at every stage of examination; andb. providing special legal protection for child victims of violence which may be carried out through the appointment of guardianship in accordance with applicable regulations.

## Part Five

Coordination and Cooperation

#### Article 12

Coordination and cooperation as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (3) letter d include:
a. coordinating and cooperating in handling cases of violence with integrated services at the regency/city level;  
b. coordinating and cooperating with service provider institutions for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children; danc. coordinating and consulting with the Regional Government.

## Part Six

Increasing Community Participation

#### Article 13

1. Increasing Community Participation as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (3) letter e is to provide the widest possible opportunity for the community to participate in assisting the prevention and handling of gender-based violence and violence against children.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by:a. providing protection for victims; b. providing emergency assistance;  
c. providing advocacy to victims and or the community regarding the handling of cases of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
d. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection orders;  
e. providing assistance in the form of money, goods, or services; and/atauf. conveying information to the competent authorities related to cases of gender-based violence and violence against children.

## Part Seven

Monitoring and Reporting

#### Article 14

Monitoring and reporting as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (3) letter f shall be conducted on all aspects of integrated service activities in the form of supervision, documentation and evaluation of cases of gender-based violence and violence against children.

#### Article 15

The implementation of Integrated Services shall be coordinated by the relevant SKPD.

# CHAPTER VI

## ASSISTANCE

#### Article 16

Assistance shall be provided by persons or official institutions that have professional competence in their field and cooperate with Integrated Services.

# CHAPTER VII

## COOPERATION

#### Article 17

1. In providing protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children, the Regional Government cooperates with the Government, Provincial Government, and Regency/City Government, and other institutions.  
2. Cooperation between the Regional Government and the Government and Provincial Government as referred to in paragraph (1) includes consultation, coordination and reporting.  
3. Cooperation between the Regional Government and Regency/City as referred to in paragraph (1) includes coordination, advocacy, referral, repatriation, social reintegration, facilitation of integrated service system development, monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

# CHAPTER VIII

## CONTROL, GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 18

1. Control, guidance and supervision of the provision of protection to victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall be carried out by the Regent.  
2. The implementation of control, guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be assisted by the relevant SKPD.

# CHAPTER IX

## FINANCING

#### Article 19

All activities related to the provision of protection to victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall be charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget and other lawful and non-binding sources.

# CHAPTER X

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 20

Matters not yet regulated in this Regional Regulation insofar as they concern implementation shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 21

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Kudus Regency.

##### I. GENERAL

Women and children have an important role in the development of a nation. Women as mothers of the nation who will give birth to children and children as the next generation of the nation are development capital of the nation that need to obtain legal protection from acts of gender-based violence and violence against children.

Gender-based violence and violence against children are currently increasing in number. Several forms of acts of violence against women and children that occur in society can be in the form of social harassment, rape, trafficking of women and children, pornography, sexual exploitation, neglect, domestic violence. Therefore, legal instruments are needed that can guarantee the implementation of protection of the rights of women and children both at the national and regional levels.

In order to realize the protection of the rights of women and children from the threat of acts of violence as referred to above, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

Sufficiently clear

#### Article 2

Point a What is meant by "non-discrimination" is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.

Point b What is meant by "best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Point c What is meant by "gender justice" is fair treatment given to women and men.

What is meant by "gender equality" is a condition and position that describes a harmonious, aligned and balanced relationship between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in access, participation, control and benefits in the implementation of development as well as enjoying the results of development in family life as well as in society, nation and state.

Point d What is meant by "victim protection" is providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

Point e What is meant by "child survival" is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity.

What is meant by "child growth and development" is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to create and to recreate.

Point f What is meant by "respect for the child's opinion" is respect for children's rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making that can be taken into consideration, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.

Point g What is meant by "openness" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is transparent among integrated service providers.

Point h What is meant by "integration" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is carried out by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal assistance, psychologists, religious counselors, social workers, police.

Point i What is meant by "empowering" is that every effort given must be able to strengthen victims, both physically, psychologically, socially and economically.

Point j What is meant by "victim confidentiality" is every action taken to ensure victims are in a safe condition from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychology of victims.
