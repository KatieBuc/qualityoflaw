# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

1. Region is the City of Semarang.  
2. Regional Government is the Regional Head as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Semarang.  
4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.  
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regional Head and DPRD in administering governmental affairs that are the authority of the region.  
6. Regional Long-Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJPD, is a regional planning document for a period of 20 (twenty) years.  
7. Regional Medium-Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMD, is a regional planning document for a period of 5 (five) years.  
8. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  
9. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and verbal that causes danger to life, body and/or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.  
10. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.  
11. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.  
12. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on human differences on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.  
13. Trafficking in Persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, forgery, fraud and abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.  
14. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb.  
15. Victim of violence is a woman and child who experiences suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experiences neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights.  
16. Prevention is a direct effort carried out by the Regional Government and/or the community to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.  
17. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to provide services and rehabilitation to women and children who are victims of violence comprehensively including information services, assistance and legal aid, counseling services, medical services and safe houses through referral.  
18. Task Force for the Prevention and Handling of Criminal Acts of Trafficking in Persons, hereinafter referred to as the Task Force, is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating prevention and handling efforts of criminal acts of trafficking in persons in the City of Semarang.  
19. Safe house is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.  
20. Social reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, school environment, work environment and community environment that can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.  
21. Victim recovery is services provided in the form of strengthening and developing the capacity of a person who experiences acts of violence against women and children to be more empowered both physically, psychologically, sexually, economically and socially.  
22. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy and spiritual guidance, for the self-empowerment of women and children from acts of violence to resolve the problems faced.  
23. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward to the third degree.  
24. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.  
25. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of women and children from acts of violence is organized based on the principles of:

a. Humanity;  
b. Justice and gender equality;  
c. Non-discrimination;  
d. Public order and legal certainty;  
e. Transparency;  
f. Protection.

#### Article 3

The objectives of protecting women and children from acts of violence are to:

a. Prevent acts of violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;  
b. Eliminate all forms of acts of violence and exploitation against women and children;  
c. Protect, provide a sense of security for women and children;  
d. Provide services to women and children from acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses; and  
e. Empower women and children victims of acts of violence to be more empowered both physically, psychologically, socially, and economically.

# CHAPTER III

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 4

Forms of violence include:

a. Physical violence;  
b. Psychological violence;  
c. Sexual violence;  
d. Neglect;  
e. Maltreatment;  
f. Exploitation; and/or  
g. Other violence.

# CHAPTER IV

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Women and children victims of acts of violence receive the following rights:

a. Right to be respected for their dignity as human beings;  
b. Right to recovery;  
c. Right to determine their own decisions;  
d. Right to obtain information;  
e. Right to confidentiality;  
f. Right to social rehabilitation;  
g. Right to complaint handling in a fast, appropriate, comfortable and needs-based manner;  
h. Right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process;  
i. Right to assistance; and  
j. Right to a sense of security.

#### Article 6

Child victims of acts of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 5, also receive special rights, as follows:

a. Right to survival, growth and development;  
b. Right to basic population services;  
c. Right to equal protection;  
d. Right to be free from various stigma; and  
e. Right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 7

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence are the shared responsibility of:

a. Regional Government;  
b. Community.

#### Article 8

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 7 letter a, include:
   a. establishing, implementing policies, programs, and conducting cooperation activities in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
   b. facilitating the establishment of institutions for the protection of women and children from acts of violence and providing support for facilities and infrastructure;
   c. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity;
   d. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
   e. providing protection services for women and children victims of acts of violence;
   f. encouraging and increasing community participation;
   g. appointing parents and/or substitute family caregivers as a protective measure for children who are victims of acts of violence.
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Mayor shall establish programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in one Regional Action Plan as a basis for Regional Apparatus in implementing the protection of women and children from acts of violence.
3. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), constitutes part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.
4. Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 9

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 7 letter b, shall be implemented in the form of community participation.
2. The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
   a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
   b. providing protection to women and children from acts of violence;
   c. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
   d. participating in the handling of victims of acts of violence.
3. The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 10

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence against women and children is obliged to make efforts in accordance with their capacity to:

a. Prevent and stop the continuation of acts of violence;
b. Provide protection to victims;
c. Provide emergency assistance; and/or
d. Assist in the process of submitting an application for protection determination.

# CHAPTER VI

## INSTITUTIONS

#### Article 11

1. The Regional Government shall establish an Integrated Service Center (PPT) in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.
2. The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be established at the City and Sub-district levels.
3. The membership of the PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of elements of the Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, researchers and academics.
4. Further provisions regarding the Organization and Working Procedures of the PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 12

In addition to establishing the PPT as referred to in Article 11, in order to support the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence, the Mayor shall establish:

a. Task force on trafficking in persons;
b. Regional action committee for the elimination of the worst forms of child labor.

#### Article 13

1. The task force as referred to in Article 12 letter a, shall consist of representatives from the Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, and researchers/academics.
2. The task force as referred to in paragraph (1), constitutes a coordinating body tasked with coordinating the prevention and handling of trafficking in persons.
3. Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties, and functions of the task force as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 14

1. The committee as referred to in Article 12 letter b, shall consist of elements from the Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, researchers and academics.
2. Further provisions regarding the duties and functions of the committee as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 15

1. The Regional Government shall conduct empowerment and awareness to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented by way of:
   a. providing material on the prevention of acts of violence against women and children in both formal and informal education;
   b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services in accordance with applicable provisions;
   c. opening employment opportunities for women;
   d. building community participation and concern towards the prevention of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
   e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
   f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, educational institutions, and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children; and
   g. opening an integrated service system for the protection of women and children from acts of violence in every urban village.

#### Article 16

1. The prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 15, shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
   a. social affairs;
   b. health;
   c. education;
   d. manpower;
   e. population and civil registration;
   f. law;
   g. women's empowerment and child protection;
   h. cooperatives and Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises;
   i. mental and spiritual matters; and
   j. peace and order.
2. The prevention of acts of violence by Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

## Part Two

Legal Protection

#### Article 17

Legal protection includes:

a. Providing protection in a safe house (shelter);
b. Providing legal information to victims;
c. Providing assistance for victims as witnesses from the investigation process to the verdict;
d. Providing special legal protection for child victims of acts of violence may be carried out by the appointment of guardianship in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

## Part Three

Recovery

#### Article 18

Recovery includes:

a. Providing physical recovery at health service institutions;
b. Providing medicolegal services;
c. Assisting the repatriation of victims;
d. Providing temporary protection in a safe house (shelter);
e. Providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;
f. Providing spiritual guidance services;
g. Preparing the family, school, work and community environment, as well as economic empowerment.

## Part Four

Coordination

#### Article 19

Coordination includes:

a. Conducting coordination and cooperation in handling cases of violence with integrated service institutions;
b. Conducting coordination and cooperation in handling cases of violence with integrated services between regions.

## Part Five

Community Participation

#### Article 20

Community Participation is carried out by way of:
a. Fostering community awareness of cases of violence against women and children;  
b. Encouraging the community to actively participate in providing information and reporting acts of violence against women and children;  
c. Fostering local wisdom in handling cases of violence;  
d. Organizing strengthening of community groups in handling acts of violence against women and children;  
e. Disseminating information about laws and regulations related to acts of violence against women and children.

# CHAPTER VIII

## COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 21

1. The Regional Government develops cooperation patterns in order to achieve the objectives of protecting women and children from acts of violence in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out between the Regional Government and:
   a. The Central Government;  
   b. The Provincial Government;  
   c. Other Regency/City Governments;  
   d. Universities;  
   e. Social and religious institutions;  
   f. Non-Governmental Organizations; and/or  
   g. Media.  
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) includes:  
   a. exchange of data and information;  
   b. rehabilitation of victims of violence;  
   c. repatriation and social reintegration; and  
   d. provision of evidence and witnesses.  
4. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 22

1. The Regional Government may organize partnerships with the business sector in the protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
2. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through:
   a. notification of employment opportunity information for women victims of violence;  
   b. education and training for women victims of violence;  
   c. educational assistance for women and children victims of violence who have been withdrawn from their education; and  
   d. fostering and increasing the economic independence of women victims of violence.  
3. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 23

1. The Regional Government is obliged to conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
   a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;  
   b. technical guidance and training;  
   c. coordination;  
   d. monitoring;  
   e. evaluation; and  
   f. reporting.

#### Article 24

The community may conduct supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence organized by the Regional Government with a mechanism for conveying aspirations to the Mayor or to the Regional People's Representative Council (DPRD).

#### Article 25

Further provisions regarding the procedures for implementing guidance and supervision as referred to in article 23 and article 24 are regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER X

## FINANCING

#### Article 26

Financing for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence may be sourced from:
a. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget;  
b. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget;  
c. Non-binding community donations;  
d. Non-binding foreign assistance; and/or  
e. Other lawful and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 27

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations that implement the protection of women and children from discrimination and acts of violence.  
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with regional financial capacity, and is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

##### I. GENERAL

Women and children have become a serious concern of the international community, considering their vulnerable position as victims of violence from their families and environment. Various acts of violence both against women and children within the household and outside the household continue to increase from time to time. This condition, if without serious handling, will destroy the life of households, society, nation and state. Therefore, the Government of Semarang City is obliged to provide protection to women and children victims of acts of violence which is an implementation of various laws and regulations, including Law Number 39 Year 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 21 Year 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Crime of Trafficking in Persons, Law Number 35 Year 2014 Concerning Amendments to Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection.  

Protection of women and children from acts of violence is the obligation of the Regional Government in the context of regional autonomy, which is manifested in the form of a Regional Regulation as regulated in Article 236 paragraph (1), Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government.  

On that basis, the Regional Government of Semarang City has taken steps as an effort to provide protection to women and children, but has not been able to provide maximum and adequate protection against acts of violence that occur in the social life of the community, especially to women and children, among others caused by the still low understanding of the community regarding the rights of women and children, handling has not been well coordinated, implementation has not been sustainable, and so on. Therefore, a regulation in the form of a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children is needed to answer while providing legal certainty in the protection of women and children victims from acts of violence that occur.  

The existence of a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children, gives responsibility to the Regional Government starting from the prevention of acts of violence to the handling of victims of acts of violence. In its implementation the Regional Government cooperates with government agencies, other regional governments and the community. In addition, adequate funding support both from the government, Regional Government and community participation of the business sector and the community, it is hoped that violence against women and children can be reduced and eventually will be eliminated from Semarang City.

#### Article 2

Point a The principle of humanity serves as the foundation for the concept of protection of women and children victims of violence, constituting respect for human rights and the dignity of every citizen and resident of Indonesia proportionally (second principle of Pancasila).

Point b The principle of justice and gender equality, that gender justice is a process to be fair to men and women. Gender equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, governmental activities and equality in enjoying the results of development.

Point c The principle of non-discrimination, that in providing protection to women and children victims of violence there is no differentiation based on age, sex, race, ethnicity, tribe, religion and between groups.

Point d The principle of order and legal certainty can create order in society and protection for women and children victims of violence through the guarantee of legal certainty.

Point e The principle of openness reflects the implementation of protection for victims of violence in a transparent manner.

Point f The principle of protection provides protection to victims of violence, guarantees confidentiality, security, safety of life and psychological well-being of victims.

#### Article 3

#### Article 4

Point a Physical violence is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Point b Psychological violence is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Point c Sexual violence is caused by:

1. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
2. forced sexual intercourse;
3. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
4. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Point d Neglect is caused by:

1. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
2. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
3. acts of neglecting a person within their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they must provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
4. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Point e Abuse/mistreatment is abuse/mistreatment of a child's physical and emotional state including neglecting their education and health as well as sexual abuse resulting from human misconduct toward the child.

Point f Exploitation is caused by:

1. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
2. acts with or without the victim's consent including prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
3. any form of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

Point g Other violence is caused by:

1. threats of violence include: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that create fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and
2. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is made to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.
