# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by: Region is Tambrauw Regency.  

Regional Government is the Regional Head as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  

Regent is the Regent of Tambrauw.  

Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.  

Discrimination is any distinction, harassment and exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on human differentiation on the grounds of religion, tribe, race, ethnicity, social status group, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural fields and other aspects of life.  

Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.  

Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether in the domestic sphere or public in private life.  

Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexual, psychological, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.  

Domestic violence is any act against a person, especially women, that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts of coercion or unlawful deprivation of liberty within the scope of the household.  

Trafficking in Persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by means of threat of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud and abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payments or benefits, so as to obtain the consent of a person having control over another person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in a person being exploited.  

Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age including a child in the womb.  

Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person recognized by law as a woman.  

Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.  

Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.  

Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or fainting and/or causes death.  

Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, distrust or severe psychological suffering to a person.  

Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwilling with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  

Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence.  

Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.  

Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.  

Assistant is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.  

Institution is an agency/institution/body/within the scope of regional government and/or Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) that provides assistance.  

Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an institution providing services to victims of violence based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psychosocial and legal services.  

Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.  

Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.  

Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service forum established by the Regional Government in the context of protection services for victims of violence against women and children in Tambrauw Regency.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The Implementation and Protection of Women and Children victims of acts of violence is carried out based on the principles of: Humanity;  

Respect for the rights of victims;  

Justice and gender equality Protection (Pengayoman);  

Non-discrimination; and Best interests of the victim.

#### Article 3

The objectives of protection of women and children victims of acts of violence are to: Prevent acts of violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;  

Eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;  

Protect, provide a sense of security for women and children victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses;  

Provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence;  

Facilitate and mediate cases of domestic violence to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household; and Empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 4

Women and children victims of acts of violence receive the following rights: The right to be respected for their dignity as human beings;  

The right to health and psychological services for the suffering experienced by victims;  

The right to legal assistance;  

The right to obtain information;  

The right to confidential handling;  

The right to social rehabilitation;  

The right to complaint handling;  

The right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and Guarantee of rights related to status as family members and society.

#### Article 5

Child victims of acts of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 4, also receive special rights as follows: The right to survival, growth and development;  

The right to basic service needs;  

The right to equal protection; and The right to obtain freedom;

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 6

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence are the joint responsibility of: regional government;  

community;  

family; and parents.

#### Article 7

The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 6 letter a, include: establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;

conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;

providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity.

The duties of the Regional Government in the protection of women and children from acts of violence are in the form of: implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;

collecting data and information about women and children in the context of efforts to protect them from acts of violence;

socializing laws and regulations related to the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence; and conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.

#### Article 8

The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation.

The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes: preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;

providing protection to women and children from acts of violence, in the form of establishing rules and regulations in their respective areas starting from the Neighborhood Association level;

providing information and or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties;

participating in the handling of victims of acts of violence; and accompanying victims to service units.

The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out responsibly in accordance with laws and regulations.

#### Article 9

The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER V

## PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 10

To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government conducts socialization, empowerment, and awareness to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and / or counseling.

In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government undertakes efforts as follows: increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal as well as informal;

opening employment opportunities for women;

building community participation and concern for the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence; and building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children.

#### Article 11

The prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 10, is implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of: social affairs;

health;

education;

manpower;

women's empowerment and child protection;

mental and spiritual; and public order and security.

The prevention of acts of violence by SKPD as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner.

##### CHAPTERVI

##### SERVICES FOR VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 12

Forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence, as follows: complaint services;

Medical services; and medicolegal services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field.

Legal aid services: independence or empowerment services; and social rehabilitation services.

The forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented by relevant SKPD in accordance with the programs, activities, and budgets available in each in the fields of: social affairs;

health;

women's empowerment and child protection;

mental and spiritual;

manpower; and education.

# CHAPTER VII

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article  13

In the context of providing protection services to women and children from victims of violence, the Regional Government establishes P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children from acts of violence including victims of acts of violence.

P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) functions as an integrated service center in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.

P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) above consists of the Health Office, Hospital, Police, Social Affairs Office, Manpower Office, Women's Empowerment and Family Planning Office, and Non-Governmental Organizations.

P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (3), namely the Community, Social Community Organizations, and Higher Education Institutions, is stipulated by Regent's Decree.

P2TP2A of Tambrauw Regency in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with service unit networks and competent parties in making efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence.

#### Article 14

For the implementation of services for victims of trafficking in persons, the Regent of Tambrauw establishes a task force on the criminal act of trafficking in persons.

The task force on the criminal act of trafficking in persons as referred to in paragraph (1) is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating the prevention and handling of trafficking in persons.

Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties, and functions of the task force on the criminal act of trafficking in persons as referred to in paragraph (1) are established by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 15

In the context of preventing acts of violence against children including the protection of children from the worst forms of child labor, a committee for the elimination of acts of violence against children shall be established by Regent's Decree.

Further provisions regarding the duties and functions of the committee for the elimination of acts of violence against children as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent's Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

## REPORTING

#### Article 16

P2TP2A of Tambrauw Regency is obliged to report the implementation of the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence to the Regent.

Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: Administration;

Finance;

Services; and Performance.

Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out at least once every 3 (three) months.

# CHAPTER IX

## FINANCING

#### Article 17

Financing for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence is sourced from: Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBD) of Tambrauw Regency, and Other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 18

The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations that implement the protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination.

Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with regional financial capacity, and is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

# CHAPTER X

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 19

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

So that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Tambrauw Regency.

##### I. GENERAL

The State has the obligation to provide Protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. That Women and Children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience violence and therefore need to receive protection. Violence against Women and Children constitutes a violation of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed without discrimination.  

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, Violence against Women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or hardship to Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child. The existence of Women and Children Victims of Violence has not yet received adequate services so that minimum services for Victims of Violence are required.  

In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in Badung Regency so that they are protected from Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.  

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children Victims of Violence particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of Women and Children Victims of Violence in Badung Regency.
