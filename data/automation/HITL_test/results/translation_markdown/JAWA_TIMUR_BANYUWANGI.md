# CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE AND HUMAN TRAFFICKING

# BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

# THE REGENT OF BANYUWANGI,

Considering:  
a. that violence against women and children as well as human trafficking constitute acts that degrade human dignity and the degree of humanity, therefore they need to be provided with adequate services and protection;  
b. that the level of human trafficking and violence against women and children is still high, therefore it is the obligation of the regional government to regulate and serve the interests of the community, in this case women and children victims of violence and human trafficking;  
c. that in order for the handling of human trafficking and violence against women and children to be implemented effectively and efficiently, it is necessary to regulate the protection of women and children victims of violence and human trafficking;  
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b and c, it is necessary to establish a regional regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence and Human Trafficking.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

1. Region is Banyuwangi Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration.  
3. Head of Region is the Regent of Banyuwangi.  
4. SKPD responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection is the Regional Apparatus Organization that has the main duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment and child protection in Banyuwangi Regency.  
5. Woman is a person of female gender.  
6. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child still in the womb.  
7. Trafficking in persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, dispatch, transfer, relocation, or receipt of a person by means of threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage, or giving payments or benefits so as to obtain the consent of a person having control over another person, whether conducted within the country or between countries for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in a person being exploited.  
8. Rights of the child are part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and fulfilled by parents, family, community, government, and the state.  
9. Violence is any act unlawfully, with or without the use of means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life, body, or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.  
10. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, mentally, socially, psychosocially, and sexually.  
11. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, or wounds to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting, and/or causes death.  
12. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.  
13. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  
14. Victim is a woman including female workers working abroad and children who experience misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of such misery.  
15. Exploitation is an action with or without the victim's consent which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.  
16. Sexual exploitation is any form of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution and indecency.  
17. Recruitment is an action that includes inviting, gathering, bringing, or separating a person from their family or community.  
18. Dispatch is the act of dispatching or disembarking/landing a person from one place to another place.  
19. Debt bondage is the act of placing a person in a status or condition of pledging or being forced to pledge themselves or their family or people under their responsibility or their personal services as a form of debt repayment.  
20. Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.  
21. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and the fulfillment of their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from acts of violence and discrimination.  
22. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing, and knowing that violence against the victim is occurring or has occurred.  
23. Assistant is a person who has the expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.  
24. Social worker is a person who has the expertise to listen empathetically and explore problems to provide counseling in efforts to strengthen the victim psychologically.  
25. Commission for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter referred to as KPPA is an institution that handles the protection of women and children whose membership consists of relevant agencies, NGOs and Academics.  
26. Medicolegal is an effort to collect evidence for the purposes of proof in the judicial process.  
27. Women Crisis Center is a service center for women and child victims of violence.  
28. Safe House is a temporary residence for victims, in order to obtain a sense of security and not be reached by the perpetrator or the perpetrator's messenger, while their case has not been resolved.  
29. Restitution is the payment of compensation charged to the perpetrator based on a court decision that has permanent legal force for material and/or immaterial losses suffered by the victim or their heirs.  
30. Rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological, and social conditions in order to carry out their role again properly both in the family and in society.  
31. Reintegration is the process of reuniting victims with their family, or substitute family and community supported by the provision of educational assistance or skills enhancement and assistance.
32. Standard Operating Procedure hereinafter referred to as SPO is a procedure that serves as a reference for service actions established by Regent's Decree.
33. Discrimination against women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex, which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women;  
34. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service center in efforts to empower women in various fields of development, as well as protection of women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons, established by the government or community-based, which includes: referral centers, business consultation centers, reproductive health consultation centers, legal consultation centers, integrated crisis centers (PKT), integrated service centers (PPT), trauma recovery centers (trauma centers), women's crisis handling centers (women crisis centers), training centers, safe houses (shelters), transit houses, or other forms.  
35. Community institution is an institution formed by members of the Indonesian citizen community voluntarily on the basis of shared vision, mission, profession, function and activities to participate in development in order to achieve national goals within the framework of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia based on Pancasila, consisting of religious organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, private organizations, social organizations, political organizations, mass media, and other forms of organizations.  
36. Companion institution is an institution that provides assistance to women and child victims of violence and trafficking in persons in Banyuwangi Regency.  
37. Empathy is a mental condition that makes a person feel themselves to be in the same state of feeling or thought as the person or group who is a victim of violence and trafficking in persons.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

## Part One

Principles

#### Article 2

The principles in the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons are:
a. Legal Certainty;  
b. Justice and Gender Equality;  
c. Non-discrimination;  
d. Best interests of victims;  
e. Respect for the rights of women and children as fulfillment of Human Rights.

## Part Two

Objectives

#### Article 3

The objectives of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence and Trafficking in Persons are:
a. To protect women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons;  
b. To take action against perpetrators of violence and trafficking in persons against women and children;  
c. To provide a sense of security to women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons;  
d. To restore the physical, psychological, social, and economic conditions of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons;  
e. To ensure comprehensive services in implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF VICTIMS AND COMPANIONS

## Part One

Rights of Victims

#### Article 4

Every victim has the right to:
a. obtain protection from individuals, groups or regional government as well as private institutions;  
b. obtain information about the location of complaint centers, KPPA, companions, social workers and religious counselors, psychologists and psychiatrists, from individuals, groups, or government, regional government and national or international private institutions;  
c. obtain integrated services in accordance with applicable laws and regulations;  
d. file claims and/or legal suits through judicial bodies, and may report to the International Court;  
e. obtain restitution or compensation payments;  
f. obtain information about laws and regulations that protect victims;  
g. special handling related to victim confidentiality;  
h. obtain information and be involved in every decision-making process related to assistance and development of case services;  
i. obtain guarantees of their rights related to their status as wife, mother or child and household member, as well as community member;  
j. obtain psychological, medical, spiritual and legal assistance at every level of examination and during the judicial process;  
k. obtain continuous handling up to the rehabilitation and reintegration stage.

#### Article 5

Restitution or compensation payments as referred to in Article 4 letter e include:
a. loss of wealth or income;  
b. suffering;  
c. costs for medical and/or psychological treatment;  
d. other losses suffered by victims as a result of trafficking in persons and violence.

## Part Two

Obligations of Companion Institutions

#### Article 6

Companion Institutions are obligated to:
a. encourage victims to provide information to the authorities and be willing to become witnesses;  
b. facilitate victims to be involved in efforts to prevent additional victims together with the community.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

Obligations and Responsibilities of Regional Government

#### Article 7

Regional government is obligated and responsible to:
a. carry out all efforts to prevent the occurrence of criminal acts of trafficking in persons, and violence against women and children;  
b. provide and organize integrated services for victims;  
c. guarantee the implementation of protection for victims with due regard to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons legally responsible for victims;  
d. supervise the implementation of services to victims, with service standards involving the community;  
e. Open the widest possible access to information to the community, especially to women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons.

## Part Two

Obligations and Community Participation

#### Article 8

The community participates to carry out:
a. efforts to prevent the occurrence of criminal acts of trafficking in persons and violence;  
b. supervision and reporting of the occurrence of criminal acts of trafficking in persons and violence to the competent authorities;  
c. proposals regarding the formulation and policies on protection;  
d. protection efforts and moral and/or material support to victims.

# CHAPTER V

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Protection System

#### Article 9

1. The system for implementing protection for victims of trafficking in persons and violence against women and children is carried out through 2 (two) methods, namely:
   a. Preventive or prevention method; and
   b. Repressive or handling method
2. The preventive or prevention method is carried out through various forms of protection, both by the community and the government, which are intended to prevent the occurrence of criminal acts of trafficking in persons and violence or victims of violence against women and children.  
3. The repressive or handling method is carried out through various forms of protection carried out by the government through regulatory activities, implementation and enforcement of laws and regulations governing the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence and Trafficking in Persons.  
4. Guidelines and Technical Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence and Trafficking in Persons are further regulated through Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Institutional

#### Article 10

1. The implementation of protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the P2TP2A or similar institution.  
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is established by Regent's decree.  
3. P2TP2A is located in Banyuwangi regency.

## Part Three

Service Principles

#### Article 11

The implementation of services to victims is carried out with the following principles:
a. Free of charge;  
b. Fast;  
c. Safe;  
d. Empathy;  
e. Non-judgmental;  
f. Non-discrimination;  
g. Easily accessible;  
h. Confidential; and  
i. Integrated.

## Part Four

Forms And Service Mechanisms

#### Article 12

Forms of services to victims organized by P2TP2A include:
a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;  
b. medicolegal services are a form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;  
c. psychosocial services are services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various forms of threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength, and independence in resolving their problems;  
d. legal services are services to assist victims in carrying out the judicial process;  
e. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent;  
f. spiritual services include providing explanations about rights, obligations and strengthening faith and piety;  
g. continuous services at the rehabilitation and reintegration stage.

#### Article 13

Service mechanisms are organized according to SOPs which are subsequently regulated in Regent Regulation.

## Part Five

Assistance

#### Article 14

1. Assistance includes all integrated efforts to restore and strengthen the condition of victims carried out by the Police, Prosecutor's Office, Courts, Advocates and social workers, Women's Crisis Center, women's organizations and social organizations concerned with women and children;  
2. Assistance by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, and advocates as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with laws and regulations.

#### Article 15

1. The duties of Social Workers as companions are: a. to provide sufficient information to victims about their rights;  
   b. to build an equal relationship with victims so they are willing to open up in expressing their problems;  
   c. to ask what they want to do and what assistance is needed;  
   d. to provide information and connect with institutions or individuals who can help overcome problems;  
   e. to help provide information about legal consultation services;  
   f. to accompany victims during the medicological process;  
   g. to accompany victims during the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office, and Court;  
   h. to monitor the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office, and Court;  
   i. to maintain the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including coverage by mass media;  
   j. to coordinate with other companions;  
   k. to provide continuous handling/assistance up to the rehabilitation and reintegration stage
(2) Services to victims are carried out in an integrated and continuous manner by service providers.

# CHAPTER VI

## PLANNING

#### Article 16

The Regent integrates policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women into regional development planning.

#### Article 17

1. The integration of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons is incorporated into planning and budget documents which include the Regional Long-Term Development Plan (RPJPD), Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD), Strategic Plan of Regional Work Units (Renstra SKPD), Regional Government Work Plan (RKPD), and Work Plan and Budget of Regional Work Units (RKA-SKPD) and related SKPD RKA.  
2. The preparation of planning and budget documents as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be harmonized among related Regional Work Units (SKPD).  
3. The SKPD in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection together with P2TP2A shall facilitate the integration of policies, programs, and activities for empowerment, protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons into the preparation of planning and budget documents as set forth in paragraph (1).  
4. In implementing as referred to in paragraph (3), the SKPD in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection together with P2TP2A shall coordinate with the Regional Development Planning Agency.

# CHAPTER VII

## IMPLEMENTATION

#### Article 18

1. The Regent implements policies, programs, and activities to eliminate all forms of discrimination and acts of violence against women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons.  
2. The implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be coordinated by the Vice Regent.  
3. The Office of Women's Empowerment and Family Planning or the SKPD in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection together with P2TP2A shall implement the policies, programs and activities that have been established, and in their implementation cooperate with related SKPDs, law enforcement, community institutions, universities, and civil society.  
4. The implementation as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be in the form of: policy analysis, coordination, advocacy, socialization, communication, information and education (KIE), training, service facilitation, service provision, development of women and child protection models and other forms.

#### Article 19

1. The implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons shall be carried out by forming, developing, strengthening, or utilizing task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions.  
2. The membership of task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of related SKPDs, law enforcement, community institutions, universities, and civil society.  
3. The formation of task forces, forums, working groups, or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be established by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 20

1. In implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons, the district government may cooperate with international institutions.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 21

The Regent in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons, shall undertake efforts to:

a. coordinate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities among SKPDs;  
b. cooperate with other districts and cities within one province, and with districts and cities in other provinces, in the implementation of policies, programs and activities in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
c. facilitate and provide services;  
d. implement affirmative action for the protection of women and children and trafficking in persons; and  
e. prepare a data system for the protection of women and children, including a data system for violence against women and children and trafficking in persons.

#### Article 22

1. Facilitation and provision of services as referred to in Article 21 letter c, shall be in the form of victim identification, legal aid, medical rehabilitation, psychosocial rehabilitation, social reintegration, repatriation assistance, or other forms.  
2. Facilitation and provision of services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided for women and children victims of violence including victims of trafficking in persons, women and children in conflict and disaster-prone areas, women and child workers, elderly women, women and children with disabilities, women and children victims of sexual exploitation, women heads of households, and women and children in other vulnerable groups.

#### Article 23

Facilitation and provision of services as referred to in article 22 paragraph (2) shall be carried out to avoid all forms of discrimination and acts of violence which are implemented through P2TP2A, or other similar institutions.

# CHAPTER VIII

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 24

1. To ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons, the District Government shall conduct monitoring.  
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities that have been established.  
3. Monitoring shall be carried out periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of SKPDs that implement policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons.  
4. Monitoring shall be carried out from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons for the current year.

#### Article 25

1. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons shall be carried out at the end of each fiscal year.  
2. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons shall be used as input for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women for the following year.  
3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

#### Article 26

1. The Regency Government shall prepare Minimum Service Standards for protection activities for women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons.  
2. The Minimum Service Standards as referred to in paragraph (1), shall include services: a. handling complaints/reports of victims of violence against women and children;  
   b. health services for women and children victims of violence;  
   c. social rehabilitation for women and children victims of violence;  
   d. law enforcement and legal assistance for women and children victims of violence; and e. repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER X

## FUNDING

#### Article 27

All funds required for protection activities for women and children victims of violence/trafficking in persons, including the recovery process, shall be charged to the Regional Budget of Banyuwangi Regency, as well as other lawful and non-binding receipts.

# CHAPTER XI

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 28

1. The Regional Head shall conduct guidance and supervision to the SKPD responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons.  
2. The Regional Head through the SKPD responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, shall conduct guidance and supervision to P2TP2A in implementing protection activities for women and children victims of violence and trafficking in persons.

# CHAPTER XII

## CRIMINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

Any person who commits a criminal act of violence that is physical, psychological, sexual in nature, neglect, trafficking in persons, exploitation or that falls under the category of trafficking in persons, as well as other violence, shall be subject to criminal penalties as regulated in the applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XIII

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 30

1. In the event that the P2TP2A institution has not yet been formed, then all implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence and Trafficking in Persons shall be carried out by KPPA.  
2. The P2TP2A institution must be formed no later than 1 (one) year from the date this Regional Regulation is stipulated.

# CHAPTER XIV

Matters not yet regulated in this Regional Regulation insofar as concerning its implementation shall be further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 32

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE AND TRAFFICKING IN PERSONS

##### I. GENERAL ELUCIDATION

The practice of exploitation and violence against women and children including trafficking in persons has recently continued to increase in number. Perpetrators of exploitation and violence are general in nature, having no relevance to level of education, occupation, and income. Perpetrators of violence also have no connection with social status, religion and belief, as well as particular nationality, ethnicity, or race. One of the causes among them is the patriarchal cultural factor that views women as lower than men. Violence against women and children can and will continue to occur as long as the imbalance in the relationship between men and women is still believed and manifested in social life.  

That violence and trafficking in persons that has occurred so far is like the iceberg theory, meaning that cases that are recorded and reported are only the number on the surface, while far below the surface there are many more that are not recorded and not reached by assistance nor receive serious attention from the authorities.  

According to a report by the American Center for International Labor Solidarity (ACILS) and the Indonesian Child Countermeasures Network (JARAK), specifically for East Java Province, areas that are vulnerable and have potential for women and child trafficking are Banyuwangi, Malang, Blitar, Tulungagung, and Trenggalek. Therefore there is a need for firm law enforcement, because in addition to the state, the Regional Government also has the obligation and responsibility to prevent and sanction acts of trafficking in women and children, as well as provide protection, assistance, healing and compensation to victims of violations.  

Thus it is expected that the main objective of the preparation of this Draft Regional Regulation can be achieved properly, namely to provide a legal basis for efforts to protect Women and Children Victims of Violence and Trafficking in Persons and to ensure the implementation of the regional government's obligations in carrying out the mandate of laws and regulations in preventing and combating violence and trafficking.

##### II. ELUCIDATION ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1
