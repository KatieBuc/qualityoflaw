## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article I

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Buoati is the Regent of Donggala.
2. The Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of Donggala Regency as an element of regional government administration.
3. Regional Government is the Regent of Donggala and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
4. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is a regional apparatus work unit of Donggala Regency that handles the interests of women and children.
5. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as UKPD, is a work unit of regional apparatus located within the SKPD of Donggala Regency.

7. Woman is a human being of the female sex.

Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.

Protection is all actions and services to fulfill rights, as well as guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence.

10. Child protection is all efforts to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

Referral is an effort made to protect victims of violence.

Violence against women and children is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering and physical, sexual, psychological suffering, including threats of certain acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.

Physical violence is an act that results in pain, injury, or causes miscarriage or unconsciousness.

14. Psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, a sense of helplessness or severe psychological suffering in a person.

Sexual violence is the coercion of sexual relations, coercion of sexual relations with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

16. Victim of acts of violence, hereinafter referred to as victim, is an individual or group of people who suffer physically, mentally or economic loss, emotional, deprivation of basic rights as a result of an act of violation of human rights (HAM), or a criminal act and acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or regional government apparatus or other persons.

17.

Service is an immediate activity and action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.

Assistance is an activity and action carried out by an assistant

18. during the service process.

19.

Repatriation is an effort to return victims of acts of violence to their family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.

Institution is a non-governmental organization (NGO) that provides assistance.

20. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related agencies and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children who are victims of acts of violence.

Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.

Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or adoptive mother.

Supervision is an activity of observing, listening, examining, reviewing, assessing the implementation of protection of women and children.

## PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection of women and children is carried out based on the principles of:
 a. respect for the rights of women and children;  
 b. non-discrimination:c. justice.

#### Article 3

The objectives of protection of women and children are to:
 a. provide and increase a sense of security for women and children;  
 b. help prevent acts of violence against women and children; c. help prevent exploitation activities against women and children; d. provide services to women and children in facing problems.  
 e. help improve the quality of life of children

#### Article 4

The scope of regulation of protection for women and children includes: a. Rights of women and children;  
 b. Duties and authoritiesc. Handling of acts of violence;  
 e. Financing;  
 f. Supervision;  
 g. Community participation.

## FULFILLMENT OF THE RIGHTS OF CHILDREN AND WOMEN

## Part One

Rights of Children

#### Article 5

In the implementation of child protection in the region, children have the right:
 a. to live, grow and develop and participate reasonably in accordance with human dignity and worth and receive guidance and protection from violence, negligence and discrimination;  
 b. to a valid birth certificate obtained and/or sought by their parents in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations; c. to worship according to their religion, think and express themselves in accordance with their level of intelligence and age under parental guidance;  
 d. to know, be raised and cared for by their own parents; e. in the event that for some reason parents cannot guarantee the growth and development of the child or the child is in a neglected state, then the child has the right to be cared for or adopted as a foster child or adopted child by another person in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations; f. to obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual and social needs; g. to obtain education and teaching according to their abilities, interests and talents: h. to express opinions and have their opinions heard; a. to rest and utilize time to develop talents and abilities: j. to receive protection from family, community and Regional Government against discriminatory treatment and exploitation both economically and socially, neglect, cruelty, violence, abuse, injustice and other mistreatment;  
 k. to regain formal or non-formal education for children who drop out of school;l. to receive exclusive breast milk (ASI) as a priority;  

 m. to receive guidance
 n. to obtain other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER V

## HANDLING ACTS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST

## WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 20

1. The regional government is obliged to prepare medical personnel at community health centers (Puskesmas) or in hospitals that specifically handle victims.  
2. Medical personnel as referred to in paragraph (1) are obliged to provide complete health services to victims.

#### Article 21

The regional government is obliged to assist in providing adequate special rooms for victims at the Resort Police Station (Polres) and at every Sector Police station.

#### Article 22

1. Social workers or volunteer personnel as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (1) letter b are tasked with accompanying victims in facing the process of resolving acts of violence.  
2. Social workers or volunteer personnel, in addition to being tasked as referred to in paragraph (1), carry out activities including:  
   a. counseling or guidance to strengthen and provide a sense of security for victims;  
   b. providing information regarding the rights of victims to obtain protection from the police and the establishment of protection orders from the court;  
   c. escorting victims to safe houses or alternative residences; and  
   d. conducting integrated coordination in providing services to victims with agencies or related parties needed by victims.

#### Article 23

1. The regional government is obliged to prepare Safe Houses to be used in temporary handling for victims of violence against women and children.  
2. Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (2) are provided in every Sub-district.

#### Article 24

Advocates as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (1) letter c assist:  

1. in providing legal services for victims or providing legal consultation in the form of information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;  
2. Advocates are obliged to accompany and/or represent victims at the investigation level, prosecution, and examination in court hearings and assist victims to fully describe the violence they have experienced.  
3. Information on violence from victims that is private in nature must be kept confidential by advocates.  
4. Advocates may coordinate with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 25

Clergy as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (1) letter d are tasked:  

1. with providing spiritual guidance or strengthening faith and piety to victims.  
2. In addition to providing spiritual guidance, clergy are obliged to provide explanations regarding the rights and obligations of victims.

# CHAPTER VI

## FINANCING

#### Article 26

1. Activity costs related to the implementation of women’s and children’s protection are charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget as well as other lawful receipts in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
2. Expenditure for activities sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget is budgeted at the Regional Apparatus Work Unit that handles the affairs of implementing women’s and children’s protection in accordance with the needs and financial capacity of the Region.  
3. Budget allocation as referred to in paragraph (1) must be budgeted by the Regent and DPRD and stipulated every fiscal year in the APBD.

# CHAPTER VII

## SUPERVISION AND GUIDANCE

#### Article 27

1. Supervision and guidance of the implementation of women’s and children’s protection is carried out by the Regent.  
2. Supervision and guidance activities as referred to in paragraph (1) on a day-to-day basis are carried out by the Regional Apparatus Work Unit that handles child protection.  
3. Results of supervision and guidance activities by the Regional Apparatus Work Unit must be reported to the Regent.

##### I. GENERAL

The state through the government and regional governments currently places very great attention on the existence of children. Based on their existence as the next generation of a quality and dignified nation, the government and regional governments are obliged to recognize the existence of children's rights in their humanity. The human rights inherent in children include basic rights as human beings, namely the Right to Life, the Right to Grow and Develop, the Right to Protection and the Right to Participation. To fulfill the Right to life, children need food in sufficient quantities, healthy and nutritious, as well as optimal health access. In supporting the realization of children to grow and develop, children need various adequate educational facilities and infrastructure, development of talents and interests. In order to realize the right to protection and the right to participation, children need serious attention from various parties, both the government, regional governments and the community.  

Specifically regarding protection efforts from violence that threatens the safety and health of children and women must be provided from an early age. Protection is needed to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. For example, women and children who are victims of abuse, neglect, eviction and mistreatment by parents or others. For women and children experiencing such problems, the government must provide Safe Houses and adequate budgets, the role of various parties to help handle violence problems faced by women and children including social community organizations, volunteers, social workers, medical personnel, advocates and clergy.  

The condition of children's decline along with their rights and the condition of violence against women and children that often occurs in the region should be a common concern for all stakeholders, including regional governments, to play a role in preventing and overcoming such decline and violence.  

For the regional government of Donggala Regency in facing the universe of conditions above, various actions are taken to fulfill children's rights and: prevent and assist in handling acts of violence against women and children which are endeavored by establishing a Regional Regulation on Protection for Women and Children.
