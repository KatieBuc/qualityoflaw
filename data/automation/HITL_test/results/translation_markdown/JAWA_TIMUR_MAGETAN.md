#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Magetan Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Government of Magetan Regency.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Magetan.  
4. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.  
5. Woman is a human being of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.  
6. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.  
7. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.  
8. Protection is all preventive, service and empowerment actions to protect and guarantee the rights of women and children from acts of violence.  
9. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.  
10. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing of the impending, ongoing or occurred violence against victims.  
11. Empowerment is the strengthening of women victims of violence to be able to work and earn a living independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.  
12. Companion is a person who has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.  
13. Accompaniment is activities and actions carried out by companions to women and child victims of violence during the service and empowerment process.  
14. Counseling is the provision of assistance by an expert/trained person in such a way that the psychological understanding and ability of the victim and/or perpetrator increases in solving the problems faced;  
15. Integrated Service Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is technical service units in the Region that carry out integrated service functions for women and child victims of crime and/or violence, including as a center for information on women's empowerment and child protection.  
16. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is one form of UPT established by the Regional Government that functions as an information center, service center and empowerment center for women and children. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.  
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children.  
18. Household is family members and relatives including among others grandchildren, nieces/nephews, older siblings, younger siblings, grandfathers, grandmothers, cousins and non-relatives including among others domestic helpers and drivers who live and eat from 1 (one) kitchen and reside in 1 (one) house.

## Part Two

### PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence is based on:
a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of victims;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination;  
d. best interests of victims; and e. empowerment.

#### Article 3

This Regional Regulation aims to:
a. prevent all forms of violence against women and children;  
b. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;  
c. provide services to women and child victims of acts of violence;  
d. provide protection to reporters and witnesses; and e. carry out empowerment of women and fulfillment of the rights of child victims of violence.

# CHAPTER II

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 4

Forms of violence include:
a. physical violence;  
b. psychological violence;  
c. sexual violence;  
d. neglect;  
e. exploitation; and/or f. other violence.

# CHAPTER III

## RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 5

The implementation of protection for victims of violence is the joint responsibility of:

a. regional government;  

b. community;  

c. family; and

d. parents.

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government is responsible for implementing efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence and protection of victims of violence.  
2. Prevention of the occurrence of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of: a. collecting data and information about women and children as well as related laws and regulations;  
b. conducting education on anti-violence values; and c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of prevention and protection of victims of violence.  

Protection of victims of violence as referred to (3) in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving community elements;  
b. facilitating accompaniment, legal aid and legal services according to the needs of victims;  
c. providing housing both safe houses and alternative housing along with handling mechanisms, psychosocial and spiritual services;  
d. carrying out continuous handling up to the rehabilitation and social reintegration stage;  
e. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for victims of violence; and f. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims of violence.  

In order to carry out the responsibilities (4) as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government establishes programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in one Regional Action Plan for the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence by referring to the Regional Action Plan for the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence of East Java Province.  

The Regional Action Plan for the Protection of Women (5) and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (2) is established for a period of 3 (three) years by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 7

The responsibility of the community as referred to in Article 5 letter b is implemented in the form of: a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children; and
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties.

#### Article 8

Family and/or parents as referred to in Article 5 letters c and d have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore women and children need to be protected in their dignity and honor and guaranteed their right to life in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

Violence against women and children not only causes physical impact but also psychological impact which in general can affect the quality of life of women and children victims of violence.

On a national scale, the Government of Indonesia has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 of 1948 concerning the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women). In addition, the Government has also enacted Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 297, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5606) and Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419).

Furthermore, based on Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 and Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government as last amended several times by Law Number 9 of 2015, women's empowerment and child protection constitute governmental affairs that must be implemented by Regional Governments.

As occurs in various regions, including Magetan Regency, there is currently a phenomenon of increasing acts of violence against women and children. To address this matter, regional regulations are needed that provide a basis for Regional Government and/or the community in realizing protection, so that victims are prevented and freed from violence or threats of violence, torture, or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Magetan Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of Regional Regulations. Therefore, policies are needed that can guarantee protection to free them from acts of violence, as well as improve their quality of life. Based on the considerations mentioned above, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation of Magetan Regency concerning the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

#### Article 4

Letter a Physical violence referred to is caused by acts that result in pain, illness, or serious injury and or result in injury, or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Forms of physical violence include slapping, hitting, spitting, pulling hair/grabbing, kicking, burning with hot objects, hitting/injuring with weapons.

Letter b Psychological violence referred to is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Forms of psychological violence include emotional abuse such as insults, hurtful or demeaning comments, isolating the wife from the outside world, threatening or intimidating as a means of imposing one's will.

Letter c Sexual violence referred to is caused by:

a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;  

b. forced sexual intercourse;  

c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or

d. forced sexual intercourse with other persons for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Letter d Neglect referred to is caused by:

a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  

b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;  

c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of one's household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or

d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Letter e Exploitation referred to is caused by:

a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  

b. acts with or without the victim's consent including prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or

c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

Letter f Other violence referred to is caused by:

a. threats of violence include: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and/or

b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is made to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.
