CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

First Section
Definitions

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. City Region hereinafter referred to as Region is the Cirebon City Region.

2. City Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.

3. Mayor is the Mayor of Cirebon.

4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and the Regional People's Representative Council in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the region.

5. Office is the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

6. Women's Empowerment is an effort to obtain access and control over economic, political, social, and cultural resources, so that women can manage themselves and increase self-confidence to be able to play a role and actively participate in solving problems, so as to be able to build capacity and self-concept.

7. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.

8. Women Victims are women who experience physical, psychological, sexual, economic, social suffering, and other losses resulting from gender-based violence, exploitation and/or discrimination that occurs both in the public sphere and in the sphere of personal life.

9. Vulnerable Women are women who live in conditions at risk of experiencing violence, exploitation and discrimination due to age, disability, poverty, geography, disaster, education, inequality and other conditions so that they require special protection and support.

10. Prevention is a direct effort made by the City Regional Government to prevent acts of violence against women.

11. Discrimination Against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status on the basis of equality between men and women.

12. Violence Against Women is any act based on gender differences that results in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, economic and social exploitation, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in personal life.

13. Exploitation is an action with or without the consent of the victim which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, physical, sexual, reproductive organ exploitation, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits.

14. Services are all efforts and actions to fulfill the rights of women victims of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women which include complaint services, health, social rehabilitation, legal assistance and enforcement, repatriation, social reintegration, and restitution.

15. Assistance is all efforts and actions to provide a sense of security and comfort for women victims so that they have the readiness and/or ability to obtain services and live their lives.

16. Technical Implementation Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is the UPT for the Protection of Women and Children.

17. Integrated Services is a comprehensive, integrated, cross-sectoral and professional, quality and sustainable service delivery system for women victims that includes health, legal, social and economic services.

18. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a non-structural institution that coordinates the implementation of an integrated service system for women victims.

19. Service Institution is an institution that provides protection services for women victims, both government-owned and community service institutions.

20. Social Rehabilitation is all efforts to restore and develop the ability of women victims so that they can carry out their social roles and functions both in family and/or community life.

21. Social Reintegration is all efforts to strengthen the readiness and ability of women victims to regain their welfare in social, political, economic, and cultural life with dignity, including strengthening the acceptance and support of family and/or community for the protection and fulfillment of the rights of women victims.

22. Restitution is compensation payment charged to the perpetrator based on a court decision that has permanent legal force for material and/or immaterial losses suffered by the victim or their heirs.

23. Legal Aid Organization is a community organization that provides legal aid services.

24. Community is individuals, families, communities, religious leaders, community leaders, traditional leaders, business world and corporations, community organizations, social organizations, women's and gender study centers, mass media or similar others.

25. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line up or down and in a collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is the dependent of a woman and/or child.

26. Boarding Educational Institution is a formal or non-formal Educational Institution that organizes general education, and/or religious education or integrated education (general education with religious education, or general education with vocational education or religious education with vocational education), where some or all of the students reside while pursuing education.

Second Section
Principles, Objectives and Scope

Paragraph 1
Principles

Article 2

The Protection of Women is based on:
a. respect for human dignity;
b. non-discrimination;
c. the best interests of victims;
d. justice;
e. benefit; and
f. legal certainty.

Paragraph 2
Objectives

Article 3

The Protection of Women aims to:
a. prevent all forms of violence, exploitation, and discrimination against women;
b. provide services and recovery for women victims of violence, exploitation and discrimination;
c. realize the capacity of the City Regional Government in the implementation of the Protection of Women from violence, exploitation and discrimination;
d. increase community participation in the Protection of Women; and
e. realize a social life that is safe and free from all forms of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women.

Paragraph 3
Scope

Article 4

The scope of regulation for the implementation of the Protection of Women includes:
a. women's rights;
b. prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women;
c. services;
d. coordination and cooperation;
e. community participation;
f. data and information systems;
g. institutions;
h. obligations of the City Regional Government;
i. control, guidance and supervision;
j. awards; and
k. financing.

CHAPTER II
WOMEN'S RIGHTS

Article 5

Every Woman has the right to:
a. obtain information related to the Protection of Women, victim service procedures and case handling developments;
b. report, provide statements and views without pressure;
c. participate in case handling and in efforts to eliminate violence against women;
d. obtain protection of confidentiality;
e. obtain protection for the security of themselves, their families and their property from threats and violence;
f. be free from all forms of stereotypes, stigma and other discrimination;
g. obtain the services and recovery needed;
h. obtain restitution; and
i. form and join organizations.

CHAPTER III
PREVENTION OF VIOLENCE, EXPLOITATION AND DISCRIMINATION AGAINST WOMEN

Article 6

The City Regional Government together with educational institutions, religious institutions, health service provider institutions, professional institutions, business world, service institutions, Islamic boarding schools, youth organizations, legal aid organizations, communities and media, are responsible for implementing the prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women.

Article 7

The prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women is intended to create a safe environment for women and to reduce the vulnerability of violence, exploitation and discrimination to vulnerable women groups, as follows:
a. women in disaster and social conflict situations;
b. women with disabilities;
c. women with HIV/AIDS;
d. migrant women workers;
e. girls;
f. women heads of households;
g. domestic women workers;
h. home-based women workers;
i. elderly women;
j. informal women workers;
k. women in situations of intolerance; and
l. other vulnerable women groups.

Article 8

(1) The prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women is carried out in the following fields:
a. education;
b. health;
c. religion;
d. security;
e. justice;
f. public works and spatial planning;
g. public housing and settlement areas;
h. social;
i. labor;
j. women's empowerment and child protection;
k. defense;
l. environment;
m. population administration and civil registration;
n. population control and family planning;
o. transportation;
p. communication and informatics;
q. cooperatives, small and medium enterprises;
r. youth and sports;
s. culture;
t. tourism;
u. trade and industry; and
v. other fields.

(2) The prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out by:
a. developing communication media, information, education and public campaigns through media;
b. developing educational materials and curricula according to regional authority;
c. developing safe transportation systems and public spaces;
d. building early detection systems, security and integrated complaint services in industrial areas, companies, residential environments, educational environments, boarding educational environments, and other public spaces;
e. implementing consultation services for families, prospective brides, students, santri, youth, university students and vulnerable groups;
f. encouraging behavioral change of parents and the community;
g. developing regional policies, schools, boarding schools, and universities that are safe and friendly for women and integrated with regional spatial planning policies;
h. forming and developing cadres, communities, young people, students, santri, university students, journalists, social media influencers, educators, community leaders and religious leaders for prevention;
i. conducting education and advocacy to owners, managers and/or users of social media;
j. developing protection systems and special support for vulnerable women groups;
k. conducting awareness for perpetrators;
l. increasing the capacity of the City Regional Government, government agencies, educational institutions, religious institutions, health service provider institutions, professional institutions, business world, service institutions, youth organizations, legal aid organizations, media, religious leaders, community leaders and communities;
m. conducting cooperation with religious institutions, community social institutions, service institutions, universities, media, youth organizations, religious leaders and community leaders; and
n. conducting socialization of laws and regulations.

(3) The fields and forms of prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) are implemented by offices, regional apparatus, agencies, community institutions, organizations, communities, professional institutions that are in charge of related affairs according to their authority based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 9

The prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women is carried out in an integrated and integrated manner with the City Regional Government, related agencies, educational institutions, religious institutions, health service provider institutions, professional institutions, business world, service institutions, Islamic boarding schools, youth organizations, legal aid organizations, communities, and media coordinated by the Office.

Article 10

Provisions regarding the prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women as referred to in Articles 6 through 9, are further regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

CHAPTER IV
SERVICES

First Section
Service Principles

Article 11

(1) Services are implemented to fulfill the rights of women victims and are carried out with the following principles:
a. respect for human rights;
b. non-discrimination;
c. confidentiality;
d. empathy;
e. inclusion;
f. empowerment;
g. the best interests of victims;
h. honesty;
i. professionalism;
j. integration; and
k. sustainability.

(2) Services to women victims must consider special conditions and needs based on the vulnerability experienced by women as referred to in Article 7.

Second Section
Forms of Services

Paragraph 1
General

Article 12

(1) Forms of protection services for women victims of violence, exploitation and discrimination, include services for:
a. complaints;
b. health rehabilitation;
c. reproductive health;
d. social rehabilitation;
e. social reintegration; and
f. legal assistance and accompaniment.

(2) Services for women victims are implemented quickly, completely, with quality and integrated with services provided by the City Regional Government, community, educational institutions, boarding educational institutions, religious institutions, professional institutions, business world through an integrated service system.

Paragraph 2
Complaint Services

Article 13

(1) Complaint services as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (1) letter a, consist of:
a. receiving complaints;
b. special complaints;
c. providing information;
d. outreach;
e. crisis or emergency services;
f. assessment; and
g. other forms of complaint services needed by victims.

(2) Complaint services as referred to in paragraph (1), are carried out by trained officers in a special room that is safe and comfortable.

Article 14

Further provisions regarding complaint services as referred to in Article 13, are further regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

Paragraph 3
Health Rehabilitation Services

Article 15

(1) Health rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (1) letter b, are adjusted to the needs of victims based on medical and non-medical considerations, consisting of:
a. physical and mental health examination and treatment;
b. psychological examination and recovery;
c. pregnancy, childbirth, and reproductive health services;
d. medical resume and psychological examination results;
e. visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum;
f. deoxyribonucleic acid test; and
g. other forms of health services needed.

(2) Health rehabilitation services for women victims are carried out by trained officers in a special room that is safe and comfortable.

(3) Health rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1), are carried out through a special mechanism of hospital-based integrated services and are the responsibility of the Regional Apparatus in charge.

Article 16

Further provisions regarding health rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 15, are further regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

Paragraph 4
Reproductive Health Services

Article 17

(1) Reproductive health services as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (1) letter c, are a state of complete physical, mental, and social health related to the reproductive system, functions, and processes, not merely the absence of disease or disability.

(2) Reproductive health services are implemented with activities in the form of:
a. early detection of disease or screening;
b. treatment;
c. rehabilitation;
d. counseling; and
e. provision of contraceptive devices.

Paragraph 5
Social Rehabilitation Services

Article 18

(1) Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (1) letter d, are implemented with the following activities:
a. counseling;
b. assistance;
c. provision of certified language experts and translators;
d. spiritual guidance;
e. safe houses;
f. shelter/temporary shelter;
g. strengthening based on family and/or group support;
h. mobility support for victims with disabilities;
i. provision of social assistance for victims; and
j. other forms of social rehabilitation services needed by victims.

(2) Social rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1) letters a, b, c, d, e and g, are the responsibility of the Office.

(3) Social rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1) letters f, h, i and j, are the responsibility of the Regional Apparatus in charge of social affairs.

Paragraph 6
Social Reintegration Services

Article 19

(1) Social reintegration services as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (1) letter e, are implemented with the following activities:
a. repatriation and/or picking up victims;
b. population document or identity services;
c. alternative families;
d. scholarships and educational support facilities;
e. non-formal education;
f. skills training and economic business;
g. financial assistance and business capital;
h. economic business assistance;
i. provision of food and/or nutrition programs for victims and their families;
j. provision of social protection programs and poverty alleviation programs; and
k. other forms of repatriation and social reintegration services needed by victims.

(2) Social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1), are provided to victims, families and people whose lives depend on victims with the aim that victims have the readiness and ability to live in the community.

(3) Social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1), are carried out in an integrated manner with the Integrated Referral Service System for Social Protection and Poverty Alleviation and the Child Welfare Center.

(4) Social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1), are the responsibility of the Regional Apparatus in charge of social affairs, population administration and civil registration affairs, labor affairs, small and medium enterprise cooperative affairs, education affairs, health affairs, public works and spatial planning affairs, land affairs, women's empowerment and child protection affairs and other related affairs.

Article 20

Further provisions regarding social reintegration services as referred to in Article 19, are further regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

CHAPTER V
COORDINATION AND COOPERATION

First Section
Coordination

Article 21

In the context of implementing the Protection of Women from violence, exploitation and discrimination, the City Regional Government implements coordination and cooperation with the central government, the regional government of West Java Province, other district/city governments, related agencies, law enforcement agencies, service institutions, educational institutions, boarding educational institutions, religious institutions, community social institutions, professional organizations, legal aid organizations, youth organizations, broadcasting supervisory institutions, media, communities, professional institutions, business world and other related stakeholders.

Second Section
Cooperation

Article 22

(1) In the context of protecting women from violence, exploitation and discrimination, the City Regional Government may conduct cooperation.

(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 23

(1) The City Regional Government has an obligation to strengthen community participation in the implementation of the protection of women.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out in the form of:
a. prevention;
b. services and referrals;
c. development of data information systems;
d. strengthening service institutions;
e. research and policy advocacy;
f. control, guidance and supervision; and
g. financing.

(3) Strengthening community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), can be carried out by:
a. strengthening cooperation; and
b. strengthening the institutional capacity of prevention and services from the community, boarding educational institutions, and business world.

(4) The business world has responsibilities in the implementation of the Protection of Women in accordance with the targets of Social and Environmental Responsibility of Business Entities.

(5) The form of responsibility of the business world as referred to in paragraph (4), is carried out as follows:
a. financial assistance to service institutions;
b. empowerment;
c. capacity building;
d. publication and information media;
e. provision of infrastructure;
f. establishment of special institutions for prevention and violence complaint services; and
g. non-binding social assistance.

Article 24

Further provisions regarding the form and procedure of community participation as referred to in Article 23, are further regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

CHAPTER VII
DATA AND INFORMATION SYSTEMS

Article 25

(1) The City Regional Government uses data and information systems in the implementation of the Protection of Women.

(2) The data and information system as referred to in paragraph (1), is provided by the Ministry that administers Government Affairs in the Field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in an integrated and integrated manner.

(3) The data and information system as referred to in paragraph (1), involves vertical agencies, City Regional Government, law enforcement agencies, regional apparatus, service institutions, community social institutions, media, universities, professional institutions, business world, and other related stakeholders.

(5) The data and information system for the Protection of Women as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

a. UPT; and

I. GENERAL

Violence against women still occurs in society today. Various forms of such violence include rape, sexual harassment, human trafficking, domestic violence, sexual exploitation, violence against domestic workers, pornography, bullying, cyber bullying, and cyber porn.

This situation requires serious handling because it relates to the quality of human beings and the future of the Indonesian Nation. Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, and Law Number 21 of 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Crime of Trafficking in Persons, provide obligations to the Government and City Regional Government to implement protection for women. The implementation of protection for women, according to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government and its amendments, includes prevention, referral services for women victims of violence, strengthening and developing service institutions for the protection of women.

Services for women victims of violence include complaint services, referrals, health rehabilitation, legal assistance and protection, social rehabilitation, repatriation and social reintegration, and encouraging community participation in prevention and recovery efforts. Based on Article 12 paragraph (2) of Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government and its amendments, the sub-affair of women's protection is a mandatory non-basic service affair. Based on Article 217, the Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment and child protection affairs is implemented by the Office.
