CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Gunung Mas Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Gunung Mas.

- 4 -
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
6. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from Violence and discrimination.
7. Woman is a person who is female.
8. Child is a person who has not reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child still in the womb.
9. Community is individuals, community groups, including parents and families.
10. Prevention is all efforts to eliminate all risks and adverse impacts of violence against Women and Children.
11. Violence is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.
12. Forum for Handling Victims of Violence Against Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as FPK2PA, is a coordination forum for handling victims of Violence against Women and Children whose implementation is tiered at the regency and sub-district levels.
13. Integrated Service Center for Women Empowerment and Child Protection, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service center in efforts to empower Women in various fields of development, as well as protection of Women and Children from various types of discrimination and acts of Violence, including trafficking in persons established by the government or community-based.
14. Child-Friendly Village/Sub-District, hereinafter abbreviated as K/DRA, is village/sub-district area development that unites the commitment and resources of village/sub-district government involving the Community and business world located in the village/sub-district in order to promote, protect,

- 5 -
fulfill and respect children's rights, which are planned consciously and sustainably.
Article 2
Protection of Women and Children is organized based on the principles of:
a. respect, fulfillment, protection, advancement, and enforcement of rights; and
b. legal certainty.
Article 3
Protection of Women and Children is implemented based on the principles of:
a. responsive to rights;
b. non-discrimination;
c. maintaining confidentiality;
d. empowerment; and
e. participation.
Article 4
The objectives of Protection of Women and Children include:
a. ensuring the fulfillment of rights;
b. providing justice and equality of rights;
c. providing protection and a sense of security; and
d. conducting empowerment.
Article 5
The scope of Protection of Women and Children includes:
a. Protection of Women;
b. Child Protection;
c. coordination and cooperation;
d. responsibility of Regional Government;
e. community participation;
f. guidance and supervision;
g. evaluation;
h. reporting; and
i. funding.

CHAPTER II
PROTECTION OF WOMEN
Part One
General
Article 6
Protection of Women includes:
a. services;
b. empowerment;
c. prevention; and
d. monitoring.
Part Two
Services
Article 7
(1) Forms of Protection of Women services as referred to in Article 6 letter a include:
a. legal aid;
b. health services;
c. social rehabilitation services;
d. complaint, consultation and counseling services;
e. social reintegration services; and
f. pre-marital guidance services.
(2) Pre-marital guidance services as referred to in paragraph (1) letter f, are implemented by every Woman who will carry out marriage.
(3) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) is conducted to provide understanding and knowledge regarding family resilience, health, psychology, religion, social and culture.
(4) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (3) is implemented by the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women empowerment and Child Protection.
(5) In implementing guidance as referred to in paragraph (4), the Regional Apparatus may involve:
a. related agencies;
b. religious leaders; and
c. customary leaders.
(6) Further provisions regarding procedures for Protection of Women services are regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER III
CHILD PROTECTION
Part One
General
Article 11
Child Protection includes:
a. services;
b. fulfillment of Children's rights;
c. prevention; and
d. monitoring.
Part Two
Services
Article 12
(1) Forms of Child Protection services as referred to in Article 11 letter a include:
a. legal aid;
b. social rehabilitation services; and
c. social reintegration services.
(2) Further provisions regarding Child Protection services as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in Regent Regulation.
Part Three
Fulfillment of Children's Rights
Article 13
(1) Fulfillment of Children's rights as referred to in Article 11 letter b includes:
a. guarantee of civil rights and freedoms;
b. guarantee of family environment and alternative care;
c. guarantee of basic health and welfare;
d. education, leisure time, culture and recreation; and
e. special protection.
(2) Special protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e, is carried out for:
a. children in emergency situations;
b. children in conflict with the law;
c. children from minority and isolated groups;
d. children who are exploited economically and/or sexually;
e. children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic and other addictive substance abuse;
f. children who are victims of pornography;

- 9 -
g. children with HIV/AIDS;
h. child victims of kidnapping, sale and/or trafficking;
i. child victims of physical and/or psychological Violence;
j. child victims of sexual crimes;
k. child victims of terrorism networks;
l. children with disabilities;
m. child victims of mistreatment and neglect;
n. children with deviant social behavior; and
o. children who are victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their parents' condition.
(3) Regulation regarding the fulfillment of Children's rights is organized in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.
Part Four
Prevention
Article 14
(1) Prevention of violations of Children's rights is implemented by the Regional Government and the Community.
(2) Prevention of violations of Children's rights implemented by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by:
a. forming a work network;
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of Prevention based on partnership patterns with the Community, private sector, non-governmental organizations; and
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to Child Protection.
(3) Prevention of violations of Children's rights implemented by the Community as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by:
a. increasing Children's productivity;
b. developing Children's self-potential;
c. preventing pregnancy at a young age;
d. reducing maternal and infant mortality rates; and
e. preventing the occurrence of domestic Violence.
(4) Formation of a work network as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a, includes work partners of the Regional Government in implementing prevention of rights violations in the form of family partners.
(5) Further provisions regarding Prevention of violations of Children's rights as referred to in paragraph (3) are regulated in Regent Regulation.

- 10 -
Part Five
Monitoring
Article 15
Provisions regarding monitoring as referred to in Article 10 apply mutatis mutandis to the implementation of monitoring of the fulfillment of Children's rights and special protection of Children.
CHAPTER IV
COORDINATION AND COOPERATION
Article 16
(1) Implementation of coordination and cooperation on Protection of Women and Children aims to:
a. increase efforts to fulfill the rights of Women and Children;
b. improve synergistic and harmonious working relationships; and
c. obtain data and information in the implementation of Protection of Women and Children.
(2) In order to achieve the objectives as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government cooperates with:
a. other Regional Governments;
b. related agencies;
c. non-governmental institutions;
d. universities; and
e. non-governmental organizations.
(3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) includes:
a. exchange of data and information;
b. socialization or legal counseling lectures;
c. rehabilitation of victims of acts of Violence; and
d. repatriation and social reintegration.
CHAPTER V
RESPONSIBILITY OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT
Part One
General
Article 17
(1) The Regional Government is responsible for protecting the rights of Women and Children.
(2) Responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) include:

- 11 -
a. education;
b. health;
c. politics;
d. law;
e. economy;
f. culture; and
g. social.
(3) The Regional Government's responsibility for protecting Women's rights as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by:
a. formulating gender-responsive Regional policies;
b. institutionalizing gender mainstreaming within the Regional Government;
c. empowering Women in the political, legal, economic, cultural and social fields in community organizations at the regency level;
d. strengthening and developing service provider institutions for Women's empowerment at the regency level;
e. implementing efforts to Prevent the occurrence of Violence against Women.
(4) The Regional Government's responsibility for protecting Children's rights as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by:
a. formulating policies, programs, and Regional development activities with a child rights perspective that are contained in Regional development planning documents;
b. formulating norms, standard procedures and criteria for assessing the fulfillment and protection of Children's rights that refer to norms, standards, procedures and criteria established by the government;
c. implementation of child-friendly regency is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
d. facilitating the implementation of Regional Children's forums;
e. increasing community participation in the implementation and protection of Children's rights;
f. monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of Children's fulfillment and Protection programs;
g. guidance of Child Protection institutions;
h. providing special protection for Children; and
i. implementing efforts to Prevent the occurrence of Violence against Children.
Article 18
(1) The Regional Government in carrying out the responsibility of institutionalizing Gender Mainstreaming as referred to

- 12 -
in Article 17 paragraph (3) letter b regarding Protection of Women and Children must organize an information system for Women and Children data.
(2) The Women's data information system as referred to in paragraph (1) is an inseparable part of the Children's data information system.
(3) The Women and Children information system as referred to in paragraph (2) is further regulated by Regent Regulation.
(4) Gender mainstreaming as referred to in paragraph (1) is organized in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
Article 19
(1) In order to ensure the effectiveness of the implementation of Protection of Women and Children, the Regional Government must conduct cross-sectoral coordination with related institutions.
(2) In organizing coordination of Protection of Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes a coordination team for Protection of Women and Children.
(3) In coordination forum activities as referred to in paragraph (2), the coordination team for Protection of Women and Children has the task of:
a. preparing materials needed related to the implementation of Protection of Women and Children; and
b. conveying results, obstacles, and solutions taken to overcome obstacles in the implementation of Protection of Women and Children.
(4) The coordination forum as referred to in paragraph (3) is conducted periodically, at least 2 (two) times in 1 (one) year.
Part Two
Prevention of Violence Against Women and Children
Article 20
(1) The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to implement efforts to Prevent the occurrence of Violence against Women and Children as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (3) letter e and paragraph (4) letter i carried out in the form of:
a. collecting data and organizing an information system for Protection of Women and Children victims of Violence;
b. conducting education on anti-Violence values against Women and Children; and

- 13 -
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to women's empowerment and Protection of Children victims of Violence.
(2) The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
a. establishing and organizing technical service institutions for victims involving community elements; and
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims of Violence.
Part Three
Institutions
Article 21
(1) Technical Service institutions as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (2) letter a are implemented by P2TP2A and FPK2PA.
(2) In organizing Protection of Women and Children, the Regional Government must establish a Regional technical implementing unit for Protection of Women and Children.
(3) The establishment of a Regional technical implementing unit as referred to in paragraph (2) is stipulated by Regent Regulation after obtaining written approval from the Governor.
(4) In implementing technical services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may form a task force.
(5) The task force as referred to in paragraph (4) can also be strengthened institutionally through cooperation and coordination with:
a. KLA task force;
b. KRA task force;
c. K/DRA task force;
d. schools and/or educational institutions;
e. Children's forums;
f. children's studios;
g. community organizations;
h. Women's organizations; and
i. other institutions concerned with the fulfillment of rights and Child Protection.
(6) The task force as referred to in paragraph (4) is a coordinative institution at the regency and sub-district levels that coordinates policies, programs and activities to realize sub-districts, child-friendly villages/sub-districts.
(7) Further provisions regarding technical services as referred to in paragraph (3) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

P2TP2A
Article 22

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in the form of functional relationship patterns among components directed to achieve common goals.
Article 23

(2) Counseling fields as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of:

Article 25

a. medical services;
b. medicolegal services;
c. psychosocial services;
d. legal services;
e. social reintegration services; and
f. economic independence services.

Article 26
Provisions regarding the implementation of services by P2TP2A as referred to in Article 22 paragraph (1) are regulated in Regent Regulation.
Paragraph 2
FPK2PA
Article 27
(1) In order to protect and guarantee the rights of Women and Children from acts of Violence, FPK2PA is formed.
(2) FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
a. Regional FPK2PA;
b. sub-district FPK2PA; and

CHAPTER VI
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION
Part One
Obligations and Responsibilities of Parents and/or Families
Article 28
Parents are obliged and responsible to:
a. care for, nurture, educate, manage and protect family members;
b. develop family members according to their abilities, talents, and interests; and
c. Prevention of marriage at Child age.
Article 29
Increasing the role of families in Protection of Women and Children is carried out through:
a. Prevention of marriage at Child age;
b. bringing Women closer to health services; and
c. increasing family resilience.

- 16 -
Part Two
Community Involvement
Article 30
(1) The Community is involved in Protection of Women and Children.
(2) Community involvement as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of participation through Prevention, services, empowerment, monitoring, supervision and fulfillment of the rights of Women and/or Children.
(3) The role of the Community in organizing Protection of Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by:
a. providing communication and information through socialization and education regarding the rights of Women and Children;
b. providing suggestions and input in the formulation of policies related to Protection of Women and Children;
c. reporting to the authorities if there are violations of the rights of Women and Children;
d. playing an active role in the rehabilitation and social reintegration process for Women and Children;
e. forming family partners at the neighborhood/hamlet or village/sub-district level by the Community;
f. forming Women and Child Protection units within community organizations and educational institutions;
g. establishment and management of orphanages;
h. establishment of Child rehabilitation centers;
i. provision of shelters and/or social houses;
j. work skills training; and
k. providing first aid when violations of the rights of Women and Children occur.
(4) Further provisions regarding community participation are regulated by Regent Regulation.
CHAPTER VII
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION
Article 31
(1) The Regent is obliged to conduct guidance and supervision of Protection of Women and children.
(2) Implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women empowerment and Child Protection.

- 17 -
(3) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. coordination;
b. guidance;
c. education and training;
d. monitoring and evaluation; and
e. reporting.
(4) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) to realize the objectives of Protection of Women and Children in accordance with standard procedure norms and criteria implemented by the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women empowerment and Child Protection.
(5) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out with professional and transparent principles.
(6) In implementing guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women empowerment and Child Protection must report regularly to the Regent.
(7) In terms of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women empowerment and Child Protection, may cooperate and coordinate with other Regional Apparatus, the Community, agencies and/or related organizational institutions.
CHAPTER VIII
EVALUATION
Article 32
(1) Evaluation of Protection of Women and Children is carried out based on documents of implementation of services, empowerment, Prevention, fulfillment and monitoring results of the implementation of Protection of Women and Children.
(2) Implementation of evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women empowerment and Child Protection.
(3) Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) includes process, impact and results evaluation.
(4) Evaluation results as referred to in paragraph (3) are material for the Regional Apparatus to prepare reports which are subsequently submitted to the Regent.
(5) Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (4) is implemented at least 1 (one) time in 1 (one) year.

- 18 -
(6) Further provisions regarding evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent Regulation.
CHAPTER IX
REPORTING
Article 33
(1) Reporting of evaluation results as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (5) as material for the Regent's consideration in making policies for the implementation of Protection of Women and Children.
(2) Reports on the implementation of Protection of Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented 1 (one) time in 1 (one) year and at any time if necessary.
CHAPTER X

I. GENERAL
Children are the future of the nation and the next generation of the nation's ideals, so that every Child has the right to survival, growth and development, participation and the right to protection from acts of Violence and discrimination as well as civil rights and freedoms.
Likewise, Women who play a role as mothers who give birth to healthy, strong, resilient, noble, potential and quality children. Through the role of Women in the family, society and state, tough generations will be born who will continue the nation's ideals.
The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, especially Article 27 paragraph (1) which states that "All citizens are equal in law and government and must uphold the law and government without exception". Although the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia has guaranteed equality of position for every citizen, to date Women and Children still experience various obstacles in obtaining their rights in almost all fields.
On the other hand, Women have special needs compared to men, so that in accordance with their nature, in certain conditions the Regional Government must be able to guarantee that their rights are fulfilled according to those needs. Whereas children, they are a trust and gift from Almighty God, in whom inherent dignity and dignity as a complete human being. Considering the position and expectations for children as the potential and future of the nation so that children deserve attention from all parties so that children can grow and develop normally and avoid wrong treatment and actions, Violence, discrimination that will damage children's development both physically, mentally and socially. For this reason, children need to be given the widest possible opportunity to be able to grow and develop optimally, both physically, mentally, socially and with noble character.

- 21 -
Starting from this, a regulation is needed that becomes a guideline for all parties, especially the Regional Government in ensuring the implementation of Protection of Women and Children in Gunung Mas Regency in accordance with the authority possessed as stated in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government, as well as other laws and regulations that regulate the Protection of Women and Children.

Letter c
What is meant by responsibility for protecting Women's rights in the field of Politics is that the Regional Government is responsible for being able to facilitate the increase of Women's knowledge in the political field through socialization to increase awareness of Women's rights and Women's participation in the political field, encourage increased involvement of Women in community organizations and decision-making activities within the Regional Government, encourage the formation of Women's organizations/forums.
Letter d
What is meant by responsibility for protecting the rights of Women and Children in the field of law is that the Regional Government is responsible for facilitating the increase of legal knowledge and awareness for Women and Children, encouraging the formation of organizations that provide legal services free of charge for Women and Children.
Letter e
What is meant by responsibility for protecting the rights of Women and Children in the economic field is that the Regional Government is responsible for facilitating Women and Children in obtaining access and facilities in the economic field, facilitating Women in efforts to improve family economy, providing capital assistance or training for Women.
