REGIONAL REGULATION OF PURWOREJO REGENCY
NUMBER 13 OF 2022
CONCERNING
THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION
OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD
THE REGENT OF PURWOREJO,

Considering: a. that the purpose of the state is to protect the entire homeland of the Indonesian Nation by providing guarantees for security, equality, and welfare;
b. that women and children are still in vulnerable positions from various forms of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and neglect so that strategic efforts are needed from the Regional Government together with related parties to provide systematic and comprehensive protection;
c. that to provide a legal foundation and guidance in the implementation of protection of women and children in the Region, integrated and comprehensive regulation is needed to realize a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Purworejo Regency;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b, and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;

In View of: 1. Article 18 Paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 13 of 1950 concerning the Formation of Regency Areas within the Province of Central Java (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1950 Number 42);
3. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587), as amended several times, most recently by Law Number 11 of 2020 concerning Job Creation (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2020 Number 245, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6573);

With Mutual Approval of
THE REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF PURWOREJO REGENCY
And
THE REGENT OF PURWOREJO

HAS DECIDED:

To Establish: REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN.

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Purworejo Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous Region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Purworejo.
4. Regional Apparatus is an assistant element to the Regent and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Technical Regional Apparatus is the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
6. Village is a village and customary village or called by another name, hereinafter referred to as Village, is a legal community unit that has territorial boundaries authorized to regulate and manage government affairs, the interests of the local community based on community initiative, rights of origin, and or traditional rights that are recognized and respected in the government system of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia and located in Purworejo Regency.
7. Village Government is the Village Head or called by another name who is assisted by village apparatus as an element of Village Government administration.
8. Kelurahan is part of the territory of the Sub-district as a Sub-district apparatus.
9. Women Victim is a woman who experiences suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, economically, socially, and other losses resulting from violence, exploitation and/or gender-based discrimination that occurs both in the public sphere and in the sphere of personal life.
10. Child Victim is a person who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old including a child still in the womb who experiences suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, economically, socially, and other losses resulting from violence, exploitation and/or discrimination that occurs both in the public sphere and in the sphere of personal life.
11. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
12. Protection of Children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
13. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is direct or indirect based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in reduction of deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
14. Violence is any act of abuse of physical force with or without using means, unlawfully and causing misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.
15. Exploitation is an action with or without the consent of the victim which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or Services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, physical, sexual, reproductive organ utilization, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits.
16. Maltreatment of Children is all forms of violence against children committed by those who should be responsible and/or those who have power over the child, who should be trusted namely parents, close family, teachers, supervisors, law enforcement officers, caregivers and companions.
17. Prevention is all efforts to prevent Discrimination, Violence, and Exploitation, as well as recurrence of Women and Children.
18. Service is all efforts and actions to fulfill the rights of Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and neglect which includes complaint Services, health, social rehabilitation, legal assistance and enforcement, repatriation, social reintegration, and restitution.
19. Assistance is all efforts and actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for self-strengthening of Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and neglect of Women and Children to resolve the problems faced so as to provide a sense of security and comfort and have readiness and/or ability to obtain Services and live their lives.
20. Companion is a person or group or organization that is trusted and/or has the skills and knowledge to accompany Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and Exploitation with the aim that Women Victims and Child Victims feel safe and comfortable in accessing Services.
21. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a Regional technical implementation unit formed by the Regional Government in providing services for Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment, neglect and other problems.
22. Forum for Protection of Violence Victims hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK is a coordination forum for Prevention, handling and empowerment of Women Victims and Child Victims of Violence and other problems whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner and formed at the Regional, Sub-district, and Village levels.
23. Integrated Service Center is a non-structural institution that coordinates the implementation of an integrated Service system for Women Victims and Child Victims.
24. Social Reintegration is all efforts to strengthen the readiness and ability of Women Victims and Child Victims to regain their welfare in social, political, economic, and cultural life with dignity, including strengthening acceptance and support into the family and/or community environment for the protection and fulfillment of the rights of Women Victims and Child Victims.
25. Restitution is compensation payment given to Women Victims, Child Victims and/or the victim's family by the perpetrator based on a court decision that has permanent legal force for material and/or immaterial losses suffered by Women Victims, Child Victims and/or the victim's family.
26. Legal Aid Provider is a legal aid institution or community organization that provides legal aid services based on laws and regulations.
27. Business World is a commercial organization, the entire industrial and goods/services production environment, including State-Owned Enterprises, and Regional-Owned Enterprises as well as private or entrepreneurs and their networks, who will carry out their social responsibilities.

Article 2
The implementation of protection of women and children is based on:
a. humanity;
b. respect and fulfillment of the rights of women and children;
c. non-discrimination and non-exploitation;
d. harmony;
e. protection;
f. justice and gender equality;
g. welfare; and
h. proportionality.

Article 3
The implementation of protection of women and children is intended to provide a legal basis in implementing protection of women and children in an integrated and comprehensive manner and to realize synergy between the Regional Government and the community in protecting women and children as vulnerable groups who are easily victimized by Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment and/or neglect towards a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency.

Article 4
The implementation of protection of women and children aims to:
a. prevent all forms of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women;
b. prevent all forms of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect;
c. encourage the reduction of risks of various forms of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect of women and children;
d. provide protection guarantees for women and children;
e. provide ease in accessing protection services for women and children;
f. realize a social life that is safe, comfortable, peaceful and free from threats directed at women and children;
g. create a social system that encourages respect for women and compassion for children so as to form social security and social protection for women and children; and
h. realize Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency.

Article 5
The scope of this regional regulation includes:
a. rights of women and children;
b. rights of victims;
c. duties and responsibilities;
d. efforts to protect women and children;
e. implementation of Protection of Women;
f. implementation of Protection of Children;
g. Integrated Service Center;
h. role of Village/Kelurahan Government;
i. institutions;
j. cooperation and partnership;
k. Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency; and
l. data and information system for women and children;
m. awards; and
n. funding.

CHAPTER II
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Part One
Rights of Women

Article 6
(1) The Regional Government respects and maintains the rights of women in full based on laws and regulations.
(2) Every woman has the right to:
a. receive equal and equitable treatment;
b. obtain equal and fair opportunities in various fields, both education, employment, politics, and social;
c. obtain complete information for protection and legal assistance;
d. obtain protection and defense from all forms of Discriminatory actions;
e. obtain protection and defense from acts of Violence;
f. obtain protection and defense from all forms of Exploitation; and
g. receive excellent and professional services.

Part Two
Rights of Children

Article 7
(1) The Regional Government maintains and ensures the full fulfillment of children's rights based on the provisions of laws and regulations.
(2) Every child has the right to:
a. live, grow, develop, and participate properly in accordance with human dignity and dignity;
b. obtain self-identity from birth;
c. worship according to their religion, think, and express themselves according to their level of intelligence and age, under the guidance of parents or Guardians;
d. know their parents, be raised, and cared for by their own parents and receive guarantees for a good survival and outpouring of affection;
e. obtain health Services and social security according to physical, mental, spiritual, and social needs;
f. receive protection and defense from Discriminatory treatment, Violence, Exploitation, neglect, cruelty, persecution, injustice, and/or other maltreatment;
g. obtain protection guarantees from destructive influences;
h. obtain protection from abuse in political activities, involvement in armed conflicts, social unrest, events containing elements of Violence and war;
i. obtain protection from targets of persecution, torture, or imposition of inhuman punishment and freedom in accordance with the law;
j. receive legal assistance and other assistance;
k. obtain education and teaching in the context of personal development and intelligence level according to interests and talents;
l. receive protection in educational units from sexual crimes and Violence committed by educators, education personnel, fellow students, and/or other parties;
m. obtain special education and children who have advantages are entitled to receive special education for children with disabilities;
n. receive a good environment and food intake for growth and development according to their age;
o. think, express, state and be heard their opinions, receive, seek, and provide information according to the level of intelligence and age for self-development in accordance with the values of decency and propriety; and
p. obtain time to rest, and utilize free time, socialize with peers, play, recreate, and create according to interests, talents, and intelligence level for self-development.

CHAPTER III
RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Part One
Rights of Women Victims

Article 8
(1) The Regional Government is responsible for fulfilling the rights of Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.
(2) The rights of Women Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. obtain information related to the protection of women, victim Service procedures and developments in case handling;
b. report and have their reports followed up, provide statements, and views without pressure;
c. participate in case handling and in efforts to eliminate Violence against women;
d. obtain protection of confidentiality;
e. obtain protection for the security of themselves, their families, and their property from threats;
f. be free from all forms of stereotypes, stigma and other discrimination;
g. obtain the Services and recovery needed;
h. obtain restitution; and
i. form and join organizations;
(3) Fulfillment of rights as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out in a friendly and professional manner that is victim-oriented.

Part Two
Rights of Child Victims

Article 9
(1) The Regional Government is responsible for fulfilling the rights of Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect.
(2) The rights of Child victims as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. receive legal protection and Legal Assistance;
b. receive counseling Assistance;
c. receive education guarantees;
d. receive personal and family protection guarantees;
e. be free from various stigmas;
f. guarantees of housing and living needs for survival, growth and development;
g. protection of personal property or wealth;
h. confidentiality guarantees;
i. receive rehabilitation; and/or
j. receive restitution.

CHAPTER IV
DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One
General

Article 10
The Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus, community, Business World, parents and/or guardians have duties and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children so that welfare is realized both physically, spiritually, and socially.

Part Two
Duties and Responsibilities of the Regional Government

Article 11
(1) The duties and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 10, include:
a. make policies to encourage Prevention efforts of various forms of threats to women and children and provide comprehensive protection;
b. implement policies with a victim orientation;
c. collect data and verify data related to information on women and children;
d. conduct various forms of cooperation to improve efforts to protect women and children;
e. prepare supporting facilities and infrastructure;
f. conduct guidance and empowerment of women;
g. provide safe houses for children that support development and growth and food intake and attention; and
h. allocate budgets according to needs and regional financial capacity;
i. actively involve Village Government, community and Business World in making efforts to protect women and children; and
j. realize Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency.
(2) The duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve other related Regional Apparatus.
(3) The duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out with a victim orientation based on laws and regulations.

Part Three
Duties and Responsibilities of the Community

Article 12
(1) The duties and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 10 include:
a. pay attention to women and children's issues as vulnerable groups to acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect;
b. prevent various actions and/or threats of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect of women and children;
c. provide information and/or report suspected acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect of women and children to authorized institutions;
d. be part of social security to protect women and children; and
e. participate in realizing Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency.
(2) In carrying out duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Part Four
Duties and Responsibilities of the Business World

Article 13
(1) The Business World must carry out duties and responsibilities as referred to in Article 10 including:
a. make company regulations that are friendly to female workers;
b. make policies that prevent and protect female workers from acts of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation in the work environment;
c. provide counseling or socialization in the work environment for Prevention, protection and reporting related to actions or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women;
d. refuse child workers under age according to laws and regulations;
e. make products that are safe for children;
f. provide corporate social responsibility for women and child protection programs;
g. provide special rooms for breastfeeding mothers or lactation rooms;
h. not involve children in company activities that endanger safety and/or are contrary to laws and regulations;
i. report if there are actions or threats of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect of women and children in the work environment;
j. provide special childcare facilities for certain companies in accordance with laws and regulations; and
k. participate in realizing Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency.
(2) The Business World carries out duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
(3) Business World that does not carry out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) is subject to sanctions in the form of verbal warnings and/or written warnings.
(4) In the event that the sanctions as referred to in paragraph (3) are not implemented, temporary license revocation actions may be taken and may be continued with permanent license revocation.
(5) License revocation as referred to in paragraph (4) is carried out by the related Regional Apparatus in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Part Five
Responsibilities of Parents/Guardians

Article 14
The duties and responsibilities of parents/guardians as referred to in Article 10 include:
a. care for and educate children according to their intelligence based on religious values and Pancasila;
b. provide good and nutritious food intake according to their age;
c. develop children according to their abilities, talents and interests;
d. provide protection from Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, neglect and/or injustice;
e. prevent marriage at child age;
f. encourage children's ability to express opinions or thoughts as appreciation for children; and
g. guide children who engage in deviant social behavior.

CHAPTER V
EFFORTS TO PROTECT WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Article 15
(1) Efforts to protect women and children are carried out by the Regional Government by:
a. conducting Prevention of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect of women and children;
b. conducting Prevention of trafficking of women and children;
c. conducting vulnerability risk management;
d. conducting handling and Assistance for Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect;
e. conducting empowerment of Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect;
f. implementing protection strategies for women and children; and
g. strengthening the capacity of Regional Apparatus and Village Government in implementing protection of women and children.
(2) In making protection efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may involve:
a. family;
b. community;
c. Business World; and
d. media.

CHAPTER VI
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN

Part One
Prevention

Article 16
(1) The Regional Government implements Protection of Women by conducting Prevention of various acts of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.
(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. conduct socialization or counseling on protection of women;
b. increase awareness, participation and public concern for the impacts and legal consequences of acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment and/or neglect through program integration for families, communities, and Business World;
c. internalization of anti-Discrimination, anti-Violence, and anti-Exploitation values through formal education and non-formal education;
d. provide accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement, and social Services;
e. encourage the formation of women's forums as a forum for joint learning, support systems and cadre formation;
f. guidance and empowerment of women to be able to be independent and defend themselves;
g. create safe and women-friendly transportation and work environments;
h. build and provide digital-based information systems and media that are easy to access for information, protection requests and reports;
i. increase the capacity of government institutions through knowledge enhancement, capability enhancement and service improvement; and
j. build networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women.
(3) In implementing prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government may involve and/or cooperate with media, community, Business World, and/or educational institutions.

Part Two
Protection

Article 17
(1) The Regional Government provides comprehensive protection to women from various actions:
a. threats;
b. Discrimination;
c. Violence; and
d. Exploitation.
(2) Every form of threatening action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a that is carried out either verbally or non-verbally that is intentionally directed at women so as to make themselves threatened with life or safety is prohibited for any reason.
(3) Every form of Discrimination action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b that is carried out leading to gender, racial, or equality injustice so as to degrade the status, freedom and opportunities of women is prohibited for any reason.
(4) Every form of Violence action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c that is carried out either physically, psychologically, or sexual violence so as to cause trauma to women is prohibited for any reason.
(5) Every form of Exploitation action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d that is carried out in any form that degrades the dignity, dignity, and honor of women and causes harm to women is prohibited for any reason.

Article 18
The Regional Government provides protection to women by:
a. forming social security;
b. building women-friendly follow-up referral services; and
c. providing supporting facilities and infrastructure for the protection of women.

Paragraph 1
Social Security

Article 19
(1) Social security as referred to in Article 18 letter a is carried out by the Regional Government together with Village/Kelurahan Government, Business World, and community.
(2) Social security as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out by:
a. reporting if there is information on the occurrence of acts of threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women;
b. implementing protection of Women Victims of acts of threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation;
c. conducting Assistance and empowerment of Women Victims of acts of threats, Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation; and/or
d. conducting security of perpetrators of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against Women who have the potential to endanger life safety.

Paragraph 2
Follow-up Referral Services

Article 20
(1) Follow-up referral services as referred to in Article 18 letter b are built by the Regional Government to provide quick and appropriate protection.
(2) Follow-up referral services as referred to in paragraph (1), are carried out by including:
a. make and implement standard guidelines for handling complaints or reports that are excellent and women-friendly;
b. integrate complaint or reporting systems between Regional Apparatus in the fields of communication, information, public services, villages, empowerment, women, security, legal assistance and enforcement;
c. integrate village and Kelurahan-based complaint or reporting systems;
d. integrate legal protection and assistance systems with every designated legal aid organization;
e. open digital media or digital application-based complaint or reporting services; and
f. integrate victim needs for counseling, health services, visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum, as well as other service needs.
(3) Follow-up referral services as referred to in paragraph (1) are provided free of charge to victims in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Paragraph 3
Provision of Supporting Facilities and Infrastructure
for Protection of Women

Article 21
(1) Provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in Article 18 letter c aims to maximize a sense of security, comfort and peace for Women Victims of acts of threats, Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.
(2) Provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. provision of temporary safe houses;
b. provision of daily living needs during the handling Assistance process in temporary safe houses;
c. provision of Companions in the field of health and counseling if needed;
d. conduct empowerment to increase women's capacity in the future; and
e. provision of spiritual mental companions.
(3) Provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out by the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus.

Part Three
Handling

Article 22
(1) Handling of Women Victims and Child Victims aims to provide appropriate action in handling victims and witnesses in acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women and children.
(2) Handling as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. complaints or reports;
b. protection needs;
c. physical and psychological health;
d. rehabilitation;
e. Social Reintegration;
f. legal assistance; and
g. Restitution.
(3) Handling as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out by the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus.
(4) Further provisions regarding legal assistance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter f, are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

Paragraph 1
Complaints or Reports

Article 23
(1) Complaints or reports of acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women are made easily, quickly, and appropriately.
(2) Every person who sees or knows of acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women makes complaints or reports.
(3) Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may be addressed to:
a. Village/Kelurahan Government;
b. FPKK;
c. Legal Aid Provider;
d. health service facilities;
e. law enforcement apparatus;
f. Regional Apparatus that implements government affairs in the field of public order and security;
g. non-governmental organizations; and/or
h. Social Media managed by related Regional Apparatus.
(4) Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may be made by:
a. directly;
b. by telephone;
c. via email or direct message to social media; and/or
d. by letter.
(5) Recipients of complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (3) keep the identity of the reporter confidential.

Paragraph 2
Protection Needs

Article 24
(1) Protection needs aim to protect the identity and personal security of reporters, victims and/or witnesses.
(2) Report recipients as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (3) to protect reporters and victims.
(3) In the event that the report recipient does not have protection facilities and infrastructure, the report recipient coordinates with FPKK and/or law enforcement apparatus that have facilities and infrastructure.

Paragraph 3
Physical and Psychological Health

Article 25
(1) Physical and psychological health for Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation is aimed at reducing and treating physical and psychological trauma.
(2) FKPP facilitates or conducts Assistance or handling of Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.

Paragraph 4
Rehabilitation

Article 26
(1) Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect who experience trauma undergo a gradual rehabilitation process both physically, psychologically, and socially.
(2) Rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus and/or the community to help accelerate the rehabilitation process.
(3) The Regional Government guarantees the fulfillment of the living needs of Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect during the rehabilitation process.

Paragraph 5
Restitution

Article 27
(1) Provision of Restitution is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
(2) The amount of Restitution paid by the perpetrator to Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect or their heirs is based on:
a. mediation agreement results; and/or
b. court decision.
(3) The Regional Government through related Regional Apparatus oversees the Restitution payment process to be appropriate and on target.

CHAPTER VII
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF CHILDREN

Part One
Prevention

Article 28
(1) The Regional Government makes policies and programs aimed at Prevention of various acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect of children.
(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), are carried out by:
a. conducting socialization or counseling;
b. increasing awareness of the impacts and legal consequences through program integration for families, communities, and Business World;
c. internalization of anti-Violence, anti-Exploitation, anti-maltreatment, and anti-neglect values through formal education and non-formal education;
d. encourage the formation of children's forums as a forum for joint learning, support systems, and cadre formation;
e. guidance and education for parents, guardians, or foster parents regarding parenting patterns according to growth and development, appreciation, and outpouring of affection;
f. create safe and child-appropriate transportation;
g. build and provide digital-based information systems and media that are easy to access for information, protection requests, and reports; and
h. guidance on the capacity of families, substitute families, or care institutions in the ability to care that is oriented towards child development and appreciation.
(3) In implementing prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government may involve and/or cooperate with media, community, Business World and/or educational institutions.

Part Two
Protection

Article 29
(1) The Regional Government provides active and comprehensive protection to children from various actions:
a. threats;
b. Violence;
c. Exploitation;
d. neglect;
e. Maltreatment of Children; and
f. Injustice.
(2) Every form of threatening action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a that is carried out either verbally or non-verbally that is intentionally directed at children so as to make themselves threatened with life or safety is prohibited for any reason.
(3) Every form of violence action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b that is carried out either physically, psychologically, or sexual violence so as to cause trauma to the child is prohibited for any reason.
(4) Every form of exploitation action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c that is carried out in any form and not in accordance with the child's developmental age according to general views, laws and regulations or local wisdom and causes harm to the child is prohibited for any reason.
(5) Every form of neglect action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d so as to neglect the responsibility as parents, guardians, or foster parents towards children is prohibited for any reason.
(6) Every form of maltreatment action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e that is carried out by utilizing closeness or abuse of responsibility to children so as to cause physical or psychological trauma or is deemed contrary to laws and regulations is prohibited for any reason.
(7) Every form of injustice action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter f, that is carried out by parents towards children who discriminate or play favorites and injustice in equality between men and women.

Article 30
Forms of protection provided by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 29 include:
a. vulnerability risk reduction;
b. forming social security; and/or
c. provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure for child protection.

Paragraph 1
Vulnerability Risk Reduction

Article 31
(1) The Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus or related Regional Apparatus reduces vulnerability risks with comprehensive and targeted vulnerability risk management.
(2) Vulnerability risk reduction efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. facilitation of vulnerability risk reduction implementation;
b. facilitation of justice implementation in accordance with laws and regulations; and
c. facilitation of community capacity strengthening.
(3) Facilitation of vulnerability risk reduction implementation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a, includes:
a. identification and early detection of potential in the community, institutions or Business World related to acts of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and neglect of children;
b. take immediate action to mitigate vulnerability risks to children based on identification and early detection results;
c. provide guidance and assistance to parents or guardians who are identified as experiencing difficulties and/or obstacles in caring for, caring for, and looking after children; and
d. provide guidance and assistance to families who are identified as experiencing difficulties and/or obstacles in building healthy and democratic relationships.
(4) Facilitation of justice implementation in accordance with laws and regulations as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b, includes:
a. use of diversion or mediation approaches in resolving problems of children suspected of dealing with the law in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
b. determination of restitution value that must be paid by the perpetrator to the victim in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
c. punishment of perpetrators of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations and justice that lives in society;
(5) Facilitation of community capacity strengthening as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is carried out including:
a. conduct counseling and paralegal training to the community to be able to conduct initial advocacy to victims;
b. form and foster children's forums as a space for appreciation of children's aspirations and support systems; and/or;
c. provide financial support for advocacy efforts by Legal Aid Providers related to handling children as victims.

Paragraph 2
Social Security

Article 32
(1) The Regional Government together with Village Government, community, and Business World conduct social security.
(2) Social security as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. reporting if there is information on Violence, Exploitation, neglect and/or Maltreatment of Children;
b. protection of child witnesses and Child Victims by keeping them away from perpetrators in temporary care facilities;
c. Assistance for Child Victims; and
d. security of perpetrators of violence who have the potential to endanger life safety.

Paragraph 3
Provision of Facilities and Infrastructure

Article 33
(1) The Regional Government provides supporting facilities and infrastructure to maximize a sense of security, comfort, and peace for Child Victims.
(2) Provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. provision of temporary safe houses;
b. provision of education, play opportunities and good food intake, as well as daily living needs during the handling Assistance process in temporary safe houses;
c. provision of Companions in the field of health and counseling if needed;
d. provision of Companions in the field of religious spiritual mental; and
e. provide social security for vulnerable families.

Article 34
(1) Victim handling aims to provide appropriate action in handling victims and witnesses in acts of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect.
(2) Victim handling as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. complaints or reports;
b. protection needs;
c. physical and psychological health;
d. rehabilitation;
e. Social Reintegration;
f. legal assistance; and
g. Restitution.
(3) Further provisions regarding legal assistance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter f, are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

Paragraph 1
Complaints and Reports

Article 35
(1) Complaints or reports of acts of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children and/or neglect are made easily, quickly and appropriately.
(2) Every person who sees or knows of actions as referred to in paragraph (1) makes complaints or reports.
(3) Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (2) may be addressed to:
a. Village/Kelurahan Government;
b. FPKK;
c. Legal Aid Provider;
d. health service facilities;
e. law enforcement apparatus;
f. Satpol PP;
g. non-governmental organizations; and/or
h. Social Media managed by related Regional Apparatus.
(4) Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (2) may be made by:
a. directly;
b. by telephone;
c. via email or direct message to social media; and/or
d. by letter.
(5) Recipients of complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (3) must keep the identity of the reporter confidential.

Paragraph 2
Protection Needs

Article 36
(1) Protection needs aim to protect the identity and personal security of reporters, victims, and/or witnesses.
(2) Report recipients as referred to in Article 35 paragraph (3) to protect reporters, victims, and witnesses.
(3) If the report recipient does not have protection facilities and infrastructure, then the report recipient coordinates with FKPP and/or law enforcement apparatus that have facilities and infrastructure.

Paragraph 3
Physical and Psychological Health

Article 37
(1) Physical and psychological health for Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect aims to reduce and treat physical and psychological trauma.
(2) FKPP facilitates checking and treatment of physical and psychological health for Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect.
(3) FKPP facilitates visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum for Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect for investigation purposes.

Paragraph 4
Rehabilitation

Article 38
(1) Child Victims of acts of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect who experience trauma undergo gradual rehabilitation both physically, psychologically, and socially.
(2) Rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus and/or the community to help accelerate the rehabilitation process.
(3) The Regional Government guarantees the living needs of Child Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) during the rehabilitation process.
(4) During the rehabilitation process, provision of food intake, education, play world, and appreciation for children who experience trauma as referred to in paragraph (1) is adjusted to age and growth and development needs.

Paragraph 5
Restitution

Article 39
(1) Provision of Restitution in accordance with laws and regulations.
(2) The amount of Restitution paid by the perpetrator to Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect through parents, guardians, or Regional Government is based on:
a. mediation agreement results; and/or
b. court decision.
(3) The Regional Government through related Regional Apparatus oversees the Restitution payment process to be used for the needs of Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect.

CHAPTER VIII
INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER

Part One
Service Principles

Article 40
(1) Services in fulfilling the rights of women and children are implemented based on the following principles:
a. empathy;
b. confidential;
c. fast and responsive;
d. humanity;
e. documented;
f. inclusion;
g. impartial;
h. victim-oriented;
i. easy and excellent;
j. professional; and
k. sustainability.
(2) Service provision is carried out taking into account the situation and condition of reporters, victims, and/or witnesses.
(3) To improve service provision as referred to in paragraph (1) the Regional Government forms community-based integrated women and child protection institutions at the Village/Kelurahan level.

Part Two
Forms and Scope of Services

Article 41
(1) Forms and scope of Integrated Service Centers to realize protection of women and children include:
a. Complaint or report and protection Services;
b. Health and rehabilitation Services;
c. Legal Services;
d. Education and empowerment Services;
e. Economic independence Services; and
f. Spiritual Services.
(2) Provision of Services as referred to in paragraph (1) is provided to victims and/or witnesses free of charge in accordance with laws and regulations.

CHAPTER IX
ROLE OF VILLAGE/KELURAHAN GOVERNMENT

Article 42
(1) Village/Kelurahan Government is responsible for implementing protection of women and children in the Village/Kelurahan environment.
(2) Village/Kelurahan Government may involve Village Guidance Non-Commissioned Officers and/or Community Security and Order Guidance Police to provide protection for women and children.
(3) Village/Kelurahan Government may form special institutions to implement protection of women and children.

Article 43
(1) Forms of protection of women and children as referred to in Article 42 include:
a. receive complaints or reports from residents;
b. provide protection to reporters, victims, and/or witnesses;
c. conduct social security for perpetrators;
d. conduct mediation to resolve disputes;
e. impose sanctions on village apparatus who commit acts of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women; and
f. impose sanctions on village apparatus who commit acts of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect.
(2) The Regional Government provides guidance to Village/Kelurahan Government in increasing capacity to implement protection of women and children.
(3) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) is in the form of:
a. Assistance;
b. education and training; and
c. regular monitoring.

CHAPTER X
INSTITUTIONS

Article 44
(1) The Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus conducts protection of women and children.
(2) In implementing protection of women and children as referred to in paragraph (1), may involve Regional Apparatus that implements government affairs in the fields of:
a. community and village empowerment;
b. social, population control and family planning;
c. health;
d. communication and informatics;
e. industry, labor, and transmigration;
f. law;
g. mental and spiritual;
h. population administration and civil registration; and/or
i. public order, security and community protection.
(3) The Regional Government forms UPTD PPA to provide maximum Services and coordinate between Regional Apparatus in implementing protection of women and children.

Article 45
(1) UPTD PPA performs coordination functions with FPKK in implementing protection of women and children.
(2) Implementation of coordination functions with FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. coordinate and synchronize prevention, services, and empowerment of Women Victims and Child Victims;
b. maintain and develop FPKK in prevention, services, and empowerment of Women Victims and Child Victims in a networked manner and referral system;
c. conduct education on non-Discrimination, non-Violence, non-Exploitation, non-Maltreatment of Children and/or neglect values towards women and children;
d. socialize laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection of women and children;
e. collect, compile, and present data reports on Women Victims and Child Victims;
f. conduct monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of women and children.
(3) UPTD PPA may conduct guidance and Assistance for:
a. Purworejo women's forum;
b. Purworejo children's forum;
c. women and child protection task force;
d. women and child protection task force;
e. non-governmental organizations; and/or
f. other organizations related to women and children.
(4) Guidance and Assistance as referred to in paragraph (3) aims to encourage community participation in implementing protection of women and children.

Article 46
Further provisions regarding the formation of UPTD PPA are regulated by Regent Regulation established no later than 1 (one) year after this Regional Regulation is promulgated.

I. GENERAL

Constitutionally, the purpose of the state is none other than to protect the entire homeland, advance general welfare, and also educate the life of the nation. This is clearly stated in the preamble to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. The phrase protecting the entire nation contains a philosophical and moral side, where the state without discrimination must be able to be a protector of all its people. This commitment must be realized in various regulations and policies made by the state, so that it is no longer something that can be negotiated.

As is known, the position of women and children is very vulnerable to various kinds of violations including violations of their basic rights. Strategic steps need to be taken that are supported by policies, such as socializing the importance of protection and at the same time for the fulfillment of their rights both in the family, community and at every level of education. The rights and protection of women and children are basically also mandated in the 1945 Constitution, so it has become the duty and responsibility of the state to protect them. The presence of the state in this context can be realized not only by the central government, but also by Regional Governments.

Regional Government as a representation of the state has the most strategic position in implementing protection of women and children. To be able to realize it, the Regional Government needs to have a legal basis to be able to encourage various policies that provide protection for women and children. Here, the idea to be realized is to encourage Regional Governments to make programs or policies that are women-friendly and child-appropriate. The goal is none other than how later in community life, the world of education, and the business world become parts that are mutually synergistic to realize the implementation of protection of women and children.

The Regional Government of Purworejo Regency needs to anticipate the emergence of complexity of problems that occur in fulfilling protection for women and children. Policies implemented must be able to provide a sense of security and comfort for women and children. In addition, policies for implementing protection of women and children must be followed by efforts to strengthen the involvement of Regional Government, Village Government, Business World, educational institutions and the community. With this responsibility, it will be able to build a Society that cares, respects, and appreciates women and children. Efforts to empower and protect women and children in the Region require a legal umbrella that functions as a guide and direction for all parties. For this reason, based on the authority possessed by the Regional House of Representatives and the Regional Government, it is necessary to form a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children.
