CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Demak Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Demak.
4. Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit in Demak Regency.
5. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
6. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including children still in the womb.
7. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
8. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
9. Implementation of protection is a series of activities aimed at preventing violence, providing protection and recovery services and social reintegration, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation coordinated by the SKPD responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
10. Violence is any act that results in or can result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, or psychological to victims.
11. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
12. Violence Against Children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children
the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
13. Victim of Gender-Based Violence is a person who because of their gender experiences physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from state policy, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of households and society.
14. Child Victim of Violence is a child who receives harsh treatment whether physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual, and other losses resulting from state policy, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of households and society, including children who become perpetrators of violence.
15. Victim Recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, whether physically, psychologically, socially, economically or sexually.
16. Psychosocial Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the purpose of self-strengthening of victims of gender-based violence and children to resolve the problems they face
17. Integrated Services is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unit of implementation, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
18. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing society and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes who can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.
19. Safe House (shelter) is temporary housing used to provide protection for victims in accordance with determined operational standards.
20. Society is individuals, families, groups, social organizations, and/or community organizations.
Article 2
Protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:
a. Non-discrimination
b. Best interests of the child
c. Right to life, survival and development
d. Respect for the views of the child
e. Confidentiality
f. Empowerment
g. Not blaming the victim
h. Decision in the hands of the victim
i. Integration
Article 3
The objectives of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children include:
a. providing a sense of security for victims;
b. obtaining legal handling; and
c. seeking recovery and social reintegration.

CHAPTER II
RIGHTS OF VICTIMS
Article 4
(1) Every victim of gender-based violence and child victim of violence has the right to:
a. obtain protection for personal, family and property security, and be free from threats related to testimony that will be, is being, or has been given;
b. participate in the process of selecting and determining forms of protection and security support;
c. obtain information regarding case developments and court decisions;
d. obtain integrated services that are fast, appropriate, comfortable, and according to needs;
e. recovery and social reintegration;
f. obtain legal, psychological, spiritual, economic, social assistance and interpreters;
g. special handling related to victim confidentiality;
h. obtain guarantees of their rights related to their status as wife/mother, husband/father, child and household member as well as community member; and
i. obtain information about laws and regulations that protect victims.
(2) Victim Rights as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented in accordance with statutory provisions.
CHAPTER III
OBLIGATIONS AND AUTHORITY OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT
Article 5
(1) In implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children, Regional Government is obligated to:
a. carry out all efforts to prevent violence;
b. provide protection for victims of violence;
c. provide recovery and social reintegration services for victims;
d. encourage and increase community participation;
e. cooperate with service providers in prevention, protection and recovery efforts for victims of violence; and
f. supervise the implementation of services for victims involving the community.
(2) To carry out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1), Regional Government has the authority to:
a. formulate policies and programs concerning the elimination of gender-based violence and violence against children;
b. facilitate the implementation of integrated services;
c. provide facilities and infrastructure;
d. increase the capacity of service provider institutions;
e. conduct coordination and cooperation in implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children; and
f. conduct monitoring and evaluation.
(3) Regional government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must consider the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives or other persons legally responsible for victims.

CHAPTER IV
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION
Part One
Institutional
Article 6
Integrated services assist Regional Government in implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence.
Part Two
Integrated Services
Article 7
(1) Integrated services as referred to in Article 6 are established by Regional Government in accordance with statutory regulations.
(2) The establishment of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of members from regional government elements, non-governmental institutions, health service institutions, law enforcement officials, professional personnel, volunteer companions, social workers, clergy, safe houses (shelters), and social rehabilitation centers.
(3) Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for Integrated Services as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent Regulation.
Article 8
The duties of integrated services include:
a. seeking prevention;
b. recovery and social reintegration;
c. providing legal protection;
d. conducting mediation, coordination and cooperation;
e. seeking to increase community participation; and
f. monitoring and evaluation.
Part Three
Service Principles
Article 9
In carrying out duties as referred to in Article 8, integrated services apply the following principles:
a. easy;
b. appropriate; and
c. comfortable;
Part Four
Prevention Efforts
Article 10
Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 8 letter a include:
a. conducting communication, information, and education about gender-based violence and violence against children; and
b. socialization of laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and violence against children.

Part Five
Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts
Article 11
Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts as referred to in Article 8 letter b, include:
a. providing physical recovery at health service institutions;
b. providing medicolegal services;
c. assisting victim repatriation;
d. providing temporary protection in safe houses (shelters);
e. providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;
f. providing spiritual guidance services; and
g. conducting family and community preparation, economic empowerment and return to school and/or other educational institutions.
Part Six
Legal Protection
Article 12
Legal protection as referred to in Article 8 letter c includes:
a. providing assistance in legal processes;
b. providing special legal protection for child victims of violence can be done by appointing guardianship in accordance with applicable regulations.
Part Seven
Mediation, Coordination and Cooperation
Article 13
Mediation, coordination and cooperation as referred to in Article 8 letter d include:
a. conducting mediation in problem resolution with related parties;
b. conducting coordination and cooperation in handling violence cases with Provincial integrated services;
c. conducting coordination and cooperation with integrated services between regencies;
d. conducting coordination and cooperation with service provider institutions for victims of gender-based violence and children; and
e. conducting coordination with Regional Government.
Part Eight
Community Participation
Article 14
Community participation as referred to in Article 8 letter e is carried out through:
a. providing protection for victims;
b. providing emergency assistance;
c. providing advocacy for victims and/or society regarding handling cases of gender-based violence and violence against children;
d. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection determination;
e. conveying information to authorized officials related to cases of gender-based violence and violence against children;
f. fostering local wisdom in handling gender-based violence and violence against children;
g. organizing strengthening of community groups in handling gender-based violence and violence against children; and
h. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and violence against children;
Part Nine
Monitoring and evaluation
Article 15
Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in Article 8 letter f include monitoring, documentation and evaluation of cases of gender-based violence and violence against children.
Article 16
Implementation of integrated services is coordinated by the SKPD responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
CHAPTER V
COOPERATION
Article 17
(1) In implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children, regional government cooperates with Central Government, Provincial Government and Regency/City Government and other institutions.
(2) Cooperation between Regional Government and Central Government and Provincial Government as referred to in paragraph (1) includes consultation, coordination and reporting.
(3) Cooperation between Regional Government and Regency/City as referred to in paragraph (1) includes coordination, advocacy, referral, repatriation, social reintegration, facilitation of Integrated Service System development, monitoring, evaluation and reporting.
CHAPTER VI
CONTROL, DEVELOPMENT AND SUPERVISION
Article 18
(1) The Regent conducts control, development and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children.
(2) Implementation of control, development and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out daily by the SKPD responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
CHAPTER VII
FINANCING
Article 19
All activities related to the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children are charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget as well as other legal and non-binding receipts.

CHAPTER VIII
TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
Article 20
At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all implementing regulations related to protection of victims of gender-based violence and children are declared to remain valid as long as they do not conflict with this Regional Regulation.
CHAPTER IX
CLOSING PROVISIONS
Article 21
This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.
So that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Demak Regency.

I. GENERAL
In the National Action Plan for the Elimination of Violence Against Women Year 1999 it is stated that Women as mothers of the nation and children as successors of the nation are creatures created by Almighty God, so they must be protected and their honor, dignity, and self-worth safeguarded appropriately and proportionally, both legally, economically, politically, socially and culturally, without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race and group in community, national and state life.
To realize the achievement of enforcement and fulfillment of human rights, the government is obligated to provide protection and security for women and children as outlined in a policy both at national and regional levels.
The Government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, economically and socially to prevent, suppress, reduce and eliminate all forms of violence against women and children whether in the form of policies applicable both at national and regional levels.
Acts of violence against women and children can occur in the public sphere as well as in the domestic sphere (within households) and can occur at any time, in peaceful situations or conflict. Acts of violence against women and children are actions that violate, hinder, eliminate and ignore the human rights of women and children's rights.
II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE
Article 1
Sufficiently clear.
Article 2
letter a
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race, group, gender, ethnicity, culture, and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.
letter b
What is meant by "best interests of the child" is all actions concerning child victims of violence carried out by government, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, then the best interests of the child must be the primary consideration
letter c
What is meant by "life" is the most basic human right for children protected by the state, government, society, family and parents.
What is meant by "survival" is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity.
What is meant by "child development" is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.
letter d
What is meant by "respect for the views of the child" is respect for children's rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.
letter e
What is meant by "confidentiality" is every action taken to ensure victims are in safe conditions from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychology of victims.
letter f
What is meant by "Empowerment" is every action to motivate victims to be able to control their own lives to shape the future according to their wishes.
letter g
What is meant by "not blaming the victim" is an attitude and treatment of not blaming the victim for the occurrence of violence they experienced.
letter h
What is meant by "Decision in the hands of the victim" is the right of victims of gender-based violence to determine the best choice in resolving their problems.
letter i
What is meant by "integration" is that in implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is carried out by building coordination between service providers, including medical services, legal companions, psychologists, clergy, social workers, police.
