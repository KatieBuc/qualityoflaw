REGIONAL REGULATION
OGAN KOMERING ULU REGENCY
NUMBER 1 OF 2022
CONCERNING
WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND CHILD PROTECTION BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY
THE REGENT OF OGAN KOMERING ULU,

Considering : a. that every woman and child has the right to survival, growth, and proper development and the right to protection from violence and discrimination as mandated in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

b. that discriminatory treatment and violence against women and children will impact the quality of human resources in the future;

c. that in order to realize the vision and mission of development in the field of women's empowerment and child protection, respect, protection, fulfillment, enforcement, and advancement of the rights of women and children, it is deemed necessary to establish policies that can ensure the implementation of women's empowerment and child protection;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on Women's Empowerment and Child Protection;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Regency is Ogan Komering Ulu Regency.
2. Regency Government is the Government of Ogan Komering Ulu Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Ogan Komering Ulu.
4. Regional Apparatus hereinafter abbreviated as PD is the Regional Apparatus within the Government of Ogan Komering Ulu Regency.
5. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
6. Gender Equality hereinafter abbreviated as KG is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and security activities and equality in enjoying the results of development.
7. Gender Analysis is a process built systematically to identify and understand the division of labor or roles of men and women, access to and control over development resources, participation in the development process and the benefits they enjoy.
8. Gender Mainstreaming hereinafter abbreviated as PUG is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension

-3-

of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of regulations, policies, programs and development activities in the regions.

9. Gender responsive is a policy, program, activity, and budgeting that reduces differences, needs, experiences, and aspirations of men and women.
10. Gender Responsive Budget hereinafter abbreviated as ARG is a budget that is responsive to the needs of women and men with the aim of realizing gender equality and justice.
11. Women's empowerment is an effort by women to gain access to and control over economic, political, social, and cultural resources, so that women can manage themselves and increase self-confidence to be able to play a role and participate actively in solving problems, so as to be able to build capacity and self-concept.
12. Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
13. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child who is still in the womb.
14. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
15. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
16. Violence is any unlawful act that results in suffering or suffering physically, sexually, mentally, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens and/or endangers life and body and degrades dignity.
17. Special Protection for Children is a form of protection received by children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
18. Children in Emergency Situations are Children who are in an environmental situation that threatens and disrupts the life and livelihood of Children caused by natural, non-natural, and/or social factors.

-4-

19. Children in Conflict with the Law are Children who are in conflict with the law, Children who are victims of criminal acts, and Children who are witnesses to criminal acts.
20. Children from Minority and Isolated Groups are Children who are left behind, foremost, outermost in an environment with different cultures, traditions, ethnicities, races, religions from other children whose numbers are far fewer than other groups of Children.
21. Children Who Are Economically Exploited are Children who are victims of acts of prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or abilities of Children by other parties or other similar actions to obtain material benefits.
22. Children Who Are Sexually Exploited are Children who are exploited to obtain benefits through sexual organs or other body organs of Children, including but not limited to all prostitution and indecency activities.
23. Children Who Are Victims of Narcotics, Alcohol, Psychotropic, and Other Addictive Substances Abuse are Children who are persuaded, deceived, tricked, forced, and/or threatened to use narcotics, alcohol, psychotropics, and other addictive substances.
24. Children Who Are Victims of Pornography are Children who experience trauma or suffering as a result of the crime of pornography.
25. Human Immunodeficiency Virus hereinafter abbreviated as HIV is a virus that attacks the immune system and if not treated can reduce the human body's resistance until the condition of Acquired Immuno Deficiency Syndrome occurs.
26. Acquired Immuno Deficiency Syndrome hereinafter abbreviated as AIDS is a collection of symptoms of reduced self-defense ability caused by the entry of the HIV virus into a person's body.
27. Children with HIV and AIDS are Children who are infected with HIV and/or AIDS either transmitted from parents or from other risk factors.
28. Child Victims of Kidnapping are Children who are taken by someone unlawfully with the intention of placing the Child under his power or the power of another person or to place the Child in a helpless state.

-5-

29. Child Victims of Sale are Children who are transferred by a person or group of people to another party for compensation or other reasons.
30. Child Victims of Trafficking are Children who experience psychological, mental, physical, sexual, economic, and/or social suffering resulting from the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transferring, or receiving with threats of violence, use of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person who has control over the other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the exploitation of the Child.
31. Child Victims of Physical Violence are Children who experience violence that causes pain, illness, or serious injury.
32. Child Victims of Psychological Violence are Children who experience fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness, and/or severe psychological suffering.
33. Child Victims of Sexual Crimes are Children who experience forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural and/or unwanted manner, and forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
