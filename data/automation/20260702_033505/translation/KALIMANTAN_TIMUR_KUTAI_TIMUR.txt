a. that every woman has the right to protection of her person, family, honor, dignity, and property under her control, and has the right to a sense of security and protection from threats of fear to do or not do something which is a basic human right;
b. that to provide a guideline or reference for local government and related stakeholders regarding the improvement of women's quality of life and protection of women, especially for women victims of violence;
c. that to provide direction, foundation and legal certainty to all parties involved in the empowerment of women and children, it is necessary to regulate the establishment of an Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a through c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Kutai Timur Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region of Kutai Timur Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Kutai Timur.
4. Agency is the Agency for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection of Kutai Timur Regency.
5. Village is a legal community unit that has territorial boundaries with the authority to regulate and manage government affairs, the interests of the local community based on community initiative, rights of origin and/or traditional rights that are recognized and respected in the government system of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia.
6. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and in a collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman.
7. Protection...

REGENT OF KUTAI TIMUR
EAST KALIMANTAN PROVINCE
- 4-

7. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
8. Service is all efforts and actions to fulfill the rights of victims which include complaint services, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance.
9. Violence Against Women is any action that violates, hinders, eliminates enjoyment, and neglects the human rights of women.
10. Women Victims of Violence are women who experience acts of violence and discrimination whose rights are not fulfilled.
11. Health Rehabilitation is the recovery of Women Victims of violence from health disorders they suffer.
12. Social Rehabilitation is all efforts to restore and develop the abilities of Women Victims of Violence so that they can carry out their social roles and functions both in family life and/or society.
13. Repatriation is an effort to return Women Victims of Violence to their family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfill their needs.
14. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Women Victims of Violence with their family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfill the needs of victims.
15. Legal Assistance...

REGENT OF KUTAI TIMUR
EAST KALIMANTAN PROVINCE

15. Legal Assistance is legal services provided by legal companions and advocates to carry out the process of accompanying witnesses and/or Women Victims of Violence.
16. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for Women Victims of Violence.
17. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is an integrated service unit established by the Regional Government to provide Services to Women Victims of Violence and children comprehensively including complaint service, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance.
18. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for UPTD PPA services in providing complaint services, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for Women Victims of Violence and children.
19. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to Women Victims of Violence in accordance with predetermined standards.

Article 3

This Regional Regulation aims to:

e. increase the commitment of the Regional Government in implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women; and
f. encourage institutions that handle the duties and functions of women's empowerment in the Regional Government and community institutions in the region to improve performance in efforts to protect women.

CHAPTER II
RIGHTS OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

Every woman victim of violence has the right:
a. to be respected for her dignity and dignity as a human being;
b. to receive protection from family, community, Regional Government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court;
c. to recovery of physical, psychological and sexual health according to the suffering experienced by Women Victims of Violence;
d. to special handling related to the confidentiality of Women Victims of Violence;
e. to assistance by social workers and legal assistance at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
f. to religious guidance services; and
g. to determine their own decisions.

1. GENERAL

Protection of women is important because women are a vulnerable or subordinated group, especially in connection with the many cases of violence against women who become victims.

Furthermore, in the National Action Plan for the Elimination of Violence against Women of 1999 it is stated that women as mothers of the nation and children as successors of the nation are creatures created by Almighty God, so they must be protected and their honor, dignity and self-esteem must be maintained in a reasonable and proportional manner, both legally, economically, politically, socially and culturally without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race and class.

Although efforts have been made to protect women and there are government policies through laws and regulations that have firmly formulated efforts to protect women, including efforts to protect women victims of violence, in reality the intensity or number of violence against women in Kutai Timur Regency is still high while services and protection for women in Kutai Timur Regency have not been carried out optimally. Empirically in Kutai Timur Regency, an important factor that influences the process of prevention and protection of women so far is because in existing laws and regulations, there are no clear regulations regarding the procedures and procedures for prevention and protection.
