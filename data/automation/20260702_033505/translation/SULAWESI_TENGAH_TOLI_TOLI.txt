REGENT OF TOLITOLI
CENTRAL SULAWESI PROVINCE
REGIONAL REGULATION OF TOLITOLI REGENCY NUMBER 2 YEAR 2023
CONCERNING
PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY
REGENT OF TOLITOLI,

Considering: a. that violence against women and children is a crime against human rights, thus it becomes the state's obligation to protect from all forms of violence and discrimination;
b. that to fulfill the rights of women and children as well as part of the regional government's efforts to protect women and children from exploitation, neglect and discrimination;
c. that to provide direction, foundation and legal certainty in the protection of women and children, regional regulation is required;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

In View of: 1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 29 Year 1959 concerning the Establishment of Level II Regions in Sulawesi (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 1959 Number 74, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 1822);
3. Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 244, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 Year 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2015 Number 58, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);

With Joint Approval of
REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF TOLITOLI REGENCY
and
REGENT OF TOLITOLI

DECIDES:

To Establish: REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE.

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Child is a person who has not reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a Child still in the womb.
2. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and community organizations and/or associations.
3. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
4. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty;
5. Violence against Women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
6. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child.
7. Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of Violence.
8. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that Violence against Women will occur, is occurring or has occurred;
9. Protection of Children is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from Violence and discrimination.
10. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
11. Service is action taken as soon as possible to Victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that Violence against Victims will occur, is occurring or has occurred.
12. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has expertise in accompanying Victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of Violence Victims.
13. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for strengthening Violence Victims to resolve the problems they face.
14. Repatriation is an effort to return Women and Child Victims of Violence to family members, or communities that can provide protection and fulfill their needs.
15. Counseling is a process of providing objective and complete information, conducted systematically with interpersonal communication guidance, counseling techniques and mastery of clinical knowledge aimed at helping someone recognize their current condition, problems being faced, and determine solutions or efforts to overcome these problems.
16. Rehabilitation is the recovery of Victims from psychosocial disorders and restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
17. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family, society, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
18. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service provider institution for Victims established by the Regional Government.
19. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing complaint handling services, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance as well as repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Child Victims of Violence.
20. Social Welfare Organizing Institution hereinafter abbreviated as LPKS is an institution or place of social service that implements social welfare for Children in conflict with the law, established by the ministry of social affairs.
21. Safe House is temporary housing, provided to give protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
22. Region is Tolitoli Regency.
23. Regent is the Regent of Tolitoli.
24. Regional Government is the Regent of Tolitoli as an element of Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
25. Regional Apparatus is an assistant element to the Regent of Tolitoli and the Regional House of Representatives in the Implementation of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Regency Region.
26. Regional House of Representatives of Tolitoli Regency hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.

Article 2

(1) The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:
a. rights of women and children victims of violence;
b. Protection of Women victims of violence;
c. protection of children;
d. P2TP2A;
e. cooperation, obligations and responsibilities;
f. information and reporting system;
g. guidance and supervision; and
h. funding.
(2) Protection of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, includes:
a. prevention of acts of Violence;
b. integrated services for Victims of acts of Violence; and
c. empowerment of Victims of acts of Violence.
(3) Protection of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, includes:
a. Protection of Children who become Victims of acts of Violence; and
b. Special Protection of children.

CHAPTER II
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 3

Women and Child Victims of acts of Violence receive rights, including:
a. right to be respected for dignity and worth as human beings;
b. right to recovery;
c. right to determine their own decisions;
d. right to obtain information;
e. right to confidentiality;
f. right to social Rehabilitation;
g. right to complaint handling that is fast, appropriate, comfortable and according to needs;
h. right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process;
i. right to assistance;
j. right to security; and
k. right to restitution.

Article 4

Child Victims of acts of Violence in addition to receiving rights as referred to in Article 3, also receive special rights, including:
a. right to survival, growth and development;
b. right to basic population services;
c. right to equal protection;
d. right to be free from various stigma; and
e. right to obtain freedom.

CHAPTER III
PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Part One
General

Article 5

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of Violence that occur in public or private life in the form of:
a. physical violence;
b. sexual violence;
c. economic violence;
d. social violence;
e. psychological violence;
f. domestic neglect;
g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and
h. threats of certain actions.

Article 6

Every person who hears, sees or knows of the occurrence of Violence as referred to in Article 5 makes efforts to:
a. prevent the continuation of criminal acts;
b. provide protection to victims;
c. provide emergency assistance; and
d. assist in the complaint process and submission of applications for protection orders.

Part Two
Prevention of Acts of Violence

Article 7

(1) The Regional Government conducts Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in Article 2 paragraph (2) letter a by:
a. socializing laws and regulations;
b. conducting empowerment and awareness to families, parents and communities by providing information, guidance and/or counseling;
c. making efforts to increase the quantity and quality of formal, non-formal and informal education;
d. making efforts to build community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of Women and Children from Violence;
e. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement officials, government officials, universities, religious institutions/organizations and various non-governmental organizations in efforts to prevent Violence against Women and Children;
f. establishing a Violence prevention system, mapping locations or areas prone to Violence;
g. increasing community awareness that respects Women and protects Children;
h. establishing P2TP2A at the Regency level and Women and Children Protection Service Posts at the Sub-district, Village and Sub-district levels; and
i. establishing a women and children protection task force.
(2) In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be implemented by:
a. Family and/or closest relatives;
b. Community;
c. vertical agencies;
d. law enforcement officials;
e. educational institutions;
f. health institutions;
g. religious institutions/organizations
h. social community institutions; and
i. business world and other institutions.
(3) Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus that has the main tasks and functions in the field of Women empowerment and Children protection.

Article 8

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to prepare programs or activities for Prevention as referred to in Article 7 in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan.
(2) Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

Part Three
Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 9

(1) The Regional Government implements integrated services for Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 2 paragraph (2) letter b through activities:
a. complaint services;
b. health services;
c. Counseling;
d. psychological services
e. spiritual guidance;
f. social rehabilitation services;
g. legal assistance and Assistance services;
h. safe house services; and/or
i. repatriation and social reintegration.
(2) In the event that the Regional Government does not yet have counseling personnel as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, it may request assistance from social workers, volunteer Companions, and/or spiritual counselors.
(3) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:
a. special service rooms in police lines;
b. expert and professional personnel;
c. service centers and Safe Houses; and/or
d. other facilities and infrastructure needed for victim recovery including perpetrators.
(4) The implementation of services for Victims of acts of Violence is carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A and other social institutions.
(5) P2TP2A and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (4) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner.
(6) Integrated Services for Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), paragraph (3), paragraph (4) and paragraph (5) are implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures.

Article 10

(1) Complaint services as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) letter a are implemented to receive reports of acts of Violence.
(2) Reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may come from:
a. victims;
b. referrals; or
c. outreach.

Article 11

(1) Health services as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) letter b, are implemented using health facilities owned by the Regional Government or other referral hospitals by providing medical treatment and health recovery services for Victims.
(2) Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) are performed by health personnel to Victims in accordance with professional standards, standard operating procedures and the medical needs of Victims.
(3) Health services as referred to in paragraph (1), through efforts:
a. anamnesis to Victims;
b. examination of Victims;
c. providing first aid;
d. health recovery both physical and psychological, making efforts to prevent disease and other health disorders;
e. counseling;
f. referring to more adequate health service facilities if necessary, may refer to other parties with a multidisciplinary approach, including health services, medicolegal, psychosocial Assistance and legal assistance; and/or
g. providing information to the police.
(4) In addition to the efforts as referred to in paragraph (3) for certain cases, health personnel may provide reproductive health services according to medical needs.
(5) In providing services as referred to in paragraph (3) and paragraph (4), health personnel must make medical records in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
(6) For every medical action to be performed by health personnel, there must be informed consent from the Victim or their family in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
(7) For investigation purposes, authorized health personnel must make a visum et repertum and/or visum et repertum psychiatricum or make a medical certificate.
(8) Health personnel have the right to receive legal protection.

Article 12

Legal assistance and protection services as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) letter g include:
a. providing protection in Safe Houses;
b. providing legal information to Victims;
c. providing Assistance for Victims as witnesses from the investigation process to the verdict;
d. providing special legal protection for Child Victims of acts of Violence may be done by appointing guardianship in accordance with laws and regulations.

Article 13

(1) Repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) letter i are implemented with the following principles:
a. voluntary;
b. safe and dignified;
c. respect for rights.
(2) In the event that the regional government cannot yet implement Repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in paragraph (1), it may request assistance from related parties.
(3) Social services to organize social welfare for Children in conflict with the law are carried out by the Regional Government together with LPKS and other social institutions.
(4) LPKS and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) are rehabilitation and temporary shelter places for Children in conflict with the law before repatriation and social reintegration are implemented.

Part Four
Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 14

(1) Empowerment of Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 2 paragraph (2) letter c conducted by the Regional Government in the form of:
a. job training;
b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
c. capital assistance.
(2) Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.

CHAPTER IV
PROTECTION OF CHILDREN

Part One
Protection of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

Paragraph 1
Stages of Protection of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 15

Stages of protection for Child Victims of acts of Violence, include:
a. prevention of acts of Violence against Children;
b. handling of Child Victims of acts of Violence; and
c. rehabilitation of Child Victims of acts of Violence.

Paragraph 2
Prevention of Violence Against Children

Article 16

(1) Prevention of acts of Violence against Children as referred to in Article 15 letter a is implemented by:
a. forming a work network in efforts to prevent Violence against Children;
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of Violence prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, private sector and Non-Governmental Organizations; and
c. socializing laws and regulations related to the protection of Child Victims of Violence.
(2) Prevention of acts of Violence against Children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus that has the main tasks and functions in the field of Women empowerment and Children protection.

Article 17

Prevention of acts of Violence against Children in addition to being implemented by regional apparatus as referred to in Article 16 paragraph (2), also involves:
a. parents and/or family;
b. community;
c. educational institutions;
d. child social welfare institutions; and/or
e. other institutions engaged in child protection care.

Paragraph 3
Handling of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 18

Handling of Child Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 15 letter b is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Paragraph 4
Rehabilitation of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 19

(1) Rehabilitation of Child Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 15 letter c is carried out in the form of:
a. legal assistance services;
b. health rehabilitation services;
c. social rehabilitation services; and/or
d. repatriation and social reintegration services.
(2) In rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with:
a. vertical agencies;
b. provincial government;
c. other regency/city governments; village government;
d. non-governmental organizations;
e. private parties;
f. community; and/or
g. family.

Part Two
Special Protection of Children

Article 20

(1) Special protection of Children as referred to in Article 2 paragraph (3) letter b is given to:
a. Child Victims of physical, psychological, sexual Violence and neglect;
b. Children in conflict with the law;
c. Children from Minority and Isolated Groups;
d. Children who are exploited economically and/or sexually;
e. Children who become Victims of Pornography;
f. Child Victims of Sexual Crimes;
g. Children who become Victims of Narcotics, Alcohol, Psychotropic and Other Addictive Substance Abuse;
h. Children with HIV/AIDS;
i. Children with Disabilities;
j. Child Victims of maltreatment and neglect;
k. Children with Deviant Social Behavior;
l. Child Victims of Terrorism Networks;
m. Child Victims of Kidnapping, Sale and/or Trafficking; and
n. Children who become Victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their parents' condition.
(2) The Regional Government in implementing handling of special protection of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER V
INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER FOR PROTECTION OF
WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Article 21

(1) In order to provide services for Victims of acts of Violence, the Regional Government establishes P2TP2A.
(2) The appointment of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Regent's Decree.

Article 22

(1) P2TP2A as referred to in Article 21 has the duty to:
a. provide services;
b. provide protection to Victims of acts of Violence, reporters and witnesses; and
c. conduct empowerment of Victims of acts of Violence.
(2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a include:
a. receiving complaints/reports of Violence against Women and/or Children, consultation, and counseling;
b. receiving and sending case referrals from Regency/City Regional Governments or institutions established by Regency/City Regional Governments;
c. providing legal Assistance assistance;
d. health;
e. social rehabilitation;
f. legal services; and
g. repatriation and social reintegration.

CHAPTER VI
COOPERATION, OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One
Cooperation

Article 23

In handling Child Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 18, the Regional Government may cooperate with:
a. vertical agencies;
b. provincial government;
c. other regency/city governments;
d. village government;
e. non-governmental organizations; and
f. private parties;

Article 24

(1) In certain cases the Regional Government may cooperate with:
a. Other regional governments;
b. Police;
c. Advocates;
d. health personnel;
e. psychologist personnel;
f. social workers;
g. volunteer Companions;
h. spiritual counselors;
i. Other law enforcers, to assist Victims in the court process;
j. National Commission on Violence Against Women;
k. Indonesian Child Protection Commission or Child Protection Institution; and/or
l. certain parties desired for the benefit of Victims.
(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:
a. exchange of data and information
b. socialization;
c. provision of legal assistance;
d. health services;
e. psychiatric/psychological services;
f. victim Assistance;
g. Counseling;
h. social rehabilitation;
i. spiritual guidance; and/or
j. repatriation and social reintegration.

Part Two
Obligations and Responsibilities

Article 25

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to Women and Children from acts of Violence are the joint responsibility of the Regional Government and the Community.

Article 26

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 25, include:
a. establishing, implementing policies, programs, and conducting cooperation activities in the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;
b. facilitating the establishment of Women and Children protection institutions from acts of Violence and providing support for facilities and infrastructure;
c. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence according to regional financial capacity;
d. guiding and supervising the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;
e. providing protection services for Women and Child Victims of acts of Violence;
f. encouraging and increasing community participation; and
g. appointing parents and/or substitute family caregivers as a protection measure for Children who become Victims of acts of Violence.

CHAPTER VII
COMMUNITY AND BUSINESS WORLD PARTICIPATION

Article 27

(1) Every person may participate in providing protection to Women and Children from acts of Violence.
(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be done by:
a. preventing and stopping the continuation of acts of Violence;
b. providing protection to Victims;
c. providing first aid to Victims of acts of Violence;
d. reporting acts of Violence against Women and/or Children;
e. independently socializing the rights of Women and Children;
f. strengthening community social institutions in handling acts of Violence against Women and Children; and/or
g. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to acts of Violence against Women and Children.

Article 28

(1) The Regional Government encourages the business world to participate in the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.
(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:
a. socialization to employees within the company environment regarding the dangers of acts of Violence against Women and Children; and
b. assistance as a form of corporate social responsibility.

CHAPTER VIII
INFORMATION AND REPORTING SYSTEM

Article 29

(1) The Regional Government prepares and compiles an information and reporting system for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.
(2) The information and reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER IX
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 30

(1) The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of P2TP2A.
(2) Guidance and supervision are carried out by the Regional Apparatus that has the duties and responsibilities in the field of Women empowerment and Children protection.
(3) The results of the implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (2) must be reported to the Regional Government.

CHAPTER X
FUNDING

Article 31

Funding for the Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence, may be sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and/or
b. other lawful sources that are non-binding in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 32

(1) The Regional Government may provide funding assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or non-governmental organizations that implement protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.
(2) Funding assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with regional financial capacity and is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

CHAPTER XI
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 33

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Tolitoli Regency.

I. GENERAL

The Preamble to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia states that the State has an obligation to provide protection to every citizen. Article 28 G paragraph (1) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia clearly states that: every person has the right to protection of self, family, honor, dignity, and property under their control, and has the right to a sense of security and protection from the threat of fear to do or not do something that is a human right. Violence is a manifestation of human emotional behavior, rather than rational behavior.

In the context of protection of crime victims, there are preventive and repressive efforts carried out, both by the community and the government (through its law enforcement officials), such as providing protection or supervision from various threats that can endanger the victim's life, providing adequate medical and legal assistance. Protection means sheltering something from dangerous things, that something can be interests or objects or goods. In addition, protection also contains the meaning of patronage given by someone to someone weaker. Thus, legal protection can be interpreted as all government efforts to guarantee legal certainty to provide protection to its citizens so that their rights as citizens are not violated, and for those who violate them, sanctions may be imposed in accordance with applicable regulations.

Article 5
Letter a
What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or defects on someone's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causing death.

Letter b
What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Letter c
What is meant by "economic violence" is an action that restricts humans from working inside or outside the home to produce money and goods, including allowing humans who work to be exploited, while someone does not meet the economic needs of the family.

Letter d
What is meant by "social violence" is as an act of a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life of a person or can cause physical damage or damage to other people's property.

Letter e
What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering in a person.

Letter f
What is meant by "Domestic Neglect" is:
a. actions that result in non-fulfillment;
b. children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
c. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should;
d. actions of neglecting people within their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person;
e. actions that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Sufficiently clear.

Letter h
What is meant by "threats of certain actions" is any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict someone's fundamental freedom.
