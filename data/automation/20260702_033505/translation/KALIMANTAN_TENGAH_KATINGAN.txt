CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is Katingan Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Government of Katingan Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Katingan.
4. Regional People's Representative Council hereinafter referred to as DPRD is the Regional People's Representative Council of Katingan Regency.
5. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
6. Woman is an adult human of female sex and a person recognized by law as a woman.
7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a Child in the womb.
8. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public and/or in private life.
9. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in physical, mental, sexual, psychological misery or suffering, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens the integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.

- 4 -
10. Violence against women is any action or possible result of misery or suffering to women physically, mentally, sexually or psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public realm or private life.
11. Women and child victims of violence are individuals or groups who experience misery and/or suffering as a result of violence and/or threats of violence.
12. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear of or have occurred violence against women.
13. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
14. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing of, during or after violence against women and children has occurred.
15. Companion is a person or representative from an institution/organization/agency providing services to victims of violence against women and children who are trained or have expertise in accordance with their competence and authority in providing assistance to victims of violence in accordance with the type of service provided.
16. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction to be able to carry out social functions normally.
17. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
18. Legal Aid is legal services provided by companions and/or legal apparatus including provision of legal consultation, exercising power of attorney, representing, accompanying, defending and taking other legal actions for victims of violence in accordance with their competence and authority.
19. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a functional technical implementation unit as organizer of integrated services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination and other problems.
20. Standard Operating Procedures hereinafter abbreviated as SOP are standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from handling complaints, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, legal aid, psychological recovery/services, mediation to repatriation and social reintegration.
21. Minimum Service Standards abbreviated as SPM are performance benchmarks for UPTD PPA services in providing complaint handling services, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, legal aid, psychological recovery/services, mediation to repatriation and social reintegration.
22. Social Protection House is temporary housing, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE OF REGULATION

Article 2

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;

- 5 -
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. best interests of victims;
e. local wisdom;
f. right to life, survival and development of children;
g. respect for victims' opinions; and
h. legal certainty.

Article 3

Protection of Women and Children victims of violence aims to:
a. prevent all forms of violence against women and children;
b. protect women and children and provide services to Women and Child victims of violence; and
c. empower Women and Child victims of violence.

Article 4

The Scope of Regulation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence includes:
a. victims' rights;
b. authority, obligations and responsibilities;
c. institutions;
d. minimum service standards;
e. social protection house;
f. reporting;
g. guidance and supervision;
h. community participation; and
i. funding.

CHAPTER III
VICTIMS' RIGHTS

Article 5

Every Woman and Child victim of violence has the right:
a. to have their dignity respected as human beings;
b. to receive protection from family, community, Regional Government and other parties either temporarily or based on determination of Protection Order from the Court;
c. to receive recovery health services physical, psychological or sexual according to the suffering experienced by victims of violence;
d. to receive special handling related to victim confidentiality;
e. to receive assistance by companions and legal aid at every level of examination process in accordance with statutory provisions;
f. to receive spiritual guidance services.

Article 6

Child victims of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 5, also receive special rights as follows:
a. respect for survival, growth and development;
b. to education, health and access to parents during the handling process; and
c. to receive equal protection related to status, citizenship, race, skin color, gender, language, religion, politics, disability, birth or other status.

CHAPTER IV
AUTHORITY, OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 7

(1) The Regional Government has authority and responsibility for Protection of Women and Children victims of violence.
(2) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. guaranteeing the rights of women and children without discrimination in accordance with statutory regulations;
b. implementing policies for protection of Women and Children victims of violence based on statutory regulations;
c. establishing policies, programs and activities for protection of Women and Children victims of violence;
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of violence;
e. allocating budget in organizing protection of Women and Children victims of violence;
f. fostering and supervising the organization of protection of Women and Children victims of violence;
g. improving quality and competence of human resources providing services/assistance to victims of violence against women and children;
h. cooperating with the community and/or community organizations in organizing women's protection in accordance with statutory regulations;
i. taking preventive actions in order to prevent violations of women's and children's rights.
(3) The responsibilities of the Regional Government in efforts to protect women and children as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (1) are carried out by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.

Article 8

(1) The community can participate and be responsible in services, prevention, assistance, recovery and monitoring of victims of violence.
(2) Community participation and responsibility as referred to in paragraph (1) are organized in the form of:
a. preventing acts of violence against Women and Children;
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against Women and Children to authorized parties;
c. providing emergency assistance; and
d. providing protection to victims of violence.

CHAPTER V
INSTITUTIONS

Article 9

(1) The established UPTD PPA can accommodate institutions/work units that provide services for victims of violence such as safe houses, child social protection houses, trauma center protection houses, women's social protection houses, shelters and other service activities in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations.
(2) Forms of services implemented by UPTD PPA include:
a. complaint handling;
b. health rehabilitation;
c. social rehabilitation;
d. legal aid;
e. psychological recovery/services;

- 7 -
f. mediation; and
g. repatriation and social reintegration.
(3) Provision of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (2) is implemented in accordance with SPM.

CHAPTER VI
MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

Article 10

(1) SPM is implemented by the Regional Technical Implementation Unit for Protection of Women and Children aimed at ensuring Women and Children victims of violence receive the minimum services needed.
(2) SPM as referred to in paragraph (1) includes services for:
a. handling complaints/reports of victims of violence against women and children;
b. health rehabilitation for women and children victims of violence;
c. social rehabilitation for women and children victims of violence;
d. legal aid for women and children victims of violence;
e. psychological recovery/services for women and children victims of violence;
f. mediation for women and children victims of violence; and
g. repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence.

Article 11

(1) SPM as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (3) has performance indicators including:
a. coverage of Women and Children victims of violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers within UPTD PPA;
b. coverage of Women and Children victims of violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at Community Health Centers and Hospitals;
c. coverage of social rehabilitation services provided by trained social rehabilitation officers for Women and Children victims of violence within UPTD PPA;
d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for Women and Children victims of violence within UPTD PPA;
e. coverage of legal aid services for women and children victims of violence;
f. coverage of psychological recovery/services for women and children victims of violence;
g. coverage of mediation services for women and children victims of violence;
h. coverage of repatriation services for women and children victims of violence; and
i. coverage of social reintegration services for women and children victims of violence.
(2) Determination of SPM UPTD PPA performance indicators for Women and Children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is the minimum target that must be achieved.

CHAPTER VII
SOCIAL PROTECTION HOUSE

Article 12

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to establish a Social Protection House and/or Safe House.
(2) The Social Protection House and/or Safe House as referred to in paragraph (1) is to ensure implementation of UPTD PPA services.

- 8 -
(3) For organizing services as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government can utilize social protection houses and/or Safe Houses owned by the community or other social service institutions.

CHAPTER VIII
REPORTING

Article 13

(1) The Regent is responsible for making reports on implementation of SPM through UPTD PPA, submitted to the Governor with copies submitted to the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection and the Minister of Home Affairs.
(2) Reporting of implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted annually.
(3) The form of reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with statutory provisions.

CHAPTER IX
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 14

(1) The Regent conducts guidance and supervision of UPTD PPA organization.
(2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. supervision;
d. provision of facilities;
e. monitoring; and
f. evaluation.
(3) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) is in order to realize the objectives of protection of women and Child victims of violence according to minimum service standards.

CHAPTER X
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 15

(1) In organizing protection of Women and Children victims of violence, the community can:
a. form family partners at the village and sub-district level coordinating with Damang or Mantir Adat and Community Leaders;
b. form women and child protection units within community organizations;
c. independently socialize the rights of Women and Children;
d. provide first aid to victims; and
e. report to authorized agencies if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, family partners, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector, and mass media.

CHAPTER XI
FUNDING

Article 16

Funding for organizing protection of Women and Children victims of violence is sourced from:

- 9 -
a. State Budget
b. Regional Budget; and
c. other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with statutory provisions.

CHAPTER XII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 17

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

So that everyone knows of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Katingan Regency.

I. GENERAL

Children are the future of the nation and the next generation of the nation's ideals, so that every child has the right to survival, growth and development, participation and the right to protection from acts of violence and discrimination as well as civil rights and freedoms.

Likewise women who play a role as mothers who give birth to healthy, strong, resilient, noble, potential and quality children. Through the role of women and families, society and the state will produce resilient generations who will continue the nation's ideals.

Acts of violence against women and children are violations of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and nature without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Katingan Regency so as to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity, protection of Women and Children victims of violence needs to be carried out so it needs to be regulated in a Regional Regulation.

So far, statutory regulations governing protection of women and Child victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the regions so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, especially in terms of prevention, organization, handling, empowerment, guidance and supervision of women's protection in Katingan Regency.

Article 2
Letter a
What is meant by "respect and fulfillment of victims' rights" is the guarantee of fulfillment of victims' basic rights.

Letter b
What is meant by "justice" is a state where every person, both men and women, are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to achieve welfare.

What is meant by "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

-11 -
Letter c
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is attitudes and treatment toward victims without making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and class.

Letter d
What is meant by "best interests of victims" is all best actions concerning victims carried out by government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, so the best interests of victims must be the primary consideration.

Letter e
What is meant by "local wisdom" is good cultural values that exist in Katingan society.

Letter f
What is meant by "right to life, survival and development of children" is a moral principle that every person has the right to live, maintain life and livelihood, and improve their standard of living.

Letter g
What is meant by "respect for victims' opinions" is that the government recognizes and ensures that victims can express their opinions and are given the opportunity to express views freely on everything that affects them.

Letter h
What is meant by "legal certainty" is the existence of legal norms that do not conflict with other regulations and provide legal security for victims of violence from the arbitrariness of certain parties.
