REGIONAL REGULATION OF KONAWE KEPULAUAN REGENCY
NUMBER 7 YEAR 2021
CONCERNING
PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY
REGENT OF KONAWE KEPULAUAN,

Considering:
a. that every citizen has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and has the right to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence;

b. that protection of women and children victims of violence is one aspect of the duties and responsibilities of regional government as mandated by law in providing protection and services to the community;

c. that the Regulation of the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection Number 1 Year 2010 concerning Minimum Service Standards for Integrated Services for women and children victims of violence requires regulation regarding handling of women and children victims of violence in the region;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b, and c above, it is deemed necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

In View of:

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Konawe Kepulauan Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Government of Konawe Kepulauan Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Konawe Kepulauan.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Konawe Kepulauan Regency.
5. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
6. Woman is a person who has female genitalia, can experience menstruation, pregnancy, childbirth, breastfeeding and includes persons recognized by law as Women.

-4-

7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child in the womb.
8. Violence is any act against a person that results in suffering or distress physically, sexually, psychologically, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.
9. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or distress to women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.
10. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in suffering or distress physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.
11. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, serious wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or unconsciousness and/or causes death.
12. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, distrust or severe psychological suffering to a person.
13. Sexual violence is any act in the form of forced sexual intercourse, sexual abuse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural and/or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
14. Exploitation is an action with or without the victim's consent which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or abilities of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits.

-5-

15. Victim is a woman and child who experiences physical suffering, psychological suffering, sexual suffering, either directly or indirectly resulting from acts of violence.
16. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
18. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
19. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by Professional personnel according to their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
20. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for self-strengthening of victims of violence to resolve the problems they face.
21. Repatriation is an effort to return women and children victims of violence to family or community who can provide protection and fulfill their needs.
22. Rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological, and social conditions so that they can carry out their roles again properly both in the family and in society.
23. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection and fulfill the needs of victims.
24. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organizations that provide assistance.
25. Integrated Service Center hereinafter referred to as PPT is a generic term that can be used by various institutions that provide services for victims of violence such as Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, Safe House, Child Social Protection House, Trauma Center Protection House, Women's Social Protection House, Shelter, etc.

- 6-

26. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing services for handling reports/complaints, health services, Social Rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance, as well as Repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Children Victims of Violence.
27. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in carrying out activities for organizing prevention and protection of women and children victims of violence.
28. Safe House is temporary housing, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with established standards.

CHAPTER II
SCOPE

Article 2

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:
a. forms of violence;
b. victims' rights;
c. obligations and responsibilities;
d. organization of protection;
e. guidance and supervision;
f. reporting; and
g. funding;

CHAPTER III
FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 3

Forms of violence against women and children are:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect; and
e. exploitation.

-7-

Article 4

Physical violence as referred to in Article 3 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, serious wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or cause death.

Article 5

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 3 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 6

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 3 letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Article 7

Neglect as referred to in Article 3 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, carried out by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should, carried out by parents, guardians or any other party responsible for their care;

- 8-

c. acts of neglecting a person within their household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Article 8

Exploitation as referred to in Article 3 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or abilities of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or sexual abuse activities.

CHAPTER IV
VICTIMS' RIGHTS

Article 10

Every victim has the right to obtain:
a. protection from individuals, groups and institutions both governmental and non-governmental.
b. access and information about the existence of complaint centers, PPT, and all matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and involvement in the decision-making process related to assistance and case development.
c. services that include medical, extensive medicolegal, psychosocial and legal services.

-9-

d. handling that does not stop at physical and psychological healing, but until the victim can live life again in society including restoration of their good name.
e. confidential handling which includes guarantees for victims that their identity will not be disseminated, medical care and legal handling.
f. assistance provided by psychologists to victims suffering from trauma/other mental problems to restore the victim's mental condition.
g. legal aid assistance provided by persons and/or legal aid institutions to victims at every level of examination and during the legal process.
h. guarantees of rights related to victim status

CHAPTER V
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 10

Obligations and responsibilities in organizing protection of women and children victims of violence are the joint responsibility of:
a. regional government;
b. community;
c. family and parents.

Article 11

(1) Regional Government is obliged and responsible for providing Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence.
(2) The obligations and responsibilities of Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. implementing protection policies for women and children victims of violence applied by the government;
b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for protection of women and children victims of violence;
c. Collecting data and information about women and children victims of violence;

-10-

d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for implementing Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
e. establishing an Integrated Service Center;
f. allocating budget in organizing Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
g. Guiding, supervising and evaluating the organization of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

Article 12

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 10 letter b, are organized in the form of community participation.
(2) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
a. preventing acts of Violence against victims;
b. establishing complaint posts at the village/kelurahan level;
c. establishing women and child protection units within community organizations;
d. independently conducting socialization of women's rights and children's rights;
e. providing first aid to victims; and
f. reporting to the competent authority if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
(3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector, and mass media.
(4) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 13

The obligations of family and parents as referred to in Article 10 letter c, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as part of family members.

-11-

CHAPTER VI
ORGANIZATION OF PROTECTION

Part One
Institutional

Article 14

(1) In order to provide services and protection to women and children from violence, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center as a service center for women and children victims of violence.
(2) The Integrated Service Center as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of elements from the Office of Population Control, Family Planning, Women's Protection and Child Protection, Health Office, Education and Culture Office, Social Office, Population Administration and Civil Registration Office, Community and Village Empowerment Office, Civil Service Police Unit, Regional General Hospital, Police Resort, Non-Governmental Organizations, and Universities.
(3) The Integrated Service Center as referred to in paragraph (2), is established by Regent's Decree.
(4) Further provisions regarding the organizational structure of the Integrated Service Center are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 15

(1) In providing Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence, the Integrated Service Center is obliged to:
a. provide services as quickly as possible and free of charge to Victims;
b. organize Protection and fulfillment of victims' rights to Medical Rehabilitation, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, social reintegration and legal assistance;
c. cooperate with certain institutions in providing translators and volunteer assistants needed for Victims;
d. network with government or private hospitals for treatment and health recovery of Victims and cooperate with witness and Victim protection institutions, social Protection houses or trauma centers owned by government, community, or other institutions for health recovery of Victims;

- 12 -

e. provide convenience, comfort, and safety for Victims;
f. maintain confidentiality of Victims;
g. provide economic empowerment;
h. provide fulfillment of legal assistance for Victims;
i. in the case that integrated service organization is carried out in a networked manner, the Integrated Service Center remains responsible for the entire referral process of services needed by Victims.
(2) Provision of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with Minimum Service Standards.

Part Two
Minimum Service Standards

Article 16

Minimum Service Standards for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence, include services for:
a. handling complaints/reports of Victims of Violence against Women and Children;
b. health services for Women and Children Victims of Violence;
c. law enforcement and legal assistance for Women and Children Victims of Violence;
d. repatriation for Women and Children Victims of Violence;
e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medicolegal for Women and Children Victims of Violence;
f. psychological services for Women and Children Victims of Violence;

Article 17

Minimum Service Standards as referred to in Article 16 have performance indicators including:
a. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers in integrated service units;

- 13-

b. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at Community Health Centers and Hospitals;
c. coverage of Social Rehabilitation services provided by trained Social Rehabilitation officers for Women and Children Victims of Violence in integrated service units;
d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for Women and Children Victims of Violence in integrated service units;
e. coverage of law enforcement from investigation level to court decisions on cases of Violence against Women and Children;
f. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive legal aid services;
g. coverage of Repatriation services for Women and Children Victims of Violence; and
h. coverage of social reintegration services for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

Part Three
Forms and Mechanisms of Prevention

Article 18

Forms of prevention of violence against women and children carried out by the Integrated Service Center, can be implemented through:
a. socialization activities of laws and regulations to the community related to protection of women's and children's rights; and
b. training of Integrated Service Center members related to implementation of their duties in preventing violence against children and women.

Article 19

(1) The Integrated Service Center in carrying out its duties and functions can coordinate with Sub-district Integrated Service Centers, and competent parties in making efforts to prevent violence against women and children.

- 14-

(2) Further provisions regarding the mechanism for implementing prevention duties as referred to in paragraph (1), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Part Four
Forms and Mechanisms of Services

Article 20

(1) Forms of services provided to women and children victims of violence, are as follows:
a. complaint services;
b. health services;
c. legal assistance;
d. repatriation;
e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medicolegal;
f. psychological services; and
g. economic empowerment.
(2) Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented according to minimum service standards established by the government and implemented by regional apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
a. social;
b. health;
c. women's empowerment and child protection; and
d. mental and spiritual.
(3) In carrying out duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (2), the regional government cooperates with government agencies, provincial government, other regency/city governments, community, family and parents.
(4) Further provisions regarding service procedures, and handling of women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 21

(1) In carrying out service duties to women and children victims of violence, the Integrated Service Center coordinates with Sub-district Integrated Service Centers.

- 15-

(2) The mechanism for implementing duties as referred to in paragraph (1), is organized according to Standard Operating Procedures regulated further by Regent Regulation.

Part Five
Assistance

Article 22

(1) Assistance is carried out by persons and/or institutions that cooperate with the Integrated Service Center.
(2) The mechanism of assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) regulated further by Regent Regulation.

Part Six
Principles of Services and Assistance

Article 23

Organization of services and assistance to victims, is carried out with principles of:
a. fast, safe, and empathetic;
b. guarantee of confidentiality;
c. easily accessible; and
d. free of charge.

CHAPTER VII
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 24

(1) Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the organization of protection of women and children victims of violence.
(2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. provision of facilities;

-16-

d. monitoring; and
e. evaluation.
(3) Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out with principles of:
a. professional;
b. transparent; and
c. accountable.

Article 25

Guidance and supervision as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), are carried out in order to realize the objectives of organizing protection of women and children victims of violence according to minimum service standards established by laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VIII
REPORTING

Article 26

(1) The Integrated Service Center reports the implementation of organizing protection of women and children victims of violence to the Regent.
(2) Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) is submitted in writing, including:
a. administration;
b. finance;
c. services; and
d. performance.
(3) Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2), is carried out at least every 3 (three) months.

- 17 -

CHAPTER IX
FUNDING

Article 27

Funds for organizing protection of women and children victims of violence, are sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
b. other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 28

(1) Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or non-governmental organizations and universities that implement protection of women and children victims of violence.
(2) Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with regional financial capacity and is implemented according to the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

CHAPTER X
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 29

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

I. GENERAL

Every citizen has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and has the right to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence. Violence is any act against a person that results in suffering or distress physically, sexually, psychologically, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.

Women and children are groups vulnerable to experiencing violence. All forms of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and crimes against human dignity, therefore legal protection is needed to protect their dignity and ensure their right to life in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.

This view is based on Article 28G paragraph (1) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia which states that "Every person has the right to protection of self, family, honor, dignity, and property under their control, and has the right to a sense of security and protection from threats of fear to do or not do something that is a human right".

Regional Government is obliged and responsible to implement efforts to protect women and children victims of violence through implementing protection policies for women and children victims of violence applied by the government, establishing policies, programs, and activities for protection of women and children victims of violence, establishing PPT, ensuring implementation of service convenience to victims, seeking effectiveness and efficiency for the victim recovery process, and seeking creation of cooperation and coordination in victim recovery efforts.

-2-

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Konawe Kepulauan Regency so they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity, protection of women and children victims of violence needs to be carried out in the form of regional regulations.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, especially in terms of services, assistance, guidance, supervision, reporting and funding for women and children victims of violence in Konawe Kepulauan Regency.

Article 23

- 4-

Letter a
What is meant by "fast" is immediate action taken without complications or simplified procedures.

What is meant by "safe" is a guarantee of protection services that are not disturbed, and served with friendly, respectful and appreciative service that feels comfortable.

What is meant by "empathetic" is actions of appreciating, respecting, loving, being friendly, and making happy that aim to please and calm the victim's heart.

Letter b
What is meant by "guarantee of confidentiality" is an effort to guarantee certainty for victims that their identity, medical care and legal handling will not be disseminated.

Letter c
What is meant by "easily accessible" is organization of services and assistance for everyone regardless of their social status, so that the service is affordable for the underprivileged or relatively sufficient for the capable.

Letter d
What is meant by "free of charge" is that service and assistance activities carried out by the Integrated Service Center are not charged to victims
