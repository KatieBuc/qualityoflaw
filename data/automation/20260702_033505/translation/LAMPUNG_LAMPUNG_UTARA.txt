CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is North Lampung Regency.

2. Regional Government is the regional head as an element of Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.

3. Regent is the Regent of North Lampung.

4. Regional People's Representative Council, abbreviated as DPRD, is the DPRD of North Lampung Regency.

5. Regional Apparatus Organization is an auxiliary element of the regional head in the administration of governmental affairs that are the authority of the Region.

6. Woman is a person who has the predicate as an adult girl, and/or as a wife/mother, who by nature and law is recognized as a woman.

7. Child is someone who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old, including a child still in the womb.

8. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband, wife, or husband, wife and their children, or father and children or mother and children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward to the third degree.

9. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.

10. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.

11. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, sexually, economically, socially and psychologically.

12. Acts of violence are women and children who experience misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.

13. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will occur, is occurring or has occurred.

14. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.

15. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.

16. Cooperation is an activity or effort carried out by several people to achieve common goals.

17. Forum for Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FP2ATK, is a forum established based on this Regional Regulation whose membership comes from relevant agencies and communities concerned with women's empowerment and child protection from acts of violence.

18. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will occur, is occurring or has occurred.

19. Companion is a social worker who has professional competence in their field.

20. Victim recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of domestic violence to be more empowered, both physically and psychologically.

21. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TPA, is a generic term that can be used by various institutions that provide services for victims of violence such as Integrated Service Centers for Women's Empowerment and Children, Safe Houses, Child Social Protection Houses, Trauma Center Protection Houses, Women's Social Protection Houses, Shelters, or integrated service provider institutions for victims established by the Regional Government.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of Victims of Acts of Violence;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. the best interests of Victims of acts of violence; and
e. the principle of the right to life, survival and development.

Article 3

The objectives of protecting women and children from acts of violence are to:

a. prevent violence against women and children including human trafficking;
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
d. provide services and Empowerment to women and children victims of violence, reporters and witnesses;
e. facilitate and mediate domestic disputes to realize harmonious and prosperous family integrity; and
f. facilitate the recovery of rights, dignity and personal value of victims.

CHAPTER III
SCOPE

Article 4

(1) The Scope of this Regional Regulation consists of:
a. protection of women; and
b. child protection.

(2) Protection of women as referred to in Article 4 letter a includes:
a. prevention of acts of violence;
b. integrated services for victims of acts of violence; and
c. empowerment of victims of acts of violence.

(3) Child protection as referred to in Article 4 letter b includes:
a. protection of children who are victims of acts of violence; and
b. special protection of children.

CHAPTER IV
IMPLEMENTATION OF
WOMEN'S PROTECTION

Part One
General

Article 5

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of violence that occur in public or private life in the form of:

a. physical violence;
b. sexual violence;
c. economic violence;
d. social violence;
e. psychological violence;
f. domestic neglect;
g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and
h. threats of certain actions.

Article 6

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence as referred to in Article 5 is obliged to make efforts to:

a. prevent the continuation of criminal acts;
b. provide protection to victims of acts of violence;
c. provide emergency assistance; and/or
d. provide assistance in the process of submitting requests for stipulation of protection orders.

Part Two
Prevention of Acts of Violence

Article 7

(1) The Regional Government conducts prevention as referred to in Article 6 letter a by:
a. socializing laws and regulations;
b. providing counseling/guidance;
c. providing education on the dangers of domestic violence;
d. conducting seminars/workshops or the like;
e. forming work networks in efforts to prevent violence;
f. coordinating, integrating, synchronizing violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
g. establishing violence prevention systems, mapping locations or areas prone to violence; and
h. increasing public awareness in behaving in accordance with religious norms and customs.

(2) In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be implemented by:
a. religious institutions;
b. family and/or closest relatives;
c. traditional leaders;
d. traditional institutions;
e. community;
f. educational institutions;
g. health institutions;
h. social community institutions; and
i. business world and other institutions.

Article 8

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to prepare prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (1) in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan;

(2) Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

Article 9

(1) Regional Apparatus Organizations that administer governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment implement prevention as referred to in Article 8;

(2) Regional Apparatus Organizations as referred to in paragraph (1) in conducting prevention may cooperate with vertical agencies.

Part Three
Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 10

(1) The Regional Government implements integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b through activities:
a. complaint services;
b. health services;
c. counseling;
d. spiritual guidance;
e. social rehabilitation services;
f. legal assistance and accompaniment services; and/or
g. repatriation and reintegration.

(2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:
a. special service rooms;
b. expert and professional personnel;
c. service centers and safe houses; and/or
d. other facilities and infrastructure needed for victim recovery.

(3) Implementation of services for victims of acts of violence is carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A and other social institutions;

(4) P2TP2A and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner;

(5) Integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (4) are implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures;

(6) Provisions regarding Standard Operating Procedures as referred to in paragraph (5) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Part Four
Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 11

(1) Empowerment of victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c conducted by the Regional Government is in the form of:
a. job training;
b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
c. capital assistance.

(2) Empowerment as referred to in paragraph 1 is in accordance with the duties of the Regional Apparatus Organization.

CHAPTER V
CHILD PROTECTION

Part One
Protection of Child Victims
of Acts of Violence

Article 12

(1) Protection of children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is the responsibility of the Regional Government.

(2) Protection of child victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by:
a. formulating policies for prevention, risk reduction for vulnerable children, handling victims and data and information systems for children;
b. organizing maintenance, care, and social rehabilitation of neglected children, both within and outside institutions;
c. providing shelters, maintenance and care for neglected children; and
d. organizing special protection for children.

Article 13

(1) Provisions regarding prevention and services for acts of violence in women's protection as referred to in Articles 7 through 12 apply mutatis mutandis to child protection from acts of violence;

(2) In addition to conducting prevention as referred to in paragraph (1), it is also done by increasing family capacity in child protection and child care skills.

Part Two
Special Protection of Children

Article 14

Special protection of children is given to:

(1)
a. Children in emergency situations;
b. Children in conflict with the law;
c. Children from minority and isolated groups;
d. Children who are exploited economically and/or sexually;
e. Children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse;
f. Children who are victims of pornography;
g. Children with HIV/AIDS;
h. Child Victims of kidnapping, sale, and/or trafficking;
i. Child victims of physical and/or psychological Violence;
j. Child victims of sexual crimes;
k. Child victims of terrorism networks;
l. Children with Disabilities;
m. Child victims of maltreatment and neglect;
n. Children with deviant social behavior; and
o. Children who are victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their Parents' condition.

(2) The regional government in implementing handling as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

CHAPTER VI
COOPERATION AND COORDINATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Part One
General

Article 15

(1) The regional government conducts cooperation and coordination for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.

(2) Cooperation and coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by Regional Apparatus Organizations that administer governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Part Two
Institutional Framework for Protection of Women and Children

Article 16

(1) The Regional Government in conducting cooperation and coordination on Protection of Women and children from acts of violence, establishes the Forum for Caring for Women and Children from Acts of Violence (FP2ATK);

(2) FP2ATK membership consists of elements:
a. regional government;
b. vertical agencies;
c. educational institutions;
d. health institutions;
e. social institutions;
f. labor institutions; and
g. community organizations concerned with the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

(3) The Organizational Structure of FP2ATK as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of:
a. policy formulation working group;
b. socialization working group;
c. service working group; and
d. data, information, monitoring and evaluation working group.

(4) Establishment and term of office of FP2ATK members as referred to in paragraph (1) are stipulated by Regent Decree.

Article 17

FP2ATK has the tasks of:

a. providing input in policy formulation;
b. coordinating with Regional Apparatus Organizations and other social institutions;
c. socializing laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
d. collecting and developing data and information related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. conducting studies, monitoring, evaluation and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
f. providing reports, suggestions, input and considerations to the Regent.

Article 18

FP2ATK as referred to in Article 16 paragraph (2) is assisted by a Secretariat at the Regional Apparatus Organization that handles women's empowerment and child protection affairs.

Article 19

Further provisions regarding the mechanism and working procedures of FP2ATK are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 20

(1) The Regional Government in order to provide services to victims of acts of violence, establishes P2TP2A.

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) has the tasks of:
a. providing services, including:
1. receiving complaints/reports of violence against women and/or children, consultation, and counseling;
2. receiving and sending case referrals from Regency/City Regional Governments or institutions established by Regency/City Regional Governments;
3. providing legal assistance and accompaniment;
4. health;
5. social rehabilitation;
6. legal services; and
7. repatriation and social reintegration.
b. providing protection to victims of acts of violence, reporters and witnesses.

(3) Appointment of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Regent Decree.

(4) Regional Apparatus supervise P2TP2A in carrying out tasks as referred to in paragraph (2).

Part Three
Cooperation

Article 21

(1) In certain cases the Regional Government may cooperate on women and children from acts of violence with:
a. other regional governments;
b. police;
c. advocates;
d. health workers;
e. social workers;
f. volunteer companions;
g. spiritual counselors;
h. other law enforcers, to assist victims in court proceedings;
i. national commission on violence against women;
j. Indonesian child protection commission or Child Protection Institution; and/or
k. certain parties desired for the benefit of victims.

(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:
a. socialization;
b. provision of legal assistance;
c. health services;
d. victim accompaniment;
e. counseling;
f. social rehabilitation;
g. spiritual guidance; and/or
h. repatriation and social reintegration.

Part Four
Coordination

Article 22

(1) The Regent coordinates protection of women and children from acts of violence with:
a. government;
b. other Regency regional governments;
c. vertical agencies;
d. educational institutions; and
e. community institutions.

(2) Coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. religious affairs;
b. health affairs;
c. education affairs;
d. social affairs;
e. legal affairs;
f. labor affairs; and
g. other affairs.

CHAPTER VII
COMMUNITY AND BUSINESS WORLD PARTICIPATION

Article 23

(1) Every person may participate in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence;

(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be done by:
a. reporting acts of violence against women and/or children;
b. providing first aid to victims of Acts of Violence;
c. independently socializing the rights of women and children; and/or
d. establishing independent social community institutions.

Article 24

In the case that social community institutions implement protection of women and children from acts of violence, they must coordinate with FP2ATK.

Article 25

(1) The Regional Government encourages the business world to participate in the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:
a. socialization to employees within the company environment regarding the dangers of acts of violence against women and children; and
b. assistance as a form of corporate social responsibility.

CHAPTER VIII
INFORMATION AND REPORTING SYSTEM

Article 26

(1) The Regional Government prepares and compiles an information and reporting system for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;

(2) The Information and reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER IX
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 27

(1) The Regional Government implements guidance and supervision of FP2ATK and other social institutions.

(2) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by Regional Apparatus Organizations that implement women's and children's empowerment affairs.

(3) Results of implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (2) must be reported to the regional government.

CHAPTER X
FINANCING

Article 28

Financing for activities of Protection of Women and Children from acts of violence may be sourced from:

a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and/or
b. other sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER XI
TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Article 29

All implementing regulations that do not conflict with this Regional Regulation remain valid.

CHAPTER XII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 30

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order for everyone to know about it, it is ordered that this Regional Regulation be promulgated by placing it in the Regional Gazette of North Lampung Regency.

Article 31

Promulgation of this Regional Regulation is carried out on the date of promulgation.

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in North Lampung Regency so they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity, protection must be provided to women and children victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation to regulate protective efforts so that institutional and regulatory support is needed that can guarantee its implementation, especially in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in North Lampung Regency.

Article 5
Sufficiently clear

Letter a.
What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or defects on a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Letter b.
What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Letter c.
What is meant by "economic violence" is an action that limits humans from working inside or outside the home to produce money and goods, including allowing working humans to be exploited, while someone does not fulfill the economic needs of the family.

Letter d.
What is meant by "social violence" is an act of a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life of a person or may cause physical damage or damage to another person's property.

Letter e.
What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering in a person.

Letter f.
What is meant by "Domestic Neglect" is:
1. actions that result in the failure to fulfill a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
2. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as appropriate;
3. actions of neglecting people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person;
4. actions that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Letter g.
What is meant by "labor neglect" is the action of any person or legal entity that results in unfair treatment of female workers whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide adequate protection from unlawful actions against workers such as haphazard recruitment, unclear shelters and workplaces, arbitrary business owners.

Letter h.
What is meant by "coercion or deprivation" of liberty is:
1. Coercion is any act or action of taking, bringing, inviting a child or woman with persuasion and/or coercion to be married or to be employed illegally.
2. Deprivation of liberty is any act or action of taking, bringing, inviting, kidnapping a child or woman to be made a wife or employed by confining, detaining or imprisoning unlawfully in a room or certain place.

Letter i.
What is meant by "threats of certain actions" is any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols or body movements, either with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom.
