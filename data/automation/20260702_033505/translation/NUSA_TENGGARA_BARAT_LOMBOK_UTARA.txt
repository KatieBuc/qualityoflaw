REGENT OF NORTH LOMBOK
WEST NUSA TENGGARA PROVINCE

REGIONAL REGULATION OF NORTH LOMBOK REGENCY
NUMBER 1 YEAR 2017

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

THE REGENT OF NORTH LOMBOK,

Considering
:
a.




that to realize the constitutional rights of every citizen in accordance with the principle of equality before the law, the Regional Government needs to strive to guarantee the protection of human rights for Women and Children;




b.
that Women and Children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience Violence, therefore they need to receive optimal Protection;




c.
that Protection as referred to in letter b, is included in mandatory affairs which are the duties, authority and responsibility of the Regional Government and its prevention must involve all parties;




d.
that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b, and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation and Protection of Women and Children.








Considering
:
1.
Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;




2.
Law Number 39 Year 1999 concerning Human Rights (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 1999 Number 165, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3886);




3.
Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2002 Number 109, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4235) as amended by Law Number 35 Year 2014 concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 297, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5606);




4.
Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2004 Number 95, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419);




5.
Law Number 26 Year 2008 concerning Establishment of North Lombok Regency in West Nusa Tenggara Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2008 Number 99, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4872);




6.
Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 244, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 Year 2015 concerning Second Amendment to Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2015 Number 58, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);




7.
Government Regulation Number 4 Year 2006 concerning Implementation and Cooperation for Recovery of Victims of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2006 Number 15, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5121);


With Mutual Approval

REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF NORTH LOMBOK REGENCY
and
REGENT OF NORTH LOMBOK

HAS DECIDED:

To Establish	: 	REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN


CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Part One
Definitions

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
Region is North Lombok Regency;
Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of Regional Government administration;

Regent is the Regent of North Lombok;
Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit within the North Lombok Regency Government;
Implementation of Protection is all actions and service efforts to guarantee and protect the rights of Women and Children organized by the Regional Government and Society;
Child is a person who has not reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a Child still in the womb;
Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by Professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery;
Assistance is activities and actions carried out by Assistants during the service process;
Assistant is a person or representative from an Institution who has expertise in providing Assistance/social worker who has professional competence in their field;
Institution is a non-governmental organization (NGO) that provides Assistance;
Supervision is a systematic monitoring process of the entire Implementation of Protection of legal assistance for Children and Women;
Society is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations;
Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the annual financial plan of the Region established by Regional Regulation;
Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an empowerment and Protection service for Women and Children established by the Government which is community-based;
Violence is any act against a Child that results in suffering or hardship physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty;
Women victims of Violence are Women who experience actions that result in suffering or hardship physically, sexually, economically or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life;
Child victims of Violence are Children who experience actions that result in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically or neglect that threatens life, body and degrades human dignity;
Children in emergency situations means Children who become refugees, Children victims of riots and unrest, Children victims of disasters and Children in conflict situations;
Children in disaster situations are natural disasters, fire disasters and riots;
Complaint handling is a series of actions carried out by integrated service providers to follow up on reports of acts of Violence against Women and Children submitted by victims, families or Society;
Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction to be able to carry out social functions properly;


Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or Society that can provide Protection and fulfillment of needs for victims;
Integrated service unit or abbreviated as UPT is a unified unit that carries out integrated service functions for Women and Children victims of Violence;
Safe house is a temporary shelter that aims to provide Protection and a sense of security to victims of Violence;
Child Protection Institution or abbreviated as LPA is an Independent Institution in the field of Child Protection and fulfillment of Children's rights that is community-based;
North Lombok Regency Integrated Service Center or abbreviated as PPT KLU is a form of integrated service delivery based on community health centers to handle victims of Violence against Women and Children which includes medical, medicolegal, and psychosocial services.

Part Two
Principles and Objectives

Article 2

Protection of Women and Children is carried out with attention to religion, customs, social culture of Society by prioritizing the basic principles of Women's rights and Children's rights.
The basic principles of Protection of Women's and Children's rights as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
non-discrimination;
the best interests of Women and Children;
the right to life, survival, and development;
respect for the Child's opinion;
legal certainty (Protection of victims' rights);
local wisdom;
justice and gender equality;
transparency;
accountability; and
sustainability.

Article 3
The Implementation of Protection of Women and Children aims to:
provide guidance to the Regency Government in planning policies and strategies for Protection of Women and Children;
prevent all forms of Violence against Women and Children and violations of Women's and Children's rights;
protect and provide services to Women and Children Victims of Violence;
strengthen the protective environment for Women and Children from all forms of Violence, exploitation, abuse and neglect;
increase empowerment of Women and Children; and
increase the role of government institutions, business entities, media and/or NGOs in the Region.





CHAPTER II
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One
General

Article 5

The Regional Government, Society, families, and parents are obligated and responsible for efforts to Implement Protection of Women and Children.

Part Two
Obligations and Responsibilities of Regional Government

Article 6

The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to:
respect and guarantee the human rights of every Woman and Child without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture, and language, legal status of the Child, birth order of the Child, and physical and/or mental condition;
provide support for facilities and infrastructure in the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children, including providing breastfeeding places for Children, playgrounds, recreation areas, protection houses, and establishing integrated service centers for Protection of Women and Children;
supervise the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;
guarantee the availability of a safe and comfortable environment for optimal Child development;
allocate budget to Institutions that have programs in the field of Protection of Women and Children;
coordinate programs, budgets and services that ensure ease of service to victims;
formulate implementation guidelines for Protection of Women and Children;
provide services for Women and Children Victims of Violence;
monitoring, supervision and evaluation of the Implementation of Protection and Assistance for Women and Children;
prevent, reduce risks, and carry out handling of all forms of Violence, exploitation, abuse, Children in conflict with the law and Children in emergency situations;
strengthen family and community-based Child care;
increase Child participation in policy making that affects them, including in family life, education and Society; and
increase community participation in Protection of Women and Children including prevention of risk reduction and handling.
Provision of facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, for fulfillment of service standards;
Provision of facilities and infrastructure by the Regional Government is carried out in stages adjusted to the financial capacity of the Region.



Part Three
Obligations and Responsibilities of Society

Article 7

Society is obligated and responsible for the Protection of Women and Children through community participation activities in the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children in the form of:
providing information and/or reporting every occurrence of vulnerability risks and Violence that is known;
facilitating consensus deliberation for the creation of restorative justice for Children in conflict with the law through informal channels and carried out at the village/sub-district government level and institutional authorities;
identifying/early detection and initial handling of Children at risk;
mediation and guidance for families experiencing domestic violence, household conflicts, and divorce;
preparation of families and Society in accepting Children who are victims of abuse and/or Children at risk; and
the process of repatriation, Social rehabilitation, and Social reintegration.

Part Four
Obligations and Responsibilities of Families and Parents

Article 8

families and parents are obligated and responsible for Children in the form of:
caring for, maintaining, educating, and protecting Children;
preventing early marriage;
ensuring the continuity of Children's education according to their abilities, talents and interests; and
reporting every birth of a Child to the authorized institution for birth registration.
In the event that parents do not exist or their whereabouts are unknown or for some reason, cannot carry out their obligations and responsibilities, then the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) may transfer to the family, which is carried out in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.


CHAPTER III
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Part One
General

Article 9

Every Woman and Child has basic rights as a human being that must be protected, advanced, upheld and fulfilled in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.





Article 10

Every Woman has the right to:
a. 	live, maintain life and improve their standard of living;
b. 	live peacefully, safely, peacefully, happily, prosperously physically and spiritually;
c. 	enjoy a good and healthy living environment;
d. 	live in a family in a legal marriage bond based on the provisions of laws and regulations;
e. 	obtain work according to ability, conditions and fair and decent wages;
f. 	especially for Women with disabilities, have the right to obtain facilities and special treatment in public services;
g. 	play an active role in politics and government in accordance with their potential and abilities;
h. 	obtain information and legal services;
i. 	obtain services to increase their income; and
j. 	obtain other rights in accordance with their human dignity and based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 11

Every Child has the right to:
a. 	obtain education and teaching according to age level, physical and mental condition, intelligence and interests and talents;
b. 	live, grow, develop and participate properly in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive Protection from acts of Violence, exploitation, discrimination and neglect;
c. 	have their needs fulfilled by the family;
d. 	receive religious guidance;
e. 	obtain their identity; and
f. 	obtain other rights in accordance with their human dignity and based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 12

The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to facilitate efforts to Protect, advance, enforce and fulfill the rights of Women and Children according to their authority based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

Part Two
Implementation of Protection of Women and Children

Article 13

The Implementation of Protection of Women and Children includes:
a. 	prevention and Protection;
b. 	Handling, recovery of victims of Violence;
c. 	Rehabilitation, Reintegration and empowerment;
d. 	law enforcement services and legal assistance;
e. 	facilitation of juvenile justice; and
f. 	cultivation of a gender-just social system.





Paragraph 1
Prevention and Protection

Article 14

(1) Related SKPDs in charge of Protection of Women and Children organize prevention and Protection in the form of:
a. 	dissemination of information;
b. 	developing data centers;
c. 	organizing education on prevention of Violence against Women and Children;
d. 	developing child-friendly school models;
e. 	encouraging increased Child participation in preventing Violence against Children and socialization and promotion of Children's rights through the establishment of Children's Forums/Children's Councils.
f. 	preparing codes of ethics for educators and orphanage staff and parties involved in handling Children;
g. 	providing guidelines or SOPs on handling Violence against Children;
h. 	preparing implementation guidelines for recovery and Social reintegration for Child victims of Violence;
i. 	preparing guidelines for community participation in providing recovery and Reintegration services;
j. 	involving community participation in recovery and Reintegration services for Child victims of Violence;
k. 	raising awareness about safe sex, dangers of cigarettes, alcohol, drug and psychotropic abuse, child marriage, pornography, pornographic acts and HIV/AIDS to families, Society and Children;
l. 	increasing the capacity of families and Child care Institutions regarding Child Protection and child care skills.
m.	increasing the capacity of all Child Protection services;
n. guaranteeing continuity of character education in accordance with the 12 (twelve) year compulsory education program;
o. fulfillment of the right to legal identity for Children and Women including birth certificates and marriage certificates;
p. 	guaranteeing standard health services;
q. 	guaranteeing the Child's right to receive exclusive breastfeeding with the obligation of public spaces to provide breastfeeding corners;
r. 	guaranteeing Protection and fulfillment of Children's rights from the womb and Women's rights;
s. 	fulfillment of Children's needs according to the Child's age;
t. 	Assistance and reproductive and psychological health services for adolescent Children and Women;
u. 	development of Marriage Age Maturation Programs;
v. strengthening the function of Institutions or non-governmental organizations working in the field of Child empowerment; and
w. encouraging and facilitating the development of Child-Friendly Regencies.
(2) 	Implementation of prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) may cooperate with Educational Institutions, Religious Institutions, Media, Business Entities and/or NGOs.

Article 15

(1) 	Everyone must protect Women and Children from the threat of danger of Violence against Women and Children.
(2) 	Every person or Institution is prohibited from falsifying data/documents that result in Violence against Women and Children and/or non-fulfillment of Children's rights.

Paragraph 2
Handling, Recovery of Victims of Violence

Article 16

(1) 	Related SKPDs in charge of social affairs, health, and Protection of Women and Children, organize Handling and Recovery in the form of:
a. 	providing Assistance in legal processes;
b. 	conducting mediation and/or dispute resolution;
c. 	accompanying referrals, psychological and medical recovery;
d. 	accompanying the legal identity process for Women and Children; and
e. 	conducting counseling.
(2) 	Implementation of Handling and Recovery as referred to in paragraph (1) may cooperate with the Police, Hospitals, Integrated Service Centers, Legal Aid Institutions, Business Entities and/or NGOs.

Paragraph 3
Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment

Article 17

(1) 	Related SKPDs in charge of social affairs and Protection of Women and Children organize Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment in the form of:
a. 	Assistance for Women and Child victims;
b. 	provision of Safe houses;
c. 	healing or recovery;
d. 	return to family and Society;
e. 	facilitating development of interests and talents for Women and Children;
f. 	developing economic enterprises for adult Women;
g. 	developing creative spaces for Children; and
h. 	conducting Supervision, monitoring and evaluation of the Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment process.
(2) 	Implementation of Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) letters a through d may cooperate with educational institutions, Rehabilitation centers, Safe houses, private institutions and/or NGOs.
(3) 	The mechanism for Implementation of Protection from Violence against Women and Children is organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) and Service Standards (SP) which will be further regulated by Regent Regulation.










Paragraph 4
Legal Assistance

Article 18

Legal assistance services are aimed at providing Assistance and Protection to Women and Children victims of Violence with a gender-responsive approach so that victims and perpetrators obtain justice and certainty of their legal rights.
(2) 	Types of legal assistance services include:
a. 	litigation legal assistance; and
b. 	non-litigation legal assistance.
(3) 	The Regional Government organizes legal assistance in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
(4) 	In providing legal assistance as referred to in paragraph (3) the Regional Government cooperates with advocate organizations in the Region.

Paragraph 5
Handling Children in Conflict with the Law

Article 19

(1) The Regional Government plays a role in facilitating the handling of Children in conflict with the law.
(2) 	handling of Children in conflict with the law as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. capacity building for government officials, social workers and Society;
b. 	increasing coordination with law enforcement officials;
c. 	provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure for implementation of the juvenile criminal justice system;
d. 	development of effective methods through information support, facilities and positive activities and taking immediate action on gaps that encourage Children and/or groups of Children to commit legal violations;
e. 	provision and provision of legal and psychosocial assistance services in realizing the process of restorative justice and diversion for Children in conflict with the law;
f. 	encouraging paradigm change in Society toward Children in conflict with the law; and
g. 	developing diversion mechanisms for Children in conflict with the law in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
(3) 	Related SKPDs in charge of social affairs and education organize Social rehabilitation and education for Children in conflict with the law in the form of:
a. 	provision of Safe houses;
b. 	psychosocial Rehabilitation;
c. 	spiritual mental Rehabilitation;
d. 	fulfillment of education rights; and
e. 	Social reintegration.






Paragraph 6
Cultivation of a Gender-Just Social System

Article 20

(1) 	Cultivation of a gender-just social system includes:
a. 	gender-based planning, budgeting and development systems;
b. 	gender-based human resource capacity building; and
c. 	gender-based political rights.
(2) 	The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented in the form of gender mainstreaming in every policy and community life.


Article 21

Further provisions regarding the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children are regulated in Regent Regulation.


CHAPTER IV
PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Part One
General

Article 22

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect Women and Children from acts of Violence that occur in the form of:
a. 	physical Violence;
b. 	sexual Violence;
c. 	economic Violence;
d. 	social Violence;
e. 	psychological Violence;
f. 	domestic neglect;
g. 	coercion or deprivation of liberty; and
h. 	threats of certain actions.

Article 23

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of Violence as referred to in Article 22 is obligated to make efforts to:
a. 	prevent the continuation of criminal acts;
b. 	provide Protection to victims of acts of Violence;
c. 	provide emergency assistance; and/or
d. 	assist in the process of submitting applications for Protection orders.

Part Two
Prevention of Acts of Violence

Article 24

(1) 	The Regional Government conducts prevention of Violence against Women and Children by:
a. 	socializing laws and regulations;
b. 	providing counseling/guidance;
c. 	providing education on the dangers of domestic violence;
d. 	conducting seminars/workshops or the like;
e. 	forming work networks in efforts to prevent Violence;
f. 	conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of Violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
g. 	forming Violence prevention systems, mapping locations or areas prone to Violence; and
h. 	raising public awareness in behaving in accordance with religious norms and decency.
(2) 	In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be carried out by:
a. 	family and/or closest relatives;
b. 	Society;
c. 	educational institutions;
d. 	health institutions;
e. 	community social institutions; and
f. 	business world and other Institutions.

Article 25

(1) The Regional Government is obligated to prepare prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) in the Regional Work Plan and Strategic Plan.
(2) Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

Part Three
Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 26

(1) 	The Regional Government implements Integrated Services for Women and Children victims of acts of Violence through activities:
a. 	complaint Services;
b. 	health Services;
c. 	counseling;
d. 	spiritual guidance;
e. 	Social rehabilitation Services;
f. 	legal assistance and Assistance Services; and/or
g. 	repatriation and Reintegration.
(2) 	Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:
a. 	special service rooms in police lines;
b. 	expert and professional personnel;
c. 	service centers and safe houses; and/or
d. 	other facilities and infrastructure needed for victim recovery.
(3) 	Implementation of Services for victims of acts of Violence is carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A with other social Institutions.
(4) 	P2TP2A and other social Institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner.
(5) 	Integrated Services for victims of acts of Violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (4) are implemented in accordance with Standard Operating Procedures.



Article 27

Procedures and procedures for Protection of Women and Children victims of Violence are further regulated by Regent Regulation.


CHAPTER V
STRENGTHENING AND DEVELOPMENT OF INSTITUTIONS PROVIDING PROTECTION SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 28

In order to Protect Women and Children, the following are established in the Region:
Integrated Service Center, in the form of Integrated Service Center for
Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A);

b. 	Child Protection Institution (LPA); and
c. 	Other institutions according to needs and/or provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 29

P2TP2A as referred to in Article 28 letter a functions as a coordinative Institution between the Regional Government, law enforcement officials, service institutions, educational institutions, Legal Aid Institutions, Individuals, Private sector and/or NGOs, which have capacity in Protection of Women and Children.
The duties of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. providing Services, including:
preparing and developing information related to Women and Children and coordinating throughout the Regency and reporting to the Province/West Nusa Tenggara Province;
developing a database center obtained from the Region regarding the condition of Violence against Women and Children;
receiving complaints both in writing and orally;
providing Services, mediation and Assistance to victims of Violence against Women and Children;
conducting education/training; and
coordinating with related institutions/individuals both in other Regencies/Cities, Provincial Government in order to provide Protection to Women and Children.
b. 	providing Protection to victims of acts of Violence, reporters and witnesses.
(3) 	Appointment of P2TP2A management as referred to in paragraph (1) is established by Regent Decree.
(4)	P2TP2A is under the coordination of SKPD or work unit that handles women's empowerment and Child Protection.

Article 30

In order to increase the effectiveness of Implementation of Child Protection, the Regent establishes LPA as referred to in Article 28 letter b which is independent in nature.
LPA as referred to in paragraph (1) is tasked with:
conducting Supervision of the implementation of Protection and fulfillment of Children's rights;

providing input and proposals in formulating policies on Implementation of Child Protection;
collecting data and information regarding Child Protection;
receiving and conducting reviews of community complaints regarding violations of Children's rights;
conducting mediation on disputes over violations of Children's rights;
conducting cooperation with community institutions, Government and NGOs in the form of work networks, for the fulfillment and Protection of Children's rights; and
conducting handling activities for cases of violations and neglect of Children's rights.


CHAPTER VI
PARTICIPATION

Part One
Community Participation

Article 31

(1) 	Society may participate in the Protection of Women and Children.
(2) 	Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out in the form of:
a. 	assisting the Regional Government in the form of Supervision of Protection of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;
b. 	reporting to the competent authorities if they know of Women and Children who experience Violence; and
c. 	participating in the process of prevention, recovery, Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment of victims.

Part Two
Child Participation

Article 32

(1) 	Every Child has the right to participate in expressing opinions, ideas, thoughts and others and be involved in decision-making processes concerning their lives either directly or indirectly, which is carried out with the consent and will of all Children based on awareness and understanding, according to age and level of thinking maturity.
(2) 	Child participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out through Children's Forums/Children's Councils starting from the Village, Sub-district, and Regency levels.
(3) 	Children's Forums/Children's Councils as referred to in paragraph (2) represent Children, both representation of Children's geographical domicile, Children's socio-cultural groups, Children with special needs and Children's educational backgrounds.
(4) 	Formation of Children's Forums/Children's Councils as referred to in paragraph (2) is facilitated by the Regional Government.
(5) 	Formation of Children's Forums/Children's Councils as referred to in paragraph (3) is established by Regent Decree.



Article 33

Funding sources for Children's Forums/Children's Councils may come from:
a. 	donations from Society/Business Entities that are non-binding in nature;
b. 	assistance from the Regional Government; and/or
c. 	other funding sources in accordance with laws and regulations.

Article 34

(1) 	In every formulation of policies related to Children, the Regional Government considers and accommodates the opinions of Children submitted through Children's Forums/Children's Councils.
(2) 	The Regional Government may include representatives of Children's Forums/Children's Councils in Regional Development Planning Consultations.


CHAPTER VII
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 35

The Regent is obligated to conduct guidance and Supervision of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children.
Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
organizing technical guidance and training; and
providing facilities for fulfillment of Service Standards.
Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out to realize the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children according to Service Standards implemented by P2TP2A and Institutions that implement fulfillment and Protection services for Women and Children.

Article 36

Supervision as referred to in Article 35 paragraph (1) includes:
conducting monitoring and evaluation of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children; and
receiving and following up on reports.
Implementation of Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out transparently and accountably.


CHAPTER VIII
REPORTING

Article 37

(1) 	SKPDs that carry out duties and functions in the field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection submit reports to the Regent regarding the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children in the Region.
(2) 	Reports as referred to in paragraph (1) are submitted at least 1 (one) time per year.




CHAPTER IX
FINANCING

Article 38

Financing for implementation of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children is charged to the APBD and other legal and non-binding funding sources.


CHAPTER X
CRIMINAL PROVISIONS

Part One
Criminal Sanctions

Article 39

Every person or Institution that violates the provisions as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) is threatened with imprisonment of a maximum of 6 (six) months and/or a fine of a maximum of Rp. 50,000,000,- (fifty million rupiah).
The criminal act as referred to in paragraph (1) is a violation.
Every person or legal entity that violates the provisions of Article 23 is threatened with criminal sanctions based on the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.
Part Two
Administrative Sanctions

Article 40

For Civil Servants who commit an action or fail to commit an action that can hinder or make it difficult for one of the parties served resulting in losses to those served in the processing of civil rights for the fulfillment of Children's rights as referred to in Article 11 letters e and f, are subject to disciplinary punishment in accordance with applicable laws and regulations and/or administrative sanctions in the form of temporary dismissal from office.
The mechanism for imposing sanctions as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) is further regulated by Regent Regulation.

















CHAPTER XI
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 41
Implementing regulations of this Regional Regulation must be established no later than 1 (one) year from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

Article 42
This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

GENERAL
The State has an obligation to provide Protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. That Women and Children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience Violence therefore they need to receive Protection. Violence against Women and Children is a violation of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed without discrimination.
Violence is any act unlawfully with or without means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in deprivation of a person's liberty. Furthermore, Violence against Women is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or hardship for Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and abuse that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child.
The existence of Women and Children Victims of Violence has not received adequate Services therefore minimum Services are needed for victims of Violence. In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in North Lombok Regency to avoid Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, it is necessary to Implement Protection of Women and Children in the form of Regional Regulation. This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children Victims of Violence especially in terms of Services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and Supervision.
