CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is Magelang Regency.
-3-

2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Magelang.

4. Regional House of Representatives of Magelang Regency, hereinafter referred to as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution as an element of regional government administration.

5. Regional Apparatus is an assistant element to the Regent and DPRD in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.

6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

7. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and verbal that causes danger to life, body and/or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.

8. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or distress to women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.

9. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in suffering or distress physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.

10. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion that is direct or indirect based on human differences on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural fields and other aspects of life.

11. Trafficking in Persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, forgery, fraud and
-4-
abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether conducted within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.

12. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb.
13. Victim of violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering either physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals, including the victim's heirs.

14. Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children Victims of Violence and Discrimination, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to provide services and rehabilitation to women and children victims of violence comprehensively including information services, assistance and legal aid, counseling services, medical services and safe houses through referral.

15. Task Force for the Prevention and Handling of the Criminal Act of Trafficking in Persons, hereinafter referred to as the Task Force, is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating prevention and handling efforts for the criminal act of trafficking in persons in Magelang Regency.

16. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction to be able to carry out social functions normally.

17. Safe house (shelter) is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with the determined operational standards.

18. Social reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the work environment, family substitutes that can provide protection and fulfillment of victim needs.

19. Victim recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of violence against women and children to be more empowered, both physically, psychologically, socially, economically and socially.
-5-

20. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy and spiritual guidance, for the purpose of self-strengthening of women and children victims of violence to resolve the problems faced.

21. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward to the third degree.

22. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.

23. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect for human rights;

b. justice and gender equality;

c. non-discrimination;

d. victim protection;

e. empowerment; and

f. integration.

Article 3

The objectives of protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination are to:

a. prevent acts of violence and discrimination against women and children including trafficking in persons;

b. eliminate all forms of violence, discrimination and exploitation against women and children;

c. protect women and children from acts of violence and discrimination in obtaining their legitimate constitutional rights

d. protect, provide a sense of security for women and children;
e. provide services and protection to women and children victims of acts of violence and discrimination, reporters, and witnesses; and

f. facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize intact, harmonious and prosperous households.
-6-

CHAPTER III
PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE AND DISCRIMINATION
Article 4

(1) To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence and discrimination against women and children, the Regional Government conducts empowerment and awareness-raising to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.

(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness-raising as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government undertakes efforts as follows:
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;

b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;

c. facilitating the fulfillment of employment for women;
d. encouraging community participation and concern for the prevention of protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination;

e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;

f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, educational institutions and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children; and

g. opening an integrated service system for the protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination in every village.

Article 5

(1) Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence and discrimination against women and children as referred to in Article 4, is implemented by Regional Apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:

a. social affairs;

b. health;

c. education;

d. manpower;

e. women's empowerment and child protection;
f. people's welfare; and

g. peace and order.

(2) Prevention of acts of violence by Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Action Plan.
-7-

CHAPTER IV
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE AND DISCRIMINATION

Article 6

Women and children victims of acts of violence and discrimination receive the following rights:

a. the right to have their dignity and worth as human beings respected;
b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by victims;

c. the right to determine their own decisions;

d. the right to obtain information;

e. the right to confidentiality;

f. the right to social rehabilitation;

g. the right to complaint handling;

h. the right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process;

i. the right to assistance;

j. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;

k. the right to basic services;

l. the right to equal protection;

m. the right to be free from various stigmas; and

n. the right to obtain freedom.

CHAPTER V
SERVICES FOR VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE AND DISCRIMINATION
Article 7

(1) The Regional Government is obligated to provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence and discrimination.

(2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) are provided to witnesses and/or victims of violence and discrimination.

(3) In the context of services as referred to in paragraph (1), Standard Operating Procedures for Services are prepared.

Article 8

(1) Forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence and discrimination as referred to in Article 7 include:

a. complaint services;

b. health services;

c. legal aid services;
-8-

d. repatriation services;

e. rehabilitation and social reintegration services; and
f. education services for children who are victims of acts of violence and discrimination.

(2) Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented according to minimum service standards established by the government and implemented by Regional Apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:

a. social affairs;

b. health;

c. education;

d. women's empowerment and child protection; and
e. mental and spiritual affairs.

(3) Further provisions regarding the procedures for handling services for women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER VI
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 9

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence and discrimination are the joint responsibility of:

a. Regional Government;

b. Village Government;

c. Community; and

d. Family.

Article 10

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 10 letter a, include:

a. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination;
b. implementing protection policies for women and children from acts of violence established by the government;

c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;

d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence according to regional financial capacity;
-9-

f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;

g. providing recovery and social reintegration services;
h. encouraging and increasing community participation; and
i. providing Human Resources in the implementation of protection of women and children victims of acts of violence and discrimination.

(2) In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes action programs and activities for the protection of women and children in the Regional Action Plan as a basis for Regional Apparatus in implementing the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

(3) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD).

(4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 11

The obligations and responsibilities of the Village Government as referred to in Article 10 letter b, include:

a. implementing protection policies for women and children from acts of violence and discrimination established by the government;

b. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination;
c. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination;

d. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination according to village financial capacity;

e. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination; and
f. encouraging and increasing community participation.

Article 12

(1) The community participates in the protection of women and children victims of violence and discrimination, both individually and in groups.
-10-

(2) The role of the community as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, women and child protection institutions, social welfare institutions, community organizations, educational institutions, mass media, and the business world.

(3) The role of the community in the implementation of protection of women and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by:

a. providing information through socialization and education regarding the protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination;

b. providing input in the formulation of policies related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination;

c. reporting to the authorities if violations of acts of violence and discrimination against women and children occur;

d. playing an active role in the process of rehabilitation and social reintegration for women and children victims of acts of violence and discrimination;

e. conducting monitoring and supervision of the protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination;
f. providing facilities and infrastructure in order to support the protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination;

g. playing an active role in eliminating negative labeling of women and children victims of acts of violence and discrimination; and

h. providing space for women and children to be able to participate and express opinions.

Article 13

The obligations of the family as referred to in Article 10 letter d have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and discrimination and protect women and children as family members.

I. GENERAL

Women and children have become a serious concern of the international community, considering their vulnerable position as victims of violence and discrimination from their families and environment. Various acts of violence against both women and children within the household and outside the household continue to increase from time to time. This condition, if without serious handling, will destroy household life, society, nation and state. Therefore, the government of Magelang Regency is obligated to provide protection to women and children against acts of violence and discrimination which is an implementation of various laws and regulations, including Law Number 39 Year 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 21 Year 2007 concerning the Eradication of Trafficking in Persons, Law Number 35 Year 2014 Concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection.

Protection of women and children against acts of violence and discrimination is an obligation of the Regional Government in the context of regional autonomy, which is manifested in the form of a Regional Regulation as regulated in Article 236 paragraph (1), Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government.

On that basis, the Regional Government of Magelang Regency has taken steps as an effort to provide protection to women and children, including by issuing Regent Regulation Number 26 Year 2010 Concerning The existence of these regulations is felt to be not yet able to provide maximum and adequate protection against acts of violence and discrimination that occur in the social life of the community. In addition to the problem of these laws and regulations, the factor of low public understanding of the rights of women and children has implications for low legal awareness of the community to report acts of violence or utilize the means provided by law to resolve their problems.
-17-

Based on this thinking, a regulation in the form of a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children against acts of violence and discrimination is needed that is able to answer while providing legal certainty in the protection of women and children against acts of violence and discrimination that occur.

This Regional Regulation is intended to provide responsibility to the Regional Government starting from the prevention of acts of violence to the handling of victims of acts of violence. This regional regulation also regulates the rights of victims of violence, Handling of Victims of violence, Government Responsibility up to the Participation of Government, Community and Family in the prevention and resolution of violence and discrimination against women and children.

In its implementation, the Regional Government is also allowed to establish cooperation with government agencies, other regional governments and the community. In addition, regulation regarding adequate funding sourced from the State Budget, Regional Budget, Village Budget as well as from the government, Regional Government or other non-binding sources is expected to guarantee the implementation of protection for women and children against victims of violence and discrimination.
