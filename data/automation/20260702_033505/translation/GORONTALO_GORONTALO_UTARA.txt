REGENT OF NORTH GORONTALO

Considering: a. that in order to protect the constitutional rights of women and children in accordance with the mandate of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, the Regional Government of North Gorontalo Regency is responsible for the implementation of protection of women and children against acts of violence.

b. that North Gorontalo Regency which has the Philosophy of Custom Based on Sharia, Sharia Based on the Book of Allah, upholds Religious Values and Customs as well as noble Character, the Regional Government of North Gorontalo Regency is morally fully responsible for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

c. that every citizen has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades dignity, self-worth and human dignity and has the right to obtain security and freedom from all forms of violence

d. that the increase in violent treatment of women and children in North Gorontalo Regency is an act that degrades human dignity, so that the role of the Regional Government of North Gorontalo Regency is required so that women and children are protected from acts of violence

e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children Against Acts of Violence

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1
In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is North Gorontalo Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of North Gorontalo.
4. Regional People's Representative Council hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution which is positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.
5. Regional Apparatus is an assistant element to the regional head and DPRD in the administration of Government Affairs which are the authority of the Region.
6. Forum for the Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence hereinafter abbreviated as FP2ATK is a forum established based on this Regional Regulation whose membership comes from relevant agencies and communities concerned with protection from acts of violence.
7. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including children still in the womb.
8. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and community organizations and/or associations.
9. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband, wife, or husband wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
10. Every person is an individual or corporation.
11. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.
12. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.
13. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, sexually, economically, socially and psychologically.
14. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
15. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
16. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
17. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their development.
18. Cooperation is an activity or effort carried out by several people to achieve common goals.
19. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.
20. Companion is a social worker who has professional competence in their field.
21. Recovery of victims is all efforts to strengthen victims of domestic violence to be more empowered, both physically and psychologically.
22. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service provider institution for victims established by the Regional Government.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2
Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of Victims of Acts of Violence;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. for Victims of acts of violence; and
e. legal certainty.

Article 3
Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence aims to:
a. prevent all forms of Violence against Women and Children;
b. protect Women and provide services to Women and Children Victims of Acts of Violence; and
c. empowerment of Women and Children Victims of Acts of Violence.

CHAPTER III
SCOPE

Article 4
The Scope of this Regional Regulation consists of:
a. protection of women; and
b. protection of children.

Article 5
Protection of women as referred to in Article 4 letter a includes:
a. prevention of acts of violence;
b. integrated services for victims of acts of violence;
c. empowerment of victims of acts of violence.

Article 6
Protection of children as referred to in Article 4 letter b includes:
a. protection of children who are victims of acts of violence; and
b. special protection of children.

CHAPTER IV
PROTECTION OF WOMEN

Part One
General

Article 7
The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of violence that occur in public or private life in the form of:
a. physical violence;
b. sexual violence;
c. economic violence;
d. social violence;
e. psychological violence;
f. domestic neglect;
g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and
h. threats of certain actions.

Article 8
Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence as referred to in Article 7 is obliged to make efforts to:
a. prevent the continuation of criminal acts;
b. provide protection to victims of acts of violence;
c. provide emergency assistance; and/or
d. assist in the process of submitting an application for protection determination

Part Two
Prevention of Acts of Violence

Article 9
(1) The Regional Government conducts prevention as referred to in article 5 letter a by:
a. socializing laws and regulations;
b. providing counseling/guidance;
c. providing education on the dangers of domestic violence;
d. conducting seminars/workshops or the like;
e. forming work networks in violence prevention efforts;
f. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
g. forming a violence prevention system, mapping locations or areas prone to violence; and
h. increasing public awareness in behaving in accordance with religious norms.

(2) In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be implemented by:
a. family and/or closest relatives;
b. community;
c. educational institutions;
d. health institutions;
e. social community institutions; and
f. business world and other institutions.

Article 10
(1) The Regional Government is obliged to prepare prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan;
(2) Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

Article 11
(1) Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment implements prevention as referred to in Article 9;
(2) Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1) in conducting prevention can cooperate with Vertical Agencies.

Part Three
Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 12
(1) The Regional Government implements integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b through activities:
a. complaint services;
b. health services;
c. counseling;
d. spiritual guidance;
e. social rehabilitation services;
f. legal assistance and support services; and/or
g. repatriation and reintegration.

(2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:
a. special service rooms in police lines;
b. expert and professional personnel;
c. service centers and safe houses; and/or
d. other facilities and infrastructure needed for victim recovery.

(3) Implementation of services for victims of acts of violence is carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A with other social institutions;
(4) P2TP2A and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) can receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner;
(5) Integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (4) are implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures;
(6) Provisions regarding Standard Operating Procedures as referred to in paragraph (5) are regulated by Governor Regulation.

Part Four
Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 13
(1) Empowerment of victims of acts of violence as referred to in article 5 letter c conducted by the Regional Government in the form of:
a. job training;
b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
c. capital assistance.

(2) Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.

CHAPTER V
CHILD PROTECTION

Part One
Protection of Children Who Are
Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 14
(1) Protection of children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in article 6 letter a is the responsibility of the Regional Government;
(2) Protection of child victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by:
a. formulating policies for prevention, risk reduction, victim handling and child data and information systems;
b. organizing maintenance, care, and social rehabilitation of neglected children, both within and outside institutions;
c. providing shelter, maintenance and care for neglected children; and organizing special protection for children.

Article 15
(1) Provisions regarding prevention and service for acts of violence in the protection of women as referred to in Articles 7 through Article 13 apply mutatis mutandis to the protection of children from acts of violence;
(2) In addition to conducting prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) also by increasing family capacity in child protection and child care skills.

Part Two
Special Protection of Children

Article 16
(1) Special protection of children as referred to in Article 6 letter b is given to:
a. Children in emergency situations;
b. Children in conflict with the law;
c. Children from minority and isolated groups;
d. Children who are exploited economically and/or sexually;
e. Children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse;
f. Children who are victims of pornography;
g. Children with HIV/AIDS;
h. Child victims of kidnapping, sale, and/or trafficking;
i. Child victims of physical and/or psychological Violence;
j. Child victims of sexual crimes;
k. Child victims of terrorism networks;
l. Children with Disabilities;
m. Child victims of abuse and neglect;
n. Children with deviant social behavior; and
o. Children who are victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their Parents' condition.

(2) The Regional Government in implementing handling of special protection of children as referred to in paragraph (1) in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI
COOPERATION AND COORDINATION
PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Part One
General

Article 17
(1) The Regional Government conducts cooperation and coordination in order to ensure the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
(2) Cooperation and coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Part Two
Institutional Protection of Women and Children

Article 18
(1) The Regional Government in conducting cooperation and coordination on the Protection of Women and children from acts of violence, establishes FP2ATK;
(2) FP2ATK membership consists of elements:
a. regional government;
b. vertical agencies;
c. educational institutions; and
d. community organizations concerned with the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

(3) The Organizational Structure of FP2ATK as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of:
a. policy formulation working group;
b. socialization working group;
c. service working group; and
d. data, information, monitoring and evaluation working group.

(4) Establishment and term of office of FP2ATK members as referred to in paragraph (1), is determined by Regent Decree.

Article 19
(1) FP2ATK has the task of:
a. providing input in policy formulation;
b. conducting coordination with Regional Apparatus and other social institutions;
c. socializing laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
d. collecting and developing data and information related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. conducting review, monitoring, evaluation, and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
f. providing reports, suggestions, input, and considerations to the Regent.

Article 20
FP2ATK as referred to in article 18 paragraph (2) is assisted by the Secretariat at the SKPD that handles women's empowerment and child protection affairs.

Article 21
Further provisions regarding the mechanism and working procedures of FP2ATK are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 22
(1) The Regional Government in order to provide services to victims of acts of violence, establishes P2TP2A;
(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) has the task of:
a. providing services, including:
1. receiving complaints/reports of violence against women and/or children, consultation, and counseling;
2. receiving and sending case referrals from Regency/City Regional Government or institutions established by Regency/City Regional Government;
3. providing legal assistance;
4. health;
5. social rehabilitation;
6. legal services; and
7. repatriation and social reintegration.
b. providing protection to victims of acts of violence, reporters and witnesses.

(3) Appointment of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is determined by Regent Decree.
(4) Regional Apparatus provides guidance to P2TP2A in carrying out tasks as referred to in paragraph (2).

Part Three
Cooperation

Article 23
(1) In certain cases the Regional Government can cooperate with:
a. other regional governments;
b. police;
c. advocates;
d. health workers;
e. social workers;
f. volunteer companions;
g. spiritual counselors;
h. other law enforcers, to assist victims in the court process;
i. national commission on violence against women;
j. Indonesian child protection commission or Child Protection Institution; and/or
k. certain parties desired for the benefit of the victim.

(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) can be in the form of:
a. socialization;
b. provision of legal assistance;
c. health services;
d. victim assistance;
e. counseling;
f. social rehabilitation;
g. spiritual guidance; and/or
h. repatriation and social reintegration.

Part Four
Coordination

Article 24
(1) The Regent coordinates the protection of women and children from acts of violence with:
a. government;
b. Regency/City regional government;
c. vertical agencies;
d. educational institutions; and
e. community institutions.

(2) Coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. religious affairs;
b. health affairs;
c. education affairs;
d. social affairs;
e. legal affairs;
f. labor affairs; and
g. other affairs.

CHAPTER VII
COMMUNITY AND BUSINESS PARTICIPATION

Article 25
(1) Every person can participate in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence;
(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) can be done by:
a. reporting acts of violence against women and/or children;
b. providing first aid to victims of Acts of Violence;
c. independently socializing the rights of women and children; and/or
d. forming independent social community institutions.

Article 26
In the case that social community institutions implement protection of women and children from acts of violence they must coordinate with FP2ATK.

Article 27
(1) The Regional Government encourages the business world to participate in the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) can be in the form of:
a. socialization to employees within the company environment regarding the dangers of acts of violence against women and children; and
b. assistance as a form of corporate social responsibility.

CHAPTER IX
INFORMATION AND REPORTING SYSTEM

Article 28
(1) The Regional Government prepares and compiles an information and reporting system for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
(2) Information and reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER X
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 29
(1) The Regional Government implements guidance and supervision of FP2ATK and other social institutions;
(2) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) by Regional Apparatus that implements women's and children's empowerment affairs;
(3) Results of implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (2) must be reported to the regional government.

CHAPTER X
FINANCING

Article 30
Financing for activities of Protection of Women and Children from acts of violence, can be sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and/or
b. other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER XI
TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Article 31
In the case that FP2ATK as referred to in Article 18 has not been formed, the existing P2TP2A, continues to carry out the tasks and functions of integrated services for Women and children victims of acts of Violence in the Region.

CHAPTER XII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 32
Implementation regulations of this Regional Regulation must be established no later than 6 (six) months from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

Article 33
This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation. So that everyone knows it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of North Gorontalo Regency.

I. GENERAL
Acts of violence against women and children are violations of human rights so they need to be protected in their dignity and honor and their right to life is guaranteed in accordance with their nature and nature without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in North Gorontalo Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and dignity, it is necessary to provide protection to women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations. So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation. Regional Regulations regulate protection efforts for victims, especially in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in North Gorontalo Regency

Letter a
What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or defects on a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causing death

Letter b
What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Letter c
What is meant by "economic violence" is an action that limits humans from working inside or outside the home to produce money and goods, including allowing humans who work to be exploited, while someone does not meet the economic needs of the family.

Letter d
What is meant by "social violence" is as an act of a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life of a person or can cause physical damage or damage to other people's property

Letter e
What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering in a person.

Letter f
What is meant by "Domestic Neglect" is:
a. actions that result in the unfulfillment of
b. children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
c. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should;
d. actions of neglecting people within the scope of their household, even though according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or agreement they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person;
e. actions that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Sufficiently clear.

Letter h
What is meant by "threats of certain actions" is any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's inherent freedom.
