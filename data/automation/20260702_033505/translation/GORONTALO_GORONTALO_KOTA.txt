MAYOR OF GORONTALO,

Considering: a. that Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia guarantee the right of every citizen to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence;

b. that all forms of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their self-worth and dignity need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and essence without discrimination;

c. that women's empowerment and child protection are concurrent governmental affairs delegated to regions to become the basis for implementing regional autonomy and become mandatory governmental affairs not related to basic services as referred to in Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government;

d. that in order to provide regulatory strengthening, direction, and foundation in the protection of women and children victims of violence, regulation is needed related to the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

- 2 -

e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, letter c, letter d, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:

1. Region is Gorontalo City.

2. Mayor is the Regional Head.

3. Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous Region.

4. Regional Governance is the implementation of governmental affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional People's Representative Council according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the broadest possible autonomy in the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.

5. Woman is a person of female gender.

6. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.

7. Violence is any act that results in or can result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.

- 5 -

8. Violence against women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.

9. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.

10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring in the Gorontalo City area.

11. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security conducted by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will occur, is occurring or has occurred.

12. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

13. Service is action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will occur, is occurring or has occurred.

14. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has expertise in accompanying victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

15. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the Region to provide services in the context of empowerment of women and children victims of violence.

16. Safe House is a shelter for victims during the accompaniment process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and danger from perpetrators.

17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct lineage upward or downward to the third degree.

18. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers who reside in that household.

19. Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner.

Article 2

Protection of Victims is based on:

a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of victims;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. the best interests of victims; and
e. empowerment.

Article 3

Protection of Victims aims to:

a. prevent violence against women and children;
b. provide services to Victims; and
c. empower women victims of violence.

Article 4

The scope of protection for Victims includes prevention, service and empowerment efforts for victims of violence in Gorontalo City.

CHAPTER II
FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

Forms of violence include:

a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. exploitation; and/or
f. other violence.

Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:

a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes

Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:

a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;

b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as they should, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;

c. acts of neglecting people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or

d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:

a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;

b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, physical, sexual, reproductive organ exploitation, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or abilities of a person by other parties to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or

c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:

a. threats of violence which include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's inherent freedom; and

b. coercion, including: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

CHAPTER III
RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 12

Every Victim receives the right:

a. to have their dignity and worth as a human being respected;
b. recovery of health and psychology from the suffering experienced by the victim;
c. to determine their own decisions;
d. to obtain information;
e. confidentiality of their identity;
f. compensation;
g. social rehabilitation;
h. complaint handling;
i. to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or
j. accompaniment.

Article 13

Child victims of violence in addition to receiving rights as referred to in Article 12, also receive special rights, consisting of:

a. respect for survival, growth and development;
b. basic services;
c. equal protection;
d. freedom from various stigma; and/or
e. obtaining freedom.

CHAPTER IV
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One
Regional Government

Article 14

(1) The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to implement protection efforts for women and children victims of violence through:

a. policy determination;
b. preparation of program and activity planning;
c. establishment of P2TP2A;
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure;
e. allocating budget; and
f. establishment of FPKK.

(2) To anticipate the occurrence of violence, the Regional Government organizes services for victims in the form of:

a. facilitating the establishment of P2TP2A;
b. facilitating P2TP2A facilities and infrastructure according to capacity; and
c. facilitating FPKK as a network forum for handling victims.

Article 15

The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to:

a. supervise the implementation of services to victims, with minimum service standards; and

b. provide funds for victim protection through the Regional Budget and/or other lawful regional financial sources.

Part Two
Community and Family

Article 16

The community and family have obligations and responsibilities which include:

a. preventing the occurrence of violence against women and children;

b. reporting if violence occurs;
c. protecting victims; and
d. providing emergency assistance.

CHAPTER V
INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER FOR WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT
AND CHILD PROTECTION

Article 17

(1) P2TP2A is established by the Regional Government and/or community institutions.

(2) The organizational structure of P2TP2A established by institutions is formed according to needs.

Article 18

(1) P2TP2A provides services and temporary protection in the form of a safe house for victims of violence.

(2) In the event that P2TP2A does not have a safe house as referred to in paragraph (1), victims of violence are referred to P2TP2A that has a safe house.

Article 19

(1) The organizational structure of P2TP2A established by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) consists of:

a. general chairperson or other equivalent title;
b. deputy general chairperson;
c. executive chairperson;
d. deputy executive chairperson;
e. secretary;
f. treasurer; and
g. executive chairperson overseeing fields.

(2) Further provisions regarding the structure and duties of P2TP2A established by the Region as referred to in paragraph (1) are stipulated by Mayor's Decree.

- 12 -

Article 20

(1) P2TP2A established by the Regional Government has at least three counselors.

(2) Counselors as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. counselor in the field of law;
b. counselor in the field of health; and
c. counselor in the field of psychology.

(3) Implementation of counseling carried out by counselors can be implemented in a networked manner as follows:

a. counseling in the field of law by involving the police, prosecutor's office, district court, religious court, legal advocacy institutions and Non-Governmental Organizations;

b. counseling in the field of health by involving the Health Office, Regional General Hospital and other health services;

c. counseling in the field of psychology by involving psychological companions and/or psychologists.

CHAPTER VI
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Part One
Prevention

Article 21

(1) Prevention efforts against violence against women and children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by regional apparatus that administer governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:

a. forming a network in violence prevention efforts;
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;

- 13 -

c. forming a violence prevention system;
d. conducting socialization about laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence; and
e. providing critical education about the rights of women and children to the community.

Article 22

In addition to the Regional Government, prevention efforts as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (1) are carried out by:

a. family and/or closest relatives;
b. community;
c. educational institutions; and
d. Non-Governmental Organizations.

Part Two
Service

Article 23

(1) Implementation of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) can receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.

Article 24

Implementation of services to victims is carried out with:
a. speed;
b. safety and comfort;
c. sense of empathy;
d. non-discrimination;
e. easy accessibility;
f. no charge; and
g. guaranteed confidentiality.

Article 25

Forms of service to victims include:

a. complaint, consultation, and counseling services;
b. accompaniment services;
c. health services;
d. social rehabilitation services;
e. legal services; and
f. repatriation and social reintegration services.

Article 26

Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Article 25 letter a include:

a. initial identification or recording of victims; and
b. consent to take action.

Article 27

Accompaniment services as referred to in Article 25 letter b include:

a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;
b. accompanying victims during the medicolegal process;
c. accompanying victims during the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and court;
d. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;
e. maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media;
f. coordinating with other companions; and
g. providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

- 15 -

Article 28

Health services as referred to in Article 25 letter c include:

a. first aid to victims;
b. care and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel; and
c. referral to health services.

Article 29

(1) Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 25 letter d are services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims.

(2) Social rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:

a. providing spiritual guidance to victims; and
b. psychological recovery of victims.

Article 30

(1) Legal services as referred to in Article 25 letter e are to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process.

(2) Legal services as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:

a. providing legal consultation covering information regarding victims' rights and the judicial process;

b. accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and court hearing examination levels and helping victims to fully describe the violence they experienced;

c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

Article 31

(1) Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 25 letter f aim to return victims to their families and social environment.

- 16 -

(2) Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with:
a. District/City Governments within one Gorontalo Province area; and
b. related government and non-government agencies and institutions.

Part Three
Empowerment

Paragraph 1
Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

Article 32

Empowerment of women victims of violence includes:
a. job training;
b. productive economic enterprises and/or joint business groups; and
c. capital assistance.

Article 33

Job training as referred to in Article 32 letter a includes:
a. skills training;
b. field work practice; and
c. apprenticeship.

Article 34

Productive economic enterprises and/or joint business groups as referred to in Article 32 letter b include:

a. entrepreneurship skills training;
b. facilitation of joint business group formation; and
c. business implementation accompaniment.

Article 35

Capital assistance as referred to in Article 32 letter c includes:

a. work facilities and infrastructure assistance; and
b. facilitation of working capital assistance.

- 17 -

Paragraph 2
Fulfillment of Rights of Child Victims of Violence

Article 36

(1) The Regional Government, P2TP2A, and the community are obliged to fulfill the rights of child victims of violence.

(2) Forms of fulfillment of rights of child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) include fulfillment of children's basic rights according to their needs.

Part Four
Coordination of Victim Protection

Article 37

(1) In an effort to protect and organize service handling for victims, the Regional Government establishes FPKK.

(2) FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
a. Regional FPKK;
b. Sub-district FPKK; and
c. Village FPKK.

(3) Establishment of Regional FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a is stipulated by Mayor's Decree.

(4) Establishment of Sub-district FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b is stipulated by Sub-district Head's Decree.

(5) Establishment of Village FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is stipulated by Village Head's Decree.

(6) Further provisions regarding the organizational structure and elements of FPKK are regulated by Mayor's Regulation.

Article 38

The duties and functions of FPKK as referred to in Article 37 paragraph (1) are:

a. coordinating and synchronizing prevention, service, and empowerment of victims of violence against women and children;

- 18 -

b. maintaining and developing FPKK in prevention, service, and empowerment of victims of violence in a networked manner and referral system;

c. conducting education on non-discrimination values toward women and children;

d. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence;

e. collecting, compiling, and presenting reports on data of victims of violence; and

f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence.

CHAPTER VII
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 39

(1) The community participates in the implementation of protection of victims.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. forming family partners at the village level by the community;
b. forming women and child protection units within community organizations;
c. conducting independent socialization of women's and children's rights;
d. providing first aid to victims; and
e. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.

(3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector, and mass media.

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their self-worth and dignity need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and essence without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Gorontalo City so they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity, protection of women and children victims of violence needs to be carried out in the form of regional regulations.

So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, especially in terms of prevention, service and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in Gorontalo City.
