CONCERNING
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF PRINGSEWU,

Considering
a. that every citizen has human rights that must be guaranteed by the state to obtain a sense of security, be protected and be free from all forms of violence.

b. that women and children are vulnerable groups, so they need to receive protection and services from the state that can provide prevention, protection and services to victims of violence;

c. that regional government together with the community must implement prevention, protection and recovery efforts for child and women victims of violence for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1
1. Region is Pringsewu Regency.

2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of Regional Government administration.

3. Regent is the Regent of Pringsewu.

4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution as an element of Regional Government administration.

5. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of Pringsewu Regency.

6. Regional Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is a regional apparatus in Pringsewu Regency.

7. Woman is an adult human being of female gender.

8. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child in the womb.

9. Child victim of violence is a child who receives harsh treatment either physically, psychologically, economically, socially, sexually, and other losses resulting from state policy, acts of violence and or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.

10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.

11. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering either physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim.

12. Violence against women is any act of violence against women that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.

13. Violence against children is any action that results in misery or suffering to children physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom.

14. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.

15. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

16. Economic violence is any act that deliberately neglects family members in the form of not providing livelihood, care or maintenance in a proper manner.

17. Neglect is the act of neglecting women and or children within the household scope which according to applicable law for the person concerned or due to agreement or contract he is obliged to provide livelihood, care or maintenance to that person. Neglect also applies to anyone who causes economic dependence by limiting and or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

18. Psychological violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

19. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women has occurred or will occur.

20. Law enforcement is the action of state-authorized apparatus to implement legislation.

21. Legal assistance is legal services provided by legal companions and advocates to carry out the process of accompanying witnesses and or victims of violence against women and children.

22. Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

23. Implementation of protection is a series of activities aimed at preventing violence, providing protection and recovery services and social reintegration, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation carried out by the Integrated Service Center.

24. Prevention is a direct effort made by the Regional Government to prevent acts of violence against women and children.

25. Complaint is a notification either in writing or orally by an interested party to an authorized institution, in which the person concerned suffers losses due to the actions of others.

26. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by Professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

27. Companion is a person or institution that has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy, advocacy for strengthening and self-recovery of victims of violence.

28. Integrated Service is a series of activities to provide protection for women and child victims of violence carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as a unified implementation of prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.

29. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for victims of violence in the region.

30. Victim Recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, both physically, psychologically, socially, economically and sexually.

31. Accompaniment is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for strengthening victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems they face.

32. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes who can provide protection and fulfill the needs of victims.

33. Safe House (shelter) is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2
The Implementation of Protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence is carried out based on the principles of respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity, non-discrimination, gender equality and justice and protection of the human rights of Women and Children and the best interests of victims.

Article 3
Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence aims to provide protection and services that include aspects of prevention, service and accompaniment, reunification and empowerment.

Article 4
The implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence aims to:
a. prevent all forms of violent acts including trafficking in persons;
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation of women and children;
c. protect and provide a sense of security for victims of violence;
d. provide services to victims of violence and;
e. implement comprehensive recovery for victims.

CHAPTER III
SCOPE

Article 5
The scope of protection for women and child victims of violence includes prevention efforts, services and empowerment of victims of violence in the region.

CHAPTER IV
FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 6
Forms of violence include:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. exploitation; and/or
f. other violence.

Article 7
Physical violence as referred to in Article 6 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or cause death.

Article 8
Psychological violence as referred to in Article 6 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 9
Sexual violence as referred to in Article 6 letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or;
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Article 10
Neglect as referred to in Article 6 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting people within the household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to him or due to agreement or contract he is obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or;
d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Article 11
Exploitation as referred to in Article 6 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and or body tissues or exploiting the labor or abilities of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or obscenity activities.

Article 12
Other violence as referred to in Article 6 letter f is caused by:
a. threats of violence include: any unlawful act in the form of words, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether or not using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and
b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is forced to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

CHAPTER V
VICTIM RIGHTS

Article 13
Every victim has the right to receive:
a. protection;
b. information;
c. optimal service;
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;
e. confidential handling;
f. psychological and legal accompaniment; and
g. guarantees of rights related to the victim's status as a family member and community member.

Article 14
In addition to the rights as referred to in Article 13, child victims of violence also receive special rights including:
a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic services;
c. the right to equal protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigmas; and/or
e. the right to obtain freedom;
f. the right of victims and families to obtain facilities in the judicial process.

CHAPTER VI
DUTIES AND OBLIGATIONS OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

Article 15
(1) The Regional Government is tasked with carrying out prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against Women and Children, by:
a. collecting data and information about Women and Child Victims of Violence;
b. socializing legislation related to the protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence;
c. conducting education on anti-violence values against children and women; and
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for Women and Child victims of violence.

(2) The Regional Government is obliged to carry out prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against Women and Children, to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
a. providing and facilitating the formation of integrated services for victims by involving institutions and community elements;
b. encouraging community awareness about the importance of protection for victims; and
c. empowering the community to prevent acts of violence.

CHAPTER VII
INSTITUTIONS IMPLEMENTING PROTECTION FOR VICTIMS

Article 16
(1) The Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT) as the Implementing, Prevention and Protection Institution for Women and Child Victims of Violence in the region.

(2) PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of elements:
a. health;
b. psychology;
c. law enforcement;
d. social and empowerment;
e. education and religion;
f. non-governmental organizations; and
g. private sector.

(3) Further provisions regarding PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER VIII
PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 17
(1) The Government in an effort to prevent acts of violence against women and children empowers families, parents, communities by providing information, guidance and or counseling.

(2) In addition to empowerment and support as referred to in paragraph (1), the regional government makes efforts as follows:
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;
c. seeking the creation of employment opportunities for women;
d. building community participation and concern for prevention and protection from acts of violence against women and children;
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and or care about women and child victims of violence.

Article 18
(1) Prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 16, is carried out by SKPDs whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
a. social;
b. health;
c. education;
d. employment;
e. women's empowerment and child protection;
f. mental;
g. security and order;

(2) Prevention of acts of violence by SKPD as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated and continuous manner based on existing regulations.

CHAPTER IX
IMPLEMENTATION, FORMS OF PROTECTION AND SERVICES

Article 19
(1) Implementation of Protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the PPT framework.

(2) PPT in handling medical, legal, medicolegal, psychological and economic protection which in its implementation can partner with Social Community Institutions engaged in the protection of Women and Children.

(3) Implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) must pay attention to religious norms, customs and the rights and obligations of parents/guardians, husbands and/or other persons who are legally responsible for victims.

(4) The mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) which are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 20
(1) Forms of protection provided to Victims, witnesses and reporters organized by PPT are in the form of protection:
a. medical;
b. legal;
c. medicolegal (Forensic Medicine);
d. psychological; or
e. economic.

(2) The form of medical protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, is in the form of treatment and recovery from physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.

(3) The form of legal protection for Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, is in the form of services and accompaniment to assist victims in undergoing legal and judicial processes.

(4) The form of medicolegal protection for Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, is in the form of medical services for the purposes of proof in the field of law.

(5) The form of psychological protection for Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, is in the form of accompaniment in order to restore traumatic conditions including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving.

(6) The form of economic protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1), is in the form of services for skills and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

CHAPTER X
REPORTING

Article 21
(1) PPT reports prevention, handling and protection activities for women and child victims of violence to the SKPD in charge of women's empowerment and child protection.

(2) The Head of SKPD in charge of women's empowerment reports prevention, handling and protection activities for women and child victims of violence to the Regent.

(3) The Regent reports protection of women and child victims of violence to the Governor.

(4) Further provisions regarding procedures and reporting mechanisms as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (3) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER XI
CONTROL, GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 22
(1) The Regent implements control, guidance, and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of violence against women and children.

(2) Control, guidance, and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the SKPD in charge of women's empowerment and child protection.

CHAPTER XII
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 23
(1) The community participates in the implementation of protection for victims.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. forming family partners at the village/sub-district level by the community;
b. forming women and child protection units within community organizations;
c. conducting independent socialization of women's and children's rights;
d. providing first aid to victims; and
e. reporting to authorized agencies if violence against victims occurs in their environment.

(3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector, and mass media.

CHAPTER XIII
FUNDING

Article 24
(1) The regional government is obliged to allocate a budget to the integrated service center (PPT) in implementing protection for women and child victims of violence.

(2) Budget allocation as referred to in paragraph (1) is adjusted to regional financial capacity and implemented in accordance with applicable legislation.

CHAPTER XIV
SANCTION PROVISIONS

Article 25
(1) Officials appointed to implement integrated protection, services and recovery who do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities are subject to actions and or sanctions in accordance with applicable legislation;

(2) PPT managers who in carrying out the task of protection, services and recovery for victims of violence, violate the principles of implementation of protection, services and recovery may be subject to sanctions in accordance with PPT internal mechanisms and sanctions in accordance with applicable legislation.

CHAPTER XV
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 26
This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Pringsewu Regency.

I. GENERAL

One of the materials in the Amendment to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia is the guarantee of human rights and constitutional rights of citizens. The amendment can be interpreted as a manifestation of awareness about the importance of protecting human rights and constitutional rights of citizens, which grew and became the spirit of reform. Acts of violence against women and children are violations of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and nature without discrimination.

Every citizen has human rights that must be guaranteed by the state to obtain a sense of security, be protected and be free from all forms of violence. Women and children are vulnerable groups, so they need to receive protection from the state that can provide prevention and protection for victims of violence.

Regional government together with the community must implement prevention, protection and recovery efforts for child and women victims of violence for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity.

So far, legislation regulating the protection of women and child victims of violence has not regulated protection efforts in the Region so institutional and regulatory support is needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, especially in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and child victims of violence in Pringsewu Regency.

Article 1
Sufficiently clear.

Article 2
What is meant by "respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity" is a series of actions to respect, appreciate and ensure the fulfillment of victims' rights in order to uphold human dignity.

What is meant by "non-discrimination" is not making distinctions for any reason and in any way, whether concerning religion and beliefs, ethnicity, race, gender, skin color, language, and politics.

What is meant by "gender equality and justice" is a process to be fair for both men and women in obtaining opportunities and their rights as human beings to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, defense and security activities as well as equality in enjoying development results.

What is meant by "protection of women's and children's human rights" is providing human rights in the form of a sense of security for women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court decisions.

What is meant by "best interests of victims" is all actions concerning victims carried out by government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, must be the primary consideration.
