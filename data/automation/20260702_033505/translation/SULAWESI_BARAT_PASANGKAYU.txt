REGIONAL REGULATION OF PASANGKAYU REGENCY NUMBER 9 YEAR 2019
CONCERNING
PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD
REGENT OF PASANGKAYU,

Considering: a. that women and children are creations of Almighty God who constitute important elements of Indonesian society and Nation;
b. that in social life, women and children often experience violence or unpleasant treatment;
c. that all forms of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children.

Bearing in Mind: 1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 7 Year 2003 concerning Formation of East Luwu Regency and North Mamuju Regency in South Sulawesi Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2003 Number 27, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4270);
3. Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 244, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as last amended by Law Number 9 Year 2015 concerning Second Amendment to Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2015 Number 58, Supplement to State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);

With Joint Approval of
REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF PASANGKAYU REGENCY and
REGENT OF PASANGKAYU

HAS DECIDED:

To Establish: REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN.

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Pasangkayu Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Pasangkayu.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and DPRD in administering Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Woman is an adult human of female gender and a person recognized by law as a Woman.
6. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a Child in the womb.
7. Protection is all service actions to fulfill rights, as well as guarantee and protect the rights of victims of violence.
8. Protection of Women is an effort aimed at protecting Women and their rights in order to obtain security from acts of violence.
9. Protection of Children is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and honor, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
10. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.
11. Victim of violence hereinafter referred to as victim is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.
12. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
13. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related agencies and the community in carrying out activities for the implementation of protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.
14. Safe House is temporary housing provided to give protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

CHAPTER II
OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

Article 2
The objectives of protection of Women and Children are to:
a. provide and increase security for Women and Children;
b. help prevent acts of Violence against Women and Children;
c. help prevent exploitation activities against Women and Children.
d. provide Services to Women and Children in facing legal problems; and
e. help improve the quality of life of Women and Children.

Article 3
The scope of regulation in this Regional Regulation includes:
a. forms of Violence;
b. rights of Women and Children;
c. duties and authorities;
d. handling of acts of Violence;
e. funding;
f. guidance and supervision; and
g. community participation.

CHAPTER III
FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 4
Forms of violence against Women and Children are:
a. Physical violence;
b. Psychological violence;
c. Sexual violence;
d. neglect; and/or
e. exploitation.

Article 5
Physical violence as referred to in Article 4 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or cause death.

Article 6
Psychological violence as referred to in Article 4 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 7
Sexual violence as referred to in Article 4 letter c is in the form of:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse; and/or
c. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Article 8
Neglect as referred to in Article 4 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a Child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a Child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians or other parties responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the Victim is under the control of that person.

Article 9
Exploitation as referred to in Article 4 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the Victim's consent that include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or Services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the Victim to obtain benefits not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

CHAPTER IV
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Part One
Rights of Women

Article 10
(1) Regional Government is obliged to protect the rights of Women in the Region.
(2) Rights of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of rights to:
a. basic needs;
b. comfort and security;
c. maximum health Services in facing acts of Violence;
d. assistance services, legal aid and spiritual guidance in facing acts of Violence; and
e. confidentiality regarding problems faced.

Part Two
Rights of Children

Article 11
(1) Regional Government is obliged to protect the rights of Children in the Region.
(2) Rights of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of rights to:
a. be able to live, grow, develop and participate properly in accordance with human dignity and honor and receive guidance and Protection from Violence and discrimination;
b. worship according to their religion, think and express themselves in accordance with their level of intelligence and age under parental guidance;
c. a birth certificate obtained and/or arranged by their parents in accordance with applicable laws and regulations;
d. receive protection from family, community and Regional Government against discriminatory treatment and exploitation whether economic or social, neglect, cruelty, violence, persecution, injustice and other mistreatment;
e. regain formal or non-formal education for Children who drop out of school;
f. obtain health Services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual and social needs;
g. obtain education and teaching according to their abilities, interests and talents; and
h. obtain other rights in accordance with laws and regulations.

CHAPTER V
DUTIES AND AUTHORITIES

Part One
Duties

Paragraph 1
Duties of Regional Government in Protection of Women

Article 12
(1) Regional Government is obliged to carry out Protection of Women in the Region.
(2) Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by preparing a strategic plan for Protection of Women for the long term, medium term, and short term.
(3) Strategic preparation as referred to in paragraph (2) must be integrated with Regional development planning documents.
(4) Regional Government in preparing the strategic plan as referred to in paragraph (3) may involve the community, social institutions and/or other institutions that carry out activities related to Protection of Women.

Article 13
(1) Regional Government is obliged to prevent acts of Violence against Women.
(2) Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in the form of activities:
a. Regional Action Plan;
c. preparation of an effective information system; and
d. cooperation with institutions that have activities in preventing acts of Violence against Women.

Article 14
Regional Government is obliged to assist in handling acts of Violence against Women.

Paragraph 2
Duties of Regional Government in Protection of Children

Article 15
(1) Regional Government is obliged to carry out Protection of Children in the Region.
(2) Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by preparing a strategic plan for Protection of Children for the long term, medium term, and short term.
(3) Strategic preparation as referred to in paragraph (2) must be integrated with Regional development planning documents.
(4) Regional Government in preparing the strategic plan as referred to in paragraph (3) may involve the community, social institutions and/or other institutions that carry out activities related to Protection of Children.

Article 16
Regional Government provides guidance and direction for Children to embrace religion in accordance with the religion and beliefs of their parents.

Article 17
(1) Regional Government is obliged to provide comprehensive health facilities for Children.
(2) Comprehensive health facilities as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of community health centers and hospitals.
(3) Comprehensive health as referred to in paragraph (1) includes promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, both for basic health Services and referrals.
(4) Comprehensive health as referred to in paragraph (3) is provided free of charge for families who are unable or poor families.
(5) Regional Government carries out activities to support the provision of breast milk to infants.

Article 18
(1) Regional Government is obliged to organize primary and secondary education for Children.
(2) In organizing education as referred to in paragraph (1), Regional Government provides education cost assistance or free education for students from poor families and/or neglected Children.

Article 19
(1) Regional Government is obliged to accommodate and/or care for Children who are neglected by their parents and/or family.
(2) Accommodation and/or care of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out through cooperation with institutions, social foundations, or Orphanages.

Article 20
(1) Regional Government is obliged to provide special Protection for Children Victims of riots or Victims of natural disasters.
(2) Special protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of fulfillment of needs:
a. basic needs consisting of food, clothing, housing, education, health, learning and recreation, security guarantees, and equal treatment; and
b. special needs for Children with disabilities and Children experiencing psychosocial disorders.

Article 21
(1) Regional Government is obliged to prevent efforts and acts of violence against Children.
(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out in the form of activities that include:
a. early detection and intervention;
b. socialization, seminars or workshops;
c. preparation of an effective information system; and
d. cooperation with institutions that have activities in preventing acts of violence against Children.

Article 22
Regional Government is obliged to assist in handling acts of Violence against Children.

Part Two
Authorities

Article 23
(1) Regional Government has the authority to:
a. accommodate Victims of acts of Violence against Women and Children; and
b. accommodate and/or care for neglected Children.
(2) Regional Government in exercising the authority as referred to in paragraph (1) may cooperate with related parties.

Article 24
(1) Related parties as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) are:
a. social community organizations, institutions or orphanages that have activities related to protection, care, accommodation, guidance, empowerment of Children and/or Women;
b. social workers or volunteers;
c. advocates; and
d. religious leaders.
(2) Cooperation as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) is carried out in the form of a cooperation agreement.

CHAPTER VI
HANDLING OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Article 25
(1) Regional Government prepares medical personnel at community health centers or hospitals specifically to handle Victims of Violence against Women and Children.
(2) Medical personnel as referred to in paragraph (1) are obliged to provide complete health services to Victims of Violence against Women and Children.

Article 26
(1) Regional Government is obliged to assist in providing special rooms for Victims of Violence against Women and Children.
(2) Special rooms as referred to in paragraph (1) are located at the Police Resort and at every Police Sector in the Region.

Article 27
(1) Social workers or volunteer personnel as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter b are tasked with accompanying Victims in facing the process of resolving acts of violence.
(2) In carrying out duties as referred to in paragraph (1), social workers or volunteer personnel carry out activities:
a. counseling or guidance to strengthen and provide security for Victims;
b. provide information regarding the rights of Victims to obtain Protection from the police and determination of protection orders from the court;
c. escort Victims to Safe Houses or alternative residences; and
d. carry out integrated coordination in providing services to Victims with agencies or related parties needed by Victims.

Article 28
(1) Regional Government is obliged to prepare Safe Houses.
(2) Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (1) are used for temporary handling of Victims of Violence against Women and Children.
(3) Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (2) are provided in every Sub-district.

Article 29
(1) Advocates as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter c help provide legal services to Victims or provide legal consultation in the form of information regarding Victims' rights and the judicial process;
(2) Advocates are obliged to accompany and/or represent Victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings.
(3) Violence information from Victims that is personal in nature must be kept confidential by advocates.
(4) Advocates may coordinate with fellow law enforcers, companion volunteers, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

Article 30
Religious leaders as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter d are tasked with:
a. providing spiritual guidance or strengthening of faith and piety to Victims; and
b. providing explanations regarding the rights and obligations of Victims.

CHAPTER VII
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Part One
Guidance

Article 31
(1) Regional Government carries out guidance for Women and Children
(2) Guidance for Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through:
a. socialization;
b. guidance; and/or
c. training.
(3) Guidance for Women and Children is carried out by Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of empowerment of Women and Children.

Part Two
Supervision

Article 32
(1) Regional Government conducts supervision in the context of Protection of Women and Children.
(2) Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by monitoring and evaluation.
(3) Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out periodically at least 2 (two) times a year.

CHAPTER VIII
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 33
(1) The community participates in efforts to protect Women and Children.
(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in the form of:
a. providing data or information regarding Women and Children who have not received fulfillment of rights from the Regional Government;
b. providing information on every condition that tends to lead to acts of violence against Women and Children to the competent authorities and/or Regional Government; and/or
c. promoting Protection of Women and Children.

CHAPTER IX
FUNDING

Article 34
(1) Funding for the implementation of Protection of Women and Children is charged to:
a. state revenue and expenditure budget;
b. regional revenue and expenditure budget; and
c. other legal and non-binding sources.
(2) Funding as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is budgeted for Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of empowerment of Women and Children.

CHAPTER X
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 35
This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Pasangkayu Regency.

I. GENERAL

Protection from violence that threatens the safety of Women and Children must be provided from an early stage. Protection is needed to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. For example, Women and Children become Victims of persecution, neglect, eviction and mistreatment by parents or others. For Women and Children experiencing such problems, Regional Government must provide Safe Houses and adequate budget.

Likewise, the role of various parties to help handle violence problems faced by Women and Children must be in synergy with Regional Government programs and activities. Social community organizations, volunteers, social workers, medical personnel, advocates and religious leaders must also be mobilized and can participate in providing and increasing security for Women and Children.

For the Regional Government of Pasangkayu Regency, a concrete step to create a legal umbrella to provide and increase security and prevent acts of violence against Women and Children is by establishing a Regional Regulation concerning Protection for Children and Women. Furthermore, the implementation of protection of Women and Children is carried out based on the principles of: justice; respect for the rights of Victims; justice and gender equality; non-discrimination; and the best interests of Victims.
