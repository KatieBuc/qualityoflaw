Article I

Several provisions in Regional Regulation of Bantul Regency Number 15 Year 2013 concerning Protection of Children and Women Victims of Violence (Regional Gazette of Bantul Regency Year 2013 Number 15, Supplement to Regional Gazette of Bantul Regency Number 28), are amended as follows:

1. The provisions of Article 1 are amended, to read as follows:
Article 1

1. Government is the Central Government.
2. Region is Bantul Regency.
3. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.

5 2015

4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Bantul Regency as an element of Regional Government administration.

5. Regent is the Regent of Bantul.

6. Regional Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the regional work unit of Bantul Regency.

7. Woman is a person who is female by sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.

8. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.

9. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life against victims.

10. Violence against women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.

11. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, economically, and other violence.

12. Physical Violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or causes death.

13. Psychological Violence is any act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, sense of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

14. Sexual Violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

15. Economic Violence is any act that neglects family members in the form of not providing livelihood, care or maintenance in a proper manner.

16. Exploitation Against Children is any act involving children in activities that can harm their welfare and development or endanger the safety of children with the aim of enabling others to obtain economic, sexual, social, or political benefits, including if it contains restrictions or elimination of opportunities for children to obtain their rights.

6 2015

17. Sexual harassment is any action with sexual nuances conveyed through physical or non-physical contact that targets sexual body parts or a person's sexuality, including by using whistles, winking, comments or sexually nuanced speech, displaying pornographic materials and sexual desires, pinching or touching body parts, movements or gestures that are sexual in nature resulting in discomfort, offense, feeling degraded in dignity, and possibly causing health and safety problems.

18. Victim is a Child and Woman who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring in the area of Bantul Regency.

19. Prevention is a direct effort made by the Regional Government to prevent acts of violence against women and children.

20. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.

21. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security for women carried out by family members, police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.

22. Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

23. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will, is or has occurred.

24. Women's Empowerment is any effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social capacity, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.

25. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has expertise in accompanying victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

26. Spiritual guidance is assistance in the form of services for spiritual strengthening so that sick people can rise to be closer to God so that a sense of calm, security arises, and mental health is achieved.

27. Judicial body is a general court that has the authority to receive, examine, and adjudicate, as well as resolve every case submitted to it, to realize law enforcement and justice.

7 2015

28. Prosecution is the action of the public prosecutor to transfer a case to the competent district court in the manner and according to the procedure regulated by law with a request that it be examined and decided by a judge in a court session.

29. Lawsuit is a notification accompanied by a request by an interested party to a judge to take action according to law against someone who has harmed them in a civil manner.

30. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organizations (NGOs) that provide assistance to victims of violence.

31. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for victims of violence.

32. Regional Action Plan is the foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in carrying out activities for organizing prevention and protection of children and women victims of violence.

33. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers of perpetrators in accordance with predetermined standards.

34. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes who can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.

35. Standard Operating Procedure hereinafter abbreviated as SOP is the standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims from complaints/identification, rehabilitation, health, social rehabilitation, legal services to repatriation and social reintegration of witnesses and/or victims.

36. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line up or down to the third degree.

37. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, foster care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.

38. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

39. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of Bantul Regency

40. Gender responsive budget is a budget that provides and accommodates justice and equality for women and men to access and obtain access, as well as benefits from development programs and participate in decision-making processes and have control over resources.

8 2015

41. Violence Victim Protection Forum hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against Children and Women whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner.

I. GENERAL

As a manifestation of the Regional Government's responsibility in taking action both legally, politically, economically and socially to prevent, suppress, reduce, and eliminate all forms of violence against children and women, Regional Regulation of Bantul Regency Number 15 Year 2013 concerning Protection of Children and Women Victims of Violence has been established.

Until now, the said Regional Regulation has been implemented in the administration of government in the Region. However, in accordance with the supervision mechanism for regional legal products, the Regional Government of the Special Region of Yogyakarta has provided clarification on Regional Regulation of Bantul Regency Number 15 Year 2013 concerning Protection of Children and Women Victims of Violence. From the results of said clarification, there are several substantive changes that must be followed up with an Amendment to the Regional Regulation.

2 2015

Based on the above considerations, Regional Regulation of Bantul Regency Number 15 Year 2013 concerning Protection of Children and Women Victims of Violence needs to be amended in its Articles and explanations.

Article 2

Letter a

What is meant by "respect and fulfillment of victims' rights" is a series of actions to respect, appreciate and ensure the fulfillment of victims' rights.

Letter b

What is meant by "justice and gender equality" is a process to be fair to men and women and equal conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, national defense and security activities and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

Letter c

What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment toward victims without making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d

What is meant by "the best interests of victims" is that all recovery, rehabilitation, reunification actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, must be the primary consideration.

3 2015

Letter e

What is meant by "empowerment" is strengthening victims of violence to be able to work and work independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.

Paragraph (3)

Letter a

What is meant by the "prevention" aspect is a protection strategy effort through:

a. Primary prevention, all people, families, communities and the state in efforts to increase knowledge capacity, understanding and ensure that violence against women and children does not occur, including policy socialization, adequate services, supportive workplace policies, and life skills training for women and children. What is meant by life skills training includes conflict resolution without violence, stress management skills, resource management, effective decision making, effective interpersonal communication, guidance on psychosocial development of women and children.

4 2015

b. Secondary prevention, aimed at community groups at high risk in efforts to improve skills, including training and victim services to ensure that violence against women and children does not occur in the next generation. Activities carried out here include conducting home visits for parents who have just had children to conduct self-assessment of whether they are at risk of committing violence against children in the future.

c. Tertiary prevention, intended to improve parenting abilities that ensure that violence against women and children is not repeated, what is done here is integrated services for women and children who are victims of violence, through counseling, stress management training.

Letter b

What is meant by the "service and assistance" aspect is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals and assistants according to their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
