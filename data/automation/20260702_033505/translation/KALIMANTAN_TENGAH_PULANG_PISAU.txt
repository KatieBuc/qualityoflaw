BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF PULANG PISAU,

Considering: a. that protection of women and children is an integral part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and respected by the state;

b. that violence against women and children in the Region tends to increase, therefore the Regional Government and/or community need to play an active role optimally to provide protection, so that women and children are prevented and free from violence or threats of violence, torture, or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth;

c. that protection of women and children is a mandatory concurrent affair that is the authority, obligation, and responsibility of the Regional Government, therefore regulation is needed as a basis for comprehensive and integrated implementation of protection for women and children;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Implementation of Protection for Women and Children;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Pulang Pisau Regency.

- 5 -

2. Regional Government is the regional head as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.

3. Regent is the Regent of Pulang Pisau.

4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of regional government administration.

5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regional Head and Regional House of Representatives in implementing Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.

6. Child is a person who has not reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child still in the womb.

7. Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as PPA, is a handling effort to protect and fulfill the rights of women and children from all forms of violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.

8. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life, body or results in deprivation of a person's freedom.

9. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or hardship for women physically, sexually or psychologically, economic or social exploitation, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.

10. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, economic or social exploitation, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.

11. Domestic violence is any act against a person, especially women, that results in suffering or hardship physically, sexually, psychologically and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts of coercion or unlawful deprivation of freedom within the household sphere.

- 6 -

12. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causes death.

13. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

14. Economic violence is any act that deliberately neglects family members in the form of not providing livelihood, care or maintenance in a proper manner.

15. Psychological violence is any act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

16. Trafficking in persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payments or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that person, whether conducted within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in exploitation of the person.

17. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on human differences on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that results in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.

18. Maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth is action or attitude that does not conform to prevailing moral, ethical, or legal norms, encompassing various actions that harm others, whether physically, emotionally, or psychologically.

19. Exploitation is action with or without the victim's consent that includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, physical, sexual, reproductive organ utilization, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or abilities of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits.

- 7 -

20. Sexual Exploitation is any form of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution and obscenity activities.

21. Threat of violence is any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without means that causes fear or restricts a person's fundamental freedom.

22. Victim is a woman and child who experiences and/or suffers either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.

23. Rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological, and social conditions in order to perform their role again properly both in the family and in society.

24. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for Protection of Women and Children, abbreviated as UPTD-PPA, is an Integrated Service Center established by the Regional Government to provide services to women and child victims of violence comprehensively including information services, legal assistance and accompaniment, counseling services, medical services and safe houses through referral.

25. Task Force for Prevention and Handling of Violence Against Women and Children, hereinafter referred to as Task Force, is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating efforts to prevent and handle criminal acts of trafficking in persons in Pulang Pisau Regency.

26. Companion is a person who has expertise in accompanying victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

27. Optimal Service is a dynamic effort consisting of various regularly related parts, followed by performance offered by one party to another party by providing benefits, in order to achieve the best goal.

Article 2

Implementation of PPA is conducted based on the principles of:

a. humanity;

- 8 -

b. justice and gender equality;

c. protection;

d. best interests of women and children; and

e. non-discrimination.

Article 3

Regulation of the fundamentals of PPA implementation aims to:

a. prevent acts of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and worth of women and children;

b. eliminate all forms of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and worth of women and children;

c. protect and provide security for women and children;

d. provide services to women and child victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses; and

e. facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize harmonious and prosperous household integrity.

CHAPTER II
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Article 4

Women and child victims of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth receive the following rights:

a. Right to guarantee space for community participation in government, including in decision/policy making;

b. Right to population documents and civil registration;

c. Right to Legal Aid;

d. Right to be respected for dignity and worth as human beings;

e. Right to health and psychological recovery from suffering experienced by victims;

f. Right to determine their own decisions;

g. Right to obtain information;

h. Right to confidentiality;

i. Right to compensation;

j. Right to social rehabilitation;

k. Right to complaint handling;

- 9 -

l. Right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and

m. Right to accompaniment.

Article 5

Child victims of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 4, also receive special rights, as follows:

a. Right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;

b. Right to basic services;

c. Right to equal protection;

d. Right to be free from various stigma;

e. Right to employment;

f. Right to decent housing and environment;

g. Right to Women's empowerment; and

h. Right to violence prevention.

CHAPTER III
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 6

Obligations and responsibilities in implementing protection for women and children are the joint responsibility of:

a. regional government;

b. community;

c. family;

d. parents; and

e. non-governmental institutions including Customary Institutions and Business Entities.

Article 7

Obligations and responsibilities of the regional government as referred to in Article 6 letter a, include:

a. implementing policies for protection of women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth established by the government;

- 10 -

b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for protection of women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth;

c. conducting cooperation in implementing protection for women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth;

d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth;

e. allocating budget for implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth for women and children according to regional financial capacity; and

f. fostering and supervising implementation and protection for women and children from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth.

Article 8

(1) Obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letter b are implemented in the form of community participation.

(2) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

a. preventing acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth against women and children;

b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and

c. participating in providing assistance and/or handling to victims of acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth against women and children.

- 11 -

(3) Forms of participation are implemented responsibly in accordance with statutory provisions.

Article 9

Obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 letters c and d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

Article 10

(1) Obligations and responsibilities of Customary Institutions in implementing protection for women and children as referred to in Article 6 letter e, include:

a. customary institutions have responsibility to maintain the dignity and human rights of women and children in accordance with highly upheld humanitarian values;

b. responsible for preventing all forms of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and worth of women and children in the customary environment;

c. ensuring gender equality and children's rights are respected, without discrimination based on gender or age;

d. responsible for raising awareness of customary communities regarding the rights of women and children, including awareness of the importance of protection and respect for their rights;

e. conducting outreach and training programs for members of customary communities, especially customary leaders, about the rights of women and children and effective protection methods;

f. providing psychosocial and legal support to victims of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and worth of Women and children including facilitating victim access to protection and legal institutions;

g. playing an active role in the reporting and law enforcement process for cases of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and worth of women and children in customary communities;

- 12 -

h. facilitating the formation of PPA institutions at the customary level tasked with protecting their rights and providing assistance and support to victims;

i. integrating customary values that support equality and PPA into community outreach and education programs; and

j. building partnerships with non-governmental organizations, protection institutions, and educational institutions to strengthen PPA efforts.

(2) Obligations and responsibilities of Business Entities in implementing protection for women and children as referred to in Article 6 letter e, include:

a. obligated to comply with all laws, regulations, and rules related to PPA at national and regional levels;

b. providing a safe and supportive work environment, free from harassment, discrimination, violence, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades the dignity and worth of women and children;

c. providing protection houses for female workers at the workplace;

d. forming partnerships with regional government in PPA;

e. providing empowerment for women and children as joint responsibility with regional government;

f. conducting outreach programs in communities around the company to raise awareness about PPA;

g. creating policies, programs, and PPA activities;

h. supporting social programs that increase women's economic independence and support children's education;

i. supporting the establishment of protection centers and services for victims of violence against women and children; and

j. supporting PPA programs in Pulang Pisau Regency through Corporate Social Responsibility instruments and/or assistance in other forms in accordance with applicable statutory provisions.

- 13 -

CHAPTER IV
DUTIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

Article 11

The Regional Government is tasked with conducting prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against Women and Children, by:

a. collecting data and information about Women and Child Victims of acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth;

b. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to PPA;

c. conducting education about anti-violence values in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth against women and children for children and women;

d. conducting prevention of child marriage;

e. providing rehabilitation facilities; and

f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of PPA against acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth.

CHAPTER V
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Article 12

(1) Implementation of protection for victims is conducted in an integrated manner within the UPTD-PPA framework.

(2) Provisions concerning UPTD-PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) will be further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

(3) In addition to establishing UPTD-PPA, to support the implementation of protection for women and children, the Regent establishes:

a. task force for violence against women and children;

b. regional action committee for elimination of worst forms of child labor.

- 14 -

Article 13

(1) The task force as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (3) letter a, consists of representatives from Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, and researchers/academics.

(2) The task force as referred to in Article 11 paragraph (3) letter a, is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating prevention and handling of acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth against women and children.

Article 14

(1) The committee as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (3) letter b, consists of representatives from Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, and researchers/academics.

(2) Further provisions regarding the duties and functions of the committee are stipulated by Regent's decree.

Article 15

Implementation of services to victims is conducted with the principles of:

a. no fees charged;

b. fast;

c. safe;

d. empathy;

e. non-discrimination;

f. easily accessible; and

g. confidentiality.

CHAPTER VI
COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

Article 16

(1) In order to achieve the objectives of PPA implementation as referred to in Article 3, the Regional Government cooperates with:

a. provincial government;

b. other Regency/City governments;

c. Police;

- 15 -

d. Prosecutor's Office;

e. Court; and/or

f. non-governmental institutions.

(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:

a. exchange of data and information;

b. rehabilitation of victims of violence;

c. repatriation and social reintegration;

d. provision of evidence and witnesses;

e. followed up in accordance with applicable law; and

f. Prevention efforts to prevent criminal acts against Women and children.

(3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) is set forth in the form of joint agreement.

Article 17

(1) The regional government establishes partnerships with the business world in implementing PPA.

(2) Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted through:

a. notification of information on employment opportunities for women victims of violence;

b. education and training for women victims of violence;

c. educational assistance for women and child victims of violence who are withdrawn from their education; and

d. fostering and increasing economic independence of women victims of violence.

(3) Partnership as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) is set forth in the form of agreement.

CHAPTER VII
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 18

(1) The Regional Government is obligated to conduct guidance and supervision of PPA from all forms of violence, discrimination, special protection and other problems.

(2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:

a. guidelines and standards for needs fulfillment;

b. technical guidance and training;

c. provision of facilities;

- 16 -

d. monitoring; and

e. evaluation.

(3) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2), is in order to realize PPA objectives that comply with minimum service standards.

Article 19

The community may conduct supervision of PPA implementation from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth implemented by the Regional Government with a mechanism for conveying aspirations to the Regent or to the DPRD.

CHAPTER VIII
ACCOMPANIMENT

Article 20

Accompaniment is conducted by persons or institutions that have expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and have cooperated with UPTD-PPA.

CHAPTER IX
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 21

(1) The community participates in PPA both individually and in groups.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted in the form of:

a. preventing acts of violence against Women and Children;

b. submitting reports to authorities if acts of violence against Women and Children occur; and

c. providing assistance to victims.

Article 22

(1) Social community organizations that participate in implementing protection for victims as referred to in article 21 paragraph 2, are given guidance by the Regional Government.

(2) Guidance may be in the form of social guidance, skills guidance and operational technical guidance.

- 17 -

CHAPTER X
FUNDING

Article 23

Funding for PPA implementation is sourced from:

1. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget;

2. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and

3. other lawful and non-binding funding sources in accordance with statutory provisions.

Article 24

(1) The Regional Government may provide funding assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations that implement PPA from acts of violence in various forms, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and/or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth.

(2) Funding assistance, according to regional financial capacity, and implemented in accordance with statutory provisions in the field of regional finance.

CHAPTER XI
REPORTING

Article 25

(1) UPTD-PPA is obligated to report in writing the implementation of PPA Implementation to the Regent.

(2) The report as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

a. administration;

b. finance;

c. services; and

d. performance.

(3) Submission of reports as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) is conducted at least once every 3 (three) months.

CHAPTER XII
SANCTION PROVISIONS

Article 26

(1) Any person who intentionally does not provide protection for women and children resulting in violence, allows violence to occur, and/or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, is subject to sanctions as regulated in applicable laws and regulations.

- 18 -

(2) Officials appointed to implement protection who do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities are subject to administrative action or sanctions.

(3) UPTD-PPA managers who carry out service duties, if they violate service principles, may be subject to sanctions in accordance with UPTD-PPA internal mechanisms.

CHAPTER XIII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 27

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Pulang Pisau Regency.

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so their dignity and worth need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and character without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Pulang Pisau Regency so they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, protection for women and children needs to be conducted in the form of regional regulations.

So far, laws and regulations governing PPA from acts of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, therefore institutional and regulatory support is needed that can guarantee its implementation.

The Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for women and children, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for women and children from acts of violence in Pulang Pisau Regency.
