CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:

1. Region is Seluma Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Government of Seluma Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Seluma.
4. Regional People's Representative Council hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional People's Representative Council of Seluma Regency.
5. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of Seluma Regency.
6. Regional Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is the Regional Work Unit of the Regional Government of Seluma Regency.
7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb.
8. Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence and/or threats of violence that include physical, sexual, psychological, economic and/or social violence, in the family, community and/or society, in conflict situations, post-conflict, and state.
9. Implementation is all actions that include Protection, Services, and Recovery for victims of violence.
10. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by regional government, family, advocates/paralegals, social institutions, society, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties.
11. Special protection is protection given to children in emergency situations, children in conflict with the law, children in minority and isolated groups, children who are exploited economically and/or sexually, children who are trafficked, children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic and other addictive substance abuse (drugs), children victims of kidnapping, sale, trafficking, children victims of violence both physical and/or mental, children victims of conflict who experience physical violence and trauma, children with disabilities, and children victims of mistreatment and neglect.
12. Recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of violence to be more empowered, both physically, psychologically, socially, economically, culturally and politically.
13. Implementer is a government institution as well as social institutions, religious institutions, customary institutions, in accordance with their respective duties and functions, including providing facilities needed for victim recovery.
14. Violence is any act and/or threat of act that results in or can result in misery or suffering both physical, psychological, sexual or neglect.
15. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
16. Violence against children is any act against children that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and mistreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of children.
17. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or bodily defects to a person, miscarriage, fainting and/or causes death.
18. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
19. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
20. Sexual Harassment is any unwanted sexual action/behavior/gesture in verbal (words) or written form, physical, non-verbal, and visual for sexual purposes, having sexual content, thus causing anger, feelings of humiliation, shame, discomfort, and insecurity for others.
21. Domestic neglect is the act of neglecting women and/or children within the scope of the household which according to the applicable law for the person concerned or due to agreement or contract he/she is obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance for that person. Neglect also applies to anyone who causes economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.
22. Service is an action that must be immediately taken for victims when seeing, hearing and knowing of the impending, ongoing or occurred violence against victims.
23. Assistance is all actions carried out in the form of complaint services, health, advocacy and legal aid, social rehabilitation including spiritual guidance, counseling, psychological therapy and economic empowerment, repatriation and social reintegration for strengthening and advocacy as well as recovery of victims of violence.
24. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has expertise in assisting victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and self-recovery of victims of violence.
25. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is an institution providing services for victims of violence, hospital-based, managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psychological, and legal services involving health workers, education workers, social institutions, law enforcement officials, psychologists, psychiatrists, volunteer assistants and/or spiritual counselors.
26. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is an institution for victim recovery of violence from health, education and economic independence aspects.
27. Safe house is a temporary residence used to provide protection for victims in accordance with determined operational standards.
28. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood family in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
29. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
30. Society is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES, PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out based on the principles of:

a. respect for human rights;
b. gender equality and justice;
c. non-discrimination;
d. best interests of victims; and
e. respect for the rights of women and children.

Article 3

Regulation of implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is intended to provide protection and empowerment for women and children victims of violence.

Article 4

Implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence aims to:

a. prevent all forms of acts of violence including human trafficking;
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
c. protect, provide a sense of security for victims of acts of violence;
d. provide services to victims of violence; and
e. implement comprehensive recovery for victims.

CHAPTER III
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 5

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection for women and children victims of violence are the joint responsibility of:

a. Regional Government;
b. Society;
c. Family; and
d. Parents.

Article 6

(1) Obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 5 letter a, include:

a. establishing and implementing policies, programs and activities for protection of women and children victims of violence;
b. conducting cooperation in implementing protection of women and children from victims of violence;
c. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for implementation of protection of women and children from victims of violence;
d. allocating budget for implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity; and
e. fostering and supervising implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence.

(2) In carrying out obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes programs and activities for action on protection of women and children in one Regional Action Plan as a basis for SKPD in implementing protection of women and children victims of violence.

(3) Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

(4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 7

(1) Obligations and responsibilities of society as referred to in Article 5 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation.

(2) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

a. caring for, maintaining, educating, and protecting children;
b. developing children according to their abilities, talents and interests;
c. preventing marriage at child age;
d. preventing acts of violence against women and children;
e. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children victims of violence to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
f. participating in handling victims of acts of violence.

(3) In the event that parents do not exist, or their whereabouts are unknown, or due to some reason, cannot carry out their obligations and responsibilities, then the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), may transfer to the family, which is implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

(4) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) are implemented responsibly in accordance with laws and regulations.

Article 8

Obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 5 letters c and d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of acts of violence and protect women and children as family members.

CHAPTER IV
PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 9

(1) Regional Government in efforts to prevent acts of violence against women and children conducts empowerment and awareness to families, parents and society by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.

(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), Regional Government conducts efforts as follows:

a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;
c. opening employment opportunities for women;
d. building community participation and concern for prevention of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. building and providing complete and easily accessible information systems; and
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women and children victims of violence.

Article 10

(1) Prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 9, is implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:

a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. education;
d. manpower;
e. women's empowerment and child protection;
f. mental and spiritual; and
g. peace and order.

(2) Prevention of acts of violence by SKPD as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Action Plan.

CHAPTER V
SERVICES FOR VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 11

(1) Forms of services provided to women and children victims of violence, are as follows:

a. complaint services;
b. health services;
c. legal aid;
d. repatriation;
e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medicolegal; and
f. psychological services.

(2) Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented in accordance with minimum service standards established by the Regional Government and implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:

a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. women's empowerment and child protection; and
d. mental and spiritual. In implementing services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with Government agencies, other Regional Governments and society.

(3) Further provisions regarding procedures for handling services for protection of women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER VI
INSTITUTIONS

Article 12

Regional Government is obliged to provide complaint service units for protection of women and children victims of violence.

Article 13

(1) Regional Government and society or social service institutions may establish recovery houses or safe houses for victims of violence.

(2) Society or social institutions that establish safe houses as referred to in paragraph (1) must meet requirements as referred to in laws and regulations.

Article 14

Further provisions regarding complaint services and social services as referred to in Article 12 and Article 13, are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 15

(1) Regional Government in the context of protection services for women and children victims of violence, establishes P2TP2A as an integrated service center.

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), functions as an integrated service center in providing protection to women and children victims of violence.

(3) Further provisions regarding Organization and Work Procedures of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), are stipulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER VII
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 16

Women and children victims of violence receive the following rights:

a. right to be respected for dignity and honor as human beings;
b. right to health and psychological recovery from suffering experienced by victims;
c. right to determine their own decisions;
d. right to obtain information;
e. right to confidentiality;
f. right to social rehabilitation;
g. right to compensation;
h. right to complaint handling;
i. right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and
j. right to assistance.

Article 17

Children who become victims of acts of violence, in addition to receiving rights as referred to in Article 16, also receive special rights, as follows:

a. right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;
b. right to basic services;
c. right to equal protection;
d. right to be free from various stigma; and
e. right to obtain freedom.

CHAPTER VIII
COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

Part One
Cooperation

Article 18

(1) In order to achieve the objectives of protection of women and children victims of violence as referred to in Article 4, the Regional Government may cooperate with:

a. Government;
b. Provincial government
c. Other Regencies/Cities; and
d. Non-governmental institutions.

(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

a. exchange of data and information;
b. rehabilitation of victims of acts of violence;
c. repatriation and social reintegration; and
d. provision of evidence and witnesses in accordance with applicable law.

(3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), is set forth in the form of Joint Agreement.

Part Two
Partnership

Article 19

(1) Regional Government establishes partnerships with the business world in protection of women and children victims of violence.

(2) Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted through:

a. notification of employment opportunity information for women victims of violence;
b. education and training for women victims of violence;
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of violence who are withdrawn from their education; and
d. fostering and increasing economic independence of women victims of violence.

(3) Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), is set forth in the form of Agreement.

CHAPTER IX
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 20

(1) Regional Government is obliged to conduct guidance and supervision of implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence.

(2) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. provision of facilities;
d. monitoring; and
e. evaluation.

(3) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) in order to realize the objectives of protection of women and children victims of violence according to minimum service standards.

Article 21

Society may conduct supervision of implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence organized by the Regional Government with a mechanism for conveying aspirations to the Regent or to the DPRD.

CHAPTER X
FINANCING

Article 22

Budget allocation for implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence is sourced from:

a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget;
b. Other lawful revenue sources in accordance with laws and regulations; and
c. Other revenue sources that are non-binding in nature.

Article 23

(1) Regional Government is obliged to allocate budget to P2TP2A in implementing protection of women and children victims of violence.

(2) Budget allocation as referred to in paragraph (1), is adjusted to regional financial capacity, and is implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

CHAPTER XI
SANCTION PROVISIONS

Article 24

(1) Officials appointed to implement integrated protection, services and recovery who do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities are subject to actions and or sanctions in accordance with applicable laws and regulations;

(2) P2TP2A managers who in carrying out protection, service and recovery duties for victims of violence, violate the principles of implementation of protection, services and recovery may be subject to sanctions in accordance with P2TP2A internal mechanisms and sanctions in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

CHAPTER XII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 25

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.
In order that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Seluma Regency.

I. GENERAL

Various acts of violence both against women and children within the household and outside the household continue to increase from time to time. This condition, if without serious handling, will destroy household life, society, nation and state. Therefore, the government of Seluma Regency is obliged to provide protection to women and children victims of acts of violence which is an implementation of various laws and regulations, including Law Number 39 Year 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 21 Year 2007 concerning Eradication of Human Trafficking Crimes, Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection.

On that basis, the government of Seluma Regency has taken steps as efforts to provide protection to women and children victims of acts of violence, but has not been able to provide maximum and adequate protection against acts of violence that occur in social life of society especially to women and children, among others caused by still low public understanding of the rights of women and children, handling is not yet well coordinated, implementation is not yet sustainable, and so on. Therefore, a regulation is needed in the form of Regional Regulation concerning Implementation of protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence to answer while providing legal certainty in protection of women and children victims from acts of violence that occur.

The existence of Regional Regulation concerning Implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, gives responsibility to the Regional Government starting from prevention of acts of violence to handling victims of acts of violence. In its implementation the Regional Government cooperates with government agencies, other regional governments and society. In addition, adequate funding support both from government, Regional Government and community participation of the business world and society, it is hoped that violence against women and children can be reduced and even in time will be eliminated from beloved Seluma Regency.
