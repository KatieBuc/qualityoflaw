REGENT OF MAGETAN,

Considering: a. that violence against women and children is an act that violates the rights and dignity of women and children as human beings;
b. that protection efforts for women and children victims of violence in the Region have not been optimal, therefore institutional strengthening is needed and regulations that can guarantee its implementation in accordance with applicable provisions;
c. that based on Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 Year 2015, women's empowerment and child protection are governmental affairs that must be implemented by Regional Government;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
First Section
Definition of Terms

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is Magetan Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Government of Magetan Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Magetan.
4. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child still in the womb.
5. Woman is a human being of female sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.
6. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without using means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.
7. Victim is a woman and child who experiences suffering and/or distress either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
8. Protection is all preventive actions, services and empowerment to protect and guarantee the rights of women and children from acts of violence.
9. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
10. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing of the impending, ongoing or completed occurrence of violence against victims.
11. Empowerment is strengthening of women victims of violence to be able to work and earn independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
12. Companion is a person who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
13. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by companions to women and children victims of violence during the service and empowerment process.
14. Counseling is the provision of assistance by an expert/trained person in such a way that the psychological understanding and ability of the victim and/or perpetrator increases in solving the problems faced;
15. Integrated Service Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is technical service units in the Region that carry out integrated service functions for women and children victims of crime and/or violence, including as a center for information on women's empowerment and child protection.
16. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is one form of UPT established by the Regional Government that functions as an information center, service center and empowerment center for women and children. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and children, or father and children, or mother and children.
18. Household is family members and relatives including grandchildren, nieces/nephews, older siblings, younger siblings, grandfathers, grandmothers, cousins and non-relatives including domestic helpers and drivers who live and eat from 1 (one) kitchen and reside in 1 (one) house.

Second Section
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is based on:
a. respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. best interests of victims; and
e. empowerment.

Article 3

This Regional Regulation aims to:
a. prevent all forms of violence against women and children;
b. protect and provide security for women and children;
c. provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence;
d. provide protection to reporters and witnesses; and
e. empower women and fulfill the rights of child victims of violence.

CHAPTER II
FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 4

Forms of violence include:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. exploitation; and/or
f. other violence.

CHAPTER III
RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 5

Implementation of protection for victims of violence is the joint responsibility of:
a. regional government;
b. community;
c. family; and
d. parents.

Article 6

(1) The Regional Government is responsible for implementing efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence and protection of victims of violence.
(2) Prevention of the occurrence of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. collecting data and information about women and children and related legislation;
b. conducting education on anti-violence values; and
c. conducting socialization of legislation related to the implementation of prevention and protection of victims of violence.
(3) Protection of victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for victims involving community elements;
b. facilitating assistance, legal aid and legal services according to victims' needs;
c. providing housing both safe houses and alternative housing along with handling mechanisms, psychosocial and spiritual services;
d. conducting continuous handling up to the rehabilitation and social reintegration stage;
e. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for victims of violence; and
f. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims of violence.
(4) In order to carry out the responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government establishes programs and action activities for protection of women and children in one Regional Action Plan for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence guided by the Regional Action Plan for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence of East Java Province.
(5) The Regional Action Plan for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (2) is established for a period of 3 (three) years by Regent's Decree.

Article 7

The responsibility of the community as referred to in Article 5 letter b is implemented in the form of:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children; and
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties.

Article 8

Family and/or parents as referred to in Article 5 letters c and d have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

CHAPTER IV
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION
First Section
Prevention

Article 9

(1) Efforts to prevent violence against women and children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government and coordinated by regional apparatus organizations that administer affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:
a. conducting guidance towards harmonious household integrity;
b. forming work networks in violence prevention efforts;
c. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
d. forming a violence prevention system;
e. conducting socialization regarding legislation related to protection of women and children victims of violence; and
f. providing critical education regarding the rights of women and children to the community.

Article 10

In addition to the Regional Government, prevention efforts as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) are carried out by:
a. family and/or closest relatives;
b. community;
c. educational institutions; and/or
d. non-governmental organizations.

Second Section
Services

Article 11

Implementation of services to victims is carried out with:
a. speed;
b. safety and comfort;
c. empathy;
d. non-discrimination;
e. easy accessibility;
f. no charge; and
g. guaranteed confidentiality.

Article 12

Forms of services to victims include:
a. complaint, consultation, and counseling services;
b. assistance services;
c. health services;
d. social rehabilitation services;
e. legal services;
f. social reintegration services; and
g. economic independence services.

Third Section
Empowerment of Women and Fulfillment of Rights of Child Victims of Violence
Paragraph 1
Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

Article 13

Forms of empowerment of women victims of violence include:
a. job training;
b. productive economic business and/or joint business groups; and
c. capital assistance.

Article 14

Job training as referred to in Article 13 letter a includes:
a. skills training;
b. field work practice; and
c. apprenticeship.

Article 15

Productive economic business and/or joint business groups as referred to in Article 23 letter b include:
a. entrepreneurial skills training;
b. facilitation of joint business group formation; and
c. business implementation assistance.

Article 16

Capital assistance as referred to in Article 13 letter c includes:
a. work facilities and infrastructure assistance; and
b. facilitation of working capital assistance.

Paragraph 2
Fulfillment of Rights of Child Victims of Violence

Article 17

(1) The Regional Government, UPT and community are obliged to fulfill the rights of child victims of violence.
(2) Forms of fulfillment of rights of child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) are the fulfillment of children's basic rights according to their needs.

CHAPTER V
UPT/P2TP2A
First Section
UPT

Article 18

(1) Implementation of women's empowerment and protection of child victims of violence is realized among others through the establishment of integrated, one-stop and networked UPT established by the government, Regional Government, community, non-governmental organizations, donor institutions or other institutions.
(2) UPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is P2TP2A or similar institutions that have the same functions and roles.

Second Section
P2TP2A
Paragraph 1
Establishment

Article 19

(1) P2TP2A is established in the Region.
(2) The institutional structure of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is established by Regent's Decree.
(3) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is under the coordination of Regional Work Units or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection.
(4) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) does not have authority in terms of policy formulation related to women's empowerment and child protection.

Paragraph 2
Coordination Mechanism

Article 20

(1) Implementation of P2TP2A functions as referred to in Article 19 is carried out through coordination with regional apparatus organizations or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection.
(2) Coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in the form of:
a. coordination meetings with work networks;
b. consultation;
c. submission of data and information; and/or
d. follow-up on case handling.

CHAPTER VI
VICTIMS' RIGHTS

Article 21

(1) In the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence, every victim receives the following rights:
a. right to have their dignity respected as human beings;
b. right to health and psychological recovery from suffering experienced by victims;
c. right to determine their own decisions;
d. right to obtain information;
e. right to confidentiality of identity;
f. right to social rehabilitation;
g. right to complaint handling;
h. right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or
i. right to assistance.
(2) Children who become victims of violence in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in paragraph (1) also receive special rights, as follows:
a. right to respect for survival, growth and development;
b. right to basic services;
c. right to receive spiritual guidance;
d. right to equal protection;
e. right to be free from stigma; and/or
f. right to obtain freedom.

CHAPTER VII
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 22

(1) The community participates in the implementation of protection for victims.
(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. forming family partners at the village/sub-district level;
b. forming women and child protection units within community organizations;
c. conducting independent socialization of women's and children's rights;
d. providing first aid to victims; and
e. reporting to authorized agencies if violence against victims occurs in their environment.

CHAPTER VIII
MONITORING AND REPORTING

Article 23

(1) Monitoring of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out regularly and in an integrated manner every 6 (six) months by regional apparatus organizations that administer affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
(2) Reporting on the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out 1 (one) time per year and coordinated by regional apparatus organizations that administer affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

CHAPTER IX
FUNDING

Article 24

(1) Funding for the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget;
b. other non-binding funding sources based on provisions of legislation.
(2) Budget management for the implementation of protection for victims of violence is carried out with principles of fairness, efficiency, transparency and accountability.

CHAPTER X
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 25

P2TP2A as referred to in Article 19 shall be established no later than 1 (one) year from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

Article 26

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.
In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placement in the Regional Gazette of Magetan Regency.

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that women and children need to be protected in their dignity and honor and guaranteed their right to life in accordance with their nature and character without discrimination.

Violence against women and children not only causes physical impacts but also psychological impacts that generally can affect the quality of life of women and children victims of violence.

On a national scale, the Government of Indonesia has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 Year 1948 concerning Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women). In addition, the Government has also enacted Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 Year 2014 concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 297, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5606) and Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419).

Furthermore, based on Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 Year 2014 and Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government as most recently amended several times by Law Number 9 Year 2015, women's empowerment and child protection are governmental affairs that must be implemented by Regional Government.

As occurs in various regions, including Magetan Regency, there is currently a phenomenon of increasing acts of violence against women and children. To address this, regional regulations are needed that provide a basis for Regional Government and/or the community in realizing protection, so that victims are prevented and freed from violence or threats of violence, torture, or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Magetan Regency so they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, protection must be provided to women and children victims of violence in the form of Regional Regulations. Therefore, policies are needed that can guarantee protection to free them from acts of violence, as well as improve their quality of life. Based on the above considerations, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation of Magetan Regency concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

Article 2
Letter a
What is meant by "respect and fulfillment of victims' rights" is the guarantee of fulfillment of victims' basic rights.

Letter b
What is meant by "justice and gender equality" is a condition where women are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to gain access and welfare.
What is meant by "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering work opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

Letter c
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is attitudes and treatment toward victims without making distinctions based on age, sex, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d
What is meant by "best interests of victims" is all best actions concerning victims carried out by the Regional Government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, so that the best interests of victims must be the primary consideration.

Article 4
Letter a
Physical violence referred to is caused by acts that result in pain, illness, or serious injury and or result in injury, or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or cause death.
Forms of physical violence include slapping, hitting, spitting on, pulling hair, kicking, burning with hot objects, hitting/injuring with weapons.

Letter b
Psychological violence referred to is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
Forms of psychological violence include emotional abuse such as insults, hurtful or degrading comments, isolating the wife from the outside world, threatening or frightening as a means of imposing one's will.

Letter c
Sexual violence referred to is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual relations;
c. forced unnatural or unwanted sexual relations; and/or
d. forced sexual relations with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Letter d
Neglect referred to is caused by:
a. acts that result in the non-fulfillment of children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should, carried out by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Letter e
Exploitation referred to is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the victim's consent including prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

Letter f
Other violence referred to is caused by:
a. threats of violence include: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and/or
b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is made to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

Article 12
Letter a
Complaint, consultation, and counseling services include:
a. initial identification or recording of victims; and
b. informed consent, namely medical action consent given by the victim or their closest family after receiving complete explanation regarding medical action to be performed on the victim.

Letter b
Assistance services include:
a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;
b. accompanying victims during the medicolegal process, namely medical services to provide optimal professional assistance in utilizing medical science for law enforcement and justice purposes. Medicolegal services include among others: visum et repertum and visum et psychiatrikum;
c. accompanying victims during the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;
d. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;
e. maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media;
f. coordinating with other companions; and
g. providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

Letter c
Health services include:
a. first aid to victims;
b. care and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel; and
c. referral to health services.

Letter d
Social rehabilitation services are services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims.
Forms of social rehabilitation services through provision of safe houses and social support.
Social support is carried out through:
a. spiritual guidance to victims; and
b. psychological recovery of victims.
Safe House (Shelter) is temporary housing used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.

Letter e
Legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by:
a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding victims' rights and the judicial process;
b. accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and court examination levels and helping victims to fully describe the violence they experienced; and
c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

Letter f
Social reintegration services aim to return victims to their families and social environment.
Social reintegration services are carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with:
a. Regency/City Governments within one Provincial Region of East Java;
b. related agencies and institutions both government and non-government; and
c. village community institutions and/or local community leaders.

Letter g
Economic independence services through providing economic access are given to victims of violence, especially women victims of violence so they can be independent in running and maintaining their lives. Such economic services can be carried out by providing training that matches the skills or talents possessed by victims, so that victims can develop the skills or talents they possess when victims return to community life. In addition, PPT can provide business information and job vacancies related to the skills or abilities possessed by victims of violence.
