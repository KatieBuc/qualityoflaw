b. that violence against women and children in the region continues to increase, so protection efforts are needed;

c. that laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts in the regions so that institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c above, it is necessary to regulate and stipulate by Regional Regulation of Kotawaringin Timur Regency concerning Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1
In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is Kotawaringin Timur Regency
2. Regional Government is the Regional Government of Kotawaringin Timur Regency
3. Regent is the Regent of Kotawaringin Timur
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Kotawaringin Timur Regency
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.
6. Woman is a person of female gender.
7. Violence is any act that results in or can result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.
8. Violence against women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.
10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring in the territory of Kotawaringin Timur Regency.
11. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing security carried out by the Regional Government, police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear about what will or has occurred in violence against women.
12. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
13. Service is action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing about, during or after the occurrence of violence against the victim.
14. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has expertise in accompanying victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
15. Integrated Service Center for Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for victims of violence.
16. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the accompaniment process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and danger from perpetrators.
17. Standard Operating Procedure hereinafter abbreviated as SOP is standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from complaint/identification, rehabilitation, health, social rehabilitation, legal services up to repatriation and social reintegration of witnesses and/or victims.
18. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
19. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.
20. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of Kotawaringin Timur Regency
21. Coordination Forum for Protection of Victims of Violence hereinafter abbreviated as FKPKK is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner.
22. Integrated Service Center for Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as PPTPPA is a community-based institution whose members are multistakeholder observers of women and children at government and non-government levels.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2
The principles of implementing protection for Victims are:
a. Humanity;
b. Respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;
c. Justice and gender equality;
d. Non-discrimination;
e. Best interests of the victim; and
f. Empowerment.

Article 3
The objectives of Victim Protection are:
a. To prevent violence against women and children;
b. To provide services to Victims; and
c. To carry out empowerment and rehabilitation for victims of violence.

Article 4
The scope of protection for Victims includes prevention, service and rehabilitation empowerment efforts for victims of violence in Kotawaringin Timur Regency.

CHAPTER III
VIOLENCE

Article 5
Forms of violence include:
a. Physical violence;
b. Psychological violence;
c. Sexual violence;
d. Neglect;
e. Exploitation; and/or
f. other violence.

Article 6
Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter (a) is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Article 7
Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter (b) is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or suffering to a person.

Article 8
Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter (c) is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Article 9
Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should be carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.
e. Neglect in the household.

Article 10
Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or obscenity activities

Article 11
Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:
a. threats of violence include: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and
b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

CHAPTER IV
VICTIMS' RIGHTS

Article 12
Every Victim receives the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as a human being;
b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
c. the right to determine their own decisions;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidentiality of identity;
f. the right to compensation;
g. the right to social rehabilitation;
h. the right to complaint handling;
i. the right to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and/or
j. the right to accompaniment.

Article 13
Child victims of violence in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 12, also receive special rights, as follows:
a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic services;
c. the right to obtain proper education;
d. the right to equal protection;
e. the right to be free from various stigma; and/or
f. the right to obtain freedom in accordance with applicable norms.

CHAPTER V
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One
Obligations of Regional Government

Article 14
(1) The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to implement victim protection efforts, in the form of:
a. Establishing policies;
b. Forming FKPKK;
c. Preparing program and activity planning;
d. Providing support for facilities and infrastructure;
e. Allocating budget; and
f. conducting guidance and supervision of protection of women and children through related agencies.

(2) In implementing the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government prepares a Regional Action Plan for victim protection.

Article 15
The Regional Government organizes services for victims in the form of:
a. Facilitating facilities and infrastructure for P2TP2A according to capacity; and
b. Facilitating FPKK as a network forum for handling victims.

Article 16
(1) The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to:
a. Supervise the implementation of services to victims, with minimum service standards; and
b. Provide funds for victim protection through APBD in the form of grants to P2TP2A.

(2) Funding for victim protection activities carried out by the Regional Government is charged to the APBD and/or other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 17
In implementing the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 14, Article 15, and Article 16, the Regent grants authority to agencies that have duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Part Two
Obligations of Society and Family

Article 18
Society and family have the following obligations and responsibilities:
a. Preventing violence against women and children;
b. Reporting if violence occurs;
c. Protecting victims; and
d. Providing emergency assistance.

CHAPTER VI
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 19
(1) The community participates in the implementation of protection for Victims.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. Forming family partners at the village/kelurahan level by the community;
b. Forming women and child protection units within community organizations;
c. Conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to protection of women and children victims of violence independently;
d. Providing first aid to victims; and
e. Reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.

(3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector, and mass media.

CHAPTER VII
INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER FOR PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN (P2TP2A)

Article 20
(1) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is a functional work unit that has the main tasks and functions of providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is tasked with providing services and temporary protection in the form of safe houses for victims of violence.

(3) In the event that P2TP2A does not have a safe house (shelter) as referred to in paragraph (3), then victims of violence are referred to PPT that has a safe house.

Article 21
(1) The organizational structure of P2TP2A formed by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (1) consists of:
a. General Chairperson or other equivalent designation;
b. Secretary;
c. Treasurer; and
d. Executive Chairperson overseeing fields.

(2) The fields as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, at a minimum include:
a. Complaint service field;
b. Health service field;
c. Social rehabilitation service field;
d. Repatriation and social reintegration field; and
e. Legal aid service field.

Article 22
The complaint service field as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter a has the task of:
a. Conducting interviews and observation of the victim's condition;
b. Making recommendations for follow-up services;
c. Coordinating and referring to follow-up services and related parties; and
d. Administering the complaint process.

Article 23
The health service field as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter b has the task of:
a. Conducting examination, treatment, and follow-up care for victims;
b. Coordinating the implementation of health rehabilitation and medico-legal;
c. Conducting medico-legal examinations including collection of evidence from victims and preparation of visum et repertum;
d. Conducting supporting examinations and laboratory tests on evidence;
e. Consulting with expert doctors or making referrals; and
f. Making case reports.

Article 24
The social rehabilitation service field as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter c has the task of:
a. Providing accompaniment during the case handling process; and
b. Conducting counseling.

Article 25
The repatriation and social reintegration field as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter d has the task of:
a. Coordinating with related agencies for victim repatriation;
b. Making progress reports on the accompaniment process for repatriation and social rehabilitation; and
c. Conducting monitoring for at least three months after the victim is returned to their family.

Article 26
The legal aid service field as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter e has the task of:
a. Accompanying and defending every legal service process; and
b. Making progress reports on legal handling.

Article 27
(1) P2TP2A formed by the Regional Government has at least three counselors.

(2) Counselors as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. Counselor in the legal field;
b. Counselor in the health field; and
c. Counselor in the psychology field.

CHAPTER VIII
VICTIM PROTECTION

Part One
Prevention

Article 28
(1) Efforts to prevent violence against women and children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by agencies that have main tasks and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:
a. Forming a work network in violence prevention efforts;
b. Coordinating, integrating, synchronizing violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
c. Forming a violence prevention system;
d. Socializing laws and regulations related to protection of women and children victims of violence; and
e. Providing critical education about the rights of women and children to the community.
f. Implementing curfews for children which will be further regulated by Regent Regulation of Kotawaringin Timur.

Article 29
In addition to prevention efforts carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 28, prevention efforts must also be carried out by:
a. Family and/or closest relatives;
b. Community; and
c. Educational institutions.

Part Two
Service

Article 30
(1) Implementation of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) can receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.

Article 31
Implementation of services to victims is carried out with:
a. Speed;
b. Safety and comfort;
c. Sense of empathy;
d. non-discrimination;
e. Easy accessibility;
f. No charge; and
g. Guaranteed confidentiality.

Article 32
Forms of services to victims include:
a. Complaint, consultation, and counseling services;
b. Accompaniment services;
c. Health services;
d. Social rehabilitation services;
e. Legal services; and
f. Repatriation and social reintegration services.

Article 33
Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Article 32 letter a include:
a. Initial identification or recording of victims; and
b. Consent for action (informed consent).

Article 34
Accompaniment services as referred to in Article 32 letter b include:
a. Accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;
b. Accompanying victims during the medicolegal process;
c. Accompanying victims during the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and court;
d. Monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;
e. Maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media;
f. Coordinating with other companions; and
g. Providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

Article 35
Health services as referred to in Article 32 letter c include:
a. First aid to victims;
b. Care and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel; and
c. Referral to health services.

Article 36
Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 32 letter d are services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength, and independence in solving their problems, by:
a. Providing spiritual guidance to victims; and
b. Psychological recovery of victims.

Article 37
Legal services as referred to in Article 34 letter e to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by:
a. Providing legal consultation that includes information about victims' rights and the judicial process;
b. Accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and helping victims to fully describe the violence they experienced;
c. Coordinating with law enforcement volunteer companions and social workers so the judicial process proceeds properly.

Article 38
(1) Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 32 letter e aim to return victims to their families and social environment.

(2) Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with related government and non-government agencies and institutions.

Part Three
Empowerment

Paragraph 1
Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

Article 39
Forms of empowerment of women victims of violence include:
a. Job training;
b. Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
c. Capital assistance.

Article 40
Job training as referred to in Article 39 letter a includes:
a. skills training;
b. field work practice; and
c. apprenticeship.

Article 41
Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups as referred to in Article 39 letter b include:
a. Entrepreneurial skills training;
b. Facilitation of joint business group formation; and
c. Business implementation accompaniment.

Article 42
Capital assistance as referred to in Article 39 letter c includes:
a. Assistance for work facilities and infrastructure; and
b. Facilitation of working capital assistance.

Paragraph 2
Fulfillment of Rights of Child Victims of Violence

Article 43
(1) The Regional Government, P2TP2A, and the community are obliged to fulfill the rights of child victims of violence.

(2) Forms of fulfillment of the rights of child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) include fulfillment of children's basic rights according to their needs.

Part Four
Coordination of Victim Protection

Article 44
(1) In an effort to provide and organize service handling for victims, the Regional Government forms FPKK.

(2) FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) aims to:
a. Coordinate and synchronize P2TP2A service handling;
b. Maintain and develop networks and referral systems; and
c. Collect, compile and present violence reports.

(3) The management and membership of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Regent Decree.

(4) FPKK membership as referred to in paragraph (3) is grouped into the following roles:
a. Health role;
b. Psychology role;
c. Legal role;
d. Social role; and
e. Economic role.

CHAPTER IX
INFORMATION/REPORTING SYSTEM

Article 45
(1) The Regent implements an information/reporting system for protection of women and children victims of violence.

(2) The information/reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) is organized in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

(3) Submitted periodically, monthly reports, quarterly and annual reports.

CHAPTER X
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 46
(1) The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of minimum service standards for P2TP2A.

(2) Guidance and Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. Coordination;
b. Guidance;
c. Education and training; and
d. Monitoring and evaluation.

(3) Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a covers aspects related to planning and implementation.

(4) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b covers aspects related to planning, implementation, procedures, funding, quality, control and supervision.

(5) Education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is carried out in accordance with laws and regulations.

(6) Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d is carried out periodically.

CHAPTER XI
OBLIGATIONS

Article 47
(1) The Regional Government and Community establish and guarantee the operation of P2TP2A integrated service institutions to protect victims against women and children.

(2) Facilitate the formation of service institutions.

(3) The Regional Government encourages the community about the importance of services for women and children.

(4) Provide funds for protection of women and children through the APBD of Kotawaringin Timur Regency according to capacity.

(5) Every person/government organizer is obliged to provide protection for women and children victims of violence.

CHAPTER XII
SANCTIONS

Article 48
(1) If government organizers and/or the community do not implement the provisions as in Article 47, sanctions will be imposed in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

(2) Sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of oral and written warnings three (3) times.

(3) Violations as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) are in the form of imprisonment for 3 (three) months and/or fines in accordance with applicable regulations.

CHAPTER VIII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 49
This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation. So that everyone knows, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette.

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Kotawaringin Timur Regency so they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity, protection needs to be provided for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts in the Region so that institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, service and empowerment for women and children victims of violence in Kotawaringin Timur Regency.

Critical education for women victims of violence is an effort to build women's awareness about their rights. The purpose of implementing critical education is to help make decisions and not become repeat victims of violence. Forms of critical education for women victims of violence consist of: training on gender equality and justice, education on reproductive health for women, and providing understanding of laws and regulations on Elimination of Domestic Violence.

What is meant by "other service units" is a unit that organizes integrated service functions for women and children victims of violence. Letter b
What is meant by "medicolegal" is medical services to provide optimal professional assistance in utilizing medical science for the purposes of law enforcement and justice. Medicolegal services include: visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum.

What is meant by "visum et repertum" is a written statement made by a doctor in forensic medicine at the request of the authorized investigator regarding the results of medical examination of the victim based on their expertise and under oath, for the purposes of judicial proceedings.

What is meant by "visum et psikiatrikum" is a statement given by a Psychiatric Doctor about the mental health condition of the victim needed to clarify a case and for judicial proceedings.
