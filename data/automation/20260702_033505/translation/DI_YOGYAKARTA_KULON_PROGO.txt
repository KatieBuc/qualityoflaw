is an act that violates the rights and
dignity of women and children as human beings;

b. that protection efforts for women and child
victims of violence have not been optimal,
thus requiring strengthening of regulations
and institutions that can guarantee its implementation;

c. that based on the considerations as
referred to in letters a and b, it is
necessary to establish a Regional Regulation
concerning Protection of Women and
Children Victims of Violence;

Pasal 3

This Regional Regulation aims to:
a. maintain the integrity of the household so
that a harmonious family is realized;

b. prevent violence against women and
children;

c. protect and provide security for
women and children;

d. provide services to women and
children victims of violence;

e. provide protection to reporters and
witnesses; and

f. conduct empowerment for women
victims of violence.

Pasal 4

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:
a. prevention;
b. services and companionship; and
c. empowerment.

Pasal 2

Letter a
What is meant by "respect and
fulfillment of victims' rights"
is the guarantee of fulfillment of victims' basic rights.

Letter b
What is meant by "justice" and gender
equality is a condition where women
are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities
to gain access, as well as welfare;
What is meant by "gender equality" is
equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and
decision-making between women and
men including in entering employment
opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

Letter c
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is
an attitude and treatment toward victims without
making distinctions based on age, gender,
race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d
What is meant by "best interests of
victims" is all best actions
concerning victims carried out by
the Regional Government, the community, the legislative body
and the judicial body, so that the best
interests of victims must be the primary
consideration.

Letter b
What is meant by "medicolegal" is
medical services to provide
professional and optimal assistance in
utilizing medical science for
the purposes of law enforcement and justice.
Medicolegal services include, among others:
visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum.
What is meant by "visum et repertum"
is a written statement made by
a doctor in forensic medical science at the
request of an authorized
investigator regarding the results of medical examination of
victims based on their scientific expertise and
under oath, for the purposes of the
judicial process.
What is meant by "visum et psikiatrikum"
is a statement given by a
Psychiatrist regarding the mental health
condition of victims needed to clarify
a case and for the purposes of
the judicial process.
