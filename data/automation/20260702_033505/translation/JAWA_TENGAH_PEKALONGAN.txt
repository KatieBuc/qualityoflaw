CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Pekalongan Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
3. Agency for Community Empowerment, Women and Family Planning hereinafter referred to as BPMPKB is the Agency for Community Empowerment, Women and Family Planning of Pekalongan Regency as the agency for formulating and implementing regional policies that are specific in the field of community empowerment, women and family planning.

4. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child still in the womb.

5. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.

6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

7. Protection of Gender is all efforts aimed at providing security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, regional government, or other parties who know or hear that violence against men or women will or has occurred either temporarily or based on court determination.

8. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and honor and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

9. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.

10. Violence against children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.

11. Victims of gender-based violence are people who because of their gender experience physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.

12. Integrated Services is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unit of implementation, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

13. Companion is a person or institution and/or body that has expertise in providing assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

14. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a unified unit that organizes integrated services for women and child victims of violence.

15. Medical Services is a facility that provides services that are clinical in nature in the field of diagnostics, and/or inpatient care for victims.

16. Social rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial condition disorders and restoration of normal social functioning both in the family and in society.

17. Legal Assistance is the provision of legal assistance to victims seeking justice who are unable and face difficulties in the legal field outside or before the court without compensation.

18. Repatriation is the act of returning victims to their region of origin or country of origin while prioritizing protection services and fulfillment of their needs.

19. Social Reintegration is the reunification of victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.

20. Safe House is a shelter for victims during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers from perpetrators.

21. Service Standards are benchmarks used as guidelines for service delivery and reference for assessing service quality as obligations and promises of organizers to the community in the context of quality, appropriate, easy, affordable and measurable services.

22. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.

23. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, foster care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.

24. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of Pekalongan Regency.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:
a. non-discrimination;
b. best interests of victims;
c. justice and gender equality;
d. victim protection;
e. maternal survival;
f. child survival, growth, and development;
g. respect for children's opinions;
h. openness;
i. integration;
j. not blaming victims;
k. empowering;
l. victim confidentiality;
m. decision-making in the hands of victims.

Article 3

The purpose of implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence is to provide services and protection for male and female victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence that occurs within the household and/or outside the household.

CHAPTER III
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 4

(1) The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to implement efforts to prevent violence in the form of:
a. providing data and information about gender and child victims of violence in accordance with laws and regulations;
b. conducting education on anti-violence values based on gender and violence against children; and
c. socializing laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection against gender-based violence and child victims of violence.

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

(2) To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government is obligated to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
a. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving elements of SKPD, Agencies, Institutions, Community Organizations, Community;
b. providing facilities and infrastructure;
c. increasing the capacity of service provider institutions;
d. conducting coordination and cooperation in implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children;
e. conducting monitoring and evaluation.
f. encouraging public awareness of the importance of victim protection.

(3) The Regional Government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for victims.

CHAPTER IV
FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

(1) Forms of Gender-Based Violence include:
a. Physical Violence;
b. Psychological Violence;
c. Sexual Violence;

(2) Forms of Violence against Children include:
a. Physical Violence;
b. Psychological Violence;
c. Sexual Violence;
d. Neglect;
e. Exploitation;
f. Maltreatment

Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Article 7

Psychological Violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to victims.

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment both physical and psychological;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should, carried out by parents, guardians, any other party responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to agreement or contract they are obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that victims are under the control of that person.

Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the victim's consent that include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or abilities of a person by other parties to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities or sexual abuse.

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

Article 11

Maltreatment as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter f is caused by:
a. all acts or actions whether intentional or unintentional carried out by others that make individuals sick or disturbed in their feelings, or receive unpleasant feelings that make someone sad, disappointed, annoyed, angry and afraid.
b. all sexual violations committed or permitted to be committed against young people by adults or others who are legally responsible for them, including touching children with the intention of sexual satisfaction or forcing children to touch an adult, sexual intercourse, showing sexual activities to children, pornography or allowing children to engage in sexual intercourse that is not appropriate for their development.

CHAPTER V
VICTIMS' RIGHTS

Article 12

Every victim of gender-based violence and children receives the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as human beings;
b. the right to determine their own decisions;
c. the right to obtain information;
d. the right to confidentiality of their identity;
e. the right to complaint handling;
f. the right to health and psychological recovery from suffering experienced by victims;
g. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process;
h. the right to obtain repatriation efforts and social reintegration of victims of gender-based violence and children.
i. the right to social rehabilitation.

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Part One
Institutional

Article 13

In carrying out the obligation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT) institution.

Part Two
Integrated Service Center

Article 14

(1) PPT as referred to in Article 13 is established by the Regional Government by involving Regional Work Units, institutions, agencies and community organizations that have competence in implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children.

(2) PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is a functional work unit that has the main duties and functions of providing protection to victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence in the form of seeking prevention, recovery, and social reintegration, providing legal protection, conducting coordination and, seeking increased community participation, as well as monitoring and reporting.

Article 15

(1) The organizational structure of PPT as referred to in Article 14 consists of:
a. Chairperson;
b. Vice Chairperson;
c. Secretary; and
d. Members.

(2) Fields in the PPT organizational structure include at least:
a. complaint service field;
b. health service field;
c. legal assistance and law enforcement service field;
d. social rehabilitation service field; and
e. repatriation and social reintegration field.

Article 16

The complaint service field as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) letter a has the tasks of:
a. conducting interviews and observation of victim conditions;

CHAPTER VII
VICTIM EMPOWERMENT

Article 22

Empowerment of Gender and Child Victims of Violence is implemented in the form of:
a. Empowerment of victims of gender-based violence includes job training, productive economic enterprises and joint business groups with capital assistance.
b. Empowerment of child victims of violence who are not in school is to ensure child victims of violence receive the right to continue education, and/or receive job training with capital assistance.

CHAPTER VIII
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 23

(1) The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children.
(2) Implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out daily by BPMPKB.

CHAPTER IX
FUNDING

Article 24

Funding for activities of implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out by the Regional Government is sourced from APBD, Provincial APBD and/or APBN as well as other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER X
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 25

(1) The community participates in implementing protection for victims.
(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. providing protection for victims;
b. providing emergency assistance;
c. providing advocacy to victims and/or the community regarding handling cases of gender-based violence and children;
d. assisting the process of submitting applications for protection determination;
e. conveying information to authorized officials related to cases of gender-based violence and children;
f. organizing strengthening of community groups in handling gender-based violence and children; and
g. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and children.

CHAPTER XI
TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Article 26

If there is a change in the Regional Apparatus Organization structure, then the coordination task of guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children as stated in Article 23 paragraph (2) is carried out by the Regional Apparatus in charge of Women's and Children's Empowerment.

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.

In order to prevent and address gender-based violence and children in Pekalongan Regency so they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, protection must be provided for gender and child victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

So far, laws and regulations governing protection of gender and child victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the regions, so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, especially in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of gender and child victims of violence in Pekalongan Regency.

Article 2
Letter a
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.

Letter b
What is meant by "best interests of victims" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, the best interests of victims must be the primary consideration.

Letter c
What is meant by "gender justice" is fair treatment given to women and men.
What is meant by "gender equality" is a condition and position that describes harmonious, balanced and equal relations between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances to access, participate, control and benefit in development implementation and enjoy development results in family life, as well as in society, nation and state.

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

Letter d
What is meant by "victim protection" is providing security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

Letter e
What is meant by "maternal survival" is ensuring that a mother does not experience death that occurs during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum, whether caused by physical or non-physical conditions.

Letter f.
What is meant by "child growth and development" is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.
What is meant by "child survival" is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity and the right to enjoy the highest attainable health status.

Letter g.
What is meant by "respect for children's opinions" is respect for children's rights to participate and express opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.

Letter h
What is meant by "openness" is that in implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is transparent among integrated service providers.

Letter i
What is meant by "integration" is that in implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is carried out by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal assistants, psychologists, clergy, social workers, police.

Letter j
What is meant by "not blaming victims" is an attitude and treatment of not blaming victims for the occurrence of violence they experienced.

Letter k
What is meant by "empowering" is that every effort provided must be able to strengthen victims, both physically, psychologically, socially and economically.

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

Letter l
What is meant by "victim confidentiality" is every action taken to ensure victims are in a safe condition from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychology of victims.

Letter m
What is meant by "decision-making in the hands of victims" is the right of victims to determine the best choice in resolving their problems.
