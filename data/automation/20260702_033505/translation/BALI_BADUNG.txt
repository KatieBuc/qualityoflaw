CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1
In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is Badung Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Badung.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Badung Regency.
5. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to Victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on a court protection order.
6. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person who is recognized by law as a Woman.
7. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a Child who is still in the womb.
8. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.
9. Violence against Women is any act based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or hardship for Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
10. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child.
11. Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences suffering and/or hardship as a result of Violence and/or threat of Violence.
12. Victim of Violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering either physically, mentally or emotionally, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of Violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.
13. Health Rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of Victims including physical, psychological, and sexual health so that Victims can carry out their roles again properly and naturally both in the family and in society.
14. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person who experiences social dysfunction so that they can carry out social functions normally.
15. Repatriation is an effort to return Victims of Violence from the Region to their region of origin.
16. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide Protection and fulfillment of needs for Victims.
17. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for witnesses and/or Victims of acts of Violence.
18. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to carry out the functions and main tasks of integrated services for Women and Children Victims of Violence, including complaint services, Health Rehabilitation, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, Social Reintegration, and Legal Assistance.
19. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing services for handling reports/complaints, health services, Social Rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance, as well as Repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2
Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of Victims;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. best interests of Victims; and
e. legal certainty.

Article 3
Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to:
a. prevent all forms of Violence against Women and Children;
b. protect Women and provide services to Women and Children Victims of Violence; and
c. empower Women and Children Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER III
RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 4
Every Woman and Child Victim of Violence has the right:
a. to have their dignity respected as human beings;
b. to receive Protection from family, community, Regional Government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court;
c. to restoration of physical, psychological and sexual health according to the suffering experienced by Victims of Violence;
d. to special handling related to the confidentiality of Victims;
e. to accompaniment by social workers and legal assistance at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
f. to spiritual guidance services; and
g. to determine their own decisions.

CHAPTER IV
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 5
(1) The Regional Government is obligated and responsible for providing Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence.
(2) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. implementing policies for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence based on laws and regulations;
b. establishing policies, programs and activities for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
c. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
d. allocating budget in the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
e. fostering and supervising the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

Article 6
(1) The community is obligated and responsible for providing Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence.
(2) The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out in the form of:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women and Children;
b. providing Protection to Victims;
c. providing emergency assistance;
d. providing information and/or reporting acts of Violence against women and Children to the authorities; and
e. participating in handling Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER V
INSTITUTIONS

Article 7
(1) In organizing Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region, the Regent may establish a PPT.
(2) The establishment and development of PPT is adjusted to the development of priority needs, and the financial capacity of the Region as well as the institutional and personnel capacity available in the Region.
(3) PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may accommodate institutions/work units that provide services for Victims of Violence such as P2TP2A, Safe Houses, Child Social Protection Houses, Trauma Center Protection Houses, Women's Social Protection Houses, Shelters and other service activities in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
(4) Forms of services implemented by PPT include:
a. complaints;
b. Health rehabilitation;
c. Social rehabilitation;
d. legal assistance;
e. repatriation; and
f. social reintegration.
(5) In providing Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence, PPT is obligated to:
a. provide services as quickly as possible and free of charge to Victims;
b. organize Protection and fulfillment of victims' rights to Health Rehabilitation, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, social reintegration and legal assistance;
c. cooperate with certain institutions in providing translators and volunteer companions needed for Victims;
d. network with government or private hospitals for treatment and health recovery of Victims and cooperate with witness and Victim protection institutions, social protection houses or government-owned trauma centers, community, or other institutions for health recovery of Victims;
e. provide convenience, comfort, and safety for Victims;
f. maintain confidentiality of Victims;
g. provide fulfillment of legal assistance for Victims;
h. in the event that integrated service delivery is carried out in a networked manner, PPT remains responsible for the entire referral service process required by Victims.
(6) The provision of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (3) is implemented in accordance with SPM.

Article 8
(1) Community Institutions may establish PPT.
(2) In establishing PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), it is guided by the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI
MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

Article 9
SPM for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence includes services for:
a. handling complaints/reports of Victims of Violence against Women and Children;
b. health services for Women and Children Victims of Violence;
c. Social rehabilitation for Women and Children Victims of Violence;
d. law enforcement and legal assistance for Women and Children Victims of Violence; and
e. repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

Article 10
SPM as referred to in Article 9 has performance indicators including:
a. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers within integrated service units;
b. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive health services by trained health workers at Community Health Centers and Hospitals;
c. coverage of Social Rehabilitation services provided by trained Social Rehabilitation officers for Women and Children Victims of Violence within integrated service units;
d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for Women and Children Victims of Violence within integrated service units;
e. coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of Violence against Women and Children;
f. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive legal assistance services;
g. coverage of Repatriation services for Women and Children Victims of Violence; and
h. coverage of social reintegration services for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER VII
MONITORING AND EVALUATION

Article 11
(1) To ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, the Regional Government conducts monitoring.
(2) Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is to determine developments and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region.
(3) Monitoring is conducted periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of Regional Work Units that implement policies, programs, activities for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.
(4) Monitoring is conducted from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence for the current year.

Article 12
(1) Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Protection of Women and Children is conducted at the end of each fiscal year or if necessary according to needs.
(2) The results of evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Protection of Women and Children are used as input for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for Protection of Women and Children for the following year.
(3) Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VIII
REPORTING

Article 13
(1) The Regent is responsible for making reports on the implementation of SPM for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region to the Governor with copies submitted to the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection and the Minister of Home Affairs.
(2) Reporting of implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted annually.

CHAPTER IX
FUNDING

Article 14
Funding for the implementation of Protection for Women and Children from acts of Violence is sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
b. other legitimate and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER X
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 15
(1) The Regent conducts guidance and supervision over the implementation of SPM for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region.
(2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes providing implementation instructions, guidance, supervision, monitoring and evaluation of service implementation for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER XI
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 16
(1) In organizing Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence, the community may:
a. form family partners at the sub-district/village level by the community;
b. conduct socialization of the rights of Women and Children independently;
c. provide first aid to Victims; and
d. report to the competent authorities if Violence against Victims occurs in their environment.
(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, community social institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector, and mass media.

CHAPTER XII
TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Article 17
In the event that PPT as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (1) has not been established, the existing P2TP2A continues to carry out the duties and functions of integrated services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region.

CHAPTER XIII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 18
This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Badung Regency.

I. GENERAL

The state has an obligation to provide Protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. That Women and Children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience Violence and therefore need to receive Protection. Violence against Women and Children constitutes a violation of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed without discrimination.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, Violence against Women is any act based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or hardship for Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child. The existence of Women and Children Victims of Violence has not received adequate services so minimum services are needed for Victims of Violence.

In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in Badung Regency so that they are protected from Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children Victims of Violence, particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of Women and Children Victims of Violence in Badung Regency.
