REGIONAL REGULATION OF MOROWALI REGENCY NUMBER 11 YEAR 2022
CONCERNING
PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE WITH THE BLESSING OF ALMIGHTY GOD
REGENT OF MOROWALI,

Considering: a. that children and women are creations of Almighty God who constitute important elements of society, and in the future children will become actors in all fields of social and state life;

b. that Indonesian women have an important role in community activities, development and family life as well as having the obligation to care for, protect and raise Indonesian children to grow and develop as the nation's next generation;

c. that based on the Provisions in Article 17 paragraph (1) of Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government as amended by Law Number 1 Year 2022 concerning Financial Relations between the Central Government and Regional Governments, Regions have the right to establish policies for the protection of women and children which constitute mandatory affairs in accordance with their authority;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children From Acts of Violence.

Bearing in mind: 1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

2. Law Number 51 Year 1999 concerning the Establishment of Buol Regency, Morowali Regency and Banggai Kepulauan Regency (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 1999 Number 179, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3900) as amended by Law Number 11 Year 2000 concerning Amendment to Law Number 51 year 1999 concerning the Establishment of Buol Regency, Morowali Regency and Banggai Kepulauan Regency (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2000 Number 78, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3966);

3. Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587), as last amended by Law Number 1 Year 2022 concerning Financial Relations Between the Central Government and Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2022 Number 4, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6757);

With Mutual Approval
REGIONAL PEOPLE'S REPRESENTATIVE COUNCIL OF MOROWALI REGENCY

REGENT OF MOROWALI,

HAS DECIDED:

To Establish: REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including a child in the womb.

2. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.

3. Protection is all service actions to fulfill rights, as well as guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence.

4. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.

5. Protection of women is an effort aimed at protecting women and their rights in order to obtain security from acts of violence.

6. Violence is any act that results in or causes suffering and distress whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.

7. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or unconsciousness and/or causes death.

8. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness or severe psychological suffering to a person.

9. Victim of acts of violence hereinafter referred to as victim is an individual or group of people who experience suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by individuals.

10. Regional Technical Service Unit for Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a regional technical implementing unit, established by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection and other problems.

11. Coordinating Forum for Protection of Violence Victims hereinafter abbreviated as FKPKK is a coordinating forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked and collaborative manner.

12. Region is Morowali Regency.

13. Regional Apparatus is an element assisting the Regent in administering Government affairs that are the authority of the Region.

14. Regional Government is the Government of Morowali Regency.

15. Regional Governance is the administration of government affairs by the Government and DPRD according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy in the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.

16. Regent is the Regent of Morowali.

17. Office of Village Community Empowerment, Women's Empowerment and Child Protection hereinafter abbreviated as Dinas PMDP3A is the regional apparatus of Morowali Regency that carries out government affairs in the field of women and children.

Article 2

The scope of regulation for Protection of Women and Children from acts of violence includes:
a. forms of violence;
b. rights of Women and Children from acts of violence;
c. obligations and responsibilities;
d. prevention;
e. services for victims of acts of Violence;
f. empowerment;
g. cooperation;
h. guidance and Supervision;
i. funding; and
j. criminal provisions.

CHAPTER II
FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 3

Forms of Violence include:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence; and/or
d. domestic neglect.

Article 4

Physical violence as referred to in Article 3 letter a results in:
a. the victim experiencing pain or serious injury;
b. death of the victim; and
c. causing illness or hindrance to carrying out work, position or livelihood or daily activities.

Article 5

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 3 letter b results in:
a. Fear;
b. loss of self-confidence;
c. loss of ability to act;
d. feelings of helplessness; and/or
e. severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 6

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 3 letter c includes:
a. acts of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual relations in an unnatural or unwanted manner;
c. forced sexual relations with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes; and
d. sexual violence through social media.

Article 7

Domestic neglect as referred to in Article 3 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of the Child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social carried out by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or treat the Child as they should carried out by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to law they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

CHAPTER III
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 8

Women and Children victims of acts of Violence receive the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as human beings;
b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
c. the right to determine their own decisions;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidentiality of identity;
f. the right to restitution;
g. the right to social rehabilitation;
h. the right to complaint handling;
i. the right to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and/or
j. the right to assistance.

Article 9

(1) In the event that victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 8 require ongoing handling, they have the right to stay in a safe house or alternative residence.

(2) Ongoing handling as referred to in paragraph (1) is handling at the rehabilitation stage carried out either by individuals, groups or Government and non-Government institutions.

Article 10

(1) Safe house as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) provides temporary shelter and/or accommodation for victims of Violence until their rights are restored.

(2) The operation and/or management of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by Dinas PMDP3A.

(3) For the safety and at the request of victims of Violence, the manager or operator of the safe house as referred to in paragraph (2) may place victims of Violence in a certain confidential location.

(4) Further provisions regarding the operation of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

Article 11

Children as victims of acts of Violence in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 8, also receive special rights, as follows:
a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic services;
c. the right to equal protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and/or
e. the right to obtain freedom.

CHAPTER IV
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One
Regional Government

Article 12

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to:
a. implement policies for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence established by the government;
b. establish policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children from acts of violence; and
c. cooperate with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, as well as educational institutions, health, hospitals, and various Non-Governmental Organizations that care about Women and Children in the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

(2) The Regional Government is responsible for:
a. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence; and
b. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence according to the financial capacity of the Region.

(3) In implementing the obligations of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) and implementing the responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government prepares a Regional Action Plan for victim protection.

(4) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3) is part of the Medium-Term Development Plan.

Article 13

The Regent in carrying out obligations as referred to in Article 12 delegates to Dinas PMDP3A and/or related Regional Apparatus according to their authority.

Part Two
Community and Family

Article 14

(1) The community and family have the following obligations and responsibilities:
a. prevent the occurrence of Violence against Women and Children;
b. provide information and/or report acts of Violence against Women and Children to law enforcement or authorized parties;
c. participate in assisting the handling of victims of acts of Violence;
d. protect victims; and
e. provide emergency assistance.

(2) Forms of community and family participation are carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER V
PREVENTION

Article 15

(1) Efforts to prevent Violence against Women and Children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by Dinas PMDP3A;

(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:
a. building networks and collaboration with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, as well as educational institutions, health, hospitals, various Non-Governmental Organizations that care about Women and Children;
b. building community and family participation and concern for the prevention and protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;
c. establishing a violence prevention system that provides a complete and easily accessible information system;
d. conducting socialization about laws and regulations related to the protection of Women and Children victims of Violence;
e. providing critical education about the rights of Women and Children as victims of Violence to the community; and
f. opening complaint posts for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

Article 16

(1) Prevention of acts of Violence against Women and Children as referred to in Article 15, is carried out by Dinas PMDP3A and Regional Apparatus that administer affairs in the fields of:
a. education;
b. manpower;
c. health;
d. social;
e. women's empowerment and child protection;
f. mental and spiritual;
g. peace and order; and
h. communication and information.

(2) Prevention of acts of Violence by Dinas PMDP3A and related Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out in an integrated and continuous manner.

CHAPTER VI
SERVICES FOR VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 17

(1) Forms of services provided to Women and Children victims of acts of Violence, include:
a. complaint services;
b. health services;
c. legal aid;
d. clergy services;
e. repatriation and social reintegration; and
f. rehabilitation.

(2) Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government in cooperation with related Regional Apparatus/institutions are implemented according to minimum service standards established by the Regional Government and implemented by related Regional Apparatus/institutions whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
a. education;
b. manpower;
c. health;
d. social;
e. Women's empowerment and Child protection;
f. mental and spiritual; and
g. peace and order.

(3) In carrying out the duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with related Institutions, other Regional Governments, and community parties.

Article 18

Complaint services as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) letter a include:
a. initial identification or recording of victims; and
b. consent for action to be taken.

Article 19

Health services as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) letter b include:
a. first aid to victims;
b. care and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics; and
c. referral to more adequate Health services if necessary can refer to other parties with a multidisciplinary approach including:
1. health services;
2. medicolegal;
3. psychological assistance.

Article 20

Legal aid as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) letter c to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by:
a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding victim rights and the judicial process;
b. accompanying victims at the reporting, investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and helping victims to fully describe the Violence they experienced; and
c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer assistants, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

Article 21

(1) Report submission services as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) letter d are intended to accompany Women and Children as victims of acts of violence after unsuccessful mediation by UPTD PPA and other Women and Children complaint units.

(2) Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) letter d aim to return victims to be accepted back into their social environment.

(3) Repatriation and social reintegration services for victims are carried out after socialization regarding the rights of Women and Children as victims of violence has been implemented.

Article 22

(1) Rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) letter e include:
a. motivation and psychosocial diagnosis;
b. care and nurturing;
c. vocational training and entrepreneurship development;
d. mental spiritual guidance;
e. physical guidance;
f. social guidance and psychosocial counseling;
g. accessibility services;
h. social assistance and assistance;
i. resocialization guidance;
j. follow-up guidance; and/or
k. referral.

(2) Forms of vocational training and entrepreneurship development as mentioned in paragraph (1) letter c, include:
a. skills training;
b. field work practice;
c. apprenticeship;
d. entrepreneurial skills training;
e. facilitation of joint business group formation;
f. business capital in the form of business support tools; and
g. business implementation assistance.

Article 23

Psychological services as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) letter g are actions taken to restore the psyche of Women and Children as victims of acts of violence.

Article 24

(1) Implementation of services to victims as referred to in Article 17 is carried out with:
a. every day (including holidays);
b. fast;
c. safe and comfortable;
d. sense of empathy;
e. non-discrimination;
f. outreach;
g. not charged; and
h. guaranteed confidentiality.

(2) Service operators or managers are prohibited from collecting any fees from victims of violence.

Article 25

(1) Protection services for Women and Children from acts of violence are carried out in an integrated manner by UPTD PPA as an integrated service center for the protection of Women and Children as victims of acts of violence.

(2) Protection services as referred to in paragraph (1) may be delegated by UPTD PPA to other service units in a networked and collaborative manner.

Article 26

(1) In an effort to provide and organize services for victims, the Regional Government establishes FKPKK.

(2) Further provisions regarding the establishment, main duties, and functions as well as membership of FKPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 27

(1) The community participates in the implementation of protection for victims.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
a. forming family partners at the RT/RW or Village level by the community;
b. forming women and child protection units within community organizations;
c. conducting independent socialization of Women and Children's rights;
d. providing first aid to victims; and
e. reporting to the authorized related Regional Apparatus/institutions if violence against victims occurs in their environment.

(3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, mental and spiritual development institutions, private sector, and mass media.

CHAPTER VII
EMPOWERMENT

Article 28

Forms of empowerment of Women victims of Violence include:
a. job training;
b. productive economic business and joint business families; and
c. capital assistance.

CHAPTER VIII
COOPERATION

Article 29

(1) In order to achieve the goal of protecting the rights of Women from acts of Violence, the Regional Government cooperates with:
a. other Regional Governments; and
b. non-governmental Institutions.

(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. exchange of data and information;
b. rehabilitation of victims of acts of Violence;
c. repatriation and social reintegration; and
d. provision of evidence and witnesses followed up in accordance with applicable law.

CHAPTER IX
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 30

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

(2) Guidance and Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. coordination;
b. guidance;
c. education and training; and
d. monitoring and evaluation.

(3) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (2) in order to realize the goal of protecting Women and Children from acts of Violence according to minimum service standards implemented by Dinas PMDP3A and the community.

(4) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (3) are carried out with professional and transparent principles.

(5) Dinas PMDP3A is obliged to report regularly to the Regent regarding the implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of Violence.

CHAPTER X
FUNDING

Article 31

Costs of activities related to the implementation of protection of Women and Children are charged to:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
b. other legal receipts in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

CHAPTER XI
CRIMINAL PROVISIONS

Article 32

Any person who discloses the whereabouts of victims of violence who are being placed in a confidential location as referred to in Article 10 paragraph (3) shall be punished with imprisonment of a maximum of 6 (six) months or a fine of a maximum of Rp. 50,000,000,- (fifty million rupiah).

Article 33

Service operators or managers proven to collect fees from victims of Violence as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (2) letter g shall be punished with imprisonment of a maximum of 6 (six) months or a fine of a maximum of Rp. 50,000,000,- (fifty million rupiah).

CHAPTER XII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 34

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Morowali Regency.

I. GENERAL

Protection from violence that threatens the safety and health of women and children must be provided from an early age. Protection is needed to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. For example, women and children victims of abuse, neglect, eviction and mistreatment by parents or others. For women and children experiencing these problems, the government must provide Safe Houses and adequate budget, the role of various parties to help handle violence problems faced by children and women including social community organizations, volunteers, social workers, medical personnel, advocates and clergy.

The condition of deterioration of children along with their rights and the condition of violence against children and women that often occurs in the region should be a common concern for all stakeholders including regional governments to play a role in preventing and overcoming such deterioration and violence.

For the regional government of Morowali Regency in facing the universe of conditions above, various actions are taken to fulfill children's rights and prevent and assist in handling acts of violence against children and women which are endeavored by establishing a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children From Acts of Violence.
