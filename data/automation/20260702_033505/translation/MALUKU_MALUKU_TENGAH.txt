PROTECTION OF WOMEN
AND CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE
BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD
REGENT OF CENTRAL MALUKU,

Considering : a. that as citizens, women and children have the right to obtain protection from every act of violence committed against them which may result in victims of physical, psychological, sexual violence, or domestic neglect;

b. that protection for women and child victims of violence is an effort to provide a sense of security and freedom from all acts of violence guaranteed by the State;

c. that the government including Regional Government, families, social organizations and/or other community organizations have the responsibility to prevent acts of violence and protect women and child victims of violence in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations;

d. that the Regional Regulation on the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence requires substantial changes related to institutions to be adjusted to the latest regulations at the National level, and has not comprehensively regulated efforts to implement protection that guarantees the fulfillment of victims' rights to truth, justice, recovery and guarantees of non-recurrence of cases, so a new Regional Regulation is needed that can guarantee its implementation.

e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b, c and d, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is Central Maluku Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent together with related Regional Apparatus Organizations as elements of Regional Government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Central Maluku.
4. Regional People's Representative Council hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of Central Maluku Regency as an element of Regional Government administration.
5. Agency is the Agency that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
6. Technical Implementation Unit for Women's and Children's Protection hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA is an institution established by the provincial or regency/city regional government to provide services to women and children experiencing criminal and civil problems.
7. Integrated Service Center for Women's and Children's Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to provide prevention, protection, handling and fulfillment of the rights of women and child victims of violence by providing complaint services, medical services, social rehabilitation, legal aid, as well as social reintegration and repatriation.
8. Child is a person who has not reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including children still in the womb.
9. Woman is a woman who has reached 18 (eighteen) years of age or more.
10. Violence against women (and children) is any act against a person, especially women (and children) which results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or economic loss.
11. Victim of violence is a woman and/or child who experiences suffering either physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or economic loss as a result of an act of violence.
12. Prevention is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women and/or children carried out by the government, families, social institutions or other community institutions, police, prosecutors or other parties to prevent acts of violence against women and/or children.

13. Campaign for 16 Days of Anti-Violence Against Women (and Children) is a campaign to encourage efforts to eliminate violence against women (and children) throughout the world, which is held annually for 16 days from November 25 to December 10.

14. Protection of women and children is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by the government, families, social institutions or other community institutions, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties either temporarily or based on court decisions or determinations, in connection with acts of violence against women and children.

15. Spiritual guidance service is recovery carried out with a spiritual, belief and religious approach.

16. Complaint service is a series of actions taken by UPT PPA organizers to follow up on reports of acts of violence against victims of violence.

17. Health rehabilitation service is the recovery of victims of violence from health problems they suffer.

18. Medicolegal service is medical service and operational service in the field of medicine and law, including visum examination.

19. Social Rehabilitation Service is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction to be able to carry out social functions properly.

20. Legal Aid Service is legal services provided by legal aid providers free of charge to legal aid recipients.

21. Repatriation service is an effort to return victims of violence to their area of origin or to family, substitute family or community.

22. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims of violence with family, substitute family and community that can provide protection and fulfill the needs of victims.

23. Recovery is medical/psychiatric and psychological action toward victims of violence as an effort to restore the physical and psychological condition of victims to their original state.

24. Companion is a person trusted by the victim to accompany them during the criminal justice process.

25. Paralegal Assistance is legal assistance by a companion who does not have qualifications as a legal practitioner.

26. Health facility is a hospital, community health center, auxiliary community health center, clinic, village health post, practice place of midwife/nurse/paramedic/doctor.

27. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and child victims of violence.

28. Safe House is temporary housing provided to provide protection to victims in accordance with predetermined operational standards.

29. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

30. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband wife and children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward or sideways up to the third degree.

31. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother.

Article 2
Principles

(1) Protection of women and children is based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia as well as the principles and basic rights as regulated in the Convention on Women and Children, including:
a. respect for human rights;
b. non-discrimination;
c. the best interests of women and/or children;
d. the right to life, survival and development;
e. respect for the opinions of women and children;
f. gender equality and justice; and
g. victim protection;

(2) Legal Certainty and Local Wisdom.

Article 3
Objectives

The objectives of protection for women and child victims of violence are:
a. to ensure the fulfillment of the rights of women and/or children in accordance with applicable provisions;
b. to prevent and protect women and/or children from acts of violence;
c. to encourage the community to carry out their obligations in prevention and protection of women and children in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations; and
d. to restore victims from the effects of acts of violence experienced.

Article 4
Scope

The scope of implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence includes:
a. institutional implementation of protection for women and children;
b. duties and responsibilities for prevention, protection and recovery;
c. forms and mechanisms of prevention; and
d. provision of medical services, medicolegal, special examination rooms, legal aid, expert personnel, psychological recovery, safe houses, social companions, clergy, customary institution support, repatriation, social reintegration, economic empowerment and other services needed by women and child victims of violence to support the fulfillment of the rights of women and child victims of violence.

CHAPTER II
VICTIMS' RIGHTS

Article 5

Victims have the right to obtain:
a. protection from family, Regional Government, police, prosecutors, courts, legal aid institutions, social institutions or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court;
b. special handling related to the security and confidentiality of victims;
c. physical, mental health services and psychological services according to the suffering experienced by victims;
d. accompaniment by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations;
e. spiritual guidance services; and
f. guarantee of continuity of education especially for children who are victims of violence.

Article 6

In the event of acts of violence, every victim has the right to receive assistance both psychologically and legally and to receive guarantees of their rights related to their status as wife, mother, child, family member or as a member of society.

CHAPTER III
DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
FOR PREVENTION, PROTECTION AND RECOVERY

Article 7

Prevention, protection and recovery of women and child victims of violence are the duties and responsibilities including:
a. regional government;
b. community, social organizations and/or other community organizations in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations; and
c. family, and/or parents.

Article 8

(1) The duties and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 7 letter a, include:
a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;
b. establishing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
c. cooperating in the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence;
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence to related agencies both vertical and regional;
e. allocating budget for the protection of women and child victims of violence according to the Regional financial capacity; and
f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence.

(2) In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes programs and activities for the protection of women and children in a Regional action plan.

(3) The Regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (2) is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

(4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 9

The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 7 letter b, include:
a. preventing acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing protection to victims;
c. providing emergency assistance;
d. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to the competent authorities; and
e. participating in handling victims of violence.

Article 10

The obligations and responsibilities of families and/or parents as referred to in Article 7 letter c include:
a. preventing all forms of violence and protecting women and children as family members.
b. providing support for women and child victims of violence who are part of their family members.

CHAPTER IV
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Part One
Institutions

Article 11

(1) To coordinate the duties and responsibilities as referred to in Article 7, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Unit.

(2) The Integrated Service Unit as referred to in paragraph (1) is P2TP2A.

(3) P2TP2A as UPT PPA in the Region may be in the form of Regional Coordinator or other designation established by the Regent.

Article 12

(1) P2TP2A is located in the capital of Central Maluku Regency with a working area covering the entire area of Central Maluku Regency.

(2) P2TP2A can encourage the availability of women's and children's service centers at the sub-district and/or Negeri and Administrative Negeri levels.

Article 13

(1) The purpose of establishing P2TP2A is:
a. to facilitate the needs of women and child victims of violence in fulfilling victims' rights, namely the right to truth, justice, recovery and guarantee of non-recurrence of cases; and
b. to realize welfare, justice and gender equality in various fields of life for women and children comprehensively.

(2) The main tasks of P2TP2A are:
a. to make efforts to prevent violence against women and children;
b. to make efforts to handle women and child victims of violence; and
c. to make rehabilitation efforts including recovery, empowerment, repatriation and social reintegration for women and child victims of violence.

(3) The functions of P2TP2A are:
a. as a data and information center on women and children;
b. as a service center for women and child victims of violence; and
c. as a center for women's and children's empowerment.

Article 14

The position of P2TP2A is under the Regional Government and is responsible to the Regent.

Article 15

(1) P2TP2A consists of at least one Chairperson/Head, one Vice Chairperson/Vice Head, one Secretary, one Treasurer and fields in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations.

(2) P2TP2A membership consists of at least representatives of related regional apparatus organizations within the scope of the Regional Government, Police, Prosecutors, Courts, Non-Governmental Organizations, Community Leaders, Religious Leaders and Legal Aid Institutions.

(3) To be appointed as a member of the P2TP2A management must meet the requirements as regulated in applicable Laws and Regulations.

(4) The term of office of the P2TP2A management is 3 (three) years from the date of inauguration, and can subsequently be reappointed for one subsequent period.

Article 16

P2TP2A is obliged to:
a. provide services and handling as quickly as possible to victims;
b. provide ease and comfort of services for victims;
c. provide services free of charge to victims;
d. maintain the security and confidentiality of victims; and
e. strive to achieve justice and legal certainty for victims.

Part Two
Prevention

Article 17

Prevention of acts of violence against women and children can be implemented through:
a. socialization of Laws and Regulations to the community related to the protection of women and children on a regular basis;
b. campaign for 16 Days of Anti-Violence against women and children every year;
c. integrating issues of violence against women and children into the education curriculum in schools;
d. organizing referral mechanisms in handling and recovery of victims of violence against women and children; and
e. coordination between institutions that have main tasks and functions related to prevention and handling of violence against women and children.

Article 18

In addition to prevention efforts as referred to in Article 17, the Regional Government makes efforts to raise awareness of the community, families and parents through:
a. increasing the number and quality of education both formal, non-formal and informal;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services; and
c. building community participation and concern to carry out prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence.

Article 19

(1) Prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 17 and Article 18, is carried out by regional apparatus organizations according to their duties and functions in the fields of:
a. women's empowerment and child protection;
b. health;
c. mental and spiritual;
d. social;
e. education;
f. manpower; and
g. peace and order.

(2) Prevention of acts of violence by regional apparatus work units as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out based on the Regional Action Plan.

Part Three
Services for Victims

Article 20

(1) Services for victims are organized in cooperation between related institutions/agencies, including
a. legal services in the form of legal aid and paralegal assistance;
b. medical services;
c. medicolegal services;
d. psychological services;
e. rehabilitation services;
f. social reintegration and repatriation services; and
g. support services for economic independence.

(2) Coordination of services for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by P2TP2A.

Article 21

(1) In addition to the forms of services as referred to in Article 20, the Regional Government through P2TP2A is obliged to provide temporary protection services for victims including, but not limited to:
a. initial rescue of victims from acts of violence; and
b. medical emergency handling for victims.

(2) To provide protection as referred to in paragraph (1), P2TP2A can cooperate with security forces, Negeri and Administrative Negeri Government, kelurahan, customary leaders and/or groups in the community that provide assistance services for women and child victims of violence

Article 22

(1) The Regent establishes minimum service standards provided to women and child victims of violence.

(2) In addition to the minimum service standards as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes special Minimum Service Standards for victims with disabilities.

Article 23

(1) In addition to services for victims as referred to in Article 20, Article 21, Article 22, the Regional Government is obliged to provide assistance services for victims.

(2) Assistance services for victims can be carried out in cooperation with persons or institutions providing services for victims.

(3) The assistance mechanism as referred to in paragraph (2), is carried out according to standard operating procedures established by Regent Regulation.

Part Four
Empowerment of Victims of Violence

Article 24

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to empower women and child victims of violence.

(2) Empowerment of victims as referred to in paragraph (1), involves P2TP2A.

(3) In empowering women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government prepares empowerment programs in the economic field, which include:
a. seeking the needs required for entrepreneurship training for women victims of violence to increase knowledge, attitudes and business skills;
b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;
c. providing assistance in developing economic businesses;
d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large companies, as well as financial institutions to develop businesses of women victims of violence;
e. seeking provision of capital for women victims of violence; and
f. expanding access to information and promoting products of women victims of violence.

(4) In empowering women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government can establish cooperation with job training institutions organized by the private sector.

CHAPTER V
COMPLAINT AND HANDLING PROCEDURES

Article 25

(1) Complaints of victims of violence to P2TP2A can be submitted by victims, families, community members, police officers, volunteer companions, or social workers.

(2) Leaders or officers at P2TP2A are obliged to serve victims based on the provisions as referred to in Article 21.

(3) Leaders or officers of P2TP2A immediately handle victims in accordance with established procedures.

(4) Leaders or officers of P2TP2A within a maximum of 24 (twenty-four) hours from receiving the victim's complaint are obliged to report it to the nearest police officer.

Article 26

In the event that the victim is outside their area of origin, P2TP2A is obliged to immediately report to the Regent, so that the Regent coordinates with the Regent/Mayor of the victim's origin to take protective actions or steps and repatriation of the victim to their area of origin.

Article 27

(1) In handling victims, P2TP2A is obliged to network with government or private hospitals for treatment and recovery of their health.

(2) If necessary, P2TP2A can also network with social protection homes or government or community trauma centers, or government or community safe houses, or other social service institutions.

Article 28

Further provisions regarding complaint and handling procedures for victims of violence are further regulated in the P2TP2A Standard Operating Procedures.

CHAPTER VI
COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

Part One
Cooperation

Article 29

(1) The Regional Government can cooperate in order to achieve the objectives of implementing protection for women and child victims of violence.

(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out with:
a. Government;
b. Provincial government;
c. Other regency/city governments; and
d. Non-governmental institutions.

(3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. Exchange of data and information;
b. Rehabilitation of victims of violence;
c. Repatriation and social reintegration; and
d. Provision of evidence and witnesses and followed up in accordance with Laws and Regulations.

(4) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), is carried out in the form of a Joint Agreement.

Part Two
Partnership

Article 30

(1) The Regional Government establishes partnerships with the business world in implementing protection for women and children.

(2) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1), are carried out through:
a. notification of information on job opportunities for women victims of violence;
b. education and training for women and child victims of violence;
c. educational assistance for women and child victims of violence; and
d. fostering and increasing economic independence of women victims of violence.

(3) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) are implemented in the form of joint agreements.

CHAPTER VII
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 31

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to provide guidance on the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence including technical guidance and training.

(2) The Regional Government is obliged to supervise and evaluate the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence.

(3) Supervision and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2), are carried out with professional, transparent, accountable principles.

(4) In addition to supervision carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (2), the community can supervise the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence.

(5) Supervision carried out by the community as referred to in paragraph (4) is by conveying aspirations to the Regent or DPRD.

CHAPTER VIII
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 32

(1) The community participates in the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) can be in the form of:
a. forming protection units or complaint posts for women and child victims of violence within community organizations and/or within Negeri, Administrative Negeri/kelurahan communities
b. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
c. preventing violence;
d. providing first aid to victims; and
e. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.

(3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector and mass media.

CHAPTER IX
REPORTING

Article 33

(1) P2TP2A is obliged to report the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence to the Regent through the related Agency.

(2) Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) is submitted in writing, including administration, finance, services and performance.

(3) Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2), is carried out at least every 1 (one) month.

CHAPTER X
FINANCING

Article 34

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to provide expenditure budget every year through the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget as well as other legitimate and non-binding sources of income to finance the implementation of tasks for prevention, protection and recovery of women and child victims of violence.

(2) The budget for prevention, protection and recovery activities for women and child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is attached to the related Agency and managed by P2TP2A in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Article 35

(1) The Regional Government can provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or civil society organizations that implement protection for women and child victims of violence.

(2) Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1) is provided in accordance with Regional Financial capacity and is implemented in accordance with Laws and Regulations in the field of regional finance.

CHAPTER XI
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 36

With the establishment of this Regional Regulation, Regional Regulation of Central Maluku Regency Number 12 Year 2014 concerning Implementation of Protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence in Central Maluku Regency is declared invalid.

Article 37

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Central Maluku Regency.

I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide protection to every citizen in accordance with the Preamble to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. That women and children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience violence so they need to receive protection. Violence against women and children is a violation of human rights, so victims need to be protected in their dignity and guaranteed their right to life without discrimination.

Violence against women and children is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the child.

The existence of women and child victims of violence has not received adequate services so comprehensive Protection for Women and Children is needed.

In 2014 the Regional Government and DPRD established Regional Regulation Number 12 concerning Implementation of Protection for Women and Children. However, the said PERDA needs changes related to institutions to be adjusted to the latest regulations at the National level. In addition, the PERDA has not comprehensively regulated efforts to implement protection that guarantees the fulfillment of victims' rights to truth, justice, recovery and guarantee of non-recurrence of cases, so a new regional regulation is needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This new Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children, especially in terms of institutions, forms and mechanisms of prevention, prevention of acts of violence, assistance mechanisms, services for victims of violence and empowerment of victims of violence.

Article 2

Letter a
What is meant by "respect for human rights" is appreciation and treatment of Women and Children in accordance with the rights they possess as part of human rights.

Letter b
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is all actions and deeds carried out without differentiating based on economic, social, cultural, political background, gender, age, religion, race and so on.

Letter c
What is meant by "the best interests of women and children" is all actions concerning Women and/or children carried out by the Government, Community, Legislative Body, and Judicial Body where the best interests of women and/or children must receive primary consideration.

Letter d
What is meant by "the right to life, survival, and development" is the most basic human right for Women and/or Children protected by the state, government, community, family and parents.

Letter e
What is meant by "respect for the opinions of women and children" is respect for the rights of women and/or children to participate and express their opinions in making decisions, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.

Letter f
What is meant by "gender equality and justice" is respect for the position of women including girls that is equal and equivalent to other members of society, fairly without discriminating against them in all aspects of life.

Letter g
What is meant by "victim protection" is actions taken to prevent and protect women and/or children who receive acts of violence both physically and psychologically.

Letter b
What is meant by Special handling is handling for women and child victims of violence who need security protection guarantees. Special handling is carried out through Safe House services and in cooperation with the police and the Witness and Victim Protection Agency (LPSK).

Letter c
What is meant by physical health services is in the form of: visum and treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel.

What is meant by mental health services is examination and treatment aimed at restoring the psyche/soul of victims as a result of acts of violence experienced, carried out by clinical psychologists and/or psychiatrists

All costs arising from health services are borne by the Regional Government through the APBD or other legitimate and non-binding sources of income.

Article 21

Paragraph 1

Point a
What is meant by "Initial rescue of victims from acts of violence" is that victims have the right to rescue actions when acts of violence occur if the victim's security is threatened. This rescue action must be carried out by village/kelurahan officials at the victim's residence or at the location where the act of violence occurred. Village/kelurahan officials must immediately coordinate with the nearest security officers.

Point b
What is meant by "Medical emergency handling for victims" is when the victim experiences quite severe injuries, then village/kelurahan officials or medical personnel or security officers or family/community who know of the occurrence of acts of violence are obliged to bring the victim to the nearest service facility to provide emergency assistance before the victim is taken to the hospital for further treatment.
