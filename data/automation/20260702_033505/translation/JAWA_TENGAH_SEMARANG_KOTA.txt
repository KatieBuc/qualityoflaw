CENTRAL JAVA PROVINCE
REGIONAL REGULATION OF SEMARANG CITY
NUMBER 5 YEAR 2016
CONCERNING
PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY
MAYOR OF SEMARANG,

Considering: a. that the fulfillment of constitutional rights and protection of human rights of women and children against acts of violence is one of the values contained in Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution;

b. that violence against women and children in Semarang City continues to increase and spread, causing citizens to feel unsafe in carrying out their lives, thus requiring integrated protection efforts;

c. that to provide direction and legal certainty to all those involved in efforts to provide legal protection, regulation is needed regarding the protection of women and children from acts of violence;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation of Semarang City concerning the Protection of Women and Children From Acts of Violence.

In View of: 1. Article 18 Paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Semarang City.
2. Regional Government is the Regional Head as an element of the Regional Government organizer who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Semarang.
4. Regional People's Representative Council, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.
5. Regional Apparatus is an assistant element to the Regional Head and DPRD in administering government affairs that are the authority of the region.
6. Regional Long-Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJPD, is a regional planning document for a period of 20 (twenty) years.
7. Regional Medium-Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMD, is a regional planning document for a period of 5 (five) years.
8. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
9. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without means against physical and verbal that causes danger to life, body and/or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.
10. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or hardship for women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
11. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.
12. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on human differences on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural fields and other aspects of life.
13. Human Trafficking is the act of recruiting, transporting, harboring, sending, transferring, or receiving a person by threat of violence, use of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud and abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
14. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb.
15. Victim of violence is a woman and child who experiences suffering physically, mentally or emotionally, economic loss, or experiences neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights.
16. Prevention is a direct effort carried out by the Regional Government and/or the community to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
17. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to provide services and rehabilitation to women and children who are victims of violence comprehensively including information services, assistance and legal aid, counseling services, medical services and safe houses through referral.
18. Task Force for the Prevention and Handling of Criminal Acts of Human Trafficking, hereinafter referred to as the Task Force, is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating prevention and handling efforts for criminal acts of human trafficking in Semarang City.
19. Safe house is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.
20. Social reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, school environment, work environment and community environment that can provide protection and fulfillment of victim needs.
21. Victim recovery is services provided in the form of strengthening and developing the abilities of a person who experiences acts of violence against women and children to be more empowered physically, psychologically, sexually, economically and socially.
22. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy and spiritual guidance, for the self-empowerment of women and children from acts of violence to resolve the problems faced.
23. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line up or down to the third degree.
24. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or adoptive mother.
25. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of women and children from acts of violence is organized based on the principles of:
a. Humanity;
b. Justice and gender equality;
c. Non-discrimination;
d. Order and legal certainty;
e. Openness;
f. Protection.

Article 3

The objectives of protecting women and children from acts of violence are to:
a. Prevent acts of violence against women and children including human trafficking;
b. Eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
c. Protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
d. Provide services to women and children from acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses; and
e. Empower women and children victims of violence to be more empowered physically, psychologically, socially, and economically.

CHAPTER III
FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 4

Forms of violence include:
a. Physical violence;
b. Psychological violence;
c. Sexual violence;
d. Neglect;
e. Maltreatment;
f. Exploitation; and/or
g. Other violence.

CHAPTER IV
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

Women and children victims of violence receive the following rights:
a. Right to be respected for their dignity as human beings;
b. Right to recovery;
c. Right to determine their own decisions;
d. Right to obtain information;
e. Right to confidentiality;
f. Right to social rehabilitation;
g. Right to complaint handling that is fast, appropriate, comfortable and according to needs;
h. Right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process;
i. Right to assistance; and
j. Right to a sense of security.

Article 6

Child victims of violence, in addition to receiving rights as referred to in Article 5, also receive special rights, as follows:
a. Right to survival, growth and development;
b. Right to basic population services;
c. Right to equal protection;
d. Right to be free from various stigma; and
e. Right to obtain freedom.

CHAPTER V
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 7

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence are the joint responsibility of:
a. Regional Government;
b. Community.

Article 8

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 7 letter a, include:
a. establishing, implementing policies, programs, and conducting cooperation activities in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
b. facilitating the establishment of institutions for the protection of women and children from acts of violence and providing support for facilities and infrastructure;
c. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence according to regional financial capacity;
d. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. providing protection services for women and children victims of violence;
f. encouraging and increasing community participation;
g. appointing parents and/or substitute family caregivers as a protective measure for children who are victims of violence.

(2) In order to carry out obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Mayor establishes programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in one Regional Action Plan as a basis for Regional Apparatus in implementing the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

(3) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

(4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3), are regulated by Mayoral Regulation.

Article 9

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 7 letter b, are organized in the form of community participation.
(2) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing protection to women and children from acts of violence;
c. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
d. participating in handling victims of violence.
(3) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of legislation.

Article 10

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence against women and children is obliged to make efforts according to their capabilities to:
a. Prevent and stop the continuation of acts of violence;
b. Provide protection to victims;
c. Provide emergency assistance; and/or
d. Assist in the process of submitting applications for protection determination.

CHAPTER VI
INSTITUTIONS

Article 11

(1) The Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT) in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.
(2) PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is established at the City and Sub-district levels.
(3) The membership of PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of elements of the Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, researchers and academics.
(4) Further provisions regarding the Organization and Work Procedures of PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayoral Regulation.

Article 12

In addition to establishing PPT as referred to in Article 11, to support the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence, the Mayor establishes:
a. Task force for human trafficking;
b. Regional action committee for the elimination of the worst forms of child labor.

Article 13

(1) The task force as referred to in Article 12 letter a, consists of representatives from the Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, and researchers/academics.
(2) The task force as referred to in paragraph (1), is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating the prevention and handling of human trafficking.
(3) Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties, and functions of the task force as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayoral Regulation.

Article 14

(1) The committee as referred to in Article 12 letter b, consists of elements from the Regional Government, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, researchers and academics.
(2) Further provisions regarding the duties and functions of the committee as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayoral Regulation.

CHAPTER VII
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Part One
Prevention of Acts of Violence

Article 15

(1) The Regional Government conducts empowerment and awareness to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.
(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), it is implemented by:
a. providing material on the prevention of acts of violence against women and children in both formal and informal education;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services in accordance with applicable provisions;
c. opening employment opportunities for women;
d. building community participation and concern for the prevention of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, educational institutions, and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children; and
g. opening an integrated service system for the protection of women and children from acts of violence in every sub-district.

Article 16

(1) Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 15, is carried out by Regional Apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. education;
d. manpower;
e. population and civil registration;
f. law;
g. women's empowerment and child protection;
h. cooperatives and Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises;
i. mental and spiritual; and
j. peace and order.
(2) Prevention of acts of violence by Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

Part Two
Legal Protection

Article 17

Legal protection includes:
a. Providing protection in safe houses (shelters);
b. Providing legal information to victims;
c. Providing assistance for victims as witnesses from the investigation process to the verdict;
d. Providing special legal protection for child victims of violence can be done by appointing guardianship in accordance with applicable legislation.

Part Three
Recovery

Article 18

Recovery includes:
a. Providing physical recovery at health service institutions;
b. Providing medicolegal services;
c. Assisting victim repatriation;
d. Providing temporary protection in safe houses (shelters);
e. Providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;
f. Providing spiritual guidance services;
g. Preparing family, school, work and community environments, as well as economic empowerment.

Part Four
Coordination

Article 19

Coordination includes:
a. Conducting coordination and cooperation in handling violence cases with integrated service institutions;
b. Conducting coordination and cooperation in handling violence cases with integrated services between regions.

Part Five
Community Participation

Article 20

Community Participation is carried out by:
a. Fostering community awareness of cases of violence against women and children;
b. Encouraging the community to actively participate in providing information and reporting acts of violence against women and children;
c. Fostering local wisdom in handling cases of violence;
d. Organizing strengthening of community groups in handling acts of violence against women and children;
e. Disseminating information about legislation related to acts of violence against women and children.

CHAPTER VIII
COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

Part One
Cooperation

Article 21

(1) The Regional Government develops cooperation patterns in order to achieve the objectives of protecting women and children from acts of violence in accordance with the provisions of legislation.
(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out between the Regional Government and:
a. Central Government;
b. Provincial Government;
c. Other Regency/City Governments;
d. Universities;
e. Social and religious institutions;
f. Non-Governmental Organizations; and/or
g. Media.
(3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) includes:
a. exchange of data and information;
b. rehabilitation of victims of violence;
c. repatriation and social reintegration; and
d. provision of evidence and witnesses.
(4) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of legislation.

Part Two
Partnership

Article 22

(1) The Regional Government may organize partnerships with the business world in protecting women and children from acts of violence;
(2) Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through:
a. notification of employment opportunity information for women victims of violence;
b. education and training for women victims of violence;
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of violence who are withdrawn from their education; and
d. fostering and increasing economic independence of women victims of violence.
(3) Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), is carried out in accordance with the provisions of legislation.

CHAPTER IX
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 23

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.
(2) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. coordination;
d. monitoring;
e. evaluation; and
f. reporting.

Article 24

The community may conduct supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence organized by the Regional Government with a mechanism for conveying aspirations to the Mayor or to the DPRD.

Article 25

Further provisions regarding the procedures for implementing guidance and supervision as referred to in article 23 and article 24 are regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

CHAPTER X
FINANCING

Article 26

Financing for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence may be sourced from:
a. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget
b. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget;
c. Non-binding community contributions;
d. Non-binding foreign assistance; and/or
e. Other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of legislation.

Article 27

(1) The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations that implement protection of women and children from discrimination and acts of violence.
(2) Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with regional financial capacity, and is carried out in accordance with the provisions of legislation in the field of regional finance.

CHAPTER XI
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 28

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Semarang City.

I. GENERAL

Women and children are a serious concern of the international community, considering their vulnerable position as victims of violence from family and their environment. Various acts of violence both against women and children within the household and outside the household continue to increase from time to time. This condition, if without serious handling, will destroy household life, society, nation and state. Therefore, the Semarang City government is obliged to provide protection to women and children victims of violence which is an implementation of various legislation, including Law Number 39 Year 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 21 Year 2007 concerning the Eradication of Human Trafficking, Law Number 35 Year 2014 Concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection.

Protection for women and children from acts of violence is the obligation of the Regional Government in the context of regional autonomy, which is manifested in the form of Regional Regulations as regulated in Article 236 paragraph (1), Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government.

On that basis, the Regional Government of Semarang City has taken steps as an effort to provide protection to women and children, but has not been able to provide maximum and adequate protection against acts of violence that occur in the social life of the community, especially to women and children, among others caused by the still low understanding of the community regarding the rights of women and children, handling has not been well coordinated, implementation has not been sustainable, and so on. Therefore, regulation is needed in the form of a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children to answer while providing legal certainty in the protection of women and children victims of acts of violence that occur.

The existence of a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children, gives responsibility to the Regional Government from the prevention of acts of violence to the handling of victims of violence. In its implementation, the Regional Government cooperates with government agencies, other regional governments and the community. In addition, adequate funding support both from the government, Regional Government and the participation of the business community and society, it is hoped that violence against women and children can be reduced and even in time will be eliminated from Semarang City.

Article 4
Letter a
Physical violence is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Letter b
Psychological violence is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Letter c
Sexual violence is caused by:
1) acts in the form of sexual harassment;
2) forced sexual intercourse;
3) forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
4) forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Letter d
Neglect is caused by:
1) acts that result in the unfulfillment of children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
2) acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should be carried out by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
3) acts of neglecting people within their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of agreement or contract they must provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
4) acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Letter e
Maltreatment is maltreatment of a child's physical and emotional including neglecting their education and health as well as sexual abuse as a result of human behavior that is wrong toward children.

Letter f
Exploitation is caused by:
1) acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
2) acts with or without the victim's consent including prostitution, forced work or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
3) all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

Letter g
Other violence is caused by:
1) threat of violence includes: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and
2) coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.
