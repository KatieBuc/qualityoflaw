CONCERNING
THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN
AND CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

THE REGENT OF BANGKALAN,

Considering:
a. that until now the number of violence against women and children in Bangkalan Regency is still high, while services and protection have not been carried out optimally;
b. that in connection with the consideration referred to in letter a, it is deemed necessary to regulate the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence through a Regional Regulation.

To Establish:
REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE.

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1
In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Regional Government is the Government of Bangkalan Regency.
2. Regent is the Regent of Bangkalan.
3. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.
4. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, or psychological to the victim.
5. Physical violence is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes death.

6. Psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
7. Sexual violence is an act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or for certain purposes.
8. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
9. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will occur, is occurring or has occurred.
10. Companion is a person who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
11. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an institution providing services to victims of violence, based in a Hospital, managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psycho-social and legal services.
12. Safe house is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.
13. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
14. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband-wife or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, and mother and children.
15. Household is family members and relatives (grandchildren, nieces/nephews, older siblings, younger siblings, grandfathers, grandmothers, cousins and so on) and non-relatives (servants, drivers, and so on) who live and eat from one kitchen and reside in one house.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2
The principles of implementing protection for women and child victims of violence are:
a. respect for the rights of victims.
b. justice and gender equality.
c. non-discrimination.
d. best interests of the victim.

Article 3
The objective of implementing protection for women and child victims of violence is to provide protection and services to women and child victims of violence based on gender equality and the best interests of the child occurring in the household and/or public.

CHAPTER III
RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 4
(1) Every community or individual has the right to report incidents of violence against children and women.
(2) Every victim has the right to obtain protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage and confidential handling from individuals, groups or institutions whether Regional Government or non-governmental.

Article 5
In the event of violence, every victim has the right to receive assistance both psychologically and legally and to receive guarantees of their rights related to their status as wife, mother, child, family member or as a member of society.

CHAPTER IV
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 6
(1) The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to carry out efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence, in the form of:
a. collecting data and information about women and child victims of violence and legislation;
b. conducting education on anti-violence values against women and children;
c. conducting socialization of legislation related to the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence;
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence.
(2) To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government is obligated to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:

a. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving community elements;
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims.
(3) The Regional Government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

CHAPTER V
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Part One
Institutional

Article 7
(1) Implementation of protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the PPT framework.
(2) PPT Bangkalan may continue case referrals from sub-districts in Bangkalan.
(3) Provisions concerning PPT will be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

Part Two
Forms and Mechanisms of Service

Article 8
(1) Forms of services to victims organized by PPT include:
a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;
b. medicolegal services, which are forms of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;
c. psychosocial services, which are services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including provision of safe houses to protect victims from threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength, and independence in resolving their problems;
d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process;
e. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

(2) Service mechanisms as referred to in paragraph (1) are organized according to standard operating procedures (SOP) which will be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

Part Three
Service Principles

Article 9
Implementation of services to victims is carried out free of charge, quickly, safely, empathetically, non-discriminatory, easily accessible, and with guaranteed confidentiality.

Article 10
PPT managers are obligated to organize services in accordance with service principles as referred to in Article 9.

CHAPTER VI
ASSISTANCE

Article 11
Assistance is carried out by persons or institutions that have expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and have cooperated with PPT.

CHAPTER VII
SANCTION PROVISIONS

Article 12
(1) Any person who deliberately does not provide protection to women and children resulting in the occurrence of violence, allows violence to occur, and/or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the provisions of legislation;
(2) If officials appointed to implement protection do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities, they shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the provisions of legislation;
(3) PPT managers who carry out service duties that violate service principles may be subject to sanctions in accordance with PPT internal mechanisms.

CHAPTER VIII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 13
This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Bangkalan Regency.
