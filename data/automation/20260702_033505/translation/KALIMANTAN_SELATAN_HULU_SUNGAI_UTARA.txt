BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD
THE REGENT OF HULU SUNGAI UTARA,

Considering: a. that in the framework of protecting the rights of women and children as mandated by the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, improving the quality of life of women and children and addressing all forms of violence against women and children, there needs to be concern from all parties, both the community and related government institutions and law enforcement officials;

b. that there is a tendency of increasing victims and cases of violence against women and children, which have not been handled optimally so that integrated services are needed by related agencies and community institutions/organizations to provide protection for women and children victims of violence;

c. that in an effort to facilitate women and children to improve their skills and independence and obtain consultation services and problem-solving for problems experienced by women and children, there is a need for an integrated service center for women and children empowerment;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Hulu Sungai Utara Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the regional government administration of Hulu Sungai Utara Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Hulu Sungai Utara.
4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of Hulu Sungai Utara Regency.
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb.
6. Woman is a person of female gender and a person recognized by law as a woman.
7. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
8. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.
9. Violence is any act that results in or may result in suffering or distress, whether physical, sexual, economic, social, or psychological to victims.
10. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causes death.
11. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
Regional Regulation of HSU Regency Year 2016 Number 3 concerning Protection of Women and Children

12. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
13. Victim is a woman and child who experiences suffering and/or distress either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
14. Victim of violence is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of basic rights, as a result of human rights violations (HAM), or criminal acts and acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.
15. Service is an action that must be immediately taken to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing of, during or after the occurrence of violence against victims.
16. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.
17. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has expertise in assisting victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
18. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of violence to the family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfill their needs.
19. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
20. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions or other social environments that can provide protection.
21. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of Regional Government, and/or Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) that provide assistance.
22. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is a functional work unit located in sub-districts and regencies to organize integrated services for witnesses and/or victims of violence in the framework of empowerment of women and children victims of violence.
23. Family Information and Consultation Center (PIK) is an institution located in villages/sub-districts that provides consultation services and social guidance to individuals, family members, communities facing disruptions to their social functions.
Regional Regulation of HSU Regency Year 2016 Number 3 concerning Protection of Women and Children

24. Implementing officers or functional officers are health workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, legal aid workers who come from civil servants within their respective agencies or special personnel employed at P2TP2A and PIK Family.
25. Special Service Room, hereinafter abbreviated as RPK, is a safe and comfortable room specifically intended for witnesses and/or victims of criminal acts including suspects of criminal acts consisting of women and children who deserve to be treated or need special treatment, and whose cases are being handled at the Police office.
26. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community in carrying out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of violence.
27. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with established operational standards.
28. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
29. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, and mother and children.
30. Household is family members and relatives (grandchildren, nephews/nieces, older siblings, younger siblings, grandfathers, grandmothers, cousins, etc.) and non-relatives (servants, drivers, etc.), who live and eat from one kitchen and reside in one house.
31. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or adoptive mother.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

The principles of implementing protection of women and children are:
a. respect for the rights of women and children;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. best interests of women and children.

Article 3

(1) The objectives of implementing protection of women and children are to:
a. prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
d. provide services to women and children, reporters, and witnesses;
Regional Regulation of HSU Regency Year 2016 Number 3 concerning Protection of Women and Children

e. empower women for the fulfillment of the rights of women and children; and
f. facilitate and mediate domestic disputes to realize harmonious and prosperous household integrity.

(2) The objectives of implementing protection of women and children as referred to in paragraph (1), include aspects of:
a. prevention;
b. service and assistance;
c. reunification;
d. empowerment.

CHAPTER III
RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 4

Every victim has the right to obtain:
a. protection;
b. information;
c. optimal service;
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;
e. confidential handling;
f. psychological and legal assistance; and
g. guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member.

Article 5

Children, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 4, also obtain special rights, as follows:
a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth, development and participation;
b. the right to basic services;
c. the right to equal protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigmas; and
e. the right to obtain freedom.

CHAPTER IV
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 6

Obligations and responsibilities in implementing protection of women and children are the joint responsibility of:
a. regional government;
b. community;
c. family; and
d. parents.

Article 7

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 6 letter a, include:
Regional Regulation of HSU Regency Year 2016 Number 3 concerning Protection of Women and Children

a. implementing protection policies for women and children established by the government;
b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for protection of women and children;
c. conducting cooperation in implementing protection of women and children;
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for implementing protection of women and children;
e. allocating budget for implementing protection of women and children according to regional financial capacity;
f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children.

(2) In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government establishes action programs and activities for protection of women and children in the Regional Action Plan as a basis for implementing protection of women and children.

(3) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

(4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 8

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letter b, are organized in the form of community participation.

(2) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
c. participating in providing assistance and/or handling victims of violence.

(3) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of legislation.

Article 9

The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 letters c and d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.
Regional Regulation of HSU Regency Year 2016 Number 3 concerning Protection of Women and Children

CHAPTER V
DUTIES AND AUTHORITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

Article 10

(1) The Regional Government has the duty to carry out efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children, in the form of:
a. collecting data and information about women and children victims of violence, and legislation;
b. socializing legislation related to the implementation of protection of women and children;
c. conducting education on anti-violence values against women and children; and
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of women and children.

(2) The Regional Government in anticipating the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, has the authority to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
a. providing and facilitating the formation of integrated services for victims of violence by involving institutions and community elements;
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims; and
c. empowering the community for prevention of acts of violence.

CHAPTER VI
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Part One
Institutional

Article 11

(1) In order to provide services and protection to women and children, the Regional Government establishes P2TP2A as an Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection located in the Regency and Sub-district, and the Family Information and Consultation Center (PIK) located in Villages/Sub-districts.

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with: Health Office, Education Office, Youth Sports Culture and Tourism Office, Social Manpower and Transmigration Office, Cooperatives SME Industry and Trade Office, Population and Civil Registration Office, Agency for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, Community Empowerment and Village Government Agency, Civil Service Police Unit, Pambalah Batung Amuntai Regional General Hospital, Hulu Sungai Utara Police Resort, Non-Governmental Organizations, and Universities.

(3) P2TP2A in its management is established by Regent Regulation.

(4) Regency P2TP2A may receive case referrals from Sub-district P2TP2A and Village/Sub-district PIK Family as well as Community Health Centers.

(5) Further provisions regarding P2TP2A and PIK Family as referred to in paragraph (4), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Part Two
Forms and Service Mechanisms

Article 12

Forms of prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children carried out by P2TP2A and PIK Family, can be implemented through:
a. socialization activities of legislation to the community related to the protection of the rights of women and children; and
b. training of P2TP2A and PIK Family members related to the implementation of their duties in preventing acts of violence against women and children.

Article 13

(1) Regency P2TP2A in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with Sub-district P2TP2A, and competent parties in making efforts to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.

(2) Further provisions regarding the mechanism for implementing prevention duties as referred to in paragraph (1), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 14

Forms of services for victims organized by P2TP2A include:
a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;
b. medicolegal services are forms of medical services for the purposes of proof in the legal field;
c. psychosocial services are services provided by assistants in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in solving their problems;
d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process;
e. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and can provide economic access so that victims can be independent.

Article 15

(1) In carrying out the task of serving victims of violence against women and children, Regency P2TP2A coordinates with Sub-district P2TP2A.

(2) The service mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) is organized according to standard operating procedures which will be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

Part Three
Prevention of Acts of Violence

Article 16

(1) To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government empowers and raises awareness among families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.

(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government makes efforts as follows:
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income improvement and social services;
c. opening employment opportunities for women;
d. building community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
f. imposing curfews for children (under 18 years) to leave the house at night no later than 11:00 pm, if more than that time must be accompanied by parents or family;
g. restricting children's access to the internet at internet cafes (warnet) during school hours;
h. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children; and
i. opening complaint posts for protection of women and children from acts of violence.

Article 17

(1) Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 16, is carried out by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:

Empowerment of Victims of Violence

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to empower women victims of violence.

(3) In empowering women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government prepares empowerment programs in the economic field, which include:
a. providing the needs required for entrepreneurship training for women, in order to increase knowledge, attitudes, and business skills;
b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;
c. providing assistance in developing productive economic businesses;
d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large companies, as well as financial institutions to develop women's businesses;
e. seeking the provision of capital for women victims of violence; and

f. expanding access to information and promoting women's products.

(4) In empowering women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may establish cooperation with job training institutions organized by the private sector.

(2) Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with regional financial capacity and is implemented in accordance with the provisions of legislation.

CHAPTER XII
INVESTIGATION

Article 33

(1) In addition to general investigation officials, investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children in this Regional Regulation,
Regional Regulation of HSU Regency Year 2016 Number 3 concerning Protection of Women and Children

may also be carried out by Civil Servant Investigators (PPNS) as referred to in the Criminal Procedure Law.

(2) PPNS as referred to in paragraph (1), are certain civil servant officials within the Regional Government appointed by authorized officials in accordance with the provisions of legislation.

(3) In carrying out investigation duties, PPNS as referred to in paragraph (2), has the authority to:
a. receive reports or complaints from someone about the existence of criminal acts of violence against women and children;
b. take first action at that time at the scene and conduct an examination;
c. interrogate a suspect and examine the suspect's identification;
d. seize objects and/or letters;
e. take fingerprints and photograph a person;
f. summon people to be heard and examined as suspects or witnesses;
g. bring in experts needed in connection with case examination;
h. stop the investigation after receiving instructions from the General Investigator that there is insufficient evidence, or the incident is not a criminal act and subsequently through the General Investigator notify this to the Public Prosecutor, the suspect or their family; and/or
i. take other actions necessary for the smooth investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children, which according to law can be accounted for.

(4) PPNS as referred to in paragraph (2), notifies the commencement of investigation and submits the results of its investigation to the Public Prosecutor through the Investigation Official of the Republic of Indonesia Police, in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Criminal Procedure Law.

CHAPTER XIII
SANCTION PROVISIONS

Article 34

(1) Any person who intentionally does not provide protection to women and children so as to cause violence, allows violence to occur and/or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, is subject to sanctions as regulated in applicable legislation.

(2) If Officials appointed to implement protection do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities, they are subject to sanctions in accordance with applicable legislation.

CONCERNING
THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

I. GENERAL

The state has an obligation to provide a sense of security to its citizens from threats and actions that can disturb and damage psychological, physical, sexual and economic security. This is philosophically stated in the preamble to the 1945 Constitution, that the purpose of establishing the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia is to protect the entire Indonesian nation and all Indonesian territory.

Because the Indonesian Government has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 Year 1984 concerning the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women), it is obliged to fulfill these provisions. Human Rights state that, the enforcement and fulfillment of Human Rights is the responsibility of the State, especially the Government.

Through Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 Year 2014, the Government recognizes the existence of children's rights. The inherent rights of children include basic rights as human beings, namely the Right to Life, Right to Growth and Development, Right to Protection and Right to Participation.

To fulfill the Right to life, children need food in sufficient quantities, healthy and nutritious, as well as optimal health access. Protection from violence that threatens their safety and health must be provided from an early age. Protection is needed to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. To fulfill the right to growth and development, children need space to play, exercise, education that is appropriate to their physical and psychological development. In fulfilling children's rights, every government administrator, community and parents must understand and care about children's rights. The existence of legal instruments and legal apparatus that defend children's interests is needed for this protection effort. Legislation that is principled on defending the best interests of the child is needed to realize legal protection.

Furthermore, it is not uncommon to find cases of women and children who experience problems of physical, psychological, sexual and social violence. For example, women and children victims of persecution, neglect, eviction and abuse by parents or others. For women and children who experience these problems, the government must provide Safe Houses (children protection homes) or Integrated Service Centers (PPT) for women and children victims of violence. Safe Houses or PPT are safe places and friendly for women and children. The PPT is equipped with assistants from educators, doctors, psychologists, clerics/clergy and networked with lawyers for women and children and RPK Units at the Police.

The fate of women and children victims of violence must be given attention by the government. The many cases of violence against women and children are often caused by factors that develop in society, such as low economic levels, education, environments in the industrial sector. Therefore, victims of violence like this need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights.

For empowerment of victims of violence, the Regional Government may establish an Integrated Service Center as a service center in the framework of empowering women victims of violence which specifically has the main duties and functions for empowerment of women and children victims of violence.

From the above framework, the Regional Government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, economically and socially to prevent, suppress, reduce, and eliminate all forms of violence against women and children. From these various efforts, a legal framework will be realized in implementing protection of women and children.

II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

Article 1
Sufficiently clear.

Article 2
Letter a
What is meant by "respect for the rights of victims" is a series of actions to respect, appreciate and ensure the fulfillment of victims' rights.

Letter b
What is meant by "justice and gender equality" is a process to be fair to men and women and equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, national defense and security activities and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

Letter c
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims by not making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d
What is meant by "Best interests of victims" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, must be the main consideration.

Article 3
Paragraph (1)
Sufficiently clear.

Paragraph (2)
Letter a
What is meant by the "prevention" aspect is a protection strategy effort through:
a. primary prevention, all people, families, communities and the state in efforts to increase the ability of knowledge, understanding and maintain so that violence against women and children does not occur, including policy socialization, adequate services, supportive workplace policies, and life skill training for women and children. What is meant by life skill training includes conflict resolution without violence, stress management skills, resource management, effective decision making, effective interpersonal communication, guidance on psychosocial development of women and children.

b. secondary prevention, aimed at community groups at high risk in efforts to improve skills, including training and victim services to maintain so that violence against women and children does not occur in the next generation. Activities carried out here include conducting home visits for parents who have just had children to conduct self-assessment whether they are at risk of committing violence against children in the future.

c. tertiary prevention, intended to improve parenting abilities that maintain so that violence against women and children does not recur, here what is done is integrated services for women and children who are victims of violence, through counseling, stress management training.

Letter b
What is meant by the "service and assistance" aspect is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals and assistants according to their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

Letter c
What is meant by the "reunification" aspect is an effort to restore and recover the physical and psychological condition of victims which then unites them with the victim's family and community.

Letter d
What is meant by the "empowerment" aspect is a process of increasing the role of the community to participate in implementing protection for women and children victims of violence which aims to obtain access and participation in the protection process of women and children broadly.

Article 4
Letter a
What is meant by "obtaining protection" is obtaining protection from individuals, groups and institutions both government and non-government.

Letter b
What is meant by "obtaining information" is access and information about the existence of complaint centers, P2TP2A, and all matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and involvement in the decision-making process related to assistance and case developments.

Letter c
What is meant by "optimal service" is service that includes medical, extensive medicolegal, psychosocial and legal services.

Letter d
What is meant by "continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage" is handling that does not stop at physical and psychological healing, but until the victim can live life again in society including in restoring their good name.

Letter e
What is meant by "confidential handling" is an effort to guarantee certainty for victims not to disseminate their identity, medical care and legal handling.

Letter f
What is meant by "obtaining psychological assistance" is assistance provided by psychologists to victims suffering from trauma/other psychological problems to restore the victim's psychological condition. Meanwhile, legal assistance is an assistance effort provided by persons and/or legal aid institutions to victims at every level of examination and during the legal process.

Letter g
What is meant by "guarantee of rights related to victim status" is an effort to provide certainty and protection for victims as family members and community.

Article 19
Letter a
What is meant by "fast" is immediate action taken without complications or simplified procedures. What is meant by "safe" is a guarantee of service protection that feels comfortable, not disturbed, and served in a friendly manner, respecting and appreciating.
What is meant by "empathy" is an action of appreciating, respecting, loving, being friendly, and making happy which aims to please and calm the victim's heart.

Letter d
What is meant by "free of charge" is that the implementation of service and assistance activities carried out by P2TP2A is not charged to victims.
