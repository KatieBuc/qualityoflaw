REGENT OF HULU SUNGAI SELATAN,

Considering: a. that every citizen has the right to be free from acts of violence or treatment that degrades human dignity and has the right to obtain a sense of security, especially for women and children;

b. that violence against women and children in Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency continues to increase and spread, causing a sense of insecurity in carrying out life, so integrated protection efforts are needed;

c. that based on the provisions of Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended several times most recently by Law Number 17 Year 2016 concerning Stipulation of Government Regulation in Lieu of Law Number 1 Year 2016 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 Year 2002 Concerning Child Protection into Law and the provisions of Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning Elimination of Domestic Violence, Regional Governments are obligated and responsible for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in the Region;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regional Head as an element of the Regional Government organizer who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regional Head hereinafter referred to as Regent is the Regent of Hulu Sungai Selatan.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government organizer.
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regional Head and DPRD in organizing government affairs that are regional authority.
6. Regional Long-Term Development Plan hereinafter abbreviated as RPJPD is a regional planning document for a period of 20 (twenty) years.
7. Regional Medium-Term Development Plan hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMD is a regional planning document for a period of 5 (five) years.
8. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
9. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without means against physical and verbal that causes danger to life, body and/or results in deprivation of a person's freedom.
10. Violence Against Women is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
11. Violence Against Children is any act against a Child that results in suffering or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child.
12. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion that is direct or indirect based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that results in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
13. Trafficking in Persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud and abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether conducted within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
14. Child is a person who has not reached 18 (eighteen) years of age and has not married including a Child still in the womb.
15. Victim of Acts of Violence is a woman and Child who experiences suffering both physical, mental and emotional, economic loss, or experiences neglect, reduction or deprivation of basic rights.
16. Prevention is direct efforts carried out by the Regional Government and/or the Community to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and Children.
17. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a regional technical implementation unit formed by the Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency Government in providing services for women and Children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection and other problems.
18. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.
19. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the Community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, school environment, work environment and Community environment that can provide protection and fulfillment of victim needs.
20. Victim Recovery hereinafter Recovery is services provided in the form of strengthening and developing the abilities of a person who experiences acts of violence against women and Children to be more empowered both physically, psychologically, sexually, economically and socially.
21. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy and spiritual guidance, for self-strengthening of women and Children from acts of violence to resolve problems faced.
22. Family is the smallest unit in the Community consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line up or down to the third degree.
23. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
24. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.

Article 2

Protection of women and children from acts of violence is organized based on the principles of:
a. humanity;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. order and legal certainty;
e. openness; and
f. protection.

Article 3

The purpose of protection of women and children from acts of violence is to:
a. prevent acts of violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;
b. eliminate all forms of acts of violence and exploitation against women and children;
c. protect, provide a sense of security for women and children;
d. provide services to women and children from acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses; and
e. strengthen women and children victims of acts of violence to be more empowered both physically, psychologically, socially, and economically.

Article 4

The scope of regulation of protection of women and children from acts of violence includes:
a. implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
b. obligations and responsibilities;
c. forms of violence;
d. rights of women and children from acts of violence;
e. institutions;
f. cooperation and partnerships;
g. guidance and supervision;
h. awards; and
i. funding;

CHAPTER II
IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN
AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Part One
General

Article 5

(1) The Regional Government organizes protection of women and children from acts of violence in the Region.
(2) The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Apparatus that organizes government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
(3) The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) involves stakeholders in the Region.
(4) The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in the region is guided by the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 6

The Regional Government in organizing Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence as referred to in Article 5 conducts:
a. prevention of acts of violence against women and children;
b. legal protection;
c. Recovery;
d. coordination; and
e. encouraging community participation.

Part Two
Prevention of Acts of Violence

Article 7

(1) The Regional Government conducts prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 6 letter a through empowerment and awareness to Families, Parents, and the Community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.
(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), it is implemented by:
a. providing material on prevention of acts of violence against women and children in both formal and informal education;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services in accordance with applicable provisions;
c. opening employment opportunities for women;
d. building community participation and concern for prevention of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, educational institutions, and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children; and
g. opening an integrated service system for protection of women and children from acts of violence in every sub-district.

Article 8

(1) Prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 7 is carried out by Regional Apparatus that organize government affairs in the fields of:
a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. education;
d. manpower;
e. population administration and civil registration;
f. women's empowerment, protection of women and children;
g. cooperatives and Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises; and
h. public order, public order, and community protection
(2) Prevention of acts of violence by Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated and sustainable manner based on RPJPD and RPJMD.

Part Three
Legal Protection

Article 9

The Regional Government conducts legal protection as referred to in Article 6 letter b, including:
a. providing protection in Safe Houses;
b. providing legal information to victims;
c. providing assistance for victims as witnesses from the investigation process to the verdict; and
d. providing special legal protection for child victims of acts of violence can be done by appointing guardianship in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Part Four
Recovery

Article 10

The Regional Government conducts recovery as referred to in Article 6 letter c, including:
a. providing physical recovery at health service institutions;
b. providing medicolegal services;
c. assisting victim repatriation;
d. providing temporary protection in Safe Houses;
e. providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;
f. providing spiritual guidance services; and/or
g. preparing family, school, work and community environments, as well as economic empowerment.

Part Five
Coordination

Article 11

The Regional Government conducts coordination as referred to in Article 6 letter d, including:
a. coordinating and cooperating in handling violence cases with integrated service institutions; and/or
b. coordinating and cooperating in handling violence cases with integrated services between Regions.

Part Six
Community Participation

Article 12

The Regional Government encourages community participation as referred to in Article 6 letter e, by:
a. fostering community awareness of cases of acts of violence against women and children;
b. encouraging the community to actively participate in providing information and reporting acts of violence against women and children;
c. fostering local wisdom in handling cases of acts of violence;
d. organizing strengthening of community groups in handling acts of violence against women and children;
e. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to acts of violence against women and children.

CHAPTER III
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One
General

Article 13

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence are the joint responsibility of:
a. Regional Government; and
b. Community.

Part Two
Regional Government

Article 14

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 13 letter a, include:
a. establishing, implementing policies, programs, and conducting cooperation activities in organizing protection of women and children from acts of violence;
b. facilitating the establishment of institutions for protection of women and children from acts of violence and providing support for facilities and infrastructure;
c. allocating budget for organizing protection of women and children from acts of violence according to regional financial capacity;
d. guiding and supervising the organization of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. providing protection services for women and children victims of acts of violence;
f. encouraging and increasing community participation; and
g. appointing parents and/or substitute family caregivers as a protection measure for children who are victims of acts of violence.
(2) In order to carry out obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes programs and activities for protection of women and children in the Regional Action Plan as a basis for Regional Apparatus in implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence.
(3) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) is part of the RPJMD.
(4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Part Three
Community

Article 15

(1) the obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 13 letter b, are organized in the form of community participation.
(2) forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing protection to women and children from acts of violence;
c. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
d. participating in handling victims of acts of violence.
(3) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) are carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 16

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence against women and children is obligated to:
a. prevent and stop the ongoing acts of violence;
b. provide protection to victims;
c. provide emergency assistance; and/or
d. assist in the process of submitting applications for protection orders.

CHAPTER IV
FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 17

Forms of violence against women and children include:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. maltreatment;
f. exploitation; and/or
g. other violence.

CHAPTER V
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 18

Women and children victims of acts of violence receive the following rights:
a. the right to be respected for dignity and worth as human beings;
b. the right to recovery;
c. the right to determine their own decisions;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidentiality;
f. the right to social rehabilitation;
g. the right to quick, appropriate, comfortable and needs-based complaint handling;
h. the right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process;
i. the right to assistance; and
j. the right to a sense of security and protection.

Article 19

Child victims of acts of violence in addition to receiving rights as referred to in Article 18, also receive special rights as follows:
a. the right to survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic population services;
c. the right to equal Protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and
e. the right to obtain freedom.

Article 20

The rights of children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 18 and Article 19 experienced by children with special needs are handled by the Regional Government and the community guided by service standards for children with special needs.

Article 21

Fulfillment of rights as referred to in Article 18, Article 19 and Article 20 is guided by norms, standards, procedures, and criteria established by the government in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI
INSTITUTIONS

Article 22

(1) The Regional Government establishes UPTD PPA in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.
(2) UPTD PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) is established at the Regency level.
(3) Further provisions regarding the organization and work procedures of UPTD PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER VII
COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIPS

Part One
Cooperation

Article 23

(1) The Regional Government develops cooperation patterns for protection of women and children from acts of violence.
(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in order to achieve the objectives of protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
(3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted between the Regional Government and:
a. Central Government;
b. Provincial Government;
c. other Regional Governments;
d. Universities;
e. Social and Religious Institutions;
f. Non-Governmental Organizations; and/or
g. Media.
(4) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) includes:
a. exchange of data and information;
b. rehabilitation of victims of acts of violence;
c. repatriation and social reintegration; and
d. provision of evidence and witnesses.
(5) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Part Two
Partnerships

Article 24

(1) The Regional Government organizes partnerships with the business world in protection of women and children from acts of violence.
(2) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1) are in order to achieve the objectives of protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
(3) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1) are conducted through:
a. notification of employment opportunity information for women victims of acts of violence;
b. education and training for women victims of acts of violence;
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of acts of violence who are withdrawn from their education; and
d. fostering and increasing economic independence of women victims of acts of violence.
(4) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (3) are implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VIII
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 25

(1) The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the organization of protection of women and children from acts of violence.
(2) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. coordination;
d. monitoring;
e. evaluation; and
f. reporting.

Article 26

The Community can conduct supervision of the organization of Protection of women and children from acts of violence organized by the Regional Government with a mechanism for conveying aspirations to the Regent or to the DPRD.

Article 27

Further provisions regarding procedures for implementing guidance and supervision as referred to in Article 24 and Article 25 are regulated in Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER IX
AWARDS

Article 28

(1) The Regional Government provides awards to every person or entity that participates in efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence in the Region.
(2) Awards as referred to in paragraph (1) are given in the form of:
a. certificates/plaques; and
b. Regional incentives.
(3) Incentives as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b can be in the form of:
a. fiscal; and
b. non-fiscal.
(4) Fiscal incentives as referred to in paragraph (3) letter a can be in the form of:
a. reduction or exemption of regional taxes; and/or
b. reduction of regional levies.
(5) Non-fiscal incentives as referred to in paragraph (3) letter b can be in the form of ease of regional licensing in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
(6) Procedures and forms of providing incentives as referred to in paragraph (2) are guided by the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER X
ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS

Article 29

(1) Every person who does not fulfill their obligations as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) and Article 16 is subject to administrative sanctions.
(2) Administrative sanctions to persons as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:
a. verbal warning;
b. written warning; and/or
c. administrative fine
(3) Administrative sanctions to persons as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:
a. written warning;
b. temporary suspension of activities;
c. permanent cessation of activities;
d. temporary revocation of permit;
e. permanent revocation of permit; and/or
f. administrative fine.
(4) Administrative fines as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c and paragraph (3) letter f constitute Regional revenue.
(5) Further provisions regarding procedures for imposing administrative sanctions are regulated in Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER XI
FUNDING

Article 30

Funding for the organization of protection of women and children from acts of violence regulated in this Regional Regulation is charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget and other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 31

(1) The Regional Government can provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations that implement protection of women and children from discrimination and acts of Violence.
(2) Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with regional financial capacity, and is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

CHAPTER XII
TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Article 32

Regional policies regarding protection of women and children from acts of violence that existed before this Regional Regulation was promulgated, remain valid as long as they do not conflict with the provisions of laws and regulations and this Regional Regulation.

CHAPTER XIII
CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 33

Implementing regulations of this Regional Regulation must be established no later than 6 (six) months from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

Article 34

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency.

I. GENERAL

Women and Children are a serious concern of the international community, considering their vulnerable position to become victims of Violence from their families and environment. Various acts of Violence both against women and Children within the household and outside the household continue to increase from time to time. This condition, if without serious handling, will destroy household life, Community, nation and state. Therefore, the government of Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency is obligated to provide Protection to women and Children victims of acts of Violence which is an implementation of various laws and regulations, including Law Number 39 Year 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 21 Year 2007 concerning Eradication of Trafficking in Persons, Law Number 35 Year 2014 Concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection.

Protection for women and Children from acts of Violence is the obligation of the Regional Government in the context of regional autonomy, which is manifested in the form of Regional Regulations as regulated in Article 236 paragraph (1), Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government.

On that basis, the Regional Government of Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency has taken steps as efforts to provide Protection to women and Children, but has not been able to provide maximum and adequate Protection against acts of Violence that occur in the social life of the Community, especially to women and Children, among others caused by the still low understanding of the Community regarding the rights of women and Children, handling has not been well coordinated, implementation has not been sustainable, and so on. Therefore, a regulation in the form of a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children is needed to answer while providing legal certainty in the Protection of women and Children victims of acts of violence that occur.

The existence of a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children, gives responsibility to the Regional Government starting from Prevention of acts of violence to handling victims of acts of violence. In its implementation, the Regional Government cooperates with government agencies, other regional governments and the Community. In addition, adequate funding support both from the government, Regional Government and community participation from the business world and the Community, it is hoped that violence against women and Children can be reduced and even in time will be eliminated from Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency.

Physical violence is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Letter b
Psychological violence is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Letter c
Sexual violence is caused by:
1) acts in the form of sexual harassment;
2) forced sexual intercourse;
3) forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse; and/or
4) forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Letter d
Neglect is caused by:
1) acts that result in the unfulfillment of Children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social carried out by Parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
2) acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of Children as they should be carried out by Parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
3) acts of neglecting people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or agreement they must provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
4) acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Letter e
Maltreatment is maltreatment of the physical and emotional of a Child including neglecting their education and health and sexual abuse as a result of human misconduct toward Children.

Letter f
Exploitation is caused by:
1) acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
2) acts with or without the consent of the victim including prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or abilities of a person by other parties to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
3) all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or obscenity activities.

Letter g
Other violence is caused by:
1) threats of violence include: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and
2) coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.
