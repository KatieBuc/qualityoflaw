# CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

# BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY THE REGENT OF SOUTHEAST MINAHASA,

Considering:  
a. that the State guarantees every person including women and children the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and the right to obtain security and freedom from all forms of violence and discrimination as guaranteed by the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;  
b. that all forms of acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination;  
c. that violence against women and children in the Region continues to increase, so that protection efforts are needed for women and children victims of violence through institutional support and Regional Regulations that guarantee protection for women and children victims of violence;  
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

# CHAPTER I GENERAL PROVISIONS

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

1. Region is Southeast Minahasa Regency.
2. Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives in accordance with the Principle of Autonomy and assistance duties with the principle of the widest possible Autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
3. Regional Government is the Regional Head as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
4. Regent is the Regent of Southeast Minahasa.
5. Provincial Government is the Provincial Government of North Sulawesi.
6. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional People's Representative Institution of Southeast Minahasa Regency as an element of Regional Government administration.
7. Regional Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is a Regional Government Work Unit related to the implementation of child protection.
8. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the Regional financial plan that is discussed and jointly approved by the Regional Government and DPRD and stipulated by Regional Regulation.
9. Victim is a woman and child who experiences suffering and/or hardship either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
10. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
11. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow and develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence, exploitation, discrimination, and neglect.
12. Special protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
13. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
14. Guardian is a person or body who in fact exercises parental authority as a parent over a child.
15. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband, wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
16. Community is family, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.
17. Social Organization hereinafter referred to as Orsos is an institution/foundation/social association formed by the community whether legally incorporated or not legally incorporated that functions as a means of community participation in implementing Social Welfare Efforts (UKS).
18. Child Protection Social House hereinafter abbreviated as RSPA is a child protection service unit for child victims of violence and child trafficking as a continuation of temporary shelter that functions to provide protection, recovery, rehabilitation, advocacy and referral.
19. Social Services for Children are physical, mental and social services aimed at helping children to grow and develop properly.
20. Social Services for Neglected Children are social services for children whose parents do not have the ability to meet the needs of children to grow and develop properly.
21. Child-friendly school is an educational institution that recognizes and respects the rights of children to obtain education, health, opportunities to play and have fun, protection from violence and abuse, to express views freely, and to participate in decision-making according to their capacity.
22. Social Work Profession is a profession based on a framework of science, values and technical skills and can be used as a vehicle in the implementation of social welfare efforts.
23. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a unified unit that organizes integrated services for witnesses and/or victims of criminal acts of trafficking in persons as well as women and children victims of violence.
24. Violence Victim Protection Forum hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK is a coordination forum for prevention, handling and empowerment of victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is networked and established at the Regency, District and Village levels.

Article 2 The principles of Victim protection are:
a. respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. best interests of victims; and e. empowerment.

#### Article 3

The objectives of Victim Protection are:
a. to prevent violence against women and children;
b. to provide services to Victims; and c. to empower women victims of violence.

Article 4 The scope of protection for victims includes prevention, service, empowerment and handling efforts for victims of violence in the Region.

# CHAPTER II VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence against women and children include:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. domestic neglect;
e. exploitation; and f. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is that which results in pain, illness or serious injury.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c includes:
a. forced sexual intercourse committed against a person residing within the scope of that household and/or outside the scope of that household; and b. forced sexual intercourse against a person within the scope of their household with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Domestic neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is:
a. an act of neglecting a person within the scope of the household whereas according to the law applicable to the person concerned or because of an agreement or contract he/she is obliged to provide life, care or maintenance to that person. b. an act of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to him/her or because of an agreement or contract he/she is obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and c. an act that results in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:
a. an act that exploits economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. an act with or without the victim's consent which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and c. any form of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:
a. threat of violence includes: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, image, symbol, or body movement, whether or not using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; andb. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER III RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 12

Every Victim obtains the following rights: a. the right to have their dignity respected as a human being; b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim; c. the right to determine their own decision; d. the right to obtain information; e. the right to confidentiality of their identity; f. the right to compensation; g. the right to social rehabilitation; h. the right to complaint handling; i. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and j. the right to assistance.

#### Article 13

Child victims of violence in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 13, also obtain special rights, as follows:
a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;  
b. the right to basic services;  
c. the right to equal protection;  
d. the right to be free from various stigma; ande. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER IV OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One Regional Government

#### Article 14

1. The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to implement efforts to protect victims of violence, in the form of:a. establishing PPT;  
b. establishing FPKK;  
c. determining policies;  
d. preparing program and activity planning;  
e. providing facilities and infrastructure support; andf. allocating budget.  
2. In the implementation of the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government prepares a Regional Action Plan for the protection of victims of violence.

#### Article 15

The Regional Government provides services for victims of violence in the form of:
a. facilitating the establishment of PPT;  
b. facilitating PPT facilities and infrastructure according to regional financial capacity; andc. facilitating FPKK as a network forum for handling victims of violence.

#### Article 16

The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to:
a. supervise the implementation of services for victims of violence, with minimum service standards; andb. provide funds for victim protection through the APBD and/or other lawful regional financial sources.

#### Article 17

In implementing the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 14, Article 15 and Article 16, the Regent grants authority to the agency that has the main duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

## Part Two

Community

#### Article 18

In addition to the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 14, Article 15 and Article 16, the community has the following obligations and responsibilities:
a. preventing the occurrence of violence against women and children;  
b. reporting if violence occurs;  
c. protecting victims; andd. providing emergency assistance;

## Part Three

Family

#### Article 19

The Family is obliged and responsible to implement efforts to prevent and protect victims in the form of:
a. providing understanding about the prevention of domestic violence;  
b. supervising every family member;  
c. parents are prohibited from employing underage children in fulfilling the family economy; andd. parents must provide opportunities for school-age children to obtain adequate education.

##### CHAPTER V PROTECTION OF VICTIMS

#### Article 20

1. Efforts to prevent violence against women and children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the agency that has the main duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.  
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:a. forming a work network in efforts to prevent violence;  
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;  
c. forming a violence prevention system; andd. conducting socialization about laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 21

In addition to prevention efforts carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 20, prevention efforts must also be carried out by:
a. family and/or closest relatives;  
b. community;  
c. educational institutions; andd. other community institutions.

# CHAPTER VI INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER

#### Article 22

1. PPT is established by the Regional Government in the form of a Technical Implementation Unit (UPT).  
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is a functional work unit that has the duties, main tasks, and functions of providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.  
3. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is tasked with providing services and temporary protection in the form of a safe house for victims of violence.  
4. In the event that PPT does not have a safe house (shelter) as referred to in paragraph (3), then victims of violence are referred to PPT that has a safe house.  
5. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is established by Regional Regulation.

#### Article 23

1. The organizational structure of PPT established by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 22 paragraph (1) consists of:a. General Chairperson:b. Secretary:c. Treasurer; andd. Executive Chairperson who oversees fields.  
2. The fields as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, at a minimum include:a. complaint service field:b. health service field:c. social rehabilitation service field;  
d. repatriation and social reintegration field; ande. legal aid service field.  
3. In carrying out duties, PPT is responsible to the Regent through the Head of the agency that has the main duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 24

The complaint service field as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter a has the duties:
a. conducting interviews and observation of the victim's condition;  
b. making recommendations for further services;  
c. conducting coordination and referral to further services and related parties; andd. conducting administration of the complaint process.

#### Article 25

The health service field as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter b has the duties:
a. conducting examination, treatment, and further care for victims;  

b.conducting coordination of health rehabilitation implementation and medico-legal;  
c. conducting supporting examinations and laboratory tests on evidence;  
d. conducting consultation with specialist doctors or making referrals; ande. making case reports.

#### Article 26

The social rehabilitation service field as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter c has the duties:
a. providing assistance during the case handling process; andb. conducting counseling.

#### Article 27

The repatriation and social reintegration field as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter d has the duties:
a. conducting coordination with relevant agencies for victim repatriation;  
b. making progress reports on the repatriation assistance process and social rehabilitation; andc. conducting monitoring for at least three months after the victim is returned to their family.

#### Article 28

The legal aid service field as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter e has the duties:
a. accompanying and defending every legal service process; andb. making progress reports on legal handling.

#### Article 29

1. PPT established by the Regional Government has at least three counselors.  
2. Counselors as referred to in paragraph (1) include:a. counselor in the legal field;  
b. counselor in the health field; andc. counselor in the psychology field.

#### Article 30

Further provisions regarding the development, mechanism and networked working relationship of PPT shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part One

Services

#### Article 31

1. The provision of services to victims shall be carried out in an integrated manner by PPT.  
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.

#### Article 32

The provision of services to victims shall be implemented with:
a. speed;  
b. safety and comfort;  
c. sense of empathy;  
d. non-discrimination;  
e. easy accessibility;  
f. no charges; andg. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 33

Forms of services to victims include:
a. complaint, consultation, and counseling services;  
b. assistance services;  
c. health services;  
d. social rehabilitation services;  
e. legal services; andf. repatriation and social reintegration services.

#### Article 34

Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Article 33 letter a include:
a. initial identification or recording of victims; andb. informed consent.

#### Article 35

Assistance services as referred to in Article 33 letter b include:
a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;  
b. accompanying victims during the medico-legal process;  
c. accompanying victims during the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and court;  
d. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;  
e. protecting the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including coverage by mass media;  
f. coordinating with other assistants; andg. providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 36

Health services as referred to in Article 33 letter c include:
a. first aid to victims;  
b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel; andc. referral to health services.

#### Article 37

Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 33 letter d constitute services provided by assistants in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength, and independence in resolving their problems, by means of:
a. providing spiritual guidance to victims; andb. psychological recovery of victims.

#### Article 38

Legal services as referred to in Article 33 letter e to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by means of:
a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;  
b. accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and helping victims to fully describe the violence they experienced;  
c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer assistants, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 39

1. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 33 letter f aim to return victims to their families and social environment.  
2. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with:a. Regency/City Governments within one Provincial area; andb. related government and non-government agencies and institutions.

## Part Two Empowerment

### Paragraph 1 Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 40

Forms of empowerment of women victims of violence include:
a. job training;  
b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; andc. capital assistance.

#### Article 41

Job training as referred to in Article 40 letter a includes:
a. skills training;  
b. field work practice; andc. apprenticeship.

#### Article 42

Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups as referred to in Article 40 letter b include:
a. entrepreneurial skills training;  
b. facilitation of joint business group formation; andc. business implementation assistance.

#### Article 43

Capital assistance as referred to in Article 40 letter c includes:
a. work facilities and infrastructure assistance; andb. facilitation of working capital assistance.

### Paragraph 2 Fulfillment of Rights of Child Victims of Violence

#### Article 44

1. The Regional Government, PPT, and the community are obligated to fulfill the rights of child victims of violence.  
2. Forms of fulfillment of the rights of child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) include the fulfillment of children's basic rights according to their needs.

## Part Three

Coordination of Victim Protection

#### Article 45

1. In an effort to provide and organize service handling for victims, the Regional Government shall establish FPKK.

b. maintain and develop networks and referral systems; andc. collect, compile and present reports on violence.  
3. The management and membership of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent Decree.  
4. The membership of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be grouped into the following roles:a. health role;  
b. psychology role;  
c. legal role;  
d. social role; ande. economic role.  
5. Further provisions regarding the establishment, main duties, and functions as well as membership of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 46

2. Guidance and Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) include:a. coordination;  
b. guidance;  
c. education and training; andd. monitoring and evaluation.

# CHAPTER VIII FUNDING

#### Article 47

Funding for victim protection activities carried out by the Regional Government shall be charged to the APBD and/or other lawful and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 48

1. The community shall participate in the provision of protection to Victims.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of:a. establishing family partners at the sub-district/village level by the community;  
b. establishing women and child protection units within community organizations;  
c. conducting independent socialization of women's and children's rights;  
d. providing first aid to victims; ande. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

#### Article 49

3. Every person who directly witnesses violence as referred to in Article 5 and does not report it to the competent authorities shall be subject to criminal sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the applicable Laws and Regulations.

## I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and therefore their dignity and honour must be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Southeast Minahasa Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades their degree and human dignity, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.  

Thus far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection measures at the Regional level, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation.  

This Regional Regulation regulates protection measures for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for women and children victims of violence in Southeast Minahasa Regency.
