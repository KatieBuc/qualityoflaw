# CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

# REGENT OF LABUHANBATU SELATAN,

Considering:  
a. that violence against women and children in Labuhanbatu Selatan Regency continues to increase, causing community members to feel unsafe in carrying out their lives, thus requiring integrated protection efforts;  
b. that in order to optimize the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in Labuhanbatu Selatan Regency, it is necessary to provide direction, foundation, and legal certainty to all parties involved in the implementation of protection of women and children;  
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a and letter b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is South Labuhanbatu Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of South Labuhanbatu.
4. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on human differences on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life, both individual and collective, in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
5. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering, whether physical, sexual, psychological, and/or domestic neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of a person's freedom.
6. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a Child who is still in the womb.
7. Woman is a person who is female.
8. Violence against Women is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Violence against Children is any act that results in or may result in suffering of Children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.
10. Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences violence and/or threat of violence.
11. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the Regional Government, police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that Violence against Women will, is, or has occurred.
12. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from acts of Violence and discrimination.
13. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the Victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that Violence against the Victim will, is, or has occurred.
14. Companion is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to accompany Victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy, integrated for the strengthening and recovery of Violence Victims.
15. Gender is the difference in nature, role, function and status between Women and men which is not based on biological differences, but rather based on socio-cultural relations influenced by the broader structure of society.
16. Gender Mainstreaming is a strategy built to integrate Gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of policies, programs, and development activities in the region.
17. Office of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, hereinafter abbreviated as Dinas PPPA, is the office that administers the affairs of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
18. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated Service center in the effort to empower Women and Children established by the Regional Government with active participation of the Community which includes an information center, consultation center, complaint service, initial health care, counseling, legal assistance, repatriation and social reintegration, shelter/temporary safe house, as well as a referral center.
19. Safe House is a shelter/temporary protection house for Victims, during the accompaniment process, for the safety and comfort of Victims from threats and danger from perpetrators, and is only known to interested parties such as certain personnel at UPT P2TP2A for reasons of Victim safety and security.
20. Standard Operating Procedure, hereinafter abbreviated as SOP, is the standard steps that must be taken in protecting Victims starting from complaints/identification, rehabilitation, health, Social Rehabilitation, advocacy to repatriation and social reintegration of witnesses and/or Victims.
21. Family is the smallest unit in the Community consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their Child, or father and his Child, or mother and her Child or blood Family in a direct line upward or downward up to the third degree and/or if any up to the fourth degree.
22. Household is husband, wife, and Child, people who have Family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, foster care, representation, and/or Household workers and reside in that Household.
23. Community is individuals, Families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.
24. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget, hereinafter abbreviated as APBD, is the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of South Labuhanbatu Regency.
25. Social Rehabilitation is a process of refunctionalization and development to enable a person to be able to carry out social functions properly in Community life.

#### Article 2

Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect for Human Rights;
b. the right to life, survival, and development;
c. non-discrimination;
d. the best interests of the Child;
e. justice and Gender equality; and
f. victim protection.

#### Article 3

This Regional Regulation is intended to provide a legal basis for the Regional Government in providing protection for Women and Children from acts of Violence.

#### Article 4

This Regional Regulation aims to:
a. respect and protect Human Rights;
b. guarantee the rights of every Woman and Child to survival, growth and development;
c. non-discrimination;
d. protect Women and Children from threats, fear, torture or treatment that degrades the degree, self-esteem and dignity of humanity;
e. prevent forms of violence against Women and Children;
f. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against Women and Children;
g. protect and provide a sense of security for Women and Children;
h. provide services to Women and Children who are victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses;
i. protect and prevent all forms of discrimination against Women and Children in the region;
j. Women's Empowerment;
k. the best interests of the Child; and
l. realize a region that is suitable for Women and Children.

#### Article 5

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:
a. forms of Violence;
b. rights of Victims;
c. obligations and responsibilities;
d. implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;
e. coordination and cooperation;
f. participation of the Community and business world;
g. guidance and supervision;
h. reporting; and
i. sources of funds.

# CHAPTER II

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 6

Forms of Violence against Women and Children may include:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect; and
e. exploitation.

#### Article 7

Physical violence as referred to in Article 6 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

#### Article 8

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 6 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 9

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 6 letter c is caused by: a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;  
b. forced sexual intercourse;  
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or d. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 10

Neglect as referred to in Article 6 letter d is caused by: a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of a Child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for care; b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a Child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians or any other party responsible for their care; c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of their Household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the Victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 11

Exploitation as referred to in Article 6 letter e is caused by: a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  
b. acts with or without the consent of the Victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or Services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/orc. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the Victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 12

Every Victim has the right to receive:
a. protection;  
b. information;  
c. health services in accordance with medical needs;  
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;  
e. special handling related to the confidentiality of the Victim; f. assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations; g. guarantees of rights related to status as a Family member or member of the Community;andh. religious guidance services.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

General

#### Article 13

Obligations and responsibilities in the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence are the joint responsibility of: a. Regional Government;  
b. community;  
c. family; andd. parents or guardians.

## Part Two

Obligations and Responsibilities of Regional Government

#### Article 14

The Regional Government is obliged and responsible for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

#### Article 15

1. The Regional Government is obliged to:a. implement policies for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence established by the government;  
b. establish policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence; andc. cooperate with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, as well as educational institutions, health institutions, hospitals, and various NGOs concerned with Women and Children in the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.  
2. The Regional Government is responsible for providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.  
3. In order to carry out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, the Regional Government establishes programs and action activities for the protection of Women and Children in 1 (one) Regional Action Plan as a basis for regional apparatus in implementing protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.  
4. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3), is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD).  
5. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3), is further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 16

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Community as referred to in Article 13 letter b, are implemented in the form of Community participation.  
2. Forms of Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include: a. forming family partners at the Village/Sub-district level;  
b. forming women and children protection units within community organizations;  
c. conducting independent socialization of the rights of Women and Children; d. providing first aid to Victims; ande. reporting to the competent authority if Violence against Victims occurs in their environment.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.  
4. Forms of Community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 17

The obligations of the Family and/or parents as referred to in Article 13 letters c and d have full responsibility to prevent all forms of Violence and protect Women and Children as Family members.

# CHAPTER V

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 18

In order to provide Services and protection to Women and Children from acts of Violence, the Regional Government establishes P2TP2A.

## Part Two

Establishment of P2TP2A

#### Article 19

1. P2TP2A as referred to in Article 18 consists of elements of the Community, universities, professional organizations, community organizations concerned with the Community.  
2. The establishment of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Regent's decree.  
3. The implementation of Services to Victims is carried out in an integrated manner by Village/Sub-district P2TP2A, District P2TP2A, and Regency P2TP2A.  
4. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) has the duty to:a. receive complaints/reports of Violence against Women and/or Children, consultation, and counseling;  
b. receive and send to other Service units in a tiered manner;  
c. provide legal assistance;  
d. health;  
e. Social Rehabilitation;  
f. legal Services;  
g. repatriation and social reintegration; andh. provide protection to Victims, reporters and witnesses.  
5. P2TP2A may provide temporary services and protection in the form of a Safe House.  
6. In the event that P2TP2A does not yet have a Safe House as referred to in paragraph (5), then Victims of Violence are referred to P2TP2A that has a Safe House.  
7. P2TP2A consists of several Divisions consisting of:a. Complaints Service division;  
b. health Service division;  
c. legal aid consultation; andd. rehabilitation, Repatriation and Social Reintegration.

#### Article 20

1. In carrying out the task of Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (4), P2TP2A coordinates with the Sub-district P2TP2A.  
2. The mechanism for implementing tasks as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted in accordance with the SOP.  
3. Further provisions regarding the duties of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

Prevention Mechanism and Forms of P2TP2A Services

#### Article 21

The Regional Government shall prevent acts of Violence against Women and Children, through:
a. socialization activities of laws and regulations to the Community related to the protection of the rights of Women and Children;  
b. conducting empowerment and awareness to Families, parents and the Community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling. c. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;  
d. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social Services;  
e. opening employment opportunities for Women;  
f. building community participation and concern to carry out prevention and protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;  
g. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;  
h. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various community non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for Women and Children;  
i. opening complaint posts for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence; andj. training P2TP2A members related to the implementation of their duties in preventing Violence against Women and Children.

#### Article 22

1. Prevention of Violence against Women and Children as referred to in Article 21 shall be implemented by regional apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:a. social affairs;  
b. health;  
c. education;  
d. manpower;  
e. Women's empowerment and Child Protection;  
f. mental and spiritual; andg. peace and order.  
2. Prevention of Violence by regional apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in an integrated and continuous manner based on the Regional action plan.  
3. Further provisions regarding the mechanism for implementing prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 23

Forms of P2TP2A Services include:
a. Medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of Victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;  
b. Psychosocial services are services provided in order to restore the traumatic condition of Victims, including the provision of Safe Houses to protect Victims from various threats and intimidation for Victims and provide social support so that Victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in resolving their problems;  
c. Medico-legal services are forms of medical services for the purposes of evidence in the legal field;  
d. Legal services to assist Victims in undergoing legal processes; and e. Economic independence services in the form of skills training and providing economic access so that Victims can be independent.

## Part Four

Assistance Mechanism by Persons and/or Institutions Cooperating with P2TP2A

#### Article 24

1. Assistance for Women and Children Victims of acts of Violence shall be implemented by persons and/or institutions cooperating with P2TP2A.  
2. Assistance as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted according to the SOP.  
3. Further provisions regarding assistance as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Five

Services and Assistance

#### Article 25

The implementation of Services and assistance to Victims shall be carried out with the principles of:
a. fast, safe, and empathetic;  
b. guarantee of confidentiality;  
c. easily accessible; andd. free of charge.

#### Article 26

1. Forms of Services provided to Women and Children Victims of Violence are as follows:a. Complaint services;  
b. Health services;  
c. legal aid;  
d. repatriation;  
e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medico-legal;  
f. Identification services; andg. Psychological services.  
2. Forms of Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with minimum service standards established by the government and implemented by regional apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:a. social affairs;  
b. health;  
c. Women's empowerment and Child protection; andd. mental and spiritual.  
3. In carrying out the duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (2), the regional government cooperates with government agencies, provincial government, other district/city governments, the Community, Families and parents.  
4. Further provisions regarding the procedures for Services and handling of Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraphs (1), (2), and (3) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Six

Data and Information Management

#### Article 27

1. The Regional Government through the regional apparatus in charge of Women and Children shall compile data and information regarding the protection of Women and Children against acts of Violence.  
2. Data and information as referred to in paragraph (1) include: a. Women and Children who are Victims of acts of Violence; b. fulfillment of the rights of Women and Children;  
c. Women and Children at risk or vulnerable; andd. Women and Children who require special protection.  
3. In managing data and information on Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (2), it is mandatory to:a. compile a data and information system;  
b. create disaggregated data;  
c. conduct studies on the development of problems of Women and Children against acts of Violence; andd. publish data and information periodically that can be accessed by the public.  
4. As material for the completeness of data and information as referred to in paragraph (3), the regional apparatus in charge of the protection of Women and Children cooperates with and requests data from related agencies.

#### Article 28

1. Data and information as referred to in Article 27 shall become material for the formulation of policies, programs, budgets, monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of Women and Children against acts of Violence.  
2. The formulation of policies, programs, budgets, monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented with the mechanism of: a. making mapping, long-term, medium-term and short-term plans; b. making agreements on the periodization of monthly and annual work meetings; c. making agreements on the collection, mapping and presentation of data between Regional apparatus organizations;  
d. incorporating agreements into programs and budgets; and e. scheduling monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

#### Article 29

1. The regional apparatus in charge of Women and Children shall formulate service policies regarding the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.  
2. The formulation of service policies as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented with the mechanism of:a. formulating case reporting policies related to Women and Children who are Victims of acts of Violence;  
b. compiling reporting and referral mechanisms between regional apparatus organizations in charge of the protection of Women and Children against acts of Violence with related agencies and with P2TP2A; andc. compiling a budget for the implementation of policies for the protection of Women and Children against acts of Violence.

## Part Seven

Fulfillment of Facilities and Infrastructure

#### Article 30

1. Regional Government, Community and private sector provide facilities and infrastructure in the protection of Women and Children, among others providing breastfeeding places for Children, child-friendly play areas, recreation and creation places, Safe Houses, as well as establishing Integrated Service Centers for the Protection of Women and Children.
2. Facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (1) must meet the following criteria:a. ensure safety, comfort and health;
b. motivate Children's creativity; andc. contain educational elements.
3. Provision of facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (2) for the fulfillment of minimum service standards.
4. Provision of facilities and infrastructure by the Regional Government is carried out in stages adjusted to the financial capacity of the Region.

## Part Eight

Social Welfare

#### Article 31

1. Fulfillment of social welfare of Women and Children aims at prevention, reduction of vulnerability risks and handling of Victims of acts of Violence.
2. Fulfillment of social welfare of Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (1) is coordinated by the regional apparatus in charge of social affairs.

#### Article 32

1. Fulfillment of social welfare of Women and Children for prevention as referred to in Article 31 paragraph (1) includes:a. enhancement of the role of Regional Government towards the protection of Children including Children with special needs, Children with disabilities and other vulnerable Children;
b. raising awareness of the Community including Children towards the protection and participation of Women and Children;
c. capacity building of the Community, Family, including Children and social welfare institutions, regarding Child protection;
d. capacity building of protection services for Women and Children;
e. guarantee of continuity of education in accordance with the 12 (twelve) year Compulsory Basic Education program; andf. guarantee of obtaining basic health Services.
2. Fulfillment of social welfare of Women and Children for reduction of vulnerability risks, includes:a. identification and/or early detection of vulnerable Women and Children;b. mediation and counseling for vulnerable Women and Children;
c. services and facilitation of support for vulnerable Women and Children; andd. care support for Children who need alternative care.
3. Fulfillment of social welfare of Women and Children for handling Victims, includes:a. fulfillment of the rights of Women and Children who become Victims;b. referral services;
c. rehabilitation;andd. reintegration.
4. Handling of Child Victims as referred to in paragraph (3) have the right to receive protection from local mass media coverage both electronic and print to ensure the future of the Child.

## Part Nine

Gender Mainstreaming

#### Article 33

To implement the protection of Women and Children, the Regional Government conducts Gender Mainstreaming which includes:
a. providing guidance for regional apparatus in formulating Gender integration strategies carried out through planning, formulation, implementation, budgeting, monitoring and evaluation of development policies, programs and activities.b. developing Gender-based information systems;
c. facilitating institutional strengthening and development of Gender Mainstreaming mechanisms in government institutions, women's study centers, research and development institutions, as well as non-governmental institutions;d. implementing development, education, health, economy, law, Human Rights and politics, environment and social that takes into account Gender aspects and is child-friendly;
e. providing protection for Women especially protection for Victims of Violence, female workers, Women Victims of trafficking in persons, elderly Women, and Women with disabilities, and disaster-affected areas; andf. facilitating the strengthening and development of networks of community institutions or organizations and the business world for the implementation of Gender Mainstreaming.

#### Article 34

1. Every regional apparatus is obliged to conduct Gender-responsive budgeting planning.
2. Gender-responsive budgeting planning as referred to in paragraph (1) is coordinated by the regional apparatus in the field of Women and Children.

#### Article 35

1. The Regional Government may establish a Gender Mainstreaming Working Group.
2. The Working Group as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of members from regional apparatus and related vertical agencies as well as non-governmental institutions, universities, business sectors and religious-based institutions as well as Communities working for the protection of Women and Children.
3. The establishment of the Gender Mainstreaming Working Group as referred to in paragraph (2) is stipulated by Regent's Decree.
4. Each member of the Working Group as referred to in paragraph (2) may appoint a Gender Mainstreaming Focal Point.

#### Article 36

1. The duties of the Gender Mainstreaming Working Group as referred to in Article 35 paragraph (1), include:a. facilitating the implementation of Gender Mainstreaming to regional apparatus, Sub-districts, Urban Villages and Villages;
b. formulating work programs in the context of implementing Gender Mainstreaming;
c. developing work networks in accordance with duties and functions as well as authority to encourage the implementation of Gender Mainstreaming;d. carrying out socialization, advocacy, coordination, and training on Gender Mainstreaming;
e. raising and discussing Gender issues that may potentially cause gaps through Forum Group Discussion (FGD);f. collecting, formulating reports from SKPD, Sub-district Heads, Urban Village Heads, and Village Heads regarding the implementation of Gender Mainstreaming in their respective work units; andg. preparing and submitting reports on the implementation of Gender Mainstreaming to the Regent.
2. Further provisions regarding the duties of the Gender Mainstreaming Working Group as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent's Regulation.

## Part Ten

Social Behavior Change in Favor of Children

#### Article 37

1. Social behavior change is carried out with the aim of changing ways of thinking, attitudes and actions in accordance with parenting and education patterns that protect Children.
2. Behavior change is intended to change policies, programs and budget allocations along with their action plans to change the social behavior of individuals, groups, government and non-government institutions to implement Child protection.
3. The Regional apparatus authorized to implement behavior change as regulated in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) are Regional apparatus in charge of social affairs, religion, health, education, Community empowerment, population, manpower, information and communication as well as Child protection under the coordination of the Regional Apparatus Organization in charge of the protection of Women and Children.

#### Article 38

Forms of behavioral change that can be carried out to implement Child Protection as referred to in Article 37 paragraph (2) include: a. developing Children according to their abilities, talents, and interests;  
b. preventing marriage at child age;  
c. caring for, maintaining, educating and protecting Children;  
d. preventing brawls between students;  
e. providing opportunities for mothers to provide exclusive breastfeeding for Children; f. instilling noble character and respect for parents; g. instilling cultural identity, local language, love for the environment; and h. providing special protection to Children in emergency situations, Children in conflict with the law, Children from minority and isolated groups, Children exploited economically and/or sexually, Children who are trafficked, Children who are Victims of narcotics abuse, alcohol, psychotropic substances, and other addictive substances (drugs), Child Victims of kidnapping, sale and trafficking, Child Victims of Violence both physical and/or mental, Children with disabilities, and Child Victims of abuse and neglect, Child Victims of HIV/AIDS transmission and Child Victims of social conflict.

## Part Eleven

Child-Friendly Regency Development Policy

#### Article 39

1. Child-Friendly Regency Development aims to build Regional Government initiatives that lead to efforts to transform the concept of children's rights into policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights.  
2. Child-Friendly Regency Development Policy as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented based on principles that include: a. good governance;  
b. non-discrimination;  
c. culture and local wisdom;  
d. best interests of the Child; and e. respect for the views of Children;  
f. inclusive; and g. multi-cultural.  
3. Child-Friendly Regency Development Policy is directed at strengthening institutions for the fulfillment of children's rights and special protection, including: a. civil rights and freedoms;  
b. rights to Family environment and alternative care;  
c. rights to basic health and Child welfare;  
d. rights to education, leisure time and cultural activities; and e. rights to special protection.  
4. Further provisions regarding Child-Friendly Regency Development as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 42

#### Article 43

3. Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of: a. socialization to employees within the company environment regarding the dangers of acts of Violence against Women and Children; and b. assistance as a form of corporate social responsibility.

#### Article 44

3. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;  
b. technical guidance and training;  
c. provision of facilities;  
d. monitoring; and e. evaluation.  
4. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out with the principles of: a. professional;  
b. transparent; and c. accountable.

#### Article 45

##### CONCERNING

##### PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Violence against Women and Children constitutes a violation of Human Rights and therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed without discrimination. Based on empirical evidence it is revealed that Women and Children are groups that often become victims of violence. Many factors cause the occurrence of violence against Women and Children including the patriarchal cultural factor that still occurs widely in society which views Women as lower than men. In addition, wrong perceptions about violence against Women and Children are also still commonly found, which consider violence as normal and as the right of the perpetrator. Violence against Women and Children can be seen from the type, perpetrator, location of incident, time, age and consequences of the act of violence. Violence against Women and Children above is general and has no relevance to the type of education, occupation and income, social position, religion and belief, nationality, ethnicity and race attached to men and Women. This means that in all types of social strata, violence against Women and Children can and continues to occur as long as the imbalance in the relationship between men and Women is still believed and manifested in social life. In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in South Labuhanbatu Regency so that they are protected from Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, a legal umbrella is needed in the form of regulations in the Region.

So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence have not yet regulated protection efforts in the Region, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation.

The drafting of this Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is intended to provide a legal basis for the Regional Government in providing protection for Women and Children in the South Labuhanbatu Regency area.

#### Article 2

Point a What is meant by "the principle of respect for human rights" is that the implementation of protection for Women and Children victims of violence is to ensure the fulfillment of the rights of victims of violence as a manifestation of respect for human rights.

Point c What is meant by "Non-discriminatory" is an attitude and treatment toward victims by not making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Point f What is meant by "the principle of protection of victims' Rights" is the protection of personal self, Family, honor, dignity, and property under their control, a sense of security and protection from threats of fear, freedom from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity.
