# WEST JAVA PROVINCE

# REGIONAL REGULATION OF KARAWANG REGENCY

# NUMBER 2 OF 2017

# CONCERNING

# EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION OF WOMEN

# BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

# THE REGENT OF KARAWANG,

Considering:  
a. that women as one of the community groups whose existence is a potential and asset for development need to be empowered so that they can actualize their potential optimally to participate in development;  
b. that women as an asset of the nation who play a role in the process of continuation and creation of quality generations need to receive guarantees for the fulfillment of their rights and protection from acts of violence and discrimination;  
c. that in order to make effective a comprehensive and integrative system in empowering and protecting women in Karawang Regency, it is necessary to regulate the empowerment and protection of women;  
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Empowerment and Protection of Women;  

In View of:  
1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;  

2. Law Number 14 of 1950 concerning the Establishment of Regency Regions within West Java Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 9) as amended by Law Number 4 of 1968 concerning the Establishment of Purwakarta Regency and Subang Regency by amending Law Number 14 of 1950 concerning the Establishment of Regency Regions within West Java Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 1968 Number 31, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 2851);  
3. Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 1984 Number 29 Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3277);  
4. Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419);  
5. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number
5679. .

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Karawang Regency.  
2. Regent is the Regent of Karawang.  
3. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration who lead the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and DPRD in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.  
5. Women are human beings of female sex starting from infants, adolescents, adults up to the elderly.  
6. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social, knowledge and skills capabilities so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.  
7. Women's Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties, either temporarily or based on court determination.  
8. Task force is a semi-permanent working group, consisting of related regional apparatus work units, community, universities, and/or community organizations chaired by the Regional Secretary, who are given additional tasks within a certain period of time, to implement certain programs/activities in the field of women's empowerment and protection.  
9. Gender Mainstreaming is a strategy carried out rationally and systematically to achieve and realize gender equality and justice in a number of aspects of human life (household, community and state), through policies and programs that take into account the experiences, aspirations, needs and problems of women and men in the planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of all policies and programs in various fields of life and development.

# CHAPTER II

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 2

Every woman has the right:
a. to live and improve her standard of living;  
b. to security;  
c. to a good and healthy environment;  
d. to have a family and continue offspring;  
e. to work and receive fair wages for her work;  
f. to obtain proper education;  
g. to obtain care, education, training and/or special assistance;  
h. to personal freedom; andi. to health.

#### Article 3

Women's Rights in Employment include:
a. Having equal employment opportunities with men; b. Equal opportunities from the selection process, work facilities, benefits to the right to receive equal wages; c. Obtaining paid leave including maternity leave; andd. Women cannot be dismissed by the employer on the grounds of pregnancy or marital status.

#### Article 4

Women's rights in the field of Health include:
a. Freedom from death during childbirth; b. Obtaining health services especially family planning services, pregnancy, childbirth and post-childbirth.

#### Article 5

Women's rights in the field of education are to obtain opportunities to attend education and receive scholarships.

#### Article 6

Women's rights in marriage and family are to: a. Choose her husband freely;  
b. Marriage is based on the consent of both parties;  andc. Have the same rights and responsibilities both as parents to their children and as husband and wife.

#### Article 7

Women's rights in public and political life are the right to vote and be elected and to have equal opportunities to participate in the formulation of government policies up to their implementation.

#### Article 8

1. Women's empowerment is implemented to improve the position, role and quality of Women in family, community, and national life.  
2. Women's Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) is directed so that Women:a. Obtain their opportunities and rights as human beings;  
b. Are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and security activities;  andc. Have equality in enjoying the results of development.

#### Article 9

1. Women who are victims of acts of violence have the right to receive protection, information, integrated services and continuous handling.  
2. Continuous handling as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out up to the rehabilitation stage.  
3. Continuous handling as referred to in paragraph (2) may be carried out by individuals, groups, regional government institutions, and/or non-governmental institutions.

# CHAPTER III

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 10

The Regional Government facilitates the establishment of Women's Empowerment and Protection institutions in the Region.

#### Article 11

1. The Regent establishes policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women to eliminate all forms of discrimination and acts of violence against women.  
2. The implementation of policies, programs and activities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Regional Apparatus responsible for the empowerment and protection of women.  
3. The Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (2) may cooperate with the community, universities, community organizations, other regency/city governments, and/or international institutions in the empowerment and protection of women activities.  
4. The form of cooperation as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be carried out through:a. policy analysis;  
b. implementation coordination;  
c. advocacy;  
d. socialization;  
e. public communication;  
f. information dissemination;  
g. education;  
h. training;  
i. service facilities;  
j. service provision;  
k. model development; andl. other activities.  
5. The integration of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be set forth in the documents:a. Regional long-term development plan;  
b. Regional medium-term development plan; c. Regional Apparatus strategic plan;  
d. Regional Government work plan; ande. Regional Apparatus work plan and budget.  
6. The provisions regarding the establishment of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 12

1. The implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women in the Region as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (2) shall be carried out by forming, developing, strengthening or utilizing task forces, forums, working groups or other institutions.  
2. The membership of task forces, forums, working groups or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of relevant Regional Apparatus, community, universities and/or community organizations.  
3. The provisions regarding the formation and membership of task forces, forums, working groups or other institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 13

1. The provision of services for the empowerment and protection of women as referred to in Article 11 paragraph (4) letter j, shall include:a. identification of victims of criminal violence;  
b. legal assistance;  
c. medical rehabilitation;  
d. psychosocial rehabilitation;  
e. social reintegration;  
f. repatriation assistance; andg. other forms.  
2. Services for the empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided to women:a. victims of violence;  
b. victims of human trafficking;  
c. in conflict-prone and disaster areas;  
d. workers;  
e. elderly;  
f. persons with disabilities;  
g. victims of sexual exploitation;  
h. who are heads of households;  
i. pregnant and/or breastfeeding; andj. other vulnerable victims.

# CHAPTER IV

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 14

1. The community may participate in the implementation of the empowerment and protection of women.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, groups and/or social community organizations.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) may take the form of:a. cooperation;  
b. active role in policy formulation; andc. complaints/reports.  
4. Further provisions regarding the procedures for community participation as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 15

1. The Regional Government shall conduct monitoring to ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the empowerment and protection of women.  
2. The monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women in the Region.  
3. The monitoring as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of the Regional Apparatus implementing the policies, programs and activities implementing the policies, programs, and activities for the empowerment and protection of women. (4) The monitoring as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be carried out from the planning stage, up to the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women for the current year.

#### Article 16

1. The Regional Government shall conduct an evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the empowerment and protection of women at the end of each fiscal year in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
2. The results of the evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be used as input for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women for the following year.

# CHAPTER VI

## FUNDING

#### Article 17

Funding for the implementation of the empowerment and protection of women shall be sourced from:
a. Regional revenue and expenditure budget; andb. Other legitimate and non-binding sources, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 18

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all Regional laws and regulations relating to the empowerment and protection of women shall remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to the provisions in this Regional Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 19

The implementing regulations of this Regional Regulation must be stipulated no later than 1 (one) year from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

#### Article 20

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Karawang Regency.  

Enacted in Karawang on 12 April 2017
