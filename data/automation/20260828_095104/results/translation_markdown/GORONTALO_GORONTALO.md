##### CHAPTER I

##### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Gorontalo Regency.
2. Regent is the Regent of Gorontalo.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs which are the authority of the autonomous Region.
4. Regional Governance is the administration of government affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
5. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.
6. Woman is a person who is female.
7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.
8. Violence against women is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Violence against children is any act that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.
10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly.
11. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
12. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
13. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.
14. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
15. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the Region to provide services in the context of empowerment of women and children victims of acts of violence.
16. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers of perpetrators.
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
18. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, foster care, and those within the household.
19. Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner.

#### Article 2

Protection of Victims is based on:
a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of victims,
b. justice and gender equality,
c. non-discrimination,
d. the best interests of victims, and
e. empowerment.

#### Article 3

Protection of Victims aims to:
a. prevent violence against women and children,
b. provide services to Victims, and
c. empower women victims of violence.

#### Article 4

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes prevention, service and empowerment efforts for victims of violence in the Region.

# CHAPTER II

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence include:
a. physical violence,
b. psychological violence,
c. sexual violence,
d. neglect,
e. exploitation, and/or
f. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a causes injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse,
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care,
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should be carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care,
c. acts of neglecting people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person, and/or,
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others,
b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits, and/or
c. any form of exploitation of sexual organs or other organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:
a. threats of violence which include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict the fundamental freedom of a person, and
b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

Il. GENERAL Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.  

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Gorontalo Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection to women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.  

So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation.  

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in Gorontalo Regency.
