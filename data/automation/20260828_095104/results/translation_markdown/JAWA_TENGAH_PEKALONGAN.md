4. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
5. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

Protection against Gender is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, regional government, or other parties who know or hear that violence against men or women will or has occurred either temporarily or based on court determination.
8. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
9. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which are and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
10. Violence against children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which are and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
11. Victims of gender-based violence are people who because of their gender experience physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from State policies, acts of violence and or threats of violence within the scope of the household and society.
12. Integrated Services are a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unit of implementation, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and jdih.pekalongankab.go.id legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
13. Companion is a person or institution and/or body that has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
14. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a single unit that organizes integrated services for women and children victims of violence.
15. Medical Services are facilities that provide services that are clinical in nature in the field of diagnostics, and/or inpatient care for victims.
16. Social rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial condition disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning both in the family and in society.
17. Legal Aid is the provision of legal assistance to victims seeking justice who are unable and face difficulties in the legal field outside or before the court without remuneration.
18. Repatriation is the act of returning victims to their region of origin or country of origin while prioritizing protection services and fulfillment of their needs.
19. Social Reintegration is the reunification of victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
20. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers of perpetrators.
21. Service Standard is a benchmark used as a guideline for service delivery and a reference for assessing service quality as an obligation and promise of the organizer to the community in the context of quality, appropriate, easy, affordable and measurable services.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:
a. non-discrimination;
b. best interests of victims;
c. justice and gender equality;
d. victim protection;
e. maternal survival;
f. child survival, growth, and development;
g. respect for the child's opinion;
h. transparency;
i. integration;
j. not blaming the victim;
k. empowering;
l. victim confidentiality;
m. decision-making in the hands of the victim.

#### Article 3

The purpose of implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence is to provide services and protection to men and women victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence that occurs within the household and/or outside the household.

# CHAPTER III

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 4

1. Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement efforts to prevent violence in the form of:a. providing data and information on gender and child victims of violence in accordance with laws and regulations;  
b. conducting education on anti-violence values based on gender and violence against children; and c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection against gender-based violence and child victims of violence.  

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id (2) To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government has the obligation to provide and organize services for victims in the form of: a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving elements of SKPD, Agencies, Institutions, Community Organizations, Communities;  
b. providing facilities and infrastructure;  
c. increasing the capacity of service provider institutions;  
d. conducting coordination and cooperation in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children;  
e. conducting monitoring and evaluation.  f. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for victims.  
3. The Regional Government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

# CHAPTER IV

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

1. Forms of Gender-Based Violence include a. Physical Violence;  
b. Psychological Violence;  
c. Sexual Violence;  
2. Forms of Violence against Children include  a. Physical Violence;  
b. Psychological Violence;  
c. Sexual Violence;  
d. Neglect;  
e. Exploitation;  
f. Maltreatment

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or cause death.

#### Article 7

Psychological Violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter b is caused by acts that result in jdih.pekalongankab.go.id fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to the victim.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment both physical and psychological;  
b. forced sexual intercourse;  
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/ord. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of the child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of the child as appropriate carried out by parents, guardians, any other party responsible for their care;  
c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/ord. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  
b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs jdih.pekalongankab.go.id or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/orc. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities or indecency.

#### Article 11

Maltreatment as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter f is caused by:
a. any act or action whether intentional or unintentional committed by another person that makes an individual sick or disturbed in their feelings, or receives an unpleasant feeling that makes someone sad, disappointed, annoyed, angry and afraid.  b. any sexual violation committed or permitted to be committed against a young person by an adult or another person legally responsible for them, including touching a child with the intention of sexual gratification or forcing a child to touch an adult, sexual intercourse, showing sexual activities to a child, pornography or allowing a child to engage in sexual intercourse that is not appropriate to their development.

# CHAPTER V

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 12

Every victim of gender-based violence and children receives the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as a human being;  
b. the right to determine their own decisions;  
c. the right to obtain information;  
d. the right to confidentiality of their identity;  
e. the right to complaint handling;  
f. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;  
g. the right to obtain facilities in the judicial process;  
h. the right to obtain efforts for repatriation and social reintegration of victims of gender-based violence and children.  i. the right to social rehabilitation.

### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.

In order to prevent and address gender-based violence and violence against children in Pekalongan Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for gender and child victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation. So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of gender and child victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts at the regional level, so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for gender and child victims of violence in Pekalongan Regency.
