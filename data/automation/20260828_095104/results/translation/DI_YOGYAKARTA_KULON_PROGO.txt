PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF KULON PROGO,

Considering:
a. that violence against women and children is an act that violates the rights and dignity of women and children as human beings;
b. that protection efforts for women and children victims of violence have not been optimal, and require institutional strengthening and regulations that can guarantee its implementation;

c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a and b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

Article 2

Letter a What is meant by "respect for victims' rights" is the guarantee of fulfillment of victims' basic rights.

Letter b What is meant by "justice" is a state where women and gender equality are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to obtain access, as well as welfare;

What is meant by "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

Letter c What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims without making distinctions on the basis of age, sex, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d What is meant by "the best interests of victims" is all the best actions concerning victims carried out by the Regional Government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, so that the best interests of victims must be the primary consideration.

Article 36

Paragraph (1) Letter a Sufficiently clear Letter b What is meant by "medicolegal" is to provide optimal professional medical services that utilize medical science to assist in law enforcement and justice.

Medicolegal services include among others: visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum.

What is meant by "visum et repertum" is a written statement made by a doctor in forensic medical science at the request of an authorized investigator regarding the results of medical examination of the victim based on their expertise and under oath, for the purposes of judicial proceedings.

What is meant by "visum et psikiatrikum" is a statement given by a Psychiatrist regarding the mental health condition of the victim required to clarify a case and for the purposes of judicial proceedings.
