REGIONAL REGULATION OF EAST LUWU REGENCY

NUMBER 4 OF 2021

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AGAINST VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF EAST LUWU,

Considering:
a. that in order to fulfill the constitutional rights of women to be free from torture and treatment that degrades human dignity and to improve the quality of life, women have the right to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence;
b. that Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, provides affirmation of the need for protection and provision of a sense of security to women;
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, and letter b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women Against Violence;

In View of:
1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

2. Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1999 Number 165, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3886);

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is East Luwu Regency.
2. Regent is the Regent of East Luwu.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
4. East Luwu Regency Regional House of Representatives hereinafter referred to as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of regional government administration.
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and the Regency Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Regency Region.
6. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a regional technical implementation unit established by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.
7. Woman is a person (human) who has genitals, can menstruate, become pregnant, give birth to children, and breastfeed. 8. Victim is a Woman who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
9. Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights from various types of acts of violence by providing consistent and systematic attention.
10. Service is an activity or series of activities in the context of fulfilling the needs of victims of violence.
11. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without using means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body, or causes the deprivation of a person's freedom.
12. Violence against women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, economic exploitation, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom arbitrarily, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
13. Prevention is all efforts made by government and non-government institutions aimed at providing a sense of security to women.
14. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim, when seeing, hearing and knowing of, during or after the occurrence of violence against the victim.
15. Assistance is all forms of efforts made by related institutions to conduct counseling, therapy, advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
16. Social rehabilitation service is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person who experiences social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly
17. Legal aid service is legal services provided by advocates to victims of violence.
18. Repatriation service is an effort to return victims of violence from abroad to the debarkation point/entry point or from the receiving area/area where violence occurred to the area of origin or family party, family/substitute institution, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of the needs of victims of violence.
19. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family parties or communities that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
20. Social Worker is a State Civil Apparatus who is given the task, responsibility, authority and rights in full by the authorized official to implement social welfare in the government agency environment or other social organizations.
21. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with the determined operational standards.
22. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
23. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children.

CHAPTER II

PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

The purpose of this regional regulation is to provide protection for women and provide a sense of security in the fulfillment of women's rights.

Article 3

This Regional Regulation aims to:
a. prevent all forms of violence against women; b. protect women against violence;
c. guarantee the fulfillment of the rights of women victims of violence;
d. provide services to women victims of violence; and e. realize community participation in the implementation of protection of women.

CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 4

Women victims of violence have the right to:
a. have their dignity respected as human beings;
b. obtain information on the protection of women;
c. obtain social rehabilitation and reintegration;
d. obtain recovery of physical, psychological and sexual health according to the suffering experienced by victims of violence;
e. obtain empowerment and legal assistance; and f. spiritual guidance services.

CHAPTER IV

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

Part One

General

Article 5

1. Protection of women victims of violence is organized by the Regional Government.
2. Protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by the Regional Apparatus in charge of women.

Article 6

Provision of services in the protection of women victims of violence is implemented by UPTD PPA;

Part Two

Prevention of Violence

Article 7

1. In the protection of women, prevention of violence is carried out. (2) Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by: a. socializing laws and regulations;
b. providing education on the dangers of violence against women; c. conducting seminars/workshops or the like;
d. establishing cooperation with government agencies or non-government institutions in efforts to prevent violence; and e. establishing a prevention system, mapping of locations or areas prone to violence.
3. Prevention of violence as referred to in paragraph (2) may coordinate with other institutions.

Part Three

Services for Women Victims of Violence

Article 8

Services to victims of violence are implemented based on the principles of:  a. no fees charged;
b. fast;
c. safe and comfortable;
d. sense of empathy;
e. non-judgmental;
f. non-discriminatory; and g. guaranteed confidentiality.

Article 9

Types of services to victims of violence include:
a. complaint and consultation services;
b. assistance services;
c. legal aid services;
d. health services;
e. social rehabilitation services;
f. repatriation services; and g. social reintegration services.

Article 10

1. Each type of service follows standard operational procedures. (2) Each type of service must have a form.
3. The form as referred to in paragraph (2) aims to achieve administrative order and data collection.
4. The form as referred to in paragraph (2) is provided by UPTD PPA.

Article 11

1. Services as referred to in Article 9 may be carried out in cooperation with the central government, provincial government, other regency/city governments, communities, and/or other social institutions.
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is set forth in a cooperation agreement.

Paragraph 1

Complaint and Consultation Services

Article 12

Complaint and consultation services as referred to in Article 9 letter a, include:
a. conducting the service administration process for reporters;
b. conducting interviews with reporters; and c. consent to take action.

Paragraph 2

Assistance Services

Article 13

1. Accompaniment Services as referred to in Article 9 letter b, are carried out in every type of health service, rehabilitation, legal aid, social reintegration, and repatriation.
2. Accompaniment is carried out by experts who have expertise in conducting counseling, therapy and/or advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of women victims of violence.
3. Experts who provide accompaniment as referred to in paragraph (2) cooperate with UPTD PPA.
4. Experts as referred to in paragraph (2) must be provided by the Regional Apparatus responsible for women.
5. Experts as referred to in paragraph (2) consist of: a. health workers;
b. advocates;
c. social workers;
d. spiritual counselors; ande. psychologists or psychiatrists.

Paragraph 3

Legal Aid Services

Article 14

1. Legal aid services as referred to in Article 9 letter c, are carried out by advocates.
2. Legal aid services include litigation and/or non-litigation criminal law matters.
3. Legal aid services as referred to in paragraph (1) include exercising power of attorney, accompanying, representing, defending, and/or performing other legal actions for the legal interests of victims.
4. Implementation of legal aid services is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Paragraph 4

Health Services

Article 15

1. Health services as referred to in Article 9 letter d, are carried out by health workers.
2. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out at health facilities owned by the regional government or private sector.
3. Health services as referred to in paragraph (2) aim to restore the condition of victims.
4. Restoration of the condition of victims as referred to in paragraph (3) includes:a. first aid to victims; andb. follow-up services in the form of outpatient care, inpatient care according to medical provisions.

Article 16

1. Health workers are obliged to create medical records in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. Every medical action performed by health workers must obtain informed consent for medical action from the victim or their family in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Paragraph 5

Social Rehabilitation Services

Article 17

1. Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 9 letter e, are carried out by social workers, spiritual counselors and psychologists or psychiatrists.
2. Social rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out in a safe house or other place determined by UPTD PPA.
3. Social rehabilitation services are carried out by:a. providing spiritual guidance to victims;
b. building empathic communication with victims;
c. providing counseling services according to the needs of victims. d. restoration of the psychological condition of victims carried out by psychologists and/or psychiatrists; ande. psychological therapy on a regular basis.

Article 18

Social rehabilitation services carried out by social workers, spiritual counselors and psychologists or psychiatrists must obtain consent from the victim or their family.

Paragraph 6

Repatriation Services

Article 19

1. Repatriation Services as referred to in Article 9 letter f, are carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with relevant agencies.
2. Repatriation as referred to in paragraph (1) must obtain the consent of the victim.

Paragraph 7

Social Reintegration Services

Article 20

1. Social reintegration services as referred to in Article 9 letter g, are carried out by UPTD PPA together with other agencies and the community.
2. Social reintegration as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by:a. reuniting victims with family and community; b. monitoring/follow-up guidance; andc. conducting monitoring for at least 3 (three) months after the victim returns to their family.

CHAPTER V

COOPERATION

Article 21

1. In order to achieve the objectives of protecting women from acts of violence, the Regional Government may cooperate with: a. Ministries;
b. Provincial Regional Government;
c. Regency/City Regional Government;
d. Universities; ande. Non-governmental institutions.
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: a. exchange of data and information;
b. rehabilitation of victims of violence;
c. repatriation and social reintegration; andd. provision of evidence and witnesses.

CHAPTER VI

GUIDANCE, SUPERVISION AND EVALUATION

Article 22

The regional government is obliged to conduct guidance, supervision and evaluation of the protection of women.

Part One

Guidance

Article 23

1. Regional government guidance includes:a. guidelines for the protection of women; andb. technical guidance and training.
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by the regional government at least once a year.

Part Two

Supervision

Article 24

1. The Regent supervises the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women.
2. Implementation of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) the Regent conducts monitoring through the Regional Apparatus handling the field of women.
3. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women for the current year.
4. Monitoring results as referred to in paragraph (3) are reported to the Regent every 6 (six) months.

Part Three

Evaluation

Article 25

1. The Regional Government conducts an evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women at the end of each fiscal year.
2. Evaluation results as referred to in paragraph (1) are used as input for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women in the following year.
3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VII

PARTICIPATION OF THE COMMUNITY AND PRIVATE SECTOR

Article 26

1. The Regional Government in protecting women victims of violence, may involve the participation of the community and private sector.
(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, groups or community organizations.

Article 27

1. Individuals, groups and community organizations participating in the protection of women as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (2), are provided guidance by the Regional Government through the Regional Apparatus responsible for women.
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in the form of technical guidance and training.

Article 28

Participation of the private sector is based on a cooperation agreement with UPTD PPA.

Article 29

The community may supervise the protection of women by conveying aspirations to the Regent through the regional apparatus responsible for women or the DPRD.

CHAPTER VIII

REPORTING

Article 30

1. The protection of women must report activities for the protection of women victims of violence periodically to the Regent. (2) The Regent as referred to in paragraph (1) is obliged to report activities for the protection of women victims of violence periodically to the Governor.

CHAPTER IX

AWARDS

Article 31

1. The Regional Government may give awards to any person and/or institution that is fully committed to giving full attention to the protection of women.
2. Awards as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:a. certificates; andb. other forms.

CHAPTER X

FINANCING

Article 32

Financing for the protection of women is sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; andb. Other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER XI

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 33

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by its placement in the Regional Gazette of East Luwu Regency.

Enacted in Malili on 12 April 2021

I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide Protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. Every citizen has the right to live free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, and to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence. Acts of violence, especially against women, constitute a violation of human rights. Women in this case are among vulnerable groups, who tend to experience Violence and therefore need to receive Protection. Violence against Women is a violation of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and/or psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, Violence against Women is any act based on gender differences that results in misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. The existence of Women as Victims of Violence has not yet received adequate services so that minimum services are needed for women victims of violence.

In order to prevent and overcome acts of Violence against Women in East Luwu Regency, it is necessary to provide Protection for Women Victims of Violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women Victims of Violence, particularly in terms of services, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of Women Victims of Violence in East Luwu Regency.
