 This Law defines the legal, economic, social and organizational foundations of the activities of state bodies, local self-government bodies, organizations and citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan for the prevention of domestic violence.  

 Article 1 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version)

### Article 1

Basic concepts used in this Law This Law uses the following basic concepts: 1) victim — a natural person in respect of whom there are grounds to believe that moral, physical and (or) property harm has been directly caused to him or her by domestic violence; 1-1) assistance organizations - legal entities providing victims with special social services and (or) assistance in accordance with this Law; 2) family-domestic relations - relations between spouses, former spouses, persons living or having lived together, close relatives, persons having a common child (children); 3) domestic violence — an intentional unlawful act (action or inaction) of one person in the sphere of family-domestic relations in respect of another (others), causing or containing a threat of causing physical and (or) mental suffering; 4) prevention of domestic violence — a complex of legal, economic, social and organizational measures carried out by subjects of prevention of domestic violence, aimed at protecting the constitutional rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of man and citizen in the sphere of family-domestic relations, preventing and suppressing domestic violence, as well as identifying and eliminating the causes and conditions contributing to their commission; 5) subjects of prevention of domestic violence — state bodies, local self-government bodies, organizations and citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan carrying out the prevention of domestic violence. See: Recommendation of the International Council under the Supreme Court of the Republic of Kazakhstan on studying international experience in sentencing for sexual and domestic violence, harassment (approved by protocol decision of the International Council under the Supreme Court of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated June 5, 2020)

### Article 2

Legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the prevention of domestic violence 1. The legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the prevention of domestic violence is based on the Constitution of the Republic of Kazakhstan, consists of this Law and other regulatory legal acts of the Republic of Kazakhstan. 2. If an international treaty ratified by the Republic of Kazakhstan establishes rules other than those contained in this Law, the rules of the international treaty shall apply.

### Article 3

Principles of prevention of domestic violence The prevention of domestic violence is based on the principles of: 2) guaranteeing observance of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of man and citizen; 3) inadmissibility of causing physical and (or) mental suffering to man and citizen; 4) support and preservation of the family; Subparagraph 6 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 20.04.23 No. 226-VII (entered into force on July 1, 2023) (see previous version) 6) individual approach to each man and citizen in need of special social services; 7) priority of preventive measures for the prevention of domestic violence over repressive ones; 8) complexity and consistency.  
 Article 4 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version)

### Article 4

Types of domestic violence 1. Domestic violence may be expressed in the form of physical, psychological, sexual and (or) economic violence. 2. Physical violence — intentional harm to health through the use of physical force and infliction of physical pain. 3. Psychological violence — intentional impact on the psyche of a person, humiliation of honor and dignity by means of threats, insults, blackmail or coercion (inducement) to commit offenses or acts that pose a danger to life or health, as well as leading to violation of mental, physical and personal development. 4. Sexual violence — intentional unlawful act encroaching on the sexual integrity or sexual freedom of a person, as well as actions of a sexual nature in relation to minors. 5. Economic violence — intentional deprivation of a person of housing, food, clothing, property, means to which he or she has a right provided by law. See: Recommendation of the International Council under the Supreme Court of the Republic of Kazakhstan on studying international experience in sentencing for sexual and domestic violence, harassment (approved by protocol decision of the International Council under the Supreme Court of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated June 5, 2020)

### Article 5

Special social services Paragraph 1 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 20.04.23 No. 226-VII (entered into force on July 1, 2023) (see previous version) 1. A victim recognized in the manner established by law as a person in need of special social services shall be provided with special social services. 2. Special social services include a guaranteed volume of special social services and paid special social services. 3. Provision of the guaranteed and additional volume of special social services provided in excess of the guaranteed volume is carried out on the basis of a decision of local executive bodies of districts, cities of regional significance. Paragraph 4 is set forth in the wording of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 20.04.23 No. 226-VII (entered into force on July 1, 2023) (see previous version) 4. Standards for the provision of special social services, the procedure for their provision, the rights and obligations of a person (family) in need of special social services are determined by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on social protection.

## Chapter 2. Subjects of prevention of domestic violence

### Article 6

Competence of the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan The Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan: 1) develops the main directions of state policy in the field of prevention of domestic violence; 2) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see old ed.) 3) ensures interaction of subjects of prevention of domestic violence and coordination of their activities; 4) exercises other powers provided for by the Constitution, laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan and acts of the President of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### Article 7

Competence of local representative and executive bodies 1. Local representative bodies: 1) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see old ed.) 2) approve and monitor the execution of local budgets in terms of expenditures on prevention of domestic violence; 3) facilitate the implementation by citizens and organizations of the norms of this Law. 2. Local executive bodies: 1) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see old ed.) 2) ensure interaction of subjects of prevention of domestic violence at the local level; 3) create organizations for providing assistance and ensure their functioning; 4) identify and maintain records of minors who have suffered from domestic violence and disadvantaged families; Subparagraph 5 has been amended in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 20.04.23 No. 226-VII (entered into force on July 1, 2023) (see old ed.) 5) organize the provision of special social services to victims in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on social protection; Paragraph supplemented with subparagraph 6 in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 24.11.21 No. 75-VII 6) ensure the analysis of the needs of students and pupils who have been subjected to domestic violence in the provision of special social services in accordance with the standards for providing special social services and submit proposals to the authorized body in the field of education.

### Article 8

Commissions on women's affairs and family-demographic policy Commissions on women's affairs and family-demographic policy: 1) interact with subjects of prevention of domestic violence on issues of prevention of domestic violence; 2) submit proposals to local executive bodies on the adoption of organizational and practical measures aimed at preventing domestic violence; 3) jointly with organizations for providing assistance organize and conduct activities for the prevention of domestic violence and rehabilitation of victims.

### Article 9

Competence of commissions on minors and protection of their rights Commissions on minors and protection of their rights: 1) interact with subjects of prevention of domestic violence on issues of prevention of domestic violence among minors; 2) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see old ed.) 3) submit proposals to local executive bodies on the adoption of organizational and practical measures aimed at preventing domestic violence among minors; 4) jointly with organizations for providing assistance organize and conduct activities for the prevention of domestic violence and rehabilitation of minors who have suffered from domestic violence.  
 Article 10 has been amended in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see old ed.)

### Article 10

Competence of internal affairs bodies Internal affairs bodies: 1) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see old ed.) 2) participate in the development of draft regulatory legal acts in the field of prevention of domestic violence; 3) implement measures for the prevention of domestic violence; 4) identify parents or persons replacing them who do not fulfill or improperly fulfill their obligations to raise children, committing unlawful acts against them; 5) maintain preventive records and carry out preventive control; 6) consider applications and reports on facts of domestic violence or threats of their commission with departure to the scene and take measures to suppress them; 7) at the request of victims, refer them to organizations for providing assistance or healthcare organizations; 8) conduct preventive conversations; 9) carry out the delivery of a person who has committed domestic violence to internal affairs bodies; 10) issue a protective order; 11) petition the prosecutor for authorization to extend the term of validity of a protective order; 12) carry out administrative detention; 13) petition the court to establish special requirements for the behavior of a person who has committed domestic violence; 14) carry out criminal prosecution, proceedings on cases of administrative offenses; 15) apply measures of criminal procedural coercion; 16) apply criminal-legal measures of victim safety; 17) ensure the organization of special training courses on issues of prevention of domestic violence for employees of internal affairs bodies; 18) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 13.01.14 No. 159-V (see old ed.)

### Article 11

Competence of the authorized body in the field of education The authorized body in the field of education: 1) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see old ed.) Subparagraph 2 set forth in the wording of the Law of the RK dated 24.11.21 No. 75-VII (see old ed.) 2) ensures monitoring of the analysis conducted by local executive bodies of the needs of students and pupils who have been subjected to domestic violence in the provision of special social services in accordance with the standards for providing special social services; 3) participates in the development of regulatory legal acts in the field of prevention of domestic violence; 4) develops and implements in the practice of educational organizations programs and methods aimed at forming law-abiding behavior of students and pupils of educational organizations.

### Article 12

Competence of the authorized body in the field of healthcare The authorized body in the field of healthcare: 1) develops and implements in the practice of healthcare organizations methodological recommendations for providing medical and psychological assistance to victims; 2) participates in the development of draft regulatory legal acts in the field of prevention of domestic violence; 3) excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 07.07.20 No. 361-VI (see old ed.)

### Article 13

Competence of healthcare organizations Healthcare organizations: 1) provide narcological, psychological, psychiatric, therapeutic and preventive assistance and conduct medical rehabilitation of victims and persons who have committed domestic violence; 2) notify internal affairs bodies of facts of victims' applications and provision of medical assistance to them; Subparagraph 3 set forth in the wording of the Law of the RK dated 07.07.20 No. 361-VI (see old ed.) 3) implement measures to prevent non-medical use of psychoactive substances.

### Article 15

Assistance organizations 1. Assistance organizations: 1) receive victims; 2) organize the provision of necessary psychological, pedagogical, medical, legal assistance to victims, and if necessary refer them to healthcare organizations for medical assistance and further rehabilitation; 3) in accordance with their capabilities provide temporary accommodation to victims; 3-1) provide special social services to victims, and also apply on their behalf to local executive bodies of districts, cities of regional significance with an application for the provision of a guaranteed and (or) additional volume of special social services provided in excess of the guaranteed volume; 4) conduct psycho-correctional programs with persons who have committed domestic violence; 5) inform internal affairs bodies about facts of domestic violence or threats of their commission; 6) carry out legal education work on issues of prevention of domestic violence; 7) carry out interaction with natural and legal persons on issues of prevention of domestic violence. Assistance provided for in subparagraphs 1), 2), 3), 4) and 5) of part one of this paragraph is provided to victims regardless of place of residence. 2. Assistance organizations are created by local executive bodies, as well as natural and legal persons in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan. 3. Financing of the activities of assistance organizations is carried out at the expense of budgetary funds and other sources not prohibited by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

## Chapter 3. Measures for the prevention of domestic violence

### Article 16

Regulation of relations arising in the application of measures for the prevention of domestic violence Regulation of relations arising in the application of measures for the prevention of domestic violence is carried out in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the prevention of offenses, taking into account the features established by this chapter.

### Article 17

Measures of individual prevention of domestic violence 1. Measures of individual prevention of domestic violence are applied for systematic purposeful influence on the legal consciousness and behavior of a person who has committed domestic violence, in order to prevent the commission of new offenses on his part and ensure the safety of the victim. 2. Measures of individual prevention of domestic violence are: 2) delivery to internal affairs bodies of a person who has committed domestic violence, for drawing up a protocol on an administrative offense or issuing a protective order; 4) administrative detention; 5) compulsory measures of a medical nature; 6) establishment of special requirements for the behavior of the offender; 7) administrative penalty; 8) deprivation or restriction of parental rights, cancellation of adoption of a child, release and removal of guardians and trustees from the performance of their duties, early termination of an agreement on the transfer of a child for upbringing to a foster parent; 9) measures of procedural coercion and security measures for victims in criminal proceedings; 10) measures taken by court sentence. 3. Measures of individual prevention of domestic violence are determined taking into account the individual characteristics of the person in respect of whom they are applied, the nature and degree of public danger of the offenses committed by him. 4. A decision on the application of measures of individual prevention may be appealed by interested persons in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### Article 18

Grounds for taking measures of individual prevention of domestic violence 1. The ground for taking measures of individual prevention of domestic violence is one of the following circumstances: 1) receipt of an application or report from natural and legal persons; 2) direct detection by an employee of an internal affairs body of the fact of commission of domestic violence or an attempt to commit it; 3) materials received from state bodies and local self-government bodies. 2. Applications and reports on the commission of domestic violence or the threat of its commission are considered by state bodies in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### Article 19

Preventive conversation 1. The main tasks of a preventive conversation are to identify the causes and conditions of the commission of domestic violence, explain the social and legal consequences of domestic violence and convince of the need for law-abiding behavior. 2. A preventive conversation is conducted by a subject of prevention of domestic violence with a person who has committed domestic violence, or in respect of whom there are grounds for taking measures of individual prevention of domestic violence. 3. A preventive conversation is conducted in the official premises of subjects of prevention of domestic violence, as well as at the place of residence, study, work or directly at the place of commission of domestic violence, and may not continue for more than one hour. 4. A person with whom a preventive conversation is conducted is warned of the need to cease unlawful actions. 5. A preventive conversation with a minor is conducted in the presence of his parents, teachers or other legal representatives.

### Article 20

Protective order Paragraph 1 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 05.07.14 No. 236-V (see previous version); the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 02.11.15 No. 388-V (entered into force on January 1, 2016) (see previous version); set forth in the version of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 12.07.18 No. 180-VI (see previous version) 1. In order to ensure the safety of the victim and in the absence of grounds for administrative detention in the manner prescribed by the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan on Administrative Offences, or detention in the manner prescribed by Article 128 of the Criminal Procedure Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, a protective order is issued by the heads of the territorial police body, local police service of the internal affairs body, their deputies, district police inspectors, inspectors for juvenile affairs and for the protection of women from violence, taking into account the opinion of the victim, which is handed over for execution to the person who committed domestic violence or from whom a threat of its commission emanates, against signature. In case of refusal to sign, a record of this is made in the protective order. The victim must be provided with a copy of the protective order against signature on the day of its issuance with an explanation of his or her rights, as well as the legal consequences in case of violation of the protective order by the person against whom it was issued. A copy of the protective order within twenty-four hours from the moment of its delivery to the person against whom it was issued is sent to the prosecutor. 2. A protective order is issued against a sane person who has reached the age of sixteen at the time of its issuance. 3. A protective order prohibits committing domestic violence, searching for, pursuing, visiting, conducting oral, telephone negotiations and entering into contact by other means with the victim against his or her will, including minor and (or) incapacitated members of his or her family. 4. The protective order indicates: the time and place of its issuance, by whom and against whom it was issued, the place, time and circumstances of the commission or threat of commission of domestic violence, the established restrictions in relations with the victim, the legal consequences in cases of continuation of unlawful actions and violation of the protective order. 5. The term of validity of the protective order is thirty days from the moment of its delivery to the person against whom it was issued. The frequency of verification is at least once every seven calendar days. 6. Excluded in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version) 7. Violation of a protective order entails liability established by the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan on Administrative Offences. 8. A person against whom a protective order has been issued is placed on preventive registration by the internal affairs bodies and preventive control is exercised over him or her.

### Article 21

Administrative detention 1. In order to suppress domestic violence containing the elements of an administrative offence, and if there are grounds to believe that the issuance of a protective order is insufficient to ensure the safety of the victim, an official of the internal affairs bodies carries out administrative detention of the person who committed domestic violence, which consists in his or her temporary deprivation of freedom of action and movement with forced detention in a special facility. 2. The procedure, terms of administrative detention, rights and obligations of persons subjected to administrative detention are determined by the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan on Administrative Offences.  
 Article 22 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version)

### Article 22

Establishment of special requirements for the behavior of the offender 1. In order to ensure the safety of the victim, the court may establish special requirements for the behavior of the person who committed domestic violence. 2. The establishment of special requirements for the behavior of the offender is a measure of administrative and legal impact and is applied both in conjunction with the imposition of an administrative penalty and instead of it when releasing a person who committed an administrative offence from administrative liability. 3. A person against whom special requirements for behavior have been established may be prohibited from: 1) searching for, pursuing, visiting, conducting oral, telephone negotiations and entering into contact by other means with the victim against his or her will, including minor and (or) incapacitated members of his or her family; 2) acquiring, storing, carrying and using firearms and other types of weapons; The paragraph was supplemented with subparagraph 3 in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 12.07.18 No. 180-VI; set forth in the version of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 07.07.20 No. 361-VI (see previous version) 3) non-medical use of psychoactive substances. In exceptional cases, for the protection and defense of the victim and members of his or her family, the court has the right to also apply a measure of administrative and legal impact in the form of a prohibition for the person who committed domestic violence to reside in an individual residential house, apartment or other dwelling with the victim in case this person has another dwelling. 4. During the term of validity of special requirements for the behavior of the offender, he or she may be obliged to appear at the internal affairs bodies from one to four times a month for a preventive conversation. 5. The procedure for establishing special requirements for the behavior of the offender, the terms of its validity, the rights and obligations of participants in proceedings on administrative offences are determined by the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan on Administrative Offences. 6. A person against whom special requirements for behavior have been established is placed on preventive registration by the internal affairs bodies and preventive control is exercised over him or her.

### Article 23

Measures of procedural coercion and security measures in criminal proceedings Paragraph 1 is set forth in the version of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 03.07.14 No. 227-V (see previous version) 1. In order to suppress domestic violence containing the elements of a criminal offence, and to ensure the safety of the victim, witness and other persons participating in criminal proceedings, members of their families and close relatives, measures of procedural coercion and procedural security measures are applied by the bodies carrying out criminal prosecution and conducting criminal proceedings. 2. The grounds for applying measures of procedural coercion and procedural security measures, the procedure for their application, the rights and obligations of persons participating in criminal proceedings are determined by the Criminal Procedure Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

## Chapter 4. Final provisions

### Article 24

set forth in the version of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 11.07.17 No. 91-VI (see previous version)

### Article 24

Supervision over compliance with the law in the field of prevention of domestic violence Supreme supervision over compliance with the law in the field of prevention of domestic violence is carried out by the prosecutor's offices.

### Article 25

Observance of confidentiality in providing assistance to victims Information about the personal and family life of victims is confidential information and is not subject to disclosure. Disclosure of confidential information by a person who, by virtue of his or her official position, became aware of the fact of domestic violence, entails liability established by the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### Article 26

Procedure for Entry into Force of this Law This Law shall enter into force upon the expiration of ten calendar days after its first official publication.
