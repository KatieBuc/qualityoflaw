CRIMINAL CODE OF THE REPUBLIC OF UZBEKISTAN
of September 22, 1994 No. 2012-XII (as amended on 10-05-2023)

General part
Section one. General provisions

## Chapter I. Tasks and principles of the Criminal code

#### Article 1. Criminal legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan

The criminal legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan is based on the Constitution and the universally recognized norms of international law and consists of this Code.

#### Article 2. Tasks of the Code

The Criminal Code has the task of protection from criminal encroachments on the person, their rights and freedoms, the interests of society and the state, property, the environment, peace, the security of mankind, and also the prevention of crimes, education of citizens in the spirit of observance of the Constitution and the laws of the republic.  
For the implementation of these tasks the Code determines the bases and the principles of responsibility, what socially dangerous acts are crimes, establishes punishments and other measures of legal impact which can be applied to persons who committed socially dangerous acts.

#### Article 3. Principles of the Code

The Criminal Code is based on the principles of legality, equality of citizens before the law, democracy, humanity, justice, guilty responsibility, inevitability of responsibility.

#### Article 4. Principle of legality

Crime, punishability of an act and other consequences in law of its commission are determined only by the Criminal Code.  
Nobody can be found guilty of committing a crime and be subject to punishment otherwise than according to a court verdict and according to the law. A person found guilty of committing a crime has the rights and performs the duties established by the law.

#### Article 5. Principle of equality of citizens before the law

Persons who committed crimes have identical rights and obligations and are equal before the law without distinction as to sex, race, nationality, language, religion, social origin, beliefs, personal and social standing.

#### Article 6. Principle of democracy

Public associations, self-government institutions of citizens or collectives can be involved in the cases provided by the law in the correction of persons who committed crimes.

#### Article 7. Principle of humanity

Punishment and other measures of legal impact do not aim at causing physical suffering or humiliation of human dignity.  
To a person who committed a crime a penalty shall be imposed or other measure of legal impact which is necessary and sufficient for their correction and the prevention of new crimes is applied.  
Severe measures of punishment can be appointed only on condition that the goals of punishment cannot be achieved by means of application of softer measures provided by the relevant article of the Special part of this Code.

#### Article 8. Principle of justice

Punishment or other measure of legal impact applied to a person guilty of committing a crime shall be fair, that is correspond to the gravity of the crime, degree of fault and public danger of their personality.  
Nobody can bear responsibility for the same crime twice.

#### Article 9. Principle of guilty responsibility

A person is subject to responsibility only for those socially dangerous acts in the commission of which their guilt, in the procedure established by the law, will be proved.

#### Article 10. Principle of inevitability of responsibility

Each person in whose act the presence of corpus delicti is established shall be subject to responsibility.

## Chapter II. Limits of operation of the Code

#### Article 11

Operation of the Code concerning persons who committed crimes in the territory of Uzbekistan
A person who committed a crime in the territory of Uzbekistan is subject to responsibility under this Code.  
As a crime committed in the territory of Uzbekistan it is necessary to recognize such an act which:
a)	is begun, ended or interrupted in the territory of Uzbekistan;  
b)	is committed outside Uzbekistan, and the criminal result occurred on its territory;  

c)	is committed in the territory of Uzbekistan, and the criminal result came beyond its limits;  
d)	forms in total or along with other acts a crime which part is committed in the territory of Uzbekistan.  
In case of committing a crime on an air, ocean or river craft which is outside Uzbekistan and not in the territory of a foreign state, responsibility comes under this Code if the said vessel is flying the flag or is registered to the port of Uzbekistan.  
The question of responsibility of foreign citizens who according to current laws, international treaties or agreements are not subject to the jurisdiction of courts of Uzbekistan, in case of commission of a crime by them in the territory of the Republic of Uzbekistan is resolved on the basis of rules of international law.

#### Article 12

Operation of the Code concerning persons who committed crimes outside Uzbekistan
Citizens of the Republic of Uzbekistan, and also persons without citizenship who are permanently living in Uzbekistan for crimes committed in the territory of another state are subject to responsibility under this Code if they were not punished according to the court verdict of the state in the territory of which the crime was committed.  
A citizen of Uzbekistan cannot be extradited for a crime committed in the territory of a foreign state if otherwise is not provided by international treaties or agreements.  
Foreign citizens, and also persons without citizenship who are not permanently living in Uzbekistan for crimes committed outside its limits are subject to responsibility under this Code only in the cases provided by international treaties or agreements.

#### Article 13. Operation of the law in time

Crime and punishability of an act is determined by the law existing during its commission. Time of commission of a crime is recognized as the time of commission of a socially dangerous act if an Article of this Code determines the moment of the termination of the crime as the moment of commission of action or failure to act. Time of commission of a crime is recognized as the time of occurrence of criminal consequences if an Article of this Code determines the termination of the crime as the moment of occurrence of such consequences.  
The law eliminating the criminality of an act, commuting penalty or otherwise improving the provision of a person, has retroactive force, that is extends to persons who committed the corresponding act before entry into force of such law including to persons serving or who served a sentence, but having a criminal record.  
The law establishing the criminality of an act, strengthening punishment or otherwise worsening the situation of a person, has no retroactive force.  
Section second. Bases of responsibility

## Chapter III. Crime

#### Article 14. Concept of crime

Crime is recognized as a guilty socially dangerous act (action or failure to act) forbidden by this Code under the threat of punishment.  
An act which causes or creates a real threat of harm to the objects protected by this Code is recognized as socially dangerous.

#### Article 15. Classification of crimes

Crimes depending on nature and degree of public danger are subdivided into: not constituting great public danger; less serious; serious; especially serious.  
Intentional crimes for which the law prescribes custodial sanction not exceeding three years, and also crimes committed through negligence for which the law prescribes custodial sanction not exceeding five years belong to crimes not constituting great public danger.  
Intentional crimes for which the law prescribes custodial sanctions for a term of more than three years, but not exceeding five years, and also crimes committed through negligence for which the law prescribes custodial sanction for a term of more than five years belong to less serious crimes.  
Intentional crimes for which the law prescribes custodial sanction for a term of more than five, but not exceeding ten years belong to serious crimes.  
Intentional crimes for which the law prescribes custodial sanction for a term exceeding ten years or lifelong imprisonment belong to especially serious crimes.

#### Article 16. Responsibility for crime and its basis

Responsibility for crime is the legal consequence of commission of a socially dangerous act which is expressed in condemnation, application of punishment or other measures of legal impact by a court to a person guilty of crime commission.  
The basis of responsibility is commission of an act containing all elements of the actus reus provided by this Code.

## Chapter IV. Persons which are subject to responsibility

#### Article 17. Responsibility of physical persons

Physical, responsible persons who before crime commission had attained sixteen years are subject to responsibility.  
Persons who before crime commission had attained fourteen years are subject to responsibility for crimes provided by Articles 97, 98, 104 - 106, 118, 119, parts four - eight of Article 126-1,
Articles 137, 164 - 166, 169, parts two and three of Article 173, Articles 220, 222, 247, 252, 263, 267, 271, parts two and three of Article 277 of this Code.  
Persons who before crime commission had attained eighteen years are subject to responsibility for crimes provided by Articles 122, 123, 125-1, parts one and two of Article 126-1, Articles 127, 127-1, 128-1, 144, 146, 193-195, 205-210, 225, 226, 230-
232, 234, 235, 279-302 of this Code.  

Responsibility of persons who committed crime aged under eighteen years arises according to general provisions and taking into account the features provided by Section six of the General part of this Code.

#### Article 18. Sanity

A person who during crime commission understood the socially dangerous nature of the actions (failure to act) and directed them is responsible.  
A person who during commission of a socially dangerous act was in a state of insanity is not subject to responsibility, that is could not realize the significance of the actions (failure to act) or direct them owing to chronic or temporary mental disturbance, weak-mindedness or other mental disturbance.  
To a person who committed a socially dangerous act in a state of insanity, compulsory measures of medical nature can be appointed by a court.

#### Article 18-1

Responsibility of person suffering from mental disturbance which does not exclude sanity
A responsible person who during crime commission owing to mental disturbance could not fully realize the significance of the actions (failure to act) or direct them, is subject to responsibility.  
To a person suffering from mental disturbance which does not exclude sanity, by a court along with punishment compulsory measures of medical nature can be appointed

#### Article 19. Responsibility for crime committed in state of intoxication

A person who committed a crime in alcohol intoxication or under the influence of drugs, their analogs, psychotropic or other substances influencing intellectual and volitional activities is not exempted from liability. Such condition cannot be the basis for recognition of a person as insane.

## Chapter V. Guilt

#### Article 20. Fault forms

A person can be found guilty of a crime if the socially dangerous act provided by this Code was committed intentionally or through negligence.

#### Article 21. Intentional crime

A crime the completion of which this Code determines by the moment of accomplishment of a socially dangerous act is recognized as intentional if the person who committed it understood the socially dangerous nature of the act and wished its commission.  
A crime the completion of which this Code determines by the moment of occurrence of socially dangerous consequences can be committed with direct or indirect intent.  
A crime is recognized as committed with direct intention if the person who committed it understood the socially dangerous nature of the act, anticipated its socially dangerous consequences and wished their occurrence.  

A crime is recognized as committed with indirect intent if the person who committed it understood the socially dangerous nature of the act, anticipated its socially dangerous consequences and consciously allowed their occurrence.

#### Article 22. Careless crime

A careless crime is recognized as a socially dangerous act committed through overconfidence or negligence.  
A crime is recognized as committed through overconfidence if the person who committed it anticipated the possibility of occurrence of socially dangerous consequences of the behavior provided by the law and, consciously without observing precautionary measures, groundlessly calculated that these consequences would not occur.  
A crime is recognized as committed due to negligence if the person who committed it did not anticipate the possibility of occurrence of socially dangerous consequences of the behavior provided by the law though it should and could have anticipated them.

#### Article 23. Crime with complex fault

If as a result of intentional crime commission a person causes other socially dangerous consequences through negligence with which the law connects increased responsibility, such crime is recognized as committed intentionally.

#### Article 24. Innocent causation

An act is recognized as committed innocently if the person who committed it did not understand, should not and could not understand the socially dangerous nature of the act or did not anticipate its socially dangerous consequences and based on the circumstances of the matter should not and could not anticipate them.

## Chapter VI. Unfinished crime

#### Article 25. Preparation for crime and attempted crime

Preparation for crime is recognized as an act of a person creating conditions for commission or concealment of an intentional crime interrupted prior to its commission on circumstances not dependent on it.  
Attempted crime is recognized as the beginning of commission of an intentional crime, unfinished on circumstances independent from the person.  
Responsibility for preparation and attempt arises under the same Article of the Special part of this Code as for a completed crime.

#### Article 26. Voluntary refusal of crime commission

Voluntary refusal of crime is recognized as the termination by a person of preparatory actions or the termination of crime commission if the person understood the opportunity to finish the crime, and also prevention of occurrence of criminal result if the person understood the possibility of its occurrence.  
Voluntary refusal of crime commission excludes responsibility.  

A person who voluntarily refused finishing the crime to the end is subject to responsibility under this Code if the act actually committed by it comprises all elements of another actus reus.

## Chapter VII. Partnership in crime

#### Article 27. Concept of partnership in crime

Partnership is recognized as joint participation of two or more persons in commission of an intentional crime.

#### Article 28. Types of assisting offenders

Assisting offenders along with perpetrators, organizers, instigators and aiders are recognized.  
The perpetrator is a person who fully or partially directly committed a crime or a crime with the use of other persons not subject to liability under this Code, or by other means.  
The organizer is a person who directed the preparation or execution of a crime.  
The instigator is a person who incited the execution of a crime.  
The aider is a person who promoted the execution of a crime by advice, instructions, provision of means or removal of obstacles, and also by promising in advance to hide the criminal, the tools, traces or means of crime execution or objects obtained by criminal means, and equally by promising in advance to acquire or sell such objects.

#### Article 29. Partnership forms

Forms of partnership in crime are recognized: simple partnership; complex partnership; organized group; criminal society.  
Simple partnership is participation in the execution of a crime by two or more persons without prior agreement.  
Complex partnership is participation in the execution of a crime by two or more persons by prior agreement.  
Organized group is preliminary consolidation of two or more persons in a group for joint criminal activities.  
Criminal society is preliminary consolidation of two or more organized groups for engaging in criminal activities.

#### Article 30. Limits of responsibility for partnership in crime

Organizers, instigators and aiders bear responsibility under the same Article of the Special part of this Code as perpetrators.  
Organizers, and also members of groups by prior agreement, organized groups and criminal societies bear responsibility for all crimes in the preparation or commission of which they took part.  

Persons who created an organized group or criminal society or directed them bear responsibility for all crimes committed by the criminal formation if they were covered by their intention.  
A person who committed an act which is not covered by the intention of other accomplices bears responsibility for that act.  
The voluntary refusal of the organizer, instigator or aider excludes responsibility for partnership in crime if the person timely took all measures depending on it for its prevention.

#### Article 31. Accessory to crime

Failure to report to authorities about a crime authentically known to be preparing, being committed or committed, not promised in advance, attracts responsibility only in the cases provided by Articles 155-1 and 241 of this Code.  
Concealment of the criminal, the tools and means of crime execution, traces of crime or objects obtained by criminal means, not promised in advance, attracts responsibility only in cases stipulated in Article 241 of this Code.  
Close relatives of the suspect, the person accused or the defendant are not subject to responsibility for failure to report or concealment not promised in advance.

## Chapter VIII. Plurality of crimes

#### Article 32. Frequency of crimes

Frequency is the commission of two or more crimes occurring at different times provided by the same part, Article, and in cases specially specified in this Code by different Articles of the Special part for one of which the person was not convicted. Both completed crimes, and punishable preparations or attempts at crimes, and also crimes committed in partnership are recognized as repeated.  
The crime is not recognized as repeated if the person is exempted for earlier committed acts from liability or punishment.  
A crime consisting of a number of identical criminal actions covered by common intent and directed to a single purpose and constituting in total one continued crime is not recognized as repeated.  
A crime consisting in prolonged failure to perform a duty, characterizing continuous implementation of the structure of one continuing crime is not recognized as repeated.

#### Article 33. Cumulative offenses

Cumulative offenses is the commission of two or more criminal actions provided by different Articles or different parts of the same Article of the Special part of this Code for one of which the person was not convicted and for which it is subject to responsibility.  

If the act committed by a person contains signs of crimes provided by different parts of the same Article of the Special part of this Code, responsibility comes under that part of the Article which establishes more severe punishment.

#### Article 34. Recurrence of crimes

Recurrence is the committing by a person of a new intentional crime after conviction for an earlier committed intentional crime.  
Dangerous recurrence is the committing by a person of a new intentional crime identical to that for which it was convicted earlier, and in cases specially specified in this Code under other Articles of the Special part.  
Especially dangerous recurrence is the committing by a person of a new intentional crime for which it is sentenced to imprisonment for a term of at least five years:
a)	for an especially serious crime if it was convicted for an especially serious crime or twice for serious crimes earlier and for each of them custodial sanction for a term of at least five years was imposed;  
b)	for a serious crime if it was twice convicted for serious crimes or in any sequence for a serious and especially serious crime earlier and for each of them custodial sanction for a term of at least five years was imposed.  
A person according to the court verdict can be recognized as an especially dangerous recidivist.  
By consideration of the question of recognition of a person as an especially dangerous recidivist, criminal records on sentences of courts of other states can also be considered.  
By consideration of the question of recognition of a person as an especially dangerous recidivist, the criminal record for crimes committed by it at the age of up to eighteen years and also criminal records extinguished or removed in the procedure established by law is not considered.  
Articles of the Special part of this Code providing responsibility for crime execution by an especially dangerous recidivist are applied in cases when the person was recognized in the procedure established by law as an especially dangerous recidivist before commission of this crime.  
Section third. The circumstances excluding crime of act

## Chapter IX. Concept and types of the circumstances excluding crime of act

#### Article 35. Concept of the circumstances excluding crime of act

Circumstances under which an act containing the signs provided by this Code is not a crime, in view of lack of public danger, illegality or fault are recognized as excluding crime.  
The circumstances excluding crime are recognized: insignificance of act; justifiable defense; emergency; infliction of harm during detention of a person who committed a socially dangerous act; execution of an order or other obligation; justified professional or economic risk; commission of an act as a result of physical or mental compulsion or threat.

#### Article 36. Insignificance of act

An action or failure to act which falls under signs of an act provided by this Code as a crime, but not having public danger owing to insignificance is not a crime.

#### Article 37. Justifiable defense

An act committed in a state of justifiable defense is not a crime, that is in case of protection of the personality or rights of the defender or another person, interests of society or the state against unlawful encroachment by causing harm to the encroacher if at the same time the limits of justifiable defense were not exceeded.  
Exceeding the limits of justifiable defense is recognized as explicit discrepancy of the defense to the nature and danger of the encroachment.  
The right to justifiable defense belongs to a person irrespective of the available opportunity to ask for the help of other persons or authorities or to avoid the encroachment in a different way.  
Intentional provocation of encroachment for the purpose of causing harm is not justifiable defense.

#### Article 38. Emergency

An act which caused harm to rights and interests protected by law, committed in a state of emergency, that is for elimination of danger threatening the personality or rights of this person or other citizens, interests of society or the state, if the danger under these circumstances could not be eliminated by other means and if the damage suffered is less considerable than that prevented, is not a crime.  
An act committed in a state of emergency is lawful if the person did not exceed its limits.  
Exceeding the limits of emergency is causing damage to rights and interests protected by law if the danger could be eliminated by other means or if the damage suffered is more considerable than that prevented.  
In case of assessment of legitimacy of an act committed in a state of emergency, the nature and degree of the prevented danger, reality and proximity of its approach, the actual ability of the person to prevent it, their state of mind in the circumstances and other facts of the case are considered.  
Voided according to the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan of 09.01.2018 No. ZRU-459

#### Article 39. Causing harm during detention of person who committed socially dangerous act

Causing harm during detention of a person who committed a socially dangerous act for the purpose of transferring them to the authorities is not a crime if at the same time the measures necessary for detention were not exceeded.  
Exceeding measures of detention is explicit discrepancy of the means and methods of detention to the danger of the act and the person who committed it, and also the detention situation, as a result of which harm not caused by the need for detention is intentionally inflicted on the person.  

In case of assessment of legitimacy of causing harm during detention of a person who committed a socially dangerous act, their actions to avoid detention, the force and ability of the detainer, their state of mind and other circumstances connected with the fact of detention are considered.  
Along with persons who are specially authorized for that, victims and other citizens also have the right to detain a person who committed a socially dangerous act.

#### Article 40. Execution of the order or other obligation

Causing harm in case of lawful execution of an order or other instruction, and equally job responsibilities, is not a crime.  
A person who committed a crime under an obviously criminal order or other instruction is subject to liability in accordance with general practice.  
A person is not subject to liability for non-execution or violation of an order, instruction or job responsibilities if they were assigned to them illegally. Liability arises only if the act actually committed by them comprises all elements of another criminal offense.

#### Article 41. Justified professional or economic risk

Causing harm to rights and interests protected by law is not a crime in case of justified professional or economic risk for achievement of a socially useful purpose.  
The risk is recognized as justified if the action committed corresponds to modern scientific and technical knowledge and experience, and the intended objective could not be achieved by actions not connected with risk, and the person who took the risk took necessary measures for prevention of harm to rights and interests protected by law.  
In case of justified professional or economic risk, liability for damage suffered does not arise even if the desirable socially useful result was not achieved and the harm was more considerable than the pursued socially useful purpose.  
The risk is not recognized as justified if it was obviously associated with a threat of death of people, environmental disaster or other grave consequences.  
Failure to fulfill contractual commitments by subjects of entrepreneurship before banks and other financial organizations on services rendered to them, including issued credits, connected with entrepreneurial and other commercial risks, is not the basis for criminal prosecution of employees of banks and other financial organizations.

#### Article 41-1. Physical or mental compulsion or threat

Causing harm to rights and interests protected by this Code, inflicted as a result of physical or mental compulsion or threat of application of such compulsion, is not a crime if owing to such compulsion or threat the person could not control their actions (inaction).  

The question of criminal liability for causing harm to rights and interests protected by this Code, inflicted as a result of physical or mental compulsion or threat of application of such compulsion owing to which the person retained the ability to control their actions (inaction), is resolved taking into account provisions of article 38 of this Code.

Section fourth. Punishment and its appointment

## Chapter X. Concept, purposes and types of punishments

#### Article 42. Concept and purposes of punishment

Punishment is an enforcement measure applied on behalf of the state according to a court verdict to a person found guilty of committing a crime, and consisting in the deprivation or restriction of certain rights and freedoms of the convict provided by law.  
Punishment is applied for the purpose of correction, prevention of continuation of criminal activities, and also the prevention of commission of new crimes both by the convict and other persons.

#### Article 43. System of punishments

The following primary punishments can be applied to persons found guilty of commission of crimes:

b)	deprivation of certain right;1) obligatory social work
c)	corrective labor;  
d)	restriction on service;

e) assignment to disciplinary unit;  
g)	imprisonment;  
h)	lifelong imprisonment.  
Except the main punishment, additional punishment in the form of deprivation of military or special rank can be applied to convicts.  
Punishments in the form of restriction on service or assignment to disciplinary unit are applied only to military personnel.  
Deprivation of certain right can be applied not only as the basic, but also as additional punishment.

#### Article 44. Penalty

The penalty is the cash collection in the income of the state imposed on the guilty in the amounts established by this Code.  
The penalty is established ranging from five up to six hundred basic settlement amounts.  
In case of evasion by the convicted person from payment of the fine which is ordered to be paid as punishment within the terms established for forced execution, or impossibility of recovery of the penalty during the term established for forced execution due to absence of property of the debtor on which collection can be levied, and also in case of failure to pay the penalty after the term of delay or violation of conditions of payment of the penalty by installments, the court replaces the outstanding amount of the penalty with punishment in the form of obligatory social work, corrective work, restriction on service, restriction of freedom or imprisonment. In this case obligatory social work is appointed at the rate of two and a half hours for the penalty amount corresponding to one basic settlement amount, but for the term of not more than four hundred eighty hours, corrective work, restriction on service, restriction of freedom or imprisonment - at the rate of one month for the penalty amount corresponding to sixteen basic settlement amounts, but for the term of not more than three years.

#### Article 45. Deprivation of certain right

Deprivation of certain right of a person consists in prohibition to the guilty person during the term appointed by the court to hold certain positions at enterprises, in institutions or organizations or to be engaged in certain activities. The type of such position or activity is determined by the court in the judgment of conviction.  
Deprivation of certain right is established for crimes which are directly connected with the position held by the guilty person or their activity for a period of one year up to five years as primary punishment and for a period of one year up to three years as additional punishment.  
If deprivation of certain right is not appointed to the guilty person as primary punishment, it can be appointed by the court as punishment additional to any kind provided by the relevant article of the Special part of this Code.  
In case of imposition of deprivation of certain right as additional punishment to imprisonment, the provision in the judgment extends to all the time of serving by the convict of the primary punishment and, moreover, for the term established by the sentence. In case of imposition of this punishment as additional to other primary punishments and in case of conditional conviction, its term is calculated from the moment of the entry of the sentence into legal force.  
Penalty in the form of deprivation of the right to engage in business activity concerning persons who are engaged in business activity, except as specified, which entailed the death of a person or other grave consequences is not imposed.

#### Article 45-1. Obligatory social work

Obligatory social work consists in forced involvement of the convict in accomplishment of free socially useful work. In the presence of work or study by the convict obligatory social work is performed during time free from work or study.  

Places (objects) at which convicts can perform obligatory social work and the type of obligatory social work are determined by the bodies supervising execution of this punishment.  
Obligatory social work is established for a period of one hundred twenty to four hundred eighty hours and not more than four hours a day within six months, and in case of circumstances not depending on the convict - up to one year.  
Obligatory social work is not applied to persons who have reached retirement age, have not reached sixteen years, to pregnant women, women having children under three years, to persons with disability of the first and second group, to military personnel, foreign citizens and persons not residing permanently in the Republic of Uzbekistan.  
In case of evasion by the convicted person from serving the sentence the court replaces the unexpired term of obligatory social work with punishment in the form of restriction of freedom or imprisonment at the rate of one day of restriction of freedom or imprisonment for four hours of obligatory social work. Evasion time is not credited in the time of the served sentence.

#### Article 46. Corrective work

Corrective work consists in obligatory involvement of a person in work with deduction from ten to thirty percent of the salary in the income of the state and is performed according to the court verdict at the place of employment of the convict or in other places determined by the bodies supervising execution of this punishment.  
Corrective work is established for a period of six months up to three years.  
Corrective work is not applied to persons who have reached retirement age, to disabled persons, pregnant women and women having children under three years, to military personnel.  
In case of evasion in total over one tenth of the appointed term of serving of corrective work the court replaces the unserved sentence of corrective work with punishment in the form of restriction of freedom or imprisonment for the same term. Evasion time is not credited in the time of the served sentence.

#### Article 47. Restriction on service

Restriction on service consists in deprivation of a serviceman passing military service under contract of certain rights and privileges with deduction in the income of the state in the amount of ten to thirty percent of monetary pay, temporarily during the term determined by the court.  
Restriction on service is applied for a period of two months up to three years in cases stipulated in articles of the Special part of this Code. For a crime not constituting great public danger or a negligent crime which did not entail grave consequences the court may, considering the facts of the case and the identity of the convict, instead of imprisonment for the term of not more than three years, or corrective work apply restriction on service for the same term.  

During the term of punishment in the form of restriction on service the convict cannot be promoted, military or special rank is not assigned and this time is not credited to their length of service for assignment of the next military or special rank, and also for award of pension.
