# LAW NO. 20.066

"DOMESTIC VIOLENCE LAW

### Paragraph 1. On domestic violence

## Article 1

Object of the law. The purpose of this law is to prevent, punish and eradicate domestic violence and to provide protection to its victims.

## Article 2

Obligation of protection. It is the duty
of the State to adopt measures conducive to guaranteeing the life, personal integrity and safety of family members.

## Article 3

Prevention and Assistance. The State shall adopt policies aimed at preventing domestic violence, especially against women, older adults and children, and at providing assistance to
victims.	Law 20427
Among other measures, it shall implement the following:	Art. 1 No. 1
a)	Incorporate into study plans and programs	O.G. 18.03.2010 content aimed at modifying behaviors that
favor, stimulate or perpetuate domestic violence;  
b)	Develop training plans for public officials who intervene in the application of this law;  
c)	Develop public safety policies and programs to prevent and eradicate domestic violence;  
d)	Favor civil society initiatives to achieve the objectives of this law;  
e)	Adopt the necessary measures to comply with

the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women, the Convention on the Rights of the Child and other international instruments signed by the State of Chile, and
f)	Create and maintain information systems and statistical records in relation to domestic violence.

## Article 5

Domestic violence. Any mistreatment that affects the life or physical or psychological integrity of a person who has or has had the status of spouse of the offender or a cohabitation relationship with him; or is a relative by consanguinity or by affinity in the entire direct line or in the collateral line up to and including the third degree, of the offender or of his spouse or of his current cohabitant, shall constitute domestic violence.  
There shall also be domestic violence when the conduct referred to in the preceding paragraph occurs between the parents of a common child, or affects a minor, older adult or disabled person who is under the care or dependence of any of the members of the
family group.	Law 20427
Likewise, conduct exercised in the context of affective or	Art. 1 No. 2 family relationships, which has as its direct object the violation	O.G. 18.03.2010 of the economic autonomy of women, or patrimonial violation, or of the economic subsistence of the family or of the children, such as the repeated failure to fulfill the duty to provide support, which is carried out with the purpose of exercising control over her, or over her economic or patrimonial resources, generating dependence or generating
a diminution of said patrimony or that of her sons and daughters, constitutes domestic violence.	Law 21389

### Paragraph 2. On Domestic Violence under the jurisdiction of Family Courts

## Article 6

Acts of domestic violence that do not constitute a crime shall be under the jurisdiction of family courts and shall be subject to the procedure established in Law No. 19.968.

## Article 7

Situation of risk. When there is a situation of imminent risk for one or more persons to suffer mistreatment constituting domestic violence, even when it has not been carried out, the court, on the sole merit of the complaint, must adopt the appropriate protection or precautionary measures.  
It shall be presumed that there is a situation of imminent risk as described in the preceding paragraph when there has been prior intimidation to cause harm by the
offender or when there also exist, with respect to the latter, circumstances or background such as: drug addiction, alcoholism, one or more complaints for domestic violence, prior conviction for domestic violence, pending proceedings or prior convictions for a felony or misdemeanor against persons or for any of the offenses established in paragraphs 5 and 6 of Title VII, of Book Two of the Penal Code or for violation of Law No. 17.798, or psychiatric or psychological background denoting characteristics of a violent personality. Likewise, it shall be presumed that there is a situation of imminent risk
when the accused violently opposes his refusal to accept the termination of an affective relationship that he has
recently maintained with the victim.	Law 20480 In addition, the court shall especially protect cases	Art. 2 No. 1
in which the victim is pregnant, is a person	O.G. 18.12.2010 with a disability or has a condition that makes her
vulnerable. The fact that an older adult, owner or possessor, under any title, of a property that he occupies for residence, is expelled from it, relegated to secondary sectors or his movement within that real property is restricted or limited, by any of the relatives
indicated in Article 5, shall be especially considered as a situation of imminent risk.	Law 20427 Art. 1 No. 3
O.G. 18.03.2010

## Article 8

Sanctions. Mistreatment constituting domestic violence shall be punished, according to its severity, with a fine of half to fifteen monthly tax units for the benefit of the regional government of the domicile of the complainant or plaintiff, to be allocated to victim assistance centers for domestic violence existing in the respective region and that are publicly or privately funded.  
The convicted person must prove payment of the fine

within five days following the date of notification of the judgment, unless the judge, for justified reasons, extends said term for up to fifteen days.  
In case of non-compliance, the court shall refer the background to the Public Prosecutor's Office, in accordance with the provisions of Article 240 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Article 9

Accessory measures. In addition to the provisions of the preceding article, the judge must apply in the sentence one or more of the following accessory measures:
a) Obligation of the offender to leave the home shared with the victim.  
b) Prohibition from approaching the victim or their	LAW 20286 home, place of work or study, as well as any	Art. 6 other place that the victim regularly attends or visits. c) Prohibition from carrying and possessing and, where applicable, the seizure of firearms. This shall be reported, as appropriate, to the General Directorate of Mobilization, the Garrison Command or the respective Service Director, for the corresponding legal and regulatory purposes.  
d) Mandatory attendance at therapeutic or family counseling programs. The institutions that develop such programs shall inform the respective court of the treatment that the aggressor must follow, its commencement and completion.  
e) Obligation to appear regularly before the
police unit determined by the judge.	Law 20480

Without prejudice to the foregoing, the judge, in the final judgment, shall set the definitive alimony, the regime of personal care and direct and regular contact with the children if any, and any other family matter submitted to their knowledge by the parties.

## Article 10

Sanctions. In case of non-compliance with the precautionary or accessory measures decreed, with the exception of that provided in letter d) of article 9, the judge shall bring the matter to the attention of the Public Prosecutor's Office for the purposes provided in the second paragraph of article 240 of the Code of

Civil Procedure, without prejudice to imposing on the offender, as a coercive measure, arrest for up to fifteen days.  
The police must detain anyone caught in flagrant violation of the measures mentioned in the preceding paragraph.

## Article 11.- Expenses and patrimonial damages

The sentence shall establish the obligation of the convicted person to pay the victim the expenses and damages of a patrimonial nature that have been caused by the execution of the act or acts constituting intrafamily violence that are the subject of the trial, including the replacement in money or in kind of damaged, destroyed or lost property. These damages shall be determined prudently by the judge.

## Article 12

Register of sanctions and accessory measures. The Civil Registry and Identification Service must maintain a Special Register of persons who have been convicted, by final judgment, as perpetrators of intrafamily violence, as well as of other resolutions that the law orders to be registered.  
The court, once the sentence becomes final, must notify the Civil Registry, identifying the convicted person and the principal sanction and the accessory sanctions applied for the act of intrafamily violence, with the exception of that provided in letter d) of article 9, circumstances
that the aforementioned Service shall also record in the respective certificate of background. This Special Register shall be brought to the attention of the court at its request, in the cases regulated by law.

## Article 15

Precautionary measures. At any stage	Art. 5 N° 3
of the investigation or proceedings concerning crimes	O.G. 18.11.2021 constituting intrafamily violence, and even before

## Article 18

Sanctions. In case of non-compliance with the measures referred to in articles 15, 16 and 17, the provisions of article 10 shall apply.

## Article 19.- Inadmissibility of reparatory agreements

In proceedings for crimes constituting intrafamily violence, article 241 of the Code of Criminal Procedure shall not apply.

## Article 20

For the fulfillment of the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the Service may enter into agreements with public or private entities.

## Article 23

## Article 24

## Article 25

## Article 26
