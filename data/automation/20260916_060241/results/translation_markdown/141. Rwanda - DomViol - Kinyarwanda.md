CHAPTER ONE: GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article One: Purpose of the Law

This Law is aimed at preventing and suppressing gender-based violence.

Article 2: Definitions of terms

In this Law, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

1° gender-based violence: any act whatsoever committed against a person, whether physical, psychological, sexual or economic, because that person is female or male. Such act violates his or her rights and has negative consequences on him or her. Such violence may be committed within or outside the home;

# CHAPTER ONE: GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article One: Purpose of the law

This law provides for prevention and punishment of all forms of gender based violence.

#### Article 2: Definitions of terms

Under this Law, the following terms shall have the following definitions:

1° gender based violence: any act exercised against a person whether of physical, psychological, sexual and economic character because they are female or male. Such act causes deprivation of freedom and negative consequences. This violence may be committed at home or elsewhere;

2° marriage: a contract of union between a woman and a man concluded in accordance with the law;

3° polygamy: the fact of contracting a second marriage while the first marriage is still valid;

4° concubinage: de facto union between a man and a woman while they are not married, one of them being legally married.

5° adultery: sexual relations between a legally married person with another person who is not their spouse;

6° rape: sexual intercourse imposed on a person without their consent, by means of force, threat, trickery and others;

7° conjugal rape: any act aimed at sexual relations with one's spouse in the absence of their consent, by recourse to violence, threat, trickery and others;

8° abduction: kidnapping of a person by force or by trickery with a view to marital union;

9° sexual slavery: the fact of abusing authority, assets or any other situation for the purpose of pleasure based on sex. It may be aimed at sexual intercourse, caresses, undressing, pornography, exhibition of nudity or eroticism, application of sex on other parts of the body and others;

10° indecent acts: acts or behaviour contrary to good morals which violate the modesty of a person;

11° elderly person: any person aged over sixty five (65) years;

12° harassment: repeated and destabilising behaviour towards a person such as persecuting them, addressing reproaches to them, maliciously reminding them of wrong they did in the past, blaming them, insulting them and others;

## CHAPTER II: PREVENTION AND PROTECTION AGAINST GENDER BASED VIOLENCE

#### Article 3: Threatening to deprive a person of their rights

It is forbidden to threaten someone to deprive them of their rights with a view to exercising on them any act of gender based violence.

#### Article 4: Distorting tranquility of one's spouse

It is forbidden to distort tranquility of one's spouse due to polygamy, concubinage or adultery.

It is forbidden to distort tranquility of one's spouse because of dowry, reproduction and their natural physionomy.

#### Article 4: Atteinte à la tranquilité du conjoint

Il est interdit de porter atteinte à la tranquilité de l‟un des conjoints, par voie de polygamie, de concubinage ou d‟adultère.  
Il est interdit de porter atteinte à la tranquilité de l‟un  des  conjoints  pour  raison  de  dot,  de
reproduction et de sa constitution naturelle.  

Il est interdit de porter atteinte à la tranquilité, aux droits au patrimoine et à l‟emploi de son conjoint.

#### Article 5: Viol conjugal

Les conjoints jouissent des droits égaux en matière de relations sexuelles, de la santé de reproduction et de planning familial.  

Il est interdit de faire des relations sexuelles sans consentement de son conjoint.

#### Article 6: Sévices comme cause de divorce

La violence basée sur le genre est l‟un des causes de demande de divorce.  

Pendant l‟instance du divorce, les intérêts des enfants issus du mariage sont pris en considération conformément à la loi.

#### Article 7: Protection de l'enfant contre la violence basée sur le genre

Le parent, le tuteur de l‟enfant ou toute autre personne en charge de l‟enfant est tenu de le protéger contre toute situation susceptible de l‟exposer à la violence basée sur le genre.  

Il est interdit à une personne de ne pas prendre soins de l‟enfant sous sa tutelle pour des raisons basées sur le genre.

#### Article 8: Congé de maternité

Une femme qui a accouché bénéficie d‟un congé de maternité de trois (3) mois et d‟une heure d‟allaitement par jour de travail, convenue entre elle et son employeur, et ce pendant douze (12) mois à compter de la fin du congé de maternité.  

Sans préjudice des autres dispositions légales relatives au congé de circonstance, une femme qui accouche d‟un enfant qui meurt avant d‟avoir atteint un mois bénéficie d‟un congé de quatre (4) semaines après l‟accouchement.

#### Article 9: Droits d'une femme enceinte ou qui a accouché

Il est interdit de licencier une femme sous pretexte qu‟elle est enceinte ou en congé de maternité.  

La grossesse et l‟accouchement pour une étudiante ne doivent pas être la cause de privation de son droit à l‟éducation.

#### Article 10: Prévention de la violence et assistance aux victimes de la violence

Il est interdit d‟user des stupéfiants, des images, des signes, des langages et d‟autres moyens visant à exercer la violence basée sur le genre.  
Toute personne doit prévenir la violence basée sur le genre, secourir et appeler au secours la victime de cette violence.  

Un Arrêté du Premier Ministre détermine les modalités selon lesquelles les institutions de l‟Etat préviennent la violence basée sur le genre. Il détermine également les modalités d‟accueil, de soulagement, de défense, de traitement et d‟assistance de la victime en vue de la réhabilitation de sa santé.

#### Article 11: Prévention des actes indécents

Il est interdit à toute personne d‟avoir une conduite et un comportement indécent.

## CHAPITRE III: AFFAIRES ET SANCTIONS RELATIVES A LA VIOLENCE BASEE SUR LE GENRE

Section première: Affaires relatives à la violence basée sur le genre

#### Article 12: Lieu de jugement

Sans préjudice du droit de recours, l‟affaire relative à l‟infraction de violence basée sur le genre est jugée et prononcée au lieu où l‟infraction a été commise dans la mesure du possible.

#### Article 12: Place of hearing

Where it is convenient for the victim, gender based violence related cases shall be heard and pronounced at the scene of the crime if
possible.

#### Article 13: Production of evidences and testimonies

Notwithstanding other legal provisions, evidences or testimonies related to gender based violence shall be produced in the courts by any person holding them.

Testimonies given by children and other people living in the household as well as those produced by neighbours shall be taken into account.

### Section 2: Penalties for gender based violence offences

#### Article 14: Penalty for adultery

Any person convicted with adultery shall be liable to imprisonment sentence of between six (6) months and two (2) years.

Penalties provided for in the preceding paragraph shall apply to the co-offender envolved in adultery.

#### Article 15: Prosecution for adultery

Prosecution for adultery shall not be started unless the offender's spouse so claims.

The offender's spouse may, at any step of procedings, decide to withdraw the case. He/she may also suspend jugement execution.

Such withdrawal or suspension shall apply to both persons envolved in adultery.

#### Article 16: Penalty for rape

Any person who is guilty with rape shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) years to fifteen (15) years.

Where rape has resulted in a bodily or a mental illness, the person guilty with rape shall be liable to imprisonment of fifteen (15) years to twenty (20) years and medical care fees for the person raped shall be bone by him/her.

Where rape has resulted in a terminal illness or death, the guilty person shall be liable to life imprisonment.

#### Article 17: Penalty for abduction

Abduction shall be liable to imprisonment of five (5) years to eight (8) years and a fine between one hundred thousand Rwandan francs (100. 000 Rwf) and two hundred
thousand Rwandan francs (200,000 Rwf).

The penalty and fine provided for in paragraph One of this Article shall apply to any person envolved in abduction.

#### Article 18: Penalty for discrimination against a child based on sex

Any person who discriminates against his/her child because he/she is a boy or a girl, and harasses him/her or harasses the other parent, shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to three (3) years.

Any person who discriminates against a child under his/her care based on sex shall be liable to the penalties provided for in paragraph One of this Article.

#### Article 19: Penalty for forcing one's spouse to have sexual intercourse

Any person who forces his/her spouse to have sexual intercourse shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years.

#### Article 20: Penalty for harassing one's spouse

Any person found guilty of harassing his/her spouse shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years.

#### Article 18: Penalty for child neglect because of his/her sex

Any person guilty of neglecting his/her child simply because he/she is a boy or a girl, who harasses him/her or exercises abuse on his/her partner because of that child, shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to three (3) years.  

Any person guilty of neglecting a child in his/her care because of the sex of the latter shall be liable to penalties referred to in the paragraph One of this Article.

#### Article 19: Penalty for conjugal rape

Any person who forces his/her spouse to have sexual intercourse shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years.

#### Article 20: Penalty for harassing one's spouse

Any person who exercises an act of harassment on his/her spouse shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years.  

#### Article 21: Penalty for concubinage

Any person guilty of an act of concubinage shall be liable to imprisonment of two (2) years to four (4) years and a fine between one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) and two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.  

Any person who consents to concubinage shall be liable to penalties referred to in the paragraph One of this Article.  

#### Article 22: Penalty for polygamy

Any person who concludes a second marriage while the first marriage contract is still valid shall be liable to imprisonment of three (3) years to five (5) years and a fine between three hundred thousand (300,000 Rwf) and five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.  

Any person who consents to marriage knowing that his/her partner is still engaged in another marriage shall be liable to penalties referred to in the paragraph One of this Article.  

Any person having contributed in good faith to the act of polygamy by issuing documents or validating the marriage contract shall be liable to imprisonment of five (5) years to eight (8) years.  

#### Article 23: Penalty for sexual slavery

Any person guilty of the crime of sexual slavery based on sex shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) years to fifteen (15) years and a fine between five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) and one million (1,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.  

#### Article 24: Penalty for sexual harassment of one's subordinate

An employer or any other person exercising functions who sexually harasses a person he/she leads using orders, intimidation and terror for the purpose of sexual pleasure shall be liable to imprisonment of two (2) years to five (5) years and a fine between one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) and two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.  

#### Article 25: Penalty for someone who kills his/her spouse

Without prejudice to the provisions of the Penal Code, any person who kills his/her spouse shall be liable to life imprisonment.

#### Article 25: Penalty for murder of one's spouse

Subject to the provisions of the Penal Code, any person found guilty of the murder of his or her spouse shall be liable to life imprisonment.

#### Article 26: Penalty for distorbing tranquility of one's spouse on sexual grounds

Any person guilty of the offence referred to in paragraph 2 and 3 of Article 4 of this Law shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years and a fine between fifty thousand (50,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs and two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 27: Penalty for sexual torture

Any person guilty of violence by exercising sexual torture or intending to commit sexual torture shall be liable to life imprisonment with special provisions.

#### Article 28: Penalty for gender based human trafficking

Any person guilty of gender based human trafficking shall be liable to imprisonment of fifteen (15) years to twenty (20) years and a fine between five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs and two million (2,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 29: Penalty for intentionally transmitting a terminal disease

Any person guilty of intentionally transmitting a terminal disease by sexual way to someone else shall be liable to life imprisonment.

#### Article 30: Penalty for violence caused by the use of drugs, narcotics, pictures, signs, language or writing

Any person guilty of using drugs, narcotics, pictures, signs, language or writing to stir up sexual violence shall be liable to imprisonment sentence of between five (5) years and eight (8) years and a fine between one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) and two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 31: Penalty for committing sexually indecent acts

Any person guilty of committing sexually indecent acts against someone shall be liable to imprisonment sentence of between two (2) years and five (5) years and a fine between one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) and two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 32: Penalty for violence against an elderly person

Any person guilty of sexual violence against an elderly person shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) years to fifteen (15) years and a fine between five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) and one million (1,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 33: Penalty for violence against a handicapped person

Any person guilty of sexual violence against a handicapped person shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) years to fifteen (15) years and a fine between five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) and one million (1,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 34: Penalty for violence aimed at degrading a person or his or her work

Any person guilty of insulting or threatening another person because he or she is a woman or a man, with the aim of degrading him or her or his or her work, shall be liable to imprisonment of two (2) years to five (5) years and a fine between one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) and three hundred thousand (300,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 32: Penalty for violence against elderly persons

Shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years and a fine between five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) and one million (1,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs, any person who has committed gender based violence against an elderly person.

#### Article 33: Penalty for violence against persons with disabilities

Shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years and a fine between five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) and one million (1,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs, any person who has committed gender based violence against a person with disabilities.

#### Article 34

Penalty for violence aiming at defaming a person or his/her work

Shall be liable to imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years and a fine between one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) and three hundred thousand (300,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs, any person guilty of defaming or harassing a person on the grounds of being a woman or a man, with the aim of damaging his/her esteem or that of his/her work.

#### Article 35: Penalty for violence based on threats of loss of rights

Any person threatening another person with deprivation of his/her rights with the aim of attempting to commit or committing any gender based act on that person, shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years and a fine between one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) and five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 36

Penalty for non-assistance to the victim of violence or refusal to testify on the violence

Any person who does not assist the victim of gender based violence or refuses to testify on the violence committed against himself or herself or against a third person shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years and a fine between fifty thousand (50,000 Rwf) and two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs or one of those penalties.

#### Article 37: Penalty for false accusation of gender based violence

Any person found guilty of having falsely accused another person of having committed an offence of gender based violence provided for by this law shall be punished in accordance with the relevant penal legal provisions.

## CHAPTER IV: MISCELLANEOUS AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 38: Damages

The victim of gender based violence and any other affected person has the right to bring action before the courts for damages.

#### Article 39: Legalisation of illegal unions and division of common property

Households maintaining illegal conjugal unions shall marry according to the principle of monogamous marriage.  

Where a person concerned by the provisions of the preceding paragraph of this Article was living with several women or men, he or she shall first proceed to the equal division among those women or men of their common property from before his or her marriage.  

The division of property referred to in paragraph 2 of this Article shall not deprive children of the rights conferred upon them by law.  

The modalities of such distribution shall be determined by an Order of the Minister in charge of Local Government.  

#### Article 40: Repeal of legal provisions inconsistent with this Law

All prior legal provisions inconsistent with this Law are hereby repealed.  

#### Article 41: Commencement of this Law

This Law shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Rwanda.
