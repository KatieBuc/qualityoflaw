Anteriority of Law

###### Art. 1

ºThere is no crime without prior law defining it. There is no penalty
without prior legal provision.  
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Criminal law in time

###### Art. 2º - No one may be punished for an act that a subsequent law ceases

to consider a crime, thereby terminating the execution and criminal effects of the
conviction sentence.  
(Wording given
by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

(Added
by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

omission, even if the moment of the result is different.  
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

###### Art. 13 - The result, upon which the existence of the crime depends, is only

imputable to the person who caused it. An action or omission without which the

(Wording given
by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)
Supervening
independent cause
(Added
by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 1º

The supervening of a relatively independent cause excludes
imputation when, by itself, it produced the result; the prior facts, however,
are imputed to the person who committed them.  
(Added by
Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)
Relevance
of omission
(Added by Law No.
7.209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 2

ºOmission is criminally relevant when the person who omitted had a duty and
was able to act to prevent the result. The duty to act falls upon those who:
(Added by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)
a) have a legal obligation of care, protection or supervision;  
(Added by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)
b) otherwise, assumed responsibility to prevent the result;  
(Added by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)
c) by their prior conduct, created the risk of the result occurring.  
(Added by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

(Wording given

###### Art. 27

(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

### TÍTULO IV

OF THE CONCURRENCE OF PERSONS

###### Art. 29 - Whoever, in any manner, concurs in the crime incurs the

punishments imposed thereon, to the extent of his culpability.  
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 1

º If the participation is of lesser importance, the punishment may be
reduced by one sixth to one third.  
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 2

º If any of the concurring parties intended to participate in a less serious crime,
the punishment for that crime shall be applied to him; that punishment shall be increased up to half, in the event that
the more serious result was foreseeable.  
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Non-communicable circumstances

###### Art. 30 - Circumstances and conditions of

personal character are not communicated, except when they are elements of the crime.  
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Cases of impunity

###### Art. 31 - The agreement, determination or instigation and the assistance, except

express provision to the contrary, are not punishable, if the crime does not reach, at
least, the stage of attempt.  
(Wording given by Law
No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### SEÇÃO I

OF PUNISHMENTS DEPRIVING LIBERTY
Imprisonment and detention

###### Art. 33 - The punishment of imprisonment must be served in closed,

semi-open or open regime. That of detention, in semi-open or open regime, except
necessity of transfer to closed regime.  
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 1

º The following are considered:
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
a) closed regime the execution of the punishment in a maximum or medium security establishment;  
b) semi-open regime the execution of the punishment in an agricultural, industrial colony or
similar establishment;  
c) open regime the execution of the punishment in a halfway house or adequate establishment.

###### § 2

º Punishments depriving liberty must be executed in
progressive form, according to the merit of the convicted person, observing the following criteria and
excepting the hypotheses of transfer to a more rigorous regime:
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
a) the person convicted to a punishment exceeding 8 (eight) years must begin to serve it in closed
regime;  
b) the non-recidivist convicted person, whose punishment exceeds 4 (four) years and does not exceed
8 (eight), may, from the beginning, serve it in semi-open regime;  
c) the non-recidivist convicted person, whose punishment is equal to or less than 4 (four) years,
may, from the beginning, serve it in open regime.

###### § 3

º The determination of the initial regime for serving the punishment
shall be made with observance of the criteria provided in art. 59 of this Code.  
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 4
