THE CRIMINAL LAW

##### CHAPTER I

BASIC PRINCIPLES

###### Article 1.- Principle of legality

No one shall be punished with a penalty or measure unless the prerequisites for the punishability of the conduct and the applicable sanction are expressly and strictly described in a law in force prior to the action or omission that motivates the sanction122.

###### Article 3.- Principle of prevention

Criminal sanctions shall have as their purpose the protection of legal interests and the rehabilitation of the author to a life without crime129.

##### CHAPTER II

APPLICATION OF THE LAW

###### Article 4.- Application of the First Book to special laws

The provisions of the First Book of this Code shall apply to all punishable acts130 provided for by special laws.

###### Article 5.- Application of the law in time

1º Sanctions are governed by the law in force at the time of the commission of the punishable act.  
2º When the sanction changes during the commission of the punishable act, the law in force at the end thereof shall apply131.  
3º When before the sentence the law in force at the time of the commission of the punishable act is modified, the law most favorable to the accused shall apply132.

###### Article 10.- Time of the act

###### Article 11.- Place of the act

1º The act shall be deemed to have been committed in all places where the perpetrator or participant executed the action or, in case of omission, should have executed it; or where the result provided for in the law occurred or where it should have occurred according to the representation of the perpetrator.  
2º The participant151 is considered to have committed the act also in the place where the perpetrator would have committed it.  
3° Paraguayan law shall be applicable to the participant in an act committed abroad, when he has acted in national territory, even if the act lacked criminal sanction according to the law in force in the place where it was committed.

###### Article 12.- Application to minors

This Code shall apply to acts committed by minors, unless the legislation on juvenile offenders provides otherwise152.

###### 2. Article 19.- Legitimate defense

One who carries out conduct described in the legal type of a punishable act does not act unlawfully when it is necessary and rational to repel or deflect a present and unlawful aggression against one's own or another's legal interest186.

###### Article 20.- Justifying state of necessity

1º One who, in a situation of present danger to one's own or another's legal interest, injures another interest to prevent a greater harm that cannot be avoided otherwise does not act unlawfully187.  
2º One who carries out the legal type of a punishable act by omission188 does not act unlawfully when the action could not be performed without violating another duty of equal or greater rank.

###### Article 21

Criminal liability of minors A person who has not reached fourteen years of age is exempt from criminal liability189.

###### Article 22.- Error of prohibition

One who is unaware of the unlawfulness of the act when committing it is not culpable when the error was unavoidable. If the perpetrator could have avoided the error, the penalty shall be mitigated in accordance with article 67190.

###### Article 23.- Mental disorder

1º One who, at the moment of action or omission, due to mental disorder, incomplete or retarded psychological development, or severe disturbance of consciousness, was incapable of knowing the unlawfulness of the act or of acting in accordance with that knowledge is not culpable191.  
2º When, for the reasons indicated in the preceding paragraph, the perpetrator has acted with a considerable diminution of capacity to know the unlawfulness of the act or to act in accordance with this knowledge, the penalty shall be mitigated in accordance with article 67192.

###### Article 24.- Excess due to confusion or terror

One who commits an unlawful act by exceeding the limits of legitimate defense or of a justifying state of necessity due to confusion or terror shall be exempt from penalty193.

###### Article 25.- Inexigibility of other conduct

One who commits an unlawful act to repel or deflect from oneself, a relative194 or another person close to oneself, a present danger to life, physical integrity or liberty, shall be exempt from penalty when, considering all circumstances, no other conduct could have been demanded. In case other conduct could have been demanded, the penalty may be mitigated in accordance with article 67195.

ATTEMPT

###### Article 26.- Acts that constitute it

There is an attempt when the perpetrator executes the decision to commit a punishable act through acts that, taking into account the perpetrator's representation of the act, are immediately prior to the consummation of the legal type196.

###### Article 27.- Punishability of attempt

1º The attempt of crimes197 is punishable; the attempt of offenses198 is punishable only in cases expressly provided by law.  
2º To attempts the penalty frameworks provided for consummated punishable acts are applicable.  
3º When the perpetrator has not yet performed all the acts that, according to the perpetrator's representation of the act, are necessary to achieve its consummation, the penalty shall be mitigated in accordance with article 67.

##### CHAPTER III

PLURALITY OF PARTICIPANTS

###### Article 29.-Perpetration

1º One who commits the act acting on one's own or using another for that purpose shall be punished as perpetrator.  
2º One who acts in agreement with another in such a way that, through one's contribution to the act, shares with the other control over its commission shall also be punished as perpetrator201.

###### Article 30.- Instigation

One who induces another to commit an intentional unlawful act shall be punished as instigator. The penalty shall be that provided for the perpetrator202.

###### Article 31.- Complicity

One who assists another in committing an intentional unlawful act shall be punished as accomplice. The penalty shall be that provided for the perpetrator and mitigated in accordance with article 67203.

###### Article 32.- Special personal circumstances

1º When the instigator or accomplice does not possess the conditions, qualities or personal relations provided in article 16 that establish the punishability of the perpetrator, the penalty shall be mitigated in accordance with article 67204.  
2º Special personal circumstances that increase, diminish or exclude the penalty shall be taken into account only for the perpetrator or participant in whom they exist.

###### Article 33.- Individual punishability

Each participant in the act shall be punished according to one's own culpability, independently of the culpability of the others205.

###### Article 34.- Attempted instigation to a crime

###### Artículo 52.- Fine sentence

1º The fine sentence consists of the payment to the State of a determined sum of money, calculated in day-fines. Its limit is five day-fines as a minimum and, unless the law provides otherwise, three hundred sixty day-fines as a maximum241.  
2º The amount of one day-fine shall be fixed by the court considering the personal and economic conditions of the perpetrator. Regard shall be had, primarily, to the average net income that the perpetrator has or can obtain in one day. One day-fine shall be determined at, at least, twenty percent of a minimum daily wage for diverse non-specified activities and at five hundred ten wages of the same category, as a maximum242.  
3º Where there is no basis for determining the amount of one day-fine, the court may estimate the income, assets and other relevant economic data. In addition, it may require reports from the Treasury offices and from the banks.  
4º The judgment shall state the number and amount of day-fines.  
5º In the event that the legal category of minimum wages and salaries in labor legislation is abolished, the amounts established in paragraph 2 shall be updated annually by means of the Consumer Price Index rate, officially published on December 31 of each year by the Central Bank of Paraguay or the institution in charge of preparing it, taking as reference the last amount that has been in force243.

1º When an intentional unlawful act has been committed, the objects produced and the objects with which it was committed or prepared may be confiscated.
