ACT
of 29 July 2005  

on counteracting domestic violence

Recognising that domestic violence violates fundamental human rights, including the right to life and health and respect for personal dignity, and that public authorities have a duty to ensure equal treatment of all citizens and respect for their rights and freedoms, and in order to increase the effectiveness of counteracting domestic violence, the following is hereby enacted:

## Art. 1. The Act defines

1) tasks in the field of counteracting domestic violence;  
2) rules of procedure towards persons affected by domestic violence;  
3) rules of procedure towards persons using domestic violence.

## Art. 2. Whenever the Act refers to

1) a family member – this shall mean a close person within the meaning of Article 115 § 11 of the Act of 6 June 1997 – the Penal Code (Journal of Laws of 2019, items 1950 and 2128), as well as another person cohabiting or sharing a household;  
2) domestic violence – this shall mean a single or repeated intentional act or omission violating the rights or personal interests of the persons referred to in point 1, in particular exposing those persons to the danger of loss of life, health, violating their dignity, bodily integrity, freedom, including sexual freedom, causing damage to their physical or mental health, as well as causing suffering and moral harm to persons affected by violence.

## Art. 3

1. A person affected by domestic violence shall be provided with free assistance, in particular in the form of:
1) medical, psychological, legal, social, vocational and family counselling;  
2) crisis intervention and support;  
3) protection against further harm, by preventing persons using violence from using the dwelling occupied jointly with other family members and prohibiting contact with and approaching the victim;  
4) providing a person affected by domestic violence with safe shelter in a specialised support centre for victims of domestic violence;  
5) medical examination to determine the causes and type of bodily injuries related to the use of domestic violence and issuing a medical certificate in this regard;  
6) providing a person affected by domestic violence who has no legal title to the premises occupied jointly with the perpetrator of violence with assistance in obtaining housing.  
2. The minister responsible for health matters shall specify, by way of regulation, the template of a medical certificate on the causes and type of bodily injuries related to the use of domestic violence, taking into account the usefulness of the certificate for the legal protection of a person affected by domestic violence.

## Art. 4

Towards persons using domestic violence, measures provided for in this Act shall be applied aimed at preventing their contact with victims and correctional and educational interventions.

## Art. 5

The minister responsible for social security matters shall specify, by way of regulation:
1) the standard of basic services provided by specialised support centres for victims of domestic violence,
2) the qualifications of persons employed in specialised support centres for victims of domestic violence,

3) detailed directions for conducting correctional and educational interventions towards persons using domestic violence,
4) the qualifications of persons conducting correctional and educational interventions
– taking into account the need to adapt the scope of assistance to the situation and needs of persons affected by domestic violence, as well as the effectiveness of services provided by specialised support centres for victims of domestic violence and the effectiveness of correctional and educational interventions towards persons using domestic violence.

## Art. 6

1. Tasks in the field of counteracting domestic violence are carried out by government administration bodies and local government units on the principles set out in the provisions of the Act of 12 March 2004
on social assistance (Journal of Laws of 2019, items 1507, 1622, 1690, 1818 and 2473) or the Act of 26 October 1982
on upbringing in sobriety and counteracting alcoholism (Journal of Laws of 2019, item 2277), unless the provisions of this Act provide otherwise.
2. The own tasks of the municipality include in particular the creation of a municipal system for counteracting domestic violence, including:
1) development and implementation of a municipal programme for counteracting domestic violence and protection of victims of domestic violence;
2) provision of counselling and intervention in the field of counteracting domestic violence, in particular through educational activities aimed at strengthening the care and educational competences of parents in families at risk of domestic violence;
3) provision of places in support centres for persons affected by domestic violence;
4) creation of interdisciplinary teams.
3. The own tasks of the county include in particular:
1) development and implementation of a county programme for counteracting domestic violence and protection of victims of domestic violence;
2) development and implementation of programmes for preventive measures aimed at providing specialised assistance, especially in the field of promoting and implementing proper educational methods towards children in families at risk of domestic violence;
3) provision of places in support centres for persons affected by domestic violence;
4) provision of places in crisis intervention centres for persons affected by domestic violence.
4. Tasks within the scope of government administration carried out by the county include in particular:
1) creation and operation of specialised support centres for victims of domestic violence;
2) development and implementation of correctional and educational intervention programmes for persons using domestic violence.
5. Funds for the implementation and service of the tasks referred to in paragraph 4 are provided by the state budget.
6. The own tasks of the voivodeship self-government include in particular:
1) development and implementation of a voivodeship programme for counteracting domestic violence;
2) inspiring and promoting new solutions in the field of counteracting domestic violence;
3) development of framework programmes for the protection of victims of domestic violence and framework programmes for correctional and educational interventions for persons using domestic violence;
4) organisation of training for persons carrying out tasks related to counteracting domestic violence.
Art. 6a. Persons managing specialised support centres for victims of domestic violence must meet the qualification requirements set out in Article 122 of the Act of 12 March 2004 on social assistance.

## Art. 7

1. The tasks of the voivode include in particular:
1) development of instructional materials, recommendations, procedures for intervention in crisis situations related to domestic violence for persons carrying out these tasks;
2) monitoring the phenomenon of domestic violence;
3) appointment and dismissal of the Voivodeship Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for Counteracting Domestic Violence;

4) monitoring the implementation of the National Programme for Counteracting Domestic Violence with the assistance of the Voivodeship Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for Counteracting Domestic Violence;
5) supervision over the implementation of tasks in the field of counteracting domestic violence carried out by municipal, county and voivodeship self-government;
6) control of the implementation of tasks in the field of counteracting domestic violence performed by non-public entities on the basis of contracts with government administration and local government bodies.
2. The supervision and control referred to in paragraph 1 points 5 and 6 shall apply accordingly to the provisions of Articles 126–133 of the Act of 12 March 2004 on social assistance.
3. The minister competent for social security matters shall specify, by way of regulation:
1) the organisation and procedure for conducting supervision and control, the qualifications of inspectors authorised to perform supervisory and control activities, as well as the template of the identity card authorising the performance of supervisory and control activities,
2) the qualifications of the Voivodeship Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for Counteracting Domestic Violence – taking into account the need to ensure an appropriate level of task performance.

## Art. 8

The tasks of the minister competent for social security matters include in particular:
1) commissioning and financing research, expert opinions and analyses concerning the phenomenon of domestic violence;
2) conducting activities promoting the raising of social awareness regarding the causes and effects of domestic violence;
3) appointment and dismissal of the National Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for Counteracting Domestic Violence with the rank of secretary or undersecretary of state in the office serving the minister competent for social security matters;
4) monitoring the implementation of the National Programme for Counteracting Domestic Violence with the assistance of the coordinator referred to in point 3;
5) development and issuance at least once every two years of guidelines for conducting training in the field of counteracting domestic violence;
6) development and financing of protective programmes in the field of counteracting domestic violence;
7) financial support for programmes in the field of counteracting domestic violence implemented by:
a) local government units,
b) non-governmental organisations working to counteract domestic violence,
c) organisational units operating on the basis of provisions on the relationship of the State to the Catholic Church in the Republic of Poland, the relationship of the State to other churches and religious associations and on the guarantee of freedom of conscience and religion, if their statutory objectives include conducting activities in the field of counteracting domestic violence.
Art. 8a. The tasks of the Prosecutor General include the development and issuance at least once every two years of guidelines concerning the principles of procedure of common organisational units of the prosecutor's office in the field of counteracting domestic violence.

## Art. 9

1. Government and local government administration bodies cooperate with non-governmental organizations as well as churches and religious associations in the scope of providing assistance to persons affected by violence, influencing persons using violence and raising public awareness about the causes and effects of domestic violence.  
2. Government or local government administration bodies may commission the performance of tasks specified in the Act in the manner provided for in the Act of 24 April 2003 on Public Benefit Activity and Volunteer Work (Journal of Laws of 2019, items 688, 1570 and 2020).  

Art. 9a. 1. The municipality undertakes activities for the prevention of domestic violence, in particular within the framework of work in an interdisciplinary team.  
2.	The interdisciplinary team is appointed by the commune head, mayor or city president.  
3.	The interdisciplinary team consists of representatives of:
1)	organizational units of social assistance;  
2)	the municipal commission for solving alcohol problems;  
3)	the Police;  
4)	education;  
5)	health care;  
6)	non-governmental organizations.  
4.	The interdisciplinary team also includes court probation officers.  
5.	The interdisciplinary team may also include prosecutors and representatives of entities other than those specified in paragraph 3, acting for the prevention of domestic violence.  
6.	The chairperson of the interdisciplinary team is elected at the first meeting of the team from among its members.  
7.	Meetings of the interdisciplinary team are held depending on needs, but not less frequently than once every three months.  
8.	The interdisciplinary team operates on the basis of agreements concluded between the commune head, mayor or city president and the entities referred to in paragraph 3 or 5.  
9.1)  Organizational and technical support for the interdisciplinary team is provided by the social assistance center, and in the case of transformation of the social assistance center into a social services center on the basis of the provisions of the Act of 19 July 2019 on the provision of social services by social services centers (Journal of Laws item 1818) – the social services center.  
10.	The interdisciplinary team may create working groups in order to solve problems related to the occurrence of domestic violence in individual cases.

1)	organizational units of social assistance;  
2)	the municipal commission for solving alcohol problems;  
3)	the Police;  
4)	education;  
5)	health care.  
12.	The working groups may also include court probation officers, as well as representatives of other entities, specialists in the field of preventing domestic violence.  
13.	Members of the interdisciplinary team and working groups perform tasks within the framework of their official or professional duties.  
14.	Work within the framework of working groups is conducted depending on needs reported by the interdisciplinary team or resulting from problems occurring in individual cases.  
15.	The municipal council shall determine, by way of resolution, the mode and manner of appointing and dismissing members of the interdisciplinary team and the detailed conditions of its functioning.

Art. 9d. 1. Undertaking intervention in the environment towards a family affected by violence takes place on the basis of the "Blue Cards" procedure and does not require the consent of the person affected by domestic violence.  
2.	The "Blue Cards" procedure includes all activities undertaken and implemented by representatives of organizational units of social assistance, municipal commissions for solving alcohol problems, the Police, education and health care, in connection with a justified suspicion of domestic violence.

Initiation of the "Blue Cards" procedure takes place by completing the "Blue Card" form in the event of suspicion, in the course of official or professional activities, of the use of violence against family members or as a result of a report made by a family member or by a person who is a witness to domestic violence.  
5.	The Council of Ministers shall determine, by way of regulation, the "Blue Cards" procedure and the templates of "Blue Card" forms completed by representatives of entities implementing the "Blue Cards" procedure, taking into account the effectiveness of actions towards persons affected by domestic violence and the welfare of these persons.

## Art. 10

1. In order to create conditions for effective prevention of domestic violence, the Council of Ministers shall adopt the National Programme for the Prevention of Domestic Violence, specifying detailed actions in the area of:
1) ensuring protection and providing assistance to persons affected by domestic violence;
2) corrective and educational measures towards persons using domestic violence;
3) raising public awareness of the causes and effects of domestic violence and promoting non-violent parenting methods;
4) disseminating information on the possibilities and forms of providing assistance to both persons affected by violence and those using domestic violence.
2. The National Programme for the Prevention of Domestic Violence at the central level shall be implemented by the coordinator referred to in Article 8 point 3.
3. The National Programme for the Prevention of Domestic Violence at the voivodeship level shall be implemented by the coordinator referred to in Article 7(1) point 3.
Art. 10a. 1. A Monitoring Team for the Prevention of Domestic Violence, hereinafter referred to as the "Team", shall be established as an advisory and consultative body to the minister competent for social security matters.
2. The term of office of the Team shall be 3 years.
3. The tasks of the Team shall include in particular:
1) initiating and supporting actions aimed at preventing domestic violence;
2) monitoring actions in the field of preventing domestic violence;
3) expressing opinions on matters concerning the application of the Act and initiating amendments to provisions in the field of preventing domestic violence;
4) expressing opinions in the event of disputes between public administration bodies and non-governmental organisations implementing tasks in the field of preventing domestic violence;
5) expressing opinions on public tasks in the area of preventing domestic violence and on entrusting these tasks for implementation by the entities referred to in Article 9(1);
6) developing standards for assistance to victims of domestic violence and work with persons using domestic violence;
7) creating, in cooperation with the entities referred to in Article 9(1), mechanisms for informing about standards for providing assistance to victims of domestic violence and work with persons using domestic violence;
8) disseminating the results of monitoring actions in the field of preventing domestic violence.

Art. 10b. 1. The minister competent for social security matters shall appoint to the Team:
1) the National Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for the Prevention of Domestic Violence;
2) seven representatives of government administration bodies and units subordinate to them or supervised by them, appointed from among persons nominated by these bodies and persons managing these units;
3) five representatives of local government units appointed from among persons nominated by the local government side in the Joint Commission of the Government and Local Government;
4) ten representatives of non-governmental organisations, associations and alliances of non-governmental organisations, and churches and religious associations, appointed from among persons nominated by these entities.
2. The minister competent for social security matters shall dismiss a member of the Team before the end of the term of office:
1) at his or her request;
2) at the request of the entity which he or she represents;
3) in the event of conviction by a final judgment for an intentional offence or an intentional fiscal offence.
Art. 10c. 1. The Chairperson of the Team shall be the National Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for the Prevention of Domestic Violence.
2. Meetings of the Team shall be convened by the Chairperson of the Team or at the request of at least one quarter of the number of members of the Team, but not less frequently than once every six months.
3. Members of the Team shall be entitled to leave from work in order to participate in meetings of the Team and shall be entitled to reimbursement of travel costs from the state budget from the part administered by the minister competent for social security matters.
Art. 10d. Government administration bodies and state organisational units subordinate to them shall, at the request of the chairperson of the Team, provide the Team with comprehensive assistance in the performance of its tasks, and in particular shall provide the necessary information or documents.
Art. 10e. Administrative and office services for the Team shall be provided by the office serving the minister competent for social security matters.
Art. 10f. The minister competent for social security matters shall specify, by way of regulation, the procedure for appointing members of the Team and the organisation and procedure for the operation of the Team, as well as the rules for participation in its work, taking into account the need to ensure an appropriate level of performance by the Team of its tasks.

## Art. 11

The Council of Ministers shall submit annually to the Sejm and Senate of the Republic of Poland, by 30 September, a report on the implementation of the National Programme for the Prevention of Domestic Violence.
Art. 11a. 1. If a family member jointly occupying a dwelling, by his or her behaviour consisting in the use of domestic violence, makes cohabitation particularly burdensome, a person affected by violence may demand that the court oblige him or her to leave the dwelling.
2. The court shall hear the case under the provisions of the Act of 17 November 1964 – Code of Civil Procedure (Journal of Laws of 2019, item 1460, as amended4)) on non-contentious proceedings. The decision shall be issued after a hearing, which should take place within one month from the date of receipt of the application. It shall become enforceable upon its announcement and may be amended or revoked in the event of a change in circumstances.
3. The provisions on enforcement of the obligation to vacate premises serving to satisfy the housing needs of the debtor shall apply accordingly to the enforcement of the obligation adjudicated pursuant to paragraph 1.

## Art. 12

1. Persons who, in connection with the performance of their official or professional duties, have formed a suspicion of the commission of an ex officio prosecuted crime involving domestic violence, shall immediately notify the Police or the prosecutor thereof.  
2. Persons who are witnesses to domestic violence should notify the Police, the prosecutor or another entity acting to counteract domestic violence thereof.  

4)  Amendments to the consolidated text of the aforementioned Act were published in the Journal of Laws of 2019, items 1469, 1495, 1649, 1655, 1798, 1802,
1818, 2070, 2089, 2128 and 2217.  

Art. 12a. 1. In the event of a direct threat to the life or health of a child in connection with domestic violence, a social worker performing official duties has the right to remove the child from the family and place them with another non-cohabiting close person, within the meaning of Article 115 § 11 of the Act of 6 June 1997 – the Penal Code, in a foster family or in a care and educational institution.  
2.	The procedure for placing children in a foster family or in a care and educational institution is regulated by the provisions of the Act of 9 June 2011 on supporting the family and the foster care system (Journal of Laws of 2019, items 1111, 924 and 1818).  
3.	The decision referred to in paragraph 1 shall be taken by the social worker jointly with a Police officer, as well as with a doctor, or paramedic, or nurse. The provisions of Article 59810, Article 59811 § 3 and Article 59812 § 1 first sentence of the Act of 17 November 1964 – the Code of Civil Procedure shall apply accordingly.  
4.	The social worker has the obligation to immediately notify the guardianship court, no later than within 24 hours, of the removal of the child from the family and their placement with a non-cohabiting close person, in a foster family or in a care and educational institution.  
5.	The provisions concerning the placement of a child in a foster family or in a care and educational institution shall apply accordingly to the placement of a child with a non-cohabiting close person.  
6.	The minister competent for internal affairs, in consultation with the minister competent for social security, shall specify, by way of regulation, the procedure for Police action when performing the activities referred to in paragraphs 1 and 3, as well as the method of documenting the activities carried out by the Police, taking into account the need to provide effective assistance to children.  
Art. 12b. 1. Parents, legal guardians or de facto guardians have the right to lodge a complaint with the guardianship court against the removal of the child referred to in Article 12a. In the complaint, one may request an examination of the legitimacy and legality of the removal of the child and the correctness of its execution.  
2.	The complaint may be lodged through the social worker or Police officer who carried out the removal of the child. In such a case, the complaint shall be immediately forwarded to the guardianship court.  
3.	The court shall examine the complaint immediately, but no later than within 24 hours. In the event of finding the removal of the child to be unjustified or illegal, the court shall order the immediate transfer of the child to the parents, legal guardians or de facto guardians from whom the child was removed.  
4.	In the event of finding the removal of the child to be unjustified, illegal or incorrect, the court shall notify the superiors of the persons who carried out the removal thereof.  
Art. 12c. The social worker or Police officer shall instruct the parents, legal guardians or de facto guardians of the child about the right to lodge a complaint, together with an indication of the guardianship court competent by location to examine it. This instruction shall be handed over in writing.  
Art. 12d. 1. In the event that a professional probation officer's motion to order the execution of a conditionally suspended sentence of imprisonment or to revoke conditional early release concerns a person convicted of a crime committed with the use of violence or unlawful threat against a family member, who during the probationary period grossly violated the legal order by again using violence or unlawful threat against a family member, the court competent to examine the motion shall order the detention and forced bringing of the convicted person to the hearing on the examination of the motion.  
2.	The detained person, upon their request, shall be immediately enabled to establish contact with a lawyer in an accessible form, as well as to have a direct conversation with them.  
3.	The court's decision to order the execution of the sentence or to revoke conditional early release shall be subject to execution from the moment of its issuance; the court that issued the decision or the court competent to examine the complaint may suspend the execution of the decision.
