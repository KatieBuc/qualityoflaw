I. Chapter. Definitions and standing.

## Art. 1

By proximity ban according to this Act is meant those cases when a person is prohibited from coming to a specific place or area, pursuing, visiting or otherwise making contact with another person.

## Art. 2

By expulsion from home according to this Act is meant those cases when a person is expelled from his home or place of residence and prohibited from returning there for a specified time.

## Art. 3

The person who has been wronged, the victim, his family or another person close to him, may submit a request to the police that a person, the accused, who has committed an offence against him or otherwise disturbed his peace, be subject to a proximity ban and/or expulsion from home.

The same authority under para. 1 is held by the legal guardian of the victim and the person who acts on behalf of social services and/or [child protection services] ¹) in the municipality where the person concerned is resident.

The Commissioner of Police may also take up a case for processing according to this Act on his own initiative if he considers there is reason to do so.

¹) Act 107/2021.

## Art. 44. II

Chapter. Conditions.

## Art. 4

A proximity ban may be applied if:

a. there is reasonable suspicion that the accused has committed a punishable offence or otherwise disturbed the peace of the victim, or

b. there is a risk that the person concerned will commit an offence against the victim under subpara. a.

## Art. 5

Expulsion from home may be applied if:

a. there is reasonable suspicion that the accused has committed a punishable offence against the provisions of Chapters XXII–XXIV of the General Penal Code and/or Art. 108, Art. 233, Art. 233 b, Art. 253 and/or Art. 257 of the same Act and the act has been directed at another person who is close to him and their relationship is considered to have increased the seriousness of the act, provided that

the offence carries imprisonment of up to six months, or

b. there is a risk that the person concerned will commit an offence against the victim under subpara. a.

A proximity ban may be applied simultaneously with expulsion from home if it is deemed necessary to secure the interests of the victim.

The police are authorised to arrest the accused at home or wherever he can be reached for the purpose of processing and decision regarding proximity ban and/or expulsion from home.

## Art. 6

A proximity ban and/or expulsion from home shall only be applied when it does not appear likely that the privacy of the victim will be protected by other and milder means. Care shall then be taken not to proceed more severely than necessary.

In the assessment under para. 1 it is permissible to take into account whether the accused has previously been subject to a proximity ban and/or expulsion from home as well as whether his conduct at earlier stages has been such that there is considered to be a risk that he will commit conduct as described in Art. 4 and para. 1 of Art. 5.

[The Director of Public Prosecutions may issue general instructions on milder measures under para. 1, including what such measures may be, what conditions must be present and how their implementation shall be carried out.] 1)

1) Act 12/2019.

## Art. 1. III

Chapter. Case processing by the Commissioner of Police.

## Art. 7. T

he Commissioner of Police, or his legally qualified representative, at the victim's domicile takes a decision on proximity ban and/or expulsion from home, on the basis of a request under para. 1 or 2 of Art. 3, or if public interests, private interests or interests relating to investigation and/or court proceedings in a criminal case so require.

[Processing of a case shall be expedited and a decision under para. 1 taken as quickly as possible. A decision on expulsion from home shall however be taken no later than twenty-four hours after a request has been received under para. 1 or 2 of Art. 3 or a case has arisen in another manner, but a decision on proximity ban no later than seventy-two hours after a request has been received or a case has arisen as aforesaid.] 1)

A proximity ban shall be set for a specified time, however not longer than one year at a time. It is not permissible to extend a proximity ban unless a new decision is made, provided that the conditions of Art. 4 are still present.

Expulsion from home shall be set for a specified time, however not longer than four weeks at a time. It is not permissible to extend expulsion from home unless a new decision is made, provided that the conditions of Art. 5 are still present.

1) Act 12/2019.

## Art. 2. Art. 8

The Commissioner of Police is obliged to appoint a defence counsel for the accused in connection with the processing of a case according to this Act and such appointment is governed by Chapter IV of the Act on Criminal Procedure. The victim shall also be appointed a legal representative and such appointment is governed by Chapter V of the Act on Criminal Procedure.

If the accused or the victim does not understand Icelandic sufficiently well, the police shall, if deemed necessary, call upon a sworn court interpreter or another qualified person to interpret what takes place. If the accused or the victim is unable to communicate in spoken language, a specialist shall be called upon in the same manner to provide assistance. Remuneration for an interpreter or specialist and other costs relating to their work shall be paid from the State Treasury. Regarding their work and qualifications, the provisions of Art. 12 of the Act on Criminal Procedure otherwise apply as appropriate.

## Art. 9

A decision on proximity ban and/or expulsion from home shall be in writing, reasoned and specify the accused and the victim. Time limits, scope and commencement of the proximity ban and/or expulsion shall also be specified in the decision.

The decision shall be served on the accused or on his defence counsel or other person of legal age who has received written authority from him to accept service. The legal effects of the decision are calculated from the service of the decision. Upon service, guidance shall be given on the legal effects of the decision and case processing before the district court under Chapter IV of this Act.

## Art. 10

A request under para. 1 or 2 of Art. 3 shall be refused if the conditions of Chapter II of this Act are not considered to be present. It is not obligatory to give those who have an interest in the case an opportunity to express themselves before such a decision is taken.

If a request is refused, the Commissioner of Police is then obliged to notify the victim thereof together with reasoning. The victim shall also be informed that he is entitled to appeal the decision to the Director of Public Prosecutions within one month from the time he is notified of it, or he obtained knowledge of it in another manner.

The Director of Public Prosecutions shall take a position on the appeal within one week from the time the case documents relating to the appealed decision

are received from the Commissioner of Police. If the Director of Public Prosecutions annuls the decision of the Commissioner of Police, the Commissioner of Police shall bring the case before the district court according to the rules of Chapter IV of this Act unless the Director of Public Prosecutions directs otherwise.

## Art. 11

When the reasons that formed the basis for a decision on the application of measures under Art. 4 and/or Art. 5 are no longer present, the Commissioner of Police shall annul the ban and/or expulsion by a new decision. It is obligatory to give those who have an interest in the case an opportunity to express themselves before such a decision is taken.

If a decision on measures under Art. 4 and/or Art. 5 is annulled, the police are then obliged to notify the victim and the accused thereof together with reasoning.

A decision by the police to annul a proximity ban and/or expulsion from home cannot be brought before a judge but it may be appealed according to the same rules that apply to appeal of a decision by the police to discontinue investigation of a criminal case.

IV. Chapter. Case processing before court.

## 12. gr. L

The chief of police shall submit a decision on the application of a measure pursuant to … 1) Article 5 to a district court for confirmation as soon as possible and no later than three days after the decision was served on the accused. The same applies to a decision on the extension of … 1) expulsion.

[The accused may request that the chief of police submit his decision on the application of a measure pursuant to Article 4 to a court for confirmation. A request for this shall be directed in writing to the chief of police within two weeks from the service of the decision, and the chief of police shall then submit his decision to a district court as soon as possible and no later than three days after the request of the accused was received. The same applies to a decision on the extension of a restraining order.] 1)

Proceedings before the district court do not suspend the legal effects of the decision unless the chief of police has decided otherwise.

1) Act 12/2019, Article 3: regarding transitional provisions see Article 4 of the same Act.

## 13. gr

After a request from the chief of police has been received by the district court, the judge determines the place and time of a court session where the case will be heard. The court session shall be held as soon as possible and no later than three days after the case documents have been received by the district court.

The judge issues a summons to the accused specifying the place and time of the court session together with a request to him to attend court. If the judge considers that the position of the accused on the request is sufficiently clear from the police documents, the judge may state in the summons that the absence of the accused will be considered equivalent to his position during the handling of the case by the chief of police and that a ruling may be issued in the case even if he does not attend court. Otherwise, it shall be stated in the summons that the police may bring the accused before the court, by force if necessary, if he fails to do so.

The judge shall also notify the victim, or his legal representative, when the court session will be held.

The summons shall be served on the accused or on his lawyer or other legally competent person who has received written authorization from him to accept service.

## 14. gr

The accused shall be appointed a defence counsel for the handling of the case before the court, and such appointment shall be made pursuant to Chapter IV of the Criminal Procedure Act. The victim shall also be appointed a legal representative, and such appointment shall be made pursuant to Chapter V of the Criminal Procedure Act.

## 15. gr

Regarding proceedings before the district court, the provisions of Chapter XV of the Criminal Procedure Act apply as appropriate. If the accused attends court at the pronouncement of the ruling, the ruling is considered served on him, provided that a copy of the ruling is immediately available to him. If the ruling cannot be served at the court session, the ruling shall then be served on the accused or on his lawyer or other legally competent person who has received written authorization from him to accept service.

The legal effects of a district court ruling are calculated from service unless the judge has decided otherwise.

[A judge's ruling on whether a restraining order or expulsion from home shall be imposed, as well as a ruling issued in a case concerning such a request, may be appealed to the Court of Appeal if it may be appealed according to the general rules of paragraph 1 of Article 192 of the Criminal Procedure Act. Other rulings of the Court of Appeal than those provided for in paragraph 1 of Article 211 of the Criminal Procedure Act may not be appealed to the Supreme Court.] 1)

1) Act 76/2019.

## 40. gr. 16. gr

When a person has been expelled from his home pursuant to this Act, the police shall notify the expulsion to the social services of the relevant municipality. For this purpose, the police are authorized to transmit information about the names of the accused and household members to the extent that it is deemed necessary for the processing of the case.

## 17. gr

If a child lives in a home where the expulsion of a person takes place, the police shall always notify the expulsion to [child protection services]. 1) The same applies when a person is subjected to a restraining order.

1) Act 107/2021, 44.
