# PENAL CODE OF HONDURAS DECREE NUMBER 144-83

PENAL CODE OF HONDURAS DECREE NUMBER 144-83

THE NATIONAL CONGRESS, DECREES:
The following:
PENAL CODE BOOK ONE GENERAL PART

## TITLE I

APPLICATION OF CRIMINAL LAW

#### ARTICLE 1

No one shall be punished for infractions that are not determined in a law prior to the perpetration of a crime.

#### ARTICLE 2

No penalties or security measures other than those previously established by Law shall be imposed.

#### ARTICLE 2

A. Actions or omissions susceptible to being classified according to two or more provisions of this Code shall be sanctioned observing the following rules:
1)	The special provision shall be applied with preference to the general provision;  

2)	The subsidiary provision shall be applied in default of the principal provision when such subsidiarity is expressly declared or it appears in tacit form; and,
3)	The complex penal provision shall absorb those that sanction the infractions consumed therein.

#### ARTICLE 2

B. Every person to whom a crime or misdemeanor is attributed has the right to be treated with the respect due to the dignity inherent to the human being. Consequently, penalties or security measures that imply inhuman or degrading treatment may not be imposed.

#### ARTICLE 2

C. No penalty or security measure may be imposed if the action or omission does not injure or effectively endanger a legal interest protected by criminal law.

#### ARTICLE 2

D. Penalties and security measures shall only be imposed when they are necessary and in proportion to the gravity of the act committed.  

In no case may a security measure be imposed if it is not as a consequence of an action or omission described as a crime by criminal law.

#### ARTICLE 3

Honduran Criminal Law shall apply to any person who commits a punishable act in the national territory and other places subject to the jurisdiction of Honduras, except for the exceptions stipulated in International Law.

#### ARTICLE 4

Honduran Criminal Law shall apply to those who have committed abroad crimes against public health, public faith, the economy or the external or internal security of the State, regulated in Titles V, IX, X, XI and XII of Book Two of this Code. It shall also apply when a Honduran public official or employee has committed abroad crimes against the national public administration regulated in Title XIII of the aforementioned Book Two.

#### ARTICLE 5

Honduran courts shall also have jurisdiction over crimes committed abroad when the accused is in Honduras and any of the following situations occurs:

1)	When he has not been tried for the crime perpetrated aboard a Honduran vessel or aircraft, merchant or private, or when having been tried he has escaped and has not served the sentence totally or partially;  
2)	If the accused being Honduran, his extradition is requested by the State in whose territory the punishable act was committed;  

3)	If the person responsible for the crime is an official of the Government of Honduras and enjoys diplomatic or official immunity;  

4)	If the person responsible for the crime committed against a Honduran has not been tried in the country where it was perpetrated nor has his extradition been requested or when having been tried he has escaped and failed to serve the sentence totally or partially; and,
5)	When in accordance with international agreements of which Honduras is a party, the crime is subject to Honduran criminal law for reasons other than those mentioned in the preceding numerals or seriously injures universally recognized human rights. Preference shall be given, however, to the claim of the State in whose territory the punishable act was committed provided that it asserts it before the respective criminal action is exercised in the competent Honduran court.

#### ARTICLE 6

Foreign criminal sentences regarding the crimes referred to in the preceding articles 3 and 4 shall not have the value of res judicata. However, the penalty that the convict has served, totally or partially, by virtue of any of said sentences, or those issued in relation to numerals 1), 2) and 4) of article 5, above, shall be deducted from that imposed in accordance with Honduran law if both are of similar nature; if they are not, the new penalty shall be prudently mitigated.

#### ARTICLE 7

With the exception of the cases stated in the preceding article, the foreign acquittal criminal sentence shall have the value of res judicata for all legal effects. The conviction shall have it to determine the recidivism or habitual nature of the convict, and for the civil effects of the sentence which shall be governed by Honduran Law.

#### ARTICLE 8

Honduran Criminal Law shall not apply to foreign Heads of State who are in the national territory nor to diplomatic agents and other persons who, according to International Law, enjoy immunity.

#### ARTICLE 9

Criminal laws have retroactive effect insofar as they favor the accused, even if upon their entry into force a final sentence has been issued and the convict is serving the sentence.

#### ARTICLE 10

In no case shall extradition be granted to Hondurans who having committed a crime abroad are found in the national territory. Extradition of foreigners may only be granted by virtue of Law or Treaty, for common crimes that merit a penalty of not less than one year of deprivation of liberty; and never for political crimes, even if a common crime results as a consequence thereof.

#### ARTICLE 11

Judicial authorities may not create any type of criminal figures.

#### ARTICLE 12

The general provisions of this Code shall apply to all crimes punished by special laws, insofar as the latter do not provide otherwise.

## TITLE II THE CRIME

#### ARTICLE 13

A crime may be committed by action or by omission and must necessarily be intentional or negligent.  

A crime is intentional when the result corresponds to the intention held when executing it or when the author knows, or is obliged to know, that as a consequence of the action or omission there is the possibility that a harmful effect constituting a crime will occur, notwithstanding which he executes the act and accepts, therefore, the consequences that derive therefrom.  
A crime is negligent when it is the result of recklessness, lack of skill or negligence or when it is the product of the non-observance of a law, a regulation or orders, resolutions or duties, considering the circumstances and personal situation of the offender. A negligent crime shall only be punishable in cases expressly determined by law.  

In no case may the penalty for a negligent crime be greater than that corresponding to that same crime if it had been committed intentionally; when this occurs, the penalty for the intentional crime reduced by one-sixth shall be imposed.

#### ARTICLE 13

A. For criminal purposes, political crimes are considered to be those included in Chapters I, II and III of Title XI and II, V, VI and VII of Title XII of Book Two of the Penal Code.  

Common crimes connected with political crimes are those that have a direct or immediate relationship with a political crime or are a natural and frequent means of preparing, carrying out or favoring it.

#### ARTICLE 14

A crime is consummated when all the elements of its legal classification concur therein.

#### ARTICLE 15

There is an attempt when, with the intention of committing a specific crime, unequivocal acts of execution are performed and it is not consummated due to causes beyond the agent's will.

#### ARTICLE 16

If the attempt is carried out with inadequate means or on improper objects, the penalty may be mitigated or the act declared not punishable, according to the dangerousness revealed by its author.

#### ARTICLE 17

Conspiracy and proposal to commit a crime are only punishable in cases where the law expressly declares so.  

Conspiracy exists when two or more persons agree to the execution of the crime.  
Proposal is configured when one who has resolved to commit a crime proposes its execution to another person or persons.

#### ARTICLE 18

The crime is considered committed at the moment of the action or omission, even when the moment of the result is different.

#### ARTICLE 19. The crime is considered committed

1)	In the place where the criminal activity of authors or participants was carried out, in whole or in part.  
2)	In the place where the result occurred or should have occurred.  

3)	In omissive crimes, in the place where the omitted action should have been executed.

#### ARTICLE 20

One who by error or by any other accident commits a crime to the detriment of a person other than the one against whom he has directed his action, shall be charged with the crime, but not the aggravating circumstances that proceed from the offended party or from ties with the latter. The mitigating circumstances that would arise from the act if it had been perpetrated to the harm of the other person, shall be considered in his favor.

#### ARTICLE 21

There is no crime if, on the occasion of performing a lawful act with due diligence, the author causes harm by mere accident.

## TITLE III

CAUSES THAT EXEMPT FROM LIABILITY

#### ARTICLE 22

The causes exempting from criminal liability are of three classes, namely:

1)	Causes of non-imputability.  
2)	Causes of justification.  

3)	Causes of non-culpability.

### CHAPTER I

CAUSES OF NON-IMPUTABILITY

#### ARTICLE 23. Not imputable is

1)	The minor under twelve (12) years of age. Both the latter and the person over said age but under eighteen (18) years of age shall be subject to a special law; and,

2)	Whoever at the moment of the action or omission suffers from psychosis, severe mental retardation or transitory psychosis and lacks, for that reason, the capacity to understand the unlawful character of the act or to determine himself in accordance with that understanding, except when the transitory mental disorder has been provoked by the agent intentionally or negligently.

### CHAPTER II

CAUSES OF JUSTIFICATION

#### ARTICLE 24. Exempt from criminal liability is

1)	Whoever acts in defense of his person or rights provided that the following circumstances concur:
a)	Unlawful aggression;  

b)	Rational necessity of the means employed to prevent or repel it; and,
c)	Lack of sufficient provocation on the part of the person defending himself.  

It shall be understood that the first two circumstances concur with respect to one who during the night repels the scaling or breaking of fences, walls or entrances of an inhabited house or apartment, or its dependencies, or employs violence against the individual foreign to it who is surprised within the indicated places. If the acts occur during the day, only the unlawful aggression shall be understood to concur.  

2)	One who acts in defense of the person or rights of his spouse or his relatives up to the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity, or relatives by adoption in the same degrees, provided that the circumstances provided in the preceding subparagraphs a) and b) concur, and that, in case sufficient provocation by the defended person has preceded, the defender has not participated in it;  

3)	One who acts in defense of the person or rights of a stranger, provided that the circumstances provided in the preceding paragraph concur and that the defender is not impelled by revenge, resentment or other illegitimate motive;  
4)	Whoever has committed an act compelled by the necessity of saving himself or saving another or others from a danger not caused by him voluntarily nor avoidable in another manner, provided that the act is proportionate to the danger.  

This exemption extends to one who has caused damage to another's property, if the following conditions concur:

a)	Reality of the harm sought to be avoided;  

b)	That said harm is greater than that caused to avoid it; and,
c)	That there is no other practicable and less harmful means to prevent it.  

One who had the duty to face the danger cannot allege state of necessity.  

5)	Whoever acts in fulfillment of a duty or in the legitimate exercise of a right, office or position.  
It shall be understood that this last circumstance exists with respect to the authority or its agents and the persons who concur in its aid, who in case of disobedience or resistance or to prevent the escape of a criminal employ proportionate means of repression, provided that formal warning precedes;  

6)	Whoever executes an act by legitimate obedience, provided that:

a)	The order emanates from competent authority;  
b)	The agent has the obligation to comply with it; and,

c)	The action or omission ordered does not violate or restrict the exercise of the rights and guarantees enshrined in the Constitution of the Republic and in the international treaties on human rights to which Honduras is a party.

### CHAPTER III

CAUSES OF NON-CULPABILITY

#### ARTICLE 25. Also not incurring criminal liability are

1)	Whoever acts impelled by irresistible physical force or insurmountable fear.  

2)	Whoever incurs in some omission, being impeded by legitimate or insurmountable cause.  

3)	Whoever in the rational belief that an unjust aggression exists against his person, reacts against the supposed aggressor, provided that the reaction is proportionate to the supposed risk.

## TITLE IV

CIRCUMSTANCES THAT MODIFY CRIMINAL LIABILITY

### CHAPTER I

MITIGATING CIRCUMSTANCES

#### ARTICLE 26. Mitigating circumstances are

1)	Those expressed in the preceding Title, when not all the requirements necessary to exempt from liability in their respective cases concur.  

2)	The guilty party being under twenty-one years of age and over seventy.  
3)	Executing the act in a state of intoxication, when this is not habitual or subsequent to the project of committing the crime, provided that these situations are scientifically proven.  

4)	Having been preceded immediately, on the part of the offended party, by provocation or threat proportionate to the crime.  
5)	Having executed the act in proximate vindication of a grave offense, caused to the author of the crime, his spouse or person with whom he lives maritally, his ascendants, descendants, siblings or relatives by affinity up to the second degree.  

6)	Acting by stimuli so powerful that they have naturally produced rapture or obsession.  

7)	The guilty party having sought, with effective means, to repair the harm caused or prevent its pernicious consequences.  
8)	If, being able to elude the action of justice by flight or other suitable means, the accused has presented himself voluntarily to the competent authority.  

9)	There being no other direct evidence in the proceeding than the confession of the accused.  
10)	Having proceeded impelled by collective or tumultuous suggestion, provided that the guilty party has not provoked it nor acted in it as director of the group.  

11)	The woman having acted under the influence of physiological disorders proper to her sex.  
12)	Having acted for noble, altruistic or compassionate motives.  

13)	The delinquent not having had the intention to cause harm of such gravity as that which he produced.  

14)	Any other circumstance analogous to the preceding ones.

### CHAPTER II

AGGRAVATING CIRCUMSTANCES

#### ARTICLE 27. The following are aggravating circumstances

1) Acting for trivial or despicable motives.
2) Executing the crime with treachery.

There is treachery when the guilty party commits any of the crimes against life and bodily integrity, employing means, methods or forms in the execution that tend

directly and especially to ensure it, without risk to his person that might proceed from the defense that the victim could make.
3) Committing the crime by means of price, reward or remunerative promise.

4) Executing it by means of flood, fire, poisons, explosion, derailment, stranding or damage of a vessel or other device that may cause great havoc.

5) Deliberately increasing the gravity of the crime, causing other harm unnecessary for its execution.

6) Acting with known premeditation or employing cunning, fraud or disguise
7) Abusing superiority or employing means that weaken the defense.

8) Acting with abuse of trust.

9) Taking advantage of the public character that the guilty party may have.
10) Employing means or causing circumstances to concur that add ignominy to the effects proper to the act.

11) Committing the crime on the occasion of fire, shipwreck, or other calamity or misfortune.
12) Executing it with the aid of armed people or of persons who ensure or provide impunity.

13) Executing the act in an uninhabited place or taking advantage of the darkness of night.
The Courts may not take this circumstance into consideration according to the nature and circumstances of the crime.

14) Committing the act with contempt or offense of the public authority, or in its presence, or where it exercises its functions.

15) Executing it in a place that deserves respect or reverence or in the dwelling of the victim, when the latter has not provoked the event.

16) Executing the act with offense or contempt of the respect that the victim deserves by reason of dignity, age or sex.

17) Executing it with scaling.

18) Executing the act with breaking of wall, ceiling or pavement, or with fracture of doors or windows.

19) Executing it in a gang.

20) Deliberately becoming intoxicated or drugged to prepare or execute the crime, provided that this situation is scientifically proven.

21) Committing it using an automobile, vessel or aircraft, or any other analogous means of sufficient efficacy to ensure the aggression or the escape.
22) Executing the crime by interrupting beforehand the means of communication, or after cutting or interrupting the exterior or interior electrical service, the elevator service at the place of the event or any of those that the guilty party may have to use.

23) The aggrieved party and the offender being linked by marriage or de facto union, or being ascendants and descendants to each other by consanguinity, or collaterals up to the fourth degree; by ties of adoption, or by affinity up to the second degree.
This circumstance may not be taken into consideration by the Courts, or be appreciated as mitigating, according to the nature, circumstances and effects of the crime.
24) The violation of special duties that relations of respect, friendship, gratitude, dependence or hospitality impose on the accused with respect to the victim.

25) Being a recidivist.
26) Taking advantage of non-imputable subjects for the commission of the crime.

### CHAPTER III

RECIDIVISM AND HABITUAL OFFENDING

#### ARTICLE 28

A person is a recidivist who incurs a new crime before five years have elapsed since the conviction for a previous crime committed in the country or abroad, whether having served the sentence or not. The indicated period of five years shall not include the time in which the agent remained deprived of liberty, by preventive detention or by the sentence.

#### ARTICLE 29

A person is considered a habitual offender who, having been convicted for two or more previous crimes, committed in the country or abroad, whether having served the sentences or not, manifests a definite tendency toward crime, in the opinion of the court, by the way of life he leads, his inclination to idleness, inferiority of the environment in which he acts, relations he cultivates, motives of the crime, and other antecedents of analogous character.

#### ARTICLE 30

There is no recidivism or habitual offending between intentional crime and negligent crime; between common crime and military crime; between common crime and political crime; between military crime and political crime; and, between crime and misdemeanor.

## TITLE V

CONCURRENCE OF OFFENDERS AND OF CRIMES

### CHAPTER I

PARTICIPATION IN THE CRIME

#### ARTICLE 31. Criminally responsible for the crime are the principals and the accomplices

#### ARTICLE 32

Considered principals are those who take direct part in the execution of the act, those who force or directly induce others to execute it and those who cooperate in the execution of the act by an act without which it would not have been carried out.
In crimes by omission, principals are those who fail to do what the law commands, cause the omission or cooperate in it.

#### ARTICLE 33

Accomplices are those who, not being included in the preceding Article, cooperate in the execution of the act by prior or simultaneous acts.
If from the particular circumstances of the proceeding it results that the accused of complicity only wished to cooperate in an act less serious than that committed by the principal, the penalty shall be applied to the accomplice only by reason of the act he intended to execute.

#### ARTICLE 34

When dealing with crimes committed by a crowd, the following provisions shall apply:

1) If the gathering had as its object to commit specific crimes, all those who participated materially in their execution shall be liable as principals, as well as those who, without having had material participation, assumed the character of directors.
2) If the gathering did not have as its object to commit crimes and these were committed afterwards by impulse of the crowd in tumult, all those who participated materially in the execution shall be liable as accomplices; and as principals, those who took on the character of instigators, whether or not they had material participation in the execution of the criminal acts. Those who participated in the crowd and were not principals or accomplices shall be exempt from penalty.

#### ARTICLE 34

A. For crimes committed in the name and on behalf of a legal person, the legal representatives thereof who made the unlawful action or omission possible shall be personally liable.
Civil liability, however, shall fall upon the legal person.

### CHAPTER II

CONCURRENCE OF CRIMES

#### ARTICLE 35

The guilty party of two or more crimes shall be imposed all the penalties corresponding to the various infractions.

The sentenced person shall serve his sentences simultaneously, if possible. When it is not possible, or if any of the penalties results illusory therefrom, he shall serve them successively, beginning with the greater ones, or those assigned to the most serious crimes.

However, the duration of the penalties accumulated for several crimes shall not exceed thirty years.

#### ARTICLE 36

The provisions of the preceding article shall not apply in the case that a single act constitutes two or more crimes or when one of them is a necessary means to commit the other.

In these cases the penalty corresponding to the crime that has the greater sanction assigned shall be imposed, increased by one fourth.

#### ARTICLE 37

When the same crime against property is committed two or more times, whether at a single moment or at different moments, through actions or omissions executed in fulfillment of a preconceived plan or taking advantage of identical or similar circumstances, such crimes shall be considered as a single continuing offense.

In such situation the most severe penalty shall be applied to the agent, increased by two thirds. In case of doubt, the penalty having the highest maximum shall be considered the most severe.

The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not apply if the imposition of all penalties applicable to the concurrent crimes is more favorable to the offender.

What is prescribed in the last part of the second paragraph of this Article and in the preceding paragraph shall be applicable to what is established in article 36.

## TITLE VI PENALTIES

### CHAPTER I CLASSES OF PENALTIES

#### ARTICLE 38. Penalties are divided into principal and accessory

Principal penalties are: imprisonment, prison, fine, absolute disqualification and special disqualification.

Accessory penalties are: civil interdiction and confiscation.

Absolute or special disqualification shall be imposed as an accessory penalty to imprisonment, provided that the law does not impose it as a principal penalty for a particular crime.

### CHAPTER II

DURATION, NATURE AND EFFECTS OF PENALTIES

#### ARTICLE 39

The penalty of imprisonment subjects the convict to work for the duration of the sentence in public works or in labor within the establishment, in accordance with the law that regulates the penitentiary system.

#### ARTICLE 40

When imprisonment exceeds three years, it shall be served in a national penitentiary; and when it does not exceed said period, it must be served in departmental or sectional jails, without prejudice to the provisions of the following article.

#### ARTICLE 41

The Executive Power, whenever it deems it convenient for reasons of greater security or for any other reason, may, with the knowledge of the Supreme Court of

Justice, order that convicts sentenced to imprisonment who are serving their sentences in other jails be transferred to a penitentiary.

#### ARTICLE 42

If imprisonment does not exceed six months, women of good reputation and persons over seventy years of age or in poor health may be detained in their own homes. The same treatment shall be given to these persons if the applicable penalty were prison.

#### ARTICLE 43

Women and males under twenty-one years of age and over eighteen shall serve the penalty of imprisonment in special establishments; and, if there are none, in different and independent sections, where they shall perform work appropriate to their condition.

#### ARTICLE 44

The following are exempt from the obligation to perform work in the manner set forth in Article 39:

1)	Convicts who have reached seventy years of age.

2)	Convicts who have a physical impediment or suffer from an illness that makes work impossible or dangerous for them, in accordance with the corresponding medical opinion.

#### ARTICLE 45

When penal centers are not adequately equipped, the penalty depriving liberty of women who are pregnant shall not be executed until six (6) months after childbirth has occurred. If the child dies, the penalty depriving liberty shall begin to be served four (4) weeks after childbirth or miscarriage. In these cases, and in that of preventive detention, the provisions of article 42 shall apply.

#### ARTICLE 46

Convicts for political crimes shall be kept separate from convicts for common crimes.

#### ARTICLE 47

The penalty of prison subjects the convict to deprivation of liberty in local jails, with the obligation to work in labor within the establishment.

#### ARTICLE 48

Absolute disqualification applies to public offices or positions, political rights and professional titles during the duration of the sentence and produces:

1)	Deprivation of all public offices or positions and exercise of professional titles that the convict was in possession of, even when the offices are by popular election.

2)	Deprivation of all political rights and incapacity to obtain them.

3)	Incapacity to obtain the public offices or positions, professions and rights mentioned.

#### ARTICLE 49

The penalty of special disqualification applies to a particular public office or position, political right or professional title for the duration of the sentence and produces:

1)	Deprivation of the office, position, right or exercise of the profession upon which it falls.

2)	Incapacity to obtain said office, position, right, profession or similar ones.

#### ARTICLE 50. (REPEALED)

#### ARTICLE 51

The penalty of fine obliges the convict to pay to the State the sum of money that this Code or special laws determine or that the judge sets in each case within the legal limits, taking into account the economic capacity of the convict and the severity of the damage caused by the crime.

Fines shall be made effective in the General Treasury of the Republic or in the Institution of the National Financial System that the Secretariat of State in the Office of Finance has authorized for such purpose.

#### ARTICLE 52

Upon granting of real or personal security, payment of the fine in installments may be authorized, the amount and payment date of which shall be set by the judge, taking into account the economic conditions of the sentenced person.

#### ARTICLE 53

If the criminal fine is not paid in whole or in part, whether voluntarily or by means of enforcement, it shall be commuted to prison at the rate of one (1) day for every five Lempiras (L.5.00) when it corresponds to a misdemeanor, or to imprisonment at the rate of one (1) day for every ten Lempiras (L.10.00) per day when it corresponds to a crime.

The commuted prison pursuant to the preceding paragraph may not exceed six (6) months and imprisonment five (5) years. The convict, however, may pay the fine that has been imposed upon him, in which case his release shall be decreed. From the fine shall be deducted the value corresponding to the time that the convict has been detained, at the rate of five Lempiras (L.5.00) per day when the fine corresponds to a misdemeanor and ten Lempiras (L.10.00) per day when it corresponds to a crime.

The imprisonment and prison to which this Article refers shall not give rise to the application of accessory penalties nor to the provision of services in public works or other kinds of labor inside or outside the penal establishment.

Payment of the maximum fine shall extinguish any liability for the quantifiable excess thereof.

#### ARTICLE 54

Civil interdiction consists of the suspension of the rights of parental authority, guardianship, custody and administration of property; but the interdicted person may dispose of his own property by will.

#### ARTICLE 55

Confiscation consists of the forfeiture of the effects that result from a crime or misdemeanor, and of the instruments with which it is executed, unless they belong to a third party not responsible for the act.

#### ARTICLE 56

In all cases in which the payment of costs is appropriate, both procedural and personal costs shall be included and, in addition, the expenses occasioned by the trial and not included in the costs, which shall be assessed in the same manner as the former.

#### ARTICLE 57

If the property of the guilty party is not sufficient to cover the pecuniary liabilities, these shall be satisfied in the following order:

1)	Procedural and personal costs.

2)	Expenses occasioned by the trial.

3)	Compensation for damages and losses.

4)	The fine.

In case of insolvency or bankruptcy, these credits shall be ranked as a single one among those that do not enjoy preference.

#### ARTICLE 58

The penalties of imprisonment and prison shall begin to be counted from the day on which the convict is apprehended, deducting the time that he remains released.  
Absolute disqualification and special disqualification, as principal penalties, shall be counted from the declaration of guilt or of having been declared with grounds for prosecution.

#### ARTICLE 59. Shall not be considered as penalties

1)	The detention and preventive imprisonment of the accused.  

2)	The suspension from employment or public office agreed upon during the process or to institute it.  

3)	The fines and other corrections that, in the exercise of governmental or disciplinary powers, superiors impose on their subordinates or those under their administration.  

4)	The deprivations of rights (sic) and the reparations that civil laws establish in penal form.

#### ARTICLE 60

For the purposes of this Code, days shall be counted as twenty-four consecutive hours, and months and years from date to date.

#### ARTICLE 61. Only the following shall be commutable by right

1)	Prison at the rate of five Lempiras (L.5.00) per day; and,

2)	Imprisonment of up to five (5) years, at the rate of ten Lempiras (L.10.00) per day.  

Commutations shall be paid in cash at the General Treasury of the Republic or at the institution of the national financial system that the Secretariat of State in the Office of Finance has authorized for this purpose.  
Commutation shall not proceed in case of recidivism.

### CHAPTER III

PENALTIES THAT CARRY OTHER ACCESSORY PENALTIES

#### ARTICLE 62

Imprisonment for more than five years carries as accessory penalties absolute disqualification for the duration of the sentence and civil interdiction.

#### ARTICLE 63

The penalty of imprisonment not exceeding five years carries as accessory penalties special disqualification and civil interdiction.

#### ARTICLE 64

The conviction sentence shall include confiscation and payment of costs in cases where applicable.

### CHAPTER IV

APPLICATION OF PENALTIES

#### ARTICLE 65

Whenever the law generally prescribes the penalty for a crime, it shall be understood to impose it on the perpetrator of a consummated crime.

#### ARTICLE 66

The perpetrator of an attempt and the accomplice of a consummated crime shall be punished with the penalty applicable to the perpetrator of the consummated crime reduced by one third.  

In the case that the crime is punishable with deprivation of liberty for life, they shall be given twenty (20) to thirty (30) years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 67

The accomplice of an attempt shall have four to five sixths of the penalty applicable to the perpetrator of a consummated crime reduced.  

In the case that the crime is punishable with deprivation of liberty for life, the accomplice of an attempt shall be given the penalty of twenty (20) to thirty (30) years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 68

When this Code provides that a penalty be increased or decreased by a determined quota or fraction, the maximum and minimum shall be increased or decreased, as the case may be, in the corresponding proportion, thus establishing the new penalty, within whose limits its application shall be graduated according to the following Article.

#### ARTICLE 69

The judge shall determine in the sentence the penalty applicable to the accused within the maximum and minimum prescribed by law for each crime and the circumstances in which it was committed. For this purpose, he shall take into account his personal background, his greater or lesser dangerousness, the mitigating and aggravating circumstances that have occurred in the act assessed both by their number and, above all, by their magnitude and importance, and the greater or lesser extent of the harms produced by the crime, particularly those of an economic nature.  

In the reasoning of the sentence, the judge shall expressly record the circumstances indicated in the preceding paragraph that he has taken into account to determine the extent of the penalty.

### CHAPTER V

CONDITIONAL SUSPENSION OF THE EXECUTION OF THE PENALTY

#### ARTICLE 70

In the conviction sentence, the courts may suspend the execution of the penalty, for a probationary period of five years, in the case of crimes, and two years, in the case of misdemeanors, if the following requirements are met:

1)	That the sentence consists of deprivation of liberty not exceeding three years.  
2)	That the accused has not been previously convicted of a crime or misdemeanor.  

3)	That the nature or modalities of the criminal act charged, the character or background of the convict and the motives that drove him to commit the crime, duly investigated, lead the Judge to the conviction that the agent is not dangerous and may presume, consequently, that he will not commit crimes again.

#### ARTICLE 71

The benefit established in the preceding Article shall not be granted to anyone who, according to the rules given in this Code, must be subjected to security measures.

#### ARTICLE 72

The conditional suspension of the execution of the penalty shall not extend to accessory penalties and other effects of the conviction. Nor shall it exempt from civil obligations arising from the crime.

#### ARTICLE 73

The judge of the case shall make to the convict, personally, the necessary warnings about the nature of the benefit granted and the reasons that may produce its cessation, which shall be recorded in the file by minutes.

#### ARTICLE 74

The conditionally suspended penalty must be enforced when the convicted person within the established periods:

1)	Commits a crime again or does not fulfill the civil obligations arising from the crime or misdemeanor.  

2)	Is imposed another sentence for a crime or misdemeanor committed prior to the one that was the subject of the suspension of the penalty.

#### ARTICLE 75

If during the probationary period, the offender does not incur in the acts referred to in the preceding article, the conviction shall be considered extinguished by resolution of the sentencing Court.

### CHAPTER VI

CONDITIONAL RELEASE

#### ARTICLE 76

The court of first instance that heard the case may grant conditional release to the convict who has served more than half of the sentence, in cases of sentences to imprisonment exceeding three years and not exceeding twelve; or who has served three quarters of the sentence, when it exceeds twelve years, and the following circumstances also concur, in both cases:

1)	That the convict has not been previously convicted by final judgment for another intentional crime.  
2)	Having observed good conduct during his stay in the penal establishment and acquired habits of work, order and morality, that demonstrate his repentance and purpose of amendment.  

3)	That he has returned the thing and repaired the damage in cases of crimes against property and fulfilled the other civil obligations arising from the crime, or demonstrates his economic incapacity to satisfy the latter.

#### ARTICLE 77

In the resolution granting the benefit of conditional release, the Judge may impose any of the security measures indicated in subsections d),
e) and f) of Article 83.

#### ARTICLE 78

The probationary period to which one who enjoys the benefit of conditional release shall be subject shall be equal to what remains to complete the imposed sentence.  

If during the probationary period he commits a new crime or violates the imposed security measures, the conditional release shall be revoked and the part of the sentence that he has failed to serve shall be enforced, without computing in it the time that he has remained at liberty.

#### ARTICLE 79

Once the probationary period has elapsed without the beneficiary having incurred in the acts that give rise to the revocation of conditional release, the penalty shall be considered extinguished.

## TITLE VII

SECURITY MEASURES

#### ARTICLE 80

Security measures shall not be decreed without legal provision authorizing them, or outside the cases that the law determines.

#### ARTICLE 81

Security measures may be decreed by the Judge in the sentence, whether conviction or acquittal, but at any time during the proceedings, before the ruling, said official may order, on a provisional basis, the internment of the non-imputable person covered by numeral 2 of Article 23, in the corresponding establishment.

#### ARTICLE 82

Unless otherwise provided by law, security measures shall be applied for an indeterminate period. At any time judges may modify or revoke their resolutions in this regard, if the state of dangerousness of the accused is modified or ceases.

#### ARTICLE 83. The security measures that may be applied are the following

1)	Internment in a psychiatric establishment.  

2)	Internment in a work institution or penal farm.  

3)	Internment in a re-educational establishment or special treatment facility.  

4)	Supervised release.  

5)	Prohibition from residing in a determined place.  

6)	Prohibition from attending certain places.  

7)	Bond for good conduct.  
8)	Expulsion of foreigners.  

When the measures included in numerals 4), 5) and 7) are applied, the sanctioned person shall be obligated to declare before the Judge having knowledge of the matter, his current domicile and to inform of any changes to said domicile.

#### ARTICLE 84

Judges who declare accused persons exempt from penalty, in the cases of numerals 2 and 3 of Article 23, shall order their internment in a psychiatric establishment, for at least one year.

#### ARTICLE 85

They may also order, after the penalty has been served if they still consider the offender dangerous, that the deaf-mute or the person suffering from mental abnormality that does not result in absolute non-imputability, be interned in an educational establishment or special treatment facility.

#### ARTICLE 86

The offenders referred to in Article 29 shall be subjected, according to the degree of dangerousness they demonstrate, to the work regime corresponding to the institutions mentioned in numeral 2 of Article 83; internment that shall be decreed when, once the sentence has been served, the Judge considers that the penalty has been ineffective with regard to the rehabilitation of the offender.

#### ARTICLE 87

In the cases of Article 16, the Judge shall subject the accused, according to their degree of dangerousness, to a special work regime in one of the institutions mentioned in numeral 3 of Article 83.

#### ARTICLE 88

Where there is no adequate establishment, the internment measure, according to its nature, shall be served in an annex or special section of a penal establishment.

#### ARTICLE 89

The internment measure shall not cease, except by virtue of a judicial resolution issued with hearing of the Public Prosecutor, and subject to medical opinion, demonstrating that the accused may be subjected to supervised release without danger of causing harm.

#### ARTICLE 90

Supervised release, while the causes that motivated it last, shall consist for the mentally ill, drug addicts or habitual drunkards, in entrusting them to the care of their family or a guardian, under the immediate inspection of the competent authority, with the obligation to subject them to medical treatment and to periodically inform the respective judge.  

In other cases the surveillance shall correspond to the judicial police in the manner provided by the judge.  

When applying this measure, the judge shall prescribe the rules of conduct designed to prevent new violations of criminal law. The judicial police shall be organized and shall be under the dependence of the Supreme Court of Justice.

#### ARTICLE 91

When circumstances so require, the judge may, at his prudent discretion, impose on the convicted person who has served a penalty or a security measure, the prohibition from residing in a determined place, for a minimum of one year.

#### ARTICLE 92

The prohibition from attending certain places shall be imposed on the person convicted of an offense under the influence of alcohol or toxic, narcotic, or stupefacient drugs. These prohibitions shall last at least one year and their violation shall require that it be replaced by supervised release.

#### ARTICLE 93

The bond for good conduct consists of personal, mortgage, pledge or deposit guarantee, provided to the satisfaction of the judge and for the term indicated in the sentence, that the dangerous subject will not perpetrate new punishable acts and that he will comply with the conduct norms imposed on him during a probationary period, which shall not be less than one year nor more than five.  

This measure shall be applied in all cases in which the judge deems it appropriate, especially to the perpetrators of danger offenses, without prejudice to the penalty when applicable. The bond shall be made effective in favor of the Treasury when the person subjected to this measure violates the imposed conduct norms; otherwise, at the end of the term, the restitution of the deposited sum, the extinction of the bond or the cancellation of the pledge or mortgage obligation that has been constituted shall be ordered.

#### ARTICLE 94

The judge who imposes a penalty of more than three years of imprisonment on a foreigner, or when the latter is a repeat offender, whatever the penalty, may decree his expulsion from the national territory, in accordance with the law, which shall be executed once the penalty has been served.

#### ARTICLE 95

The imposition of security measures shall not prevent the administrative expulsion of the foreigner in the cases provided for by Law.

## TITLE VIII

EXTINCTION OF CRIMINAL LIABILITY AND ITS EFFECTS

#### ARTICLE 96. Criminal liability is extinguished

1.	By the death of the convict;  

2.	By the fulfillment of the sentence, which produces by law the rehabilitation of the convicted person;  

3.	By amnesty, which extinguishes the penalty and completely all its legal effects, except as provided in Article 103;  

4.	By pardon, which only extinguishes the principal penalty when it does not include the accessory ones; but does not favor the pardoned person with regard to recidivism and other effects of the penalties that the laws expressly determine.  
The pardoned person may not inhabit, without the consent of the offended party, the domicile in which the latter lives, or, in his absence, his spouse, ascendants, descendants and siblings. This

prohibition shall last for the entire time that, had he not been pardoned, the sentence should last;  
5.	By the express pardon of the offended party or of whoever has his legal representation in offenses that are only prosecutable by virtue of a complaint or denunciation by the aggrieved party. The provisions of this paragraph shall not be applicable to those responsible for the crime of rape nor to those responsible for crimes or misdemeanors committed to the detriment of boys or girls.  

In crimes or misdemeanors committed against incapacitated persons not included in the last part of the preceding paragraph, judges or courts shall reject the effectiveness of the pardon granted by their representatives if there are rational grounds to believe that it was granted in consideration of pecuniary interests or direct personal interests of the respective representative. In such case, they shall order the continuation of the proceeding with the intervention of the Public Prosecutor or the fulfillment of the sentence.  

To reject the pardon referred to in the preceding paragraph, the judge or court must hear the representative of the incapacitated person.  

6.	By the prescription of the criminal action; and,
7.	By the prescription of the penalty.  

Notwithstanding the provisions of numerals 6) and 7) of this Article, the criminal action and the penalty are imprescriptible in those crimes to which deprivation of liberty for life is applicable or where this is established as its maximum limit.

#### ARTICLE 97. Criminal action prescribes

1) By the passage of a period equal to the maximum duration of the sanction established for the crime, increased by half, if it were imprisonment.  
However, the prescription term shall not be, in any case, less than two (2) years;  

2) At five (5) years when it concerns an act for which disqualification is established as the principal penalty.  

3) In three (3) years, when the fine is imposed as the principal penalty; and

4) At six (6) months, if it concerns misdemeanors.  
The preceding rules are understood without prejudice to those established by the Constitution of the Republic.

#### ARTICLE 98

The prescription of criminal action shall begin to run from the day on which the offense was committed; and in continuing crimes, from the day on which the last act was executed or the last action was carried out.  

In the case of attempt, the prescription shall be counted from the day on which its execution was suspended.  

The prescription of criminal action in the crime of bankruptcy shall run from the day on which the declaration of fraudulent or culpable insolvency becomes final.

#### ARTICLE 99

The prescription of criminal action shall be interrupted from the moment proceedings are initiated against the guilty party, with the prescription time running again from the moment its prosecution is paralyzed for any circumstance.

#### ARTICLE 100

Penalties imposed by final judgment prescribe within the terms set forth in Article 97.  
The time of this prescription shall be counted from the date on which the judgment becomes final, or from the day of the breach of the sentence, as applicable.

#### ARTICLE 101

The prescription of the penalty shall be interrupted, rendering the elapsed time without effect, when the convict commits another crime, without prejudice to it beginning to run again.

#### ARTICLE 102

The exercise of the action to claim civil liabilities derived from the crime does not interrupt its prescription, that of the criminal action or of the penalty.

#### ARTICLE 103

Amnesty and pardon do not extinguish the right to compensation for damage caused by the crime.

#### ARTICLE 104

When the convict appears or is apprehended after half the time necessary to prescribe the criminal action or the penalty has elapsed, the Judge must take said period into account to make a reduction of one-third to one-half in the penalty that corresponds to apply or in that imposed by the judgment.  
This reduction shall not apply to prescriptions that do not exceed one year.

## TITLE IX CIVIL LIABILITY

#### ARTICLE 105

Anyone who incurs criminal liability for a crime or misdemeanor is also civilly liable.

#### ARTICLE 106

The exemption from criminal liability declared in numerals 2 and 3 of Article 23, in numeral 2 of Article 24 and in numeral 1 of Article 25, does not include exemption from civil liability, which shall be made effective subject to the following rules:

1) In the cases of non-imputability mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the mentally ill and mentally deficient persons or deaf-mutes are liable with their property for the damages they cause. If they are insolvent, those who have them under their authority or legal guardianship shall respond subsidiarily, unless they demonstrate their lack of culpability.  

In both situations there shall be room for the benefit of competence.  

2) In the case of numeral 2 of Article 24, the persons for whose benefit the harm was prevented are liable in proportion to the benefit they have obtained.  

The courts shall determine, according to their prudent discretion, the proportional share for which each interested party must respond.  
3) In the case of numeral 1 of Article 25, those who caused the fear or exercised the force shall respond.

#### ARTICLE 107. Civil liability comprises

1) Restitution.  

2) Reparation of material and moral damages.  

3) Indemnification for losses.

#### ARTICLE 108

Restitution must be made of the same thing, whenever possible, with payment for deterioration or impairment at the judgment of the Court and even if the thing is in the possession of a third party who has acquired it legally, without prejudice to their right to recover against whom it may concern. The latter does not apply to a third party who has acquired the thing in the form and with the requirements established by law to make it non-recoverable.

#### ARTICLE 109

Reparation of material damage shall be made through a pecuniary indemnification that shall be fixed by valuing the extent of all patrimonial damages caused by the punishable action or omission, taking into account the price of the thing, and whenever possible, the sentimental value it had for the aggrieved party; and it shall only be required when there is no room for restitution.

#### ARTICLE 110

Reparation of moral damage, in offenses against honor, dignity or decency, or in other cases of damage to interests of a moral order, shall consist of a pecuniary indemnification that the Judge shall determine prudently according to the circumstances of the offense, the conditions of the offended person and the nature and consequences that have occurred or are possible from the injury suffered.

#### ARTICLE 111

Indemnification for losses shall comprise not only those that have been caused to the offended party, but also those that have been inflicted, by reason of the crime, on their family or on a third party. The Court shall regulate the amount of this indemnification under the same terms established in Articles 109 and 110 for reparation of damage.

#### ARTICLE 112

The obligation to restore, repair damages and indemnify losses is transmitted to the heirs of the responsible party.  
The action to request restitution, reparation and indemnification is likewise transmitted to the heirs of the injured party.

#### ARTICLE 113

In case there are two or more persons who must respond civilly for a crime, the judgment must specify the share of liability of each one.  
The principals and accomplices, however, each within their respective class, shall be jointly and severally liable among themselves for their shares and subsidiarily for those corresponding to those of the other class.  

Subsidiary liability shall be made effective, first in the property of the principals and then in that of the accomplices.  
Both in cases where joint and several liability is made effective and subsidiary liability, the right to recover of the one who has paid for another shall remain intact.

#### ARTICLE 114

Whoever, without having participated in any crime, has obtained from it some economic benefit, is obligated to return the amount by which they have profited.

#### ARTICLE 115

Civil liability for crime is extinguished according to the modes established by the Civil Code with respect to obligations of this nature.

## TITLE I

CRIMES AGAINST LIFE AND BODILY INTEGRITY

### CHAPTER I HOMICIDE

#### ARTICLE 116

Whoever causes the death of a person without the circumstances mentioned in the following Articles of this Chapter being present, commits the crime of simple homicide, and shall incur the penalty of fifteen (15) to twenty (20) years of imprisonment.  
The penalty shall be twenty (20) to thirty (30) years of imprisonment, when the victim of the crime is a judicial authority, police officer, member of the Public Prosecutor's Office, official or employee of Penal Centers, when the crime is committed on the occasion of or in the exercise of their position or function.

#### ARTICULO 117

A person is guilty of murder who causes the death of another person by executing it with the concurrence of any of the following circumstances:
1)	Treachery;  

2)	With known premeditation;  
3)	By means of flooding, fire, poisoning, explosion, derailment, overturning, running aground or shipwreck or other device that may cause great havoc, provided there is malice and intent; and,

4)	With cruelty, deliberately and inhumanly increasing the pain of the victim.  
The penalty for murder shall be twenty (20) to thirty (30) years of imprisonment and when committed by means of payment, reward or remunerative promise, or accompanied by robbery or rape, the penalty shall be thirty (30) years to life deprivation of liberty.

#### ARTICULO 118

A person is guilty of parricide who causes the death of any of their ascendants or descendants, their spouse or the person with whom they live in a marital relationship, and shall suffer the penalty of thirty (30) to forty (40) years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICULO 119

If death has been produced by several persons fighting among themselves, confusedly and tumultuously without being able to determine the cause of the injuries with mortal effect, a penalty of three to six years of imprisonment shall be imposed on all those who have exercised violence upon the victim.

#### ARTICULO 120

Whoever with the purpose of causing injuries to a person produces their death when the means employed should not reasonably have caused it, shall be sanctioned with the penalty applicable to simple homicide reduced by one third.

#### ARTICULO 121

The perpetrator of a culpable homicide shall be punished with three (3) to five (5) years.  

If the perpetrator had committed the act as a consequence of being in a state of intoxication or having consumed drugs prohibited by law, they shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years.

#### ARTICULO 122. (DEROGADO)

#### ARTICULO 123

The mother who, to conceal her dishonor, causes the death of a child who has not reached three (3) days of age, shall be sanctioned with six (6) to nine (9) years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICULO 124

Whoever attempts suicide shall be subject to a security measure consisting of adequate psychiatric treatment.

#### ARTICULO 125

Whoever induces another to commit suicide or provides assistance to do so, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to six years, if the suicide is consummated. In the event that the suicide is not consummated, the collaborator in the attempt thereof shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one to three years.

Abortion is the death of a human being at any time during pregnancy or during childbirth. Whoever intentionally causes an abortion shall be punished:

The same sanctions shall apply to medical practitioners, paramedics, nurses, midwives or birth attendants who commit or participate in the commission of abortion.

The woman who produces her own abortion or consents that another person cause it, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

A person commits the crime of injuries who causes damage that affects the body or the physical or mental health of another person.

The mutilation of a principal limb or organ of a human being, executed purposely, shall be punished with imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years; if it is a non-principal limb or organ, with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

#### ARTICLE 140

Carnal access with a person of either sex by means of violence or threat of causing to the victim, their spouse or domestic partner, or one of their relatives within the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity, a serious and imminent harm, constitutes the crime of rape.

Special cases of rape are carnal access with a person of either sex when any of the following circumstances occur:
1)	That the victim is under fourteen (14) and over twelve (12) years of age;

2)	That the victim is deprived of reason or will or when for any cause cannot offer resistance.
The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever intentionally drugs or intoxicates a person for the purpose of raping them;

3)	When the perpetrator is in charge of the guard or custody of the victim and takes advantage of their position of authority to have access to them; and,
4)	When the culprit impersonates another person.

The perpetrator of the crime of rape shall be punished with imprisonment of nine (9) to thirteen (13) years. If the victim is under twelve (12) or over seventy (70) years of age or if the rape is committed by more than one person, or by a repeat offender, the penalty shall be fifteen (15) to twenty (20) years.
The penalty referred to in the preceding paragraph shall also apply to those who knowingly being carriers of Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome/Human Immunodeficiency Virus (AIDS/HIV) or an incurable contagious disease, commit rape.

For the purposes of this Article, carnal access shall be understood as that which occurs vaginally, anally or orally.

#### ARTICLE 141

Whoever, taking advantage of the conditions or employing the means indicated in the preceding Article, makes another person a victim of acts of lewdness other than carnal access, shall be punished with three (3) to five (5) years of imprisonment.

When the acts of lewdness consist of the introduction of objects or instruments of any nature into the sexual organs or other natural or artificial orifices that simulate the sexual organs of the body of the victim, the culprit shall be punished with nine (9) to thirteen (13) years of imprisonment.

Any other indecent abuse committed with any of the circumstances provided in this Article shall be punished with a penalty of two (2) to four (4) years of imprisonment.

The penalties applicable to the indecent abuses referred to in this article and the first paragraph of the preceding one shall be increased by one third if the offended person is under fourteen (14) years of age even when they consented to the act, or if being over that age the victim suffers from mental illness or incomplete or retarded psychological development or is deprived of reason or will or when for any cause cannot offer resistance.

#### ARTICLE 143

Whoever in a public place or place exposed to the public executes obscene acts or utters expressions of analogous character commits outrage to decency. This crime shall be punished with a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand (L.10,000.00) Lempiras.

Whoever with dishonest intentions and by means of force, intimidation or deceit abducts or detains a person over eighteen (18) years of age, shall incur in the crime of abduction and shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

The abduction of a person under eighteen (18) years of age shall be punished with the penalty provided in the preceding Article, increased by one third.

## TITLE III

CRIMES AGAINST HONOR

### CHAPTER I

SLANDER, INSULT AND DEFAMATION

#### ARTICLE 155

Slander or false imputation of a crime of those that give rise to ex officio proceedings, shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to three (3) years.  
If the slandered person so requests, the operative part of the judgment declaring the slander shall be published in one of the newspapers with the largest circulation in the country, at the expense of the accused.

#### ARTICLE 156

The person accused of the crime of slander shall be exempt from all punishment by proving the criminal act that he had imputed.

#### ARTICLE 157

Whoever utters an expression or performs an action in dishonor, discredit or contempt of another person shall be punished for insult with imprisonment of one (1) to two (2) years.

#### ARTICLE 158

The person accused of insult shall not be admitted proof of the truth of the imputation, except when the offended party is a public official or employee and it concerns facts relating to the exercise of his office. In this case the accused shall be acquitted if he proves the imputation to be true.

#### ARTICLE 159

When the insults are reciprocal, the Judge may, according to the circumstances, declare them not punishable with respect to both parties or to one of them.  

The judge shall have the same power when it concerns insults uttered in a state of anger, determined by the unjust act of another and immediately after learning of it.

#### ARTICLE 160

Defamation is committed, and the penalty for slander or insult, as applicable, increased by one third, shall be imposed on the guilty party, when the imputations constituting insult or slander are made in a form or by means of dissemination that may incite hatred or public contempt against the offended party.

#### ARTICLE 161

Whoever publishes or reproduces, by any means, insults or slander inflicted by another, shall be punished as the author of the insults or slander in question.

### CHAPTER II COMMON PROVISIONS

#### ARTICLE 162

The crime of slander, insult or defamation is committed not only manifestly, but also by means of allegories, caricatures, emblems or allusions.

#### ARTICLE 163. There shall be no grounds to continue the prosecution for insult, slander or defamation

1)	If the accused publicly retracts before answering the complaint or in the act of doing so and the offended party accepts the retraction.  

2)	If in the case of concealed or equivocal slander or insults, the accused gives satisfactory explanations before answering the complaint or at the moment of doing so.

#### ARTICLE 164

The owners, managers or directors of media outlets are obliged to exhibit the signature covering the original writing, or the magnetic tape or

film containing the recordings or images, in whose publications slander, insult or defamation has occurred; and failing to do so, they shall be responsible for the crime in question.

#### ARTICLE 165

If the offended party so requests, the directors and failing that, the owners or managers of the media in which the slander, insult or defamation has been made public, shall insert in them the retraction, satisfactory explanation or conviction judgment, within three (3) days following the date of its receipt or the term that the court has set.  

The violation of this rule, after a second request of equal term, shall be sanctioned with a fine of fifteen thousand (L.15,000.00) to thirty thousand (L.30,000.00) Lempiras, without prejudice to the respective publication.

#### ARTICLE 166

The crimes of slander, insult or defamation can only be prosecuted by virtue of a complaint by the aggrieved party, except when the offense is directed against public authority or State institutions, and, in general, if they constitute another crime specially punished in this Code.  

For this purpose, the heads of friendly or allied nations, their diplomatic representatives and others who, according to International Law, should be included in this provision are also deemed to be authority. In these cases proceedings may only be initiated at the request of the Executive Power.

#### ARTICLE 167

No one may bring an action for slander or insult caused in trial without prior authorization from the Judge or Court hearing it.

#### ARTICLE 168

If the offended party dies before the expiration of the term set for the prescription of the action, or the crime was committed against the memory of a deceased person, the complaint may be filed by the spouse or any of the ascendants, descendants and siblings of the deceased or his heirs.

#### ARTICLE 169

The pardon of the offended party extinguishes the crimes of slander, insult and defamation against private individuals, or the penalty as the case may be.

## TITLE IV

CRIMES AGAINST CIVIL STATUS AND THE ORDER OF THE FAMILY

### CHAPTER I

SIMULATION OF BIRTHS AND USURPATION OF CIVIL STATUS

#### ARTICLE 170

The simulation of births and the substitution of one child for another, shall be punished with a penalty of three (3) to six (6) years of imprisonment. The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever:

1)	Conceals or exposes a child with intent to make him lose his civil status;  

2)	Falsely reports or registers in the National Registry of Persons the birth, death or any other fact that alters the civil status of a person or knowingly takes advantage of the false registration; and
3)	Usurps the civil status of another person.

### CHAPTER II

CELEBRATION OF ILLEGAL MARRIAGES

#### ARTICLE 171

The person who contracts a second or subsequent marriage without the previous one being legally dissolved, shall be punished with a penalty of two to five years of imprisonment. The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever, being single, contracts marriage knowingly with a married person.

#### ARTICLE 172. Shall incur the penalty of imprisonment of one to four years

1)	Whoever, deceiving a person, simulates marriage with him or her.  
2)	Whoever contracts marriage knowing that he has a non-dispensable diriment impediment.

#### ARTICLE 173

Officials who authorize marriages prohibited by law, knowingly, or without the concurrence of any of the requirements of existence or validity thereof, shall be sanctioned with a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) and special disqualification of four (4) to six (6) years.

#### ARTICLE 174

In all cases of this Chapter the fraudulent contracting party shall be sentenced to compensate according to his ability the other contracting party, if the latter had contracted marriage in good faith.

#### ARTICLE 175

The fine provided in Article 173, preceding, shall be applicable to ministers of any religion who authorize religious marriage without the celebration of civil marriage taking place.

### CHAPTER III INCEST

#### ARTICLE 176

Carnal access between ascendants and descendants or between siblings shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.  
Whoever commits incest with a descendant or sibling under eighteen (18) years of age shall be punished with four (4) to seven (7) years of imprisonment. Said minors shall not incur any penalty, but shall be subject to the protective measures that special laws determine.  

In the crime of incest proceedings shall be initiated by virtue of a complaint by the offended party or his legal representative if he is absolutely or relatively incapable. To this crime shall be applied the provisions of numerals 1, 2 and 3 of the second paragraph of Article 152, preceding.

### CHAPTER IV

DENIAL OF FAMILY SUPPORT

#### ARTICLE 177

Whoever, being legally obligated, or by virtue of a final judgment, after having been duly required, without just cause fails to provide for the subsistence of the spouse, of children under twenty-one years of age or of the ward under their guardianship, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years.

The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever, under the same circumstances, fails to provide the necessary resources to their ascendants or descendants who are disabled, ill or for any reason incapacitated for work.

#### ARTICLE 178

Whoever, in order to evade compliance with the maintenance obligation, places themselves in a situation of insolvency, transfers their assets to third parties, renounces their employment, simulates obligations or employs any other fraudulent means, shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years.

#### ARTICLE 179

The penalties established in this Chapter shall not exempt the accused from their maintenance obligations.

### CHAPTER V

ON DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

#### ARTICLE 179

A. Whoever employs force, intimidation or subjects to persecution their spouse or ex-spouse, the person with whom they cohabit or have maintained a common-law relationship or the person with whom they have procreated a child, with the purpose of causing physical or emotional harm or to damage their property, shall be punished with imprisonment of one
(1) to three (3) years, without prejudice to the penalty corresponding to the injuries or damages caused. The same penalty shall apply when the violence is exercised upon common children or upon the children of the persons mentioned who are subject to parental authority, or upon the minor or incapacitated person subject to guardianship or curatorship or upon the ascendants.

#### ARTICLE 179

B. Whoever subjects to physical mistreatment their spouse, ex-spouse, concubine or ex-concubine or the person with whom they have procreated a child, shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years in any of the following circumstances:
a)	Enters the dwelling of the person or the place where they are lodged or sheltered to consummate the act;

b)	Inflicts serious bodily harm upon them;

c)	Performs the action with a deadly weapon even if they have not acted with the intention to kill or mutilate;
d)	Acts in the presence of minors;

e)	Induces, incites or compels the person to consume drugs, narcotics or other psychotropic or intoxicating substances;
f)	Also subjects a minor to mistreatment; and

g)	Uses as a pretext to restrict their freedom that the victim suffers from an illness or mental defect.

The provisions of this Article shall be understood without prejudice to the penalty corresponding to the other crimes committed.

#### ARTICLE 179

C. Notwithstanding the provisions of article 15 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, the crimes contemplated in this Chapter and in the previous one shall be subject to public prosecution.

## TITLE V

CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC HEALTH

#### ARTICLE 180

Whoever intentionally propagates a dangerous disease or causes an epidemic through the dissemination of pathogenic germs, shall be imposed imprisonment of three to six years.

#### ARTICLE 181

Whoever knowingly distributes or sells food products, medicines or consumable substances in general, even without nutritional value, that represent a risk to health or may cause diseases due to their contamination, improper preparation or poor hygiene, shall be punished depending on the severity of the injury, with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years plus a fine of up to twenty (20) times the value of the product without prejudice to the penalties corresponding to other crimes committed as a consequence of the same actions.

#### ARTICLE 181

A. Whoever contaminates all or part of the national territory, including waters, with waste, refuse, garbage or substances brought from abroad that produce or are likely to produce harm to the health of persons or to the ecosystem, shall be punished with imprisonment of six (6) to twelve (12) years and a fine of one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) to five hundred thousand lempiras (L.500,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 181

B. The penalties established in the preceding Article shall also be imposed upon whoever within or outside the country promotes or in any manner manages the introduction into the national territory of waste, refuse, garbage or substances that cause or are likely to cause contamination to the environment or harm to the health of persons.

#### ARTICLE 182

Whoever manufactures food or therapeutic substances in a manner dangerous to health, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to five years.

The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever knowingly imports, distributes, sells, exports or keeps in storage for distribution or sale, substances harmful to health or falsified, adulterated, deteriorated, contaminated or expired foods.

#### ARTICLE 182

A. Whoever without being legally authorized for such purpose dispenses substances, narcotics, stimulants or similar substances of strictly pharmaceutical regulation, dispenses or serves as an intermediary in the sale to persons under twenty-one (21) years of age of products containing organic compounds such as plastic cements, paint thinners and all types of adhesives that when inhaled produce habit, habituation or dependence, whether psychic or psychophysical, shall be punished with imprisonment of one (1) to six (6) years.

#### ARTICLE 183

Whoever, exercising the trade of medicines, duly authorized by law, dispenses them without a medical prescription, when such is necessary, or in disagreement with it, or supplies them in species, quantity or quality different from that prescribed by the physician, shall be punished with a penalty of one to three years of imprisonment.

The same penalty shall apply to whoever, in the same case, sells medicinal substances knowing that they have lost their therapeutic properties.

#### ARTICLE 184

If from the crimes configured in the four preceding articles the death of any person results, the responsible party shall be punished with the penalty for simple homicide or for aggravated homicide, according to the circumstances concurrent in the act.

#### ARTICLE 185

In any case, the harmful food or medicinal substances shall be confiscated, and remitted to the Secretariat of Public Health, for the corresponding legal purposes.

#### ARTICLE 186

Whoever violates the measures adopted by the health authority with the purpose of preventing the introduction or propagation of an epidemic, or of an epizootic susceptible to affecting human beings, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years.

The penalty shall be increased by one fourth when the perpetrator is a health official or employee, physician, pharmacist or dentist, or exercises any of the auxiliary activities of these professions.

#### ARTICLE 187

Imprisonment of one to three years shall be imposed upon whoever corrupts or fouls a spring, well or river whose water serves as drinking water, rendering it harmful to health.

#### ARTICLE 188

The physician or professional who, having intervened in the diagnosis or treatment of a communicable disease, omits the notification provided for in the Sanitary Code, shall incur imprisonment of three months to one year and a fine of one hundred to three hundred lempiras.

#### ARTICLE 189

Whoever without title or authorization for the exercise of professions related to health, or exceeding the limits of their authorization, announces, prescribes, administers or applies any chemical means intended for the treatment of diseases of persons, even gratuitously, shall be punished with one to two years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 190

Whoever practices inhumation, exhumation or transfer of corpses or human remains in contravention of the pertinent legal or regulatory provisions, shall be punished with a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras (L.10,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 191

Crimes against public health that are committed in a culpable manner shall be punished with the corresponding penalty, reduced by two thirds.

## TITLE VI

CRIMES AGAINST LIBERTY AND SECURITY

### CHAPTER I

KIDNAPPING AND ILLEGAL DETENTIONS

#### ARTICLE 192

Whoever kidnaps a person to obtain from them or from a third party, as the price of their freedom, money, goods, titles or documents that produce profit or utility in favor of the kidnapper or another person designated by them, shall be punished as follows:

1)	With a penalty of deprivation of liberty for thirty (30) years to deprivation of liberty for life if they cause or give rise to the death of the kidnapped person, even if they do not achieve their objective;

2)	From thirty (30) to forty (40) years, if the kidnapped person dies during the rescue process;

3)	From twenty (20) to thirty (30) years, if they release the person having collected the demanded price; and,
4)	From five (5) to ten (10) years, if they desist by releasing them and have not obtained the demanded price.

If during the liberation members of the authority die, the provisions of subsection 2) of this Article shall apply. If for the same reason injuries are caused to members of the authority, the penalties shall be increased by one third.

The same penalties referred to in this Article shall be applicable to whoever kidnaps or detains a person with the purpose of making them do or refrain from doing something for publicity purposes of a political nature.

#### ARTICLE 193

Whoever outside the cases provided for in the preceding article unjustly deprives another of their liberty, shall be punished with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years.

#### ARTICLE 194

Where applicable, the penalties indicated in Articles 192, 193 and 195 shall be increased by one third when the following circumstances concur:

1)	Threat or cruel treatment of the kidnapped person;
2)	Deprivation of liberty for more than twenty-four (24) hours; and

3)	Application of drugs or any other means that weakens or nullifies the will of the kidnapped person.

#### ARTICLE 195

Whoever traffics Hondurans or persons of any nationality or origin, conducting them or having them conducted through the national territory, to introduce them illegally into another State for any purpose, shall be punished with imprisonment from six
(6) to nine (9) years.

The penalty shall be increased by one third when those responsible for the crime are public employees or officials.

If as a consequence of the commission of this crime the victims suffer deprivation of liberty abroad, are victims of crimes of any kind or die from violent causes, even if accidentally, the penalty referred to in the first paragraph shall be increased by two thirds.

#### ARTICLE 196. (REPEALED)

#### ARTICLE 196

A. Hondurans who participate or collaborate in Honduras with foreign organizations or authorities in the illegal detention of fellow nationals so that they may be tried abroad for crimes committed within or outside the national territory, shall be punished with imprisonment from nine (9) to twelve (12) years. If they are public officials the penalty shall be from twelve (12) to fifteen (15) years, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

### CHAPTER II

ABDUCTION OF MINORS

#### ARTICLE 197

The abduction of a minor under twelve (12) years of age by persons other than their parents shall be punished with imprisonment from six (6) to eight (8) years. In the same case, the abduction of a person over twelve (12) years of age and under eighteen (18) years of age shall be punished with imprisonment from four (4) to six (6) years.

#### ARTICLE 198

The same penalty established in the preceding article shall be incurred by whoever, being in charge of the person of a minor, does not present them to their parents or guardians, nor gives satisfactory explanation about their disappearance.

#### ARTICLE 199

Whoever induces a minor under eighteen (18) years of age to abandon the home of their parents, guardians or those in charge of their care, shall be punished with imprisonment from two (2) to four (4) years.

#### ARTICLE 200

Whoever having charge of the upbringing of a minor under eighteen (18) years of age delivers them to a public establishment or to another person without the consent of the one who had entrusted them to them, or of the authority, in default thereof, shall be punished with imprisonment from two (2) to four (4) years.

### CHAPTER III

PROVISION COMMON TO THE TWO PRECEDING CHAPTERS

#### ARTICLE 201

Whoever kidnaps any person with motives other than those indicated in Article 192 shall be punished with a penalty of twenty (20) to thirty (30) years if the person is found unharmed; when they do not account for their whereabouts or do not prove having released them, the penalty shall be from thirty (30) to forty (40) years.

### CHAPTER IV TRESPASS OF DWELLING

#### ARTICLE 202

A private individual who enters another's dwelling against the will of its occupant or having entered with the express or tacit consent thereof, remains in it despite having been ordered to leave it, shall be punished with three months to one year of imprisonment.

If the foregoing acts are executed with violence or intimidation or simulation of authority, the penalty shall be from one to three years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 203

An agent of authority, official or public employee who enters a house without complying with the requirements prescribed by law, shall be punished with two (2) to five (5) years of imprisonment and special disqualification for a time equal to double that applied to the imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 204

The provision of the first paragraph of Article 202 is not applicable to whoever enters another's dwelling to avoid serious harm to themselves, to the occupants or to a third party, nor to whoever does so to provide some service to humanity or when required by judicial authority.

#### ARTICLE 205

The provisions of this Chapter do not apply with respect to cafés, bars and other establishments serving the public while they are open.

### CHAPTER V

COERCION AND THREATS

#### ARTICLE 206

Whoever without being legitimately authorized prevents another with violence from doing what the law does not prohibit, or compels them to execute what they do not wish, whether just or unjust, shall suffer imprisonment from three months to two years.

#### ARTICLE 207

A private individual who threatens another with causing harm to them or their family, in their person, honor or property, whether it constitutes a crime or not, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years, and in addition, the security measures that the Judge determines.

#### ARTICLE 208

Whoever with violence seizes a thing belonging to their debtor to obtain payment with it, shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment from three months to one year.

#### ARTICLE 209

An agent of authority, official or public employee who in order to obtain the confession of being responsible for a certain crime or with another similar purpose threatens with physical or moral violence any person, shall be punished with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years, and absolute disqualification for a time equal to double that applied to the imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 209

A. An employee or public official, including those of penitentiary institutions or centers for the protection of minors, commits torture when, abusing their position and with the purpose of obtaining a confession or information from any person or punishing them for any act they have committed or are suspected of having committed, subjects them to conditions or procedures that by their nature, duration or other circumstances cause them physical or mental suffering, the suppression or diminution of their faculties of knowledge, discernment or decision, or that in any other way violate their moral integrity. The person guilty of torture shall be punished with imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years if the harm is serious, and five (5) to ten (10) years if it is not, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

The foregoing penalties shall be understood without prejudice to those that may be applicable to injuries or harm to the life, bodily integrity, health, sexual freedom or property of the victim or a third party.

When the crime of torture is committed by private individuals, the penalties provided in the first paragraph of this article shall be reduced by one third.

### CHAPTER VI

CRIMES AGAINST FREEDOM OF WORSHIP, RELIGIOUS SENTIMENT AND RESPECT FOR THE DECEASED

#### ARTICLE 210

Whoever by means of violence compels another to perform a religious act or prevents them from participating in a ceremony of the same nature, shall be punished with imprisonment of three months to one year.

#### ARTICLE 211

The same penalty as established in the preceding article shall be incurred by whoever interrupts or prevents, without justified cause, the celebration of a ceremony or religious function, of any worship permitted in the nation.

#### ARTICLE 212

Whoever causes damage to objects intended for worship or to the symbols of any legally permitted religion, or publicly offends such worships or their members by reason of their investiture, shall incur imprisonment of three months to one year.

#### ARTICLE 213

Whoever violates a grave, sepulcher or a funeral home, or in any other serious manner desecrates a human corpse or its remains, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years.

### CHAPTER VII

VIOLATION AND DISCLOSURE OF SECRETS

#### ARTICLE 214

Whoever without proper judicial authorization, for any purpose, seizes the papers or correspondence of another, intercepts or causes to be intercepted their telephone, telegraph, electronic media or computer, facsimile or any other nature communications, including electronic ones, shall be punished with six (6) to eight (8) if a private individual and eight (8) to twelve (12) years if a public official or employee.

#### ARTICLE 215

Whoever reveals without just cause or uses for their own or another's benefit a secret of which they have learned by reason of their office, employment, profession or art and thereby causes harm to someone, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

### CHAPTER VII

CRIMES AGAINST POLITICAL FREEDOM

#### ARTICLE 216

Whoever outside the cases provided for in the respective special laws, by means of violence or threats prevents or restricts the exercise of any political right, shall be punished with six months to two years of imprisonment.

## TITLE VII

CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY

### CHAPTER I ROBBERY

#### ARTICLE 217

Whoever seizes movable property belonging to others, animals included, using violence or intimidation against persons or force against things, commits the crime of robbery.
Snatching by surprise from the victim the thing they carry with them or the use of means that weaken or nullify their resistance is equivalent to violence against persons.

#### ARTICLE 218

The person guilty of robbery shall be punished with five (5) to nine (9) years of imprisonment.

The person guilty of the crime of robbery of land motor vehicles, aircraft, ships and other similar property, shall be subject to a penalty of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years of imprisonment.
The applicable penalty for the crime of robbery of land motor vehicles, aircraft, ships and other similar property, shall be a penalty of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years of imprisonment.

The penalty applicable to robbery of large livestock shall be seven (7) to ten (10) years of imprisonment. Robbery of small livestock shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to seven (7) years.

An aggravating circumstance of this crime is the robbery of three (3) or more head of large or small livestock.

#### ARTICLE 219

The penalties indicated in the preceding Article shall be increased by one third when the robbery is committed:

1)	In an uninhabited area or in a gang;
2)	With the criminals carrying weapons, in an inhabited house or in a public office, in a building intended for religious worship, in a public or private educational or cultural center, in a commercial, banking or social assistance establishment, provided that any of the following circumstances concur:

a)	Scaling;

b)	Breaking of walls, ceilings, floors or pavements or breaking of doors or windows;
c)	Using false keys, lock picks or other similar instruments. The legitimate key stolen from the owner shall be considered a false key; and

d)	Breaking of cabinets, safes or other kinds of sealed or closed furniture or objects or by stealing them to be opened or violated outside the place of the robbery.

The increase referred to in the first paragraph shall also be made when any of the circumstances mentioned in Article 225 concur.

#### ARTICLE 220

An inhabited house is considered to be any shelter that constitutes the dwelling of one or more persons, even if they are accidentally absent from it when the robbery takes place.
Yards, warehouses, granaries, haylofts, garages, stables and other departments or enclosed sites adjacent to the building and in interior communication with it and with which they form a single whole are considered dependencies of an inhabited house, or of the establishments listed in subsection 2 of the preceding article.

#### ARTICLE 221

Whoever has in their possession lock picks or other instruments specially intended to execute the crime of robbery, and does not give sufficient explanation of their acquisition or possession, shall be punished with imprisonment of three months to one year.

The same penalty shall be incurred by those who manufacture said instruments for the same purpose. If they are locksmiths, they shall be subject to one to two years of imprisonment.

### CHAPTER II

EXTORTION AND BLACKMAIL

#### ARTICLE 222. Shall incur imprisonment of three to nine years

1)	Whoever by means of violence or threats, compels someone to do or refrain from doing something, in order to obtain for themselves or for others an unjust benefit.

2)	Whoever in order to defraud another compels them with violence or intimidation to sign, grant, deliver or destroy a public deed or any other public or private document.

3)	Whoever with threats of imputations against honor or prestige, or of violation or disclosure of secrets with harm in either case to the offended party, their family or the entity in whose management they intervene or have an interest, demands the delivery of a sum of money, reward or effects.

### CHAPTER III THEFT

#### ARTICLE 223. Commits the crime of theft whoever

1)	Without the will of their owner takes movable property belonging to others, animals included, without violence or intimidation against persons or force against things;

2)	Finding a lost thing does not deliver it to the authority or to its owner if they know who it is and seizes it with the intent of an owner; and,

3)	Removes or uses the fruits or objects of the damage they have caused, except in the cases provided for in Book Three.

Electrical energy, the radio spectrum and other kinds of waves or energy in telephone, television, facsimile, computer or any other systems that have economic value are equivalent to movable property.

#### ARTICLE 224

Theft shall be punished with two (2) to five (5) years of imprisonment if the value of the stolen thing does not exceed five thousand Lempiras (L.5,000.00) and with four (4) to seven (7) years if it exceeds said amount.

Theft of land motor vehicles, aircraft, ships and other similar goods shall be punished with imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years.

#### ARTICLE 225

The penalties applicable according to the preceding article shall be increased by one third if the act is committed:

1)	By a domestic employee or involving serious abuse of trust.

2)	Taking advantage of a public or private calamity or a common danger.
3)	At night, or if, to execute it, the agent remains surreptitiously in a building or place intended for habitation.

4)	With the cooperation of two or more persons or by a single person who pretends to be an agent of authority, or an employee of a public service.
5)	In the luggage of travelers, in any kind of vehicles, in stations, docks, hotels, or in establishments where food or beverages are served.

6)	In houses intended for worship or public ornamental use or when it affects funerary monuments.
7)	In objects of scientific, artistic or cultural value that are found in museums or other public establishments or that belong to the national historical heritage, even if they have remained hidden or undiscovered.

#### ARTICLE 226

Theft of large livestock shall be punished with imprisonment of six (6) to nine (9) years if the value of the stolen property exceeds five thousand lempiras (L.5,000.00) and four (4) to seven (7) years if it does not exceed said value.

Theft of small livestock shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to six (6) years, if the value of the stolen property exceeds five thousand Lempiras (L.5,000.00), and two (2) to five (5) years if it does not exceed said value.
The theft of three (3) or more head of large or small livestock constitutes an aggravating circumstance of this crime.

### CHAPTER IV USURPATION

#### ARTICLE 227

Whoever usurps immovable property or a real right shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years, without prejudice to the judge hearing the case ordering the eviction of the property in question or the restoration of the usurped right as soon as the corresponding right is established in the proceedings.

#### ARTICLE 228

Whoever alters boundaries or landmarks of towns, or estates, or any kind of markers intended to fix the limits of adjoining properties shall incur the same penalties as in the preceding article.

#### ARTICLE 229

Whoever, outside the mentioned cases, disturbs with violence or threats against persons the peaceful possession of immovable property, shall be punished with three months to one year of imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 230

Whoever without due authorization and to the detriment of another dams, diverts or detains the waters of a river, ravine, stream, canal or spring, or usurps any right related to the course of such waters, shall be punished with imprisonment of one
(1) to three (3) years, without prejudice to destroying the works improperly made.

#### ARTICLE 231

Whoever occupies the land or space corresponding to the right of way, highway, street, garden, park, green area, promenade or other places of public use or domain or any other real property of the State or municipalities, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years, without prejudice to vacating the occupied land or space. When the occupied property is a beach the penalty shall be increased by two thirds.

#### ARTICLE 232. (REPEALED)

### CHAPTER V PUNISHABLE INSOLVENCY

#### ARTICLE 233

A merchant declared in fraudulent bankruptcy in accordance with the Commercial Code shall be punished with imprisonment of two to six years and special disqualification of three to seven years.

#### ARTICLE 234

A merchant declared in culpable bankruptcy for any of the causes included in the Commercial Code shall incur imprisonment of six months to two years and special disqualification of one to three years.

#### ARTICLE 235

Those who execute any of the following acts shall be punished as accomplices to the crime of fraudulent bankruptcy:

1)	Conspiring with the bankrupt to suppose credits against him or to increase them, alter their nature or maturity date in order to take precedence in ranking, to the detriment of other creditors, even if this occurs before the declaration of bankruptcy.
2)	Having assisted the bankrupt in the removal, subtraction or concealment of his assets.

3)	Concealing from the bankruptcy administrators the existence of assets that, belonging to the bankruptcy estate, are in the possession of the guilty party, or delivering them to the bankrupt and not to said administrators.

4)	Making private arrangements with the bankrupt to the detriment of other creditors.

#### ARTICLE 236

When a commercial enterprise is declared bankrupt, any director or administrator thereof who has cooperated in the execution of any of the unlawful acts that motivated it, shall be punished with the penalty of the fraudulent or culpable bankrupt, as the case may be.

#### ARTICLE 237

A debtor who, in order to evade payment of his obligations, alienates or encumbers his assets without notifying his creditors in authentic form at least fifteen days in advance; or conceals his assets, simulates alienations or credits, or moves abroad without leaving a person to represent him, or sufficient assets to respond to the payment of his debts, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to six years, if he is a merchant, and two to five, if he is not.

#### ARTICLE 238

A non-merchant debtor in composition whose insolvency is the result, in whole or in part, of any of the following acts shall incur imprisonment of one to two years:
1)	Having made excessive domestic or personal expenses, disproportionate to his fortune, considering the circumstances of his rank and family.

2)	Having suffered, in any kind of gambling, losses that exceed what a good family man would risk by way of recreation in entertainment of this kind.
3)	Having had losses in substantial wagers, simulated purchases and sales and other speculation operations, whose success depends exclusively on chance.

4)	Having alienated, with notable depreciation, assets whose price is still owed.

5)	Delaying his presentation in composition proceedings, when his liabilities are greater by twenty-five percent in relation to his assets.

#### ARTICLE 239

Those who execute with respect to the debtor in composition any of the acts enumerated in Article 235 shall be punished as accomplices to the crime provided for in the preceding article.

### CHAPTER VI

FRAUD AND OTHER FRAUDS

#### ARTICLE 240

Whoever commits the crime of fraud by using a false name, false titles, simulated influence or quality, abuse of trust, pretending to be the owner of assets, credits, enterprises or business or using any artifice, cunning or deceit, induces another into error, defrauding him for his own or another's benefit.

#### ARTICLE 241. The crime of fraud shall be punished

1)	With two (2) to five (5) years of imprisonment when the value of what is defrauded does not exceed five thousand lempiras (L.5,000.00);
2)	With four (4) to seven (7) years of imprisonment if the amount exceeds five thousand lempiras (L.5,000.00) and does not exceed one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00); and

3)	With six (6) to nine (9) years of imprisonment if the amount exceeds one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) Lempiras.
The agent, in addition, shall be punished with a fine equal to ten percent (10%) of the defrauded value. The foregoing shall be understood without prejudice to the provisions of Articles 36, 37 and 53, when applicable.

#### ARTICLE 242. Shall incur the penalties of the preceding article

1)	Whoever defrauds another in the substance, quality or quantity of things delivered to them by virtue of contract or obligatory title.  

2)	Whoever, in order to defraud someone, causes them to sign, destroy or mutilate, by means of deceit, any document.  
3)	Whoever commits any fraud by abuse of another's signature in blank, extending with it any document to the detriment of the same person or of a third party.  

4)	Whoever, to the detriment of another, grants simulated contracts or false receipts.  
5)	The commission agent, agent, administrator or mandatory who commits fraud by altering in their accounts the prices or conditions of contracts, inventing expenses or exaggerating those which they had incurred.  

6)	Whoever defrauds by means of concealment, substitution or mutilation of any proceeding, file, document or other important paper.  
7)	Whoever in gambling uses artifice or deceit to ensure the outcome.  

8)	Whoever to the detriment of another appropriates or diverts money, effects or any other movable thing which they had received on deposit, commission or administration, or by another title that entails an obligation to deliver or return it, or denies having received it.  
9)	Whoever sells or encumbers, as free, property that is in litigation, seized or encumbered, and whoever sells, encumbers or leases, the property of others as their own.  

10)	Whoever defrauds another under pretext of supposed remuneration, gratuity or gift to judges or other public employees, without prejudice to the action for defamation that corresponds to them.  
11)	Whoever, in order to obtain the price of insurance, or some undue benefit, destroys, damages or conceals the insured thing or any other of their property.  

12)	Whoever, with identical purpose, causes themselves or through a third party, a personal injury or aggravates that caused by an accident; and

13)	Whoever, abusing the needs, inexperience, or passions of a minor, or the state of illness or mental deficiency of a person, causes them to sign a document or execute any other act that has legal effect.

#### ARTICLE 243

Whoever defrauds or harms another using any deceit that is not included in the preceding Articles of this Chapter, shall be punished with a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras (L.10,000.00). In case of recidivism the sanction shall be equal to double the previous one.

### CHAPTER VII USURY AND SPECULATION

#### ARTICLE 244

The lender who demands from their debtors in any form, an interest greater than the average interest, in force in the National Financial System on the date of the loan contract, increased by six points, even when said interest is concealed or disguised in any manner or given another name, commits the crime of usury.  
Loans made by persons registered as non-bank lenders or who are recognized as such by notoriety shall be considered as loans even when the obligation is formalized by means of a negotiable instrument or any other document.  

The crime of usury shall be punished with imprisonment of four (4) to six (6) years, plus a fine equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the amount of the credit.

#### ARTICLE 245

The same penalty of the preceding article shall be incurred by whoever, taking advantage of the pressing need, recklessness or inexperience of a person, causes them to give or propose in any form, for themselves or for another, a pecuniary advantage exceeding thirty percent of their performance, or grants a guarantee of an extortionate nature.

#### ARTICLE 246

Whoever habitually lends money with or without guarantee and is not registered in the corresponding official registry or does not keep accounting books, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 247

Whoever increases the prices of merchandise or public services above those set by the competent authorities in accordance with the Law, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of twenty thousand (L.20,000.00) to fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) Lempiras.

### CHAPTER VIII

CRIMES AGAINST SPECIAL PROPERTIES

#### ARTICLE 248

Whoever violates the rights of authors of literary, scientific or artistic works and others protected by the Law on Copyright and Related Rights, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years, plus a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 248

A. With the same penalties of Article 248, natural or legal persons who without authorization from the respective holders of copyright or related rights use for commercial purposes, television signals transmitted by satellite or reproduce or project videos, films or other analogous works that, by their nature, are or should be protected by the Law on the Matter, shall be punished.  
With the same penalties shall be punished those who use the frequencies of the radio spectrum without authorization from the corresponding authority.

#### ARTICLE 249

The same penalty of the preceding Article 248 shall be incurred by whoever manufactures or offers for sale articles that by their name, brand, wrapping, presentation or appearance may be confused with similar products patented or registered in the name of another.

#### ARTICLE 250. (REPEALED)

#### ARTICLE 251

With the penalties provided in the preceding Article 248, those who shall be punished:
1)	Falsify, imitate or fraudulently use any of the figures or legal assets protected by the Industrial Property Law;  

2)	Knowing that said figures or legal assets are falsified, negotiate them in any form; and
3)	Knowingly market the merchandise, articles or products covered by the indicated figures or legal assets falsified, imitated or fraudulently used.

#### ARTICLE 252

Those who use or permit the use of the elements referred to in the preceding article, contravening morality, public order, good customs, or that ridicule persons or offend religious sentiments, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years.

#### ARTICLE 253

Those who commit any of the infractions provided in Title VI of the Industrial Property Law that are not included in the preceding Articles of this Chapter shall be punished with the penalties provided in the preceding Article 248.

### CHAPTER IX DAMAGES

#### ARTICLE 254

Imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years shall be imposed on whoever destroys, renders useless, causes to disappear or in any way, damages movable or immovable things or animals belonging to another, provided that the act does not constitute a crime of those provided in the following chapter.  

The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever by any means destroys, alters, renders useless or in any other way damages data, programs or electronic documents belonging to another, contained in networks, media or computer systems.

#### ARTICULO 255

Whoever causes any of the damages referred to in the preceding Article shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years:

1) With the purpose of preventing the free exercise of authority or in revenge for its determinations, whether the crime is committed against public officials or employees or against private individuals who, as witnesses or in any other manner, have contributed or may contribute to the application of the laws;

2) By any means that produces infection or contagion in animals or plants;

3) Using poisonous, corrosive, explosive or flammable substances;
4) In a gang or in an uninhabited area;

5) On objects of scientific, historical, welfare, educational, cultural or artistic interest or in laboratories, archives, libraries, museums, monuments or on property of social utility;
6) On means or routes of communication or transit, bridges, canals, parks, promenades or other property of the State or national property for public use;

7) Through the destruction of property to the detriment of a creditor; and
8) Destroying forests or large plantations.

Negligent damages shall be punished with a penalty equal to half of that corresponding to intentional damage. If the perpetrator committed the act as a consequence of having consumed alcohol or drugs, he shall be punished with two-thirds of the penalty applicable to the corresponding intentional damage.

### CAPITULO X

FIRE AND OTHER DEVASTATION

#### ARTICULO 256

Whoever causes a fire, endangering the life, bodily integrity or property of another, shall incur imprisonment of three to six years. The penalty shall be six to twelve years if the fire is committed:

1) With intent to profit, for one's own or another's benefit.

2) In a building, farmhouse, hut or shelter inhabited or intended for habitation.

3) In a public building or one intended for public use or for social welfare or cultural work.
4) In a vessel, aircraft, convoy or collective transport vehicles.

5) In an airport, railway station or motor vehicles.

6) In a shipyard, factory or workshop.
7) In a depot of explosive or flammable substances.

8) In an oil well or mine gallery.
9) In a cultivated field, pasture field or forest.

#### ARTICULO 257

Those who cause devastation by means of the collapse of a building, flood, explosion, and in general, by any agent or means of destruction as powerful as those mentioned, shall incur imprisonment of six to twelve years.
The same penalty shall apply to those who, at the time of the fire, flood, shipwreck or any other disaster, remove, conceal or render useless materials, instruments or other means intended to prevent such disasters.

#### ARTICULO 258

If death of a person results from the fire or devastation, the penalty shall be ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICULO 259

Whoever causes fire or other devastation in a negligent manner shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years.

If death of any person results from the omission or negligent act, the imprisonment shall be two to five years.

### CAPITULO XI GAMES

#### ARTICULO 260

The owners and administrators of casinos, gambling houses or houses of chance, wager or hazard not legally authorized, shall be punished with imprisonment of three
(3) to seven (7) years, plus a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00), without prejudice to the definitive closure of the business.

Those who gamble in said houses shall be punished with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years, plus a fine equal to half of the previous one.

#### ARTICULO 261

The producers and sellers of lottery tickets not legally authorized and those who conduct raffles that have also not been legally authorized, shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years plus a fine of ten thousand (L.10,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras (L.50,000.00). Raffles conducted for charitable, educational or promotional purposes for the arts or sports by centers or establishments dedicated to these activities are excepted.

Those who use fraudulent means in games or raffles shall be punished as swindlers.

#### ARTICULO 262

The money or effects and the instruments and utensils intended for gambling or raffles, shall be subject to confiscation.

### CAPITULO XII GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### ARTICULO 263. (REPEALED)

#### ARTICULO 264

The following are exempt from criminal liability and subject only to civil liability for thefts, frauds or damages that they reciprocally cause each other:
1) Spouses, the man and woman who live together as a couple and ascendants or descendants by blood or affinity.

2) The widower or widow, with respect to things belonging to their deceased spouse, as long as they have not passed into the possession of another.
3) Siblings and siblings-in-law, if they live under the same roof.

The exemption of this article is not applicable to strangers who participate in the crime.

## TITULO VIII

CRIMES AGAINST MEANS OF COMMUNICATION AND OTHER PUBLIC SERVICES

#### ARTICULO 265

Whoever impedes or disturbs railway service in any of the following forms shall be punished with imprisonment of two to five years:
1) Destroying, damaging or disrupting, totally or partially, railway line, rolling stock or traction equipment, work or installation.

2) Placing obstacles on the track.
3) Transmitting false notice about the movement of railway vehicles.

4) Performing any other act from which disaster may result.

#### ARTICULO 266

If disaster results from the acts referred to in the preceding Article, the penalty shall be imprisonment of four to twelve years.

#### ARTICULO 267

Whoever endangers a vessel or aircraft, one's own or another's, or performs any act tending to impede or hinder water or air navigation, shall be punished with imprisonment of four to twelve years.

#### ARTICULO 268

If the act referred to in the preceding article results in shipwreck or grounding of a vessel, or the crash or destruction of an aircraft, the penalty shall be imprisonment of four to twelve years.

#### ARTICULO 269

Whoever exposes another means of public transport to danger, impedes or hinders its operation, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to two years.

If disaster results from the act, the penalty shall be imprisonment of four to twelve years.

#### ARTICULO 270

In any of the crimes provided for in the preceding Articles of this Title, if death or injuries of those included in Article 135 result from the act, the penalty shall be imprisonment of six to fifteen years, unless the crime committed merits a greater penalty according to other provisions of this Code.
If the act were negligent it shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to four years, this penalty being increased by half if death or injuries of those mentioned in the preceding paragraph result.

#### ARTICULO 271

Whoever destroys or damages the postal, telegraph, telephone, electrical, radio service or other means serving telecommunications, shall be punished with imprisonment of six (6) to nine (9) years.
Whoever obstructs or interrupts the services referred to in the preceding paragraph or impedes or hinders their restoration shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

#### ARTICULO 272

Whoever attacks a carrier of public correspondence to intercept or detain it, or to seize it or in any way render it useless, shall incur imprisonment of two to six years.

#### ARTICULO 273

Imprisonment of six months to two years shall be imposed on whoever by any means harms the safety or normal operation of an establishment or installation intended to distribute to the public water, light, energy, heat or other good for public use.

If harm results from the act, not only to the establishment or installation, but also to the public service, the imprisonment shall be two to six years.

## TITULO IX

CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC FAITH

### CHAPTER I

COUNTERFEITING OF CURRENCY, BANKNOTES AND SECURITIES

#### ARTICLE 274. Shall incur imprisonment of three to twelve years

1)	Whoever counterfeits or alters national or foreign currency within or outside the country, that has legal or commercial tender.  
2)	Whoever knowingly introduces into the country counterfeited or altered currency that imitates that which has legal tender in the Republic.  

3)	Whoever, knowingly, acquires or receives counterfeited or altered coins and puts them into circulation in any manner.

#### ARTICLE 275

Whoever clips legitimate coins or knowingly introduces into the country clipped coins or puts them into circulation, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of five
(5) to eight (8) years.

#### ARTICLE 276

Whoever having received in good faith counterfeit, altered or clipped coins puts them into circulation knowing that they are illegitimate, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

#### ARTICLE 277

For the purposes of the three preceding articles, the counterfeiting or alteration of legally authorized banknotes, of titles and coupons of the national or municipal public debt, of drafts or warrants of the State or Municipal treasury and of titles, certificates or shares issued by banks or companies authorized to do so is equated to the counterfeiting or alteration of coins.

#### ARTICLE 278

The counterfeiting, clipping or alteration of foreign coins or banknotes that do not have legal tender in the Republic or of securities, public debt titles and other foreign credit documents referred to in the preceding Article, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of four (4) to six (6) years in the case of Article 274; of three (3) to five (5) years in the case of Article 275 and of two (2) to four (4) years in the case of Article 276.

#### ARTICLE 279

The public official or employee or the director, manager or administrator of a bank or of a company who authorizes the manufacture or issuance of coins or banknotes or any titles, certificates or bearer shares in amounts exceeding those authorized or under conditions different from those agreed upon or prescribed for the case, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years, plus a fine equal to triple the value of the operation in question.  
The public officials or employees and the others to whom this Article refers shall also be sanctioned with absolute disqualification of eight (8) to ten (10) years.

### CHAPTER II

COUNTERFEITING OF SEALS, STAMPED PAPER AND OTHER OFFICIAL EFFECTS

#### ARTICLE 280. Shall be punished with imprisonment of one to five years

1)	Whoever counterfeits official seals.  
2)	Whoever counterfeits stamped paper, postage stamps, telegraph stamps, revenue stamps or any other class of sealed or stamped effects, whose issuance is reserved to the State or its institutions, that have as their purpose the collection of taxes, duties or services.  
In these cases, as in those included in the following articles, the fraudulent printing or use of the genuine seal shall be considered counterfeiting.  

3)	Whoever sells counterfeited fiscal species or under conditions that make their use and circulation illicit.

#### ARTICLE 281. Imprisonment of six months to three years shall be imposed

1)	On whoever counterfeits marks, countersigns or signatures used in public offices to verify weights or measures or identify any object.  
2)	On whoever applies legitimate seals, marks or countersigns of official use to objects, works or articles other than those to which they should be applied.

#### ARTICLE 282

Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years whoever causes to disappear from the seals, stamps, marks or countersigns of official use referred to in the preceding Articles, the sign or mark placed by the authorities to denote that it has already been used or that it was rendered unusable for its circulation or sale.  

The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever, knowingly, offers for sale the adulterated seals, stamps, marks or countersigns or uses them or delivers them to another person to use them or gives rise to their being able to be used.

#### ARTICLE 283

When the person responsible for any of the offenses included in the preceding articles is a public official or employee and commits the act by abusing his position, he shall also be imposed absolute disqualification of two to ten years.

### CHAPTER III

COUNTERFEITING OF DOCUMENTS IN GENERAL

#### ARTICLE 284

Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three to nine years, whoever makes in whole or in part a false public document or alters a true one, in such a way that harm may result, executing any of the following acts:

1)	Counterfeiting or forging handwriting, signature or rubric.  

2)	Supposing in an act the intervention of persons who have not had it.  
3)	Attributing to those who have intervened in it, declarations or statements different from those they would have made.  

4)	Falsifying the truth in the narration of the facts.  

5)	Altering the true dates and amounts.  
6)	Making in a true document any alteration or insertion that varies its meaning.  

7)	Giving a copy in authentic form of a supposed document or stating in it something contrary to or different from what the true original contains.  
8)	Unduly inserting any writing in a protocol, register or official book.  

9)	Destroying, mutilating, suppressing or concealing a document.  
Shall also incur the penalty indicated in the first paragraph of this article, the religious minister who commits any of the acts included in the preceding numerals, with respect to acts or ecclesiastical documents that may produce effects on the status of persons or in the civil order.

#### ARTICLE 285

Whoever to the detriment of a third party or with intent to cause it commits in a private document any of the falsifications designated in the preceding article, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to three years.

#### ARTICLE 286

The official or employee of the telecommunications service who suppresses or falsifies a telegraphic dispatch, shall incur the penalty of imprisonment of one to two years.  
Whoever makes use of the false dispatch with intent of profit or intent to harm another, shall be punished as the author of the falsification.

#### ARTICLE 287

Whoever counterfeits lottery tickets or tickets or those used for raffles or by transportation companies, public spectacles or other similar entities and circulates them or takes advantage of them personally or benefits another person, and whoever knowing that they are counterfeited circulates them, shall be sanctioned

with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years, plus a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand lempiras (L.200,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 288

Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of ten thousand (L.10,000.00) to twenty thousand lempiras (L.20,000.00) the physician who issues a certificate or statement that contains false information about the existence or nonexistence, present or past, of any disease or injury, even if no harm results from it.  

The penalty shall be doubled when as a consequence of the false certificate or statement a healthy person is confined or detained in a psychiatric treatment center, hospital or other similar establishment; it serves to obtain undue benefits from social security or other equivalent services or to achieve the release from prison of a person or evade the action of justice. The same penalty shall be applied to whoever requests the false certificate or statement.

#### ARTICLE 289

Whoever, knowingly, makes use of a document false in whole or in part shall be sanctioned as if he were the author of the falsification.

#### ARTICLE 290

When any of the offenses provided for in this Chapter is committed by a public official or employee with abuse of his position, the guilty party shall suffer, in addition, absolute disqualification of two to ten years.

#### ARTICLE 291

Whoever manufactures, imports or keeps in their possession machinery or equipment, instruments or materials intended to commit any of the falsifications set forth in this Title shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

### CHAPTER IV

USURPATION OF FUNCTIONS AND TITLES AND IMPROPER USE OF NAMES, UNIFORMS, INSIGNIA AND DECORATIONS

#### ARTICLE 292

Whoever without legitimate title or cause exercises acts proper to an authority or official attributing official character to themselves, shall be punished with two to three years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 293

Whoever improperly attributes to themselves an academic title or degree, exercises a profession or maintains an office, clinic, law firm, laboratory or premises with the purpose of offering academic, professional or technical services without having the legally required authorization from a public or private entity, shall be punished with imprisonment of three
(3) to six (6) years.

#### ARTICLE 294

The following shall incur a fine of ten thousand (L.10,000.00) to fifty thousand Lempiras (L.50,000.00):

1)	Whoever usurps the functions proper to a minister of a religious cult that has proselytes in Honduras;  

2)	The public official who, abusing their office, attributes to any person a title, rank or name that does not belong to them;  

3)	Whoever uses publicly or improperly a uniform proper to a public office they do not hold or insignia or decorations that have not been legally granted to them.  
Whoever publicly uses a false name with the purpose of concealing a crime, evading a penalty or causing harm, shall be subject to imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years.

## TITLE X

CRIMES AGAINST THE ECONOMY

#### ARTICLE 295

Whoever maliciously and as the sole consequence of their acts endangers the national economy or public credit, shall be punished with imprisonment of three
(3) to six (6) years and a fine of twenty thousand (L.20,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras (L.50,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 296

Whoever destroys raw materials, agricultural or industrial products or instruments of production with the purpose of causing harm to the country's economy or to consumers, shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years and a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand lempiras (L.10,000.00). If the harm is serious, the penalties specified shall be doubled.

#### ARTICLE 297

Whoever fraudulently causes in the market or in the trade or securities exchanges an increase or decrease in wages or in the price of foodstuffs, goods, merchandise, shares, securities, currencies or other effects, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of twenty-five thousand (L.25,000.00) to fifty thousand Lempiras (L.50,000.00).  

If the crime is committed by public officials or employees, exchange or stock agents or trade brokers, the foregoing penalties shall be increased by one third.

#### ARTICLE 298. (REPEALED)

#### ARTICLE 299

Whoever does the following shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00):

1)	Hoards articles used in the construction industry with the purpose of provoking a rise in prices;  

2)	Through improper acts or procedures obstructs free competition in the production and marketing of goods or the practice of public tenders or auctions;  

3)	Executes acts of unfair competition, according to the rules established by the Commercial Code, other special laws or international agreements;  

4)	Exports articles of basic necessity or basic raw materials, without permission from the competent authority when required, if this produces scarcity or high prices.  
The public official who, knowing that scarcity or high prices of certain articles of basic necessity or

basic raw materials may be produced, issues the permit and as a consequence thereof scarcity or high prices are effectively produced, shall incur the same penalty;  
5)	Being duly authorized by the Central Bank of Honduras to conduct transactions in foreign currency, does so in contravention of legal provisions or those issued by the latter;  

6)	Not being part of the institutions of the National Financial System or even being part of such System, performs operations for which it is not authorized by law; excepting those foundations or non-profit organizations that strengthen micro and small enterprises; and,

7)	Whoever refuses to comply with the pecuniary sanctions established by the Law on Income from Foreign Exchange Proceeds from Exports contained in Decree 108-90 of September 20, 1990 and its amendments. In addition to the sanction contemplated in this Article, offenders shall be subject to confiscation of the objects or effects of the crime in cases where applicable. The corresponding values shall be deposited with the General Treasury of the Republic or with the Institution of the National Financial System that the Secretariat of State in the Office of Finance has authorized for such purpose.

## TITLE XI

CRIMES AGAINST THE EXISTENCE AND SECURITY OF THE STATE

### CHAPTER I TREASON

#### ARTICLE 302

Whoever executes acts that directly tend to undermine the territorial integrity of the Republic, subject it totally or partially to foreign domination, compromise its sovereignty or attack the unity of the State commits the crime of treason and shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment of fifteen to twenty years.

#### ARTICLE 303

A Honduran, even if they have lost their nationality, or a foreigner who owes obedience to Honduras by reason of their public employment or function, who takes part in an act of military hostility against the nation, or enters the service of a foreign enemy in the event of armed conflict, shall incur ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.  

If it concerns a foreigner who does not owe special obedience to the country by reason of their public employment or function, the sanction shall be reduced by half.

#### ARTICLE 304

In the case referred to in the preceding article, the penalty shall be fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment if as a consequence of the services rendered to the enemy, any part of the national territory, body of troops, depot of war materiel, provisions and foodstuffs, or any other element indispensable for the defense of the State falls into the enemy's power, or if the arms of the Republic suffer defeat.

#### ARTICLE 305

A Honduran, even if they have lost their nationality, or a foreigner who owes obedience to Honduras by reason of their public employment or function, who with the purpose of provoking aggression or hostilities against Honduras from one or more nations, executes acts that directly tend to that end, shall suffer imprisonment of ten to fifteen years.  
If aggression or hostilities from a foreign State or States are actually produced, the penalty shall be fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.  
The final paragraph of Article 303 shall be applicable as appropriate.

#### ARTICLE 306

Whoever, being entrusted by the Honduran Government to deal with State affairs with a foreign government or with persons or groups from another country, acts disloyally in the exercise of their mandate, shall incur five to ten years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICULO 307

Whoever reveals political, diplomatic or military secrets relating to the security of the State, whether by communicating or publishing documents, drawings, plans or other data relating to military material, fortifications or operations or any other matter essential to the defense of national interests, or by facilitating their disclosure in any other manner, shall incur imprisonment of four (4) to seven (7) years, a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to five hundred thousand Lempiras (L.500,000.00), plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

If the secrets are revealed to a foreign government or to its agents or subjects, the imprisonment shall be five (5) to eight (8) years, the fine equal to double the previous amount and the disqualification equal to double the duration of the imprisonment sentence. If the revelation of secrets is made to a State that is at war with Honduras, or to its agents or subjects, the penalty shall be ten (10) to fifteen (15) years of imprisonment, the fine equal to triple that established in the first paragraph of this Article and absolute disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment.
The foregoing penalties shall be increased by one third if the responsible party had learned of the secrets by virtue of his character as a public official or employee or had used violence or fraud to obtain such knowledge.

#### ARTICULO 308

Whoever without being duly authorized draws up plans of fortifications, barracks, ships, arsenals, roads or military works intended for the defense of the country or whoever for such purpose enters clandestinely or fraudulently into places whose access is prohibited by military authorities, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand lempiras (L.200,00.00).

#### ARTICULO 309

Whoever destroys or removes the boundary markers, buoys or signs that mark the national borders shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand Lempiras (L.200,000.00).

If as a consequence of the destruction or removal of the boundary markers, buoys or border signs Honduras becomes involved in any conflict, or if such destruction or removal is carried out during a war with a bordering State, the foregoing penalties shall be doubled.

#### ARTICULO 310

Whoever during an armed conflict with a foreign country induces Honduran troops or officers to desert or to serve the enemy, or puts into practice any other means to achieve that end, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six (6) to ten
(10) years, a fine of two hundred thousand (L.200,000.00) to five hundred thousand Lempiras (L.500,000.00) and absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

#### ARTICULO 310

A. The crimes of treason against the Homeland typified in Articles 2 and 19 of the Constitution of the Republic shall be sanctioned with the penalty of imprisonment of fifteen
(15) to twenty (20) years.

#### ARTICULO 310

B. Honduran nationality may only be acquired in the manner prescribed by the Constitution of the Republic and International Conventions or Treaties. Whoever grants it following different procedures shall commit the crime of treason against the homeland and shall be sanctioned with the penalty provided in the preceding Article.

#### ARTICULO 311

The attempt of any of the crimes included in the preceding Articles shall be punished as if it were a consummated crime. The conspiracy to commit any of said crimes shall be sanctioned with the penalty of the consummated crime reduced by two thirds. The proposal shall be sanctioned with the same penalty reduced by five sixths.

Desistance from the conspiracy or proposal exempts from all penalty if notice is given to the authority or its circumstances are brought to its knowledge before the execution of the crime has begun.
If the responsible party effectively achieves the proposed purpose when the consummation of the crime does not require that said purpose be achieved, the corresponding penalty shall be increased by one third.

### CAPITULO II

CRIMES THAT COMPROMISE THE PEACE, EXTERNAL SECURITY OR DIGNITY OF THE NATION

#### ARTICULO 312

Whoever commits any of the crimes provided for in the preceding chapter, against a State allied with Honduras in war or armed conflict against a common enemy, shall incur the respective penalty reduced by one third.

#### ARTICULO 313

Whoever provokes the rupture of Honduras' relations with another State giving rise to the imminence of a warlike conflict or to Hondurans suffering vexations or reprisals in their persons or property, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of four (4) to seven (7) years, a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to three hundred thousand Lempiras (L.300,000.00) and absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment. If war results from the rupture of relations, the penalty shall be eight (8) to twelve (12) years, a fine equal to double the previous amount and absolute disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment.

#### ARTICULO 314

Whoever violates the truces or armistices agreed between the Republic and another State or between the armed forces of both countries, or the safe-conducts duly issued, and whoever prevents or disturbs the fulfillment of a treaty or agreement concluded with another State, shall be subject to imprisonment of one to three years.

#### ARTICULO 315

Whoever outrages any of the national symbols shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years, a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00) and special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

#### ARTICULO 316

Whoever violates the immunity of a foreign Head of State or of a diplomatic agent accredited to the government of Honduras shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years.

#### ARTICULO 317

Whoever recruits troops in Honduras for the service of a foreign nation, whatever the purpose intended, shall be sanctioned with three to six years of imprisonment.

Those who use the national territory to invade or attack another nation shall incur the same penalty.

### CAPITULO III

CRIMES AGAINST THE LAW OF NATIONS

#### ARTICULO 318

Whoever kills a foreign Head of State or Head of Government, who is in Honduras in an official capacity, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of thirty
(30) to deprivation of liberty for life.

#### ARTICULO 319

Whoever, with the purpose of destroying totally or partially a national, ethnic or religious group, performs any of the following acts shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of sixteen (16) to twenty (20) years plus absolute disqualification for an equal time:
1)	Killing any member of the group;

2)	Seriously injuring the physical or mental integrity of any of the members of the group;

3)	Subjecting the group to conditions of existence likely to bring about its physical destruction or cause it serious moral harm;
4)	Adopting measures aimed at preventing the birth of children within the group; or
5)	Compulsorily transferring children under eighteen (18) years of age from one group to another.

The imprisonment shall not be less than twenty (20) years when those responsible for the crime of genocide are civil or military public officials or employees.

The proposal and conspiracy shall be punished with imprisonment of eight (8) to twelve (12) years; direct incitement shall be sanctioned with the penalty applicable to the perpetrator and indirect incitement shall be punished with imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years.

#### ARTICLE 320. Shall be punished as a pirate, with imprisonment of five (5) to ten (10) years

1) Whoever without political purposes violently seizes or under threat of aircraft or public transport vehicles; and
2) Whoever in the territorial sea or in the rivers of the Republic seizes a ship or practices acts of violence or depredation against the vessel or against the persons or things found therein.

The provisions of this article shall be understood without prejudice to the crimes resulting against life, bodily integrity, honesty, property and liberty, which shall be punished separately.

#### ARTICLE 321

Shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of thirty thousand (L.30,000.00) to fifty thousand Lempiras (L.50,000.00) whoever discriminates against another person on grounds of sex, race, age, class, religion, party or political militancy, suffering from any disability and any other injurious to human dignity. If the responsible party is a foreigner, he shall be expelled from the national territory once the sentence has been served.

## TITLE XII

CRIMES AGAINST THE INTERNAL SECURITY OF THE STATE

### CHAPTER I

CRIMES AGAINST HIGH OFFICIALS OF THE STATE

#### ARTICLE 322

Whoever causes the death of the President of a Branch of the State, shall be punished with thirty (30) years to life imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 323

Whoever offends the President of the Republic in his bodily integrity or in his liberty shall be punished with eight to twelve years of imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 324

Conspiracy to commit any of the crimes included in the preceding articles shall be punished with two to six years of imprisonment; and the proposal, with one to three years.

#### ARTICLE 325

The crimes referred to in the three preceding articles committed against the Secretaries of State, Deputies to the National Congress and Justices of the Supreme Court of Justice, shall be punished respectively with the penalties indicated in said articles, reduced by one fifth.

#### ARTICLE 326

Those who violently or with intimidation invade the place where the National Congress, the Supreme Court of Justice or the Council of Ministers is meeting, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years.

#### ARTICLE 327. Shall incur imprisonment of six months to two years

1) Those who violently or with intimidation invade the premises where the office of a Secretary of State is constituted.

2) Those who restrict or in any way place obstacles to the freedom of the Ministers meeting in Council.
3) Those who employ force or serious intimidation, to prevent a Minister from attending his office or the Council of Ministers.

### CHAPTER II

CRIMES AGAINST THE FORM OF GOVERNMENT

#### ARTICLE 328

Commit crimes against the form of Government and shall be punished with imprisonment of six to twelve years, those who execute acts directly aimed at achieving by force, or outside legal channels, any of the following purposes:

1) Replace the republican, democratic and representative Government with any other form of Government.

2) Alter the constitution of any of the Branches of the State, Legislative, Executive or Judicial, or attack its independence.
3) Deprive in whole or in part the Congress, the Executive Branch or the Supreme Court of Justice, of the prerogatives and powers attributed to them by the Constitution.

4) Vary the legitimate order of succession to the Presidency, or deprive the successor of the President of the powers granted to him by the Constitution.
5) Deprive the Council of Ministers or the person in charge of the Executive Branch, of the power to provisionally govern the State in the cases provided for in the Constitution.

#### ARTICLE 329

When the perpetrators of these crimes are officials they shall be punished, in addition, with absolute disqualification from six to twelve years.

#### ARTICLE 330

Shall be punished with imprisonment of six to ten years whoever having exercised under any title the Presidency of the Republic, promotes or executes acts violating the constitutional article that prohibits him from exercising the Presidency of the Republic again or holding said office again under any title.

The same penalty shall be incurred by those who directly support him or propose to reform said article.
When the perpetrators of these crimes are officials they shall be punished in addition with absolute disqualification for ten years counted from the date of the violation or its attempted reform.

### CHAPTER III

CRIMES COMMITTED BY PRIVATE INDIVIDUALS, EXCEEDING THE EXERCISE OF THE RIGHTS GUARANTEED TO THEM BY THE CONSTITUTION

#### ARTICLE 331

Shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years and a fine of thirty thousand Lempiras (L.30,000.00) to sixty thousand Lempiras (L.60,000.00), those who convene or direct in an unlawful manner any meeting or demonstration. Shall have the

character of unlawful all those meetings attended by persons with weapons, explosive devices or blunt objects or in any other dangerous manner, with the purpose of committing a crime.
Those attending an unlawful meeting or demonstration who carry weapons, explosive devices or blunt objects or in any other dangerous manner, shall be punished with the same penalty as those who convene or direct them. Mere attendees shall be punished with half of the previous penalties.

Persons who on the occasion of holding an unlawful meeting or demonstration perform acts of violence against the authority, its agents, persons or public or private property, shall be punished with the penalty provided in the first paragraph of this Article, without prejudice to those corresponding to the other crimes committed.

Those who on their own initiative attend a lawful meeting or demonstration, carrying weapons, explosive devices or blunt objects or in any other dangerous manner with the purpose of committing a crime, shall be punished with the same penalty applicable to those who convene or direct an unlawful meeting or demonstration.

#### ARTICLE 332

Shall be punished with three (3) to six (6) years of imprisonment and a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand Lempiras (L.200,000.00) the founders, ringleaders or leaders of gangs or unlawful groups. The other members shall be punished with the same penalties reduced by one third.

Shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras (L.10,000.00) and confiscation whoever without corresponding authorization or permit is found carrying national or war weapons. Whoever carries a commercial weapon without the proper permit shall have it confiscated. The confiscated weapons shall be delivered to the national police after an inventory drawn up for that purpose.

### CHAPTER IV

CRIMES COMMITTED BY OFFICIALS AGAINST THE EXERCISE OF RIGHTS GUARANTEED BY THE CONSTITUTION

#### ARTICLE 333

The penalty of imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) shall be applied to the official or public employee who:

1) Illegally detains or holds incommunicado a person or does not immediately comply with the writ of habeas corpus issued by competent authority;
2) Does not timely order the release of a detainee when legally appropriate or who retains him after having received the order for his release;

3) Subjects to vexations or illegal duress the persons entrusted to his custody;
4) Does not process or resolve within the legal terms a petition for habeas corpus or amparo or by any means obstructs its processing; and,

5) Orders, executes or consents to the expatriation of a Honduran.

#### ARTICLE 334

With the penalties provided in the preceding Article, public officials or employees who commit any of the following acts shall be sanctioned:

1)	Retain, conceal, destroy or violate postal, telegraphic, facsimile or any other class of correspondence;  

2)	Illegally restrict the freedom of movement of a person within the national territory;  

3)	Force a person to change domicile, except by sentence of a competent judge;  
4)	Force a private individual to provide their personal services without legal remuneration, except by judicial sentence;  

5)	Force a person to become part of an association dedicated to the execution of illicit acts.  

6)	Prevent or suspend a legally authorized meeting;  

7)	Prevent or hinder the free circulation of a printed matter whose authors or publishers have complied with the legal requirements for its publication and sale;  

8)	Deprive a person of their books and publications of a scientific, philosophical, political or artistic nature or of any other printed materials with the intention of restricting or preventing the free circulation of ideas; and,

9)	Impose on prisoners or sentenced persons sanctions, deprivations or regulations not provided for or authorized by law or that exceed them.

### CHAPTER V TERRORISM

#### ARTICLE 335

Those who with political purposes attack the security of the State by executing any of the following acts commit the crime of terrorism:

1)	Being part of the crew of a civil or military aircraft, national or foreign, that is flying over Honduran territory or whose destination is an airport of the Republic, or that is located at one of these, they rebel against the commander or captain thereof, taking its control or seizing its cargo;  

2)	Assault and seize a civil or military aircraft, national or foreign, diverting it from its destination, taking it from the place where it is located or retaining it against the wishes of its owner, commander or captain or forcing any of these to execute acts against their will;  

3)	Manufacture, possess, trade or transport without the proper legal authorization, firearms, explosives, detonators, flammable products and communication equipment, clothing, uniforms and any other material or equipment for the exclusive use of the Armed Forces of Honduras;  
4)	Perform acts that have as their purpose to damage, deteriorate, harm or destroy the physical installations of a productive enterprise or sabotage its operation;  

5)	Plan, organize, coordinate or participate in the kidnapping or illegal detention of one or more persons;  
6)	Form bands, gangs or armed groups that invade or assault towns, hospitals, health centers, banking, financial or insurance institutions, shopping or work centers, temples and other similar places or take or obstruct highways or other public roads;  

7)	Form bands, gangs or armed groups that exercise violence against persons or seize through threats property of any kind or force their owners, possessors, administrators or guardians to deliver them or collect periodic sums of money or valuables under the pretext of guaranteeing, defending or ensuring respect for the life or rights of one or more persons;  

8)	Damage the property of others through the use of bombs, explosives, chemical or flammable substances or other analogous means;  

9)	Force another person to deliver, send, deposit or place at their disposal property, money or other valuable objects, or documents capable of producing legal effects, by means of threats or violence or by impersonating a public authority or simulating an order thereof; and,
10)	Force another person, by any of the means provided in the preceding numeral, to sign or destroy documents or securities in their possession.  
The crime of terrorism shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of fifteen (15) to twenty (20) years and absolute disqualification for the same period of time. The attempt, in the cases referred to in numerals 1) and 2) of this Article, shall be sanctioned with the indicated penalty reduced by one quarter.

### CHAPTER VI REBELLION

#### ARTICLE 336

Those who rise up in arms to overthrow the legally constituted government or to change or suspend in whole or in part the existing constitutional regime with regard to the formation, functioning or renewal of public powers are guilty of rebellion.  

The promoters, leaders or ringleaders of the crime of rebellion shall be punished with imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years, a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand Lempiras (L.200,000.00) and absolute disqualification for the duration of the imprisonment.  

Those who by designation of the rebels perform administrative or jurisdictional functions shall suffer the same penalties reduced by one third.  
The mere executors of the rebellion shall incur imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years, plus a fine of thirty thousand (L.30,000.00) to fifty thousand Lempiras (L.50,000.00), if there has been combat between the rebel force and the public force loyal to the government or the former has caused havoc to private property, of the State or its institutions, if it has destroyed or interrupted any public service, exercised serious violence against persons, or demanded contributions or diverted public funds from their legitimate investment; and the penalty of imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years, plus a fine of ten thousand (L.10,000.00) to thirty thousand Lempiras (L.30,000.00) when such circumstances do not occur.  

The proposal and conspiracy to commit the crime of rebellion shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years, plus a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras (L.10,000.00).

### CHAPTER VII SEDITION

#### ARTICLE 337

Those who without being included in the crime of rebellion rise up publicly and tumultuously to achieve by force or outside legal channels any of the following purposes are guilty of sedition:

1)	Prevent the holding of elections for national, departmental or municipal authorities;  

2)	Prevent legitimately elected or appointed officials from taking possession of their positions;  
3)	Prevent any authority from the free exercise of its functions or the fulfillment of its resolutions;  

4)	Prevent the approval, sanction, promulgation, publication or execution of any law;  
5)	Perform any act of hatred or revenge against private individuals or against servants of the State or against their property with political or social purpose; or

6)	Break into penal centers or attack the custodians of prisoners, either to rescue or to mistreat them.  
Those guilty of sedition shall be punished with imprisonment of five (5) to ten (10) years, a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) and absolute disqualification for the duration of the imprisonment, if they act as instigators, ringleaders or leaders, and with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of twenty-five thousand (L.25,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras (L.50,000.00) if they are mere executors.

#### ARTICLE 338

The proposal and conspiracy to commit the crime of sedition shall be punished with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years and a fine of three thousand (L.3,000.00) to six thousand lempiras (L.6,000.00).

### CHAPTER VIII

PROVISIONS COMMON TO THE TWO PRECEDING CHAPTERS

#### ARTICLE 339

When the rebels or seditionists dissolve or submit to the summons made to them by the legitimate authority, the mere executors of any of those crimes are exempt from penalty, if they are not public employees.

#### ARTICLE 340

Those who publicly incite formally or directly to a rebellion or sedition, communicate instructions or indicate the means to consummate said crimes, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years and a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand lempiras (L.10,000.00) when those have not been attempted or consummated.

#### ARTICLE 341

Persons who, participating or not in a rebellion or sedition or who by reason of it, commit other special crimes typified in this Code, shall be sanctioned in accordance with what is established in the corresponding chapters.

#### ARTICLE 342

When it is not possible to discover the perpetrators, the principal leaders of the rebellion or sedition shall be punished as such.

### CHAPTER IX. ATTACK

#### ARTICLE 343. Commit attack

1)	Those who, without rising publicly, employ force or intimidation for any of the objects indicated in the crimes of rebellion and sedition.  

2)	Those who attack the authority or its agents, or employ force against them, or intimidate them gravely, or offer them also grave resistance, while they are exercising the functions of their positions or on occasion of them.

#### ARTICLE 344

The attacks included in the preceding article shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment of one to three years, provided that any of the following circumstances concur:
1)	If the aggression is carried out armed.  
2)	If the accused are officials.  

3)	If they lay hands on the authority.  

4)	If as a consequence of the coercion, the authority has acceded to the demands of the accused.

## TITLE XIII

CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION

### CHAPTER I CONTEMPT

#### ARTICLE 345

Whoever threatens, slanders, insults, offends or in any other way offends in their dignity a public authority on occasion of the exercise of their functions, whether in fact, by word or in writing, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years.  

If the offended party is the President of the Republic or any of the high officials referred to in Article 325 of this Code, the imprisonment shall be two (2) to five (5) years.

### CHAPTER II DISOBEDIENCE

#### ARTICLE 346

Whoever disobeys an authority by openly refusing to give due compliance to sentences, resolutions or orders issued within the limits of its competence and clothed with legal formalities, shall be punished with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years.

#### ARTICLE 347

The foreigner who, legally expelled from Honduras, enters the national territory again violating the corresponding order, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six (6) months to three (3) years and once the sentence is served shall be expelled from the country.

#### ARTICLE 348

Whoever refuses or declines to perform a public office of popular election without justified cause or after it has been dismissed by the competent authority, shall incur a fine of ten thousand (L.10,000.00) to thirty thousand lempiras (L.30,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 348

A. The penalty provided in Article 348, above, plus imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years, shall be imposed on whoever, legally summoned as an expert or witness, abstains without just cause from providing the respective declaration or from appearing before the competent judge.

### CHAPTER III

ABUSE OF AUTHORITY AND VIOLATION
OF THE DUTIES OF OFFICIALS

#### ARTICLE 349

Shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and special disqualification for double the time that the imprisonment lasts, the official or public employee who:
1)	Refuses to give due compliance to orders, sentences, rulings, resolutions, agreements or decrees issued by judicial or administrative authorities within the limits of their respective competences and with legal formalities;  

2)	Issues or executes orders, sentences, rulings, resolutions, agreements or decrees contrary to the Constitution of the Republic or to the laws or abstains from complying with what is provided by any of said legal systems;  

3)	Omits, refuses or delays any act that must be executed in accordance with the duties of their position;  
4)	Required by competent authority does not provide due cooperation for the effective administration of justice or another public service. When the lack of cooperation consists of not complying by malice or negligence with an arrest order issued by competent authorities, the penalty shall be increased by one third; or

5)	Reveals or facilitates the revelation of a fact of which they have knowledge by reason of their position and which must remain secret. When the revelation is not of grave significance, the penalty shall be reduced by one sixth.  

The same penalty shall apply to the officer or agent of the public force who refuses, omits or delays, without justified cause, the provision of assistance legally required by competent authority.

#### ARTICLE 350

Whoever begins to perform a public position or employment without having rendered the bond required by law or without having made the sworn declaration of assets ordered by the Law against Illicit Enrichment of Public Servants or without having taken the corresponding oath of law, shall incur a fine equal to triple the monthly salary of the corresponding employee or official, without prejudice to the obligation to fulfill the legal requirements.  
The official or public employee who has made it possible for a subordinate to begin performing the position or employment before having complied with any of the requirements indicated in the preceding paragraph shall incur the same sanction.

#### ARTICLE 350

A. Whoever continues performing an employment, commission or public position after they should cease in it in accordance with the law, shall be sanctioned with a fine of fifteen thousand (L.15,000.00) to twenty thousand lempiras (L.20,000.00) and special disqualification of one (1) year to two (2) years.

#### ARTICLE 351

The person responsible for any of the crimes referred to in the two preceding Articles who has received salaries, wages, fees or emoluments by reason of the position or employment, either before being able to perform it or after having been legally suspended, must return to the Treasury the sums received. If they do not do so, the restitution shall be commuted to imprisonment in the manner established for the fine.

#### ARTICLE 352

The official or public employee who abandons their position without having had their resignation accepted shall be sanctioned with a fine equal to the last three monthly salaries they have earned and with special disqualification for two (2) to four (4) years, if the abandonment causes damages to the State. If it does not cause such damages, the penalty shall be special disqualification of one (1) to two (2) years.  

If the official or public employee abandons the position in order not to prosecute or punish any of the crimes against the existence and security of the State or against the internal security of the State, they shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of four (4) to seven (7) years, plus special disqualification for double the time that the imprisonment lasts. If the purpose sought is not to prosecute or punish any other crime, they shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years, plus special disqualification for double the time that the imprisonment lasts.  

The provisions of this Article shall not be applicable if within thirty (30) days following the date of submission of the resignation it has not been accepted or rejected.

#### ARTICLE 353

A fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras (L.10,000.00) and special disqualification of three (3) to six (6) years shall be imposed on the official or employee who, knowingly, detains, prosecutes or judges a public official who enjoys immunity without having previously exhausted the procedures established by law.

#### ARTICLE 354

The public official or employee who usurps functions proper to another position shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years, plus a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand lempiras (L.10,000.00) and special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 355

The official who, legally required to recuse themselves, continues acting before the question of competence is resolved, shall be sanctioned with a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand lempiras (L.10,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 356

The public official or employee, civil or military, who directs orders or demands or in any way interferes in causes, matters or business that are within the exclusive competence of a judicial authority, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three
(3) to six (6) years and a fine equal to triple the last salary received by the corresponding public official or employee, civil or military.

#### ARTICLE 357

The public official or employee who, knowingly, proposes or appoints to a public position or employment persons who do not meet the requirements established by Law, shall be punished with a fine of twenty-five thousand (L.25,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras (L.50,000.00) and special disqualification of one (1) to three (3) years.  
Those cases in which the appointment is interim in nature and is made based on special laws are excepted.

### CHAPTER IV

VIOLATION OF SEALS AND DOCUMENTS

#### ARTICLE 358

Anyone who violates seals placed by a public official or employee to ensure the preservation, identity or privacy of the contents of a thing shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years, plus a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to seventy-five thousand lempiras (L.75,000.00).  
If the perpetrator is a public official or employee and has committed the act with abuse of their position, in addition to the penalty provided in the preceding paragraph they shall be sanctioned with special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.  

If the act has been committed through the fault of a public official or employee, they shall be sanctioned with a fine of twenty-five thousand (L.25,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras (L.50,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 358

A. Anyone who removes, conceals, destroys or renders useless records or documents entrusted to the custody of a public official or another person in the interest of the public service or objects intended to serve as evidence before a competent authority, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.  
If the perpetrator of the act is the custodian themselves, in addition to the preceding penalty they shall be imposed the penalty of absolute disqualification for double the time of the sentence.  
If the act is committed through the fault of the custodian, they shall be imposed a fine of ten thousand (L10,000.00) to thirty thousand lempiras (L.30,000.00).

#### ARTICLE 359

The public official or employee who, not being included in the preceding Article, opens or consents to the opening, without due authorization, of closed or sealed papers or documents whose custody has been entrusted to them, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years.

#### ARTICLE 360

The penalties indicated in the three preceding articles are also applicable to ministers of any religion and to private individuals accidentally charged with the dispatch or custody of documents or papers by commission of the Government, or of officials to whom those have been entrusted by reason of their position.

### CHAPTER V BRIBERY

#### ARTICLE 361

The public official or employee who solicits, receives or accepts, by themselves or through another person, gifts, presents, offers, promises or any other undue advantage to execute an act contrary to their duties that constitutes a crime, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of five (5) to seven (7) years, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment, without prejudice to the penalty applicable to the crime committed by reason of the gift or promise.

#### ARTICLE 362

The public official who solicits, receives or accepts, directly or indirectly, gifts, presents, offers, promises or any other undue advantage to perform an unjust act not constituting a crime relating to the exercise of their position, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years. If said act is not consummated, the offender shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years. In both cases absolute disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment shall be applied.

#### ARTICLE 363

When the gift or present solicited, received or promised has as its object that the public official or employee refrain from executing an act that they should perform in fulfillment of their legal obligations, the penalty shall be imprisonment of two
(2) to five (5) years plus special disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 364

The provisions of the three preceding articles shall apply to arbitrators, arbiters, experts or any persons who perform a public function.

#### ARTICLE 365

The public official or employee who accepts a gift or benefit of any kind from someone who has a matter submitted to their knowledge, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one (1) to four (4) years, plus special disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 366

Anyone who with gifts, presents, offers or promises corrupts or attempts to corrupt a public official or employee, shall be sanctioned with the same penalties applicable to passive bribery, increased by two thirds.

#### ARTICLE 367. (REPEALED)

#### ARTICLE 368

The gifts or presents referred to in the preceding Articles shall be confiscated and publicly delivered to the municipal corporation having jurisdiction in the place where the crime was committed for works of social interest.

#### ARTICLE 369

The judge who accepts a gift, a present, a promise or a loan to issue, delay or refrain from issuing a ruling, resolution or judgment in any matter of which they have knowledge, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of five (5) to seven (7) years, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.  

The penalties provided in this Article and in the preceding ones of this Chapter shall be applicable, as the case may be, to anyone who being a member of a collegiate body or tribunal, votes in a certain direction thanks to bribery.

### CHAPTER V-A

#### ARTICLE 369

A. The public official or employee who influences another public official or employee by taking advantage of the powers of their position or any other situation derived from their personal or hierarchical relationship with them or with another public official or employee to obtain a resolution that may generate directly or indirectly an economic benefit or of any other nature for themselves or for a third party, shall incur the penalties of imprisonment of four (4) to seven (7) years, plus a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to one hundred fifty thousand Lempiras (L.150,000.00) and absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment. If they obtain the benefit sought, the imprisonment shall be six (6) to nine (9) years, the fine equal to double the benefit obtained and the disqualification of five (5) to eight (8) years.

#### ARTICLE 369

B. The private individual who influences a public official or employee by taking advantage of any situation derived from their personal relationship with them or with another public official or employee to obtain a resolution that may generate, directly or indirectly, an economic benefit or of any other nature for themselves or for a third party, shall be sanctioned, as the case may be, with the penalties of imprisonment and fine established in the preceding Article.

#### ARTICLE 369

C. Those who, offering to carry out the conduct described in the preceding Articles, solicit from third parties gifts, presents or any other remuneration or gratuity, or accept an offer or promise, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years plus a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00).

In any of the cases referred to in this Article, the judicial authority shall also impose the suspension of the activities of the natural or legal person, organization or office and the closure of its establishments or offices open to the public for a term of two (2) to four (4) years.

#### ARTICLE 369

D. In the cases provided for in this Chapter and in the preceding one, the gifts, presents, commissions or gratuities shall be confiscated.

### CHAPTER VI

MISAPPROPRIATION OF PUBLIC FUNDS

#### ARTICLE 370

The public official or employee who appropriates funds, property or effects whose administration, collection or custody has been entrusted to him by reason of his office or who, without having been entrusted with them, intervenes in such acts for any reason, shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years if the value thereof does not exceed one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00) and six (6) to twelve (12) years if it exceeds said amount, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.
The provisions of this Article shall also be applicable to the directors of trade unions, peasant associative enterprises, cooperatives, patronages, charitable or sports associations and, in general, to all other analogous civil entities.

#### ARTICLE 371

The public official or employee and the directors of the associations referred to in the preceding Article who negligently give rise to another person appropriating the funds, property or effects referred to in the same provision, shall be punished with a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) and special disqualification of two (2) to four (4) years.

#### ARTICLE 372

The public official or employee who allocates the funds, property or effects that he administers to a purpose different from that which corresponds to them and if thereby he does not cause damage to the patrimonial interests of the State, shall be punished with a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) and special disqualification of three (3) to five (5) years.

If he causes damage to said interests or hinders a public service, the fine shall be equal to one hundred percent (100%) of the value of the damage caused or of the expenses that the State must incur to normalize the corresponding public service, plus absolute disqualification of five (5) to eight (8) years. In no case shall the fine be less than that indicated in the preceding paragraph.

#### ARTICLE 373

The public official or employee who, having available funds, unjustifiably delays a legally demandable payment shall be punished in the manner provided for in the first paragraph of the preceding Article.
The same punishment shall be imposed on the public official or employee who, being legally required, refuses to deliver a sum of money or the funds, property or effects that are under his administration or custody.

#### ARTICLE 373

A. The provisions of this Chapter shall be applicable to those who are in charge by any title of the management of departmental or municipal funds, revenues or effects or that belong to an educational or charitable institution, as well as to the administrators or depositaries of funds seized, sequestered or deposited by a public authority even if they belong to private individuals.

### CHAPTER VII

TRANSACTIONS INCOMPATIBLE WITH THE EXERCISE OF PUBLIC FUNCTIONS

#### ARTICLE 374

The public official or employee who directly or through another person, or by simulated acts, takes an interest, with intent of personal profit, in any contract or transaction in which he is participating by reason of his office, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be applicable to experts and private accountants who have participated in the appraisal, partition or adjudication of property and to guardians or curators and to the trustees of a bankruptcy.

#### ARTICLE 375

The punishment established in the preceding article shall be applied to the official or employee who, with the purpose of profit, interposes his influence to obtain a resolution from any authority, or an opinion to be pronounced before it.

### CHAPTER VIII

FRAUD AND ILLEGAL EXACTIONS

#### ARTICLE 376

The public official or employee who by reason of his office participates in any legal act in which the State has an interest and reaches an agreement with any of the interested parties to defraud the Treasury or with that same purpose takes advantage of his position to favor a third party or to facilitate his personal participation, direct or indirect, in the benefits that such matters may produce or uses any other artifice with the same purpose, shall be punished with imprisonment of six (6) to nine (9) years, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 377

The public official or employee who demands payment of a tax or tribute, contribution or fee knowing that it is illegal or who, it being legal, employs vexatious or burdensome means for its collection or recovery or falsely invokes a judicial order or other legitimate authorization, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years, plus special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

The public official or employee who uses for his own benefit or that of third parties the exactions referred to in the preceding paragraph, shall be punished with imprisonment of four (4) to eight (8) years, plus absolute disqualification for a time equal to double that of the imprisonment.

### CHAPTER IX MALFEASANCE

#### ARTICLE 378

The judge who with malice or awareness of the injustice renders a judgment contrary to law to favor or harm an accused in criminal matters shall incur imprisonment of three (3) to nine (9) years, plus special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 379. Shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years

1)	The judge who with malice and true awareness of its injustice renders a judgment contrary to law in a non-criminal trial; and,
2)	The official who with malice or awareness of the injustice issues a resolution, agreement or decree contrary to law in purely administrative matters.

#### ARTICLE 380

Shall incur the penalty of special disqualification of three (3) to five (5) years:
1)	The Judge who refuses to rule claiming obscurity, insufficiency or silence of the law;

2)	The Judge who through negligence or inexcusable ignorance renders a manifestly illegal judgment; and,
3)	The administrative official who through negligence or inexcusable ignorance issues a manifestly illegal agreement or resolution, in a purely administrative matter.

#### ARTICLE 381

The notary, attorney, licentiate in legal and social sciences or procurator who through abuse in the performance of his mandate or through negligence or inexcusable ignorance harms his client or discloses secrets thereof that he has learned due to the exercise of his profession shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years, plus special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

#### ARTICLE 382

The legal professional Procurator who has acted or is acting as representative of a person and represents without the latter's consent the opposing party or advises it in the same matter, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years.

### CHAPTER X

DENIAL AND DELAY OF JUSTICE

#### ARTICLE 383

A Judge who does not give course to a petition, complaint, accusation, criminal complaint or denunciation presented in legal form or who maliciously or irresponsibly delays the administration of justice, shall be sanctioned with special disqualification from three (3) to six
(6) years.

#### ARTICLE 384

The public official or employee who, failing in the obligations of his office, does not adopt the necessary measures to achieve the detention and prosecution of a presumed offender, shall be sanctioned with a fine of thirty thousand (L.30,000.00) to sixty thousand Lempiras (L.60,000.00) and special disqualification from three (3) to six (6) years.

### CHAPTER XI

FALSE TESTIMONY, FALSE ACCUSATION AND DENUNCIATION

#### ARTICLE 385

The witness, expert or interpreter who in his deposition, report, translation or interpretation, made before competent authority, totally or partially falsifies the truth or conceals it, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to five (5) years.

The sanction shall be from three (3) to six (6) years, if the false testimony is committed in a criminal case to the detriment of the accused.

In all cases, absolute disqualification from three (3) to eight (8) years shall also be imposed.

#### ARTICLE 386

Whoever knowingly presents false witnesses in court shall incur the respective penalties of the preceding article.

#### ARTICLE 387

The crime of false accusation or denunciation is committed when facts are imputed to a person which, if true, would constitute crimes giving rise to ex officio proceedings, if the imputation is made before an administrative or judicial official who by reason of his office must proceed to its investigation or punishment. However, proceedings may not be brought against the denouncer or accuser except after the case has been dismissed or an acquittal has been rendered.
False accusation or denunciation shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years.

### CHAPTER XII

CONCEALMENT

#### ARTICLE 388

Whoever, without prior agreement with the authors or accomplices of a crime, but with sufficient reasons to suppose its commission, shall incur imprisonment from three (3) to five (5) years:
1)	Conceals the offender or facilitates his flight to avoid his prosecution;
2)	Procures the disappearance of evidence of the crime;

3)	Keeps, hides, buys, sells or receives in pledge or exchange the effects or instruments of the crime;
4)	Denies the authority, without justified reason, permission to enter his domicile to capture the offender who is found therein;

5)	Fails to communicate to the authority the information he may have about the commission of any crime when he is obliged to do so by his profession or employment. In this case, in addition to the established sanction, special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment shall be imposed; or

6)	Assists the authors or accomplices to benefit from the product or price of objects proceeding from the crime or personally takes advantage of the mentioned product or price.

When the concealment is executed with intent to profit, the penalty shall be increased by one third. If the concealer executes the acts referred to in this Article habitually, the penalty shall be increased by fifty percent (50%).

#### ARTICLE 389

Those who are concealers of their spouse or of the person with whom they live as married, or of their relatives within the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity, shall not be sanctioned, except when they have taken advantage themselves or have assisted the offenders to take advantage of the effects of the crime.

### CHAPTER XIII

ESCAPE

#### ARTICLE 390

Whoever escapes while legally detained or convicted shall be sanctioned in addition with imprisonment from five (5) to ten (10) years. If the escape is produced by means of intimidation or violence against persons or force against things, the penalty shall be increased by one third, without prejudice to the sanction that must be applied to the crimes to which the intimidation, violence or force give rise.

#### ARTICLE 391

Whoever collaborates in the escape of a detainee or convict shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from five (5) to ten (10) years. If the person who has favored the escape is a public servant, in addition to the increase by one third in the penalty due to his condition as such, special disqualification for double the time established for imprisonment shall be imposed.

However, they are exempt from criminal liability if the person who collaborates in the escape of a detainee or convict is his spouse or person with whom he lives as married, or his relatives within the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity, of the person who has escaped, excepting public servants.

#### ARTICLE 392

The custodian through whose fault the escape of a detainee or convict has occurred shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from one (1) to three (3) years. If the escape is through the fault of the warden or other penitentiary official, the imprisonment shall be from three (3) to six
(6) years.
If the escape is produced by fraud of the custodian, warden or other penitentiary official or employee, he shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from four (4) to nine (9) years, a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to seventy thousand lempiras (L.70,000.00), plus special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

### CHAPTER XIV GENERAL PROVISION

#### ARTICLE 393

For all purposes of this Code, every natural person who, by provision of law or by appointment of competent authority, participates in the exercise of public functions or performs a public office or employment, shall be deemed a public official or employee.
Municipal mayors and aldermen shall also be deemed public officials. BOOK THREE
MISDEMEANORS

## TITLE I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### ARTICLE 394

The provisions contained in Book One of this Code are applicable to misdemeanors, as pertinent, with the following modifications:
1)	Only misdemeanors committed in the national territory are punishable.

2)	Only consummated misdemeanors are punished.

3)	Only the authors are liable for misdemeanors.
4)	Conditional suspension of sentence shall be applicable to imprisonment imposed for misdemeanors, during a probationary period of two years.

5)	The security measures established in Article 83 of this Code may be applied to the authors of misdemeanors, but in no case shall it exceed one year.
6)	Recidivism in misdemeanors may not be assessed after one year has elapsed from the date of the conviction.

7)	The confiscation of the instruments and effects of misdemeanors shall be decreed by the courts at their prudent discretion according to the circumstances.

#### ARTICLE 395

The judge may exempt from penalty in the sentence whoever commits a misdemeanor for the first time, when due to the levity of the act, the determining motives and the minimal dangerousness of the subject, there are probabilities that he will not offend again, without prejudice to the corresponding civil liability.

## TITLE II

MISDEMEANORS AGAINST PERSONS

#### ARTICLE 396. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from thirty to ninety days

1)	Whoever causes another injuries that produce illness or incapacity for work for up to ten days.
2)	Whoever, finding a minor under twelve years of age abandoned or lost, does not notify his family or the authority, or fails to take him to a safe place within forty-eight hours following the finding.

3)	Whoever in a tumultuous brawl has exercised any violence against the person of the offended party, provided that the latter has not suffered more injuries than those mentioned in the first numeral of this Article, and it is not established who was its author.

4)	Parents or legal guardians, whose faculties permit them, who do not provide the necessary means to give education to their children while they are minors.

#### ARTICULO 397

The following shall incur the penalty indicated in the preceding article, if the act is not included in Book Two of this Code.

1) Whoever does not aid or assist a person found in an uninhabited place, injured or in danger of perishing, when he could do so without risk or detriment to himself.

2) Whoever verbally, in the heat of anger, threatens another with causing him harm that constitutes a crime, and by his subsequent acts demonstrates that he does not persist in the idea signified by his threat.

3) Whoever causes another unjust coercion or vexation.
4) Spouses who scandalize with their domestic disputes.

What is said of spouses applies to the man and woman who live together as a couple.

5) Whoever threatens another with a weapon, or draws it in a fight except in lawful self-defense.

#### ARTICULO 398. Shall suffer imprisonment of fifteen to sixty days

1) Whoever causes injuries that do not prevent the offended party from engaging in his habitual work.

2) Whoever mistreats his wife or the woman with whom he lives as a couple, when he does not cause her injury.
3) Whoever throws stones at any person or throws objects or substances at him without causing him harm.

4) Whoever assaults a pregnant woman, without causing her harm, when the pregnancy is evident, or her condition is known to him.
5) Whoever strikes or physically mistreats a person without causing him injury.

6) Whoever verbally threatens another with causing him harm that does not constitute a crime.
7) Parents or guardians who exceed the correction of their children or wards; provided they do not cause them injuries.

8) Guardians who abandon their wards exposing them to corruption or not providing them assistance and education.

#### ARTICULO 399. Shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to thirty days

1) The woman who physically or verbally mistreats her husband or partner in marital life, without causing him injury.
2) Whoever has himself accompanied by minors under eighteen years of age in vagrancy or begging, or makes them work in violation of labor laws.

3) Whoever slightly insults another, if claimed by the offended party, whose pardon shall extinguish the penalty. Articles 159 and 163 are applicable, as the case may be.
4) Whoever, requested by another or others to prevent harm, fails to provide the aid requested, if no prejudice is to result.

## TITULO III

OFFENSES AGAINST PROPERTY

#### ARTICULO 400. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of thirty to ninety days

1) Whoever commits theft of movable property whose value does not exceed ten lempiras.

2) The perpetrator of fraud that causes patrimonial damage not greater than ten lempiras.
3) Whoever, finding a lost thing, does not deliver it to the authority or to its owner if he knows who it is and appropriates it with intent to profit, when its value does not exceed ten lempiras.

4) Whoever for interest or profit interprets dreams, makes predictions or divinations, or abuses public credulity in another similar manner.
5) Whoever fraudulently obtains a service or performance knowing that he cannot pay for them, if their value does not exceed ten lempiras.

6) Whoever acquires objects of suspicious origin, purchased from a minor or from a person who can be presumed not to be their legitimate owner.

7) Whoever destroys, diminishes or damages another's property causing him damage not exceeding ten lempiras.

#### ARTICULO 401. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one to twenty days

1) Whoever enters another's land or field to pick fruits and eat them on the spot.

2) Whoever in the same manner picks fruits, crops and other forest products to feed them on the spot to horses or livestock, if the value does not exceed ten lempiras.
3) Whoever without the owner's permission enters another's land or field, before the harvests have been completely gathered, to take advantage of gleaning and other remains thereof.

4) Whoever enters another's enclosed land, if the prohibition to enter is manifest.

5) Whoever enters to hunt or fish in enclosed land or prohibited field without the owner's permission.

6) Whoever for any reason or pretext crosses plantings or sown fields without the owner's permission.

7) Whoever, carrying vehicles of any kind, horses or harmful animals, commits any of the excesses provided in the two preceding subsections, if by reason of the damage he does not deserve a greater penalty.

8) Whoever destroys or damages huts, shelters, hedges, fences, palisades or other defenses of properties, if the act does not deserve a greater penalty.

9) Whoever causes damage by throwing stones, materials or projectiles of any kind, if the act does not deserve a greater penalty.

#### ARTICULO 402

Shall be sanctioned with a fine of five hundred (L.500.00) to nine hundred Lempiras (L.900.00), the owner of livestock that enters another's enclosed land or field

and causes damage. If they do not cause damage, the fine shall be two hundred (L.200.00) to five hundred lempiras (L.500.00).

#### ARTICULO 403

If the livestock were introduced on purpose, or by abandonment or negligence of the owners or those in charge, in addition to paying the fine last expressed, they shall suffer in their respective cases, imprisonment of thirty to ninety days, unless the act constitutes a crime.

#### ARTICULO 404

Shall be sanctioned with the penalty of imprisonment of thirty to ninety days whoever executes a fire of any kind not punished in Book Two of this Code.

#### ARTICULO 405

Shall be sanctioned with a fine of one hundred (L.100.00) to four hundred lempiras (L.400.00):
1) Whoever violates the regulations or ordinances on burning of stubble or forest goods or products; and,

2) Whoever violates the ordinances on hunting and fishing.

#### ARTICULO 406

Whoever causes damage whose value does not exceed five hundred lempiras (L.500.00), shall be sanctioned with a fine equal to double the respective value.

If the perpetrator of the damage removes or uses the objects or fruits of the damage caused, in addition to the fine provided in the preceding paragraph he shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of twenty (20) to thirty
(30) days.

#### ARTICULO 407. (REPEALED)

#### ARTICULO 408

Whoever, taking advantage of waters belonging to another or diverting them from their course, causes damage whose amount does not exceed ten lempiras, shall incur imprisonment of five to twenty days.

#### ARTICULO 409

Whoever intentionally, through negligence or carelessness causes any damage not punished in this book or in the preceding one, shall be punished with a fine of one hundred (L.100.00) to two hundred lempiras (L.200.00).

## TITULO IV

OFFENSES AGAINST GOOD MORALS

#### ARTICULO 410

Shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine of seven hundred (L.700.00) to one thousand Lempiras (L.1,000.00):

1) Whoever offends modesty in public form;

2) Whoever exhibits himself naked offending public decency;
3) Whoever becomes intoxicated and provokes scandal or endangers his own safety or that of others. If the intoxication is habitual, the judge may apply the security measure he considers appropriate; and,

4) Whoever in public places addresses a woman in a vulgar manner or with disrespectful phrases or propositions; or molests her with acts or attitudes offensive to modesty.

## TITULO V

OFFENSES AGAINST GENERAL INTERESTS AND REGIME OF POPULATIONS

#### ARTICLE 411. Shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to thirty days

1) Whoever fails to fulfill the responsibility over persons whom the law has placed under their supervision.

2) Whoever having received counterfeit currency in good faith and after being aware of its falsity circulates it in an amount not exceeding ten lempiras.

3) Owners or managers of establishments who sell or serve adulterated beverages or foodstuffs harmful to health, or do not observe in the use and preservation of utensils intended for service, the established rules or customary precautions when the act does not constitute a crime.

4) Whoever violates the sanitary provisions issued by authority regarding the transportation of corpses and burials, in cases not provided for in Book Two of this Code.

5) Whoever, with acts that do not constitute a crime, desecrates corpses, cemeteries or burial places.

#### ARTICLE 412

Shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00), the physician or medical student, dentist or dental student, pharmacist or chemistry and pharmacy student, paramedic, nurse or midwife who, having provided assistance to a person regarding whom it may be suspected that they have participated in the commission of a crime, does not immediately report to the competent authority.

#### ARTICLE 413

Shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00) whoever violates the regulations or ordinances regarding the production and custody of flammable or corrosive materials or chemical products that may cause havoc.

#### ARTICLE 414. In the manner provided in the preceding Article shall be punished whoever

1) Violating the orders of the authority does not carry out the repair or demolition of ruinous, unhealthy or unsightly buildings;

2) Throws into the street or public place water, stones or other objects that may cause harm to persons or things, if the act does not have a greater penalty assigned in the preceding book;

3) Having left debris, materials or other objects, or made pits, ditches or excavations in a place of public transit, does not place signs or protective barriers or does not alert passersby about the existence of the danger;

4) On balconies, windows, parapets or other exterior points of buildings places or suspends objects that, in case of falling, may cause harm to passersby or neighbors;

5) In violation of traffic or police provisions drives animals or vehicles of any kind in places where there is a concentration of people; and,

6) Drives or leaves in the public thoroughfare a draft, pack or racing animal, or any other animal, without having taken sufficient precautions so that it does not cause harm.

## TITLE VI

VIOLATIONS RELATING TO THE EXPRESSION OF THOUGHT

#### ARTICLE 415

Shall incur imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand Lempiras (L.1,000.00), whoever:

1) Disseminates false news from which some danger to public order or damage to the interests or credit of the State may result, if the act does not merit a greater penalty; and,

2) Without committing a crime, publicly incites disobedience of the laws or of the constituted authorities, makes an apology for actions or omissions constituting a crime or offends morality, good customs or public decency.

## TITLE VII

VIOLATIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ORDER

#### ARTICLE 416

Whoever causes damage or deterioration to streets, parks, gardens or promenades, to public lighting, to aqueducts or sewers, to telephone or telegraph services; to bridges or other infrastructure works, damages or steals traffic signs or ornamental objects, artistic objects, historical, archaeological or public utility or recreational monuments, even when they belong to private individuals, shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00), if the act by its gravity does not constitute a crime.

#### ARTICLE 417

Shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00) whoever:

1) With words, shouts, loud or repeated whistles or by means of sound instruments or in any other way disturbs public order, the normal functioning of a court of justice or other state office or the peaceful development of a meeting or demonstration or other analogous public act, if the act by its gravity does not constitute a crime;

2) Announces disasters, accidents or nonexistent dangers and thereby causes public alarm.

#### ARTICLE 418

Shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand (L.1,000.00) Lempiras, whoever:

1) By means of prints or engravings or in any other manner offends morality and good customs;

2) Fails to show the respect and consideration due to a public authority or fails to observe a provision legally issued for reasons of justice, public safety, public order or hygiene;

3) Offends or disobeys agents of authority when they act in the exercise of their functions;

4) Does not provide to the public authority the assistance it requests in case of crime, fire, shipwreck, accident, flood or other calamity, being able to do so without harm or personal risk;

5) With noises or uproar or by means of sound instruments or acoustic signals disturbs the occupations or rest of persons; and,

6) In a public place or place open to the public or by means of the telephone, doorbells or other sound means, by impertinence or for another reprehensible reason, causes annoyance or displeasure to a person.

The provisions of this Article shall not apply if by gravity the act is constitutive of a crime.

#### ARTICLE 419

In the same penalty as that established in the preceding article shall incur whoever conceals their true name, status or domicile from the authority or official who asks them for reasons of their office.

#### ARTICLE 420

In the same sanction of Article 413, shall incur whoever exercises without a degree acts of a profession that requires it, when the act does not have a greater penalty assigned in this Code or in other laws.

FINAL TITLE SOLE CHAPTER
TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### ARTICLE 421

If according to this Code the acts being tried or on which no sentence has been rendered, do not constitute a crime or violation, the courts shall order dismissal or the imposed penalty shall cease to be executed.

#### ARTICLE 422

The penalties of imprisonment, relegation, confinement and banishment applied in accordance with the Penal Code of 1906, other Codes and other special laws, shall be replaced by reclusion without any modification regarding their duration, unless the sanctions contemplated in this Code are more lenient, in which case these shall be applicable; likewise, the penalty of suspension shall be converted ex officio into that of special disqualification.

#### ARTICLE 423

The institutions of "Conditional Suspension of the Execution of the Penalty" and "Conditional Release" shall be applicable to those who have been convicted under previous laws.

#### ARTICLE 424

The time periods established in this Code for the prescription of the crime, of the criminal action and of the penalty shall apply to those who have violated previous criminal laws, provided that said time periods are more favorable.

#### ARTICLE 425

Only persons responsible for crimes whose maximum penalty does not exceed five years may be heard at liberty during the trial, if they provide the respective bond in accordance with the law.

#### ARTICLE 426

This Code shall enter into force one year after its publication in the Official Gazette "La Gaceta", and from that date the Penal Code decreed on the eighth of February nineteen hundred and six, its amendments and other provisions that oppose it shall be repealed.
