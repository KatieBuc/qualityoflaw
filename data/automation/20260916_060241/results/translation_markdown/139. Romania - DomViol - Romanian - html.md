## Chapter I

General provisions +

### Article 1

(1) The protection and support of the family, the development and consolidation of family solidarity, based on friendship, affection and moral and material mutual assistance among family members, constitute an objective of national interest. (2) The prevention and combating of domestic violence are part of the integrated policy of protection and support of the family and represent an important public health issue. (3) The Romanian State, through the competent authorities, elaborates and implements policies and programmes intended for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, as well as for the protection of victims of domestic violence. + Article 2The protection and promotion of the rights of victims of domestic violence are carried out in accordance with the following principles: a) the principle of legality; b) the principle of respect for human dignity; c) the principle of prevention of the commission of acts of domestic violence; d) the principle of celerity; e) the principle of partnership; f) the principle of equal opportunities and treatment between women and men; g) the principle of protection of the life and safety of the victim; h) the principle of respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms; i) the principle of integrated approach. +

### Article 3

For the purposes of this law, domestic violence means any intentional inaction or action of physical, sexual, psychological, economic, social, spiritual or cyber violence, which occurs in the family or domestic environment or between spouses or former spouses, as well as between current or former partners, regardless of whether the aggressor lives or has lived together with the victim. +

### Article 4

(1) Domestic violence manifests itself in the following forms:a) verbal violence - addressing through offensive, brutal language, such as the use of insults, threats, degrading or humiliating words and expressions;b) psychological violence - imposing one's will or personal control, causing states of tension and psychological suffering in any way and by any means, through verbal threats or in any other manner, blackmail, demonstrative violence against objects and animals, ostentatious display of weapons, neglect, control of personal life, acts of jealousy, constraints of any kind, unlawful stalking, surveillance of the dwelling, workplace or other places frequented by the victim, making telephone calls or other types of communications by means of remote transmission, which through their frequency, content or the moment they are made create fear, as well as other actions with similar effect;c) physical violence - bodily harm or harm to health through hitting, pushing, slamming, hair pulling, pricking, cutting, burning, strangling, biting, in any form and of any intensity, including disguised as being the result of accidents, through poisoning, intoxication, as well as other actions with similar effect, subjection to exhausting physical efforts or to activities with a high degree of risk to life or health and bodily integrity, other than those under letter e);d) sexual violence - sexual assault, imposition of degrading acts, harassment, intimidation, manipulation, brutality with a view to maintaining forced sexual relations, marital rape;e) economic violence - prohibition of professional activity, deprivation of economic means, including deprivation of primary means of subsistence, such as food, medicines, basic necessities, the action of intentional misappropriation of the person's goods, prohibition of the right to possess, use and dispose of common goods, inequitable control over common goods and resources, refusal to support the family, imposition of heavy and harmful labour to the detriment of health, including on a minor family member, as well as other actions with similar effect;f) social violence - imposing the isolation of the person from family, community and friends, prohibition of attending educational institution or workplace, prohibition/limitation of professional achievement, imposition of isolation, including in the common dwelling, deprivation of access to living space, dispossession of identity documents, intentional deprivation of access to information, as well as other actions with similar effect;g) spiritual violence - underestimation or diminution of the importance of satisfying moral-spiritual needs through prohibition, limitation, ridicule, penalisation of the aspirations of family members, of access to cultural, ethnic, linguistic or religious values, prohibition of the right to speak in the mother tongue and to teach children to speak in the mother tongue, imposition of adherence to unacceptable spiritual and religious beliefs and practices, as well as other actions with similar effect or with similar repercussions;h) cyber violence - online harassment, online hate speech messages based on gender, online stalking, online threats, non-consensual publication of intimate information and graphic content, unlawful access to interception of communications and private data and any other form of abusive use of information technology and communications through computers, smart mobile phones or other similar devices that use telecommunications or can connect to the internet and can transmit and use social or e-mail platforms, with the purpose of shaming, humiliating, frightening, threatening, silencing the victim.(2) In no form and under no circumstances may custom, culture, religion, tradition and honour be considered as justification for any type of acts of violence defined in this law. +

### Article 5

(1) For the purposes of this law, family member means:a) ascendants and descendants, brothers and sisters, spouses and their children, as well as persons who have become relatives through adoption, according to the law;b) the husband/wife and/or former husband/former wife; the brothers, parents and children from other relationships of the husband/wife or of the former husband/former wife;c) persons who have established relationships similar to those between spouses or between parents and children, current or former partners, regardless of whether they have lived or not with the aggressor, the ascendants and descendants of the partner, as well as their brothers and sisters;d) the guardian or other person who exercises in fact or in law the rights towards the child;e) the legal representative or other person who cares for the person with mental illness, intellectual disability or physical handicap, with the exception of those who fulfil these duties in the exercise of professional tasks.(2) For the purposes of this law, victim means the natural person who is subjected to one or more forms of violence provided for in Article 4, including children who witness these forms of violence. + Article 6The victim of domestic violence has the right:a) to respect for their personality, dignity and private life;b) to information regarding the exercise of their rights;c) to special protection, appropriate to their situation and needs;d) to counselling, rehabilitation, social reintegration services, as well as to free medical assistance, under the conditions of this law;e) to free legal counselling and assistance, under the conditions of the law. +

### Articolul 7

(1) Central and local public administration authorities have the obligation to take the necessary measures for the prevention of domestic violence, for the prevention of situations of repeated violation of the fundamental rights of victims of domestic violence, including by providing information and education programs on the ways in which cases of violence can be prevented, avoided, recognized and reported.(2) Central and local public administration authorities have the obligation to ensure the exercise of the right to information of victims of domestic violence, according to their respective competences, as the case may be, with regard to:a) institutions and non-governmental organizations that provide psychological counseling or any other forms of assistance and protection of the victim, depending on the victim's needs;b) the criminal investigation body to which they can file a complaint;c) the right to legal assistance and the institution to which they can address for the exercise of this right;d) the conditions and procedure for granting free legal assistance;e) the procedural rights of the injured person, the injured party and the civil party;f) the conditions and procedure for granting financial compensation by the state, according to the law;g) the measures that can be ordered by the provisional protection order and, as the case may be, by the protection order, the necessary steps for their issuance and the judicial procedure.(3) Central and local public administration authorities competent in the prevention and combating of domestic violence have the obligation to collect relevant statistical data, disaggregated and at regular intervals regarding cases of domestic violence. These data are collected for the purpose of monitoring the functioning of social services intended for victims, as well as for the purpose of studying the causes and effects of domestic violence and are centralized by the National Agency for Equal Opportunities between Women and Men, which has the obligation to carry out annually a national study broken down by counties regarding domestic violence.(4) Central public administration authorities support annually the conduct of representative analyses or studies at national level regarding the prevalence and trends of all forms of domestic violence.(5) Central and local public administration authorities ensure confidentiality and respect for anonymity in the process of disseminating statistical data and the results of national analyses or studies.(6) Local authorities have the obligation to ensure within existing social services accommodation for aggressors in residential centers for homeless persons and night shelters, which operate in accordance with the Social Assistance Law no. 292/2011, as subsequently amended and supplemented.(7) The attributions of the county commission in the field of equal opportunities between women and men are supplemented as follows:a) promoting the integrated approach to the principle of equal opportunities between women and men for the purpose of preventing and combating domestic violence;b) evaluating the stage of application and compliance with legislation in the field of prevention and combating of domestic violence at local level;c) elaborating recommendations for local public administration authorities with a view to implementing specific policies and programs for the prevention and combating of domestic violence at local level;d) promoting proposals for the local strategy for implementing measures for the prevention and combating of domestic violence at local level, with emphasis on the needs of the community;e) coordinating and streamlining inter-institutional mechanisms in the field of prevention and combating of domestic violence, including the collection and centralization of data in the field of domestic violence at county level;f) analyzing cases of domestic violence with a high degree of risk and formulating recommendations regarding their resolution. +

## Capitolul II

Institutions with attributions in the prevention and combating of domestic violence +

### Article 8

(1) Ministries and other central specialized bodies of public administration, through their territorial structures, shall designate personnel with responsibilities in the field of prevention and combating of domestic violence.(2) Ministries and other central specialized bodies of public administration, local public administration authorities, non-governmental organizations and other representatives of civil society shall carry out, separately or, as the case may be, in cooperation, activities for the prevention and combating of domestic violence.(3) The National Agency for Equal Opportunities between Women and Men, based on its role as a central structure with the role of methodological coordination of decentralized specialized structures that can ensure intervention and provision of social services for victims of domestic violence, in partnership with other central public authorities that can intervene or can provide social services for different categories of victims of domestic violence, including children witnessing domestic violence, and together with local public administration authorities with responsibilities regarding ensuring the provision of social services shall monitor local inter-institutional cooperation for the prevention and combating of domestic violence.(4) The Ministry of Labour and Social Protection is the central public authority that develops social assistance policy and promotes the rights of victims of domestic violence.(5) The National Agency for Equal Opportunities between Women and Men, a specialized body of the central public administration, with legal personality, under the subordination of the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection, exercises the functions of strategy, regulation, representation and state authority in the field of domestic violence, with responsibilities in the development, coordination, application of Government strategies and policies in the field of domestic violence.(6) The National Agency for Equal Opportunities between Women and Men manages the national database on victims of domestic violence and aggressors who benefit from the provision of social services existing at the level of each county. In order to ensure a unitary character of the collection, centralization, elaboration and dissemination of relevant statistical indicators regarding victims of domestic violence and aggressors, the National Agency for Equal Opportunities between Women and Men may extend the scope of data collected, through inter-institutional collaboration with ministries and institutions with responsibilities in the field.(7) The National Agency for Equal Opportunities between Women and Men ensures coordination together with other ministries in relation to the integration of a perspective of equality between women and men in all policies, programmes and research in the field of artificial intelligence, in order to avoid the potential risks of technology that perpetuates sexism, gender stereotypes and cyber violence.(8) The Ministry of Labour and Social Protection, through its specialized structures at central and territorial level, develops and applies special measures for labour market integration of victims of domestic violence.(9) Ministries and other central specialized bodies of public administration have the responsibility of developing a national level strategy for the prevention and combating of the phenomenon of domestic violence, including an internal mechanism for coordination and monitoring of activities undertaken, which shall be approved by Government decision*), at the proposal of the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection, the Ministry of Internal Affairs and the Ministry of Public Works, Development and Administration.*) See Government Decision no. 365/2018 for the approval of the National Strategy on promoting equal opportunities and treatment between women and men and preventing and combating domestic violence for the period 2018-2021 and of the Operational Plan for the implementation of the National Strategy on promoting equal opportunities and treatment between women and men and preventing and combating domestic violence for the period 2018-2021, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 465 of 6 June 2018. +

### Article 9

(1) The Ministry of Labour and Social Protection, the Ministry of Internal Affairs, the Ministry of Education and Research, the Ministry of Health and the Ministry of Transport, Infrastructure and Communications, shall develop and disseminate documentary materials regarding the prevention, causes and consequences of domestic violence.(2) The Ministry of Health has the obligation to develop instructions to ensure that family doctors and specialist doctors from the public or private health system record in the patient's file suspicions of domestic violence.(3) The Ministry of Transport, Infrastructure and Communications, through the bodies under its coordination responsible for prevention, analysis, identification and response to incidents within cyber infrastructures, has the obligation to carry out the necessary steps to develop public awareness campaigns regarding cyber violence and to offer practical assistance to central and local authorities in the prevention and response to cyber violence. Together with the institutions provided for in art. 8, the Ministry of Transport, Infrastructure and Communications shall establish, promote and budget programmes intended for a wide range of users, for digital literacy. +

### Article 10

(1) The Ministry of Education and Research has the obligation to carry out the necessary steps to introduce at the level of the curriculum implemented in the classroom learning activities regarding: equal opportunities and treatment between women and men, mutual respect, non-violent resolution of conflicts in interpersonal relationships, gender-based violence and the right to personal integrity, as well as combating discriminatory stereotypes based on gender roles, adapted to the age and understanding of pupils. Judges, prosecutors, police representatives, non-governmental organizations, as well as public institutions carrying out activity in the field may carry out extracurricular activities in pre-university education, on topics related to domestic violence. (2) The Ministry of Education and Research shall establish the procedure applicable by county school inspectorates for the urgent transfer, on a temporary basis, of children who are victims or witnesses of domestic violence to the school unit recommended by the institutions that offer social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence.(3) The Ministry of Education and Research shall carry out, with the support of other ministries involved and in collaboration with non-governmental organizations with activity in the field, educational programmes for teaching staff, parents and children, in order to prevent domestic violence, harassment and sexual violence, including school-community-family partnerships. + Article 11The probation service shall carry out, according to legal competences, activities for social reintegration of persons sanctioned with non-custodial measures or penalties for offences which, according to this law, may be considered as domestic violence. + Article 12The authorities provided for in art. 8 shall ensure the training and continuous professional development of persons designated with responsibilities in the field of prevention and combating of domestic violence. +

### Article 13

(1) Local public administration authorities have the obligation to take the following specific measures:a) to include the issue of preventing and combating domestic violence in regional, county and local development strategies and programmes;b) to provide logistical, informational and material support to departments with responsibilities in preventing and combating domestic violence;c) to establish directly, in public partnership or, as the case may be, in public-private partnership, social services for preventing and combating domestic violence and to support their operation;d) to develop programmes for preventing and combating domestic violence;e) to support the access of family aggressors to psychological counselling, psychotherapy, psychiatric treatments, detoxification and alcohol treatment;f) to develop and implement projects in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence;g) to provide in their annual budget amounts for supporting social services and other social assistance measures for victims of domestic violence and for other measures aimed at preventing and combating domestic violence;h) to bear, from the local budget, in cases of domestic violence established following the registration of the victim by public social assistance services, the expenses for drawing up legal documents, as well as those necessary for obtaining forensic medical certificates for victims of domestic violence;i) to bear, from the local budget, in cases of domestic violence established following the registration of the victim by public social assistance services, the expenses for medical assistance of victims of domestic violence who do not have medical insurance;j) to collaborate in implementing a system for recording, reporting and managing cases of domestic violence.(2) Local public administration authorities shall designate specialised personnel to implement the system for recording, reporting and managing cases of domestic violence.(3) Mayors and local councils shall cooperate with religious organisations, non-governmental organisations, as well as with any other legal and natural persons involved in charitable actions, providing them with the necessary support in order to fulfil the obligations provided for in paragraphs (1) and (2).(4) At the level of counties and sectors of Bucharest Municipality, the intersectoral team in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence shall be established, under the county general directorates of social assistance and child protection/of the sectors of Bucharest Municipality, with an advisory role.(5) The intersectoral team shall include one representative each of the police, gendarmerie, public health directorate, the domestic violence department within the general directorate of social assistance and child protection, social services for preventing and combating domestic violence, as well as non-governmental organisations active in the field.(6) The intersectoral team may also include, but is not limited to, representatives of probation services, forensic medicine units, as well as other institutions with responsibilities in the field.(7) The intersectoral team shall propose measures to improve activity in the field, ensure cooperation between the institutions provided for in paragraphs (5) and (6) and evaluate annually the activity in the field.(8) The establishment and manner of organisation and operation thereof shall be approved by decision of the county council, respectively of the local councils of the sectors of Bucharest Municipality. + Article 14Persons designated by public authorities for handling cases of domestic violence shall have the following main responsibilities:a) monitoring cases of domestic violence in the sector or territorial unit served; collecting information on them; maintaining separate records; ensuring access to information at the request of judicial bodies and of the parties or their representatives;b) informing and supporting police officers who in the course of their specific activity encounter situations of domestic violence;c) identifying risk situations for the parties involved in the conflict and directing them to specialised services;d) collaborating with local child protection institutions and reporting cases, in accordance with legislation in force;e) handling the case together with the social worker. + Article 15Public authorities with responsibilities in the field may designate for handling cases of domestic violence the following categories of professionals, without however being limited to these: social workers, psychologists and legal counsellors or persons with social assistance responsibilities, employees of general directorates of social assistance and child protection, social assistance directorates, public social assistance services, community police officers, social workers, psychologists and legal counsellors, as well as personnel with social assistance responsibilities of non-governmental organisations or authorised social service providers with which one of the public authorities has concluded a service provision contract regarding this activity. +

## Chapter III

Social services for preventing and combating domestic violence +

### Article 16

(1) Social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence may be organised in residential regime, day regime or with continuous programme, with or without legal personality, of local or county interest.(2) Social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence organised in residential regime, intended for victims of domestic violence, with accommodation for a determined period, are:a) emergency reception centres;b) recovery centres;c) sheltered housing.(3) Social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence organised in day regime are:a) centres for the prevention and combating of domestic violence;b) centres for information services and public awareness;c) assistance centres intended for aggressors.(4) Social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence with continuous programme are:a) information and counselling services for victims of domestic violence of the emergency telephone line type - help-line;b) integrated emergency services intended for victims of sexual violence.(5) Social services intended for aggressors are organised in day regime and have as objective their rehabilitation and social reintegration, by ensuring education and counselling measures. Under the conditions of the law, the centres may monitor the treatment of addictions.(6) Specialised social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence are offered free of charge to victims.(7) The National Agency for Equal Opportunities between Women and Men elaborates and submits for approval the draft decision regarding the completion of Government Decision no. 867/2015 for the approval of the Nomenclature of social services, as well as of the framework regulations for the organisation and functioning of social services, as subsequently amended and completed*), in order to regulate the social services provided for in para. (4) letter a).*) See Government Decision no. 476/2019 for the amendment and completion of the Methodological Norms for the application of the provisions of Law no. 197/2012 regarding quality assurance in the field of social services, approved by Government Decision no. 118/2014, and of Government Decision no. 867/2015 for the approval of the Nomenclature of social services, as well as of the framework regulations for the organisation and functioning of social services, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 581 of 16 July 2019.(8) The National Agency for Equal Opportunities between Women and Men elaborates and submits for approval to the minister of labour and social protection the draft order regarding the approval of minimum quality standards for social services, organised as information and counselling services for victims of domestic violence of the emergency telephone line type - helpline.*)*) See Order of the minister of labour and social justice no. 28/2019 regarding the approval of minimum quality standards for the accreditation of social services intended for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 90 of 5 February 2019. +

### Article 17

(1) Social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence may be public, private or in public-private partnership.(2) Social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence may be established only by social service providers, accredited under the conditions of the law.(3) The establishment, organisation and functioning of social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence are approved by decisions of county councils or, as the case may be, of local councils, respectively of the sectors of the municipality of Bucharest and of the General Council of the Municipality of Bucharest.(4) The financing of social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, organised in the public system or, as the case may be, in public or public-private partnership, is ensured from local budgets, from financing from the state budget through programmes of national interest, as well as from various types of non-reimbursable or reimbursable financing or, as the case may be, from the state budget. For the purpose of financing social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, financial resources from donations, sponsorships and from other sources provided by law may be used.(5) In the case of social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, the use of amounts allocated from the state budget or, as the case may be, from local budgets is subject to the control of bodies authorised by law.(6) The institution that granted the financing or subsidy to social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, public, private and in public-private partnership, monitors the use of allocated funds.(7) The assistance or, as the case may be, accommodation of victims, respectively the assistance of aggressors in the social services provided for in art. 16 para. (2) and (3) is done on the basis of the conclusion of a contract for the provision of social services. For minors, the contract for the provision of social services is signed by the accompanying parent or, as the case may be, by the legal representative. +

### Article 18

(1) Local public administration authorities have the obligation to ensure the creation and functioning of social services provided for in art. 16, in an adequate geographical distribution, which ensures unrestricted access to social services and assistance to all victims subjected to the forms of violence provided for by this law.(2) Local public administration authorities have the obligation to ensure the creation and functioning of social services provided for in art. 16, at least at county level, depending on the identification and assessment of the needs of persons subjected to the forms of domestic violence provided for by law, from the area of the county, within 2 years from the entry into force of these legal provisions.**)**) Paragraph (2) of art. 18 was introduced by Law no. 174/2018 regarding the amendment and completion of Law no. 217/2003 for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 618 of 18 July 2018. +

### Article 19

(1) Emergency reception centres, hereinafter referred to as shelters, are social assistance units, with or without legal personality, of residential type, which provide protection, accommodation, care and counselling to victims of domestic violence.(2) Shelters provide free of charge, for a determined period, family assistance to both the victim and the minors in the care of the victim, protection against the aggressor, medical assistance and care, food, accommodation, psychological counselling and legal counselling, according to the organisation and functioning instructions elaborated by the authority.(3) The reception of victims in the shelter is done only in case of emergency or, as the case may be, with the approval of the centre's management, when the isolation of the victim from the aggressor is required as a protection measure. Persons who have committed the act of aggression are prohibited from accessing the premises of the shelter where the victims are located.(4) The location of shelters is secret to the general public.(5) The isolation of victims from aggressors is done with their consent or, as the case may be, of the legal representative.(6) All shelters must conclude a collaboration agreement with a hospital or with another health unit, which ensures medical and psychiatric care. The agreement is concluded by local councils, respectively by the councils of the sectors of the municipality of Bucharest or, as the case may be, by county councils, as well as by the management bodies of accredited private social service providers. +

### Article 20

(1) Recovery centres for victims of domestic violence are residential social assistance units, with or without legal personality, which provide accommodation, care, legal and psychological counselling, support for adaptation to an active life, professional integration of victims of domestic violence, as well as their rehabilitation and social reintegration.(2) Recovery centres for victims of domestic violence shall conclude agreements with the county employment authorities and those of the sectors of Bucharest Municipality with a view to providing support for work integration, readaptation and professional retraining of assisted persons.(3) The provisions of art. 19 para. (5) and (6) shall apply accordingly. +

### Article 21

(1) Protected housing units are social assistance units organised on a residential basis for a fixed term, with or without legal personality, which provide emergency accommodation, care, social assistance, legal and psychological counselling and guidance for victims of domestic violence.(2) The address of protected housing units is secret from the general public. +

### Article 22

(1) Assistance centres for aggressors are social assistance units operating on a day basis, with or without legal personality, which provide their rehabilitation and social reintegration, educational measures, as well as counselling and family mediation services.(2) Psychiatric, dealcoholisation and detoxification treatments provided through assistance centres for aggressors shall be ensured in hospitals or health units with which agreements have been concluded, under the conditions provided for in art. 19 para. (6).(3) Assistance centres for aggressors may provide beneficiaries with outpatient treatment in the case of various types of addictions, on the recommendation of a specialist doctor, under the conditions provided for in art. 19 para. (6).(4) Social reintegration activities for offenders convicted of domestic violence offences may include the use of social services provided within centres for aggressors. + Article 23Centres for the prevention and combating of domestic violence are social assistance units on a day basis, with or without legal personality, which provide social assistance, psychological and legal counselling, as well as information and guidance for victims of domestic violence. + Article 24Centres for public information and awareness services are social assistance units, with or without legal personality, which offer information and education services, social assistance and an emergency telephone service for information and counselling. + Article 25Emergency telephone line services are free social services which provide confidential counselling to callers in connection with all forms of violence provided for in art. 3 and 4. + Article 26Integrated emergency centres in case of sexual violence provide medical and medico-legal examination, post-traumatic assistance and counselling for victims of sexual violence. +

### Article 27

(1) Persons convicted of domestic violence offences are obliged to participate in special counselling and social reintegration programmes organised by the institutions responsible for the execution of the sentence on whose records they are.(2) Cases of domestic violence may be subject to mediation at the request of the parties, in accordance with the law.(3) In resolving divorce cases in which the fault of one of the spouses has been established with regard to the commission of acts of domestic violence, the competent courts may order, ex officio or at the express request made by the other spouse, that the aggressor take the measure of participating in special psychological counselling programmes organised by public or private specialist services.(4) The Ministry of Labour and Social Protection in collaboration with the Ministry of Health and the College of Psychologists of Romania, through consultation with the Ministry of Justice, shall draw up the methodology regarding the manner of participation in special psychological counselling programmes organised by public or private specialist services.*)*) See Order of the Minister of Labour and Social Justice no. 2,524/2018 for the approval of the Methodology regarding the manner of participation in special psychological counselling programmes, organised by public or private specialist services, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 74 of 30 January 2019. +

## Chapter IV

Provisional protection order + Section 1 Jurisdiction and substantive conditions for issuance +

### Article 28

(1) The provisional protection order shall be issued by police officers who, in the exercise of their duties, establish that there is an imminent risk that the life, physical integrity or freedom of a person may be endangered by an act of domestic violence, for the purpose of diminishing this risk.(2) Police officers shall establish the existence of the imminent risk provided for in para. (1) on the basis of the assessment of the factual situation resulting from:a) evidence obtained as a result of the verification of notifications concerning domestic violence, when the acts of domestic violence are not the subject of investigation in respect of the commission of offences;b) evidence gathered in accordance with the provisions of Law no. 135/2010 on the Code of Criminal Procedure, as amended and supplemented, when the acts of domestic violence are the subject of investigation in respect of the commission of acts falling within the scope of the provisions of art. 199 of Law no. 286/2009 on the Criminal Code, as amended and supplemented.(3) Police officers shall assess the factual situation on the basis of the risk assessment form and in accordance with the methodology for its use, established in accordance with the provisions of art. 37.(4) Where, as a result of the assessment of the factual situation, it is established that the conditions for issuing the provisional protection order, provided for in para. (1), are met, police officers shall issue the provisional protection order, in accordance with the provisions of art. 30 and in accordance with the procedure and model established in accordance with the provisions of art. 37.(5) Where, as a result of the assessment of the factual situation, it is established that the conditions for issuing the provisional protection order, provided for in para. (1), are not met, police officers shall have the obligation to inform persons who claim to be victims of domestic violence of the possibility of submitting an application for the issuance of a protection order in accordance with the provisions of art. 38 and to make available to them the application form provided for in art. 41.(6) The issuance of the provisional protection order shall not prevent the taking of a preventive measure in accordance with the provisions of Law no. 135/2010, as amended and supplemented. + Section 2 Verification of notifications concerning domestic violence +

### Article 29

(1) For the verification of reports regarding domestic violence, the establishment of the truth and the fair resolution of the report, police officers have the right to obtain evidence through the following means:a) observation through their own senses and recording of the observations in a written document or recording of the observations by technical means;b) consultation of databases to which they have access according to their service duties and recording of the observations in a written document;c) statements of persons involved in acts of domestic violence, of persons who witnessed the occurrence of acts of domestic violence and of other persons who can communicate information regarding persons involved in acts of domestic violence;d) video or audio recordings or photographs, regardless of their origin;e) written documents, including those in the nature of messages or posts in the electronic environment and/or mobile telephony.(2) For the verification of reports regarding domestic violence and for the purpose of obtaining evidence regarding the aspects that are the subject of the report, police officers have the right to enter the domicile or residence of any natural person, without their consent, as well as the headquarters of any legal person, without the consent of its legal representative, if the report expressly indicates that acts of domestic violence are taking place or have taken place in those spaces.(3) Police officers may use force and equipment, in an adequate and proportional manner, to enter the spaces provided for in para. (2).(4) The actions of police officers in the spaces provided for in para. (2), including those of entry, may be recorded with the audio-video or photo equipment available, without the consent of the persons, the recordings or photographs constituting evidence within the meaning of para. (1) lit. a) and following the regime of evidence provided for by Law no. 134/2010 on the Code of Civil Procedure, republished, as subsequently amended and supplemented, or by Law no. 135/2010, as subsequently amended and supplemented, as the case may be.(5) Police officers collect evidence according to the provisions of Law no. 135/2010, as subsequently amended and supplemented, when, during the verification of reports regarding domestic violence, they find that the conditions provided by law are met for the acts of domestic violence committed to be the subject of investigation regarding the commission of offences. + Section 3 Formal conditions for the issuance of the provisional protection order +

### Article 30

(1) The provisional protection order mandatorily includes mentions regarding:a) the date, time and place where it is issued;b) the surname, first name, capacity and police unit to which the police officer who issues it belongs;c) data to ensure the identification of the aggressor/aggressors against whom obligations or prohibitions are ordered through it;d) data to ensure the identification of the victim/victims or other persons in whose favour protection measures are ordered through it;e) description of the factual reasons that determined its ordering and indication of the evidence on the basis of which the factual situation was assessed;f) the legal basis for its issuance;g) the date and time at which the application of the protection measures ordered according to art. 31 begins, as well as the date and time at which they cease;h) the right to contest the provisional protection order, the time limit for exercising this right and the court where the contestation may be filed.(2) The protection order must be signed by the police officer who issues it. + Section 4 Protection measures that may be ordered through the provisional protection order, for the purpose of diminishing the established risk +

### Article 31

(1) Through the provisional protection order, for a period of 5 days, one or more protection measures are ordered, capable of contributing to the diminution of the imminent risk established, from among the following obligations or prohibitions:a) temporary eviction of the aggressor from the common dwelling, regardless of whether they are the holder of the property right;b) reintegration of the victim and, as the case may be, the children in the common dwelling;c) obligation of the aggressor to maintain a determined minimum distance from the victim, from the members of their family, as defined according to the provisions of art. 5, or from the residence, workplace or educational institution of the protected person;d) obligation of the aggressor to permanently wear an electronic surveillance device; (on 21-05-2021, the phrase: electronic surveillance system was replaced by Article 46, Title II of LAW no. 146 of 17 May 2021, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 515 of 18 May 2021) e) obligation of the aggressor to surrender to the police the weapons held.(2) The protection measures provided for in para. (1) lit. a) and b) are ordered together.(3) The protection measure provided for in para. (1) lit. d) is ordered if the following conditions are met:a) the measure provided for in para. (1) lit. c) has been ordered;b) when the obligation of the aggressor to maintain a minimum distance from the victim and, as the case may be, the members of their family has been ordered, the protected persons express their agreement to wear an electronic surveillance device that allows verification of compliance with the aggressor's obligation. (on 21-05-2021, the phrase: electronic surveillance system was replaced by Article 46, Title II of LAW no. 146 of 17 May 2021, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 515 of 18 May 2021) (4) The provisional protection order shall also include the mention that the violation of any of the measures ordered in para. (1) constitutes an offence, according to the provisions of art. 47 para. (2).(5) If the provisional protection order has taken the measure of temporary eviction of the aggressor, and they do not have accommodation ensured from another source, they shall be immediately ensured information and guidance, at their request, towards residential centres that offer accommodation for homeless persons or night shelters, managed by local public administration authorities, or any other adequate place. In the situation where the aggressor requests accommodation in a residential centre of the category mentioned above, they shall be immediately guided and taken to the residential centres managed by local public administration authorities or, as the case may be, to other adequate social centres by the mobile team provided for in art. 51.(6) In the case where the aggressor does not wish to benefit from the accommodation made available according to para. (5) and opts to live with a relative or any other person, they shall be requested to give a statement regarding the address where they will live and the person who will ensure their accommodation. In the case where the aggressor refuses to give the respective statement, this fact shall be recorded in the minutes drawn up according to the provisions of art. 33 para. (4).(7) The competent public authorities and institutions have the obligation that, ex officio or at the request of police units or any interested person, they implement urgent and specific protection measures regarding minors, persons with disabilities or persons with special needs, covered by the provisional protection order. + Section 5 Implementation of the provisional protection order +

### Article 32

(1) The obligations and prohibitions imposed on aggressors through provisional protection orders become mandatory immediately after their issuance, without notice and without the passage of any term.(2) The 5-day period provided for in Article 31 paragraph (1) is calculated in hours, that is, it has a duration of 120 hours, and begins to run from the moment the provisional protection order was issued.(3) Police officers may use force and equipment, in an adequate and proportional manner, for the implementation of protection measures ordered through provisional protection orders.(4) The protection measures ordered according to Article 31 paragraph (1) letter e) are executed on the spot by the police officers present at the place of issuance of the provisional protection orders, if the weapons are located in the spaces in which checks were made according to the provisions of Article 29 paragraph (2) or in the vicinity thereof.(5) In the event that it is not possible to execute the protection measures ordered according to the provisions of Article 31 paragraph (1) letter e) under the conditions of paragraph (4), the police officers who issue the provisional protection orders take the necessary measures for their execution as soon as possible. +

### Article 33

(1) The provisional protection order is communicated to the aggressor and the victim.(2) Communication is carried out by handing over, under signature, a copy or duplicate, as the case may be, of the provisional protection order, at the place of its issuance, immediately after issuance.(3) The provisional protection order is considered communicated to the aggressor also in the following situations:a) the aggressor refuses to receive a copy or refuses to sign for receipt;b) the aggressor leaves the place of issuance of the protection order, after being informed that it is necessary to wait for the communication of the result of the checks being carried out in connection with the notification regarding domestic violence.(4) The police officer who issues the provisional protection order prepares a report in which he records the situations provided for in paragraph (3).(5) The provisional protection order is considered communicated to the aggressor also in the situation where he was not present at the carrying out of checks in connection with the notification regarding domestic violence or at the issuance of the provisional protection order, but proof can be made, with witness statements, with video or audio recordings, regardless of their origin, or with documents, including those in the nature of messages or posts in the electronic or mobile telephony environment, that the fact that a provisional protection order was issued was communicated to the aggressor, as well as the content of the protection measures ordered through it.(6) In the cases provided for in paragraphs (3) and (5), the aggressor has the right, at any time during the validity period of the provisional protection order, to request and obtain, from the police unit of which the police officer who issued it is a member, a copy thereof. + Section 6 Confirmation and contestation of the provisional protection order +

### Article 34

(1) The provisional protection order is forwarded by the police unit of which the police officer who issued it is a member, for confirmation, to the prosecutor's office attached to the competent court in whose territorial area it was issued, within 24 hours from the date of issuance.(2) The provisional protection order is forwarded to the competent prosecutor's office according to the provisions of paragraph (1) accompanied by the risk assessment form and the means of evidence obtained according to the provisions of Article 29 or gathered according to the provisions of Law no. 135/2010, as subsequently amended and supplemented. The means of evidence gathered according to the provisions of Law no. 135/2010, as subsequently amended and supplemented, may be forwarded in a copy certified by the criminal investigation body.(3) The prosecutor from the competent prosecutor's office according to the provisions of paragraph (1) decides on the necessity of maintaining the protection measures ordered by the police body within 48 hours from the issuance of the provisional protection order.(4) The prosecutor confirms the necessity of maintaining the protection measures ordered by the police body through the provisional protection order, by applying a resolution of an administrative nature on the original copy thereof.(5) In the event that he finds that it is no longer necessary to maintain the protection measures ordered, the prosecutor may order, with reasons, the cessation of the protection measures, with mention of the moment from which they cease. The prosecutor communicates this immediately to the police unit that forwarded the provisional protection order, which takes measures for the immediate information of the persons who were the subject thereof.(6) Immediately after the confirmation provided for in paragraph (4), the prosecutor forwards the provisional protection order, accompanied by the documents that formed the basis for its issuance and confirmation, to the competent court in whose territorial area it was issued, accompanied by an application for the issuance of the protection order, prepared according to the provisions of Article 40 paragraph (3) letter a) and Article 41.(7) In the situation of forwarding the provisional protection order according to the provisions of paragraph (6), the initial duration for which it was ordered is extended, by operation of law, by the duration necessary to complete the judicial procedure for issuing the protection order, with the information of the aggressor about this fact. +

### Article 35

(1) The provisional protection order may be contested before the competent court according to the provisions of Article 34 paragraph (1), within 48 hours from communication.(2) The contestation is judged with the summoning of the parties. Their non-appearance does not prevent the judgment of the case.(3) The contestation is resolved urgently, but no later than the date on which the term for which the provisional protection order was issued expires, in the council chamber, with the summoning of the finding body.(4) The participation of the prosecutor is mandatory.(5) The decision by which the contestation is resolved is final. + Article 36The provisions of this chapter are supplemented accordingly with those regarding the protection order. + Section 7 Subsequent acts +

### Article 37

(1) The manner of management by police officers of cases of domestic violence is established by order of the minister of internal affairs and the minister of labour and social protection.*)*) See Order of the minister of internal affairs and the minister of labour and social justice no. 146/2.578/2018 regarding the manner of management of cases of domestic violence by police officers, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 1110 of 28 December 2018.(2) The order provided for in paragraph (1) includes:a) the procedure for the intervention of police officers in cases of domestic violence and for cooperation with the other institutions with responsibilities in the prevention and combating of domestic violence;b) the model of the risk assessment form and the methodology for its use;c) the procedure for issuance and the model of the provisional protection order;d) the procedure for enforcement of the provisional protection order.(3) In establishing, according to paragraph (1), the risk assessment form and the methodology for its use, at least the following are taken into account:a) the determination of risk assessment criteria that may be relevant for domestic violence, among which the following criteria are mandatorily included: the form of domestic violence exercised, the repetitive nature of acts of domestic violence, the vulnerability of the person/persons against whom the domestic violence is exercised, the context of the occurrence of acts of domestic violence and the conduct of the person/persons who exercise acts of domestic violence;b) the presentation of the manner of using the risk assessment criteria and of interpreting the results obtained by applying the respective criteria. +

## Chapter V

The protection order +

### Article 38

(1) A person whose life, physical or mental integrity or freedom is endangered by an act of violence from a family member may request the court to issue, for the purpose of removing the state of danger, a protection order by which one or more of the following measures - obligations or prohibitions - shall be ordered on a provisional basis:a) temporary eviction of the aggressor from the family dwelling, regardless of whether the aggressor holds the property right;b) reinstatement of the victim and, as the case may be, the children, in the family dwelling;c) limitation of the aggressor's right of use to only part of the common dwelling when it can be partitioned in such a way that the aggressor does not come into contact with the victim;d) accommodation/placement of the victim, with his/her consent, and, as the case may be, the children, in an assistance centre among those provided for in art. 19;e) obligation of the aggressor to maintain a determined minimum distance from the victim, from the members of his/her family, as defined according to the provisions of art. 5, or from the residence, workplace or educational institution of the protected person;f) prohibition for the aggressor to travel to certain localities or determined areas that the protected person frequents or visits periodically;g) obligation of the aggressor to permanently wear an electronic surveillance device; (on 21-05-2021, the phrase: electronic surveillance system was replaced by Article 46, Title II of LAW no. 146 of 17 May 2021, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 515 of 18 May 2021) h) prohibition of any contact, including by telephone, by correspondence or in any other way, with the victim;i) obligation of the aggressor to surrender to the police the weapons held;j) entrusting of minor children or establishment of their residence;k) prohibition for the aggressor to collect the state allowance for children and authorisation of its collection by the parent/person to whom the child has been entrusted for upbringing and education or at whose residence the child has been established. The prohibition shall be communicated immediately to the county agency for payments and social inspection or of the municipality of Bucharest, as the case may be. The allowance shall be collected by the parent/person who is the beneficiary of the protection measure for the duration of the validity of the protection order and as long as the child has been entrusted to him/her or in the situation where the child has his/her residence established at his/her place. (on 13-05-2023, Paragraph (1) of Article 38, Chapter V was supplemented by Article I of LAW no. 114 of 9 May 2023, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 402 of 10 May 2023) (2) The measure provided for in para. (1) letter g) shall be ordered if the following conditions are met:a) one of the measures provided for in para. (1) letters e) or f) has been ordered;b) when the obligation of the aggressor to maintain a minimum distance from the victim and, as the case may be, the members of his/her family has been ordered, the protected persons express their consent to wear an electronic surveillance device that allows verification of compliance with the aggressor's obligation. (on 21-05-2021, the phrase: electronic surveillance system was replaced by Article 46, Title II of LAW no. 146 of 17 May 2021, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 515 of 18 May 2021) (3) By the same judgment, the court may also order that the aggressor bear the rent and/or maintenance for the temporary dwelling where the victim, minor children or other family members live or are to live due to the impossibility of remaining in the family dwelling.(4) In addition to any of the measures ordered according to para. (1), the court may order the aggressor to undergo psychological counselling, psychotherapy and may recommend voluntary hospitalisation or, as the case may be, may request involuntary hospitalisation, under the conditions of the Law on mental health and the protection of persons with mental disorders no. 487/2002, republished. In the event that the aggressor is a consumer of psychoactive substances, the court may order, with his/her consent, his/her integration into a programme of assistance for drug consumers, according to art. 22 of Law no. 143/2000 on the prevention and combating of illicit drug trafficking and consumption, republished, with subsequent amendments and supplements.(5) By the same judgment, the court may order the taking of a measure to control compliance with the protection order and to prevent its violation, such as:a) obligation of the aggressor to appear periodically, at a time interval established by the court according to the circumstances, at the police station competent to supervise compliance with the protection order;b) obligation of the aggressor to provide information to the police body regarding the new dwelling, in the event that the order has provided for his/her eviction from the family dwelling;c) periodic and/or spontaneous checks regarding the place where the aggressor is located.(6) The operative part of the judgment shall also include the mention that violation of any of the measures ordered by the protection order constitutes an offence, according to art. 47 para. (1). +

### Article 39

(1) The duration of the measures ordered by the protection order shall be established by the judge, without being able to exceed 12 months from the date of issuance of the order.(2) If the judgment does not contain any mention regarding the duration of the measures ordered, they shall produce effects for a period of 12 months from the date of issuance of the order. (on 23-07-2023, Article 39, Chapter V was amended by Point 1., THE SOLE ARTICLE of LAW no. 240 of 19 July 2023, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 669 of 20 July 2023) +

### Article 40

(1) The application for the issuance of the protection order falls within the jurisdiction of the court of first instance in the territorial area where the victim has his/her domicile or residence.(2) The application for the issuance of the order may be filed by the victim personally or through a legal representative.(3) The application may be filed on behalf of the victim also by:a) the prosecutor;b) the representative of the competent authority or structure, at the level of the administrative-territorial unit, with responsibilities in the matter of protection of victims of domestic violence;c) the representative of any of the providers of social services in the field of prevention and combating of domestic violence, accredited according to the law, with the victim's consent. +

### Article 41

(1) The application regarding the issuance of the protection order shall be drawn up according to the application form provided in the annex which forms an integral part of this law.(2) The application is exempt from the stamp duty. +

### Article 42

(1) Applications for the issuance of a protection order shall be heard in chambers, with the mandatory participation of the prosecutor.(2) Legal assistance for the person requesting the protection order is mandatory.(3) Legal assistance for the person against whom the protection order is requested is mandatory.(4) The trial shall be conducted urgently and as a priority. The provisions of Articles 200 and 201 of Law No. 134/2010, republished, as subsequently amended and supplemented, regarding the verification of the application and its regularisation, respectively the setting of the first hearing date, are not applicable. The summoning of the parties shall be carried out according to the rules regarding summoning in urgent cases.(5) In cases of exceptional urgency, the court may issue the protection order even without summoning the parties, even on the same day, ruling on the basis of the application and the documents submitted, without the parties' conclusions.(6) In resolving the application, evidence whose administration requires a lengthy period of time is not admissible.(7) The prosecutor has the obligation to inform the person requesting the protection order about the legal provisions regarding the protection of victims of crime.(8) The pronouncement may be postponed by a maximum of 24 hours, and the reasoning of the order shall be made within a maximum of 48 hours from the pronouncement.(9) The resolution of applications may not exceed a period of 72 hours from the filing of the application, except in the case where a provisional protection order has been previously issued, the initial duration for which it is ordered being extended, by operation of law, by the duration necessary to complete the judicial procedure for issuing the protection order, with the aggressor being informed of this fact. +

### Article 43

In the cases provided for in Article 40 paragraph (3) and Article 34 paragraph (6), the victim may renounce, in accordance with the provisions of Article 406 of Law No. 134/2010, republished, as subsequently amended and supplemented, the hearing of the application regarding the protection order. +

### Article 44

(1) The protection order is enforceable.(2) The execution of the decision shall be carried out without summons or without the passage of any period.(3) Compliance with the protection order is also mandatory for the person protected through it. +

### Article 45

(1) The decision by which the application for the issuance of a protection order is resolved is subject only to appeal, within 3 days from the pronouncement if it was given with the summoning of the parties, and from the communication, if it was given without summoning them.(2) The court of appeal may suspend the execution until the hearing of the appeal, but only with the payment of a security deposit whose amount shall be established by it.(3) The appeal shall be heard with the summoning of the parties. The provisions of Article 42 paragraphs (1)-(4), (6) and (8) are applicable accordingly. (on 23-07-2023, Paragraph (3), Article 45, Chapter V was amended by Point 2., THE SOLE ARTICLE of LAW No. 240 of 19 July 2023, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE No. 669 of 20 July 2023 ) (4) The participation of the prosecutor is mandatory. +

### Article 46

(1) A copy of the operative part of the decision by which the application for the issuance of a protection order was granted shall be communicated, within a maximum of 5 hours from the moment of pronouncement of the decision, to the structures of the Romanian Police within whose territorial jurisdiction the residence of the victim and/or the aggressor is located, as well as to the Directorate for Records of Persons and Database Administration.(2) The protection order by which any of the measures provided for in Article 38 paragraph (1) is ordered shall be put into execution immediately, by or, as the case may be, under the supervision of the police.(3) For the execution of the protection order, the police officer may enter the family dwelling and any annex thereof, with the consent of the protected person or, in the absence thereof, of another family member.(4) The police authorities have the duty to supervise the manner in which the court decision by which the protection order was issued is complied with and to notify the criminal investigation body in case of evasion from execution.(5) Together with the communication of the protection order by which the measure of temporary eviction of the aggressor was taken, the police authority has the obligation to inform the aggressor about the right to have the possibility to request accommodation in residential centres for homeless persons or night shelters, which operate in accordance with the provisions of Law No. 292/2011, as subsequently amended and supplemented, or in other suitable places. The access of aggressors to centres for homeless persons and night shelters is carried out on the basis of the finding that the aggressor falls within the definition provided for in Article 6 letter z) of Law No. 292/2011, as subsequently amended and supplemented, with the exception of situations in which the aggressor represents a risk to the safety of the beneficiaries.(6) In exercising the duties of supervising compliance with the protection order, the police authorities shall implement measures to prevent non-compliance with the decision, which may consist of: conducting unannounced visits to the residence of the injured person, making control telephone calls to the victim/aggressor, requesting information from neighbours, colleagues from the victim's/aggressor's workplace, from the educational institution attended by the victim/aggressor or from other persons who could provide relevant information, as well as any other methods specific to police activity.(7) In the event that evasion from the execution of the protection order is found, the criminal investigation body shall be notified.(8) In the event that the person protected by the protection order violates its provisions, they shall be obliged to cover the expenses generated by the issuance and execution of the order.(9) The method of calculating the expenses generated by the issuance and execution of protection orders to which the protected person may be obliged shall be established by joint order of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Internal Affairs. +

(1) The violation by the person against whom a protection order has been issued of any of the measures provided for in Article 38 paragraphs (1), (4) and (5) letters a) and b) and ordered by the protection order constitutes a criminal offence and is punishable by imprisonment from 6 months to 5 years.(2) The violation by the person against whom a provisional protection order has been issued of any of the measures provided for in Article 31 paragraph (1) and ordered by the provisional protection order constitutes a criminal offence and is punishable by imprisonment from 6 months to 5 years. + Article 48Upon expiry of the duration of the protection measures, the protected person may request a new protection order, if there are indications that, in the absence of protection measures, their life, physical or mental integrity or freedom would be endangered. +

## Chapter VI

Emergency intervention +

### Article 51

(1) Emergency intervention is carried out from the perspective of providing social services through a mobile team formed of representatives of the Public Social Assistance Service, hereinafter referred to as SPAS.(2) The mobile team has the role of verifying reports, initial assessment and carrying out the necessary steps to overcome the immediate risk, consisting of: transport to the nearest health unit in situations where the victim requires medical care, notification of criminal investigation bodies, notification of competent bodies for issuing a provisional protection order, referral to the General Directorate of Social Assistance and Child Protection, hereinafter referred to as DGASPC, or, as the case may be, to SPAS, with a view to accommodation in residential centres appropriate to the needs and application of case management for victims and aggressors.(3) The reporting of urgent situations of domestic violence for which support from social services is necessary is carried out through the telephone lines of public institutions empowered to intervene in cases of domestic violence, including emergency telephone lines.(4) Staff within SPAS carry out the assessment of the degree of risk, from the perspective of providing social services, based on a specific instrument, which is approved, together with the procedure for emergency intervention in cases of domestic violence, by order issued by the minister of labour and social protection.*) *) See Order of the minister of labour and social justice no. 2,525/2018 on the approval of the Procedure for emergency intervention in cases of domestic violence, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 95 of 6 February 2019. +

### Article 52

(1) For the verification of reports regarding domestic violence, representatives of SPAS have the right of access to the headquarters or work points of legal persons, as well as to the domicile of natural persons.(2) Case management is ensured by the domestic violence department within DGASPC. ## Capitolul I

General provisions +

### Articolul 1

(1) The protection and support of the family, the development and consolidation of family solidarity, based on friendship, affection and moral and material mutual assistance of family members, constitute an objective of national interest.(2) The prevention and combating of domestic violence are part of the integrated policy of protection and support of the family and represent an important public health issue.(3) The Romanian state, through the competent authorities, elaborates and implements policies and programmes intended for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, as well as the protection of victims of domestic violence. + Articolul 2The protection and promotion of the rights of victims of domestic violence is carried out in accordance with the following principles:a) the principle of legality;b) the principle of respect for human dignity;c) the principle of prevention of the commission of acts of domestic violence;d) the principle of celerity;e) the principle of partnership;f) the principle of equal opportunities and treatment between women and men;g) the principle of protection of the life and safety of the victim;h) the principle of respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms;i) the principle of integrated approach. +

### Articolul 3

For the purposes of this law, domestic violence means any intentional inaction or action of physical, sexual, psychological, economic, social, spiritual or cyber violence, which occurs in the family or domestic environment or between spouses or former spouses, as well as between current or former partners, regardless of whether the aggressor resides or has resided together with the victim. +

### Article 4

(1) Domestic violence manifests itself in the following forms:a) verbal violence - addressing through offensive, brutal language, such as the use of insults, threats, degrading or humiliating words and expressions;b) psychological violence - imposing one's will or personal control, causing states of tension and psychological suffering in any way and by any means, through verbal threat or in any other manner, blackmail, demonstrative violence against objects and animals, ostentatious display of weapons, neglect, control of personal life, acts of jealousy, constraints of any kind, unlawful stalking, surveillance of the dwelling, workplace or other places frequented by the victim, making telephone calls or other types of communications by means of remote transmission, which by their frequency, content or the moment they are issued create fear, as well as other actions with similar effect;c) physical violence - bodily harm or harm to health through hitting, pushing, slamming, hair pulling, pricking, cutting, burning, strangling, biting, in any form and of any intensity, including disguised as being the result of accidents, through poisoning, intoxication, as well as other actions with similar effect, subjection to exhausting physical efforts or to activities with a high degree of risk to life or health and bodily integrity, other than those under point e);d) sexual violence - sexual assault, imposition of degrading acts, harassment, intimidation, manipulation, brutality with a view to maintaining forced sexual relations, marital rape;e) economic violence - prohibition of professional activity, deprivation of economic means, including deprivation of primary means of subsistence, such as food, medicines, basic necessities, the action of intentional misappropriation of the person's goods, prohibition of the right to possess, use and dispose of common goods, inequitable control over common goods and resources, refusal to support the family, imposition of heavy and harmful labour to the detriment of health, including on a minor family member, as well as other actions with similar effect;f) social violence - imposing the isolation of the person from family, community and friends, prohibition of attending educational institution or workplace, prohibition/limitation of professional achievement, imposition of isolation, including in the common dwelling, deprivation of access to living space, dispossession of identity documents, intentional deprivation of access to information, as well as other actions with similar effect;g) spiritual violence - underestimation or diminution of the importance of satisfying moral-spiritual needs through prohibition, limitation, ridicule, penalisation of family members' aspirations, of access to cultural, ethnic, linguistic or religious values, prohibition of the right to speak in the mother tongue and to teach children to speak in the mother tongue, imposition of adherence to unacceptable spiritual and religious beliefs and practices, as well as other actions with similar effect or with similar repercussions;h) cyber violence - online harassment, online hate speech messages based on gender, online stalking, online threats, non-consensual publication of intimate information and graphic content, unlawful access to interception of communications and private data and any other form of abusive use of information technology and communications through computers, smart mobile phones or other similar devices that use telecommunications or can connect to the internet and can transmit and use social or e-mail platforms, with the purpose of shaming, humiliating, frightening, threatening, silencing the victim.(2) In no form and under no circumstances may custom, culture, religion, tradition and honour be considered as justification for any type of acts of violence defined in this law. +

### Article 5

(1) For the purposes of this law, family member means:a) ascendants and descendants, brothers and sisters, spouses and their children, as well as persons who have become relatives through adoption, according to the law;b) the husband/wife and/or former husband/former wife; the brothers, parents and children from other relationships of the husband/wife or of the former husband/former wife;c) persons who have established relationships similar to those between spouses or between parents and children, current or former partners, regardless of whether they have lived or not with the aggressor, the ascendants and descendants of the partner, as well as their brothers and sisters;d) the guardian or other person who exercises in fact or in law the rights towards the child;e) the legal representative or other person who cares for the person with mental illness, intellectual disability or physical handicap, with the exception of those who fulfil these duties in the exercise of professional tasks.(2) For the purposes of this law, victim means the natural person who is subjected to one or more forms of violence provided for in Article 4, including children who witness these forms of violence. + Article 6The victim of domestic violence has the right:a) to respect for their personality, dignity and private life;b) to information regarding the exercise of their rights;c) to special protection, appropriate to their situation and needs;d) to counselling, rehabilitation, social reintegration services, as well as to free medical assistance, under the conditions of this law;e) to free legal counselling and assistance, under the conditions of the law. +

### Articolul 7

(1) Central and local public administration authorities have the obligation to take the necessary measures for the prevention of domestic violence, for the prevention of situations of repeated violation of the fundamental rights of victims of domestic violence, including by providing information and education programs on the ways in which cases of violence can be prevented, avoided, recognized and reported.(2) Central and local public administration authorities have the obligation to ensure the exercise of the right to information of victims of domestic violence, according to their respective competences, as the case may be, with regard to:a) institutions and non-governmental organizations that provide psychological counseling or any other forms of assistance and protection of the victim, depending on the victim's needs;b) the criminal investigation body to which they can file a complaint;c) the right to legal assistance and the institution where they can apply for the exercise of this right;d) the conditions and procedure for granting free legal assistance;e) the procedural rights of the injured person, the injured party and the civil party;f) the conditions and procedure for granting financial compensation by the state, according to the law;g) the measures that can be ordered by the provisional protection order and, as the case may be, by the protection order, the necessary steps for their issuance and the judicial procedure.(3) Central and local public administration authorities competent in the prevention and combating of domestic violence have the obligation to collect relevant statistical data, disaggregated and at regular intervals regarding cases of domestic violence. violence.(5) Central and local public administration authorities ensure confidentiality and respect for anonymity in the process of disseminating statistical data and the results of national analyses strategy for implementing measures for the prevention and combating of domestic violence at local level, with emphasis on the needs of the community;e) coordinating and streamlining ### Article 17

For the purpose of financing social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, financial resources from donations, sponsorships and from other sources provided by law may be used.(5) In the case of social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, the use of amounts allocated from the state budget or, as the case may be, from local budgets is subject to the control of bodies authorised by law.(6) The institution that granted the financing or subsidy to social services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, public, private and in public-private partnership, monitors the use of allocated funds.(7) The assistance or, as the case may be, accommodation of victims, respectively the assistance of aggressors in the social services provided for in art. 16 para. (2) and (3) is done on the basis of the conclusion of a contract for the provision of social services. For minors, the contract for the provision of social services is signed by the accompanying parent or, as the case may be, by the legal representative. +

### Article 18

(1) Local public administration authorities have the obligation to ensure the creation and functioning of the social services provided for in art. 16, in an adequate geographical distribution, which ensures unrestricted access to social services and assistance to all victims subjected to the forms of violence provided for by this law.(2) Local public administration authorities have the obligation to ensure the creation and functioning of the social services provided for in art. 16, at least at county level, depending on the identification and assessment of the needs of persons subjected to the forms of domestic violence provided for by law, from the area of the county, within 2 years from the entry into force of these legal provisions.**)**) Paragraph (2) of art. 18 was introduced by Law no. 174/2018 regarding the amendment and completion of Law no. 217/2003 for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 618 of 18 July 2018. +

### Article 19

(1) Emergency reception centres, hereinafter referred to as shelters, are social assistance units, with or without legal personality, of residential type, which provide protection, accommodation, care and counselling to victims of domestic violence.(2) Shelters provide free of charge, for a determined period, family assistance to both the victim and the minors in the care of the victim, protection against the aggressor, medical assistance and care, food, accommodation, psychological counselling and legal counselling, according to the organisation and functioning instructions elaborated by the authority.(3) The reception of victims in the shelter is done only in case of emergency or, as the case may be, with the approval of the centre's management, when the isolation of the victim from the aggressor is required as a protection measure. Persons who have committed the act of aggression are prohibited from accessing the premises of the shelter where the victims are located.(4) The location of shelters is secret to the general public.(5) The isolation of victims from aggressors is done with their consent or, as the case may be, of the legal representative.(6) All shelters must conclude a collaboration agreement with a hospital or with another health unit, which ensures medical and psychiatric care. The agreement is concluded by local councils, respectively by the councils of the sectors of the municipality of Bucharest or, as the case may be, by county councils, as well as by the management bodies of accredited private social service providers. +

### Article 20

(1) Recovery centres for victims of domestic violence are residential social assistance units, with or without legal personality, which provide accommodation, care, legal and psychological counselling, support for adaptation to an active life, professional integration of victims of domestic violence, as well as their rehabilitation and social reintegration.(2) Recovery centres for victims of domestic violence shall conclude agreements with the county employment authorities and those of the sectors of Bucharest Municipality with a view to providing support for work integration, readaptation and professional retraining of assisted persons.(3) The provisions of art. 19 para. (5) and (6) shall apply accordingly. +

### Article 21

(1) Sheltered housing units are social assistance units organised on a residential basis for a fixed period, with or without legal personality, which provide emergency accommodation, care, social assistance, legal and psychological counselling and guidance for victims of domestic violence.(2) The address of sheltered housing units is secret from the general public. +

### Article 22

(1) Assistance centres for aggressors are social assistance units operating on a day basis, with or without legal personality, which provide their rehabilitation and social reintegration, educational measures, as well as counselling and family mediation services.(2) Psychiatric, dealcoholisation and detoxification treatments provided through assistance centres for aggressors shall be provided in hospitals or health units with which agreements have been concluded, under the conditions provided for in art. 19 para. (6).(3) Assistance centres for aggressors may provide beneficiaries with outpatient treatment in the case of various types of addictions, on the recommendation of a specialist doctor, under the conditions provided for in art. 19 para. (6).(4) Social reintegration activities for offenders convicted of domestic violence offences may include the use of social services provided within centres for aggressors. + Article 23Centres for the prevention and combating of domestic violence are day-based social assistance units, with or without legal personality, which provide social assistance, psychological and legal counselling, as well as information and guidance for victims of domestic violence. + Article 24Centres for public information and awareness services are social assistance units, with or without legal personality, which offer information and education services, social assistance and an emergency telephone service for information and counselling. + Article 25Emergency telephone line services are free social services which provide counselling to callers, confidentially, in connection with all forms of violence provided for in art. 3 and 4. + Article 26Integrated emergency centres in cases of sexual violence provide medical and medico-legal examination, post-traumatic assistance and counselling for victims of sexual violence. +

### Article 27

(1) Persons convicted of domestic violence offences are obliged to participate in special counselling and social reintegration programmes organised by the institutions responsible for the execution of the sentence on whose records they are.(2) Cases of domestic violence may be subject to mediation at the request of the parties, in accordance with the law.(3) In resolving divorce cases in which the fault of one of the spouses has been established with regard to the commission of acts of domestic violence, the competent courts may order, ex officio or at the express request made by the other spouse, that the aggressor be required to participate in special psychological counselling programmes organised by public or private specialist services.(4) The Ministry of Labour and Social Protection in collaboration with the Ministry of Health and the College of Psychologists of Romania, through consultation with the Ministry of Justice, shall draw up the methodology regarding the manner of participation in special psychological counselling programmes organised by public or private specialist services.*)*) See Order of the Minister of Labour and Social Justice no. 2,524/2018 for the approval of the Methodology regarding the manner of participation in special psychological counselling programmes, organised by public or private specialist services, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 74 of 30 January 2019. +

## Chapter IV

Provisional protection order + Section 1 Jurisdiction and substantive conditions for issuance +

### Article 28

(1) The provisional protection order shall be issued by police officers who, in the exercise of their duties, establish that there is an imminent risk that the life, physical integrity or freedom of a person may be endangered by an act of domestic violence, for the purpose of diminishing this risk.(2) Police officers shall establish the existence of the imminent risk provided for in para. (1) on the basis of an assessment of the factual situation resulting from:a) evidence obtained as a result of the verification of reports concerning domestic violence, when the acts of domestic violence are not the subject of investigation in respect of the commission of offences;b) evidence gathered in accordance with the provisions of Law no. 135/2010 on the Code of Criminal Procedure, as amended and supplemented, when the acts of domestic violence are the subject of investigation in respect of the commission of acts falling within the scope of the provisions of art. 199 of Law no. 286/2009 on the Criminal Code, as amended and supplemented.(3) Police officers shall assess the factual situation on the basis of the risk assessment form and in accordance with the methodology for its use, established in accordance with the provisions of art. 37.(4) Where, as a result of the assessment of the factual situation, it is established that the conditions for issuing the provisional protection order, provided for in para. (1), are met, police officers shall issue the provisional protection order, in accordance with the provisions of art. 30 and in accordance with the procedure and model established in accordance with the provisions of art. 37.(5) Where, as a result of the assessment of the factual situation, it is established that the conditions for issuing the provisional protection order, provided for in para. (1), are not met, police officers shall have the obligation to inform persons who claim to be victims of domestic violence of the possibility of submitting an application for the issuance of a protection order in accordance with the provisions of art. 38 and to make available to them the application form provided for in art. 41.(6) The issuance of the provisional protection order shall not prevent the taking of a preventive measure in accordance with the provisions of Law no. 135/2010, as amended and supplemented. + Section 2 Verification of reports concerning domestic violence +

### Article 29

(1) For the verification of reports regarding domestic violence, establishing the truth and the fair resolution of the report, police officers have the right to obtain evidence through the following means:a) observation through their own senses and recording of the observations in a written document or recording of the observations by technical means;b) consultation of databases to which they have access according to their service duties and recording of the findings in a written document;c) statements of persons involved in acts of domestic violence, of persons who witnessed the occurrence of acts of domestic violence and of other persons who can communicate information regarding persons involved in acts of domestic violence;d) video or audio recordings or photographs, regardless of their origin;e) written documents, including those in the nature of messages or posts in the electronic environment and/or mobile telephony.(2) For the verification of reports regarding domestic violence and for the purpose of obtaining evidence regarding the aspects that are the subject of the report, police officers have the right to enter the domicile or residence of any natural person, without their consent, as well as the headquarters of any legal person, without the consent of its legal representative, if the report expressly indicates that acts of domestic violence are taking place or have taken place in those spaces.(3) Police officers may use force and equipment, in an adequate and proportional manner, to enter the spaces provided for in para. (2).(4) The actions of police officers in the spaces provided for in para. (2), including those of entry, may be recorded with audio-video or photo equipment, without the consent of the persons, the recordings or photographs constituting evidence within the meaning of para. (1) lit. a) and following the regime of evidence provided by Law no. 134/2010 on the Code of Civil Procedure, republished, with subsequent amendments and completions, or by Law no. 135/2010, with subsequent amendments and completions, as the case may be.(5) Police officers collect evidence according to the provisions of Law no. 135/2010, with subsequent amendments and completions, when, during the verification of reports regarding domestic violence, they find that the conditions provided by law are met for the acts of domestic violence committed to be the subject of investigation regarding the commission of offences. + Section 3 Formal conditions for issuing the provisional protection order +

### Article 30

(1) The provisional protection order shall mandatorily include mentions regarding:a) the date, time and place where it is issued;b) the surname, first name, capacity and police unit to which the police officer issuing it belongs;c) data ensuring the identification of the aggressor/aggressors against whom obligations or prohibitions are ordered through it;d) data ensuring the identification of the victim/victims or other persons in whose favour protection measures are ordered through it;e) description of the factual reasons that determined its issuance and indication of the evidence on the basis of which the factual situation was assessed;f) the legal basis for issuing it;g) the date and time at which the application of the protection measures ordered according to art. 31 begins, as well as the date and time at which they cease;h) the right to contest the provisional protection order, the time limit for exercising this right and the court where the contestation may be filed.(2) The protection order must be signed by the police officer who issues it. + Section 4 Protection measures that may be ordered through the provisional protection order, for the purpose of diminishing the established risk +

### Article 31

(1) Through the provisional protection order, one or more protection measures are ordered for a period of 5 days, capable of contributing to the diminution of the imminent risk established, from among the following obligations or prohibitions:a) temporary evacuation of the aggressor from the common dwelling, regardless of whether they are the holder of the property right;b) reintegration of the victim and, as the case may be, the children in the common dwelling;c) obliging the aggressor to maintain a determined minimum distance from the victim, from the members of their family, as defined according to the provisions of art. 5, or from the residence, workplace or educational institution of the protected person;d) obliging the aggressor to permanently wear an electronic surveillance device; (on 21-05-2021, the phrase: electronic surveillance system was replaced by Article 46, Title II of LAW no. 146 of 17 May 2021, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 515 of 18 May 2021) e) obliging the aggressor to surrender to the police the weapons held.(2) The protection measures provided for in para. (1) lit. a) and b) are ordered together.(3) The protection measure provided for in para. (1) lit. d) is ordered if the following conditions are met:a) the measure provided for in para. (1) lit. c) has been ordered;b) when the obligation of the aggressor to maintain a minimum distance from the victim and, as the case may be, the members of their family has been ordered, the protected persons express their consent to wear an electronic surveillance device that allows verification of compliance with the aggressor's obligation. (on 21-05-2021, the phrase: electronic surveillance system was replaced by Article 46, Title II of LAW no. 146 of 17 May 2021, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 515 of 18 May 2021) (4) The provisional protection order shall also include the mention that violation of any of the measures ordered in para. (1) constitutes an offence, according to the provisions of art. 47 para. (2).(5) If the provisional protection order has taken the measure of temporary evacuation of the aggressor, and they do not have accommodation ensured from another source, they shall be immediately ensured information and guidance, at their request, towards residential centres that offer accommodation for homeless persons or night shelters, managed by local public administration authorities, or any other adequate place. In the situation where the aggressor requests accommodation in a residential centre of the category mentioned above, they shall be guided and immediately taken to the residential centres managed by local public administration authorities or, as the case may be, to other adequate social centres by the mobile team provided for in art. 51.(6) In the case where the aggressor does not wish to benefit from the accommodation made available according to para. (5) and opts to live with a relative or any other person, they shall be requested to give a statement regarding the address where they will live and the person who will ensure their accommodation. In the case where the aggressor refuses to give the respective statement, this fact shall be recorded in the minutes drawn up according to the provisions of art. 33 para. (4).(7) The competent public authorities and institutions have the obligation that, ex officio or at the request of police units or any interested person, they implement urgent and specific protection measures regarding minors, persons with disabilities or persons with special needs, covered by the provisional protection order. + Section 5 Implementation of the provisional protection order +

### Article 32

(1) The obligations and prohibitions imposed on aggressors through provisional protection orders become mandatory immediately after their issuance, without summons and without the passage of any term.(2) The 5-day period provided for in art. 31 para. (1) is calculated in hours, that is, it has a duration of 120 hours, and begins to run from the moment the provisional protection order was issued.(3) Police officers may use force and equipment, in an adequate and proportional manner, for the implementation of protection measures ordered through provisional protection orders.(4) The protection measures ordered according to art. 31 para. (1) let. e) are executed on the spot by the police officers present at the place of issuance of the provisional protection orders, if the weapons are located in the spaces where checks were made according to the provisions of art. 29 para. (2) or in the vicinity thereof.(5) In the event that it is not possible to execute the protection measures ordered according to the provisions of art. 31 para. (1) let. e) under the conditions of para. (4), the police officers who issue the provisional protection orders take the necessary measures for their execution as soon as possible. +

### Article 33

(1) The provisional protection order is communicated to the aggressor and the victim.(2) The communication is carried out by handing over, under signature, a copy or duplicate, as the case may be, of the provisional protection order, at the place of its issuance, immediately after issuance.(3) The provisional protection order is considered communicated to the aggressor also in the following situations:a) the aggressor refuses to receive a copy or refuses to sign for receipt;b) the aggressor leaves the place of issuance of the protection order, after being informed that it is necessary to wait for the communication of the result of the checks being carried out in connection with the notification regarding domestic violence.(4) The police officer who issues the provisional protection order prepares a report in which he records the situations provided for in para. (3).(5) The provisional protection order is considered communicated to the aggressor also in the situation where he was not present at the carrying out of checks in connection with the notification regarding domestic violence or at the issuance of the provisional protection order, but proof can be made, with witness statements, with video or audio recordings, regardless of their origin, or with documents, including those in the nature of messages or posts in the electronic or mobile telephony environment, that the fact that a provisional protection order has been issued was communicated to the aggressor, as well as the content of the protection measures ordered through it.(6) In the cases provided for in para. (3) and (5), the aggressor has the right, at any time during the validity period of the provisional protection order, to request and obtain, from the police unit of which the police officer who issued it is a member, a copy thereof. + Section 6 Confirmation and contestation of the provisional protection order +

### Article 34

(1) The provisional protection order is forwarded by the police unit of which the police officer who issued it is a member, for confirmation, to the prosecutor's office attached to the competent court in whose territorial area it was issued, within 24 hours from the date of issuance.(2) The provisional protection order is forwarded to the competent prosecutor's office according to the provisions of para. (1) accompanied by the risk assessment form and the means of evidence obtained according to the provisions of art. 29 or gathered according to the provisions of Law no. 135/2010, with subsequent amendments and completions. The means of evidence gathered according to the provisions of Law no. 135/2010, with subsequent amendments and completions, may be forwarded in a copy certified by the criminal investigation body.(3) The prosecutor from the competent prosecutor's office according to the provisions of para. (1) decides on the necessity of maintaining the protection measures ordered by the police body within 48 hours from the issuance of the provisional protection order.(4) The prosecutor confirms the necessity of maintaining the protection measures ordered by the police body through the provisional protection order, by applying a resolution of an administrative nature on the original copy thereof.(5) In the event that he finds that it is no longer necessary to maintain the protection measures ordered, the prosecutor may order, with reasons, the cessation of the protection measures, with mention of the moment from which they cease. The prosecutor communicates this immediately to the police unit that forwarded the provisional protection order, which takes measures for the immediate information of the persons who were the subject thereof.(6) Immediately after the confirmation provided for in para. (4), the prosecutor forwards the provisional protection order, accompanied by the documents that formed the basis for its issuance and confirmation, to the competent court in whose territorial area it was issued, accompanied by an application for the issuance of the protection order, prepared according to the provisions of art. 40 para. (3) let. a) and art. 41.(7) In the situation of forwarding the provisional protection order according to the provisions of para. (6), the initial duration for which it was ordered is extended, by law, by the duration necessary to complete the judicial procedure for issuing the protection order, with information of the aggressor about this fact. +

### Article 35

(1) The provisional protection order may be contested before the competent court according to the provisions of art. 34 para. (1), within 48 hours from communication.(2) The contestation is judged with the summoning of the parties. Their non-appearance does not prevent the judgment of the case.(3) The contestation is resolved urgently, but no later than the date on which the term for which the provisional protection order was issued expires, in the council chamber, with the summoning of the finding body.(4) The participation of the prosecutor is mandatory.(5) The decision by which the contestation is resolved is final. + Article 36The provisions of this chapter are completed correspondingly with those regarding the protection order. + Section 7 Subsequent acts +

### Article 37

(1) The manner of management by police officers of cases of domestic violence is established by order of the minister of internal affairs and the minister of labour and social protection.*)*) See Order of the minister of internal affairs and the minister of labour and social justice no. 146/2.578/2018 regarding the manner of management of cases of domestic violence by police officers, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 1110 of 28 December 2018.(2) The order provided for in para. (1) includes:a) the procedure for intervention of police officers in cases of domestic violence and cooperation with other institutions with responsibilities in the prevention and combating of domestic violence;b) the model of the risk assessment form and the methodology for its use;c) the procedure for issuance and the model of the provisional protection order;d) the procedure for enforcement of the provisional protection order.(3) In establishing, according to para. (1), the risk assessment form and the methodology for its use, at least the following are taken into account:a) the determination of risk assessment criteria that may be relevant for domestic violence, among which the following criteria are included, mandatorily: the form of domestic violence exercised, the repetitive nature of acts of domestic violence, the vulnerability of the person/persons against whom the domestic violence is exercised, the context of the occurrence of acts of domestic violence and the conduct of the person/persons who exercise the acts of domestic violence;b) the presentation of the manner of using the risk assessment criteria and interpreting the results obtained by applying the respective criteria. +

## Chapter V

The protection order +

### Article 38

(1) A person whose life, physical or mental integrity or freedom is endangered by an act of violence from a family member may request the court to issue, for the purpose of removing the state of danger, a protection order by which one or more of the following measures - obligations or prohibitions - shall be ordered on a provisional basis:a) temporary eviction of the aggressor from the family dwelling, regardless of whether the aggressor holds the property right;b) reintegration of the victim and, as the case may be, the children, into the family dwelling;c) limitation of the aggressor's right of use to only part of the common dwelling when it can be partitioned in such a way that the aggressor does not come into contact with the victim;d) accommodation/placement of the victim, with their consent, and, as the case may be, the children, in an assistance centre among those provided for in art. 19;e) obligation of the aggressor to maintain a determined minimum distance from the victim, from the members of their family, as defined according to the provisions of art. 5, or from the residence, workplace or educational institution of the protected person;f) prohibition for the aggressor to travel to certain localities or determined areas that the protected person frequents or visits periodically;g) obligation of the aggressor to permanently wear an electronic surveillance device; (on 21-05-2021, the phrase: electronic surveillance system was replaced by Article 46, Title II of LAW no. 146 of 17 May 2021, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 515 of 18 May 2021) h) prohibition of any contact, including by telephone, by correspondence or in any other way, with the victim;i) obligation of the aggressor to surrender to the police any weapons held;j) entrusting of minor children or establishment of their residence;k) prohibition for the aggressor to collect the state allowance for children and authorisation of its collection by the parent/person to whom the child has been entrusted for upbringing and education or with whom the child's residence has been established. The prohibition shall be communicated immediately to the county agency for payments and social inspection or of the municipality of Bucharest, as the case may be. The allowance shall be collected by the parent/person who is the beneficiary of the protection measure for the duration of the validity of the protection order and as long as the child has been entrusted to them or in the situation where the child has their residence established with them. (on 13-05-2023, Paragraph (1) of Article 38, Chapter V was supplemented by Article I of LAW no. 114 of 9 May 2023, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 402 of 10 May 2023) (2) The measure provided for in para. (1) let. g) shall be ordered if the following conditions are met:a) one of the measures provided for in para. (1) let. e) or f) has been ordered;b) when the obligation of the aggressor to maintain a minimum distance from the victim and, as the case may be, the members of their family has been ordered, the protected persons express their consent to wear an electronic surveillance device that allows verification of compliance with the aggressor's obligation. (on 21-05-2021, the phrase: electronic surveillance system was replaced by Article 46, Title II of LAW no. 146 of 17 May 2021, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 515 of 18 May 2021) (3) By the same judgment, the court may also order that the aggressor bear the rent and/or maintenance for the temporary dwelling where the victim, minor children or other family members reside or are to reside due to the impossibility of remaining in the family dwelling.(4) In addition to any of the measures ordered pursuant to para. (1), the court may also order the aggressor to undergo psychological counselling, psychotherapy and may recommend voluntary admission or, as the case may be, may request involuntary admission, under the conditions of the Law on mental health and the protection of persons with mental disorders no. 487/2002, republished. In the event that the aggressor is a consumer of psychoactive substances, the court may order, with their consent, their integration into a programme of assistance for drug consumers, in accordance with art. 22 of Law no. 143/2000 on the prevention and combating of illicit drug trafficking and consumption, republished, as subsequently amended and supplemented.(5) By the same judgment, the court may order the taking of a measure to control compliance with the protection order and to prevent its violation, such as:a) obligation of the aggressor to appear periodically, at a time interval established by the court according to the circumstances, at the police station competent to supervise compliance with the protection order;b) obligation of the aggressor to provide information to the police body regarding the new dwelling, in the event that the order has provided for their eviction from the family dwelling;c) periodic and/or spontaneous checks regarding the place where the aggressor is located.(6) The operative part of the judgment shall also include the mention that violation of any of the measures ordered by the protection order constitutes an offence, in accordance with art. 47 para. (1). +

### Article 39

(1) The duration of the measures ordered by the protection order shall be established by the judge, without being able to exceed 12 months from the date of issuance of the order.(2) If the judgment does not contain any mention regarding the duration of the measures ordered, they shall produce effects for a period of 12 months from the date of issuance of the order. (on 23-07-2023, Article 39, Chapter V was amended by Point 1., THE SOLE ARTICLE of LAW no. 240 of 19 July 2023, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE no. 669 of 20 July 2023) +

### Article 40

(1) The application for the issuance of the protection order falls within the jurisdiction of the court of first instance in the territorial area where the victim has their domicile or residence.(2) The application for the issuance of the order may be filed by the victim personally or through a legal representative.(3) The application may also be filed on behalf of the victim by:a) a prosecutor;b) the representative of the competent authority or structure, at the level of the administrative-territorial unit, with responsibilities in the matter of protection of victims of domestic violence;c) the representative of any of the providers of social services in the field of prevention and combating of domestic violence, accredited in accordance with the law, with the victim's consent. +

### Article 41

(1) The application regarding the issuance of the protection order shall be drawn up in accordance with the application form provided in the annex which forms an integral part of this law.(2) The application is exempt from the stamp duty. +

### Article 42

(1) Applications for the issuance of a protection order shall be heard in chambers, with the mandatory participation of the prosecutor.(2) Legal assistance for the person requesting the protection order is mandatory.(3) Legal assistance for the person against whom the protection order is requested is mandatory.(4) The trial shall be conducted urgently and as a priority. The provisions of Articles 200 and 201 of Law No. 134/2010, republished, as subsequently amended and supplemented, regarding the verification of the application and its regularization, respectively the setting of the first hearing date, are not applicable. The summoning of the parties shall be done according to the rules regarding summoning in urgent cases.(5) In cases of exceptional urgency, the court may issue the protection order even without summoning the parties, even on the same day, ruling on the basis of the application and the documents submitted, without the parties' conclusions.(6) In resolving the application, evidence whose administration requires a lengthy time is not admissible.(7) The prosecutor has the obligation to inform the person requesting the protection order about the legal provisions regarding the protection of crime victims.(8) The pronouncement may be postponed by a maximum of 24 hours, and the reasoning of the order shall be done within a maximum of 48 hours from the pronouncement.(9) The resolution of applications may not exceed a period of 72 hours from the filing of the application, except in the case where a provisional protection order has been previously issued, the initial duration for which it is ordered being extended, by operation of law, by the duration necessary to complete the judicial procedure for issuing the protection order, with the aggressor being informed of this fact. +

### Article 43

In the cases provided for in Article 40 paragraph (3) and Article 34 paragraph (6), the victim may renounce, according to the provisions of Article 406 of Law No. 134/2010, republished, as subsequently amended and supplemented, the hearing of the application regarding the protection order. +

### Article 44

(1) The protection order is enforceable.(2) The execution of the decision shall be done without summons or without the passage of any period.(3) Compliance with the protection order is also mandatory for the person protected through it. +

### Article 45

(1) The decision by which the application for the issuance of a protection order is resolved is subject only to appeal, within 3 days from the pronouncement if it was given with the summoning of the parties, and from the communication, if it was given without summoning them.(2) The court of appeal may suspend the execution until the hearing of the appeal, but only with the payment of a security deposit whose amount shall be established by it.(3) The appeal shall be heard with the summoning of the parties. The provisions of Article 42 paragraphs (1)-(4), (6) and (8) are applicable accordingly. (on 23-07-2023, Paragraph (3), Article 45, Chapter V was amended by Point 2., SOLE ARTICLE of LAW No. 240 of 19 July 2023, published in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE No. 669 of 20 July 2023 ) (4) The participation of the prosecutor is mandatory. +

### Article 46

(1) A copy of the operative part of the decision by which the application for the issuance of a protection order was granted shall be communicated, within a maximum of 5 hours from the moment of pronouncement of the decision, to the structures of the Romanian Police within whose territorial jurisdiction the victim's and/or the aggressor's residence is located, as well as to the Directorate for Records of Persons and Database Administration.(2) The protection order by which any of the measures provided for in Article 38 paragraph (1) is ordered shall be put into execution immediately, by or, as the case may be, under the supervision of the police.(3) For the execution of the protection order, the police officer may enter the family home and any annex thereof, with the consent of the protected person or, in the absence thereof, of another family member.(4) The police authorities have the duty to supervise the manner in which the court decision by which the protection order was issued is complied with and to notify the criminal investigation body in case of evasion from execution.(5) Together with the communication of the protection order by which the measure of temporary eviction of the aggressor was taken, the police authority has the obligation to inform the aggressor about the right to have the possibility to request accommodation in residential centers for homeless persons or night shelters, which operate in accordance with the provisions of Law No. 292/2011, as subsequently amended and supplemented, or in other suitable places. The access of aggressors to centers for homeless persons and night shelters is carried out on the basis of the finding that the aggressor falls within the definition provided for in Article 6 letter z) of Law No. 292/2011, as subsequently amended and supplemented, with the exception of situations in which the aggressor represents a risk to the safety of the beneficiaries.(6) In exercising the duties of supervising compliance with the protection order, the police authorities implement measures to prevent non-compliance with the decision, which may consist of: conducting unannounced visits to the injured person's residence, making control telephone calls to the victim/aggressor, requesting information from neighbors, colleagues from the victim's/aggressor's workplace, from the educational institution attended by the victim/aggressor or from other persons who could provide relevant information, as well as any other methods specific to police activity.(7) In the event that evasion from the execution of the protection order is found, the criminal investigation body shall be notified.(8) In the event that the person protected by the protection order violates its provisions, they shall be obliged to cover the expenses generated by the issuance and execution of the order.(9) The method of calculating the expenses generated by the issuance and execution of protection orders to which the protected person may be obliged shall be established by joint order of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Internal Affairs. +

### Article 47

(1) The violation by the person against whom a protection order has been issued of any of the measures provided for in Article 38 paragraphs (1), (4) and (5) letters a) and b) and ordered by the protection order constitutes an offense and is punishable by imprisonment from 6 months to 5 years.(2) The violation by the person against whom a provisional protection order has been issued of any of the measures provided for in Article 31 paragraph (1) and ordered by the provisional protection order constitutes an offense and is punishable by imprisonment from 6 months to 5 years. + Article 48Upon the expiration of the duration of the protection measures, the protected person may request a new protection order, if there are indications that, in the absence of protection measures, their life, physical or mental integrity or freedom would be endangered. +

## Chapter VI

Emergency intervention +

### Article 51

(1) Emergency intervention is carried out from the perspective of providing social services through a mobile team formed of representatives of the Public Social Assistance Service, hereinafter referred to as SPAS.(2) The mobile team has the role of verifying reports, of initial assessment and of carrying out the necessary steps to overcome the immediate risk, consisting of: transport to the nearest health unit in situations where the victim requires medical care, notification of the criminal investigation bodies, notification of the competent bodies for issuing a provisional protection order, referral to the General Directorate of Social Assistance and Child Protection, hereinafter referred to as DGASPC, or, as the case may be, to SPAS, with a view to accommodation in residential centres appropriate to the needs and application of case management for victims and aggressors.(3) The reporting of urgent situations of domestic violence for which support from social services is necessary is carried out through the telephone lines of the public institutions empowered to intervene in cases of domestic violence, including emergency telephone lines.(4) The staff within SPAS carries out the assessment of the degree of risk, from the perspective of providing social services, on the basis of a specific instrument, which is approved, together with the procedure for emergency intervention in cases of domestic violence, by order issued by the minister of labour and social protection.*) *) See Order of the minister of labour and social justice no. 2,525/2018 on the approval of the Procedure for emergency intervention in cases of domestic violence, published in the Official Gazette of Romania, Part I, no. 95 of 6 February 2019. +

### Article 52

(1) For the verification of reports regarding domestic violence, SPAS representatives have the right of access to the headquarters or work points of legal persons, as well as to the domicile of natural persons.(2) Case management is ensured by the domestic violence department within DGASPC. In order to ensure unrestricted access to accommodation in emergency situations, as well as to the rest of the existing social services for victims of domestic violence, as well as for aggressors, DGASPC takes the necessary steps with SPAS and private providers of social services within the territorial area of the county. Victims may be accommodated in any social service with this purpose, regardless of their domicile, where there is an available place. +

## Chapter VII

Financing in the field of prevention and combating of domestic violence + Article 53Activities in the field of prevention and combating of domestic violence are financed from the following sources:a) the state budget;b) the budgets of funds from external credits contracted or guaranteed by the state and whose repayment, interest and other costs are ensured from public funds;c) the budgets of non-reimbursable external funds;d) the local budgets of counties, respectively of the sectors of the municipality of Bucharest and of the General Council of the Municipality of Bucharest, as well as of municipalities, towns and communes; local authorities have the responsibility to estimate the local dimension of domestic violence situations and to allocate an adequate budget to activities in the field;e) donations, sponsorships and other sources, under the conditions of the law. +

services for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, as well as training personnel within institutions with competences in the field designated to handle cases of domestic violence;e) ## Chapter VIII

Contraventions +

### Article 57

(1) The following acts constitute contraventions, if under criminal law they do not constitute offences, and are punishable by a fine between 1,000 lei and 5,000 lei:a) refusal to admit to a shelter or refusal to provide, at the reasoned request of the social worker, free medical care to a person in visible suffering, in order to remove the consequences of violence;b) changing the purpose of the shelter.(2) It constitutes a contravention and is punishable by a fine between 500 lei and 1,000 lei to refuse to leave the shelter, regardless of reason, at the moment when the conditions that determined admission have ceased.(3) It constitutes a contravention and is punishable by a fine between 500 lei and 1,000 lei for a person who has committed acts of aggression to attempt to enter the premises of the shelter where the victim is or is believed to be.(4) The following acts constitute contraventions, if under criminal law they do not constitute offences, and are punishable by a fine from 2,000 lei to 7,000 lei:a) failure to comply with the obligation to take the specific measures provided for in Article 13 paragraph (1);b) failure to comply with the obligation provided for in Article 18 paragraph (2).(5) The finding of contraventions and the application of sanctions provided for in paragraph (4) are carried out by social inspectors, in accordance with the legal provisions applicable in contravention matters.(6) Contraventions are found and sanctions are applied, in accordance with the law, by social workers, the mayor or his authorised representatives.(7) The provisions of Government Ordinance no. 2/2001 on the legal regime of contraventions, approved with amendments and completions by Law no. 180/2002, with subsequent amendments and completions, apply to contraventions. + Article 58Supervision of compliance with the protection measures ordered pursuant to Article 31 paragraph (1) and Article 38 paragraph (1), with the aid of an electronic surveillance
