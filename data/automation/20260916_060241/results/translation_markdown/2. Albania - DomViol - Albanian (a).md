# LAW

LAW
No. 9669, dated 18.12.2006
ON MEASURES AGAINST VIOLENCE IN FAMILY RELATIONS
(Amended by law no. 9914, dated 12.5.2008, no. 10 329, dated 30.9.2010, no. 47/2018, dated
23.7.2018, no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)
(Updated)
Pursuant to articles 78 and 83 point 1 of the Constitution, upon the proposal of 20 thousand voters,
THE ASSEMBLY
OF THE REPUBLIC OF ALBANIA DECIDED:

## CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1

Purpose
(amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, point 2 amended by law no. 125/2020, dated
15.10.2020)
This law aims to:
1.	Prevent and reduce domestic violence in all its forms, through appropriate legal measures and other necessary measures.  
2.	Guarantee protection through legal measures and other necessary measures for family members who are victims of domestic violence, paying special attention to women, girls, children, the elderly and persons with disabilities, subject of this law, according to its article 3.

### Article 2

Object
(word amended in letter "c" by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
This law has as its object:
a)	the creation of a coordinated network of institutions responsible for the protection, support and rehabilitation of victims, mitigation of consequences and prevention of domestic violence;  
b)	orientation of work for the creation of structures and responsible bodies at central and local level for the support of victims and prevention of domestic violence;  
c)	empowerment of the judicial system for taking protection measures against domestic violence; ç) ensuring/guaranteeing for victims of domestic violence fast, free and simple service in accordance with the law, before the court or other competent bodies for the implementation of the law.

### Article 3

Definitions
(amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, point 3 amended by law no. 125/2020, dated
15.10.2020)

In this law, except when expressly provided otherwise in its special provisions, the following terms have these meanings:
1.	"Violence" is any action or inaction of a person towards another person, which results in violation of physical, moral, psychological, sexual, social, economic integrity.  
2.	"Domestic violence" is any act of violence, as defined in point 1 of this article, that occurs within the family or household unit, exercised between family members, as defined in point 7 of this article, who are or have been in a family relationship, regardless of whether the abuser shares or has shared the same residence with the victim.  
3.	"Abuser" is any person suspected and/or sued for exercising violence in family relations before the competent bodies.  
4.	"Child" is any person under the age of 18. The term "minor", used in this law, has the same meaning.  
5.	"Coordinated Referral Mechanism for cases of domestic violence" (hereinafter CRM) is the organized network of responsible institutions at the local level for the prevention, protection, support and rehabilitation of victims of domestic violence.  
6.	"Household unit" is the dwelling or any environment used and adapted as such, regardless of whether it is owned or used by the family.  
7.	"Family members" are:
a)	the spouse or cohabitant or ex-spouse or ex-cohabiting partner;  
b)	brothers, sisters, including, as the case may be, also their spouse or cohabitant, as well as the nephew and niece, if the latter are members of the household unit;  
c)	ancestors and descendants in direct line, without limitations, including also cases when this relationship is created through adoption;  
ç) the parents of the spouse or cohabitant, including the stepfather/stepmother, even if this connection stems from adoption;  
d)	relatives in direct line, including also parents, also adopted children of the spouse or cohabitant;  
dh) the brothers and sisters of the spouse, cohabitant;  
e)	the children of spouses or cohabiting partners;  
ë) persons who are or have been in intimate relationships, not necessarily accompanied by cohabitation, enjoy the same protection;  
f)	the guardian and the person who is in his care during the time the guardianship lasts, according to the definitions of guardianship over minors and guardianship for persons with disabilities and the person whose capacity to act has been removed or limited according to the provisions of the Family Code.".  
8.	"Intimate relationship" is an interpersonal relationship that includes physical or emotional intimacy.  
9.	"Victim" is the person upon whom the violence defined in point 1 of this

### article has been exercised. 10

"Protection order" is the order issued by court decision, which provides protective measures for the victim, children and as the case may be, measures for the rehabilitation of the abuser.  
11.	"Immediate protection order" is the order issued temporarily by court decision, valid until the issuance of the protection order by court decision.  
12.	"Order for preliminary measures of immediate protection" is the reasoned order issued according to the provisions of article 13/1 of this law, which contains immediate measures for stopping violence, taken by the responsible bodies for handling cases of domestic violence at the State Police, until the court decision is taken.

### Article 3/1

Applicable legislation
(added by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
1.	This law is based on the principles enshrined in the Constitution of the Republic of Albania, in the United Nations Convention "On the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women", the Council of Europe Convention "On preventing and combating violence against women and domestic violence" and in other international acts ratified by the Republic of Albania.  
2.	The implementation of the provisions of this law, especially the measures for the protection of the rights of victims, are ensured without discrimination, for any reason provided by the legislation in force for protection from discrimination.  
3.	The special protective measures, provided in article 10 and in article 13/1 of this law, which are necessary for the prevention and protection from violence in family relations, are not considered discrimination if applied according to the rules and criteria provided in this law.

### Article 4

Subjects
(amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
1.	The subjects protected by this law are all subjects defined in article 3, point 7 of this law, who are Albanian citizens with residence or stay in the territory of the Republic of Albania.  
2.	Subjects protected by this law are also foreign citizens or stateless persons who have requested international protection, according to the legislation in force for asylum in the Republic of Albania, and foreigners with regular stay for various reasons, according to the legislation in force for foreigners, who are included in the category of subjects defined in article 3, point 7 of this law.

## CHAPTER II RESPONSIBLE BODIES

### Article 5

Responsible authorities
(letter "d" added by law no.9914, dated 12.5.2008, amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
1.	The main authority responsible for the implementation of this law is the ministry responsible for gender equality issues and the fight against domestic violence.  
2.	Other responsible line authorities are:
a)	the ministry responsible for social issues;  
b)	the ministry responsible for public order and security issues;  
c)	the ministry responsible for health issues; ç) the ministry responsible for justice issues;  
d)	the ministry responsible for education issues; dh) the ministry responsible for housing issues;  
e)	the ministry responsible for employment and vocational training issues; ë) local self-government units.  
3.	The list of other responsible line authorities, provided in point 2, of this article, changes if one of them takes the role of the main responsible authority. In this case, this authority also has a main responsible role and covers issues in the respective field.

### Article 6

Duties of the main responsible authority
(letters "f" and "g" added by law no.9914, dated 12.5.2008, letter "gj" added by law no.10 329, dated 30.9.2010, letters "c", "ç", "d", "g", "gj" amended and letters "h" and "i" added by law no.  
47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
The main responsible authority has these duties:
a)	the drafting and implementation of national strategies and programs in the field of protection and care for victims of violence in family relations;  
b)	the financing or co-financing of projects, which aim to protect and consolidate the family, as well as care for victims of violence in family relations;  
c)	support in establishing support structures, in cooperation with the referral mechanism, for the coordination, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of policies and measures to prevent and combat all forms of violence, as well as their completion with all necessary infrastructure, in accordance also with the principle of "reasonable accommodation", which serves for support and fulfillment of all needs for victims of domestic violence, including financial assistance, as well as medical and social services, according to the legislation in force;  
ç) the organization of training for the prevention and treatment of domestic violence issues for social service workers, who provide services, at each local self-government unit, State Police structures, medical staff and for employees of NGOs licensed to provide social services. Training may be organized in cooperation with the Albanian School of Public Administration, with the School of Magistrates, with the Order of Psychologists and the Order of Social Workers or with organizations and agencies accredited to provide training on topics related to violence issues in family relations, for all responsible administrative, law enforcement and judicial authorities;  
d)	the coordination of work for maintaining and collecting statistical data, for cases of domestic violence, including also the analysis, publication and annual reporting to the responsible parliamentary commission/subcommission for gender equality issues and the prevention of violence against women.  
The storage and processing of data for cases of domestic violence is done in accordance with the legislation in force for the protection of personal data. Their publication is done according to the rules provided in the legislation in force for official statistics.  
dh) the support and supervision of the establishment of rehabilitation centers for victims of domestic violence;  
e)	the support and supervision of the establishment of rehabilitation centers for perpetrators of violence in family relations;  
ë) the licensing of various NGOs that will provide social services for victims and abusers;  
f)	taking measures for the education of the young generation with the mentalities and rules of good behavior, as well as the prohibition of violence in family relations;  
g)	drafts policies and supervises the taking of measures for the elimination of the consequences of child abuse by parents or legal guardians or, in the case when children live in families where parents or guardians abuse each other, through placement in alternative care or the provision of emergency protection measures, according to the provisions of the legislation in force for the rights and protection of the child;  
gj) the provision and supervision of the service at the National Center for Social Care for victims of domestic violence.  
h)	the drafting, in cooperation also with other line authorities, of procedures, standards or protocols, related to risk assessment and intervention plans in cases of domestic violence, as well as monitoring their implementation;  

i)	the coordination and monitoring of the establishment and functioning of social care services for victims of domestic violence, which will be provided at local self-government units.

Duties of responsible line authorities

b/1) the approval, by joint instruction with the ministry responsible for matters of gender equality and the fight against domestic violence, of the model and procedures for risk assessment for cases of domestic violence.  
2.	The ministry responsible for health care ensures medical assistance in the family, in emergency services, family doctor services and in health centers at local self-government units:
a)	to provide at all times medical, social and psychological assistance to victims of domestic violence;  
b)	to carry out at all times the necessary examinations in the respective public health institutions;  
c)	to record cases of domestic violence in the respective medical documentation approved by the Ministry of Health;  
ç) to provide the victim with the respective medical report;  
d)	to refer and direct the victim to other support and protection services from domestic violence.  
2/1. The ministry responsible for health care also performs the following duties:
a)	organizes training and specialization for medical, psychological and social worker staff in the field of domestic violence, also preparing a list of professionals trained and specialized in these fields, in cooperation with the orders established according to the legislation in force;

c)	provides for the establishment of crisis management centers, for cases of sexual violence, to ensure the possibility of functioning of rooms, which serve for emergencies at hospitals where the victim is treated. The standards for the establishment and functioning of crisis management centers, for cases of sexual violence, are determined by instruction of the minister responsible for health care.  
3.	The ministry responsible for justice has the following duties:
a)	to train medico-legal experts in the recognition, diagnosis, assessment and reporting of domestic violence and acts of violence against children;  
b)	takes measures for the professional preparation and specialization of judicial bailiffs in the execution of protection orders, as well as supervises their activity in the fulfillment of the obligations provided for in this law;  
b/1) cooperates with the High Judicial Council, the High Prosecutorial Council, the School of Magistrates, the National Chamber of Advocates, for the preparation of training programs for issues related to domestic violence, as well as makes available to the responsible authorities the updated list of those trained on these issues;  

c)	supervises the functioning of the system of providing legal aid to victims of domestic violence, according to the rules determined by the legislation in force for legal aid guaranteed by the state;  
ç) cooperates with the High Judicial Council, for the determination of rules for the creation of a special database for domestic violence cases in courts and ensures the unification of the registration of these cases;  
d)	drafts special forms for the processing and reporting of statistical data on domestic violence, as well as publishes this data in the statistical yearbook;  
dh) through the responsible structures under its authority, ensures the implementation of the obligation to prepare the medico-legal and psychiatrico-legal report by the legal medicine staff, based on the request of the competent institutions and/or the victim.  
3/1. The ministry responsible for education and the ministry responsible for employment and vocational training, according to the field of responsibility they cover, have the following duties:
a)	drafts curricula for secondary and higher schools on the rules of conduct in
the family;  
b)	prepares school textbooks and other supplementary materials for the education of pupils
and/or students with the mentality of prohibiting violence in family relationships;  
c)	provides programs for pre-university and vocational education of minors and young people from 18 to 21 years of age, who are perpetrators, and of minors who are victims, with the aim of their rehabilitation, reintegration and resocialization.  
4.	Local self-government units, based on the provisions of the legislation in force for local self-government, within the framework of the implementation of this law, have the following duties:
a)	engagement in the creation of specialized structures of social service for cases of domestic violence, regarding the manner of referral of cases of domestic violence, as well as monitoring the execution of protection orders and immediate protection orders;  
b)	the establishment of social and rehabilitation centers for victims and the provision of services for them; coordination of work with existing centers, giving priority to centers specialized in the respective fields and emergency centers for the immediate treatment of victims of domestic violence;  
c)	the establishment of public structures and centers for the treatment, training and rehabilitation of perpetrators;  
ç) taking measures, in cooperation with law enforcement agencies, for the separation of the perpetrator from the family, with the aim of leaving the family dwelling for use by the victim and children;  
d)	taking measures for the implementation of the court decision, by the local authorities of the victim's place of residence and/or domicile;  
dh) cooperation with the probation service, for monitoring the execution of protection orders, according to the rules provided for in the legislation in force for electronic monitoring of persons whose movement is restricted by court decision.  
For the implementation of the obligations provided for in this article, local self-government units may cooperate with each other through the conclusion of joint agreements, in accordance with the legislation in force for local self-government.  
5.	The ministry responsible for housing matters, supports with financing the local self-government units for the provision of specialized housing, according to the legislation in force for social housing.

### Article 7/1

Guarantee for protection
(added by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018 )
1.	Every body or authority, which is responsible for dealing with domestic violence, to which the victim applies, guarantees protection according to this law, regardless of the victim's place of residence or domicile.  

2.	This protection implies assistance for temporary shelter, transportation to a safe shelter or to the requested services, accompaniment to emergency or necessary medical services or to the protection services for victims of domestic violence.

### Article 8

Responsibilities of the responsible authorities
(points 7 and 8 added by law no. 10 329, dated 30.9.2010, letter "dh" of point 3 added by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
1. The responsible authorities have the duty to create special structures and appoint responsible persons for the implementation of this law. The fulfillment of this obligation is supervised by the ministry responsible for issues of gender equality and the fight against domestic violence.
2. The responsible authorities have the obligation to respond to every notification made by the victim or other persons indicated in this law, in cases of violence or threat of violence or also in cases of violation of the protection order or immediate protection order. They keep the relevant report and deliver a copy to the victim or their companion.
3. The responsible authorities use reasonable means to protect the victim and to prevent the continuation of violence by means of:
a) informing the victim or their companion about the measures that will be taken according to the law in force and about the institutions to which they should address;
b) informing the victim or their companion regarding existing social services and accompanying them to the relevant centers and institutions;
c) providing transport for the victim and their companion to medical or social service centers;
ç) making available a police officer in cases of danger to life.
dh) drafting by the social worker for domestic violence issues at the local self-government unit and the needs assessment and referral unit, in cooperation with the Coordinated Referral Mechanism for domestic violence cases, of an individual intervention plan that contains the necessary measures and services that must be taken to protect the victim from ongoing violence, the provision of immediate services and minimization of the consequences of violence and a report on the mental health and psychosocial condition of the victim.
4. If there is suspicion that the abuser has threatened or has exercised domestic violence or has broken protection orders, issued by court decision, the police authorities will make immediate findings.
5. Persons who become aware due to duty or authority entrusted for the implementation of this law and do not act in its implementation, are charged with administrative and/or criminal responsibility, applying sanctions specified in articles 248 and 251 of the Criminal Code.
6. The police authorities have the duty to register the relevant findings in a written report, as well as to initiate investigations primarily. The victim is informed by the police of the relevant number of the reported case.
7. Employees of the service and social care for victims of domestic violence, at public institutions and licensed non-profit organizations, as well as lawyers and legal representatives of victims of domestic violence maintain the confidentiality of personal data and information that the victim provides about their situation, except when otherwise determined in writing by the victim.
8. The mechanism for coordinating work between the responsible authorities for the referral of cases of violence in family relationships, as well as the manner of its procedure for the support and rehabilitation of victims of violence are determined by decision of the Council of Ministers.

### Article 9

Subjects that may notify the responsible authorities

(word changed in point 1 by law no. 10 329, dated 30.9.2010, word replaced in point 1 by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)
1. In case of domestic violence, the victim may address by means of a request to the nearest police station (of the area where they live or are located), the relevant local unit (commune, municipality), the health center of the area where they live or are located or with a request-lawsuit to the district court of their residence, their location or that of the abuser, to take the necessary measures.
2. Any person who observes a case of the exercise of domestic violence may address by means of a request to the above authorities to undertake the necessary measures.

## CHAPTER III PROTECTIVE MEASURES

### Article 10

Protective measures against domestic violence
(amended words in point 1 and point 4 by law no.10 329, dated 30.9.2010, amended letters "j", "k", "m", added letter "m/1" and points 3/1 and 3/2 by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, amended first paragraph of point 1, replaced words in letters "a", "b", "ç", "d", "e", "f", "gj", "h", "l", "dh", repealed letters "c", "m" and "m/1, amended point 2 and point 3 by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)
1.	The court, as a protective measure against domestic violence, immediately orders the removal of the abuser from the dwelling for a specified period of time, when the victim and the abuser reside in the same shelter. The court, when appropriate, in imposing this measure takes into consideration the special needs of the minor abuser, the elderly or the person with disabilities and in these cases the order of removal from the dwelling is imposed only when no other measure guarantees the protection of the victim from violence.  
In addition to the measure of removal of the abuser from the dwelling, protection against domestic violence, in implementation of this law, is also ensured in the following ways:
a)	by immediately ordering the respondent (abuser) not to commit or threaten to commit an act of domestic violence against the applicant (victim) or other members of the victim's family, as defined in article 3 point 3 of this law or as named in the order;  
b)	by immediately ordering the respondent (abuser) not to violate, harass, contact or communicate directly or indirectly with the victim or members of the victim's family, as defined in article 3 point 3 of this law or as named in the order;  
c)	repealed;  
ç) by immediately prohibiting the respondent (abuser) from approaching beyond a specified distance the victim or members of the victim's family, as defined in article 3 point 3 of this law or as named in the order;  
d)	by immediately prohibiting the respondent (abuser) from approaching the home, place of work, residence of the family of origin or residence of the future couple or of other persons and furthermore the children's school, or places which are frequented more by the victim, with the exception of cases when attendance is for work reasons;  
dh) by immediately placing the victim or victims and minors in temporary shelters, taking into account in every case the highest interest, that of the minor;  
e)	by limiting or prohibiting the respondent (abuser) from meeting with the victim's child, according to conditions which may be appropriate;  
ë) by prohibiting the respondent (abuser) from entering or staying in the temporary or permanent dwelling of the victim, or in any part of it, regardless of the property or possession rights of the abuser;  

f)	by ordering a person authorized by the court (law enforcement officer or bailiff) to accompany the victim or the respondent (abuser) to the victim's dwelling and to supervise the removal of personal property;  
g)	by ordering law enforcement agencies to seize any weapon belonging to the abuser during the control carried out or to order the abuser to surrender any weapon belonging to him;  
gj) by ordering the respondent (abuser) to allow the victim to possess the dwelling which the victim and the abuser use together or any part of it;  
h)	by ordering the respondent (abuser) to pay the rent of the permanent or temporary dwelling of the victim, as well as the maintenance obligation for the victim, children or other family members in his care;  
i)	for as long as the protection order is in force, the treatment of property is carried out in accordance with the Family Code, articles 57, 58 and 60;  
j)	by removing from the abuser, temporarily, parental responsibility or, if appropriate, guardianship over the minor, guardianship for the person with disabilities, or for the person who has been deprived of or limited in capacity to act. When the court decides to remove the exercise of parental responsibility from both parents or from the sole parent, it appoints temporary guardianship over the children, applying the rules provided in the Family Code and the procedural legislation in force;  
k)	by ordering the competent authorities, social services of the respective local self-government units and/or organizations that provide services, that, as the case may be, support with psychosocial, health and financial assistance persons abused in the family, as well as monitor compliance with protection orders by the parties, presenting the respective reports before the local coordinator for the referral of cases of violence in family relations;  
l)	by ordering the respondent (abuser) to make a periodic payment in favor of cohabiting persons, who, as a result of the above measure, remain deprived of means of subsistence. To secure the payment, the court may decide that the amount be paid by the employer in favor of the beneficiary. Such a decision constitutes an enforceable title;  
ll) by including the victim of domestic violence in rehabilitation programs;  
m)	repealed m/1) repealed
2.	In the protection order, issued by court decision, in addition to the measure provided in the first paragraph of point 1 of this article, depending on the circumstances of the case, one or more of the protective measures provided in letters "a" to "ll" of point 1 of this article may also be imposed.  
3.	In the immediate protection order, issued by court decision, in addition to the measure provided in the first paragraph of point 1 of this article, depending on the circumstances of the case, one or more of the measures provided in letters "a" to "g" of point 1 of this article may also be imposed.  
3/1 The court, primarily, in the immediate protection order or/and in the protection order, may provide protective measures for children who have suffered violence or have been present when violence was exercised in the family. The court, according to its assessment, may also apply protective measures and procedures provided by the legislation in force for the rights and protection of the child, as long as the time limits of the judicial process provided by this law are not violated.  
3/2 The decision for dissolution of marriage, declared by the court during the time of implementation of the IPO/PO for simultaneous consequences, begins to be implemented from the day of completion of the IPO/PO. In case the court has imposed different maintenance obligations for the children in these decisions, the most favorable decision for the children is applied.  
4.	In the case of implementation of letter "g", the court decision provides:
a)	suspension of the weapon permit until completion of the term of the protection order, as well as notification of the respective administrative authority, if the weapon has been seized and the person has an

authorization for possession of a weapon with a permit;  
b)	return of the seized weapon, if the person has an authorization for possession of a weapon with a permit, only after the expiration of the term specified in the protection orders.

### Article 10/1

Rehabilitation of the abuser
(added by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1.	In the protection order, issued by court decision, the abuser is ordered to participate in psychosocial rehabilitation programs and/or in parental skills programs, organized by public or private entities.  
2.	The heads of the rehabilitation program report to the local coordinator for the referral of cases of domestic violence, the participation of the defendant in the program and his progress periodically and the results of the abuser's rehabilitation. If the abuser does not participate in the program, except in cases when there are objective reasons and justified causes for non-participation, upon request of the ad-hoc meeting of the Coordinated Referral Mechanism for domestic violence cases or of the entities provided for in article 13 of this law, the provisions of the Criminal Code for actions committed in violation of the court decision for protection orders shall apply.  
3.	In the protection order, issued by court decision, the defendant or the abuser is ordered, as the case may be, to participate in rehabilitation programs at hospital centers, outpatient centers or community centers, which provide mental health services, alcoholism services or toxicology services.  
4.	The heads of the rehabilitation program inform the local coordinator at the Coordinated Referral Mechanism for domestic violence cases about the participation of the defendant in the program and his progress.  
5.	An abuser, sentenced by court decision to serve the sentence in penal decision execution institutions, may participate in psychosocial rehabilitation programs. Participation and cooperation in the program is taken into account in the risk assessment of the convicted person in cases of conditional release, reward or special leaves, visits.

### Article 11

Effects of the protection order
(word replaced in point 1 by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
1.	Notwithstanding any other order or decision issued by the court or any other institution, the protection order containing the measures provided for in article 10 of this law shall be issued by court decision for the cases provided for by this law.  
2.	The protection order or the immediate protection order, issued by court decision, or the termination of its effects does not permanently affect property or custody rights.

## CHAPTER IV

TRIAL FOR THE ISSUANCE OF THE PROTECTION ORDER

### Article 12

Competent body for the issuance of protective orders in case of domestic violence
(word replaced in point 1 by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
1.	The competent body for the issuance of protective orders in case of domestic violence is the court of general jurisdiction of the first instance.  
To decide on securing the protective measures mentioned in article 10 of this law, the court issues protection orders and immediate protection orders.  
2.	The interested party may request the court, as the case may be, in accordance with the provisions of this law, the issuance of the protection order, without previously requesting the issuance of the immediate protection order.  
3.	After the court has issued an immediate protection order, the interested party may request the issuance of a protection order, as provided in this law. The respective protection order serves to reconfirm the continuity of the immediate protection order, as well as ensures the protective measures specified in article 10 of this law.

### Article 13

Entities that have the right to request the protection order
(letters "dh" and "e" of point 2 added by law no.10 329, dated 30.9.2010)
1.	The request for a protection order may be submitted by:
a)	the victim himself/herself;  
b)	the legal representative or lawyer of the victim;  
c)	the police / prosecution.  
2.	The request for a protection order of a minor may be submitted by:
a)	the parent or guardian of the minor;  
b)	the legal representative or lawyer of the victim;  
c)	relatives;  
ç) representatives of the social services office at the municipality or commune, where the victim has permanent or temporary residence, who have knowledge of the exercise of violence;  
d)	centers and services for the protection and rehabilitation of victims of domestic violence, recognized/licensed by the ministry responsible for gender equality issues and the fight against domestic violence.  
dh) the police/prosecution;  
e)	persons legally responsible for children.  
3.	The request for an immediate protection order may be submitted by:
a)	the victim himself/herself;  
b)	the legal representative or lawyer of the victim;  
c)	the police/prosecution;  
ç) the person with whom the victim has a family relationship;  
d)	representatives of the social services office at the municipality or commune, where the victim has permanent or temporary residence, who have knowledge of the exercise of violence;  
dh) centers and services for the protection and rehabilitation of victims of domestic violence, recognized/licensed by the ministry responsible for gender equality issues and the fight against domestic violence.  
4.	In the case when the request is submitted by the police/prosecution, the withdrawal of the victim from the process does not result in the non-trial of the case initiated.

### Article 13/1

Order for preliminary measures of immediate protection
(added by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, words replaced in point 1, first paragraph of point 2 amended, words replaced in letters "a", "b", "c" of point 2, point 3/1 added by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1.	When the risk assessment shows that the violence exercised poses a risk to the life, health and freedom of family members, the structures responsible for handling cases of domestic violence, at the State Police, immediately take preliminary measures to protect the victim or victims and to prevent violence.  
2.	In the order for preliminary measures of immediate protection, according to point 1 of this article, until the court issues the immediate protection order or the protection order, the immediate removal of the abuser from the dwelling is ordered, when the victim and the abuser live in the same shelter, except in cases when the abuser is a minor, elderly or person with limited abilities, and/or one or more of the following measures are determined:
a)	ordering the abuser not to commit or threaten to commit further acts of domestic violence against the victim or other members of the victim's family;  
b)	ordering the abuser not to violate, harass, contact or communicate directly or indirectly with the victim or members of the victim's family;  
c)	immediate prohibition of the abuser from approaching the shelter, workplace, dwelling of the family of origin or the dwelling of other persons and educational institutions, or places which are frequented more by the victim, except in cases when frequenting is done for work reasons;  
ç)	immediate placement of the victim in a residential or emergency center for victims of domestic violence until the court decision is taken;  
d)	ordering a State Police officer to accompany the victim to the residential or emergency center, according to letter "ç" of this point;  
dh)	ordering a State Police officer to accompany the victim to his/her residence and supervise the collection of personal belongings;  
e)	blocking any licensed weapon belonging to the abuser during the control carried out or seizure of any unlicensed weapon belonging to him, until the court decision is taken.  
3.	The order for preliminary measures of immediate protection is issued by the head of the responsible structure of the State Police, in whose jurisdiction the violence is reported according to the procedure and model, determined by joint instruction of the minister responsible for matters of public order and security and the minister responsible for matters of gender equality and the fight against domestic violence.  
A copy of the order for preliminary measures of immediate protection is immediately notified to the local coordinator for referral of cases of violence in family relations.  
3/1.	When extraordinary measures are imposed throughout the country or in a part of its territory, respectively, the head of the responsible structure of the State Police is obliged to issue the order for preliminary measures for immediate protection, according to point 2 of this article, in every case when he finds that violence has been exercised. During the entire period of the duration of the extraordinary measures, in the request addressed to the court for the assessment of preliminary protection measures, taken through the order for preliminary measures for immediate protection issued, the police must request the court to issue the protection order, without first requesting the issuance of the immediate protection order.  
4.	In cases when violence is exercised against or in the presence of children, the State Police immediately takes the child under protection and simultaneously refers the case to the Child Protection Unit in the local self-government unit, according to the rules provided in the legislation in force for the rights and protection of the child.  
5.	Within 48 hours from taking the measures, according to the determination of point 1 of this article, the bodies responsible for handling cases of domestic violence, at the State Police, submit before the court a request for assessment of preliminary measures of immediate protection and a lawsuit for issuance of the immediate protection order.  
6.	The right to request assessment of the order for preliminary measures of immediate protection before the court is also held by the victim and the subjects provided by article 13, point 3, of this law, within 48 hours from the day of its issuance.  
7.	The court, 48 hours from the day of submission of the request, in court session decides:

a)	assessment of the protective measures imposed in the order for preliminary measures of immediate protection and issuance of the immediate protection order;  
b)	annulment of the order for preliminary measures of immediate protection and termination of its consequences, if the facts and circumstances for which it was issued are not proven.  
8.	The court, for the needs of the trial, may summon the representative of the State Police and/or the local coordinator for referral of cases of violence in family relations, to appear in court session to give explanations.

### Article 14

Form of the claim
(a sentence added at the end of point 3 and point 4 amended by law no. 10 329, dated 30.9.2010, points 3, 4, 5 amended and point 6 added by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, words replaced in letter "b" of point 1, by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)
1.	The claim for the issuance of protection orders and immediate protection orders must also contain:
a)	personal data about the family or kinship relations between the victim and the abuser;  
b)	a clear description of the facts and circumstances in which the act of domestic violence occurred, including the reasons explaining the violation of the safety, health or well-being of the victim by the defendant (abuser) provided for in article 3 points 1 and 2 of this law;  
c)	the specific protective measures requested; ç ) the signature of the applicant.  
2.	The claim for a protection order may be submitted at any time before the court by persons who are legitimized to request it. In cases where immediate assistance is required, the request may be completed and submitted at the nearest police station and the police officer acts in accordance with the law "On the State Police".  
3.	For the drafting of the claim, the preparation of documents and representation in court proceedings, the applicant is assisted by a lawyer who provides secondary legal aid, in accordance with the conditions and rules provided by the legislation in force for legal aid guaranteed by the state.  
4.	The applicant for IPO/PO and the holder of IPO are exempted from:
a)	payment of court fees and court costs, in accordance with the legislation in force for legal aid guaranteed by the state;  
b)	the obligation to prepay the fee for the enforcement of the order at the state judicial enforcement service, in accordance with the legislation in force for legal aid guaranteed by the state;  
c)	payment for psychosocial expert reports, sign language interpreters, medico-legal expert reports, expert reports for the assessment of the risk of domestic violence or any other expert report.  
After the issuance of the IPO/PO, the court costs are charged to the party that has exercised domestic violence.  
5.	The claim is registered in a separate register, recorded on the day of its submission and assigned by lot the next day. The court conducts the trial within the time limits provided by this law and applies the forms of notification provided in the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure, for the parties or other subjects. For the needs of the development of the trial, when the circumstances of the case dictate, the court, as necessary, may hold the court hearing in the absence of the parties, regardless of confirmation of receipt of notice.  
6.	The court, in the decision to issue the protection order, also decides on the cases provided for in point 4 of this article.

### Article 15

Evidence in court proceedings
(the last sentence of point 2 amended and point 2/1 added by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
1.	Evidence that must be submitted to the court may be: testimony, police reports, medical report, expert report, examinations and explanations/statements of the parties, other documents issued by employees of the social services office at local units, documents issued by legal persons (NGOs), registered according to the provisions in force.  
2.	When it becomes clear from the data of the request that police stations, local government authorities or health centers have written evidence related to the exercise of domestic violence, they immediately issue a certified copy (copy with seal) for this based on the request submitted by the applicant or the court itself. In cases where the applicant declares the impossibility of securing the documentation provided for in this point, as well as in any other case when the court considers that the fulfillment of the request may cause delays, which violate the essence of the victim's rights, the court may order the submission of the necessary documentation from public administration bodies that have it.  
2/1 Failure to issue the documentation specified in point 2 of this article constitutes grounds for initiating disciplinary proceedings against the responsible person, based on the provisions of special legislation.  
3.	When it does not have all the evidence provided for in point 2 of this article, the court issues by decision the protection order, based only on the description of the circumstances and facts in which domestic violence was committed, based on its own conviction.

### Article 16

Trial for the issuance of the protection order
(point 1 amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, words replaced in letter "b" of point 2, by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)
1.	The court decides on the issuance of the protection order within 15 days from the day of registration of the request.  
2.	During the examination of the request for a protection order, the court hears in turn the following persons:
a)	the victim, legal representative or his/her lawyer;  
b)	the defendant (abuser), legal representative or his/her lawyer;  
c)	the prosecutor, if he/she has submitted the request; ç ) police representatives;  
d)	representatives of the social services office at the unit in which the mentioned person lives permanently or temporarily, when the applicant is under 18 years of age, lacks capacity to act or the domestic violence exercised has an impact on the aforementioned persons;  
dh) employees of health centers or service and rehabilitation centers, who have assisted the victim after the exercise of violence against him/her;  
e)	witnesses, whom the court considers necessary.  
3.	In the case where the request has been submitted by the police/prosecutor's office, the withdrawal of the victim from the process does not result in the non-trial of the initiated case.

### Article 17

Court decision on issuing a protection order
(substituted words in letter "b" of point 1, removed phrase in letter "ç" of point 3, added point 6 by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. The court may issue by decision a protection order only against the abuser mentioned in the request-complaint. This decision contains only the protective measures described in article 10 of this law. The court issues by decision the protection order by determining one or more of the measures specified in article 10 of this law, if it verifies that:
a) there are reasonable grounds to believe that the abuser poses a risk for committing any act of domestic violence;
b) the issuance of the protection order by court decision is necessary to protect the safety, health, well-being of the victim or victims, an order in which, in addition to the applicant, the court deems that other persons may also be included, with whom the applicant has family or intimate ties, who may become a cause for the exercise of violence against the latter.
2. The court may not refuse the issuance of the protection order due to another judicial process, in which one of the parties is involved.
3. The final decision of the court on issuing the protection order, which, in addition to fulfilling the requirements of article 310 of the Code of Civil Procedure, contains:
a) the measure determined by the court;
b) the duration of the measure, which may not be longer than 12 months, but with the right of extension;
c) notification that violation of the protection order, issued by court decision, constitutes a criminal offense within the meaning of article 320 of the Criminal Code;
ç) notification of the right to appeal against the protection order.
4. The protection order, issued by court decision, is immediately notified to the abuser, who was not present during the court hearing, according to article 316 of the Code of Civil Procedure. The victim must be provided by the court with two copies of the original decision, so that one of them is kept for themselves and the other is made available to the police at the moment the need arises.
5. The court, within 24 hours after issuing the protection order by court decision, sends a copy of it to the following persons:
a) the victim and other persons indicated in the decision;
b) the prosecutor, if they have made a request;
c) the office of social services of the local units, where the victim and other persons mentioned in the decision have permanent or temporary residence;
ç) the police commissariat in the region where the victim and other persons mentioned in the decision have permanent or temporary residence.
6. The decision on the protection order and the immediate protection order is registered in a special register, which is administered at each court according to the model, rules and procedures approved by decision of the High Judicial Council.

### Article 18

Trial for issuing an immediate protection order
(amended point 1 by law no. 10 329, dated 30.9.2010, amended letter "b" of point 2 by law no.
125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. The court decides on the request for immediate protection:
a) of a minor, within 24 hours after submission of the request;
b) of other subjects of violence in family relations, within 48 hours after submission of the request.
2. In the case of examination of the request for an immediate protection order, the court holds a hearing where the following persons are heard:
a) the victim, their legal representative or lawyer;
b) the abuser, their representative or lawyer;

c) the prosecutor, if they participate;
ç) other applicants, provided for in article 13 of this law;
d) witnesses, whom the court considers necessary.

### Article 19

Court decision on issuing immediate protection orders
(added letter "d" in point 5 by law no.10 329, dated 30.9.2010, added point 2/1, repealed letter "ç" of point 3 and amended point 6 by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, substituted words in
letters "a" and "b" of point 1 by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)
1. The court issues by decision the immediate protection order, by determining one or more of the measures specified in article 10 of this law, if it finds that:
a) there are grounds to believe that the defendant (abuser) has committed or has threatened to commit any act of domestic violence; or
b) the defendant (abuser) poses a direct and immediate threat to the safety, health or well-being of the victim and other members of the victim's family; or
c) the issuance of the immediate protection order by court decision is necessary to protect the safety, health, well-being of the victim or other members of the victim's family, who are protected by means of the protection order.
2. The court may not refuse the issuance of the immediate protection order due to another judicial process, in which one of the parties is involved.
2/1 The court issues the protection order even in cases when the parties claim that the conflict has been resolved or should be resolved by reconciliation or mediation.
3. The final decision of the court on issuing the immediate protection order, which must fulfill the requirements of article 310 of the Code of Civil Procedure, contains:
a) the measure determined by the court;
b) the duration of the measure, which ends at the moment the protection order imposed by the court begins to be implemented;
c) notification that violation of the court decision on issuing the immediate protection order constitutes a criminal offense, within the meaning of article 320 of the Criminal Code;
ç) Repealed;
d) the date of the hearing for verification of the immediate protection order, which is within 20 days from its issuance by court decision.
4. The immediate protection order, issued by court decision, is immediately notified to the abuser, who was not present during the court hearing, according to article 316 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
The victim must be provided by the court with two copies of the original decision, so that one of them is kept for themselves and the other is made available to the police at the moment the need arises.
5. The court, within 24 hours after issuing the decision, sends a copy of it to the following persons:
a) the victim and other persons indicated in the decision;
b) applicants within the meaning of article 13 of this law;
c) the office of social services of the local units, where the victim and other persons mentioned in the decision have permanent or temporary residence;
ç) the police commissariat in the region where the victim and other persons mentioned in the decision have permanent or temporary residence.
d) the enforcement offices of the place of execution.
6. The court decision on issuing the immediate protection order constitutes an enforcement title and may not be appealed. The decision is executed immediately by the police authorities, in cooperation with the judicial enforcement service and the office of social services of the local self-government unit where the victim and other persons, provided for in the decision, have permanent or temporary residence.

### Article 20

Hearing for the verification of the immediate protection order
(introductory sentence amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
1. The court conducts the hearing, pursuant to article 16 of this law, no later than 20 days from the day of issuance of the immediate protection order. At the conclusion of the judicial process, the court:
a) decides the annulment, as well as the termination of the effects of the immediate protection order, issued previously by court decision, when the latter fails to be proven;
b) refuses the issuance of the protection order by court decision, when the immediate protection order, issued previously by court decision, fails to be proven;
c) issues the protection order by court decision on the basis of article 17 of this law. According to need, this decision may modify the conditions of the immediate protection order, issued previously by court decision.

### Article 21

Appeal
(point 1 repealed and point 2 amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, amended by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)
1. Against the court's decision for the issuance of the protection order, the verification of the immediate protection order, as well as the non-issuance of the immediate protection order, an appeal may be made within 5 days from the day after notification of the reasoned decision. The court of appeal renders a decision within 15 days from the day of registration of the appeal. Against the decision of the court of appeal, recourse to the High Court is not permitted. Against the decision of the court that has issued the protection order, appeal by the victim is not permitted, except in the case when the victim claims that the measures imposed do not guarantee protection.
2. The filing of the appeal does not suspend the execution of the court's decision for the issuance of the immediate protection order or the protection order.

### Article 22

Circumstances for the modification, termination and continuation of the protection order
(words replaced in point 1 by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. If the circumstances have changed fundamentally, the victim (his/her representative), the abuser or the prosecutor, if he/she has participated in the process, may file a request for the modification or termination of the protection order.
2. After receiving the request for the modification or termination of the protection order, the court continues the examination of the request, on the basis of article 16 of this law. At the end of the examination the court decides:
a) to leave in force the protection order;
b) to modify the protection order if this is required by the circumstances that have changed fundamentally; or
c) to terminate it, if the criteria defined in article 10 point 1 of this law no longer apply, due to the fundamental change of circumstances.
3. The submission of the request for the modification or termination of the protection order does not suspend the execution of the decision for the issuance of the protection order.
4. The victim or his/her authorized representative, 15 days before the end of the effects of

the decision for the issuance of the protection order, may submit the request for its continuation. If the request is not submitted, the protection order is terminated automatically on the day of the end of its effects.
5. After receiving the request for the continuation of the protection order, the court continues with the examination of the request, on the basis of article 16 of this law. At the end of the examination the court decides:
a) the verification of the termination of the protection order on the date of the end of its effects;
or
b) the continuation of the effects of the decision for the issuance of the protection order, if
the criteria defined in article 17 point 1 of this law apply.

### Article 23

Execution of the judicial decision
(points 1 and 6 amended, point 3/1 added and points 2 and 5 repealed by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)
1. The judicial decision for the issuance of the protection order is final and constitutes an executive title from the day of its pronouncement. Together with the immediate protection order, the court also issues the order for its execution. The decision is executed immediately by the structures of the State Police, in cooperation with the judicial bailiff service and the social services office of the local unit where the victim and other persons mentioned in the decision have permanent or temporary residence.
2. Repealed.
3. Social workers and police officers take all measures for the immediate and continuous execution of the protective measures imposed in accordance with article 10 of this law.
3/1 During the duration of the judicial decision for the IPO/PO, the social services office of the local self-government unit monitors the respect of the protection order by the parties and every 60 days prepares a report, about which, in every case, it informs the State Police. The respective parties in the judicial decision cooperate to ensure home visits by representatives of the social services office of the local self-government unit and the responsible institutions, according to the court's decision for the immediate protection order and/or the protection order, also providing the necessary information.
4. Institutions, residential centers, service centers, licensed NGOs for service provision implement the measures provided in the judicial decision and coordinate their work with local government units and police directorates, as direct executors of these decisions.
5. Repealed.
6. In case of voluntary non-execution by the parties or obstruction of the execution of protection orders by the implementing bodies, as provided in the provisions of this law and/or in the court's decision, against the responsible persons the provisions of the Criminal Code for actions committed in contravention of the court's decision, in connection with the obligations arising from protection orders, are applied.

## CHAPTER V FINAL PROVISIONS

### Article 24

Criminal proceedings
(sentence added at the end of the paragraph by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)
The issuance of the immediate protection order or the protection order by court

decision does not prohibit the interested parties from initiating criminal proceedings, with regard to actions or omissions that constitute elements of criminal offenses. In every case, the police officer, to whom the victim appears for protection, refers the case to the prosecutor's office for the criminal prosecution of the abuser, according to the rules defined in the Code of Criminal Procedure.

### Article 25

Bylaws
The Council of Ministers issues the bylaws for the implementation of this law within 3 months from its entry into force.

Entry into Force
This law enters into force on 1 June 2007.
