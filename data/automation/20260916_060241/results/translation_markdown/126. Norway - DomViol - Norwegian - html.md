### § 271

Assault
Any person who uses violence against another person or otherwise physically violates him shall be liable to a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 1 year.  
An assault may be exempted from punishment if
a.  
it has been reciprocated with an assault or bodily injury, or
b.  
it reciprocates a preceding assault, bodily injury or particularly provocative statement.

### § 272

Aggravated assault
Aggravated assault shall be punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 years. In deciding whether the assault is aggravated, particular weight shall be given to whether it has resulted in severe pain, injury or death, and otherwise whether it
a.  
has occurred without provocation and has the character of an attack,  
b.  
has been committed against a defenceless person,  
c.  
has the character of abuse,  
d.  
has been committed by several persons acting together,  
e.  
has been motivated by the victim's colour, national or ethnic origin, religion, belief, sexual orientation, gender identity or gender expression or reduced functional capacity, or
f.  
has been committed using a knife or other particularly dangerous instrument.

Bodily injury
Any person who injures another person's body or health, renders another person physically powerless or induces unconsciousness or a similar state in another person shall be liable to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 years.

### § 274

Aggravated bodily harm
Aggravated bodily harm is punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years. In determining whether the bodily harm is aggravated, particular weight shall be given to whether it has resulted in incurable defect or injury, illness or incapacity for work of some duration or severe pain, significant injury or death, and furthermore whether it
a.  
has occurred without provocation and has the character of an assault,  
b.  
has been committed against a defenceless person,  
c.  
has the character of abuse,  
d.  
has been committed by several persons acting together,  
e.  
is motivated by the injured party's colour, national or ethnic origin, religion, belief, sexual orientation, gender identity or gender expression or reduced functional capacity, or
f.  
has been perpetrated by use of a knife or other particularly dangerous instrument.  
Any person who causes significant injury to another person's body or health is punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 15 years.

Aggravated abuse in close relationships is punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 15 years. In determining whether the abuse is aggravated, particular weight shall be given to whether it has resulted in significant injury or death, and furthermore
a.  
its duration,  
b.  
whether it has been carried out in a particularly painful manner, or has resulted in significant pain, or
c.  
whether it has been committed against a defenceless person.

### § 284

Female genital mutilation
Any person who performs an intervention in a woman's genitals that damages the genitals or causes permanent changes to them is punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 years. Reconstruction of female genital mutilation is punishable in the same manner.  
Consent does not exempt from punishment.

### § 286

Violence against particularly vulnerable occupational groups
Any person who by violence seeks to influence the professional practice of a person from a particularly vulnerable occupational group is punishable by a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years.  
By particularly vulnerable occupational group is meant
a.  
health personnel who provide medically justified health care, and fire and rescue personnel who provide services on assignment,  
b.  
persons who provide generally accessible passenger transport, such as railway, metro, tram, bus, aircraft, taxi or ferry, and
c.  
persons who have responsibility for education in primary school or upper secondary school.

### § 287

Failure to render assistance
Any person who fails
a.  
to the best of his or her ability to assist a person who is in obvious danger of losing his or her life or being caused significant injury to body or health, or
b.  
by reporting or otherwise to the best of his or her ability to avert fire, flood, explosion or similar accident that entails danger to human life or significant injury to body and health,  
is punishable by a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months.  
Breach of the duty to render assistance pursuant to the first paragraph is not punishable when the duty could not be fulfilled without exposing oneself or others to particular danger or sacrifice.

### § 288

Abandonment in a helpless condition, etc.  
Anyone who abandons another person in a helpless condition with danger to life, body or health shall be liable to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years. The same penalty shall apply to anyone who fails to provide assistance to a person in a helpless condition for whom he or she has a duty to care.
