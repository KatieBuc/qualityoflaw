# REPUBLIC OF SAN MARINO

REPUBLIC OF SAN MARINO
We the Captains Regent
of the Most Serene Republic of San Marino

Having regard to Article 4 of Constitutional Law no.185/2005 and Article 6 of Qualified Law no.186/2005;  
We promulgate and order the publication of the following ordinary law approved by the Grand and General Council in the session of 18 June 2008.  

LAW OF 20 JUNE 2008 NO.97

PREVENTION AND SUPPRESSION OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE

### CAPO I

GUIDING PRINCIPLES AND MEASURES FOR AWARENESS-RAISING AND PREVENTION OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE, INCLUDING
DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

#### Art.1

(Objective of the law)
This law aims to prevent and combat violence against women and gender-based violence, including domestic violence.

#### Art. 2

(Notion of violence against women)
Violence against a person constitutes any act of violence based on sexual identity that causes or is likely to cause, for the target, harm or suffering of a physical, sexual or psychological nature, including the threat of carrying out such acts, coercion, arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether in public life or in private life.

#### Art. 3

(Mass media and discriminatory disclosures)
Social media must promote the protection and safeguarding of equality between men and women and must avoid any discrimination between them.  

It is prohibited to use, even for advertising purposes, images and expressions that are harmful to the dignity and identity of the person, or having discriminatory content, including those containing references to the sexual orientation of the person or to gender identity.  
The Authority for Equal Opportunities, appointed for this purpose, on its own initiative or upon written notification by anyone having an interest, may request the Commissioner of Law:
a)	to prohibit the dissemination of images, news, or references in breach of the prohibition referred to in the second paragraph;  
b)	to prohibit their continuation and to eliminate their effects;  
without prejudice to the right of the person portrayed or subject to the references to request compensation for damages.  
The Commissioner of Law shall proceed by decree, after having heard the party against whom the measure must be executed. In cases of particular urgency, and where the party against whom the measure must be executed does not have residence or headquarters in the territory of the Republic, or an authorized representative to appear in court, the Commissioner of Law may order the execution of the measure, even without ordering the hearing.  
Anyone who fails to comply with the order issued pursuant to the preceding paragraph shall be punished pursuant to Article 366 of the Criminal Code.  
An appeal for nullity to the Court of Appeal is permitted against the decree of the Commissioner of Law, but the appeal does not suspend the execution of the measure, unless the Court of Appeal decides otherwise.  
The legal assistance of the Authority for Equal Opportunities in the proceedings is assumed by the State Attorney's Office.  
The acts of the proceedings are exempt from judicial tax.

#### Art. 4

(Assistance to victims of violence)
The State ensures to victims of family and sexual violence:
a)	information on the measures provided by law regarding the protection, safety and rights of assistance and relief for victims of violence;  
b)	the existence of services to which the relevant social assistance responsibilities are assigned, equipped with specialized personnel, easily identifiable and accessible to victims;  
c)	that the services are able to perform emergency intervention functions, including psychological, and subsequent medium-term management of situations, also for the purpose of family reconciliation;  
d)	the provision of social support actions, protection, support for education, training and professional integration;  
e)	in the most serious cases, in which remaining in the family is harmful, the placement of victims in family-type communities for a period sufficient to implement a social reintegration project;  
f)	the preparation of protection and social reintegration programs for the victim of violence, where necessary, including meeting housing needs and maintaining the residence permit, should it expire during the proceedings, at least for the duration of the criminal trial, professional reintegration and the needs for care and support of dependent children.  
g)	specific training for judges to whom the judicial proceedings under this law are entrusted and for Law Enforcement.  
The identification and organization of services and the concrete determination of measures shall be carried out by specific delegated decree to be issued within six months of the entry into force of this law.

### CAPO II

AMENDMENTS TO THE CRIMINAL CODE

#### Art. 5

(Aggravating circumstance in homicide)
In Article 150, second paragraph, of the Criminal Code, after number 1) the following is inserted: "1 bis) against the spouse, a cohabiting person or someone who was or is bound by an emotional relationship, even not involving cohabitation".

#### Art. 6

(Aggravating circumstances of personal injury)
Article 156 of the Criminal Code is replaced as follows:
"When the injury has resulted in abortion, danger to life, illness healed beyond sixty days or incurable, permanent disfigurement of the face, loss or significant weakening of a sense, an organ or its functionality, or the loss of the capacity to procreate, or genital mutilation, even if inflicted for religious reasons, imprisonment and disqualification of the third degree shall apply.  
The same penalty applies if the victim is or was a spouse or person who is or was bound in an analogous emotional relationship, even without including cohabitation, or if the victim is a particularly vulnerable person who lives with the offender".

#### Art. 7

(Reduction or maintenance in slavery or servitude)
Article 167 of the Criminal Code is amended as follows:

"Art. 167
Reduction or maintenance in slavery or servitude

Anyone who exercises over a person powers corresponding to those of the right of ownership or anyone who reduces or maintains a person in a state of continuous subjection, forcing them to perform work or sexual services or begging or in any case services that involve their exploitation, shall be punished with imprisonment of the fifth degree and disqualification of the fourth degree.  
The reduction or maintenance in a state of slavery takes place when the conduct is carried out through violence, threat, deception, abuse of authority or taking advantage of a situation of physical or psychological inferiority or a situation of necessity, or through the promise or giving of sums of money or other advantages to those who have authority over the person.  
The penalty is increased by one degree if the acts referred to in the first paragraph are committed against a minor under eighteen years of age or are aimed at the exploitation of prostitution or for the purpose of subjecting the victim to organ removal.".

#### Art. 8

(Trafficking in persons)
Article 168 of the Criminal Code is replaced as follows:

"Trafficking in persons

Anyone who commits trafficking or otherwise trades in a person who is in the conditions referred to in Article 167, or, for the purpose of reducing or maintaining a person in slavery or servitude, induces them by deception or coerces them by violence, threat, abuse of authority or taking advantage of a situation of physical or psychological inferiority or a situation of necessity, or by promise or giving of sums of money or other advantages to the person who has authority over them, to enter or stay or leave the territory of the State or to move within it, is punished with imprisonment of the sixth degree and disqualification of the fourth degree.  
The penalty is increased by one degree if the acts referred to in the first paragraph are committed against a minor under eighteen years of age or are directed at the exploitation of prostitution or for the purpose of subjecting the victim to organ removal.".

#### Art. 9

(Repeal of trafficking for the purposes of prostitution and placement of inducement to prostitution among offences against personal freedom)
Article 268 of the Criminal Code is repealed.  
Article 269 of the Criminal Code constitutes an offence against personal freedom, and is placed in Chapter II of the Criminal Code with the Article number 168 bis (inducement to prostitution).

#### Art. 10

(Aggravating circumstance in the offence of violation of sexual freedom)
The following paragraph is added to Article 172 of the Criminal Code: "The penalty is increased by one degree if the act referred to in the first paragraph is committed by the spouse or cohabitant, or by one who has or has had an emotional relationship with the victim.  
The penalty is increased by one degree if the act is committed against a person with disabilities".

#### Art. 11

(Group violence)
The following is inserted after Article 172 of the Criminal Code:
"Art.172 bis
Group sexual violence
Group sexual violence consists in the participation, by at least two persons gathered together, in acts of violation of sexual freedom referred to in Article 171.  
Participation in acts of violation of sexual freedom also exists for those who do not materially carry out the violence, but who have nevertheless provided a causal contribution to the commission of the offence, consisting also in mere presence at the place and time when the violence is perpetrated.  
Anyone who commits acts of group sexual violence is punished with imprisonment of the fourth degree and with a fine.  

The penalty is reduced by one degree for the participant whose contribution has been of minimal importance in the preparation or execution of the offence, as well as for one who was induced to commit the offence when the conditions established by numbers 2 and 3 of Article 90 concur.".

#### Art. 12

(Prosecutability in offences of violation of sexual freedom)
Article 178 of the Criminal Code is replaced as follows:
"For the prosecutability of the offences provided for in Articles 171, 172, 172 bis, 173, 175, 176 and 177, a complaint by the victim is required.  
Withdrawal of the complaint can no longer be made after the expiry of the term referred to in the third paragraph of Article 7 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.  
In the case where the victim is a minor, the limitation period for the offence, as well as the time limit for filing the complaint, begins to run from the attainment of the age of majority.  
Proceedings are initiated ex officio if the act is committed by an ascendant, guardian or adopter or by the person who has care or custody of the victim.  
Proceedings are also initiated ex officio if the act is committed in conjunction with an offence prosecutable ex officio, or in case of repeated recidivism".

#### Art. 13

(Persecutory acts – Stalking-Mobbing)
The following is inserted after Article 181 of the Criminal Code:

"Art. 181 bis
Persecutory acts

Anyone who repeatedly harasses or threatens a person so as to cause them serious moral suffering and damage to their dignity, to the point of disturbing their normal living conditions or placing them in a state of subjection or serious physical or psychological distress, or of justified fear for the personal safety of themselves or others connected to them by stable emotional relationships, is punished, upon complaint by the victim, with imprisonment of the first degree and a fine.  
Where the harassment or threats referred to in the first paragraph are carried out in the workplace, in the form of systematic and repeated bullying and vexatious practices carried out by the employer or by colleagues with the aim of professionally devaluing, humiliating, isolating a worker in an attempt to induce them, after having caused them serious psycho-physical suffering, to resign, the penalty is increased by one degree.  
Proceedings are initiated upon complaint by the victim.  
Proceedings are initiated ex officio and the penalty of imprisonment is increased by one degree if the act is committed with a weapon, with violence or with a threat of particular gravity.  
Proceedings are also initiated ex officio if the act is connected with another offence for which ex officio prosecutability is provided.".

#### Art. 14

(Abduction and retention of a minor abroad)
The following is added after Article 231 of the Criminal Code:

"Art. 231 bis
Abduction and retention of a minor abroad

Anyone who abducts a minor from the person who has custody, regardless of the exercise of parental authority, taking them abroad or failing to have them return to San Marino, against the will of the same parent or guardian is punished with imprisonment of the second degree and a fine.  
If the act is committed against a minor who has reached the age of fourteen and with their consent, imprisonment of the first degree applies.  
If the act is committed by one of the parents, disqualification of the second degree from parental authority also applies.".

#### Art. 15

(Ill-treatment of family members and cohabitants)
Article 235 of the Criminal Code is replaced by the following:

"Ill-treatment of family members and cohabitants

Anyone who ill-treats a member of the family or in any case a cohabitant, or a person subject to their authority or entrusted to them, is punished with imprisonment of the second degree.  
The imprisonment is of the third degree if the act is committed against a person under the age of fourteen.  
The imprisonment is of the fourth degree if the act results in one of the aggravating events indicated in Article 156 and of the fifth degree if death results therefrom".

### CAPO III

JUDICIAL MEASURES FOR THE PROTECTION AND SAFETY OF VICTIMS

## TITOLO I GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Art. 16

(Protection of the victim's privacy)
In civil or criminal proceedings relating to violence against the person, including in the family context, the privacy and intimacy of the victim, their personal data, those of their children or of any other person who is in their custody must be protected.  
The disclosure of the personal details and image of victims is always prohibited.  
Anyone who discloses or publishes data, information, news or images in violation of the preceding prohibitions is punished with a fine of 12,000.00 euros.

#### Art. 17

(Legal assistance)
In all proceedings, civil, criminal or administrative, the victim of violence is guaranteed legal assistance, even outside the conditions for free legal aid, when they are not objectively able to provide for their own procedural defence.

The Order of Lawyers and Notaries shall establish a list of registered experts available to accept appointments in favour of victims, communicating it to the competent Social Services, the Police Forces, the Court and the Authority for Equal Opportunities.
The Order of Lawyers and Notaries shall provide permanent and specific training for those registered on the list, organising interdisciplinary training courses.
The lawyer registered on the list may not refuse the appointment, except for serious and proven reasons.
Legal assistance is free of charge. The lawyer, however, is entitled to payment of fees at the expense of the guilty party or of the person who committed the act, when the violence has been established by final civil or criminal judgment or results from the records by means of a specific declaration issued in adversarial proceedings by the Judge ordering the dismissal of the criminal proceedings or civil case, or when protection orders have been issued against them.
In cases of absolute necessity and urgency, legal assistance to the victim shall be provided by the Court-Appointed Lawyer, who shall promptly contact a lawyer registered on the list, by whom, once the urgency has ceased, they shall be replaced.
Procedural acts performed in the interest of the victim of violence are exempt from all taxes.
The State shall advance the expenses necessary for the performance of or participation in procedural acts, including judicial expert reports, necessary for the protection of the victim of violence. The State has the right of recourse against the guilty party when the violence has been established by final civil or criminal judgment or results from the records by means of a specific declaration issued in adversarial proceedings by the Judge ordering the dismissal of the criminal proceedings or civil case, or when protection orders have been issued against them.

## TITOLO II

PROTECTION MEASURES IN CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS

#### Art. 18

(Representation of minors in criminal proceedings)
Where the victim of offences against personal freedom or of ill-treatment is a minor and the act is committed by an ascendant, guardian, adoptive parent, or by other family members or third parties having significant relationships with the minor or with their parents, the representation of the minor in the proceedings, for the protection of their rights, shall be entrusted to a special guardian, specifically appointed by the Guardianship Judge, to whom the Investigating Judge must immediately make a request.
If the offence referred to in the first paragraph is not prosecutable ex officio, the complaint shall be filed by the special guardian, and the time limit for filing the complaint shall run from the appointment.
Procedural acts detrimental to the interest of the minor in which the special guardian has not participated shall be null and void.
For the legal assistance of the minor represented by the guardian, the provisions of Article 17 of this law shall apply.

#### Art. 19

(Reporting obligations)
Social Services, Police Forces, and all persons exercising a health profession, whether public or private, are required to report to the Commissioner of Law exercising the functions of civil Guardianship Judge acts of violence against women, minors or gender-based violence, of which they become

aware by reason of their office or profession, even if these are offences prosecutable upon complaint by the victim.
Teachers of any order and level are required to promptly report to the Minor Service the acts referred to in the above paragraph of which they have become aware.
The report does not constitute a violation of official or professional secrecy; the Commissioner of Law shall ensure that the report and the proceedings are kept confidential.
Violation of the reporting obligation shall result in an administrative pecuniary sanction imposed by the Commissioner of Law of 500 euros.
The Commissioner of Law, having received the report, shall instruct the Social Services to carry out all appropriate verifications; upon completion, on the basis of the specific report prepared, shall summon the victim, and, if necessary, activate the protection measures provided for by this law, instructing the competent Services.
Where the acts constitute offences prosecutable ex officio, or, if requiring private initiative, in the event that the victim has filed a complaint, the report referred to in the first paragraph must be made to the Commissioner of Law Investigating Judge, who shall activate the protection measures and programmes, if necessary. If the victim of the offence of violence is a minor, the Investigating Judge is required to give prompt notification of the report of the offence to the Guardianship Judge, for measures within their competence.

#### Art. 20

(Right of intervention in criminal proceedings)
In proceedings for violence against women, against minors or gender-based violence, the Authority for Equal Opportunities has the right to intervene and to constitute itself as a civil party.
For this purpose, the Investigating Judge shall give prompt notification of the existence of the criminal proceedings to the Authority for Equal Opportunities.

#### Art. 21

(Prohibition of questions on the private life or sexuality of victims)
In criminal proceedings for sexual offences, including during police investigations, questions or the formulation of expert questions on the private life or sexuality of the injured person are not permitted unless they are necessary for the reconstruction of the facts.

#### Art. 22

(Special precautionary measures in criminal proceedings)
The Investigating Judge, when proceeding for an offence against personal safety, personal freedom or family mistreatment, committed by a cohabiting person, at the request of the injured person, may order the removal of the suspect or the accused from the family home, with an order not to return and not to access it without his authorization, indicating, if necessary, the terms of visitation.
The Investigating Judge, when there are needs to protect the safety of the injured person or his close relatives, always at the request of the injured person, may also prescribe to the suspect or the accused not to approach certain places habitually frequented by the injured person, in particular the place of work, the domicile of the family of origin or of close relatives, unless the attendance is necessary for work reasons. In this latter case the Judge prescribes the relevant terms and may impose limitations.

The Judge, having received the request, obtains all appropriate information, and provides by reasoned decree, having heard the accused and, if necessary, the applicant, except in cases of urgency.
The Investigating Judge, at the request of the injured person, and in compliance with the adversarial principle, may also order the periodic payment of an allowance in favor of cohabiting persons who, as a result of the precautionary measure ordered, are left without adequate means. The Judge determines the amount of the allowance taking into account the circumstances and income of the obligor and establishes the methods and terms of payment. He may order, if necessary, that the allowance be paid directly to the beneficiary by the employer of the obligor, deducting it from the remuneration due to him. The payment order has the effect of an enforcement order.
The measures referred to in the second and fourth paragraphs may also be adopted subsequently to the measure referred to in the first paragraph, provided that the latter has not been revoked or has not otherwise lost effect. Such measures, even if adopted subsequently, lose effect if the measure referred to in the first paragraph is revoked or otherwise loses effect. The measure referred to in the fourth paragraph, if in favor of the spouse or children, also loses effect if the measure of the Civil Judge in the judicial separation proceedings or another measure of the Civil Judge regarding the economic-patrimonial relations between the spouses or the maintenance of the children supervenes.
The measure referred to in the fourth paragraph may be modified if the conditions of the obligor or the beneficiary change, and is revoked if cohabitation resumes.

#### Art. 23

(Psychological support for victims of violence in criminal proceedings and other protective measures in criminal investigation)
When proceeding for offences against personal safety, personal freedom or for mistreatment committed to the detriment of the person, psychological support must be ensured to the victim by experts when the victim is heard as a witness or during confrontations with the suspect or with other witnesses.
When in proceedings for one of the offences referred to in the first paragraph a judicial and medico-legal expert examination must be carried out, the expert must be chosen, preferably, from professionals of the same sex as the victim.
The hearing of the injured person must take place in a manner that avoids repetition of the same. To this end the Investigating Judge adopts every appropriate measure, including video recording of the hearing.
In the case where the injured person is a minor, the Investigating Judge proceeds with the examination of the victim of the offence, in confrontation with the accused or with witnesses, through the use of a one-way mirror and an intercom system or through the use of other suitable instruments that guarantee confidentiality; the hearing must be video recorded. The minor must always be assisted by an expert in child psychology auxiliary to the Judge.

#### Art. 24

(Protection of victims at trial)
In criminal proceedings for offences against personal safety, personal freedom or for mistreatment, the trial always takes place in closed session if the injured person is a minor, and at the request of the injured person who is an adult.
Testimony and confrontations are not repeated if the right to defense of the accused was guaranteed during the investigation, and, in any case, when there is video recording.

In the event that the hearing or confrontation must be repeated, the provisions referred to in Article 23 are observed. If the injured person is a minor, repetition may not be ordered when there is a concrete danger of worsening the minor's condition, which must be ascertained through judicial expert examination, in adversarial proceedings with the technical consultants of the parties.

#### Art. 25

(Probation to social services for those convicted of sexual and family violence)
Probation to social services for those convicted of sexual or family violence, in cases where it is permitted by law, may take place only if accompanied by the participation of the convicted person in a specific rehabilitation program.

## TITOLO III MISURE DI TUTELA CIVILE

#### Art. 26

(Protection against family abuse)
When the conduct of the spouse or other cohabitant causes serious harm to the physical or moral integrity or to the freedom of the other spouse or cohabitant, the Judge, when the act does not constitute an offence prosecutable ex officio or, if by private initiative, when the complaint has not been filed, upon application by a party, may adopt by decree one or more of the measures referred to in the following article.
In any case, the conduct referred to in the first paragraph constitutes just cause for removal from the family home pursuant to Article 30 of Law No. 49 of 26 April 1986.
The rules referred to in this title also apply, insofar as compatible, in the case where the prejudicial conduct was carried out by another member of the family unit other than the spouse or cohabitant. In that case the application is brought by the member of the family unit to whose detriment the prejudicial conduct is carried out.

#### Art. 27

(Protection orders against domestic abuse)
The Judge orders the spouse or cohabitant who has engaged in prejudicial conduct to cease such conduct, and orders the removal from the family home of the spouse or cohabitant who has engaged in the prejudicial conduct, also prescribing, where necessary, that they not approach places habitually frequented by the applicant, and in particular the place of work, the domicile of the family of origin, or the domicile of other close relatives or other persons and in proximity to the places of education of the couple's children, unless they must frequent the same places for work requirements.  
The Judge may also order, where necessary, the intervention of social services or a family mediation centre, as well as associations whose statutory purpose is the support and reception of women and minors or other subjects who are victims of abuse and mistreatment; the periodic payment of an allowance in favour of cohabiting persons who, as a result of the measures referred to in the first paragraph, are left without adequate means, fixing the methods and terms of payment and prescribing, if necessary, that the sum be paid directly to the entitled person by the obligor's employer, deducting it from the remuneration due to them.  

By the same decree the Judge, in the cases referred to in the preceding paragraphs, establishes the duration of the protection order, which runs from the day of its execution. This may not exceed six months and may be extended, upon application by a party, only if serious reasons exist for the time strictly necessary.  
By the same decree the Judge determines the methods of implementation. Where removal from the family home is ordered, the Commissioner of the Law provides for the assistance of the Police Forces and the coercive removal of the addressee of the order who does not comply voluntarily. The Commissioner of the Law may also indicate measures suitable to prevent subsequent violations of the provisions of the measure, including surveillance and the assistance of the Police Forces.  
The decree is always communicated to the Gendarmerie and to the Neuro-Psychiatric Service for the possible adoption of measures concerning weapons and ammunition.

#### Art. 28

(Procedure for protection orders against domestic abuse)
The application is also filed by the party personally. The Judge, in such case, having received the application, appoints a defence counsel from among those registered in the list referred to in the second paragraph of article 17.  
The Commissioner of the Law, having heard the parties, proceeds in the manner deemed most appropriate to the necessary evidentiary acts, acquiring, also ex officio, all appropriate information, and provides by reasoned decree immediately enforceable.  
In cases of urgency, the Judge, having obtained, where necessary, summary information, may immediately adopt the protection order, fixing the hearing for the appearance of the parties before them within a period not exceeding fifteen days. At the hearing the Judge confirms, modifies or revokes the protection order.  
Against the decree by which the Judge adopts the protection order or rejects the application, pursuant to the second paragraph, or confirms, modifies or revokes the protection order previously adopted in the case referred to in the preceding paragraph, an appeal for nullity to the Civil Court of Appeal is permitted. The appeal does not suspend the execution of the protection order, unless the Court of Appeal decides otherwise.

#### Art. 29

(Sanctions)
Whoever evades the protection order provided for by article 27 of this law, or a measure of equal content adopted in the proceedings for legal separation of spouses or in the proceedings for dissolution or termination of the civil effects of marriage is punished with the penalty established by article 366 of the Criminal Code.

#### Art. 30

(Scope of application of protection orders)
The provisions of articles 27 and 28 of this law do not apply when the prejudicial conduct is engaged in by the spouse who has filed or against whom an application for legal separation or for dissolution or termination of the civil effects of marriage has been filed if in the related proceedings the hearing for the appearance of the spouses referred to in articles 110 and 127 of Law No. 49 of 26 April 1986 has taken place. In such case the Judge may adopt protection orders in such proceedings.  

The protection order adopted pursuant to articles 27 and 28 loses effect if a decree containing temporary and urgent measures is subsequently pronounced, in the proceedings for legal separation or dissolution or termination of the civil effects of marriage initiated by the applicant spouse or against them.

#### Art. 31

(Suspension of parental authority)
When the act of violence is committed to the detriment of minors, and until the establishment of responsibility, the Commissioner of the Law may suspend the exercise of parental authority from the suspect or from the parent who has tolerated the violence.

## TITLE IV

PREVENTIVE INTERVENTIONS BY THE POLICE FORCES

#### Art. 32

(Request for assistance to the Police Forces)
Where the victim of violence or third parties who have witnessed the fact report the violence to the Police Forces, they must intervene immediately, and in any case not later than one hour from the report, unless serious reasons exist.  
The Police Forces may enter the victim's dwelling or other places or appurtenances of private property where the victim is located, even coercively; they must put an end to the harmful behaviour; they must inform the victim of their rights, including the right to request protection orders; if there is fear of serious and irreparable harm, they must make an immediate report to the competent social services, unless it is an offence prosecutable ex officio or the victim has filed a complaint, in which case the report must be made to the Examining Judge, who may adopt appropriate precautionary measures, including those indicated by this law.  
The Police Forces in any case proceed to seize weapons present in the abuser's dwelling, giving notice thereof to the Commissioner of the Law and to the Command of the Gendarmerie for the activation of the procedure for suspension or revocation of the gun licence or hunting licence.  
The obligation of immediate intervention by the Police Forces also exists in the case where they receive from anyone a report of abusing subjects who are or are about to drive vehicles while intoxicated, adopting the necessary preventive and precautionary measures.  
If the request for intervention concerns acts of persecution as defined by law, the Police Forces, even independently of the complaint of the injured person, are required to remove the harasser, making the reports referred to in this law.  
For all interventions a specific report must be drawn up, which must be transmitted to the Command of the Gendarmerie and to the Neuropsychiatric Service.  
The data thus collected are transmitted to the Authority for Equal Opportunities and are also available to the Civil Judge requested to issue protection orders.

#### Art. 33

Article 3 of Law No. 26 of 25 February 2004 is amended as follows:

"Art. 3 (Composition)

The Commission is appointed by the Grand and General Council at the beginning of the legislature and for its duration. It is composed of: eight members chosen proportionally from the groups present in the Grand and General Council; one member designated by the Trade Union Organizations; one member designated by the Consulta of San Marino Cultural Associations and Cooperatives.  
The members of the Commission may not be members of the Grand and General Council.".

#### Art. 34

(Transitional provision)
The Commission for Equal Opportunities established by Law No. 26 of 25 February 2004 is replaced for the competences provided by this law by the Authority for Equal Opportunities.  
The Commission for Equal Opportunities in the exercise of the functions and powers provided in article 2 points a, g, i, j, k, l of Law No. 26 of 25 February 2004, from the entry into force of this law, shall be assisted by the Authority for Equal Opportunities.  
The Authority for Equal Opportunities is composed of three members appointed by the Grand and General Council who remain in office for 4 years from among experts in legal matters, representatives of associations or non-governmental organizations active in the field of Equal Opportunities, communication and psychology experts.  
The composition of the Authority for Equal Opportunities must ensure the participation of each of the professional figures identified above.  
The Authority for Equal Opportunities collects data relating to violence against women and gender-based violence on a six-monthly basis.  
The Authority for Equal Opportunities prepares an annual report which must be publicly disseminated.

#### Art. 35

(Entry into force)
This law enters into force on the fifth day following that of its legal publication.
