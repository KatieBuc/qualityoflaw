Law on Protection from Domestic Violence of FBiH
LAW
ON PROTECTION FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
("Official Gazette of FBiH", No. 20/2013 and 75/2021)
I. BASIC PROVISIONS

## Article 1

Subject of the Law
This law regulates: protection from domestic violence, the concept of family and domestic violence, the type and purpose of protective measures for persons who have committed acts of domestic violence, the manner and procedure for imposing protective measures, protection of victims of domestic violence, mutual connection of all subjects that function in the protection from domestic violence and other issues of importance for protection from domestic violence.

## Article 2

Meaning of terms
(1)
Violent person
within the meaning of this law is a family member who commits acts of violence from Article 7 of this law.  
(2)
Victim of violence
within the meaning of this law is every family member who is exposed to acts of domestic violence from Article 7 of this law.  
(3)
Child
within the meaning of this law is every family member who has not reached 18 years of age.  
(4) Person of trust is a family member or other natural person or employee of the guardianship authority, institution competent for social and health protection, other public institution or non-governmental organization from Article 3 paragraph (5) of this law, in whom the victim of violence has trust.  
(5) Grammatical expressions used in this law to denote male or female gender include both genders.

## Article 3

General principles of providing protection and exemption from application of the law
The principles of protection from domestic violence regulated by this law and other regulations, as well as international standards accepted by Bosnia and Herzegovina that regulate the area of domestic violence, shall be applied in all cases in order to ensure the most effective protection for victims of domestic violence.  
The application and implementation of this law in accordance with their competences shall be ensured by the municipal court - misdemeanor department of the court (hereinafter: competent court), police, guardianship authority and other institutions competent for social and health protection, with the obligation of joint and coordinated action.  
The institutions from paragraph 2 of this article should ensure that procedures with victims of violence and violent persons are carried out by employees educated to work with the mentioned persons.

## Article 4

Urgency of resolution
For cases of domestic violence, the competent subjects from Article 3 paragraph 2 of this law are obliged to ensure urgency in resolution.

## Article 5

Access to the competent court
This law ensures unhindered access to the competent court, without any costs for victims of domestic violence.  
II. CONCEPT OF FAMILY AND DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## Article 6

Concept of family and family relations
The family, within the meaning of this law, consists of:
1) marital and extramarital partners and their children (joint or from previous unions),
2) relatives: blood relatives and relatives from full adoption relationships in direct line without limitation, and in collateral line up to and including the fourth degree; stepfather, stepmother; adoptee and adopter from incomplete adoption relationships; relatives by affinity up to and including the second degree,
3) guardian and ward, foster parent and foster child,
4) former marital and extramarital partners and their children (joint or from previous unions) and their parents, including stepfather and stepmother.  
Relations among family members are based on humane principles that imply mutual respect, assistance, affection, maintenance of harmonious relations while developing and expressing the best qualities, while bearing in mind especially the obligation to protect children, respect gender equality and voluntariness of entering into marriage and extramarital union.  
In mutual relations, family members shall respect the rights, freedoms and security of other family members in a manner that they will not limit, prevent or obstruct them in the realization of their rights and freedoms that family members have according to existing regulations.  
A family member shall refrain from violating the physical or mental integrity of another family member, from violation and discrimination based on gender and age and from placing in a state of subordination on any basis.

## Article 7

Concept of domestic violence
Within the meaning of this law, it shall be considered that domestic violence exists if there are grounds for suspicion that acts have been committed by which a family member inflicts physical, mental or sexual pain or suffering and/or economic damage, as well as threats that cause fear of physical, mental or sexual violence and/or economic damage to another family member.  
Acts of domestic violence or threats of such acts, within the meaning of paragraph 1 of this article, are:
1) any application of physical force on the physical or mental integrity of a family member,
2) any conduct of one family member that may cause or provoke danger that it will cause physical or mental pain or suffering,
3) causing fear or personal endangerment or violation of dignity of a family member by blackmail or other coercion,
4) physical attack of a family member on another family member, regardless of whether physical injury occurred or not,
5) verbal attack, insult, swearing, calling by derogatory names, and other ways of rude harassment of a family member by another family member,
6) sexual harassment,
7) stalking and all other similar forms of harassment of another family member,
8) damage or destruction of joint property or property in possession,
9) use of physical violence or causing fear with the aim of depriving the right to economic independence by prohibition of work or keeping a family member in a relationship of dependence or subordination,
10) use of physical and mental violence towards children and neglect in their upbringing,
11) physical and mental violence towards elderly, infirm persons and neglect in their care and treatment,
12) violent isolation or restriction of freedom of movement of a family member and
13) omission of due care and failure to provide assistance and protection to a family member despite the obligation under the law.

## Article 8

Reporting of domestic violence
Health and social workers, teachers, educators, medical, educational and other institutions and bodies, as well as non-governmental organisations which, in the performance of their duties, become aware of committed acts of domestic violence from Article 7 paragraph 2 of this law, are obliged to report the committed acts of domestic violence to the competent police administration immediately upon becoming aware of them.  
The report from paragraph 1 of this Article must also be submitted by family members, as well as any citizen who becomes aware of committed acts of domestic violence, to the competent police administration.  
The report may also be submitted by the victim of domestic violence.  
The report from paragraph 1 of this Article must also be submitted by family members, as well as any citizen who becomes aware of committed acts of domestic violence from Article 7 paragraph 2 of this law, particularly if the victim of violence is a child.  
A person who fails to fulfil the obligation to report acts of domestic violence from Article 7 paragraph 2 of this law commits a misdemeanour, except in cases where the domestic violence is reported by the victim of violence.  
III. TYPES AND PURPOSE OF PROTECTIVE MEASURES

## Article 9

Types of protective measures
The following protective measures shall be imposed on perpetrators of domestic violence:
1) removal from an apartment, house or other residential space and prohibition of return to the apartment, house or other residential space,
2) prohibition of approaching the victim of violence,
3) prohibition of harassment and stalking of the person exposed to violence,
4) mandatory psychosocial treatment,
5) mandatory treatment for addiction,
6) temporary deprivation of liberty and detention.

## Article 10

Purpose of protective measures
The purpose of protective measures is to ensure the necessary protection of the health and safety of persons exposed to violence, prevent domestic violence, and undertake effective measures for the re-education and treatment of violent persons.

## Article 11

Removal from an apartment, house or other residential space
The protective measure of removal from an apartment, house or other residential space and prohibition of return to the apartment, house or other residential space may be imposed on a person who has committed violence against a family member with whom they live in an apartment, house or other residential space, if the competent court assesses that there is a danger that without the implementation of this measure the violent person could commit acts of violence again.  
A person to whom the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article has been imposed is obliged to immediately leave the apartment, house or other residential space, as necessary, in the presence of a police officer.  
The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined for a duration which may not be shorter than one month nor longer than two years.  
The regulation on the manner of implementation of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be adopted by the federal minister of internal affairs.

## Article 12

Prohibition of approaching the victim of domestic violence
The protective measure of prohibition of approaching the victim of domestic violence may be imposed on a person who has committed domestic violence.  
In the decision by which the court imposes the measure of prohibition of approaching the victim of domestic violence, the competent court shall determine the places or areas, and the distance below which the violent person may not approach the victim of domestic violence.  
The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined for a duration which may not be shorter than one month nor longer than two years, unless the court decides that a longer time period is in the interest of the victim of violence.  
The regulation on the manner of implementation of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be adopted by the federal minister of internal affairs.

## Article 13

Prohibition of harassment and stalking of the person exposed to violence
The protective measure of prohibition of harassment and stalking may be imposed on a person who harasses or stalks a family member, and there is a danger that such behaviour will be repeated.  
The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined for a duration which may not be shorter than one month nor longer than two years, unless the court decides that a longer time period is in the interest of the victim of violence.  
The regulation on the manner of implementation of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be adopted by the federal minister of internal affairs.

## Article 14

Mandatory psychosocial treatment
The protective measure of mandatory psychosocial treatment may be imposed on a violent person for the purpose of eliminating the causes of their violent behaviour or if there is a danger that this person will repeat the violence.  
The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined for a duration which may not be shorter than six months continuously nor longer than two years.  
The regulation on the manner and place of implementation of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be adopted by the federal minister of labour and social policy, with the consent of the federal minister of health.

## Article 15

Mandatory treatment for addiction
The protective measure of mandatory treatment for addiction shall be imposed by the competent court on a violent person who committed violence under the influence of addiction to alcohol, narcotic drugs or other psychoactive substances, if there is a danger that the acts of violence will be repeated.  
The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined for a duration which may not be shorter than one month nor longer than two years.  
The regulation on the manner and place of implementation of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be adopted by the federal minister of health.

## Article 16

Deprivation of liberty and detention
The police administration is obliged to go to the scene immediately upon receipt of a report for every reported case of domestic violence.  
The police administration is obliged to deprive of liberty and detain any person for whom there are grounds for suspicion that they have committed domestic violence, if the conditions from Article 153 of the Law on Criminal Procedure of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina ("Official Gazette of the Federation of BiH", Nos. 35/03, 37/03, 56/03, 78/04, 28/05, 55/06, 27/07, 53/07, 9/09 and 12/10) are met.  
IV. IMPOSITION OF PROTECTIVE MEASURES

## Article 17

Submission of requests for imposition of protective measures
The request for imposition of a protective measure shall be submitted to the competent court by the police administration.  
Exceptionally, the request from paragraph 1 of this Article may also be submitted by the prosecutor's office when there are justified reasons for doing so.

## Article 18

Deadlines for submission of requests and mandatory elements of the request for imposition of a protective measure
The police administration is obliged to submit a request for imposition of a protective measure to the competent court within 12 hours of becoming aware of acts of violence from Article 7 of this law for every reported case of domestic violence.  
The request from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be accompanied by collected evidence and necessarily an extract from the official records if that person has been reported for cases of domestic violence.  
The request for imposition of protective measures shall be submitted to the competent court according to the place in whose territory the victim of violence has residence or domicile.  
The mandatory elements of the request for imposition of a protective measure are: full name of the body submitting the request; protocol number; place and date; name of the court to which the request is submitted; legal basis on which the request is submitted; basic data about the violent person for whom the imposition of a protective measure is requested; name of the protective measure(s) which is/are requested from the court to impose; acts of violence from Article 7 for which there are grounds for suspicion that the violent person committed them; name and surname of the victim(s) and relationship with the violent person; reasoning; signature of the authorised official person and attachments to the request.

## Article 19

Deadlines for imposition of protective measures, manner of imposition and legal remedies
The competent court is obliged to act upon the request and issue a decision within 12 hours of receipt of the request for imposition of a protective measure from Article 9 points 1), 2) and 3) of this law.  
The competent court is obliged to secure an expert opinion, if necessary, within seven days of receipt of the request for imposition of protective measures from Article 9 points 4) and 5) of this law, and to act upon the request and issue a decision.  
If the competent court requires that the violent person be brought before the court, it may summon that person through the competent police administration.  
When imposing a protective measure, the competent court is not limited by special formal evidentiary rules for the purpose of establishing facts about the commission of acts of violence from Article 7 of this law, as well as the consequences that have occurred.  
The decision on imposition of a protective measure determines the duration of the protective measure imposed on the violent person, which begins to run from the day of issuing the decision.

## Član 20

Imposition of protective measure and replacement with another protective measure
The competent court may impose multiple protective measures on the perpetrator if it deems it justified.  
The competent court, following a report on the execution of the protective measure submitted by the authorities responsible for the execution of the imposed protective measure, may replace the imposed measure with another protective measure, revoke the decision imposing the protective measure, or impose a monetary fine for failure to comply with the imposed protective measure from Article 45 of this law.

## Član 21

Mandatory elements of the decision imposing a protective measure
The mandatory elements of the decision from Article 19, paragraph 1 of this law are:
1) name of the court;  
2) number and date;  
3) introduction containing:
a) name of the court issuing the decision,
b) number and date of issuance of the decision imposing the protective measure,
c) legal basis for jurisdiction and issuance of the decision,
d) name of the authority that submitted the request for imposition of the protective measure, as well as a brief content of the request;  
4) operative part containing:
a) personal data about the person to whom the protective measure is imposed,
b) acts of violence from Article 7 of this law on the basis of which the protective measure is imposed, with indication of the factual description of acts of violence, with mandatory indication of personal data of the victim/s of domestic violence,
c) type of imposed protective measure,
d) duration of the protective measure,
e) name of the authority responsible for execution of the protective measure,
f) statement that appeal does not delay execution of the decision;  
5) reasoning containing:
a) brief presentation of the request for imposition of the protective measure,
b) attached evidence and factual situation,
c) reasons that were decisive in the assessment of evidence,
d) reasons which, in view of the established factual situation, indicate the justification of the type and duration of the imposed protective measure,
e) legal basis for imposition of the protective measure, as well as the basis from this law according to which appeal does not delay execution of the decision;  
6) instruction on legal remedy containing:
a) name of the court through which the appeal is submitted, as well as the name of the court to which the appeal is submitted and within what time limit and
7) signature of the judge.

## Član 22

Service of decision
The decision imposing a protective measure shall be served by the competent court without delay to the person to whom the protective measure is imposed, the applicant, the authority responsible for execution of the imposed protective measure and the victim of domestic violence.  
The court is obliged to serve the decision imposing protective measures to the guardianship authority in whose area the person to whom the protective measure is imposed has residence or domicile for the purpose of registration no later than within three days from the issuance of the decision.  
Član 22a
The authorities responsible for execution of the imposed protective measure specified in the decision imposing the protective measure are obliged to regularly monitor the execution of the measure, prepare and submit to the competent court a report on their execution, propose termination or replacement of the measure when they deem it necessary. The authority responsible for execution of the protective measure is obliged to notify the competent court upon learning of non-execution or violation of the imposed measure.

## Član 23

Termination of proceedings
The court shall dismiss the request for imposition of a protective measure and terminate the proceedings when it is established that:
1) the acts stated in the request do not constitute acts established in Article 7 of this law,
2) the court does not have jurisdiction to impose a protective measure,
3) the request was not submitted by an authorized authority.

## Član 24

Filing an appeal
An appeal against the decision imposing a protective measure may be filed by the person to whom the protective measure is imposed and the authorized authorities from Article 17 of this law.

## Član 25

Time limits for filing an appeal and content of the appeal
Against the decision from Article 19, paragraph 1 of this law, an appeal is allowed within three days from the date of receipt of the decision. The appeal does not delay execution of the decision.  
Against the decision from Article 19, paragraph 2 of this law, an appeal is allowed within five days from the date of receipt of the decision. The appeal does not delay execution of the decision.  
The appeal from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article must contain the number and date of the decision against which it is filed, the signature of the appellant, as well as the reasons for which it is filed.

## Član 26

Grounds for appeal
The decision on the imposed protective measure may be challenged due to:
1) essential violation of the procedure prescribed by this law,
2) violation of substantive provisions of this law,
3) decision on the imposed protective measure.

## Član 27

Essential violations of procedure
Essential violations of the procedure for imposing a protective measure exist if:
1) the decision imposing the protective measure was made by a judge who should have been excluded from the procedure for imposing the protective measure,
2) the court did not have subject-matter jurisdiction to impose the protective measure,
3) the request for imposition of the protective measure was submitted by an unauthorized authority,
4) the court failed to apply or incorrectly applied the provisions of this law which resulted in significant harm to the appellant.

## Član 28

Service of appeal
The appeal is submitted in two copies to the court that issued the decision imposing the protective measure.

## Član 29

Consideration of appeal
An untimely or incomplete appeal shall be dismissed by the second-instance court by decision, if the first-instance court has not done so.  
The second-instance court shall consider the appeal on the basis of the file and the appeal, without holding a hearing and is obliged to issue a decision within three days from the date of receipt of the appeal.

## Član 30

Deciding on appeal
The appeal is decided by a single judge.  
When deciding on the appeal, the second-instance court may accept or reject the appeal.  
The second-instance court serves copies of its decision to the parties by mail and returns the case file to the first-instance court.  
V. OTHER FORMS OF PROTECTION OF VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## Član 31

Ensuring basic living needs
A victim of domestic violence has the right to ensuring basic living needs in terms of necessary health, social and material care and the right to ensuring legal assistance for resolving social, economic and other status.  
Protection of the victim of domestic violence from paragraph 1 of this Article is ensured in accordance with the applicable laws that prescribe the right to health and social protection and realization of the right to legal assistance.

## Član 32

Financing basic forms of protection and temporary assistance to the victim
Funds for financing the basic form of protection of a victim of domestic violence who cannot immediately be provided protection in accordance with Article 31 of this law within the existing health and social protection systems, shall be ensured within temporary assistance.  
With the aim of realizing temporary assistance from paragraph 1 of this Article, the cantonal government shall adopt a regulation within six months from the date of entry into force of this law which shall prescribe the manner of realizing temporary forms of assistance, including legal assistance for victims of domestic violence.

## Član 33

Manner of accommodating the victim
In order to ensure physical protection and realization of the rights and interests of the victim of domestic violence, without fear and danger to life, the police and guardianship authority are obliged, with the prior consent of the victim, to temporarily accommodate the victim of domestic violence in a safe house/shelter (hereinafter: safe house), another appropriate institution or with another family.  
A victim of domestic violence is temporarily accommodated in a safe house at the request of the police or guardianship authority for a duration of up to six months at most.  
The time limit from paragraph 2 of this Article may be extended at the request of the safe house with the written consent of the competent guardianship authority.

## Član 34

Accommodating the victim in another appropriate institution or with another family
A victim of domestic violence is accommodated in another appropriate institution or with another family at the request of the guardianship authority when the guardianship authority assesses that it is more favorable for the victim and with the consent of the victim of violence.

## Article 35

Establishment and financing of safe houses
A safe house may be established by a legal or natural person in accordance with the law.  
Funds for financing temporary accommodation of victims of violence in a safe house, other appropriate institution or with another family shall be secured in accordance with the regulations of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina and the canton in percentages from:
1) the canton budget in the amount of 30%,
2) the budget of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina in the amount of 70%.  
The criteria and standards for the establishment, operation and financing of safe houses shall be prescribed by the federal minister of labour and social policy within six months from the date of entry into force of this law.

## Article 36

Strategy for the prevention of domestic violence
The Government of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina shall adopt a strategy for the prevention of violence in the family which will define strategic objectives for the prevention/suppression of domestic violence and the manner of securing funds for financing the implementation of strategic objectives.

## Article 37

Programme of measures for the prevention, protection and combating of domestic violence
The cantonal government shall adopt a two-year programme of measures for the prevention, protection and combating of domestic violence for the territory of the canton.  
The programme of measures from paragraph 1 of this article shall mandatorily contain:
1) obligations of cantonal and municipal authorities with the aim of preventive action, suppression of all forms of domestic violence and protection of victims of violence;  
2) measures necessary for work with the violent person, including counselling support to all family members;  
3) activities on the promotion of non-violent behaviour;  
4) activities on the education of police officers, judges, representatives of guardianship authorities, health workers, teachers, educators and other subjects in the field of domestic violence;  
5) the obligation to maintain statistical databases on violent persons and victims of domestic violence, taking into account the protection of personal data;  
6) the manner of securing funds for financing the programme;  
7) the obligation to establish a coordination body which will coordinate the work of all competent institutions in the implementation of this programme of measures and
8) all necessary measures for the protection and safety of victims of violence in a safe house.  
The programme of measures from paragraph 1 of this article shall be adopted by the cantonal government within six months from the date of entry into force of this law.

## Article 38

Financing of programmes/projects
Financing of certain programmes/projects whose aim is to create conditions for preventive action and improvement of conditions for the protection of victims of violence shall be secured from the budget of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina if those programmes are of interest to the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina.  
VI. MULTIDISCIPLINARY APPROACH IN THE PROCEDURE FOR PROTECTION OF VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND IMPLEMENTATION OF IMPOSED PROTECTIVE MEASURES

## Article 39

Protocol on cooperation
The competent institutions from Article 8 paragraph 1 of this law as well as the competent judicial authorities are obliged to sign a protocol on cooperation for the territory of one or more municipalities which will establish mutual rights and obligations in the procedure for reporting cases of domestic violence, providing protection to victims of domestic violence, as well as in work with violent persons.

## Article 40

Keeping of records
The Police Administration is obliged to keep records on reported cases of domestic violence and on the implementation of imposed protective measures from Articles 11, 12 and 13 of this law.  
The regulation on the manner of implementation of measures from Articles 11, 12 and 13 of this law shall contain the necessary records and a form for keeping records from paragraph 1 of this article.  
The competent court is obliged to keep records on submitted requests for the imposition of protective measures and on imposed protective measures.  
The content and form of keeping records from paragraph 3 of this article shall be prescribed by the federal minister of justice within 60 days from the date of entry into force of this law.  
The guardianship authority is obliged to keep records on imposed protective measures, persons who are protected by a protective measure and violent persons to whom protective measures have been imposed.  
The content and form of records from paragraph 5 of this article shall be prescribed by the federal minister of labour and social policy within 60 days from the date of adoption of this law.  
The regulation on the manner of implementation of the measure from Article 15 of this law shall also contain the necessary records on imposed measures and a form for keeping those records.  
All records from this article must be kept in accordance with Article 18 of the Law on Gender Equality in Bosnia and Herzegovina ("Official Gazette of BiH", Nos. 16/03 and 102/09).  
The records from this article shall mandatorily be submitted to the Gender Centre of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina no later than 10 January for the previous year.

## Article 41

Obligations of the Police Administration
The Police Administration is obliged to immediately notify the guardianship authority of a case of domestic violence, as well as to submit official notes that it keeps related to cases of domestic violence for the purpose of records and prevention.  
Upon learning of non-compliance with an imposed protective measure by the person to whom a protective measure has been imposed, the Police Administration is obliged to notify the competent court without delay with a proposal for the imposition of a monetary fine in accordance with Article 45 of this law.

## Article 42

Obligations of the guardianship authority
The guardianship authority is obliged to monitor the execution of the imposed measure, inform the competent court about the execution of the measure, propose termination or extension or replacement of the measure with another measure.  
The guardianship authority is obliged to submit a report to the court on the execution of the protective measure no later than within six months, and at the request of the court, as needed, earlier.  
VII. SUPERVISION OVER THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THIS LAW

## Article 43

Supervision over implementation
Supervision over the implementation of this law shall be carried out by the Federal Ministry of Justice.  
The Government of the Federation is obliged to submit once a year a regular annual report on the implementation of the Law on Protection from Domestic Violence to the Parliament of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina.  
VIII. PENAL PROVISIONS

## Article 44

Misdemeanour sanctions
An official person who does not act in accordance with Article 8 paragraph 1 of this law shall be punished for a misdemeanour with a monetary fine in the amount from 500.00 KM to 1,500.00 KM.  
Persons who do not act in accordance with Article 8 paragraph 2 of this law shall be punished for a misdemeanour with a monetary fine in the amount from 100.00 KM to 500.00 KM.  
An official person who does not act in accordance with Article 8 paragraph 1 of this law, if a child is a victim of acts of domestic violence from Article 7 paragraph 2 of this law, shall be punished for a misdemeanour with a monetary fine in the amount from 500.00 KM to 3,000.00 KM.  
Persons who do not act in accordance with Article 8 paragraph 2 of this law, if a child is a victim of acts of domestic violence from Article 7 paragraph 2 of this law, shall be punished for a misdemeanour with a monetary fine in the amount from 100.00 KM to 3,000.00 KM.

## Article 45

Non-compliance with imposed protective measures
A person who does not act in accordance with an imposed protective measure shall be punished for a misdemeanour with a monetary fine in the amount from 1,000.00 KM to 1,500.00 KM.  
IX. TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

## Article 46

Validity of by-laws
By-laws: the Rulebook on the manner of implementation of protective measures which are within the competence of the police ("Official Gazette of the Federation of BiH", No. 9/06), the Rulebook on the manner and place of implementation of the protective measure of mandatory psychosocial treatment of perpetrators of domestic violence ("Official Gazette of the Federation of BiH", No. 60/06) and the Rulebook on the manner and place of implementation of the protective measure of mandatory treatment for addiction to alcohol, narcotic drugs or other psychotropic substances of perpetrators of domestic violence ("Official Gazette of the Federation of BiH", No. 23/08) adopted on the basis of the Law from Article 47 of this law shall remain in force and shall be harmonised with the provisions of this law within three months from the date of entry into force of this law.

## Article 47

Cessation of validity
On the date of entry into force of this law, the Law on Protection from Domestic Violence ("Official Gazette of the Federation of BiH", Nos. 22/05 and 51/06) shall cease to be valid.

## Article 48

Entry into Force
This law shall enter into force on the eighth day from the day of publication in the "Official Gazette of the Federation of BiH".
