## Art. 2.

If penal legislation has changed from the time an act was committed until judgment is rendered, judgment shall be made according to the new law, both regarding the punishability of the act and the punishment. However, punishment may never be imposed unless there was authority for it in law when the act was committed, and not a more severe punishment than would have been imposed under that law. If a penal provision of law has been repealed for reasons that do not indicate a changed assessment by the legislature of the punishability of the act, judgment shall be made according to the law that was in force when the offence was committed.  

If the punishability of an act ceases for reasons other than those last mentioned, the punishment that has been imposed for that act shall lapse, to the extent that it has not already been executed. Also lapse then are other consequences of the act that its punishability under the former law entailed, with the exception of the obligation to pay legal costs. It may then be brought before the court that judged the case in the district court, or a court at the parties' domicile jurisdiction, whether the punishment according to the judgment shall lapse or be reduced, if the judgment also covers several offences. The decision of a district court may be appealed.

## Chapter II. General conditions for punishment.

## Article 12

An act is not punishable which persons perform in self-defence, to the extent that it has been necessary to defend against or avert an unlawful attack which has begun or is imminent, provided that defence has not been used which is manifestly more dangerous than the attack and the damage which could be expected from it gave cause for.

If a person has exceeded the limits of lawful self-defence, and the reason for this is that he has become so frightened or confused that he could not fully control himself, he shall not be punished.

## Article 13

An act is not punishable which necessity required to be performed for the purpose of protecting lawful interests against imminent danger, even though other interests are thereby impaired which must be considered to be of much lesser importance.

## Article 14

A person shall not be punished for an act which he committed before he reached the age of 15 years.

## Article 15

Persons shall not be punished who, due to mental illness, mental underdevelopment or deterioration, impairment of consciousness or other comparable condition, were completely incapable at the time they performed the act of controlling their actions.

## Article 16

If the person who performed the act was mentally below par, such as due to underdevelopment, deterioration, sexual maldevelopment or other disturbance, but this condition of his is not at as high a level as Article 15 provides, he shall then be punished for the offence if it may be expected according to circumstances and after medical opinion has been sought that punishment can be effective.

If an institution is established for such persons as this Article provides for, it may be determined in a criminal judgment that the convicted person shall serve [his sentence] 1) in the institution.

1) Act No. 82/1998, Article 1.

## Article 17

Punishment shall be applied even though an offence has been committed in intoxication or under the influence of other intoxicants. If there has been complete impairment of consciousness, however, punishment shall not be imposed unless the accused had known in advance or had complete reason to expect that he would commit the offence while under the influence, or that it would result from this condition of his.

## Article 18

An act for which punishment is imposed in this Act is not culpable unless it is performed intentionally or negligently. Negligent offences shall only be punished if there is specific authorization for this in the law.

## Article 19

If the punishability of an act, or increased punishment for it, is subject to the condition according to this Act that the act has resulted in specific consequences, that condition is not considered fulfilled unless the accused can at least be blamed for negligence regarding the consequences, or for not having done what he could to avert the danger which the act entailed when he became aware of it.

## Chapter III. Attempt and complicity.

## Article 20

Every person who has made a decision to perform an act for which punishment is imposed in this Act, and has unequivocally shown that intention in an act which is directed at or is intended to be directed at the execution of the offence, has, when the offence is not completed, become guilty of an attempt to commit it.

For an attempt to commit an offence, a lower punishment may be imposed than is prescribed for a completed offence. This shall especially be done when it may be inferred from the attempt that the offender is not as dangerous and his will not as hardened as may be expected to be the case with persons who complete such offences.

If the interests which the act is directed at, or the act itself, are such that the attempt could not have led to a completed offence, it may be determined that punishment shall lapse.

## Article 21

Punishment for an attempt lapses if the person who has become guilty of it voluntarily abandons his intention to commit the offence before it is completed, provided that its execution does not fail or the intended result is not achieved due to obstacles or other chance, and he has furthermore, if he has by his act caused or believed himself to have caused a risk that the offence would be completed, prevented it or taken such measures as would have prevented it if the completion of the offence had not, without his knowledge, been averted in another way or been unexecutable.

## 22. gr

Every person who, by assistance in word or deed, persuasion, incitement or in any other way, takes part in the commission of an offence under this Act, shall be subject to the punishment prescribed for the offence.

If the participation of any participant in the offence is minor, or consists in strengthening the intention of another person which has already been formed, and also when an offence is not completed or intended participation has failed, he may be sentenced to a lighter punishment than that prescribed for the offence.

When the circumstances are as stated in the second paragraph, and likewise if a person has through negligence come to take part in an offence, the punishment may be waived if the offence falls under a penal provision where no heavier punishment is prescribed than [imprisonment up to 1 year]. 1)

When an offence is completed, a person who provides assistance to the offender himself or to others in order to maintain the unlawful state which has been created by the offence, or benefits from it, shall be subject to punishment according to the provisions of this Article, provided that other provisions of law do not apply to his conduct.

1) Act No. 82/1998, Art. 2.

## 23. gr

A participant shall not be punished if he, in the manner referred to in Art. 21, prevents the offence or takes measures which would have prevented it if its execution had not, without his knowledge, been prevented in another way, failed or been impracticable.
