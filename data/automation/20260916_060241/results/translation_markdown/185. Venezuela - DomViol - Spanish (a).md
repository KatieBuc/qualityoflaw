# OFFICIAL GAZETTE OF THE BOLIVARIAN REPUBLIC OF VENEZUELA

OFFICIAL GAZETTE OF THE BOLIVARIAN REPUBLIC OF VENEZUELA

Caracas, Monday, April 23, 2007	No. 38.668 THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY
OF THE BOLIVARIAN REPUBLIC OF VENEZUELA

DECREES

the following,

ORGANIC LAW ON THE RIGHT OF WOMEN TO A LIFE FREE OF VIOLENCE

## CHAPTER I: GENERAL PROVISIONS

Purpose Article 1.- The purpose of this Law is to guarantee and promote the right of women to a life free of violence, creating conditions to prevent, address, punish and eradicate
violence against women in any of its manifestations and spheres, promoting changes in the sociocultural patterns that sustain gender inequality and power relations over women, in order to favor the construction of a just, democratic, participatory, equal and leading society.  
Guiding principles

#### Article 2

Through this Law, a comprehensive set of measures is articulated to achieve the following purposes:

1.	To guarantee all women the effective exercise of their enforceable rights before the organs and entities of the Public Administration, and to ensure rapid, transparent and effective access to the services established for that purpose.  
2.	To strengthen public policies for the prevention of violence against women and the eradication of gender discrimination. To this end, the Public Powers shall be provided with effective instruments in the educational, labor, social services, health, advertising and media spheres.  
3.	To strengthen the existing criminal and procedural framework to ensure comprehensive protection for women victims of violence from the jurisdictional instances.  
4.	To coordinate the budgetary and institutional resources of the different Public Powers to ensure the attention, prevention and eradication of acts of violence against women, as well as the

appropriate punishment of those responsible for them and the implementation of socio-educational measures to prevent their recurrence.  
5.	To promote the participation and collaboration of entities, associations and organizations that act against violence towards women.  
6.	To guarantee the principle of mainstreaming of awareness, prevention, detection, security and protection measures, so that in their application the rights, needs and specific demands of all women victims of gender violence are taken into account.  
7.	To promote the specialization and awareness of professional groups involved in the process of information, attention and protection of women victims of gender violence.  
8.	To guarantee the economic, professional, technological, scientific and any other resources that allow the sustainability of plans, projects, programs, actions, missions and any other initiative aimed at the prevention, punishment and eradication of violence against women and the full exercise of their rights.  
9.	To establish and strengthen security and protection measures, and precautionary measures that guarantee the rights protected in this Law and the personal, physical, emotional, labor and patrimonial protection of women victims of gender violence.  
10.	To establish a comprehensive system of guarantees for the exercise of the rights developed in this Law.  

Protected rights

#### Article 3.- This Law encompasses the protection of the following rights

1.	The right to life.  
2.	The protection of the dignity and physical, psychological, sexual, patrimonial and legal integrity of women victims of violence, in the public and private spheres.  
3.	The equality of rights between men and women.  
4.	The protection of women particularly vulnerable to gender-based violence.  
5.	The right of women victims of violence to receive full information and advice appropriate to their personal situation, through the services, agencies or offices that the National, State and Municipal Public Administration is obliged to create. Such information shall include the measures contemplated in this Law relating to their protection and security, and the rights and assistance provided therein,

as well as that referring to the place of provision of attention, emergency, support and comprehensive recovery services.  
6.	The others enshrined in the Constitution of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela and in all international conventions and treaties on the matter, signed by the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela, such as the Law Approving the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW) and the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women (Convention of Belem do Pará).

## CHAPTER II: ON THE GUARANTEES FOR THE EXERCISE OF RIGHTS

On the guarantees Article 4.- All women regardless of their nationality, ethnic origin, religion or any other personal, legal or social condition or circumstance, shall have at their disposal the necessary mechanisms to make effective the rights
recognized in this Law:

1. Information, comprehensive social assistance and legal assistance to women in situations of gender-based violence are the responsibility of the Venezuelan State.  
2. In the case of women who belong to especially vulnerable groups, the National Women's Institute, as well as the regional and municipal institutes, must ensure that the information provided to them is offered in an accessible and comprehensible format, ensuring the use of Spanish and indigenous languages, other modalities or communication options, including alternative and augmentative systems. In short, the necessary means shall be articulated so that women in situations of gender-based violence who due to their personal and social circumstances may have greater difficulty in comprehensive access to information, have guaranteed the effective exercise of this right.  
3. Women victims of gender-based violence have the right to social services of care, emergency, protection, support and shelter, and comprehensive recovery. In each state and municipality such services shall be created, charged to the annual budget. The care provided by such services must be: permanent, urgent, specialized and professionally multidisciplinary and they shall be financed by the State.  
4. The services listed in the preceding paragraph

shall act in coordination and collaboration with citizen security agencies, male and female judges, male and female prosecutors, health services and the National Ombudsman for Women's Rights. Children and adolescents who are under the parental authority or care responsibility of women victims of violence shall also have the right to comprehensive social assistance through these social services.  
5. The governing body for public policies directed toward women, the regional and municipal women's institutes, as well as other organizations, associations or community forms that fight for women's rights, shall guide and evaluate the plans, projects, programs and actions that are executed, and shall issue recommendations for their improvement and effectiveness.  
6. The Ombudsman's Office, the National Women's Institute and the state, metropolitan and municipal institutes, shall ensure the correct application of this Law and of the instruments consonant with it. It shall correspond to the National Ombudsman for Women's Rights and to the state, metropolitan and municipal ombudsmen to ensure respect for and effective exercise of the right to justice of women victims of gender-based violence who prove insufficient resources to litigate, these having the right to judicial and extrajudicial representation, and to be provided with the necessary legal sponsorship to guarantee the effectiveness of the rights enshrined herein. This right shall also assist the successors in case of death of the assaulted woman.  
7. The bar associations of lawyers, of male and female doctors, of male and female psychologists, of male and female nurses of the different states and metropolitan districts, must establish free specialized comprehensive advisory services to women victims of gender-based violence.  
8. The female worker in a situation of gender-based violence shall have the right to the reduction or reorganization of her working time, to be geographically mobilized or to change her work center. If her condition requires a work suspension, it must be accredited with the protection order of the male or female judge, upon report and request of the Public Prosecutor's Office, the accreditation of indications being sufficient.  
9. The State shall develop public policies directed at women victims of violence who lack work, and may be inserted in the programs, missions and projects of training for employment, as permitted by the physical and

psychological conditions in which she finds herself. If the assaulted woman has an officially recognized disability that prevents or hinders access to employment, she shall receive special attention that allows her labor insertion and training. For this purpose, programs, projects and missions shall be established. The State shall create tax exemptions for companies, cooperatives and other entities that promote employment, insertion and reinsertion in the labor and productive market of women victims of gender-based violence.  
10. Women victims of gender-based violence shall have priority for the aid and assistance created by the Public Administration, National, State or Municipal.  
11. Women victims of gender-based violence shall have priority in access to housing, land, credit and technical assistance in government plans.  

Obligation of the State Article 5.- The State has the unavoidable obligation to adopt all administrative, legislative, judicial and any
other measures that are necessary and appropriate to ensure compliance with this Law and guarantee the human rights of women victims of violence.  
Participation of society

#### Article 6

Society has the right and duty to participate in a leading manner to achieve the full and effective validity of this Law, through community and social organizations.  
Education and prevention

#### Article 7

The State, with the active participation of society, must guarantee permanent programs of education and prevention on gender-based violence.  
Procedural principles

#### Article 8

In the application and interpretation of this Law, the following principles and procedural guarantees must be taken into account:

1.	Free of charge: The requests, petitions, complaints and other actions relating to the matters referred to in this Law, as well as the certified copies issued thereof, shall be made on plain paper and without stamps. The officials of the Public Powers who intervene in any way shall process them with all preference and may not charge any emolument or fee whatsoever.  
2.	Celerity: The bodies receiving complaints, auxiliary to the administration of justice under the terms of article 111 of the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure and the competent courts, shall give preference to the knowledge and processing of the acts provided for in this Law, without any delay, within the time periods provided for therein, under warning of the administrative measure corresponding to the official

who has received the complaint.  
3.	Immediacy: The judge who is to pronounce the sentence must be present at the hearing and the incorporation of the evidence from which he or she obtains his or her conviction, except in cases where the Law permits judicial commission for the taking of some evidentiary means necessary for the demonstration of the controverted facts, the results of which shall be debated at the trial hearing. The evidence contained in the file duly incorporated at the hearing shall be assessed.  
4.	Confidentiality: The officials of the bodies receiving complaints, of the care and treatment units, and of the competent courts, must maintain the confidentiality of the matters submitted for their consideration.  
5.	Orality: The proceedings shall be oral and only the written forms provided for in this Law and in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure shall be admitted.  
6.	Concentration: Once the hearing has begun, it must conclude on the same day. If this is not possible, it shall continue for the fewest number of consecutive days.  
7.	Publicity: The trial shall be public, unless at the request of the woman victim of violence the court decides that it be held totally or partially in closed session, the woman having been informed previously and in a timely manner that she may make use of this right.  
8.	Protection of victims: The victims of the punishable acts described herein have the right to access the specialized bodies of civil and criminal justice free of charge, expeditiously, without undue delays or useless formalities, without prejudice to the rights of the accused or charged persons. The protection of the victim and the reparation of the damage to which she is entitled shall also be the objective of the procedure provided for herein.  

Security and Protection Measures and Precautionary Measures

#### Article 9

Security and protection measures, and precautionary measures are those imposed by the competent authority designated in this Law, to safeguard the life, protect the physical, emotional, psychological integrity and the patrimonial assets of women victims of violence.  
Supremacy of this Law

#### Article 10

The provisions of this Law shall be of preferential application as it is an Organic Law.

#### Article 11

Jurisdiction In all crimes provided for in this Law, no special jurisdiction shall be recognized, except those expressly contained in the Constitution of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela and laws of the Republic.

#### Article 12

#### Article 13

Preeminence of the Special Procedure The prosecution of the crimes dealt with in this Law shall be conducted by the special procedure provided for herein, except for the special case contained in the sole paragraph of article 65, the knowledge of which corresponds to the ordinary criminal courts.  
Intervention of interdisciplinary team In the reception of complaints and in the procedural investigation of the acts dealt with in this Law, duly sensitized, aware and trained personnel in gender violence shall be used. The respective offices shall be equipped with waiting rooms for accused persons, separate from those intended for victims.

## CHAPTER III: DEFINITION AND FORMS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

#### Article 14

Definition Violence against women referred to in this Law comprises any sexist act that has or may have as a result physical, sexual, psychological, emotional, labor, economic or patrimonial harm or suffering; coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, as well as the threat of executing such acts, whether they occur in the public or private sphere.

#### Article 15

Forms of violence The following are considered forms of gender-based violence against women:

1. Psychological violence: Any active conduct or omission exercised in dishonor, discredit or contempt of personal worth or dignity, humiliating and degrading treatment, constant surveillance, isolation, marginalization, negligence, abandonment, pathological jealousy, destructive comparisons, threats and acts that lead women victims of violence to diminish their self-esteem, to harm or disturb their healthy development, to depression and even to suicide.  
2. Harassment or stalking: Any abusive conduct and especially behaviors, words, acts, gestures, writings or electronic messages directed at pursuing, intimidating, blackmailing, pressuring, bothering and monitoring a woman that may threaten her emotional stability, dignity, prestige, physical or psychological integrity, or that may endanger her employment, promotion, recognition in the workplace or outside it.  
3. Threat: The verbal announcement or through acts of the execution of physical, psychological, sexual, labor or patrimonial harm with the purpose of intimidating the woman, both in the domestic context and outside it.  
4. Physical violence: Any action or omission that directly

or indirectly is directed at causing harm or physical suffering to the woman, such as: Internal or external injuries, wounds, bruises, burns, pushing or any other mistreatment that affects her physical integrity.  
5. Domestic violence: Any active conduct or omission, constant or not, of use of physical force or psychological violence, intimidation, persecution or threat against the woman by the spouse, cohabitant, ex-spouse, ex-cohabitant, person with whom she maintains or maintained an affective relationship, ascendants, descendants, collateral relatives, consanguineous and by affinity.  
6. Sexual violence: Any conduct that threatens or violates the woman's right to decide voluntarily and freely her sexuality, this comprising not only the sexual act, but any form of contact or sexual access, genital or non-genital, such as lascivious acts, violent lascivious acts, violent carnal access or rape properly so called.  
7. Violent carnal access: A form of sexual violence, in which the man through violence or threats, compels the spouse, cohabitant, person with whom he has marital life or maintains a stable union de facto or not, to a carnal act by vaginal, anal or oral route, or introduces objects whatever their kind, through any of these routes.  
8. Forced prostitution: Forced prostitution is understood as the action of forcing a woman to perform one
or more acts of a sexual nature by force or by means of the threat of force, or by means of coercion such as that caused by fear of violence, intimidation, psychological oppression or abuse of power, expecting to obtain or having obtained pecuniary or other advantages or benefits, in exchange for the acts of a sexual nature of the woman.  
9. Sexual slavery: Sexual slavery is understood as the illegitimate deprivation of liberty of the woman, for her sale, purchase, loan or barter with the obligation to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature.  
10. Sexual harassment: The solicitation of any act or behavior of sexual content, for oneself or for a third party, or seeking any type of unwanted sexual approach that a man makes by taking advantage of a situation of labor, teaching or analogous superiority, or on the occasion of relationships derived from professional practice, and with the express or tacit threat of causing the woman harm related to the legitimate expectations that she may have in the scope of said relationship.  
11. Labor violence: Discrimination against women

in work centers: public or private that obstruct their access to employment, promotion or stability in it, such as requiring requirements regarding marital status, age, physical appearance or good presence, or the request for results of clinical laboratory tests, that make the hiring, promotion or permanence of the woman in employment conditional. It also constitutes gender discrimination in the labor sphere to violate the right of equal pay for equal work.  
12. Patrimonial and economic violence: All active conduct or omission that directly or indirectly, in the public and private spheres, is directed at causing harm to movable or immovable property to the detriment of the patrimony of women victims of violence or to common property, as well as disturbance to the possession or ownership of their property, removal, destruction, retention or misappropriation of objects, personal documents, goods and valuables, patrimonial rights or economic resources intended to satisfy their needs; economic limitations aimed at controlling their income; or the deprivation of the economic means indispensable for living is considered patrimonial and economic violence.  
13. Obstetric violence: Obstetric violence is understood as the appropriation of the body and reproductive processes of women by health personnel, which is expressed in dehumanizing treatment, in an abuse of medicalization and pathologization of natural processes, bringing with it loss of autonomy and capacity to decide freely about their bodies and sexuality, negatively impacting the quality of life of women.  
14. Forced sterilization: Forced sterilization is understood as performing or intentionally causing to the woman, without providing her with due information, without her voluntary and informed consent and without the same having had justification, a medical or surgical treatment or other act that results in her sterilization or the deprivation of her biological and reproductive capacity.  
15. Media violence: Media violence is understood as the exposure, through any means of dissemination, of the woman, girl or adolescent, that directly or indirectly exploits, discriminates, dishonors, humiliates or threatens her dignity for economic, social or domination purposes.  
16. Institutional violence: Actions or omissions carried out by authorities, male and female officials, professionals, personnel and agents belonging to any organ, entity or institution

public, whose purpose is to delay, obstruct or prevent women from having access to public policies and exercising the rights provided in this Law to ensure them a life free of violence.  
17. Symbolic violence: Messages, values, icons, signs that transmit and reproduce relations of domination, inequality and discrimination in the social relations established between people and naturalize the subordination of women in society.  
18. Trafficking of women, girls and adolescents: All acts that involve their recruitment or transport within or across borders, using deception, coercion or force, with the purpose of obtaining a financial or other material benefit of an illicit nature.  
19. Trade of women, girls and adolescents: The recruitment, transportation, transfer, harboring or receipt of women, girls and adolescents, resorting to the threat or use of force or other forms of coercion, abduction, fraud, deception, abuse of power or of a situation of vulnerability or the granting or receipt of payments or benefits to obtain the consent of a person having authority over women, girls or adolescents, for purposes of exploitation, such as prostitution, sexual exploitation, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, servitude or the extraction of organs.

## CHAPTER IV: PUBLIC POLICIES FOR PREVENTION AND CARE

#### Article 16

#### Article 17

Definition and content Public policies for prevention and care are the set of guidelines and directives issued by the competent bodies, in order to guide actions aimed at ensuring the rights and guarantees enshrined in this Law.  
Programs Programs are an articulated set of actions developed by natural or legal persons of a public or private nature, with the purpose of preventing, detecting, monitoring, addressing and eradicating violence against women.

#### Article 18

Co-responsibility The State and society are co-responsible for the execution, monitoring and control of policies for the prevention and care of violence against women in accordance with this Law. It is the responsibility of the National Women's Institute, as the governing body, to formulate policies for the prevention and care of violence against women.  

The National Executive shall provide the necessary resources to finance plans, programs, projects and actions for the

prevention and care of gender violence, promoted by the Communal Councils, women's organizations and other grassroots social organizations.

#### Article 19

Binding nature Public policies adopted in accordance with this Law are binding on all bodies of the Public Administration, within their respective areas of competence.

#### Article 20

#### Article 21

Classification of programs In order to develop public policies and allow the implementation of the measures referred to in this Law, the following programs are established on an indicative basis:

1.	Prevention: to prevent the occurrence of forms of violence against women, by raising awareness, training and educating society as a whole in human rights and gender equality.  
2.	Awareness, training, education and capacity building: to meet the needs for awareness and training of persons engaged in the care of victims of violence, as well as the training and education needs of those who work with aggressors.  
3.	Support and guidance for women victims of violence and their families: to inform them, support them in making assertive decisions and accompany them in the process of developing their skills to overcome interpersonal relationships of control and submission, present and future.  
4.	Shelter: to assist women victims of gender violence or other members of their family who need it, in case of imminent danger or threat to their physical integrity.  
5.	Communication: for the dissemination of women's right to live free from violence.  
6.	Guidance and care for the aggressor: to promote cultural changes and encourage values of respect and equality between men and women that prevent recidivism by aggressors.  
7.	Promotion and defense: to enable women and other family members to know their right to live free from violence and the means to make this right effective.  
8.	Cultural: for the formation and respect of values and the culture of gender equality.  

Powers of the National Women's Institute The National Women's Institute, as the body responsible for policies and programs for the prevention and care of violence against women, shall have the following powers:

1.	To formulate, guide, execute, coordinate and implement public policies for prevention and care to be implemented in the different bodies of the National, State and Municipal Public Power, in order to form and articulate the comprehensive protection system referred to in this Law.  
2.	To design, jointly with the ministry with competence in matters of interior and justice and the Supreme Court of Justice, training plans and programs for officials belonging to the administration of justice and the penitentiary system, and other entities involved in the treatment of acts of violence contemplated by this Law.  
3.	To design, jointly with the ministries with competence in matters of Health and Popular Participation and Social Development, plans, projects and training and information programs for officials who carry out support activities, services and medical and psychosocial care for the appropriate treatment of women victims of violence and their families, as well as for the aggressor.  
4.	To design, jointly with the ministries with competence in matters of Education, Sports, Higher Education, Health, Participation and Social Development, Communication and Information and with any other entity responsible for educational functions, plans, projects and prevention and education programs aimed at training for equality, exalting the values of non-violence, respect, gender equity and preparation for family life with shared rights and obligations and, in general, equality between men and women in society.  
5.	To promote the active and leading participation of public and private organizations dedicated to addressing violence against women, as well as Communal Councils and grassroots social organizations, in the definition and implementation of public policies related to the matter regulated by this Law.  
6.	To maintain a registry of organizations specialized in the matter regulated by this Law, and may enter into agreements with them for the prevention, investigation and comprehensive care of women in situations of violence and the guidance of aggressors.  
7.	To prepare the draft Regulations of this Law.  
8.	Others assigned to it by other laws and regulations.

Education and Sports

#### Article 24

#### Article 25

The ministries with competence in matters of education and sport must incorporate into the plans, projects and study programs, at all their levels and modalities, content aimed at transmitting to students, teachers and administrative staff, the values of gender equality, respect, mutual tolerance, self-esteem, understanding, peaceful resolution of conflicts and preparation for family and civic life, with domestic rights and obligations shared between men and women and, in general, equality of conditions between men and women, boys, girls and adolescents.  
Likewise, the ministries with competence in matters of education and sport shall take the necessary measures to exclude from the study plans, texts and support materials, all those stereotypes, criteria or values that express any type of discrimination or violence against women.  
Powers of the ministry with competence in matters of

Likewise, it shall take the necessary measures for universities to include in their undergraduate and postgraduate programs subjects that address the issue of

gender-based violence and shall promote the development of research lines on the matter.

It shall likewise contemplate in its plans, special programs for the care and guidance of aggressors. It shall also establish programs aimed at guaranteeing women deprived of liberty the exercise of the rights provided for in this Law.

#### Article 31

The ministry with competence in matters of health shall execute training and information plans, jointly with the National Women's Institute, so that health personnel who exercise support, service and medical and psychosocial care activities, act adequately in the care, investigation and prevention of the acts provided for in this Law.  
Prevention programs in mass media The ministry with competence in matters of infrastructure and the National Telecommunications Council, in accordance with the Law on Social Responsibility in Radio and Television, shall supervise the effective inclusion of messages and programs intended to prevent and eliminate violence against women in the programming of mass media. Shelters The National, State and Municipal Executive, in order to make the protection of women in situations of violence more effective, with the assistance, advice and training of the National Women's Institute and the regional and municipal women's institutes, shall create in each of their dependencies shelters intended for their accommodation, in cases where remaining in the domicile or residence implies imminent threat to their integrity.

## CHAPTER V: WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 33

#### Article 34

Care for women victims of violence The bodies receiving complaints must grant women victims of the acts of violence provided for in this Law, dignified treatment of respect and support in accordance with their condition as affected persons, seeking to facilitate to the maximum their participation in the procedures in which they must intervene.  

Consequently, they must:

1.	Advise women victims of violence on the importance of preserving evidence.  
2.	Provide assaulted women with information about the rights that this Law confers upon them and about the governmental or non-governmental services available for their care and treatment.  
3.	Prepare a report of those circumstances that serve to clarify the facts, which must accompany the complaint.  
4.	Any other information that the receiving bodies consider important to point out to the woman in a situation of violence for her protection.  

Labor rights Women workers or officials who are victims of violence shall have the right, under the terms provided in the respective laws, to the reduction or reorganization of their work time, to geographical mobility, to change of workplace, to suspension of the employment relationship with reservation of position and to leave of absence under the terms to be determined.  

Sole Paragraph. Total or partial absences from work motivated by the physical or psychological condition derived from gender-based violence suffered by women workers or officials, shall be considered justified when so determined by public or private health care centers, under the terms provided in the respective legislation.

#### Article 35

Medical Certificate For the purposes of certifying the physical condition of the woman victim of violence, she may present a medical certificate issued by health professionals who provide services in any public institution. If this is not possible, the medical certificate may be issued by a private institution; in both cases, it must be confirmed by a forensic expert, upon request of the Public Prosecutor's Office.

#### Article 36

Free legal assistance In those cases where the victim lacks legal assistance, they may request from the competent judge the appointment of a legal professional, who will provide proper guidance and exercise the defense of their rights from the initial acts of the investigation. For such purposes, the court shall make the selection from existing lawyers, coming from the National Ombudsman for Women's Rights, from state and municipal ombudsmen, from the bar associations of each jurisdiction or from any public or private organization dedicated to the defense of the rights established in this Law.

#### Article 37

#### Article 38

Intervention in the procedure The aggrieved person, the National Ombudsman for Women's Rights and the social organizations referred to in item six of article 70 of this Law, may intervene in the procedure even if they have not been constituted as complainants.  
Request for simple and certified copies The woman victim of violence may request before any instance simple or certified copies of all proceedings contained in the case being prosecuted for one of the offenses typified in this Law, which shall be granted to her expeditiously, except in the case of confidentiality of proceedings referred to in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure

## CHAPTER VI: OFFENSES

#### Article 39

#### Article 40

Psychological violence Whoever through humiliating and degrading treatment, offenses, isolation, permanent surveillance, destructive comparisons or constant generic threats, attacks the emotional or psychological stability of the woman, shall be punished with imprisonment of six to eighteen months.  
Harassment Whoever through behaviors, verbal or written expressions, or electronic messages executes acts of intimidation, blackmail, harassment that attack the emotional, labor, economic, family or educational stability of the woman, shall be punished with imprisonment of eight to twenty months.

#### Article 41

Threat Whoever through verbal expressions, writings or electronic messages threatens a woman with causing her serious and probable harm of a physical, psychological, sexual,

labor or patrimonial nature, shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to twenty-two months.  

If the threat or act of violence is carried out in the domicile or residence of the woman object of violence, the penalty shall be increased from one third to one half.  

If the author of the offense is a public official belonging to any police or military body, the penalty shall be increased by one half.  

If the act is committed with bladed weapons or firearms, the imprisonment shall be two to four years.

#### Article 42

Physical violence Whoever through the use of physical force causes damage or physical suffering to a woman, bruises, slaps, shoves or injuries of a minor or very minor nature, shall be punished with imprisonment of six to eighteen months.  

If in the execution of the offense, the victim suffers serious or very serious injuries, as provided in the Penal Code, the penalty corresponding to the injury inflicted provided in said Code shall be applied, plus an increase of one third to one half.  

If the acts of violence referred to in this article occur in the domestic sphere, the author being the spouse, cohabitant, ex-spouse, ex-cohabitant, person with whom they maintain an affective relationship, even without cohabitation, ascendant, descendant, collateral relative, consanguineous or by affinity of the victim, the penalty shall be increased from one third to one half.  

The jurisdiction to hear the offense of injuries as provided in this article shall correspond to the courts of violence against women, according to the special procedure provided in this Law.

#### Article 43

Sexual Violence Whoever through the use of violence or threats compels a woman to consent to unwanted sexual contact that includes penetration by vaginal, anal or oral route, even through the introduction of objects of any kind through any of these routes, shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to fifteen years.  

If the author of the offense is the spouse, cohabitant, ex-spouse, ex-cohabitant, person with whom the victim maintains or maintained an affective relationship, even without cohabitation, the penalty shall be increased from one quarter to one third.  

The same penalty increase shall apply in cases where the author is the ascendant, descendant, collateral relative,

consanguineous or by affinity of the victim.  

If the act is executed to the detriment of a girl or adolescent, the penalty shall be fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.  

If the victim turns out to be a girl or adolescent, daughter of the woman with whom the author maintains a relationship as spouse, cohabitant, ex-spouse, ex-cohabitant, person with whom they maintain or maintained an affective relationship, even without cohabitation, the penalty shall be increased from one quarter to one third.

#### Article 45

Lascivious acts Whoever through the use of violence or threats and without the intention of committing the offense referred to in article 43, compels a woman to consent to unwanted sexual contact, affecting her right to freely decide her sexuality, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to five years.  

If the act is executed to the detriment of a girl or adolescent, the penalty shall be two to six years of imprisonment.  

The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever executes the lascivious acts to the detriment of the girl or adolescent, even without violence or threats, taking advantage of their relationship of authority or kinship.

#### Article 46

Forced prostitution Whoever through the use of physical force, the threat of violence, psychological coercion or abuse of power, compels a woman to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature with the object of obtaining in exchange advantages of a pecuniary nature or of another kind, for their own benefit or that of a third party, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.

#### Article 50

Whoever illegally deprives a woman of her liberty for purposes of sexually exploiting her through purchase, sale, loan, barter or other analogous transaction, forcing her to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature, shall be punished with a sentence of fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.

Labor violence The person who, by establishing requirements related to sex, age, physical appearance, marital status, condition of being a mother or not, submission to laboratory or other tests to rule out pregnancy, obstructs or conditions access, promotion or stability in employment of women, shall be punished with a fine of one hundred (100 U.T.) to one thousand tax units (1,000 U.T.), according to the seriousness of the act.

Patrimonial and economic violence The legally separated spouse or the cohabitant in a situation of duly proven de facto separation, who removes, deteriorates, destroys, diverts, retains, orders the blocking of bank accounts or performs acts capable of affecting the community property or the woman's own patrimony, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years.

In the event that the acts referred to in this article are intentionally directed at depriving the woman of the economic means indispensable for her subsistence, or preventing her from satisfying her needs and those of the family unit, the penalty shall be increased by one third to one half.

If the perpetrator of the crime referred to in this article, without being a spouse or cohabitant, maintains or maintained a relationship of affection with the woman, even without cohabitation, the penalty shall be six to twelve months of imprisonment.

In the cases referred to in this article, reparatory agreements may be concluded as provided in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure.

#### Article 52

Obstetric violence Acts constituting obstetric violence shall be considered those executed by health personnel, consisting of:

1.	Failure to attend to obstetric emergencies in a timely and effective manner.
2.	Forcing the woman to give birth in a supine position and with her legs raised, when the necessary means exist for vertical delivery.
3.	Obstructing the early attachment of the child with his or her mother, without justified medical cause, denying her the possibility of holding him or her and breastfeeding him or her immediately after birth.
4.	Altering the natural process of low-risk childbirth, through the use of acceleration techniques, without obtaining the voluntary, express and informed consent of the woman.
5.	Performing delivery by cesarean section, when conditions exist for natural childbirth, without obtaining the voluntary, express and informed consent of the woman.

In such cases, the court shall impose on the person responsible a fine of two hundred and fifty (250 U.T.) to five hundred tax units (500 U.T.), and shall send a certified copy of the final conviction to the respective professional association or guild institution, for purposes of the corresponding disciplinary procedure.
Forced sterilization Whoever intentionally deprives a woman of her reproductive capacity, without providing her with due information, nor obtaining her express, voluntary and informed consent, when there is no duly proven medical or surgical reason to justify it, shall be punished with a sentence of imprisonment of two to five years.

The sentencing court shall send a copy of the final conviction to the professional association or guild institution, for purposes of the corresponding disciplinary procedure.

## CHAPTER VII: CIVIL LIABILITY

#### Article 61

Indemnification All acts of violence provided for in this Law shall entail payment of compensation by the aggressor to women victims of violence or to their heirs in the event that the woman has died as a result of those crimes; the amount of such compensation shall be fixed by the competent specialized jurisdictional body, without prejudice to the aggressor's obligation to pay for the medical or psychological treatment that the victim may need.

#### Article 62

#### Article 63

Reparation Whoever is convicted of the punishable acts provided for in this Law, which have caused property damage to the movable and immovable property of women victims of violence, shall be obligated to repair them by paying for the deterioration they have suffered, which shall be determined by the competent specialized jurisdictional body. When their repair is not possible, their loss shall be compensated by paying the market value of such property.
Indemnification for sexual harassment Whoever is found responsible for sexual harassment must compensate the woman victim of violence in the following terms:

1.	For a sum equal to twice the amount of damages that the act has caused to the harassed person in their access to employment or position to which they aspire, promotion or performance of their activities.

2.	For a sum of not less than one hundred (100 T.U.) nor more than five hundred tax units (500 T.U.), in those cases where pecuniary damages cannot be determined. When the compensation cannot be satisfied by the convicted person due to a duly accredited state of insolvency, the competent enforcement court may convert it into community work at the rate of one day of work per tax unit.

## CHAPTER VIII: COMMON PROVISIONS

#### Article 64

#### Article 65

Supplementary and complementary nature of norms The provisions of the Penal Code and Organic Code of Criminal Procedure shall apply supplementarily, insofar as they do not conflict with those provided herein.

In cases of intentional homicide in all its qualifications, typified in the Penal Code and the special case referred to in the sole paragraph of Article 65 of this Law, jurisdiction corresponds to the ordinary criminal courts according to the procedure established in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure. However, the courts shall apply the aggravating circumstances provided herein when applicable and, in general, shall observe the principles and purposes of this Law.
Aggravating circumstances The following shall be aggravating circumstances of the crimes provided for in this Law, giving rise to an increase in the sentence from one-third to one-half:

1.	Entering the residence of the assaulted woman or the place where she resides, when the conjugal or marital relationship of the woman victim of violence with the accused is in a situation of de facto or de jure separation, or when the marriage has been dissolved by final judgment.
2.	Entering the residence of the woman victim of violence or the place where she resides, taking advantage of the bond of consanguinity or affinity.
3.	Executing it with weapons, objects or instruments.
4.	Executing it to the detriment of a pregnant woman.
5.	Executing it in a gang or with a group of persons.
6.	If the perpetrator of the crime is a public official in the exercise of their functions.
7.	Perpetrating it to the detriment of especially vulnerable persons, with physical or mental disabilities.
8.	That the accused has been sanctioned with a final judgment for the commission of any of the crimes provided for in this Law.

9.	Intentionally transmitting to the woman victim of violence infections or diseases that put her health at risk.
10.	Performing actions that deprive the victim of the capacity to discern as a consequence of the use of fraudulent means or narcotic or stimulant substances.

Sole Paragraph: In cases of intentional homicide in all its qualifications, typified in the Penal Code, when the perpetrator is the spouse, ex-spouse, cohabitant, ex-cohabitant, person with whom the victim maintained marital life, stable de facto union or affective relationship, with or without cohabitation, the sentence to be imposed shall be twenty-eight to thirty years of imprisonment.

#### Article 72

#### Article 73

Sole Paragraph: Indigenous peoples and communities shall constitute complaint-receiving bodies, composed of legitimate authorities in accordance with their customs and traditions, without prejudice to the assaulted woman being able to resort to the other bodies indicated in this article.  
Obligations of the complaint-receiving body
The complaint-receiving body must:

1. Receive the complaint, which may be submitted orally or in writing.  
2. Order the necessary and urgent proceedings, among others, the performance of the corresponding medical examinations of the assaulted woman in public or private health centers of the locality.  
3. Provide timely guidance to the woman in a situation of gender-based violence.  
4. Order the mandatory appearance of the alleged aggressor, for the purposes of the corresponding statement and other necessary proceedings that allow the clarification of the reported facts.  
5. Impose the pertinent protection and security measures established in this Law.  
6. Form the respective file.  
7. Prepare a report of those circumstances that serve to clarify the facts, which must accompany the complaint, attaching any other data or document that is necessary in the judgment of the complaint-receiving body.  
8. Refer the file to the Public Prosecutor's Office.  

Contents of the file The file to be formed must have consecutive nomenclature and must be duly sealed and foliated, and must also contain:

1. Complaint record explaining the manner in which the acts of violence occurred, making express mention of the place, time and date on which the complainant was assaulted, as well as the date and time at which the complaint is filed.  
2. Identity data of the person identified as the aggressor and their relationship with the woman victim of violence.  
3. Information about acts of violence attributed to the alleged aggressor, specifying if possible, the date on which they occurred, and whether there was a formal complaint before a competent receiving body.  
4. Record of the state of the affected movable or immovable property owned by the victim woman, when it involves patrimonial violence.  
5. Notification form to the alleged aggressor.  
6. Records of each of the acts performed, this being able to be corroborated by means of the minutes drawn up for such purposes, duly signed by the parties and the official of the receiving body.  
7. Record of referral of the assaulted woman to the pertinent medical examination.  
8. Results of the expert examinations, tests or evaluations performed on the woman victim of violence and the alleged aggressor.  
9. Specification of the protection measures for the woman victim of violence with their due justification.

#### Article 74

Fifth Section: On Apprehension in Flagrante Delicto
Definition and Manner of Proceeding Any crime provided for in this Law that is being committed or that has just been committed shall be considered flagrant. Also considered flagrant shall be that for which the aggressor is pursued by the police authority, by the assaulted woman, by a private individual or by public outcry, or when requests for help are made to specialized services for attention to violence against women, made through telephone calls, emails or fax, that allow establishing its commission unequivocally, or in which he is surprised shortly after the act has been committed, in the same place or near the place where it was committed, with weapons, instruments or objects that in some way give founded reason to presume that he is the perpetrator.

In these cases, every authority must and any private individual may apprehend the aggressor. When the apprehension is carried out by a private individual, he must immediately deliver him to the nearest authority, who in any case shall place him at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office within a period not exceeding twelve hours from the moment of apprehension.

It shall be understood that the act has just been committed when the victim or another person who has had knowledge of the act, goes within twenty-four hours following the commission of the punishable act to the receiving body and sets forth the facts of violence related to this Law. In this case, once the commission of the punishable act is known, the receiving body or the authority having knowledge must proceed within a period not exceeding twelve hours to the place where the acts occurred, shall gather the elements that prove its commission and once the assumptions referred to in this article are verified, shall proceed to the apprehension of the alleged aggressor, who shall be placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office, according to the preceding paragraph.

The Public Prosecutor's Office, within a term not exceeding forty-eight hours counted from the apprehension of the alleged aggressor, must present him before the Court of Violence Against Women in Control, Hearing and Measures Functions, which, in a hearing with the parties and the victim, if she is present, shall decide whether to maintain the deprivation of liberty or substitute it with a less onerous one.

The decision must be duly founded and shall observe the grounds for deprivation of liberty contained in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure, adjusted to the nature of the crimes contained in this Law, according to the act in question and attending to the objectives of protection of victims, without prejudice to the rights of the alleged aggressor.

#### Article 100

On Initiation before Another Receiving Body When the complaint or ex officio investigation is known by a receiving body other than the Public Prosecutor's Office, it shall proceed to issue the protection and security measures that the case warrants and to immediately notify the corresponding Prosecutor of the Public Prosecutor's Office, so that he or she may issue the order to initiate the investigation, shall carry out all necessary proceedings to prove the commission of the punishable act, as well as the pertinent psychophysical medical examinations of the woman victim of violence.
Referral to the Public Prosecutor's Office Once the protection and security measures have been issued, as well as all necessary and urgent proceedings carried out, which may not exceed fifteen continuous days, the receiving body must refer the proceedings to the Public Prosecutor's Office, so that it may continue the investigation.
Violation of Constitutional Rights and Guarantees When one of the parties does not agree with the measure issued by the receiving body, it may request its review before the Court of Violence against Women in Control, Hearing and Measures functions, which shall require the proceedings from the Public Prosecutor's Office or the corresponding receiving body, if applicable.

If upon receipt by the Prosecutor of the Public Prosecutor's Office of the proceedings from another receiving body, he or she observes violation of constitutional rights and guarantees, he or she shall immediately proceed to request their review with reasons before the Control, Hearing and Measures judge; for this purpose he or she shall refer the original proceedings, leaving in the Prosecutor's Office a simple copy thereof to continue with the investigation.
Review and Decision on Measures Within three business days following receipt of the proceedings, the Control, Hearing and Measures judge shall review the measures, and by reasoned order shall rule modifying, substituting, confirming or revoking them.

#### Article 101

Referral of Proceedings On the day following publication of the decision referred to in the preceding article, the Control, Hearing and Measures Court shall refer the original proceedings to the Public Prosecutor's Office or to the

corresponding receiving body if applicable, so that it may continue with the procedure.

#### Article 102

#### Article 103

End of the investigation Upon conclusion of the investigation, in accordance with the provisions of Article 79 or the special case provided for in Article 103 of this Law, the Public Prosecutor's Office shall proceed to issue the corresponding conclusive act.  
Extraordinary extension for prosecutorial omission If upon expiration of all time periods, the Prosecutor of the Public Prosecutor's Office has not issued the corresponding conclusive act, the Control, Hearing and Measures judge shall notify such omission to the Superior Prosecutor, who within the following two days must commission a new Prosecutor to present the conclusions of the investigation within a period that shall not exceed ten continuous days counted from the notification of the commission, without prejudice to the civil, criminal and administrative sanctions that may be applicable to the omissive Prosecutor.  

Once the extraordinary extension referred to in this article has elapsed, without action by the Public Prosecutor's Office, the Control, Hearing and Measures Court shall decree judicial filing, in accordance with the provisions of the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure.

#### Article 104

Of the preliminary hearing Once the accusation is presented before the Court of Violence Against Women in Control, Hearing and Measures Functions, it shall set the hearing to hear the parties, within the following ten working days.  

Before the expiration of said period, the parties shall proceed to offer the evidence that will be presented at the oral trial hearing and raise the exceptions they deem appropriate. The court shall rule at the hearing.  

In this act the accused may admit the facts, but the penalty to be imposed may only be reduced by one third.  

Upon conclusion of the hearing, the judge shall present his or her decision with reasons regarding the parties' arguments. In case of admitting the accusation, he or she shall issue the order opening the trial and remit the proceedings to the corresponding trial court.  

The order opening the trial shall not be subject to appeal. Seventh Section: Of the Oral Trial

Of the oral trial

#### Article 105

Upon receipt of the proceedings, the Trial Court shall set the date for the oral and public hearing, within a period that may not be less than ten working days nor more than twenty.

#### Article 106

Of the oral trial hearing At the Trial Hearing only one professional judge shall act. The debate shall be oral and public, and the judge may

decide to conduct it, totally or partially in closed session, upon request of the victim. The judge must inform the victim of this right before the beginning of the act. The hearing shall be conducted in a single day; if not possible, it shall continue on the fewest number of consecutive working days. It may be suspended for a maximum period of five days, only in the following cases:

1.	Due to force majeure.  
2.	Due to lack of interpreter.  
3.	When the defense counsel or the Public Prosecutor's Office requests it by reason of the expansion of the accusation.  
4.	To resolve incidental matters or the performance of some act outside the hearing room.  
5.	Any other reason considered relevant by the court.

#### Article 107

#### Article 108

Of the decision Upon conclusion of the debate, minutes shall be drawn up of everything that occurred, which shall be read aloud and signed by the participants.  

The judge shall proceed to render judgment in the room designated for such purpose, to which the parties shall not have access at any time. The judgment shall be issued on the same day, proceeding to its reading and the parties being thus notified. The original document shall be filed. The parties may request a copy of the judgment.  

In the event that the drafting of the judgment is not possible on the same day, the judge shall present to the parties the grounds thereof and shall read the operative part.  

Publication shall take place within the five working days following the pronouncement of the operative part.  
Of the appeal Against the judgment issued at the oral hearing, an appeal shall be filed before the court that issued it and may be exercised within three working days following the date of publication of the full text of the ruling.  
Formalities

#### Article 109

The appeal may only be based on:

1.	Violation of rules relating to orality, immediacy and concentration of the trial.  
2.	Lack, contradiction or manifest illogicality in the reasoning of the judgment, or when it is based on illegally obtained evidence or incorporated in violation of the principles of the oral hearing.  
3.	Breach or omission of substantial forms of acts that cause defenselessness.  
4.	Incurring in violation of the law due to non-observance or

erroneous application of a legal norm.

#### Article 110

Response to the appeal Once the appeal is filed, the other parties shall respond to it within three working days following the expiration of the period for its filing. Upon expiration of this period, the court shall remit the proceedings to the Court of Appeals for it to decide.

#### Article 111

Of the Court of Appeals Upon receipt of the proceedings, the Court of Appeals shall have a period of three working days following the date of its receipt to decide on the admissibility of the appeal. Once it is admitted, it shall set an oral hearing that must be held within a period of not less than three working days nor more than five, counted from the date of admission. The order opening the trial shall not be subject to appeal.

#### Article 112

Of the hearing At the hearing the judges may question the parties; they shall rule with reasons based on the evidence that is offered and is useful and pertinent. Upon conclusion of the hearing they must issue the corresponding pronouncement. When the complexity of the case warrants it, they may decide within the following five working days.

#### Article 113

Cassation The exercise of the Cassation Appeal shall be governed by the provisions of the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure.  

Eighth Section: Of the Jurisdictional Bodies and the Public Prosecutor's Office
Powers of the Prosecutors of the Public Prosecutor's Office

#### Article 114

The powers of the Prosecutors of the Public Prosecutor's Office specialized in violence against women are:

1.	To exercise the corresponding criminal action.  
2.	To ensure compliance with the provisions set forth in this Law.  
3.	To investigate the acts that are classified as crimes in this Law.  
4.	To request and provide evidence and participate in its production.  
5.	To direct and supervise compliance with the functions of the Investigation Police.  
6.	To request with grounds from the jurisdictional body the pertinent precautionary measures.  
7.	To request from the jurisdictional body the substitution, modification, confirmation or revocation of the protection measures issued by the receiving bodies or of the precautionary measures it may have issued.  
8.	To request with grounds from the jurisdictional body the definitive confiscation of the weapon seized by the receiving body. In cases where appropriate,

to also request the prohibition of carrying weapons.  
9.	To gather the elements of conviction leading to the preparation of the conclusive act, in whose procedures the rules set forth in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure shall be observed.  
10.	Any other action provided for in the legal system.

#### Article 116

#### Article 117

Jurisdiction The courts of violence against women and the Criminal Cassation Chamber of the Supreme Court of Justice have jurisdiction to resolve matters submitted to their decision, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the laws of judicial organization and internal regulations.  
Creation of courts of violence against women Courts of violence against women are hereby created, which shall have their seat in Caracas and in each state capital, in addition to the localities determined by the Supreme Court of Justice through the Executive Directorate of the Magistracy.  
Constitution of courts of violence against women The courts of violence against women shall be organized in judicial circuits, in accordance with what is determined by the Executive Directorate of the Magistracy, which may create more than one judicial circuit in the same jurisdiction, when necessary for reasons of service. Their organization and operation shall be governed by the provisions established in this Law, in the corresponding organic laws and in the Internal Regulations of the Judicial Circuits.  

In each judicial circuit, the courts of violence against women shall be constituted in first instance by control, hearing and measures judges; trial judges and execution judges. In second instance they are formed by the Courts of Appeals.

#### Article 118

Jurisdiction The courts of violence against women shall have jurisdiction in criminal matters over the offenses provided for in this Law, as well as the offense of injuries in all its classifications typified in the Criminal Code in the cases established in article 42 of this Law and in accordance with the special procedure established herein.  

In civil matters, they shall have jurisdiction over all matters of a patrimonial nature.

Auxiliary services

#### Article 121

#### Article 122

#### Article 123

Objectives of the interdisciplinary team Each Court of Violence Against Women must have a multidisciplinary team that shall be organized as an auxiliary service of an independent and impartial nature, to provide the exercise of the jurisdictional function with bio-psycho-social-legal expertise in a collegial and interdisciplinary manner. This team shall be composed of professionals in medicine, psychiatry, education, psychology, social work, law, criminology and other professions with expertise in the matter. In areas where necessary, bilingual intercultural experts in indigenous languages shall be available.  
Powers of the interdisciplinary team The powers of the interdisciplinary teams of the courts of violence against women are:

1.	To issue opinions, through comprehensive technical reports on the appropriateness of protecting women victims of violence, through specific precautionary measures.  
2.	To intervene as independent and impartial experts of the Justice System in judicial proceedings, performing expert assessments through comprehensive technical reports.  
3.	To provide comprehensive advice to persons subject to precautionary measures.  
4.	To advise the judge in obtaining and assessing the opinion or testimony of children and adolescents, according to their age and degree of maturity.  
5.	To assist the courts of violence against women in the execution of judicial decisions.  
6.	Others established by law.  

Resources Courts of violence against women must be provided with the facilities, equipment and personnel necessary for the fulfillment of their functions; among other areas, they must have:

1.	A space specifically directed to the care of the assaulted woman, separate from that intended for the aggressor.  
2.	An appropriate space and resources for carrying out the functions of the interdisciplinary team.

TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

First.- Until such time as specialized courts in matters of violence against women are created, the Supreme Court of Justice shall provide what is necessary for the functions of these courts to be fulfilled by the ordinary criminal courts in control, trial and execution functions to which exclusive competence in matters of violence against women shall be conferred by resolution of the Executive Directorate of the Magistracy, at the time this Law enters into force.  

The Supreme Court of Justice shall take the necessary steps to ensure that the creation of specialized courts in violence against women is executed within one year from the entry into force of this Law. During said period, judges, as well as officials who are to intervene as operators of justice in matters of violence against women, shall be trained by professionals assigned to the National Women's Institute, Office of the Ombudsman, National Office of the Defense of Women's Rights, universities, non-governmental organizations, international organizations, and any other entity specialized in gender justice.  
Second.- Until such time as units for care and treatment of acts of violence against women are created, judges in sentencing may consider reports issued by any public or private health organization.  

The states and municipalities shall provide what is necessary to create and put into operation the care and treatment units, within the year following the entry into force of this Law. During said period they shall proceed to train the officials who will comprise them. The reports and recommendations issued by the experts of non-governmental organizations, specialized in the care of acts of violence contemplated in this Law, may also be considered by judges.  
Third.- Until such time as places for serving sentences of those responsible for acts of violence against women are created, the ministry with competence in the matter shall take measures to adapt detention facilities and facilitate the reeducation of aggressors.  

The creation of said centers must be developed within a maximum period of one year, after the entry into force of

this Law. During said period, officials and all those persons who will intervene in the treatment of those sentenced for crimes provided for in this Law shall be trained.  
Fourth.-	Within a period of no more than one year, counted from the publication of this Law, the Nation, the states and municipalities must provide what is necessary for the creation and adaptation of the units, entities and bodies provided for herein. Within the same period, the necessary regulations must be issued for the purpose of executing its provisions.  
Fifth.- In accordance with article 24 of the Constitution of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela, the procedural provisions provided for in this Law shall be applied from the moment they enter into force, even to proceedings that are in progress, without prejudice to the principle of non-retroactivity insofar as they favor the accused male or female, the defendant male or female, the sentenced male or female.  

Appeals already filed, the presentation of evidence already admitted, as well as terms or periods that have begun to run, shall be governed by the previous provisions.  

The Public Prosecutor's Office shall provide what is necessary for cases that are in the investigation phase to be processed expeditiously and for the corresponding conclusive act to be presented within six months following the entry into force of this Law.  
Sixth.-	The National Executive shall include in the annual budget laws, beginning the year immediately following the enactment of this Law, the necessary resources for the operation of the bodies, entities and programs provided for herein.  
Seventh.-	Official and private publications of this Law must be preceded by its statement of reasons.  
REPEALING PROVISION

Single.-	The Law on Violence against Women and the Family dated September 3, 1998, published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Venezuela No. 36,531, is repealed, as well as provisions contrary to this Law.
