# Organic Law No. 2017-58 of 11 August 2017, on the elimination of violence against women

Organic Law No. 2017-58 of 11 August 2017, on the elimination of violence against women

In the name of the people,

The Assembly of the Representatives of the People having adopted,
The President of the Republic promulgates the organic law the content of which follows:

## CHAPTER ONE – General Provisions

#### Article 1

This law aims to establish measures likely to eliminate all forms of violence against women based on discrimination between the sexes in order to ensure equality and respect for human dignity, by adopting a comprehensive approach based on combating the different forms of violence against women, through prevention, prosecution and punishment of the perpetrators of such violence, and the protection and care of victims.

#### Art. 2

This law concerns all forms of discrimination and violence suffered by women based on discrimination between the sexes, whatever the perpetrators or the domain.

#### Art. 3 – For the purposes of this law, the following terms mean

	Woman: any person of female sex of any age,
	Child: any person of male or female sex, within the meaning of the child protection code,
	Violence against women: any physical, moral, sexual or economic harm against women, based on discrimination founded on sex and which results for them in prejudice, suffering or bodily, psychological, sexual or economic harm and also includes the threat of causing such harm, pressure or deprivation of rights and freedoms, whether in public or private life,
	Physical violence: any harmful act or ill-treatment affecting the integrity or physical security of the woman or her life, such as blows, kicks, injuries, pushing, disfigurement, burns, mutilation of certain parts of the body, sequestration, torture and homicide,
	Moral violence: any verbal aggression, such as defamation, insult, coercion, threat, abandonment, deprivation of rights and freedoms, humiliation, neglect, mockery, belittlement and other acts or words affecting the human dignity of the woman or aimed at intimidating or dominating her,
	Sexual violence: any act or word by which the perpetrator aims to subject the woman to his own sexual desires or to the sexual desires of others, by means of coercion, fraud, pressure or other means, of a nature to weaken or affect the will, regardless of the relationship of the perpetrator with the victim,
	Political violence: any act or practice based on discrimination between the sexes by which the perpetrator aims to deprive the woman or prevent her from exercising any political, partisan, associative activity or any fundamental right or freedom,
	Economic violence: any act or omission of a nature to exploit women or deprive them of economic resources, whatever their origin, such as deprivation of funds, salary or income, control of salaries or income and prohibition from working or coercion to work,
	Discrimination against women: any distinction, exclusion or restriction which has the effect or purpose of impairing the recognition to women, of human rights and freedoms, on the basis of complete and effective equality, in the civil, political, economic, social and cultural fields, or of compromising this recognition or the enjoyment or exercise of these rights by women, whatever the colour, race, religion, thought, age,

nationality, economic and social conditions, marital status, state of health, language or disability.  
Positive procedures and measures aimed at accelerating the establishment of equality between the two sexes are not considered discriminatory.  
	Situation of vulnerability: the situation of fragility linked to young or advanced age, serious illness, pregnancy, or mental or physical deficiency affecting the victim's capacity to resist the perpetrator of the acts.  
The victim: the woman and the children who reside with her, who have suffered physical, moral, psychological, economic harm or have been deprived of the enjoyment of their freedoms and rights by acts, words or cases of abandonment constituting a violation of the laws in force.

#### Art. 4

The State undertakes to care for women victims of violence and the children who reside with her in accordance with the following general principles:
	to consider violence against women as being a form of discrimination and a violation of human rights,
	to recognize the status of victim to the woman and the children who reside with her, who have suffered violence,
	to respect the will of the victim to make the decision that matters to her,
	to respect and guarantee the secrecy of the private life and personal data of the victim,
	to allow equal opportunities for access to services in the different zones and regions,
	to provide legal counsel to victims of violence and grant them legal aid,
	to ensure the accompaniment of victims of violence in coordination with the competent services in order to provide them with the necessary social, health and psychological assistance and to facilitate their integration and accommodation.

#### Art. 5

The State undertakes to develop national policies, strategic plans and joint or sectoral programmes and to take the regulations and measures necessary for their implementation with the aim of eliminating all forms of violence against women in the family space, the social environment, the educational milieu, vocational training, health, cultural, sports and media.

## CHAPTER II

On the prevention and protection from violence against women Section one – On the prevention of violence against women

#### Art. 6

The State takes all necessary measures to eliminate all discriminatory practices against women, particularly at the level of remuneration and social coverage in the different sectors, and to prohibit the economic exploitation of women and employing them in difficult, degrading or harmful working conditions to their health, safety and dignity.

#### Art. 7

The ministries responsible for education, higher education, vocational training, culture, health, youth, sport, childhood, women and religious affairs must take all necessary measures to prevent and combat violence against women in institutions under their jurisdiction, through:
	the development of didactic, educational and cultural programmes aimed at eliminating and combating violence and discrimination against women, enshrining the principles of human rights and gender equality, as well as health and sexuality education,
	the training of educators and personnel working in the field of education on equality, non-discrimination and the fight against violence in order to help them address issues of violence in the educational space,

	the organisation of specific training sessions in the fields of human rights, women's rights, their protection and the fight against violence against them, for the benefit of officials working in these fields,
	the taking of all necessary measures to combat early school dropout, particularly among girls in all regions,
	the creation of listening units, social action offices and health clubs in cooperation with interested parties,
	the dissemination and consolidation of the culture of human rights education among younger generations.

#### Art. 8

The ministry responsible for health is called upon to establish integrated programmes to combat violence against women in medical and paramedical education, and to train personnel working in the health field, at all levels, to detect, assess and prevent all forms of violence against women, as well as examination, treatment and follow-up with a view to providing care for women and children residing with them who are victims of violence.  
It must also reserve spaces for receiving victims of violence and providing them with psycho-health services.

#### Art. 9

The ministry responsible for social affairs is called upon to ensure adequate training for the various social workers, including in particular social workers, in order to enable them to acquire the tools for intervention and care for women victims of violence.  
The structures, care establishments, social institutions and specialised associations, under agreement with the ministry responsible for social affairs, undertake to integrate the fight against violence against women into field intervention programmes, specific training programmes, intervention plans, and related partnership programmes, whether in terms of awareness-raising or early detection, reporting, intervention or support for women victims of violence and children residing with them.

#### Art. 10

The ministries of justice and the interior shall develop integrated programmes to combat violence against women in education and training within institutions under their jurisdiction, in order to develop methods for handling complaints and cases related to violence against women.  
The ministry of justice shall also take all necessary measures to rehabilitate the perpetrator of the offence of violence against women and reintegrate them into the family and social environment.

#### Art. 11

Public and private media shall raise awareness of the dangers of violence against women and methods of combating and preventing such violence and shall ensure that personnel working in the media field are trained to address violence against women, in compliance with professional ethics, human rights and equality.  
Advertising and dissemination, by all media means and supports, of materials containing stereotypical images, scenes, words or acts prejudicial to the image of women, or embodying violence against them or minimising its seriousness, are prohibited.  
The independent high authority for audiovisual communication must take the measures and sanctions provided for by law to combat the violations mentioned in the preceding paragraph of this article.

#### Art. 12

The ministry responsible for women's affairs shall ensure coordination between the various stakeholders mentioned in articles 6 to 11 of this law and the establishment of partnership, support and coordination mechanisms with the civil society organisations concerned for the purpose of monitoring the implementation of what has been approved.  
The ministry responsible for women's affairs shall prepare an annual report to this effect which shall be submitted to the presidency of the assembly of people's representatives and to the Presidency of the government.

### Section 2 – Protection from violence against women

#### Art. 13

Women victims of violence and children residing with them shall benefit from the following rights
:
	legal protection appropriate to the nature of the violence exercised against them, so as to ensure their safety, physical and psychological integrity and dignity, as well as the administrative, security and judicial measures required to this effect, in compliance with their specificities,
	access to information and legal advice concerning the provisions governing judicial procedures and available services,
	the benefit of legal aid,
	fair reparation for victims of violence in the event of impossibility of enforcement against the person responsible for the act of violence. The State shall in this case subrogate the victims in the recovery of the amounts disbursed,
	health and psychological follow-up, appropriate social support and, where applicable, the benefit of public and associative care, including listening,
	immediate accommodation within the limits of available means.

#### Art. 14

Any person, including those bound by professional secrecy, must alert the competent authorities to any case of violence within the meaning of this law, as soon as they become aware of it, observe it or ascertain its effects.  
No one may be prosecuted before the courts for raising the alert in good faith within the meaning of this law.  
It is prohibited for any person to disclose the identity of the person who raised the alert except with their consent or in the event that legal procedures so require.

## CHAPTER III – Offences of violence against women

#### Art. 15

The provisions of articles 208, 226 ter, 227, 227 bis, 229, paragraph 2 of article 218, paragraph 3 of article 219, paragraph 2 of article 222 and paragraph 2 of article 228 of the penal code are repealed and replaced by the following:

#### Art. 208 (nouveau)

The offender is punished by twenty (20) years of imprisonment, if the blows struck or injuries inflicted voluntarily, but without intention to cause death, have nevertheless caused it. The penalty is life imprisonment, if:

	the victim is a child,
	the perpetrator is an ascendant or descendant of the victim, whatever the degree,
	the perpetrator has authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by his or her functions,
	the perpetrator is one of the spouses, ex-spouses, fiancés or ex-fiancés,
	the victim is in a situation of vulnerability related to young or advanced age, serious illness, pregnancy, or mental or physical deficiency affecting his or her capacity to resist the perpetrator of the acts.  
	the victim is a witness, an injured person or a civil party, either to prevent him or her from making a statement, reporting the offence or filing a complaint, or by reason of his or her report, complaint or statement,
	there is premeditation of blows and injuries,
	the assault is preceded or committed with use or threat of use of a weapon,
	the offence was committed by a group of persons acting as principal perpetrators or accomplices,

	the assault is accompanied by an order or subject to a condition.

#### Art. 218 (paragraphe 2 nouveau)

The penalty is two (2) years of imprisonment and a fine of two (2) thousand dinars, if:
	the victim is a child,
	the perpetrator is an ascendant or descendant of the victim, whatever the degree,
	the perpetrator has authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by his or her functions,
	the perpetrator is one of the spouses, ex-spouses, fiancés or ex-fiancés,
	the offence committed is facilitated by the apparent situation of vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator,
	the victim is a witness, an injured person or a civil party, either to prevent him or her from making a statement, reporting the offence or filing a complaint, or by reason of his or her report, complaint or statement.

#### Art.219 (paragraphe 3 nouveau)

The penalty is increased to twelve (12) years of imprisonment whatever the rate of incapacity, if:
	the victim is a child,
	the perpetrator is an ascendant or descendant of the victim, whatever the degree,
	the perpetrator has authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by his or her functions,
	the perpetrator is one of the spouses, ex-spouses, fiancés or ex-fiancés,
	the offence committed is facilitated by the apparent situation of vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator,
	the victim is a witness, an injured person or a civil party, either to prevent him or her from making a statement, reporting the offence or filing a complaint, or by reason of his or her report, complaint or statement,
	the offence is committed by a group of persons acting as principal perpetrators or accomplices,
	the assault is preceded or committed with use or threat of use of a weapon,
	the assault is accompanied by an order or subject to a condition.

#### Art. 222 (paragraphe 2 nouveau) – La peine est portée au double, si

	the victim is a child,
	the perpetrator is an ascendant or descendant of the victim, whatever the degree,
	the perpetrator has authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by his or her functions,
	the perpetrator is one of the spouses, ex-spouses, fiancés or ex-fiancés,
	the offence committed is facilitated by the apparent situation of vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator,
	the victim is a witness, an injured person or a civil party, either to prevent him or her from making a statement, reporting the offence or filing a complaint, or by reason of his or her report, complaint or statement,
	the offence is committed by a group of persons acting as principal perpetrators or accomplices,
	the threat is accompanied by an order or subject to a condition even if this threat is only verbal.

#### Art. 226

ter (nouveau) – Is punished by two (2) years of imprisonment and a fine of five (5) thousand dinars whoever commits sexual harassment.  
Is considered as sexual harassment any assault on another person by acts or gestures or words containing sexual connotations that violate his or her dignity or affect his or her modesty, with the aim of inducing him or her to submit to the sexual desires of the aggressor or those of another person, or by exerting on him or her dangerous pressure likely to weaken his or her capacity to resist.  
The penalty is doubled, if:
	the victim is a child,
	the perpetrator is an ascendant or descendant of the victim, whatever the degree,
	the perpetrator has authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by his or her functions,
	the offence committed is facilitated by the apparent situation of vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator,
The limitation period for public prosecution concerning the offence of sexual harassment committed against a child runs from his or her majority.

#### Art. 227 (nouveau)

Is considered rape, any act of sexual penetration, whatever its nature, and the means used committed on a person of female or male sex without his or her consent the perpetrator of rape is punished by twenty years of imprisonment.  
Consent is considered non-existent when the age of the victim is below sixteen (16) years completed.  
Is punished by life imprisonment, the perpetrator of rape committed:
1)	With violence, use or threat of use of a weapon or with the use of products, pills, narcotic or stupefying drugs or medications.  
2)	On a child of female or male sex aged less than sixteen (16) years completed.  
3)	By incest on a child by:
	the ascendants whatever the degree,
	the brothers and sisters,
	the nephew or one of the descendants,
	the father of one of the spouses, the spouse of the mother, the wife of the father or the descendants of the other spouse,
	persons one of whom is the wife of the brother or the spouse of the sister,
4)	by a person having authority over the victim or abusing the authority conferred by his or her functions,
5)	by a group of persons acting as principal perpetrators or accomplices,
6)	If the victim is in a situation of vulnerability due to his or her advanced age, or a serious illness, or a pregnancy, or a mental or physical deficiency, weakening his or her capacity to resist the aggressor.  
The limitation period for public prosecution concerning the offence of rape committed on a child runs from his or her majority.

#### Art. 227

bis (Nouveau) – Is punished by five (5) years of imprisonment, whoever voluntarily subjects to a sexual act a child whether of female or male sex whose age is above sixteen (16) years completed, and below eighteen (18) years completed, with his or her consent.  
The penalty is doubled in the following cases, if:
	the perpetrator is the teacher of the victim, or one of his or her servants or one of his or her doctors,
	the perpetrator has authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by his or her functions,

	the offence is committed by a group of persons acting as principal perpetrators or accomplices,
	the victim is in a situation of vulnerability related to advanced age, serious illness, pregnancy, or mental or physical deficiency affecting his or her capacity to resist the perpetrator of the acts.  
The attempt is punishable.  
When the offence is committed by a child, the court applies the provisions of article 59 of the child protection code.  
The limitation period for public prosecution concerning the offence of sexual act committed on a child with his or her consent runs from his or her majority.

#### Art. 228 (new paragraph 2) – The penalty is doubled

	if the victim is a child,
	if the perpetrator is:
•	an ascendant or descendant of any degree,
•	a brother or sister,
•	the nephew or one of their descendants,
•	the son-in-law or daughter-in-law or one of their descendants,
•	the father of one of the spouses, the spouse of the mother, the wife of the father or the descendants of the other spouse,
•	persons one of whom is the wife of the brother or spouse of the sister,
	if the perpetrator is a person having authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by their functions,
	if the offence committed is facilitated by the apparent vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator,
	if the offence is committed by a group of persons acting as principal perpetrators or accomplices.  
The limitation period for public prosecution concerning the offence of indecent assault committed against a child runs from their majority.

#### Art. 16

A paragraph 3 is added to article 221, a paragraph 2 to article 223, a paragraph 2 to article 224 and article 224 bis to the penal code as follows:

#### Art. 221 (paragraph 3)

The same penalty is incurred by the perpetrator of the assault if it results in disfigurement or partial or total mutilation of the female genital organ.

#### Art. 223 (paragraph 2) – The penalty is doubled, if

	the victim is a child,
	the perpetrator is an ascendant or descendant of the victim, of any degree,
	the perpetrator has authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by their functions,
	the perpetrator is one of the spouses, ex-spouses, fiancés or ex-fiancés,
	the offence committed is facilitated by the apparent vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator,
	the victim is a witness, an injured person or a civil party, either to prevent them from making their statement, reporting the offence or filing a complaint, or by reason of their report, complaint or statement.

#### Art. 224 (paragraph 2)

Incurs the same penalties provided for in the preceding paragraph, anyone who habitually mistreats their spouse or a person in a situation of apparent vulnerability or known by the perpetrator, or having authority over the victim.

#### Art. 224 (bis)

Is punished by six (6) months to one year imprisonment and a fine of one thousand dinars, anyone who commits against their spouse repeated assault likely to harm the dignity of the victim, or their consideration or to impair their physical or psychological security by use of words, signals and acts.  
The same penalty is incurred, if the acts are committed against one of the ex-spouses, fiancés or ex-fiancés and if the relationship between the perpetrator and the victim is the sole motive for assault.

#### Art. 17

Is punished by a fine of five hundred (500) to one thousand dinars anyone who deliberately harasses a woman in a public place, by any act, word or gesture likely to harm her dignity, her consideration or to affect her modesty.

#### Art. 18

Is punished by a fine of one thousand dinars anyone who commits political violence. The penalty is increased to six (6) months imprisonment in case of recidivism.

#### Art. 19

Is punished by a fine of two (2) thousand dinars the perpetrator of violence or economic discrimination based on sex, if their act results in:

	the deprivation of the woman of her economic resources or the use of her income,
	wage discrimination for work of equal value,
	discrimination in professional career including promotion and advancement in functions.  

The penalty is doubled in case of recidivism. Attempt is punishable.

#### Art. 20

Is punished by three (3) to six (6) months imprisonment and a fine of two (2) to five (5) thousand dinars, anyone who deliberately and directly or indirectly employs children as domestic workers.  
Incurs the same penalty provided for by the preceding paragraph, anyone who acts as intermediary to employ children as domestic workers.  
The penalty is doubled in case of recidivism. Attempt is punishable.

#### Art. 21

Is punished by one month to two (2) years imprisonment and a penalty of one thousand to five (5) thousand dinars or one of these two penalties, anyone who deliberately exercises discrimination within the meaning of this law if their act results in:
	the deprivation or restriction for the victim of benefiting from their rights or obtaining a good or service,
	the prohibition for the victim to exercise their activities normally,
	the refusal to employ the victim, their dismissal or the sanction of the victim.

## CHAPTER IV

Procedures, services and institutions First section – Procedures

#### Art. 22

The Public Prosecutor assigns one or more of their deputies to receive complaints relating to violence against women and to follow up on related investigations.

#### Art. 23

Spaces separate from the courts of first instance are reserved for magistrates specializing in cases of violence against women, at the level of the public prosecutor's office, the investigating magistracy and family justice.

#### Art. 24

A specialized unit is created within each national security and national guard police station, in all governorates, to investigate offences of violence against women in accordance with the provisions of this law. It must include women among its members.  
A special numbered register relating to these offences is made available to this specialized unit.

#### Art. 25

As soon as notified of a case of flagrant offence of violence against women, the agents of the specialized unit must proceed without delay to the scene to conduct investigations after having informed the Public Prosecutor.  
Is punished by one to six (6) months imprisonment, the agent belonging to the specialized unit for investigating offences of violence against women, who deliberately exerts pressure, or any type of coercion, on the victim in order to induce them to renounce their rights, to modify their statement or to retract.

#### Art. 26

The specialized unit must mandatorily inform the victim of all their rights provided for by this law, including the claim of their right to protection before the family judge.  
The specialized unit may, with authorization from the Public Prosecutor, and before the protection order is issued, take one of the following protection measures:
	the transfer of the victim and the children residing with them, if necessary, to secure locations, in coordination with the competent structures and the child protection delegate,
	the transfer of the victim to receive first aid when they have suffered bodily harm,
	removing the accused from the home or prohibiting them from approaching the victim or being near their home or place of work, in case of danger threatening the victim or their children residing with them.  
The protection procedures continue to take effect until the protection order is issued.

#### Art. 27

The specialized unit prepares every six months a report on the minutes relating to violence against women, of which it has been seized and their follow-up. Said report is submitted to the administrative and judicial supervisory authority and to the national observatory for the elimination of violence against women provided for by article 40 of this law.

#### Art. 28

Confrontation with the accused may only take place with the consent of the victim of the violent offence, unless the confrontation is the only means that guarantees them the right to be exonerated.  
The victim of sexual offences may request to be heard in the presence of a psychologist or a social worker.

#### Art. 29

The child victim of sexual offences must be heard in the presence of a psychologist or a social worker. The observations of the latter are recorded in a report drawn up for this purpose.  
The child victim of sexual offences may only be heard once. Their hearing must be recorded in such a way as to preserve the voice and image.  
Confrontation with the accused in sexual offences is prohibited when the victim is a child.

### Section 2 – De la demande de protection

#### Art. 30

The family judge is seized of the examination of the application for protection following a written request from:

	the victim in person or their representative,
	the public prosecutor with the victim's consent,
	the child protection delegate if the victim is a child or in the event of the existence of a child.  

The family judge may seize themselves of the examination of the granting of protection.  

The seizing of the family judge with the application for protection does not preclude the victim's right to bring substantive proceedings before the competent civil and criminal courts.

#### Art. 31

The application for protection includes a statement of reasons, the measures requested, their duration, and where applicable, the amount of maintenance and housing allowance. The necessary supporting documents are attached to the application for protection.

#### Art. 32

The family judge rules on the application for protection in accordance with the procedures provided for summary proceedings before the cantonal court.  
The family judge receives the statements of the parties and hears any person whose hearing is deemed useful. They may be assisted in their work by officers of the public social services.

#### Art. 33

The family judge may take, by virtue of the protection order, one of the following measures:
	prohibit the defendant party from contacting the victim or the children residing with them, at the family home, at the place of work or place of study, at the accommodation centre or in any place where they may be,
	in the event of danger threatening the victim or their children residing with them, require the defendant party to leave the family home where the victim and their children reside, while allowing them to recover their personal effects, by virtue of a report drawn up for this purpose, at their expense, by a notary bailiff,
	require the defendant party not to cause harm to the private property of the victim or their children covered by the protection order, or to the common property, and not to dispose of it,
	designate the accommodation of the victim and the children residing with them, and where applicable, require the defendant party to pay the housing allowance, unless the competent court has been seized of the matter or a judgment has been pronounced to that effect,
	allow the victim in person or their representative, in the event of departure from the family accommodation, to recover their personal effects and the necessary belongings of their children, by virtue of a report drawn up for this purpose by a notary bailiff, at the expense of the defendant party,
	deprive the defendant party of custody or guardianship and set the procedures for visitation rights while giving priority to the best interests of the child,
	determine the amount of maintenance for the wife who is a victim of violence and the children, and where applicable, the contribution of each of the spouses to the maintenance, unless the competent court has been seized of the examination of maintenance or a judgment has been pronounced to that effect.

#### Art. 34

The order must provide for the duration of protection, which must not exceed, in all cases, six
(6) months.  
The family judge may extend the duration of the protection order issued by them or by the court of appeal, once only for the same duration, by virtue of a reasoned decision subject to the same procedures provided for in articles 30, 31 and 32 of this law.

#### Art. 35

The decisions of the family judge are subject to appeal. They are not subject to appeal in cassation.

#### Art. 36

The public prosecutor executes the protection orders and those for their extension.

#### Art. 37

Anyone who resists or prevents the execution of orders and protective measures is punished by a maximum of six (6) months' imprisonment and a fine of one thousand dinars or both penalties.  
The attempt is punishable.

#### Art. 38

Anyone who wilfully violates the orders and protective measures after their execution is punished by one year's imprisonment and a fine of five thousand dinars.  
The attempt is punishable.

### Section 3 – Des services et institutions

#### Art. 39

Persons responsible for protecting women from violence, including judicial police officers, child protection delegates, health personnel, women's affairs, family, social affairs, education and others, must:
	respond without delay to any request for assistance and protection, submitted directly by the victim,
	respond immediately to any request for assistance or protection within the meaning of article 14 of this law,
	give priority to alerts concerning the commission of violence threatening the physical, sexual and psychological safety of the woman and the children residing with her,
	ensure listening and examination when receiving complaints, by meeting the parties and witnesses, including children, in separate rooms while ensuring their integrity,
	inform the complainant of all her rights,
	intervene, in the event of loss of accommodation, due to violence, to ensure accommodation in protection centres for women victims of violence.

#### Art. 40

A national observatory for the fight against violence against women is created, under the supervision of the ministry responsible for women.  
The observatory is responsible, in particular, for the following missions:
	detecting cases of violence against women, in light of the reports and information collected, while archiving these cases and their impacts in a database created for this purpose,
	monitoring the implementation of legislation and policies, evaluating their effectiveness and efficiency in eliminating violence against women, and publishing reports to this effect while proposing necessary reforms,
	conducting the necessary scientific and field research concerning violence against women in order to evaluate the required interventions and to address the forms of violence as provided for by this law,
	contributing to the development of national strategies, common and sectoral practical measures, and defining the guiding principles for the elimination of violence against women in accordance with this law,
	ensuring cooperation and coordination with civil society organizations, constitutional bodies and other public bodies concerned with monitoring and controlling respect for human rights, with a view to developing and strengthening the system of rights and freedoms,

	issuing opinions on training and learning programs, empowering all actors in the field of violence against women, proposing appropriate mechanisms to develop them and ensuring their monitoring,
The observatory shall prepare an annual report on its activity, including in particular statistics on violence against women, the conditions of reception, accommodation, monitoring, support and integration of victims of violence, the follow-up of protection orders, related actions and judgments, proposals and recommendations for developing national mechanisms for the elimination of violence against women.  
The report is submitted to the President of the Republic, to the president of the assembly of people's representatives and to the head of government during the first quarter of each year. Said report is made public.  
The observatory may also issue statements on its activities and programs.  
The administrative and financial organization of the observatory, as well as its operating procedures are fixed by governmental decree.

#### Art. 41

The ministry of women receives from all ministries and public bodies concerned, each in what concerns it, reports and data relating to violence against women which it transmits to the national observatory for the prevention of violence against women.

## CHAPITRE V – Final provisions

#### Art. 42

The provisions of paragraph 4 of article 218, articles 226 quater, 228 bis, 229, 239 and paragraph 2 of article 319 of the penal code are repealed.

#### Art. 43

The provisions of articles 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 10 of law no. 65-25 of 1 July 1965, relating to the situation of domestic employees, as amended by law no. 2005-32 of 4 April 2005, are repealed.

#### Art. 44

The provisions of this law enter into force within a period of six (6) months from its publication in the Official Gazette of the Tunisian Republic.  
This law shall be published in the Official Gazette of the Tunisian Republic and executed as law of the State.
