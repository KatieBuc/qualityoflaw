The National Assembly, in protection of the fundamental human rights to life, physical integrity and dignity that belong to every person, in accordance with international conventions and the Fundamental Law, adopts the following act in order to reduce domestic violence:2
I. Chapter
Interpretative provisions

## 1. § (1) Domestic violence shall mean

a) activity carried out by the abuser to the detriment of the abused person that seriously and directly endangers dignity, life, the right to sexual self-determination, as well as physical and mental health,
b) omission by the abuser to the detriment of the abused person that seriously and directly endangers dignity, life, as well as physical and mental health (hereinafter together: domestic violence).  
(2) The abused person is the relative to whose detriment domestic violence is committed.  
(3) The abuser is the relative with legal capacity who commits domestic violence, or on whose account another person commits domestic violence, provided that the abuser agrees with this.  
(3a)3 In proceedings for the recognition and enforcement in Hungary of a protection measure ordered in a Member State of the European Union under Regulation (EU) No 606/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 June 2013 on mutual recognition of protection measures in civil matters (hereinafter: Regulation 606/2013/EU), the abused person referred to in paragraph (2) shall also mean the protected person within the meaning of Regulation 606/2013/EU, and the abuser referred to in paragraph (3) shall also mean the person causing the risk within the meaning of Regulation 606/2013/EU.  
(4) 4 For the purposes of this Act, a child shall mean a minor as defined in the Civil Code (hereinafter: Ptk.).  
(5) 5 For the purposes of this Act, a relative shall mean close relatives and relatives as defined in the Ptk., as well as former spouses, former cohabitants, registered partners, former registered partners, guardians, persons under guardianship, custodians, persons under custody.  
(6) For the purposes of this Act, a time limit established in days shall mean continuity.  
II. Chapter
The institutional system serving the prevention of domestic violence and the obligation to report

## 2. §

(1) Tasks related to the prevention of domestic violence shall be performed within the framework of their statutory core activities by
a) health service providers, in particular the health visitor service, general practitioners, general paediatricians,
b) providers of personal care services, in particular family support services, family support centres, child welfare services, child welfare centres, temporary homes for children or families,
c) 6 educational institutions, vocational training institutions and educational counselling services,
d) the guardianship authority,
e) the police,
f) the prosecution service,
g) the courts,
h) the probation service,
i) organisations performing victim support and damage mitigation tasks,
j) refugee reception centres, temporary accommodation for refugees,
k) 7 associations, foundations.  
(2) The institutions and persons specified in paragraph (1) shall be obliged to report to the body responsible for family protection coordination if they detect a risk of domestic violence.  
(3) Persons other than the institutions specified in paragraph (1) may also make the report specified in paragraph (2).  
(4) The institutions specified in paragraph (1) and the body responsible for family protection coordination shall be obliged to cooperate with each other and to inform each other mutually in order to prevent domestic violence and to reduce the harm caused by domestic violence that has occurred. Where another person as referred to in paragraph (3) makes a report or initiates proceedings, the obligation of the institutions specified in paragraphs (1)-(2) and the body responsible for family protection coordination towards that other person shall be limited to providing feedback on the initiation of proceedings.

(5) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall initiate disciplinary proceedings against the person referred to in paragraph (1)(a)-(j) or the employee of the body if they fail to comply with the obligation to report and cooperate as set out in paragraphs (2) and (4).

## 3. §

(1) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall hear the abused person in person upon their request. In this case, the abuser shall also be heard, provided that at the request of the abused person, the abuser and the abused person shall be heard separately. At the request of the abused person, appropriate arrangements shall also be made to ensure that the abuser cannot make contact with the abused person immediately before and immediately after the personal hearing.  
(2) The body responsible for family protection coordination may, following receipt of a report of domestic violence or the risk thereof, having regard to the exceptional gravity of domestic violence, summon the abused person and the abuser to appear for a personal hearing. The abuser and the abused person shall be obliged to appear at the place and time specified in the summons of the body responsible for family protection coordination. The rules set out in paragraph (1) for the protection of the abused person shall apply mutatis mutandis to summonses to appear for a personal hearing.  
(3) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall inform the abused person of the measures applicable in cases of domestic violence, the rights of the abused person, the social institutional system and services for the protection of families and children, and whether there are grounds for initiating criminal or misdemeanour proceedings in respect of domestic violence, as well as the consequences of false accusation. At the request of the abused person, the body responsible for family protection coordination shall ensure that the abused person receives legal, medical, psychological and mental health assistance.  
(4) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall inform the abuser of the consequences of continuing domestic violence.  
(5) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall inform the abused person and the abuser of the therapeutic treatments and other assistance and conflict management options available. It shall provide assistance in accessing these upon request.  
(6) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall record in minutes what was said at the personal hearing and, taking into account the will of the abused person, shall take the necessary measures to prevent or stop domestic violence.  
(7) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall inform the police without delay of its proceedings and the facts established.

## 4. §

(1) The body responsible for family protection coordination may process the following personal data relating to the abuser and the abused person:
a) natural identification data,
b) address of residence, address of place of stay, address for notification (correspondence), address of accommodation,
c) facts and data relating to domestic violence or the risk thereof.  
(2) The personal data specified in paragraph (1) may be processed by the body responsible for family protection coordination until the initiation of proceedings initiated on account of domestic violence.  
(3) If no proceedings are initiated, the data specified in paragraph (1) may be processed for a maximum of three months.  
III. Chapter
TEMPORARY PREVENTIVE AND PREVENTIVE RESTRAINING ORDERS

General provisions

## 5. §

(1) Temporary preventive restraining and preventive restraining temporarily restrict the abuser's freedom of residence, the right to free choice of place of residence, parental custodial rights, and the right to contact with his or her child.
(2) A person against whom temporary preventive restraining or preventive restraining has been ordered is obliged during the period of restraining to
a) keep away from the abused person,
b) keep away from the property serving as the habitual residence of the abused person,
c) keep away from other persons specified in the temporary preventive restraining order or the preventive restraining order, and
d) refrain from contacting the abused person directly or indirectly.
(3) If the abused person uses the property serving as his or her habitual residence jointly with the abuser—regardless of the legal title of use—the abuser is obliged to keep away from the property serving as the habused person's habitual residence if the abused person has a legal title to use the property other than courtesy accommodation, or raises a child in common with the abuser.
(4) The temporary preventive restraining order or preventive restraining order does not affect the abuser's maintenance obligations existing under other legislation or contract, nor the payment obligations relating to the maintenance of the property used jointly with the abused person.
(5) The same abused person may request the ordering of temporary preventive restraining or preventive restraining against the same abuser again only if the conditions for their ordering as defined in this Act are fulfilled again.
(6) 8 When issuing the temporary preventive restraining order or preventive restraining order, the police or the court shall inform the abuser of the location, address and conditions of use of institutions providing overnight accommodation available at his or her place of residence and in the vicinity, and shall inform the abused person of the possibility and conditions of issuing a certificate under Regulation 606/2013/EU.
(7) Temporary preventive restraining or preventive restraining ceases to have effect if the court orders restraining in criminal proceedings brought against the abuser.
Temporary preventive restraining

## 6. §

(1)9 The provisions of Act CL of 2016 on the General Rules of Administrative Procedure (hereinafter: Ákr.) shall apply to proceedings related to temporary preventive restraining due to domestic violence between relatives, with the following derogations.
(2) The police may order temporary preventive restraining
a) ex officio on the basis of facts relating to domestic violence between relatives observed during on-site intervention,
b) 10 upon notification by the abused person, or a close relative and relative of the abused person as defined in the Civil Code, as well as a registered partner,
c) upon notification—made on the basis of facts relating to domestic violence between relatives that came to his or her knowledge in the course of performing his or her duties—by a person employed in a position defined in the Act on the Protection of Children and Guardianship Administration and in the Act on Social Administration and Social Benefits,

d) upon notification—made on the basis of facts relating to domestic violence between relatives that came to its knowledge in the course of performing duties defined by law for the prevention and elimination of child endangerment—by a health care service provider, personal care service provider, or public education institution performing tasks related to the child protection system pursuant to the Act on the Protection of Children and Guardianship Administration.
(3) The police shall order temporary preventive restraining if the commission of domestic violence between relatives can be reasonably inferred from all the circumstances of the case, including in particular the facts presented by the abuser and the abused person, the scene of the domestic violence between relatives, signs indicating domestic violence between relatives, the conduct of the abuser and the abused person and their relationship to each other.
(4) In the case referred to in paragraph (3), the police shall order temporary preventive restraining for 72 hours.
(5) 11 At the request of the abused person, the police shall issue a certificate pursuant to Article 5 of Regulation 606/2013/EU (hereinafter: protection certificate).
Time limit for settlement

## 7. §

(1) The police shall issue the temporary preventive restraining order without delay, on site. If the police apply the police measure of apprehension against the abuser during its proceedings, the police shall issue the order no later than the expiry of the duration of apprehension.
(1a)12 In the case of on-site intervention, the police shall issue the protection certificate on site, or if the conditions therefor are not met, no later than 12 hours from the time of the on-site proceedings. If the police apply the police measure of apprehension against the abuser during its proceedings, the police shall issue the protection certificate simultaneously with the issuance of the order on temporary preventive restraining.
(2)13 Simultaneously with ordering temporary preventive restraining, the police shall, by simultaneously sending the temporary preventive restraining order and the protection certificate, initiate the ordering of preventive restraining at the competent district court.
Temporary preventive restraining order
8. §14 The operative part of the temporary preventive restraining order shall contain:
a) the decision of the police that
aa) the abuser is obliged to leave the property serving as the habitual residence of the abused person and may not return there during the period of validity of the temporary preventive restraining order, or
ab) the obligation set out in Section 5(2)(b) does not apply to the abuser during the period of temporary preventive restraining—pursuant to Section 5(3),
b) the address of the property serving as the habitual residence of the abused person,
c) the decision of the police that the abuser is obliged to keep away from the abused person and from other persons specified in the order,
d) the decision of the police that the abuser is prohibited from contacting the abused person directly or indirectly, or other persons with regard to him or her, including in particular other persons specified in the order ordering temporary preventive restraining,

e) the finding that the parental custodial right of the abuser parent, or the right to contact with his or her minor child, is suspended,
f) provisions on the immediate enforceability of the order, the method of enforcement, the procedure for implementation,
g) a warning that the temporary preventive restraining order does not affect the abuser's maintenance obligations existing under other legislation or contract, nor the payment obligations relating to the maintenance of the property used jointly with the abused person,
h) a warning that breach of the obligations prescribed in the temporary preventive restraining order constitutes a petty offence.
Service of the temporary preventive restraining order and the protection certificate15

## 9. §

(1) The decision ordering temporary preventive restraining must be communicated to the abuser and the abused person, and must be handed over to them or delivered by the shortest possible route. In the absence of a specified address, the decision may be collected at the police station.
(2) If the abuser does not collect the decision at the police station, the decision shall be deemed to have been served on the third day following its adoption. Failure to meet this deadline may be excused in accordance with the provisions of the Ákr.
(2a) The police shall communicate to the abused person the protection certificate, and to the abuser the notification concerning the protection certificate pursuant to Article 8(1) of Regulation (EU) No 606/2013, in accordance with paragraphs (1) and (2).
(3) At the request of the abused person, or with their consent, the police shall send the decision to the competent family support service, and to the child welfare service if a minor lives in the household of the abused person. If the abused person is a person under guardianship or curatorship, the police shall also send the decision to the guardianship authority according to the permanent residence of the ward or the person under curatorship.
(4) On the basis of the notification by the police, within twenty-four hours following the adoption of the restraining decision, the family support service, or in the case of a cohabiting minor relative the child welfare service, is obliged to contact the abused person and the abuser and initiate a helping relationship with them, and to take measures to eliminate the situation endangering the minor.
Appeal
10. § The decision is not subject to appeal.
Information
11. § At the commencement of the proceedings, the police is obliged to provide information to the abused person about victim support, social and legal assistance bodies, organisations and institutions, civil support organisations, and also about the available financial assistance possibilities, and furthermore, at the request of the abused person, to provide assistance in connection with the use of the protection certificate in another Member State.

Preventive restraining

## 13. §

(1) The court shall decide on the ordering of preventive restraining in civil non-contentious proceedings.
(2) In non-contentious proceedings for ordering preventive restraining, the provisions of Act CXX of 2016 on the Code of Civil Procedure (hereinafter: Pp.) shall apply, with derogations arising from the specificities of non-contentious proceedings and the general provisions on judicial civil non-contentious proceedings of the Act on the rules to be applied in judicial civil non-contentious proceedings and on certain judicial non-contentious proceedings, to those procedural matters which are not regulated differently by this Act.
(3) The district court of the place where the abused person habitually resides has jurisdiction over the proceedings.
(4) The provisions of the Pp. on exclusion shall be applied with the proviso that a police officer acting in the temporary preventive restraining proceedings, as well as their relative within the meaning of the Ptk., may not act as a judge or prosecutor.
(5) The prosecutor may participate in the proceedings for judicial ordering of preventive restraining; the court conducting the proceedings shall notify the prosecutor without delay of the commencement of the proceedings.
(6) A court clerk may not act in the proceedings.

(1) Judicial non-contentious proceedings for ordering preventive restraining shall be initiated ex officio by the police if it has ordered temporary preventive restraining. (2) In proceedings initiated ex officio by the police, or in the case of initiation of judicial non-contentious proceedings for ordering preventive restraining by the body responsible for family protection coordination, all available files and documents shall be made available to the court.
(3) In proceedings initiated upon application, the application must contain:

## 15. §

(1) During the proceedings, the court is obliged to attempt to hear the parties in person at least once. The court may also request the police — if necessary by electronic means — to deliver the summons for personal hearing. The summons is proper if the party has been demonstrably notified of the hearing.
(2) In proceedings initiated ex officio, it is not an obstacle to decision-making if neither party appears in person at the hearing.
(3) In proceedings initiated upon request, if the battered applicant does not appear in person and does not excuse their absence with good reason, and if the application does not contain the elements specified in Section 14(3), the court shall terminate the proceedings. In proceedings initiated upon request, it is not an obstacle to making a decision if the batterer does not appear in person at the hearing.
(3a) The parties must be warned in the summons for personal hearing of the provisions set out in subsections (2) and (3).
(4) In the proceedings, rectification of deficiencies is only possible in proceedings initiated upon request, in exceptionally justified cases, in the event of deficiencies in the application. In such case, the court shall call upon the parties to rectify deficiencies within one day of receipt of the application.
(5) There is no place for suspension or stay in the proceedings.
(6) In the case of proceedings initiated ex officio, the proceedings must be continued within three days from the starting time of the temporary preventive restraining order imposed by the police; in the case of proceedings initiated upon request, within three working days from receipt of the application.
(7) At the personal hearing, the court — having regard to the facts contained in the police files or in the application, in particular the manner and regularity of domestic violence, the vulnerable situation of the battered person — may dispense with hearing the battered person and the batterer in each other's presence, in part or in full. In justified cases, the court shall also ensure that the battered person and the batterer do not meet in person in the court building. In such case, Section 16(3) shall apply mutatis mutandis to the communication of the decision.

## 16. §

(1) The court shall order preventive restraining if, from all the circumstances of the case, in particular from the facts presented by the batterer and the battered person, from signs indicating domestic violence, from the conduct and relationship of the batterer and the battered person, the commission of domestic violence can be reasonably inferred.
(2) The court may order preventive restraining for a maximum of sixty days.
(2a) The court's decision is provisionally enforceable. In its decision, the court also warns the batterer that violation of the rules of the restraining order constitutes a petty offence punishable by detention.
(2b) Simultaneously with ordering preventive restraining — upon the battered person's request to that effect — the court shall issue a protection certificate.
(3) The court shall communicate its decision to the parties at the hearing and hand it over to them. If the batterer did not appear at the hearing, the court shall arrange for service through the police — if necessary by electronic means. The police are obliged to ensure service of the decision and to inform the court without delay of the completion of service.
(3a) The court shall communicate the protection certificate to the battered person and the notification concerning the protection certificate pursuant to Article 8(1) of Regulation 606/2013/EU to the batterer in accordance with subsection (3).
(4) The effect of the temporary preventive restraining order imposed by the police shall cease upon the entry into effect of the preventive restraining order imposed by the court.
(5) The effect of the temporary preventive restraining order imposed by the police shall also cease if the court does not order preventive restraining.
(6) In the event of non-compliance with the provisions of the decision, there is no place for judicial enforcement.
(7) An appeal may be lodged against the court's decision within three working days from communication. The person entitled to appeal may also announce the appeal orally at the personal hearing.
(8) In the event of an appeal being lodged, the first instance court — once the appeal deadline has expired for all persons entitled to appeal — shall immediately submit the files to the second instance court. The parties may submit their observations on the appeal to the first instance court until the files are submitted, and to the second instance court after the files are submitted. If a party submits their observations on the appeal to the first instance court after the files have been submitted, the first instance court shall immediately forward them to the second instance court.
(9) The second instance court shall adjudicate the appeal on the basis of the files within 3 working days. Setting aside the first instance decision pursuant to Section 381 of the Code of Civil Procedure is not possible. The second instance court shall communicate its decision to the parties through the police.
(10) If the first instance court issued a protection certificate to the battered person together with ordering preventive restraining, upon the request of the battered person or the batterer
a) in the case of correction, supplementation or revocation of the protection certificate within its own jurisdiction, of that fact,
b) if, as a result of the appeal, the second instance court modified or set aside the decision ordering preventive restraining, of that fact
the first instance court that issued the protection certificate shall issue a certificate pursuant to Article 14 of Regulation 606/2013/EU.

## 17. §

If the court detects circumstances giving rise to temporary preventive restraining in civil proceedings, it shall communicate the data relating to that fact to the police — as a matter of priority.

Proceedings in connection with requests submitted on the basis of a protective measure ordered in another Member State

## 17/A. §

(1) The rules on preventive restraining shall apply mutatis mutandis to the recognition and enforcement in Hungary of a protective measure ordered in another Member State, initiated pursuant to Article 4 of Regulation 606/2013/EU, with the derogations set out in this Section, with the proviso that a request within the meaning of Article 4 of the Regulation (hereinafter in the application of this Section: request) shall be considered an application.
(2) The court shall examine the request and within 3 working days
a) call upon the battered person to rectify deficiencies if the request does not comply with the requirements laid down in the Regulation,
b) order preventive restraining by modifying the protective measure underlying the request pursuant to Article 11 of Regulation 606/2013/EU,
c) order preventive restraining in accordance with the content of the protective measure underlying the request, or
d) reject the request upon the batterer's request to that effect pursuant to Article 13(1) of Regulation 606/2013/EU.
(3) If the battered person does not comply with the call referred to in subsection (2)(a), the court shall reject the request. A rejected request may be submitted again.
(4) In the cases referred to in subsection (2)(a) and (c), the court shall not attempt, and in the cases referred to in subsection (2)(b) and (d), shall only attempt as necessary, to hear the parties in person.
(5) The court shall order preventive restraining for the period specified in the protective measure underlying the request, but for a maximum of 12 months.
(6) The battered person and the batterer may lodge an appeal against the court's decision pursuant to subsection (2)(d) and subsection (3), as well as against the part of the decision pursuant to subsection (2)(b) that modifies the protective measure underlying the request.

## 17/B. §

## §52

(1) The certificate pursuant to Article 14(2) of Regulation (EU) No 606/2013 (hereinafter referred to in this section as: certificate) shall be submitted on the basis of the protective measure underlying the certificate to the court that ordered the preventive restraining order pursuant to Section 17/A(2) point b) or c).  
(2) Within 3 working days following the submission of the certificate, the court shall
a) amend its decision ordering the preventive restraining order if, according to the certificate, the protective measure underlying the preventive restraining order or its enforcement has been modified in the Member State of origin,
b) suspend it if, according to the certificate, the protective measure underlying the preventive restraining order or its enforcement has been suspended in the Member State of origin, or
c) repeal its decision ordering it if, according to the certificate, the protective measure underlying the preventive restraining order or the certificate issued on its basis pursuant to Article 5 of Regulation (EU) No 606/2013 has been revoked in the Member State of origin.  
(3) No appeal shall lie against the decision of the court set out in paragraph (2).
