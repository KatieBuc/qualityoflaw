C R I M I N A L C O D E (KZ-1)

(unofficial consolidated text No. 12)

GENERAL PART

First Chapter FUNDAMENTAL PROVISIONS

Enforcement of criminal liability

## 1. člen

(1) Criminal liability in the Republic of Slovenia may be enforced with respect for constitutionally guaranteed human rights and fundamental freedoms in a democratic order and on the principles of the rule of law.  
(2) Under this Code, criminal liability is enforced by punishing adults for committed criminal offences on the basis of established guilt.  
(3) Criminal liability is not enforced against a person whose guilt is excluded because at the time of commission they were not of sound mind. Such a person may be imposed measures determined by this Code for an act that has the characteristics of a criminal offence and is contrary to law (unlawful act).  

No criminal offence and no punishment without law

## 2. člen

No one may be imposed a punishment or other criminal sanction for an act which the law has not determined to be a criminal offence before it was committed, and for which a punishment or other criminal sanction has not been prescribed by law.  

System of criminal sanctions

## 3. člen

(1) Criminal sanctions are: punishments, warning sanctions and security measures.  
(2) For a criminal offence, a punishment is always prescribed by law, which is imposed on the perpetrator (hereinafter: perpetrator) in proportion to the gravity of the act and their guilt. Under the conditions determined in the general part of this Code, the perpetrator may be imposed warning sanctions instead of punishment and, in addition to punishment or warning sanction, also security measures.  
(3) Under the conditions determined by this Code, the perpetrator is also imposed confiscation of pecuniary benefit and publication of the judgment for a committed criminal offence.  
(4) Where regulations provide that, due to the conviction itself for a criminal offence, the convicted person (hereinafter: convicted person) shall, in addition to the imposed criminal sanctions, be deprived of or restricted in any rights, the prohibition from the preceding Article also applies to such legal consequences.  
(5) Under the conditions determined by this Code, a perpetrator who is not guilty because at the time of commission of the unlawful act they were not of sound mind may be imposed security measures, confiscation of pecuniary benefit and publication of the judgment. The provisions on legal consequences of conviction also apply to such perpetrators.  

Second Chapter VALIDITY OF THE CRIMINAL CODE

1. Personal validity

Equal validity of the criminal law

## 4. člen

(1) Unless otherwise exceptionally provided in this Code, the criminal code applies equally to all adults, regardless of whether they are citizens (hereinafter: citizen) of the Republic of Slovenia or foreigners (hereinafter: foreigner).  
(2) A criminal law provision which exceptionally relates only to citizens of the Republic of Slovenia shall not apply to citizens of other Member States of the European Union and other foreigners.  
(3) If a criminal law provision exceptionally applies only to foreigners, it may also determine when citizens of other Member States of the European Union are not considered foreigners.  

Special personal validity

## 5. člen

(1) If the law provides that only persons with special characteristics, rights or position are punished for a criminal offence, the criminal law applies equally to all such persons.  
(2) Special criminal laws determine the criminal liability of juveniles (hereinafter: juvenile) and legal persons.  
(3) A special law determining the criminal liability of juveniles may also provide that persons who were already adults at the time of commission of the criminal offence but were not yet twenty-one years old (younger adults) may, instead of punishment, be imposed criminal sanctions for juveniles according to their personal development.  

Exclusion of personal validity

## 6. člen

(1) The criminal law does not apply to acts of persons whose criminal liability is excluded due to immunity under the provisions of the constitution or rules of international law.  
(2) Where the law provides that a perpetrator is prosecuted for a criminal offence upon a motion or upon a private action, such provision does not apply against a person against whom the injured party (hereinafter: injured party) has not submitted a motion or has not filed a private action or has withdrawn it.  

2. Temporal validity

Application of a subsequent, more lenient law for the perpetrator

## 7. člen

(1) For the perpetrator of a criminal offence, the law that was in force at the time of commission of the criminal offence applies.  

(2) If after the commission of the criminal offence the law changes (once or several times), the law that is more lenient for the perpetrator applies.  

Temporally limited laws

## 8. člen

If a law or other regulation to which the criminal law refers is valid only for a certain time, it applies to a criminal offence committed during the time of its validity, even after the expiry of that time, unless otherwise provided.  

Concurrent validity of the general part of this Code and other criminal laws

## 9. člen

(1) The provisions of the general part of this Code also apply to criminal offences determined by other laws or ratified and published international treaties or acts of the European Union, unless otherwise provided by these acts.  
(2) If laws determining criminal liability for juveniles, legal persons or other special types of perpetrators or acts (special criminal laws) mention in their general provisions the concurrent application of the general part of this Code, the last valid code applies.  

3. Territorial validity

Validity of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia for anyone who commits a criminal offence on its territory

## 10. člen

(1)	The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia applies to anyone who commits a criminal offence on the territory of the Republic of Slovenia.  
(2)	The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to anyone who commits a criminal offence on a domestic vessel, regardless of where the vessel was at the time of commission of the offence.  
(3)	The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to anyone who commits a criminal offence on a domestic civil aircraft during flight or on a state aircraft, regardless of where the aircraft was at the time of commission of the offence.  

Validity of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia for certain criminal offences committed abroad

## 11. člen

The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia applies to anyone who commits abroad
	a criminal offence under Article 243 of this Code and criminal offences under Articles 332, 333 and 334 of this Code, if they are committed in the ecological protection zone or continental shelf of the Republic of Slovenia, and	criminal offences under Article 108 and Articles 348 to 360 of this Code.  

Validity of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia for a citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who commits a criminal offence abroad

## 12. člen

The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia applies to a citizen of the Republic of Slovenia, even if he commits abroad any other criminal offence in addition to the criminal offences listed in the preceding Article.  

Validity of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia for a foreigner who commits a criminal offence abroad

## 13. člen

(1)	The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to a foreigner who commits outside the Republic of Slovenia, against it or its citizen, a criminal offence, even if these are not criminal offences under Article 11 of this Code.  
(2)	The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to a foreigner who commits against a foreign state or against a foreigner abroad a criminal offence, if he is apprehended on the territory of the Republic of Slovenia and is not extradited to a foreign state. In such a case, the court may not impose a more severe penalty than that prescribed by the law of the state in which the criminal offence was committed.  
(3)	The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to anyone who commits abroad any other criminal offence which, under an international treaty or under general legal principles recognised by the international community, is prosecuted in all states, regardless of where it was committed.  

Special conditions for prosecution

## 14. člen

(1)	If in the cases under Article 10 and the first indent of Article 11 of this Code criminal proceedings have been initiated or concluded abroad, the perpetrator is prosecuted in the Republic of Slovenia with the permission of the Minister of Justice, with an indication of the conditions under which prosecution does not violate the prohibition of double jeopardy.  
(2)	In the cases under Article 12 and the first and second paragraphs of Article 13 of this Code, the perpetrator is not prosecuted:
1)	if the penalty for which he was convicted abroad has been fully served or if it has been determined in accordance with an international treaty that he will serve the penalty imposed abroad in the Republic of Slovenia;  
2)	if he was acquitted abroad by a final judgment or the penalty was remitted or the enforcement of the penalty has become time-barred;  

3)	if the criminal offence is prosecuted under foreign law at the request of the injured party, and such request has not been filed or has been withdrawn.  
(3)	In the cases under Article 12 and the first and second paragraphs of Article 13 of this Code, the perpetrator is prosecuted only if the act is also punishable under the law of the state in which it was committed.  
(4)	If in the case under Article 12 of this Code the act is not punishable under the law of the state in which it was committed, but this act, committed against the Republic of Slovenia or its citizen, the perpetrator may be prosecuted only with the permission of the Minister of Justice.  
(5)	In all other cases except the cases under the second indent of Article 11, the third paragraph of Article 13 and the fourth paragraph of this Article of this Code, when the act was committed abroad but in the state in which it was committed is not punishable, the perpetrator may be prosecuted with the permission of the Minister of Justice, if the act was considered a criminal offence at the time it was committed under general legal principles recognised by the international community.  
(6)	In the case under Article 10 of this Code, the prosecution of a foreigner may be transferred to a foreign state under the conditions determined by law.  
(7)	In the cases under the third paragraph of Article 13 of this Code, the perpetrator is prosecuted only with the permission of the Minister of Justice.  

Crediting of deprivation of liberty abroad

## 15. člen

Detention ordered abroad and any deprivation of liberty during extradition proceedings shall be credited towards the sentence from a conviction before a domestic court, and likewise any sentence already served pursuant to a judgment of a foreign court shall be credited towards it, if it becomes known later. If the penalties are not of the same type, the court shall decide how it is to be credited. If the convicted person has served more penalties in total than the sentence imposed on him in the conviction before the domestic court, the excess shall be deemed unjustly served.  

3.a Prosecution of criminal offences against minors

Manner of prosecution of perpetrators of criminal offences committed to the detriment of minors

15.a	člen
In cases where a criminal offence from the chapters against life and limb, against human rights and freedoms, against sexual integrity or another criminal offence under this Code with signs of violence is committed against a minor, the provisions of this Code on filing a motion or private prosecution do not apply with regard to the manner of criminal prosecution and the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio.  

Third Chapter
GENERAL PROVISIONS ON CRIMINAL OFFENCES

1.	Criminal offence and perpetrator

Criminal offence

## 16. člen

A criminal offence is an unlawful human act which the law, for the necessary protection of legal values, defines as a criminal offence and at the same time determines its elements and the penalty for the guilty perpetrator.  

Manner of commission of a criminal offence

## 17. člen

(1)	A criminal offence may be committed by commission or omission.  
(2)	A criminal offence may be committed by omission only if the perpetrator omitted an act which he should have performed.  
(3)	A criminal offence which the law does not define as an omissive criminal offence may also be committed by omission if the perpetrator does not prevent the prohibited consequence. In such a case, the perpetrator is punished for omission only if he was required to prevent the occurrence of the prohibited consequence and if the omission is of equal significance for the occurrence of such consequence as commission.  

Time of commission of a criminal offence

## 18. člen

A criminal offence is committed at the time when the perpetrator acted or should have acted, regardless of when the consequence occurs.  

Place of commission of a criminal offence

## 19. člen

(1)	A criminal offence is committed at the place where the perpetrator acted or should have acted, as well as at the place where the prohibited consequence occurred.  
(2)	An attempt of a criminal offence is deemed to have been committed at the place where the perpetrator acted, as well as at the place where, according to his intent, the prohibited consequence was to occur or could have occurred.  

Perpetrator and co-perpetrator

## 20. člen

(1)	The perpetrator of a criminal offence is anyone who commits it personally or by exploiting and directing the conduct of another (indirect perpetrator).  
(2)	The perpetrator of a criminal offence is also anyone who commits a criminal offence together with another by consciously participating in the commission or otherwise decisively contributing to the commission (co-perpetrator).  

Limitations on criminal liability with regard to the age of perpetrators

## 21. člen

A person who committed an unlawful act when he was not yet fourteen years old (child) cannot be a perpetrator of a criminal offence.  

Self-defence

## 22. člen

(1)	An act committed in self-defence is not unlawful.  
(2)	Self-defence is defence that is necessarily required for the perpetrator to avert from himself or another a simultaneous unlawful attack.  
(3)	A perpetrator who has exceeded the limits of self-defence may be punished more leniently; if he exceeded self-defence due to strong agitation or fear caused by the attack, the penalty may also be remitted.  

Duress

## 23. člen

An act committed under the influence of force which the perpetrator could not resist is not a criminal offence.  

2.	Guilt and punishability of perpetrators

Guilt

## 24. člen

A perpetrator is guilty if, at the time of committing the criminal offence, they were of sound mind and acted with intent or negligence, while being aware or should and could have been aware that they were acting contrary to law, and if there are no grounds that exclude guilt.

Intent

## 25. člen

A criminal offence is committed with intent if the perpetrator was aware of their act and wanted to commit it (direct intent), or if they were aware that they could commit the act and consented to it (eventual intent).

Negligence

## 26. člen

A criminal offence is committed through negligence if the perpetrator did not act with the necessary care, even though they were aware that they could commit the act but recklessly thought that it would not happen or that they could prevent it (conscious negligence), or if they were not aware that they could commit the act but, according to the circumstances and their personal characteristics, should and could have been aware of it (unconscious negligence).

Punishability of negligence

## 27. člen

(1)	For a criminal offence committed through negligence, the perpetrator is punished only if the law so provides.
(2)	The court may remit the sentence of a perpetrator who has committed a criminal offence through negligence if the consequences of the act affect the perpetrator to such an extent that punishment in such a case would manifestly not be justified.

Liability for a graver consequence

## 28. člen

If a graver consequence has resulted from a criminal offence, for which the law prescribes a more severe sentence, such sentence may be imposed if the perpetrator acted negligently with regard to that consequence.

Unsoundness of mind

## 29. člen

(1)	A person who was not of sound mind at the time of committing an unlawful act is not guilty.
(2)	A perpetrator is not of sound mind if, at the time of committing the unlawful act, they could not understand the meaning of their act or could not control their conduct due to a mental disorder or mental underdevelopment.
(3)	A perpetrator of a criminal offence whose capacity to understand the meaning of their act or capacity to control their conduct was substantially diminished due to a condition from the preceding paragraph or due to some other permanent and serious mental disturbance may be punished more leniently.

(4)	A perpetrator of a criminal offence who brought about unsoundness of mind by using alcohol, drugs, other psychoactive substances or in some other way is guilty if, prior to that, their guilt for the criminal offence as required by law for that act was established.

Mistake of fact

## 30. člen

(1)	A person who, in a mistake of fact, commits an act that the law defines as an intentional criminal offence is not guilty.
(2)	A criminal offence is committed in a mistake of fact if the perpetrator, at the time of commission, was not aware of the circumstances that the law defines as elements of the criminal offence, or mistakenly thought that the circumstances were such that the act would be permissible.
(3)	For a criminal offence committed through negligence, the guilt of the perpetrator cannot be excluded if they were mistaken about circumstances of which, within the limits of necessary care, they should and could have been aware.

Mistake of law

## 31. člen

(1)	A perpetrator of a criminal offence who, for justified reasons, did not know that the act was contrary to law is not guilty.
(2)	There are no justified reasons under the first paragraph of this Article if the perpetrator did not know of legal rules with which they could have become acquainted under the same conditions as others in their broader environment, or if, given their work, role or other position, they should have known special legal rules.
(3)	If the perpetrator committed a criminal offence in a mistake of law that could have been avoided, they may be punished more leniently.

Necessity

## 32. člen

(1)	An act committed in order for the perpetrator to avert from themselves or another person an immediate danger that could not be averted otherwise is not unlawful if the harm caused is less than the harm threatened (justifiable necessity).
(2)	A perpetrator who commits an unlawful act in order to avert from themselves or another person an immediate danger not caused by their fault to life, bodily integrity or personal freedom, which could not be averted otherwise, is not guilty if the harm caused was not disproportionately greater than the harm threatened, and if the perpetrator was not obliged to expose themselves to the danger (excusable necessity).
(3)	A perpetrator who acted in an avoidable mistake that the circumstances of necessity from the preceding paragraph existed is liable for a criminal offence through negligence if the law provides for the punishability of negligence for that act.

(4)	If, in the case under the second paragraph of this Article, the perpetrator themselves caused the danger, but through negligence, or if, in the case under the first or second paragraph of this Article, the perpetrator exceeded the limits of necessity, they may be punished more leniently, but if they exceeded the limits of necessity in particularly mitigating circumstances, the sentence may be remitted.

Exclusion of punishability

## 33. člen

(1)	The law may provide that, due to personal relationships, characteristics or special circumstances, the perpetrator of an unlawful act is not punished.
(2)	The exclusion of prosecution due to non-punishability is not an obstacle to a different legal liability being asserted against the perpetrator for the act committed.

3.	Attempt of a criminal offence

Attempt

## 34. člen

(1)	A person who has commenced an intentional criminal offence but has not completed it is punished for an attempt if it is a criminal offence for which a sentence of three years' imprisonment or a more severe sentence may be imposed under the law; for attempts of other criminal offences, only if the law expressly provides that the attempt is also punishable.
(2)	The perpetrator is punished for an attempt within the limits of the sentence prescribed for the criminal offence, but may also be punished more leniently.

Impossible attempt

## 35. člen

A perpetrator who attempts to commit a criminal offence with an unsuitable means or against an unsuitable object may have the sentence remitted.

Voluntary withdrawal

## 36. člen

(1)	A perpetrator who has attempted to commit a criminal offence but has voluntarily withdrawn from its commission may have the sentence remitted.
(2)	If the perpetrator voluntarily withdraws from the commission of a criminal offence, they are punished for those acts that constitute some other independent criminal offence.

(3)	A perpetrator may have the sentence remitted if they sincerely and appropriately endeavoured to prevent the consequence, even if it subsequently did not occur for another reason.

4.	Participation in a criminal offence

Participant

36.a	člen
The provisions of this Code that apply to the perpetrator also apply to a female or male participant (hereinafter: participant) who, in the context of instigation or aiding and abetting, participates in a criminal offence, unless the law provides otherwise.

Instigation

## 37. člen

(1)	A person who intentionally instigates another to commit a criminal offence is punished as if they had committed it themselves.
(2)	A person who intentionally instigates another to commit a criminal offence for which a sentence of three years' imprisonment or a more severe sentence may be imposed under the law is punished as for an attempt of a criminal offence, even if there was no attempt to commit the act.

Aiding and abetting

## 38. člen

(1)	A person who intentionally aids the perpetrator in an intentional criminal offence is punished as if they had committed it themselves, but may also be punished more leniently.
(2)	Aiding and abetting in the commission of a criminal offence is considered to include, in particular: if a person gives the perpetrator advice or instructions on how to commit a criminal offence, if they make means available to them or remove obstacles to commission, if they promise in advance to conceal the criminal offence, the perpetrator, the means by which the criminal offence will be committed, the traces of the criminal offence, objects created by the criminal offence or the pecuniary benefit obtained by the criminal offence.

Punishment of instigator and aider and abettor for attempt

## 39. člen

If the criminal offence remains at the attempt stage, the female or male instigator (hereinafter: instigator) and the female or male aider and abettor (hereinafter: aider and abettor) are punished as for an attempt.

Limits of punishability of perpetrators and participants

## Article 40

(1) The perpetrator and co-perpetrator shall be punished for criminal offences within the limits of their intent or negligence, and the instigator and accessory within the limits of their intent.
(2) The instigator or accessory may be exempted from punishment if he or she voluntarily prevented the criminal offence.
(3) The same applies if the instigator or accessory sincerely and appropriately endeavoured to prevent the consequence, even if it subsequently did not occur for another reason.
(4) Personal relationships, characteristics and special circumstances on the basis of which the law excludes guilt or culpability or permits exemption, reduction or increase of punishment may be taken into account only with respect to that perpetrator or participant in whom such relationships, characteristics and circumstances are established.

Liability of members and leaders of a criminal organisation

## Article 41

(1) A more severe punishment prescribed for a criminal offence committed in a criminal organisation shall be imposed on a female or male member (hereinafter: member) of an organisation of at least three persons if he or she commits a criminal offence for the purpose of implementing the criminal plan of that organisation in connection with at least one other member as co-perpetrator or participant.
(2) In the case referred to in the first paragraph of this Article, the leader of the organisation who led the implementation of the criminal plan or disposed of the unlawfully obtained property benefit of that organisation at the time of committing the criminal offence arising from that criminal plan shall be punished in the same way as the perpetrator, regardless of whether he or she directly participated in its implementation as perpetrator or participant under Articles 20 or 37 and 38 of this Code.

5. Culpability of legal persons

Liability of legal persons for criminal offences

## Article 42

(1) Criminal liability shall be established against a legal person for a criminal offence committed by a perpetrator in the name, on behalf of or for the benefit of the legal person and which is specified in the law governing the liability of legal persons for criminal offences as one for which the legal person is liable.
(2) The criminal liability of legal persons does not exclude the liability of natural persons as perpetrators, instigators or accessories in the same criminal offence.
(3) The law governing the liability of legal persons for criminal offences shall determine the conditions for criminal liability of legal persons, punishments, warning sanctions or security measures and the legal consequences of conviction for legal persons.

Chapter Four PUNISHMENTS

1. Types of punishments and conditions for their imposition

Types of punishments

## Article 43

For criminal offences, the following punishments may be imposed on guilty perpetrators: imprisonment; fine; prohibition of driving a motor vehicle; expulsion of a foreigner from the country.

Principal and accessory punishments

## Article 44

(1) Imprisonment may be imposed only as a principal punishment.
(2) A fine may be imposed as a principal and also as an accessory punishment.
(3) Prohibition of driving a motor vehicle and expulsion of a foreigner from the country may be imposed only as an accessory punishment in addition to a sentence of imprisonment, a fine or a suspended sentence.
(4) In addition to the principal punishment, one or both accessory punishments may be imposed.

Legality in the imposition of punishments

## Article 45

(1) A perpetrator of a criminal offence shall be imposed the punishment prescribed for the criminal offence committed; a more lenient punishment than that prescribed may be imposed only under the conditions determined by this Code.
(2) For criminal offences committed out of greed, a fine may be imposed as an accessory punishment even when it is not prescribed by law or when it is prescribed by law that the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment or a fine, but the court imposes imprisonment as the principal punishment.

Purpose of punishment

Article 45a
Through punishment under the provisions of this Code, the state protects the fundamental values and principles of the legal order, establishes awareness in the perpetrator of the criminal offence and others of the inadmissibility of committing criminal offences, and above all, while respecting the human dignity and personality of the perpetrator of the criminal offence, enables the perpetrator to be provided with an appropriate sanction that allows for dignified integration into the common social environment.

Imprisonment

## Article 46

(1) Imprisonment may not be shorter than one month and not longer than thirty years.
(2) For criminal offences of genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes and aggression, and under the conditions of point 1 of the second paragraph of Article 53 of this Code for two or more criminal offences under the fifth paragraph of Article 108, under Article 116, under Article 352, under the second paragraph of Article 360, under the fourth paragraph of Article 371 and under the third paragraph of Article 373, a sentence of life imprisonment may be imposed.

(3) A sentence of imprisonment of up to two years shall be prescribed without a minimum measure of that punishment.
(4) Imprisonment shall be imposed in whole years and whole months, and up to six months also in whole

Fine

## Article 47

(1) A fine shall be imposed by multiplying a specified number of daily amounts that the perpetrator must pay, as determined in the judgment, by the amount of the daily amount determined by the court according to the property circumstances of the perpetrator.
(2) The number of daily amounts may not be less than ten and not more than three hundred and sixty, and for criminal offences committed out of greed, not more than one thousand five hundred. The court shall determine it taking into account the general rules on the measurement of punishment, except for circumstances relating to the property circumstances of the perpetrator.
(3) The court shall determine the amount of the daily amount according to the property circumstances of the perpetrator on the basis of data on his or her earnings, other income, the value of his or her property, the average costs of his or her subsistence and his or her family obligations. The daily amount may not exceed 1,000 euros.
(4) In determining the amount of the daily amount, the court shall take into account data that are not more than one year old at the time of imposing the punishment. If the court cannot obtain data for determining the amount of the daily amount or if their acquisition would be associated with disproportionate difficulties or delay, or if a penal order is to be issued, it shall determine the amount of the daily amount on the basis of the data at its disposal and other established circumstances concerning the property circumstances of the perpetrator.
(5) In the judgment, the court shall determine the time limit for payment of the fine, which may not be shorter than fifteen days and not longer than three months. In justified cases, the court may, before the expiry of the time limit for payment, allow the convicted person to pay the fine in instalments, whereby the time limit for payment may not be longer than two years. If the perpetrator is late in paying an individual instalment, the court may order immediate payment by decision within a time limit that may not be longer than three months.

Prohibition of driving a motor vehicle

## Article 48

(1) A perpetrator of a criminal offence who has committed a criminal offence against the safety of public traffic as a female or male driver (hereinafter: driver) of a motor vehicle may be imposed a prohibition of driving a motor vehicle of a certain type or category by the court.
(2) The court shall determine the duration of the punishment referred to in the preceding paragraph, which may not be shorter than six months and not longer than two years, counting from the day the judgment becomes final. Time spent in prison or in a health institution for treatment and care shall not be included in the duration of this punishment.
(3) If the punishment referred to in the first paragraph of this Article is imposed on a person who has a foreign driving licence for a motor vehicle, this punishment includes a prohibition on the use of that licence on the territory of the Republic of Slovenia.
(4) The law may determine the mandatory imposition of a prohibition of driving a motor vehicle.
(5) The court shall not impose or enforce a prohibition of driving a motor vehicle if it has imposed on the driver of a motor vehicle a security measure of revocation of the driving licence.

Expulsion of a foreigner from the country

Article 48a
(1) The court may impose on a foreigner expulsion from the territory of the Republic of Slovenia for a period of one to five years if it has imposed on him or her a punishment for a criminal offence for which a punishment of more than two years' imprisonment may be imposed, and if, on the basis of his or her personal circumstances, there is a serious threat to public order or public safety.
(2) The duration of expulsion shall be counted from the day the judgment becomes final. Time spent in prison shall not be included in the duration of this punishment.
(3) If the punishment of expulsion of a foreigner from the country is executed more than two years after the judgment imposing the expulsion becomes final, the court shall, before expulsion, reassess whether the personal circumstances that were the basis for the decision on expulsion are still present.

2. Measurement of punishment

General rules for the measurement of punishment

## Article 49

(1) The court shall impose a sentence on the perpetrator of a criminal offence within the limits prescribed by law for that offence, taking into account the gravity of the offence committed and the perpetrator's guilt.
(2) In doing so, the court shall take into account all circumstances that affect whether the sentence should be lesser or greater (mitigating and aggravating circumstances), in particular: the degree of the perpetrator's guilt, the motives from which the offence was committed, the degree of endangerment or violation of the protected legal value, the circumstances in which the offence was committed, the perpetrator's previous life, his personal and financial circumstances, his conduct after the commission of the offence, in particular whether he has compensated for the damage caused by the criminal offence, and other circumstances relating to the perpetrator's personality and the expected effect of the sentence on the perpetrator's future life in the social environment.

(3) If the motive for the commission of the criminal offence was the victim's national, racial, religious or ethnic affiliation, his sex, colour, origin, financial status, education, social position, political or other conviction, disability, sexual orientation or any other personal circumstance, this shall be taken into account as an aggravating circumstance.
(4) In imposing a sentence on a perpetrator who has committed a criminal offence after having already been finally convicted or after having served the sentence or after it has become statute-barred or after it has been remitted (recidivism), the court shall take into account in particular whether the previous offence was of the same type as the new one, whether both offences were committed from the same motives, and how much time has elapsed since the previous conviction or since the served, remitted or statute-barred sentence.

Mitigation of sentence

## Article 50

The court may impose a sentence on the perpetrator below the limit prescribed by law or apply a milder type of sentence: if the law provides that the perpetrator may be punished more leniently; if it finds special mitigating circumstances that justify the imposition of a mitigated sentence.

Limits of mitigation of imprisonment sentence

## Article 51

(1) Where the conditions for mitigation of sentence from the previous article are met, the court shall mitigate it within these limits:
1) if fifteen years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum sentence for the criminal offence, it may mitigate it to ten years of imprisonment;
2) if three or more years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum sentence for the criminal offence, it may mitigate it to one year of imprisonment;
3) if one year of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum sentence for the criminal offence, it may mitigate it to three months of imprisonment;
4) if less than one year of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum sentence for the criminal offence, it may mitigate it to one month of imprisonment;
5) if a sentence of imprisonment is prescribed for the criminal offence and no minimum sentence is specified, it may impose a fine instead of imprisonment.
(2) For a perpetrator who, under the law governing criminal procedure, admits guilt when first making a statement on the indictment in which mitigation of sentence is proposed for such a case, or admits it in an agreement with the state prosecutor, the court shall mitigate the sentence within these limits:
1) if ten or more years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum sentence for the criminal offence, it may mitigate it to three years of imprisonment;
2) if three to ten years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum sentence for the criminal offence, it may mitigate it to three months of imprisonment;
3) if less than three years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum sentence for the criminal offence, it may mitigate it to one month of imprisonment;
4) if less than one year of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum sentence for the criminal offence, it may impose a fine instead of imprisonment.

Remission of sentence

## Article 52

(1) The court may remit the sentence to the perpetrator of a criminal offence where the law specifically so provides.
(2) Where the court has the right to remit the sentence to the perpetrator of a criminal offence, it may mitigate it without the limitations prescribed for mitigation of sentence.

Concurrence of criminal offences

## Article 53

(1) If the perpetrator has committed two or more criminal offences by one act or by several acts for which he is being tried simultaneously, the court shall first determine the sentence for each individual criminal offence and then impose a single sentence for all those criminal offences.
(2) The court shall impose a single sentence according to the following rules:
1) if it has determined a sentence of life imprisonment for any criminal offence in concurrence, or if it has determined a sentence of thirty years of imprisonment for two or more criminal offences from the second paragraph of Article 46 of this Code in concurrence, it shall impose a single sentence of life imprisonment;
2) if it has determined a sentence of thirty years of imprisonment for any criminal offence in concurrence and the case under point 1) does not apply, it shall impose only that sentence;
3) if it has determined sentences of imprisonment for criminal offences in concurrence, the single sentence must be greater than each individual determined sentence, but may not reach the sum of the individual sentences and may not exceed thirty years of imprisonment;
4) if sentences of imprisonment of up to one year are prescribed for all criminal offences in concurrence, the single sentence may not be longer than five years of imprisonment;
5) if it has determined only fines for criminal offences in concurrence, it shall increase the highest determined fine, which may not exceed the sum of the determined fines and may not exceed three hundred and sixty daily amounts, or if one or more criminal offences were committed out of greed, it may not exceed one thousand five hundred daily amounts;
6) if it has determined a sentence of imprisonment for some criminal offences in concurrence and fines for other criminal offences, it shall impose one sentence of imprisonment and one fine according to points 2), 3), 4) and 5) of this paragraph;
7) if it has determined several accessory sentences of the same type for criminal offences in concurrence, it shall impose a single accessory sentence such that it may not reach their sum and may not exceed the highest general limit of the sentence.
(3) The court shall impose an accessory sentence even if it was determined for only one criminal offence in concurrence; however, if it has determined several fines, it shall impose only one fine according to point 5) of the second paragraph of this article.

Continuing criminal offence

## Article 54

(1) A person who, out of greed or harmful motives, simultaneously or successively commits or attempts to commit two or more identical or similar property criminal offences which, in terms of place, manner or other identical circumstances, constitute a unified activity, commits a continuing criminal offence.

(2) The provisions on concurrence from the previous article of this Code shall not apply to a continuing criminal offence, but the perpetrator shall be imposed one sentence for all criminal offences together within the limits of the sentence prescribed for the most serious criminal offence.
(3) A perpetrator who, through the activity under the first paragraph of this article, together obtains a greater or large property benefit or causes greater or large property damage for which a more severe sentence is prescribed for the criminal offence, shall be imposed that more severe sentence under the second paragraph of this article if his intention was to obtain such benefit or cause such damage through simultaneously or successively committed criminal offences.

Imposition of sentence on a convict

## Article 55

(1) If the court tries a convict for a criminal offence that he committed before he began serving the sentence under a previous conviction, or for a criminal offence that he committed while serving imprisonment, it shall impose a single sentence for all criminal offences according to Article 53; in doing so, it shall take into account that the previously imposed sentence has already been determined. The sentence or part of the sentence that the convict has already served shall be credited towards the imposed sentence of imprisonment.
(2) For a criminal offence committed while serving imprisonment, the court shall impose a sentence on the perpetrator regardless of previously imposed sentences if the unserved part of the previously imposed sentence would be disproportionately small when applying the provisions of Article 53.

Crediting of detention and previous sentence

## 56. člen

(1) Time spent in detention and any deprivation of liberty in connection with a criminal offence shall be credited towards the imposed sentence of imprisonment and fine.  
(2) If criminal proceedings are instituted for several criminal offences and detention is not ordered for each of them, the time spent in detention shall be credited towards the imposed sentence of imprisonment and fine for the criminal offence for which the accused (hereinafter: the accused) is convicted.  
(3) A fine, deprivation of liberty or any other sanction which the convicted person has served or paid for a misdemeanour, as well as a sentence or disciplinary deprivation of liberty which he or she has served for a breach of military discipline, shall be credited towards the sentence imposed for a criminal offence whose elements also include the elements of a misdemeanour or breach of military discipline.  
(4) One day of detention, one day of deprivation of liberty, one day of imprisonment and two daily amounts of a fine, as well as 42 euros of a fine imposed for a misdemeanour, are equated in every crediting.  

Part Five WARNING SANCTIONS

1. Suspended sentence

General provisions on suspended sentences

## 57. člen

(1) The court may, under the conditions laid down in this Code, impose a suspended sentence on the perpetrator of a criminal offence instead of a sentence.  
(2) By a suspended sentence, the court determines a sentence for the perpetrator of a criminal offence, which shall not be imposed if the convicted person does not commit a new criminal offence during the period determined by the court, which may not be less than one year and not more than five years (probationary period).  
(3) In a suspended sentence, the court may determine that a specified sentence shall also be imposed if the convicted person does not, within a specified period, return the pecuniary benefit obtained by the criminal offence, does not compensate for the damage caused by the criminal offence, or does not fulfil other obligations provided for in criminal law provisions. The period for the fulfilment of these obligations shall be determined by the court within the limits of the probationary period.  
(4) Security measures imposed together with a suspended sentence shall be executed.  

Imposition of a suspended sentence

## 58. člen

(1) A suspended sentence may be imposed if the court has determined a sentence of imprisonment of up to two years or a fine for the perpetrator.  
(2) A suspended sentence may not be imposed for criminal offences for which a sentence of imprisonment of at least three years is prescribed.  
(3) The court shall impose a suspended sentence if, having regard to the personality of the perpetrator, his or her previous life, his or her behaviour after the commission of the criminal offence, the degree of guilt and other circumstances in which the act was committed, it finds that it can be expected that he or she will not repeat criminal offences.  
(4) If the court determines accessory sentences in a suspended sentence, it may order that all or individual accessory sentences be executed.  
(5) A perpetrator who, under the law governing criminal procedure, admits guilt when first making a statement on the indictment in which the imposition of a suspended sentence is proposed for such a case, or admits it in an agreement with the state prosecutor, may be given a suspended sentence by the court even for criminal offences for which a sentence of imprisonment of at least five years is prescribed, determining a sentence of imprisonment of up to five years and a probationary period of up to ten years.  

Revocation of a suspended sentence due to a new criminal offence

## 59. člen

(1) The court shall revoke a suspended sentence if the convicted person commits, during the probationary period, one or more criminal offences for which it has imposed a sentence of imprisonment of two years or a longer sentence.  

(2) If the convicted person commits, during the probationary period, one or more criminal offences for which the court has determined a sentence of imprisonment of less than two years or a fine, the court shall decide whether to revoke the suspended sentence after assessing all the circumstances relating to the criminal offences committed and the perpetrator, in particular the similarity of the criminal offences committed, their significance and the motives from which they were committed. In doing so, it is bound by the prohibition that it may not impose a suspended sentence if, for the criminal offences established in the suspended sentence and for the new criminal offences, it is necessary to impose a sentence of more than two years' imprisonment (first paragraph of the preceding article), or in cases of admissions of guilt and plea agreements, more than five years' imprisonment (fifth paragraph of the preceding article).  
(3) If the court revokes a suspended sentence, it shall impose, pursuant to Article 53 of this Code, a single sentence for the previously committed and new criminal offence; in doing so, it shall take into account that the sentence from the revoked suspended sentence has already been determined.  
(4) If the court does not revoke the suspended sentence, it may impose a suspended sentence or a sentence for the new criminal offence. If it finds that a suspended sentence must also be imposed for the new criminal offence, it shall determine, pursuant to Article 53 of this Code, a single sentence for the previously committed and new criminal offence and determine a new probationary period, which may not be shorter than one year and not longer than five years, or in cases of admissions of guilt and plea agreements not longer than ten years, and shall be counted from the finality of the new judgment. For a convicted person who has been sentenced to imprisonment for a new criminal offence, the time of serving this sentence shall not be counted in the probationary period determined by the suspended sentence for the previous criminal offence.  

Revocation of a suspended sentence due to a previously committed criminal offence

## 60. člen

(1) The court shall revoke a suspended sentence if, after its imposition, it establishes that the convicted person committed a criminal offence before being given a suspended sentence, and if it considers that there would have been no basis for a suspended sentence if this act had been known. In such a case, it shall act in accordance with the third paragraph of the preceding article.  
(2) If the court does not revoke the suspended sentence, it shall act in accordance with the fourth paragraph of the preceding article.  

Revocation of a suspended sentence due to non-fulfilment of imposed obligations

## 61. člen

(1) If the convicted person has been ordered by a suspended sentence to fulfil any obligation under the third paragraph of Article 57 of this Code, but has not fulfilled it within the period specified in the judgment, the court may, within the limits of the probationary period, extend the period for the fulfilment of the obligation or revoke the suspended sentence and impose the sentence that was determined in the suspended sentence. If it finds that the convicted person cannot fulfil the imposed obligation for justified reasons, the court may release him or her from its fulfilment or replace it with another appropriate obligation determined by law.  
(2) If, within the period for the fulfilment of the obligation under the third paragraph of Article 57 of this Code, which was determined for the convicted person in the judgment, personal bankruptcy proceedings have been initiated against him or her, the running of the period for the fulfilment of this obligation shall be suspended for the duration of the personal bankruptcy, and the period within which the court may make the decisions under the preceding paragraph shall, notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, be extended beyond the limit of the determined probationary period for the duration of such suspension.  

Time limits for revocation of a suspended sentence

## 62. člen

(1) A suspended sentence may be revoked during the probationary period. If the convicted person commits a criminal offence during this period which results in the revocation of the suspended sentence, but this is only established by judgment after the expiry of the probationary period, the suspended sentence may be revoked no later than one year after the expiry of the probationary period.  
(2) If the convicted person does not fulfil the obligation under the third paragraph of Article 57 of this Code within the given period, the court may revoke the suspended sentence and impose the sentence that was determined in the suspended sentence no later than one year after the expiry of the probationary period or after the expiry of the period under the second paragraph of the preceding article.  

2. Suspended sentence with protective supervision

Protective supervision

## 63. člen

(1) Under the conditions laid down in this Code, the court may determine that a perpetrator who has been given a suspended sentence shall be under protective supervision for a specified period during the probationary period.  
(2) Protective supervision includes assistance, supervision or protection determined by law.  

Conditions for the imposition of protective supervision

## 64. člen

(1) The court shall impose a measure of protective supervision if it finds that the perpetrator needs some form of such supervision during the probationary period (second paragraph of the preceding article). The court shall impose this measure for a specified period within the limits of the probationary period determined by the suspended sentence.  
(2) If the court establishes that protective supervision is no longer necessary, it may stop the execution of this measure before the expiry of the probationary period.  

Selection of instructions

## Article 65

(1) When imposing a measure of protective supervision, the court may also order one or more instructions which the convicted person must follow.
(2) In selecting the instructions, the court shall take into account in particular the perpetrator's age, his mental characteristics, the motives from which he committed the act, personal circumstances, his previous life,

the circumstances in which he committed the act, and his behaviour after the commission of the criminal offence. It must take special care that the choice of instructions does not violate the convicted person's human dignity or cause him particular difficulties.
(3) The court's instructions may include the following tasks:
1) treatment in an appropriate health institution, with consent also treatment for alcohol or drug addiction;
2) attendance at an appropriate vocational, psychological or other counselling service;
3) training for a profession or acceptance of employment that corresponds to the convicted person's health, abilities and inclination;
4) use of income in accordance with maintenance obligations;
5) prohibition of association with certain persons;
6) prohibition of establishing direct or indirect contact with one or more specified persons, including by use of electronic means of communication;
7) prohibition of approaching the victim or any other person;
8) prohibition of access to individual places;
9) inclusion in one or more social welfare services of the social work centre.
(4) During the probationary period, the court may modify or revoke the instructions ex officio, at the proposal of the counsellor or the convicted person.

Activity of the counsellor

## Article 66

(1) Protective supervision is carried out by a counsellor appointed by the authority responsible for
probation.
(2) The counsellor provides assistance and protection to the convicted person in accordance with the law governing probation and supervises the fulfilment of the instructions issued by the court to the convicted person.

Consequences of non-compliance with instructions

## Article 67

(1) If the convicted person does not comply with the instructions during the probationary period, avoids contact with the counsellor or otherwise violates the personal plan prepared in accordance with the law governing probation, the court may warn him, modify the instructions, extend the protective supervision beyond the limit of the specified probationary period for the duration of non-compliance with the instructions, avoidance of contact or other violation of the personal plan, or revoke the suspended sentence and impose the sentence that was specified in the suspended sentence.
(2) If the convicted person also during the extended protective supervision under the preceding paragraph does not comply with the instructions, avoids contact with the counsellor or otherwise violates the personal plan prepared in accordance with the law governing probation, the court may revoke the suspended sentence and impose the sentence that was specified in the suspended sentence.

3. Judicial admonition

Conditions for imposing a judicial admonition

## Article 68

(1) A judicial admonition may be imposed for criminal offences for which a fine or imprisonment of up to one year is prescribed, if they are committed in such mitigating circumstances as to make them particularly minor.
(2) For certain criminal offences, a judicial admonition may be imposed under the conditions specified by law, even when imprisonment of up to three years is prescribed for them.
(3) The court may impose a judicial admonition for several criminal offences committed in concurrence, if the conditions under the first or second paragraph of this Article are met for each of these offences.
(4) In deciding whether to impose a judicial admonition, the court shall take into account the personality of the perpetrator, his previous life, his behaviour after the commission of the criminal offence, the degree of guilt and other circumstances in which he committed the act.
(5) To a perpetrator who, under the law governing criminal procedure, admits guilt when first making a statement on the indictment in which the imposition of a judicial admonition is proposed for such a case, or admits it in an agreement with the state prosecutor, the court may impose a judicial admonition also for criminal offences for which imprisonment of up to three years is prescribed.

Chapter Six SECURITY MEASURES

Types of security measures

## Article 69

The following security measures may be imposed on perpetrators of criminal offences:
1) mandatory psychiatric treatment and protection in a health institution;
2) mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty;
3) prohibition of practising a profession;
4) prohibition of approaching or communicating with the victim;
5) revocation of driving licence;
6) confiscation of objects.

Imposition of security measures

## Article 70

(1) The court may impose one or more security measures on the perpetrator of a criminal offence when the conditions determined by this code are met.  
(2) When imposing a security measure, the court shall, according to the principle of proportionality, take into account the gravity of the criminal offence committed and the acts which it reasonably concludes the perpetrator could commit if the security measure were not imposed.  

(3) Mandatory psychiatric treatment and custody in a health institution and mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty shall be imposed independently on a non-accountable perpetrator of an unlawful act if the safety of people cannot otherwise be ensured. In addition to these two measures, the court may also impose a prohibition on practising a profession, revocation of a driving licence and confiscation of objects.  
(4) Revocation of a driving licence and confiscation of objects may be imposed if the perpetrator has been sentenced to a punishment, conditional sentence, judicial admonition or if the punishment has been remitted.  
(5) A prohibition on practising a profession may be imposed if the perpetrator has been sentenced to imprisonment or if imprisonment has been determined in a conditional sentence.  
(6) A prohibition on approaching or communicating with the victim may be imposed if the perpetrator has been sentenced to a punishment, warning sanction or if the punishment has been remitted.  

Mandatory psychiatric treatment and custody in a health institution

Article 70.a
(1) The court shall impose mandatory psychiatric treatment and custody in a health institution on a perpetrator who has committed an unlawful act in a state of non-accountability or a criminal offence in a state of substantially diminished accountability and for which a sentence of imprisonment of one year or more may be imposed, if on the basis of the gravity of the act committed and the degree of the perpetrator's mental disturbance it determines that at liberty he or she could commit a serious criminal offence against life, body, sexual integrity or property and that such danger can only be eliminated by treatment and custody in a forensic psychiatric ward of a health institution that meets the special security conditions determined by law.  
(2) The court shall terminate the measure from the preceding paragraph or replace it with the measure from Article
70.b of this code when it determines that treatment and custody in a health institution are no longer necessary. After six months have elapsed, the court shall decide each time anew whether further treatment and custody in a health institution are still necessary.

(4) If a person commits a criminal offence during the serving of a sentence for which the court imposes a security measure in accordance with paragraph one of this article, the serving of the sentence shall be interrupted for the duration of the execution of the security measure.  
(5) For a perpetrator who has committed a criminal offence in a state of substantially diminished accountability and has been sentenced to imprisonment, the execution of the measure from paragraph one of this article shall be terminated when he or she has spent in the health institution the time for which the sentence was imposed. The time spent in the health institution shall be counted towards the time of serving the sentence. If this time is shorter than the sentence imposed, the court may order that the convicted person must serve the remainder of the sentence or that he or she be released on conditional release. When deciding on the conditional release of a convicted person, the court shall take into account in particular the success of treatment, his or her health condition, the time spent in the health institution and the remainder of the sentence not served.  

Mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty

Article 70.b

(1) The court shall impose mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty on a perpetrator who has committed an unlawful act in a state of non-accountability if it determines that this is necessary and sufficient to prevent the perpetrator from repeating more serious criminal offences.  
(2) The court may also impose mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty on a perpetrator whose accountability is substantially diminished and who has been released on conditional release pursuant to paragraph five of the preceding article.  
(3) Mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty may last for a maximum of two years. After six months have elapsed, the court shall decide each time anew whether mandatory treatment at liberty is still necessary.  
(4) If the perpetrator does not wish to undergo treatment at liberty, or if he or she arbitrarily abandons treatment, or if treatment is not successful, the court may, under the conditions from paragraph one of the preceding article, order that this measure be executed in a health institution. Even in such a case, this measure may last in total for a maximum of two years. After six months have elapsed, the court shall decide again whether this measure is still necessary.  

Prohibition on practising a profession

## Article 71

(1) The court may prohibit the perpetrator of a criminal offence from practising a profession, independent activity or any duty if he or she has abused his or her profession, position, activity or duty for a criminal offence and if the court reasonably concludes that it would be dangerous if he or she were to continue practising such activity.  
(2) The court shall determine how long the measure from the preceding paragraph shall last; it may not last less than one year and not more than five years, counted from the finality of the decision, whereby the time spent in prison or a health institution for treatment and custody shall not be counted towards the duration of this measure.  
(3) When imposing a conditional sentence, the court may determine that this sentence shall be revoked if the perpetrator violates the prohibition on practising a profession.  
(4) The court may decide that this security measure shall cease if two years have elapsed from the commencement of its execution. The court shall decide on this at the request of the convicted person if it determines that the reasons for imposing this measure have ceased.  

Prohibition on approaching or communicating with the victim of a criminal offence

Article 71.a
(1) The court may impose on the perpetrator of a criminal offence against life and body, against honour and reputation, against sexual integrity, against human health or another criminal offence with signs of violence a prohibition on approaching or communicating with the victim of any of these criminal offences for a period from one month to three years.  
(2) Within the framework of the measure from the preceding paragraph, the court may determine: a prohibition on approaching the victim or his or her close persons, a prohibition on establishing direct or indirect contacts or communicating with the victim or his or her close persons, including the use of electronic communication means,
 a prohibition on access to individual places where the victim or his or her close persons regularly move, or a prohibition on associating with the victim or his or her close persons.  
(3) The court may impose this measure if the perpetrator has harmed the physical or mental integrity of the victim with the criminal offence and if it assesses that the perpetrator's contacts or communication with the victim or his or her close persons would pose a danger of repetition of the same type or commission of another criminal offence against the victim or his or her close persons. The court shall select one or more methods of execution of the measure from the preceding paragraph and shall in particular precisely define the persons to whom the measure relates and the relationship between the perpetrator and the victim or his or her close person, appropriate distances of the perpetrator from the victim or his or her close person or places that must be respected, define individual methods of prohibited forms of communication or determine prohibited forms of association.  
(4) When imposing a conditional sentence, the court may decide that the sentence shall be revoked if the perpetrator violates the prohibition on approaching or communicating with the victim or his or her close person.  
(5) The duration of the prohibition on approaching or communicating shall be counted from the finality of the judgment. The time spent in prison or in a health institution for treatment and custody shall not be counted towards the duration of this measure.  
(6) The court may decide that this security measure shall cease if six months have elapsed from the commencement of execution. The court shall decide on this at the request of the convicted person if it determines that the reasons for imposing this measure have ceased.  

Revocation of driving licence

## Article 72

(1) For the perpetrator of a criminal offence against the safety of public traffic, the court may revoke the driving licence for individual categories of motor vehicles and determine that a new licence may not be issued to them for one to five years. If the perpetrator does not have a driving licence, the court shall order that a licence may not be issued to them.  
(2) The court may impose this measure if it finds that the perpetrator's continued participation in public traffic would pose a danger to public traffic due to their behaviour, personal characteristics or inability to safely operate motor vehicles.  
(3) The driving licence ceases to be valid upon the finality of the court decision. Time spent in prison or a health institution for treatment and care shall not be counted towards the duration of this measure.  
(4) After the expiry of the time determined by the court within the minimum and maximum limits of this measure, the perpetrator may obtain a new driving licence subject to the general conditions prescribed for obtaining individual categories of driving licences.  
(5) The court may decide that this security measure shall cease and that the perpetrator may obtain a new driving licence if two years have elapsed from the commencement of its implementation. The court shall decide on this upon the request of the convicted person if it finds that the reasons for imposing this measure have ceased.  

Seizure of objects

## Article 73

(1) Objects that have been used or intended for a criminal offence or have been created by a criminal offence may be seized if they are the property of the perpetrator.  
(2) Objects referred to in the preceding paragraph may also be seized when they are not the property of the perpetrator if required by public safety or moral reasons, but the right of others to claim compensation from the perpetrator is not thereby affected.  
(3) The law may provide for mandatory seizure of objects, even if they are not the property of the perpetrator.  

Chapter Seven
CONFISCATION OF PECUNIARY BENEFIT ACQUIRED BY CRIMINAL OFFENCE

Grounds for confiscation of pecuniary benefit

## Article 74

(1) No one may retain pecuniary benefit that has been acquired by or due to a criminal offence.  
(2) The benefit referred to in the preceding paragraph of this Article shall be confiscated by a court decision by which a criminal offence has been established under the conditions specified in this Code.  

Manner of confiscation of pecuniary benefit

## Article 75

(1) Money, valuables and any other pecuniary benefit acquired by or due to a criminal offence shall be confiscated from the perpetrator or other recipient of the benefit (hereinafter: the recipient), and if it cannot be confiscated from them, property corresponding to the pecuniary benefit shall be confiscated from them.  
(2) If it is not possible to confiscate the pecuniary benefit or property corresponding to the pecuniary benefit from the perpetrator or other recipient of the benefit, they shall be ordered to pay a monetary amount corresponding to that pecuniary benefit. In justified cases, the court may allow the monetary amount corresponding to the pecuniary benefit to be paid in instalments, provided that the payment period may not exceed two years.  
(3) Pecuniary benefit acquired by or due to a criminal offence may also be confiscated from those to whom it has been transferred free of charge or for payment that does not correspond to the actual value, if they knew or should have known that it was acquired by or due to a criminal offence.  
(4) If pecuniary benefit acquired by or due to a criminal offence has been transferred to close relatives of the perpetrator of the criminal offence (relationships referred to in Article 224 of this Code), or if any other property of theirs has been transferred to these persons in order to prevent confiscation of pecuniary benefit under the first paragraph of this Article, it shall be confiscated from them unless they prove that they paid the actual value for it.  

(5) If pecuniary benefit has been acquired by several persons jointly, the share acquired by each person shall be confiscated; if this share cannot be determined more precisely, it shall be determined by the court taking into account all the circumstances of the case.  

Protection of the injured party

## Article 76

(1) If a property claim has been awarded to the injured party in criminal proceedings, the court shall order confiscation of pecuniary benefit when it exceeds the property claim awarded to the injured party.  
(2) An injured party who has been referred by the court to civil proceedings regarding a property claim may demand payment from the confiscated value if they commence civil proceedings within six months from the finality of the decision by which they were referred to civil proceedings, and if they demand payment from the confiscated value within three months from the finality of the decision by which their claim was established.  
(3) An injured party who did not file a property claim in criminal proceedings may demand payment from the confiscated value if they commenced civil proceedings to establish their claim within three months from the day they learned of the decision by which the pecuniary benefit was confiscated, but no later than two years from the finality of that decision, and if within three months from the finality of the decision by which their claim was established, they demand payment from the confiscated value.  

Confiscation of pecuniary benefit from a legal person

## Article 77

If pecuniary benefit has been acquired by or due to a criminal offence by a legal person, such benefit shall be confiscated from it. Pecuniary benefit or property corresponding to the pecuniary benefit shall also be confiscated from a legal person if the persons referred to in the first paragraph of Article 75 of this Code transfer it to the legal person free of charge or for payment that does not correspond to the actual value.  

Confiscation of property for criminal offences committed in a criminal organisation

Article 77a
(1) Under the provisions of this Chapter, pecuniary benefit or property that has been acquired by or due to a criminal offence or that is disposed of by a criminal organisation shall also be confiscated.  
(2) From a perpetrator who has committed a criminal offence in a criminal organisation, property that the court finds originates from criminal activities committed in that organisation shall also be confiscated as pecuniary benefit acquired by a criminal offence.  

Confiscation of property acquired in connection with a criminal offence

Article 77b
Under the provisions of this Chapter, property that the perpetrator or other persons from whom property is confiscated use for their exclusive or predominant benefit with the consent of persons to whom the property belongs shall also be confiscated as property acquired in connection with or due to a criminal offence, if those persons knew or should have known that the property was acquired by or due to a criminal offence or that it was used to prepare, commit or conceal a criminal offence or that it was acquired with the intention of using it for a criminal offence.  

Presumption of gratuitousness

Article 77c
(1) Regardless of the legal basis of the transfer, it shall be presumed that pecuniary benefit or property that is the subject of confiscation under the provisions of this Chapter was transferred free of charge or for payment that does not correspond to the actual value if the perpetrator or their close relatives (relationships referred to in Article 224 of this Code) transferred it directly or indirectly to a company or other legal person that is majority-owned by them or in which they have the right to exercise predominant influence or control.  
(2) Pecuniary benefit or property shall not be confiscated from the company or person referred to in the preceding paragraph if it proves that it paid the actual value for it.  

Chapter Eight LEGAL CONSEQUENCES OF CONVICTION

Arising of legal consequences of conviction

## Article 78

(1) Convictions for individual criminal offences or individual punishments may have as a legal consequence the termination or loss of individual rights or the prohibition of acquiring individual rights.  
(2) Legal consequences of conviction cannot arise if a fine, suspended sentence or judicial admonition was imposed on the perpetrator for the criminal offence or if the punishment was remitted.  
(3) Legal consequences of conviction may only be prescribed by law and arise by operation of the law by which they are prescribed.  
(4) Only those legal consequences of conviction that were determined by law at the time of commission of the criminal offence may be applied to the perpetrator.  

Types of legal consequences of conviction

## Article 79

(1) The legal consequence of a conviction relating to the termination or loss of individual rights is the termination of the performance of certain public functions or powers of an official person or the termination of employment or the loss of a foreigner's right to residence in the Republic of Slovenia.  
(2) Legal consequences consisting of a prohibition on acquiring individual rights are:
1) prohibition on performing individual public functions or powers of an official person;  
2) prohibition on acquiring an individual profession or concluding an employment contract;  
3) prohibition on acquiring individual permits or approvals granted by state authorities by their decision.  

Commencement and duration of legal consequences of conviction

## Article 80

(1) Legal consequences take effect from the day the conviction becomes final.  
(2) Legal consequences of a conviction consisting of a prohibition on acquiring individual rights may last a maximum of five years from the day the sentence was served, remitted or became time-barred, unless a shorter or longer period is prescribed by law for individual legal consequences.  
(3) After the expiry of two years from the day the sentence was served, became time-barred or was remitted, the court may, at the request of the convicted person, order that the legal consequence of the conviction relating to the prohibition on acquiring an individual right shall cease.  
(4) In assessing whether to order the cessation of a legal consequence of conviction, the court shall take into account the conduct of the convicted person after conviction, whether he or she has compensated for the damage caused by the criminal offence, and also other circumstances indicating that the cessation of the legal consequence of conviction is justified.  
(5) The cessation of legal consequences of conviction does not affect those rights of others based on the conviction.  
(6) With the expungement of the conviction, its legal consequences cease.  

Chapter Nine
REHABILITATION, EXPUNGEMENT OF CONVICTION AND CONDITIONS FOR PROVIDING DATA FROM THE CRIMINAL RECORD

Legal status of a convicted person after serving the sentence

## Article 81

(1) After a sentence has been served, paid or otherwise executed in accordance with the law, remitted or become time-barred, former convicted persons have all the rights provided for in the constitution, laws and other regulations, and may acquire all rights, except those limited to them due to an imposed security measure or due to legal consequences of conviction that have arisen.  
(2) The preceding paragraph also applies to those who are on parole.  

Statutory rehabilitation and expungement of conviction

## Article 82

(1) By statutory rehabilitation, the conviction is expunged from the criminal record and all its legal consequences cease, and the convicted person is deemed not to have been convicted.  
(2) A conviction means a final decision as well as amendments to that decision by amnesty or pardon.  
(3) A conviction is expunged from the criminal record within a specified period from the time the sentence was executed, became time-barred or was remitted, if the convicted person does not commit a new criminal offence within that period.  
(4) The periods referred to in the preceding paragraph are:
1) for a conviction by which the perpetrator was given a judicial admonition, and for a conviction by which the sentence was remitted – one year from the finality of the court decision;  
2) for a suspended sentence – one year from the expiry of the probationary period or the period referred to in the second paragraph of Article 61 or Article 67 of this Code;  
3) for a conviction to a fine, accessory sentence or imprisonment of up to one year – three years;  
4) for a conviction to imprisonment of more than one year up to three years – five years;  
5) for a conviction to imprisonment of more than three years up to five years – eight years;  
6) for a conviction to imprisonment of more than five up to ten years – ten years;  
7) for a conviction to imprisonment of more than ten up to fifteen years – fifteen years.  

measures.  

(5) A conviction to imprisonment of more than fifteen years shall not be expunged.  
(6) A conviction cannot be expunged from the criminal record as long as security measures are in force.  

(7) A security measure of mandatory psychiatric treatment and care in a health institution and mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty, which was imposed independently, shall be expunged three years from the time its execution ceased or the execution became time-barred.  

Judicial rehabilitation

## Article 83

The court may, at the request of the convicted person, decide that the conviction shall be expunged from the criminal record and that the convicted person shall be deemed not to have been convicted, if half of the period prescribed by law after which the conviction is expunged has elapsed, provided the convicted person has not committed a new criminal offence during that time. In deciding on expungement, the court shall take into account the conduct of the convicted person after serving the sentence, the nature of the criminal offence and other circumstances relevant to the expungement of the conviction.  

Providing data from the criminal record

## Article 84

(1) Data on imposed judgments are kept in criminal records. The scope of data and the provision of data from criminal records before expungement shall be determined by law.  
(2) At the request of kindergartens, carers of preschool children, schools, other institutes, institutions or associations to which children or minors are entrusted for learning, education, care or custody, for the purpose of

verifying the fulfilment of conditions for their employee or another person who performs or will perform work for them, data from criminal records shall also be provided for expunged convictions for criminal offences under Article 170, Article 171, Article 172, Article 173, Article 173.a, the second paragraph of Article 174, committed against a minor, the second paragraph of Article 175 and Article 176 of this Code, and for expunged convictions for comparable criminal offences imposed on citizens of the Republic of Slovenia by foreign courts.  
(3) The law shall provide that convictions for criminal offences referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be entered in the register of expunged convictions for criminal offences against sexual integrity and shall prescribe the conditions, limitations and procedure for providing data on such convictions; in cases not covered by the preceding paragraph, the conviction shall be deemed expunged despite being retained in this register (first paragraph of this Article).  

Chapter Ten
FUNDAMENTAL PROVISIONS ON THE EXECUTION OF CRIMINAL SANCTIONS

Status of a convicted person serving a prison sentence

## Article 85

(1) The rights guaranteed by the constitution and laws of those against whom criminal sanctions are executed may be withdrawn or restricted in accordance with the law only to the extent necessary to execute an individual criminal sanction.  
(2) A person against whom a criminal sanction is being executed must not be tortured and must not otherwise be treated cruelly, inhumanely or degradingly. If he or she has been treated in such a manner, judicial protection must be ensured.  
(3) Convicted persons must be treated humanely, their personal dignity must be respected and their physical and mental integrity must be protected.  
(4) Convicted persons must be provided with appropriate health care, with their consent also including treatment for alcohol or drug addiction.  

Manner of execution of imprisonment

## Article 86

(1) Convicted persons serve their prison sentence in institutions for serving sentences (hereinafter: institution), which are determined by law.  
(2) Convicted persons are classified into institutions according to how much their freedom must be restricted.  
(3) If a convicted person has been sentenced to imprisonment of up to five years, the court may order that it be served in an open or semi-open institution or unit; if a sentence of imprisonment of up to eight years has been imposed, the court may order that it be served in a semi-open institution or unit.  
(4) A prison sentence of up to three years, except for a criminal offence against sexual integrity, may also be executed for a convicted person who meets the conditions determined by the law governing the execution of criminal sanctions, in such a way that the convicted person continues to work

or study and reside at home during the serving of the prison sentence, except on days off, usually at the end of the week, when he or she must be in the institution. More detailed conditions of execution shall be determined by the institution.  
(5) A prison sentence of up to nine months may also be executed by house arrest if, given the danger of the convicted person, the possibility of repeating the act and the personal, family and professional circumstances of the convicted person at the time of execution of the sentence, there is no need to serve the sentence in an institution or if, due to illness, disability or old age of the convicted person, the sentence must be and can be executed in an appropriate public institution.  
(6) During the serving of the sentence, the convicted person may not leave the building or individual part of the building where the house arrest is being executed, unless the court exceptionally permits this for a specified time when it is unavoidably necessary for the convicted person to secure the most essential necessities of life or medical assistance, or to perform work. The court in the judgment by which it determined that the sentence is to be executed by house arrest, according to the provisions of this code on conditional sentence, shall also determine protective supervision, and may also determine one or more instructions. The court may restrict or prohibit the convicted person from having contact with persons who do not reside with him or her or do not treat or care for him or her and shall determine in more detail the manner of execution of house arrest.  
(7) If the convicted person leaves the building or individual part of the building where the house arrest is being executed without the permission of the court, or does so outside the permitted time or does not comply with the restriction or prohibition of contacts or violates other rules determined by the court regarding the execution of house arrest, the court may decide by order that the remainder of the imposed sentence be executed in an institution.  
(8) A prison sentence of up to two years, except for a criminal offence against sexual integrity, may also be executed in such a way that the convicted person, instead of imprisonment, performs work in the general interest within a period of at most three years from the enforceability of the judgment. The scope of work is determined so that one day of imprisonment is replaced by two hours of work. The authority responsible for execution, when determining work for the convicted person within the framework of available work at implementing organisations, takes into account the convicted person's professional knowledge and abilities, and may also take into account those urgent interests of his or hers regarding urgent family, educational or professional obligations, the non-observance of which would cause difficult to repair or irreparable damage to the fulfilment of these obligations. Work in the general interest is performed without remuneration.  
(9) When deciding on the execution of a prison sentence in the manner from the previous paragraph, the court takes into account in particular the conduct of the convicted person at the time of decision-making, the danger of repeating the act at liberty, the possibility and ability to perform appropriate work, and the personal and family circumstances of the convicted person at the time of the planned execution of the sentence.  
(10) The court in the judgment in which it determined that the sentence is to be executed by work in the general interest, according to the provisions of this code on conditional sentence, shall also determine protective supervision, and may also determine one or more instructions.  
(11) If the convicted person does not fulfil the tasks within the framework of work in the general interest in whole or in part, or does not fulfil the instructions within the framework of protective supervision to a significant extent, or evades contact with the counsellor or otherwise violates the obligations from work in the general interest, the court shall decide by order that the imposed prison sentence be executed to the extent of work not performed.  
(12) The admissibility of execution of a prison sentence in the manners determined by this article shall be decided by the court on the proposal of the accused by the judgment by which it imposes the prison sentence, or on the proposal of the convicted person by a special order.  
(13) The admissibility of execution of a prison sentence in the manners determined by this article for an accused who, according to the law governing criminal procedure, admits guilt when he or she first makes a statement on the indictment in which the manner of execution of the sentence is proposed for such a case, or admits it in

an agreement with the state prosecutor, shall be decided by the court in accordance with this proposal or agreement.  

Manner of execution of fine

## Article 87

(1) If a fine cannot be collected even by force, the court shall execute it by determining one day of imprisonment for every two daily amounts of the fine commenced, whereby the imprisonment may not be longer than six months.  
(2) If the convicted person pays only part of the fine, the remainder shall be converted proportionally into imprisonment, and if he or she pays the remainder, the execution of the prison sentence shall be suspended.  
(3) After the death of the convicted person, the fine shall not be executed.  
(4) A fine of up to three hundred and sixty daily amounts may also be executed in such a way that the convicted person, within a period of at most one year, instead of paying the fine, performs work in the general interest without remuneration. The scope of work is determined so that one daily amount is replaced by one hour of work.  
(5) Regarding issues not regulated in the previous paragraph, the provisions of the eighth to thirteenth paragraphs of Article 86 of this code shall apply mutatis mutandis.  

Conditional release

## 88. člen

(1)	A convicted person who has served half of the prison sentence may be released from serving the sentence on the condition that, until the expiry of the time for which the sentence was imposed, they do not commit a new criminal offence.  
(2)	A convicted person to whom the court has imposed a sentence of more than fifteen years' imprisonment may be conditionally released from serving the sentence when they have served three-quarters of the sentence.  
(3)	A convicted person to whom the court has imposed a sentence of life imprisonment may be conditionally released from serving the sentence when they have served twenty-five years' imprisonment.  
(4)	The body that decides on conditional release and the procedure for deciding on conditional release are determined by law.  
(5)	A convicted person may be conditionally released if it can be reasonably expected that they will not repeat the criminal offence. In assessing whether the convicted person should be conditionally released, particular account is taken of recidivism, any criminal proceedings pending against the convicted person for criminal offences committed before commencing the prison sentence, the convicted person's attitude towards the criminal offence committed and the injured party, their behaviour during the serving of the sentence, success in treating addiction and conditions for integration into life at liberty.  
(6)	Exceptionally, a convicted person who has served only one-third of the sentence may also be conditionally released if the condition from the fifth paragraph of this Article is met and if special circumstances relating to the convicted person's personality indicate that they will not repeat the criminal offence.  

(7)	A convicted person who is to be conditionally released may be placed under protective supervision by the body that decides on conditional release. Protective supervision is carried out by a counsellor who has the same tasks as in conditional conviction with protective supervision.  
(8)	The body that decides on conditional release may impose the following tasks on a convicted person who is conditionally released:
1)	treatment in an appropriate health institution, with their consent also treatment for alcohol or drug addiction;  
2)	attendance at an appropriate vocational, psychological or other counselling service;  
3)	training for a profession or acceptance of employment that corresponds to the convicted person's health, abilities or inclination;  
4)	expenditure of income in accordance with maintenance obligations;  
5)	prohibition of association with certain persons;  
6)	prohibition of establishing direct or indirect contact with one or more specified persons, including by using electronic means of communication;  
7)	prohibition of approaching the victim or any other person;  
8)	prohibition of access to individual places.  
(9)	The provisions of this Article also apply to conditional release from house arrest. In assessing whether the convicted person should be conditionally released, for the period of house arrest, instead of behaviour during the serving of the sentence, compliance with restrictions in its execution is taken into account.  

Revocation of conditional release

## 89. člen

(1)	The court shall revoke conditional release if the convicted person on conditional release commits one or more criminal offences for which it has imposed a prison sentence of more than one year.  
(2)	The court may revoke conditional release if the conditionally released person commits one or more criminal offences for which it has imposed a prison sentence of up to one year. In assessing whether to revoke conditional release, the court takes into account in particular the similarity of the criminal offences committed, their significance, the motives from which they were committed, and other circumstances indicating that revocation of conditional release is justified. The court may also revoke conditional release if the conditionally released person does not perform the tasks imposed on them by the body that decides on conditional release.  
(3)	When the court revokes conditional release, it imposes a sentence pursuant to Article 53 and the second paragraph of Article 55 of this Code; in doing so, it takes as determined that part of the previously imposed sentence which the convicted person has not yet served.  
(4)	The first, second and third paragraphs of this Article also apply when the court convicts the conditionally released person for an act they committed before being conditionally released.  
(5)	If the conditionally released person is sentenced to imprisonment of up to one year and the court does not revoke conditional release, the conditional release is extended for the time when the convicted person was serving the prison sentence.  
(6)	If the convicted person commits a criminal offence during conditional release which results in revocation of conditional release, but this is only established by judgment after its expiry, conditional release may be revoked no later than one year from the time when the period of conditional release expired.  

Chapter Eleven
LIMITATION

Limitation of criminal prosecution

## 90. člen

(1)	Unless otherwise provided in this Code, criminal prosecution is no longer permitted if the following period has elapsed:
1)	fifty years from the commission of a criminal offence for which imprisonment of thirty years may be imposed by law, unless it is a criminal offence not subject to limitation;  
2)	thirty years from the commission of a criminal offence for which imprisonment of more than ten years may be imposed by law;  
3)	twenty years from the commission of a criminal offence for which imprisonment of more than five years may be imposed by law;  
4)	ten years from the commission of a criminal offence for which imprisonment of more than one year may be imposed by law;  
5)	six years from the commission of a criminal offence for which imprisonment of up to one year or a fine may be imposed by law.  
(2)	If several penalties are prescribed for a criminal offence, the limitation period is determined according to the most severe prescribed penalty.  
(3)	Notwithstanding the first paragraph of this Article, for the criminal offence of placing in a slavery relationship and the criminal offence of trafficking in human beings, and for criminal offences against sexual integrity and criminal offences against marriage, family and children committed against a minor, the period for limitation of criminal prosecution begins to run from the injured party's attainment of majority.  

Running and interruption of limitation of criminal prosecution and time limit for new trial

## 91. člen

(1)	Limitation of criminal prosecution begins on the day when the criminal offence was committed.  
(2)	If a final judgment is set aside in proceedings with an extraordinary legal remedy or with a constitutional appeal, the time limit for a new trial is five years from the setting aside of the final judgment.  
(3)	Limitation and the time limit for a new trial do not run during the time when, by law, prosecution may not be commenced or continued or when the perpetrator is beyond the reach of state authorities.  
(4)	Limitation and the time limit for a new trial are interrupted if the perpetrator, during the time when the limitation period or the time limit for a new trial is running, commits an equally serious or more serious criminal offence, and after the interruption limitation or the time limit for a new trial begins to run anew.  

Limitation of execution of sentence

## 92. člen

Unless otherwise provided in this Code, an imposed sentence may no longer be executed when the following period has elapsed:
1)	fifty years from conviction to thirty years' imprisonment, unless it is a criminal offence not subject to limitation;  
2)	thirty years from conviction to imprisonment of more than ten years;  
3)	twenty years from conviction to imprisonment of more than five years;  
4)	ten years from conviction to imprisonment of more than one year;  
5)	six years from conviction to imprisonment of up to one year or to a fine.  

Limitation of execution of accessory penalties and security measures

## 93. člen

(1)	Execution of a fine as an accessory penalty becomes time-barred when four years have elapsed from the finality of the judgment by which this penalty was imposed.  
(2)	Execution of the prohibition of driving a motor vehicle as an accessory penalty becomes time-barred when the principal penalty becomes time-barred.  
(3)	Execution of the security measures of mandatory psychiatric treatment and care in a health institution, mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty, prohibition of approaching or communicating with the victim, and revocation of driving licence and confiscation of objects becomes time-barred when six years have elapsed from the finality of the decision by which these measures were imposed.  
(4)	Execution of the security measure of prohibition of practising a profession becomes time-barred when the time for which the measure was imposed has elapsed.  

Running and interruption of limitation

## Article 94

(1) The limitation period for the execution of a sentence begins on the day the judgment becomes final; if a suspended sentence is revoked, on the day the decision on revocation becomes final.  
(2) The limitation period does not run during the time when, according to law, the sentence may not be executed.  
(3) The limitation period for the execution of a sentence does not run during the time when the convicted person has not responded to the summons to serve the sentence or the commencement of the sentence cannot be ensured because he is on the run, is hiding or is otherwise evading the execution of the sentence or is otherwise unreachable by state authorities, or when the commencement of the execution of the sentence has been postponed in accordance with the law.  
(4) The limitation period for the execution of a prison sentence ceases to run on the day the sentence is commenced. If the convicted person escapes from serving the sentence, the execution of the remainder of the sentence does not become time-barred.  
(5) The second and third paragraphs of this article also apply to the limitation period for security measures.  

Non-limitation of criminal offences and special limitation period

## Article 95

(1) Criminal prosecution and execution of a sentence do not become time-barred for criminal offences for which a sentence of life imprisonment may be imposed under this Code, for criminal offences under Articles 100 to 105 of this Code, and also not for those criminal offences for which limitation is not possible under international treaties.  
(2) The execution of a sentence of life imprisonment does not become time-barred.  
(3) Criminal prosecution for criminal offences under Articles 170 to 173 and the second paragraph of Article 174, the second paragraph of Article 175 and the second paragraph of Article 176 of this Code is no longer permitted if a period of time equal to three times the period determined for the limitation of criminal prosecution by the first paragraph of Article 90 of this Code has elapsed since the commission of the criminal offence.  

Chapter Twelve AMNESTY AND PARDON

Amnesty

## Article 96

By amnesty, persons who benefit from it are released from prosecution, fully or partially released from the execution of a sentence, the imposed sentence is changed to a more lenient one, the conviction is expunged or the legal consequence of the conviction is removed.  

Pardon

## Article 97

By pardon, a named individual person is released from prosecution or fully or partially released from the execution of a sentence, the imposed sentence is changed to a more lenient one or to a suspended sentence, or the conviction is expunged or the legal consequence of the conviction is removed or its duration is shortened.  

Application of the general part in amnesty and pardon

## Article 98

(1) If a provision on criminal sanctions is changed by amnesty or pardon, the general part of this Code applies.  
(2) If a sentence of life imprisonment has been imposed, a prison sentence of twenty-five to thirty years shall be imposed by pardon or on the basis of amnesty.  
(3) The rights of others based on the conviction are not affected by amnesty or pardon.  

Chapter Thirteen

Meaning of terms in this Code

## Article 99

(1) An official person under this Code may be:
1) a member of the National Assembly, a member of the National Council and a member of a local or regional representative body;  
2) a judge of the Constitutional Court, a judge, a lay judge, a state prosecutor or a senior state attorney;  
3) a person who performs official duties or holds an official function with managerial powers and responsibilities at a state authority or an authority of a self-governing local community or at another public law entity;  
4) another person who performs individual official duties on the basis of authorisations given to him by law or by regulations issued on the basis of law (public authorisation) or on the basis of an arbitration agreement concluded under law;  
5) a military person who is designated as such by special regulations, when it concerns special criminal offences in which an official person is mentioned but are not designated as criminal offences against military duty;  
6) a person who performs a legislative, executive or judicial task or other official duty at any level in a foreign state and in substance meets the conditions under points 1, 2 or 3 of this paragraph;  
7) a person to whom an international public organisation gives the status of an official person;  
8) a person who performs judicial, prosecutorial or other official duty or task at an international court.  
(2) A military person under this Code is considered to be: a soldier, officer, non-commissioned officer and military employee who professionally performs military service, a soldier on compulsory or voluntary military service and a person liable for compulsory or contractual reserve when performing military service.  
(3) Elections and voting under this Code are considered to be elections of the President of the Republic, members of the National Assembly, members of the National Council, members of the European Parliament, local or regional elections and legislative referendum and other referendums prescribed by the Constitution.  
(4) An extramarital union under this Code is considered to be a long-lasting life partnership of a man and a woman who have not entered into marriage.  
(5) A document is any writing, data carrier or other object suitable and intended for proving a fact that has value for legal relations.  
(6) Movable property under this Code also includes any acquired or collected energy for light, heat, radiation, propulsion, movement or transmission of sound, image or text over a distance, as well as virtual currency.  
(7) Force also includes the use of hypnosis, drugs or other special means to make someone become unconscious or incapable of resistance against their will.  
(8) A motor vehicle is considered to be any means of transport with motor propulsion in land, water and air traffic.  
(9) Pecuniary benefit, damage or value is considered to be an amount at the time of commission of the criminal offence which
1) in the case of minor does not exceed 500 euros;  

2) in the case of major exceeds 5,000 euros;  
3) in the case of large exceeds 50,000 euros.  
(10) Economic activity under this Code is considered to be:
1) any activity performed for payment on the market;  
2) any activity performed professionally or in an organised manner for agreed or prescribed payment.  
(11) Performing economic activity or economic operations under this Code is considered to be:
1) execution, management, decision-making, representation, direction and supervision within the framework of activities under the tenth paragraph of this article;  
2) management of real estate, movables, monetary resources, income, receivables, capital investments, other forms of financial assets and other assets of legal persons of public or private law, disposal of these assets and supervision over them.  
(12) A large quantity of counterfeit money, securities and valuable papers under this Code is considered to be a nominal value exceeding 50,000 euros.  
(13) A commodity spot contract under this Code is considered to be a contract for the supply of goods, except wholesale energy products, which is traded on the spot market and which is delivered immediately upon settlement of the transaction, and a contract for the supply of goods which is not a financial instrument, including a physically settled forward contract.  

SPECIAL PART

Chapter Fourteen
CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST HUMANITY

Genocide

## Article 100

(1) Whoever orders or commits, with the intent to destroy, in whole or in part, a national, ethnic, racial or religious group, the following acts: killing members of such a group, causing serious bodily injury or impairment of mental health to members of such a group, deliberately subjecting such a group to living conditions intended to bring about its complete or partial physical destruction, implementing measures preventing births within such a group, forcibly transferring children of one group to another group, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.  
(2) The same punishment shall be imposed on whoever commits an act from the preceding paragraph against any group for reasons under point eight of Article 101.  

Crimes against humanity

## Article 101

Whoever demonstrates or commits acts that are constituent parts of a widespread or systematic attack against a civilian population with the perpetrator's knowledge that it is such an attack: murder; extermination, which means creating such living conditions, including deprivation of access to food and medicine, that are intended to lead to the partial destruction of the population; enslavement, which means exercising any or all of the powers attaching to the right of ownership over a person and includes exercising such powers in the course of trafficking in persons, in particular women and children; deportation or forcible transfer of population, which means forced displacement of persons by expulsion or other coercive acts from the area in which they are lawfully present, without grounds permitted under international law; imprisonment or other severe deprivation of liberty in violation of fundamental rules of international law; torture, which means the intentional infliction of severe pain, physical or mental suffering on a person in the custody of the perpetrator, whereby torture does not include pain or suffering arising only from, or being incidental to, lawful sanctions; rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, which means the unlawful confinement of a woman forcibly made pregnant, with the intent of affecting the ethnic composition of any population or carrying out other grave violations of international law, forced sterilization, or any other form of sexual violence of comparable gravity; persecution, which means the intentional and severe deprivation of fundamental rights contrary to international law by reason of the identity of a group or collectivity, namely on political, racial, national, cultural, religious grounds, grounds related to gender, or other grounds that are universally recognized as impermissible under international law, where such persecution is connected with criminal offences under this Article and under Articles 100, 102 and 103; enforced disappearance of persons, which means the arrest, detention, abduction or any other form of deprivation of liberty of a person by order of a state or political organization or with its authorization, support or acquiescence, which then refuses to acknowledge such deprivation of liberty or to give information about the fate of these persons or their whereabouts, thereby removing legal protection from these persons; apartheid, which means inhumane acts similar to those specified in this Article, committed in the context of an institutionalized regime of systematic oppression and domination by one racial group over any other racial group or groups with the intention of maintaining such a regime; other similar inhumane acts by which great suffering or serious bodily injury or impairment of mental or physical health is intentionally caused,
shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

War crimes

## Article 102

Whoever orders or commits war crimes, especially if they are committed as part of a plan or policy or as part of a large-scale commission of such crimes, which are:
1) grave breaches of the Geneva Conventions of 12 August 1949 (Act on Notification of Succession regarding Council of Europe Conventions, Geneva Conventions and Additional Protocols on the Protection of Victims of War and International Agreements in the Field of Arms Control for which the Three Main Nuclear Powers are Depositaries, (Official Gazette of the RS, No. 14/1992)), namely any of the following acts against persons or property protected by the relevant Geneva Conventions: wilful killing; torture or inhuman treatment, including biological experiments; wilfully causing great suffering or serious injury to body or health;
 extensive destruction or appropriation of property, not justified by military necessity and carried out unlawfully and wantonly; compelling a prisoner of war or other protected person to serve in the forces of a hostile Power; wilfully depriving a prisoner of war or other protected person of the rights of fair and regular trial; unlawful deportation or unlawful confinement; taking of hostages;

2) other violations of the laws and customs applicable in international armed conflicts under applicable international law, namely any of the following acts: intentional attacks on the civilian population as such or against individual civilians not taking direct part in hostilities; intentional attacks on civilian objects, that is, objects which are not military objectives; intentional attacks on personnel, objects and facilities, material, units or vehicles involved in humanitarian assistance or a peacekeeping mission in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations, as long as they are entitled under the international law of armed conflict to the same protection as civilians or civilian objects; intentionally launching an attack in the knowledge that such attack will cause incidental death and injury to civilians or civilian objects or widespread, long-term and severe damage to the natural environment which would be excessive in relation to the concrete and direct overall military advantage anticipated; attacking or bombarding undefended towns, villages, dwellings or buildings which are not military objectives, by whatever means; killing or wounding a combatant who has surrendered at discretion, after he has laid down his arms or no longer has means of defence; improper use of a flag of truce or of the flag or of the military insignia or uniform of the enemy or of the United Nations, the emblem or flag of the Red Cross or corresponding emblems and other distinctive signs under the Geneva Conventions or markings of cultural property under the Hague Convention (Convention for the Protection of Cultural Property in the Event of Armed Conflict with Regulations for its Execution (Official Gazette of the FPRY – International Treaties, No. 4/56) and the Second Protocol to the Hague Convention of 1954 for the Protection of Cultural Property in the Event of Armed Conflict (Official Gazette of the RS, No. 22/2003)), resulting in death or serious injury to a person; direct or indirect transfer by the Occupying Power of parts of its own civilian population into the territory it occupies, or the deportation or transfer of all or parts of the population of the occupied territory within or outside this territory; intentional attacks on buildings dedicated to religion, education, art, science or charitable purposes, cultural or historical monuments, cultural property with special distinctive marking, natural monuments, hospitals and places where the sick and wounded are collected, provided they are not military objectives; subjecting persons who are in the power of the adverse party to physical mutilation or to medical or scientific experiments of any kind which are neither justified by the medical, dental or hospital treatment of the person concerned nor carried out in his interest, and which cause death to or seriously endanger the health of such person or persons; killing or wounding treacherously individuals belonging to the hostile nation or army; improperly taking things from the killed and wounded on the battlefield; declaring that no quarter will be given; destroying or seizing the enemy's property unless such destruction or seizure be imperatively demanded by the necessities of war; declaring that rights and actions of nationals of the hostile party are abolished, suspended or inadmissible in a court of law; compelling the nationals of the hostile party to take part in the operations of war directed against their own country, even if they were in the service of the belligerent before the commencement of the war; pillaging a town or place, even when taken by assault;

 employing poison or poisoned weapons; employing asphyxiating, poisonous or other gases, and all analogous liquids, materials or devices; employing bullets which expand or flatten easily in the human body, such as bullets with a hard envelope which does not entirely cover the core or is pierced with incisions; employing weapons, projectiles and material and methods of warfare which cause superfluous injury or unnecessary suffering or are inherently indiscriminate in violation of the international law of armed conflict, provided that such weapons, projectiles and material and methods of warfare are the subject of a comprehensive prohibition; outrages upon personal dignity, in particular humiliating and degrading treatment; rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, as defined as the unlawful confinement of a woman forcibly made pregnant, with the intent of affecting the ethnic composition of any population or carrying out other grave violations of international law, enforced sterilization, or any other form of sexual violence also constituting a grave breach of the Geneva Conventions; utilizing the presence of a civilian or other protected person to render certain points, areas or military forces immune from military operations; using cultural property under enhanced protection or its immediate surroundings in support of military action; intentional attacks on buildings, material, medical units and transport, and personnel using the distinctive emblems of the Geneva Conventions; intentionally using starvation of civilians as a method of warfare by depriving them of objects indispensable to their survival, including wilfully impeding relief supplies as provided for under the Geneva Conventions; conscripting or enlisting children under the age of fifteen years into the national armed forces or using them to participate actively in hostilities;

3) in an armed conflict not of an international character, but not including internal disturbances and tensions, such as riots, isolated and sporadic acts of violence or other acts of a similar nature, in case of serious violations of Article 3 common to the four Geneva Conventions of 12 August 1949, namely, any of the following acts committed against persons taking no active part in the hostilities, including members of armed forces who have laid down their arms and those placed hors de combat by sickness, wounds, detention or any other cause: violence to life and person, in particular murder of all kinds, mutilation, cruel treatment and torture; outrages upon personal dignity, in particular humiliating and degrading treatment; taking of hostages; the passing of sentences and the carrying out of executions without previous judgment pronounced by a regularly constituted court, affording all judicial guarantees which are generally recognized as indispensable;

4) other serious violations of the laws and customs applicable in armed conflicts not of an international character, under applicable international law, namely any of the following acts: intentional attacks directed against the civilian population as such or against individual civilians not taking direct part in hostilities; intentional attacks on buildings, material, medical units and transport and personnel using the distinctive emblems of the Geneva Conventions in conformity with international law; intentional attacks on personnel, objects and facilities, material, units or vehicles involved in a humanitarian assistance or peacekeeping mission in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations (Act on Notification of Succession regarding Conventions for which the Government of the USA is Depositary, Hague Conventions and Conventions on Intellectual Property (Official Gazette of the RS, No. 24/1992)), as long as they are entitled to the protection given to civilians or civilian objects under the international law of armed conflict;

 intentional attacks on buildings dedicated to religion, education, art, science or charitable purposes, cultural monuments, hospitals and places where the sick and wounded are collected, provided they are not military objectives; pillaging a town or place, even when taken by assault; rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, as defined as the unlawful confinement of a woman forcibly made pregnant, with the intent of affecting the ethnic composition of any population, enforced sterilization, and any other form of sexual violence also constituting a serious violation of Article 3 common to the four Geneva Conventions; conscripting or enlisting children under the age of fifteen years into armed forces or groups or using them to participate actively in hostilities; ordering the displacement of the civilian population for reasons related to the conflict, unless the security of the civilians involved or imperative military reasons so demand; killing or wounding treacherously a combatant of the adverse party; declaring that no quarter will be given; subjecting persons who are in the power of another party to the conflict to physical mutilation or to medical or scientific experiments of any kind which are neither justified by the medical, dental or hospital treatment of the person concerned nor carried out in his or her interest, and which cause death to or seriously endanger the health of such person or persons; destroying or seizing the property of an adversary unless such destruction or seizure be imperatively demanded by the necessities of the conflict; employing poison or poisoned weapons; employing asphyxiating, poisonous or other gases, and all analogous liquids, materials or devices; employing bullets which expand or flatten easily in the human body, such as bullets with a hard envelope which does not entirely cover the core or is pierced with incisions,
shall be punished by imprisonment of not less than fifteen years.

Aggression

## Article 103

(1) An official or other person in a position of effective control or the possibility of directing the political or military action of a state, who plans, prepares, initiates or executes an act of aggression which by its nature, gravity and scale constitutes a manifest violation of the Charter of the United Nations, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.  
(2) An act of aggression is the use of military force directed against the sovereignty, territorial integrity or political independence of another state or in any other manner inconsistent with the Charter of the United Nations. The following acts constitute aggression, regardless of a declaration of war:
1) invasion or armed attack on the territory, sea, aircraft, ports or vessels of another state, with temporary or permanent military occupation or forcible annexation of the territory of that state or part thereof;  
2) bombardment or use of any weapons against the territory of another state;  
3) blockade of ports or coast of another state;  
4) use of armed forces which are on the territory of another state with its consent, in contravention of the conditions of that consent or retention of those forces after the expiry of the time specified in that consent;  
5) allowing another state to use the territory of the Republic of Slovenia, which is at the disposal of that state, to carry out aggression against another state;  
6) sending armed groups, mercenaries or paramilitary units with the aim of carrying out such acts which by their gravity correspond to the acts listed above.  

Responsibility of military commanders and other superiors

## Article 104

(1) A military commander for criminal offences under Articles 100 to 103 or the third paragraph of Article 134 of this Code, which were committed by units under his actual command and control, because he did not properly exercise control over those units and did not take all appropriate and necessary measures within his powers to prevent or stop those criminal offences or to refer the matter to the competent authorities for investigation and prosecution, even though he knew that his units had committed or in the given circumstances could have committed such criminal offences, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to eight years.  
(2) For acts under the preceding paragraph, a person who actually acts as a military commander, or a person who actually exercises managerial authority and control in a civilian organisation or enterprise, shall be punished equally.  
(3) For acts under the first and second paragraphs of this Article, a military commander or a person who actually acts as a military commander, or a person who actually exercises managerial authority and control in a civilian organisation or enterprise, who should have or could have known that his units or other subordinates had committed or in the given circumstances could have committed criminal offences under Articles 100 to 103 or the third paragraph of Article 134 of this Code, shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years.  

Association and incitement to genocide, crimes against humanity or aggression

## Article 105

(1) Whoever establishes a criminal association for the commission of criminal offences under Articles 100 to 103 of this Code shall be punished by imprisonment of one to ten years.  
(2) Whoever becomes a member of the association under the preceding paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years.  
(3) The perpetrator of a criminal offence under the first or second paragraph of this Article who prevents the commission of criminal offences specified in the first paragraph or reports them in time shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years, but may also be exempted from punishment.  
(4) Whoever calls for or incites the direct commission of criminal offences under Articles 100 to 103 shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years.  

Recruitment of military mercenaries and persons under eighteen years of age

## Article 106

(1) Whoever during war, armed conflict or occupation or whoever in carrying out or in support of a policy of any state or organisation as part of a large-scale systematic attack orders the recruitment or recruits persons who are not yet eighteen years of age into state or other armed forces and exploits them or orders their exploitation for active participation in hostilities shall be punished by imprisonment of ten to fifteen years.  

(2) Whoever recruits, trains or finances the training of military mercenaries shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Unjustified delay of repatriation of prisoners of war or civilians

## Article 107

Whoever in violation of the rules of international law after the end of a war or armed conflict orders the delay or himself delays the repatriation of prisoners of war or civilians shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years.  

Terrorism

## Article 108

(1) Whoever, with the intent to destroy or seriously endanger the constitutional, economic, social or political foundations of the Republic of Slovenia or another state or international organisation, to seriously intimidate the population, or to compel the government of the Republic of Slovenia or another state or an international organisation to do or refrain from doing something, commits or threatens to commit one or more of the following acts: an attack on life and body or on human rights and freedoms, abduction or hostage-taking, substantial destruction of state or public facilities or representations of foreign states, transport system, infrastructure, public place or private property, unauthorised interference with an information system which constitutes serious disruption or interruption of its operation by inputting, transmitting, damaging, deleting or altering data or by preventing or disabling access to data, and which causes serious damage or was committed using a computer programme, passwords or access codes designed or adapted for the purpose of committing the act, or unauthorised interference with an information system of critical infrastructure which constitutes serious disruption or interruption of its operation by inputting, transmitting, damaging, deleting or altering data or preventing or disabling access to data, unauthorised deletion, damaging or altering of data in an information system of critical infrastructure or preventing or disabling access to such data, seizure or taking control of a fixed platform in the continental shelf by use of force or threat or other means of intimidation, or violent conduct against a person on the platform if such act endangers its safety, or destruction of a fixed platform in the continental shelf or causing damage to it which may endanger its safety, or placing a device for the purpose of destroying a fixed platform or endangering safety on it, or injuring or causing the death of a person in connection with any of the aforementioned acts, hijacking of an aircraft, vessel, means of cargo transport or public means of transport, manufacture, possession, acquisition, transport, supply or use of weapons, explosives, nuclear, biological or chemical weapons, research and development of nuclear, biological or chemical weapons, endangering safety by releasing hazardous substances or causing fires, floods or explosions, disruption or interruption of supply of water, electricity or other natural resources essential for human life, which may endanger human life,
shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(2) Whoever seeks to achieve the purpose referred to in the preceding paragraph by using or threatening to use nuclear or other radioactive material or device, by damaging a nuclear facility so that it releases radioactive material or enables its release, by demanding nuclear or other radioactive material, device or nuclear facility by threat or use of force, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to fifteen years.
(3) Whoever prepares or assists in the preparation of criminal offences referred to in the preceding paragraphs by unlawfully acquiring or making available material resources necessary for the commission of these criminal offences, or by coercion prepares another person to participate in these criminal offences, or forges official or public documents necessary for the commission of these criminal offences, or uses such documents, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.
(4) If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from eight to fifteen years.
(5) If the perpetrator, in committing the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article, intentionally takes the life of one or more persons, he shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.
(6) If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article is committed in a criminal organisation or group which has the purpose of carrying out the criminal offences (hereinafter terrorist criminal organisation or group) referred to in those paragraphs, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from eight to fifteen years.
(7) Whoever participates in a terrorist criminal organisation or group which has the purpose of committing criminal offences referred to in the first, second, fourth or fifth paragraph of this Article shall be punished by imprisonment of up to eight years.
(8) Whoever establishes or leads a criminal organisation referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Travel abroad for the purpose of terrorism

Article 108a
(1) Whoever travels abroad with the purpose of committing acts referred to in Article 108 of this Code shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.
(2) Whoever travels abroad with the purpose of committing acts referred to in the second or third paragraph of Article 111 of this Code shall be punished by imprisonment of up to eight years.
(3) Whoever, knowing that it is for the purpose referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article, organises or otherwise facilitates travel referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Financing of terrorism

## Article 109

(1) Whoever provides or collects money or property with the intent or knowing that it will be used in whole or in part for the commission of an act referred to in Articles 108, 108a, 110, 111, 307, 329, 330, 352, 353, 354, 355, 371 and 373 of this Code shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) The perpetrator of the act referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be punished in the same manner even if the money or property provided or collected with intent was not actually used for the commission of the criminal offences referred to in the preceding paragraph.
(3) The same punishment shall apply to whoever provides or collects money or property with the intent or knowing that it will be used in whole or in part by a terrorist, terrorist criminal organisation or group for carrying out terrorist criminal offences.
(4) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraphs was committed in a terrorist criminal organisation or group for carrying out terrorist criminal offences, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.
(5) The money and property referred to in the preceding paragraphs shall be confiscated.

Incitement and public glorification of terrorist acts

## Article 110

(1) Whoever incites the commission of criminal offences referred to in Article 108 of this Code by disseminating messages or making them available to other persons in any other manner and thereby causes a danger for the commission of one or more such criminal offences shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.
(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever directly or indirectly publicly glorifies or advocates criminal offences referred to in Article 108 or a criminal offence referred to in the preceding paragraph by disseminating messages or making them available to the public and thereby causes a danger for the commission of one or more such acts.
(3) Prosecution for criminal offences referred to in the preceding paragraphs shall be initiated with the authorisation of the Minister of Justice.

Recruitment and training for terrorism

## Article 111

(1) Whoever recruits for terrorism by encouraging another person to commit a criminal offence referred to in Article 108 of this Code or to participate in ordering such a terrorist criminal offence or to join a terrorist criminal organisation or group for carrying out terrorist criminal offences committed by that criminal organisation or group shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.
(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever trains others in the manufacture and use of explosives, firearms or other weapons, harmful or dangerous substances, or in other special methods and techniques with the intent of committing or participating in the commission of a criminal offence referred to in Article 108 of this Code.
(3) The same punishment shall apply to whoever, through active participation, trains in the manufacture and use of explosives, firearms or other weapons, harmful or dangerous substances, or in other special methods and techniques with the intent of committing or participating in the commission of a criminal offence referred to in Article 108 of this Code.

Placing in a relationship of slavery

## 112. člen

(1)	Whoever, in violation of the rules of international law, places another person in slavery or a similar relationship or keeps them in such a relationship, buys, sells, hands over to another person or acts as an intermediary in the purchase, sale or handover of such a person, or incites another to sell their freedom or the freedom of a person they support or care for, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(2)	Whoever transports persons in slavery or a similar relationship from one country to another shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(3)	Whoever commits an act from the first or second paragraph of this article against a minor shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  

Trgovina z ljudmi

## 113. člen

(1)	Whoever, for the purpose of exploitation through prostitution or other forms of sexual abuse, forced labour, begging, slavery or a similar relationship, servitude, commission of criminal offences or trade in organs, human tissues or blood, buys, takes over, accommodates, transports, sells, hands over or otherwise disposes of another person, or recruits such a person, exchanges or transfers control over them, or acts as an intermediary in such conduct, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine, regardless of any consent of that person.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed against a minor or by force, threat, deception, abduction or abuse of a subordinate or dependent position, or by giving or receiving payments or benefits to obtain the consent of a person having control over another person, or with the intention of forcing pregnancy or artificial insemination, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  
(3)	Whoever, with the intention of committing an act from the first or second paragraph of this article, withholds, takes away, conceals, damages or destroys a public document by which the identity of a victim of human trafficking is established, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years and a fine.  
(4)	Whoever knows that a person is a victim of human trafficking and uses their services that are the result of exploitation of that person as described in the first and second paragraphs of this article, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years and a fine.  
(5)	Whoever commits an act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article as a member of a criminal organisation for the commission of such acts, or if a large material benefit was obtained by such act, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years and a fine.  
(6)	The same as in the preceding paragraph shall be punished an official or public employee who, in the performance of their duties, commits an act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article.  

Prepovedana tvorba živih bitij

## 114. člen

(1)	Whoever produces or participates in the production or attempts at production or crossing of humans or other species, which is harmful to humanity and prohibited by regulations and international law, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.  
(2)	If the acts from the preceding paragraph involve the creation of a human being that is genetically identical to another living or dead human being, the creation of human embryos for research, industrial or commercial purposes, or the replacement of significant human body parts or organs, which is not permitted by regulations and international law, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.  
(3)	Whoever carries out genetic tests that can predict hereditary diseases or enable the determination of gene carriage responsible for a disease, or detect genetic predisposition or susceptibility to a disease, but these tests are not carried out exclusively for health purposes or as health research for health purposes, or whoever, when carrying out tests, omits appropriate genetic counselling, or in violation of regulations carries out scientific research in the field of biology and medicine that is prohibited by regulations and international law, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to three years.  
(4)	The same as in the preceding paragraph shall be punished whoever, in research on human embryos, endangers the integrity or life of a human embryo.  
(5)	Whoever enables acts from the first or second paragraph of this article by financing, by making available facilities, devices or substances for the production of living beings or their crossing, by recruiting collaborators or by otherwise organising production, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years.

Uboj

## 115. člen

(1)	Whoever takes the life of another shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by two or more persons who have joined together to commit homicide, the perpetrator shall be punished by a sentence from ten to fifteen years.  

Umor

## 116. člen

Whoever murders someone by taking their life
1)	in a gruesome or treacherous manner;  
2)	because of action in official acts of protecting public safety or in pre-criminal proceedings, or because of decisions of state prosecutors, or because of proceedings and decisions of judges, or because of a denunciation or testimony in court proceedings;  
3)	because of a violation of equality;  

4)	out of bloodlust, out of greed, in order to commit or conceal another criminal offence, out of ruthless revenge or out of other base motives;  
5)	by an act committed in a criminal organisation for the commission of such acts, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.  

Uboj na mah

## 117. člen

Whoever kills someone in the heat of passion because they were, without their fault, strongly provoked by an attack or serious insults, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  

Povzročitev smrti iz malomarnosti

## 118. člen

Whoever causes the death of another through negligence shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  

Detomor

## 119. člen

A mother who takes the life of her child during childbirth or immediately after it, while still under its influence, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.  

Napeljevanje k samomoru in pomoč pri samomoru

## 120. člen

(1)	Whoever intentionally incites someone to commit suicide or assists them in it and they commit it, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2)	Whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph against a minor who is already fourteen years old, or against a person whose ability to understand the meaning of their act or to control their conduct was substantially diminished, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(3)	If the act from the first paragraph of this article is committed against a minor who is not yet fourteen years old, or against a person who could not understand the meaning of their act or control their conduct, the perpetrator shall be punished as for homicide or murder.  
(4)	Whoever treats cruelly or inhumanely someone who is subordinate to or dependent on them, and because of such treatment they commit suicide, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(5)	Whoever assists someone in suicide and they commit it, but there are special mitigating circumstances, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.  

(6)	If someone has only attempted suicide because of any act from the preceding paragraphs, the court may punish the perpetrator more leniently.  

Nedovoljen poseg v nosečnost

## 121. člen

(1) Whoever, contrary to the health conditions and method of artificial termination of pregnancy determined by law, terminates the pregnancy of a pregnant woman with her consent, begins to terminate it, or assists her in terminating the pregnancy, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2) Whoever terminates or begins to terminate the pregnancy of a pregnant woman without her consent shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(3) Whoever, by using methods of medically assisted fertilisation, influences the selection of the sex of the future child, except where this is intended to avoid a serious hereditary disease linked to sex, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(4) The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph of this Article shall apply to whoever unlawfully carries out a procedure of biomedically assisted fertilisation for the purpose of surrogate motherhood.  
(5) The same punishment as in the third paragraph of this Article shall apply to whoever trades in sperm cells, unfertilised egg cells and early human embryos.  
(6) If an act from the preceding paragraphs results in particularly serious bodily injury to the woman, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(7) If the woman dies as a result of an act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  

Minor bodily injury

## 122. člen

(1) Whoever injures another person bodily in such a way that a part of their body or their organ is temporarily impaired or weakened, or the injured person's capacity for work is temporarily reduced, or their appearance is affected, or their health is temporarily impaired, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) If the injury from the preceding paragraph is inflicted with a weapon, dangerous tool, other means, or in such a manner that the body can be seriously injured or health seriously impaired, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(3) The court may impose a judicial admonition on the perpetrator of the act from the preceding paragraph, especially if the perpetrator was provoked by indecent or cruel behaviour of the injured person.  
(4) Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this Article shall be initiated upon a motion.  

Serious bodily injury

## 123. člen

(1) Whoever injures another person bodily or causes such harm to their health that the life of the injured person could be endangered, or a part of their body or an organ is destroyed or permanently and substantially weakened, or an important part of the body or an important organ is temporarily and substantially weakened, or the injured person is thereby rendered temporarily incapable of any work, or their capacity for work is permanently reduced or has been temporarily considerably reduced, or they are temporarily disfigured, or their health is temporarily seriously or permanently to a lesser extent impaired, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2) If the injured person dies as a result of the injury from the preceding paragraph, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(3) Whoever commits the act from the first paragraph of this Article by negligence shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(4) If the perpetrator committed the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article on the spur of the moment because the injured person, without the perpetrator's fault, strongly provoked them by an attack or serious insults, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Particularly serious bodily injury

## 124. člen

(1) Whoever injures another person so seriously bodily or causes such serious harm to their health that the life of the injured person was endangered, or an important part of their body or an important organ is destroyed or permanently and very severely weakened, or the injured person has thereby become permanently incapable of any work, or has remained disfigured, or their health has been permanently seriously impaired, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(2) If the injured person dies as a result of the injury from the preceding paragraph, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  
(3) Whoever commits the act from the first paragraph of this Article by negligence shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(4) If the perpetrator committed the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article on the spur of the moment because the injured person, without the perpetrator's fault, strongly provoked them by an attack or serious insults, they shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  

Exclusion of criminal offence in case of bodily injury with the consent of the injured person

## 125. člen

(1) Causing minor bodily injury (Article 122) is not unlawful if the injured person consented to it. In this case, in the case of a minor or incapacitated person, consent is taken into account if it is given in accordance with the law in care for their health by the person who represents such persons.  
(2) Intentional causing of serious (Article 123) or particularly serious (Article 124) bodily injury is not unlawful if the injured person consented to it and the interests of another person were not affected or any common legal value endangered.  

(3) Notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, intentional causing of serious or particularly serious bodily injury in treatment or medical activity is not unlawful if consent was given in the form and under the conditions determined by law.  

Participation in a brawl

## 126. člen

Whoever participates in a brawl in which someone is killed or seriously bodily injured shall be punished for the participation alone by imprisonment of up to one year.  

Endangerment with a dangerous tool in a brawl or quarrel

## 127. člen

(1) Whoever, in a brawl or quarrel, reaches for a weapon, dangerous tool or any other means with which the body can be seriously injured or health seriously impaired, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to six months.  
(2) Prosecution shall be initiated upon a motion.  

Causing danger

## 128. člen

Whoever leaves someone without assistance in mortal danger which they themselves caused shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Abandonment of an incapacitated person

## 129. člen

Whoever leaves a person who has been entrusted to them or for whom they must otherwise care without assistance in circumstances that are dangerous for life or health shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Failure to render assistance

## 130. člen

Whoever does not assist a person who is in immediate mortal danger, although they could do so without danger to themselves or to another person, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

Violation of equality

## 131. člen

(1) Whoever, on the basis of a difference in nationality, race, colour, religion, ethnic affiliation, sex, language, political or other conviction, sexual orientation, property status, birth, genetic heritage, education, social position or any other circumstance, deprives someone of any of the human rights or fundamental freedoms recognised by the international community or determined by the constitution or law, or restricts such a right or freedom, or whoever, on the basis of such differentiation, gives someone any special right or advantage, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever persecutes an individual or organisation for advocating equality of people.  
(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article is committed by an official person by abuse of official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Coercion

## 132. člen

(1) Whoever, by force or serious threat, coerces someone to do or omit something or to suffer something, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) Prosecution shall be initiated upon a motion.  

Forced conclusion of marriage or establishment of a similar community

132.a člen
(1) Whoever, by force or threat to use force, or by abuse of the subordinate or dependent position of another, coerces them into concluding a marriage or into establishing a similar community which, in accordance with the law, is equated with marriage in certain legal consequences, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2) Whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph against a minor or an incapacitated person shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  

Unlawful deprivation of liberty

## Article 133

(1) Whoever unlawfully confines another person, keeps them confined or otherwise restricts their freedom of movement, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person by abusing their position or their powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  

(3) An attempt to commit the act from the first paragraph of this article is punishable.  
(4) Whoever unlawfully deprives another person of their liberty for more than one week or does so in a cruel manner, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  

Abduction and enforced disappearance

## Article 134

(1) Whoever abducts another person with the intention of forcing them or another person to do, omit or suffer something, or whoever in connection with the abduction forces the abducted person or another person to do, omit or suffer something, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2) Whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph against a minor or threatens to kill or seriously injure the abducted person, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(3) Whoever arrests, detains, abducts or otherwise deprives another person of their liberty on the order of a state or political organisation or with its authorisation, support or consent, which then does not acknowledge such deprivation of liberty or refuses to provide information about the fate of such person or their whereabouts, thereby depriving that person of legal protection, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(4) The perpetrator of acts from the first or second paragraph of this article who voluntarily releases the abducted person before their demand for which they abducted them is fulfilled, may be punished more leniently or may have their punishment remitted.  

Stalking

Article 134.a
(1) Whoever stalks another person or their relative by repeated observation, following or intrusive attempts to establish direct contact or contact via electronic communication means and thereby causes fear or a sense of threat in them or their relative, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2) If the stalked person is a minor or a vulnerable person, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to three years.  
(3) Prosecution for the act from the first and second paragraphs of this article shall be initiated upon proposal.  

Threat

## Article 135

(1) Whoever, in order to intimidate or disturb another person, seriously threatens to attack their life or body or liberty or to destroy their property of great value, or to commit these acts against their relative, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to six months.  

(2) Whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph against two or more persons or with abusive conduct or with a weapon, dangerous tool, other means or in such a manner that the body can be seriously injured or health seriously impaired, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(3) Prosecution for the act from the first and second paragraphs of this article shall be initiated upon proposal.  

Torture

Article 135.a
(1) Whoever intentionally inflicts severe pain or suffering on another person, whether physical or mental, in order to obtain from them or a third person information or a confession, to punish them for an act committed by them or a third person or of which they or a third person is suspected, to intimidate them or exert pressure on them or to intimidate a third person or exert pressure on them or for any other reason based on any form of violation of equality, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(2) If the pain or suffering from the preceding paragraph is inflicted or caused by an official person or another person acting with official status or authorisation or at the instigation of such person or with their express or tacit consent, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three to twelve years.  

Unauthorised personal search

## Article 136

(1) Whoever unlawfully searches another person or things they have on them or with them, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person by abusing their official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(3) An attempt to commit the acts from the first and second paragraphs of this article is punishable.  
(4) Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this article shall be initiated upon proposal.  

Unauthorised eavesdropping and audio recording

## Article 137

(1) Whoever unlawfully eavesdrops with special devices on a conversation or statement not intended for them, or audio records it, or whoever directly transmits such conversation or such statement to a third person or plays such recording to them or otherwise enables them to become directly acquainted with it, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever audio records a confidential statement of another person intended for them without their consent with the intention of abusing such statement, or whoever directly transmits such statement to a third person or plays such recording to them or otherwise enables them to become directly acquainted with it.  

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article is committed by an official person by abusing their official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(4) Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this article shall be initiated upon proposal, and for the act from the second paragraph upon private action.  

Unauthorised image recording

## Article 138

(1) Whoever unlawfully image records or makes an image recording of another person or their premises without their consent and thereby significantly interferes with their privacy or whoever directly transmits such recording to a third person or shows such recording to them or otherwise enables them to become directly acquainted with it, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person by abusing their official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(3) Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this article shall be initiated upon proposal.  

Violation of secrecy of communications

## Article 139

(1) Whoever unlawfully opens another person's letter, another person's telegram or any other sealed writing or shipment belonging to another person, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to six months.  
(2) The following shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year:
1) whoever, by using technical or chemical means, without opening another person's letter, another person's telegram or any other sealed shipment belonging to another person, unlawfully becomes acquainted with their content;  
2) whoever, by using technical means, unlawfully becomes acquainted with a message transmitted by telephone or any other electronic communication means;  
3) whoever unlawfully opens a sealed object protecting a message and unlawfully becomes acquainted with the message in it.  
(3) The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall apply to whoever, by any of the acts specified in the first and second paragraphs of this article, enables another person to become directly acquainted with the content of the message or shipment.  
(4) Whoever unlawfully withholds, conceals, destroys or hands over to another person another person's letter, telegram or any other shipment before the recipient has become acquainted with its content, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(5) If the act from the preceding paragraphs of this article is committed by an official person by abusing their official position or official powers, a postal or other worker entrusted with receiving, transporting or delivering other people's letters, other people's telegrams or any other writings or shipments, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(6) Prosecution for the acts from the first to fourth paragraphs of this article shall be initiated upon proposal.  

Unauthorised publication of private writings

## Article 140

(1) Whoever, without the permission of an authorised person, where such permission is required, publishes a diary, letter or any other private writing, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2) Prosecution shall be initiated upon private action.  

Violation of inviolability of dwelling

## 141. člen

(1)	Whoever unlawfully enters another person's dwelling or enclosed premises or whoever fails to leave upon the request of the person entitled or otherwise prevents them from using such premises, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever unlawfully searches a dwelling or premises referred to in the first paragraph of this Article.  
(3)	If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article is committed by an official person by abusing their official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(4)	An attempt to commit the acts referred to in the first, second and third paragraphs of this Article is punishable.  
(5)	If the court imposes a suspended sentence, it may order the perpetrator to vacate the dwelling or premises within a specified period.  
(6)	Prosecution for the acts referred to in the first and second paragraphs of this Article shall be initiated upon a motion.  

Unauthorised Disclosure of Professional Secrets

## 142. člen

(1)	Whoever unlawfully discloses a secret which they learned as a defence counsel, lawyer, doctor, priest, social worker, psychologist or as any other person in the performance of their profession, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	The act referred to in the preceding paragraph shall not be punishable if the person discloses the secret in the general interest or legitimate interest of the public or in the interest of another person, if such interest is greater than the preservation of the secret, or if the law provides for a release from the duty to protect the secret.  
(3)	Prosecution shall be initiated by private action.  

Misuse of Personal Data

## 143. člen

(1)	Whoever, without a basis in law or in the personal consent of the individual to whom the personal data relate, transmits for public disclosure or publicly discloses personal data that are processed on the basis of law or personal consent of the individual, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever breaks into or unlawfully accesses a computerised database with the intention of obtaining any personal data for themselves or another person.  
(3)	Whoever publicly discloses on the world wide web or otherwise, or enables another person to disclose, personal data of victims of criminal offences, victims of violations of rights or freedoms, protected witnesses, which are contained in court files of judicial proceedings where the presence of the public or identification of victims or protected witnesses and personal records about them in connection with the judicial proceedings is not permitted by law or by court decision, on the basis of which these persons can be determined or are determinable, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(4)	Whoever, by assuming the identity of another person or by processing their personal data, exploits their rights, obtains pecuniary or non-pecuniary benefit at their expense or injures their personal dignity, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to three years.  
(5)	Whoever commits the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article by transmitting for public disclosure or publicly disclosing sensitive personal data, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(6)	Whoever publicly discloses recordings or messages of another person with sexual content without the consent of that person and thereby seriously injures their privacy, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to three years.  
(7)	If the act referred to in the preceding paragraphs of this Article is committed by an official person by abusing their official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(8)	Prosecution under the fourth and sixth paragraphs of this Article shall be initiated upon a motion.  

Violation of the Right to a Legal Remedy or Petition

## 144. člen

(1)	Whoever, in the performance of their work, prevents another person from exercising their right to appeal, objection or any other legal remedy, to an application or proposal, or from making political or other initiatives of general importance, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person by abusing their position or powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Prevention or Obstruction of a Public Assembly

## 145. člen

(1)	Whoever by force, serious threat, deception or otherwise unlawfully prevents or obstructs the convening or course of a peaceful public assembly, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	If the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article is committed by an official person by abusing their official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Prevention of Printing and Broadcasting

## 146. člen

Whoever unlawfully prevents the printing, sale or distribution of a newspaper, book or other printed matter or the broadcasting of a radio or television programme, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  

Violation of Moral Copyright

## 147. člen

(1)	Whoever publishes, displays, performs or transmits another person's copyrighted work or part thereof under their own name or the name of another person, or permits this to be done, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	Whoever distorts, mutilates or otherwise unlawfully interferes with another person's copyrighted work, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  
(3)	Prosecution shall be initiated upon a motion.  

Violation of Material Copyright

## 148. člen

(1)	Whoever unlawfully uses one or more copyrighted works or copies thereof, the total market value of which represents a greater pecuniary value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2)	If the market value of the copyrighted works referred to in the preceding paragraph represents a large pecuniary value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(3)	If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article resulted in the acquisition of a large unlawful pecuniary benefit and the perpetrator intended to obtain such pecuniary benefit for themselves or another person, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(4)	Copies of copyrighted works and devices for their reproduction shall be seized.  
(5)	In determining the pecuniary value under the provisions of this Article and Article 149 of this Code, the benefit from the unauthorised use of material copyright or the unauthorised reproduction, making available to the public, distribution or rental of related rights for profit-making purposes shall be taken into account.  

Violation of Related Rights

## 149. člen

(1)	Whoever unlawfully reproduces, makes available to the public, distributes or rents one or more performances, phonograms, videograms, radio and television broadcasts or databases, the total market value of which represents a greater pecuniary value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2)	Whoever unlawfully reproduces, makes available to the public, distributes or rents one or more performances, phonograms, videograms, radio and television broadcasts or databases, the total market value of which represents a large pecuniary value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(3)	If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article resulted in the acquisition of a large unlawful pecuniary benefit and the perpetrator intended to obtain such pecuniary benefit for themselves or another person, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(4)	Copies of performances, phonograms, videograms, radio and television broadcasts or databases and devices for their reproduction shall be seized.  

Sedemnajsto poglavje
KAZNIVA DEJANJA ZOPER VOLILNO PRAVICO IN VOLITVE

Violation of Electoral Rights

## 150. člen

An official person who, with the intention of preventing someone from exercising their electoral right, unlawfully fails to enter them in the electoral register or deletes them from it, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  

Violation of Free Decision of Voters

## 151. člen

(1)	Whoever by force, serious threat, bribery, deception or other impermissible means compels or influences someone to vote or not to vote in elections or voting, or not to vote validly, or to vote for or against a particular proposal, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person in the performance of their duty in connection with elections or voting, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(3)	The bribe given shall be seized.  

Abuse of Electoral Rights

## 152. člen

Whoever votes in elections or voting in place of another person under their name or votes more than once in the same voting, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  

Violation of Free Expression

## 153. člen

Whoever calls upon a voter during elections or voting to answer for their vote or demands that they disclose how they voted or why they did not vote shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  

Destruction or falsification of electoral documents

## 154. člen

(1)	Whoever during elections or voting destroys, damages, conceals or falsifies any document relating to elections or voting or any object that serves as evidence for determining the outcome of elections or voting shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person in the performance of their duty in connection with elections or voting, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Falsification of electoral results

## 155. člen

An official person who during elections or voting alters the number of votes cast by adding or removing ballots or votes during counting or announces the result of elections or voting that does not correspond to the voting conducted shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Violation of voting secrecy

## 156. člen

(1)	Whoever in any way violates the secrecy of voting during elections or voting shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person in the performance of their duties in connection with elections or voting, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Accepting a bribe in elections

## 157. člen

(1)	Whoever, in order not to vote in elections or voting or to vote for or against a particular proposal or not to vote validly, demands or accepts a reward, gift or any other pecuniary or non-pecuniary benefit for themselves or for another person shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	The accepted reward, gift or other pecuniary benefit shall be confiscated.  

Osemnajsto poglavje
KAZNIVA DEJANJA ZOPER ČAST IN DOBRO IME

Insult

## 158. člen

(1)	Whoever insults someone shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three months.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed through the press, radio, television or another means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  
(3)	A person who expresses themselves insultingly about someone in a scientific, literary or artistic work, in serious criticism, in the performance of an official duty, journalistic profession, political or other social activity, in the defence of a right or protection of legitimate interests shall not be punished if it is evident from the manner of expression or from other circumstances that they did not do so with the intention of denigration.  
(4)	If the insulted person returned the insult, the court may punish both parties or one of them or remit the punishment.  

Defamation

## 159. člen

(1)	Whoever asserts or spreads something untrue about someone that may harm their honour or reputation, even though they know that what they assert or spread is untrue, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed through the press, radio, television or another means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(3)	If what is untruthfully asserted or spread is of such a nature that it has serious consequences for the injured party, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Offensive accusation

## 160. člen

(1)	Whoever asserts or spreads something about someone that may harm their honour or reputation shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three months.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed through the press, radio, television or another means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  
(3)	If what is asserted or spread is of such a nature that it has serious consequences for the injured party, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(4)	If they prove the truthfulness of their assertion or if they prove that they had reasonable grounds to believe in the truthfulness of what they asserted or spread, the perpetrator shall not be punished for offensive accusation, but may be punished for insult (158. člen) or imputation of a criminal offence with the intention of denigration (162. člen).  
(5)	If someone asserts or spreads that someone committed a criminal offence for which the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio, the truthfulness that the injured party committed the criminal offence may be proven only by a final judgment, and by other evidence only if prosecution or trial is not possible or not permitted.  
(6)	If the offensive accusation that the injured party committed a criminal offence for which the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio was made in the circumstances from the third paragraph of
158. člen of this code, the perpetrator shall not be punished for offensive accusation, even though there is no final judgment, if they prove that they had reasonable grounds to believe in the truthfulness of what they asserted or spread.  

Gossip

## 161. člen

(1)	Whoever asserts or spreads something from the personal or family life of a person that may harm their reputation shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three months.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed through the press, radio, television or another means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  
(3)	If what someone asserts or spreads is of such a nature that it has serious consequences for the injured party, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(4)	The truthfulness or untruthfulness of what is asserted or spread from the personal or family life of another person cannot be proven, except in the cases from the fifth paragraph of this article.  
(5)	Whoever asserts or spreads something from the personal or family life of another person in the performance of an official duty, political or other social activity, in the defence of a right or protection of legitimate interests shall not be punished if they prove the truthfulness of their assertion or if they prove that they had reasonable grounds to believe in the truthfulness of what they asserted or spread.  

Imputation of a criminal offence with the intention of denigration

## 162. člen

(1)	Whoever with the intention of denigration imputes to someone that they committed a criminal offence or that they were convicted of a criminal offence or tells this to someone with the same intention shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three months.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed through the press, radio, television or another means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  

Defamation of the Republic of Slovenia

## 163. člen

(1)	Whoever publicly commits an act from 158. to 162. člen of this code against the Republic of Slovenia or the President of the Republic in connection with the performance of their duties shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever publicly defames the flag, coat of arms or anthem of the Republic of
Slovenia.  

Defamation of a foreign state or international organisation

## 164. člen

(1)	Whoever publicly commits an act from 158. to 162. člen of this code against a foreign state, the head of a foreign state or a diplomatic representative of a foreign state in the Republic of Slovenia or whoever publicly defames the flag, coat of arms or anthem of a foreign state shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever commits an act from the preceding paragraph against an international organisation recognised by the Republic of Slovenia or against its representative or its symbols.  

Defamation of the Slovenian nation or national communities

## 165. člen

Whoever publicly commits an act from 158. to 160. člen of this code against the Slovenian nation or against the Italian or Hungarian national community or against the Roma community living in the Republic of Slovenia shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  

Javna objava kaznivih dejanj zoper čast in dobro ime

## 166. člen

(1)	The editor-in-chief or the person replacing them shall be punished for criminal offences under Articles 158 to 165 of this Code, committed by public disclosure of these acts in newspapers and magazines, radio and television programmes, electronic publications, teletext or other forms of daily or periodical publications or on websites, within the limits of the penalty prescribed for the criminal offence, under one of the following conditions:
1)	if the author remained unknown until the end of the main hearing before the first instance court;  
2)	if the information was published without the author's consent;  
3)	if, at the time the information was published, there were factual or legal obstacles to the prosecution of the author which still persist.  
(2)	Under the conditions of the preceding paragraph, the publisher or printer shall be punished equally if the public disclosure of criminal offences under Articles 158 to 165 of this Code was committed through a non-periodical printed publication, and the producer if it was committed through a gramophone record, compact disc, film, DVD and other video media, audio or similar media intended for a wider circle of people.  
(3)	The editor-in-chief or the person replacing them shall not be punished for the act under the first paragraph of this Article if it was a live broadcast which they could not prevent or a publication on websites that allow users to publish content in real time or without prior supervision.  

Remission of punishment for criminal offences under Articles 158 to 162

## 167. člen

If the perpetrator of a criminal offence under Articles 158 to 162 and 166 of this Code was provoked by indecent or cruel conduct of the injured party or apologises to the injured party before the court or retracts before the court what they claimed or disseminated, the court may remit the punishment.  

Special provisions on prosecution

## 168. člen

(1)	Prosecution for criminal offences under Articles 158 to 162 and 166 of this Code shall be initiated by private action.  
(2)	If the acts under Articles 158 to 162 and 166 of this Code were committed against a state body or a municipal or regional body or against an official or military person in connection with the performance of their service in that body, prosecution shall be initiated upon a motion, except for official persons under point 1 of the first paragraph of Article 99, functionaries of the Government of the Republic of Slovenia and functionaries of a local self-government community, where prosecution shall be initiated by private action.  
(3)	Prosecution for a criminal offence under Article 164 of this Code shall be initiated with the permission of the Minister of Justice.  
(4)	If the acts under Articles 158 to 162 and 166 of this Code were committed against a deceased person, prosecution shall be initiated by private action of the spouse, the person with whom the deceased lived in an extramarital community, the partner from a registered same-sex partnership, their children or adoptees, parents or adopters, brothers or sisters.  

Publication of judgment

## 169. člen

Upon conviction for criminal offences under Articles 158 to 165 of this Code, committed through the press, radio, television or other means of public information or on websites, the court may, at the request of the injured party, decide that the judgment be published at the expense of the convicted person in the same manner as the act was committed, in whole or in extract.  

Chapter Nineteen
CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST SEXUAL INTEGRITY

Rape

## 170. člen

(1)	Whoever, without the consent of another person, achieves that person to engage in sexual intercourse or equivalent sexual conduct shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2)	Consent under the preceding paragraph of this Article is given if the person, according to their outwardly perceptible, unambiguous and free will, consented to sexual intercourse or equivalent sexual conduct and was capable of making such a decision.  
(3)	Whoever coerces another person into sexual intercourse or equivalent sexual conduct by using force or threatening with an immediate attack on life or body shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(4)	If the act under the preceding paragraph was committed in a cruel or particularly degrading manner or if the act was committed by several persons successively or against convicts or other persons deprived of their liberty, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  
(5)	Whoever coerces another person into sexual intercourse or equivalent sexual conduct by threatening to disclose something about them or their relatives that would harm their honour or reputation, or to cause great pecuniary damage to them or their relatives, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(6)	If the acts under the first, third or fifth paragraph of this Article were committed against a person with whom the perpetrator lives in a marital, extramarital community or registered same-sex community, prosecution shall be initiated upon a motion.  

Sexual violence

## 171. člen

(1)	Whoever, without the consent of another person, achieves that person to commit or suffer any sexual act not covered by the preceding Article shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(2)	Consent under the preceding paragraph is given if the person, according to their outwardly perceptible, unambiguous and free will, consented to the sexual act under the preceding paragraph and was capable of making such a decision.  

(3)	Whoever uses force or threatens another person with an immediate attack on life or body and thereby coerces them to commit or suffer any sexual act under the first paragraph of this Article shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to ten years.  
(4)	If the act under the preceding paragraph was committed in a cruel or particularly degrading manner or if the act was committed by several persons successively or against convicts or other persons deprived of their liberty, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  
(5)	Whoever coerces another person to commit or suffer any sexual act under the first paragraph of this Article by threatening to disclose something about them or their relatives that would harm their honour or reputation, or to cause great pecuniary damage to them or their relatives, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(6)	If the acts under the first, third or fifth paragraph of this Article were committed against a person with whom the perpetrator lives in a marital, extramarital community or registered same-sex partnership community, prosecution shall be initiated upon a motion.  

Sexual abuse of a helpless person

## 172. člen

(1)	Whoever engages in sexual intercourse or commits any other sexual act with another person without their consent by abusing their mental illness, temporary mental disorder or severe mental underdevelopment, due to which they are not capable of giving consent, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(2)	Consent under the preceding paragraph is given if the person, according to their outwardly perceptible, unambiguous and free will, consented to sexual intercourse or any other sexual act and was capable of making such a decision.  
(3)	Whoever, in the circumstances under the first paragraph of this Article, otherwise violates the sexual integrity of a helpless person shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  

Sexual assault on a person under fifteen years of age

## 173. člen

(1)	Whoever has sexual intercourse or commits any other sexual act with a person of the other or same sex who is not yet fifteen years old shall be punished by imprisonment from three to eight years.  
(2)	Whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph with a feeble-minded person who is not yet fifteen years old, or by using force or threatening with a direct attack on life or body, or achieves the commission of the act with another person in such a manner, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.  
(3)	A teacher, educator, guardian, adopter, parent, priest, doctor or other person who, by abusing their position, has sexual intercourse or commits any other sexual act with a person who is not yet fifteen years old and who is entrusted to them for teaching, education, medical treatment, care or custody, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years.  

(4)	Whoever in the circumstances from the first, second and third paragraphs of this article otherwise violates the sexual integrity of a person who is not yet fifteen years old shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.  
(5)	The act from the first paragraph of this article is not unlawful if it was committed with a person of comparable age and if it corresponds to the level of their mental and physical maturity.  

Pridobivanje oseb, mlajših od petnajst let, za spolne namene

173.a	člen
(1)	Whoever solicits a person younger than fifteen years old via information or communication technologies for a meeting with the intention of committing against them a criminal offence from the first paragraph of Article 173 of this code or for the purpose of producing images, audiovisual or other materials of pornographic or other sexual content, and the solicitation is followed by concrete acts to realise the meeting, shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.  
(2)	The act from the preceding paragraph is not unlawful if it was committed for the purpose of realising the act from the first paragraph of Article 173 and under the conditions from the fifth paragraph of Article 173 of this code.  

Kršitev spolne nedotakljivost z zlorabo položaja

## 174. člen

(1)	Whoever abuses their position and thereby induces a person of the other or same sex who is subordinate to them or dependent on them to have sexual intercourse, or to commit or tolerate any other sexual act, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.  
(2)	A teacher, educator, guardian, adopter, parent or other person who, by abusing their position, has sexual intercourse or commits any other sexual act with a person over fifteen years old who is entrusted to them for teaching, education, care and custody, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Zloraba prostitucije

## 175. člen

(1)	Whoever for the purpose of exploitation participates in the prostitution of another person or whoever by force, threat or deception induces, procures or encourages another person to prostitution shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(2)	Whoever for the purpose of exploitation participates in the prostitution of a minor or resorts to the prostitution of a minor or whoever by force, threat, deception, recruitment or solicitation induces, procures or encourages a minor to prostitution shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(3)	If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this article were committed against several persons or within a criminal organisation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.  

Prikazovanje, izdelava, posest in posredovanje pornografskega gradiva

## 176. člen

(1)	Whoever sells, shows or by public display or in any other way makes accessible to a person younger than fifteen years old writings, images, audiovisual or other materials of pornographic content, or shows them a pornographic or other sexual performance, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to two years.  
(2)	Whoever by force, threat, deception, exceeding or abusing authority, recruitment, solicitation or for the purpose of exploitation induces, procures or encourages a minor for the production of images, audiovisual or other materials of pornographic or other sexual content, for participation in a pornographic or other sexual performance, or whoever knowingly attends such a performance, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.  
(3)	The same as in the preceding paragraph shall be punished whoever for themselves or for another procures, produces, distributes, sells, imports, exports or otherwise offers pornographic or other sexual material involving minors or their realistic images, or whoever possesses such material, obtains access or intentionally accesses such material by means of information or communication technologies, or discloses the identity of a minor in such material.  
(4)	If the act from the second or third paragraph of this article was committed in a criminal organisation for the commission of such criminal offences, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(5)	The act from the third paragraph of this article in the part that constitutes procuring, producing, possessing or obtaining access by means of information or communication technologies to pornographic or other sexual material is not unlawful if it was committed between minors of comparable age who consented to the act, it corresponds to the level of their mental and physical maturity and depicts such persons.  
(6)	Pornographic or other sexual material from the second, third and fourth paragraphs of this article shall be seized or its use appropriately prevented.  

Dvajseto poglavje
KAZNIVA DEJANJA ZOPER ČLOVEKOVO ZDRAVJE

Prenašanje nalezljivih bolezni

## 177. člen

(1)	Whoever does not comply with regulations or orders by which the competent authority orders examination, disinfection, isolation of patients or any other measures for the suppression or prevention of contagious diseases in humans and thereby causes a contagious disease to spread shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to one year.  
(2)	The same shall be punished whoever does not comply with regulations or orders by which the competent authorities prescribe measures for the suppression or prevention of infectious diseases in animals that can be transmitted to humans and thereby causes an infectious disease to be transmitted to humans.  

(3)	Whoever commits the act from the first or second paragraph of this article by negligence shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to six months.  
(4)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph by imprisonment up to eight years, and for the acts from the third paragraph by imprisonment up to five years.  

Opustitev zdravstvene pomoči

## 178. člen

(1)	A doctor or other health worker who, contrary to their professional duty, does not help a patient or someone else who is in danger to life shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.  
(2)	The act from the preceding paragraph is not unlawful if the doctor omits a certain method of treatment, intervention or medical procedure at the express written request of the patient or other person who is capable of deciding for themselves and refuses help even after being informed of the necessity of help and of the possible consequences of refusal and even after the doctor has again tried to persuade them to change their decision.  

Malomarno zdravljenje in opravljanje zdravilske dejavnosti

## 179. člen

(1)	A doctor who, in the performance of medical activity, acts negligently contrary to the rules of medical science and profession and thereby causes someone's health to significantly deteriorate shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.  
(2)	The same shall be punished
(a)	a health worker who, in their health activity, acts negligently contrary to the rules of the profession and thereby causes someone's health to significantly deteriorate or
(b)	a healer who, in the performance of permitted healing activity, negligently inappropriately selects and uses a healing system or healing method and thereby causes someone's health to significantly deteriorate.  
(3)	If due to the act from the first or second paragraph someone dies, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Mazaštvo

## 180. člen

(1)	Whoever engages in treatment or the practice of healing, even though they do not have the prescribed qualifications, and thereby dissuades a patient from timely seeking medical assistance, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.
(2)	The perpetrator of the act from the preceding paragraph who causes significant harm to the health of a person who did not consent or was not capable of consenting to the treatment procedures shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.

(3)	If the act from the first paragraph results in the death of the patient, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.
(4)	Instruments intended for or used in treatment under the first paragraph of this article shall be confiscated.

Unlawful transplantation of parts of the human body and alteration of the human genome

## 181. člen

(1)	Whoever, contrary to regulations in the field of procurement and transplantation of parts of the human body for the purpose of treatment, removes from a living or deceased person a part of the human body for transplantation or other purposes, or whoever transplants a part of the human body to someone contrary to these regulations, or whoever transplants or uses for another purpose a part of the human body which they know was removed contrary to regulations in the field of procurement and transplantation of parts of the human body for the purpose of treatment, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.
(2)	The penalty from the preceding paragraph shall be imposed on whoever unlawfully removes sex cells, blood or blood components, unlawfully handles them or violates the anonymity of the donor.
(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article is committed by a person abusing their position, or if the act was committed in a criminal association for the commission of such acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.
(4)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in particularly serious bodily injury or corresponding impairment of health of one or more persons, or if the act was committed against a minor or other vulnerable person, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.
(5)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.
(6)	Whoever attempts or carries out an intervention the purpose of which is to alter the human genome and which is not performed for preventive, diagnostic or therapeutic purposes, or whose aim is to introduce changes into the genome of descendants, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Trafficking in parts of the human body, sex cells, blood and blood components

181.a	člen
(1)	Whoever, in order to obtain payment or other pecuniary benefit for themselves or another, advertises the availability of or need for parts of the human body or solicits or recruits someone to be a donor or recipient of parts of the human body, or otherwise intermediates in the giving of body parts of a living or deceased person for transplantation, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.
(2)	The same as in the preceding paragraph shall be punished whoever, in order to obtain payment or other pecuniary benefit for themselves or another, advertises the availability of or need for sex cells, blood or blood components or solicits or recruits someone to be a donor or recipient of sex cells, blood or blood components, or otherwise intermediates in the giving of sex cells, blood or blood components.

(3)	The same as in the first paragraph of this article shall be punished whoever removes or obtains a removed part of the human body, sex cells, blood or blood components for which the donor receives payment or other pecuniary benefit, whoever unlawfully disposes of a removed part of the human body, sex cells, blood or blood components or whoever uses the human body, its parts, sex cells, blood or blood components with the intention of obtaining pecuniary benefit for themselves or another.
(4)	The penalty from the first paragraph of this article shall be imposed on whoever prepares, preserves, stores, transports, transfers, receives, imports or exports parts of the human body, sex cells, blood or blood components that were removed contrary to regulations in the field of procurement and transplantation of parts of the human body, sex cells, blood or blood components for the purpose of treatment.
(5)	If the act from the first, second, third or fourth paragraph of this article is committed by a person abusing their position, or if the act was committed in a criminal association for the commission of such acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.
(6)	If the act from the first, second, third or fifth paragraph of this article results in particularly serious bodily injury or corresponding impairment of health of one or more persons, or if the act was committed against a minor or other vulnerable person, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.
(7)	If the act from the first, second, third or fifth paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.

Negligent performance of pharmaceutical activity

## 182. člen

A pharmacist or other person authorised to dispense medicines who, through negligence, fails to prepare a medicine in the prescribed ratio or quantity or dispenses a different medicine or substance instead of the prescribed or requested medicine or otherwise acts contrary to the rules of science and profession in the preparation or dispensing of medicines and thereby causes a significant deterioration in someone's health, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Production and trade of harmful means for treatment

## 183. člen

(1)	Whoever manufactures, prepares, produces, sells or offers for sale or for the purpose of sale or placing on the market purchases or stores, or intermediates in the sale or purchase, imports or exports, brings in or takes out or in any other way places on the market medicines, medicinal substances, auxiliary substances, medical devices or their parts or accessories or other means of treatment that are harmful to health, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever acquires, prepares, produces, processes, sells or offers for sale or for the purpose of sale or placing on the market purchases or stores, or intermediates in the sale or purchase, imports or exports, brings in or takes out or in any other way places on the market infected blood or other tissue or a substance for treatment made therefrom.  
(3)	Whoever commits the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article through negligence shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  

(4)	If the act referred to in the first, second or third paragraph of this Article results in serious or particularly serious bodily injury or corresponding impairment of health of one or more persons, or if the act is committed by a person who has abused trust placed in them, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article by imprisonment from one to ten years, and for the act referred to in the preceding paragraph by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(5)	If the act referred to in the first, second or third paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in the first or second paragraph by imprisonment from two to fifteen years, and for the act referred to in the third paragraph by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(6)	Harmful medicines, medicinal substances, auxiliary substances, medical devices, their parts, accessories or other means of treatment, infected blood, other tissue or a substance for treatment made therefrom shall be seized.  

Manufacture and trade in counterfeit means of treatment, medicines without marketing authorisation, or medical devices that do not meet conformity requirements

183.a	člen
(1)	Whoever manufactures, prepares, produces, sells or offers for sale or for the purpose of sale or placing on the market purchases or stores, or intermediates in the sale or purchase, imports or exports, brings in or takes out or in any other way places on the market medicines, medicinal substances, auxiliary substances, medical devices or their parts or accessories or other means of treatment that are counterfeit, and a more serious criminal offence under this Code is not established, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever commits the act referred to in the preceding paragraph with medicines that do not have marketing authorisation, or with medical devices that do not meet conformity requirements.  
(3)	If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article is committed by a person who has abused trust placed in them, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.  
(4)	Medicines, medicinal substances, auxiliary substances, medical devices, their parts, accessories or other means of treatment referred to in the first and second paragraphs of this Article shall be seized.  

Manufacture and trade in foodstuffs and other products harmful to health

## 184. člen

(1)	Whoever manufactures, sells or in any other way places on the market foodstuffs that are harmful to human health, and thereby causes danger to the life or health of people, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever manufactures, sells or in any other way places on the market personal care products, children's toys or similar products for mass consumption that are harmful to health.  

(3)	Whoever commits the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article through negligence shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(4)	If the act referred to in the first, second or third paragraph of this Article results in serious or particularly serious bodily injury or corresponding impairment of health of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in the first or second paragraph by imprisonment of up to eight years, and for the act referred to in the third paragraph by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(5)	If the act referred to in the first, second or third paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in the first or second paragraph by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act referred to in the third paragraph by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(6)	Harmful foodstuffs and other products shall be seized.  

Unconscientious inspection of meat for consumption

## 185. člen

(1)	A veterinarian or other authorised person who acts unconscientiously in the inspection of slaughter animals or meat intended for consumption or fails to carry out the inspection despite regulations and thereby enables meat that is harmful to human health to be placed on the market, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph is committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  

Unauthorised manufacture and trade in illicit drugs, prohibited substances and methods in sport, and precursors for the manufacture of illicit drugs

## 186. člen

(1)	Whoever without authorisation manufactures, processes, sells or offers for sale or for the purpose of sale or placing on the market purchases, stores or transports or intermediates in the sale or purchase or in any other way without authorisation places on the market plants or substances that are classified as illicit drugs or prohibited substances in sport, or precursors used for the manufacture of illicit drugs, or agents or components used for methods that are classified as prohibited in sport, or methods that are classified as prohibited in sport, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(2)	Whoever sells, offers for sale or distributes free of charge an illicit drug, or a prohibited substance in sport, or a precursor for the manufacture of illicit drugs, or whoever sells, offers or carries out prohibited methods in sport to a minor, a mentally ill person, a person with a temporary mental disorder, severe mental retardation or a person undergoing withdrawal from addiction or rehabilitation, or if the act is committed in educational or training institutions or in their immediate vicinity, in prisons, in military units, in public premises or at public events, or the act referred to in the first paragraph is committed by a public official, priest, doctor, social worker, teacher or educator and in doing so exploits their position, or whoever uses minors to carry out the said act, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  

(3)	If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph is committed in a criminal organisation for carrying out such acts, or if the perpetrator of this act has organised a network of dealers or intermediaries, they shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.  
(4)	Whoever without authorisation manufactures, procures, possesses or makes available for use equipment, substances or precursors, knowing that they are intended for the manufacture of illicit drugs or prohibited substances in sport or for the use of prohibited methods in sport, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(5)	Illicit drugs, prohibited substances in sport, agents or components that enable the use of prohibited methods in sport, and means for their manufacture shall be seized. Means of transport used for the transport and storage of drugs, prohibited substances in sport or agents that enable the use of prohibited methods in sport shall be confiscated if they have specially adapted spaces for the transport and storage of drugs or prohibited substances and methods in sport, or if their owner knew or could have known that they would be used for such purpose.  

Enabling the consumption or use of illicit drugs or prohibited substances or methods in sport

## Article 187

(1) Whoever incites another person to use prohibited drugs or prohibited substances in sport or to use prohibited procedures in sport, or gives them to be used by that person or someone else, or whoever makes premises available for the use or application of prohibited drugs, prohibited substances or procedures in sport, or in any other way enables another person to use or apply prohibited drugs, prohibited substances or procedures in sport, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.  
(2) Whoever commits the act from the first paragraph against several persons, against a minor, a mentally ill person, a person with a temporary mental disorder, severe mental retardation or a person undergoing withdrawal treatment or rehabilitation, or if the act is committed in educational or training institutions or in their immediate vicinity, in prisons, in military units, in public premises or at public events, or if the act from the first paragraph is committed by a public official, priest, doctor, social worker, teacher or educator exploiting their position, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eleven years.  
(3) Prohibited drugs, prohibited substances in sport and accessories for their use, as well as means and components enabling the use of prohibited procedures in sport, shall be confiscated.  
(4) The act from the first and second paragraphs of this article is not unlawful if the perpetrator acts under a programme of addiction treatment or supervised drug use that is confirmed in accordance with the law and is carried out within or under the supervision of public health services.  

Twenty-first Chapter
CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST MARRIAGE, FAMILY AND CHILDREN

Bigamy

## Article 188

(1) Whoever enters into a new marriage although already married shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever enters into marriage with a person knowing that person to be married.  
(3) If the previous marriage has ceased or has been annulled, prosecution shall not be commenced, or if it has been commenced, it shall be discontinued.  

Alteration of family status

## Article 189

Whoever substitutes or exchanges a child or in any other way alters their family status shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Abduction of a minor

## Article 190

(1) Whoever unlawfully abducts a minor from a parent, adoptive parent, guardian, institution or person to whom the minor has been entrusted, or detains the minor and prevents the person entitled to the minor from having them, or whoever maliciously prevents the enforcement of an enforceable decision concerning a minor, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) Where the act has caused deterioration of the mental or physical health of the minor or has endangered their development, the punishment shall be imprisonment from one to five years.  
(3) If the court imposes a suspended sentence, it may order the perpetrator to hand over the minor to the person entitled or to enable the enforcement of the enforceable decision concerning the minor.  
(4) If the perpetrator of the act from the first paragraph of this article has voluntarily handed over the minor to the person entitled or has enabled the enforcement of the enforceable decision, the punishment may be remitted.  

Domestic violence

## Article 191

(1) Whoever in a family community treats another person badly, beats them or otherwise treats them painfully or humiliatingly, expels them from the common residence by threatening an immediate attack on life or body or restricts their freedom of movement, stalks them, forces them to work or to refrain from work, or in any other way by violently restricting their equal rights places them in a subordinate position within the community, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph in any other permanent cohabitation community.  

(3) If the act from the first paragraph has been committed against a person with whom the perpetrator lived in a family or other permanent community that has dissolved, but the act is connected with that community, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Neglect of a minor and cruel treatment

## Article 192

(1) A parent, guardian, foster parent or other person who seriously violates their duties towards a minor shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2) A parent, guardian, foster parent or other person who forces a minor to excessive work or to work not appropriate to their age or to refrain from work, or out of self-interest induces them to begging or other acts harmful to their development, or treats them cruelly or torments them, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  

Violation of family obligations

## Article 193

(1) Whoever seriously neglects family obligations which they have under the law and thereby leaves a family member who cannot take care of themselves in a difficult situation shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(2) If the court imposes a suspended sentence, it may order the perpetrator to regularly fulfil their duties of care, upbringing and maintenance.  

Non-payment of maintenance

## Article 194

(1) Whoever does not provide maintenance for a person whom they are obliged to maintain under the law and for whom the amount of their maintenance obligation has been determined by an enforcement title, although able to do so, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) If due to the act from the preceding paragraph the maintenance of the beneficiary is endangered or could be endangered, or if the perpetrator evades providing maintenance, the punishment shall be imprisonment of up to three years.  
(3) If the court imposes a suspended sentence, it may order the perpetrator of the act from the first or second paragraph of this article to regularly pay maintenance, and may also order them to settle maintenance arrears or other awarded obligations arising from maintenance.  

Incest

## Article 195

A person who has sexual intercourse with a minor blood relative in the direct line or with a minor brother or sister shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Twenty-second Chapter
CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST EMPLOYMENT RELATIONSHIP AND SOCIAL SECURITY

Violation of fundamental rights of workers

## Article 196

(1) Whoever does not act in accordance with regulations on the conclusion of employment contracts and on the termination of employment relationships, wages and other benefits from employment relationships, working time, breaks, rest, annual leave or absence from work, protection of women, youth and disabled persons, protection of workers due to pregnancy and parenthood, protection of older workers, prohibition of overtime or night work, or payment of prescribed contributions, and thereby deprives one or more workers or jobseekers of rights to which they are entitled or restricts those rights, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years and a fine.  
(2) If by the act from the preceding paragraph the rights of at least twenty workers have been violated, or if the rights of one or more workers have been violated over a period of at least two years, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years and a fine.  
(3) The punishment from the first paragraph of this article shall be imposed on whoever makes the conclusion of an employment contract conditional on a female worker not becoming pregnant or giving birth to a child during employment, or requires a statement from a female worker during employment that in such a case she will resign from the employment relationship or accept termination of the employment relationship by mutual agreement.  

Harassment in the workplace

## Article 197

(1) Whoever at the workplace or in connection with work, by sexual harassment, psychological violence, bullying or unequal treatment, causes humiliation or intimidation to another employee shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph results in psychological, psychosomatic or physical illness or reduction of work performance of the employee, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Violation of rights in employment or unemployment

## Article 198

(1) Whoever denies or restricts someone's right to free employment under conditions as determined by regulations shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever knowingly does not act in accordance with regulations on the rights of unemployed persons and thereby deprives an unemployed person of a right to which they are entitled or restricts that right.  

Illegal employment

## 199. člen

(1)	Whoever, contrary to regulations, employs two or more workers and fails to register them for appropriate insurance or employs two or more foreigners or stateless persons without appropriate work permits, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	Whoever consecutively or continuously or in substantial numbers employs foreigners who are not citizens of a member state of the European Union and are illegally residing on the territory of the Republic of Slovenia, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article was committed by employing workers who are not qualified to perform work with special authorisations or with the right to interfere with the physical or mental integrity of an individual or under particularly exploitative working conditions or by exploiting a victim of human trafficking or by employing a minor, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Violation of rights to participation in management and violation of trade union rights

## 200. člen

(1)	Whoever, by violating regulations or general acts, prevents or makes it impossible for workers to exercise their rights to participation in management or abuses these rights or obstructs their exercise, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever, by violating regulations or general acts, prevents or makes it impossible for workers to freely associate and engage in trade union activities or obstructs the exercise of trade union rights or takes over a trade union.  

Endangering safety at work

## 201. člen

(1)	Whoever destroys, damages or removes safety devices in a mine, factory, construction site or any other workplace and thereby causes danger to human life, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2)	A person who is responsible for safety and health at work in mines, factories, workshops, construction sites or other workplaces and does not install safety devices or does not ensure their proper condition or does not ensure their operation when necessary, or otherwise does not act in accordance with regulations or technical rules on safety measures and thereby causes danger to human life, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(4)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph of this article by imprisonment of up to five years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment of up to three years.  

(5)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph by imprisonment of one to twelve years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment of one to eight years.  

Violation of rights arising from social insurance

## 202. člen

Whoever does not act in accordance with regulations on social insurance and thereby deprives someone of a right to which they are entitled, or limits it, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  

Abuse of rights arising from social insurance

## 203. člen

(1)	Whoever feigns or causes themselves illness or incapacity for work and thereby achieves that a right is granted to them on the basis of social insurance to which they would not be entitled, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2)	Whoever submits false documents to the competent authority or makes a false statement and thereby deceives it into issuing a decision on a pension, disability benefit or monetary social supplement, even though the conditions for issuing such a decision are not met, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  

Triindvajseto poglavje
KAZNIVA DEJANJA ZOPER PREMOŽENJE

Tatvina

## 204. člen

(1)	Whoever takes someone else's movable property in order to unlawfully appropriate it, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2)	If the value of the stolen property is small and the perpetrator intended to appropriate property of such value, they shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(3)	Prosecution for the act from the previous paragraph shall be initiated upon proposal.  
(4)	If the perpetrator returned the stolen property to the injured party before learning that criminal proceedings had been instituted, the punishment may be remitted.  

Velika tatvina

## 205. člen

(1)	The perpetrator of theft from the first paragraph of the previous article shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years:
1)	if they committed the theft by breaking in, entering or otherwise overcoming major obstacles to enter a closed building, room, safe, cabinet or other closed spaces;  
2)	if the theft was committed by two or more persons who joined together for the purpose of stealing;  
3)	if they committed the theft in a particularly audacious manner;  
4)	if they had on them any weapon or dangerous tool for attack or defence;  
5)	if they committed the theft during a fire, flood or similar natural disaster;  
6)	if they committed the theft by exploiting the helplessness or misfortune of another.  
(2)	The perpetrator of theft shall be punished in the same manner if the stolen property is of special cultural significance or a natural value or if the stolen property is of great value and the perpetrator intended to appropriate such property or property of such value.  
(3)	If by the act from the first paragraph of this article property of special cultural significance or property of great value was obtained and the perpetrator intended to appropriate such property or property of such value or if the act from the second paragraph of this article was committed in a criminal organisation, they shall be punished by imprisonment of one to eight years.  

Rop

## 206. člen

(1)	Whoever takes someone else's movable property in order to unlawfully appropriate it by using force against a person or threatening them with an immediate attack on life or body, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to ten years.  
(2)	If the robbery was committed by two or more persons who joined together for the purpose of robbing, or if the value of the stolen property is great and the perpetrator intended to appropriate property of such value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of three to fifteen years.  
(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article was committed in a criminal organisation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of five to fifteen years.  

Roparska tatvina

## 207. člen

(1)	Whoever is caught in the act of theft and, in order to retain the stolen property, uses force against someone or threatens them with an immediate attack on life or body, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to ten years.  
(2)	If the value of the stolen property is great and the perpetrator intended to appropriate property of such value, they shall be punished by imprisonment of three to fifteen years.  

Zatajitev

## 208. člen

(1)	Whoever unlawfully appropriates someone else's movable property that has been entrusted to them, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(2)	If the value of the misappropriated property is small and the perpetrator intended to appropriate property of such value, they shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  
(3)	If the act from the first paragraph of this article is committed by a guardian, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(4)	If the misappropriated property is of special cultural significance or a natural value or if the misappropriated property is of great value and the perpetrator intended to appropriate such property or property of such value, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(5)	Whoever unlawfully appropriates someone else's movable property that they found or came upon by chance, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(6)	Prosecution for the act from the first, second and fifth paragraphs of this article shall be initiated upon proposal.  

Poneverba in neupravičena uporaba tujega premoženja

## 209. člen

(1)	Whoever unlawfully appropriates money, a movable thing or another part of another's property entrusted to him in connection with employment or in the performance of economic, financial or business activity or in the performance of the duties of a guardian, or left to him as an official person in service, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person against another's property accessible to him during a search of a dwelling, premises or persons, during enforcement in judicial or administrative proceedings, or in connection with tasks of protecting persons or property, he shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  
(3)	If the act from the first paragraph of this article concerns property of small value and the perpetrator intended to appropriate such value, he shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(4)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article concerns property of great value and the perpetrator intended to obtain such pecuniary benefit, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(5)	If the perpetrator unlawfully uses entrusted or accessible things from the first or second paragraph of this article, he shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to three years.  

Odvzem motornega vozila

## 210. člen

(1)	Whoever takes another's motor vehicle with the intention of unlawfully using it for driving shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2)	An attempt is punishable.  

(3)	If the perpetrator of the criminal offence from the first paragraph of this article destroys the motor vehicle, renders it unusable, or abandons it in an unknown place, he shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  

Goljufija

## 211. člen

(1)	Whoever, in order to obtain an unlawful pecuniary benefit for himself or another, induces someone into error by false representation or concealment of factual circumstances or leaves him in error and thereby induces him to do or omit something to the detriment of his own or another's property, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	Whoever, with the intention from the preceding paragraph of this article, in insurance upon conclusion of a contract provides false information or conceals important information, concludes prohibited double insurance or concludes an insurance contract after the insured event or loss event has already occurred, or falsely represents a loss event, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(3)	If fraud is committed by two or more persons who have joined together to commit fraud, or if the perpetrator by the act from the first paragraph of this article caused great pecuniary damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(4)	If the act from the first or third paragraph of this article was committed in a criminal organisation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(5)	If the act from the first paragraph of this article caused small pecuniary damage and the perpetrator intended to obtain small pecuniary benefit, he shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(6)	Whoever, in order to cause damage to another, induces someone into error by false representation or concealment of factual circumstances or leaves him in error and thereby induces him to do or omit something to the detriment of his own or another's property, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(7)	Prosecution for the act from the fifth and sixth paragraphs of this article shall be initiated upon a motion.  

Organiziranje denarnih verig, nedovoljeno prirejanje iger na srečo in nedovoljeno prirejanje rezultatov športnih tekmovanj

## 212. člen

(1)	Whoever organises, participates or assists in organising or carrying out money chains in which participants pay certain amounts of money to organisers or other participants who joined the game or activity before them and expect payment of certain amounts of money from participants who are to join such game or activity after them, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever, in order to obtain a greater unlawful pecuniary benefit for himself or another, organises, participates or assists in organising games of chance for which no permit or concession has been issued by the competent authority.  

(3)	The same punishment as in the first paragraph of this article shall apply to a person who, in order to obtain a pecuniary or any other benefit for himself or another, agrees to an unauthorised change of the result or course of an official sports competition or does or omits something for such a change.  
(4)	If by the acts from the first or third paragraph of this article he himself or another obtained a greater pecuniary benefit or caused another greater pecuniary damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  
(5)	If by the acts from the first, second or third paragraph of this article he himself or another obtained great pecuniary benefit or caused another great pecuniary damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Izsiljevanje

## 213. člen

(1)	Whoever, in order to obtain an unlawful pecuniary benefit for himself or another, by force or serious threat compels someone to do or omit something to the detriment of his own or another's property, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever, in order to obtain an unlawful pecuniary benefit for himself or another, threatens someone that he will disclose something about him or his relatives that would harm their honour or good name, and thereby compels him to do or omit something to the detriment of his own or another's property.  
(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article is committed by two or more persons or if it is committed using a weapon or dangerous tool or in a particularly brutal and humiliating manner, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(4)	If the act from the preceding paragraphs was committed in a criminal organisation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  

Oderuštvo

## 214. člen

Whoever accepts or secures for himself or another a manifestly disproportionate pecuniary benefit for a service he renders to someone, by exploiting his poor financial situation, severe housing conditions, distress, insufficient experience or recklessness, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years and by a fine.  

Izneverjenje

## 215. člen

(1)	Whoever represents the pecuniary interests of a person or manages his property and knowingly fails to fulfil his obligation to act in his interest or acts to his detriment, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  

(2)	If the perpetrator in committing the act from the preceding paragraph exploits the authorisation to obtain pecuniary benefit for himself or another, he shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(3)	If the act from the second paragraph of this article is committed by a lawyer, notary, guardian, enforcement agent or bankruptcy administrator, or if the perpetrator obtains great pecuniary benefit, he shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  

Zloraba izvršbe

## 216. člen

(1)	Whoever in enforcement unlawfully collects more than someone is obliged to pay, or appropriates an erroneously overpaid debt, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever in enforcement defrauds a creditor of his claim by agreement with auction participants.  
(3)	An attempt is punishable.  

Prikrivanje

## 217. člen

(1)	Whoever purchases, accepts as a pledge, otherwise acquires, conceals or disposes of movable or immovable property, knowing that it was obtained through a criminal offence, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2)	Whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph, but should and could have known that the property was obtained through a criminal offence, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article was committed by two or more persons who joined together for the purpose of concealment, or if the value of the property from the first or second paragraph of this Article is greater, or if the property is of special cultural significance or a natural asset, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment for up to three years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(4)	If the concealed property was obtained through a criminal offence for which the perpetrator is prosecuted on private charge or motion, the perpetrator of the acts from the first and second paragraphs of this Article shall be prosecuted on private charge or motion respectively.  
(5)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article was committed in a criminal organisation for the commission of criminal offences, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  

Nedovoljena posest in promet s stvarmi, ki so posebnega kulturnega pomena ali naravne vrednote

## 218. člen

(1)	Whoever, without permission from the competent authority, takes abroad or brings from abroad in contravention of international law a property that is of special cultural significance or a natural asset, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever places on the market or otherwise offers for sale property from the preceding paragraph, knowing that it was obtained unlawfully, or whoever unlawfully possesses property that is of special cultural significance or a natural asset.  
(3)	If the property from the first or second paragraph of this Article is of great or exceptional cultural significance, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  

Poškodovanje, odstranitev ali uničenje stvari, ki so posebnega kulturnega pomena ali naravne vrednote

## 219. člen

(1)	Whoever unlawfully damages, removes or destroys property that is of special cultural significance, a natural asset or other protected natural resource, or property that is a public good, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  
(2)	If the damaged or destroyed property is a cultural monument or natural asset of great or exceptional significance for the Republic of Slovenia, or if the damage caused is great, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to eight years.  

Poškodovanje tuje stvari

## 220. člen

(1)	Whoever damages, destroys or renders unusable another person's property shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2)	If the damage caused is great, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  
(3)	Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this Article shall be initiated on motion.  

Napad na informacijski sistem

## 221. člen

(1)	Whoever unlawfully enters or breaks into an information system, or whoever unlawfully intercepts data during non-public transmission to or from an information system, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2)	Whoever unlawfully uses, modifies, copies, transmits, destroys data in an information system, or unlawfully enters any data into an information system, obstructs transmission or access to data, or obstructs the operation of an information system, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  

(3)	If the act from the preceding paragraph, using the devices from the third paragraph of Article 306 of this Code, obstructed the operation or otherwise affected three or more information systems, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to four years.  
(4)	If the act from the second paragraph of this Article caused great damage, or if the act was committed in a criminal organisation, or against an information system of a critical infrastructure operator, or against an information system of an entity bound by the law governing information security, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  

Požig

## 222. člen

(1)	Whoever sets fire to another person's house or other building intended for habitation, farm building or business building, building or other immovable property in public use, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(2)	If the property from the preceding paragraph is owned by the person who set the fire, but acted out of malicious or other base motives, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  
(3)	If the act from the first paragraph of this Article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(4)	If the act from the first paragraph of this Article was committed by two or more persons, or if the property set on fire is of special cultural significance, a natural asset or of great value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  

Oškodovanje tujih pravic

## 223. člen

(1)	Whoever, in order to prevent someone from asserting a property right, alienates, destroys, damages or takes their own property on which that person has a pledge right or usufruct right, and thereby causes them damage, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever, in order to prevent payment to a creditor, during enforcement proceedings destroys, damages, alienates or conceals parts of their assets and thereby causes damage to the creditor.  
(3)	Prosecution for the act from the first and second paragraphs of this Article shall be initiated on motion.  

Pregon, kadar je storilec v bližnjem razmerju z oškodovancem

## 224. člen

For a criminal offence from Articles 204, 205, first, second, fourth and fifth paragraphs of 208, 210, 211, first and second paragraphs of 215 and 220 of this Code, committed against a spouse or a person with whom they live in an extramarital union, or a partner from a registered same-sex partnership, a blood relative in direct line, a brother or sister or another blood relative in collateral line up to and including the third degree, a relative by affinity up to and including the second degree, an adopter or adoptee, a foster parent or foster child, or against other persons with whom the perpetrator lives in a common household, prosecution shall be initiated on private charge.

Protipravno omejevanje konkurence

## 225. člen

(1)	Whoever, in the course of conducting economic activity, in contravention of regulations governing the protection of competition, violates the prohibition of restrictive agreements between undertakings, abuses the dominant position of one or more undertakings, or creates a prohibited concentration of undertakings, and thereby prevents, significantly impedes or distorts competition in the Republic of Slovenia or in the market of the European Union or a substantial part thereof, or significantly affects trade between Member States, resulting in great pecuniary benefit for that undertaking or those undertakings or great pecuniary damage to another undertaking, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2)	A perpetrator of the criminal offence of violation of the prohibition of restrictive agreements from the preceding paragraph, who reported the act before it was discovered or before learning that it was discovered and cooperated in its investigation and elimination of consequences, and did not coerce others to participate in the restriction of competition, nor coerce them to continue participating in it, may be exempted from punishment.  

Oškodovanje upnikov z goljufijo ali nevestnim poslovanjem

## 226. člen

(1)	Whoever knows that they themselves or another person as a debtor is insolvent, and prevents or reduces the possibility of settling obligations to creditors by:
1.	concealing, damaging, removing, fictitiously, gratuitously or for an unreasonably low price alienating or destroying assets or a part thereof;  
2.	concluding a false debt contract or acknowledging a false claim;  
3.	concealing, destroying, altering or keeping business books, documents or reports in such a way that the actual financial status, solvency or capital adequacy of the debtor cannot be determined in a timely or correct manner, or not keeping business books, documents or reports at all;  
4.	in another fraudulent manner fictitiously or actually worsening their own financial status or the financial status of another debtor,
shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2)	Whoever knows that they themselves or another person as a debtor is insolvent, and fictitiously or actually worsens their own financial status or the financial status of another debtor by:
1.	wastefully spending funds, disproportionately incurring debt, failing to timely secure and enforce claims or concluding speculative or harmful transactions;  
2.	without necessity transferring to other persons, encumbering or otherwise reducing the value of their own assets or assets or enterprise which they manage;  

3.	not fulfilling statutory obligations upon the occurrence of insolvency or violating fundamental rules in insolvency proceedings;  
4.	in some other way manifestly violating their duties in conducting economic activity or financial operations,
shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.  
(3)	If major property damage has resulted from the acts under the first or second paragraph of this Article, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Giving preference to creditors

## 227. člen

(1)	Whoever knows that they themselves or another person as a debtor is insolvent, and pays a debt or otherwise intentionally places a creditor in a more favourable position and thereby causes property damage to other creditors, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.  
(2)	If major property damage has been caused by the act under the preceding paragraph, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Commercial fraud

## 228. člen

(1)	Whoever in conducting economic activity in the conclusion or performance of a contract or transaction deceives another by representing that obligations will be fulfilled, or by concealing that obligations will not or will not be able to be fulfilled, and due to partial or complete non-fulfilment of obligations obtains a property benefit or property damage is caused to the other party or another person, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.  
(2)	If a major property benefit has been obtained or major property damage has been caused due to the act under the preceding paragraph, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(3)	If a minor property benefit has been obtained or minor property damage has been caused due to the act under the first paragraph of this Article, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to one year.  

Fraud to the detriment of the European Union

## 229. člen

(1)	Whoever avoids expenditure by using or submitting false, incorrect or incomplete statements or documents or by not disclosing information and thereby causes damage to the general budget of the European Union, a budget administered by the European Union, or a budget administered on behalf of the European Union, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever misappropriates, unlawfully withholds or improperly uses funds from the general budget of the European Union, a budget administered by the European Union, or a budget administered on behalf of the European Union.  

(3)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever obtains funds from the budgets under the first and second paragraphs of this Article by using or submitting false, incorrect or incomplete statements or documents or by not disclosing information.  
(4)	If a major property benefit has been obtained or major property damage has been caused by the act under the preceding paragraphs, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(5)	The punishments under the preceding paragraphs of this Article shall apply to managers of enterprises or other persons authorised to make decisions or exercise supervision in enterprises, if they enable or do not prevent criminal offences by perpetrators under the preceding paragraphs who are subordinate to them and act on behalf of the enterprise.  

Deception in obtaining and using loans or benefits

## 230. člen

(1)	Whoever for themselves or for another person obtains a loan, investment funds, a subsidy or any other benefit for conducting economic activity, although they do not fulfil the required conditions, by submitting to the lender or another person competent to grant or approve such a benefit false or incomplete information about financial status, balance sheets, profit or loss or other information relevant to the approval of the loan or benefit, or by concealing such information, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to three years.  
(2)	Whoever deceives the person who granted or approved the loan or benefit under the preceding paragraph regarding the purpose of use or conceals from them that they have used it for purposes other than those approved or agreed upon, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to two years.  

Deception in trading securities

## 231. člen

(1)	Whoever for the purpose of trading in shares, other securities or other financial instruments falsely represents the financial status, information about profit or loss or other information in a prospectus, in the publication of an annual report or in another manner that significantly affects their value, and thereby induces one or more persons to purchase, sell or otherwise transact with them, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to two years.  
(2)	If the act under the preceding paragraph concerned securities or other financial instruments of great value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.  

Deception of buyers

## 232. člen

(1)	Whoever, in order to deceive buyers, distributes on a larger scale products with a label containing information that does not correspond to the content, type, origin or quality of the goods, or distributes products that are not as heavy or of such quality as is normally assumed, or distributes

products without a label about the content, type, origin, quality or durability of the product, if such a label is prescribed, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to two years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever concludes contracts with false statements about the delivery deadline or manner of fulfilment of obligations as an essential component of the contract.  
(3)	Whoever, in order to deceive buyers or users of services, falsely announces that the price of goods has been reduced or that goods are being sold off or that prices will be increased or uses any other false advertisement, shall be punished by a fine.  

Unauthorised use of another's designation or design

## 233. člen

(1)	Whoever in economic operations unlawfully uses another's firm name, trademark, geographical indication or other special designation for goods or services or uses a substantial part of such designation as their own firm name, trademark or other sign for designating goods or services, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever in economic operations unlawfully uses
another's design.  
(3)	Objects under the first and second paragraphs of this Article and tools and devices used for their manufacture shall be confiscated.  

Unauthorised use of another's invention or topography

## 234. člen

(1)	Whoever in economic operations unlawfully uses another's invention protected by a patent or supplementary protection certificate, or a registered topography of a semiconductor product, or a new plant variety protected by a plant breeder's right, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.  
(2)	Products manufactured on the basis of unauthorised use under the preceding paragraph shall be
confiscated.  

Forgery or destruction of business documents

## 235. člen

(1)	Whoever enters false data into business books, files or other business documents and records that must be kept pursuant to law or on the basis of other regulations issued pursuant to law, and which are important for business transactions with other legal or natural persons or are intended for decisions in connection with economic or financial activity or as a basis for tax supervision, or fails to enter any important data or confirms with his signature such a book, document or file with false content or enables the compilation of a book, document or file with false content, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2)	Whoever uses a false business book, document or file as genuine or whoever destroys, conceals, considerably damages or otherwise renders unusable business books, documents or files from the preceding paragraph shall be punished in the same manner.
(3)	An attempt to commit the acts from the first and second paragraphs of this Article is punishable.

Disclosure and unauthorised acquisition of business secrets

## 236. člen

(1)	Whoever unlawfully, in breach of his duties regarding the protection of business secrets, communicates or hands over to someone data that constitute a business secret, or otherwise enables him to obtain them, or collects them with the intention of handing them over to an unauthorised person, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.
(2)	Whoever unlawfully obtains data that are protected as a business secret with the intention of using them unlawfully shall be punished in the same manner.
(3)	If the data from the first or second paragraph of this Article are particularly important, if someone hands over such data so that someone takes them abroad, or if the act was committed out of greed, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.
(4)	If the act from the first or third paragraph of this Article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.
(5)	Business secrets shall be deemed to be documents and data that have been declared by law, statute, rules or other general act or order of a competent authority or other authorised person to be an industrial, banking or other business secret and are so important that their disclosure has evidently caused or could cause more serious harmful consequences.

Abuse of an information system

## 237. člen

(1)	Whoever in economic operations unlawfully enters or breaks into an information system or unlawfully uses it by using, modifying, copying, transmitting, destroying or entering any data into the information system, obstructs the transmission of or access to data or obstructs the operation of the information system or unlawfully intercepts data during non-public transmission to an information system, in order to obtain for himself or someone else an unlawful pecuniary benefit or to cause pecuniary damage to another, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.
(2)	If a large pecuniary benefit was obtained or large pecuniary damage was caused by the act from the preceding paragraph and the perpetrator intended to obtain such pecuniary benefit for himself or someone else or to cause such pecuniary damage to another, or if it was committed in a criminal organisation or against an information system of a critical infrastructure operator or against an information system of an obliged entity under the law governing information security, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.

Abuse of inside information

## 238. člen

(1)	Whoever acquires inside information that could significantly affect the price of a security or other financial instrument admitted to trading on an organised market or other trading venue in the Republic of Slovenia or in at least one Member State of the European Union or for which an application for admission to trading on such a market has been made, regardless of whether the transaction was concluded on that market or outside it, in connection with his position with the issuer of the security or ownership share in the capital of the issuer of the security, his employment or in the performance of an activity, and exploits it for himself or for someone else for the direct or indirect acquisition or disposal of that security or other financial instrument or for the cancellation or amendment of an order to trade, if the order was placed before the person had inside information, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.
(2)	A person who communicates inside information to an unauthorised person or on the basis of inside information recommends to a third person the direct or indirect acquisition or disposal of that security or other financial instrument shall be punished in the same manner.
(3)	A person who obtains inside information without authorisation and exploits it for the direct or indirect acquisition or disposal of that security or other financial instrument for himself or for someone else shall be punished in the same manner as in the first paragraph.
(4)	A person who commits the act from the first paragraph of this Article with securities or other financial instruments on the over-the-counter market, if their price or value depends on or affects the price or value of financial instruments on the markets from the first paragraph of this Article, shall be punished in the same manner as in the first paragraph of this Article.
(5)	If the acts from the preceding paragraphs involved securities or other financial instruments of large value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Market abuse of financial instruments

## 239. člen

(1) Whoever, in order to obtain for himself or someone else an unlawful pecuniary benefit, abuses the market of financial instruments through prohibited conduct by:
1)	concluding a transaction, issuing an order to trade or otherwise acting in such a way as to give market participants a false or misleading impression regarding the supply, demand or price of a financial instrument or a related spot commodity contract, or thereby secures for one or more related persons the price of one or more financial instruments or related spot commodity contracts at an abnormal or artificial level;
2)	using fictitious means or other forms of fraudulent conduct when concluding a transaction or issuing an order to trade;
3)	disseminating false or misleading information about financial instruments or related spot commodity contracts, disseminating rumours and false and misleading news through the media, the internet or in another similar manner with the same objective;
4)	providing false or misleading information or providing false or misleading input data or otherwise acting in such a way as to manipulate the calculation of a benchmark,
shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.
(2) If a large pecuniary benefit was obtained or large pecuniary damage was caused by the act from the preceding paragraph and the perpetrator intended to obtain such pecuniary benefit for himself or someone else or to cause such pecuniary damage to another, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Abuse of position or trust in economic activity

## 240. člen

(1)	Whoever in the performance of economic activity abuses his position or trust given, exceeds the rights or omits the duties that he has on the basis of law, other regulation, act of a legal person or legal transaction regarding the disposal of another's property or benefits, their management or representation, and thereby obtains for himself or someone else an unlawful pecuniary benefit or causes pecuniary damage, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.
(2)	If a large pecuniary benefit was obtained or large pecuniary damage was caused by the act from the preceding paragraph and the perpetrator intended to obtain such pecuniary benefit for himself or someone else or to cause such pecuniary damage to another, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.
(3)	If the perpetrator obtained for himself or someone else a non-pecuniary benefit by the act from the first paragraph of this Article, he shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Unlawful acceptance of gifts

## 241. člen

(1)	Whoever, in the course of conducting business activity, for themselves or for another person, demands or accepts an unauthorised reward, gift or any other benefit or promise or offer of such benefit, in order to neglect the interests of their organisation or another natural person or to cause them damage for the purpose of obtaining or retaining business or other unauthorised benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to six years and a fine.  
(2)	The perpetrator of the act from the preceding paragraph who demands or accepts an unauthorised reward, gift or any other benefit or promise or offer of such benefit for themselves or for another person as a quid pro quo for obtaining or retaining business or other benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years and a fine.  
(3)	The perpetrator of the act from the first paragraph of this article who, after the conclusion of business or the performance of a service or the obtaining of another unauthorised benefit, for themselves or for another person, demands or accepts an unauthorised reward, gift or any other benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment up to four years and a fine.  
(4)	The accepted reward, gift or any other benefit shall be confiscated.  

Nedovoljeno dajanje daril

## 242. člen

(1)	Whoever promises, offers or gives an unauthorised reward, gift or any other benefit to a person conducting business activity, for them or for another person, in order to obtain for themselves or for another person any unjustified advantage in obtaining or retaining business or other unauthorised benefit from the first paragraph of Article 241, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to six years and a fine.  

(2)	Whoever promises, offers or gives an unauthorised reward, gift or any other benefit to a person conducting business activity, for them or for another person, as a quid pro quo for obtaining or retaining business or other benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment up to four years and a fine.  
(3)	The perpetrator from the preceding paragraphs who gave an unauthorised reward, gift or any other benefit upon request, but reported the act before it was discovered or before learning that it had been discovered, may be exempted from punishment if this does not conflict with the rules of international law.  
(4)	The given reward, gift or any other benefit shall be confiscated, and in the case from the preceding paragraph may be returned to the person who gave them.  

Ponarejanje denarja

## 243. člen

(1)	Whoever counterfeits money in order to put it into circulation as genuine, or whoever alters genuine money in order to put it into circulation, or whoever puts such counterfeit money into circulation, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever imports, exports, transports, receives or otherwise obtains counterfeit money in order to put it into circulation as genuine.  
(3)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever, using lawful devices or materials of the authority competent for issuing money, produces money in violation of the rights or conditions under which the competent authorities may issue money.  
(4)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article involves a large quantity or large property value of counterfeit money, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(5)	Whoever puts into circulation counterfeit money which they received as genuine, or whoever knows that money has been counterfeited or that such money has been put into circulation but fails to report it, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to six months.  
(6)	Counterfeit money shall be confiscated.  
(7)	Money is coined or paper money which is in circulation pursuant to law or is intended for circulation in the Republic of Slovenia or another state.  

Ponarejanje in uporaba ponarejenih vrednotnic ali vrednostnih papirjev

## 244. člen

(1)	Whoever counterfeits stamps or postage stamps or other securities or whoever alters any such genuine security in order to use it as genuine or give it for use to another person, or whoever uses counterfeit securities as genuine or obtains them for that purpose, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.  
(2)	Whoever counterfeits valuable papers or whoever alters any such genuine valuable paper in order to use it as genuine or give it for use to another person, or whoever uses such

counterfeit valuable papers as genuine or obtains them for that purpose, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(3)	If the act from the preceding paragraphs involves a large quantity of securities or valuable papers, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(4)	Whoever removes a stamp by which securities from the first paragraph of this article are cancelled, or whoever otherwise endeavours to make these securities appear as if they had not been used, or whoever reuses or sells such already used securities as if they were valid, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to one year.  
(5)	Counterfeit securities and counterfeit valuable papers shall be confiscated.  
(6)	For the purposes of this code, securities are stamps and other securities issued and in circulation pursuant to the law of the Republic of Slovenia, as well as foreign securities.  

Pranje denarja

## 245. člen

(1)	Whoever accepts, exchanges, stores, disposes of, uses in business activity or in any other manner specified by the law on the prevention of money laundering, money or property which they know was obtained through a criminal offence, thereby concealing or attempting to conceal its origin through laundering, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph and is simultaneously the perpetrator or participant in the criminal offence through which the money or property from the preceding paragraph was obtained.  
(3)	If the money or property from the first or second paragraph of this article is of large value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment up to eight years and a fine.  
(4)	If the act from the preceding paragraphs was committed in a criminal organisation for the execution of such acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine.  
(5)	Whoever should have and could have known that the money or property was obtained through a criminal offence, and commits the act from the first or third paragraph of this article, shall be punished by imprisonment up to two years.  
(6)	The money and property from the preceding paragraphs shall be confiscated.  

Zloraba negotovinskega plačilnega sredstva

## 246. člen

(1)	Whoever, by misusing a cheque, credit or payment card or other non-cash means of payment which they are authorised to use, obliges a bank or other issuer, contrary to the agreement on the use of that means, to pay an amount which they know is not covered by the account, and thereby obtains a property benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment up to two years.  
(2)	Whoever uses a cheque, credit or payment card or other non-cash means of payment in its original form, which they are not authorised to use and which was obtained in an unlawful

manner, and thereby obtains for themselves or for another person an unlawful property benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.  
(3)	Whoever, with the intention of unlawful use, possesses, purchases or otherwise obtains for themselves or for another person, transfers, imports, exports, sells or otherwise distributes an unlawfully obtained non-cash means of payment, shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.  
(4)	If a larger property benefit was obtained through the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.  
(5)	If a large property benefit was obtained through the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article or if it was committed in a criminal organisation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(6)	Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this article shall be initiated upon request.  

Uporaba ponarejenega negotovinskega plačilnega sredstva

## Article 247

(1) Whoever installs on a bank ATM or card payment terminal a device for copying data from payment, credit or other cards or other non-cash means of payment, or obtains such data for themselves or for another on the entire internet or in another manner, or counterfeits a non-cash means of payment, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(2) Whoever uses a counterfeit payment, credit or other card or other non-cash means of payment or their data and thereby obtains a pecuniary benefit shall be punished in the same manner.  
(3) Whoever, with the intent of unlawful use, possesses, purchases, transfers, imports, exports, sells or otherwise distributes a counterfeit non-cash means of payment or its data shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(4) If a large pecuniary benefit was obtained by the act referred to in the first, second or third paragraph of this Article, or if it was committed within a criminal organisation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of one to eight years.  

Production, acquisition and disposal of counterfeiting instruments

## Article 248

(1) Whoever produces, stores, transfers, acquires, sells or makes available for use instruments for counterfeiting money, securities, valuable papers or protective elements intended for protection against counterfeiting, or a device for copying the record of a credit, payment or other card or other non-cash means of payment, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(2) The instruments for counterfeiting shall be confiscated.  
(3) An attempt to commit the criminal offence referred to in the first paragraph of this Article is punishable.  

Tax evasion

## Article 249

(1) Whoever, by one or more acts, in order to completely or partially evade payment of taxes, contributions or other prescribed obligations of natural or legal persons, or to unlawfully obtain a full or partial tax refund in the Republic of Slovenia or other Member States of the European Union, provides false information about acquired income, expenses, objects, goods or other circumstances affecting the determination of taxes and other prescribed obligations, or otherwise deceives the authority competent for assessment or supervision of the calculation and payment of these obligations, and the total amount of outstanding obligations or obligations evaded, or the tax unlawfully refunded, regardless of the type of obligation or tax in a period of no more than twelve consecutive months, reaches a large pecuniary value, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to eight years.  
(2) Whoever, with the intent referred to in the preceding paragraph, fails to declare acquired income or other circumstances affecting the determination of taxes, contributions or other prescribed obligations of natural or legal persons once or several times, where declaration is mandatory, and the total amount of outstanding obligations or obligations evaded, regardless of the type of obligation in a period of no more than twelve consecutive months, reaches a large pecuniary value, shall be punished in the same manner.  
(3) Whoever, with the intent to prevent the determination of actual tax liability, upon request of the competent tax authority, fails to provide data, fails to maintain or submit business books and records that they are obliged to maintain, or the books and records are substantively incorrect, or fails to provide explanations in connection with the subject of tax supervision or obstructs tax supervision, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(4) If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article was committed within a criminal organisation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of three to twelve years.  

Smuggling

## Article 250

(1) Whoever transports goods of large pecuniary value across the customs line of the European Union while evading customs supervision measures, or transports them using force or threat, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years and a fine.  
(2) Whoever engages in transporting goods into the customs territory of the European Union while evading customs supervision measures, transports them within it, provides hiding places or storage, offers them or achieves their sale, where the goods together reach a greater pecuniary value, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to ten years and a fine.  
(3) An official person who, by abuse of official position or rights, enables the smuggling of goods into the customs territory of the European Union or their transport within it shall be punished with the penalty referred to in the preceding paragraph.  
(4) If the perpetrator, by the acts referred to in the second or third paragraph of this Article, obtains a large pecuniary benefit for themselves or another, or causes danger to human life or health, or supports terrorist activity, or commits such acts as a member of a criminal organisation, they shall be punished by imprisonment of three to fifteen years and a fine.  
(5) Whoever acquires or collects smuggled goods of large pecuniary value for transport into the customs territory of the European Union, procures counterfeit documents or transport to

and within the customs territory of the European Union, or otherwise organises the concealment, storage or sale of smuggled goods, shall be punished by imprisonment of three to twelve years and a fine.  
(6) The preceding paragraphs shall also apply to criminal offences committed abroad if the state in which they were committed has, like the Republic of Slovenia, accepted a common international legal obligation to prevent such criminal offences regardless of where they are committed, and has correspondingly defined the acts in its law as criminal offences in the same manner.  
(7) The value of goods under this Article shall be determined according to their market value in the territory of the Republic of Slovenia.  
(8) Smuggled goods shall be confiscated.  

Chapter Twenty-Five
CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST LEGAL TRANSACTIONS

Forgery of documents

## Article 251

(1) Whoever forges a document or alters a genuine document in order that such document be used as genuine, or whoever uses a forged or altered document as genuine, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(2) An attempt is punishable.  
(3) Whoever forges a public document, a will, a public or official book or any other book that must be maintained pursuant to law, alters such a genuine document, or whoever keeps such a forged or altered document for use or uses it as genuine, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Special cases of forgery of documents

## Article 252

(1) The following shall be punished for forgery of documents under the first paragraph, or if it concerns a document referred to in the third paragraph of the preceding Article, under the third paragraph of the preceding Article:
1) whoever unlawfully completes any paper, blank form or any other document signed by someone with any statement having significance for legal relations;  
2) whoever deceives someone about the content of any document and they sign it, believing that they are signing some other document or some other content;  
3) whoever issues a document in the name of any person without their authorisation or in the name of a person who does not exist;  
4) whoever, as the issuer of a document, adds to their signature any position or title, although they do not hold such position or title, and this substantially affects the evidentiary value of the document;  
5) whoever produces a document by unlawfully using a genuine seal or mark.  
(2) Whoever produces as commercial goods for another a doctoral dissertation, master's thesis, diploma thesis, examination paper, school-leaving examination paper or

seminar paper, or composes for another any other written assignment necessary for obtaining education, or whoever uses such an assignment as their own, shall be punished with the penalty referred to in the third paragraph of the preceding Article.  

Certification of false content

## Article 253

(1) Whoever misleads the competent authority or a notary and thereby achieves that they certify in a public document, record, book or business document anything false that is to serve as evidence in legal transactions shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2) Whoever uses a public document, record, book or business document referred to in the preceding paragraph, although they know it is false, shall also be punished in the same manner.  

Unlawful provision of legal assistance

## Article 254

(1) Whoever, without appropriate education or without appropriately fulfilled conditions, engages for payment in providing legal assistance in judicial or administrative proceedings shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) The acquired pecuniary benefit shall be confiscated.  

Issuance and use of false medical or veterinary certificate

## Article 255

(1) A physician who knowingly issues a false medical certificate or other medical attestation shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2) Anyone who knowingly uses a medical certificate or attestation from the preceding paragraph shall be punished in the same manner.  
(3) A veterinarian who knowingly issues a false veterinary certificate shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(4) Anyone who knowingly uses a certificate from the preceding paragraph shall be punished in the same manner as in the preceding paragraph.  

Forgery of marks for marking goods, measures and weights

## Article 256

(1) Anyone who, in order to use them as genuine, forges marks for marking domestic or foreign goods, such as seals, stamps, brands or other prescribed markings with which gold, silver, livestock, timber or any other goods are marked, or alters or removes genuine such

marks or uses forged marks as genuine, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2) Anyone who forges measures or weights or who uses them in measuring as genuine shall be punished in the same manner.  
(3) Anyone who unlawfully manufactures, acquires, sells or puts into use devices for producing marks for marking or designating goods as well as forged measures and weights shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(4) Forged marks, measures and weights as well as devices for their forgery shall be confiscated.  

Chapter Twenty-Six
CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST OFFICIAL DUTY, PUBLIC POWERS AND PUBLIC FUNDS

Abuse of official position or official rights

## Article 257

(1) An official person or public employee who abuses their position or exceeds the limits of official rights or fails to perform an official duty and thereby obtains for themselves or another any non-pecuniary benefit or causes damage to someone, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2) If the perpetrator by the act from the preceding paragraph causes greater damage or more seriously violates the rights of another, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(3) An official person or public employee who abuses their official position or exceeds the limits of official rights or fails to perform an official duty and thereby obtains for themselves or another an unlawful pecuniary benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(4) The perpetrator from the preceding paragraph who exploits their position or influence to unlawfully increase their own property of greater value shall be punished in the same manner as in the preceding paragraph.  
(5) If the perpetrator by the act from the third and fourth paragraphs obtained for themselves or another a large unlawful pecuniary benefit and intended to obtain such benefit, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Article 257a
Damaging public funds
(1) An official person, public employee or other authorised person of a user of public funds who, in procuring, acquiring, managing such funds or disposing of them, knowingly violates regulations, omits due supervision or otherwise causes or enables unlawful or improper use of public funds, although they foresee or should and could have foreseen that greater pecuniary damage to public funds may occur as a result, and such damage does occur, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years and by a fine.  
(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph resulted in large pecuniary damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years and by a fine.  

(3) A user of public funds under this article is a legal person under public law or its unit or a legal person under private law or a private individual, if they use such funds or at their expense perform public service or other activities in the public interest or provide public goods on the basis of a concession or other exclusive or special right.  
(4) Public funds under this article are immovable property, movable property, monetary funds, receivables, capital investments and other forms of financial assets of the state, self-governing local community, the European Union or other legal person under public law.  

Negligent conduct in service

## Article 258

An official person or public employee who knowingly violates laws or other regulations, omits their supervision or otherwise manifestly acts negligently in service, although they foresee or should and could have foreseen that a more serious violation of the rights of another or damage to a public good or pecuniary damage may occur as a result, and such violation or greater damage does occur, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to three years.  

Forgery or destruction of an official document, book, file or archival material

## Article 259

(1) An official person who enters false data in an official document, book or file or fails to enter any important data or with their signature or seal certifies an official document, book or file with false content or with their signature or official seal enables the composition of an official document, book or file with false content, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2) An official person who uses an official document, book or file with false content in service as genuine or who destroys, conceals, considerably damages or otherwise renders unusable an official document, book or file shall be punished in the same manner.  
(3) Anyone who unlawfully alienates, destroys or conceals archival material, or renders it unusable, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to three years.  

Disclosure of classified information

## Article 260

(1) An official person or other person who, contrary to their duties of protecting classified information, communicates or hands over classified information to someone or otherwise enables them to obtain it, or collects such information in order to hand it over to an unauthorised person, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2) Anyone who unlawfully obtains classified information in order to use it without authorisation, as well as anyone who publicly discloses such information without permission, shall be punished in the same manner.  
(3) A person who fulfils the elements of the criminal offence from the first paragraph of this article shall not be punished if it concerns classified information that reveals an unlawful interference with human rights or

fundamental freedoms, other constitutional or statutory rights, serious abuses of authority or powers or other serious irregularities in the exercise of authority, public powers or performance of public service, and the act was not committed out of self-interest and does not endanger people's lives or have more serious or irreparable harmful consequences for the security or legally protected interests of the Republic of Slovenia.  
(4) Notwithstanding the provision of the second paragraph of this article, anyone who publicly discloses, acquires, transmits or possesses classified information with the intention of disclosure to the public shall not be punished if, according to the circumstances of the case, the public interest in disclosing the classified information prevails over the public interest in maintaining its secrecy, and if the act does not directly endanger the life of one or more persons.  
(5) If the act from the first paragraph of this article was committed out of self-interest or if the disclosure directly endangered people's lives or if the disclosure had more serious or irreparable harmful consequences for the security or legally protected interests of the Republic of Slovenia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to eight years.  
(6) If the act from the first paragraph of this article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  

Accepting a bribe

## 261. člen

(1)	An official or public employee who, for themselves or for another, demands or accepts a reward, gift or any other benefit or a promise or offer of such a benefit, in order to perform, within the scope of their official powers, an official act which they should not perform, or in order not to perform an act which they should or could perform, or to otherwise abuse their position, or whoever mediates in such bribery, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years and a fine.  
(2)	An official or public employee who, for themselves or for another, demands or accepts a reward, gift or any other benefit or a promise or offer of such a benefit, in order to perform, within the scope of their official powers, an official act which they should or could perform in any case, or in order not to perform an act which they should not perform in any case, or to otherwise use their position, or whoever mediates in such bribery of an official, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine.  
(3)	An official or public employee who, after performing or not performing an official act referred to in the preceding paragraphs, demands or accepts in connection therewith a reward, gift or any other benefit, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to four years and a fine.  
(4)	The accepted reward, gift and any other benefit shall be confiscated.  

Dajanje podkupnine

## 262. člen

(1)	Whoever promises, offers or gives to an official or public employee a reward, gift or any other benefit for them or for another, in order that they perform, within the scope of their official powers, an official act which they should not perform, or in order that they not perform an act which they

should or could perform, or in order that they otherwise abuse their position, or whoever mediates in such bribery, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to six years and a fine.  
(2)	Whoever promises, offers or gives to an official or public employee a reward, gift or any other benefit for them or for another, in order that they perform, within the scope of their official powers, an official act which they should or could perform in any case, or in order that they not perform an act which they should not perform in any case, or in order that they otherwise use their position, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to four years and a fine.  
(3)	The perpetrator of the criminal offence referred to in the preceding paragraphs who gave a reward, gift or any other benefit at the request of an official or public employee, but reported the act before it was discovered or before they learned that it was discovered, may be exempted from punishment if this does not conflict with the rules of international law.  

Sprejemanje koristi za nezakonito posredovanje

## 263. člen

(1)	Whoever, for themselves or for another, demands or accepts a reward, gift or any other benefit or a promise or offer of such a benefit, in order to exploit their position or their real or supposed influence and mediate so that some official act is performed or not performed, shall be punished by imprisonment up to four years and a fine.  
(2)	Whoever exploits their position or their real or supposed influence and mediates in order that an official act which should not be performed is performed, or in order that an official act which should or could be performed is not performed, shall be punished in the same manner.  
(3)	If the perpetrator, for mediation referred to in the preceding paragraph, before or after the mediation, accepts for themselves or for another a reward, gift or any other benefit, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to six years and a fine.  
(4)	The accepted reward, gift or any other benefit shall be confiscated.  

Dajanje daril za nezakonito posredovanje

## 264. člen

(1)	Whoever promises, offers or gives to another a reward, gift or any other benefit for them or for another, in order that they exploit their position or their real or supposed influence and mediate so that some official act is performed or not performed, shall be punished by imprisonment up to four years and a fine.  
(2)	Whoever promises, offers or gives to another a reward, gift or any other benefit for them or for another, in order that they exploit their position or their real or supposed influence and mediate so that an official act which should not be performed is performed, or so that an official act which should or could be performed is not performed, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to six years and a fine.  
(3)	The perpetrator of the criminal offence referred to in the preceding paragraphs who gave a reward, gift or any other benefit at the request of the person who mediated unlawfully, but reported the act before it was discovered or before they learned that it was discovered, may be exempted from punishment.

## 265. člen (črtan)

Kršitev človeškega dostojanstva z zlorabo uradnega položaja ali uradnih pravic

## 266. člen

An official who, in the performance of their duties, by abusing their official position or official powers, treats someone badly, insults them, may physically injure them or generally treats them in such a way as to violate their human dignity, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.  

Izsiljevanje izjave

## 267. člen

(1)	An official who, in the performance of their duties or public powers, uses force, threat or any other unlawful means or unlawful manner in order to extort from an accused, witness, expert or anyone else a testimony or any other statement, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(2)	If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph was committed with serious violence or if the perpetrator, by extorting testimony, causes serious consequences for the accused in criminal proceedings, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Protipravna prilastitev stvari ob preiskavi ali izvršbi

## 268. člen

(1)	An official who, during a search of premises, spaces or persons or during enforcement in judicial or administrative proceedings, takes an item in order to unlawfully appropriate it, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2)	If the item is of great value and the perpetrator intended to appropriate an item of such value, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Sedemindvajseto poglavje
KAZNIVA DEJANJA ZOPER VOJAŠKO SLUŽBO

Neizpolnitev ukaza in odrekanje poslušnosti

## 269. člen

(1)	A military person who does not carry out an order of a superior military person in connection with service or refuses obedience to them, and thereby creates a danger to human life or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment up to two years.  

(2)	A military person who resists a guard, watch, patrol, duty officer or other military person when, in the performance of their military duty, they are ensuring safety and order in a military unit, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to one year.  

Upiranje nadrejenemu

## 270. člen

(1)	A military person who, together with other military persons, resists an order of a superior military person in connection with service or refuses to carry it out or refuses them in order to perform their duty, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(2)	A military person who, in committing the act referred to in the preceding paragraph, uses a weapon, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(3)	The punishment referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be imposed on whoever organised the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article, as well as on a military superior who participated in such an act.  

Odklonitev sprejema ali uporabe orožja

## 271. člen

A military person who, contrary to regulations and without justified reason, refuses to accept a weapon or refuses to use it according to an order or the rules of service, shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.  

Lažna ustna poročanja in lažna poročila

## 272. člen

A military person who, in the performance of service, makes a false oral report or gives a false report or conceals therein any fact which they should not conceal, and thereby endangers human life or property of great value, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to one year.  

Samovoljna odstranitev in beg iz vojske

## 273. člen

(1)	A military person who arbitrarily leaves their unit or service during the performance of an important task or during heightened combat readiness of the unit shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2)	A military person who knows of the circumstances from the first paragraph of this article but does not arbitrarily return from authorised absence from the unit or service, and a military person who arbitrarily leaves their unit or service for more than ten days, shall be punished in the same manner.  

(3)	A military person who goes into hiding in order to avoid military service shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(4)	A military person who leaves the country and remains abroad in order to avoid military service shall be punished by the penalty from the preceding paragraph.  

Cruel treatment of a subordinate

## 274. člen

(1)	A superior military person who treats a subordinate cruelly in service or in connection with service, or treats them in a manner that insults their human dignity, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	If a superior military person commits the act from the preceding paragraph against several persons, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  

Violation of regulations on guard duty

## 275. člen

(1)	A military person who does not act in accordance with regulations on guard duty, patrol, duty service or other service for maintaining security and order in a military unit, command or institution or for military protection of facilities or areas and thereby causes danger to human life or property of great value shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(3)	If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this article result in serious bodily injury to one or more persons or great material damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to five years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(4)	If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this article result in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Failure to take measures for the safety of people and property in a military unit

## 276. člen

(1)	A commanding military person who does not take prescribed or ordered measures for the safety of the life and health of people entrusted to them, for the protection and proper maintenance of facilities, objects and means for combat readiness, for the necessary supply of the unit entrusted to them with food, equipment or means, or does not take action that must be taken to ensure that protective works are carried out in a timely and proper manner and thereby endangers human life or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  

(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(3)	If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this article result in serious bodily injury to one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to five years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(4)	If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this article result in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Failure to provide protection during military exercises

## 277. člen

(1)	A military person who does not take prescribed or ordered safety or precautionary measures during an exercise, instruction or trials and thereby endangers human life or property of great value shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(3)	If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this article result in serious bodily injury to one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to five years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(4)	If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this article result in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment from one to eight years.  

Liability for a criminal offence committed on the order of a superior

## 278. člen

A subordinate shall not be punished if they commit a criminal offence on the order or command of a superior military person and this order or command relates to military duty, except where it concerns a war crime or some other serious criminal offence, or if they knew that the execution of the order or command constitutes a criminal offence.  

Milder punishment due to provocation

## 279. člen

If the perpetrator of a criminal offence under Article 269, the first paragraph of Article 270 or the first paragraph of Article 273 of this code was provoked by unlawful or cruel treatment by a military person, they may be punished more mildly or the penalty may be remitted.  

Twenty-eighth chapter CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST THE ADMINISTRATION OF JUSTICE

Failure to report that a criminal offence is being prepared

## 280. člen

(1)	Whoever knows that a criminal offence is being prepared for which the law prescribes a penalty of three or more years' imprisonment, but does not report it at a time when it could still be prevented and the act was attempted or completed, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph concerned a criminal offence for which the law prescribes a penalty of at least fifteen years' imprisonment or life imprisonment, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(3)	Whoever does not report that a criminal offence from the first paragraph of this article is being prepared shall not be punished if the perpetrator is a spouse, a person with whom they live in an extramarital union or registered same-sex partnership, a blood relative in direct line, a brother or sister, an adopter or adoptee. If any of the persons specified in this paragraph is not punished for failure to report under the first paragraph of this article, neither their spouse nor the person with whom they live in an extramarital union nor the partner from a registered same-sex partnership shall be punished for failure to report.  

Failure to report a criminal offence or perpetrator

## 281. člen

(1)	Whoever knows of the perpetrator of a criminal offence for which the law prescribes a penalty of at least fifteen years' imprisonment or life imprisonment, or whoever merely knows that such an act was committed, but does not report it, even though such a report is necessary for the perpetrator or the act to be discovered in time, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	An official person who knowingly fails to report a criminal offence of which they become aware in the performance of their service, if the law prescribes a penalty of three or more years' imprisonment for it and the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(3)	Whoever fails to report shall not be punished if the perpetrator is a spouse, a person with whom they live in an extramarital union or registered same-sex partnership, a blood relative in direct line, a brother or sister, an adopter or adoptee, or if they are the perpetrator's defence counsel, doctor or confessor. Since any of the persons from this paragraph except a defence counsel, doctor or confessor is not punished for failure to report under the first paragraph of this article, neither their spouse nor the person with whom they live in an extramarital union nor the partner from a registered same-sex partnership shall be punished for failure to report.  

Assisting the perpetrator after commission of a criminal offence

## 282. člen

(1)	Whoever conceals the perpetrator of a criminal offence prosecuted ex officio, or helps him by hiding tools, traces or in any other way so that he is not discovered, or whoever conceals a convicted person or does anything else to prevent the execution of a sentence or imposed security measure or to prevent the application of an educational measure of placement in an educational institution or re-education home, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  

(2)	If it concerns assistance to the perpetrator of a criminal offence under the preceding paragraph, for which a sentence of imprisonment of at least fifteen years or life imprisonment is prescribed, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  
(3)	The penalty for the act under the first paragraph of this article may not be more severe, either in type or in degree, than the penalty prescribed for the criminal offence of the person whom the perpetrator helped.  
(4)	The spouse of the perpetrator, a person living with him in an extramarital community or in a registered same-sex partnership, or a blood relative in direct line, brother or sister, adopter or adoptee shall not be punished for the act under the first or second paragraph of this article. Since one of the persons under this paragraph is not punished for the criminal offence under the first or second paragraph, neither his spouse or person living with him in an extramarital community nor the partner from a registered same-sex partnership shall be punished for assisting the perpetrator in committing the criminal offence under the first or second paragraph of this article.  
(5)	If the prosecution of the perpetrator of a criminal offence is initiated upon a motion, the perpetrator of the criminal offence under the first paragraph of this article shall also be prosecuted upon a motion.  

Kriva ovadba

## 283. člen

(1)	Whoever reports someone as having committed a criminal offence prosecuted ex officio, knowing that he did not commit it, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever plants traces of a criminal offence or in any other way causes criminal proceedings to be instituted for a criminal offence for which the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio, against someone whom he knows did not commit it.  
(3)	Whoever reports himself as having committed a criminal offence prosecuted ex officio, even though he did not commit it, shall be punished by a fine.  
(4)	The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall also apply to whoever reports that a criminal offence prosecuted ex officio has been committed, even though he knows that no such act was committed, and thereby causes state authorities to take action.  
(5)	If an official person commits the act under the first or second paragraph of this article by abusing his official position, he shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Whoever escapes from prison or detention by force or threat to directly attack life or body, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Upor oseb, ki jim je vzeta prostost

(2) The perpetrator of the act from the preceding paragraph who has used force or threat shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  

Enabling escape of a person deprived of liberty

## Article 293

(1) Whoever by force, threat, deception or in any other way enables the escape of someone serving a prison sentence, or someone who is detained, or who is undergoing a security measure of mandatory psychiatric treatment and care in a health institution, or a juvenile who is in a correctional home, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed in an organized group, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  

Chapter Twenty-Nine
CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST PUBLIC ORDER AND PEACE

Criminal association

## Article 294

(1) Whoever participates in a criminal association that has the purpose of committing a criminal offence for which a sentence of more than three years' imprisonment or life imprisonment may be imposed, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.  
(2) Whoever establishes or leads an association from the preceding paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.  
(3) The perpetrator of a criminal offence from the preceding paragraphs who prevents the further commission of these acts or discloses information relevant to the investigation and proving of criminal offences already committed, shall have the sentence for these acts mitigated in accordance with Article 51 of this Code.  

Agreement to commit a criminal offence

## Article 295

Whoever agrees with someone to commit a criminal offence for which a sentence of five years' imprisonment or a more severe sentence may be imposed, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  

Violent behaviour

## Article 296

(1) Whoever treats another person rudely, beats them or otherwise painfully or humiliatingly punishes them, by force or threat of immediate attack on life or body drives them away or deprives them of freedom of movement, compels them to work or to refrain from work or in any other way by violently restricting their equal rights brings them into a subordinate position, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by two or more persons or if several persons have been seriously humiliated or if the perpetrator can easily cause bodily injury to another, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(3) The same as in the preceding paragraph shall be punished whoever causes violence or threatens the safety of others at sporting events or in connection with these events.  

Public incitement to hatred, violence or intolerance

## Article 297

(1) Whoever publicly incites or stirs up hatred, violence or intolerance based on national, racial, religious or ethnic affiliation, sex, skin colour, origin, property status, education, social position, political or other conviction, disability, sexual orientation or any other personal circumstance, and the act is committed in a manner that can endanger or disturb public order and peace, or by using threats, insults or affronts, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2) The same shall be punished whoever in the manner from the preceding paragraph publicly spreads ideas about the superiority of one race over another or provides any assistance in racist activity or denies, diminishes the significance of, approves, justifies, ridicules or advocates genocide, the holocaust, crimes against humanity, war crimes, aggression or other criminal offences against humanity as defined in the legal order of the Republic of Slovenia.  
(3) If the act from the preceding paragraphs is committed by publication in the mass media or on websites, the penalty from the first or second paragraph of this Article shall also be imposed on the responsible editor or the person who replaced them, unless it was a live broadcast that they could not prevent or a publication on websites that enable users to publish content in real time or without prior supervision.  
(4) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article is committed by coercion, rude treatment, endangering safety, defamation of ethnic, national or religious symbols, damage to other people's property, desecration of monuments, memorials or graves, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(5) If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this Article are committed by an official person by abuse of official position or rights, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  

(6) Means and objects with messages from the first and second paragraphs of this Article, as well as accessories intended for their production, reproduction and distribution, shall be seized or their use appropriately prevented.  

Participation in a group that commits a criminal offence

## Article 298

(1) Whoever participates in a group that by joint action commits some violence against people, destroys or damages property of greater value or attempts to commit such a criminal offence, shall be punished for mere participation by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2) If in the action of the group from the preceding paragraph someone was killed or seriously bodily injured, the participant of the group shall be punished for mere participation by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(3) The leader of a group that has committed an act from the first or second paragraph of this Article shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  

Prevention of an official act or revenge against an official person

## Article 299

(1) Whoever by force or threat of immediate use of force prevents or attempts to prevent an official person from performing an official act that they intended to perform within the scope of their rights, or in the same manner compels them to perform an official act, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to two years.  
(2) If the perpetrator in committing the act from the first paragraph of this Article seriously insults an official person, treats them rudely or inflicts a minor bodily injury on them or threatens them with the use of a weapon or dangerous object or means, they shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(3) Whoever commits an act from the first or second paragraph of this Article against an official person when they are performing police or state security tasks or pursuing the perpetrator of a criminal offence or guarding someone deprived of their liberty or performing acts of criminal prosecution, acts of administrative inspection supervision, conducting an investigation or adjudicating in criminal proceedings, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.  
(4) The same as in the preceding paragraph shall be punished whoever takes revenge on an official person who performs or has performed acts in misdemeanour proceedings or criminal prosecution, police tasks, acts of administrative inspection supervision, conducts or has conducted an investigation, or adjudicates or has adjudicated in criminal proceedings, because of acts that they or another official person performed within the scope of their rights, in such a way that the life, body, personal safety or property of the official person or their close relatives is endangered.  
(5) If the perpetrator of an act from the first to fourth paragraphs of this Article is provoked by unlawful conduct of an official person, they shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to six months, but the sentence may be remitted.  

Attack on an official person when performing security tasks

## Article 300

(1) Whoever attacks or seriously threatens to attack an official person or another person whom they know is assisting an official person when they are performing police or state security tasks or protecting public order, or a person performing tasks related to the execution of criminal sanctions, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to three years.  
(2) If the perpetrator in committing the act from the preceding paragraph threatens an official person or another person whom they know is assisting them with a weapon or dangerous object or means or treats them rudely or inflicts a minor bodily injury on them, they shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(3) The penalty from the preceding paragraph shall be imposed on whoever commits an act from the first paragraph of this Article against two or more persons.  

Participation in a group that prevents an official person from performing an official act

## Article 301

(1) Whoever participates in a group which by joint action prevents or attempts to prevent an official person from performing an official act or in the same manner compels them to perform an official act, shall be punished for participation alone by imprisonment from three months to two years.  
(2) The leader of a group which has committed the act from the preceding paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to three years.  

Incitement to resistance

## Article 302

(1) Whoever incites others to violent resistance to lawful decisions or measures of state authorities or against an official person performing an official act shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) If due to the act from the preceding paragraph a decision or measure of a state authority was not executed or its execution was greatly impeded or if this act was committed by the leader of a group, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  

Removal or damage of an official seal or sign

## Article 303

Whoever removes or damages an official seal or sign which an authorised official person has used to secure objects or premises, or whoever enters such premises without removing or damaging the seal or sign, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.  

Taking or destruction of an official seal or official documents

## Article 304

(1) Whoever unlawfully takes, conceals, destroys or damages or otherwise renders unusable an official seal, official book, file or document which belongs to or is held by a state authority, commercial company, other legal person or individual exercising public powers, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(2) Attempt is punishable.  

False representation as an official or military person

## Article 305

(1) Whoever, in order to obtain some benefit for themselves or another or to cause damage to someone, falsely represents themselves as an official or military person or unlawfully wears the insignia of an official or military person, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.  
(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever performs an act which only an official or military person is authorised to perform.  

Manufacturing and obtaining weapons and devices intended for a criminal offence

## Article 306

(1) Whoever manufactures or obtains or stores weapons, explosives or devices with which they can be made, or poisons, knowing that they are intended for a criminal offence, or enables someone to obtain them, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2) Whoever makes or transfers to someone a counterfeit key, lock pick or any other device for break-in, knowing that it is intended for a criminal offence, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.  
(3) The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall apply to whoever, with the intention of committing a criminal offence, possesses, manufactures, sells, makes available for use, imports, exports or otherwise provides devices for intrusion, unauthorised access or interference with an information system or part thereof or for unauthorised interference with data in an information system or for their interception.  

Unlawful production and trafficking of weapons or explosives

## Article 307

(1) Whoever unlawfully assembles, manufactures, offers, sells, exchanges, delivers, imports, exports, brings into the country or takes out of it firearms, chemical, biological or nuclear weapons, ammunition or explosives or military weapons and military equipment, the trafficking, acquisition and possession of which by individuals, legal persons and entrepreneurs are not permitted or are restricted, or acts as an intermediary therein, or whoever unlawfully acquires or stores such weapons, ammunition or explosives, except for firearms or ammunition for which a firearms permit may be issued, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  

(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph involves weapons, ammunition or explosive substances or any other combat means in a large quantity or value or poses a danger or if the act was committed in a criminal organisation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  
(3) If the act from the first paragraph of this Article involves an individual firearm or a small quantity of ammunition for such a weapon or if the perpetrator acquires or stores firearms or ammunition for which a firearms permit may be issued with the intention of unlawful sale, or if they store it in a large quantity or value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(4) The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall apply to whoever counterfeits or unlawfully destroys, removes or alters markings on firearms.  
(5) Whoever unlawfully manufactures, obtains, offers, sells, exchanges, sends, delivers, imports, exports, brings into the country or takes out of it component or spare parts of weapons, ammunition, explosives, explosive devices and explosive weapons or military weapons and military equipment, substances, ingredients, software or technology, knowing that they will be used for the production or operation of objects from the preceding paragraphs, stores them for this purpose or acts as an intermediary therein, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.  

Prohibited crossing of the border or territory of the state

## Article 308

(1) Whoever forcibly crosses the state border of the Republic of Slovenia or enters its territory armed and unlawfully shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to three years or by a fine.  
(2) The same punishment shall apply to a foreigner who does not have a residence permit in the Republic of Slovenia if they remain on its territory in the manner from the preceding paragraph or resist lawful removal from it.  
(3) Whoever engages in unlawfully bringing foreigners who do not have permission to enter the Republic of Slovenia or reside in it onto its territory, transporting them across it or helping them to hide or whoever unlawfully brings one or more such foreigners across the border or territory of the state, transports them across it or enables unlawful residence on it with the intention of obtaining material benefit for themselves or for someone else, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years and a fine.  
(4) The punishment from the preceding paragraph shall apply to an official person who enables a foreigner unlawful entry onto the territory of the Republic of Slovenia or unlawful residence on it.  
(5) Whoever recruits or gathers people for unlawful migration, procures forged documents or transport for them or otherwise organises unlawful migration or organises migration by deceiving the competent authorities regarding the true purpose of entry onto the territory of the Republic of Slovenia shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years and a fine.  
(6) If the perpetrator by acts from the third, fourth or fifth paragraph of this Article obtains for themselves or someone else a disproportionate material benefit or procures unlawful labour or exposes someone to danger to life or health or commits such acts as a member of a criminal organisation, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years and a fine.  
(7) The preceding paragraphs shall also apply to criminal offences committed abroad if the state in which they were committed has, like the Republic of Slovenia, accepted a common

international legal obligation to prevent such criminal offences regardless of where they are committed, and has correspondingly defined the acts in its law as criminal offences in the same manner. If the criminal offence was committed in the territory of the European Union, nationals of its member states shall not be considered foreigners for the purposes of the second, third, fourth and fifth paragraphs of this Article.  
(8) A means of transport used for transporting one or more persons who are crossing the border or territory of the state in a prohibited manner shall be confiscated if it is the perpetrator's property. If the means of transport is not the perpetrator's property, in addition to the cases from the second paragraph of Article 73 of this Code, it shall also be confiscated if it is specially adapted, modified or adjusted for transporting such persons, or if the owner or transferor of the means of transport knew or could have known that the vehicle would be used for such a purpose.  

Misuse of signs for assistance and danger

## 309. člen

(1)	Whoever abuses a sign for help or a sign for danger or unjustifiably calls for help or falsely reports that danger is imminent and thereby causes state authorities or other competent organisations to take action without necessity or to use the means of the protection, rescue and assistance system without justification, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph prevented or obstructed the work of judicial or other state authorities and thereby prevented the performance of an official act or caused major property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  

Samovoljnost

## 310. člen

(1)	Whoever arbitrarily takes his own right or a right which he believes belongs to him, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to six months.  
(2)	Whoever arbitrarily takes his own right or a right which he believes belongs to him, by use of force or serious threat of attack on life or body, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.  
(3)	The same as in the preceding paragraph shall be punished whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph for another person.  
(4)	Whoever for himself or another person by the act from the second paragraph of this Article collects a debt, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(5)	Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this Article shall be initiated by private action, and for the acts from the second and third paragraphs upon a motion.  

Oviranje verskih obredov

## 311. člen

Whoever obstructs, prevents or disturbs a religious ceremony or whoever behaves insultingly in a place intended for that purpose, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  

Oviranje pogreba in skrunitev groba

## 312. člen

(1)	Whoever obstructs, prevents or disturbs a funeral, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2)	The same shall be punished whoever without authorisation digs up or tears open a grave or any other place where the dead are buried, or otherwise seriously desecrates it.  
(3)	If the acts from the preceding paragraphs are committed by two or more persons or if two or more graves were desecrated, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  

Skrunitev trupla

## 313. člen

Whoever without authorisation conceals, removes, damages, destroys or otherwise desecrates a corpse, part of a corpse or mortal remains, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.

Povzročitev splošne nevarnosti

## 314. člen

(1)	Whoever by fire, flood, explosion, poison or poisonous gas, ionising radiation, motor power, electric or any other energy or any other generally dangerous act or means or by omission of an act which he should have committed to ensure the general safety of people and property, causes danger to the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  
(2)	Whoever with explosives or other dangerous act and means, with the intention of extortion, intimidation, coercion to commit or omit some other act, revenge or obtaining property or non-property benefit for himself or another person, plans, attempts or carries out a dangerous act in which the safety of people or property of greater value is or would be endangered, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(3)	Whoever commits the act from the first paragraph by negligence, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(4)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons or major property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first and second paragraphs by imprisonment for up to ten years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment for up to five years.  

(5)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to fifteen years, for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment of at least fifteen years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment for up to eight years.  

Povzročitev nevarnosti pri gradbeni dejavnosti

## 315. člen

(1)	A person who is responsible for planning or supervising plans for the preparation or management of construction or building works, and in doing so acts contrary to regulations or generally recognised technical rules and thereby causes danger to the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons or major property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to five years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(4)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment for up to eight years.

Ogrožanje okolja s hrupom ali svetlobo

## 317. člen

(1)	Whoever by violation of regulations causes excessive noise or excessively strong lighting with which more serious damage to people's health can be caused, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to two years.  
(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  

Poškodovanje ali uničenje javnih naprav

## 318. člen

(1)	Whoever destroys, damages or removes electric lines, gas pipelines, water pipelines, heating pipelines, oil pipelines, telecommunication facilities, submarine cables, sewage facilities and environmental protection facilities or other similar public facilities and thereby causes disruptions in the supply to the population or economy, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.  

(2)	If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  

Prevažanje ali prenašanje razstreliva ali nevarnih snovi v nasprotju s predpisi

## 319. člen

Whoever contrary to regulations on traffic in explosives or easily flammable substances or other dangerous substances or dangerous waste transports, hands over for transport by public means of transport such substances or carries them himself when using a public means of transport, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  

Zloraba telekomunikacijskih znamenj

## 320. člen

Whoever knowingly without necessity sends an internationally agreed sign of call for help or a sign that danger is imminent, or whoever by telecommunication sign deceives that safety is given, or whoever otherwise abuses an internationally agreed telecommunication sign, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  

Neodvrnitev nevarnosti

## 321. člen

(1)	Whoever does not report in time to the competent authority or organisation or otherwise does not do everything necessary to avert fire, flood, explosion, traffic accident, ecological accident or any other danger to the life of people or excessive environmental burden or property of greater value, although he could have done so without danger to himself or another person, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2)	Whoever by force, threat or deception prevents someone from doing what is necessary to avert danger to the life of people, the environment or property of greater value, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.  

Odklonitev sodelovanja pri odvračanju splošne nevarnosti

## 322. člen

Whoever contrary to the order of the competent authority or organisation without justified reason does not participate in averting general danger or eliminating consequences, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to six months.

## 323. člen

Dangerous driving in road traffic

## 324. člen

1)	driving under the influence of alcohol with more than 1.10 grams of alcohol per kilogram of blood or more than 0.52 milligrams of alcohol per litre of exhaled air or driving under the influence of prohibited drugs, psychoactive medicines or other psychoactive substances, or
2)	exceeding the speed limit by more than 30 km/h in a pedestrian zone, in a traffic calming zone or in a speed limit zone, or by more than 50 km/h on a road, or

(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article results in a traffic accident with minor bodily injury to one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by a fine or imprisonment from three months to three years and by prohibition of driving a motor vehicle, and for the act from the second paragraph of this article by a fine or imprisonment of up to two years.  
(4)	If the act from the first paragraph of this article results in a traffic accident with serious bodily injury to one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years and by prohibition of driving a motor vehicle.

Endangering special types of public transport

(3)	If the act from the first paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to eight years and by prohibition of driving a motor vehicle.  

Endangering public transport by a dangerous act or means

## 326. člen

(1)	Whoever destroys or damages traffic facilities, means of transport, traffic signs and signalling devices, traffic safety devices, gives false signals or signs, places obstacles on traffic routes or acts in another similar manner and thereby causes danger to human life or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(2)	If the act from the previous paragraph was committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons or great material damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment of up to five years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(4)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment of up to eight years.

## 327. člen

(3)	If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article was committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.  
(4)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons or great material damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first and second paragraph by imprisonment of up to five years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment of up to three years.  
(5)	If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first and second paragraph by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment of up to eight years.  

Abandoning an injured person in a traffic accident without assistance

## 328. člen

(1)	A driver of a motor vehicle or other means of transport who leaves without assistance a person who was injured by that means of transport or because of it, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

## 329. člen

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever endangers safety at an airport by using force or threatening to use force to compel airport personnel to interrupt operations related to aircraft flights, or by using explosives or other similar devices or substances to destroy or damage an aircraft or facilities intended for the safety of air traffic.
(3) If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons or the destruction of an aircraft or airport, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

Whoever destroys, damages or removes a sign intended for the safety of air traffic shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Chapter Thirty-Two
CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST THE ENVIRONMENT, SPACE AND NATURAL RESOURCES

Pollution and destruction of the environment

## Article 332

(1) Whoever, in violation of regulations:
1) by discharges, emissions or inputs of quantities of substances or ionising radiation into the air, soil or water, endangers the life of one or more persons or causes a risk of serious bodily injury or actual damage to the quality of air, soil or water or to animals or plants;
2) collects, transports, processes or disposes of waste or supervises such procedures or activities after the cessation of operation of a waste disposal facility, or trades in waste or brokers it in such a way as to endanger the life of one or more persons or cause a risk of serious bodily injury or actual damage to the quality of air, soil or water or to animals or plants;
3) within the meaning of point 35 of Article 2 of Regulation (EC) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2006 on shipments of waste, ships non-negligible quantities of waste in one shipment or in several shipments that appear to be linked;
4) operates a plant in which a hazardous activity is carried out or in which hazardous substances or preparations are stored, which could cause the death of one or more persons or serious bodily injury or actual damage to the quality of air, soil or water or to animals or plants outside the plant;
5) causes a risk of significant deterioration of a habitat within a protected area;
6) produces, imports, exports, places on the market or uses ozone-depleting substances, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.
(2) If the act referred to in point 1), 2), 4) or 5) of the preceding paragraph results in serious bodily injury or actual damage to the quality of air, soil or water or to animals or plants, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to eight years.
(3) If the act referred to in point 1), 2) or 4) of the first paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

(4) If the act is committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article by a fine or imprisonment of up to two years, for the act referred to in the second paragraph of this Article by imprisonment of up to three years, and for the act referred to in the third paragraph of this Article by imprisonment from one to eight years.
(5) If the act referred to in the first, second or third paragraph of this Article is committed within a criminal organisation for the commission of these acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

Pollution of the sea and waters from vessels

## Article 333

(1) Whoever, in violation of regulations, discharges or orders the discharge of oil, chemicals or other pollutants from vessels into the sea, lake or river waters and thereby pollutes the sea, waters or coasts, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.
(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever pollutes through repeated discharges of a smaller scale which in individual cases do not cause deterioration of the quality of the sea, lake or river water, but in the case of repeated discharges cause such deterioration.
(3) If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article results in deterioration of human health or irreparable damage to or destruction of waters or coasts, animal or plant life, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.
(4) If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to twelve years.
(5) If the act is committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article by imprisonment of up to three years, for the act referred to in the third paragraph of this Article by imprisonment from six months to five years, and for the act referred to in the fourth paragraph of this Article by imprisonment from one to eight years.
(6) If the act referred to in the first, second, third or fourth paragraph of this Article is committed within a criminal organisation for the commission of these acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

Unlawful handling of nuclear or other hazardous radioactive substances

## Article 334

(1) Whoever, contrary to regulations or technical rules on safety measures, receives, possesses, uses, transfers, alters, deposits, discards, enables the acquisition of, disseminates, produces, processes, stores, transports, imports, exports or disposes of nuclear substances or other hazardous radioactive substances, which could cause death or serious bodily injury or great damage to property or great actual damage to the environment, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.
(2) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph results in serious bodily injury or great damage to property or great actual damage to the environment, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.

(3) If the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.
(4) Whoever obtains nuclear substances by theft, robbery, misappropriation, embezzlement, fraud, abuse of position or authority, threat or use of force or other means of intimidation, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.
(5) Whoever, by an act directed against a nuclear facility or an act that interferes with the operation of a nuclear facility, causes serious bodily injury or death of one or more persons, great damage to property or great actual damage to the environment due to exposure to radiation or release of radioactive substances, or whoever causes a risk of any of the stated consequences, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.
(6) Whoever threatens to use nuclear substances in such a way as to cause serious bodily injury or death of one or more persons, great damage to property or great actual damage to the environment, or to commit an act referred to in the fourth or fifth paragraph of this Article in order to compel any natural or legal person to do or omit any act, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to fifteen years.
(7) If the act is committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article by imprisonment of up to three years, for the act referred to in the second paragraph of this Article by imprisonment from six months to five years, and for the act referred to in the third paragraph of this Article by imprisonment from one to eight years.
(8) If the act referred to in the first, second, third, fourth, fifth or sixth paragraph of this Article was committed within a criminal association for the commission of these acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.

Thirty-third chapter
CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST THE SOVEREIGNTY OF THE REPUBLIC OF SLOVENIA AND ITS DEMOCRATIC CONSTITUTIONAL ORDER

High treason

## 348. člen

Whoever, by force or threat to use force, endangers the existence of the Republic of Slovenia or thereby attempts to change its constitutional order or overthrow the highest state authorities shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.  

Attack on territorial integrity

## 349. člen

Whoever attempts by force or threat to use force to secede part of the territory of the Republic of Slovenia or to annex part of this territory to another state shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Attack on the independence of the state

## 350. člen

A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who attempts to bring the Republic of Slovenia into subordination or dependence in relation to another state shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Violation of territorial integrity

## 351. člen

Whoever invades the territory of the Republic of Slovenia in order to violate its territorial integrity shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Murder of the president of the republic

## 352. člen

Whoever, in order to endanger the constitutional order or security of the Republic of Slovenia, takes the life of the President of the Republic of Slovenia or of the person replacing him shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Violence against the highest representatives of the state

## 353. člen

Whoever, in order to endanger the performance of duties in a democratic order or the security of the Republic of Slovenia, abducts any of the highest representatives of the state, a member of parliament, a member of the National Council, a member of the government, a judge of the Constitutional Court or the Supreme Court, or commits any other violence against him or his close relative, or attacks his official premises, private residence or means of transport, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Violence against representatives of foreign states or international organisations

## 354. člen

Whoever, in order to endanger the constitutional order or security of the Republic of Slovenia or to harm a foreign state, abducts a representative of a foreign state or international organisation, or commits any other violence against him or his close relative, or attacks his official premises, private residence or means of transport, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

Armed rebellion

## 355. člen

(1)	Whoever organises or leads an armed rebellion in order to endanger the existence of the Republic of Slovenia, change its constitutional order or overthrow its highest state authorities shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.
(2)	Whoever participates in an armed rebellion referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Sabotage

## 356. člen

Whoever, in order to endanger the constitutional order or security of the Republic of Slovenia, demolishes, sets fire to or otherwise destroys or damages any economic facility, means of transport or transport installation, installation intended for a communications system, public installation for water or energy transmission or any other facility important for the safety of people, supply of the population or the economy, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Sabotage

## 357. člen

(1)	Whoever, in order to endanger the constitutional order or security of the Republic of Slovenia, covertly, treacherously or in another similar manner causes great damage to a state authority or organisation in which he works, or to another state authority or organisation, in the performance of his work obligation, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.
(2)	If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph is committed at a nuclear facility, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

Espionage

## 358. člen

(1)	Whoever serves a foreign state or foreign organisation or its agent by collecting confidential military, economic or official data or documents, or communicates or hands them over to it, or enables it to obtain them, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.
(2)	Whoever, to the detriment of the Republic of Slovenia, creates an intelligence service for a foreign state or foreign organisation or leads it shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.
(3)	Whoever joins a foreign intelligence service referred to in the preceding paragraph or supports its work shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Incitement to violent change of the constitutional order

## 359. člen

(1)	Whoever, in order to endanger the existence, constitutional order or security of the Republic of Slovenia, calls for or incites the direct commission of criminal offences referred to in Articles 348 to 357 of this Code shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.
(2)	Whoever commits an act referred to in the preceding paragraph with financial or other assistance from abroad shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.
(3)	Whoever produces or reproduces material calling for or inciting the commission of criminal offences referred to in the first paragraph of this Article, in order that he or another person may distribute it, or whoever, with the same intention, stores a large quantity of such material or distributes it, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Punishment for the most serious forms of criminal offences

## 360. člen

(1)	For a criminal offence referred to in Articles 348 to 351 and 354 and 357 of this Code which results in the death of one or more persons, serious violence or great devastation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.
(2)	If the perpetrator, in committing the acts referred to in the preceding paragraph, intentionally takes the life of one or more persons, he shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

KAZNIVA DEJANJA ZOPER OBRAMBNO MOČ DRŽAVE

Evasion of defence duties

## 361. člen

(1)	Whoever, when a state of emergency or state of war is declared, hides or does not respond to a call to fulfil a defence duty shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.
(2)	Whoever, in order to evade a defence duty during a declared state of emergency or state of war as incapable of such duty, feigns illness, uses a forged document or otherwise deceives the competent authority shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.
(3)	The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall apply to whoever commits the act referred to in the preceding paragraph by injuring himself or otherwise rendering himself incapable of defence duty, either alone or with the help of another.

Evasion of defence duties by deception

## 362. člen

(1)	Whoever, when a state of emergency or state of war is declared, feigns illness, uses a forged document or otherwise deceives the competent authority in order to evade military service or other defence duty, and thereby induces it to declare him incapable of performing military service or military duty, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.
(2)	The same punishment shall apply to whoever, in order to evade the service or obligation referred to in the preceding paragraph or to be assigned to lighter service or obligation, injures himself or otherwise renders himself incapable, or allows another to render him incapable.
(3)	Whoever physically injures another with or without his consent or otherwise renders him incapable in order to evade military service or other military obligation shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Force against a military person in the performance of military duty

## 363. člen

(1)	Whoever by force or threat to use force directly prevents a military person from performing military duty or in the same manner compels him to perform military duty shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.
(2)	Attempt is punishable.
(3)	If the perpetrator, in committing the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article, insults a military person, treats him badly or inflicts minor bodily injury on him or threatens him with the use of a weapon, he shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.
(4)	If the perpetrator of the act referred to in the first to third paragraphs of this Article was provoked by unlawful or cruel treatment by a military person, he shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months, but the punishment may also be remitted.

Attack on a military person performing service

## 364. člen

(1)	Whoever attacks or seriously threatens to attack a military person performing service shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.
(2)	If the perpetrator, in the act referred to in the preceding paragraph, threatens a military person with a weapon, treats him badly or inflicts minor bodily injury on him, he shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.
(3)	The punishment referred to in the preceding paragraph shall apply to whoever commits the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article against two or more military persons.

Negligent handling of means of defence

## 365. člen

Whoever acts in contravention of regulations concerning weapons, ammunition, explosives, combat devices or other means intended for the defence of the state, which have been entrusted to him for storage, repair, maintenance or use, even though he foresees or should and could have foreseen that the means may be lost, destroyed or damaged, and thereby causes great material damage, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.

Undermining defence measures

## 366. člen

Whoever destroys defence installations, defence facilities, positions, weapons or other defence means, renders them unusable or in any other way obstructs or abandons defence measures of the state, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Preventing combat against the enemy

## 367. člen

A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who during war or armed conflict prevents citizens of the Republic of Slovenia or citizens of its allies from fighting against the enemy, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Service in the enemy's army

## 368. člen

A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who during war or armed conflict serves in the enemy's army or in other enemy armed forces or participates in war or armed conflict as a combatant against the Republic of Slovenia or its allies, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to ten years.

Recruitment for a foreign army

## 369. člen

Whoever recruits citizens of the Republic of Slovenia or citizens of other states or stateless persons for service in the enemy's army or other enemy armed forces or for participation in war or armed conflict against the Republic of Slovenia or its allies, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Assistance to the enemy

## 370. člen

(1) A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who during war assists the enemy in the seizure of movables, taking of food or other goods or in any other measures against the population, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.
(2) A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who during war politically or economically cooperates with the enemy shall be punished in the same manner.

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST INTERNATIONAL LAW

Endangering persons under international protection

## 371. člen

(1) Whoever endangers the safety of a person who is under international legal protection by seriously threatening to attack him, his official premises, private residence or means of transport, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.
(2) Whoever kidnaps a person who is under international legal protection or commits any other violence against him or attacks his official premises, private residence or means of transport, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least one year.
(3) If the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
(4) If the perpetrator in committing the act referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article intentionally takes the life of one or more persons, he shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Violation of a parliamentarian's right

## 372. člen

Whoever in contravention of the rules of international law during war or armed conflict insults a parliamentarian or his escort, treats them badly, detains them or

prevents their return or in any other way violates their inviolability, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Taking of hostages

## 373. člen

(1) Whoever kidnaps any person and threatens to kill, injure or detain him as a hostage in order to compel any state or international organisation to do or refrain from doing something as an express or tacit condition for the release of the hostage, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.
(2) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
(3) If the perpetrator in committing the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article intentionally takes the life of one or more persons, he shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Piracy

## 374. člen

(1) A crew member or passenger on a vessel or aircraft, other than a military ship or aircraft and a public ship or aircraft, who in violation of the rules of international law with the intention of obtaining for himself or another a material or non-material benefit or of seriously damaging another, on the high seas or in a place not under the authority of any state, commits an unlawful act of violence, detention or any plundering against another vessel or aircraft, persons or things on them, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.
(2) Piracy referred to in the preceding paragraph shall also be deemed to have been committed if the act is committed by a crew member of a military or public ship or aircraft which has unlawfully mutinied and taken control of the ship or aircraft.
(3) If the acts referred to in the first or second paragraph of this Article result in the death of one or more persons or great material damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

Violation of restrictive measures

374.a člen
(1) Whoever in contravention of the restrictions laid down in regulations on the implementation of restrictive measures adopted on the basis of legal acts and decisions of international organisations, or restrictions which are directly applicable in the Republic of Slovenia in accordance with the legal order of international organisations, offers, sells, transfers, transmits, exchanges, delivers, imports, exports, brings into the country or takes out of the country goods, technology, money or property or intermediates in this or enables access to such goods, technology, money or property or to benefits from them or does not prevent this or whoever unlawfully acquires or stores such goods, technology, money or property and thereby obtains a great material benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) The goods, technology, money and property referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be seized.
