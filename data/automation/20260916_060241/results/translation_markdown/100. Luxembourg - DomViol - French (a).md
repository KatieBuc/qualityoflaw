We Henri, Grand Duke of Luxembourg, Duke of Nassau, Our Council of State having been heard;  
With the assent of the Chamber of Deputies;  
Having regard to the decision of the Chamber of Deputies of 15 July 2003 and that of the Council of State of 18 July 2003 stating that there is no need for a second vote;  

Have ordered and order:
Art. Ier. (1) Within the framework of its missions of preventing offences and protecting persons, the Police, with the authorisation of the State Prosecutor, expels from their home and its appurtenances persons against whom there are indications that they are preparing to commit against a close person with whom they cohabit an offence against life or physical integrity, or that they are preparing to commit again against that person, already a victim, an offence against life or physical integrity.  
To be considered as a close person within the meaning of this article are the spouse or the person with whom the person who is the subject of an expulsion measure habitually lives, his or her legitimate, adoptive or natural ascendants and descendants as well as the legitimate, adoptive or natural ascendants and descendants of the spouse or of the person with whom the person who is the subject of an expulsion measure habitually lives. With regard to the descendants of the spouse or of the person with whom the person who is the subject of an expulsion measure habitually lives, only minor or disabled descendants are taken into account.  
The person who is the subject of an expulsion measure may not invoke any real or personal rights in relation to the home to oppose the measure.  
(2)	The expulsion entails a prohibition for the expelled person to enter the home and its appurtenances.  
If the necessity arises for the expelled person to enter the home or its appurtenances, notwithstanding the prohibition referred to in the first paragraph, he or she may only do so in the presence of a Police officer.  
(3)	The Police gives the person who is the subject of the expulsion measure the possibility to take away the personal belongings of which he or she has an urgent need and to inform himself or herself about his or her accommodation possibilities.  
The person concerned communicates to the Police the address at which he or she can be reached. If he or she cannot or does not wish to provide an address, he or she is deemed to elect domicile at the municipal administration of the place of the home, in particular for the purposes of the summonses and notifications provided for in articles 1017-3 and 1017-4 of the new Code of Civil Procedure and for those of the service of a possible summons pursuant to article 1017-9 of the new Code of Civil Procedure, and the Police informs him or her thereof.  
In addition, the Police has all keys giving access to the home and its appurtenances handed over and notifies the person concerned that if he or she enters or attempts to enter his or her home or its appurtenances notwithstanding the expulsion measure, he or she is exposed to the criminal sanctions provided for by article 439 of the penal code.  
(4)	When the person concerned refuses to comply voluntarily with the expulsion measure ordered, the Police is authorised to expel him or her by force if necessary.  
(5)	The expulsion is the subject of a report to be drawn up immediately by the Police officers and to be transmitted to the State Prosecutor. This report mentions in particular:	the indications on which the expulsion measure is based;	the day and time of the measure ordered;	the address provided by the expelled person enabling him or her to be reached.  
If the execution of the measure ordered had to be ensured by force, mention is made thereof in the report. The report mentions all other statements that the expelled person wishes to make.  
The report is presented to the expelled person for signature. A copy is given to him or her. In case of refusal to sign, or in case of refusal to accept the delivery of the copy, mention is made of the refusal and the reasons therefor.  
A copy of the report is also given to the close person to be protected. If that person, for reasons of fact or law, is not able to be given a copy, and if there is no other person able to receive the copy in place of the close person to be protected, mention is made thereof.  
(6)	The expulsion measure ends automatically at 17.00 hours, on the 10th day following that of its entry into force, subject to the provisions of article 1017-2 of the new Code of Civil Procedure.  

Upon expiry of the expulsion measure and in the absence of a prohibition to return to the home pronounced on the basis of articles 1017-1 et seq. of the new Code of Civil Procedure, the Police returns the keys to the person concerned who requests them.

## Art. II

Likewise, the Police informs a service for assistance to victims of domestic violence of the expulsion measure and communicates to it the address and identity of the protected person.  
The expression "service for assistance to victims of domestic violence" refers to any private or public law body whose purpose is to assist, guide and advise persons who are victims of domestic violence by actively seeking contact with them.  
The body must possess, for the exercise of the activities referred to above, written approval pursuant to the law of 8 September 1998 regulating relations between the State and bodies working in the social, family and therapeutic fields. In addition to the legal conditions provided for in article 2 of the aforementioned law of 8 September 1998 and the regulatory conditions adopted in execution of that law, the body must guarantee that its activities are carried out in collaboration with the Police, the judicial authorities and other competent State authorities, taking into account the specificity of the roles respectively assigned to them, as well as in respect of the will and dignity of the protected person.  
Any person who, in any capacity whatsoever, participates in the activities of a service for assistance to victims of domestic violence, obtains or receives communication of personal data pursuant to this article is bound by professional secrecy under the conditions and penalties of article 458 of the Penal Code.

## Art. III

The Minister of Justice, the Police, the Public Prosecutor's Office, the services for assistance to victims of domestic violence establish each year statistics broken down by sex, age and relationship between perpetrator and victim and indicating for each heading the existence or absence of a cohabitation situation between the perpetrator and the victim, each as far as it is concerned, on the number of complaints, denunciations, expulsion measures and other types of police intervention, social interventions, prosecutions and convictions for the offences referred to in the following articles:
327 to 330 combined with article 330-1,
372 to 377,
395,
396,
401 bis,
409,
434 to 438, combined with article 438-1 and
439 paragraph 2 of the Penal Code.

## Art. IV

A cooperation committee among professionals in the field of combating violence is hereby established, composed of representatives of state authorities responsible for implementing this law as well as representatives of approved domestic violence victim assistance services, responsible for centralising and studying the statistics referred to in the preceding article, examining the implementation and any practical application problems of Articles I to II of this law, Articles 1017-1 to 1017-12 of the new Code of Civil Procedure and Article 3-1 of the Code of Criminal Instruction and submitting to the Government such proposals as it deems useful. A Grand-Ducal regulation shall determine its composition, organisation and operating procedures.  
Amending provisions

## Art. V

Paragraph 2 of Article 33 of the law of 31 May 1999 on the Police and the General Inspectorate of the Police is replaced as follows:
"To this end, it ensures general surveillance and controls in places to which it has legal access, exercises the powers defined by Articles I to III of the law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence, executes administrative police measures and takes material administrative police measures within its competence."

## Art. VI. The Penal Code is amended and supplemented as follows

1°. Article 329 is replaced as follows:
"Art. 329 The threat by gestures or emblems of an attack against property, punishable by a criminal penalty, shall be punished by imprisonment of eight days to three months and a fine of 251 euros to 1,000 euros.  
The threat by gestures or emblems of an attack against persons, punishable by a criminal penalty or a penalty of imprisonment of at least six months, shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to one year and a fine of 251 euros to 3,000 euros."
2°. Following article 330 is inserted a new article 330-1 the content of which is as follows:
"Art. 330-1. The minimum penalties provided by articles 327, 329 and 330 shall be increased in accordance with article 266, if the offender has committed the threat of attack against

1° the spouse or divorced spouse, the person with whom he lives or has habitually lived; 2° a legitimate or natural ascendant or his adoptive father or mother;  
3° a legitimate, natural or adoptive descendant; 4° a brother or sister;  
5° a legitimate or natural ascendant, the adoptive father or mother, a descendant, a brother or sister of a person referred to sub 1°;  
6° a person whose particular vulnerability, due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or a state of pregnancy, is apparent or known to their perpetrator;  
7° a person who is bound to him by ties of subordination."
3°. Before the last paragraph of article 377 is inserted a new paragraph, worded as follows:
"If the victim is
1° the spouse or divorced spouse, the person with whom the offender lives or has habitually lived, 2° a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant of the offender;  
3° a brother or sister;  
4° a legitimate or natural ascendant, the adoptive father or mother, a descendant, a brother or sister of a person referred to sub 1°."
4°. Article 409 is replaced as follows:
"Art. 409. Shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years and a fine of 251 euros to 5,000 euros, whoever has voluntarily caused injuries or struck blows
1° to the spouse or divorced spouse, to the person with whom he lives or has habitually lived; 2° to a legitimate or natural ascendant or his adoptive father or mother;  
3° to a legitimate, natural or adoptive descendant of fourteen years or more; 4° to a brother or sister;  
5° to a legitimate or natural ascendant, to the adoptive father or mother, to a descendant of fourteen years completed, to a brother or to a sister of a person referred to sub 1°;  
6° to a person whose particular vulnerability, due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or a state of pregnancy, is apparent or known to their perpetrator;  
7° to a person who is bound to him by ties of subordination.  
When the blows or injuries have been premeditated, the penalties shall be imprisonment of one year to five years and a fine of 501 euros to 5,000 euros.  
If the voluntary blows or injuries referred to in paragraph 1 have resulted in an illness or incapacity for personal work, the penalties shall be imprisonment of 1 year to 5 years and a fine of 501 euros to 25,000 euros in the absence of premeditation and, otherwise, criminal imprisonment of 5 years to 10 years and a fine of 1,000 euros to
30,000 euros.  
If the voluntary blows or injuries referred to in paragraph 1 have resulted in either an apparently incurable illness, or a permanent incapacity for personal work, or the loss of absolute use of an organ, or a serious mutilation, the penalties shall be criminal imprisonment of 10 years to 15 years and a fine of 2,500 euros to 50,000 euros in the absence of premeditation and, otherwise, criminal imprisonment of 15 years to 20 years and a fine of 3,000 euros to 50,000 euros.  
If the voluntary blows or injuries referred to in paragraph 1 have caused death, without intention to cause it, the offender shall be punished by criminal imprisonment of 20 years to 30 years, in the absence of premeditation of these acts of violence, and by life imprisonment, otherwise.  
If the voluntary blows or injuries referred to in this article have been committed against a person with whom the offender cohabits, the court may furthermore pronounce against the convicted person all or part of the following prohibitions:	the prohibition from approaching the victim's dwelling by more than a distance to be determined;	the prohibition from making contact with the victim;	the prohibition from approaching the victim by more than a distance to be determined."
5°. Following article 438 is inserted an article 438-1 worded as follows:
"Art. 438-1. In the cases mentioned in articles 434 to 438, the minimum penalties provided by these articles shall be increased in accordance with article 266, when the offender has committed the crime or offence against

1° his spouse or divorced spouse, the person with whom he lives or has habitually lived; 2° a legitimate or natural ascendant or his adoptive father or mother;  
3° a legitimate, natural or adoptive descendant; 4° a brother or sister;  
5° a legitimate or natural ascendant, the adoptive father or mother, a descendant, a brother or sister of a person referred to sub 1°;  
6° a person whose particular vulnerability, due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or a state of pregnancy, is apparent or known to their perpetrator;  
7° a person who is bound to him by ties of subordination."
6°. Article 439 is supplemented by a new paragraph worded as follows:
"Shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years and a fine of 501 euros to 5,000 euros, whoever has entered or attempted to enter a house, apartment, room or dwelling inhabited by a person with whom he has cohabited, or their outbuildings, either by means of threats or violence against persons, or by means of breaking and entering, climbing or false keys, or even by means of keys if he acts in violation of an expulsion measure governed by article I of the law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence, of an interim order provisionally allocating the common dwelling to his spouse, of an order prohibiting him from returning to the domicile in accordance with article 1017-1 or 1017-7 of the new Code of Civil Procedure."
7°. Article 448 is supplemented by a new paragraph the content of which is as follows:
"When the offender has committed the offence against
1° his spouse or divorced spouse or the person with whom he lives or has habitually lived; 2° a legitimate or natural ascendant or his adoptive father or mother;  
3° a legitimate, natural or adoptive descendant; 4° a brother or sister;  
5° a legitimate or natural ascendant, the adoptive father or mother, a descendant, a brother or sister of a person referred to sub 1°;  
6° a person whose particular vulnerability, due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or a state of pregnancy, is apparent or known to their perpetrator;

## Art. VII. The Code of Criminal Instruction is amended and supplemented as follows

1°. Following the current article 3, a new article 3-1 is inserted, worded as follows:
"Art. 3-1. Any association of national importance, having legal personality and approved by the Minister of Justice may exercise the rights recognised to the civil party with regard to facts constituting an offence within the meaning of articles 375, 401 bis or 409 of the Criminal Code or of articles 444 (2), 453, 454, 455, 456, 457, 457-1, 457-2, 457-3 and 457-4 of the Criminal Code and causing direct or indirect harm to the collective interests which they have as their object to defend, even if they do not justify a material or moral interest and even if the collective interest in which they act is entirely covered by the social interest whose defence is ensured by the public prosecutor.  
Where an offence within the meaning of articles 444 (2), 453, 454, 455, 456, 457, 457-1, 457-2, 457-3 and 457-4 of the Criminal Code is committed against persons considered individually or an offence within the meaning of articles 375, 401bis or 409 of the Criminal Code, the association may exercise by principal action the rights recognised to the civil party only on condition that those persons expressly declare in writing that they do not object thereto."
2°. Article 24 (5) is replaced as follows:
"(5) The State Prosecutor may, prior to his decision on the public prosecution, decide to resort to mediation if it appears to him that such a measure is likely to ensure reparation of the damage caused to the victim, or to put an end to the disturbance resulting from the offence or to contribute to the rehabilitation of the perpetrator of the offence. However, recourse to mediation is excluded in the presence of offences against persons with whom the perpetrator cohabits.  
The mediator is bound by professional secrecy."

## Art. VIII. The new Code of Civil Procedure is amended and supplemented as follows

In the second part, in Book I, following Title VII entitled "Judicial intervention concerning the rights of spouses", a new Title VII bis entitled "Judicial intervention in cases of domestic violence" is inserted, worded as follows:
"Title VII bis. - Judicial intervention in cases of domestic violence
Section 1: Prohibition of return to the home following expulsion - administrative police measure

## Art. 1017-1

In cases where one of the persons listed in the following paragraph has benefited from the protection of an expulsion measure based on article I of the law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence, he or she may, by simple application, request the president of the district court to order against the expelled person a prohibition of return to the home for a maximum period of three months following the expiry of the expulsion measure, without regard to any real or personal rights of the expelled person in relation to the home.  
The following are entitled to make such an application:
1° the spouse of the expelled person or the person with whom the expelled person habitually lives, the legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants and descendants of the expelled person;  
2° the legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants, the legitimate, natural or adoptive descendants who are minors or suffering from a disability, of the spouse of the expelled person or of the person with whom the expelled person habitually lives,
provided that they cohabited with the expelled person before his or her expulsion and justify the fact that the home satisfies their urgent housing needs.  
The prohibition referred to in the first paragraph terminates automatically as soon as a decision is made to settle the residence of the spouses during divorce proceedings.

## Art. 1017-2

The application must be submitted no later than the tenth day following the entry into force of the expulsion measure based on article I of the law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence referred to above. If the application has been lodged in accordance with the provisions of this paragraph, the expulsion shall continue to produce its effects pending the order of the president to be issued.  
It is made at the registry by the interested party or by his or her representative and recorded in a special register, on unstamped paper, kept at the registry of the court.  
The declaration contains, on pain of nullity:
1° the surnames, first names, professions of the applicant and defendant parties;  
2° the home in question as well as the address that the defendant party communicated to the Police in application of article I (3), paragraph 2 of the law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence, unless he or she failed to do so, in which case mention of the home suffices;  
3° the subject of the application and the statement of grounds.  
A copy of the report drawn up at the time of expulsion is attached to the application.

## Art. 1017-3

The registrar summons the parties by registered post, informing them of the day, time and place of the hearing. He attaches a copy of the application for the defendant. The summons contains, on pain of nullity, mention of article 80 paragraph 1. A copy of the summons is also sent to the police.  
The hearing may be fixed on a public holiday or on a day habitually not worked. The application is ruled upon urgently by order of the president.  
The order pronouncing the prohibition of return to the home is enforceable provisionally and without security, on the minute and before registration.  
The order is notified through the registry. The registrar also sends a copy of the order to the Police.  
"Art. 1017-4. The order may be appealed within fifteen days from notification.  
The appeal is brought before the Court of Appeal. It is made by filing a reasoned application at the registry of the district court. The date of filing is recorded by the registrar on the original of the application. Within three days of the filing of the application, the file is transmitted to the Court of Appeal. The appeal is judged according to the same procedure as at first instance.  
In case of default, the order is subject to opposition within eight days from notification, which runs concurrently with the appeal period. Opposition consists of a declaration to be made at the registry of the district court.  
The judgment rendered on appeal is not subject to opposition."

## Art. 1017-5

(1) The parties shall be required to appear in person or through a lawyer.  
(2) The parties may be assisted or represented by:
a lawyer,
their spouse or the person with whom they habitually live, their relatives or relatives by marriage in the direct line,
their relatives or relatives by marriage in the collateral line up to and including the third degree,
persons exclusively attached to their personal service or to their business, a collaborator of a service providing assistance to victims of domestic violence.  
The representative, if not a lawyer, must justify a special power of attorney.

## Art. 1017-6

The president may, at the request of the applicant party, order penalty payments.  
Section 2: Various other prohibitions and injunctions in matters of violence

## Art. 1017-7

Where a person makes the continuation of common life intolerable for a close person, either because they assault them or threaten to assault them or because they have behaviour towards them which seriously harms their mental health, the president of the district court shall order them, upon application by the person concerned, to leave the home and its appurtenances and prohibit them from returning there before the expiry of a maximum period of three months, regardless of any real or personal rights of the defendant party in relation to the home.  
The following are entitled to make such an application:
1° the spouse or the person with whom the defendant person habitually lives, legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants and descendants;  
2° legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants, legitimate, natural or adoptive descendants who are minors or have a disability, of the spouse or of the person with whom the defendant party habitually lives,
provided they establish that the home satisfies their urgent housing needs and that they cohabit or have cohabited with the defendant party during the three months preceding the application.  
The prohibition referred to in the first paragraph shall terminate automatically as soon as a decision is made to settle the residence of the spouses during divorce proceedings.

## Art. 1017-8

Where a person assaults or threatens to assault a close person or where they have behaviour towards them which seriously harms their mental health and thus makes any encounter with them intolerable, the president of the district court shall order, upon application by the person concerned, all or part of the injunctions or prohibitions listed below, provided that they do not conflict with fundamental and legitimate interests of the defendant party:	the prohibition on making contact with the applicant party;	the prohibition on sending messages to the applicant party;	the prohibition on approaching the applicant party closer than a distance to be defined;	the prohibition on establishing their home in the same neighbourhood as the applicant party;	the prohibition on frequenting certain places;	the prohibition on taking certain routes;	the prohibition, renewable, for a period to be fixed by the President, on accommodating their child or children or seeing their child or children outside a specialised facility designated by them pending any other judicial decision to be made in matters of custody and visitation rights;	the injunction to allow the applicant party to enter the common home to remove their personal belongings. The following are entitled to make such an application:
1° the spouse or divorced spouse, the person with whom the defendant party lives or has habitually lived, legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants, legitimate, natural or adoptive descendants;  
2° legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants, legitimate, natural or adoptive descendants who are minors or have a disability of the spouse or divorced spouse or of the person with whom the defendant party lives or has habitually lived.

## Art. 1017-9

The application shall be brought by summons to a hearing held on the day and at the time indicated by the president. The hearing may be held on a public holiday or a day usually not worked.  
The summons is exempt from stamp and registration duties and from the formality of registration.

## Art. 1017-10

(1) The parties shall be required to appear in person or through a lawyer.  
(2) The parties may be assisted or represented by:
a lawyer,
their spouse or the person with whom they habitually live, their relatives or relatives by marriage in the direct line,
their relatives or relatives by marriage in the collateral line up to and including the third degree,
persons exclusively attached to their personal service or to their business, a collaborator of a domestic violence victim assistance service.  
The representative, if not a lawyer, must provide proof of a special power of attorney.

## Art. 1017-11. The application shall be decided upon urgently

## Art. 1017-12. The provisions of articles 938 to 940 are applicable.»
