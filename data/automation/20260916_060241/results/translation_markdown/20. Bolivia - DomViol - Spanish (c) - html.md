###### Art. 1º.- (En cuanto al espacio)

Este Código se aplicará:
1)
To crimes committed in the territory of Bolivia or in places subject to its jurisdiction.  
2)
To crimes committed abroad, whose results have occurred or should have occurred in the territory of Bolivia or in places subject to its jurisdiction.  
3)
To crimes committed abroad by a Bolivian, provided that he is found in national territory and has not been punished in the place where he committed the crime.  
4)
To crimes committed abroad against State security, public faith and the national economy. This provision shall be extended to foreigners, if they are obtained by extradition or are found within the territory of the Republic.  
5)
To crimes committed on Bolivian vessels, aircraft or other means of transport in a foreign country, when they are not tried in that country.  
6)
To crimes committed abroad by officials in the service of the Nation, in the performance of their office or commission.  
7)
To crimes that by treaty or convention the Republic has undertaken to suppress, even if they were not committed in its territory.

###### Art. 2º.- (Sentencia extranjera)

In the cases provided for in the preceding article, when the agent is tried in Bolivia, having already been sentenced abroad, the part of the sentence served there shall be computed if it is of the same kind and if it is of a different kind, the judge shall in any case reduce the sentence imposed on the perpetrator.

###### Art. 3º.- (Extradicción)

No person subject to the jurisdiction of Bolivian laws may be surrendered by extradition to another State, unless an international treaty or reciprocity agreement provides otherwise.  
The granting or denial of extradition shall be decided by the Supreme Court.  
In case of reciprocity, extradition may not be carried out if the act for which it is requested does not constitute a crime under the law of the State requesting extradition and of the State that must grant it.

###### Art. 4º.- (EN CUANTO AL TIEMPO)

No one may be convicted or subjected to a security measure for an act that is not expressly provided as a crime by criminal law in force at the time it was committed, nor subjected to penalties or criminal security measures that are not established therein.  
If the law in force at the time the crime was committed is different from that existing when the judgment is rendered or from that in force in the intermediate time, the most favorable one shall always be applied.  
If during the serving of the sentence a more lenient law is enacted, it shall be the one applied.  
Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraphs, laws enacted to govern only for a determined time shall apply to all acts committed during their validity.

###### Art. 8º.- (Tentativa)

He who by suitable or unequivocal acts begins the execution of the crime and does not consummate it due to causes beyond his will, shall be punished with two-thirds of the penalty established for the consummated crime.

###### Art. 9º.- (Desistimiento y arrepentimiento eficaz)

Shall not be punished with any penalty.  
1)
He who voluntarily desists from the commission of the crime.  
2)
He who in the same manner prevents or contributes to preventing the result from occurring, unless the acts performed constitute crimes in themselves.

###### Art. 10º.- (Delito imposible)

If the result does not occur because the means employed are not suitable or because of the impropriety of the object, the judge may only impose security measures.

###### Art. 12º.- (STATE OF NECESSITY)

The person who, in order to avoid injury to a legal interest of their own or another's, not avoidable by other means, commits an act defined in a criminal statute, is exempt from liability when the following requirements are met:
1)
That the injury caused is not greater than that which is sought to be avoided, taking into account, principally, the equivalence in the quality of the legal interests involved;  
2)
That the injury to be avoided is imminent or current, and important;  
3)
That the situation of necessity has not been intentionally provoked by the subject; and,
4)
That the person in need does not have, by virtue of their office or position, the obligation to face the danger.

###### Art. 16º.- (ERROR)

1)
(ERROR OF TYPE).- Invincible error regarding a constitutive element of the criminal type excludes criminal liability for this offense. If the error, considering the circumstances of the act and the personal characteristics of the perpetrator, were vincible, the infraction shall be punished with the penalty for the negligent offense, when the law threatens it with punishment.  
Error regarding a fact that qualifies the infraction or regarding an aggravating circumstance shall prevent the application of the aggravated penalty.  
The offense committed by vincible error regarding circumstances that would have justified or exculpated the act shall be punished as a negligent offense when the law threatens it with punishment.  
2)
(ERROR OF PROHIBITION).- Invincible error regarding the unlawfulness of the act constituting the criminal type excludes criminal liability. If the error were vincible, the penalty may be mitigated in accordance with article 39.

###### Art. 28º.- (WORK PERFORMANCE)

The penalty of work performance for the benefit of the community obliges the convicted person to provide his work in activities of public utility that do not violate his dignity and are in accordance with his capacity.  
Work performance shall not interfere with the normal work activity of the convicted person, shall be performed in public establishments and in associations of general interest during the hours determined by the judge. It shall have a maximum duration of forty-eight weeks, and weekly may not exceed sixteen hours, nor be less than three hours.  
Work performance may only be executed with the consent of the convicted person. In the event that the convicted person does not give his consent, the sanction shall be converted into a penalty of deprivation of liberty. For this purpose, one day of deprivation of liberty equals two weekly hours of work. This substitution shall be carried out only once, and once carried out may not cease to be executed.  
The supervising judge must request reports on work performance from the employing entity. In the event that the reports are not favorable, it shall be converted into deprivation of liberty in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

###### Art. 29º.- (DAY-FINES)

The fine consists of payment to the Reparations Fund of an amount of money that shall be set by the judge in day-fines, based on the economic capacity of the convicted person, his daily income, his aptitude for work and his family burdens, considered at the time the sentence is issued. The minimum shall be one day-fine and the maximum five hundred.  
The installments that the convicted person must pay shall not exceed the maximum attachable amount of his salary, if this were his only source of income. The maximum total amount of the day-fine may not exceed twenty-five monthly national minimum wages.  
If the convicted person does not provide sufficient information about his income, assets or other bases for calculating a daily installment, then it may be assessed by estimation.  
The resolution shall indicate the number of day-fines, amount of the daily installment and the payment term.

###### Art. 30º.- (CONVERSION)

When a fine penalty of day-fines and a custodial sentence are imposed jointly, conversion of the day-fines into deprivation of liberty shall not proceed. In other cases, conversion shall proceed when the solvent convicted person does not pay the fine.  
Before conversion, the judge may authorize the convicted person to amortize the pecuniary penalty through provision of work. The judge may also authorize payment of the fine in installments, setting the amount and date of payments according to the convicted person's economic condition, or arrange for satisfaction of the fine by making it effective against the convicted person's property, wages or other income.  
Payment of the fine at any time renders the conversion without effect, deducting the time of confinement that the convicted person may have served, in the established proportion.  
For purposes of conversion and amortization, one day of confinement equals three day-fines and one day of work of four hours equals one day-fine.

###### Art. 36º.- (APPLICATION OF SPECIAL DISQUALIFICATION)

Special disqualification from six months to ten years shall be imposed, after completion of the principal penalty, when the crime committed involves violation or disregard of the rights and duties corresponding to the mandate, office, employment or commission, incompetence or abuse of the professions or activities referred to in article 34 and involves crimes committed:
1)         By public officials, mandataries, commissioners, in the exercise of their functions;  
2)         By doctors, lawyers, engineers, financial auditors and other professionals in the exercise of their professions; or,
3)         By those who perform industrial, commercial or other activity.  
In the foregoing cases special disqualification is inherent to the time of completion of the custodial sentence.  
The minimum special disqualification penalty shall be five years, in the following cases:
1)         If the death of one or more persons occurs as a consequence of a serious culpable violation of the duty of care.  
2)         If the crime was committed by a public official in the exercise of his or her functions.

##### CAPITULO II

APPLICATION OF PENALTIES

###### Art. 37º.- (Determination of the penalty)

It is the responsibility of the judge, considering the personality of the perpetrator, the greater or lesser seriousness of the act, the circumstances and the consequences of the crime:
1)
To obtain direct knowledge of the subject, the victim and the circumstances of the act, to the extent required for each case.  
2)
To determine the penalty applicable to each crime, within the legal limits.

###### Art. 38º. (Circumstances)

1)
To assess the personality of the perpetrator, the following shall be taken into account primarily:
a)      The age, education, customs and prior and subsequent conduct of the subject, the motives that impelled him or her to commit the crime and his or her economic and social situation.  
b)      The special conditions in which he or she was at the time of execution of the crime and other personal background and conditions, as well as his or her ties of kinship, friendship or arising from other relationships, the quality of the offended persons and other circumstances of a subjective nature.  
Premeditation, base or antisocial motive, treachery and cruelty shall also be taken into account.  
2)
To assess the seriousness of the act, the following shall be taken into account: nature of the action, the means employed, the extent of the damage caused and the danger incurred.

###### Art. 39º.- (SPECIAL MITIGATING CIRCUMSTANCES)

In cases where this code expressly provides for special mitigation, the following procedure shall apply:
1)      The penalty of thirty years' imprisonment shall be reduced to fifteen.  
2)      When the crime is threatened with a penalty of imprisonment with a minimum exceeding one year, the penalty imposed may be mitigated to the legal minimum of the penal scale for imprisonment.  
3)      When the crime is threatened with a penalty of imprisonment whose minimum is one year or a penalty of confinement with a minimum exceeding one month, the penalty imposed may be mitigated to the legal minimum of the penal scale for confinement.

###### Art. 40º.- (General mitigating circumstances)

The penalty may also be mitigated:
1)
When the perpetrator has acted for an honorable motive, or impelled by poverty, or under the influence of serious and unjust moral suffering, or under the impression of a serious threat, or by the ascendancy of a person to whom he or she owes obedience or on whom he or she depends.  
2)
When he or she has distinguished himself or herself in prior life by particularly meritorious behavior.  
3)
When he or she has demonstrated repentance through acts and especially by repairing the damages, to the extent that it has been possible.  
4)
When the agent is an indigenous person lacking instruction and his or her ignorance of the law can be proven.

##### CAPITULO VI

COMMON PROVISIONS

###### Art. 70º.- ("Nulla poena sine juditio")

No one shall be condemned to any sanction without having been heard and tried in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure.  
No sanction may be executed except by virtue of a judgment issued by competent judicial authority and in compliance with a law, nor executed in a manner different from that established therein.

###### Art. 71º.- (Forfeiture)

The commission of a crime entails the loss of the instruments with which it was executed and of the effects derived from it, which shall be forfeited, unless they belonged to a non-responsible third party, who may recover them.  
Forfeited instruments may be sold at public auction if they are of lawful commerce, to cover civil liability in cases of insolvency; if they are not, they shall be destroyed or rendered useless.  
They may also eventually pass into State ownership.

##### CAPITULO I

CIVIL LIABILITY

###### Art. 87.- (Civil liability)

Every person who is criminally liable is also civilly liable and is obligated to repair the material and moral damages caused by the crime.

###### Art. 88.- (Preference)

Civil liability shall have preference over payment of the fine and any other obligation that the liable party may have contracted after the crime was committed.

###### Art. 89.- (Exemption from liability)

Only those who are protected by a justification defense are exempt from civil liability, except the person who caused the state of necessity.  
In cases where the person who caused it is not determined, the persons in whose favor the harm was prevented shall be obligated to civil liability, in proportion to the benefit obtained by each of them, and subsidiarily, the State.

###### Art. 90.- (Legal mortgage, seizure and retention)

From the moment a crime is committed, the immovable property of those liable shall be deemed specially mortgaged for civil liability.  
The judge may also order the seizure of movable property, and retention as appropriate.

###### Art. 91.- (Scope)

Civil liability includes:
1)
The restitution of the property of the injured party, which shall be delivered even if held by a third-party possessor.  
2)
Reparation for the damage caused.  
3)
Compensation for all harm caused to the victim, their family or a third party, the amount to be fixed prudently by the judge, in the absence of full proof. Every compensation shall always include the expenses incurred by the victim for their treatment, recovery and re-education.

###### Art. 92.- (Joint liability and transmissibility of obligations)

Civil liability shall be joint among all those liable for the crime.  
This obligation passes to the heirs of the liable party and the right to demand it is transmitted to the heirs of the victim.

###### Art. 93.- (Participation in the proceeds of the crime)

Anyone who for profit participates in the proceeds of a crime shall be obligated to make restitution, up to the amount by which they benefited.  
If the liable party or participants acted as agents of someone or as representatives or members of a legal entity and the proceeds or profit of the crime benefited the principal or represented party, they shall be equally obligated to make restitution in the same proportion.

###### Art. 110.- (TOTAL OR PARTIAL SUBMISSION OF THE NATION TO FOREIGN DOMINATION)

Whoever carries out the acts provided for in the preceding article, tending to submit the Nation totally or partially to foreign domination or to undermine its independence or integrity, shall be sanctioned with thirty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 111.- (Espionage)

Whoever procures secret documents, objects or information of a political or military nature relating to security, means of defense or foreign relations, for purposes of espionage in favor of other countries in time of peace, that endanger the security of the State, shall incur the penalty of thirty years of imprisonment without right to pardon.

###### Art. 112.- (Clandestine introduction and possession of means of espionage)

Whoever in time of war introduces himself clandestinely, with deceit or violence, into a military place or zone or is caught in such places or in their vicinity in unjustified possession of means of espionage, shall incur deprivation of liberty of five to ten years.

###### Art. 113.- (Offenses committed by foreigners)

Foreigners residing in Bolivian territory are included in the preceding articles and the sanctions specified therein shall be imposed on them, except as established by treaties or by the law of nations concerning diplomatic officials.

###### Art. 114.- (Hostile acts)

Whoever without the knowledge or influence of the Government commits hostilities against any foreign power and thereby exposes the State to the serious danger of a declaration of war or to vexations or reprisals being made against its nationals abroad or to the rupture of diplomatic relations, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty of two to four years.  
If war results from such hostilities, the penalty shall be ten years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 115.- (Revelation of secrets)

Whoever reveals secrets of a political or military nature concerning the security of the State, the means of defense or foreign relations, shall incur deprivation of liberty of one to six years.  
The sanction shall be increased by one third, if the agent perpetrates this offense by abusing the function, employment or commission conferred by public authority.

###### Art. 116.- (Offense by fault)

If the revelation of the secrets mentioned in the preceding article is committed by fault of the person who is in possession by virtue of his employment or office, the sanction shall be confinement of six months to two years.

###### Art. 117.- (Infidelity in State business)

The representative or commissioner of the Government of Bolivia to negotiate a treaty, agreement or convention with another State, who departs from his instructions in a manner that may produce harm to the national interest, shall incur imprisonment of two to six years.  
The sanction shall be increased by one half, if the offense is perpetrated for purposes of profit or in time of war.

###### Art. 118.- (Sabotage)

Whoever in time of war destroys or renders useless installations, roads, works or other means of defense, communication, transport, supply, etc., with the purpose of harming the capacity or war effort of the Nation, shall be sanctioned with thirty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 119.- (Breach of contracts of military interest)

Whoever in time of war does not duly fulfill contractual obligations relating to the needs of the armed forces or national defense, shall incur imprisonment of two to six years.

###### Art. 120.- (Offenses against an allied State)

The provisions established in the preceding articles shall also apply when the acts provided for therein are committed against a power allied with Bolivia, at war against a common enemy.

###### Art. 121.- (Armed uprisings against the security and sovereignty of the State)

Those who rise up in arms with the purpose of changing the Political Constitution or the form of government established therein, deposing any of the public powers of the national government or preventing, even temporarily, the free exercise of their constitutional powers or their renewal within their legal terms, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty of five to fifteen years.  
Those who organize or integrate irregular armed groups, urban or rural, under internal or external influence, to promote armed confrontations with regular or public security forces, or to commit attacks against the life and security of persons, the territorial integrity or the sovereignty of the State, shall be sanctioned with the penalty of fifteen to thirty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 122.- (Granting of extraordinary powers)

Members of Congress or those who in a popular assembly grant the Executive Power extraordinary powers, the sum of Public Power or supremacies by which the life, property and honor of Bolivians are left at the mercy of the Government or any person, shall incur deprivation of liberty of two to six years.

###### Art. 123.- (Sedition)

Those who, without disavowing the authority of the legally constituted Government, rise up publicly and in open hostility to depose any public official or employee, prevent their taking office or oppose the enforcement of laws, decrees or judicial or administrative resolutions, exercise any act of hatred or revenge against the person or property of any authority or private individuals, or disturb or disrupt public order in any other way, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years.  
Public officials who have not resisted a rebellion or sedition by all means at their disposal shall incur imprisonment of one to two years.

###### Art. 124.- (Assuming the Rights of the People)

With the same penalty shall be punished those who form part of an armed force or a gathering of persons who assume the rights of the people and attempt to exercise such rights in their name.

###### Art. 125.- Common Provisions for the Crimes of Rebellion and Sedition)

In the event that the rebels or seditionists submit to the first request of the public authority, without having caused any damage other than the momentary disturbance of order, only the promoters or leaders shall be punished, to whom half of the penalty prescribed for the crime shall be applied.

###### Art. 126.- (Conspiracy)

Whoever takes part in a conspiracy of three or more persons to commit the crimes of rebellion or sedition shall be punished with the penalty for the crime that was to be perpetrated, reduced by one half.  
Participants who voluntarily desist before the execution of the proposed act and those who spontaneously prevent the commission of the crime shall be exempt from penalty.

###### Art. 127.- (Seduction of Troops)

Whoever seduces troops or usurps command of them or illegally retains a political or military command in order to commit a rebellion or sedition shall be punished with half the penalty corresponding to the crime that was to be perpetrated.

###### Art. 128.- (Attacks against the President and Other State Dignitaries)

Whoever attacks the life or safety of the President of the Republic, Vice President, Ministers of State and President of the National Congress shall be punished with a penalty of five to ten years of deprivation of liberty.  
If death results as a consequence of the attack committed, the maximum penalty corresponding to it shall be applied; if serious injuries result to the victim, the applicable sanction for the act shall be increased by one third.

###### Art. 129.- (Outrage to National Symbols)

Whoever publicly outrages the flag, coat of arms or anthem of the Nation shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years.

###### Art. 168.- (Self-Incrimination)

Whoever, by means of a statement or confession made before the competent authority to initiate the first judicial police proceedings or to conduct the process, falsely incriminates himself for having committed a crime subject to public prosecution or a crime of the same nature perpetrated by another, shall be punished with community service from one month to one year.  
If the act were executed in the interest of a close relative or a person of intimate friendship, the perpetrator may be exempted from punishment.

###### Art. 169.- (False Testimony)

A witness, expert, interpreter, translator or any other person who is questioned in a judicial or administrative proceeding, who affirms a falsehood or denies or conceals the truth, in whole or in part of what he knows about the fact or what concerns it, shall incur imprisonment from one to fifteen months.  
If the false testimony were committed in criminal proceedings, to the detriment of the accused, the penalty shall be deprivation of liberty from one to three years.  
When false testimony is perpetrated by means of bribery, the preceding penalty shall be increased by one third.

###### Art. 170.- (Bribery)

Whoever offers or promises money or any other appreciable advantage to the persons referred to in the preceding article, with the purpose of obtaining false testimony, even if the offer or promise has not been accepted or, if accepted, the falsehood was not committed, shall incur imprisonment from one to two years and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 171.- (Concealment)

Whoever, after a crime has been committed, without prior promise, helps someone to elude the action of justice or omits to report the act being obligated to do so, shall incur imprisonment from six months to two years.

###### Art. 172.- (Receiving Stolen Goods)

Whoever, after a crime has been committed, helps someone to secure the benefit or result thereof or receives, conceals, sells or purchases knowingly the instruments that served to commit the crime or the things obtained by criminal means, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years.

###### Article 185 ter

The Financial Investigations Unit is hereby created, which shall form part of the organizational structure of the Superintendency of Banks and Financial Entities. The directors, managers, administrators or officers responsible for reporting possible cases of legitimation of illicit gains to the Financial Investigations Unit shall be exempt from administrative, civil and criminal liability, provided that the report complies with the norms established in the regulatory decree.  
The highest executive authority of the Financial Investigations Unit shall substantiate the determination of administrative liability and the Superintendent shall apply the consequent sanctions, subject to the legally established regime. To determine the appropriate sanction, the seriousness of the breach and the degree of participation and culpability of the responsible subjects shall be taken into account. In these cases, the regime of challenges and appeals of its resolutions shall be subject to that established by law.  
Financial entities and their organs may not invoke banking secrecy when agents of the Financial Investigations Unit require information for the adequate fulfillment of their functions. ###### Art. 186.- (Counterfeiting of currency)

Whoever counterfeits metallic currency or paper money of legal tender, national or foreign, by manufacturing, altering or clipping it, and whoever introduces, spends or puts it into circulation, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from two to eight years.  
Art 187.- (Circulation of counterfeit currency received in good faith).  
Whoever, having received counterfeit currency in good faith, puts it into circulation with knowledge of the falsity, shall be punished with a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 188.- (Equivalence of securities to currency)

For the purposes of criminal law, the following are equivalent to currency:
1)
Banknotes legally authorized.  
2)
National debt bonds.  
3)
Bearer titles, certificates and shares, legally issued by Banks, entities, companies or corporations authorized to do so.  
4)
Checks.

###### Art. 189.- (Illegal issuance)

The person in charge of the issuance or manufacture of currency, who knowingly authorizes, issues or manufactures currency that does not comply with legal requirements, or puts into circulation currency that no longer has legal tender, shall incur imprisonment from one to five years.  
The same penalty shall apply to whoever issues bearer titles, certificates or shares in an amount greater than that authorized.

##### CHAPTER II

STAMPED PAPER, STAMPS,
MARKS AND COUNTERSIGNS

###### Art. 190.- (Counterfeiting of seals, stamped paper and stamps)

Whoever counterfeits official seals, stamped paper, official lottery tickets, postage stamps, any stamped effect or printed forms, the issuance of which is reserved to the authority, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from one to six years.  
The same sanction shall be imposed on whoever knowingly introduces, spends or uses them.

###### Art. 191.- (Fraudulent printing of official seal)

Whoever fraudulently prints an authentic official seal shall be punished with imprisonment from one to three years.

###### Art. 192.- (Receipt in good faith)

Whoever, having received in good faith the securities and effects indicated in Art. 190, and knowing thereafter of their falsity, introduces or puts them into circulation, shall be punished with a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 193.- (Counterfeiting and improper application of marks and countersigns)

Whoever counterfeits marks, countersigns or signatures officially used to verify weights or measures, identify any object or certify its quality, quantity or content, and whoever applies them to objects other than those to which they should be applied, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to three years.  
The same sanction shall be incurred by whoever performs the same acts affecting private factories or establishments.

###### Art. 194.- (Counterfeiting of tickets of public transport companies)

Whoever counterfeits or alters tickets of public or private transport companies shall be punished with imprisonment from one to six months or a fine of twenty to one hundred twenty days.  
Whoever introduces, spends or puts them into circulation shall incur the same sanction.

###### Art. 195.- (Counterfeiting of admission tickets)

Whoever counterfeits or alters, for profit, admission tickets or tickets that permit access to a public spectacle, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to six months or a fine of twenty to one hundred twenty days.

###### Art. 196.- (Use of already used items)

Whoever, with the purpose of using or selling seals, stamps, marks, countersigns or other stamped effects, causes the sign indicating their use to disappear, shall be punished with a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 197.- (Instruments for counterfeiting)

Whoever manufactures, introduces into the country, keeps in his possession or trades in materials or instruments unequivocally intended to commit any of the counterfeitings legislated in the two preceding chapters, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years.

Whoever inserts or causes to be inserted in a true public instrument false declarations concerning a fact that the document must prove, in such a way that harm may result, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from one to six years.

###### Art. 240.- (Bigamy)

Whoever contracts a new marriage knowing that the previous one to which he was bound is not dissolved, shall incur deprivation of liberty from two to four years.

###### Art. 241.- (Other illegal marriages)

The following shall be punished:
1)
With deprivation of liberty from one to three years, whoever not being married knowingly contracts marriage with a married person.  
2)

3)
With deprivation of liberty from two to four years, whoever has concealed a legal impediment regarding his own civil status or that of the other contracting party.

###### Art. 242.- (Liability of the Civil Registry officer)

The Civil Registry officer who knowingly authorizes a marriage of those described in articles 240 and 241, or proceeds to the celebration of a marriage without having observed the formalities required by law, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from two to six years.

###### Art. 243.- (Simulation of marriage)

Whoever attributes to himself authority for the celebration of a marriage, or whoever simulates marriage through deception, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from two to six years.

###### Art. 244.- (Alteration or substitution of civil status)

The following shall incur imprisonment from one to five years:
1)
Whoever has a non-existent person registered in the Civil Registry.  
2)
Whoever in the birth registry has false facts inserted that alter the civil status or order of a newborn.  
3)
Whoever by concealment, substitution or exposure, even if it does not involve abandonment, leaves a newborn without civil status, makes uncertain or alters the one that corresponds to him.  
4)
She who feigns pregnancy or childbirth to give a supposed child rights that do not correspond to him.  
If the Civil Registry officer knowingly authorizes the registrations referred to in subsections 1) and 2), the penalty for him shall be increased by one third.

###### Art. 248.- (Abandonment of family)

Whoever without just cause fails to fulfill the obligations of sustenance, housing, clothing, education and assistance inherent to parental authority, guardianship or status as spouse or cohabitant, or abandons the family domicile or evades compliance with the indicated obligations, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years or a fine of one hundred to four hundred days.

###### Art. 249.- (Breach of duties of assistance)

The father, guardian, curator of a minor or incapacitated person shall incur deprivation of liberty from six months to two years, and shall be disqualified from exercising parental authority, guardianship or curatorship, in the following cases:
1)
If he fails without just cause to provide for the primary education of a minor of school age.  
2)
If he permits the minor to frequent gambling houses or houses of ill repute or to cohabit with a vicious or immoral person.  
3)
If he permits the minor to frequent shows capable of corrupting him or that offend decency, or for the minor to participate in performances of a similar nature.  
4)
If he authorizes the minor to reside or work in a house of prostitution.  
5)
If he permits the minor to beg or to serve a beggar to inspire compassion.

###### Art. 282.- (Defamation)

Whoever publicly, tendentiously and repeatedly reveals or discloses a fact, a quality or a conduct capable of affecting the reputation of an individual or collective person, shall incur community service of one month to one year or a fine of twenty to two hundred forty days.

###### Art. 283.- (Slander)

Whoever by any means falsely imputes to another the commission of a crime, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty of six months to two years and a fine of one hundred to three hundred days.

###### Art. 284.- (Offense to the memory of the deceased)

Whoever offends the memory of a deceased person with defamatory expressions or slanderous imputations, shall incur the same penalties as the two preceding articles.

###### Art. 285.- (Dissemination of offenses)

Whoever disseminates or reproduces by any means the acts referred to in articles 282, 283 and 284, shall be sanctioned as the author thereof.

###### Art. 286.- (Exception of truth)

The author of defamation and slander shall not be punishable if the imputations consist of true statements, but the accused may only prove the truth of the imputation:
1)
When it concerns offenses directed at a public official and with reference to his functions.  
2)
When the complainant requests proof of the imputation, provided that such proof does not affect the rights or secrets of a third person.

###### Art. 287.- (Insult)

Whoever by any means and in a direct manner offends another in his dignity or decorum, shall incur community service of one month to one year and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.  
If the act provided for in Art. 283 and the insult referred to in this article are committed by means of printed, typewritten or handwritten material, its author shall be considered guilty of defamatory libel and sanctioned with a fine of sixty to one hundred fifty days, without prejudice to the corresponding penalties.

###### Art. 288.- (Prohibition of proof)

Proof shall not be admitted except in the cases indicated in article 286.

###### Art. 289.- (Retraction)

The person accused of a crime against honor shall be exempt from penalty, if he retracts before or at the time of giving his statement.  
A second retraction concerning the same act shall not be admitted.

###### Art. 290.- (Reciprocal offenses)

If the offenses or imputations are reciprocal, the judge may, according to the circumstances, exempt both parties or either of them from penalty.

###### Art. 300.- (Violation of correspondence and private papers)

Whoever unlawfully opens a letter, a sealed document or a telegraphic, radiotelegraphic or telephone communication, addressed to another person, or whoever, without opening the correspondence, by technical means learns of its content, shall be punished with imprisonment of three months to one year or a fine of sixty to two hundred forty days.

###### Art. 301.- (Violation of secrets in correspondence not intended for publication)

Whoever records the words of another not intended for the public, without his consent, or whoever by technical procedures listens to private statements not addressed to him, or whoever does the same with private papers or with epistolary or telegraphic correspondence even if they had been addressed to him, provided that the act may cause some harm, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty of three months to one year.

###### Art. 302.- (Disclosure of professional secret)

Whoever having knowledge of secrets by virtue of his status, ministry, profession, employment, occupation, art or commission, discloses them without just cause, or uses them for his own or another's benefit, if any harm follows therefrom, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty of three months to one year and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Article 342. (Deception of Incapacitated Persons)

Whoever, in order to obtain for himself or for others some benefit, abusing the needs, passions or inexperience of a person under eighteen years of age or abusing the state of illness or mental deficiency of a person, even if not under interdiction or incapacitated, induces such person to perform an act that implies some legal effect detrimental to him or her or to others, shall incur deprivation of liberty from three to eight years.

###### Art. 343.- (Bankruptcy)

A penalty of deprivation of liberty from two to six years shall be imposed on the merchant, company agent or legal representatives who exercise commerce in the name of minors or incapacitated persons, whose bankruptcy is declared fraudulent in accordance with the Commercial Code.  
If the bankruptcy is declared culpable, the sanction shall be reduced by one third.  
Accomplices and instigators who knowingly induce, before or after the declaration of bankruptcy, to perform the unlawful acts referred to in the Commercial Code shall incur half of the penalty established in this article.

###### Art. 344.- (Absconding with assets or civil insolvency)

Whoever, not being a merchant, absconds with his assets or conceals them or commits other fraud, with the purpose of harming his creditors, shall incur deprivation of liberty from two to six years.

##### CAPITULO IX

USURY

###### Art. 360.- (Usury)

Whoever, taking advantage of the need, lightness or inexperience of a person, gives in any form, for himself or for others, values or species in exchange for interest higher than that fixed by law or other pecuniary advantages evidently disproportionate to the consideration, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three months to two years and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.  
The same penalty shall apply to whoever knowingly acquires, transfers or enforces a usurious credit, or to the intermediary, front man or cooperator.

###### Art. 361.- (Aggravated usury)

The sanction shall be aggravated by one half and a fine of up to one hundred days:
1)
If the perpetrator were a professional or habitual usurious lender or commission agent.  
2)
When any artifice or deceit has been employed to obtain the consent of the victim.  
3)
If the act were concealed by means of other forms of contract, even as a penalty clause that fixes interest.  
4)
If the act constitutes any of the forms of compound interest.

###### Artículo 363

quater. (FINANCIAL CRIMES).  
A natural or legal person, through their legal representative, commits a financial crime when by action or omission they incur any of the criminal offenses detailed below::
a)
Financial Intermediation without Authorization or License.  
Whoever, on their own behalf or on behalf of another, directly or indirectly, carries out financial intermediation activities without having prior authorization from the Supervisory Authority of the Financial System, shall incur deprivation of liberty of five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days.  
b)
Improper Use of Influence for Granting Credit.  
The director(s), member of the board of directors and supervisory board, executive or official of a financial intermediation entity, who with the intention of favoring themselves or the entity in some way or obtaining economic benefits for themselves or a third party, knowingly authorizes or approves the granting of credits to borrowers or borrower groups linked to the entity, shall incur deprivation of liberty of three (3) to eight (8) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to three hundred (300) days.  
If as a result of this activity damage is caused to third parties or to the entity itself, the penalty shall be increased by one half.  
c)
Misappropriation of Financial Funds.  
Whoever without authorization and through the use of technological means or other fraudulent maneuvers, takes possession of or procures the transfer of funds, whether for their own benefit or that of third parties, shall incur deprivation of liberty of five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days.  
When the offense is committed by an employee of the financial entity taking advantage of their position or the error of another, the penalty shall be increased by one half.  
d)
Forging of Illicit Financial Results.  
Whoever with the purpose of procuring an undue benefit, carries out fraudulent maneuvers to alter the price of negotiable securities or public offerings by disguising or concealing true facts or circumstances or affirming or suggesting false or misleading facts or circumstances shall incur deprivation of liberty of five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days.  
The penalty shall be increased by half for whoever with the intention of obtaining a benefit for themselves or for a third party or harming another market participant, causes the price to rise, fall or remain stable, or facilitates the sale or purchase of securities, through the affirmation or simulation of false facts or circumstances or the distortion or concealment of true facts or circumstances, in such a way as to induce error about the essential characteristics of the investment.  
e)
Falsification of Accounting Documentation.  
Whoever knowingly or with the purpose of concealing situations of illiquidity or insolvency of a financial entity or complementary financial services company, materially or ideologically falsifies the financial statements of the entity, accounting entries or other financial information shall incur deprivation of liberty of five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days.  
f)
Dissemination of False Financial Information.  
The individual who by any means disseminates or commissions the dissemination of false information about the Bolivian financial system or its entities, which induces or provokes the massive withdrawal of deposits from one or more financial intermediation entities, incites or induces clients not to comply with acquired financial commitments, damaging or deteriorating the image and stability of a financial intermediation entity or the national financial system. Studies, analyses and opinions of a scientific nature that, based on authentic and verifiable information, are oriented to evaluate or qualify the financial system or its actors, seeking to maximize its efficiency and development, are excluded from the scope of this subsection, shall incur deprivation of liberty of five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days.
