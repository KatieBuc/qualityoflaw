Basis and limitation of criminal law coercion

## Article 1

Criminal offences and criminal law sanctions are prescribed only for those behaviours which violate or endanger personal freedoms and human rights and other rights and social values guaranteed and protected by the Constitution of the Republic of Croatia and international law to such an extent that their protection could not be achieved without criminal law coercion.  
Principle of legality

## Article 2

No one may be punished for an act which, before it was committed, was not defined by law or international law as a criminal offence, nor may a penalty or other criminal law sanction be imposed on them which was not prescribed by law.

Place of commission of a criminal offence

## Article 9

(1) A criminal offence is committed in the place where the perpetrator acted or was obliged to act and in the place where the consequence from the statutory description of the criminal offence occurred in whole or in part or was supposed to occur according to their intention.  
(2) In cases of complicity, the criminal offence is committed in the place specified in paragraph 1 of this Article and in the place where any of the participants acted or was obliged to act or where, according to their intention, the consequence from the statutory description of the criminal offence was supposed to occur.

## Article 10

Application of criminal legislation for criminal offences committed outside the Republic of Croatia against its legal interest

## Article 13

The criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia applies to anyone who commits outside its territory:
1. a criminal offence against the Republic of Croatia from Chapter XXXII of this Code,
2. a criminal offence of counterfeiting money, securities and tokens of value of the Republic of Croatia from Article 274, Article 275 and Article 276 of this Code,
3. a criminal offence against a Croatian state official or civil servant in connection with their service,
4. a criminal offence of giving false testimony from Article 305 of this Code, if the false testimony was given in proceedings before Croatian competent authorities,
5. criminal offences against electoral rights from Chapter XXXI of this Code,
6. a criminal offence from Article 193, Article 194, Article 196, Article 197 and Article 198 of this Code when committed in the protected ecological and fisheries zone, the continental shelf or on the high seas.  
Application of criminal legislation for criminal offences committed outside the territory of the Republic of Croatia by its nationals
(1) The criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia applies to a Croatian national and a person who has residence in the Republic of Croatia, who commits outside the territory of the Republic of Croatia any other criminal offence except those covered by the provisions of Article 13 and Article 16 of this Code, if that criminal offence is also punishable under the law of the state in which it was committed.  
(2) The provision of paragraph 1 of this Article shall also apply when the perpetrator acquires Croatian citizenship after the commission of the criminal offence.  
(3) In the cases referred to in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article, for criminal offences from Article 115 paragraphs 3 and 4, Article 116, Article 153, Article 154, Article 158, Article 159, Article 161, Article 162, Article 163, Article 164, Article 166 and Article 169 of this Code and other criminal offences where this is provided for by an international treaty to which the Republic of Croatia is a party, the criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall be applied even when the criminal offence is not punishable under the law of the state in which it was committed.  
(4) When Croatian nationals participate in peace operations or other international activities outside the territory of the Republic of Croatia and commit a criminal offence in such operations or activities, the application of the legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall be governed by the provisions of this Code, unless otherwise provided by an international treaty to which the Republic of Croatia is a party.  
Application of criminal legislation for criminal offences committed against a national of the Republic of Croatia outside its territory

## Article 15

(1) The criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall apply to a foreigner who, outside the territory of the Republic of Croatia, commits against a citizen of the Republic of Croatia, a person who has residence in the Republic of Croatia or a legal person registered in the Republic of Croatia, any criminal offence not covered by the provisions of Article 13 and Article 16 of this Act, if that criminal offence is punishable also under the law of the state in which it was committed.  
(2) In the case referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article, the court may not impose a more severe penalty than that prescribed by the law of the country in which the criminal offence was committed.

## Article 16

Application of criminal legislation for other criminal offences committed outside the territory of the Republic of Croatia

## Article 17

(1) The criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall apply to a foreigner who, outside the territory of the Republic of Croatia, commits a criminal offence for which, under Croatian legislation, a prison sentence of five years or a more severe penalty may be imposed, and it is not a case referred to in Articles 13 to 16 of this Act, if that criminal offence is punishable also under the law of the state in which it was committed and if the extradition of the perpetrator is permitted by law or international treaty, but has not taken place.  
(2) In the case referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article, the court may not impose a more severe penalty than that prescribed by the law of the country in which the criminal offence was committed.

Crediting of deprivation of liberty and executed criminal sanctions in a foreign state

## Article 19

In cases of application of the criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia, time spent in detention, investigative detention or serving a sentence, as well as any other deprivation of liberty in a foreign state, shall be credited towards the prison sentence imposed by the domestic court for the same criminal offence. Other executed criminal sanctions shall be credited according to the fair assessment of the court.  
Manner of committing a criminal offence

## Article 20

(1) A criminal offence may be committed by an act or an omission.  
(2) Whoever fails to prevent the occurrence of a consequence of a criminal offence described by law shall be liable for an omission if legally obliged to prevent the occurrence of such consequence and if the omission is, in its effect and significance, equivalent to the commission of that act by an act.  
(3) A perpetrator who has committed a criminal offence by omission may be punished more leniently, unless it is a criminal offence that can only be committed by omission.

Extreme necessity

## Article 22

(1) The unlawfulness of an act committed in order to avert from oneself or another a simultaneous danger which could not be averted in another way is excluded if the harm done is less than that which was threatened.  
(2) A person who commits an unlawful act in order to avert from oneself or another a simultaneous danger not caused by fault which could not be averted in another way is not guilty if the harm done was not disproportionately greater than the harm which was threatened and if the person was not obliged to expose himself or herself to the danger. Elements of guilt

## Article 23

A perpetrator is guilty of a criminal offence if, at the time of committing the criminal offence, he or she was of sound mind, acted with intent or negligence, was aware or was obliged and able to be aware that his or her act was prohibited, and no excusing reason exists.

Genocide

## Article 88

(1) Whoever, with the aim of destroying, in whole or in part, a national, ethnic, racial or religious group as such:
1. kills members of the group,
2. causes serious bodily injury to members of the group or seriously impairs their health,
3. places the group in living conditions aimed at bringing about its complete or partial destruction,
4. applies measures aimed at preventing births within the group, or

shall be punished by imprisonment of at least ten years or by long-term imprisonment.

3. blockade of ports or coasts of one State by the armed forces of another State,

(1) A military person who does not take prescribed, ordered or other clearly necessary measures for preserving the life and health of people entrusted to him, for securing and maintaining in proper condition facilities, objects and means serving combat readiness, for proper supply of food, equipment and material to the unit entrusted to him, for guarding and care of official animals, or for timely and proper performance of securing works or securing of facilities entrusted to him, thereby causing danger to the life or body of people,
shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2) If the criminal offence from paragraph 1 of this Article caused serious bodily injury to a person or significant property damage,
the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(3) If the criminal offence from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article was committed during a state of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia,
the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  
(4) If the criminal offence from paragraph 2 of this Article was committed through negligence the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years, and if the criminal offence from paragraph 3 of this Article was committed through negligence by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
Failure to secure at military exercises

## Article 366

(1) Whoever at a military exercise, training or in the performance of tests does not take prescribed, ordered or clearly necessary measures of securing or precaution, thereby causing danger to the life or body of people,
shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2) If the criminal offence from paragraph 1 of this Article caused serious bodily injury to a person, or significant property damage, or if the criminal offence from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed during a state of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia,
the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(3) If the criminal offence from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed through negligence,
the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(4) If the criminal offence from paragraph 3 of this Article caused serious bodily injury to a person, or significant property damage, or if the criminal offence from paragraph 3 of this Article was committed during a state of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia,
the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
Failure to respond to call and evasion of military service

## Article 367

(1) Whoever during a state of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia without justified reason does not arrive at the specified time for performance of military obligation although he was called by individual or general call,
shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2) Whoever calls or incites multiple persons to commit the criminal offence from paragraph 1 of this Article,
shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(3) The perpetrator of the criminal offence from paragraph 1 of this Article who voluntarily reports to the competent state body for performance of military obligation may be exempted from punishment.  
Evasion of military obligation by incapacitation or fraud

## Članak 368

(1) Whoever, with the aim that he or another person avoids military obligation or is assigned to lighter duty, injures himself or another person with that person's consent, or otherwise incapacitates himself or another for military service or any duty, or allows another person to incapacitate him for military service or any duty,
shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2) The punishment from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be imposed on whoever, with the aim that he or another person avoids military obligation or is assigned to lighter duty, simulates illness, uses a false document for himself or another, or acts in another fraudulent manner.  
(3) Whoever commits the act from paragraph 1 of this Article in time of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia,
shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
Unlawful exemption from military service

## Članak 369

Whoever, by abuse of his position or authority, causes a military person or a person subject to military obligation to be exempted from duty or to be assigned to lighter duty in time of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia,
shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
Improper and negligent handling of entrusted armament and military equipment and other means for defence needs

## Članak 370

(1) Whoever improperly or negligently holds, keeps or handles entrusted armament, military equipment and other means for defence needs, thereby causing their damage to a greater extent, destruction or disappearance,
shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.  
(2) A warehouse manager of the means from paragraph 1 of this Article who fails to take measures for their security or maintenance, thereby causing damage, destruction or disappearance of those means,
shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(3) If the criminal offence from paragraph 2 of this Article has caused substantial property damage,
the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
(4) If the criminal offence from paragraph 2 of this Article was committed through negligence,
the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(5) If the criminal offence from paragraph 4 of this Article has caused the consequence from paragraph 3 of this Article,
the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
Unauthorised entry into military facilities and making sketches or drawings of military facilities and combat means

## Članak 371

(1) Whoever, in time of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, for the purpose of reconnaissance, enters a military facility without authorisation, although he knows that access is prohibited,
shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2) Whoever, in time of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, without authorisation makes sketches or drawings of military facilities or combat means, or photographs them, or otherwise records them,
shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
Crossing over and surrender to the enemy

## Članak 372

(1) A military person who during war or armed conflict crosses over to the ranks of the enemy, or the opposing side,
shall be punished by imprisonment of at least five years.  
(2) A military person who during war surrenders to the enemy, or the opposing side, before having exhausted all possibilities of defence,
shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  
Failure to perform or abandonment of duty during combat operations

## Članak 373

(1) A military person who during or immediately before combat operations fails to perform his duty, thereby causing harmful consequences for a military unit or for the combat situation,
shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  
(2) The punishment from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be imposed on a military person who during or immediately before combat operations fails to perform his duty or arbitrarily or fraudulently abandons his duty.  
Abandonment of position contrary to orders

## Članak 374

A military person who during or immediately before combat operations, contrary to orders, abandons a position with the unit entrusted to him before having exhausted all possibilities of defence,
shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.  
Abandonment of damaged ship or aircraft before time

## Članak 375

The commander of a warship or a crew member of a warship or military aircraft who during war or armed conflict abandons a damaged ship or aircraft before having performed his duty according to regulations on ship service, or according to regulations on flying and use of aircraft,
shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
Leaving undamaged combat means to the enemy

## Članak 376

(1) A military person who allows a substantially undamaged military warehouse, ship, aircraft, tank, or other similar combat means to fall into the hands of the enemy,
shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2) The punishment from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be imposed on whoever, contrary to orders, allows substantially undamaged installations or other facilities important for defence to fall into the hands of the enemy.  
Weakening of combat morale and combat situation

## Članak 377

(1) A military person who during or immediately before combat operations, by throwing away weapons or ammunition, spreading fear, creating disorder or confusion, or in another manner weakens the combat morale of a unit, combat readiness or causes harm to the combat situation,
shall be punished by imprisonment from three to twelve years.  
(2) A military person who fails to take necessary measures against a subordinate who during or immediately before combat operations spreads fear among soldiers, creates disorder or confusion in a unit, or in another manner weakens the combat morale of a unit or causes harm to the combat situation,
shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.  
Failure to fulfil material obligation

## Članak 378

(1) Whoever, in time of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, contrary to an obligation established by law, without justified reason, fails to respond or resists the registration or inspection of personnel or the registration or inspection of material means that are on the list of material means for defence needs, or whoever during such registration or inspection gives inaccurate statements or data,
shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
(2) The punishment from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be imposed on whoever, in time of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, contrary to an obligation established by law, without justified reason, fails to make available at the specified time and in the prescribed condition to the body competent for defence matters material means that are on the list of material means for defence needs.  
Failure to fulfil duty in the implementation of mobilisation

## Članak 379

(1) A military or official person who, in the implementation of mobilisation in time of war or immediate threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, contrary to his duty, fails to ensure the reception, deployment and accommodation or supply of mobilised personnel, facilities, vehicles, machinery, equipment and other means that are on the list of material means for defence needs, thereby causing serious harmful consequences,
shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.  
(2) If the criminal offence from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed through negligence,
the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.  
Responsibility for a criminal offence committed on the order of a superior

## Članak 380

A military person who commits an unlawful act that fulfils the elements of a criminal offence on order is guilty only if he knew or it was obvious that by executing the order he was committing an unlawful act. The criminal offences from Articles 88 and 90 of this Law are always obviously unlawful.
