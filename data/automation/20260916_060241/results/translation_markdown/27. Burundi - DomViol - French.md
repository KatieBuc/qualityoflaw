# [NB - Law No. 1/27 of 29 December 2017 revising the Penal Code]

[NB - Law No. 1/27 of 29 December 2017 revising the Penal Code]

## Book 1 - Offences and punishment in general

### Title 1 - General provisions Chapter 1 - Offences in general

###### Art.1

An offence is an action or omission that manifests itself as an infringement of the social order and which the law punishes by a penalty.

###### Art.2

Only the law defines the constituent elements of an offence and determines the applicable penalties.

###### Art.3.- The criminal law is to be strictly interpreted

###### Art.4

No offence may be punished by penalties that were not provided for by law before the offence was committed.  
However, in the event of a conflict between two criminal laws, one old under which the offence was committed and the other promulgated since the offence and before a final judgment has been rendered, the new law alone must be applied if it prescribes a less severe penalty.

###### Art.5

The following apply immediately to offences committed before their entry into force:
1° Laws on jurisdiction and judicial organization, as long as a judgment on the merits has not been rendered at first instance;  
2° Laws establishing the procedures for prosecution and the forms of procedure;  

3° Laws relating to the regimes for application and execution of penalties insofar as they provide for more favourable conditions for the convicted person;  
4° Laws relating to the limitation of public prosecution and penalties if they provide for shorter time limits;  
5° Laws having as their object the decriminalization of acts.

###### Art.6

Remedies are undertaken and procedural time limits are calculated according to the laws in force at the time the acts concerned are performed.

###### Art.7

The immediate application of the new law has no effect on the validity of acts performed in accordance with the old law.

###### Art.8

Any offence committed on the territory of Burundi by Burundians or foreigners is, subject to international conventions on diplomatic and consular immunities, punished in accordance with the criminal law of Burundi.  
Diplomatic or consular immunities do not apply in the case of the crime of genocide, crime against humanity or war crime.

###### Art.9

Offences committed on board boats, ships, trains or aircraft registered in Burundi or abroad and carrying out their activity in Burundi or against them are punished in accordance with the criminal law of Burundi.

###### Art.10

Any misdemeanour or crime committed outside the national territory by a Burundian or a foreigner is, subject to conventions on extradition, punished by the criminal law of Burundi if the perpetrator is in Burundi or if the victim has Burundian nationality and the act is punished by the legislation of the country where the offence was committed.  
In offences other than those relating to the counterfeiting of State seals and national currencies, those relating to acts of torture, terrorism, genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes, the prosecution and trial of offences committed abroad are subject to the filing of a complaint by the injured party or to the official denunciation by the authority of the country where the offence was committed.  
The jurisdiction of Burundian courts, with regard to the crime of genocide, crime against humanity and war crimes, is not subject to these crimes being punished by the legislation of the country where they were committed nor to conventions on extradition.

###### Art.11

When the offence was committed abroad, no prosecution takes place if the accused proves that he has been finally tried and in the case of conviction, that he has served or his sentence has been time-barred, obtained his pardon or benefited from amnesty.

#### Chapter 2 - Classification of offences

###### Art.12

According to the degree of their gravity, offences are classified as crimes, misdemeanours, or contraventions.  
Offences punishable by a maximum of two months of penal servitude are contraventions.  
Offences whose penalty is between two months and five years of penal servitude are misdemeanours.  
Offences punishable by more than five years of penal servitude are crimes.

###### Art.13

When the penalty punishing an offence is expressed by a minimum and a maximum, only the latter is taken into consideration for the application of the provisions of the preceding article.  
When the punishment of an offence is increased by the effect of aggravating circumstances, the maximum of the aggravated penalty alone is taken into consideration for the application of the provisions of the preceding article.  
When the penalty incurred by the perpetrator of the offence is increased by the effect of recidivism, this increase is not taken into consideration for the application of the provisions of the preceding article.

#### Chapter 3 - Attempt

###### Art.14

There is a punishable attempt when the resolution to commit the offence has been manifested by external acts which form the commencement of execution of that offence and which were suspended or which failed to have their effect only by circumstances independent of the will of the perpetrator.

###### Art.15

An attempted offence is punished by half the penalty of the completed misdemeanour or crime.  
If the completed offence is punished by penal servitude for life, the perpetrator of the attempt is punished by fifteen years of penal servitude.

###### Art.16

There is an impossible attempt when a potential offender has done everything in his power to commit an offence, when it could not be realized due to an impossibility of which he was unaware.  
An impossible attempt is punished by a quarter of the penalty of the failed offence.  
If the offence is punished by penal servitude for life, the perpetrator of the impossible attempt is punished by ten years of penal servitude.

###### Art.17

An attempted contravention is punishable only in cases determined by law.

#### Chapter 4 - Criminal liability

##### Section 1 - Principle

###### Art.18

Criminal liability is personal: no one is punishable except by reason of his own act, without prejudice to the special provisions appearing in this Code.

###### Art.19

The perpetrator of the offence is the one who personally commits the various material and intellectual elements as defined by law.

###### Art.20

The intellectual perpetrator is the one who conceives the offence and has all or certain material acts carried out by a third party.

###### Art.21

With the exception of those cited in Article 24, legal persons are criminally liable for offences committed by their directors or legal representatives acting on behalf of these persons or in the defence of their interests or on the occasion of any other act closely linked to their corporate purpose.

###### Art.22

The criminal liability of legal persons does not exclude that of natural persons who are perpetrators or accomplices of the same acts.

###### Art.23

The following are assimilated to the legal persons referred to in Article 21: 1° Temporary associations;  
2° Civil or commercial companies in formation;  
3° Non-profit or mutualist associations in formation; 4° De facto associations.

###### Art.24

The following cannot be considered as legal persons for the application of Article 21: the State, Communes and Public Establishments of a commercial, industrial, administrative and/or scientific nature.

##### Section 2

Subjective causes of criminal irresponsibility or mitigation of penalty

###### Art.25

A person is not punishable if they suffered from a mental illness or deficiency that deprived them of the ability to understand the wrongful nature or the nature of their conduct, or to control it in order to conform to the requirements of the law.

###### Art.26

However, a person who voluntarily deprived themselves of the use of their mental faculties at the time of the offence remains criminally liable, even if this deprivation was not caused for the purpose of committing the offence.

###### Art.27

A person who acted under duress of a force which they could not resist is not punishable.  
However, duress can never be used as an argument by the defence in cases of genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes and other crimes falling under international law, but it may only be taken into account for a reduction of sentence.

###### Art.28

Minors under fifteen years of age are criminally irresponsible. Offences committed by them give rise only to civil reparations.

###### Art.29

When the perpetrator or accomplice of an offence is a minor of fifteen years of age or over and under eighteen years of age at the time of the offence, the sentences are pronounced as follows:
1° If they should have incurred the penalty of penal servitude for life, they are sentenced to a penalty of five to ten years of principal penal servitude;  
2° If they have incurred a fixed-term sentence or a fine, the penalties that may be pronounced against them may not exceed four years.

###### Art.30

The measures of protection, education and supervision that may be pronounced against a minor are as follows:
1° Warning; 2° Reminder of the law;  
3° Return to parents, guardian or a trusted person; 4° Educational assistance;  
5° Placement in a social institution, a school establishment or another authorized educational institution.  
At the same time as pronouncing a principal penalty other than penal servitude, the judge hearing the case may grant the minor the benefit of educational assistance or order placement in a foster family or in an authorized institution that it determines.  
The judge hearing the case may at any time, either on their own motion, or at the request of the Public Prosecutor, the parents or legal representatives, or on the report of the social worker, modify the measures of protection, supervision or education taken with regard to the minor or terminate them.

##### Section 3 - Des causes objectives d'irresponsabilité pénale

###### Art.31.- Il n'y a pas d'infraction

1° When the act was ordered or authorized by law or commanded by legitimate authority, unless the act was manifestly illegal.  
However, hierarchical order can never be used as an argument by the defence in cases of genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes and other crimes falling under international law, but it may only be taken into account for a reduction of sentence.  
2° In case of necessity, which is the position of a person who, faced with a serious and imminent danger to themselves, others or property, or in order to interrupt a crime or an offence, commits an act that falls under criminal law in order to ensure the safeguarding of an interest superior to that sacrificed. The means employed for this purpose must be proportional to the seriousness of the threat.  
However, necessity can never be used as an argument by the defence in cases of genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes and other crimes falling under international law, but it may only be taken into account for a reduction of sentence.  
The offences provided for under the chapter on voluntary homicides are not covered by the content of point 2°.  
3° In case of self-defence which is the reaction of a person who, faced with an unjustified aggression against themselves or others, performs an act that falls under criminal law, provided that the means used are proportional to the seriousness of the aggression.

##### Section 4 - Des excuses

###### Art.32

No crime or offence may be excused except in cases determined by law.

###### Art.33

Legal excuses leave the offence and liability intact, but ensure for offenders either impunity when they are exculpatory, or a moderation of the penalty when they are mitigating.

#### Chapitre 5 - Des circonstances atténuantes

###### Art.34

The judge has sovereign discretion to assess the circumstances which, prior to, concurrent with or subsequent to the offence, mitigate the culpability of its perpetrator.  

However, decisions concerning the reduction of sentence must not be taken at the entire discretion of judges but only in accordance with appropriate factors, taking into account mitigating circumstances.

###### Art.35

The decision that admits mitigating circumstances indicates, enumerates and gives reasons for them.

###### Art.36

If there are mitigating circumstances, the penalties of penal servitude and fine may be reduced to the extent determined by the judge.

#### Chapitre 6

De la participation à l'infraction Art.37.- Sont considérés comme auteurs:
1° Those who, personally, took direct part in the execution of the offence or cooperated directly in its execution;  
2° Those who, by any act, provided assistance for the execution such that, without their assistance, the offence could not have been committed.

###### Art.38

Are considered accomplices to an offence, those who, without direct participation in it and without their contribution being indispensable, have:
1° Incited to action by gift, promise, threats, abuse of authority and power, culpable machinations or artifices or given instructions to commit it;  
2° Procured weapons, instruments or any other means that served the action knowing that it was to serve that purpose;  
3° With knowledge, aided by any means or assisted the perpetrator or perpetrators of the action in the acts that prepared or facilitated it or in those that consummated it;  
4° With knowledge of their criminal conduct, habitually provided lodging, place of retreat or meeting to one or more wrongdoers;  
5° Either by incitement to commit the offence through speeches delivered in meetings or in public places, or through writings or printed matter sold or distributed, offered for sale or displayed in public places or meetings or through placards or posters displayed to public view, directly provoked the perpetrator or perpetrators to commit this action;  
6°, Concealed or aided wrongdoers under the conditions provided for in article 319.

###### Art.39

A person who, intentionally, induced another person to commit an offence incurs, if it has been committed, the penalty applicable to the perpetrator of the offence.

###### Art.40

When the offence has not been committed solely due to the voluntary abstention of the person who was to commit it, the instigator incurs half of the penalty provided for that offence.

###### Art.41

Except for particular provisions establishing other penalties, co-perpetrators and accomplices are punished as follows:
1° Co-perpetrators, with the penalty established by law with regard to perpetrators;  
2° Accomplices, with a penalty that does not exceed half of that which they would have incurred if they had themselves been perpetrators;  
3° When the penalty provided by law is penal servitude for life, the penalty applicable to the accomplice is twenty years of penal servitude.

###### Art.42

Personal circumstances from which result the aggravation, mitigation or exemption of penalty, have effect only with regard to the sole participant to whom they relate.

###### Art.43

Objective circumstances inherent to the offence that aggravate or diminish the penalty of those who participated in that offence have effect against them or in their favour depending on whether or not they had knowledge of them.

### Title 2 - Penalties in general Chapter 1 - Classification of penalties

##### Section 1 - Penalties applicable to natural persons

###### Paragraph 1 - Principal penalties

###### Art.44.- The principal penalties applicable to natural persons are

•	1° penal servitude;  
•	2° fine;  
•	3° community service work.  
1.	Penal servitude

###### Art.45

The duration of principal penal servitude is either perpetual or temporary.

###### Art.46

The duration of principal fixed-term penal servitude is a minimum of one day and a maximum of thirty years according to the cases specified by law, except in cases of recidivism or others where the law has determined other limits.  

It is calculated by day, month and year of the Gregorian calendar. The penalty of one day is twenty-four hours. That of one month is thirty days.

###### Art.47

Those sentenced to penal servitude serve their sentence in prisons determined by the Minister with Justice in his or her attributions. They are employed either inside these establishments or outside, in one of the works authorised by the regulations of the establishment unless they are exempted from it, in exceptional cases, by the Minister with Justice in his or her attributions.

###### Art.48

Any detention undergone before the irrevocable conviction as a result of the offence that gave rise to that conviction is credited in full against the entire duration of the penal servitude sentence pronounced.  
2.	Fine

###### Art.49

A fine is a pecuniary penalty that consists in the obligation to pay a sum of money to the public treasury. It is at least one thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.50

A fine is pronounced individually against each of those convicted in respect of the same offence. There is no collective fine.

###### Art.51

In default of payment within eight days following the conviction becoming irrevocable and in the case of an immediately enforceable judgment, within eight days following the pronouncement of the judgment or ruling, the fine may be replaced by penal servitude the duration of which is fixed by the judgment of conviction according to the circumstances or the amount of the fine imposed on the convicted person.

###### Art.52

The duration of subsidiary penal servitude in case of a fine cannot exceed twelve months. In all cases, the convicted person may discharge himself or herself from this penal servitude by paying the fine. He or she cannot escape proceedings against property by offering to undergo penal servitude.  
3.	Community service work

###### Art.53

Community service work consists in the conviction for a misdemeanour or petty offence to personally perform unpaid work for the benefit of a legal person under public law or an association authorised to implement community service work.

###### Art.54

In the case where the judge believes he or she can pronounce a penal servitude sentence the duration of which does not exceed two years, it may be waived and substituted with a conviction to community service work the duration of which does not exceed two thousand eight hundred and twenty hours. In the application of this article, the penalty of one month of penal servitude corresponds to one hundred and twenty hours of community service work.  
The judgment specifies the penal servitude and/or fine sentence that the convicted person undergoes who evades the execution of community service work.  

The penalty of community service work and the penalty of fine or penal servitude cannot be pronounced cumulatively.

###### Art.55

In choosing the nature of community service work, the judge takes into account the social environment, age as well as any other criterion of vulnerability of the convicted person.

###### Art.56

For each convicted person, the institution responsible for having the community service work executed informs the territorially competent Prosecutor of the official designated to ensure supervision of the execution.

###### Art.57

The designated official reports to the Prosecutor on the progress of the execution of the work as well as all incidents that occur during its execution.  
In the case where the perpetrator of the offence is a minor under eighteen years of age, the Prosecutor of the Republic transmits a copy of said report to the judge seized of the file.

###### Art.58

At the expiration of the period stated in the judgment, the institution for the benefit of which the community service work was performed sends a final report to the Prosecutor of the Republic, establishing the manner in which the work was executed.  
In the case where the perpetrator of the offence is a minor under eighteen years of age, the Prosecutor of the Republic transmits a copy of said report to the judge seized of the file.

###### Art.59

The nature and modalities of execution of community service work are specified in the judgment.

###### Paragraph 2 - Complementary penalties applicable to natural persons

###### Art.60.- The complementary penalties applicable to natural persons are

•	1° special confiscation;  
•	2° prohibition;  
•	3° socio-judicial supervision;  
•	4° placement at the disposal of the Government;  
•	5° closure of establishment;  
•	6° publication of the conviction except if the perpetrator of the offence is a minor under eighteen years of age;  
•	7° temporary or permanent withdrawal of driving licence;  
•	8° presentation of the convicted person to the public.  
1.	Special confiscation

###### Art.61

In case of a felony or misdemeanour, special confiscation of property that forms the corpus of the offence or that was used or was intended to commit it, or that was the product of it may be pronounced in addition to the principal penalty, when the ownership of said property belongs to the convicted person.  

When the ownership of the property described above does not belong to the convicted person, as well as in matters of petty offences, special confiscation may only be pronounced in the cases provided for by law.  
Special confiscation applies to the thing that was used or was intended to commit the offence or to the thing that is the product of it with the exception of objects susceptible to restitution.

###### Art.62

When the thing to be confiscated has not been seized or cannot be produced, confiscation is ordered in value. For the recovery of the sum representing the value of the thing to be confiscated, the provisions relating to imprisonment for debt are applicable.  
The confiscated thing is, except contrary special provisions providing for its destruction or attribution, devolved to the State; it nevertheless remains encumbered, up to its value, with real rights lawfully constituted for the benefit of third parties.

###### Art.63

The penalty of confiscation is pronounced ex officio for objects that the judge deems dangerous or harmful to public order and security.

###### Art.64

General confiscation covering the totality of the present and future patrimony of the convicted person is prohibited.  
2.	Prohibition

###### Art.65

In cases determined by law, the following prohibitions may be imposed:
1° Prohibition of civic, civil and family rights;  
2° Prohibition from exercising a public function, a professional or social activity; 3° Exclusion from public procurement;  
4° Prohibition from issuing cheques;  
5° Prohibition from using payment cards; 6° Prohibition from leaving the territory;  
7° Prohibition from residing in certain areas and assignment to residence; 8° Prohibition from the territory;  
9° Prohibition of access to the marital home for a spouse convicted of serious violence against their spouse;  
10° Prohibition of the right to frequent certain places of public gathering, places of childcare and education.

###### Art.66

The prohibition of civic, civil and family rights covers: 1° The right to vote;  
2° Eligibility;  
3° The right to exercise a judicial function or to be an expert before a court, to represent or assist a party before the courts;  
4° The right to testify in court other than to make simple statements;  
5° The right to be a guardian or curator except for one's own children, after the opinion of the Family Council;  
6° The right to bear arms;  
7° The right to wear any decoration.  
The prohibition of civic, civil and family rights may not exceed a period of five years.

###### Art.67

The prohibition of civic, civil and family rights imposed by courts and tribunals has the effect of depriving the convicted person of one or more rights listed in the preceding article without being able to cover all of these rights.  
The prohibition of civic, civil and family rights may only be imposed in addition to a sentence of penal servitude exceeding ten years.  
However, in cases expressly provided for by law, the prohibition of civic, civil and family rights may be total or partial. It may be suspended during execution under the same conditions as penal servitude.  
It may be reduced or erased following the rehabilitation procedure, after a term and the fulfilment of conditions left to the discretion of the court that imposed the sentence.

###### Art.68

The prohibition from exercising a public function, a professional or social activity may relate either to the professional or social activity in the exercise of which or on the occasion of which the offence was committed, or to any other professional or social activity defined by the law that punishes the offence.  
The prohibition from exercising a public function or exercising a professional or social activity may not exceed a period of twenty years.

###### Art.69

Exclusion from public procurement consists in the prohibition from participating directly or indirectly in any contract concluded by the State and public establishments, local authorities as well as by enterprises granted concessions or controlled by the State or by local authorities.  

Exclusion from public procurement may not exceed five years.

###### Art.70

The prohibition from issuing cheques requires the convicted person to return to the banker who had issued them the forms in their possession and in that of their agents.  
The prohibition from issuing cheques may not exceed a period of five years.

###### Art.71

The prohibition from using payment cards consists, for the convicted person, in the injunction to return to the banker the cards in their possession and in that of their agents.  
The prohibition from using payment cards may not exceed a period of five years.

###### Art.72

The prohibition from leaving the territory prohibits the convicted person from leaving the territory of Burundi before the final execution of their obligations arising from the judgment or ruling.

###### Art.73

In cases provided for by law, the penalty of prohibition from Burundian territory may be imposed permanently or for a period of up to ten years against a foreigner guilty of a crime or offence.  
Prohibition from the territory automatically entails the escorting of the convicted person to the border, where applicable, upon expiry of the principal sentence.

###### Art.74

Prohibition from residing in certain areas consists in the prohibition made to a convicted person from appearing in certain places determined by the judge.

###### Art.75

Assignment to residence consists in the obligation imposed on the convicted person to reside in certain places determined by the judgment.  
The duration of the prohibition from residing in certain areas may not exceed one year.

###### Art.76

Prohibition from residing in certain areas and assignment to residence may be imposed:
1° Against any person convicted of having committed an offence punishable by a principal sentence of penal servitude of a maximum of six months or when the applicable sentence must not exceed six months by reason of the circumstances;  
2° Against anyone who has committed, within ten years, at least two offences which each resulted in penal servitude of at least two months.

###### Art.77

The judgment that imposes the penalties of prohibition from residing in certain areas and assignment to residence determines the date on which they take effect.  
3.	Socio-judicial supervision

###### Art.78

In cases provided for by law, courts and tribunals may order socio-judicial supervision. Socio-judicial supervision entails, for the convicted person, the obligation to

submit, under the control of the Officer of the Public Prosecutor and for a period determined by the trial court, to surveillance and assistance measures intended to prevent recidivism. The duration of socio-judicial supervision may not exceed five years in case of conviction for offences or ten years in case of conviction for a crime.  
The conviction decision also sets the maximum duration of incarceration of the convicted person in case of failure to observe the obligations imposed on them. This incarceration may not exceed two years in case of conviction for an offence and five years in case of conviction for a crime.

###### Art.79

Socio-judicial supervision may include an injunction to undergo treatment or training. The injunction to undergo treatment may be imposed by the trial court, if it is established, after medical examination, ordered under the conditions provided for by the Code of Criminal Procedure, that the person prosecuted is likely to be the subject of treatment.  
In case of refusal of the treatment or training offered to them, the incarceration imposed pursuant to paragraph two of the preceding article is put into execution.

###### Art.80

When socio-judicial supervision accompanies a custodial sentence without suspension, it applies from the day of deprivation of liberty.  
When the measures prescribed within the framework of socio-judicial supervision cannot be executed in a detention facility, any incarceration of the convicted person suspends the legal period of their socio-judicial supervision.  
Imprisonment ordered by reason of failure to observe the obligations resulting from socio-judicial supervision is cumulative with custodial sentences imposed for offences committed during the execution of the measure.

###### Art.81

The surveillance measures applicable to a person subject to socio-judicial supervision are those provided for in Article 123.  
He may also be subject to one or more of the following obligations:
1° To refrain from appearing in any place or any category of specially designated places, and in particular places usually accommodating minors;  
2° To refrain from associating with or entering into contact with certain persons or certain categories of persons and in particular minors, except, where applicable, those designated by the court;  
3° Not to engage in any professional or voluntary activity involving habitual contact with minors.  
4.	Placement at the disposal of the Government

###### Art.82

Any person who has committed, within ten years, at least three offences each of which resulted in a penal servitude of at least six months and who shows a persistent tendency towards delinquency may, by the judgment or decision of conviction, be placed at the disposal of the Government for a period not exceeding ten years after expiry of the sentence of penal servitude.

###### Art.83

The proceedings relating to the convictions serving as the basis for placement at the disposal of the Government are attached to the prosecution file and the reasons for the decision are specified therein by indicating the circumstances establishing the persistent tendency towards delinquency.

###### Art.84

Where a convicted person has been placed at the disposal of the Government by two successive decisions for non-concurrent offences, if the first decision has not reached its term upon expiry of the principal sentence of penal servitude pronounced by the second decision, the second placement at the disposal of the Government only takes effect after expiry of the first.

###### Art.85

When the convicted person is released conditionally, the sentence of placement at the disposal of the Government takes effect from the date of conditional release. Its execution is suspended in the event of revocation of conditional release, from the time of arrest.

###### Art.86

When, during the execution of placement at the disposal of the Government, the convicted person is arrested, even preventively, pursuant to a judicial decision, the execution of the sentence of placement at the disposal of the Government is suspended for the duration of the detention.

###### Art.87

The convicted person placed at the disposal of the Government is interned, if necessary, in an establishment designated by the Minister responsible for Justice.

###### Art.88

Upon expiry of the principal sentence, the Minister responsible for Justice decides whether he is released or interned. If the convicted person is released, he may, at any time, for misconduct, be interned by decision of the Public Prosecutor of the jurisdiction where the misconduct occurred.  
The convicted person may lodge an appeal against the decision of the Prosecutor with the Procureur Général at the Court of Appeal.

###### Art.89

The convicted person placed at the disposal of the Government may apply to be relieved of the effects of this conviction. The application is addressed to the Procureur Général at the Court of Appeal of the jurisdiction of the court that ordered the placement at the disposal of the Government.  
The Procureur Général at the Court of Appeal investigates the application and refers the matter, through his submissions, to the court that convicted; the latter rules by reasoned decision, the convicted person being duly summoned and heard.  

In the event of rejection, a new application may not be made before the expiry of a period of one year.  
5.	Closure of establishment

###### Art.90

Where the offence is committed in the context of commercial, artisanal or industrial activities by enterprises and in all cases expressly provided for by law, the courts may, in addition to principal sentences, order the closure of the convicted person's establishment for a period of up to two years.  
In this case, the convicted person may transfer all or part of his stock, in particular perishable goods, to another professional. The transfer price may not be paid without the agreement of the treasury, which enjoys a special privilege over this price, for the payment of criminal or tax fines payable by the convicted person.  
6.	Publication of the conviction

###### Art.91

In cases determined by law, at the request of an interested party or of its own motion at the discretion of the court, the judge may order that the conviction decision be published in full or in extracts in the Official Bulletin of Burundi in one or more other press publications or in one or more audiovisual communication services or by posting in places that he determines, all at the expense of the convicted person.  
The costs of publication may not exceed the sum fixed for this purpose by the conviction decision and the duration of posting may not exceed one month.  
In the event of the convicted person's inability to pay the costs of publication, it is carried out at the initiative of the Public Prosecutor.  
7.	Temporary or permanent withdrawal of driving licence

###### Art.92

Temporary withdrawal of a driving licence as a complementary sentence is a measure resulting in the cancellation of the driving licence for repeated failures to comply with the obligations contained in the Road Traffic Code.  
The holder of a driving licence subject to temporary withdrawal must present his driving licence within ten days and may not begin the procedure for obtaining a new driving licence until after a period of one year. Both theoretical and practical tests must be retaken.

###### Art.93

Permanent withdrawal of a driving licence as a complementary sentence is a measure resulting in the permanent withdrawal of the right to drive due to serious and repeated failures to comply with the obligations contained in the Road Traffic Code and their consequences.  
8.	Presentation of the convicted person to the public

###### Art.94

In cases expressly determined by law, the sentence of presentation to the public may be pronounced against a convicted person in addition to penal servitude of at least ten years.  
The sentence of presentation of the convicted person to the public may only be executed when the conviction judgment has become final.  
The conviction judgment determines the place where the convicted person is presented to the public.  
The presentation of the convicted person to the public is carried out at the initiative of the Prosecutor in collaboration with the Communal Administrator of the convicted person's place of residence.

###### Paragraphe 3 - Restitution and damages

###### Art.95

Any criminal conviction is pronounced without prejudice to restitution and damages that may be due to the parties, at their request or at the request of the Public Prosecutor.

###### Art.96

The court may fix the amount of damages and order restitution and damages of its own motion that are due under the law or local customs.

###### Art.97

Enforcement of convictions for restitution, damages and costs may be pursued by means of imprisonment for debt.

###### Art.98

Imprisonment for debt is treated, for its execution, as penal servitude; its duration does not discharge the payment obligation.

###### Art.99

The duration of imprisonment for debt is proportional to the amount of sums due at the rate of six months per tranche or part of a tranche of one hundred thousand francs.

###### Art.100

The convicted person who proves his insolvency is released after this finding.

###### Art.101

Imprisonment for debt is neither exercised nor maintained against convicted persons who have reached their sixtieth year or who suffer from an incurable disease at a very advanced stage confirmed by an ad hoc Medical Commission.

###### Art.102

Where the property of the convicted person is insufficient to cover the sentences of fine, restitution and damages, the latter two sentences have priority.

###### Art.103

In case of concurrence of the fine with court costs owed to the State, payments made by the convicted persons are credited first to these costs.

###### Paragraphe 4

Protection and safeguard measures applicable to minor children aged 15 to 18 years

###### Art.104

At the same time as pronouncing a principal sentence other than penal servitude, the judge may order the placement of the child in conflict with the law in a foster family or in a specialized institution that he determines.

###### Art.105

In the case where the judge could pronounce a sentence of penal servitude less than or equal to two years, it may be derogated from and substituted with community service work the duration of which does not exceed two hundred and forty hours.  
The judgment specifies the sentence of penal servitude and/or fine that the convicted person undergoes who evades the execution of community service work.  
The sentence of community service work and the sentence of fine or penal servitude cannot be pronounced cumulatively.

###### Art.106

At the same time as sentencing to community service work, the judge may prescribe the socio-judicial supervision of the minor in conflict with the law. The judgment specifies the authority responsible for ensuring this supervision.

##### Section 2 - Sentences applicable to legal persons

###### Art.107

The sentences incurred by legal persons are the fine as well as one or more complementary sentences listed in article 108.

###### Art.108

For legal persons having the status of commercial company, the judge applies the following sentences:
1° Where the law provides for the offence a sentence of deprivation of liberty for life, the judge applies a fine equal to half of the turnover of the previous financial year.  
2° Where the law provides for the offence a sentence of deprivation of liberty for a fixed term, the judge sentences to a minimum fine equal to one twentieth of the turnover of the previous financial year while the maximum amounts to a fine equal to one quarter of the turnover of the previous financial year.

###### Art.109

Without prejudice to the relevant provisions on complementary sentences, for non-profit legal persons, the judge applies the following sentences:
1° Where the law provides for an offence a sentence of deprivation of liberty for life, the judge applies a fine equal to one million Burundian francs at minimum and a fine of fifty million Burundian francs at maximum;  
2° Where the law provides for a crime a sentence of deprivation of liberty for a fixed term, the judge applies a fine of five hundred thousand Burundian francs at minimum and a fine of twenty million Burundian francs at maximum;  

3° Where the offence constitutes a misdemeanour, the judge applies a fine of one hundred thousand to five million Burundian francs;  
4° Where the offence constitutes a petty offence, the judge applies a fine which cannot exceed one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.110

Where the law so provides, the Courts and Tribunals may pronounce one or more of the following sentences:
1° Dissolution;  
2° Prohibition, permanently or for a period of five years at most, from exercising directly or indirectly one or more professional or social activities;  
3° Permanent closure or for a period of five years at most of the establishments or of one or more establishments of the enterprise having committed the offence within the meaning of article 21;  
4° Exclusion from public procurement either permanently or for a period not exceeding five years
5° Prohibition, for a period of five years at most, from making public appeals for savings;  
6° Prohibition, for a period of one year at most, from issuing cheques or using payment cards,
7° Confiscation of the thing which served or was intended to commit the offence or of the thing which is the product thereof;  
8° Publication of the conviction.

###### Art.111

The sentences provided for in the preceding article are not applicable to the State, local authorities as well as any legal person under public law.

#### Chapitre 2 - Sentences applicable in case of concurrence of several offences

###### Art.112

There is concurrence of offences where several offences have been committed by the same perpetrator without a final conviction having occurred for at least one of them.

###### Art.113.- There is ideal concurrence

1° Where the single fact from a material point of view is susceptible of several qualifications;  

2° Where the action comprises facts which, constituting distinct offences, are united among themselves as proceeding from a single criminal intention or as being some of them aggravating circumstances of the others.  
In both cases, only the strongest sentence is pronounced.

###### Art.114

There is real concurrence where the facts, distinct from a material point of view, have succeeded one another and have constituted distinct offences. In this case sentences are pronounced for each offence and the sentences pronounced are cumulated subject to the following provisions:
1° Penal servitude for life absorbs by right any sentence of deprivation of liberty;  
2° The total of cumulated sentences of penal servitude for a fixed term and fines cannot exceed double the maximum of the strongest sentences provided for by one or the other offence retained against the convicted person;  
3° The total of sentences of prohibition from residence and assignment to residence cannot exceed twenty years;  
4° The total of sentences of placement at the disposal of the Government absorbs by right the sentences of prohibition from residence and assignment to residence;  
5° The total of sentences of temporary prohibition of civic, civil and family rights cannot exceed twenty years.

###### Art.115

The strongest sentence is that whose maximum is the highest. If two sentences have the same maximum, the strongest sentence is that whose minimum is the highest. If two sentences have the same maximum and the same minimum, the strongest sentence is that accompanied by a sentence of fine.

###### Art.116

A sentence of fine is always less strong than a sentence of penal servitude.

#### Chapitre 3 - Recidivism

###### Art.117

Whoever having been, by final decision, convicted for an offence to a sentence greater than or equal to one year of penal servitude, has committed, within a period of five years after the expiration of this sentence or its prescription, an offence which must be punished by penal servitude of more than two months, is sentenced to double the sentence provided by law.

###### Art.118

If the first conviction was penal servitude for life and the second offence is punishable by the same sentence, the convicted person may not claim conditional release until after a safety period of thirty years.

###### Art.119

There is no recidivism, where the sentence pronounced for the first offence has been erased by amnesty or if the convicted person has been irrevocably rehabilitated.

###### Art.120

One who has been convicted by a military tribunal is, in case of subsequent offence, liable to the sentences of recidivism only if the first conviction was pronounced for an offence punishable under common law.

#### Chapitre 4 - Conditional sentencing

###### Art.121

The Courts and Tribunals, in sentencing to one or more sentences of principal or complementary penal servitude, may order by reasoned decision, that execution of the judgment or decision is suspended with respect to this or these sentences, for a period of which they fix the duration from the date of pronouncement of the judgment or decision but which cannot exceed five years.

###### Art.122.- The granting of a suspended sentence is subject to the following conditions

1° That no principal sentence of penal servitude exceeding two years or a principal fine of one hundred thousand francs be imposed on the convicted person;  
2° That the convicted person has not previously incurred, during the last five years, any conviction to penal servitude or for an offence committed in Burundi, punishable, independently of the fine, by penal servitude of more than six months. However, this restriction is not applicable to minors.  
3° That the convicted person has fully restituted the sums or any other property obtained by means of the offence.

###### Art.123

In case of conviction to a sentence of penal servitude with suspension, the trial court may specifically impose on the convicted person the observance of one or more of the following obligations:
1° To respond to summonses from the Officer of the Public Prosecution;  2° To follow education or vocational training;  
3° To submit to measures of medical examination, treatment or care even under the regime of hospitalization;  
4° To refrain from driving certain vehicles determined by the categories of permits provided for by the Road Code;  
5° Not to engage in the professional activity in the exercise of which or on the occasion of which the offence was committed;  
6° To refrain from appearing in any specially designated place;  

7° Not to frequent drinking establishments;  
8° To refrain from entering into contact with certain persons, in particular the victim of the offence;  
9° Not to possess or carry a weapon;  
10° To submit to one or more obligations provided for by the Code of Criminal Procedure regarding provisional release.

###### Art.124

Non-observance of any of the obligations set out in the preceding article entails automatic revocation of the suspension.

###### Art.125

The judgment or order imposing conviction is not executed, with regard to the sentence or sentences of penal servitude, if, during the fixed period, the convicted person does not incur new convictions for offences punishable, independently of the fine, by penal servitude of more than six months.  
In the contrary case, the sentences for which suspension was granted and those which were the subject of the new conviction are cumulated.

###### Art.126

In case of suspension applicable to subsidiary penal servitude, the execution extends to the fine.

###### Art.127

In case of conviction for rape, torture, crime of genocide, crime against humanity, war crime, or in case of conviction for attempt or complicity in war crime, crime against humanity or crime of genocide, the courts and tribunals may not grant suspension.

###### Art.128

The conviction for crime or offence accompanied by suspension is deemed null and void if the convicted person who benefits from it has not committed, within five years from that conviction, a common law crime or offence followed by a new conviction without suspension which entails revocation.

### Titre 3

Certain modalities of execution of the sentence of penal servitude Chapter 1 - Conditional release

###### Art.129

Convicted persons who have to serve one or more sentences involving deprivation of liberty may be released conditionally when they have completed one quarter of these sentences, provided that the duration of incarceration already served exceeds three months.  
Persons sentenced to life imprisonment may be released conditionally when the duration of incarceration already served exceeds ten years.  

The duration of incarceration already prescribed in the two preceding paragraphs may be reduced when the convicted person has already reached the age of seventy years or if, in the opinion of a Panel of three medical experts appointed by the Minister of Justice, prolonged incarceration may endanger the life of the convicted person.

###### Art.130

Conditional release may only occur in favour of convicted persons after having repaired the damage caused by the offence.

###### Art.131

Release may always be revoked for reasons of misconduct or violations of the conditions set out in the release order.

###### Art.132

Definitive release is acquired by the convicted person if revocation has not occurred before the expiration of a period equal to twice the term of incarceration that the person still had to serve on the date on which release was ordered in their favour.

###### Art.133

Conditional release is ordered by the Minister responsible for Justice after the opinion of the Public Prosecution and the Prison Director.  
It is revoked by the same Minister at the initiative of the Public Prosecution.  
The provisional re-arrest of the conditional releasee may be ordered by the Prosecutor General of the Republic or one of his Deputy Prosecutors General subject to immediately notifying the Minister responsible for Justice.

###### Art.134

Reintegration takes place, by virtue of the revocation order, for the completion of the term of incarceration that the execution of the sentence still entailed on the date of release.

###### Art.135

The prescription of sentences does not run while the convicted person is on conditional release by virtue of a release order that has not been revoked.

###### Art.136

The Minister responsible for Justice determines the form of release permits, the conditions to which release may be subject and the method of supervision of conditional releasees.

#### Chapitre 2 - Incompressible sentences

###### Art.137

A sentence is said to be incompressible when the convicted person is required to execute the entirety of their sentence without being able to benefit from any measure of alleviation.

###### Art.138

In case of conviction for crimes of genocide, crime against humanity, war crime, voluntary homicide, sexual assault, torture and armed robbery, the convicted person executes the entirety of the sentence without being able to benefit from the application of provisions on conviction and conditional release.

### Titre 4

Extinction of public prosecution, sentences and erasure of convictions

#### Chapitre 1 - General provisions

###### Art.139

Public prosecution is extinguished by the death of the accused, the dissolution of the legal person, the abrogation of the criminal law, res judicata, amnesty or prescription.  
The dissolution of the legal person does not prejudice criminal proceedings brought against the directors of the dissolved company.

###### Art.140

Public prosecution may also be extinguished by settlement and by withdrawal of the complaint when the law expressly so provides.

###### Art.141

The sentence is extinguished by its execution, by the death of the convicted person, pardon, amnesty, prescription or the dissolution of the legal person.  
However, recovery of the fine and legal costs as well as execution of confiscation may be proceeded with after the dissolution of the legal person until the closure of liquidation operations.

###### Art.142

The sentence may also be modified or erased by pardon, conditional release or rehabilitation.

#### Chapitre 2 - Withdrawal of the complaint

###### Art.143

For offences that can only be prosecuted upon complaint of the injured party, the withdrawal of the latter extinguishes the public prosecution.  
Withdrawal is only admissible if it extends to all those who participated in the perpetration of the offence.

###### Art.144

Withdrawal is judicial or extrajudicial. It is tacit when the complainant has performed acts incompatible with the will to persist in their complaint.

###### Art.145.- Express or tacit withdrawal cannot be retracted

###### Art.146

To produce its effects, the withdrawal must, except in cases where the law provides otherwise, occur before the conviction becomes final.

###### Art.147

If the complaint has been filed by several victims in relation to the same offence, the public prosecution is only extinguished if all complainants have withdrawn.

#### Chapitre 3 - De la prescription de l'action publique

###### Art.148.- The public prosecution resulting from an offence is prescribed

1° After one year has elapsed if the offence committed constitutes a petty offence; 2° After three years have elapsed, if the offence committed constitutes a misdemeanour;  
3° After ten years if the offence committed constitutes a crime punishable by five to ten years of penal servitude;  
4° After twenty years if the offence committed constitutes a crime punishable by more than ten years of penal servitude;  
5° After thirty years, if the offence committed constitutes a crime punishable by penal servitude for life.

###### Art.149

The limitation period begins to run on the day when all the constituent elements of the offence are brought together in the case of instantaneous offences; it runs from the day when the unlawful state ceased in the case of continuing or continued offences.

###### Art.150

The limitation period is interrupted by acts of investigation or prosecution carried out within the time limits of one year, three years or ten years, twenty years or thirty years from the day when the offence was committed.

###### Art.151

The limitation period for public prosecution of crimes committed against minors only begins to run from the civil majority of the latter.

###### Art.152

The public prosecution relating to crimes of genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes is not subject to a limitation period.

###### Art.153

The civil action arising from an offence is prescribed according to the rules of civil law.  
However, if the limitation period for the civil action was acquired while that of the public prosecution is not yet completed, the civil action is only prescribed according to the rules relating to the public prosecution.

#### Chapitre 4 - De la prescription des peines

###### Art.154

Fines of less than five thousand Burundian francs are prescribed after two years have elapsed, fines of five thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs are prescribed after four years have elapsed, fines of more than one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs after ten years have elapsed and fines of more than one million Burundian francs after thirty years.

###### Art.155

Sentences of penal servitude are prescribed after two years or five years have elapsed depending on whether they relate to petty offence or misdemeanour matters.

###### Art.156

Sentences of penal servitude in criminal matters are prescribed after a period equal to double the sentence pronounced without this period exceeding twenty years.  
Life sentences are prescribed after thirty years.

###### Art.157

Sentences pronounced for crimes of genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes are not subject to a limitation period.

###### Art.158

The time limits of the provisions of this chapter run from the date of the day when the judgment or decision rendered is final and has acquired the force of res judicata.

###### Art.159

The complementary penalty is prescribed within the same time limit as the penalty of which it is an accessory.

###### Art.160

The escaped convict's sentence is prescribed after a period equal to triple the sentence not yet served. This period begins to run from the day of escape.

###### Art.161

The limitation period for the sentence is interrupted by the arrest of the convict; his detention results in the suspension of the limitation period with regard to complementary penalties.

###### Art.162

Civil convictions pronounced by criminal courts are prescribed according to the rules of the Civil Code.

#### Chapitre 5 - De la grâce

###### Art.163

Pardon consists in the total or partial remission by the executive power of the execution of sentences pronounced or in their commutation into other less serious sentences determined by law.

###### Art.164

Pardon may apply to all principal or complementary penalties. It does not apply to physical constraint exercised for the recovery of fines nor to legal costs, nor to civil reparations.

###### Art.165

Only sentences that are enforceable and resulting from a final conviction may be the subject of a pardon measure.

###### Art.166

A suspended sentence cannot be the subject of a pardon as long as the suspension is not revoked.

###### Art.167

Pardon may be either unconditional, or subject to the execution of a conviction stated by the pardon decision. If this condition is not fulfilled, the revocation of the pardon takes place automatically and the conviction is brought back to execution.  

In this case, the limitation period for the sentence is suspended between the notification and the revocation of the pardon.

###### Art.168

Pardon does not extinguish complementary penalties not covered by the pardon decision nor the effects of the conviction, in particular those relating to recidivism, the application of suspension in the event of subsequent prosecutions and civil convictions such as restitutions and damages.

###### Art.169

Applications for pardon are examined by the Public Prosecutor at the court that pronounced the conviction.  
They may also be examined by the Public Prosecutor's Office in the jurisdiction where the applicant resides or the place of his detention.

###### Art.170

When it is general, pardon is proposed at the initiative of the Minister responsible for Justice; the application is submitted by the convict or any interested party acting in his name when it is individual.

###### Art.171

After examination, the pardon files are sent to the Minister responsible for Justice who presents a report to the Head of State for discretionary decision.

###### Art.172

Pardon does not extinguish sentences pronounced for genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes.

#### Chapitre 6 - De l'amnistie

###### Art.173

Amnesty is the act by which the legislative power prohibits the exercise or continuation of criminal prosecutions and erases convictions pronounced.  
Genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes cannot be the subject of any amnesty law.

###### Art.174

Amnesty is in principle general; however, it may be limited to certain categories of offences.

###### Art.175

Amnesty is a matter of public policy: it is acquired automatically and without the knowledge of and despite those who benefit from it.

###### Art.176

Amnesty sometimes erases certain determined offences independently of the sentence pronounced, sometimes it is based solely on the amount of sentences pronounced.

###### Art.177

The power to interpret amnesty laws belongs to the judicial power and more precisely to the court that pronounced the conviction.  

The implementation of amnesty laws is entrusted to a Commission established by the Minister responsible for Justice.

###### Art.178

Amnesty extinguishes the public prosecution; erases or reduces any conviction of a criminal nature but leaves intact provisions not having a punitive character; it cannot be invoked against the rights of the State and third parties. Fines already collected and costs paid remain acquired by the treasury.

#### Chapitre 7 - De la grâce amnistiante

###### Art.179

Amnesty pardon is the combination of pardon and amnesty to which the legislator resorts to introduce more justice in the application of amnesty.

###### Art.180

The legislature establishes in an amnesty law the criminal acts to which the measure of clemency must extend, but it leaves to the Head of State the task of subsequently determining, by way of individual pardon, which persons, among the perpetrators of the acts provided for, are the sole beneficiaries of amnesty.

###### Art.181

Amnesty pardon erases or reduces criminal convictions; it leaves intact the other effects of the public action or of the conviction.

#### Chapitre 8 - De l'effacement des peines et de la réhabilitation des condamnés

##### Section 1 - De l'effacement des peines

###### Art.182

Criminal sanctions pronounced as a final decision are transcribed on the criminal record of the convicted person.  
The erasure of these convictions is acquired automatically for the convicted natural person who has not, within the time limits determined below, incurred any new conviction for a criminal or correctional offence:
1° For conviction to a fine, after a period of three years from the day of payment of the fine, the expiry of the subsidiary penal servitude or the completion of the limitation period;  
2° For conviction to a correctional penalty not exceeding one year, erasure is acquired after a period of five years from the execution of the penalty or its limitation.

###### Art.183

In case of conviction to one or more complementary penalties, erasure may only occur after having executed the latter.

##### Section 2 - De la réhabilitation

###### Art.184

Rehabilitation is an act of the judicial power which restores to the convicted person the rights lost and causes the effects resulting from the conviction to cease for the future without prejudice to the rights of third parties.

###### Art.185

Any person convicted of an offence committed in Burundi may be rehabilitated.

###### Art.186

Rehabilitation may only be requested in court, during the lifetime of the convicted person, by the latter, or if he is under interdiction, by his legal representative; in case of death and if the legal conditions are met, the request may be made by his spouse or by his ascendants or descendants, but within the period of five years from the date of death.

###### Art.187

The request must cover all the convictions pronounced which have not been erased by a prior rehabilitation.

###### Art.188.- La réhabilitation est soumise aux conditions suivantes

1° The pecuniary or custodial penalty must have been served or remitted by virtue of the right of pardon or be considered null and void as a result of the conditional conviction;  
2° The application for rehabilitation may only be made after a period of five years for those convicted to a correctional penalty and ten years for those convicted to a criminal penalty
This period runs, for those convicted to a fine, from the day the conviction became final and, for those convicted to a custodial penalty, from the day of their final release or from the day of conditional release if the latter was not followed by revocation. This period is fifteen years for the recidivist and the person who has allowed his penalty to lapse.  
3° During this period, the convicted person must have been of good conduct and have had a certain residence;  
4° He must not have already enjoyed the benefit of rehabilitation;  
5° He must prove, except in the case of limitation, payment of court costs, the fine and damages or the remission granted to him.  
In the absence of such proof, he must establish that he has served the time of imprisonment for debt determined by law, or that the treasury or the victims of the offence have waived this means of execution.  
If he is convicted for bankruptcy, he must prove payment of the liabilities of the bankruptcy in principal, interest and costs or the remission granted to him.  

However, if the convicted person proves that he is in an absolute state of inability to discharge the pecuniary convictions imposed on him, he may be rehabilitated, even if these convictions have not been paid or have only been partially paid.

###### Art.189

If the injured party cannot be found or if it refuses to receive the sum due, the latter is deposited in a public fund.  
If the party does not present itself within a period of five years to be allocated the deposited sum, this sum is paid to the treasury at the initiative of the judge who pronounced the conviction.

###### Art.190

The convicted person addresses the application for rehabilitation to the Public Prosecutor of his residence. This application specifies the date of the conviction and the places where the convicted person has resided since his release.  
The Public Prosecutor conducts a morality inquiry on the convicted person.  
He has delivered to him a copy of the conviction judgments, an extract from the register of places of detention where the penalty was served and establishing what the conduct of the convicted person was, as well as a criminal record bulletin. He transmits the documents with his opinion to the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal.

###### Art.191

The Court is seized by the Prosecutor General and rules within two months on the submissions of the latter, the party or his counsel having been heard or duly summoned.

###### Art.192

In case of rejection of the application, a new application may not be filed before the expiry of a period of two years, unless the rejection of the first was motivated by the insufficiency of the probationary period; in that case, the application may be renewed upon the expiry of that period.

###### Art.193

Mention of the judgment pronouncing rehabilitation is made in the margin of the conviction judgment and on the criminal record.

###### Art.194

Rehabilitation erases the mention of the conviction from the criminal record of the rehabilitated person.

###### Art.195

Rehabilitation is revoked by operation of law if the rehabilitated convicted person commits, within five years, an offence punishable by penal servitude equal to or greater than five years, and followed by a conviction to imprisonment; for this purpose, the Public Prosecutor at the court which pronounced the conviction to imprisonment must inform the Prosecutor General, who himself seizes the Court of Appeal for the purpose of having the revocation of rehabilitation established, the party or his counsel being duly summoned.  
In case of revocation, rehabilitation is considered as never having been granted.

###### Art.196.- Les frais de la procédure de réhabilitation sont à charge du requérant

## Livre 2 - Des infractions et de leur répression en particulier

### Titre 1 - Des infractions contre les personnes

#### Chapitre 1 - Du génocide, des crimes contre l'humanité et des crimes de guerre

###### Art.197

The crime of genocide means any of the following acts committed with intent to destroy, in whole or in part, a national, ethnical, racial or religious group, as such:
1° Killing members of the group;  
2° Causing serious bodily or mental harm to members of the group;  
3° Deliberately inflicting on the group conditions of life calculated to bring about its physical destruction in whole or in part;  
4° Imposing measures intended to prevent births within the group; 5° Forcibly transferring children of the group to another group.

###### Art.198

A crime against Humanity means any of the following acts when committed as part of a widespread or systematic attack directed against any civilian population and with knowledge of the attack:
1° Murder;  
2° Extermination;  
3° Enslavement;  
4° Deportation or forcible transfer of population;  
5° Imprisonment or other severe deprivation of physical liberty in violation of fundamental rules of international law;  
6° Torture;  
7° Rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, enforced sterilization, or any other form of sexual violence of comparable gravity;  
8° Persecution against any identifiable group or collectivity on political, racial, national, ethnic, cultural, religious, gender grounds as defined in Article 199, 10°, or on other grounds that are universally recognized as impermissible under international law within the jurisdiction of the Court;  
9° Enforced disappearance of persons;  

10° Crimes of apartheid;  
11° Other inhumane acts of a similar character intentionally causing great suffering, or serious injury to body or to physical or mental health.

###### Art.199.- For the purposes of the preceding article

1° "Attack directed against any civilian population" means a course of conduct involving the multiple commission of acts referred to in the preceding article against any civilian population, pursuant to or in furtherance of a State or organizational policy to commit such attack;  
2° "Extermination" includes the intentional infliction of conditions of life, inter alia the deprivation of access to food and medicine, calculated to bring about the destruction of part of a population;  
3°. "Enslavement" means the exercise of any or all of the powers attaching to the right of ownership over a person and includes the exercise of such power in the course of trafficking in persons, in particular women and children;  
4° "Deportation or forcible transfer of population" means forced displacement of the persons concerned by expulsion or other coercive acts from the area in which they are lawfully present, without grounds permitted under international law;  
5° "Torture" means the intentional infliction of severe pain or suffering, whether physical or mental, upon a person in the custody or under the control of the accused; except that torture shall not include pain or suffering arising only from, inherent in or incidental to, lawful sanctions;  
6° "Forced pregnancy" means the unlawful confinement of a woman forcibly made pregnant, with the intent of affecting the ethnic composition of any population or carrying out other grave violations of international law;  
7°. "Persecution" means the intentional and severe deprivation of fundamental rights contrary to international law by reason of the identity of the group or collectivity;  
8° "The crime of apartheid" means inhumane acts of a character similar to those referred to in the preceding article, committed in the context of an institutionalized regime of systematic oppression and domination by one racial group over any other racial group or groups and committed with the intention of maintaining that regime;  
9° "Enforced disappearance of persons" means the arrest, detention or abduction of persons by, or with the authorization, support or acquiescence of, a State or a political organization, followed by a refusal to acknowledge that deprivation of freedom or to give information on the fate or whereabouts of those persons, with the intention of removing them from the protection of the law for a prolonged period of time;  
10° The term "gender" refers to the two sexes, male and female, within the context of society. It does not indicate any meaning other than the above.

###### Art.200

"War crimes" means crimes that are part of a plan or policy or when they are part of a series of similar crimes committed on a large scale, in particular:
1) Any of the grave breaches of the Geneva Conventions of 12 August 1949 as follows:
• 1° wilful killing;
• 2° torture or inhuman treatment, including biological experiments;
• 3° wilfully causing great suffering, or serious injury to body or health;
• 4° extensive destruction and appropriation of property, not justified by military necessity and carried out unlawfully and wantonly on a large scale;
• 5° compelling a prisoner of war or other protected person to serve in the forces of a hostile Power;
• 6° wilfully depriving a prisoner of war or any other protected person of the rights of fair and regular trial;
• 7° unlawful deportation or transfer or unlawful confinement;
• 8° taking of hostages.
2) Other serious violations of the laws and customs applicable to international armed conflicts, within the established framework of International Law, namely, any of the following acts:
• 1° intentionally directing attacks against the civilian population as such or against individual civilians not taking direct part in hostilities;
• 2° intentionally directing attacks against civilian objects, that is to say, objects which are not military objectives;
• 3° intentionally directing attacks against personnel, installations, material, units or vehicles involved in a humanitarian assistance or peacekeeping mission in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations, as long as they are entitled to the protection given to civilians and civilian objects under the International Law of armed conflict;
• 4° intentionally launching an attack in the knowledge that such attack will cause incidental loss of life or injury to civilians, injury to civilian persons, damage to civilian objects or widespread, long-term and severe damage to the natural environment which would be clearly excessive in relation to the concrete and direct overall military advantage anticipated;
• 5° attacking or bombarding, by whatever means, towns, villages, dwellings or buildings which are undefended and which are not military objectives;
• 6° killing or wounding a combatant who, having laid down his arms or having no longer means of defence, has surrendered at discretion;

• 7° making improper use of a flag of truce, of the flag or of the military insignia and uniform of the enemy or of the United Nations, as well as of the distinctive emblems of the Geneva Conventions, resulting in death or serious personal injury;
• 8° the transfer, directly or indirectly, by the Occupying Power of parts of its own civilian population into the territory it occupies, or the deportation or transfer of all or parts of the population of the occupied territory within or outside this territory;
• 9° intentionally directing attacks against buildings dedicated to religion, education, art, science or charitable purposes, historic monuments, hospitals and places where the sick and wounded are collected, provided they are not military objectives;
• 10° subjecting persons who are in the power of an adverse party to physical mutilation or to medical or scientific experiments of any kind which are neither justified by the medical, dental or hospital treatment of the person concerned nor carried out in his or her interest, and which cause death to or seriously endanger the health of such person or persons;
• 11° killing or wounding treacherously individuals belonging to the hostile nation or army;
• 12° declaring that no quarter will be given;
• 13° destroying or seizing the enemy's property unless such destruction or seizure be imperatively demanded by the necessities of war;
• 14° declaring abolished, suspended or inadmissible in a court of law the rights and actions of the nationals of the hostile party;
• 15° compelling the nationals of the hostile party to take part in the operations of war directed against their own country, even if they were in the belligerent's service before the commencement of the war;
• 16° pillaging a town or place, even when taken by assault;
• 17° employing poison or poisoned weapons;
• 18° employing asphyxiating, poisonous or other gases, and all analogous liquids, materials or devices;
• 19° employing bullets which expand or flatten easily in the human body, such as bullets with a hard envelope which does not entirely cover the core or is pierced with incisions;
• 20° employing weapons, projectiles and material and methods of warfare of a nature to cause superfluous injury or unnecessary suffering or to strike indiscriminately in violation of the International Law of armed conflict, provided that such weapons, projectiles and material and methods of warfare are the subject of a comprehensive prohibition;
• 21° committing outrages upon personal dignity, in particular humiliating and degrading treatment;
• 22° rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, as defined in article 199, 6°, enforced sterilization, or any other form of sexual violence also constituting a grave breach of the Geneva Conventions;

• 23° utilizing the presence of a civilian or other protected person to render certain points, areas or military forces immune from military operations;
• 24° intentionally directing attacks against buildings, material, medical units and transport without avoiding that the latter become the target of military operations;
• 25° intentionally directing attacks against buildings, material, medical units and transport, and personnel using, in conformity with International Law, the distinctive emblems of the Geneva Conventions;
• 26° intentionally using starvation of civilians as a method of warfare by depriving them of objects indispensable to their survival, including wilfully impeding relief supplies as provided for under the Geneva Conventions;
• 27° conscripting or enlisting children under the age of 15 years into the national armed forces or using them to participate actively in hostilities.
3) In the case of an armed conflict not of an international character, serious violations of article 3 common to the four Geneva Conventions of 12 August 1949, namely, any of the following acts committed against persons taking no active part in the hostilities, including members of armed forces who have laid down their arms and those placed hors de combat by sickness, wounds, detention or any other cause:
• 1° violence to life and person, in particular murder of all kinds, mutilation, cruel treatment and torture;
• 2° committing outrages upon personal dignity, in particular humiliating and degrading treatment;
• 3° taking of hostages;
• 4° the passing of sentences and the carrying out of executions without previous judgement pronounced by a regularly constituted court, affording all judicial guarantees which are generally recognized as indispensable.
4) Point 3 applies to armed conflicts not of an international character and does not apply to situations of internal disturbances and tensions, such as riots, isolated and sporadic acts of violence or other acts of a similar nature;
5) Other serious violations of the laws and customs applicable to armed conflicts not of an international character, within the established framework of international law, namely, any of the following acts:
• 1° intentionally directing attacks against the civilian population as such or against individual civilians not taking direct part in hostilities;
• 2° intentionally directing attacks against buildings, material, medical units and transport, and personnel using, in conformity with International Law, the distinctive emblems of the Geneva Conventions;

• 3° intentionally directing attacks against personnel, installations, material, units or vehicles involved in a humanitarian assistance or peacekeeping mission in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations, as long as they are entitled to the protection given to civilians and civilian objects under the international law of armed conflicts;
• 4° intentionally directing attacks against buildings dedicated to religion, education, art, science or charitable purposes, historic monuments, hospitals and places where the sick and wounded are collected, provided that such buildings are not military objectives;
• 5° pillaging a town or place, even when taken by assault;
• 6° rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, as defined in article 197, 6°, enforced sterilization, or any other form of sexual violence constituting a serious violation of the Geneva Conventions;
• 7° conscripting or enlisting children under the age of 15 years into armed forces or groups or using them to participate actively in hostilities;
• 8° ordering the displacement of the civilian population for reasons related to the conflict, unless the security of the civilians involved or imperative military reasons so demand;
• 9° killing or wounding treacherously a combatant adversary;
• 10° declaring that no quarter will be given;
• 11° subjecting persons of another party to the conflict who are in its power to physical mutilation or to medical or scientific experiments of any kind which are neither justified by the medical, dental or hospital treatment of the person concerned nor carried out in his or her interest, and which cause death to or seriously endanger the health of such person or persons;
• 12° destroying or seizing the property of an adversary unless such destruction or seizure be imperatively demanded by the necessities of the conflict.
6) Point 5 applies to armed conflicts not of an international character and does not therefore apply to situations of internal disturbances and tensions, such as riots, isolated and sporadic acts of violence or other acts of a similar nature. It applies to armed conflicts that take place in the territory of a State when there is protracted armed conflict between the authorities of the Government of that State and organized armed groups or between such groups.

###### Art.201

Nothing in points 3 and 5 of the preceding article affects the responsibility of a government to maintain or restore public order or to defend the unity and territorial integrity by all legitimate means.

###### Art.202

The perpetrator or co-perpetrator of any of the acts constituting the crime of genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes is punished by life imprisonment.

###### Art.203

Whoever conceives or plans the crime of genocide, the crime against humanity and the war crime is liable to life imprisonment.

###### Art.204

Whoever orders or publicly incites the commission of the crime of genocide, the crime against humanity or the war crime incurs life imprisonment.

###### Art.205

Natural or legal persons found guilty of the crime of genocide, crime against humanity or war crime incur at least one of the complementary penalties set out in Article 60.

#### Chapitre 2 - De la torture et autres traitements cruels, inhumains ou dégradants

###### Art.206

Torture is considered to be any act by which severe pain or suffering, whether physical or mental, is intentionally inflicted on a person for such purposes as obtaining from him or a third person information or a confession, punishing him for an act he or a third person has committed or is suspected of having committed, or intimidating or coercing him or a third person, or for any reason based on discrimination of any kind, when such pain or suffering is inflicted by or at the instigation of or with the consent or acquiescence of a public official or other person acting in an official capacity.  
This term does not extend to pain or suffering arising only from, inherent in or incidental to lawful sanctions.

###### Art.207

Whoever subjects a person to torture or other cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment is punished by imprisonment of ten to fifteen years and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.

###### Art.208

The offence is punished by imprisonment of twenty years when it is committed:
1° Against a minor under eighteen years of age;  
2° Against a vulnerable person by reason of age, state of health, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or state of pregnancy;  
3° Against a witness, victim or civil party, either to prevent him from reporting the facts, filing a complaint or testifying in court, or by reason of his report, complaint or testimony;  
4° By several persons acting as perpetrators or accomplices; 5° With use or threat of a weapon.

###### Art.209

The offender is punished by twenty years' imprisonment when the torture and other cruel, inhuman or degrading punishment or treatment has resulted in mutilation or permanent disability or when it is accompanied by sexual assault.  

He is punished by life imprisonment when it has resulted in the death of the victim.

###### Art.210

No exceptional circumstances whatsoever, whether a state of war or a threat of war, internal political instability or any other public emergency, may be invoked as a justification of torture and other cruel, inhuman or degrading punishment or treatment.  
An order from a superior officer or a public authority may not be invoked as a justification of torture.

###### Art.211

The penalties provided for in Articles 207, 208, and 209 are not subject to reduction. The judge shall pronounce, in addition to the principal penalties, the prohibition from exercising the function in connection with which the torture was practised, without prejudice to other complementary penalties provided for by this Code.

#### Chapitre 3 - De l'homicide et des lésions corporelles volontaires

##### Section 1 - De l'homicide

###### Art.212

Homicide committed and injuries caused with the intent to attack the person of a specific individual or of one who is found or encountered are qualified as voluntary, even if this intent were dependent on some circumstance or some condition and even if the perpetrator were mistaken as to the person of the victim of the attack.

###### Art.213.- Il est puni de la servitude pénale à perpétuité

Any act by which a person voluntarily causes the death of another is qualified as murder. Murder having as its object either to prepare or facilitate an offence or a crime, or to favour the flight or ensure the impunity of the perpetrator or accomplice of a crime or an offence is punished by life imprisonment.

###### Art.214

The murder of the father, mother or other legitimate ascendants as well as the murder of the natural father or mother is qualified as parricide. It is punished by life imprisonment.  
Also punished by life imprisonment is murder committed against one's legitimate or natural children, brothers or sisters.  
Murder committed by the legitimate or natural father and/or mother against a newborn child is qualified as infanticide. It is punished by life imprisonment.

###### Art.215

Murder committed with premeditation is qualified as assassination. It is punished by life imprisonment.  
There is premeditation when the intent to commit the homicide was formed before the action.

###### Art.216

Murder committed by means of substances that can cause death more or less promptly in whatever manner these substances have been used or administered is qualified as poisoning. It is punished by life imprisonment.

###### Art.217

Whoever has voluntarily administered substances that can cause death or which, without being of a nature to cause death, can nevertheless seriously impair health is punished by imprisonment of one year to twenty years and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.

###### Art.218

Those who, for the execution of the crimes qualified in the preceding articles in this section, resort to acts of barbarity are punished by life imprisonment.

###### Art.219

Whoever intentionally transmits an incurable disease to another is punished by life imprisonment.

###### Art.220.- Les peines prévues par cette section sont incompressibles

The judge, at the same time as imposing a principal penalty, shall pronounce a measure of socio-judicial supervision without prejudice to other complementary sanctions provided for by this Code.

##### Section 2 - Des lésions corporelles volontaires

###### Art.221

Whoever has voluntarily inflicted injuries or struck another is punished by imprisonment of two months to eight months and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.  
In case of premeditation, the offender is sentenced to imprisonment of one month to two years and a fine of two hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.222

If the blows or injuries have caused an illness or permanent incapacity for work; or if they have resulted in the loss of absolute use of an organ or serious mutilation, or if they have been inflicted against a pregnant woman whose condition was known to the perpetrator, the penalties are imprisonment of two to ten years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.223

The imprisonment provided for by the two preceding articles shall be doubled when the blows and injuries have been inflicted on either an ascendant, or a spouse, or a child under eighteen years of age, or any person living in the same house as the perpetrator of the offence, or any other relative or in-law up to the 4th degree.

###### Art.224

Any person who has intentionally mutilated the body of a person, one of their limbs or organs or rendered that limb or organ unfit for its function, or has caused a person permanent incapacity to work, infirmity or mental illness, or has disfigured a person in a serious and permanent manner, is

punished by penal servitude of ten to twenty years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
Female genital mutilation practices are assimilated to mutilation.

###### Art.225

When blows struck or injuries inflicted voluntarily but without intention to cause death have nevertheless caused it, the offender is punished by penal servitude of five to twenty years and a fine of one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

##### Section 3 - Des voies de fait

###### Art.226

Punishable by a maximum of seven days penal servitude and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or only one of these penalties, are the perpetrators of assault or minor violence exercised voluntarily, provided that they have not injured or struck anyone, particularly those who have voluntarily, but without intention to insult, recklessly thrown at a person any object likely to inconvenience or soil them.

#### Chapitre 4 - De l'homicide et des lésions corporelles involontaires

##### Section 1 - De l'homicide involontaire

###### Art.227

Guilty of involuntary homicide is any person who has caused death through lack of foresight or precaution, through clumsiness, imprudence, inattention, negligence or failure to comply with an obligation of safety or prudence imposed by law or regulation, but without intention to harm the life of another.

###### Art.228

Any person who has involuntarily caused the death of a person is punished by penal servitude of three months to two years and a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or only one of these penalties.

##### Section 2 - Des lésions corporelles involontaires

###### Art.229

If the failures cited in the preceding article have only resulted in blows or injuries, the offender is punished by penal servitude of one month to one year and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs, or only one of these penalties.

###### Art.230

Punished by the penalties provided for in article 228 is any person who has, involuntarily, caused another person an illness or permanent incapacity to work by administering substances that are likely to cause death or seriously impair their health.

#### Chapitre 5 - Des épreuves superstitieuses et des pratiques barbares

##### Section 1 - Des épreuves superstitieuses

###### Art.231

Punished by penal servitude of one month to two years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or only one of these penalties, are the perpetrators of any superstitious ordeal consisting of subjecting, willingly or by force, a person to real or supposed physical harm, with a view to deducing from the effects produced, the imputability of an act or event or any other conclusion.  
If the ordeal has caused an illness or incapacity for personal work, or if it has resulted in the loss of absolute use of an organ or serious mutilation, the perpetrators are punished by penal servitude of two to twenty years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, or only one of these penalties.  
They are punished by penal servitude for life if the ordeal has caused the death of the victim.

###### Art.232

Authors or accomplices of the superstitious ordeal referred to in the preceding article are those who have participated in it according to the modes of criminal participation provided for in articles 37 and following of this Code.  
Also considered as authors or accomplices of this same offence are those who, in any manner whatsoever, have intentionally given rise to the resolution to demand, order or practice it.  
The person who has consented to undergo the physical harm constituting the ordeal is considered neither as author nor as accomplice.

###### Art.233

When a superstitious ordeal, whether or not it constitutes an offence, is the direct cause of an offence, those who have participated in it are punished as accomplices of the consequent offence, unless they could not have foreseen that it would be committed.  
There is no ground for prosecution when the offence consequent to the ordeal is a theft or detention not accompanied by ill-treatment of persons or another less serious offence.

###### Art.234

Considered as having participated in the superstitious ordeal not constituting an offence referred to in the preceding article are those who have lent their assistance according to the modes of participation provided for by this Code and those who, in any manner whatsoever, have intentionally given rise to the resolution to demand, order or practice the ordeal.

###### Art.235

Any person recognized as a member of a sect or association of a religious or other nature having as its object to harm the physical integrity of the human person is punished by penal servitude of five to twenty years.

##### Section 2 - Des pratiques barbares

###### Paragraphe 1 - De la mutilation d'un cadavre

###### Art.236

Punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs is any person who has maliciously mutilated a human corpse.

###### Art.237

Punished by penal servitude of three months to one year and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or only one of these penalties, is any person who has, with criminal intent, searched a person in a state of unconsciousness or found dead.

###### Paragraphe 2 - De la profanation des cimetières

###### Art.238

Punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article is any person who has destroyed or damaged tombs or gravestones.

###### Paragraphe 3 - De l'anthropophagie

###### Art.239

Any person who has provoked or prepared acts of cannibalism, participated in them, or has been found in possession of human flesh intended for acts of cannibalism, is punished by the penalty of penal servitude for life.

###### Paragraphe 4 - Du duel

###### Art.240

Provocation to a duel is punished by a fine of ten thousand to thirty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.241

Any person who, by any insult whatsoever, has given rise to provocation to a duel, is punished by a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.242

Any person who has fought in a duel is punished by penal servitude of six months to three years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs, or only one of these penalties.

###### Art.243

Any person who, in a duel, has caused the death of their adversary, is punished by penal servitude of one year to ten years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs.

#### Chapitre 6

Des atteintes à la liberté individuelle et à l'inviolabilité de la vie privée

##### Section 1 - De la traite et du trafic des êtres humains

###### Paragraphe 1 - Des peines principales

###### Art.244

Any person who has concluded an agreement having as its object to alienate, either gratuitously or for consideration, the freedom of a third person, is punished by penal servitude of five to ten years.  
Money, goods and other objects of value received in execution of the agreement are confiscated.  
Punished by the same penalties are persons who have concluded such an agreement for the purposes of sexual or domestic exploitation of the victim.

###### Art.245

Punished by the penalties provided for in article 244 is the act of introducing into Burundi individuals intended to be the subject of the aforementioned agreement, or of removing individuals from the country with a view to said agreement to be concluded abroad.  
However, the penalty is increased to twenty years if the person who has been the subject thereof, either inside or outside Burundi, is a child under eighteen years of age.

###### Art.246

Is punished by penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, whoever, for the purpose of exploitation, recruits, transports, transfers, harbours or receives a person by means of the threat or use of force or other forms of coercion, by abduction, fraud, deception, abuse of authority or of a position of vulnerability, or by the giving or receiving of payments or benefits to achieve the consent of a person having control over another person.  
The recruitment, transportation, transfer, harbouring or receipt of a child for the purpose of exploitation is punished by the same penalty without recourse to the aforementioned means.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Des peines complémentaires

###### Art.247

Without prejudice to the other provisions of this Code relating to complementary penalties applicable to natural persons, the person to be convicted on the basis of the preceding article may be subject, wholly or partially, to penalties relating to the deprivation of civic, civil and family rights.

###### Art.248

Irrespective of whether the operator, owner, lessee or manager is a natural or legal person, the court may order the temporary or permanent, partial or total closure of the establishment in which the offence provided for in article 246 was committed.

###### Art.249

The special confiscation provided for by the Penal Code is applied to the person convicted on the basis of article 246, even when the ownership of the things to which it relates does not belong to the convicted person, without however such confiscation being able to prejudice the rights of third parties in good faith.

###### Paragraphe 3 - Des infractions connexes à la traite des personnes

###### Art.250

Whoever benefits, in any form whatsoever, from advantages resulting from the services or work of another person knowing or being unable to ignore that they are provided in one or more of the conditions referred to in article 246, is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of two to five years or a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.251

Is punished by penal servitude of six months to three years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, whoever withholds, procures, destroys, conceals, causes to disappear, confiscates, alters, reproduces or possesses a travel or identity document of another person or facilitates its fraudulent use, with the intention of committing an offence referred to in article 246 or facilitating its commission.

###### Art.252

Whoever discloses or makes known without being authorized to do so, information obtained in the course of their official duties and which makes it possible to identify a victim or witness of the offence provided for in article 246, is punished by penal servitude of six months to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.253

Any commercial carrier who fails to verify that each passenger is in possession of identity documents for entry into the country of transit or destination concerned, is punished by a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs as well as the suspension or withdrawal of their authorization to transport, as well as payment of the costs of stay, accommodation or removal occasioned by illegal entry into the territory.

###### Paragraphe 4 - Des circonstances aggravantes

###### Art.254

The offence provided for by article 246 is punished by penal servitude of ten to fifteen years and a fine of three hundred thousand to two million Burundian francs when it is committed:
1° By a person who abuses the authority or facilities conferred upon them by their functions;  
2° By a public official in the exercise of their functions;  
3° When it constitutes an act of participation in the principal or accessory activity of an association, whether or not the offender has the capacity of leader.

###### Art.255

The offence provided for by article 246 is punished by penal servitude of fifteen to twenty years and a fine of five hundred thousand to ten million Burundian francs in the following cases:
1° When it is committed against a child;  
2° When it is committed by abusing the vulnerability in which a person finds themselves by reason of their illegal or precarious administrative situation, their age or state of pregnancy, an illness, an infirmity or a physical or mental deficiency, such that the person has in fact no other real and acceptable choice but to submit to this abuse;  
3° When it is committed by using, directly or indirectly, fraudulent manoeuvres, violence, threats or any form of coercion;  
4° When the victim is deliberately or through gross negligence placed in danger;  
5° When the offence causes an incurable disease, permanent physical or psychological incapacity, complete loss of an organ or the use of an organ, or serious mutilation;  
6° When the activity concerned constitutes a habitual activity;  
7° When the offence constitutes an act of preparation for a principal or accessory activity of a criminal organization, whether or not the offender has the capacity of leader.

###### Art.256

The offence provided for by article 246 is punished by penal servitude for life and a fine of seven hundred and fifty thousand to twenty million Burundian francs in the case where the offence causes the death of the victim, including death by suicide.

##### Section 2 - De l'enlèvement

###### Art.257

Is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years, whoever, by violence, trickery or threats, has sequestered or caused to be sequestered, has abducted or caused to be abducted any person whatsoever.  
If the abduction or sequestration was carried out either with the aid of a uniform or a regulatory or apparently regulatory insignia, or under a false name or on a false order of the public authority, the penalty is penal servitude of ten to twenty years.  
The same penalty is applicable if the abduction was carried out with the aid of a motorized means of transport or if the victim suffered threats of any nature whatsoever.  
If the abduction or sequestration was carried out by a member of the defence and security forces, the offender is punished by penal servitude of twenty to thirty years

In any event, when the abducted or sequestered person has been subjected to a ransom demand, to acts of barbarity or when these acts have caused death, the offender is punished by penal servitude for life.

###### Art.258

Is punished by the penalties provided for by and according to the provisions of the preceding article, whoever has abducted or caused to be abducted, arrested or caused to be arrested, detained or caused to be detained any persons whatsoever in order to sell them as slaves, or who has disposed of persons placed under their authority for the same purpose.

##### Section 3 - De la violation de domicile

###### Art.259

Is punished by penal servitude of eight days to two years and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, whoever, without order of the authority and outside the cases where the law permits entry into the domicile of private individuals against their will, has entered a house, room or dwelling inhabited by another or their dependencies, either by means of threats or violence against persons, or by means of breaking and entering, scaling or false keys.  
Any official of the administrative order, any magistrate, any judicial police officer or agent of the public force who, acting in that capacity, has entered the domicile of private individuals against their will outside the cases provided for and without the formalities prescribed by law, is punished by penal servitude of one year to two years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.260

Any individual who, outside the cases provided for by the preceding article, enters against the will of the occupant into a house, an apartment, a room, a hut, a cabin, a dwelling or their enclosed dependencies, is punished by penal servitude of eight days to one month and a fine of thirty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

##### Section 4 - De la violation du secret des correspondances

###### Art.261

Any person who, outside the cases provided for by law, has opened or suppressed letters, postcards or other objects entrusted to the post, or ordered or facilitated the opening or suppression of these letters, cards or objects, is punished by a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs for each case.  
The fine may be increased to two hundred thousand Burundian francs if the letter or dispatch was registered or insured, or if it contained realizable securities.  
Independently of the fine, the offender may be punished by penal servitude of six months maximum if he is a postal agent or officially commissioned as such.

###### Art.262

Any postal agent or any person officially commissioned to ensure postal service who, outside the cases where the law would oblige him to do so, has revealed the existence or content of a letter, a postcard or any other dispatch entrusted to the post, is punished

by penal servitude of one month to six months and a fine of one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

#### Chapitre 7 - De la révélation du secret professionnel

###### Art.263

Any person who is a depositary by status or by profession of secrets entrusted to him who, outside the case where he is called to give testimony in court and that where the law obliges him to make these secrets known, has revealed them, is punished by penal servitude of one year to two years and a fine of twenty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
The penalty is increased to five years of penal servitude when the offence is committed by a member of the defence and security forces.

#### Chapitre 8

Des imputations dommageables, des injures, de l'aversion raciale, des dénonciations calomnieuses et du harcèlement

##### Section 1 - De l'imputation dommageable

###### Art.264

Whoever has maliciously and publicly imputed a specific fact which is of a nature to harm the honour and consideration of that person or to expose him to public contempt, is punished by penal servitude of one month to one year and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

##### Section 2 - Des injures

###### Art.265

Whoever has publicly insulted a person is punished by penal servitude of one month to one year and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

##### Section 3 - De l'aversion raciale

###### Art.266

Whoever has manifested racial or ethnic aversion or hatred or has incited or encouraged, or committed an act of a nature to provoke this aversion or this hatred, is punished by penal servitude of six months to two years and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only,

##### Section 4 - Des dénonciations calomnieuses

###### Art.267

Is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only
1° Whoever has made in writing or verbally to a judicial authority or to a public official who has the duty to refer the matter to said authority, a calumnious denunciation;  

2° Whoever has made in writing or verbally to a person calumnious imputations against his subordinate.

###### Art.268

Is punished by penal servitude of eight days to one month and a fine of fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, whoever has directed against a person insults other than those provided for in the preceding provisions of this chapter.

##### Section 5 - Du harcèlement

###### Art.269

Is qualified as harassment, the act of making malicious and repeated telephone calls, sending anonymous letters or leaflets to another person or uttering threats of any kind to him with a view to disturbing his tranquility.  
Whoever has harassed a person within the meaning of the preceding paragraph is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of one month to two years and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

##### Section 6 - De la disposition commune

###### Art.270

For all the offences provided for in this chapter, the judge may order, at the expense of the convicted person, the publication of the judgment as a complementary penalty.

### Titre 2 - Des infractions contre les propriétés

#### Chapitre 1 - Des vols et des extorsions

##### Section 1 - Du vol simple

###### Art.271

Whoever has fraudulently misappropriated a thing which does not belong to him is guilty of theft.  
The fraudulent misappropriation of telephone impulses to the prejudice of another person is assimilated to theft.

###### Art.272

The fraudulent misappropriation of water and electricity to the prejudice of another person is assimilated to theft. Constitute acts of fraudulent misappropriation of water or electricity:
1° The act of preventing all or part of the water or electricity to be used from passing through the metering equipment;  
2° The act of installing a device making it possible to prevent the meter from recording the consumption of water or electricity;  

3° The act, by whatever process, of turning back the indexes indicated by the metering equipment;  
4° The act of manipulating the metering equipment with a view to preventing it from recording all the quantity of water or electricity actually consumed;  
5° The act of obtaining a supply of water or electricity by means of a connection without having subscribed to the corresponding subscription with the distributor.

###### Art.273

Commits theft of use whoever against the will of the owner makes use of a thing belonging to another person for a limited time, then abandons it or returns it to him.

###### Art.274

In the absence of one or other of the aggravating circumstances specified in article 275 below, theft as provided for in articles 271, 272 and 273 above is punished by penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

##### Section 2 - Aggravated theft

###### Art.275

Theft is punished by five to seven years of penal servitude if it is committed with one of the following circumstances:
•	1° when it is committed by a person holding public authority or charged with a public service mission;  
•	2° if the offender acted by assuming the title or insignia of a public official or by alleging a false order from public authority;  
•	3° if the theft was committed in a dwelling or its outbuildings or in a place used or intended for the storage of funds, securities, goods or materials;  
•	4° if the theft involved a motorized vehicle;  
•	5° if the theft involved livestock;  
•	6° if the theft was committed by a person habitually working in the dwelling where they stole;  
•	7° if the theft involved standing crops.  
Theft is punished by five to ten years of penal servitude if it was committed with two of the circumstances specified below:
•	1° when it is committed by several persons acting as perpetrators or accomplices without constituting an organized gang;  
•	2° when it is committed by a person holding public authority or charged with a public service mission;  
•	3° if the offender acted by assuming the title or insignia of a public official or by alleging a false order from public authority;  
•	4° if the theft was committed at night;  
•	5° if the theft was committed in a dwelling or its outbuildings or in a place used or intended for the storage of funds, securities, goods or materials;  
•	6° if the perpetrator used breaking and entering, scaling or false keys;  
•	7° if the theft involved a motorized vehicle;  

•	8° if the theft involved livestock;  
•	9° if the theft was committed by a person habitually working in the dwelling where they stole;  
•	10° if the theft was committed with violence resulting in incapacity of eight days or more.  
Theft is punished by five to fifteen years of penal servitude:
•	1° if it was committed with at least three of the circumstances specified above in the preceding paragraph;  
•	2° if to facilitate the offense or to ensure their escape, the offender used a vehicle or any other motorized or non-motorized device, obtained through a crime or an offense;  
•	3° if it was committed by an organized gang.  
Theft is punished by five to twenty years of penal servitude:
•	1° if it was committed with violence resulting in permanent incapacity or serious mutilation;  
•	2° if the theft was committed with the use or threat of use of a weapon;  
•	3° if it was committed by an organized gang and was preceded, accompanied or followed by violence against others.  
Theft by an organized gang is punished by ten to thirty years of penal servitude when it is committed either with use or threat of a weapon.  
Theft is punished by life imprisonment when it is preceded, accompanied or followed by violence resulting in death.

##### Section 3 - Misappropriation of pledge or seized object

###### Art.276

The act by a debtor, borrower or third party pledgor of destroying or misappropriating the object constituted as pledge is punished by two to five years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or only one of these penalties.

###### Art.277

The act by the person whose property is seized of destroying or misappropriating an object seized in their hands as security for the rights of a creditor and entrusted to their custody or that of a third party is punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article.

##### Section 4 - Extortion

###### Art.278

Any person who has extorted, by means of violence or threats, either funds, securities, movable objects, obligations, notes, promises, receipts, or the signature or delivery of any document containing or effecting an obligation, disposition or discharge, is punished by penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of fifty thousand francs to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  

A public representative, official, magistrate or member of the defense and security forces who carries out a seizure not recorded by a report in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure or by a discharge document serving as such is punished by the same penalties.

###### Art.279.- The penalty provided for in the preceding article is increased to ten years

1° When the extortion is preceded, accompanied or followed by violence against others resulting in total incapacity to work for at least eight days;  
2° When the extortion is committed against a vulnerable person by reason of their age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or a state of pregnancy apparent or known to its perpetrator;  
3° When the extortion is committed by a member of the defense and security forces, a public representative, an official or a magistrate.

###### Art.280

Extortion is punished by twenty years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand francs to five hundred thousand Burundian francs when it is preceded, accompanied or followed by violence against others resulting in incapacity, mutilation or permanent infirmity.

###### Art.281

Extortion is punished by twenty years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs when it is committed with use or threat of any weapon.

###### Art.282

Extortion by an organized gang is punished by twenty years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand francs to five hundred thousand francs.  
The penalty is increased to thirty years of penal servitude when the extortion is preceded, accompanied or followed by violence against others, resulting in mutilation or permanent infirmity.  
Extortion by an organized gang is punished by thirty years of penal servitude when it is committed with use or threat of any weapon.

###### Art.283

Extortion is punished by life imprisonment and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, when it is preceded, accompanied or followed by violence resulting in death.

##### Section 5 - Blackmail

###### Art.284

Blackmail is the act of obtaining, by threatening to reveal or impute facts likely to harm honor or reputation, either a signature, commitment or waiver, or the disclosure of a secret, or the delivery of funds, securities or any property.  

Blackmail is punished by one to five years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
The penalty may be increased to ten years when the perpetrator has carried out their threat.

##### Section 6 - Meaning of terms used in this title

###### Paragraphe 1 - Theft committed at night

###### Art.285

Theft committed during the night is theft committed between sunset and sunrise.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Inhabited dwelling

###### Art.286

Any building, apartment, lodging, lodge, cabin, even mobile, or other place used for habitation is deemed an inhabited dwelling.

###### Paragraphe 3 - Outbuildings of an inhabited dwelling

###### Art.287

Courtyards, farmyards, gardens and all other enclosed land, as well as barns, stables, stalls and all other buildings enclosed therein, whatever their use, even if they form a separate enclosure within the main enclosure, are deemed outbuildings of an inhabited dwelling.

###### Art.288

Mobile pens intended to contain livestock in the countryside, however they are made, are deemed outbuildings of an inhabited dwelling when they are established on the same piece of land, with mobile cabins or other shelters intended for the guards.

###### Paragraph 4 - Weapons

###### Art.289

The word "weapon" includes any machine, instrument, utensil or other sharp, piercing or blunt object which has been seized to kill, injure or strike, even if it has not been used.

###### Paragraph 5 - Violence and threats

###### Art.290

By "violence", the law means acts of physical constraint exercised on persons.  
By "threat", the law means all means of moral constraint through fear of imminent harm.

###### Paragraph 6 - Breaking and entering

###### Art.291

Breaking and entering consists of forcing, breaking, damaging, demolishing or removing any closing device of a house, building, any construction or its outbuildings, a boat, a wagon, a vehicle, forcing cupboards or closed furniture intended to remain in place and to protect the effects they contain.

###### Art.292

Assimilated to theft with breaking and entering are the removal of the furniture referred to in the preceding article, theft committed by means of breaking seals.

###### Paragraph 7 - Scaling

###### Art.293

"Scaling" means any entry into houses, buildings, courtyards, farmyards, any edifices, gardens, parks, enclosures, executed over walls, doors, roofs or any other type of enclosure; entry through an opening other than that which has been established to serve as an entrance.

###### Paragraph 8 - False keys

###### Art.294.- "False keys" means

1.	All picks, skeleton keys, master keys, imitated, counterfeited or altered keys;  
2.	Keys which have not been intended by the owner, tenant, innkeeper or lodger for the locks, padlocks or any closures for the opening of which the perpetrator has used them;  
3.	Lost or stolen keys which have been used to commit the theft.  
However, the use of false keys constitutes an aggravating circumstance only if it took place to open objects whose breaking and entering resulted in an aggravation of the penalty.

###### Art.295

Whoever has fraudulently counterfeited or altered keys is sentenced to penal servitude of three months to two years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or only one of these penalties.  
If the offender is a locksmith by profession, he is punished by penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or only one of these penalties.

#### Chapter 2 - Fraud

##### Section 1 - Bankruptcy

###### Art.296

Punishable by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs is the merchant declared bankrupt who, fraudulently:
1° Has misappropriated or concealed part of his assets or acknowledged himself debtor of sums he did not owe;  
2° Has removed his books or removed, erased or altered their content.

###### Art.297

Punishable by penal servitude of three months to one year and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs is the merchant declared bankrupt who:
1° After cessation of payments, has favoured a creditor to the detriment of the estate; 2° Has, for his personal needs or those of his household, made excessive expenditures;  
3° Has consumed large sums in gambling, in operations of pure chance or fictitious operations;  
4° Has, with the intention of delaying his bankruptcy, made purchases to resell above the market price or, in the same situation, engaged in loans, circulation of bills and other ruinous means of procuring funds.

###### Art.298

Punishable by the penalties provided for in the preceding article are persons who have simulated expenditures or losses or have not been able to justify the existence or use of all or part of the assets as they appear in the accounting documents and books at the date of cessation of payments and of all property, of whatever nature, obtained subsequently.

###### Art.299

Punishable by penal servitude of three months to one year and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs is the merchant declared bankrupt:
1° Who has not kept the commercial books or made the inventories prescribed by legal and regulatory provisions;  
2° Whose books or inventories are incomplete, irregular or written in a language other than that whose use, in this matter, is prescribed by law;  
3°. Whose books or inventories do not show his true active and passive situation, without however there being fraud;  
4° Who has contracted, without receiving values in exchange, commitments deemed too considerable, having regard to his situation when he contracted them;  

5° Who without being unfortunate and in good faith, has already previously been bankrupt;  
6° Who, following a previous bankruptcy, has not fulfilled all the obligations of a concordat in progress or against whom the resolution of the concordat has been pronounced;  
7° Who has not made the declaration of cessation of payments under the conditions and within the time limits provided for by the legislation on bankruptcy;  
8° Who, without lawful cause, has absented himself without the authorization of the judge or has not appeared in person at the summonses which have been made to him by the judge or the curator.

##### Section 2 - Cases assimilated to bankruptcy

###### Art.300

Punishable by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs are the presidents, administrators, directors, managers or liquidators of a commercial company declared bankrupt and, in general, any person having, in law or in fact, directly or through an intermediary, administered, managed or liquidated such a company under cover or in place of its legal representatives who, fraudulently:
1° Have misappropriated or concealed part of the assets or acknowledged the company as debtor of sums they did not owe;  
2° Have removed the company's books or removed, erased or altered their content;  
3° Have failed to publish the company's deed or the deeds amending it in the forms and time limits provided for by law;  
4° Have, in these deeds, made indications contrary to the truth; 5° Have caused the bankruptcy of the company.

###### Art.301

Punishable by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs are the presidents, administrators, directors, managers or liquidators of a commercial company declared bankrupt and, in general, any person having, in law or in fact, directly or through an intermediary, administered, managed or liquidated such a company under cover or in place of its legal representatives who, fraudulently:
1° After cessation of the company's payments, have favoured a creditor to the detriment of the estate;  
2° Have committed the company to excessive expenditures or costs;  
3° Have, on behalf of the company, consumed large sums in gambling, or who have made for it operations of pure chance or fictitious operations;  

4° Have, with the intention of delaying the bankruptcy of the company, made purchases to resell below the market price, or, with the same intention, engaged in loans, circulation of bills and other ruinous means of procuring funds;  
5° Have simulated expenditures or losses, or do not justify the existence or use of the assets of the company's last inventory and the monies, securities, furniture and effects, of whatever nature they may be, which subsequently came to the company;  
6° Have distributed among the members of the company dividends not taken from actual profits.

###### Art.302

Are punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article, the presidents, directors, managers, or liquidators of a commercial company declared bankrupt and, in general, any person having, in law or in fact, directly or through an intermediary, administered, managed or liquidated such a company under cover or in place of its legal representatives when, through their fault:
1° The books required by legislation on commercial matters have not been kept, the inventories required by the same legislation have not been made; they have been written in a language other than that whose use, in this matter, is prescribed by law; they are incomplete or irregular, the same books and inventories do not reflect the true assets and liabilities of the company, without there having been fraud;  
2° The acknowledgment of the cessation of payment of the company has not been made under the conditions and within the time limits provided for by the legislation on bankruptcies.

###### Art.303

Are punished by the penalties provided for in article 301 the presidents, directors, managers, representatives of creditors or liquidators of a commercial company declared bankrupt, and in general, any person having, in law or in fact, directly or through an intermediary, administered, managed or liquidated such a company under cover or in place of its legal representatives:
1° Have not provided the information requested of them, either by the judge or by the trustee, or who have given inaccurate information;  
2° Have deliberately harmed the interests of creditors or the debtor, either by using for their own benefit sums received in the performance of their mission, or by granting themselves advantages which they knew were not due;  
3° Have made, in their own interest, use of the powers at their disposal which they knew to be contrary to the interests of the creditors or the debtor;  
4° Have acquired the debtor's property or used it for their own benefit;  
5° Have not appeared in person at the summons of the judge or the trustee without legitimate impediment.

###### Art.304

Is punished by penal servitude of two months to one year and a fine of twenty thousand to fifty thousand francs any person who, having participated in any capacity in the proceedings, acquires, directly or indirectly, property of the debtor or uses it for their own benefit.

###### Art.305

Are punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand francs to five hundred thousand francs:
1° Those who, in the interest of the bankrupt, have removed, concealed or received all or part of his property
2° Those who, fraudulently, have presented false or exaggerated claims in the bankruptcy;  
3° The trustee who has been guilty of malfeasance in his management.

###### Art.306

Are punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article, those who have stipulated, either with the bankrupt or with any other persons, special advantages by reason of their vote in the declaration of bankruptcy, or who have made a special agreement from which would result, in their favor, an advantage at the expense of the estate.

##### Section 3 - De l'organisation frauduleuse de l'insolvabilité

###### Art.307

The act, by a debtor, even before the judicial decision establishing his debt, of organizing or aggravating his insolvency, either by increasing the liabilities or by decreasing the assets of his patrimony, or by decreasing or concealing all or part of his income, or by concealing some of his property, with a view to evading the execution of a conviction of a patrimonial nature pronounced by a criminal court or, in delictual, quasi-delictual or maintenance matters, pronounced by a civil court, is punished by penal servitude of one year to three years and a fine of five hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.  
Is punished by the same penalties the joint and several co-debtor who organizes his insolvency by any means whatsoever with a view to placing his obligations on the other debtors.

##### Section 4 - Des abus de confiance

###### Art.308

Whoever has fraudulently, either misappropriated or dissipated to the detriment of another, effects, money, merchandise, bills, receipts, writings of any nature containing or creating an obligation, disposition or discharge, and which had been entrusted to him on condition of returning them or making a specific use or employment of them, is punished by penal servitude of two months to three years and a fine of fifty thousand francs to two hundred thousand francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.309

The penalties may be increased from two years to five years of penal servitude and a fine of five hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs or one of these penalties only when the breach of trust is committed:

1° By a person who appeals to the public in order to obtain the remittance of funds or securities, either for his own account or as a director or agent in law or in fact of an industrial or commercial enterprise;  
2° By any other person who, habitually, engages in or lends his assistance, even on an ancillary basis, to transactions involving the property of third parties on whose behalf he collects funds or securities.

###### Art.310

The penalties may be increased to ten years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only when the breach of trust is committed by an officer of the court or by a public or ministerial officer, either in the exercise of or on the occasion of the exercise of his functions, or by reason of his capacity.

##### Section 5 - Du stellionat et des cas assimilés

###### Art.311

Is punished by penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of five hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, whoever has sold or mortgaged immovable property that does not belong to him.

###### Art.312

Is punished by penal servitude of two months to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, whoever has sold or pledged the same movable property or any right to two or more persons.

##### Section 5 - De l'usure

###### Art.313

Is punished by penal servitude of three months to two years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, whoever, taking advantage of the weaknesses, passions, needs or ignorance of the debtor, has himself promised, by reason of a credit transaction, a loan contract or any other contract indicating a remittance of movable value, whatever the apparent form of the contract, for himself or for another an interest or other advantages manifestly exceeding normal interest.

##### Section 6 - Du détournement de main d'œuvre

###### Art.314

Is punished by penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, whoever has fraudulently used for his own benefit or for the benefit of a third party, the services of employees placed under his orders by the master with a view to work to be performed for the latter or for another.

##### Section 7 - Fraud and deception

###### Paragraphe 1 - Fraud

###### Art.315

Whoever, with the intent to appropriate property belonging to another, has obtained or had delivered to them funds, movables, obligations, receipts, either by using false names or false qualities, or by employing fraudulent maneuvers to persuade the existence of false enterprises, of an imaginary power or credit, to create hope or fear of a success, an accident or any other chimerical event, to otherwise abuse confidence or credulity, is punished by penal servitude of two months to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.316

The penalties are two years to five years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs or one of these penalties only when the fraud is committed:
1° By a person who appeals to the public with a view to issuing securities or with a view to collecting funds for humanitarian or social mutual aid purposes;  
2° By a person who is a depositary of public authority or charged with a public service mission, in the exercise of their functions or mission;  
3° By a person who unduly appropriates the quality of a person who is a depositary of public authority or charged with a public service mission;  
4° Against a vulnerable person by reason of their age, illness, infirmity, apparent or known physical or mental deficiency of its perpetrator;  
5° By an organized gang.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Deception

###### Art.317

Is punished by three months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, whoever has deceived the buyer:
1° As to the identity of the thing sold, by fraudulently delivering a thing other than the determined object on which the transaction related;  
2° As to the nature or origin of the thing sold, by fraudulently selling or delivering a thing which, similar in appearance to that which they purchased or believed they purchased, disappoints the buyer in what they principally sought.

###### Art.318

Is punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article whoever, by fraudulent maneuvers, has deceived:

1° The buyer or the seller as to the quality of the things sold; 2° The buyer or the seller as to the quantity of the things sold;  
3° The parties engaged in a contract for the hire of work, or one of them, as to the elements which must serve to calculate the salary.

##### Section 8 - Receiving property obtained by means of an offense

###### Art.319

Whoever has received in whole or in part things obtained by means of an offense is punished by penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of twenty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

##### Section 9 - Fraudulent concealment

###### Art.320

Are punished by penal servitude of eight days to two years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, those who, having found a movable thing belonging to another, or having obtained possession of it by chance, have fraudulently concealed it or delivered it to third parties.

##### Section 10 - Defrauding an innkeeper

###### Art.321

Is punished by penal servitude of eight days to two years and a fine of twenty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only whoever, knowing that they are in the absolute impossibility of paying:
1° Has had served to them, in an establishment intended for that purpose, drinks or food which they have consumed in whole or in part;  
2° Has obtained lodging in a hotel where they presented themselves as a traveler; 3° Has rented a hired vehicle;  
4° Has themselves transported in a bus or any other means of public transport providing remunerated transportation of persons;  
5° Has had supplied to them fuel or lubricant;  
6° Has themselves offered a telephone, fax, Internet or secretarial service; 7° Has themselves offered water and electricity.

###### Art.322

The offenses provided for in the preceding article may only be prosecuted upon complaint by the injured party. Payment by the perpetrator of the price and costs advanced before judgment by the complaining party or the withdrawal by the latter extinguishes the public action.

##### Section 11 - Special provisions

###### Art.323

Are not punishable and may only give rise to civil reparations, thefts committed:
1° By ascendants against their children and other descendants; 2° By descendants against their ascendants;  
3° By one spouse against the other spouse, except in cases of divorce proceedings or separation.

###### Art.324

Thefts committed between relatives, collaterals or relatives by marriage up to and including the fourth degree, may only be prosecuted upon complaint by the injured person. Withdrawal of the complaint ends the prosecution.

###### Art.325

The provisions of the two preceding articles do not apply to all other persons who have participated in the theft or receiving of stolen objects.

##### Section 12 - Instruments without provision

###### Art.326

Is punished by penal servitude of one month to two years and a fine equivalent to twenty percent of the amount shown on the drawn instrument or one of these penalties only, whoever has fraudulently procured funds, securities or discharge by means of an instrument drawn either on a person who does not exist, or on a person who had not authorized them to draw on them and whom they knew not to be their debtor or not to be so at maturity.

###### Art.327

Is punished by penal servitude of three months to five years and a fine equivalent to twenty percent of the amount shown on the check or one of these penalties only:
1° Whoever, in bad faith, issues a check without prior and available provision or with provision less than the amount of the check;  
2° Whoever, except for lawful opposition in case of loss or their incapacity to receive, withdraws, after issuance, all or part of the provision or forbids the drawee to pay;  
3° Whoever transfers a check knowing that there is no provision, or that the provision is insufficient or that it is not available;  
4° Whoever, knowingly, agrees to receive a check issued under the conditions provided for in the preceding paragraphs.

###### Art.328

In the cases referred to in the two preceding articles, the applicable penalty does not exceed one quarter of the maximum imprisonment and fine provided for by these articles or one of these penalties only, if the holder has been compensated before the court has been seized.  

The judge pronounces at the same time the withdrawal of the checkbook from the issuer for a period not exceeding one year.

#### Chapitre 3 - Destruction, degradation, damage

##### Section 1 - Arson

###### Art.329

Are punished by penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, those who have intentionally set fire to buildings or any other buildings whatsoever belonging to another but uninhabited at the time of the fire.  
If the fire has reached buildings which, to the knowledge of the perpetrator, sheltered livestock, a stock of food or movable property, the penalty is increased to fifteen to twenty years.

###### Art.330

Are punished by penal servitude for life, those who have set fire either to buildings, ships, warehouses, or any other places whatsoever used for habitation or to any places, even uninhabited, and containing one or more persons at the time of the fire, if, according to the circumstances, the perpetrator should have presumed that one or more persons were there at the time of the offense.

###### Art.331

Where the fire has caused the death of one or more persons who, to the knowledge of the perpetrator, were in the premises set on fire at the time of the offence, and if the death must be considered as a necessary or probable consequence thereof, the offender is punished by penal servitude for life.

###### Art.332

Those who, outside the cases covered by the regulations on the burning of grasses and standing vegetation, have set fire to forests, woods and standing or cut crops are punished by penal servitude of ten to twenty years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.333

Exclusive owners of the things referred to in the two preceding articles who have set fire to them with malicious or fraudulent intent are punished by penal servitude of ten to twenty years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
Anyone who, with the intention of committing one of the acts provided for in the preceding articles, has set fire to any objects placed in such a way as to communicate it to the thing he wished to destroy is punished as if he had directly set fire to that latter thing.

###### Art.334

Those who, without prejudice to the provisions relating to the regulations on the burning of grasses and standing vegetation, have, through lack of foresight and precaution, set fire to forests, woods and standing or cut crops are punished by penal servitude of two months to one year and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.335

Fire to movable or immovable property of others which has been caused by lack of foresight or precaution is punished by penal servitude of eight days to three months and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, or by one of these penalties only.

##### Section 2 - De la destruction des constructions, machines et autres monuments

###### Art.336

Anyone who has destroyed, overturned or damaged, by any means whatsoever, in whole or in part, buildings, bridges, dikes, roads, railways, telegraphic or telephonic apparatus, or other constructions belonging to others, is punished by penal servitude of one month to five years and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.337

Anyone who has destroyed or damaged commemorative signs of monuments, statues or other objects intended for public utility or decoration is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

##### Section 3

De la destruction et de la dégradation d'arbres, de récoltes ou d'autres propriétés

###### Art.338

Those who, in enclosed or unenclosed places, have maliciously destroyed or damaged trees, crops, agricultural instruments or other property, movable or immovable, belonging to others are punished by penal servitude of one month to one year and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.339

Anyone who has, even without malicious intent, destroyed or damaged, without title or right, trees, crops, agricultural instruments or other property, movable or immovable, is punished by penal servitude of one month to one year and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

##### Section 4 - De la destruction d'animaux

###### Art.340

Anyone who has maliciously and without necessity, killed or seriously injured livestock or domestic animals belonging to others is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.341

Anyone who undertakes without prior authorization any activity likely to harm rare, threatened or endangered animal species, as well as their natural environment, is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.

##### Section 5 - De l'enlèvement, du déplacement ou du dépassement des bornes

###### Art.342

Those who, without being validly authorized, have removed, displaced or exceeded and those who have maliciously damaged boundary markers, signals or geodetic markers, have modified their appearance, indications or inscriptions are punished by penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of ten thousand francs to one hundred thousand francs or by one of these penalties only.  
The penalties are one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only if the boundary markers referred to were fixed by a judicial authority.

### Titre 3 - Des infractions contre la foi publique

#### Chapitre 1

De la contrefaçon, de la falsification et de l'imitation des signes monétaires

###### Art.343

Those who have counterfeited or fraudulently altered or falsified metallic coins or bearer notes having legal tender in Burundi or abroad, and those who have introduced or issued on the territory of Burundi counterfeited, falsified or fraudulently altered coins or bearer notes are punished by penal servitude of five to twenty years and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.

###### Art.344

Those who, without being guilty of participation, have knowingly obtained metallic coins or bearer notes referred to in the preceding article and have put them into circulation are punished by penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
Those who, with the aim of putting them into circulation, have received or obtained metallic coins or bearer notes referred to in the preceding article are punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.345

Those who, having received as good metallic coins or bearer notes having legal tender in Burundi or abroad, counterfeited or falsified, have put them into circulation with knowledge of the defects are punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.346

Those who have manufactured, distributed or put into circulation, either tokens, medals or metallic pieces, or printed matter or forms, obtained by any process whatsoever and presenting by their external form, with coins or bearer notes having legal tender in Burundi or abroad, a resemblance having the purpose of facilitating their acceptance in place of the imitated values are punished by penal servitude of two to ten years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.347

Those who have given or offered in payment tokens, medals, metallic pieces, printed matter or forms, obtained by any process whatsoever and presenting by their external form with coins or bearer notes having legal tender in Burundi or abroad, a resemblance of a nature to facilitate their acceptance in place of the imitated values are punished as guilty of deception as provided for in article 317.

#### Chapitre 2

De la contrefaçon ou, falsification des sceaux, des timbres, poinçons ou autres marques de l'autorité

###### Art.348

Those are punished by penal servitude of five to fifteen years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only:
1° Those who have counterfeited or falsified the seals, stamps, hallmarks or other marks of Burundi, of a foreign State or of national or international public administrations;  
2° Those who have fraudulently made use of these counterfeited or falsified objects; 3° Those who have knowingly displayed or offered them for sale.

###### Art.349.- Are punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article

1° Those who have received or unduly obtained the true seals, stamps, hallmarks or other marks of the State, or of a foreign State, of national or international public administrations, and have made an application or use thereof prejudicial to the rights and interests of the State, of any authority whatsoever or even of a private individual;  
2° Those who have manufactured or falsified papers or printed materials with official letterheads in use in the assemblies established by the Constitution, public administrations, public prosecutors' offices or courts;  
3° Those who have put up for sale, distributed or made any use of such papers with letterheads thus manufactured or falsified.

###### Art.350

Are punished by a penalty of five to ten years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only:
1° Those who have counterfeited or falsified the seals, stamps or other mark of any authority whatsoever, whether of a private establishment, bank, industry or commerce, or of a private individual;  
2° Those who have made use of such seals, stamps or other marks of authority thus counterfeited or falsified.

###### Art.351

Are punished by penal servitude of three to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, those who, without

having the right to do so, have used the flag, emblem or name of the United Nations or of the Red Cross or other similar insignia that may lead to confusion.  
The penalty may be increased to ten years, if the flag or emblem of the Red Cross has been used abusively in time of war by a person who is not entitled to do so under the Geneva Convention of 6 July 1906 on the amelioration of the condition of the wounded and sick in armies in the field.

###### Art.352

Those who, with intent to defraud, have subjected postage stamps or postcards of Burundi or of foreign States or international organizations to any alteration or preparation whatsoever or who have, with or without fraudulent intent, counterfeited such stamps or such cards, are punished by a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs for each case.

###### Art.353

Natural persons guilty of the offences listed above in this chapter may also incur at least one of the following additional penalties:
1° Publication of the judgment or decision; 2° Presentation of the convicted person to the public; 3° Deprivation of civil rights;  
4° Prohibition from exercising a public or professional function;  
5° Permanent prohibition from the territory if the convicted person is a foreigner;  
6° Confiscation of the object, whether forming the corpus of the offence, or produced by the offence, or used to commit the offence.

#### Chapitre 3 - De l'usurpation des fonctions publiques

###### Art.354

Whoever has falsely attributed to himself the quality or rank of public official or has publicly worn any insignia or emblem intended to create belief in the existence of a public mandate is punished by penal servitude of one year to three years and a fine of ten thousand francs to one hundred thousand francs or one of these penalties only.  
If the insignia or emblem is not intended but is simply of a nature to create belief in the existence of a public mandate, the person who, publicly, has worn it, allowed or caused it to be worn by a person in his service or under his authority, is punished by penal servitude of eight days to one month and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.355

The use, without right, of a title attached to a profession regulated by public authority or of an official diploma or of a quality whose conditions of attribution are fixed by public authority is punished by one year to three years of penal

servitude as principal penalty and a fine of one hundred thousand francs to three hundred thousand francs or one of these penalties only.

#### Chapitre 4 - Du port illégal de décoration

###### Art.356

Any person who has publicly worn a decoration, ribbon, or other insignia of an order that does not belong to him, is punished by penal servitude of eight days to one month and a fine of ten thousand to thirty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

#### Chapitre 5 - Des faux commis en écriture et dans les dépêches électroniques

##### Section 1 - De la définition

###### Art.357

Constitutes the offence of forgery, any alteration of the truth of a nature to cause prejudice to another and committed with culpable intent:
1° Either by distorting the substance or circumstances of an act;  
2° Either by writing agreements other than those drawn up by the parties;  
3° Either by recording as true facts that are false or as acknowledged facts that were not;  
4° Either by fabrication of all or part of a document;  
5° Either by counterfeiting or alteration of writings or signatures; 6° Either by false signature;  
7° Either by substitution of persons;  
8° Either by addition, subtraction or alteration of clauses, declarations or facts that any act was intended to receive or record.

##### Section 2 - Des faux en écriture

###### Paragraphe 1 - Des faux en écriture publique ou authentique

###### Art.358

Is punished by penal servitude of one year to ten years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, any magistrate, official or public officer who, in the exercise of his functions, has committed a forgery:
1° Either by false signatures;  

2° Either by alteration or removal of acts, writings or signatures; 3° Either by impersonation or substitution of persons;  
4° Either by writings made or inserted in registers.

###### Art.359

Is punished by penal servitude of one year to ten years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, any magistrate, any official or public officer who, in drafting acts of his function, fraudulently distorts the substance or circumstances thereof, either by writing agreements other than those that were drawn up or dictated by the parties, or by recording as true facts that he knew to be false, or by falsely attesting that the facts had been acknowledged or had occurred in his presence, or by omitting or voluntarily modifying declarations received by him.

###### Art.360

Is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs, any person, other than those designated in the preceding article who commits a forgery in authentic or public writing:
1° Either by counterfeiting or alteration of writings or signatures;  
2° Either by fabrication of agreements, dispositions, obligations or discharges, or by their subsequent insertion in such acts;  
3° Either by addition, omission or alteration of clauses, declarations or facts that such acts were intended to receive and record;  
4° Either by impersonation or substitution of persons.

###### Art.361

Is punished by penal servitude of six months to two years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs, any person not party to the act, who makes, before a public officer, a declaration that he knew was not in conformity with the truth.  
However, benefits from an absolute excuse, the person who, having as a witness before a public officer, made a declaration not in conformity with the truth, retracted before any prejudice to another resulted from the use of the act and before he himself was the subject of prosecution.

###### Art.362

In the cases referred to in this chapter, the person who makes use of the document that he knew to be false, is punished by penal servitude of one year to ten years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Forgery in private, commercial or banking documents

###### Art.363

Any person who, by one of the means provided for in Article 360, commits forgery in commercial or banking documents, is punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.

The penalty may be increased to double the maximum provided for in the first paragraph when the offender is a banker, a company director and, in general, a person who has made a public appeal for the issue of shares, bonds, vouchers, bearer instruments or any securities whatsoever, either of a company or of a commercial or industrial enterprise.

###### Art.364

Any person who, by one of the means provided for in Article 360, commits forgery in private documents, is punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of fifty thousand francs to one hundred thousand francs.

###### Art.365

In the cases referred to in this chapter, whoever has made use of a document which he knew to be false, is punished by the penalties for forgery according to the distinctions provided for in Article 363.

###### Paragraphe 3 - Forgery committed in certain administrative documents and certificates

###### Art.366

Whoever counterfeits, falsifies or alters permits, certificates, booklets, cards, bulletins, receipts, passports, mission orders, route sheets, passes or other documents issued by public administrations for the purpose of establishing a right, an identity or a capacity, or of granting an authorization, is punished by penal servitude of six months to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.367.- The penalties provided for in the preceding article are applied to

1° Whoever knowingly makes use of said counterfeited, falsified or altered documents;
2° Whoever makes use of one of the documents referred to in the preceding article, knowing that the particulars appearing therein have become incomplete or inaccurate.

###### Art.368

Whoever unlawfully obtains one of the documents designated in Article 366, either by making false declarations, or by taking a false name or a false capacity, or by providing false information, certificates or attestations, is punished by penal servitude of three months to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand francs, without prejudice to the special provisions applicable in the matter.
The same penalties are applied to whoever makes use of such a document, obtained under the aforementioned conditions, or issued under a name other than his own.
The official who issues or causes to be issued one of the documents designated in Article 366 to a person whom he knows not to be entitled thereto, is punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, unless the act constitutes another more severely punished offence.

###### Art.369

Whoever, without authority, issues under the name of a public official or officer, a certificate of good conduct, of indigence or relating to other circumstances likely to attract benevolence, or certificates intended to procure credit or

assistance for him, is punished by penal servitude of six months to two years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.370.- The penalties provided for in the preceding article are applied to

1° Whoever falsifies an authentic certificate to make it applicable to a person other than the one to whom it had been initially issued;
2° Any individual who has knowingly made use of a certificate thus fabricated or falsified.
If the certificate is issued under the name of a simple private individual, its fabrication or use is punished by penal servitude of one month to six months.

###### Art.371

Is punished by penal servitude of two months to two years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these two penalties only, whoever:
1°, Knowingly issues an attestation or certificate relating materially inaccurate facts;
2° Falsifies or modifies in any manner whatsoever an attestation or certificate originally sincere;
3° Knowingly makes use of an inaccurate or falsified attestation or certificate.

###### Art.372

The forgeries punished in this chapter, when they are committed to the prejudice of the Public Treasury or of a third party, are punished according to their nature, either as forgery in public or authentic documents, or as forgery in private, commercial or banking documents.

##### Section 3 - Forgery committed in electronic dispatches

###### Art.373

Officials, employees and agents of an electronic service, who have committed forgery in the exercise of their functions, by fabricating or falsifying electronic dispatches, are punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

#### Chapitre 6 - Offences in connection with the keeping of civil status records

###### Art.374

Is punished by a fine of ten thousand to thirty thousand Burundian francs, any person who, obliged to make declarations of birth or death, does not make them within the legal time limit, or who, summoned by the civil status officer to make declarations of birth or death, refuses to appear or to testify.

###### Art.375

Are punished by penal servitude of two months to two years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only, the

false declarations made before civil status officers as to the particulars that the records must contain, either by persons obliged by law to make declarations of birth or death, or by those who would have been summoned by the civil status officer to make a civil status declaration, or by any other persons who, without being required to make declarations, have voluntarily appeared before the civil status officer.
The same penalties are applied to those who have given the mission to commit the false declarations mentioned in the preceding paragraph, if this mission has been executed.

###### Art.376

If the false declarations have been made to cover another offence or to commit one, the penalty of penal servitude is one to five years and the fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.377

Is punished by eight days to one month and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs, any civil status officer who, through negligence, has performed any civil status act without being assured of the required consents.
If the acts have been deliberately committed for the purpose of fraud or of procuring an advantage of any kind for himself or for another, the penalty is one to five years and the fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

#### Chapitre 7 - False declarations

###### Art.378

Is punished by penal servitude of two months to two years and a fine of ten thousand to thirty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only, whoever, being required by the authority to declare his identity, has declared as his own, either an identity which belongs to another, or a purely imaginary identity.

###### Art.379

Is punished by penal servitude of six months to three years and a fine of thirty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only, whoever, either by presenting as belonging to him documents or objects relating to a specific person, issued or endorsed by a national or foreign authority, or by any other manoeuvre, has deceived the authority as to his identity.

###### Art.380

Is punished by the same penalties as in the preceding article, whoever, with the aim of deceiving the authority as to his identity, has handed over documents or objects of this kind not relating to the person making use of them.

### Title 4 - Offences against public order

#### Chapter 1

Offences committed by persons exercising a public function

##### Section 1 - Abuse of authority

###### Art.381

Any representative or agent of the government of whatever rank, who, using their authority, has opposed the execution of either a law, a regulation, a judicial warrant or a judicial decision, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to three years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.382

Penal servitude is one to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only if the offender has used violence, has requisitioned or ordered, had requisitioned or ordered the action or use of public force to oppose the ordered execution.

###### Art.383

If the orders or requisitions have been the direct cause of other offences, the penalties are two to five years of penal servitude and a fine of two hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.384

When a representative of the State, a public officer or an executor of a judicial warrant or judicial decisions or of any other order or decision of the authority, has, without legitimate reason, used or caused to be used violence against persons in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of their functions, they shall be punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.385

Any officer, non-commissioned officer or agent of the public force who, after having been legally requisitioned by the public authority empowered by law, has refused to deploy the force placed under their orders, shall be punished by penal servitude of one month to six months and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

##### Section 2 - Prolonged exercise of public authority

###### Art.386

Any representative or public official who has been revoked, dismissed or suspended and who, after having had official knowledge thereof, has continued the exercise of their functions, shall be punished by penal servitude of one month to six months and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

#### Chapter 2 - Offences against public administration committed by private individuals

##### Section 1 - Rebellion

###### Art.387

Any attack, any resistance with violence or threats against holders or agents of authority or of the public force, acting for the execution of laws, orders or ordinances of the public authority, judgments or other enforceable acts, is qualified as rebellion.

###### Art.388

Rebellion committed by a single person is punished by six months to one year of penal servitude and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.389

If the rebellion was committed by several persons and following prior agreement, the penal servitude is increased to one to five years and the fine is ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
The penal servitude is increased to five to ten years and the fine to fifty thousand to one hundred and fifty thousand Burundian francs against rebels who have used weapons or have been found carrying them.

###### Art.390

In case of rebellion with a gang or gathering, rebels without function or employment in the gang who withdrew at the first warning from the public authority or even afterwards, if they were seized outside the place of rebellion without new resistance and without weapons, are punished by penal servitude of one month to six months and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.391

The following shall be punished by eight days to two months and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only:
1° Anyone who, in public, commits any unlawful act, or makes remarks likely to show or provoke contempt towards the established powers, agents of public authority, emblems or insignia adopted by agents of authority to reveal the existence of a public mandate or towards documents or objects delivered in execution of legal or regulatory provisions;  
2° Anyone who refuses to provide information requested by agents of the administration, magistrates or judicial police officers, or agents of the public force acting in the execution of their functions or who, knowingly, gives a false answer to such a request.

###### Art.392

Anyone who, in public, refuses to comply with a public order from an agent of public authority acting in the exercise of their duties, shall be punished by seven days to one month of penal servitude and a fine of twenty thousand to thirty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.  

The same penalties apply to anyone who, except in cases of force majeure, does not respond to a written and nominative summons from a magistrate, by an authority empowered in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

##### Section 2

Insults and violence against holders of authority or of the public force

###### Art.393

Acts of insult consist of words, gestures or threats, writings of any kind or the sending of any objects addressed to a person entrusted with a public service mission, in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of their mission and, of an insulting or defamatory nature, and likely to undermine their dignity or the respect due to the function with which they are invested.

###### Art.394

Insult committed against the Head of State is punished by penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.395

Violence or assault committed against the Head of State is punished by penal servitude of ten to twenty years, if it has not caused bloodshed, injury or illness.  
If it has caused bloodshed, injury or illness, or if there was premeditation, the penalty is penal servitude of thirty years.  
If death has resulted, or if the violence was committed with the intention of causing death, the offender is punished by life penal servitude and this penalty is incompressible.

###### Art.396

When the insult is addressed to the Head of State, a parliamentarian, a member of government, a magistrate, an official or agent of public authority or of the public force in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of their functions, the perpetrator is punished by six months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.  
When the insult takes place during a session or public meeting or during a hearing of a Court or Tribunal, the penalties are six months to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.397

Anyone who has struck any person referred to in the preceding article in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of their functions, is punished by penal servitude of one to three years and a fine of one hundred thousand to three hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
If the blows inflicted have caused mutilation or permanent disability, the penal servitude is increased to three to ten years and the fine from one hundred thousand francs to three hundred thousand francs.  

If the blows inflicted, without intention of causing death, have nevertheless caused it, the penal servitude is increased to fifteen to thirty years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.398

Is punished by two months to five years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, whoever has publicly and with contempt, removed, destroyed, damaged, replaced or insulted the flag or official insignia of the sovereignty of the Republic.

##### Section 3 - Obstruction of the execution of public works

###### Art.399

Whoever, by acts of violence, has opposed the execution of works ordered or authorized by the competent authority, is punished by penal servitude of eight days to two months and a fine of ten thousand to thirty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
If the opposition to these works takes place by unlawful assembly and violence, acts of violence or threats, the guilty parties are punished by penal servitude of two to three years and a fine of thirty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

##### Section 4 - Publication and distribution of writings

###### Art.400

Any person who is the author or has knowingly contributed to the publication or distribution of any writing in which there is no true indication of the name and address of the author or printer is punished by penal servitude of eight days to two months and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
However, penal servitude may not be pronounced when the writing published without the required indications is part of a publication whose origin is known by its prior publication.

###### Art.401

Exempted from the penalty provided for by the preceding article are those who have disclosed the author or printer as well as criers, bill-posters, sellers, or distributors, who have disclosed the person from whom they obtained the incriminated writing.

#### Chapitre 3 - Obstruction of administration and interference with the authority of justice

##### Section 1 - Obstruction of access to justice

###### Paragraphe 1 - Disappearance of evidence

###### Art.402

Is punished by one to three years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, the act, with a view to obstructing the manifestation of the truth:
1° Of modifying the state of the scene of a crime or offence, either by alteration, falsification or erasure of traces or indications, or by the addition, displacement or removal of any objects;  
2° Of destroying, removing, concealing or altering a public or private document or an object likely to facilitate the discovery of a crime or offence, the search for evidence or the conviction of the guilty parties.  
When the acts provided for in this article are committed by a person who, by virtue of their functions, is called upon to contribute to the manifestation of the truth, the penalty is one year to five years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to one hundred and fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Pressure exerted on the victim

###### Art.403

Any threat or other act of intimidation against anyone, committed with a view to inducing the victim of a crime or offence not to file a complaint or to retract, is punished by one year to three years of penal servitude and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 3 - Harbouring

###### Art.404

Are punished by six months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only, those who have harboured or caused to be harboured persons whom they knew to be prosecuted or convicted for an offence that the law punishes by penal servitude of five years or more.

###### Art.405

Whoever has harboured or caused to be harboured, hidden or caused to be hidden the corpse of a person killed by homicide for fetishism purposes, or who died as a result of assault and battery, is punished by three months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of twenty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.406

Not covered by the penal provisions provided for under this paragraph are the ascendants, descendants, spouses even if divorced, the

brothers or sisters and relatives by marriage to the second degree of the perpetrators or accomplices of the homicide, assault and battery except if their individual responsibility is established.

##### Section 2 - Obstruction of the exercise of justice

###### Paragraphe 1 - Denial of justice and violation of procedural time limits

###### Art.407

The act for a magistrate, of refusing to render justice after having been requested to do so is punished by eight days to one month of principal penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
Is exposed to the sanctions provided for in the preceding paragraph, the Judicial Police Officer or the Investigating Magistrate who, without valid excuse, exceeds the time limits prescribed by the Code of Criminal Procedure.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Violation of the secrecy of the investigation

###### Art.408

Without prejudice to the rights of the defence, the act, for any person who, by virtue of their functions, has knowledge, in application of the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure, of information arising from an ongoing inquiry or investigation concerning a crime or offence, of revealing it, directly or indirectly to persons likely to be implicated as perpetrators, co-perpetrators, accomplices or receivers, in the commission of these offences, when this revelation is likely to hinder the conduct of investigations or the manifestation of the truth is punished by three months to one year of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
They may also be subject to disqualification from exercising a public function for a period of two to five years.

###### Paragraphe 3 - Threats and intimidation

###### Art.409

Any threat or act of intimidation committed against a magistrate, an arbitrator, an interpreter, an expert or a party's lawyer with a view to influencing their behaviour in the exercise of their functions is punished by two to five years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.410

If the threat or act of intimidation emanates from the hierarchically superior authority, the penalty is increased to ten years of penal servitude and the fine to five hundred thousand francs.

###### Paragraphe 4 - Hit and run offence

###### Art.411

The act, for any driver of a vehicle or land, river or maritime craft, knowing that they have just caused or occasioned an accident, of not stopping and thus attempting to escape the criminal or civil liability that they may incur, is punished by one month to six months of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
Temporary withdrawal of the driving licence for a period of three months to one year or permanently may be pronounced against the guilty party.

###### Paragraphe 5 - Failure to testify in favour of an innocent person

###### Art.412

The act, for anyone, knowing the proof of the innocence of a person held in provisional detention or tried for a crime or offence, of voluntarily refraining from immediately providing testimony thereof to the judicial or administrative authorities is punished by one month to one year of penal servitude and fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs fine.  
Is exempted from penalty, whoever provides their testimony belatedly but spontaneously. Exempted from the provisions of the first paragraph are:
1° Direct line relatives and their spouses of the perpetrator or accomplice of the offence that motivated the prosecution, as well as their brothers and sisters and their spouses;  
2° The perpetrator or accomplice of the offence that motivated the prosecution, their direct line relatives and their spouses;  
3° Relatives by marriage up to the second degree.

###### Paragraphe 6 - Refusal to give evidence

###### Art.413

Any person having knowledge of the perpetrators of a crime or an offence who refuses to answer questions put to them in this regard by a judicial police officer, an officer of the public prosecutor's office or a judge is punished by one month to three months of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Paragraphe 7 - False testimony

###### Art.414

False testimony given under oath before the courts is punished by six months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
However, the false witness is exempted from the penalty if they spontaneously retracted before the decision ending the proceedings rendered by the court.  

The penalty is increased to one year to five years of penal servitude and the fine to one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs:
1° When the false testimony is induced by the giving of a gift or any reward;  
2° When the person against whom or in favour of whom the false testimony was committed is liable to a criminal penalty.  
If the accused has been sentenced to penal servitude for life, the false witness who testified against them is sentenced to penal servitude for life.

###### Paragraphe 8 - False oath

###### Art.415

A false oath is one that is invoked in support of a false affirmation. It is punished by one month to three months of penal servitude and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Paragraphe 9 - Subornation of witness or expert

###### Art.416

The act of using promises, offers, gifts, manoeuvres or artifices during ongoing judicial proceedings in order to induce another person either to make or deliver false testimony, a false declaration or a false attestation is punished by two years to five years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 10 - Threats or intimidation directed against witnesses

###### Art.417

Anyone who has threatened or intimidated witnesses by reason of their testimony in court is punished by six months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 11 - Distortion of translations by an expert

###### Art.418

The act by an expert in any matter of distorting or falsifying, in their written reports or oral presentations, the data or results of the expert assessment is punished by one year to five years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 12 - Refusal to comply by an expert

###### Art.419

The refusal by an expert to comply with a requisition or to take an oath is punished by one month to three months and a fine not exceeding one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Paragraphe 13 - Identity theft

###### Art.420

The act of assuming the complete or partial identity of a third party in circumstances which have led or could have led to criminal proceedings against that person is punished by one year to three years of imprisonment and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
The penalties imposed for this offence are cumulative with those imposed for the offence on the occasion of which the impersonation was committed.  
A false declaration relating to the civil status of a person, which has led or would have led to criminal proceedings against a third party, is punished by the penalties provided for in the first paragraph.

###### Paragraphe 14 - Publication of commentary

###### Art.421

The publication, before the final judicial decision is made, of commentary tending to exert pressure with a view to influencing the statements of witnesses is punished by one month to three months of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
When the offence is committed through the written or audiovisual press, the particular provisions of the laws governing these matters are applicable with regard to the determination of the persons responsible.

##### Section 3 - Offences against the authority of justice

###### Paragraphe 1 - False denunciation

###### Art.422

Without prejudice to the application of article 14 of law no. 1/12 of 18 April 2006 on measures for the prevention and suppression of corruption and related offences, the act of reporting to the judicial or administrative authority false facts constituting a crime or an offence which have exposed the judicial authorities to unnecessary investigations is punished by one month to three months of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Breaking of seals

###### Art.423

Anyone who has, intentionally, broken or removed an official mark, in particular a seal, affixed to enclose or identify an object or who has defeated its effect is punished by penal servitude of six months to two years and a fine of thirty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.424

If the breaking of seals is committed with violence against persons, the offender is punished by penal servitude of two years to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.425

If the offence was committed by the custodian themselves or the official who carried out the affixing, the penal servitude may be increased to five years and the fine to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

### Titre 5

Offences against rights guaranteed to individuals Chapter 1 - Offences against freedom of worship

###### Art.426

Any person who, by violence, insults or threats, by disturbances or disorders, has infringed freedom of worship or its free public exercise, and freedom of conscience is punished by penal servitude of six months to two years and a fine of fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

#### Chapitre 2

Offences committed by public officials against rights guaranteed to individuals

###### Art.427

Any arbitrary act infringing the freedoms and rights guaranteed to individuals by laws, decrees, ordinances and orders, ordered or executed by a public official or officer, by a depositary or agent of authority or of the public force, shall be punished by penal servitude of fifteen days to one year and a fine of ten thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

### Titre 6

Crimes and offences of an economic nature and against public property Chapter 1 - Offences against the proper functioning of the national economy

###### Art.428

Anyone who, by means of threats, violence, assault or fraudulent manoeuvres, has brought about or maintained a concerted cessation of work with the aim of forcing an increase or decrease in wages or of infringing the free exercise of industry or labour is punished by six months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.429

Workers and management agents who, without authorization, communicate manufacturing secrets of their enterprise to persons outside it are punished by six months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.

###### Art.430

Is punished by one to five years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs or only one of these penalties, whoever, by concerted action, by using inaccurate or tendentious information or by using threats, acts of violence, or by concealing stocks of foodstuffs or materials that he holds or has held, has obstructed free commercial competition or the normal supply of retail traders or the public.

###### Art.431

The penalties provided for in the preceding article are applicable to those who, in public auctions or in the awarding of public contracts, have obstructed or disturbed the freedom of bids or tenders by acts of violence, threats, promises, false news, price agreements or any fraudulent concerted action.

###### Art.432

Are punished by five to ten years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to five million Burundian francs:
1° Any person who, acting on behalf of the State, public administrations, local authorities, public establishments, parastatal or mixed economy enterprises, banking organizations, self-managed units of consumption, industrial or agricultural production, any private law organization ensuring the management of a public service, enters into, for personal purposes, a contract, agreement or contract that he knows to be contrary to the fundamental economic interests of the Nation;  
2° Without prior authorization, any trader, industrialist, artisan, contractor, or, in general any person, who enters into, even occasionally, a contract or contract with the State or one of its organizations referred to in the preceding paragraph, by taking advantage of the authority or influence of the agents of the aforementioned organizations to increase the prices they normally and habitually practice or to modify to their advantage the quality of goods or delivery times;  
3° Without prior authorization, any unauthorized intermediary who, without real needs corresponding to the necessities of the distribution system, contributes to the artificial increase of prices, modifies to his advantage the quality of goods or disrupts delivery times.

###### Art.433.- Commits an offense against exchange regulations whoever

1° Violates an obligation or prohibition relating to fund transfers, declarations of assets, possession or trade in precious metals or precious stones;  
2° Offers to sell or buy foreign currency, cash, securities, even when these offers are not accompanied by any delivery or presentation;  
3° Offers his services as an intermediary, either to bring together sellers and buyers, or to facilitate negotiations even when this intermediation is not remunerated.

###### Art.434

Is punished by two months to two years of penal servitude and a fine equal to the legal value of the corpus delicti that was the subject of the offense, any person who commits one of the offenses against exchange regulations referred to in the preceding article. In case of recidivism, the penalty of penal servitude is fixed according to article 117.

###### Art.435

Independently of the penalties provided for in the preceding article, the corpus delicti is confiscated.  
When, for any reason, the corpus delicti could not be seized or is not produced, the offender is sentenced to a fine in an amount equal to the value of the corpus delicti.

#### Chapitre 2 - De la corruption et des infractions connexes

##### Section 1 - De la corruption

Sous-section 1 - De la corruption des fonctionnaires publics

###### Paragraphe 1 - De la corruption passive

###### Art.436

Is punished by penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine ranging from single to double the value of the illicit profit acquired, any person holding public authority, charged with a public service mission or invested with an elective public mandate, who has solicited or accepted, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, for his own account or for the account of others, offers, gifts, presents or any advantages whatsoever to perform or have performed, to abstain or have abstained from performing an act of his function or his mandate or facilitated by his function, his mission or his mandate.

###### Art.437

Is punished by penal servitude of ten to fifteen years and a fine ranging from single to double the value of the illicit profit acquired, any person holding public authority, charged with a public service mission or invested with an elective public mandate, who has solicited or accepted, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, for his own account or for the account of others, offers, gifts, presents or any advantages whatsoever to perform or have performed an unjust act.

###### Art.438

Without prejudice to the application of other criminal provisions, the offender is punished by penal servitude of fifteen to twenty years and a fine ranging from single to double the value of the illicit profit acquired, if he has solicited or accepted, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, for his own account or for the account of others, offers, gifts, presents or any advantages whatsoever, to commit or have committed in the exercise of his function, his employment or his mission any offense whatsoever.  

Is punished by the same penalties, any judge or arbitrator who is guilty of corruption during a dispute that he settles. The same applies to any public agent charged with the fight against corruption or any public authority who allows himself to be corrupted in the context of his functions.

###### Art.439

Is punished by penal servitude of twelve to fifteen years and a fine ranging from single to double the value of the illicit profit acquired, any agent of the judicial order, any Officer of the Public Prosecutor's Office or of the Judicial Police who receives or accepts to receive offers or promises for the purpose of having a decision taken that should not have been taken.

###### Art.440

Is punished by penal servitude of twelve to fifteen years and a fine of two hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, whoever, mentioned in articles 436 to 439 of this Code has, explicitly or implicitly, at any time, demanded, benefited from, subjected to acts of a sexual nature or has accepted the promise thereof in order to perform or have performed, abstain or have abstained from performing an act that falls within his attributions.  
The penalty is increased to servitude of fifteen to twenty years and a fine of five hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs if the offender is a male or female teacher who has committed these acts against his schoolchild, his pupil or his student regardless of their sex.

###### Art.441

Is punished by penal servitude of one to three years and a fine of two hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, any public agent or any public authority who accepts gifts from a person or any advantage likely to have influenced or to influence the handling of a procedure or transaction related to his functions.  
The same penalties are applicable to whoever grants gifts or any advantage to a public agent or any public authority for having provided him with any public service.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Active corruption

###### Art.442

Is punished by penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine ranging from the simple to double the value of the illicit profit acquired, anyone who has proposed, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers, promises, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever to obtain from a person holding public authority, charged with a public service mission or invested with an elective public mandate, that they accomplish or have accomplished, that they abstain or have abstained from accomplishing an act of their function, mission or mandate or facilitated by their function or mandate.  
Is punished by the same penalties the act of yielding to a person holding public authority, charged with a public service mission or invested with an elective public mandate, who solicits, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers, promises, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever to accomplish or

have accomplished, to abstain or have abstained from accomplishing an act referred to in the preceding paragraph.  

Sous-section 2 - Corruption of persons not exercising a public function

###### Art.443

Is punished by penal servitude of two to five years and a fine ranging from the simple to double the value of the illicit profit acquired, any person who has proposed, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers, promises, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever to obtain from a person, who without being a holder of public authority or charged with a public service mission, exercises within the framework of a professional or social activity, a management function or work for a natural or legal person, or any organization whatsoever, that they accomplish or have accomplished, abstain or have abstained from accomplishing an act of their activity or function or facilitated by their activity or function, in violation of their legal, contractual or professional obligations.

###### Art.444

Is punished by the same penalties, anyone who has yielded to a person referred to in the preceding article who solicits, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever to accomplish or have accomplished, to abstain or have abstained from accomplishing an act referred to in the same article, in violation of their legal, contractual or professional obligations.

###### Art.445

Is punished by penal servitude of two to five years and a fine ranging from the simple to double the value of the illicit profit acquired, any person who, without being a holder of public authority or charged with a public service mission, exercises within the framework of a professional or social activity, a management function or work for a natural or legal person, or any organization whatsoever, has solicited or accepted, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers, promises, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever to accomplish or have accomplished, to abstain or have abstained from accomplishing an act of their activity or function, or facilitated by their activity or function, in violation of their legal, contractual or professional obligations.

###### Art.446

Is punished by penal servitude of twelve to fifteen years and a fine of two hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, any person who, without being a holder of public authority or charged with a public service mission, exercises within the framework of a professional or social activity, a management function or work for a natural or legal person, or any organization whatsoever has, explicitly or implicitly, at any time, demanded, benefited from, caused to be suffered acts of a sexual nature or has accepted the promise thereof in order to perform or have performed, abstain or have abstained from performing an act that falls within their attributions.

##### Section 2 - Repression of offences connected to corruption

###### Paragraphe 1 - Extortion by public officials

###### Art.447

Is punished by penal servitude of five to twenty years and a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, any person holding public authority or charged with a public service mission, who receives, demands or orders the collection as duties or contributions, taxes or levies, fine or security, revenues or interest, a sum that they know is not due, or exceeds what is due.  
Is punished by the same penalties, the act, by the same persons, of granting in any form and for any reason whatsoever an exemption or franchise from duties, contribution, taxes or levies, fine or security, revenues or interest in violation of legal and regulatory texts and of effecting, gratuitously or at a low price, the delivery of public goods.  
The quality of the persons referred to in the first paragraph of this article is assessed both during the exercise of functions by the interested parties and after their cessation.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Influence peddling

###### Art.448

Is punished by penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, any person holding public authority, charged with a public service mission or invested with an elective public mandate, who has solicited or accepted, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, for their own account or for the account of another, offers, promises, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever to use or have used their real or supposed influence with a view to obtaining from an authority or a public administration distinctions, employment, contracts or any other favourable decision.

###### Art.449

Is punished by the penalties set out in the preceding article, anyone who proposes, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers, promises, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever to obtain from a person holding public authority, charged with a public service mission or invested with an elective public mandate, that they use or have used their real or supposed influence with a view to obtaining from an authority or a public administration distinctions, employment, contracts or any other favourable decision.

###### Art.450

Is punished by the same penalties provided for in article 448, anyone who has yielded to a person holding public authority, charged with a public service mission or invested with an elective public mandate who solicits, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers, promises, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever to use or have used their influence in the conditions referred to in the preceding article.

###### Art.451

Is punished by penal servitude of three to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs, anyone who solicits or accepts, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever to use or have used their real or supposed influence with a view to obtaining from an authority or a public administration distinctions, employment, contracts or any other favourable decision.  
Is punished by the same penalties the act of yielding to the solicitations provided for in the preceding paragraph, or of proposing without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers, promises, donations, gifts or any advantages whatsoever so that a person uses or has used their real or supposed influence with a view to obtaining from an authority or a public administration distinctions, employment, contracts or any other favourable decision.

###### Paragraphe 3 - Misappropriation and embezzlement of property

###### Art.452

Any person holding public authority, entrusted with a public service mission, any public accountant, any public depositary or one of their subordinates, who destroys, embezzles or misappropriates a deed or title, public or private funds, effects, documents or titles in lieu thereof, or any other object entrusted to them by reason of their functions or mission, shall be punished with penal servitude of ten to twenty years and a fine of fifty thousand to one million Burundian francs.

###### Art.453

When the destruction, embezzlement or misappropriation by a third party of the property referred to in the preceding article results from the negligence of a person holding public authority or entrusted with a public service mission, a public accountant or a public depositary, such person shall be punished with penal servitude of one year to two years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 4 - Fraudulent management

###### Art.454

Any person holding public authority, entrusted with a public service mission or invested with an elective public mandate, any public accountant, any public depositary or one of their subordinates, who commits for fraudulent purposes irregularities in the execution of the accounts and budgets of the State, public administrations, local authorities, public establishments, companies with public participation or mixed economy companies, banking organizations, self-managed units of consumption, industrial or agricultural production, any private law organization ensuring the management of a public service, shall be punished with penal servitude of ten to twenty years and a fine of fifty thousand to one million Burundian francs. Prosecutions shall be brought against all those who took part in the offence with knowledge of the non-authenticity of the monies or securities.

###### Paragraphe 5 - Favoritism

###### Art.455

Any person holding public authority, entrusted with a public service mission or invested with an elective public mandate or exercising specific functions in state structures, public administrations, local authorities, public establishments, companies with public participation or mixed economy companies, banking organizations, self-managed units of consumption, industrial or agricultural production, any private law organization ensuring the management of a public service, who has procured for another person an unjustified advantage by an act contrary to legislative and regulatory provisions intended to guarantee equal access and equality of candidates to public functions, in public procurement and contractual transfers of management of public services, shall be punished with penal servitude of one year to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 6 - Illegal taking of interest

###### Art.456

Any person holding public authority, or entrusted with a public service mission or a person invested with an elective public mandate, who takes, receives or retains, directly or indirectly, any interest whatsoever in an enterprise or in an operation of which they have, at the time of the act, in whole or in part, responsibility for ensuring the supervision, administration, liquidation or payment, shall be punished with penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine of five hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.  
Any person having been entrusted, as a public official or agent or employee of a public administration responsible, by reason of their very function, to exercise supervision or direct control of a private enterprise, or to express their opinion on operations carried out by a private enterprise and who, either on leave, placed on availability, or after admission to retirement, or after resignation, dismissal or revocation and for a period of three years from the cessation of the function, exercises in that enterprise any mandate whatsoever or a remunerated activity in any manner whatsoever, shall be punished with penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of two hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 7 - Money laundering

###### Art.457

Commits the offence of money laundering and shall be punished with a sentence of penal servitude of ten to fifteen years and a fine of up to ten times the value of the property subject to laundering, whoever proceeds:
1° To the conversion, transfer or assignment of property in full knowledge that such property is the proceeds of an offence with a view to concealing or disguising the illicit origin of said property or to assisting any person involved in the commission of the offence to escape the consequences of their action;  

2° To the concealment or disguising of the true nature, origin, location, disposition, movement or ownership of property or rights, proceeds of an offence;  
3° To the acquisition, possession or use of property whose origin, at the time of acquisition, possession or use, is the proceeds of an offence;  
4° To participation in one of the acts referred to in the three preceding points, association to commit the act, the fact of aiding, inciting or advising someone to commit it or the fact of facilitating its execution.

###### Paragraphe 8

Active corruption of foreign public officials, officials of public international organizations and non-governmental organizations

###### Art.458

Whoever has proposed, offered or granted, without right, directly or indirectly, offers, promises, gifts, presents or any advantages whatsoever to obtain from a foreign public official, an official of a public international organization or a non-governmental organization, that they perform or refrain from performing an act in the exercise of their official functions or to retain a contract or other advantage in connection with international trade activities, shall be punished with penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine ranging from the simple to double the value of the illicit profit acquired.  
Any person having served as an intermediary in the commission of the offence referred to in this article shall be punished with the same penalties.

###### Paragraphe 9 - Complementary penalties

###### Art.459

Natural persons guilty of one of the offences provided for in this chapter may also incur at least one of the following complementary penalties:
1° Confiscation as provided for by the relevant provisions of this Code;  
2° Permanent prohibition from Burundian territory for a specified period which may not be less than five years for any foreigner;  
3° Prohibition for a period of up to ten years from exercising a public function or exercising the professional or social function in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of which the offence was committed;  
4° Prohibition of civic, civil, political and family rights as determined by law;  
5° Posting or dissemination of the decision pronounced.

###### Art.460

Legal persons guilty of one of the offences provided for in this chapter may also incur at least one of the following additional penalties:
1° Special confiscation as provided for in this Code; 2° For a maximum period of five years:
•	prohibition from exercising directly or indirectly the professional or social activity in which or on the occasion of the exercise of which the offence was committed;  
•	closure of the company's establishments that were used to commit the incriminated acts;  
•	exclusion from public procurement;  
3° Display or dissemination of the decision rendered.

###### Art.461

Persons convicted of offences provided for in this chapter may not benefit from pardon, amnesty, or amnesty pardon as long as they have not executed the civil reparations ordered by the trial court.

###### Paragraphe 10 - Des exemptions et atténuation des peines

###### Art.462

Except in case of recidivism in matters of corruption, any person, perpetrator or accomplice of active corruption who, before any prosecution, has revealed the offence to the administrative or judicial authority and enabled the identification of the other persons implicated, is exempted from penalty.  
Apart from the case provided for in the preceding paragraph, the maximum penalty incurred by any person, perpetrator or accomplice of one of the corruption offences who, after the commencement of prosecution, has enabled or facilitated the arrest of the other persons involved, is reduced by half.  
In addition, such person is exempted from the additional penalties provided for in this Code.  
In all cases, restitution is never made to the briber of the things delivered by him. They are confiscated for the benefit of the Treasury.

#### Chapitre 3 - Des jeux de hasard, des loteries et des concours de pronostics

##### Section 1 - Des jeux de hasard

###### Art.463

Games of chance are those in which chance is the essential and preponderant element and predominates over the skill, equality or combinations of the players who engage in them, in the hope of realizing an appreciable gain, sums of money relatively considerable having regard to their contributory capacity.  

The keeping of games of chance consists in the act of organizing or facilitating another person's passion for these games, with a view to deriving a personal pecuniary profit therefrom.  
Games of chance are prohibited in public places or places open to the public, in all non-enclosed places where the public can have direct view, as well as in all other places, even private, where anyone wishing to engage in gambling is freely admitted to enter.  
Games of chance are prohibited for minors under eighteen years of age.

###### Art.464

Is punished by one to two years of penal servitude and a fine of up to fifty thousand francs or one of these penalties only:
1° Whoever has kept games of chance in one of the places and under the conditions referred to in the preceding article;  
2° Whoever has played games of chance in these same places and conditions.

##### Section 2 - Des loteries

###### Art.465.- Les loteries sont prohibées

Are deemed lotteries, all operations offered to the public under whatever name and intended to give rise to the expectation of a gain that would be acquired by lot.

###### Art.466

The authors, entrepreneurs, administrators, agents or employees of lotteries are punished by one to two years of penal servitude and a fine of up to fifty thousand Burundian francs.  
Are confiscated, movable objects put in the lottery and those used or intended for its service.  
When immovable property has been put in the lottery, special confiscation is replaced by a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.467.- Sont punis des mêmes peines

1° Those who have placed, hawked or distributed lottery tickets;  
2° Those who, by notices, announcements, posters, or by any other means of publication, have made known the existence of lotteries or facilitated the issue of their tickets.  
In all cases, the tickets, notices, announcements or posters are seized and destroyed.

###### Art.468

Are exempted from the penalties provided for in the preceding article, town criers and bill posters who have made known the person from whom they hold these tickets or the writings mentioned above.

###### Art.469

Are exempted from these provisions, lotteries and games of chance organized or authorized by public authorities and exclusively intended for acts of charity, the encouragement of industry, arts or sports or for any other purpose of public utility, when they have been authorized:
1° By the Minister of the Interior, if the issue of tickets is made and announced or published in more than one province;  
2° By the Provincial Governor, if the issue of tickets is made and announced or published only in one province.

###### Art.470.- Sont également exemptées

1° Financial operations of the State, made with premiums or redeemable by lot;  
2° Financial operations of the same nature made by foreign powers, when the issue of securities relating to these operations has been authorized by the President of the Republic or his delegate;  
3° Financial operations of the same nature made by communes, as well as operations of companies making incidentally redemptions with premiums by lot, when they have been authorized by the President of the Republic or his delegate.

##### Section 3 - Des concours de pronostics

###### Art.471

Is punished by eight days to three months of penal servitude and a fine of up to two hundred thousand Burundian francs, or one of these penalties only:
1° Whoever, for the purpose of profit, has organized or operated for his own account or for the account of another forecasting competitions;  
2° Whoever, free of charge or for remuneration, has served as intermediary in a prohibited forecasting competition, either by transferring funds, or by disseminating bulletins or advertisements of the enterprise that organizes or operates this competition.

###### Art.472

In all cases, the funds at stake, bulletins, advertisements and operating equipment are confiscated.

###### Art.473

Are exempted from these provisions, forecasting competitions organized under the conditions provided for in article 469.

#### Chapitre 4 - Des infractions relatives aux sociétés publiques et privées

##### Section 1 - Des infractions en rapport avec la constitution des sociétés

###### Art.474

Are punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs the partners and founders of companies who, knowingly, have made in the articles of association, at the time of incorporation or capital increase, a false declaration concerning the distribution of shares and their payment.

###### Art.475

Are punished by penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only:
1° Those who have solicited, either subscriptions or payments, or purchases of shares, bonds or other securities of companies:
•	by simulation of subscription or payment to a company;  
•	by publication of subscriptions or payments that they know do not exist;  
•	by publication of names of persons designated as being or to be attached to the company in any capacity when they know these designations to be contrary to the truth;  
•	by publication of any other facts that they knew to be false.  
2° Those who, directly or through an intermediary, have opened a public subscription to shares, the sale of bonds or other securities of a company that is not authorized to make a public appeal for savings.

##### Section 2 - Offences relating to the operation of companies

###### Art.476

Managers, chief executive officers, directors, members of the management board or supervisory board, or company directors who fail to do the following shall be punished by a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs:
1° Have not prepared for each financial year the balance sheet and other required accounting documents and a report on the operations of the financial year;  
2° Have refused or neglected to communicate to the partners and shareholders before the general meeting the balance sheet and other documents required to assess their management;  
3° Have refused or neglected to prepare the minutes of proceedings of a general meeting; have omitted to publish within the regulatory time limit the resolutions resulting in modification of the articles of association, the powers of managers, chief executive officers, directors, members of the management board or supervisory board, or company directors, or the share capital with regard to capital companies.

###### Art.477

Managers, chief executive officers, directors,

members of the management board or supervisory board, or company directors who do the following shall be punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs:
1° Fraudulently, have attributed to a contribution in kind a valuation higher than its actual value;  
2° In the absence of accounts or by means of fraudulent accounts, have knowingly distributed fictitious dividends among the partners;  
3° Even in the absence of any dividend distribution, have knowingly presented to the partners and shareholders an inaccurate balance sheet in order to conceal the true situation of the company;  
4° In bad faith, have made use of the company's assets or credits, the powers they possessed or the votes at their disposal, in a manner they knew to be contrary to the interests of the company, for personal purposes or to favour another company or enterprise in which they had a direct or indirect interest.

###### Art.478

The following shall be punished by penal servitude of three months to two years and a fine of fifty thousand to three hundred thousand Burundian francs:
1° Those who, by presenting themselves as owners of shares or bonds that do not belong to them, have taken part in voting at a meeting of shareholders or bondholders;  
2° Those who have handed over the shares or bonds for the use provided for above.

###### Art.479

The provisions of articles 476 and 477 are applicable to any person who, directly or through an intermediary, has in fact exercised the management of a limited liability company under cover of or in place of its legal representatives.

##### Section 3 - Offences relating to the supervision of companies

###### Art.480

Any statutory auditor, any independent auditor, who has knowingly given or confirmed false information about the situation of the company shall be punished by three months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
Half of the penalties provided for in the preceding paragraph are applicable to managers, chief executive officers, directors, members of the management board or supervisory board, or company directors who have knowingly obstructed the verifications and controls of members of state control institutions, statutory auditors and independent auditors or who have refused them on-site access to all documents useful for the exercise of their mission and in particular contracts, books, accounting documents and registers and minutes.

##### Section 4 - Misuse of company assets

###### Art.481

Any officer or agent of a public or private company who makes use of its assets in a manner he knew to be contrary to the interests of the company shall be punished by penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

##### Section 5 - Offences relating to the liquidation of companies

###### Art.482

The penalties provided for in article 477 are applicable to the liquidator of the company who refuses or neglects to communicate the liquidation accounts to the partners, to convene the general meeting.  
The penalties of article 480 are applicable to the liquidator of the company who, in bad faith:
1° Has made use of the assets or credit of the company in liquidation in a manner he knew to be contrary to the interests of the latter, for personal purposes or to favour another company or enterprise in which he had a direct or indirect interest;  
2° Has transferred all or part of the assets of the company in liquidation below its market value;  
3° Has knowingly given or confirmed false information in the accounts or liquidation report causing prejudice to the rights of the partners.

#### Chapter 5 - Computer crime

##### Section 1 - Computer forgery

###### Art.483

Anyone who commits forgery by introducing into a computer system, modifying or deleting data that is stored, processed or transmitted by a computer system, or by modifying by any technological means the possible use of data in a computer system, and thereby modifies the legal scope of such data, shall be punished by penal servitude of five to twenty years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
Anyone who makes use of data thus obtained, while knowing that it is false, shall be punished as if he were the author of the forgery.

##### Section 2 - Computer fraud

###### Art.484

Anyone who obtains, for himself or for another, a fraudulent pecuniary advantage by introducing into a computer system, modifying or deleting data that is stored, processed or transmitted by a computer system, or

by modifying by any technological means the possible use of data in a computer system, shall be punished by penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.485

Any person who, knowing that he is not authorised to do so, accesses a computer system or remains in it is punished by penal servitude of three months to one year and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
If the offence referred to in the first paragraph is committed with fraudulent intent, the penalty of imprisonment is six months to three years and a fine of one hundred thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
Any person who with fraudulent intent or with the aim of causing harm exceeds his power of access to a computer system is punished by the same penalties as in the preceding paragraph.  
Any person who is in one of the situations referred to in paragraphs 1 and 3 who is punished by penal servitude of one year to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only:
•	1° either retrieves, in any manner whatsoever, data stored, processed or transmitted by the computer system;  
•	2° either makes any use of a computer system belonging to a third party or uses the computer system to access the computer system of a third party;  
•	3° either causes any damage, even unintentionally, to the computer system or to the data that are stored, processed or transmitted by that system or to the computer system of a third party or to the data that are stored, processed or transmitted by that system.  
Any person who orders the commission of one of the offences referred to in paragraphs 1 to 5 or who incites it, is punished by penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
Any person who, knowing that data have been obtained by the commission of one of the offences referred to in paragraphs 1 to 4, holds them, discloses them to another person or divulges them, or makes any use of the data thus obtained, is punished by penal servitude of six months to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.486

Any person who, with the aim of causing harm, directly or indirectly, introduces into a computer system, modifies or deletes data, or who modifies by any technological means the possible use of data in a computer system, is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of six months to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only;  
Any person who, following the commission of one of the offences referred to in the preceding paragraph, causes damage to data in the computer system concerned or in any other computer system, is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of six months to five years and

a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only;  
Any person who, following the commission of one of the offences referred to in the first paragraph, prevents, totally or partially, the correct functioning of the computer system concerned or of any other computer system, is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only
Any person who, with fraudulent intent or with the aim of causing harm, designs, makes available, disseminates or markets data stored, processed or transmitted by a computer system, when he knows that such data may be used to cause damage to data or to prevent, totally or partially, the correct functioning of a computer system, is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of six months to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

### Titre 8 - Offences against public security

#### Chapitre 1

Association formed for the purpose of attacking persons and property

###### Art.487

A criminal association constitutes any group formed or agreement established with a view to the preparation characterised by one or more material facts, of one or more crimes or one or more offences punishable by at least five years of penal servitude.

###### Art.488

Any association formed for the purpose of attacking persons or property is an offence which exists by the mere fact of the organisation of the gang.  
If the association has as its purpose the perpetration of an offence punishable by at least ten years of penal servitude, the instigators of this association, the leaders of this gang and those having exercised any command are punished by ten to twenty years of penal servitude.

###### Art.489

The same persons are punished by five to ten years of penal servitude if the association was formed only with a view to perpetrating offences punishable by less than ten years of penal servitude.

###### Art.490

Any person who has knowingly and voluntarily supplied the gang or association with arms and ammunition, vehicles, is punished by five to ten years of penal servitude.

###### Art.491

Any other person forming part of the association or any person who has knowingly and voluntarily supplied the gang with information, material, places of retreat or meeting or any other assistance useful for the perpetration and consummation

of the offences which are the objects of the association, is sentenced to penal servitude of two to five years.

###### Art.492

Those offenders who, before any attempt at an offence forming the object of the association and before any prosecution, have disclosed to the public authorities the existence of these gangs and the names of their leaders or those responsible are exempted from the penalties provided for in the preceding article.

#### Chapitre 2 - Threats of attack against persons or against property

###### Art.493

Any person who, by anonymous or signed writing, has threatened with an order or under condition, an attack against persons or property punishable by at least five years of penal servitude is sentenced to penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand francs or one of these penalties only.  
The verbal threat made with an order or under condition, or the threat by gestures or emblems of an attack against persons or property punishable by at least five years of penal servitude, is punished by three months to two years and a fine of ten thousand francs to fifty thousand francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.494

Any person who has intentionally caused alarm among the population by the threat or false announcement of a danger to life, health or property is punished by three months to one year of penal servitude and a fine of twenty thousand francs to one hundred thousand francs or one of these penalties only.

#### Chapitre 3 - Endangering public security by means of weapons

###### Art.495

Weapons within the meaning of the provisions of this chapter constitute the different types of weapons or any other device classified as such in application of laws and regulations, in particular on the regime of firearms and their ammunition.

###### Art.496

Anyone who imports, acquires, possesses, transfers, rents or causes to be rented, abandons, manufactures, repairs, transits or exports weapons or ammunition in violation of the laws and regulations in force on the firearms regime is liable to penal servitude of two to ten years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five million Burundian francs.  
The penalty is doubled when the acts provided for in the preceding paragraph are committed by a public representative, a civil servant, a magistrate or a member of the defence and security forces.  
The penalty of penal servitude for life may be pronounced when the facts mentioned in the first paragraph of this article are connected to a collective enterprise aimed at overthrowing the constitutional order.  

Other offences against the laws and regulations on the firearms regime and its implementing measures are punishable by penal servitude of up to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
Also liable to the penalties mentioned in the preceding paragraph of this article are persons who, through their negligence or lack of precautions in the custody of weapons or ammunition they possess, have made their disappearance possible.  
In all cases, special confiscation of weapons and ammunition may be pronounced. It is pronounced in the case of prohibited firearms, bladed weapons and special devices.

#### Chapitre 4 - Des manquements à la solidarité publique

###### Art.497

Anyone who, having knowledge of an offence punishable by more than five years of penal servitude, already attempted or completed, has not immediately notified the public authorities when it was still possible to prevent or limit its effects, is punished by one quarter of the penal servitude that he himself would incur if he were the perpetrator of the offence.  
When the offence was punishable by penal servitude for life, the perpetrator of the offence provided for in the preceding paragraph is punished by penal servitude of ten to twenty years.

###### Art.498

Anyone who, being able to prevent by his immediate action, without risk to himself or to third parties, either an offence against persons or an offence against property, voluntarily refrains from doing so is punished by penal servitude of one to three years and a fine of twenty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, or one of these penalties only.  
Punished by the same penalties is anyone who voluntarily refrains from providing assistance to a person in danger that, without risk to himself or to third parties, he could provide, either by his personal action or by summoning help.

#### Chapitre 5 - De l'évasion des détenus et de la rupture de ban

##### Section 1 - De l'évasion des détenus

###### Art.499.- Est considérée comme détenue toute personne

1° Qui est placée en garde à vue ;  
2° Qui se trouve en instance ou en cours de présentation à l'autorité judiciaire à l'issue d'une garde à vue ou en exécution d'un mandat d'amener ou d'arrêt ;  

3° Qui s'est vue notifier un mandat de dépôt ou un mandat d'arrêt continuant de produire effet ;  
4° Qui exécute une peine privative de liberté ou qui a été arrêtée pour exécuter cette peine
5° Qui est placée sous écrou extraditionnel ;  
6° Qui, pour des raisons de santé, est placée dans un établissement sanitaire ou hospitalier
7° Qui, condamnée, se soustrait au contrôle auquel elle est soumise alors qu'elle avait bénéficié d'une mesure d'assouplissement ;  
8° Qui, condamnée, refuse de réintégrer l'établissement pénitentiaire à l'issue d'une permission de sortie ;  
9° Qui, condamnée, est soumise à une mesure de sûreté.

###### Art.500

The act by a detainee of escaping from the custody to which he is subject, by violence, threats, breaking or corruption is punished by penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of five thousand to twenty thousand Burundian francs.  
If the offence provided for in the preceding paragraph is committed by a convicted person who is serving a sentence of penal servitude for life, the judge pronounces the measure of incompressibility of the sanction being executed.

###### Art.501

Those who have procured or facilitated the escape of a detainee are punished by half the penalty incurred by the escapee for the offence on the basis of which he was being prosecuted.

###### Art.502

If the offence has been committed by a person assigned to the conduct or custody of detainees, the penalty is penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.503

If the escape took place with violence, threats or breaking, the penalties against those who facilitated it, either by their cooperation or by providing instruments or weapons suitable for carrying it out, are penal servitude of three to ten years and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

##### Section 2 - De la rupture de ban

###### Art.504

The convicted person who contravenes the obligation to stay away from certain places or a certain region or to reside in a specified place provided for in articles 74 to 77, is punished by penal servitude of a maximum of six months.

#### Chapitre 6 - D'autres faits susceptibles de porter atteinte à la sécurité publique

##### Section 1 - Des stupéfiants

###### Art.505

Narcotics within the meaning of the provisions of this section are substances or plants classified as such in application of the provisions of the order of the Minister responsible for public health.

###### Art.506

The cultivation, sale, transport, possession and consumption of narcotics are prohibited except in the cases and conditions determined by the order of the Minister responsible for public health.

###### Art.507

Punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to two hundred and fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only are those who have contravened the regulatory provisions concerning substances classified as narcotics.

###### Art.508

Anyone who has illegally produced, imported, manufactured or exported narcotics is punished by ten to fifteen years of penal servitude and a fine of up to one million Burundian francs.  
The penalties provided for in the first paragraph are doubled when the acts are committed by an organized gang.  
The penalties are incurred even when the various acts constituting the elements of the offence have been accomplished in different countries.

###### Art.509

Punished by penal servitude of one to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one million Burundian francs are those who have facilitated the use of narcotics by others, for consideration or free of charge, either by providing premises for this purpose or by any other means.  
The guilty parties are punished by double the penalties stated in the preceding paragraph if the perpetrators are affiliated with an organized gang.  
The same penalties are pronounced against those who, by means of fictitious prescriptions or prescriptions of convenience, have obtained said substances and against those who, knowing the fictitious or convenience nature of these prescriptions, have, upon presentation of these prescriptions, delivered narcotics.  
If the use or delivery of these narcotics has been made to minors under eighteen years of age, the penalty of penal servitude is increased to ten to twenty years.

###### Art.510

Punished by penal servitude of one to five years, and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, or one of these penalties only, are those who have, in an illicit manner, consumed or possessed one of the substances classified as narcotics.

###### Art.511.- Are punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article

1° Those who, by any means whatsoever, have incited one of the offences punished by articles 504 to 508 even though such incitement has not been followed by effect, or who have presented them in a favourable light;  
2° Those who, by any means whatsoever, have incited, even though such incitement has not been followed by effect, the use of substances presented as having the effects of narcotic substances or plants.

###### Art.512

In all cases provided for in articles 506 to 510, the court orders the confiscation of substances or plants classified as narcotics and the confiscation of equipment and installations having been used for the consumption, manufacture and transport of said substances or plants.  
The court orders the destruction of crops and confiscated substances or plants.  
The destruction of these crops is provided for ex officio by the authority, and at the expense of the offenders.

###### Art.513

For any offence of consumption of narcotics, the judge pronounces socio-judicial supervision as an additional penalty in accordance with the provisions of articles 78 to 81.

##### Section 2 - De l'ivresse publique et du tapage nocturne

###### Art.514

Is punished by a fine of ten thousand to twenty thousand Burundian francs, anyone who has been found in a manifest state of intoxication in streets, squares, roads, drinking establishments, performance halls or other public places, as well as in non-enclosed places on which the public may have direct view.

###### Art.515

Are punished by a fine of twenty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs, sellers of beverages as well as their agents who have given drink to manifestly intoxicated persons or who have received them in their establishments.

###### Art.516

Are punished by one month to two months and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only, sellers of beverages who have received in their establishments minors under eighteen years of age not accompanied by their parents or guardians.  
Are punished by the same penalties, operators of nightclubs who admit into their establishments minors under eighteen years of age not accompanied by their parents or guardians.

###### Art.517

Sellers of beverages who have contravened the regulatory provisions in this matter are punished by a fine of twenty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.518

In case of recidivism, the court may order the temporary or permanent closure of the establishment.

###### Art.519

Is punished by penal servitude of a maximum of one month and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only, anyone who has been guilty of noise and nocturnal disturbance of a nature to disturb the tranquillity of the inhabitants.

##### Section 3 - Des boissons prohibées

###### Art.520

Constitute prohibited beverages within the meaning of the provisions of this section alcoholic or non-alcoholic beverages manufactured artisanally at local level or imported, classified as such in application of the provisions of the order of the Minister having public health in his attributions.

###### Art.521

The importation or manufacture, purchase, possession or circulation with a view to sale, sale or offer free of charge of the beverages specified in the preceding article is punished by penal servitude of one year to two years and a fine of five hundred thousand to two million Burundian francs.  
The products and apparatus having been used to commit the offence as well as the beverages already manufactured are seized and immediately destroyed. The official report of destruction is added to the case file.  
In case of recidivism, imprisonment of four years may in addition be pronounced.

###### Art.522

Consumers of the beverages specified in article520 are punished by penal servitude of three months to six months and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.523

The penalty is doubled if the offences provided for in articles 521 and 522 are committed in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of his functions by a person holding public authority or charged with a public service mission.

##### Section 4 - Du vagabondage et de l'exploitation de la mendicité

###### Art.524

Is a vagrant, any person who wanders without exercising a profession or trade, without possessing means of subsistence and who does not justify a certain domicile.  
The vagrant is punished by penal servitude of fourteen days to two months and a fine of five to ten thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.525

Any able-bodied person who exploits charity as a professional beggar or who, through idleness, drunkenness or dissolute morals, lives in a habitual state of begging, is punished by fourteen days to two months and a fine of five to ten thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.

###### Art.526

The exploitation of begging is the act of anyone in any manner whatsoever:
1° Of organizing the begging of another person in order to derive profit therefrom;  
2° Of deriving profit from the begging of another person, of deriving benefits therefrom or of receiving subsidies from a person habitually engaging in begging;  
3° Of recruiting, leading or diverting a person and delivering him to begging or of exercising pressure on him so that he begs or continues to do so.

###### Art.527

The exploitation of begging is punished by three to five years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.  
The exploitation of begging is punished by five to ten years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs when it is committed:
1° Against a minor;  
2° A person whose particular vulnerability, due to his age, an illness, an infirmity, a physical or mental deficiency or a state of pregnancy, is apparent or known to its perpetrator;  
3° Against several persons;  
4° Against a person who has been incited to engage in begging either outside the territory of the Republic, or upon his arrival on the territory of the Republic;  
5° By a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant of the person who begs or by a person who has authority over him or abuses the authority conferred upon him by his functions;  
6° With the use of constraint, violence or fraudulent manoeuvres on the person engaging in begging, on the family or on the person being in habitual relation with him;  
7° By several persons acting in the capacity of perpetrators or accomplices, without their constituting an organized gang.  
The exploitation of the begging of another person is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of ten to fifteen years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs when it is committed by an organized gang.

### Titre 8

Des infractions contre la famille et la moralité publique Chapitre 1 - Des infractions contre l'ordre des familles

##### Section 1 - De l'avortement

###### Art.528

Anyone who, by food, beverages, medicines, has, intentionally, caused a woman to abort, outside the cases provided for by law, is punished by penal servitude of one year to two years and a fine of twenty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.529

Where an abortion has been caused by violence exercised voluntarily, but without intention to produce it, the offender is punished by six months to two years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
If the violence was committed with premeditation and with knowledge of the victim's condition, the penalty is five to ten years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.530

Anyone who has incited a pregnant woman to terminate her pregnancy is punished by penal servitude of two months to one year and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.531

If the offenders practice a medical or paramedical profession or are in the course of studies to obtain the diploma conferring the right to practice such a profession, they are punished by penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.532

If the abortive maneuvers have caused the death of the woman, the persons cited in the preceding article are punished by twenty years of penal servitude.

###### Art.533

A woman who has voluntarily caused herself to abort is punished by penal servitude of one year to two years and a fine of twenty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.534

The criminal sanctions provided for in the preceding articles are not applicable when the pregnancy has been terminated by a qualified medical doctor, with the written consent of the pregnant person and upon the conforming opinion of a second qualified medical doctor, with a view to averting a danger impossible to avert otherwise and threatening the life of the mother or seriously threatening her health with a grave and permanent injury.  
If the pregnant person is incapable of manifesting her will, the written consent of her legal representative is required.  
In the exercise of public prosecution and upon eventual conviction under the provisions of articles 531 to 533, account is taken of the social requirements of the environment in which the act was accomplished.  

There is no offense when the pregnancy has been terminated by decision of two medical doctors in case of emergency or impossibility of obtaining the manifestation of the will of the pregnant person or of the person authorized to represent her.

##### Section 2 - Offenses against the child

###### Art.535

For the purposes of this section, the term child means any person under eighteen years of age.

###### Art.536

Those who have exposed, caused to be exposed, abandoned or caused to be abandoned a child or an incapable person unable to protect himself by reason of his physical or mental condition, are punished by this fact alone:
1° By two months to one year of penal servitude and a fine of twenty thousand Burundian francs if the act took place in a non-solitary place;  
2° By one year to three years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand Burundian francs if the act took place in a solitary place.  
These penalties are doubled if the offenders are ascendants or are legally charged with the custody of the child or the incapable person.  
Exposure or abandonment that has resulted in mutilation or infirmity is punished by penal servitude of ten years.  
The offender is punished by twenty years of penal servitude if the exposure or abandonment has been followed by death.

###### Art.537

Whoever has abducted or caused to be abducted, diverted or caused to be diverted, displaced or caused to be displaced children from the places where they had been placed by those having parental authority over them or by persons to whom they had been entrusted, is punished by one year to five years of penal servitude and a fine of ten thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
The penal servitude is increased from five to ten years if the acts were committed with violence, fraud or threats.  
It is increased from ten years to twenty years if the offenders acted with the aim of obtaining a ransom or obtaining the execution of an order or a condition.  
If the abduction has been followed by the death of the child, the offenders are punished by penal servitude for life.  
The father or mother who has abducted or caused to be abducted, displaced or caused to be displaced a child without the consent of the other is punished by the same penalties.  
The father or any other person who abducts or causes to be abducted, displaces or causes to be displaced a child still at the maternal breast, without the consent of the child's mother, is punished by penal servitude of five to ten years.

###### Art.538

The act, for the legitimate, natural or adoptive father or mother of evading his legal obligations is punished by six months to one year of penal servitude and one hundred thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs fine or one of these penalties only.  
The penal servitude would be doubled, if the act of evading his obligations had compromised the health, safety, morality or education of his child.

###### Art.539

Anyone who has, with fraudulent intent, obtained for himself an adoption contravening the provisions of the law on adoption is punished by penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.540

Any person who has intervened as an intermediary in obtaining an adoption for another without being a member of an organization previously approved for this purpose, has obtained an adoption contravening the provisions of the law on the matter, is punished by the same penalties.

###### Art.541

The act of directly inciting a child to commit an unlawful act or one likely to compromise his health or his morality or his development is punished by two to five years of penal servitude and a fine of ten thousand to twenty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.542

Whoever has used, recruited or offered a child for purposes of prostitution, production of pornographic material or shows is punished by penal servitude of three years to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.543

Any person who is guilty of an act or transaction involving the transfer of a child to someone against remuneration or any other advantage is punished by penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine of twenty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.544

Any person who has used a child for sexual activities against remuneration or any other form of advantage is punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article.

###### Art.545

Whoever has used a child for work which, by its nature or the conditions under which it is performed, is likely to harm his health, his safety or his morality, is punished by penal servitude of three to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.546

The penalties provided for in articles 542 to 545 of this section are doubled:
1° When the offense is committed by a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant or by any other person having authority over the victim;  

2° When the offense is committed by a person who abuses the authority conferred upon him by his functions;  
3° When the offense is committed by several persons acting as perpetrators or accomplices.

###### Art.547

When a decision has been made on the custody of a child by enforceable court decision, the father, mother or any other person who does not present the child to those who have the right to claim him, or who abducts him from those to whom his custody has been entrusted or from the places where they had placed him, is punished by one month to one year of penal servitude and twenty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs fine or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.548

Those who have attributed to a woman who was not pregnant the child born of another woman, in order to obtain for her the civil status to which she had no right, shall be punished by one to five years of penal servitude.  
Those who have substituted one child for another or who have attempted to prevent proof of the civil status of the child, have concealed the birth of a child or have passed it off as dead, shall be punished by the same penalties.  
The same penalties are applied to those who have given the mission to commit the acts mentioned under this article insofar as this mission has been carried out or failed through the intervention of a third person.  
The penalties provided for in the preceding paragraphs are doubled if the person acted in the course of exercising their functions.

##### Section 3 - Offences against marriage

###### Paragraphe 1 - Adultery

###### Art.549

Adultery is defined as the sexual union of a legally married person whose marriage is not dissolved, with a person other than their spouse.

###### Art.550

The spouse convicted of adultery is punished by a fine of twenty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.551.- The penalty set out in the preceding article is applied to the accomplice

###### Art.552

Prosecution or conviction for adultery may only take place upon complaint by the spouse who claims to be offended. The complainant may, at any stage of the proceedings, by withdrawing their complaint, halt the procedure.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Polygamy and polyandry

###### Art.553

Whoever, being bound by the ties of marriage, has contracted one or more others before the dissolution of the previous one, shall be punished for polygamy or polyandry by penal servitude of six months to two years and a fine of twenty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
In no case may the spouse in such a union be considered as a dependent person within the meaning of tax, social or administrative legislation.

###### Paragraphe 3 - Concubinage

###### Art.554

The spouse convicted of having maintained a male or female concubine is sentenced to a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
The penalty is doubled when the concubinage is maintained in the conjugal home.  
Prosecution or conviction may only take place upon complaint by the spouse who claims to be offended. The complainant may, at any stage of the proceedings, by withdrawing their complaint, halt the procedure.

##### Section 4 - Offences against family morality

###### Paragraphe 1 - Incest

###### Art.555

The following are considered as incest and punished by penal servitude of two to five years, sexual relations between:
1° Relatives in direct descending and ascending line, whether the ties of kinship are legitimate, natural or adoptive;  
2° Brothers and sisters of the whole blood, of the half blood on the father's side or on the mother's side;  
3° A person and a child of their brothers or sisters of the whole blood, of the half blood on the father's side or on the mother's side, or with a descendant of that child;  
4° The stepfather or stepmother and the descendant of the other spouse.  
In all cases, if incest is committed by an adult with a minor under eighteen years of age, the penalty incurred by the perpetrator is that provided for rape with violence.  
The conviction pronounced against the perpetrator of the offence includes: 1° Loss of parental authority or legal guardianship;  

2° Deprivation of civic, civil and family rights; 3° Publication of the conviction;  
4° Presentation of the convicted person to the public.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Abandonment of family

###### Art.556

The following are punished by penal servitude not exceeding two months and a fine of twenty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only:
1° The father or mother of a family who abandons without serious reason, for more than two months, the family residence and evades all or part of the moral or material obligations arising from parental authority or legal guardianship; the period of two months may only be interrupted by a return to the home implying the will to resume family life definitively;  
2° The spouse who, without valid reason, voluntarily abandons for more than two months their spouse and who refuses to provide the maintenance and assistance that spouses owe each other according to the relevant provisions of the Code of Persons and the Family;  
3° The husband who, without serious reason, voluntarily abandons for more than two months his wife, knowing her to be pregnant;  
4° The father or mother, whether or not deprivation of parental authority is pronounced against them, who compromises by ill-treatment, by pernicious examples of habitual drunkenness or notorious misconduct, by lack of care or by lack of necessary supervision, either the health, or the safety, or the morality of their children or of one or more of them.

###### Art.557

Whoever, having been sentenced by a judicial decision that has become res judicata, to provide maintenance to their spouse, descendants or ascendants, has voluntarily remained for more than two months without paying the instalments, is punished by two months to six months of penal servitude and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only.  
Failure to pay is presumed voluntary until proof to the contrary. Insolvency resulting from habitual misconduct, laziness, drunkenness or commitments made after the court was seized is in no case a valid excuse for the debtor.

##### Section 5 - Domestic violence

###### Art.558

Whoever subjects their spouse, child or any other person living under the same roof to cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment is punished by penal servitude of three years to five years and a fine of fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.559

Whoever compels a woman to conceive and carry a pregnancy to term is punished by penal servitude of three to five years. Whoever forces a woman to have an abortion is punished by the same penalties.

###### Art.560

Any person who expels from the family home their spouse, child or any person in their care who is not able to take care of themselves is punished by penal servitude of one year to two years.

#### Chapitre 2 - Offences against public morals

##### Section 1 - Prostitution

###### Art.561

The act of delivering one's body to the pleasure of others and making it a profession constitutes an act of prostitution.

###### Paragraphe 1 - Incitement to debauchery and prostitution

###### Art.562

Whoever has committed an offence against morals by inciting, facilitating or favouring, to satisfy the passions of others, the debauchery, corruption or prostitution of persons of either sex aged or apparently aged over twenty-one years, is punished by one to five years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
The penalty is increased from five to ten years if the person upon whom the debauchery, corruption or prostitution was committed is aged or apparently aged under twenty-one years.  
The age of the victim may be determined by medical examination in the absence of civil status records.

###### Art.563

The penalties set out in the first paragraph of the preceding article are applicable to:
1° Whoever has recruited, enticed or diverted, for the purpose of debauchery or prostitution, another adult or minor person, even if consenting;  
2° Whoever maintains, for the same purposes, an adult or minor person, even if consenting.

###### Art.564

Any person who, by threat, pressure, maneuver or any other means, obstructs the action of prevention, assistance or rehabilitation undertaken by qualified organizations in favor of persons engaging in prostitution or in danger of prostitution, is punished by six months to five years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 2 - Du proxénétisme

###### Art.565

Any person who, directly or through an intermediary, directs, manages, or knowingly finances or contributes to financing a house of prostitution, is punished by two to five years of penal servitude and a fine of one hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.

###### Paragraphe 3 - Des facilités en vue de la prostitution

###### Art.566

Any person who, in any form whatsoever, profits from prostitution, shares the proceeds of the prostitution of a major or minor person, even if the latter consents, or receives subsidies from a person habitually engaging in prostitution, is punished by one year to five years of penal servitude and a fine of twenty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.567

Any person who, in any manner whatsoever, knowingly aids, assists or protects the prostitution of another, or soliciting for the purpose of prostitution, is punished by penal servitude of one year to three years and a fine of twenty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.568

The penalties provided for in the preceding article are applicable to any person who acts as an intermediary, in any capacity whatsoever, between persons habitually engaging in prostitution and individuals who exploit or remunerate the prostitution of another.

###### Art.569

Any person who knowingly gives or takes on lease, in whole or in part, any building or premises for the purpose of the prostitution of another is punished by two years to five years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to one million Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.570

The penalties provided for by the provisions of this section are doubled when one of the following circumstances is established in the case:
1° The offense was committed against a person under eighteen years of age; 2° The offense was committed against a non-consenting person;  
3° The offense was committed against a person whose particular vulnerability due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or state of pregnancy, is apparent or known to its perpetrator;  
4° The offense was committed by an organized gang or by several persons acting as perpetrators, co-perpetrators or accomplices;  
5° The perpetrator of the offense acted by trickery, threats, or violence; 6° The offense was committed by an ascendant of the victim;  
7° The offense was committed by a person who has authority over the victim;  

8° The offense was committed by a servant of the victim;  
9° The offense was committed by a public official or a Minister of religion.

###### Paragraphe 4 - Du racolage

###### Art.571

Constitutes an act of soliciting, the fact that a person who engages in prostitution accosts clients or engages, in public, in acts of any nature whatsoever, with a view to attracting clients.  
It is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of one month to six months and a fine of five thousand to twenty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

##### Section 2 - De l'attentat à la pudeur

###### Art.572

Constitutes an act of indecent assault, any act of a sexual nature contrary to Burundian morals exercised intentionally and directly on a person.  
The assault exists as soon as there is commencement of execution.

###### Art.573

Indecent assault committed without violence, trickery or threats on persons of either sex is punished by penal servitude of six months to two years and a fine of twenty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.574

Indecent assault committed with violence, trickery or threats on persons of either sex is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.575

Any indecent assault committed without violence, trickery or threats on the person or with the aid of the person of a child aged or apparently aged less than eighteen years, is punished by penal servitude of five to fifteen years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
The age of the child may be determined in particular by medical examination in the absence of civil status records.  
If the assault was committed with violence, trickery or threats, the penalty of penal servitude is five years to twenty years.

###### Art.576

The minimum penalties provided by the articles of this section are doubled:
1° When the assault is committed by a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant or descendant of the victim;  
2° When the assault is committed by a person having authority over the victim;  

3° When the assault is committed by a person who has abused the authority conferred by their functions;  
4° When the assault was committed by doctors, surgeons, midwives, towards persons entrusted to their care;  
5° When the indecent assault is committed on a vulnerable person by reason of illness, infirmity or physical or mental deficiency or state of pregnancy,
6° When the indecent assault is committed under threat of a weapon; 7° When the assault is committed by a Minister of religion.

##### Section 3 - Du viol

###### Art.577

Is deemed rape with violence any act of sexual penetration, of whatever nature and by whatever means, committed by a criminally responsible person on a minor under eighteen years of age even if consenting.  
Is also deemed rape with violence, the mere fact of carnal union of the sexes committed on a minor under eighteen years of age, even if consenting.  
Domestic rape is punished by penal servitude of eight days and a fine of ten thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

###### Art.578

Commits rape, either by means of violence or serious threats or by constraint against a person, directly or through a third party, or by surprise, by psychological pressure, or on the occasion of a coercive environment, or by abusing a person who, by reason of illness, by the alteration of their faculties or by any other accidental cause would have lost the use of their senses or would have been deprived thereof by some artifice, and even if the victim is the spouse of that person:
1° Any man, whatever his age, who introduces his sexual organ, even superficially into that of a woman or any woman, whatever her age, who has forced a man to introduce, even superficially, his sexual organ into hers;  
2° Any man who has caused to penetrate, even superficially, through the anal passage, the mouth or any other orifice of the body of a woman or a man his sexual organ, any other part of the body or any other object whatsoever;  
3° Any person who introduces, even superficially, any other part of the body or any object whatsoever into the female sex organ;  
4° Any person who forces a man or a woman to penetrate, even superficially, their anal orifice, their mouth with a sexual organ;  

Is punished by five years to fifteen years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand francs to one hundred thousand francs.

###### Art.579

Rape is punished by fifteen to twenty-five years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs:
1° When it is committed against a minor between fifteen and eighteen years of age;  
2° When it is committed by a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant or descendant, by a brother or sister, by a stepfather or stepmother of the victim;  
3° When it is committed by a person in the service of the victim;  
4° When it is committed by a person who abuses the authority conferred by their functions;  
5° When it is committed by an educator;  
6° When the rape is committed by a Minister of religion;  
7° When it is committed by doctors, surgeons, midwives or other medical personnel towards persons entrusted to their care;  
8° When it is committed against a vulnerable person by reason of their age, an illness, an infirmity, a physical or mental deficiency or a state of pregnancy, which is apparent or known to the perpetrator.

###### Art.580

Rape is punished by twenty to thirty years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs:
1° When it is committed by several persons acting as perpetrator or accomplice;  
2° When the perpetrator is carrying a weapon;  
3° When it has caused the victim a serious alteration of their health and/or left serious physical and/or psychological sequelae, notably a mutilation, a permanent infirmity or the transmission of a disease;  
4° When it is committed with use or threat of a weapon;  
5° When the rape was committed against a child between twelve and fifteen years of age; 6° When it is committed in public.

###### Art.581.- Rape is punished by penal servitude for life

1° When the perpetrator knew themselves to be carrying a sexually transmitted disease known to be incurable;  

2° When the rape resulted in the death of the victim;  
3° When the rape was committed against a child under 12 years of age;  
4° When the rape was preceded, accompanied or followed by acts of torture or barbarity.

###### Art.582

The penalties provided for by the provisions of this section are non-reducible, imprescriptible, non-amnistiable and non-pardonable.

###### Art.583

The official capacity of the perpetrator of an offence relating to sexual violence may in no case exonerate them from responsibility or constitute a ground for reduction of the penalty.

###### Art.584

Hierarchical order or the command of a legitimate civil or military authority in no way exonerates the perpetrator of an offence relating to sexual violence from their responsibility.

###### Art.585

With regard to the offences of indecent assault and rape, the judge shall pronounce, in addition to the principal penalty, at least one of the following complementary penalties:
1° Publication of the conviction;  
2° Presentation of the convicted person to the public;  
3° Prohibition from exercising civic, civil and family rights; 4° Prohibition from residing in certain areas;  
5° Socio-judicial supervision.  
If the judge pronounces the penalty provided for in point 1°, the identity of the victim is neither published nor disclosed to the public.

##### Section 4 - Du harcèlement sexuel

###### Art.586

Constitutes an act of sexual harassment the fact of using against another person orders, threats or physical or psychological constraint, or serious pressure, with the aim of obtaining favours of a sexual nature, by abusing the authority conferred by one's functions.  
It is punished by one month to two years of penal servitude and one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs fine.  
If the victim of the harassment is a minor under eighteen years of age, the penalties are doubled.

##### Section 5

Des outrages publics aux bonnes mœurs, de la zoophilie et de l'homosexualité

###### Paragraphe 1 - Des outrages publics aux bonnes mœurs

###### Art.587

Whoever has exposed, sold or distributed songs, pamphlets or other writings, printed or not, figures, images, emblems or other objects contrary to good morals, is sentenced to a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
Is punished by the same penalties, whoever has, with a view to trade or distribution, held, imported or caused to be imported, transported or caused to be transported, delivered to a transport or distribution agent, advertised by any means of publicity songs, pamphlets, writings, figures, images, emblems or objects contrary to good morals.  
In the cases provided for by the preceding paragraphs, the author of the writing, of the figure, of the image, the person who printed or reproduced them, the manufacturers of the emblem or of the object are punished by a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
Whoever has sung, read, recited, caused to be heard or uttered obscenities in public meetings or places before several persons and in a manner to be heard by those persons, is punished by a fine of ten thousand to twenty thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.588

Whoever has publicly outraged morals by actions that offend decency is punished by a fine of fifty thousand francs to one hundred thousand francs.

###### Paragraphe 2 - De la zoophilie

###### Art.589

Whoever has voluntarily had sexual relations with an animal is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of one year to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.  
Is punished by a penalty of penal servitude of five years to ten years and a fine of twenty thousand to fifty thousand Burundian francs whoever has compelled a person to have sexual relations with an animal. The penalty is doubled if the person compelled is a minor.

###### Paragraphe 3 - De l'homosexualité

###### Art.590

Whoever engages in sexual relations with a person of the same sex is punished by penal servitude of three months to two years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs or one of these penalties only.

### Titre 9

Des atteintes à la sûreté de l'Etat Chapitre 1 - Des atteintes à la sûreté extérieure de l'Etat

##### Section 1 - De la trahison et de l'espionnage

###### Art.591

Is guilty of treason and punished by penal servitude for life, any Burundian who bears arms against Burundi.

###### Art.592

Is guilty of treason and punished by penal servitude for life, any Burundian who:
1° Maintains intelligence with a foreign power or with its agents, to induce that power to undertake hostilities against Burundi or to provide it with the means to do so;  
2° Delivers to a foreign power or to its agents defence works, posts, ports, warehouses, materiel, munitions, vessels, buildings or aircraft belonging to Burundi;  
3° With a view to harming national defence, destroys or damages a ship, an aircraft, materiel, supplies, a construction or any installation whatsoever, or who, with the same aim, introduces into them either before or after their completion, defects of a nature to damage them or to cause an accident.

###### Art.593

Is guilty of treason and punished by penal servitude for life, any Burundian who, in time of war:
1° Incites military personnel or sailors to enter the service of a foreign power, facilitates the means for them to do so or carries out enlistments for a power at war with Burundi;  
2° Maintains intelligence with a foreign power or with agents with a view to favouring the enterprises of that power against Burundi;  
3° Knowingly participates in an enterprise of demoralisation of the Army or of the Nation having as its object to harm national defence.

###### Art.594

Is guilty of treason and punished by penal servitude for life, any Burundian who, in time of war:
1° Delivers to a foreign power or its agents, in whatever form and by whatever means, information, an object, document or process that must be kept secret in the interest of national defence;  
2° Secures, by whatever means, possession of such information, object, document or process, with a view to delivering it to a foreign power or its agents;  

3° Destroys or allows to be destroyed such information, object, document or process with a view to favouring a foreign power.

###### Art.595

Is guilty of espionage and punished by penal servitude for life, any foreigner who commits one of the acts referred to in the two preceding articles.

###### Art.596

Without prejudice to the application of articles 37 and 38, are punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs:
1° The offer or proposal to commit one of the offences provided for in articles 592 to 593
2° The acceptance of such offer or proposal.

##### Section 2 - Des autres atteintes à la sûreté extérieure de l'Etat

###### Art.597

Is punished by penal servitude of two to ten years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs anyone who, without intent of treason or espionage:
1° Secures, being without authority, possession of information, an object, document or process that must be kept secret in the interest of national defence or the knowledge of which may lead to the discovery of a national defence secret;  
2° Destroys, removes, allows to be destroyed or removed, or allows to be reproduced such information, object, document or process;  
3° Brings or allows to be brought to the knowledge of an unauthorised person, or of the public, such information, object, document or process, or has extended its disclosure.

###### Art.598

Is punished by penal servitude of six months to one year and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, anyone who, without intent of treason or espionage, has brought to the knowledge of an unauthorised person or of the public, military information not made public by the competent authority and the disclosure of which is manifestly likely to harm national defence.

###### Art.599

Is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs anyone who:
1° Enters under a disguise or a false name, or by concealing their status or nationality, into a defence work, post, depot or military store, into a warship or a commercial vessel employed for national defence, into a military establishment or into an establishment or shipyard of interest to national defence;  

2° Even without disguising themselves, or without concealing their name, status or nationality, has organised in a covert manner any means of correspondence or remote transmission likely to harm national defence.

###### Art.600

Is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, anyone who maintains with the agents of a foreign power, intelligence likely to harm the military, political or economic situation of Burundi.

###### Art.601

Is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand francs to one hundred thousand francs, anyone who, in time of war:
1° Maintains, without authorisation of the Government, correspondence or relations with the agents of an enemy power;  
2° Carries out directly or through an intermediary, acts of commerce with the subjects or agents of an enemy power, in defiance of the prohibitions enacted.

###### Art.602

Is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, anyone who has, by hostile acts not approved by the Government, exposed Burundi to hostilities on the part of a foreign power.  
If hostilities have ensued, the penal servitude is five to twenty years.

###### Art.603

The penalties provided for in articles 593 to 596, 600 and 601, 1° are doubled if the perpetrator of the offence is a Burundian.  
The penalty provided for in article 597 is penal servitude of thirty years if the perpetrator of the offence is a Burundian.

##### Section 3 - Du mercenariat

###### Art.604.- Le terme mercenaire s'entend de toute personne

1° Who is specially recruited in the country or abroad to fight in an armed conflict;  
2° Who, in fact, takes a direct part in hostilities;  
3° Who takes part in hostilities with a view to obtaining a personal advantage and to whom is effectively promised, by a party to the conflict or on its behalf, material remuneration;  
4° Who is neither a national of a party to the conflict nor a resident of the territory controlled by a party to the conflict;  
5° Who is not a member of the armed forces of a party to the conflict;  

6° Who has not been sent by a State other than a party to the conflict on an official mission as a member of the armed forces of that State.

###### Art.605

Is guilty of mercenarism and is punished by five years to twenty years of penal servitude and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs, anyone who, with the aim of opposing armed violence to a process of self-determination, to the stability or to the territorial integrity of another State, has, on national territory:
1° Sheltered, organised, financed, assisted, equipped, trained, supported or employed, in whatever manner, bands of mercenaries;  
2° Enlisted, engaged in the said bands.

###### Art.606

The penalty of penal servitude for life is pronounced against any person who has assumed command of mercenaries, against one who has given them orders, or against one guilty of the crime of mercenarism directed against Burundi.

#### Chapitre 2 - Des atteintes à la sûreté intérieure de l'Etat

##### Section 1 - Des attentats et complots contre le Chef de l'Etat

###### Art.607

An attack against the life or against the person of the Head of State is punished by penal servitude for life.  
If it has caused him neither bloodshed, nor wound, nor illness, the attack against his person is punished by penal servitude of thirty years.

###### Art.608

A plot against the life or against the person of the Head of State is punished by penal servitude of ten to fifteen years and a fine of two million to five million Burundian francs if any act has been committed to prepare its execution.  
If there has been a proposal made and not agreed to form a plot against the life or against the person of the Head of State, the one who made such a proposal is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of five hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.

##### Section 2

Des attentats, complots et autres infractions contre l'autorité de l'Etat et l'intégrité du territoire

###### Art.609

An attack the purpose of which has been, either to destroy or to change the constitutional regime, or to incite citizens or inhabitants to arm themselves against the authority of the State or to arm themselves against each other, or to undermine the integrity of the national territory, is punished by penal servitude of thirty years.

###### Art.610

A conspiracy formed for one of the purposes mentioned in the preceding article is punished by penal servitude of ten to fifteen years and a fine of two million to five

million Burundian francs if any act has been committed or commenced to prepare its execution.  
If there has been a proposal not accepted to form a conspiracy to achieve one of the ends mentioned in article 609, the person who made such a proposal is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of one million to two million Burundian francs.

###### Art.611

Whoever, outside the cases provided for in articles 613 and 614, has undertaken by any means whatsoever to undermine the integrity of the national territory, is punished by penal servitude of five years to ten years and a fine of one million to three million Burundian francs.

###### Art.612

Those who have raised or caused to be raised armed troops, engaged or enlisted, caused to be engaged or enlisted soldiers, or have supplied them with arms or ammunition, without order or authorization from the Government, are punished by penal servitude of five to twenty years and a fine of one million to five million Burundian francs.

###### Art.613

The following are punished by penal servitude of five to twenty years and a fine of one million to five million Burundian francs:
1° Those who, without right or legitimate reason, have taken any military command whatsoever;  
2° Those who, against the order of the Government, have retained such command;  
3° Those who have kept their army or troop assembled after its discharge or separation has been ordered.

##### Section 3

Attacks and conspiracies tending to bring about massacre, devastation and pillage

###### Art.614

An attack the purpose of which has been to bring about massacre is punished by penal servitude for life.  
An attack the purpose of which has been to bring about devastation or pillage is punished by penal servitude of fifteen to twenty years.

###### Art.615

A conspiracy formed for one of the purposes mentioned in the preceding article is punished by penal servitude of ten to fifteen years and a fine of one million to five million Burundian francs, if any act has been committed or commenced to prepare its execution.  
If there has been a proposal made and not accepted to form a conspiracy to achieve one of the ends mentioned in article 611, the person who made such a proposal is punished by penal servitude of five to ten years and a fine of one million to two million Burundian francs.

##### Section 4 - Participation in armed bands

###### Art.616

Any person who, with a view to disturbing the State by one of the attacks provided for in articles 609 and 611, by the invasion or division of public or private properties, or by making attack or resistance against the public force acting against the perpetrators of these offences, has placed himself at the head of armed bands or has exercised any command therein, is punished by penal servitude for life.  
The same penalty is applied to those who have directed the association, raised or caused to be raised, organized or caused to be organized the bands.

###### Art.617

Individuals forming part of the bands referred to in the preceding article, without exercising any command therein and who have been seized at the scene of the seditious gathering, are punished by penal servitude of ten to fifteen years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.618

In the case where one of the attacks provided for in articles 609 and 612 has been committed by an armed band, the penalty of penal servitude for life is applied without distinction of rank, to all individuals forming part of the band and who have been seized at the scene.  
Any person who has directed the sedition, or has exercised any command in the band, is punished by the same penalty, even if not seized at the scene.

###### Art.619

No penalty is pronounced, for the act of sedition, against those who, having formed part of an armed band without exercising any command therein, withdrew at the first warning from the civil or military authorities, or even thereafter, when they were seized only outside the scene of the seditious gathering, without opposing resistance and without arms.  
In this case, they are punished only for the particular offences that they personally committed.

##### Section 5 - Participation in an insurrectional movement

###### Art.620

Individuals who, in an insurrectional movement, are punished by penal servitude of two to ten years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs:
1° Have made or helped to make barricades, entrenchments or any other works having the purpose of hindering or stopping the exercise of the public force;  
2° Have prevented, by means of violence or threats, the intervention or assembly of the public force or who have provoked or facilitated the gathering of insurgents, either by the distribution of orders or proclamations, or by the carrying of flags or other rallying signs, or by any other means of appeal;  

3° Have, in order to make attack or resistance against the public force, invaded or occupied buildings, posts or other public establishments, inhabited or uninhabited houses. The penalty is the same with regard to the owner or tenant who, knowing the purpose of the insurgents, has procured for them without constraint the entry to said houses.

###### Art.621

Individuals who, in an insurrectional movement, are punished by penal servitude of five to twenty years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs:
1° Have seized arms, ammunition or materials of all kinds, either by means of violence or threats, or by the pillage of shops or public establishments, or by the disarming of agents of the public force;  
2° Have carried visible arms or maintained caches of ammunition.  
Individuals who have made use of their arms are punished by penal servitude for life.

###### Art.622

Those who have directed or organized an insurrectional movement having made use of arms are punished by penal servitude for life.

##### Section 6 - Other attacks on the internal security of the State

###### Art.623

Any person who, for propaganda purposes, has distributed, put into circulation or exposed to public view, leaflets, bulletins or flags of foreign origin or inspiration of a nature to harm the national interest, is punished by penal servitude of two to five years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs.  
Any person who has held such bulletins or flags with a view to distribution, circulation or display for propaganda purposes is punished by the same penalties.

###### Art.624

Any person who receives from a foreign person or organization, directly or indirectly, in any form and under any title whatsoever, gifts, presents, loans or other advantages, intended or used in whole or in part to conduct or remunerate in Burundi an activity or propaganda of a nature to undermine the integrity or independence of Burundi, or to shake the loyalty that citizens owe to the State and the Institutions of Burundi, is punished by penal servitude of one year to five years and a fine of one hundred thousand to five hundred thousand Burundian francs.

###### Art.625

Shall be punished by penal servitude of one to three years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs or by one of these penalties only
1° Whoever has publicly attacked the binding force of laws or directly incited disobedience to them;  
2° Whoever has knowingly spread false rumours likely to alarm the population or to incite them against the public authorities or to civil war;  

3° Whoever, with a view to disturbing public peace, has knowingly contributed to the publication, dissemination or reproduction by any means whatsoever, of false news or of fabricated, falsified or falsely attributed documents to third parties;  
4° Whoever has displayed or caused to be displayed, in public places or places open to the public, drawings, posters, engravings, paintings, photographs, any objects or images likely to disturb public peace.

###### Art.626

Shall be punished by penal servitude of ten to twenty years and a fine of fifty thousand to two hundred thousand Burundian francs whoever has undermined the economy or national security by stealing, destroying, overturning or damaging, by any means whatsoever, in whole or in part, buildings, bridges, dikes, roads, railways, telegraphic or telephonic apparatus or other constructions belonging to the State or to other state or para-state organs.

##### Section 7 - Des définitions portant sur les dispositions des sections 1 à 6

###### Art.627.- An attempt exists as soon as there is a punishable attempt

###### Art.628

There is a conspiracy as soon as the resolution to act has been agreed upon between two or more persons.

###### Art.629

Included in the word "weapons" are all machines, all instruments, utensils or other sharp, piercing or blunt objects which one has seized to kill, injure or strike, even if one has not used them.

###### Art.630

By "insurrectional movement" is meant a collective movement which manifests itself, either by acts undermining the established powers and institutions, or by assaults against persons, devastation or pillage.

#### Chapitre 3 - Des dispositions communes aux deux chapitres précédents

###### Art.631

Shall be punished by penal servitude of three months to two years and a fine of fifty thousand to one hundred thousand Burundian francs, or by one of these penalties only, whoever, having knowledge of plans or acts of treason, espionage or other activities likely to harm national defence, attempts or conspiracies against the internal security of the State, does not report them to the military, administrative or judicial authorities, from the moment he became aware of them.

###### Art.632

In addition to the persons designated in article 38, shall be punished as an accomplice whoever, other than the perpetrator or the accomplice:
1° Provides without constraint and with knowledge of their intentions, subsidies, means of subsistence, lodging, place of retreat or meeting to the perpetrators of offences against the security of the State;  

2° Knowingly carries the correspondence of the perpetrators of such offences, or knowingly facilitates in any manner whatsoever, the search for, concealment, transport or transmission of the object of the offence.

###### Art.633

In addition to the persons designated in article 320, shall be punished as a receiver whoever, other than the perpetrator or the accomplice of an offence against the security of the State:
1° Knowingly receives the objects or instruments having served or intended to serve to commit the offence or the objects, materials or documents obtained by the offence;  
2° Knowingly destroys, removes, conceals, hides or alters a public or private document likely to facilitate the investigation of the offence, the discovery of evidence or the punishment of its perpetrators.  
In the case provided for in this article, the court may exempt from the penalty incurred the relatives or in-laws of the offender up to and including the fourth degree.

###### Art.634

Shall be exempted from the penalty incurred whoever, before execution or attempt of an offence against the security of the State, gives first notice of it to the administrative or judicial authorities.  
Exemption from penalty is also discretionary if the denunciation occurs after the commission or attempt of the offence but before the commencement of prosecution.  
Exemption from penalty is also discretionary with regard to the offender who, after the commencement of prosecution, enables the arrest of the perpetrators and accomplices of the same offence, or of other offences of the same nature or of the same gravity.

###### Art.635

The confiscation of the object of the offence and of the objects having served to commit it is always pronounced.  
Property which does not belong to the perpetrator of the offence is confiscated in case of proven complicity of the owner.  
The remuneration received by the offender, or the amount of its value when the remuneration could not be seized, is declared forfeited to the Treasury.

###### Art.636

Any person guilty of treason, attempt or conspiracy against the internal security of the State is deprived for a period of five years of the right to vote and the right to stand for election.

#### Chapitre 4 - Des actes de terrorisme et de bioterrorisme

###### Art.637

Constitute acts of terrorism, the following intentional acts committed within the framework of an individual or collective enterprise having the purpose of seriously disturbing public order by intimidation or terror:

1° Intentional attacks on the life and physical integrity of the person, kidnapping and sequestration as well as the hijacking of aircraft, ships or any other means of transport;  
2° Thefts, extortions, destructions, damages and deteriorations; 3° The manufacture or possession of machines, murderous or explosive devices;  
4° The production, sale, import or export of explosive substances;  
5° The acquisition, possession, transport or illegitimate carrying of explosive substances or devices manufactured using said substances.

###### Art.638.- Also constitute acts of terrorism

1° The act of introducing into the atmosphere, on the ground, in the subsoil or in the waters, of rivers or the lake and belonging to the territory of Burundi, a substance likely to endanger the health of human populations or animals, with the purpose of seriously disturbing public order by intimidation and terror;  
2° The act of participating in a group formed or in an agreement established with a view to the preparation, characterized by one or more material acts, of one of the acts of terrorism set out in article 637.

###### Art.639

Also qualified as an act of terrorism is the act of financing a terrorist enterprise, by providing, collecting or managing funds, securities or property of any kind or by giving advice for this purpose, with the intention of having these funds, securities or property used or knowing that they are intended to be used, in whole or in part, with a view to committing any one of the acts of terrorism provided for under this chapter, regardless of the possible occurrence of such an act.

###### Art.640

All the acts of terrorism listed above, all acts of import, export, acquisition, possession, transfer, abandonment, manufacture, transport, transit and/or use of biological agents, chemical, radiological, nuclear and explosive weapons or materials with the purpose of harming or killing human beings, animals or plants or with the intention of intimidating or frightening constitute acts of terrorism.  
Biological agents include bacteria, viruses, moulds, fungi, rickettsiae, toxins.  
Radiological and nuclear elements include Alpha particles, Beta particles, Gamma radiation, neutrons.

###### Art.641

An act of terrorism is punishable by a sentence of penal servitude of ten to twenty years and a fine of two hundred thousand to one million Burundian francs.  
Where such act has resulted in the death of one or more persons, it is punishable by a sentence of penal servitude for life.  

The penalties provided above are not subject to reduction.

###### Art.642

Natural or legal persons guilty of the offences provided for in this chapter also incur one of the following additional penalties:
1° Deprivation of civil rights;  
2° Prohibition from exercising a public function or from exercising a professional or social activity in the exercise of which or on the occasion of which the offence was committed;  
3°, Prohibition from residing in certain areas where the perpetrator is a foreigner.

### Titre 10 - Transitional and final provisions

###### Art.643

Regulatory acts adopted in execution of the law, administrative and police regulations of public authority and local authorities may not establish penalties other than administrative fines:
1° Not exceeding a fine of fifty thousand Burundian francs for decrees;  
2° Not exceeding a fine of twenty thousand Burundian francs, for ordinances;  
3° Not exceeding a fine of five thousand Burundian francs, for acts of local authorities.  
An exception is made to the preceding provisions with regard to administrative fines for offences in the tax and customs fields, as well as in foreign exchange regulations or of an economic nature.

###### Art.644

The penalties provided for by regulatory acts and administrative and police regulations enacted prior to the entry into force of this Code are reduced, where necessary, to the maxima set out in the preceding article.

###### Art.645

Offences and contraventions of decrees, orders, ordinances, decisions, administrative and police regulations, in respect of which the law does not determine particular penalties, are punished ex officio by penalties not exceeding the maxima of those provided for in article 643 according to the distinctions made therein.

###### Art.646

Special laws certain penal provisions of which have been incorporated into this Code remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to this law.
