# THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF THE REPUBLIC OF EL SALVADOR, CONSIDERING:

THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF THE REPUBLIC OF EL SALVADOR, CONSIDERING:
1.	That the Constitution of the Republic in its article 32, recognizes the Family as the fundamental basis of society, and the State is obligated to enact the necessary legislation for its protection and create the appropriate agencies and services for its integration, welfare and social, cultural and economic development;  
2.	That it is the responsibility of the State to adapt internal legislation to international Treaties and Conventions relating to the family, women and children, in order to comply with article 144 of the Constitution of the Republic;  
3.	That violence committed by or against any of the members of the family, constitutes a constant aggression against the right to a life free from fear, against the physical, mental, moral and sexual integrity of the human person and their dignity and security;  
4.	That intrafamily violence is a complex social phenomenon that has remained hidden, which has enabled the impunity of the offender and the lack of protection of the victim; and,
5.	THEREFORE, That in order to prevent, punish and eradicate intrafamily violence and confront it in all its magnitude, it is convenient to enact the necessary and adequate legislation, in exercise of its constitutional powers and at the initiative of Deputies Mercedes Gloria Salguero Gross, Julio Antonio Gamero Quintanilla, José Rafael Machuca Zelaya, Alfonso Aristides Alvarenga, Ana Guadalupe Martínez Menéndez, Carmen Elena Calderón de Escalón, Walter René Araujo Morales, José Eduardo Sancho Castañeda, Gustavo Rogelio Salinas Olmedo, David Acuña, Herbert Maurice Aguilar Zepeda, Amado Aguiluz Aguiluz, Alex René Aguirre Guevara, Sonia del Carmen Aguiñada C., Selin Ernesto Alabí Mendoza, José Gilberto Alegría Morales, José Merzan López, Alfredo Arbizú Zelaya, Oscar Armando Salinas, Arturo Argumedo, Juan Antonio Ascencio Oliva, José Ramón Benítez, Félix Blanco, Jorge Alberto Carranza Alvarez, José Armando Cienfuegos Mendoza, Luis Alberto Cruz, Eugenio Chicas Martínez, Jorge Augusto Díaz Rivas, Elí Avileo Díaz, Juan Duch Martínez, Miguel Antonio Espinal Lazo, Hermes Alcides Flores, Francisco Guillermo Flores Pérez, José Victor García, Josefina de Maza, Norma

## CHAPTER I Fundamental Provisions Articles 1 to 5 Purposes

### ARTICLE 1

The present law has the following purposes
1.	To establish adequate mechanisms to prevent, sanction and eradicate intrafamily violence, in the relationships of family members or in any other interpersonal relationship of said members, whether or not they share the same dwelling;  
2.	To apply the preventive, precautionary and protective measures necessary to guarantee the life, integrity and dignity of the victims of intrafamily violence;  
3.	To regulate rehabilitation measures for offenders; and,
4.	TO PROVIDE SPECIAL PROTECTION TO VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE IN COUPLE RELATIONSHIPS, CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS, OLDER ADULTS AND PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES. THIS SPECIAL PROTECTION IS NECESSARY TO REDUCE THE INEQUALITY OF POWER THAT EXISTS AMONG THE PERSONS WHO CONSTITUTE A FAMILY AND TO TAKE INTO ACCOUNT THE SPECIAL SITUATION OF EACH ONE OF THEM.  
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002;  
D.O. N° 137,  
T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.  
For the purposes of this law, family relationships are understood as the relationships between spouses, ex-spouses, cohabitants, ex-cohabitants, ascendants, descendants, collateral relatives by consanguinity, affinity, adoption, those subject to guardianship or custody, as well as any other interpersonal relationship that may generate this type of violence.

Concept and Forms of Intrafamily Violence
Article cited in: 4 judgments ARTICLE 3
Intrafamily violence constitutes any action or omission, direct or indirect, that causes harm, physical, sexual, psychological suffering or death to the persons who are members of a family.  
The forms of intrafamily violence are:
1.	Psychological violence: Direct or indirect action or omission whose purpose is to control or degrade the actions, behaviors, beliefs and decisions of other persons, by means of intimidation, manipulation, direct or indirect threat, humiliation, isolation or any other conduct or omission that produces harm to psychological health, self-determination, integral development and personal possibilities;  
2.	Physical violence: Actions, behaviors or omissions that threaten or injure the physical integrity of a person;  
3.	Sexual violence: Actions that compel a person to maintain physical or verbal sexualized contacts, or to participate in them through force, intimidation, coercion, blackmail, bribery, manipulation, threat or other mechanism that nullifies or limits personal will. Likewise, it shall be considered sexual violence when the aggressor compels the victim to perform any of these acts with third parties.  
4.	PATRIMONIAL VIOLENCE: ACTION OR OMISSION BY ONE WHO AFFECTS OR IMPEDES THE ADEQUATE ATTENTION TO THE NEEDS OF THE FAMILY OR ANY OF THE PERSONS REFERRED TO IN THE PRESENT LAW; DAMAGES, LOSES, REMOVES, DESTROYS, RETAINS, DIVERTS OR APPROPRIATES OBJECTS, INSTRUMENTS OR PROPERTY
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002;  
D.O. N° 137,  
T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.  
Scope of the Law
Article cited in: 6 judgments ARTICLE 4

This law shall be applied preventively and shall sanction acts of intrafamily violence, without prejudice to criminal liability.

## CHAPTER II

State policies for the prevention of intrafamily violence Articles 6 to 9 Objectives of the Policies

### ARTICLE 6

It is the obligation of the State to prevent, sanction and eradicate intrafamily violence and for that purpose the following actions shall be developed:
1.	To incorporate into school, academic, formal and non-formal technical training, the teaching of ethical, civic and social values; respect for the dignity of the human person, for the rights and duties of family members, children, persons with disabilities and older adults in accordance with what is established in current legislation and international instruments ratified by El Salvador;  
2.	To carry out dissemination campaigns, with the purpose of sensitizing society about the aforementioned social problem, disseminating the scope of the present law and speaking out against acts of intrafamily violence;  
3.	TO PROMOTE THE STUDY AND INVESTIGATION OF THE CAUSES AND CONSEQUENCES OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE, ITS INDICATORS, ITS DYNAMICS AND THE WAY TO PREVENT IT;  
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002;  
D.O. N° 137,  
T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.  
4.	To establish effective legal mechanisms to assist victims of intrafamily violence, through simple, agile and formality-free procedures that enable the adoption of precautionary measures;  
5.	To promote the active participation of public entities and civil society organizations dedicated to the protection of children, the family, women, persons with disabilities and older adults, for the development of preventive and control work in the execution of precautionary and protective measures for victims of intrafamily violence and the rehabilitation of offenders;  
6.	TO CREATE WITHIN THE NATIONAL CIVIL POLICE A DIVISION SPECIALIZED IN THE ATTENTION AND HANDLING OF CASES OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE AND DEFENSE OF HUMAN RIGHTS;"
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002;

7.	TO PROMOTE THE TRAINING OF PERSONNEL FROM INSTITUTIONS INVOLVED IN THE DYNAMICS OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE; AS WELL AS, IN A SPECIAL MANNER, THE

PERMANENT TRAINING OF OFFICIALS AND FORENSIC EXPERTS, SO THAT THEY ASSUME AN EFFECTIVE ROLE IN ITS ERADICATION;"
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002;  
D.O. NO 137,  
T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.  
8.	To sensitize the judicial officials competent to resolve acts of intrafamily violence;  
9.	TO INCORPORATE INTO THE STUDY PROGRAMS OF HIGHER EDUCATION CAREERS, OF STATE AND PRIVATE UNIVERSITIES AND OF THE NATIONAL PUBLIC SECURITY ACADEMY, TRAINING IN THE DYNAMICS OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE, AS WELL AS THE CORRESPONDING LEGAL REGULATIONS, FORMS OF PREVENTION AND ITS TREATMENT.

Protection Measures ARTICLE 7
To prevent, punish and eradicate the different forms of domestic violence, the following measures are established:
1.	Judicial Order to the aggressor to refrain from harassing, pursuing, intimidating, threatening or carrying out other forms of mistreatment against the victims of violence or any other member of the family group who may or may not share the same dwelling;  
2.	Judicial Order to the persons involved in the reported acts of violence to refrain from carrying out acts of harassment, intimidation, provocation, threats or other similar acts that may give rise to or foster domestic violence and the others indicated by the Family Procedural Law;  
3.	Prohibit the aggressor from threatening the victim both in the private sphere and in the public sphere;  
4.	Prohibit the aggressor from consuming alcoholic beverages, narcotics, hallucinogens or substances that generate physical or psychological dependence at the prudent discretion of the judge;  

5.	Judicial Order to the aggressor to leave the common domicile immediately. If he or she resists, the National Civil Police shall be called upon for assistance;  
6.	Assign to the victim, if so requested, a different domicile from the common one, which protects him or her from future aggressions;  
7.	Judicial Order for entry into the dwelling, when domestic violence seriously endangers the physical, sexual, psychological and patrimonial integrity of any of its inhabitants;  
8.	SUSPEND THE AGGRESSOR'S PERMIT TO CARRY WEAPONS WHILE THE PROTECTION MEASURES ARE IN FORCE AND ORDER THE CONFISCATION OF THE WEAPONS IN HIS OR HER POSSESSION;  
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;  
D.O. N° 137,  
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.  
9.	PROVISIONALLY SUSPEND FROM THE AGGRESSOR THE PERSONAL CARE, CUSTODY, UPBRINGING AND EDUCATION OF HIS OR HER MINOR CHILDREN AND HIS OR HER RIGHT TO VISIT THEM IN CASE OF AGGRESSION;  
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;  
D.O. N° 137,  
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.  
10.	Prohibit the aggressor's access to the permanent or temporary domicile of the victim and to his or her place of work or study;  
11.	ESTABLISH A PROVISIONAL CHILD SUPPORT PAYMENT; ONCE FIXED, IT SHALL BE ENFORCED EX OFFICIO;  
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;  
D.O. N° 137,  
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.  
12.	Grant the exclusive use for a determined period of the household furnishings of the dwelling to the victim. Special safeguards must be provided for the dwelling and furnishings covered by the family patrimony regime;  
13.	Issue a Judicial Order of protection and police assistance, directed to the Public Security authority of his or her neighborhood. The victim shall carry a copy of this order so that he or she may go to the nearest authority in case of threat of aggression outside his or her domicile; and,
14.	Any other measure provided for in the current family legal system. Failure to attend a judicial proceeding

## CHAPTER III Procedure Articles 10 to 36

FIRST SECTION Police intervention Articles 10 to 36 Notice to the National Civil Police

### ARTÍCULO 10

Whenever the National Civil Police has knowledge or receives notice that a person is a victim of intrafamily violence, it must take the necessary measures to prevent said person from being mistreated and must carry out the following actions:
1.	IF THE VICTIM STATES THAT HE OR SHE HAS SUFFERED BLOWS OR WOUNDS EVEN IF THEY ARE NOT VISIBLE, EMOTIONAL DAMAGE OR WHEN HE OR SHE IS FOUND UNCONSCIOUS, OR IN ANY CASE REQUIRING MEDICAL ATTENTION, IT SHALL ASSIST HIM OR HER AND MAKE THE NECESSARY ARRANGEMENTS FOR HIM OR HER TO RECEIVE THE MEDICAL TREATMENT NEEDED AND SHALL PROVIDE TRANSPORTATION TO A CARE CENTER OR MEDICAL SERVICE, WHERE HE OR SHE CAN BE ATTENDED;  
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002;  
D.O. NO 137,  
T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.  
2.	If the victim or family members express concern for his or her personal safety, that of his or her children, or any other family member, it shall make the necessary arrangements to take them to an appropriate place, where they can be attended;  
3.	Advise the victim of intrafamily violence about the importance of preserving evidence;  
4.	IT SHALL PROVIDE THE VICTIM WITH INFORMATION ABOUT THE RIGHTS CONFERRED BY THIS LAW AND OTHER INFORMATION RELATED TO THE MATTER, AND ABOUT GOVERNMENTAL OR PRIVATE SERVICES AVAILABLE TO VICTIMS OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE;  
D.L. N° 591, 18 DE DICIEMBRE DE 2013;  
D.O. N° 11,  
T. 402, 20 DE ENERO DE 2014.  

5.	IT SHALL DETAIN THE ALLEGED AGGRESSOR IF THE EXISTENCE AND PARTICIPATION OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE CONDUCT IS VERIFIED, WHEN THE ACTS CONSTITUTE OTHER CRIMES THAT ARE MANIFESTATIONS DERIVED FROM IT AND WHEN IT INVOLVES THE CRIME OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE CONTEMPLATED IN ART. 200 OF THE PENAL CODE. IN ANY CASE IT SHALL TAKE THE PERTINENT MEASURES TO PREVENT THE AGGRESSOR FROM CONTINUING WITH VIOLENT ACTS AND SHALL PROTECT THE VICTIM; and,

D.O. N° 137,

IN ITS DUTY TO ASSIST VICTIMS, AND IN THOSE CASES IN WHICH INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE DOES NOT YET CONSTITUTE A CRIME; EVEN WHEN THE VICTIM DOES NOT REQUEST IT; THE NATIONAL CIVIL POLICE MAY ISSUE THE TEMPORARY SPECIAL PROTECTION MEASURE, WHICH SHALL CONSIST OF ORDERING THE ALLEGED AGGRESSOR TO LEAVE THE SCENE OF THE EVENTS, FOR A MAXIMUM PERIOD OF FORTY-EIGHT HOURS. DISOBEDIENCE TO THIS ORDER SHALL ENTAIL CRIMINAL LIABILITY.

TO CARRY THEM OUT HE OR SHE SHALL BE ASSISTED BY THE INSTITUTE OF LEGAL MEDICINE OR ANY GOVERNMENTAL, NON-GOVERNMENTAL ORGANIZATION OR THE MULTIDISCIPLINARY TEAM ASSIGNED TO THE FAMILY COURT OR THE ORGANIZATIONS INDICATED IN THIS ARTICLE. AND WHEN HE OR SHE CONSIDERS IT NECESSARY, HE OR SHE MAY ALSO ORDER THE PSYCHOSOCIAL EXPERT ASSESSMENT OF THE AGGRESSOR AND OF THE BOYS AND GIRLS.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;
D.O. N° 137,
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.

Expert Opinion
Article cited in: 2 rulings ARTICLE 25
THE EXPERT OPINION SHALL BE ISSUED IN WRITING AND SHALL BE SUBMITTED NO LATER THAN SEVENTY-TWO HOURS AFTER BEING REQUESTED. IN CASE THE OPINION IS REQUIRED URGENTLY, IT MAY BE RENDERED VERBALLY AND SHALL BE RECORDED IN THE MINUTES.
IF FROM THE OPINION RECEIVED IT RESULTS THAT THE ACT OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE CONSTITUTES A CRIME, THE FAMILY OR PEACE JUDGE SHALL CONTINUE THE PROCEDURE FOR THE SOLE PURPOSE OF ENFORCING THE IMPOSED MEASURES AND SHALL CERTIFY THE RELEVANT MATTERS TO THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC TO INITIATE THE CORRESPONDING PROCEEDINGS.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;
D.O. N° 137,
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.

Scheduling and Summons for Hearing ARTICLE 26
IMMEDIATELY UPON RECEIPT OF THE EXPERT OPINION AND IF THE ACT DOES NOT CONSTITUTE A CRIME, THE JUDGE SHALL SUMMON THE VICTIM AND THE ACCUSED TO A PRELIMINARY HEARING WITHIN A PERIOD OF FIVE WORKING DAYS, IN ORDER TO HEAR THE FACTS, IN WHICH THEY MAY OR MAY NOT BE ACCOMPANIED BY AN ATTORNEY OR AN ASSISTANT ATTORNEY OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;
D.O. N° 137,
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
Preliminary Hearing
Article cited in: 2 rulings ARTICLE 27
The victim and the accused shall personally attend the scheduled hearing and may both be accompanied by a lawyer and minutes shall be taken.
The judge shall personally preside over said hearing and shall give the victim an opportunity under equal conditions to reaffirm, expand or modify the complaint and the accused to make his or her own assessments, admit to the facts or contradict them.
AFTER HEARING THEM, HE OR SHE SHALL PROMOTE A DIALOGUE WITH THE ATTENDEES ABOUT THE HARMFUL EFFECTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND ITS REPERCUSSIONS ON THE FAMILY AND SHALL PROPOSE MECHANISMS TO AVOID THE REPETITION OF THE ACTS CONSTITUTING THE SAME, REGARDING WHICH CONCILIATION SHALL NOT PROCEED.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;
D.O. N° 137,
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
He or she must also make the accused aware of the criminal sanctions that may be incurred if the violent action is repeated and of the measures that this law provides to sanction domestic violence.
Resolution
Article cited in: 2 rulings ARTICLE 28
In the same hearing the judge, based on what is stated by those appearing, provided that the facts do not require proof and in consideration of commitments assumed by the accused and accepted by the victim, shall resolve:
1. To establish the facts constituting domestic violence as reported;
2. TO ATTRIBUTE THE VIOLENCE TO WHOEVER OR WHOMEVER MAY HAVE GENERATED IT;
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;
D.O. N° 137,
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
3. To impose on the aggressor compliance with the commitment acquired by him or her in the hearing;
4. To decree the preventive, precautionary or protective measures that are necessary, if they have not been previously agreed upon.
5. TO IMPOSE ON THE AGGRESSOR THE OBLIGATION TO PAY THE VICTIM FOR THE EMERGENT DAMAGE FROM THE VIOLENT CONDUCT OR BEHAVIOR, SUCH AS THE CASES OF

HEALTH SERVICES, PRICE OF MEDICINES, VALUE OF GOODS AND OTHER EXPENSES DERIVED FROM THE VIOLENCE EXERCISED;
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;
D.O. N° 137,
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
6. TO IMPOSE ON THE AGGRESSOR PSYCHOSOCIAL, PSYCHIATRIC OR SPECIALIZED SELF-HELP GROUP TREATMENT FOR DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, THROUGH ATTENDANCE AT THERAPIES ON DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, USING THE VARIOUS PROGRAMS DEVELOPED BY FAMILY PROTECTION INSTITUTIONS. THIS MEASURE MAY ALSO BE APPLIED FROM THE BEGINNING OF THE PROCEDURE AND IN ANY CASE PSYCHOSOCIAL FOLLOW-UP SHALL BE PROVIDED.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;
D.O. N° 137,
T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.

In the same resolution the aggressor shall be warned of the criminal sanctions that will be incurred in case of non-compliance or repetition of acts of domestic violence.
Scheduling of the Public Hearing
Article cited in: 2 rulings ARTICLE 29
IF THE ACCUSED DOES NOT ADMIT TO THE FACTS THAT REQUIRE PROOF, HE OR SHE SHALL SCHEDULE A HEARING TO RECEIVE IT, WITHIN A PERIOD NOT TO EXCEED TEN WORKING DAYS AFTER THE PRELIMINARY HEARING, WITHIN WHICH THE INSPECTION AND PSYCHOSOCIAL INVESTIGATION OR ANY OTHER PROCEEDING SHALL BE CONDUCTED.

Public Hearing ARTICLE 30
On the scheduled day, the parties shall be received in a public hearing and orally, the statements of the witnesses and other evidence presented by the parties and those that the judge has ordered.
Likewise, the studies of the social workers and opinions of the experts shall be evaluated. The parties and their lawyers may directly cross-examine the witnesses and experts.
Judgment ARTICLE 31
Once the evidence offered has been produced, the judge shall in the same hearing render his or her ruling and order the measures provided for in this law or absolve the accused of responsibility.
Remedies ARTICLE 32
The resolutions pronounced by the judge in which preventive precautionary or protective measures are imposed on the aggressor, or the accused is absolved shall be appealable before the Family Chambers, even if they are pronounced by a peace judge.

THE APPELLATE COURT SHALL RESOLVE THE APPEAL WITH ONLY THE REVIEW OF THE PROCEEDINGS, WITHIN EIGHT WORKING DAYS AFTER HAVING RECEIVED IT; THIS RESOLUTION SHALL NOT ADMIT AN APPEAL FOR CASSATION.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;
D.O. N° 137,

The appeal may be filed orally or in writing at the time of notification or within the following three
working days.
Control of the Execution of the Judgment
Article cited in: 8 rulings ARTICLE 33
DURING THE COURSE OF THE PROCEEDINGS AND AFTER THE SAME, THE JUDGE SHALL CONTROL FOR THE TIME HE OR SHE DEEMS APPROPRIATE THE RESULT OF THE MEASURES AND DECISIONS ADOPTED AND IMPOSED IN THE JUDGMENT THROUGH THE MULTIDISCIPLINARY TEAM ASSIGNED TO THE FAMILY COURT, WHO SHALL PROVIDE REPORTS WITH THE FREQUENCY THAT THE JUDGE INDICATES.
D.L. NW 403, AUGUST 12, 2004;

Non-compliance with the Judgment
Article cited in: one ruling ARTICLE 34
WHENEVER IT IS POSSIBLE TO VERIFY NON-COMPLIANCE WITH THE PREVENTIVE, PRECAUTIONARY OR PROTECTIVE MEASURES IMPOSED BY THE JUDGE, AT ANY STAGE OF THE PROCEEDINGS, AS WELL AS THE AGREED COMMITMENTS, HE OR SHE SHALL ISSUE AN OFFICIAL LETTER TO THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC, WITH CERTIFICATION OF THE PERTINENT PASSAGES, SO THAT IT MAY FILE THE REQUEST FOR THE CRIME OF DISOBEDIENCE. WITHOUT PREJUDICE TO THE VICTIM BEING ABLE TO REPORT THE FACTS PERSONALLY TO THE SAME INSTITUTION OR REQUEST THE ASSISTANCE OF THE NATIONAL CIVIL POLICE, AS THE CASE MAY BE.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002;

Mandatory Appearance of Victims and Accused
Article cited in: 2 rulings ARTICLE 35
IN THE JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS PROVIDED FOR IN THIS SECTION, THE APPEARANCE OF VICTIMS AND ACCUSED AT THE HEARINGS TO WHICH THEY ARE SUMMONED SHALL BE MANDATORY, EXCEPT FOR JUST CAUSE.
THE NON-APPEARANCE OF THE VICTIMS OR THE ACCUSED SHALL CAUSE THEM TO INCUR A FINE THAT THE JUDGE SHALL IMPOSE IN SAID HEARING WITHOUT PREJUDICE TO THE POWER HE OR SHE HAS TO COMPEL THEM TO APPEAR BY COERCION.
THE FINES SHALL BE ENFORCED OR RENDERED WITHOUT EFFECT IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE PROVISIONS OF ARTICLE 8 OF THIS LAW.

## CHAPTER IV

General Provisions and Effectiveness Articles 37 to 43 Confidentiality of Proceedings and Actions

### ARTICLE 37

The proceedings and actions conducted in application of this Law shall be confidential except for the parties, attorneys, solicitors, prosecutors and specialized personnel who participate in them.  
Legal Assistance ARTICLE 38
IN THE PROCEEDINGS AND ACTIONS CONDUCTED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THIS LAW, THE PARTIES MAY OR MAY NOT BE ASSISTED BY AN ATTORNEY.  
IF THE PARTIES OR ONE OF THEM LACK ECONOMIC RESOURCES AND REQUEST LEGAL ASSISTANCE FROM THE JUDGE, THE STATE THROUGH THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC SHALL PROVIDE THEM WITH A SPECIFIC SOLICITOR TO ASSIST THEM.  
D.L. NW 892, 27 JUNE 2002;  
D.O. N° 137,  
T. 356, 24 JULY 2002.  
Power of the judge ARTICLE 39
Judges may make use of public security to enforce their resolutions or orders. Request for Assistance from Organizations or Entities

### ARTICLE 40

JUDGES MAY REQUEST COLLABORATION FROM ALL PUBLIC OR PRIVATE ENTITIES AND ORGANIZATIONS DEDICATED TO THE PROTECTION OF CHILDREN, ADOLESCENTS, WOMEN, ELDERLY PERSONS AND PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES, IN ORDER TO PROVIDE FREE ASSISTANCE AND ACCOMPANIMENT TO VICTIMS AFFECTED BY REPORTED ACTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, AND TO ASSIST IN THE APPLICATION OF PROTECTION MEASURES IMPOSED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THIS LAW, THE FAMILY CODE, AND THE CODE FOR CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS.  
JUDICIAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICIALS MAY ALLOW, WITH THE EXPRESS CONSENT OF THE VICTIM, PSYCHOLOGICAL AND EMOTIONAL ACCOMPANIMENT WHEN REPORTING THE ACT AND IN THE RESPECTIVE HEARINGS, WHETHER BY A NATURAL PERSON OR BY CIVIL SOCIETY ORGANIZATIONS WORKING FOR THE PREVENTION AND CARE OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, DURING THE JUDICIAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE PROCESS, WHICH SHALL BE RECORDED IN THE MINUTES. THE VICTIM MAY TERMINATE SUCH ACCOMPANIMENT AND REPLACE IT WITH ANOTHER PERSON OR INSTITUTION, IN THE SAME MANNER.

D.O. N° 137,  
T. 356, 24 JULY 2002.  

THE VICTIM SHALL ALSO HAVE THE RIGHT TO BE TECHNICALLY ASSISTED BY THE MULTIDISCIPLINARY TEAM ASSIGNED TO THE FAMILY COURT, FOR THE PERIOD ESTABLISHED BY IT.  
D.L. NW 403, 12 AUGUST 2004;  
D.O. N° 178,

Suspension of Criminal Proceedings ARTICLE 41
If in the course of criminal proceedings the judge determines that the acts are subject to the application of this law, he or she shall suspend the proceedings and initiate the procedure provided in Section Three of Chapter III of this law, if he or she is competent. If not competent, he or she shall refer the proceedings to the competent judge. In any case, the actions taken by the investigating judge shall be valid.  
MEASURES IN CRIMINAL MATTERS ARTICLE 42
WHEN DEALING WITH ACTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE SUBJECT TO CRIMINAL JURISDICTION AND PERSONS SUBJECT TO THIS LAW, THE JUDGE DURING THE PROCEEDINGS SHALL APPLY THE RELEVANT PREVENTIVE, PRECAUTIONARY AND PROTECTION MEASURES REGULATED IN THIS LEGISLATION.  
THESE MAY BE MAINTAINED OR DECREED WHEN ISSUING THE CORRESPONDING SENTENCE AND TO ENSURE THEIR EFFECTIVE COMPLIANCE, THE COURTS OF BOTH JURISDICTIONS THAT HAVE INTERVENED IN THESE CASES SHALL EXCHANGE DIRECT INFORMATION ON THE STATUS OF THE PROCEEDINGS IN ORDER TO BE CONSIDERED IN THEIR JUDICIAL DECISION.  
SUCH DECISION MAY BE FOLLOWED UP WITH THE SUPPORT OF THE PSYCHOSOCIAL CARE CENTERS OF THE JUDICIAL BRANCH, THE MULTIDISCIPLINARY TEAMS ASSIGNED TO THE FAMILY COURTS AND THE SPECIALIZED COURTS FOR A LIFE FREE OF VIOLENCE FOR WOMEN IN CASES INVOLVING CRIMES OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN, AND THOSE PUBLIC AND PRIVATE INSTITUTIONS; AS WELL AS NON-GOVERNMENTAL ORGANIZATIONS THAT PROVIDE CARE TO PERSONS IN DOMESTIC VIOLENCE.
