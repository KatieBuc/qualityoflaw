# LAW ON PROTECTION FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE OF REPUBLIKA SRPSKA

LAW ON PROTECTION FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE OF REPUBLIKA SRPSKA
("Official Gazette of Republika Srpska", Nos. 102/2012, 108/2013, 82/2015 and 84/2019) I - BASIC PROVISIONS

## Article 1

This law regulates protection from violence in the family or family community (hereinafter: domestic violence), persons who are considered members of the family or family community within the meaning of this law, subjects of protection and the procedure for protection of victims of domestic violence, establishment of the Council for Combating Domestic Violence, measures for protection of victims of domestic violence and other issues of importance for protection from domestic violence.

## Article 2

The basic objective of this law is the protection of victims of domestic violence by preventing and combating domestic violence, which violates fundamental human rights and freedoms guaranteed by the Constitution and laws.

## Article 3

Protection from domestic violence is achieved through the application of this law and other regulations governing the realization of the rights of victims of domestic violence.

## Article 4

(1)	For the purpose of protection from domestic violence, the National Assembly of Republika Srpska adopts the Strategy for Combating Domestic Violence of Republika Srpska (hereinafter: the Strategy), upon proposal of the Government of Republika Srpska (hereinafter: the Government).  

(2)	Annual action plans are adopted by the Government upon proposal of the Ministry of Family, Youth and Sports (hereinafter: the Ministry).  
(3)	The Strategy is adopted for a period of at least five years.

## Article 5

Grammatical expressions used in this law to denote the masculine or feminine gender include both sexes.  

II	- ACTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND SUBJECTS OF PROTECTION

## Article 6

(1)	Domestic violence, within the meaning of this law, exists if there is a basis for suspicion that a member of the family or family community has committed acts of physical, sexual, psychological and/or economic

violence, as well as threats that cause fear of physical, sexual, psychological and/or economic harm to another member of the family or family community.  
(2)	Acts of domestic violence, within the meaning of paragraph 1 of this Article, are:
a)	use of force against the physical or psychological integrity of a member of the family or family community,

b)	conduct that may cause or create a danger of causing physical or psychological pain or suffering to a member of the family or family community,

v) causing fear or personal endangerment or violation of the dignity of a member of the family or family community by blackmail or other coercion,

g) verbal attack, insult, swearing, calling derogatory names, and other forms of gross harassment of a member of the family or family community,
d)	sexual violence,
đ) preventing access to health care and nursing,
e)	stalking and all other similar forms of harassment of a member of the family or family community,

ž) intentional damage or destruction of property of a member of the family or family community, joint property, or property in possession,

z)	denial or deprivation of the right to economic independence by prohibition of work or keeping a member of the family or family community in a relationship of dependence or subordination,
i)	raising children in a manner of degrading treatment,
j)	taking away children or eviction from the apartment of a member of the family or family community,

k)	exhaustion by work, starvation, deprivation of sleep or necessary rest of a member of the family or family community,
l)	failure to comply with the decision of the competent authority establishing personal contact of children with parents,
lj) withholding a travel document or any other document of a member of the family or family community,
m)	forced isolation or restriction of freedom of movement of a member of the family or family community and
n)	any other act that constitutes violence in the family or family community.  
(3)	Threats of acts referred to in paragraph 2 of this Article are also considered acts of domestic violence.

## Article 7

A member of the family or family community, within the meaning of this law, is considered to be:
a)	spouses or former spouses and their children and children of each of them,

b)	extramarital partners or former extramarital partners, their children or children of each of them,

v) relatives by affinity up to the second degree regardless of the fact that the marital union has ceased,

g) parents of current and former marital and extramarital partners,
d)	relatives from full adoption in the direct line without limitation, and in the collateral line up to
the fourth degree, as well as relatives from incomplete adoption,
đ) persons bound by a guardianship relationship,
e)	persons who live or have lived in the same family household, regardless of kinship,

ž) persons who have a common child or the child has been conceived, although they have never lived in the same family household,

z) persons who have been or are still in an emotional or intimate relationship with each other, regardless of whether the perpetrator shares or has shared a household with the victim.

## Article 8

(1)	A victim of domestic violence has the right to psychosocial assistance and social and medical protection in accordance with laws in the field of health and social protection.  
(2)	Special assistance and protection in accordance with this law is enjoyed by a victim who is:
a)	a child,
b)	an elderly person,
v) a person with a disability and
g) a person under guardianship.  

(3)	A child is a victim even if it was present during the commission of violence against another family member, although acts of violence were not undertaken against it.

## Article 9

(1)	Protection, assistance and support to victims of domestic violence must be provided by members of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (hereinafter: the police), the prosecutor's office, social work centers, i.e. social protection services, health and educational institutions and the competent court (hereinafter: subjects of protection).  

(2)	Subjects of protection are obliged to act in accordance with the provisions of this law and to provide protection, support and assistance to victims of domestic violence and to prevent repetition of violence regardless of whether criminal or misdemeanor proceedings have been initiated against the perpetrator.

## Article 10

(1)	Victims of domestic violence have the right to access all subjects of protection and are exempt from all costs of the procedure.  

(2)	Subjects of protection are obliged at the first contact to inform the victim, in a manner understandable to them, of all rights they have in accordance with this and other regulations and of institutions, bodies, legal entities and organizations that provide assistance, support and protection.  

(3)	In procedures for exercising their rights and protection, victims have the right to free legal aid in accordance with regulations governing the field of free legal aid.  
III	- PROCEDURE FOR REALIZING PROTECTION OF VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## Article 11

(1)	Subjects of protection are obliged without delay to ensure urgent resolution of cases of domestic violence, taking into account that the interest and welfare of the victim are a priority in those procedures, especially if the victim is a child, an elderly person, a person with a disability and a person under guardianship.  

(2)	Subjects of protection in the process of protection, assistance and support to victims of domestic violence cooperate in the best interest of the victim.  

(3)	In the territory of the local self-government unit, a protocol on conduct is concluded and a group for coordination and cooperation is formed from representatives of all institutions, bodies and organizations that provide protection, assistance and support to victims of domestic violence.  

(4)	Based on annual reports, the Ministry monitors and analyzes the implementation of the protocol referred to in paragraph 3 of this Article.  

(5)	The group for coordination and cooperation referred to in paragraph 3 of this Article shall be formed within 30 days from the date of entry into force of this law.

## Article 12

(1) Family members, subjects of protection, as well as employees in educational, social and health institutions, as well as any other citizen, are obliged immediately upon learning that domestic violence has been committed or that there is a basis for suspicion that domestic violence has been committed, to report it to the police.

(2) After receiving a report that domestic violence has been committed, the competent police officer is obliged to immediately inform the social welfare centre, which will immediately directly provide social protection and psychosocial assistance to the victim, take other measures within its competence, and prepare an official report thereof.

(3) The health institution shall enable the victim a free medical examination in order to determine the existence of injuries to physical or psychological integrity.

(4) The competent police officer immediately informs the competent public prosecutor about the committed domestic violence and, together with the report, submits the collected evidence and risk assessment.

(5) The competent public prosecutor, after completion of the investigation, in written form, informs the competent police officer about the filing of an indictment against the suspected person or about the suspension of the investigation, as well as about the reasons for the suspension of the investigation.

(6) The competent police officer submits the notifications and risk assessment, in addition to the competent public prosecutor, also to the social welfare centre.
Article 12a
(1) A risk assessment is carried out for every report of domestic violence.
(2) The competent police officer carries out the risk assessment based on collected information about:

a) whether the perpetrator has previously or immediately before the risk assessment committed domestic violence or some other form of violence and whether he is ready to repeat it,

b) whether he has threatened murder or suicide,
c) whether he possesses weapons,
d) whether he is mentally ill or abuses psychoactive substances,

e) whether there is a conflict over custody of the child or over the manner of maintaining personal contact between the child and the parent who is the perpetrator,
f) whether an urgent measure or protective measure against domestic violence has been imposed on the perpetrator,
g) whether the victim experiences fear and how she assesses the risk of repeated violence and h) other facts and circumstances relevant to the risk assessment.
(3) The Minister of Internal Affairs adopts the Rulebook on the procedure and manner of conducting risk assessment.
1. Urgent protective measures

## Article 13

(1) In order to eliminate immediate danger to physical and psychological integrity, to prevent the repetition of violence and to guarantee the safety of the victim, urgent protective measures may be imposed on the perpetrator of domestic violence before the initiation of proceedings or during the proceedings.

(2) Urgent protective measures are imposed by the competent court in misdemeanour proceedings

(3) The proposal for the imposition of an urgent protective measure is submitted by the competent police officer, the social welfare centre or the victim of domestic violence or family community, and they are imposed no later than within 24 hours from the receipt of the proposal, that is, from bringing the perpetrator of domestic violence before the court.
(4) Urgent protective measures are:
a) removal of the perpetrator of violence from the apartment, house or other residential space and/or
b) prohibition of approaching and contacting by the perpetrator of violence with the victim of domestic violence.

## Article 14

(1) Urgent protective measures are imposed for a duration that cannot be longer than 30 days.

(2) Urgent protective measures from paragraph 1 of this Article are imposed by the judge of the misdemeanour department of the competent basic court by decision, against which an appeal may be filed within three days from the day of imposition.

(3) An appeal against the court's decision on the imposed urgent protective measure does not postpone its execution.

(4) The appeal is decided upon by the council of the competent basic court that issued the decision within 48 hours from the receipt of the appeal.

(5) When leaving the apartment, house or other space for residence, the perpetrator of domestic violence has the right to take his personal belongings necessary for everyday life and the obligation to hand over the keys to the apartment, house or other space for residence.

(6) The court that imposed it submits the decision on its imposition to the competent organizational unit of the Ministry of Internal Affairs for implementation and examines its implementation and justification of duration in the period for which it was imposed.

(7) The manner of implementing urgent protective measures is regulated by the rulebook from Article 26 paragraph 3 of this law.
2. Manner of providing care for victims of domestic violence

## Article 15

(1) The social welfare centre, that is, the social protection service with the assistance of the police may, with the prior consent of the victim of domestic violence, in order to ensure physical protection and realization of the rights and interests of the victim, issue a decision on temporary accommodation of the victim in a safe house.

(2) The victim of domestic violence, due to the violence suffered, fear and distress, in order to ensure physical protection and realization of their rights and interests, as well as prevention of repeated violence, has the right to submit to the competent social welfare centre, that is, the social protection service a request for the use of a special support measure - temporary accommodation in a safe house.

(3) A safe house represents a special support measure by which safe accommodation and assistance is provided to victims of domestic violence, which may be realized by a legal entity.

(4) In order to protect and secure the victim, the police will escort the victim to the house, apartment or other residential space so that she may take her own and personal belongings of other persons who left that space with her, which are necessary to meet everyday needs.

(5) The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article may be determined for a duration that is necessary to achieve the goal of the measure, provided that it cannot last longer than six months.

(6) In justified cases, the duration of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article may be extended for another six months, that is, until the completion of the proceedings and execution of the decision by which a protective measure is imposed on the perpetrator of violence.

## Article 16

(1) The safe house from Article 15 of this law must meet the established standards regarding space, equipment and staff.

(2) The fulfilment of the standards from paragraph 1 of this Article is determined by a commission, which is appointed by the minister of family, youth and sports (hereinafter: the minister).

(3) Based on the proposal of the commission, the minister issues a decision on the fulfilment of the standards from paragraph 1 of this Article.

(4) The decision from paragraph 3 of this Article is subject to revision after the expiry of a period of two years from the day of receipt of the decision.
(5) The Ministry ex officio takes care of the expiry of the period from paragraph 4 of this Article.

(6) The decision from paragraph 3 of this Article is final and an appeal is not allowed against it, but an administrative dispute may be initiated before the competent court.
(7) The minister adopts a rulebook on standards for the realization of a safe house.

## Article 17

(1) Based on the decision from Article 16 paragraph 3 of this law, safe houses are entered in the Register of Safe Houses, which is maintained by the Ministry.
(2) The minister adopts a rulebook on the content and manner of maintaining the Register of Safe Houses.

## Article 18

Funds for temporary care and accommodation of victims of domestic violence in safe houses are provided from the budget of Republika Srpska (hereinafter: the budget of the Republic) in the amount of 70%, and from the budget of local self-government units in the amount of 30% of the determined price of accommodation of the victim.

## Article 19

(1) Payment of costs for temporary accommodation of victims of domestic violence in a safe house which are provided from the budget of the Republic shall be made through the Ministry, and payment of costs for accommodation of victims of domestic violence in a safe house which are provided from the budget of local self-government units shall be made in accordance with the acts of local self-government units.

(2) To exercise the right to payment of costs for accommodation of victims of domestic violence which are provided from the budget of the Republic, the Ministry shall announce a public call.
(3) The public call shall determine:
a) the deadline for submitting applications,
b) the period to which the public call relates,
v) documentation to be submitted with the application for the public call,

g) financial report for the previous year,
d) work plan,
đ) financial plan for the period to which the public call relates and
e) other issues relevant to the implementation of the public call.

(4) Safe houses registered in the Register referred to in Article 17, paragraph 1 of this law shall compete under equal conditions for exercising the right to payment of costs for accommodation of victims of domestic violence which are provided from the budget of the Republic.
(5) The Minister shall adopt a rulebook on the manner of allocation of funds to safe houses.
(6) The Minister shall adopt a decision on the price of accommodation of victims of domestic violence in a safe house.
(7) The decision referred to in paragraph 6 of this Article shall be published in the "Official Gazette of Republika Srpska".

## Article 20

(1) A legal entity which, after the conducted public call, has been approved for payment of costs for accommodation of victims of domestic violence in a safe house from the budget of the Republic shall conclude a contract with the Ministry.
(2) The contract shall regulate mutual rights and obligations between the Ministry and the legal entity referred to in paragraph
1 of this Article.
(3) The Ministry shall supervise the purposeful use of funds.

(4) A legal entity which has been approved for accommodation of a victim of violence in a safe house shall be obliged, at the request of the Ministry, to submit all data relating to the number of victims and documentation on the manner of expenditure of funds from the budget of the Republic, within 30 days.

## Article 21

(1) The social welfare centre shall form an expert team composed of representatives of that institution, bodies and services of the local self-government unit, police, non-governmental organizations and experts dealing with family issues and domestic violence in order to determine a plan of assistance to the victim and coordinate activities in the process of assisting the victim, in accordance with their needs and choice.

(2) The plan of assistance to the victim shall specifically contain measures that need to be taken in accordance with the law governing social, health and child protection.

(3) If the victim is a child, the plan of assistance to the victim shall also contain measures for the protection of the child in accordance with the law governing family relations and regulations governing the protection of children's rights.

Article 21a

(1) The victim may choose, before or during the proceedings, a person of trust who will be present during one or more proceedings or actions related to protection from domestic violence.
(2) A person of trust may be any adult person except the perpetrator of violence.

(3) Competent authorities are obliged to enable the presence of a person of trust in all proceedings and actions in which the victim is involved, in accordance with regulations within their competence.
(4) A victim of violence has the right to a legal representative in the proceedings.

## Article 22

Information on the basis of which the victim or a family member of the victim can be identified shall not be publicly disclosed about the victim or perpetrator of domestic violence, unless an adult victim has expressly consented thereto.
IV - PROTECTIVE MEASURES

## Article 23

(1) Measures for the protection of victims from domestic violence are protective measures.

(2) The purpose of protective measures is to ensure necessary protection and safety of the victim, and to eliminate conditions or circumstances that may influence or may favour or encourage the perpetrator to commit acts of domestic violence in the future.
(3) Protective measures are:
a) removal from the apartment, house or some other residential space,

b) prohibition of approaching the victim of domestic violence or the family community (hereinafter: prohibition of approaching the victim),

v) prohibition of harassing or stalking the victim of domestic violence or the family community (hereinafter: prohibition of harassing or stalking the victim),
g) mandatory psychosocial treatment and
d) mandatory treatment for addiction.

(4) Protective measures shall be imposed by the competent court in misdemeanour proceedings, upon the proposal of the competent police officer, social welfare centre or victim of domestic violence.

(5) The court is obliged to impose protective measures within 30 days from the date of submission of the Proposal for imposition of a protective measure.
Article 23a

Protective measures referred to in Article 23, paragraph 3 of this law shall be imposed independently and are subject to direct enforcement by the authorities competent for their implementation, in accordance with the provisions of this law.

(deleted)

## Article 24

(1) The protective measure - removal from the apartment, house or some other residential space shall be imposed on the perpetrator of violence if they have committed violence against a family member with whom they live in an apartment, house or some other residential space if the competent court assesses that there is a danger that without the implementation of this measure the perpetrator of domestic violence could commit violence again.

(2) A person to whom the measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article has been imposed is obliged, without delay, to leave the apartment, house or some other residential space in the presence of a police officer.

(3) The measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined for a duration that cannot be shorter than 30 days nor longer than six months.

## Article 25

(1) The protective measure - prohibition of approaching the victim shall be imposed on the perpetrator of violence if there is a danger that they could commit violence again or if the presence of the perpetrator of violence in the family near the victim would create a high degree of mental suffering for the victim that prevents normal mental activities of the victim.

(2) In the decision by which the court imposes the protective measure - prohibition of approaching the victim, the court shall determine places or areas and a distance of at least 200 metres at which the perpetrator of domestic violence must not approach the victim.

(3) The measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined for a duration that cannot be shorter than 30 days nor longer than one year.

## Article 26

(1) The protective measure - prohibition of harassing or stalking the victim shall be imposed on the perpetrator of violence who has committed violence by harassing or stalking, and there is a danger that they could repeat the harassment or stalking of the victim.

(2) The measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined for a duration that cannot be shorter than 30 days nor longer than one year.

(3) The Minister of Internal Affairs shall adopt a rulebook on the manner of implementation of urgent and protective measures that are within the competence of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, which are prescribed in Articles 13, 24, 25 and 26 of this law.

## Article 27

(1) The protective measure - mandatory psychosocial treatment shall be imposed on the perpetrator of violence in order to eliminate the causes of violent behaviour and re-education, that is, with the aim of reducing and eliminating the danger of repetition of the committed violence.

(2) The measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article may last until the cessation of the reason for which it was determined, but cannot last longer than one year.

(3) The Minister of Health and Social Welfare shall adopt a rulebook on the manner of implementation of the protective measure - mandatory psychosocial treatment.

## Article 28

(1) The protective measure - mandatory treatment for addiction shall be imposed on the perpetrator of violence who has committed violence under the influence of addiction to alcohol, narcotic drugs and other psychotropic substances, and there is a danger that due to that addiction they will commit violence again.

(2) The measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be determined for the duration necessary for treatment, based on the opinion of specialized experts responsible for treatment, but cannot last longer than one year.

(3) The Minister of Health and Social Welfare shall adopt a rulebook on the manner of implementation of the protective measure - mandatory treatment for addiction.

## Article 29

(1) The competent court may impose one or more protective measures on the perpetrator of violence when the conditions for their imposition prescribed by this law exist.
(2) The competent court may request from the social welfare centre:
a) to provide assistance in obtaining necessary evidence and
b) to submit its opinion on the appropriateness of the requested protective measure.

## Article 30

(1) An appeal against a decision determining a protective measure may be filed with the competent court within three days from the day of service of the decision.
(2) The appeal shall be decided by the competent district court within three days from the day of receipt of the appeal.
(3) The appeal shall not postpone the execution of the decision determining a protective measure.

## Article 31

(1) The perpetrator of domestic violence is obliged to act in accordance with the imposed protective measure.

(2) A protection entity that, in the scope of its work, learns that the perpetrator of domestic violence is not acting in accordance with the imposed protective measure is obliged to inform the competent court and the social welfare centre thereof.

## Article 32

(1) The competent court is obliged to deliver the decision on the imposed protective measure to the body or institution competent for carrying out the execution within three days from the day of adoption of the decision.

(2) The decision on the imposed protective measure from Articles 24, 25 and 26 of this Law shall be delivered to the police for execution.

(3) The decision on the imposed protective measure from Articles 27 and 28 of this Law shall be delivered to the social welfare centre and the body, that is, institution that executes the protective measure in accordance with the law governing the treatment and rehabilitation of persons dependent on alcohol, narcotic drugs and other psychotropic substances.

## Article 33

(1) When imposing protective measures under this Law, account shall be taken of the purpose, severity, duration of the imposed measure and its effectiveness.

(2) The bodies competent for implementing protective measures are obliged to monitor their execution and report thereon to the court, propose termination, extension or replacement with another measure.

(3) The body competent for implementing a protective measure is obliged to submit a report to the court on the execution of protective measures no later than within six months from the day of imposition of the protective measure, and if necessary, earlier.
V - RECORDS AND TRAINING

## Article 34

(1) Protection entities and other bodies authorised to act under this Law are obliged to keep records of actions taken under this Law and data on the number of initiated and completed proceedings and other measures taken, and to submit reports thereon to the Ministry.
(2) The Ministry collects, processes and records data on domestic violence.
(3) The Minister shall adopt a rulebook on the content of records and reports on domestic violence from paragraph 1 of this
Article.

## Article 35

The Centre for Education of Judges and Prosecutors of Republika Srpska shall take care of the acquisition of special knowledge and continuous professional training and development of judges and prosecutors in the field of domestic violence.
VI - COUNCIL FOR COMBATING DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## Article 36

For the purpose of monitoring and assessing the implementation of policies and measures for preventing and combating domestic violence, and improving coordinated and effective action in the field of domestic violence, the Government shall establish a Council for Combating Domestic Violence and in the Family Community (hereinafter: the Council).

## Article 37

(1) The members of the Council shall be appointed and dismissed by the Government, on the proposal of the Ministry.

(2) The Council shall have nine members, consisting of representatives of the competent ministries and other republican bodies, public institutions and professional services.

(3) The Council may, as necessary, involve in its work representatives of institutions, as well as non-governmental organisations, and experts from the academic and research community in fields relevant to the improvement of policies in the field of domestic violence.

## Article 38

(1) The mandate of Council members shall last four years.
(2) The Council shall elect a president from among its members.
(3) The work of Council members is voluntary and unpaid.
(4) The procedure for election and appointment of new Council members, due to replacement or resignation of previous
members, shall be identical to the procedure and manner of election and appointment of the members being replaced.

## Article 39

The Council shall, within its competences, perform the following tasks:

a) monitor the implementation and assess policies and measures for preventing and combating domestic violence, and in that regard provide recommendations and opinions,

b) provide recommendations and opinions to the Ministry in the procedure of proposing and adopting legal regulations related to domestic violence,

v) create and participate in the realisation of scientific, professional, research and other projects in the field of domestic violence,

g) participate in the activities of social partners in working on the prevention and combating of domestic violence, and
d) consider other issues relevant to the prevention and combating of domestic violence.

## Article 40

(1) Administrative and technical tasks for the Council shall be performed by the Ministry.
(2) The work and functioning of the Council shall be regulated by the Rules of Procedure of the Council.

(3) The Rules of Procedure from paragraph 2 of this Article shall be adopted by the Council by a majority vote of more than half of the total number of members.
VII - SUPERVISION AND PENAL PROVISIONS

## Article 41

(1) Supervision over the implementation of this Law shall be carried out by the Ministry.

(2) The Gender Centre shall monitor the application of this Law from the aspect of fulfilling obligations arising from domestic and international standards for combating and preventing violence against women and gender-based violence and standards for gender equality.

## Article 42

(1) A fine of 1,000 KM to 3,000 KM shall be imposed for a misdemeanour on an employee in an educational, social or health institution who fails to report domestic violence.

(2) A fine of 300 KM to 900 KM shall be imposed for a misdemeanour on a citizen who fails to report domestic violence.

(3) A fine of 300 KM to 900 KM shall be imposed for a misdemeanour on a family member or member of a family community who fails to report domestic violence committed against a child, except in the case where he or she is also a victim of domestic violence.

(4) A fine of 3,000 KM to 5,000 KM shall be imposed for a misdemeanour on protection entities and other bodies authorised to act under this Law that fail to act in accordance with Article 34 paragraph 1 of this Law.
Article 42a

A police officer who fails to act in accordance with the obligations under this Law upon a report of domestic violence shall bear responsibility in accordance with the law.

## Article 43

Whoever violates the urgent protection measures and protective measures determined by the court through the application of this Law shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of the law prescribing criminal offences.
Article 43a

A fine of 3,000 KM to 7,000 KM shall be imposed on a legal entity that implements a special support measure that fails to act in accordance with Article 20 paragraph 4 of the Law.
