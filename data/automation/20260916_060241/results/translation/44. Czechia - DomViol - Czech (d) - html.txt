Transitional Provision

PART SEVEN
Amendment to the Criminal Code (Art. 8)
Art. VIII

§ 194

of Act No. 40/2009 Coll., the Criminal Code, including the heading, reads:
"§ 194
Bigamy and Partnership
| (1) | Whoever, during the subsistence of their marriage or partnership under the Civil Code, enters into another marriage or another partnership shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years. |
| (2) | Whoever enters into a marriage or partnership under the Civil Code with a person who is already in another marriage or in another partnership shall be punished in the same manner.".

PART TEN
COMMON PROVISION (Art. 11)
Art. XI
Persons who entered into a registered partnership with each other before the date of entry into force of this Act may enter into a partnership under the Civil Code; the registered partnership thereby ceases to exist.
PART ELEVEN
Entry into Force (Art. 12)
Art. XII
This Act shall enter into force on 1 January 2025, with the exception of the provisions of Part Four, Art. IV, points 1, 2, 4, 22 and 28, which shall enter into force on 1 January 2027.
