His Majesty King Maha Vajiralongkorn Phra Vajiraklaochaoyuhua is graciously pleased to proclaim that
Whereas it is expedient to revise the law on the protection of persons subjected to domestic violence; This Act contains certain provisions relating to the restriction of rights and freedoms of persons, in respect of which section 26 in conjunction with section 28, section 32, section 33, section 37 and section 38 of the Constitution of the Kingdom of Thailand so permit by virtue of the provisions of law; The reasons and necessity for restricting the rights and freedoms of persons under this Act are to carry out operations concerning the promotion and development of families, and the protection of welfare, which will be beneficial in creating family strength and developing the family institution, and the enactment of this Act
is in accordance with the conditions prescribed in

Section 1

This Act shall be called the "Act on Promotion of Family Development and Protection, B.E. 2562 (2019)".

Section 2

This Act shall come into force upon the expiration of ninety days from the date of its publication in the Royal Gazette onwards.

Section 3

The Act on Protection of Persons Subjected to Domestic Violence, B.E. 2550 (2007), is hereby repealed.

Chapter 1 General Provisions

Section 4 In this Act:

"domestic violence" means any act committed by a family member against another with intent to cause, or in a manner likely to cause, danger to life, body, mind, health, liberty, or reputation of a family member, or to compel or exercise unlawful domination over a family member to do, not do, or submit to any act unlawfully;
"family member" means ascendant, descendant, spouse, former spouse, person who cohabits or has cohabited as husband and wife without marriage registration, adopted child, as well as any person who is dependent and resides in the same household;
"State agency" means ministry, bureau, department, or other government unit with the status of a department, regional administration, local administration, and other State agencies;
"State enterprise" means a State enterprise established by an Act or Royal Decree;
"Court" means the Juvenile and Family Court under the law on the Juvenile and Family Court and Juvenile and Family Case Procedure;
"Committee" means the Committee on Promotion of Family Development and Protection;
"member" means a member of the Committee on Promotion of Family Development and Protection;
"competent official" means a person appointed by the Minister of Social Development and Human Security to perform duties under this Act, and includes administrative officials or police under the Code of Criminal Procedure;

"Head of Centre" means the head of a Family Promotion and Protection Centre;
"Director-General" means the Director-General of the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development;
"Minister" means the Minister having charge and control of the execution of this Act.

Section 5

The Minister of Social Development and Human Security, the Minister of Interior, and the Minister of Public Health shall have charge and control of the execution of this Act, and shall have the power to issue regulations and notifications for the implementation of this Act, in the part relating to the affairs of that Ministry.
The Minister of Social Development and Human Security shall have the power to appoint competent officials to perform duties under this Act.
Such regulations and notifications, when published in the Royal Gazette, shall come into force.

Chapter 2 Committee

Section 6

There shall be a committee called the "Committee on Promotion of Family Development and Protection", consisting of:
(1) the Prime Minister or a Deputy Prime Minister assigned by the Prime Minister, as Chairperson;
(2) the Minister of Social Development and Human Security, as Vice Chairperson;
(3) ex officio members, namely: the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Social Development and Human Security, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Interior, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Justice, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Education, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Public Health, the Attorney-General, and the Commissioner-General of the Royal Thai Police;
(4) fifteen expert members appointed by the Prime Minister through selection from persons with knowledge, ability and proven experience in the field of family promotion and development, the field of child and youth promotion and development, the field of promotion and development of quality of life of persons with disabilities, the field of promotion and development of quality of life of the elderly, the field of women's promotion and development, the field of law, the field of culture and religion, the field of occupational development, the field of educational development, the field of public health, the field of protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, or other fields beneficial to the promotion and development, protection, prevention, or rehabilitation of family members, at least one person per field.

The selection of expert members under (4) shall be in accordance with the criteria, procedures and conditions prescribed by the Minister of Social Development and Human Security in regulations.
The Director-General shall be a member and secretary, and shall have the power to appoint not more than two officials of the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development as assistant secretaries.

Section 7

An expert member must possess the qualifications and not have the prohibited characteristics as follows:
(1) having Thai nationality;
(2) having appropriate attitude and conduct to perform the duty of protecting persons subjected to domestic violence, or promoting and developing the family institution;
(3) never having been examined by the National Human Rights Commission and found to have committed an act constituting a violation of human rights, and never having been examined by the Bangkok Child Protection Committee or a Provincial Child Protection Committee and found to have committed an act constituting improper treatment of a child;
(4) not being an incompetent or quasi-incompetent person;
(5) not being a bankrupt or having been a dishonest bankrupt;
(6) never having been punished by dismissal, removal or discharge from a State agency;
(7) never having been subject to welfare protection measures arising from the commission of domestic violence under this Act;
(8) never having been punished for an offence under the law on the protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, the law on child protection, the law on the Juvenile and Family Court and Juvenile and Family Case Procedure, or the law on the prevention and suppression of human trafficking;
(9) never having been sentenced to imprisonment by a final judgment, except for an offence committed through negligence or a petty offence.

Section 8

An expert member shall hold office for a term of three years and may be reappointed, but shall not hold office for more than two consecutive terms.

Section 10

A meeting of the Committee must have no fewer than half of all committee members in attendance to constitute a quorum.
In a meeting of the Committee, if the Chairperson is not present at the meeting or is unable to perform duties, the Vice Chairperson shall act in place of the Chairperson. Meetings of the Committee shall be held at least three times per year.

Section 11 The Committee shall have the following duties and powers:

(1) To determine policies, strategies, and master plans for the promotion and development of families and the protection of welfare, with the approval of the Council of Ministers, as well as to determine programs, measures, and guidelines for the promotion and development of families and the protection of welfare;
(2) To monitor and evaluate the implementation of policies, strategies, master plans, programs, measures, and guidelines for the promotion and development of families and the protection of welfare, as well as to report results concerning the promotion and development of families and the protection of welfare to the Council of Ministers and the Parliament for acknowledgment at least once per year;
(3) To submit opinions and observations to the Council of Ministers concerning the enactment or amendment of laws relating to the promotion and development of families and the protection of welfare, as well as to submit opinions and observations concerning Thailand's role at the national and international levels in the promotion and development of families and the protection of welfare;
(4) To promote the support, coordination, monitoring, and assistance of government agencies, the private sector, and civil society that carry out the promotion and development of families and the protection of welfare;

Chapter 4 Community Family Development Centers

Section 15

Any private organization having objectives or activities related to the promotion and development of families or the protection of welfare may apply for registration as a Community Family Development Center, abbreviated as "CFDC."
The qualifications of applicants for registration, the application for registration, and the registration as a CFDC shall be in accordance with the criteria, procedures, and conditions prescribed by the Minister of Social Development and Human Security in regulations.

Section 16

A CFDC may file an application for a government agency to consider providing assistance or support in financial matters, resources, academic matters, personnel development, or any other matters in carrying out operations to promote and develop families or protect welfare.
The filing of an application for such assistance or support shall be in accordance with the criteria, procedures, and conditions prescribed by the Minister in regulations.

Chapter 5 Promotion and Development of Families

Section 17

For the benefit of the promotion and development of families, the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development shall coordinate with the Ministry of Interior to ensure that registrars under the law on family registration, government officials performing duties related to the promotion and development of families, CFDCs, or private organizations having objectives in the promotion and development of families have knowledge and understanding in the promotion and development of families.

In carrying out operations under paragraph one to provide knowledge and understanding in the promotion and development of families, such knowledge shall include the following:
(1) Knowledge concerning the relationship between husband and wife, property between husband and wife, the validity of marriage, the termination of marriage, parentage with children, the rights and duties of parents and children, adopted children, maintenance, as well as matters of inheritance and wills;
(2) Knowledge concerning the protection of welfare, or other knowledge relating to families and the institution of the family;
(3) Knowledge and skills in choosing a spouse, married life, emotional changes and emotional control;
(4) Knowledge in preparing before and after pregnancy, knowledge and skills in raising children;
(5) Knowledge in pursuing occupations, creating jobs and income, saving, managing property, and accessing sources of capital for pursuing occupations to support families;
(6) Knowledge of the rights and duties of citizenship, rights and welfare, and guidelines for requesting assistance from government agencies or private organizations;
(7) Any other knowledge concerning the promotion and development of families.
In carrying out operations under paragraphs one and two, the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development shall carry out operations to achieve cooperation and coordination among various agencies in order to achieve integration of the promotion and development of the institution of the family.

หมวด ๖ Welfare Protection

มาตรา ๒

๒ Welfare protection means actions to ensure that persons in families receive safety, have good relationships, and prevent repeat offenses, and includes the following actions:
(๑) Protection or prevention of persons in families from being subjected to domestic violence and prevention of criminal offenses characterized as domestic violence.
(๒) Promotion or support to prevent domestic violence.
(๓) Mediation or actions to achieve compromise when domestic violence occurs.
(๔) Remediation or alleviation of suffering for persons subjected to domestic violence. (๕) Treatment and rehabilitation of persons involved in domestic violence.
(๖) Follow-up care for persons in families when domestic violence occurs.

มาตรา ๒

๓ Any person who witnesses or becomes aware of domestic violence shall report information or news of such case to an official, the Social Assistance Center of the Ministry of Social Development and Human Security, or directly to the Family Promotion and Protection Center for welfare protection.
Reporting under paragraph one may be made orally, in writing, by telephone, by electronic means, or by any other method. In this regard, such reporting when made in good faith shall be protected and shall not incur civil, criminal, or administrative liability.

มาตรา ๒

๙ A Family Support and Protection Center that files a petition for the court to issue a protection order may propose protection measures to resolve the problem of domestic violence or to prevent repeated domestic violence for the court's consideration.
Protection measures may be any of the following actions:
(๑) Requiring the domestic violence perpetrator to meet with officials at specified intervals.
(๒) Prohibiting the domestic violence perpetrator from causing incidents or provoking in any manner, whether directly or indirectly, that would cause domestic violence.
(๓) Prohibiting the domestic violence perpetrator from consuming alcohol or any addictive substances for a specified period.
(๔) Prohibiting the domestic violence perpetrator from approaching or entering the residence of the domestic violence victim, or requiring them to leave the residence of the domestic violence victim.
(๕) Prohibiting the domestic violence perpetrator from causing distress or harassment to the domestic violence victim, including threatening, stalking, tracking, photographing, telephoning, sending harassing messages by electronic media, or by any other means causing distress or harassment, whether directly or indirectly.
(๖) Requiring the domestic violence perpetrator to receive psychosocial counseling or corrective treatment and rehabilitation at a medical facility or specialized institution.
(๗) Requiring the domestic violence perpetrator to receive corrective treatment and rehabilitation for alcohol addiction, gambling, any addictive substances, or any other behavior that is the cause of the violence at a medical facility or treatment center.
(๘) Requiring the domestic violence perpetrator to enter into a bond, when there is reasonable cause to believe that they may cause distress or harassment under (๕) or cause harm or danger to the domestic violence victim or family members again, by having that person enter into a bond for an amount of money as the court deems appropriate that they will not cause harm again throughout the specified period, with or without requiring security.
(๙) Requiring the domestic violence perpetrator to surrender any weapons to which they hold ownership rights to the Family Support and Protection Center for safekeeping until the domestic violence perpetrator's behavior changes, or if the domestic violence perpetrator is merely in possession of such weapons, to surrender them to the Family Support and Protection Center, and for the Family Support and Protection Center to notify the owner who holds ownership rights to reclaim such weapons.
(๑๐) Requiring the domestic violence perpetrator to pay compensation for relief to the domestic violence victim or family members affected by domestic violence.
(๑๑) Requiring the domestic violence perpetrator to receive corrective treatment and rehabilitation or other treatment in addition to (๖) and (๗) according to announcements of the Ministry of Public Health.
(๑๒) Requesting the court to order the revocation of parental authority of the domestic violence perpetrator in whole or in part, or prohibiting the domestic violence perpetrator from contacting the child, whether directly or indirectly, for such period as the court deems appropriate, in cases where the domestic violence perpetrator has committed violence against a child whom they have a duty to support and care for.
(๑๓) Requesting the court to order the removal of guardianship or curatorship in cases where that person has committed domestic violence against a person under their guardianship or curatorship.

In cases where the head of the Center has not filed a petition with the court but the court considers it beneficial for protection, the court may order the Family Support and Protection Center to appoint an official of the Family Support and Protection Center to provide opinions on protection to the court before the court issues a protection order.

๑ In cases where a request is made to the court for protection, if the court considers that there is a necessary reason to provide protection or assistance to the domestic violence victim or family members, the court may order the Family Support and Protection Center to provide protection or assistance to such persons.

มาตรา ๔

๐ In performing duties under this Act, competent officials shall have the following duties and powers:
(๑) To enter a dwelling or place where an incident occurred, when having witnessed an act of domestic violence or having received a notification under

มาตรา ๒๓

(๒) To issue a written summons for the domestic violence perpetrator, the domestic violence victim, or any related person to give statements or facts, as well as to submit documents or any other evidence for consideration in carrying out welfare protection.
(๓) To examine, assess the condition of the problem that has occurred, gather facts and causes concerning the act of domestic violence, report opinions or examination results of relevant professionals, and compile a summary for the head of the center to consider issuing a welfare protection order, or to submit to the court for consideration to issue a welfare protection order, or to take any other action under this Act.

(๔) To take action so that the domestic violence victim receives assistance and protection on a preliminary temporary basis, receives medical examination and treatment according to medical standards, receives counseling and advice from psychiatrists, psychologists, social workers, as well as receives remedies, rehabilitation, or any other assistance as appropriate.
(๕) To coordinate with administrative officials or police, officials of medical establishments, or competent officials under other laws related to welfare protection in order to carry out actions under (๔).
(๖) To search a dwelling or place where an incident occurred in order to find and assist the domestic violence victim.

หมายเหตุ:- เหตุผลในการประกาศใช้พระราชบัญญัติฉบับนี้ คือ เนื่องจากพระราชบัญญัติคุ้มครองผู้ถูกกระทํา ด้วยความรุนแรงในครอบครัว พ.ศ. ๒๕๕๐ มีการกําหนดฐานความผิดอาญาที่ไม่ได้เป็นไปตามเจตนารมณ์ใน การลดการกระทําความรุนแรงในครอบครัวที่ประสงค์ให้ผู้กระทําความรุนแรงในครอบครัวมีโอกาสกลับตัวและยับยั้ง การกระทําความผิดซ้ํา และหน้าที่และอํานาจของพนักงานเจ้าหน้าที่ไม่สอดคล้องกับสภาพปัญหาความรุนแรง ในครอบครัวในปัจจุบัน นอกจากนี้ ไม่มีบทบัญญัติในการบูรณาการความร่วมมือกันทุกภาคส่วนในการแก้ปัญหา เกิดความซ้ําซ้อนกับการกระทําความผิดตามประมวลกฎหมายอาญา สมควรยกเลิกความผิดอาญาฐานกระทํา ความรุนแรงในครอบครัว ปรับปรุงมาตรการคุ้มครองสวัสดิภาพบุคคลในครอบครัวให้มีความเหมาะสมยิ่งขึ้น ซึ่งจะเป็นการเสริมสร้างความสัมพันธ์ที่ดีแก่บุคคลในครอบครัว และกําหนดมาตรการในการส่งเสริม พัฒนา และคุ้มครองสถาบันครอบครัว เพื่อให้การป้องกันและแก้ไขปัญหาความรุนแรงในครอบครัวเป็นไปอย่างมี ประสิทธิภาพ ประกอบกับรัฐธรรมนูญแห่งราชอาณาจักรไทย กําหนดให้รัฐพึงเสริมสร้างความเข้มแข็งของ ครอบครัวอันเป็นองค์ประกอบพื้นฐานที่สําคัญของสังคม รวมทั้งคุ้มครองป้องกันบุคคลในครอบครัวจาก การกระทําความรุนแรงหรือปฏิบัติอย่างไม่เป็นธรรมและกําหนดให้มีการบําบัด ฟื้นฟู และเยียวยาผู้ถูกกระทําการ ดังกล่าว จึงจําเป็นต้องตราพระราชบัญญัตินี้
