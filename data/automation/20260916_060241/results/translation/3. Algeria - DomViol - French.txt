Article 1. — This law is intended to amend and supplement Ordinance No. 66-156 of 8 June 1966 establishing the Penal Code.

Art. 2

The provisions of Ordinance No. 66-156 of 8 June 1966, referred to above, are supplemented by articles
266 bis and 266 bis 1, worded as follows:
"Art. 266 bis — Whoever voluntarily causes injuries or strikes his or her spouse is punished as follows:
1-	by imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years if the injuries or blows have not caused any illness or total incapacity for work of more than fifteen (15) days.
2-	by imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years if there has been total incapacity for work of more than fifteen (15) days.
3-	by imprisonment for a term of ten (10) to twenty (20) years, if the injuries or blows have been followed by mutilation, amputation or deprivation of the use of a limb, blindness, loss of an eye or other permanent infirmities.
4-	by life imprisonment, if the blows struck or injuries inflicted voluntarily, but without intent to cause death, have nevertheless caused it.
The offence is established whether or not the perpetrator resides in the same domicile as the victim.
The offence is also established if the violence is committed by the former spouse and it is found that it is related to the previous marital relationship.
The perpetrator may not benefit from mitigating circumstances if the victim is pregnant or disabled or if the offence was committed in the presence of minor children or under threat of a weapon.

In the cases provided for in (1) and (2), referred to above, the victim's pardon terminates criminal proceedings.
In the case provided for in (3), and when there is pardon from the victim, the sentence is five (5) to ten (10) years' imprisonment".
"Art. 266 bis 1 — Any person who commits against his or her spouse any form of assault, or repeated verbal or psychological violence placing the victim in a situation that undermines his or her dignity or physical or psychological integrity is punished by imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years.
The state of domestic violence may be proved by any means.
The offence is established whether or not the perpetrator resides in the same domicile as the victim.
The offence is also established if the violence is committed by the former spouse and it is found that it is related to the previous marital relationship.
The perpetrator may not benefit from mitigating circumstances if the victim is pregnant or disabled or if the offence was committed in the presence of minor children or under threat of a weapon.
The victim's pardon terminates criminal proceedings".

TITRE 1

DISPOSITIONS GENERALES
Article 1er. — The purpose of this framework law is to establish the fundamental principles and general rules governing scientific research and technological development.

Art. 2

Scientific research and technological development are national priorities.

Art. 3. — This framework law aims

—	to guarantee the development of scientific research and technological development, including university scientific research;
—	to strengthen the scientific and technological foundations of the country;
—	to understand the changes that society is experiencing in order to identify and analyze the systems, norms, values and phenomena that govern it;
—	to study and promote the history and national cultural heritage;
—	to identify and bring together the means necessary for scientific research and technological development;
—	to promote the function of scientific research within higher education and training establishments, scientific research and other establishments and to stimulate the valorization of research results;
—	to strengthen State funding of scientific research and technological development activities;
—	to enhance institutional and regulatory structures for more efficient handling of scientific research and technological development activities.

Art. 4

Economic operators may invest in the national effort to promote scientific research and technological development. In return, they benefit from incentive and encouragement measures defined by finance laws.

Art. 5

Economic operators carrying out research and development activity may benefit from credits from the national budget for scientific research and technological development, in accordance with the conditions and procedures established by regulation.

CHAPITRE ler
DEFINITIONS

Art. 6. — For the purposes of this law, the following are understood as

Scientific research and technological development: two segments of the same process of producing knowledge and know-how, covering all fields of knowledge, their use and exploitation for new applications, in response to social and cultural expectations, economic needs and the imperatives of sustainable development.
Programming of research activities: process of identifying and defining research axes consisting of generic research themes that perfectly illustrate the scientific and technological objectives to be achieved.

Evaluation of research activities: activity that ensures the relevance and efficiency of the national research system. It intervenes at all stages of the process of elaboration, implementation and achievement of research objectives. Strategic evaluation concerns research policies, while scientific evaluation concerns research activities.
Development plan: Instrument by which national research programmes are defined, assigned their scientific and socio-economic objectives, as well as the means to be implemented for their achievement for a given multi-year period.

Thematic research agency: pilot establishment situated between the central administration and the entities executing research activities, responsible for coordinating and promoting the implementation of research programmes falling within its field of competence.
A posteriori financial control: designates the control exercised over the authorising officer after commitment and after authorisation of a public expenditure, in order to verify its budgetary regularity, by an agent reporting to the ministry of finance.

Public establishment of a scientific and technological nature (EPST): legal category of public establishments applicable to scientific research establishments endowed with legal personality and financial autonomy adapted to the specificity of their missions.

Entities executing research activities endowed with management autonomy: research team, research laboratory and research unit, created within establishments giving researchers a working framework.

Common research service: designates all specific means and technical and scientific equipment pooled for the benefit of research establishments and entities, with a view to carrying out scientific research and technological development activities.
Valorisation: designates any activity making it possible to render usable or marketable the results, knowledge and skills of research.
Technological watch: permanent updating process aimed at the systematic organisation of the collection of information on scientific and technical achievements relating to products, procedures, methods and information systems in order to deduce development opportunities therefrom.

Technology transfer: process of formal transfer to industry of discoveries resulting from research with the aim of marketing them in the form of new products and/or services.
Innovation: implementation of a new or substantially improved product (good or service) or process, a new marketing method or a new organisational method in business practices, and the organisation of the workplace or external relations. Innovation is therefore distinguished from invention in that it is part of an applicative perspective.

Seed fund: designates the first financial contribution to the capital of a company. This fund is generally used to finance the marketing of the company's first product.
Innovative enterprise: designates an enterprise taking charge of the implementation of fundamental or applied research projects, or one that carries out research and development activities.
Innovation and technology transfer centre (CITT): scientific establishment bringing together actors from the professional world and research in a given field. It provides scientific and technological expertise to enterprises that do not have the means to create a research and development centre, and

also enables technology transfer between research structures and the professional world (creation of innovative enterprises, exploitation of patents, etc.).
Technopole: designates a site bringing together innovative enterprises and teaching and research structures in various technological fields.

TITRE 2

PROGRAMMATION NATIONALE DES ACTIVITES DE RECHERCHE SCIENTIFIQUE ET DE DEVELOPPEMENT TECHNOLOGIQUE

Art. 9

The national programming of scientific research and technological development activities is part of the implementation of the national strategy for overall development.

Art. 10

For the achievement of the objectives set out in Article 7 of this law, scientific research and technological development activities are organised into national research programmes.

Each programme is subdivided into domains, domains into axes, axes into themes and themes into research projects.

Art. 11

The research axes assigned their themes which illustrate the scientific and socio-economic objectives to be achieved, developed by the permanent sectoral committees and the intersectoral commissions, each as far as it is concerned, are reinforced by the thematic research agencies and integrated into intersectoral and multidisciplinary national research programmes with a view to their submission to the national council for scientific research and technological development.

Art. 12

The order of priority among national research programmes is determined on the basis of the Government's priorities in terms of sustainable development strategy.

In addition, account is taken in particular of the human scientific potential, research infrastructure and equipment.

Art. 13

The national research programmes, developed by the bodies and structures established for this purpose, are implemented through development plans containing the human, material and financial resources necessary for their implementation, determined by regulation.

They are readjusted, as necessary, in the same forms.

Art. 14. — Research projects are selected according to objective criteria

Within the framework of respect for the principle of adversarial examination, the author of the research project may defend his project before the body empowered to select research projects.

The procedures for implementing this article are determined by regulation.

TITRE 3

EVALUATION DES ACTIVITES DE RECHERCHE SCIENTIFIQUE ET DE DEVELOPPEMENT TECHNOLOGIQUE

Art. 15

Scientific research and technological development activities are subject to periodic evaluation which complies with objective criteria.

Art. 16

The evaluation covers both the activities of researchers, research entities and research programmes.
The evaluation of scientific research and technological development activities is carried out by peers, in a collective and adversarial framework and in accordance with a code of ethics. Its results are communicated to the parties concerned, with respect for the anonymity of the expert evaluators.
The rules and procedures for evaluation are brought to the attention of the parties concerned.
The procedures for implementing this article are determined by regulation.

Art. 17

The reports on the implementation of research activities are drawn up by the permanent sectoral committees, the intersectoral commissions and the thematic research agencies, each as far as it is concerned, and give rise to a report on the assessment and prospects, which is presented annually by the minister responsible for scientific research to the national council for scientific research and technological development and may be published after completion of the evaluation phases on all appropriate media.

Art. 18

The national council for scientific research and technological development annually assesses the report on the review and prospects of scientific research and technological development provided for in Article 17 above, which is presented to it. This assessment is discussed in the Council of Ministers.

TITLE 4

VALORISATION - SCIENTIFIC AND TECHNICAL SERVICES
CHAPTER 1 VALORISATION

Art. 19

The State undertakes through the ministry responsible for scientific research and the authorized bodies the adequate provisions in order to valorise the results of scientific research and technological development, in particular, with a view to:
—	valorising value-added technologies and the available engineering capacities and technological equipment;

—	raising the capacities for adaptation of technologies;
—	strengthening standardisation;
—	facilitating the transfer of the results of scientific research and technological development to the socio-economic sector;
—	valorising knowledge and know-how;
—	providing public authorities with the expertise necessary for decision-making.

Art. 20

Valorisation structures and technical and socio-economic study structures may be created within higher education and scientific research institutions, according to conditions and procedures set by regulation.

Art. 21

Innovative enterprises may benefit from a seed fund according to conditions and procedures set by regulation.

Art. 22

Incentives for scientific production may be granted, particularly in the area of patents for invention capable of industrial application according to conditions and procedures set by regulation.

Art. 23

With a view to addressing socio-economic concerns, doctoral theses may be carried out in a professional environment according to conditions and procedures set by regulation.

Art. 24

To stimulate the activities of transfer, exploitation and dissemination of research results, the State provides through the ministry responsible for scientific research and the relevant ministries the necessary means to facilitate and encourage the publication and protection of the results of research work, the production and dissemination of scientific and technical periodicals and publications.

CHAPTER 2 SCIENTIFIC AND TECHNICAL SERVICES

COOPERATION

Art. 25

The State works through the ministry responsible for scientific research, with the assistance of the relevant ministerial departments, to promote the establishment of a national network for technological watch and transfer of scientific and technical information.

Art. 26

The State undertakes through the ministry responsible for scientific research and the relevant ministries the necessary provisions to enable researchers to access international scientific and technical information sources, to obtain this information and to encourage intersectoral and international cooperation in scientific research and technological development, in accordance with the legislation and regulations in force.

TITLE 5

ORGANISATIONAL FRAMEWORK

CHAPTER 1
GUIDANCE AND MANAGEMENT BODIES

Art. 27

The permanent sectoral committees for scientific research and technological development at the level of each ministerial department are responsible for ensuring the promotion, coordination and evaluation of scientific research and technological development activities at the sector level.

The missions, organisation and functioning of these committees are set by regulation.

Art. 28

The intersectoral commissions are responsible for the programming, coordination, promotion and evaluation of scientific research and technological development activities.
The missions, organisation and functioning of these commissions are set by regulation.

Art. 29

The national evaluation council, placed under the minister responsible for scientific research, is responsible for the evaluation of the national policy on scientific research and technological development, its choices, its impacts, as well as the development of evaluation mechanisms and the monitoring of their implementation.
The council is composed mainly of independent scientific personalities.

The missions, composition and functioning of the council are set by regulation.

Art. 30

The national council for scientific research and technological development is responsible for defining the orientations of the national policy in this area, for determining the priorities among the national research programmes, and for assessing their execution.

The missions, composition and functioning of the council are set by regulation.

Art. 31

The permanent national directing body is responsible for the implementation of the national policy on scientific research and technological development, within a collegial and intersectoral framework.

The permanent national directing body is placed under the authority of the minister responsible for scientific research. It is endowed with management autonomy.

The missions and organisation of this body are set by regulation.

Art. 32

The national conference of public institutions of a scientific and technological nature is a framework for consultation and coordination around the activities of the network of research institutions, as well as the implementation of the national policy on scientific research and technological development.
The missions, organisation and functioning of the national conference are set by regulation.

CHAPTER 2

INTERMEDIATION INSTITUTIONS

Art. 33

The thematic research agency is responsible for the coordination, monitoring of the execution and valorisation of scientific research activities relating to a family of scientific sectors.
For the performance of its missions, the agency relies on the intersectoral commissions within its area of competence.
The missions, organisation and functioning of the thematic research agency are set by regulation.

CHAPTER 3 ENTITIES FOR THE EXECUTION

OF RESEARCH ACTIVITIES

Art. 34

For the implementation of scientific research and technological development programmes, a public institution of a scientific and technological nature is created.
The public institution is subject to rules adapted to the specificity of its missions, in particular budgeting by the State, the keeping of accounts in accordance with the financial accounting system, and financial control ø posteriozi.
The public institution of a scientific and technological nature may create subsidiaries and take participations.
The missions, organisation and functioning of the public institution of a scientific and technological nature are set by regulation.

Art. 35

The public institution created in Article 34 of this law may contribute to higher education and training according to conditions and procedures set by regulation.

Art. 36

Scientific research and technological development units specific to the establishment, associated or joint units with management autonomy and a posteriori financial control may be created within higher education and training institutions and other public institutions.

The rules for the creation, organisation and operation of these units are established by regulation.

Art. 37

Scientific research and technological development laboratories specific to the establishment, associated or joint laboratories with management autonomy and a posteriori financial control may be created within higher education and training institutions and other public institutions.

The procedures for the creation, organisation and operation of these laboratories are established by regulation.

Art. 38

Scientific research and technological development teams specific to the establishment, joint or associated teams with management autonomy and a posteriori financial control may be created within higher education and training institutions and other public institutions.

The procedures for the creation and operation of research teams are established by regulation.

Art. 39

Research units, research laboratories and research teams may also be created within economic enterprises as well as within associations and foundations of a scientific nature according to conditions and procedures established by regulation.

Art. 40

The military establishment of a scientific and technological nature is governed by a specific text.

CHAPITRE 4

ESTABLISHMENTS FOR VALORISATION, INNOVATION AND TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER

Art. 41

The thematic research agency has the mission of implementing, in coordination with the relevant structures and bodies, the national strategy for technological development, particularly through the transfer of research results and their valorisation.

Art. 42

The innovation and technology transfer centre provides enterprises with the expertise they need and ensures the transfer of technology from entities executing research activities to the socio-economic sector.

The missions, organisation and operation of this centre are established by regulation.

CHAPITRE 5

RESEARCH NETWORKS AND COMMON SERVICES

Art. 43

In order to bring together skills, pool resources and promote collective work with a view to optimal management of research projects of common interest, thematic research networks may be created according to conditions and procedures established by regulation.

Art. 44

Common research services intended for the grouping of skills and scientific equipment may be created within higher education and training institutions, university hospital establishments, scientific research establishments and thematic research agencies.

The missions, organisation and operation of common research services are established by regulation.

TITRE 6 DEVELOPMENT OF HUMAN RESOURCES

Art. 45

To achieve the objectives of scientific research and technological development, the numbers of research personnel must increase at a rate corresponding to the needs of the development plans provided for in Article 13 of this law.

Art. 46

The human resources development policy aims at mobilising national scientific skills, particularly through:
—	increasing the full-time researcher potential in research structures;
—	increased involvement of teacher-researchers in higher education and training institutions;
—	the contribution of researchers working part-time;
—	the use by enterprises and organisations of qualified human resources and the national human potential of expertise in direct activities carried out in response to the requirements induced by socio-economic changes;
—	training through research, for research and higher education;
—	the optimal use of researchers residing in Algeria and the contribution of Algerian scientific skills working abroad, in the fields of training, teaching and research as well as foreign researchers in the capacity of visiting researcher;

—	the establishment of networks of research teams ensuring the development of cooperative research;
—	the establishment of appropriate mechanisms allowing the mobility of researchers between higher education and training institutions and research entities, organisations and enterprises, in accordance with the legislation and regulations in force;
—	the establishment of appropriate mechanisms allowing doctorate holders who are not employees to carry out research within their entities under employment contracts.
The procedures for implementing this article are established, as necessary, by regulation.

Art. 47

Scientific research and technological development activities are carried out by permanent researchers.
Teacher-researchers and/or permanent researchers and executives from different sectors of activity may be called upon to carry out these activities part-time.
The conditions for exercising and remunerating scientific research and technological development activities part-time are established by regulation.

Art. 48

The rights and obligations of permanent researchers and research support personnel as well as incentive measures are established by a specific statute.

Art. 49

The specific statute provided for in Article 48 of this law as well as the texts governing part-time researchers guarantee the independence of the scientific approach, freedom of analysis, access to information, mobility, contribution to the dissemination of knowledge and to continuing training, particularly through participation in scientific meetings.
The specific statute guarantees career monitoring and the most adequate and stable conditions in terms of employment, remuneration and encouragement while establishing the obligation to present a report on scientific research and technological development activities.
Permanent researchers, part-time researchers and research support personnel are subject, in the exercise of their missions, to confidentiality and rules of ethics, in accordance with the legislation and regulations in force.

Art. 50

Permanent researchers, teacher-researchers and research support personnel may be placed in a secondment position with subsidiaries created by the establishments to which they report, for a maximum period of five (5) years, in accordance with the legislation and regulations in force.

TITLE 7 FINANCIAL PROVISIONS

Art. 51

The budget for scientific research and technological development provided annually by the finance laws brings together all operating and equipment appropriations granted for the financing of scientific research and technological development activities, carried out by the various higher education institutions and scientific research centres under the various ministerial departments concerned and other research institutions, as well as the appropriations intended for the financing of national research programmes.

Art. 52

The State provides the material and financial means necessary for the implementation of scientific research and technological development activities.

Art. 53

To achieve the objectives of scientific research and technological development, the appropriations allocated by the State correspond to those set out in the development plans provided for in Article 13 of this law.
Scientific research and technological development expenditure shall experience balanced growth.

Art. 54

The resources intended for the financing of scientific research and technological development programmes come from the State budget and:
—	public own funds;
—	income from the products of subsidiaries and equity participation;
—	research contracts and service provision;
—	private funds;
—	international cooperation;
—	donations and bequests.

Art. 55

The appropriations devoted to scientific research and technological development are allocated essentially:
—	to national research programmes;
—	to research and technological development entities and bodies with a view to maintaining and strengthening the research environment;
—	to higher education and training institutions with a view to developing research-training;
—	to the promotion of research in national enterprises, public or private, participating in scientific research, technological development, innovation and valorisation activities.

Access to financial allocations intended for the financing of scientific research and technological development activities is opened by means of an agreement established between the ministry responsible for scientific research and the beneficiary institutions and entities.

Art. 57

The use of appropriations devoted to scientific research and technological development is subject to rules adapted to the specificity of this activity, in particular ex post financial control and the direct use of revenues generated under contracts and agreements and flexibility in the award of public contracts.

The minister responsible for scientific research shall present the national budget for scientific research and technological development, prepared in accordance with the provisions of Article 51 of this law, before Parliament within the framework of the finance law.
