no. 93, containing urgent provisions on security and for combating gender-based violence, as well as on civil protection and the commissioning of provinces, is converted into law with the amendments set out in the annex to this law.

no. 95, converted, with amendments, by Law 7 August 2012,
no. 135, is suspended.

Art. 3

Preventive measure for domestic violence conduct
1. In cases where law enforcement agencies receive a report, (( in non-anonymous form )), of a fact that must be considered attributable (( to the offences referred to in articles 581, as well as 582, second paragraph, completed or attempted, of the criminal code, )) in the context of domestic violence, the quaestor, even in the absence of a complaint, may proceed, having obtained the necessary information from the investigative bodies and having heard the persons informed of the facts, to issue a warning to the perpetrator of the act. For the purposes of this article, domestic violence means (( one or more acts, serious or non-episodic, )) of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence that occur within the family or family unit (( or between persons linked, currently or in the past, by a bond of marriage or by an emotional relationship, )) regardless of whether the perpetrator of such acts shares or has shared the same residence with the victim.
2. The provisions of article 8, paragraphs 1 and 2, of decree-law 23 February 2009,
no. 11, converted, with amendments, by law 23 April 2009,
no. 38, (( as amended by this decree )), apply insofar as compatible. The quaestor may request from the prefect of the place of residence of the recipient of the warning the application of the measure of suspension of the driving licence for a period from one to three months. The prefect orders the suspension of the driving licence pursuant to article 218 of (( the highway code, referred to in )) legislative decree 30 April 1992,
no. 285. The prefect does not proceed with the suspension of the driving licence if, taking into account the economic conditions of the family unit, it appears that the work requirements of the person concerned cannot be guaranteed by the issue of the permit referred to in article 218, (( paragraph 2, )) of the aforementioned legislative decree no. 285 of 1992.
3. The Ministry of the Interior - Department of Public Security, also through the data contained in the Data Processing Centre referred to in article 8 of law 1 April 1981,
no. 121, prepares annually a criminological analysis of gender-based violence which constitutes an autonomous section of the annual report to Parliament referred to in article 113 of the aforementioned law no. 121 of 1981.
4. In every act of the procedure for the adoption of the warning referred to in paragraph 1, the personal details of the person reporting must be omitted, (( unless the report proves to be manifestly unfounded. The report may be used only for the purposes of initiating the procedure. ))
5. The measures referred to in paragraph 1 of article 11 of decree-law 23 February 2009,
no. 11, converted, with amendments, by law 23 April 2009,
no. 38, also apply in cases where law enforcement agencies, health facilities and public institutions receive from the victim information (( of the offences referred to in articles 581 and 582 of the criminal code in the context of domestic violence referred to in paragraph 1 of this article.
5-bis. When the quaestor proceeds with the warning pursuant to article 8 of decree-law 23 February 2009,
no. 11, converted, with amendments, by law 23 April 2009,
no. 38, as amended by this decree, and this article, he informs without delay the perpetrator of the act about the services available in the territory, including family counselling centres, mental health services and addiction services, as identified by the Plan referred to in article 5, aimed at intervening with respect to perpetrators of domestic or gender-based violence. ))

Art. 4

Protection for foreign victims of domestic violence
1. After article 18 of the consolidated text of provisions concerning the regulation of immigration and rules on the status of foreigners referred to in legislative decree 25 July 1998,
no. 286, the following is (( inserted )):
"Art. 18-bis
(Residence permit for victims of domestic violence)
"1. When, in the course of police operations, investigations or proceedings for any of the offences provided for in articles 572, 582, 583, 583-bis, 605, 609-bis and 612-bis of the criminal code or for one of the offences provided for in article 380 of the code of criminal procedure, committed on national territory in the context of domestic violence, situations of violence or abuse against a foreigner are ascertained and a concrete and current danger to his or her safety emerges, as a consequence of the choice to escape from the same violence or as a result of statements made during the preliminary investigations or the trial, the quaestor, (( with the favourable opinion of the proceeding judicial authority or on the proposal of the latter, )) issues a residence permit pursuant to article 5, paragraph 6, to enable the victim to escape from the violence. For the purposes of this article, domestic violence means (( one or more acts, serious or non-episodic, )) of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence that occur within the family or family unit or (( between persons linked, currently or in the past, by a bond of marriage or by an emotional relationship, )) regardless of whether the perpetrator of such acts shares or has shared the same residence with the victim.
2. With the proposal or opinion referred to in paragraph 1, the elements from which the existence of the conditions indicated therein results are communicated to the quaestor, with particular reference to the seriousness and current nature of the danger to personal safety.
3. The same residence permit may be issued by the quaestor when situations of violence or abuse emerge in the course of assistance interventions (( by anti-violence centres, territorial social services or )) social services specialised in assisting victims of violence. In that case, the existence of the elements and conditions referred to in paragraph 2 is assessed by the quaestor on the basis of the report drawn up by the same social services. (( For the purposes of issuing the residence permit, the opinion of the competent judicial authority pursuant to paragraph 1 is in any case required. ))
4. The residence permit referred to in paragraphs 1 and 3 is revoked in the event of conduct incompatible with the purposes thereof, reported by the public prosecutor or, as far as concerns their competence, by the social services referred to in paragraph 3, or in any case ascertained by the quaestor, or when the conditions that justified its issue cease to exist.
(( 4-bis. Against the foreigner convicted, even by a non-final sentence, including that adopted following application of the penalty on request pursuant to article 444 of the code of criminal procedure, for one of the offences referred to in paragraph 1 of this article, committed in the context of domestic violence, the revocation of the residence permit and expulsion pursuant to article 13 of this consolidated text may be ordered. ))
5. The provisions of this article also apply, insofar as compatible, to citizens of Member States of the European Union and their family members.".

Art. 5

(( Extraordinary action plan against sexual and gender-based violence
1. The Minister for Equal Opportunities, also making use of the Fund for policies relating to rights and equal opportunities, referred to in Article 19, paragraph 3, of decree-law No. 223 of 4 July 2006,
converted, with amendments, by law No. 248 of 4 August 2006,
prepares, with the contribution of the administrations concerned, women's associations engaged in the fight against violence and anti-violence centres, and adopts, following agreement in the Unified Conference pursuant to legislative decree No. 281 of 28 August 1997,
an "Extraordinary action plan against sexual and gender-based violence", hereinafter referred to as the "Plan", which must be prepared in synergy with the new programming of the European Union for the period 2014-2020.
2. The Plan, with the objective of ensuring homogeneous actions throughout the national territory, pursues the following purposes:
a) to prevent the phenomenon of violence against women through information and awareness-raising of the community, strengthening the awareness of men and boys in the process of eliminating violence against women and in resolving conflicts in interpersonal relationships;
b) to raise awareness among media sector operators for the realisation of communication and information, including commercial, that respects the representation of gender and, in particular, of the female figure, including through the adoption of self-regulatory codes by the operators themselves;
c) to promote adequate training of school personnel in relationships and against violence and gender discrimination and to promote, within the framework of the national guidelines for the curriculum of nursery schools and the first cycle of education, the national guidelines for secondary schools and the guidelines for technical and vocational institutes, in the curricular and extra-curricular educational programming of schools of all levels, the awareness-raising, information and training of students in order to prevent violence against women and gender discrimination, including through adequate emphasis on the theme in textbooks;
d) to strengthen forms of assistance and support for women victims of violence and their children through homogeneous methods of strengthening the network of territorial services, anti-violence centres and assistance services for women victims of violence;
e) to ensure the training of all professionals who come into contact with acts of gender-based violence or stalking;
f) to increase the protection of victims through the strengthening of collaboration between all the institutions involved;
g) to promote the development and activation, throughout the national territory, of actions, based on consolidated methodologies and consistent with specially prepared guidelines, for the recovery and support of persons responsible for acts of violence in affective relationships, in order to promote their recovery and limit cases of recidivism;
h) to provide for a structured and periodically updated collection, at least annually, of data on the phenomenon, including the census of anti-violence centres, also through the coordination of existing databases;
i) to provide for specific positive actions that also take into account the competences of the administrations engaged in the prevention, combating and support of victims of gender-based violence and stalking and the experiences of associations providing assistance in the sector;
l) to define a structured governance system among all levels of government, which is also based on the various experiences and good practices already implemented in local networks and in the territory.
3. The Minister for Equal Opportunities transmits annually to the Chambers a report on the implementation of the Plan.
4. For the financing of the Plan, the Fund for policies relating to rights and equal opportunities is increased by 10 million euros for the year 2013. The related expenditure shall be covered by a corresponding reduction in the expenditure authorisation referred to in Article 61, paragraph 22, of decree-law No. 112 of 25 June 2008,
converted, with amendments, by law No. 133 of 6 August 2008,
and subsequent amendments.
5. The implementation of the provisions contained in this article, except as provided for in paragraph 4 of the same article and in Article 5-bis, shall be carried out using the human, instrumental and financial resources available under current legislation, without new or additional burdens on public finances. ))

The Minister delegated for equal opportunities, following agreement in the Permanent Conference for relations between the State, the regions and the autonomous provinces of Trento and Bolzano, shall proceed annually to distribute among the regions the resources referred to in paragraph 1 taking into account:
a) regional programming and interventions already operational to combat violence against women;
b) the number of public and private anti-violence centres already existing in each region;
c) the number of public and private shelters already existing in each region;
d) the need to rebalance the presence of anti-violence centres and shelters in each region, reserving one third of the available funds for the establishment of new centres and new shelters in order to achieve the objective set out in the recommendation of the Expert Meeting on violence against women - Finland, 8-10 November 1999.
3. Anti-violence centres and shelters, for which anonymity is guaranteed, are promoted by:
a) local authorities, individually or in associated form;
b) associations and organisations operating in the field of support and assistance to women victims of violence, which have acquired specific experience and expertise in the field of violence against women, which use a reception methodology based on relationships between women, with specifically trained personnel;
c) entities referred to in letters a) and b), jointly, in agreement or in consortium form.
4. Anti-violence centres and shelters operate in an integrated manner with the network of territorial social, health and welfare services, taking into account the fundamental needs for the protection of persons suffering violence, even when they perform functions of specialist services.
5. Irrespective of the intervention methodologies adopted and the specific professional profiles of the operators involved, the training of professional figures in anti-violence centres and shelters promotes an integrated approach to the phenomena of violence, in order to ensure recognition of the different dimensions of violence suffered by persons, at relational, physical, psychological, social, cultural and economic level. The training of operators in anti-violence centres and shelters also includes recognition of the dimensions of violence attributable to gender inequalities.
6. The regions receiving the resources subject to distribution shall submit to the Minister delegated for equal opportunities, by 30 March of each year, a report concerning the initiatives adopted in the previous year using those resources.
7. On the basis of the information provided by the regions, the Minister delegated for equal opportunities shall submit to the Chambers, by 30 June of each year, a report on the state of use of the resources allocated pursuant to this article. ))
