L A W
ON PREVENTION AND PROTECTION FROM VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

I.	GENERAL PROVISIONS

Subject of the law

Article 1

This law regulates the conduct of institutions with due diligence in taking measures for prevention of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, the conduct of institutions for protection of women from gender-based violence and domestic violence, mutual coordination of institutions and organizations, protection services for victims and collection of data on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence.

Purpose of the law

Article 2

The purpose of the law is prevention and suppression of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, effective protection of victims from any form of gender-based violence against women as well as victims of domestic violence with respect for fundamental human freedoms and rights guaranteed by the Constitution of the Republic of North Macedonia and international agreements ratified in accordance with the Constitution of

the Republic of North Macedonia (hereinafter: ratified international agreements). The purpose of the law is based on the principle of equality and elimination of stereotypes about gender roles.

Glossary

Article 3

Certain terms used in this law have the following meaning:
1) "Violence against women" is a violation of human rights, discrimination against women and denotes all acts of gender-based violence that result or are likely to result in physical, sexual, psychological or economic harm or suffering to women, including direct and indirect threats and intimidation for such acts, coercion, arbitrary restriction and/or deprivation of liberty, regardless of whether they occur in public or private life;
2) "Gender-based violence against women" is violence directed against a woman because she is a woman or which disproportionately affects her. Gender-based violence against women encompasses the causes and result of the unequal power relationship between women and men as a result of a social, not an individual problem. Gender-based violence against women also refers to violence against women defined in points 1) and 3) of this article;
3) "Domestic violence" is mistreatment, insult, endangering of safety, bodily injury, sexual or other psychological, physical or economic violence which causes a feeling of insecurity, endangerment or fear, including threats of such acts, against a spouse, parents or children or other persons living in a marital or extramarital union or common household, as well as against a current or former spouse or extramarital partner or persons who have a common child or are in close personal relationships, regardless of whether the perpetrator shares or shared the same residence with the victim or not;
4) "Close personal relationships" are personal relationships between persons who are or have been in partnership relationships, regardless of whether the perpetrator shares or shared the same residence with the victim or not;
5) "Intimate partner violence" is behaviour by a current or former spouse or partner that causes physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence;
6) "Sex" means the physical characteristics of an individual (reproductive system, chromosomes, hormones) according to which the sex assigned to individuals at birth is designated, based on a combination of bodily characteristics and internal reproductive organs;
7) "Gender" means the socially constructed roles, behaviours, activities and attributes that a given society considers appropriate for women and men;
8) "Transgender person" is a person who does not identify by gender with the sex assigned at birth;
9) "Victim" is any natural person who is exposed to violence specified in points 1), 2), 3) and 5) of this article, on the territory of the Republic of North Macedonia;
10) "Perpetrator" is any natural and legal person who has committed or has not undertaken any of the acts from points 1), 2), 3) and 5 of this article;
11) "Woman" includes girls under the age of 18;
12) "Non-refoulement" is a principle on the basis of which the state undertakes not to expel or in any way return women and children asylum seekers, persons with refugee status or persons under subsidiary protection to the borders of a state in which

there is an immediate danger to life and body, from violence against women or domestic violence or their freedom would be in danger due to their race, religion, nationality, membership of a particular social group or political belief or where they would be subject to torture, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment;
13) "Child" is any person under the age of 18;
14) "Physical violence" is any act of use of physical force or act which impairs the health and bodily and psychological integrity of the victim.
15) "Psychological violence" is any behaviour using coercion, intimidation or threat, which causes a feeling of fear, endangerment, disturbance or injury to the dignity and psychological integrity of the victim;
16) "Stalking" is any intentional behaviour of repeated threatening directed towards another person which causes the person to fear for their safety;
17) "Economic violence" is any act of restriction or prevention in the realisation and/or disposal of personal income and financial resources, for maintaining the common household and for care of the child, thereby causing economic dependence of the victim;
18) "Sexual violence and rape" is any act of a sexual nature, with any body part or object, on the body of another person without his/her consent. Sexual violence also exists when another person is induced or coerced to engage in acts of a sexual nature with a third person without his consent. Consent must be expressed voluntarily as a result of the free will of the person, assessed in the context of the circumstances;
19) "Sexual harassment" is any verbal, non-verbal or physical behaviour of a sexual nature that has the purpose or effect of violating the dignity of a person, especially when creating a threatening, hostile, degrading, humiliating or offensive environment;
20) "Sexual harassment via the internet" is any verbal, non-verbal or other conduct of a sexual nature, which has the purpose or effect of violating dignity or creating a threatening, hostile, humiliating or intimidating environment, access or practice, through electronic means of communication;
21) "Forced marriage" is coercing, luring, misleading, threatening or otherwise forcing an adult or child to enter into marriage or extramarital union;
22) "Female genital mutilation" is the cutting, infibulation or any other mutilation in whole or in part of the labia majora, labia minora or clitoris of a woman;
23) "Forced abortion and forced sterilisation" is the termination of pregnancy on a woman without prior information and written consent, as well as performing an operation on a woman without prior information and written consent, that is, understanding of the procedure whose purpose or effect is to terminate her capacity for natural reproduction;
24) "Trafficking in women" is an act by which through force, serious threat the victim is misled or other forms of coercion, abduction, fraud, by abuse of one's position or the state of pregnancy, helplessness or physical or mental incapacity of another or by giving or receiving money or other benefit for obtaining the consent of a person who has control over another person or otherwise recruits, transports, transfers, buys, sells, shelters or accepts persons for the purpose of exploitation through prostitution or other forms of sexual exploitation, pornography, forced

labour or servitude, slavery, forced marriages, forced pregnancy, illegal adoption or similar relationship, begging or exploitation for the purpose of an activity prohibited by law or impermissible transplantation of parts of the human body and
25) "Coercive control over women" is abuse of power, use of threats, force or other forms of coercion, fraud or misrepresentation with the aim of controlling the behaviour and life of women.

Article 4

(1) The state authorities, legal entities exercising public powers established by law, officials, civil servants and other authorized persons performing work on behalf of the state have an obligation to refrain from participating in acts of violence and to take all measures and activities to prevent any act of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence.
(2) The subjects from paragraph (1) of this article have an obligation to take all appropriate legislative, administrative, judicial and other measures to prevent, protect, investigate, punish and provide fair compensation to victims or restoration to the original position for acts of violence committed by natural or legal persons.
(3) Officials in institutions performing activities in the field of social protection, internal affairs, health, child protection and education, as well as competent courts and public prosecutor's offices in proceedings for gender-based violence against women and domestic violence are obliged to act urgently, with due regard to the interests and needs of the victim.

II. BASIC PRINCIPLES

Non-discrimination

Article 5

(1) Gender-based violence against women is discrimination against women that restricts them from enjoying rights and freedoms on an equal basis with men.
(2) The measures, activities and services from this law are undertaken with the aim of implementing the principle of equality, eliminating stereotypes about gender roles, prevention and protection from all forms of discrimination against women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence.
(3) The measures, activities and services for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence are implemented without discrimination on the basis of race, skin color, origin, national or ethnic affiliation, sex, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, membership in a marginalized group, language, citizenship, social origin, education, religion or religious belief, political belief, other belief, disability, age, family or marital status, property status, health condition, personal characteristic and social status or any other basis.
(4) The measures, activities and services from this law, which are undertaken for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and domestic violence are not considered discrimination.

Prohibition of victimization

Article 6

Any action undertaken with the aim of intentionally or unintentionally punishing or placing in a less favorable position a person who has taken actions for protection from gender-based violence and domestic violence is prohibited, that is, if the person has reported violence, initiated proceedings for protection from gender-based violence and domestic violence, testified during the proceedings or otherwise participated in proceedings for protection from gender-based violence and domestic violence.

Vulnerable women

Article 7

The subjects from articles 11 and 24 of this law are obliged when undertaking measures and activities to take into account the consequences that gender-based violence has on vulnerable categories of women, especially on pregnant women, women with children and children with disabilities, single mothers, women with disabilities, women from rural areas, women who use drugs, sex workers, migrants, refugees, asylum seekers, women without citizenship, lesbians, bisexuals and transgender people, women living with HIV, homeless women, women victims of human trafficking, elderly women, materially insecure women and others.

Reasonable accommodation

Article 8

The measures, activities and services for victims of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence should be reasonably accommodated to the specific needs of women with disabilities.
Principle of gender-responsive policies

Article 9

(1) The measures, activities and services for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and domestic violence should be gender-responsive and recognize the characteristics and differences arising from the gender roles of women and men, so that the results of the measures, activities and services contribute to gender equality.
(2) Gender-responsive policies include the gender perspective as a criterion that is integrated in all phases of the policy-making process, assessment of situations, implementation and evaluation of policies arising from this law.

Principle of empowerment of women who suffer violence

Article 10

(1) The measures, activities and services for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and from domestic violence are provided in a manner that enables strengthening of the potential and capacities of the victim for independent organization of life and self-help in the future.

(2) The victim has the right to participate in the assessment of her condition and needs, to be informed about the measures, activities and services for protection and to participate in the selection of services and measures for her assistance, support and protection.
(3) Without the consent of the victim, that is, her legal representative, measures, activities and services for protection cannot be undertaken, except when there is immediate danger to her life and body.

III. COMPETENT INSTITUTIONS AND INTEGRATED POLICIES

Competence for implementation of the law

Article 11

(1) Competent for implementation of this law are all state administration bodies, courts, public prosecutor's office, local self-government units and legal entities exercising public powers established by law.
(2) The subjects from paragraph (1) of this article are obliged to ensure that the work within their competence is performed by professional persons trained for implementation of the work specified by this law.

Institutional and multisectoral cooperation

Article 12

(1) The subjects from article 11 of this law are obliged to cooperate with each other for achieving the goals of this law by preparing a protocol for mutual cooperation.
(2) The local self-government units with institutions and associations prepare a protocol for mutual cooperation for undertaking measures for prevention, suppression and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in the local community.

Assembly of the Republic of North Macedonia

Article 13

(1) The Assembly of the Republic of North Macedonia, on the proposal of the Government of the Republic of North Macedonia, adopts a National Strategy for Prevention and Protection from Gender-Based Violence against Women and Domestic Violence (hereinafter: the Strategy).
(2) The Strategy is adopted for a period of eight years, and is implemented through two national action plans lasting four years each and annual operational plans at the level of competent institutions.
(3) The Assembly monitors the implementation of the Strategy and the spending of funds from the Budget from the annual operational plans through the annual report of the National Coordinating Body from article 15 of this law.

Government of the Republic of North Macedonia

Article 14

The Government of the Republic of North Macedonia (hereinafter: the Government), within its competences, undertakes measures and activities for prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence through inclusion of the principle of equality and elimination of stereotypes about gender roles in draft

laws and other regulations, organizing and financing national campaigns to raise awareness about all forms of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and other.
National Coordinating Body for implementation of the Council of Europe Convention on preventing and combating violence against women and domestic violence

Article 15

(1) The Government shall establish a National Coordinating Body for the Implementation of the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence (hereinafter: National Coordinating Body), with a mandate of five years.
(2) The National Coordinating Body referred to in paragraph (1) of this article shall be composed of one representative from the General Secretariat of the Government, three senior officials from the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy, a member and deputy member from the Ministry of Health, a member and deputy member from the Ministry of Justice, a member and deputy member from the Ministry of Education and Science, a member and deputy member from the Ministry of Internal Affairs, judges and public prosecutors, a representative of the local self-government units and a representative of associations, trade unions and employers' associations, one representative each from the Commission for Equal Opportunities for Women and Men and from the Club of Members of Parliament of the Assembly of the Republic of North Macedonia.
(3) The Judicial Council of the Republic of North Macedonia shall nominate the representative of judges as a member of the National Coordinating Body.
(4) The Council of Public Prosecutors of the Republic of North Macedonia shall nominate the representative of public prosecutors as a member of the National Coordinating Body.
(5) The Association of Local Self-Government Units shall nominate the representative of local self-government units as a member of the National Coordinating Body.
(6) The Government shall issue a public call for the selection of three members of the National Coordinating Body from among associations, which must have at least five years of work experience in the field of violence against women and domestic violence.
(7) The manner of work of the National Coordinating Body shall be regulated by rules of procedure.
(8) The National Coordinating Body shall be chaired by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy.
(9) The administrative and professional work for the needs of the National Coordinating Body shall be performed by the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy.
Scope of the National Coordinating Body

Article 16

The National Coordinating Body shall perform the following tasks:
1) prepare and monitor the implementation of the Strategy;
2) prepare and monitor the National Action Plans;
3) coordinate the work of institutions in the area of prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
4) monitor the implementation of the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence and national policies in the field of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
5) monitor and analyse the situation regarding gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;

6) monitor the planned and spent financial resources allocated to the bodies referred to in Articles 11 and 24 of this law;
7) provide opinions and recommendations for improving policies and adopting measures and activities in the field of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
8) monitor the implementation of recommendations of international and regional bodies on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and propose activities and measures for their implementation;
9) promote the principle of equality and elimination of stereotypes about gender roles;
10) provide an opinion on the methodology for integrated data collection on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence from all relevant institutions and organisations responsible for implementing this law;
11) provide an opinion on programmes for initial and continuous education of professionals providing specialised services to women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence at the request of the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy;
12) coordinate and monitor the collection of data on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence according to pre-determined indicators and
13) submit an annual report on the activities undertaken and progress achieved regarding the situation of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in accordance with the Strategy to the Assembly of the Republic of North Macedonia for consideration, no later than the end of June for the previous year.
Ministry of Labour and Social Policy

Article 17

(1) The Ministry of Labour and Social Policy shall perform the following tasks:
1) create policies for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence;
2) prepare a programme for initial and continuous education of professionals providing specialised services to women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence, through the Institute for Social Activities;
3) implement initial and continuous education for professionals from social work centres for performing tasks within their competence regarding gender-based violence against women and domestic violence through the Institute for Social Activities;
4) give consent to the programme of initial and continuous education on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence implemented by the entities referred to in Articles 18, 19 and 20 of this law;
5) provide an opinion on the programme of initial and continuous education on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence implemented by the entity referred to in Article 23 of this law;
6) maintain a register of trained persons for implementing initial and continuous education on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
7) provide financial resources for support of services for prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in the field of social protection in accordance with Article 27 of this law;
8) collect data on the number of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence in the social protection system disaggregated by: sex, gender, age, community affiliation, place of residence, relationship between victim and perpetrator, type and number of

services provided to victims and perpetrators and other data determined by law from institutions in the social protection system and from other institutions responsible for implementing this law;
9) monitor and analyse the situation regarding gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and inform the National Coordinating Body thereof at least once a year;
10) cooperate with associations, foundations and other organisations in the promotion, prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and
11) perform other tasks in the field of prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in accordance with law.
(2) The manner of issuing consent to the programme of initial and continuous education referred to in paragraph (1) point 4) of this article shall be prescribed by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy.
(3) The form, content and manner of maintaining the register referred to in paragraph (1) point 6) of this article shall be prescribed by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy.

Ministry of Internal Affairs

Article 18

The Ministry of Internal Affairs performs the following tasks:
1) creates policies for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence;
2) prepares a program for implementation of initial and continuous education for police officers who work on prevention and protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence and submits it to the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy for obtaining consent;
3) implements initial and continuous education for police officers for performing tasks within their competence regarding gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
4) takes actions for prosecution of perpetrators of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in accordance with law;
5) provides financial resources for support of activities for prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in accordance with Article 27 of this law;
6) monitors and analyzes the situation with gender-based violence against women and victims of domestic violence and reports thereon to the National Coordinating Body at least once a year;
7) collects data on the number of reported cases of gender-based violence against women and victims of domestic violence disaggregated by: sex, gender, age, ethnic community, disability, place of residence, relationship between victim and perpetrator, qualification of the criminal offense and other data determined by law;
8) cooperates with associations, foundations and other organizations in promotion, prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
9) takes urgent measures for protection of women from gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence and
10) performs other tasks within its competence with the aim of prevention and protection of women victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence in accordance with law.

Ministry of Health

Article 19

The Ministry of Health performs the following tasks:
1) creates policies for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence;
2) ensures provision of health services in the network of health institutions for victims and perpetrators of gender-based violence and domestic violence;
3) plans and gives recommendations to health institutions for establishment of referral centers for victims of sexual violence;
4) provides financial resources for support of activities and establishment and sustainability of services for prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in the field of health protection in accordance with Article 27 of this law;
5) informs the public and health workers about the availability of existing services for treatment and support of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence;
6) prepares a program for implementation of initial and continuous education for health workers who work on protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence and submits it to the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy for obtaining consent;
7) implements initial and continuous education for health workers for early detection, care and assistance of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence;
8) collects data on the situation with women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence who have sought help and intervention through the health protection system disaggregated by: sex, gender, age, ethnic community, disability, place of residence, relationship between victim and perpetrator, type and number of services provided to victims and other data in accordance with the Law on Records in the Field of Health, as well as for perpetrators;
9) monitors and analyzes the situation with gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, based on data from the National System for Electronic Records in Health maintained in accordance with the Law on Records in the Field of Health and reports thereon to the National Coordinating Body at least once a year;
10) cooperates with associations, foundations and other organizations in promotion, prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and
11) performs other tasks within its competence with the aim of prevention and protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence, in accordance with law.
Ministry of Justice

Article 20 The Ministry of Justice performs the following tasks

1) provides free legal aid in accordance with law;
2) provides financial resources for compensation for women victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence in accordance with law;
3) provides financial resources for support of activities for prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in accordance with Article 27 of this law;

4) prepares a program for implementation of initial and continuous education for authorized officials of the Ministry of Justice, authorized associations and legal clinic and lawyers for protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence and submits it to the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy for obtaining consent;
5) implements initial and continuous education for authorized officials providing free legal aid, for support and assistance of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence through trainers registered in the register of the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy;
6) collects data on the number of requests for free legal aid by women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence disaggregated by: sex, gender, age, ethnic affiliation, disability, place of residence, relationship between victim and perpetrator, type and number of services provided to victims and other data determined by law;
7) monitors and analyzes the situation with gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and reports thereon to the National Coordinating Body at least once a year;
8) cooperates with associations, foundations and other organizations in promotion, prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and
9) performs other tasks within its competence with the aim of prevention and protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence in accordance with law.
Units of local self-government

Article 21

The units of local self-government perform the following tasks:
1) create local policies and programs for the protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence;
2) establish care centers for victims, counseling centers for victims, counseling centers for perpetrators and other services for prevention of gender-based violence and protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence in accordance with the Law on Social Protection;
3) provide financial resources for the functioning of services for prevention and protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence at the local level in accordance with Article 27 of this law;
4) implement initial and continuous education for employees for the performance of tasks within their competence on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence through trainers registered in the register of the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy;
5) establish mutual and coordinated cooperation at the local level among centers for social work, police stations, health institutions, preschool and educational institutions;
6) cooperate with associations, foundations and other organizations in the promotion, prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
7) within the municipal councils for social protection, the situation with gender-based violence against women and domestic violence is reviewed and
8) perform other tasks within their competence with the aim of prevention and protection of victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence in accordance with the law.

Agency for Audio and Audiovisual Media Services

Article 22

The Agency for Audio and Audiovisual Media Services performs the following tasks:
1) within its competences, ensures the protection of the interests, respect for their human rights and dignity of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence;
2) implements initial and continuous education for employees for the performance of tasks within their competence on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence through trainers registered in the register of the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy;
3) periodically conducts research and analysis on the portrayal of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in the programs of audio and audiovisual media service providers;
4) takes measures in accordance with the Law on Audio and Audiovisual Media Services for sanctioning audio and audiovisual media service providers who present women in a degrading manner and do not respect the human rights of women victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence;
5) cooperates with associations, foundations and other organizations in the promotion and prevention of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and
6) performs other tasks within its competence with the aim of prevention and protection of women victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence in accordance with the law.
Academy for Judges and Public Prosecutors

Article 23

The Academy for Judges and Public Prosecutors performs the following tasks:
1) prepares a program for the implementation of initial and continuous education for candidates, judges and public prosecutors for the protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence and submits it to the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy for opinion;
2) implements initial and continuous education for candidates, judges and public prosecutors on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence through trainers registered in the register of the Ministry of Labor and Social Work;
3) conducts research and analysis in the field of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
4) cooperates with institutions, associations, foundations and other organizations in the promotion and prevention of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and
5) performs other tasks within its competence with the aim of prevention and protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence in accordance with the law.

Associations

Article 24 Associations perform the following tasks

1) provide initial and continuous training for persons who implement activities for prevention and protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence through trainers registered in the register of the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy;

2) provide specialized services for the protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence;
3) conduct research and analysis on the situation with gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
4) collect data on the number of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence for whom they have provided assistance and support, disaggregated by: sex, gender, age, ethnicity, disability, place of residence, relationship between victim and perpetrator, type and number of services provided to victims and perpetrators and other data determined by law and
5) cooperate with other institutions with the aim of ensuring timely and effective protection of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence.
Trade Unions

Article 25

Trade unions conduct research and analysis on the situation with gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, report cases of gender-based violence against women, cooperate with associations, foundations and other organizations in the promotion and prevention of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence.
Legal Entities

Article 26

Legal entities that exercise public powers take measures and actions to promote social and cultural values of behavior based on equality of women and men and elimination of stereotypes about gender roles and prevention of gender-based violence against women in the workplace in accordance with the law.
Budget for Implementation of the Law

Article 27

State authorities, bodies of local self-government units and legal entities exercising public powers established by law are obliged within their respective budgets to allocate funds for the implementation of the competences established by this law.
IV. COLLECTION AND PROTECTION OF DATA
Collection of Data

Article 28

(1) The entities from Articles 11 and 24 of this law collect statistical and administrative data on the situation of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, based on sex, gender, age, community affiliation, place of residence and other data determined by this or another law, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Personal Data Protection.
(2) The Ministry of Labour and Social Policy on a daily basis, using the national interoperability platform – Macedonian Information Highway (hereinafter: MIH), takes over the data from the entities specified in paragraph
(1) of this article that are users of MIH, in accordance with the regulations in the field of electronic governance and electronic services.

(3) The entities from paragraph (1) of this article that are not users of MIH and do not have the possibility for direct data exchange through MIH are obliged to develop web services on an appropriate platform that meets the conditions specified by the regulations in the field of electronic governance and electronic services, which will contain the requested data from the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy.
(4) The Ministry of Labour and Social Policy, using the previously collected data from the entities from paragraph (1) of this article, performs analysis and statistical processing of the same in its system for social rights and services. The analyses and reports that do not contain data protected by the Law on Personal Data Protection, the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy publishes on an annual basis in a form and content determined by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy.
(5) The data from paragraph (1) of this article that do not contain personal data, the competent institutions are obliged in processed form to publicly publish on their web pages, as well as in the Central Catalogue of Public Sector Data in accordance with the Law on Use of Public Sector Data.
(6) The form, content and manner of integrated collection of data from paragraph
(1) of this article, the entry of data into the system for social rights and services from paragraph (4) of this article and the content of data from paragraph (5) of this article, are prescribed by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy in agreement with the Minister of Internal Affairs, the Minister of Health and the Minister of Justice.

Courts

Article 29

(1) The courts establish and maintain a special record of the number of cases of gender-based violence against women and victims of domestic violence based on sex, gender, age, ethnic affiliation, disability, place of residence and other grounds, as well as data relevant for monitoring the situation of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, in accordance with law.
(2) The content and manner of collecting data from paragraph (1) of this article are prescribed by the Minister of Justice.

Public Prosecution

Article 30

(1) The public prosecution offices establish and maintain a special record of cases of gender-based violence against women and victims of domestic violence based on sex, gender, age, ethnic affiliation, disability, place of residence and other grounds, as well as data relevant for monitoring the situation of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in accordance with law.
(2) The content and manner of collecting data from paragraph (1) of this article are prescribed by the Minister of Justice.

National Survey

Article 31

(1) The State Statistical Office conducts a national survey on the situation of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence.

(2) The survey aims to provide comprehensive information on the number of cases of individual forms of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence according to sex, gender, age, ethnic affiliation, disability, geographical affiliation of the victim and perpetrator and other information relevant for monitoring the situation of violence against women.

Personal Data Protection

Article 32

(1) The entities from Articles 11 and 24 of this law collect, process, use, store and delete personal data in accordance with the Law on Personal Data Protection.
(2) Personal data collected in accordance with this law are immediately deleted in cases when it is determined that they are not accurate or the reasons, that is, the conditions due to which the personal data was entered into the record have ceased.
(3) Personal data collected in accordance with this law, until their deletion, may be provided under conditions determined by the Law on Personal Data Protection.
(4) The right of the personal data subject to access and correction of personal data held by the entities from Articles 11 and 24 of this law, as well as the obligation to protect personal data are ensured under conditions determined by the Law on Personal Data Protection.
(5) The entities from Articles 11 and 24 of this law are obliged to ensure confidentiality and protection of the processing of personal data of the personal data subject, whereby they must apply appropriate technical and organisational measures for protection against accidental or unlawful destruction of personal data or their accidental loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access, especially when the processing includes transmission of data over a network and protection against any unlawful forms of processing.
V. PREVENTION OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

Article 33

(1) The state administration bodies, local self-government units, the public and private sector, political parties, the media, associations and foundations, informal groups, social partners, trade unions, religious organisations and other organisations are obliged to undertake preventive measures to raise awareness of all forms of gender-based violence, promote gender equality and eliminate stereotypes about gender roles based on culture, customs, religion, traditions and other practices that are based on the idea of subordination of women and men.
(2) The mechanisms for gender equality in the state administration bodies and in the local self-government units determined by law perform coordination and give initiative for undertaking preventive measures in accordance with this law.

General Preventive Measures

Article 34

The entities from Article 33 of this law undertake the following general preventive measures:
1) adopt a programme for planning and implementing preventive measures and activities in accordance with this law;

2) conduct campaigns for recognising the consequences of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence;
3) establish a system for regular publication of information on safety measures, advice and other useful information for prevention of violence and
4) mutually cooperate in promotion and prevention of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence.

Ministry of Education and Science

Article 35

The Ministry of Education and Science, in addition to the general preventive measures from Article 34 of this law, undertakes the following special preventive measures:
1) adopts programs for equality between women and men, non-violent conflict resolution, respect for differences, promotion of gender equality and elimination of stereotypes about gender roles based on culture, customs, religion, traditions and other practices that are based on the idea of subordination of women and men in institutions for care and upbringing of children of preschool age on the proposal of the Bureau for Development of Education;
2) introduces content in curricula that promotes gender equality, non-violent conflict resolution, respect for differences, information on all forms of gender-based violence and comprehensive sexual education in primary and secondary schools;
3) introduces content in curricula that promotes gender equality, non-violent conflict resolution, respect for differences, information on all forms of gender-based violence and in adult education and
4) implements initial and continuous education for educational staff on gender equality, non-violent conflict resolution, respect for differences, information on all forms of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and comprehensive sexual education through the Bureau for Development of Education.

Ministry of Labour and Social Policy

Article 36

The Ministry of Labour and Social Policy, in addition to the general preventive measures from Article 34 of this law, undertakes the following special preventive measures:
1) implements programs for non-violent conflict resolution, respect for differences, promotion of gender equality and elimination of stereotypes about gender roles based on culture, customs, religion, traditions and other practices that are based on the idea of subordination of women and men in institutions for care and upbringing of children of preschool age in accordance with the Law on Child Protection;
2) provides measures for inclusion of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence in active employment measures as well as their employment and
3) implements campaigns or programs to raise awareness and understanding of the general public for recognition and consequences of violence against women and domestic violence.

Media

Article 37

Media established in accordance with the Law on Media apply the general preventive measures from Article 34 points 2), 3) and 4) of this law, in accordance with their capabilities and independent and autonomous editorial policy, and undertake the following special preventive measures:
1) when creating and broadcasting informative and educational programs, take into account raising awareness among the general public of all forms of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, promotion of gender equality and elimination of stereotypes about gender roles and
2) independently or through professional associations of journalists and media workers establish cooperation with associations to strengthen their capacities for professional reporting on issues related to violence against women and domestic violence.

Religious communities

Article 38

Churches, religious communities and religious groups are obliged to refrain from statements, announcements or publications that justify gender-based violence against women and domestic violence as a result of religion, culture, tradition or custom.
Treatment of perpetrators of domestic violence

Article 39

Psychosocial treatment of perpetrators of domestic violence is provided in a counselling centre for perpetrators of violence in accordance with the Law on Social Protection and regulations in the field of health protection.

VI. PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AND VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

Reporting by a citizen

Article 40

(1) Every natural person who has knowledge of the existence of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence is obliged to report to a police station, centre for social work, public prosecutor's office, association or S.O.S. line.
(2) The competent institutions from paragraph (1) of this article are obliged to act also on an anonymous report.

Reporting by officials

Article 41

(1) The responsible person and the head of the institution that performs activities in the field of social and child protection, employment, internal affairs, health and education, are obliged to report knowledge of the existence of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence to a police station or centre for social work.

(2) Every responsible and head person in a legal entity is obliged to report knowledge of the existence of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence to a police station or centre for social work.
(3) Every responsible and head person in a legal entity when having knowledge of the existence of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, which causes immediate danger to life and body, is obliged to report to the public prosecutor's office.

Article 42

(1) Information about the victim or the perpetrator of violence, on the basis of which the victim or members of her family can be identified, must not be publicly disclosed, unless the victim consents to it.
(2) Institutions, parents, guardians, carers or adoptive parents are obliged to protect the child from exposure to the public.

Accompanying person

Article 43

(1) The victim has the right to choose a person who will accompany her during the procedure before the competent authority.
(2) The accompanying person may be any adult person, except the person who is a perpetrator of gender-based violence and/or domestic violence in the procedure.
(3) The accompanying person assists the victim in protecting her interest and provides support in procedures before officials and institutions and helps to find a solution for her case and provides support to the victim.
(4) The official conducting the procedure will not allow the accompanying person to accompany the victim, in case when he/she does not meet the conditions from paragraph (2) of this article as well as if he/she assesses that the accompanying person cannot provide support to the victim from paragraph (3) of this article, due to kinship relations or other relations with the victim or the perpetrator of violence against women and/or domestic violence.
(5) Children and persons with limited or revoked legal capacity in procedures before competent institutions are accompanied by the parent or guardian, except the parent or guardian who is a perpetrator of violence against women and/or domestic violence.
VII. MEASURES FOR PROTECTION OF VICTIMS

Measures for protection from revictimization

Article 44

The competent institutions from Article 40 paragraph (1) of this law are obliged to treat victims in a manner that will protect them additionally from victimization or revictimization, by undertaking the following measures, in accordance with their competences:
1) protection of the identity and personal data of the victim;
2) taking a statement at the place of reporting;
3) taking the statement of the victim in a police station or public prosecutor's office using video and audio recording which is used as evidence in the procedure with prior consent of the victim;
4) conversation in special premises adapted for conversation with victims;
5) limitation of confrontation with the reported person;
6) limitation of giving a statement to a maximum of two times during the procedure for protection from violence and

7) other measures that will protect the integrity of the victim.

Centre for social work

Article 45

(1) The Centre for Social Work is obliged to initiate proceedings immediately, and no later than within 12 hours of receiving information that gender-based violence against a woman or domestic violence has been committed, and to undertake measures for the protection of the victim, in accordance with the risk assessment.
(2) The Centre for Social Work initiates proceedings upon receiving information that gender-based violence against a woman or domestic violence has been committed, ex officio, at the request of the victim, upon a report from a citizen, official, institution or association.
(3) The Centre for Social Work in cases where the victim is a child or a person who is unable to care for themselves or a person with limited or revoked legal capacity, undertakes protection measures, regardless of whether there is consent from the parent or guardian.

Article 46

The Centre for Social Work undertakes the following measures for the protection of victims:
1) provides temporary care;
2) ensures that the victim receives necessary medical assistance and, if necessary, accompanies them to the nearest health institution;
3) ensures that the victim exercises the right to social and health protection in accordance with the law;
4) provides appropriate psycho-social intervention and treatment;
5) ensures that the victim receives psycho-social treatment in a counselling centre from professional persons at the centre for social work, association, counselling centre for women victims of violence and other victims of domestic violence;
6) provides assistance to the family for regular schooling of the child;
7) provides legal assistance and representation;
8) refers the victim to the relevant state and other bodies competent for economic empowerment of the victim and their active inclusion in the labour market, through the competent employment centre and
9) in case of high risk and undertaking of urgent protection measures, as well as when the victim uses temporary accommodation services, and no later than within 12 hours of receiving the report, issues a decision on temporary entrusting of care and upbringing of children to the parent victim.

Article 47

(1) The Centre for Social Work when the victim is a child or a person who is unable to care for themselves or a person whose legal capacity has been limited or revoked, in addition to the measures from Article 46 of this law, also undertakes measures in accordance with the Law on Family that relate to the regulation of relations between parents and children, supervision over the exercise of parental rights and guardianship and in accordance with the Law on Juvenile Justice.
(2) In proceedings for entrusting the child to one of the parents, the centre for social work takes into account the best interests of the child, in a manner that will ensure that it does not endanger the rights and safety of the victim and/or the children.

(3) The Centre for Social Work may temporarily restrict or prohibit personal relations and direct contacts of the child with the parent with whom they do not live together, in cases where this is in the best interests of the child, while the risk and consequences of the committed violence persist.

Article 48

(1) The Centre for Social Work whenever it has information that gender-based violence has been committed by a person who possesses a firearm immediately notifies the Ministry of Internal Affairs, and no later than within 24 hours submits a written notification.
(2) The Centre for Social Work whenever it has information that violence has been committed by a person who has access to and handles an official firearm, immediately, and no later than within 24 hours, submits a written notification to the institution or legal entity in which the person is employed and notifies the Ministry of Internal Affairs thereof.

Ministry of Internal Affairs

Article 49

(1) A police officer whenever there is a report of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, and for the purpose of removing immediate and serious danger to the life and physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of their family, is obliged to go to the scene of the incident and to prepare a police report immediately, and no later than within 12 hours of the intervention at the incident.
(2) The police officer in cases of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, when undertaking police work and applying police powers, operational-technical and preventive measures established by the Law on Police, always makes an assessment of the risk of serious danger to the life and physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of their family and the risk of repetition of violence, with the aim of protecting the victim, through appropriate risk management.
(3) The police officer ensures that the victim immediately receives appropriate health protection from Article 51 of this law.
(4) The police officer when acting under paragraph (1) of this article, temporarily seizes the weapon from the perpetrator and initiates proceedings provided by law for revocation of the weapon permit, the collector's weapon permit or the weapon carrying permit.
(5) The police officer at the request of the victim accompanies them to the victim's residence to take personal belongings that are necessary for daily life, and if this is prevented by the perpetrator or other persons living in the same residence, in cooperation with the competent public prosecutor and an order issued by the court, takes the victim's personal belongings.
(6) In case of immediate and serious danger to the life and physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of their family, the police officer is obliged immediately, and no later than within 12 hours of the intervention at the incident, to notify the competent public prosecutor.
(7) The police officer is obliged within the time limit established in paragraph (1) of this article to submit to the competent court a proposal for imposing an urgent protection measure - removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home and a police report prepared by the police officer.

(8) The police officer is obliged within the time limit established in paragraph (1) of this article, to notify the competent centre for social work for the purpose of undertaking urgent measures for the protection of the victim.
(9) The police officer when assessing that at the scene itself there is a high risk of immediate and serious danger to the life, physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of their family shall deprive the perpetrator of liberty in accordance with the law.
Risk Assessment

Article 50

(1) The competent institutions from Articles 45 and 49 of this law, when performing the work within their competence, are obliged to make an assessment of the risk of serious danger to the life and physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of their family, an assessment of the risk of repetition of violence with the aim of risk management and ensuring measures for coordinated assistance and protection of the victim.
(2) The risk assessment is made urgently within the first 12 hours of reporting. Throughout the entire procedure, the risk assessment is made in cooperation with the victim according to their needs.
(3) The manner of conducting the assessment of the risk of serious danger to the life and physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of their family of the victim and the risk of repetition of violence, the appropriate risk management, implementation and monitoring of protection measures for women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence, undertaken by the centre for social work and the necessary forms are prescribed by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy.
(4) The manner of assessing the risk of serious danger to the life and physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of their family and the risk of repetition of violence, the appropriate risk management, the form of the police report and the proposal for imposing an urgent protection measure, removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home are prescribed by the Minister of Internal Affairs.
Health Institutions

Article 51

(1) The health institution and health workers are obliged to immediately undertake measures for the protection of the victim, namely: examination of injuries and provision of medical treatment, documentation of injuries and collection of evidence for the existence of harm to physical and psychological integrity, issuance of medical documentation for the established injury that clearly and comprehensibly describes the type and extent of the injuries, informing victims of their rights, the protection procedure and available services for assistance and support, and undertaking other measures for the protection of victims in accordance with the needs of the victims.
(2) For the measures from paragraph (1) of this article, victims are exempt from payment of costs.
(3) The measures for health protection of women victims of violence and victims of domestic violence from paragraph (1) of this article are provided through an annual program adopted by the Minister of Health.

Associations

Article 52

(1) An association may undertake protection measures from Article 46 points 1), 2), 4), 5), 7) and
8) of this law, independently or in cooperation with the competent social work center.
(2) The association is obliged for the undertaken protection measures from paragraph (1) of this article, within 24 hours from undertaking the measures, to notify the social work center competent according to the place of residence of the victim.

Local self-government units

Article 53

(1) Local self-government units are obliged to provide funds in the budget for financial support to victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence.
(2) The scope, coverage, amount and duration of financial support for victims is regulated by each local self-government unit in accordance with law.

Educational institutions

Article 54

Educational institutions in the territory of local self-government units are obliged to provide urgent enrollment of child victims of violence in schools or kindergarten in their territory without requiring consent from both parents.

Multisectoral expert team

Article 55

(1) In cases when there is knowledge of endangerment of the life and health of women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence and family members, a safety plan for assistance is prepared by a multisectoral expert team.
(2) The competent social work center, according to the residence, that is, place of stay of the victim, forms a multisectoral expert team from paragraph (1) of this article which is composed of professional persons from the social work center, competent police station and health institution, with the aim of undertaking coordinated activities in providing assistance to the victim in accordance with her needs.
(3) In the work of the multisectoral expert team from paragraph (2) of this article, representatives of associations participate, who may propose and implement measures and activities from the safety plan for assistance to the victim.
(4) In case when there is knowledge of endangerment of the life and health of a child, the multisectoral expert team in the safety plan for assistance to the parent provides also measures and activities that should be implemented for assistance to the child.

Article 56

All measures and services for victims of gender-based and domestic violence that are provided by this law, which are available to citizens of the Republic of North Macedonia, are equally available to refugees, asylum seekers, stateless persons and persons without documents or personal identification, regardless of the procedure for regulating their status.

VIII. URGENT AND TEMPORARY PROTECTION MEASURES

Urgent measure

Article 57

(1) For the purpose of removing immediate and serious danger to the life and physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of her family, an urgent protection measure is issued, namely removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home, upon proposal of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, without the consent of the victim.
(2) The proposal for issuing an urgent protection measure, the Ministry of Internal Affairs submits to the competent court after the risk assessment made under Article 50 paragraph (2) of this law and the police report from Article 49 paragraph (7) of this law.
(3) The urgent protection measure from paragraph (1) of this article is issued for a duration of at least ten and at most up to 30 days.

Temporary protection measures

Article 58

(1) For the purpose of stopping violence, removing the consequences of committed violence and undertaking effective measures toward the perpetrator of violence, for the purpose of eliminating the causes for repeated commission of violence, the court may issue to the perpetrator of gender-based violence against women and victims of domestic violence the following temporary protection measures:
1) prohibition to threaten to commit violence;
2) prohibition to mistreat, harass, telephone, contact or otherwise directly or indirectly communicate with the victim;
3) prohibition to approach at a distance less than 100 meters from the residence, school, workplace or specific place regularly visited by the victim;
4) removal from the home regardless of ownership for a duration of ten to 30 days;
5) prohibition to possess firearms or other weapons or to have them confiscated;
6) obligation to return items that are necessary for satisfying the daily needs of the victim and the family;
7) mandatory legal maintenance of the family;
8) mandatory attendance of counseling for perpetrators of violence against women or domestic violence;
9) mandatory treatment of the perpetrator if he uses alcohol, drugs and other psychotropic substances or has a mental illness;
10) the perpetrator is obliged to compensate the medical and other costs arising from the violence, and
11) any other measure that the court deems necessary to ensure the safety and well-being of the victim and other family members.
(2) In proceedings in which the court decides on temporary protection measures, the provisions of the Law on Civil Procedure apply, unless otherwise regulated by this law.

Article 59

(1) The Ministry of Internal Affairs is obliged to submit a proposal for issuing an urgent protection measure from Article 57 of this law to the competent court for the purpose of removing immediate and serious danger to the life and physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of her family.

(2) The victim may submit a proposal for issuing a temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law to the competent court directly or through the social work center at her request.
(3) The social work center may submit a proposal for issuing a temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law to the competent court with the consent of the victim.
(4) The parent or guardian may submit a proposal for issuing a temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law, on behalf of a minor child and a person with limited or revoked legal capacity, as well as for a person over whom parental rights have been extended.
(5) The social work center is obliged to submit to the court a proposal for issuing a temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law, on behalf of children and legally incapacitated persons whenever the parent, guardian or legal representative fails to do so and without their consent.
(6) The social work center in the proposal to the court for issuing a temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law, for a child may propose that the temporary protection measure also apply to the parent with whom the child lives, in cases when this is required by the interests of the child for his protection and safety.

Article 60

The social work center with the proposal for issuing a temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law, submits findings and opinion of an expert team in which it also gives a proposal for issuing a temporary measure/s depending on the established needs for protection of the victim.

Article 61

A proposal for issuing a temporary measure may be submitted regardless of whether criminal proceedings are being conducted.

Article 62

(1) The temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law may last at least three months and at most up to one year.
(2) If violence continues after the expiration of the period for which the temporary protection measure was imposed within the period of one year from paragraph (1) of this article, the victim or the social work center may submit a request for extension of the measure(s).
(3) After the expiration of one year from the imposed temporary protection measure from paragraph (1) of this article, in the event that violence against women and domestic violence occurs again, the victim or the social work center submits a new proposal to the court.

Article 63

(1) The victim or the social work center may submit a proposal to the competent court for revocation of the imposed temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law even before the expiration of the period for which the measure was imposed, if it is assessed that it has achieved the purpose for which it was imposed.
(2) The victim or the social work center may submit a proposal for modification of the imposed temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law or for its extension, if the imposed measure has not provided adequate protection to the victim or changed circumstances have occurred.

Imposition of temporary protection measures

Article 64

In proceedings in which the court decides on temporary protection measures, the provisions of the Law on Civil Procedure apply, unless otherwise determined by this law.

Article 65

(1) In proceedings for imposition of temporary protection measures for gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, a single judge decides.
(2) In the proceedings from paragraph (1) of this article of this law, the public is excluded from the hearing.
(3) By way of exception from paragraph (2) of this article, the judge may allow scientific and public workers who deal with problems of marriage and family, as well as persons proposed by the parties, to attend the hearing.

Article 66

(1) The court, immediately and at the latest within 24 hours of receiving the proposal from the Ministry of Internal Affairs and the police report, without holding a hearing, shall decide on the imposition of an urgent protection measure of removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home from Article 57 of this law.
(2) The court shall act immediately on the proposal of the victim or the social work center for imposition of a temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law, and at the latest within seven days from the day of receipt of the request shall issue a decision.
(3) The court within 24 hours without holding a hearing shall decide on the imposition of an urgent protection measure from Article 57 of this law, when there is reasonable suspicion of serious danger to the life and health of the victim or a family member, based on an expert finding and opinion of the social work center.

Article 67

(1) The hearing is held in the presence of the victim, the opponent (the perpetrator of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence) and a representative from the social work center, when it is the submitter of the proposal for imposition of a temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law.
(2) When the victim is a child and/or a person with limited or revoked legal capacity, at the hearing they are represented by the parent who is not the opponent in the proceedings or the guardian.
(3) The summons for holding the hearing is delivered by the court to the opponent within two days from the day of receipt of the proposal.
(4) At the court's assessment, other persons may also be summoned to the hearing.
(5) By way of exception from paragraph (1) of this article, when there is reasonable suspicion of serious danger to the life and health of the victim or a family member, the court may impose an urgent protection measure from Article 57 of this law, in the absence of the opponent and based on an expert finding and opinion of the social work center, if other evidence has not been obtained.

Article 68

The court, upon proposal of the social work center, or of the woman victim of gender-based violence and the victim of domestic violence, may extend, revoke or modify the imposed temporary protection measure(s) from Article 58 of this law.

Article 69

In the decision by which the urgent protection measure from Article 57 of this law and the temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law are imposed, the court shall oblige the perpetrator of gender-based violence against women and the victim of domestic violence to respect the court decision.

Article 70

(1) The decision on the imposed urgent protection measure of removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home from Article 57 of this law, the court within six hours of issuing the decision delivers to the competent police station at the place of enforcement, which immediately and at the latest within 12 hours of receiving the decision, delivers it to the perpetrator of gender-based violence against women and other victim of domestic violence.
(2) In the event that delivery cannot be carried out within the period from paragraph (1) of this article, the court, upon receiving notification from the Ministry of Internal Affairs, publishes the decision on the bulletin board of the court, whereby the delivery is considered proper.
(3) The Ministry of Internal Affairs is obliged to remove the perpetrator from the home within two hours, counted from the moment when the decision on the imposed urgent protection measure of removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home from Article 57 of this law is served, in the event that they do not want to leave the home voluntarily.

Article 71

(1) A copy of the decision on the urgent protection measure of removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home from Article 57 of this law, the court delivers to the public prosecutor and the social work center.
(2) A copy of the decision on imposition of a temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law, the court delivers to the parties, the social work center and the institution competent for enforcement of the temporary protection measure.

Article 72

(1) An appeal against a decision of the basic court on an imposed urgent protection measure of removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home from Article 57 of this law or temporary protection measure(s) from Article 58 of this law may be filed within three days from the day of receipt of the decision.
(2) An appeal filed against the court's decision does not delay its enforcement.

Enforcement of urgent and temporary protection measures

Article 73

(1) The Ministry of Internal Affairs enforces the imposed urgent protection measure of removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home from Article 57 of this law and the temporary protection measures from Article 58 paragraph (1) points 1), 2), 3), 4) and
5) of this law, the Ministry of Health enforces the imposed temporary protection measure from Article 58 paragraph (1) point 9) of this law, the counseling center for perpetrators of domestic violence enforces the imposed temporary protection measure from Article 58 paragraph
(1) point 8) of this law, and the enforcement of the imposed temporary protection measures from Article 58 paragraph (1) points 6), 7), 10) and 11) of this law is carried out in accordance with the law.
(2) The counseling center for perpetrators of gender-based violence and domestic violence enforces a court decision for protective supervision in the part of the determined obligation for the convicted person for a criminal offense of gender-based violence and domestic violence to attend a program for working with convicts for criminal offenses committed in the commission of gender-based violence and domestic violence and notifies the court of the fulfillment of the obligation.

Article 74

(1) The manner of execution of the imposed urgent protection measure of removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home from Article 57 of this law and the temporary protection measures from Article 58 paragraph (1) points 1), 2), 3), 4) and 5) of this law shall be prescribed by the Minister of Internal Affairs.
(2) The manner of execution of the temporary protection measure from Article 58 paragraph (1) point 9) of this law shall be prescribed by the Minister of Health.
(3) The manner of execution of the imposed temporary protection measure from Article 55 paragraph (1) point 8) of this law shall be prescribed by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy.

Article 75

(1) The social work centre shall monitor the execution of the imposed temporary protection measure/s from Article 58 of this law.
(2) For the monitoring of the temporary protection measures from paragraph (1) of this article, the social work centre shall cooperate with citizens, legal entities, institutions and associations.
(3) The social work centre shall inform the court of the course and effects of the execution of the imposed temporary protection measure/s from paragraph (1) of this article.

Article 76

(1) The court may request notification from the Ministry of Internal Affairs on the manner of implementation and compliance with the imposed urgent protection measure of removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home from Article 57 of this law.
(2) The court may request notification from the social work centre on the implementation of the imposed temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law during the monitoring period.

Article 77

The victim shall inform the social work centre of non-compliance with and/or violation of the imposed temporary protection measure/s from Article 58 of this law in proceedings upon his/her request.

Article 78

(1) The Ministry of Internal Affairs is obliged to immediately inform the court of non-compliance with or violation of the imposed urgent protection measure from Article 57 of this law.
(2) The social work centre is obliged to immediately inform the court of non-compliance with or violation of the imposed temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law.

Article 79

The Ministry of Internal Affairs, when an urgent protection measure from Article 57 of this law is violated in the part concerning the prohibition of approaching the home, shall submit a proposal to the public prosecutor for the determination of precautionary measures, in accordance with the Law on Criminal Procedure.

Article 80

(1) The social work centre is obliged to file a criminal complaint against the perpetrator for non-compliance with the court decision on the imposed temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law.
(2) The social work centre is obliged to file a criminal complaint against the perpetrator for non-compliance with the court decision on the imposed temporary protection measure from Article 58 of this law, whenever the violence is committed against a child or in the presence of a child.
(3) The public prosecutor is obliged to inform the social work centre in writing of the initiated criminal proceedings, following the criminal complaint from paragraphs (1) and (2) of this article.

Article 81

The manner of implementation and the manner of monitoring of the imposed temporary protection measures from Article 58 of this law shall be prescribed by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy.

IX. JUDICIAL PROTECTION

Judicial protection of victims

Article 82

(1) Victims have the right to judicial protection, in civil proceedings and criminal proceedings before locally and materially competent courts.
(2) Victims have the right to special measures of procedural protection in accordance with the Law on Criminal Procedure and the Law on Justice for Children.

Claim for establishing liability for failure to act with due diligence

Article 83

(1) The victim has the right to file a claim before a civil court for establishing liability for failure to act with due diligence of the subjects from Article 4 of this law.
(2) In the claim from paragraph (1) of this article, the following may be asserted:
1) to establish the liability of the defendant for failure to act with due diligence established by this law;
2) to award compensation for damages to the victim due to failure to act with due diligence on the part of the defendant and
3) to oblige the defendant to undertake actions for the protection of the victim in accordance with the provisions and deadlines established by this law.
(3) In the proceedings, the provisions of the Law on Civil Procedure shall apply accordingly, unless otherwise regulated by this law.

Local jurisdiction

Article 84

In all civil proceedings for the protection of victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence, locally competent, in addition to the court with general local jurisdiction, is also the court in whose territory is the place of residence, registered temporary residence of the plaintiff or the seat of the accommodation centre where the victim is accommodated.

Burden of proof

Article 85

The plaintiff who claims that in accordance with the provisions of this law the principle of due diligence has been violated is obliged to present the facts that make the claim probable and then the burden of proof passes to the defendant to prove that he/she acted with due diligence.

Amicable settlement of disputes

Article 86

In proceedings relating to the protection of victims, the court may not refer to mandatory amicable settlement of the dispute.

Compensation of victims

Article 87

(1) Victims have the right to compensation for material and non-material damage in accordance with law.
(2) A victim who cannot collect his/her property claim awarded by a final court judgment from the perpetrator, may request that it be paid from a state fund in accordance with law.
(3) Victims are exempt from payment of all costs of proceedings for compensation for damages until the final collection of the property claim in accordance with law.

X. SERVICES FOR WOMEN VICTIMS OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AND DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

General services for victims

Article 88

(1) Women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence have access to health and social services.
(2) Officials in institutions that provide health and social services are obliged to give them support and assistance and to refer them to other appropriate specialized service providers.

Specialized services for victims

Article 89

(1) Specialized services for women victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence are services that respond to the specific needs of victims of different types of violence.
(2) The specialized services from paragraph (1) of this article are provided without compensation in a manner that protects the privacy, dignity and reputation of the victims.
(3) The specialized services from paragraph (1) of this article are available to all victims including their children regardless of the victim's willingness to testify in proceedings against the perpetrator.

(4) The specialized services from paragraph (1) of this article are financed from funds from the Budget of the Republic of North Macedonia, from the budgets of local self-government units and other sources of financing.

Information and referral services

Article 90

(1) The information and referral service is provided through an S.O.S. line.
(2) The S.O.S. line provides 24-hour accurate, timely and confidential information on the protection of victims, available services and telephone counselling to women victims of violence and victims of domestic violence.
(3) The service from paragraph (1) of this article is provided by social service providers in accordance with the Law on Social Protection.

Counselling service

Article 91

(1) The counselling service includes psycho-social support, counselling and treatment of women victims of gender-based violence, children victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence.
(2) The counselling service for children victims of abuse, neglect and violence is provided through implementation of an individual plan prepared by the social work centre in accordance with the Law on Social Protection.
(3) The services for children victims of criminal offences from paragraph (2) of this article are provided in accordance with the Law on Justice for Children.
(4) Within the framework of the counselling service, victims may also receive referral and/or accompaniment to institutions or associations in accordance with the Law on Social Protection.

Temporary accommodation service

Article 92

(1) The temporary accommodation service provides care in a temporary accommodation centre when there is knowledge of a real threat to the life and health of the victim or in the absence of other care resources for the following categories of persons:
1) women victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence and their children;
2) women victims of sexual violence and rape;
3) victims of trafficking in human beings and
4) victims of violence due to sexual orientation and gender identity.
(2) Emergency accommodation is provided in an acute situation of violence when there is a serious real threat to the life and health of the victim for a duration of 24 to 72 hours.
(3) In addition to care in the centre from paragraph (1) of this article, victims receive protection, psycho-social assistance and support, treatment for overcoming trauma, legal counselling, inclusion in active employment measures for the victim with the aim of inclusion in the labour market and achieving financial independence and other services depending on the needs of the victim in accordance with law.
(4) The temporary accommodation service may last a maximum of three months with the possibility of extension up to a maximum of one year.

Article 93

The specialised services from articles 90, 91 and 92 of this law are provided by the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy and other providers of social services in accordance with the norms and standards for provision of social services in accordance with the Law on Social Protection.

Service for protection and support of women victims of sexual violence and rape

Article 94

(1) The service for protection and support of women victims of sexual violence and rape is carried out within the referral centre for victims of sexual violence.
(2) In the health institution from paragraph (1) of this article, identification, assessment, medical examination, psychological crisis support, referral of women victims of sexual violence and rape is provided.
(3) The service from paragraph (1) of this article is provided in accordance with a Standard Operating Procedure adopted by the Minister of Health.

Service for long-term treatment of women victims of sexual violence and rape

Article 95

(1) The service for long-term treatment of women victims of sexual violence and rape is provided in a centre for long-term counselling and psychotherapy for women victims of sexual violence and rape.
(2) The service from paragraph (1) of this article includes individual and/or group psychotherapy, psychosocial counselling, connection with other general and specialised services, accompaniment to institutions and organisations and representation of victims in other institutions and organisations.
(3) The service from paragraph (1) of this article is provided in accordance with a Standard Operating Procedure adopted by the Minister of Health.

Article 96

The specialised services from articles 94 and 95 of this law are provided by the Ministry of Health in accordance with regulations in the field of health protection.

Free legal aid service

Article 97

(1) The free legal aid service is provided in accordance with law.
(2) Associations may provide a free legal aid service for women victims of violence and victims of domestic violence financed also from other sources.
Service providers

Article 98

The specialised services may be provided by providers of specialised services, namely: institutions in the social protection system, health institutions, associations, local self-government units, domestic and foreign legal entities and natural persons, under conditions determined by law.

Reintegration of victims of violence

Article 99

(1) The subjects from articles 11 and 24 of this law are obliged within the framework of their competences to take care of the process of reintegration of victims of violence through recognition of victims as a particularly vulnerable category and development of programmes and measures for assistance and support based on their needs.
(2) The programme for reintegration of victims of violence includes models for temporary housing, psychological counselling with mentoring, various types of financial assistance specifically intended for women victims of violence, opportunities for education and training in various fields, as well as employment measures determined by law.
(3) The employment measures and other measures for assistance and support from paragraph (1) of this article should be adapted according to the specific needs of the victims.
(4) The programme for reintegration of victims of violence is adopted by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy.

XII. MISDEMEANOUR PROVISIONS

Article 101

(1) A fine in the amount of 800 to 1,000 euros in denar equivalent shall be imposed for a misdemeanour on a legal entity that fails to act in accordance with articles 41, 45, 46, 47, 48,
49, 50, 51, 54 and 55 paragraphs (2) and (4), 57 paragraph (1), 59 paragraphs (1), (3), (5) and (6), 73 paragraph (1), 75,
78, 79 and 80 paragraphs (1) and (2) of this law.
(2) A fine in the amount of 100 to 500 euros in denar equivalent shall be imposed for the misdemeanours from paragraph (1) of this article on the responsible person in the legal entity.
(3) A fine in the amount of 50 to 250 euros in denar equivalent shall be imposed for the misdemeanours from paragraph (1) of this article on the official person.

Article 102

(1) For the misdemeanors established in Article 101 of this law, the competent inspector, before submitting a request to initiate misdemeanor proceedings, is obliged to offer the responsible person or a person authorized by them a settlement procedure by issuing a misdemeanor payment order in accordance with the Law on Misdemeanors.

(2) When the perpetrator of the misdemeanor agrees to the settlement procedure, the inspector compiles a record and issues a misdemeanor payment order to the responsible person or a person authorized by them.
(3) The record and the payment order are signed by the inspector and the perpetrator of the misdemeanor. Upon receipt of the misdemeanor payment order, the perpetrator is obliged to pay the fine imposed in the misdemeanor payment order within eight days from the day of receipt of the misdemeanor payment order.
(4) When a legal entity appears as the perpetrator of a misdemeanor, the record and the misdemeanor payment order are signed by the responsible person or a person authorized by them.
(5) The perpetrator who pays the fine within the deadline from paragraph (3) of this article will pay only half of the imposed fine, which right is instructed in the legal instruction.
(6) If the perpetrator does not pay the fine within the deadline from paragraph (3) of this article, the inspector will submit a request to initiate misdemeanor proceedings to the competent authority.
(7) In the settlement procedure, the fine in the misdemeanor payment order is imposed in a fixed amount prescribed by law, and if the fine is prescribed in a range, the minimum prescribed amount for the misdemeanor is imposed.
(8) In the procedure that will end with payment of a misdemeanor payment order, no costs of the procedure are paid.
(9) If the perpetrator does not agree to be issued a misdemeanor payment order, this will be noted in the record of the established misdemeanor, and the inspector submits a request to initiate misdemeanor proceedings before the competent court.

Article 103

The determination of the amount of the fine for legal entities is carried out in accordance with the Law on Misdemeanors.

Article 104

For the misdemeanors from Article 101 of this law, misdemeanor proceedings are conducted and misdemeanor sanctions are imposed by a competent court.

XIII. TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

Article 105

The National Coordinating Body from Article 15 of this law will be formed within three months from the day of entry into force of this law.

Article 106

Proceedings initiated until the day of commencement of application of this law will be completed according to the provisions of this law.

Article 107

Laws that contain provisions relating to prevention and protection from violence against women and domestic violence will be harmonized with this law within six months from the day of entry into force of this law.

Article 108

The by-laws provided for by this law will be adopted within three months from the day of entry into force of this law.

Article 109

On the day of commencement of application of this law, the Law on Prevention, Combating and Protection from Domestic Violence ("Official Gazette of the Republic of Macedonia" numbers 138/14, 33/15 and 150/15) ceases to be valid.

Article 110

On the day of commencement of application of this law, the mandate of the president and members of the National Coordinating Body for Combating and Protection from Domestic Violence ceases.

Article 111

The provisions of Article 20 point 2) and Article 87 paragraphs (2) and (3) of this law will begin to be applied within two years from the day of entry into force of this law.

Article 112

This law enters into force on the eighth day from the day of publication in the "Official Gazette of the Republic of North Macedonia", and will begin to be applied three months from the day of entry into force of this law.
