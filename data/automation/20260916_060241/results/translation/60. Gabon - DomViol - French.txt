OFFICIAL GAZETTE NO. 131 OF 16 SEPTEMBER 2021
Decision No. 026/CC of 30/07/2021 relating to the application submitted by the Prime Minister, seeking constitutional review of law no. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women

IN THE NAME OF THE GABONESE PEOPLE; THE CONSTITUTIONAL COURT;
Having regard to the application registered at the Registry of the Court on 14 June 2021, under no. 008/GCC, by which the Prime Minister referred to the Constitutional Court, for the purposes of constitutional review, law no. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women;
Having regard to the Constitution;
Having regard to organic law no. 9/91 of 26 September 1991 on the Constitutional Court, as amended by organic law no. 004/2018 of 30 July 2018;
Having regard to the rules of procedure of the Constitutional Court no. 035/CC/06 of 10 November 2006, as amended by the rules of procedure of the Constitutional Court no. 047/CC/2018 of 20 July 2018;

The Rapporteur having been heard;
l-Whereas by the aforementioned application, the Prime Minister submitted to the Constitutional Court, for the purposes of constitutional review, law no. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women;
On article ler
2-	Whereas article ler of law no. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women states:
"This law, adopted in application of article 47 of the Constitution, aims to establish in the Gabonese Republic measures to protect women against all forms of violence and discrimination, to prevent, prosecute and eliminate them in all environments, particularly the family environment.
It also aims to promote real equality between men and women.";
3-	Whereas the use in paragraph 2 of the said article ler of the adverbial phrase "par ailleurs" suggests that the promotion of equality between men and women constitutes a secondary objective whereas, in addition to the protection of women against all forms of violence and discrimination, the law under examination aims equally at promoting equality between men and women; that to be declared in conformity with the Constitution, article ler must be reformulated as follows:
"Article 1er nouveau: This law, adopted in application of article 47 of the Constitution, aims to establish in the Gabonese Republic measures to protect women against all forms of violence and discrimination, to prevent, prosecute and eliminate them in all environments, particularly the family environment.
It also aims to promote equality between men and women.";
On article 2
4-	Whereas article 2 stipulates in its first bullet point: "For the purposes of this law, the following terms mean: woman: any person of female sex or gender, of any age";
5-	Whereas this article 2 provides definitions of both woman and all forms of violence and discrimination; that as such, woman is notoriously defined as any person of female sex; that by extending the definition of woman to female gender, the legislator introduces in this case another notion that has not been recognized by the Constitution, particularly in its article ler point 14; that consequently, to be declared in conformity with the Constitution, article 2 first bullet point must be reformulated as follows:
"Article 2 nouveau: For the purposes of this law, the following terms mean: woman: any person of female sex, of any age";
6-	Whereas, on bullet points 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8 of the same article 2, the legislator merely provides definitions of violence without specifying that it concerns violence against women; that to be declared in conformity with the Constitution, bullet points 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8, relating to physical violence, psychological or moral violence, sexual violence, patrimonial violence and economic violence, must be worded as follows:
"- physical violence against women: any act causing or likely to cause harm to the physical integrity or security of the woman or to her life, regardless of its perpetrator or the means used to commit it; psychological or moral violence against women: any act, committed intentionally, causing or likely to cause harm to the psychological integrity of the woman, particularly through coercion, threats or control;
sexual violence against women: any act of violence, physical or psychological, which manifests itself in a sexual manner and is exercised in such a way as to affect the physical integrity or sexual dignity of the woman, regardless of the means used; patrimonial violence against women: any intentional act, negligence or wrongful omission affecting the subsistence of the woman or her patrimonial situation and consisting of transforming, removing, destroying, retaining or misappropriating objects, personal documents, goods and valuables, rights or economic resources intended to cover her needs and which may extend to damage caused to common or personal property of the woman, as well as any act of spoliation; economic violence against women: any act of domination or control consisting of depriving the woman of means, particularly financial, or preventing her from satisfying her needs or monitoring her economic activities with the aim of preventing her from achieving her financial autonomy.
There is also economic violence when any natural or legal person, abusing the state of dependence in which the woman finds herself in relation to them, obtains from her a commitment or waiver to which she would not have consented in the absence of such dependence and derives an abusive advantage therefrom";
7-	Whereas bullet point 10, still of article 2 of law no. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women, provides: "discrimination against women: any discrimination, exclusion or restriction, based on sex, which has the effect or purpose of compromising or destroying the recognition, enjoyment or exercise by women, regardless of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women, of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil and civic fields or in any other field";
8-	Whereas as stated, this provision creates confusion as to its understanding; that for better readability thereof, it should be rewritten as follows: "discrimination against women: any discrimination, exclusion or restriction, based on sex, which has the effect or purpose, in violation of equality between men and women, of compromising or destroying the recognition, enjoyment or exercise by women, regardless of their marital status, of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil and civic fields or in any other field";
On article 20
9-	Whereas article 20, third paragraph of law no. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women, prescribes: "Upon receipt of the application for a protection order, and at the latest within 2 days, the judge summons, by any appropriate means, for a hearing, the applicant party and the defendant party, assisted, where applicable, by a lawyer, as well as the Public Prosecutor for an opinion. The hearing of the applicant party and the defendant party must take place separately and no mediation may be envisaged or proposed in the context of an application for a protection order.";
10-	Whereas as drafted, the provisions of the third paragraph of article 20 are imprecise as to the role of the Public Prosecutor in the procedure for issuing the protection order; that for better readability and to be declared in conformity with the Constitution, paragraph 3 of article 20 of the text under examination must be rewritten as follows:
"Article 20 nouveau: Upon receipt of the application for a protection order, and at the latest within 2 days, the judge summons, by any appropriate means, for a hearing, the applicant party and the defendant party, assisted, where applicable, by a lawyer, in the presence of the Public Prosecutor. The hearing of the applicant party and the defendant party must take place separately and no mediation may be envisaged or proposed in the context of an application for a protection order. v ,
On article 28 alinêa 3

11-	Whereas article 28 provides in its paragraph 3 "The salaried person or public official who is a victim of violence may benefit, if their physical or psychological condition justifies the absences referred to in paragraph ler above and after the favourable opinion of the occupational physician, from daily allowances for incapacity for work calculated on the same basis as daily allowances for occupational accidents.";
12-	Whereas it is a general rule of law that criminal liability is individual, that is to say that it is the perpetrator of the offence who is sanctioned and must ensure the reparation of damages arising from the offence they have committed; that it follows that the aforementioned provisions of paragraph 3 of article 28, by transferring to the employer the criminal liability for violence committed by a third party on their employee, contravene the general principle of law set out above; that to be declared in conformity with the Constitution, paragraph 3 of article 28 must be reformulated as follows: "The salaried person or public official who is a victim of violence, committed either by the employer or within the enterprise, may benefit, if their physical or psychological condition justifies the absences referred to in paragraph ler above, and after the favourable opinion of the occupational physician, from daily allowances for incapacity for work under the conditions and procedures established by regulation.";
On article 36
13-	Whereas article 36 of the law under examination prescribes "Rape constitutes any act of sexual penetration, of any nature whatsoever, committed on the person of another, with violence, coercion, threats, surprise or deceit regardless of the nature of the relationship between the aggressor and their victim, including if they are married. If the victim is a minor under fifteen years of age, the absence of consent is always presumed.";
14-	Whereas this article, as worded, is limited to repeating the definition of the offence concerned as it has already been given in chapter ler of the law under examination, without providing for the sanctions to be applied to perpetrators of rape; that for better applicability of the said article, it is necessary to complete it by adding two other paragraphs; that consequently, article 36 must henceforth read as follows: "Article 36 nouveau: Rape constitutes any act of sexual penetration, of any nature whatsoever, committed on the person of another, with violence, coercion, threats, surprise or deceit, regardless of the nature of the relationship between the aggressor and their victim, including if they are married. If the victim is a minor under fifteen years of age, the absence of consent is always presumed.
The perpetrator of rape is punished by fifteen years of criminal imprisonment and a fine of up to 50,000,000 francs.
The person guilty of rape is punished by life imprisonment and a fine of up to 50,000,000 francs: when the acts have resulted in the death of the victim; when the rape is preceded, accompanied or followed by torture or acts of barbarity; when it has resulted in mutilation or permanent disability.";
On article 49
15-	Whereas article 49 stipulates: "The provisions of article 18 and chapter 5 of this law are integrated into the Penal Code by a law amending the Penal Code.";
16-	Whereas it is unequivocal that the provisions of law no. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women, which is a special law, apply directly, without it being necessary to refer to the Penal Code; that it follows that article 49 is superfluous and must therefore be removed from the text

17-	Whereas following the deletion of the said article 49, law no. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women must be restructured and its other articles renumbered accordingly;

18-	Whereas all the other provisions of law no. 006/2021 under examination are not tainted by any unconstitutionality; that they should be declared in conformity with the Constitution.
D E C I D E S:
Article ler: Articles ler, 2 and 20 of law no. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women are in conformity with the Constitution, subject to reformulating them as follows:
"Article ler: This law, adopted in application of article 47 of the Constitution, aims to establish in the Gabonese Republic measures to protect women against all forms of violence and discrimination, to prevent, prosecute and eliminate them in all environments, particularly the family environment.
It also aims to promote equality between men and women."
"Article 2: For the purposes of this law, the following terms mean: woman: any person of female sex, of any age; violence against women: any act of violence exercised on women and which is likely to result for them in damage or suffering of a physical, sexual, psychological or economic nature, including the threat of acts of violence, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty in public or private life; physical violence against women: any act causing or likely to cause harm to the physical integrity or security of the woman or to her life, regardless of its perpetrator or the means used to commit it; psychological or moral violence against women: any act, committed intentionally, causing or likely to cause harm to the psychological integrity of the woman, particularly through coercion, threats or control; sexual violence against women: any act of violence, physical or psychological, which manifests itself in a sexual manner and is exercised in such a way as to affect the physical integrity or sexual dignity of the woman, regardless of the means used; violence in the family environment: any act of physical, sexual, psychological, patrimonial or economic violence, which occurs within the family or household, committed by a member of a family against any other member of the family, whether the aggressor is or has been related to the victim by kinship, by blood or by marriage, or between former or current spouses or cohabitants or persons maintaining or having maintained a de facto relationship, regardless of whether the perpetrator of the offence shares or has shared the same domicile as the victim; patrimonial violence against women: any intentional act, negligence or wrongful omission affecting the subsistence of the woman or her patrimonial situation and consisting of transforming, removing, destroying, retaining or misappropriating objects, personal documents, goods and valuables, rights or economic resources intended to cover her needs and which may extend to damage caused to common or personal property of the woman, as well as any act of spoliation; economic violence against women: any act of domination or control consisting of depriving the woman of means, particularly financial, or preventing her from satisfying her needs or monitoring her economic activities with the aim of preventing her from achieving her financial autonomy.
There is also economic violence when any natural or legal person, abusing the state of dependence in which the woman finds herself in relation to them, obtains from her a commitment or waiver to which she would not have consented in the absence of such dependence and derives an abusive advantage therefrom; rape: rape constitutes any act of sexual penetration, of any nature whatsoever, committed on the person of another, with violence, coercion, threat, surprise or deceit, regardless of the nature of the relationship between

the aggressor and their victim, including if they are married. If the victim is a minor under 15 years of age, the absence of consent is always presumed; discrimination against women: any discrimination, exclusion or restriction, based on sex, which has the effect or purpose, in violation of equality between men and women, of compromising or destroying the recognition, enjoyment or exercise by women, regardless of their marital status, of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil and civic fields or in any other field; harassment: an act of harassment constitutes the act of imposing, repeatedly, remarks or behaviour which either infringe the dignity of the person by reason of their degrading or humiliating character, or create an intimidating, hostile or offensive situation against them; harmful traditional practices against women: any act, any practice, physical and/or moral, infringing the dignity, integrity and fundamental rights of women, based on and/or justified by traditional norms, usages, customs.";
"Article 20: The application for a protection order may be made by the victim themselves or their representative, the Public Prosecutor or the child protection delegate if the victim is a minor.
The issuance of a protection order is not conditional on the existence of a prior criminal complaint.
The testimony of the victim or a sworn statement on their part constitutes sufficient evidence for the issuance of a protection order and no independent evidence, emanating from a doctor or a third party, may be required.
Upon receipt of the application for a protection order, and at the latest within 2 days, the judge summons, by any appropriate means, for a hearing, the applicant party and the defendant party, assisted, where applicable, by a lawyer, in the presence of the Public Prosecutor. The hearing of the applicant party and the defendant party must take place separately and no mediation may be envisaged or proposed in the context of an application for a protection order."

Article 2

The provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 28 of Law No. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women are in conformity with the Constitution subject to reformulating them as follows: "The salaried person or public servant who is a victim of violence, committed either by the employer or within the enterprise, may benefit, if their physical or psychological condition justifies the absences referred to in paragraph 1 above, and after favorable opinion from the occupational physician, from daily allowances for incapacity to work under the conditions and procedures established by regulation."

Article 3

Article 36 of Law No. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women is supplemented and now reads as follows:
"Article 36: Constitutes rape, any act of sexual penetration, of whatever nature, committed on the person of another, with violence, constraint, threats, surprise or deceit, whatever the nature of the relations between the aggressor and their victim, including if they are married. If the victim is a minor under fifteen years of age, the absence of consent is always presumed.
The perpetrator of rape is punished by fifteen years of criminal imprisonment and a fine of up to 50,000,000 francs.
The person guilty of rape is punished by criminal imprisonment for life and a fine of up to 50,000,000 francs:when the acts have resulted in the death of the victim;when the rape is preceded, accompanied or followed by torture or acts of barbarity;when it has resulted in mutilation or permanent disability."

Article 4

The provisions of Article 49 of Law No. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women are censured and separated from the entire text. Following the deletion of this Article 49, the aforementioned law is restructured and its other articles renumbered accordingly.

Article 5

All other provisions of Law No. 006/2021 on the elimination of violence against women are in conformity with the Constitution.

Article 6

This decision shall be notified to the applicant, to the President of the Republic, to the President of the Senate, to the President of the National Assembly and published in the Official Journal of the Gabonese Republic or in a legal notices journal.
