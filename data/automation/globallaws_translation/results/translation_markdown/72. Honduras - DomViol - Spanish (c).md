# Equal Opportunities for Women Act

Equal Opportunities for Women Act

## TITLE I

OF THE OBJECT AND NATURE CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In Honduras all men and women are born free and equal in rights.

#### Article 2

The purpose of this Act is to integrate and coordinate the actions that the State and civil society must execute to eliminate all types of discrimination against women and to obtain equality between men and women before the law, prioritizing the areas of family, health, education, culture, media, environment, work, social security, credit, land, housing and participation in decision-making within power structures.

#### Article 3

For the purposes of this Act, discrimination against women means any distinction, exclusion, or restriction based on sex, which has the purpose or result of impairing or nullifying the recognition, enjoyment or exercise by women, regardless of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women, of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural and civil spheres or in any other aspect.

#### Article 4

To comply with this principle of non-discrimination against women, the following actions are established:
1)	The State guarantees equality and equity between men and women, in the design and application of public policies for the execution and coordination of programs and projects; and,

2)	Civil society must include the gender dimension in social dialogue forums to promote and encourage organizations that work for, with and on behalf of women.

#### Article 5

The State, through its competent bodies, is obliged to promote scientific research on equal opportunities and must create or improve the production, dissemination and evaluation of statistical records on the condition and situation of men and
women and their evolution in different areas of social life, in order to periodically establish equal opportunity practices.

#### Article 6

The State, civil society and women in particular, must ensure that the normative development of the principle of equal opportunities and its interpretation in the legal system is done with equity criteria, creating effective mechanisms that fully guarantee the rights of women.

#### Article 7

The National Women's Institute (INAM) is the state body responsible for the coordination, promotion, development and monitoring of public policy for the promotion of equal opportunities and treatment.

## TITLE II

OF EQUAL OPPORTUNITY RIGHTS

### CHAPTER I

EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES IN THE AREA OF FAMILY

#### Article 8

The State recognizes equal rights between men and women in their couple relationships, regardless of their marital status. The de facto union between persons legally capable of contracting marriage is recognized. The Law shall indicate the conditions for it to have the effects of civil marriage. Adoption is recognized as a legal form of building a family.

#### Article 9

The Family, as the first sphere of socialization, transmission and learning of models of identity, behaviors, attitudes and values, must constitute a space for generating equal rights and opportunities for its members.

#### Article 10

The State is obliged to promote within the family, through formal and alternative non-formal education, the redistribution of family responsibilities derived from the care and reproduction of the family, to comply with equal opportunities and the correct sense of division of labor.

#### Article 11

The State through the educational system and social media must disseminate the concept of shared responsibility within family life, with emphasis on the needs of working mothers or female heads of household.

#### Article 12

Women must ensure compliance with the provisions of the Family Code, regarding the personal and property rights of spouses or couples who have established de facto unions.

#### Article 13

The State has the obligation to help, prevent, combat, eradicate domestic and intra-family violence, providing comprehensive attention to the problem, as established
in Decree No. 132-97 dated September 11, 1997, which contains the "Law Against Domestic Violence" and the Penal Code.

### CHAPTER II

EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES IN THE AREA OF HEALTH AND ENVIRONMENT

#### Article 14.-The right to health protection is recognized

It is the duty of everyone to participate in the promotion and preservation of personal and community health.

The State shall maintain an adequate environment to protect the health of persons.

#### Article 15

The State through the Secretariat of State in the Office of Health must attend to women's health with a comprehensive approach and establish the interrelation in the aspects of information, promotion, prevention and care considering all stages of women's lives and not only their reproductive function.

#### Article 16

The gender approach is incorporated at the state and private level in physical and mental health programs and policies. To this effect, educational programs will be developed aimed at achieving full, responsible and comprehensive sexuality and with sufficient information about
sexually transmitted diseases, their prevention, symptoms and treatments.

#### Article 17

In the training of health professionals, universities and other educational institutions must take into account the specific needs of women, according to their psychological, physiological, social, cultural and age differences.

#### Article 18

All women health service providers, including those who work in the kitchen and cleaning areas, deserve greater attention in cases of wear and
fatigue from their professional or occupational practice.

#### Article 19

Women must exercise their reproductive rights and by mutual agreement with their partner, decide on the number of sons and daughters and the spacing of their pregnancies.

#### Article 20

The Secretariat of State in the Office of Health shall take appropriate measures for the prevention or adequate treatment of teenage pregnancy and its risk factors. Likewise, it shall take measures to prevent and attend to high-risk pregnancies in women of reproductive age.

#### Article 21

Pregnant women deprived of liberty by mandate of Law shall be subject to special treatment and the State shall provide the corresponding facilities.

#### Article 22

The State undertakes to incorporate and ensure the validity of the gender concept and promote the effective participation of women in the plans, programs and projects of the institutions responsible for promoting the development and conservation

of the environment, taking into
consideration the prevailing local conditions.

#### Article 23

The State and civil society commit to providing appropriate and accessible technology that responds to the needs of women in urban and rural areas, that
INAM Compendium of Laws on Women's Rights 5 contribute to the protection of the environment and to encourage their active participation in decision-making on projects in favor of their natural environment.

#### Article 24

The State, civil society, municipal corporations shall promote educational programs and projects that rescue and foster the experience and wisdom of women in the management and conservation of natural resources.

#### Article 25

The State, in coordination with civil society organizations, undertakes to promote and stimulate the design and implementation of projects with a gender focus, on the
intensive production of food, with a view toward the country's food self-sufficiency, taking into account the prevailing local conditions.

#### Article 26

Women shall have equal opportunity as men, to form part of the salaried workforce in irrigation and forestry programs, as well as in other programs and projects necessary to improve the quality of the urban and rural environment. The Secretariat of State in the Office of Labor and Social Security shall ensure its compliance.

### CHAPTER III

EQUALITY OF OPPORTUNITIES IN EDUCATION, CULTURE AND COMMUNICATION

#### Article 27

Education is an essential function of the State for the conservation, promotion and dissemination of culture, which must project its benefits to society without discrimination of any nature.
National education shall be secular and shall be based on the essential principles of democracy, shall instill and foster in students profound Honduran sentiments and must be directly linked to the country's economic and social development process.

#### Article 28

The State guarantees the integral and equal education of women and men, under the concept of shared responsibility, as the basis of society and shall assume the task of:

1)	Incorporating a non-sexist perspective into the educational processes of elaboration and transmission of knowledge, culture and information;
2)	Ensuring the principle of equality of opportunities and treatment prevails at all levels of the formal education system, as well as alternative non-formal education;

3)	Guaranteeing educational offerings without discrimination based on sex;
4)	Eliminating from texts used in the state and private education system, the stereotyped roles of men and women and, avoiding that the image of women continues to be used as the only resource to symbolize domestic tasks performed in the home;
5)	Promoting the diversification of school options both intellectual, as well as technical and scientific for men and women and ensuring equality of opportunity in access to INAM Compendium of Laws on Women's Rights 6 all these forms of teaching-learning;
6)	Facilitating women's work opportunities, preserving and strengthening the system of nurseries and childcare centers; and,
7)	Reviewing and modernizing educational legislation, eliminating from its regulations the division of labor by sex.

#### Article 29

The State shall train and update teaching personnel to optimize their work performance.

#### Article 30

The State shall promote bilingual education in ethnic groups and indigenous peoples, respecting their multicultural identity and preserving the authenticity of the native language.

#### Article 31

State and private educational institutions, at all levels, must eliminate discrimination based on gender, in aspects such as: Decisions on administration, access to training courses, participation in sports activities, advisorships and academic scholarships or study grants.

#### Article 32

The authorities of educational establishments are obligated to warn all personnel under their jurisdiction of the legal consequences arising from abuse and sexual harassment and when this occurs, to resolve it in accordance with the law.

#### Article 33

The State and civil society organizations must provide educational opportunities to women, promote and encourage them to participate in programs that train them in activities that allow them to join on equal terms with men, to contribute to the sustainable development of the nation.

#### Article 34

In the educational programs of the final years of basic and secondary education, population education content must be incorporated, emphasizing topics referring to sexuality and reproduction, and scientific information on pregnancy prevention and
sexually transmitted diseases.

#### Article 35

Pregnant students in educational centers shall be granted maternity leave, without jeopardizing the continuity of their education.

#### Article 36

Both the State and civil society are obligated to reduce the country's illiteracy, facilitating the incorporation of women into education and productive work fully.

#### Article 37

State social media are at the service of education and culture. Private media are obligated to contribute to the achievement of said purposes and to comply with the programming that specific laws order them. The State shall regulate this matter.

#### Article 38

The State guarantees the participation and initiative of women in cultural development, respecting the diversity of identities, values and experiences.

#### Article 39

The educational role of mass media must contribute significantly to the cultural change necessary to improve the social condition of women.

#### Article 40

It is the State's responsibility to safeguard the cultural and territorial heritage of indigenous peoples, developing training and awareness programs for their communities, aimed at eliminating discrimination against women within them.

#### Article 41

The State and civil society must command women and men who occupy positions of power, to show solidarity and work for the aspirations of all women of
all ethnicities and disadvantaged socio-economic strata of the country.

#### Article 42

In messages of all types disseminated by social media, the use of discriminatory and pejorative images of women must be eliminated and, preferably, greater spaces shall be allocated to inform about their rights and duties; as well as opportunities and advances in all fields of human knowledge, with emphasis on science and technology.

The State shall ensure faithful compliance with this provision.

#### Article 43

With the support of the State and civil society, women are obligated to enhance their participation as spokespersons on public issues, at the decision-making levels of the media and, as viewers thereof, shall demand high-level educational programming and the elimination of sexist violence.

### CHAPTER IV

EQUALITY OF OPPORTUNITIES IN WORK AND SOCIAL SECURITY

#### Article 44

The Secretariat of State in the Office of Labor and Social Security shall require that equal work receive equal pay, without any discrimination,

provided that the position, workday and conditions of efficiency and length of service are also equal.

#### Article 45

Women, like men, have the right to security of their economic means of subsistence, in case of temporary and/or permanent incapacity to work or obtain paid work.
The State shall take all necessary measures so that the Honduran Social Security Institute (IHSS) effectively fulfills the objectives for which it was created, reviewing its current coverage in order to expand it for the benefit of women in rural and urban areas throughout the
country to the extent possible.

#### Article 46

The State shall not permit any kind of discrimination based on gender or on the age of the man or woman, in order to nullify or alter equality of opportunities or
treatment in employment or training.

Employers are prohibited from requesting proof of pregnancy as a prerequisite to apply for employment.

#### Article 47

Female workers who are HIV/AIDS carriers shall have the right to have their job stability respected, in accordance with the laws of the country.

#### Article 48

Employers, both male and female, must provide equal opportunities under similar conditions to women, in the aspects of selection, employment, work assignment and promotion, as well as in training, education and capacity building; likewise prohibit gender discrimination in staff reductions and dismissals.

#### Article 49

The State shall ensure that companies and other workplaces hire women and men equitably under equal conditions of work and remuneration.

#### Article 50

Women who work in domestic service and who are not included in commercial, social and other comparable enterprises, shall be protected by the Honduran Social Security Institute (I.H.S.S.), and subject to a special regime, which shall be regulated by the Secretariat of State in the Offices of Labor and Social Security, within six (6) months from the effective date of this Law.

#### Article 51

The State and social actors shall promote the effective protection of women during their pregnancy and the postnatal period, adopting strict measures aimed at eliminating discrimination in employment and ensuring their job stability and prohibiting the performance of certain types of work that affect their health.

#### Article 52

Male and female workers in the manufacturing industry and in agro-industrial enterprises, shall enjoy all the benefits and social security enjoyed by the formal sector.

#### Article 53

To set the amount of salary in each type of work, the intensity and quality of the work, climate and living conditions and the length of service of the male or female worker within the same company must be taken into account.

#### Article 54

The female worker who adopts a minor under five (5) years of age, shall have the right to the same post-partum leave as a woman in a state of pregnancy. This leave shall begin from the day the minor is delivered to her and for which she must present the corresponding judicial resolution.

#### Article 55

Employers, both male and female, are prohibited from advertising their job offers by any means and specifying as a requirement the sex, age, religion or marital status of the person; except when by the nature of the work or employment it requires special characteristics. In this case, the male or female employer must previously have authorization from the Labor Inspectorate to publish said notice.

#### Article 56

The salaried work of women outside the home does not have to interfere with their family responsibility, the same situation must prevail for the work of men, who are also obliged to share equally the work in the home.

#### Article 57

The State shall ensure an effective incorporation of women into production and guarantee their participation in the preparation of national development plans, in their access to credit programs, land and technology, as well as a fair and direct form of the benefits and opportunities offered by sustainable development, such as training and instruction in the different areas of the economy.

#### Article 58

The State through the Secretariat of State in the Office of Finance, is obliged to design credit and technical assistance mechanisms with local resources and from international organizations, to stimulate self-management and business development of women.

#### Article 59

Employers, both male and female, are obliged to promote the adaptation of spaces that allow the satisfaction of basic needs in the workplace (sanitary services, dining halls, pantries, infirmary and child care centers).
As for child care centers, their adaptation shall be mandatory by the employer who has in their service more than 30 female workers, with the contribution of parents according to their economic capacity, with the purpose of caring for children under seven (7) years of age, children of male and female workers.

#### Article 60

Sexual harassment committed by the employer or head of the State or of a private company, empowers the female worker or public servant as the case may be, to terminate the employment relationship without prior notice and without responsibility on her part, retaining the right to legal benefits and compensation as in the case of unjust dismissal. When the one who commits the harassment is a worker, he must be immediately dismissed without responsibility for the employer or state institution.

#### Article 61

The State and the private sector shall promote the participation and full integration of women in the business area until achieving for them sources of financing and opening of new markets, in order to maintain and increase their participation, eliminating discriminatory legal and administrative obstacles, which prevent them from enjoying real access to credit, on equal terms through loan guarantees, technical advice and marketing development services.

#### Article 62

The State must recognize and encourage women, both in their role as users and as agents of change in science and technology, in order to contribute to developing in them technological and business skills.

#### Article 63

The Central Government as well as the municipal and organized civil society, shall provide the necessary resources so that through the respective agencies, women are trained for the exercise of traditional and non-traditional activities, likewise in micro, small, medium and large industry, with innovative approaches in training matters, for which they must prepare and disseminate learning material and train female instructors.

#### Article 64

The State and civil society jointly shall support traditional crafts, home agro-industries and small industrial and agricultural activities, carried out by women through the granting of credits, training services, marketing opportunities and technological guidance.

#### Article 65

Production cooperatives must encourage women to actively participate in the establishment and administration of micro, small or medium enterprises, that allow them to improve their standard of living until they become owners.

#### Article 66

The State through the municipal government shall improve the infrastructure and management of markets, social services and transportation, to increase the efficiency, safety and income of women entrepreneurs and reduce their workload and risks to their health and that of their family.

#### Article 67

It is the responsibility of the State to promote the equal presence of women at all levels of public administration, contributing to eliminate discrimination against women in access and promotion within it.

#### Article 68

The State and civil society shall provide social coverage to all female workers, whether from the formal or informal sector, such principle shall be developed in the respective Regulation.

### CHAPTER V

EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES IN LAND TENURE, CREDIT AND HOUSING.

#### Article 69

Women are recognized, as are men, the right to decent and proper housing.

The State shall formulate and execute social interest housing programs, offering greater flexibility in terms of requirements for access to housing, in the case of women heads of household who lack the support of a household partner.

The Law shall regulate the ownership and rental of housing and premises, the use of urban land and their construction, in accordance with the general interest.

#### Article 70

Internal or external credits and loans that the State obtains for housing purposes shall be regulated by a Law for the benefit of the final user of the credit, taking into account the parameters of priority and accessibility that facilitate the acquisition of housing in a rational and equitable manner.

#### Article 71

The State shall evaluate the housing needs of women, encouraging the design and implementation of innovative projects that promote women's access to financing services and means. It shall repeal laws or administrative practices that restrict or limit women's possibility of acquiring housing as owners or as tenants.

#### Article 72

Women and private development organizations must participate in housing construction projects and infrastructure works, benefit from them on an equal footing with men, participate in the design and construction technology, and in the administration and maintenance of the facilities.

#### Article 73

Family patrimony consists of urban or rural real estate property acquired by final users of social interest programs or land titling programs that are financed directly or indirectly by the State. Such property must be registered in the Property Registry in the name of both spouses or of persons living under a common-law union regime, registered in the Civil Registry or even when it is not legally recognized.
In case of dissolution of the bond, the interest of minor or dependent children shall be satisfied first; once their interest is satisfied, said patrimony shall correspond in equal parts to the aforementioned spouses, which shall be liquidated in accordance with the provisions of the Family Code.

#### Article 74

Preference shall be given to women heads of household in obtaining bank loans for housing and when she belongs to the peasant sector without any discrimination, she shall enjoy the benefits of the Agrarian Reform Law on equal terms with men.

### CHAPTER VI

EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES IN PARTICIPATION AND DECISION-MAKING WITHIN THE POWER STRUCTURE

#### Article 75

The State shall guarantee participatory democracy and the full exercise of citizen rights for both men and women, with equal opportunities.

#### Article 76

The State through the National Electoral Tribunal shall guarantee that in the internal structures of political parties, there is no discrimination that excludes or limits the participation of women.

#### Article 77

At the state level, social organizations shall be strengthened, both at the national, departmental, municipal and local levels, and women shall be encouraged to exercise true leadership in these instances, and efforts must be made to promote and supervise the incorporation of women in the various Boards of Directors, progressively, until their incorporation on an equal footing is achieved.

#### Article 78

The State has the obligation to develop training and capacity-building programs to support the emergence and consolidation of women's organizations and to promote their active participation in social, political, economic, business, cooperative, union, religious and student organizations.

#### Article 79

The State must incorporate women's organizations in instances of state, municipal and communal participation, incorporating them in the search for solutions to problems and specific interests and visions of the State, in all instances of formulation of social, national and international agreements or in resolution of conflicts of any nature.

#### Article 80

Civil society shall promote the equal participation of women in decision-making processes of community groups, non-governmental organizations, unions, cooperatives, professional associations and others, until equitable incorporation in Boards of Directors is achieved.

#### Article 81

The State through the respective institutions shall take effective measures to achieve an equitable distribution of men and women in popularly elected positions, accelerating the process aimed at making effective the exercise of political rights of men and women with equal opportunities.

To achieve the effective participation of women, a base of thirty percent (30%) is established progressively, until equity between men and women is achieved, applicable with respect to leadership positions in political parties, principal and alternate deputies to the National Congress, to the Central American Parliament, Mayors, Vice-Mayors and Councilors in eligible positions in accordance with a scale based on the results of three preceding elections.
In those departments where representation falls to a single deputy or where will and participation have not been expressed, these provisions shall not be applicable.

#### Article 82

The State and civil society are obligated to inform the citizenry in general about the value of suffrage and the consequences of the good or bad use made of it in a true participatory democracy.

#### Article 83

Civil society shall demand that politicians formulate strategies and programs that ensure a democracy where equal participation is the foundation of sustainable development and social peace.

#### Article 84

The State shall guarantee real participation of qualified women in high-level executive positions in the different instances of the public sector.

#### Article 85

The State shall promote and include in sectoral dialogue instances the analysis of the situation of women in any of the areas contained in this Law.

For their part, women shall develop a critical attitude toward state management and shall generate public opinion by establishing the demands that the welfare of the population requires.

#### Article 86

Acts of discrimination shall be null and void. Authorities or private persons who violate the articles of this Law shall be sanctioned with a fine of five thousand lempiras (L.5,000.00) for the first time.

#### Article 87

The Public Prosecutor's Office through the Women's Prosecutor's Office shall oversee and execute the appropriate actions for the correct compliance with this Law and shall have the necessary support of the National Women's Institute (INAM), the Secretariats of State and other competent bodies.

#### Article 88

This Law repeals legal provisions whose content discriminates against or limits the opportunities of women in the use and enjoyment of the rights enshrined therein.

#### Article 89

This law enters into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette La Gaceta.

Given in the city of Tegucigalpa, municipality of the Central District, in the Session Hall of the National Congress, on the eleventh day of the month of April of the year 2000
