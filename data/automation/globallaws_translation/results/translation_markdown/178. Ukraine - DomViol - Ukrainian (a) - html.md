On Prevention and Counteraction to Domestic Violence

This Law defines the organizational and legal framework for the prevention and counteraction to domestic violence, the main directions of implementation of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence, aimed at protecting the rights and interests of persons who have suffered from such violence.
1. In this Law, the terms are used in the following meaning:
1) child offender - a person who has not reached 18 years of age and has committed domestic violence in any form;
2) child who has suffered from domestic violence (hereinafter - affected child) - a person who has not reached 18 years of age and has suffered domestic violence in any form or has become a witness (eyewitness) to such violence;
3) domestic violence - acts (actions or inaction) of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence committed in the family or within the place of residence or between relatives, or between former or current spouses, or between other persons who live together (lived together) as one family but are not (were not) in family relations or in marriage with each other, regardless of whether the person who committed domestic violence lives (lived) in the same place as the affected person, as well as threats to commit such acts;
4) economic violence - a form of domestic violence that includes intentional deprivation of housing, food, clothing, other property, funds or documents or the possibility of using them, leaving without care or custody, obstruction in obtaining necessary services for treatment or rehabilitation, prohibition to work, coercion to work, prohibition to study and other offenses of an economic nature;
5) prevention of domestic violence - a system of measures carried out by executive authorities, local self-government bodies, enterprises, institutions and organizations, as well as citizens of Ukraine, foreigners and stateless persons who are in Ukraine on legal grounds, and aimed at raising the level of public awareness of the forms, causes and consequences of domestic violence, forming an intolerant attitude to the violent model of behavior in private relations, a caring attitude to affected persons, especially to affected children, eradicating discriminatory perceptions of social roles and responsibilities of women and men, as well as any customs and traditions based on them;
6) offender - a person who has committed domestic violence in any form;
7) restrictive order against the offender - a measure of temporary restriction of rights or imposition of obligations on a person who has committed domestic violence, established by court order, aimed at ensuring the safety of the affected person;
8) person who has suffered from domestic violence (hereinafter - affected person) - a person who has suffered domestic violence in any form;
9) risk assessment - assessment of the probability of continuation or repeated commission of domestic violence, occurrence of serious or especially serious consequences of its commission, as well as death of the affected person;
10) program for the offender - a set of measures formed on the basis of risk assessment results and aimed at changing the violent behavior of the offender, forming a new, non-aggressive psychological model of behavior in private relations, responsible attitude to their actions and their consequences, including the upbringing of children, eradicating discriminatory perceptions of social roles and responsibilities of women and men;
11) program for the affected person - a set of measures aimed at eliminating emotional dependence, lack of self-confidence and forming in the affected person the ability to defend their own dignity, protect their rights in private relations, including with the help of authorized state authorities, local self-government bodies;
12) counteraction to domestic violence - a system of measures carried out by executive authorities, local self-government bodies, enterprises, institutions and organizations, as well as citizens of Ukraine, foreigners and stateless persons who are in Ukraine on legal grounds, and aimed at stopping domestic violence, providing assistance and protection to the affected person, compensating them for the damage caused, as well as proper investigation of cases of domestic violence, bringing offenders to justice and changing their behavior;
13) preventive registration - implementation by authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine of organizational and practical measures to control the behavior of the offender in order to prevent repeated commission of domestic violence, compliance with temporary restrictions of their rights and performance of obligations imposed on them in connection with the commission of domestic violence;
14) psychological violence - a form of domestic violence that includes verbal insults, threats, including against third parties, humiliation, harassment, intimidation, other actions aimed at restricting the expression of will of a person, control in the reproductive sphere, if such actions or inaction caused the affected person to fear for their safety or the safety of third parties, caused emotional insecurity, inability to protect themselves or caused harm to the mental health of the person;
15) sexual violence - a form of domestic violence that includes any actions of a sexual nature committed against an adult person without their consent or against a child regardless of their consent, or in the presence of a child, coercion to an act of a sexual nature with a third party, as well as other offenses against sexual freedom or sexual integrity of a person, including those committed against a child or in their presence;
16) urgent restraining order against the offender - a special measure of counteraction to domestic violence applied by authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine as a response to the fact of domestic violence and aimed at immediately stopping domestic violence, eliminating danger to the life and health of affected persons and preventing continuation or repeated commission of such violence;
17) physical violence - a form of domestic violence that includes slaps, punches, pushing, pinching, whipping, biting, as well as unlawful deprivation of liberty, infliction of beatings, torture, infliction of bodily injuries of varying degrees of severity, leaving in danger, failure to provide assistance to a person who is in a life-threatening condition, causing death, commission of other offenses of a violent nature.

### Article 2. Legislation on Prevention and Counteraction to Domestic Violence

1. Legislation on prevention and counteraction to domestic violence consists of the Constitution of Ukraine, international treaties approved by the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine, this Law and other regulatory legal acts on the prevention of violence.

### Article 3

Scope of legislation on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence
1. The subject of regulation of this Law is legal relations arising in the process of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
2. The effect of legislation on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, regardless of the fact of cohabitation, extends to the following persons:
4) mother (father) or children of one of the spouses (former spouses) and the other spouse (former spouse);
5) persons who cohabit (cohabited) as one family but are not (were not) married to each other, their parents and children;
6) persons who have a common child (children);
7) parents (mother, father) and child (children);
8) grandfather (grandmother) and grandchild (granddaughter);
9) great-grandfather (great-grandmother) and great-grandchild (great-granddaughter);
10) stepfather (stepmother) and stepson (stepdaughter);
12) other relatives: uncle (aunt) and nephew (niece), cousins (brothers and sisters), second cousin grandfather (grandmother) and second cousin grandchild (granddaughter);
13) children of spouses, former spouses, fiancés, persons who have a common child (children), who are not common or adopted;
14) guardians, custodians, their children and persons who are (were) under guardianship, custody;
15) foster parents, parent-educators, patronage educators, their children and foster children, pupil children, children who live (lived) in the family of a patronage educator.
3. The effect of legislation on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence also extends to other relatives, other persons who are connected by common household, have mutual rights and obligations, subject to cohabitation, as well as to subjects that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.

### Article 4. Basic principles of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence

1. Activities aimed at prevention and counteraction of domestic violence are based on the following principles:
1) guaranteeing victims safety and fundamental human and civil rights and freedoms, in particular the right to life, liberty and personal integrity, to respect for private and family life, to a fair trial, to legal assistance, taking into account the case law of the European Court of Human Rights;
{Paragraph 1 of part one of Article 4 as amended by Law No. 3022-IX of 10.04.2023}
2) due attention to each fact of domestic violence during the implementation of measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
3) taking into account the disproportionate impact of domestic violence on women and men, children and adults, observance of the principle of ensuring equal rights and opportunities for women and men during the implementation of measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
4) recognition of the public danger of domestic violence and ensuring intolerance towards any manifestations of domestic violence;
5) respect and impartial and caring attitude towards victims on the part of subjects implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, ensuring priority of rights, legitimate interests and safety of victims during the implementation of measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
6) confidentiality of information about victims and persons who reported the commission of domestic violence;
7) voluntary receipt of assistance by victims, except for children and incapacitated persons;
8) taking into account the special needs and interests of victims, in particular persons with disabilities, pregnant women, children, incapacitated persons, elderly persons;
9) effective interaction of subjects implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence with public associations, non-governmental organizations, media and other interested persons.
2. In case the victim is a child, any actions taken in relation to him or her are based on the principles defined by the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, the Council of Europe Convention on the Protection of Children against Sexual Exploitation and Sexual Abuse, the European Convention on the Exercise of Children's Rights and legislative acts of Ukraine in the sphere of protection of children's rights.
3. Measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence are implemented without discrimination on any grounds.
4. Customs, religious beliefs, faith, traditions cannot be considered as justification for any forms of domestic violence provided for by this Law, or be grounds for exempting the perpetrator from liability.

### Article 5

Main directions of implementation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence
1. State policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence is aimed at ensuring a comprehensive integrated approach to overcoming domestic violence, providing comprehensive assistance to victims and affirming the non-violent nature of private relations.
2. The main directions of implementation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence are:
1) prevention of domestic violence;
2) effective response to facts of domestic violence through the introduction of a mechanism of interaction of subjects implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
3) provision of assistance and protection to victims, ensuring compensation for damage caused by domestic violence;
4) proper investigation of facts of domestic violence, bringing perpetrators to liability provided by law and changing their behavior.

### Article 6

Entities that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence
1. The entities that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence are:
1) specially authorized bodies in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
2) other bodies and institutions charged with functions of implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
3) general and specialized support services for victims;
4) citizens of Ukraine, foreigners and stateless persons who are in Ukraine on legal grounds.
2. The specially authorized bodies in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence are:
1) the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
2) the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
3) the Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, local state administrations, including their structural subdivisions, whose powers include the implementation of measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
4) village, settlement, city, district in cities (if established) councils, their executive bodies, whose powers include the implementation of measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
3. Other bodies and institutions charged with functions of implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence include:
2) authorized subdivisions of the bodies of the National Police of Ukraine;
3) education management bodies, educational institutions, establishments and organizations of the education system;
4) health care bodies, health care institutions and establishments;
5) centers for the provision of free legal aid;

8) authorized bodies on probation matters.
4. General support services for victims include establishments that, inter alia, provide assistance to victims:
1) centers of social services for family, children and youth;
3) centers of social and psychological rehabilitation of children;
4) social rehabilitation centers (children's towns);
5) centers of social and psychological assistance;
6) territorial centers of social services (provision of social services);
7) other establishments, institutions and organizations that provide social services to victims.
Specialized support services for victims include shelters for victims, centers of medical and social rehabilitation of victims, a call center on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children, mobile teams of social and psychological assistance to victims and persons who have suffered from gender-based violence, as well as establishments and institutions intended exclusively for victims and persons who have suffered from gender-based violence.
5. Enterprises, institutions and organizations regardless of form of ownership, public associations and foreign non-governmental organizations, individual entrepreneurs who meet the criteria of activity of entities providing social services, as well as natural persons providing social services, including child patronage services, may participate in the implementation of measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, in particular in identifying facts of domestic violence, providing assistance and protection to victims.

## Section II

ENTITIES THAT IMPLEMENT MEASURES IN THE SPHERE OF PREVENTION AND COUNTERACTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 7

Powers of the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, and of the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence
1. The powers of the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence include:
1) formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
2) regulatory and legal regulation in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
3) coordination of activities of entities that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
4) ensuring the development and approval of standard programs for victims, as well as methodological recommendations for their implementation;
5) ensuring the development and approval of standard programs for perpetrators, as well as methodological recommendations for their implementation, including taking into account the age, health status, sex of the perpetrator;
6) providing methodological support to local state administrations and local self-government bodies in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
7) approval of standards for the provision of social services to victims and methodology for determining the needs of territorial communities in creating specialized support services for victims;
8) implementation of international cooperation in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
2. The powers of the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence include:
1) implementation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
2) ensuring the creation and functioning of the Unified State Register of Cases of Domestic Violence and Gender-Based Violence, exercising the powers of the holder of this register;
3) ensuring the functioning of support services for victims and exercising control over their activities;
4) coordination of training of specialists who represent entities that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
5) organization and conduct of nationwide sociological, legal, psychological, pedagogical and other studies on domestic violence, its causes and consequences;
6) collection, analysis and dissemination in accordance with the law of information on domestic violence, improvement of the system of indicators in forms of state statistical reporting on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
7) ensuring the monitoring of the effectiveness of legislation on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, the practice of its application, measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, provision of methodological and practical assistance to entities that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
8) preparation and publication of an annual report on the state of implementation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
9) maintaining records of data on the activities of general and specialized support services for victims at the national level.

### Article 8

Powers of the Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, local state administrations and local self-government bodies in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence
1. The powers of the Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, regional, Kyiv and Sevastopol city state administrations in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence include:
1) ensuring the implementation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence at the regional level, in particular by exercising constant control over the timeliness and appropriateness of measures taken in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence;
2) ensuring the development, approval and implementation of regional programmes in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence;
3) ensuring coordination of the activities of entities implementing measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence, and their interaction at the regional level;
4) maintaining records of entities implementing measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence at the regional level;
5) ensuring the training of specialists whose competence includes issues of preventing and combating domestic violence, including specialists who implement programmes for offenders;
6) ensuring the preparation of methodological recommendations on the organisation and provision of activities and professional development of specialists whose competence includes issues of preventing and combating domestic violence;
7) ensuring the implementation of information and educational activities (including the preparation and dissemination of relevant materials) on the forms, causes and consequences of domestic violence, measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence, forming citizens' intolerant attitude towards violent models of behaviour in private relationships;
8) organising and/or conducting regional sociological, psychological-pedagogical and other research on the forms, causes and consequences of domestic violence;
9) monitoring the state of implementation by entities implementing measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence of the tasks set in the process of implementing state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence at the regional level, providing them with methodological and practical assistance, clarifying problematic issues in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence and taking exhaustive measures to resolve them;
10) reporting to the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence.
2. The powers of district, district in the cities of Kyiv and Sevastopol state administrations and executive bodies of village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their establishment) councils in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence include:
1) ensuring the implementation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence in the territory of the respective administrative-territorial unit;
2) ensuring the provision of social services in the territory of the respective administrative-territorial unit, including through social procurement in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence;
3) determining the need for establishing specialised support services for victims, ensuring their establishment and functioning, exercising control over their activities;
4) participating in the training of specialists whose competence includes issues of preventing and combating domestic violence, including specialists who implement programmes for offenders;
5) receiving and considering applications and notifications about the commission of domestic violence, ensuring the application of measures to stop it, providing assistance to victims;
6) ensuring coordination of the activities of entities implementing measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence, and their interaction in the territory of the respective administrative-territorial unit;
7) informing victims about their rights, measures and social services they can use;
8) collecting, analysing and disseminating in accordance with the law information about domestic violence in the territory of the respective administrative-territorial unit;
9) reporting to the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence;
10) exercising the powers of the guardianship and trusteeship authority.
3. The specifics of exercising by district state administrations and executive bodies of village, settlement, city (cities of district subordination) councils the powers provided for in part two of this article shall be determined by the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine.
The specifics of exercising by executive bodies of district in city councils the powers provided for in part two of this article shall be determined by the city council of the respective city.
4. The structural subdivisions of local state administrations whose powers include implementing measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence are structural subdivisions on family matters.
The executive bodies of village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their establishment) councils whose powers include implementing measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence are executive bodies on family matters.

### Article 9

Powers of guardianship and trusteeship bodies, children's services in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence
1. The powers of guardianship and trusteeship bodies in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence concerning a child and with the participation of a child include:
1) protection of the rights and interests of a child victim, a child offender, including by applying to court, representing the rights and interests of the child in court when considering issues related to acts of domestic violence, in particular regarding the issuance of a restrictive order;
2) direct provision of assistance and protection to child victims, child offenders who have the status of orphans and children deprived of parental care;
3) consideration, in the procedure established by the Family Code of Ukraine, of the question of the expediency of removing a child or depriving parental rights concerning a child if the child's offenders are parents (adopters) or one of them;
4) consideration, in the procedure established by law, of questions regarding the expediency of removing a child from a guardian (custodian), foster parents, parent-educators, releasing a person from the duties of guardian (custodian) of a child, terminating the patronage agreement over a child, cancelling the decision on placing a child in a family-type children's home or in a foster family in case of domestic violence concerning a child or with the participation of a child;
5) giving consent to receive social services by a child victim, a child offender, if the parents or other legal representatives of the child are offenders or evade the protection of the rights and interests of the child;
6) giving consent to enter personal data about a child who reported the commission of violence or is a victim into the Unified State Register of Cases of Domestic Violence and Gender-Based Violence, if the parents or other legal representatives of the child are offenders or evade the protection of the rights and interests of the child;
7) placing a child in the family of a patronage educator in case of impossibility of the child living with their parents or other legal representatives in connection with the commission of domestic violence concerning this child or with their participation.
2. The powers of children's services in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence concerning children include:
1) development and implementation of measures to protect the rights and legitimate interests of a child victim;
2) development and implementation of measures to protect the rights and legitimate interests of a child offender;
3) receiving and considering applications and reports on domestic violence concerning children and with the participation of children, including reports received by the call centre on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence concerning children;
4) informing a child victim, their parents, other legal representatives, if they are not offenders of the child, about the rights, measures and services they can use;
5) informing a child offender, their parents, other legal representatives about the rights of the child, measures and services they can use;
6) placing a child in a centre for social and psychological rehabilitation of children, a shelter for children of the children's service, other institutions for children regardless of form of ownership and subordination, in which appropriate conditions for living, upbringing, education and rehabilitation of the child according to their needs are created, as well as exercising control over the conditions of their stay and providing assistance in case of impossibility of the child living with their parents or other legal representatives in connection with the commission of domestic violence concerning this child or with their participation;
7) conducting preventive work with parents and other legal representatives of the child on preventing domestic violence concerning children and with the participation of children;
8) raising before executive authorities and local self-government bodies the question of bringing to responsibility in accordance with law officials in case of non-performance or improper performance by them of duties during the detection of facts of domestic violence, work with a child victim, a child offender;
9) interaction with other subjects implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, in accordance with Article 15 of this Law;
10) implementation of other measures provided by law in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence concerning children or with the participation of children;
11) reporting to the central executive authority implementing state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the procedure determined by the central executive authority ensuring the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
3. The powers of guardianship and trusteeship bodies in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence concerning incapacitated persons and persons whose civil legal capacity is limited include:
1) resolving the question of receiving social services by a victim who is an incapacitated person, in case a legal representative has not been appointed to such person or the legal representative is an offender or evades the protection of the rights and interests of such person;
2) giving consent to enter personal data about an incapacitated person who reported the commission of violence or is a victim into the Unified State Register of Cases of Domestic Violence and Gender-Based Violence, if the legal representative of such person is an offender or evades the protection of the rights and interests of the incapacitated person;
3) placing a victim who is an incapacitated person in a social protection institution in case living of such person in a family or with a guardian poses a threat to their life and health in connection with the commission of domestic violence;
4) taking measures to provide psychiatric assistance to an incapacitated person who is an offender on a compulsory basis in case living of such person in a family or with a guardian poses a threat to the life and health of family members or the guardian in connection with the commission of domestic violence;
5) raising before the court the question of releasing in the procedure established by law from the powers of guardian or custodian in case of commission by them of domestic violence concerning an incapacitated person or a person whose civil legal capacity is limited;
6) protection of rights and representation of interests of a victim who is an incapacitated person in state authorities, local self-government bodies or in court (if necessary);
7) raising before executive authorities and local self-government bodies the question of bringing in the procedure established by law to responsibility officials in case of non-performance or improper performance by them of duties during the detection and work with victims who are incapacitated persons or persons whose civil legal capacity is limited;
8) implementation of other measures provided by law in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence concerning incapacitated persons and persons whose civil legal capacity is limited, or with their participation.

### Стаття 10

Powers of authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence
1. The powers of authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence include:
1) identifying facts of domestic violence and responding to them in a timely manner;
2) receiving and considering applications and reports on the commission of domestic violence, including consideration of reports received by the call centre on preventing and combating domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children, taking measures to stop it and providing assistance to victims taking into account the results of risk assessment in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence, jointly with the National Police of Ukraine;
3) informing victims about their rights, measures and social services they can use;
4) issuing urgent restraining orders against perpetrators;
5) placing perpetrators on preventive record and conducting preventive work with them in the manner determined by law;
6) exercising control over the implementation by perpetrators of special measures to combat domestic violence during the period of their validity;
7) cancelling permits for the right to purchase, store, carry weapons and ammunition to their owners in case they commit domestic violence, as well as seizing weapons and ammunition in the manner determined by law;
8) interacting with other subjects that implement measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence, in accordance with Article 15 of this Law;
9) reporting to the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence.
2. Authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine exercise powers in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence taking into account international standards for law enforcement response to cases of domestic violence and risk assessment.
Police officers may enter a person's dwelling without a reasoned court decision in urgent cases related to stopping an act of domestic violence being committed, in case of immediate danger to the life or health of the victim.

### Стаття 11

Powers of education management bodies, educational institutions and establishments of the education system in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence
1. The powers of education management bodies in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence in accordance with their competence include:
1) ensuring the training of specialists of appropriate qualifications and fields of knowledge for the purpose of proper performance by them of functions on preventing and combating domestic violence;
2) ensuring the introduction into the educational process at all educational levels, including inclusion in curricula and plans, of issues of preventing and combating domestic violence;
3) ensuring the inclusion in educational and professional programmes during the establishment of state education standards of issues of preventing and combating domestic violence;
4) participation in the training (retraining, advanced training) of specialists who represent subjects that implement measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence;
5) ensuring the inclusion in educational and upbringing programmes of issues of preventing and combating domestic violence;
6) methodological support of educational institutions on issues of preventing and combating domestic violence;
7) reporting to the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence.
2. Educational institutions and establishments of the education system when implementing measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence:
1) conduct educational work with participants in the educational process on preventing and combating domestic violence;
2) notify no later than one day the children's affairs service, authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine in case of identifying facts of domestic violence against children or receiving relevant applications or reports;
3) conduct information and educational activities with participants in the educational process on issues of preventing and combating domestic violence, including regarding children and with the participation of children, paying special attention to forming students' caring attitude towards affected children, awareness of the need for immediate notification of teachers about cases of domestic violence that became known to them, reporting such cases to the call centre on preventing and combating domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children;
4) organise the work of a practical psychologist and/or social pedagogue with affected children;
5) interact with other subjects that implement measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence, in accordance with Article 15 of this Law;
6) report to the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence.

### Стаття 12

Powers of health care bodies, institutions and establishments in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence
1. The powers of health care bodies in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence include:
1) developing and approving a standard for providing medical assistance to victims or persons who have likely suffered from domestic violence;
2) approving the procedure for conducting and documenting the results of medical examination of victims;
3) methodological support of health care institutions and establishments on issues of preventing and combating domestic violence;
4) reporting to the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence.
2. Health care institutions and establishments when implementing measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence:
1) notify authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine about the identification of injuries that could have arisen as a result of committing domestic violence, and in case of identifying injuries in a child - also the children's affairs service;
2) in case of identifying bodily injuries ensure in the established manner the conduct of medical examination of victims;
3) in case of identifying injuries of a sexual nature refer victims for testing for HIV infection;
4) provide medical assistance to victims taking into account individual needs;
5) inform victims about measures and social services they can use;
6) interact with other subjects that implement measures in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence, in accordance with Article 15 of this Law;
7) report to the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of preventing and combating domestic violence.

## Section III

PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 18. Tasks in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence

1. Tasks in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence include:
1) determining the state, causes and preconditions of the spread of domestic violence;
2) raising the level of public awareness about forms, manifestations, causes and consequences of domestic violence;
3) promoting society's understanding of the nature of domestic violence, its disproportionate impact on women and men, in particular on persons with disabilities, pregnant women, children, incapacitated persons, elderly persons;
4) forming in society an intolerant attitude towards violent models of behavior, a caring attitude towards victims, primarily child victims, awareness of domestic violence as a violation of human rights;
5) eradicating discriminatory perceptions about social roles and responsibilities of women and men, as well as any customs and traditions based on them;
6) encouraging all members of society, primarily men and boys, to actively contribute to the prevention of domestic violence.

### Article 19. Measures in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence

1. In fulfillment of tasks in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence, entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence ensure:
1) studying the situation and collecting sex-disaggregated statistical data on facts of domestic violence;
2) organization and conduct of sectoral and intersectoral research on the state, causes and preconditions of the spread of domestic violence, effectiveness of legislation in the sphere of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence and the practice of its application;
3) organization and conduct among the population, including among children and youth, of information campaigns on prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence, explanation of its forms, manifestations and consequences;
4) development and implementation in educational institutions of educational and upbringing programs on issues of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence, including concerning children;
5) engaging media in conducting educational campaigns aimed at fulfilling tasks in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence, in particular forming a caring attitude towards victims, primarily child victims, awareness of the need for immediate reporting of cases of domestic violence, including to the call center on issues of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children;
6) organization and conduct of joint and specialized trainings and seminars for specialists working in the sphere of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence, as well as for law enforcement officers and judges.
2. Entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence take into account the nature of domestic violence, its disproportionate impact on women and men, in particular on persons with disabilities, pregnant women, children, incapacitated persons, elderly persons, the need for support and protection of victims, consequences of domestic violence leading to violations of human rights.
3. Citizens of Ukraine, foreigners and stateless persons lawfully present in Ukraine who become aware of the commission of domestic violence, in particular if children have become or may become victims, are obliged to immediately report this to district, district in the cities of Kyiv and Sevastopol state administrations, executive bodies of village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their establishment) councils, authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine or to the round-the-clock call center on issues of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children.

## Section IV

PROVISION OF ASSISTANCE AND PROTECTION TO VICTIMS

### Article 20. Provision of Assistance and Protection to Victims

1. Entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence, in accordance with their competence, ensure the provision of effective assistance and protection to victims, taking into account the basic principles of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence defined by Article 4 of this Law.
2. Provision of assistance and protection to victims is carried out in the following areas:
1) providing victims with information about their rights and possibilities for exercising such rights in a language understandable to them or through an interpreter or an involved third person who speaks a language understandable to the victims;
2) ensuring access to general and specialized support services for victims to receive social services, medical, social, psychological assistance;
3) providing, if necessary, temporary shelter for safe accommodation of victims;
4) ensuring victims' access to justice and other mechanisms of legal protection, including by providing free legal aid in the procedure established by the Law of Ukraine "On Free Legal Aid";
{Paragraph 4 of part two of Article 20 as amended by Law No. 3022-IX of 10.04.2023}
5) establishment of a round-the-clock free call center on prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children to ensure immediate response to cases of domestic violence, provision of consultations on all forms of domestic violence defined by Article 1 of this Law, to subscribers anonymously or with proper observance of the legal regime of information with restricted access.
3. When providing assistance and protection to victims, their age, state of health, sex, religious beliefs, ethnic origin, special needs of such persons are taken into account.
4. Assistance to victims is provided at the place of application.
5. Provision of assistance to victims does not depend on such persons' application to law enforcement authorities or court, on their participation in criminal or civil proceedings.
6. Issues of providing assistance to victims who are incapacitated persons are decided by their guardians, and if the guardians are perpetrators of the victims or evade the protection of their rights and interests - by guardianship and trusteeship authorities.
7. Issues of providing assistance to child victims are decided by their parents, other legal representatives of the child, and if the parents, other legal representatives of the child are perpetrators of the child or evade the protection of the child's rights and interests - by guardianship and trusteeship authorities, including upon application of the child's relatives (grandmother, grandfather, adult brother, sister), stepmother or stepfather of the child, if they are not perpetrators.

### Article 21. Rights of Victims

1. A victim has the right to:
1) effective, efficient and immediate protection in all cases of domestic violence, prevention of repeated cases of domestic violence;
2) apply personally or through their representative to entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence;
3) receive full and comprehensive information from entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence about their rights and social services, medical, social, psychological assistance that they can use;
4) receive free of charge, in accordance with legislation, social services, medical, social and psychological assistance according to their needs;
5) free legal aid in the procedure established by the Law of Ukraine "On Free Legal Aid";
{Paragraph 5 of part one of Article 21 as amended by Law No. 3022-IX of 10.04.2023}
6) respect for honor and dignity, attentive and humane treatment by entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence;
7) confidentiality of personal information that became known to entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence during work with the victim, and protection of personal data;
8) choice of specialist by sex (if possible);
9) compensation by perpetrators for material losses and harm caused to physical and mental health, in the procedure determined by legislation;
10) apply to law enforcement authorities and court with the aim of bringing perpetrators to liability, applying to them special measures for counteraction to domestic violence;
11) timely receipt of information about final court decisions and procedural decisions of law enforcement authorities related to consideration of the fact of committing domestic violence against them, including those related to isolation of the perpetrator or their release;
12) other rights provided by legislation in the sphere of prevention of and counteraction to domestic violence, as well as international treaties, consent to the binding nature of which has been granted by the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine.

### Article 22. Rights of a Child Victim

1. A child victim has all the rights of a victim, the exercise of which is ensured taking into account the best interests of the child, their age, sex, state of health, intellectual and physical development.

2. If, in connection with the commission of domestic violence against a child, they cannot live with their parents, other legal representatives, for the time of overcoming the causes and consequences of domestic violence, the child may be placed with relatives, in the family of a foster educator, in a center for social and psychological rehabilitation of children, a shelter for children of services for children's affairs, other institutions for children regardless of form of ownership and subordination, in which appropriate conditions for living, upbringing, education and rehabilitation of the child according to their needs have been created.

4. During consideration by a court and/or guardianship and trusteeship authority of disputes regarding participation of one of the parents in the upbringing of the child, determination of the child's place of residence, taking away the child, deprivation and restoration of parental rights, visits with the child by the mother, father of the child who are deprived of parental rights, taking away the child from a person who keeps them not on legal grounds or not on the basis of a court decision, facts of committing domestic violence against the child or in their presence are necessarily taken into account.

### Article 23. Implementation of Programs for Victims

## Section V

SPECIAL MEASURES FOR COUNTERACTION TO DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 24. Special Measures for Counteraction to Domestic Violence

1. Special measures for counteraction to domestic violence include:
1) urgent restraining order against the perpetrator;
2) restrictive order against the perpetrator;
3) placing the perpetrator on preventive record and conducting preventive work with them;
4) referring the perpetrator to undergo a program for perpetrators.

### Article 25. Urgent restraining order against the offender

1. An urgent restraining order is issued against the offender by authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine in case of an immediate threat to the life or health of the victim in order to immediately stop domestic violence, prevent its continuation or repeated commission.
2. An urgent restraining order may contain the following measures:
1) obligation to leave the place of residence (stay) of the victim;
2) prohibition to enter and stay at the place of residence (stay) of the victim;
3) prohibition to contact the victim in any way.
3. When deciding on the issuance of an urgent restraining order, priority is given to the safety of the victim. This requirement also applies to the place of joint residence (stay) of the victim and the offender regardless of their property rights to the respective dwelling.
Officers of the authorised unit of the National Police of Ukraine may apply police coercive measures in the manner prescribed by law to evict the offender from the dwelling if the urgent restraining order provides for the obligation to leave the place of residence (stay) of the victim and the offender refuses to leave it voluntarily.
4. An urgent restraining order is issued upon the application of the victim, as well as on the own initiative of an officer of the authorised unit of the National Police of Ukraine based on the results of risk assessment.
5. An urgent restraining order is issued for a period of up to 10 days.
6. An urgent restraining order is served on the offender, and a copy thereof is served on the victim or their representative.
7. The effect of an urgent restraining order ceases in case of the court imposing an administrative penalty in the form of administrative arrest on the offender or choosing a preventive measure in the form of detention in criminal proceedings against them.
8. An offender against whom an urgent restraining order has been issued, according to which they must leave the place of joint residence (stay) with the victim, is obliged to inform the authorised unit of the National Police of Ukraine at the place of commission of domestic violence about the place of their temporary stay.
9. A person against whom an urgent restraining order has been issued may appeal it to the court in the general procedure provided for appealing decisions, actions or inaction of officers of authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine.
10. An urgent restraining order may not contain measures provided for in points 1 and 2 of part two of this Article if the offender is a person who has not reached the age of eighteen on the day of issuance of the order and has a common place of residence (stay) with the victim.
11. An urgent restraining order is issued in the procedure approved by the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine.

### Article 26. Restraining order against the offender

1. The right to apply to the court with an application for the issuance of a restraining order against the offender is held by:
1) the victim or their representative;
2) in case of domestic violence against a child - parents or other legal representatives of the child, relatives of the child (grandmother, grandfather, adult brother, sister), stepmother or stepfather of the child, as well as the guardianship and custody authority;
3) in case of domestic violence against an incapacitated person - guardian, guardianship and custody authority.
2. A restraining order determines one or more of the following measures of temporary restriction of the offender's rights or imposition of obligations on them:
1) prohibition to stay at the place of joint residence (stay) with the victim;
2) removal of obstacles to the use of property that is the object of joint common property or personal private property of the victim;
3) restriction of communication with the victim child;
4) prohibition to approach within a specified distance to the place of residence (stay), study, work, other places frequently visited by the victim;
5) prohibition to search for the victim personally and through third parties if they are at their own will in a place unknown to the offender, to persecute them and to communicate with them in any way;
6) prohibition to conduct correspondence, telephone conversations with the victim or to contact them through other means of communication personally and through third parties.
3. A decision on the issuance of a restraining order or on the refusal to issue a restraining order is made on the basis of risk assessment.
4. A restraining order is issued for a period from one to six months.
5. Upon the application of persons specified in part one of this Article, on the basis of risk assessment, a restraining order may be extended by the court for a period of no more than six months after the expiry of the period established by the court decision in accordance with part four of this Article.
6. On the issuance of a restraining order against the offender, the judge within the time limit established by law informs the authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine at the place of residence (stay) of the victim for placing the offender on preventive record, as well as district, district in the cities of Kyiv and Sevastopol state administrations and executive bodies of village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their creation) councils at the place of residence (stay) of the victim.
7. A restraining order may not contain measures that restrict the right of residence or stay of the offender at the place of their permanent residence (stay) if the offender is a person who has not reached the age of eighteen on the day of issuance of such order.
8. The procedure for the issuance of a restraining order by the court is determined by the Civil Procedural Code of Ukraine.
9. The victim may demand from the offender compensation for their expenses for treatment, obtaining consultations or for renting housing that they rent (rented) in order to prevent domestic violence against them, as well as periodic expenses for their maintenance, maintenance of children or other family members who are (were) dependent on the offender, in the procedure provided by law.
10. In case of initiation of criminal proceedings in connection with the commission of domestic violence, the list of measures for temporary restriction of rights or imposition of obligations on a person who is suspected, accused of committing a criminal offence related to domestic violence, or found guilty of its commission, as well as the procedure for applying such measures are determined by the Criminal Code of Ukraine and the Criminal Procedural Code of Ukraine.

### Article 27

Placing offenders on preventive record and conducting preventive work with them
1. The authorised unit of the National Police of Ukraine places the offender on preventive record from the moment of detection of the fact of commission of domestic violence by them for the period established by law and conducts preventive work with them.
2. Removal of the offender from preventive record is carried out by the authorised unit of the National Police of Ukraine that placed them on preventive record, automatically after the expiry of the established period, unless otherwise provided by law.
3. The procedure for placing on preventive record, conducting preventive work and removing from preventive record of the offender is approved by the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine.

### Article 28. Implementation of Programs for Perpetrators

1. The entities responsible for the implementation of programs for perpetrators are local state administrations and local self-government bodies.
2. The entity responsible for the implementation of programs for perpetrators organizes and ensures the completion of such programs by perpetrators.
3. The implementation of programs for perpetrators with respect to child perpetrators is carried out taking into account the age and psychological characteristics of children.
4. In order to prevent the repeated commission of domestic violence and to ensure the implementation of the program for the perpetrator, a child perpetrator may be temporarily placed with relatives, in the family of a foster care educator, or in an institution for children regardless of form of ownership and subordination, in which appropriate conditions for living, upbringing, education and rehabilitation of the child in accordance with his or her needs have been created.
5. The implementation of programs for perpetrators is ensured by specialists who have undergone appropriate training.
6. A perpetrator may be referred by a court to complete a program for perpetrators for a period from three months to one year in cases provided for by law.
7. A perpetrator must have the opportunity to attend a program for perpetrators on his or her own initiative on a voluntary basis.
8. In the event of a perpetrator's failure to appear for completion of a program for perpetrators or evasion of completing the program without valid reasons, the entities responsible for the implementation of programs for perpetrators shall provide within three working days written notification thereof to the authorized unit of the National Police of Ukraine for taking measures.
9. Holding a perpetrator liable for failure to complete a program for perpetrators does not release him or her from the obligation to complete such program.
10. In the event of holding a perpetrator, including a child perpetrator, criminally liable, the court may impose on him or her the obligation to complete a probation program in accordance with point 4 of part two of Article 76 of the Criminal Code of Ukraine.

## Section VI

LIABILITY IN THE SPHERE OF PREVENTION AND COUNTERACTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 29. Liability for Committing Domestic Violence

1. A perpetrator who has violated the requirements of special measures for counteraction of domestic violence bears liability in accordance with the law.

### Article 30

Compensation for Material Losses and Moral Damage Caused as a Result of Committing Domestic Violence
1. For a person who has the right to compensation for material losses and moral damage caused as a result of domestic violence, moral damage is compensated regardless of material losses that are subject to compensation and is not related to their amount.
2. The procedure for compensation for material losses and moral damage caused as a result of domestic violence is determined by the Civil Code of Ukraine and other legislative acts.

### Article 31

Liability of Officials for Non-Compliance with the Requirements of Legislation in the Sphere of Prevention and Counteraction of Domestic Violence
1. Officials and officers recognized as guilty of violating the requirements of legislation in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence bear liability in accordance with the law.
2. An affected person and a perpetrator have the right to appeal decisions, actions or inaction of entities that carry out measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, in the procedure established by law.

## Section VII

FINANCIAL SUPPORT OF MEASURES IN THE SPHERE OF PREVENTION AND COUNTERACTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 32

Financing of Expenses Related to Prevention and Counteraction of Domestic Violence
1. Financial support of measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, provision of assistance to affected persons, as well as creation and ensuring the functioning of the Unified State Register of Cases of Domestic Violence and Gender-Based Violence is carried out at the expense of state and local budgets, funds of enterprises, institutions and organizations, trade unions, voluntary contributions of legal entities and individuals, and other sources not prohibited by law.
2. Financial support of the activities of entities that carry out measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, of state and communal forms of ownership, is carried out at the expense of the respective budget and other sources not prohibited by law.
3. Maintenance of specialized support services for affected persons that do not belong to state and communal forms of ownership, ensuring the development of their material base and the provision of social services by them are carried out at the expense of funds of their founders and other funds not prohibited by law, including on the terms of social contracting.

## Section VIII

CONTROL OVER COMPLIANCE WITH LEGISLATION IN THE SPHERE OF PREVENTION AND COUNTERACTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 33. Control in the Sphere of Prevention and Counteraction of Domestic Violence

1. The Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine exercises parliamentary control in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence within the powers and in the manner determined by the Constitution of Ukraine.
2. Other state authorities exercise control in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence within the powers and in the manner determined by the Constitution and laws of Ukraine.

### Article 34

Public Control over Compliance with Legislation in the Sphere of Prevention and Counteraction of Domestic Violence
1. Public associations, members or authorized representatives of such associations, in compliance with the legal regime of information with restricted access, may exercise public control over the implementation of measures and the realization of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
2. Reports on the results of public control are brought to the attention of specially authorized bodies in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence and are taken into account by them when determining priorities and main directions of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, as well as when developing relevant regional programs.

### Article 21

3. Provision of assistance and protection to affected persons
Entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence, in accordance with their competence, ensure the provision of assistance and protection to persons who have suffered from such violence.
The provision of assistance and protection to affected persons is carried out in the following areas:
providing the affected person with information about their rights and possibilities for their realisation in a language understandable to them or through an interpreter or an involved third person who speaks a language understandable to the affected person;
ensuring affected persons' access to specialised support services for affected persons and obtaining social services that they provide;
providing, if necessary, temporary shelter for the safe accommodation of affected persons, especially women with children;
ensuring affected persons' access to justice and other legal protection mechanisms, including through the provision of free legal aid in the manner established by the Law of Ukraine "On Free Legal Aid";
establishment of a round-the-clock free call centre on issues of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children to ensure immediate response by authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine, other state authorities and local self-government bodies defined by this Law, to cases of gender-based violence, providing consultations on gender-based violence to subscribers anonymously or with due regard to confidentiality requirements and protection of personal data;
providing qualified consultations in case of commission of gender-based violence in compliance with the legal regime of information with restricted access.
Assistance to affected persons is provided at the place of application.
The provision of assistance to an affected person does not depend on such person's application to law enforcement agencies or to court and on their participation in criminal or civil proceedings.

### Article 21

4. Rights of affected persons
Affected persons have the right to:
effective, efficient and immediate protection in case of commission of violence, prevention of repeated cases of violence;
apply personally or through their representative to entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence;
receive full and comprehensive information from entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence regarding their rights, measures and social services they can use;
free receipt in accordance with legislation of medical, social and psychological assistance, other social services;
free legal aid in the manner established by the Law of Ukraine "On Free Legal Aid";
respect for honour and dignity, attentive and humane treatment by entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence;
confidentiality of information of a personal nature that became known during work with such persons;
apply to court with an application for issuance of a restrictive order against the offender in the manner prescribed by law;
other rights provided by legislation in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence.

### Article 21

5. Restrictive order against the offender
An affected person or their representative, and in case of commission of gender-based violence against a child - parents or other legal representatives of the child, relatives of the child (grandmother, grandfather, adult brother, sister), stepmother or stepfather of the child, as well as the guardianship and custody authority have the right to apply to court with an application for issuance of a restrictive order against the offender.
A restrictive order establishes one or several of the following measures of temporary restriction of the offender's rights or imposition of the following obligations on them:
prohibition to stay in the place of joint residence (stay) with the affected person;
removal of obstacles to the use of property that is an object of joint common property right or personal private property of the affected person;
restriction of communication with the affected child;
prohibition to approach within a specified distance to the place of residence (stay), study, work, other places frequently visited by the affected person;
prohibition to search for the affected person personally and through third persons if they are at their own will in a place unknown to the offender, to persecute them and to communicate with them in any way;
prohibition to conduct correspondence, telephone conversations with the affected person or to contact them through other means of communication personally and through third persons.
The decision on issuance of a restrictive order or on refusal to issue a restrictive order is made on the basis of risk assessment.
A restrictive order is issued for a period from one to six months.
Upon application of persons defined by part one of this article, on the basis of risk assessment, a restrictive order may be extended by the court for a period of no more than six months after the expiry of the period established by the court decision in accordance with part four of this article.
On the issuance of a restrictive order against the offender, the judge within the period established by law informs the authorised units of the National Police of Ukraine at the place of residence (stay) of the affected person for placing the offender on preventive record, as well as district, district in the cities of Kyiv and Sevastopol state administrations and executive bodies of village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their establishment) councils at the place of residence (stay) of the affected person.
A restrictive order may not contain measures that restrict the right of residence or stay of the offender in the place of their permanent residence (stay) if the offender is a person who has not reached the age of eighteen on the day of issuance of such order.
The procedure for issuance by the court of a restrictive order is determined by the Civil Procedural Code of Ukraine.
An affected person may demand from the offender compensation for their expenses for treatment, receiving consultations or for rent of housing that they rent (rented) with the purpose of preventing commission of domestic violence against them, as well as periodic expenses for their maintenance, maintenance of children or other family members who are (were) dependent on the offender, in the manner prescribed by legislation.
In case of initiation of criminal proceedings in connection with the commission of gender-based violence, the list of measures for temporary restriction of rights or imposition of obligations on a person who is suspected, accused of committing a criminal offence related to domestic violence, or found guilty of its commission, as well as the procedure for application of such measures are determined by the Criminal Code of Ukraine and the Criminal Procedural Code of Ukraine.

### Article 21

6. Placing the offender on preventive record and conducting preventive work with them
The authorised unit of the National Police of Ukraine places the offender on preventive record for the period established by legislation and conducts preventive work with them.
The procedure for placing on preventive record, conducting preventive work and removing from preventive record of the offender is approved by the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine.

### Стаття 21

7. Interaction of entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence
Interaction of entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence includes:
mutual notification no later than one day about identified facts of gender-based violence in compliance with the legal regime of information with restricted access;
response to facts of committing gender-based violence in accordance with competence and taking into account the risk assessment threatening the victim;
coordination of response measures to cases of gender-based violence and provision of effective assistance to victims, carried out by various entities;
development and implementation of programs for perpetrators in accordance with competence;
development of programs for prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence in accordance with competence;
organization of implementation of measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence in accordance with competence;
exchange of experience in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence;
coordinated interdepartmental training of specialists representing entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence;
monitoring of compliance with the requirements of legislation in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence by entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence, submission of proposals for improvement of legislation and practice of its application.
The procedure for interaction of entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence is approved by the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine.
Entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence report to the specially authorized central executive body on ensuring equal rights and opportunities for women and men on the results of exercising powers in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence in the manner determined by the specially authorized central executive body on ensuring equal rights and opportunities for women and men.
