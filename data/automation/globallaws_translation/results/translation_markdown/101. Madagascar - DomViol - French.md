# Law No. 2019-008

Law No. 2019-008
on the fight against Gender-Based Violence

STATEMENT OF REASONS

By ratifying international instruments on human rights, the Malagasy State has committed itself to the fight against Gender-Based Violence. Through the Constitution, Madagascar has embraced sacrosanct principles such as respect for and protection of fundamental freedoms and rights, the establishment of a State of law and the elimination of all forms of injustice, corruption, inequalities and discrimination.

However, despite the efforts undertaken, Gender-Based Violence remains a Malagasy reality. Indeed, the question of gender remains a problem in our society in which social roles concerning the feminine and the masculine are institutionalized, and rooted in mentalities and culture. This situation generates inequalities and even violence mainly to the detriment of women.

Furthermore, it must be noted that Madagascar does not have any specific text allowing it to fight effectively against this Gender-Based Violence. It is in this context that it appeared important to develop a specific law on the matter.

This law strengthens existing repressive provisions and introduces innovations in terms of prevention and care for victims.

A participatory approach was adopted throughout the process of developing this law, which was also examined within the Penal System Reform commission.

This law establishes a national mechanism to fight against Gender-Based Violence. It further punishes new categories of offenses such as marital rape, harmful traditional practices, sexist insult and economic violence, and establishes a victim protection system.

It comprises twenty-two articles subdivided into four Titles:

-	Title I on "General Provisions" comprises two Chapters on the Scope of application (

### Chapter I) and on definitions (Chapter II);

-	Title II concerning repression comprises ten articles (Articles 3 to 12);
-	Title III relating to prevention, protection and care is divided into eight articles (Articles 13 to 20);
-	Title IV on final provisions comprises two articles (Articles 21 and 22).

Such is the purpose of this law.

## TITLE ONE GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Chapter One: Scope of application

#### Article 1

Without prejudice to the provisions of the Penal Code and the Code of Criminal Procedure, the purpose of this law is to strengthen the legal regime for the prevention, prosecution, repression of acts of Gender-Based Violence, care and reparation as well as the protection of victims.

### Chapter II

Definitions Article 2: For the purposes of this law, the terms:
1° "Gender" means the roles, behaviors, activities and attributions that a given society considers at a given time as appropriate for men and women.

2° "Gender-Based Violence means any act of violence directed against a person because of their sex, and causing or likely to cause harm or physical, sexual or psychological suffering whether in public life or in private life.

Gender-Based Violence is understood as encompassing, without being limited to, the forms of violence listed below:

-	physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence exercised within the family, including beatings, sexual abuse inflicted on children, harmful traditional practices to both sexes, violence within the couple, and violence related to exploitation;

-	physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence exercised within society, including sexual abuse, sexual harassment, procuring and forced prostitution;
-	physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence perpetrated or tolerated by the State, wherever it occurs,

3° Union is understood as the bond between a man and a woman who are married or who behave as such.
4° Victim: Any person who has suffered an act of violence or ill-treatment or abuse.
5° Harmful traditional practices: means any act drawn from customs and traditions that violates human rights.

6° Unnatural sexual act: Any sexual act committed on the person of another, contrary to good morals and the natural order of things.

## TITLE II: ON REPRESSION

#### Article 3

Forced sterilization is the act of committing on a man or a woman, without their free consent or without a justified medical decision, any act of a nature to deprive him or her of their biological reproductive capacities. It is punished by one year to five years of imprisonment and a fine of one hundred thousand Ariary to five hundred thousand Ariary (100,000 Ariary to 500,000 Ariary).

#### Article 4

Any act of intimidation; threat of reprisals or reprisals against victims of Gender-Based Violence as well as members of their family, witnesses and informants with the aim of hindering care or criminal prosecution, constitutes an offense punishable by a sentence of six months to two years of imprisonment and a fine of one hundred thousand Ariary to five hundred thousand Ariary (100,000 Ariary to 500,000 Ariary).

#### Article 5

Any individual who commits an act drawn from customs and traditions that violates the physical integrity of a child or a woman is punished by six months to five years of imprisonment and a fine of one hundred thousand Ariary to one million Ariary (100,000 Ariary to 1,000,000 Ariary).

#### Article 6

Any act of sexual penetration, of any nature whatsoever, committed on the spouse or on the person engaged in a union, by violence, coercion, threat is an offense punished by two years to five years of imprisonment and a fine of one hundred thousand Ariary to one million Ariary (100,000 Ariary to 1,000,000 Ariary).

#### Article 7

Unnatural sexual practice on the person of another, by violence, coercion or threat is punished by two years to five years of imprisonment and a fine of two million Ariary to four million Ariary (2,000,000 Ariary to 4,000,000 Ariary).

#### Article 8

Any individual who gives an order, uses words, gestures, writings, messages, and this repeatedly, utters threats, composes coercions, or uses any other means for the purpose of obtaining, from a person, favors of any nature whatsoever, including sexual, for their benefit or a third party against the will of the harassed person, will be punished by one year to three years of imprisonment and a fine of one hundred thousand Ariary to one million Ariary (100,000 Ariary to 1,000,000 Ariary).

When the act is committed by two or more persons with or without collusion against a single person, the sentence will be two years to five years of imprisonment and a fine of two hundred thousand Ariary to two million Ariary (200,000 Ariary to 2,000,000 Ariary).

#### Article 9

Any person who perpetrates acts and/or utters words resulting in a degradation of the psychological, mental or physical health of the victim is punished by one year to three years of imprisonment and/or a fine of two hundred thousand Ariary to two million Ariary (200,000 Ariary to 2,000,0000 Ariary).

#### Article 10

Any act consisting of depriving a person of their fundamental freedoms and/or isolating them from the outside world outside of any legal provisions or judicial decision, shall be punished by six months to five years of imprisonment and a fine of one hundred thousand Ariary to one million Ariary (100,000 Ariary to 1,000,000 Ariary).

#### Article 11

Any statement made or any act with sexist connotations against a person that undermines their dignity by reason of its degrading or humiliating nature or creates an intimidating, hostile or offensive situation against them, constitutes sexist outrage.

Sexist outrage is punished by a fine of one hundred thousand Ariary to five hundred thousand Ariary (100,000 Ariary to 500,000 Ariary).

#### Article 12

Whoever deprives or restricts their spouse or the person engaged with them in a union, of their rights related to access to financial resources is punished by a fine of one hundred thousand Ariary to five hundred thousand Ariary (100,000 Ariary to 500,000 Ariary).

## TITRE III : DE LA PREVENTION,

DE LA PROTECTION ET DE LA PRISE EN CHARGE

#### Article 13

The State formulates and implements the policy to combat Gender-Based Violence. It mobilizes the necessary resources in this matter.

A national mechanism to combat Gender-Based Violence ensures the coordination, management and monitoring of actions. The implementation of this mechanism is established by regulatory means.

#### Article 14

The State ensures health care, psychosocial support and legal-judicial assistance for victims to assert their rights.

#### Article 15

Any person, in particular family members, neighbors, friends, local authorities, religious dignitaries, social workers, medical personnel, having knowledge of a case of gender-based violence, must report it to the competent administrative or judicial authorities under penalty of the sanctions provided for by article 62 al. 1 of the Penal Code.

#### Article 16 : Le signalement peut être fait verbalement ou par écrit

The author of the report may remain anonymous if they wish.
The authority seized must record the report declaration and follow up on it. It has the obligation to transmit it to the Judicial Police and/or to the competent Court as soon as possible.

#### Article 17

The President of the competent Court, at the request of the victim or their legal representative, after communication to the Public Prosecutor, may issue a protection order. They may in particular:

-	without prejudice to the right of Misintaka, authorize the victim to temporarily leave the common or marital home and prohibit the alleged perpetrator from approaching the victim;
-	authorize the concealment of the victim's domicile and the election of domicile. The order determines the duration and extent of the measure.
It is subject to appeals in accordance with the provisions of article 235 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

#### Article 18

The summary proceedings judge may at any time and after opinion of the Public Prosecutor increase, restrict the extent of the prescribed measures or terminate them, at the request of one of the parties in the event of the occurrence of new facts.

#### Article 19

The trial relating to a case of Gender-Based Violence may be held in camera in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure. The decision relating thereto is pronounced in public hearing.

#### Article 20

The authorities having received the report or any other person carrying out the care of the victim are bound by the obligation of confidentiality. Failure to comply with this obligation is punishable by the penalties provided for by article 378 of the Penal Code.

## TITRE IV : DISPOSITIONS FINALES

#### Article 21

Regulatory texts shall be adopted as necessary for the application of certain provisions of this law.

#### Article 22 : La présente loi sera publiée au Journal Officiel de la République

It shall be executed as law of the State.
