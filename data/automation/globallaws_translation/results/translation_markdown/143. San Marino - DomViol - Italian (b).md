# REPUBLIC OF SAN MARINO

REPUBLIC OF SAN MARINO
We the Captains Regent
of the Most Serene Republic of San Marino

Having regard to Article 4 of Constitutional Law no.185/2005 and Article 6 of Qualified Law no.186/2005;
We promulgate and order the publication of the following ordinary law approved by the Grand and General Council in the session of 2 May 2016:

LAW OF 6 MAY 2016 no.57

PROVISIONS FOR THE ADAPTATION OF THE SAN MARINO LEGAL SYSTEM TO THE PROVISIONS OF THE COUNCIL OF EUROPE CONVENTION ON PREVENTING AND COMBATING VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (ISTANBUL CONVENTION)

## Art.1

(Declarations of principle)

In compliance with the provisions of the Council of Europe Convention on preventing and combating violence against women and domestic violence opened for signature in Istanbul on 11 May 2011, the Republic of San Marino undertakes:
1. to inform the activity of its social and health assistance, support, protection, prevention and safeguarding services of the general principles established by the Convention;
2. to implement effective policies aimed at promoting equality between women and men and the empowerment and self-determination of women;
3. to adopt a gender perspective in the design of implementing measures of the Convention and in the assessment of their impact.

## Art.2

(Supplementary definitions)

Article 2 of Law of 20 June 2008 no.97 is replaced as follows:

"Art.2
(Notion of violence against women and gender-based violence, domestic violence)

Violence against a person constitutes any act of violence based on sex or gender that results in, or is likely to result in, physical, sexual, psychological or economic harm or suffering to the victim, including threats of such acts, coercion, arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
The term gender refers to the socially constructed roles, behaviours, activities and attributes that a given society considers appropriate for women and men.
Domestic violence constitutes acts of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence which occur within the family or domestic unit or between current or former spouses or partners considered equivalent to a spouse, regardless of whether the perpetrator of the violence is or has been a cohabitant of the victim.
The term woman is also to be understood as including girls under the age of eighteen.".

## Art.3

(Extension of protection and assistance measures to victims)

The protection and assistance measures referred to in Law of 20 June 2008 no.97 shall be understood as extended to all victims of all forms of violence falling within the scope of the Convention.

## Art.4

(Assistance and recovery of victims)

Pursuant to Article 4 of Law no.97/2008, in order to meet the specific needs for assistance and recovery of victims, legal advice, psychological support, financial assistance, housing, education, training and assistance in finding employment shall be ensured where necessary.
In addition to the tasks provided for in Article 1 of Delegated Decree of 31 May 2012 no.60, the Authority for Equal Opportunities is responsible for the organisation and coordination of the assistance measures guaranteed, through the conclusion of appropriate protocols with:
- the Order of Lawyers and Notaries for the provision of assistance and advisory services also in the out-of-court field and for information assistance on access to channels available for individual and collective complaints;
- the competent structures of the ISS for the provision of psychological support services;
- the offices responsible for providing services and assistance for access to education, training and employment pathways;
- adequate reception facilities, for placement in suitable accommodation in emergency situations.
Legal assistance and advisory services shall be provided by experts registered in the list prepared by the Order of Lawyers and Notaries pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 17 of Law of 20 June 2008 no.97.

## Art.5

(Fund for financial assistance to victims of violence)

In the State budget, among the expenditure items pertaining to the Department of Institutional Affairs and Justice, a Fund for financial assistance to victims is established, allocated to the Authority for Equal Opportunities.

The Authority for Equal Opportunities is the body responsible for identifying the need to provide appropriate financial assistance to victims on the basis of the availability of the Fund and in relation to the specificities of the case.
Victims of acts of violence referred to in the Convention, committed or attempted on the territory of the Republic, who are San Marino citizens, residents or staying in the territory of the Republic and who are in a state of economic difficulty, even temporary, may access the Fund.
The Authority for Equal Opportunities, having ascertained the need referred to in the second paragraph of this article, shall arrange for prompt disbursement by the Department of Institutional Affairs and Justice.

## Art.6

(Protection and support measures for child victims of witnessed violence)

The measures referred to in Article 4, letters b), c), d), e) and f) of Law of 20 June 2008 no.97 as well as the protection and support measures referred to in this law, shall be understood as extended to minors who are witnesses to all forms of violence falling within the scope of the Convention.

## Art.7

(Confidentiality regarding the identity of reporting or notifying persons)

The judicial and police authorities shall adopt, through Regulation of the Congress of State, adequate measures to ensure confidentiality regarding the identity of the natural person who has reported or notified any act of violence falling within the scope of the Convention.

## Art.8

(Compensation for failure to adopt appropriate prevention and protection measures)

Victims of acts of violence contemplated by the Convention are granted the right to bring proceedings before the competent judicial authorities to obtain compensation for any damage resulting from the failure to adopt, through wilful misconduct or gross negligence, by the State authorities, prevention or protection measures, within their respective competences, in relation to the offences referred to in the Convention.

## Art.9

(Forced marriage)

After Article 176 of the Criminal Code the following article is inserted:

"Art.176 bis
(Forced marriage)

Anyone who forces a person to contract marriage is punished with imprisonment of the third degree.

Anyone who intentionally lures by deception a person to the territory of a State

other than that in which the latter resides, for the purpose of forcing that person to contract marriage, is punished with imprisonment of the second degree.".

## Art.10

(Female genital mutilation)

After Article 156 of the Criminal Code the following article is inserted:

"Art.156 bis
(Female genital mutilation)

Anyone who performs to the detriment of another person, or forces that person to undergo, excision, infibulation or any other mutilation of all or part of the labia majora, labia minora or removal of the clitoris, is punished with imprisonment and disqualification of the third degree.
Outside cases of complicity, anyone who induces a person to undergo the acts referred to in the preceding paragraph is punished with imprisonment of the second degree.".

## Art.11

(Forced sterilisation)

After article 154 of the Criminal Code the following article is inserted:

"Art.154 bis
(Forced sterilisation)

Whoever performs a surgical intervention that has the purpose and effect of permanently interrupting the reproductive capacity of a person without their prior informed consent or without the person understanding its effects, shall be punished with third degree imprisonment.
Fourth degree imprisonment combined with fourth degree disqualification shall apply if the offender exercises a health profession.".

## Art.12

(Ill-treatment against family members and cohabitants, domestic violence)

Article 235 of the Criminal Code is replaced by the following:

"Art.235
(Ill-treatment against family members and cohabitants, domestic violence)

Whoever uses ill-treatment against a family member or in any case a cohabitant, or against a person who was or is bound by an affective relationship, even not implying cohabitation, shall be punished with second degree imprisonment.
The imprisonment is of the fourth degree if one of the aggravating events indicated in article 156 results from the act and of the fifth degree if death results.".

## Art.13

(Discrimination, hatred or violence on racial, ethnic, national, religious grounds or related to sexual orientation and gender)

Article 179 bis of the Criminal Code is replaced by the following:

"Art.179 bis
(Discrimination, hatred or violence on racial, ethnic, national, religious grounds or related to sexual orientation and gender identity)

Whoever disseminates in any way ideas based on racial or ethnic superiority or hatred, or incites to commit or commits acts of discrimination on racial, ethnic, national, religious grounds or related to sexual orientation and gender identity, or incites to commit or commits violence or acts of provocation to violence on racial, ethnic, national, religious grounds or related to sexual orientation and gender identity, shall be punished with second degree imprisonment.
For offences aggravated by the circumstance of racial, ethnic, national or religious discrimination, or related to sexual orientation and gender, referred to in article 90, paragraph 1, point 1, proceedings shall be instituted ex officio in every case.".

## Art.14

(Unacceptable justifications for offences)

For offences falling within the scope of application of the Convention, culture, uses and customs, religion, traditions or the motive of honour do not constitute grounds for justification or mitigation of the penalty.

## Art.15

(Criteria of jurisdiction)

The citizen or resident on the territory of the Republic who commits outside the territory of the State the offences contemplated by the Convention is subject to San Marino jurisdiction.
The person who commits outside the territory of the State the offences contemplated by the Convention against a San Marino citizen or resident on the territory of the Republic is subject to San Marino jurisdiction.
The provisions referred to in articles 154 bis, 156 bis and 176 bis, as introduced by this law, as well as the provisions referred to in articles 153, third paragraph no.1), 171, 172, 172 bis of the Criminal Code, shall also apply in the event that the acts do not constitute an offence in the legal system of the State in which they were committed, or even where the acts constitute an offence in the legal system of the State in which they were committed but criminal proceedings can be initiated only following the complaint of the victim or an action brought by the State of the place where the offence was committed.
No proceedings shall be instituted pursuant to the first and second paragraphs where one of the conditions provided for by article 7 of the Criminal Code applies.

## Art.16

(Aggravating circumstances)

The following constitute further aggravating circumstances of the offences contemplated by the provisions referred to in articles 154 bis, 156 bis and 176 bis, 235 as introduced or amended by this law, as well as the provisions referred to in articles 171 and 172 bis of the Criminal Code when the offender has committed the offence:
a)	against a minor or in the presence of the latter;
b)	against a person who is in a condition of infirmity or mental deficiency or state of pregnancy;
c)	with the use or threat of a weapon;
d)	or any other offence, correlated more than once over a certain period of time.
Abuse of family relations, constituting an aggravating circumstance provided for by article 90, point 2) of the Criminal Code, shall also mean abuse perpetrated by a former spouse or partner.
In the presence of the above aggravating circumstances the judge may increase the penalty by one
degree.

## Art.17

(Ex officio proceedings and withdrawal of complaint)

Article 178 of the Criminal Code is replaced as follows:

"Art.178
(Ex officio proceedings and withdrawal of complaint)

For the prosecution of the misdemeanours provided for by articles 175 and 177 a complaint by the injured party is required.
For the offences referred to in the preceding paragraph, withdrawal of the complaint can no longer be effected after expiry of the term referred to in the third paragraph of article 7 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.
For the offences contemplated by the provisions referred to in articles 153, third paragraph no.1), 154 bis, 156 bis, 171, 172, 172 bis, 176, 176 bis, withdrawal of the complaint by the injured party does not extinguish the offence and has no effect on any criminal proceedings instituted.
In the event that the injured party is a minor, the limitation period for the offence, as well as the time limit for filing the complaint, begins to run from the attainment of the age of majority.
Proceedings shall be instituted ex officio if the act is committed by an ascendant, guardian or adopter or by the person who has care or custody of the injured party.
Proceedings shall also be instituted ex officio if the act is committed in conjunction with a misdemeanour prosecutable ex officio, or in case of repeated recidivism.".

## Art.18

(Resident status of victims of forced marriage)

The Permanent Council Commission for Foreign Affairs, Emigration and Immigration, upon request of the interested party and after verification of the requirements to obtain it, shall grant residence to victims of the offence referred to in article 176 bis of the Criminal Code who, having been taken to another State for the purpose of contracting a forced marriage, have lost their resident status due to the occurrence of the condition referred to in article 17, paragraph 5, letter a) of Law 28 June 2010 no.118 and subsequent amendments.

## Art.19

(Exemption from taxes for acts performed in the interest of the victim)

The exemption from taxes granted by article 17, seventh paragraph of Law no.97/2008 must be understood as extended to all acts performed in the interest of the victim of violence in the context of civil, criminal and administrative proceedings.

## Art.20

(Activities of the Authority for Equal Opportunities)

In order to ensure the effectiveness and efficacy of the provisions of Law 20 June 2008 no.97, Delegated Decree 31 May 2012 no.60 and this law and to recognise the full operational autonomy of the Authority for Equal Opportunities, within ninety days from the entry into force of this law a specific delegated decree shall be issued providing for the interventions necessary for this purpose.

## Art.21

(Transitional provisions)

In order to allow the preparation and implementation, within the necessary technical timeframes, by the competent services and structures of the measures of prevention, protection, punishment and assistance guaranteed by this law in favour of victims of the offences of violence contemplated therein, the faculty regulated by the preceding article 8 must be understood as granted from the sixth month following the entry into force of this law.

## Art.22

(Entry into force)

This law shall enter into force on the fifth day following that of its legal publication.
