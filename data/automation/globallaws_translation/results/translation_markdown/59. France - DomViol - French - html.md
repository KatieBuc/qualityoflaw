### Article 222-8

Version in force since 11/07/2025
Version in force since 11 July 2025
Modified by LAW No. 2025-623 of 9 July 2025 - art. 1
The offence defined in
article 222-7
is punished by twenty years' criminal imprisonment when it is committed:
1° Against a minor under fifteen years of age;
2° Against a person whose particular vulnerability, due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or state of pregnancy, is apparent or known to its perpetrator;
2° bis Against a person whose state of psychological or physical subjection, within the meaning of article 223-15-3, is known to its perpetrator;
3° Against a legitimate or natural ascendant or against adoptive parents;
4° Against a magistrate, a juror, a lawyer, a public or ministerial officer, a member or agent of the International Criminal Court, a member of the national gendarmerie, an official of the national police, customs, prison administration or any other person holding public authority, a firefighter or marine firefighter, a sworn building or group of buildings caretaker or an agent exercising building caretaking or surveillance functions for residential buildings on behalf of a lessor pursuant to article
L. 271-1
of the internal security code, in the exercise of or by reason of their functions, when the victim's status is apparent or known to the perpetrator;
4° bis A Against a person exercising a private security activity mentioned in articles
L. 611-1
or
L. 621-1
of the internal security code in the exercise of or by reason of their functions, when the victim's status is apparent or known to the perpetrator;
4° bis Against a teacher or any member of staff working in school educational establishments, against an agent of a public passenger transport network operator or any person entrusted with a public service mission, as well as against a health professional or a person working within a health establishment, health centre, health house, birth centre, private practice office of a health profession, pharmacy, home health service provider, medical biology laboratory, social or medico-social establishment or service, in the exercise of or by reason of their functions, when the victim's status is apparent or known to the perpetrator;
4° ter Against the spouse, ascendants or descendants in direct line or against any other person habitually living at the home of the persons mentioned in 4°, 4° bis A and 4° bis, by reason of the functions exercised by the latter;
5° Against a witness, victim or civil party, either to prevent them from reporting the facts, filing a complaint or testifying in court, or by reason of their report or complaint, or because of their testimony before a national court or before the International Criminal Court;
5° bis and 5° ter (repealed)
5° quater Against a person who engages in prostitution, including occasionally, if the facts are committed in the exercise of this activity;
6° By the victim's spouse or cohabitant or the partner bound to the victim by a civil solidarity pact;
6° bis Against a person in order to compel them to contract a marriage or conclude a union or by reason of their refusal to contract this marriage or this union;
7° By a person holding public authority or entrusted with a public service mission in the exercise of or on the occasion of the exercise of their functions or mission;
7° bis By a person exercising a private security activity mentioned in articles L. 611-1 or L. 621-1 of the internal security code in the exercise of or on the occasion of the exercise of their functions or mission;
8° By several persons acting as perpetrator or accomplice;
9° With premeditation or with ambush;
10° With use or threat of a weapon;
11° By a person acting in a state of manifest intoxication or under the manifest influence of narcotic drugs.
The penalty incurred is increased to thirty years' criminal imprisonment when the offence defined in article 222-7 is committed:
a) Against a minor under fifteen years of age by a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant or by any other person having authority over the minor;
b) While a minor witnesses the facts and these are committed by the victim's spouse or cohabitant or the partner bound to the victim by a civil solidarity pact or, if the victim is a minor, by a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant or by any other person having authority over the minor victim.
The first two paragraphs of
article 132-23
relating to the safety period are applicable to the offences provided for by this article.
