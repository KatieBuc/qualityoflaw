# THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF THE REPUBLIC OF EL SALVADOR, CONSIDERING:

THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF THE REPUBLIC OF EL SALVADOR, CONSIDERING:
1.	That the Constitution of the Republic in its article 32, recognizes the Family as the fundamental basis of society, and the State is obligated to enact the necessary legislation for its protection and to create the appropriate agencies and services for its integration, welfare and social, cultural and economic development;
2.	That it is the responsibility of the State to adapt internal legislation to international Treaties and Conventions relating to the family, women and children, in order to comply with article 144 of the Constitution of the Republic;
3.	That violence committed by or against any member of the family constitutes a constant aggression against the right to a life free from fear, against the physical, psychological, moral and sexual integrity of the human person and their dignity and security;
4.	That intrafamily violence is a complex social phenomenon that has remained hidden, which has enabled the impunity of the offender and the lack of protection of the victim; and,
5.	THEREFORE, That in order to prevent, punish and eradicate intrafamily violence and confront it in all its magnitude, it is convenient to enact the necessary and appropriate legislation, in exercise of its constitutional powers and at the initiative of Deputies Mercedes Gloria Salguero Gross, Julio Antonio Gamero Quintanilla, José Rafael Machuca Zelaya, Alfonso Aristides Alvarenga, Ana Guadalupe Martínez Menéndez, Carmen Elena Calderón de Escalón, Walter René Araujo Morales, José Eduardo Sancho Castañeda, Gustavo Rogelio Salinas Olmedo, David Acuña, Herbert Maurice Aguilar Zepeda, Amado Aguiluz Aguiluz, Alex René Aguirre Guevara, Sonia del Carmen Aguiñada C., Selin Ernesto Alabí Mendoza, José Gilberto Alegría Morales, José Merzan López, Alfredo Arbizú Zelaya, Oscar Armando Salinas, Arturo Argumedo, Juan Antonio Ascencio Oliva, José Ramón Benítez, Félix Blanco, Jorge Alberto Carranza Alvarez, José Armando Cienfuegos Mendoza, Luis Alberto Cruz, Eugenio Chicas Martínez, Jorge Augusto Díaz Rivas, Elí Avileo Díaz, Juan Duch Martínez, Miguel Antonio Espinal Lazo, Hermes Alcides Flores, Francisco Guillermo Flores Pérez, José Victor García, Josefina de Maza, Norma

DECREES the following:
LAW AGAINST INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE

## CHAPTER I Fundamental Provisions Articles 1 to 5 Purposes

### ARTICLE 1

This law has the following purposes
1. To establish adequate mechanisms to prevent, sanction and eradicate intrafamily violence, in the relationships of family members or in any other interpersonal relationship of said members, whether or not they share the same dwelling;
2. To apply the preventive, precautionary and protective measures necessary to guarantee the life, integrity and dignity of victims of intrafamily violence;
3. To regulate rehabilitation measures for offenders; and,
4. TO PROVIDE SPECIAL PROTECTION TO VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE IN COUPLE RELATIONSHIPS, CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS, ELDERLY PERSONS AND PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES. THIS SPECIAL PROTECTION IS NECESSARY TO REDUCE THE INEQUALITY OF POWER THAT EXISTS AMONG THE PERSONS WHO CONSTITUTE A FAMILY AND TO TAKE INTO ACCOUNT THE SPECIAL SITUATION OF EACH ONE OF THEM.
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
For the purposes of this law, family members are understood to be the relationships between spouses, ex-spouses, cohabitants, ex-cohabitants, ascendants, descendants, collateral relatives by consanguinity, affinity, adoption, those subject to guardianship or custody, as well as any other interpersonal relationship that may generate this type of violence.
Guiding Principles
Article cited in: 6 judgments ARTICLE 2
In the application and interpretation of this law, the following principles must be taken into account:

1. RESPECT FOR THE LIFE, DIGNITY AND PHYSICAL, PSYCHOLOGICAL, SEXUAL AND PATRIMONIAL INTEGRITY OF THE PERSON;
D.L. N° 591, 18 DE DICIEMBRE DE 2013; D.O. N° 11, T. 402, 20 DE ENERO DE 2014.
2. The equality of rights of men, women and sons and daughters;
3. The right to a dignified life free from violence, in the public sphere as well as in the private sphere;
4. The protection of the family and of each of the persons who constitute it; and,
5. THE PRINCIPLES ESTABLISHED IN THE COMPREHENSIVE SPECIAL LAW FOR A LIFE FREE FROM VIOLENCE FOR WOMEN, LAW OF EQUALITY, EQUITY AND ERADICATION OF DISCRIMINATION AGAINST WOMEN, AND THE OTHER PRINCIPLES CONTAINED IN INTERNATIONAL CONVENTIONS AND TREATIES AND THE CURRENT FAMILY LEGISLATION.
D.L. N° 591, 18 DE DICIEMBRE DE 2013; D.O. N° 11, T. 402, 20 DE ENERO DE 2014.
Concept and Forms of Intrafamily Violence
Article cited in: 4 judgments ARTICLE 3
Intrafamily violence constitutes any action or omission, direct or indirect, that causes harm, physical, sexual, psychological suffering or death to the members of a family.
The forms of intrafamily violence are:
1. Psychological violence: Direct or indirect action or omission whose purpose is to control or degrade the actions, behaviors, beliefs and decisions of other persons, by means of intimidation, manipulation, direct or indirect threat, humiliation, isolation or any other conduct or omission that produces harm to psychological health, self-determination, integral development and personal possibilities;
2. Physical violence: Actions, behaviors or omissions that threaten or injure the physical integrity of a person;
3. Sexual violence: Actions that force a person to maintain physical or verbal sexualized contacts, or to participate in them through force, intimidation, coercion, blackmail, bribery, manipulation, threat or other mechanism that nullifies or limits personal will. Likewise, it shall be considered sexual violence when the aggressor forces the victim to perform any of these acts with third parties.
4. PATRIMONIAL VIOLENCE: ACTION OR OMISSION BY SOMEONE WHO AFFECTS OR PREVENTS THE ADEQUATE ATTENTION TO THE NEEDS OF THE FAMILY OR ANY OF THE PERSONS REFERRED TO IN THIS LAW; DAMAGES, LOSES, REMOVES, DESTROYS, RETAINS, DIVERTS OR APPROPRIATES OBJECTS, INSTRUMENTS OR GOODS
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
Scope of the Law
Article cited in: 6 judgments ARTICLE 4

This law shall be applied preventively and shall sanction acts of intrafamily violence, without prejudice to criminal liability.
APPLICATION
Article cited in: one judgment ARTICLE 5
FOR THE FULFILLMENT OF THIS LAW, THE FAMILY AND PEACE COURTS, THE PUBLIC PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE, THE MINISTRY OF GOVERNANCE, THE SALVADORAN INSTITUTE FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF WOMEN AND THE GOVERNMENTAL INSTITUTIONS THAT PROTECT THE FAMILY, WOMEN, CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS, PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES AND ELDERLY PERSONS SHALL INTERVENE.
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.

## CHAPTER II

State policies for the prevention of intrafamily violence Articles 6 to 9 Policy Objectives

### ARTICLE 6

It is the obligation of the State to prevent, sanction and eradicate intrafamily violence and for that purpose the following actions shall be developed:
1. To incorporate into school, academic, formal and non-formal technical training, the teaching of ethical, civic and social values; respect for the dignity of the human person, for the rights and duties of family members, children, persons with disabilities and elderly persons in accordance with current legislation and international instruments ratified by El Salvador;
2. To carry out dissemination campaigns, with the purpose of raising awareness in society about the aforementioned social problem, publicizing the scope of this law and speaking out against acts of intrafamily violence;
3. TO PROMOTE THE STUDY AND INVESTIGATION OF THE CAUSES AND CONSEQUENCES OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE, ITS INDICATORS, ITS DYNAMICS AND THE WAY TO PREVENT IT;
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
4. To establish effective legal mechanisms to assist victims of intrafamily violence, through simple, agile and formality-free procedures that enable the adoption of precautionary measures;
5. To promote the active participation of public entities and civil society organizations dedicated to the protection of children, the family, women, persons with disabilities and elderly persons, for the development of preventive and control work in the execution of precautionary and protective measures for victims of intrafamily violence and the rehabilitation of offenders;
6. TO CREATE WITHIN THE NATIONAL CIVIL POLICE A SPECIALIZED DIVISION IN THE ATTENTION AND HANDLING OF CASES OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE AND DEFENSE OF HUMAN RIGHTS;"
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
7. TO PROMOTE THE TRAINING OF PERSONNEL OF INSTITUTIONS INVOLVED IN THE DYNAMICS OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE; AS WELL AS, IN A SPECIAL MANNER, THE

PERMANENT TRAINING OF OFFICIALS AND FORENSIC EXPERTS, SO THAT THEY ASSUME AN EFFECTIVE ROLE IN ITS ERADICATION;"
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. NO 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
8. To raise awareness among judicial officials competent to resolve acts of intrafamily violence;
9. TO INCORPORATE INTO THE STUDY PROGRAMS OF HIGHER EDUCATION CAREERS, OF STATE AND PRIVATE UNIVERSITIES AND OF THE NATIONAL PUBLIC SECURITY ACADEMY, TRAINING IN THE DYNAMICS OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE, AS WELL AS THE CORRESPONDING LEGAL REGULATIONS, FORMS OF PREVENTION AND ITS TREATMENT.
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002. GOVERNING ENTITY

### ARTICLE 6-A

THE SALVADORAN INSTITUTE FOR WOMEN'S DEVELOPMENT SHALL ACT AS THE GOVERNING BODY RESPONSIBLE FOR DESIGNING, DIRECTING, ADVISING, COORDINATING AND ENSURING COMPLIANCE WITH POLICIES, PROGRAMS, PLANS AND PROJECTS RELATED TO THE PREVENTION AND ATTENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE.
FOR THE EFFECTIVE FULFILLMENT OF ITS MISSION, THE SALVADORAN INSTITUTE FOR WOMEN'S DEVELOPMENT SHALL PROMOTE THE PARTICIPATION OF GOVERNMENTAL INSTITUTIONS AND NON-GOVERNMENTAL ORGANIZATIONS, LOCAL GOVERNMENTS, PRIVATE ENTERPRISE, CHURCHES, INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS AND OTHERS; AND SHALL ESTABLISH THE NECESSARY COORDINATION MECHANISMS TO INTEGRATE THE DIFFERENT INSTITUTIONS OF THE STATE AND SOCIETY TO PREVENT, ATTEND TO, PROTECT AND CONTRIBUTE TO RESOLVING THE PROBLEM OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
Protective Measures ARTICLE 7
To prevent, punish and eradicate the different forms of domestic violence, the following measures are established:
1.	Judicial Order to the aggressor to refrain from harassing, pursuing, intimidating, threatening or engaging in other forms of mistreatment against the victims of violence or any other member of the family group who may or may not share the same dwelling;
2.	Judicial Order to the persons involved in the reported acts of violence to refrain from engaging in acts of harassment, intimidation, provocation, threats or other similar acts that may give rise to or foster domestic violence and the others indicated by the Family Procedural Law;
3.	Prohibit the aggressor from threatening the victim both in the private sphere and in the public sphere;
4.	Prohibit the aggressor from consuming alcoholic beverages, narcotics, hallucinogens or substances that generate physical or psychological dependence at the prudent discretion of the judge;

5.	Judicial Order to the aggressor to leave the common domicile immediately. If resistance occurs, assistance from the National Civil Police shall be sought;
6.	Establish for the victim, if so requested, a different domicile from the common one, which protects them from future aggressions;
7.	Judicial Order for entry into the dwelling, when domestic violence seriously endangers the physical, sexual, psychological and patrimonial integrity of any of its inhabitants;
8.	SUSPEND THE AGGRESSOR'S PERMIT TO CARRY WEAPONS WHILE THE PROTECTIVE MEASURES ARE IN FORCE AND ORDER THE CONFISCATION OF THE WEAPONS IN THEIR POSSESSION;
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
9.	PROVISIONALLY SUSPEND FROM THE AGGRESSOR THE PERSONAL CARE, CUSTODY, UPBRINGING AND EDUCATION OF THEIR MINOR CHILDREN AND THEIR RIGHT TO VISIT THEM IN CASE OF AGGRESSION;
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
10.	Prohibit the aggressor's access to the permanent or temporary domicile of the victim and to their place of work or study;
11.	ESTABLISH A PROVISIONAL CHILD SUPPORT PAYMENT; ONCE ESTABLISHED, ENFORCEMENT SHALL PROCEED EX OFFICIO;
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
12.	Grant the exclusive use for a determined period of the household furnishings of the dwelling to the victim. Special safeguards must be provided for the dwelling and furnishings protected under the family patrimony regime;
13.	Issue a Judicial Order of protection and police assistance, directed to the Public Security authority of their neighborhood. The victim shall carry a copy of this order so that they may turn to the nearest authority in case of threat of aggression outside their domicile; and,
14.	Any other measure provided for in the current family legal system. Failure to Appear at a Judicial Proceeding
Article cited in: 7 judgments ARTICLE 8
FAILURE TO APPEAR AT A JUDICIAL PROCEEDING SHALL BE SANCTIONED WITH THE EQUIVALENT OF THREE TO TEN DAYS' SALARY; IF IT CANNOT BE DETERMINED, THE BASE SHALL BE THE MINIMUM WAGE IN FORCE IN THE PLACE AND AT THE TIME OF THE RESOLUTION.
TO MAKE THIS SANCTION EFFECTIVE, THE JUDGE SHALL NOTIFY THE OFFENDER OF THE RESPECTIVE RESOLUTION, WHO MAY PRESENT THE PERTINENT EXCULPATORY EVIDENCE WITHIN THE TERM OF TEN WORKING DAYS. WHETHER OR NOT THE OFFERED EVIDENCE IS PRESENTED, A DECISION SHALL BE MADE WITHIN THE TERM OF TWENTY-FOUR HOURS.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.

Duration of Measures ARTICLE 9
THE DURATION OF THE PREVENTIVE, PRECAUTIONARY OR PROTECTIVE MEASURES IMPOSED ON AGGRESSORS SHALL BE ESTABLISHED BY THE JUDGE ACCORDING TO THE CIRCUMSTANCES, RECURRENCES AND IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE REGULATIONS OF THE FAMILY PROCEDURAL LAW AND THE COMPREHENSIVE SPECIAL LAW FOR A LIFE FREE OF VIOLENCE FOR WOMEN.
D. L. No. 546, NOVEMBER 24, 2016; D. O. No. 232, T. 413, DECEMBER 13, 2016.
WHEN THE MEASURES HAVE LAPSED AND ARE NOT EXTENDED EX OFFICIO, THE VICTIM SHALL HAVE THE RIGHT TO REQUEST THAT OTHERS BE DECREED OR THAT THOSE ALREADY DECREED BE EXTENDED.
THE REQUEST SHALL BE TAKEN BY THE COMPETENT COURT ON DUTY; WHEN IT IS NOT POSSIBLE TO DO SO BEFORE THE COURT HEARING THE CASE, AND IT SHALL BE STATED THEREIN WHETHER THESE HAD ALREADY BEEN DECREED PREVIOUSLY, AS WELL AS THE COURT THAT ISSUED THEM FOR THE PURPOSE OF CONSOLIDATION AS APPROPRIATE.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.

## CHAPTER III Procedure Articles 10 to 36

FIRST SECTION Police Intervention Articles 10 to 36 Notice to the National Civil Police

### ARTÍCULO 10

Whenever the National Civil Police has knowledge or receives notice that a person is a victim of intrafamily violence, it must take the necessary measures to prevent said person from being mistreated and must carry out the following actions:
1.	IF THE VICTIM STATES THAT HE OR SHE HAS SUFFERED BLOWS OR WOUNDS EVEN IF THEY ARE NOT VISIBLE, EMOTIONAL DAMAGE OR WHEN HE OR SHE IS UNCONSCIOUS, OR IN ANY CASE REQUIRING MEDICAL ATTENTION, IT MUST ASSIST HIM OR HER AND MAKE THE NECESSARY ARRANGEMENTS FOR HIM OR HER TO RECEIVE THE MEDICAL TREATMENT NEEDED AND PROVIDE TRANSPORTATION TO A CARE CENTER OR MEDICAL SERVICE WHERE HE OR SHE CAN BE TREATED;
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. NO 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
2.	If the victim or family members express concern for his or her personal safety, that of his or her sons, daughters or any other family member, it must make the necessary arrangements to take them to an appropriate place where they can be cared for;
3.	Advise the victim of intrafamily violence about the importance of preserving evidence;
4.	IT SHALL PROVIDE THE VICTIM WITH INFORMATION ABOUT THE RIGHTS CONFERRED BY THIS LAW AND OTHER INFORMATION RELATED TO THE MATTER, AND ABOUT GOVERNMENTAL OR PRIVATE SERVICES AVAILABLE TO VICTIMS OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE;
D.L. N° 591, 18 DE DICIEMBRE DE 2013; D.O. N° 11, T. 402, 20 DE ENERO DE 2014.

5.	IT SHALL DETAIN THE ALLEGED AGGRESSOR IF THE EXISTENCE AND PARTICIPATION OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE CONDUCT IS ESTABLISHED, WHEN THE ACTS CONSTITUTE OTHER CRIMES THAT ARE MANIFESTATIONS DERIVED FROM IT AND WHEN IT INVOLVES THE CRIME OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE CONTEMPLATED IN ART. 200 OF THE PENAL CODE. IN ALL CASES IT SHALL TAKE THE PERTINENT MEASURES TO PREVENT THE AGGRESSOR FROM CONTINUING WITH VIOLENT ACTS AND SHALL PROTECT THE VICTIM; and,
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
6.	IN ITS DUTY TO ASSIST VICTIMS, AND IN THOSE CASES IN WHICH INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE DOES NOT YET CONSTITUTE A CRIME; EVEN WHEN THE VICTIM DOES NOT REQUEST IT; THE NATIONAL CIVIL POLICE MAY ISSUE THE TEMPORARY SPECIAL PROTECTION MEASURE, WHICH SHALL CONSIST OF ORDERING THE ALLEGED AGGRESSOR TO LEAVE THE SCENE OF THE EVENTS, FOR A MAXIMUM PERIOD OF FORTY-EIGHT HOURS. DISOBEDIENCE TO THIS ORDER SHALL ENTAIL CRIMINAL LIABILITY.
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
D.L. N° 591, 18 DE DICIEMBRE DE 2013; D.O. N° 11, T. 402, 20 DE ENERO DE 2014.
Informe Policial ARTÍCULO 11
HAVING CARRIED OUT THE PROVISIONS OF THE PRECEDING ARTICLE, THE NATIONAL CIVIL POLICE SHALL IMMEDIATELY NOTIFY THE COMPETENT COURT, ACCOMPANYING SAID NOTICE WITH A REPORT OF THE PROCEEDINGS CARRIED OUT. SAID REPORT SHALL INCLUDE IN A CONCISE MANNER ANY STATEMENT BY THE VICTIM REGARDING THE FREQUENCY AND SEVERITY OF THE INCIDENTS OF INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE; AND IN ADDITION, IT MUST REPORT ON THE ADOPTION OF THE SPECIAL TEMPORARY PROTECTION MEASURE IN FAVOR OF THE VICTIM, WHEN IT HAS BEEN USED.
IF A PERSON HAS BEEN DETAINED IN FLAGRANTE DELICTO, THE CRIMINAL PROCEDURE MUST BE FOLLOWED.
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
Prueba Testimonial ARTÍCULO 12
Agents of the National Civil Police may be accepted as witnesses if the aggressor is captured in flagrante delicto.
SECCIONSEGUNDA INTERVENCIONDEL MINISTERIOPUBLICO Denuncia ARTÍCULO 13
ANY PERSON WHO HAS KNOWLEDGE OF AN ACT CONSTITUTING INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE MAY REPORT IT OR GIVE NOTICE TO THE NATIONAL CIVIL POLICE, TO THE COMPETENT COURTS AND TO THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC. THE COMPLAINT MAY BE MADE IN WRITING OR VERBALLY, IN PERSON OR THROUGH AN ATTORNEY AND IN IT THE PRECAUTIONARY, PREVENTIVE OR PROTECTION MEASURES DEEMED PERTINENT MAY BE REQUESTED. WHEN IT IS A VERBAL COMPLAINT IT SHALL BE RECORDED IN MINUTES. THE COMPLAINT SHALL CONTAIN, INSOFAR AS POSSIBLE, A DETAILED ACCOUNT OF THE ACT, WITH INDICATION OF THE AGGRESSOR, INJURED PARTIES, WITNESSES AND OTHER ELEMENTS THAT MAY LEAD TO ITS VERIFICATION.
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
Obligación de dar aviso a los Funcionarios Competentes
Artículo citado en: una sentencia ARTÍCULO 14
The following shall have the obligation to give notice of acts constituting intrafamily violence:
1.	Officials who become aware of them in the exercise of their functions; and,
2.	DOCTORS, PHARMACISTS, NURSES, TEACHERS AND OTHER PERSONS WHO PRACTICE PROFESSIONS RELATED TO HEALTH, EDUCATION AND SOCIAL ASSISTANCE, WHO BECOME AWARE OF SUCH ACTS WHEN PROVIDING THEIR SERVICES WITHIN THEIR PROFESSION.
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
Denuncia cuando la víctima fuere Menor de Edad, Incapaz o Discapacitado
Artículo citado en: una sentencia ARTÍCULO 15
When the victim is a minor, incapacitated or disabled, the acts may be reported by their legal representatives, by the victim and by welfare, social and educational institutions, as well as any authority or person who has knowledge of the acts of intrafamily violence.
Actuación de la Procuraduría General de la República ARTÍCULO 16
THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC, WHEN IT HAS KNOWLEDGE OF ACTS CONSTITUTING INTRAFAMILY VIOLENCE, BY NOTICE OR BY COMPLAINT FILED BEFORE IT, SHALL SUMMON THE PARTIES IN CONFLICT AND SHALL SEEK CONCILIATION; IF THE VICTIM REQUESTS IT OR IF IT IS APPROPRIATE. IF FROM THE FACTS NARRATED THE IMMEDIATE NEED TO PROTECT THE VICTIM IS EVIDENT, THE PERTINENT PROTECTION MEASURES SHALL BE REQUESTED. IF THERE IS NO CONCILIATION OR IT HAS NOT BEEN REQUESTED, THE PROCEDURE REFERRED TO IN THIS LAW SHALL BE INITIATED BEFORE THE COMPETENT COURT.
LIKEWISE, THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC IS OBLIGATED TO PROVIDE EVIDENCE IF THE JUDICIAL PROCEDURE REFERRED TO IN THIS LAW IS INITIATED.
D.L. NW 892, 27 DE JUNIO DE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 DE JULIO DE 2002.
Actuación de la Fiscalía General de la República ARTÍCULO 17
When the act of intrafamily violence constitutes a crime, the Office of the Attorney General of the Republic is obligated to investigate and provide evidence in the criminal proceedings initiated in the corresponding courts.

Remisión de Diligencias ARTÍCULO 18
If the parties in conflict do not appear for the summons a second time or by compulsion, or appearing, they cannot be brought to agreement, the Assistant Attorneys of the Attorney General of the Republic must immediately send a report of the actions taken together with the respective proceedings to the competent judicial official, for the initiation of the process referred to in section three of this chapter.
Visitas Periódicas a Dependencias Policiales ARTÍCULO 19
The Attorney General of the Republic, the Attorney General of the Republic and the Ombudsman for the Defense of Human Rights, personally or through their Auxiliary Agents and Assistant Attorneys, shall visit monthly the corresponding Division of the National Civil Police, in order to learn about the acts of intrafamily violence investigated, with the purpose of providing a public statistical report every three months and thus recommend the pertinent measures to the corresponding authorities.
In all cases the privacy of the assaulted persons and the aggressor shall be respected, consequently no data that directly or indirectly enables the identification of the victims shall be published.

### ARTICLE 22

In proceedings conducted pursuant to this law, the respective judge shall apply the principles of orality, immediacy, concentration, celerity, equality, economy, probity and officiousness. In the evaluation of evidence, Judges shall apply sound judgment.
Measures

THE PROTECTION MEASURES SHALL REMAIN IN FORCE; NOTWITHSTANDING THE INITIATION OF CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS IN CASE OF A CRIME, AND THE PEACE COURTS, FAMILY COURTS OR SPECIALIZED COURTS FOR A LIFE FREE OF VIOLENCE AND DISCRIMINATION FOR WOMEN SHALL PROVIDE THE CORRESPONDING FOLLOW-UP.

Article cited in: 2 judgments ARTICLE 25
THE EXPERT OPINION SHALL BE ISSUED IN WRITING AND SHALL BE SUBMITTED NO LATER THAN SEVENTY-TWO HOURS AFTER BEING REQUESTED. IN CASE THE OPINION IS REQUIRED URGENTLY, IT MAY BE RENDERED VERBALLY AND SHALL BE RECORDED IN THE MINUTES.
IF FROM THE OPINION RECEIVED IT RESULTS THAT THE ACT OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE CONSTITUTES A CRIME, THE FAMILY OR PEACE JUDGE SHALL CONTINUE THE PROCEEDINGS FOR THE SOLE PURPOSE OF ENFORCING THE IMPOSED MEASURES AND SHALL CERTIFY THE RELEVANT MATTERS TO THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC TO INITIATE THE CORRESPONDING PROCEEDINGS.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.

Scheduling and Summons for Hearing ARTICLE 26
IMMEDIATELY UPON RECEIPT OF THE EXPERT OPINION AND IF THE ACT DOES NOT CONSTITUTE A CRIME, THE JUDGE SHALL SUMMON THE VICTIM AND THE ACCUSED TO A PRELIMINARY HEARING WITHIN A PERIOD OF FIVE WORKING DAYS, IN ORDER TO HEAR THE FACTS, IN WHICH THEY MAY OR MAY NOT BE ACCOMPANIED BY AN ATTORNEY OR AN ASSISTANT PROSECUTOR OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
Preliminary Hearing
Article cited in: 2 judgments ARTICLE 27
The victim and the accused shall personally attend the scheduled hearing and may both be accompanied by a lawyer, and minutes shall be taken.
The judge shall personally preside over said hearing and shall give the victim an opportunity under equal conditions to reaffirm, expand or modify the complaint and the accused to make his or her own assessments, admit to the facts or contradict them.
AFTER HEARING THEM, HE OR SHE SHALL PROMOTE A DIALOGUE WITH THE ATTENDEES ABOUT THE HARMFUL EFFECTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND ITS REPERCUSSIONS ON THE FAMILY AND SHALL PROPOSE MECHANISMS TO PREVENT THE REPETITION OF THE ACTS CONSTITUTING THE SAME, REGARDING WHICH CONCILIATION SHALL NOT PROCEED.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
He or she must also make the accused aware of the criminal sanctions that may be incurred if the violent action is repeated and of the measures that this law provides to sanction domestic violence.
Resolution
Article cited in: 2 judgments ARTICLE 28
In the same hearing, the judge, based on what is stated by those appearing, provided that the facts do not require proof and in consideration of commitments assumed by the accused and accepted by the victim, shall resolve:
1.	To establish as proven the facts constituting the reported domestic violence;
2.	TO ATTRIBUTE THE VIOLENCE TO THE PERSON OR PERSONS WHO GENERATED IT;
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
3.	To impose on the aggressor compliance with the commitment acquired by him or her in the hearing;
4.	To decree the preventive, precautionary or protection measures that are necessary, if they have not been previously agreed upon.
5.	TO IMPOSE ON THE AGGRESSOR THE OBLIGATION TO PAY THE VICTIM FOR THE DIRECT DAMAGES ARISING FROM THE VIOLENT CONDUCT OR BEHAVIOR, SUCH AS CASES OF

HEALTH SERVICES, PRICE OF MEDICINES, VALUE OF GOODS AND OTHER EXPENSES DERIVED FROM THE VIOLENCE EXERCISED;
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
6.	TO IMPOSE ON THE AGGRESSOR PSYCHOSOCIAL, PSYCHIATRIC OR SPECIALIZED SELF-HELP GROUP TREATMENT FOR DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, THROUGH ATTENDANCE AT THERAPIES ON DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, USING THE VARIOUS PROGRAMS DEVELOPED BY FAMILY PROTECTION INSTITUTIONS. THIS MEASURE MAY ALSO BE APPLIED FROM THE BEGINNING OF THE PROCEEDINGS AND IN ANY CASE PSYCHOSOCIAL FOLLOW-UP SHALL BE PROVIDED.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
D.L. NW 403, AUGUST 12, 2004; D.O. No. 178, T. 364, SEPTEMBER 27, 2004.
In the same resolution, the aggressor shall be warned of the criminal sanctions that will be incurred in case of non-compliance or repetition of acts of domestic violence.
Scheduling of the Public Hearing
Article cited in: 2 judgments ARTICLE 29
IF THE ACCUSED DOES NOT ADMIT TO THE FACTS THAT REQUIRE PROOF, HE OR SHE SHALL SCHEDULE A HEARING TO RECEIVE IT, WITHIN A PERIOD NOT EXCEEDING TEN WORKING DAYS AFTER THE PRELIMINARY HEARING, WITHIN WHICH THE INSPECTION AND PSYCHOSOCIAL INVESTIGATION OR ANY OTHER PROCEEDING SHALL BE CARRIED OUT.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
D.L. NW 403, AUGUST 12, 2004; D.O. No. 178, T. 364, SEPTEMBER 27, 2004.
Public Hearing ARTICLE 30
On the scheduled day, the parties shall be received in a public hearing and, orally, the statements of the witnesses and other evidence presented by the parties and those ordered by the judge shall be received.
Likewise, the studies of the social workers and opinions of the experts shall be evaluated. The parties and their lawyers may directly cross-examine the witnesses and experts.
Judgment ARTICLE 31
Once the evidence offered has been produced, the judge shall in the same hearing render his or her ruling and order the measures provided for in this law or absolve the accused of responsibility.
Remedies ARTICLE 32
The resolutions pronounced by the judge imposing preventive, precautionary or protection measures on the aggressor, or absolving the accused, shall be appealable before the Family Chambers, even if pronounced by a peace judge.

THE APPELLATE COURT SHALL RESOLVE THE APPEAL SOLELY UPON REVIEW OF THE PROCEEDINGS, WITHIN EIGHT WORKING DAYS AFTER HAVING RECEIVED IT; THIS RESOLUTION SHALL NOT ADMIT AN APPEAL FOR CASSATION.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
The appeal may be filed orally or in writing at the time of notification or within the following three
working days.
Control of the Execution of the Judgment
Article cited in: 8 judgments ARTICLE 33
DURING THE COURSE OF THE PROCEEDINGS AND AFTER THE SAME, THE JUDGE SHALL MONITOR FOR SUCH TIME AS HE OR SHE DEEMS APPROPRIATE THE RESULT OF THE MEASURES AND DECISIONS ADOPTED AND IMPOSED IN THE JUDGMENT THROUGH THE MULTIDISCIPLINARY TEAM ASSIGNED TO THE FAMILY COURT, WHICH SHALL PROVIDE REPORTS WITH THE FREQUENCY THAT THE JUDGE INDICATES.
D.L. NW 403, AUGUST 12, 2004; D.O. No. 178, T. 364, SEPTEMBER 27, 2004.
Non-Compliance with the Judgment
Article cited in: one judgment ARTICLE 34
WHENEVER IT IS POSSIBLE TO VERIFY NON-COMPLIANCE WITH THE PREVENTIVE, PRECAUTIONARY OR PROTECTION MEASURES IMPOSED BY THE JUDGE, AT ANY STAGE OF THE PROCEEDINGS, AS WELL AS THE AGREED COMMITMENTS, HE OR SHE SHALL ISSUE AN OFFICIAL LETTER TO THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC, WITH CERTIFICATION OF THE PERTINENT PASSAGES, SO THAT IT MAY FILE THE REQUEST FOR THE CRIME OF DISOBEDIENCE. WITHOUT PREJUDICE TO THE VICTIM BEING ABLE TO REPORT THE FACTS PERSONALLY TO THE SAME INSTITUTION OR REQUEST THE ASSISTANCE OF THE NATIONAL CIVIL POLICE, AS APPLICABLE.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.
Mandatory Appearance of Victims and Accused
Article cited in: 2 judgments ARTICLE 35
IN THE JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS PROVIDED FOR IN THIS SECTION, THE APPEARANCE OF VICTIMS AND ACCUSED AT THE HEARINGS TO WHICH THEY ARE SUMMONED SHALL BE MANDATORY, EXCEPT FOR JUST CAUSE.
THE NON-APPEARANCE OF THE VICTIMS OR THE ACCUSED SHALL CAUSE THEM TO INCUR A FINE THAT THE JUDGE SHALL IMPOSE AT SAID HEARING WITHOUT PREJUDICE TO THE POWER HE OR SHE HAS TO COMPEL THEIR APPEARANCE.
THE FINES SHALL BE ENFORCED OR RENDERED WITHOUT EFFECT IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE PROVISIONS OF ARTICLE 8 OF THIS LAW.
D.L. NW 892, JUNE 27, 2002; D.O. No. 137, T. 356, JULY 24, 2002.

## CAPÍTULO IV

General Provisions and Effectiveness Articles 37 to 43 Confidentiality of Proceedings and Procedures

### ARTÍCULO 37

The proceedings and procedures conducted in application of this Law shall be confidential except for the parties, lawyers, attorneys, prosecutors, and specialized personnel who participate in them.
Legal Assistance ARTÍCULO 38
IN THE PROCEEDINGS AND PROCEDURES CONDUCTED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THIS LAW, THE PARTIES MAY OR MAY NOT BE ASSISTED BY A LAWYER.
IF THE PARTIES OR ONE OF THEM LACK ECONOMIC RESOURCES AND REQUEST LEGAL ASSISTANCE FROM THE JUDGE, THE STATE THROUGH THE ATTORNEY GENERAL'S OFFICE SHALL PROVIDE THEM WITH A SPECIFIC ATTORNEY TO ASSIST THEM.
D.L. NW 892, 27 JUNE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 JULY 2002.
Power of the judge ARTÍCULO 39
Judges may make use of public security to enforce their resolutions or orders. Request for Assistance to Organizations or Entities

### ARTÍCULO 40

JUDGES MAY REQUEST COLLABORATION FROM ALL PUBLIC OR PRIVATE ENTITIES AND ORGANIZATIONS DEDICATED TO THE PROTECTION OF CHILDREN, ADOLESCENTS, WOMEN, ELDERLY PERSONS AND PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES, SO THAT FREE ASSISTANCE AND ACCOMPANIMENT IS PROVIDED TO VICTIMS AFFECTED BY REPORTED ACTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, AND SO THAT THEY MAY ASSIST IN THE APPLICATION OF PROTECTION MEASURES IMPOSED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THIS LAW, THE FAMILY CODE, AND THE CODE FOR CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS.
JUDICIAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICIALS MAY ALLOW, WITH THE EXPRESS CONSENT OF THE VICTIM, PSYCHOLOGICAL AND EMOTIONAL ACCOMPANIMENT WHEN REPORTING THE ACT AND IN THE RESPECTIVE HEARINGS, WHETHER BY A NATURAL PERSON OR BY CIVIL SOCIETY ORGANIZATIONS WORKING FOR THE PREVENTION AND CARE OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, DURING THE JUDICIAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE PROCESS, WHICH SHALL BE RECORDED IN THE MINUTES. THE VICTIM MAY TERMINATE SUCH ACCOMPANIMENT AND REPLACE IT WITH ANOTHER PERSON OR INSTITUTION, IN THE SAME MANNER.
D.L. NW 892, 27 JUNE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 JULY 2002.

THE VICTIM SHALL ALSO HAVE THE RIGHT TO BE TECHNICALLY ASSISTED BY THE MULTIDISCIPLINARY TEAM ASSIGNED TO THE FAMILY COURT, FOR THE PERIOD ESTABLISHED BY IT.
D.L. NW 403, 12 AUGUST 2004; D.O. N° 178, T. 364, 27 SEPTEMBER 2004.
Suspension of Criminal Proceedings ARTÍCULO 41
If in the course of criminal proceedings the judge finds that the acts are subject to the application of this law, he or she shall suspend the proceedings and initiate the procedure provided in Section Three of Chapter III of this law, if he or she is competent. If not competent, he or she shall refer the proceedings to the competent judge. In any case, the actions taken by the investigating judge shall be valid.
MEASURES IN CRIMINAL MATTERS ARTÍCULO 42
WHEN DEALING WITH ACTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE SUBJECT TO CRIMINAL JURISDICTION AND PERSONS SUBJECT TO THIS LAW, THE JUDGE DURING THE PROCESSING OF THE PROCEEDINGS SHALL APPLY THE PERTINENT PREVENTIVE, PRECAUTIONARY AND PROTECTION MEASURES REGULATED IN THIS LEGISLATION.
THESE MAY BE MAINTAINED OR DECREED WHEN ISSUING THE CORRESPONDING SENTENCE AND TO ENSURE THEIR EFFECTIVE COMPLIANCE, THE COURTS OF BOTH JURISDICTIONS THAT HAVE INTERVENED IN THESE CASES SHALL EXCHANGE DIRECT INFORMATION ON THE STATUS OF THE PROCEEDINGS SO THAT IT MAY BE CONSIDERED IN THEIR JUDICIAL DECISION.
SUCH DECISION MAY BE MONITORED WITH THE SUPPORT OF THE PSYCHOSOCIAL CARE CENTERS OF THE JUDICIAL BRANCH, THE MULTIDISCIPLINARY TEAMS ASSIGNED TO THE FAMILY COURTS AND THE SPECIALIZED COURTS FOR A LIFE FREE OF VIOLENCE FOR WOMEN IN CASES INVOLVING CRIMES OF GENDER VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN, AND THOSE PUBLIC AND PRIVATE INSTITUTIONS; AS WELL AS NON-GOVERNMENTAL ORGANIZATIONS THAT PROVIDE CARE TO PERSONS IN DOMESTIC VIOLENCE.
D.L. NW 892, 27 JUNE 2002; D.O. N° 137, T. 356, 24 JULY 2002.
D.L. NW 403, 12 AUGUST 2004; D.O. N° 178, T. 364, 27 SEPTEMBER 2004.
D. L. No. 560, 8 DECEMBER 2016, D. 0. No. 240, T. 413, 23 DECEMBER 2016.
Prohibition of Immunity ARTÍCULO 43
In matters of Domestic Violence no immunity or privileges of any kind by reason of office shall be permitted.

Supplementary Rule ARTÍCULO 44
In everything not provided for in this law with respect to procedures and evaluation of evidence, the provisions of the Family Procedural Law and the Code of Civil Procedure shall apply.

Effectiveness
