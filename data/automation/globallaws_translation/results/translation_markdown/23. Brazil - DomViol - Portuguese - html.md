THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC, using the authority conferred upon him by article 180 of the Constitution, decrees the following Law:
GENERAL PART

### TÍTULO I

APPLICATION OF CRIMINAL LAW
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Anteriority of Law

###### Art. 1

º
- There is no crime without prior law defining it. There is no penalty without prior legal provision.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Criminal law in time

###### Art. 2º - No one may be punished for an act that a subsequent law ceases

to consider a crime, with the execution and criminal effects of the conviction sentence ceasing by virtue of it.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Parágrafo único - A subsequent law that in any way favors the agent applies to prior acts, even if decided by a final conviction sentence.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Exceptional or temporary law
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Art. 3º - An exceptional or temporary law, even after the expiration of

its duration period or the cessation of the circumstances that determined it, applies to acts committed during its validity.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
Time of crime

###### Art. 4º - The crime is considered committed at the moment of the action or

omission, even if the moment of the result is different.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
Territoriality

###### Art. 5º - Brazilian law applies, without prejudice to

conventions, treaties and rules of international law, to crimes committed in national territory.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

###### § 1º

For criminal purposes, Brazilian vessels and aircraft, of a public nature or in the service of the Brazilian government wherever they may be, as well as Brazilian aircraft and vessels, merchant or privately owned, that are, respectively, in the corresponding airspace or on the high seas, are considered as extensions of national territory.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

###### § 2º

Brazilian law is also applicable to crimes committed aboard foreign aircraft or vessels of private ownership, when the former are landed in national territory or in flight in the corresponding airspace, and the latter are in port or in Brazilian territorial waters.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
Place of crime
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

###### Art. 6º - The crime is considered committed in the place where the action

or omission occurred, in whole or in part, as well as where the result was produced or should have been produced.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
Extraterritoriality
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

###### Art. 7º - They are subject to Brazilian law, even if committed

abroad:
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
I - crimes:
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
a) against the life or liberty of the President of the Republic;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
b) against the property or public faith of the Union, the Federal District, a State, a Territory, a Municipality, a public company, mixed-capital company, autarchy or foundation established by the Public Authority;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
c) against public administration, by those in its service;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
d) of genocide, when the agent is Brazilian or domiciled in Brazil;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
II - crimes:
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
a) that, by treaty or convention, Brazil has undertaken to suppress;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
b) committed by a Brazilian;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
c) committed on Brazilian aircraft or vessels, merchant or privately owned, when in foreign territory and not tried there.
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

###### § 1º

In the cases of item I, the agent is punished according to Brazilian law, even if acquitted or convicted abroad.
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

###### § 2º

In the cases of item II, the application of Brazilian law depends on the concurrence of the following conditions:
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
a) the agent enters national territory;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
b) the act is also punishable in the country where it was committed;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
c) the crime is included among those for which Brazilian law authorizes extradition;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
d) the agent has not been acquitted abroad or has not served the sentence there;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
e) the agent has not been pardoned abroad or, for another reason, punishability is not extinguished, according to the most favorable law.
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

###### § 3º

Brazilian law also applies to crimes committed by a foreigner against a Brazilian outside Brazil, if, meeting the conditions provided in the previous paragraph:
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
a) extradition was not requested or was denied;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
b) there was a request from the Minister of Justice.
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)
Sentence served abroad
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Art. 8º - A sentence served abroad mitigates the sentence imposed in Brazil

for the same crime, when different, or is computed in it, when identical.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Effectiveness of foreign sentence
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Art. 9º - A foreign sentence, when the application of Brazilian law

produces the same consequences in the case, may be recognized in Brazil to:
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
I - oblige the convicted person to repair the damage, make restitutions and other civil effects;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
II - subject him to a security measure.
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Parágrafo único
- Recognition depends on:
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
a) for the effects provided in item I, a request from the interested party;
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
b) for other effects, the existence of an extradition treaty with the country from whose judicial authority the sentence emanated, or, in the absence of a treaty, a request from the Minister of Justice.
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Counting of period
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Art. 10

The starting day is included in the computation of the period. Days, months and years are counted by the common calendar.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Non-computable fractions of sentence
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Art. 11 - Fractions of a day are disregarded in custodial sentences and in

restrictive sentences of rights, and, in fines, fractions of cruzeiro.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Special legislation
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Art. 12 - The general rules of this Code apply to acts incriminated

by special law, if the latter does not provide otherwise.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

CRIME
Causal relationship
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Art. 13 - The result, on which the existence of the crime depends, is only

attributable to the one who caused it. The action or omission without which the result would not have occurred is considered a cause.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Supervening
independent cause
(Added by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 1º

- The supervening of a relatively independent cause excludes
attribution when, by itself, it produced the result; the prior acts, however,
are attributed to the person who committed them.
(Included by
Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Relevance
of omission
(Included by Law No.
7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 2

º
- An omission is criminally relevant when the person who omitted had a duty and
was able to act to prevent the result. The duty to act falls upon whoever:
(Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
a) has a legal obligation of care, protection or supervision;
(Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
b) otherwise, assumed responsibility to prevent the result;
(Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
c) by prior conduct, created the risk of the result occurring.
(Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

(Wording

(Wording given by Law
No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Art. 17 - An attempt is not punished when, due to absolute ineffectiveness of

the means or absolute impropriety of the object, it is impossible to consummate the crime.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

### TÍTULO IV

PARTICIPATION IN CRIMES

###### Art. 29 - Whoever, in any manner, participates in the crime incurs the

penalties imposed thereon, to the extent of his culpability.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 1

º
- If the participation is of lesser importance, the penalty may be
reduced by one sixth to one third.
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 2

º
- If any of the participants intended to participate in a less serious crime,
the penalty for that crime shall be applied to him; this penalty shall be increased up to half, in the event that
the more serious result was foreseeable.
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Non-communicable circumstances

###### Art. 30 - Circumstances and conditions of a

personal character are not communicated, except when they are elements of the crime.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Cases of impunity

###### Art. 31 - Agreement, determination or instigation and assistance, except

express provision to the contrary, are not punishable, if the crime does not reach, at
least, the attempt stage.
(Wording given by Law
No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### SEÇÃO I

PENALTIES OF DEPRIVATION OF LIBERTY
Imprisonment and detention

###### Art. 33 - The penalty of imprisonment must be served in a closed,

semi-open or open regime. Detention, in a semi-open or open regime, except
in case of need for transfer to a closed regime.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 1

º
- The following are considered:
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
a) closed regime the execution of the penalty in a maximum or medium security establishment;
b) semi-open regime the execution of the penalty in an agricultural, industrial colony or
similar establishment;
c) open regime the execution of the penalty in a halfway house or adequate establishment.

###### § 2

º
- Penalties of deprivation of liberty must be executed in
progressive form, according to the merit of the convicted person, observing the following criteria and
subject to the exceptions of transfer to a more rigorous regime:
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
a) the person convicted to a penalty exceeding 8 (eight) years must begin serving it in a closed
regime;
b) the non-recidivist convicted person, whose penalty exceeds 4 (four) years and does not exceed
8 (eight), may, from the beginning, serve it in a semi-open regime;
c) the non-recidivist convicted person, whose penalty is equal to or less than 4 (four) years,
may, from the beginning, serve it in an open regime.

###### § 3

º
- The determination of the initial regime for serving the penalty
shall be made with observance of the criteria provided in art. 59 of this Code.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 4

o
The person convicted for a crime against public administration
shall have the progression of regime for serving the penalty conditioned upon reparation
of the damage he caused, or the return of the product of the illicit act practiced, with legal
additions.
(Added by Law No. 10,763, of
12.11.2003)
Rules of the closed regime

###### Art. 34

- The convicted person shall be submitted, at the beginning of
serving the penalty, to a criminological classification examination for individualization of
execution.
(Wording given by Law No.
7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 1

º
- The convicted person is subject to work during the daytime period and to
isolation during nighttime rest.
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 2

º
- Work shall be in common within the establishment, in
conformity with the aptitudes or previous occupations of the convicted person, provided they are compatible
with the execution of the penalty.
(Wording given by
Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 3º

- External work is admissible, in the closed regime, in
public services or works.
(Wording given
by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Rules of the semi-open regime

###### Art. 35

- The rule of art. 34 of this Code,
caput, applies to the convicted person who begins serving the penalty in a semi-open regime.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 1

º
- The convicted person is subject to work in common during the
daytime period, in an agricultural, industrial colony or similar establishment.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 2

º
- External work is admissible, as well as attendance at
supplementary vocational courses, secondary or higher education.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Rules of the open regime

###### Art. 36 - The open regime is based on the self-discipline and sense of

responsibility of the convicted person.
(Wording given
by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 1º - The convicted person must, outside the establishment and without surveillance, work,

attend courses or engage in other authorized activity, remaining confined during the
nighttime period and on days off.
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### § 2º

The convicted person shall be transferred from the open regime, if he commits an act defined as
an intentional crime, if he frustrates the purposes of execution or if, being able, he does not pay the fine
cumulatively applied.
(Wording given by
Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Special regime

###### Art. 37 - Women serve their penalty in their own establishment,

observing the duties and rights inherent to their personal condition, as well as, where applicable, the provisions of this Chapter.
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Rights of the prisoner

###### Art. 38 - The prisoner retains all rights not affected by the loss of

liberty, imposing on all authorities respect for his physical and
moral integrity.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of
11.7.1984)
Work of the prisoner

###### Art. 39 - The work of the prisoner shall always be remunerated, being

guaranteed the benefits of Social Security.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Special legislation

###### Art. 40 - Special legislation shall regulate the matters provided for in arts

38 and 39 of this Code, as well as specify the duties and rights of the prisoner, the

(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)
Supervening mental illness

###### Art. 41 - The convict who develops mental illness must be committed

establishment.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209,
of 11.7.1984)
Deduction

###### Art. 42 - There shall be computed, in the custodial sentence and in the security

measure, the time of provisional detention, in Brazil or abroad, that of administrative

(Wording given by Law No. 7,209,
of 11.7.1984)

7,209, of July 11, 1984)

constitute or qualify the crime:
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of July 11, 1984)
I - recidivism;
(Wording given
by Law No. 7,209, of July 11, 1984)
II - the agent having committed the crime:
(Wording
given by Law No. 7,209, of July 11, 1984)
a) for a futile or base motive;
b) to facilitate or ensure the execution, concealment, impunity or advantage of
another crime;
c) by betrayal, by ambush, or by means of dissimulation, or another resource that hindered
or made the defense of the victim impossible;
d) with the use of poison, fire, explosive, torture or another insidious or cruel means, or one that
could result in common danger;
e) against an ascendant, descendant, sibling or spouse;
f) with abuse of authority or taking advantage of
domestic relations, cohabitation or hospitality, or with violence

of 2003)

(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of July 11, 1984)

(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of July 11, 1984)
IV - executes the crime, or participates in it, by means of payment or promise of reward.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of July 11, 1984)

(Wording given by
Law No. 7,209, of July 11, 1984)
II - military crimes proper and political crimes are not considered.
(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of July 11, 1984)

###### Art. 65 - Circumstances that always mitigate the penalty

(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)
I - the agent being under 21 (twenty-one) years of age, on the date of the act, or over 70

(Wording
given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)
II - ignorance of the law;
(Wording given
by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)
III - the agent having:
(Wording given by Law
No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)
a) committed the crime for a motive of relevant social or moral value;
b) sought, by his spontaneous will and with efficiency, immediately after the crime,

c) committed the crime under coercion which he could resist, or in compliance with an order from a

d) spontaneously confessed, before the authority, the authorship of the crime;
