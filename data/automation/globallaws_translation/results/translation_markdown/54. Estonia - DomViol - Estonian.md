GENERAL PROVISIONS

## § 1. Objective and scope of regulation of Act

[RT 12008, 56, 315 - entry into force 01.01.2009]
(1) The objective of this Act is to ensure gender equal treatment arising from the Constitution of the Republic of Estonia and to promote equality between women and men as one of the fundamental human rights and a general good in all areas of social life.
(2) To achieve the objective, this Act provides for:
1) prohibition of discrimination on the basis of gender in the private and public sector;
2) obligation of state and local government agencies, educational and research institutions and employers to promote equality between women and men;
3) arising of the right to claim compensation for damage.

## § 2. Scope of application of Act

[RT 12008, 56, 315 - entry into force 01.01.2009]
(1) This Act applies in all areas of social life.
(2) The requirements of this Act do not apply to:
1) profession of faith and practice of religion or working as a clergyman in religious associations entered in the register;
2) family or private life relations.
(3) Administrative procedure provided for in this Act is subject to the Administrative Procedure Act, taking into account the specific features of this Act.

## § 3. Definitions

(1) The following definitions are used in this Act:
1) gender equality means equal rights, obligations, opportunities and responsibilities of women and men in working life, in acquiring education and in participation in other areas of social life;
2) equal treatment of women and men means absence of direct and indirect gender discrimination;
3) direct gender discrimination takes place when one person is treated less favourably on the basis of his or her sex than another person is, has been or would be treated in a comparable situation. Direct gender discrimination also includes less favourable treatment of a person in connection with pregnancy and childbirth, being a parent, fulfilment of family obligations, including care of a person who needs significant care or support due to a serious health problem, or other circumstances related to gender, as well as sexual and gender

harassment and less favourable treatment resulting from rejection of or submission to harassment; [RT I, 22.12.2021, 3 - entry into force 01.04.2022]
4) indirect gender discrimination takes place when an apparently neutral provision, criterion, practice or activity places persons of one sex at a particular disadvantage compared with persons of the other sex, unless that provision, criterion, practice or activity has an objectively justifiable legitimate aim and the means of achieving that aim are appropriate and necessary;
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
5) sexual harassment takes place when any unwanted verbal, non-verbal or physical conduct or activity of a sexual nature occurs, the purpose or actual effect of which is the violation of the dignity of a person, in particular by creating an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating or offensive environment;
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
6) gender harassment takes place when unwanted conduct or activity related to sex occurs, the purpose or actual effect of which is the violation of the dignity of a person and the creation of an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating or offensive environment.
[RT 1 2009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
(2) For the purposes of this Act:
1) an employee is a person working on the basis of an employment contract or a contract for provision of services, an official and other persons specified in § 2 of the Public Service Act. A person seeking employment or service is also deemed to be an employee;
[RT I, 06.07.2012, 1 - entry into force 01.04.2013]
2) an employer is a natural or legal person who provides work on the basis of an employment contract or a contract for provision of services, as well as a state or local government agency.
[RT 12008, 56, 315 - entry into force 01.01.2009]

## § 4. Shared burden of proof

(1) A person applying to a court, labour dispute committee or the Gender Equality and Equal Treatment Commissioner must present in the application the factual circumstances on the basis of which it may be presumed that gender discrimination has taken place.
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
(2) The person against whom the application is filed must prove in the proceedings that he or she has not violated the principle of equal treatment. If a person refuses to provide proof, the refusal is equated with acknowledgement of discrimination.
[RT 12008, 56, 315 - entry into force 01.01.2009]
(3) Shared burden of proof does not apply in administrative court and criminal proceedings. [RT 12008, 56, 315 - entry into force 01.01.2009]

PROHIBITION OF GENDER DISCRIMINATION

## § 5. Gender discrimination

(1) Direct and indirect gender discrimination, including giving an order to discriminate, is prohibited. [RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
(11) Discrimination also includes malicious treatment of a person, as well as causing negative consequences for him or her because the person has relied on the rights and obligations provided for in this Act or has supported another person in the protection of his or her rights provided for in this Act.
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
(2) The following are not deemed to be direct or indirect gender discrimination:
1) provisions containing special protection of women in connection with pregnancy and childbirth;
2) establishment of compulsory Defence Forces conscription only for men;
3) admission of only women or only men as members of a non-profit association if this arises from the articles of association of the association;
4) different treatment on the basis of a person's sex in access to employment or to training necessary for such employment if, by reason of the nature of the particular occupational activities concerned or of the context in which they are carried out, sex constitutes an essential and determining occupational requirement and the objective of such different treatment is legitimate and the requirement proportionate to the objective;
41) different treatment of persons on the basis of their sex in the supply of goods or services in accordance with subsection 4 of § 265 of the Insurance Activities Act or where the supply of goods or services exclusively or primarily to members of one sex is justified by a legitimate aim and the means of achieving that aim are proportionate to the aim;
[RT I, 07.07.2015, 1 - entry into force 01.01.2016]
5) application of temporary special measures promoting gender equality which give advantages to the underrepresented sex or reduce gender inequality.
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]

## § 6. Discrimination in working life

(1) Cases in working life are considered discriminatory when an employer selects for work or a position, hires or takes on for work practice, promotes, selects for training or for the performance of a task or sends to training a person of one sex, leaving aside a person of the opposite sex with higher qualifications, except in cases where their action has weighty reasons or it arises from circumstances unrelated to sex.
(2) An employer's action is also considered discriminatory if they:
1) when making the decisions listed in this section, leave a person aside or treat them in another way less favourably due to pregnancy, childbirth,

being a parent, fulfilling family obligations or some other circumstance related to gender affiliation; [RT 1 2009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
2) establish conditions in hiring that place persons of one sex in a less favourable situation compared to persons of the other sex;
3) establish for an employee or employees of one sex less favourable conditions for remuneration of work or the granting and receiving of benefits related to employment than for an employee or employees of the other sex performing the same or equivalent work;
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
4) direct work, distribute work tasks or establish working conditions in such a way that an employee or employees of one sex are in a worse situation compared to an employee or employees of the opposite sex;
5) harass a person on the basis of their sex or sexually or fail to fulfil the obligation set out in § 11 subsection 1 point 4 of this Act.
The employer is liable for failure to fulfil the duty of care in the event that they knew or should have known of the occurrence of gender-based or sexual harassment and did not apply the necessary measures to stop the harassment;
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
6) [repealed - RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
7) punishes an employee under disciplinary procedure, transfers an employee to other work, terminates the employment relationship or facilitates its termination on grounds related to gender affiliation.
(3) Discrimination is also considered to be less favourable treatment of a person on the basis of their sex in connection with their membership in organisations of employees or employers or other organisations whose members work in a certain profession, as well as in connection with their participation in their work and in connection with benefits granted by those organisations.
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
(4) An entrepreneur engaged in employment mediation referred to in § 13 subsection 2 point 2 of the Labour Market Services and Benefits Act, and an employer may not require from a person who has approached them with a wish to find work data concerning the circumstances referred to in subsection 2 point 1 of this section.
[RT I, 07.03.2023, 5 - entry into force 01.01.2024]

## § 7. Employer's obligation to provide explanations

(1) An employer is obliged to provide a written explanation to a person who requests it in the case of suspicion of discriminatory conduct towards them referred to in § 6 subsection 1 of this Act, within ten working days from the submission of a written request. In the explanation, the employer must present the following data about the person in whose favour the choice was made in order to justify their choice:
1) length of service;
2) education;
3) work experience and other skills necessary for the work;
4) other skills or justifications giving clear advantages.
(2) An employer is obliged to provide a written explanation of their action to a person who believes that they have been discriminated against on the basis referred to in § 6 subsection 2 of this Act within 15 working days from the submission of the request.
(3) An employee has the right to demand from the employer clarification regarding the bases for calculating wages and to receive other necessary information on the basis of which it is possible to decide on the existence of discrimination referred to in § 6 subsection 2 point 3 of this Act.
(4) An employer is obliged to provide an explanation to a person whose request submitted on the basis of § 11 subsection 3 of this Act has been refused or whose flexible working conditions have been postponed within 15 working days from the submission of the request.
[RT I, 22.12.2021, 3 - entry into force 01.04.2022]
1 Obligation of a provider of goods or services to provide explanations
A provider of goods or services is obliged to provide a written explanation of their action to a person who believes that they have been discriminated against on the basis of their sex in connection with the availability or provision of goods or services within 15 working days from the receipt of a written request containing the factual circumstances of possible discrimination.
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]

## § 8. Discriminatory job and training offer

Job and training offers that are directed only to persons of one sex are prohibited, unless there are reasons referred to in § 5 subsection 2 points 4 and 5 of this Act.
3. Chapter
PROMOTION OF GENDER EQUALITY

## § 9

State and local government institutions as promoters of gender equality
(1) State and local government institutions are obliged to promote gender equality systematically and purposefully. Their task is to change conditions and circumstances that prevent the achievement of gender equality.
(2) When planning, implementing and assessing national, regional and institutional strategies, policies and action plans, the institutions referred to in subsection 1 of this section must proceed from the different needs and social status of women and men and take into account how the implemented and implementable measures affect the situation of women and men in society.
(21) When planning the fulfilment of the obligations imposed in subsections 1 and 2 of this section and when fulfilling those obligations, state and local government institutions consult as necessary with relevant interest groups and non-profit associations that have a legitimate interest in

helping to combat gender discrimination, in support of compliance with the principle of equal treatment. [RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
(3) The minister responsible for the field issues and publishes recommendations for the fulfilment of the obligations referred to in subsections 1 and 2 of this section. [RT I, 30.06.2023, 1 - entry into force 01.07.2023]
(4) In commissions, councils and other collegial bodies formed by state and local government institutions, both sexes must be represented where possible.

## § 1

0. Promotion of gender equality in the field of education and training
Educational and research institutions and institutions engaged in organising training must ensure equal treatment of women and men in career counselling, acquisition of education, special and vocational further training and retraining. Curricula, educational materials used and studies conducted must contribute to the elimination of inequality between women and men and the promotion of equality.

## § 1

1. Employers as promoters of gender equality
(1) An employer must, in promoting equality between women and men:
1) act in such a way that their activity supports the candidacy of both men and women for vacant jobs and that persons of both sexes are hired;
[RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
2) hire women and men in as equal a proportion as possible for different positions and ensure equal treatment for them in promotion;
3) shape working conditions to be suitable for both women and men and enhance the reconciliation of work and family life, taking into account the needs of employees;
4) ensure that an employee is protected in the working environment from gender-based and sexual harassment; [RT 12009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
5) inform the employee of the rights guaranteed in this Act;
6) regularly provide employees and/or their representatives with relevant information on the equal treatment of men and women in the organisation and on measures adopted to promote equality.
(2) An employer must collect gender-based statistical data on work, which, if necessary, enable relevant institutions to monitor and assess compliance with the principle of equal treatment in employment relationships. The procedure for collecting data and the list of data is established by a regulation of the Government of the Republic.
(3) An employee has the right to request from the employer possibilities for reconciling work and family life, including flexible working conditions, to which the employer must respond within a reasonable time.
[RT I, 22.12.2021, 3 - entry into force 01.04.2022]

## § 1

2.	Resolution of gender discrimination disputes
Discrimination disputes are resolved by a court or labour dispute committee. The Chancellor of Justice resolves discrimination disputes through conciliation proceedings.
[RT 12008, 56, 315 - entry into force 01.01.2009]

## § 1

3.	Compensation for damage
(1)	If a person's rights have been violated due to discrimination, he or she may demand from the person who violated the rights, on the bases and in the procedure provided by law, cessation of the conduct causing damage and compensation for damage.
[RT 1 2009, 48, 323 - entry into force 23.10.2009]
(2)	The victim may demand that, in addition to what is provided in subsection 1 of this section, a reasonable sum of money be paid to him or her as compensation for non-pecuniary damage caused by the violation.
(3)	In determining the amount of compensation, the court or labour dispute committee shall take into account, among other things, the extent, duration and nature of the discrimination. [RT 12008, 56, 315 - entry into force 01.01.2009]
(4)	Persons seeking employment and service with whom the employer did not conclude an employment contract or service provision contract or who were not appointed or elected to office on the basis of sex may not demand the conclusion of an employment contract or service provision contract or appointment or election to office.
[RT 12008, 56, 315 - entry into force 01.01.2009]

## § 1

4.	Limitation of claim for compensation for damage
The claim provided for in § 13 of this Act expires within one year from the date on which the injured person became aware or should have become aware of the occurrence of the damage.

## § 1

5.	Commissioner for Gender Equality and Equal Treatment

(1)	The Commissioner for Gender Equality and Equal Treatment (hereinafter the Commissioner) is an independent and impartial expert who monitors compliance with the requirements of this Act and the Equal Treatment Act and performs other tasks assigned to him or her by law, and who acts independently.
(2)	The appointment of the Commissioner to office, the termination of his or her powers and restrictions on his or her activities, and the competence of the Commissioner are provided for in the Equal Treatment Act.

## § 2

2.	Tasks of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications
[RT I, 30.06.2023, 1 - entry into force 01.07.2023]
The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications:
1)	provides advice on issues related to the application of the principle of equal treatment and gives instructions for the application of this Act;

## § 2

3.	Counselling
(1)	An official of the equality structural unit of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications (hereinafter the counsellor) provides advice to everyone on issues related to the application of the principle of equal treatment of women and men. Counselling may take place orally or in writing.
[RT I, 30.06.2023, 1 - entry into force 01.07.2023]
(2)	If necessary, the counsellor shall explain to the person which institution has the competence to resolve a possible case of discrimination. At the request of the person, assistance shall be provided in the written preparation of an application and it shall be forwarded to the competent body. The application shall not be forwarded to a court or labour dispute committee.

## § 2

4.	Gender Equality Council
(1)	The Gender Equality Council is an advisory body under the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications, which: [RT I, 30.06.2023, 1 - entry into force 01.07.2023]
1)	approves the general directions of gender equality policy and performs the tasks provided for in this Act and the statutes;
2)	advises the Government of the Republic on issues of gender equality promotion strategy;
3)	submits to the Government of the Republic its position on the compliance of national programmes submitted by ministries with § 9 of this Act.
(2)	The working procedure of the Gender Equality Council shall be provided for in the statutes. The statutes of the Gender Equality Council shall be established by the Government of the Republic.
(3)	The composition of the Gender Equality Council shall be approved by the Government of the Republic.
