## CHAPTER 1

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1. Basic terms and their definitions used in this Law

The following basic terms and their definitions are used in this Law:
close relatives - parents, children, adopters, adopted persons, siblings, grandfather, grandmother, grandchildren, as well as spouse;
actions of a psychological nature - impact on the psyche of a citizen of the Republic of Belarus, a foreign citizen and a stateless person (hereinafter, unless otherwise defined, - citizen) by means of threat, humiliation of honor and dignity, commission of other immoral actions that objectively give the citizen grounds to fear for his safety or the safety of persons close to him;
actions of a sexual nature - encroachment on sexual freedom or sexual inviolability;
actions of a physical nature - infliction of bodily injury, pain, torment, infliction of beatings;
domestic violence - intentional unlawful or immoral actions of a physical, psychological or sexual nature by close relatives, former spouses, citizens having a common child (children), or other citizens who live (lived) together and maintain (maintained) a common household, in relation to each other, causing physical and (or) mental suffering;
individual prevention of offenses - activity of subjects of prevention of offenses to exert corrective influence on a citizen in order to prevent the commission of offenses, carried out in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation;
general prevention of offenses - activity of subjects of prevention of offenses to identify the causes of offenses and conditions conducive to their commission, and to take measures to eliminate them, to influence social processes and phenomena in order to prevent unlawful behavior of citizens, carried out in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation;
victim of domestic violence - a citizen who, as a result of the commission of domestic violence, has suffered physical and (or) mental suffering;
offense - an unlawful culpable action (inaction) for the commission of which criminal or administrative liability is provided;
prevention of offenses - activity of applying measures of general and (or) individual prevention of offenses by subjects of prevention of offenses in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation;
preventive measure - a set of actions of a subject of prevention of offenses, organized and conducted by it within the framework of general and (or) individual prevention of offenses.
Definitions of other terms are contained in separate articles of this Law.

### Article 2. Scope of application of this Law

This Law regulates public relations arising in the implementation of prevention of offenses, unless otherwise provided by part two of this article.
Prevention of offenses in the conduct of criminal and administrative proceedings, execution of punishment and other measures of criminal liability is carried out in accordance with other legislative acts.
Prevention of offenses committed by minors is carried out in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Belarus of May 31, 2003 No. 200-Z "On the Fundamentals of the System for the Prevention of Child Neglect and Juvenile Offenses".
Citizens participate in activities for the prevention of offenses in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Belarus of June 26, 2003 No. 214-Z "On the Participation of Citizens in the Protection of Public Order", this Law and other acts of legislation.

### Article 3. Legal regulation of relations in the sphere of prevention of offenses

Relations in the sphere of prevention of offenses are regulated by legislation in the sphere of prevention of offenses, as well as by international treaties of the Republic of Belarus.
Legislation in the sphere of prevention of offenses is based on the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus and consists of this Law and other acts of legislation.
If an international treaty of the Republic of Belarus establishes rules other than those contained in this Law, the rules of the international treaty shall apply.

### Article 4

Basic principles of activity of subjects of prevention of offenses in the sphere of prevention of offenses
The activity of subjects of prevention of offenses in the sphere of prevention of offenses is based on the principles of:
legality;
humanism;
planning and systematicity;
implementation of individual prevention of offenses taking into account the personality of the citizen;
protection and observance of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of citizens, rights and legitimate interests of organizations;
coordination of actions and operational interaction of subjects of prevention of offenses;
responsibility of officials of subjects of prevention of offenses for violation of legislation in the sphere of prevention of offenses;
preservation of traditional family values;
respect for private life;
inadmissibility of considering customs, beliefs, traditions as justification for offenses;
priority of preventive measures over measures of liability.

### Article 5. Subjects of Offence Prevention

The subjects of offence prevention are:
internal affairs bodies;
prosecution bodies;
state security bodies;
border service bodies;
customs bodies;
state protection body;
bodies of the State Control Committee;
bodies and units for emergency situations;
the Armed Forces of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter - the Armed Forces), internal troops of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (hereinafter - internal troops), other state military formations and paramilitary organizations;
local executive and administrative bodies;
the Ministry of Health, structural subdivisions of regional executive committees, the Minsk City Executive Committee exercising state authority powers in the sphere of health care, health care management bodies of other republican state administration bodies and other organizations subordinate to the Government of the Republic of Belarus, and state health care organizations subordinate to them (hereinafter - health care management bodies);
state health care organizations;
the Ministry of Education, structural subdivisions of regional executive committees, the Minsk City Executive Committee, city, district executive committees, local administrations of districts in cities, exercising state authority powers in the sphere of education (hereinafter - education management bodies);
educational institutions;
the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection, structural subdivisions of regional executive committees, the Minsk City Executive Committee, city, district executive committees exercising state authority powers in the sphere of labour, employment and social protection, structural subdivisions of local administrations of districts in cities exercising state authority powers in the sphere of social protection (hereinafter - bodies for labour, employment and social protection);
social service institutions;
the Ministry of Justice, structural subdivisions of regional executive committees, the Minsk City Executive Committee exercising state authority powers in the sphere of justice;
the State Inspectorate for Protection of Flora and Fauna under the President of the Republic of Belarus;
legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media;
councils of public law and order protection posts;
voluntary squads;
public associations and other organizations (hereinafter - other organizations).

### Article 6. Coordination of Activities on Offence Prevention

Coordination of activities on offence prevention within the limits of competence is carried out by the Prosecutor General and lower prosecutors of territorial and transport prosecutor's offices, including by means of organizing the work of coordination meetings on combating crime and corruption.

## CHAPTER 2

POWERS OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF BELARUS, STATE BODIES, OTHER ORGANIZATIONS IN THE SPHERE OF OFFENCE PREVENTION

### Article 7

Powers of the President of the Republic of Belarus in the Sphere of Offence Prevention
The President of the Republic of Belarus in the sphere of offence prevention:
determines state policy;
exercises control over the activities of subjects of offence prevention subordinate (accountable) to the President of the Republic of Belarus;
exercises other powers in accordance with the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus, this Law and other legislative acts.

### Article 8

Powers of the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus in the Sphere of Offence Prevention
The Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus in the sphere of offence prevention:
implements state policy;
exercises control over the activities of subjects of offence prevention subordinate to the Government of the Republic of Belarus;
develops jointly with the Prosecutor General's Office comprehensive plans for combating crime and corruption and submits them for approval to the republican coordination meeting on combating crime and corruption;
exercises other powers in accordance with the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus, this Law, other laws and acts of the President of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 9

Powers of Subjects of Offence Prevention in the Sphere of Offence Prevention
Subjects of offence prevention in the sphere of offence prevention within the limits of their competence in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation:
take part in the implementation of state policy;
identify causes of offences and conditions conducive to their commission, and take measures to eliminate them;
develop and conduct preventive measures;
take part in the implementation of comprehensive plans for combating crime and corruption and regional comprehensive plans for offence prevention;
provide in accordance with legislation information on preventive measures being conducted and conducted;
place in public places, buildings (premises) of organizations, state mass media, including those distributed using the global computer network Internet, on official websites of subjects of offence prevention information on the formation of law-abiding behaviour, healthy lifestyle, skills for ensuring personal and property security of citizens;
conduct systematic monitoring of legislation and submit in the established manner proposals for its improvement;
exercise other powers provided for by this Law and other acts of legislation.
Organizations in the sphere of offence prevention conduct preventive measures to prevent offences among employees of these organizations, including for the purpose of ensuring safe working conditions and preventing emergency situations of natural and technogenic character (hereinafter - emergency situations) on territories and facilities belonging to (subordinate to) them, provide on the basis of requests from other subjects of offence prevention space on information stands for placing information on the formation of law-abiding behaviour, healthy lifestyle, skills for ensuring personal and property security of citizens, exercise other powers provided for by this Law and other acts of legislation.
Legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, in the sphere of offence prevention within the limits of their competence in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation:
promote the participation of citizens and organizations in activities on offence prevention;
disseminate information on the activities of subjects of offence prevention, analytical and other materials on offence prevention, protection of rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of citizens, rights and legitimate interests of organizations, including demonstrating by specific examples the inevitability of punishment for committed crimes;
carry out activities on placing (distributing) social advertising, and also inform citizens about forms and methods of self-protection from criminal encroachments;
exercise other powers provided for by this Law and other acts of legislation.

## CHAPTER 3

GENERAL PREVENTION OF OFFENCES

### Article 10. Measures of General Prevention of Offences

Measures of general prevention of offences are:
development and approval of regional comprehensive plans for offence prevention and conducting preventive measures;
legal education of citizens;
submission of representations, issuance of instructions;
other measures provided for by this Law and other legislative acts.

### Статья 11. Regional Comprehensive Plans for the Prevention of Offenses

On the basis of the provisions of this Law, comprehensive plans to combat crime and corruption, proposals of subjects of prevention of offenses, regional comprehensive plans for the prevention of offenses are annually developed and approved by local executive and administrative bodies of the regional and basic territorial levels, which are implemented by subjects of prevention of offenses within their competence in the territory of the corresponding administrative-territorial units.
Regional comprehensive plans for the prevention of offenses must ensure the conduct of preventive measures for the prevention of:
crimes against the state and the order of exercise of power and administration;
offenses creating conditions for corruption, and corruption offenses;
offenses against life and health, public order and public morality;
offenses contributing to the occurrence of emergency situations and loss of life from them;
offenses against property and the order of exercise of economic activity;
domestic violence;
offenses committed by citizens in a state of alcoholic intoxication or in a state caused by the consumption of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, toxic or other intoxicating substances;
offenses committed by citizens released from correctional institutions, open-type correctional institutions, arrest houses, as well as citizens who have returned from special educational institutions and special medical-educational institutions;
other offenses.
The corresponding coordination meetings on combating crime and corruption coordinate regional comprehensive plans for the prevention of offenses before their approval by local executive and administrative bodies of the regional and basic territorial levels, and also monitor the implementation of preventive measures provided for in them.

### Статья 12

Basic Preventive Measures for the Prevention of Crimes Against the State and the Order of Exercise of Power and Administration
Internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, border service bodies, the state protection body, internal troops, other state military formations and paramilitary organizations within their competence conduct preventive measures for the prevention of the creation and activity of illegal armed formations, organized criminal groups and criminal organizations, for the prevention of extremist activity, organization of illegal migration and other crimes against the state and the order of exercise of power and administration.
The state protection body, internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, the Armed Forces, border service bodies, emergency bodies and units, internal troops within their competence conduct preventive measures to ensure the security of persons subject to state protection in places of their stay, eliminate circumstances hindering the implementation of state protection, prevent other crimes against the state and the order of exercise of power and administration.
State bodies not specified in parts one and two of this Article, other state organizations within their competence conduct preventive measures for the prevention of crimes against the state and the order of exercise of power and administration.

### Статья 13

Basic Preventive Measures for the Prevention of Offenses Creating Conditions for Corruption, and Corruption Offenses
Prosecutor's offices, internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, bodies of the State Control Committee, other state bodies, other state organizations within their competence take measures to identify and suppress offenses creating conditions for corruption, and corruption offenses, eliminate their consequences, as well as prerequisites and causes of corruption.
Local executive and administrative bodies, other state bodies, other state organizations plan and conduct preventive measures to combat corruption, including exercising control over the selection and placement of personnel, systematic work on compliance with restrictions and special requirements aimed at ensuring financial control in relation to state officials.
State bodies, other state organizations in cooperation with legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, inform citizens about the results of the fight against corruption, promote rejection of manifestations of corruption.

### Article 14

Basic preventive measures for the prevention of offenses against life and health, public order and public morality
Internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, border service bodies, customs bodies within their competence identify and eliminate causes and conditions conducive to illegal trafficking in weapons, ammunition, explosives. These state bodies, in cooperation with legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, explain to citizens the necessity of voluntary surrender of illegally stored weapons, ammunition, explosives.
Internal affairs bodies, border service bodies, customs bodies within their competence identify owners of weapons who have been subjected to administrative penalties for committing administrative offenses that constitute grounds for annulment of permits for storage and carrying of weapons.
The Armed Forces, state security bodies, border service bodies, internal troops, other state military formations and paramilitary organizations within their competence conduct preventive measures to ensure the safety of storage of weapons, ammunition, explosives and other means of destruction.
Internal affairs bodies, in cooperation with educational institutions, legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, explain to citizens the possible negative consequences of employment abroad without the participation of legal entities or individual entrepreneurs holding a license to carry out activities related to employment outside the Republic of Belarus.
Internal affairs bodies, divisions and organizations of the Ministry of Transport and Communications, local executive and administrative bodies within their competence take organizational and other measures ensuring the safety of passenger transportation.
Internal affairs bodies, in cooperation with educational institutions, legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, organize explanatory work on the prevention of knowingly false reports of danger.
Local executive and administrative bodies take measures to organize proper lighting of streets and yard territories of populated areas.
Local executive and administrative bodies, upon proposal of internal affairs bodies, take measures to equip public places of mass presence of citizens, public places where offenses are most frequently committed, with video surveillance systems and emergency communication devices controlled by internal affairs bodies.
Local executive and administrative bodies take measures to ensure in the city of Minsk and regional centers the connection of route vehicles with the intersection control center of the automated traffic management system.
Local executive and administrative bodies take organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access of citizens to vacant houses, buildings, structures, basements and attics of residential buildings, buildings and structures.
Internal affairs bodies, in cooperation with organizations carrying out operation of housing stock and (or) providing housing and communal services, organize appropriate training of employees of organizations carrying out operation of housing stock and (or) providing housing and communal services, to provide assistance to internal affairs bodies in protecting public order in yard territories.
Internal affairs bodies, in cooperation with legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, inform citizens about methods and means of ensuring personal safety.

### Article 15

Basic preventive measures for the prevention of offenses conducive to the occurrence of emergency situations and loss of human life from them
Local executive and administrative bodies, in cooperation with emergency situation bodies and divisions, health management bodies, state health organizations, labor, employment and social protection bodies, social service institutions, organizations carrying out operation of housing stock and (or) providing housing and communal services, and educational institutions, conduct preventive measures aimed at preventing fires and other emergency situations and loss of human life from them, targeted inspections of the condition of residential premises of single and solitary living elderly citizens and disabled persons, large families and families in which children are in socially dangerous situations, organize inspections of the condition of residential premises of other categories of citizens during reviews of the fire safety condition of residential buildings and take necessary measures to bring the housing stock into a fire-safe condition, conduct briefings on fire safety measures in everyday life, take measures in the established manner to provide targeted social assistance to citizens to ensure safe living conditions.
Emergency situation bodies and divisions organize informing the population about the state of fire safety, provide assistance to republican state administration bodies, local executive and administrative bodies, other organizations in training citizens in fire safety measures in everyday life and at work, provide assistance to volunteer fire formations in organizing fire prevention work.
State supervision bodies in the field of industrial, nuclear and radiation safety, local executive and administrative bodies, other authorized state bodies and other organizations conduct preventive measures to ensure industrial, nuclear and radiation safety.
State energy supervision bodies conduct targeted inspections of the technical condition of electrical and heat-using installations, conditions of their operation in residential premises of citizens, take measures to identify and eliminate causes and conditions conducive to violation of rules for the installation and technical operation of electrical and heat-using installations, safety rules during their operation, rules for the use of electrical and thermal energy by consumers of electrical and thermal energy.
State gas supervision bodies conduct targeted inspections of the technical condition of gas-using equipment (installations) of gas consumers, conditions of their operation in residential premises of citizens, take measures to identify and eliminate causes and conditions conducive to violation of rules for the installation and technical operation of gas-using equipment (installations), safety rules during their operation by gas consumers.
State bodies and other organizations carrying out activities for the development and implementation of fire-resistant materials, within their competence, take measures for the development and implementation of fire-resistant materials in construction and everyday life.

### Статья 16

Basic preventive measures for the prevention of offences against property and the procedure for carrying out economic activity
Internal affairs bodies identify the causes and conditions conducive to the commission of theft of property of citizens and organizations, within their competence take measures to eliminate these causes and conditions, and ensure the safety of commodity and material assets.
Bodies of the State Control Committee, other state bodies, other state organizations conducting inspections of the financial and economic activities of organizations and individual entrepreneurs, conduct in the established procedure inspections of the expenditure of budgetary funds, as well as preventive measures for the prevention of offences in the real sector of the economy and the social sphere.
Bodies of the State Control Committee take measures to improve the methodology for conducting inspections of the financial and economic activities of organizations and individual entrepreneurs.
Internal affairs bodies, in cooperation with organizations carrying out the operation of the housing stock and (or) providing housing and communal services, legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, carry out the informing of organizations and citizens about the need to equip premises with security and safety devices, entrance doors in building entrances with intercoms, code locks and video surveillance devices.
Local executive and administrative bodies take measures for the construction of guarded parking facilities for vehicles and the creation of garage construction cooperatives, the equipping and maintenance in technically sound condition of electrical lighting systems in garage areas, at vehicle parking facilities, the allocation in residential complexes during their design and construction of spaces for garages and parking for vehicles of citizens at the rate of one vehicle per one apartment.

### Article 17

Basic preventive measures for the prevention of domestic violence
Local executive and administrative bodies, internal affairs bodies, prosecutor's offices, bodies for labor, employment and social protection, social service institutions, health management bodies, state health organizations, education management bodies, educational institutions in the field of prevention of domestic violence within their competence:
take measures to form a negative attitude of society towards domestic violence, to identify and eliminate the causes and conditions contributing to it;
conduct information and educational work on the prevention of domestic violence;
ensure training, retraining and advanced training of specialists of subjects of crime prevention, including in the field of interdepartmental interaction;
organize the implementation (implement) of educational programs of training courses (lecture series, thematic seminars, practical classes, trainings, officer courses and other types of training courses) for specialists of subjects of crime prevention;
inform victims of domestic violence about organizations where they can receive assistance;
conduct sectoral and intersectoral studies of the state, causes and conditions of the spread of domestic violence, the effectiveness of legislation and the practice of its application;
provide assistance in conducting a free "hotline" on the issue of counseling victims of domestic violence.
Local executive and administrative bodies in the field of prevention of domestic violence:
ensure the planned and systematic conduct of preventive measures for the prevention of domestic violence, take measures to study and comprehensively respond to citizens' requests taking into account local characteristics;
in the relevant territory create interdepartmental councils for providing assistance to victims of domestic violence, which determine the procedure for implementing a set of measures to provide assistance to victims of domestic violence within the framework of interdepartmental interaction protocols in accordance with the model regulation on the interdepartmental council for providing assistance to victims of domestic violence, approved by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus, and the model form of the interdepartmental interaction protocol, established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus;
if necessary, protect the rights of a victim of domestic violence recognized by a court as incapacitated or with limited capacity, and represent his or her interests in state bodies or in court;
promote the development of cooperation with other organizations and citizens in activities for the prevention of domestic violence;
make decisions on the creation of state organizations, their structural subdivisions for the round-the-clock provision of temporary shelter services to victims of domestic violence, provision of social, psychological and other assistance to them, and also take, if necessary, in the manner established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus, measures to provide places of temporary stay for citizens against whom a protective order has been applied;
organize within their competence the conduct of correctional programs;
carry out accounting, generalization, systematization and analysis of data on the provision of assistance to victims of domestic violence, on measures taken against citizens who have committed domestic violence.
Internal affairs bodies in the field of prevention of domestic violence:
receive and consider applications and reports on the commission of domestic violence, take measures to suppress it, prepare materials for making decisions in the established manner on bringing citizens who have committed domestic violence to administrative or criminal liability, on recognizing them as having limited capacity;
organize the conduct of preventive measures for the prevention of domestic violence;
inform guardianship and trusteeship bodies about the identification of facts of domestic violence against minors, citizens recognized by a court as incapacitated or with limited capacity;
organize within their competence the conduct of correctional programs;
carry out accounting, generalization, systematization and analysis of information on facts of domestic violence.
Prosecutor's offices in the field of prevention of domestic violence:
take part in the conduct of preventive measures for the prevention of domestic violence;
inform guardianship and trusteeship bodies about the identification of facts of domestic violence against minors, citizens recognized by a court as incapacitated or with limited capacity.
Bodies for labor, employment and social protection in the field of prevention of domestic violence provide methodological support for the activities of social service institutions on issues of prevention of domestic violence and provision of assistance to victims of domestic violence.
Social service institutions in the field of prevention of domestic violence within their competence provide social services to victims of domestic violence in the manner and on the conditions provided for by legislation, assistance in obtaining social assistance, social guarantees and benefits, other types of assistance provided for by legislation.
Health management bodies in the field of prevention of domestic violence provide methodological support for the activities of state health organizations on issues of prevention of domestic violence and provision of assistance to victims of domestic violence.
Education management bodies in the field of prevention of domestic violence provide methodological support for the activities of educational institutions on issues of prevention of domestic violence and provision of assistance to minors.
Bodies for labor, employment and social protection, social service institutions, health management bodies, state health organizations, education management bodies, educational institutions in the field of prevention of domestic violence within their competence:
inform internal affairs bodies and guardianship and trusteeship bodies about the identification of circumstances indicating domestic violence committed primarily against minors, citizens recognized by a court as incapacitated or with limited capacity;
take part in the conduct of correctional programs, preventive measures for the prevention of domestic violence;
carry out accounting, generalization, systematization and analysis of data on the provision of assistance or social services to victims of domestic violence.
Other organizations in the field of prevention of domestic violence have the right in accordance with legislation and their charters:
to take part in the conduct of correctional programs, preventive measures for the prevention of domestic violence;
to provide temporary shelter services to victims of domestic violence;
to provide social, psychological, legal and other assistance to victims of domestic violence;
to organize the conduct of a free "hotline" on the issue of counseling victims of domestic violence;
to conduct other preventive measures for the prevention of domestic violence.

### Статья 18

Основные профилактические мероприятия по предупреждению правонарушений, совершаемых гражданами, находящимися в состоянии алкогольного опьянения либо в состоянии, вызванном потреблением наркотических средств, психотропных веществ, их аналогов, токсических или других одурманивающих веществ

Internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, border service bodies, customs authorities, state healthcare organizations within their competence identify the causes and conditions conducive to the illicit trafficking of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their precursors and analogues. These state bodies and state organizations, in cooperation with legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, explain to citizens the need for voluntary surrender of illegally stored narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their precursors and analogues.
Local executive and administrative bodies, in cooperation with internal affairs bodies, healthcare management bodies, state healthcare organizations, education management bodies, educational institutions, and other organizations, ensure the conduct of information and educational work on preventing the distribution and consumption of alcoholic beverages, low-alcohol beverages and beer, and the consumption of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, toxic or other intoxicating substances.
Local executive and administrative bodies, internal affairs bodies, educational institutions within their competence conduct preventive measures with students to prevent offenses committed in a state of alcoholic intoxication or in a state caused by the consumption of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, toxic or other intoxicating substances.
State healthcare organizations in the established manner ensure the provision of medical care to citizens suffering from mental and (or) behavioral disorders caused by alcohol consumption, consumption of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, toxic or other intoxicating substances.
Internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, border service bodies, customs authorities within their competence identify places of illegal production and storage of alcoholic products, non-food alcohol-containing products, ethyl alcohol, narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their precursors and analogues.
Internal affairs bodies within their competence prepare materials and submit applications to court for sending citizens to medical-labor preventoria in the established manner, identify citizens consuming narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, for referring them in the established manner to state healthcare organizations for the provision of medical care.
Local executive and administrative bodies in the manner established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus, coordinate with internal affairs bodies the operating hours of retail facilities and public catering facilities engaged in the sale of alcoholic beverages, as well as the sale of low-alcohol beverages and beer during nighttime.
Labor, employment and social protection bodies in the manner established by legislation on employment of the population, provide assistance to citizens who have ceased to be in medical-labor preventoria, as well as to citizens to whom compulsory measures of safety and treatment were applied by court decision, in job selection and employment.

### Статья 19

Основные профилактические мероприятия по предупреждению правонарушений, совершаемых гражданами, освобожденными из исправительных учреждений, исправительных учреждений открытого типа, арестных домов, а также гражданами, вернувшимися из специальных учебно-воспитательных учреждений и специальных лечебно-воспитательных учреждений

Internal affairs bodies ensure control over the arrival of citizens released from correctional institutions, open-type correctional institutions, arrest houses, to their chosen place of residence.
Local executive and administrative bodies take measures to provide housing and employment for citizens released from correctional institutions, open-type correctional institutions, arrest houses, as well as citizens who have returned from special educational institutions and special medical-educational institutions.
Local executive and administrative bodies in the manner established by legislation, provide support to organizations and individual entrepreneurs providing jobs for the employment of citizens released from correctional institutions, open-type correctional institutions, arrest houses.
Organizations and individual entrepreneurs providing jobs for the employment of citizens released from correctional institutions have the right to partial compensation of labor remuneration costs for such persons at the expense of funds allocated for financing measures to ensure employment of the population, and other sources not prohibited by legislation.
The procedure for organizing and financing measures for the employment of citizens released from correctional institutions, including partial compensation of labor remuneration costs for such persons, is determined by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Статья 20. Правовое просвещение граждан

Legal education of citizens is the formation and enhancement of the level of legal consciousness and legal culture of citizens, carried out by subjects of prevention of offenses within their competence.
Legal education of citizens is carried out by:
conducting conferences, round tables, seminars, lectures and presentations on issues of prevention of offenses;
placing in public places, buildings (premises) of organizations, state mass media, including those distributed using the global computer network Internet, on official websites of subjects of prevention of offenses, information on the formation of law-abiding behavior, healthy lifestyle, skills for ensuring personal and property security of citizens;
in other forms in accordance with acts of legislation.

### Article 21

Representation on Elimination of Causes and Conditions Conducive to Commission of Offenses, and Instruction on Elimination of Violations of Legislation
A representation on elimination of causes and conditions conducive to commission of offenses (hereinafter - representation) is a written demand of an internal affairs body, prosecutor's office body, state security body, border service body, customs body, state protection body, financial investigations body of the State Control Committee, emergency situations body and unit to eliminate causes and conditions conducive to commission of offenses.
A representation is submitted by the head of the offense prevention entity specified in part one of this Article, or by his deputy, to an official, organization or individual entrepreneur who are authorized to take measures to eliminate causes and conditions conducive to commission of offenses.
A representation is subject to immediate consideration with adoption of necessary measures to fulfill the requirements contained therein. The respective organization, official, individual entrepreneur to whom the representation has been submitted shall, within one month from the date of its receipt, inform in writing the offense prevention entity that submitted the representation of the measures taken.
When a representation is considered by a collegial body, the offense prevention entity that submitted it is notified in advance of the date, time and place of consideration of the representation. The offense prevention entity has the right to send its representative to participate in the consideration of the representation.
An instruction on elimination of violations of legislation (hereinafter - instruction) is a written demand of an internal affairs body, prosecutor's office body, state security body, border service body, customs body, state protection body, financial investigations body of the State Control Committee, emergency situations body and unit for immediate elimination of a violation of legislation that is of an obvious nature and may cause substantial harm to the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of citizens, including individual entrepreneurs, rights and legitimate interests of organizations, state or public interests, if it is not immediately eliminated.
An instruction is sent by the head of the offense prevention entity specified in part five of this Article, or by his deputy, to the official, organization or individual entrepreneur who committed the violation of legislation, or to a higher state body, official, other organization authorized to eliminate such violation. An instruction must contain an indication of the act of legislation that has been violated, the nature of the offense and specific proposals on measures for its elimination.
An instruction is subject to immediate execution. The results of execution of the instruction are reported without delay to the offense prevention entity that issued the instruction.
When inspections are conducted by controlling (supervisory) bodies, instructions are issued by these bodies in accordance with legislative acts on control (supervisory) activity.
The offense prevention entities specified in parts one and five of this Article monitor the actual fulfillment of the requirements of the representations submitted by them and the instructions issued by them.

### Article 22. Other Measures of General Prevention of Offenses

Other measures of general prevention of offenses include:
informing about preventive measures being conducted and conducted;
organizing public opinion polls on the activities of offense prevention entities and coverage of their results in state mass media, including those distributed using the global computer network Internet, on official websites of offense prevention entities;
ensuring incentives for citizens for providing reliable information about offenses being prepared or committed;
conducting criminological expertise of draft legal acts and legal acts in the procedure established by the President of the Republic of Belarus;
other measures provided for by legislative acts.

## CHAPTER 4

INDIVIDUAL PREVENTION OF OFFENSES

### Article 23. Measures of Individual Prevention of Offenses

Measures of individual prevention of offenses are:
preventive conversation;
official warning;
preventive registration;
protective instruction;
correctional program;
other measures provided for by legislative acts.

### Article 24. Preventive Conversation

A preventive conversation is an oral explanation to a citizen of the public danger of preparation and commission of offenses, legal consequences arising as a result of commission of offenses, as well as persuasion of the citizen of the inadmissibility of their commission.
A preventive conversation is conducted with a citizen:
released from a correctional institution, open-type correctional institution, arrest house and who has arrived at the chosen place of residence;
whose behavior in public places, at place of residence, work, study or lifestyle has led to commission of an offense or gives grounds to believe the possibility of commission of an offense by him.

### Article 25. Procedure for Conducting a Preventive Conversation

With a citizen released from a correctional institution, open-type correctional institution, arrest house, a preventive conversation is conducted by an official of an internal affairs body within ten days after this citizen's arrival at the chosen place of residence.
A citizen released from a correctional institution, open-type correctional institution, arrest house and who has arrived at the chosen place of residence is summoned by the internal affairs body for conducting a preventive conversation to the premises of the internal affairs body. The summons is carried out in written form, by telephone message or telegram, SMS message, using facsimile communication, the global computer network Internet, including electronic mail, as well as other means of communication ensuring recording of the summons. A preventive conversation with the specified citizen may be conducted at his place of residence or place of stay, study, work or in another place determined by the official of the internal affairs body if this citizen did not appear upon summons.
A preventive conversation with a citizen whose behavior in public places, at place of residence, work, study or lifestyle has led to commission of an offense or gives grounds to believe the possibility of commission of an offense by him is conducted by an official of an offense prevention entity.
When conducting a preventive conversation, the official of the offense prevention entity explains to the citizen his rights and obligations provided for by Article 32 of this Law.
The duration of conducting a preventive conversation is determined by the official of the offense prevention entity individually, as a rule, within one hour.
During the conduct of a preventive conversation, demonstration of visual materials and video clips may be carried out.

### Article 26. Official Warning

An official warning is a written explanation to a citizen of the inadmissibility of preparation or commission of offenses for the purpose of preventing commission of offenses by him.
An official warning is issued to a citizen in respect of whom information has been received about commission of acts that may create a threat to national security, cause harm to state or public interests, rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of other citizens or lead to commission of a crime.
A court, body conducting administrative proceedings, body of criminal prosecution shall within five days send to state security bodies, border service bodies, customs bodies, taking into account their competence, the information provided for by part two of this Article.

### Article 27. Procedure for Issuing and Announcing an Official Warning

The head of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs body or their deputy, an official of a subject of offence prevention considering a case of an administrative offence, within ten days after receiving information about the commission of acts that may create a threat to national security, cause harm to state or public interests, rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of other citizens or lead to the commission of a crime, in respect of citizens specified in part two of Article 26 of this Law, shall issue an official warning to such citizens.
A citizen in respect of whom an official warning has been issued is summoned by an official of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs body for the announcement of the official warning to the premises of the relevant subject of offence prevention. The summons is carried out in written form, by telephone message or telegram, SMS message, using facsimile communication, the global computer network Internet, including email, as well as other means of communication ensuring the recording of the summons. An official warning may be announced to the citizen in respect of whom it has been issued at their place of residence or place of stay, study, work or at another place determined by an official of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs body, if that citizen did not appear upon summons.
An official of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs body announces the official warning to the citizen in respect of whom it has been issued, hands them a copy of the official warning and explains their rights and obligations provided for by Article 32 of this Law. A corresponding entry regarding receipt of a copy of the official warning and explanation of rights and obligations is made in the official warning by the citizen in respect of whom the official warning has been issued and by the official who handed over the copy of the official warning and explained the rights and obligations. In the event that the citizen in respect of whom the official warning has been issued refused to sign the official warning, the official who handed over the copy of the official warning makes an entry to that effect in the official warning.
The form of the official warning is established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 28. Preventive Registration

Preventive registration is the observation of the behaviour of a citizen in respect of whom a decision has been made to carry out preventive registration, for the purposes of preventing the preparation or commission of offences on their part and exerting preventive influence on them.
Preventive registration is carried out in respect of a citizen:
who has committed domestic violence and in connection with this has been brought to administrative liability for intentional infliction of bodily injury and other violent actions or violation of a protective order;
who has committed domestic violence and in respect of whom in connection with this a verification is being conducted in accordance with the requirements of criminal procedural legislation or a criminal case has been initiated concerning a crime against life and health, sexual inviolability or sexual freedom or personal freedom, honour and dignity;
who has fully served the main and additional punishment, and also in respect of whom the application of compulsory educational measures has been terminated in connection with the convicted person reaching the age of majority, with the exception of citizens over whom preventive supervision has been established or in respect of whom preventive observation is being carried out;
in respect of whom information has been received about the commission of acts that may create a threat to national security, cause harm to state or public interests, rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of other citizens or lead to the commission of a crime.
The court, bodies and institutions executing punishment and other measures of criminal liability, shall send documents on release from serving punishment, on termination of application of compulsory educational measures, on payment of a fine by citizens specified in paragraph four of part two of this article, on the day of release of these citizens (ten days before their release - in case of release upon expiry of the term of punishment imposed by court sentence), termination of application of compulsory educational measures, payment of a fine to internal affairs bodies at their chosen place of residence.

### Article 29. Procedure for Implementing Preventive Registration

Preventive registration is carried out in relation to citizens specified in:
paragraphs two through four of part two of Article 28 of this Law - by internal affairs bodies;
paragraph five of part two of Article 28 of this Law - by state security bodies, border service bodies, customs authorities in accordance with their competence.
The decision to implement preventive registration is made by the head of the offence prevention entity or their deputy within ten days from the moment the grounds specified in part two of Article 28 of this Law arise.
A citizen in respect of whom a decision to implement preventive registration has been made is summoned by an official of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs authority to the premises of the relevant offence prevention entity for delivery of a copy of this decision. The summons is carried out in written form, by telephone message or telegram, SMS message, using facsimile communication, the global computer network Internet, including email, as well as other means of communication ensuring recording of the summons.
An official of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs authority announces the decision to implement preventive registration to the citizen in respect of whom it has been made, delivers to them a copy of this decision and explains their rights and obligations provided for by Article 32 of this Law. If the citizen did not appear upon summons, a copy of the specified decision may be delivered to this citizen at their place of residence or place of stay, study, work or at another place determined by an official of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs authority. A corresponding record of receipt of a copy of the decision to implement preventive registration and explanation of rights and obligations is made in this decision by the citizen in respect of whom it has been made and by the official who delivered the copy of the named decision and explained the rights and obligations. In the event that the citizen refused to sign the decision to implement preventive registration, the official who delivered its copy makes a record of this in the specified decision.
The decision to implement preventive registration enters into force:
from the moment of delivery to the citizen in respect of whom it has been made of a copy of this decision - upon the arising of grounds specified in paragraphs two, three and five of part two of Article 28 of this Law;
from the moment of adoption of such decision - upon the arising of grounds specified in paragraph four of part two of Article 28 of this Law.
On the day the decision to implement preventive registration enters into force, a preventive case file is opened by the offence prevention entity. The preventive case file is maintained in electronic form, and in the absence of such possibility - in printed form.
An official of an internal affairs body, border service body, customs authority:
visits the citizen in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out at their place of residence or place of stay, study, work or at another place determined by an official of the relevant offence prevention entity, once per month, unless a different frequency of visits is determined by the head of the relevant offence prevention entity or their deputy;
determines the place and time of conducting preventive measures, ensures the participation in them of the citizen in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out.
A citizen in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out appears upon summons of an official of an internal affairs body, border service body, customs authority and participates in preventive measures. Preventive measures are conducted once per month, unless a different frequency is determined by the head of the relevant offence prevention entity or their deputy, in the form of lectures, demonstration of films of preventive orientation, including on the consideration of criminal cases, civil cases on recognizing citizens as having limited legal capacity, on removal of a child without deprivation of parental rights or on deprivation of parental rights, commitment to a medical-labor dispensary, consideration of cases on administrative offences, in other forms provided for by legislation on offence prevention. Preventive measures are conducted at a time when the citizen in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out is not engaged in work and study. The duration of a preventive measure is determined by an official of an internal affairs body, border service body, customs authority individually within the limits of one hour.
The preventive measures specified in parts seven and eight of this Article are not conducted during periods of detention in custody of citizens in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out, their serving of administrative arrest, their stay in medical-labor dispensaries, their departure beyond the borders of the Republic of Belarus, provision of medical care to them in inpatient conditions.
The procedure for conducting preventive measures with the participation of citizens in respect of whom preventive registration is carried out by state security bodies is determined by the State Security Committee.
The forms of decisions to implement preventive registration and to terminate it, the form of the preventive case file are established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 30. Termination of Implementation of Preventive Registration

Implementation of preventive registration of citizens specified in paragraphs two and three of part two of Article 28 of this Law is terminated upon expiration of one year from the day of detection of the last fact of domestic violence.
Implementation of preventive registration of citizens specified in paragraph four of part two of Article 28 of this Law is terminated from the moment of expungement or removal of criminal record.
Implementation of preventive registration of citizens specified in paragraph five of part two of Article 28 of this Law is terminated in the event of persons' refusal to commit acts that served as grounds for implementation of preventive registration, as well as due to elimination of a threat to national security, causing harm to state or public interests, rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of other citizens.
Implementation of preventive registration is terminated before the occurrence of the terms specified in parts one and two of this Article in the event of:
termination in respect of a citizen of a case on an administrative offence that served as grounds for implementation of preventive registration;
termination of preliminary investigation in a criminal case or criminal prosecution or adoption of a decision to terminate verification of a statement or report on a crime, refusal to initiate a criminal case that served as grounds for implementation of preventive registration;
entry into legal force of a court sentence on conviction of a citizen to punishments specified in items 1, 3-10 of part 1 of Article 48 of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Belarus, or other measures of criminal liability in accordance with Articles 77-79 of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Belarus;
conscription of a citizen for military service;
expulsion or deportation of a citizen from the Republic of Belarus;
death of a citizen.
A citizen in respect of whom a decision to terminate implementation of preventive registration has been made is notified of this in writing by an official of the relevant offence prevention entity within three days after adoption of this decision, with the exception of cases specified in paragraphs four through seven of part four of this Article.

### Article 31. Protective Order

A protective order is the establishment of temporary prohibitions on performing certain actions and obligations for a citizen who has committed domestic violence, for the protection of the life and health of the victim of domestic violence.
A protective order may be applied to a citizen who has committed domestic violence and in respect of whom in connection therewith:
preparation is being carried out for consideration of a case concerning an administrative offense of intentional infliction of bodily injury and other violent actions or violation of a protective order;
a check is being conducted in accordance with the requirements of criminal procedure legislation or a criminal case has been initiated concerning a crime against life and health, sexual inviolability or sexual freedom, or personal freedom, honor and dignity.
A protective order may prohibit the citizen to whom it is applied from:
attempting to ascertain the whereabouts of the victim (victims) of domestic violence, if this victim (victims) is (are) in a place unknown to the citizen who committed domestic violence;
visiting places where the victim (victims) of domestic violence is (are) located, if this victim (victims) is (are) temporarily located outside the common place of residence or place of stay;
communicating with the victim (victims) of domestic violence, including by telephone, using the global computer network Internet;
disposing of property held in common joint ownership with the victim (victims) of domestic violence.
A protective order may establish an obligation for the citizen to whom it is applied to temporarily leave the dwelling shared with the victim (victims) of domestic violence.
A protective order is applied or extended by an authorized official of an internal affairs body with the written consent of the adult victim (victims) of domestic violence, taking into account an assessment of the likelihood of continuation or repeated commission of domestic violence, occurrence of grave or especially grave consequences of its commission, including the death of the victim of domestic violence (hereinafter - risk of domestic violence).
The procedure for assessing the risk of domestic violence is determined by the Ministry of Internal Affairs in agreement with the Prosecutor General's Office.
In the absence of written consent of the adult victim (victims) of domestic violence, if this victim (victims) is (are) dependent on the citizen who committed domestic violence, or for other reasons is (are) unable to independently protect his or her (their) rights and lawful interests, and where there is a high level of risk of domestic violence, a protective order is applied by the head of the internal affairs body or his deputy in agreement with the corresponding prosecutor.
A protective order is immediately announced to the citizen to whom it is applied, with delivery of a copy of the protective order and explanation of his rights and obligations provided for by Article 32 of this Law. A corresponding entry concerning receipt of a copy of the protective order and explanation of rights and obligations is made in the protective order by this citizen and the official who delivered the copy of the protective order and explained the rights and obligations. In the event that the citizen refused to sign the protective order, the official who delivered the copy of the protective order makes an entry to this effect in the protective order.
A protective order enters into force from the moment of its announcement to the citizen to whom it is applied.
A citizen to whom a protective order is applied with establishment of the obligation specified in part four of this Article is given the opportunity to take with him only personal belongings. The specified citizen is obliged to inform an official of the internal affairs body of his place of temporary stay, to hand over keys to the dwelling shared with the victim (victims) of domestic violence for transfer to the victim (one of the victims) of domestic violence.
Within the next working day from the moment of announcement of the protective order to the citizen to whom it is applied, copies of the protective order are sent (provided) to the adult victim (victims) of domestic violence, and also to the corresponding prosecutor, to the corresponding local executive and administrative body for conducting, if necessary, preventive measures to prevent domestic violence in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation.
The prohibitions and obligation specified in parts three and four of this Article are established for a period of up to fifteen days from the moment of announcement of the protective order to the citizen to whom it is applied.
A decision of the head of the internal affairs body or his deputy to extend the protective order for a period of up to thirty days is agreed with the corresponding prosecutor. The decision to extend the protective order must set forth the grounds and reasons for such extension. Within the next working day from the moment of agreement of the decision to extend the protective order, a copy thereof is sent (provided) to the citizen to whom the protective order is applied, to the place of temporary stay indicated by him, which constitutes proper notification, and also to the adult victim (victims) of domestic violence, to the corresponding local executive and administrative body.
Control over compliance by the citizen to whom a protective order is applied with the established prohibitions and obligation, observation of his behavior at the place of temporary stay are ensured by the internal affairs body.
The effect of a protective order ceases:
upon expiration of the period for which it was applied or extended;
in the event of termination in respect of the citizen of the case concerning an administrative offense that served as the basis for application of the protective order;
in the event of termination of preliminary investigation in a criminal case or criminal prosecution, or adoption of a decision to terminate a check on an application or report of a crime, to refuse to initiate a criminal case, that served as the basis for application of the protective order.
A decision to terminate a protective order may be adopted by the head of the internal affairs body or his deputy upon application of the adult victim (victims) of domestic violence.
The form of a protective order and the form of a decision to extend a protective order are established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 311. Correctional Program

A correctional program is a set of measures for providing psychological assistance to a citizen who has committed domestic violence, aimed at correcting (adjusting) the citizen's behavior, forming skills of non-violent communication in him or her, and preventing the repeated commission of domestic violence.
The basis for conducting a correctional program is a recommendation of an official of a subject of crime prevention. A correctional program is conducted with the consent of the citizen who has committed domestic violence.
The conduct of a correctional program is ensured in accordance with legislation in the field of providing psychological assistance and is carried out on a gratuitous basis.
Local executive and administrative bodies, medical-labor preventive facilities, institutions of the penal system of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, other state organizations, when carrying out activities to provide psychological assistance within their competence, determine the state organization, other organization or psychologist responsible for conducting the correctional program, other subjects of crime prevention involved in providing psychological assistance, and inform the citizen who has committed domestic violence thereof.
The state organization, other organization or psychologist responsible for conducting the correctional program, after the direct application of the citizen who has committed domestic violence, draw up a document defining, taking into account the personality of this citizen and available information about his or her unlawful behavior, a set of measures for providing psychological assistance, the place, time, sequence and terms of their conduct, as well as the type, form and method of providing psychological assistance.
With the consent of the citizen who has committed domestic violence and the adult victim of domestic violence, psychological assistance may be provided with the participation of the latter.
To increase the effectiveness of providing psychological assistance, the internal affairs bodies and other subjects of crime prevention, within their competence, transfer to the state organization, other organization or psychologist responsible for conducting the correctional program, with the consent of the citizen who has committed domestic violence, information about offenses committed by him or her and other personal data of his or hers.

### Article 312. Consent to Transfer Information about Domestic Violence

Consent to transfer information about domestic violence is a free, unambiguous, informed expression of the will of a victim of domestic violence regarding the transfer of information about domestic violence committed against him or her and other personal data of his or hers necessary for protecting the victim of domestic violence, providing him or her with assistance and social services provided by legislation by the relevant subjects of crime prevention.
Consent to transfer information about domestic violence is sent within the next working day from the moment of its receipt by the subject conducting preventive measures to prevent domestic violence to subjects of crime prevention according to their competence.
The presence of consent to transfer information about domestic violence or the direct application of a victim of domestic violence to a subject of crime prevention are grounds for protecting the victim of domestic violence, providing him or her, on the initiative of the relevant subject of crime prevention, with assistance and social services.
The form of consent to transfer information about domestic violence is established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 313. Register of Information on Facts of Domestic Violence

The register of information on facts of domestic violence is a state information system intended for collecting, processing, registering, accumulating, preserving and using information on facts of domestic violence.
The functioning of the register of information on facts of domestic violence is ensured by the Ministry of Internal Affairs, which is its owner.
The Ministry of Internal Affairs ensures:
formation of the register of information on facts of domestic violence;
preservation and protection of information on facts of domestic violence;
compliance with the procedure for providing stored information on facts of domestic violence.
Other subjects of crime prevention subordinate to the Government of the Republic of Belarus that conduct preventive measures to prevent domestic violence, within their competence, ensure the registration and transfer of information on facts of domestic violence to the internal affairs bodies.
When registering information on facts of domestic violence, the following are recorded:
the name of the subject of crime prevention, information about the official, the date and basis for entering information about a fact of domestic violence into the register of information on facts of domestic violence;
the place, date, time and circumstances of the commission of domestic violence;
the surname, given name, patronymic (if any), place of residence and contact telephone number of the citizen who reported the fact of domestic violence;
the identification number (if available), surname, given name, patronymic (if any), day, month, year and place of birth, education, place of work (study) and position of employee (profession of worker), place of residence and contact telephone number of the victim of domestic violence;
the identification number (if available), surname, given name, patronymic (if any), day, month, year and place of birth, education, place of work (study) and position of employee (profession of worker), place of residence and contact telephone number of the citizen who committed domestic violence;
data on the provision of assistance to the victim of domestic violence;
measures taken against the citizen who committed domestic violence, their result.
For the purposes of forming the register of information on facts of domestic violence and effective application of measures of individual crime prevention, the processing of personal data is carried out by officials of subjects of crime prevention who conduct preventive measures to prevent domestic violence, without the consent of the subject of personal data, in compliance with the requirements of legislation on personal data.
Personal data placed in the register of information on facts of domestic violence are stored for five years. Upon expiration of this period, personal data placed in the register of information on facts of domestic violence are deleted.
Issues of the functioning of the register of information on facts of domestic violence not regulated by this Law and other legislative acts are regulated in the manner established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 32

Rights and obligations of citizens in respect of whom individual prevention of offences is carried out
Citizens in respect of whom individual prevention of offences is carried out have the right to:
know the grounds for applying to them measures of individual prevention of offences;
familiarise themselves with the materials of preventive files opened in respect of them;
exercise other rights provided for by this Law and other legislative acts.
Citizens in respect of whom individual prevention of offences is carried out are obliged to:
appear in a timely manner upon summons by officials of authorised subjects of prevention of offences;
participate in preventive measures conducted by officials of authorised subjects of prevention of offences;
receive copies of official warnings issued in respect of them;
receive copies of decisions on the implementation of preventive registration adopted in respect of them;
receive protective orders issued in respect of them, not violate the prohibitions, fulfil the obligations contained in these protective orders;
fulfil other obligations provided for by this Law and other legislative acts.
Restriction of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of citizens in respect of whom individual prevention of offences is carried out, including restriction of rights to secrecy of correspondence, telephone and other communications, free movement and choice of place of residence, inviolability of dwelling and other lawful possessions of citizens, is not permitted unless otherwise provided for by this Law and other legislative acts.

### Article 321. Rights of victims of domestic violence and other offences

Victims of domestic violence and other offences, regardless of registration at place of residence and consent to the transfer of information about domestic violence, have the right in accordance with legislation to receive:
protection and prompt response in all cases of offences committed against them;
full and comprehensive information about the types of assistance provided within the competence of state bodies and other organisations;
free of charge psychological assistance, social services in the form of urgent social services, including temporary shelter services;
free medical and legal assistance;
access to education, including for minor children residing with them.
Protection of a victim of domestic violence and other offences, provision of assistance and social services to them do not depend on their readiness to file an application for bringing a citizen who committed domestic violence or another person who committed an offence to criminal or administrative liability or to testify against them.
The rights of minor victims of domestic violence and other offences are protected taking into account the interests of the child, their age, sex, state of health, intellectual and physical development. The exercise of protection of the rights and legitimate interests of minors is entrusted to their parents and other legal representatives, and in the event of their absence or commission by them of domestic violence against minors - to other relatives or guardianship and trusteeship bodies.

## CHAPTER 5

OTHER ISSUES OF ACTIVITIES FOR THE PREVENTION OF OFFENCES

### Article 33

Liability for violation of legislation in the sphere of prevention of offences
Violation of legislation in the sphere of prevention of offences entails liability established by legislative acts.

### Article 34

Procedure for appealing decisions and actions (inaction) of officials of subjects of prevention of offences
Citizens who believe that their rights, freedoms and legitimate interests have been infringed, as well as organisations that believe that their rights and legitimate interests have been infringed by decisions and actions (inaction) of subjects of prevention of offences, their officials, have the right to appeal these decisions and actions (inaction) in the established procedure to a higher state body (higher official), and thereafter - to the prosecutor or to a court.

### Article 35. Control and supervision in the sphere of prevention of offences

Control over the activities of subjects of prevention of offences is exercised by higher state bodies and officials.
Supervision over the precise and uniform enforcement of legislation in the sphere of prevention of offences is exercised by the Prosecutor General and prosecutors subordinate to him.

### Article 36

Financing and material and technical support of activities for the prevention of offences
Financing and material and technical support of activities for the prevention of offences are carried out at the expense of funds from the republican and local budgets, as well as other sources not prohibited by legislation.

"CHAPTER 41
COUNCILS OF PUBLIC POINTS

### Article 231. Formation of councils of public points

Councils of public points are an organisational form of combining the efforts of voluntary squads, organisations and citizens in activities for the protection of public order and prevention of offences, as well as their interaction with subjects of prevention of offences defined by legislative acts of the Republic of Belarus.
Councils of public points are formed from among citizens recommended by subjects of prevention of offences, with the consent of these citizens, including from among officials of subjects of prevention of offences, and carry out their activities under the general guidance of the relevant local executive and administrative bodies.
Decisions on the establishment and abolition of councils of public points, approval of their personal composition are adopted by the relevant local executive and administrative bodies. The procedure for the establishment and abolition of councils of public points, their interaction with subjects of prevention of offences, as well as measures to stimulate citizens and public associations participating in activities for the protection of public order and prevention of offences, are determined by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.
The permanent location of councils of public points are public points - special premises determined by the relevant local executive and administrative bodies.

### Article 232. Powers of public point councils

Public point councils:
study the state of public order in the territory assigned to the public point council, provide assistance to subjects of crime prevention in activities for the prevention and suppression of offenses;
coordinate the work of organizations represented in the public point council, facilitate the establishment of permanent interaction and exchange of experience in the prevention and suppression of offenses between these organizations and subjects of crime prevention located in the territory assigned to the public point council;
organize duty shifts of members of the public point council at the public point, hear their reports on the performance of duties to participate in the protection of public order;
organize discussion of issues of strengthening public order, as well as the behavior of citizens brought to administrative or criminal responsibility, at their meetings, at gatherings of citizens at their place of residence or place of stay, study or work, with the submission of appropriate recommendations to subjects of crime prevention;
develop and submit to subjects of crime prevention recommendations on issues of prevention and suppression of offenses in the territory assigned to the public point council;
petition the subjects specified in part one of Article 22 of this Law for the encouragement of citizens actively participating in activities for the prevention and suppression of offenses.

### Article 233. Support for the activities of public point councils

Subjects of crime prevention provide assistance in the functioning of public point councils. Necessary conditions are created at public points for the work of employees of internal affairs bodies, emergency situation bodies and units, border service bodies of the Republic of Belarus, officials of other subjects of crime prevention.
Internal affairs bodies, emergency situation bodies and units, border service bodies of the Republic of Belarus provide public point councils with instructional and methodological manuals and provide them with information necessary for their activities in the volume determined by the heads of these bodies and units or their deputies.
Provision of public point councils with necessary premises, means of communication, furniture, other material and technical means, payment for utilities, communication services, operational expenses of said points is carried out at the expense of funds from corresponding local budgets and other sources not prohibited by the legislation of the Republic of Belarus.".

### Article 38. Recognition of a law and a separate provision of a law as having lost force

Recognize as having lost force:
the Law of the Republic of Belarus of November 10, 2008 "On the Fundamentals of Activities for the Prevention of Offenses" (National Register of Legal Acts of the Republic of Belarus, 2008, No. 277, 2/1549);
Article 18 of the Law of the Republic of Belarus of December 28, 2009 "On Amendments and Additions to Certain Laws of the Republic of Belarus" (National Register of Legal Acts of the Republic of Belarus, 2010, No. 5, 2/1630).

### Article 39. Implementation of the provisions of this Law

The Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus within three months shall:
bring decisions of the Government of the Republic of Belarus into compliance with this Law;
ensure that republican bodies of state administration subordinate to the Government of the Republic of Belarus bring their regulatory legal acts into compliance with this Law;
take other measures necessary for the implementation of the provisions of this Law.

### Article 40. Entry into force of this Law

This Law enters into force in the following order:
Articles 1-38 - three months after the official publication of this Law;
other provisions - after the official publication of this Law.
