FIRST PART
Fundamental Principles, Definitions and Scope of Application

FIRST CHAPTER
Fundamental Principles and Definitions Purpose of the Penal Code

## Article 1

(1) The purpose of the Penal Code is to protect individual rights and freedoms, public order and security, the rule of law, public health and the environment, social peace, and to prevent the commission of crimes. In the Code, the fundamental principles of criminal liability and the types of crimes, punishments and security measures are regulated for the realization of this purpose.

Principle of legality in crime and punishment

## Article 2

(1) No one may be punished or subjected to a security measure for an act that is not expressly defined as a crime by law. No punishment or security measure other than those prescribed by law may be imposed.
(2) Crimes and punishments may not be established by regulatory acts of the administration.
(3) Analogy may not be used in the application of provisions of laws containing crimes and punishments. Provisions containing crimes and punishments may not be interpreted broadly in a manner that would lead to analogy.

Principle of justice and equality before the law

## Article 3

(1) A person who commits a crime shall be sentenced to a punishment and security measure proportionate to the gravity of the act committed.
(2) In the application of the Penal Code, no distinction may be made between persons on the basis of race, language, religion, sect, nationality, color, sex, political or other ideas or thoughts, philosophical belief, national or social origin, birth, economic or other social status, and no privilege may be granted to anyone.

SECOND CHAPTER
Scope of Application of the Law Application in terms of time

## Article 7

(1) No one may be punished or subjected to a security measure for an act that is not considered a crime under the law in force at the time it was committed. No one may be punished or subjected to a security measure for an act that is not considered a crime under the law that enters into force after it was committed. If such a punishment or security measure has been imposed, its execution and legal consequences shall automatically cease.
(2)	If the provisions of the law in force at the time the crime was committed and the laws that subsequently enter into force are different, the law more favorable to the offender shall be applied and executed.
(3)	(Amended: 29/6/2005 – 5377/2 art.) Except for those relating to suspension of imprisonment, conditional release and recidivism; provisions relating to the execution regime shall be applied immediately.
(4)	Temporary or time-limited laws shall continue to be applied to crimes committed during the period they were in force.

Application in terms of place

## Article 8

(1) Turkish laws shall apply to crimes committed in Turkey. If the act is committed partially or wholly in Turkey or the result occurs in Turkey, the crime shall be deemed to have been committed in Turkey.
(2) A crime;
a)	In Turkish land and airspace and in Turkish territorial waters,
b)	On the high seas and in the airspace above it, in or by Turkish sea and air vessels,
c)	In or by Turkish naval and air war vessels,
d)	On fixed platforms established on Turkey's continental shelf or in its exclusive economic zone or against them,
shall be deemed to have been committed in Turkey when committed.

Conviction in a foreign country

## Article 9

(1) A person who has been convicted in a foreign country for a crime committed in Turkey shall be retried in Turkey.

Official crimes

## Article 10

(1) A person who has undertaken an office or duty on behalf of Turkey in a foreign country and commits a crime in connection therewith shall be retried in Turkey even if a conviction has been rendered against him in the foreign country in relation to this act.

Crime committed by a citizen

## Article 11

(1) If a Turkish citizen commits in a foreign country, other than the crimes specified in Article 13, a crime requiring imprisonment with a lower limit of not less than one year under Turkish laws, and he is in Turkey, he shall be punished under Turkish laws provided that no conviction has been rendered in the foreign country for this crime and prosecutability exists in Turkey.

(2) If the crime requires imprisonment with a lower limit of less than one year, prosecution shall depend on the complaint of the injured party or the foreign government. In this case, the complaint must be made within six months from the date the citizen enters Turkey.

Crime committed by a foreigner

## Article 12

(1) A foreigner who commits an offense against Turkey in a foreign country, other than the offenses specified in Article 13, which requires a minimum sentence of at least one year of imprisonment according to Turkish laws, and who is found in Turkey, shall be punished according to Turkish laws. Prosecution is subject to the request of the Minister of Justice.
(2) In the event that the offense specified in the above paragraph is committed against a Turkish citizen or a private law legal entity established under Turkish laws and the perpetrator is found in Turkey, the perpetrator shall be punished according to Turkish laws upon the complaint of the victim of the offense, provided that no judgment has been rendered in a foreign country for this offense.
(3) If the victim is a foreigner, the perpetrator shall be prosecuted upon the request of the Minister of Justice in the presence of the following conditions:
a) The offense requires a minimum sentence of imprisonment of not less than three years according to Turkish laws.
b) There is no extradition agreement or the extradition request has not been accepted by the government of the country where the offense was committed or the state of which the perpetrator is a national.
(4) A foreigner who has been convicted by a foreign court for an offense falling within the scope of the first paragraph, or whose case or sentence has been dropped for any reason, or who has been acquitted, or whose offense has ceased to be prosecutable, shall be retried in Turkey upon the request of the Minister of Justice.

SECOND PART
Principles of Criminal Liability

FIRST CHAPTER
Personal Nature of Criminal Liability, Intent and Negligence Personal nature of criminal liability

## Article 20

(1) Criminal liability is personal. No one can be held responsible for another person's act.
(2) Criminal sanctions cannot be applied to legal entities. However, security measures of a sanction nature provided for in the law due to an offense are reserved.

Intent

## Article 21

(1) The existence of an offense depends on the existence of intent. Intent is the knowing and willing realization of the elements in the legal definition of the offense.
(2) If a person commits the act despite foreseeing that the elements in the legal definition of the offense may be realized, there is possible intent. In this case, a sentence of life imprisonment shall be imposed for offenses requiring aggravated life imprisonment, a sentence of imprisonment from twenty to twenty-five years for offenses requiring life imprisonment; in other offenses, the basic penalty shall be reduced from one-third to one-half.

Negligence

## Article 22

(1) Acts committed through negligence shall be punished in cases expressly specified by law.
(2) Negligence is the realization of a behavior without foreseeing the consequence specified in the legal definition of the offense due to violation of the obligation of care and attention.
(3) If the consequence occurs despite the person not wanting the consequence they foresaw, there is conscious negligence; in this case, the penalty for the negligent offense shall be increased from one-third to one-half.
(4) The penalty to be imposed for an offense committed through negligence shall be determined according to the perpetrator's fault.
(5) In offenses committed through negligence by more than one person, each person shall be responsible for their own fault. The penalty of each perpetrator shall be determined separately according to their fault.

(6) If the consequence caused by the negligent act has led to the perpetrator becoming a victim to such a degree that imposing a penalty is no longer necessary solely in terms of the perpetrator's personal and family situation, no penalty shall be imposed; in case of conscious negligence, the penalty to be imposed may be reduced from one-half to one-sixth.

Offense aggravated by consequence

## Article 23

(1) In the event that an act causes the formation of a consequence more severe or different than intended, in order for a person to be held responsible for this, they must have acted at least negligently with respect to this consequence.

SECOND CHAPTER
Reasons that Remove or Reduce Criminal Liability Provision of law and order of superior

## Article 24- (1) No penalty shall be imposed on a person who executes the provision of law

(2) A person who executes an order given by a competent authority, the execution of which is mandatory by virtue of duty, shall not be responsible.
(3) An order constituting an offense can under no circumstances be executed. Otherwise, both the executor and the person who gave the order shall be responsible.
(4) In cases where the review of the lawfulness of the order is prevented by law, the person who gave the order shall be responsible for its execution.

Legitimate defense and state of necessity

## Article 25

(1) No penalty shall be imposed on the perpetrator for acts committed out of the necessity to repel an unlawful attack directed against a right belonging to oneself or another, which has occurred, is occurring or is certain to occur or recur, in a manner proportionate to the attack according to the circumstances at that moment.
(2) No penalty shall be imposed on the perpetrator for acts committed out of the necessity to escape or to rescue another from a severe and certain danger directed against a right belonging to oneself or another, which one did not knowingly cause and from which there is no other means of protection, provided that there is proportionality between the severity of the danger and the subject matter and means used.

Exercise of rights and consent of the person concerned

## Article 26- (1) No penalty shall be imposed on a person who exercises their right

(2) No penalty shall be imposed on anyone for an act committed within the scope of the consent expressed by a person, in relation to a right over which that person has absolute power of disposal.

Exceeding the limit

## Article 27

(1) In cases where the limit is exceeded without intent in circumstances that eliminate criminal liability, if the act is also punishable when committed through negligence, a sentence shall be imposed by reducing the penalty prescribed by law for the negligent offence by one-sixth to one-third.

(2) If exceeding the limit in legitimate defence arises from excusable agitation, fear or panic, no penalty shall be imposed on the perpetrator.

Coercion and violence, intimidation and threat

## Article 28

(1) No penalty shall be imposed on a person who commits an offence as a result of coercion and violence that they cannot resist or escape from, or as a result of certain and severe intimidation or threat. In such cases, the person who uses coercion and violence, intimidation and threat shall be deemed the perpetrator of the offence.

Unjust provocation

## Article 29

(1) A person who commits an offence under the influence of anger or severe distress caused by an unlawful act shall be sentenced to imprisonment from eighteen years to twenty-four years instead of aggravated life imprisonment, and from twelve years to eighteen years instead of life imprisonment. In other cases, the sentence to be imposed shall be reduced by one-quarter to three-quarters.

Mistake

## Article 30

(1) A person who does not know the material elements in the legal definition of the offence during the commission of the act does not act intentionally. Liability for negligence due to this mistake is reserved.
(2) A person who is mistaken about the existence of qualified circumstances of an offence that require more severe or less severe punishment shall benefit from this mistake.
(3) A person who falls into an unavoidable mistake about the existence of conditions relating to circumstances that eliminate or reduce criminal liability shall benefit from this mistake.
(4) (Additional paragraph: 29/6/2005 – Article 4 of Law No. 5377) A person who falls into an unavoidable mistake about the fact that the act they committed constitutes wrongdoing shall not be punished.

Minority

## Article 31

(1) Children who have not completed twelve years of age at the time of committing the act have no criminal liability. No criminal prosecution may be conducted against such persons; however, security measures specific to children may be applied.
(2) (Amended: 29/6/2005 – Article 5 of Law No. 5377) Those who have completed twelve years of age but have not completed fifteen years of age at the time of committing the act have no criminal liability if they are unable to perceive the legal meaning and consequences of the act they committed or if their capacity to direct their behaviour is not sufficiently developed. However, security measures specific to children shall be imposed on such persons. In the presence of the capacity to perceive the legal meaning and consequences of the act committed and to direct their behaviour in relation to this act, if the offence requires aggravated life imprisonment, such persons shall be sentenced to imprisonment from twelve years to fifteen years; if it requires life imprisonment, from nine years to eleven years. Half of other sentences shall be reduced and in this case the imprisonment sentence to be imposed for each act may not exceed seven years.

(3) (Amended: 29/6/2005 – Article 5 of Law No. 5377) For persons who have completed fifteen years of age but have not completed eighteen years of age at the time of committing the act, if the offence requires aggravated life imprisonment, a sentence of imprisonment from eighteen years to twenty-four years shall be imposed; if it requires life imprisonment, from twelve years to fifteen years. One-third of other sentences shall be reduced and in this case the imprisonment sentence to be imposed for each act may not exceed twelve years.

Mental illness

## Article 32

(1) No penalty shall be imposed on a person who, due to mental illness, is unable to perceive the legal meaning and consequences of the act they committed or whose capacity to direct their behaviour in relation to this act is significantly diminished. However, a security measure shall be imposed on such persons.
(2) For a person whose capacity to direct their behaviour in relation to the act they committed is diminished, although not to the degree specified in the first paragraph, twenty-five years of imprisonment shall be imposed instead of aggravated life imprisonment, and twenty years of imprisonment instead of life imprisonment. In other cases, the sentence to be imposed may be reduced by up to one-sixth. The sentence imposed may also be applied, partially or wholly, as a security measure specific to the mentally ill, provided that the duration is the same.

Deaf-muteness

## Article 33

(1) The provisions of this Code relating to children who have not completed twelve years of age at the time of committing the act shall also apply to deaf-mutes who have not completed fifteen years of age; the provisions relating to those who have completed twelve years of age but have not completed fifteen years of age shall also apply to deaf-mutes who have completed fifteen years of age but have not completed eighteen years of age; the provisions relating to those who have completed fifteen years of age but have not completed eighteen years of age shall also apply to deaf-mutes who have completed eighteen years of age but have not completed twenty-one years of age.

Temporary causes, being under the influence of alcohol or drugs

## Article 34

(1) No penalty shall be imposed on a person who, due to a temporary cause or under the influence of alcohol or drugs taken involuntarily, is unable to perceive the legal meaning and consequences of the act they committed or whose capacity to direct their behaviour in relation to this act is significantly diminished.
(2) The provision of the first paragraph shall not apply to a person who commits an offence under the influence of alcohol or drugs taken voluntarily.

PART THREE
Attempt to Commit an Offence
Attempt to commit an offence

## Article 35

(1) If a person, having commenced the direct commission of an offence they intended to commit through suitable acts, fails to complete it due to reasons beyond their control, they shall be held liable for attempt.

(2) In case of attempt to commit an offence, the perpetrator shall be punished, according to the severity of the harm or danger that occurred, with imprisonment from thirteen years to twenty years instead of aggravated life imprisonment, from nine years to fifteen years instead of life imprisonment. In other cases, one-quarter to three-quarters of the sentence to be imposed shall be reduced.

Voluntary abandonment

## Article 36

(1) If the perpetrator voluntarily abandons the acts of commission of the offence or prevents the completion of the offence or the occurrence of the result through their own efforts, they shall not be punished for attempt; however, if the completed part itself constitutes an offence, they shall be punished only with the penalty for that offence.

PART FOUR
Participation in an Offence
Perpetration

## Article 37

(1) Each of the persons who jointly carry out the act specified in the legal definition of the offence shall be liable as a perpetrator.
(2) A person who uses another as an instrument in the commission of the offence shall also be held liable as a perpetrator. The penalty of a person who uses those without capacity for culpability as an instrument in the commission of the offence shall be increased by one-third to one-half.

Instigation

## Article 38

(1) A person who instigates another to commit an offence shall be punished with the penalty for the offence committed.
(2) In case of instigation to commit an offence by using influence arising from the relationship of ascendants and descendants, the penalty of the instigator shall be increased by one-third to one-half. In case of instigation of children to commit an offence, the existence of the relationship of ascendants and descendants shall not be required for the increase of the penalty according to the provision of this paragraph.
(3) If the instigator is not identified, the perpetrator or other accomplice who enables the identification of who they are may be sentenced to imprisonment from twenty years to twenty-five years instead of aggravated life imprisonment, from fifteen years to twenty years instead of life imprisonment. In other cases, a reduction of one-third may be made in the sentence to be imposed.

Aiding and abetting

## Article 39

(1) A person who aids in the commission of a crime shall be sentenced to imprisonment from fifteen to twenty years if the crime committed requires an aggravated life imprisonment sentence; from ten to fifteen years if it requires a life imprisonment sentence. In other cases, half of the sentence shall be reduced. However, the sentence to be imposed in this case cannot exceed eight years.
(2) In the following cases, a person shall be held responsible as an aider for the crime committed:
a) Encouraging the commission of a crime or strengthening the decision to commit a crime or promising to provide assistance after the commission of the act.

b) Guiding on how to commit the crime or providing the means used in the commission of the act.
c) Facilitating its execution by providing assistance before or during the commission of the crime.

Principle of dependency

## Article 40

(1) For participation in a crime, the existence of an intentionally and unlawfully committed act is sufficient. Each person who participates in the commission of the crime shall be punished according to their own culpable act, without regard to personal reasons that prevent the punishment of the other.
(2) In specific crimes, only a person who possesses the special perpetrator qualification can be a principal. Other persons who participate in the commission of these crimes shall be held responsible as instigators or aiders.
(3) In order to be held responsible for participation in a crime, the relevant crime must have reached at least the attempt stage.

Voluntary withdrawal in crimes committed with participation

## Article 41

(1) In crimes committed with participation, only the accomplice who voluntarily withdraws benefits from the provisions on voluntary withdrawal.
(2) The provisions on voluntary withdrawal shall also apply in cases where the crime:
a) Has not been committed for a reason other than the effort shown by the person who voluntarily withdrew,
b) Has been committed despite all the efforts of the person who voluntarily withdrew.

FIFTH CHAPTER
Concurrence of Crimes
Compound crime

## Article 42

(1) A crime that is considered a single act because one constitutes an element or aggravating reason of the other is called a compound crime. In such crimes, the provisions on concurrence do not apply.

Continuing crime

## Article 43

(1) In the event that the same crime is committed more than once against one person at different times within the scope of the execution of a decision to commit a crime, a single sentence shall be imposed. However, this sentence shall be increased from one-fourth to three-fourths. The basic form of a crime and its qualified forms requiring more severe or less severe punishment are considered the same crime. (Additional sentence: 29/6/2005 – Article 6 of Law No. 5377.) This paragraph provision shall also apply to crimes without a specific victim.
(2) In the event that the same crime is committed against more than one person with a single act, the provision of the first paragraph shall apply.
(3) The provisions of this article do not apply to intentional killing, intentional injury, torture and robbery crimes.4

Ideal concurrence

## Article 44

(1) A person who causes the formation of more than one different crime with a single act committed shall be punished for the crime requiring the most severe punishment among them.

THIRD PART
Sanctions

Punishments

Fixed-term imprisonment sentence

Judicial fine

## Article 52

(1) A judicial fine consists of the payment to the State Treasury by the convict of the amount calculated by multiplying the number of full days determined as not less than five days and, in cases where there is no provision to the contrary in the law, not more than seven hundred and thirty days, by the amount determined as the equivalent of one day.
(2) The amount of the judicial fine per day, which is a minimum of twenty and a maximum of one hundred Turkish Liras, shall be determined taking into account the person's economic and other personal circumstances.
(3) In the judgment, the number of full days taken as basis in determining the judicial fine and the amount determined as the equivalent of one day shall be shown separately.
(4) The judge, taking into account the economic and personal circumstances, may grant the person a period of not more than one year from the date of finalization of the judgment to pay the judicial fine, as well as decide that this fine be paid in certain installments. The installment period cannot exceed two years and the number of installments cannot be less than four. The judgment shall state that if one of the installments is not paid on time, the entire remaining part will be collected and the unpaid judicial fine will be converted into imprisonment.

FIRST CHAPTER
Genocide and Crimes Against Humanity
Genocide

## Article 76

(1) The commission of one of the following acts against members of a national, ethnic, racial or religious group with the intent to destroy that group, in whole or in part, in the execution of a plan, constitutes the crime of genocide:
a) Intentional killing.
b) Causing serious bodily or mental harm to persons.
c) Forcing the group to live under conditions that would result in its destruction, in whole or in part.
d) Taking measures aimed at preventing births within the group.
e) Forcibly transferring children of the group to another group.
(2) The perpetrator of the crime of genocide shall be sentenced to aggravated life imprisonment. However, with respect to the crimes of intentional killing and intentional injury committed within the scope of genocide, the provisions on real concurrence shall apply for the number of victims determined.
(3) Security measures shall also be imposed on legal persons for these crimes.
(4) There shall be no statute of limitations for these crimes.

Crimes against humanity

## Article 77

(1) The systematic commission of the following acts against a section of society in accordance with a plan for political, philosophical, racial or religious motives constitutes a crime against humanity:
a) Intentional killing.
b) Intentional injury.
c) Torture, torment or enslavement.

d) Deprivation of personal liberty.
e) Subjecting to scientific experiments.
f) Sexual assault, sexual abuse of children.

## Article 108

(1) If coercion is used against a person to make them do or not do something or to allow them to do it, the penalty to be imposed for the offence of intentional injury shall be increased from one-third to one-half.

Deprivation of liberty

## Article 109

(1) A person who unlawfully deprives someone of their freedom to go to a place or to remain in a place shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one year to five years.
(2) If the person uses force, threat or fraud in order to commit the act or while committing it, imprisonment from two years to seven years shall be imposed.
(3) If this offence is committed;
a) With a weapon,
b) By more than one person together,
c) By reason of the public duty performed by the person,
d) By abusing the influence provided by public duty,
e) Against an ascendant, descendant or spouse or divorced spouse,42
f) Against a child or a person who is unable to defend himself or herself physically or mentally,
The penalty to be given according to the above paragraphs shall be increased by one fold.
(4) If this offence causes an economically significant loss to the victim, a judicial fine of up to one thousand days shall also be imposed.
(5) If the offence is committed for sexual purposes, the penalties to be given according to the above paragraphs shall be increased by half.
(6) In the event that aggravated forms of the offence of intentional injury due to the result occur for the purpose of or during the commission of this offence, the provisions relating to the offence of intentional injury shall also be applied.

## Article 181

(4) If a person who uses narcotic or stimulant substances, before an investigation is initiated against them for purchasing, accepting or possessing narcotic or stimulant substances for use, applies to official authorities or health institutions requesting to be treated, they shall not be sentenced to punishment. Manufacturing and trafficking of poisonous substances

## Article 193

(1) A person who unlawfully produces, possesses, sells or transports a substance containing poison and whose production, possession or sale is subject to permission shall be punished with imprisonment from two months to one year.

Supply of substances dangerous to health

## Article 194

(1) A person who gives or offers for consumption substances that may pose a danger to health to children, mentally ill persons or volatile substance users shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to one year.

Acting contrary to measures regarding contagious diseases

## Article 195

(1) A person who fails to comply with measures taken by competent authorities for the quarantine of a place where a person infected with or deceased from one of the contagious diseases is located shall be punished with imprisonment from two months to one year.

Irregular burial of the dead

(1) A person who buries or causes to be buried a dead body in places other than those designated for burial of the dead shall be punished with imprisonment up to six months.

FOURTH CHAPTER
Crimes Against Public Trust
Counterfeiting of currency

## Article 197

(1) A person who produces, brings into the country, transports, keeps or puts into circulation currency that is legally in circulation in the country or in foreign countries as counterfeit shall be punished with imprisonment from two years to twelve years and a judicial fine up to ten thousand days.
(2) A person who knowingly accepts counterfeit currency shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to three years and a judicial fine.
(3) A person who puts into circulation currency that they accepted without knowing it was counterfeit, knowing this quality, shall be punished with imprisonment from three months to one year.

Values considered equal to currency

## Article 198

(1) Bonds, shares, bonds and coupons issued by the State and written to bearer, notes, bonds and documents issued by authorized institutions and legally in circulation, and national ornamental gold are considered currency.

Counterfeiting of valuable stamps

## Article 199

(1) A person who produces, brings into the country, transports, keeps or puts into circulation valuable stamps as counterfeit shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to five years and a judicial fine.
(2) A person who knowingly accepts valuable stamps produced as counterfeit shall be punished with imprisonment from three months to one year and a judicial fine.

(4) Stamped papers, stamps and postage stamps, and stamps used to certify that a certain amount of tax or fee has been paid are considered valuable stamps.

Instruments for making currency and valuable stamps

## Article 200

(1) A person who unlawfully produces, brings into the country, sells, transfers, purchases, accepts or keeps tools or materials used in the production of currency and valuable stamps shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to four years and a judicial fine.

Effective remorse

## Article 201

(1) A person who produces, brings into the country, transports, keeps or accepts counterfeit currency or valuable stamps, if before putting this currency or valuable stamps into circulation and before being informed by official authorities, reports to the competent authority the other accomplices and the places where the counterfeit currency or valuable stamps are produced or stored, shall not be sentenced to punishment if the information provided ensures the capture of the accomplices and the seizure of the counterfeit currency or valuable stamps.
(2) A person who unlawfully produces, brings into the country, sells, transfers, purchases, accepts or keeps tools and materials used in the production of counterfeit currency or valuable stamps, if before being informed by official authorities, reports to the relevant authority the other accomplices and the places where this material is produced or stored, shall not be sentenced to punishment if the information provided ensures the capture of the accomplices and the seizure of this material.

Counterfeiting of seals

## Article 202

(1) A person who produces or uses as counterfeit the seal used by the Presidency, the Presidency of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey and the Prime Ministry shall be punished with imprisonment from two years to eight years.
(2) A person who produces or uses as counterfeit an authenticating or certifying seal used by public institutions and organizations or professional organizations with the status of public institutions shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to six years.

Breaking seals

## Article 203

(1) A person who removes a seal placed to ensure the preservation or maintenance of the existence of something as it is pursuant to law or the order of competent authorities, or acts contrary to its purpose of placement, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to three years or a judicial fine.

Forgery of official documents

## Article 204

(1) A person who prepares an official document as false, alters a genuine official document in a manner to deceive others, or uses a false official document shall be punished with imprisonment from two years to five years.
(2) A public official who prepares as false an official document that they are authorized to prepare by virtue of their duty, alters a genuine document in a manner to deceive others, prepares a document contrary to the truth, or uses a false official document shall be punished with imprisonment from three years to eight years.
(3) If the official document is of the nature of a document that is valid until its falsity is proven by force of law, the penalty to be imposed shall be increased by half.

Destroying, eliminating or concealing an official document

## Article 205

(1) A person who destroys, eliminates or conceals a genuine official document shall be punished with imprisonment from two years to five years. If the crime is committed by a public official, the penalty to be imposed shall be increased by half.

False statement in the preparation of an official document

## Article 206

(1) A person who makes a false statement to a public official who has the authority to prepare an official document shall be punished with imprisonment from three months to two years or a judicial fine.

Forgery of private documents

## Article 207

(1) A person who prepares a private document as false or alters a genuine private document in a manner to deceive others and uses it shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to three years.
(2) A person who uses a false private document knowing this quality shall also be punished according to the provision of the above paragraph.

Destroying, eliminating or concealing a private document

## Article 208

(1) A person who destroys, annihilates or conceals a genuine private document shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to three years.

Misuse of signature in blank

## Article 209

(1) A person who fills in a signed and partially or completely blank paper delivered to him to be filled in and used in a specific manner, in a way different from the reason for its delivery, shall be punished, upon complaint, with imprisonment from three months to one year.

Documents having the force of official documents

(2) A physician, dentist, pharmacist, midwife, nurse or other health professional who prepares a document contrary to the truth shall be punished with imprisonment from three months to one year. Where the prepared document provides an unlawful benefit to a person or is of a nature to produce a result to the detriment of the public or persons, a penalty shall be imposed according to the provisions on forgery of official documents.

Circumstance requiring lesser punishment

## Article 211

(1) Where the crime of forgery of a document is committed for the purpose of proving a claim based on a legal relationship or documenting a real situation, the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced by half.

Joinder

## Article 212

(1) Where a forged official or private document is used during the commission of another crime, separate penalties shall be imposed for both forgery and the relevant crime.

Offences Relating to Economy, Industry and Commerce Rigging a tender

## Article 235

(1) (Amended: 11/4/2013-Article 12 of Law No. 6459) A person who rigs tenders relating to the purchase or sale of goods or services or leases made on behalf of public institutions or organisations or construction tenders shall be punished with imprisonment from three years to seven years.
(2) A tender shall be deemed to have been rigged in the following cases:
a) By fraudulent conduct;
1. Preventing persons who have the eligibility or conditions to participate in the tender from participating in the tender or in the transactions in the tender process,
2. Enabling persons who do not have the eligibility or conditions to participate in the tender to participate in the tender,
3. Excluding the goods offered from evaluation on the grounds that they do not possess the qualities specified in their specifications, although they do possess them,
4. Taking into evaluation the goods offered on the grounds that they possess the qualities specified in their specifications, although they do not possess them.
b) Enabling others to access information relating to bids that must be kept confidential according to tender legislation or specifications.
c) Preventing persons who have the eligibility or conditions to participate in the tender from participating in the tender or in the transactions in the tender process by using force or threat or by other unlawful conduct.
d) Persons who wish to participate or participate in the tender making open or secret agreements among themselves to influence the tender conditions and especially the price.
(3) (Amended: 11/4/2013-Article 12 of Law No. 6459) Where the offence of rigging a tender;
a) Is committed by using force or threat, the lower limit of the basic penalty cannot be less than five years. However, where the qualified forms of the offence of intentional injury or threat requiring heavier penalty are realised, a penalty shall also be imposed for these offences.

(4) Officials who obtain benefit by reason of rigging a tender shall also be punished according to the relevant offence provision for this reason.
(5) The provisions of the above paragraphs shall also be applied where auctions or tenders made through public institutions or organisations or rigging is committed in the purchase or sale of goods or services or leases made on behalf of professional organisations having the nature of public institutions, companies established with the participation of public institutions or organisations or professional organisations having the nature of public institutions, foundations operating within them, associations or cooperatives working for the public benefit.

Rigging the performance of an obligation

## Article 236

(1) A person who rigs the performance of an obligation undertaken towards public institutions or organisations, professional organisations having the nature of public institutions, companies established with their participation, foundations operating within them, associations or cooperatives working for the public benefit shall be punished with imprisonment from three years to seven years.
(2) Where the following acts are committed fraudulently, the performance of the obligation shall be deemed to have been rigged:
a) Delivery or acceptance of goods other than the goods whose description is specified in the tender decision or contract.
b) Delivery or acceptance of goods in a quantity less than that specified in the tender decision or contract.
c) Acceptance of the obligation as if it had been performed on time, although it was not performed within the period specified in the tender decision or contract.

Embezzlement

## Article 247

(1) A public official who embezzles property the possession of which has been transferred to them by reason of their duty or which they are obliged to protect and supervise, for themselves or another, shall be punished with imprisonment from five years to twelve years.
(2) If the offense is committed with fraudulent conduct aimed at ensuring that the embezzlement is not discovered, the penalty to be imposed shall be increased by half.
(3) If the offense of embezzlement is committed with the intention of returning the property after temporary use, the penalty to be imposed may be reduced by up to half.

Effective remorse

## Article 248

(1) If the embezzled property is returned in kind or the damage suffered is fully compensated before the investigation begins, two thirds of the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced.
(2) If the embezzled property is voluntarily returned in kind or the damage suffered is fully compensated before the prosecution begins, half of the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced. If effective remorse occurs before the judgment, one third of the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced.

Circumstance requiring lesser penalty

## Article 249

(1) Due to the insignificance of the value of the property constituting the subject of the offense of embezzlement, the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced from one third to half.

## Article 250

(1) (Amended: 2/7/2012-6352/Article 86) A public official who coerces a person into providing a benefit to himself or another, or into making a promise to that effect, by abusing the influence afforded by his duty, shall be punished with imprisonment from five years to ten years. PART FIVE
Crimes Against the Constitutional Order and the Functioning of This Order Violation of the Constitution

## Article 309

(1) Those who attempt, by using force and violence, to abolish the order envisaged by the Constitution of the Republic of Turkey or to introduce another order in place of this order or to prevent the actual implementation of this order shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment.
(2) If other crimes are committed during the commission of this crime, penalties shall also be imposed for these crimes according to the relevant provisions.
(3) In respect of the commission of the crimes defined in this article, security measures specific to legal persons shall be imposed on legal persons.

Assassination and physical assault against the President

## Article 310

(1) A person who commits an assassination against the President shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment. If this act is attempted, a penalty shall be imposed as if the crime had been completed.
(2) A person who commits other physical assaults against the President shall be sentenced to the penalty related to the relevant crime increased by half. However, the penalty to be imposed in this manner cannot be less than five years.

Crime against the legislative body

## Article 311

(1) Those who attempt, by using force and violence, to abolish the Grand National Assembly of Turkey or to prevent the Grand National Assembly of Turkey from performing its duties partially or completely shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment.
(2) If other crimes are committed during the commission of this crime, penalties shall also be imposed for these crimes according to the relevant provisions.

Crime against the Government

## Article 312

(1) A person who attempts, by using force and violence, to abolish the Government of the Republic of Turkey or to prevent it from performing its duties partially or completely shall be sentenced to aggravated life imprisonment.
(2) If other crimes are committed during the commission of this crime, penalties shall also be imposed for these crimes according to the relevant provisions.

Armed insurrection against the Government of the Republic of Turkey

## Article 313

(1) A person who incites the people to armed insurrection against the Government of the Republic of Turkey shall be sentenced to imprisonment from fifteen years to twenty years. If the insurrection is realized, the person who incited it shall be sentenced to imprisonment from twenty years to twenty-five years.
(2) A person who leads an armed insurrection against the Government of the Republic of Turkey shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment. Other persons who participate in the insurrection shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six years to ten years.

(3) If the crimes defined in the first and second paragraphs are committed by taking advantage of the facility provided by the State being in a state of war, aggravated life imprisonment shall be imposed.
(4) If other crimes are committed during the commission of the crimes defined in the first and second paragraphs, punishment shall also be imposed for these crimes according to the relevant provisions.

Armed organization

## Article 314

(1) A person who establishes or directs an armed organization for the purpose of committing the crimes contained in the fourth and fifth sections of this part shall be punished with imprisonment from ten years to fifteen years.
(2) Those who are members of the organization defined in the first paragraph shall be sentenced to imprisonment from five years to ten years.
(3) Other provisions relating to the crime of establishing an organization for the purpose of committing crimes shall be applied exactly for this crime.

Providing weapons

## Article 315

(1) A person who, knowing the purposes of these organizations, provides, transports or stores weapons to these organizations by manufacturing, purchasing or bringing them into the country for the purpose of being used in the activities of the organizations defined in the above article shall be punished with imprisonment from ten years to fifteen years.

Agreement for crime

## Article 316

(1) If two or more persons agree in a manner determined by material facts to commit any of the crimes contained in the fourth and fifth sections of this part by suitable means, imprisonment from three years to twelve years shall be imposed according to the degree of severity of the crimes.
(2) Those who withdraw from this alliance before the intended crime is committed or before an investigation is initiated due to the agreement shall not be punished.

SIXTH SECTION
Crimes Against National Defense Usurpation of military commands

## Article 317

(1) Those who, although they are not legally authorized or have not been appointed by the State, take command of a military unit or navy or warship or war air fleet or a fortress or fortified position or a military base or facility, a port or city shall be sentenced to life imprisonment.

## Article 318

(1) (Amended: 11/4/2013-6459/Article 13) Those who encourage or suggest in a manner that will cause those performing military service to desert or will dissuade those who will participate in military service from performing this service shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six months to two years.
(2) If the act is committed through press and publication, the punishment shall be increased by half.

Inciting soldiers to disobedience

## Article 319

(1) Those who direct and incite soldiers or other persons working under military administration to disobedience to the laws or to break their oaths or to violate military discipline or their duties related to military service, and those who praise or say they approve of actions contrary to laws, oaths or discipline or other duties in front of soldiers, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one year to three years.
(2) If the act is committed publicly, imprisonment from two years to five years shall be imposed.
(3) If the act is committed during wartime, the punishment shall be increased by one fold.

Recruiting soldiers for foreign service, being recruited

## Article 320

(1) A person who, without the permission of the Government, recruits soldiers from among citizens or arms citizens to work in the service of a foreigner or foreign State or in their favor within the Country shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three years to six years.
(2) If there are soldiers or those of military age among those recruited or armed, the punishment shall be increased by one third.
(3) A person who accepts the service in the first paragraph shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one year to three years.

Failure to comply with orders during wartime

## Article 321

(1) A person who knowingly acts contrary to the orders or decisions of the competent authorities and bodies of the State during wartime shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one year to six years.

Obligations during wartime

## Article 322

(1) During wartime, a person who does not fulfill partially or completely the obligations in the contract made with the State or a public institution or an organization performing public services or providing public needs to do business or provide goods for the needs of the State's armed forces or the people shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three years to ten years and judicial fine up to ten thousand days.
(2) If the partial or complete non-fulfillment of the obligations arises from negligence, up to three-fourths of the punishment may be reduced.
(3) If the partial or complete non-fulfillment of the obligation is caused by intermediaries who have a contract with the principal obligors or their representatives, the same punishments shall also be applied to them.

(4) Those persons mentioned in the above paragraphs who commit fraud in the fulfillment of obligations during wartime shall be sentenced to imprisonment from ten years to fifteen years and judicial fine up to ten thousand days.

Spreading false news during war

## Article 323

(1) A person who, during war, disseminates or transmits unfounded or exaggerated or specially motivated rumors or news in a manner that will cause the public to feel anxiety and excitement or will undermine the morale of the people or will reduce the country's resistance against the enemy, or who engages in any activity that may harm fundamental national interests, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from five years to ten years.
(2) If the act is committed:
a) By propaganda,
b) Directed at soldiers,
c) As a result of an agreement with a foreigner,
The punishment to be imposed shall be imprisonment from ten years to twenty years.
(3) If the act is committed as a result of an agreement with the enemy, life imprisonment shall be imposed.
(4) A person who, during wartime, engages in actions aimed at reducing the value of foreign currencies or affecting the value of public credit papers in a manner that will endanger the nation's resistance against the enemy shall be sentenced to imprisonment from five years to ten years and judicial fine up to three thousand days.
(5) If the act mentioned in the fourth paragraph is committed as a result of an agreement with a foreigner, the punishment shall be increased by half; if committed as a result of an agreement with the enemy, it shall be increased by one fold.

Neglect of duty related to mobilization

## Article 324

(1) A public official who neglects or delays duties related to mobilization during peacetime shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six months to three years.

Acceptance of titles and similar ranks from the enemy

## Article 325

(1) A citizen who accepts an academic degree or honorary title, decoration and other honorary rank or salary or other benefits pertaining to them from a state at war with Turkey shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one year to three years.

SEVENTH SECTION
Crimes Against State Secrets and Espionage Documents relating to the security of the State

## Article 326

(1) A person who partially or completely destroys, demolishes documents or papers relating to the security of the State or its internal or external political interests, or commits forgery on them, or uses them, even temporarily, in a place other than where they were allocated, or obtains or steals them by fraud, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from eight years to twelve years.

(2) If the above-mentioned acts are committed during war or have endangered the State's war preparations or war effectiveness or military operations, life imprisonment shall be imposed.

Obtaining information relating to the security of the State

## Article 327

(1) A person who obtains information which, by its nature, must remain secret in terms of the security of the State or its internal or external political interests shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three years to eight years.
(2) If the act is committed during wartime or endangers the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations, a sentence of life imprisonment shall be imposed.

Political or military espionage

## Article 328

(1) A person who obtains information which, by its nature, must remain secret in terms of the security of the State or its internal or external political interests, for the purpose of political or military espionage shall be sentenced to imprisonment from fifteen years to twenty years.
(2) If the act;
a)	Is committed for the benefit of a state at war with Turkey,
b)	Is committed during wartime or endangers the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations,
The perpetrator shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment.

Disclosure of information concerning the security and political interests of the State

## Article 329

(1) A person who discloses information which, by its nature, must remain secret in terms of the security of the State or its internal or external political interests shall be sentenced to imprisonment from five years to ten years.
(2)	If the act is committed during wartime or endangers the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to imprisonment from ten years to fifteen years.
(3)	If the act occurs as a result of the perpetrator's negligence, in the case specified in the first paragraph, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six months to two years, and in the event of the existence of one of the cases specified in the second paragraph, to imprisonment from three years to eight years.

Disclosure of information that must remain secret

## Article 330

(1) A person who discloses information which, by its nature, must remain secret in terms of the security of the State or its internal or external political interests, for the purpose of political or military espionage shall be sentenced to life imprisonment.
(2) If the act is committed during wartime or exposes the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations to danger, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to aggravated life imprisonment.

International espionage

## Article 331

(1) A citizen who obtains information which, by its nature, must remain secret in terms of the security or internal or external political interests of a foreign state, for the purpose of political or military espionage on behalf of another foreign state, or a foreigner who has obtained this in Turkey shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one year to four years.

Entering military prohibited zones

## Article 332

(1) Those who enter places prohibited from entry by reason of the military interests of the State, secretly or by deception, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from two years to five years.
(2) If the act is committed during wartime, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three years to eight years.

Exploiting State secrets, disloyalty in State services

## Article 333

(1) A person who uses or causes to be used for his own or another's benefit scientific discoveries or new inventions or industrial innovations which he has learned by reason of his duty and which the security of the State requires to remain secret, shall be punished with imprisonment from five years to ten years and a judicial fine of up to three thousand days.
(2)	If the act is committed for the benefit of a state at war with Turkey or endangers the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to life imprisonment.
(3)	A person assigned by the Turkish State to perform a specific task belonging to the State in a foreign country, if he does not perform this duty faithfully and damage occurs as a result of this act, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from five years to ten years.
(4)	Those who receive information that the crimes defined in this article will be committed but do not report them to the authorities in time shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six months to two years, even if the crime remains at the attempt stage.

Obtaining prohibited information

## Article 334

(1) A person who obtains information which the competent authorities have prohibited from disclosure according to laws and regulatory acts and which by its nature must remain secret shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one year to three years.
(2) If the act exposes the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations to danger, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to imprisonment from five years to ten years.

Obtaining prohibited information for the purpose of espionage

## Article 335

(1) A person who obtains information which the competent authorities have prohibited from disclosure according to laws and regulatory acts and which by its nature must remain secret, for the purpose of political or military espionage shall be sentenced to imprisonment from eight years to twelve years.
(2) If the act is committed for the benefit of a state at war with Turkey or exposes the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations to danger, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to aggravated life imprisonment.

Disclosure of prohibited information

## Article 336

(1) A person who discloses information which the competent authorities have prohibited from disclosure according to laws and regulatory acts and which by its nature must remain secret shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three years to five years.
(2)	If the act is committed during wartime or endangers the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to imprisonment from ten years to fifteen years.
(3)	If the act occurs as a result of the perpetrator's negligence, in the case specified in the first paragraph, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six months to two years, and in the case specified in the second paragraph, to imprisonment from three years to eight years.

Disclosure of prohibited information for the purpose of political or military espionage

## Article 337

(1) A person who discloses information which the competent authorities have prohibited from disclosure according to laws and regulatory acts and which by its nature must remain secret, for the purpose of political or military espionage shall be sentenced to imprisonment from ten years to fifteen years.
(2) If the act is committed during wartime or exposes the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations to danger, aggravated life imprisonment shall be imposed.

Commission of espionage acts as a result of negligence

## Article 338

(1) If the commission of the crimes defined in this chapter has been made possible or facilitated as a result of the relevant persons acting contrary to their duty of care and diligence, the perpetrator who acted negligently shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six months to three years.
(2) If the act is committed during wartime or exposes the State's war preparations or war activities or military operations to danger, the perpetrator who acted negligently shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three years to eight years.

Possession of documents related to State security

## Article 339

(1) A person who is caught with documents suitable for obtaining information which must remain secret in terms of the security of the State or its internal or external political interests, or matters which the competent authorities have prohibited from disclosure and which by their nature must remain secret, or with anything of this nature, for the possession of which no acceptable reason can be shown, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one year to five years.
(2) If the act is committed during wartime, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three years to eight years.

CHAPTER EIGHT
Crimes Against Relations with Foreign States Crime against a foreign head of state

## Article 340

(1) The penalty to be imposed on a person who commits a crime against the head of one of the foreign states shall be increased by one-eighth. If the crime requires a sentence of life imprisonment, aggravated life imprisonment shall be imposed.
(2) If the act is among the crimes whose investigation and prosecution are subject to complaint, the investigation and prosecution are subject to the complaint of the foreign state.

Insult to a foreign state flag

## Article 341

(1) A person who publicly insults an officially displayed foreign state flag or other symbols of sovereignty shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three months to one year.
(2) Investigation and prosecution for this crime are subject to the complaint of the relevant state.

Crime against foreign state representatives

## Article 342

(1) Foreign state representatives permanently or temporarily assigned in the Republic of Turkey and their diplomatic officers, or representatives of international organizations and their officers granted diplomatic privileges and immunities, shall be considered public officials with respect to crimes committed against them due to their duties; persons who commit crimes shall be sentenced according to the relevant provisions of this Code.
(2) If the crime committed is insult, investigation and prosecution shall be subject to the complaint of the victim.

Condition of reciprocity

## Article 343

(1) The application of the provisions written in this chapter is subject to the condition of reciprocity.

NINTH CHAPTER
Final Provisions Provisional Article 1 – (Added: 11/4/2013-6459/14 art.)
(1) In cases pending due to the amendment made to Article 235 of this Code by the Law that introduced this article, no decision of lack of jurisdiction may be rendered.
