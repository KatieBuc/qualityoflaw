I
GENERAL PART

## CHAPTER I

CRIMINAL LAW AND ITS APPLICATION

### Article 1

Criminal law and classification of criminal offences

Criminal law defines criminal offences, punishments and other measures taken against their perpetrators.
Criminal offences are divided into crimes and criminal contraventions. Their distinction is made in each case in the provisions of the special part of this Code.

### Article 1/a

Bases of criminal legislation
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The Criminal Code is based on the Constitution of the Republic of Albania, on the general principles of international criminal law, as well as on international agreements ratified by the Albanian state.
Criminal legislation consists of this Code and other laws that provide for criminal offences.

### Article 1/b

Tasks of criminal legislation
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The criminal legislation of the Republic of Albania has the task of protecting the independence of the state and the integrity of its territory, human dignity, human rights and freedoms, the constitutional order, property, the environment, coexistence

and understanding between Albanians and national minorities, as well as religious coexistence from criminal offences, as well as their prevention.

### Article 1/c

Principles of the Criminal Code
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001, words added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
The Criminal Code is based on the constitutional principles of the rule of law, equality before the law, justice in determining guilt and punishment, protection of the best interests of children, as well as humanism.
Application of criminal law by analogy is not permitted.

### Article 2

No punishment without law

No one may be punished criminally for an act that was not previously expressly provided for by law as a crime or criminal contravention.
No one may be punished with a type and measure of punishment not provided for by law.

### Article 3

Temporal application of criminal law

No one may be punished for an act that, according to the law at the time it was committed, did not constitute a criminal offence.
A new law that does not punish the criminal offence has retroactive force. In case the person has been punished, the execution of the punishment cannot begin and, if it has begun, it ceases.
When the law at the time the criminal offence was committed and the subsequent law are different, the law whose provisions are more favourable to the person who committed the criminal offence shall apply.

### Article 4

Ignorance of the law

Ignorance of the law that punishes the criminal offence does not constitute grounds for exemption from criminal liability, except in cases where the ignorance is objectively unavoidable.

### Article 5

Territory of the Republic of Albania

The territory of the Republic of Albania, within the meaning of criminal law, means the land space, the breadth of territorial and internal sea waters, the airspace extending over the land space and territorial and internal sea waters, as well as any other place where the sovereignty of the Albanian state extends, such as the premises of Albanian diplomatic and consular representations, ships flying the flag of the Republic of Albania, ships of the naval forces, military or civil aviation wherever they are located.

### Article 6

Application of criminal law for criminal offences committed by Albanian citizens
(Sentence added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

For criminal offences committed by Albanian citizens within the territory of the Republic of Albania, the criminal law of the Republic of Albania applies.

The criminal law of the Republic of Albania is also applicable to an Albanian citizen who commits a crime in the territory of another state, when the crime is simultaneously punishable and as long as a final decision has not been given for it by a foreign court. The condition for simultaneous punishability in the territory of the other state does not apply in cases of corruption crimes in the public or private sector, as well as the exercise of unlawful influence.
For the purposes of this article, Albanian citizens shall also be considered those persons who, in addition to Albanian citizenship, also enjoy other citizenship.

### Article 7

Application of criminal law for criminal offences committed by foreign citizens
(Letter "d" amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; letter "h" added by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003; letter "i" added by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; letter "c" amended by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; letter "j" added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; letter "e" removed and letters "h" and "i" amended by law no.
23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

A foreign citizen who commits a criminal offence in the territory of the Republic of Albania is liable under the criminal law of the Republic of Albania.
The criminal law of the Republic of Albania is also applicable to a foreign citizen who, outside the territory of the Republic of Albania, commits to the detriment of the interests of the state or of an Albanian citizen one of the following crimes:
a)	crimes against humanity;
b)	crimes against independence and constitutional order;
c)	acts with terrorist purposes;
d)	organization of prostitution, illegal trafficking of persons, children and women, illegal production and trafficking of weapons, drugs, other narcotic and psychotropic substances, nuclear substances, pornographic materials, as well as illegal trafficking of works of art and objects of historical, cultural and archaeological value;
f)	forgery of the Albanian state seal, Albanian money and securities;
g)	crimes that harm the life and health of an Albanian citizen, for which the law provides for punishment of more than five years of imprisonment or any more severe punishment;
h)	laundering of proceeds of crime or criminal activity;
i)	corruption crimes in the public or private sector, as well as the exercise of unlawful influence;
j)	criminal offences in the field of information technology.

### Article 7/a

Universal jurisdiction
(Added by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; letter "dh" added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

The criminal law of the Republic of Albania is also applicable to a foreign citizen who is located in the territory of the Republic of Albania and has not been extradited, who outside the territory of the Republic of Albania has committed one of the following acts:
a)	crimes against humanity;
b)	war crimes;
c)	genocide;
ç) acts with terrorist purposes;
d)	torture;
dh) financing of terrorism.

The criminal law of the Republic of Albania is also applicable to a foreign citizen who, outside the territory of the Republic of Albania, commits any of the criminal offences for which special laws or international agreements to which the Republic of Albania is a party determine the applicability of Albanian criminal legislation.

### Article 8

Application of criminal law for criminal offenses committed by stateless persons
(Amended by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)

For a stateless person who commits a criminal offense in the territory of the Republic of Albania and a crime outside this territory, the rules of articles 7 and 7/a of this Code apply.

### Article 9

Internationally protected persons
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

The provisions of this Code also apply to criminal offenses committed against internationally protected persons.
An internationally protected person, except when international agreements ratified by the Albanian state provide otherwise, is:
a)	the head of a state, including a member of a collegial body that performs the functions of the head of state, according to the constitution of that state, the head of government or the minister of foreign affairs, when they are in another state, as well as the family members accompanying them;
b)	any representative or official of a state or any official or agent of an international organization of an intergovernmental character, who, at the time and place of the commission of the criminal offense against him, his office, private residence or means of transport, enjoys, in accordance with international law, special protection from any attack on his person, freedom and dignity, as well as the members of his family.

### Article 9/a

Liability for foreign persons who enjoy immunity
(Renumbered by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

The issue of liability for foreign nationals who commit a criminal offense in the territory of the Republic of Albania and who enjoy immunity under international law is resolved through diplomatic channels.

### Article 10

Force of criminal decisions of foreign courts

Criminal decisions rendered by foreign courts against Albanian nationals that establish the commission of a criminal offense, when not otherwise provided by bilateral or multilateral agreements, are valid in Albania within the limits of Albanian law also for the following:
a)	for the purpose of qualifying the person who committed the criminal offense as a repeat offender;
b)	for the enforcement of decisions containing supplementary punishments;
c)	for the enforcement of security measures;
d)	for the compensation of damage or other civil effects.

### Article 11

Extradition

Extradition may be permitted only when expressly provided for in international agreements to which the Republic of Albania is a party.
Extradition is permitted when the criminal offense that is the subject of the extradition request is provided as such simultaneously by both Albanian and foreign law.
Extradition is not permitted:
a)	if the person to be extradited is an Albanian national, except in cases where the agreement provides otherwise;
b)	if the criminal offense that is the subject of the extradition request has a political or military character;
c)	when there are grounds to suspect that the person sought to be extradited will be persecuted, punished or sought for reasons of his political, religious, national, racial or ethnic beliefs.
d)	if the person sought to be extradited has been tried by a competent Albanian court for the criminal offense for which extradition is requested.

## CHAPTER II CRIMINAL LIABILITY

### Article 12

Age for criminal liability

A person who has reached the age of fourteen at the time of committing a crime has criminal liability.
A person who commits a criminal misdemeanor has liability when he has reached the age of sixteen.

### Article 13

Causal link

No one has criminal liability when there is no causal link between his action or omission and the consequences or the possibility of their occurrence.

### Article 14

Fault
No one may be punished for an action or omission provided by law as a criminal offense if the offense was not committed with fault.
A person who commits the offense intentionally or negligently is considered to have acted with fault.

### Article 15

Intent
A criminal offense is committed intentionally when the person foresees the consequences of the criminal offense and desires their occurrence or, although he foresees and does not desire them, consciously allows their occurrence.

### Article 16

Negligence

A criminal offense is committed negligently when the person, although he does not desire the consequences, foresees the possibility of their occurrence and frivolously hopes to avoid them, or does not foresee them, although according to the circumstances he should have and could have foreseen them.

### Article 17

Irresponsibility due to mental condition

A person who at the time of committing the offense suffered from a psychic or neuropsychic disorder that completely disrupted his mental equilibrium and consequently was not able to control his actions or omissions or to understand that he was committing a criminal offense has no criminal liability.
A person who at the time of committing the criminal offense suffered from a psychic or neuropsychic disorder that reduced his mental equilibrium to fully understand and control his actions or omissions is liable, but this circumstance is taken into account by the court in determining the measure and type of punishment.

### Article 18

Commission of a criminal offense in a state of intoxication

A person who has committed a criminal offense in a state of intoxication is not excluded from liability.
When intoxication was caused in circumstances of chance and has resulted in a reduction of mental equilibrium, this circumstance is taken into account for mitigation of punishment against him.
When intoxication was done with premeditation to commit the criminal offense, this circumstance is taken into account for aggravation of punishment.
The above rules also apply when the criminal offense is committed under the effect of narcotics or other stimulants.

### Article 19

Necessary defense

A person who has committed the offense while being compelled to defend the life, health, rights and interests of himself or another from an unlawful, real and immediate attack has no criminal liability, provided that the nature of the defense is proportionate to the dangerousness of the attack.
Manifest disproportion between them constitutes exceeding the limits of necessary defense.

### Article 20

Extreme necessity

A person who has committed the offense out of necessity to face a real and immediate danger that threatens him, another person or property from serious and unavoidable harm by other means has no criminal liability, provided that it was not provoked by him and the harm caused is not greater than the harm averted.

### Article 21

Exercise of a right or fulfillment of duty

A person who acts to exercise rights or fulfill duties specified by law or by an order given by a competent person has no criminal liability, except in the case when the order is manifestly unlawful.
When the criminal offense was committed as a result of an unlawful order, the person who gave the order is liable.

## CHAPTER III ATTEMPT

### Article 22

Meaning of attempt

A criminal offense is considered to have remained in attempt when, although the person undertakes direct actions to commit it, the offense is interrupted and not completed due to circumstances independent of his will.

### Article 23

Liability for attempt

A person who attempts to commit a crime is liable for it.
The court, depending on the degree of proximity of the consequence, as well as the reasons for which the crime remained in attempt, mitigates the punishment and may reduce it below the minimum provided by law or determine a milder type of punishment than that provided by law.

### Article 24

Withdrawal from commission of the criminal offense

A person who voluntarily and definitively withdraws from the commission of the criminal offense, despite the possibilities he has for its commission, has no criminal liability.
When the previous actions contain the elements of some other criminal offense, the person is liable for the offense committed.

## CHAPTER IV

COOPERATION OF PERSONS FOR THE COMMISSION OF A CRIMINAL OFFENSE

### Article 25

Meaning of cooperation

Cooperation is the commission of a criminal offense by two or more persons by agreement among them.

### Article 26

Accomplices

Accomplices in the commission of a criminal offense are: organizers, perpetrators, instigators and aiders. Organizers are those persons who organize and direct the activity for the commission of the criminal offense. Perpetrators are persons who carry out direct actions for the realization of the criminal offense.
Instigators are persons who incite other accomplices to commit the criminal offense.
Aiders are persons who, through advice, instructions, provision of means, removal of obstacles, giving promises to hide the accomplices, traces or objects resulting from the criminal offense, assist in its commission.

### Article 27

Liability of accomplices

Organizers, instigators and aiders have the same liability as perpetrators for the criminal offense committed by them.
In determining the punishment for accomplices, the court must take into account the degree of participation of each and the role played in the commission of the criminal offense.

### Article 28

Special forms of complicity
(Amended by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; repealed second paragraph of point 1, amended point 2, by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)
1.	A criminal organization is the highest form of complicity, in which three or more persons participate and which is distinguished by a special degree of organization, structuring, stability, duration, as well as by the purpose of committing one or more criminal offenses, to realize material and non-material benefits.
2.	A terrorist organization is a special form of criminal organization, composed of two or more persons, who have a stable cooperation over time, with the aim of committing acts with terrorist purposes.
3.	An armed gang is a special form of complicity which, possessing weapons, war ammunition and other necessary means, aims to commit criminal offenses provided for in chapters V, VI and VII of the special part of this Code.
4.	A structured criminal group is a special form of complicity, in which three or more persons participate, for the commission of one or more criminal offenses, to realize material and non-material benefits.
A structured criminal group for the commission of a criminal offense is not formed randomly and it is not necessary to be distinguished by stable membership, division of tasks, developed organization and structuring.
5.	The creation and participation in a criminal organization, terrorist organization, armed gang or structured criminal group are qualified as criminal offenses and are punished according to the provisions of the special part of this Code or other special criminal provisions.
6.	Members of a criminal organization, terrorist organization, armed gang or structured criminal group are liable for all criminal offenses committed by them in fulfillment of the purposes of their criminal activity.
7.	A member of a criminal organization, terrorist organization, armed gang or structured criminal group benefits from exemption from punishment or its reduction, when he provides assistance that is deemed decisive for the knowledge of their activity, other accomplices, assets owned directly or indirectly by them, as well as for investigative activities carried out against criminal organizations, terrorist organizations, armed gangs and structured criminal groups.

## CHAPTER V PUNISHMENTS

### Article 29

Principal punishments
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; added last paragraph by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003; repealed last paragraph by decision of the Constitutional Court no. 47, dated 26.7.2012)
For persons who have committed crimes, the following principal punishments are given:

1.	Life imprisonment
2.	Imprisonment
3.	Fine
For persons who have committed criminal contraventions, the following principal punishments are given:
1.	Imprisonment
2.	Fine

### Article 30

Supplementary punishments
(Amended point 2, by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003; amended point 1 by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; added point 10 by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)
For persons who have committed crimes or criminal contraventions, together with the principal punishment, one or more of the following supplementary punishments may also be given:
1.	Prohibition of the right to exercise public functions.
2.	Confiscation of the means of committing the criminal offense and the products of the criminal offense.
3.	Prohibition to drive vehicles.
4.	Removal of decorations, honorary titles.
5.	Removal of the right to exercise an activity or craft.
6.	Removal of the right to exercise managerial duties at legal persons.
7.	Prohibition to stay in one or more administrative units.
8.	Expulsion from the territory.
9.	Obligation for publication of the court decision.
10.	Loss of parental responsibility.
The court, in special cases, when the imposition of principal punishments is deemed inappropriate and when the law provides for imprisonment up to 3 years or other lighter punishments for the offense committed, may be satisfied only with the determination of the supplementary punishment.

### Article 31

Punishment of life imprisonment
(Amended first paragraph, second paragraph and title by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; repealed last paragraph by law no.
9686, dated 26.2.2007)

Punishment of life imprisonment is given by court decision for the commission of a serious crime.
Punishment of life imprisonment is not given to persons who at the time of committing the crime had not reached the age of eighteen years, as well as to women.

### Article 32

Punishment of imprisonment
(Amended first paragraph by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Punishment of imprisonment for crimes is given for a period from five days to thirty-five years. For criminal contraventions, punishment of imprisonment is given for a period from five days to two years.

### Article 33

Manner of serving the punishment of life imprisonment and imprisonment

Punishment of life imprisonment and punishment of imprisonment are served in institutions designated specifically for this purpose.

The rules on the manner of serving punishments, the rights and duties of convicts are determined by law. Minors serve the punishment of imprisonment in separate places from adults.
Women serve the punishment of imprisonment in separate institutions from men.

### Neni 34

Punishment by fine
(Third and fourth paragraphs amended by law no. 8175, dated 23.12.1996, fifth paragraph added, seventh paragraph amended, by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; fourth paragraph amended by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; paragraph 8 repealed by Constitutional Court decision no. 19, dated 1.6.2011; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; fifth paragraph amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Punishment by fine consists of payment in favor of the state of a sum of money within the limits provided by law.
Punishment by fine is given for persons who commit a crime or criminal contravention. For persons who commit a crime, the fine is given from 100 thousand to 10 million lekë.
For persons who commit criminal contraventions, the fine is given from 50 thousand to 3 million lekë.
For persons who commit crimes with motives of gaining property or securing any other kind of material benefit, the court decides according to article 36 of this Code, the confiscation of the means of committing the criminal offense and the products of the criminal offense or, in their absence, punishment by fine in the amount from 100 thousand to 5 million lekë.
The court gives punishment by fine after having investigated the person's ability to pay. The ability to pay is determined by the personal and property situation of the person, as well as by other circumstances related to them. Punishment by fine is paid within the time limit specified in the court's decision.
The court, taking into account the economic situation of the convicted person, may allow the fine to be paid in installments, specifying the installments and the time limit for their payment.
When the convicted person does not pay the fine and the fine cannot be collected through compulsory execution, the court decides the replacement of the fine with imprisonment, calculating five thousand lekë for one day of imprisonment.
When the convicted person cannot pay the fine on time through no fault of his own and if the criteria on which the fine was determined have changed after the decision was given and do not clearly justify payment of the fine, then the convicted person may request:
a)	postponement of the time limit for payment of the fine up to six months;
b)	performance of work of public interest.
If the court orders the performance of work in the public interest, the rules of article 63 of this Code apply.
If the fine is not paid even after postponement of the time limit or if the convicted person does not perform the work in the public interest, the court decides the replacement of the fine with imprisonment.
When the fine has been given for the commission of a crime, its replacement with imprisonment cannot exceed three years, while when it has been given for the commission of a criminal contravention, the replacement cannot exceed one year of imprisonment, but always without exceeding the maximum of the imprisonment penalty provided by the relevant provision. If the criminal provision does not provide for imprisonment penalty, the maximum of the imprisonment penalty is considered six months.
When the convicted person as above during the execution of imprisonment pays the fine, the court revokes the decision, making the calculations according to the criterion of paragraph 8 of this article.

### Neni 35

Removal of the right to exercise public functions
(Reformulated by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004)

Removal of the right to exercise public functions, for a time of not less than five years, is given mandatorily against a person who has committed a crime related to duty, by abusing public function or has committed a crime for which the court has imposed a sentence of imprisonment of not less than ten years.
Removal of the right to exercise public functions may be given for a time from three to five years, when the court has imposed a sentence from five to ten years of imprisonment, and from one to three years, when a sentence of up to three years of imprisonment has been imposed.

### Neni 36

Confiscation of the means of committing the criminal offense and the products of the criminal offense
(Amended by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003)

1.	Confiscation is given mandatorily by the court and concerns the taking and transfer in favor of the state:
a)	of things that have served or are designated as means for the commission of the criminal offense;
b)	of the products of the criminal offense, which includes any kind of property, as well as documents or legal instruments that certify titles or other interests in property that derives or is obtained directly or indirectly from the commission of the criminal offense;
c)	of rewards, given or promised, for the commission of the criminal offense;
ç) of any other property, the value of which corresponds to that of the products of the criminal offense;
d)	of things, the production, use, possession or disposal of which constitutes a criminal offense, even when no conviction decision has been given.
2.	If the products of the criminal offense have been transformed or converted partially or completely into other property, the latter are subject to confiscation.
3.	If the products of the criminal offense have been combined with property acquired lawfully, the latter are confiscated up to the value of the products of the criminal offense.
4.	Income or other benefits from the products of the criminal offense, from property into which the products of the criminal offense have been transformed or converted, or from property with which these products have been mixed, are also subject to confiscation, in the same measure and manner as the products of the criminal offense.

### Neni 37

Prohibition from driving vehicles

The prohibition from driving vehicles is given by the court, for a time from one to five years, against persons who have committed a criminal offense, when it is assessed that it will have a preventive effect or is consistent with the nature of the offense committed.

### Neni 38

Removal of decorations and titles

Removal of decorations and honorary titles is decided for persons who have committed a criminal offense punishable by imprisonment and it is assessed that their retention is not consistent with the nature of the criminal offense committed.
Removal of decorations and honorary titles is permanent when the person is sentenced for a crime over ten years and from one to five years, when sentenced up to ten years of imprisonment.

### Neni 39

Removal of the right to exercise a profession or an activity
(Amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Removal of the right to exercise a profession or an activity prohibits the convicted person from exercising the profession or activity for which special permission, certificate, authorization or license has been given by the competent body.
Removal of the right to exercise an activity or a profession is given for a time from one month to five years and is a consequence of any conviction for criminal offenses committed by abusing them, or when it is assessed that the further exercise of the activity or profession conflicts with the legal relationship that the specific criminal offense aims to protect.

### Neni 40

Removal of the right to exercise managerial duties
(Last paragraph added by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004)

Removal of the right to exercise managerial duties at legal persons removes the possibility for the convicted person to exercise the duties of director, administrator, entrepreneur, liquidator, as well as any other duty related to the capacity of representative of the legal person.
Removal of the right to exercise managerial duties at legal persons is a consequence of any conviction for criminal offenses and is given for a time from one month to five years, when the convicted person has abused functions or has acted in violation of the rules related to the duty.
When the sentence given by the court is not less than five years of imprisonment, this right may be removed for a time from five to ten years.

### Neni 41

Prohibition from staying in one or more administrative units

The prohibition from staying in one or more administrative units is given by the court for a time from one to five years, when it is assessed that the stay of the convicted person in these places constitutes a danger to public security.

### Neni 42

Expulsion from the territory

Expulsion from the territory of the Republic of Albania is imposed by the court against a foreign national and a stateless person who commits a crime and it is deemed that his further stay in the territory of the Republic of Albania should not continue in the future.
The decision is revoked by the court, upon the request of the convicted person, when the foreign national or stateless person acquires Albanian citizenship.

### Neni 43

Publication of the court decision

Publication of the court decision is imposed by the court when it deems that knowledge of the content of the decision is of interest to legal and natural persons.
Publication of the court decision consists of the obligation of the convicted person to publish at his own expense in one or more newspapers or radio-television stations wholly or partially the court decision, according to the order made by the court.
The date of publication and its duration are determined by the court.
Press and radio-television bodies are obliged to publish the court decision sent by the court.
Publication of the court decision is not imposed when it risks the disclosure of state secrets, violates the intimate life of persons or affects public morals.

### Neni 43/a

Loss of parental responsibility
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

Loss of parental responsibility is imposed by the court against a person who exercises parental responsibility, when he is convicted as a perpetrator or accomplice in a criminal offense against a child or as an accomplice with a child in the commission of a criminal offense.

### Neni 44

Manner of serving supplementary punishments

When the court, together with a sentence of imprisonment, also imposes one or more of the supplementary punishments mentioned in Article 30 of this Code, their execution begins together with the serving of the principal punishment.
For numbers 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of Article 30 of this Code, their execution begins after serving the sentence of imprisonment. The rights affected by the supplementary punishments cannot be exercised by the convicted person during the time of serving the sentence of imprisonment.

### Neni 45

Application of criminal law to legal persons
(Repealed by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; added by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004)

Legal persons, with the exception of state institutions, are criminally liable for criminal offenses committed in their name or for their benefit by their bodies and representatives.
Local government units are criminally liable only for acts committed during the exercise of their activity, which may be exercised through the delegation of public services.
The criminal liability of legal persons does not exclude that of natural persons who have committed or are accomplices in the commission of the same criminal offenses.
The criminal offenses and corresponding punitive measures that apply to legal persons, as well as the procedure for their imposition and execution, are regulated by special law.

### Neni 46

Medical and educational measures
(First and third paragraphs amended by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

Medical measures may be imposed by the court against irresponsible persons who have committed a criminal offense, while educational measures may be imposed against minors who are excluded from punishment or who due to age have no criminal liability.
Medical measures are:
1.	Compulsory outpatient treatment
2.	Compulsory treatment in a medical institution.
The court decision on medical and educational measures is revocable at any time when the circumstances for which it was given disappear, but in any case, the court is primarily obliged, after the passage of one year from the date of issuing the decision, to review its decision.
The rules for revoking a court decision containing medical measures are provided in the Code of Criminal Procedure. The rules for imposing or revoking medical and educational measures for minors are provided by the Juvenile Justice Code.

## KREU VI DETERMINATION OF PUNISHMENT

### Neni 47

Manner of determining punishment

The court determines the punishment by respecting the provisions of the general part of this Code and the limits of punishments provided by law for the criminal offense.
In determining the punishment against a person, it takes into account the dangerousness of the criminal offense, of its perpetrator, the degree of guilt, as well as mitigating and aggravating circumstances.

### Neni 48

Mitigating circumstances
(Two paragraphs added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The following circumstances mitigate the punishment:
a)	When the act was committed driven by motives with positive moral and social values;
b)	when the act was committed under the influence of psychological shock caused by provocation or unlawful actions of the victim or any other person;
c)	when the act was committed under the influence of unlawful actions or instructions of a superior; ç) when the person who committed the act shows deep remorse;
d)	when the person has compensated for the damage caused by the criminal offense or has actively helped to eliminate or reduce the consequences of the criminal offense;
dh) when the person appears before the competent authorities after committing the criminal offense;
e)	when the relations between the person who committed the criminal offense and the victim have been normalized.
The mitigating circumstance provided in letter "a" of the first paragraph of this article does not mitigate the punishment when the criminal offense was committed in the circumstances provided in letter "j" of Article 50 of this Code.
The mitigating circumstance provided in letter "e" of the first paragraph of this article does not mitigate the punishment of a person who has committed a criminal offense against children or a criminal offense of domestic violence.

### Neni 49

The court, regardless of the circumstances mentioned in Article 48 of this Code, may also take into consideration other circumstances as long as it considers them such as to justify mitigation of the punishment.

### Neni 50

Aggravating circumstances
(Letter "b" amended, letters "h" and "i" added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; letter "dh" amended by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; letter "j" added by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007;
letter "ç/1" and "e/1" added and letter "g" and "j" amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; letter "f/1 added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
The following circumstances aggravate the punishment:
a)	commission of the act driven by base motives;
b)	commission of the act to place before liability or to conceal the criminal liability of another or to avoid punishment for another criminal offense or to realize, or to secure for oneself or for third parties property gains, or any other kind of material benefit;
c)	commission of the criminal offense with brutality and cruelty;
ç) commission of a crime after imposition of a punishment for a crime committed previously;
ç/1) commission of the criminal offense after placing the person under electronic monitoring;

d)	commission of actions that aggravate or increase the consequences of the criminal offense;
dh) commission of the criminal offense by abusing public office or religious service;
e)	when the act was committed against children, pregnant women or persons who, for various reasons, cannot defend themselves;
e/1) commission of the criminal offense during or after the issuance of court protection orders from domestic violence;
f)	when the act was directed against representatives of other states;
f/1. When the act was directed against elected persons and public officials, because of their duty.
g)	when the act was committed by taking advantage of family relations, cohabitation, friendship, hospitality;
gj) commission of the criminal offense in cooperation;
h)	commission of the criminal offense more than once;
i)	when the act was committed using weapons, war ammunition, explosive, incendiary, poisonous and radioactive substances.
j)	when the act was committed driven by motives related to gender, race, color, ethnicity, language, gender identity, sexual orientation, political, religious or philosophical beliefs, health condition, genetic predispositions or limited ability.

### Neni 51

Punishment for minors
(Amended by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

For minors who at the time of committing the criminal offense have not reached the age of 18, the punishment cannot be more than half of the punishment provided by law for the criminal offense committed. The rules for determining the punishment for minors are provided in the Code of Criminal Justice for Minors.

### Neni 52

Exemption of minors from punishment

The court, based on the low dangerousness of the criminal offense, the concrete circumstances of its commission, the previous behavior of the minor, may exempt him from punishment.
In these cases the court may decide to send the minor to an educational institution.

### Neni 52/a

Exemption or reduction of punishment for justice collaborators and victims
(Added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013, reference amended by law no. 146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)

The person who has promised or given a reward or other benefits, according to articles 164/a, 244, 244/a, 245, 312, 319, 319/a, 319/b, and 319/c of this Code may benefit from exemption from serving the punishment or its reduction, if he makes a denunciation and provides assistance in the criminal proceedings of these offenses. In rendering the decision, the court also takes into account the time when the denunciation was made, whether or not the consequences of the offense occurred.
The person harmed by criminal offenses related to trafficking in persons may benefit from exemption from punishment for the commission of criminal offenses during the period of trafficking and to the extent that he was obliged to commit those unlawful acts or omissions.
The person who has committed one of the criminal offenses related to trafficking in narcotics, weapons or ammunition, trafficking in persons or for criminal offenses committed by criminal organizations, who cooperates and assists the criminal prosecution authorities in the fight against them or, as the case may be, in the detection of other persons who commit such crimes, cannot be punished more than half of the punishment provided for the offense committed by him. In special cases, when mitigating circumstances also concur in his favor, this person may be exempted from punishment.

### Neni 52/b

Exemption from punishment for crimes in the field of elections
(Added by law no. 146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)

The person who has committed one or more criminal offenses provided in chapter X of this Code, who cooperates and assists the criminal prosecution authorities for the investigation, as well as for the detection of other persons, collaborators in the commission of the criminal offense or perpetrators of other related offenses, is exempted from punishment for the criminal offense committed by him.

### Neni 53

Reduction of punishment below the limits provided by law
(Words added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The court, in special cases, when it considers that the offense and its perpetrator present low dangerousness and several mitigating circumstances are present, and no aggravating circumstance is present, has the right to determine a punishment below the minimum or a milder type of punishment than that provided by the relevant provision.

### Neni 53/a

Replacement of imprisonment with payment of a sum of money in favor of the state
(Added by law no. 98, dated 31.7.2014)

The court, in cases when expressly provided by the provision, when it considers that the criminal offense and its perpetrator present low social dangerousness and when it determines a punishment up to two years imprisonment, may decide to replace the imprisonment with payment of a sum of money in favor of the state, calculating one day of imprisonment as five thousand lekë.
This replacement may be decided when requested by the perpetrator of the criminal offense.
In case the sum of money determined by the court is not paid within 10 days from the moment the decision becomes final, upon request of the prosecution, the court decides to revoke the decision to replace the imprisonment.

### Neni 54

Acceptance of payment of fine

For criminal contraventions for which, in addition to the fine, imprisonment is also provided, the court, upon request of the perpetrator of the criminal contravention may accept that he pay immediately a sum of money in favor of the state budget, equal to half of the maximum fine provided for criminal contraventions in the General Part of this Code.
The request may be submitted at any stage of the trial until before the rendering of the final decision of the first instance.
When the court does not accept the request, it gives the punishment for the offense committed.
Acceptance of the request is not allowed for persons who have previously been punished even for criminal contraventions.

### Neni 55

Determination of punishments for several criminal offenses

(Second paragraph added by law no. 144/2013, dated 2.5.2013 and repealed by decision of the Constitutional Court no. 9, dated 26.2.2016; second paragraph amended by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015 and repealed by law no.
82/2016, dated 25.7.2016)

When the acts or omissions contain the elements of several criminal offenses, as well as when the person has committed several criminal offenses for which no decision has yet been rendered, the court first determines the punishment for each criminal offense and finally gives a single punishment, which consists of the most severe punishment increased.
The most severe punishment increased cannot exceed the total sum of the punishments determined separately, nor the highest limit provided for the type of punishment given.
When the court considers that the commission of multiple criminal offenses does not indicate great dangerousness of the guilty person, it may give as final punishment the most severe punishment it has determined for one of the criminal offenses.
The court in the final decision gives one or more of the supplementary punishments given separately for each particular offense.

### Neni 56

Combination of punishments

When the convicted person, before full serving of the punishment, is punished for a criminal offense committed before the rendering of the decision, the rules of the above article apply and the served part of the punishment is counted in the new punishment.
When the convicted person, after the rendering of the decision, but before full serving of the punishment, commits a new criminal offense, the court combines the new punishment with the remaining part of the previous punishment, following the rules provided by article 55 of this Code.

### Neni 57

Calculation of pre-trial detention
(Third paragraph amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The time of pre-trial detention is calculated in the punishment of imprisonment or fine, as well as in the obligation to perform work in the public interest as follows:
One day of pre-trial detention equals one and a half days of imprisonment. One day of pre-trial detention equals 5 thousand lekë fine.
One day of pre-trial detention equals eighteen hours of work in the public interest.

## KREU VII ALTERNATIVES TO IMPRISONMENT

### Neni 58

Semi-liberty
(Amended by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008)

For punishment up to one year imprisonment, the court, due to the obligations of the convicted person for work, education, professional qualification or training, essential family responsibilities or for the need for medical treatment or rehabilitation, may decide the execution of the imprisonment decision with semi-liberty.
The convicted person who serves the punishment with semi-liberty is obliged to return to prison after having fulfilled the obligations outside prison, within the time period determined by the court.
When the convicted person does not fulfill the obligations according to this article, article 62 of this Code applies.

### Article 59

Suspension of execution of imprisonment sentence and probation

(Amended by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; third paragraph added, fourth paragraph amended by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

1. Due to the low dangerousness of the person, age, health or mental conditions, way of life and needs, especially those related to family, education or work, circumstances of the commission of the criminal offense, as well as behavior after the commission of the criminal offense, the court, when imposing a prison sentence of up to five years, may order that the convicted person maintain contact with the probation service and be placed on probation, suspending the execution of the sentence, on condition that during the probation period he does not commit another criminal offense.
2. The court orders that the convicted person fulfill one or more obligations provided for in Article 60 of this Code.
3. For sentences given up to two years imprisonment, suspended by the court, the probation period is twice the duration of the imprisonment sentence given by the court.
4. The probation period, with the exception of the rule provided for in paragraph 3 of this article, is 24 months to 5 years. The probation period begins the day after the announcement of the court decision that has decided on the suspension of the imprisonment sentence. The probation period may be shortened or extended depending on the manner of execution of probation and the personal conditions of the convicted person, but without prejudice to the minimum and maximum period provided for in this paragraph.
5. If the convicted person does not maintain contact with the probation service or does not fulfill the obligations provided for in Article 60, as ordered by the court, the court decides to replace the first sentence with another sentence, extend the supervision period, within the probation period, or revoke the suspension of execution of the decision.

### Article 59/a

House arrest
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; second paragraph added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

For prison sentences up to two years or when this time is the remaining part of the sentence, according to a decision for a longer period of imprisonment, the court may decide that the sentence given be served by house arrest of the convicted person, in another private house or in a public care and health center, when the following circumstances exist:
a) For pregnant women or mothers with children, under the age of 10, who live with her.
b) For fathers, who have parental responsibility for the child, who lives with him, under the age of 10, when his mother has died or is unable to care for him.
c) For persons, in serious health conditions, who require continuous care from the health service, outside prison.
ç) For persons over 60 years of age, who are incapacitated from a health standpoint.
d) For young people, under the age of 21, with documented health, study, work or family responsibility needs.
The court, in the circumstances provided for in letters "a" and "b" of the first paragraph of this article, may not decide that the sentence given be served by house arrest, for convicted persons who have committed a crime against their spouse, cohabitant or child.
The court may allow persons sentenced to house arrest to leave their residence, to fulfill essential family needs, to engage in work, education or training activities or for rehabilitation programs, for which the probation service agrees.
In this case the court determines the measures that the probation service must take.
The court revokes house arrest and replaces it with another punishment, when the conditions provided for in the first paragraph of this article no longer exist. If the convicted person leaves the residence without authorization of the court or violates the obligations specified in the court decision, Article 62 of this Code applies.

### Article 60

Obligations of the convicted person placed on probation
(Amended by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; point 12 of the first paragraph amended, one sentence in the second paragraph amended and third paragraph added by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)
The convicted person placed on probation may be obligated by the court to fulfill one or more of the following obligations:
1. To exercise a professional activity or to receive an education or professional training.
2. Use of salary and other income or property to fulfill financial obligations.
3. To repair the civil damage caused.
4. To be prohibited from driving specified vehicles.
5. Not to exercise professional activity when the criminal offense is related to this activity.
6. Not to frequent specified places.
7. Not to frequent establishments that serve alcoholic beverages.
8. To remain in his residence at specified hours.
9. Not to associate with specified persons, mainly with convicted persons or accomplices of the criminal offense.
10. Not to possess, hold or use weapons.
11. Treatment or rehabilitation in a health institution or submission to a treatment, medical or rehabilitation program.
12. To submit to a treatment, or medical or rehabilitation program, with the aim of stopping the use of alcohol or narcotic substances.
In determining the obligations for the convicted person, the court takes into account the age of the convicted person, mental state, way of life and his needs, especially those related to family, education or work, motives for committing the criminal offense, attitude after committing the criminal offense, as well as other circumstances, which affect the establishment of obligations, according to this article and their supervision. The obligations provided for in points 11 and 12, of paragraph 1, of this article, are assigned only with the consent of the convicted person.
The period for fulfilling an obligation provided for in paragraph 1, of this article, is determined by the court within the specified probation period.

### Article 60/a

Obligation to abstain from use of alcohol or drugs
(Added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; words added in the third paragraph by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

The court, against the convicted person placed on probation, who is in conditions of chronic dependence on alcohol or narcotic substances, imposes the obligation to submit to medical treatments for abstaining from use of alcohol or drugs.
Medical treatment for abstaining from use of alcohol or drugs is done in the specialized medical institution, according to the determination made by the Ministry of Health, based on the request of the probation service.
The probation service supervises the execution of the court decision and reports immediately to the prosecutor, when the convicted person does not fulfill the obligation imposed according to the provisions of Article 60 of this Code.

### Article 61

Obligations of the convicted person during the probation period
(Amended by law no. 10 023, dated 27.12.2008)
During the probation period the convicted person is obligated:

a) to appear regularly and to continuously inform the probation service about the fulfillment of the conditions and obligations specified by the court;
b) to obtain permission from the probation service for changing residence, place of work or for frequent movements within the country.

### Article 62

Violation of conditions and obligations during the probation period
(Amended by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008)

If the convicted person, during the probation period, commits another criminal offense, the court may change the obligations it has imposed, replace the given sentence with another sentence, or revoke, wholly or partially, the decision of suspension.
If the convicted person, during the probation period, violates the conditions or obligations that have been imposed, the probation service immediately reports to the prosecutor.
For minor and first-time violations of the conditions or obligations imposed by the court, the prosecutor has the right to give a warning, which is registered in the personal file of the convicted person.
For serious or repeated violations, the prosecutor requests the court to change the imposed obligation, add other obligations, replace them with another sanction, or revoke the decision to suspend the sentence and serve the remaining part of the sentence in prison.

### Article 63

Suspension of execution of the imprisonment decision and obligation to perform work in the public interest
(Amended by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008)

The court, due to the low dangerousness of the person and the circumstances of the commission of the criminal offense, when it has imposed a sentence of up to one year imprisonment, may decide to suspend the execution of the imprisonment decision and replace it with the obligation that the convicted person perform work in the public interest.
Work in the public interest means the performance by the convicted person of work, with his consent and without remuneration, in favor of the public interest or the association specified in the court's decision, for a period from forty to two hundred and forty hours.
This obligation cannot be imposed when the convicted person refuses the suspension in the court hearing. Work in the public interest is performed within a period of six months.
In its decision, the court determines the number of hours of work and the obligation that the convicted person maintain contact with the probation service. The probation service determines the type of work to be performed, specifies the place of performance of the work and the days of the week when it will be performed, taking into account, as far as possible, the regular employment of the convicted person or his family obligations. The duration of work in the public interest does not exceed eight hours per day.
After completion of the work, the sentence is considered not to have existed.
If the convicted person does not perform the work in the public interest, does not maintain contact with the probation service or violates the conditions or other obligations imposed by the court, the prosecutor immediately informs the court. The court, in this case, decides according to Article 62 of this Code.

### Article 64

Conditional release
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008;
words added to the third paragraph by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; the third subparagraph of paragraph 1 amended and paragraph 5 added by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

A person sentenced to imprisonment may be conditionally released from serving the sentence only for special reasons, if by his conduct and work he shows that the purpose of his education has been achieved with the sentence served, and has served:
-	not less than half of the sentence given for criminal contraventions;
-	not less than two-thirds of the sentence given for crimes with sentencing measures up to five years imprisonment;
-	not less than three-quarters of the sentence given for crimes with sentencing measures over 5 years up to the maximum provided by law, with the exception of the provisions in paragraph 3 of this article.
The time benefited by amnesty or pardon is not counted in the measure of the sentence served.
Conditional release is not permitted for repeat offenders for crimes committed intentionally as well as for those convicted of committing the criminal offenses provided for in Articles 78/a, 79/a, 79/b, 79/c or the third paragraph of Article 100.
Conditional release is revoked by the court when the convicted person, for a criminal offense committed intentionally, during the conditional period, commits another criminal offense intentionally, applying the provisions for the combination of sentences.
The court orders the convicted person to maintain contact with the Probation Service during the probation period and to fulfill one or more of the obligations provided for in Article 60 of this Code. When the convicted person does not maintain contact with the Probation Service or when he does not fulfill the obligations provided for in Article 60 of this Code, ordered by the court, the latter decides to replace the first sentence with another sentence, extend the supervision period during the probation period or revoke the decision for early conditional release.

### Article 65

(First and second paragraphs amended by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; words amended in the second paragraph and third paragraph added by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

1.	Conditional release is not permitted for a person sentenced to life imprisonment.
2.	Only in exceptional cases may a person sentenced to life imprisonment be conditionally released when:
He has served not less than thirty-five years imprisonment and during the serving of the sentence has maintained exemplary conduct and it is assessed that the purpose of the sentence for his education has been achieved.
3.	From this rule, those convicted of the criminal offenses provided for in Articles 78/a, 79/a, 79/b, 79/c and the third paragraph of Article 100 are excluded.

### Article 65/a

Security period
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

The court, in rendering the decision, may also decide to set a security period, during which the application of Article 64 of this Code is not permitted, in cases when one of these circumstances exists:
a)	criminal offense, the measure of the sentence of which is over five years;
b)	the commission of the criminal offense was done with brutality and cruelty;
c)	when the offense was committed against children, pregnant women or persons who, for various reasons, cannot defend themselves;
ç) when the offense was committed by taking advantage of family or cohabitation relationships;
d)	when the commission of the criminal offense was prompted by motives related to gender, race, religion, nationality, language, political, religious or social beliefs.
The security period extends for the time period from three-quarters of the sentence given by the court to the full duration of the criminal sentence.

## CHAPTER VIII

EXTINGUISHMENT OF CRIMINAL PROSECUTION, OF SENTENCES AND THEIR NON-EXECUTION

### Article 66

(Amended by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)
Criminal prosecution cannot be conducted when from the commission of the offense until the moment of taking the person as a defendant have passed:
a)	forty years in the case of crimes that provide for a sentence of life imprisonment;
b)	twenty years for crimes that provide for a sentence of not less than ten years imprisonment or other more severe sentence;
c)	ten years for crimes that provide for a sentence from five to ten years imprisonment; ç) five years for crimes that provide for a sentence up to five years imprisonment or fine;
d)	three years for criminal contraventions that provide for a sentence up to two years imprisonment; dh) two years for criminal contraventions that provide for a sentence of fine.
The criminal offenses of Chapter II, Section I, Articles 76 - 79/c, are not subject to prescription.

### Article 67

Non-prescription of criminal prosecution

Crimes of war and those against humanity are not subject to prescription of criminal prosecution.

### Article 68

Prescription of execution of sentence

The sentence decision is not executed when from the day it became final have passed:
a)	twenty years for a decision containing a sentence of fifteen to twenty-five years imprisonment;
b)	ten years for a decision containing a sentence of five to fifteen years imprisonment;
c)	five years for decisions containing a sentence of imprisonment up to five years or other lighter sentences.

### Article 69

Rehabilitation

The following are considered not convicted:
a) those who have been sentenced to imprisonment for a period of up to six months or to any other lighter sentence and who during two years from the day of serving the sentence have not committed another criminal offense;
b) those who have been sentenced to imprisonment for a period from six months to five years and who during five years from the day of serving the sentence have not committed another criminal offense;
c) those who have been sentenced to imprisonment for a period from five to ten years and who during seven years from the day of serving the sentence have not committed another criminal offense;
ç) those who have been sentenced to imprisonment for a period from ten to twenty-five years and who during ten years from the day of serving the sentence have not committed another criminal offense.

### Article 70

Pardon

By the act of pardon the competent body exempts the person wholly or partially from serving the sentence given by a judicial decision or replaces the sentence with a lighter type of sentence.

### Article 71

Amnesty

By the act of amnesty the competent body exempts from criminal prosecution, from serving the sentence wholly or partially, or replaces the sentence with a lighter type of sentence.
Amnesty extends to those criminal offenses committed up to one day before its proclamation, except when the respective act provides otherwise.

### Article 72

Application of the provisions of the general part

The provisions of the general part of this Code apply also to other criminal offenses provided as such by special laws.

SPECIAL PART

## CHAPTER I

CRIMES AGAINST HUMANITY

### Article 73

Genocide
(The words "or with death" removed by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The implementation of a premeditated plan aimed at destroying wholly or partially a national, ethnic, racial or religious group directed against members of the group and accompanied by the following acts, such as: intentional killing of members of the group, serious physical and mental harm, placing in severe living conditions that cause physical destruction, implementation of measures aimed at preventing births, as well as forced transfer of children of one group to another group, is punished by imprisonment of not less than ten years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 74

Crimes against humanity
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Killings, forced disappearance, exterminations, enslavement, internments and deportations, as well as any kind of torture or other inhuman violence, committed according to a concrete premeditated plan, or in a systematic manner, against a group of civilian population, for political, ideological, racial, ethnic and religious motives are punished by not less than fifteen years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 74/a

Computer distribution of materials promoting genocide or crimes against humanity
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008)

Offering to the public or intentional distribution to the public, through computer systems, of materials that deny, minimize in a significant manner, approve or justify acts that constitute genocide or crime against humanity, is punished by imprisonment from three to six years.

### Article 75

War crimes

(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Acts committed by various persons in time of war, such as killing, mistreatment or deportation for slave labor, as well as for any other kind of inhuman exploitation to the detriment of the civilian population or in occupied territory, killing or mistreatment of prisoners of war, killing of hostages, destruction of private or public property, destruction of cities, communes or villages, not dictated by military necessity, is punished by deprivation of liberty of not less than fifteen years or by life imprisonment.

## CHAPTER II

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST THE PERSON CRIMES AGAINST LIFE
SECTION I
CRIMES AGAINST LIFE COMMITTED INTENTIONALLY

### Article 76

Intentional murder

Murder committed intentionally is punished by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Article 77

Intentional murder in connection with another crime
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Intentional murder that precedes, accompanies, conceals or follows another crime is punished by imprisonment of not less than twenty years.

### Article 78

Premeditated murder
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; words added to the second paragraph by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; second paragraph amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Premeditated murder is punished by imprisonment from fifteen to twenty-five years.
Murder committed for interest or revenge is punished by imprisonment of not less than twenty years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 78/a

Murder for blood feud
(Added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Intentional murder for blood feud is punished by imprisonment of not less than thirty years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 79

Murder in other aggravating circumstances
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; letter "c" amended by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; letter "c" repealed by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Intentional murder committed:
a) against a minor;
b) against a person with physical or mental disabilities, seriously ill or pregnant, when the qualities of the victim are visible or known;
c) repealed;
ç) against the informant, witness, injured party or other judicial parties;
d) more than once;
dh) against two or more persons;
e) in such a manner as to cause special suffering to the victim; ë) in a manner dangerous to the life of many persons,
is punished by imprisonment of not less than twenty years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 79/a

Murder of public officials
(added by law no. 144/2013)

Intentional murder of a deputy, judge, prosecutor, lawyer, military person or other public officials, during duty or because of it, when the qualities of the victim are visible or known, is punished by imprisonment of not less than thirty years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 79/b

Murder of employees of the State Police
(Added by law no. 144/2013)

Intentional murder of employees of the State Police, during duty or because of it, when the qualities of the victim are visible or known, is punished by imprisonment of not less than thirty years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 79/c

Murder because of family relationships
(Added by law no. 144/2013)

Intentional murder of a person who is a spouse, ex-spouse, cohabitant or ex-cohabitant, close relative or close in-law with the perpetrator of the criminal offense, is punished by imprisonment of not less than twenty years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 80

Providing conditions and material means to commit murder is punished by imprisonment up to five years.

### Article 81

Infanticide
(Words amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Murder of an infant committed intentionally by the mother, immediately after birth, is punished by imprisonment up to five
years.

### Article 82

Murder committed in a state of severe mental shock

Intentional murder committed in a state of severe mental shock of the moment caused by violence or serious insult by the victim is punished by imprisonment up to eight years.

### Article 83

Murder committed by exceeding the limits of necessary defense

Murder committed in the conditions of exceeding the limits of necessary defense is punished by imprisonment up to seven years.

### Article 83/a

Serious threat for revenge or for blood feud
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; the part providing for punishment by fine as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, repealed by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Serious threat for revenge or blood feud made to a person to be locked in the house is punished by imprisonment up to three years.

### Article 83/b

Incitement to blood feud
(Added by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal penalty, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Incitement of others to revenge or blood feud, when it does not constitute another criminal offense, is punishable by imprisonment up to three years.

### Article 84

Threat

A serious threat of murder or serious injury made to a person constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to one year.

### Article 84/a

Threat with motives of racism and xenophobia through computer systems
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008)
A serious threat of murder or serious injury made to a person through computer systems, on grounds of ethnic origin, nationality, race or religion, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to three years.
SECTION II
CRIMES AGAINST LIFE CAUSED BY NEGLIGENCE

### Article 85

Manslaughter by negligence

Killing committed by negligence is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to five years.

SECTION III
CRIMINAL OFFENSES COMMITTED INTENTIONALLY

AGAINST HEALTH

### Article 86

Torture
(Amended by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)
The intentional commission of acts by which severe physical or mental suffering is inflicted on a person by a person exercising public functions, or at his instigation or with his consent or acquiescence, for the purpose of:
a)	obtaining from him or a third party information or confessions;
b)	punishing him for an act committed or suspected to have been committed by him/or another person;
c)	intimidating or coercing him or another person;
ç) any other purpose based on any form of discrimination;
d)	any other inhuman or degrading act; is punishable by imprisonment from four to ten years.

### Article 87

Torture with serious consequences

Torture, as well as any other inhuman act, when it has resulted in mutilation, disfigurement or any permanent damage to the health of the person or death, is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Article 88

Intentional serious injury
(Second paragraph amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Injury committed intentionally that has resulted in mutilation, disfigurement or any other permanent damage to health, termination of pregnancy, or has been life-threatening at the moment of its infliction, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.
The same offense, when committed against several persons, against a person who is a spouse, ex-spouse, cohabitant or ex-cohabitant, close blood relative or close in-law of the perpetrator of the criminal offense, or when it has resulted in death, is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

### Article 88/a

Serious injury in a state of severe psychological shock
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Serious injury committed in a state of severe psychological shock of the moment, caused by violence or serious insult by the victim, is punishable by imprisonment up to five years.

### Article 88/b

Serious injury exceeding the limits of necessary defense
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Serious injury committed by exceeding the limits of necessary defense is punishable by imprisonment up to three years.

### Article 89

Intentional light injury

Injury committed intentionally that has caused temporary incapacity for work for more than 9 days constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years.

### Article 89/a

Illegal trafficking in organs
(Added by law no. 8204, dated 10.4.1997; amended by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)
Trafficking in transplants, as well as any activity related to the illegal removal and implantation of organs, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.
This offense committed by a person who abuses his position towards a child or another unprotected person is punishable by imprisonment from seven to ten years.
This offense, when committed more than once or in collaboration, is punishable by imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.
When the above offense causes the death of the victim or this offense is committed by a structured criminal group or criminal organization, it is punishable by fifteen to twenty-five years imprisonment.

### Article 89/b

Spread of infectious diseases
(Added by law no. 35/2020, dated 16.4.2020)
Intentional spread of an infectious disease with high risk to health, through the commission of acts or omissions by a person who is diagnosed as a carrier of the disease or by a person who intends to spread it, is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.
When this offense is committed by negligence, it is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years.
The same offense, when it has caused serious consequences to health or danger to the lives of people, is punishable by imprisonment from three to eight years.

### Article 90

Other intentional injuries

Beating, as well as any other act of violence, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine.
The same offense, when it has caused temporary incapacity for work up to 9 days, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to six months.
SECTION IV
CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST HEALTH COMMITTED BY NEGLIGENCE

### Article 91

Serious injury by negligence

Serious injury committed by negligence constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to one year.

### Article 92

Light injury by negligence

Light injury committed by negligence constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine.

SECTION V
CRIMINAL OFFENSES ENDANGERING LIFE AND HEALTH FROM TERMINATION OF PREGNANCY OR FAILURE TO PROVIDE ASSISTANCE

### Article 93

Termination of pregnancy without the woman's consent

Termination of pregnancy without the woman's consent, except in cases where the termination is dictated by a justified health reason, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to five years.

### Article 94

Termination of pregnancy performed in unauthorized places and by unauthorized persons

Termination of pregnancy performed not in public hospital facilities or specially authorized private clinics, or by a person who is not a doctor or beyond the permissible time for termination except in cases where this is dictated by a justified health reason, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years.
When the offense has caused endangerment of life or death, it is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to five
years.

### Article 95

Provision of means for termination of pregnancy

Provision of means that serve for termination of pregnancy to a woman with the intention that she perform the termination herself or with the help of another, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to one year.

### Article 96

Negligence in medical treatment
(Second paragraph added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001, amended by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Negligence in medical treatment of patients by a health professional, when it has endangered the person's life or seriously damaged his health, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to one year.
The same offense, when it has caused the death of the person, is punishable by imprisonment up to two years.

### Article 97

Failure to provide assistance

Failure to provide assistance, without reasonable cause, by a person who by law or by reason of duty was obliged to provide it, when as a consequence thereof serious damage to health, endangerment of life or death has been caused, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years.

### Article 98

Failure to provide assistance by the ship's captain

Failure to provide assistance by the ship's captain to people who are drowning at sea or in other waters, when such assistance could be provided without serious risk to the ship, crew and passengers, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to four years.

### Article 99

Causing suicide
(Amended wording by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Causing the suicide or attempted suicide of a person, as a result of systematic ill-treatment or other systematic conduct that seriously affects dignity, committed by a person who has them in dependency or by a person with whom they have a family or cohabitation relationship, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.
SECTION VI SEXUAL CRIMES

### Article 100

Sexual or homosexual relations with minors
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended wording in the second and third paragraphs by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
The commission of sexual or homosexual relations with a minor child who has not reached the age of fourteen years or with a minor who has not reached sexual maturity, is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
When the sexual or homosexual relation is committed in cooperation, more than once or with violence, or when serious consequences to health are caused to the injured child, it is punishable by imprisonment of not less than twenty-five years.
When the act has resulted in the death or suicide of the minor child, it is punishable by imprisonment of not less than thirty years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 101

Sexual or homosexual relations with violence with minors aged 14-18 years
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The commission of sexual or homosexual relations, with violence, with a minor child aged fourteen to eighteen years and who has reached sexual maturity, is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
When the sexual or homosexual relation, with violence, is committed in cooperation or more than once, or when serious consequences to health are caused to the injured child, it is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.
When the act has resulted in the death or suicide of the minor child, it is punishable by imprisonment of not less than twenty years.

### Article 102

Sexual relations with violence with adults
(Amended first and second paragraphs by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended first paragraph by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
The commission of sexual relations with violence, with adults or between spouses or cohabitants, without the consent of one of them, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.
When the sexual relation with violence is committed in cooperation or more than once, or when serious consequences to health are caused to the injured person, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
When the act has resulted in the death or suicide of the injured person, it is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Article 102/a

Homosexual relations with violence with an adult
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

The commission of homosexual relations with violence with an adult is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven
years.
When the homosexual relation with violence is committed in cooperation or more than once, or when serious consequences to health are caused to the
injured person, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.
When the act has resulted in the death or suicide of the injured person, it is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Article 103

Sexual or homosexual relations with persons unable to protect themselves
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)
The commission of sexual or homosexual relations, by taking advantage of the physical or mental incapacity of the injured person or, by placing them in a state of unconsciousness, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.
When the sexual or homosexual relation is committed in cooperation or more than once, or when serious consequences to health are caused to the injured person, it is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
When the act has resulted in the death or suicide of the injured person, it is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Article 104

Sexual or homosexual relations with threat of use of weapon
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)
The commission of sexual or homosexual relations, by threatening the injured person with immediate use of a weapon, is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

### Article 105

Sexual or homosexual relations by abusing duty
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The commission of sexual or homosexual relations by abusing relations of dependency or duty, is punishable by imprisonment up to three years.

### Article 106

Sexual or homosexual relations with persons in kinship or under guardianship
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The commission of sexual or homosexual relations between parent and child, brother and sister, between brothers, sisters, between other persons who are kin in direct line or with persons who are in relations of guardianship or adoption, is punishable by imprisonment up to seven years.

### Article 107

Sexual or homosexual relations in public places
(Added wording by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; added article 107/a by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The commission of sexual or homosexual relations in public places or in environments exposed to the view of people, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.

### Article 107/a

Sexual violence
The exercise of sexual violence, through the commission of acts of a sexual nature on the body of another person with objects, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.
When this act is committed in cooperation, against several persons, more than once, or against children aged fourteen to eighteen years, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
When this act is committed against a minor who has not reached the age of fourteen years or a minor who has not reached sexual maturity, regardless of whether it is committed with violence or not, it is punishable by imprisonment of not less than twenty years.
When this act has resulted in the death or suicide of the injured person, it is punishable by imprisonment of not less than twenty-five years.

### Article 108

Indecent acts
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; added paragraphs by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The commission of indecent acts with minors who have not reached the age of fourteen years, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.
The same act, when committed against a minor who has not reached the age of fourteen years, with whom the perpetrator has a family relationship, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.
The intentional inclusion as a witness, in actions of a sexual nature, of a minor who has not reached the age of fourteen years or of a minor who has not reached sexual maturity, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
A proposal made by an adult, by any means or form, to meet a minor who has not reached the age of fourteen years or a minor who has not reached sexual maturity, with the purpose of committing any of the criminal offenses provided for in this section or in section VIII of chapter II of this Code, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

### Article 108/a

Sexual harassment
(Added article 108/a by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The commission of conduct of a sexual nature, which violates the dignity of a person, by any means or form, by creating a threatening, hostile, degrading, humiliating or offensive environment, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
When this act is committed in cooperation, against several persons, more than once, or against children, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.

SECTION VII
CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST THE FREEDOM OF THE PERSON

### Article 109

Kidnapping or hostage-taking of a person
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; added by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004;
the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, repealed by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Kidnapping or hostage-taking of a person with the purpose of gaining property or any other kind of benefit, to prepare the creation of facilitating conditions for the commission of a crime, to assist in the concealment or escape of the perpetrators or collaborators in the commission of a crime, to evade punishment, to compel the fulfillment of certain demands or conditions, for political purposes or any other purpose, is punished by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.
The same act committed against a minor under the age of 14 is punished by imprisonment of not less than fifteen years.
Kidnapping or hostage-taking of a person or of a minor under the age of 14, preceded or accompanied by physical or psychological torture, when committed against several persons or more than once, is punished by imprisonment of not less than twenty years and, when death has been caused, by life imprisonment.

### Article 109/a

Kidnapping or hostage-taking of a person in mitigating circumstances
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

When the kidnapped or held hostage person is released voluntarily before the end of seven days from the kidnapping or hostage-taking, without achieving the purpose of the crime and, when no torture or other health damage has been inflicted on the person, it is punished by imprisonment from three to five years.

### Article 109/b

Coercion by means of threat or violence for the giving of property
(Added by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, repealed by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Coercion by means of threat or violence to perform or not perform a specific action, to unlawfully gain property or any other kind of benefit, for oneself or for a third party, is punished by imprisonment from two to eight years.
The same act, when committed by using or threatening to use weapons, torture, inhuman and degrading acts that have caused health damage, is punished by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
When the act has resulted in death, it is punished by life imprisonment.

### Article 109/c

Enforced disappearance
(Added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Enforced disappearance, through arrest, imprisonment, kidnapping or any other form of deprivation of liberty of a person, by public officials or by persons acting with their authorization, support or approval, accompanied by refusal to acknowledge the fact of deprivation of liberty or by concealment of the fate of the person or the place where he is located, denying him the necessary assistance and protection, according to law, constitutes a criminal offense and is punished by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.

A superior who:
a)	has knowledge that subordinates placed under his effective authority and control are committing or will commit enforced disappearances, or disregards information and data that clearly indicate this fact;
b)	exercises his responsibility and effective control over the activities with which the enforced disappearance is connected; or
c)	has not taken all necessary and reasonable measures within his competence to prevent or punish the person who gives authorization, support and approval for the enforced disappearance, or to refer the matter to the competent authorities for criminal prosecution, is punished by imprisonment from three to seven years.
When this act is committed against children, pregnant women or persons who, for various reasons, cannot defend themselves, or is accompanied by severe physical suffering, is committed in collaboration, against several persons or more than once, it is punished by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.
When this act has resulted in the death of the person, it is punished by imprisonment of not less than thirty years or by life imprisonment.
The unlawful taking of children who are the subject of an enforced disappearance, or of children whose father, mother or legal representative has been made the subject of an enforced disappearance, or of children born during the period of enforced disappearance, constitutes a criminal offense and is punished by imprisonment from five to ten years.

### Article 110

Unlawful deprivation of liberty
(Second paragraph amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Unlawful deprivation of liberty of a person constitutes a criminal contravention and is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.
When this act is accompanied by severe physical suffering, is committed in collaboration, against several persons or more than once, it is punished by imprisonment from three to seven years.

### Article 110/a

Trafficking in adults
(Added by law 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 9188, dated 12.2.2004;
the title, words in the first paragraph and the third paragraph amended and the second paragraph added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, repealed
by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The recruitment, transportation, transfer, harboring or receipt of persons by means of the threat or use of force or other forms of coercion, abduction, fraud, abuse of duty or taking advantage of social, physical or psychological condition or the giving or receiving of payments or benefits to obtain the consent of a person having control over another person, for the purpose of exploitation of prostitution of others or other forms of sexual exploitation, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, the use or transplantation of organs, as well as other forms of exploitation, both within and outside the territory of the Republic of Albania, are punished by imprisonment from eight to fifteen years.
When this act is committed against an adult female person it is punished by imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.
The organization, direction and financing of trafficking in persons are punished by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.

When this act is committed in collaboration, more than once, is accompanied by mistreatment and coercion with physical or psychological violence against the victim, to perform various actions, brings serious consequences to health, or endangers his life, it is punished by imprisonment of not less than fifteen years.
When the act has resulted in the death of the victim, it is punished by imprisonment of not less than twenty years or by life imprisonment.
When the criminal offense is committed through the exploitation of state function or public service, the imprisonment is increased by ¼ of the sentence given.

### Article 110/b

Obtaining or using services offered by trafficked persons
(Added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Obtaining or using services offered by trafficked persons, or services that are the object of exploitation from trafficking, knowing that the person is trafficked, is punished by imprisonment from two to five years.
When this act is committed against a child it is punished by imprisonment from three to seven years.

### Article 110/c

Actions that facilitate trafficking
(Added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Falsification, possession or procurement of identity document, passports, visas or other travel documents or the holding, removal, concealment, damage or destruction thereof, which have served for the trafficking of adults, but without having knowledge of this fact, constitutes a criminal offense and is punished by imprisonment from two to five years.
The same act, when committed in collaboration, more than once, or is committed by a person whose duty is to issue the identity card, passport, visa or travel document, or has enabled the trafficking of children, is punished by imprisonment from four to eight years.
The same act, when it has brought serious consequences, is punished by imprisonment of not less than five years.

### Neni 111

Hijacking of aircraft, ships and other vehicles

Taking possession and placing under control of aircraft, ships and other means of transport where people are present, by using violence or by threatening with weapons or other means, is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Neni 112

Violation of dwelling

Entering or remaining in another person's dwelling without their consent constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three months.
When the act is committed with violence or with threat to use weapons, it constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.

When this act is committed against a minor, a pregnant woman or a person unable to defend themselves, as well as when committed by a masked person or accompanied by carrying or using weapons, the punishment is increased up to one half of the given sentence.

The same act, committed with the aim of gain or to harm another person, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

Abandonment of minor children

The abandonment of a child under the age of 16 by the parent or person who is obliged to care for him/her, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to three years.
When the act has caused serious damage to the health or death of the child, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Neni 124/a

Solicitation or receipt of compensation for adoption procedures
(Added by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013, article 48)

(Added by law no. 9859, dated 21.1.2008; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; second paragraph amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Physical or psychological maltreatment of a minor by parents, sister, brother, grandfather, grandmother, legal guardian or any person who is obliged to care for him/her, is punishable by imprisonment from three months to two years.
Forcing, exploiting, inciting or using a minor to work, to secure income, to beg or to perform actions that harm his/her mental and/or physical development, or his/her education, is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.

Unlawful taking of a child, by removing him/her from the person exercising parental authority or to whom he/she has been entrusted for upbringing and education, as well as failure to hand over the child to the other parent in execution of a court decision, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to six months.

### Neni 128

Switching of children

Switching of children committed through negligence by personnel where children are kept for upbringing, treatment or in maternity homes, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years.

### Neni 128/a

Concealment or intentional switching of a child

Concealment or intentional switching of a child with another, committed by medical personnel, is punishable by imprisonment from three to eight years.

### Neni 128/b

Trafficking of minors
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001, amended by law no. 9188, dated 12.2.2004; added by law no. 9859, dated 21.1.2008; words amended in the first paragraph by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Recruitment, sale, transportation, transfer, concealment or reception of minors for the purpose of exploitation of prostitution or other forms of sexual exploitation, forced labor or services, slavery or forms similar to slavery, use or transplantation of organs, as well as other forms of exploitation, is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.
Organization, direction and financing of trafficking of minors is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.
When this act is committed in cooperation or more than once, or is accompanied by maltreatment and coercion with physical or psychological violence of the victim, to perform various actions, or brings serious consequences for health, it is punishable by imprisonment of not less than fifteen years.

When the criminal offense is committed through abuse of state function or public service, the imprisonment is increased by ¼ of the sentence given.

### Neni 129

Incitement or attraction of minors under the age of fourteen to commit crime, is punishable by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 130

When this offense is committed in cooperation, more than once or has caused serious consequences, it is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.

(Amended by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
Storage, warehousing, keeping or processing of goods, which are known to be smuggled, is punishable by a fine or by imprisonment of up to three years.
The same offence, when committed with goods, for which excise is paid or which are prohibited or restricted, is punishable by imprisonment of up to five years.
The same offence, when committed in cooperation or more than once, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.

### Neni 179/a

Non-declaration at the border of cash and valuables
(Added by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003)

The non-declaration, upon entry or exit from the territory of the Republic of Albania, of amounts in cash, of any form of bank check, precious metals or stones, as well as other valuables, beyond the value provided by law, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

### Neni 179/b

Breaking, removal, replacement of security marks of customs authorities
(Added by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
The breaking, removal of customs seals, replacement, alteration or falsification of other security marks placed by customs authorities for purposes of supervision or control or for the suspension of commercial activity in economic premises or placed on means of transport or on goods or falsification in any manner of the identification number of the means of transport, with the aim of evading customs control, is punishable by imprisonment up to three years.
The same offence, when it concerns excise or prohibited or restricted goods, is punishable by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 179/c

Removal of goods from customs zones without paying obligations
(Added by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
The removal of goods from customs zones without the permission of customs authorities and without paying the obligations that must be paid or without guaranteeing their payment, with the exception of cases of exemption from the obligation to provide guarantees, as provided by customs legislation, is punishable by imprisonment up to five years.
The same offence, when it concerns excise goods, is punishable by imprisonment up to seven years.

### Neni 179/ç

Smuggling by captain, pilot or crew
(Added by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
The transport of goods without a manifest or the absence or refusal to present the manifest and relevant documents, the loading, unloading or transport of goods, passengers and their luggage without the permission of customs authorities, staying in places where there is no customs office or stopping near the port or airport without the permission of customs authorities, unloading or transhipment of goods in violation of customs legislation, the absence on board the means of transport of goods that should be found there, based on the manifest and other customs documents, committed, as the case may be, by the captain, pilot or crew, is punishable by imprisonment up to seven years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration or more than once, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.
SECTION VI
CRIMINAL OFFENCES IN CONNECTION WITH TAXES AND DUTIES

### Neni 180

Concealment of income
(Amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; words repealed in the first paragraph by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)

The concealment or evasion of payment of tax obligations, through non-submission of documents or non-declaration of necessary data, according to legislation in force, submission of falsified documents, or false declarations or information, with the aim of material gain, for oneself or for others, through inaccurate calculation of the amount of tax, duty, or contribution, constitutes a criminal offence and is punishable by imprisonment up to three years.
When this offence is committed with the aim of concealing or evading payment of a tax obligation with a value higher than five million lekë, it is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.
When this offence is committed with the aim of concealing or evading payment of a tax obligation with a value higher than eight million lekë, it is punishable by imprisonment from four to eight years.

### Neni 180/a

(Added by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
The non-issuance of a tax coupon, certificate or tax invoice by a person who has the obligation to issue a tax coupon, certificate or tax invoice, when an administrative measure has been taken previously, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.
The exercise of an illegal or unregistered commercial activity with tax authorities, not equipped with a fiscal cash register, according to legal provisions in force, or the giving of orders in violation of the law, with the aim of non-issuance of a tax coupon, certificate or tax invoice, when an administrative measure has been taken previously, is punishable by imprisonment up to three years.

### Neni 181

Non-payment of taxes and duties

The non-payment of taxes and duties within the specified deadline, despite the possibility of paying them, by a person against whom administrative measures have been taken previously for this purpose, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.

### Neni 181/a

Non-performance of duties by tax authorities

(Added by law no. 8279, dated 15.1.1998; amended by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
The non-performance of duties related to the collection within the specified legal deadline of taxes and duties by employees of tax authorities and other official persons who are charged with these duties, when done through their fault, is punishable by imprisonment up to seven years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.

### Neni 182

Alteration of measuring devices
(Words "by fine" repealed by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)

The alteration or interference with measuring and registering devices and cash registers for issuing coupons, or the use of altered devices and cash registers, or allowing others to use irregular devices and cash registers, with the aim of evading full payment of tax, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by imprisonment up to two years.

### Neni 182/a

Destruction of marks of blocking or suspension of commercial activity
(Added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; words "by fine" repealed by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)

The intentional destruction of distinctive marks placed by the tax administration for blocking or suspension of commercial activity, or the exercise of commercial activity after notification of the decision of the tax administration for its blocking or suspension, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by imprisonment up to one year.

SECTION VII
COUNTERFEITING OF CURRENCY AND SECURITIES

### Neni 183

Counterfeiting of currency
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The counterfeiting or putting into circulation of counterfeit currency is punishable by imprisonment up to five years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration, more than once or has caused serious consequences, is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

### Neni 184

Counterfeiting of securities
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The counterfeiting or putting into circulation of counterfeit checks, bills of exchange, credit cards, traveller's checks or other securities, is punishable by imprisonment up to five years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration, more than once or has caused serious consequences, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Neni 185

Production of means for counterfeiting
(Second paragraph added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The production or possession of means for counterfeiting currency, checks, bills of exchange, credit cards, traveller's checks or other securities, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment from one to three years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration, more than once or has caused serious consequences, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.
SECTION VIII FALSIFICATION OF DOCUMENTS

Falsification of documents

The falsification or use of falsified documents is punishable by imprisonment up to three years.
When this offence is committed in collaboration or more than once or has caused serious consequences, it is punishable by imprisonment from six months to four years.
When the falsification is done by a person who has the duty to issue the document, it is punishable by imprisonment from one year to seven years.

### Neni 186/a

Computer forgery

The unauthorised input, alteration, deletion or suppression of computer data, for the creation of false data, with the intent to present and use them as authentic, regardless of whether the created data are directly readable or understandable, is punishable by imprisonment from six months to six years.
When this offence is committed by a person who has the duty to maintain and administer computer data, in collaboration, more than once or has caused serious consequences for the public interest, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Neni 187

Forgery of school documents

The forgery or use of forged school documents is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
When the forgery is committed by a person who has the duty to issue the document, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 188

Forgery of health documents

The forgery or use of forged health documents is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
When the forgery is committed by a person who has the duty to issue the document, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 189

Forgery of identity cards, passports or visas
(Amended by law no. 9188, dated 12.2.2004; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
The forgery or use of forged identity cards, passports or visas is punishable by imprisonment from six months to four years.

When this offence is committed in collaboration or more than once or has caused serious consequences, it is punishable by imprisonment from six months to five years.
When the forgery is committed by a person who has the duty to issue the identity card, passport or visa, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.

### Neni 190

Forgery of seals, stamps or forms
(Amended by law no. 9188, dated 12.2.2004; amended word by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004, article 15; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine as a principal punishment by decision of the Constitutional Court no. 47, dated 26.7.2012)
The forgery or use of forged seals, stamps or forms or the presentation of false circumstances in the latter, which are addressed to state bodies, is punishable by imprisonment from six months to four years.
When this offence is committed in collaboration or more than once or has caused serious consequences, it is punishable by imprisonment from six months to five years.
When the forgery is committed by a person who has the duty to draft them, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.

### Neni 191

Forgery of civil status documents
(Amended by law no. 9188, dated 12.2.2004; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine as a principal punishment by decision of the Constitutional Court no. 47, dated 26.7.2012,)
The forgery or use of forged civil status documents is punishable by imprisonment from three months to two years.
When this offence is committed in collaboration or more than once or has caused serious consequences, it is punishable by imprisonment from six months to four years.
When the forgery is committed by a person who has the duty to issue the document, it is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

### Neni 192

Production of means for forgery of documents

The production or possession of means to commit forgery of documents constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

### Neni 192/a

Destruction and theft of documents
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The destruction by any means of archive or library documents, as well as the destruction or theft of documents that are of particular importance, in violation of the requirements of the law, are punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
The theft of archive or library documents that are of particular importance, or their export in violation of the requirements of the law, are punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 192/b

Unauthorised computer access
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008)

Unauthorised access or access in excess of authorisation to enter a computer system or a part thereof, by breaching security measures, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
When this offence is committed in military computer systems, national security, public order, civil protection, health or any other computer system of public importance, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.
SECTION IX
CRIMINAL OFFENCES IN THE FIELD OF BANKRUPTCY

### Neni 193

Provoked bankruptcy

Deliberately bringing a legal person into a state of bankruptcy is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.

### Neni 194

Concealment of the state of bankruptcy

Entering into economic trade relations with third parties by a legal person, with the intent to conceal the state of bankruptcy, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 195

Concealment of assets after bankruptcy

The concealment of assets after the bankruptcy of a legal person, with the intent to avoid its consequences, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to seven years.

### Neni 196

Breach of obligations

The breach by a legal person of the obligations that burden it on the occasion of bankruptcy constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

SECTION X
UNAUTHORISED CONDUCT OF GAMES OF CHANCE

### Neni 197

Organisation or conduct of games of chance activities in violation of the law
(Amended by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
The organisation or conduct of games of chance activities in violation of the law is punishable by imprisonment from one to three years.
The same offence, if persons under the age of 18 participate in the game, is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.
When this offence is committed by a person charged with state functions or in public service, who has a connection with games of chance activities, the imprisonment is increased by one quarter of the sentence given.

### Neni 197/a

Predetermination of results in sports competitions
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The commission of actions or omissions for the predetermination of results in sports competitions, national and international, in violation of the principles of fair play, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by imprisonment from three months to two years.
The same offence, committed for purposes of financial gain, is punishable by imprisonment from two to seven years.

### Neni 197/b

Distortion of competition in sports competitions
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008)

The distortion of competition in sports competitions by participants, through the use of prohibited substances, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

### Neni 198

Making premises available for unauthorised games

Making premises available for the organisation or playing of lotteries, games of chance or gambling, in violation of legal provisions, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to six months.

CRIMINAL OFFENCES THAT VIOLATE THE LEGAL REGIME OF LAND AND BUILDINGS
(Amended by law no. 176/2014, dated 18.12.2014)

### Neni 199

Misuse of land

The misuse of land in violation of its designation constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to six months.

### Article 199/a

Illegal construction
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; amended by law no. 176/2014, dated 18.12.2014, a paragraph added after the first paragraph by law no. 146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)
Construction carried out without a permit, in serious violation of the permit or under the conditions of a revoked permit on land owned by oneself, is punishable by imprisonment of up to one year.
The same offence, committed for the fulfilment of shelter needs according to law, is punishable by fine or imprisonment of up to three months.
The same offence, committed on public or state land or on the land of another, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
The same offence, when it has caused serious consequences or is committed for profit purposes, is punishable by imprisonment from three to eight years.

### Article 199/b

Illegal design, supervision, implementation or approval of constructions
(Added by law no. 176/2014, dated 18.12.2014 and amended by law no. 146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)

Design, supervision, implementation or approval of construction works in violation of the law, planning documents, professional standards in force or in violation of the development or construction permit

by persons against whom administrative measures have previously been taken for these violations, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
Inciting with or without remuneration, or compelling the designer, supervisor, implementer or approver of construction works to violate the rules that guarantee the execution of constructions in accordance with the law, planning documents, professional standards in force or in violation of the development or construction permit, according to the first paragraph of this article, if it does not constitute another criminal offence, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
When serious material consequences have resulted from the criminal offence, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.
When serious consequences for the life and health of people have been caused, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

### Article 200

Occupation of land

Occupation of land constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years.

## CHAPTER IV

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST THE ENVIRONMENT

### Article 201

Pollution of air, water and land
(Amended by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Pollution of air, pollution of surface and underground waters, pollution on the surface or in depth of land, serious damage to animals or plants, through the discharge, emission or introduction of ionizing radiation or of a quantity of materials into air, land or water, beyond the limit of norms permitted by law, are punishable by imprisonment of up to three years.
The same offence, when committed in zones specially protected by law, such as zones with environmental, cultural, artistic, historical, architectural or archaeological values, or when it has caused or has the potential to cause serious damage to the ecosystem, biodiversity, flora or fauna, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
When the criminal offence provided for in the first paragraph of this article has caused or has the potential to cause light injury to one or more persons, it is punishable by imprisonment from one to seven years.
When this offence has caused or has the potential to cause serious injury to one or more persons, it is punishable by imprisonment from two to ten years.
The same offence, when it has caused or has the potential to cause the death of one or more persons, is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

### Article 201/a

Waste management
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Collection, transport, recovery or disposal of waste, including supervision of these operations, care of disposal sites, as well as actions undertaken in violation of the requirements of legislation in force for waste management, when it causes or has the potential to cause serious damage to air quality, land quality or water quality, to animals or to plants, is punishable by fine or imprisonment of up to five years.
The same offence, when it causes or has the potential to cause serious damage to human health, is punishable by imprisonment from two to ten years.

When the offence has resulted or has the potential to result in the death of a person, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
When this offence is committed through negligence, it is punishable by fine or imprisonment of up to two years.

### Article 201/b

Transport of waste
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Transport of waste entering, leaving or transiting through the territory of Albania, in a non-negligible quantity, carried out in a single transport or in several transports apparently linked to each other, in violation of the requirements of legislation in force for waste management or of permits and authorizations issued by competent authorities, is punishable by fine or imprisonment of up to five years.

### Article 201/c

Hazardous activities
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Operation of a plant where a hazardous activity is carried out or where hazardous substances or preparations are stored or used, in violation of the requirements of the relevant legislation or of permits and authorizations issued by competent authorities, which cause or have the potential to cause serious damage outside the plant to air quality, land quality or water quality, to animals or to plants, is punishable by imprisonment from two to eight years.
The same offence, when it causes or has the potential to cause serious damage to human health, is punishable by imprisonment from two to ten years.
When the offence has resulted or has the potential to result in the death of a person, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
When this offence is committed through negligence, it is punishable by fine or imprisonment of up to five years.

### Article 201/ç

Nuclear materials and hazardous radioactive substances
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Production, processing, treatment, use, storage, warehousing, transport, import, export or disposal of nuclear materials or other hazardous radioactive substances, in violation of the requirements of the relevant legislation or of permits and authorizations issued by competent authorities, when it causes or has the potential to cause serious damage to air quality, land quality or water quality, or to animals or to plants, is punishable by fine or imprisonment of up to five years.
The same offence when it causes or has the potential to cause serious damage to human health, is punishable by imprisonment from two to ten years.
When the offence has resulted or has the potential to result in the death of a person, it is punishable by imprisonment from fifteen years to twenty years.
When this offence is committed through negligence, it is punishable by fine or imprisonment of up to four years.

### Article 202

Damage to protected species of wild flora and fauna
(Amended by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)

Killing, destruction, possession, taking of specimens of protected species of wild flora and fauna or parts or by-products thereof, in violation of the requirements of legislation in force for the protection of wild fauna and for protected areas or of permits and authorizations issued by competent authorities, except in cases where such a thing has occurred over a negligible quantity of these specimens and has

a negligible impact on the conservation status of the species, is punishable by fine or imprisonment of up to seven years.

### Article 202/a

Trade in protected species of wild flora and fauna
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)

Trade in specimens of protected species of wild flora and fauna or parts or by-products thereof, in violation of the requirements of legislation in force for the protection of wild fauna and for protected areas or of permits and authorizations issued by competent authorities, except in cases where such a thing has occurred over a negligible quantity of these specimens and has a negligible impact on the conservation status of the species, is punishable by fine or imprisonment of up to three years.

### Neni 202/b

Damage to habitats in protected environmental areas
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)

Actions that violate the requirements of the legislation in force for protected areas or of permits and authorizations issued by competent authorities and that cause serious deterioration of a habitat located within a protected environmental area, shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 203

Ozone-depleting substances
(Amended by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)

The production, import, export, placing on the market or use of ozone-depleting substances, in violation of the requirements of the relevant legislation or of permits and authorizations issued by competent authorities, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to seven years.

### Neni 204

Prohibited fishing

Fishing at prohibited times, with prohibited means, in prohibited places and in prohibited ways constitutes a criminal contravention and shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three months.
Fishing carried out with means of common danger such as explosives, poisonous substances, etc., constitutes a criminal contravention and shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

### Neni 205

Illegal cutting of forests

Cutting or damaging forests without permission or at prohibited times and in prohibited places, when the act does not constitute an administrative contravention, constitutes a criminal contravention and shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.

### Neni 206

Cutting of decorative and fruit trees

Cutting of decorative trees and damaging of gardens and parks in cities constitute a criminal contravention and shall be punished by fine.

Cutting of trees in orchards, olive groves and vineyards, after permission for cutting has been previously refused by competent authorities, constitutes a criminal contravention and shall be punished by imprisonment up to three months.

### Neni 206/a

Destruction by fire of forests and forest environment
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; words in the first, second and third paragraphs amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Intentional destruction or damage, with serious material consequences, of forest fund, forest nursery, forest stock or any unit similar to them, by fire, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to eight years.
The same act, committed with the purpose of changing the category and destination of the land, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
The same act, when it has caused serious consequences for property, health or life of people or causes serious and prolonged damage to the environment or protected areas, shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Neni 206/b

Destruction by negligence with fire of forests and forest environment
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; words in the first and second paragraphs amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Destruction or damage by negligence, with serious material consequences, of forest fund, forest nursery, forest stock or any unit similar to them, by fire, shall be punished by imprisonment from two to five years.
The same act, when it has caused serious consequences for property, health or life of people or causes serious and prolonged damage to the environment or protected areas, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to eight years.

### Neni 207

Violation of quarantine of plants and animals

Violation of the rules for quarantine of plants or animals, when it has brought serious material consequences or that endanger the life and health of people, constitutes a criminal contravention and shall be punished by fine.

### Neni 207/a

Abandonment of companion animal
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
When from the abandonment of a companion animal or from failure to place a protective muzzle on a companion animal, in public environments or in places open to the public, consequences have resulted in damage to the health of a person, shall be punished by fine or imprisonment up to six months.
When from the criminal offense serious injury of a person has resulted, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.
When from the criminal offense the death of a person has resulted, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Neni 207/b

Intentional killing of companion animal
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Intentional killing of a companion animal shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to six months.

### Neni 207/c

Mistreatment of animal
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Mistreatment or torture of an animal, causing permanent health damage to it, shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three months.
When the act has resulted in the death of the animal, shall be punished by imprisonment up to six months.

### Neni 207/ç

Fights between animals
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Promotion, organization or direction of fights between animals that cause suffering or torture of animals, shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three months.
Providing animals for fights, raising or training animals with the purpose of use or sale for fights, shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to six months.
Placing bets on fights held between animals, shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to two months.
When the act has resulted in the death of the animal, shall be punished by imprisonment up to six months.

## KREU V

CRIMES AGAINST INDEPENDENCE AND CONSTITUTIONAL ORDER

SEKSIONI I
CRIMES AGAINST INDEPENDENCE AND INTEGRITY

### Neni 208

Surrender of territory
(Words "a me vdekje" removed by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Surrender wholly or partially of territory to a foreign state or power, with the purpose of undermining the independence and integrity of the country, shall be punished by imprisonment not less than fifteen years or by life imprisonment.

### Neni 209

Surrender of armed forces
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Surrender wholly or partially of armed forces or defensive materials or supply with weapons and ammunition of a foreign state or power, with the purpose of undermining the independence and integrity of the country, shall be punished by imprisonment not less than fifteen years or by life imprisonment.

### Neni 210

Agreement for surrender of territory

Agreement made with foreign powers or states for surrender partially or wholly of territory or of armed forces and defensive materials, with the purpose of undermining the integrity of the country, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to ten years.

### Neni 211

Provocation of war

Commission of actions that aim to provoke war or to place the Republic of Albania before the danger of intervention by foreign powers, shall be punished by imprisonment not less than fifteen years.

### Neni 212

Agreement for armed intervention

Agreement made with foreign powers or states to cause armed intervention against the territory of the Republic of Albania, shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

### Neni 213

Disclosure of secret information

Disclosure of secret information of military character or of any other kind to a foreign power to undermine the independence of the country, shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Neni 214

Obtaining of information

Obtaining secret information of military character or of any other kind, with the purpose of giving it to a foreign power, to undermine the independence of the country, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Neni 215

Damage to defensive objects

Destruction or damage of means, equipment, apparatus, armament, military technology or military defensive objects, with the purpose of reducing the defensive capacity of the country, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

### Neni 216

Obtaining means for destruction of military technology

Production or possession of means to destroy or damage equipment, apparatus, armament, means of military technology or military defensive objects, with the purpose of reducing the defensive capacity of the country, shall be punished by imprisonment up to ten years.

### Article 217

Receipt of rewards

The receipt or agreement to receive a reward or other material benefits, to commit in favor of foreign states or powers one of the crimes provided for in this section, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.

### Article 218

Placing oneself in the service of foreign states

The placing in the service of a foreign state or power of any Albanian citizen, with the purpose of committing acts against the independence and integrity of the Republic of Albania, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

SECTION II
CRIMES AGAINST THE CONSTITUTIONAL ORDER

### Article 219

Assassination
(The words "or with death" removed by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The commission of murders, kidnappings, torture or other acts of violence against the highest representatives of the state, with the purpose of overthrowing the constitutional order, is punishable by imprisonment of not less than fifteen years or by life imprisonment.

### Article 220

Conspiracy

The making of decisions and the creation of material conditions by a group of people to commit assassination is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

### Article 221

Insurrection
(Words removed in the second paragraph by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Participation in mass violent actions, such as placing obstacles or barricades to stop the forces of order, armed resistance or their disarmament, forcible seizure of buildings, commission of looting, gathering or placing at disposal of weapons, ammunition and people, creation of facilities in favor of insurgents, done to overthrow the constitutional order, are punishable by imprisonment from fifteen to twenty-five years.
Participation as a leader or organizer in the above actions is punishable by life imprisonment.

### Article 222

Calls for arming or unlawful taking of command

Calls for arming against the constitutional order, creation or organization of armed forces in violation of the law, unlawful taking of command of armed forces to carry out military actions, with the purpose of opposing the constitutional order, are punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.

### Article 223

Public calls for violent actions

Calls made publicly to commit acts of violence against the constitutional order are punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.

### Article 224

Creation of anti-constitutional parties and associations

The creation of or participation in parties, organizations or associations that aim at the violent overthrow of the constitutional order is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.

Acts with terrorist purposes include:
a)	acts against the person, which may cause death or serious injury;
b)	hijacking of aircraft, ships, other means of transport or fixed platforms or the unlawful exercise of control over them, by violence or through the threat of use of violence or by means of any other form of threat;
c)	commission of acts of violence against a person on board an aircraft in flight, on board a ship or on board a fixed platform, when these acts may endanger the safety of the aircraft, the vessel or the fixed platform;

e)	dissemination of information known to be false, endangering the safety of an aircraft in flight or a ship in navigation;
ë) murder or kidnapping of an internationally protected person, according to article 9 of this Code, or any other attack against him or his freedom;
f)	violent attack against the office, private residence or means of transport of an internationally protected person, according to article 9 of this Code, when this attack endangers the person or his freedom;
g)	hostage-taking or kidnapping of a person and threatening to kill, to injure or to continue holding him hostage;
gj) taking possession, possessing, using, transferring, alienating, disposing or disseminating nuclear material, intentionally and without being legally authorized, when death or serious injury is caused or may be caused to any person or serious damage to property;
h)	theft, embezzlement or obtaining through fraud of nuclear materials;
i)	demanding nuclear materials, using coercion, violence or any other form of threat;
j)	production, possession, purchase, transport or trade of explosive substances, firearms, biological, chemical or nuclear weapons, as well as scientific research for the production of weapons of mass destruction;
k)	commission of acts of violence, using any device, substance or weapon, against a person at an
international civil aviation airport, when these acts cause or may cause serious injury or death of persons;
l)	destruction or serious damage to facilities or equipment at an international civil aviation airport or to an aircraft located at this airport that is not in flight or interruption of airport services, using any device or weapon, when this act endangers or may endanger the safety of the airport;
ll) dissemination, placement, discharge or detonation of explosive substances or other deadly devices, in public places, in the offices of a state or government, public transport system or in public infrastructure, as well as distribution in the environment of hazardous substances, causing fires, floods, explosions, with the purpose of causing death or serious bodily injury or with the purpose of causing massive destruction of the aforementioned places, facilities or systems, when this destruction brings or may bring major economic losses;
m)	serious and large-scale destruction of public property, public infrastructure, transport system, information system and private property, endangering the lives of persons;
n)	causing interruption of the supply of water, electricity or any other important resource;

or any other act that aims to cause death or serious injury to civilians or any other person not taking active part in military operations in a situation of armed conflict, committed for the purposes specified in the first paragraph of this article.

Incitement to hatred and quarrels, on grounds of race, ethnicity, religion or sexual orientation, as well as the preparation, dissemination or storage, with the purpose of dissemination, of writings with such content, committed by any means or form, is punished by imprisonment from two to ten years.

### Neni 265/a

Participation in combat operations in a foreign state
(Added by law no. 98, dated 31.7.2014)

Participation in combat formations, military or paramilitary organizations in an armed conflict taking place in the territory of a foreign state or participation in any kind of training conducted by these structures, without being a citizen of the foreign state, without being a member of the armed forces of one of the parties to the conflict or official military missions of the armed forces of a state that is not a party to the conflict or official military missions of an international organization, is punished by imprisonment from three to eight years.
When this criminal offence is committed with the purpose of overthrowing the constitutional order or violating the territorial integrity of a foreign state, it is punished by imprisonment from five to ten years.

### Neni 265/b

Organization for participation in combat operations in a foreign state
(Added by law no. 98, dated 31.7.2014)

Incitement, recruitment, organization, direction, training, provision of equipment, creation or use of funds or other means for financing, material support of persons, in any form and manner, to commit the criminal offence provided for by article 265/a is punished by imprisonment from eight to fifteen years.

### Neni 265/c

Call for participation in violent combat operations in a foreign state
(Added by law no. 98, dated 31.7.2014)

Public call by any form, means and manner to commit the criminal offence provided for by article 265/a or 265/b, is punished by imprisonment up to three years.

### Neni 266

Call for national hatred
(Added word by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Endangering public peace by calling for hatred against parts of the population, by insulting or slandering them, by seeking the use of violence or arbitrary actions against them, is punished by imprisonment from two to eight years.

### Neni 267

Dissemination of false information causing panic

The dissemination of false information or notifications, by word, in writing or by any other means, with the purpose of causing a state of insecurity and panic among people, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 268

Degradation of the Republic and its symbols
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)
Intentional damage to the flag or emblem of the Republic, displayed in state institutions, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three months.
Public degradation of the flag or national anthem, during the conduct of an activity organized by state authorities, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three months.

### Neni 269

Violent obstruction of the activity of political parties

Violent obstruction of the lawful activity of parties, political organizations or associations constitutes a criminal contravention and is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

### Neni 270

Rebellion of prisoners

The use of violence by prisoners against an employee performing a state duty or in public service, committed to prevent them from exercising their duty or service or because of their activity, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.
When the violence is exercised by a group of persons or is accompanied by disturbances and disorder or by threats and intimidation, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to ten years.

### Neni 271

Misinformation of emergency teams

Deliberate misinformation of emergency teams to disrupt their readiness, committed by any means of notification or calling, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.

### Neni 272

False notification to law enforcement authorities

False notification made to law enforcement authorities about the commission of a criminal offense, to put them in a state of readiness and alarm, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.

### Neni 273

Leaving the scene of an accident

Leaving the scene of an accident by the driver of a vehicle or any motorized means, to avoid criminal, civil or administrative liability, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.

### Neni 274

Disturbance of public peace
(A paragraph added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)

Throwing stones or other objects at a citizen's dwelling, creating disturbing noises such as by firing weapons or various explosions, using vehicle sirens outside the rules, or any other kind of unpleasant behavior, in streets, squares and public places, that significantly violate peace and morals or show open disregard for the environment, constitute a criminal contravention and are punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.
Conducting economic activity that emits/generates noise in violation of the law or in excess of the norms permitted by law, which significantly violates peace in residential areas or in public environments, when an administrative measure has been taken previously, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

### Neni 274/a

Assault on athlete, coach, referee, sports intermediary
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Assault or other acts of violence against an athlete, coach, referee, sports intermediary, because of sports activity, by persons outside this activity, is punishable by imprisonment from one to three years.
The same offense, when committed in sports environments, more than once, or committed by directors or members of sports clubs, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

### Neni 274/b

Violent actions in sports activities
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Entry onto the playing field, during the time of the sports activity, by unauthorized persons, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by a fine from 50,000 to 100,000 lekë.
When this offense has resulted in the obstruction of the normal conduct of the sports activity, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three months.

Throwing solid objects onto the playing field or at the crowd, possession or use of pyrotechnic materials, fireworks, flares, during the conduct of a sports activity, are punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.

### Neni 275

Malicious use of telephone calls

Malicious telephone calls made with the purpose of disturbing the peace of another, constitute a criminal contravention and are punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.

### Neni 276

Unauthorized use of the Red Cross emblem

Unauthorized use of the emblem of the Red Cross or Red Crescent, when it has caused serious material consequences, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.
When the criminal offense has resulted in death or serious injury to the health of persons, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to ten years.

### Neni 277

Self-justice

Exercise of a right by a person that belongs to them or that they believe belongs to them and that is not recognized by another without addressing the competent state authority, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three months.

### Neni 278

Unlicensed possession and production of weapons, explosive weapons and ammunition
(First and second paragraphs added by law no. 8279, dated 15.1.1998;
first, second and third paragraphs amended, and fourth paragraph added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; added and amended by law no. 98, dated 31.7.2014;
two paragraphs added by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015; fifth and sixth paragraphs repealed by decision of the Constitutional Court no. 9, dated 26.2.2016; completely amended by law no. 82/2016, dated 25.7.2016, first paragraph amended by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Possession of weapons, explosive weapons or explosive materials in vehicles or in any other motorized means, in public environments or in environments open to the public, without the permission of the competent state authorities, is punishable by imprisonment from five to seven years.
The same offense, when committed more than once or in large quantities, is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
Possession of military ammunition, without the permission of the competent state authorities, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.
Possession of weapons at home, without the permission of the competent state authorities, is punishable by imprisonment from one to three years.
Possession of explosive weapons or explosive materials at home, without the permission of the competent state authorities, is punishable by imprisonment from one to four years.
Production, sale, purchase, offering for sale, trade and transportation of weapons and military ammunition, explosive materials, explosive weapons, without the permission of the competent state authorities, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.
The same offense provided for in the fifth paragraph of this article, when committed in large quantities, in cooperation, more than once or has caused serious consequences, is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.

Falsification or erasure, removal or illegal alteration of markings on weapons and military ammunition is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

### Neni 278/a

Trafficking of weapons and ammunition
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; words added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Import, export, transit and trade, in violation of the law, of weapons and military ammunition, or their component parts with the purpose of material gain or any other benefit, is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
The same offense, when committed in cooperation or more than once or causes serious consequences, is punishable from ten to twenty years.

### Neni 278/b

Production, possession, trade of nuclear or chemical weapons
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)

Production, possession, trade of nuclear or chemical weapons of mass destruction of the population, is punishable by imprisonment from fifteen to twenty years.

### Neni 279

Production, possession, purchase or sale without license of cold weapons
(Amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; words amended in the second paragraph by law no. 82/2016, dated 25.7.2016)

Production, possession, purchase or sale of cold weapons such as swords, bayonets, knives and other means prepared and intended specifically for assault against persons or for self-defense, without the permission of the competent authorities, are punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
Possession, purchase or sale in vehicles or in any other motorized means, in public environments or in environments open to the public, of cold weapons such as swords, bayonets, knives and other means prepared and intended specifically for assault against persons or for self-defense, without the permission of the competent authorities, are punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

### Neni 280

Illegal production and possession of hunting and sporting weapons

The production, possession, sale or purchase of hunting or sporting weapons, as well as their ammunition, without a permit from the competent state authorities, constitutes a criminal offence and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment for up to two years.

### Neni 281

Violation of rules on poisonous substances

The violation of rules established for the possession, production, use, storage, transportation and sale of poisonous substances with strong effect, constitutes a criminal offence and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment for up to two years.
When the criminal offence has resulted in death or serious injury to people's health or has caused serious material consequences, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment for up to ten years.

### Neni 282

Violation of rules on explosive and flammable substances

(Amended by law no. 98, dated 31.7.2014; amended wording by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015, amended title, amended wording in the first paragraph by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
The violation of rules established for the possession, production, use, storage, transportation and trading of explosive or flammable substances, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
When the criminal offence has resulted in death or serious injury to people's health or has caused serious material consequences, it is punishable by imprisonment from two to ten years.

### Neni 282/a

Trafficking of explosive, flammable and poisonous substances
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended title, amended wording in the first paragraph by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
The import, export, transit and trading, in violation of the law, of explosive, flammable or poisonous substances, with the aim of material gain or any other benefit, is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration or more than once or causes serious consequences, is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Neni 282/b

Training for the illegal production and use of weapons and other dangerous substances
(Added by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)

The preparation, training and giving of instructions, in any form, even anonymously or by electronic means, in violation of the law, for the production or use of explosive substances, firearms and military ammunition, other weapons and chemical, bacteriological, nuclear or any other substances, harmful and dangerous to people and property, if it does not constitute another criminal offence, is punishable by imprisonment from two to seven years.

### Neni 282/c

(Added by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
The import, export, transit, trading, production, possession, transportation or distribution of basic chemical substances or any other substances, technologies, equipment and materials, in case it is known that they are used or will be used for the production or trafficking of weapons of mass destruction and chemical and biological weapons, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration or more than once, is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
The organization, direction or financing of this activity is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Neni 283

Production and sale of narcotics
(Amended first paragraph, added second paragraph, by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The sale, offering for sale, giving or taking in any form, distribution, trading, transportation, sending, delivery, as well as possession, except in the case of personal use and in small doses, of narcotic and psychotropic substances, as well as seeds of narcotic plants, in violation of the law or in excess of their content, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.

The same offence, when committed in collaboration or more than once, is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
The organization, direction or financing of this activity, is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Neni 283/a

Trafficking of narcotics
(Added by law no. 8279, dated 15.1.1998; amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The import, export, transit and trading, in violation of the law, of narcotic or psychotropic substances, as well as seeds of narcotic plants, is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration or more than once, is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.
The organization, direction or financing of this activity is punishable by imprisonment of not less than fifteen
years.

### Neni 283/b

Creation of facilities for taking and using drugs
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The creation by persons, who by reason of duty administer narcotic or psychotropic substances, of facilities for taking and using these substances, in violation of the relevant legal provisions, is punishable from three to seven years.

### Neni 284

Cultivation of narcotic plants
(Amended first and third paragraph, added second paragraph, by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)
The cultivation of plants, which serve or are known to serve for the production and extraction of narcotic and psychotropic substances, without permission and authorization according to law, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration or more than once, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.
The organization, direction or financing of this activity, is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.

### Neni 284/a

Organization and direction of criminal organizations
(Added by law no. 8279, dated 15.1.1998)
The organization, direction and financing of criminal organizations with the aim of cultivating, producing, manufacturing or illegal trafficking of narcotics is punishable by imprisonment from 10 to 20 years.
The creation of conditions or facilities for such activities by persons with state functions, is punishable by imprisonment from 5 to 15 years.

### Neni 284/b

Assistance in the detection of crimes
(Added by law no. 8279 dated 15.1.1998; repealed by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

### Neni 284/c

Production and manufacturing of narcotic and psychotropic substances
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The production, manufacturing, extraction, refining, preparation without a license or in excess of its content of narcotic and psychotropic substances, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.

The same offence, when committed in collaboration or more than once, is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
The organization, direction or financing of this activity is punishable by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

### Neni 284/ç

Illegal production, trading and use of precursors
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The production, import, export, transit, trading and possession, in violation of the relevant legal provisions, of precursors indicated by law in the relevant tables, is punishable by imprisonment for up to five years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration or more than once, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.
The organization, direction or financing of this activity is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

### Neni 284/d

Production and trade of counterfeit or dangerous medicines and medical devices for life and health
(Added by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
The production, possession for trading purposes, sale or offering for sale, supply, distribution, export, import of medicines, active substances or auxiliary materials for their production, as well as medical products and devices, accessories, component parts or their materials that are counterfeit or dangerous for life and health, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.
When this offense is committed during the exercise of a professional activity, through the internet, in cooperation or more than once, it shall be punished by imprisonment from three to seven years.
The same offense, when it has caused serious consequences to life or health, shall be punished by imprisonment of not less than ten years.

### Neni 285

Possession, production and transportation of chemical substances
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The production, possession, transportation or distribution of basic chemical substances or other substances of any kind, equipment, materials, in case it is known that they are used or will be used for the production or illegal trafficking of narcotic or psychotropic substances, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Neni 285/a

Adaptation of premises for drug use (Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)
The adaptation or permission for adaptation of premises, dwellings, motor vehicles and any other public or private means for the gathering of persons with the purpose that they consume narcotic or psychotropic substances, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 285/b

Throwing or abandonment of syringes
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)
The throwing or abandonment of syringes, in public places or places open to the public, as well as in private environments of shared use of syringes or dangerous instruments, used for taking narcotic or psychotropic substances, constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and shall be punished by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.

### Neni 286

Incitement to drug use
(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)
The incitement of others to use narcotic and psychotropic substances or giving them for use or injecting them to others, without their knowledge and consent, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to ten years.
When the incitement or forced injection is done to children or in penal, school, sports institutions or any other social activity, it shall be punished by imprisonment of not less than fifteen years.

### Neni 286/a

Illegal use of high technology
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The production and use of telematic systems, means and equipment of high technology, in cases of criminal offenses provided for in articles 283 to 286/a of this Code or to enable or facilitate the consumption of narcotic and psychotropic substances or to transmit or disseminate advertising notices for stimulating their use, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 287

Laundering of proceeds of criminal offense or criminal activity
(Amended by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003; added letter "dh", by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; amended letter "a" of point 1, repealed letter "ç" by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
The laundering of proceeds of criminal offense or criminal activity, through:
a)	the exchange or transfer of property, with the purpose of concealing or covering its illegal origin, knowing that this property is a product of criminal offense or criminal activity;
b)	the concealment or covering of the true nature, source, location, disposition, movement, ownership or rights in relation to property, knowing that this property is a product of criminal offense or criminal activity;
c)	the acquisition of ownership, possession or use of property, knowing at the moment of taking delivery of it, that this property is a product of criminal offense or criminal activity;
ç) the performance of financial operations or fragmented transactions to avoid reporting, according to the legislation for the prevention of money laundering;
d)	the investment in economic or financial activity of money or goods, knowing that they are products of criminal offense or criminal activity;
dh) the counseling, assistance, incitement or public call for the commission of each of the acts specified above;
shall be punished by imprisonment from five to ten years.
When this offense is committed during the exercise of a professional activity, in cooperation or more than once, it shall be punished by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.
The same offense, when it has caused serious consequences, shall be punished by not less than fifteen years of imprisonment. The provisions of this article shall also apply when:
a)	the criminal offense, the proceeds of which are laundered, was committed by a person who cannot be taken as a defendant or who cannot be punished;

b)	the criminal prosecution for the offense, the proceeds of which are laundered, has been prescribed or amnestied;
c)	the person who commits the laundering of proceeds is the same as the person who committed the offense, from which the proceeds resulted;
ç) for the criminal offense, from which the proceeds came, no criminal case has ever been initiated or no sentence has been given by a final criminal decision;
d)	the criminal offense, the proceeds of which are laundered, was committed by a person, regardless of his citizenship, outside the territory of the Republic of Albania and is simultaneously punishable, both in the foreign state and in the Republic of Albania.
The knowledge and purpose, according to the first paragraph of this article, are derived from the circumstances of objective fact.

### Neni 287/a

Opening of anonymous accounts
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The opening of deposits or bank accounts, anonymous or with fictitious names, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.

### Neni 287/b

Appropriation of money or goods resulting from criminal offense or criminal activity
(Added by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Whoever buys, receives, conceals or, in another manner, appropriates for himself or a third party, or assists in the purchase, receipt, concealment or use of money or other goods, knowing that another person has obtained this money or goods, as a result of the commission of a criminal offense or criminal activity, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to three years.
The first paragraph of this article shall apply regardless of the legal prohibition to hold criminally liable the person who committed the criminal offense, from which the appropriation of money or other goods results.

### Neni 288

Import, production, sale and storage of food dangerous to the health or life of people
(Amended by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
The import, production, storage, sale or placing into circulation in any other manner of food or the introduction into the production of food articles of chemicals, materials or additives, that endanger the health or life of people, in violation of the requirements of special legislation, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.
When from this offense has resulted death or serious damage to the health of a person, it shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years.
When from the offense has been caused death or serious damage to the health of several persons, it shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to twenty-five years.

### Article 288/a

Illegal production of industrial and food articles and goods
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The illegal production of industrial and food articles and goods constitutes a criminal offence and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration, more than once or has caused serious consequences, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Article 288/b

Trading and release for consumption of combustible materials in violation of legal quality standards
(Added by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
Falsification, trading or release for consumption of falsified petroleum by-products, which are used as combustible materials, as provided for in the legislation in force for the processing, transportation and trading of petroleum and its by-products, is punishable by imprisonment up to five years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration, more than once or has caused serious material consequences for health or has damaged the ecosystem, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Article 288/c

Fraud with food products
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)

Importation, production, storage, offering for sale, falsification, sale or placing into circulation in any other manner of foodstuffs, materials or additives in the production or processing of food articles, in which data are indicated that do not correspond to the content, type, categorization, origin, quantity or quality of the product or products without the above data, as provided for in the legislation in force, or presentation of false data in the labelling of food products, which endanger the health or life of people, is punishable by imprisonment up to three years.
The same offence, when committed in collaboration, more than once or has caused serious consequences for human health, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Article 288/ç

Tampering with security seals of state bodies in the field of food safety
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)
Deliberate tampering with security seals, placed by state bodies in the field of food safety for purposes of supervision, control or suspension of commercial activity in economic premises, on means of transport or on goods, with the aim of moving food goods or resuming activity, is punishable by imprisonment up to three years.

### Article 289

Violation of occupational safety rules

Causing death or serious injury to the health of a person resulting from non-compliance with rules relating to work, production, service, defined in law, in acts of the Council of Ministers or in relevant regulations on technical safety, technical discipline, occupational safety, hygiene and fire safety by persons charged with compliance with the rules and taking measures to implement them, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to ten years.
When the criminal offence has caused death or serious injury to the health of several persons, it is punishable by imprisonment of not less than five years.

### Article 290

Violation of road traffic rules
(Amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Violation of road traffic rules, when it has caused slight injury to several persons, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.
Violation of road traffic rules, when it has caused serious injury to one person, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
Violation of road traffic rules, when it has caused the death of one person, is punishable by imprisonment from two to ten years.
When the criminal offence has caused death or serious injury to several persons, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to twenty years.

### Article 291

Driving vehicles irregularly
(Amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013; amended by law no. 98, dated 31.7.2014)

Driving vehicles or other motorized means in a drunken state, under the influence of narcotic substances or without the relevant certificate of competence is punishable by imprisonment from ten days to three years.

### Article 292

Violation of work discipline in transport

Violation of work discipline in rail, water or air transport by employees of this transport, which has caused death or serious injury to the health of a person, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to ten years.
When the criminal offence has caused death or serious injury to the health of several persons, it is punishable by imprisonment of not less than five years.

### Article 293

Obstruction of traffic of means of transport
(Added by law no. 44/2019, dated 18.7.2019)

Placing obstacles or preventing, by any means and manner, the traffic of means of automobile, rail, water and air transport, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.

### Article 293/a

Illegal interception of computer data
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008)

Illegal interception by technical means of non-public transmissions, of computer data from/or within a computer system, including electromagnetic emissions from a computer system, which carries such computer data, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.
When this offence is committed from/or within military computer systems, of national security, of public order, of civil protection or in any other computer system, of public importance, it is punishable by imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.

### Article 293/b

Interference with computer data
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; third paragraph added by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

Damaging, distorting, altering, deleting or unauthorized suppression of computer data is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.
When this offence is committed on military computer data, of national security, of public order, of civil protection, of health or on any other computer data, of public importance, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.
In cases when the actions provided for in the first paragraph have been committed by a minor, the provisions of the Code of Justice for Minors shall be applied to him.

### Article 293/c

Interference with computer systems
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008; third paragraph added by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

Creating serious and unauthorized obstacles to impair the functioning of a computer system, through the input, damaging, distorting, altering, deleting or suppression of data, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.
When this offence is committed on military computer systems, of national security, of public order, of civil protection, of health or on any other computer system, of public importance, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
In cases when the actions provided for in the first paragraph have been committed by a minor, the provisions of the Code of Justice for Minors shall be applied to him.

### Article 293/ç

Misuse of devices
(Added by law no. 10 023, dated 27.11.2008)

Production, possession, sale, provision for use, distribution or any other action, for making available a device, which also includes a computer program, a computer password, an access code or such similar data, which have been created or adapted for access to a computer system or a part thereof, with the aim of committing criminal offences, provided for in articles 192/b, 293/a, 293/b and 293/c of this Code, is punishable by imprisonment from six months to five years.

### Neni 293/d

Unauthorized sale of SIM cards
(Added by law no. 98, dated 31.7.2014)

Violation of the rules established for the distribution, sale and provision of SIM products/cards constitutes a criminal contravention and is punished by imprisonment from thirty days to six months.
SECTION IV
CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST STATE SECRETS AND STATE BORDERS

### Neni 294

Disclosure of state secrets by a person entrusted with them

The disclosure, dissemination and communication of facts, figures, contents of documents or materials which, according to the law publicly known, constitute state secrets, by a person entrusted with them or who has been able to learn of them by reason of duty, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.
The same offense, committed publicly, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to ten years.

### Neni 295

Disclosure of state secrets by citizens

The disclosure, dissemination and communication of facts, figures, contents of documents or materials which, according to the law publicly known, constitute state secrets, by anyone who has been able to learn of them, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
The same offense, committed publicly, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 295/a

Disclosure of secret acts or data
(Added by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)
The disclosure to third parties of data or assistance in disclosing data which the law defines as secret, by a public official or a person charged with a public service, in violation of the proper performance of duty or by abusing one's qualities, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.
The disclosure to third parties of data which constitute industrial or professional trade secrets, by public persons who have the duty to preserve them, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
The disclosure of secret acts or data containing secret acts by the prosecutor or judicial police officer, as well as failure to comply with the obligations set forth in article 103 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, is punished by imprisonment from one to five years.
The disclosure of secret acts or data contained in secret acts by other persons who have knowledge of data about criminal proceedings and who have been warned by the prosecutor or judicial police officer not to disclose them, is punished by imprisonment up to three years.
The disclosure of secret data relating to the identity, cooperation process, protection or location of witnesses and justice collaborators who benefit from special protection, according to the laws in force, is punished by imprisonment from two to six years.
The disclosure of secrets that has resulted in death, serious injury or has seriously endangered the life or health of witnesses or justice collaborators, their family members or police officers charged with their protection, is punished by imprisonment from three to eight years.

### Neni 295/b

Illegal exploitation of commercial data
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

Trading in goods or offering a service through the use of information or data that constitutes a trade secret or privileged information by persons who have or should have this information or data, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to four years.

### Neni 296

Loss of secret documents

The loss of documents or other materials which, according to the law publicly known, constitute state secrets, by the person entrusted with them for safekeeping or use, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.

### Neni 297

Illegal crossing of the state border
(Added by law no. 8279, dated 15.1.1998; amended by law no. 9188, dated 12.2.2004)

Illegal crossing of state borders constitutes a criminal contravention and is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

### Neni 298

Assistance for illegal border crossing
(Added a paragraph by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 9188, dated 12.2.2004; amended the title and first paragraph by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine as a principal punishment alongside imprisonment by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Sheltering, accompanying, making available or using vessels, aircraft or other means of transport or any other assistance, with the purpose of illegally crossing the border of the Republic of Albania or for the illegal entry of a person into another state, without being a citizen thereof or without having a residence permit in that state, is punished by imprisonment from one to four years.
When assistance is given for profit purposes, it is punished by imprisonment from three to seven years.
When this offense is committed in cooperation or more than once, or has caused serious consequences, it is punished by imprisonment from five to ten years.
When the offense has resulted in the death of the victim, it is punished by imprisonment of not less than fifteen years or by life imprisonment.
When the criminal offense is committed through exploitation of state function or public service, the imprisonment is increased by ¼ of the sentence given.

### Neni 299

Violation of rules on flights

Violation of rules on international flights such as entry into or exit from the territory of the Republic of Albania without flight permission, failure to comply with flight routes, landing sites, air corridors or designated flight altitude, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

## KREU IX

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST JUSTICE

### Neni 300

Failure to report a crime

Failure to report to criminal prosecution authorities, to court, to public order authorities, to authorities of power or administration, of a crime that is being committed or has been committed, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
Exempted from the obligation to report are ascendants and descendants, brothers and sisters, spouse, adopter and adoptees, as well as persons who are obliged to keep a secret known by reason of duty or profession.

### Neni 301

Actions that hinder the discovery of truth

Carrying out actions to alter the place where the criminal offense was committed by destroying, altering or erasing its traces or by moving, hiding, destroying, stealing, falsifying an object or document with the purpose of making difficult and hindering the discovery of the criminal offense and its perpetrator, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.

### Neni 302

Support of the perpetrator of a crime
(Added second paragraph by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; added second paragraph by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)

Supplying the perpetrator of a crime with food, with other means of subsistence or securing a dwelling, residence or in any other way with the purpose of helping him escape search, capture or arrest, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.
The same offense when committed in connection with the criminal offenses provided for in articles 73, 74, 75, 79, 219, 220, 221, 230, 230/a, 230/b, 231, 232, 232/a, 234/a, 234/b, 284/a, 333 and 333/a of this Code, is punished by imprisonment from two to seven years.
Exempted from criminal liability are ascendants and descendants, brothers and sisters, spouse, adopters and adoptees.

### Neni 303

Concealment or destruction of a corpse

The concealment or destruction of the corpse of a person, victim of murder or other violent offenses, done with the purpose of helping the perpetrator of the crime escape search, capture and arrest, is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 304

Detyrimi për të kallëzuar provën

Failure to appear immediately to report or testify before the prosecution authorities, the court or law enforcement authorities, regarding known evidence that proves the innocence of another person who is accused or has been convicted of a criminal offense, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.
Exempted from the obligation to report are the perpetrator of the criminal offense, as well as persons who become aware of the evidence due to their duty or profession and are obligated not to report or testify about it.

### Neni 305

Kallëzimi i rremë

Reporting the commission of a crime that is known not to have been committed or the commission of a crime by a person who is known not to have committed it, as well as the creation of false evidence with the intent to initiate criminal prosecution, are punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 305/a

Deklaratat e rreme përpara prokurorit
(Shtuar me ligjin nr. 9686, datë 26.2.2007)

Anyone who, during investigations or criminal proceedings, when asked by a prosecutor to provide appropriate information related to the investigation, provides information, orally or in writing, that he knows to be, wholly or partially, false or conceals facts or evidence, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.
The provisions of this article are not applicable in case the act is committed at any stage of the criminal proceedings by a person suspected or accused of the criminal offense or by someone who should have been exempted from the request for information or to testify, for any lawful reason, or who was not warned of the right not to testify or to answer questions.

### Neni 305/b

Deklaratat e rreme përpara oficerit të policisë gjyqësore
(Shtuar me ligjin nr. 9686, datë 26.2.2007)

Anyone who, during an investigation, when asked by a judicial police officer to provide appropriate information, provides information, orally or in writing, that he knows to be, wholly or partially, false or conceals facts or evidence, constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to six months.
The provisions of this article are not applicable in case the act is committed at any stage of the criminal proceedings by a person suspected or accused of the criminal offense or by someone who should have been exempted from the request for information or to testify, for any lawful reason, or who was not warned of the right not to testify or to answer questions.

### Neni 306

Dëshmia e rreme
(Ndryshuar paragrafi i parë me ligjin nr. 8733, datë 24.1.2001; shtuar paragrafi i tretë, me ligjin nr. 9275, datë 16.9.2004; ndryshuar fjalë në paragrafin e tretë me ligjin nr. 9686, datë 26.2.2007)

False testimony given before the court constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.
When false testimony is given for the purpose of gain or any other interest given or promised, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
The same offense when committed in connection with the criminal offenses provided for in articles 234/a, 234/b, 284/a 333 and 333/a of this Code, is punishable by imprisonment from two to six years.

### Neni 307

Refuzimi për të dëshmuar
(Ndryshuar paragrafi i dytë, shtuar paragrafi i fundit, me ligjin nr. 9686, datë 26.2.2007)

Refusal to answer questions regarding knowledge of the commission of a criminal offense or of its perpetrator, constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.
When the refusal to testify is done for the purpose of gain or any other interest given or promised, it is punishable by imprisonment from one to four years.
The provisions of this article are not applicable in case the act is committed at any stage of the criminal proceedings by a person suspected or accused of the criminal offense or by someone who should have been exempted from the request for information or to testify, for any lawful reason, or who was not warned of the right not to testify or to answer questions.

### Neni 308

Përkthimi i rremë

Intentional distortion of the content of a document or writing given for translation by the criminal prosecution authorities or the court or false translation before them, constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.
When false translation is done for the purpose of gain or any other interest given or promised, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.

### Neni 309

Ekspertimi i rremë

Intentionally providing false results in expert reports made in writing or orally before the criminal prosecution authorities or the court, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.
When false expert examination is done for the purpose of gain or any other interest given or promised, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 310

Mosparaqitja e dëshmitarit, ekspertit apo përkthyesit

Failure to appear of the witness, expert or translator, without reasonable cause or their refusal to perform the duties assigned by the criminal prosecution authority or the court, constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to six months.

### Neni 311

Kanosja për të mos kallëzuar
(Ndryshuar me ligjin nr. 9686, datë 26.2.2007; ndryshuar me ligjin nr. 23/2012, datë 1.3.2012)

Threatening the victim of a criminal offense, with the intent that he not report, complain or withdraw the report or complaint made, is punishable by imprisonment from one to four years.

### Neni 312

Korrupsioni aktiv i dëshmitarit, i ekspertit ose përkthyesit
(Ndryshuar me ligjin nr. 8733, datë 24.1.2001; nr. 9275, datë 16.9.2004; shfuqizuar pjesa që parashikon edhe dënimin me gjobë, si dënim kryesor, krahas dënimit me burgim, me ligjin nr. 144, datë 2.5.2013)

The promise, proposal or giving, directly or indirectly, of any irregular benefit, for oneself or for third parties, to the witness, expert or translator, to secure false declarations or testimony, expert examination or translation or to refuse the performance of their duties before the criminal prosecution authorities and the court, are punishable by imprisonment up to four years.

### Neni 312/a

Kanosja për deklarime a dëshmi, ekspertim ose përkthim të rremë
(Shtuar me ligjin nr. 9275, datë 16.9.2004; ndryshuar me ligjin nr. 9686, datë 26.2.2007)

Threatening or other acts of violence, committed against a person to secure false declarations or testimony, expert examination or translation or to refuse the performance of their duties before the criminal prosecution authorities and the court is punishable by imprisonment from one to four years.

### Neni 313

Fillimi i paligjshëm i ndjekjes penale

Unlawful initiation of criminal prosecution by the prosecutor against a person who is known to be innocent is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 313/a

Zhdukja ose humbja e fashikullit
(Shtuar me ligjin nr. 8733, datë 24.1.2001)

Disappearance or loss by any means of the investigation or trial file, as well as removal from them of documents, writings or other data that are attached to them, when it has caused serious consequences to the detriment of the interests of citizens or the state, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to five years.

### Neni 313/b

Prohibition of providing and disclosing data in violation of the law
(Added by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; amended point 1, added points 2 and 3, and renumbered paragraphs, by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)
1.	The provision or publication in any form, in violation of the law, of data of a classified and confidential nature that endangers the life, physical integrity or freedom of protected persons, according to the legislation in force for the protection of witnesses and collaborators of justice, with the purpose of revealing these persons, is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.
2.	When the commission of this criminal offense has resulted in serious consequences for their health, it is punishable by imprisonment from 6 months to three years.
3.	When this offense is committed by one of the persons who have the responsibility to preserve the classified and confidential nature of the data, it is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years and, when the commission of this offense has resulted in serious consequences for their health, by imprisonment from two to five years.
4.	When the offense has resulted in death, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Neni 314

Use of violence during investigations

The use of violence by the person charged with conducting investigations to force a citizen to make a statement, to testify or to admit his or another's guilt, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

Resisting and assaulting a judge

Violent resistance, assaults and other acts of violence committed against a judge or members of the adjudicating body, prosecutor, lawyer, experts, any arbitrator appointed for a case, to prevent them from performing their duty or because of it, are punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to seven years.

### Neni 317

Threatening a judge

Threats made to a judge or members of the adjudicating body, prosecutor, lawyer or any arbitrator appointed for a case because of their activity, are punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three years.

### Neni 318

Insulting a judge

Insulting a judge or members of the adjudicating body, prosecutor, lawyer or member of arbitration, because of their activity in a case, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to three months.

The promise, proposal or giving, directly or indirectly, of any undue advantage, for oneself or for other persons, to a judge, prosecutor or any other employee of the justice bodies, to perform or not perform an act related to his duty or function, is punishable by imprisonment from one year to four years.

### Neni 319/a

Active corruption of a judge or official of international courts
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

### Neni 319/b

Active corruption of domestic and foreign arbitrators
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
The promise, proposal or giving, directly or indirectly, of any undue advantage, for oneself or for other persons, to a domestic or foreign arbitrator, to perform or not perform an act related to his duty or function, is punishable by imprisonment from one year to four years.

### Neni 319/c

Active corruption of members of foreign judicial juries

The promise, proposal or giving, directly or indirectly, of any undue advantage, for oneself or for other persons, to members of foreign judicial juries, to perform or not perform an act related to his duty or function, is punishable by imprisonment from one year to four years.

Passive corruption of judges, prosecutors and other officials of justice bodies (Added by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; numbered by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; repealed the part providing for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The solicitation or acceptance, directly or indirectly, of any kind of undue advantage or such a promise, for oneself or for other persons, or the acceptance of an offer or promise arising from an undue advantage, by a judge, prosecutor or other officials of justice bodies, to perform or not perform an act related to his duty or function, is punishable by imprisonment from three to ten years.

### Neni 320

Obstruction of the execution of court decisions

Concealment, alienation, consumption, damage or destruction of objects for which a court decision has been rendered, or the performance of other acts done with the purpose of preventing or obstructing the execution

of the judicial decision, constitutes a criminal contravention and is punishable by fine or by imprisonment up to two years.

### Article 320/a

Unjustified non-execution of a court decision
(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Unjustified non-execution of a criminal or civil court decision by an official charged with the execution of decisions constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years.
When this act is committed with the aim of gain or any other interest given or promised, as well as to favor persons who have an interest in the non-execution of the decision, it is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to three years.

### Article 321

Actions in violation of a court decision
(Paragraph added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

Carrying out actions in violation of a court decision in relation to obligations arising from supplementary punishments imposed by it constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years.
Carrying out actions in violation of a court decision in relation to duties arising from protection orders issued by it constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by imprisonment up to two years.

### Article 322

Breaking of seals and marks

Intentional breaking of seals and other marks placed on various objects by criminal prosecution and judicial authorities constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to six months.

### Article 323

Removal of a prisoner from the place of detention

Removal of a detainee, arrestee or person sentenced to imprisonment from the place of mandatory detention or during his transport from one place to another is punishable by imprisonment up to five years.
When the criminal offense is committed with violence or with the use of weapons, incendiary, explosive and poisonous substances, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

### Article 324

Providing assistance to a prisoner for removal
(First paragraph amended by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
Providing advice, information, means to a detainee, arrestee or prisoner, with the aim of removing them from the place of mandatory detention, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.
When assistance is provided by a person charged with guarding, supervising or transporting, or who by reason of function has the right to enter prison institutions or to have contact with detainees, arrestees or prisoners, it is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.

### Article 324/a

Introduction or possession of prohibited items in the institution for execution of imprisonment decisions
(Added by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015)
Introduction or possession of prohibited items in the institution for execution of imprisonment decisions, which, according to legislation in force, are prohibited, is punishable by imprisonment from one to three years.
The same act, when committed in cooperation, more than once or by a person who has the duty of guarding and physical security or who by reason of duty or profession has the right to enter prison institutions, is punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.

## CHAPTER X

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AFFECTING FREE ELECTIONS AND THE DEMOCRATIC ELECTORAL SYSTEM

### Article 325

Obstruction of electoral subjects
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012, word amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

Obstruction by means of threat, violence, or in any other manner of electoral subjects or candidates to carry out their activity in accordance with the law during the electoral campaign is punishable by imprisonment from one year to five years.

### Article 326

Falsification of electoral material and election results
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; the part providing for punishment by fine as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, repealed by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Falsification, distribution or use of ballots, documents and electoral material, with the aim of changing the election result through the presentation therein of data known to be inaccurate, replacement of accurate with false data or through the illegal insertion of ballots into the ballot box is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
The same act when committed by persons who have the duty to administer the electoral process, or has brought serious consequences to the proper conduct of voting, has violated the integrity of the election result or has brought about their invalidity, is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.

### Article 326/a

Intentional damage to electoral material
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; the part providing for punishment by fine as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, repealed by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
Damage, destruction, intentional demolition, or replacement in violation of the law of equipment, seals, security codes or any other electoral material provided for by law is punishable by imprisonment from six months to five years.
The same acts when committed by persons responsible for electoral administration or in cooperation, or more than once, or when they have brought serious consequences to the proper conduct of elections, have brought about their invalidity or have violated the voting result, are punishable by imprisonment from three to eight years.

### Article 327

Violation of voting secrecy
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012 and by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)
Violation of the rules guaranteeing voting secrecy by the voter, through photographing the ballot, or filming it, or documenting by any means and form the manner in which he has voted, displaying them to other persons, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from three months to three years. Violation of the rules guaranteeing voting secrecy by persons charged with elections
constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.
Incitement with or without reward, or coercion of the voter to violate the rules guaranteeing voting secrecy, according to the first paragraph of this article, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable from one year to four years by imprisonment.

### Article 327/a

Voting more than once or without being identified
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; third paragraph amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

Voting more than once in the same elections, voting for other persons, by presenting false identification documents or by using documents of other voters is punishable by imprisonment from one to three years.
Intentional permission of the commission of this act by electoral commissioners is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
Intentional permission by electoral commissioners of voting without carrying out the identification of the citizen according to law constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.

### Article 328

Active corruption in elections
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012 and by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017, word removed by law no.
146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)
Offering or giving money, material goods, promise of employment or other favors in any form, to the voter or other persons connected with him, with the aim of obtaining a signature for the presentation of a candidate in elections, to vote in a certain manner, to participate or not in voting, or to engage in illegal activities for the support of a candidate or political party, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one year to five years.

### Neni 328/a

Use of public office for political or electoral activities
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017, words removed in the last paragraph by law no. 146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)
The participation of an employee who performs a state duty in the civil service or in a non-political function in the state administration, in violation of the law, in the activities or electoral campaign of a political party or candidate in elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.
The coercion or organization to participate in electoral activities of an electoral subject of pre-university education students by employees who perform a state duty in public education, or a duty or function in non-public education, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.
The coercion or demand directed at citizens, by an employee who performs a state duty, that against their will or under the threat of using administrative or disciplinary measures, to participate

in electoral activities of an electoral subject, to participate or not participate in elections, to support or not support a political party or a candidate in elections, or to vote in a certain manner, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one year to three years.
The use by an employee who performs a state duty, of public goods, function or state activity, or of financial or human resources, with the aim of favoring a political party or candidate in elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one year to three years.

### Neni 328/b

Passive corruption in elections
(Added by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017, words removed by law no. 146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)

The request or acceptance by a voter of money, material goods, or other favors in any form, for oneself or others, with the aim of giving a signature for the presentation of a candidate in elections, to vote in a certain manner, to participate or not participate in voting, or to engage in illegal activities in support of a candidate or political party, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

### Neni 329

Threat or violence against participants in elections
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

The threat made to a voter to vote in a certain manner or to participate or not participate in voting, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.
The threat or use of violence made to a commissioner, observer, counter as well as any other official charged with elections to prevent him from performing his duty or because of his activity in electoral administration is punishable by imprisonment from one year to four years.
When this offense is committed in cooperation or more than once it is punishable by imprisonment from two to five
years.

### Neni 330

Obstruction of the voter
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; words amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

The obstruction of the voter from voting at his voting center, by violating voting rules, by taking or damaging his identification document, or in any other form, is punishable by imprisonment from one year to five years.
When this offense is committed more than once, against more than one voter or when it is committed by electoral commissioners, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.

### Neni 330/a

Abandonment of duty by members of election commissions
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; words amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

The abandonment of duty or refusal to perform duty by persons charged with the administration of the voting and counting process, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.
When the above actions are committed by taking away or destroying electoral materials, or when they have brought serious consequences for the voting process or have led to the invalidity of elections, it is punishable by imprisonment from two to seven years.

### Article 328/b

Passive corruption in elections
(Added by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017, words removed by law no. 146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)

The request or acceptance by a voter of money, material goods, or other favors in any form, for himself or others, with the aim of giving a signature for the presentation of a candidate in elections, to vote in a certain manner, to participate or not participate in voting, or to engage in illegal activities in support of a candidate or political party, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

### Article 329

Threat or violence against participants in elections
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

The threat made to a voter to vote in a certain manner or to participate or not participate in voting, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.
The threat or use of violence made to a commissioner, observer, counter as well as any other official charged with elections to prevent him in the performance of duty or because of his activity in electoral administration is punishable by imprisonment from one year to four years.
When this offense is committed in cooperation or more than once it is punishable by imprisonment from two to five
years.

### Article 330

Obstruction of the voter
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; words amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

The obstruction of the voter to vote at his voting center, by violating voting rules, by taking or damaging his identification document, or in any other form, is punishable by imprisonment from one year to five years.
When this offense is committed more than once, against more than one voter or when committed by electoral commissioners, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.

### Article 330/a

Abandonment of duty by members of election commissions
(Added by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; words amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

The abandonment of duty or refusal to perform duty by persons charged with the administration of the voting and counting process, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.
When the above actions are committed by taking away or destroying electoral materials, or when they have brought serious consequences for the voting process or have led to the invalidity of elections, it is punishable by imprisonment from two to seven years.

### Article 331

Violation of the right to vote
(Amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; the part providing also for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, repealed by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)
The deliberate non-inclusion in the voters' lists of persons who have the right to vote or the deliberate registration in them of persons who do not have this right, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.
The same offense committed in cooperation, when it has brought serious consequences for the interests of voters or the proper conduct of the electoral process is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.

### Article 331/a

Illegal taking or use of identification documents
(Added by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)
The giving of an identification document with the aim of using it illegally for elections, guaranteeing non-participation in voting, to influence the manner of voting, or for any other illegal purpose related to elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one year to three years.
The taking of identification documents of other citizens, with the aim of using it illegally for elections, not allowing to vote, to influence the manner of voting, or for any other illegal purpose related to elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

### Article 332

Abuse of military authority
(Words amended by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

The abuse of military authority by an officer or military cadre to influence voting among other military personnel under his command, by giving orders, advice or with any kind of propaganda, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.

### Article 332/a

Abuse of police authority
(Added by law no. 89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

The abuse of police authority by an employee of the State Police or Prison Police to influence the support of a political party or candidate in elections, through non-exercise of the function with impartiality according to law, participation in political activities of a political party or candidate in elections, by performing any action or giving orders, advice or any other type of propaganda that favors a political party or candidate in elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one year to five years.

## CHAPTER XI

CRIMINAL OFFENSES COMMITTED BY ARMED GANGS AND CRIMINAL ORGANIZATIONS

### Article 333

Criminal organization
(Amended by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004)

The creation, organization or direction of criminal organizations are punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.
Participation in a criminal organization is punishable by imprisonment from four to eight years.

If the criminal organization is armed and its members possess weapons and explosives for purposes of fulfilling its criminal activity, even if they are hidden or kept in separate places, the imprisonment is increased by one third.
When economic activities, undertaken or controlled by members of the criminal organization, are financed wholly or partially with proceeds of criminal offenses, the measure of punishment, according to the above-mentioned paragraphs in this article, is increased by one third to one half of it.

### Article 333/a

Structured criminal group
(Added by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004)

The creation, organization or direction of a structured criminal group for the commission of criminal offenses are punishable by imprisonment from three to eight years.
Participation in the structured criminal group is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.

### Article 334

Commission of criminal offenses by criminal organizations and structured criminal groups
(Words removed by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004)

1.	The commission of criminal offenses by members of a criminal organization and structured criminal group shall be punished according to the relevant penal provisions, adding to the punishment for the criminal offense committed an additional five years of imprisonment, as well as a fine in the amount of one third, but without exceeding the maximum limit of imprisonment.
2.	When the relevant reference provision contains a sentence of imprisonment or life imprisonment, it shall be punished with twenty-five years of imprisonment or life imprisonment.
3.	When the relevant reference provision contains only a sentence of life imprisonment, it shall be punished with life imprisonment.

### Article 334/1

(Added by law no. 9017, dated 6.3.2003)

Notwithstanding article 278, persons who, in accordance with the legislation in force, voluntarily surrender weapons by the date of 31.5.2005 are excluded from criminal prosecution for unlawful possession of weapons and military ammunition.
In any case, persons who have committed a criminal offense using weapons and military ammunition as a means for this purpose are not excluded from criminal prosecution for unlawful possession of weapons.
Persons who, after the entry into force of this law, declare that they do not have weapons and military ammunition and from inspections carried out in support of the relevant provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure are found to have hidden weapons and ammunition are also not excluded from criminal prosecution.

### Article 335

This code enters into force on 1 June 1995. The legal acts that are repealed, as well as the effects and manner of its entry into force, shall be determined by special law.
Transitional provision
(provided by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

Criminal prosecutions are terminated for cases under investigation, for cases not examined by the courts, as well as for denunciations that are in these bodies and in public order bodies, for criminal offenses that are repealed upon the entry into force of this law.
