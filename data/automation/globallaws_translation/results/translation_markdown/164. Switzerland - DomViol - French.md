c. Electronic monitoring

Art. 28c
1 The judge who orders a prohibition pursuant to the provision on violence, threats and harassment and the judge responsible for execution may, if the applicant so requests, order the perpetrator of the infringement to wear a non-removable electronic device enabling his or her location to be determined and recorded at any time.
2 The measure may be ordered for a maximum of six months. It may be extended several times, for a maximum of six months each time. On a provisional basis, it may be ordered for a maximum of six months.

2 The public prosecutor or the court may require the accused to attend a violence prevention programme during the suspension of the proceedings. It shall communicate the measures taken to the cantonal service responsible for domestic violence issues.
3 The proceedings may not be suspended:
a.	if the accused has been convicted of a felony or misdemeanour against life, bodily integrity, liberty or sexual integrity;
b.	if a penalty or measure has been ordered against him or her, and
c.	if the accused has committed the punishable act against a victim within the meaning of para. 1, let. a.
4 The suspension is limited to six months. The public prosecutor or the court shall resume the proceedings if the victim or, where he or she does not have the exercise of civil rights, his or her legal representative so requests, or if it appears that the suspension is not stabilising or improving the situation of the victim.
5 Before the end of the suspension, the public prosecutor or the court shall carry out an evaluation. If the situation of the victim has stabilised or improved, it shall order the discontinuance of the proceedings.
