# Act LXXII of 2009

Act LXXII of 2009

Act LXXII of 2009
on restraining orders applicable in cases of domestic violence1
16.06.2023
Parliament, in protection of the fundamental human rights to life, physical integrity and dignity that belong to every person, in accordance with international conventions and the Fundamental Law, adopts the following act for the purpose of reducing domestic violence:2
I. Chapter
Interpretative provisions

## 1. § (1) Domestic violence shall mean

a) activity carried out by the abuser to the detriment of the abused person that seriously and directly endangers dignity, life, the right to sexual self-determination, as well as physical and mental health,
b) omission by the abuser to the detriment of the abused person that seriously and directly endangers dignity, life, as well as physical and mental health (hereinafter together: domestic violence).
(2) Abused person means the relative to whose detriment domestic violence is committed.
(3) Abuser means the relative with legal capacity who commits domestic violence, or on whose account domestic violence is committed by another person, provided that the abuser agrees with this.
(3a)3 In proceedings for the recognition and enforcement in Hungary of a protection measure ordered in a Member State of the European Union under Regulation (EU) No 606/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 June 2013 on mutual recognition of protection measures in civil matters (hereinafter: Regulation 606/2013/EU), the abused person referred to in paragraph (2) shall also mean the protected person within the meaning of Regulation 606/2013/EU, and the abuser referred to in paragraph (3) shall also mean the person causing the risk within the meaning of Regulation 606/2013/EU.
(4) 4 For the purposes of this Act, a child shall mean a minor as defined in the Civil Code (hereinafter: Ptk.).
(5) 5 For the purposes of this Act, a relative shall mean close relatives and relatives as defined in the Ptk., as well as former spouses, former cohabiting partners, registered partners, former registered partners, guardians, persons under guardianship, custodians, persons under custody.
(6) For the purposes of this Act, a time limit established in days shall mean continuity.
II. Chapter
The institutional system serving the prevention of domestic violence and the obligation to report

## 2. §

(1) Tasks related to the prevention of domestic violence shall be performed within the framework of their statutory core activities by
a) health service providers, in particular the health visitor service, general practitioners, paediatricians,
b) providers of personal care services, in particular family support services, family support centres, child welfare services, child welfare centres, temporary homes for children or families,
c) 6 educational institutions, vocational training institutions and educational counselling services,
d) guardianship authorities,
e) the police,
f) the prosecution service,
g) courts,
h) probation services,
i) organisations performing victim support and damage mitigation tasks,
j) refugee reception centres, temporary accommodation for refugees,
k) 7 associations, foundations.
(2) The institutions and persons specified in paragraph (1) shall be obliged to report to the body responsible for family protection coordination if they detect a risk of domestic violence.
(3) Persons other than the institutions specified in paragraph (1) may also make the report specified in paragraph (2).
(4) The institutions specified in paragraph (1) and the body responsible for family protection coordination shall be obliged to cooperate with each other and to inform each other mutually for the purpose of preventing domestic violence and reducing the harm caused by domestic violence that has occurred. Where another person as referred to in paragraph (3) makes a report or initiates proceedings, the obligation of the institutions specified in paragraphs (1)-(2) and the body responsible for family protection coordination towards such other person shall be limited to providing feedback on the initiation of proceedings.

(5) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall initiate disciplinary proceedings against the person referred to in points (a) to (j) of paragraph (1) or an employee of the body if they fail to comply with the obligation to report and cooperate set out in paragraphs (2) and (4).

## 3. §

(1) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall hear the abused person in person upon their request. In this case, the abuser shall also be heard, provided that at the request of the abused person, the abuser and the abused person shall be heard separately. At the request of the abused person, appropriate arrangements shall also be made to ensure that the abuser cannot make contact with the abused person immediately before and immediately after the personal hearing.
(2) The body responsible for family protection coordination may, following receipt of a report of domestic violence or the risk thereof — having regard to the exceptional gravity of domestic violence — summon the abused person and the abuser to appear for a personal hearing. The abuser and the abused person shall be obliged to appear at the place and time specified in the summons of the body responsible for family protection coordination. The rules set out in paragraph (1) for the protection of the abused person shall apply mutatis mutandis to summons to appear for a personal hearing.
(3) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall provide information to the abused person on the measures applicable in cases of domestic violence, the rights of the abused person, the social institutional system and services for the protection of families and children, and whether there are grounds for initiating criminal or misdemeanour proceedings in respect of domestic violence, as well as on the consequences of false accusation. At the request of the abused person, the body responsible for family protection coordination shall ensure that the abused person receives legal, medical, psychological and mental health assistance.
(4) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall provide information to the abuser on the consequences of continuing domestic violence.
(5) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall inform the abused person and the abuser about available therapeutic treatments and other assistance and conflict management options. It shall provide assistance in accessing these upon request.
(6) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall record what was said at the personal hearing in minutes and — taking into account the will of the abused person — shall take the necessary measures to prevent or stop domestic violence.
(7) The body responsible for family protection coordination shall inform the police without delay of its proceedings and the facts established.

## 4. §

(1) The body responsible for family protection coordination may process the following personal data relating to the abuser and the abused person:
a) natural identification data,
b) address, address of place of residence, contact (mailing) address, address of accommodation,
c) facts and data relating to domestic violence or the risk thereof.
(2) The personal data specified in paragraph (1) may be processed by the body responsible for family protection coordination until the initiation of proceedings initiated in respect of domestic violence.
(3) If no proceedings are initiated, the data specified in paragraph (1) may be processed for a maximum of three months.
III. Chapter
TEMPORARY PREVENTIVE AND PREVENTIVE RESTRAINING ORDERS

General provisions

## 5. §

(1) Temporary preventive restraining and preventive restraining temporarily restrict the abuser's freedom of residence, the right to free choice of place of residence, parental custodial rights, and the right to contact with his or her child.
(2) A person against whom temporary preventive restraining or preventive restraining has been ordered is obliged during the period of restraining to
a) keep away from the abused person,
b) keep away from the property serving as the habitual residence of the abused person,
c) keep away from other persons specified in the temporary preventive restraining order or the preventive restraining order, and
d) refrain from making direct or indirect contact with the abused person.
(3) If the abused person uses the property serving as his or her habitual residence — regardless of the legal title of use — jointly with the abuser, the abuser is obliged to keep away from the property serving as the habitual residence of the abused person if the abused person has a legal title to use the property other than courtesy accommodation, or is raising a child in common with the abuser.
(4) The temporary preventive restraining order or preventive restraining order does not affect the abuser's maintenance obligations existing under other legislation or contract, nor the payment obligations relating to the maintenance of the property used jointly with the abused person.
(5) The same abused person may request the ordering of temporary preventive restraining or preventive restraining against the same abuser again only if the conditions for their ordering as defined in this Act are fulfilled again.
(6) When issuing the temporary preventive restraining order or preventive restraining order, the police or the court shall inform the abuser about the location, address and conditions of use of institutions providing overnight accommodation available in his or her place of residence and its vicinity, and shall inform the abused person about the possibility and conditions of issuing a certificate under Regulation 606/2013/EU.
(7) Temporary preventive restraining or preventive restraining shall cease to have effect if the court orders restraining in criminal proceedings initiated against the abuser.
Temporary preventive restraining

## 6. §

(1) The provisions of Act CL of 2016 on the General Rules of Administrative Procedure (hereinafter: Ákr.) shall apply to proceedings related to temporary preventive restraining due to domestic violence between relatives, with the following derogations.
(2) The police may order temporary preventive restraining
a) ex officio on the basis of facts relating to domestic violence between relatives observed during on-site intervention,
b) upon notification by the abused person, or a close relative and relative of the abused person as defined in the Civil Code, as well as a registered partner,
c) upon notification — made on the basis of facts relating to domestic violence between relatives that came to his or her knowledge in the course of performing his or her duties — by a person employed in a position defined in the Act on the Protection of Children and Guardianship Administration and in the Act on Social Administration and Social Benefits,

d) upon notification — made on the basis of facts relating to domestic violence between relatives that came to its knowledge in the course of performing duties defined by law for the prevention and elimination of child endangerment — by a health care service provider, personal care service provider, or public education institution performing tasks related to the child protection system pursuant to the Act on the Protection of Children and Guardianship Administration.
(3) The police shall order temporary preventive restraining if the commission of domestic violence between relatives can be reasonably inferred from all the circumstances of the case, in particular from the facts presented by the abuser and the abused person, from the scene of the domestic violence between relatives, from signs indicating domestic violence between relatives, and from the conduct of the abuser and the abused person and their relationship to each other.
(4) In the case referred to in paragraph (3), the police shall order temporary preventive restraining for 72 hours.
(5) At the request of the abused person, the police shall issue a certificate pursuant to Article 5 of Regulation 606/2013/EU (hereinafter: protection certificate).
Time limit for settlement

## 7. §

(1) The police shall issue the temporary preventive restraining order without delay, on site. If the police apply the police measure of apprehension against the abuser during its proceedings, the police shall issue the order no later than the expiry of the duration of apprehension.
(1a) In the case of on-site intervention, the police shall issue the protection certificate on site, or if the conditions therefor are not met, no later than 12 hours from the time of the on-site proceedings. If the police apply the police measure of apprehension against the abuser during its proceedings, the police shall issue the protection certificate simultaneously with the issuance of the order on temporary preventive restraining.
(2) Simultaneously with ordering temporary preventive restraining, the police shall, by simultaneously sending the temporary preventive restraining order and the protection certificate, initiate the ordering of preventive restraining at the competent district court.
The temporary preventive restraining order

a) the police's decision that
aa) the abuser is obliged to leave the property serving as the habitual residence of the abused person and may not return there during the period of validity of the temporary preventive restraining order, or
ab) the obligation specified in

paragraph (2) point b) does not apply to the abuser during the period of temporary preventive restraining — pursuant to

b) the address of the property serving as the habitual residence of the abused person,
c) the police's decision that the abuser is obliged to keep away from the abused person and from other persons specified in the order,
d) the police's decision that the abuser is prohibited from making direct or indirect contact with the abused person or, with regard to him or her, with another person, in particular with other persons specified in the order ordering temporary preventive restraining,

e) the finding that the parental custodial right of the abuser parent, or the right to contact with his or her minor child, is suspended,
f) a provision on the immediate enforceability of the order, the method of enforcement, the procedure for implementation,
g) a warning that the temporary preventive restraining order does not affect the abuser's maintenance obligations existing under other legislation or contract, nor the payment obligations relating to the maintenance of the property used jointly with the abused person,
h) a warning that violation of the obligations prescribed in the temporary preventive restraining order constitutes a petty offence.
Service of the temporary preventive restraining order and the protection certificate

## 9. §

(1) The decision ordering temporary preventive restraining must be communicated to the abuser and the abused person, and must be handed over to them or delivered by the shortest possible route. In the absence of a specified address, the decision may be collected at the police station.
(2) If the abuser does not collect the decision at the police station, the decision shall be deemed to have been communicated on the third day following its adoption. Justification is available against the failure to meet this deadline in accordance with the provisions of the Ákr.
(2a) The police shall communicate to the abused person the protection certificate, and to the abuser the notification concerning the protection certificate pursuant to Article 8(1) of Regulation 606/2013/EU, in accordance with paragraphs (1) and (2).
(3) At the request of the abused person, or with their consent, the police shall send the decision to the competent family support service, and to the child welfare service if a minor lives in the abused person's household. If the abused person is a person under guardianship or curatorship, the police shall also send the decision to the guardianship authority according to the permanent residence of the ward or person under curatorship.
(4) Based on the notification of the police, within twenty-four hours following the adoption of the restraining decision, the family support service, or in the case of a cohabiting minor relative the child welfare service, is obliged to contact the abused person and the abuser and initiate a helping relationship with them, or to take measures to eliminate the situation endangering the minor.
Appeal
10. § There is no right of appeal against the decision.
Information

Proceedings in connection with requests submitted on the basis of a protection measure ordered in another Member State50

17/A.

## §1

(1) The rules on preventive restraining orders shall apply mutatis mutandis to the recognition and enforcement in Hungary of a protection measure ordered in another Member State, initiated pursuant to Article 4 of Regulation 606/2013/EU, with the derogations set out in this §, with the proviso that a request within the meaning of Article 4 of the Regulation (hereinafter in the application of this §: request) shall be considered as a request.
(2) The court shall examine the request and within 3 working days shall
a) call upon the abused person to rectify deficiencies if the request does not comply with the requirements laid down in Regulation 606/2013/EU,
b) order a preventive restraining order by modifying the protection measure underlying the request pursuant to Article 11 of Regulation 606/2013/EU,
c) order a preventive restraining order in accordance with the content of the protection measure underlying the request, or
d) reject the request upon the request of the abuser to that effect pursuant to Article 13(1) of Regulation 606/2013/EU.
(3) If the abused person does not comply with the call referred to in point a) of paragraph (2), the court shall reject the request. A rejected request may be resubmitted.
(4) The court shall not attempt to hear the parties in person in the cases referred to in points a) and c) of paragraph (2), and shall only attempt to do so as necessary in the cases referred to in points b) and d) of paragraph (2).
(5) The court shall order the preventive restraining order for the period specified in the protection measure underlying the request, but for a maximum of 12 months.
(6) The abused person and the abuser may lodge an appeal against the court's decision pursuant to point d) of paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), and against the part of the decision pursuant to point b) of paragraph (2) that modifies the protection measure underlying the request.
