# LAW No. 1674

LAW No. 1674
LAW OF DECEMBER 15, 1995
LAW OF DECEMBER 15, 1995
GONZALO SANCHEZ DE LOZADA
CONSTITUTIONAL PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC
Whereas the Honorable National Congress has enacted the following Law:
THE H. NATIONAL CONGRESS,
DECREES:
LAW AGAINST
VIOLENCE IN THE FAMILY OR DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1. (SCOPE)

This law establishes the State policy against violence in the family or domestic violence, the acts that constitute violence in the family, the sanctions corresponding to the perpetrator and the measures of prevention and immediate protection for the victim.

### Article 2. (PROTECTED INTERESTS)

The legally protected interests under this law are the physical, psychological, moral and sexual integrity of each of the members of the family unit.

### Article 3. (PREVENTION)

The eradication of violence in the family constitutes a national strategy.
The State through its specialized institutions and in coordination with civil associations and private institutions related to the matter:
a)            Shall promote the incorporation in curricular and extra-curricular teaching-learning processes, of orientations and values of respect, solidarity and self-esteem of children, youth and adults of both sexes; fostering access, use and enjoyment of citizen rights without discrimination based on sex, age, culture and religion.
b)            Shall promote a process of modification of socio-cultural patterns of conduct of men and women, including the design of formal and non-formal education programs appropriate to all levels of the educational process, to counteract prejudices, customs and all other types of practices based on the supposed inferiority or superiority of either gender or on stereotyped roles for men and women that legitimize or exacerbate violence.
c)            Shall disseminate the rights and protection of women within the family as well as access to health, avoiding discrimination or acts of violence that harm or alter their health.
d)            Shall sensitize the community through mass campaigns about the care that should be provided to pregnant women, avoiding all types of violence that may affect her or affect the unborn child.
e)            Shall instruct health service personnel to provide good treatment and comprehensive care to victims of violence in the family, considering their intimacy and privacy, and avoiding the repetition of clinical examinations that affect their psychological integrity.
f)              Shall coordinate joint actions of health services with comprehensive legal services to provide adequate care to victims of violence in the family.
g)            Shall train and create awareness in justice administration personnel, police and other officials responsible for the application of this law, on measures of prevention, sanction and elimination of violence in the family.
h)            Shall conduct awareness campaigns through interactive group and mass media towards the community as a whole, to strengthen the rejection of violence in the family.
i)              Shall conduct communication campaigns sectorized by regions, ages and socio-economic situation, through traditional and alternative means of communication to disseminate women's rights and the conviction that family violence is an attack on human rights.
j)              Shall incorporate in the language and discourse of mass media the permanent dissemination of rejection of family violence and the full exercise of rights, through special programs, participation in interviews and regular news streams.
k)            Shall disseminate the United Nations Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women and the Inter-American Convention of the Organization of American States for the Prevention, Punishment and Eradication of Violence against Women.
l)              Shall disseminate the text of this law to specialized audiences, levels of political decision-making, union and party leaders, and opinion leaders.
m)          Shall promote the study and investigation of the causes and consequences of violence in the family and shall adopt measures to promote its eradication.
n)            The National Police shall deploy mobile control patrols to centers with the highest incidence of domestic violence.
o)            Shall promote the establishment of temporary shelters for victims of violence and the creation of institutions for the treatment of aggressors.
p)            Shall promote and support the dissemination of the Law against Violence in the Family or Domestic Violence through the National Education System.
q)            Shall insert as a curricular training subject in Military Institutes and the National Police Academy the Law against Violence in the Family or Domestic Violence.
r)             Shall encourage the formation of psychological clinics for the diagnosis and therapy of victims of violence.

## CHAPTER II

VIOLENCE IN THE FAMILY OR DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 4. (VIOLENCE IN THE FAMILY)

Violence in the family or domestic violence is understood as physical, psychological or sexual aggression, committed by:
1)            The spouse or cohabitant;
2)            Ascendants, descendants, siblings, civil or affinal relatives in direct and collateral line;
3)            Guardians, curators or those in charge of custody.

### Article 5. (DOMESTIC VIOLENCE)

Acts of domestic violence are considered to be aggressions committed between ex-spouses, ex-cohabitants or persons who have procreated children in common legally recognized or not, even if they had not cohabited.

### Article 6. (FORMS OF VIOLENCE). It is considered

a)            Physical violence, conduct that causes internal or external injury or any other mistreatment that affects the physical integrity of persons;
b)            psychological violence, conduct that emotionally disturbs the victim, harming their psychic and emotional development; and
c)            sexual violence, conduct, threats or intimidation that affect the sexual integrity or sexual self-determination of the victim.
d)            Likewise, acts of violence in the family are considered when parents, guardians or those in charge of custody endanger the physical or psychological integrity of minors, by abuse of corrective or disciplinary means or by imposition of excessive and inadequate work for the age or physical condition of the minor.
Equally, acts performed against incapacitated elderly persons are considered acts of violence in the family.

## CHAPTER III

SANCTIONS AND ALTERNATIVE MEASURES

### Article 7. (SANCTIONS)

Acts of violence in the family or domestic violence, covered by this law, and which do not constitute crimes typified in the Penal Code, shall be sanctioned with penalties of fine or arrest.

### Article 8. (FINE)

The fine penalty in favor of the State shall be set by the judge up to a maximum of 20% of the national minimum wage and up to ten times that amount, according to the seriousness of the acts and the economic capacity of the perpetrator.
The fine shall be paid within three days.
Non-compliance shall result in the conversion of the fine into arrest, which may not exceed the maximum duration set by the following article.

### Article 9. (ARREST)

The arrest penalty consists of deprivation of liberty for a period that shall be set by the judge and that may not exceed four days, and its fulfillment may be deferred to weekends.
Arrest shall be served in police facilities.

### Artículo 10º. (AGRAVANTES)

Sanctions shall be aggravated up to double the maximum provided, in the following cases:
1)            When the victim is disabled, over sixty years of age or is pregnant.
2)            When several actions constituting family violence have been committed.
3)            When, having served the sanction, the responsible party commits another act or acts constituting family violence.

### Artículo 11º. (MEDIDAS ALTERNATIVAS A LA EJECUCIÓN DE LA SANCIÓN)

The judge may suspend the execution of the sanction, ordering according to the nature of the act and the personality of the perpetrator, as an alternative measure psychological therapy or provision of community work.
These measures may only be made effective if the consent of the responsible party is obtained. If consent is not given, the imposed sanction shall be executed.
Once compliance with the measure is proven, the judge shall declare the imposed sanction extinguished. Otherwise, the sanction whose compliance was suspended shall be executed.

### Artículo 12º. (TERAPIA PSICOLÓGICA)

Psychological therapy shall be carried out in private offices of qualified professionals, at the expense of the perpetrator. Persons of limited resources shall be referred to the Secretariat of Ethnic, Gender and Generational Affairs, ONAMFA or any accredited non-profit social service.
The specialist shall determine the duration and modality of the psychological therapy and shall inform the judge about these circumstances.

### Artículo 13º. (TRABAJOS COMUNITARIOS)

Community work shall consist of the provision of work in favor of the community or the State, which shall be performed outside regular working hours and according to the profession, trade or occupation of the perpetrator.
The duration of the work may not exceed the time equivalent to four days.
The work must be supervised by the person or authority designated by the judge, who shall report on its compliance.

## CAPITULO IV

COMPETENCIA

### Artículo 14º. (COMPETENCIA)

Knowledge of acts of family or domestic violence, covered by this law, shall be the competence of family examining magistrates.
In places where there are no family examining magistrates, examining magistrates shall have competence.

### Artículo 15º. (ACTOS DELICTIVOS)

Acts of violence that constitute offenses typified in the Penal Code are the exclusive competence of criminal judges.

### Artículo 16º. (AUTORIDADES COMUNITARIAS)

In indigenous and peasant communities, it shall be the community and natural authorities who resolve family violence disputes, in accordance with their customs and practices, provided they do not conflict with the Political Constitution of the State and the spirit of this law.

## CAPITULO V

MEDIDAS CAUTELARES Y PROVISIONALES

### Artículo 17º. (MEDIDAS CAUTELARES)

The judge ex officio, at the request of a party or the Public Prosecutor's Office, may order the appropriate precautionary measures, intended to guarantee the safety and physical or psychological integrity of the victim. He may also order the assistance of the police force for its enforcement.
At any time during the proceedings the judge, ex officio or at the request of a party, by resolution, may extend, modify, substitute or render without effect the precautionary measures.

### Artículo 18º. (CLASES). Son medidas cautelares

1)            Prohibit or temporarily restrict the presence of the accused in the marital home.
2)            Order the restitution of the victim to the home from which he or she had been removed by violence.
3)            Authorize the victim to leave the common home and order the immediate delivery of his or her personal effects.
4)            Order the inventory of movable and immovable property owned by the community property.
5)            Prohibit or limit the attendance of the accused at the victim's workplace.

### Artículo 19º. (TEMPORALIDAD DE LAS MEDIDAS)

The precautionary measures listed in the preceding article are essentially temporary in nature and may not exceed the duration of the proceedings.

### Artículo 20º. (MEDIDAS PROVISIONALES)

The judge hearing the case may issue provisional measures for family support and custody of children, as appropriate. These measures shall be in effect only until the conclusion of the proceedings.

## CAPITULO VI

PROCEDIMIENTO

### Artículo 21º. (DENUNCIA)

The complaint may be filed orally or in writing, with or without the assistance of a sponsoring attorney, before the competent judge, the Public Prosecutor's Office or the National Police.

### Artículo 22º. (LEGITIMACIÓN PARA DENUNCIAR)

The victim's blood relatives, relatives by affinity or civil relatives, or any person who knows of these facts, are entitled to request protection on behalf of the victim, reporting acts of physical or psychological violence.
Acts of sexual violence may only be reported by the victim, unless he or she is under eighteen years of age or an incapacitated adult, in which case the subjects indicated in the preceding paragraph are entitled to report.

### Artículo 23º. (LEGITIMACIÓN PARA INTERVENIR EN EL PROCESO)

In cases of physical violence or when the victim of sexual or psychological violence is a minor under eighteen years of age or an incapacitated adult, the victim and the Public Prosecutor's Office are entitled to bring the action.
In other cases of sexual violence only the victim is entitled to bring the action.

### Artículo 24º. (OBLIGATORIEDAD DE DENUNCIAR)

Health workers in public or private establishments who receive or provide care to victims of violence are obliged to report these acts for their respective prosecution.

### Artículo 25º. (DENUNCIA ANTE LA POLICÍA)

When the complaint is filed with the Police, it shall forward the background information to the knowledge of the competent judge, within 24 hours of receiving the complaint, at no cost.

### Artículo 26º. (BRIGADAS DE PROTECCIÓN A LA FAMILIA)

The Family Protection Brigades shall be responsible for carrying out the procedures aimed at identifying the perpetrators and participants, gathering or securing the elements of proof and providing the necessary and immediate assistance to the victim.
Where Family Protection Brigades do not exist, the existing police authorities shall fulfill these functions.

### Artículo 27º. (FLAGRANCIA)

In case of flagrancy the perpetrator may be apprehended even without a warrant by any person, for the sole purpose of being immediately brought before the competent authority.

### Artículo 28º. (DENUNCIA ANTE EL MINISTERIO PÚBLICO)

When the complaint is filed with the Public Prosecutor's Office, the family prosecutor or prosecuting attorney shall immediately summon the accused and the victim to a conciliation hearing, which shall be held within 24 hours of receiving the complaint.
In the event that the parties summoned do not appear or conciliation does not occur, the prosecutor shall refer the case to the competent judge.
At the time of referring the case, the prosecutor may request from the judge the appropriate precautionary measures.

### Artículo 29º. (ADMISIÓN DE LA DENUNCIA)

Upon receipt of the complaint, the judge upon admitting it, shall set the day and time for the hearing which shall take place within a period not exceeding 48 hours, shall rule on the appropriateness of the precautionary measures and shall order the summons of the accused and whoever is entitled to bring the action.

### Artículo 30º. (CITACIÓN)

The summons to the accused may be made on any day or time and at the place where he or she may be found.
The summons shall contain the reason for the complaint and the precautionary measures that the judge has ordered for immediate compliance.

### Artículo 31º. (INCOMPARECENCIA DEL DENUNCIADO)

When the accused fails to appear without justified cause, having been legally summoned, the judge shall order their appearance with the assistance of the public force.

### Artículo 32º. (DESISTIMIENTO)

If the person entitled to bring the action does not appear, the action shall be deemed withdrawn, unless legal impediment is proven, in which case a new date and time for the hearing shall be set within the same period established in Article 29 of this law.

### Artículo 33º. (AUDIENCIA)

On the day of the hearing, the judge shall order the reading of the complaint, hear the parties, receive the evidence they offer, and propose the bases for a possible conciliation.
The accused may be assisted by a defense attorney.
If one of the parties is assisted at the hearing by a sponsoring attorney, for the sake of equity, the Judge shall appoint a defender for the other.

### Artículo 34º. (PRUEBA)

All elements of evidence, legally obtained, that may lead to knowledge of the reported facts shall be admitted as means of proof.
The evidence shall be assessed by the judge, setting forth the reasoning on which his legal evaluation is based.

### Artículo 35º. (TESTIGOS)

Relatives or dependents of the complainant or the accused may also be witnesses, provided that their statement is voluntary.

### Artículo 36º. (RESOLUCIÓN)

The judge shall issue a ruling at the same hearing, expressing the grounds on which it is based.
The ruling, as appropriate, may:
1)            Approve the agreements reached by the parties in conciliation;
2)            Declare the complaint proven when the guilt of the accused has been demonstrated.
3)            Declare the complaint unproven.
In the event of declaring the complaint proven, the judge shall impose the corresponding sanction and order payment of all expenses incurred by the victim as a consequence of the act and the processing of the proceedings. In the same ruling, the judge may order that the sanction be suspended, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 11, 12 and 13 of this law.

### Artículo 37º. (CERTIFICADOS MÉDICOS)

Any medical certificate issued by a professional working in public health institutions shall be admitted as documentary evidence.

### Artículo 38º. (MEDIDAS PARA MEJOR PROVEER)

According to the circumstances of the case, the judge may order psychological expert examination of the accused and of the family members involved in the acts of violence.
The expert report must be submitted to the judge within a period not exceeding seven working days. After this period has elapsed, with or without the expert report, the judge shall issue a ruling.

### Artículo 39º. (APELACIÓN)

The parties may file an appeal verbally at the same hearing or in writing within 24 hours, before the same judge who issued the ruling.
Once the appeal is filed, the judge shall summon the other party to respond to the appeal within the same period. Then, without further proceedings, within the following 24 hours the proceedings must be forwarded to the second instance judge, under the responsibility of the clerk.
The appeal shall be granted with suspensive effect before the family district judge on duty or before the district judge in the provinces.

### Artículo 40º. (RESOLUCIÓN DE LA APELACIÓN)

Once the proceedings are received, the second instance judge shall issue a ruling within the following three days, without further appeal.

### Artículo 41º. (RESERVA DEL TRAMITE)

Proceedings for acts of family or domestic violence are absolutely confidential. The file may only be exhibited or testimonies or certificates of the documents contained therein may be granted at the request of an entitled party and with a judicial order.

## CAPITULO VII

DISPOSICIONES FINALES

### Artículo 42º. (INCIDENTE)

If during the processing of divorce, separation or unilateral dissolution of common-law union proceedings acts of family or domestic violence occur, the judge hearing the case shall hear and resolve these complaints by way of incidental proceedings in accordance with the procedure established in this law.

### Artículo 43º. (DELITOS DE ORDEN PUBLICO A INSTANCIA DE PARTE)

Article 7 of the Code of Criminal Procedure is amended, excluding from it the crimes of statutory rape, rape of persons over the age of puberty, indecent assault, outrage to decency and corruption of adults; which shall be considered public action crimes upon complaint by the victim.
In the cases covered by this article, proceedings shall not be instituted except upon accusation or complaint by the victim, their guardian or their legal representatives. However, complaint by the victim shall not be required when the crime is committed against a minor who has no parents, guardian or legal representatives, or when it is committed by one of the parents, guardian, legal representative or person in charge of their custody.
In all cases the name of the victim shall be kept strictly confidential.
Once the action is initiated upon complaint by the victim, the Public Prosecutor's Office shall continue the proceedings ex officio.

### Artículo 44º. (DEROGATORIA). Article 276 of the Criminal Code is repealed

### Artículo 45º. (NORMAS SUPLETORIAS)

The provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure are applicable insofar as they do not conflict with the provisions of this law.
Remit to the Executive Branch for constitutional purposes.
