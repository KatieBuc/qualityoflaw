LAW ON PROTECTION AGAINST DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (TITLE AMENDED - SG, ISSUE 102 OF 2009, IN FORCE FROM 22.12.2009)
Promulgated SG Issue 27 of 29 March 2005, amended SG Issue 82 of 10 October 2006, amended SG Issue 102 of 22 December 2009, amended SG Issue 99 of 17 December 2010, supplemented SG Issue 50 of 3 July 2015, amended SG Issue 24 of 22 March 2019, amended SG Issue 101 of 27 December 2019, amended and supplemented SG Issue 66 of 1 August 2023, amended and supplemented SG Issue 69 of 11 August 2023.
Draft: 354-01-22/17.04.2003
Chapter One.
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Art. 1. (1) (Amended - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) This law regulates:
1. the rights of persons who have suffered from domestic violence or are at risk;
2. the measures for protection against domestic violence;
3. the bodies and mechanisms for implementing state policy for prevention and protection against domestic violence and the interaction between them;
4. the programmes for prevention and specialised services for providing protection, assistance and support to persons who have suffered from domestic violence or are at risk;
5. the proceedings for imposing measures for protection against domestic violence;
6. the measures for the application of Regulation (EU) No 606/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 June 2013 on mutual recognition of protection measures in civil matters (OJ, L 181/4 of 29 June 2013), hereinafter referred to as "Regulation (EU) No 606/2013".
(2) (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The state conducts a consistent policy in the field of domestic violence through coordination, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of policies and measures for prevention and protection against domestic violence.
(3) (Supplemented - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, former para. 2, amended - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The measures under this law do not exclude the civil, administrative-penal and criminal liability of the perpetrator.
Art. 1a. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The purpose of the law is to provide rapid and effective protection and to ensure assistance and support to persons who have suffered from domestic violence or are at risk, and to exercise a preventive and deterrent effect on the perpetrator of the violence.
					Art. 2. (Amended - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009) (1) (Amended - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024, supplemented - SG, Issue 69 of 2023) Domestic violence is any act of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence, as well as an attempt at such violence, forced restriction of personal life, personal freedom and personal rights, committed against persons who are in a kinship relationship, who are or have been in a family relationship or in de facto cohabitation or in an intimate relationship.
(2) (Amended - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) Psychological violence against a child is also considered to be any domestic violence committed in his or her presence.
(3) (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The acts under para. 1 and 2 may be committed through action or omission.
Art. 3. Protection under this law may be sought by any person who has suffered from domestic violence committed by:
1. a spouse or former spouse;
2. (supplemented - SG, Issue 69 of 2023) a person with whom he or she is or has been in de facto cohabitation or in an intimate relationship;
3. a person with whom he or she has a child;
4. an ascendant;
5. a descendant;
6. (amended - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009) a person with whom he or she is in a collateral kinship relationship up to and including the fourth degree;
7. (amended - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009) a person with whom he or she is or has been in a relationship by affinity up to and including the third degree;
8. (amended - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) a guardian, trustee, person to whom care for the child has been entrusted, or person to whom the child has been entrusted;
9. (new - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024, amended - SG, Issue 69 of 2023) a person who is a relative in a direct or collateral line up to and including the fourth degree, of the person with whom he or she is or has been in de facto cohabitation or in an intimate relationship;
10. (new - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, supplemented - SG, Issue 69 of 2023) a person with whom the parent is or has been in de facto cohabitation or in an intimate relationship;
11. (new - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) a person who is a spouse or former spouse of the parent.
Art. 4. (1) In case of domestic violence, the victim has the right to turn to the court for protection.
(2) (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) At the request of the victim, state and municipal bodies, medical establishments and legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence are obliged within 24 hours to forward to the respective district court the application for initiating proceedings for issuing a protection order.
(3) (Amended - SG, Issue 82 of 2006, amended - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, former para. 2 - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) In cases where there is evidence of danger to the life or health of the victim, he or she may also submit an application to the bodies of the Ministry of the Interior for taking measures in accordance with the Law on the Ministry of the Interior.
(4) (Former para. 3 - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) At the request of the victim, every doctor is obliged to issue a document in which to certify in writing the injuries or traces of violence established by him or her.
Art. 5. (1) (Amended - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009) The measures for protection against domestic violence are:
1. obliging the perpetrator to refrain from committing domestic violence;
2. removal of the perpetrator from the jointly inhabited dwelling for the period determined by the court;
3. (supplemented - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, supplemented - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) prohibition on the perpetrator from approaching the victim, the dwelling, the workplace and the places for social contacts and recreation of the victim under conditions and for a period determined by the court;
4. (new - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) prohibition on the perpetrator from making contact with the victim in any form, including by telephone, through electronic or ordinary mail and fax, as well as through any other means and communication systems under conditions and for a period determined by the court;
5. (former item 4, supplemented - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) temporary determination of the child's place of residence with the victim parent or with the parent who did not commit the violence, under conditions and for a period determined by the court, if this does not contradict the interests of the child;
6. (former item 5, supplemented - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) obliging the perpetrator of the violence to attend specialised programmes for overcoming aggression and anger management;
7. (former item 6, amended - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) referring the victims to specialised services for protection, assistance and support or recovery programmes;
8. (new - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) referring the child victims to specialised services for protection, assistance and support of children who are victims or witnesses of violence.
(2) (Amended - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amended and supplemented - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The measures under para. 1, items 2 - 5 are imposed for a period of three to 18 months with deduction of the period of validity of the measures when an order for immediate protection under Art. 18 and 19 has been issued.
(3) (New - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) In proceedings under this law, the court makes a mandatory assessment for imposing the measure under para. 1, item 5, when the victim and the perpetrator have a child.
(4) (Former para. 3 - SG, Issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The court may order as a protective measure that the regime of personal relations, determined under the procedure of the Family Code, between the child and the parent who is the perpetrator of domestic violence, be carried out for the period under para. 2 in a protected environment, provided in a specialised service under this law or provided by a provider of a social service at the current address of the child or at his or her place of residence, under the supervision of a psychologist or social worker.
Art. 6. (Repealed - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024)
Chapter One "a".
NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR PREVENTION AND PROTECTION AGAINST DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (NEW - SG, ISSUE 66 OF 2023, IN FORCE FROM 01.08.2023)
Art. 6a. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.08.2023) (1) A National Council for Prevention and Protection against Domestic Violence, hereinafter referred to as "the National Council", is established at the Council of Ministers.
(2) The National Council is a specialised permanent collective and consultative body for implementing state policy on prevention and protection against domestic violence through coordination, monitoring and evaluation of policies and measures for prevention and protection against domestic violence.
(3) The Chairman of the National Council is a Deputy Prime Minister appointed by the Council of Ministers. The Deputy Chairman of the National Council is the Minister of Justice.
(4) The composition of the National Council includes the Minister of the Interior, the Minister of Labour and Social Policy, the Minister of Health, the Minister of Education and Science, the Minister of Youth and Sports, a representative of the Supreme Court of Cassation, the Chairman of the State Agency for Child Protection, the Executive Director of the Social Assistance Agency, the Executive Director of the National Association of Municipalities in the Republic of Bulgaria.
(5) The composition of the National Council includes representatives of non-profit legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence. The organisations that represent these members of the council and related persons may not participate with projects for funding under the procedure of Art. 6e, para. 6.
(6) In the meetings of the National Council, at the invitation of the chairman, the Ombudsman of the Republic of Bulgaria, members of parliament, representatives of the judicial authorities, representatives of academic circles, representatives of legal entities and international organisations with representations in the country that carry out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence, heads of other state bodies, representatives of local self-government and local administration bodies, of professional and civil organisations, other officials and experts may participate.
(7) The administrative-technical, organisational, expert and financial servicing of the National Council is carried out by the administration of the Council of Ministers.
(8) The Council of Ministers adopts rules for the structure, organisation and activity of the National Council.
(9) The conditions and procedure for the selection of non-profit legal entities under para. 5 are determined in the rules under para. 8.
Art. 6b. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.08.2023) (1) The National Council:
1. determines the priorities in policies in the field of prevention and protection against domestic violence;
2. carries out coordination, monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of policies and measures in the field of prevention and protection against domestic violence;
3. develops and updates a coordination mechanism for assistance and support to persons who have suffered from domestic violence, and presents it for adoption by the Council of Ministers, monitors its effective functioning and gives instructions and recommendations on its application;
4. develops a national programme for prevention and protection against domestic violence based on the determined priority activities for funding and submits it for adoption by the Council of Ministers, making proposals for its updating when necessary;
5. requests and receives information from state bodies, legal entities and others that are related to prevention and protection against domestic violence;
6. creates and maintains a national information system for cases of domestic violence committed on the territory of the country, or in relation to Bulgarian citizens abroad who have sought assistance and support, and a list of legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence under the procedure of this law, in compliance with the requirements of the Personal Data Protection Act;
7. summarises and analyses the results of the implementation of policies in the field of prevention and protection against domestic violence and proposes measures to increase their effectiveness;
8. publishes and disseminates statistical information in connection with domestic violence in compliance with the requirements of the Statistics Act and the Personal Data Protection Act;
9. organises the conduct of information, awareness-raising and training campaigns on issues related to domestic violence;
10. develops and updates draft strategies, programmes and plans in the field of prevention and protection against domestic violence and monitors and reports on their implementation;
11. gives opinions and makes proposals for amending existing and adopting new normative acts in the field of prevention and protection against domestic violence;
12. develops draft normative acts in connection with prevention and protection against domestic violence and submits them to the Council of Ministers through the Chairman of the National Council;
13. approves methodological instructions and participates jointly with other representatives of organisations, institutions and judicial bodies in the development of documents related to risk assessment under this law;
14. carries out international cooperation with international organisations and institutions with activities in the sphere of prevention and protection against domestic violence, by exchanging information and good practices;
15. approves the procedures for selection of activities for prevention of domestic violence and specialised services for protection against domestic violence, financed under the procedure of Art. 6e, para. 6, and exercises control over the fulfilment of the conditions under the funding contract;
16. approves training programmes for employees to perform functions in connection with prevention and protection against domestic violence;
17. maintains and develops a national helpline for assistance to persons who have suffered from domestic violence and are at risk;
18. monitors and evaluates the impact of measures for prevention and protection against domestic violence.
(2) The Chairman of the National Council presents an annual report on its activities to the Council of Ministers.
Chapter One "b".
INTERACTION OF THE NATIONAL COUNCIL WITH BODIES AND ORGANISATIONS (NEW - SG, ISSUE 66 OF 2023, IN FORCE FROM 01.01.2024)
Art. 6v. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) (1) For carrying out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence, the National Council interacts with the executive authorities, the judicial authorities, the local self-government and local administration bodies and the legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence.
(2) The interaction under para. 1 is carried out through:
1. exchange of information on identified cases of violence, risk assessment, referral to the competent bodies and/or organisations, determination of measures for protection and support, applications for protection against domestic violence and regarding the implementation of the measures and/or
2. carrying out joint actions in prevention and protection against domestic violence.
Art. 6g. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) (1) The interaction for protection, assistance and support of victims of domestic violence is carried out under the conditions and according to the procedure of a coordination mechanism for assistance and support to persons who have suffered from domestic violence.
(2) The coordination mechanism under para. 1 regulates the basic procedures for interaction between the executive authorities, the local self-government and local administration bodies and the legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence under the procedure of this law. The interaction is carried out at the following stages:
1. notification and referral:
a) identification of cases of violence;
b) risk assessment from the point of view of the life and health of the victim with the aim of adequate planning of protection measures and for a coordinated response;
c) referral for use of specialised services;
2. determination of protection measures in case of:
a) direct and immediate danger of domestic violence;
b) risk;
3. measures for long-term support after cessation of violence:
a) psycho-social counselling and psychotherapeutic work;
b) development of skills for realisation on the labour market and for realisation in the social environment;
c) social counselling, advocacy and active social work.
Chapter One "c".
NATIONAL INFORMATION SYSTEM (NEW - SG, ISSUE 66 OF 2023, IN FORCE FROM 03.08.2024)
Art. 6d. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 03.08.2024) (1) The National Council creates and maintains a National Information System for Prevention and Protection against Domestic Violence, in which data on cases of domestic violence committed on the territory of the country, or in relation to Bulgarian citizens abroad who have sought assistance and support, are stored, hereinafter referred to as "National Information System".
(2) The National Information System contains data on:
1. acts of domestic violence;
2. the victims, the perpetrators and the relationships between them;
3. the type of violence - physical, psychological, sexual, economic, restriction of rights and others;
4. the duration of the violence;
5. the harm caused to the victim;
6. the history of the violence;
7. the submitted applications for issuing a protection order, an order for immediate protection or requests of the prosecutor under the procedure of Art. 8, para. 2;
8. the imposed measures under this law, the Law on the Ministry of the Interior, the Child Protection Act and the provided social services under the procedure of the Social Services Act in relation to persons who have suffered from domestic violence;
9. the legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence under the procedure of this law;
10. the services used under the procedure of this law.
(3) The National Council ensures the registration, storage and maintenance in an up-to-date state of the data under para. 2.
(4) The information subject to entry in the National Information System, the procedure for its collection and provision, the organisation and functioning of the system and the level of access are determined by the implementing regulations of the law.
(5) The executive authorities, the judicial authorities, the local self-government and local administration bodies and the legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence under the procedure of this law provide the National Council with the necessary data under para. 2 according to a procedure determined in the implementing regulations of the law.
(6) Officials from the Ministry of the Interior, the Social Assistance Agency, the State Agency for Child Protection and the judicial authorities have the right of access to the data in the National Information System.
(7) The personal data in the system under para. 1 are stored for a period of no more than twenty years.
(8) For the purposes of the system under para. 1, the National Council has the right of access to data on perpetrators of domestic violence in the Unified Information System for Combating Crime under Art. 378 of the Judicial System Act according to a procedure determined by the implementing regulations of this law.
Chapter One "d".
PROGRAMMES FOR PREVENTION AND SPECIALISED SERVICES FOR PROTECTION AGAINST DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (NEW - SG, ISSUE 66 OF 2023, IN FORCE FROM 01.01.2024)
Chapter One "d".
PROGRAMMES FOR PREVENTION AND SPECIALISED SERVICES FOR PROTECTION AGAINST DOMESTIC VIOLENCE
Art. 6e. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) (1) The state creates conditions for the implementation of programmes for prevention of domestic violence and specialised services for protection, assistance and support to persons who have suffered from domestic violence and are at risk.
(2) The executive authorities, the National Institute of Justice and the local self-government and local administration bodies organise training for persons who carry out activities under this law.
(3) The executive authorities, the local self-government and local administration bodies and the legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection of persons who have suffered from domestic violence or are at risk, under the procedure of this law develop, organise the implementation and implement the programmes and services provided for in Art. 5, para. 1, items 6 - 8.
(4) The Council of Ministers adopts a three-year national programme for prevention and protection against domestic violence by 31 March of the respective year.
(5) The funds for financing the fulfilment of the obligations under the national programme under para. 4 are determined on the basis of medium-term three-year financial forecasts prepared by the respective executive authorities having commitments to the implementation of activities from the programme.
(6) By the law on the state budget of the Republic of Bulgaria for the respective year, funds are determined in the budget of the Council of Ministers for financing projects of the persons under para. 3, provided that they carry out activities under this law, for developing and implementing:
1. programmes for prevention of domestic violence concerning:
a) work with the judicial authorities;
b) work with the bodies of the Ministry of the Interior, including training of police officers and other employees of law enforcement bodies for identifying cases of domestic violence, on protocols for response and application of the relevant legislation; the programme is adopted by the Ministry of the Interior;
c) training in educational establishments for preventing domestic violence and promoting equality and respect between the sexes; the programme is adopted by the Ministry of Education and Science;
d) training of persons who carry out prevention and protection under the law;
e) monitoring of the application of the law;
f) preparation and conduct of information campaigns, seminars and conferences;
2. specialised services for providing protection, assistance and support to persons who have suffered from domestic violence or are at risk, which include:
a) a national helpline for assistance to persons who have suffered from domestic violence or are at risk;
b) social, psychological and legal counselling and specialised programmes for recovery and/or protection of persons or children who are victims of domestic violence or witnesses, in a counselling centre;
c) social, psychological and legal counselling and specialised programmes for recovery and/or protection of persons or children who are victims of domestic violence or witnesses, in a sheltered dwelling;
3. specialised programmes for overcoming aggression and anger management for perpetrators of domestic violence, which include social and psychological counselling.
(7) The procedures for selection of legal entities that carry out prevention of domestic violence under the procedure of this law, the conditions and procedure for financing, reporting and control of prevention activities are determined by the implementing regulations of the law in compliance with the rules on state aid.
Art. 6zh. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) (1) To a person who has suffered from domestic violence, the following specialised services are provided independently or as a complex:
1. a national helpline for assistance to persons who have suffered from domestic violence or are at risk;
2. a counselling centre for persons who have suffered from domestic violence;
3. a sheltered dwelling.
(2) The specialised services under para. 1 are provided according to the wish and personal choice of the persons who have suffered from domestic violence or are at risk, and are free of charge. The services are provided regardless of whether the victims have expressed consent to cooperate in identifying the perpetrator of the domestic violence.
(3) The use of a specialised service under para. 1 does not exclude the use of other services.
Art. 6z. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) To a person who has committed domestic violence, a specialised programme for overcoming aggression and anger management is provided. The programme may be part of a complex of specialised services or may be provided independently.
Art. 6i. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) When using a specialised service or programme under the procedure of this law, the persons under Art. 6zh and 6z are provided with appropriate and timely information in a language understandable to them.
Art. 6k. (New - SG, Issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The conditions, procedure and minimum standards for providing specialised services and programmes for protection against domestic violence under the procedure of this law, as well as their financing and control are regulated by the implementing regulations of the law.
Chapter Two.
PROCEEDINGS FOR IMPOSING MEASURES FOR PROTECTION AGAINST DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (TITLE AMENDED - SG, ISSUE 102 OF 2009, IN FORCE FROM 22.12.2009)

### Section I

General Provisions
Art. 7. (Amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The district court in the district where the victim actually resides at the time of filing the application is competent to impose a protection measure.
Art. 8. (1) (Amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, previous text of Art. 8, amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) Proceedings for issuing a protection order may be instituted upon application by:
1. (supplemented - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) the victim, if he/she has reached 14 years of age or has been placed under full or limited interdiction;
2. brother, sister or person who is related in a direct line to the victim;
3. (supplemented - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) the guardian or trustee of the victim, the person to whom care for the child has been entrusted, or the person to whom the child has been entrusted;
4. (amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) the director of the "Social Assistance" directorate, when the victim:
a) is a minor or juvenile;
b) is in a helpless condition as a result of severe disability, illness or old age or has been placed under interdiction.
(2) (New - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) When the victim cannot protect himself/herself due to a helpless condition or dependence on the perpetrator, the proceedings may also be instituted at the request of the prosecutor.
(3) (New - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) When the victim is a minor, has been placed under interdiction or is in a helpless condition as a result of severe disability, illness or old age, the court may decide to provide legal aid under the procedure of the Legal Aid Act.
Art. 9. (1) (Amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009) The application is in writing and contains:
1. (amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) the names, uniform civil number or other identification number and date of birth of the applicant; in cases where the person resides short-term on the territory of the country - also an address at which to be summoned; at the request of the applicant, his/her summons may be effected by telephone, electronic address for service, telex, fax or telegram;
2. (amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) the names and address at which the perpetrator may be summoned, as well as the telephone number;
3. data on the family, kinship or de facto relationship between the victim and the perpetrator;
4. (amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009) the date, place, manner and other facts and circumstances of the domestic violence committed;
5. signature.
(2) (Amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) In the cases under Art. 8, para. 1, items 2 - 4 and para. 2, the court ex officio constitutes the victim as a party.
(3) (Amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) To the application under Art. 8, para. 1, item 1, a declaration by the applicant regarding the violence committed may also be attached.
(4) (Amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The court ex officio requests for the parties certificates of criminal record and of measures imposed under this law and certificates whether they are registered on psychiatric record.
(5) (New - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The court also makes an inquiry regarding the family and/or kinship relationship between the parties.
(6) (New - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The court may also request ex officio other inquiries for the purposes of the proceedings.
Art. 10. (1) (Amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.08.2023) The application is filed within three months from the act of domestic violence, unless this is objectively impossible, but not later than 6 months.
(2) (New - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.08.2023) The time limit under para. 1 is not considered missed when the application to the court has been filed through the municipality, the mayoralty or the bodies of the Ministry of Internal Affairs.
(3) (Repealed, amended and supplemented - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, previous para. 2 - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.08.2023) The application is registered in a special register with separate sequential numbering (index) of cases and is assigned on the day of receipt.
Art. 11. (1) (Amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, supplemented - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) Upon filing an application under Art. 8, para. 1, no state fee is paid.
(2) Upon issuing the order, the court imposes the state fee and the costs of the case on the perpetrator of the domestic violence.
(3) (Amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) In case of refusal to issue an order or in case of revocation of the order, the state fee and the costs of the case are paid by the applicant, except when the application is for protection of persons who have not reached 18 years of age, as well as persons who are in a helpless condition as a result of severe disability, illness or old age or have been placed under interdiction.
Art. 11a. (New - SG, issue 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) When the proceedings are instituted at the request of the prosecutor, Art. 9 - 11 apply.

### Section II

Examination of the case
Art. 12. (1) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009) On the day of receipt of the application, the court schedules an open court hearing within a period not later than one month, and together with the summons and the copy of the application with the annexes, notifies the respondent of his obligation to present evidence.
(2) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) In the cases under Art. 8, para. 1, items 2 - 4 and para. 2, the victim is also summoned.
(3) Where necessary, the summons is served with the assistance of the police authorities or the mayor.
Art. 13. (1) (Supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) In the proceedings for issuing a protection order, the means of evidence under the Civil Procedure Code are admissible, and the court also collects evidence on its own initiative.
(2) Means of evidence in the proceedings under para. 1 may also be:
1. (supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) protocols, reports, risk assessments and other acts issued by the "Social Assistance" directorates, by the Ministry of Internal Affairs, by doctors, as well as by psychologists who have counselled the victim;
2. (amended - SG, No. 24 of 2019, effective 01.07.2020, amended regarding entry into force - SG, No. 101 of 2019, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) documents issued by providers of specialized services for protection from domestic violence.;
3. the declaration under Art. 9, para. 3.
(3) (Supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Where there is no other evidence for specific facts and circumstances, the court issues a protection order solely on the basis of the attached declaration under Art. 9, para. 3.
Art. 14. (1) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Where it is evident from the data in the application that the authorities of the Ministry of Internal Affairs and other state authorities, as well as the persons specified in Art. 13, para. 2, items 1 and 2, possess written evidence of committed domestic violence, they immediately issue certified copies thereof upon request by the victim, his representative or attorney, or at the request of the court.
(2) For failure to issue a document or copy of a document under para. 1, the person who was required to issue it is imposed a fine in the amount of 100 BGN by the court under the procedure of the Civil Procedure Code.
Art. 15. (Amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) (1) In the hearing for examination of the application, the court does not invite the parties to reconciliation and does not guide them towards mediation.
(2) When examining the case and rendering the decision, the court also takes into account acts of violence committed before the period under Art. 10, para. 1, if there is evidence for them, for the purpose of individualizing the protection measures under Art. 5, para. 1.
(3) If before the conclusion of the judicial examination in the first instance proceedings the respondent commits a new act of domestic violence against the applicant, the latter may file a supplementary application for protection, which the court examines within the framework of the initiated proceedings.
(4) The court performs a risk assessment according to a methodology defined in the implementing regulations of the law.
(5) Where in the course of the proceedings data is established for a committed crime of a general nature, the court sends copies of the materials containing such data to the respective prosecutor's office.
(6) The existence of a rendered decision by which a protection order has been issued is not an obstacle for the applicant to file a new application for protection against the same respondent in cases where the respondent has committed an act of domestic violence against the same applicant after the conclusion of the judicial examination in the first instance proceedings.
(7) The court pronounces its decision in an open hearing. In case of factual and legal complexity, the court may indicate the day on which it will announce its decision, from which day the period for appealing it runs.
(8) Upon granting the application, the court issues a protection order, which has effect for the period under Art. 5, para. 2.
(9) In cases of a pending court dispute between the parents regarding the exercise of parental rights, determination of the child's place of residence or the regime of personal relations, a copy of the immediate protection order and/or the protection order containing a measure under Art. 5, para. 1, item 5, is sent to the respective court, which within a one-week period from receipt of the copy of the order pronounces by ruling on the necessity of ex officio imposition of interim measures or amendment of already determined interim measures within the framework of the pending dispute.
(10) The issued protection order under para. 8 or the immediate protection order under Art. 18 and 19 is applied with priority over imposed interim measures or decisions in disputes concerning parental rights.
Art. 16. (1) With the protection order, the court imposes one or more protection measures.
(2) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) The order must contain the warning of the consequences of non-compliance also under Art. 21, para. 4 and 5.
(3) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Where a measure under Art. 5, para. 1, items 1 - 5 has been imposed, the decision and the order are served on the parties, and a copy thereof is also sent to the regional directorates of the Ministry of Internal Affairs at the current address and at the address of the perpetrator established in the course of the proceedings, as well as at the address indicated by the victim.
(4) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) In the cases under Art. 8, para. 1, item 4 and para. 2, the decision and the order are also served on the "Social Assistance" directorate with an instruction to monitor the case for a period not shorter than one year.
Art. 17. (1) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) The decision may be appealed before the regional court within a 7-day period from its pronouncement in an open hearing or from the day on which it is indicated that the decision will be announced. The appeal is filed through the court that rendered the decision, with a copy for the other party.
(2) (New - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009) Upon filing the appeal, no state fee is paid.
(3) (Previous para. 2 - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009) The appeal does not stay the execution of the order.
(4) (Previous para. 3 - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009) The district court sends a copy of the appeal with the annexes to the other party, who within a three-day period from their receipt may make objections, as well as indicate new evidence. After the expiry of this period, the appeal together with the annexes and objections is sent to the regional court.
(5) (Previous para. 4 - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009) The regional court examines the appeal within a 14-day period in an open hearing with summoning of the parties under the procedure of Art. 12 and pronounces a decision on the merits, by which it upholds, reverses or amends the appealed decision. Where it amends the order, the court issues a new order.
(6) (Previous para. 5 - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009) The decision of the regional court is final.
Art. 18. (1) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Where the application contains data of direct, immediate or subsequent danger to the life or health of the victim, the district court in a closed hearing without summoning the parties issues an immediate protection order within a period of up to 24 hours from receipt of the application. The immediate protection order is subject to immediate execution.
(2) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) The order under para. 1 is served on the parties. A copy of the order is sent ex officio, including by fax or electronic mail, to the regional directorates of the Ministry of Internal Affairs at the current address and at the address of the perpetrator established in the course of the proceedings, as well as at the address indicated by the victim.
(3) (Amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Where it is evident from the data in the case that it is necessary to take measures under the Child Protection Act, and upon imposition of measures under Art. 5, para. 1, items 6 - 8, the court notifies the director of the "Social Assistance" directorate at the current address and at the address of the perpetrator established in the course of the proceedings, as well as at the address indicated by the victim.
(4) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009) The court schedules an open court hearing within a period not later than one month, and together with the summons and the copy of the application with the annexes, notifies the respondent of his obligation to present evidence.
(5) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) In the cases under Art. 8, para. 1, items 2, 3 and 4 and para. 2, the victim is also summoned.
(6) Where necessary, the summons is served with the assistance of the police authorities or the mayor.
Art. 19. (Supplemented - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) (1) An immediate protection order is issued, amended or revoked at the request of the applicant/victim or ex officio by the respective district or regional court before which the case is pending, in any state of the case, regardless of a previously rendered refusal or revocation of an issued immediate protection order.
(2) The immediate protection order is not subject to appeal and has effect until the issuance of the protection order or until the pronouncement of the court's refusal to issue a protection order.
(3) Upon termination of the proceedings in the case, the issued immediate protection order is invalidated and the decision that has entered into force is sent to the regional directorates of the Ministry of Internal Affairs and to the "Social Assistance" directorate at the current address and at the address of the perpetrator established in the course of the proceedings, as well as at the address indicated by the victim.

### Section III

Enforcement of the protection order
Art. 20. The protection order is subject to immediate enforcement.
Art. 21. (1) (Amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Where a measure under Art. 5, para. 1, items 1 - 5 has been imposed, the police authorities shall monitor the enforcement of the order.
(2) (New - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Where a measure under Art. 5, para. 1, item 2 has been imposed, the perpetrator shall be removed from the jointly occupied dwelling with the assistance of the police authorities of the district directorate of the Ministry of Internal Affairs at the location of the dwelling.
(3) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Where a measure under Art. 5, para. 1, item 6 has been imposed, the legal entities implementing the programmes shall monitor the enforcement of the order.
(4) (Former para. 2 - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, former para. 3, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) In the cases under para. 1, where the court order is not complied with, the police authority that has established the violation shall detain the violator and immediately notify the prosecution authorities.
(5) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Where the imposed measure under Art. 5, para. 1, item 6 is not complied with, the legal entities implementing the programmes shall immediately notify the prosecution.
Art. 22. (Amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) For the awarded state fees and costs, the court shall issue an enforcement order ex officio.

Supplementary provisions

#### § 1

(New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) For the purposes of this Act:
1. "National helpline for assistance to persons affected by domestic violence or at risk, with a single harmonised telephone number" is a free telephone line with national coverage and a single harmonised telephone number for reporting cases of domestic violence, informing, counselling, referring persons affected by domestic violence or persons at risk, while respecting confidentiality and anonymity for the persons. The telephone line is accessible around the clock, seven days a week, every day of the year.
2. "Counselling centre for persons affected by domestic violence" is a stand-alone service or a complex of interdisciplinary specialised services for adults and/or children affected by domestic violence, which provides psychological, social and legal assistance and which is aimed at ensuring support for the victims simultaneously with or after the use of other specialised services.
3. "Sheltered housing" is a specialised service for the protection of adults and their children affected by domestic violence, which consists of temporary accommodation in a safe environment.
4. "Related persons" within the meaning of Art. 6a, para. 5 are:
a) persons in whose management body the same legal or natural person participates;
b) persons in whose supreme body the same legal or natural person participates, which can impose or prevent the taking of decisions;
c) persons, one of which is a member of the National Council and whose representative in the National Council is in a hierarchical dependence on the other by virtue of an employment relationship;
d) persons, one of which is a member of the National Council and has received remuneration from the other by virtue of a civil law relationship in a total amount exceeding BGN 10,000 during the last twelve months from the date of his or her election as a member of the National Council;
e) persons, one of which is a member of the National Council and has received a donation and/or grant funding from the other in a total amount exceeding BGN 10,000 during the last twelve months from the date of his or her election as a member of the National Council;
f) persons, one of which exercises control over the other;
g) persons whose activity is controlled by a third person or by its subsidiary legal entity.
5. "Control" within the meaning of letters e) and g) of item 4 exists when the controlling party:
a) holds directly or indirectly or by virtue of an agreement with another person more than half of the votes in the supreme body of another person, or
b) has the possibility to determine directly or indirectly more than half of the members of the management or supreme body of another person, or
c) can otherwise exercise decisive influence on decision-making in connection with the activity of the legal entity.
6. (new - SG, No. 69 of 2023) "Intimate relationship" represents a totality of voluntary and lasting personal, intimate and sexual relations between two natural persons of male and female sex, regardless of whether they share one household and whose emergence, content and termination are not the subject of legal regulation by another law. Lasting within the meaning of the preceding sentence are relations with a duration of at least 60 days.
