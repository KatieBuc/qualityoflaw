##### Article 6

Article 4 is modified, with the wording as follows:

Guarantees

##### Article 6

All women regardless of their age, ethnic origin, phenotypic traits, race, color, lineage, culture, nationality, citizenship, language, creed, ideology, political or philosophical affiliation, marital status, type of occupation, level of education, disability, pregnancy, place of birth, socioeconomic status, migratory status, health status, physical difference, sexual orientation, gender identity or gender expression, as well as any other personal or collective condition, temporary or permanent, shall have the necessary mechanisms to make effective the guarantees of the rights recognized in this Law:
1.	Information, comprehensive social care and free legal assistance to women victims of gender-based violence are the responsibility of the State.
2.	In the case of women in vulnerable situations, the Ministry of People's Power with competence in matters of women and gender equality, through the National Women's Institute, as well as regional and municipal institutes, must ensure that information is offered to them in an accessible and understandable format, in the Spanish language, indigenous languages, Venezuelan sign language and other modalities or communication options, including alternative and augmentative systems. In short, the necessary means shall be articulated so that women in situations of gender-based violence who due to their personal and social circumstances may have greater difficulty in comprehensive access to information, have guaranteed the effective exercise of this right.
3.	Women victims of gender-based violence have the right to social services of care, emergency, protection, support, shelter and comprehensive recovery. In each state and municipality such services shall be created, charged to the annual budget. The care provided by such services must be free, of quality, permanent, urgent, specialized and professionally multidisciplinary and shall be financed by the State.
4.	The services listed in the preceding paragraph shall act in coordination and collaboration with citizen security bodies, the justice system, health services and the National Ombudsman for Women's Rights. Girls, boys and adolescents who are under the parental authority or care responsibility of women victims of violence shall also have the right to comprehensive social care through these social services.
5.	The Ministry of People's Power with competence in matters of women and gender equality, the National Women's Institute and the state and municipal women's institutes, as well as other associations, organizations including community organizations that promote the defense of women's human rights, shall guide and evaluate the plans, projects, programs and actions that are executed and shall issue recommendations for their improvement and effectiveness.
6.	The Ombudsman's Office, the Ministry of People's Power with competence in matters of women and gender equality, through the National Women's Institute, and the state and municipal institutes shall ensure the correct application of this Law and the instruments derived from it. It shall correspond to the National Ombudsman for Women's Rights and to the state, municipal and communal ombudsmen to ensure respect for and effective exercise of the right to justice of women victims of gender-based violence, ensuring that they are provided with the necessary services that guarantee the effectiveness of the rights enshrined herein. This right shall also assist the successors in case of death of the assaulted woman.
7.	The bar associations of female and male lawyers, female and male doctors, female and male psychologists and female and male nurses of the different states must establish free comprehensive specialized advisory services to women victims of gender-based violence.
8.	Female workers in situations of gender-based violence shall have the right to the reduction or reorganization of their working time, to be geographically mobilized or to change their workplace. If their condition requires a work suspension, it must be accredited with the protection order of the female or male judge, upon report and request of the Public Prosecutor's Office, the accreditation of indications being sufficient.
9.	The State shall develop public policies directed at women victims of gender-based violence who lack employment, and may be inserted in training programs, missions and projects for employment and entrepreneurship, as permitted by the physical and psychological conditions in which they find themselves. If the assaulted women have an officially recognized disability that prevents or hinders access to employment, they shall receive special attention that allows their labor insertion and training. For this purpose, programs, projects and missions shall be established. The State shall create tax exemptions for companies, cooperatives and other entities that promote employment, insertion and reinsertion in the labor and productive market of women victims of gender-based violence.

10.	Women victims of gender-based violence shall have priority for aid and assistance created by the national, state or municipal public administration.
11.	Women victims of gender-based violence shall have priority in access to housing, land, credit and technical assistance in government plans.

Security and protection measures and precautionary measures

##### Article 11

Security and protection measures, and precautionary measures are those imposed by the competent authority designated in this Law, to safeguard life, protect the dignity, physical, psychological, sexual integrity and patrimonial assets of women victims of violence. Suitability, necessity, urgency, proportionality and purpose are essential elements for their granting, and the mandate to guarantee the right of women to a life free of violence must prevail.

##### Article 8

Article 10 is modified, the wording remaining in the following terms:

Supremacy and public order Article 12. The provisions of this Law shall be of preferential application. The respect, guarantee and protection of the human rights of women is a matter of public order and general interest. Consequently, all female and male public servants must act ex officio in situations of threat or violation of the human rights of women, without the request, intervention or initiative of the interested persons being necessary.
In case of doubt in the interpretation or application of this Law, the one that most favors the protection of the human rights of women victims of gender-based violence and their families shall be adopted.

##### Article 11

Numerals 3, 5, 6, 7, 11 are modified and 4 additional numerals are added to article 15, the wording being as follows:
Forms of violence Article 19. The following are considered forms of gender-based violence against women:
3. Threat: It is the verbal, written or other acts executed by any means, including media and information and communication technologies, to execute psychological, sexual, labor, patrimonial or physical harm, with the purpose of intimidating the woman, both in the public and private spheres.

5.	Family violence: Any active or omissive conduct, constant or not, of use of physical force or psychological violence, intimidation, harassment, stalking, persecution, humiliation or

threat against the woman by the person with whom she maintains or maintained marriage, de facto stable union or affective relationship, with or without cohabitation, ascendants, descendants, collateral relatives, consanguineous and by affinity, whether exercised by themselves or by third parties. Family violence shall also be understood as any act of violence, actions or omissions that affect family members who are ascendants, descendants and collateral consanguineous and by affinity, and persons in the care of women, that have the purpose of causing conditions of violence, fear, harm, breaking the will of the woman and even her death.
6.	Sexual violence: Any conduct that threatens or violates the right of the woman to decide voluntarily and freely her sexuality, this comprising not only the sexual act, but any form of contact or sexual access, genital or non-genital, such as sexual violence, sexual act with an especially vulnerable victim and sexual abuse without penetration.
7.	Sexual violence in the couple relationship: It is a form of sexual violence, in which through violence or threats the spouse, person with whom a de facto stable union or affective relationship is maintained is constrained to an act that constitutes sexual violence.
11. Labor violence: It is all discrimination, threat, harassment and stalking toward the woman based on gender in workplaces, public or private, that obstruct her access to employment, promotion or stability therein or generate a hostile work environment, such as requiring requirements regarding
marital status, age, physical appearance or good presence, or the request for results of clinical laboratory tests that make the hiring, promotion or permanence of the woman in employment conditional. Gender discrimination in the labor sphere also constitutes the violation of the right to equal pay for equal work or failure to grant the respective permits so that women victims of violence can comply with the activities that require their presence in accordance with what is established in this Law.
18.	Information technology violence: It is any act that involves as a means for the commission of a crime of violence against women the use of information and communication technologies, through the use or dissemination of audiovisual material, images, data and any other information of a woman to exercise psychological violence, harassment, stalking, sexual harassment, media violence, symbolic, political or any other form of violence.
19.	Political violence: It is any act that, through coercion, threat or any other form of violence, limits, diminishes or makes impossible the free exercise of political participation of women in conditions of equality and equity in positions of popular election, in the exercise of public office and in organizations with political, union, educational, cultural, sports, professional purposes, community associations, social movements and popular power.

##### Article 30

The Ministry of Popular Power with competence in matters of interior relations, justice and peace shall provide what is necessary for the execution of continuous, permanent and mandatory training plans and programs for public servants directly involved in the application of this Law, with special attention to those who have responsibility in receiving complaints of violence against women, girls and adolescents based on gender. 5.	Communication: for the dissemination, information and promotion of women's human rights, including the right to live free from all forms of violence based on gender.
6.	Productive: to guarantee measures that promote the prioritization of financing, empowerment and economic autonomy of women victims of violence, as well as their labor insertion in applicable cases.

##### Article 38

The National Ombudsman for Women's Rights is the technical and specialized instance attached to the National Women's Institute, whose function is the defense of women's right to a life free of violence, through guidance, legal assistance and representation of women victims of violence based on gender and their families, in judicial and extrajudicial processes to ensure justiciability and

Sexual Violence

##### Article 57

Whoever by the use of violence or threats compels a woman to consent to an unwanted sexual act that includes penetration by vaginal, anal or oral route, even through the introduction of objects of any kind through any of these routes, shall be punished with imprisonment of twelve to eighteen years.

If the victim turns out to be a girl or adolescent, daughter of the woman with whom the perpetrator maintains or maintained marriage, common-law union or affective relationship, even without cohabitation, the penalty shall be increased from one quarter to one third.

The purpose of this Law is to guarantee and promote the right of women to a life free of violence in the public and private spheres, creating conditions to prevent, address, sanction and eradicate violence against them in any of its forms and spheres, rooted in systemic discrimination against women especially when they are in situations of vulnerability, promoting changes in the sociocultural patterns that sustain unequal power relations over women, to favor the construction of a just, democratic, participatory, equal, leading and violence-free society.

Gratuity: Requests, petitions, claims and other actions relating to the matters referred to in this Law, as well as certified copies issued thereof, shall be made on plain paper and without stamps. Female and male officials of the public powers who intervene in any way shall process them with all preference and may not charge any emolument or fee whatsoever.

2.	Celerity: The bodies receiving complaints, auxiliary to the administration of justice in the terms of article 114 of the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure and the competent courts, shall give preference to the knowledge and processing of the facts provided for in this Law, without any delay, within the time periods provided for therein, under warning of the administrative measure corresponding to the female or male official who has received the complaint.
3.	Immediacy: The female or male judge who is to pronounce the sentence must be present at the hearing and the incorporation of the evidence from which he or she obtains conviction, except in cases where the law permits the judicial commission for the evacuation of some evidentiary means necessary for the demonstration of the controverted facts, the results of which shall be debated at the trial hearing. The evidence contained in the file duly incorporated at the hearing shall be assessed.
4.	Confidentiality: Female and male officials of the bodies receiving complaints, of the care and treatment units, and of the competent courts, must maintain the confidentiality of the matters submitted for their consideration.
5.	Orality: Proceedings shall be oral and only the written forms provided for in this Law and in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure shall be admitted.
6.	Concentration: Once the hearing has begun, it must conclude on the same day. If this is not possible, it shall continue for the fewest number of consecutive days.

7.	Publicity: The trial shall be public, unless at the request of the woman victim of violence the court decides that it be held totally or partially behind closed doors, and the woman must be informed in advance and in a timely manner that she may exercise this right.
8.	Protection of victims: Victims of the punishable acts described herein have the right to access specialized civil and criminal justice bodies free of charge, expeditiously, without undue delays or useless formalities, without prejudice to the rights of accused or charged persons. The protection of the victim and the reparation of the damage to which she is entitled shall also be the objective of the procedure provided for herein.
Medidas de seguridad y protección y medidas cautelares

##### Article 11

Security and protection measures, and precautionary measures are those imposed by the competent authority designated in this Law, to safeguard the life, protect the dignity, physical, psychological, sexual integrity and patrimonial assets of women victims of violence. Suitability, necessity, urgency, proportionality and purpose are essential elements for their granting, and the mandate to guarantee the right of women to a life free of violence must prevail.
Supremacy and public order

##### Article 12

The provisions of this Law shall be of preferential application. The respect, guarantee and protection of the human rights of women is a matter of public order and general interest. Consequently, all public servants must act ex officio in situations of threat or violation of the human rights of women, without the request, intervention or initiative of the interested persons being necessary.
In case of doubt in the interpretation or application of this Law, the one that most favors the protection of the human rights of women victims of gender-based violence and their families shall be adopted.

Prohibition of mediation and conciliation Article 13. For the purposes of guaranteeing the rights of women victims of gender-based violence, the following are prohibited:
1.	The exposure of women victims of violence and their families to situations of incomprehension or unnecessary reiterations that subject them to a new process of victimization.
2.	The application of mediation, conciliation and other alternative means of conflict resolution in proceedings of violence against women based on gender, in cases involving violation or threat to the right to life and physical integrity. In other situations, these means may exceptionally be employed provided that a prior evaluation by a multidisciplinary team guarantees the free and informed consent of the victims and there are no indicators of new risks for the victims or their families. Under no circumstances shall violence against women based on gender be mandatorily referred to alternative conflict resolution procedures nor shall it constitute an obstacle to their effective access to justice.
3.	The use of victims and their families to carry out notifications and summons provided for in this Law.
Public servants who fail to comply with this provision shall be sanctioned for the commission of the crime of institutional violence.

Jurisdiction Article 14. In all crimes provided for in this Law, no special jurisdiction shall be recognized, except those expressly contained in the Constitution of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela and laws of the Republic.

Preeminence of the special procedure Article 15. The trial of the crimes dealt with in this Law shall be conducted by the special procedure provided herein.
Competence Article 16. The Courts with competence in matters of Crimes of Violence against Women are competent to hear the applications, actions, appeals and crimes contemplated in this Law and by reference in other laws. Likewise, they are competent to hear those crimes contained in other organic, special or general laws by connection, connexity or real concurrence with the matter of which they have knowledge, regardless of whether women and men victims concur.
The Supreme Court of Justice shall ensure the existence of specialized courts in border areas and areas of difficult access.
Intervention of interdisciplinary team Article 17. In the reception of complaints and in the procedural investigation of the facts dealt with in this Law, duly sensitized, aware and trained personnel in gender violence shall be used. The respective offices shall be equipped with waiting rooms for accused persons, separate from those intended for victims.

### Chapter III

Definition and forms of violence against women
Definition Article 18. Violence against women referred to in this Law comprises any sexist act that has or may have as a result death, physical, sexual, psychological, emotional, labor, economic or patrimonial harm or suffering; coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, as well as the threat of executing such acts, whether they occur in the public or private sphere.
Forms of violence Article 19. The following are considered forms of gender-based violence against women:

1. Psychological violence: Any active or omissive conduct exercised in dishonor, discredit or contempt of personal value or dignity, humiliating and degrading treatment, constant surveillance, isolation, marginalization, negligence, abandonment, pathological jealousy, destructive comparisons, threats and acts that lead women victims of violence to diminish their self-esteem, to harm or disturb their healthy development, to depression and even to suicide.
2. Harassment or stalking: Any abusive conduct and especially behaviors, words, acts, gestures, writings or electronic messages directed at pursuing, intimidating, blackmailing, pressuring, bothering and monitoring a woman that may threaten her emotional stability, dignity, prestige, physical or psychological integrity, or that may endanger her employment, promotion, recognition in the workplace or outside it.
3. Threat: It is the verbal, written or other acts executed by any means, including media and information and communication technologies, to execute psychological, sexual, labor, patrimonial or physical harm, with the purpose of intimidating the woman, both in the public and private sphere.
4. Physical violence: Any action or omission that directly or indirectly is directed at causing physical harm or suffering to the woman, such as internal or external injuries, wounds, bruises, burns, pushing or any other mistreatment that affects her physical integrity.
5. Family violence: Any active or omissive conduct, constant or not, of use of physical force or psychological violence, intimidation, harassment, stalking, persecution, humiliation or threat against the woman by the person with whom she maintains or maintained marriage, common-law stable union or affective relationship, with or without cohabitation, ascendants, descendants, collateral relatives, consanguineous and by affinity, whether exercised by themselves or by third parties. Family violence shall also be understood as any act of violence, actions or omissions that affect ascendant, descendant and collateral consanguineous and affinity relatives, and persons in the care of women, that have as their purpose

6. Sexual violence: Any conduct that threatens or violates the right of the woman to decide voluntarily and freely her sexuality, this comprising not only the sexual act, but any form of contact or sexual access, genital or non-genital, such as sexual violence, sexual act with especially vulnerable victim and sexual abuse without penetration.
7. Sexual violence in the couple relationship: It is a form of sexual violence, in which through violence or threats the spouse, person with whom a stable common-law union or affective relationship is maintained is constrained to an act that constitutes sexual violence.
8. Forced prostitution: It is the action of forcing a woman to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature by force or by threat of force, or by coercion such as that caused by fear of violence, intimidation, psychological oppression or abuse of power, expecting to obtain or having obtained pecuniary or other material advantages or benefits, in exchange for the acts of a sexual nature of the woman.
9. Sexual slavery: It is the illegitimate deprivation of liberty of the woman, for her sale, purchase, loan or exchange with the obligation to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature.
10. Sexual harassment: Request for any act or behavior of sexual content, for oneself or for a third party, or seeking any type of unwanted sexual approach made by a man taking advantage of a situation of labor, teaching or similar superiority, or on the occasion of relationships derived from professional practice, and with the express or tacit threat of causing the woman harm related to the legitimate expectations that she may have in the sphere of said relationship.
11. Labor violence: It is all discrimination, threat, harassment and stalking toward the woman for reasons of gender in workplaces, public or private, that obstruct her access to employment, promotion or stability therein or generate a hostile work environment, such as requiring requirements regarding marital status, age, physical appearance or good presence, or the request for results of clinical laboratory tests, that make the hiring, promotion or permanence of the woman in employment conditional. It also constitutes gender discrimination in the labor sphere to violate the right of equal pay for equal work or not to grant the respective permits so that women victims of violence can comply with the activities that require their presence in accordance with what is established in this Law.
12. Patrimonial and economic violence: Any active or omissive conduct that directly or indirectly, in the public and private spheres, is directed at causing harm to movable or immovable property to the detriment of the patrimony of women victims of violence or to common property, as well

13. Obstetric violence: It is the appropriation of the body and reproductive processes of women by health personnel, which is expressed in dehumanizing treatment, in an abuse of medicalization and pathologization of natural processes, bringing with it loss of autonomy and capacity to decide freely about their bodies and sexuality, negatively impacting the quality of life of women.

21. 22. Trafficking of women, girls and adolescents: All acts that involve their recruitment or transport within or across borders, using deception, coercion or force, with the purpose of obtaining a financial or other material benefit of an illicit nature.
23. 24. Femicide: It is the extreme form of gender-based violence, caused by hatred or contempt for her condition as a woman, that degenerates into her death, produced both in the public and private sphere.
25. Inducement or assistance to suicide: It is the extreme consequence of psychological violence, harassment, stalking and threat that generate the conditions to cause the death of a woman for gender motivations.

### Chapter V

Women victims of violence
Attention to women victims of violence

##### Article 41

The agencies receiving complaints must grant women victims of acts of violence provided for in this Law dignified treatment with respect and support in accordance with their condition as affected persons, seeking to facilitate to the maximum their participation in the procedures in which they must intervene.
Consequently, they must:
1. Advise women victims of violence on the importance of preserving evidence.
2. Provide assaulted women with information about the rights conferred upon them by this Law and about governmental or non-governmental services available for their attention and treatment.

3. Prepare a report on those circumstances that serve to clarify the facts, which must accompany the complaint.
4. Any other information that the receiving agencies consider important to point out to the woman in a situation of violence for her protection.
5. Others established in the regulations, resolutions and protocols derived from this Law.

In each federal entity a unit for attention to women victims of gender-based violence shall be created, which shall function as a specialized, interinstitutional and multidisciplinary unit for receiving complaints of crimes provided for in this Law, without prejudice to the powers corresponding to each agency receiving complaints. These units shall have the participation of the competent agencies and entities in matters of prevention, attention, punishment and eradication of violence against women. The unit for attention to women victims of gender-based violence may be created at the municipal and communal level in cases where necessary.
Labor rights

##### Article 42

Female workers or public servants who are victims of gender-based violence shall have the right, under the terms provided in the respective laws, to the reduction or reorganization of their working time, to geographical mobility, to change of workplace, to suspension of the employment relationship with reservation of position and to leave of absence under the terms to be determined. Total or partial absences from work, motivated by the physical or psychological condition derived from gender-based violence suffered by female workers or public servants, shall be considered justified when so determined by public or private health care centers, under the terms provided in the respective legislation.

Certificate of physical and mental health

##### Article 43

Victims, before or after filing a complaint, may go to a public or private health institution so that the female or male physician, without the need for swearing in as an expert, may make the diagnosis and record, through a report, the condition of physical and mental health, the characteristics of the injury, the healing time and the disability it causes. In the special procedure for violence and for the purposes of preventing the disappearance of physical evidence, this medical report shall have the same evidentiary value as the forensic examination. To this end, the Public Prosecutor's Office and the courts shall consider for all legal purposes the medical reports on physical and mental health issued under the terms of this article for the adoption of the decision corresponding to each agency. The omission of this obligation by the female or male physician or the health institution in the diagnosis, issuance and timely delivery of the report shall be punished with the crime of institutional violence established in this Law. Public and private health establishments must safeguard the adequate obtaining, preservation and documentation of evidence of acts of violence.
Free legal assistance

##### Article 44

In those cases where the victim lacks legal assistance, she may request from the competent female or male judge the appointment of a female or male legal professional, who shall properly guide her and exercise the defense of her rights from the initial acts of the investigation. For such purposes, the court shall make the selection from existing female or male lawyers from the National Ombudsperson's Office for Women's Rights, from state and municipal ombudsperson's offices, from bar associations of each jurisdiction or from any public or private organization dedicated to the defense of the rights established in this Law.
Intervention in the procedure

##### Article 45

The aggrieved person, the National Ombudsperson for Women's Rights and the social organizations referred to in item six of Article 73 of this Law, may intervene in the proceedings even if they have not been constituted as complainants.
Request for simple and certified copies

##### Article 46

Women victims of gender-based violence or their successors in interest may request directly before any instance, without the presence of a legal professional, simple or certified copies of all proceedings in administrative and jurisdictional procedures. The request for simple or certified copies must be processed and granted expeditiously, except in the case of confidential proceedings. Likewise, each institution must provide mechanisms to ensure effective access to justice for women victims or their successors in interest in this type of procedure.

### Chapter VII Offenses

Psychological violence

##### Article 53

Whoever, through humiliating and degrading treatment, offenses, isolation, permanent surveillance, destructive comparisons or constant generic threats, attacks the emotional or psychological stability of a woman, shall be punished with imprisonment of six to eighteen months.

Harassment or stalking

##### Article 54

The person who, through behaviors, verbal or written expressions, or electronic messages, executes acts of intimidation, blackmail, harassment or stalking that attack the emotional, labor, economic, family or educational stability of a woman, shall be punished with imprisonment of eight to twenty months.
Threat

##### Article 55

Any person who, through verbal expressions, writings or electronic messages, threatens a woman with causing her serious and probable harm of a physical, psychological, sexual, labor or patrimonial nature, shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to twenty-two months.
If the threat or act of violence is carried out at the domicile or residence of the woman object of violence, the penalty shall be increased by one third to one half.

If the perpetrator of the crime is a public official belonging to any police or military body, the penalty shall be increased by one half.
If the act is committed with bladed or firearms, the imprisonment shall be two to four years.
Physical violence

##### Article 56

Whoever, through the use of physical force, causes harm or physical suffering to a woman, bruises, slaps, shoves or injuries of a minor, very minor or less serious nature, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to two years.

If by the commission of the crime, the victim suffers serious injuries, as provided in the Penal Code, he shall be punished with imprisonment of four to six years.
If by the commission of the crime, the victim suffers very serious injuries, as provided in the Penal Code, he shall be punished with a penalty of five to ten years of imprisonment.
If the person who commits the crime provided in this article is the person with whom the victim maintains or maintained marriage, de facto stable union or affective relationship, even without cohabitation, ascendants, descendants, collateral relatives, consanguineous and related by affinity to the victim, the penalty shall be increased by one third to one half.
Jurisdiction to hear the crime of injuries as provided in this article shall correspond to the Courts with jurisdiction in Matters of Crimes of Violence against Women, according to the special procedure provided in this Law.
Sexual violence

##### Article 57

Whoever, through the use of violence or threats, compels a woman to consent to an unwanted sexual act that includes penetration by vaginal, anal or oral route, even through the introduction of objects of any kind through any of these routes, shall be punished with imprisonment of twelve to eighteen years.
If the person who commits the crime is the person with whom the victim maintains or maintained marriage, de facto stable union or affective relationship, even without cohabitation, ascendants, descendants, collateral relatives, consanguineous and related by affinity to the victim, the penalty shall be increased by one fourth to one third.
If the act is executed to the detriment of a girl or adolescent, the penalty shall be twenty to twenty-five years of imprisonment.
If the victim turns out to be a girl or adolescent, daughter of the woman with whom the perpetrator maintains or maintained marriage, de facto stable union or affective relationship, even without cohabitation, the penalty shall be increased by one fourth to one third.
If the act is executed collectively, all persons who participated, whether men or women, the penalty shall be increased to the maximum limit.
The crime provided in this article is committed by whoever executes the punishable act by taking advantage of threats related to the dissemination of audiovisual material, images, messages through information and communication technologies that may affect the dignity, honor and reputation of the victim.

Sexual act with especially vulnerable victim

##### Article 58

The crime provided in the preceding article is committed and shall be punished with imprisonment of twenty to twenty-five years, whoever executes the sexual act, even without violence or threats, in the following cases:
1.	To the detriment of a vulnerable woman, by reason of her age or in any case under thirteen years of age.
2.	When the perpetrator has taken advantage of his relationship of superiority or kinship with the victim, whose age is under sixteen years.

Sexual slavery

##### Article 61

Whoever illegally deprives a woman of her liberty for purposes of sexually exploiting her through purchase, sale, loan, barter or other analogous transaction, forcing her to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature, shall be punished with imprisonment of twenty to twenty-five years.
Sexual harassment

##### Article 62

Whoever requests from a woman an act or behavior of sexual content for himself or for a third party, verbally or physically seeks an unwanted sexual approach or makes
insinuations or observations of a sexual type, including the exhibition of pornography, taking advantage of a situation of labor or teaching superiority or on the occasion of relationships derived from professional practice, with the threat of causing her harm related to the legitimate expectations she may have in the sphere of said relationship, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to seven years.
With the same penalty shall be punished whoever commits the conduct described in this article when the woman has sufficient reasons to believe that her refusal could cause her problems in relation to her work, including with hiring or promotion or when it generates a hostile work environment.
Labor violence

##### Article 63

The person who, through the establishment of requirements referring to sex, age, physical appearance, marital status, condition of being a mother or not, submission to laboratory tests or of another kind to rule out state of pregnancy, obstructs or

conditions access, promotion or stability in the employment of women, shall be punished with between one hundred (100) and one thousand (1,000) times the official exchange rate of the currency of greatest value, published by the Central Bank of Venezuela, according to the seriousness of the act, without prejudice to the civil and administrative actions to which there may be grounds.

If it concerns an employment policy of a public institution or State enterprise, the sanction shall be imposed on its highest authority. In the case of private companies, franchises or transnational companies, the sanction shall be imposed on whoever exercises the highest representation in the country.
The same sanction shall apply when, through administrative, deceptive or fraudulent practices, the right to legal and fair salary of the female worker or the right to equal pay for equal work is affected.
Patrimonial and economic violence

##### Article 64

The legally separated spouse or the person in a stable union in a situation of duly verified de facto separation, who removes, damages, destroys, diverts, retains, orders the blocking of bank accounts or performs acts capable of affecting the community property or the woman's own patrimony, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years.
The same penalty shall apply in the event that there is no legal separation, but the perpetrator has been subjected to the protective measure of removal from the home by a complaint receiving body or to a similar precautionary measure by the competent control, hearing and measures court.
In the event that the acts referred to in this article are intentionally directed at depriving the woman of the economic means indispensable for her subsistence or preventing her from satisfying her needs and those of the family unit, the penalty shall be increased by one third to one half.
If the person who commits the offense referred to in this article, without being a spouse or person in a de facto stable union, maintains or maintained a relationship of affection with the woman victim, even without cohabitation, the penalty shall be six to twelve months of imprisonment.
In the cases referred to in this article, reparatory agreements may be concluded in accordance with the provisions of the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure.
Political violence

##### Article 65

Whoever by means of coercion, threat or any other form of violence, limits, undermines or prevents the free exercise of political participation of women in conditions of equality and equity in positions of popular election, in the exercise of public office and in organizations with political, union, educational, cultural, sports, professional purposes, community associations, social movements and popular power shall be punished with imprisonment of three to eight years.
Obstetric violence

##### Article 66

The following acts executed by health personnel shall be considered constitutive acts of obstetric violence:

Information Technology Violence

##### Article 68

Whoever uses information technologies as a means for the commission of the crimes of psychological violence, harassment, stalking, sexual harassment, media violence, symbolic, political or multicausal violence, shall be punished with an increase of one third of the penalty corresponding to the crime.
Public offense for reasons of gender

##### Article 69

The communication professional or whoever without being one exercises any trade related to that discipline, and in the exercise of that trade or occupation, offends, insults or denigrates a woman for reasons of gender through a means of communication, must compensate the woman victim of violence with the payment of a sum not less than two hundred (200) nor greater than five hundred (500) times the official exchange rate of the currency of highest value, published by the Central Bank of Venezuela and make public his or her

apologies through the same medium used to make the offense and with the same extension of time and space.
Institutional violence Article 70. Whoever in the exercise of public office, regardless of rank, delays, obstructs, denies due attention or prevents the woman from accessing the right to timely response in the institution to which she goes, for the purposes of processing any procedure related to the rights guaranteed by this Law, shall be punished with a fine between fifty
(50) and one hundred fifty (150) times the official exchange rate of the currency of highest value, published by the Central Bank of Venezuela, without prejudice to the civil and administrative actions that may arise.
The competent court shall send a certified copy of the final conviction to the body to which the guilty person is assigned, for the purposes of the corresponding disciplinary procedure.
Trafficking of women, girls and adolescents

##### Article 71

Whoever promotes, induces, constrains, favors, facilitates, executes, finances or collaborates in any way, in the displacement, transfer or transport by land, sea or air, of women, girls and adolescents, to procure illegal entry or exit from the country, whether as destination or origin or as transit to another country, without compliance with legal requirements, with the purpose of obtaining, directly or indirectly, a financial benefit or other material benefit for himself or herself or a third party, shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to fifteen years.
Trafficking of women, girls and adolescents

##### Article 72

Whoever promotes, induces, favors, facilitates, executes, finances or solicits the recruitment, transport, transfer, harboring or
reception of women, girls and adolescents, by resorting to the threat or use of force or other forms of coercion, abduction, fraud, deception, abuse of power or of a situation of vulnerability or the giving or receiving of payments or benefits to obtain the consent of a person having authority over women, girls or adolescents, for purposes of sexual exploitation, prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, servitude, irregular adoption or organ removal shall be punished with imprisonment of twenty to twenty-five years.
If the victim is a girl or adolescent the penalty shall be twenty-five to thirty years.
Femicide

##### Article 73

Whoever intentionally causes the death of a woman motivated by hatred or contempt for the condition of being a woman, commits the crime of femicide and shall be punished with a sentence of twenty to twenty-five years of imprisonment.
Hatred or contempt for the condition of being a woman is considered to exist when any of the following circumstances occur:

Obligation to implement corrective measures

##### Article 78

Any hierarchical authority in employment centers, education or of any other nature, who, having knowledge of acts of sexual harassment by persons under his or her responsibility, does not execute adequate actions to correct the situation and prevent its repetition, shall be punished with a fine of one thousand five hundred (1,500) to five thousand (5,000) times the official exchange rate of the currency of greatest value, published by the Central Bank of Venezuela.
The competent specialized jurisdictional body shall assess for purposes of imposing the fine, the seriousness of the facts and the diligence applied in correcting them.
Recidivism

##### Article 79

Recidivism shall be considered to exist when after a final conviction or after the sentence has been extinguished, the person commits a new punishable act of those provided for in this Law.

Whoever is found responsible for the crime of sexual harassment must compensate the woman victim, in the following terms:

### Chapter IX Common provisions

Jurisdiction, special procedure and supplementary application

##### Article 83

The specialized courts on violence against women have jurisdiction to hear acts of violence in which the victim is a woman, in order to determine whether there is commission of any of the crimes provided for in this Law, including femicide and inducement or assistance to
suicide, in accordance with the special procedure provided for in this Law.

The provisions of the Penal Code and Organic Code of Criminal Procedure shall apply supplementarily, insofar as they do not conflict with those provided herein.
Aggravating circumstances

##### Article 84

The following shall be aggravating circumstances of the offenses provided for in this Law, giving rise to an increase in penalty from one third to one half:

1. Entering the residence of the assaulted woman or the place where she lives, when the conjugal or marital relationship of the woman victim of violence with the accused is in a situation of de facto or de jure separation, or when the marriage has been dissolved by final judgment.
2. Entering the residence of the woman victim of violence or the place where she lives, by means of the bond of consanguinity or affinity.
3. Executing it with weapons, objects or instruments.
4. Executing it to the detriment of a pregnant woman.
5. Executing it in a gang or with a group of persons.
6. If the perpetrator of the offense were a public official, in the exercise of his or her functions.

7. Perpetrating it to the detriment of especially vulnerable persons, with physical or mental disability.
8. That the accused has been sanctioned with a final judgment for the commission of any of the offenses provided for in this Law.
9. Intentionally transmitting to the woman victim of gender-based violence, infections or diseases that put her health at risk.
10. Performing actions that deprive the victim of the capacity to discern, as a consequence of the use of fraudulent means or narcotic or stimulating substances.
11. Executing it by reason of the real or presumed membership of the victim in a certain ethnic group, phenotypic features, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression or for any other discriminatory reason.
12. Having committed the forms and offenses of violence in the presence of daughters and sons, consanguineous relatives, relatives by affinity and close persons.
Accessory penalties

##### Article 85

In the conviction judgment, the accessory penalties that are applicable in each case shall be expressly established, according to the nature of the facts subject to conviction. The following are accessory penalties:
1. Political disqualification during the time of the conviction.
2. Subjection to the surveillance of the authority for one fifth of the time of the conviction from the time it ends.
3. Definitive deprivation of the possession and carrying of weapons, notwithstanding that his or her profession, position or occupation is police, military or security.
4. Suspension or temporary removal from office or exercise of the profession, when the offense has been committed in the exercise of his or her functions or on the occasion thereof, a certified copy of the judgment must be sent to the administrative labor file and to the corresponding professional association, if applicable.

5. Extinction of parental authority, in cases of femicide, sexual violence, sexual violence with an especially vulnerable victim and sexual abuse without penetration.
6. Extinction of the community property and deprivation of the share that would correspond to him or her as inheritance in cases of femicide, granting these rights of the convicted person to the daughters and sons or ascendants of the victim, if any.
Guidance programs

##### Article 86

Those who are found guilty of acts of violence against women must participate mandatorily in guidance, care and prevention programs aimed at modifying their violent behaviors and avoiding recidivism. The conviction judgment shall establish the modality and duration, according to the limits of the penalty imposed.

### Chapter X

Initiation of the process

#### First Section Complaint

Standing to file a complaint

##### Article 89. The offenses referred to in this Law may be reported by

1. The assaulted woman.
2. Consanguineous or affinity relatives.
3. Health personnel of public and private institutions who have knowledge of the cases of violence provided for in this Law.
4. Women's rights ombudsman offices at the national, metropolitan, state and municipal levels, attached to the national, metropolitan, regional and municipal institutes, respectively.
5. Communal councils and other social organizations.
6. Organizations defending women's rights.
7. Any other person or institution that has knowledge of the punishable acts provided for in this Law.
Bodies receiving complaints

##### Article 90

The complaint of crimes of violence against women may be filed orally, in writing or in Venezuelan sign language, through any means, with or without the assistance of a female or male lawyer, before any of the following bodies:
1. Public Prosecutor's Office.
2. Communal Peace Courts.
3. Prefectures and civil headquarters.
4. Corps of Scientific, Criminal and Criminalistics Investigations.
5. National, state and municipal police bodies.
6. Border command units of the Bolivarian National Armed Force.
7. Municipal courts in localities where the aforementioned bodies do not exist.
8. Any other to which this competence is attributed.
Each of the aforementioned bodies must create offices with specialized personnel for the reception of complaints of the acts of violence referred to in this Law.
Indigenous peoples and communities shall constitute complaint receiving bodies, composed of legitimate authorities in accordance with their customs and traditions, without prejudice to the assaulted woman being able to go to the other bodies indicated in this article.

Obligations of the body receiving the complaint

##### Article 91. The complaint-receiving body must

1. Receive the complaint, which may be presented orally, in writing or in Venezuelan sign language, by any means.
2. Order the necessary and urgent proceedings, among others, the practice of the corresponding medical examinations of the assaulted woman in public or private health centers of the locality.
3. Provide timely guidance to the woman in a situation of gender-based violence verbally or in writing.
4. Order the mandatory appearance of the alleged aggressor, for the purposes of the corresponding statement and other necessary proceedings that allow the clarification of the reported facts.
5. Impose protection and security measures adapted to each case that prevent new acts of violence, without any type of limitations, in a manner that guarantees the life and other human rights of women.
6. Form the respective file.
7. Prepare a report of those circumstances that serve to clarify the facts, which must accompany the complaint, attaching any other data or document that is necessary in the judgment of the complaint-receiving body.
8. Remit the duly foliated file to the Public Prosecutor's Office, with the actions it considers pertinent for the criminal investigation.
The National Commission for the Guarantee of Women's Right to a Life Free of Violence shall prepare a single protocol of care for women victims of violence in any of its

##### Article 92

Second section Investigation
Purpose

##### Article 94

The investigation has the purpose of recording the commission of a punishable act, the circumstances that affect

its classification, the collection and preservation of evidence related to its perpetration, the identification of the alleged author or authors of the crime and the elements that support their culpability.
Jurisdiction

##### Article 95

The specialized female or male Prosecutor of the Public Prosecutor's Office shall direct the investigation in cases of punishable acts and shall be assisted by the police forces. The opening of the investigation shall be immediately notified to the Court of Violence against Women in control, hearing and measures functions.

Protection and security measures

##### Article 106

Protection and security measures are preventive in nature to protect women victims of violence in their physical, psychological, sexual and patrimonial integrity, and from any action that violates or threatens the rights contemplated in this Law, avoiding new acts of violence, and shall be immediately applicable by the receiving bodies of complaints at the time of the complaint. The following are protection and security measures:
1. Refer assaulted women who so require to specialized centers so that they receive the respective guidance and attention.
2. Process the admission of women victims of violence, as well as their daughters and sons who require protection, to the shelter houses established in this Law, in cases where remaining in their domicile or residence implies imminent threat or violation of rights provided in this Law. The stay in shelter houses shall be temporary in nature.
3. Order the departure of the alleged aggressor from the common residence, regardless of its ownership, if cohabitation implies a risk to the integral security: physical, psychic, patrimonial or sexual freedom of the woman, preventing him from removing the household goods for family use, authorizing him to take only his personal effects, instruments and work tools. In case
the accused refuses to comply with the measure, the receiving body with the assistance of the public force, security or public order, shall execute it immediately apprehending under flagrancy the alleged aggressor, upon refusing to comply with an order issued by a complaint receiving body.
4. Reintegrate to the domicile women victims of violence, ordering the simultaneous departure of the alleged aggressor, when it concerns a common dwelling, proceeding in accordance with what is established in the previous numeral.
5. Prohibit or restrict the alleged aggressor by himself or through third persons, from approaching the assaulted woman; consequently, impose on the alleged aggressor the prohibition of approaching the place of work, study and residence of the assaulted woman.
6. Prohibit the alleged aggressor, by himself or through third persons, from performing acts of persecution, intimidation or harassment against the assaulted woman or any member of her family.
7. Request from the competent jurisdictional body the measure of transitory arrest, which must be decided immediately, in case apprehension under the modality of flagrancy is not possible.
8. Order the stationing or police patrol at the site of residence of the victim woman, who denounces or her relatives or successors in interest, for the time deemed convenient, having to prepare a record that must be signed by the woman victim of violence or her relatives as proof that it is being complied with.

Fifth section Apprehension in flagrante delicto
Definition and manner of proceeding

##### Article 112

Any crime provided for in this Law that is being committed or has just been committed shall be considered flagrant. Also considered flagrant is that for which the aggressor is pursued by the police authority, by the assaulted woman, by a private individual or by public outcry, or when requests for help are made to specialized services for attention to violence against women, made through calls or telephone messages, emails or fax, that allow establishing its commission unequivocally, or in which the person is surprised shortly after the act has been committed, in the same place or near the place where it was committed, with weapons, instruments or objects that in some way give reasonable grounds to presume that he is the perpetrator.

In these cases, every authority must and any private individual may apprehend the aggressor. When the apprehension is carried out by a private individual, he must immediately deliver him to the nearest authority, who in any case shall place him at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office within a period not exceeding twelve hours from the moment of apprehension.
It shall be understood that the act has just been committed when the victim or another person who has had knowledge of the act, goes within twenty-four (24) hours following the commission of the punishable act to the receiving body and sets forth the facts of violence related to this Law. In this case, once the commission of the punishable act is known, the receiving body or the authority having knowledge must go within a period that must not exceed twelve hours, to the place where the acts occurred, shall gather the elements that prove its commission and once the assumptions referred to in this article are verified, shall proceed to the apprehension of the alleged aggressor, who shall be placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office.
The Public Prosecutor's Office, within a term not exceeding forty-eight hours counted from the apprehension of the allegedly aggressor person, must present her before the Court of Violence Against Women in control, hearing and measures functions, which, in a hearing with the parties and the victim, if she is present, shall resolve whether to maintain the deprivation of liberty or substitute it with another less onerous one.
The decision must be duly grounded and shall observe the assumptions of admissibility for deprivation of liberty contained in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure, adjusted to the nature of the crimes contained in this Law, according to the act in question and attending to the objectives of protection of victims, without detriment to the rights of the allegedly aggressor person.

##### Article 113

The trial of the crimes dealt with in this Law shall be followed by the special procedure stipulated herein, even in the cases of flagrante delicto provided for in the previous article, with the exception enshrined in the sole paragraph of article 82 of this Law, for the case in which a measure depriving liberty has been decreed against the alleged aggressor.
Forms of initiation of the procedure

##### Article 114

The investigation of an act that constitutes one of the crimes provided for in this Law shall be initiated ex officio, by oral or written complaint or by criminal complaint filed before the competent jurisdictional body.
All these crimes are subject to public prosecution; however, for the initiation of the investigation in the cases referred to in articles 39, 40, 41, 48, 49 and 53 of this Law, the complaint of the act by the persons or institutions legitimized to file it is required.
Investigation by the Public Prosecutor's Office

##### Article 115

When the Public Prosecutor's Office has knowledge of the commission of a punishable act of those provided for in this Law, without loss of time it shall order the initiation of the investigation and shall provide that all necessary proceedings be carried out to demonstrate the commission of the punishable act, as well as the criminal responsibility of the persons identified as perpetrators or participants, immediately imposing the protection and security measures that the case warrants.

##### Article 116

##### Artículo ll

The GACETA OFICIAL, created by Executive Decree of October 11, 1872, shall continue to be published at the National Printing Office under the name GACETA OFICIAL DE LA REPÚBLICA BOLIVARIANA DE VENEZUELA.

##### Artículo 12. La GACETA OFICIAL DE LA REPÚBLICA BOLIVARIANA

DE VENEZUELA shall be published every business day, without prejudice to the publication of extraordinary issues whenever necessary; and official acts that must be published shall be inserted therein without delay.
Sole Paragraph: The extraordinary editions of the GACETA OFICIAL shall have a special numbering

##### Artículo 13. En la GACETA OFICIAL DE LA REPÚBLICA BOLIVARIANA

DE VENEZUELA shall be published the acts of the Public Powers that must be inserted and those whose inclusion is considered convenient by the National Executive.

##### Artículo 14

Laws, decrees and other official acts shall have a public character by virtue of appearing in the GACETA OFICIAL DE LA REPÚBLICA BOLIVARIANA DE VENEZUELA, whose copies shall have the force of public documents.
