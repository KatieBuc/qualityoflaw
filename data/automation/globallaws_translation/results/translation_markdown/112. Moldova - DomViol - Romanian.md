The prevention and combating of domestic violence are part of the national policy for the protection and support of the family and represent an important public health issue. For the consolidation, protection and support of the family, for ensuring respect for the fundamental principles of legislation on the family, recognizing that the achievement of gender equality between women and men constitutes a key element in the prevention of violence against women, that domestic violence affects women disproportionately, but also that children and men may also be victims of violence,
Parliament adopts this organic law.

## Chapter I GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1. Object of the law

This law establishes the legal and organizational bases for the activity of prevention and combating of domestic violence, the authorities and institutions empowered with functions of prevention and combating of domestic violence, the mechanism for reporting and resolving cases of violence.

### Article 2. Main notions

For the purposes of this law, the following notions mean:

domestic violence – acts of physical, sexual, psychological, spiritual or economic violence, with the exception of actions of self-defense or defense of another person, including the threat of such acts, committed by a family member against another member of the same family, by which material or moral prejudice has been caused to the victim;
physical violence - intentional injury to bodily integrity or health through hitting, pushing, throwing down, hair pulling, pricking, cutting, burning, strangling, biting, in any form and of any intensity, through poisoning, intoxication, other actions with similar effect;
sexual violence - any violence of a sexual nature or any illegal sexual conduct within the family or in other interpersonal relationships, such as marital rape, prohibition of the use of

contraceptive methods, sexual harassment; any unwanted sexual conduct, imposed; forcing the practice of prostitution; any illegal sexual behavior in relation to a minor family member, including through caresses, kisses, posing of the child and through other unwanted touches with sexual connotation; other actions with similar effect;
psychological violence – imposition of will or personal control; causing states of tension and psychological suffering through offenses, ridicule, swearing, insults, nicknaming, blackmail, demonstrative destruction of objects, verbal threats, ostentatious display of weapons or beating of domestic animals, neglect; involvement in personal life; acts of jealousy; imposition of isolation through detention, including in the family dwelling; isolation from family, from community, from friends; prohibition and/or creation of impediments in professional achievement or prohibition and/or creation of impediments in the achievement of the educational program in the educational institution; persecution by contacting or attempting to contact by any means or through another person the victim who has been caused a state of anxiety, fear for their own safety or that of close relatives, being forced to modify their life conduct; deprivation of identity documents; intentional deprivation of access to information; other actions with similar effect;
[Art.2 notion as worded by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force 07.09.20]
spiritual violence - underestimation or diminution of the importance of satisfying moral-spiritual needs through prohibition, limitation, ridicule, penalization of the aspirations of family members, through prohibition, limitation, ridicule or punishment of access to cultural, ethnic, linguistic or religious values; imposition of a personally unacceptable value system; other actions with similar effect or with similar repercussions;
economic violence - deprivation of economic means, including deprivation of primary means of existence, such as food, medicines, objects of primary necessity; abuse of various situations of superiority to extract the person's goods; prohibition of the right to own, use and dispose of common goods; inequitable control over common goods and resources; refusal to support the family; imposition of heavy and harmful labor to the detriment of health, including of a minor family member; other actions with similar effect;
violence against women – acts of gender-based violence, which cause or may cause women physical, sexual or psychological suffering, including the threat of such acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, committed in the public or private sphere;
[Art.2 notion introduced by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force 07.09.20]
adult victim – adult person who has been subjected to acts of domestic violence, in cohabitation;
[Art.2 notion introduced by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force 07.09.20]
child victim – person who has not reached the age of 18, who has been subjected to or has witnessed acts of domestic violence, in cohabitation;
[Art.2 notion introduced by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force 07.09.20]
moral prejudice – causing physical and/or psychological suffering, which leads to humiliation, fear,

degradation, incapacity to defend against violence, to feelings of frustration and to other consequences of a similar nature;
material prejudice - material damages, susceptible to financial/pecuniary evaluation or estimation, resulting from any act of domestic violence, in cohabitation, as well as the costs for the investigation of cases of domestic violence;
aggressor - person who commits acts of domestic violence, in cohabitation. In the case of complaints from several family members, with reciprocal accusations of committing acts of violence, the aggressor will be identified taking into consideration the following circumstances: which of the subjects involved acted in defense of themselves or of another person; the probability of the occurrence of critical situations for each person; the manner of action within the scene of violence and the severity of the injuries caused by each person; previous complaints of domestic violence, other circumstances that indicate the initiator of the acts of violence;
[Art.2 notion "victim" excluded by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force 07.09.20]
protection order - legal act by which the court applies measures for the protection of the victim;
crisis situation/critical situation – totality of circumstances that present imminent danger to the life and/or physical and/or psychological integrity of the person, requiring intervention in an emergency regime with protection measures;
emergency restraining order – administrative act issued by the police, by which protection measures are applied meant to ensure the immediate removal of the aggressor from the dwelling of the family subjected to violence and the establishment of prohibitions provided by law, in order to prevent the repetition/commission of acts of violence, thus guaranteeing safety to the victim and other family members in their dwelling and outside it.
[Art.2 notion as worded by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force 07.09.20]

### Articolul 3. Subjects of domestic violence

(1) Subjects of domestic violence are the aggressor and the victim who are members of the same family.

(2) Family members, within the meaning of this law, are:

a) in the condition of cohabitation: persons in marriage, in divorce, under guardianship and curatorship, in respect of whom a judicial protection measure has been instituted, relatives, their in-laws, spouses of relatives, persons in relationships similar to those between spouses (cohabitation) or between parents and children;
[Art.3 al.(2), lit.a) modified by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force 07.09.20]
b) in the condition of separate residence: persons in marriage, in divorce, relatives, their in-laws, adopted children, persons under curatorship, in respect of whom a judicial protection measure has been instituted, persons who are or have been in relationships similar to those between spouses (cohabitation).
[Art.3 al.(2), lit.b) modified by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in

force 07.09.20]

### Articolul 4. Legislative framework

(1) The legislation on the prevention and combating of domestic violence consists of the Constitution of the Republic of Moldova, this law, other normative acts, and international treaties to which the Republic of Moldova is a party.
(2) If an international treaty to which the Republic of Moldova is a party establishes different rules than this law, the rules of the international treaty shall apply.

### Articolul 5

Basic principles of prevention and combating of domestic violence The prevention and combating of domestic violence is carried out on the following principles:
a) legality;

b) equality and non-discrimination;

c) confidentiality;

d) access to justice;

e) protection and security of the victim;

f) cooperation of public administration authorities with civil society and with international organizations.

### Articolul 6. Scope of the law

The provisions of this law apply equally to citizens of the Republic of Moldova, foreign citizens and stateless persons residing on its territory.

## Capitolul III

MECHANISM FOR RESOLVING ACTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Articolul 11. Dreptul la protecţie al victimei

(1)	The victim is guaranteed the protection of legitimate rights and interests.

(2)	Persons with positions of responsibility, other persons who know of the existence of a danger to the life and health of a potential victim must report this fact to the authorities empowered with functions of prevention and combating of domestic violence.
(21) The victim has the right to assistance for physical, psychological and social recovery through special medical, psychological, legal and social actions. The provision of protection and assistance services is not conditioned on the victim's willingness to make statements and participate in proceedings for prosecution of the aggressor. The right to private life and confidentiality of information concerning the victim are guaranteed.
(3)	Authorities empowered with functions of prevention and combating of domestic violence are obliged to respond promptly to any notification and to inform victims about their rights, about the authorities and institutions with functions of prevention and combating of domestic violence; about the type of services and organizations to which they can turn for help; about the assistance available to them; where and how they can file a complaint; about the procedure that follows after filing a complaint and their role in such procedures; how they can obtain protection; to what extent and under what conditions they have access

to legal advice or assistance; whether there is a danger to their life or health in case of release of a detained or convicted person; whether the protection order has been cancelled.
(4)	The victim of domestic violence has the right to request from the aggressor reparation, under the conditions of the law, of the material and moral damage caused by acts of violence, and from the state – reparation of material and moral damage as a result of failure to provide adequate assistance and protection.
(41) The victim of domestic violence who has suffered serious harm to bodily integrity or health benefits from financial compensation from the state for the damage caused by the offence, under the conditions established by legislation.
(5)	The victim has the right to free primary and qualified legal assistance in accordance with the legislation on state-guaranteed legal assistance.
(6)	The victim is provided medical assistance by medical-sanitary institutions in accordance with the Law on Compulsory Health Insurance. The bodily examination of the victim and the completion of documents for medico-legal determination of the severity of harm to bodily integrity or health are carried out free of charge in cases provided by law.
(7)	The private life and identity of the victim are protected. The recording, storage and use of personal data concerning the victim are carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Protection of Personal Data.

### Articolul 12. Sesizarea cazurilor de violenţă în familie

(1)	The victim has the right to report any case of domestic violence and to request protection.

(2)	Representatives of authorities empowered with functions of prevention and combating of domestic violence are prohibited from taking actions intended to discourage the victim from reporting the acts of violence to which they are subjected.
(3)	Any person who witnesses acts of domestic violence or who has reasonable suspicion that such acts have been committed or that acts of violence are possible has the right to report this to the authorities empowered with functions of prevention and combating of domestic violence.
(4)	Persons with positions of responsibility and professionals who are required to ensure confidentiality are obliged to report to the competent authorities about acts of domestic violence that endanger the life or health of the victim or about the imminent danger of such acts of violence occurring. In other cases, reporting shall be carried out only with the victim's consent.

### Article 121. Issuance of the emergency restraining order

(1) In case of establishment at the scene, as a result of risk assessment, of circumstances from which a reasonable suspicion arises that acts of domestic violence have been committed and/or an imminent danger of repetition or commission of violent actions persists, the police body is obliged to immediately order the issuance of the emergency restraining order against the aggressor to remove the crisis situation and, concurrently, undertake the necessary actions to establish the commission of the offence of domestic violence.
(11) The police body has the right to apply against the aggressor one or more of the following measures:
a) obligation to temporarily leave the common dwelling or to stay away from the victim's dwelling;

b) prohibition to approach the victim, respecting the distance that would ensure the victim's security and excluding any visual contact with the victim and/or children;
c) prohibition of any contact, including by telephone or by any other means of communication, with the victim and/or children;
d) prohibition to keep and carry weapons.

[Art.121 para.(11) introduced by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force 07.09.20]
(2) The emergency restraining order is issued for a period of up to 10 days and is immediately put into effect, the aggressor and the victim (in the case of children – the legal representative of the victim) being informed about the restrictions applied, the rights and obligations incumbent upon them and about the liability for non-compliance with the requirements of the restraining order.
(3) Supervision of the fulfilment of the measures established in the emergency restraining order is exercised by the employees of the police station or police sector in the locality in the manner provided by law.
(4) The aggressor has the right to challenge the emergency restraining order in court under the conditions of administrative litigation. The filing of the application does not suspend the effect of the restraining order.
(5) The victim has the right, during the period of effect of the emergency restraining order, to request, under the conditions of the law, the issuance of the protection order. The effect of the emergency restraining order, in this case, is extended by law and ceases upon the implementation of the protection measures established by the court.

### Article 13. Filing of the application

(1) The application concerning the commission of acts of domestic violence is filed:

a) with the police bodies;

b) in court;

c) with the social assistance and family and child protection body;

d) with the local public administration authority.

(2) The application is filed at the place:

a) of domicile of the victim;

b) of temporary residence of the victim if the victim has left the domicile to avoid the continuation of direct violence;
c) of residence of the aggressor;

d) where the victim sought assistance;

e) where the act of violence took place.

(21) The application for the issuance of the protection order is filed by the victim personally or through a representative. In case of impossibility of filing the application by the victim due to health, age, other well-founded reasons, at the victim's request, the application for the issuance of the protection order may be filed, in the interests of the victim, by the police body, the social assistance body or by the prosecutor. The application for the issuance of the protection order in the interests of the child or of the person in respect of whom a judicial guardianship measure has been instituted may be filed by the local guardianship authority or by the prosecutor even in the absence of a request from the victim or the victim's legal representative.
(3) No state fee is paid for filing in court the application for issuance of the protection order.

### Article 14. Manner of examining the application

(1) The application filed with the police body is examined according to the procedure provided by the legislation in force.
(2) The application or request for the issuance of the protection order filed in court is examined according to the Code of Civil Procedure, the Code of Criminal Procedure.
(3) The application filed with any authority empowered with functions of prevention and combating of domestic violence is redirected according to competence within one working day.

### Articolul 15. Protection measures

a) obligation to temporarily leave the common dwelling or to stay away from the victim's dwelling, without deciding on the manner of ownership of the property;
b) obligation to stay away from the victim's location, at a distance that would ensure her safety, excluding any visual contact with the victim or her children, with other persons dependent on her;
[Art.15 para.(1), let.b) amended by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force 07.09.20]

### Articolul 151. Revocation of protection measures

(1) At the reasoned request of the victim, the court may revoke the established protection measures before the term.
(2) Revocation may be ordered if the following conditions are cumulatively met:

a) the aggressor has respected the prohibitions and obligations established;

b) the aggressor has followed/is following the counseling or treatment that was established for him.

(3) The court will examine whether the victim's will is freely expressed, ensuring that she has had real access to assistance and protection.
(4) The revocation application is resolved with the summoning of the parties and the representative of the police who exercised supervision of the execution of the protection order whose revocation is requested.

### Articolul 152

Supervision of the execution of the emergency restraining order and the protection order
(1) Supervision of the execution of the protection measures for the victim of domestic violence, imposed by the emergency restraining order and by the protection order, is within the competence of the police.
(2) The police informs and explains to the aggressor the established protection measures for the victim.

(3) In the case where the aggressor must leave the family dwelling, the police must require him to immediately hand over all keys to the dwelling, which are subsequently transmitted to the victim or are taken for provisional safekeeping at the police body. Upon leaving the dwelling, the aggressor may take, under police supervision, only strictly necessary personal use items (clothes, documents, hygiene items).
(4) If the aggressor refuses to voluntarily leave the dwelling, the police body has the right to undertake the measures that are required, under the conditions of the law, to overcome the resistance opposed to legal requirements.
(5) During the period of validity of the victim's protection measures, the police body will ensure the carrying out of unannounced visits to the victim's dwelling, as well as immediate intervention upon any communication about non-compliance or attempted violation of the protection measures by the aggressor.
(6) The aggressor's refusal or evasion from executing the requirements of the emergency restraining order/protection order entails liability according to the law.

### Articolul 153. Assessment and management of risks of repetition/commission of acts

of domestic violence

(1) The process of assessment and management of risks of repetition/commission of acts of domestic violence is intended for response with immediate and adequate measures designed to ensure the protection of the victim or potential victim following the actions or potential actions of the aggressor.
(2) The organization and conduct of the assessment and management of the risk of repetition/commission of acts of domestic violence take place in the manner and conditions established by the internal procedures of the competent authority and/or by joint orders.

### Articolul 16. Financing

(1) The activities for implementing this law are financed from the state budget, from the budgets of administrative-territorial units, within the limits of the financial means established annually, from other sources not prohibited by law.
(2) Financing of the centers/services for assistance and protection of victims of domestic violence and their children and of the centers/services for assistance and counseling for family aggressors is carried out from the allocations provided in the state budget, from donations, grants, from other sources, according to the legislation in force. In the case of limited resources, priority will be given to the allocation of funds for supporting the centers/services for assistance and protection of victims of domestic violence.
