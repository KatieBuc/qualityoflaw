VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN BASED ON GENDER

## CHAPTER I - GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

(Purpose and scope).- This law has as its purpose to guarantee the effective enjoyment of the right of women to a life free from gender-based violence. It includes women of all ages, trans women, of diverse sexual orientations, socioeconomic status, territorial belonging, belief, cultural and ethnic-racial origin or disability status, without any distinction or discrimination. Comprehensive mechanisms, measures and policies for prevention, care, protection, sanction and reparation are established.

#### Article 2

(Declaration of public order and general interest).- The provisions of this law are of public order and general interest. The eradication of violence exercised against women, children and adolescents is declared a priority, and the State must act with due diligence for this purpose.

#### Article 3

(Interpretation and integration).- For the interpretation and integration of this law, the values, purposes, general principles of law and the provisions of the Constitution of the Republic and of international Human Rights instruments shall be taken into account, in particular the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence Against Women (Convention of Belem Do Para), the International Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women (CEDAW), the International Convention on the Rights of the Child (CRC), the International Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD) and the Inter-American Convention on the Protection of the Rights of Older Persons.
In case of conflict or doubt about the application of the provisions contained in this law, the interpretation most favorable to women in situations of gender-based violence shall prevail.

#### Article 4

(Definition of gender-based violence against women).- Gender-based violence is a form of discrimination that affects, directly or indirectly, the life, liberty, dignity, physical, psychological, sexual, economic or patrimonial integrity, as well as the personal security of women.
Gender-based violence against women is understood as any conduct, action or omission, in the public or private sphere that, based on an unequal power relationship based on gender, has as its object or result the impairment or nullification of the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights or fundamental freedoms of women.
This includes conduct perpetrated by the State or its agents, as well as by private institutions or individuals.

#### Article 5

(Guiding principles and guidelines).- The following are guiding principles and guidelines for the application of this law:
A)   Priority of human rights. Actions against gender-based violence
against women must prioritize the human rights of victims.
B)   State responsibility. The State is responsible for preventing,
investigating and punishing gender-based violence against women,
as well as protecting, caring for and providing reparation to
victims in case of failure of service.
C)   Equality and non-discrimination. Any form of distinction,
exclusion or restriction based on birth, nationality,
ethnic-racial origin, sex, age, sexual orientation or gender
identity, marital status, religion, economic, social, cultural
status, disability status, place of residence or other factors
that have as their object or result the impairment or
nullification of the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human
rights and fundamental freedoms of women is prohibited.
D)   Gender equality. The State, through its various forms of action,
must promote the elimination of relations of domination based on
sociocultural stereotypes of inferiority or subordination of
women. The actions of private institutions, the community and
individuals in particular must be oriented in the same direction.
E)   Comprehensiveness. Policies against violence against women must
address its different dimensions, manifestations and
consequences. For such purposes, State organs and agencies must
articulate and coordinate budgetary and institutional resources.
F)   Autonomy of women. Actions against violence against women, and in
particular care and reparation services, must respect and promote
their own decisions and projects, overcoming tutelary and
assistance-based interventions. In the case of girls and
adolescents, their progressive autonomy must be respected
according to age and maturity.
G)   Best interests of girls and adolescents. In all measures
concerning girls and adolescents, their best interests must
prevail, which consists of the recognition and respect of the
rights inherent to their status as human persons.
H)   Quality. Actions for compliance with this law must aim to be
inter and multidisciplinary, be in charge of operators
specialized in the subject matter and have material resources to
provide quality services.
I)   Citizen participation. Plans and actions against gender-based
violence against women shall be developed, implemented and
evaluated with the active participation of women and
representative social organizations from throughout the country
with involvement in the subject matter.
J)   Transparency and accountability. The State must inform and
justify to citizens the policies, actions and public services it
executes to guarantee women a life free from violence.
K)   Promptness and effectiveness. The provisions of this law must be
complied with in an effective and timely manner.

#### Article 6

(Forms of violence).- The following constitute manifestations of gender-based violence, not mutually exclusive nor excluding others that may not be explicitly stated:
A)   Physical violence. Any action, omission or pattern of conduct that
harms the bodily integrity of a woman.
B)   Psychological or emotional violence. Any action, omission or pattern
of conduct aimed at disturbing, degrading or controlling the
conduct, behavior, beliefs or decisions of
a woman, through humiliation, intimidation, isolation or
any other means that affects her psychological or
emotional stability.
C)   Sexual violence. Any action that implies the violation of
a woman's right to decide voluntarily about her sexual
or reproductive life, through threats, coercion, use of
force or intimidation, including rape within
marriage and other bonding or kinship relationships,
whether or not there is cohabitation, the intentional transmission of
sexually transmitted infections (STIs), as well as forced
prostitution and sexual trafficking.
Also sexual violence is the involvement of girls, boys and
adolescents in sexual activities with an adult or with
any other person who is in a position of advantage
over them, whether due to their age, due to reasons of their greater
physical or mental development, due to the kinship relationship,
affective or trust relationship that binds them to the boy or girl, due to their
position of authority or power. Forms of sexual violence include,
among others, sexual abuse, sexual exploitation and
use in pornography.
D)   Violence based on prejudice toward sexual orientation, gender identity
or gender expression. That which aims to
repress and sanction those who do not comply with
traditional gender norms, whether due to their sexual orientation, gender
identity or gender expression.
E)   Economic violence. Any conduct aimed at limiting, controlling
or preventing economic income of a woman, including the
contumacious non-payment of maintenance obligations, with the purpose of
undermining her autonomy.
F)   Patrimonial violence. Any conduct aimed at affecting the free
disposal of a woman's patrimony, through the removal,
destruction, diversion, damage, loss, limitation or retention
of objects, personal documents, work instruments,
goods, valuables and patrimonial rights.
G)   Symbolic violence. That exercised through messages,
values, symbols, icons, images, signs and social,
economic, political, cultural impositions and religious
beliefs that transmit, reproduce and consolidate relations of
domination, exclusion, inequality and discrimination, which
contribute to naturalizing the subordination of women.
H)   Obstetric violence. Any action, omission and pattern of conduct
by health personnel in the reproductive processes of a
woman, that affects her autonomy to decide freely about her
body or abuse of invasive techniques and procedures.
I)   Labor violence. That exercised in the labor context, through
acts that obstruct a woman's access to
work, promotion or stability therein, such as
moral harassment, sexual harassment, the requirement of prerequisites regarding
marital status, age, physical appearance, the request for
results of clinical laboratory tests, beyond what
is established in the applicable legal frameworks, or the reduction
of the salary corresponding to the task performed due to the fact of
being a woman.
J)   Violence in the educational sphere. Violence exercised against
a woman due to her condition as such in an educational relationship, with
abuse of power, including sexual harassment, that damages
self-esteem, health, integrity, freedom and security of the victim
and violates equality.
K)   Street sexual harassment. Any act of a sexual nature or connotation
exercised in public spaces by a person
against a woman without her consent, generating discomfort,
intimidation, hostility, degradation and humiliation.
L)   Political violence. Any act of pressure, persecution,
harassment or any type of aggression against a woman or her
family, in her capacity as candidate, elected official or in exercise of
political representation, to prevent or restrict the free
exercise of her office or induce her to make decisions against
her will.
M)   Media violence. Any publication or dissemination of messages and
images through any mass media, that
directly or indirectly promotes the exploitation of
women or their images, insults, defames, discriminates, dishonors,
humiliates or violates the dignity of women, legitimizes
unequal treatment or constructs sociocultural patterns
that reproduce inequality or generate violence against
women,
N)   Femicidal violence. The action of extreme violence that violates
the fundamental right to life and causes the death of a
woman because of being one, or that of her daughters, sons or other
persons in her care, with the purpose of causing her suffering or
harm.
O)   Domestic violence. Domestic violence constitutes any action or
omission, direct or indirect, that undermines by illegitimately
limiting the free exercise or enjoyment of the human rights
of a woman, caused by a person with whom she has or has
had a relationship of kinship, marriage, courtship, affective
relationship or cohabitation.
P)   Community violence. Any action or omission that through
individual or collective acts in the community, transgresses the
fundamental rights of one or several women and promotes their
denigration, discrimination, marginalization or exclusion.
Q)   Institutional violence. Any action or omission by any
authority, official or personnel of the public sphere or
private institutions, that discriminates against women or has as
its purpose to undermine, obstruct or prevent the enjoyment and exercise of the
rights and fundamental freedoms of women, as well as
that which obstructs women's access to policies and
services intended to prevent, address, investigate, sanction and
eradicate the manifestations, types and modalities of violence
against women provided for in this law.
R)   Ethnic Racial Violence. This type of violence constitutes any
physical, moral, verbal or psychological aggression, humiliating
or offensive treatment, exercised against a woman by virtue of her
ethnic belonging or in allusion thereto; provoking in the
victim feelings of intimidation, shame, contempt,
denigration. Whether this type of violence is exercised in
public, in private, or regardless of the sphere in which
it occurs.

#### Article 7

(Rights of women victims of violence).- In addition to the rights recognized for all persons in applicable national and international legislation in force, every woman victim of any form of gender-based violence has the right to:
A)   Respect for her dignity, privacy, autonomy as well as not to be
subjected to any form of discrimination.
B)   To be respected in her sexual orientation and gender identity.
C)   To receive clear, accessible, complete, truthful, timely
information, appropriate to age and socio-cultural context, in
relation to her rights and to the mechanisms and procedures
contemplated in this law and other applicable regulations.
D)   To have an interpreter, language adaptation and augmentative
communication as well as other necessary supports and reasonable
adjustments that allow guaranteeing her rights, when in a
situation of disability.
E)   To have the confidentiality and privacy of her personal data
guaranteed, as well as those of her descendants or any other
person who is under her care or custody.
F)   To receive timely comprehensive protection and care for herself,
her sons and daughters or other persons in her charge, through
adequate and effective services.
G)   To receive free guidance, counseling and legal representation,
depending on the socioeconomic position of the woman. Such
assistance must be immediate, specialized and comprehensive, and
must cover the various matters and processes that her situation
requires.
H)   To receive specialized and comprehensive medical, psychological
and psychiatric assistance for herself and her sons and daughters.
I)   To respect and protection of her sexual and reproductive rights,
including to exercise all rights recognized by the laws on Sexual
and Reproductive Health (Law No. 18,426, of December 1, 2008) and
Voluntary Termination of Pregnancy (Law No. 18,987, of October
22, 2012), whatever her nationality and even if she has not
reached one year of residence in the country, provided that the
acts of violence have occurred in the national territory, which
constitutes an exception to article 13 of Law No. 18,987, of
October 22, 2012. (*)

#### Article 8

(Rights of women victims of violence in administrative or
judicial proceedings).- In administrative or judicial proceedings
the following rights must be guaranteed:
A)   To have efficient and accessible mechanisms to file complaints.
B)   To communicate freely and privately with her legal counsel,
before, during or after acts of the judicial or administrative
process.
C)   To be heard by the judge or administrative authority, as
appropriate, and obtain a timely and effective response. Her
opinion must be considered in the decision that affects her,
especially considering the context of violence and intimidation in
which she may find herself.
D)   To receive immediate and preventive judicial protection, when her
rights are threatened or violated.
E)   To free administrative and judicial proceedings as appropriate.
F)   To participate in proceedings related to the situation of
violence that affects her, as appropriate.
G)   To attend all judicial instances with a companion of her trust.
H)   That her testimony not be devalued based on gender stereotypes
based on the inferiority or submission of women, or on other
factors of discrimination such as age, disability status, sexual
orientation or gender identity, ethnic-racial origin, territorial
belonging, beliefs or cultural identity.
I)   To receive humane treatment, taking into account her age,
disability status or other conditions or circumstances that
require special attention. Actions that have the purpose or result
of causing suffering to direct or indirect victims of acts of
violence are prohibited.
J)   To non-confrontation, including her family nucleus with the
aggressor, prohibiting any form of mediation or conciliation in
protection or criminal proceedings.
K)   That her informed consent be obtained prior to conducting physical
examinations or other actions that affect her privacy or intimacy.
In cases of sexual violence it is her right to choose the sex of
the professional or technician for such practices, who must be
specialized and trained with a gender perspective.
L)   To truth, justice and reparation through a simple and rapid
remedy before competent Courts. (*)

#### Article 9

(Rights of girls, boys and adolescents in administrative and judicial proceedings).- Girls, boys and adolescents, whether victims or witnesses of acts of violence, are recognized, without prejudice to the rights established by applicable regulations, the right to:
A)   Be informed by their defense about their rights, the status and
scope of administrative proceedings, the deadlines and judicial
rulings in the case, in a manner accessible to their age, taking
into account their maturity and degree of autonomy.
B)   That their account of the reported facts be obtained by
specialized technical personnel, in places suitable for that
purpose and avoiding its repetition.
C)   To the maximum possible restriction of attendance at judicial or
police headquarters, as well as to being questioned directly by
the court or by police personnel.
D)   Be protected in their physical and emotional integrity, as well as
their family and witnesses, against possible reprisals, ensuring
that they do not coincide in common places with the persons
reported in judicial and police spaces.
E)   In hearings the person reported as aggressor may not be present
and the defense may not ask questions of the girl, boy or
adolescent except with prior authorization of the Court and only
through specialized technical personnel.
F)   Respect for the privacy of the victim and reporting family members
with respect to third parties, keeping their identity and image
confidential and adopting necessary measures to prevent their use
by the media.
G)   Receive prior information accessible to their age and maturity.
For conducting examinations or other actions that affect their
intimacy, they may be accompanied by the trusted adult that they
themselves choose. (*)

## CHAPTER II

INTERINSTITUTIONAL SYSTEM FOR RESPONDING TO GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

#### Article 10

(Interinstitutional system).- The system for responding to gender-based violence against women must be comprehensive, interinstitutional and interdisciplinary, and include at a minimum: prevention actions, care services, mechanisms that guarantee effective and timely access to justice, reparation measures, registration and organization of information, training and capacity building of operators and evaluation and accountability.

#### Article 11

(National Women's Institute).- The National Women's Institute is the governing body for public policies for a life free of violence for women, responsible for the promotion, design, coordination, articulation, monitoring and evaluation thereof.
In particular, it must:
A)   Ensure faithful compliance with this law.
B)   Articulate and coordinate actions with the different state areas
involved, at national, departmental and municipal levels and with
university, union, business and religious spheres, organizations
for the defense of women's rights, children's and adolescents'
rights and other civil society organizations with competence in
the matter.
C)   Provide mechanisms and processes to mainstream the subject in the
sectoral policies of the Executive Branch as well as its
articulation with the Judicial Branch, the Legislative Branch and
the Departmental Governments.
D)   Prepare, within the framework of the National Council for a Life
Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women, the National Plan,
as well as other specific plans, programs and actions for the
implementation of this law.
E)   Generate minimum standards for detection and approach to
situations of violence, to ensure that actions are oriented
towards strengthening women's autonomy and take into account
diversity according to age, sexual orientation, gender identity,
ethnic-racial origin, territorial belonging, disability status,
beliefs, among others. For such purposes, it shall agree with
state organs or bodies the guidelines for the inclusion of the
gender perspective in the different areas.
F)   Develop technical assistance programs for the different organs,
bodies or institutions involved, aimed at prevention, early
detection, care, protection, inter-institutional articulation and
the preparation of protocols for the different levels of
intervention that are adapted to the diversity characteristics
referred to in the preceding paragraph.
G)   Provide permanent training, education and training on the subject
to personnel of public, state, departmental and municipal organs
and bodies. Such training shall be provided in a comprehensive
and specific manner according to each area, in accordance with
the contents of this law.
H)   Promote training on the matter in the different universities and
professional associations.
I)   Promote and coordinate specialized training for legislators on
violence against women.
J)   Generate quantitative and qualitative data records on gender-
based violence, which contemplate variables such as age,
disability status, ethnic-racial origin, religion, territoriality,
among other dimensions of discrimination. Measures must be
adapted in order to guarantee the confidentiality of personal
data so that the person to whom they refer is not identifiable
(Law No. 18,331, of August 11, 2008).
K)   Coordinate with other registries the criteria for the collection
and selection of data on gender-based violence.
L)   Formulate recommendations to public and private entities with
competence in the subject, to guarantee women a life free of
gender-based violence.
M)   Evaluate compliance with public policies to guarantee women a
life free of violence and report on actions and results in a
public and transparent manner.

#### Article 12

(National Advisory Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women).- The National Advisory Council for the Fight against Domestic Violence, created by Law No. 17,514, of July 2, 2002, is replaced by the National Advisory Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women, with national competence and which shall have the following purposes:
A)   Advise the Executive Branch, in matters within its competence.
B)   Ensure compliance with this law and its regulations.
C)   Design and submit for consideration by the Executive Branch the
National Plan against Gender-Based Violence against Women as well
as other specific plans, programs and actions for the
implementation of this law.
D)   Supervise and monitor compliance with the National Plan against
Gender-Based Violence against Women.
E)   Articulate the implementation of sectoral policies to combat
gender-based violence against women.
F)   Create, support and strengthen the Departmental and Municipal
Commissions for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against
Women, establishing the directives and guidelines for their
operation and compliance with this law.
G)   Be consulted mandatorily in the preparation of reports that the
State must make within the framework of International Conventions
ratified by the country related to the issues of gender-based
violence referred to in this law.
H)   Give mandatory opinion on draft laws and programs whose purpose
is gender-based violence against women. Failure to issue an
express pronouncement within a period of thirty days shall be
understood as approval.
I)   Issue an opinion regarding actions or situations relating to
gender-based violence against women of which it becomes aware,
communicating it to the competent authorities.
J)   Prepare an annual report on compliance with its duties and on the
situation of gender-based violence in the country.
This report must be presented publicly and sent to the National Coordinating Council for Gender Equality Public Policies, the National Human Rights Institution and Ombudsman's Office and the General Assembly.

#### Article 13

(Composition of the National Advisory Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women).- The Council shall be composed as follows:
A)   One representative of the National Women's Institute of the
Ministry of Social Development, who shall preside over it.
B)   One representative of the Ministry of National Defense.
C)   One representative of the Ministry of Education and Culture.
D)   One representative of the Ministry of the Interior.
E)   One representative of the Planning and Budget Office of the
Presidency of the Republic.
F)   One representative of the Ministry of Public Health.
G)   One representative of the Ministry of Labor and Social Security.
H)   One representative of the Ministry of Housing, Territorial
Planning and Environment.
I)   One representative of the Judicial Branch.
J)   One representative of the Office of the Attorney General of the
Nation.
K)   One representative of the National Public Education
Administration.
L)   One representative of the Social Security Bank.
M)   One representative of the Congress of Mayors.
N)   One representative of the Child and Adolescent Institute of
Uruguay.
O)   Three representatives of the Uruguayan Network against Domestic
and Sexual Violence.
In the meetings of the National Advisory Council, a representative of the National Human Rights Institution and Ombudsman's Office may participate with voice and without vote.
The representatives of public organs and bodies must be of the highest ranks.

#### Article 14

(Internal regulations).- The National Advisory Council shall issue its internal operating regulations within thirty days from the approval of this law.
It may create thematic commissions for the better fulfillment of its duties.
The National Women's Institute shall be in charge of the technical secretariat of the National Advisory Council and shall provide the infrastructure for its meetings and those of the thematic commissions.

#### Article 15

(Powers and duties).- The National Consultative Council may convene in consultation representatives of the Ministries and other public bodies, and non-governmental organizations and private institutions fighting gender-based violence.
It shall meet at least once a year with social or trade organizations linked to the subject matter, in order to hear the suggestions, proposals or recommendations they raise, with the aim of strengthening its work.
Likewise, it must provide such organizations with information on the implementation of policies to combat violence against women and statistical monitoring and evaluation reports.

#### Article 16

(Departmental Commissions for a Life Free from Gender-Based Violence against Women).- The National Consultative Council for a Life Free from Gender-Based Violence against Women shall create, in each department of the country, a Departmental Commission for a Life Free from Gender-Based Violence against Women, composed of representatives of the institutions that comprise it and shall regulate its composition and operation, taking into account the particularities of each place, in consultation with local actors.
The presidency and technical secretariat of the Departmental Commissions shall be in charge of the National Women's Institute, which shall provide the infrastructure for their operation.

#### Article 17

(Functions of the Departmental Commissions).- The Departmental Commissions for a Life Free from Gender-Based Violence against Women shall have the following functions:
A)   Ensure compliance with this law and the National Plan for a
Life Free from Gender-Based Violence against Women in the
department.
B)   Implement in the territory the resolutions and directives of the
National Council for a Life Free from Gender-Based Violence against
Women.
C)   Promote the coordination of policies and actions against
gender-based violence against women in the department.
D)   Advise in the department the national, departmental and municipal
authorities, in coordination with the
National Consultative Council for a Life Free from Gender-Based Violence
against Women.

#### Article 18

(Observatory on Gender-Based Violence against Women).- The Observatory on Gender-Based Violence against Women is hereby created, intended for the monitoring, collection, production, registration and permanent systematization of data and information on violence against women.
It shall be in charge of an Honorary Interinstitutional Commission composed of the Ministry of Social Development, which shall preside over it, the Ministry of the Interior and the Office of the Attorney General of the Nation. Said Commission may convene Civil Society organizations dedicated to the fight against gender-based violence.
The members of said commission shall be persons of proven experience appointed by each of the Institutions.
It shall operate within the scope of the National Women's Institute, which shall provide the technical and administrative secretariat, as well as the
necessary infrastructure for its operation. (*)

#### Article 19

(Functions of the Observatory).- The functions of the Observatory on Gender-Based Violence against Women are:
A)   Collect, process, register, analyze, publish and disseminate
periodic and systematic information on gender-based
violence against women, taking into account
sexual, racial, age and socio-economic diversity,
disability status, among other aspects that intersect with
gender.
B)   Conduct studies and research on the evolution,
prevalence, types and modalities of violence against women,
its consequences and effects, identifying those
social, cultural, economic and political factors that are associated
or may constitute a cause of violence.
(*)
D)   Create and maintain an updated documentary database, with free
public access, that ensures accessibility in situations of
disability.
E)   Systematize and disseminate good practices -in matters of
prevention and eradication of violence against women and
innovative experiences.
F)   Encourage and promote the organization and periodic celebration of
public debates, with participation of research centers,
academic institutions, civil society organizations and
representatives of public and private bodies and organizations,
national and international with competence in the matter,
fostering the exchange of experiences and identifying relevant
themes and problems for the public agenda.
G)   Request from public bodies and organizations and private
institutions the information necessary for the fulfillment of its
functions.
H)   Coordinate actions with the National Council for a Life Free from
Gender-Based Violence against Women and other governmental
bodies and organizations, with social organizations and with
other observatories that exist at the departmental, national and
international level.
I)   Enter into cooperation agreements with public or private bodies
and organizations, national or international, with the
purpose of developing studies and research.
J)   Conduct studies on the proper compliance with the law in the
administrative and judicial sphere.
(*)

## CHAPTER III - GUIDELINES FOR PUBLIC POLICIES

#### Article 20

(Compliance and coordination of national policy against gender-based violence).- State institutions, within the scope of their competencies, must adopt and execute programs and actions for the eradication of gender-based violence against women in accordance with applicable legislation, national and international, particularly as provided in the National Plan against Gender-Based Violence against Women.

#### Article 21

(Guidelines for educational policies).- Without prejudice to the provisions of paragraph 2 of article 202 and paragraph 2 of article 204 of the Constitution of the Republic, the bodies and agencies responsible for educational policies at all levels (initial, primary, secondary, teacher training, tertiary, university, non-formal education) and all educational institutions, within the scope of their competencies, must:
A)   Design and implement in the educational institutions under their responsibility
a comprehensive plan to mainstream the gender perspective in
their actions, plans and programs, including the Plan on Human Rights
Education, to promote equality between men and
women, overcome stereotypes based on the inferiority or
subjugation of women and prevent, sanction, protect and
repair the damages caused by violence against women.
B)   Adopt measures for the effective protection of the right to
education -in relation to academic performance and
educational inclusion- of female students who face situations
of gender-based violence.
C)   Include in the minimum curricular contents the gender
perspective, respect and freedom in
interpersonal relationships, the human right to a life free from violence,
equality between men and women, the democratization of
family relationships and the delegitimization of violent
models.
D)   Design and disseminate informative and educational materials for the
prevention and early detection of gender-based violence
against women, following accessibility standards for
persons with disabilities and taking into account
age diversity.
E)   Guide and raise awareness among teaching staff, so that the
teaching materials they use do not contain gender stereotypes
or discriminatory criteria in order to promote
equality of rights, opportunities and treatment between women and
men of all ages. The endorsement of books and teaching
materials by educational authorities must
require compliance with the established conditions.
F)   Prohibit any discriminatory measure against students, teaching
and non-teaching staff, based on their sexual orientation or gender
identity. Trans persons, regardless of their age, position,
function or participation in the educational center, shall have the right to
enter it with the attire characteristic of the gender with which
they identify, and to express, without discrimination and
in equality of rights, their gender identity.
G)   Design, test, implement and disseminate in public and private
educational centers, action protocols that allow the
promotion of rights, prevention and early detection as well
as the timely and responsible reporting, intervention and referral
in cases of gender-based violence against women.
The bodies, agencies and institutions with competence in the
supervision of educational centers must ensure their
compliance. Reporting mechanisms must ensure their
accessibility according to age and disability status.
H)   Investigate, sanction and repair gender-based violence that
occurs within the institution, adopting the necessary measures
to guarantee victims the rights provided for in
articles 7, 8 and 9 of this law.
I)   Provide ongoing training to all staff of educational
centers on women's human rights and the
prevention of gender-based violence.
J)   Conduct interdisciplinary research aimed at creating
models for prevention, detection and intervention in response to
violence against women of all ages.
K)   Keep updated records of situations of gender-based
violence that are detected or that occur in educational
settings, disaggregated by age, disability status,
ethnic-racial origin, sexual orientation, gender identity,
beliefs, territorial belonging among other variables,
in accordance with the guidelines of the National Women's
Institute, ensuring in all cases the protection and confidentiality of
personal data.
L)   Establish as a hiring requirement for all staff
of formal education institutions, the absence of
administrative or criminal records in matters of physical, psychological, sexual, domestic violence, considering it
disqualifying for the teaching function.

#### Article 22

(Guidelines for health policies).- The Ministry of Public Health and all other organs and organisms linked to health policies and health service provider institutions, within the scope of their competencies, must:
A)   Promote the right to a life free from gender-based violence against
women as a priority objective of public health, mainstreaming the
gender perspective and prevention actions in institutional plans,
programs and actions.
B)   Develop policies aimed at addressing gender-based violence as a
public health problem, disseminating information about programs
and services for its prevention.
C)   Eradicate practices based on discriminatory stereotypes against
women and adopt measures to guarantee respect for the autonomy,
self-determination and human dignity of women, without distinction
on grounds of age, sexual orientation or gender identity,
disability situation, ethnic-racial origin, religious beliefs
among other factors.
D)   Ensure universal coverage and access to health care for all women
in situations of gender-based violence, for prevention, reduction
of risk factors, timely treatment and rehabilitation. The Ministry
of Public Health shall determine the conditions that health
service provider institutions must meet, whether public or
private, comprehensive or partial.
E)   Guarantee that all health interventions respect the free
expression of will of women, in relation to any matter affecting
their autonomy, integrity or well-being. They must have the
necessary information according to their communication needs, this
being of quality, non-discriminatory and understandable,
including all existing options, their risks and benefits. Women
may revoke their decision at any time and for any reason without
this entailing any disadvantage or prejudice.
F)   Guarantee that all girls, boys and adolescents have access to
prophylaxis and treatments for HIV, syphilis, hepatitis and other
sexually transmitted infections. Likewise, access to sexual and
reproductive health services as well as education and guidance on
sexual health matters, contraceptive methods without requiring
prior authorization from legal representatives, as well as
voluntary termination of pregnancy under the terms and conditions
provided for in applicable legislation is guaranteed.
G)   Guarantee confidentiality and respect for the private life of
women of all ages in all health services.
H)   Adopt measures to ensure the existence of complaint mechanisms in
health services, agile and accessible to all women, taking
special account of those in situations of disability, girls and
adolescents, older women, women attended by mental health
services, as well as those admitted to hospital or residential
centers.
I)   Issue directives to ensure that all health providers develop
ongoing training actions for staff (professional, technical and
administrative) in relation to the prevention of gender-based
violence and care and rehabilitation for affected women,
incorporating the generational perspective, sexual diversity,
ethnic-racial and disability situations.
J)   Establish protocols for interventions regarding intersex persons,
prohibiting unnecessary medical procedures on girls, boys and
adolescents.
K)   Promote records of intra-institutional gender-based violence
situations, detected or attended to in health services, in
coordination with the National Women's Institute, including data
on prevalence, risk factors and health repercussions of
gender-based violence.
L)   Promote quantitative and qualitative studies and research on the
impact of gender-based violence on women's health and on care
models for victims for greater effectiveness in responses.
M)   Promote the approval of protocols and create spaces for care of
men who exercise violence, based on existing systematized
knowledge, in order to contribute to stopping the intrafamily and
community transmission of violent modes of bonding and the
management of gender relations, in coordination with the National
Women's Institute.

#### Article 23

(Guidelines for labor and social security policies).- The Ministry of Labor and Social Security and all other organs and organisms linked to labor and social security policies, within the scope of their competencies, must:
A)   Promote measures that guarantee the exercise of women's labor
rights, both in the public and private sectors, in particular the
right to equal remuneration for work of equal value, without
discrimination based on sex, age, disability situation, marital
status or maternity.
B)   Develop awareness-raising and information actions for the
prevention of gender-based violence against women in the workplace
as well as promote such actions in social dialogue and collective
bargaining.
C)   Incorporate the gender perspective in the plans, programs and
services of the Ministry of Labor and Social Security, regulating
actions for the prevention, detection, investigation and sanction
of gender-based violence in the workplace.
D)   Implement programs for the training and inclusion in work of women
with restricted employment possibilities as a consequence of
gender-based violence.

#### Article 24

(Guidelines for security policies).- The Ministry of the Interior and all other organs and organisms linked to security policies, within the scope of their competencies, must:
A)   Mainstream the gender perspective in public security policy, integrating the right to a life free of violence as a human right of women of all ages.
B)   Design and disseminate informative materials on prevention measures, security and preservation of evidence, in situations of gender-based violence against women, in all areas in which they occur and in all their forms of expression. The materials shall take into account accessibility standards for persons with disabilities, as well as different age groups.
C)   Approve the regulations, protocols and guides necessary so that police intervention in situations of gender-based violence is timely, of quality and effective, avoids revictimization, ensures the protection of women and facilitates due investigation. Special account shall be taken of the situation of women who are prevented from going to the police station due to situations of disability or dependency.
D)   Develop investigation models appropriate to the specific characteristics of sexual offenses or those based on other forms of gender-based violence, which are based on technical and scientific evidence and which avoid centering the evidence on the testimony of victims.
E)   Create specialized police units on gender-based violence and strengthen existing ones, ensuring that they are accessible, including for rural women and for women with disabilities, providing them with the necessary resources for a quality response.
F)   Register complaints and police actions in situations of gender-based violence, in coordination with the National Women's Institute.
G)   Systematize information on the manifestations, characteristics and context in which criminal conduct linked to gender-based violence occurs, taking into account the realities of the different departments of the country, in a way that allows the monitoring of security policies and contributes transparency to management.
H)   Train all personnel on a permanent basis on gender-based violence.
I)   Include in the curricula of training at all educational levels of the National Directorate of Police Education, theoretical and practical training in women's rights, sexual and reproductive rights, sexual diversity and gender-based violence, from a human rights perspective and taking into account the diversity of ages and situations of disability.

#### Article 25

(Guidelines for the establishment of national defense policies).- The Ministry of National Defense must:
A)   Develop plans and actions to prevent, investigate, sanction and remedy gender-based violence in the military sphere (including the three forces: the Navy, the Army and the Air Force) and combat stereotyped practices and patterns of behavior that naturalize gender-based violence against women. The actions to be developed must incorporate a multidisciplinary approach and shall include the dissemination of regulations relating to the human rights of women and the protocols for their application, awareness-raising and training and information on complaint mechanisms.
B)   Instruct all personnel in the most rigorous respect for the sexual rights of women, in the context of armed conflicts or prevention or assistance actions, rejecting all forms of use of sexual violence or any form of abuse of power as a weapon of war. In particular, military and civilian personnel must be trained on this subject prior to their participation in peace missions in which the country is involved.
C)   Adopt preventive, corrective and sanctioning measures for all forms of gender-based violence, whether intra-institutional or exercised with respect to the population whose security must be ensured, both in the country and in missions abroad, taking into account the recommendations and resolutions of international organizations on the matter.
D)   Review military regulations to eliminate all forms of discrimination against women in entry or continuation of military careers.
E)   Include in the curricula of military training schools training in human rights of women and gender-based violence.

#### Article 26

(Guidelines for communication policies).- The organs and organisms linked to communication policies, within the scope of their competencies, must:
A)   Promote knowledge of the rights of women, especially the right to a life free of violence. The designs, supports and contents of communication must be accessible to persons with disabilities.
B)   Contribute to the modification of socio-cultural patterns of conduct based on the idea of inferiority or subordination of women.
C)   Promote codes of ethics that combat content that reinforces, justifies or tolerates violence against women.
D)   Guarantee respect for the rights of women, their image and their privacy and, in particular, the rights of girls, boys and adolescents.

#### Article 27

(Guidelines for foreign relations policies).- Consular and diplomatic missions abroad, must:
A)   Provide information to Uruguayan women who are abroad about their rights, gender-based violence and its manifestations and the procedures to follow for filing complaints and requesting protection if applicable.
B)   Accompany and support Uruguayan women in situations of gender-based violence to carry out the necessary procedures before the judicial and police authorities of the country in which they are located.
C)   Facilitate the contact of women victims of gender-based violence with national authorities and with their family members insofar as it is necessary for their protection and safety or that of their sons and daughters or other persons in their care.
D)   Facilitate the documentation necessary for the return to Uruguay of Uruguayan women victims of gender-based violence abroad and of persons in their care, provided that they so request, as well as their repatriation, as appropriate.

#### Article 28

(Guidelines for childhood and adolescence policies).- The Institute for Children and Adolescents of Uruguay, the National Institute for Adolescent Inclusion and all other organs and bodies linked to childhood and adolescence policies and institutions providing care to girls, boys and adolescents, within the scope of their competencies, must:
A)   Contribute to the development of interinstitutional policies for
the prevention of gender-based violence, its early detection
and comprehensive care for girls, boys and adolescents,
implementing strategies aimed at preventing
institutionalization and the loss of family care.
B)   Promote the overcoming of gender stereotypes in
public policies, in care programs and services, in
the image of adolescent girls and girls in the
media, in public performances and in advertising.
C)   Develop campaigns for the prevention of gender-based
violence towards girls and adolescents.
D)   Provide information to girls and adolescents, appropriate to the
different stages of growth, socio-cultural context and to
situations of disability, about their rights as women, in
particular, about equality and non-discrimination based on
gender stereotypes as well as about the right to a life free
from violence.
E)   Ensure equal treatment, non-discrimination and non-
reproduction of gender roles and stereotypes in
institutions that serve girls, boys or adolescents,
including pregnant and mother adolescents.
F)   Incorporate the gender perspective in interventions with the
families of children and adolescents, promoting the
co-responsibility of men in care, avoiding
stereotyped patterns that identify women as solely
responsible for care or for the loss of care.
G)   Adopt measures for the prevention, early detection, care,
protection and reparation of gender-based violence in the
intra-institutional sphere.
H)   The institutionalization of girls, boys or adolescents must be
oriented towards the transitory nature of the measure and the restoration of
family life free from violence in the shortest possible time.
Territorial proximity to the place of
residence of family members or other adult references with whom
they have a positive bond must be ensured, contacts between
them must be facilitated and siblings must not be separated.
I)   Promote the issuance of procedures for reporting, investigating
and sanctioning intra-institutional violence, which ensure
immediate protection and guarantee non-repetition. Any form of
sexual violence or mistreatment by officials or workers of
care services for girls, boys or adolescents shall be
considered a serious offense.
J)   Establish as a requirement for entry and hiring of Personnel,
the absence of administrative or criminal records in matters
of physical, psychological, sexual, domestic violence,
considering it a disqualifying requirement for the function.

#### Article 29

(Guidelines for policies on older persons).- The National Institute for the Elderly, all organs and bodies linked to policies on older persons, as well as institutions providing care to them, must:
A)   Mainstream the gender perspective in actions for
prevention, protection, care and reparation of violence
against older women.
B)   Disseminate information directed to older women in particular
and to society as a whole regarding the various
manifestations of violence in old age, providing
tools for its identification and prevention.
C)   Train and raise awareness about the various forms of gender-
based violence among all personnel of organs and bodies
with responsibility in the subject matter, those in charge of
social and health services, personnel in charge of the
care and care of older women in
long-term care services or home services, in order to
provide them with dignified treatment and prevent negligence and
actions or practices of violence and mistreatment.
D)   Adopt measures to guarantee the right to self-determination
of older women residing in medium-
and long-term establishments, respect for gender identity and
sexual orientation, privacy of partner visits and intimacy
for acts of personal hygiene.
E)   Develop control and supervision programs for
care services for older women that allow the prevention,
detection and sanction of gender-based violence.
F)   Facilitate the access of older women to
appropriate and effective reporting mechanisms and reparation for gender-
based violence, taking especially into account
situations of greater vulnerability. Generate protocols for
actions to follow in agreement with police and
judicial authorities.
G)   Detect and promote the elimination of
administrative or financial practices that discriminate against older
women in the exercise of their property or
economic rights.

#### Article 30

(Guidelines for policies on disability).- The National Honorary Commission on Disability, the National Disability Program of the Ministry of Social Development, as well as all organs and bodies competent in the matter of care for persons in situations of disability must:
A)   Provide accessible information to women in situations of
disability, to their families, caregivers and to the general
population with the purpose of preventing, recognizing and reporting gender-
based violence towards women with disabilities.
B)   Develop actions to strengthen the personal and
collective identity of women in situations of disability and promote
the overcoming of stereotypes, prejudices and harmful
practices based on gender.
C)   Guarantee that all services and programs directed to
persons in situations of disability are supervised in a manner
to prevent, protect, sanction and repair gender-
based violence.
D)   Strengthen the mechanisms and processes for reporting and investigating
gender-based violence towards women with
disabilities, mainstreaming the disability perspective
in the programs, plans, actions and protocols of the
institutions involved.
E)   Develop actions to guarantee respect for the sexual
and reproductive rights of women in situations of
disability, ensure that they have access to information
appropriate for their age, offer the necessary means that
allow them to exercise their rights and respect the right to the
exercise of their sexuality, their reproductive capacity, their
gender identity and their sexual orientation.
F)   Develop studies and research on the forms of
gender-based violence towards women in situations of
disability.
G)   Ensure quality sexual and reproductive health services,
accessible to women in situations of disability.
H)   Provide appropriate assistance to women in situations of
disability for the performance of their
parenting responsibilities and guarantee that in no case shall a child
or adolescent be separated from his or her mother by reason of the disability, of
the child, of the mother or of both.

## CHAPTER IV

NETWORK OF CARE SERVICES FOR WOMEN IN SITUATIONS OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE

#### Article 31

(Service Network for Care).- The Service Network for Care of Women in Situations of Gender-Based Violence must be multisectoral, with each body, agency or institution being responsible for providing responses according to its competence, in accordance with legal provisions and policies formulated by the National Women's Institute.
The Network shall promote immediate response services, psychosocial health care and legal representation. Likewise, the Network shall promote responses for permanence in the educational and labor systems, emergency and medium-term housing for women and socialization services for men who have exercised violence.

#### Article 32

(Care services).- Care services shall be free of charge and shall be provided in all departments of the country. They shall offer psychosocial care, legal advice and representation and shall be integrated with specialized interdisciplinary teams. To serve persons with disabilities, the services shall coordinate with the National Disability Program for the purpose of having specialized personnel.
Care services shall be provided by the National Women's Institute and the Institute for Children and Adolescents of Uruguay coordinating actions with each other and with health, education, housing services, as well as with educational and labor insertion programs and the National Integrated Care System, among others, and may enter into agreements with civil society organizations.
The National Women's Institute shall coordinate actions with the National Institute for the Elderly in order to ensure adequate support during the complaint and care process, as well as to respond to housing needs that may result as a consequence of the situation of gender-based violence.

#### Article 33

(Socialization services for men).- The National Women's Institute shall promote the implementation of care services for the socialization of men who exercise violence against women.

#### Article 34

(Mobile teams).- Providers of care services to women in situations of gender-based violence shall promote access to them by women in rural areas or with mobility difficulties, through mobile teams to provide initial responses. These teams must be composed of specialized personnel and operate with the frequency and composition that allow for effective and quality intervention.

#### Article 35

(Health care in situations of gender-based violence).- Health care institutions, public or private, with partial or comprehensive coverage, must provide comprehensive health services to women who have experienced situations of gender-based violence, as well as to their dependent daughters and sons, in accordance with regulations issued by the Ministry of Public Health in coordination with the National Women's Institute.
Services must ensure differentiated care according to the particular needs and circumstances of women and their sons and daughters and especially, of those who are in conditions of vulnerability or risk. In addition they must:
A)   Have a specific multidisciplinary reference team on gender-based
violence, in which at least one of its members is a male or female
physician.
B)   Implement measures for prevention, early detection, care and
intervention in situations of gender-based violence against women.
C)   Provide specific responses in emergency and urgent care services
for the comprehensive assistance of women and their sons and
daughters.
D)   Ensure universal access to emergency contraception and post-
exposure prophylaxis, in situations of sexual violence.
E)   Conduct monitoring and evaluation of the impact on the health of
women affected by violence, giving special attention to mental and
emotional health.
F)   Ensure timely care for persons in the care of women victims of
femicide or attempted femicide or other forms of gender-based
violence.
G)   Provide institutional mechanisms for reporting in situations that
require it in accordance with current regulations and according to
the protocols that are defined
The Ministry of Public Health shall issue directives to ensure the continuous training of technical teams and coordination among the different health services, with the participation of health care providers in the instances to which they are convened being mandatory.

#### Article 36

(Housing responses).- The National Women's Institute must have different housing responses for women in processes of leaving situations of gender-based violence, both for moments of crisis and risk to life, as well as during the process of socio-labor strengthening to contribute to their economic autonomy. They shall include accommodation, protection and guidance for women and their dependent sons and daughters, if any, and have building accessibility for persons with disabilities.
For such purposes, the National Women's Institute may execute these services directly, in coordination with the Ministry of Housing, Territorial Planning and Environment or through agreements with departmental or municipal governments or with civil society organizations with recognized experience in the matter.

#### Article 37

Those responsible for the programs of the Public Housing System shall provide the necessary measures to guarantee, for victims of gender-based violence, permanence in the dwelling they inhabit. In cases where this permanence involves the payment of a monetary installment to the program, it shall be adjusted to the economic possibilities of the victims. Outside of these cases, the permanence objective established in this article shall be taken into account for the purpose of adjusting the obligations provided for each allocation modality.

#### Article 38

(Assignment of loan or lease contracts).- When in judicial proceedings a measure of removal from the home has been ordered with respect to the holder of a loan or lease contract for a property intended as a dwelling, the legal assignment of the contract in favor of the victim who cohabited with that person shall always operate provided that the consent of the victim and the guarantor is given, and must be communicated to the lessor or lender and where applicable to the guarantor within a period not exceeding sixty working days.

(Measures to ensure the permanence of victims of gender-based violence in the education system).- All educational institutions, public and private, must:
A)   Provide measures to guarantee the immediate enrollment of

B)   Implement concrete actions to give effect to the

C)   Ensure that protection measures for women victims of

education if they are students, in particular, provide that
absences from educational centers may be justified due to
their attendance at police or judicial proceedings or due
to possible transfers of their place of residence.
D)   Provide measures to ensure that victims of sexual
harassment in the educational environment are not harmed in
the exercise of their right to education.

#### Article 40

(Measures to ensure the permanence of women in work).-Women victims of gender-based violence have the following rights:
A)   To receive full payment of their salary or wage for the
time required to attend hearings, expert assessments or
other proceedings or administrative or judicial instances
that are arranged within the framework of the processes
provided for in Chapter IV of this law.
B)   To extraordinary leave with pay for a period of twenty-
four hours from the filing of the complaint with the police
or judicial authorities, extendable for an equal period in
the event that precautionary measures are ordered by the
court.
C)   To the flexibility and change of their schedule or place of
work, provided that the possibility exists and they so
request.
D)   To ensure that the protection measures adopted in the face
of the situation of gender-based violence do not affect
their right to work and functional or labor career.
E)   To ensure that measures are provided so that gender-based
violence in the workplace does not negatively affect the
functional career and the exercise of the right to work.
F)   To stability in their job position. For a period of six
months from the imposition of precautionary measures for
acts of gender-based violence, women in whose favor they
have been ordered may not be dismissed. If they are, the
employer must pay them an amount equivalent to six months'
salary plus the legal compensation that corresponds.

#### Article 41

(Measures for the labor insertion of women).- The Ministry of Social Development and the Ministry of Labor and Social Security must provide quotas, or other mechanisms they deem appropriate, to facilitate the integration of women victims of gender-based violence into labor insertion, training and micro-enterprise programs.

#### Article 42

(Measures for the care of girls, boys and adolescents in the charge of women victims of gender-based violence).- The National Secretariat of Care of the National Integrated Care System must provide measures to respond to the situation of women victims of gender violence with dependents in their charge.

#### Article 43

(Migrant women).- Migrant women victims of gender-based violence in the country of origin or in the national territory, shall be included in the provisions set forth in article 162 of Law No. 19,355, of December 19, 2015.

## CHAPTER V

PROCESSES OF PROTECTION, INVESTIGATION AND PENALIZATION OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

### SECTION I - PROVISIONS COMMON TO ALL PROCESSES

#### Article 45

(Priority interest).- In the face of situations of gender-based violence, the priority must be the comprehensive protection of human dignity and the safety of the victim and their family environment, with special guarantee of the rights recognized in articles 7, 8 and 9 of this law.
References to the article

#### Article 46

(Assessment of evidence).- Without prejudice to the provisions of article 140 of the General Code of Procedure, it must be especially taken into account that acts of violence constitute, in general, situations linked to intimacy or that are carried out without the presence of third parties. The silence, lack of resistance or the previous or subsequent sexual history of the victim of a sexual assault, must not be assessed as demonstration of acceptance or consent to the conduct. The difference in age, economic condition, gifts, presents and other forms of compensation, shall be assessed as indicators of abuse of power in situations of sexual abuse against girls, boys or adolescents.
In all cases, the right and best interest of girls, boys and adolescents to give their opinion shall be respected, which must be analyzed applying the rules of sound criticism. It shall not be valid to use technical arguments to diminish the credibility of their testimonies.
References to the article

(Intra-institutional sphere).- Public and private organs, organisms and institutions must adopt measures for the prevention, protection, investigation and sanction of gender-based violence that occurs in the intra-institutional sphere, exercised by their personnel with respect to other officials, workers or users of the services.

### SECTION IV - PROTECTION PROCEEDINGS IN THE JUDICIAL SPHERE

#### Article 59

(Complaint).- Any person who becomes aware of an act of gender-based violence may, by any means, give notice to the competent Court or Prosecutor's Office, which shall immediately adopt the urgent protection measures they deem pertinent in accordance with the provisions of this law. Provided that the notice is credible, no liability of any kind shall attach to the person who gave it. Within the first and most urgent proceedings, the court or the prosecutor's office shall ensure that the victim has legal defense assured, ordering what is necessary for that purpose. The protection proceeding in the judicial sphere shall be governed by the provisions of the General Code of Procedure, insofar as they do not conflict with this law.

#### Article 62

(Duty to appear).- The accused is obliged to appear under penalty of being brought before the Court with the assistance of the police. #### Article 63

(Prohibition of confrontation).- The parties must be heard separately under the most serious responsibility of the acting Judge and in no case may they be present together in the same room. Effective measures must also be adopted to guarantee the safety of the victim and separate presence in the judicial premises or space.
In these proceedings, mediation and conciliation are prohibited.
References to the article

#### Article 64

(Generic precautionary measures).- Whenever it is established that a fundamental human right is violated or threatened, the Court must order, ex officio, at the request of a party or of the Public Prosecutor's Office, in a reasoned manner, all measures aimed at protecting the life, physical or emotional integrity of the victim, his or her freedom and personal safety, as well as the economic assistance and patrimonial integrity of the victim and his or her family unit.
In no case may reciprocal measures be ordered or measures at the expense of the victims or that restrict their rights.
If the Court decides not to adopt any measure, its decision must state the grounds for such determination.
References to the article

#### Article 65

(Special precautionary measures).- For the fulfillment of the precautionary purpose, the Court may adopt any of the following measures, or other analogous ones, setting the corresponding term:
A)   Order the aggressor to cease acts of disturbance or intimidation
that, directly or indirectly, he or she carries out towards the
victim.
B)   Prohibit the aggressor from communicating, relating,
interviewing or developing any similar conduct -by himself or
herself or through third parties- in relation to the victim, his
or her sons and daughters and other affected persons, witnesses
or complainants of the act.
C)   Prohibit, restrict or limit the presence of the aggressor at the
domicile or residence of the victim, places of work, study or
others frequented by her or his daughters and sons or other
persons in his or her care, being able to provide monitoring
mechanisms that ensure strict compliance with the measure
ordered, such as technology systems for verification of presence
and location of persons or other analogous ones.
In cases where the right to life or physical integrity of a
victim of domestic or gender-based violence is considered at
risk, the court may order the placement of an electronic
monitoring device on adolescents. For these purposes, the court
must justify what other type of protection is insufficient or
unsuitable. (*)
D)   Order the immediate restitution of the personal objects of the
victim, his or her sons and daughters or other persons in his or
her care.
E)   Seize the weapons that the aggressor has in his or her
possession, which shall remain in custody of the Court, in the
manner it deems appropriate.
F)   Prohibit the aggressor from using, possessing or carrying
firearms, notifying the competent authority for such purposes.
G)   Order the employer to arrange the transfer or suspension of the
accused person, when the violence occurs at the victim's
workplace.
H)   Order corrective measures and other measures to prevent
discrimination or violence against women in the workplace or
institutional environment.
I)   Order medical, educational or analogous services that it deems
essential, by public agencies or other responsible institutions.
J)   Enable the change of health provider, maintaining the rights and
conditions established with respect to the previous provider.
K)   Order the transfer of the victim who is institutionalized in a
residential, hospital or prison center to another place that
ensures his or her fundamental rights.
L)   Order the change of administrator of economic income of any
nature received by women in a situation of disability or in any
other situation of dependence, when the aggressor was the person
who fulfilled that function.
M)   Order the mandatory attendance of the aggressor to
rehabilitation programs.
N)   Order the removal of the aggressor from the common residence and
the immediate delivery of his or her personal effects in the
presence of the Bailiff, it being irrelevant who is the owner of
the property. Likewise, a judicial inventory shall be made of the
movable property that is removed and of that which remains on the
premises, and testimony may be issued at the request of the
parties.
O)   Order the return of the victim to the domicile or residence, in
the presence of the Bailiff, when he or she has left it due to
the situation of gender-based violence.
P)   Order the immediate revocation of mandates that the victim may
have granted to the aggressor for the administration of common
property, notifying the corresponding Registry.
Q)   Prohibit the performance of acts of disposition without the
written consent of the victim or judicial authorization, with
respect to the property of family businesses, including the
property of the family agricultural enterprise when the victim is
the owner or collaborating spouse therein.

#### Article 66

(Minimum term of special precautionary measures).- The minimum duration of the measures provided for in literals B) and C) of article 65 is one hundred eighty days, without prejudice to the possibility of their modification or cessation. The measure of removal from home (literal N) shall be applied with a self-satisfactory character, not being subject to a subsequent term or condition.
In case of non-compliance with the measures ordered by the Court, the aggressor shall be considered to have incurred the offense provided for in article 173 of the Penal Code.

#### Article 67

(Protection measures).- In situations of intrafamily violence against a woman, the resolution ordering protection measures must also resolve:
A)   Provisional alimony in favor of the woman and her sons and
daughters or other persons in her care, in cases where
applicable.
B)   Provisional custody of daughters and sons under eighteen years
of age, who may in no case remain in the care of the aggressor.
C)   Suspension of the aggressor's visits with respect to daughters
and sons under eighteen years of age. These may resume once a
minimum period of three months has elapsed without the
repetition of acts of violence and the aggressor having complied
with the measures imposed.
Exceptionally, and if so requested by the sons or daughters and
it is considered that there is no risk of violation of their
rights, supervised visits may be arranged by an institution or by
an adult person of their trust, who shall be responsible for
their compliance under safe conditions. In no case shall visits
take place during the night or at police premises.
For such purposes, compliance must be given to the provisions of article 8 of the Code of Childhood and Adolescence.
The same criteria must be followed with respect to adults declared incapacitated.

#### Article 68

(Complementary diagnosis).- If necessary, the Court ex officio, at the request of the Prosecutor or the victim, may order a complementary diagnosis to that provided for in literal B) of article 61 of this law.
References to the article

#### Article 69

(Evaluative hearing).- More than thirty days in advance of the cessation of the measures ordered, the Court must convene an evaluative hearing of the situation, in order to determine whether it is appropriate to order the continuity of the measures, their substitution by other measures or their cessation.
In case of non-appearance, the Judge shall order the aggressor's compulsory attendance.
References to the article

#### Article 70

(Exemption from counter-bond).- In proceedings for the adoption of precautionary measures, in everything not provided for by this law, articles 313, 314 and 315 of the General Code of Procedure apply insofar as they are applicable.
No provision of counter-bond shall be required nor shall the validity of protection measures be conditioned on the initiation of any other subsequent proceeding.

### SECTION V - FAMILY PROCEEDINGS

#### Article 71

(Scope of application).- Proceedings in family matters arising from situations of gender-based, domestic or sexual violence shall be governed by the provisions of the General Code of Procedure and the Code of Childhood and Adolescence.
In these proceedings, the context of gender-based violence must be especially taken into account, in order to ensure that the resolutions adopted strengthen the human rights and autonomy of the affected persons.

### SECTION VI - CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS

#### Article 75

(Scope of application).- Criminal proceedings processed before the Specialized Courts on Gender-Based, Domestic and Sexual Violence shall be governed by the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure (Law No. 19,293, of December 19, 2014).
The regime provided for victims and intimidated witnesses (articles 163 and 164 of said legal text) shall apply to all victims of gender-based violence, regardless of their age.
The hearing shall not be public when so requested by the victim and the presence of an emotional companion shall be admitted. Whenever possible, the victim's testimony must be filmed to avoid its repetition.

#### Article 77

(Defense of the victim).- In the proceedings provided for in subsection D) of article 51, the victim may designate specialized institutions in the defense of victims' rights to appear and exercise their rights and interests on their behalf.

#### Article 78

(Criminal action and prescription).- The criminal action with respect to the offenses provided for in articles 272, 272 bis, 272 ter, 273, 273 bis and 274 of the Penal Code is public, not requiring the instance of the offended party.
The prescription of the criminal action is suspended while the victim is a minor and until having reached the age of majority they file the complaint themselves or ratify the one filed by their legal representatives during their minority. If as a consequence of any of the indicated offenses the death of the minor has occurred, it shall begin to run from the day on which they would have reached the age of majority.

#### Article 79

(Suspension of the exercise of parental authority and disqualification for the exercise of public and private functions).- Persons subject to proceedings for the offenses provided for in articles 272, 272 bis, 272 ter, 273, 273 bis, and 274 of the Penal Code and in Law No. 17,815, of September 6, 2004, are suspended in the exercise of parental authority or guardianship and disqualified for the exercise of public and private functions in the educational area, health and all those that involve direct contact with girls, boys and adolescents, persons with disabilities and elderly persons in situations of dependency, for a period of ten years if a conviction is handed down or until their dismissal or acquittal.
The suspension and disqualification for the exercise of parental authority and functions established in this article shall be double that provided for therein in case of recidivism. (*)

#### Article 92

(Disclosure of images or recordings with intimate content).- Whoever disseminates, reveals, exhibits or transfers to third parties images or recordings of a person with intimate or sexual content, without their authorization, shall be punished with a penalty of six months imprisonment to two years penitentiary.
In no case shall authorization granted by a person under eighteen years of age be considered valid. This offense is configured even when the person who disseminates the images or recordings has participated in them.
The administrators of internet sites, portals, search engines or similar that, notified of the lack of authorization, do not take down the images immediately, shall be sanctioned with the same penalty provided for in this article.

#### Article 93

(Special aggravating circumstances).- The penalty provided for in the preceding article shall be increased by one third to one half when:
A)   The disseminated images or recordings have been obtained without the
consent of the affected person.
B)   It was committed with respect to the spouse, cohabitant or person who is or
has been united by an analogous relationship of affection, even without
cohabitation.
C)   The victim was under eighteen years of age.
D)   The victim was a person in a situation of disability.
E)   The acts had been committed with a lucrative purpose.

## CHAPTER VII - FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 95

Law No. 17,514, of July 2, 2002, shall be applicable to situations of domestic violence with respect to male victims, including boys and adolescents.

Articles 24 to 29 of Law No. 17,514, of July 2, 2002, are repealed.
