# LAW OF THE KYRGYZ REPUBLIC

LAW OF THE KYRGYZ REPUBLIC

of April 27, 2017 No. 63
On Protection and Defense from Domestic Violence

Adopted by the Jogorku Kenesh of the Kyrgyz Republic

March 16, 2017

(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)
See also:
Resolution of the Government of the KR of August 1, 2019 No. 390 "On the Procedure for Implementation of Protection and Defense from Domestic Violence"

## Chapter 1

General Provisions Article 1. Basic Concepts
The following basic concepts are used in this Law:
1) safe place - a territory or premises in which, in the opinion of a person who has suffered from domestic violence, or their legal representative, there is no danger to their life or health;
2) consultative-preventive center (crisis center) - an organization providing social-psychological, as well as legal and medical assistance to persons who have suffered from domestic violence. A shelter may be established at the consultative-preventive center;
3) temporary protective order - a document providing state protection to a person who has suffered from domestic violence and entailing the application of measures of influence established by this Law to a person who has committed domestic violence;
4) person who has suffered from domestic violence - a family member or a person equated to a family member against whom domestic violence has been committed, as well as a minor who has become a witness to domestic violence;
5) neglectful treatment - intentional failure to fulfill obligations to care for family members and persons equated to them;

6) suppression of domestic violence - measures aimed at stopping domestic violence, ensuring the protection of life, health, honor and dignity of persons who have suffered from domestic violence;
7) persons equated to family members - persons in actual marital relations; a person providing support to a disabled or minor dependent, and directly the disabled or minor dependent themselves; parents of spouses; other relatives living together;
8) prevention (prophylaxis) of domestic violence - a system of social, legal, medical, psychological, educational, informational and other measures carried out by subjects of protection and defense from domestic violence, aimed at identifying and eliminating causes and conditions creating a danger of committing domestic violence;
9) psychological violence - threat of committing physical, sexual, economic violence, as well as intentional humiliation of honor and dignity, coercion to commit offenses or acts that pose a danger to life or lead to violation of mental or physical health, as well as restriction of adult persons in the right to communication;
10) domestic violence - intentional actions of a physical, psychological, economic nature or their threat, as well as neglectful treatment, committed by one family member/person equated to them against another family member/person equated to them;
11) social service - an organization, institution, regardless of organizational-legal forms and forms of ownership, providing social services and assistance to persons who have suffered from domestic violence;
12) shelter (temporary residence center for persons who have suffered from domestic violence) - an organization providing safe temporary accommodation to persons who have suffered from domestic violence. Shelters are organized for women and men separately;
13) physical violence - direct or indirect intentional physical impact of one family member/person equated to them on another family member/person equated to them, deprivation of the ability to perform vital functions, deprivation or restriction of freedom of movement, housing, food, clothing, coercion to heavy physical labor, as well as evasion of obligations for care and support;
14) family members - spouses, parents and children, grandmothers/grandfathers and grandsons/granddaughters, guardians/trustees and wards, adoptive parents and adopted children, foster parents and foster children, other persons who have taken children for upbringing in the manner established by law, former spouses, stepmothers/stepfathers and stepdaughters/stepsons, brothers and sisters (full and half), actual educators and actual pupils;
15) economic violence - intentional failure by one family member/person equated to a family member to fulfill obligations to support another family member/person equated to them, as well as intentional deprivation or restriction of the right to receive or dispose of property or income intended by law for a person, and/or use of funds and property by a trusted person to the detriment of the trustor.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Article 2

Legislation of the Kyrgyz Republic on Protection and Defense from Domestic Violence
The legislation of the Kyrgyz Republic on protection and defense from domestic violence is based on the Constitution of the Kyrgyz Republic, consists of this Law and other normative legal acts of the Kyrgyz Republic, as well as international treaties that have entered into force in the manner established by law, to which the Kyrgyz Republic is a party, and generally recognized principles and norms of international law.

### Article 3. Scope of Application of this Law

1. This Law defines the legal basis for the prevention and suppression of domestic violence, ensuring social and legal protection and defense of persons who have suffered from domestic violence.
2. The effect of this Law extends to family members and persons equated to family members living together.

### Article 4. Principles of this Law

This Law is based on the following principles:
1) compliance with legislation and international standards in the field of human rights and gender equality;
2) equality of access to protection and defense from domestic violence;
3) non-admission of folk customs and traditions that infringe upon human rights and freedoms and contribute to domestic violence;
4) responsibility for domestic violence committed;
5) preventive orientation;
6) responsibility of state bodies and local self-government bodies for compliance with the right of citizens to protection from domestic violence;
7) participation of the public in protection and defense from domestic violence;
8) provision of protection from domestic violence regardless of the existence of criminal proceedings or proceedings on a misdemeanor.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Article 5. Rights of Persons Who Have Suffered from Domestic Violence

1. A person who has suffered from domestic violence has the right to receive legal, social, medical, psychological assistance and protection from domestic violence from state bodies, local self-government bodies and other subjects carrying out protection and defense from domestic violence, within their competence.
2. The grounds and procedure for providing protection, assistance and defense from domestic violence are determined by this Law.

## Chapter 2. Subjects Carrying Out Protection and Defense from Domestic

Violence

### Article 6. Range of Subjects Carrying Out Protection and Defense from Domestic Violence

The subjects carrying out protection and defense from domestic violence include:

1) the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic;
2) the authorized state body for coordinating the activities of subjects on protection and defense from domestic violence, determined by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic;
3) court;
4) prosecutor's office;
5) internal affairs bodies;
6) social development bodies;
7) health care bodies;
8) education bodies;
9) justice bodies;
10) authorized state body for the protection of children;
11) Akyikatchy (Ombudsman) of the Kyrgyz Republic;
12) bar;
13) local state administrations;
14) local self-government bodies;
15) aksakal court;
16) mass media;
17) other organizations within their competence.

### Article 7

Powers of the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic on protection and defense against domestic violence
The Government of the Kyrgyz Republic in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence:
1) ensures the implementation of a unified state policy in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
2) develops and implements state programs on protection and defense against domestic violence;
3) determines the powers of state bodies in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
4) determines the authorized state body for coordinating the activities of subjects on protection and defense against domestic violence;
5) determines the procedure for interaction of state bodies carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
6) adopts within its competence normative legal acts, and also initiates draft laws aimed at protection and defense against domestic violence;
7) exercises control over the activities of state bodies in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
8) provides assistance to local self-government bodies in their activities on protection and defense against domestic violence;

9) approves state standards for the provision of services in accordance with legislation on state and municipal services and the provision of assistance in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
10) implements other powers in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence provided for by the legislation of the Kyrgyz Republic.

### Article 8

Authorized state body for coordinating the activities of subjects on protection and defense against domestic violence
For the purposes of implementing the powers determined by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic, the authorized state body for coordinating the activities of subjects on protection and defense against domestic violence (hereinafter - the authorized body):
1) coordinates activities and interacts with other subjects on protection and defense against domestic violence defined by this Law;
2) carries out collection, generalization and analysis of data on domestic violence, conducts assessment of the effectiveness of the system of measures on protection and defense against domestic violence and submits proposals for its improvement;
3) provides methodological, informational, consultative and organizational assistance to subjects carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
4) coordinates the development of correctional programs for work with persons who have committed domestic violence;
5) carries out monitoring of the activities of subjects on protection and defense against domestic violence taking into account the guarantee of their independence defined by relevant laws, and sends recommendations for improving their activities in this sphere;
6) organizes the conduct of scientific and other research in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
7) carries out informational and educational activities in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
8) provides information on protection and defense against domestic violence to mass media and electronic publications;
9) annually compiles and publishes reports on the implementation of this Law;
10) ensures access to information in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence, including by posting it on its website;
11) provides assistance to subjects carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence in attracting extra-budgetary funds and developing cooperation with international organizations, natural and legal persons;
12) carries out other activities in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence within its competence.

### Article 9. Powers of the procuracy bodies on protection and defense against domestic violence

The procuracy bodies in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence:

1) exercise supervision over compliance with legislation in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
2) apply to court for the purpose of protecting the rights and interests of persons who have suffered from domestic violence who, due to their state of health, age or other reasons, cannot personally defend their rights and freedoms in court;
3) carry out advanced training of procuracy employees on issues of protection and defense against domestic violence;
4) exercise other powers in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence provided for by normative legal acts of the Kyrgyz Republic.

### Article 10

Powers of internal affairs bodies on protection and defense against domestic violence
1. For the purposes of implementing the powers determined by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic, the Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Kyrgyz Republic:
1) organizes the activities of internal affairs bodies on prevention, protection and defense against domestic violence;
2) includes in the training programs of educational institutions of internal affairs bodies issues of protection and defense against domestic violence, ensures professional training, retraining courses, advanced training and other relevant types of training of its employees in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
3) carries out monitoring of the activities of internal affairs bodies in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
4) interacts with other subjects carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
5) submits an annual report, as well as information on its activities on protection and defense against domestic violence to the authorized body;
6) posts on its website statistical and other information on the activities of internal affairs bodies on protection and defense against domestic violence.
2. Internal affairs bodies in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence:
1) carry out suppression of domestic violence;
2) control the fulfillment of conditions of a temporary protection order, and also assist in the execution of a court decision on facts of domestic violence;
3) maintain records of persons who have committed domestic violence and persons who have suffered from domestic violence;
4) notify the authorized state body for the protection of children of identified facts of domestic violence against minors;
5) notify subjects of protection and defense against domestic violence indicated by the person who has suffered from domestic violence, with his or her consent, of facts of domestic violence;
6) refer the person who has suffered from domestic violence, with his or her consent, for medical examination, forensic medical examination. In the event that a minor has suffered from his or her parents or other legal

representatives, he or she is referred for medical examination, forensic medical examination without their consent;
7) send, with the consent of the person who has suffered from domestic violence, information on the committed violence to the place of work of the person who committed domestic violence;
8) interact with other subjects carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
9) conduct preventive and informational work with the population in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
10) carry out other activities on protection and defense against domestic violence within their competence.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Статья 11

Powers of social development bodies for protection and defense against domestic violence
1. In order to implement the powers defined by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic, the authorized state body in the field of social development:
1) organizes the activities of social development bodies for protection and social defense against domestic violence;
2) monitors the activities of social development bodies in the field of protection and social defense against domestic violence, including compliance with minimum standards for the provision of social services and the procedure for their provision by state and municipal organizations and institutions in the field of protection and defense against domestic violence;
3) coordinates the activities of social development bodies and institutions in the field of protection and social defense against domestic violence;
4) provides methodological and consultative assistance to territorial social development bodies and local self-government bodies on issues of protection and social defense against domestic violence;
5) ensures the organization and development of state and municipal social services providing social services on issues of protection and social defense against domestic violence;
6) organizes programs for advanced training of social development body employees and social workers on identification, prevention and protection against domestic violence;
7) participates in the development of correctional programs for work with persons who have committed domestic violence, and provides assistance to territorial social development bodies in their implementation;
8) determines the procedure for identification, registration and recording by social development bodies of cases of domestic violence;
9) interacts with other entities carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
10) submits an annual report, as well as information about its activities on protection and defense against domestic violence to the authorized body;

11) posts on its website statistical and other information about the activities of social development bodies in the field of protection and defense against domestic violence, as well as information about social services providing social services on issues of protection and defense against domestic violence, minimum service standards and the procedure for their provision.
2. Social development bodies in the field of protection and defense against domestic violence:
1) provide social services and assistance in the field of protection and social defense against domestic violence;
2) notify within twenty-four hours the internal affairs bodies of identified facts of domestic violence with the consent of the person who suffered from domestic violence. The consent of victims is not required in cases of domestic violence committed against minors and/or persons declared legally incapable;
3) conduct monitoring and analysis of the needs of persons who suffered from domestic violence in the provision of social services and assistance;
4) implement correctional programs for work with persons who have committed domestic violence;
5) carry out information and educational activities in the field of protection and defense against domestic violence;
6) interact with other entities carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
7) submit reports on activities for protection and defense against domestic violence to the authorized state body in the field of social development;
8) carry out other activities for protection and defense against domestic violence within their competence.

### Статья 12

Powers of health bodies in the field of protection and defense against domestic violence
1. In order to implement the powers defined by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic, the authorized state body in the field of health:
1) organizes the activities of health organizations for protection and defense against domestic violence;
2) introduces into the practice of health organizations standards of activity of health bodies for protection, defense and provision of medical services in the field of domestic violence and monitors their implementation;
3) monitors the activities of health organizations in the field of protection and defense against domestic violence;
4) organizes training and retraining of medical personnel on issues of prevention and identification of domestic violence and provision of medical and sanitary assistance in cases of domestic violence;
5) carries out information and educational activities in the field of protection and defense against domestic violence;
6) collects data in the field of provision of medical and sanitary assistance related to domestic violence;

7) interacts with other entities carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
8) submits an annual report, as well as information about its activities on protection and defense against domestic violence to the authorized body;
9) posts on its website statistical and other information about the activities of health bodies in the field of protection and defense against domestic violence.
2. Health organizations in the field of protection and defense against domestic violence:
1) provide medical and sanitary assistance, including medical rehabilitation of persons who suffered from domestic violence and persons who have committed domestic violence;
2) notify internal affairs bodies of facts of appeals by persons who suffered from domestic violence and provision of medical assistance to them with their consent within twenty-four hours from the moment of appeal. The consent of persons who suffered from domestic violence is not required in cases of domestic violence committed against minors and/or persons declared legally incapable;
3) issue to the patient an extract from the outpatient medical card or inpatient card;
4) interact with other entities carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
5) carry out other activities for protection and defense against domestic violence within their competence.

### Article 13

Powers of education authorities in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence
1. For the purposes of implementing the powers defined by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic, the authorized state body in the sphere of education:
1) organizes the activities of education authorities on protection and defense against domestic violence;
2) conducts monitoring and analysis of the activities of educational organizations regarding the needs of students subjected to domestic violence in the provision of social services and assistance;
3) includes in state educational standards issues of protection and defense against domestic violence in the context of human rights;
4) ensures the training and retraining of education workers on the identification, prevention and protection against domestic violence;
5) develops educational and upbringing programs for parents (persons replacing them) and children on the prevention and suppression of domestic violence and carries out monitoring and evaluation of their implementation;
6) carries out informational and educational activities in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
7) organizes counseling for students who have suffered from domestic violence;

8) carries out interaction with other subjects of protection and defense against domestic violence;
9) submits an annual report, as well as information about its activities on protection and defense against domestic violence to the authorized body;
10) posts on its website statistical and other information about the activities of education authorities in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence.
2. Educational organizations, regardless of forms of ownership, in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence:
1) notify internal affairs bodies of identified facts of domestic violence against minors;
2) implement educational and upbringing programs for students on the prevention and suppression of domestic violence;
3) inform students, parents or persons replacing them, about the inadmissibility of domestic violence and measures for protection and defense;
4) maintain records, conduct monitoring and analysis of the needs of students subjected to domestic violence in the provision of social services and assistance;
5) interact with other subjects carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
6) carry out other activities on protection and defense against domestic violence within their competence.

### Article 14. Powers of justice authorities in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence

For the purposes of implementing the powers defined by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic, the Ministry of Justice of the Kyrgyz Republic:
1) provides consultative legal assistance, as well as qualified legal aid in accordance with the requirements of legislation on state-guaranteed legal aid;
2) disseminates regulatory legal acts and other information on issues of protection and defense against domestic violence;
3) interacts with other subjects carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
4) submits an annual report, as well as information about its activities on protection and defense against domestic violence to the authorized body;
5) posts on its website statistical and other information about the activities of justice authorities in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence.

### Article 15

Powers of the authorized state body for the protection of children in the sphere of protection and defense of children against domestic violence
For the purposes of implementing the powers defined by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic, the authorized state body for the protection of children:
1) organizes, coordinates and controls the activities of its territorial subdivisions on the protection and defense of children against domestic violence;

2) implements a set of measures for the protection and defense of children who have suffered from domestic violence, in accordance with this Law, the Code of the Kyrgyz Republic on Children;
3) posts on its website statistical and other information about the activities of child protection authorities in the sphere of protection and defense of children against domestic violence, information about social services providing services for the protection and defense of children who have suffered from domestic violence, service standards and the procedure for their provision;
4) provides methodological and consultative assistance to its territorial bodies and other bodies of the child protection system on issues of protection and defense of children against domestic violence, including professional development of their employees;
5) interacts with other subjects carrying out protection and defense of children against domestic violence;
6) submits an annual report, as well as information about its activities on the protection and defense of children against domestic violence to the authorized body.

### Article 16

Powers of the Akyikatchy (Ombudsman) of the Kyrgyz Republic in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence
The Akyikatchy (Ombudsman) of the Kyrgyz Republic:
1) sends to state bodies, local self-government bodies, enterprises, institutions and organizations regardless of forms of ownership representations with a petition to initiate disciplinary proceedings or pre-trial proceedings in a criminal case against officials and employees whose decisions or actions (inaction) resulted in violation of human rights and freedoms in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
2) based on the results of consideration of applications and complaints of citizens, appeals to court in defense of persons whose rights and freedoms have been violated by decisions or actions (inaction) of a state body, local self-government body in the sphere of protection against domestic violence or their official, and who due to health condition or other valid reasons cannot do so independently;
3) prepares special reports on issues of domestic violence and/or includes these issues in annual reports on the state of observance of human and civil rights and freedoms;
4) posts on its website statistical and other information about the activities of the Akyikatchy (Ombudsman) of the Kyrgyz Republic in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Article 17

Powers of the Bar of the Kyrgyz Republic in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence
An advocate in carrying out advocacy activities:
1) provides consultations on legal issues both in oral and written form;
2) drafts applications, complaints, petitions and other documents of a legal nature;

3) participates in civil, administrative, criminal proceedings and proceedings on violations;
4) provides other assistance in accordance with the law on advocacy.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Article 18

Powers of local state administrations in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence
Local state administrations:
1) ensure and coordinate the coordinated activities of state territorial subdivisions of ministries, state committees, administrative departments and other state bodies in cooperation with local self-government bodies and exercise state control over their activities on delegated functions and powers in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence;
2) include in programs of socio-economic development of the territory and social protection of the population measures for protection and defense against domestic violence, control their implementation;
3) carry out consolidation and analysis of information received from local self-government bodies on activities for protection and defense against domestic violence;
4) organize programs for professional development of employees of local state administrations and local self-government on issues of protection and defense against domestic violence;
5) interact with other subjects carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence;
6) organize informational, educational activities on issues of protection and defense against domestic violence;
7) attract financial, technical and other assistance in accordance with legislation for the implementation of programs on protection and defense against domestic violence;
8) submit an annual consolidated report on activities for protection and defense against domestic violence to the authorized body.

### Article 19

Powers of local self-government bodies for protection and defense against domestic violence
1. Local keneshes (city, ayil) in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence:
1) approve and monitor the implementation of measures for protection and defense against domestic violence within the framework of programs for socio-economic development of the territory and social protection of the population;
2) approve standards for the provision of municipal services in accordance with legislation on municipal services and the provision of assistance by local self-government bodies for protection and defense against domestic violence;
3) provide for funds in the local budget for the implementation of relevant programs for the prevention of domestic violence and the creation of social services carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence.
2. Executive bodies of local self-government (mayoralties, ayil okmotu):

1) organize the provision of social services on issues of protection and defense against domestic violence in accordance with legislation on the fundamentals of social services for the population;
2) develop and implement programs for the prevention of domestic violence and create social services carrying out protection and defense of citizens against domestic violence;
3) carry out monitoring and evaluation of the effectiveness and efficiency of adopted programs;
4) attract investments and grants for the implementation of local programs for protection and defense against domestic violence;
5) maintain records of data on domestic violence in their territory;
6) provide assistance for interdepartmental coordination and interaction of entities carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence at the local level through the creation of special bodies - local committees for protection and defense against domestic violence;
7) inform internal affairs bodies of the presence of danger of committing domestic violence;
8) participate in programs for professional development of local self-government employees on issues of protection and defense against domestic violence;
9) publish on their websites information on the implementation of measures for protection and defense against domestic violence.

### Article 20. Powers of aksakal courts in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence

Aksakal courts consider and resolve cases related to domestic violence in the manner provided by the law on aksakal courts.

### Article 21

Powers of mass media financed from the republican and local budgets in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence
1. Mass media:
1) interact with entities of protection and defense against domestic violence;
2) cover issues of protection and defense against domestic violence with the aim of forming in public consciousness rejection of any forms of violence;
3) provide information on their activities for protection and defense against domestic violence at the request of the authorized body;
4) ensure in their editorial policy the prevention of propaganda of gender discrimination and violence;
5) participate in programs for professional development of correspondents and journalists on covering issues of protection and defense against domestic violence.
2. Mass media are obliged to place information on issues of protection and defense against domestic violence within the limits of airtime or print space allocated for social advertising.

### Article 22

Participation of commercial and non-commercial organizations and citizens in protection and defense against domestic violence

1. Commercial and non-commercial organizations and citizens have the right to:
1) provide social, legal, juridical, psychological and other support and assistance in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence in accordance with legislation, including:
a) inform persons who have suffered from domestic violence of the right to seek protection from state bodies or public organizations;
b) provide persons who have suffered from domestic violence with appropriate consultations;
c) notify, with the consent of persons who have suffered from domestic violence, internal affairs bodies, prosecutor's offices and local self-government, social protection of facts of domestic violence, with the exception of cases concerning minors and persons declared legally incapable, when notification of the indicated bodies is mandatory;
2) carry out informational and educational activities on issues of protection and defense against domestic violence;
3) interact with other entities of protection and defense against domestic violence.
2. Commercial and non-commercial organizations for protection and defense against domestic violence have the right in cases provided by law to receive financing from the republican and local budgets and other sources.
3. Commercial and non-commercial organizations for protection and defense against domestic violence provide information on their activities for protection and defense against domestic violence at the request of the authorized body.

## Chapter 3

Provision of protection and defense against domestic violence Article 23. Prevention (prophylaxis) of domestic violence
1. Prevention (prophylaxis) of domestic violence is carried out by all entities for protection and defense against domestic violence within their competence.
2. Measures of prevention (prophylaxis) include:
1) development and implementation of programs for the prevention of domestic violence, including special programs for persons who have suffered from domestic violence and for persons who have committed domestic violence;
2) preventive conversations, consultations, including by means of helplines with the aim of preventing and not allowing repetition of cases of domestic violence, as well as ensuring the safety of the victim;
3) registration and record-keeping of persons who have committed domestic violence;
4) monitoring compliance with the conditions of a temporary protective order;
5) informing persons who have committed domestic violence of the inadmissibility and unlawfulness of domestic violence, as well as measures for protection and defense against domestic violence;
6) informational and educational campaigns, events in the sphere of protection and defense against domestic violence aimed at protecting the rights and freedoms

of citizens, promoting non-violent and conflict-free behavior, preventing discrimination against any person.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Article 24. Suppression of domestic violence

1. Types of suppression of domestic violence carried out by internal affairs bodies:
1) taking immediate measures to eliminate the threat to the life or health of a person who has suffered from domestic violence and other family members or persons equated to them;
2) detention of a person who has committed domestic violence in accordance with criminal procedural legislation;
3) issuance and extension of a temporary protective order;
4) bringing a person who has committed domestic violence to criminal responsibility;
5) immediate placement of a person who has suffered from domestic violence, whose life and health are threatened by danger, in a safe place or shelter with his or her consent.
2. Local self-government bodies, social development bodies are obliged to provide assistance to internal affairs bodies in the immediate placement in a safe place of persons who have suffered from domestic violence.
3. Other entities of protection and defense against domestic violence defined by this Law provide assistance to internal affairs bodies in the immediate placement in a safe place of persons who have suffered from domestic violence.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Статья 25

Actions of internal affairs bodies in response to reports of domestic violence
1. Internal affairs bodies are obliged to:
1) take all measures provided for by this Law, the Criminal Procedure Code of the Kyrgyz Republic, the law on internal affairs bodies;
2) accept and register a statement about domestic violence from any persons;
3) explain the procedure for issuing and extending a temporary protection order, the procedure for bringing a person who committed domestic violence to criminal responsibility;
4) provide the person who suffered from domestic violence with information about social services;
5) organize, if necessary, transportation of the person who suffered from domestic violence to a medical institution or other safe place;
6) issue a temporary protection order and monitor compliance with the conditions contained therein;

7) upon application of the person who suffered from domestic violence, extend the temporary protection order and monitor compliance with the conditions contained therein;
8) in case of domestic violence against a minor, immediately inform the territorial subdivision of the authorized state body for the protection of children;
9) with the consent of the person who suffered from domestic violence, inform within three days from the moment of extension of the temporary protection order the territorial subdivisions of social development bodies;
10) perform all necessary actions for transferring materials for bringing the person who committed domestic violence to criminal responsibility.
2. An officer of the internal affairs body has the right, in accordance with the law on internal affairs bodies, to enter unimpeded into premises and onto the territory of citizens if there are grounds to believe that persons whose life and health are threatened are located there, in order to take measures to suppress domestic violence.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Статья 26. Grounds for issuing a temporary protection order

1. The grounds for issuing a temporary protection order is a report by any person about the fact of committing domestic violence to the internal affairs body.
2. The fact of domestic violence is established by the internal affairs body by verifying the information contained in the report about domestic violence.
A temporary protection order is issued to the person who suffered from domestic violence and to the person who committed domestic violence who have reached eighteen years of age.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Статья 27. Issuance and extension of a temporary protection order

1. A temporary protection order is mandatorily issued for a period of three days by the internal affairs body at the place of residence of the person who committed domestic violence, within twenty-four hours from the moment of establishing the fact of committing domestic violence.
2. Upon application of the person who suffered from domestic violence, the internal affairs body extends the temporary protection order for thirty days.
3. An officer of the internal affairs body is obliged to familiarize the person who committed domestic violence with the conditions of the temporary protection order and the consequences of its non-compliance under signature.
4. An officer of the internal affairs body is obliged to inform the person who committed domestic violence and received a temporary protection order about the possibility and conditions of undergoing correctional programs for changing violent behavior under signature.
5. A person who committed domestic violence and received a temporary protection order is obliged to fulfill all conditions specified therein. Non-compliance by such person with the conditions of the temporary protection order entails criminal responsibility and does not terminate its effect.

6. Information about the issuance and/or extension of a temporary protection order is sent to the officer of the internal affairs body authorized to monitor compliance with the conditions of the temporary protection order.
7. Information about the issuance and/or extension of a temporary protection order is sent to the territorial social development body and the local self-government body, as well as to the place of work of the person who committed domestic violence, with the consent of the person who suffered from domestic violence.
8. Issuance, extension of a temporary protection order or refusal in these actions may be appealed to the prosecutor's office or to the court.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Статья 28

Temporary protection order in relation to persons who suffered from domestic violence who have not reached fourteen years of age, or persons declared incapacitated by a court
1. A temporary protection order in relation to persons who suffered from domestic violence who have not reached fourteen years of age or persons declared incapacitated by a court is issued to their legal representative or to the territorial subdivision of the authorized state body for the protection of children, or to an officer of the internal affairs body for juvenile affairs.
2. On behalf of persons who have not reached fourteen years of age, as well as persons declared incapacitated by a court, an application for extension of a temporary protection order for thirty days may be submitted by their legal representative or the territorial subdivision of the authorized state body for the protection of children, or an officer of the internal affairs body for juvenile affairs.
3. Information about the issuance of a temporary protection order is sent by the territorial subdivision of the authorized state body for the protection of children to the territorial social development body, the local self-government body.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Статья 29. Conditions of a temporary protection order

1. A temporary protection order provides for:
1) prohibition to commit domestic violence;
2) prohibition on direct and indirect contacts with the person who suffered from domestic violence.
2. A temporary protection order contains information about the right of the person who suffered from domestic violence to apply to court with the demands specified in part 1 of Article 32 of this Law, as well as information about the responsibility of the person who committed domestic violence for non-compliance with the conditions of the temporary protection order.
3. In case of extension of a temporary protection order, in addition to the conditions listed in part 1 of this Article, it must include a condition on undergoing by the person who committed domestic violence a correctional program for changing violent behavior in accordance with the standard correctional program approved by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic.

4. The form of a temporary protection order is approved by the Government of the Kyrgyz Republic.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Статья 30. Application to court in cases related to domestic violence

1. The right to apply to court for protection of the rights and legitimate interests of a person who suffered from domestic violence is held by:
1) the person who suffered from domestic violence, or his/her representative;
2) the prosecutor;
3) territorial subdivisions of the authorized state body for the protection of children and persons declared incapacitated by a court, in case the person who suffered from domestic violence is a minor or a person declared incapacitated by a court.
2. A copy of the temporary protection order or a copy of a judicial act, or a decision of the aksakals' court on the fact of committing domestic violence against the person who committed it, is attached to the statement of claim on the demands provided for in paragraphs 1 and 2 of part 1 of Article 32 of this Law.
(As amended by the Law of the KR of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Article 31. Time Limits for Filing and Consideration of a Statement of Claim

1.	For claims provided for in paragraphs 1 and 2 of part 1 of Article 32 of this Law, a statement of claim may be filed within six months from the date of issuance of a temporary protection order or from the date of entry into legal force of a judicial act regarding the fact of domestic violence against the person who committed it.
2.	A statement of claim in cases related to domestic violence shall be considered by the court within ten days from the date of receipt of the application by the court, not counting the time provided for by civil procedural legislation for acceptance of the statement of claim for court proceedings and preparation of the case for trial.
3.	For claims provided for in paragraphs 3 and 4 of part 1 of Article 32 of this Law, an application may be filed within the time limits provided for by civil legislation.
(As amended by the Law of the Kyrgyz Republic of April 15, 2020 No. 41)

### Article 32

Claims on Which a Person Affected by Domestic Violence Has the Right to Apply to Court
1.	Persons specified in part 1 of Article 30 of this Law have the right to apply to court with one or more claims against the person who committed domestic violence:
1)	to temporarily evict from the place of joint residence;
2)	to restrict parental rights with respect to contact with minor children;
3)	to recognize the person who committed domestic violence as having limited legal capacity in accordance with civil legislation;
4)	other claims provided for by civil and family legislation.

2.	Restriction of rights under the claims specified in paragraphs 1 and 2 of part 1 of this article shall be established by the court for a period from one to six months.

### Article 33. Execution of Court Decision

1.	A court decision rendered on claims provided for in paragraphs 1 and 2 of part 1 of Article 32 of this Law shall enter into legal force immediately after its pronouncement. The procedure for appealing a court decision shall be established by civil procedural legislation.
2.	A court decision shall be subject to execution in the manner established by legislation on enforcement proceedings.

### Article 34. Assistance to Persons Affected by Domestic Violence

1.	Assistance provided to persons affected by domestic violence includes legal, social, medical, psychological and other types of assistance provided by entities defined by this Law, within their powers and competence.
2.	Legal assistance includes provision of consultations, preparation of legal documents, representation on behalf of a person affected by domestic violence in courts and state bodies, other types of legally significant actions defined by legislation. State-guaranteed legal assistance shall be provided in the manner established by the law on state-guaranteed legal assistance.
3.	Persons affected by domestic violence shall be provided with all types of medical and sanitary assistance defined by legislation on protection of citizens' health.
4.	Social and psychological assistance to persons affected by domestic violence includes:
1)	implementation of social protection measures;
2)	provision of assistance in obtaining necessary documents and social benefits in accordance with legislation on state benefits;
3)	provision of consultations of a social worker, psychologist;
4)	provision of a safe place in social services for the purpose of ensuring their safety;
5)	assistance in social rehabilitation;
6)	social work with families in which cases of domestic violence have been identified.

### Article 35. Rights of Persons Affected by Domestic Violence

1.	Persons affected by domestic violence have the right to receive social and psychological assistance in state and municipal institutions, as well as in social service institutions: consultative and preventive centers (crisis centers), shelters, as well as in other institutions in accordance with this Law and legislation on social services for the population.
2.	The basis for provision of social and psychological assistance shall be an application for such assistance by persons affected by domestic violence, as well as entities carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence, defined by this Law.

3.	A person affected by domestic violence has the right to placement in a state or municipal shelter for safe temporary residence. Stay in a shelter shall be carried out free of charge on the basis of an agreement in accordance with the rules defined in the charter or regulations of the shelter.
4.	A person affected by domestic violence has the right to receive information about opportunities for education, vocational training and retraining, employment and other forms of social rehabilitation.

## Chapter 4

Statistical Accounting and Reporting on Domestic Violence Article 36. Statistical Accounting of Cases of Domestic Violence
1.	The National Statistical Committee of the Kyrgyz Republic shall ensure coordination of statistical activities of state bodies, local self-government bodies and consultative and preventive centers by coordinating the list and composition of indicators, statistical and accounting forms, the procedure for collection, frequency and processing of statistical information, as well as instructional and methodological materials necessary for performance of departmental statistical work in the field of domestic violence.
2.	The National Statistical Committee of the Kyrgyz Republic shall ensure dissemination and public access to statistical information on domestic violence by publishing statistical collections, publications, bulletins, reviews, posting on the website, as well as by other means.

### Article 37. Statistical Reporting on Domestic Violence

Statistical reporting on domestic violence shall include gender-disaggregated indicators reflecting the number of registered cases of domestic violence, types of measures taken, socio-demographic data on persons who committed domestic violence and persons affected by domestic violence, as well as on the degree of kinship between the victim and the person who committed domestic violence, and other statistical data.

## Chapter 5

Liability for Non-Compliance with this Law Article 38. Liability for Non-Compliance with this Law
For non-compliance or improper compliance with this Law, entities carrying out protection and defense against domestic violence and their officials shall bear liability provided for by the legislation of the Kyrgyz Republic.
(As amended by the Law of the Kyrgyz Republic of April 15, 2020 No. 41)
