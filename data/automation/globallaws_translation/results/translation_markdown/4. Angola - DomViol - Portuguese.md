## CHAPTER I GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1 (Object)

This law establishes the legal regime for the prevention of domestic violence, protection and assistance to victims and has the following purposes:

● a)- to prevent, combat and punish perpetrators of acts of domestic violence;

● b)- to inform victims of crimes of domestic violence about their rights;

• c)- to ensure swift and effective police and judicial protection for victims of domestic violence;

• d)- to create specialised services for assisting victims of domestic violence within the competent bodies;

• e)- to encourage associations and other civil society organisations dedicated to the prevention of domestic violence;

• f)- to promote awareness policies in the areas of education, information, health and social support;

• g)- to hold perpetrators of acts of domestic violence administratively, civilly and criminally liable;

• h)- to create spaces for counselling and temporary shelter for those involved;

• i)- to discourage any act that, based on customs and traditions, violates the dignity of the human person;

### Artigo 3.º (Definition and type of domestic violence)

1. For the purposes of this law, domestic violence is understood as any action or omission that causes injury or physical deformity and temporary or permanent psychological harm that violates the human person within the scope of the relationships provided for in the previous article.

2. Domestic violence is classified as:

o a)- sexual violence — any conduct that compels a person to witness, maintain or participate in sexual relations by means of violence, coercion, threat or placing the person in a state of unconsciousness or impossibility to resist;

o b)- patrimonial violence — any action that constitutes the retention, subtraction, partial or total destruction of objects, documents, work instruments, movable or immovable property, values and rights of the victim;

o c)- psychological violence — any conduct that causes emotional harm, diminution of self-esteem or that prejudices and disturbs full psycho-social development;

o d)- verbal violence — any action that involves the use of insults, accompanied or not by offensive gestures, that has the purpose of humiliating and disregarding the victim, constituting slander, defamation or insult;

o e)- physical violence — any conduct that offends the integrity or bodily health of the person;

• f)- family abandonment — any conduct that disrespects, in a serious and repeated manner, the provision of assistance under the terms of the law.

## CAPÍTULO III PREVENTION AND SUPPORT MEASURES

### Artigo 7.º (Education)

It is incumbent upon the State to integrate, in the curricular teaching programmes, subjects aimed at preventing domestic violence crimes, providing children and young people with basic concepts, especially:

• a)- about the phenomenon of violence and its various manifestations, origins and consequences;

• b)- about the right and respect for intimacy and privacy of private life;

• c)- about parental behaviours and interrelationships in family life;

• d)- about symbolic violence and its structural and institutional character;

• e)- about power relations that mark personal, group and social interactions;

The State ensures the promotion of domestic violence prevention policies, through:

• a)- preparation of educational guides and products for families;

• b)- awareness-raising and information about education for gender equality among communities;

• c)- expansion of the knowledge base and exchange with national and foreign entities, of information, identification and dissemination of good practices.

### Artigo 9.º (Social assistance)

1. The State must foster the emergence of institutions dedicated to family counselling with a view to preventing domestic violence.

2. For the purposes of the previous number, institutions must rely, preferably, on the areas of social, human and medical sciences.

### Artigo 10.º (Training)

Training on gender issues and domestic violence is ensured for professionals who intervene in domestic violence proceedings.

### Artigo 16.º (Cessation of victim status)

1. The status of victim of domestic violence ceases with the archiving of the case in the preparatory investigation phase, by order of non-indictment or after the final judgment becomes res judicata.

2. The cessation of victim status does not prevent the competent authorities, having considered the concrete circumstances, from maintaining the modalities of social support that have been established.

### Artigo 17.º (Free assistance)

Medical, psychological, social and legal support to victims of domestic violence is guaranteed free of charge, until the victim status ceases, by specialised public services and other civil society organisations dedicated to this purpose.

### Artigo 19.º (Shelter and protection spaces)

1. It is the responsibility of the State to promote and coordinate the establishment of shelter spaces for victims and perpetrators of domestic violence, to be defined in specific regulations.

2. In the creation of the shelter spaces provided for in paragraph 1 of this article, consideration must be given to the gender, age and safety of those involved.

3. Without prejudice to the provisions of the preceding paragraph, those involved in domestic violence must be accommodated in separate shelter spaces, depending on whether they are victims or perpetrators of the crime.

### Artigo 20.º (Support for the perpetrator of the crime)

1. The State must promote the creation of necessary conditions for psychological and psychiatric support for the perpetrator of the crime of domestic violence.

2. Recovery programmes for perpetrators of the crime of domestic violence must be developed and implemented.

### Artigo 23.º (Detention outside flagrante delicto)

Without prejudice to the provisions of the criminal procedural law, the detention of the perpetrator of the crime of domestic violence may be carried out by order of the Public Prosecutor's Office, if there is danger of continuation of criminal activity or if it proves essential to the safety of the victim under the terms provided for by law.

### Artigo 24.º (Complaint, denunciation and withdrawal)

1. The complaint may be made by the injured party or by anyone who has standing under the terms of the law.

2. The denunciation may be made by any person or authority who has knowledge of the criminal act.

3. The victim of domestic violence may, without prejudice to cases where the law prohibits it, withdraw the complaint at any stage of the proceedings.

4. The complaint or denunciation may be submitted, verbally, in writing, by telephone, by electronic means or by any other sufficient means, before the police authority or to the Public Prosecutor's Office.

5. Upon receipt of the complaint or denunciation, the competent authorities must ascertain its veracity, for purposes of criminal proceedings, under the terms of the law.

6. Anyone who makes a false complaint or denunciation incurs the penalty applicable to the crime of calumnious denunciation.

### Artigo 29.º (Duty to appear)

Anyone who is legally notified by the competent authority and does not appear, for a reason attributable to them, is liable for the crime of disobedience, punishable under the terms of the law.

### Artigo 30.º (Right to compensation)

1. The victim of domestic violence is recognised as having the right to obtain, from the perpetrator of the crime, in an expeditious manner, compensation for the damages suffered.

2. The compensation referred to in the preceding paragraph must be awarded taking into account the seriousness of the aggression and the economic condition of those involved.

3. If the civil compensation claim has not been brought in the criminal proceedings or separately in the event of conviction, the court may award an amount by way of reparation for the damages suffered by the victim.
