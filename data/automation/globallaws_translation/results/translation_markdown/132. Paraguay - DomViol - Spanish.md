THE CRIMINAL LAW

##### CHAPTER I

BASIC PRINCIPLES

###### Article 1.- Principle of legality

No one shall be punished with a penalty or measure unless the prerequisites for the punishability of the conduct and the applicable sanction are expressly and strictly described in a law in force prior to the action or omission that motivates the sanction122.

3.	the degree of possibility with which this act or these acts will be committed.

###### Article 3.- Principle of prevention

Criminal sanctions shall have as their purpose the protection of legal interests and the rehabilitation of the author to a life without crime129.

##### CHAPTER II

APPLICATION OF THE LAW

###### Article 4.- Application of the First Book to special laws

The provisions of the First Book of this Code shall apply to all punishable acts130 provided for by special laws.

###### Article 5.- Application of the law in time

1º Sanctions are governed by the law in force at the time of the commission of the punishable act.
2º When the sanction changes during the commission of the punishable act, the law in force at the end thereof shall apply131.
3º When before the sentence the law in force at the time of the commission of the punishable act is modified, the law most favorable to the accused shall apply132.

###### Article 11.- Place of the act

1º The act shall be deemed to have been committed in all places where the perpetrator or participant executed the action or, in case of omission, should have executed it; or where the result provided for in the law occurred or where it should have occurred according to the perpetrator's representation.
2º The participant151 is considered to have committed the act also in the place where the perpetrator would have committed it.
3° Paraguayan law shall be applicable to the participant in an act committed abroad, when he has acted in national territory, even if the act lacked criminal sanction according to the law in force in the place where it was committed.

###### Article 12.- Application to minors

This Code shall apply to acts committed by minors, unless the legislation on juvenile offenders provides otherwise152.

###### 2. Article 19.- Legitimate defense

A person who commits conduct described in the legal type of a punishable act does not act unlawfully when such conduct is necessary and rational to repel or deflect a present and unlawful aggression against one's own or another's legal interest186.

###### Article 20.- Justifying state of necessity

1º A person who, in a situation of present danger to one's own or another's legal interest, injures another interest to prevent a greater harm that cannot be avoided in any other way187 does not act unlawfully.
2º A person who commits the legal type of a punishable act by omission188 does not act unlawfully when the action could not be performed without violating another duty of equal or greater rank.

###### Article 21

Criminal responsibility of minors A person who has not reached fourteen years of age189 is exempt from criminal responsibility.

###### Article 22.- Error of prohibition

A person who, in committing the act, is unaware of its unlawfulness is not culpable when the error was unavoidable. If the perpetrator could have avoided the error, the penalty shall be reduced in accordance with article 67190.

###### Article 23.- Mental disorder

1º A person who, at the time of the action or omission, due to mental disorder, incomplete or retarded psychological development, or serious disturbance of consciousness, was incapable of knowing the unlawfulness of the act or of acting in accordance with that knowledge191 is not culpable.

###### Article 24.- Excess due to confusion or terror

A person who commits an unlawful act by exceeding the limits of legitimate defense or of a justifying state of necessity due to confusion or terror shall be exempt from penalty193.

###### Article 25.- Inexigibility of other conduct

A person who commits an unlawful act to repel or deflect from himself, a relative194 or another person close to him, a present danger to life, physical integrity or liberty, shall be exempt from penalty when, considering all the circumstances, no other conduct could have been demanded. There is an attempt when the perpetrator executes the decision to commit a punishable act through acts that, considering his representation of the act, are immediately prior to the consummation of the legal type196.

##### CHAPTER III

PLURALITY OF PARTICIPANTS

###### Article 29.-Perpetration

1º A person who commits the act acting on his own or using another for that purpose shall be punished as perpetrator.
2º A person who acts in agreement with another in such a way that, through his contribution to the act, he shares with the other control over its commission201 shall also be punished as perpetrator.

###### Article 30.- Instigation

A person who induces another to commit an unlawful intentional act shall be punished as instigator. The penalty shall be that provided for the perpetrator202.

###### Article 31.- Complicity

A person who assists another in committing an unlawful intentional act shall be punished as accomplice. The penalty shall be that provided for the perpetrator and reduced in accordance with article 67203.

###### Article 32.- Special personal circumstances

1º When the instigator or accomplice does not possess the conditions, qualities or personal relations provided in article 16 that establish the punishability of the perpetrator, the penalty shall be reduced in accordance with article 67204.
2º Special personal circumstances that increase, diminish or exclude the penalty shall be taken into account only for the perpetrator or participant in whom they exist.

###### Article 33.- Individual punishability

Each participant in the act shall be punished according to his culpability, independently of the culpability of the others205.

###### Article 52.- Fine sentence

1º The fine sentence consists of the payment to the State of a determined sum of money, calculated in day-fines. Its limit is five day-fines as a minimum and, unless the law provides otherwise, three hundred sixty day-fines as a maximum241.
