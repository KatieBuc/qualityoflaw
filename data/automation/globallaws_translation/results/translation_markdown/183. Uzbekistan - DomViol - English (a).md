CRIMINAL CODE OF THE REPUBLIC OF UZBEKISTAN
of September 22, 1994 No. 2012-XII (as amended on 10-05-2023)

General part
Section one. General provisions

## Chapter I. Tasks and principles of the Criminal code

#### Article 1. Penal legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan

The penal legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan is based on the Constitution and the universally recognized norms of international law and consists of this Code.

#### Article 2. Code tasks

The criminal code has the task of protection from criminal encroachments of the personality, its rights and freedoms, interests of society and state, property, the environment, peace, safety of mankind, and also the prevention of crimes, education of citizens in the spirit of observance of the Constitution and the laws of the republic.
For implementation of these tasks the Code determines the bases and the principles of responsibility, what socially dangerous acts are crimes, establishes punishments and other measures of legal impact which can be applied to persons who committed socially dangerous acts.

#### Article 3. Principles of the Code

The criminal code is based on the principles of legality, equality of citizens before the law, democratism, humanity, justice, guilty responsibility, inevitability of responsibility.

#### Article 4. Principle of legality

Crime, punishability of act and other consequence in law of its commission are determined only by the Criminal code.
Nobody can be found guilty of crime commission and be subject to punishment otherwise than according to the court verdict and according to the law. Person found guilty of crime commission has the rights and performs the duties established by the law.

#### Article 5. Principle of equality of citizens before the law

Persons who committed crimes have the identical rights and obligations and are equal before the law without distinction as to sex, race, nationality, language, religion, social origin, beliefs, personal and social standing.

#### Article 6. Principle of democratism

Public associations, self-government institutions of citizens or collectives can be involved in the cases provided by the law in correction of persons who committed crimes.

#### Article 7. Principle of humanity

Punishment and other measures of legal impact do not aim at causing physical sufferings or humiliation of human dignity.
To person who committed crime penalty shall be imposed or other measure of legal impact which are necessary and sufficient for its correction and the prevention of new crimes is applied.
Severe measures of punishment can be appointed only under condition if the goals of punishment cannot be achieved by means of application of softer measures provided by the relevant article of the Special part of this Code.

#### Article 8. Concept of justice

Punishment or other measure of legal impact applied to person guilty of crime commission shall be fair, that is correspond to gravity of crime, degree of fault and public danger of his personality.
Nobody can bear responsibility for the same crime twice.

#### Article 9. Principle of guilty responsibility

Person is subject to responsibility only for those socially dangerous acts in which commission his guilt, in the procedure established by the law will be proved.

#### Article 10. Principle of inevitability of responsibility

Each person in whose act actus reus availability is established shall be subject to responsibility.

## Chapter II. Limits of operation of the Code

#### Article 11

Operation of the Code concerning persons who committed crimes in the territory of Uzbekistan
Person who committed crime in the territory of Uzbekistan is subject to responsibility under this Code.
As the crime committed in the territory of Uzbekistan it is necessary to recognize such act which:
a)	it is begun, ended or interrupted in the territory of Uzbekistan;
b)	it is committed outside Uzbekistan, and the criminal result occurred on its territories;

c)	it is committed in the territory of Uzbekistan, and the criminal result came beyond its limits;
d)	forms in total or along with other acts crime which part is committed in the territory of Uzbekistan.
In case of crime commission on the air, ocean or river craft which is outside Uzbekistan and not in the territory of foreign state, responsibility comes under this Code if the called vessel is flying the flag or is attributed to the port of Uzbekistan.
The question of responsibility of foreign citizens who according to current laws, international treaties or agreements are not jurisdictional to courts of Uzbekistan, in case of commission of crime by them in the territory of the Republic of Uzbekistan is solved on the basis of rules of international law.

#### Article 12

Operation of the Code concerning persons who committed crimes outside Uzbekistan
Citizens of the Republic of Uzbekistan, and also persons without citizenship who are constantly living in Uzbekistan for the crimes committed in the territory of other state are subject to responsibility under this Code if they were not punished according to the court verdict of the state in the territory of which the crime was committed.
The citizen of Uzbekistan cannot be extradited for the crime committed in the territory of foreign state if other is not provided by international treaties or agreements.
Foreign citizens, and also the persons without citizenship who are not living constantly in Uzbekistan for the crimes committed out of its limits are subject to responsibility under this Code only in the cases provided by international treaties or agreements.

#### Article 13. Operation of the law in time

Crime and punishability of act is determined by the law existing during its commission. Time of crime commission time of commission of socially dangerous act is recognized if Article of this Code determines the moment of the termination of crime the moment of commission of action or failure to act. Time of crime commission time of occurrence of criminal effects is recognized if Article of this Code determines the termination of crime the moment of occurrence of such effects.
The law eliminating crime of act, commuting penalty or otherwise improving provision of person, has retroactive force, that is extends to persons who committed the corresponding act before entry into force of such law including to the persons serving or who served sentence, but having criminal record.
The law establishing crime of act, strengthening punishment or otherwise worsening situation of person, has no retroactive force.
Section second. Responsibility bases

## Chapter III. Crime

#### Article 14. Concept of crime

Crime is recognized guilty socially dangerous act (action or failure to act) forbidden by this Code under the threat of punishment.
Act which causes or creates real threat of harm to the objects protected by this Code is recognized socially dangerous.

#### Article 15. Classification of crimes

Crimes depending on nature and degree of public danger are subdivided into: not constituting great public danger; less serious; serious; especially serious.
Intentional crimes for which the law prescribes custodial sanction not exceeding three years, and also crimes committed through negligence for which the law prescribes custodial sanction not exceeding five years belong to crimes not constituting great public danger.
Intentional crimes for which the law prescribes custodial sanctions for a term of more than three years, but not exceeding five years, and also crimes committed through negligence for which the law prescribes custodial sanction for a term of more than five years belong to less serious crimes.
Intentional crimes for which the law prescribes custodial sanction for a term of more than five, but not exceeding ten years belong to serious crimes.
Intentional crimes for which the law prescribes custodial sanction for a term exceeding ten years or lifelong imprisonment belong to especially serious crimes.

#### Article 16. Responsibility for crime and its basis

Responsibility for crime is the legal consequence of commission of a socially dangerous act which is expressed in condemnation, application of punishment or other measures of legal impact by a court to a person guilty of crime commission.
The basis of responsibility is commission of an act containing all elements of the actus reus provided by this Code.

## Chapter IV. Persons which are subject to responsibility

#### Article 17. Responsibility of physical persons

Physical, responsible persons who before crime commission have attained sixteen years are subject to responsibility.
Persons who before crime commission have attained fourteen years are subject to responsibility for crimes provided by Articles 97, 98, 104 - 106, 118, 119, parts four - eight of Article 126-1,
Articles 137, 164 - 166, 169, parts two and three of Article 173, Articles 220, 222, 247, 252, 263, 267, 271, parts two and three of Article 277 of this Code.
Persons who before crime commission have attained eighteen years are subject to responsibility for crimes provided by Articles 122, 123, 125-1, parts one and two of Article 126-1, Articles 127, 127-1, 128-1, 144, 146, 193-195, 205-210, 225, 226, 230-
232, 234, 235, 279-302 of this Code.

Responsibility of persons who committed crime aged under eighteen years arises according to general provisions and taking into account the features provided by Section six of the General part of this Code.

#### Article 18. Sanity

A person who during crime commission understood the socially dangerous nature of their actions (failure to act) and directed them is responsible.
A person who during commission of a socially dangerous act was in a state of insanity is not subject to responsibility, that is could not realize the significance of their actions (failure to act) or direct them owing to chronic or temporary mental disturbance, weak-mindedness or other mental disturbance.
To a person who committed a socially dangerous act in a state of insanity, compulsory measures of medical nature can be appointed by a court.

#### Article 18

1. Responsibility of person suffering from mental disturbance which does not exclude sanity
A responsible person who during crime commission owing to mental disturbance could not fully realize the significance of their actions (failure to act) or direct them, is subject to responsibility.
To a person suffering from mental disturbance which does not exclude sanity, by a court along with punishment compulsory measures of medical nature can be appointed

#### Article 19. Responsibility for crime committed in state of intoxication

A person who committed a crime in alcohol intoxication or under the influence of drugs, their analogs, psychotropic or other substances affecting intellectual and volitional activities is not exempted from liability. Such condition cannot be the basis for recognition of a person as insane.

## Chapter V. Guilt

#### Article 20. Forms of guilt

A person can be found guilty of a crime if the socially dangerous act provided by this Code was committed intentionally or through negligence.

#### Article 21. Intentional crime

A crime the completion of which this Code determines by the moment of commission of a socially dangerous act is recognized as intentional if the person who committed it understood the socially dangerous nature of the act and wished its commission.
A crime the completion of which this Code determines by the moment of occurrence of socially dangerous consequences can be committed with direct or indirect intent.
A crime is recognized as committed with direct intent if the person who committed it understood the socially dangerous nature of the act, anticipated its socially dangerous consequences and wished their occurrence.

A crime is recognized as committed with indirect intent if the person who committed it understood the socially dangerous nature of the act, anticipated its socially dangerous consequences and consciously allowed their occurrence.

#### Article 22. Negligent crime

A negligent crime is recognized as a socially dangerous act committed through overconfidence or carelessness.
A crime is recognized as committed through overconfidence if the person who committed it anticipated the possibility of occurrence of socially dangerous consequences of the conduct provided by law and, consciously without observing precautionary measures, groundlessly calculated that these consequences would not occur.
A crime is recognized as committed through carelessness if the person who committed it did not anticipate the possibility of occurrence of socially dangerous consequences of the conduct provided by law though they should and could have anticipated them.

#### Article 23. Crime with complex guilt

If as a result of intentional crime commission a person causes other socially dangerous consequences through negligence with which the law connects increased responsibility, such crime is recognized as committed intentionally.

#### Article 24. Innocent causation

An act is recognized as committed innocently if the person who committed it did not understand, should not and could not understand the socially dangerous nature of the act or did not anticipate its socially dangerous consequences and based on the circumstances of the matter should not and could not anticipate them.

## Chapter VI. Unfinished crime

#### Article 25. Preparation for crime and attempted crime

Preparation for crime is recognized as an act of a person creating conditions for commission or concealment of an intentional crime interrupted prior to its commission on circumstances not dependent on them.
Attempted crime is recognized as the beginning of commission of an intentional crime, unfinished on circumstances independent from the person.
Responsibility for preparation and attempt arises under the same Article of the Special part of this Code as for completed crime.

#### Article 26. Voluntary refusal of crime commission

Voluntary refusal of crime is recognized as the termination by a person of preparatory actions or the termination of crime commission if the person understood the opportunity to complete the crime, and also prevention of occurrence of criminal result if the person understood the possibility of its occurrence.
Voluntary refusal of crime commission excludes responsibility.

A person who voluntarily refused completing the crime to the end is subject to responsibility under this Code if the act actually committed by them comprises all elements of another actus reus.

## Chapter VII. Complicity in crime

#### Article 27. Concept of complicity in crime

Complicity is recognized as joint participation of two or more persons in commission of an intentional crime.

#### Article 28. Types of assisting offenders

Assisting offenders along with perpetrators, organizers, instigators and accomplices are recognized.
The perpetrator is a person who fully or partially directly committed a crime or a crime with the use of other persons not subject to liability under this Code, or by other means.
The organizer is a person who directed the preparation or execution of a crime. The instigator is a person who incited the execution of a crime.
The accomplice is a person who facilitated the execution of a crime by advice, instructions, provision of means or removal of obstacles, and also by promising in advance to conceal the criminal, the instruments, traces or means of crime execution or objects obtained by criminal means, and equally by promising in advance to acquire or sell such objects.

#### Article 29. Partnership forms

Forms of partnership in crime are recognized: simple partnership; complex partnership; organized group; criminal association.
Simple partnership is participation in the execution of a crime by two or more persons without prior agreement.
Complex partnership is participation in the execution of a crime by two or more persons by prior agreement.
Organized group is preliminary consolidation of two or more persons in a group for joint criminal activities.
Criminal association is preliminary consolidation of two or more organized groups for engaging in criminal activities.

#### Article 30. Limits of responsibility for partnership in crime

Organizers, instigators and accomplices bear responsibility under the same Article of the Special Part of this Code as perpetrators.
Organizers, and also members of groups by prior agreement, organized groups and criminal associations bear responsibility for all crimes in the preparation or commission of which they took part.

Persons who created an organized group or criminal association or directed them bear responsibility for all crimes committed by the criminal formation if they were covered by their intention.
A person who committed an act not covered by the intention of other accomplices bears responsibility for that act.
The voluntary refusal of the organizer, instigator or accomplice excludes responsibility for partnership in crime if the person timely took all measures depending on it for its prevention.

#### Article 31. Complicity in crime

Failure to report to authorities about a crime known with certainty to be preparing, being committed or committed, not promised in advance, attracts responsibility only in the cases provided by Articles 155-1 and 241 of this Code.
Concealment not promised in advance of the criminal, the instruments and means of crime execution, traces of crime or objects obtained by criminal means attracts responsibility only in cases stipulated in Article 241 of this Code.
Close relatives of the suspect, the accused person or the defendant are not subject to responsibility for failure to report or concealment not promised in advance.

## Chapter VIII. Plurality of crimes

#### Article 32. Frequency of crimes

Frequency is recognized as the commission of two or more crimes occurring at different times provided by the same part, Article, and in cases specially specified in this Code by different Articles of the Special Part for none of which the person was convicted. Both completed crimes and punishable preparations or attempts at crimes, and also crimes committed in partnership are recognized as repeated.
The crime is not recognized as repeated if the person is exempted for earlier committed acts from liability or punishment.
A crime consisting of a number of identical criminal actions covered by common intent and directed to a single purpose and constituting in total one continuing crime is not recognized as repeated.
A crime consisting in prolonged failure to perform a duty, characterizing continuous implementation of the elements of one ongoing crime is not recognized as repeated.

#### Article 33. Cumulative offenses

Cumulative offenses is recognized as the commission of two or more criminal actions provided by different Articles or different parts of the same Article of the Special Part of this Code for none of which the person was convicted and for which it is subject to responsibility.

If the act committed by a person contains signs of crimes provided by different parts of the same Article of the Special Part of this Code, responsibility comes under that part of the Article which establishes more severe punishment.

#### Article 34. Recurrence of crimes

Recurrence is recognized as the commission by a person of a new intentional crime after conviction for an earlier committed intentional crime.
Dangerous recurrence is recognized as the commission by a person of a new intentional crime identical to that for which it was convicted earlier, and in cases specially specified in this Code under other Articles of the Special Part.
Especially dangerous recurrence is recognized as the commission by a person of a new intentional crime for which it is sentenced to imprisonment for a term of at least five years:
a)	for an especially serious crime if it was convicted earlier for an especially serious crime or twice for serious crimes and for each of them a custodial sentence for a term of at least five years was imposed;
b)	for a serious crime if it was convicted twice earlier for serious crimes or in any sequence for a serious and especially serious crime and for each of them a custodial sentence for a term of at least five years was imposed.
A person may be recognized as an especially dangerous recidivist according to a court verdict.
When considering the question of recognition of a person as an especially dangerous recidivist, criminal records under sentences of courts of other states may also be considered.
When considering the question of recognition of a person as an especially dangerous recidivist, the criminal record for crimes committed by it under the age of eighteen years and also criminal records extinguished or removed in the procedure established by law are not considered.
Articles of the Special Part of this Code providing responsibility for crime execution by an especially dangerous recidivist are applied in cases when a person was recognized in the procedure established by law as an especially dangerous recidivist before commission of this crime.
Section third. The circumstances excluding crime of act

## Chapter IX. Concept and types of the circumstances excluding crime of act

#### Article 35. Concept of the circumstances excluding crime of act

Circumstances under which an act containing the signs provided by this Code is not a crime, in view of lack of public danger, illegality or fault are recognized as excluding crime.
The circumstances excluding crime are recognized: insignificance of act; justifiable defense; emergency; infliction of harm during detention of a person who committed a socially dangerous act; execution of an order or other obligation; justified professional or economic risk; commission of an act as a result of physical or mental compulsion or threat.

#### Article 36. Insignificance of act

An action or failure to act which falls under the signs of an act provided by this Code as a crime, but not having public danger owing to insignificance is not a crime.

#### Article 37. Justifiable defense

An action committed in a condition of justifiable defense is not a crime, that is in case of protection of the personality or the rights of the defending person or other person, interests of society or the state against unlawful encroachment by causing harm to the encroacher if at the same time exceeding of limits of justifiable defense was not allowed.
Exceeding of limits of justifiable defense is recognized as obvious discrepancy of defense to the nature and danger of encroachment.
The right to justifiable defense belongs to a person irrespective of the available opportunity to ask for the help of other persons or authorities or to avoid encroachment in a different way.
Intentional provocation of encroachment for the purpose of causing harm is not justifiable defense.

#### Article 38. Emergency

The act which did harm to the rights and the interests protected by the law, made in emergency condition that is for elimination of the danger threatening to the personality or the rights of this person or other citizens, to interests of society or state if danger under these circumstances could not be eliminated with other means and if the damage suffered is less considerable, than prevented is not crime.
The act made in emergency condition is lawful if person did not allow exceeding of its limits.
Exceeding of limits of emergency is damnification to the rights and interests protected by the law if danger could be eliminated with other means or if the damage suffered is more considerable, than prevented.
In case of assessment of legitimacy of the act made in emergency condition nature and degree of the prevented danger, reality and proximity of its approach, the actual possibility of person on its prevention, its state of mind in the circumstances and other facts of the case are considered.

#### Article 39. Damnification during detention of person who made socially dangerous act

Damnification during detention of person who made socially dangerous act for the purpose of transfer to its authorities is not crime if at the same time exceeding of the measures necessary for detention was not allowed.
Exceeding of measures of detention is explicit discrepancy of means and methods of detention of danger of the act and person which made it, and also detention situation therefore the harm which is not caused by need of detention is intentionally done to person.

In case of assessment of legitimacy of damnification during detention of person who made socially dangerous act its actions for avoidance of detention, force and opportunity detaining, its state of mind and other circumstances connected with the detention fact are considered.
Along with persons which are specially authorized on that, also the victims and other citizens have possessory lien of person who made socially dangerous act.

#### Article 40. Execution of the order or other obligation

Damnification in case of lawful execution-faced the order or other order, and equally job responsibilities is not crime.
Person who committed crime under obviously criminal order or other order is subject to responsibility in accordance with general practice.
Person is not subject to responsibility for non-execution or violation of the order, the order or job responsibilities if they were assigned to it illegally. Responsibility comes only if the act which is actually made by it comprises all signs of other actus reus.

#### Article 41. Justified professional or economic risk

Damnification to the rights and interests protected by the law is not crime in case of justified professional or economic risk for achievement of the socially useful purpose.
The risk is recognized justified if committed action corresponds to modern scientific and technical knowledge and experience, and the effective objective could not be achieved by the actions which are not connected with risk and person which allowed risk took necessary measures for prevention of harm to the rights and the interests protected by the law.
In case of justified professional or economic risk responsibility for damage suffered does not come and if the desirable socially useful result was not achieved and harm was more considerable, than the pursued socially useful purpose.
The risk is not recognized justified if it was obviously integrated to threat of death of people, environmental disaster or other heavy effects.
Failure to carry out of contractual commitments by subjects of entrepreneurship before banks and other financial organizations on rendered to them to services including issued to the credits, connected with entrepreneurial and other commercial risks, is not the basis for criminal prosecution of employees of banks and other financial organizations.

#### Article 41

1. Physical or mental compulsion or threat
Damnification to the rights and interests protected by this Code, put as a result of physical or mental compulsion or threat of application of such coercion is not crime if owing to such coercion or threat person could not direct the actions (failure to act).

The question of criminal liability for damnification to the rights and interests protected by this Code, put as a result of physical or mental compulsion or threat of application of such coercion owing to which person kept opportunity to direct the actions (failure to act), is solved taking into account provisions of article 38 of this Code

Section fourth. Punishment and its appointment

## Chapter X. Concept, purposes and types of punishments

#### Article 42. Concept and purposes of punishment

Punishment is the enforcement measure applied on behalf of the state according to the court verdict to person found guilty of crime execution, and consisting in the deprivation or restriction of certain rights and freedoms of the convict provided by the law.
Punishment is applied for the purpose of correction, hindrance to continuation of criminal activities, and also the prevention of making of new crimes both the convict, and other persons.

Punishments in the form of restriction on service or the directions in disciplinary part are applied only to the military personnel.
Deprivation of certain right can be applied not only as the basic, but also as additional punishment.

#### Article 44. Penalty

The penalty is the cash collection in the income of the state imposed on guilty of the sizes established by this Code.
The penalty is established ranging from five up to six hundred basic settlement sizes.
In case of evasion of the fine condemned from payment which is ordered to pay as punishment in the terms established for forced execution, or impossibility of recovery of penalty during the term established for forced execution because of absence at the debtor of property on which collection can be turned, and it is equal in case of failure to pay penalty after the term of delay or violation of conditions of payment of penalty by installments, court replaces outstanding amount of penalty with punishment in the form of obligatory social jobs, corrective works, restriction on service, restriction of freedom or imprisonment. In this case obligatory social jobs are appointed at the rate of two and a half hours for the penalty amount corresponding to one basic settlement size, but for the term of not over four hundred eighty hours, corrective works, restriction on service, restriction of freedom or imprisonment - at the rate of one month for the penalty amount corresponding to sixteen basic settlement sizes, but for the term of not over three years.

#### Article 45. Deprivation of certain right

Deprivation of certain right of person consists in prohibition to the guilty person during the term appointed by court to hold certain positions at companies, in organizations or institutions or to be engaged in certain activities. The type of such position or activities is determined by court in case of the resolution of conviction.
Deprivation of certain right is established for the crimes which are directly connected with the position held by the guilty person or its activities for a period of one year up to five years as primary punishment and for a period of one year up to three years as additional punishment.
If deprivation of certain right is not appointed guilty of quality of primary punishment, it can be appointed by court as punishment, additional to any kind, provided by the relevant article of the Special part of this Code.
In case of purpose of deprivation of certain right as additional punishment to imprisonment, the direction in disciplinary part it extends to all the time of serving by the convict of primary punishment and, moreover, for the term established by sentence. In case of purpose of this punishment as additional to other primary punishments and in case of conditional condemnation, its term is estimated from the moment of the introduction of sentence in legal force.
Penalty in the form of deprivation of the right to occupation by business activity concerning the persons who are engaged in business activity, except as specified, entailed the death of the person or other heavy effects is not imposed.

#### Article 45

1. Obligatory social jobs
Obligatory social jobs consist in forced involvement of the convict to accomplishment of free socially useful works. In the presence at the convict of work or study obligatory social jobs are left for time, free from work or study.

Places (objects) on which convicts can leave obligatory social jobs and type of obligatory social jobs are determined by the bodies knowing execution of this punishment.
Obligatory social jobs are established for a period of one hundred twenty till four hundred eighty hours and not over four hours a day within six months are left, and in case of the circumstances which are not depending on the convict - up to one year.
Obligatory social jobs are not applied to the persons which reached retirement age, did not reach sixteen years, to expectant mothers, women having children under three years, to persons with disability of the first and second group, to the military personnel, foreign citizens and persons who are not living constantly in the Republic of Uzbekistan.
In case of the evasion condemned court from serving sentence replaces the unexpired term of obligatory social jobs with punishment in the form of restriction of freedom or imprisonment at the rate of one day of restriction of freedom or imprisonment in four hours of obligatory social jobs. Evasion time of the served sentence is not set off in time.

#### Article 46. Corrective works

Corrective works consist in obligatory involvement of person to work with deduction from ten to thirty percent of the salary in the income of the state and are left according to the court verdict on place of employment of the convict or in other places determined by the bodies knowing execution of this punishment.
Corrective works are established for a period of six months up to three years.
Corrective works are not applied to persons which reached retirement age, not to the able-bodied, expectant mothers and women having children under three years, to the military personnel.
In case of evasion in total over one tenth from the appointed term of serving of corrective works the court replaces not served sentence of corrective works with punishment in the form of restriction of freedom or imprisonment for the same term. Evasion time of the served sentence is not set off in time.

#### Article 47. Restriction on service

Restriction on service consists in deprivation of the serviceman passing military service under the contract, certain rights and privileges with deduction in the income of the state in the amount of ten to thirty percent of monetary pay, temporary during the term determined by court.
Restriction on service is applied for a period of two months up to three years in cases, stipulated in Article the Special part of this Code. For the crime which is not constituting big public danger or the careless crime which did not entail heavy effects the court can, considering the facts of the case and the identity of the convict, instead of imprisonment for the term of not over three years, or corrective works to apply restriction on service for the same term.

In term of imprisonment in the form of restriction on service the convict cannot be promoted, military or special rank and this time is not set off to it lengths of service for assignment of the next military or special rank in time, and also for award of pension.
