## Chapter I General Provisions

### Article 1 This Law is formulated for the purposes of preventing and stopping domestic violence, protecting the lawful rights and interests of family members, safeguarding equal, harmonious and civilized family relationships, and promoting family harmony and social stability.

### Article 2 For the purposes of this Law, domestic violence refers to physical, mental and other violations committed among family members by means of beating, binding, maiming, restricting personal freedom, as well as habitual verbal abuse, intimidation and the like.

### Article 3 Family members shall help each other, care for each other, live in harmony, and fulfill family obligations.

Combating domestic violence is the common responsibility of the State, society and every family.
The State prohibits all forms of domestic violence.

### Article 4 The agencies responsible for women's and children's affairs of the people's governments at or above the county level shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, guiding and supervising relevant departments in doing a good job in combating domestic violence.

Relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, judicial organs, people's organizations, social organizations, residents' committees, villagers' committees, and enterprises and institutions shall, in accordance with this Law and relevant legal provisions, do a good job in combating domestic violence.
People's governments at all levels shall provide necessary financial support for work combating domestic violence.

### Article 5 Work combating domestic violence shall follow the principle of giving priority to prevention and combining education, correction and punishment.

Work combating domestic violence shall respect the true wishes of victims and protect the privacy of the parties concerned.
Where minors, the elderly, persons with disabilities, women during pregnancy and lactation, or persons with serious illnesses suffer domestic violence, special protection shall be given.

## Chapter II Prevention of Domestic Violence

### Article 6 The State shall carry out publicity and education on family virtues, popularize knowledge on combating domestic violence, and enhance citizens' awareness of combating domestic violence.

Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, and federations of persons with disabilities shall, within the scope of their respective work, organize and carry out publicity and education on family virtues and combating domestic violence.
Radio, television, newspapers, the internet and the like shall carry out publicity on family virtues and combating domestic violence.
Schools and kindergartens shall carry out education on family virtues and combating domestic violence.

### Article 7 Relevant departments of the people's governments at or above the county level, judicial organs, and women's federations shall incorporate the prevention and stopping of domestic violence into professional training and statistical work.

Medical institutions shall maintain proper medical records for victims of domestic violence.

### Article 8 Township people's governments and sub-district offices shall organize and carry out work on the prevention of domestic violence, and residents' committees, villagers' committees, and social work service agencies shall provide cooperation and assistance.

### Article 9 People's governments at all levels shall support social work service agencies and other social organizations in providing services such as mental health counseling, family relationship guidance, and education on knowledge for the prevention of domestic violence.

### Article 10 People's mediation organizations shall mediate family disputes in accordance with law, and prevent and reduce the occurrence of domestic violence.

### Article 11 Where an employing unit discovers that personnel of the unit have domestic violence situations, it shall provide criticism and education, and do a good job in mediating and resolving family conflicts.

### Article 12 Guardians of minors shall conduct family education in a civilized manner, lawfully perform guardianship and education duties, and shall not commit domestic violence.

## Chapter III Disposition of Domestic Violence

### Article 13

Victims of domestic violence and their legal agents or close relatives may lodge complaints with, report to, or seek help from the unit where the perpetrator or victim is located, residents' committees, villagers' committees, women's federations and other units. After receiving complaints, reports or requests for help regarding domestic violence, the relevant units shall provide assistance and handle the matter.
Victims of domestic violence and their legal agents or close relatives may also report the case to public security organs or lawfully file a lawsuit with the people's court.
Where units or individuals discover domestic violence occurring, they have the right to promptly dissuade.

### Article 14

Where schools, kindergartens, medical institutions, residents' committees, villagers' committees, social work service agencies, assistance and management institutions, welfare institutions and their staff discover in the course of their work that persons without capacity for civil conduct or persons with limited capacity for civil conduct have suffered or are suspected of having suffered domestic violence, they shall promptly report the case to public security organs. Public security organs shall keep confidential the information of those reporting cases.

### Article 15 After receiving a report of domestic violence, public security organs shall promptly dispatch police, stop the domestic violence, investigate and collect evidence in accordance with relevant provisions, and assist victims in seeking medical treatment and assessing injuries.

Where persons without capacity for civil conduct or persons with limited capacity for civil conduct suffer serious physical injury due to domestic violence, face threats to personal safety, or are in a dangerous state such as being without care, public security organs shall notify and assist civil affairs departments in placing them in temporary shelters, assistance and management institutions, or welfare institutions.

### Article 16 Where domestic violence is relatively minor and does not warrant administrative punishment for public security in accordance with law, public security organs shall provide criticism and education to the perpetrator or issue a written admonition.

The written admonition shall include the identity information of the perpetrator, a statement of the facts of the domestic violence, and content prohibiting the perpetrator from committing domestic violence.

### Article 17 Public security organs shall deliver the written admonition to the perpetrator and the victim, and notify the residents' committee or villagers' committee.

Residents' committees, villagers' committees, and public security police stations shall conduct follow-up visits with perpetrators and victims who have received written admonitions, and supervise perpetrators to refrain from committing domestic violence again.

### Article 18 People's governments at the county level or at the level of a city divided into districts may establish temporary shelters independently or by relying on assistance and management institutions, to provide temporary living assistance to victims of domestic violence.

### Article 19 Legal aid institutions shall provide legal aid to victims of domestic violence in accordance with law.

People's courts shall, in accordance with law, postpone, reduce or waive litigation fees for victims of domestic violence.

### Article 20 When people's courts hear cases involving domestic violence, they may determine the facts of domestic violence on the basis of evidence such as police dispatch records of public security organs, written admonitions, and opinions on injury assessment.

### Article 21

Where a guardian commits domestic violence that seriously infringes upon the lawful rights and interests of the ward, the people's court may, based on applications by the ward's close relatives, residents' committees, villagers' committees, the civil affairs department of the people's government at the county level, or other relevant persons or units, revoke the guardian's qualification as guardian in accordance with law and appoint another guardian.
A perpetrator whose guardian qualification has been revoked shall continue to bear corresponding maintenance, support and upbringing expenses.

### Article 22

Trade unions, the Communist Youth League, women's federations, federations of persons with disabilities, residents' committees, villagers' committees and the like shall provide rule of law education to perpetrators who commit domestic violence, and where necessary may provide psychological counseling to perpetrators and victims.

## Chapter IV Personal Safety Protection Orders

### Article 23 Where a party applies to a people's court for a personal safety protection order due to suffering domestic violence or facing a realistic danger of domestic violence, the people's court shall accept the application.

Where a party is a person without capacity for civil conduct or a person with limited capacity for civil conduct, or is unable to apply for a personal safety protection order due to reasons such as being subjected to coercion or intimidation, their close relatives, public security organs, women's federations, residents' committees, villagers' committees, or assistance and management institutions may apply on their behalf.

### Article 24 Applications for personal safety protection orders shall be submitted in written form; where there are genuine difficulties in submitting a written application, an oral application may be made, and the people's court shall record it in the transcript.

### Article 25 Cases involving personal safety protection orders shall be under the jurisdiction of the basic-level people's court in the place of residence of the applicant or respondent, or where the domestic violence occurred.

### Article 26 Personal safety protection orders shall be issued by people's courts in the form of rulings.

### Article 27 The issuance of a personal safety protection order shall meet the following conditions:

(1) There is a clear respondent;
(2) There is a specific request;
(3) There are circumstances of having suffered domestic violence or facing a realistic danger of domestic violence.

### Article 28 After accepting an application, the people's court shall issue a personal safety protection order or reject the application within 72 hours; in urgent circumstances, it shall do so within 24 hours.

### Article 29 Personal safety protection orders may include the following measures:

(1) Prohibiting the respondent from committing domestic violence;
(2) Prohibiting the respondent from harassing, stalking, or contacting the applicant and their related close relatives;
(3) Ordering the respondent to move out of the applicant's residence;
(4) Other measures to protect the personal safety of the applicant.

### Article 30 The validity period of a personal safety protection order shall not exceed six months and shall take effect from the date of issuance. Before a personal safety protection order expires, the people's court may revoke, modify or extend it based on the applicant's application.

### Article 31

Where an applicant disagrees with the rejection of an application or a respondent disagrees with a personal safety protection order, they may apply to the people's court that issued the ruling for reconsideration once within five days from the date the ruling takes effect. Where a people's court issues a personal safety protection order in accordance with law, the execution of the personal safety protection order shall not be suspended during the reconsideration period.

### Article 32

After a people's court issues a personal safety protection order, it shall serve it on the applicant, the respondent, public security organs, and relevant organizations such as residents' committees and villagers' committees. Personal safety protection orders shall be executed by people's courts, and public security organs, residents' committees, villagers' committees and the like shall assist in execution.

## Chapter V Legal Liability

### Article 33 Where a perpetrator commits domestic violence, constituting conduct violating public security administration, administrative punishment for public security shall be imposed in accordance with law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

### Article 34

Where a respondent violates a personal safety protection order, constituting a crime, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law; where a crime is not yet constituted, the people's court shall provide an admonition, and may, based on the severity of the circumstances, impose a fine of not more than 1,000 yuan or detention of not more than 15 days.

### Article 35

Where schools, kindergartens, medical institutions, residents' committees, villagers' committees, social work service agencies, assistance and management institutions, welfare institutions and their staff fail to report cases to public security organs in accordance with

### Article 14 of this Law, causing serious consequences, the higher-level competent department or the unit itself shall impose sanctions on the directly responsible persons in charge and other directly liable persons in accordance with law.

### Article 36 Where state functionaries bearing responsibilities for combating domestic violence are derelict in their duties, abuse their authority, or engage in malpractice for personal gain, sanctions shall be imposed in accordance with law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be investigated in accordance with law.

## Chapter VI Supplementary Provisions

### Article 37 Violent conduct committed among persons living together who are not family members shall be handled with reference to the provisions of this Law.

### Article 38 This Law shall come into force on March 1, 2016.
