Article One: Purpose of the Law
This Law is aimed at preventing and punishing all forms of gender-based violence.

# CHAPTER ONE: GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1: Purpose of the law

This law provides for prevention and punishment of all forms of gender based violence.

#### Article 2: Definitions of terms

Under this Law, the following terms shall have the following definitions:

1° gender based violence: any act exercised against a person whether of physical, psychological, sexual and economic nature because they are female or male. Such an act causes deprivation of freedom and negative consequences. This violence may be committed at home or elsewhere;

2° marriage: a contract of union between a woman and a man concluded in accordance with the law;

3° polygamy: the fact of contracting a second marriage while the first marriage is still valid;
4° concubinage: de facto union between a man and a woman while they are not married,
one of them being legally married.

5° adultery: sexual relations between a legally married person with another person who is not their spouse;

6° rape: sexual intercourse imposed on a person without their consent, using force, threat, trickery and others;

7° conjugal rape: any act aimed at sexual intercourse with one's spouse in the absence of their consent, by resorting to violence, threat, trickery and others;

8° abduction: kidnapping of a person by force or by trickery with a view to marital union;

9° sexual slavery: the fact of abusing authority, assets or any other situation for the purpose of pleasure based on sex. It may aim at sexual intercourse, caresses, undressing, pornography, exhibition of nudity or eroticism, application of sex on other parts of the body and others;

10° indecent acts: acts or behaviour contrary to good morals which violate a person's decency;
11° elderly person: any person aged over sixty five (65) years;

12° harassment: repeated and destabilising actions towards a person such as persecuting them, addressing reproaches to them, maliciously reminding them of wrongs they did in the past, blaming them, insulting them and others;

## CHAPTER II: PREVENTION AND PROTECTION AGAINST GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE

#### Article 3: Threatening to deprive a person of their rights

It is forbidden to threaten someone to deprive them of their rights with a view to exercising on them any act of gender based violence.

#### Article 4: Disturbing the tranquility of one's spouse

It is forbidden to disturb the tranquility of one's spouse due to polygamy, concubinage or adultery.
It is forbidden to disturb the tranquility of one's spouse because of dowry, reproduction and
their natural physionomy.

#### Article 11 : Prévention des actes indécents

Il est interdit à toute personne d‟avoir une conduite et un comportement indécent.

#### Article 12 : Lieu de jugement

Dans la mesure où cela convient à la victime, l‟affaire relative à l‟infraction de violence basée sur le genre est jugée et prononcée sur le lieu de commission de l‟infraction si possible.

#### Article 11: Prevention against acts of outrage to public morals

Conduct and behaviour offensive to decency towards anyone are prohibited.

## CHAPTER III: CASES AND PENALTIES FOR GENDER BASED VIOLENCE

### Section One: Cases on gender based violence

#### Article 12: Place of trial

Without prejudice to the victim, the judgment relating to the offence of gender based violence shall be rendered and pronounced as far as possible at the place where the offence was committed.

#### Article 13: Production of testimonies and evidence

Notwithstanding the provisions of other laws, evidence or testimonies related to gender based violence shall be produced in courts by any person holding information thereof.

Testimonies given by children, other people living in the household and neighbours shall be taken into account.

### Section 2: Penalties for gender based violence offences

#### Article 14: Penalty for adultery

Any person convicted of adultery shall be liable to imprisonment of between six (6) months and two (2) years.

The penalties provided for in the preceding paragraph shall apply to the co-offender involved in adultery.

#### Article 15: Prosecution for the offence of adultery

Prosecution for the offence of adultery may not be instituted unless the offended spouse so claims.

The victim spouse may terminate the proceedings, at any stage of the case, wherever it may be, if he/she retracts and withdraws his/her complaint. He/she may also suspend the execution of the judgment.

Such suspension of prosecution or execution shall apply to the co-offenders of that offence.

#### Article 16: Penalty for rape

Any person convicted of the offence of rape shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) years to fifteen (15) years.

Where the person raped has suffered an illness, whether physical or mental, the offender shall be liable to imprisonment of fifteen (15) years to twenty (20) years and shall also pay the medical care fees for the person raped.

Where the illness is incurable or has resulted in death, the offender shall be liable to life imprisonment.

#### Article 17: Penalty for abduction or complicity in abduction

Abduction shall be liable to imprisonment of five (5) years to eight (8) years and a fine of between one hundred thousand (100,000 Frw) and two hundred thousand (200,000 Frw) Rwandan francs.

Any person who is an accomplice in the act of abduction shall be liable to the penalties referred to in paragraph one of this Article.

#### Article 18: Penalty for child neglect based on sex

Any person who neglects a child he/she has given birth to because the child is a boy or a girl, and harasses him/her or harasses his/her spouse, shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to three (3) years.

Any person who neglects a child he/she is responsible for raising based on sex shall be liable to the penalties referred to in paragraph one of this Article.

#### Article 19: Penalty for coercing one's spouse to sexual intercourse

Any person who coerces his/her spouse to sexual intercourse shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years.

#### Article 20: Penalty for harassing one's spouse

Any person found guilty of the act of harassing his/her spouse shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years.

#### Article 18: Penalty for abandonment of a child on the basis of sex

Any person guilty of abandoning his or her child for the simple reason that he is a boy or she is a girl, who harasses or abuses his or her partner because of that child, shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to three (3) years.

Any person guilty of abandoning a child in his or her care because of the sex of that child shall be liable to the penalties referred to in the first paragraph of this Article.

#### Article 19: Penalty for marital rape

Any person who forces his or her spouse to have sexual relations shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years.

#### Article 20: Penalty for harassment of one's spouse

Any person who commits an act of harassment against his or her spouse shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years.

#### Article 21: Penalty for concubinage

Any person guilty of an act of concubinage shall be liable to imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) to two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

Any person who consents to concubinage shall be liable to the penalties referred to in the first paragraph of this Article.

#### Article 22: Penalty for polygamy

Any person who enters into a second marriage while the first marriage contract is still valid shall be liable to imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of three hundred thousand (300,000 Rwf) to five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

Any person who consents to marriage knowing that his or her partner is still bound by another marriage shall be liable to the penalties referred to in the first paragraph of this Article.

Any person who intentionally contributes to an act of polygamy by issuing documents or validating the marriage contract shall be liable to imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years.

#### Article 23: Penalty for sexual slavery

Any person guilty of the crime of sexual slavery based on sex shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years and a fine of five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) to one million (1,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 24: Penalty for sexual harassment of one's subordinate

Any employer or any other person in charge of work who sexually harasses a subordinate by using orders, intimidation and terror for the purpose of sexual gratification shall be liable to imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) to two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 25: Penalty for killing one's spouse

Without prejudice to the provisions of the Penal Code, any person who kills his or her spouse shall be liable to life imprisonment.

#### Article 26: Penalty for disturbing the tranquility of one's spouse on the basis of sex

Any person guilty of the offence of violence referred to in paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 4 of this Law shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years and a fine of fifty thousand (50,000 Rwf) to two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 27: Penalty for damaging reproductive organs

Any person who commits violence, damages or intends to damage reproductive organs shall be liable to special life imprisonment.

#### Article 28: Penalty for trafficking in persons on the basis of sex

Any person guilty of an act of trafficking in persons on the basis of sex shall be liable to imprisonment of fifteen (15) to twenty (20) years and a fine of five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) to two million (2,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 26: Penalty for disturbing one's spouse's peace for sexual reasons

Any person found guilty of the offence referred to in paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 4 of this law shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years and a fine of fifty thousand (50,000 Rwf) to two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 27: Penalty for sexual torture

Any person guilty of torture or attempted sexual torture shall be liable to life imprisonment.

#### Article 28: Penalty for sexual trafficking of persons

Any person found guilty of sexual trafficking of persons shall be liable to imprisonment of fifteen (15) to twenty (20) years and a fine of five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) to two million (2,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 29: Penalty for intentionally transmitting a terminal disease

Any person who intentionally transmits a terminal disease to another person through sexual intercourse shall be liable to life imprisonment.

#### Article 30: Penalty for violence caused by the use of drugs, narcotics, images, gestures, language or writing

Any person using narcotics, drugs, images, gestures, language or writing, with the aim of committing gender based violence shall be liable to imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) to two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 31: Penalty for indecent acts based on gender

Any person who commits acts against public decency based on gender against another person shall be liable to imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) to two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 32: Penalty for violence against an elderly person

Any person having committed gender based violence against an elderly person shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years and a fine of five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) to one million (1,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 33: Penalty for violence against handicapped persons

Any person having committed gender based violence against a handicapped person shall be liable to imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years and a fine of five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) to one million (1,000,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 34: Penalty for defaming a person or his/her work

Any person guilty of insulting or threatening another person because they are a woman or a man, with the aim of disparaging their dignity or their work, shall be liable to imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) to three hundred thousand (300,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 34

Penalty for violence aimed at defaming a person or his/her work

Any person guilty of defamation or harassment against a person on the pretext that she is a woman or he is a man, with the aim of undermining his/her esteem or that of his/her work, shall be liable to imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) to three hundred thousand (300,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 35: Penalty for violence on the grounds of depriving someone of his/her rights

Any person who threatens another person with deprivation of his/her rights with the aim of attempting to commit or committing any act based on gender against him/her, shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (100,000 Rwf) to five hundred thousand (500,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs.

#### Article 36

Penalty for a person refusing to assist the victim of violence or to testify over the violence

Any person who refuses to assist the victim of gender based violence or refuses to testify over the violence committed against himself or herself or against someone else shall be liable to imprisonment of six (6) months to two (2) years and a fine of fifty thousand (50,000 Rwf) to two hundred thousand (200,000 Rwf) Rwandan francs or one of those penalties.

#### Article 37: Penalty for lying against somebody of having committed gender based violence

Any person found guilty of having falsely accused another person of having committed an offence of gender based violence provided for by this law shall be punished in accordance with the relevant penal legal provisions.

## CHAPTER IV: MISCELLANEOUS AND FINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 38: Damages

The victim of gender based violence and any other person affected has the right to claim for damages.

#### Article 39: Legalizing unlawful marriages and common assets distribution

Those people entertaining unlawful marriages shall be married in accordance with the monogamous marriage principle.

If a person concerned with the provisions of the previous paragraph of this Article was living with many women or men, he/she shall first of all share equally with those women or men their common belongings from before his/her marriage.

The property distribution referred to in paragraph 2 of this Article shall not deprive the children of the right that the law confers on them.

Modalities of such distribution shall be determined by an Order of the Minister in charge of Local Government.

#### Article 40: Abrogating provisions

All prior legal provisions contrary to this Law are hereby repealed.

#### Article 41: Commencement

This Law shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Rwanda.
