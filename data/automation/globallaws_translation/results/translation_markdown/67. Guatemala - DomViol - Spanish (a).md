# DECREE NUMBER 97-96

DECREE NUMBER 97-96
Law to Prevent, Sanction and Eradicate Domestic Violence

THE CONGRESS OF THE REPUBLIC OF GUATEMALA

WHEREAS

The following:

DECREES:

That the State of Guatemala guarantees the equality of all human beings in dignity and rights and that men and women, whatever their civil status, have equal opportunities and responsibilities.
WHEREAS:
That Guatemala ratified through Decree Law 49-82 the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, and through Decree Number 69-94 the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Sanction and Eradicate Violence against Women, and as a State party undertook to adopt all appropriate measures, including legislative measures, to modify or repeal laws, regulations, customs and practices that constitute discrimination against women and to issue all those laws that are necessary for such purpose.
WHEREAS:
That the problem of domestic violence is a problem of a social nature, due to the unequal relations existing between men and women in the social, economic, legal, political and cultural fields.
WHEREAS:
That the State of Guatemala in article 47 of the Political Constitution of the Republic guarantees the social, economic and legal protection of the family.
WHEREAS:
That it is necessary to take legislative measures to reduce and subsequently put an end to domestic violence, which causes so much harm to Guatemalan society, and to contribute in this way to the construction of families based on equality and respect for the human dignity of men and women.
THEREFORE:
In exercise of the powers conferred upon it by literal
a) of article 171 of the Political Constitution of the Republic of Guatemala.

LAW TO PREVENT, SANCTION AND ERADICATE DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## ARTICLE 1

Domestic Violence. Domestic violence constitutes a violation of human rights and for the purposes of this law, it must be understood as any action or omission that directly or indirectly causes harm or physical, sexual, psychological or patrimonial suffering, both in the public and private spheres, to a person who is a member of the family group, by relatives or cohabitant or former cohabitant, spouse or former spouse or with whom children have been procreated.

## ARTICLE 2

Application of this law. This law shall regulate the application of protection measures necessary to guarantee the life, integrity, security and dignity of victims of domestic violence. It also aims to provide special protection to women, boys, girls, youth, elderly men and women and persons with disabilities, taking into consideration the specific situations of each case.
The protection measures shall be applied independently of the specific sanctions established by the Penal Code and the Criminal Procedure Code, in the case of acts constituting a crime or misdemeanor.

## ARTICLE 3

Filing of complaints. The complaint or request for protection regulated by this law may be made in written or verbal form with or without the assistance of a lawyer and may be filed by:
a)	Any person, regardless of age, who has been a victim of an act constituting domestic violence.
b)	Any person, when the aggrieved victim suffers from physical or mental incapacity, or when the person is prevented from requesting it by themselves.

c)	Any member of the family group, for the benefit of another member of the group, or any witness to the act.
d)	Members of health or educational services, physicians who by reason of occupation have contact with the aggrieved person, for whom the complaint shall be mandatory in accordance with article 298 of Decree Number 51-92 of the Congress of the Republic. Whoever fails to make this complaint shall be sanctioned as established in article 457 of the Penal Code.

e)	Non-governmental organizations and social organizations whose purpose is the protection of the rights of women, minors and, in general, those that address family problems among their purposes.

f)	If the victim is a minor, they shall be represented by the Public Prosecutor's Office, when the following circumstances concur:
1)	When the aggression comes from the person exercising parental authority, and,
2)	When it concerns minors who lack guardianship and legal representation.

## ARTICLE 4

Institutions. The institutions responsible for receiving the type of complaints mentioned in the previous article shall be:
a)	The Public Prosecutor's Office, through the Women's Prosecutor's Office, permanent attention and victim assistance office.
b)	The Attorney General's Office, through the Unit for the Protection of Women's Rights.
c)	The national police.
d)	Family courts.
e)	Public Legal Aid Offices.
f)	The Human Rights Ombudsman.
Whoever receives the complaint must refer it to a family court or criminal court, as appropriate, within a period not exceeding twenty-four (24) hours.

## ARTICLE 5

Mandatory registration of complaints. All the institutions mentioned in the previous article shall be obligated to register complaints of domestic violence and refer them to Judicial Statistics, for the purpose of evaluating and determining the effectiveness of measures to prevent, sanction and eradicate it and to formulate and apply the changes that are necessary.

## ARTICLE 6

Courts on duty. The justices of the peace on duty shall handle matters related to the application of this law, so that cases that due to schedule or distance could not be attended during normal hours may be handled, the attention provided therein being of an urgent nature.

## ARTÍCULO 7

On security measures. In addition to those contained in Article 88 of the Penal Code, the Courts of Justice, when dealing with situations of domestic violence, shall order any of the following security measures. More than one measure may be applied.
a) Order the alleged aggressor to leave the common residence immediately. If he or she resists, public force shall be used.
b) Order mandatory attendance at institutions with therapeutic or educational programs created for that purpose.
c) Order the search of the dwelling when domestic violence seriously endangers the physical, sexual, patrimonial or psychological integrity of any of its inhabitants.
d) Prohibit the introduction or keeping of weapons in the dwelling when they are used to intimidate, threaten or cause harm to any of the members of the family group.
e) Confiscate weapons in possession of the alleged aggressor, even when he or she has a carrying license.
f) Provisionally suspend the alleged aggressor's custody and care of his or her minor children.
g) Order the alleged aggressor to refrain from interfering, in any way, in the exercise of custody, upbringing and education of his or her children.
h) Suspend the alleged aggressor's right to visit his or her children, in case of sexual aggression against minors.
i) Prohibit the alleged aggressor from disturbing or intimidating any member of the family group.
j) Prohibit the alleged aggressor's access to the permanent or temporary domicile of the victim and to his or her workplace or place of study.
k) Establish a provisional maintenance obligation, in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code.
l) Order the preventive seizure of property of the alleged aggressor. To apply this measure no security deposit shall be necessary.

At the discretion of the competent judicial authority, the seizure shall apply to the family dwelling and to the property necessary to support the maintenance obligation in favor of the victim and the corresponding dependents, according to law.
m) Make an inventory of the movable property existing in the household, in particular household goods or other property that serves as a means of work for the victim.
n) Grant the exclusive use, for a determined period, of household goods to the victim. Special safeguards must be provided for housing and household goods protected under the family patrimony regime.
ñ) Order the alleged aggressor to refrain from interfering in the use and enjoyment of the work instruments of the victim. When the victim is sixty (60) years old or older or is disabled, the alleged aggressor shall not interfere in the use and enjoyment of instruments indispensable for the victim to be self-sufficient or to integrate into society.
o) Order the alleged aggressor to make reparation in cash for damages caused to the victim, or to property that is indispensable for him or her to continue his or her normal life. This includes transportation expenses, property repairs, lodging and medical expenses. The amount shall be made effective in the form and procedure that the judicial authority deems appropriate to ensure that it is complied with.
(Paragraph added by Article 22 of Decree 49-2016 of the Congress of the Republic). The security measures contained in this Law may be accompanied by a telematic control device, for the effective protection of the physical integrity of victims, for the period granted by the competent judge in the security measures applied; said device shall be placed on the alleged aggressor upon expiration of the opposition period.

## ARTÍCULO 8

Duration. Protection measures may not last less than one month nor more than six, except that set forth in subsection c) of the preceding article. However, upon expiration of the period and at the request of a party, the competent authority may extend it.

## ARTÍCULO 9

On recidivism of the aggressor. Recidivism of the aggressor shall be understood as one who has assaulted his or her victim or another member of the family group more than once. This fact may be invoked as grounds for separation or divorce.

## ARTÍCULO 10

On the obligations of the national police. The authorities of the national police shall have the obligation to intervene in situations of domestic violence, ex officio or when requested by victims or third parties. In these cases they shall:
a) Assist and provide protection to victims, even when they are inside their domicile at the time of the complaint, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 208 and 436 of the Penal Code.
b) In case of flagrancy, detain the aggressor and place him or her at the disposal of the judicial authority.
c) Prepare a report or police record on the events that occurred, for which they shall collect information from relatives, neighbors or other persons present and record their names, personal details and place where they can be located to summon them in a possible judicial proceeding.
d) Confiscate weapons and objects used to threaten or assault and place them at the disposal of the respective judicial authority.
Failure to comply with these obligations shall be sanctioned as indicated in Article 114 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

## ARTÍCULO 11

Supplementary application of the law. In everything not provided for in this law, the provisions of the Civil Code, Code of Civil and Commercial Procedure, Penal Code, Code of Criminal Procedure, Law on Family Courts and Law on the Judicial Branch shall apply supplementarily, without the foregoing implying an order of precedence.

## ARTÍCULO 12

Duties of the State. The State shall create, through the Human Rights Ombudsman, an entity responsible for coordinating the delivery of workshops, short courses, seminars and conferences for judges, court support staff, Public Prosecutor's Office, Attorney General's Office, prosecutors, forensic doctors, complaint reception offices, National Police, Ministry of Public Health and other institutions that deal with domestic violence, its seriousness and consequences.

## ARTICLE 13

Advisory body. Until the governing body is created, it shall correspond to the Office of the Attorney General of the Nation, in accordance with the functions stipulated in the Political Constitution of the Republic of Guatemala, to be the advisory body in charge of public policies that promote the prevention, attention, sanction and eradication of domestic violence. Likewise, it shall monitor compliance with the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women. To fulfill these entrusted obligations, the Office of the Attorney General of the Nation shall do so under the following terms.
1)	it shall ensure that the authorities, their officials, personnel and agents of the institutions behave in accordance with the obligations stipulated in this law.

2)	It shall suggest appropriate measures to encourage the modification of legal or customary practices that support the persistence or tolerance of domestic violence.
3)	It shall strengthen the knowledge and observance of the right of women, boys and girls, elderly men and women to a life free of violence and to have their rights respected and protected.
4)	It shall recommend the modification of sociocultural patterns of conduct of men and women, including the design of formal and informal education programs, appropriate for all levels of the educational process, in order to counteract prejudices, customs and all types of practices that are based on the premise of the inferiority of either gender or on stereotypes for men and women, that legitimize or exacerbate violence against persons.
5)	It shall promote the education and training of personnel in the administration of justice, police and other officials responsible for law enforcement, as well as personnel in charge of implementing policies to prevent, punish and eliminate domestic violence.
6)	It shall stimulate educational programs, governmental and from the private sector, aimed at raising awareness among the population about problems related to domestic violence, legal remedies and corresponding reparation.
7)	It shall encourage the media to develop appropriate dissemination guidelines and thus contribute to eradicating domestic violence in all its forms and, especially, to enhance respect for human dignity.
8)	It shall stimulate research and compilation of statistics and relevant information on the causes, consequences and frequency of domestic violence, in order to evaluate state measures.
9)	It shall promote, with international cooperation, the promotion of programs aimed at protecting the right to a life without violence and the exchange of ideas and experiences on the subject.
The State shall endeavor to offer treatment and rehabilitation alternatives to aggressor persons, taking into account, among others, their dual condition as victims and aggressors.

## ARTICLE 14

This decree shall enter into force thirty days after its publication in the official gazette.
