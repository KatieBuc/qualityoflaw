Section II - Offences against personal liberty
(1)
Anyone who, outside the cases provided for in said article, commits sexual acts with a person who, at the time of the act:
1) has not reached the age of fourteen
(2)
;
2) has not reached the age of sixteen, when the offender is the ascendant, the parent, including adoptive parent, or his or her cohabitant, the guardian, or another person to whom, for reasons of care, education, instruction, supervision or custody, the minor is entrusted or who has, with the latter, a cohabitation relationship
(3)
, shall be subject to the penalty established by article
609 bis
.
Outside the cases provided for by article
609 bis
, the ascendant, the parent, including adoptive parent, or his or her cohabitant, the guardian, or another person to whom, for reasons of care, education, instruction, supervision or custody, the minor is entrusted, or who has with the latter a cohabitation relationship, who, by abusing the powers connected to his or her position, commits sexual acts with a minor who has reached the age of sixteen, is punished with imprisonment from three to six years
(4)
.
Outside the cases provided for by the preceding paragraphs, anyone who commits sexual acts with a minor who has reached the age of fourteen, by abusing the trust enjoyed with the minor or the authority or influence exercised over the same by reason of his or her own status or office held or family, domestic, employment, cohabitation or hospitality relationships, is punished with imprisonment up to four years
(5)
.
The penalty is increased:
1) if the commission of sexual acts with the minor who has not reached the age of fourteen occurs in exchange for money or any other benefit, even if only promised;
2) if the offence is committed by several persons acting together;
3) if the offence is committed by a person who is part of a criminal association and for the purpose of facilitating its activity;
4) if from the act, due to the repetition of the conduct, serious harm results to the minor;
5) if from the act danger to the life of the minor results
(6)
(7)
.
A minor who, outside the cases provided for in article
609 bis
, commits sexual acts with a minor who has reached the age of thirteen, if the age difference between the subjects does not exceed four years, is not punishable
(8)
.
In cases of lesser gravity the penalty is reduced by a measure not exceeding two thirds
(9)
.
The penalty referred to in article
609 ter
, second paragraph, applies if the victim has not reached the age of ten.

Explanation of art. 609 quater Criminal Code
Sexual liberty
is acquired, in our legal system, upon reaching the fourteenth year of age.
For these reasons, the provision in question punishes anyone who commits
sexual acts
with a person under fourteen, with the latter's consent
(otherwise the far more serious offence referred to in article
609 bis
being relevant).
The same penalty applies to anyone who commits consensual sexual acts with a person
under
sixteen years of age, when the offender is a person who exercises authority over the minor. Thus, while in other cases a person of legal age may lawfully commit sexual acts with a person over fourteen, the legislator has instead provided for punishability against those who have particular duties of care, instruction and custody towards a person under sixteen who has reached fourteen years of age.
With a lighter penalty, the same person in a position of supremacy or in whom duties of care, custody and instruction subsist is punished if he or she commits sexual acts with a minor, even over sixteen, but only in the case where an
abuse
of the powers connected to his or her position is found, thus subjugating the victim and exploiting his or her own position.
By way of derogation from the above, a minor who commits (consensual) sexual acts with a
person under thirteen years of age
is not punishable if the age difference does not exceed three years (a sixteen-year-old may validly have sexual relations with a thirteen-year-old).
The fourth paragraph provides for a
mitigating circumstance with special and indefinite effect
, where the compression of sexual liberty is minimal, in consideration of the concrete fact and circumstances, while the last paragraph provides for the application of article
609 ter
(aggravated
sexual violence
), in the case where the victim is less than ten years old, equating any consent to lack of consent, given the age of the victim and the consequent absolute impossibility of sexual self-determination.

The conduct of sexual acts with a minor by the parent or other qualified person in any case falls within the hypothesis pursuant to art. 609 quater no. 2 Penal Code even when the victim is under fourteen years of age, with consequent applicability, also in such case, of the accessory penalty of loss of parental authority.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 37509 of 18 October 2011
)
Cass. pen. no. 30548/2011
The aggravating circumstance pursuant to art. 61, no. 11 Penal Code (cohabitation) is concurrent with the aggravating circumstance provided for the offence of sexual acts with a minor, where committed against an injured party under ten years of age (

##### art. 609

quater, fifth paragraph, Criminal Code), insofar as the latter relates to the age of the victim, while the other pertains to the objective fact of abuse of relations arising from cohabitation. (In the case at hand, the Court excluded that the common aggravating circumstance could be considered absorbed by the special one).
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 30548 of 2 August 2011
)
Cass. pen. n. 35809/2010
The condition of entrustment of the minor, required for the commission of the offence of sexual acts with a minor (

##### art. 609

quater, first paragraph, no. 2, Criminal Code), is applicable to the school assistant, as a person assigned to tasks of reception, supervision and surveillance of pupils on the occasion of the temporary absence of teachers and on the occasion of their transfer within the school premises to other locations.
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 35809 of 6 October 2010
)
Cass. pen. n. 27588/2010
The offence of sexual acts with a minor is established regardless of whether or not the victim consented, not only because violence is presumed by law, but also because the victim is considered immature and incapable of knowingly disposing of their own body for sexual purposes.
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 27588 of 15 July 2010
)
Cass. pen. n. 27272/2010
The subjective elements referred to in art. 133, second paragraph, Criminal Code, are not relevant for the purposes of establishing the hypothesis of lesser gravity of the offence of sexual violence, as the mitigation of the sentence does not respond to the need to adapt to the culpability of the offender and to the circumstances relating to their person but to the lesser harmfulness of the act, to be related to the degree of violation of the legal interest of the victim's sexual freedom.
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 27272 of 14 July 2010
)
Cass. pen. n. 11252/2010
The mitigating circumstance of lesser gravity in the offence of sexual acts with a minor applies if the acts committed do not entail a significant compromise of the psycho-physical integrity of the victim, while the possible consent of the victim has no relevance whatsoever for the purposes of recognising the mitigating circumstance. (In its reasoning the Court specified that it is precisely taking advantage of relations of sympathy, confidence, affection or trust to have sexual relations with a minor that constitutes a contamination and corruption of their personality).
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 11252 of 24 March 2010
)
Cass. pen. n. 20246/2009
The constitution as civil party "in their own right" of the parents of the minor, victim of the offence of sexual acts with a minor, is admissible, since such offence, in addition to directly harming the minor, affects the parents' interest in the psycho-physical integrity of their child and their moral sphere.
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 20246 of 14 May 2009
)
Cass. pen. n. 15053/2009
The offence of corruption of a minor is committed by conduct consisting in inviting the victim to watch pornographic screenings accompanied by acts of exhibitionism and self-eroticism. (Case in which the offender did not limit himself to showing himself naked to the minor but invited him to touch himself during the screening of pornographic films).
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 15053 of 8 April 2009
)
Cass. pen. n. 12987/2009
With regard to sexual offences, for the purposes of establishing an attempt to commit sexual acts with a minor in the case where contact between the offender and the minor takes place by means of remote communication, it is necessary to ascertain, on the one hand, the unequivocal intention of the agent to satisfy their own concupiscence and, on the other hand, the objective suitability of the conduct to violate the victim's freedom of sexual self-determination. (Case in which the offender had sent an SMS by mobile phone to a minor in an attempt to induce him to perform acts of self-eroticism on his own person).
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 12987 of 25 March 2009
)
Cass. pen. n. 347/2009
With regard to sexual offences, the mitigating circumstance of the intentional participation of the victim is incompatible with the offence of sexual acts with a minor, since any consent of the victim does not constitute a cause or contributing cause of the event.
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 347 of 9 January 2009
)
Cass. pen. n. 44971/2007
With regard to the assessment of the testimony of a minor victim of the offence of sexual violence, there is no need for psychological investigation in relation to the statements of an adolescent person, whose natural maturation is connected to age, where it is possible to exclude the presence of elements, such as a particular predisposition to fanciful elaboration or suggestion, such as to cast doubt on the account.
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 44971 of 4 December 2007
)
Cass. pen. n. 36389/2007
With regard to offences against individual freedom, for the purposes of establishing the offences of sexual violence (

##### art. 609

bis Criminal Code), sexual acts with a minor (

##### art. 609

quater Criminal Code) and corruption of a minor (

##### art. 609

quinquies Criminal Code), the active or passive role assumed by the accused in the context of the relationship with the victim is irrelevant. (Case in which the accused, a catechism teacher of the victim, had argued that his homosexual tendency excluded its establishment).
–
With regard to sexual acts with a minor, the existence of a relationship of entrustment for reasons of education and instruction (

##### art. 609

quater, first paragraph, no. 2 of the Criminal Code) adopted by the legislator as the interpretative key for a liability presumed ex se. (Case in which the defendant was the catechism teacher of the victim).
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 36389 of 4 October 2007
)
Cass. pen. no. 35875/2007
The attempt of the crime of sexual violence is configurable when, even in the absence of physical contact between the defendant and the victim, the conduct adopted by the former demonstrates the subjective requirement of the intention to achieve the satisfaction of one's own sexual instincts and the objective requirement of suitability to violate the victim's freedom of self-determination in the sexual sphere. (In the present case, the occurrence of the hypothesis of attempt was excluded and that of the consummated crime was deemed to exist in the conduct of a subject who, having lured a minor into his bedroom, had made her sit on his knees and, lifting her skirt, had touched her near the vagina).
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 35875 of 1 October 2007
)
Cass. pen. no. 21815/2007
With regard to sexual acts with a minor under sixteen, the entrustment for reasons of care, education, instruction, supervision or custody is configurable even when the active subject is not the teacher of the class in which the minor is enrolled, but belongs to the same school structure, since also in this case there exists between teacher and pupil that relationship of entrustment contemplated by the criminal incriminating provision.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 21815 of 5 June 2007
)
Cass. pen. no. 19425/2007
Article 609 quater of the Criminal Code (sexual acts with a minor) configures an autonomous hypothesis of crime and not an aggravating circumstance of the crime of sexual violence provided for by the preceding article 609 bis of the Criminal Code. (Principle affirmed, in the present case, for the purposes of the applicability of the maximum duration term of pre-trial detention provided for by the combined provisions of articles 303, first paragraph, letter a), no. 3, and 407, second paragraph no. 7 bis of the Code of Criminal Procedure).
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 19425 of 18 May 2007
)
Cass. pen. no. 16843/2007
With regard to sexual crimes committed against a person under fourteen, punished by the first paragraph of article 609 quater of the Criminal Code, it must be excluded that the mere age datum entails the existence of the condition of physical or psychological inferiority of the victim provided for by the second paragraph of article 609 bis of the Criminal Code, so that, once conduct involving violence, threat or abuse of authority has been excluded, the second criminal offence cannot also be applied, which is alternative and incompatible with the first.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 16843 of 3 May 2007
)
Cass. pen. no. 5003/2007
With regard to crimes against sexual freedom, the evaluation of the content of the statements of the minor victim must contain an examination both of the psychophysical aptitude of the witness to relate the events accurately, that is to receive information, connect it with other information and express it in a complex vision, and of his or her psychological position with respect to the context of internal and external situations that have regulated his or her relations with the external world.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 5003 of 7 February 2007
)
Cass. pen. no. 29730/2006
The age of the minor, if far below the limit of ten years, in addition to being an essential element of the aggravating circumstance referred to in the last paragraph of article 609 quater of the Criminal Code, is legitimately evaluated by the judge also as a concrete element capable of excluding the mitigating circumstance of lesser gravity of the case provided for by article 609 bis, third paragraph, of the Criminal Code. (Case in which the age of the minor was four and a half years).
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 29730 of 6 September 2006
)
Cass. pen. no. 3124/2006
The position of guarantee towards one's own children, established by article 147 of the Civil Code in respect of the parent, entails the obligation for the latter to protect the life, safety and sexual morality of minors against attacks by others: it follows that the mother who does not prevent, and indeed consents to, the spouse sexually abusing minor children is liable for the crime referred to in article 609 quater of the Criminal Code, in complicity with the perpetrator of the crime, pursuant to the second paragraph of article 40 of the Criminal Code.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 3124 of 26 January 2006
)
Cass. pen. no. 45286/2005
With regard to sexual crimes with a minor, the hypothesis of attempt is configured when, even in the absence of acts of physical contact between the defendant and the victim, the conduct adopted is objectively suitable to violate the victim's freedom of self-determination in the sexual sphere and demonstrates the subjective requirement of the intention to achieve the satisfaction of one's own sexual instincts. (In the present case, the Supreme Court deemed suitable and unequivocal the acts consisting in offering on several occasions to a child, a minor under ten years of age, sweets and money with the explicit request to perform sexual acts and in the attempt to drag him into the bathroom, even in the absence of lascivious touching).
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 45286 of 14 December 2005
)
Cass. pen. no. 29662/2004
The crime referred to in article 609 quater of the Criminal Code (sexual acts with a minor) protects the physical-psychological integrity of the minor with a view to the correct development of sexual personality through absolute intangibility in the case of a minor under fourteen years of age (first paragraph no. 1) or relative with reference to specific situations of kinship or entrustment of the minor himself or herself (first paragraph no. 2) and is configured even in the absence of coercive pressures, given that in such cases a facilitation of consent or an impediment to the refusal thereof may occur.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 29662 of 8 July 2004
)
Cass. pen. no. 18058/2004
With regard to crimes against sexual freedom, statements made by the minor to his or her own parents are not usable when the direct examination of the minor has been omitted, with the sole possibility that the psychological balance of the latter is so fragile as to result in a true and manifest infirmity capable of allowing the recovery of the indirect testimony of the parents.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 18058 of 20 April 2004
)
Cass. pen. no. 15287/2004
The offence referred to in article 609 quater of the Criminal Code, which criminalises sexual acts with a minor, protects the correct development of the sexual personality of the minor by establishing his or her absolute sexual intangibility (for minors under fourteen years of age) and relative intangibility (in particular situations, for minors under sixteen years of age in relation to the active subject in a relationship of kinship, care or supervision with the minor himself or herself), is configurable in the absence of any coercive pressure and is characterised as a crime with a free form, comprising all possible forms of aggression against the minor, with the exclusion of the typical acts of coercion indicated by article 609 bis of the Criminal Code, which, having the minor as addressee, rather constitute the offence of aggravated sexual violence pursuant to article 609 ter, first paragraph, no. 1 of the Criminal Code.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 15287 of 30 March 2004
)
Cass. pen. no. 36995/2001
With regard to crimes against sexual freedom, the refusal to grant generic mitigating circumstances is legitimate on the basis of elements such as the evaluation of the claim for unnatural relations with a very young subject and the intensity of intent demonstrated by the repetition of the action over time.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 36995 of 15 October 2001
)
Cass. pen. no. 28373/2001
With regard to sexual acts with a minor, the hypothesis of attempt must be configured in cases where the perpetrator does not commit an actual and concrete intrusion into the sexual sphere of the victim, but limits himself or herself to acts which, although superficial, constitute an objective manifestation of sexuality. Such characteristics of the conduct, however, do not entail the automatic application of the reduction for lesser gravity, provided for by the last paragraph of article 609 quater of the Criminal Code, since the gravity must be evaluated with reference to the harmful extent that would have occurred in the case of a crime carried to completion. (Case in which the defendant had found satisfaction in viewing the nudity of the minor and had remunerated the latter with an invitation to a subsequent appointment).
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 28373 of 12 July 2001
)
Cass. pen. no. 26930/2001
The inducement of a minor under fourteen to commit acts of lewdness on the person of the agent, already falling within the provisions of the repealed article 530, second paragraph, of the Criminal Code, is to be considered still punishable pursuant to article 609 quater, paragraph 1, no. 1, of the same code, while the cited article 530 of the Criminal Code remains applicable, as more favourable from the point of view of sanctions, if the act was committed under the validity of that provision.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 26930 of 4 July 2001
)
Cass. pen. no. 10936/2001
With regard to crimes against sexual freedom, the hypothesis provided for by the last paragraph of article 609 quater of the Criminal Code, which punishes sexual acts committed with a person under ten years of age, does not constitute an autonomous criminal offence, but rather an aggravating circumstance of the crime contemplated by the first paragraph of the same provision (sexual acts with a minor), given that the last paragraph of the cited article refers, for the determination of the penalty in the case where the victim has not reached ten years of age, to the second paragraph of the preceding article 609 ter of the Criminal Code, whose heading, referring to aggravating circumstances, demonstrates the legislator's intention to consider certain factual elements as prerequisites for the aggravation of the penalty and not for the configuration of autonomous criminal offences. Moreover, among the aggravating circumstances referred to in the cited article 609 ter, the case is indicated where the criminal conduct was committed against a person under ten years of age, thus achieving full symmetry between the hypotheses referred to in articles 609 bis (sexual violence) and 609 quater of the cited Criminal Code.
–
With regard to crimes against sexual freedom, the crime referred to in article 609 quater (sexual acts with a minor) may be circumstanced by the reduction for an act of lesser gravity, referred to in the third paragraph, and by the aggravating circumstance of sexual acts on a minor under ten years of age, referred to in the fourth paragraph, so that, where both circumstances concur, the trial judge is called upon to make a judgment of equivalence or prevalence.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 10936 of 20 March 2001
)
Cass. pen. no. 2083/2000
The reduction provided for by article 609 quater, paragraph 3, of the Criminal Code configures a mitigating circumstance with special effect, the recognition of which in concrete terms results in a different legal qualification of the act. Consequently, the appeal of the Public Prosecutor against the decision issued pursuant to article 444 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, which frames the facts with application of the cited provision, must be deemed admissible, it being possible to denounce by appeal to the Court of Cassation the erroneous legal qualification of the act as proposed by the parties and accepted by the judge with the plea bargaining judgment. (In the present case, the Court held that the qualification of the act of sexual acts with a minor as falling within the offence of lesser gravity provided for by the cited third paragraph, made on the basis of mere consideration of the modalities of the act and not noting that in the case at hand it was a matter of sexual acts with a minor under ten years of age, could not be considered correct, given the provision of greater gravity pursuant to paragraph 4 of the same article 609 quater of the Criminal Code for acts committed with a minor under ten years of age).
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 2083 of 2 October 2000
)
Cass. pen. no. 9528/2000
With regard to sexual violence, the mitigating circumstance provided for by article 609 quater of the Criminal Code for cases of lesser gravity must be considered applicable, like the analogous one provided for by article 609 bis, paragraph 3 of the same code, in all those cases in which – having regard to the means, the methods of execution and the circumstances of the action – it is possible to consider that the personal sexual freedom of the victim has been compressed in a manner that is not serious, and implies the need for a global evaluation of the act, not limited to the objective components of the crime alone, but extended also to the subjective ones and to all the elements mentioned in article 133 of the Criminal Code.
(
Court of Cassation, Criminal Division,
Section III,
judgment no. 9528 of 7 September 2000
)
Cass. pen. no. 2841/1998
With the entry into force of Law 66 of 1996, the so-called presumed carnal violence, referred to in article 519, second paragraph, of the Criminal Code is no longer configurable. To fill this regulatory gap, the disvalue that the legal system attributes to certain behaviours remaining unaltered, the new autonomous crime of sexual acts with a minor was introduced (

##### art. 609

quater c.p.) which punishes the commission, even if non-violent, of sexual acts with minors under fourteen years of age, and with the concurrence of specific conditions in the case where the minor has not yet reached sixteen years of age. Therefore, in the case of sexual acts with persons over fourteen years of age, it is necessary to verify the existence of the specific conditions provided for by art. 609 quater.
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 2841 of 5 March 1998
)
Cass. pen. no. 4004/1998
the attempt of sexual violence referred to in the first paragraph of art. 609 quater c.p. (sexual acts with minors) is configurable in the case where the perpetrator on multiple and close occasions maintains violent attitudes (in this case grabbing by the neck) towards a minor, inviting him each time at the same time to follow him, so that he may engage in sexual practices in his presence. The repetitiveness of the episodes and the pressing manner of the request are in the abstract relevant indicators of the suitability of the action. However, it is necessary, in order to also verify the element of unequivocality, to insert the behaviour in the concrete modalities of space and time, to verify its direction towards the actual perpetration of the offence and the coincidence of the concrete case with the legal one.
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 4004 of 15 January 1998
)
Cass. pen. no. 11193/1997
With regard to crimes against sexual freedom, Law no. 66 of 15 February 1996 has not decriminalized conduct carried out during the validity of the previous legislation when it coincides with that provided for by the ius superveniens, so that the act previously framed in the provision of arts. 56 and 521 c.p. is attributable to the notion of sexual acts referred to in art. 609 quater c.p. The unlawfulness of the behaviour, since the crime of sexual violence falls among those against personal freedom and no longer among those against public morality, must be assessed according to the respect due to the human person and its aptitude to offend the freedom of determination in the sexual sphere. (In this case the Court held that the display of the member to a minor under fourteen years of age, with an invitation to touch it, did not constitute the crime of obscene acts, but rather that of violent acts of lust, in the attempt phase, with exclusion of the mitigating circumstance of lesser gravity referred to in art. 3 Law no. 66 of 15 February 1996).
(
Criminal Court of Cassation,
Section III,
judgment no. 11193 of 4 December 1997
