## CHAPTER I - GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1

Activities aimed at the prevention, early detection, attention and eradication of domestic violence are declared to be of general interest. The provisions of this law are of public order.
References to the article

### Article 2

Domestic violence constitutes any action or omission, direct or indirect, that by any means diminishes, illegitimately limiting the free exercise or enjoyment of the human rights of a person, caused by another with whom they have or have had a dating relationship or with whom they have or have had an affective relationship based on cohabitation and originating from kinship, marriage or de facto union.
References to the article

### Article 3

The following are manifestations of domestic violence, whether or not they constitute a crime:
A) Physical violence. Action, omission or pattern of conduct that harms the bodily integrity of a person.
B) Psychological or emotional violence. Any action or omission directed at disturbing, degrading or controlling the conduct, behavior, beliefs or decisions of a person, through humiliation, intimidation, isolation or any other means that affects psychological or emotional stability.
C) Sexual violence. Any action that imposes or induces sexual behaviors on a person through the use of: force, intimidation, coercion, manipulation, threat or any other means that nullifies or limits sexual freedom.
D) Patrimonial violence. Any action or omission that with manifest illegality implies damage, loss, transformation, theft, destruction, diversion, concealment or retention of goods, work instruments, documents or economic resources, intended to coerce the self-determination of another person.
References to the article

## CHAPTER II - JURISDICTION AND COMPETENCE

### Article 4

Courts with jurisdiction in family matters shall also hear non-criminal matters of domestic violence and personal or patrimonial matters arising therefrom.
References to the article

### Article 5

Courts and Prosecutors' Offices with jurisdiction in family matters shall also be competent to attend to emergency situations of domestic violence.
To this end, the Supreme Court of Justice and the Ministry of Education and Culture, upon proposal of the Court Prosecutor's Office, shall determine, as applicable, the rotation system to attend, during business and non-business hours and days, all matters requiring their intervention in accordance with this law.
References to the article

### Article 6

Justices of the Peace, in the interior of the Republic, whatever their category, shall have emergency jurisdiction to hear matters of domestic violence, being able to provisionally order the pertinent measures established in this law for the protection of alleged victims, and must refer the matters to the corresponding Court of First Instance, necessarily within forty-eight hours of having become aware of the facts, to whose resolution they shall abide.
References to the article

### Article 7

All judicial proceedings in matters of domestic violence shall mandatorily be notified to the corresponding Prosecutor, from the outset. The Prosecutor must intervene in all matters relating to the persons and interests of victims of domestic violence.
References to the article

## CHAPTER III - STANDING OF THE COMPLAINANT AND SUMMONING OF THIRD PARTIES TO TRIAL

### Article 8

Any person who becomes aware of an act of domestic violence may give notice to the competent Judge in the matter, who must adopt the measures deemed pertinent in accordance with the provisions of this law.
Provided that the notice presents plausibility, no liability of any kind shall fall upon the person who gave it.
The Judge, ex officio or at the request of the Public Prosecutor's Office, may summon third parties to the trial.
References to the article

## CHAPTER IV - PROTECTION MEASURES

### Article 9

In all matters of domestic violence, in addition to the measures provided for in article 316 of the General Code of Procedure, the Judge, ex officio, at the request of a party or of the Public Prosecutor's Office, must order all measures aimed at protecting the life, physical or emotional integrity of the victim, personal freedom and security, as well as the economic assistance and patrimonial integrity of the family unit.
The Supreme Court of Justice may order the use of expert action protocols that it shall regulate, for the purpose of detecting and qualifying situations of domestic violence. The courts may order their urgent use, prior to the adoption of the measures referred to in the following article. (*)

### Article 10

To these ends, the following measures, or similar ones, may be adopted for the fulfillment of the precautionary purpose:
1) Order the removal of the aggressor from the common residence and the immediate delivery of their personal effects in the presence of the Bailiff.
Likewise, a judicial inventory shall be made of the movable property that is removed and of that which remains in the place, and testimony may be issued at the request of the parties.
2) Order the return to the domicile or residence of the victim who has left it for reasons of personal security, in the presence of the Bailiff.
3) Prohibit, restrict or limit the presence of the aggressor at the domicile or residence, workplaces, places of study or others frequented by the victim.
4) Prohibit the aggressor from communicating, relating, meeting or developing any similar conduct in relation to the victim, other affected persons, witnesses or complainants of the act.
5) Seize the weapons that the aggressor has in their possession, which shall remain in custody of the Court, in the manner it deems pertinent. Prohibit the aggressor from using or possessing firearms, notifying the competent authority to this effect.
6) Set a provisional maintenance obligation in favor of the victim.
7) Order the mandatory attendance of the aggressor to rehabilitation programs.
8) Likewise, if applicable, provisionally resolve everything related to maintenance payments and, as the case may be, custody, care and visitation.
In the event that the Judge decides not to adopt any measure, their decision must express the grounds for such determination. (*)

### Artículo 11

In all cases, the Judge shall order the Bailiff or whomever deemed
appropriate, to supervise compliance and shall convene a hearing,
within a period not exceeding ten days from the adoption of the measure, for the
purpose of its evaluation. In case of non-appearance, the Judge shall order the
detention of the aggressor.
If the measures ordered are not complied with, the Judge shall order the arrest of
the aggressor for a maximum period of forty-eight hours, without prejudice to
the provisions of articles 21.3, 374.1, 374.2 and 374.4 of the General
Code of Procedure.
Once the precautionary measure has been adopted and the aforementioned hearing
has been held, the case files must be remitted to the Court that had been hearing
the proceedings relating to the family involved. (*)

### Artículo 12

The measures adopted shall have the scope and duration that the Judge
orders, without prejudice to the substantiation of the claim, its
modification or cessation.

### Artículo 13

The procedure for the adoption of precautionary measures shall be that
provided for by articles 313, 314 and 315 of the General Code of Procedure.
Whenever it is proven that a right intrinsic to the human being is being
violated or threatened, the Judge must, immediately, decree the
precautionary measures that correspond, in a reasoned manner. Likewise, the Judge shall proceed
when prior hearing of the aggressor may frustrate the proper purpose of the
measure.

### Artículo 14

In evidentiary matters, the provisions of the General
Code of Procedure shall apply, bearing in mind the objective and purpose of this law and
the provisions contained in the following articles.

### Artículo 15

Once the precautionary measures established in article 10
of this law have been adopted, the Court shall order ex officio the preparation of a
situational assessment among the subjects involved. It shall be
prepared in an interdisciplinary manner and shall have the purpose of determining the
physical or psychological harm suffered by the victim, evaluating the situation
of danger or risk and the social environment.
This assessment must be available to the Court at the time of
holding the hearing set in article 11 of this law. If due to
the characteristics of the situation, it is considered necessary to adopt
measures or medical, psychological or other treatments
with respect to any of the subjects involved, the Court may entrust
their implementation to one of the public or private institutions competent
in the matter. (*)

### Artículo 16

For the purposes of complying with the provisions of the preceding
article, the Ministry of Education and Culture, through the National
Institute of Family and Women, shall promote the training of experts in
domestic violence, with interdisciplinary work capacity, which shall be
incorporated within the sphere of the Forensic Technical Institute.
The corresponding regulations shall entrust the National Institute of
Family and Women to establish the requirements that interested parties must
meet to accredit their expert competence in the area of
domestic violence regulated by this law.

### Artículo 17

The Supreme Court of Justice shall incorporate this category of professionals
into the Single Registry of Experts. It shall also incorporate into this Registry those
who prove before the Ministry of Education and Culture -which shall have
for this purpose the collaboration of the University of the Republic or
authorized Universities- notorious competence in the matter at the time of
entry into force of this law.

### Artículo 18

In all cases the guiding principle shall be to prevent
secondary victimization, prohibiting the confrontation or
joint appearance of the victim and the aggressor in the case of
children and adolescents under 18 years of age.
In the case of the adult victim who requires such confrontation and it is
certified that he or she is in a condition to carry it out, it may be
conducted. The Court shall determine the form and technical means to receive
the statement, applying the principles of immediacy,
concentration and contradiction.
It may, as the case may be, request beforehand from the interdisciplinary team to
report whether the victim is in a condition to be questioned at
that time.
References to the article

### Artículo 19

Situations of domestic violence must be evaluated from the
perspective of comprehensive protection of human dignity.
Likewise, special consideration shall be given to the fact that the acts constituting
domestic violence to be proven constitute, in general, situations
linked to the privacy of the home, knowledge of which resides in the
nucleus of persons affected by the acts of violence.

## CAPITULO V - MANDATORY LEGAL ASSISTANCE

### Artículo 20

The Supreme Court of Justice must guarantee mandatory legal assistance
to the victim, for which purpose it shall be empowered to enter into
agreements with public or private entities specialized in the
matter. (*)

## CAPITULO VI - COORDINATION OF ACTIONS

### Artículo 21

When a Court with jurisdiction in criminal matters or a
Court with jurisdiction in matters of minors intervenes in a situation of
domestic violence, whatever resolution it adopts, it must
remit, within forty-eight hours of having become aware
of the facts, complete testimony of the proceedings and of the resolution
adopted to the Judge with jurisdiction in matters of domestic violence.
Likewise, when prosecution with imprisonment has been ordered, it must
communicate the release or the granting of temporary leaves or
any form of conclusion of the proceedings to the competent Court in
matters of domestic violence, prior to its effectuation. It must also
bring it to the knowledge of the victim at his or her actual domicile and of his or her
attorney at the domicile constituted, in the latter case if it is within the knowledge
of the Court, in the manner it deems most effective to obtain
the protection purpose pursued by this law.
In the same way, Courts with urgent jurisdiction in matters of
domestic violence shall communicate the facts with criminal appearance that
have come to their knowledge, within twenty-four hours, to the
Criminal Court on Duty.
The same obligation is provided for representatives of the Public
Prosecutor's Office among themselves.
References to the article

## CAPITULO VII

PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND PROMOTION OF COMPREHENSIVE CARE FOR THE VICTIM

### Artículo 22

The State must adopt all necessary measures to prevent,
punish and eradicate domestic violence and promote comprehensive
support for the victim.

### Artículo 23

The rehabilitation and social reintegration of the aggressor must form
part of a policy that seeks to protect all related
persons. Assistance and treatment must be instruments of
this policy.
