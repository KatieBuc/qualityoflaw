Full Text Regulation 8589
Law on Criminalization of Violence Against Women
Full text minutes: 1441EF
No. 8589
THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY
OF THE REPUBLIC OF COSTA RICA
DECREES:
CRIMINALIZATION OF VIOLENCE AGAINST
WOMEN

### TITLE I

GENERAL PART

#### CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

##### Article 1

Purpose. The purpose of
this law is to protect the rights of women victims of violence and
to punish forms of physical, psychological, sexual and patrimonial violence
perpetrated against them, as it is a discriminatory practice based on
gender, specifically in a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital,
common-law union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other
analogous relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, in fulfillment of
the obligations undertaken by the State in the Convention on the Elimination
of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, Law 6968, of October 2,
1984, as well as in the Inter-American Convention to Prevent,
Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women, Law 7499, of May 2,
1995.
(As amended by

##### article 1 of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)

Article file

##### Article 2- Scope of application

This law shall apply when the conduct classified as crimes is directed
against a woman, in the context of or on the occasion of a relationship or partnership bond,
whether marital, common-law union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation,
casual or other analogous relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided
that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a
greater penalty.
The scope of application
of this law is extended for the cases contemplated in article 21 bis, called
"Femicide in other contexts", as well as to
what is established in the second paragraph of articles 22 "Mistreatment",
23 "Restriction on freedom of movement" and 27 "Threats against
a woman" of this law.
(The preceding paragraph
thus added by article 1 of the Law to establish
extended femicide, No. 10022 of August 23, 2021)
(As amended by

##### article 1 of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)

Article file

##### ARTICLE 3.- Sources of

interpretation
All international human rights instruments in force
in the country constitute sources of interpretation
of this Law, which have a value similar to the Political Constitution, which,
to the extent that they grant greater rights and guarantees to persons,
prevail over the Political Constitution. In particular, the following shall be sources of
interpretation of this Law:
a) The Convention on the elimination of all forms of
discrimination against women, Law
No.
6968, of October 2,
1984.
b) The Inter-American Convention to prevent, punish and eradicate
violence against women, Law
No.
7499, of May 2,
1995.
Article file

##### ARTICLE 4.- Crimes of

public action
All crimes contemplated in this Law shall be of public action.
Article file

##### ARTICLE 5.- Obligations of

persons in public service
Those who, in the exercise of their functions, are obligated to know of
situations of violence against women, in any of its forms, or to
resolve them, must act promptly and effectively, respecting both the
procedures and the human rights of the affected women; otherwise, they
may incur the crime of breach of duties.
Article file

##### ARTICLE 6.- Guarantee of

fulfillment of a duty
A person who, in the exercise of a public function, files a formal complaint of
any of the crimes of public action contained in this Law shall not incur a crime, even if the
accused is not convicted, except when the crimes of slander
and false accusation are configured.
Article file

##### Article 7.- Protection of victims during the process

To
protect victims, the protection measures contemplated in Law No. 7586, Law against
Domestic Violence, of April 10, 1996, may be requested from the beginning of the
judicial investigation, as well as the necessary
precautionary measures provided for in Law No. 7594, Code of Criminal Procedure, of
April 10, 1996.
Likewise,
the judge may order the accused person to use the electronic device
without prejudice to linking with the victim, in order to guarantee their protection.
(As amended by article 7 of law
No. 9271 of September 30, 2014, "Electronic tracking mechanisms in criminal matters")
Article file

##### ARTICLE 8.- Circumstances

general aggravating factors of the crime
The following shall be
general aggravating circumstances of the punishable conduct described in this Law, with the exception of the
crime of femicide, and provided they are not constitutive of the type, to perpetrate the act:
a) Against a woman who has a sensory, physical or
mental disability, total or partial, temporary or permanent.
b) Against a woman over sixty-five years of age.
c) Against a woman in a state of pregnancy or during the three months
following childbirth.
d) In the presence of the minor children of the victim or
of the perpetrator of the crime.
e) With the participation of other persons, with force against things or
through the use of weapons.
f) With treachery or cruelty.
g) For a price, reward, remunerative promise or advantage of any
other nature.
h) With the use of a high degree of scientific, professional or
technological knowledge of the perpetrator in the commission of the crime.
i) With the use of animals.
The judge who imposes the sentence shall increase by up to one third the one indicated for
the corresponding crime, when one or more aggravating
circumstances concur.
Article file
SECTION I
Types of penalties

##### ARTICLE 9.- Types of penalties

for crimes
The penalties applicable to the crimes described in this Law shall be:
1.- Principal:
a) Imprisonment.
2.- Alternative:
a) Weekend detention.
b) Provision of public service.
c) Compliance with instructions.
d) Banishment.
3.- Accessory:
a) Disqualification.
Article file
SECTION
II
Definitions

##### ARTICLE 10.- Principal penalty

The
principal penalty for the crimes set forth in this Law shall be imprisonment. The judge
may opt for alternative penalties, if doing so does not place the life
or integrity of the victim at risk or if the victim is harmed in the exercise of other
rights. For such purpose, the trial court, prior to the replacement of the
imprisonment penalty, must order another complete psychological and psychiatric examination
if it considers it necessary; in addition, it must hear the victim's opinion. In case of replacement by serving half of the penalty, the
sentencing enforcement judge must hear the victim beforehand, if the victim is
locatable.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 11.- Imposition and

replacement of alternative penalties
When a person who is a first-time offender in
matters of violence against women is imposed an imprisonment penalty of less
than three years, such penalty, in accordance with Article 9 of this Law, may
be replaced by two alternative penalties from those indicated in this Law; one of
them shall necessarily be the penalty of compliance with instructions, except
when the penalty of banishment is applied.
Also, at the request of the convicted person, alternative penalties may be applied
when such person is a first-time offender in matters of violence against
women, has been imposed a penalty exceeding three years, and has
served at least half of it. The alternative penalty may not exceed the
amount of the principal penalty imposed.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 12.- Penalty of

weekend detention
The penalty of weekend detention
shall consist of a limitation of freedom of movement and shall be served in a
penitentiary center or in a rehabilitation center for periods
corresponding to weekends, with a minimum duration of twenty-four
hours and a maximum of forty-eight hours per week.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 13.- Penalty of

provision of public service
The penalty of provision of public service
shall consist of the convicted person providing service at the
places and times determined by the judge, in favor of public welfare establishments or community service organizations, or social organizations, under the
control of the authorities of such centers, in such a manner that does not violate the human rights of the convicted person, does not disturb their
work activity, and does not place the victim or third parties at risk. The
periods for serving this penalty shall be eight to sixteen hours
per week.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 14.- Revocation of

an alternative penalty
The non-compliance with an alternative penalty shall authorize the sentencing enforcement judge
to revoke it and order that the convicted person be subjected to the
imprisonment penalty for the remaining time of the sentence to be served.
Upon commission of a new crime, the judge shall have the authority to
revoke the alternative penalty, if the person is subsequently sentenced in
other criminal cases for violence against women.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 15.- Accessory

penalties
Accessory penalties shall be applied
together with the imprisonment penalty or alternative penalties. The replacement of the
principal penalty with alternative penalties shall not affect the serving of the
accessory penalty. The foregoing shall be carried out respecting, at all times, the right of
the accused to due process of law in criminal matters.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 16.- Penalty of compliance

with instructions
The penalty of compliance with
instructions shall consist of submission to a conduct plan while at liberty,
which shall be established by the judge issuing the sentence or by the sentencing enforcement judge and may contain the following instructions:
a) Subject the person to an addiction treatment program for
control of consumption of alcohol, narcotic substances, psychotropic substances, or
narcotic drugs, when such addiction is related to the sanctioned conduct
or its circumstances.
b) Subject the person to a specialized program for offenders,
oriented toward control of violent conduct and complete treatments,
psychological and psychiatric.
c) Prohibition of residence: this penalty consists of the prohibition of
residing in a determined place and of going to it or passing through it without
judicial authorization. The judge shall determine the place, which may be a
neighborhood, a district, a canton, or a province, taking into account the need
for protection of the victims. This instruction may in no case assume the
form of a punishment of exile.
d) Limitation on use of weapons: it shall consist of the prohibition of obtaining
permits for possession, registration, and
carrying
of
weapons of any type. The final judgment imposing this penalty must be
communicated to the National Arsenal of the Ministry of Public Security, which shall maintain
a file of such judgments, for the purpose of considering any application for
registration or
carrying
of firearms made by
the sentenced person.
For the purposes of subsections a) and b) of this article, the
National Institute for Women and the Ministry of Justice shall send each
year, to the Supreme Court of Justice, the list of accredited institutions,
public and private, to which the competent judicial authority may
refer for the serving of this penalty. The expenses incurred for
this treatment shall be borne by the State, unless the convicted person
has sufficient resources to pay for them.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 17.- Penalty of

disqualification
The penalty of disqualification shall produce
the suspension or restriction to exercise one or more of the rights indicated
in this article. In a reasoned judgment, the judge shall apply the
pertinent penalties, according to the crime committed.
The penalty of disqualification shall consist of:
a) Impediment to exercise public office, including those of popular
election, the profession, trade, or activity on the occasion of whose performance
the crime was committed.
b) Impediment to exercise guardianship, curatorship, or judicial
administration of property, when the crime was committed by taking advantage of these legal
situations.
The penalty of disqualification may not be less than one year nor more than
twelve years.
The replacement of the principal penalty shall not affect the serving of the
disqualification penalty.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 18.- Rehabilitation

The person convicted to the penalty of
disqualification may be rehabilitated when half of the
term thereof has elapsed, if the disqualification has not been violated and if the damage has been repaired to
the satisfaction of the victim.
When the disqualification has entailed the loss of a public office,
rehabilitation shall not entail reinstatement in that office.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 19.- Penalty of

banishment
When a foreign person is
imposed an imprisonment penalty of five years or less, in judgment or during its
execution, it may be replaced by the obligation to immediately leave the
national territory and not to reenter it for double the time of the
sentence. This penalty shall not be applied when it seriously harms the
property interests of the victim or when it makes impossible the fulfillment of
family duties. Reentry into the country shall imply the revocation of the
replacement, without prejudice to other liabilities. For migration
control, the General Directorate of Migration and Foreigners shall maintain a
special index of this type of convicted persons.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 20

Institutional responsibilities in the execution of alternative penalties
The Ministry of Public Security
shall assist the Judicial Branch and the Ministry of Justice and Grace in the
formulation and operationalization of a system for execution of the alternative penalties
contemplated
Ficha articulo

##### Article 21

Femicide. A prison sentence of twenty to thirty-five years shall be imposed on whoever causes the death of a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty.
(As amended by

##### article 1 of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)

Article file

##### Article 21 bis

Femicide in other contexts. A prison sentence of twenty to thirty-five years shall be imposed on whoever causes the death of a woman of legal age or minor, when one of the following circumstances is present:
a) When the perpetrator has taken advantage of a relationship or bond of trust, friendship, kinship, authority or a power relationship that he had with the woman victim or it occurs within family relationships of consanguinity or affinity up to the third degree, whether or not they share or have shared the same domicile.
b) When the perpetrator has a history of violence perpetrated against the woman victim, in the family, work, educational, community or religious sphere, even when the facts have not been previously reported.
c) When the perpetrator is an exploitative sexual client, trafficker or pimp of the woman victim.
d) When the woman victim had refused to establish or reestablish, with the perpetrator, a permanent or casual relationship or partnership bond, or to have any type of sexual contact.
e) When the perpetrator commits the act to prepare, facilitate, consummate or conceal a sexual crime.
f) When the perpetrator has committed the act using the woman victim as an act of revenge, reprisal or debt collection in organized crimes of drug trafficking or other related crimes.
g) When the perpetrator has committed the act by reason of the participation, position or political activity of the woman victim.
(As added by article 1 of the Law to establish expanded femicide, No. 10022 of August 23, 2021)
Article file

##### Article 22

Mistreatment. Whoever by any means strikes or physically mistreats a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, without incapacitating her for her habitual occupations, shall be imposed a prison sentence of three months to one year.
The same penalty shall be imposed when the mistreatment conduct is committed in any of the circumstances established in

##### article 21 bis of this law

(As added the preceding paragraph by article 3 of the Law to establish expanded femicide, No. 10022 of August 23, 2021)
If the action results in an incapacity for her habitual work of less than five days, a prison sentence of six months to one year shall be imposed, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty.
Whoever causes damage to the body or health of a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty that produces an incapacity for her habitual occupations for a time greater than five days and up to one month, shall be imposed a prison sentence of eight months to two years.
(As amended by

##### article 1 of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)

Article file

##### Article 23- Restriction of

freedom of movement. Whoever, without profit motive, deprives or restricts the freedom of movement of a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be punished with a prison sentence of two to ten years.
The same penalty shall be imposed when the conduct is committed in any of the circumstances established in article 21 bis of this law.
(As added the preceding paragraph by article 3 of the Law to establish expanded femicide, No. 10022 of August 23, 2021)
(As amended by

##### article 1 of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)

Article file

##### ARTICLE 24.- Penalty of

disqualification
The perpetrator of the crimes contemplated in this chapter shall be imposed, in addition, the penalty of disqualification from one to twelve years.
Article file

#### CHAPTER II

PSYCHOLOGICAL VIOLENCE

##### Article 25- Offenses to

dignity.
Whoever offends in word the dignity or decorum of a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be punished with a prison sentence of six months to two years.
(As amended by article 1 of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)
Article file

##### Article 26- Restriction of

self-determination. A prison sentence of two to four years shall be imposed on whoever, through the use of threats, violence, intimidation, blackmail, pursuit or harassment, compels a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, to do, refrain from doing or tolerate something to which she is not obligated.
(As amended by

##### article 1 of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)

Article file

##### Article 27- Threats against a

woman. Whoever threatens a woman, her family or a third person intimately linked, with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be punished with a prison sentence of six months to two years.
The same penalty shall be imposed when the threatening conduct is committed in any of the circumstances established in

##### article 21 bis of this law

(As added the preceding paragraph by article 3 of the Law to establish expanded femicide, No. 10022 of August 23, 2021)
(As amended by

##### article 1 of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)

Article file

##### ARTICLE 28.- Penalty of

disqualification
The perpetrator of the crimes contemplated in this chapter shall be imposed, in addition, the penalty of disqualification from one to six years.
Article file

#### CHAPTER III

SEXUAL VIOLENCE

##### Artículo 29- Violation against a

woman. Whoever introduces the penis, orally, anally or vaginally, to a woman
with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership bond, be
it matrimonial, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation,
casual or other analogous relationship, even when divorce, separation or breakup
has occurred, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime
or one provided with a greater penalty, against her will, shall be punished with
imprisonment of twelve to eighteen years.
The same penalty shall be applied to
whoever introduces any object, animal or body part, vaginally or anally, to
whoever forces the victim to introduce, anally or vaginally, any body part or
object to the perpetrator or to herself.
(As amended by article
1° of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)
Ficha articulo

##### Artículo 30- Abusive sexual

conduct. A penalty of imprisonment of three to six years shall be imposed on
whoever forces a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship
or partnership bond, be it matrimonial, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation,
non-cohabitation, casual or other analogous relationship, even when divorce,
separation or breakup has occurred, provided that the conduct does not
constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, to
endure during sexual intercourse acts that cause her pain or humiliation, to
perform or view acts of exhibitionism, to view or listen to pornographic
material or to view or listen to acts with sexual content.
(As amended by

##### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

Ficha articulo

##### Artículo 31- Sexual exploitation

of a woman. Whoever forces a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a
relationship or partnership bond, be it matrimonial, de facto union, courtship,
cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other analogous relationship, even
when divorce, separation or breakup has occurred, provided that the conduct does
not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, to
have sexual relations with third parties, without profit motive, shall be
punished with imprisonment of two to five years.
(As amended by

##### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

Ficha articulo

##### ARTÍCULO 32.- Aggravated

forms of sexual violence
The penalty for the crimes referred to in
the three preceding articles shall be increased by up to one third, if from the
commission of the act any of the following consequences result:
a) Pregnancy of the victim.
b) Transmission of a sexually transmitted disease to the victim.
c) Permanent psychological damage.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTÍCULO 33.- Penalty of

disqualification
The perpetrator of the crimes contemplated
in this chapter shall be imposed, in addition, the penalty of disqualification
from three to twelve years.
Ficha articulo

#### CAPÍTULO IV

VIOLENCIA PATRIMONIAL

##### Artículo 34- Patrimonial

misappropriation. Whoever unlawfully misappropriates any property or asset from
the possession or patrimony of a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained
a relationship or partnership bond, be it matrimonial, de facto union,
courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other analogous
relationship, even when divorce, separation or breakup has occurred, provided
that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a
greater penalty, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three
years.
(As amended by

##### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

Ficha articulo

##### Artículo 35

Patrimonial damage. The
person who to the detriment of a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained
a relationship or partnership bond, be it matrimonial, de facto union,
courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other analogous
relationship, even when divorce, separation or breakup has occurred, destroys,
renders useless, causes to disappear or damages property of her ownership,
possession or holding, or property that is susceptible to being community
property, shall be punished with imprisonment of three months to two years,
provided it does not constitute another crime punished more severely.
(As amended by

##### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

Ficha articulo

##### Artículo 36- Limitation on the

exercise of property rights. The person who prevents, limits or prohibits the
use, enjoyment, administration, transformation, alienation or disposition of one
or more properties that form part of the patrimony of the woman with whom he
maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership bond, be it
matrimonial, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual
or other analogous relationship, even when divorce, separation or breakup has
occurred, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or
one provided with a greater penalty, shall be punished with imprisonment of
eight months to three years.
(As amended by

##### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

Ficha articulo

##### Artículo 37- Fraud by simulation

regarding property susceptible to being community property. The person who
simulates the execution of an act, contract, proceeding, legal or judicial
writing, regarding property susceptible to being community property, to the
detriment of the rights of a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a
relationship or partnership bond, be it matrimonial, de facto union or
cohabitation, even when divorce, separation or breakup has occurred, provided
that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a
greater penalty, shall be imposed a penalty of imprisonment of two months to
three years, if the amount defrauded does not exceed ten times the base salary
and, with imprisonment of six months to ten years, if the amount defrauded
exceeds ten times the base salary.
(As amended by

##### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

Ficha articulo

##### Artículo 38- Diversion of the

profits from family economic activities. The person who unilaterally
misappropriates the profits derived from a family economic activity or disposes
of them for his exclusive personal benefit and to the detriment of the rights of
a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or partnership
bond, be it matrimonial, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation,
non-cohabitation, casual or other analogous relationship, even when divorce,
separation or breakup has occurred, provided that the conduct does not
constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be
punished with imprisonment of six months to one year.
(As amended by

##### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

Ficha articulo

##### Artículo 39- Economic

exploitation of the woman. The person who, through the use of force,
intimidation or coercion, causes himself to be maintained, totally or partially,
by a woman with whom he maintains or has maintained a relationship or
partnership bond, be it matrimonial, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation,
non-cohabitation, casual or other analogous relationship, even when divorce,
separation or breakup has occurred, provided that the conduct does not
constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be
punished with imprisonment of six months to three years.
(As amended by

##### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

Ficha articulo

##### ARTÍCULO 40.- Penalty of

disqualification
The perpetrator of the crimes contemplated
in this chapter shall be imposed, in addition, the penalty of disqualification
from one to six years.
Ficha articulo

#### CAPÍTULO V

INCUMPLIMIENTO DE DEBERES

##### ARTÍCULO 41.- Obstruction of access to justice

The person who, in the exercise of
a public function promotes, by unlawful means, impunity or obstructs the police,
judicial or administrative investigation for actions of physical, sexual,
psychological or patrimonial violence, committed to the detriment of a woman,
shall be punished with imprisonment of three months to three years and
disqualification for a period of one to four years from the exercise of public
function.
Ficha articulo

##### ARTICLE 42.- Aggravated breach

of duties
The penalty of disqualification for the
crime of breach of duties shall be two to six years, if the
breach occurs in a situation of risk to the personal integrity
or economic necessity of the female victim.
Article file

#### CHAPTER VI

BREACH OF A
PROTECTIVE
MEASURE

##### ARTICLE 43.- Breach of

a protective measure
Anyone who breaches a protective measure issued by
a competent authority, within a domestic violence proceeding in
application of the Law against domestic violence, shall be punished with a prison sentence
of six months to two years.
Article file

#### CHAPTER VII

FINAL PROVISIONS

##### ARTICLE 44.- Application of the

general part of the Criminal Code
For the purposes of this Law,
the provisions of the general part of the Criminal Code shall be applied, in accordance
with the purposes set forth in article 1 of this Law.
Article file

##### ARTICLE 45.- Addition to the

Code of Criminal Procedure
Subsection d) is added
to article 239 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, the text of which shall read:
"

##### Article 239

.- Grounds for pretrial detention
[...]
d) There exists danger to the victim, the reporting person or the witness.
When the victim is in a situation of risk, the judge shall take into account
the need to order this measure, especially in the context of the
investigation of crimes attributable to a person with whom the victim
maintains or has maintained a relationship of marriage, in a declared or undeclared
de facto union."
Article file

##### ARTICLE 46.- Amendment of the

Law against domestic violence
The final paragraph of article 3 of the Law
against domestic violence is amended. The text shall read:
"Article 3
.- Protective
measures
[...]
If one or more of these measures are breached in contravention of an
order issued by the competent judicial authority, the latter must certify
copies to the corresponding prosecutor's office, so that the investigation for
the crime of breach of a protective measure may be initiated."
