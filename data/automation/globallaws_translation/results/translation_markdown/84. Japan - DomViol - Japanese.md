## Chapter VI Penal Provisions (Articles 29 and 30)

● Supplementary Provisions

In our country, the Constitution of Japan proclaims respect for the individual and equality under the law, and efforts have been made toward the protection of human rights and the realization of gender equality.

However, despite the fact that spousal violence is a serious human rights violation that includes acts constituting crimes, relief for victims has not necessarily been sufficiently provided. Moreover, victims of spousal violence are in many cases women, and the fact that spouses inflict violence on women who have difficulty achieving economic independence violates individual dignity and hinders the realization of gender equality.

In order to improve such circumstances and achieve the protection of human rights and the realization of gender equality, it is necessary to implement measures to prevent spousal violence and protect victims. This is also in line with efforts in the international community that are striving to eradicate violence against women.

Here, by establishing systems for reporting, counseling, protection, independence support, etc. related to spousal violence, this Act is enacted for the purpose of preventing spousal violence and protecting victims.

## Chapter I General Provisions

(Definitions)

### Article 1

In this Act, "spousal violence" means physical violence from a spouse (meaning unlawful attacks on the body that endanger life or body; the same shall apply hereinafter) or verbal or other conduct that has harmful effects on the mind and body equivalent thereto (hereinafter in this paragraph and

### Article 28

-2, collectively referred to as "physical violence, etc."), and in cases where a person has divorced or had their marriage annulled after receiving physical violence, etc. from a spouse, includes physical violence, etc. continuously received from the person who was the spouse.

2

In this Act, "victim" means a person who has received spousal violence.

3

"Spouse" in this Act includes persons who have not filed a marriage notification but are in circumstances similar to a marital relationship in fact, and "divorce" includes cases where persons who were in circumstances similar to a marital relationship in fact without filing a marriage notification enter into circumstances similar to divorce in fact.

(Responsibilities of the National and Local Governments)

### Article 2 The national and local governments have the responsibility to prevent spousal violence and to provide appropriate protection, including supporting the independence of victims.

## Chapter I-2 Basic Policy and Prefectural Basic Plans, etc.

(Basic Policy)

### Article 2

-2 The Prime Minister, the National Public Safety Commission, the Minister of Justice, and the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare (hereinafter in this Article and paragraph 5 of the next Article referred to as "competent ministers") shall establish a basic policy concerning measures for the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims (hereinafter in this Article and paragraphs 1 and 3 of the next Article referred to as "Basic Policy").

2

In the Basic Policy, matters to be specified in the following items shall be established as guidelines for the prefectural basic plans under paragraph 1 of the next Article and the municipal basic plans under paragraph 3 of the same Article.

Basic matters concerning the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims

Matters concerning the content of measures for the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims

Other important matters concerning the implementation of measures for the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims

3

When the competent ministers intend to establish or revise the Basic Policy, they must consult in advance with the heads of relevant administrative organs.

4

When the competent ministers have established or revised the Basic Policy, they must make it public without delay.

(Prefectural Basic Plans, etc.)

### Article 2

-3 Prefectures shall, in accordance with the Basic Policy, establish a basic plan concerning the implementation of measures for the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims in the prefecture (hereinafter in this Article referred to as "prefectural basic plan").

2

In the prefectural basic plan, the following matters shall be specified:

Basic policy concerning the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims

Matters concerning the implementation content of measures for the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims

Other important matters concerning the implementation of measures for the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims

Municipalities (including special wards; the same shall apply hereinafter) shall, in accordance with the Basic Policy and taking into consideration the prefectural basic plan, endeavor to establish a basic plan concerning the implementation of measures for the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims in the municipality (hereinafter in this Article referred to as "municipal basic plan").

When a prefecture or municipality has established or revised a prefectural basic plan or municipal basic plan, it must make it public without delay.

The competent ministers shall endeavor to provide necessary advice and other assistance for the preparation of prefectural basic plans or municipal basic plans to prefectures or municipalities.

## Chapter II Spousal Violence Counseling and Support Centers, etc.

(Spousal Violence Counseling and Support Centers)

### Article 3

Prefectures shall ensure that women's consulting offices and other appropriate facilities established by the prefecture fulfill the functions of spousal violence counseling and support centers.

Municipalities shall endeavor to ensure that appropriate facilities established by the municipality fulfill the functions of spousal violence counseling and support centers.

Spousal violence counseling and support centers shall perform the following services for the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims:

Responding to consultations concerning various problems related to victims or introducing women's counselors or consultation institutions;

Providing medical or psychological guidance and other necessary guidance to restore the physical and mental health of victims;

Ensuring the safety of victims in emergencies and providing temporary protection (where victims are accompanied by their families, for victims and their accompanying families; the same shall apply in the next item, item 6,

### Article 5, Article 8-3 and Article 9);

四

Providing information, advice, coordination with relevant institutions, and other assistance concerning the promotion of employment, securing of housing, use of support systems, etc. in order to promote the independent living of victims;

五

## Providing information, advice, contact with relevant institutions, and other assistance concerning the use of the protection order system specified in Chapter IV;

六

Providing information, advice, coordination with relevant institutions, and other assistance concerning the use of facilities that house and protect victims.

4

The temporary protection under item 3 of the preceding paragraph shall be conducted by women's consulting offices themselves or by delegation to persons who meet the standards established by the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare.

5

In performing their services, spousal violence counseling and support centers shall endeavor to cooperate with private organizations that conduct activities for the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims, as necessary.

(Consultation, etc. by Women's Counselors)

### Article 4 Women's counselors may respond to consultations from victims and provide necessary guidance.

(Protection at Women's Protection Facilities)

### Article 5 Prefectures may provide protection for victims at women's protection facilities.

## Chapter III Protection of Victims

(Reporting, etc. by Persons Who Discover Spousal Violence)

### Article 6

Persons who discover someone receiving spousal violence (limited to physical violence from a spouse or person who was a spouse; the same shall apply hereinafter in this Chapter) shall endeavor to report that fact to a spousal violence counseling and support center or police officer.

2

When physicians and other medical personnel discover, in the course of their duties, a person who appears to have been injured or contracted an illness due to spousal violence, they may report that fact to a spousal violence counseling and support center or police officer. In this case, they shall endeavor to respect the person's intentions.

The provisions of the Penal Code (Act No. 45 of 1907) concerning the crime of divulging secrets and other provisions of laws concerning confidentiality obligations shall not be construed as preventing reporting under the preceding two paragraphs.

When physicians and other medical personnel discover, in the course of their duties, a person who appears to have been injured or contracted an illness due to spousal violence, they shall endeavor to provide that person with information they possess concerning the use of spousal violence counseling and support centers, etc.

(Explanation, etc. Concerning Protection by Spousal Violence Counseling and Support Centers)

### Article 7

When a spousal violence counseling and support center receives a report or consultation concerning a victim, it shall, as necessary, explain and advise the victim concerning the content of services performed by spousal violence counseling and support centers pursuant to the provisions of Article 3, paragraph 3, and recommend that the victim receive necessary protection.

(Prevention of Harm by Police Officers)

### Article 8

When police officers recognize through reports, etc. that spousal violence is being committed, they shall endeavor to take necessary measures to stop the violence, protect victims, and prevent the occurrence of harm from spousal violence, in accordance with the provisions of the Police Act (Act No. 162 of 1954), the Police Duties Execution Act (Act No. 136 of 1948), and other laws and regulations.

(Assistance by Superintendents General of Metropolitan/Prefectural Police Headquarters, etc.)

### Article 8

-2 When the Superintendent General of the Tokyo Metropolitan Police or the superintendent general of a prefectural police headquarters (for areas other than the area including the location of the prefectural police headquarters, the area headquarters chief;

### Article 15

paragraph 3, the same shall apply) or a police station chief receives a request from a person receiving spousal violence for assistance to prevent harm from spousal violence by themselves, and finds the request reasonable, they shall provide that person receiving spousal violence with instruction on measures to prevent such harm by themselves and other necessary assistance to prevent the occurrence of harm from spousal violence, in accordance with the provisions of the Rules of the National Public Safety Commission.

(Independence Support by Welfare Offices)

### Article 8

-3 Welfare offices as defined in the Social Welfare Act (Act No. 45 of 1951) (referred to as "welfare offices" in the next Article) shall endeavor to take necessary measures to support the independence of victims in accordance with the provisions of the Public Assistance Act (Act No. 144 of 1950), the Child Welfare Act (Act No. 164 of 1947), the Act on Welfare of Mothers and Fathers and Widows (Act No. 129 of 1964), and other laws and regulations.

(Cooperation and Coordination Among Relevant Institutions for the Protection of Victims)

### Article 9

Spousal violence counseling and support centers, prefectural police, welfare offices, child guidance centers, and other relevant institutions of prefectures or municipalities and other relevant institutions shall endeavor to cooperate with each other while coordinating with one another so that appropriate protection is provided when protecting victims.

(Appropriate and Prompt Handling of Complaints)

### Article 9

-2 When the relevant institutions under the preceding Article receive a complaint from a victim concerning the execution of duties by personnel involved in the protection of victims, they shall endeavor to handle it appropriately and promptly.

## Chapter V Miscellaneous Provisions

(Consideration by Persons Related to Duties, etc.)

### Article 23

Persons related to duties in the protection, investigation, trial, etc. of victims of spousal violence (referred to as "persons related to duties" in the following paragraph) shall, in performing their duties, respect the human rights of victims regardless of their nationality, presence or absence of disabilities, etc., and give full consideration to ensuring their safety and maintaining confidentiality, taking into account the physical and mental condition of the victims and the environment in which they are placed.

2

The national and local governments shall provide necessary training and awareness-raising for persons related to duties in order to deepen their understanding of the human rights of victims, the characteristics of spousal violence, etc.

(Education and Awareness-Raising)

### Article 24 The national and local governments shall endeavor to provide education and awareness-raising to deepen the public's understanding of the prevention of spousal violence.

(Promotion of Research, etc.)

### Article 25

The national and local governments shall endeavor to promote research on methods of guidance for the rehabilitation of perpetrators, methods of restoring the physical and mental health of victims, etc., and to train personnel involved in the protection of victims and improve their qualifications, in order to contribute to the prevention of spousal violence and the protection of victims.

(Assistance to Private Organizations)

### Article 26 The national and local governments shall endeavor to provide necessary assistance to private organizations that engage in activities to prevent spousal violence and protect victims.
