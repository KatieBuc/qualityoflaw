SOLE CHAPTER
RULES FOR ITS APPLICATION

###### Art. 1.- (As to space)

This Code shall apply:
1)
To crimes committed in the territory of Bolivia or in places subject to its jurisdiction.
2)
To crimes committed abroad, whose results have occurred or should have occurred in the territory of Bolivia or in places subject to its jurisdiction.
3)
To crimes committed abroad by a Bolivian, provided that he is found in national territory and has not been punished in the place where he committed the crime.
4)
To crimes committed abroad against the security of the State, public faith and the national economy. This provision shall be extended to foreigners, if they are obtained by extradition or are found within the territory of the Republic.
5)
To crimes committed in Bolivian vessels, aircraft or other means of transport in a foreign country, when they are not tried therein.
6)
To crimes committed abroad by officials in the service of the Nation, in the performance of their office or commission.
7)
To crimes that by treaty or convention the Republic has undertaken to suppress, even when they were not committed in its territory.

###### Art. 2.- (Foreign sentence)

In the cases provided for in the preceding article, when the agent is tried in Bolivia, having already been sentenced abroad, the part of the sentence served there shall be computed if it is of the same kind and if it is of a different kind, the judge shall in any case reduce the sentence imposed on the perpetrator.

###### Art. 3.- (Extradition)

No person subject to the jurisdiction of Bolivian laws may be surrendered by extradition to another State, unless an international treaty or reciprocity agreement provides otherwise.
The granting or denial of extradition shall be decided by the Supreme Court.
In case of reciprocity, extradition may not be carried out if the act for which it is requested does not constitute a crime according to the law of the State requesting extradition and of the State that must grant it.

###### Art. 4.- (AS TO TIME)

No one may be convicted or subjected to a security measure for an act that is not expressly provided as a crime by penal law in force at the time it was committed, nor subjected to penalties or penal security measures that are not established therein.
If the law in force at the time the crime was committed is different from that existing when the judgment is rendered or from that in force in the intermediate time, the most favorable one shall always be applied.
If during the serving of the sentence a more lenient law is enacted, it shall be the one applied.
Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraphs, laws enacted to govern only for a determined time shall apply to all acts committed during their validity.

Penal law does not recognize any privilege or personal immunity, but its provisions shall apply to persons who at the time of the act were over fourteen (14) years of age. THE CRIME GROUNDS FOR PUNISHABILITY AND THE OFFENDER

##### CHAPTER I

FORMS OF APPEARANCE OF
CRIME

###### Art. 8.- (Attempt)

He who by suitable or unequivocal acts begins the execution of the crime and does not consummate it due to causes beyond his will, shall be punished with two-thirds of the penalty established for the consummated crime.

###### Art. 9.- (Desistance and effective repentance)

Shall not be punished with any penalty.
1)
He who voluntarily desists from the commission of the crime.
2)
He who in the same manner prevents or contributes to preventing the result from occurring, unless the acts performed constitute crimes in themselves.

###### Art. 10.- (Impossible crime)

If the result does not occur because the means employed are not suitable or because of impropriety of the object, the judge may only impose security measures.

###### Art. 12º.- (STATE OF NECESSITY)

The person who, in order to avoid harm to their own or another's legal interest, which cannot be overcome in any other way, commits a criminal offense, is exempt from liability when the following requirements are met:
1)
That the harm caused is not greater than the harm sought to be avoided, taking into account, primarily, the equivalence in the quality of the legal interests involved;
2)
That the harm to be avoided is imminent or current, and important;
3)
That the situation of necessity has not been intentionally provoked by the subject; and,
4)
That the person in need does not have, by virtue of their profession or position, the obligation to face the danger.

###### Art. 15º.- (NEGLIGENCE)

A person acts negligently when they fail to exercise the care to which they are obligated according to the circumstances and their personal conditions and, consequently:
1)
They do not become aware that they are committing the legal offense.
2)
They consider the commission of the criminal offense possible and, notwithstanding this foresight, commit it in the confidence that they will avoid the result.

###### Art. 16º.- (ERROR)

1)
(ERROR OF FACT).- Unavoidable error regarding a constitutive element of the criminal offense excludes criminal liability for this offense. If the error, considering the circumstances of the act and the personal characteristics of the perpetrator, were avoidable, the infraction shall be punished with the penalty for the negligent offense, when the law threatens it with punishment.
Error regarding a fact that qualifies the infraction or regarding an aggravating circumstance shall prevent the application of the aggravated penalty.
An offense committed by avoidable error regarding circumstances that would have justified or exculpated the act shall be punished as a negligent offense when the law threatens it with punishment.
2)
(ERROR OF PROHIBITION).- Unavoidable error regarding the unlawfulness of the act constituting the criminal offense excludes criminal liability. If the error were avoidable, the penalty may be mitigated in accordance with article 39.

###### Art. 20º.- (AUTHORS)

Authors are those who commit the act by themselves, jointly, through another, or those who intentionally provide cooperation of such a nature that without it the intentional unlawful act could not have been committed.
An indirect author is one who intentionally uses another as an instrument for the commission of the crime.

###### Art. 24º.- (NON-COMMUNICATION)

Each participant shall be punished according to his culpability, without taking into account the culpability of the others.

###### Art. 28º.- (WORK SERVICE)

The penalty of work service for the benefit of the community obliges the convicted person to provide his work in activities of public utility that do not violate his dignity and are in accordance with his capacity.
The work service shall not interfere with the normal work activity of the convicted person, shall be performed in public establishments and in associations of general interest during the hours determined by the judge. It shall have a maximum duration of forty-eight weeks, and weekly may not exceed sixteen hours, nor be less than three hours.
The work service may only be executed with the consent of the convicted person. In the event that the convicted person does not give his consent, the sanction shall be converted into a penalty of deprivation of liberty. For this purpose, one day of deprivation of liberty equals two weekly hours of work. This substitution shall be carried out only once, and once carried out may not cease to be executed.
The supervising judge must request reports on work performance from the employing entity. In the event that the reports are not favorable, it shall be converted into deprivation of liberty in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

###### Art. 29º.- (DAY-FINES)

The fine consists of payment to the Reparations Fund of an amount of money that shall be fixed by the judge in day-fines, based on the economic capacity of the convicted person, his daily income, his aptitude for work and his family burdens, considered at the time the sentence is issued. The minimum shall be one day-fine and the maximum five hundred.
The installments that the convicted person must pay shall not exceed the maximum attachable amount of his salary, if this were his only source of income. The maximum total amount of the day-fine may not exceed twenty-five monthly national minimum wages.
If the convicted person does not provide sufficient information about his income, assets or other bases for calculating a daily installment, then it may be assessed by estimation.
The resolution shall indicate the number of day-fines, amount of the daily installment and the payment term.

###### Art. 30º.- (CONVERSION)

When a fine penalty of days-fine and a custodial sentence are imposed jointly, conversion of the days-fine into deprivation of liberty does not proceed. In other cases, conversion shall proceed when the solvent convicted person does not pay the fine.
Before conversion, the judge may authorize the convicted person to amortize the pecuniary penalty through provision of work. The judge may also authorize payment of the fine in installments, setting the amount and date of payments according to their economic condition, or arrange for satisfaction of the fine by making it effective against the property, salaries or other income of the convicted person.
Payment of the fine at any time renders the conversion without effect, deducting the time of confinement that the convicted person may have served, in the established proportion.
For purposes of conversion and amortization, one day of confinement is equivalent to three days-fine and one day of work of four hours is equivalent to one day-fine.

###### Art. 36º.- (APPLICATION OF SPECIAL DISQUALIFICATION)

Special disqualification of six months to ten years shall be imposed, after completion of the principal penalty, when the crime committed involves violation or disregard of the rights and duties corresponding to the mandate, position, employment or commission, incompetence or abuse of the professions or activities referred to in article 34 and involves crimes committed:
1)         By public officials, mandataries, commissioners, in the exercise of their functions;
2)         By doctors, lawyers, engineers, financial auditors and other professionals in the exercise of their professions; or,
3)         By those who perform industrial, commercial or other activity.
In the preceding cases, special disqualification is inherent to the time of completion of the custodial sentence.
The minimum penalty of special disqualification shall be five years, in the following cases:
1)         If the death of one or more persons occurs as a consequence of a serious culpable violation of the duty of care.
2)         If the crime was committed by a public official in the exercise of their functions.

##### CAPITULO II

APLICACION DE LAS PENAS

###### Art. 37º.- (Fijación de la pena)

It is the responsibility of the judge, taking into account the personality of the perpetrator, the greater or lesser seriousness of the act, the circumstances and the consequences of the crime:
1)
To obtain direct knowledge of the subject, the victim and the circumstances of the act, to the extent required for each case.
2)
To determine the penalty applicable to each crime, within the legal limits.

###### Art. 38º. (Circunstancias)

1)
To assess the personality of the perpetrator, the following shall be taken into account primarily:
a)      The age, education, customs and prior and subsequent conduct of the subject, the motives that impelled them to commit the crime and their economic and social situation.
b)      The special conditions in which they found themselves at the time of execution of the crime and other personal background and conditions, as well as their ties of kinship, friendship or arising from other relationships, the quality of the offended persons and other circumstances of a subjective nature.
Premeditation, base or antisocial motive, treachery and cruelty shall also be taken into account.
2)
To assess the seriousness of the act, the following shall be taken into account: nature of the action, the means employed, the extent of the damage caused and the danger incurred.

###### Art. 39º.- (SPECIAL MITIGATING CIRCUMSTANCES)

In cases where this code expressly provides for special mitigation, the following procedure shall apply:
1)      The penalty of imprisonment of thirty years shall be reduced to fifteen.
2)      When the crime is punishable by a penalty of imprisonment with a minimum exceeding one year, the imposed penalty may be mitigated to the legal minimum of the penal scale for imprisonment.
3)      When the crime is punishable by a penalty of imprisonment whose minimum is one year or a penalty of confinement with a minimum exceeding one month, the imposed penalty may be mitigated to the legal minimum of the penal scale for confinement.

###### Art. 40º.- (Atenuantes generales)

The penalty may also be mitigated:
1)
When the perpetrator has acted for an honorable motive, or impelled by poverty, or under the influence of serious and unjust moral suffering, or under the impression of a serious threat, or by the ascendancy of a person to whom they owe obedience or on whom they depend.
2)
When they have distinguished themselves in their previous life by particularly meritorious behavior.
3)
When they have demonstrated their repentance through acts and especially by repairing the damages, to the extent that it has been possible for them.
4)
When the agent is an indigenous person lacking instruction and their ignorance of the law can be proven.

##### CAPITULO VI

COMMON PROVISIONS

###### Art. 70º.- ("Nulla poena sine juditio")

No one shall be condemned to any sanction without having been heard and tried in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure.
No sanction may be executed except by virtue of a judgment issued by competent judicial authority and in compliance with a law, nor executed in a manner different from that established therein.

###### Art. 71º.- (Confiscation)

The commission of a crime entails the forfeiture of the instruments with which it was executed and of the effects derived from it, which shall be confiscated, unless they belonged to a non-responsible third party, who may recover them.
Confiscated instruments may be sold at public auction if they are of lawful commerce, to cover civil liability in cases of insolvency; if they are not, they shall be destroyed or rendered unusable.
They may also eventually pass to the property of the State.

###### Art. 73º.- (Computation of preventive detention)

###### Art. 87.- (Civil liability)

Every person criminally liable is also civilly liable and is obligated to repair the material and moral damages caused by the crime.

###### Art. 88.- (Preference)

Civil liability shall take precedence over payment of the fine and any other obligation that the responsible person may have contracted after the crime was committed.

###### Art. 89.- (Exemption from liability)

Only those who are protected by a justification defense are exempt from civil liability, except the person who caused the state of necessity.
In cases where the person who caused it is not determined, the persons in whose favor the harm was prevented shall be obligated to civil liability, in proportion to the benefit obtained by each of them, and subsidiarily, the State.

###### Art. 90.- (Legal mortgage, seizure and retention)

From the moment of the commission of a crime, the immovable property of those responsible shall be deemed specially mortgaged for civil liability.
The judge may also order the seizure of movable property, and retention as appropriate.

###### Art. 91.- (Scope)

Civil liability includes:
1)
The restitution of the property of the injured party, which shall be delivered even if held by a third party possessor.
2)
The reparation of the damage caused.
3)
The indemnification of all harm caused to the victim, their family or a third party, the amount to be fixed prudentially by the judge, in the absence of full proof. Every indemnification shall always include the expenses incurred by the victim for their cure, recovery and re-education.

###### Art. 92.- (Joint liability and transmissibility of obligations)

Civil liability shall be joint among all those responsible for the crime.
This obligation passes to the heirs of the responsible party and the right to demand it is transmitted to the heirs of the victim.

###### Art. 93.- (Participation in the proceeds of the crime)

Whoever participates for profit in the proceeds of a crime shall be obligated to make restitution, up to the amount by which they benefited.
If the responsible party or participants acted as agents of someone or as representatives or members of a legal entity and the proceeds or profit of the crime benefited the principal or represented party, they shall be equally obligated to make restitution in the same proportion.

###### Art. 110.- (TOTAL OR PARTIAL SUBMISSION OF THE NATION TO FOREIGN DOMINATION)

Whoever carries out the acts provided for in the preceding article, tending to submit the Nation totally or partially to foreign domination or to undermine its independence or integrity, shall be punished with thirty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 111.- (Espionage)

Whoever procures secret documents, objects or information of a political or military nature relating to security, means of defense or foreign relations, for purposes of espionage in favor of other countries in time of peace, that endanger the security of the State, shall incur the penalty of thirty years of imprisonment without right to pardon.

###### Art. 112.- (Clandestine introduction and possession of means of espionage)

Whoever in time of war introduces himself clandestinely, with deceit or violence, into a military place or zone or is caught in such places or in their vicinity in unjustified possession of means of espionage, shall incur deprivation of liberty of five to ten years.

###### Art. 113.- (Crimes committed by foreigners)

Foreigners residing in Bolivian territory are included in the preceding articles and shall be imposed the sanctions indicated therein, except as established by treaties or by the law of nations concerning diplomatic officials.

###### Art. 114.- (Hostile acts)

Whoever without the knowledge or influence of the Government commits hostilities against any foreign power and thereby exposes the State to the serious danger of a declaration of war or to vexations or reprisals being made against its nationals abroad or to the rupture of diplomatic relations, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty of two to four years.
If war results from such hostilities, the penalty shall be ten years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 115.- (Revelation of secrets)

Whoever reveals secrets of a political or military nature concerning the security of the State, the means of defense or foreign relations, shall incur deprivation of liberty of one to six years.
The sanction shall be increased by one third, if the agent perpetrates this crime by abusing the function, employment or commission conferred by public authority.

###### Art. 116.- (Crime by fault)

If the revelation of the secrets mentioned in the preceding article is committed by fault of the person who is in possession by virtue of his employment or office, the sanction shall be confinement of six months to two years.

###### Art. 117.- (Infidelity in State business)

The representative or commissioner of the Government of Bolivia to negotiate a treaty, agreement or convention with another State, who departs from his instructions in a manner that may produce harm to the national interest, shall incur imprisonment of two to six years.
The sanction shall be increased by one half, if the crime is perpetrated for purposes of profit or in time of war.

###### Art. 118.- (Sabotage)

Whoever in time of war destroys or renders useless installations, roads, works or other means of defense, communication, transport, supply, etc., with the purpose of harming the capacity or war effort of the Nation, shall be punished with thirty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 119.- (Breach of contracts of military interest)

Whoever in time of war does not duly fulfill contractual obligations relating to the needs of the armed forces or national defense, shall incur imprisonment of two to six years.

###### Art. 120.- (Crimes against an allied State)

The provisions established in the preceding articles shall also apply when the acts provided for therein are committed against a power allied with Bolivia, at war against a common enemy.

###### Art. 121.- (Armed uprisings against the security and sovereignty of the State)

Those who rise up in arms with the purpose of changing the Political Constitution or the form of government established therein, deposing any of the public powers of the national government or preventing, even temporarily, the free exercise of their constitutional powers or their renewal within their legal terms, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty of five to fifteen years.
Those who organize or integrate irregular armed groups, urban or rural, under internal or external influence, to promote armed confrontations with regular or public security forces, or to commit attacks against the life and security of persons, the territorial integrity or the sovereignty of the State, shall be punished with the penalty of fifteen to thirty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 122.- (Granting of extraordinary powers)

Members of Congress or those who in a popular assembly grant the Executive Power extraordinary powers, the sum of Public Power or supremacies by which the life, property and honor of Bolivians are left at the mercy of the Government or any person, shall incur deprivation of liberty of two to six years.

###### Art. 123.- (Sedition)

Those who without disavowing the authority of the legally constituted Government, rise up publicly and in open hostility, to depose any public official or employee, prevent his taking office or oppose the enforcement of laws, decrees or judicial or administrative resolutions, exercise any act of hatred or revenge against the person or property of any authority or private individuals or disturb or disrupt public order in any other way, shall be punished with confinement of one to three years.
Public officials who have not resisted a rebellion or sedition by all means at their disposal shall incur confinement of one to two years.

###### Art. 124.- (Attributing to oneself the rights of the people)

With the same penalty shall be punished those who form part of an armed force or a gathering of persons that attribute to themselves the rights of the people and attempt to exercise such rights in their name.

###### Art. 125.- Common provisions for the crimes of rebellion and sedition)

In the event that rebels or seditious persons submit to the first requirement of public authority, without having caused any damage other than the momentary disturbance of order, only the promoters or directors shall be sanctioned, to whom half of the penalty prescribed for the crime shall be applied.

###### Art. 126.- (Conspiracy)

Whoever takes part in a conspiracy of three or more persons to commit the crimes of rebellion or sedition shall be sanctioned with the penalty for the crime that was to be perpetrated, reduced by one half.
Participants who voluntarily desist before the execution of the proposed act and those who spontaneously prevent the commission of the crime shall be exempt from penalty.

###### Art. 127.- (Seduction of troops)

Whoever seduces troops or usurps command of them or illegally retains a political or military command, to commit a rebellion or sedition, shall be sanctioned with half of the penalty corresponding to the crime that was to be perpetrated.

###### Art. 128.- (Attacks against the President and other State dignitaries)

Whoever commits an attack against the life or security of the President of the Republic, Vice President, Ministers of State and President of the National Congress, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of five to ten years of deprivation of liberty.
If as a consequence of the attack committed death occurs, the maximum penalty that would correspond shall be applied; if serious injuries result to the victim, the applicable sanction for the act shall be increased by one third.

###### Art. 129.- (Outrage to national symbols)

Whoever publicly outrages the flag, the coat of arms or the anthem of the Nation shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to two years.

###### Art. 166.- (False accusation and denunciation)

Whoever knowingly accuses or denounces as perpetrator or participant in a crime of public action a person who did not commit it, giving rise to the initiation of the corresponding criminal proceeding, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty from one to three years.
If as a consequence the conviction of the person denounced or accused ensues, the penalty shall be deprivation of liberty from two to six years.

###### Art. 167.- (Simulation of crime)

Whoever knowingly denounces or causes an authority to believe that a non-existent crime of public action has been committed that gives rise to the instruction of a proceeding to verify it, shall be sanctioned with provision of work from three months to one year.

###### Art. 168.- (Self-slander)

Whoever through declaration or confession made before the competent authority to conduct the first judicial police proceedings or to instruct the proceeding, falsely incriminates themselves for having committed a crime of public action or a crime of the same nature perpetrated by another, shall be sanctioned with provision of work from one month to one year.
If the act were executed in the interest of a close relative or person of intimate friendship, the perpetrator may be exempted from penalty.

###### Art. 169.- (False testimony)

The witness, expert, interpreter, translator or any other person who is questioned in a judicial or administrative proceeding, who affirms a falsehood or denies or conceals the truth, in whole or in part of what they know about the fact or what concerns it, shall incur imprisonment from one to fifteen months.
If the false testimony is committed in criminal trial, to the detriment of the accused, the penalty shall be deprivation of liberty from one to three years.
When false testimony is perpetrated through bribery, the preceding penalty shall be increased by one third.

###### Art. 170.- (Bribery)

Whoever offers or promises money or any other appreciable advantage to the persons referred to in the preceding article, with the purpose of obtaining false testimony, even if the offer or promise has not been accepted or, having been accepted, the falsity was not committed, shall incur imprisonment of one to two years and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 171.- (Concealment)

Whoever, after a crime has been committed, without prior promise, helps someone to elude the action of justice or omits to report the act being obligated to do so, shall incur imprisonment of six months to two years.

###### Art. 172.- (Receiving Stolen Goods)

Whoever, after a crime has been committed, helps someone to secure the benefit or result thereof or receives, conceals, sells or purchases knowingly the instruments that served to commit the crime or the things obtained by criminal means, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to two years.
Whoever conceals his or her ascendants, descendants or spouse shall be exempt from penalty.

##### CHAPTER I

###### Art. 186.- (Counterfeiting of currency)

Whoever counterfeits metallic currency or paper money of legal tender, national or foreign, by manufacturing, altering or clipping it, and whoever introduces, spends or puts it into circulation, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from two to eight years.
Art 187.- (Circulation of counterfeit currency received in good faith).
Whoever, having received counterfeit currency in good faith, puts it into circulation with knowledge of the falsity, shall be punished with a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 188.- (Equivalence of securities to currency)

For the purposes of criminal law, the following are equivalent to currency:
1)
Bank notes legally authorized.
2)
National debt bonds.
3)
Bearer titles, certificates and shares, legally issued by Banks, entities, companies or corporations authorized to do so.
4)
Checks.

###### Art. 189.- (Illegal issuance)

The person in charge of the issuance or manufacture of currency, who knowingly authorizes, issues or manufactures currency that does not comply with legal requirements, or puts into circulation currency that no longer has legal tender, shall incur imprisonment from one to five years.
The same penalty shall apply to whoever issues bearer titles, certificates or shares in an amount greater than that authorized.

##### CHAPTER II

COUNTERFEITING OF SEALS,
STAMPED PAPER, STAMPS,
MARKS AND COUNTERSIGNS

###### Art. 190.- (Counterfeiting of seals, stamped paper and stamps)

Whoever counterfeits official seals, stamped paper, official lottery tickets, postage stamps, any stamped effect or printed forms, the issuance of which is reserved to the authority, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from one to six years.
The same sanction shall be imposed on whoever knowingly introduces, spends or uses them.

###### Art. 191.- (Fraudulent printing of official seal)

Whoever fraudulently prints an authentic official seal shall be punished with imprisonment from one to three years.

###### Art. 192.- (Receipt in good faith)

Whoever, having received in good faith the securities and effects indicated in Art. 190, and knowing thereafter of their falsity, introduces or puts them into circulation, shall be punished with a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 193.- (Counterfeiting and improper application of marks and countersigns)

Whoever counterfeits marks, countersigns or signatures officially used to verify weights or measures, identify any object or certify its quality, quantity or content, and whoever applies them to objects other than those to which they should be applied, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to three years.
The same sanction shall be incurred by whoever performs the same acts affecting private factories or establishments.

###### Art. 194.- (Counterfeiting of tickets of public transport companies)

Whoever counterfeits or alters tickets of public or private transport companies shall be punished with imprisonment from one to six months or a fine of twenty to one hundred twenty days.
Whoever introduces, spends or puts them into circulation shall incur the same sanction.

###### Art. 195.- (Counterfeiting of admission tickets)

Whoever counterfeits or alters, for profit, admission tickets or tickets that allow access to a public spectacle, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to six months or a fine of twenty to one hundred twenty days.

###### Art. 196.- (Use of already used items)

Whoever, with the purpose of using or selling seals, stamps, marks, countersigns or other stamped effects, causes the sign indicating their use to disappear, shall be punished with a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 197.- (Tools for counterfeiting)

Whoever manufactures, introduces into the country, keeps in his possession or trades materials or instruments unequivocally intended to commit any of the counterfeitings legislated in the two preceding chapters, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years.

##### CHAPTER III

COUNTERFEITING OF
DOCUMENTS IN GENERAL

###### Art. 198.- (Material falsity)

Whoever forges in whole or in part a false public document or alters a true one, in such a way that harm may result, shall incur deprivation of liberty from one to six years.

###### Art. 199.- (Ideological falsity)

Whoever inserts or causes to be inserted in a true public instrument false declarations concerning a fact that the document must prove, in such a way that harm may result, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from one to six years.
In both falsities, if the perpetrator is a public official and commits them in the exercise of his functions, the sanction shall be deprivation of liberty from two to eight years.

###### Art. 200.- (Counterfeiting of private document)

Whoever materially or ideologically falsifies a private document shall incur deprivation of liberty from six months to two years, provided that its use may cause some harm.

###### Art. 201.- (Ideological falsity in medical certificate)

The physician who issues a false certificate, referring to the existence or non-existence of any disease or injury, shall be punished with imprisonment from one month to one year and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.
If the false certificate has the consequence that a healthy person is admitted to an asylum or health facility, he shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 202.- (Suppression or destruction of document)

Whoever suppresses, conceals or destroys, in whole or in part, a file or a document, in such a way that harm may result, shall incur the sanction of Art.

###### 200. Art. 203.- (Use of falsified instrument)

Whoever knowingly makes use of a false or adulterated document shall be sanctioned as if they were the author of the falsification.

Whoever when driving a vehicle, by failure to observe Traffic provisions or for any other cause originates or gives rise to a danger to common security, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to two years.

###### Art. 282.- (Defamation)

Whoever in a public, tendentious and repeated manner, reveals or divulges a fact, a quality or a conduct capable of affecting the reputation of an individual or collective person, shall incur provision of work from one month to one year or fine from twenty to two hundred forty days.

###### Art. 283.- (Slander)

Whoever by any means falsely imputes to another the commission of a crime, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from six months to two years and fine from one hundred to three hundred days.

###### Art. 284.- (Offense to the memory of the deceased)

Whoever offends the memory of a deceased person with defamatory expressions or with slanderous imputations, shall incur the same penalties of the two preceding articles.

###### Art. 285.- (Propagation of offenses)

Whoever propagates or reproduces by any means the acts referred to in articles 282, 283 and 284, shall be punished as author thereof.

###### Art. 286.- (Exception of truth)

The author of defamation and slander shall not be punishable if the imputations consist of true statements, but the accused may only prove the truth of the imputation:
1)
When it concerns offenses directed at a public official and with reference to their functions.
2)
When the complainant requests proof of the imputation, provided that such proof does not affect the rights or secrets of a third person.

###### Art. 287.- (Insult)

Whoever by any means and in a direct manner offends another in their dignity or decorum, shall incur community service of one month to one year and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.
If the act provided for in Art. 283 and the insult referred to in this article are committed by means of printed, typewritten or handwritten material, its author shall be considered guilty of defamatory libel and sanctioned with a fine of sixty to one hundred fifty days, without prejudice to the corresponding penalties.

###### Art. 288.- (Prohibition of proof)

Proof shall not be admitted except in the cases indicated in article 286.

###### Art. 289.- (Retraction)

The person accused of a crime against honor shall be exempt from penalty if they retract before or at the time of giving their statement.
A second retraction on the same fact shall not be admitted.

###### Art. 290.- (Reciprocal offenses)

If the offenses or imputations are reciprocal, the judge may, according to the circumstances, exempt both parties or one of them from penalty.

###### Art. 300.- (Violation of correspondence and private papers)

Whoever improperly opens a letter, a sealed document or a telegraphic, radiotelegraphic or telephone communication addressed to another person, or whoever, without opening the correspondence, by technical means learns of its content, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three months to one year or a fine of sixty to two hundred forty days.
With the same penalty shall be sanctioned whoever in the same manner seizes, conceals or destroys a letter, a document, a dispatch or other private paper, even if they are open, or whoever arbitrarily diverts from its destination correspondence that does not belong to them.
The maximum sanction shall be raised to two years when the author of such acts discloses the content of the correspondence and dispatches indicated.

###### Art. 301.- (Violation of secrets in correspondence not intended for publication)

Whoever records the words of another not intended for the public, without their consent, or whoever by technical procedures listens to private statements not directed to them, or whoever does the same with private papers or with epistolary or telegraphic correspondence even if they have been addressed to them, provided that the act may cause some harm, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty of three months to one year.

###### Art. 302.- (Disclosure of professional secret)

Whoever having knowledge of secrets by virtue of their status, ministry, profession, employment, occupation, art or commission, discloses them without just cause, or uses them for their own or another's benefit, if any harm follows from it, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty of three months to one year and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

On the occasion of a disaster or popular commotion.
3.
Taking advantage of an accident or a particular misfortune.

On movable property of the Bolivian Cultural Heritage.
5.
On things of artistic, historical, religious and scientific value.
6.
On things that are outside the control of the owner.
7.
On things of first necessity or destined for a public service, provided that the taking causes harm to it, or a situation of shortage.

###### Art. 360.- (Usury)

Whoever, taking advantage of the need, recklessness or inexperience of a person, gives in any form, for himself or for others, values or goods in exchange for interest higher than that fixed by law or other pecuniary advantages evidently disproportionate to the service, shall be sanctioned with confinement from three months to two years and a fine from thirty to one hundred days.
The same penalty shall apply to whoever knowingly acquires, transfers or enforces a usurious credit, or to the intermediary, front man or cooperator.

###### Art. 361.- (Aggravated usury)

The sanction shall be aggravated by one half and a fine of up to one hundred days:
1)
If the perpetrator is a professional or habitual usurious lender or commission agent.
2)
When any artifice or deceit has been employed to obtain the consent of the victim.
3)
If the act is concealed by means of other forms of contract, even as a penalty clause that fixes interest.
4)
If the act constitutes any of the forms of compound interest.

###### Article 363

quater. (FINANCIAL CRIMES).
A natural or legal person, through their legal representative, commits a financial crime when by action or omission they incur in any of the criminal typifications detailed below: :
a)
Financial Intermediation without Authorization or License.
Whoever, on their own behalf or on behalf of another, directly or indirectly, carries out financial intermediation activities without having prior authorization from the Financial System Supervisory Authority, shall incur deprivation of liberty from five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days.
b)
Improper Use of Influence for Granting of Credit.
The director or directors, member of the board of directors and supervisory board, executive or official of a financial intermediation entity, who with the intention of favoring themselves or the entity in some way or obtaining economic benefits for themselves or a third party, knowingly authorizes or approves the granting of credits to borrowers or groups of borrowers linked to the entity, shall incur deprivation of liberty from three (3) to eight (8) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to three hundred (300) days.

Misappropriation of Financial Funds.

d)
Forging of Illicit Financial Results.

e)
Falsification of Accounting Documentation.

f)
Dissemination of False Financial Information.
The individual person who by any means disseminates or commissions the dissemination of false information about the Bolivian financial system or its entities, which induces or provokes the massive withdrawal of deposits from one or more financial intermediation entities, incites or induces clients not to comply with acquired financial commitments, damaging or deteriorating the image and stability of a financial intermediation entity or the national financial system.
