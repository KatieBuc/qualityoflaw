# REPUBLIC OF LITHUANIA

LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF LITHUANIA
ON PROTECTION AGAINST DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

26 May 2011 No. XI-1425 Vilnius

FIRST SECTION GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Article. Purpose of the Law

1.	This Law aims to protect persons from domestic violence, which due to its harm to society is classified among acts of public significance, to respond quickly to emerging threats, to implement preventive measures, to apply protective measures and to provide appropriate assistance.
2.	Domestic violence is a violation of human rights and freedoms. This Law defines the concept of domestic violence, establishes the rights and responsibilities of subjects of domestic violence, the implementation of preventive measures, the provision of assistance in cases of domestic violence and the application of protective measures to persons who have experienced violence. The protection of children from violence is regulated by this Law and other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

## Article. Basic concepts of this Law

1.	Domestic environment – an environment consisting of persons linked or previously linked by marital, partnership, affinity or other close relationships, as well as persons living together and managing a common household.
2.	Non-governmental organisation – a public legal person independent of state and municipal institutions, operating on a voluntary basis for the benefit of society or its groups, whose purpose is not the pursuit of profit or political power or only the implementation of religious purposes.
3.	Assistance to persons who have experienced domestic violence (hereinafter – assistance) – psychologist, legal, social, health care, education, accommodation and other necessary services provided by state, municipal institutions and non-governmental organisations to persons who have experienced domestic violence.
4.	Person who has experienced domestic violence (hereinafter – person who has experienced violence) – a person against whom domestic violence has been used, as well as a child who has become a witness to domestic violence or is living in an environment where violence has occurred.
5.	Violence – intentional physical, psychological, sexual, economic or other impact on a person by action or inaction, as a result of which the person suffers physical, material or non-material harm.
6.	Perpetrator of violence – a person who commits violence in a domestic environment.
7.	Subject of domestic violence – a person who has experienced violence or a perpetrator of violence.
8.	Specialised comprehensive assistance to persons who have experienced violence (hereinafter – specialised comprehensive assistance) – comprehensive assistance provided by specialised assistance centres in cases of domestic violence to overcome a critical condition, specialised psychologist, legal and other assistance.
9.	Specialised assistance centre – an institution performing public functions, providing specialised comprehensive assistance to persons who have experienced violence.
10.	Other concepts of this Law are understood as they are defined in other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

## Article. Principles of protection against domestic violence

Protection against domestic violence is implemented in accordance with the following principles: cooperation, participation, comprehensiveness, accessibility and quality, solidarity,

appropriateness, coordination, legality, protection of personal rights and freedoms, proportionality, humanity, justice, effectiveness, objectivity and efficiency.

## Article. Prevention of domestic violence

1.	State and municipal institutions, establishments and non-governmental organisations, according to their competence, prepare and implement measures aimed at preventing domestic violence, implement the requirements of this and other legal acts.
2.	Preventive measures are implemented according to programmes prepared by the Government of the Republic of Lithuania (hereinafter – the Government) and its authorised institutions and municipalities, financed from state and municipal budgets and international programmes. The Government finances preventive activities carried out by non-governmental organisations.
3.	State and municipal institutions, establishments and non-governmental organisations, seeking to prevent domestic violence, according to their competence, take the following preventive measures:
1)	organise public education and information campaigns encouraging intolerance of
violence;
2)	organise training and qualification courses for judges, prosecutors, police officers, specialists and other persons working in the field of domestic violence prevention and assistance according to programmes prepared by the Government and its authorised institutions and municipalities, financed from state and municipal budgets and international programmes;
3)	organise public legal education about acts that are considered domestic violence, the legal consequences of these acts and the inevitability of liability for their commission;
4)	organise research, collection of statistical data on domestic violence and analysis of this data;
5)	prepare courses teaching peaceful ways of resolving conflicts arising in a domestic environment;
6)	do not issue a permit to possess (carry) a weapon or revoke existing permits;
7)	take other preventive measures.

SECOND SECTION
PROTECTION OF A PERSON WHO HAS EXPERIENCED VIOLENCE

## Article. Measures to ensure protection of a person who has experienced violence

1.	If a fact of domestic violence is established, the following measures to ensure protection of a person who has experienced violence are assigned:
1)	obligation for the perpetrator of violence to temporarily move out of the place of residence if he or she lives with the person who has experienced violence;
2)	obligation for the perpetrator of violence not to approach the person who has experienced violence, not to communicate, not to seek contact with him or her.
2.	The measures established in points 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of this Article are assigned until the end of the examination of the case, if the pre-trial investigation judge does not assign preventive measures established in the Code of Criminal Procedure of the Republic of Lithuania – detention or obligation to live separately from the victim. These measures are assigned by the pre-trial investigation judge by ruling not later than within 48 hours.
3.	The measures established in points 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of this Article are assigned together with the penalty, except in cases where the convicted person is assigned arrest or deprivation of liberty.
4.	The measures established in points 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of this Article are not assigned when
the perpetrator of violence is a minor.
5.	When assigning the measures established in points 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of this Article, if a person who has experienced violence or a perpetrator of violence cannot remain without supervision due to their physical or mental deficiencies or characteristics, the issue of supervision of these persons is resolved in the manner established by other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

## Article. Application of measures to ensure protection of a person who has experienced violence

1.	A police officer who has recorded an incident of domestic violence immediately takes measures to protect the person who has experienced violence and, taking into account the established circumstances, initiates a pre-trial investigation, and informs the prosecutor if a prosecutor's request is required to initiate a pre-trial investigation.
2.	An expedited process is applied to procedural actions when the circumstances of the domestic violence used are clear and the criminal case for the commission of that act must be examined in a district court.

## Article. Functions of police officers

1. Upon receiving a report of domestic violence, police officers who arrive at the scene of the incident or who themselves are witnesses to that incident shall record data about the fact of domestic violence and initiate a pre-trial investigation. The victim of violence does not file a complaint.
2. Police officers shall organise the provision of assistance in accordance with the procedure established in paragraphs 1 and 3 of Article 9 of this Law.
3. Where an obligation is imposed on the perpetrator of violence to temporarily move out of the place of residence, if he or she lives together with the victim of violence, police officers shall ensure without delay the moving out of the perpetrator of violence. The procedure for evicting the perpetrator of violence shall be established by an institution authorised by the Government.
4. Police officers shall notify the specialised assistance centre without delay, but no later than the next working day after the decision is taken, of a court-imposed obligation on the perpetrator of violence to temporarily move out of the place of residence. In cases where, upon imposing an obligation on the perpetrator of violence to move out, a minor is left without supervision, police officers shall also notify the child rights protection division thereof.
5. Police officers shall monitor how the court decision is being implemented. Monitoring shall be carried out in accordance with the procedure established by an institution authorised by the Government.

THIRD SECTION
ASSISTANCE MANAGEMENT AND ORGANISATION

## Article. Assistance management

1. Assistance management shall include the planning, administration, organisation, allocation of responsibilities and ensuring of funding for assistance measures.
2. The Government shall approve a long-term state assistance provision and funding programme prepared by an institution authorised by the Government, which shall be financed from state and municipal budget funds and international programmes.
3. Institutions authorised by the Government shall be responsible for the preparation of the programme of specialised assistance centres. Specialised assistance centres shall be established giving priority to non-governmental organisations.
4. Specialised assistance centres shall provide specialised comprehensive assistance.
5. Municipal institutions and non-governmental organisations shall provide and implement long-term assistance services in accordance with the procedure established by law.
6. The provision of assistance shall be supervised by the responsible institutions.

## Article. Organisation of assistance

1. Police officers who arrive at the scene of a domestic violence incident shall inform the victim of violence about the possibilities of receiving assistance and, with his or her knowledge, shall notify the specialised assistance centre of the incident.
2. The specialised assistance centre, upon receiving a report from police officers, shall contact the victim of violence without delay and offer him or her specialised comprehensive assistance.
3. Police officers shall notify the child rights protection division without delay, but no later than the next working day, if a minor has suffered domestic violence, has been a witness thereto, lives in an environment where violence has been committed, or if the person suspected of committing violence is a minor.

FOURTH SECTION
RIGHTS OF SUBJECTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## Article. Rights of the victim of violence

1. A victim of violence shall have the right to:
1) receive from a police officer and from a specialised assistance centre information about institutions providing assistance under this Law;
2) receive specialised comprehensive assistance provided for victims of violence;
3) apply for and receive assistance in accordance with the procedure established by this Law and other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.
2. The rights established in paragraph 1 of this Article shall also apply where police officers have not been contacted regarding domestic violence.

## Article. Rights of the perpetrator of violence

A suspected perpetrator of violence shall have the right to:
1) receive from a police officer information about institutions providing accommodation services where an obligation to move out of the place of residence is imposed;
2) provide explanations regarding domestic violence;
3) apply for and receive assistance in accordance with the procedure established by other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

FIFTH SECTION
LIABILITY FOR DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## Article. Liability for domestic violence

1. Violence shall entail criminal liability.
2. A perpetrator of violence who has caused damage to a person's health, property, environment, or who has caused non-pecuniary damage, shall be obliged to compensate it to the victim of violence in accordance with the procedure established by legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

## Article. Liability for false reporting

A person who has falsely reported domestic violence or has abused the rights of a victim of violence shall be liable in accordance with the procedure established by legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

SIXTH SECTION
FINAL PROVISIONS

## Article. Entry into force and application of the Law

1. This Law, with the exception of Article 15, shall enter into force 6 months after its publication in the publication "Valstybės žinios".
2. The provision of paragraph 4 of Article 8 of this Law shall apply after the Government or an institution authorised by it approves the programme of specialised assistance centres within the time limits established in paragraph 3 of Article 15 of this Law.

## Article. Proposals to the Government

1. The Government shall, within 3 months of the publication of this Law in the publication "Valstybės žinios", submit to the Seimas of the Republic of Lithuania draft amendments to laws related to the implementation of this Law.
2. The Government or an institution authorised by it shall adopt implementing legal acts of this Law before this Law enters into force.
3. The Government or an institution authorised by it shall, within 6 months of the publication of this Law in the publication "Valstybės žinios", approve the programme of specialised assistance centres.

I promulgate this Law adopted by the Seimas of the Republic of Lithuania.
