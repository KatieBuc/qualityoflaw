LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA ON PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, PROTECTION OF PERSONS SUBJECTED TO DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND RESTORATION OF SOLIDARITY IN THE FAMILY

LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA
Adopted on December 13, 2017
ON PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, PROTECTION OF PERSONS SUBJECTED TO DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND RESTORATION OF SOLIDARITY IN THE FAMILY
CHAPTER 1
GENERAL PROVISIONS
The National Assembly of the Republic of Armenia, having the objective to
ensure special protection of the family as the natural and fundamental cell of society, to establish the necessary legal mechanisms for the prevention of domestic violence, the safety and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, and the protection of their rights and legitimate interests,
ensure the safety of family members and promote the restoration of solidarity in the family, to regulate the activities of responsible bodies in the direction of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence,
ensure legal grounds for the activities of responsible bodies for the provision of psychological, material and social assistance to persons subjected to domestic violence and their social rehabilitation, adopts this law:

## Article 1. Subject of regulation of the Law

1. This Law regulates the organizational and legal foundations of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, defines the concept of domestic violence, the powers of competent bodies in the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, types of protection measures, grounds for their application, reconciliation of persons subjected to domestic violence and persons who committed domestic violence,

peculiarities of legal protection of information concerning persons subjected to domestic violence:

## Article 2

Principles of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence

1. State and local self-government bodies, their officials and public organizations in carrying out their activities in the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence are guided by:
1)	priority of ensuring the safety and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence,
2)	support of the family as the natural and fundamental cell of society, strengthening of traditional values in the family and restoration of solidarity in the family,
3)	priority of the best interests of the child,
4)	ensuring equality and prohibition of discrimination,
5)	accessibility of justice,
6)	inviolability of information concerning private and family life,
7)	principles of cooperation between state, local self-government bodies and civil society:

## Article 3. Domestic violence and its types

1.	Domestic violence is considered a violent act of physical, sexual, psychological or economic nature, as well as neglect, as defined by this Law, committed between family members:
2.	Types of domestic violence are:
1)	physical violence - beating and other violent actions provided for by the Criminal Code of the Republic of Armenia, intentional infliction of harm to health, unlawful deprivation of liberty, intentional infliction of severe physical pain;
2)	sexual violence - crimes against sexual integrity and sexual freedom provided for by the Criminal Code of the Republic of Armenia;
3)	psychological violence - intentional infliction of severe mental suffering, including real threat of committing physical, sexual or economic violence, intentional periodic commission of actions that cause reasonable fear in a person about danger threatening the personal safety of oneself or a family member, periodic humiliation of dignity, extreme social isolation, coercion to artificial termination of pregnancy;
4)	economic violence - depriving a person of means necessary for existence (food, clothing, housing, medicine) for the purpose of creating forced material dependence or domination over them, unlawfully restricting the rights to dispose of, possess and use property belonging by right of ownership or common ownership, restricting a person's rights to freedom of education or choice of employment;
5)	neglect - intentional failure by a parent or legal representative to provide minimum conditions necessary for a child's existence (food, clothing, housing, medical care and service, education), and by adult able-bodied children to provide minimum conditions necessary for the existence of their disabled and needy parents (food, clothing, housing, medical care and service), if the parent or legal representative or adult able-bodied children possess appropriate information and opportunities, and if the relevant services are available to them:

## Article 4. Basic concepts of the Law

1. The following basic concepts are used in this Law:
1)	family members:
a. regardless of the fact of cohabitation - spouse (including a person in de facto marital relations), former spouse, parent, including stepparent, adoptive parent, foster parent, child (also adopted, step, foster child), spouse of adoptive parent, spouse's parents,

former spouse's parents,
b. cohabiting grandmother, grandfather, sister and brother (also half-sister or half-brother), spouse's sister, spouse's brother, as well as for spouse's parents, sister and brother - son-in-law and daughter-in-law;
2)	support center - a state organization providing social services provided for by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Social Assistance";
3)	shelter (care center for persons subjected to domestic violence) - safe temporary housing provided to persons subjected to domestic violence and persons under their care in cases defined by this Law, where they receive social services provided for by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Social Assistance";
4)	person who committed domestic violence - a natural person against whom a warning, emergency intervention or protection decision has been issued in the manner prescribed by this Law;
5)	person subjected to domestic violence - a natural person who, according to a warning, emergency intervention, protection decision or a court verdict that has entered into legal force, has suffered from domestic violence;
6)	person under care - a child, elderly or disabled person who lives with a person subjected to domestic violence and whom the latter constantly cares for;
7)	authorized police officer - an officer of the police specialized subdivision in the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence;
8)	rehabilitation program - a program of comprehensive measures aimed at preventing repeated violence by a person who committed violence through the provision of psychological and social assistance, health restoration, and liberation from harmful habits (alcoholism, drug addiction, substance abuse, gambling addiction):
CHAPTER 2
MEASURES FOR PROTECTION OF PERSONS SUBJECTED TO DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## Article 5. Types of Protection Measures for Persons Subjected to Domestic Violence

1.	The protection measures for persons subjected to domestic violence are:
1)	warning;
2)	emergency intervention order;
3)	protection order.
2.	A reasoned decision is made regarding the application of protection measures. Protection measures are applied guided by the principles established by this Law and ensuring proportionality of intervention. Their application does not impede the initiation of criminal proceedings and the implementation of criminal prosecution in the manner established by law.

## Article 6. Warning

1.	A warning is applied in cases where the incident of domestic violence has been detected by the police for the first time, the committed act does not apparently contain the elements of a criminal offense, and there are no grounds for emergency intervention. The decision to apply a warning includes notification about the measures of liability provided by law in case of continuation or repetition of violence. The warning is applied within the shortest possible time after becoming aware of the incident.
2.	A copy of the reasoned decision made by the police officer to apply a warning is handed to the person who committed domestic violence against signature in person. In case of his or her absence at the scene of the incident, the content of the decision is clarified to him or her by telephone, and in case of impossibility thereof, a copy of the decision is sent to his or her place of registration by official electronic mail or registered mail.
3.	The decision to apply a warning may be appealed hierarchically within one month from the time of handing it to the person who committed domestic violence or clarifying its content to him or her by telephone, as well as in judicial proceedings within the time limits established by the Administrative Procedure Code of the Republic of Armenia.
4.	A copy of the reasoned decision to apply a warning is sent by the police officer who made it to the support center at the place of permanent residence of the person who committed domestic violence, which within the shortest possible time offers the person who committed domestic violence the services provided by the rehabilitation program for the purpose of preventing new manifestations of violence.

## Article 7. Decision on Urgent Intervention

1.	If one family member has committed violence against another, and there is reasonable suspicion of an immediately threatening danger of repetition or continuation of violence, then the authorized officer of the police shall immediately issue a decision on urgent intervention for the purpose of ensuring the life and health of the other family member. A decision on urgent intervention may also be issued in the case when, after receiving a warning, within one year, a person has committed a violent act that does not contain the elements of a crime.
2.	The term of validity of the decision on urgent intervention may not exceed twenty days. If during the validity of the urgent decision the court is examining an application for a protective order, then it remains in force until a decision is issued by the court.
3.	The following restrictions may be applied by the decision on urgent intervention:
1)	to compel the person who committed violence in the family to immediately leave the residence area of the victim of violence in the family and to prohibit his or her return there until the expiration of the term established by the decision;
2)	to prohibit the person who committed violence in the family from visiting the victim of violence in the family who does not reside in the jointly habitable area with him or her, and if necessary, also persons under his or her care, at their places of work, study, rest, residence or other places;
3)	to prohibit the person who committed violence in the family from approaching the victim of violence in the family (if necessary, also persons under his or her care) closer than such distance that would reasonably cause in the victim of violence in the family a justified fear of danger threatening his or her personal safety. When applying this restriction, the decision shall establish the specific measure of distance;
4)	to temporarily seize the weapon in the possession of the person who committed violence until the expiration of the term established by the decision. When applying this restriction, in case of the presence of a weapon in the possession of the person who committed violence in the family at the moment of notification about the decision, he or she shall immediately hand it over to the police officer who issued the decision;
5)	to prohibit the person who committed violence in the family from communicating by telephone, correspondence or other means of communication with the victim of violence in the family, and if necessary, also with persons under his or her care.
4.	The restrictions provided for by part 3 of this article may be applied separately or in combination, indicating the same or different terms for their implementation. When selecting the restriction measures, the decision-maker shall be guided by the principles established by this law, ensuring the proportionality of the restriction.
5.	If the person under immediately threatening danger is a minor or incapacitated person, then the authorized officer of the police who issued the decision shall immediately send a copy of that decision and the corresponding record to the guardianship and trusteeship body, which, assessing the situation, shall undertake actions provided for by law and its charter.
6.	If the decision on urgent intervention is applied against the sole legal representative residing jointly with a minor or incapacitated person, then the temporary care of children and incapacitated persons shall be organized by the guardianship and trusteeship body, after receiving a copy of the decision, within the shortest possible time, but not more than 24 hours, in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Armenia, being guided by their interests.
7.	The reasoned decision of the authorized officer of the police on applying urgent intervention shall enter into force from the moment of notifying the person who committed violence in the family about it. A copy thereof shall be handed to the person who committed violence in the family with signature, in person, and in case of his or her absence at the scene, the content of the decision shall be clarified to him or her by means of telephone communication, and in case of impossibility thereof, a copy of the decision shall be sent to his or her place of registration by official electronic mail or registered mail.
8.	When applying the restriction provided for by point 1 of part 3 of this article, the authorized officer of the police issuing the decision on urgent intervention shall not leave until the person who committed violence in the family collects his or her belongings and leaves the place of residence of the victim of violence in the family. The person who committed violence in the family may take his or her personal belongings from that place after the decision enters into force only once, accompanied by a police officer.
9.	The authorized officer of the police issuing the decision on urgent intervention shall immediately submit a copy of the decision and the corresponding record to the head of his or her subdivision.
10.	The victim of violence in the family shall be obliged to refrain from performing such actions that are aimed at the non-fulfillment by the person who committed violence in the family of the obligations imposed on him or her by the decision on urgent intervention. In case of deliberately performing such actions periodically by the victim of violence in the family, the decision on urgent intervention may be revoked by the authorized officer of the police who issued it. The decision on urgent intervention shall be revoked by the same person in cases of concluding reconciliation as a result of the reconciliation process provided for by this law in the family, as well as in cases of issuing a protective order by the court before the expiration of its term.
11.	Supervision over the implementation of the decision on urgent intervention by the person who committed violence in the family shall be carried out by the police.
12.	A copy of the decision on urgent intervention shall be sent by the authorized officer of the police who issued it to the support center of the place of permanent residence of the victim of violence in the family.
13.	The restriction provided for by point 3 of part 3 of this article shall not apply during a meeting organized within the framework of the reconciliation process provided for by this law between the victim of violence in the family and the person who committed violence in the family, with the permission of the support center, on the premises of the center or in the presence of the relevant specialist of the center.
14.	Non-fulfillment of the requirements of the decision on urgent intervention by the person who committed violence in the family shall give rise to liability provided for by law.
15.	The decision on urgent intervention shall indicate the term and body for appealing it, including the court to which the decision may be appealed, as well as the liability measures provided for by law for violating it. The decision on urgent intervention may be appealed through hierarchical procedure within a five-day period after notifying the person who committed violence in the family about it, as well as through judicial procedure within the terms established by the Administrative Procedure Code of the Republic of Armenia. Filing a complaint against the decision shall not suspend the execution of the decision. The administrative complaint shall be examined and a decision based on it shall be issued within a five-day period after receiving it.

## Article 8. Protection Order

1. A person subjected to or allegedly subjected to domestic violence or, with their consent, a support center may apply to the court for a protection order. If the person subjected to domestic violence is a minor or a person recognized as incapacitated or with limited capacity by judicial procedure, then close relatives, the legal representative, and the guardianship and trusteeship authority may apply to the court for a protection order.
2. The existence of an emergency intervention order in force does not exclude the possibility of applying for a protection order.
3. A protection order is issued for the purpose of protecting the person subjected to domestic violence and persons under their care and preventing new manifestations of domestic violence.
4. A protection order is issued for a period of up to 6 months and may be extended twice by a reasoned decision of the court based on an appropriate application justifying its necessity, each time for a maximum period of up to 3 months.
5. The following restrictions may be applied by a protection order:
1) to compel the person who committed domestic violence to immediately leave the place of residence of the person subjected to domestic violence and to prohibit their return there until the expiration of the period established by the order. When establishing the period of this restriction, the court takes into account the possibility and appropriateness of relocating the person subjected to domestic violence and persons under their care to a shelter, and the availability of other places of residence for the person who committed domestic violence.
2) to prohibit the person who committed domestic violence from visiting the person subjected to domestic violence who does not reside in the jointly inhabited premises with them, and if necessary, also persons under their care, at their places of work, study, rest, residence or other places.
3) to prohibit the person who committed domestic violence from approaching the person subjected to domestic violence (if necessary, also persons under their care) closer than such a distance that would reasonably cause fear in the person subjected to domestic violence about danger threatening their personal safety. When applying this restriction, the order establishes a specific measure of distance.
4) to surrender weapons that were in the possession of the person who committed violence to the police for safekeeping until the expiration of the period established by the order.
5) to impose an obligation on the person who committed domestic violence to share the care expenses of their joint minor or adult incapacitated children, as well as persons under their joint care, with the person subjected to domestic violence, in the amount payable for alimony obligations established by the Family Code of the Republic of Armenia. The court may impose an obligation on the person who committed domestic violence to cover other necessary expenses of the person subjected to domestic violence that arose as a result of the violence committed.
6) if necessary, to prohibit visits with children.
7) to prohibit the person who committed domestic violence from communicating by telephone, correspondence or other means of communication with the person subjected to domestic violence, and if necessary, also with persons under their care.
8) to impose an obligation on the person who committed domestic violence to undergo a rehabilitation program.
6. The restrictions provided for by part 5 of this Article may be applied separately or in combination, indicating the same or different periods for their implementation.
7. The requirements for protection order applications, the procedure for their examination, the acts adopted by the court, the procedure for appealing them, and the grounds for termination of a protection order are provided for by the Civil Procedure Code of the Republic of Armenia. The grounds and procedure for providing a public defender for judicial representation of a person subjected to domestic violence are provided for by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Advocacy".
8. If the person subjected to domestic violence is a minor or an incapacitated person, and the protection order is applied against the sole legal representative (or representatives) residing jointly with them, then the court instructs the guardianship and trusteeship authority to resolve the issue of temporary care of the child or incapacitated person in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Armenia, guided by their interests.
9. The police exercise supervision over the implementation of the protection order by the person who committed domestic violence. When applying the restriction provided for by point 1 of part 5 of this Article, the person who committed domestic violence may collect their belongings from the place of residence of the person subjected to domestic violence once every two months, accompanied by a police officer. When applying the restriction provided for by point 4 of part 5 of this Article, if at the time of the decision there is a weapon in the possession of the person who committed domestic violence, they shall surrender it to the police immediately, but no later than within 10 hours. The Compulsory Enforcement Service of Judicial Acts of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Armenia ensures the implementation of the obligation provided for by point 5 of part 5 of this Article, and the Authorized Body monitors the implementation of the obligation provided for by point 8 of the same part.
10. The restriction provided for by point 3 of part 5 of this Article does not apply during a meeting organized within the framework of the reconciliation process provided for by this law between the person subjected to domestic violence and the person who committed domestic violence with the permission of the support center at the center or in the presence of an appropriate specialist of the center.
11. Failure by the person who committed domestic violence to comply with the requirements of the protection order gives rise to liability provided for by law.

## Article 9

Termination of Emergency Intervention and Protection Orders in Connection with the Initiation of Criminal Proceedings

1. Within the framework of criminal proceedings initiated on the fact of domestic violence, in cases where detention is chosen as a preventive measure against the person who committed domestic violence, a decision is made on compulsory placement in a medical institution, or protection measures provided for by the Criminal Procedure Code of the Republic of Armenia are applied against the person subjected to domestic violence to ensure the personal safety of the protected person, preserve the apartment and other property, relocate the protected person to another place of residence, replace identity documents of the protected person or change their appearance, for the period of application of the respective measures, the effect of those restrictions provided for by emergency intervention and protection orders that are related to the prohibition of approaching the person, their place of residence or places of frequent presence shall cease.
2. After the initiation of criminal proceedings on the fact of domestic violence, an emergency intervention order may be issued and an application for a protection order may be submitted if detention has not been chosen as a preventive measure against the person who committed domestic violence, a decision on compulsory placement in a medical institution has not been made against them, and the body conducting the proceedings has not applied the protection measures specified in part 1 of this Article. If detention has been chosen as a preventive measure against the person who committed domestic violence, a decision on compulsory placement in a medical institution has been made against them, and the body conducting the proceedings has applied the protection measures specified in part 1 of this Article, then an emergency intervention order may be issued and an application for a protection order may be submitted if the restriction provided for by them is not related to the prohibition of approaching the person, their place of residence or places of frequent presence.

## Article 1

0. Reconciliation of persons subjected to domestic violence and persons who committed domestic violence

1. The reconciliation of persons subjected to domestic violence and persons who committed domestic violence is carried out during the validity of urgent intervention and protection decisions, in the manner established by the head of the Authorized body.
2. Reconciliation is carried out upon the application of a person who committed domestic violence or a person subjected to domestic violence, addressed to the support center. Before starting the process, the support center obtains the written consent of the other party to participate in the reconciliation. The support center terminates the reconciliation process if there is reasonable suspicion that the person subjected to domestic violence is participating in it under the influence of threats.
3. In case of concluding a reconciliation during the validity of a protection decision, the person subjected to domestic violence or the person who committed violence may apply to the court for the purpose of revoking the decision.
4. In case of concluding a reconciliation during the validity of an urgent intervention decision, the person subjected to domestic violence or the person who committed violence may apply to the competent police officer who issued it for the purpose of revoking the decision.
5. Reconciliation is not carried out if the person subjected to domestic violence is a minor or incapacitated person.

## Article 1

1. Placing a person who committed domestic violence on preventive registration and removing from registration

1. A person who committed domestic violence, against whom a warning, urgent intervention or protection decision has been issued, as well as an adult person having a conviction for a crime related to domestic violence, are placed on preventive registration by the police. Within the framework of preventive registration, monitoring of the prevention of committing domestic violence by an adult person is carried out by a social worker of the territorial body providing social services established by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Social Assistance", and in the case of a minor, by the police, in the manner established by the head of the Authorized body or the head of the police, respectively.
2. A person placed on preventive registration is removed from registration if, within one year after the last act of domestic violence, no new urgent intervention or protection decision has been issued against that person or no conviction for the corresponding crime has been rendered.
3. The police officer and the social worker of the territorial body, within the framework of preventive registration, meet at least once a month with the persons placed on registration and persons subjected to domestic violence and carry out explanatory work. The social worker of the territorial body, in case of detecting a risk of repetition of violence during monitoring, immediately informs the police thereof.

## Article 1

2. Non-application of protection measures against a minor or incapacitated person

1. Urgent intervention or protection decisions are not issued against a minor or incapacitated person.
2. A minor who committed domestic violence, if the characteristics of a criminal offense are absent in his or her act, is referred by the police to appropriate community and state centers for the implementation of restorative programs, in accordance with the procedure established by the Government in the field of prevention of juvenile delinquency.
3. In case of domestic violence being committed by an incapacitated person, the relevant provisions of the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Psychiatric Care" and the Civil Procedure Code of the Republic of Armenia apply.
CHAPTER 3
BODIES AND SPECIAL STRUCTURES CARRYING OUT FUNCTIONS OF PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND PROTECTION OF PERSONS SUBJECTED TO DOMESTIC VIOLENCE,
AND SOURCES OF THEIR FINANCING

## Article 1

3. Bodies and special structures carrying out functions of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence

1. Prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, within the scope of their functions, are carried out by:
1) the republican executive body authorized in the field of social issues (hereinafter: the Authorized body);
2) the police;
3) the republican executive body authorized in the field of education;
4) the republican executive body authorized in the field of health;
5) guardianship and trusteeship bodies;
6) special structures providing support to persons subjected to domestic violence:
a. support centers,
b. shelters.
2. State and local self-government bodies, within the scope of their powers, support the fight against domestic violence and cooperate with the Authorized body in that field. The mentioned bodies also participate in public awareness in the mentioned field, in the implementation of measures aimed at providing social and material support to persons subjected to domestic violence.

## Article 1

4. Powers of the Authorized Body in the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence

1. In the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, the Authorized Body:
1) develops the state strategy and coordinates the implementation of measures arising therefrom;
2) carries out centralized registration of cases of domestic violence in the manner established by government decision, including data provided by the police, investigative committee, special investigative service, prosecutor's office and the Judicial Department of the Republic of Armenia, as well as support centers not registered by the latter, guardianship and trusteeship bodies, authorized republican executive bodies in the fields of healthcare and education, and maintains statistics on their progress, as well as publishes those statistics annually on its official website;
3) studies the causes, conditions and consequences of domestic violence, develops programs aimed at overcoming them;
4) supervises compliance by shelters with the requirements established by the Government for shelters and their staff, as well as carries out certification of non-commercial legal entities for the provision of social services to persons subjected to domestic violence through shelters, in the manner established by the Government;
5) establishes shelters, concludes shelter financing agreements with non-commercial legal entities on the basis of the model form of agreement established by the government, and exercises supervision over the activities of shelters partially or fully financed by it, including with respect to the use of financial resources;
6) periodically organizes retraining of staff of support centers and shelters, including in cooperation with public organizations in the field;
7) periodically publishes in a publicly accessible manner the list of means of communication (including toll-free "Hotline" service telephone numbers for persons subjected to domestic violence, which support centers are required to have) by which persons subjected to domestic violence, their family members and other persons can immediately contact a support center employee or a relevant specialist engaged by the center and receive necessary immediate assistance, and in the case of persons with disabilities, also assistance accessible to them;
8) opens an account for support of persons subjected to domestic violence for the purpose of providing temporary assistance to persons subjected to domestic violence and manages it in the manner established by the government. The funds of the account are formed from state budget allocations, donations from natural and legal persons, and funds received from other sources not prohibited by law;
9) approves the procedure for organizing and implementing reconciliation between persons subjected to domestic violence and persons who committed domestic violence by the support center;
10) establishes the procedure for implementing monitoring for the purpose of preventing the commission of domestic violence by adults within the framework of preventive registration;
11) approves the rehabilitation program for persons who committed domestic violence.
2. The Council for the Prevention of Domestic Violence operates under the head of the Authorized Body, which submits proposals to him/her on the state strategy in the field of prevention of domestic violence, programs arising therefrom, legislative reforms, public awareness measures and other issues. The members of the Council carry out their activities on a voluntary basis. The Council includes in equal numbers:
1) representatives of republican executive authorities, police, Investigative Committee of the Republic of Armenia, prosecutor's office;
2) representatives of public organizations;
3) employees of support centers and shelters, upon presentation by the head of the Authorized Body.
3. The composition of the Council also includes one representative of the staff of the Human Rights Defender.
4. The functions of the Council and the procedure for its formation are established by the Government, and its individual composition by the Prime Minister of the Republic of Armenia.
(the article shall enter into force on the date of entry into force of the Law "On the Anti-Corruption Committee" by amendment of Law HO-160-N of 24.03.21)

## Article 1

5. Powers of the Police in the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence

1. In the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, the police:
1) ensures the operation of a specialized subdivision in the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence. The special uniform of the specialized subdivision is established by the Government, and the procedure for retraining and other features of activity by the Chief of Police;
2) establishes the procedure for placing on preventive registration, as well as for implementing monitoring for the purpose of preventing the commission of domestic violence by minors within the framework of preventive registration, and for that purpose meets with them at least once a month;
3) explains to persons who have applied to the police with a report of domestic violence having been committed against them their rights and the possibility of using available services, if necessary refers them to support centers, and also in cases and in the manner provided by this law makes a decision on placing persons subjected to domestic violence and persons under their care in a shelter;
4) organizes periodic retraining of its relevant officers on the prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, as well as ensures the participation of its relevant officers in retraining organized by other bodies and organizations;
5) makes decisions on urgent intervention provided by Article 7 of this law, as well as exercises supervision over compliance with the relevant requirements of urgent intervention and protection decisions in accordance with the procedure established by the Chief of Police;
6) develops the criteria for assessing imminent danger provided by Article 7 of this law and organizes mandatory retraining of its relevant officers for the purpose of familiarizing them with the features of applying those criteria;
7) provides to the Authorized Body the statistics it maintains on cases of domestic violence.

## Article 1

6. Powers of the authorized republican executive body in the field of education in the field of prevention of domestic violence

1. In the field of prevention of domestic violence, the authorized republican executive body in the field of education:
1) periodically conducts retraining with teachers and educators on their role in the field of prevention of domestic violence, the procedure for reporting cases of domestic violence to competent authorities;
2) reviews educational programs for the purpose of preventing domestic violence and eliminates manifestations of encouragement of domestic violence in textbooks and educational materials, as well as includes in educational programs materials on the nature of domestic violence, its impact on the family and society, and values in the traditional family.

## Article 1

7. Powers of the authorized republican executive body in the field of healthcare in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence

1. In the sphere of prevention of domestic violence, the authorized republican executive body in the field of healthcare:
1) ensures unconditional primary medical assistance for persons subjected to domestic violence, establishes the procedure for recording information regarding persons who have sought medical assistance as a result of domestic violence and the conditions for reporting cases of domestic violence to the police, the Investigative Committee of the Republic of Armenia or the prosecutor's office.
2) implements measures to inform employees of medical institutions regarding the conditions and procedure for reporting cases of domestic violence to the police, the Investigative Committee of the Republic of Armenia or the prosecutor's office.
3) conducts periodic retraining in medical institutions regarding informing persons who have sought medical assistance as a result of domestic violence about available psychological and other services, and referring them to support centers.

## Article 1

8. Powers of guardianship and trusteeship bodies in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence

1. For the purpose of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, guardianship and trusteeship bodies, within the scope of their powers:
1) in cases provided by law, apply to court for a protection order, and also submit reports to law enforcement bodies regarding domestic violence or real threat thereof against minors or persons declared incapacitated or with limited capacity by judicial procedure.
2) in the manner established by law and their charter, organize care for children and incapacitated persons left without care due to domestic violence.

## Article 1

9. Support center

1. The support center:
1) informs persons who have applied to the support center in a language understandable to them, and in the case of persons with disabilities in a manner accessible to them, about their rights, available services and means of protection provided by law and the procedure for using them.
2) organizes the provision of necessary and unconditional psychological and

legal assistance and other necessary social services to persons subjected to domestic violence.
3) in cases and in the manner provided by this law, makes a decision on placing persons subjected to domestic violence and persons under their care in a shelter, with their consent.
4) studies the causes and conditions of domestic violence, maintains statistics on cases of domestic violence and presents the results to the Authorized body.
5) in accordance with the procedure established by the Authorized body, organizes the rehabilitation of persons who have committed domestic violence, including, after receiving copies of warning and urgent intervention orders, as soon as possible, offers the person who has committed domestic violence to receive appropriate support as part of a rehabilitation program and participate in certain measures.
6) after receiving copies of warning and urgent intervention orders, as soon as possible, offers the person subjected to domestic violence the provision of psychological support.
7) under the conditions provided by Article 10 of this law and in the manner approved by the head of the Authorized body, carries out reconciliation or organizes its implementation between persons subjected to domestic violence and persons who have committed domestic violence.
8) assists persons subjected to domestic violence in matters of employment and receiving social assistance from the state or relevant organizations.
9) with the consent of the person who has applied to the support center, in order to ensure their safety, applies to the relevant subdivision of the police to verify the existence of grounds for making a warning or urgent intervention order, and also in cases provided by this law, applies to court with an application for making a protection order.

## Article 2

0. Shelter

1. The state takes necessary measures to ensure the availability and operation of shelters. A shelter is established by the Authorized body, a non-profit organization founded by a local self-government body, or other non-profit legal entities.
2. The Authorized body may conclude an agreement with non-profit legal entities regarding the establishment of a shelter by them or support for the operation of an already established shelter, which must also contain the procedure for the Authorized body to exercise supervision over the operation of that shelter, including over the use of provided financial resources. The location of establishing a new shelter is determined by the Authorized body under this law, based on the conclusions of needs assessment provided by territorial bodies providing social services under the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Social Assistance". The non-profit legal entity to be financed is selected through a competition held in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Armenia.
3. To establish a shelter, the relevant organizations must obtain a certificate provided by the Authorized body, the procedure for granting which is established by the Government. The Government may, in the manner established by legislation, provide support to non-profit legal entities for creating a shelter and ensuring its operation.
4. The shelter must be provided with necessary conditions of communal, sanitary-hygienic and fire safety meeting the requirements established by the legislation of the Republic of Armenia and with an appropriate security system, for children also with conditions of care and education, and must comply with other criteria established by the Government of the Republic of Armenia.
5. Persons subjected to domestic violence, upon their application, together with persons under their care (hereinafter: beneficiaries), may be placed in a shelter if there is a reasonable assumption regarding the danger of repetition or continuation of violence. Children cannot be placed in a shelter without at least one parent or other legal representative. A decision on placing a person in a shelter may be made by:
1) the police, in case of danger threatening life and health.
2) the support center, upon receiving the conclusion of the territorial body providing social services under the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Social Assistance".
The decision on placing a person in a shelter must be reasoned.
6. The shelter:
1) provides beneficiaries unconditionally with safe living space, psychological and legal

assistance, social support, involving relevant specialists when necessary, and also provides food and clothing, and for children also space with furniture and supplies necessary for homework preparation.
2) when necessary and with the consent of beneficiaries, organizes their application to healthcare institutions to receive medical assistance and service.
7. Beneficiaries are placed in a shelter for no more than 12 months. The period of stay in the shelter is determined by the shelter, upon the recommendation of specialists providing appropriate assistance to beneficiaries, taking into account the presence or absence of threats to their safety, their ability to live independently or jointly with another person, and other relevant circumstances, considering as a mandatory condition the consent of beneficiaries to remain in the shelter.

## Article 2

1. Funding sources for special structures preventing domestic violence

1. The funding of support centers and shelters is carried out at the expense of the funds of their founders, the state budget in cases established by this Law and the legislation of the Republic of Armenia, as well as other sources of funding not prohibited by law.
CHAPTER 4
LEGAL REGIME FOR MAINTAINING INFORMATION ABOUT VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND THE PROCEDURE FOR ITS PROVISION

## Article 2

2. Inviolability of private and family life

1. Private life data received by competent bodies regarding cases of domestic violence and(or) cases of crime related to victims of domestic violence or alleged victims of domestic violence are confidential. It is prohibited to publish through mass media or by other means, without the consent of the victim of domestic violence or the alleged victim of domestic violence, such information about them that would allow disclosure of their identity, unless otherwise provided by the legislation of the Republic of Armenia.
2. Police officers, employees of support centers and shelters are prohibited from disclosing the location of victims of domestic violence accommodated in the shelter and persons under their care, or other information by means of which it is possible to detect their location.
3. Violation of the right to inviolability of private or family life gives rise to liability provided by law, and damage caused to a person as a result thereof is subject to compensation in the manner established by law.
CHAPTER 5
TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

## Article 2

3. Final part and transitional provisions

1. This Law enters into force one month after its official publication, with the exception of the provisions envisaged by parts 3 and 4 of this Article.
2. Within three months after the entry into force of this Law, the Government and the bodies envisaged by this Law shall adopt the acts envisaged by this Law. The Prime Minister shall approve the individual composition of the council attached to the Authorized body, and the head of the Authorized body shall convene the first session of the Council for the Prevention of Domestic Violence.
3. Chapter 2 of this Law, as well as part 2 of Article 22, enter into force six months after the official publication.
4. The provisions regarding services provided by support centers and shelters enter into force one year after the official publication of this Law.
5. The responsible bodies under this Law shall ensure the training necessary for the application of protection measures and the provision of social services envisaged by this Law before the entry into force of the respective provisions.
