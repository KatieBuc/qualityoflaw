### 8

After § 76a, a new § 76b is inserted, which including footnote No. 33c reads:
(1) If the life, health, freedom or human dignity of the applicant are seriously threatened by the conduct of the party against whom the application is directed, the presiding judge may, by preliminary injunction, order the party against whom the application is directed, in particular, to
a) temporarily leave the dwelling or house jointly occupied with the applicant (hereinafter "joint dwelling"), as well as its immediate vicinity, or not to enter it,
b) refrain from meeting with the applicant and establishing contact with him or her.
(2) Exceptions arising from the fulfilment of obligations imposed by the preliminary injunction on the party against whom the application is directed shall be determined by the court, taking into account his or her legitimate interests.
(3) A preliminary injunction under paragraph 1 shall last for one month from its enforceability. If the decision on the application for a preliminary injunction under paragraph 1 was preceded by a decision of the Police of the Czech Republic under a special legal regulation33c), this period shall begin on the day following the day on which the period specified in that special legal regulation expired. In the case of filing an application for extension of the preliminary injunction under paragraph 4, the period shall not end before the court decides on such extension.
(4) If proceedings on the merits have been initiated before the expiry of the period referred to in paragraph 3, the presiding judge may, on the applicant's application, decide to extend the duration of the preliminary injunction; he or she shall take into account the duration of the state of threat to the applicant under paragraph 1, the content and reasons for the application to initiate proceedings on the merits, the property or other circumstances of the parties, including ownership and other relations to the joint dwelling to which the preliminary injunction relates, and other relevant circumstances. However, the preliminary injunction shall expire at the latest upon the expiry of one year from the moment of its issuance.
(5) A decision on an application for a preliminary injunction under paragraph 1 is not conditional on a prior decision of the Police of the Czech Republic under a special legal regulation.
33c) § 21a et seq. of Act No. 283/1991 Coll., on the Police of the Czech Republic, as amended.".

### 13. After § 273a, a new § 273b is inserted, which including the heading reads

Enforcement of a decision on eviction from joint dwelling and non-establishment of contact with the entitled person
(1) If the court has ordered by preliminary injunction that the obligor temporarily leave the joint dwelling, as well as its immediate vicinity, or that he or she refrain from meeting with a person designated by the court in the decision and not establish contact with him or her (§ 76b), it shall also ensure the immediate enforcement of this decision.
(2) The enforcement of the decision shall be carried out by the court, in cooperation with the competent state authorities, evicting the obligor from the joint dwelling, taking from him or her all keys to the joint dwelling that the obligor holds, and, where appropriate, prohibiting him or her from meeting with the designated person or otherwise contacting him or her. At the same time, the court shall provide the obligor with an opportunity to immediately collect from the joint dwelling, during the enforcement of the decision, his or her personal valuables and documents, as well as items that serve his or her personal needs; during the duration of the preliminary injunction under § 76b, the court shall also enable the obligor to collect items necessary for the performance of his or her business activity or profession.
(3) If the obligor is not present during the enforcement of the decision, the court shall, at his or her request, provide him or her with an opportunity during the period referred to in § 76b paragraph 4 to collect from the joint dwelling the items referred to in paragraph 2. The court shall notify the obligor of this right by a notice left on the door of the joint dwelling.
(4) The court competent to enforce the decision referred to in paragraph 1 is the court that issued the preliminary injunction.".
