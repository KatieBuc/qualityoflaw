# CONCERNING

# PROTECTION OF WOMEN

# BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

# REGENT OF BANDUNG,

Considering:  
a. that in order to improve the position, role and quality of women is an effort to realize gender equality and justice in family, community, national and state life;  
b. that all forms of violence against women constitute a violation of human rights so that women need to be protected in their dignity and honor and guaranteed their right to life in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination;  
c. that in order to protect the community, realize equity, and justice as an obligation of the regional government to provide protection and welfare for women, a legal basis is required in the implementation of women's protection;  
d. based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women;  

In view of:  
1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;  

2. Law Number 14 of 1950 concerning the Establishment of Regency Regions Within the Province of West Java (State Gazette of 1950) as amended by Law Number 4 of 1968 concerning the Establishment of Purwakarta Regency and Subang Regency by amending Law Number 14 of 1950 concerning the Establishment of Regency Regions Within the Province of West Java (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1968 Number 31, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 2851);  
3. Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1984 Number 29, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3277);  
4. Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419);  
5. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587), as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58 Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);  
6. Government Regulation Number 4 of 2006 concerning the Implementation and Cooperation for the Recovery of Victims of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2006 Number 15, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4604);  
7. Government Regulation Number 9 of 2008 concerning Procedures and Mechanisms for Integrated Services for Witnesses and/or Victims of Human Trafficking Crimes (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2008 Number 22, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4818);  
8. Regulation of the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection Number 5 of 2010 concerning Guidelines for the Establishment and Development of Integrated Service Centers (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2010 Number 446).

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Bandung Regency.  
2. Regent is the Regent of Bandung.  
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.  
5. Women are human beings of female sex starting from infants, adolescents, adults to the elderly, who have been recognized by law.  
6. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against Women.  
7. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily.  
8. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the Regional Government, community, and/or other parties who know or hear that violence against Women will or has occurred.  
9. Victim is a Woman who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence and life vulnerability.  
10. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social and psychological to the victim.  
11. Victim of Violence is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights violations or criminal acts as well as acts of Violence committed either by state apparatus, Regional Government apparatus, or individuals.  
12. Violence Against Women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering to Women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.  
13. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of the Woman.  
14. Parents are biological father and/or mother, step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother. 15. Discrimination Against Women is any distinction, exclusion, or restriction made on the basis of sex which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil, or any other field by Women, regardless of their marital status on the basis of equality between men and Women. 16. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and Women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural circumstances of society.  
17. Health Rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of Victims including physical, psychological, and sexual health so that Victims can carry out their roles again properly and naturally both in the family and in society.  
18. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person who experiences social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions normally.  
19. Repatriation is an effort to return Victims of Violence to their area of origin.  
20. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide Protection and fulfillment of needs for Victims.  
21. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service unit established by the Regional Government to carry out the functions and main tasks of integrated services for Women and child Victims of Violence, including complaint services, Health Rehabilitation, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, Social Reintegration, and legal assistance.

# CHAPTER II

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

## Part One

General

#### Article 2

Every Woman has the right:
a. to life;  
b. to form a family and continue descendants; c. to self-development;  
d. to obtain justice;  
e. to personal freedom;  
f. to security;  
g. to welfare; andh. to participate in government.

## Part Two

Rights of Women with Disabilities

#### Article 3

Every Woman with disabilities has the right to obtain:
a. education at all units, paths, types, and levels of education;  
b. decent work and livelihood in accordance with the type and degree of disability, education, and ability;  
c. equal treatment to play a role in development and enjoy its results;  
d. accessibility in the context of independence;  
e. rehabilitation, social assistance, and maintenance of social welfare standards; andf. equal rights to develop talents, abilities, and social life.

## Part Three

Rights of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 4

Every Woman Victim of Violence has the right: a. to have her dignity and worth as a human being respected;  
b. to Health Rehabilitation;  
c. to determine her own decisions;  
d. to obtain information;  
e. to confidentiality;  
f. to compensation;  
g. to Social Rehabilitation;  
h. to complaint handling;  
i. to obtain facilitation in the judicial process;  
j. to assistance;  
k. to health and psychological recovery; and l. to protection from family, community, Regional Government, and social institutions.

# CHAPTER III

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

#### Article 5

Forms of Violence Against Women include: a. Physical violence;  
b. Psychological violence;  
c. Sexual violence;  
d. Domestic Neglect;  
e. trafficking of Women;  
f. exploitation; andg. restriction of movement.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

General

#### Article 6

Protection of Women in the Region is a joint obligation and responsibility among:
a. Regional Government;  
b. Community;  
c. Family; andd. Parents.

## Part Two

Obligations and Responsibilities of Regional Government

#### Article 7

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 6 letter a include:a. implementing policies for the Protection of Women from all forms of Discrimination Against Women and acts of Violence;  
b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women;  
c. collecting data and information about Women Victims of Violence and Discrimination Against Women as well as supporting regulations;  
d. conducting education on matters related to the Protection of Women Victims of Violence and Discrimination Against Women;  
e. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the Protection of Women Victims of Violence and Discrimination Against Women;  
f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of Protection of Women Victims of Violence and Discrimination Against Women; andg. providing support for facilities and infrastructure in the implementation of Women's Protection. (2) To anticipate acts of Violence, the Regional Government:a. establishes and facilitates the operation of integrated service institutions for Victims involving community elements;  
b. facilitates legal aid assistance and legal services according to the needs of Victims;  
c. provides safe houses or alternative residences along with procedures for handling, services, psychosocial, and spiritual care;  
d. conducts continuous handling up to the rehabilitation and Social Reintegration stage;  
e. conducts monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for Victims;  
f. encourages public awareness of the importance of Protection for Victims;  
g. establishes integrated service posts to receive complaints in each sub-district;  
h. facilitates comprehensive health services including visum et repertum services and psychological services; andi. facilitates the protection guarantees needed by victims during assistance to Victims.  
3. In carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), the Regional Government takes into account the rights and obligations of Parents, guardians, husbands, or other persons who are legally responsible for Victims.  
4. To carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) through paragraph (3), the Regional Government prepares an action plan for the Protection of Women Victims of Violence.  
5. Provisions regarding the action plan for the Protection of Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (4) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 8

1. The Regent integrates policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women into Regional development planning.  
2. The integration of programs and activities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be incorporated into planning and budget documents which include:a. regional long-term development plan; b. regional medium-term development plan; c. strategic plan of Regional Apparatus;  
d. Regional Government work plan; and  e. work plan and budget of Regional Apparatus. (3) The Regional Apparatus handling the field of Women facilitates the integration of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women into the preparation of planning and budget documents as referred to in paragraph (2).  
4. In carrying out activities as referred to in paragraph (3), the Regional Apparatus handling the field of Women coordinates with Regional Apparatus that have direct relevance to the field of Women.

#### Article 9

1. The Regent implements policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women to eliminate all forms of Discrimination Against Women and acts of Violence Against Women.  
2. The Regional Apparatus handling the field of Women implements policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women that have been established in cooperation and coordination with relevant Regional Apparatus, law enforcement, community institutions, universities, and the community.  
3. The implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be in the form of:a. policy analysis;  
b. advocacy;  
c. coordination and communication;  
d. socialization;  
e. education;  
f. training;  
g. facilitation; andh. provision of services.

#### Article 10

1. The Regent in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women as referred to in Article 9 undertakes efforts to:a. coordinate implementation among Regional Apparatus; b. cooperate with the central government, provincial government, and other regency/city governments in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
c. strengthen the capacity of Gender institutions; d. facilitate services for the Protection of Women; e. provide services for the Protection of Women; f. implement affirmative action for the Protection of Women; andg. prepare a data system for the Protection of Women including a data system for Violence Against Women.  
2. The implementation of policies, programs, and activities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be coordinated by the Vice Regent.

## Part Three

Obligations and Responsibilities of Community, Family, and Parents

#### Article 11

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letters b through d shall be carried out in the form of community participation.
2. The forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) include:a. establishing Family partners at the kelurahan and/or village level;
b. establishing Women's Protection units within community organizations;
c. conducting independent socialization of Women's rights;
d. providing first aid to Victims; ande. reporting to the competent authorities if Violence Against Women occurs in their environment.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and social media.
4. The forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 12

The Family and/or Parents as referred to in Article 6 letters c and d, have full responsibility to prevent all forms of Violence and protect Women as Family members.

# CHAPTER V

PREVENTION, PROTECTION SERVICES, AND RECOVERY OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Prevention of Violence Against Women

#### Article 13

To prevent the occurrence of acts of Violence Against Women, the Regional Government shall:
a. prepare and socialize matters relating to Violence Against Women;
b. socialize Women's rights; c. endeavor to improve education for Women;
d. open skills courses for Women to support the Family's economic needs;
e. create employment opportunities for Women; f. provide counseling related to Violence Against Women;
g. empower social community organizations and Women's organizations at the Regional, sub-district, kelurahan, and/or village levels to increase Women's knowledge and insight;
h. establish Women's Protection complaint posts; andi. form networks in efforts to prevent Violence Against Women.

## Part Two

Protection Services

#### Article 14

1. Protection Services to Women shall be carried out in the form of:a. Protection of the Victim's personal security from physical and mental threats;
b. health services according to the victim's needs; c. medical services for the purposes of legal evidence;
d. confidentiality of the victim's identity;
e. provision of testimony during court examination without facing the suspect;
f. spiritual guidance services; and/org. Protection for Women who work as laborers or workers in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. Protection Services as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a shall be carried out by the Family, law enforcement officials, social institutions, and other parties either temporarily or based on a court Protection order.

#### Article 15

1. In providing Protection Services as referred to in Article 14 paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall:a. provide services and handling as quickly as possible to companions, witnesses and/or Victims;
b. provide convenience, comfort, safety, and free of charge for companions, witnesses and/or Victims;
c. maintain the confidentiality of witnesses and/or Victims; d. guarantee justice and legal certainty for witnesses and/or Victims;
e. provide special service rooms at the offices of the Indonesian National Police;
f. provide apparatus, health workers, social workers, and spiritual counselors;
g. create and develop a system and procedures for cooperation in implementing service programs involving parties easily accessible to Victims;
h. provide protection for companions, witnesses, Family, and friends of Victims;
i. provide decent work and livelihood according to the type and degree of disability, education, and ability; andj. provide protection for Women workers or laborers in accordance with the provisions of protection for laborers or workers in accordance with the provisions of legislation.
2. The Regional Government in providing Protection Services as referred to in paragraph (1) may cooperate with the central government, provincial government, other regency/city governments, the community, and/or other social institutions.
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be set forth in a cooperation agreement.

#### Article 16

1. The provision of Women's Protection Services as referred to in Article 14 shall consist of Victim identification, legal assistance, medical rehabilitation, psychosocial rehabilitation, Social Reintegration, and Repatriation assistance.
2. The provision of Women's Protection Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided for Women Victims of Violence including victims of trafficking in persons, Women in disaster-prone areas, Women workers, elderly Women, Women with disabilities, and Women Victims of sexual exploitation.

## Part Three

Recovery

#### Article 17

1. For the purposes of recovery, Victims have the right to obtain:a. health services;
b. Victim assistance;
c. counseling;
d. spiritual guidance; ande. resocialization; andf. economic empowerment.
2. For the purposes of recovery as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall provide: a. special service rooms at the offices of the Indonesian National Police;
b. expert and professional personnel;
c. service centers and safe houses; andd. other facilities and infrastructure necessary for the recovery of Victims.
3. Expert and professional personnel as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b shall consist of:a. health workers;
b. social workers;
c. volunteer companions; and/ord. spiritual counselors; and/ore. psychologists.
4. Health workers as referred to in paragraph (3) letter a shall examine Victims in accordance with their professional standards, restore, and rehabilitate the health of Victims if Victims require treatment.
5. Social workers, volunteer companions, and/or spiritual counselors as referred to in paragraph (3) letters b, c, and d shall provide services to Victims in the form of counseling to strengthen and/or provide a sense of security for Victims.
6. Violations of the provisions as referred to in paragraphs (4) and (5) may be subject to administrative sanctions in the form of:a. written warning;
b. temporary suspension from activities;
c. termination of cooperation;
d. revocation of registration certificate;
e. revocation of operational license;
f. withdrawal of facilities; andg. administrative fines.
7. Provisions regarding the procedures for implementing administrative sanctions as referred to in paragraph (6) shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 18

Further provisions regarding the procedures for prevention, protection services, and Recovery of Women Victims of Violence as referred to in Articles 13 through 17 shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VI

## INSTITUTIONS

#### Article 19

1. The Regional Government shall establish P2TP2A in the implementation of Women's Protection Services. (2) Women's protection services by P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall involve elements of:a. health;
b. psychology;
c. law;
d. clergy;
e. social; andf. economy.
3. Provisions regarding the establishment of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 20

1. In addition to establishing P2TP2A as referred to in Article 19, the Regional Government shall establish a task force in the implementation of Women's Protection services.  
2. The task force as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of elements of:a. Regional Government;  
b. Ministry conducting government affairs in the field of religion;  
c. State Police of the Republic of Indonesia;  
d. Prosecutor's Office;  
e. non-governmental organizations; andf. family welfare development mobilization team.  
3. Provisions regarding the establishment, organizational structure, duties, and functions of the task force as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 21

1. Community participation in Women's Protection may take the form of:a. provision of counseling regarding women's rights;  
b. provision of skills training to support the Family economy;  
c. establishment of community social institutions to assist Victims of Violence; andd. reporting to the competent authority if Violence Against Women occurs in their environment.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, community social institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector, and mass media.

#### Article 22

1. Any person related to the implementation of Women's Protection who knows of the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women but allows it, does not provide Protection to Victims, and/or does not report to the relevant authority may be subject to administrative sanctions in the form of:a. verbal warning;  
b. written warning;  
c. temporary suspension from activities;  
d. termination of cooperation;  
e. revocation of registered certificate;  
f. revocation of operational license;  
g. withdrawal of facilities; andh. administrative fine.  
2. Provisions regarding the procedures for imposing administrative sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated in Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

## SUPERVISION, EVALUATION, AND GUIDANCE

## Part One

Supervision

#### Article 23

1. The Regent shall supervise the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Women's Protection.  
2. To ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness in an integrated manner in the implementation of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent shall conduct monitoring through the Regional Apparatus handling the field of Women.  
3. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be conducted from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Women's Protection for the current year.  
4. The results of monitoring as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be reported to the Regent periodically.

## Part Two

Evaluation

#### Article 24

1. The Regional Government shall evaluate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Women's Protection at the end of each fiscal year.  
2. The results of evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for Women's Protection for the following year.  
3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Three

Guidance

#### Article 25

The Regent shall provide guidance on the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Women's Protection to relevant Regional Apparatus and sub-districts.

#### Article 26

Further provisions regarding supervision, evaluation, and guidance of policies, programs, and activities for Women's Protection as referred to in Article 21 through Article 23 shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

##### I. GENERAL

The position, role and quality of women in current developments have undergone changes in efforts to realize gender equality and justice, both in family life, society, nation and state. Protection of human rights has also become a spotlight, especially regarding all forms of violence against women constituting violations of human rights, so that women need to be protected in their dignity and honor and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.  

In order to protect the community, realize equity, and justice as an obligation of the regional government to provide protection and welfare for women, the Government of Bandung Regency establishes a Regional Regulation on Women's Protection. The implementation of women's protection is carried out based on the principles of Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, which includes:
a. Protection;  
b. Respect for human rights;  
c. Gender justice and equality;  
d. Non-discrimination; ande. Best interests of women.  

The objectives of establishing the Regional Regulation on Women's Protection are:
a. To ensure the fulfillment of women's rights so they can live and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and honor;  
b. To provide gender justice and equality;  
c. To provide protection and security for women victims of violence and discrimination, as well as criminal acts of trafficking in persons; d. To eliminate all forms of violence and discrimination against women;  
e. To provide services to women victims of violence and witnesses;  
f. To carry out recovery for women victims of violence. II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 2

Point a What is meant by to live is the right to live and the right to defend life and livelihood. Point b What is meant by to have a family and continue descendants is the right to form a family and continue descendants through lawful marriage.

Point c What is meant by to develop oneself is to develop oneself through the fulfillment of basic needs and to obtain education and benefit from science and technology, arts and culture, for the purpose of improving quality of life and for the welfare of humankind.

Point d What is meant by to obtain justice is to receive recognition, guarantee, protection and fair legal certainty as well as equal treatment before the law. Point e What is meant by personal freedom is to advance oneself in fighting for one's rights collectively to build society, nation and state.

Point f What is meant by sense of security is the right to protection of oneself, family, honor, dignity and property under one's control, as well as the right to a sense of security and protection from threats of fear to do or not do something which is a basic right. Point g What is meant by welfare is to live prosperously physically and mentally, to have a place to live and obtain a good and healthy living environment, as well as the right to obtain health services.

Point h What is meant by to participate in government is to obtain equal opportunity in government.

#### Article 5

Point a What is meant by physical violence is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Point b What is meant by psychological violence is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, sense of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Point c What is meant by sexual violence as referred to in Article 15 point c is caused by: a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/ord. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Point d What is meant by domestic neglect is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of the needs of women as wives in a proper manner, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by the husband, parents, guardian, or other party;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of women as wives as they should be, committed by the husband, parents, guardian or other partyc. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of one's household, whereas according to the law applicable to him or her or due to an agreement or contract he or she is obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/ord. acts that result in economic dependence by means of restricting and/or prohibiting work
Point e What is meant by Trafficking in persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether conducted within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.

Point f What is meant by Exploitation as referred to in Article 15 point f is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits whether material or immaterial; and/orc. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.
