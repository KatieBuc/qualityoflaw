# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article I

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 years, including a child in the womb.  
2. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.  
3. Protection is all service actions to fulfill rights, as well as guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence. 4. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
5. Protection of women is an effort aimed at protecting women and their rights in order to obtain a sense of security from acts of violence.  
6. Violence is any act that results in or causes suffering and distress whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty arbitrarily, whether occurring in public or in private life.  
7. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or unconsciousness and/or causes death.  
8. Psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness or severe psychological suffering to a person.  
9. Victim of acts of violence hereinafter referred to as victim is an individual or group of people who experience suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by individuals.  
10. Regional Technical Service Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UP/[D PPA is a regional technical implementing unit, established by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection and other problems.  

ll.Coordination Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence hereinafter abbreviated as FKPKK is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked and collaborative manner.  

12. Region is Morowali Regency.  
13. Regional Apparatus is an element assisting the Regent in the administration of Government affairs which are the authority of the Region.  
14. Regional Government is the Government of Morowali Regency. 15. Regional Governance is the administration of government affairs by the Government and DPRD according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy in the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.  
16. Regent is the Regent of Morowali.  
17. Office of Village Community Empowerment, Women's Empowerment and Child Protection hereinafter abbreviated as Dinas PMDP3A is the regional apparatus of Morowali Regency that carries out government affairs in the field of women and children.

# CHAPTER II

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 3

Forms of Violence include:
a. physical violence;  
b. psychological violence;  
c. sexual violence; and/ord. domestic neglect.

#### Article 4

Physical violence as referred to in Article 3 letter a results in: a. the victim experiencing pain or serious injury;  
b. death of the victim; andc. causing illness or hindrance to carrying out work, position or livelihood or daily activities.

#### Article 5

Psychological violence as referred to results in:
a. Fear;  
b. loss of self-confidence;  
c. loss of ability to act; d. feelings of helplessness; and/ore. severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 6

in Article a letter b Sexual violence as referred to in Article 3 letter c includes: a. acts of sexual harassment;  
b. coercion of unnatural or unwanted sexual relations; c. coercion of sexual relations with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes; andd. sexual violence through social media.

#### Article 7

Domestic neglect as referred to in Article 3 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of a Child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or support a Child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
c. acts that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to law they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 8

Women and Children who are victims of acts of Violence receive the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as human beings;
b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
c. the right to determine their own decisions;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidentiality of identity;
f. the right to restitution;
g. the right to social rehabilitation;
h. the right to complaint handling;
i. the right to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and/or
j. the right to assistance.

#### Article 9

1. In the event that victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 8 require ongoing treatment, they have the right to stay in a safe house or alternative residence.
2. Ongoing treatment as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes treatment at the rehabilitation stage carried out by individuals, groups or Government and non-Government institutions.

#### Article 10

1. The safe house as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) provides temporary shelter and/or accommodation for victims of Violence until their rights are restored.
2. The operation and/or management of the safe house as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the PMDP3A Office.
3. For the safety and at the request of victims of Violence, the manager or operator of the safe house as referred to in paragraph (2) may place victims of Violence in a specific location that is kept confidential.

(4) Further provisions regarding the operation of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 11

Children as victims of acts of Violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 8, also receive special rights, as follows:
a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic services;
c. the right to equal protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and/or
e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

Regional Government

#### Article 12

1. The Regional Government is obliged to:
a. implement policies for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence established by the government;
b. establish policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children from acts of violence; and
c. cooperate with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, as well as educational institutions, health institutions, hospitals, and various Non-Governmental Organizations concerned with Women and Children in the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.
2. The Regional Government is responsible for:
a. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence; and
b. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence according to the financial capacity of the Region.
(3) In implementing the obligations of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) and implementing the responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government prepares a Regional Action Plan for the protection of victims.
(4) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3) is part of the Medium-Term Development Plan.

#### Article 13

The Regent in carrying out obligations as referred to in Article 12 delegates to the PMDP3A Office and/or related Regional Apparatus according to their authority.

## Part Two

Community and Family

#### Article 14

1. The community and family have the following obligations and responsibilities:
a. prevent the occurrence of Violence against Women and Children;
b. provide information and/or report acts of Violence against Women and Children to law enforcement or authorized parties;
c. participate in assisting the handling of victims of acts of Violence;
d. protect victims; and
e. provide emergency assistance.
2. Forms of community and family participation are carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER V

## PREVENTION

#### Article 15

1. Efforts to prevent Violence against Women and Children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the PMDP3A Office;
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:
a. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, as well as educational institutions, health institutions, hospitals, various Non-Governmental Organizations concerned with Women and Children;
b. building community and family participation and concern for the prevention and protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;
c. establishing a violence prevention system that provides a complete and easily accessible information system;
d. conducting socialization about laws and regulations related to the protection of Women and Children who are victims of Violence;
e. providing critical education about the rights of Women and Children as victims of Violence to the community; and
f. opening complaint posts for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

#### Article 16

1. Prevention of acts of Violence against Women and Children as referred to in Article 15, is carried out by the PMDP3A Office and Regional Apparatus that administer affairs in the fields of:
a. education;
b. manpower;
c. health;
d. social;
e. women's empowerment and child protection;
f. mental and spiritual;
g. public order and peace; and
h. communication and information.
2. Prevention of acts of Violence by the PMDP3A Office and related Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out in an integrated and continuous manner.

##### I. GENERAL

Protection from violence that threatens the safety and health of women and children must be provided from an early age. Protection is necessary to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. For example, women and children who are victims of abuse, neglect, eviction and maltreatment by parents or other persons. For women and children experiencing such problems, the government must provide Safe Houses and adequate budget, the role of various parties to help address violence issues faced by children and women including social community organizations, volunteers, social workers, medical personnel, advocates and religious leaders.

The deteriorating condition of children along with their rights and the condition of violence against children and women that frequently occurs in the region should be a common concern for all stakeholders including regional governments to play a role in preventing and addressing such deterioration and violence.

For the regional government of Morowali Regency in facing the universe of conditions above, various actions are taken to fulfill children's rights and to prevent and assist in handling acts of violence against children and women which are endeavored by establishing a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children From Acts of Violence.
