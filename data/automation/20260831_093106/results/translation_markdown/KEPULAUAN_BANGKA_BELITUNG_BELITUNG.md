# REGENT OF BELITUNG,

Considering:  
a. that discrimination and violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and crimes against humanity, and are not in accordance with Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;  
b. that in order to fulfill the constitutional rights of women and children and to improve the quality of life of women and children is one of the mandatory affairs that becomes the duty, authority, and responsibility of the Regional Government;  
c. that violence against women and children in Belitung Regency tends to increase and spread, thus requiring integrated protection efforts;  
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation of Belitung Regency concerning the Protection of Women's and Children's Rights from Acts of Violence;

# CHAPTER III

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

Protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination shall be conducted based on the principles of:
a. respect for human rights;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination;  
d. protection of victims;  
e. empowerment; andf. integration.

#### Article 4

The objectives of protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination are:
a. to prevent acts of violence and discrimination against women and children including trafficking in persons;  
b. to eliminate all forms of violence, discrimination, and exploitation against women and children;  
c. to protect women and children from acts of violence and discrimination in obtaining their legitimate rights constitutionally;  
d. to protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;  
e. to provide services and protection to women and children who are victims of acts of violence and discrimination, reporters, and witnesses; andf. to facilitate and conduct mediation on domestic disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household.

# CHAPTER V

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 6

Women and children victims of acts of violence shall receive the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as human beings; b. the right to recovery of health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by victims;

d. the right to obtain information;  
e. the right to confidentiality;  
f. the right to compensation;  
g. the right to social rehabilitation;  
h. the right to complaint handling;  
i. the right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process; andj. the right to assistance.

##### PROTECTION OF THE RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

##### FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Women and children are a serious concern, considering their position as vulnerable to becoming victims of violence both from family and their environment. Various acts of violence against women and children both within the household and outside the household will destroy the life of families, communities, government, nation, and State, therefore the Government of Belitung Regency is obliged to provide protection to women and children from acts of violence which is an implementation of several laws and regulations, including Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 21 of 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Criminal Act of Trafficking in Persons, Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.  

Protection for women and children from acts of violence is one of the obligations of the Regional Government as regulated in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government. On that basis, the Government of Belitung Regency has made various efforts to protect the rights of women and children from acts of violence, but has not been able to provide protection to women and children from various acts of violence, among others due to the still low understanding of the community regarding the rights of women and children, handling that has not been well coordinated, implementation that has not been sustainable, and so on.  

Therefore....  

D:\PERDA PDF\2-PERDA PERLINDUNGAN HAK PEREMPUAN DAN ANAK.doc 21 Therefore, a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of the Rights of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is needed to provide legal certainty in the protection of women and children from acts of violence.  

The Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of the Rights of Women and Children from Acts of Violence, gives responsibility to the Regional Government starting from the prevention of acts of violence to the handling of victims of acts of violence. In its implementation, the Regional Government cooperates with government agencies, other regional governments and the community. In addition, adequate funding support both from the Government, Regional Government and the participation of the business sector and the community, is expected to eliminate violence against women and children. Through this Regional Regulation it is expected to further implement the rights of children and women and their protection, so that it can be implemented more comprehensively, integrated and continuously by government elements, families and the community as well as other institutions related to child and women protection efforts.

Point a The principle of humanity becomes the foundation of the concept of protection for women and children from acts of violence, which is respect for human rights and the dignity of every citizen and resident of Indonesia proportionally (the second principle of Pancasila).

Point b The principle of justice and gender equality, that gender justice is a process to be fair to men and women. Gender equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role....
