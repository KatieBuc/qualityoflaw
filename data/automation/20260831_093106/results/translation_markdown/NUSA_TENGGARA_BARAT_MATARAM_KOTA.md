# REGIONAL REGULATION OF MATARAM CITY

# NUMBER 4 OF 2012

# CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

# THE MAYOR OF MATARAM,

a. that violence against women and children is an act that violates the rights and dignity of women and children as human beings;  
b. that the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence has not been carried out optimally;  
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a and letter b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the City of Mataram.  
2. Regional Government is the Mayor and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.  
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Mataram.  
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of the City of Mataram as an element of regional government administration.  
5. Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the regional apparatus work unit of the City of Mataram.  
6. Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UKPD, is the regional apparatus work unit of the City of Mataram.  
7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child in the womb.  
8. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person recognized by law as a woman. 9. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children. 10. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center. 11. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.  
12. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or fainting and/or causes death.  
13. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of distrust or severe psychological suffering to a person.  
14. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  
15. Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence.  
16. Victim of acts of violence is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.  
17. Service is an activity and immediate action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.  
18. Assistance is an activity and action carried out by an assistant during the service process.  
19. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.  
20. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of acts of violence to their family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfill their needs.  
21. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.  
22. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.  
23. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organization (NGO) that provides assistance.  
24. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an institution providing services to victims of violence, based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psychosocial and legal services.  
25. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the regional government to provide services in the context of empowerment of women and children victims of acts of violence.  
26. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.  
27. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.  
28. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations. 29. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.  
30. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence is carried out based on the principles of:
a. respect for the rights of victims;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination; andd. the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

1. The objectives of implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence are to:a. prevent acts of violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;  
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children:c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;  
d. provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses; ande. facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household.  
2. The objectives of implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include aspects of:a. prevention;  
b. service and assistance;  
c. reunification; andd. empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

Every victim has the right to receive:
a. protection;  
b. information;  
c. optimal service;  
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage; e. confidential handling;  
f. psychological and legal assistance; and g. guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member.

#### Article 5

Child victims of acts of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 4, also receive special rights, as follows:
a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;  
b. the right to basic services;  
c. the right to equal protection;  
d. the right to be free from various stigma; ande. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 6

Obligations and responsibilities in implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence are the shared responsibility of:
a. regional government;  
b. community;  
c. family; andd. parents.

#### Article 7

1. The obligations and responsibilities of regional government as referred to in Article 6 letter a, include: a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;
b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity; and f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the regional government establishes action programs and activities for the protection of women and children in 1 (one) Regional Action Plan as a basis for SKPD and UKPD in implementing the protection of women and children from acts of violence.
3. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), constitutes part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD).
4. Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 8

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letter b, shall be implemented in the form of community participation.
2. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include: a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and c. participating in providing assistance and/or handling of victims of acts of violence.
3. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 9

The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER V

## DUTIES AND AUTHORITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 10

1. The Regional Government has the duty to carry out efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children, in the form of: a. collecting data and information about women and children victims of acts of violence, and laws and regulations;
b. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
c. conducting education on anti-violence values against women and children; and d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.
2. The Regional Government in anticipating the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, has the authority to provide and organize services for victims in the form of: a. providing and facilitating the establishment of integrated services for victims of acts of violence by involving institutions and community elements;
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims; and c. conducting community empowerment for the prevention of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER VI

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional Framework

#### Article 11

1. In order to provide services and protection to women and children from acts of violence, the regional government establishes PPT as an integrated service center for women and children victims of acts of violence.
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of elements: Health Office, Youth and Sports Education Office, Social Manpower and Transmigration Office, Cooperatives Industry and Trade Office, Population and Civil Registration Office, Women Empowerment and Family Planning Agency, Community Empowerment Agency, Civil Service Police Unit, Mataram City Regional General Hospital, Mataram City Police Resort, Non-Governmental Organizations, and Higher Education Institutions.
3. PPT as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be established by Mayor Decree.
4. PPT may receive case referrals from Sub-districts and Community Health Centers.
5. Further provisions regarding PPT as referred to in paragraph (3) and paragraph (4), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 12

1. In addition to establishing PPT as referred to in Article 11, to support the implementation of empowerment of women victims of acts of violence, the regional government may establish P2TP2A.
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of elements: Youth and Sports Education Office, Social Manpower and Transmigration Office, Cooperatives Industry and Trade Office, Population and Civil Registration Office, Women Empowerment and Family Planning Agency, Community Empowerment Agency, Civil Service Police Unit, Mataram City Regional General Hospital, Mataram City Police Resort, Non-Governmental Organizations, and Higher Education Institutions.
3. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), has the main duties and functions as an institution for empowerment of women victims of acts of violence, namely by providing training to improve capability, skills, and independence.
4. In carrying out the main duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (3), P2TP2A may coordinate and become a working partner of PPT.
5. Further provisions regarding P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), paragraph (3), and paragraph (4), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation

## Part Two

Forms and Mechanisms of Prevention

#### Article 13

Forms of prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children carried out by PPT, may be implemented through:
a. socialization activities of laws and regulations to the community related to the protection of the rights of women and children; and b. training of PPT members related to the implementation of their duties in preventing acts of violence against women and children.

#### Article 14

1. PPT in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with Sub-district PPT, and competent parties in conducting efforts to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
2. Further provisions regarding the mechanism for implementing prevention duties as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 15

Forms of services for victims organized by PPT, include:
a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;
b. medicolegal services constitute a form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;
c. psychosocial services constitute services provided in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence, in resolving their problems;
d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing legal processes; and e. economic independence services in the form of skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

#### Article 16

1. In performing the duty of serving victims of acts of violence against women and children, PPT coordinates with Sub-district PPT.
2. The mechanism for implementing duties as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) which shall be further regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Three

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 17

1. To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the regional government shall carry out empowerment and awareness-raising to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness-raising as referred to in paragraph (1), the regional government shall undertake efforts as follows:a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;
c. opening employment opportunities for women;
d. building community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women and children; andg. opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

#### Article 18

1. Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 17, shall be implemented by SKPD and UKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. education;
d. manpower;
e. women's empowerment and child protection;
f. mental and spiritual matters; andg. peace and order.
2. Prevention of acts of violence by SKPD and UKPD as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in an integrated and continuous manner based on the Regional Action Plan.

## Part Four

Assistance Mechanism

#### Article 19

1. Assistance shall be implemented by persons and/or institutions that cooperate with PPT.
2. The assistance mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) which are further regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Five

Principles of Service and Assistance

#### Article 20

The implementation of services and assistance to victims shall be carried out with the principles of:
a. fast, safe, and empathetic;
b. guarantee of confidentiality;
c. easily accessible; andd. free of charge.

## Part Six

Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 21

1. Forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence, are as follows:a. complaint services;
b. health services;
c. legal aid;
d. repatriation;
e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medicolegal services;
f. identification services; andg. psychological services.
2. Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in accordance with minimum service standards established by the government and implemented by SKPD and UKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. women's empowerment and child protection; andd. mental and spiritual matters.
3. In carrying out the duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (2), the regional government shall cooperate with government agencies, provincial government, other regency/city governments, and the community.
4. Further provisions regarding the procedures for services and handling of women and children victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 22

1. The Regional Government is obliged to provide complaint services, in order to provide protection to women and children from acts of violence.
2. The Regional Government and the community or social service institutions may establish recovery houses or safe houses.
3. Further provisions regarding complaint services and social services as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Seven

Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 23

1. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out empowerment of women victims of acts of violence.
2. Empowerment of victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented by P2TP2A.
3. In carrying out empowerment of women victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the regional government shall prepare empowerment programs in the economic field, which include:a. procuring the needs required for entrepreneurship training for women victims of acts of violence, in order to increase business knowledge, attitudes, and skills;
b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;
c. providing assistance in developing productive economic enterprises;
d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large enterprises, as well as financial institutions to develop businesses of women victims of acts of violence;
e. endeavoring to provide capital for women victims of acts of violence; andf. expanding access to information and promoting the products of women victims of acts of violence.
4. In carrying out empowerment of women victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the regional government may establish cooperation with job training institutions organized by the private sector.

#### Article 24

1. Empowerment of women victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 23, shall be carried out by providing training to improve capability, skills, and independence.
2. Training as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be carried out through:a. on the job training;
b. pre employment training; andc. understudy training.

# CHAPTER VII

## COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 25

1. In order to achieve the objectives of implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence as referred to in Article 3, the regional government shall cooperate with:a. the government;
b. the provincial government;
c. other regency/city governments; andd. non-governmental institutions.
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1), shall include:a. exchange of data and information;
b. rehabilitation of victims of acts of violence;
c. repatriation and social reintegration; andd. provision of evidence and witnesses, and followed up in accordance with applicable law.
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be set forth in the form of a joint agreement.

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 26

1. The Regional Government shall establish partnerships with the business world in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.
2. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be carried out through:a. notification of information on employment opportunities for women victims of acts of violence;
b. education and training for women victims of acts of violence;
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of acts of violence who have been withdrawn from their education; andd. fostering and increasing the economic independence of women victims of acts of violence.
3. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be set forth in the form of an agreement.

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 27

1. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), shall include:a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. provision of facilities;
d. monitoring; ande. evaluation.
3. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be carried out with the principles of:a. professional;
b. transparent; andc. accountable.

#### Article 28

The guidance and supervision as referred to in Article 27 paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), in order to realize the objectives of organizing protection for women and children from acts of violence in accordance with minimum service standards implemented by SKPD/UKPD and the community.

#### Article 29

The community may conduct supervision of the organization of protection for women and children from acts of violence organized by the regional government, with a mechanism for conveying aspirations to the Mayor or DPRD.

# CHAPTER IX

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 30

1. The community may participate in efforts to organize protection for women and children from acts of violence.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), may be carried out by individuals or organizations.
3. Forms of community participation carried out by individuals or organizations, as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2).

# CHAPTER X

## REPORTING

#### Article 31

1. PPT and P2TP2A are obliged to report the implementation of organizing protection for women and children from acts of violence to the Mayor.
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be submitted in writing, covering:a. administration;
b. finance;
c. services; andd. performance.
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be carried out at least once every 3 (three) months.

# CHAPTER XI

## SOURCES OF FUNDS

#### Article 32

Funds for organizing protection for women and children from acts of violence, are sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; andb. other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 33

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or non-governmental organizations that implement protection for women and children from acts of violence.
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be in accordance with regional financial capacity and implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

# CHAPTER XII

## INVESTIGATION

#### Article 34

1. In addition to general investigation officials, investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children in this Regional Regulation, may also be carried out by civil servant investigators as referred to in the Criminal Procedure Code.
2. Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1), are certain civil servant officials within the regional government appointed by authorized officials in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations (3) In carrying out investigation duties, investigation officials as referred to in paragraph (1), are authorized to:a. receive reports or complaints from a person regarding the existence of criminal acts of violence against women and children;
b. take first action at that time at the scene and conduct examination;
c. interrogate a suspect and examine the suspect's identification;
d. seize objects and/or documents;
e. take fingerprints and photograph a person;
f. summon persons to be heard and examined as suspects or witnesses;
g. bring in expert witnesses needed in connection with case examination;
h. terminate the investigation after receiving instructions from the General Investigator that there is insufficient evidence, or the incident does not constitute a criminal act and subsequently through the General Investigator notify this matter to the public prosecutor, suspect or their family; and/ori. take other actions necessary for the smooth investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children, which according to law can be accounted for.
4. Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1), shall notify the commencement of investigation and submit the results of their investigation to the public prosecutor through the investigator official of the Police of the Republic of Indonesia, in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Criminal Procedure Code.

# CHAPTER XIII

## SANCTION PROVISIONS

#### Article 35

1. Any person who intentionally does not provide protection for women and children resulting in the occurrence of acts of violence, allows the occurrence of acts of violence, and/or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as stipulated in applicable laws and regulations.
2. If officials appointed to organize protection, do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities, shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
3. PPT managers who carry out service and protection duties for women and children from acts of violence, violating service principles, may be subject to sanctions in accordance with PPT internal mechanisms.

##### CONCERNING

ORGANIZATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide a sense of security to its citizens from threats and actions that can disturb and damage mental, physical, sexual and economic security. This is philosophically stated in the preamble of the 1945 Constitution, that the purpose of establishing the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia is to protect the entire Nation and the Homeland of Indonesia.

Since the Government of Indonesia has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women), it is obliged to fulfill these provisions. Human Rights state that the enforcement and fulfillment of Human Rights is the responsibility of the State, especially the Government.

Through Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection, the government recognizes the existence of children's rights. The inherent human rights of children include basic rights as human beings, namely the Right to Life, the Right to Growth and Development, the Right to Protection and the Right to Participation.

To fulfill the Right to life, children need food in sufficient quantities, healthy and nutritious, as well as optimal access to health. Protection from violence that threatens their safety and health must be provided from an early age. Protection is needed to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. To fulfill the right to growth and development, children need space to play, exercise, and education that is appropriate to their physical and mental development. In fulfilling children's rights, every government administrator, community and parents must understand and care about children's rights. The existence of legal instruments and law enforcement officials who defend the interests of children is needed for this protection effort. Legislation that is principled on defending the best interest of the child is needed to realize protection that is legal in nature.

Furthermore, it is not uncommon to find cases of women and children who experience problems of physical, psychological, sexual and social violence. For example, women and children who are victims of persecution, neglect, eviction and abuse by parents or others. For women and children who experience these problems, the government must provide a Safe House (children protection home) or Integrated Service Center (PPT) for women and children victims of violence. The Safe House or PPT is a safe place and is friendly for women and children. The PPT is equipped with companions from educators, doctors, psychologists, religious leaders/clergy and networked with lawyers for women and children as well as the RPK Unit at the Police.

The fate of women and children victims of violence must be given attention by the government. The large number of cases of violence against women and children are often caused by factors that develop in society, such as low economic levels, education, environments in the industrial sector. Therefore, victims of violence like this need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights.

For the empowerment of victims of violence, local governments can establish an Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) as a center for empowering women victims of violence which specifically has the main duties and functions for the empowerment of women and children victims of violence.

From the above framework, the Government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, economically and socially to prevent, suppress, reduce, and eliminate all forms of violence against women and children. From these various efforts, a legal framework will be realized in the implementation of protection for women and children from violence.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently Clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a What is meant by "respect for the rights of victims" is a series of actions to respect, appreciate and ensure the fulfillment of the rights of victims.

Letter b What is meant by "justice and gender equality" is a process to be fair to men and women and equal conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, national defense and security activities and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

Letter c What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims by not making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d What is meant by "The best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, must be the primary consideration.

#### Article 3

Paragraph (1) Sufficiently clear.

Paragraph (2) Letter a What is meant by the "prevention" aspect is a protection strategy effort through:
a. Primary prevention, all people, families, communities and the state in efforts to increase the ability of knowledge, understanding and ensure that violence against women and children does not occur, including policy socialization, adequate services, supportive workplace policies, and life skill training for women and children. What is meant by life skill training includes conflict resolution without violence, stress management skills, resource management, making effective decisions, effective interpersonal communication, guidance on the psychosocial development of women and children.  b. Secondary prevention, aimed at community groups with high risk in efforts to increase skills, including training and victim services to ensure that violence against women and children does not occur in the next generation. Activities carried out here include conducting home visits for parents who have just had children to conduct self-assessment whether they are at risk of committing violence against children in the future.  c. Tertiary prevention, intended to increase parenting abilities that ensure that violence against women and children is not repeated, what is done here is integrated services for women and children who are victims of violence, through counseling, stress management training.

Letter b What is meant by the "service and assistance" aspect is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals and assistants in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

Letter c What is meant by the "reunification" aspect is an effort to restore and recover the physical and mental condition of victims which then reunites them with the victim's family and community.

Letter d What is meant by the "empowerment" aspect is a process of increasing the role of the community to participate in the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence which aims to obtain access and participation in the process of protection for women and children broadly.

#### Article 4

Point a What is meant by "obtaining protection" is obtaining protection from individuals, groups and institutions both governmental and non-governmental.

Point b What is meant by "obtaining information" is access and information about the existence of complaint centers, PPT, and all matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and involvement in the decision-making process related to assistance and case developments.

Point c What is meant by "optimal services" is services that include medical, extensive medicolegal, psychosocial and legal services.

Point d What is meant by "continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage" is handling that does not stop at physical and psychological recovery, but until the victim can resume life in society including the restoration of their good name.

Point e What is meant by "confidential handling" is an effort to guarantee certainty for victims that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disseminated.

Point f What is meant by "obtaining psychological assistance" is assistance provided by psychologists to victims suffering from trauma/other mental problems to restore the victim's mental condition. Meanwhile, legal assistance is assistance provided by persons and/or legal aid institutions to victims at every level of examination and throughout the legal process.

Point g What is meant by "guarantee of rights related to victim status" is an effort to provide certainty and protection for victims as members of families and communities.

#### Article 20

Point a What is meant by "prompt" is immediate action taken without complications or simplified procedures.

What is meant by "safe" is a guarantee of service protection that feels comfortable, undisturbed, and served in a friendly, respectful and appreciative manner.

What is meant by "empathy" is actions of appreciating, respecting, caring, being friendly, and making happy that aim to please and comfort the victim's heart.

Point b What is meant by "guarantee of confidentiality" is an effort to guarantee certainty for victims that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disseminated.

Point c What is meant by "easily accessible" is the provision of services and assistance for all people regardless of their social status, so that such services are affordable for the underprivileged or relatively adequate for the privileged.

Point d What is meant by "free of charge" is that service and assistance activities carried out by PPT are not charged to victims.
