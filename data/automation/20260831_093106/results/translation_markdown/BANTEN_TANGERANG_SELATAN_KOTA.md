# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this regional regulation, what is meant by:

1. Regional Government is the Mayor, and regional apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration of South Tangerang City.

8 -

2. Mayor is the Mayor of South Tangerang.
3. Implementation is the form of execution of protection for women and children victims of violence.
4. Woman is a person who has female genitalia, can experience menstruation, pregnancy, childbirth, breastfeeding and includes a person who has obtained legal status as a Woman.
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb.
6. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim.
7. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
8. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.
9. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
10. Economic violence is any act that neglects family members in the form of not providing livelihood, care or maintenance in a proper manner.
11. Psychological violence is any act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
12. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.

9 -

13. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, court, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
14. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
15. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an institution that provides services and protection for women and children especially those who have problems due to acts of violence, discrimination, human trafficking and other social problems in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psycho-social, public facility services and legal protection and assistance.
16. Community Social Institution is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and or other Community Organizations.
17. Population is South Tangerang Residents and foreigners who enter legally and reside in the South Tangerang City area.
18. Service is an action that must be immediately taken to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.
19. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy in order to provide strengthening and recovery for victims of violence as stipulated in a decision of an authorized official.
20. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy and spiritual guidance for the self-strengthening of victims of violence, especially victims of domestic violence, to resolve the problems they face.

10 -

21. Integrated Service Center hereinafter referred to as PPT is a unified unit that carries out integrated service functions for women and children victims of violence, based on hospitals, Community Health Centers, P2TP2A and other similar institutions that are managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psycho-social and legal services.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out based on respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity, non-discrimination, gender equality and justice as well as protection of the human rights of women and children and the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

The Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to provide prevention, protection, and services for women and children against gender-based violence and the best interests of children that occur in the household or public.

#### Article 4

The prevention activities against the occurrence of violence as referred to in Article 3, include:
a. Socialization;
b. Dissemination; andc. Training.

#### Article 5

The protection and service activities for victims as referred to in Article 3, include:
a. Health services;
b. Victim assistance;
c. Counseling;

11 -
d. Spiritual guidance;
e. Resocialization; andf. Empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 6

Every woman and child victim of violence has the right to:
a. Protection;
b. Obtain information;
c. Optimal services;
d. Continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;
e. Confidential handling;
f. Psychological and legal assistance;
g. Guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 7

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement prevention and mitigation efforts against the occurrence of violence against women and children.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented in the form of:a. Collecting data and information about women and children victims of violence;
b. Conducting education on anti-violence values against women and children;
c. Socializing laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence;
d. Training for PPT members;
e. Conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.

12 -
(3) To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government is obliged to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving relevant community elements;
b. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for victims.

#### Article 8

In carrying out its obligations and responsibilities as referred to in Article 7, the Regional Government must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

13 - (5) In the Implementation of Protection for women and children victims of violence which includes prevention and mitigation activities, an Integrated Service Post may be established at the sub-district and village levels.

# CHAPTER VI

## HANDLING AND MECHANISM

#### Article 10

1. The Implementation of Protection for victims shall be carried out in an integrated manner in medical handling, legal protection, medicolegal, psychological and economic aspects, the implementation of which may be carried out through partnerships with social community institutions engaged in the field of protection of women and children.
2. The implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1), must take into account religious norms as well as the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.
3. Principles of Service and Assistance:a. Fast, safe and empathetic;
b. Guarantee of confidentiality;
c. Easily accessible;
d. Free of charge.
4. The mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE AND PROTECTION

#### Article 11

1. Forms of violence against Women and Children include physical, sexual, economic and psychological.
2. Forms of physical violence include any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causing death.

14 - (3) Forms of sexual violence include sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

4. Forms of economic violence include neglect of family members in the form of not providing a proper living, care or maintenance.
5. Forms of psychological violence include acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 12

1. Every woman and child who becomes a victim of violence must receive protection in accordance with applicable provisions.
2. Protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be provided to residents of South Tangerang City whether committed in South Tangerang City or outside South Tangerang City.
3. Protection for women and children victims of violence as an initial action shall also be provided to non-residents of South Tangerang City where the incident occurred in the territory of South Tangerang City, followed by coordination with the region of origin of such residents.

#### Article 13

1. Forms of protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence include medical, legal, medicolegal (forensic medicine), economic and psychological protection.
2. Forms of medical protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.

15 - (3) Forms of legal protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.

4. Forms of medicolegal protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field.
5. Forms of psychological protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include assistance in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving.
6. Forms of economic protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include services for skills and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

3. Any person who intentionally does not provide protection for women and children resulting in the occurrence of violence, allows violence to occur, does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as regulated in applicable laws and regulations.

##### I. GENERAL

Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. The State holds the view that all forms of violence, especially domestic violence, constitute violations of human rights and crimes against human dignity as well as forms of discrimination.

The State's view is based on Article 28 of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, including its amendments. Article 28G paragraph (1) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia stipulates that "Every person shall have the right to protection of self, family, honor, dignity, and property under his/her control, and shall have the right to a sense of security and protection from the threat of fear to do or not do something which is a basic right". Article 28H paragraph (2) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia stipulates that "Every person shall have the right to receive facilities and special treatment to obtain equal opportunities and benefits in order to achieve equality and justice".

Current developments show that acts of physical, psychological, sexual violence and domestic neglect do in fact occur, thus requiring adequate legal instruments to eliminate violence, particularly domestic violence. Legal reform that favors vulnerable or subordinated groups, especially women and children, has become very necessary in connection with the many cases of violence, especially domestic violence; this also occurs in South Tangerang City, in 2010 the cases of violence reported to the Tangerang Police totaled 143 cases, at the South Jakarta Metro Police 56 cases were recorded, Tigaraksa Police - 19 - Tangerang Regency 23 cases and in 2011 70 cases were recorded at the South Jakarta Metro Police and 28 cases at the Tigaraksa Police Tangerang Regency, this does not yet reflect the actual conditions in society, because cases of violence, especially those occurring within the household, are still considered shameful in the view of society and also the courage of the community to report incidents still needs to be increased, therefore a regional regulation on the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence needs to be owned by South Tangerang City.

The emergence of cases of violence against children and women is often caused by factors that develop within society, for example low economic levels, education, social environment and others. Therefore, victims of such violence need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights. From the above framework, the Regional Government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, economically and socially to suppress, prevent, reduce and eliminate all forms of violence against children and women.

In conducting guidance, development and protection of women and children, the role of society is needed, whether through women and child protection institutions, religious institutions, non-governmental organizations, community organizations, social organizations, business world, mass media, or educational institutions.

This Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children from violence is closely related to several other laws and regulations already in force, among others, Law Number 1 of 1946 concerning the Criminal Code and its Amendments, Law Number 8 of 1981 concerning the Criminal Procedure Code, Law Number 1 of 1974 concerning Marriage, Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women, Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human - 20 - Rights and most importantly Law number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence and Law number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.

This Regional Regulation, in addition to regulating matters of prevention and protection as well as recovery for victims of violence, also regulates matters of obligations for law enforcement officers, health workers, social workers, volunteer assistants, or spiritual counselors to protect victims so that they are more sensitive and responsive. To prevent violence, the Agency or Office that has the duties and responsibilities related to the field of women's empowerment and child protection carries out preventive actions, among others, organizing communication, information, and education on violence prevention, protection of women and children.

Based on these considerations, it is time to establish a Regional Regulation in South Tangerang City concerning the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, especially Domestic Violence, which is regulated comprehensively, clearly, and firmly to protect and side with victims, as well as simultaneously provide education and awareness to the community and officials that all acts of violence constitute crimes against human dignity.

#### Article 2

The rights in this provision are intended to guarantee their lives in accordance with human dignity, increase self-confidence, and the ability to participate in community, national, and state life. What is meant by discriminatory treatment, for example, treatment that differentiates ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status, birth order of children, and physical and/or mental condition.

What is meant by "gender equality" is a state in which women and men enjoy equal status and - 21 - have the same conditions to fully realize their human rights and potential for the integrity and continuity of life proportionally.

#### Article 3

What is meant by prevention is all activities carried out to prevent, reduce and even eliminate acts of violence, among others, by organizing communication, information and education. What is meant by services and protection for women and children victims of gender-based violence is that in all service and protection actions, gender equality must be taken into account. What is meant by the best interests of the child is that in all actions concerning children carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies, and judicial bodies, the best interests of the child must be the primary consideration.

#### Article 4

Letter a What is meant by socialization is a process of instilling or transferring habits or values and rules from one generation to another generation in a group or society regarding the prevention of violence.

Letter b What is meant by dissemination is an activity aimed at target groups or individuals so that they obtain information, develop awareness, accept, and ultimately utilize that information.

Letter c What is meant by Training is a series of activities designed to improve the skills, knowledge, experience, or change the attitude of an individual. Training relates to the acquisition of certain skills or knowledge. Socialization, dissemination and training activities on violence prevention can be carried out by related Regional Work Units, related Agencies, - 22 - individuals, groups or Social Community Organizations.

Point d What is meant by spiritual guidance is counseling provided by a spiritual advisor.
