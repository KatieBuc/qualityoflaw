# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Katingan Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Government of Katingan Regency.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Katingan.  
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter referred to as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Katingan Regency.  
5. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  
6. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person recognized by law as a woman.  
7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a Child in the womb.  
8. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public and or in private life.  
9. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens the integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.  

4 -

10. Violence against women is any action or may result in misery or suffering to women physically, mentally sexually or psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or private life.  
11. Victims of violence against women and children are individuals or groups, who experience misery and or suffering as a result of violence and or threats of violence.  
12. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.  
13. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
14. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against women and children will, is or has occurred.  
15. Companion is a person or representative from an institution/organization/agency providing services to victims of violence against women and children who are trained or have expertise in accordance with their competence and authority in providing assistance to victims of violence in accordance with the type of service provided.  
16. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.  
17. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.  
18. Legal Aid is legal services provided by companions and/or legal apparatus which includes providing legal consultation, exercising power of attorney, representing, accompanying, defending and taking other legal actions for victims of violence in accordance with their competence and authority.  
19. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a functional technical implementation unit as the organizer of integrated services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination and other problems.  
20. Standard Operating Procedures hereinafter abbreviated as SOP are standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from handling complaints, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, legal aid, recovery/psychological services, mediation to repatriation and social reintegration.  
21. Minimum Service Standards abbreviated as SPM are performance benchmarks for UPTD PPA services in providing services for handling complaints, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, legal aid, recovery/psychological services, mediation to repatriation and social reintegration.  
22. Social Protection House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE OF REGULATION

#### Article 2

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, is implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;  

5 -
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination;  
d. best interests of victims;  
e. local wisdom;  
f. right to life, survival and development of children; g. respect for victims' opinions; andh. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

Protection of Women and Children victims of violence aims to:
a. prevent all forms of violence against women and children; b. protect women and children and provide services to women and Children victims of violence; andc. empower women and Children victims of violence.

#### Article 4

The Scope of Regulation for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence includes:
a. victims' rights;  
b. authority, obligations and responsibilities;  
c. institutions;  
d. minimum service standards;  
e. social protection house;  
f. reporting;  
g. guidance and supervision;  
h. community participation; andi. funding.

# CHAPTER III

## VICTIMS' RIGHTS

#### Article 5

Every Woman and Child victim of violence has the right:
a. to have their dignity respected as human beings;  
b. to receive protection from family, community, Regional Government and other parties either temporarily or based on the determination of a Protection order from the Court;  
c. to receive recovery health services physically, psychologically and sexually according to the suffering experienced by victims of violence;  
d. to receive special handling related to victim confidentiality; e. to receive assistance from companions and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
f. to receive spiritual guidance services.

#### Article 6

Child victims of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 5, also receive special rights as follows:
a. respect for survival, growth and development; b. to education, health and access to parents during the handling process; andc. to receive equal protection related to status, citizenship, race, skin color, gender, language, religion, politics, disability, birth or other status.

# CHAPTER IV

## AUTHORITY, OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 7

1. The Regional Government has the authority and responsibility for the Protection of Women and Children victims of violence.  
2. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) include:a. guaranteeing the rights of women and children without discrimination in accordance with laws and regulations;  
b. implementing policies for the protection of Women and Children victims of violence based on laws and regulations;  
c. establishing policies, programs and activities for the protection of Women and Children victims of violence;  
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of violence;  
e. allocating budget in the implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of violence;  
f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of violence;  
g. improving the quality and competence of human resources who provide services/assistance for victims of violence against women and children; h. cooperating with the community and/or community organizations in the implementation of protection of women in accordance with laws and regulations;  
i. taking preventive action in order to prevent violations of the rights of women and children.  
3. The responsibility of the Regional Government in efforts to protect women and children as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (1) shall be carried out by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.

#### Article 8

1. The community may participate and be responsible in services, prevention, assistance, recovery and monitoring of victims of violence.  
2. Community participation and responsibility as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in the form of:a. preventing acts of violence against Women and Children; b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against Women and Children to the competent authorities;  
c. providing emergency assistance; andd. providing protection to victims of violence.

# CHAPTER V

## INSTITUTIONS

#### Article 9

1. The established UPTD PPA may accommodate institutions/work units that provide services for victims of violence such as safe houses, social protection houses for Children, trauma center protection houses, women's social protection houses, shelters and other service activities in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations.  
2. The forms of services carried out by UPTD PPA include:a. handling complaints;  
b. health rehabilitation;  
c. social rehabilitation;  
d. legal aid;  
e. recovery/psychological services;  

7 -
f. mediation; andg. repatriation and social reintegration.  
3. The provision of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in accordance with SPM.

# CHAPTER VI

## MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

#### Article 10

1. SPM is implemented by the Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children with the aim of ensuring that Women and Children victims of violence receive the minimum services needed.  
2. SPM as referred to in paragraph (1) includes services: a. handling complaints/reports of victims of violence against women and children;  
b. health rehabilitation for women and children victims of violence; c. social rehabilitation for women and children victims of violence; d. legal aid for women and children victims of violence; e. recovery/psychological services for women and children victims of violence; f. mediation for women and children victims of violence; andg. repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 11

1. SPM as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (3) has performance indicators including:a. coverage of Women and Children victims of violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers within UPTD PPA; b. coverage of Women and Children victims of violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at Community Health Centers and Hospitals;  
c. coverage of social rehabilitation services provided by trained social rehabilitation officers for Women and Children victims of violence within UPTD PPA;  
d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for Women and Children victims of violence within UPTD PPA;  
e. coverage of legal aid services for women and children victims of violence;  
f. coverage of recovery/psychological services for women and children victims of violence;  
g. coverage of mediation services for women and children victims of violence; h. coverage of repatriation services for women and children victims of violence;  andi. coverage of social reintegration services for women and children victims of violence.  
2. The determination of SPM UPTD PPA performance indicators for Women and Children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes the minimum target that must be achieved.

#### Article 15

c. conduct socialization of the rights of Women and Children independently; d. provide first aid to victims; ande. report to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, family partners, community social institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

##### I. GENERAL

Children are the future of the nation and the next generation of the nation's aspirations, so that every child has the right to survival, growth and development, participation and the right to protection from acts of violence and discrimination as well as civil rights and freedoms.

Likewise, women who play a role as mothers who give birth to healthy, strong, resilient, noble, potential and quality children. Through the role of women and families, society and the state will produce resilient generations who will continue the nation's aspirations.

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Katingan Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, protection must be provided to women and children victims of violence so that it needs to be regulated in a Regional Regulation.

So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the regions, so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, implementation, handling, empowerment, guidance and supervision of the protection of women in Katingan Regency.

#### Article 2

Point a What is meant by "respect and fulfillment of victims' rights" is the guarantee of the fulfillment of the basic rights of victims.

Point b What is meant by "justice" is a state where every person, both men and women, is treated equally and obtains equal opportunities to achieve welfare.

What is meant by "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men, including in accessing employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors. i -11 - Point c What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims by not making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and class.

Point d What is meant by "the best interests of the victim" is all the best actions concerning victims carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, so the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Point e What is meant by "local wisdom" is good cultural values that exist in Katingan society.

Point f What is meant by "the right to life, survival and development of children" is a moral principle that every person has the right to live, maintain life and livelihood, and improve their standard of living.

Point g What is meant by "respect for the victim's opinion" is that the government recognizes and ensures that victims can express their opinions and are given the opportunity to express their views freely on everything that affects them.

Point h What is meant by "legal certainty" is the existence of legal norms that do not conflict with other regulations and provide legal security for victims of violence from the arbitrariness of certain parties.
