REGIONAL REGULATION OF PURWOREJO REGENCY

NUMBER 13 OF 2022

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

THE REGENT OF PURWOREJO,

Considering:
a. that the objective of the state is to protect the entire homeland of the Indonesian Nation by providing guarantees for security, equality, and welfare;
b. that women and children are still in a vulnerable position from various forms of violence, exploitation, discrimination, mistreatment and neglect so that strategic efforts are needed from the Regional Government together with related parties to provide systematic and comprehensive protection;
c. that to provide a legal basis and guidelines in the implementation of protection of women and children in the Region, there is a need for integrated and comprehensive regulation to realize Purworejo Regency as women and child friendly;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;

In View of:
1. Article 18 Paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

2. Law Number 13 of 1950 concerning Formation of Regency Regions within the Province of Central Java (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1950 Number 42);
3. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587), as amended several times, most recently by Law Number 11 of 2020 concerning Job Creation (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2020 Number 245, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6573);

With Mutual Approval of THE REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF PURWOREJO REGENCY And

THE REGENT OF PURWOREJO

HAS DECIDED:

To Establish: REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN.

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Purworejo Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous Region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Purworejo.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of governmental affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Technical Regional Apparatus is the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
6. Village is a village and customary village or called by another name, hereinafter referred to as Village, is a legal community unit that has territorial boundaries authorized to regulate and manage governmental affairs, the interests of the local community based on community initiative, rights of origin, and or traditional rights that are recognized and respected in the government system of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia and located in Purworejo Regency.
7. Village Government is the Village Head or called by another name who is assisted by village apparatus as an element of Village Government administration.
8. Sub-district is part of the territory of the District as a District apparatus.
9. Female Victim is a woman who experiences suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, economically, socially, and other losses caused by violence, exploitation and/or gender-based discrimination whether occurring in the public sphere or in the sphere of personal life.
10. Child Victim is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb who experiences suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, economically, socially, and other losses caused by violence, exploitation and/or discrimination whether occurring in the public sphere or in the sphere of personal life.
11. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
12. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
13. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion either direct or indirect based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individual and collective in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural fields and other aspects of life.
14. Violence is any act of abuse of physical force with or without the use of means, unlawfully and causing misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.
15. Exploitation is an act with or without the consent of the victim which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or Services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.
16. Child Maltreatment is all forms of violence against children committed by those who should be responsible and/or those who have power over children, who should be trusted namely parents, close family, teachers, supervisors, law enforcement officers, caregivers and companions.
17. Prevention is all efforts to prevent Discrimination, Violence, and Exploitation, as well as recurrence of occurrences against Women and Children.
18. Service is all efforts and actions to fulfill the rights of Female Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and neglect which include complaint Services, health, social rehabilitation, legal assistance and enforcement, repatriation, social reintegration, and restitution.
19. Assistance is all efforts and actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for self-empowerment of Female Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and neglect of Women and Children to resolve the problems faced so as to provide a sense of security and comfort and have readiness and/or ability to obtain Services and live their lives.
20. Companion is a person or group or organization that is trusted and/or has the skills and knowledge to accompany Female Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and Exploitation with the aim that Female Victims and Child Victims feel safe and comfortable in accessing Services.
21. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a Regional technical implementation unit established by the Regional Government in providing services for Female Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment, neglect and other problems.
22. Forum for the Protection of Violence Victims hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK is a coordination forum for Prevention, handling and empowerment of Female Victims and Child Victims of Violence and other problems whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner and established at the Regional, District, and Village levels.
23. Integrated Service Center is a non-structural institution that coordinates the implementation of an integrated Service system for Female Victims and Child Victims.
24. Social Reintegration is all efforts to strengthen the readiness and ability of Female Victims and Child Victims to regain their welfare in social, political, economic, and cultural life with dignity, including strengthening acceptance and support into the family and/or community environment for the protection and fulfillment of the rights of Female Victims and Child Victims.
25. Restitution is compensation payment given to Female Victims, Child Victims and/or the victim's family by the perpetrator based on a court decision that has permanent legal force for material and/or immaterial losses suffered by Female Victims, Child Victims and/or the victim's family.
26. Legal Aid Provider is a legal aid institution or community organization that provides legal aid services based on laws and regulations.
27. Business World is a commercial organization, the entire industrial and goods/services production environment, including State-Owned Enterprises, and Regional-Owned Enterprises as well as the private sector or entrepreneurs and their networks, who will carry out their social responsibilities.

Article 2

The administration of the protection of women and children is based on:
a. humanity;
b. respect and fulfillment of the rights of women and children; c. non-discrimination and non-exploitation;
d. harmony;
e. protection;
f. justice and gender equality;
g. welfare; andh. proportionality.

Article 3

The implementation of protection for women and children is intended to provide a legal basis for implementing integrated and comprehensive protection for women and children and to realize synergy between the Regional Government and the community in protecting women and children as vulnerable groups who are susceptible to becoming victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Mistreatment and/or neglect towards a Women-Friendly and Child-Friendly Regency.

Article 4

The implementation of protection for women and children aims to:
a. prevent all forms of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women;
b. prevent all forms of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Mistreatment of Children, and/or neglect;
c. encourage the reduction of risks of various forms of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect against women and children;
d. provide guarantees of protection for women and children;
e. provide ease in accessing protection services for women and children;
f. realize a social life that is safe, comfortable, peaceful and free from threats directed at women and children;
g. create a social system that encourages respect for women and compassion for children so as to form social security and social protection for women and children; and
h. realize Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Friendly Regency.

Article 5

The scope of this regional regulation includes:
a. rights of women and children;
b. rights of victims;
c. duties and responsibilities;
d. efforts to protect women and children;
e. implementation of Women's Protection;
f. implementation of Child Protection;
g. Integrated Service Center;
h. role of Village/Sub-district Government;
i. institutions;
j. cooperation and partnership;
k. Women-Friendly and Child-Friendly Regency; and
l. data and information system for women and children;
m. awards; and
n. funding.

CHAPTER II

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Part One

Rights of Women

Article 6

1. The Regional Government respects and safeguards the rights of women in their entirety based on laws and regulations.
2. Every woman has the right to:
a. receive equal and equitable treatment;
b. obtain equal and fair opportunities in various fields, including education, employment, politics, and social;
c. obtain complete information for legal protection and assistance;
d. obtain protection and defense from all forms of Discriminatory actions;
e. obtain protection and defense from acts of Violence;
f. obtain protection and defense from all forms of Exploitation; and
g. receive excellent and professional services.

Part Two

Rights of Children

Article 7

1. The Regional Government safeguards and guarantees the complete fulfillment of children's rights based on the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. Every child has the right to:
a. live, grow, develop, and participate reasonably in accordance with human dignity;
b. obtain self-identity from birth;
c. worship according to their religion, think, and express themselves in accordance with their level of intelligence and age, under the guidance of parents or Guardian;
d. know their parents, be raised, and cared for by their own parents and receive guarantees for a good life continuity and outpouring of affection;
e. obtain health Services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual, and social needs;
f. receive protection and defense from Discriminatory treatment, Violence, Exploitation, neglect, cruelty, abuse, injustice, and/or other mistreatment;
g. obtain guarantees of protection from destructive influences;
h. obtain protection from abuse in political activities, involvement in armed conflicts, social unrest, events containing elements of Violence and warfare;
i. obtain protection from being targets of persecution, torture, or imposition of inhuman punishment and freedom in accordance with law;
j. receive legal assistance and other assistance;
k. obtain education and teaching in the context of developing their personality and level of intelligence according to interests and talents;
l. receive protection in educational units from sexual crimes and Violence committed by educators, education personnel, fellow students, and/or other parties;

m. obtain special education and children who have excellence have the right to receive special education for children with disabilities;
n. receive a good environment and food intake for growth and development according to their age;
o. think, express, state and have their opinions heard, receive, seek, and provide information according to their level of intelligence and age for their self-development in accordance with the values of decency and propriety; and
p. obtain time to rest, and utilize leisure time, socialize with peers, play, recreate, and create according to their interests, talents, and level of intelligence for self-development.

CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Part One

Rights of Women Victims

Article 8

1. The Regional Government is responsible for fulfilling the rights of Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.
2. The rights of Women Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. obtain information related to women's protection, victim Service procedures and developments in case handling;
b. report and have their reports followed up, provide statements, and views without pressure;
c. participate in case handling and in efforts to eliminate Violence against women;
d. obtain protection of confidentiality;
e. obtain protection for the security of themselves, their families, and their property from threats;
f. be free from all forms of stereotypes, stigma and other discrimination;
g. obtain the Services and recovery needed;
h. obtain restitution; and
i. form and join organizations;
3. Fulfillment of rights as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in a friendly and professional manner that is victim-oriented.

Part Two

Rights of Child Victims

Article 9

1. The Regional Government is responsible for fulfilling the rights of Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect.
2. The rights of Child victims as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. receive legal protection and Legal Assistance;
b. receive counseling Assistance;
c. receive education guarantees;
d. receive guarantees of personal and family protection;
e. be free from various kinds of stigma;
f. guarantees of housing and living needs for survival, growth and development;
g. protection of personal property or wealth;
h. guarantees of confidentiality;
i. receive rehabilitation; and/or
j. receive restitution.

CHAPTER IV

DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One
General

Article 10

The Regional Government through Technical Regional Apparatus, the community, the Business World, parents and/or guardians have duties and responsibilities in providing protection for women and children so that welfare is realized both physically, spiritually, and socially.

Part Two

Duties and Responsibilities of the Regional Government

Article 11

1. The duties and responsibilities of Regional Government as referred to in Article 10, include: a. making policies to encourage Prevention efforts against various forms of threats to women and children as well as providing comprehensive protection;
b. implementing victim-oriented policies;
c. conducting data collection and data verification related to information on women and children;
d. conducting various forms of cooperation to enhance protection efforts for women and children;
e. preparing supporting facilities and infrastructure; f. conducting guidance and empowerment of women;
g. providing safe houses for children that support development and growth as well as food intake and care; andh. allocating budget according to needs and regional financial capacity;
i. actively involving Village Government, community and Business World in conducting protection efforts for women and children; andj. realizing Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Worthy Regency.
2. The duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve other related Regional Apparatus.
3. The duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out with victim orientation based on laws and regulations.

Part Three

Duties and Responsibilities of the Community

Article 12

1. The duties and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 10 include:a. giving attention to women and children issues as vulnerable groups to acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect;
b. preventing various acts and/or threats of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect against women and children;
c. providing information and/or reporting alleged acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect against women and children to authorized institutions;
d. being part of social security to protect women and children; ande. participating in realizing Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Worthy Regency.
2. In carrying out the duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), they shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Part Four

Duties and Responsibilities of the Business World

Article 13

1. The Business World is obliged to carry out duties and responsibilities as referred to in Article 10 including: a. making company regulations that are friendly to female workers;
b. making policies that prevent and protect female workers from acts of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation in the work environment;
c. providing counseling or socialization in the work environment for Prevention, protection and reporting related to acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women;
d. refusing underage child workers in accordance with laws and regulations;
e. making products that are safe for children;
f. providing corporate social responsibility for women and children protection programs;
g. providing special rooms for breastfeeding mothers or lactation rooms;
h. not involving children in company activities that endanger safety and/or are contrary to laws and regulations;
i. reporting if there are acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect against women and children in the work environment;
j. providing special childcare facilities for certain companies in accordance with laws and regulations; andk. participating in realizing Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Worthy Regency.
2. The Business World shall carry out the duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
3. The Business World that does not fulfill the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be subject to sanctions in the form of verbal warning and/or written warning.
4. In the event that the sanction as referred to in paragraph (3) is not implemented, action may be taken to temporarily revoke the permit and may be continued with permanent permit revocation.
5. Permit revocation as referred to in paragraph (4) shall be carried out by the related Regional Apparatus in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Part Five

Responsibilities of Parents/Guardians

Article 14

The duties and responsibilities of parents/guardians as referred to in Article 10 include:
a. caring for and educating children according to their intelligence based on religious values and Pancasila;
b. providing good and nutritious food intake according to their age;
c. developing children according to their abilities, talents and interests;
d. providing protection from Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, neglect and/or injustice;
e. preventing marriage at child age;
f. encouraging children's ability to express opinions or thoughts as appreciation for children; andg. guiding children who engage in deviant social behavior.

CHAPTER V

WOMEN AND CHILDREN PROTECTION EFFORTS

Article 15

1. Women and children protection efforts shall be carried out by the Regional Government by:a. conducting Prevention of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect against women and children;
b. conducting Prevention of trafficking of women and children;
c. conducting vulnerability risk management;
d. conducting handling and Assistance for Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect;
e. conducting empowerment of Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect;
f. implementing women and children protection strategies; andg. strengthening the capacity of Regional Apparatus and Village Government in implementing protection for women and children.
2. In conducting protection efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may involve:a. family;
b. community;
c. Business World; andd. media.

CHAPTER VI

IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN PROTECTION

Part One

Prevention

Article 16

1. The Regional Government organizes the Protection of Women by conducting Prevention of various acts of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) include:a. conducting socialization or counseling on the protection of women;
b. increasing awareness, participation and concern of the community regarding the impact and legal consequences of acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment and/or neglect through program integration for families, communities, and the Business Sector;
c. internalization of anti-Discrimination, anti-Violence, and anti-Exploitation values through formal education and non-formal education;
d. providing accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement, and social Services;
e. encouraging the establishment of women's forums as a forum for joint learning, support systems and cadre development;
f. guidance and empowerment of women to be able to be independent and defend themselves;
g. creating safe and women-friendly transportation and work environments;
h. building and providing digital-based information systems and media that are easy to access for information, protection requests and reports;
i. increasing the capacity of government institutions through knowledge enhancement, capability enhancement and service improvement; andj. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women.
3. In implementing Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government may involve and/or cooperate with the media, community, Business Sector, and/or educational institutions.

Part Two

Protection

Article 17

1. The Regional Government provides comprehensive protection to women from various acts of:a. threats;
b. Discrimination;
c. Violence; andd. Exploitation.
2. Every form of threatening act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a conducted either verbally or non-verbally that is intentionally directed at women so as to make them feel threatened in life or safety is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
3. Every form of Discrimination act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b conducted leading to gender, racial, or equality injustice so as to degrade the status, freedom and opportunities of women is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
4. Every form of Violence act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c conducted either physically, psychologically, or sexual violence so as to cause trauma to women is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
5. Every form of Exploitation act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d conducted in any form that degrades the dignity, honor, and dignity of women and causes harm to women is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.

Article 18

The Regional Government provides protection to women by:
a. establishing social security;
b. building women-friendly follow-up referral services; andc. providing supporting facilities and infrastructure for the protection of women.

Paragraph 1

Social Security

Article 19

1. Social security as referred to in Article 18 letter a is carried out by the Regional Government together with the Village/Sub-district Government, Business Sector, and community.
2. Social security as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out by:a. reporting if there is information of acts of threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women;
b. implementing protection of Women Victims of acts of threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation;
c. conducting Assistance and empowerment of Women Victims of acts of threats, Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation; and/ord. conducting security against perpetrators of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against Women who have the potential to endanger life safety.

Paragraph 2

Follow-up Referral Services

Article 20

1. Follow-up referral services as referred to in Article 18 letter b are built by the Regional Government to provide protection quickly and appropriately.
2. Follow-up referral services as referred to in paragraph (1), are carried out by including:a. creating and implementing standard guidelines for handling complaints or reports that are excellent and women-friendly;
b. integrating the complaint or report system between Regional Apparatus in the fields of communication, information, public services, villages, empowerment, women, security, legal aid and enforcement;
c. integrating the Sub-district and Village-based complaint or report system;
d. integrating the legal protection and assistance system with each designated legal aid organization;
e. opening digital media-based or digital application-based complaint or report services; andf. integrating the needs of victims for counseling, health services, visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum, as well as other service needs.
3. Follow-up referral services as referred to in paragraph (1) are provided free of charge to victims in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Paragraph 3

Provision of Supporting Facilities and Infrastructure for the Protection of Women

Article 21

1. The provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in Article 18 letter c aims to maximize the sense of security, comfort and peace for Women Victims of acts of threats, Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.
2. The provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:a. provision of temporary safe houses;
b. provision of daily living needs during the Assistance process of handling in temporary safe houses; c. provision of Assistants in the field of health and counseling if needed;
d. conducting empowerment to increase the capacity of women in the future; and e. provision of assistants in the field of mental spiritual.
3. The provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out by the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus.

Part Three

Handling

Article 22

1. Handling of Women Victims and Child Victims aims to provide appropriate action in handling victims and witnesses in acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women and children.
2. Handling as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:a. complaints or reports;
b. protection needs;
c. physical and psychological health;
d. rehabilitation;
e. Social Reintegration;
f. legal aid; andg. Restitution.
3. Handling as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out by the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus.
4. Further provisions regarding legal aid as referred to in paragraph (2) letter f, are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

Paragraph 1

Complaints or Reports

Article 23

1. Complaints or reports regarding acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women shall be made easily, quickly, and accurately.
2. Every person who sees or knows of acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women shall make a complaint or report.
3. Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may be addressed to:a. Village/Sub-district Government;
b. FPKK;
c. Legal Aid Providers;
d. health service facilities;
e. law enforcement apparatus;
f. Regional Apparatus implementing governmental affairs in the field of public peace and order;
g. non-governmental organizations; and/orh. Social Media managed by related Regional Apparatus.
4. Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may be made by:a. direct means;
b. by telephone;
c. by email or direct message to social media; and/ord. by letter.
5. Recipients of complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (3) shall keep the identity of the reporter confidential.

Paragraph 2

Protection Needs

Article 24

1. Protection needs aim to protect the identity and personal safety of the reporter, victim and/or witness.
2. Recipients of reports as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (3) shall protect both the reporter and the victim.
3. In the event that the recipient of the report does not have protection facilities and infrastructure, the recipient of the report shall coordinate with FPKK and/or law enforcement apparatus that have the facilities and infrastructure.

Paragraph 3

Physical and Psychological Health

Article 25

1. Physical and psychological health for Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation is intended to reduce and treat physical and psychological trauma.
2. FKPP shall facilitate or provide Assistance or treatment for Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.

Paragraph 4

Rehabilitation

Article 26

1. Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect who experience trauma shall undergo a gradual rehabilitation process physically, psychologically, and socially.
2. Rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus and/or the community to assist in accelerating the rehabilitation process.
3. The Regional Government shall guarantee the fulfillment of the living needs of Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect during the rehabilitation process.

Paragraph 5

Restitution

Article 27

1. Provision of Restitution shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. The amount of Restitution paid by the perpetrator to Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect or their heirs shall be based on:a. the results of mediation agreement; and/orb. court decision.
3. The Regional Government through related Regional Apparatus shall oversee the Restitution payment process to ensure it is appropriate and on target.

CHAPTER VII

IMPLEMENTATION OF CHILD PROTECTION

Part One

Prevention

Article 28

1. The Regional Government shall make policies and programs aimed at Prevention of various acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect against children.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by:a. conducting socialization or counseling;
b. raising awareness of the impacts and legal consequences through program integration for families, communities, and the Business Sector;
c. internalization of anti-Violence, anti-Exploitation, anti-maltreatment, and anti-neglect values through formal education and non-formal education;
d. encouraging the establishment of children's forums as a platform for joint learning, support systems, and cadre development;
e. guidance and education for parents, guardians, or foster parents regarding parenting patterns according to growth and development, appreciation, and affection;
f. creating safe and child-friendly transportation; g. building and providing digital-based information systems and media that are easily accessible for information, protection requests, and reports; andh. guidance on the capacity of families, substitute families, or care institutions in caregiving abilities oriented toward growth and development and child appreciation.
3. In implementing Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government may involve and/or cooperate with the media, community, Business Sector and/or educational institutions.

Part Two

Protection

Article 29

1. The Regional Government shall provide active and comprehensive protection to children from various acts of: a. threats;
b. Violence;
c. Exploitation;
d. neglect;
e. Child Maltreatment; andf. Injustice.
2. Every form of threatening act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, whether verbal or nonverbal, that is intentionally directed at a child so as to make them feel threatened in life or safety, is prohibited for any reason.
3. Every form of violent act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, whether physical, psychological, or sexual violence, that causes trauma to the child, is prohibited for any reason.
4. Every form of exploitative act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, carried out in any form and not in accordance with the child's developmental age according to general views, laws and regulations or local wisdom and causing harm to the child, is prohibited for any reason.
5. Every form of neglectful act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d that disregards the responsibility as a parent, guardian, or foster parent toward the child, is prohibited for any reason.
6. Every form of maltreatment as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e, carried out by exploiting closeness or abuse of responsibility toward the child so as to cause physical or psychological trauma or is deemed contrary to laws and regulations, is prohibited for any reason.
7. Every form of unjust act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter f, carried out by parents toward children that discriminates or shows favoritism and injustice in equality between males and females.

Article 30

Forms of protection provided by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 29 include: a. reduction of vulnerability risks;
b. establishment of social safeguards; and/orc. provision of facilities and infrastructure supporting child protection.

Paragraph 1

Reduction of Vulnerability Risks

Article 31

1. The Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus or related Regional Apparatus reduces vulnerability risk with comprehensive and well-targeted vulnerability risk management.
2. Efforts to reduce vulnerability risk as referred to in paragraph (1) include:a. facilitation of vulnerability risk reduction implementation;
b. facilitation of justice implementation in accordance with laws and regulations; andc. facilitation of community capacity strengthening.
3. Facilities for vulnerability risk reduction implementation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a, include:a. identification and early detection of potential in the community, institutions or the Business World related to acts of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and child neglect;
b. taking immediate action to mitigate vulnerability risk to children based on identification and early detection results;
c. providing guidance and assistance to parents or guardians identified as experiencing difficulties and/or obstacles in caring for, nurturing, and protecting children; andd. providing guidance and assistance to families identified as experiencing difficulties and/or obstacles in building healthy and democratic relationships.
4. Facilities for justice implementation in accordance with laws and regulations as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b, include:a. use of diversion or mediation approaches in resolving cases of children suspected of being in conflict with the law in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
b. determination of restitution value that must be paid by the perpetrator to the victim in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
c. punishment of perpetrators of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and/or neglect in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations and justice that lives in society;
5. Facilitation of community capacity strengthening as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is carried out including:a. conducting outreach and paralegal training to the community to be able to provide initial advocacy to victims;
b. establishing and fostering children's forums as a space for appreciating children's aspirations and support systems; and/or;
c. providing financial support for advocacy efforts by Legal Aid Providers related to handling children as victims.

Paragraph 2

Social Security

Article 32

1. The Regional Government together with the Village Government, community, and Business World carry out social security.
2. Social security as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:a. reporting if there is information on Violence, Exploitation, neglect and/or Child Maltreatment;
b. protection of child witnesses and Child Victims by separating them from perpetrators in temporary care facilities;
c. Assistance to Child Victims; andd. securing perpetrators of violence who potentially endanger life safety.

Paragraph 3

Provision of Facilities and Infrastructure

Article 33

1. The Regional Government provides supporting facilities and infrastructure to maximize a sense of security, comfort, and peace for Child Victims.
2. Provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:a. provision of temporary safe houses;
b. provision of education, play opportunities and good food intake, as well as daily living needs during the Assistance process for handling in temporary safe houses;
c. provision of Assistants in the health and counseling fields if needed;
d. provision of Assistants in the mental spiritual religious field; ande. providing social security for vulnerable families.

Article 34

1. Victim handling aims to provide appropriate action in handling victims and witnesses in acts of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and/or neglect.
2. Victim handling as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:a. complaints or reports;
b. protection needs;
c. physical and psychological health;
d. rehabilitation;
e. Social Reintegration;
f. legal aid; andg. Restitution.
3. Further provisions regarding legal aid as referred to in paragraph (2) letter f, are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

Paragraph 1

Complaints and Reports

Article 35

1. Complaints or reports regarding acts of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment and/or neglect are made easily, quickly and accurately.
2. Every person who sees or knows of an act as referred to in paragraph (1) makes a complaint or report.
3. Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (2) may be addressed to:a. Village/Sub-district Government;
b. FPKK;
c. Legal Aid Providers;
d. health service facilities;
e. law enforcement officers;
f. Satpol PP;
g. non-governmental organizations; and/orh. Social Media managed by related Regional Apparatus.
4. Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (2) may be made by:a. directly;
b. by telephone;
c. by email or direct message to social media; and/ord. by letter.
5. Recipients of complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (3) must keep the identity of the reporter confidential.

Paragraph 2

Protection Needs

Article 36

1. Protection needs aim to protect the identity and personal safety of reporters, victims, and/or witnesses.
2. Report recipients as referred to in Article 35 paragraph (3) to protect reporters, victims, and witnesses.
3. If the report recipient does not have protection facilities and infrastructure, then the report recipient coordinates with FKPP and/or law enforcement officers who have facilities and infrastructure.

Paragraph 3

Physical and Psychological Health

Article 37

1. Physical and psychological health for Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and/or neglect aims to reduce and treat physical and psychological trauma.
2. FKPP facilitates checking and treatment of physical and psychological health for Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and/or neglect.
3. FKPP facilitates the conduct of visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum on Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and/or neglect for investigation purposes.

Paragraph 4

Rehabilitation

Article 38

1. Child Victims of acts of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and/or neglect who experience trauma undergo rehabilitation in stages both physically, psychologically, and socially.
2. Rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus and/or the community to assist in accelerating the rehabilitation process.
3. The Regional Government guarantees the living needs of Child Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) during the rehabilitation process.
4. During the rehabilitation process, provision of food intake, education, play world, and appreciation for children experiencing trauma as referred to in paragraph (1) is adjusted to age and developmental needs.

Paragraph 5

Restitution

Article 39

1. Provision of Restitution in accordance with laws and regulations.
2. The amount of Restitution paid by the perpetrator to Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and/or neglect through parents, guardians, or the Regional Government is based on:a. mediation agreement results; and/orb. court decisions.
3. The Regional Government through related Regional Apparatus oversees the Restitution payment process so that it is used for the needs of Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and/or neglect.

CHAPTER VIII

INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER

Part One

Service Principles

Article 40

1. Services in fulfilling the rights of women and children are implemented based on the following principles:a. empathy;
b. confidentiality;
c. fast and responsive;
d. humanity;
e. documented;
f. inclusion;
g. impartial;
h. victim-oriented;
i. easy and excellent;
j. professional; andk. sustainability.
2. Provision of services is carried out with due regard to the situation and condition of the reporter, victim, and/or witness.
3. To improve the provision of services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government establishes community-based integrated women and children protection institutions at the Village/Sub-district level.

Part Two

Form and Scope of Services

Article 41

1. The form and scope of the Integrated Service Center to realize the protection of women and children include: a. Complaint or report and protection services; b. Health and rehabilitation services;
c. Legal services;
d. Education and empowerment services;
e. Economic independence services; andf. Spiritual services.
2. Provision of Services as referred to in paragraph (1) is provided to victims and/or witnesses free of charge in accordance with the laws and regulations.

CHAPTER IX

ROLE OF VILLAGE/SUB-DISTRICT GOVERNMENT

Article 42

1. The Village/Sub-district Government is responsible for organizing the protection of women and children within the Village/Sub-district environment.
2. The Village/Sub-district Government may involve Village Guidance Non-Commissioned Officers and/or Community Security and Order Guidance Police to provide protection for women and children.
3. The Village/Sub-district Government may establish special institutions to organize the protection of women and children.

Article 43

1. Forms of protection of women and children as referred to in Article 42 include:a. receiving complaints or reports from residents;
b. providing protection to reporters, victims, and/or witnesses;
c. conducting social security for perpetrators; d. organizing mediation to resolve disputes;
e. imposing sanctions on village officials who commit acts of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women; andf. imposing sanctions on village officials who commit acts of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect.
2. The Regional Government provides guidance to the Village/Sub-district Government in increasing capacity to organize the protection of women and children.
3. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) is in the form of: a. Assistance;
b. education and training; andc. periodic monitoring.

CHAPTER X

INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

Article 44

1. The Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus carries out the protection of women and children.
2. In implementing the protection of women and children as referred to in paragraph (1), it may involve Regional Apparatus that carry out governmental affairs in the fields of:a. community and village empowerment;
b. social affairs, population control and family planning;
c. health;
d. communication and informatics;
e. industry, manpower, and transmigration; f. law;
g. mental and spiritual;
h. population administration and civil registration; and/ori. public peace, order and community protection.
3. The Regional Government establishes UPTD PPA to provide maximum Services and coordinate among Regional Apparatus in organizing the protection of women and children.

Article 45

1. UPTD PPA performs coordination functions with FPKK in organizing the protection of women and children.
2. Implementation of coordination functions with FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:a. coordinating and synchronizing prevention, services, and empowerment for Women Victims and Child Victims;
b. maintaining and developing FPKK in prevention, services, and empowerment of Women Victims and Child Victims in a networked manner and referral system;
c. conducting education on the values of non-Discrimination, non-Violence, non-Exploitation, non-Maltreatment of Children and/or neglect of women and children;
d. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the organization of protection of women and children;
e. collecting, compiling, and presenting data reports on Women Victims and Child Victims;
f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the organization of protection of women and children.
3. UPTD PPA may provide guidance and Assistance to:a. Purworejo women's forum;
b. Purworejo children's forum;
c. women and children protection task force; d. women and children protection working group; e. non-governmental organizations; and/orf. other organizations related to women and children.
4. Guidance and Assistance as referred to in paragraph (3) aims to encourage community participation in organizing the protection of women and children.

Article 46

Further provisions regarding the establishment of UPTD PPA are regulated by Regent Regulation enacted no later than 1 (one) year after this Regional Regulation is promulgated.

CHAPTER XI

COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

Part One

Cooperation

Article 47

1. The Regional Government may cooperate with various parties, both governmental and non-governmental institutions, to realize the protection of women and children.
2. Cooperation with governmental institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: a. central government;
b. provincial government;
c. other regency/city governments; and/or d. other government institutions.
3. Cooperation with non-governmental institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: a. non-governmental organizations;
b. Business World;
c. community;
d. educational institutions; and/ore. media.

Part Two

Partnership

Article 48

1. The Regional Government establishes partnerships with the Business World, educational institutions or non-governmental institutions to organize the protection of women and children in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through:a. socialization and counseling;
b. education and training;
c. legal aid; and/ord. health and rehabilitation.

Article 49

The Regent conducts periodic monitoring in the implementation of the organization of protection of women and children.

CHAPTER XII

SUPERVISION

Article 50

1. The community supervises the organization of protection of women and children and if problems are found, reports them to the Regent.
2. Internal supervision of the Regional Government is carried out by the Regional Apparatus that has the duties and functions of supervising the organization of regional government.

Article 51

The community has the right to provide aspirations in the form of ideas and suggestions to the Regent for improvement of Services and facilities for organizing the protection of women and children.

CHAPTER XIII

AWARDS

Article 52

1. The Regional Government grants awards to individuals, families, groups, the Business World, non-governmental organizations, and/or other institutions that have contributions and important roles in efforts to protect women and children.
2. Awards as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:a. money;
b. business tools or equipment; and/orc. certificates and plaques.
3. Further provisions regarding the requirements and procedures for granting awards as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER XIV

COORDINATION

Article 53

The Technical Regional Apparatus serves as coordinator in organizing the protection of women and children.

CHAPTER XV

FUNDING

Article 54

1. Funding for the implementation of protection of women and children is sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget.
2. In addition to the funding source as referred to in paragraph (1), the implementation of protection of women and children may be sourced from:a. Village Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and/or b. other lawful and non-binding sources of revenue.

CHAPTER XVI

OTHER PROVISIONS

Article 55

1. In the event of changes to the nomenclature, duties, functions, and authority of Regional Apparatus, the accountability as regulated in this Regional Regulation shall automatically remain binding by adjusting proportionally to its duties and functions.
2. In the event of unclear division of duties, functions, and authority as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent shall appoint the most appropriate Regional Apparatus to carry out such obligation or responsibility.

CHAPTER XVII

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 56

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

I. GENERAL

Constitutionally, the purpose of the state is none other than to protect the entire nation, to advance general welfare, and also to educate the life of the nation. This is clearly stated in the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. The phrase protecting the entire nation contains a philosophical as well as moral dimension, where the state without discrimination must be able to be a protector of all its people. This commitment must be realized in various regulations and policies made by the state, so that it is no longer something that can be negotiated.

As is known, the position of women and children is very vulnerable to various kinds of violations including violations of their basic rights. Strategic steps need to be taken supported by policies, such as socializing the importance of protection and at the same time fulfilling their rights both in the family, society and at every level of education. The rights and protection of women and children are basically also mandated in the 1945 Constitution, so it has become the duty and responsibility of the state to protect them. The presence of the state in this context can be realized not only by the central government, but also by Regional Governments.

Regional Governments as representatives of the state have the most strategic position in implementing the protection of women and children. To be able to realize this, Regional Governments need to have a legal basis to be able to encourage various policies that provide protection for women and children. Here, the idea to be realized is to encourage Regional Governments to create programs or policies that are women-friendly and child-appropriate. The purpose is none other than how later in community life, the world of education, and the business world become parts that are mutually synergistic to realize the implementation of protection of women and children.

The Regional Government of Purworejo Regency needs to anticipate the emergence of complexity of problems that occur in fulfilling protection for women and children. Policies implemented must be able to provide a sense of security and comfort for women and children. In addition, policies for implementing protection of women and children must be followed by efforts to strengthen the involvement of Regional Governments, Village Governments, the Business World, educational institutions and the community. With this responsibility, it will be able to build a Society that cares, respects, and values women and children. Efforts to empower and protect women and children in the Region require a legal umbrella that functions as a guide and direction for all parties. For this reason, based on the authority possessed by the Regional House of Representatives and the Regional Government, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children.
