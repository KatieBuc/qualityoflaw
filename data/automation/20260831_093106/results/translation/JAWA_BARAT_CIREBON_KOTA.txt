MAYOR OF CIREBON,

a. that every woman has the right to obtain a sense of security and protection from all forms of violence, exploitation, torture, discrimination, and other mistreatment that degrades human dignity and violates human rights;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Part One

Definitions

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. City Region hereinafter referred to as Region is the City Region of Cirebon.
2. City Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Cirebon.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the region.
5. Agency is a Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
6. Women's Empowerment is an effort to obtain access and control over resources, economic, political, social, cultural, so that women can organize themselves and increase self-confidence to be able to play a role and actively participate in solving problems, so as to be able to build capacity and self-concept.
7. Women's Protection is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
8. Women Victims are women who experience suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, economically, socially, - 4 - and other losses resulting from violence, exploitation and/or gender-based discrimination that occurs both in the public sphere and in the sphere of personal life.
9. Vulnerable Women are women who live in conditions at risk of experiencing violence, exploitation and discrimination due to age, disability, poverty, geography, disaster, education, inequality and other conditions so that they require special protection and support.
10. Prevention is a direct effort made by the City Regional Government to prevent acts of violence against women.
11. Discrimination Against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status on the basis of equality between men and women.
12. Violence Against Women is any act based on gender differences that results in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, economic, social exploitation, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in personal life.
13. Exploitation is an act with or without the consent of the victim which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.
14. Service is all efforts and actions to fulfill the rights of women victims of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women which include complaint services, health, social rehabilitation, legal assistance and enforcement, repatriation, social reintegration, and restitution.
15. Assistance is all efforts and actions to provide a sense of security and comfort for women victims so that they have the readiness and/or ability to obtain services and live their lives.
16. Technical Implementation Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is the UPT for Women's and Children's Protection. 17. Integrated Service is a comprehensive, integrated, cross-sectoral and professional, quality and - 5 - sustainable service delivery system for women victims that includes health, legal, social and economic services.
18. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a non-structural institution that coordinates the implementation of an integrated service system for women victims.
19. Service Institution is an institution that provides protection services for women victims, both government-owned and community service institutions.
20. Social Rehabilitation is all efforts to restore and develop the capacity of women victims so that they can carry out their social roles and functions both in family life and/or society.
21. Social Reintegration is all efforts to strengthen the readiness and ability of women victims to regain their welfare in social, political, economic, and cultural life with dignity, including strengthening the acceptance and support of family and/or society for the protection and fulfillment of the rights of women victims.
22. Restitution is the payment of compensation charged to the perpetrator based on a court decision that has permanent legal force for material and/or immaterial losses suffered by the victim or their heirs.
23. Legal Aid Organization is a community organization that provides legal aid services. 24. Society is individuals, families, communities, religious leaders, community leaders, traditional leaders, business world and corporations, community organizations, social organizations, women's and gender study centers, mass media or other similar entities.
25. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and a collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of women and/or children.
26. Boarding Educational Institution is a formal or non-formal Educational Institution that organizes general education, and/or religious education or integrated education (general education with religious education, or general education with vocational education or religious education with vocational education), in which some or all of its students reside during their education.

Part Two

Principles, Objectives and Scope

Paragraph 1

Principles

Article 2

Women's Protection is based on: - 6 -
a. respect for human dignity; b. non-discrimination;
c. best interests of victims;
d. justice;
e. benefit; andf. legal certainty.

Paragraph 2

Objectives

Article 3

Women's Protection aims to:
a. prevent all forms of violence, exploitation, and discrimination against women;
b. provide services and recovery for women victims of violence, exploitation and discrimination; c. realize the capacity of the City Regional Government in the implementation of Women's Protection from violence, exploitation and discrimination;
d. increase community participation in Women's Protection; ande. realize a social life that is safe and free from all forms of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women.

Paragraph 3

Scope

Article 4

The scope of regulation for the implementation of Women's Protection includes:
a. women's rights;
b. prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women;
c. services;
d. coordination and cooperation;
e. community participation;
f. data and information systems;
g. institutions;
h. obligations of the City Regional Government;
i. control, guidance and supervision; j. awards; andk. financing.

CHAPTER II

WOMEN'S RIGHTS

Article 5

Every Woman has the right to:
a. obtain information related to the Protection of Women, victim service procedures and developments in case handling;
b. report, provide statements and views without pressure;
c. participate in case handling and in efforts - 7 - to eliminate violence against women;
d. obtain protection of confidentiality; e. obtain protection for the security of self, family and property from threats and violence;
f. be free from all forms of stereotypes, stigma and other discrimination;
g. obtain services and recovery needed; h. obtain restitution; and i. form and join organizations.

f. women heads of households;

h. home-based women workers;
i. elderly women;
j. informal women workers;

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN FROM VIOLENCE, EXPLOITATION AND DISCRIMINATION

I. GENERAL

Violence against women still occurs in society today. Various forms of such violence include rape, sexual harassment, human trafficking, domestic violence, sexual exploitation, violence against domestic workers, pornography, bullying, cyber bullying, and cyber porn.

This situation requires serious handling because it relates to the quality of human beings and the future of the Indonesian Nation. Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, and Law Number 21 of 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Crime of Human Trafficking, provide obligations to the Government and City Regional Government to implement protection for women. The implementation of protection for women, according to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government and its amendments, includes prevention, referral services for women victims of violence, strengthening and development of women's protection service institutions.

Services for women victims of violence include complaint services, referrals, health rehabilitation, legal assistance and protection, social rehabilitation, repatriation and social reintegration, and encouraging community participation in prevention and recovery efforts. Based on Article 12 paragraph (2) of Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government and its amendments, the Sub-affair of women's protection is a mandatory non-basic service affair. Based on Article 217, Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment and child protection affairs shall be carried out by the Agency.

Special treatment or action towards vulnerable groups of women with the aim of achieving equal rights is not included as an act of discrimination.

Point d What is meant by "justice" is fair treatment given to women and men.

Point e What is meant by "benefit" is the implementation of Women's Protection that provides benefits to women in the form of welfare, equality and justice, security and avoidance of all forms of violence, exploitation and discrimination.
