CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of Victims of Acts of Violence;
b. justice and gender equality;

c. non-discrimination;
d. the best interests of Victims of acts of violence; and e. The principle of the right to life, survival and development,

Article 3

The purpose of protecting women and children from acts of violence is to:

a. prevent violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;

b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children; d. provide services and Empowerment to women and children victims of violence, reporters and witnesses; e. facilitate and mediate domestic disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household; and f. Facilitate the restoration of the rights, dignity and personal values of victims.

CHAPTER III

SCOPE

Article 4

1. The Scope of this Regional Regulation consists of a. protection of women; and b. protection of children.
2. Protection of women as referred to in Article 4 letter a includes: a. prevention of acts of violence;
b. integrated services for victims of acts of violence; and c. empowerment of victims of acts of violence. (3) Protection of children as referred to in Article 4 letter b includes: a. protection of children who are victims of acts of violence; and b. special protection of children.

CHAPTER IV

Part One

General

Article 5

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of violence that occur in public or private life in the form of a. physical violence; b. sexual violence;

c. economic violence;

d. social violence;
e. psychological violence;
f. domestic neglect;
g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and h. threats of certain acts.

Article 6

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence as referred to in Article 5 is obliged to make efforts to:

a. prevent the continuation of criminal acts; b. provide protection to victims of acts of violence; c. provide emergency assistance; and/or assistance in the process of subsequent stages

L GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in North Lampung Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection to women and children victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation to regulate protective measures so that institutional and regulatory support is needed that can guarantee its implementation, especially in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in Lampung Regency

Sufficiently clear Letter a.

What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or causes death. Letter b.

What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Letter c.

What is meant by "economic violence" is an act that restricts a person from working inside or outside the home to earn money and goods, including allowing a person who works to be exploited, while someone does not fulfill the economic needs of the family.

Letter d.

What is meant by "social violence" is an act of a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life to a person or can cause physical damage or damage to another person's property Letter e.

What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Letter f.

What is meant by "Domestic Neglect" is:

1. actions that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
2. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as appropriate; 3. actions that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; 4. actions that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Letter g.

What is meant by "neglect of workers" is the action of any person or legal entity that results in improper treatment of female workers whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide adequate protection from lawful actions against workers such as haphazard recruitment, unclear shelters and workplaces, arbitrary business owners.

Letter h.

What is meant by "coercion or deprivation" of freedom is:

1. Coercion is any act or action of taking, bringing, inviting a child or woman with persuasion and or coercion to be made a wife or to be employed illegally.
2. Deprivation of freedom is any act or action of taking, bringing, inviting, kidnapping a child or woman to be made a wife or employed by confining, detaining or imprisoning illegally in a room or certain place.

Letter i.

What is meant by "threat of certain actions" is any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols or body movements, either with or without using means
