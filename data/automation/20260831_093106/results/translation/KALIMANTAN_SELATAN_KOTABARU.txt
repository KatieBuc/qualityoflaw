BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

a. that women and children with their dignity have the right to be protected from various acts of violence, exploitation, and discrimination that marginalize and degrade their dignity as human beings;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the Regency Region of Kotabaru.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of Regional Government Administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Kotabaru.

5 -

4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Kotabaru Regency.
5. Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit within the scope of the Kotabaru Regency Government.
6. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.
7. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person recognized by law as a woman.
8. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
9. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.
10. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.
11. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or fainting and/or causes death.
12. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of distrust or severe psychological suffering to a person.
13. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
14. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.

6 -

15. Victim of acts of violence is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of violations of human rights (HAM), or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
16. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by Professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
17. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.
18. Assistant is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.
19. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of acts of violence to their family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.
20. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
21. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
22. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organization (NGO) that provides assistance.
23. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the regional government to provide services in the context of empowerment of Women and Children victims of acts of violence.
24. Regional Action Plan hereinafter abbreviated as RAD is a foundation and guideline for relevant offices, vertical agencies, and the community, in the context of carrying out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.
25. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

7 -

26. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
27. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.
28. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
29. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the annual financial plan of the Regional Government which is discussed and approved jointly by the Regional Government and DPRD and stipulated by Regional Regulation.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

1. The implementation of protection of women and children is carried out based on the principles of:a. respect for the rights of women and children;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination in the empowerment of women and children;
d. the best interests of women and children; ande. restoration of social and economic rights for women and children who experience acts of violence or neglect.
2. The objectives of implementing protection of women and children include:a. preventing acts of violence or neglect against women and children including trafficking in persons;
b. eliminating all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children:c. protecting and providing a sense of security for women and children;
d. providing services to women and children victims of acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses;
e. facilitating and mediating domestic disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household; and - 8 -f. seeking compensation/compensation for losses suffered by victims from perpetrators of violence.

CHAPTER III

SCOPE

Article 3

1. Protection of women and children in the region includes prevention, services, and empowerment of victims of physical violence, psychological violence, sexual violence, exploitation, trafficking in persons, and domestic neglect including child deviant behavior.
2. The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence is a joint obligation and responsibility between the Regional Government, community, family, and parents.
3. In the region, P2TP2A may be established which is the result of a joint initiative between the Regional Government and non-government parties.

CHAPTER IV

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 4

1. Every woman and child who becomes a victim of physical violence, exploitation or human trafficking has the right to obtain protection and services.
2. Protection and services for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) include:a. complaints or requests for protection from the person concerned;
b. confidential handling or placement of victims in safe locations and houses whose whereabouts are kept secret to avoid intimidation and threats;
c. medical services/health treatment in the form of care and recovery of injuries or physical condition of victims by medical personnel and paramedics;
d. medicolegal services for evidence in the legal field;
e. protection by law enforcement officers;
f. provision of information regarding legal rights and obligations to victims or guardians of victims and identification of incidents;
g. continuous handling up to the Rehabilitation stage;

9 -
h. psychological assistance to restore the traumatic condition of victims and restore the self-confidence of victims;
i. assistance support for legal processes and obtaining compensation or compensation rights;
j. guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member (social reintegration); andk. economic independence services in the form of skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.
3. In the event that the victim is a child, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in paragraph (2), also obtains special rights including:a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic services;
c. the right to equal protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigma;
e. the right to obtain freedom; andf. the right to obtain compensation from the perpetrator.

CHAPTER V

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 5

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence are the shared responsibility of:
a. regional government;
b. community;
c. family; andd. parents.

Article 6

The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 5 letter a, include:
a. implementing the obligations of protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;
b. establishing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;

10 -
c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. providing shelters for women and children victims of violence;
f. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence according to regional financial capacity; andg. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.

Article 7

1. the obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 5 letter b, are carried out in the form of community participation.
2. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; andc. participating in handling victims of acts of violence.
3. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

Article 8

The obligations of the family and/or parents as referred to in Article 5 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

PREVENTION, SERVICES AND EMPOWERMENT

Part One

General

Article 16

Part Two

Prevention of Acts of Violence, Exploitation and Neglect of Women and Children

Article 17

1. The form of prevention as referred to in Article 16 is carried out through:a. socialization activities of laws and regulations related to the protection of Children and Women to the wider community in the region;

d. opening of public complaint channels by the Regional Government or P2TP2A in each area through community networks;
e. strengthening coordination channels with Sub-district, Village/Sub-district Government;
f. immediate handling of public complaints in all areas of the region and securing subjects of acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking in persons or neglect; and/org. counseling/socialization in the form of raising awareness of the wider community to the smallest community groups (families), especially parents to protect women and children.
2. The Regional Government through P2TP2A conducts data collection on areas of violence based on the incidence rate and conducts research and assessment of the level of vulnerability of areas to find solutions for reducing to stopping acts of violence, exploitation and neglect in vulnerable areas.

Services for Women and Children Experiencing Acts of Violence, Exploitation and Neglect

CHAPTER XII

INVESTIGATION

Article 28

1. In addition to general investigation officers, investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children in this Regional Regulation may also be conducted by Civil Servant Investigators whose appointment is in accordance with the laws and regulations.
2. Civil Servant Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1), are authorized to:a. receive reports or complaints from a person regarding the existence of criminal acts of violence against women and children;
b. take first action at that time at the scene and conduct examination;
c. interrogate a suspect and examine the suspect's identification;
d. seize objects and/or documents; e. take fingerprints and photograph a person;
f. summon persons to be heard and examined as suspects or witnesses;
g. bring in expert witnesses required in connection with the examination of the case;

18 -
h. terminate the investigation after receiving instructions from the General Investigator that there is insufficient evidence, or the incident does not constitute a criminal act and subsequently through the General Investigator notify the public prosecutor, the suspect or their family; and/ori. take other actions necessary for the smooth investigation of criminal acts of violence against women and children, which according to law can be accounted for.
3. The Investigator as referred to in paragraph (2), shall notify the commencement of the investigation and submit the results of the investigation to the public prosecutor through the Investigator Official of the Police of the Republic of Indonesia, in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Criminal Procedure Code.

CHAPTER XIII

SANCTION PROVISIONS

Article 29

1. Any person who intentionally fails to provide protection to women and children resulting in the occurrence of acts of violence, allows the occurrence of acts of violence, and/or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as stipulated in the Laws and Regulations.
2. If the appointed official or P3A Official to administer protection, does not carry out their obligations and responsibilities, shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the Laws and Regulations.
3. P3A Officials who carry out service and protection duties for Children and Women from acts of violence, violating service principles, may be subject to sanctions in accordance with the internal organizational mechanism.

CHAPTER XIV

SPECIAL PROVISIONS

Article 30

1. Handling of legal cases in the investigation process by law enforcement officers must be handled by special investigators who have been appointed according to their competence by law enforcement institutions for the field of protection of women and children.
2. Special investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) are: - 19 -a. in investigation cases for women or female children victims of violence must be handled by female investigators;
b. the implementation process as referred to in letter a must be in a closed room attended only by the victim's parents or guardian along with the advocacy and psychology team from P3A who have the same gender;
c. in investigation cases for male children victims of violence may be conducted by male or female investigators, preferably of the same gender and implemented as referred to in letter b; andd. The trial process is not open to the public.

Article 31

In the case of trial proceedings related to cases of violence against children, the presence of the child does not necessarily have to be presented at the trial, except in circumstances that are very compelling while still separating the meeting between the child and the perpetrator in trial processes at different times.

Article 32

In the case that the perpetrator of a criminal act is a child, the following matters apply:
a. the legal process is carried out humanely by viewing the child as still having hope for their future;
b. The Investigator must be a person who has competence in special investigation for child handling cases; c. Investigation is conducted in a closed room and attended only by the advocacy team and the child's parents/guardian; d. in the investigation process, the child is not placed in general detention, but is entrusted to their family with supervision and custody; e. the trial process is carried out specifically in the juvenile court trial room; andf. the child's punishment process is placed in a special juvenile correctional institution, with a transition period may be accompanied by their parents.

Article 33

The media is not permitted to expose news coverage for cases concerning child victims of sexual violence in order to protect the child's identity and their hope for future life unless the victim as a result of such violence loses their life.

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

I. GENERAL

The role of women is very important because they are the mothers of the nation who are the pillars of the family to build the future of human resources in various fields including empowerment of the family economy, likewise with children who are the generation of the nation who will play a role in filling development in the future.

Children and Women must receive protection from acts of violence, exploitation, human trafficking and neglect by responsible parties. Protection is intended to create a peaceful and prosperous atmosphere for all levels of society in fostering life and togetherness within the regional territory.

Violence cannot be avoided just like that, but must be prevented as early as possible from spreading to a level that is detrimental to all parties. Protection efforts are prioritized on prevention efforts and minimizing incidents.

Acts of violence against Children and Women constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of Children and Women victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region so that institutional and regulatory support is needed that can guarantee its implementation. Regional Regulations regulate protection efforts for victims, especially in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of Children and Women victims of violence in Kotabaru Regency. As one of the efforts to protect Children and Women is carried out through guidance, training and assistance to victims and families of victims of violence. Economic empowerment is also carried out for the prevention of women as victims, which can be done by growing and developing micro and small enterprises in accordance with local potential. To grow micro and small enterprises, it is necessary to increase the capacity and capability of women through assistance and guidance so that women are motivated to start and develop their businesses.

II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

Article 2

Paragraph (1) Point a Respect for the rights of Children and Women constitutes respect for the fulfillment of basic rights for women and children.

Point b What is meant by "justice and gender equality" is a condition where every person, both men and women, is treated equally and obtains equal opportunities to gain access, as well as welfare; Gender equality is the equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

Point c What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment toward victims by not making distinctions based on age, sex, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Point d What is meant by "the best interests of the victim" is all best actions concerning victims carried out by the Government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, thus the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Point e What is meant by restoration of socio-economic rights is increasing understanding, awareness, responsibility, commitment, participation, capability and independence for the purpose of economic improvement and quality of women or target groups so as to avoid problems.

Article 3

Paragraph (1) What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes What is meant by "exploitation" includes:
a. actions that exploit children economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. actions with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.  c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

What is meant by "trafficking in persons" is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.

4 - What is meant by "Domestic Neglect" is:
a. actions that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
b. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as appropriate;
c. actions that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person;
d. actions that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

What is meant by deviant behavior of children such as the use of glue that can cause children to feel addicted or intoxicated because they are invited by their friends, and deviation toward free sex behavior or use of illicit goods.
