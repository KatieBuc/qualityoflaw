REGIONAL REGULATION OF PALEMBANG CITY

NUMBER 9 OF 2022

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

MAYOR OF PALEMBANG,

Considering:
a. that every citizen has the right to obtain protection and guarantee of their human rights, and has the right to receive fair treatment to obtain equal opportunities and benefits in achieving life welfare;
b. that laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children in regulating efforts for women and children to be free from acts of violence in the region require regulations that can guarantee their implementation;
c. that all forms of acts of violence against citizens, especially women and children, constitute a form of violation of human rights so that the state, government, society, and family have the obligation and responsibility for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence;

BAB I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. City is the City of Palembang.
2. City Government is the Government of the City of Palembang.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Palembang.
4. Regional Apparatus is the Regional Apparatus within the City Government.
5. Office of Women's Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment is the Office of Women's Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment of the City of Palembang.
6. Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA, is a Technical Implementation Unit established by the government to provide services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.
7. Women are all women who are both inside and outside the household.
8. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 years including a child who is still in the womb.
9. Act of Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim.
10. Victim is a woman and/or child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of an act of violence.
11. Reporter is a person, family, and community who sees and hears that an act of violence has occurred against a woman or any woman who becomes a victim of an act of violence by another person.
12. Protection of Women and Children in Conflict with the Law is a form of service that must be carried out by law enforcement officers or security officers to provide a sense of security, both physical and psychological to victims or witnesses from threats, disturbances, terror, and violence from any party given at the stage of investigation, inquiry, prosecution and examination at trial.

Article 2

Protection of women and children from acts of violence is carried out based on the principles of:

a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of victims;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. local wisdom;
d. non-discrimination;
e. best interests of women and children;
f. active participation; and
g. restoring the situation.

Article 3

The regulation of protection of women and children from acts of violence aims to:

a. prevent violence against women and children;
b. protect, provide a sense of security for women and children;
c. provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence; and
d. carry out empowerment of women victims of violence.

Article 4

The scope of regulation of protection of women and children from acts of violence includes:

a. forms of acts of violence;
b. rights and obligations;
c. protection of women and children;
d. guidance and supervision; and
e. funding.

BAB II

FORMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

Forms of acts of violence include:

a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect; and
e. exploitation.

Article 6

1. The form of physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is that physical violence is any act that results in pain according to medical experts, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.
2. The form of psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is that psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, a sense of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
3. The form of sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is that sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, in an unnatural manner or not desired by one party for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
4. The form of neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is that neglect is an act of releasing and or abandoning the responsibility of attention, care, education and livelihood towards every person who is a family member.
5. The form of exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is that exploitation is utilizing all forms of potential both physical, psychological and intelligence in women or children for economic gain or personal pleasure of the perpetrator.

BAB III

RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS

Part One

Rights of Women and Children

Article 7

Women and children from acts of violence receive the following rights:

a. the right to have their dignity respected as human beings;
b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by victims;
c. the right to determine their own decisions;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidentiality of their identity;
f. the right to restitution;

h. the right to complaint handling;
i. the right of children to education;

j. the right to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and
k. the right to assistance.

Part Two

Obligations of Government, Community and Family

Paragraph 1

City Government

Article 8

1. In efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence, the City Government is obliged to:

a. guarantee the rights of women and children without discrimination in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
b. establish and implement policies, programs and activities for the protection of the rights of women and children;
c. facilitate the formation of community organizations concerned with women and children;
d. cooperate with the community and/or community organizations and the private sector in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. carry out rehabilitation and empowerment of women and children who are victims of acts of violence;
f. provide special protection to women and children victims of various acts of violence; and
g. take preventive actions in order to prevent violations of the rights of women and children.

(2) The responsibility in efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by regional apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.
3. Further provisions regarding the obligations of the City Government as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

Paragraph 2

Community

Article 9

1. Participate in prevention and provide support for the development of prevention programs against violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence.
2. Provide information and/or report violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence to the competent authorities.
3. Monitor the law enforcement process regarding cases of violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence.

8 - (4) Small group units in society consisting of religious scholars, intellectuals, customary community units are responsible for protecting and preventing violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence.

5. Community Participation referred to in paragraph (1) means that community responsibility is based on laws and regulations and the values of local wisdom of the local community.

Paragraph 3

Family

Article 10

The family in efforts to protect the rights of women and children is responsible for:
a. protecting women and children from conflict and violence in the household that degrades the dignity of women and children;
b. providing equal opportunities for women and children to develop themselves in the fields of education, social, economic, political and cultural matters; and
c. preventing violations of the rights of women and children in the household.

CHAPTER IV

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Part One

Prevention

Article 11

( 1) In order to prevent violations of the rights of women and children, the City Government:
a. conducts socialization regarding the prevention of violence against women and children by optimizing the role of community units, community leaders, religious leaders, community organizations and mass media;
b. provides guidelines on handling acts of violence against women and children;
c. develops an integrated database and information system on acts of violence against women and children;
d. strengthens the function of community organizations engaged in the protection and advocacy of women and children; and
e. provides public facilities that are comfortable and safe for women and children.
2. The responsibility of the City Government in efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph ( 1) is carried out by regional apparatus for women and children in accordance with their duties and functions.
3. Further provisions regarding the planning of prevention of violations of the rights of women and children are integrated by the City Government into regional development planning.

Article 12

In order to prevent violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence, the community: . . .
a. actively participates in monitoring various conditions that occur in their environment that may lead to violations of the rights of women and children;
b. strengthens the role of community organizations, religious organizations and the family environment; and
c. prevents unregistered marriages and child marriages.

Article 13

In order to prevent violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence, the family:
a. protects women and children from various violations of rights in the household;
b. encourages the formation of a harmonious, educative, independent and responsible family; and
c. serves as a place for initial counseling and mediation for every family member suspected of violations of the rights of women and children.

Part Two

Handling

Article 14

( 1) The implementation of handling women and children victims of acts of violence includes:
a. handling complaints from victims of violence;
b. health services; and
c. law enforcement and legal assistance.
2. The implementation of handling women and children victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph ( 1) is carried out through integrated services.
3. Integrated services as referred to in paragraph (2) for women and children victims of acts of violence are carried out with the principles of:
a. responsive;
b. non-discrimination;
c. equal and respectful relationships;
d. maintaining confidentiality;
e. providing a sense of security and comfort;
f. respecting individual differences;
g. non-judgmental;
h. respecting the choices and decisions of victims;
i. sensitive to the background and condition of victims;
J. using language that is appropriate and understood by victims;
k. fast and simple;
l. empathy;
m. fulfillment of the rights of women and children; and
n. legal certainty.

Article 16

2. Reporters as referred to in paragraph (1) may make complaints:
a. directly or by direct discovery;
b. by telephone or the like; and
c. by letter.
3. In addition to handling complaints submitted by reporters, officers at service institutions are also authorized to handle women and children victims of acts of violence obtained through other information.
4. Further provisions regarding the procedures for complaint handling services are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

a. medical services;

c. psychosocial counseling.
