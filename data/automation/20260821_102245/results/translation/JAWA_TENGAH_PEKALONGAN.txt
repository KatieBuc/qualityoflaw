CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Pekalongan Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
3. Agency for Community, Women and Family Planning Empowerment hereinafter referred to as BPMPKB is the Agency for Community, Women and Family Planning Empowerment of Pekalongan Regency as the agency that formulates and implements regional policies that are specific in the field of community, women and family planning empowerment.

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

4. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
5. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
7. Protection against Gender is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, regional government, or other parties who know or hear that violence against men or women will or has occurred either temporarily or based on court determination.
8. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
9. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which include and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
10. Violence against children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which include and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
11. Victims of gender-based violence are people who because of their gender experience physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from State policies, acts of violence and or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
12. Integrated Services are a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unit of implementation, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
13. Companion is a person or institution and/or body that has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
14. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a single unit that organizes integrated services for women and children victims of violence.
15. Medical Services are facilities that provide services that are clinical in nature in the field of diagnostics, and/or inpatient care for victims.
16. Social rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial condition disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning both in the family and in the community.
17. Legal Aid is the provision of legal assistance to victims seeking justice who are unable and face difficulties in the legal field outside and before the court without compensation.
18. Repatriation is the act of returning victims to their region of origin or country of origin while prioritizing protection services and fulfillment of their needs.
19. Social Reintegration is the reunification of victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
20. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the accompaniment process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers of perpetrators.
21. Service Standards are benchmarks used as guidelines for service delivery and reference for assessing service quality as obligations and promises of organizers to the community in the context of quality, appropriate, easy, affordable and measurable services.
22. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in a straight line up or down to the third degree.
23. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:
a. non-discrimination;
b. best interests of victims;
c. justice and gender equality;
d. victim protection;
e. maternal survival;
f. child survival, growth, and development;
g. respect for the child's opinion;
h. transparency;
i. integration;
j. not blaming victims;
k. empowering;
l. victim confidentiality;
m. decision-making in the hands of victims.

Article 3

The purpose of implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence is to provide services and protection to men and women victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence that occurs within the household and/or outside the household.

CHAPTER III

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 4

1. The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to implement efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence in the form of: a. providing data and information on gender and child victims of violence in accordance with laws and regulations;
b. conducting education on anti-violence values based on gender and violence against children; and
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection against gender-based violence and child victims of violence.

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

2. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government is obligated to provide and organize services for victims in the form of: a. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving elements of SKPD, Agencies, Institutions, Community Organizations, Communities;
b. providing facilities and infrastructure;
c. increasing the capacity of service provider institutions;
d. conducting coordination and cooperation in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children;
e. conducting monitoring and evaluation.
f. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for victims.

3. The Regional Government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

CHAPTER IV

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

1. Forms of Gender-Based Violence include: a. Physical Violence;
b. Psychological Violence;
c. Sexual Violence;

2. Forms of Violence against Children include: a. Physical Violence;
b. Psychological Violence;
c. Sexual Violence;
d. Neglect;
e. Exploitation;
f. Maltreatment

Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Article 7

Psychological Violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter b is caused by acts that result in

jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to the victim.

Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment both physical and psychological;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should be carried out by parents, guardians, any other party responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obligated to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs

or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/or
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities or indecent acts.

Article 11

Maltreatment as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter f is caused by:
a. all acts or actions whether intentional or unintentional committed by others that make an individual sick or disturbed in their feelings, or obtain unpleasant feelings that make someone sad, disappointed, annoyed, angry and afraid.
b. all sexual violations committed or permitted to be committed against young people by adults or others who are legally responsible for them, including touching children with the intention of sexual gratification or forcing children to touch an adult, sexual intercourse, showing sexual activities to children, pornography or allowing children to engage in sexual intercourse that is not appropriate to their development.

CHAPTER V

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 12

Every victim of gender-based violence and child victims receives the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as a human being;
b. the right to determine their own decisions;
c. the right to obtain information;
d. the right to confidentiality of their identity;
e. the right to complaint handling;
f. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
g. the right to obtain facilities in the judicial process;
h. the right to obtain efforts for repatriation and social reintegration of victims of gender-based violence and children.
i. the right to social rehabilitation.

CHAPTER VI

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Part One

Institutional

Article 13

In carrying out the obligation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT) institution

Part Two

Integrated Service Center

Article 14

1. PPT as referred to in Article 13 is established by the Regional Government by involving Regional Work Units, institutions, agencies and community organizations that have competence in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children.
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is a functional work unit that has the main duties and functions of providing protection to victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence in the form of seeking prevention, recovery, and social reintegration, providing legal protection, conducting coordination and, seeking to increase community participation, as well as monitoring and reporting

Article 15

1. The organizational structure of PPT as referred to in Article 14 consists of: a. Chairperson;
b. Vice Chairperson;
c. Secretary; and
d. Members.
2. The fields in the organizational structure of PPT at least include: a. complaint service field;
b. health service field;
c. legal aid and law enforcement service field;
d. social rehabilitation service field; and
e. repatriation and social reintegration field.

Article 16

The complaints service field as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) letter a has the duties:
a. conducting interviews and observation of the victim's condition;
b. making recommendations for follow-up services;
c. conducting coordination and referrals to services and certain parties and related parties according to the victim's needs; and
d. conducting administration of the complaint process.

Article 17

The health service field as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) letter b has the duties:
a. conducting examination, treatment, and follow-up care for victims;
b. conducting coordination of health rehabilitation implementation and medico-legal;
c. conducting medico-legal examination including collection of evidence from victims and preparation of visum et repertum;
d. conducting supporting examinations and laboratory tests on evidence;
e. conducting consultation with specialist doctors or making referrals; and
f. making case reports.

Article 18

The legal aid service field as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) letter c has the duties:
a. providing legal aid to victims of violence both litigation and non-litigation.
b. making reports on the progress of legal handling.

Article 19

The social rehabilitation service field as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) letter d has the duties:
a. providing psychological assistance and social counseling by psychologists, counselors and social workers;
b. conducting spiritual guidance by spiritual counselors;
c. conducting assistance during the case handling process; and
d. implementation of social rehabilitation is carried out at a "shelter/safe house".

Article 20

The repatriation and social reintegration field as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) letter e has the duties:
a. coordinating and facilitating the victim repatriation process and/or facilitating repatriation referrals to Provincial PPT;
b. facilitating the reintegration process of victims of violence to family, guardians, substitute families, community, educational institutions or social institutions;
c. making progress reports on the repatriation assistance and social rehabilitation process; and
c. conducting monitoring for at least three months after the victim is returned to their family, guardian, substitute family, community.

Article 21

1. The procedures and mechanisms for establishing PPT are regulated by Regent Regulation.
2. PPT Service Standards are regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER VII

EMPOWERMENT OF VICTIMS

Article 22

Empowerment of Gender and Child Victims of Violence is implemented in the form of:
a. Empowerment of gender-based violence victims includes job training, productive economic enterprises and joint business groups with capital assistance.
b. Empowerment of child victims of violence who are not in school is to ensure child victims of violence receive the right to continue their education, and/or receive job training with capital assistance.

CHAPTER VIII

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 23

1. The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children.
2. The implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out daily by BPMPKB.

CHAPTER IX

FUNDING

Article 24

Funding for activities of implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out by the Regional Government is sourced from the Regional Budget, Provincial Regional Budget and/or State Budget as well as other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER X

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 25

1. The community participates in the implementation of protection for victims.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. providing protection for victims;
b. providing emergency assistance;
c. providing advocacy to victims and or the community regarding handling of gender-based violence and child cases;
d. assisting the process of submitting applications for protection determination;
e. conveying information to authorized officials related to cases of gender-based violence and children;
f. organizing strengthening of community groups in handling gender-based violence and children; and
g. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and children.

CHAPTER XI

TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Article 26

In the event of changes to the Regional Apparatus Organization structure, the coordination duties of guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children as stated in Article 23 paragraph (2) shall be carried out by the Regional Apparatus in charge of Women and Child Empowerment.

CLOSING PROVISIONS

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

In order to prevent and address gender-based violence and children in Pekalongan Regency so as to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for gender and child victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation. So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of gender and child victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts at the regional level, so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for gender and child victims of violence in Pekalongan Regency.

Article 2

Point a What is meant by “non-discrimination” is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.

Point b What is meant by “the best interests of the victim” is all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, the community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, therefore the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Point c What is meant by “gender justice” is fair treatment given to both women and men.

What is meant by “gender equality” is a condition and position that describes a harmonious, compatible and jdih.pekalongankab.go.id balanced relationship between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in access, participation, control and benefits in the implementation of development as well as enjoying the results of development in family life, as well as in society, nation and state.

Point d What is meant by “victim protection” is providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family members, advocates, social institutions, the police, the prosecutor’s office, the courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on a court determination.

Point e What is meant by “maternal survival” is ensuring that a mother does not experience death that occurs during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum, whether caused by physical or non-physical conditions Point f.

What is meant by “growth and development” of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.

What is meant by “survival” of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity and the right to enjoy the highest attainable health status.

Point g.

What is meant by “respect for the child’s opinion” is respect for children’s rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.

Point h What is meant by “openness” is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is transparent among integrated service providers.

Point i What is meant by “integration” is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is carried out by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal assistance, psychologists, religious leaders, social workers, police.

Point j What is meant by “not blaming the victim” is an attitude and treatment of not blaming the victim for the occurrence of violence experienced.

Point k What is meant by “empowering” is that every effort given must be able to strengthen the victim, both physically, psychologically, socially and economically.

Point m What is meant by “decision-making in the hands of the victim” is the victim’s right to determine the best choice in resolving their problem.
