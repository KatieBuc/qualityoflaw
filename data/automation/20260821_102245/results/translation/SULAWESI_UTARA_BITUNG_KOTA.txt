BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

MAYOR OF BITUNG,

Considering:
a. that the guarantee of respect, protection and fulfillment of Human Rights for every Indonesian Citizen in the Region is a duty that must be carried out by the Regional Government;
b. that Bitung City as a rapidly developing industrial city as one of the economic centers in North Sulawesi Province, has created many job opportunities so that many people from various areas outside Bitung City come to work within the Bitung City area and have the potential to create social friction which can result in the vulnerability of women and children to become victims of acts of violence;
c. that in the framework of implementing regional autonomy which refers to Article 12 paragraph (2) letter b and

Article 236

of Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government, particularly in the field of women's empowerment and child protection which is included in concurrent government affairs that are the authority of Regional Government, namely mandatory government affairs not related to basic services, the Region establishes a Regional Regulation;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children from Acts of Violence in Bitung City;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the City of Bitung.
2. Regional Government is the Mayor and regional apparatus as elements of the regional government administration.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Bitung.
4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the DPRD of the City of Bitung.
5. Agency is the Agency that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection in the Region.
6. Woman is a person who has the female sex from birth or based on a court decision who is over 18 years of age.
7. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child in the womb.
8. Act of violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.
9. Victim of violence is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering, either directly or indirectly as a result of acts of violence.
10. Protection of women and children from acts of violence is action, deed, activity that includes prevention of acts of violence against women and children, handling services, assistance, recovery, reunification, empowerment of women and rehabilitation of child victims of acts of violence, carried out by the Regional Government, parents, husband, family or community, including therein is the Integrated Service Center.
11. Prevention is the process, method, act to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
12. Handling is the process, method, act to handle women and child victims as a result of acts of violence.
13. Assistance and recovery is all activities in order to assist and restore the physical, psychological condition and health disorders of women and child victims of acts of violence.
14. Reunification is an activity in order to reunite women and child victims of acts of violence with parents and/or family.
15. Empowerment is the process, method and act to empower women victims of acts of violence.
16. Rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological, and social conditions so that child victims of acts of violence can carry out their roles again properly both in the family and in society.
17. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
18. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife or husband and wife and their children or father and children or mother and children or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
19. Parent/guardian is biological father and/or mother or stepfather and/or stepmother or adoptive father and/or mother or guardian based on the provisions of laws and regulations.
20. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is various institutions that provide services for victims of violence, such as the Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children, Safe House, Child Social Protection House, Trauma Centre Protection House, Women's Social Protection House, Shelter and the like.
21. Regional Action Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RAD is the foundation and guideline for related regional apparatus, vertical agencies and the community in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and child victims of acts of violence.

Article 2

The scope of this regional regulation includes: a. acts of violence;
b. implementation of protection;
c. implementation of prevention of acts of violence against women and children;
d. implementation of services, assistance and recovery for women and children who become victims of acts of violence;
e. institutions;
f. reunification;
g. empowerment and rehabilitation;
h. guidance and supervision; andi. financing.

Article 3

The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence is carried out based on the following principles:
a. respect for Human Rights possessed by women and children;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination; dand. the best interests of women and children.

Article 4

The purpose and objectives of implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence include:  a. prevention of acts of violence against women and children;
b. handling services, assistance and recovery for women and children who become victims of acts of violence;
c. reunification; dand. empowerment of women and rehabilitation of children who become victims of acts of violence.

CHAPTER II

ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

1. The Regional Government is obliged to protect every woman and child in the Region from various kinds of acts of violence.
2. Acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect; dane. exploitation.

Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social carried out by parents, guardians or other parties responsible for care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for or take care of a child as they should carried out by parents, guardians or any other party responsible for their care;
c. acts that neglect a wife within the scope of her household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract, he is obliged to provide livelihood, care or maintenance to his wife; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence, by limiting and/or prohibiting the wife from working properly inside or outside the home so that the wife is under the control of her husband.

Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts without the consent of women and children who become victims of exploitation, including but not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits, whether physical/material in nature or non-physical/non-material; and/or
c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of women and children who become victims of exploitation to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

CHAPTER III

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

Part One

General

Article 11

Implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence is carried out by:
a. Regional Government;
b. community;
c. family; and
d. parents.

Article 12

1. The Regional Government implements protection efforts for women and children from acts of violence through:
a. implementing protection policies for women and children from acts of violence applied by the Government;
b. establishing policies, programs and activities for protection of women and children from acts of violence;
c. establishment of PPT;
d. ensuring the implementation of service facilities for women and children who become victims of acts of violence;
e. seeking effectiveness and efficiency for the recovery process of women and children who become victims of acts of violence; and
f. seeking the creation of cooperation and coordination in efforts to recover women and children who become victims of acts of violence.
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, the Regional Government establishes action programs and activities for protection of women and children from acts of violence in 1 (one) RAD as a basis for regional apparatus in implementing protection for women and children who become victims of acts of violence.
3. RAD as referred to in paragraph (2) is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.
4. RAD as referred to in paragraph (2) is further regulated by a Mayor Regulation.

Article 13

1. Forms of community participation in protecting women and children from acts of violence, include:
a. establishing Family Partners at the kelurahan level;
b. establishing Women and Child Protection Units within community organizations;
c. conducting independent socialization of women's and children's rights;
d. providing first aid to women and children who become victims of acts of violence; and
e. reporting to the competent authority if violence occurs in their environment against women and children who become victims of acts of violence.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector and mass media.
3. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 14

Family as referred to in Article 11 letter c has full responsibility to protect women and children from all forms of acts of violence.

Article 15

Parents as referred to in Article 11 letter d have full responsibility to protect women and children from all forms of acts of violence.

Part Two

Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence

Article 16

1. Implementation of protection for women from acts of violence is carried out through activities:
a. prevention of acts of violence;
b. assistance and recovery services for women who become victims of acts of violence;
c. reunification; and
d. empowerment of women who become victims of acts of violence.
2. Implementation of child protection from acts of violence is carried out through activities:
a. prevention of acts of violence;
b. handling services for children who become victims of acts of violence;
c. reunification; and
d. rehabilitation of children who become victims of acts of violence.
3. Implementation of protection as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) is carried out by the Regional Government through the Agency.

CHAPTER IV

IMPLEMENTATION OF PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE  AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN

Article 17

Implementation of prevention of acts of violence against women and children is carried out by:
a. Regional Government;
b. community;
c. family; and
d. parents.

Article 18

1. The Regional Government implements efforts to prevent acts of violence against women and children through:
a. implementation of policies for prevention of acts of violence against women and children in accordance with policies established by the Government;
b. establishment of policies, programs and activities for prevention of acts of violence against women and children;
c. establishment of PPT;
d. awareness raising to families, parents and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling;
e. increasing the quantity and quality of education, both formal and non-formal and informal;
f. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;
g. opening employment opportunities for women;
h. seeking participation and concern of the community to implement prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence;
i. developing and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
j. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children; and
k. opening complaint posts for protection and prevention of acts of violence against women and children.
2. Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by Regional Apparatus that administer governmental affairs in the fields of:
a. women's empowerment and child protection;
b. health;
c. education;
d. manpower;
e. social;
f. mental and spiritual; and
g. peace and order.
3. Prevention by regional apparatus as referred to in paragraph (2) is coordinated by the Agency.
4. Prevention of violence by regional apparatus as referred to in paragraph (2) is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner based on RAD.

Article 19

1. Forms of prevention of violence against women and children carried out by PPT, can be implemented through:
a. socialization activities of laws and regulations related to protection of women's and children's rights to the community; and
b. training of PPT members related to the implementation of their duties in preventing acts of violence against women and children.
2. PPT in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with competent parties in making efforts to prevent violence against women and children.
3. Further provisions regarding the mechanism for implementing prevention duties as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

Article 20

Forms of community participation in preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children include:
a. provision of safe houses and/or shelters;
b. establishment and management of orphanages for children;
c. establishment of child rehabilitation centers;
d. active supervision of violence against women and children and reporting to the competent authorities; ande. providing assistance to victims.

Article 21

Families have the responsibility to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, including:
a. maintaining harmonious family relationships; b. providing assistance to victims;
c. reporting all forms of domestic violence to the competent authorities; dand. assisting the government in efforts to rehabilitate and reunify victims.

Article 22

Parents have the responsibility to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, including:
a. fostering love, affection and mutual respect within the family;
b. providing assistance to victims; and c. reporting all forms of domestic violence in the family.

Article 23

1. Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women is implemented through activities:a. formation of networks;
b. implementation of coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, including the private sector and community organizations; danc. socialization of laws and regulations governing the protection of women.
2. Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against children is implemented through activities:a. formation of networks;
b. implementation of coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns with the community including the private sector and existing community organizations;
c. socialization of laws and regulations governing the protection of children; dand. education for parents.
3. Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) is implemented by involving:a. families;
b. communities;
c. the private sector;
d. community organizations; ande. educational institutions.

Article 24

Provisions regarding the form and procedures for preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children shall be further regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

CHAPTER V

PROVISION OF SERVICES, ASSISTANCE AND RECOVERY FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN WHO ARE VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 25

1. Every woman and child who becomes a victim of acts of violence has the right to receive:a. protection;
b. information;
c. optimal services;
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;
e. confidential handling;
f. psychological and legal assistance; dang. guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member.
2. The Regional Government guarantees the fulfillment of the rights of women and children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1).

Article 26

Children who are victims of acts of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 25, receive special rights as follows:
a. the right to full respect and fulfillment for survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic services;
c. the right to equal protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigma; ande. the right to obtain freedom.

Article 27

1. Handling of children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 25 is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. Provisions regarding the form and procedures for handling children who are victims of acts of violence shall be further regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

Article 28

1. Services for assistance and recovery of women and children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 25 are provided to women and children who are domiciled in the Region and who experience violence within the territory of the Region.
2. Forms of assistance and recovery services as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented in the form:a. empowerment of women;
b. rehabilitation of children;
c. complaint, consultation and counseling services; dand. assistance services.
3. Provisions regarding the form and procedures for assistance and recovery services for women and children who are victims of acts of violence shall be further regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

CHAPTER VI

INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

Part One

Establishment of PPT

Article 29

The provision of services for handling, assistance and recovery for women and children who are victims of acts of violence is implemented by the Regional Government by establishing PPT.

Article 30

1. The provision of services for handling, assistance and recovery for women and children who are victims of acts of violence is carried out in an integrated manner by PPT.
2. In addition to the provision of services for handling, assistance and recovery for women and children who are victims of acts of violence, PPT takes preventive measures against acts of violence against women and children.
3. PPT reports the implementation of the provision of services for handling, assistance and recovery for women and children who are victims of acts of violence as well as preventive measures against acts of violence against women and children to the Mayor through the Agency.

Article 31

PPT consists of elements:
a. regional apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of health;
b. regional apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of education;
c. regional apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of social affairs;
d. regional apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of trade;
e. regional apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of population;
f. regional apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection;
g. regional apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of community empowerment;
h. regional apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of public peace and order;
i. City Police Resort; danj. Non-Governmental Organizations.

Part Two

PPT Counseling Division

Article 32

1. PPT has a counseling division in the provision of assistance and recovery services for women and children who are victims of acts of violence.
2. The counseling division as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:a. legal division;
b. health division;
c. spiritual division; dand. psychology division.

Part Three

Service Mechanism for Handling, Assistance and Recovery

Article 33

1. PPT may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.
2. PPT provides services and temporary protection in the form of Safe Houses.
3. In the event that PPT does not yet have a Safe House, women and children who are victims of acts of violence are referred to PPT that has a Safe House.

Article 34

Forms of PPT services for women and children who are victims of acts of violence include:
a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of women and children who are victims of acts of violence carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;
b. medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;
c. psychosocial services;
d. legal services to assist women and children who are victims of acts of violence in undergoing legal processes; ande. economic independence services in the form of skills training and providing economic access so that women and children who are victims of acts of violence can be independent.

Article 35

Further provisions regarding the Standard Operating Procedures for the Service Mechanism for Handling, Assistance and Recovery of PPT shall be regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

Part Four

Service Mechanism for Assistance and Recovery by Persons and/or Institutions Cooperating with PPT

Article 36

Assistance is carried out by persons and/or institutions cooperating with PPT.

Article 37

The provision of services and assistance to women and children who are victims of acts of violence is carried out with the principles of:
a. fast, safe and empathetic;
b. guarantee of confidentiality;
c. easily accessible; andd. free of charge.

Article 38

1. PPT reports the implementation of protection provision for women and children who are victims of acts of violence to the Mayor through the Agency.
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) is submitted in writing, covering:a. administration;
b. finance;
c. services; andd. performance.
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out no later than once every 3 (three) months.

CHAPTER VII

REUNIFICATION

Article 39

In order to return women and children who are victims of acts of violence to their families, the Regional Government is obliged to: a. provide transportation costs and other needs for women and children who are victims of acts of violence to return home to their families; b. provide and finance accompanying personnel to return women and children who are victims of acts of violence to their families; and
c. coordinate with the Government, Regional Government of the place of residence of women and children who are victims of acts of violence and the Indonesian National Police to return women and children who are victims of acts of violence to their families.

CHAPTER VIII

EMPOWERMENT AND REHABILITATION

Article 40

1. Empowerment of women who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 16 paragraph (1) letter d is carried out by means of: a. job training; and/orb. facilitating the establishment of productive economic enterprises and joint business groups.
2. Job training as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a coordinates with Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of manpower.
3. Job training for women who are victims of acts of violence may be carried out at job training institutions organized by the Regional Government or organized by the private sector.
4. Establishment of productive economic enterprises and joint business groups as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is carried out in coordination with Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of small and medium enterprises.

Article 41

Rehabilitation of children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 17 letter d is carried out in the form of:
a. legal aid services;
b. health rehabilitation services;
c. social rehabilitation services; and/ord. repatriation and social reintegration services.

CHAPTER IX

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 42

1. The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the provision of protection for women and children who are victims of acts of violence.
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. provision of facilities;
d. monitoring; ande. evaluation.
3. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out with the principles of:a. professional;
b. transparent; andc. accountable.

Article 43

Guidance and supervision as referred to in Article 42 paragraph (1) is carried out in order to realize the objectives of providing protection for women and children who are victims of acts of violence in accordance with minimum service standards established by laws and regulations.

CHAPTER X

FINANCING

Article 44

Costs required for the implementation of duties in providing protection for women and children from acts of violence may be sourced from: a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and  b. other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 45

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations and universities that carry out protection for women and children who are victims of acts of violence.
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1) is adjusted to the financial capacity of the Region and is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER XI

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 46

Implementing regulations of this Regional Regulation are stipulated no later than 6 (six) months from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

Article 47

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

I. GENERAL

Bitung City as one of the areas that has become a center of economic activity in North Sulawesi has attracted many people from outside the City area to work in various industrial sectors that exist. Although this has a positive effect on the economic sector, viewed from the security and order side, it will greatly affect the increased risk of the emergence of security disturbances, especially for those threatening women and children as vulnerable parties.

The Regional Government, seeing this as a potential threat that must be resolved, then established this Regional Regulation as a solution to the problem of security threats that arise for women and children. In this Regional Regulation, the form of protection provided by the Regional Government and various parties involved therein is regulated so as to reduce the high security threats to women and children in Bitung City, in addition, institutional arrangements are also regulated that will ensure the implementation of holistic protection so that each party will know its duties at each stage of protection carried out by the Regional Government.
