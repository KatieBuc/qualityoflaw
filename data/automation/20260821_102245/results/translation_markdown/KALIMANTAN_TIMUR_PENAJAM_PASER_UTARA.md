# CONCERNING

# REGENT OF PENAJAM PASER UTARA,

Considering that acts of violence against women constitute

a. violations of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature without discrimination;  

that to prevent and address violence

b. against women, legal protection for women victims of violence needs to be provided;  

that Penajam Paser Utara Regency does not yet have

c. a regulatory basis regarding the implementation of protection for women victims of violence;  

that based on the considerations as referred to

d. in letters a, b, and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women Victims of Violence;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Penajam Paser Utara Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region of Penajam Paser Utara Regency.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Penajam Paser Utara.  
4. Village is a legal community unit that has territorial boundaries authorized to regulate and manage government affairs, the interests of the local community based on community initiative, rights of origin and/or traditional rights that are recognized and respected in the government system of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia.  
5. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a direct line upward or downward and in a collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of the woman.  
6. Service is an immediate activity and action carried out by professionals in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.  
7. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.  
8. Prevention is any action or effort undertaken to eliminate various factors that cause acts of violence against women.  
9. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person recognized by law as a woman.  
10. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against the physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.  
11. Violence Against Women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or hardship for Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom arbitrarily, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.  
12. Victim is a Woman who experiences acts of violence.  
13. Health Rehabilitation is the recovery of a Victim of trafficking in persons from health disorders they suffer.  
14. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction to be able to carry out their social functions properly.  
15. Repatriation is an effort to return Women Victims of Violence to the Family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.  
16. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with the Family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for Victims.  
17. Legal Aid is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocates to carry out the process of accompanying witnesses and/or Victims of Violence against Women and children that are gender sensitive.  
18. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a regional technical implementation unit established by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.  
19. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.  

#### Article 2

The Protection of Women Victims of Women aims to:

a. protect Women Victims of Violence;  

b. provide Services to Women Victims of acts of Violence; and

c. empower Women Victims of Violence;  

d. increase the capacity of women victims of violence in women's empowerment through related Regional Apparatus Organizations, Private Parties, Business World and others.

# CHAPTER II

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 3

Victims have the right to receive:

a. Protection from:
1. Family;  
2. police;  
3. prosecutor's office;  
4. court;  
5. advocates; and
6. social institutions, or other parties either temporarily or based on the determination of a Protection order from the court.  

b. Health services in accordance with medical needs;  

c. special handling related to the confidentiality of Victims; 

d. assistance by social workers and Legal Aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  

e. spiritual guidance Services; and

f. Victims have the right to determine their own decisions regarding which choices will be implemented.

# CHAPTER III

## AUTHORITY AND RESPONSIBILITY

#### Article 4

1. The Regional Government has the authority and responsibility for the Protection of Women Victims of Violence.  
2. The authority as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

a. Service activities for Women Victims of Violence; 

b. monitoring, strengthening of victims of Violence; and

c. capacity building of apparatus and other stakeholders.

# CHAPTER IV

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 5

1. The Regent establishes UPTD PPA.  
2. Further provisions regarding the procedures for establishing UPTD PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 6

1. UPID PPA services include:
   a. providing services and handling as quickly as possible to Victims;  
   b. providing convenience, comfort, safety, and free of charge for Victims;  
   c. maintaining the confidentiality of Victims; and  
   d. guaranteeing justice and legal certainty for Victims.  
2. Further provisions regarding the procedures for UPID PPA Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 7

The operation of UPTD PPA requires:

a. adequate supporting facilities and infrastructure;  
b. implementing officers; and  
c. functional officers.

#### Article 8

1. UPID PPA is tasked with carrying out technical operational activities in its working area in providing services for women and children who experience problems of violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.  
2. UPID PPA in carrying out its duties as referred to in paragraph (1) performs service functions:
   a. public complaints;  
   b. victim outreach;  
   c. case management;  
   d. temporary shelter;  
   e. mediation; and  
   f. victim assistance.  
3. UPTD PPA in carrying out the functions as referred to in paragraph (2) is conducted for basic Services in 1 (one) Regency.

# CHAPTER V

## PREVENTION AND HANDLING

#### Article 9

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to organize Prevention of Violence against Women quickly, in an integrated and coordinated manner.  

2. The organization of Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through the fields of: a. Education;  
b. Public Facilities and infrastructure;  
c. Government and institutional governance d. Economy and employment;  
e. Social welfare;  
f. Culture;  
g. Information technology;  
h. Religion; and i. Family

#### Article 10

Handling of women victims of violence is by providing integrated and coordinated services including:

a. coverage of Women Victims of Violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers in integrated Service units;  
b. coverage of Women Victims of Violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at community health centers and hospitals;  
c. coverage of Social Rehabilitation services provided by trained Social Rehabilitation officers for Women Victims of Violence in integrated Service units;  
d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for Women Victims of Violence in integrated Service units;  
e. coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of Violence Against Women;  
f. coverage of Women Victims of Violence who receive Legal Aid services;  
g. coverage of Repatriation services for Women Victims of Violence; and  
h. coverage of Social Reintegration services for Women Victims of Violence.

# CHAPTER VI

## SOCIAL PROTECTION HOUSE

#### Article 1 1

1. The Regional Government is obliged to establish a social protection house and/ or Safe House or other designation.  
2. The social protection house and/ or Safe House as referred to in paragraph (1) guarantees the implementation of Health Rehabilitation Services, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, and Social Reintegration.  
3. For the implementation of Services as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government may utilize social Protection houses and/ or Safe Houses owned by the community or other social Service institutions.

# CHAPTER VII

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 12

1. The Regional Government conducts monitoring to ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated steps in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women Victims of Violence.  
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women Victims of Violence in the Region.

#### Article 13

1. Monitoring is carried out through coordination and direct monitoring of related Regional apparatus periodically.  
2. Monitoring is carried out from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women Victims of Violence for the current year.

#### Article 14

1. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of Women is carried out at the end of each fiscal year or if required according to needs.  

(2) The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women are used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women for the following year.  

3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## REPORTING

#### Article 15

1. The Regent is responsible for making reports on the implementation of SPM in the field of integrated services for Women Victims of Violence and submitted to the Governor with a copy submitted to the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection and the Minister of Home Affairs.

# CHAPTER IX

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 16

1. The Regent conducts guidance on the development of UPTD PPA. 
(2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: 
   a. provision of implementation instructions;  
   b. guidance;  
   c. supervision; and  
   d. monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of Services for Women Victims of Violence, both apparatus and community.

#### Article 17

The Regent conducts guidance and supervision over the implementation of SPM in the field of integrated services for Women Victims of Violence in the Region.

# CHAPTER X

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 18

1. In organizing Protection for Women Victims of Violence, the community may:
   a. form Family partners at the sub-district/Village level;  
   b. form Women's Protection units within community organizations;  
   c. conduct socialization of Women's rights independently;  
   d. provide first aid to Victims; and  
   e. report to the competent authority if Violence against the Victim occurs in their environment.  

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

##### CONCERNING

##### PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Violence against Women is one form of act that violates human rights. As is known, the rights of Women have been guaranteed and regulated in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia and the Law on Human Rights. Therefore, the protection of Women's rights from violence should be respected, upheld and human dignity enhanced without discrimination, welfare and justice for Women.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or causes the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or hardship for women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the child. The existence of women victims of violence has not received adequate services so that minimum services are needed for victims of violence.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against Women in Penajam Paser Utara Regency so as to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, it is necessary to provide protection for women victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for women victims of violence, particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of women victims of violence in Penajam Paser Utara Regency.
