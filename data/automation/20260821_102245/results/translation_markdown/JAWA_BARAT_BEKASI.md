# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Regency Region is the Regency Region of Bekasi.  
2. Regent is the Regent of Bekasi.  
3. Regency Regional Government is the Regent as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
4. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of Bekasi Regency
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and Regional House of Representatives in government affairs that are the authority of the region.  
6. Woman is a person of female sex starting from infants, adolescents, adults up to the elderly, who has been recognized by law.  
7. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  

Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the Regional Government, community, and/or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.  

8.  
9. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs, which results in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, educational, social, cultural and other aspects of life.  
10. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband, wife, or husband, wife and their child, or father and his child, or mother and her child, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.  
11. Household is husband, wife, children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, milk kinship, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.  
12. Victim is a woman who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence and life vulnerability.  
13. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering either physically, psychologically, sexually, economically and socially as well as discrimination against victims that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.  
14. Violence Against Women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering either physically, psychologically, sexually, economically, socially and discrimination, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.  
15. Domestic Violence is any act against a person, especially women, that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically and/or threats of domestic neglect including threats to commit acts of coercion or deprivation of freedom unlawfully within the scope of the household.  
16. Physical Violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.  
17. Psychological Violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or other psychological suffering to a person
18. Sexual Violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain other purposes.  
19. Women's Rights is a set of rights inherent in a woman or group of people not to experience discrimination, misery or suffering either directly or indirectly committed by state apparatus or by the state or by regional government apparatus or individuals which are natural rights given by God Almighty.  
20. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and or knowing that violence against victims will, is or has occurred.  
21. Integrated Service is a series of activities to provide protection for witnesses and victims of violence carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as a unified implementation of health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance.  
22. Disability is an individual who has physical or mental limitations.  
23. Complaint is a series of actions taken by the organizer of the Integrated Service Center to follow up on reports of acts of violence against victims of violence.  
24. Health Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims of violence from the health disorders they suffer.  
25. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring the psychological condition and developing the abilities of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.  
26. Legal Assistance is legal services provided by companions, law enforcement officers which include providing legal consultation, exercising power, representing, accompanying, defending and taking other legal actions for victims of violence.  
27. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of violence from the receiving area/where violence occurred to the area of origin or family.  
28. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims of violence with family, community, institutions or other social environments that can provide protection.  
29. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA, is an institution established by the Regional Government to provide services to women and children experiencing criminal and civil problems.  
30. Safe House is a shelter for victims during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers of perpetrators.  
31. Standard Operating Procedures, hereinafter abbreviated as SOP, are standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from complaints/identification, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, legal services up to repatriation and social reintegration of witnesses and/or victims.

# CHAPTER II

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

## Part One

Basic Rights of Women

#### Article 2

1. Women have the right:
   a. to life;  
   b. to form a family and continue descendants;  
   c. to self-development;  
   d. to obtain justice;  
   e. to personal freedom;  
   f. to a sense of security;  
   g. to welfare; and  
   h. to participate in government.  
2. Every woman has the right:
   a. to live peacefully, safely, and in harmony, happily, prosperous physically and spiritually;  
   b. to be free from all threats of violence and/or exploitation;  
   c. to live in a family within the bonds of a legal marriage;  
   d. to maintain life and improve her standard of living;  
   e. to have work that she likes and is also entitled to fair employment conditions;  
   f. to obtain education and teaching in all types, levels and paths of education in accordance with the specified requirements;  
   g. to a healthy and clean environment;  
   h. to receive fair wages in accordance with her work that is comparable and commensurate with human dignity;  
   i. to elect and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting according to applicable laws and regulations;  
   j. to obtain special protection in the performance of work or profession against matters that may threaten safety and/or health related to women's reproductive function; and  
   k. to special rights inherent to women due to their reproductive function guaranteed and protected by law.  
1. to obtain legal protection and assistance for problems faced in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations  
   m. to other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
3. Every woman in conditions of disability, old age, pregnancy has the right to obtain facilities and special treatment.  
4. Every elderly woman, with physical and/or mental disability, has the right to obtain care, education, training and special assistance from the Regional Government to ensure a decent life in accordance with human dignity.  
5. The Regional Government endeavors to establish an old age protection guarantee system for elderly women.

## Part Two

Rights of Women with Disabilities

#### Article 3

Every woman with disabilities has the right:
a. to reproductive health;  
b. to accept or refuse the use of contraceptives;  
c. to obtain greater Protection from multiple Discrimination;  
d. to obtain greater Protection from acts of violence, including sexual violence and exploitation;  
e. to obtain education in all units, paths, types and levels of education;  
f. to obtain work and a decent livelihood in accordance with the type and degree of disability, education and ability;  
g. to obtain accessibility in the context of independence;  
h. to obtain rehabilitation, social assistance and maintenance of social welfare standards; and  
i. to obtain equal rights to develop talents, abilities and social life.

## Part Three

Rights of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 4

Every woman victim of violence has the right to:
a. obtain protection;  
b. determine her own decisions;  
c. obtain information;  
d. obtain complaint handling;  
e. obtain assistance;  
f. obtain continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage; and  
g. obtain confidential handling whether from individuals, groups or institutions, both government and non-government.

#### Article 5

Women victims of acts of violence have the right to obtain protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage and confidential handling whether from individuals, groups or institutions, both government and non-government.

## Part Four

Special Rights for the Protection of Women

#### Article 6

1. Women have the right to obtain special protection from matters that threaten their safety and/or health.  
2. Special protection is given to women because their reproductive function is guaranteed and protected by law.  
3. Special protection as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of health services related to:
   a. menstruation;  
   b. pregnancy;  
   c. childbirth; and  
   d. breastfeeding  
(4) Health services as referred to in paragraph (3) may be provided by parties employing female workers in accordance with health service standards.

#### Article 7

1. Women have equal opportunities without discrimination to obtain employment.  
2. Equal opportunities as referred to in paragraph (1) are realized through the fulfillment of women's rights to receive equal treatment from parties that employ.  
3. Parties employing female workers are obliged to provide protection in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
4. Protection that must be provided by parties employing female workers as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER III

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 8

Forms of violence include:
a. Physical Violence;  
b. Psychological Violence;  
c. Sexual Violence; and  
d. Domestic Violence;

#### Article 9

Physical Violence as referred to in Article 8 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, bruising, injury, wounds or defects on a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

#### Article 10

Psychological Violence as referred to in Article 8 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering in a person.

#### Article 11

1. Sexual Violence as referred to in Article 8 letter c is caused by:
   a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;  
   b. sexual abuse;  
   c. coercion of unnatural or unwanted sexual relations;  
   d. coercion of sexual relations with others and/or exploitation of women for commercial purposes and certain other purposes.  
2. Coercion of sexual relations as referred to in article 11 paragraph (1) letter c is excluded for persons with mental disorders or sexual disorders in marital relations.

#### Article 12

Domestic Violence as referred to in Article 8 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that neglect women within the household sphere by a person who is obliged to provide life, care or maintenance to such woman;  
b. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under someone's control; and  
c. acts committed by family members that result in pain, bruising, injury, wounds or defects on a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

# CHAPTER IV

## PREVENTION, PROTECTION SERVICES, AND EMPOWERMENT

## Part One

Prevention of Violence Against Women

#### Article 13

1. The Regional Government undertakes integrated efforts to prevent violence against women in the Region.  
2. The integrated efforts to prevent violence against women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be coordinated by the Regional Apparatus responsible for the protection and empowerment of women.  
3. The prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be implemented by means of:  
   a. conducting coordination, integration, and synchronization of the prevention of acts of violence based on partnership patterns that may involve community organizations, universities, professionals as well as customary heads and/or community leaders;  
   b. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of the rights of women victims of violence;  
   c. establishing an integrated violence prevention system;  
   d. providing ease of access to obtain civil rights, education, health, employment, training and funding, welfare improvement and social services;  
   e. providing education regarding women's rights for the community;  
   f. fostering and developing community participation and concern for the protection of women from acts of violence;  
   g. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system; and  
   h. establishing a cooperation network in prevention efforts from acts of violence with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women.

#### Article 14

1. Prevention of acts of violence against women as referred to in Article 13, shall be implemented by the Regional Apparatus responsible for affairs of:  
   a. health;  
   b. education;  
   c. employment;  
   d. socio-political affairs;  
   e. protection and empowerment of women; and  
   f. peace and order;

# CHAPTER X

## ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS

#### Article 40

1. Any person or institution or entity that deliberately fails to provide protection to women resulting in acts of violence against women, allows violence to occur, and/or fails to report to the relevant agency may be subject to administrative sanctions.  
2. Business Entities or non-governmental organizations are obligated to provide Protection to Women and against Violence in their workplace environment.  
3. Business Entities or non-governmental organizations are obligated to report acts of violence in their workplace environment to the relevant agency.  
4. Violations as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2) and paragraph (3) may be subject to administrative sanctions in the form of:a. verbal warning;  
b. written warning;  
c. temporary suspension from activities;  
d. termination of cooperation;  
e. revocation of registered certificate;  
f. revocation of operational license;  
g. sealing;
