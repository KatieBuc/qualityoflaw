# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One
12- Rights of Women

#### Article 10

Every Woman has the following rights:

a. to live in prosperous conditions physically and mentally;  

b. to live in a family within a legal marriage bond in accordance with the teachings of the religion she adheres to;  

f. to obtain education and teaching in all types, levels and pathways of education;  

to obtain protection from discriminatory acts;  

to obtain protection from economic and sexual exploitation;  

to obtain protection from physical, psychological, sexual violence, and neglect;  

to obtain decent work based on ability, interest and talent while upholding all matters related to her human nature;  

to obtain access in advancing the business she runs in order to create adequate income improvement;  

to obtain work leave while receiving wages/income in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  

to receive special protection in the performance of work or profession against matters that may threaten safety and/or health related to the reproductive function of Women,  

k. to obtain legal information and services according to her needs;  
13- to enjoy a good and healthy environment in accordance with the standards and provisions of laws and regulations;  

to own and manage joint property in a legal marriage in accordance with laws and regulations; and to have other rights attached in accordance with her human dignity based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two

Rights of Children

#### Article 11

Every Child has the following rights:

a. the right to live, grow and develop properly in accordance with human dignity, to participate in all fields of life,; to obtain protection from discriminatory acts; to obtain protection from economic and sexual exploitation;  

to obtain protection from physical, psychological, sexual violence and neglect;  

identity of population and citizenship status;  

f. to obtain a birth certificate;  

g. to embrace religion and worship according to the teachings of religion and belief with the assistance of parental/guardian guidance;  

not to be forced to change religion by foster parents, adoptive parents and by anyone;  

to be recognized and know their parents, to be raised and cared for by their own parents;  

k. to obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual, and social needs;  

to obtain education and teaching in accordance with interests and talents;  

to obtain care, education, training, self-improvement and ability to participate in community and state life for Children with physical, mental disabilities and special needs;  

to express and be heard their opinions;  

to rest and utilize leisure time for self-development outside school hours;  

to obtain other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Three

Special Rights for Persons with Disabilities

#### Article 12

Women and Children with disabilities, have the right to: receive various facilities in carrying out  

a. various public services; and  

b. to receive additional facilities and infrastructure in carrying out activities in public spaces.

# CHAPTER IX

## GENDER AND CHILD DATA SYSTEM

#### Article 187

The Region shall administer a Gender and Child Data System in the context of implementing gender mainstreaming and integrating children's rights into selected data as an insight opener, as well as input for gender analysis and fulfillment of children's rights.

#### Article 188

The administration of the Gender Data System is intended as a reference for the Region in providing and utilizing selected data for planning, formulating, implementing, monitoring, evaluating and reporting policies/programs/activities of development that is gender responsive and child-oriented.

#### Article 189

The administration of the Gender and Child Data System aims to:
a. strengthen and encourage institutional arrangements (regulations, institutions, mechanisms) of the data system disaggregated by sex and general conditions in the Region, which is reliable, can be presented quickly, accurately, comprehensively, and up-to-date;  
b. build or strengthen coordination mechanisms among Regions, in the implementation of collection and processing of selected data; danc. increase the availability and utilization of selected data for planning, implementation, monitoring, evaluation, and reporting of results of policies/programs/activities of development that is gender responsive and child-oriented in the Region.

#### Article 190

In administering the Gender and Child Data System as referred to in Article 187, the Region may establish or develop a forum/working group for selected data.

#### Article 191

The forum/working group for selected data as referred to in Article 190, has tasks including:
a. coordinating data management units, research units, planning units, and reporting units internally and externally in the administration of the gender and Child data system;  

encouraging implementing units/work units to collect and utilize selected data and gender and Child analysis in formulating planning, implementation, evaluation and reporting of gender responsive and child-oriented development activities; encouraging data management units, research units, reporting units to document and disseminate selected data into publications and database systems that are updated regularly;  

conducting monitoring and evaluation of the administration of the gender and Child data system at least (one) time in (one) year; and reporting the results of implementation of the administration of the gender and Child data system to the Mayor and the Institution
