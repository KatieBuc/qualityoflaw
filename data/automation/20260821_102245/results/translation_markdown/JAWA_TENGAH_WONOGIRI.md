# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  1. Region is Wonogiri Regency.  

2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as the organizer of Regional Government.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Wonogiri.  
4. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering whether physical, mental, sexual, and economic.  
5. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which are and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.  
6. Violence Against Children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which are and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.  
7. Domestic Violence is any act against a person, especially women, which results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically, and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty within the scope of the household.  
8. Physical Violence is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causing death.  
9. Psychological Violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.  
10. Sexual Violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes
11. Economic Violence is any act that neglects members in the form of not providing a proper living, care or maintenance.  
12. Victim is a person who experiences violence and/or threat of violence within the scope of the household.  
13. Victim of Gender-Based Violence is a person who because of their gender experiences physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.  
14. Child Victim of Violence is a child who receives harsh treatment whether physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.  
15. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court orders.  
16. Temporary Protection is protection directly provided by the police and/or social institutions or other parties, before the issuance of a protection order from the court.  
17. Protection Order is an order issued by the Court to provide protection to victims.  
18. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including children still in the womb.  
19. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate, optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
20. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, husband and wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.  
21. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother.  
22. Guardian is a person or body who in fact exercises custody as a parent over a child.  
23. Child Rights are part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and fulfilled by parents, family, community, government, and the state.  
24. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.  
25. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the empowerment of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems faced.  
26. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is an institution providing services to child victims of violence, which is managed jointly between the Regional Government and the community in the form of medical care (including medico-legal), psychosocial and legal services.  
27. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes who can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.  
28. Safe house (shelter) is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with specified operational standards.  
29. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural circumstances of society.  
30. Paralegal is an individual, group, association, organization/institution that does not have a legal background who provides assistance to fight for rights and justice in society.  
31. Counseling is the process of providing assistance carried out by an expert to individuals experiencing a problem that leads to the resolution of the problem faced by the client.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The Implementation of Protection Against Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children is carried out based on respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity, non-discrimination, gender equality and justice as well as protection of human rights and the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

The Implementation of Protection Against Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children aims to provide protection and services to victims of gender-based violence and children and ensure the fulfillment of children's rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, the best interests of children and receive protection from violence and discrimination, for the realization of quality, noble and prosperous Indonesian children.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 4

1. The scope of gender-based violence includes:  a. husband, wife, and children;  
b. people who have family relations with the person as referred to in letter a due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, foster care, and guardianship, who reside in the household, and/orc. people who work to help the household and reside in that household.  
2. People who work to help the household and reside in the household as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c are considered as family members for the duration of being in the household concerned.

# CHAPTER IV

## PROHIBITION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Every person is prohibited from committing domestic violence against persons within the scope of their household, by means of:
a. physical violence;  
b. psychological violence;  
c. economic violenced. sexual violence; ore. domestic neglect.

# CHAPTER V

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 6

1. Victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence are entitled to:a. protection from family, police, prosecution, courts, advocates, social institutions or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court;  
b. obtain protection for personal and family security, and property, and be free from threats related to testimony that will be, is being, or has been given;  
c. participate in the process of selecting and determining the form of protection and security support;  
d. be free from ensnaring questions;  
e. obtain information regarding case developments and court decisions;  
f. obtain services that are fast, appropriate, comfortable, and in accordance with needs.  g. health services in accordance with medical needs;  
h. special handling related to victim confidentiality;  
i. assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations and spiritual guidance services;  
j. recovery and social reintegration;  
k. obtain legal, psychological, spiritual, economic, and social assistance.  
2. The rights of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 7

1. The obligations of the Regional Government include:a. preventing the occurrence of violence;  
b. providing protection for victims of violence; c. providing recovery and social reintegration services; d. encouraging and increasing community participation; e. cooperating with service providers in efforts to prevent, protect and recover victims of violence; andf. supervising the implementation of services for victims with service standards involving the community;  (2) To carry out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government has the authority to: a. formulate policies and programs on the elimination of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
b. establish and facilitate the implementation of integrated services;  
c. provide facilities and infrastructure;  
d. increase the capacity of service provider institutions;  
e. coordinate and cooperate in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
f. encourage community participation; dang. conduct monitoring and evaluation.  
3. The Regional Government in carrying out its obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) must take into account the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

# CHAPTER VII

IMPLEMENTATION OF GENDER-BASED PROTECTION AND CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Institutional

#### Article 8

1. The Regent through the Regional Apparatus establishes a public service work unit that functions as an Integrated Service Center (PPT).  
2. The Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1) in implementing the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence may coordinate with other Regional Apparatuses.

## Part Two

Integrated Service Center

#### Article 9

1. The Integrated Service Center (PPT) as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (1) shall be established by the Regional Government in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
2. The establishment of the Integrated Service Center (PPT) as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of members from regional government elements, non-governmental organizations, health service institutions, law enforcement officials, professional personnel, volunteer assistants, social workers, religious leaders, and managers of safe houses (shelters), and social rehabilitation centers.

#### Article 10

The duties of the Integrated Service Center (PPT) are to seek prevention, recovery and social reintegration, provide legal protection, coordinate and seek to increase community participation, as well as monitoring and reporting.

#### Article 11

Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 10 include:
a. organizing communication, information, and education about gender-based violence and violence against children;  
b. socialization of laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and violence against children;

#### Article 12

Recovery and social reintegration efforts as referred to in Article 10 include:
a. providing physical recovery at health service institutions; b. assisting the return of victims;  
c. providing temporary protection in safe houses (shelters); d. providing psychosocial recovery and assistance; e. providing spiritual guidance services;  
f. preparing families and communities, economic empowerment, and returning to school and/or other educational institutions.

#### Article 13

Legal Protection as referred to in Article 10 includes:
a. providing protection in safe houses (shelters); b. providing assistance in the legal process at the police, prosecution and district court levels;  
c. providing special legal protection for child victims of violence which may be carried out by appointing guardianship in accordance with applicable regulations.

#### Article 14

Coordination and Cooperation as referred to in Article 10 include:
a. coordinating and cooperating in handling cases of violence with integrated services;  
b. coordinating and cooperating with integrated services between regencies/cities;  
c. coordinating and cooperating with institutions providing services for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
d. coordinating and consulting with the Regional Government.

#### Article 15

Increasing Community Participation as referred to in Article 10 includes:
a. fostering community awareness of cases of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
b. encouraging the community to actively participate in providing information and reporting the occurrence of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
c. fostering local wisdom in handling gender-based violence and violence against children;  
d. organizing the strengthening of community groups in handling gender-based violence and violence against children;  
e. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and violence against children.

#### Article 16

Monitoring and Reporting as referred to in Article 10, includes monitoring, documentation and reporting of cases of gender-based violence and violence against children.

#### Article 17

The implementation of integrated service delivery is coordinated by the Regional Work Unit in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

## Part Three

Cooperation

#### Article 18

1. In implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children, the Regional Government may cooperate with the Government, Provincial Government, Regency/City Government and other institutions.  
2. Cooperation between the Regional Government and the Government and Provincial Government includes consultation, coordination and reporting.  
3. Cooperation between other Regency/City Governments includes coordination, advocacy, referral, repatriation, social reintegration and facilitation of Integrated Service System development, monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

# CHAPTER VIII

## HANDLING AND MECHANISM

#### Article 19

1. The implementation of protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the PPT framework.  
2. PPT in handling medical, legal, psychological, and economic protection in its implementation may conduct partnerships with regional apparatus and social community institutions engaged in the field of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children.  
3. The implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) must take into account religious norms as well as the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands/other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.  
4. Provisions regarding the mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

## FORMS OF PROTECTION

#### Article 20

1. Every victim of gender-based violence and children must receive protection in accordance with applicable provisions.  
2. Protection for victims of gender-based violence and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is provided to Wonogiri residents, both those occurring within the Wonogiri Regency area and outside the Wonogiri Regency area.  
3. Protection for victims of gender-based violence and children in addition to being provided to Wonogiri residents as referred to in paragraph (2), also applies to non-Wonogiri residents occurring within the Wonogiri Regency area.

#### Article 21

1. Forms of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children include medical, legal, medicolegal (forensic medicine), economic, and psychological protection.  
2. The form of medical protection for victims of gender-based violence and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel free of charge.  
3. The form of legal protection for victims of gender-based violence and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.  
4. The form of medicolegal protection for victims of gender-based violence and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field.  
5. The form of economic protection for victims of gender-based violence and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of services for skills and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.  
6. The form of psychological protection for victims of gender-based violence and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of assistance in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving.

# CHAPTER X

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 22

1. The community may participate in assisting prevention and handling efforts for victims of gender-based violence and children.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is realized by:a. providing information and/or reporting any violence that is known;  
b. providing protection for victims;  
c. providing emergency assistance;  
d. providing advocacy to victims and/or the community regarding the handling of cases of gender-based violence and children;  
e. assisting the process of submitting an application for the determination of protection;  
f. assisting in the repatriation and reintegration process.  
3. Community participation may be carried out by individuals, groups or social community organizations.  
4. Social community organizations that participate in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be provided guidance by the Wonogiri Regency Government through regional apparatus in accordance with their main duties and functions.  
5. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (4) may be in the form of counseling guidance, skills guidance, paralegal training and operational technical guidance.

# CHAPTER XI

## ASSISTANCE

#### Article 23

Assistance is provided by persons or institutions that have expertise in conducting counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and have cooperated with PPT.

#### Article 24

1. Assistance includes all integrated efforts to restore and strengthen the condition of victims, carried out by the Police, Prosecutor's Office, Courts, Advocates, Social Workers, and Women's or Social Organizations concerned with women and children.  
2. Assistance by the Police, Prosecutor's Office, Courts and Advocates as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XII

## CONTROL, GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 25

1. Control, guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children shall be carried out by the Regent.  
2. The implementation of control, guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) on a daily basis shall be carried out by the Regional Apparatus responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

# CHAPTER XIII

## FINANCING

#### Article 26

All activities related to the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children shall be charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget and/or other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XIV

## CRIMINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 27

Criminal acts in the field of child protection are subject to criminal penalties as regulated in Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.

# CHAPTER XV

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 28

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all implementing regulations related to the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children are declared to remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to and/or have not been replaced by new regulations.

# CHAPTER XVI

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

### I GENERAL

Gender-based violence and violence against children is a social phenomenon that has existed since ancient times and has become increasingly prevalent recently. In fact, gender-based violence and violence against children continues to increase, both in number and in the forms and modus operandi which are increasingly diverse. Rape, sexual harassment, trafficking of women and children, domestic violence, sexual exploitation, violence against domestic workers, pornography, exploitation of migrant workers, and neglect, will apparently continue to be encountered in daily life, both in the domestic sphere (household) and public sphere.

The causal factors of gender-based violence and violence against children are very complex and interrelated with one another. These factors include, among others, legal instruments that have not been able to provide protection to victims, the concept that women and children are family property (assets), media that do not adequately support reporting on violence against women and children, suboptimal public services, customs that sometimes legalize violence, poverty issues, erroneous interpretation of religious teachings, all of which are wrapped in a patriarchal culture.

The handling of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children occurring in Wonogiri, in accordance with the provisions of Article 3 of Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection and Article 13 of Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, states that the Regional Government is obligated to establish and develop a system and mechanism of cooperation for handling violence. Furthermore, based on the provisions of Article 26 paragraph (1) letter b of Law Number 32 of 2004 concerning Regional Government, it states that the Deputy Regional Head has the duty to assist the Regional Head in coordinating the activities of vertical agencies in the region, following up on reports and/or findings from supervisory apparatus oversight, implementing women's and youth empowerment, as well as seeking the development and preservation of socio-culture and the environment. Therefore, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Violence Against Children.

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear

#### Article 2

What is meant by "non-discrimination" is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and violence against children without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, cultural ethnicity and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.

What is meant by "gender justice" is fair treatment given to women and men.

What is meant by "gender equality" is a condition and position that describes harmonious, aligned and balanced relations between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in access, participation, control and benefits in the implementation of development as well as enjoying the results of development in family life, as well as in society, nationhood and statehood.

What is meant by "best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

#### Article 3

What is meant by being able to live is the right to survival of children as contained in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity and the right to enjoy the highest attainable health status.

What is meant by "grow and develop" for children is as contained in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.
