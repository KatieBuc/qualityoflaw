REGIONAL REGULATION OF SOUTH BANGKA REGENCY NUMBER 9 OF 2018

# CONCERNING

EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION OF WOMEN BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

# REGENT OF SOUTH BANGKA,

Considering:  
a. that women and men have equal rights to participate in various fields of life;  
b. that women are a national asset who play a very important role in the process of continuation and creation of quality generations, therefore they need to receive protection guarantees from acts of violence and discrimination and need to be empowered so that they can actualize their potential optimally;  
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, and letter b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Empowerment and Protection of Women;  

In View of:  
1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;  

2. Law Number 1 of 1974 concerning Marriage (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1974 Number 1, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3019);

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is South Bangka Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the regional government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and co-administration tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region.  
4. Regent is the Regent of South Bangka.  
5. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of South Bangka Regency.  
6. Human Rights are a set of rights inherent in the nature and existence of human beings as creatures of God Almighty and are His gift which must be respected, upheld and protected by the state, law, government, and every person for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity and worth.  
7. Women's empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social capacity, knowledge and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.  
8. Women's protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties either temporarily or based on court decisions.  
9. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs, which results in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.  
10. Gender is a concept that refers to roles and responsibilities as women and as men that are created and internalized in families, society and the culture of the society in which we live including expectations, attitudes, characteristics, behavior of how to be a man and how to be a woman.  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

11. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.  
12. Violence against women is any act that violates, hinders, eliminates enjoyment and ignores the human rights of women.  
13. Disability is an individual who has physical or mental limitations.  
14. Society is individuals, families, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.  
15. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children.  
16. Manpower/Workforce is every person who is able to perform work to produce goods and/or services either to meet their own needs or for society.  
17. Worker/laborer is every person who works by receiving wages or remuneration in other forms.

## SCOPE

#### Article 2

d. Women's Empowerment;

f. Strategy for Women's Empowerment and Protection;  
g. Mechanism for Implementation of Women's Empowerment and Protection;  
h. Community Participation;  
i. Funding; and  
j. Guidance and Supervision.  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

# CHAPTER III

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

Women's Empowerment and Protection is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect for human rights;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination; and  
d. victim protection.

#### Article 4

Women's Empowerment and Protection aims to:

a. increase women's participation in the development process;  
b. improve the quality of life of women;  
c. provide guarantees to women in the fulfillment of rights as human beings; and  
d. provide a sense of security by increasing protection for women from various acts of violence.

# CHAPTER IV

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 5

(1) Every woman has the right to:

a. live and maintain life and improve her standard of living;  
b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue offspring;  
c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life to become a human being who is faithful, pious, responsible, noble, happy, and prosperous in accordance with human rights;  
d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;  
e. be involved in every stage of the development process;  
f. be free from slavery or enslavement and threats;  
g. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

h. obtain welfare and a decent life;  
i. participate in politics;  
j. perform legal acts; and  
k. freely choose a partner in her marriage.

(2) Every wife during the marriage bond has the same rights and responsibilities as her husband over all matters relating to their married life, relations with their children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
3. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to children with due regard to the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government is obliged and responsible for the empowerment and protection of women.

(2) Efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;  
b. determination of implementation guidelines;  
c. provision of services; and  
d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.  

3. Efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (2) are carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 7

Women's empowerment is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings so that they are able to play a role and participate in the fields of:

a. economy;  
b. socio-culture;  
c. politics and government;  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

d. law;  
e. education;  
f. health;  
g. employment; and  

h. social security.

## Part One

Economic Field

#### Article 8

Women's empowerment in the economic field is implemented through:

a. provision of skills and job training;  
b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;  
c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;  
d. facilitation and capital assistance; and  
e. facilitation of marketing network development.

## Part Two

Socio-Cultural Field

#### Article 9

Empowerment of women in the socio-cultural field is implemented through;  
a. enhancement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;  
b. enhancement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health;  
c. enhancement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and  
d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and the development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

## Part Three

Field of Politics and Governance

#### Article 10

1. Empowerment of women in the field of politics and governance includes:  
a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;  
b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;  
c. participation in general elections; and  
d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.  
2. Empowerment of women in the field of politics and governance as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
3. The Regional Government provides guarantees to women to have the right to vote and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting according to applicable laws and regulations.  
4. The Regional Government provides opportunities for women to be appointed as regional government officials and occupy strategic positions in regional government; and  
5. The Regional Government and/or Political Parties are responsible for providing political education for women.

## Part Four

Field of Law

#### Article 11

1. Empowerment of women in the field of law includes:  
a. enhancement of awareness and knowledge in the field of law through communication, information and education services; and  
b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.  
2. Empowerment of women in the field of law as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

## Part Five

Field of Education

#### Article 12

Empowerment of women in the field of education through:  
a. provision of formal and non-formal education for women in South Bangka Regency.  
b. Non-formal education aimed at improving women's capabilities.  
c. Non-formal education as referred to in letter b, includes women's empowerment education, skills education and job training.  
d. Formal and non-formal education as referred to in letter a, is regulated by a separate Regional Regulation.

## Part Six

Field of Health

#### Article 13

1. Women have the right to obtain health services in order to undergo reproductive life and sexual life that is healthy, safe, and free from coercion and/or violence.  
2. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. Health Services for the Period Before Pregnancy, Pregnancy, Childbirth and After Childbirth;  
a. Pregnancy regulation, contraceptive services and sexual health; and  
b. Reproductive health services.  
3. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented through promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative approaches.

## Part Seven

Field of Employment

#### Article 14

1. Women have equal opportunities without discrimination to obtain employment.  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

2. Employers who employ female workers are obliged to provide protection that includes welfare, safety and health.

## Part Eight

Field of Social Security

#### Article 15

1. The Regional Government is responsible for providing guidance facilities for women with social problems.  
2. The Regional Government provides the right to social security for women with social problems.  
3. The responsibility for providing guidance facilities for women with social problems is under the regional apparatus organization that handles women's empowerment affairs.

# CHAPTER VII

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 16

Implementation of protection of women is provided to:  
a. female workers / laborers;  
b. elderly women;  
c. women with disabilities;  
d. homeless women;  
e. domestic workers;  
f. women as family economic supporters;  
g. women former inmates of correctional institutions;  
h. women victims of disasters;  
i. commercial sex workers; and  
j. women victims of violence.

## Part One

Female Workers / Laborers

#### Article 17

1. Implementation of protection for female workers/laborers to provide protection from acts of economic exploitation www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id and cruel, inhumane treatment and to seek proper appreciation for work.  
2. Female workers/laborers as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:  
a. Recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;  
b. Health insurance and employment social security;  
c. Access to information and legal consultation services;  
d. Opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve status;

## Part Two

Elderly Women

#### Article 18

1. Implementation of protection for elderly women in order to extend life expectancy so that they can carry out their social functions and play an active role naturally in their social life.  
2. Elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection for:  
a. guarantee of continuity and personal self-development:  
b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, arts and culture as well as utilization of information and communication technology:  
c. accessibility to information, legal aid, mobility, social services; and  
d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

## Part Three

Women with Disabilities

#### Article 19

1. Implementation of Protection for women with disabilities in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination including freedom from cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity and freedom from exploitation;  
2. Women with disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection for:  
a. guarantee of continuity and personal self-development;  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id
b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, sports, arts and culture as well as utilization of information and communication technology;  
c. accessibility to information, legal aid, mobility, social services; and  
d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

## Part Four

Homeless Women

#### Article 20

1. Implementation of protection for homeless women in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.  
2. Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection for:  
a. decent housing;  
b. health and social security;  
c. population administration registration;  
d. opportunities to obtain skills to improve economic status; and  
e. access to information and legal consultation services.

## Part kelima

Domestic Workers

#### Article 21

1. Implementation of Protection for female domestic workers in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation in employment relationships.  
2. Female domestic workers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:  
   a. recognition of rights, wages, and decent working conditions;  
   b. health and employment social security guarantees;  
   c. access to information and legal consultation services; and  
   d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

## Part Six

Women as Family Economic Pillars

#### Article 22

1. Implementation of protection for women as family economic pillars in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, negative stigma, and economic empowerment for women as family economic pillars.  
2. Women as family economic pillars as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection rights for:  
   a. discriminatory actions and efforts to weaken the function of women as family economic pillars;  
   b. strengthening capacity and recognition as family economic pillars in their environment; and  
   c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.

## Part Seven

Women Former Inmates of Correctional Institutions

#### Article 23

1. Implementation of Protection for women former inmates of Correctional Institutions in order to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting in community life, so that they can carry out life normally.  
2. Women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:  
   a. rehabilitation and social reintegration; and  
   b. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Eight

Women Disaster Victims

#### Article 24

1. Implementation of Protection for women disaster victims in order to provide protection during disasters and post-disaster.  
2. Women disaster victims as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:  
   a. access to decent housing;  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id
   b. health services;  
   c. access to information services and legal consultation; and  
   d. post-disaster recovery.

## Part Nine

Women Commercial Sex Workers

#### Article 25

1. Implementation of Protection for women commercial sex workers in order to provide health protection and improvement of life skills as capital to improve social status.  
2. Protection for women commercial sex workers as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
   a. providing life skills;  
   b. facilitating reproductive health services; and  
   c. preventing human trafficking.

## Part Ten

Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 26

1. Implementation of Protection for women victims of violence in order to provide legal protection and/or health recovery.  
2. Protection for women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
   a. legal aid;  
   b. assistance after the occurrence of acts of violence;  
   c. rehabilitation and social reintegration; and  
   d. health services.

# CHAPTER VIII

## STRATEGIES FOR WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION

#### Article 27

1. Women's empowerment and protection shall be implemented by integrating gender mainstreaming and gender-disaggregated data in an integrated manner based on applicable laws and regulations.  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id
2. In women's empowerment and protection, a Working Group and Integrated Services shall be established as determined by Regent's Decree.  

3. Women's empowerment and protection shall be coordinated by the Agency handling Women's Empowerment.

# CHAPTER IX

MECHANISM FOR IMPLEMENTING WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION

#### Article 28

The mechanism for implementing women's empowerment and protection shall be carried out through stages:  
a. formulation of policy planning for programs and activities;  
b. budgeting for programs and activities;  
c. implementation of programs and activities; and  
d. monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

#### Article 29

1. Formulation of policy planning for programs and activities as referred to in Article 28 letter a, shall be implemented through:  
   a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of data;  
   b. coordination and synchronization of planning for programs and activities with related Regional Apparatus; and  
   c. determination of programs and activities in planning documents.  
2. Formulation of policy planning for programs and activities shall be further regulated in minimum service standards and standard operating procedures.  
3. Formulation of policy planning for programs and activities for implementing women's empowerment and protection, shall be coordinated by the Regional Apparatus in charge of planning.  
4. Budgeting for programs and activities as referred to in Article 28 letter b, shall be implemented through general budget policies set forth in the Regional Budget.  
5. Implementation of programs and activities as referred to in Article 28 letter c, shall be implemented through:  
   a. implementation of programs and activities set forth in the regional apparatus budget documents;  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id
   b. coordination and synchronization of program and activity implementation with related Regional Apparatus.  
6. Implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in Article 28 letter d, shall be implemented through:  
   a. conducting monitoring and evaluation periodically on the implementation of programs and activities that have been carried out;  
   b. making reports periodically; and  
   c. evaluation results as input material for formulating policies, programs and activities for the following year.

# CHAPTER X

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 30

1. The community may participate in the implementation of women's empowerment and protection.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be realized by:  
   a. actions to provide information and/or report violence against women to law enforcement or authorized parties, or participate in handling acts of violence against women.  
   b. provision of communication, information and education media about empowerment and prevention of violence against women in families, communities, educational institutions and public spaces.

# CHAPTER XI

## FUNDING

#### Article 31

Funding for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for women's empowerment and protection shall be sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of South Bangka Regency and other legitimate and non-binding sources of funds.

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

# CHAPTER XII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 32

1. The Regent or OPD responsible for women's empowerment shall conduct guidance and supervision over the empowerment and protection of women;  
2. The forms of guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) are as follows:a. providing socialization to the community and business sector regarding policies on women's empowerment and protection;  
b. providing books, leaflets, brochures regarding women's empowerment and protection, and other related issues and disseminating them to the community;  

c. facilitation of capacity building for institutions implementing women's empowerment and protection;  
d. facilitation of women's participation forums and women's organizations;  
e. facilitation of rehabilitation and reintegration services for women victims of disasters and victims of violence;  
f. coordinating the participation of the business sector and other institutions, including international assistance for women's empowerment and protection;dang. providing awards to the community and business sector both individually and institutionally who have made efforts in women's empowerment and protection.  
3. The form of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of monitoring and evaluation activities over women's empowerment and protection.

# CHAPTER XIII

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 33

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all implementing provisions related to the implementation of women's empowerment and protection are declared to remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to the provisions in this Regional Regulation.  

www.jdih.bangkaselatankab.go.id

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 34

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation. In order that everyone may know of it, the Promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered, with its placement in the Regional Gazette of South Bangka Regency.
