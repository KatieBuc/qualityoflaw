# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Indramayu Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent together with Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Indramayu.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Indramayu Regency as an element of Regional Government administration.
5. Agency is the Regional Apparatus Working Unit authorized in the field of Prevention, Protection and Recovery of Women and Children as Victims of Acts of Violence in Indramayu Regency.
6. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person recognized by law as a woman.
7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child in the womb and marriage cannot change the status of a child.
8. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
9. Recovery is rehabilitating victims until they are physically and/or psychologically healthy and accepted by the community again.
10. Protection is an activity and/or action in order to protect women and children from violence and exploitation.
11. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
12. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or fainting and/or causes death.
13. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, distrust or severe psychological suffering to a person.
14. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
15. Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence, whether physical, psychological or sexual violence.
16. Service is an immediate activity and action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
17. Assistance is an activity and action carried out by an assistant during the service process.
18. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.
19. Institution is an agency/body and/or non-governmental organization (NGO) that provides assistance.
20. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an institution providing services to victims of violence based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psychosocial and legal services.
21. Safe house (Shelter) is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
22. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
23. P2TP2A is a form of service facility for women and children in an effort to fulfill information and needs in the fields of education, health, economy, law, protection and prevention of acts of violence and trafficking against women and children.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of Prevention, Protection and Recovery of Women and Children as Victims of Acts of Violence is carried out based on the principles of:

a. Respect for the rights of victims;
b. Gender justice and welfare;
c. Non-discrimination; and
d. Best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

The purpose of implementing prevention, protection and recovery of women and children as victims of acts of violence is to provide prevention and protection covering aspects of:

a. Prevention;
b. Service and assistance;
c. Recovery; and
d. Empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

Every victim has the right to obtain:

a. Protection;
b. Information;
c. Minimum service in accordance with Minimum Service Standards (SPM);
d. Continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;
e. Confidential handling;
f. Psychological and legal assistance; and
g. Guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND AUTHORITY

## OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 5

1. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children in the form of:

a. collecting data and information about women and children victims of violence;
b. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of prevention, protection and recovery of women and children as victims of violence and exploitation;
c. conducting socialization about understanding and anti-violence values towards Women and Children through IEC (information, education and communication) both through the community, schools and universities;

d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of prevention, protection and recovery of women and children as victims of violence and exploitation.

2. The Regional Government in anticipating the occurrence of acts of violence against Women and Children, has the authority to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:

a. providing and facilitating the establishment of integrated service institutions for victims of acts of violence by involving institutions and community elements;
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims of acts of violence;

c. empowering the community for the prevention of acts of violence.

3. The obligations and authority of the Regional Government are carried out by the Agency

# CHAPTER V

## PREVENTION AND PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional

#### Article 6

1. In the framework of implementing prevention, protection and recovery of women and children victims of acts of violence in an integrated, harmonious, and sustainable manner, the Regent establishes P2TP2A at the Regency level.
2. Further provisions regarding the establishment of P2TP2A management, P2TP2A membership, and P2TP2A working procedures as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by the Regent.
3. Regency P2TP2A may receive case referrals from Hospitals, Community Health Centers, NGOs, Safe Houses (Shelters) and the Community.

## Part Two

Forms and Mechanisms of Prevention

#### Article 7

Forms of prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children carried out by P2TP2A can be implemented through:

a. socialization activities of laws and regulations and/or regional regulations to the community related to the protection of the rights of women and children;

b. training of P2TP2A members related to the implementation of their duties in preventing acts of violence against women and children.

#### Article 8

1. Regency P2TP2A in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with the Religion and Social Welfare Section of the Indramayu Regency Secretariat, sub-districts and competent parties in making efforts to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
2. The mechanism for implementing prevention duties as referred to in paragraph (1) is further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 9

Forms of services for victims organized by P2TP2A include:

a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;  

b. medico-legal services constitute a form of medical service for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;  
c. Psychological services constitute services provided in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and to provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in resolving their problems;  
d. Legal services to assist victims in undergoing legal processes;  
e. Economic independence services in the form of skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

#### Article 10

1. In carrying out the task of serving victims of acts of violence against women and children, the Regency P2TP2A coordinates with the Sub-district Community Health Center.  
2. The mechanism for implementing tasks as referred to in paragraph (1) is regulated in Standard Operating Procedures (SOP), further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

Assistance Mechanism

#### Article 11

1. Assistance is carried out by persons and institutions cooperating with P2TP2A.  
2. The assistance mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) is set forth in Standard Operating Procedures (SOP).  
3. Provisions regarding Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for the assistance mechanism as referred to in paragraph (2) are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Four

Principles of Prevention, Protection and Recovery

#### Article 12

The implementation of prevention, protection and recovery for victims of acts of violence is carried out with:

a. Fast, safe, empathetic;  
b. Guarantee of confidentiality;  
c. Easily accessible; dand. No fees charged.

# CHAPTER VI

## RECOVERY

## Part One

Minimum Service Standards

#### Article 13

1. In the context of implementing prevention, protection and recovery for women and children victims of acts of violence in an integrated, harmonious and sustainable manner, the Regional Government establishes Minimum Service Standards (SPM).  
2. Further provisions regarding Minimum Service Standards as referred to in paragraph (1) are further regulated by Regent Regulation.  
3. The Regional Government is obligated to apply Minimum Service Standards in Hospitals, Community Health Centers, NGOs, Safe Houses (Shelters) designated by the Regent.

## Part Two

Forms and Mechanisms of Recovery

#### Article 14

Forms of recovery for women and child victims carried out by P2TP2A can be implemented through:

a. rehabilitation and assistance activities are carried out for a maximum of one month; b. P2TP2A is obligated to provide competent resources such as psychiatrists and psychologists; c. P2TP2A is obligated to provide physical and psychological health tests for victims of acts of violence;

# CHAPTER VII

## GUIDANCE, CONTROL AND

## SUPERVISION

#### Article 15

1. The Regent has the authority to conduct guidance, control and supervision of all activities related to the implementation of prevention, protection and recovery of women and children victims of violence and exploitation in the region.  
2. The authority as referred to in paragraph (1) is technically and operationally implemented by the Agency.

#### Article 16

Guidance, control and supervision as referred to in Chapter VII are directed to:

a. increase the level of public awareness so as to be able to prevent, protect and/or restore the physical and or psychological condition of women and children victims of acts of violence;  
b. fulfillment of the community's need for information and protection services for women and children victims of acts of violence;  
c. provide facilities in order to support the improvement of efforts and activities for implementing prevention, protection, recovery of women and children victims of acts of violence;  
d. improve the quality of health for women and children who experience acts of violence.

# CHAPTER VIII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 17

1. The community can participate in efforts to prevent, protect and recover victims of violence against women and children.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) can be carried out by individuals or organizations.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) can be carried out in the form: a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;  
b. submitting reports to the authorities if acts of violence against women and children occur;  

c.providing assistance to victims of acts of violence against women and children.

# CHAPTER IX

## REPORTING

#### Article 18

1. P2TP2A is obligated to report the implementation of prevention, protection and recovery of women and children victims of acts of violence to the Regent.  
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:a. Administration;  
b. Finance;  
c. Services;  
d. Performance.  
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out at least once every 3 (three) months.

# CHAPTER X

## FUNDING SOURCES

#### Article 19

1. Expenditure for programs and activities for the Implementation of Prevention, Protection and Recovery of Women and Children as Victims of Acts of Violence is sourced from the Indramayu Regency Regional Budget.  
2. In addition to being sourced from the Regional Budget, program and activity expenditure as referred to in paragraph (1) may be sourced from the State Budget, Provincial Regional Budget and other legal and non-binding sources.  
3. To support the operational expenditure of P2TP2A, a budget sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBD) may be provided.

# CHAPTER XI

## CRIMINAL SANCTIONS

#### Article 20

1. Any person who obstructs, disturbs and/or hinders the implementation of programs for the implementation of prevention, protection and recovery of women and children victims of acts of violence, is threatened with imprisonment for a maximum of 3 (three) months or a fine of at most Rp. 50,000,000.00 (Fifty Million Rupiah).  
2. In addition to criminal acts as referred to in paragraph (1) whether in the form of criminal violations and/or actions resulting in losses to the Regional Government, individuals or other parties are threatened with criminal law in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XII

## INVESTIGATION

#### Article 21

1. Certain Civil Servant Officials within the Regional Government are granted special authority as Investigators to conduct investigations of criminal acts, as referred to in the Criminal Procedure Law.  
2. Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) are certain civil servant officials within the Regional Government appointed by authorized officials in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
3. The authority of Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) is:a. to receive, search for, collect, and examine information or reports concerning criminal acts so that such information or reports become more complete and clear;  
b. to examine, search for, and collect information regarding individuals or Entities concerning the truth of acts committed in connection with criminal acts;  
c. to request information and evidence from individuals or Entities in connection with criminal acts;  
d. to examine books, records, and other documents concerning criminal acts;  
e. to conduct searches to obtain evidence of bookkeeping, recording, and other documents, as well as to seize such evidence;  
f. to request the assistance of experts in the framework of carrying out criminal investigation duties;  
g. to order someone to stop and/or prohibit someone from leaving a room or place when an examination is in progress and to examine the identity of persons, objects, and/or documents carried;  
h. to photograph persons related to criminal acts;  
i. to summon persons to be heard for their statements and examined as suspects or witnesses;  
j. to terminate investigations; and/ork. to take other actions necessary for the smooth conduct of criminal investigations in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
4. Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) shall notify the commencement of investigations and submit the results of their investigations to the Public Prosecutor through the Investigator official of the Police of the Republic of Indonesia, in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Criminal Procedure Law.

# CHAPTER XIII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

##### I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide a sense of security to its citizens from threats and actions that can disturb and damage security, psychological, physical, sexual, or economic well-being. This is philosophically stated in the preamble of the 1945 Constitution, that the purpose of establishing the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia is to protect the entire nation and all of Indonesia's territory.  

Because the Indonesian government has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 Year 1984 concerning the elimination of all forms of Discrimination against Women), it is obliged to fulfill these provisions. The enforcement and fulfillment of Human Rights is the responsibility of the State.  

Through Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection, the government recognizes the existence of children's rights. Human Rights inherent in children include, among others, basic rights as human beings, namely the Right to life, the Right to Growth and Development, the Right to Protection and the Right to Participation.  

To fulfill the right to life, children need food in sufficient quantities, healthy and nutritious, as well as optimal health access. Protection from violence that threatens their safety and health must be provided from an early age. Protection is provided to prevent violence and physical, mental and sexual exploitation. To fulfill the right to growth and development, children need space to play, exercise, education that is appropriate to their physical and psychological development. In fulfilling children's rights, every government administrator, society and parents are obliged to understand and care about children's rights. The existence of legal instruments and law enforcement officials who defend the interests of children is necessary for this protection effort. Legislation that is principled on defending the best interests of the child (The best Interest of Child) is needed to realize protection that is legal in nature.  

Furthermore, it is not uncommon to find cases of women and children experiencing problems of physical, psychological, sexual and social violence. For example, women and children victims of persecution, neglect, eviction and abuse by parents or others. For women and children experiencing such problems, the government must provide a Safe House (Children protection home) or integrated service center for women and children (P2TP2A) for women and children victims of violence. A Safe House (shelter) is a safe place and is Friendly (friendly) for women and children.  

The Integrated Service Center is equipped with companions from educators, doctors, psychologists, clerics/religious leaders and is networked with lawyers for women and children as well as the RPK Unit at the Police.  

The fate of women and children victims of violence must be given attention by the government. The large number of cases of violence against women and children are often caused by factors that develop in society, such as low levels of economy, education, environments located in the industrial sector. Therefore victims of violence like this need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of truth, justice, legal certainty, equality and upholding Human Rights.  

From the above framework, the regional government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, economically and socially to prevent, suppress, reduce, and eliminate all forms of violence against women. One such effort is the realization of a legal framework in the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently Clear

#### Article 2

What is meant by "Respect for the rights of victims" is a series of actions to respect, appreciate and ensure the fulfillment of the rights of victims.

What is meant by "Justice and gender equality" is a process to be fair to men and women and equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so as to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, defense and National security activities and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

What is meant by "Non-Discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims by not making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

What is meant by "The best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by local government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, must be the primary consideration.

#### Article 3

Letter a The "prevention" aspect is a protection strategy effort through:
 a. Primary prevention, all people, families, communities and the state in efforts to increase the ability of knowledge, understanding and ensure that violence against women and children does not occur, including policy socialization, adequate services, supportive workplace policies, and life skills training for women and children.

What is meant by life skills training includes conflict resolution without violence, stress management skills, resource management, making effective decisions, effective interpersonal communication, guidance on the psychosocial development of women and children.
 b. Secondary prevention, aimed at community groups with high risk in efforts to improve skills, including training and victim services to ensure that violence against women and children does not occur in the next generation. Activities carried out here include conducting home visits for parents who have just had children to conduct self-assessment whether they are at risk of committing violence against children in the future.
 c. Tertiary prevention, intended to improve parenting abilities that ensure that violence against women and children is not repeated, here what is done is integrated services for women and children who are victims of violence, through counseling, stress management training.

Letter b The "service and assistance" aspect is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals and assistance in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

Letter c The "recovery" aspect is an effort to restore and recover the physical and psychological condition of the victim which then reunites them with the victim's family and community.

Letter d The "empowerment" aspect is a process of increasing the role of the community to participate in the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence which aims to obtain access and participation in the process of protection of women and children broadly.

#### Article 4

Letter a What is meant by "receiving protection" is receiving protection from individuals, groups and institutions both government and non-government.

Letter b What is meant by "receiving information" is access and information about the existence of complaint centers, PPT, and all matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and involvement in the decision-making process related to assistance and case developments.

Letter c What is meant by "minimum services" is services that include medical, medicolegal extension, psychosocial and legal services.

Letter d What is meant by "continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage" is handling that does not stop at physical and psychological healing, but until the victim can resume life in society including the restoration of their good name.

Letter e What is meant by "confidential handling" is an effort to guarantee certainty for victims that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disseminated.

Letter f What is meant by "receiving psychological assistance" is assistance provided by psychologists to victims suffering from trauma/other mental problems to restore the victim's mental condition. While legal assistance is assistance provided by persons and/or legal aid institutions to persons at every level of examination and during the legal process.

Letter g What is meant by "guarantee of rights related to victim status" is an effort to provide certainty and protection for victims as family members and community members.
