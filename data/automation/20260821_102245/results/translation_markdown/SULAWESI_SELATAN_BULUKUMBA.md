# REGIONAL REGULATION OF BULUKUMBA REGENCY NUMBER 3 OF 2024

# CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

# REGENT OF BULUKUMBA,

Considering:
a. that as citizens of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia, women and children have the right to obtain protection, security and freedom from all forms of violence or inhumane treatment that is contrary to the values of Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;  
b. that in its development there are still many women and children who need to obtain protection from various forms of violence, maltreatment, exploitation, and discrimination so that strategic efforts are needed to provide protection for women and children;  
c. that based on Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government in implementing concurrent government affairs, the Regency Government has the authority in implementing government affairs in the field of protection of women and children;  
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;  

In View of:  
1. Article 18 Paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;  

2. Law Number 29 of 1959 concerning the Establishment of Level II Regions in Sulawesi (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1959 Number 74, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 1822);

Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as most recently amended several times, most recently by Law Number 6 of 2023 concerning Stipulation of Government Regulation in Lieu of Law Number 2 of 2022 concerning Job Creation into Law (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2023 Number 41, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6856);  
4. Law Number 12 of 2022 concerning the Criminal Act of Sexual Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2022 Number 120, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6792);

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Bulukumba Regency.  
2. Government is a body or organization that exercises governmental power of a state. The Government has the duty to administer governmental affairs, from the central, provincial, regency, municipal, sub-district, urban village (kelurahan), to village levels.  
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government organizer who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
4. Regional Apparatus is an organization/institution in regional government that is responsible to the Regent in the context of administering government consisting of regional secretary, regional agencies and regional technical institutions, sub-districts, villages and civil service police units according to regional needs.  
5. Service (Dinas) is a Regional Apparatus that has the main duties and functions in carrying out Women's Empowerment and Child Protection of Bulukumba Regency.  
6. Village Government is the administration of governmental affairs and the interests of the local community in the governmental system of the unitary state of the Republic of Indonesia.  
7. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the annual financial plan of regional government that is discussed and approved jointly by the Regional Government and DPRD, and stipulated by Regional Regulation.  
8. Village Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter referred to as APBDes is the annual financial plan of village government that is discussed and approved jointly by the Village Head and Village Representative Body and stipulated through Village Regulation.  

9. Woman is a person who has female genitalia who can menstruate and become pregnant or has obtained legal status as a woman.  
10. Implementation of Women and Child Protection is a directed, integrated, and sustainable effort carried out by the Central Government, Regional Government, Village Government, and the community to fulfill the rights of women and children as citizens within the scope of this Regional Regulation.  
11. Women's Protection is all efforts to protect and provide a sense of security to women as well as the fulfillment of their rights through consistent, structured, and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.  
12. Women and Child Protection is a handling effort to protect and fulfill the rights of women and children from all forms of violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.  
13. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.  
14. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
15. Violence Against Children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, and or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.  
16. Violence Against Women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, and arbitrary deprivation of liberty whether occurring in the private or public sphere.  
17. Victim is a person who experiences physical suffering, women and children who experience violence and/or mental threats and or economic losses resulting from an act of violence.  
18. Victim of Violence is a person who experiences psychological, mental, physical, sexual, economic, and/or social suffering resulting from a criminal act of violence.  
19. Violence Prevention is any action or effort undertaken to eliminate various factors that cause acts of violence against women and children.  
20. Community is individual Indonesian citizens, community groups, and/or mass organizations.  
21. Technical Implementation Unit for Women and Child Protection hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA is an operational technical implementation unit in a work unit that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection, which functions as an integrated service provider for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, and other problems.  
22. Child-Friendly Regency is a regency that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of government, community and business commitments and resources that are planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights.  

23. Assistance for Victims of Violence is an effort or process to assist victims of violence in accessing the right to handling, protection, and recovery.  
24. Rapid Reaction Team hereinafter abbreviated as TRC is a team formed by the service/office in charge of Women's Empowerment and Children that is tasked with assisting UPT PPA in conducting outreach and/or assistance to expedite the process of handling cases of violence against women and children.  
25. Women and Child Protection Task Force is a coordinative institution formed by the Bulukumba Regency Government that coordinates policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children in Bulukumba Regency.  
26. Vulnerable Groups are any person or community group entitled to receive special treatment and protection relating to their specificity. Vulnerable groups as referred to in this regulation consist of women and children in conflict with the law, persons with disabilities, Indonesian migrant workers, the elderly and/or indigenous peoples of Ammatoa Kajang.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The Implementation of Women and Child Protection is conducted based on the principles of:

a. humanity;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. protection;  
d. best interests of women and children;  
e. non-discrimination; and  
f. special treatment for vulnerable groups.

#### Article 3

The Implementation of Women and Child Protection aims to:

a. prevent acts of violence against women and children;  
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;  
c. protect, provide a sense of security for women and children;  
d. provide services to women victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses;  
e. provide services to children whether as victims, witnesses or as perpetrators of violence;  
f. facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize an equal and just household; and  
g. prioritize services and protection for Vulnerable Groups.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 4

The scope of the implementation of Women and Child Protection includes:

a. violence prevention;  
b. provision of service institutions;  
c. institutional strengthening;  
d. obligations and responsibilities;  
e. cooperation;  
f. community participation; and  
g. funding.

# CHAPTER IV

## PREVENTION OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

1. Prevention of violence as referred to in Article 4 letter a is implemented through:
   a. planning and implementation of policies, programs, and activities;
   b. provision of education; and
   c. enhancement of community participation.  
2. Prevention of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented by the Regional Government in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.  
3. Planning and implementation of policies, programs and activities as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a namely:
   a. integrating women's and children's protection issues into the policies, programs and activities of Regional Apparatus; and
   b. making women's and children's protection issues one of the priorities of regional development.  
4. Provision of education as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b namely:
   a. providing information, guidance and/or counseling;
   b. providing accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;
   c. creating a friendly and safe work environment atmosphere for women; and
   d. building and providing an easily accessible information system.  
5. Enhancement of community participation through involvement of parties as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, namely:
   a. building and enhancing community participation and awareness towards prevention of women's and children's protection;
   b. making regional regulations and encouraging Village Governments to make Village Regulations related to protection of women and children; and
   c. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, private sector/business world, universities, mass media, and non-governmental organizations as well as community institutions that work and care for women and children.

# CHAPTER V

## PROVISION OF SERVICE INSTITUTIONS

#### Article 6

1. In order to provide protection services to women and children, the Regional Government establishes UPT PPA.  
2. UPT PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) performs the following service functions:
   a. public complaints;
   b. victim outreach;
   c. case management;
   d. temporary shelter;
   e. mediation; and
   f. victim assistance.  
3. In performing service functions as referred to in paragraph (2) UPT PPA requires support:
   a. infrastructure facilities and infrastructure;
   b. sufficient number of management personnel;
   c. competent human resources;
   d. budget; and
   e. safe house facilities.  
4. Further provisions regarding the organization and working procedures of UPT PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in the Regent's Regulation.

#### Article 7

1. Victim assistance as referred to in Article 6 paragraph (2) letter f includes all integrated efforts to restore and strengthen the victim's condition.  
2. Victim assistance as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by UPT PPA.  
3. Victim assistance is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 8

Assistants have the duties to:

a. provide sufficient information to victims about their rights; 
b. provide psychosocial services to victims so that victims feel safe and comfortable;  
c. accompany victims during the examination and medical recovery process; 
d. accompany victims during the medicolegal process;  
e. accompany victims during the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;  
f. accompany victims while in the safe house;  
g. monitor the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;  
h. maintain the privacy and confidentiality of victims' identities from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media; 
i. coordinate with other assistants; and 
j. provide continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 9

1. In performing the service functions of UPT PPA as referred to in Article 6 paragraph (2) UPT PPA uses a Case Management approach.  
2. The Case Management approach as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
   a. identification;
   b. assessment;
   c. intervention planning;
   d. intervention implementation;
   e. monitoring and evaluation;
   f. follow-up; and
   g. case termination.  
3. The procedures for implementing the case management approach as referred to in paragraph (2) are established by standard operating procedures.

#### Article 10

1. In order to maximize the function and role of UPT PPA, the agency in charge of Women's Empowerment and Children as the parent of UPT PPA may establish TRC which is determined through the Regent's Decree.  
2. Membership of TRC as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
   a. paralegals;
   b. women's and children's activists;
   c. health workers;
   d. mass media; and/or
   e. observers of vulnerable groups.  
3. Requirements for TRC members namely:
   a. Indonesian citizens proven by population identity;
   b. have a good perspective on women and children;
   c. physically and mentally healthy; and
   d. have good communication skills preferably those who are able to use sign language.  
4. TRC as referred to in paragraph (1) has the duties and functions of conducting outreach and victim assistance.  
5. TRC reports the results of task implementation to the head of the Agency in charge of women and children through the head of UPT PPA.

#### Article 11

1. The Regional Government establishes a Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children.  
2. Membership of the Task Force as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of Regional Apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
   a. social welfare;
   b. health;
   c. education;
   d. manpower;
   e. population and civil registration;
   f. law;
   g. women's empowerment and child protection;
   h. cooperatives and micro enterprises;
   i. community empowerment;
   j. religious affairs;
   k. planning and research;
   l. disaster and emergency; and
   m. peace and order.  
3. The Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children is established in the Regent's Decree.

#### Article 12

1. The Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children has the duties to:
   a. promote the Protection of Women and Children to the community on an ongoing basis;
   b. carry out socialization and advocacy of the Protection of Women and Children to government officials and the community;
   c. prepare a regional action plan for the Protection of Women and Children;
   d. prepare an annual work program;
   e. encourage the realization of planning and budgeting that supports the Protection of Women and children; and
   f. formulate recommendations for regent's policies.  
2. The Task Force as referred to in paragraph (1) is responsible to the Regent.  

#### Article 13

The provision of services at women's and children's protection service institutions is carried out with the principles of:

a. no fees charged;  
b. fast;  
c. safe;  
d. empathy;  
e. non-discrimination;  
f. easily accessible; and
g. confidentiality.

# CHAPTER VI

## INSTITUTIONAL STRENGTHENING

#### Article 14

In order to maximize the function and role of service officers, the following are needed:

a. increasing the number and capacity of human resources of service officers; 
b. development of communication, information, and education systems about women's and children's protection services;  
c. coordination system between UPT PPA and other service institutions at the Regional level;  
d. integrated case data system;  
e. system and/or mechanism for handling and or assisting cases; and
f. improvement of service facilities and infrastructure.

# CHAPTER VII

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 15

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children are a shared responsibility namely:

a. Regional Government;  
b. Village Government;  
c. private sector;  
d. community institutions and civil society organizations; and
e. family, parents or guardians.

## Part One

Regional Government

#### Article 16

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 15 letter a include:
   a. establishing policies for the protection of women and children;
   b. implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
   c. cooperating with all parties in the implementation of protection of women and children;
   d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children;
   e. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children, in accordance with regional financial capacity;
   f. encouraging the role of the private sector and community institutions to be involved in supporting the protection of women and children;

9 -

   g. encouraging the establishment of local institutions at the urban village and/or village level to provide protection for women and children; and
   h. encouraging the establishment of regulations at the village level governing the protection of women and children;
   i. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children; and
   j. realizing a Child-Friendly Regency.
2. In addition to the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to provide special protection to Children.
3. Special Protection for Children as referred to in paragraph (2) is provided to:
   a. children in emergency situations;
   b. children in conflict with the law;
   c. children from minority and isolated groups;
   d. children who are exploited economically and/or sexually including child marriage;
   e. children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic substances, and other addictive substances abuse;
   f. children who are victims of pornography;
   g. children with Human Immunodeficiency Virus/Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome;
   h. children who are victims of kidnapping, sale, and/or trafficking;
   i. children who are victims of physical and/or psychological violence;
   j. children who are victims of sexual crimes;
   k. children who are victims of terrorist networks;
   l. children with disabilities;
   m. children who are victims of abuse and neglect;
   n. children with deviant social behavior; and
   o. children who are victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their parents' condition.
4. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in this Article shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus or appointed Officials.

#### Article 17

1. In order to implement the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in Article 16, the Regent shall establish programs and activities for the protection of women and children in the form of a regional action plan as a basis for Regional Apparatus in implementing protection for women and children.
2. The regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (1), constitutes part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

#### Article 18

a. establishing policies for the protection of women and children at the village level;
b. implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
c. cooperating with all parties in the implementation of protection of women and children;

e. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children, in accordance with village financial capacity;
f. involving community institutions at the village level in supporting the protection of women and children;

## Part Three

Private Sector

#### Article 19

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the private sector as referred to in Article 15 letter c shall be implemented in the form of private sector participation.
(2) The role of the private sector as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
   a. providing protection for women and children through corporate social and environmental responsibility programs;
   b. providing support to the government, community institutions, civil society organizations and the community in the context of protection for women and children; and
   c. actively participating in business organizations that have a commitment to the protection of women and children.

## Part Four

Community Institutions and civil society organizations

#### Article 20

The obligations and responsibilities of Community Institutions and Civil Society Organizations as referred to in Article 15 letter d include:

a. providing information and/or reporting every occurrence of vulnerability and violence risks that they know of;
b. providing protection for women and children;
c. providing emergency assistance;
d. accompanying victims, providing shelters and/or safe houses initiated by community institutions or civil society organizations; and
e. assisting in the process of repatriation, social rehabilitation and social reintegration.

a. caring for, maintaining, educating, and protecting Children;

c. preventing marriage at the age of Children; and
d. providing character education and instilling moral values in Children - 11 -

   a. the central government;
   b. the provincial government;

   e. the community;

# CHAPTER IX

## FUNDING

#### Article 26

Financing for the implementation of protection of women and children is sourced from:

a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget;
b. Village Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
c. other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 27

1. Funding for the implementation of protection for women and children includes:
   a. prevention of violence against women and children;  
   b. service institutions including service facilities and infrastructure;  
   c. medical services, which include doctor examinations, treatment costs, hospital costs, medication costs, and medical support costs;  
   d. medicolegal services, which include examinations for visum et repertum, visum et repertum psychiatricum, and Deoxyribonucleic Acid tests;  
   e. psychosocial services, consisting of counseling and psychological therapy, safe houses and safe house management;  
   f. legal services and assistance including costs of assistance at the police, prosecutor's office, and court;  
   g. operational costs of service institutions, including outreach transportation, assistance transportation, and fulfillment of victim needs while at the service institution; and  
   h. service institution officers, including honorarium and capacity building.
2. Management of funding as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

##### IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

##### I. GENERAL

The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia has mandated to provide protection for women and children, as well as to provide equal rights for women to participate in contributing to various aspects of development. This is also reaffirmed in Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government regarding the division of government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection. In line with this, Regional Governments which have the principle of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy, also strive to realize the ideals contained in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.  

Based on this, the Regional Government of Bulukumba Regency has the desire to realize its commitment in order to provide protection for women and children so that their rights can be fulfilled and to realize gender equality and justice in all aspects in accordance with human dignity and worth, as well as to optimize women's empowerment, child protection, and gender mainstreaming in the region so that efforts need to be made involving all parties in various aspects of development.  

In an effort to realize this commitment, the Regional Government of Bulukumba Regency has established a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children which regulates clearly, firmly, and comprehensively while also being proof of the seriousness of the commitment of the Government of Bulukumba Regency in protecting the rights of women and children as well as providing a legal umbrella in fighting for their rights.

#### Article 2

Letter a What is meant by the principle of humanity is the principle of humanity emphasizes respect and protection for the basic rights of every individual regardless of gender, age, or social status.  

This means that women and children have the same right to live without violence and treatment that degrades human dignity.  

15 -

Letter b What is meant by the principle of justice and gender equality is this principle emphasizes the need to address gender inequality which is often the root cause of violence against women and children. Protection must ensure that women and children receive fair and equal treatment in the legal system and society without discrimination based on gender.  

Letter c What is meant by the principle of protection is proactive and preventive protection against acts of violence. This protection must be carried out through an approach that includes prevention, handling, rehabilitation, and support for victims as well as potential perpetrators so that violence does not occur again.  

Letter d What is meant by the principle of best interests for women and children is this principle focuses on the best interests and rights of women and children as the top priority. Protection actions and policies must always prioritize their welfare and interests, including listening to their views and aspirations in the decision-making process.  

Letter e What is meant by the principle of non-discrimination is this principle affirms that protection must be carried out without discrimination based on gender, age, ethnic background, religion, or social status. Every individual has the same right to be protected from acts of violence, without exception.

Letter f What is meant by the principle of special treatment for vulnerable groups is treatment of a group of people who experience physical and non-physical vulnerability or limitations such as women, children, persons with disabilities, pregnant women, the elderly, minority groups, people with mental disorders and the poor.
