Pasa11 In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by: 1. Regency is Penukal Abab Lematang Ilir Regency. 2. Regency Government is the Regent as an element of the regency government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.

Woman is a human being of female sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman. 7. Fulfillment of Children's Rights is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination. 8. Violence is any act that results in suffering or hardship whether physical, sexual, psychological, and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of a person's freedom. 9. Victim is a woman and/or child who experiences suffering and/or hardship either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring in the area of Penukal Abab Lematang Ilir Regency. 10. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.  
11. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
12. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children. 13. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will, is or has occurred.

15. Health Rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of victims including physical, psychological and social health so that victims can carry out their roles again properly and reasonably both in the family and in society.

# CHAPTER II

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

Rights of Women Article 4 Every Woman has the right:
a. to live, sustain life and her existence; 
b. to form a family and continue offspring through lawful marriage;  
c. to develop herself through the fulfillment of her basic needs; 
d. to obtain education and benefit from science and technology, arts and culture, in order to improve the quality of her life and for the welfare of humankind;  
e. to advance herself in fighting for her rights collectively to build society, nation and state;  
f. to recognition, guarantee, protection and fair legal certainty as well as equal treatment before the law;  
g. to work and receive fair and proper remuneration and treatment in employment relations;  
h. to obtain equal opportunities in government;  
i. to freedom of association, assembly and expression of opinion;  

j. to protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property under her control, and the right to a sense of security and protection from threats of fear to do or not do something which is a basic right;  
k. to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and the right to obtain political asylum from other countries;  
l. to live in physical and spiritual prosperity, to reside and obtain a good and healthy living environment, and the right to obtain health services;  

m. to social security that enables her full development as a dignified human being;  
n. to equality of position in law and government;  
o. to work and a livelihood worthy of humanity;  
p. in efforts to defend the state;  
q. to communicate and obtain information to develop her personality and social environment, and the right to seek, obtain, possess, store, process, and convey information using all types of available channels;  
r. to embrace her respective religion and to worship according to her religion and belief; and 
s. to obtain education.

#### Article 16

1. The Regency Government is obligated to protect the fulfillment of the rights of Child workers in the informal sector.  
2. Child workers in the informal sector as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
   a. domestic workers;  
   b. shoe shiners;  
   c. street vendors;  
   d. scavengers;  
   e. parking attendants;  
   f. children who work in the fields of agriculture, animal husbandry and plantations;  
   g. children who work in the mining sector; and  
   h. other informal sector work that employs Children.  
3. Every person who employs Children in the informal sector must observe the following requirements:  
   a. minimum age of 15 (fifteen) years, except for the category of Worst Forms of Child Labor minimum 18 (eighteen) years;  
   b. obtain written consent from the parents/guardian of the Child worker;  
   c. have a written employment agreement between the employer and the parents/guardian of the Child worker and obtain validation from the authorized agency;  
   d. not be employed at night;  
   e. working time of at most 3 (three) hours per day;  
   f. not be employed in a place or environment that can disrupt the child's growth and development process, whether physical, mental, moral and intellectual or the Child's health;  
   g. provide opportunities to receive education in accordance with their talents and interests;  
   h. employ for light types of work; and  
   1. provide one day off per week.  

#### Article 17

a. counseling to stakeholders about the rights of Children;  
b. psychological, medical and legal assistance and socio-economic reintegration;  
c. family empowerment through the provision of training, business capital stimulus and assistance;  
d. scholarships to continue formal education to a higher level; and  
e. non-formal education and skills training for those who do not pursue formal education.

# CHAPTER III

## INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER (PPT)

#### Article 24

(1) The provision of services to Women and Children victims of violence is carried out in an integrated manner by the PPT.

(2) The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is an integrated part and constitutes one unit with the PPT that provides integrated services for witnesses and/or victims of criminal acts of trafficking in persons.

(3) The provision of services to Women and Children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

a. providing fast and free services to victims;  
b. providing protection and fulfillment of victims' rights to health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration and legal assistance;  
c. cooperating with certain institutions in the provision of interpreters and volunteer assistants needed for victims;  
d. networking with government or private hospitals for the care and recovery of victims' health and cooperating with witness and victim protection institutions, social protection houses or trauma centers owned by the government, community or other institutions for the recovery of victims' health;  
e. providing convenience, comfort and safety for victims;  
f. maintaining the confidentiality of victims; and  
g. providing fulfillment of legal assistance for victims.

(4) Further provisions regarding the procedures and mechanisms for the implementation of PPT are regulated by Regent Regulation.

## REGARDING

## THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## I. GENERAL

Violence against women and children constitutes a violation of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination. The fate of women and children who are victims of violence must be given attention by the regional government. The high number of cases of violence against women and children is often caused by factors that develop in society, such as low economic levels, education, and environments located in the industrial sector. Therefore, victims of such violence need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights.

For the empowerment of victims of violence, the district government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT) which is a generic term that can be used by various institutions that provide services for victims of violence such as the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A), Safe Houses, Child Social Protection Houses (RPSA), Trauma Center Protection Houses (RPTC), Women's Social Protection Houses (RPSW), Shelters, etc.

The District Government is obligated and responsible to implement efforts to protect women and children through implementing protection policies for women and children applied by the government, establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children, establishing PPT, ensuring the implementation of service facilities for victims, seeking effectiveness and efficiency for the victim recovery process, and seeking the creation of cooperation and coordination in efforts to protect women and children.

#### Article 18

Letter a What is meant by "physical violence" is an act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes death.  

Letter b What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.  

Letter c What is meant by "sexual violence" is an act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse and/or forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  

Letter d What is meant by "neglect" is an act that results in the failure to fulfill a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care; an act of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care; an act of neglecting a person within the household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or an act that results in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.  

Letter e What is meant by "exploitation" is an act that exploits economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others; an act with or without the victim's consent including prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or any form of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.  

Letter f What is meant by "other violence" is the threat of violence including: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that create fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and coercion, including: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

Letter a What is meant by "quick" is immediate action taken without complications or simplified procedures.

Letter c What is meant by "empathy" is an act of appreciating, respecting, loving, being friendly, and making happy that aims to please and calm the victim's heart.  

Letter d What is meant by "non-discrimination" is handling of victims provided to all victims without distinguishing tribe, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.  

Letter e What is meant by "guarantee of confidentiality" is an effort to guarantee certainty for victims that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disseminated. 

Letter f What is meant by "easily accessible" is the provision of services and assistance for all people regardless of their social status, so that such services are affordable for the underprivileged or relatively sufficient for the privileged.  

Letter g What is meant by "free of charge" is that the service and assistance activities carried out by PPT are not charged to the victim.  

#### Article 22

Letter a What is meant by "complaint, consultation, and counseling services" includes initial identification or recording of victims; case identification; and consent for action to be taken.  

Letter b What is meant by "assistance services" includes accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process; accompanying victims during the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court; monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court; maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media; providing a sense of security to victims; and providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.  

Letter c What is meant by "medical rehabilitation services" includes first aid to victims; treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics; and referral to health services.  

Letter d What is meant by "social rehabilitation services" is services provided in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims. Forms of services are carried out through social support through spiritual guidance to victims; and psychological recovery of victims.  

Letter e What is meant by "legal services" is to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by providing legal consultation that includes information regarding victims' rights and the judicial process; accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and court hearing examination levels and helping victims to fully describe the violence they experienced; and coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer assistants, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.  

Letter f What is meant by "social reintegration services" is aimed at returning victims to their families and social environment. Social reintegration services are carried out by the District/Regency Government by coordinating with relevant government and non-government agencies and institutions; and village community institutions and/or local community leaders.
