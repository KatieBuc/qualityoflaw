NUMBER 5 OF 2022

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

THE REGENT OF CENTRAL MALUKU,

Considering:




a. that as citizens, women and children have the right to obtain protection from any act of violence committed against them that may result in victims of physical, psychological, sexual violence, or domestic neglect;
b. that protection for women and children victims of violence is an effort to provide a sense of security and freedom from all acts of violence guaranteed by the State;
c. that the government including Regional Government, families, social organizations and/or other community organizations have the responsibility to prevent acts of violence and protect women and children victims of violence in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations;
d. that Regional Regulations concerning the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence require substantial changes related to institutions to be adjusted to the latest regulations at the National level, and have not comprehensively regulated the efforts to implement protection that guarantee the fulfillment of victims' rights to truth, justice, recovery and guarantees of non-recurrence of cases, so that new Regional Regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation.
e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, letter c and letter d, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  
1. Region is Central Maluku Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Regent together with the relevant Regional Apparatus Organizations as elements of the Regional Government administration.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Central Maluku.  
4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of Central Maluku Regency as an element of the Regional Government administration.  
5. Agency is the Agency that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.  
6. Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA, is an institution established by the provincial or regency/city regional government to provide services to women and children experiencing criminal and civil problems.  
7. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to provide prevention, protection, handling and fulfillment of the rights of women and children victims of violence by providing complaint services, medical services, social rehabilitation, legal aid, as well as social reintegration and repatriation.  
8. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.  
9. Woman is a woman who has reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years or more.  
10. Violence against women (and children) is any act against a person, especially women (and children), that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or economic loss.  
11. Victim of violence is a woman and/or child who experiences suffering either physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or economic loss as a result of an act of violence.  
12. Prevention is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women and/or children carried out by the government, family, social institutions or other community institutions, police, prosecutors or other parties to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and/or children.  
13. 16 Days of Activism Against Violence Against Women (and Children) Campaign is a campaign to encourage efforts to eliminate violence against women (and children) throughout the world, which is held annually for 16 days from November 25 to December 10.  
14. Protection of women and children is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by the government, family, social institutions or other community institutions, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties either temporarily or based on court decisions or determinations, in connection with acts of violence against women and children.  
15. Spiritual guidance service is recovery carried out with a spiritual, belief and religious approach.  
16. Complaint service is a series of actions carried out by UPT PPA organizers to follow up on reports of acts of violence against victims of violence.  
17. Health rehabilitation service is the recovery of victims of violence from the health problems they suffer.  
18. Medicolegal service is medical service and operational service in the field of medicine and law, including visum examination.  
19. Social Rehabilitation Service is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.  
20. Legal Aid Service is legal services provided by legal aid providers free of charge to legal aid recipients.  
21. Repatriation service is an effort to return victims of violence to their area of origin or to family, substitute family or community.  
22. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims of violence with family, substitute family and community that can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.  
23. Recovery is medical/psychiatric and psychological action toward victims of violence as an effort to restore the physical and psychological condition of victims to their original state.  
24. Companion is a person trusted by the victim to accompany them during the criminal justice process.  
25. Paralegal Assistance is legal assistance by a companion who does not have qualifications as a legal practitioner.  
26. Health facility is a hospital, community health center, auxiliary community health center, clinic, village health post, midwife/nurse/paramedic/doctor practice place.  
27. Regional Action Plan is the foundation and guideline for relevant agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of violence.  
28. Safe House is a temporary residence provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined operational standards.  
29. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.  
30. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in a direct line upward or downward or sideways up to the third degree.  
31. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.

#### Article 2

Principles 

1. Protection of women and children is based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia as well as the principles and basic rights as regulated in the Convention on Women and Children, including:  
a. respect for human rights;  
b. non-discrimination;  
c. the best interests of women and/or children;  
d. the right to life, survival and development;  
e. respect for the opinions of women and children;  
f. gender equality and justice; and  
g. victim protection;  
2. Legal Certainty and Local Wisdom.

#### Article 3

Objectives The objectives of protection of women and children victims of violence are:  
a. to ensure the fulfillment of the rights of women and/or children in accordance with applicable provisions;  
b. to prevent and protect women and/or children from acts of violence;  
c. to encourage the community to carry out their obligations in the prevention and protection of women and children in accordance with applicable laws and regulations; and  
d. to restore victims from the consequences of acts of violence experienced.

#### Article 4

Scope The scope of the implementation of protection of women and children Victims of Violence includes:  
a. institutional implementation of protection of women and children;  
b. duties and responsibilities for prevention, protection and recovery;  
c. forms and mechanisms of prevention; and  
d. provision of medical services, medicolegal, special examination rooms, legal aid, expert personnel, psychological recovery, safe houses, social companions, religious leaders, customary institution support, repatriation, social reintegration, economic empowerment and other services needed by women and children victims of violence to support the fulfillment of the rights of women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER II

## VICTIMS' RIGHTS

#### Article 5

Victims have the right to receive:  
a. protection from family, Regional Government, police, prosecution, court, legal aid institutions, social institutions or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court;  
b. special treatment related to the security and confidentiality of victims;  
c. physical, mental health services and psychological services according to the suffering experienced by victims;  
d. assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations;  
e. spiritual guidance services; and  
f. guarantee of continuity of education especially for children who are victims of violence.

#### Article 6

In the event of an act of violence, every victim has the right to receive assistance both psychologically and legally and to receive guarantees of their rights related to their status as a wife, mother, child, family member or as a member of society.

# CHAPTER III

## DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## PREVENTION, PROTECTION AND RECOVERY

#### Article 7

Prevention, protection and recovery of women and children victims of violence are the duties and responsibilities including:  
a. regional government;  
b. community, social organizations and/or other community organizations in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations; and  
c. family, and/or parents.

#### Article 8

1. The duties and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 7 letter a, include:  
a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;  
b. establishing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence;  
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence to related agencies both vertical and regional;  
e. allocating budget for the protection of women and children victims of violence according to the financial capacity of the Region; and  
f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in a Regional action plan.  
3. The Regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (2) is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.  
4. Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 9

The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 7 letter b, include:  
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;  
b. providing protection to victims;  
c. providing emergency assistance;  
d. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to the competent authorities; and  
e. participating in the handling of victims of violence.

#### Article 10

The obligations and responsibilities of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 7 letter c include:  
a. preventing all forms of violence and protecting women and children as family members.  
b. providing support for women and children victims of violence who are part of their family members.

# CHAPTER IV

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional

#### Article 11

1. To coordinate the duties and responsibilities as referred to in Article 7, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Unit.  
2. The Integrated Service Unit as referred to in paragraph (1) is P2TP2A.  
3. P2TP2A as UPT PPA in the Region may take the form of a Regional Coordinator or other designation established by the Regent.

#### Article 12

1. P2TP2A is located in the capital of Central Maluku Regency with a working area covering the entire area of Central Maluku Regency.  
2. P2TP2A may encourage the availability of women's and children's service centers at the sub-district and/or Negeri and Administrative Negeri levels.

#### Article 13

1. The purpose of establishing P2TP2A is:a. to facilitate the needs of women and children victims of violence in fulfilling victims' rights, namely the right to truth, justice, recovery and guarantee of non-recurrence of cases; and  
b. to realize welfare, justice and Gender equality in various fields of life for women and children comprehensively.  
2. The main duties of P2TP2A are:  
a. making efforts to prevent violence against women and children;  
b. making efforts to handle women and children victims of violence; and  
c. making rehabilitation efforts including recovery, empowerment, repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence.  
3. The functions of P2TP2A are:  
a. as a data and information center on women and children;  
b. as a service center for women and children victims of violence; and  
c. as a center for empowerment of women and children.

#### Article 14

The position of P2TP2A is under the Regional Government and is responsible to the Regent.

#### Article 15

1. P2TP2A consists of at least a Chairman/Head, a Vice Chairman/Vice Head, a Secretary, a Treasurer and fields in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations.  
2. The membership of P2TP2A consists of at least representatives of related regional apparatus organizations within the scope of the Regional Government, Police, Prosecution, Court, Non-Governmental Organizations, Community Leaders, Religious Leaders and Legal Aid Institutions.  
3. To be appointed as a member of the P2TP2A management, one must meet the requirements as stipulated in the applicable Laws and Regulations.  
4. The term of office of the P2TP2A management is 3 (three) years from the date of inauguration, and may subsequently be reappointed for one subsequent period.

#### Article 16

P2TP2A is obliged to:  
a. provide services and handling as quickly as possible to victims;  
b. provide ease and comfort of services for victims;  
c. provide services free of charge for victims;  
d. maintain the security and confidentiality of victims; and  
e. strive to achieve justice and legal certainty for victims.

## Part Two

Prevention

#### Article 17

Prevention of acts of violence against women and children can be implemented through:  
a. socialization of Laws and Regulations to the community related to the protection of women and children on a regular basis;  
b. campaign for 16 Days of Anti-Violence against women and children every year;  
c. integrating issues of violence against women and children into the education curriculum in schools;  
d. implementation of referral mechanisms in the handling and recovery of victims of violence against women and children; and  
e. coordination between institutions that have main duties and functions related to the prevention and handling of violence against women and children.

#### Article 18

In addition to prevention efforts as referred to in Article 17, the Regional Government makes efforts to raise awareness of the community, families and parents through:  
a. increasing the number and quality of education both formal, non-formal and informal;  
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services; and  
c. building community participation and concern to carry out prevention and against women and children from acts of violence.

#### Article 19

1. Prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 17 and Article 18, shall be implemented by regional apparatus organizations in accordance with their duties and functions in the fields of:  
a. women's empowerment and child protection;  
b. health;  
c. mental and spiritual;  
d. social;  
e. education;  
f. employment; and  
g. peace and order.  
2. Prevention of acts of violence by regional apparatus working units as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented based on the Regional Action Plan.

## Part Three

Services for Victims

#### Article 20

1. Services for victims shall be organized in cooperation among related agencies/institutions, comprising:  
a. legal services in the form of legal aid and paralegal assistance;  
b. medical services;  
c. medicolegal services;  
d. psychological services;  
e. rehabilitation services;  
f. social reintegration and repatriation services; and  
g. support services for economic independence.  
2. Coordination of services for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by P2TP2A.

#### Article 21

1. In addition to the forms of services as referred to in Article 20, the Regional Government through P2TP2A is obliged to provide temporary protection services for victims comprising, but not limited to:a. initial rescue of victims from acts of violence; and b. medical emergency treatment for victims.  
2. To provide protection as referred to in paragraph (1), P2TP2A may cooperate with security apparatus, Village Government and Administrative Village, sub-district, customary leaders and or community groups that provide assistance services for women and children victims of violence

#### Article 22

1. The Regent shall establish minimum service standards provided to women and children victims of violence.  
2. In addition to the minimum service standards as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent shall establish special Minimum Service Standards for victims with Disabilities.

#### Article 23

1. In addition to services for victims as referred to in Article 20, Article 21, Article 22, the Regional Government is obliged to provide assistance services for victims.  
2. Assistance services for victims may be implemented in cooperation with persons or institutions providing services for victims.  
3. The assistance mechanism as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures established by Regent Regulation.

## Part Four

Empowerment of Violence Victims

#### Article 24

1. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out empowerment of women and children victims of violence.  
2. Empowerment of victims as referred to in paragraph (1), shall involve P2TP2A.  
3. In carrying out empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall prepare empowerment programs in the economic field, comprising:  
a. providing the necessary requirements for entrepreneurship training for women victims of violence in order to increase business knowledge, attitudes and skills;  
b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;  
c. providing assistance in developing economic enterprises;  
d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large enterprises, as well as financial institutions to develop businesses of women victims of acts of violence;  
e. endeavoring to provide capital for women victims of acts of violence; and  
f. expanding access to information and promoting products of women victims of acts of violence.  
4. In carrying out empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may establish cooperation with job training institutions organized by the private sector.

# CHAPTER V

## COMPLAINT AND HANDLING PROCEDURES

#### Article 25

1. Complaints of violence victims to P2TP2A may be submitted by victims, families, community members, police officers, volunteer assistants, or social workers.  
2. Leaders or officers present at P2TP2A are obliged to serve victims based on the provisions as referred to in Article 21.  
3. Leaders or officers of P2TP2A shall immediately handle victims in accordance with established procedures.  
4. Leaders or officers of P2TP2A within a maximum period of 24 (twenty-four) hours from receiving the victim's complaint are obliged to report it to the nearest police officer.

#### Article 26

In the event that the victim is outside the area of their origin, P2TP2A is obliged to immediately report to the Regent, so that the Regent coordinates with the Regent/Mayor of the victim's origin to take protective actions or measures and repatriation of the victim to their area of origin.

#### Article 27

1. In handling victims, P2TP2A is obliged to network with government or private hospitals for treatment and recovery of their health.  
2. Where necessary, P2TP2A may also network with social protection homes or trauma centers owned by the government or community, or safe houses owned by the government or community, or other social service institutions.

#### Article 28

Further provisions regarding complaint procedures and handling of violence victims shall be further regulated in the P2TP2A Standard Operating Procedures.

# CHAPTER VI

## COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 29

1. The Regional Government may conduct cooperation in order to achieve the objectives of organizing protection of women and children victims of violence.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted with:  
a. the Government;  
b. provincial government;  
c. other regency/city governments; and  
d. non-governmental institutions.  
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1), comprises:  
a. Exchange of data and information;  
b. Rehabilitation of victims of acts of Violence;  
c. Repatriation and social reintegration; and  
d. Provision of evidence and witnesses and followed up in accordance with Laws and Regulations.  
4. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), shall be conducted in the form of a Joint Agreement.

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 30

1. The Regional Government shall establish partnerships with the business world in organizing protection of women and children.  
2. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be conducted through:  
a. notification of information on employment opportunities for women victims of violence;  
b. education and training for women and children victims of violence;  
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of violence; and  
d. fostering and increasing economic independence of women victims of violence.  
3. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be implemented in the form of a joint agreement.

# CHAPTER VII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 31

1. The Regional Government is obliged to provide guidance on the organization of protection of women and children victims of violence comprising technical guidance and training.  
2. The Regional Government is obliged to conduct supervision and evaluation of the organization of protection of women and children victims of violence.  
3. Supervision and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be conducted with professional, transparent, accountable principles.  
4. In addition to supervision conducted by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (2), the community may conduct supervision of the organization of protection of women and children victims of violence.  
5. Supervision conducted by the community as referred to in paragraph (4) shall be by conveying aspirations to the Regent or DPRD.

# CHAPTER VIII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 32

1. The community participates in the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of:  
a. establishing protection units or complaint posts for women and children victims of violence within community organizations and/or within the community of Negeri, Administrative Negeri/kelurahan;  
b. independently conducting socialization of women's and children's rights;  
c. preventing the occurrence of violence;  
d. providing first aid to victims; and  
e. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector and mass media.

# CHAPTER IX

## REPORTING

#### Article 33

1. P2TP2A is obliged to report the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence to the Regent through the relevant Agency.  
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be submitted in writing, covering administration, finance, services and performance.  
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out at least every 1 (one) month.

# CHAPTER X

## FINANCING

#### Article 34

1. The Regional Government is obliged to provide a budget expenditure every year through the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget as well as other lawful and non-binding sources of income to finance the implementation of tasks for prevention, protection and recovery of women and children victims of violence.  
2. The budget for prevention, protection and recovery activities for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is attached to the relevant Agency and managed by P2TP2A in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 35

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or civil society organizations that carry out protection for women and children victims of violence.  
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided in accordance with the Regional Financial capacity and implemented in accordance with the provisions of legislation in the field of regional finance.

# CHAPTER XI

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 36

With the enactment of this Regional Regulation, Regional Regulation of Central Maluku Regency Number 12 of 2014 concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in Central Maluku Regency is declared invalid.

#### Article 37

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

# ELUCIDATION OF CENTRAL MALUKU REGIONAL REGULATION

NUMBER 5 OF 2022 CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide protection to every citizen in accordance with the Preamble to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. Women and children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience violence and therefore need to receive protection. Violence against women and children is a violation of human rights, so victims need to be protected in their dignity and honor and their right to life must be guaranteed without discrimination.  

Violence against women and children is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or hardship for women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the child.  

The existence of women and children victims of violence has not yet received adequate services, so comprehensive implementation of Protection for Women and Children is required.  

In 2014, the Regional Government and DPRD established Regional Regulation Number 12 concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children. However, the said PERDA needs to be amended regarding institutional matters to be adjusted to the latest regulations at the National level. In addition, the PERDA has not yet comprehensively regulated the protection implementation efforts that guarantee the fulfillment of victims' rights to truth, justice, recovery and guarantees of non-recurrence of cases, so a new regional regulation is needed that can guarantee its implementation.  

This new Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children, particularly in terms of institutions, forms and mechanisms of prevention, prevention of acts of violence, assistance mechanisms, services for victims of violence and empowerment of victims of violence.

#### Article 1

#### Article 2

Letter a  
What is meant by "respect for human rights" is respect and treatment of Women and Children in accordance with the rights they possess as part of human rights.  

Letter b  
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is all actions and deeds carried out without differentiating based on economic, social, cultural, political background, gender, age, religion, race and so forth.  

Letter c  
What is meant by "the best interests of women and children" is all actions concerning Women and/or children carried out by the Government, Society, Legislative Bodies, and Judicial Bodies where the best interests of women and/or children must receive primary consideration.  

Letter d  
What is meant by "the right to life, survival, and development" is the most fundamental human right for Women and/or Children that is protected by the state, government, society, family and parents.  

Letter e  
What is meant by "respect for the opinions of women and children" is respect for the rights of women and/or children to participate and express their opinions in making decisions, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.  

Letter f  
What is meant by "gender equality and justice" is respect for the position of women including girls that is equal and equivalent to other members of society, fairly without discriminating against them in all aspects of life.  

Letter g  
What is meant by "victim protection" is actions taken to prevent and protect women and/or children who experience acts of violence both physically and psychologically.

#### Article 21

Paragraph 1  
Item a  
What is meant by "Initial rescue of victims from acts of violence" is that victims have the right to rescue action when acts of violence occur if the victim's safety is threatened. This rescue action must be carried out by village/sub-district officials at the victim's place of residence or at the location where the act of violence occurred. Village/sub-district officials must immediately coordinate with the nearest security officers.  

Item b  
What is meant by "Medical emergency treatment for victims" is when victims experience fairly severe injuries, then village/sub-district officials or medical officers or security officers or family/community members who know of the occurrence of acts of violence are obliged to bring the victim to the nearest service facility to provide emergency assistance before the victim is taken to the hospital for further treatment.
