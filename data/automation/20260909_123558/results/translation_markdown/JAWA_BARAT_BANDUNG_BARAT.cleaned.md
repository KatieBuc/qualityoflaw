# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Part One

Definitions

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is West Bandung Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of West Bandung.
4. Agency for Women's Empowerment, Child Protection and Family Planning, hereinafter abbreviated as BP3AKB is a regional apparatus that has the main duties, functions, and affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment, Child Protection and Family Planning in the Region.
5. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
6. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
7. Women's Empowerment is an effort to strengthen the basic rights of women, improve the quality of life, and increase the participation of women in development.
8. Gender Mainstreaming hereinafter abbreviated as PUG is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of policies, programs, and development activities in the region.
9. Improvement of Women's Quality of Life hereinafter abbreviated as PKHP is an effort to improve the physical and mental conditions of women in fulfilling their rights and life needs as part of human rights from various fields of development, especially education, health, economy, science and technology (IPTEK), socio-culture, politics, law and the environment.
10. Protection of Women and Children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and women over their rights so that they can live, grow, participate, be free from all forms of violence and discrimination in accordance with human dignity, and receive protection against trafficking in children and women.
11. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
12. Gender Analysis is an analysis to identify and understand the division of work/roles of men and women, access to and control over development resources, participation in the development process, and the benefits they enjoy, the pattern of unequal relationships between men and women, which in its implementation takes into account other factors such as social class, race, and ethnicity.
13. Gender Responsive Budget is a budget that is responsive to the needs of women and men with the aim of realizing gender equality and justice.
14. Gender Budget Statement hereinafter abbreviated as GBS is a document that informs that an activity output has been gender responsive to existing gender issues, and/or a cost has been allocated to the activity output to address gender gap problems.
15. Focal Point PUG is SKPD apparatus who have the ability to carry out gender mainstreaming in their respective work units.
16. Gender Mainstreaming Working Group hereinafter referred to as Pokja PUG is a consultation forum for implementers and drivers of gender mainstreaming from various agencies/institutions in the region.
17. Toddler Family Development hereinafter referred to as BKB is a community movement directed to increase the knowledge and skills of parents in fostering the growth and development of children aged 0-5 years.
18. Loving Mother Movement hereinafter referred to as GSI is a movement carried out by the community and government to improve the quality of life of women through various activities that have an impact on efforts to accelerate the reduction of maternal mortality rates (AKI) due to pregnancy, childbirth and postpartum as well as the reduction of infant mortality rates (AKB).
19. Model Village of Advanced Independent Indonesian Women hereinafter referred to as Model Desa PRIMA is a pilot village to overcome poverty through economic efforts accompanied by reduction of health and education cost burdens for poor families, by utilizing all potential/resources both natural and human.
20. Improvement of Women's Role Towards Healthy and Prosperous Families hereinafter referred to as P2WKSS is an integrated program to increase the role of women that uses a cross-sectoral development approach in a coordinated manner to improve family quality.
21. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service center in efforts to empower women in various fields of development, as well as protection of women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons, which is established by the government or community-based, which includes: referral centers, business consultation centers, reproductive health consultation centers, legal consultation centers, integrated crisis centers (PKT), integrated service centers (PPT), trauma recovery centers (trauma centers), women's crisis handling centers (women crisis centers), training centers, science and technology information centers (PIPTEK), safe houses (shelters), transit houses, or other forms.
22. Task Force for the Prevention and Handling of Criminal Acts of Trafficking in Persons is a forum for cross-sectoral collaboration and coordination that aims to ensure the implementation of prevention and handling of criminal acts of trafficking in persons, especially women and children in the Region.
23. Community institution is an institution formed by members of Indonesian citizen society voluntarily on the basis of shared vision, mission, profession, function and activities to participate in development in order to achieve national goals within the framework of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia based on Pancasila, which consists of religious organizations, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations, private organizations, social organizations, political organizations, mass media, and other forms of organizations.
24. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.
25. Non-Governmental Organizations and/or Community Organizations and/or Community Groups are community groups formed participatively and become government partners in women's empowerment and child protection.
26. Trafficking in Persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
27. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter referred to as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing services for handling reports/complaints, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance, as well as repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence.
28. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for Regional Governments in carrying out activities for the implementation of empowerment and protection of women and children.
29. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.
30. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
31. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the child.
32. Complaint is a series of actions carried out by integrated service providers to follow up on reports of acts of violence against women and children submitted by victims, families or the community.
33. Health services are efforts that include promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative aspects.
34. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.
35. Law enforcement is the action of apparatus authorized by the state to implement laws and regulations.
36. Legal assistance is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocates to carry out the process of accompanying witnesses and/or victims of violence against women and children that is Gender sensitive.
37. Repatriation is an effort to return women and children victims of violence from abroad to the debarkation point/entry point, or from the receiving area to the area of origin.
38. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
39. Child Friendly Regency hereinafter abbreviated as KLA is a city that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of commitment and resources of government, community and business world that is planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights.

## Part Two

Purpose and Objectives

#### Article 2

The formulation of this Regional Regulation has the purpose of providing a reference for the Regional Government in implementing Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in the Region.

#### Article 3

The implementation of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection has the objectives of:  
a. ensuring the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in a systematic, comprehensive, continuous, and integrated manner;  
b. increasing the commitment of the Regional Government in implementing policies, programs and activities for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection;  
c. increasing the effectiveness of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in the Region in a systematic, comprehensive, continuous and integrated manner;  
d. encouraging institutions that handle the duties and functions of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in the Region.

## Part Three

Scope

#### Article 4

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:  
a. Gender Mainstreaming;  
b. Improvement of Women's Quality of Life;  
c. Protection of Women and Children;  
d. Child-Friendly Regency (KLA);  
e. guidance and supervision; and  
f. financing.

# CHAPTER II

## GENDER MAINSTREAMING (PUG)

## Part One

Policy for Implementation of PUG

#### Article 5

1. The Regional Government formulates policies, programs, and Gender Responsive development activities which are set forth in:  
a. Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD);  
b. SKPD Strategic Plan; and  
c. SKPD Work Plan.  
2. The formulation of policies, programs, and Gender Responsive development activities as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through Gender Analysis.

#### Article 6

1. In conducting Gender Analysis as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2), the Gender Analysis Pathway method or other analytical methods may be used.  
2. Gender Analysis of the work plans and budgets of SKPDs is carried out by each SKPD and may cooperate with higher education institutions or other parties that have capability in their field.  
3. The results of Gender Analysis as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) are set forth in the preparation of GBS.  
4. The results of Gender Analysis contained in the GBS become the basis for SKPDs in preparing the terms of reference for activities and constitute an inseparable part of the RKA/DPA SKPD documents.

## Part Two

PUG Institutions

#### Article 7

1. In an effort to accelerate the institutionalization of Gender Mainstreaming in all SKPDs, the Regent establishes a PUG Working Group which is determined by Regent's Decree.  
2. The membership of the PUG Working Group as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of all heads/leaders of SKPDs.

#### Article 8

1. The PUG Working Group as referred to in Article 7 has the duties of:  
a. promoting and facilitating PUG to each SKPD;  
b. carrying out socialization and advocacy of PUG to Sub-district Heads and Village Heads;  
c. preparing a work program every year;  
d. encouraging the realization of Gender Responsive Planning and Budgeting;  
e. preparing the work plan of the PUG POKJA every year;  
f. being accountable to the Regent;  
g. formulating policy recommendations to the Regent;  
h. preparing the Regional Gender Profile;  
i. monitoring the implementation of PUG in each agency;  
j. establishing a technical team to conduct analysis of the regional budget;  
k. preparing the Regional Action Plan (RAD) for PUG in the Region; and  
l. encouraging the implementation of the selection and determination of Focal Points in each SKPD.  
2. The Technical Team as referred to in paragraph (1) letter j consists of officials who understand Gender Responsive Budget analysis.  
3. The Regional Action Plan (RAD) for PUG as referred to in paragraph (1) letter k contains:  
a. PUG in laws and regulations in the Region;  
b. PUG in the development cycle in the Region;  
c. strengthening of PUG institutions in the Region; and  
d. strengthening the role of community participation in the Region.

## Part Three

PUG Focal Point

#### Article 9

1. The PUG Focal Point in each SKPD in the Region consists of officials and/or staff in charge of planning and/or program duties.  
2. The PUG Focal Point as referred to in paragraph (1) has the duties of:  
a. promoting Gender Mainstreaming in the work unit;  
b. facilitating the preparation of Gender Responsive SKPD work plans and budgeting;  
c. carrying out training, socialization, advocacy of Gender Mainstreaming to all officials and staff within the SKPD;  
d. reporting the implementation of PUG to the SKPD leadership;  
e. encouraging the implementation of Gender Analysis of policies, programs, and activities in the work unit; and  
f. facilitating the preparation of gender data in each SKPD.  
3. The PUG Focal Point as referred to in paragraph (1) is selected and determined by the head/leader of the SKPD.

## Part Four

Reporting, Monitoring, and Evaluation of PUG

#### Article 10

1. The Regional Government submits reports on the implementation of PUG to the Provincial Government periodically every 6 (six) months.  
2. The report on the implementation of PUG as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. implementation of programs and activities;  
b. agencies involved in the implementation of activities;  
c. activity targets;  
d. use of budgets sourced from APBD or other sources;  
e. problems faced; and  
f. efforts that have been made.  
3. The procedures for reporting the implementation of PUG in the region are further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 11

1. The Regent conducts monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of PUG in the Region.  
2. Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in each SKPD.  
3. Monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of PUG is carried out before the preparation of programs or activities for the following year.  
4. The implementation of evaluation may be carried out through cooperation with Higher Education Institutions, Women's Study Centers, or Non-Governmental Organizations for Women's Empowerment and Protection.  
5. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of PUG become input material in the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the coming year.

## Part Five

PUG Guidance

#### Article 12

The Regent conducts guidance on the implementation of PUG which includes:  
a. determination of technical guidelines for the implementation of PUG at the Regency, Sub-district, and Village scale;  
b. strengthening institutional capacity through training, consultation, advocacy, and coordination;  
c. monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of PUG in the Region;  
d. capacity building of the PUG Working Group and focal points; and  
e. performance achievement strategy.

# CHAPTER III

## IMPROVEMENT OF WOMEN'S QUALITY OF LIFE (PKHP)

#### Article 13

PKHP is directed to obtain opportunities and rights for women to be able to play a role and participate in development, especially in the fields of education, health, economy, law and human rights, politics, environment, and socio-culture in the Region.

#### Article 14

The Regional Government implements policies, programs, and PKHP activities in various development fields, through:  
a. coordination of the implementation of policies, programs, and PKHP activities among SKPDs;  
b. cooperation with other Regional Governments in the implementation of policies, programs, and PKHP activities;  
c. strengthening the capacity of PUG institutions for the implementation of PKHP;  
d. facilitation of PKHP services;  
e. provision of PKHP services;  
f. implementation of PKHP affirmative action; and  
g. preparation of a PKHP data collection system.

#### Article 15

The policies, programs, and activities of PKHP are focused on:  
a. implementation of affirmative action, especially in the fields of education, health, law, employment, social affairs, politics, environment, and economy;  
b. elimination of gender disparities that result in poverty and impoverishment of women, as well as ensuring respect, protection, and fulfillment of women's basic rights;  
c. enhancement of efforts to protect women from various acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination, including prevention and mitigation efforts;  
d. development of gender-responsive policies, monitoring of gender-based violence, increase in budget allocation for Women's Empowerment, and for improving women's welfare in each sector and field;  
e. development and improvement of regulations and policies on PKHP in various fields of development;  
f. implementation of communication, information, and education (KIE) to improve the quality of life of women;  
g. preparation of recording and reporting systems, and systems for handling and resolving cases of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women;  
h. development of integrated service centers based in hospitals and community-based in the Region, as a means of protection for women victims of violence, including women victims of domestic violence.

#### Article 16

The Regional Government facilitates the establishment and development of PKHP development models, which include:  
a. BKB;  
b. GSI;  
c. Desa PRIMA; and  
d. P2WKSS.

#### Article 17

1. The Regional Government provides PKHP services in the form of:  
a. basic social services, especially education and health;  
b. information: economy, education, health, employment, law, politics, science and technology, and others; and  
c. training: skills, management, leadership, entrepreneurship, finance, and others.  
2. The provision of PKHP services is implemented through P2TP2A coordinated by BP3AKB.

# CHAPTER IV

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

Obligations and Responsibilities

#### Article 18

The obligations and responsibilities in the implementation of protection for women and children are the joint responsibility of:  
a. the Regional Government;  
b. the community;  
c. the family; and  
d. parents.

#### Article 19

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 18 letter a, include:  
a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children established by the government;  
b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children in the Region;  
c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence;  
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence;  
e. allocating budget for the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity; and  
f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence.  
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government establishes programs and activities for the protection of women and children in the Regional Action Plan as a basis for the Regional Government in implementing protection for women and children from acts of violence.  
3. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is established by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 20

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 18 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation.  
2. The forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:  
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;  
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or the competent authorities; and  
c. participating in providing assistance and/or handling victims of acts of violence.

#### Article 21

The obligations of the family and/or parents as referred to in Article 18 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

## Part Two

Duties and Authority of the Regional Government

#### Article 22

1. The Regional Government has the duty to carry out efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children, including:  
a. managing data and information on the protection of women and children victims of acts of violence;  
b. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence;  
c. conducting education on anti-violence values against women and children; and  
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence.  
2. The Regional Government in carrying out the duties as referred to in paragraph (1), has the authority to provide and organize services for victims of violence, among others:  
a. providing and facilitating the establishment of integrated services for victims of acts of violence by involving institutions and community elements;  
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for women and children; and  
c. conducting community empowerment for the prevention of acts of violence against women and children.

## Part Three

Programs and Activities for the Protection of Women and Children

### Paragraph 1

Prevention

#### Article 23

1. To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government conducts empowerment and awareness-raising to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.  
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness-raising as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government conducts efforts as follows:  
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education, both formal and non-formal and informal;  
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;  
c. opening employment opportunities for women;  
d. building community participation and awareness to implement prevention and protection for women and children from acts of violence;  
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;  
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children; and  
g. opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
3. The prevention of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Action Plan.

### Paragraph 2

Handling

#### Article 24

1. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out handling of women and children who become victims of acts of violence, by providing protection services in the form of:  
a. complaint services;  
b. health services;  
c. legal aid;  
d. repatriation;  
e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medicolegal;  
f. identification services;  
g. psychological services;  
h. education and training services; and/or  
i. other forms of services.  
2. The provision of protection services as referred to in paragraph (1) is provided for:  
a. women who become victims of violence, including victims of trafficking in persons, women in conflict-prone and disaster areas, women workers, elderly women, women with disabilities, women victims of sexual exploitation, women heads of households, and other vulnerable groups of women;  
b. children who become victims of acts of violence, exploitation, abuse, and neglect.

#### Article 25

1. The form of service as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented by the Regional Government in accordance with minimum service standards established by the Government.  
2. In the implementation of services as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government may cooperate with government agencies, provincial governments, other district/city governments, law enforcement officials, and the community.  
3. Further provisions regarding procedures for service and handling of women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

## Part Four

Institutional Framework for the Protection of Women and Children

### Paragraph 1

#### P2TP2A

#### Article 26

1. In order to provide protection services for women and children from acts of violence, the Regional Government shall establish P2TP2A as an integrated service center in implementing programs and activities for the protection of women and children.  
2. The establishment, Organization, and Work Procedures of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 27

1. P2TP2A has the principal task of carrying out part of the Regional Government's duties in providing services quickly, appropriately and in an integrated manner in efforts to Empower Women and Protect Children from acts of violence, discrimination and trafficking in persons.  
2. In carrying out the duties as referred to in paragraph (1), P2TP2A has the following functions:  
a. implementing facilitation and provision of protection services for women and children from discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons, which are of an emergency nature quickly covering various services namely information, medical referral, legal, psychological, psychic, safe house (shelter), home visit and skills training as well as other forms of services that support the implementation of P2TP2A activities;  
b. implementing coordination and building synergistic networks with Regional Apparatus, related Agencies, and/or other institutions that have duties and functions in the field of Women's Empowerment and Protection of Women and Children; and  
c. monitoring of victims after handling by P2TP2A and/or work partners.

### Paragraph 2

Task Force and Regional Action Committee

#### Article 28

In addition to establishing P2TP2A as referred to in Article 26, in order to support the implementation of the protection of women and children, the Regent shall establish:  
a. Task Force for Prevention and Handling of Victims of Violence Against Women, Victims of Violence Against Children, and Trafficking in Persons;  
b. Regional Action Committee for the Elimination of the Worst Forms of Child Labor.

#### Article 29

1. The Task Force as referred to in Article 28 letter a, shall consist of representatives from the Regional Government, Law Enforcement, Community Organizations, Non-Governmental Organizations, Professional Organizations, and Researchers/Academics.  
2. The Task Force as referred to in paragraph (1), is a coordinative institution that has the duty to coordinate programs and activities for the prevention and handling of victims of violence against Women and Children, as well as Trafficking in Persons.  
3. Further provisions regarding the establishment, organization, duties and functions of the Task Force for Prevention and Handling of Criminal Acts of Trafficking in Persons as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 30

1. The Regional Action Committee for the Elimination of the Worst Forms of Child Labor as referred to in Article 28 letter b, shall consist of representatives from the Regional Government, Law Enforcement, Community Organizations, Non-Governmental Organizations, Professional Organizations, and Researchers/Academics.  
2. Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties and functions of the Regional Action Committee as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Regent Decree.

# CHAPTER V

## CHILD-FRIENDLY REGENCY

#### Article 31

1. To realize the Fulfillment of Children's Rights shall be implemented in an integrated and systematic manner from all sectors on an ongoing basis through the Policy for the development of KLA.  
2. The KLA Development Policy as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be directed at the fulfillment of children's rights, including:  
a. civil rights and freedoms;  
b. family environment and alternative care;  
c. basic health and welfare;  
d. education, use of leisure time, and cultural activities; and  
e. special protection.  
3. The stages of KLA Development as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 32

1. In order to ensure the effectiveness of the implementation of KLA policy in the Region, a KLA Task Force shall be established.  
2. The establishment of the KLA Task Force as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent Decree.  
3. The KLA Task Force shall consist of representatives from the Regional Government, Children's Representatives, and may involve related Agencies, the business community, religious/community/customary leaders, and the community.  
4. Further provisions regarding the organization, duties and functions of the KLA Task Force as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VI

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 33

1. The Regional Government shall conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), shall include:  
a. provision of guidelines and fulfillment of Minimum Service Standards for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection;  
b. technical guidance and training;  
c. provision of facilities;  
d. monitoring; and  
e. evaluation.  
3. Further provisions regarding guidance and supervision of the implementation of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 34

Guidance and supervision as referred to in Article 33, shall be implemented in order to realize the objectives of the implementation of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in accordance with minimum service standards established by the Government.

# CHAPTER VII

## FINANCING

#### Article 35

Financing for the implementation of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, shall be sourced from:  
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and  
b. other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## SANCTION PROVISIONS

#### Article 36

Every person who commits a violation in the field of protection of women and children shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the provisions of:  
a. Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection, as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection;  
b. Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence;  
c. Law Number 21 of 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Criminal Act of Trafficking in Persons.

# CHAPTER IX

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 37

Implementing regulations of this Regional Regulation shall be stipulated no later than 12 (twelve) months from the date of promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

#### Article 38

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.
