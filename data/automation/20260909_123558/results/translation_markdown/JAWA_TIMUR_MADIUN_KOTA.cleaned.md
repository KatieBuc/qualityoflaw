a. that forms of violence against women and children constitute one form of violation of human rights based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
b. that acts of violence against women and children will have prolonged physical, psychological, sexual, social and economic impacts, therefore it is necessary to provide a sense of security and protection;
c. that at present there is no Regional Regulation that guarantees protection for women and children victims of violence in Madiun City;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  
1. Region is the City of Madiun.  
2. Regional Government is the Government of the City of Madiun.  
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Madiun.  
4. Agency for Community Empowerment, Family Planning, and Food Security, hereinafter abbreviated as BPMKB and KP, is the Agency for Community Empowerment, Family Planning, and Food Security of the City of Madiun.  
5. Appointed official is the Head of the Agency for Community Empowerment, Family Planning, and Food Security of the City of Madiun.  
6. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.  
7. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  
8. Protection of Women is all efforts made by family, government and others aimed at women to ensure the fulfillment of women's rights as a form of respect for human rights.  
9. Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and protection from violence and discrimination.  
10. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.  
11. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, or psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom arbitrarily, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.  
12. Violence against children is any act against children that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of children.  
13. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife or husband and wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in a direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.  
14. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.  
15. Service is an action that must be immediately taken for victims when seeing, hearing and knowing of the impending, ongoing or having occurred violence against victims.  
16. Companion is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.  
17. Repatriation is an effort to return women and child victims of violence from within/outside the country to the debarkation point/entry point, or from the receiving area to the area of origin.  
18. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.  
19. Empowerment is the strengthening of victims of violence to be able to work and work independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.  
20. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.  
21. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.  
22. Integrated Service Center for Acts of Violence Against Women and Children hereinafter referred to as PPT is a form of government and non-government network that organizes community-based integrated services that handle victims of violence against women and children which includes legal, medical, and psychosocial assistance services.  
23. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing services for handling reports/complaints, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance, as well as repatriation and social reintegration for women and child victims of violence.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence is carried out based on the principles of:  
a. respect for human rights;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination;  
d. victim protection;  
e. best interests of victims; and  
f. empowerment.

#### Article 3

1. The implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence aims to:  
a. prevent all forms of violence;  
b. protect victims of violence;  
c. take action against perpetrators of violence; and  
d. maintain the integrity of harmonious and prosperous households.  
2. Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of:  
a. physical violence;  
b. psychological violence;  
c. sexual violence;  
d. economic neglect; and  
e. restriction of movement.

# CHAPTER III

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 4

1. Everyone is prohibited from allowing acts of violence against women and children to occur.  
2. Everyone is obliged to report the occurrence of violence against women and children to the competent authority.  
3. Protection of women and child victims of violence is carried out by:  
a. Regional Government;  
b. related agencies along with other social institutions;  
c. family and/or closest relatives;  
d. community; and/or  
e. educational institutions.  
4. Protection efforts for women and children in the event of violence against women and children as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:  
a. forming a network in efforts to prevent violence based on partnership patterns;  
b. providing repressive protection in the form of compensation and curative in the form of medical assistance, psychosocial rehabilitation; and  
c. conducting education and socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women and child victims of violence.  
5. Further provisions regarding efforts to protect women and children as referred to in paragraph (4) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER IV

## RESPONSIBILITY

#### Article 5

The implementation of services for women and child victims of violence is the joint responsibility of:  
a. Regional Government;  
b. community;  
c. family; and/or  
d. parents.

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government implements efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children, by means of:  
a. collecting data and information about women and children victims of violence;  
b. conducting education on anti-violence values towards women and children;  
c. socializing laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence; and  
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.  
2. To anticipate the occurrence of violence, the Regional Government is obliged to provide and organize services for women and children victims of violence by means of:  
a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving community elements;  
b. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for women and children victims of violence;  
c. conducting continuous handling up to the rehabilitation and social reintegration stage.  
3. The Regional Government in implementing efforts to prevent and anticipate violence against women and children as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of the community, family and/or parents who are legally responsible for the victim.

#### Article 7

The responsibility of the community as referred to in Article 6 paragraph (3) is organized in the form of:  
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children; and
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcers or authorized parties.

#### Article 8

The family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 paragraph (3) have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER V

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 9

Every woman and child victim of violence has the right to:  
a. receive protection from family, police, prosecution, court, advocates, social institutions, community, Regional Government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court;  
b. health and psychological services according to medical needs;  
c. special handling related to victim confidentiality;  
d. assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
e. spiritual guidance services;  
f. the right to information on case developments;  
g. the right to safe house (shelter) services;  
h. the right to restitution;  
i. the right to psychosocial services/recovery; and  
j. the right to social reintegration.

#### Article 10

1. In the event that women and children victims of violence as referred to in Article 9 require continuous handling, they have the right to stay in a safe house or alternative residence.  
2. Continuous handling as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes handling at the rehabilitation stage carried out either by individuals, groups or Government or non-Government institutions.

#### Article 11

1. The operation and/or management of safe houses is carried out by BPMKB and KP.  
2. For the safety and/or at the request of victims of violence, the manager or operator of the safe house as referred to in paragraph (1) is obliged to place victims of violence in a certain place that is kept confidential.  
3. Further provisions regarding the operation of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VI

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutions

#### Article 12

1. The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out in an integrated manner within the PPT framework.  
2. Further provisions regarding PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Two

Forms and Service Mechanisms

#### Article 13

1. Forms of protection services for women and children victims of violence organized by PPT include:  
a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;  
b. medico-legal services constitute a form of medical services for the purposes of evidence in the legal field;  
c. psycho-social services constitute services provided by companions in the context of traumatic recovery of the victim's condition, including the provision of space and victim's house to protect victims from threats and intimidation for victims;  
d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process;  
e. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.  
2. Further provisions regarding service mechanisms as referred to in paragraph (1) are organized according to Standard Operating Procedures regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Three

Service Principles

#### Article 14

The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out free of charge, quickly, safely, with empathy, non-discrimination, easily accessible and with a guarantee of confidentiality.

#### Article 15

PPT managers organize services in accordance with the service principles as referred to in Article 14.

# CHAPTER VII

## MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

#### Article 16

MSS in the Field of Integrated Services for women and children victims of violence, include:  
a. handling of complaints/reports;  
b. health services;  
c. social rehabilitation;  
d. law enforcement and legal aid; and  
e. repatriation and social reintegration.

#### Article 17

1. MSS as referred to in Article 16 has performance indicators including:  
a. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers within the integrated service unit;  
b. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at community health centers and hospitals;  
c. coverage of social rehabilitation services provided by trained social rehabilitation officers for women and children victims of violence within the PPT;  
d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for women and children victims of violence within the integrated service unit;  
e. coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of violence against women and children;  
f. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive legal aid;  
g. coverage of repatriation services for women and children victims of violence; and  
h. coverage of social reintegration services for women and children victims of violence.  
2. Further provisions regarding MSS as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

## EMPOWERMENT

## Part One

Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 18

Implementation steps for empowerment of women victims of violence include:  
a. job training;  
b. productive economic enterprises and/or joint business groups; and  
c. capital assistance.

#### Article 19

The implementation steps for empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in Article 18 shall be carried out by:  
a. providing the necessary requirements for entrepreneurship training for women victims of violence, in order to improve knowledge, attitudes and business skills;  
b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;  
c. providing assistance in developing productive economic enterprises;  
d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large enterprises as well as financial institutions to develop businesses of women victims of violence;  
e. endeavoring to provide capital for women victims of violence; and  
f. expanding access to information and promoting the results of productive economic enterprises of women victims of violence.

## Part Two

Empowerment of Child Victims of Violence

#### Article 20

The implementation steps for empowerment of child victims of violence include efforts to increase understanding among children not to commit violence.

#### Article 21

The implementation steps for empowerment of child victims of violence as referred to in Article 20 shall be carried out by:  
a. skills training according to the child's interests; and  
b. fulfillment of education both formal and non-formal.

# CHAPTER IX

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 22

1. The Regional Government conducts monitoring to ensure the continuity and effectiveness of integrated steps in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence.  
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by BPMKB, and KP.  
3. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of regional apparatus work units that implement policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence.  
4. Monitoring shall be conducted from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence every year.

#### Article 23

1. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence shall be conducted at the end of each fiscal year or when necessary according to needs.  
2. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence shall be used as input for the formulation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence for the following year.  
3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## REPORTING

#### Article 24

The Mayor through the appointed Official shall report the implementation of SPM in the field of integrated services for women and child victims of violence every year in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 25

1. The Mayor through the appointed Official shall conduct guidance and supervision over the implementation of SPM in the field of integrated services for women and child victims of violence in the Region.  
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. provision of implementation instructions;  
b. guidance;  
c. supervision; and/or  
d. monitoring, and evaluation of implementation for women and child victims of violence.

# CHAPTER XII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 26

1. In the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence, the community may:  
a. form family partners at the Kelurahan level by the community;  
b. conduct socialization of women's and children's rights independently;  
c. provide first aid to victims; and  
d. report to the competent authority if violence against women and children occurs in their environment.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by:  
a. individuals;  
b. social community institutions;  
c. non-governmental organizations;  
d. educational institutions;  
e. religious institutions;  
f. private sector; and/or  
g. mass media.

# CHAPTER XIII

## FUNDING

#### Article 27

Funding for the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence shall be sourced from:  
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and/or  
b. other lawful and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XIV

## INVESTIGATION

#### Article 28

1. Certain Civil Servant Officials within the Regional Government are granted special authority as Investigators to conduct investigations of criminal offenses violating this Regional Regulation, as referred to in the applicable Criminal Procedure Code.  
2. Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) are certain civil servant officials within the Regional Government who are appointed by the competent official in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
3. The authority of Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) is:  
a. to receive, search for, collect, and examine information or reports concerning criminal offenses so that such information or reports become complete and clear;  
b. to examine, search for, and collect information regarding individuals or Bodies concerning the truth of acts committed in connection with criminal offenses;  
c. to request information and evidence from individuals or Bodies in connection with criminal offenses;  
d. to examine books, records, and other documents concerning criminal offenses;  
e. to conduct searches to obtain evidence of bookkeeping, recording and other documents, as well as to conduct seizures of such evidence;  
f. to request the assistance of experts in the framework of carrying out the task of investigating criminal offenses;  
g. to order someone to stop and/or prohibit someone from leaving a room or place when an examination is in progress and to examine the identity of persons and/or documents carried as referred to in letter e;  
h. to photograph persons related to criminal offenses;  
i. to summon persons to be heard and examined as suspects or witnesses;  
j. to terminate the investigation; and/or  
k. to take other actions necessary for the smooth investigation of criminal offenses according to law that can be accounted for.  
4. Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) shall notify the commencement of the investigation and submit the results of their investigation to the Public Prosecutor through the Investigator Official of the Police of the Republic of Indonesia, in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Law.

# CHAPTER XV

## CRIMINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

1. Violations of the provisions as referred to in Article 11 paragraph (2) shall be punishable by imprisonment of a maximum of 3 (three) months and/or a fine of a maximum of Rp. 25,000,000.00 (twenty-five million rupiah).  
2. In the event that the act as referred to in paragraph (1) is committed by a Civil Servant, it shall be added with employment sanctions in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
3. The criminal offense as referred to in paragraph (1) is a violation.  
4. Violations of the provisions as referred to in Article 4 paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

Further provisions as implementing regulations of this Regional Regulation shall be stipulated no later than 3 (three) months from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

##### I. GENERAL

That every citizen has the right to live free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, and to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence, violence against women and children constitutes a violation of human rights and therefore needs to receive protection so that their right to life is guaranteed in accordance with their dignity and worth without discrimination.

The Regional Government has the obligation to provide protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or hardship for women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, socially, psychologically, including neglect or maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child. The existence of women and children victims of violence has not yet received adequate services, so minimum services for victims of violence are needed.

This Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence is based on Pancasila, and founded on the 1945 Constitution, and refers to many Laws, especially those regulating the protection of women and children victims of violence.

Among them are Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014, Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence and other related laws and regulations.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children victims of violence in Madiun City so that they are protected from threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for women and children victims of violence, particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of women and children victims of violence in Madiun City.

#### Article 2

letter b  

What is meant by the principle of "justice" is a state where every person, both men and women, are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to gain access and welfare.

What is meant by the principle of "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

letter c  
What is meant by the principle of "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards women and children without making distinctions based on gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

letter d  
What is meant by the principle of "victim protection" is protection of the person, family, honor, dignity, and property under their authority, a sense of security and protection from threats of fear, freedom from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth.

letter e  
What is meant by the principle of "the best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

letter f  
What is meant by "empowerment" is strengthening victims of violence to be able to work and earn a living independently after they have recovered and been provided with health and social rehabilitation services.

#### Article 3

paragraph (1)  
Sufficiently clear.

paragraph (2)  
letter a  
What is meant by "Physical violence" is an act that results in pain, illness or serious injury such as slapping, hitting, spitting, pulling hair, kicking, burning with hot objects, hitting/injuring with weapons and or causing disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causing death.

letter b  
What is meant by "Psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness, and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

letter c  
What is meant by "Sexual violence" includes but is not limited to:  
a. forced sexual intercourse committed against a person residing within the household and/or forced sexual intercourse against someone within the household with another person, for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes;  
b. by violence or threat of violence forcing a woman who is not his wife to have sexual intercourse with him;  
c. by violence or threat of violence forcing someone to commit or allow obscene acts to be committed;  
d. intentionally committing violence or threat of violence forcing a child to have sexual intercourse;  
e. intentionally committing violence or threat of violence, coercion, deception, a series of lies or persuading a child to commit or allow obscene acts to be committed.

letter d  
What is meant by "economic neglect" is neglecting the economic needs of a wife or child or family member or relative such as not providing for the wife, even spending the wife's money, not providing or withholding the wages of domestic workers or drivers and includes acts of restricting and/or prohibiting family members from working appropriately.

letter e  
What is meant by "restriction of movement" is restricting the movement of a wife or child in developing their potential both for economic and social purposes such as prohibiting them from gathering and socializing in society and restricting the movement of relatives such as preventing domestic workers or drivers from communicating or having contact with family.

#### Article 16

letter a  
Handling of reports/complaints of women and children victims of violence, with the main indicator being the coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers in integrated service units.  

Handling of complaints/reports of women and children victims of violence can also be measured through supporting indicators, namely the coverage of availability of officers in integrated service units who have the ability to follow up on community complaints about violence against women and children, in this case the ability to reach victims and follow up on complaints related to alleged violence against women and children;  

letter b  
Health services for women and children victims of violence, with the main indicator being the coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive health services by trained health workers at community health centers capable of managing violence against women/children and integrated service posts/crisis centers at hospitals.  

The health services referred to are services that include promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative aspects carried out by trained health workers, namely specialist doctors, doctors, dentists, nurses/midwives who have received training on medical management of violence against women and children victims of violence. The health services referred to are carried out at hospitals that provide integrated services and also for women and children at community health centers that provide comprehensive basic services.  

Health services for women and children victims of violence can be measured through supporting indicators:  
a. coverage of community health centers capable of managing violence against women;  
b. coverage of regional general hospitals/vertical general hospitals/regional general hospitals/private hospitals/police hospitals that implement integrated services for women and children victims of violence;  
c. coverage of trained health workers on case management of women and children victims of violence at community health centers; and  
d. coverage of trained health workers on case management of women and children victims of violence at hospitals.  

letter c  
Social rehabilitation for women and children victims of violence with the main indicator being the coverage of social rehabilitation services provided by trained social rehabilitation officers for women and children victims of violence in integrated service units and the coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for women and children victims of violence in integrated service units.  

Social rehabilitation services for women and children victims of violence can also be measured through supporting indicators, namely the coverage of trained social rehabilitation officers and the coverage of trained officers in conducting spiritual guidance.  

letter d  
Law enforcement and legal aid for women and children victims of violence with the main indicator being the coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of violence against women and children and the coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive legal aid services.  

Law enforcement is the action of apparatus authorized by the State to carry out duties and functions as enforcers of applicable laws and regulations. Legal aid is everything related to aspects related to the legal field provided to a person in the criminal or civil justice process.  

Law enforcement for women and children victims of violence can also be measured through supporting indicators:  
a. coverage of completion of handling cases of violence against women and children at the police level;  
b. coverage of availability of women and children service units (UPPA) at regional police and district/city police;  
c. coverage of availability of facilities and infrastructure at UPPA;  
d. coverage of availability of police trained in providing gender-sensitive services;  
e. coverage of availability of prosecutors trained in prosecuting cases of violence against women and children; and  
f. coverage of availability of judges trained in handling cases of violence against women and children.
