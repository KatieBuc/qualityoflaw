# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

1. Region is Banyumas Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Banyumas.
4. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is a regional apparatus assigned with specific principal duties and authorities in the region.
5. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
6. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
7. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
8. Protection Implementation is a series of activities aimed at preventing the occurrence of violence, providing protection and services for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation carried out by the Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Violence Against Children.
9. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
10. Violence Against Children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
11. Victim of Gender-Based Violence is a person who, because of their gender, experiences physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
12. Child Victim of Violence is a child who receives harsh treatment either physically, psychologically, economically, socially, sexually, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
13. Victim Recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, whether physically, psychologically, socially, economically or sexually.
14. Companion is a social worker who has professional competence in their field.
15. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the purpose of strengthening victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems they face.
16. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing victims and communities that support the reunification of victims into a family environment or family substitute that can provide protection and fulfillment of the victim's needs.
17. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children in accordance with the determined operational standards.
18. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Violence Against Children, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT PKBGA, is a special service place for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children carried out in a cross-functional and cross-sectoral manner as one unit of implementation so that victims receive maximum service in an integrated and comprehensive manner.
19. Sub-District Level Complaint Service Institution, hereinafter abbreviated as LPPTK, is a special service place for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children located at the sub-district level so that victims receive maximum service in an integrated and comprehensive manner.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

#### Article 2

The Principles of Protection Implementation for Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Violence Against Children are:
a. non-discrimination;
b. best interests of the victim;
c. gender justice and equality;
d. victim protection;
e. maternal survival;
f. child survival, growth, and development;
g. respect for the child's opinion;
h. transparency;
i. integration;
j. not blaming the victim;
k. empowerment;
1. victim confidentiality;
m. decision-making in the hands of the victim.

#### Article 3

The Objectives of Victim Protection are to:
a. prevent all forms of gender-based violence and violence against children, occurring within the scope of the household and/or community;
b. provide protection in the form of complaint services, health services, social rehabilitation services, legal assistance and enforcement services, as well as repatriation and social reintegration services;
c. seek to increase community participation.

#### Article 4

The scope of protection for Victims includes prevention, service and empowerment efforts for victims of violence.

# CHAPTER III

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence are:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. exploitation; and/or
f. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the non-fulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as appropriate, carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
c. acts that neglect a person within the scope of their household; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits;
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:  
a. threat of violence includes any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether or not using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and  
b. coercion, includes a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

Regional Government

#### Article 12

1. The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to carry out efforts to protect victims of violence in the form of:  
a. preventing the occurrence of all forms of gender-based and child violence;  
b. providing protection by providing complaint services, health services, social rehabilitation services, legal aid and enforcement services, repatriation and social reintegration services, seeking to increase community participation, as well as monitoring and reporting;  
c. encouraging and increasing community participation;  
d. cooperating with service providers in efforts to prevent, protect and recover victims of violence. (2) To carry out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government has the authority to:  
a. formulate policies and programs on the elimination of gender-based and child violence;  
b. establish an integrated service center;  
c. establish complaint service institutions at the sub-district level;  
d. facilitate the implementation of integrated service centers and activities of complaint service institutions at the sub-district level;  
e. provide facilities and infrastructure;  
f. increase the capacity of service provider institutions;  
g. coordinate and cooperate in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based and child violence;  
h. encourage and increase community participation;  
i. conduct monitoring and evaluation.  
2. In carrying out its obligations, responsibilities and authorities, the Regent may delegate to related SKPD.  
3. Related SKPD that receive delegation from the Regent as referred to in paragraph (3) in its implementation prepare a Regional Action Plan for victim protection.

## Part Two

Community, Family and Parents

#### Article 13

In the Effort to Provide protection to victims of violence, the Community, Family and Parents have obligations and responsibilities:  
a. prevent violence against women and children;  
b. report if violence occurs;  
c. protect victims; and  
d. provide emergency assistance.

# CHAPTER V

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutions

#### Article 14

In implementing protection for victims of gender-based and child violence, the Regional Government is assisted by:  
a. PPT PKBGA; and
b. LPPTK.T 10

## Part Two

### PPT PKBGA

#### Article 15

1. PPT PKBGA as referred to in Article 14 letter a is established by the Regional Government in accordance with laws and regulations.  
2. The membership of PPT PKBGA as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:  
a. elements of the Regional Government;  
b. vertical agencies;  
c. non-governmental institutions;  
d. health service institutions;  
e. law enforcement apparatus;  
f. professional personnel;  
g. companion volunteers;  
h. social workers;  
i. religious leaders;  
j. social rehabilitation centers;  
k. elements of Higher Education.

#### Article 16

PPT PKBGA as referred to in Article 14 letter a has the task of:  
a. seeking prevention;  
b. providing complaint services;  
c. providing health services;  
d. providing social rehabilitation services;  
e. providing legal aid and enforcement services;  
f. providing repatriation and social reintegration services;  
g. conducting coordination and cooperation;  
h. seeking to increase community participation;  
i. conducting monitoring and reporting.

### Paragraph 1

Prevention Efforts

#### Article 17

Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 16 letter a include:  
a. organizing communication, information, and education about gender-based and child violence;  
b. socializing laws and regulations related to gender-based and child violence.

### Paragraph 2

Complaint Services

#### Article 18

Complaint Services as referred to in Article 16, letter b include:  
a. direct complaint services;  
b. complaint services by telephone;  
c. complaint services by mail;  
d. complaint services from referrals;  
e. victim outreach services.

### Paragraph 3

Health Services

#### Article 19

Health Services as referred to in Article 16, letter c include:  
a. medical services;  
b. psychiatric medical services;  
c. medicolegal services, including: visum et repertum (VER), visum et psikiatrum and DNA identification.

### Paragraph 4

Social Rehabilitation Services

#### Article 20

Social Rehabilitation Services as referred to in Article 16, letter d include:  
a. initial counseling services;  
b. follow-up counseling services;  
c. provision of safe houses;  
d. spiritual guidance services.

### Paragraph 5

Legal Aid and Enforcement Services

#### Article 21

Legal Aid and Enforcement Services as referred to in Article 16, letter e include:  
a. legal aid services include litigation legal assistance and non-litigation legal assistance;  
b. law enforcement services include law enforcement at the Police level, law enforcement at the Prosecutor's Office level and law enforcement at the Court level;  
c. law enforcement services at the police level as referred to in letter b include law enforcement in the Investigation and/or inquiry process;  
d. law enforcement services at the Prosecutor's Office level as referred to in letter b include: law enforcement in the Pre-Prosecution and Prosecution process;  
e. law enforcement services at the Court level as referred to in letter b include law enforcement at the Pre-Trial, Trial and Sentencing stages.

### Paragraph 6

Repatriation and Social Reintegration Services

#### Article 22

1. Repatriation Services as referred to in Article 16, letter f include:  
a. repatriation of migrant workers with problems;  
b. repatriation of victims of gender-based and child violence;  
c. repatriation of victims of trafficking in persons.  
2. Social Reintegration Services as referred to in Article 16, letter f include:  
a. reintegration with family;  
b. reintegration with substitute family;  
c. reintegration with the environment;  
d. educational reintegration.

### Paragraph 7

Coordination and Cooperation

#### Article 23

Coordination and cooperation as referred to in Article 16, letter g include:  
a. coordinating and cooperating in handling cases of violence with complaint service institutions at the sub-district level;  
b. coordinating and cooperating with integrated service centers between Regencies/cities;  
c. coordinating and cooperating with institutions providing services for victims of gender-based and child violence;  
d. coordinating and consulting with the Regional Government.

### Paragraph 8

Efforts to Increase Community Participation

#### Article 24

Increasing community participation as referred to in Article 16, letter h is carried out by:  
a. fostering community awareness of cases of gender-based and child violence;  
b. encouraging the community to actively participate in providing information and reporting gender-based and child violence;  
c. fostering local wisdom in handling gender-based and child violence;  
d. organizing strengthening of community groups in handling gender-based and child violence;  
e. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to gender-based and child violence.

### Paragraph 9

Monitoring and Reporting

#### Article 25

Monitoring and reporting as referred to in Article 16 letter i. includes monitoring, documentation and reporting of cases of gender-based violence and violence against children.

#### Article 26

The implementation of PPT PKBGA is coordinated by the SKPD responsible for women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 27

1. PPT PKBGA as referred to in Article 14 letter a, is established by the Regent in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
2. Further technical and service mechanism of integrated services is regulated in a Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

### LPPTK

#### Article 28

1. LPPTK as referred to in Article 14 letter b, is established by the Regional Government in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
2. The establishment of LPPTK as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of members from:  
a. elements of the Sub-District Government;  
b. elements of the sub-district police;  
c. health service institutions;  
d. community leaders;  
e. religious leaders;  
f. elements of community organizations.

#### Article 29

LPPTK as referred to in Article 28, has the duties to:  
a. receive complaints of cases of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
b. provide initial consultation to victims of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
c. conduct crisis intervention for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
d. provide referrals for handling cases of gender-based violence and violence against children to PPT PKBGA.

#### Article 30

In carrying out the duties as referred to in Article 29, LPPTK is obliged to:  
a. coordinate and consult with PPT PKBGA;  
b. document and report every case of gender-based violence and violence against children that it handles to PPT PKBGA.

#### Article 31

LPPTK is established by a Sub-District Head Decree.

## Part Four

Rights of Victims of Violence

#### Article 32

1. Every victim of gender-based violence and violence against children has the right to:  
a. obtain protection for personal, family and property security, and be free from threats related to information that will be, is being, or has been given;  
b. participate in the process of selecting and determining the form of protection and security support;  
c. be free from ensnaring questions;  
d. obtain information regarding case developments and court decisions;  
e. obtain complaint services, health services, social rehabilitation services, legal assistance and enforcement services, as well as repatriation and social reintegration services.  
2. The rights of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Five

Cooperation

#### Article 33

1. In providing protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children, the Regional Government may cooperate with the Government, Provincial Government, other Regency/City Governments and other institutions.  
2. Cooperation between the Regional Government and the Provincial Government or the Government includes consultation, coordination, advocacy, referral, monitoring, evaluation and reporting.  
3. Cooperation between the Regional Government and Regencies/Cities includes coordination, advocacy and referral.  
4. The implementation of cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 34

1. Guidance and Supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children is carried out by the Regent.  
2. The implementation of guidance and supervision of the provision of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the SKPD responsible for women's empowerment and child protection.

# CHAPTER VII

## FUNDING

#### Article 35

Funding for activities related to protection of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children carried out by the Regional Government is charged to the APBD and/or other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 36

1. The community may participate in assisting prevention and handling efforts for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:  
a. providing information and or reporting any violence that is known;  
b. providing protection for victims;  
c. providing emergency assistance;  
d. providing advocacy to victims and or the community regarding handling of gender-based violence and violence against children;  
e. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection orders;  
f. assisting in the process of repatriation and social reintegration.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

# CHAPTER IX

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 37

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all regional legal products related to the protection of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children remain in force as long as they do not conflict with and/or have not been replaced by new regulations.

##### CLOSING PROVISIONS

##### I. GENERAL

The right to a decent life, dignity and honor of women and children should properly receive protection in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination and the possibility of acts of violence.  

The policy of protection services for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children has been normatively regulated in the Regulation of the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection of the Republic of Indonesia Number 01 of 2010 concerning Minimum Service Standards in the field of Integrated Services for women and children victims of violence.  

Concrete steps that can be taken by the Regional Government so that women and children are protected from acts of violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, can be done by implementing a legal rule contained in the form of a regional regulation. - The formulation of norms regarding the protection of women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations needs to be carried out, the enactment of regional regulations is in order to reinforce institutional support.  

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims of violence, especially in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence, community participation, implementation mechanisms, guidance, funding and its guidance and supervision.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a  
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinction of ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.  

Letter b  
What is meant by "best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, society, and the judiciary, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.  

Letter c  
What is meant by "gender justice" is fair treatment given to both women and men.  

What is meant by "gender equality" is a condition and position that describes a harmonious, aligned, and balanced relationship between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in access, participation, control and benefits in the implementation of development as well as enjoying the results of development in family life, as well as in society, nation and state.  

Letter d  
What is meant by "victim protection" is providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  

Letter e  
What is meant by "maternal survival" is ensuring that a mother does not experience death that occurs during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum, whether caused by physical or non-physical conditions.  

Letter f  
What is meant by "growth and development" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.  

What is meant by "survival" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity and the right to enjoy the highest attainable health status.  

Letter g  
What is meant by "respect for the views of the child" is respect for the rights of children to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.  

Letter h  
What is meant by "transparency" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is transparent among service providers.  

Letter i  
What is meant by "integration" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is carried out by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal assistance, psychologists, religious counselors, social workers, police.  

Letter j  
What is meant by "not blaming the victim" is an attitude and treatment of not blaming the victim for the occurrence of violence they experienced.  

Letter k  
What is meant by "empowerment" is that every effort provided must be able to strengthen the victim, both physically, psychologically, socially and economically.  

Letter 1  
What is meant by "victim confidentiality" is every action taken to ensure the victim is in a safe condition from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychology of the victim.  

Letter m  
What is meant by "decision-making in the hands of the victim" is the right of the victim to determine the best choice in resolving their problem.
