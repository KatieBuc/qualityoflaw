# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Tabanan Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Tabanan.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter referred to as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Tabanan Regency.
5. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.
6. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person recognized by law as a woman.
7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child in the womb.
8. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threat of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
9. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child committed by parties who should be responsible for the child or those who have power over the child.
10. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
11. Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence.
12. Victim of violence is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
13. Complaint service is a series of actions carried out by integrated service providers to follow up on reports of acts of violence against women and children submitted by victims, families or the community.
14. Health rehabilitation is the recovery of victims of human trafficking from health disorders they suffer.
15. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person who experiences social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.
16. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of human trafficking from the regency area to their area of origin.
17. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
18. Legal Aid is legal services provided by companions, law enforcement officers which include providing legal consultation, exercising power, representing, accompanying, defending and taking other legal actions for victims of violence.
19. Integrated Service Center hereinafter referred to as PPT is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for victims of violence.
20. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) is an integrated service unit established by the Regional Government to provide services to women and children victims of violence comprehensively including complaint service, health rehabilitation; social rehabilitation; repatriation; social reintegration, and legal aid.
21. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter referred to as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing services for handling reports/complaints, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal aid, as well as repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence.
22. Desa Pakraman is a customary law community unit in Bali Province that has a unity of tradition and etiquette of social life of the Hindu community passed down through generations in the bond of kahyangan tiga or kahyangan desa which has a certain territory and its own assets and has the right to manage its own household.
23. Social Protection House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
24. Village is a legal community unit that has territorial boundaries authorized to regulate and manage government affairs, local community interests based on community initiative, rights of origin and/or traditional rights that are recognized and respected in the government system of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The principles of organizing protection for women and children victims of violence are:
a. justice and gender equality;
b. non-discrimination; and
c. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

The objectives of protection for women and children victims of violence are:
a. to protect women and children victims of violence;
b. to provide services to women and children victims of violence; and c. empowerment of women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

The rights of victims include:
a. protection from the family;
b. protection from the police;
c. protection from the prosecutor's office;
d. protection from the court;
e. protection from advocates;
f. protection from social institutions, or other parties either temporarily or based on the determination of a protection order from the court;
g. health services in accordance with medical needs;
h. special handling related to victim confidentiality;
i. assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
j. spiritual guidance services; and
k. victims have the right to determine their own decisions regarding which choice will be implemented.

# CHAPTER IV

## AUTHORITY AND RESPONSIBILITY

#### Article 5

1. The Regional Government has the authority and responsibility for the protection of women and children victims of violence.
2. The authority as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. service activities for women and children victims of violence;
b. monitoring, strengthening of victims of violence;
c. capacity building of apparatus and other stakeholders.

# CHAPTER V

## INSTITUTIONAL

#### Article 6

1. The Regent establishes PPT;
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. P2TP2A;
b. hospital-based PPT;
c. sub-district PPT; and
d. Village PPT.
3. The procedure for establishing PPT is stipulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 7

1. PPT services include:
a. providing services and handling as quickly as possible to victims;
b. providing convenience, comfort, safety, and free of charge for victims;
c. maintaining victim confidentiality; and
d. guaranteeing justice and legal certainty for victims.
2. The procedure for PPT services as referred to in paragraph (1) is regulated by Regent Regulation;

#### Article 8

The operation of PPT requires:
a. adequate supporting facilities and infrastructure;
b. implementing officers; and
c. functional officers.

#### Article 9

1. Procedures and mechanisms for integrated services include:  
a. complaint services;  
b. health rehabilitation;  
c. social rehabilitation;  
d. repatriation;    
e. social reintegration, and  
f. legal assistance.  
3. Implementation of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with minimum service standards.

# CHAPTER VI

## MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

#### Article 10

1. The MSS for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to ensure that women and children victims of violence receive the minimum services needed.  
2. The MSS for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence includes services for:  
a. handling complaints/reports of victims of violence against women and children;  
b. health services for women and children victims of violence;  
c. social rehabilitation for women and children victims of violence;  
d. law enforcement and legal assistance for women and children victims of violence;  
e. repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence; and  
f. in the event that victims experience trauma or illness that endangers themselves as a result of acts of violence requiring immediate assistance, the regional government is obligated to provide first aid no later than 7 (seven) days after the complaint is filed.

#### Article 11

1. The MSS as referred to in Article 10 has performance indicators including:  
a. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers in integrated service units;  
b. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at Community Health Centers and Hospitals:  
c. coverage of social rehabilitation services provided by trained social rehabilitation officers for women and children victims of violence in integrated service units:  
d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for women and children victims of violence in integrated service units:  
e. coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of violence against women and children;  
f. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive legal assistance services;  
g. coverage of repatriation services for women and children victims of violence; and  
h. coverage of social reintegration services for women and children victims of violence.  
2. The determination of MSS performance indicators for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes minimum targets that must be achieved by integrated service units in stages.

# CHAPTER VII

## SOCIAL PROTECTION HOUSES

#### Article 12

1. Regional Governments are obligated to establish Social Protection Houses and/or Safe Houses or other designations.  
2. Social Protection Houses and/or Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (1) shall guarantee the implementation of health rehabilitation services, social rehabilitation, repatriation, and social reintegration.  
3. For the implementation of services as referred to in paragraph (2), regional governments may utilize social protection houses and/or safe houses owned by the community or other social service institutions.

# CHAPTER VIII

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 13

1. Regional Governments conduct monitoring to ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence.  
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence in the region.

#### Article 14

1. Monitoring is conducted through coordination and direct monitoring of related SKPDs on a regular basis.  
2. Monitoring is conducted from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence for the current year.

#### Article 15

1. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children shall be conducted at the end of each fiscal year or when necessary according to needs.  
2. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children shall be used as input for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children for the following year.  
3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## REPORTING

#### Article 16

1. The Regent is responsible for preparing reports on the implementation of the MSS for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence and submitting them to the Governor with copies submitted to the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection and the Minister of Home Affairs.  
2. Reporting of implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted annually.  
3. Further provisions regarding procedures and forms of reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER X

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 17

1. The Regent conducts guidance on the development of PPT. (2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. provision of implementation instructions;  
b. mentoring;  
c. supervision;  
d. monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of services for women and children victims of violence, both apparatus and community.

#### Article 18

The Regent conducts guidance and supervision over the implementation of the MSS for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the region.

# CHAPTER XI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 19

1. In providing protection for women and children victims of violence, the community may:  
a. establish family partners at the village/kelurahan level in coordination with Desa Pakraman;    
b. establish women and child protection units within community organizations;  
c. conduct independent socialization of women's and children's rights;  
d. provide first aid to victims; and  
e. report to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, Desa Pakraman, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

# CHAPTER XII

## FUNDING

#### Article 20

The provision of protection for women and children victims of violence shall be sourced from:  
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and  
b. other lawful sources.

# CHAPTER XIII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 21

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Tabanan Regency.

#### I. GENERAL

Violence against women and children is one form of act that violates human rights. As is known, the human rights of women and children have been guaranteed and regulated in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia and the Law on Human Rights. Therefore, the protection of the rights of women and children from violence should be respected, enforced and their human dignity enhanced without discrimination, welfare and justice for women and children.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the child. The existence of women and children victims of violence has not received adequate services so that minimum services are needed for victims of violence.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Tabanan Regency so as to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for women and children victims of violence, particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of women and children victims of violence in Tabanan Regency.
