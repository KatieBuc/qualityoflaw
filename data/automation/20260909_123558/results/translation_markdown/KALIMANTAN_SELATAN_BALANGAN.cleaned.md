# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is the Regency Region of Balangan.
2. Regional Government is the regional head as an element of Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regional Head hereinafter referred to as Regent is the Regent of Balangan Regency.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the regional people's representative institution of Balangan Regency which is positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.
5. Agency or Board is an Agency or Board whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the field of protection and empowerment of women and children in the region.
6. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.
7. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child who is still in the womb.
8. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
9. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.
10. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
11. Protection of women and children is an effort to prevent and handle all forms of violence including neglect and trafficking in persons that violate the human rights of women and children, as well as provide basic and specific service needs of women and children in the conditions and situations they experience.
12. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
13. Service is an activity and immediate action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
14. Empowerment of women and children is an effort to strengthen human rights, improve quality of life and increase the participation of women and children in their social interaction.
15. Victim of violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering either physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
16. Assistance is an activity and action carried out by an assistant during the service process.
17. Assistant is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.
18. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in the community.
19. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
20. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
21. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

1. Protection of women and children from acts of violence is organized based on the principles of:
a. respect for human rights;
b. non-discrimination; and
c. protection of victims.
2. Protection of women and children from acts of violence aims to protect, respect, guarantee the human rights of women and children, and seek the best interests of women and children.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 3

1. Every woman and child has the right to receive protection from acts of violence.
2. Acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of acts of:
a. physical violence;
b. sexual violence;
c. psychological violence;
d. intimidation;
e. forced eviction;
f. deprivation of liberty;
g. neglect; and
h. exploitation, trafficking in persons.
3. Protection of women and children from acts of violence is the shared responsibility of the Regional Government, community, family, and parents.
4. Protection of women from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (2) includes:
a. prevention of acts of violence against women and children;
b. services for women and children victims of violence; and
c. empowerment of women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER IV

## PREVENTION

#### Article 4

1. Regional Government, Community, Family, and Parents are obliged to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children to avoid the impact of situations and events.
2. Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is also carried out against:
a. acts of neglect; and
b. indications or activities that contain the potential for trafficking in persons.
3. Prevention efforts are carried out by taking into account the conditions, situations, problems and forms of handling that are appropriate and directed.

#### Article 5

1. The Regional Government in accordance with its authority carries out early prevention efforts.
2. Early prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:
a. conducting data collection and assessment of conditions, fulfillment of rights and various aspects related to women and children in social life in the community;
b. mapping areas prone to acts of violence based on data collection and assessment as referred to in letter a and providing solutions in the form/manner capable of minimizing vulnerability;
c. increasing public legal awareness through the role of community institutions/customary institutions, interfaith communication forums to provide protection for women and children;
d. increasing the role of mass media in providing dissemination and information on the protection of women and children;
e. regular counseling or socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children;
f. opening channels for public complaints, and coordination channels from the village/sub-district, sub-district and Regional Government levels;
g. immediate handling of community reports.

## CHAPTER V SERVICES

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government provides services to women and children victims of violence and neglect.
2. Services as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:
a. complaints;
b. health rehabilitation;
c. social rehabilitation;
d. social reintegration; and
e. legal assistance and accompaniment.

#### Article 7

1. Women and children victims of violence are placed in a safe house or a place whose whereabouts are kept confidential and receive basic fulfillment in accordance with their rights.
2. Child victims of violence who are placed in a safe house are provided with care according to their needs.

## BAB VI EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 8 

1. The Regional Government in accordance with its authority empowers women and children victims of violence and neglect in the form of improving life resilience and improving economic enterprises.  
2. Improving life resilience as referred to in paragraph (1) by providing guidance and assistance for mental spiritual strengthening in the form of religious knowledge.  
3. Improving economic enterprises as referred to in paragraph (1) by providing:  
a. job skills training;  
b. productive economic enterprise training and joint business groups;  
c. assistance in developing productive economic enterprises;  
d. capital assistance; and  
e. ease of access to information and marketing promotion of productive economic production results.

#### Article 9 

Children victims of violence whose parental custody rights have been revoked, their education becomes the responsibility of the Regional Government or foster parents who obtain custody rights from a court decision.

# CHAPTER VII 

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 10 

1. In order to provide services to women and children victims of violence, neglect and trafficking in persons, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A).  
2. The Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) is positioned as an independent institution.

#### Article 11 

1. The Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) has the authority to organize the protection of women and children.  
2. The duty of the Regional Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) is to implement protection for women and children in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 12 

The membership of the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) consists of elements of related agencies, vertical agencies, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, academic researchers and religious, customary, community leaders and activists for the protection of women and children in the region.

#### Article 13 

1. The Regent establishes the Management of the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A).  
2. The establishment of Management as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Regent's Decree including the secretariat forum for management and provision of supporting facilities for the implementation of activities.

#### Article 14 

1. The Management of the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) consists of leadership elements and members stipulated by Regent's Decree.  
2. Leadership elements include:  
a. chairperson;  
b. secretary; and  
c. treasurer.  
3. Membership elements are divided into the form of:  
a. service division;  
b. information division;  
c. assistance division;  
d. education and training division.

#### Article 15 

1. The Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children in carrying out its functions builds a working network with:  
a. Police;  
b. Prosecutor's Office;  
c. Court;  
d. Witness and Victim Protection Agency;  
e. Hospital/Community Health Center;  
f. Other institutions that have similar objectives.  
2. In addition to building a working network as referred to in paragraph (1), cooperation may be conducted with:  
a. Government;  
b. Provincial Government;  
c. Other Regency/City Governments; and  
d. Non-Governmental Institutions.  
e. International institutions whose existence is recognized.  
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), is set forth in the form of a joint agreement.

#### Article 16 

1. The Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children establishes partnerships with the business world for support in organizing protection for women and children in the Regional area.  
2. Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1), in the form of:  
a. information and opening of employment opportunities;  
b. provision of education and job training;  
c. provision of educational assistance for women and children victims of violence; and  
d. provision of independent business capital assistance.

#### Article 17 

The Regent as the person responsible for regional protection and empowerment of women and children has the authority to make changes to the management or dismissal of members if the existing management or members do not carry out their roles and functions.

# CHAPTER VIII 

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 18 

1. The community may participate in providing protection and empowerment for women and children victims of violence, neglect and trafficking in persons.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:  
a. providing information to authorized officials or institutions;  
b. assisting in the rescue of women and children;  
c. providing basic and or specific needs assistance for women and children;  
d. forming family partners or small units in the village/sub-district environment that are actively together protecting the environment of women and children from violence;  
e. independently socializing the rights of women and children;  
f. providing legal assistance and accompaniment.  
3. Community participation in providing protection and empowerment of women and children is carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## CHAPTER IX 

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 19 

1. The Regent conducts guidance and supervision of the organization of regional protection and empowerment of women and children.  
2. The community is authorized to actively supervise the organization of regional protection and empowerment of women and children.  
3. The authority of the community as referred to in paragraph (2) is in the form of:  
a. providing input;  
b. submitting questions and obtaining answers from responsible regional government officials;  
c. requesting realization and real activities from those given duties and authority;  
d. other matters that are constructive and provide solutions.

## CHAPTER X 

## REGIONAL ACTION PLAN

#### Article 20 

1. The Regent may establish a regional action plan related to the protection and empowerment of women and children.  
2. The regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (1) is realized in a joint commitment of all elements in the region to implement protection and empowerment of women and children and eliminate all forms of violence, neglect and trafficking in persons.

## CHAPTER XI 

## FUNDING

#### Article 21 

1. Funding for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children is charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget.  
2. In addition to funds from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget, funding may come from assistance or contributions from third parties that are not binding.  
3. Technically and for the organization of the Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children, it is placed in the budget item of the Agency determined by the Regent.  
4. The allocation of funds by the Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children is based on the work program that has been established.  
5. Accountability for the use of funds for organizing the protection of women and children is in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XIII 

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 22 

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation. So that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Balangan Regency.

#### I. GENERAL

Violence against women and children is a form of act that violates human rights, because the right to protection from violence of a woman that has been guaranteed by the 1945 Constitution and the Law on Human Rights has been violated by the perpetrator of violence, which should be respected and enforced to enhance human dignity, welfare and justice for such women. The impact of such violence causes women or children to suffer physically, psychologically, mentally, sexually, so they need to receive protection and health rehabilitation services, social rehabilitation, repatriation and legal assistance carried out in an integrated manner by victim service providers established by the Government, Regional Government and the community.

The protection and empowerment of women and children is currently a focus in development. Women and children are usually made into one unit within the scope of their regulation so that it is not focused and results in ambiguity between women and children. Women can be adults or children, while children can be female or male. In the division of authority as determined in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government, it has been focused on the respective scope of each form of handling, namely women and children are not a unity. In that context, regionally related to women and children, regional regulations must be present concerning: a). Fulfillment of Children's Rights and Special Protection of Children, b). Gender Mainstreaming, c). Protection of Witnesses/Victims and Prevention of Human Trafficking, and d). Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children From Acts of Violence. These four regional regulations are a package that must be regulated by the Regional Government in accordance with their authority.

#### Article 3

Paragraph (1)  
Sufficiently clear.  

Paragraph (2)  
Letter a  
What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causing death.  

Letter b  

What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.  

Letter c  
What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.  

Letter d  

What is meant by intimidation is the act of ordering or prohibiting by means of threatening or shackling to hurt so that the victim is unable to make choices for their best interests.  

Letter e  

What is meant by forced eviction is the act of ordering by force or with physical force not to be in a safe place or position as the right that a person has to a place that is appropriate for them.  

Letter f  
What is meant by deprivation of liberty is the act of eliminating the right to be able to live based on the best interests of a person.  

Letter g  
What is meant by neglect is:  
a. actions that result in the unfulfillment of children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;  
b. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should;  
c. actions that neglect people within their household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person;  
d. actions that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.  

Letter h  
What is meant by "exploitation" includes:  
a. actions that exploit children economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  
b. actions with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.  
c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities. What is meant by "human trafficking" is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
