# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:  
1. Region is Semarang Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.  
3. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.  
4. Regional House of Representatives of Semarang Regency hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution as an element of regional government administration.  
5. Regent is the Regent of Semarang.  
6. Regional Work Unit in charge hereinafter referred to as SKPD in charge is a Regional Work Unit that has main duties and functions in accordance with the related field.  
7. Human Rights are a set of rights inherent in the nature and existence of human beings as creatures of God Almighty and are His gifts that must be respected, upheld and protected by the state, law, Government, and every person for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity and worth.  
8. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social capabilities, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective capabilities.  
9. Women's Protection is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.  
10. Discrimination Against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status on the basis of equality between men and women.  
11. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.  
12. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.  
13. Society is individuals, families, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.  
14. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children.  
15. Labor Force is every person who is capable of performing work to produce goods and/or services either to meet their own needs or for society.  
16. Worker/Laborer is every person who works by receiving wages or remuneration in other forms.

# CHAPTER II

## SCOPE

#### Article 2

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:  
a. principles and objectives;  
b. women's rights;  
c. obligations and responsibilities of regional government;  
d. women's empowerment;  
e. women's protection;  
f. implementation of women's protection;  
g. strategy for women's empowerment and protection;  
h. mechanism for organizing women's empowerment and protection;  
i. financing;  
j. guidance and supervision;  
k. transitional provisions; and  
l. closing provisions.

# CHAPTER III

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

Women's Empowerment and Protection is implemented based on the principles of:  
a. respect for human rights;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination; and  
d. the best interests of women.

#### Article 4

The objectives of this Regional Regulation are:  
a. to empower women both individually and in groups and society at large so that they are able to live more independently; and  
b. to prevent and address the risks of violence and vulnerability of women so that their survival can be fulfilled according to the principles of equality and justice.

# CHAPTER IV

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 5

1. Every woman has the right to:  
a. live and maintain life and improve her standard of living;  
b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue offspring;  
c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life in order to become a faithful, pious, responsible, noble, happy, and prosperous human being in accordance with human rights;  
d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;  
e. be involved in every stage of the development process;  
f. be free from slavery or enslavement and threats;  
g. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;  
h. obtain welfare and a decent life;  
i. participate in politics;  
j. perform legal acts; and  
k. freely choose a partner in her marriage.  
2. Every wife during the marriage bond has the same rights and responsibilities as her husband over all matters relating to her married life, relations with her children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
3. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to children with due regard to the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government has obligations and responsibilities for women's empowerment and protection.  
2. Efforts for women's empowerment and protection as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;  
b. determination of implementation guidelines;  
c. provision of services; and  
d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.  
3. Efforts for women's empowerment and protection as referred to in paragraph (2) are carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 7

Women's empowerment is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:  
a. economy;  
b. social culture;  
c. politics; and  
d. law.

#### Article 8

Women's empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 7 letter a is implemented through:  
a. provision of skills and job training;  
b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;  
c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;  
d. facilitation and capital assistance; and  
e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 9

Empowerment of women in the socio-cultural field as referred to in Article 7 letter b shall be implemented through:  
a. enhancement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;  
b. enhancement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health;  
c. enhancement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and  
d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and the development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 10

1. Empowerment of women in the political field as referred to in Article 7 letter c shall include:  
a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;  
b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;  
c. participation in general elections; and  
d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.  
2. Empowerment of women in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the prevailing laws and regulations.

#### Article 11

1. Empowerment of women in the legal field as referred to in Article 7 letter d shall include:  
a. enhancement of awareness and knowledge in the legal field through communication, information and education services;  
b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.  
2. Empowerment of women in the legal field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the prevailing laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

#### Article 12

1. Protection of women shall include:  
a. social protection;  
b. economic protection;  
c. legal protection.  
2. Social protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is to ensure the survival of women in the form of social protection in order to ensure the fulfillment of a decent living.  
3. Economic protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is to prevent and address the risk of vulnerability resulting from shocks and social vulnerability of a person, family, group and/or community for the survival of women.  
4. Legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c shall be implemented in terms of providing protection to women who become witnesses and victims of violence and discrimination.

# CHAPTER VIII

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 13

Implementation of protection of women shall be provided to:  
a. women workers / laborers;  
b. elderly women;  
c. women with disabilities;  
d. homeless women;  
e. domestic women workers;  
f. women heads of household;  
g. women former inmates of correctional institutions;  
h. women disaster victims;  
i. women commercial sex workers; and  
j. women victims of violence.

## Part Two

Women Workers/Laborers

#### Article 14

1. Implementation of protection for women workers/laborers as referred to in Article 13 letter a is to provide protection from acts of economic exploitation and cruel, inhumane treatment and to seek proper appreciation for work.  
2. Women workers/laborers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:  
a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;  
b. health and social security;  
c. access to information and legal consultation services;  
d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve status; and  
e. witnesses and victims.

## Part Three

Elderly Women

#### Article 15

1. Implementation of protection for elderly women as referred to in Article 13 letter b is in order to extend life expectancy so that they can carry out their social functions and play an active role naturally in their social life.  
2. Elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:  
a. basic health services to maintain and improve health status including gerontological/geriatric services;  
b. use of facilities and accessibility of public facilities and infrastructure; and  
c. government administration services.

## Part Four

Women with Disabilities

#### Article 16

1. Implementation of protection for women with disabilities as referred to in Article 13 letter c is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination including freedom from cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and freedom from exploitation.  
2. Women with disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:  
a. guarantee of continuity and personal self-development;  
b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, sports, arts and culture as well as the utilization of information and communication technology;  
c. accessibility of information, legal aid, mobility, social services; and  
d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

## Part Five

Homeless Women

#### Article 17

1. Implementation of protection for homeless women as referred to in Article 13 letter d is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.  
2. Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:  
a. decent housing;  
b. health and social security;  
c. population administration registration;  
d. opportunities to obtain skills to improve economic status; and  
e. access to information and legal consultation services.

## Part Six

Domestic Women Workers

#### Article 18

1. Implementation of protection for domestic women workers as referred to in Article 13 letter e is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation in employment relations.  
2. Domestic women workers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:  
a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;  
b. health and social security;  
c. access to information and legal consultation services; and  
d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Seven

Women Heads of Household

#### Article 19

1. Implementation of protection for women heads of household as referred to in Article 13 letter f is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, negative stigma and economic empowerment for women heads of household.  
2. Women heads of household as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:  
a. discriminatory actions and efforts to weaken the function of women as the economic support of the family;  
b. strengthening of capacity and recognition as head of household in their environment; and  
c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.

## Part Eight

Women Former Inmates of Correctional Institutions

#### Article 20

1. Implementation of protection for women former inmates of correctional institutions as referred to in Article 13 letter g is in order to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting to life in society, so that they can carry out life normally.  
2. Women former inmates of correctional institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:  
a. rehabilitation and social reintegration; and  
b. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Ninth

Women Victims of Disaster

#### Article 21

1. Implementation of Protection for women victims of disaster as referred to in Article 13 letter h in order to provide protection during disaster and post-disaster.  
2. Women victims of disaster as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:  
a. access to proper housing;  
b. health services;  
c. information access services and legal consultation; and  
d. post-disaster recovery.

## Part Tenth

Commercial Sex Workers

#### Article 22

1. Implementation of Protection for commercial sex workers as referred to in Article 13 letter i in order to provide health protection and enhancement of life skills as capital to improve social status.  
2. Protection for commercial sex workers as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. providing life skills;  
b. facilitating reproductive health services; and  
c. preventing the occurrence of human trafficking.

## Part Eleventh

Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 23

1. Implementation of Protection for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 13 letter j in order to provide legal protection and/or health recovery.  
2. Protection for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. legal aid;  
b. assistance after the occurrence of acts of violence;  
c. social rehabilitation and reintegration; and  
d. health services.

# CHAPTER IX

## STRATEGY FOR WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND

## PROTECTION

#### Article 24

1. Women's empowerment and protection is implemented by integrating gender mainstreaming and gender-disaggregated data in an integrated manner based on applicable laws and regulations.  
2. In women's empowerment and protection, a Working Group and Integrated Services are established as stipulated by Regent's Decree.  
3. Women's empowerment and protection is coordinated by the Agency handling Women's Empowerment.

# CHAPTER X

## MECHANISM FOR ORGANIZING WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

## AND PROTECTION

#### Article 25

The mechanism for organizing women's empowerment and protection is carried out through stages:  
a. formulation of policy planning for programs and activities;  
b. budgeting of programs and activities;  
c. implementation of programs and activities; and  
d. monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

#### Article 26

1. Formulation of policy planning for programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter a is implemented through:  
a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of data;  
b. coordination and synchronization of planning preparation for programs and activities with relevant SKPD; and  
c. determination of programs and activities in planning documents.  
2. Formulation of policy planning for programs and activities is further regulated in minimum service standards and standard operating procedures.  
3. Formulation of policy planning for programs and activities for organizing women's empowerment and protection is coordinated by the SKPD in charge of regional development planning.  
4. Budgeting of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter b is implemented through general budget policy set forth in the APBD.  
5. Implementation of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter c is implemented through:  
a. implementation of programs and activities set forth in the SKPD budget document; and  
b. coordination and synchronization of implementation of programs and activities with relevant SKPD.  
6. Implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in Article 25 letter d is implemented through:  
a. conducting periodic monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of programs and activities that have been carried out; and  
b. making periodic reports.

# CHAPTER XI

## FINANCING

#### Article 27

All costs for Implementation of policy planning for programs and activities related to Women's Empowerment and Protection are sourced from:  
a. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget;  
b. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and  
c. Other sources that are lawful and non-binding.

# CHAPTER XII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 28

1. The Regent or SKPD in charge of women's empowerment conducts guidance and supervision over women's empowerment and protection.  
2. The form of guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) is as follows:  
a. providing socialization to the community and business world regarding women's empowerment and protection policies;  
b. providing books, leaflets, brochures regarding women's empowerment and protection, and other related issues and disseminating them to the community;  
c. facilitation of capacity building for institutions organizing women's empowerment and protection;  
d. facilitation of women's participation forums and women's organizations;  
e. facilitation of rehabilitation and reintegration services for women victims of disaster and victims of violence;  
f. coordinating the participation of the business world and other institutions, including international assistance for women's empowerment and protection; and  
g. providing awards to the community and business world both individually and institutionally that have made efforts for women's empowerment and protection.  
3. The form of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of monitoring and evaluation activities over women's empowerment and protection.

# CHAPTER XIII

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all provisions of regional laws and regulations relating to the Organization of Women's Empowerment and Protection that have existed so far, remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to the provisions in this Regional Regulation.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF SEMARANG REGENCY

##### I. GENERAL

The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia mandates the authority of regional governments to regulate and manage their own governmental affairs directed towards accelerating the realization of public welfare through improved services, empowerment and community participation. The authority of Regional Government is to make regional policies to provide services, increase participation, initiative, and community empowerment aimed at improving people's welfare.

The implementation of regional autonomy must always be oriented towards improving community welfare while always paying attention to the interests and aspirations that grow in society. Therefore, governmental affairs related to basic services such as basic education, health, fulfillment of minimum living needs, basic environmental infrastructure must be fulfilled.

Human rights are basic rights that are inherently attached to human beings, are universal and lasting, so they must be protected, respected, maintained, and may not be ignored, reduced, or deprived by anyone. In the context of Upholding Human Rights (HAM) to guarantee justice for every individual by seeking to prevent all forms of unbalanced treatment or deemed detrimental to other parties, whether individual to individual, between groups of individuals, or between individuals and between government and society, including the process of marginalization of women that causes women to be vulnerable to acts of violence, discrimination and exploitation in both domestic and public spheres.

Women have equal rights with men, however in some discriminatory conditions, the government is obliged to implement affirmative action so that the rights of women and women in vulnerable groups need to receive protection so as not to experience violence and can live a decent life according to the principles of humanity, equality and justice.

Women's empowerment and women's protection are implemented in order to realize gender equality and justice. To carry out these affairs, efforts are needed to increase the capacity of women's resources and strengthen gender mainstreaming institutions, through gender mainstreaming strategies and gender-disaggregated data.

The Semarang Regency Government as the party that has the authority to regulate and protect the citizens of Semarang Regency, deems it necessary to continuously carry out and formulate regulations in order to provide guarantees for women's empowerment and women's protection in Semarang Regency.

This Regional Regulation is expected to be able to encourage efforts to improve the physical and mental conditions of women in fulfilling their rights and life needs as part of human rights from various development sectors, increase the commitment and effectiveness of regional governments in implementing policies, programs and activities and encourage the institutional capacity of government and society that improves the quality of life of women.

#### Article 22

Commercial sex workers are women who work as sex workers of their own volition and/or are prostituted.  
Improving life skills for commercial sex workers is intended to provide additional knowledge through training and skills in accordance with the talents and abilities possessed so that they can live more independently and obtain decent work to improve their standard of living and change their social status so as not to become commercial sex workers because such work is not legalized.  

The protection of commercial sex workers in this Regional Regulation is not intended to legalize the existence of commercial sex workers, but to provide protection to the commercial sex workers in question in various sectors.
