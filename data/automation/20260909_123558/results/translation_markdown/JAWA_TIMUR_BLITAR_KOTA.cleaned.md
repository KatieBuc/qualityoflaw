a. that every citizen has the right to be free from torture, discrimination or treatment that degrades human dignity and has the right to obtain security and freedom from all forms of violence;
b. that women and children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience Violence, therefore they need to receive legal Protection;
c. that based on the provisions in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015, women's empowerment and child protection are governmental affairs that must be implemented by Regional Governments;
d. that the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence in Blitar City has not been optimal, therefore institutional strengthening is needed and there needs to be regulation that can guarantee its implementation in accordance with applicable provisions;
e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, letter c and letter d, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the City of Blitar.
2. Regional Government is the Government of the City of Blitar.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Blitar.
4. Regional apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the region.
5. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child in the womb.
6. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
7. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
8. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, community, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily.
9. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.
10. Physical violence is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or unconsciousness and/or causes death.
11. Psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, distrust or severe psychological suffering to a person.
12. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
13. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
14. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
15. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.
16. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
17. Service is an activity and immediate action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
18. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the self-empowerment of victims of violence to resolve the problems they face.
19. Repatriation is an effort to return women and children victims of violence to their families, substitute families, or communities that can provide protection and fulfill their needs.
20. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in the community.
21. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with families, communities, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
22. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organizations that provide assistance.
23. Counseling is the provision of assistance by an expert/trained person in such a way that the psychological understanding and ability of the victim and/or perpetrator increases in solving the problems faced.
24. Integrated Service Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is technical service units in the Region that carry out integrated service functions for women and children victims of crime and/or violence, including as a center for information on women's empowerment and child protection.
25. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a form of UPT established by the Regional Government that functions as an information center, service center and empowerment center for women and children.
26. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for relevant agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the prevention and protection of women and children victims of violence.
27. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to provide protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out based on the principles of:
a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of victims of violence;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. the best interests of victims;
e. empowerment of women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 3

1. The objectives of implementing protection for women and children victims of violence are to:
a. prevent violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
d. provide services to women and children victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses;
e. facilitate and mediate domestic disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household; and
f. Empower women and children victims of violence.
2. The objectives of implementing protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include aspects of:
a. prevention;
b. service and assistance;
c. reintegration and rehabilitation; and
d. empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 4

Forms of violence against women and children may include:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect; and
e. exploitation.

#### Article 5

Physical violence as referred to in Article 4 letter a includes acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or cause death.

#### Article 6

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 4 letter b includes acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 7

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 4 letter c includes:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 8

Neglect as referred to in Article 4 letter d includes:  
a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of a child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for care;  
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians or any other party responsible for their care;  
c. acts that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or  
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 9

Exploitation as referred to in Article 4 letter e includes:  
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  
b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/or  
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

# CHAPTER IV

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 10

Every victim has the right to receive:  
a. protection;  
b. information;  
c. optimal services;  
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation and reintegration stage;  
e. confidential handling;  
f. psychological and legal assistance; and  
g. guarantees of rights related to status as a family member and member of society.

#### Article 11

Child victims of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 10, also receive special rights, as follows:  
a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;  
b. the right to basic services;  
c. the right to equal protection;  
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and  
e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 12

Obligations and responsibilities in organizing protection for women and children victims of violence are the joint responsibility of:  
a. regional government;  
b. society;  
c. family; and  
d. parents.

#### Article 13

1. The Regional Government implements efforts to protect women and children victims of violence through:  
a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children victims of violence applied by the government;  
b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence;  
c. establishment and implementation of the functions of P2TP2A;  
d. ensuring the implementation of ease of service to victims;  
e. seeking effectiveness and efficiency for the victim recovery process; and  
f. seeking the creation of cooperation and coordination in victim recovery efforts.  
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, the regional government establishes action programs and activities for the protection of women and children in 1 (one) Regional Action Plan as a basis for regional apparatus in implementing protection for women and children victims of violence.  
3. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is further regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 14

1. The obligations and responsibilities of society as referred to in Article 12 letter b, are organized in the form of community participation.  
2. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:  
a. forming family partners at the sub-district level;  
b. forming women and children protection units within community organizations;  
c. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights independently;  
d. providing first aid to victims; and  
e. reporting to the competent authority if violence against victims occurs in their environment.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.  
4. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are implemented responsibly.

#### Article 15

The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 12 letters c and d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER VI

## ORGANIZATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

#### Article 16

1. The Organization of Women's Empowerment and Protection of Child Victims of Violence is realized in part through the establishment of integrated and networked UPT formed by the Regional Government, society, non-governmental organizations, donor institutions or other institutions.  
2. UPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is P2TP2A or similar institutions that have the same functions and roles.

## Part Two

### P2TP2A

### Paragraph 1

Establishment

#### Article 17

1. P2TP2A is established in the Region.  
2. The institutional structure of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Mayor Decree.  
3. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is under the coordination of the Regional Work Unit or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection.

### Paragraph 2

Coordination Mechanism

#### Article 18

1. The implementation of P2TP2A functions as referred to in Article 17, is carried out through coordination with Regional Apparatus or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection.  
2. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in the form of:  
a. Coordination meetings with work networks;  
b. Consultation;  
c. Submission of data and information; and/or  
d. Follow-up on case handling.

#### Article 19

Forms of services to victims organized by P2TP2A include:  
a. Medical services;  
b. Medicolegal services;  
c. Psychosocial services;  
d. Legal services; and  
e. Economic independence services.

#### Article 20

In carrying out the task of providing services to women and children victims of violence, P2TP2A may coordinate with government institutions and non-government institutions.

## Part Three

Prevention

#### Article 21

1. To prevent violence against women and children, the government conducts empowerment and awareness-raising to families, parents and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness-raising as referred to in paragraph (1), the regional government undertakes efforts as follows:
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;
c. opening employment opportunities for women;
d. building community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of women and children from violence;
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women and children; and
g. opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children from violence.

#### Article 22

1. Prevention of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 21, is implemented by regional apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
a. Social affairs;
b. health;
c. education;
d. manpower;
e. women's empowerment and child protection;
f. mental and spiritual; and
g. public peace and order.
2. Prevention of violence by regional apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in an integrated and continuous manner based on the Regional Action Plan.

## Part Four

Assistance Mechanism

#### Article 23

1. Assistance is implemented by persons and/or institutions that cooperate with P2TP2A.
2. The assistance mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1), is regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

## Part Five

Principles of Service and Assistance

#### Article 24

The implementation of services and assistance to victims, is carried out with the principles of:
a. fast, safe, and empathetic;
b. guarantee of confidentiality;
c. easily accessible; and
d. free of charge.

## Part Six

Services

#### Article 25

1. Forms of services provided to women and children victims of violence organized by UPT, include:
a. Medical services in the form of treatment and recovery of physical condition carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;
b. Medicolegal services are a form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;
c. Legal aid services to assist victims of violence in undergoing the judicial process;
d. Spiritual services;
e. First psychosocial assistance to victims is carried out to identify the occurrence of violence and trauma experienced by victims;
f. Assistance to victims of violence carried out by competent persons or institutions that have expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and have cooperated with UPT; and
g. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and provision of economic access so that victims can be independent.
2. Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented in accordance with minimum service standards established by the government and implemented by regional apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. women's empowerment and child protection; and
d. mental and spiritual.
3. In carrying out duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (2), the regional government cooperates with government agencies, provincial government, other district/city governments, the community, families and parents.
4. Further provisions regarding procedures for services, and handling of women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3), are regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

## Part Seven

Protection

#### Article 26

1. In the event that victims of violence require continuous handling, they have the right to stay in a safe house or alternative residence.
2. Continuous handling as referred to in paragraph (1), is handling at the rehabilitation stage carried out by individuals, groups or Government and non-Government institutions.

#### Article 27

1. Safe house as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1), provides a temporary safe place and/or shelter for victims of violence until their rights are restored.
2. The implementation and/or management of safe house as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out by the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
3. For security and at the request of victims of violence, the manager or organizer of the safe house as referred to in paragraph (2) may place victims of violence in a certain place that is kept confidential.
4. Further provisions regarding the implementation of safe house as referred to in paragraph (2) are further regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 28

1. The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;
b. technical guidance and training;
c. provision of facilities;
d. monitoring; and
e. evaluation.
3. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out with the principles of:
a. professional;
b. transparent; and
c. accountable.

#### Article 29

Guidance and supervision as referred to in Article 28 paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), are carried out in order to realize the objectives of implementing protection for women and children victims of violence in accordance with minimum service standards established by laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## REPORTING

#### Article 30

1. P2TP2A reports the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence to the Mayor.
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) is submitted in writing, including:
a. administration;
b. finance;
c. services; and
d. performance.
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2), is implemented every 3 (three) months.
4. Reporting procedures as referred to in paragraph (1), are further regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

## SOURCES OF FUNDS

#### Article 31

Funds for the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence, are sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and/or
b. other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 32

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or non-governmental organizations and universities that implement protection for women and children victims of violence.
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with regional financial capacity.

# CHAPTER X

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 33

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, the old institutions that administer women's empowerment and protection of child victims of violence may still exercise their authority until new institutions are established.

# CHAPTER XI

## CLOSING

#### Article 34

Implementing regulations of this Regional Regulation must be established no later than 1 (one) year from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

#### Article 35

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by its placement in the Regional Gazette of Blitar City.
