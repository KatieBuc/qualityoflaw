REGENT OF BLITAR

# REGIONAL REGULATION OF BLITAR REGENCY

NUMBER 11 OF 2013

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF BLITAR,

Considering:

a. that women and children are beings created by Almighty God who have noble rights and dignity in accordance with their nature as human beings;
b. that there are still many women and children who do not fully receive their basic rights, especially women and children who are victims of violence;
c. that all forms of acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of Human Rights, so that women and children who are victims of violence need to receive protection;
d. that the protection of women and children, especially victims of violence, is one of the obligations that must be carried out by the Regional Government as one of the efforts to realize community welfare;
e. that to realize protection for women and children who are victims of violence in an integrated and sustainable manner, institutional support and legislation are needed that can guarantee its implementation in a coordinated and directed manner;
f. that based on considerations a, b, c, d, and e, it is necessary to immediately establish a Regional Regulation of Blitar Regency concerning the Protection of Women and Children.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

1. Region is Blitar Regency;
2. Regional House of Representatives of Blitar Regency hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the DPRD of Blitar Regency;
3. Regent is the Regent of Blitar;
4. Regional Government is the Government of Blitar Regency; 5. Community is individuals, families, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations;
6. Woman is a creature created by God with female sex, or with reproductive organs in the form of ovaries or egg nests, ovum or egg cells, uterus, and hymen;
7. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb;
8. Protection of women and children is all efforts to protect women and children, starting from preventive actions, safeguarding and handling, aimed at providing a sense of security and comfort to women and children and their families, especially those who are victims of violence;
9. Victim of violence is a woman and child who experiences violence and/or threats of violence whether physical, psychological, sexual or economic both in the domestic sphere and in the public sphere;
10. Violence is any act or omission of obligation that results in misery or suffering whether physical, mental, sexual and economic including threats to commit acts, coercion or deprivation of freedom unlawfully;
11. Neglect is an act that results in a person's economic dependence on another person in one household;
12. Prevention is all efforts aimed at preventing the occurrence of violence and threats of violence;
13. Safeguarding is all efforts so that women and children who are vulnerable to violence do not become victims of violence;
14. Handling is all efforts aimed at handling cases of violence against women and children and their families including litigation and non-litigation services;
15. Trafficking in persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
16. Sexual exploitation is any form of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, but is not limited to all prostitution and indecency activities.
17. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) is one form of service facility for women and children consisting of government and non-government elements in an effort to fulfill information and needs in the fields of education, health, economy, politics, law, fulfillment and prevention of acts of violence and trafficking against women and children.
18. Safe house is a temporary residence used to provide fulfillment to victims in accordance with the determined standard operating procedures.
19. Child in normal or regular situation is a child who gets the opportunity to grow and develop optimally.
20. Child as victim of violence is a child who becomes a victim of one or all types of violence, whether physical, psychological, sexual, or neglected by parents and family including victims of human trafficking;
21. Child with disability is a child with a disability;
22. Parent is biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, adoptive father and/or mother;
23. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree;
24. Guardian is a person or body who in fact exercises parental authority as a parent over a child;
25. Minimum Service Standard (SPM) is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing complaint handling services, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance as well as repatriation-social reintegration especially for women and children victims of violence;
26. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is a technical guideline for the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence prepared based on Minimum Service Standards according to regional financial capacity.
27. Neglected child is a child whose needs are not properly fulfilled by those obligated according to their ability whether physical, mental and spiritual.
28. Related SKPD is an SKPD whose main tasks and functions handle the protection of women and children.
29. SKPD in charge of regional development planning is the Regional Development Planning Agency of Blitar Regency, hereinafter abbreviated as BAPEDA of Blitar Regency.
30. SKPD in charge of women's empowerment and child protection is the Agency for Women's Empowerment and Family Planning of Blitar Regency.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES, FOUNDATION AND PRINCIPLES

#### Article 2

The implementation of Protection of Women and Children is based on Pancasila and founded on the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, and is principally based on:
a. respect for human rights, women's human rights and children's rights;
b. gender justice and equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. protection for confidentiality, security and comfort of victims;
e. the best interests of the child;
f. the right to life, survival and development, and respect for the child's opinion.

# CHAPTER III

## OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

The purpose of implementing the protection of women and children, especially victims of violence, is to ensure the protection of women and children, so that they do not become victims of violence and to ensure the fulfillment of the rights of women and children and their families, both before, during and after violence occurs, as well as prevention so that violence does not occur, as well as prevention so that violence does not occur in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IV

## SCOPE

#### Article 4

The scope of implementation of protection of women and children, especially victims of violence includes:
a. implementation of protection of women and children, both before, during and after violence occurs, as well as prevention so that violence does not occur;
b. regulation of institutions and mechanisms for implementing the protection of women and children, especially victims of violence.

# CHAPTER V

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN, ESPECIALLY VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 5

1. The implementation of protection of women, children and families who are victims of violence is a comprehensive prevention system, covering protection against all forms of violence, trafficking of women and children, economic exploitation, sexual exploitation, abuse, neglect and or abandonment in all situations including women and children with disabilities, children of migrant worker families, children in conflict with the law, children in emergency situations and those vulnerable to becoming victims of violence.
2. The regional government is obligated and responsible for the implementation of protection against all forms of violence against women and children and their families.

## Part Two

Forms of Services

#### Article 6

1. Prevention services are all forms of services provided to the general public, especially women and children, both legally, socially, culturally and economically, which have an impact on increasing knowledge, skills and awareness so that they do not become victims and perpetrators of violence.
2. Protection services are all forms of services provided to the community, especially women and children who are vulnerable to becoming victims of violence, due to their situation and condition as well as their family's. The services provided include legal, social, cultural and economic services that have an impact on increasing knowledge, skills and awareness so that they do not become victims and perpetrators of violence.
3. Handling services include all forms of minimum services, both legal, medical, psychosocial, rehabilitation and social reintegration services provided to women and children who are victims of physical, psychological, sexual violence, trafficking in women and children, neglect, and exploitation.
4. The procedures, mechanisms and service standards for prevention, protection and handling, as referred to in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

Institutions

#### Article 7

1. The implementation of protection, prevention, safeguarding and handling of women and children, especially victims of violence, is carried out in an integrated manner, covering legal, medical, medicolegal and psychosocial aspects, by the Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) or another name that has the same role and function as P2TP2A.
2. The P2TP2A institution and or other similar names shall be established by Regent Decree.
3. The implementation of P2TP2A services and or other names shall be carried out based on Minimum Service Standards (SPM) and Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) in accordance with statutory provisions.

## Part Four

Forms and Mechanisms of Prevention and Protection Services

#### Article 8

1. The formulation of policies, program planning and budgets for prevention and protection services shall be implemented with the following mechanisms:
a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of data on women and children in general and those vulnerable to becoming victims of violence;
b. coordination and synchronization of program planning with related SKPD;
c. preparation of programs and activities supported by budgeting; and
d. determination of programs and activities in planning documents.
2. The implementation of prevention and protection of women and children in general and those vulnerable to becoming victims of violence shall be implemented with the following mechanisms:
a. implementation of programs and activities contained in the SKPD budgeting documents;
b. coordination and synchronization of program and activity implementation with related SKPD; and
c. monitoring, evaluation and reporting on policies, programs and activities for the implementation of protection of women and children.
3. The mechanism for formulating policies, programs and activities and services shall be further regulated in minimum service standards and standard operating procedures in the Regent Regulation.
4. The formulation of policies, programs and activities for the implementation of protection of women and children vulnerable to becoming victims of violence shall be coordinated by the SKPD in charge of Regional Development Planning.
5. Prevention and protection efforts for women and children in general and those vulnerable to becoming victims shall be implemented by:
a. forming a network in violence prevention efforts;
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns among stakeholders;
c. establishing a violence prevention system, mapping locations or areas prone to violence; and
d. conducting socialization on violence prevention against women, as well as fulfillment of the rights of children and women.

## Part Five

Forms and Mechanisms of Services for Handling Victims of Violence

#### Article 9

1. Forms of handling services for victims of violence organized by P2TP2A include:
a. medical services in the form of treatment and recovery of physical condition carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;
b. medicolegal services which are forms of medical services, especially Visum et Repertum and Visum et Repertum Psichiatricum, for evidentiary purposes in the judicial process, starting from the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court levels;
c. legal aid services to assist victims of violence in undergoing the judicial process;
d. spiritual services for moral strengthening according to the religion and beliefs adhered to;
e. first psychosocial assistance to victims carried out to identify the occurrence of violence and trauma experienced by victims;
f. assistance to victims of violence carried out by competent persons or institutions who have expertise in conducting counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and have cooperated with P2TP2T or other names;
g. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and provision of economic access so that victims can be economically independent; and
h. services to return and or remain in educational institutions for child victims of violence.
2. The implementation of handling services for women and children victims of violence shall be carried out: quickly, safely and comfortably, with empathy, non-discrimination, easily accessible, free of charge and guaranteed confidentiality.
3. The mechanism and procedures for the technical implementation of handling services for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) and further regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VI

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT, COMMUNITY, BUSINESS SECTOR

## Part One

General

#### Article 10

The Regional Government, community, and business sector are obliged and responsible for the implementation of protection of women and children, especially victims of violence and their families.

## Part Two

Regional Government

#### Article 11

The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to:
a. build a system for the implementation of protection of women and children, especially victims of violence, and their families;
b. provide infrastructure including safe houses/shelters, Human Resources comprising lawyers, doctors and psychologists-psychiatrists, volunteer companions, spiritual figures, economists, and social workers;
c. increase the institutional capacity of protection organizers, victims of violence especially for women, children and their families;
d. build a data and information center on protection, victims of violence especially for women, children and their families;
e. conduct monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection, victims of violence especially for women, children and their families.

#### Article 12

In carrying out its obligations and responsibilities, the Regional Government has the authority to:
a. make policies and programs;
b. formulate implementation guidelines;
c. organize services;
d. coordinate policies, programs, budgets and services;
e. conduct monitoring, supervision and evaluation of the implementation of protection and prevention of victims of violence especially for women, children and their families.

#### Article 13

In coordinating the implementation of protection of victims of violence especially for women, children and their families, the Regional Government shall appoint the Regional Government Work Unit (SKPD) in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection as coordinator.

## Part Three

Community

#### Article 14

1. The community may participate in carrying out obligations and responsibilities toward the implementation of protection of women and children, victims of violence especially for women, children and their families.  
2. The community who knows, hears, and sees the occurrence of violence is obligated to provide assistance and protection to victims of violence especially women, children and their families.

## Part Four

Business Community

#### Article 15

The business community may participate in carrying out obligations and responsibilities toward the implementation of protection of victims of violence especially for women, children and their families.

# CHAPTER VII

## FINANCING

#### Article 16

1. Financing of the implementation of protection of women and children, prevention, safeguarding and handling of victims of violence especially women and children, shall be charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of the Regency, through the SKPD responsible for women and children protection affairs.  
2. Budget management for the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence shall be conducted with the principles of fairness, efficiency, transparency and accountability.

# CHAPTER VIII

## EVALUATION, REPORTING AND ACCOUNTABILITY

#### Article 17

1. Evaluation, reporting and accountability by P2TP2A or other similar name as the institution implementing protection of women and children victims of violence shall be conducted periodically to the Regent.  
2. The mechanism for evaluation, reporting and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children, especially victims of violence shall be regulated by Regent's Decree.

# CHAPTER IX

## INVESTIGATION PROVISIONS

## FOR CIVIL SERVANTS

#### Article 18

1. Certain civil servant officials within the Regency Government are given authority to carry out investigation of violations of the provisions in this Regional Regulation.  
2. The authority of investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) is:  
a. to receive reports or complaints from a person or family and other persons, regarding the existence of actions or violations of the Regional Regulation;  
b. to conduct first actions and examination at the scene;  
c. to summon experts needed in connection with the examination of the case;  
d. to summon persons to be heard for their statements and examined as witnesses or suspects;  
e. to conduct actions according to law that can be accounted for.

##### CHAPTER X

##### SANCTION PROVISIONS

#### Article 19

Any person who deliberately allows the occurrence of violence against women and children and/or does not report the occurrence of violence against women and children, shall be subject to sanctions as regulated in the applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 20

1. Any person who discloses the whereabouts of victims of violence who are being placed in a confidential location, shall be threatened with imprisonment of a maximum of 3 (three) months or a fine of a maximum of Rp.50,000,000,- (fifty million rupiah).  
2. In the event that the act as referred to in paragraph (1) is committed by an official appointed to implement protection, shall be threatened with imprisonment of a maximum of 6 (six) months or a fine of a maximum of Rp.50,000,000,- (fifty million rupiah).

#### Article 21

Organizers or managers of P2TPA and/or Safe Houses who are proven to collect fees from victims of violence shall be threatened with imprisonment of a maximum of 6 (six) months or a fine of a maximum of Rp.50,000,000,- (fifty million rupiah).

#### Article 22

In the event that the act as referred to in Article 21 is committed by an organizer or manager who is a Civil Servant, the sanction shall be added with employment sanctions in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 23

At the time this Regional Regulation is stipulated and promulgated, all provisions relating to the protection of women and children, especially victims of violence shall remain valid insofar as they are not contrary to this Regional Regulation.

# CHAPTER XII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 24

All forms of operational regulations for the implementation of this Regional Regulation must be in place no later than 6 months after this Regional Regulation is stipulated and promulgated.

#### Article 25

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation. In order that everyone may know it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Blitar Regency.
