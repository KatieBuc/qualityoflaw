# VIOLENCE.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Regent is the Regent of West Bangka.
2. Regional Government is the Government of West Bangka Regency.
3. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is an institution providing services to victims of violence, based in a Hospital, managed jointly in the form of medical care (including medico-legal), psycho-social and legal services.
4. Social Community Institution is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and or other community organizations.
5. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including those in the womb.
6. Woman is a person who has female genitalia who can menstruate and become pregnant or has obtained legal status as a woman.
7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim;
8. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions. Coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life;
9. Violence against children is any action that results in misery or suffering to children physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom.
10. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.
11. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
12. Economic violence is any act that deliberately neglects family members in the form of not providing a decent living care or maintenance.
13. Psychological violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
14. Victim is a woman and child who experiences and/or suffers either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
15. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, Court, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear or violence against women has occurred.
16. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
17. Optimal Service is a dynamic effort consisting of various parts that are regularly related, followed by performance offered by one party to another party by providing benefits, in order to achieve the best goal.
18. Information is a message (speech or expression) or collection of messages consisting of an order sequence of symbols, or meaning that can be interpreted from the message or collection of messages and can be recorded or transmitted.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out based on the principles of respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity, non-discrimination, gender equality and justice as well as protection of the human rights of Women and Children and the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to provide protection and services covering aspects of prevention, service and assistance, reunification and empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

Every victim has the right to receive:
a. protection;
b. information;
c. optimal service;
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;
e. confidential handling;
f. psychological and legal assistance; and
g. guarantee of rights related to the victim's status as a family member and community member.

# CHAPTER IV

## DUTIES AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 5

1. The Regional Government has the duty to carry out prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against Women and Children, by:
a. collecting data and information about Women and Children Victims of Violence;
b. socializing laws and regulations related to the protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
c. conducting education on anti-violence values against children and women; and
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of violence.
2. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against Women and Children, providing and organizing services for victims in the form of:
a. providing and facilitating the establishment of integrated services for victims by involving institutions and community elements;
b. encouraging public awareness about the importance of protection for victims; and
c. empowering the community for the prevention of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER V

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government in implementing protection for Victims of Violence may involve community participation.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, groups or social community organizations.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) may be carried out in the form of:
a. preventing acts of violence against Women and Children;
b. submitting reports to the authorities if acts of violence against Women and Children occur; and
c. providing assistance to victims.

#### Article 7

1. Social community organizations that participate in the implementation of protection for victims as referred to in Article 6 paragraph (2), are given guidance by the Regional Government.
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of social guidance, skills guidance and operational technical guidance.

# CHAPTER VI

## INSTITUTIONS IMPLEMENTING PROTECTION FOR VICTIMS

#### Article 8

1. The Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT) as an Implementing Institution, Prevention and Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of elements of:
a. health;
b. law enforcement;
c. social and empowerment;
d. education and religion;
e. Non-Governmental Organizations; and
f. private sector.
3. Further provisions regarding PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## IMPLEMENTATION AND FORMS OF PROTECTION

#### Article 9

1. Implementation of Protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the PPT framework.  
2. PPT in handling medical, legal, medicolegal, psychological and economic protection which in its implementation may partner with Social Community Institutions engaged in the protection of Women and Children.  
3. Implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) must take into account religious norms, customs and the rights and obligations of parents/guardians, husbands and/or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.  
4. The mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) to be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 10

1. Every victim of violence has the right to receive protection in accordance with applicable provisions.  
2. Protection of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) is provided to residents of West Bangka Regency whether committed within the territory of West Bangka Regency or outside the territory of West Bangka Regency.  
3. In addition to protection of victims who are residents of West Bangka as referred to in paragraph (2), protection is also provided to non-residents of West Bangka where the incident occurred within the territory of West Bangka Regency.

#### Article 11

1. Forms of protection provided to Victims organized by PPT are in the form of protection:  
a. medical;  
b. legal;  
c. medicolegal (Forensic Medicine);  
d. psychological; ore. economic.  
2. The form of medical protection for Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, is in the form of treatment and recovery from physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.  
3. The form of legal protection for Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, is in the form of services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.  
4. The form of medicolegal protection for Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, is in the form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field.  
5. The form of psychological protection for Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, is in the form of assistance in order to restore traumatic conditions including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving.  
6. The form of economic protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1), is in the form of services for skills and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

# CHAPTER VIII

## SOURCES OF FUNDS

#### Article 12

1. The source of funds for the implementation of victim protection is charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget.  
2. In addition to the source of funds as referred to in paragraph (1), funds may come from other lawful sources.

# CHAPTER IX

## REPORTING

#### Article 13

1. PPT is obliged to report in writing the implementation of protection for Women and Children victims of violence to the Regent.  
2. The report as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. administration;  
b. finance;  
c. services; and  
d. performance.  
3. Submission of reports as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out at least once every 3 (three) months.

# CHAPTER X

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 14

Further provisions regarding technical implementation are regulated by Regent Regulation no later than 1 (one) year from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

#### Article 15

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.  

So that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of West Bangka Regency.
