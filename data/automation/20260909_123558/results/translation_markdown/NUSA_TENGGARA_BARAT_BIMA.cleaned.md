# IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND CHILD PROTECTION

REGENT OF BIMA

WEST NUSA TENGGARA PROVINCE

# REGIONAL REGULATION OF BIMA REGENCY

NUMBER 5 OF 2019

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND CHILD PROTECTION

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF BIMA,

Considering:
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
a. that every person including women and children has the right to the fulfillment of rights and protection from violence, exploitation, and discriminatory treatment in order to provide security and comfort;
b. that in order to fulfill the rights and protection of women and children from violence, exploitation, and discriminatory treatment in Bima Regency, the role of regional government is required in its implementation;
c. that the implementation of protection for women and children needs to be given direction and foundation to ensure legal certainty based on the principle of justice;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection;

In View of:
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 69 of 1958 concerning the Establishment of Level II Regions within the Level I Regions of Bali, West Nusa Tenggara and East Nusa Tenggara (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1958 Number 122, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 1655);
3. Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1984 Number 29, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3277);
4. Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 109, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4235) as has been amended several times, most recently by Law Number 17 of 2016 concerning Stipulation of Government Regulation in Lieu of Law Number 1 of 2016 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection into Law (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2016 Number 99, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5882);
5. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as has been amended several times, most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);
6. Government Regulation Number 12 of 2017 concerning Guidance and Supervision of Regional Government Administration (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2017 Number 73, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 6041);
7. Regulation of the Minister of Home Affairs Number 15 of 2008 concerning General Guidelines for the Implementation of Gender Mainstreaming in the Regions as has been amended by Regulation of the Minister of Home Affairs Number 67 of 2011 concerning Amendment to Regulation of the Minister of Home Affairs Number 15 of 2008 concerning General Guidelines for the Implementation of Gender Mainstreaming in the Regions (State News of the Republic of Indonesia of 2011 Number 927);

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:  
1. Region is Bima Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Bima.  
4. Regional House of Representatives of Bima Regency, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of regional government administration.  
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and DPRD in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.  
6. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.  
7. Child's rights are part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and fulfilled by parents, family, community, government, regional government, and the state.  
8. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
9. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.  
10. Women's Empowerment is a structured effort to realize gender equality in terms of access, participation, control, and benefits in development and control of resources in order to improve quality of life and enhance the role of women.  
11. Violence is any act that results in misery and/or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.  
12. Regional Children's Forum, hereinafter referred to as Forda, is a forum for Children's participation formed with the aim of facilitating children's involvement in decision-making processes and enjoying changes concerning their lives either directly or indirectly, which is carried out with the consent and will of all children based on awareness and understanding, in accordance with age and level of maturity of thinking.  
13. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for Women's Empowerment, Child Protection, Population Control and Family Planning, hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD P3AP2KB, is a regional technical implementation unit established by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.  
14. Gender mainstreaming, hereinafter abbreviated as PUG, is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of national development policies and programs.  
15. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural circumstances of society.  
16. Gender Responsive Planning and Budgeting, hereinafter abbreviated as PPRG, is a planning and budgeting instrument prepared to accommodate the different needs and difficulties as women and as men with the aim of realizing just planning and budgeting in order to achieve Gender Equality in development.  
17. Gender Responsive Budget, hereinafter abbreviated as ARG, is a budget that is responsive to the needs of women and men with the aim of realizing gender equality and justice
18. Gender equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, national defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.  
19. Child-Friendly Regency, hereinafter abbreviated as KLA, is a regency that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of government, community and business commitments and resources that are planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights.  
20. Child Rights Mainstreaming, hereinafter abbreviated as PUHA, is a strategy for implementing child protection by integrating children's rights in laws and regulations, policies, programs, activities and budgets starting from the planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring and evaluation stages with the principle of the best interests of the child.  
21. Child Care is an effort to meet the needs for affection, attachment, safety, and welfare that are permanent and sustainable for the best interests of the Child.  
22. Gender Mainstreaming Working Group, hereinafter referred to as Pokja PUG, is a consultation forum for implementers and movers of PUG from various agencies or institutions in the region.  
23. Gender Mainstreaming Focal Point, hereinafter referred to as Focal Point PUG, is a Regional Apparatus official who has the ability to carry out PUG in their respective work units.  
24. Regional Medium-Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMD, is a regional planning document for a period of 5 (five) years and refers to the National Medium-Term Development Plan.  
25. Regional Government Work Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RKPD, is a regional planning document for a period of 1 (one) year. 26. Regional Apparatus Work Plan, hereinafter referred to as Renja Regional Apparatus, is a work planning document of the Regional Apparatus for a period of 1 (one) year.  
27. Regional Action Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RAD, is a Regional Action Plan in implementing the protection of women and children.

#### Article 2

The implementation of women's empowerment and child protection is carried out based on the principles of:  
a. respect for human rights;  
b. gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination;  
d. victim protection;  
e. best interests of the child;  
f. survival and development of the child;  
g. child participation; and  
h. benefit.

#### Article 3

The implementation of women's empowerment and child protection aims to:  
a. realize opportunities in access, participation, control, and benefits in development and control of resources in the Region;  
b. prevent and address violence, exploitation, and discriminatory treatment against women and children;  
c. improve the quality and role of women and children; and  
d. provide data for planning women's empowerment and fulfillment and protection of children's rights.

# CHAPTER II

## AUTHORITY

#### Article 4

1. The implementation of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in the Region is carried out by the Regional Government.  
2. In implementing the administration of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, the Regent has the authority to:  
a. implement policies on Women's Empowerment and Child Protection at the regency level;  
b. provide human resources with gender and child rights perspectives, facilities, infrastructure and infrastructure that are friendly, safe, and comfortable for women and Children;  
c. coordinate and cooperate with stakeholders that are cross-sectoral in nature in the Region; and  
d. conduct technical guidance and supervision in the Region.  
3. The Regent in exercising his/her authority as referred to in paragraph (2), may assign the Regional Secretary or Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and Regional Child Protection.

#### Article 5

1. Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in providing services for women and children who experience problems of violence, discrimination, special protection, and other technical problems shall be implemented by UPTD P3AP2KB.
2. UPTD P3AP2KB as referred to in paragraph (1) shall carry out service functions:
a. public complaints;
b. victim outreach;
c. case management;
d. temporary shelter;
e. mediation; and
f. victim assistance.
3. In the implementation of service functions as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b, a task force for women's empowerment and child protection may be formed, facilitated by the Regional Apparatus in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
4. Service functions provided by UPTD P3AP2KB as referred to in paragraph (2) shall refer to service standards that have been established in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER III

## IMPLEMENTATION

## Part One

Planning

#### Article 6

Planning for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection shall be integrated into the RPJMD, RKPD, Regional Apparatus Work Plan, and RAD.

## Part Two

Quality of Life of Women

#### Article 7

Implementation of improving the Quality of Life of women shall be carried out by:
a. institutionalization of PUG in Regional Government institutions;
b. Women's Empowerment in the political, legal, social, and economic fields in community organizations at the Regional level; and
c. strengthening and development of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the Regional level.

#### Article 8

Institutionalization of PUG in government institutions as referred to in Article 7 letter a shall be carried out through the implementation of gender-responsive policies, programs, and activities at the regional level.

#### Article 9

Women's Empowerment in the political, legal, social, and economic fields in community organizations as referred to in Article 7 letter b shall be carried out through:
a. improving the quality and quantity of women in the political, legal, social, and economic fields in community organizations at the regional level; and
b. involvement of women in decision-making in the political, legal, social, and economic fields in community organizations at the regional level.

#### Article 10

Strengthening and development of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions as referred to in Article 7 letter c shall be carried out through:
a. capacity building of resources of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the regional level;
b. assistance in the implementation of Women's Empowerment services at the regional level;
c. development of Communication, Information, and Education for Women's Empowerment at the regional level;
d. strengthening networks among Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the regional level;
e. facilitation of standardization implementation of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the regional level; and
f. development of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the regional level.

## Part Three

Protection of Women

#### Article 11

Implementation of protection of women shall be carried out by:
a. prevention of violence against women involving parties in the Region;
b. provision of services for women victims of violence requiring coordination between Regional Apparatus, Vertical Agencies, and other institutions in the Region; and
c. strengthening and development of women's protection service provider institutions in the Region.

#### Article 12

Prevention of violence against women as referred to in Article 11 letter a shall be carried out through:
a. implementation of policies, programs and activities for prevention of violence against women involving parties within the regional scope;
b. provision of education on prevention of violence against women in the region;
c. increasing the participation of the community, business sector, and media in the prevention of violence against women in the region; and
d. creation and/or utilization of Citizen Information System applications in submitting reports of cases of violence against women and children.

#### Article 13

Further provisions regarding the creation and/or utilization of applications as referred to in Article 12 letter d shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 14

Provision of services for women victims of violence as referred to in Article 11 letter b shall be carried out through:
a. implementation of provision of services for women victims of violence through UPTD P3AP2KB or work units handling PPA affairs requiring coordination in the region;
b. provision of public complaint services for women victims of violence in the region;
c. provision of outreach services for women victims of violence in the region;
d. provision of case management services for women victims of violence at the district regional level;
e. provision of temporary shelter services for women victims of violence in the region;
f. provision of mediation services for women victims of violence in the region;
g. provision of assistance services for women victims of violence in the region;
h. provision of service facilities and infrastructure for women victims of violence in the region;
i. provision of information facilities and infrastructure available in public spaces; and
j. developing an emergency system responsive to the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children through applications.

#### Article 15

Strengthening and development of women's protection service provider institutions as referred to in Article 11 letter c shall be carried out through:
a. capacity building of service providers for handling and protection of the rights of women victims of violence in the region;
b. provision of specific needs for women in emergency situations and special conditions in the region; and
c. development of Communication, Information, and Education for the protection of women's rights in the region.

## Part Four

Quality of Family

#### Article 16

Implementation of improving the quality of family shall be carried out by:
a. improving the quality of family in realizing gender equality and children's rights;
b. strengthening and development of service provider institutions for improving the quality of family in realizing gender equality and children's rights; and
c. provision of services for families in realizing gender equality and children's rights.

#### Article 17

Improving the quality of family as referred to in Article 16 letter a shall be carried out through:
a. implementation of policies, programs and activities for improving the quality of family to realize gender equality and child protection in the region; and
b. development of materials and implementation of Communication, Information, and Education on gender equality and child protection for families in the region.

#### Article 18

Strengthening and development of service provider institutions for improving the quality of family as referred to in Article 16 letter b shall be carried out through:
a. capacity building of resources of service provider institutions for improving the quality of family in the region;
b. strengthening networks among service provider institutions for improving the quality of family in the region;
c. facilitation of standardization implementation of service provider institutions for improving the quality of family in the region; and
d. development of service provider institutions for improving the quality of family in the region.

#### Article 19

Provision of services for families in realizing gender equality and children's rights as referred to in Article 16 letter c shall be carried out through:
a. implementation of provision of services for families in realizing gender equality and child protection whose working area is in the region;
b. assistance in the implementation of services for improving the quality of family in the region; and
c. provision of service facilities and infrastructure for families in realizing gender equality and child protection in the region.

## Part Five

Gender and Child Data System

#### Article 20

1. The Regional Government develops a database and information system for gender and Child data that is integrated with the provincial government and central government.
2. The Regional Government in developing the database and information system for gender and Child data as referred to in paragraph (1) provides human resources and infrastructure facilities.
3. The implementation of the Gender and Child Data System is carried out by means of collection, processing, analysis, and presentation of gender and child data within the data institutions in the Region.

#### Article 21

The implementation of the Gender and Child Data System as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (3) is carried out through:
a. provision of gender and child data in the region;
b. presentation and utilization of gender and child data within data institutions at the regional level; and
c. implementation of the gender and Child data system refers to the guidelines that have been established in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Six

Fulfillment of Children's Rights

#### Article 22

The implementation of the fulfillment of Children's rights is carried out by means of:
a. institutionalization of the fulfillment of Children's rights in government institutions, non-government, media and business sectors in the Region; and
b. strengthening and development of service provider institutions for improving the quality of life of children at the Regional level.

#### Article 23

The institutionalization of the fulfillment of Children's rights as referred to in Article 22 letter a is carried out through:
a. implementation of policies, programs and activities for the fulfillment of Children's rights in efforts to prevent violations of Children's rights in the region;
b. increasing the participation and role of children's forums or the like in the region;
c. facilitating the strengthening of institutions and resources of government institutions, non-government, media and business sectors in the region;
d. facilitating the strengthening of networks among government institutions, non-government, media, and business sectors in the region; and
e. strengthening cross-institutional cooperation among government institutions, non-government, media, and business sectors to realize Child-Friendly Regency (KLA), Child-Friendly Sub-district (Kelana), and Child-Friendly Village (Dela).

#### Article 24

The strengthening and development of service provider institutions for improving the quality of life of children as referred to in Article 22 letter b is carried out through:
a. capacity building of resources of service provider institutions for improving the quality of life of children in the region;
b. assistance in the implementation of services for improving the quality of life of children in the region;
c. development of materials and implementation of Communication, Information, and Education on the fulfillment of Children's rights for service provider institutions for improving the quality of life of children in the region;
d. strengthening networks among service provider institutions for improving the quality of life of Children in the region;
e. facilitation of the implementation of standardization of service provider institutions for improving the quality of life of Children in the region; and
f. development of service provider institutions for improving the quality of life of children in the region.

## Part Seven

Special Protection for Children

#### Article 25

The implementation of Special Protection for Children is carried out by means of:
a. prevention of violence against Children;
b. provision of services for Children who require special protection through coordination of parties both government and non-government institutions in the Region; and
c. strengthening and development of service provider institutions for Children who require special protection in the Region.

#### Article 26

Prevention of violence against Children as referred to in Article 25 letter a is carried out through:
a. implementation of policies, programs and activities for the prevention of violence against Children who require special protection involving government and non-government institutions in the region;
b. provision of education on the prevention of violence against Children who require special protection in the region;
c. increasing community participation through the Community-Based Integrated Child Protection (PATBM) movement, business sectors, media and children in the prevention of violence in the region; and
d. strengthening cooperation to realize Child-Friendly Regency (KLA), Child-Friendly Sub-district (Kelana), and Child-Friendly Village (Dela).

#### Article 27

The provision of services for Children who require special protection as referred to in Article 25 letter b is carried out through:
a. implementation of the provision of services for Children who require special protection through Regional Apparatus that handles the affairs of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection and/or UPTD P3AP2KB in the region;
b. provision of public complaint services for Children who require special protection in the region;
c. provision of outreach services for Children who require special protection in the region;
d. provision of case management services for Children who require special protection in the region;
e. provision of temporary shelter services for Children who require special protection in the region;
f. provision of mediation services for Children who require special protection in the region;
g. provision of assistance services for children who require special protection in the region; and
h. provision of facilities and infrastructure for services for children who require special protection in the region.

#### Article 28

The strengthening and development of service provider institutions for Children who require special protection as referred to in Article 25 letter c is carried out through:
a. capacity building of resources of service provider institutions for protection and handling of Children who require special protection in the region;
b. provision of specific needs for Children in emergency situations and special conditions in the region;
c. provision of Communication, Information, and Education on the protection of Children who require special protection in the region;
d. strengthening networks among service provider institutions for protection of Children who require special protection in the region; and
e. facilitation of the implementation of standardization of service provider institutions for the protection of Children who require special protection in the region.

# CHAPTER IV

## GENDER MAINSTREAMING

#### Article 29

1. The implementation of Women's Empowerment is carried out through PUG.
2. The implementation of PUG as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by means of integrating Gender in the process of planning, formulating, implementing, monitoring, and evaluating policies, programs, and development activities of the Regional Government that produce ARG.
3. Policies, programs, and development activities of the Regional Government with a Gender perspective as referred to in paragraph (2) are included in the RPJMD, Strategic Plan (Renstra) of Regional Apparatus, and Renja SKPD.

#### Article 30

1. The Regent is responsible for the implementation of PUG in the Region. (2) The Regent establishes the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection as the coordinator of the implementation of PUG in the Region.
3. The success of the implementation of PUG by implementing Gender Responsive Planning and Budgeting (PPRG) is measured through human development indicators, Gender development indicators, and gender empowerment indicators in development.
4. The Regent conducts periodic evaluation of the implementation of PUG every year according to their authority to determine the achievements of the implementation of PUG.

#### Article 31

1. In an effort to accelerate the implementation of PUG, institutional strengthening of PUG is carried out by establishing a PUG Working Group and PUG Focal Point. (2) The PUG Working Group as referred to in paragraph (1) is a means of coordination for PUG driving agencies and consultation for implementers from various agencies and institutions in the Region whose members consist of all heads of Regional Apparatus.
3. The PUG Focal Point as referred to in paragraph (1) is an apparatus of the Regional Apparatus who has the ability and expertise to carry out PUG in their respective work units.

#### Article 32

Further provisions regarding PUG are regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

## CHILD-FRIENDLY REGENCY

#### Article 33

1. The Regional Government may organize CFC for the fulfillment of Children's rights and special protection of Children.  
2. The organization of CFC as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be the responsibility of the Regent.  
3. The Regent shall appoint the Regional Apparatus that administers the affairs of women's empowerment and Child Protection as the coordinator for the organization of CFC.

#### Article 34

The organization of CFC as referred to in Article 33 paragraph (1) shall be implemented in 5 (five) stages, namely:  
a. CFC planning;  
b. pre-CFC;  
c. CFC Regional Action Plan;  
d. CFC implementation; and  
e. CFC evaluation.

#### Article 35

1. At the CFC planning stage, the Regional Government shall realize its commitment by conducting a CFC declaration, establishing a CFC Task Force, and preparing a CFC profile.  
2. The regulation regarding the establishment of the CFC Task Force as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree. (3) The preparation of the CFC profile as referred to in paragraph (1) shall contain data and information that reflects the condition of implementation of CFC indicators in the Region and its measures.

#### Article 36

1. At the Pre-CFC stage, the Regional Government shall conduct a self-assessment of the CFC profile to determine the Regional CFC status before commencing the organization of CFC.  
2. The self-assessment as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be based on the achievement of 24 (twenty-four) CFC Indicators included in the institutional framework and 5 (five) CFC clusters that have been established, namely:  
a. institutional framework;  
b. civil rights and freedoms cluster;  
c. family environment and alternative care cluster;  
d. basic health and welfare cluster;  
e. education, leisure time utilization, and cultural activities cluster; and  
f. special protection cluster.  
3. The self-assessment as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted by referring to the guidelines in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 37

1. In organizing CFC, the Regional Government shall integrate policies, programs, and development activities in the RAD.  
2. The preparation of RAD as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 38

The CFC implementation stage shall be carried out based on the RAD that has been prepared by optimizing the role of the CFC Task Force and conducting monitoring.

#### Article 39

The Regent shall conduct periodic evaluations of the organization of CFC every year in accordance with his/her authority to determine the achievement of CFC organization.

#### Article 40

Further provisions regarding the organization of CFC shall be regulated by Regent's Regulation.

# CHAPTER VI

## CARE

#### Article 41

1. Every Child in the Region whose growth and development is not guaranteed or whose needs are not fulfilled or who experiences neglect from parents, guardians, or family in a proper manner, whether physical, mental, spiritual, or social, shall have the right to receive care.  
2. Care that is implemented as a result of Child separation shall be carried out in the best interests of the child.  
3. In the event that Child separation is carried out in the best interests of the child, Care must be carried out by a Child Care Institution with requirements in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 42

1. Facilitation of child care by child care institutions shall be implemented by regional apparatus in charge of women and children, social affairs, manpower, education, health, planning, and other regional apparatus as needed.  
2. Facilitation of Child Care as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out outside Social Institutions or inside Social Institutions in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 43

1. Guidance and supervision of the implementation of women's empowerment and child protection shall be carried out by the Regent.  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be coordinated by the Regional Inspectorate.  
3. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be implemented in the form of audits, reviews, monitoring, evaluation, oversight, and technical guidance, as well as other forms of guidance and supervision.  
4. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3) shall be implemented from the planning, budgeting, organizing, implementation, reporting, evaluation, and accountability stages of Regional Government administration.  
5. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented at least 1 (one) time in 1 (one) year.  
6. The results of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall constitute evaluation material for women's empowerment and child protection program policies in the Region.

# CHAPTER VIII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 44

1. The community, Family and/or parents may participate in the organization of women's empowerment and child protection.  
2. The community as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out individually or institutionally.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out, among others, by:  
a. establishing empowerment and child protection units within community organizations, community-based child protection, or professional organizations;  
b. conducting socialization regarding women's empowerment, prevention of violence against women and children, and child protection;  
c. preventing acts of violence in the community environment; and  
d. providing information and/or reporting the occurrence of violence, discrimination, exploitation, and human rights violations against women and Children to the competent authorities if violence against women and Children occurs in their environment;  
e. optimizing the function of community organizations such as PKK, integrated service institutions for the protection of women and children in the Region;  
f. conducting socialization and education on laws and regulations related to women's empowerment, protection of women and Children, such as prevention of unregistered marriages and the dangers of child marriage;  
g. providing input in the process of formulating laws and regulations in the region;  
h. prohibiting the occurrence of unregistered marriages;  
i. providing space for Children to gather and organize to express themselves and convey their opinions; and  
j. supervising Children in accessing various information.

# CHAPTER IX

## FUNDING

#### Article 45

Funding for the organization of women's empowerment and child protection that is the authority of the regional government shall be sourced from:  
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
b. other lawful and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all provisions regulating women and children shall remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to the provisions as regulated in this Regional Regulation.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF BIMA REGENCY

NUMBER 5 OF 2019 CONCERNING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND CHILD PROTECTION

##### I. GENERAL

The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia mandates Regional Governments to regulate and manage their own regions for the realization of community welfare through improved services, empowerment and community participation. One of the authorities of regional governments is to make regional policies with the aim of improving the welfare of the people in their regions.

In addition, the 1945 Constitution has also mandated in Article 28D paragraph (1) that every person has the right to recognition, guarantee, protection and fair legal certainty as well as equal treatment before the law. Article 28B Paragraph 2 also states that every child has the right to survival, growth, and development as well as the right to protection from violence and discrimination. This shows that girls should be protected by the state, especially against violence because their rights are already regulated in the constitution.

Therefore, it is necessary to make efforts to prevent and eradicate all forms of discrimination and violence against women and children, especially for the Region. The Region is one of the most authorized parties in carrying out women's empowerment and child protection by conducting and formulating regulations to provide guarantees for women's empowerment and child protection.

One form of such regulation is Regional Regulation. The regulation is expected to be able to encourage efforts to improve the physical and mental conditions of women and child victims of violence.

#### Article 1

#### Article 2

Letter a  
What is meant by "respect for human rights" is as a basic form of human rights to be free from fear, threats, and violence in accordance with related policies such as the ratification of CEDAW and the Human Rights Protection Law as described above as the basis for formulation.

Letter b  
What is meant by "the principle of gender equality" is that the implementation of gender equality must realize the fulfillment of human rights, livelihood needs, and aspirations that differ between women and men, girls and boys, the elderly, disability groups, as well as vulnerable and marginalized groups.

Letter c  
What is meant by "the principle of non-discrimination" is not distinguishing ethnicity, race, religion, gender, language, political views, national origin, economic status, physical or psychological condition of the child, or other factors.

Letter d  
What is meant by "the principle of victim protection" is prioritizing the safety of victims from all forms of violence.

Letter e  
What is meant by "the principle of the best interests of the child" is that in all actions concerning children carried out by the government, regional government, society, legislative bodies, and judicial bodies, must be based on the best interests of the child as the primary consideration.

Letter f  
What is meant by "the principle of survival and child development" is the most fundamental human right for children that is protected by the state, government, regional government, society, family, and parents.

Letter g  
What is meant by "the principle of child participation" is respect for children's rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives Letter h What is meant by "the principle of benefit" is that the implementation of women's empowerment and child protection must provide equal benefits for women and men.

#### Article 3

What is meant by "Access" is the identification of whether development policies/programs have provided fair space and opportunities for women and men.

What is meant by "Participation" is the identification of whether development policies/programs fairly involve women and men in voicing needs, constraints including in decision-making.

What is meant by "Control" is the identification of whether policies/programs provide equal opportunities for control to women and men to control development resources. What is meant by "Benefits" is the identification of whether policies/programs provide fair benefits for women and men.
