# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
2. Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and honor, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
3. Women are persons of female gender.
4. Children are persons who have not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including children still in the womb.
5. Victims are women and children who experience misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring within the territory of the Region.
6. Violence is any act against women and children that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.
7. Women's empowerment is the strengthening of women victims of violence to be able to work and earn independently after being recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
8. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural circumstances of society.
9. Social rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological and social conditions in order to be able to perform their roles again properly both in the family and in society.
10. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with their families and communities.
11. Assistance is an activity to provide counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims carried out by persons or representatives of institutions with expertise.
12. Prevention is an effort by the government together with families and communities as well as the private sector and/or business world to prevent the occurrence of violence through socialization, education, advocacy and promotion activities.
13. Handling is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing, and knowing that violence against victims will, is, and/or has occurred.
14. Services are immediate activities and actions carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy, and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
15. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct ascending or descending line up to the third degree.
16. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is a technical implementing unit of the Regional Apparatus that carries out technical operational activities and/or technical supporting activities for integrated services for women and children victims of violence.
17. Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK, is a coordination forum for prevention, handling and empowerment of women and child victims of violence whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner and is established at the Regional, sub-district, and village levels.
18. Region is Sleman Regency.
19. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
20. Regent is the Regent of Sleman.
21. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and DPRD in the administration of governmental affairs that are the authority of the Region.

#### Article 2

Protection of women and children victims of violence is implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect and recognition of rights; and
b. equal human dignity.

#### Article 3

Protection of women and children victims of violence is implemented based on the principles of:  
a. gender responsiveness;  
b. non-discrimination;  
c. equal and respectful relationships;  
d. maintaining privacy and confidentiality;  
e. providing a sense of security and comfort;  
f. respecting individual differences;  
g. non-judgmental;  
h. respecting the choices and decisions of victims;  
i. sensitivity to the background and condition of victims;  
j. use of appropriate language understood by victims;  
k. fast and simple;  
l. empathy; and  
m. best interests of victims.

#### Article 4

1. The objectives of protection of women victims of violence are directed to:  
a. create conditions that support the development of the potential of women victims of violence;  
b. strengthen the potential already possessed by women victims of violence; and  
c. improve the work skills of women victims of violence.  
2. The objectives of protection of children victims of violence are directed to:  
a. provide social rehabilitation for children victims of violence;  
b. reunite children victims of violence with their families and/or environment; and  
c. increase the empowerment of children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER II

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Stages

#### Article 5

The stages of protection of women victims of violence include:  
a. prevention of violence against women;  
b. protection services for women victims of violence; and  
c. empowerment of women victims of violence.

## Part Two

Implementation

### Paragraph 1

Prevention of Violence against Women

#### Article 6

1. Prevention of violence against women as referred to in Article 5 letter a is carried out in an integrated manner coordinated by the Regional Apparatus having the main duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.  
2. Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by involving:  
a. families;  
b. communities;  
c. private sector;  
d. community institutions;  
e. social institutions; and  
f. educational institutions.

#### Article 7

Prevention of violence against women as referred to in Article 6 is implemented among others by:  
a. forming a network in efforts to prevent violence against women;  
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns with communities, private sector and non-governmental organizations; and  
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of women.

### Paragraph 2

Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 8

1. Protection services for women as referred to in Article 5 letter b are provided to women residents of the Region and/or women who experience violence with the location of the incident in the territory of the Region.  
2. The implementation of protection services for women is carried out with the principles of:  
a. fast;  
b. safe;  
c. sense of empathy;  
d. non-discrimination;  
e. easily accessible; and  
f. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 9

1. Forms of protection services for women victims of violence include:  
a. legal aid services;  
b. health services;  
c. social rehabilitation services;  
d. repatriation and social reintegration services;  
e. complaint, consultation, and counseling services; and/or  
f. assistance services.  
2. Protection services for women as referred to in paragraph (1) include receiving and sending case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.  
3. The implementation of protection services for women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Apparatus having the main duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment.  
4. Further provisions regarding the forms and procedures for protection services for women victims of violence are regulated by Regent Regulation.

### Paragraph 3

Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 10

1. Empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is implemented through efforts including:  
a. job training; and  
b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups.  
2. The implementation of empowerment of women victims of violence is carried out with the principles of:  
a. equality of rights;  
b. responsibility;  
c. efficiency and effectiveness; and  
d. respect and enforcement of human rights.  
3. The implementation of empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by related Regional Apparatus in accordance with their main duties and functions.

# CHAPTER III

## PROTECTION OF CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Stages

#### Article 11

The stages of protection for children victims of violence include:  
a. prevention of violence against children;  
b. handling of children victims of violence; and  
c. rehabilitation of children victims of violence.

## Part Two

Implementation

### Paragraph 1

Prevention of Violence against Children

#### Article 12

1. Prevention of violence against children as referred to in Article 11 letter a is implemented among others by:  
a. forming a network in efforts to prevent violence against children;  
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns with communities, private sector and Non-Governmental Organizations; and  
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of children victims of violence.  
2. Prevention of violence against children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus having the main duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 13

Efforts to prevent violence against children in addition to being implemented by regional apparatus as referred to in Article 12 by involving:  
a. parents and/or families;  
b. communities;  
c. educational institutions;  
d. child social welfare institutions; and/or  
e. other institutions engaged in child protection care.

### Paragraph 2

Handling of Children Victims of Violence

#### Article 14

1. Handling of children victims of violence as referred to in Article 11 letter b is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
2. In handling children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with:  
a. vertical agencies;  
b. provincial government;  
c. other regency/city governments;  
d. village government;  
e. non-governmental organizations;  
f. private parties;  
g. communities; and/or  
h. families.

### Paragraph 3

Rehabilitation of Children Victims of Violence

#### Article 15

1. Rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 11 letter c are carried out in the form of:  
a. legal aid services;  
b. health rehabilitation services;  
c. social rehabilitation services; and/or  
d. repatriation and social reintegration services.  
2. In rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with:  
a. vertical agencies;  
b. provincial government;  
c. other regency/city governments;  
d. village government;  
e. non-governmental organizations;  
f. private parties;  
g. communities; and/or  
h. families.  
3. Further provisions regarding the forms and procedures for rehabilitation services for children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER IV

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 16

1. Every Victim obtains the following rights:  
a. the right to have their dignity respected as a human being;  
b. the right to health and psychological recovery;  
c. the right to determine their own decisions;  
d. the right to obtain information;  
e. the right to confidentiality of their identity;  
f. the right to compensation from the perpetrator;  
g. the right to social rehabilitation;  
h. the right to complaint handling;  
i. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or  
j. the right to assistance.  
2. Child victims of violence, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in paragraph (1), also obtain the following special rights:  
a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;  
b. the right to basic services;  
c. the right to equal protection;  
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and/or  
e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 17

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children, in the form of:  
a. collecting data and organizing an information system for the protection of women and child victims of violence;  
b. conducting education on anti-violence values against women and children; and  
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the empowerment of women and protection of child victims of violence.  
2. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to provide and organize services for Victims in the form of:  
a. establishing and organizing technical service institutions for Victims by involving community elements; and  
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for Victims of violence.

# CHAPTER VI

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

## Part One

General

#### Article 18

Technical service institutions for Victims are implemented by P2TP2A and FPKK.

## Part Two

### P2TP2A

#### Article 19

1. The provision of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.  
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.

#### Article 20

1. P2TP2A provides services and temporary protection in the form of a safe house.  
2. In the event that P2TP2A does not yet have a safe house, victims of violence are referred to other integrated service centers that have a safe house.

#### Article 21

1. P2TP2A consists of several counseling fields.  
2. The counseling fields as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of at least:  
a. law;  
b. health;  
c. spiritual; and  
d. psychology.  
3. The implementation of counseling is carried out by counselors and may be implemented in a networked manner by involving related parties.

#### Article 22

1. The forms of services to Victims organized by P2TP2A include:  
a. medical services;  
b. medicolegal services;  
c. psychosocial services;  
d. legal services;  
e. social reintegration services; and  
f. economic independence services.  
2. Further provisions regarding the service mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

### FPKK

#### Article 23

1. In order to protect and guarantee the rights of women and children from acts of violence, FPKK is established.  
2. FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:  
a. Regional FPKK;  
b. Sub-district FPKK; and  
c. Village FPKK.  
3. The establishment of Regional FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a is determined by the Regent.  
4. The establishment of Sub-district FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b is determined by the sub-district head.  
5. The establishment of Village FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is determined by the village head.  
6. Further provisions regarding the organizational structure and elements of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 24

The duties and functions of FPKK as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) consist of:  
a. coordinating and synchronizing prevention, services, and empowerment of victims of violence against women and children;  
b. maintaining and developing FPKK in the prevention, services, and empowerment of victims of violence in a networked manner and referral system;  
c. conducting education on non-discrimination values against women and children;  
d. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection of women and child victims of violence;  
e. collecting, compiling, and presenting reports on data of victims of violence; and  
f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of women and child victims of violence.

# CHAPTER VII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 25

1. The community may participate in the protection of Victims.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through:  
a. provision of safe houses and/or shelters;  
b. establishment and management of orphanages for children;  
c. establishment of child rehabilitation centers;  
d. active supervision of violence against women and children and reporting to the competent authorities;  
e. providing first aid to victims;  
f. reporting the occurrence of violence against women and children and providing protection;  
g. forming family partners at the village level by the community; and  
h. other forms of community participation related to the implementation of protection of women and protection of children.

#### Article 26

1. In addition to P2TP2A as referred to in Article 20, the community may form forums/groups at the sub-district and village levels that function as a network of P2TP2A in the framework of implementing efforts to protect women and child victims of violence.  
2. The formation of forums/groups in villages as referred to in paragraph (1) may be facilitated by the Village Government.

# CHAPTER VIII

## MONITORING, EVALUATION, AND REPORTING

#### Article 27

The implementation of protection of women and child victims of violence is coordinated by the Regional Apparatus that has the duties and responsibilities in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 28

1. The Regional Apparatus that has the duties and responsibilities in the field of women's empowerment and child protection is obligated to conduct monitoring, evaluation, and reporting of the results of activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence on a regular basis.  
2. The reporting of activity results as referred to in paragraph (1) is submitted to the Regent through the Regional Apparatus that has the duties and responsibilities in the field of regional development planning.  
3. The implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) involves the forum for handling victims of violence against women and children in a tiered manner.

# CHAPTER IX

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 29

1. Guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and child victims of violence is carried out by the Regional Apparatus that has the duties and responsibilities in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. coordination;  
b. guidance;  
c. education and training; and  
d. monitoring and evaluation.  
3. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a covers aspects related to planning and implementation.  
4. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b covers aspects related to planning, implementation, procedures, funding, quality, control and supervision.  
5. education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is carried out in accordance with laws and regulations.  
6. Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d is carried out regularly every 3 (three) months.

# CHAPTER X

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 30

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children are experiencing an increasing trend. Economic factors, social environment and technological developments have a major influence on the increase in such acts of violence. This condition requires the Regional Government to take concrete steps through program activities as an effort to prevent and protect women and children from acts of violence.

One of the efforts to prevent violence against women is carried out through women's empowerment and optimization of previously possessed potential, so that it is expected to be able to improve and develop themselves to play a role and be protected from potential acts of violence. This condition will strengthen the form of appreciation and respect for women while maintaining their role and nature as women to play a role in educating and supporting the welfare and happiness of their families.

Efforts to prevent violence against children are the obligation of parents, families, communities and the private sector. Community services are the front line when there is potential for violence against children. The mechanism for preventing violence and protecting child victims of violence involves multiple sectors coordinated by the Regional Government. Such efforts are carried out with the direction of providing social rehabilitation for child victims of violence, reuniting child victims of violence with their families and/or environment, and increasing the empowerment of child victims of violence.

In connection with this, to realize the provision of protection efforts for women and children victims of violence and for legal certainty in the implementation of women's empowerment and protection of child victims of violence in the Regional area, it is necessary to regulate it in a Regional Regulation.
