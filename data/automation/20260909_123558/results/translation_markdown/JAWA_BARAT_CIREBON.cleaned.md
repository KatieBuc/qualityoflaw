# PROTECTION, EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

REGIONAL GAZETTE OF CIREBON REGENCY

# REGIONAL REGULATION OF CIREBON REGENCY

NUMBER 1 OF 2018

CONCERNING

PROTECTION, EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

THE REGENT OF CIREBON,

Considering:
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
a. that children are a trust and gift from Almighty God in whom inherent dignity and worth as complete human beings, and are the next generation of the nation's ideals and struggles, therefore they need to receive protection and the widest possible opportunities for survival, growth and development in a proper manner;
b. that in connection with the many women and children who need protection from various forms of violence, maltreatment, exploitation, and neglect, it is deemed necessary to have strategic efforts from the Regional Government and other parties who are obliged to provide protection to the said women and children;
c. that discrimination and violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and crimes against humanity, therefore in order to fulfill the constitutional rights of women and children as referred to in letter b, the Regional Government may carry out integrated coordination functions for the implementation of its duties, authorities, and responsibilities;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on Protection, Empowerment of Women and Children.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

1. Region is Cirebon Regency;
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus of Cirebon Regency as elements of the administration of Cirebon Regency government;
3. Regional House of Representatives of Cirebon Regency, hereinafter referred to as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution as an element of regional government administration;
4. Regent is the Regent of Cirebon Regency;
5. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the Apparatus of Cirebon Regency;
6. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on human differences on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life, both individual and collective, in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life;
7. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom;
8. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, economic or social exploitation, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life;
9. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, economic or social exploitation, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child;
10. Domestic violence is any act against a woman and child that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically, economically and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts of coercion or unlawful deprivation of freedom within the household;
11. Trafficking in Persons is the act of recruiting, transporting, harboring, sending, transferring, or receiving a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, forgery, fraud and abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payments or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited;
12. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb;
13. Child marriage is a marriage conducted through civil, religious or customary law with or without registration or official consent where one or both partners are children under the age of 18 years;
14. Woman is a person (human) who has a vagina, can menstruate, become pregnant, give birth to children, and breastfeed;
15. Victim of violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals, including the victim's heirs;
16. Protection is a series of fulfillment of the rights of women and child victims of violence and discrimination starting from prevention, handling and empowerment efforts;
17. Prevention is a direct effort made by the Regional Government to prevent acts of violence against women and children and Indirect;
18. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to provide services to women and child victims of violence comprehensively including information services, legal assistance and advocacy, counseling services, medical services and safe houses through referral;
19. Task Force for Prevention and Handling of Trafficking in Persons Crimes, hereinafter referred to as the Task Force, is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating prevention and handling efforts of trafficking in persons crimes in Cirebon Regency;
20. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother;
21. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child;
22. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.
23. Child-Friendly Regency is a Regency that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of commitments and resources of government, community, business and media that are planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights and special protection of children.
24. Private Sector is Non-Governmental Institutions, Business World and Mass Media.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection and empowerment of women and children shall be conducted based on the principles of:
a. Humanity;
b. Justice and gender equality;
c. Shelter;
d. Best interests of the victim;
e. Survival of the mother;
f. Survival and development of the child;
g. Non-discrimination;
h. Confidentiality of the victim;
i. Anti-violence;
j. Empowerment of the victim;
k. Holistic.

#### Article 3

The objectives of protection and empowerment of women and children are to:
a. Fulfill children's rights;
b. Prevent acts of violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;
c. Eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
d. Protect and provide security for women and children;
e. Provide services to women and child victims of violence;
f. Protect children from child marriage and mediate domestic disputes to realize harmonious and prosperous household integrity.

# CHAPTER III

## CHILD-FRIENDLY REGENCY

#### Article 4

1. In the context of implementing the Convention on the Rights of the Child and various laws and regulations and policies related to the fulfillment of children's rights and special protection of children, the Regional Government, private sector and community institutions make efforts to realize a Child-Friendly Regency.
2. To realize a Child-Friendly Regency as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government, private sector and Community Institutions pay attention to children's rights related to the rights to life, growth, development, protection and participation.
3. The Regional Government, Private Sector and Community Institutions in realizing a Child-Friendly Regency are incorporated in the Child-Friendly Regency Task Force.
4. The Child-Friendly Regency Task Force is responsible for the fulfillment of children's rights related to:
a. Civil rights and freedoms;
b. Family environment and alternative care;
c. Basic health and welfare;
d. Education, leisure time utilization and cultural arts activities;
e. Special protection of children.

# CHAPTER IV

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

The rights of victims and their families to obtain Facilities in the service process consist of/include:  
a. The right to health recovery and the right to psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by victims;  
b. The right to legal assistance;  
c. The right to obtain information;  
d. The right to social recovery;  
e. The right to restitution (trafficking, rape);  
f. The right to spiritual services;  
g. The right to reintegration;  
h. The right to shelter services;  
i. The right to an advocate.

#### Article 6

Child victims of acts of violence, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 5, also obtain special rights, as follows:  
a. The right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;  
b. The right to basic services;  
c. The right to equal protection;  
d. The right to be free from various stigma; and  
e. The right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 7

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection and Empowerment for women and children are the joint responsibility of:  
a. Government/Regional Government and Government Institutions;  
b. Private Sector and Community Institutions;  
c. Society;  
d. Family and or Parents.

#### Article 8

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 7 letter a, include:  
a. Establishing policies for the protection and empowerment of women and children;  
b. Implementing policies, protection, empowerment of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;  
c. Conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children;  
d. Providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children;  
e. Allocating budget for the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children, according to regional financial capacity;  
f. Fostering and supervising the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children; and  
g. Realizing a child-friendly regency.  
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes programs and action activities for the protection and Empowerment of women and children in the Regional Action Plan as a basis for SKPD in implementing the protection and Empowerment of women and children.  
3. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD).  
4. Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 9

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Private Sector and society as referred to in Article 7 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation and jointly.  
2. The form of participation of the private sector and society as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:  
a. Preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;  
b. Providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and  
c. Participating in the handling of victims of acts of violence.  
3. The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), is carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 10

The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 7 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER VI

## ROLE OF GOVERNMENT, PRIVATE SECTOR AND COMMUNITY INSTITUTIONS

#### Article 11

1. To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government conducts empowerment and awareness to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling and realizing a Child-Friendly Regency.  
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government makes efforts as follows:  
a. Increasing the number and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;  
b. Providing accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;  
c. Providing employment for women;  
d. Building community participation and concern for the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
e. Building and providing an information system that is easily accessible;  
f. Building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children;  
g. Optimizing the integrated service center for women empowerment and child protection (P2TP2A) or by another name as a post for the protection of women and children from acts of violence; and  
h. Opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children based on the community.

#### Article 12

1. Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 11, is implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the field of:  
a. Social affairs;  
b. Health;  
c. Education;  
d. Manpower;  
e. Women empowerment and child protection;  
f. Mental and spiritual; and  
g. peace and order.  
3. Prevention of acts of violence by SKPD as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Action Plan.

# CHAPTER VII

## SERVICES FOR VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 13

1. The forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence, are as follows:  
a. Complaint services;  
b. Health services;  
c. Legal services;  
d. Repatriation;  
e. Recovery, social reintegration, and medicolegal;  
f. Identification services;  
g. Psychological services; and  
h. Shelter.  
2. The forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented according to minimum service standards established by the Government and implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the field of:  
a. Social affairs;  
b. Economy;  
c. Health;  
d. Women empowerment and child protection; and  
e. Mental and spiritual.  
3. In carrying out the duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government cooperates with Government Agencies, other Regional Governments, and the community.  
4. Further provisions regarding the procedures for handling services for women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 14

1. The Regional Government is obliged to provide complaint services for the protection and empowerment of women and children.  
2. The Regional Government and the community or social service institutions may establish recovery houses or safe houses.

#### Article 15

Further provisions regarding complaint services and social services as referred to in Article 14 shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

## INSTITUTIONAL

#### Article 16

1. In the context of providing protection services to women and children from acts of violence, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center for Women Empowerment and Child Protection (P2TP2A) or by another name as an integrated service center in the protection of women and children from acts of violence including victims of acts of violence.  
2. The Integrated Service Center for Women Empowerment and Child Protection (P2TP2A) or by another name as referred to in paragraph (1), functions as a leading sector for integrated service centers in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.  
3. Further provisions regarding the organization and working procedures of the Integrated Service Center for Women Empowerment and Child Protection (P2TP2A) or by another name as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 17

In addition to establishing the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) or other designation as referred to in Article 16, in order to support the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence, the Regent shall establish:  
a. Task force on trafficking in persons;  
b. Task force on prevention of child marriage;  
c. Regional action committee on elimination of the worst forms of child labor;  
d. Child-friendly regency task force or other designation.

#### Article 18

1. The task forces and regional action committee as referred to in Article 17 constitute a coordinative institution tasked with coordinating the prevention and handling of trafficking in persons.  
2. Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties, and functions of the task forces as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

## COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 19

1. In order to achieve the objectives of protection of women and children, cooperation may be conducted with:  
a. Central government;  
b. Provincial Government/other Provinces;  
c. Regency/City Government;  
d. Non-governmental institutions; and  
e. Community.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. Exchange of data and information;  
b. Recovery of victims of acts of violence;  
c. Repatriation and social reintegration;  
d. Provision of evidence and witnesses; and  
e. Followed up in accordance with applicable law.  
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be set forth in the form of a Joint Agreement.

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 20

1. The Regional Government shall establish partnerships with the business sector in the protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
2. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted through:  
a. Notification of employment opportunity information for women victims of acts of violence;  
b. Education and training for women victims of acts of violence;  
c. Educational assistance for women and children victims of acts of violence who are withdrawn from their education; and  
d. Fostering and enhancing economic independence of women victims of acts of violence.  
3. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be set forth in the form of an agreement.

# CHAPTER X

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 21

1. The Regional Government is obligated to conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection, empowerment of women and children.  
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:  
a. Guidelines and fulfillment standards;  
b. Technical guidance and training;  
c. Provision of facilities;  
d. Monitoring; and  
e. Evaluation.  
3. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2), is in order to realize the objectives of protection, empowerment of women and children in accordance with minimum service standards implemented by SKPD and the community.  
4. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be conducted with professional, transparent, and accountable principles.

#### Article 22

The community may conduct supervision of the implementation of protection empowerment, of women and children organized by the Regional Government through the mechanism of conveying aspirations to the Regent or to the DPRD.

# CHAPTER XI

## FINANCING

#### Article 23

Financing for the implementation of protection, empowerment of women and children is sourced from:  
a. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBN);  
b. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBD); and  
c. Other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 24

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations that implement protection, empowerment of women and children from discrimination and acts of violence.  
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be in accordance with regional financial capacity, and implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

# CHAPTER XII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 25

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Cirebon Regency.
