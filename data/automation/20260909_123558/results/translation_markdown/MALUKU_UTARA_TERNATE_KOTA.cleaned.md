# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Ternate City.
2. Regional Government is the Mayor, and Regional Apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration of Ternate City.
3. Regional Head is the Mayor of Ternate.
4. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb.
5. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, or psychological to the victim.
6. Physical violence is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or causes death.
7. Psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
8. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
9. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
10. Service is an action that must be immediately taken for the victim when seeing, hearing, and knowing that violence against the victim will occur, is occurring or has occurred.
11. Companion is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
12. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is an institution providing services to victims of violence based in a Hospital managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal) psycho-social and legal services.
13. Safe house is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.
14. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and or community organizations.
15. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
16. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife or husband, wife and their children or father and his children and mother and her children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
17. Household is family members and relatives (grandchildren, nieces/nephews, older siblings, younger siblings, grandfathers, grandmothers, cousins or other designations) and non-relatives (housemaids, drivers or other designations) who are present and reside in one house).
18. Implementation is a form of implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.
19. Protection for women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions or other parties.
20. Integrated Service Center for Women Empowerment and Child Protection hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a form of community-based women empowerment and child protection forum in an effort to fulfill information and needs in the fields of education, health, economy, politics, law, protection and prevention of acts of violence and trafficking against women and children.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The principles of providing protection to women and children victims of violence are:  
a. respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity;  
b. protection of the human rights of women and children;  
c. gender justice and equality;  
d. non-discrimination; and  
e. the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

The objective of providing protection to women and children victims of violence is to provide protection and services to women and children victims of gender-based violence and in the best interests of children occurring in households and in public.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

Every victim has the right to obtain protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage and confidential handling from individuals, groups or government institutions and the community.

#### Article 5

In the event of violence, every victim has the right to obtain protection and assistance both psychologically and legally and to obtain guarantees of their rights related to their status as a family member and as a member of society.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to implement efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence in the form of:  
a. collecting data and information about victims of violence against women and children;  
b. implementing education and guidance on anti-violence and protection for women and children victims of violence;  
c. implementing socialization of laws and regulations related to the provision of protection to women and children victims of violence;  
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the provision of protection to women and children victims of violence.  
2. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government is obligated to provide and organize protection for women and children in the form of:  
a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service centers for victims by involving community elements;  
b. encouraging public awareness about the importance of protection for victims.  
3. The Regional Government in carrying out its obligations as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

#### Article 7

Every person who sees, hears or knows of the occurrence of violence against women and children is obligated to prevent the occurrence of violence, provide protection to victims of violence and assist in the process of submitting applications for protection.

# CHAPTER V

## PROVISION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutions

#### Article 8

1. The provision of protection to victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A).  
2. P2TP2A Ternate City may receive case referrals from stakeholders within the Ternate City area.  
3. Protection of women and children victims of violence is provided to residents of Ternate City both for acts committed in Ternate City and outside Ternate City.  
4. The establishment and further provisions regarding P2TP2A shall be further regulated by Regional Head Regulations and/or Decrees.

## Part Two

Types of Services

#### Article 9

1. The forms of services to victims organized by P2TP2A include:  
a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;  
b. medicolegal services are forms of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;  
c. psychosocial services are services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation as well as providing social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in resolving their problems;  
d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process;  
e. economic independence services in the form of skills training and provision of economic access so that victims can live independently.  
2. The protection mechanism is organized based on Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) which will be further regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Three

Service Principles

#### Article 10

The provision of protection to victims is carried out free of charge, quickly, safely, with empathy, non-discrimination, easily accessible and with guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 11

P2TP2A managers are obligated to organize protection in accordance with the protection principles as referred to in Article 10.

# CHAPTER VI

## ASSISTANCE

#### Article 12

Assistance is carried out by persons or institutions that have expertise in conducting counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and cooperate with P2TP2A.

# CHAPTER VII

## SANCTION PROVISIONS

#### Article 13

1. Every person who intentionally does not provide protection to women and children resulting in the occurrence of violence, allows violence to occur, and or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as stipulated in the applicable laws and regulations.  
2. If officials appointed to organize protection do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities, they shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.  
3. P2TP2A managers who carry out service duties that violate service principles may be subject to sanctions in accordance with P2TP2A internal mechanisms.

# CHAPTER VIII

## SUPERVISION

#### Article 14

Supervision of the implementation of this Regional Regulation is carried out in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 15

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Ternate City.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF TERNATE CITY

NUMBER 3 OF 2013 CONCERNING THE PROVISION OF PROTECTION TO WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Every citizen has the right to obtain security and freedom from all forms of violence. Acts of violence constitute violations of human rights and crimes against human dignity. The State has an obligation to provide protection and security to its citizens in accordance with the Pancasila philosophy and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.  

Women and children often become victims of acts of violence both within the household and in society. The Government through Law of the Republic of Indonesia Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence and Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection has provided protection that favors women and children as groups vulnerable to violence. The provision of protection to women and children from acts of violence in Ternate City requires legal instruments to operate effectively with the fulfillment of victims' rights and involving community participation.

#### Article 2

Letter a  
Respect for the rights of victims is intended to ensure the fulfillment of victims' rights.  

Letter b  
Sufficiently clear.  

Letter c  
What is meant by gender justice is a condition where women and men have equal status and the same basic rights.  

What is meant by gender equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in public, economic, socio-cultural activities, national defense and security and equality in enjoying the results of such development.  

Letter d  
Non-discrimination is an attitude and treatment toward victims by not making distinctions based on race, ethnicity, religion and class.  

Letter e  
What is meant by the best interests of the victim is that all actions taken to protect victims are solely aimed at providing the best conditions for the victim

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 4

What is meant by obtaining protection is obtaining protection from individuals, groups or institutions, both Government and non-Government.  

What is meant by obtaining information is obtaining information about the location of complaint centers, PPT, and matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and being involved in every decision-making process related to assistance and the development of case handling.  

What is meant by integrated services is services that include medical, medico-legal, psychosocial and legal.  

What is meant by continuous handling is handling that does not only stop at physical and psychological recovery, but until the victim can resume their life in society including the restoration of their good name.
