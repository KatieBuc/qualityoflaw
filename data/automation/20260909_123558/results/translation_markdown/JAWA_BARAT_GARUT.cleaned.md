# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Garut Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Garut.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of regional government administration.
5. Regency Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and Regency DPRD in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Regency region.
6. Protection is a series of fulfillment of the rights of women and children victims of violence and discrimination starting from prevention efforts, services and social rehabilitation.
7. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
8. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
9. Protection of Children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
10. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.
11. Violence against Women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
12. Violence against Children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.
13. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
14. Domestic Violence hereinafter referred to as KDRT is any act against a person, especially women, that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically, and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts of coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty within the scope of the household.
15. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
16. Guardian is a person or body who in fact exercises parental authority as a Parent over a Child.
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
18. Community is individuals, families, groups, and/or community organizations.
19. Non-Governmental Organization, hereinafter referred to as LSM, is an organization/institution formed by citizens of the Republic of Indonesia voluntarily of their own will and interested in and engaged in the field of social welfare business established by the organization/institution as a manifestation of community participation in efforts to improve community welfare that emphasizes voluntary service.
20. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence that occurs in the Garut Regency area.
21. Prevention is an effort to develop the capacity and mechanisms of the Regional Government and community in creating conditions that can prevent the occurrence of violence, maltreatment, KDRT, exploitation and/or neglect.
22. Handling is action that includes identification, rescue, rehabilitation and reintegration of women and children who are victims of acts of violence, maltreatment, KDRT, exploitation, maltreatment or neglect.
23. Repatriation is an effort to return women and child victims of violence from abroad to the debarkation point/entry point, or from the receiving area to the area of origin.
24. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will, is or has occurred.
25. Assistance is an effort made by a person or representative of an institution that has expertise in assisting victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
26. Rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological, and social conditions in order to carry out their role again properly both in the family and in society.
27. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction in order to carry out their social functions properly.
28. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
29. Legal Aid is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocates to conduct the process of assisting witnesses and/or victims of violence against women and children that is gender sensitive.
30. Integrated Service Center for Women Empowerment and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service center in efforts to empower women in various fields of development, as well as protection of women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons, established by the Government or community-based, which includes: referral centers, business consultation centers, reproductive health consultation centers, legal consultation centers, Integrated Crisis Centers (PKT), Integrated Service Centers (PPT), trauma recovery centers (trauma centers), women's crisis handling centers (women crisis centers), training centers, Science and Technology Information Centers (PIPTEK), safe houses (shelters), transit houses, or other forms.
31. Women and Children Protection Task Force is a functional Work Unit that organizes protection services and handling of cases of violence against children and women in an integrated manner at the Sub-district level.
32. Women and Children Protection Task Force is a functional Work Unit that organizes protection services and handling of cases of violence against children and women in an integrated manner at the Village/Sub-district level.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES, PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of women and children from violence is based on the principles of:
a. humanity;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. protection;
d. best interests of women and children; and
e. non-discrimination.

#### Article 3

The purpose of regulating the protection of women and children from acts of violence is to provide guidance and direction for the Regional Government and community in conducting prevention, services and social rehabilitation of women and children victims of acts of violence.

#### Article 4

The objectives of protecting women and children from violence are:  
a. to prevent violence against women and children;  
b. to eliminate all forms of violence against women and children;  
c. to protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;  
d. to provide services to women and children victims of violence;  
e. to carry out rehabilitation and reintegration of women and children victims of violence; and  
f. to empower women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 5

The scope of protection of women and children from violence, is as follows:  
a. forms of violence against women and children;  
b. rights of victims;  
c. obligations and responsibilities;  
d. implementation of protection of women and children from violence;  
e. institutions;  
f. cooperation and partnerships;  
g. monitoring and evaluation;  
h. reporting;  
i. funding; and  
j. Sanctions.

# CHAPTER IV

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 6

Forms of violence against women and children, include:  
a. physical violence;  
b. psychological violence;  
c. sexual violence;  
d. neglect;  
e. exploitation; and/orf. other violence.

#### Article 7

1. Physical violence as referred to in Article 6 letter a constitutes an act that results in pain accompanied by injury, wounds or disability to the body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death of women and/or children.  
2. Psychological violence as referred to in Article 6 letter b constitutes an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, sense of helplessness and/or psychological suffering to women and/or children.  
3. Sexual violence as referred to in Article 6 letter c, includes:  
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment, consisting of:  
1\. physical harassment;  
2\. verbal harassment;  
3\. gestural harassment;  
4\. written or pictorial harassment; and  
5\. psychological/emotional harassment.  
b. forced sexual intercourse;  
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/ord. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  
4. Neglect as referred to in Article 6 letter d, includes:  
a. acts that result in the non-fulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care; and/or  
c. acts of deliberately neglecting women within their household sphere, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to such women.  
5. Exploitation as referred to in Article 6 letter e, includes:  
a. acts of economic or sexual exploitation with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  
b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and  
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, but not limited to prostitution or indecency activities.  
6. Other violence as referred to in Article 6 letter f constitutes threats of violence and coercion.  
7. Threats of violence as referred to in paragraph (6) include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom.  
8. Coercion as referred to in paragraph (6) includes a situation where a person/victim is made to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER V

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 8

Women and children victims of violence receive the following rights:  
a. the right to be respected for their dignity as human beings;  
b. the right to recovery of health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by victims;  
c. the right to determine their own decisions;  
d. the right to obtain information;  
e. the right to confidentiality;  
f. the right to compensation;  
g. the right to social rehabilitation;  
h. the right to complaint handling;  
i. the right to assistance;  
j. the right to obtain visum et repertum free of charge; and  
k. the right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process.

#### Article 9

Child victims of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 8, also receive special rights, as follows:  
a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;  
b. the right to basic services;  
c. the right to equal protection;  
d. the right to be free from various stigma;  
e. the right to obtain freedom; and  
f. the right to be tried based on the principle of restorative justice.

# CHAPTER VI

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

General

#### Article 10

Obligations and responsibilities in the implementation of protection of women and children from violence are the shared responsibility of:  
a. Regional Government;  
b. Village Government;  
c. Community; and  
d. Parents, Guardians and/or Family.

## Part Two

Regional Government

#### Article 11

1. The Regional Government as referred to in Article 10 letter a, is obliged and responsible to:  
a. formulate and implement programs and activities related to the protection of women and children;  
b. implement protection of women and children from violence;  
c. supervise services to victims of violence based on minimum service standards in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations; and  
d. allocate budget for protection of women and children from violence according to regional financial capacity.  
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government formulates programs and activities for the protection of women and children in a regional action plan which is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan

##### (RPJMD).  

## Part Three

Village Government

#### Article 12

1. The Village Government as referred to in Article 10 letter b, is obliged and responsible to:  
a. formulate and implement programs and activities related to the protection of women and children;  
b. implement protection of women and children from violence;  
c. supervise services to victims of violence based on minimum service standards in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations; and  
d. allocate budget for protection of women and children from violence according to village financial capacity.  
2. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Village Government formulates programs and activities for the protection of women and children in a Village action plan which is part of the Village Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMDes).

## Part Four

Community

#### Article 13

1. The community as referred to in Article 10 letter c has the obligation and responsibility for the protection of women and children through community participation activities in the protection of women and children.
2. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or the competent authorities; and
c. participating in the handling of victims of acts of violence.

## Part Five

Parents, Guardians and/or Family

#### Article 14

1. Parents, guardians and/or family as referred to in Article 10 letter d legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence, report when violence occurs and protect victims.
2. In the event that parents, guardians and/or family as referred to in paragraph (1) do not exist or their whereabouts are unknown or due to a certain reason cannot carry out their obligations and responsibilities, then the obligations and responsibilities may be transferred to the Regional Government and other family members implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 15

The Regional Government carries out activities for the implementation of protection of women and children from violence, which include:
a. prevention;
b. services;
c. social rehabilitation; and
d. empowerment.

## Part Two

Prevention

#### Article 16

1. Prevention as referred to in Article 15 letter a, is implemented by:
a. forming a network in efforts to prevent violence;
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
c. forming a violence prevention system;
d. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence; and
e. providing knowledge about prevention and mechanisms for overcoming violence against women and children.
2. Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in an integrated manner by Regency Regional Apparatus and related agencies that have principal tasks and functions in the fields of:
a. women's empowerment and child protection;
b. education;
c. health;
d. social affairs;
e. manpower;
f. youth and sports; and
g. mental and spiritual.
3. In addition to being implemented by Regency Regional Apparatus and related agencies as referred to in paragraph (2), prevention is also implemented by:
a. family and/or closest relatives;
b. community, NGOs, community organizations, social organizations and religious organizations; and
c. business sector.

## Part Three

Services

#### Article 17

Services as referred to in Article 15 letter b, take into account:
a. service principles; and
b. forms of services to victims.

#### Article 18

Service principles as referred to in Article 17 letter a, consist of:
a. fast;
b. safe and comfortable;
c. sense of empathy;
d. non-discrimination;
e. easily accessible;
f. not charged; and
g. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 19

Forms of services to victims as referred to in Article 17 letter b, consist of:
a. services to women and children victims of acts of violence, include:
1. complaint, consultation and counseling services;
2. assistance services;
3. health services;
4. legal aid services; and
5. repatriation and social reintegration services.

b. special services to women victims of acts of domestic violence or trafficking in persons, include:
1. complaint services;
2. pick-up services;
3. medical and psychological referral services;
4. shelter services;
5. assistance services and provision of legal aid; and
6. repatriation and social reintegration services.

#### Article 20

Complaint, consultation and counseling services as referred to in Article 18 letter a number 1, include:
a. initial identification or recording of victims; and
b. informed consent.

#### Article 21

Assistance services as referred to in Article 19 letter a number 2 include:
a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;
b. accompanying victims during the psychological recovery process;
c. accompanying victims during the medicolegal process;
d. accompanying victims during the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;
e. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;
f. maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including coverage by mass media;
g. coordinating with other assistants; and
h. providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 22

Health services as referred to in Article 19 letter a number 3 include:
a. first aid to victims by competent officers;
b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel; and
c. referral to health services according to needs.

#### Article 23

1. Legal aid services as referred to in Article 19 letter a number 4 are to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process.
2. Legal aid services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by:
a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;
b. accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and helping victims to fully explain the violence they experienced; and
c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer assistants, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 24

1. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 19 letter a number 5 are carried out to return victims to their families and social environment.
2. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with:
a. Regency/City Governments within one Provincial area or outside the Province; and/or
b. related agencies and institutions both Government and non-Government.

#### Article 25

1. Forms of services as referred to in Article 19, are implemented in accordance with minimum service standards established by the Government and implemented by Regency Regional Apparatus that have tasks and functions in the fields of:
a. women's empowerment and child protection;
b. social affairs;
c. manpower;
d. health;
e. education;
f. security and order; and
g. mental and spiritual.
2. In carrying out tasks and functions as referred to in paragraph (1) the Regional Government cooperates with the Government, Provincial Government, other Regency/City Governments, and the community.

## Part Four

Social Rehabilitation

#### Article 26

1. Social rehabilitation as referred to in Article 15 letter c constitutes services provided by companions to restore the traumatic condition of victims.
2. Social rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by:
a. providing guidance and counseling to victims and parents of victims;
b. psychological recovery of victims;
c. accompanying victims and parents of victims in the family and community environment; and
d. providing safe houses for victims of violence who require continuous handling.
3. Special social rehabilitation for women victims of domestic violence and trafficking in persons is carried out by:
a. providing guidance and counseling;
b. psychological recovery of victims; and
c. accompanying victims in the family and community environment.
4. Social rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by the Regency Regional Apparatus having the main duties and functions in the fields of:
a. social affairs;
b. women's empowerment and child protection;
c. health; and
d. mental and spiritual.
5. In addition to being implemented by the Regency Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (4), social rehabilitation may also be implemented by the community or social service institutions.

## Part Five

Empowerment

#### Article 27

Women's empowerment as referred to in Article 15 letter d, includes:
a. improving resilience;
b. improving economic enterprises; and
c. increasing women's participation as builders, mediators and peace negotiators.

#### Article 28

Improving resilience as referred to in Article 27 letter a is carried out by providing guidance and assistance for mental spiritual strengthening.

#### Article 29

Improving economic enterprises as referred to in Article 27 letter b by providing:
a. job skills training;
b. productive economic enterprise training;
c. assistance in developing productive economic enterprises; and
d. ease of access to information and marketing promotion of productive economic enterprise products.

#### Article 30

Increasing women's participation as builders, mediators and peace negotiators as referred to in Article 27 letter c is carried out by involving women:
a. in training as builders, mediators and peace negotiators; and
b. as initiators, mediators and negotiators in the process of building peace.

# CHAPTER VIII

## INSTITUTIONS

## Part One

General

#### Article 31

To assist the protection of women and children, the Regional Government may establish:
a. P2TP2A at the Regional level;
b. Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children at the Sub-District Level;
c. Unit for the Protection of Women and Children at the Village/Sub-District Level; and
d. Other institutions or organizations in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two

### P2TP2A

#### Article 32

1. P2TP2A as referred to in Article 31 letter a, constitutes a forum for women and children protection services based on the community that functions as an integrated service center in providing protection to women and children from violence.
2. The membership composition of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of, among others:
a. elements of the Regional Government;
b. academics;
c. legal experts;
d. psychologists;
e. psychiatrists;
f. religious leaders; and
g. community elements.
3. The main duties of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) are:
a. providing protection to women and children; and
b. improving the quality of life of women and children.
4. Further provisions regarding the establishment of P2TP2A are stipulated by Regent's Decree.

## Part Three

Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children at the Sub-District Level

#### Article 33

1. The Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children at the Sub-District Level as referred to in Article 31 letter b, constitutes a functional work unit that organizes integrated protection services and handling of cases of violence against children and women at the Sub-District level.
2. The Management of the Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children at the Sub-District Level as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
a. elements of Sub-District apparatus;
b. agencies/institutions at the Sub-District level;
c. elements of the Sector Police (Polsek);
d. elements of the Military District Command (Koramil);
e. community leaders/religious leaders;
f. youth elements; and
g. elements of the Sub-District PKK Driving Team.
3. The main duties of the Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children at the Sub-District Level as referred to in paragraph (1) are:
a. providing services in the context of protecting women and children;
b. receiving public complaints regarding cases of violence against women and children in the Sub-District and Sub-District/Village areas;
c. facilitating women and children who are victims of violence in resolving their problems;
d. socializing to the community about the protection of women and children; and
e. providing assistance to victims or referring to P2TP2A, Sector Police (Polsek)/Resort Police (Polres), hospitals or other institutions as required.
4. Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties, and functions of the Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children at the Sub-District Level as referred to in paragraph (1), are stipulated by Sub-District Head's Decree.

## Part Four

Unit for the Protection of Women and Children at the Village/Sub-District Level

#### Article 34

1. The Unit for the Protection of Women and Children at the Village/Sub-District Level as referred to in Article 31 letter c, constitutes a functional work unit that organizes integrated protection services and handling of cases of violence against children and women at the Village/Sub-District level.
2. The Management of the Unit for the Protection of Women and Children at the Village/Sub-District Level as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
a. elements of Village/Sub-District apparatus;
b. elements of Village Guidance Non-Commissioned Officer (Babinsa)/Sub-District;
c. elements of Community Guidance Non-Commissioned Officer (Babinmas);
d. community leaders/religious leaders;
e. youth elements; and
f. elements of the Village/Sub-District PKK Driving Team
3. The main duties of the Unit for the Protection of Women and Children at the Village/Sub-District Level as referred to in paragraph (1) are:
a. providing services in the context of protecting women and children;
b. receiving public complaints regarding cases of violence against women and children in the Village/Sub-District area;
c. facilitating women and children who are victims of violence in resolving their problems;
d. socializing to the community about the protection of women and children; and
e. providing assistance to victims or referring to the task force for the protection of women and children at the Sub-District level.
4. Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties and functions of the Unit for the Protection of Women and Children at the Village/Sub-District Level as referred to in paragraph (1), are stipulated by Village Head/Sub-District Head's Decree.

# CHAPTER IX

## COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 35

1. In order to achieve the objectives of protecting women and children as referred to in Article 4, the Regional Government may cooperate with:
a. the Government;
b. other Regional Governments; and/or
c. non-governmental institutions.
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a. exchange of data and information;
b. rehabilitation of victims of acts of violence;
c. repatriation and social reintegration;
d. provision of evidence and witnesses; and
e. empowerment.
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) is set forth in the form of a Joint Agreement and/or Cooperation Agreement in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 36

1. The Regional Government establishes partnerships with the business sector, NGOs/social organizations concerned with women and children in the protection of women and children against acts of violence.  
2. Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out through:  
a. notification of employment opportunity information for women victims of violence;  
b. education and training for women and children victims of violence;  
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of violence who have been withdrawn from their education; and  
d. fostering and enhancing the economic independence of women victims of violence.  
3. Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be set forth in the form of an agreement in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## MONITORING, EVALUATION, AND REPORTING

#### Article 37

Monitoring is conducted periodically and in an integrated manner every 6 (six) months by the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection in coordination with Regional Apparatus or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 38

Evaluation of the implementation of the women's empowerment and child protection system is conducted by the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection at least 2 (two) times in 1 (one) year.

#### Article 39

1. Reporting on the implementation of protection for women and children from violence is carried out by:  
a. P2TP2A to the Regent;  
b. Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children at the Sub-District Level to the Sub-District Head; and  
c. Task Unit for the Protection of Women and Children at the Village/Sub-District Level to the Village Head/Sub-District Head.  
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be submitted in writing, covering:  
a. administration;  
b. finance;  
c. services; and  
d. performance.  
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out at least once every 3 (three) months.

# CHAPTER XI

## FUNDING

#### Article 40

Funding for the protection of women and children is sourced from:  
a. APBD;  
b. APBDes; and  
c. other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XII

## SANCTIONS

#### Article 41

1. Any person who intentionally fails to provide protection for women and children resulting in the occurrence of violence, allows violence to occur, and/or does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as stipulated in the applicable laws and regulations.  
2. Regency Regional Apparatus that has the duties and functions of administering protection, failing to carry out their obligations and responsibilities shall be subject to administrative sanctions.  
3. Management of P2TP2A, Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children at the Sub-District Level, Task Unit for the Protection of Women and Children at the Village/Sub-District Level and other institutions or organizations in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations that carry out protection duties, if they violate protection principles, may be subject to sanctions in accordance with the mechanisms applicable within the internal institutions or respective organizations.

# CHAPTER XIII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 42

Matters not sufficiently regulated in this Regional Regulation, insofar as they concern technical implementation, shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 43

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Garut Regency.
