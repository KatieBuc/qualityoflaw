CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF TAMBRAUW,

Considering:

a. that acts and treatment of violence against women and children constitute actions that violate the rights and dignity of women and children as human beings;
b. that the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children as victims of acts of violence in Tambrauw Regency has not been carried out optimally;
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a and letter b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children From Acts of Violence;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:
1. Region is Tambrauw Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regional Head as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Tambrauw.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of the Regional Government administration.
5. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment and exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on human differentiation on the grounds of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life, whether individual or collective, in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
6. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering, whether physical, sexual, psychological, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
7. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether in the domestic sphere or public in private life.
8. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.
9. Domestic violence is any act against a person, especially women, that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically and or domestic neglect, including threats to commit acts of coercion or deprivation of liberty unlawfully within the household sphere.
10. Trafficking in Persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud and abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether conducted within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
11. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age including a child in the womb.
12. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person recognized by law as a woman.
13. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
14. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.
15. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or fainting and or causes death.
16. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, distrust or severe psychological suffering to a person.
17. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
18. Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence.
19. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
20. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.
21. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.
22. Institution is an office/agency/body/within the scope of regional government and/or Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) that provides assistance.
23. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an institution providing services to victims of violence based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psychosocial and legal services.
24. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
25. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
26. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is an integrated service forum established by the Regional Government in the context of protection services for victims of violence against women and children in Tambrauw Regency.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

The implementation and protection of women and children victims of acts of violence is carried out based on the principles of:
a.	Humanity;
b.	Respect for the rights of victims;
c.	Justice and gender equality;
d.	Protection;
e.	Non-discrimination; and
f.	Best interests of the victim.

Article 3

The objectives of protection of women and children victims of acts of violence are to:
a.	Prevent acts of violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;
b.	Eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
c.	Protect, provide a sense of security for women and children victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses;
d.	Provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence;
e.	Facilitate and mediate cases of domestic violence to realize harmonious and prosperous household integrity; and
f.	Empowerment.

CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 4

Women and children victims of acts of violence receive the following rights:
a.	The right to be respected for their dignity as human beings;
b.	The right to health and psychological services from the suffering experienced by victims;
c.	The right to legal assistance;
d.	The right to obtain information;
e.	The right to confidential handling;
f.	The right to social rehabilitation;
g.	The right to complaint handling;
h.	The right of victims and their families to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and
i.	Guarantee of rights related to status as family members and community.

Article 5

Child victims of acts of violence in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 4, also receive the following special rights:
a.	The right to survival, growth and development;
b.	The right to basic service needs;
c.	The right to equal protection; and
d.	The right to obtain freedom;

CHAPTER IV

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 6

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence are the shared responsibility of:
a.	regional government;
b.	community;
c.	family; and
d.	parents.

Article 7

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 6 letter a, include:
a.	establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
b.	conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
c.	providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
d.	allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity;
2. The duties of the Regional Government in the protection of women and children from acts of violence in the form of:
a.	Implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;
b.	Collecting data and information about women and children in the context of efforts to protect them from acts of violence;
c.	Conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
d.	Conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.

Article 8

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation.
2. The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:
a.	preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b.	providing protection to women and children from acts of violence, in the form of establishing rules of order in their respective areas starting from the Neighborhood Association level;
c.	providing information and or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties;
d.	participating in the handling of victims of acts of violence; and
e.	accompanying victims to service units.
3. The form of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out responsibly in accordance with laws and regulations.

Article 9

The obligations of the family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

CHAPTER V

PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 10

1. To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government conducts socialization, empowerment, and awareness to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and / or counseling.
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government makes efforts as follows:
a.	increasing the number and quality of education both formal and non-formal as well as informal;
b.	opening employment opportunities for women;
c.	building community participation and concern for the prevention of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and.
d.	building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children.

Article 11

1. The prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 10, is implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the field of:
a.	social affairs;
b.	health;
c.	education;
d.	manpower;
e.	women's empowerment and child protection;
f.	mental and spiritual; and
g.	peace and order.
2. The prevention of acts of violence by SKPD as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner.

CHAPTER VI

SERVICES FOR VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 12

1. The forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence, are as follows:
a.	complaint services;
b.	Medical services; and
c.	medicolegal services for the purposes of evidence in the field of law.
2. Legal aid services:
a.	independence or empowerment services; and
b.	social rehabilitation services.
3. 	The forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented by related SKPD in accordance with the programs, activities, and budgets available in each in the field of:
a.	social affairs;
b.	health;
c.	women's empowerment and child protection;
d.	mental and spiritual;
e.	manpower; and
f.	education.

CHAPTER VII

INSTITUTIONS

Article  13

1. In the context of providing protection services to women and children from victims of violence, the Regional Government establishes P2TP2A as an integrated service center in the protection of women and children from acts of violence including victims of acts of violence.
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) functions as an integrated service center in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.
3. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) above consists of the Health Office, Hospital, Police, Social Service, Manpower Office, Women's Empowerment and Family Planning Office, Non-Governmental Organizations.
4.	P2TP2A as referred to Social Community Organizations, Universities in paragraph (3) is established by Regent's decree.
5.	P2TP2A of Tambrauw Regency in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with the network of service units and competent parties in making efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence.

Article 14

1. For the implementation of services for victims of trafficking in persons, the Regent of Tambrauw establishes a task force on the crime of trafficking in persons.
2. The task force on the crime of trafficking in persons as referred to in paragraph (1) is a coordinative institution tasked with coordinating the prevention and handling of trafficking in persons.
3. Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties, and functions of the task force on the crime of trafficking in persons as referred to in paragraph (1) are established by Regent's decree.

Article 15

1. In the context of preventing acts of violence against children including the protection of children from the worst forms of child labor, a committee for the elimination of acts of violence against children will be established by Regent's Decree.
2. Further provisions regarding the duties and functions of the committee for the elimination of acts of violence against children as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent's Regulation.

CHAPTER VIII

REPORTING

Article 16

1. P2TP2A of Tambrauw Regency is obliged to report the implementation of the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence to the Regent.
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a.	Administration;
b.	Finance;
c.	Services; and
d.	Performance.
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out at least once every 3 (three) months.

CHAPTER IX

FINANCING

Article 17

Financing for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence is sourced from:
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBD) of Tambrauw Regency, and
b. Other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Article 18

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations that implement the protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination.
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), is in accordance with regional financial capacity, and is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

CHAPTER X

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 19

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by its placement in the Regional Gazette of Tambrauw Regency.

I. GENERAL

The State has the obligation to provide Protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. Women and Children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience violence and therefore need to receive protection. Violence against Women and Children constitutes a violation of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed without discrimination.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against the physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, Violence against Women is any act based on gender difference that results in or may result in suffering or hardship to Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the Child. The existence of Women and Children Victims of Violence has not yet received adequate services so that minimum services are required for Victims of Violence.

In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in Badung Regency so that they are protected from Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children Victims of Violence particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of Women and Children Victims of Violence in Badung Regency.

What is meant by "justice and gender equality" is a process to be fair to men and women and equal conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so as to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, defense and National security activities and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

Letter d
Sufficiently Clear.

Letter e
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment toward victims by not making distinctions on the basis of age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter f
What is meant by "the best interests of the victim" is all the best actions concerning victims carried out by the Regional Government, society, the legislative body and the judicial body, so that the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.
