PROTECTION OF WOMEN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

REGENT OF SANGGAU

PROVINCE OF WEST KALIMANTAN

REGIONAL REGULATION OF SANGGAU REGENCY NUMBER 12 OF 2019

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF SANGGAU,

Considering:

a. that every citizen has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and has the right to obtain security from all forms of violence;
b. that all forms of acts of violence against women constitute violations of human rights, so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination;
c. that violence against women in Sanggau Regency shows an increasing tendency so that prevention efforts and optimization of handling acts of violence against women must be carried out, thus requiring a Regional Regulation that guarantees legal protection for women;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women from Acts of Violence;

In view of:

1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 27 of 1959 concerning Stipulation of Emergency Law Number 3 of 1953 concerning Formation of Level II Regions in Kalimantan (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1959 Number 9) as Law (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1959 Number 72, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 1820);
3. Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419);
4. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as has been amended several times, most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);
5. Law Number 21 of 2017 concerning Eradication of the Criminal Act of Trafficking in Persons (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2007 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4720);
6. Government Regulation Number 4 of 2006 concerning Implementation and Cooperation for Recovery of Victims of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2006 Number 15, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4604);
7. Presidential Regulation Number 18 of 2014 concerning Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children in Social Conflict (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 44);
8. Regulation of the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection Number 01 of 2010 concerning Minimum Service Standards in the Field of Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence (State News of the Republic of Indonesia of 2010 Number 56);

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Regent is the Regent of Sanggau.
2. Region is Sanggau Regency.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social and psychological to the Victim.
6. Violence Against Women is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering to women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.
7. Victim is a woman who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of Violence and life vulnerability.
8. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the Regional Government, community, and/or other parties who know or hear that Violence Against Women will or has occurred.
9. Service/Handling is action taken as soon as possible to the Victim when seeing, hearing and/or knowing that Violence against the Victim will, is or has occurred.
10. Assistance is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance to Victims to conduct counseling, therapy and/or advocacy.
11. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an institution or functional work unit that organizes integrated services for Victims of Violence.
12. Safe House is a residence for Victims during the Assistance process for the safety and comfort of Victims from threats and dangers.
13. Complaint Handling is a series of actions carried out by integrated service providers to follow up on reports of acts of Violence Against Women submitted by Victims, Families and the community.
14. Health Services are efforts that include promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative aspects.
15. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.
16. Legal Aid is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocacy to carry out the process of Assisting witnesses and/or Victims of Violence Against Women that is gender sensitive.
17. Law Enforcement is the action of officers authorized by the state to implement laws and regulations.
18. Repatriation is an effort to return women Victims of Violence from abroad to the debarkation/entry point, or from the receiving area to the area of origin.
19. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with the Family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment for Victims.
20. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward.

CHAPTER II

SCOPE OF PROTECTION

Article 2

The scope of Protection of Women from acts of Violence includes:
a. prevention;
b. Service/Handling; and
c. empowerment.

Part One

Prevention

Article 3

1. Efforts to prevent Violence Against Women are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:
a. forming a working network;
b. conducting coordination, integration, and synchronization of Violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
c. forming a Violence prevention system;
d. disseminating laws and regulations related to the Protection of Women Victims of Violence; and
e. providing information regarding Protection of Women from acts of Violence through communication, information and education (KIE) media.

Article 4

In addition to prevention efforts carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 3, prevention efforts are also carried out by the Family and/or closest relatives, community, educational institutions and customary institutions.

Part Two

Service/Handling

Article 5

1. The implementation of Service/Handling for Victims is carried out in an integrated manner by PPT.
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from and/or to other service units in a networked manner.

Article 6

Forms of Service/Handling for Victims include:
a. Complaint Handling;
b. health/medical rehabilitation services;
c. Social Rehabilitation services;
d. Law Enforcement and Legal Aid services; and
e. Repatriation and Social Reintegration services.

Article 7

The implementation of Service/Handling for Victims is carried out with the principles of:
a. fast and precise;
b. safe and comfortable;
c. sense of empathy;
d. non-discrimination;
e. easily accessible;
f. free of charge; and
g. guaranteed confidentiality.

Part Three

Empowerment

Article 8

Forms of empowerment for women Victims of Violence include:
a. forming communities of women Victims of Violence;
b. job training;
c. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
d. capital assistance.

Article 9

Job training as referred to in Article 8 letter b includes:
a. skills training;
b. field work practice; and
c. apprenticeship.

Article 10

Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups as referred to in Article 8 letter c include:
a. entrepreneurial skills training; and
b. Business implementation assistance.

Article 11

Capital assistance as referred to in Article 8 letter d includes:
a. facilities and infrastructure assistance; and
b. working/business capital assistance facilities.

CHAPTER III

VIOLENCE

Article 12

Violence Against Women consists of:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. exploitation; and/or
f. other violence.

Article 13

Physical violence as referred to in Article 12 letter a includes acts that result in pain, wounds, injury or disability to the body, miscarriage, fainting, and/or death.

Article 14

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 12 letter b includes acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, sense of helplessness, and/or severe psychological suffering.

Article 15

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 12 letter c includes:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. coercion of unnatural or unwanted sexual relations; and/or
c. coercion of sexual relations with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Article 16

Neglect as referred to in Article 12 letter d includes:
a. acts that neglect women within the scope of their household; and/or
b. acts that result in economic dependence.

Article 17

Exploitation as referred to in Article 12 letter e includes:
a. actions that exploit women economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. actions with or without the consent of the Victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced work or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
c. any form of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the Victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

Article 18

Other violence as referred to in Article 12 letter f includes:
a. any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and/or
b. a situation where a person/Victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

CHAPTER IV

RIGHTS OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 19

Every woman Victim of Violence has the right to:
a. have her dignity respected as a human being;
b. obtain physical and psychological health recovery from the suffering experienced;
c. determine her own decisions;
d. obtain information;
e. have her identity kept confidential;
f. obtain compensation;
g. obtain Social Rehabilitation;
h. obtain Complaint Handling;
i. obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and
j. obtain Assistance.

CHAPTER V

GOVERNMENT RESPONSIBILITY AND INSTITUTIONS

Part One

Regional Government

Article 20

1. The Regional Government is responsible for implementing efforts to Protect Women from acts of Violence in the form of:
a. establishing PPT;
b. policy formulation;
c. preparing plans and implementing programs and activities;
d. providing facilities and infrastructure support; and
e. supervising the implementation of services for Victims with minimum service standards.
2. In carrying out the responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government prepares a Regional Action Plan for the Protection of Women.
3. Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 21

The responsibility of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) is coordinated by the Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment.

Part Two

Community and Family

Article 22

The Community and Family have the obligation and responsibility to:
a. prevent the occurrence of Violence Against Women;
b. report if Violence occurs;
c. protect Victims; and
d. provide emergency assistance.

Part Three

Coordination of Victim Protection

Article 23

1. Efforts to provide and implement Service/Handling for Victims are carried out in a networked manner.
2. The network as referred to in paragraph (1) aims to:
a. coordinate and synchronize PPT service handling;
b. develop networks and referral systems; and
c. collect, compile and present reports on Violence.
3. The network as referred to in paragraph (2) carries out its duties and functions in accordance with the forms of service as referred to in Article 6.

CHAPTER VI

Article 24

1. PPT may be established by the Regional Government, vertical agencies in the Region, and/or community institutions.
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of elements of the Regional Police, Regional Apparatus in charge of Health, Education, Social Affairs, Community Empowerment, Women's Empowerment and Child Protection as well as Community Social Institutions, which function to provide protection to women from acts of Violence carried out in a networked manner.
3. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is tasked with providing services and temporary protection in the form of a Safe House (shelter) for Victims.
4. In the event that PPT does not have a Safe House as referred to in paragraph (3), then the Victim is referred to a PPT that has a Safe House.
5. Provisions regarding the establishment, composition of the management team and organizational structure of PPT established by the Regional Government are stipulated by Regent Decree.

CHAPTER VII

INFORMATION AND REPORTING SYSTEM

Article 25

The Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment implements an information system and submits reports on the implementation of Women's Protection to the Regent in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VIII

FUNDING

Article 26

Funding for Women's Protection activities carried out by the Regional Government is sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget and/or other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER IX

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 27

1. The community participates in the implementation of Women's Protection.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of:
a. establishing family partners at the sub-district/village level by the community;
b. establishing women's protection units in community organizations;
c. conducting independent socialization of women's rights;
d. undertaking preventive measures if indications of Violence Against Women are found in the community;
e. providing first aid to Victims; and
f. reporting to the competent authorities if Violence Against Women occurs in their environment.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and/or mass media.

CHAPTER X

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 28

1. The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of Women's Protection.
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall include:
a. coordination;
b. guidance;
c. education and training; and
d. monitoring and evaluation.
3. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a encompasses planning and implementation.
4. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b encompasses planning, implementation, procedures, funding, quality, control, and supervision.
5. Education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
6. Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d shall be conducted based on laws and regulations periodically every 6 (six) months.

CHAPTER XI

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 29

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by its placement in the Regional Gazette of Sanggau Regency.

I. GENERAL

Acts of Violence Against Women constitute a violation of human rights, therefore it is necessary to protect their dignity and honor and to guarantee their right to life in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome Violence Against Women in Sanggau Regency so as to avoid Violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women.

Women's Protection constitutes all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by giving consistent and systematic attention.

Existing laws and regulations or those related to the Protection of Women Victims of Violence have not yet regulated efforts for Women's Protection in the Region, therefore institutional and regulatory support at the Regional level is needed to ensure its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates efforts for Women's Protection, particularly in the form of prevention, Service/Handling, and empowerment of women in Sanggau Regency.

Number 14
What is meant by efforts from the promotive aspect is the effort to promote/socialize acts of Violence to the community, with the aim of increasing community vigilance and sensitivity to the danger of Violence that threatens women.

What is meant by efforts from the preventive aspect is efforts that prioritize the prevention aspect targeting communities vulnerable to becoming/being Victims of Violence and receiving its impact. Preventive efforts can be carried out through community empowerment in the form of training activities and others.

What is meant by efforts from the curative aspect is efforts carried out in the form of handling Victims of Violence who need handling according to their needs, namely health rehabilitation handling if the Victim suffers the impact of Violence physically or psychologically, provision of Assistance and Law Enforcement for Victims who require Assistance and Law Enforcement, and so on.

What is meant by the rehabilitative aspect is efforts carried out as an effort to recover Victims of Violence both physically, psychologically and psychosocially.
