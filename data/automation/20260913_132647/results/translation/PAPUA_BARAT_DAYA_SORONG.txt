NUMBER 2 OF 2015

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF SORONG,

Considering:

a. that violence against women and children is an act that violates the rights and dignity of women and children as human beings;
b. that the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children as victims of violence in Sorong Regency has not been carried out optimally;
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a and letter b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Sorong Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus Units as elements of Regional Government Administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Sorong.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional People's Representative Institution which is positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.
5. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment and exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on human differentiation on the grounds of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs which result in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life, both individual and collective, in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
6. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering, whether physical, sexual, psychological, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.
7. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering to women physically, sexually or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether in the domestic or public sphere in private life.
8. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.
9. Domestic violence is any act against a person, especially women, that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically and or domestic neglect, including threats to commit acts of coercion or deprivation of freedom unlawfully within the scope of the household.
10. Trafficking in Persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by means of threat of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud and abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether conducted within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
11. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age including a child in the womb.
12. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person recognized by law as a woman.
13. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
14. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.
15. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or fainting and or causes death.
16. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, distrust or severe psychological suffering to a person.
17. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
18. Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence. 19. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
20. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.
21. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.
22. Institution is an agency/institution/body/within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organization (NGO) that provides assistance. 23. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an institution providing services to victims of violence based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psychosocial and legal services.
24. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
25. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

The implementation and protection of women and children from victims of violence is carried out based on the principles of:
a. humanity;
b. respect for the rights of victims;
c. justice and gender equality;
d. protection;
e. non-discrimination; and
f. best interests of victims.

Article 3

The objectives of protecting women and children from victims of violence are to:
a. prevent acts of violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;
b. eliminate all forms of violence and discrimination against women and children;
c. provide a sense of security for women and children who are victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses;
d. provide services to women and children who are victims of violence;
e. facilitate and mediate cases of domestic violence to realize a harmonious and prosperous household integrity; and
f. empowerment.

CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 4

Women and children who are victims of violence receive the following rights:
a. the right to be respected for their dignity as human beings;
b. the right to health and psychological services from the suffering experienced by victims;
c. the right to legal assistance;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidential handling;
f. the right to social rehabilitation;
g. the right to complaint handling;
h. the right of victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and
i. guarantees of rights related to status as family members and society.

Article 5

Child victims of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in article 4, also receive the following special rights:
a. the right to survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic service needs;
c. the right to equal protection; and
d. the right to obtain freedom;

CHAPTER IV

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 6

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence are the shared responsibility of:
a. regional government;
b. community;
c. family; and
d. parents.

Article 7

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 6 letter a, include:
a. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
b. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
c. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
d. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity.
2. The duties of the Regional Government in the protection of women and children from acts of violence in the form of:
a. Implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;
b. Collecting data and information about women and children in the context of efforts to protect them from acts of violence;
c. Conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
d. Conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.

Article 8

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation.
2. The forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing protection to women and children from acts of violence, in the form of making rules of order in their respective areas starting from the Neighborhood Association level;
c. providing information and / or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties;
d. Participating in the handling of victims of acts of violence; and
e. Accompanying victims to service units.
3. The forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are implemented responsibly in accordance with laws and regulations.

Article 9

The obligations of the family and / or parents as referred to in Article 6 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

CHAPTER V

PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 10

1. To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government conducts socialization, empowerment, and awareness to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and / or counseling.
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government makes efforts as follows:
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal as well as informal;
b. opening employment opportunities for women;
c. building community participation and concern for the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
d. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and / or care about women and children.

Article 11

1. The prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 10, is implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. education;
d. manpower;
e. women's empowerment and child protection;
f. mental and spiritual; and
g. peace and order.
2. The prevention of acts of violence by SKPD as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner.

CHAPTERVI

SERVICES FOR VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 12

1. The forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence, are as follows:
a. complaint services;
b. medical services; and
c. medicolegal services for the purposes of evidence in the field of law.
2. Legal aid services:
a. independence or empowerment services; and
b. social rehabilitation services.
3. The forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented by relevant SKPD in accordance with the programs, activities, and budgets available in each in the fields of:
a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. women's empowerment and child protection;
d. mental and spiritual;
e. manpower; and
f. education.

CHAPTER VII

INSTITUTIONS

Article 13

1. In the context of providing protection services to women and children from victims of violence, the Regional Government establishes P2TP2A as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children from acts of violence including victims of acts of violence.
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), functions as an integrated service center in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.
3. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), above consists of the Health Office, Hospital, Police, Social Service, Manpower Office, Office of Women's Empowerment and Family Planning, Non-Governmental Organizations.
4. P2TP2A as referred to in Community Social Organizations, Universities in paragraph (3) is established by Regent's decree. (5) P2TP2A of Sorong Regency in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with the network of service units and competent parties in making efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence.

Article 14

1. For the implementation of services for victims of trafficking in persons, the Regent of Sorong establishes a task force for the criminal act of trafficking in persons.
2. The task force for the criminal act of trafficking in persons as referred to in paragraph (1), is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating the prevention and handling of trafficking in persons.
3. Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties, and functions of the task force for the criminal act of trafficking in persons as referred to in paragraph (1), are established by Regent's regulation.

Article 15

1. In the context of preventing acts of violence against children including the protection of children from the worst forms of child labor, a committee for the elimination of acts of violence against children will be established by Regent's decree.
2. Further provisions regarding the duties and functions of the committee for the elimination of acts of violence against children as referred to in paragraph (1), are regulated by Regent's regulation.

CHAPTER VIII

REPORTING

Article 16

1. P2TP2A of Sorong Regency is obliged to report the implementation of the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence to the Regent.
2. The reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. administration;
b. finance;
c. services; and
d. performance.
3. The submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out at least once every three (3) months.
