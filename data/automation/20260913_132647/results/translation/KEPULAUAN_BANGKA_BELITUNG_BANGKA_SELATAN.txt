IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

REGENT OF SOUTH BANGKA

BANGKA BELITUNG ISLANDS PROVINCE

REGIONAL REGULATION OF SOUTH BANGKA REGENCY

NUMBER 7 OF 2015

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF SOUTH BANGKA,

Considering:

a. that the number of violence against women and children in South Bangka has increased in the number of incidents. While protection and services for victims have been carried out but are still not optimal;
b. that in South Bangka Regency an Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children (P2TP2A) for victims of violence has been established but the basis for its establishment still uses a Regent Regulation which does not yet include the mechanism or implementation of protection that can facilitate victims and protection providers in implementing it;
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a and b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is South Bangka Regency.
2. Regent is the Regent of South Bangka.
3. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of South Bangka Regency as an element of regional government administration.
5. Principle of Respect and Recognition of Human Rights and Dignity is based on Respect for the rights of victims, Justice and gender equality, Non-Discrimination and the Best interests of victims.
6. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is an institution providing services to victims of violence, based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical care (including medico-legal), psychological and legal services.
7. Social Community Institution is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and or other community organizations.
8. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including those in the womb.
9. Woman is a person who has female genitalia who can menstruate and become pregnant or has obtained legal status as a woman.
10. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim.
11. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
12. Violence against children is any action that results in misery or suffering to children physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom.
13. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.
14. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
15. Economic violence is any act that deliberately neglects family members in the form of not providing a proper living care or maintenance.
16. Psychological violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
17. Victim is a woman and child who experiences and/or suffers either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
18. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear or have experienced violence against women.
19. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
20. Optimal service is a dynamic effort consisting of various interrelated parts, followed by performance offered by one party to another party by providing benefits, in order to achieve the best goal.
21. Information is a message (speech or expression) or collection of messages consisting of an order sequence of symbols, or meaning that can be interpreted from the message or collection of messages and can be recorded or transmitted.
22. Repatriation is an effort to return Victims of violence from the Region to the Region of Origin.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

The implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out based on the principle of respect and recognition of human rights and dignity.

Article 3

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to provide protection and services covering aspects of prevention, service and assistance, rehabilitation, reunification and empowerment.

Article 4

Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent acts of violence against women and children in South Bangka.

Article 5

Rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of victims related to health recovery as well as social recovery so that they can carry out their roles and functions properly in society.

Article 6

Service and Assistance is an activity carried out to serve, protect victims of acts of violence against women and children from the beginning of reporting to the legal process and psychological recovery to the repatriation process.

Article 7

Reunification and Empowerment is an activity carried out to reunite with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection for the fulfillment of needs for victims and empower victims of violence so that they can be economically independent.

CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 8

Every victim has the right to receive:
a. protection;
b. information;
c. optimal service;
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage; and
e. confidential handling whether individual, group or institution whether government or non-government.

Article 9

In the event of violence, every victim has the right to receive assistance both psychologically and legally and to receive guarantees of their rights related to their status as wife, mother, child, family member or as a member of society.

CHAPTER IV

DUTIES AND OBLIGATIONS OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

Article 10

1. The Regional Government has the duty to carry out prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against Women and Children, by:
a. collecting data and information about Women and Children Victims of Violence;
b. socializing Laws and Regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence;
c. conducting education on anti-violence values against children and women; and
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection of Women and Children victims of violence.
2. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against women and children, provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
a. providing and facilitating the establishment of integrated services for victims by involving institutions and community elements;
b. encouraging public awareness about the importance of protection for victims; and
c. empowering the community for the prevention of acts of violence.

CHAPTER V

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 11

1. The Regional Government in the implementation of protection of Victims of Violence involves community participation.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, groups or social community organizations.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) may be carried out in the form of:
a. preventing acts of violence against women and children;
b. submitting reports to the authorities if acts of violence against women and children occur; and
c. providing assistance to victims.

Article 12

1. Social community organizations that participate in the implementation of protection for victims as referred to in Article 11 paragraph (2), shall be given guidance by the Regional Government.
2. The guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of social guidance, skills guidance and operational technical guidance. (3) The guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out by the Institution for the Protection of Women and Children established by the Regional Government.

CHAPTER VI

INSTITUTION FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR VICTIMS

Article 13

1. The Regional Government shall establish an Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children (P2TP2A) as an Implementing Institution for the Prevention and Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence in an integrated manner.
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of elements of:
a. health;
b. law enforcement;
c. social and empowerment;
d. education and religion;
e. Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs); and
f. private sector.
3. Further provisions regarding P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER VII

IMPLEMENTATION AND FORMS OF PROTECTION

Article 14

1. The implementation of protection for victims shall be carried out in an integrated manner within the P2TP2A framework.
2. P2TP2A in handling medical, legal, medicolegal, psychological and economic protection which in its implementation may conduct partnerships with social community institutions engaged in the field of protection of Women and Children.
3. The implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) must pay attention to religious norms, customs and the rights and obligations of parents/guardians, husbands and/or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.
4. The mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 15

1. Every victim of violence has the right to receive protection in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.
2. Protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided to residents of South Bangka Regency both committed within the territory of South Bangka Regency and outside the territory of South Bangka Regency.
3. In addition to protection for victims who are residents of South Bangka as referred to in paragraph (2), protection shall also be provided to non-residents of South Bangka whose incident is committed or occurs within the territory of South Bangka Regency.

Article 16

1. Forms of protection provided to Victims implemented by P2TP2A shall be in the form of protection:
a. medical;
b. legal;
c. medico-legal (forensic medicine);
d. psychological; or
e. economic.
2. The form of medical protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, shall be in the form of treatment and recovery from physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of the victim.
3. The form of legal protection for Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, shall be in the form of services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.
4. The form of medico-legal protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, shall be in the form of medical services for the purposes of legal evidence.
5. The form of psychological protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, shall be in the form of assistance in order to restore traumatic conditions including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving.
6. The form of economic protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be in the form of services for skills and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

Article 17

The implementation of protection for victims shall be carried out free of charge, quickly, safely, empathetically, non-discriminatorily, easily accessible and with a guarantee of confidentiality.

Article 18

P2TP2A management is obliged to provide services as regulated in Article 16.

CHAPTER VIII

SOURCE OF FUNDS

Article 19

1. The source of funds for the implementation of protection for victims shall be charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget.
2. Funding as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be budgeted at the Agency for Women and Children Empowerment.
3. In addition to the source of funds as referred to in paragraph (1), funds may come from other sources that are legal and non-binding.

CHAPTER IX

REPORTING

Article 20

1. P2TP2A is obliged to report in writing the implementation of the protection of Women and Children victims of violence to the Regent. (2) The report as referred to in paragraph (1) shall include:
a. administration;
b. finance;
c. services; and
d. performance.
2. Submission of reports as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out at least once every 3 (three) months.

CHAPTER X

SANCTIONS

Article 21

1. Any person who intentionally does not provide protection for women and children resulting in the occurrence of violence, allows violence to occur, and/or does not report failure to provide protection for victims, shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
2. P2TP2A management who carry out service duties, if they violate service principles may be subject to sanctions in accordance with the internal mechanisms of P2TP2A.

CHAPTER XI

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 22

Regent Regulations as implementation of this Regional Regulation shall be stipulated no later than 1 (one) Year from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

Article 23

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation. So that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of South Bangka Regency.

I. GENERAL

The National Objectives of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia, as stipulated in the preamble of the 1945 Constitution in the fourth paragraph, include: (1) Protecting the entire Indonesian nation and all Indonesian territory, (2) advancing public welfare, (3) educating the life of the nation and (4) participating in implementing world order based on (a) independence, (b) eternal peace, and (c) social justice. To achieve these objectives, in accordance with the doctrine of trias politica, there are three State powers namely Executive, Legislative, and Judiciary. In Indonesia these three powers complement each other, for the implementation of clean and authoritative governance from the national level to the regional level.

The Republic of Indonesia is a State of Law (rechtstaat) and not a State of power (machtstaat), so that all actions of its citizens, including those in the form of power, carried out by both men and women, are also regulated by law.

The rights of women and children are recognized as Human Rights (HAM), which must be upheld and respected by all parties, however in reality there are still many women and children who become victims of violence. The commitment of the Central Government to protect children and women is reflected in the legislation below it.

Regional autonomy that is implemented has strategic value so that each region forms legislation (regional regulations), not only to express local wisdom, but also to become a guideline for regional governments to implement the legislation above it (the 1945 Constitution, and other Laws) to address legal problems and social problems that occur specifically in their respective regions.

The Regional Regulation on the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence is based on Pancasila, and based on the 1945 Constitution, and refers to many Laws especially those regulating the protection of women and children victims of violence, including Law No. 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, Law No. 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law No. 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law No. 35 of 2014 concerning Amendment to Law No. 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection, and other related Laws.

With the provisions of the Laws above, we can understand how great the attention of the Central Government is to protect its citizens who are in an unpleasant position, namely when becoming victims of violence, however in reality legal processes are still rarely carried out for victims of violence which today are increasingly numerous and complex in their problems. And in South Bangka very few existing problems can be resolved legally due to ignorance of the community and the absence of specific legal regulations that become legal regulations that can be used in the region.

Hopefully with the enactment of the Regional Regulation on the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence, in South Bangka Regency in particular, the handling and rehabilitation as well as reunification can be carried out properly, and the process of preventing violence against women and children becomes smoother, so that there is less and eventually no more violence against women and children.
