# THE REGENT OF NORTH GORONTALO

Considering: a. that in order to protect the constitutional rights of women and children in accordance with the mandate of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, the Government of North Gorontalo Regency is jointly responsible in the implementation of protection of women and children against acts of violence.

   - b. that North Gorontalo Regency which has the Philosophy of Adat Bersendi Syara, Syara Bersendi Kitabullah, upholds Religious Values and Customs as well as noble Character, the Government of North Gorontalo Regency is morally fully responsible for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.
   - c. that every citizen has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades dignity, self-worth and human dignity and has the right to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence
   - d. that the increase in violent treatment against women and children in North Gorontalo Regency is an act that degrades human dignity, so that the role of the North Gorontalo Regency government is required so that women and children are protected from acts of violence
   - e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children Against Acts of Violence In View of: 1. # CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is North Gorontalo Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of North Gorontalo.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution that is positioned as an element of the Regional Government administration.
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the regional head and DPRD in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
6. Forum for the Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence hereinafter abbreviated as FP2ATK is a forum established based on this Regional Regulation whose membership comes from relevant agencies and communities that care about protection from acts of violence.
7. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child who is still in the womb.
8. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and organizations and/or community organizations.
9. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband, wife, or husband wife and their child, or father and his child, or mother and her child, or blood relatives in direct lineage upward or downward up to the third degree.
10. Every person is an individual person or corporation.
11. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of freedom unlawfully.
12. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
13. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, sexually, economically, socially and psychologically.
14. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
15. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
16. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
17. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
18. Cooperation is an activity or effort carried out by several people to achieve common goals.
19. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will, is or has occurred.
20. Companion is a social worker who has professional competence in their field.
21. Recovery of victims is all efforts to strengthen victims of violence in the household to be more empowered, both physically and psychologically.
22. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service provider institution for victims established by the Regional Government.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:

   - a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of Victims of Acts of Violence;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. non-discrimination;
   - d. for Victims of acts of violence; and
   - e. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

The Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence aims to:

   - a. prevent all forms of Violence against Women and Children;
   - b. protect Women and provide services to Women and Child Victims of Acts of Violence; and
   - c. empower Women and Child Victims of Acts of Violence.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 4

The Scope of this Regional Regulation consists of:

   - a. protection of women; and
   - b. protection of children.

#### Article 5

The protection of women as referred to in Article 4 letter a includes:

   - a. prevention of acts of violence;
   - b. integrated services for victims of acts of violence;
   - c. empowerment of victims of acts of violence.

#### Article 6

Child protection as referred to in Article 4 letter b includes:

   - a. protection of children who are victims of acts of violence; and
   - b. special protection of children.

# CHAPTER IV

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 7

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of violence that occur in public or private life in the form of:

   - a. physical violence;
   - b. sexual violence;
   - c. economic violence;
   - d. social violence;
   - e. psychological violence;
   - f. domestic neglect;
   - g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and
   - h. threats of certain actions.

#### Article 8

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence as referred to in Article 7 is obligated to make efforts to:

   - a. prevent the continuation of criminal acts;
   - b. provide protection to victims of acts of violence;
   - c. provide emergency assistance; and/or
   - d. assist in the process of submitting an application for the determination of protection

## Part Two Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 9

1. Regional Government conducts prevention as referred to in article 5 letter a by means of:
   - a. socializing laws and regulations;
   - b. providing counseling/guidance;
   - c. providing education on the dangers of domestic violence;
   - d. conducting seminars/workshops or the like;
   - e. forming work networks in efforts to prevent violence;
   - f. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
   - g. forming a violence prevention system, mapping locations or areas prone to occurrence of violence; and
   - h. increasing public awareness in behaving in accordance with religious norms.
2. In addition to Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be implemented by:
   - a. family and/or closest relatives;
   - b. community;
   - c. educational institutions;
   - d. health institutions;
   - e. social community institutions; and
   - f. business sector and other institutions.

#### Article 10

1. Regional Governments are obligated to prepare prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan;
2. Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

#### Article 11

1. Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment shall implement prevention as referred to in Article 9;
2. Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1) in conducting prevention may cooperate with vertical Agencies.

## Part Three

Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 12

1. Regional Government implements integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b through activities:
   - a. complaint services;
   - b. health services;
   - c. counseling;
   - d. spiritual guidance;
   - e. social rehabilitation services;
   - f. legal aid and assistance services; and/or g. repatriation and reintegration.
2. Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported with facilities in the form of:
   - a. special service rooms in police lines;
   - b. expert and professional personnel;
   - c. service centers and safe houses; and/or
   - d. other facilities and infrastructure needed for victim recovery.
3. Implementation of services for victims of acts of violence is carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A with other social institutions;
4. P2TP2A and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner;
5. Integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) through paragraph (4) are implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures;
6. Provisions regarding Standard Operating Procedures as referred to in paragraph (5) are regulated by Governor Regulation.

Fourth Part Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 13

1. Empowerment of victims of acts of violence as referred to in article 5 letter c conducted by the Regional Government in the form of:
   - a. job training;
   - b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and c. capital assistance.
2. Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and Functions.

# BAB V

## CHILD PROTECTION

## Part One

Protection of Children Who Become Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 14

1. Protection of children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in article 6 letter a shall be the responsibility of the Regional Government;
2. Protection of children victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by:
   - a. formulating policies for prevention, risk reduction for vulnerable groups, handling of victims and data and information systems for children;
   - b. providing maintenance, care, and social rehabilitation for neglected children, both within institutions and outside institutions;
   - c. providing shelters, maintenance and care for neglected children; and providing special protection to children.

#### Article 15

1. Provisions regarding prevention and services for acts of violence in the protection of women as referred to in Article 7 through Article 13 shall apply mutatis mutandis to the protection of children from acts of violence;
2. In addition to carrying out prevention as referred to in paragraph (1), also by way of increasing family capacity in child protection and child-rearing skills.

Second Part Special Protection of Children

#### Article 16

1. Special protection for children as referred to in Article 6 letter b is given to:
   - a. Children in emergency situations;
   - b. Children in conflict with the law;
   - c. Children from minority and isolated groups;
   - d. Children who are exploited economically and/or sexually;
   - e. Children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse;
   - f. Children who are victims of pornography;
   - g. Children with HIV/AIDS;
   - h. Children victims of kidnapping, sale, and/or trafficking;
      - i. Children victims of physical and/or psychological violence;
   - j. Children victims of sexual crimes;
   - k. Children victims of terrorist networks;
   - l. Children with Disabilities;
   - m. Children victims of maltreatment and neglect;
   - n. Children with deviant social behavior; and
   - o. Children who are victims of stigmatization from labeling related to the condition of their Parents.
2. Regional Governments in implementing the handling of special protection for children as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two

Institutional Framework for the Protection of Women and Children

#### Article 18

1. The Regional Government in conducting cooperation and coordination for the Protection of Women and Children from acts of violence, shall establish FP2ATK;
2. The membership of FP2ATK consists of elements of:
   - a. regional government;
   - b. vertical agencies;
   - c. educational institutions; and
   - d. community organizations that care about the protection of women and children from acts of violence.
3. The Organizational Structure of FP2ATK as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of:
   - a. policy formulation working group;
   - b. socialization working group;
   - c. service working group; and
   - d. data, information, monitoring and evaluation working group.
4. The establishment and term of office of FP2ATK members as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 19

1. FP2ATK has the duties to:
   - a. provide input in the formulation of policies; b. conduct coordination with Regional Apparatus and other social institutions;
   - c. conduct socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
   - d. collect and develop data and information related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
   - e. conduct review, monitoring, evaluation, and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
   - f. provide reports, suggestions, input, and considerations to the Regent.

#### Article 20

FP2ATK as referred to in article 18 paragraph (2) is assisted by the Secretariat at the SKPD that handles affairs of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 21

Further provisions regarding the mechanism and working procedures of FP2ATK shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 22

1. The Regional Government in the context of providing services to victims of acts of violence, establishes P2TP2A;
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) has the duties to:
   - a. provide services, including:
1. receive complaints/reports of violence against women and/or children, consultation, and counseling;
2. receive and send case referrals from Regency/City Regional Government or institutions established by the Regency/City Regional Government;
3. provide legal assistance and support;
4. health;
5. social rehabilitation;
6. legal services; and
7. repatriation and social reintegration.
   - b. provide protection to victims of acts of violence, reporters and witnesses.
3. The appointment of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree.
4. Regional Apparatus shall provide guidance to P2TP2A in carrying out duties as referred to in paragraph (2).

   - a. reporting acts of violence against women and/or children;
   - b. providing first aid to victims of Acts of Violence;
   - c. independently disseminating the rights of women and children; and/or
   - d. establishing independent community social institutions.

#### Article 26

   - b. assistance as a form of corporate social responsibility.

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in North Gorontalo Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades their degree and human dignity, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations. Thus far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protection measures at the Regional level, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation. Regional Regulations regulate protection measures for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in North Gorontalo Regency

#### Article 6

Sufficiently clear Letter a What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or causes death Letter b What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Letter c What is meant by "economic violence" is an action that restricts a person from working inside or outside the home to earn money and goods, including allowing a person who works to be exploited, while someone does not fulfill the economic needs of the family.

Letter d What is meant by "social violence" is an act of a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life of a person or can cause physical damage or damage to another person's property Letter e What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Letter f What is meant by "Domestic Neglect" is: a. actions that result in the non-fulfillment of

   - b. the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
   - c. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should be; d. actions that neglect people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person;
   - e. actions that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Letter g Sufficiently clear.

Letter h What is meant by "threat of certain actions" is any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that create fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom.

### Paragraph (1) Letter a What is meant by "complaint service" is a series of actions taken by integrated service providers to follow up on reports of acts of violence against women and children submitted by victims, families or the community.

Letter f What is meant by "legal aid and assistance service" is legal services provided by legal assistants or advocates to carry out the process of accompanying witnesses and/or victims of violence against women and children that is gender sensitive.

Letter g What is meant by "repatriation and reintegration" is an effort to return women and child victims of violence from abroad to the debarkation point/entry point, or from the receiving area to the area of origin.
