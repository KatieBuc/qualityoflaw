# REGIONAL REGULATION OF BULUKUMBA REGENCY NUMBER 3 OF 2024

# CONCERNING

THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

# REGENT OF BULUKUMBA,

Considering:

   - a. that as citizens of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia, women and children have the right to obtain protection, a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence or inhumane treatment that is contrary to the values of Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
   - b. that in its development there are still many women and children who need to obtain protection from various forms of acts of violence, mistreatment, exploitation, and discrimination so that strategic efforts are needed to provide protection for women and children;
   - c. that based on Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government in carrying out concurrent government affairs, the Regency Government has the authority in the administration of government affairs in the field of protection of women and children;
   - d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Administration of Protection of Women and Children;

In View of: 1. Article 18 Paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

2. Law Number 29 of 1959 concerning the Establishment of Level II Regions in Sulawesi (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1959 Number 74, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 1822);

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Bulukumba Regency.
2. Government is a body or organization that exercises the governmental power of a state. The Government has the duty to administer governmental affairs, from the central level, province, regency, municipality, sub-district, urban village, to village.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government organizer who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
4. Regional Apparatus is an organization/Institution in the regional government that is responsible to the Regent in the context of administering government consisting of regional secretary, regional agencies and regional technical institutions, sub-districts, villages and civil service police units according to regional needs.
5. Agency is a Regional Apparatus that has the main duties and functions in carrying out Women's Empowerment and Child Protection of Bulukumba Regency.
6. Village Government is the administration of governmental affairs and the interests of the local community in the governmental system of the unitary state of the Republic of Indonesia.
7. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the annual financial plan of the regional government that is discussed and approved jointly by the Regional Government and DPRD, and stipulated by Regional Regulation.
8. Village Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter referred to as APBDes is the annual financial plan of the village government that is discussed and approved jointly by the Village Head and Village Representative Body which is stipulated through Village Regulation.

- 3 -

9. Woman is a person who has female genitalia who can menstruate and become pregnant or has obtained legal status as a woman.
10. Implementation of Women and Child Protection is a directed, integrated, and sustainable effort carried out by the Central Government, Regional Government, Village Government, and community to fulfill the rights of women and children as citizens within the scope of this Regional Regulation.
11. Women's Protection is all efforts to protect and provide a sense of security to women as well as the fulfillment of their rights through consistent, structured, and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
12. Women and Child Protection is a handling effort to protect and fulfill the rights of women and children from all forms of violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.
13. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
14. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
15. Violence Against Children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, and or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.
16. Violence Against Women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, and deprivation of liberty arbitrarily whether occurring in the private or public sphere.
17. Victim is a person who experiences physical suffering, women and children who experience violence and/or mental threats and or economic loss caused by an act of violence.
18. Victim of Violence is a person who experiences psychological, mental, physical, sexual, economic, and/or social suffering caused by criminal acts of violence.
19. Violence Prevention is any action or effort undertaken to eliminate various factors that cause acts of violence against women and children.
20. Community is individual Indonesian citizens, community groups, and/or Mass Organizations.
21. Technical Implementation Unit for Women and Child Protection hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA is an operational technical implementation unit in a work unit that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection, which functions as an integrated service provider for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, and other problems.
22. Child-Friendly Regency is a regency that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of commitment and resources of government, community and business world that is planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights.

- 4 -

23. Assistance for Victims of Violence is an effort or process to assist victims of violence in accessing rights to handling, protection, and recovery.
24. Rapid Reaction Team hereinafter abbreviated as TRC is a team formed by the agency in charge of Women's Empowerment and Children that is tasked with assisting UPT PPA in conducting outreach and/or assistance to accelerate the process of handling cases of violence against women and children.
25. Task Force for Women and Child Protection is a coordinative institution formed by the Government of Bulukumba Regency that coordinates policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children in Bulukumba Regency.
26. Vulnerable Groups are any person or community group entitled to receive special treatment and protection relating to their specificity. Vulnerable groups as referred to in this regulation consist of women and children in conflict with the law, persons with disabilities, Indonesian migrant workers, the elderly and/or indigenous peoples of ammatoa Kajang.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The Implementation of Protection of Women and Children is implemented based on the principles of:

   - a. humanity;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. protection;
   - d. best interests of women and children;
   - e. non-discrimination; and
   - f. special treatment for vulnerable groups.

#### Article 3

The Implementation of Protection of Women and Children aims to: a. prevent acts of violence against women and children; b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;

   - c. protect, provide a sense of security for women and children; d. provide services to women victims of acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses;
   - e. provide services to children both as victims, witnesses as well as perpetrators of violence;
   - f. facilitate and conduct mediation on domestic disputes to realize an equal and just household; and g. prioritize services and protection for Vulnerable Groups.

# CHAPTER IV

## PREVENTION OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

1. Prevention of violence as referred to in Article 4 letter a shall be implemented through:
   - a. planning and implementation of policies, programs, and activities; b. provision of education; and
   - c. enhancement of community participation.
2. Prevention of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented by the Regional Government in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.
3. Planning and implementation of policies, programs and activities as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a namely:
   - a. integrating the issue of protection of women and children into the policies, programs and activities of Regional Apparatus; and
   - b. making the issue of protection of women and children one of the priorities of regional development.
4. Provision of education as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b namely: a. providing information, guidance and/or counseling; b. provision of accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;
   - c. creating a friendly and safe work environment atmosphere for women; and
   - d. building and providing an information system that is easily accessible; (5) Enhancement of community participation through involvement of parties as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, namely:
   - a. building and enhancing community participation and awareness towards prevention of protection of women and children; b. making regional regulations and encouraging Village Governments to make Village Regulations related to protection of women and children; and
   - c. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, private sector/business world, universities, mass media, and non-governmental organizations as well as community institutions that are engaged in and care about women and children.

##### THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

##### I. GENERAL

The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia has mandated to provide protection for women and children, as well as to provide equal rights for women to participate in contributing to various aspects of development. This is also reaffirmed in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government regarding the division of governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection. In line with this, Regional Governments which have the principles of autonomy and co-administration with the principle of autonomy as broad as possible, also strive to realize the ideals contained in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.

Based on this, the Regional Government of Bulukumba Regency has the desire to realize its commitment in order to provide protection for women and children so that their rights can be fulfilled and to realize gender equality and justice in all aspects in accordance with human dignity, as well as to optimize women's empowerment, child protection, and gender mainstreaming in the region so that efforts need to be made involving all parties in various aspects of development.

In an effort to realize this commitment, the Regional Government of Bulukumba Regency has established a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children which regulates clearly, firmly, and comprehensively while also serving as proof of the seriousness of the commitment of the Bulukumba Regency Government in protecting the rights of women and children as well as providing a legal umbrella in fighting for their rights.

#### Article 2

Letter a What is meant by the principle of humanity is the principle of humanity emphasizes respect and protection of the basic rights of every individual regardless of gender, age, or social status.

This means that women and children have equal rights to live without violence and treatment that degrades human dignity.

- 15 - Letter b What is meant by the principle of justice and gender equality is this principle emphasizes the need to address gender inequality which is often the root cause of acts of violence against women and children. Protection must ensure that women and children receive fair and equal treatment in the legal system and society without discrimination based on gender.

Letter c What is meant by the principle of protection is proactive and preventive protection against acts of violence. This protection must be carried out through an approach that includes prevention, handling, rehabilitation, and support for victims as well as potential perpetrators so that violence does not occur again.

Letter d What is meant by the principle of the best interests of women and children is this principle focuses on the best interests and rights of women and children as the main priority. Protection actions and policies must always prioritize their welfare and interests, including listening to their views and aspirations in the decision-making process.

Letter e What is meant by the principle of non-discrimination is this principle affirms that protection must be carried out without discrimination based on gender, age, ethnic background, religion, or social status. Every individual has equal rights to be protected from acts of violence, without exception Letter f What is meant by the principle of special treatment for vulnerable groups is treatment of a group of people who experience vulnerability or physical and non-physical limitations such as women, children, persons with disabilities, pregnant women, the elderly, minority groups, people with mental disorders and poor people.

Point d What is meant by "vulnerable group advocates" is a person who pays attention to every person or group of people entitled to obtain special treatment and protection relating to their specific characteristics, and may originate from communities or organizations advocating for vulnerable groups.

Vulnerable groups as referred to in this regulation consist of women and children in conflict with the law, persons with disabilities, Indonesian migrant workers, the elderly and/or the indigenous legal community of ammatoa Kajang.
