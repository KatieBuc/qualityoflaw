# REGENT OF NORTH CENTRAL TIMOR

# PROVINCE OF EAST NUSA TENGGARA

REGIONAL REGULATION OF NORTH CENTRAL TIMOR REGENCY NUMBER 3 OF 2020

# CONCERNING

# THE IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

# BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

# THE REGENT OF NORTH CENTRAL TIMOR,

Considering : a. that every person has equal dignity and worth to live in society, nation and state and has the right to obtain protection of human rights and fundamental human freedoms without discrimination as an effort to realize gender equality and justice in national and regional development;

   - b. that in order to realize gender equality and justice in development in North Central Timor Regency, it is necessary to increase the effectiveness and optimization of the implementation of the sub-affairs of women's empowerment as an inseparable part of the functional activities of government and non-government institutions in order to improve the position, role and quality of women in development in North Central Timor Regency;
   - c. that to guarantee legal certainty and to fill the legal vacuum in order to realize gender equality and justice in development in North Central Timor Regency, it is necessary to have regulations in a Regional Regulation concerning the implementation of women's empowerment;
   - d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Women's Empowerment;

In View of : 1 Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

2. Law Number 69 of 1958 concerning the Establishment of Level II Regions within the Level I Regional Areas of Bali, West Nusa Tenggara and East Nusa Tenggara (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1958 Number 122 Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 1655);
3. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587); As has been amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58 Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);

With Mutual Approval: THE REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF NORTH CENTRAL TIMOR REGENCY and

# REGENT OF NORTH CENTRAL TIMOR

# DECIDES:

To Establish : REGIONAL REGULATION ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the Regency of North Central Timor.
2. Regional Government is the Government of North Central Timor Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of North Central Timor.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of North Central Timor Regency.
5. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of North Central Timor Regency.
6. Agency is the Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and Child Protection.
7. Head of Agency is the Head of Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment.
8. Regional Apparatus hereinafter abbreviated as PD is the Regional Apparatus in North Central Timor Regency.
9. Head of Regional Apparatus is the Head of Regional Apparatus in North Central Timor Regency.
10. Board is the Regional Apparatus that administers Government affairs in the field of development planning and regional research.
11. Head of Board is the Regional Apparatus that administers Government affairs in the field of planning.
12. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social capabilities, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective capabilities.
13. Gender Mainstreaming, hereinafter abbreviated as PUG is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of regional development policies and programs.
14. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of societal construction and can change due to social and cultural circumstances of society.
15. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings so they are able to play a role and participate in the development process.
16. Gender Justice is a process to be fair toward men and women.
17. Gender Mainstreaming Working Group, hereinafter referred to as Pokja PUG is a consultation forum for implementers and mobilizers of gender mainstreaming from various agencies/institutions in the region.
18. Gender Mainstreaming Focal Point, hereinafter referred to as Focal Point PUG is PD apparatus who have the capability to conduct gender mainstreaming in their respective work units.
19. Regional Medium-Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMD is the elaboration of the vision, mission and program of the Regent whose preparation is guided by the Regional Long-Term Development Plan, which contains regional development strategies, general regional policies, regional programs and macroeconomic framework that includes an overview of the economy as a whole including the direction of fiscal policy in a work plan in the form of a regulatory framework and financing framework that is indicative in nature.
20. Gender Analysis is a process built systematically to identify and understand the division of labor or roles of men and women, access and control over development resources, participation in development and the benefits they enjoy.
21. Gender Responsive Budget is a performance-based budget allocation for certain programs or activities whose input, output, and outcome indicators can guarantee the fulfillment of the aspirations and needs of women and men fairly.
22. Communication, Information and Education hereinafter abbreviated as KIE is the delivery of messages in the form of information, ideas or facts related to gender mainstreaming that need to be known by the community directly or indirectly through communication channels to message recipients to obtain effects, as a process of behavioral change toward gender responsiveness.
23. Village/Sub-district Development Planning Deliberation hereinafter referred to as Musrembangkel is an annual village/sub-district development planning deliberation forum that involves development actors in the Village/Sub-district.
24. Sub-district Development Planning Deliberation hereinafter referred to as Musrembangcam is a deliberation forum among development actors to discuss and agree on steps for handling priority program activities listed in the list of proposed village development planning activities that are integrated with development priorities of the North Central Timor Regency region in the sub-district area coordinated by the Regional Development Planning Board and implemented by the Sub-district Head.
25. Development planning deliberation for the preparation of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan hereinafter referred to as Musrembang RPJMD is a deliberation forum among stakeholders to discuss, sharpen and agree on the draft Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.
26. Strategic Plan hereinafter referred to as Renstra is a five-year planning document that contains the vision, mission, objectives, strategies, policies, programs and development activities in accordance with the organizational tasks and functions as an elaboration of the RPJMD.
27. Work Plan hereinafter referred to as Renja is a one-year planning document that contains policies, programs, and development activities both planned directly by the Regional Apparatus and pursued by encouraging community participation as an elaboration of the Renstra.
28. Regional Government Work Plan hereinafter abbreviated as RKPD is a one-year planning document that contains policies, programs, and development activities both planned directly by the Regional Government and pursued by encouraging community participation as an elaboration of the Renstra.
29. Regional Action Plan hereinafter abbreviated as RAD is an activity planning document prepared by a technical team to be implemented in a certain period/time.
30. Gender Analysis Pathway hereinafter abbreviated as GAP is a gender analysis tool that can be used to assist planners in mainstreaming gender in planning development policies/programs/activities with the aim of identifying gender gaps and gender issues as well as simultaneously preparing development policy/program/activity plans aimed at reducing or eliminating such gender gaps.
31. Technical Team is a Team formed by Pokja PUG with the task of conducting gender-perspective budget analysis and preparing the PUG RAD.

# CHAPTER II

## SCOPE

#### Article 2

The scope of the implementation of Women's Empowerment includes:

   - a. quality of life of women;
   - b. protection of women;
   - c. quality of family; and
   - d. gender data system.

# CHAPTER III

## QUALITY OF LIFE OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 3

Quality of Life for Women includes:

   - a. institutionalization of gender mainstreaming;
   - b. Women's Empowerment in the fields of politics, law, social and economic matters in community organizations; and
   - c. strengthening and development of institutions providing women's empowerment services.

Second Part Institutionalization of Gender Mainstreaming General Paragraph

#### Article 4

The institutionalization of PUG includes:

   - a. appointment of PUG coordinator;
   - b. PUG working group;
   - c. PUG Focal Point;
   - d. Budgeting;
   - e. gender-based development planning;
   - f. monitoring and evaluation;
   - g. reporting; and
   - h. supervision.

### Paragraph 2

Coordinator

#### Article 5

1. The Regent has the authority to appoint the Head of PD who administers governmental affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment as coordinator of PUG implementation.
2. The appointment as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be by Regent's Decree.

### Paragraph 3

Working Group on Gender Mainstreaming

#### Article 6

1. The Regent has the authority to establish the PUG Working Group.
2. The establishment of the PUG Working Group as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree.
3. The Regent's Decree as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be valid for 5 (five) years.
4. The management of the PUG Working Group shall consist of:
   - a. chairperson by the head of agency;
   - b. secretariat by the head of service;
   - c. members comprising:
1. head of PD;
2. technical team consisting of:
   - a. apparatus who have attended training or education in gender-responsive budget analysis of at most 5 (five) persons.
   - b. representatives from university elements of at most 5 (five) persons;
   - c. press representatives of at most 5 (five) persons; and
   - d. representatives from non-governmental organizations having an orientation scope in the field of gender of at most 5 (five) persons.

#### Article 7

The duties of the PUG Working Group include:

   - a. promoting and facilitating dialogue among work units within the scope of regional government;
   - b. developing work networks in accordance with the main duties, functions and authorities granted by the leadership in efforts to realize gender equality and justice;
   - c. assigning technical teams to carry out the preparation of the PUG RAD in order to realize gender equality and justice adjusted to the periodization of the RPJMD;
   - d. elaborating and following up on policies and implementation programs to be gender responsive that are implicit and explicit in the RPJMD;
   - e. coordinating the establishment of PUG Focal Points in every PD or work unit;
   - f. promoting PUG in every PD;
   - g. conducting capacity strengthening for PUG Focal Points in preparing gender responsive budgets by the technical team;
   - h. overseeing the preparation of gender responsive planning and budgets in their respective PDs;
      - i. including gender indicators in the guidelines for preparing the APBD;
   - j. ensuring gender responsive budgets become a consideration in the discussion and determination of the APBD together with the DPRD;
   - k. holding coordination meetings every 6 (six) months to ensure the implementation of PUG in the Region;
   - l. preparing and submitting reports on PUG implementation to the regent every 6 (six) months; and
   - m. holding coordination meetings before the preparation of the RKPD.

### Paragraph 4

Focal Point PUG

#### Article 8

1. The Regional Secretary has the authority to establish a PUG Focal Point within the Regional Secretariat.
2. The establishment of the PUG Focal Point as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by a Decree of the Regional Secretary.
3. The management of the PUG Focal Point as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
   - a. person in charge by the regional secretary;
   - b. coordinator by an echelon IIB official overseeing governmental affairs in the field of law;
   - c. vice coordinator by an echelon IIIA official from each division in the regional secretariat except for the echelon IIB official as referred to in letter b; and
   - d. members by representatives of officials and/or staff from all divisions in the regional secretariat.

#### Article 9

1. The Head of PD is obligated to establish a Focal Point PUG PD.
2. The establishment of Focal Point PUG as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be by Decree of the Head of PD.
3. The management of Focal Point PUG as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
   - a. person in charge by the head of PD;
   - b. coordinator by echelon III A official overseeing planning affairs at the PD;
   - c. Vice Coordinator by Echelon IIIB Official who has competence in PUG; and
   - d. Members by representatives of Officials or staff from divisions and secretariat at the PD.

#### Article 10

1. The Hospital Director has the authority to establish a Hospital PUG Focal Point.
2. The establishment of the PUG Focal Point as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by a decision of the Hospital Director. (3) The management of the PUG Focal Point as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of:
   - a. person in charge by the hospital director;
   - b. coordinator by an echelon IIIB official in charge of hospital planning;
   - c. vice coordinator by an echelon IVA official who has competence in PUG; and
   - d. members by representatives of officials or staff in divisions or sections within the hospital.

#### Article 11

1. The Head of the Community Health Center has the authority to establish the Focal Point PUG Puskesmas.
2. The establishment of the Focal Point PUG as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by a Decree of the Head of the Community Health Center.
3. The management of the Focal Point PUG puskesmas consists of:8
   - a. person in charge by the Head of Puskesmas;
   - b. coordinator by an echelon IV B official in charge of Puskesmas planning; and
   - c. members consisting of Puskesmas staff.

#### Article 12

The duties of the PUG Focal Point include:

   - a. assisting policy makers of work units within the scope of duties, principal tasks and functions of their agencies to take planned steps fully when there are gender gaps;
   - b. encouraging and assisting work units to evaluate and improve mandates, policies, programs, projects, activities and budgets to be more gender responsive;
   - c. facilitating the implementation of gender sensitivity training, gender analysis training and developing gender work networks with agencies or institutions or organizations and other work units, both government and non-government;
   - d. endeavoring to ensure the implementation of gender analysis as one of the stages in every development process starting from the planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation stages;
   - e. elaborating and following up on policies and programs for development implementation to be gender responsive as implied in the Regional Strategic Plan, Regional Work Plan and Regional Government Work Plan;
   - f. participating in various activities organized by the PUG Working Group and/or the national PUG working group; and g. preparing activity reports periodically and reporting them to the regent through the chairperson of the PUG working group.

### Paragraph 5

Gender-Based Regional Development Planning Article 13 (1) The Regional Government is responsible for preparing planning for policies, programs and development activities with a Gender perspective.

2. The responsibility as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be coordinated by the PD that administers governmental affairs in the field of development planning, research and Regional development.
3. Planning as referred to in paragraph (1) includes the stages:
   - a. Musrenbangdus;
   - b. Musrembangdes/kel;
   - c. Musrembangcam;
   - d. Musrenbang at the regional level;
   - e. preparation of RPJMD;
   - f. preparation of Renstra PD;
   - g. preparation of Renja PD; and
   - h. RKPD;
      - i. preparation of RAPBD;
4. Planning as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted through Gender analysis using the Gender Analysis Pathway method or other analytical methods.
5. Implementation of Gender analysis on RPJMD and Renstra PD may collaborate with Higher Education institutions or other parties who have capability in their field.

#### Article 14

1. The Agency is responsible for preparing guidelines for gender-based development planning.
2. The guidelines as referred to in paragraph (1) must be used in facilitating:
   - a. Musrenbangdus;
   - b. Musrembangdes/kel;
   - c. Musrembangcam;
   - d. Musrenbang at the regional level;
   - e. preparation of RPJMD;
   - f. preparation of Renstra PD; and
   - g. preparation of Renja PD, that is gender responsive.
3. The preparation of guidelines as referred to in paragraph (1) may cooperate with the Technical Team of Pokja PUG.

#### Article 15

1. The Gender Analysis Pathway method as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (4) is used as a way to make planning in every PD.
2. The method as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: a. stage I analysis of gender responsive policies;
   - b. stage II formulation of gender responsive policies; c. stage III gender responsive action plan;
   - d. stage IV implementation of activities that have been prepared; and e. stage V monitoring and evaluation of each stage and step taken.

### Paragraph 6

Budgeting

#### Article 16

1. Regional Governments are obligated to allocate budget for the implementation of PUG in the Region.
2. The implementation budget as referred to in paragraph (1) is sourced from:
   - a. APBD; and
   - b. assistance from other parties that is non-binding, provided it is in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.
3. The budget as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), is used to finance the activities of Pokja PUG and Focal Point PUG PD in integrating Gender at the level of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of policies and development in the Region.

### Paragraph 7

Monitoring and Evaluation

#### Article 17

1. The Regent has the authority to conduct monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of PUG.
2. The Regent may delegate the implementation of monitoring and evaluation to the PUG Working Group.
3. Monitoring and evaluation shall be conducted at each PD and/or other institutions that implement PUG efforts.
4. Monitoring and evaluation shall be conducted at least once every 6 (six) months.

#### Article 18

Monitoring of PUG implementation is carried out through:

   - a. monitoring the preparation of gender-responsive development planning documents both at the PD level and at the regional level; b. monitoring the RAD PUG; and
   - c. verification of Reports on the implementation of RAD PUG.

#### Article 19

Evaluation of PUG implementation is conducted through:

   - a. evaluation of achievements and challenges in the implementation of RAD PUG; b. preparation of RAD PUG for the next period;
   - c. evaluation of achievements and challenges in PUG implementation every 6 (six) months; and
   - d. preparation of PUG implementation plan for the next 6 (six) months.

### Paragraph 8

PUG Reporting

#### Article 20

1. Pokja PUG is responsible for preparing the PUG Implementation Report every 6 (six) months and the five-yearly RAD PUG Implementation Report.
2. The reporting framework as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be guided by the reporting framework as referred to in the laws and regulations concerning General Guidelines for PUG Implementation in the Regions.
3. The Pokja PUG report as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be reported to the Regent.
4. The Regent is responsible for examining and declaring approval of the Pokja PUG Report as referred to in paragraph (1).
5. The Regent is responsible for sending the pokja PUG report as referred to in paragraph (1) to the Minister who administers Home Affairs governmental matters.

### Paragraph 9

Supervision

#### Article 21

1. The Regent is responsible for conducting supervision of the implementation of PUG.
2. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of: a. Gender responsive planning and budgeting conducted by the regional government budget team;
   - b. implementation of coordination between the PUG Working Group and Gender Focal Point; and
   - c. progress of RAD PUG achievement every year. (3) The responsibility as referred to in paragraph (1) can be technically and operationally implemented by the Chairperson of the PUG Working Group and the Head of the PUG Working Group Secretariat.
4. The implementation of responsibility as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be reported to the Regent.

Third Part Empowerment of Women in the Fields of Politics, Law, Social Culture and Economy in Community Organizations

### Paragraph 1

General

#### Article 22

Efforts for Women's Empowerment in the fields of politics, law, social culture and economy in community organizations include the stages of:

   - a. planning;
   - b. implementation; and
   - c. reporting and evaluation.

### Paragraph 2

Planning

#### Article 23

1. The Agency is responsible for facilitating the planning of Women's Empowerment in the fields of politics, law, social culture and economy in community organizations.
2. The planning as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated manner in development planning deliberations based on gender analysis pathway instruments.

### Paragraph 3

Implementation

#### Article 24

The implementation of Women's Empowerment in community organizations shall be carried out by the PD in accordance with the main duties and functions assigned in accordance with the regional financial capacity.

#### Article 25

1. PD that administers governmental affairs in the field of national unity and politics is responsible for facilitating the empowerment of women in the political field.
2. Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: a. implementing education and training for women in order to increase competence especially in the political field; and
   - b. encouraging the representation of women in every management of community organizations.

#### Article 26

1. PD that administers governmental affairs in the field of law is responsible for facilitating Women's Empowerment in the field of law.
2. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the field of law includes:
   - a. increasing awareness and knowledge in the field of law through communication, information and education services; and b. facilitating access and legal consultation services.

#### Article 27

1. PD that administers governmental affairs in the social sector is responsible for facilitating Women's Empowerment in the socio-cultural sector.
2. Women's Empowerment in the socio-cultural sector through: a. enhancement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;
   - b. enhancement of awareness, willingness and ability to address health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts primarily in the field of reproductive health;
   - c. enhancement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and
   - d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and traditions and development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 28

1. PD that administers governmental affairs in the field of cooperatives, small and medium enterprises and industry and trade is responsible for facilitating Women's Empowerment in the economic field.
2. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the economic field is carried out through:
   - a. provision of skills and job training;
   - b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;
   - c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;
   - d. facilitation and capital assistance; and
   - e. facilitation of marketing network development.

### Paragraph 4

Reporting and Evaluation

#### Article 29

1. Every PD is responsible for making a report on the implementation of Women's Empowerment activities that have been carried out to the Regent through the Dinas.
2. The Dinas after receiving the report submitted by the PD conducts an evaluation of the implementation of activities.
3. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of activities as referred to in paragraph (2) are reported to the Regent.
4. The results of the evaluation as referred to in paragraph (3) are used for budgetary planning for the following year.

# CHAPTER IV

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 30

Protection of Women includes:

   - a. strengthening and development of institutions providing women's protection services;
   - b. prevention of violence against women; and c. strengthening and development of institutions providing protection services for women victims of violence;

Second Part Strengthening And Development of Institutions Providing Women's Protection Services

### Paragraph 1

Regional Technical Implementation Unit for Women and Child Protection

#### Article 31

1. PD that administers governmental affairs in the field of institutions and organizations is responsible for facilitating the establishment of regional technical implementing units for the protection of women and children.
2. The establishment of UPTD P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.
3. The establishment of UPTD P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be no later than 2 (two) years after this Regional Regulation is enacted.

### Paragraph 2

Regional Action Plan

#### Article 32

1. The preparation of RAD as referred to in Article 7 letter c is implemented through the following mechanism:
   - a. establishment of a drafting team;
   - b. conducting meetings to form working groups based on indicators;
   - c. each Pokja conducts identification of objectives, targets and indicators;
   - d. determining priority programs and activities related to the achievement of each target; and
   - e. determining budget allocation for each program activity.
2. RAD shall contain at minimum:
   - a. introduction;
   - b. policy direction and achievement strategy;
   - c. monitoring and evaluation;
   - d. closing; and
   - e. appendix containing the RAD implementation matrix.
3. The preparation of RAD is implemented with due regard to:
   - a. RPJMN;
   - b. RPJMD; and
   - c. other related documents.
4. Provisions regarding the implementation of RAD shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

### Paragraph 3

Monitoring and Evaluation

#### Article 33

1. The Regent conducts monitoring of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women.
2. The monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women in the Region.
3. The monitoring as referred to in paragraph (2) is conducted periodically on Regional Apparatus through the Agency.
4. The monitoring is conducted from planning through to the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of women for the current year.

#### Article 34

1. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women shall be conducted at the end of each fiscal year.
2. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women shall be used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women for the following year.
3. The evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Third Part Prevention of Violence Against Women

#### Article 35

1. The Agency is responsible for implementing efforts to prevent the occurrence of Violence against Women.
2. Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in the form of:
   - a. conducting counseling and socialization regarding matters related to violence against women;
   - b. conducting socialization on women's rights;
   - c. endeavoring to improve education for women;
   - d. providing counseling to immediately report to local authorities if Violence against women occurs;
   - e. empowering social community organizations and women's organizations to increase women's knowledge and insight; and
   - f. establishing a network in the framework of efforts to prevent violence against women.

## Part Four

Strengthening and Development of Institutions Providing Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 36

1. The Agency may provide assistance to Service Institutions located in the region in the form of:
   - a. strengthening of Service Institutions; and
   - b. development of Service Institutions (2) Strengthening of service institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a shall be implemented through:
   - a. improvement of competence of service institution members; and/or b. facilitating paralegal training on a regular basis (3) Development of Service Institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b shall be implemented through:
   - a. providing additional facilities and infrastructure;
   - b. providing information technology-based service approaches; and/or
   - c. recruiting village cadres as assistant paralegals.

# CHAPTER V

## FAMILY QUALITY

## Part One

General

#### Article 37

Family Quality includes:

   - a. improvement of family quality in realizing gender equality; and
   - b. strengthening and development of institutions providing family quality improvement services in realizing gender equality.

Second Part Improving Family Quality in Realizing Gender Equality

#### Article 38

1. The Agency is responsible for facilitating family quality improvement activities in the Region.
2. Family quality improvement as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented through:
   - a. facilitating the establishment of service provider institutions; b. in coordination with Regional Apparatus and/or related institutions that have the main duties and functions to carry out activities:
1. improving entrepreneurial capabilities for women; or
2. developing job training for women.

## Part Three

Strengthening And Development Of Service Provider Institutions For Improving Family Quality In Realizing Gender Equality

#### Article 39

1. Facilitation of the establishment of service provider institutions as referred to in Article 36 paragraph (2) letter a shall be implemented by establishing family learning centers.
2. The stages of establishing family learning centers include: a. preparing regulations;
   - b. preparing human resources;
   - c. preparing facilities and infrastructure; and
   - d. establishment of family learning centers.
3. Family learning centers function:
   - a. as service institutions in prevention and counseling; b. as a referral liaison point as a solution for child and family problems;
   - c. strengthening family capacity in nurturing and protecting children;
   - d. as a place for family learning through education for parents; and
   - e. as a one-stop holistic integrative family service based on children's rights.

# CHAPTER VI

## GENDER DATA SYSTEM

## Part One

General

#### Article 40

The Gender Data System includes:

   - a. data collection;
   - b. data processing; and
   - c. analysis and presentation of gender data.

## Part Two Data Collection

#### Article 41

1. PD that carries out the duties of PUG focal point is responsible for reporting Gender data and PUG activities that are implemented to the Regent through the Dinas.
2. Reporting of Gender data and PUG activities as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted every 6 (six) months.

Third Part Data Processing

#### Article 42

1. The Agency after receiving reports on Gender data and PUG activities from each Gender focal point in the region shall conduct data processing.
2. Data processing as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented to sort Gender data and PUG activities with proper grouping that is easy to analyze.

Fourth Part Gender Data Analysis and Presentation

#### Article 43

1. The Agency conducts analysis of the data that has been processed.
2. The analysis as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out comprehensively based on adequate mapping so that it is easy to understand.
3. The adequate analysis as referred to in paragraph (2) is presented as regional Gender data manually and/or electronically.

# CHAPTER VII

## SANCTION PROVISIONS

#### Article 44

Regional Apparatus (PD) that do not establish a Gender Mainstreaming Focal Point as referred to in Article 8, Article 9, Article 10 and Article 11 shall be subject to administrative sanctions in the form of:

   - a. verbal warning;
   - b. written warning; or
   - c. statement of dissatisfaction.

# CHAPTER VIII

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 45

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, the existing institutions providing protection services for women shall continue to carry out their duties until the establishment of the regional technical implementing unit for the protection of women and children in accordance with the provisions of this Regional Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 46

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of North Central Timor Regency.

##### REGIONAL REGULATION OF NORTH CENTRAL TIMOR REGENCY

##### NUMBER 3 OF 2020

##### REGARDING

##### THE IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

##### I. GENERAL

The presence of Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government as has been amended several times, most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government and Law Number 33 of 2004 concerning Financial Balance Between the Central Government and Regional Governments constitutes an entry point for Women's Empowerment in regional development. Women's empowerment is an appropriate development strategy to realize gender equality and justice through gender-sensitive and gender-responsive development policies and programs in the future, particularly in North Central Timor Regency.

In North Central Timor Regency there are several conditions that need to be intervened through the establishment of a Regional Regulation on Women's Empowerment. This occurs because there is much discrimination against women in developing themselves and opportunities for the fulfillment of their rights. Women are only considered as second-class citizens. Their duty is to take care of the household and they do not need to work. This is what needs to be fought against, this stigma becomes a problem that must be resolved through empowerment programs.

The above paradigm is reflected in data on the presence of women working in Government, data shows that women are not in strategic positions to participate in making decisions or policies, especially in Government affairs, through data on Civil Servants based on gender available in the field.

Based on this thinking, it is time for North Central Timor Regency to establish a Regional Regulation on Women's Empowerment in North Central Timor Regency. This Regional Regulation will regulate the implementation of gender mainstreaming clearly, firmly, in a balanced and comprehensive manner to realize gender equality and justice as well as simultaneously provide understanding about the importance of women's empowerment as a gender analysis strategy in planning, formulating, implementing, monitoring and evaluating development policies and programs.
