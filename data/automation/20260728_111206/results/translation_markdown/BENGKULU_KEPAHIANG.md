   - b. that every citizen has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and has the right to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence and discrimination;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:

1. Region is the Regency Region of Kepahiang.
2. Regent is the Regent of Kepahiang.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are autonomous authority.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of the Regional Government administrator.
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.
6. Woman is a person who is female in gender.
7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.
8. Violence against women and children is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence occurring in the Regency area of Kepahiang.
10. Protection of women and children is a series of fulfillment of the rights of women and child victims of violence starting from prevention, handling, recovery and empowerment.
11. Service is an action carried out as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is, or has occurred.
12. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the purpose of strengthening and recovery of the victim of violence.
13. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a cooperative network that carries out the function of integrated services for the protection of women and child victims of violence.
14. Safe House is a residence for victims, during the process of assistance, and recovery for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and actions of perpetrators.
15. Standard Operating Procedure hereinafter abbreviated as SOP is standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from complaints, identification, health and psychological recovery, legal services up to repatriation and reintegration of victims into society.
16. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their child, or father and his child, or mother and her child, or blood relatives in a direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
17. Household is husband, wife, and child, and people who have family relations due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.
18. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the APBD of Kepahiang Regency.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The principles of protection for children and women who are victims of violence are:

   - a. respect and fulfillment of victims' rights as human rights; b. justice and equality;
   - c. non-discrimination;
   - d. rights-based handling; and
   - e. best interests of the victims.

#### Article 3

The objectives of protection for women and children are:

   - a. to prevent acts of violence against women and children; b. to eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
   - c. to protect and provide a sense of security for women and children; d. to provide services to women and children both as victims of acts of violence, as well as reporters and witnesses;
   - e. to guarantee the handling and resolution of acts of violence against women and children in a fair and dignified manner; and
   - f. to carry out recovery and empowerment for women and children who are victims of acts of violence.

#### Article 4

The scope of protection for women and children includes prevention efforts, services, recovery and empowerment.

# CHAPTER II

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Every woman and child victim of violence has the following rights:

   - a. the right to have their dignity and worth as a human being respected; b. the right to recovery of health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
   - c. the right to determine their own decisions;
   - d. the right to obtain information;
   - e. the right to confidentiality of their identity;
   - f. the right to complaint handling;
   - g. the right to assistance and legal aid;
   - h. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and i. the right to compensation;
   - j. the right to social recovery;

#### Article 6

Child victims of violence, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 5, also obtain special rights, as follows:

   - a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development; b. the right to basic services;
   - c. the right to equal protection;
   - d. the right to be free from various stigma; and
   - e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER III

## ACTS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 7

Forms of violence include:

   - a. physical violence;
   - b. psychological violence;
   - c. sexual violence;
   - d. neglect;
   - e. exploitation; and/or
   - f. other violence.

#### Article 8

Physical violence as referred to in Article 7 letter (a) is caused by acts or physical actions that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, loss of consciousness and/or cause death.

#### Article 9

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 7 letter (b) is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 10

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 7 letter (c) is caused by:

   - a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
   - b. forced sexual intercourse;
   - c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural manner; and/or d. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or specific purposes.

#### Article 11

Neglect as referred to in Article 7 letter d is caused by:

   - a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of a child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
   - b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
   - c. acts of neglecting a person within the household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and
   - d. acts that result in economic dependency by means of restricting and/or prohibiting from working properly inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 12

Exploitation as referred to in Article 7 letter (e) is caused by:

   - a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
   - b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by other parties to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/or
   - c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse

#### Article 13

Other violence as referred to in Article 7 letter (f) is caused by:

   - a. threat of violence includes any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and
   - b. coercion, includes a situation where a person/victim is ordered or forced to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER III

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE First Part Prevention

#### Article 14

1. Efforts to prevent violence against women and children shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the SKPD that has the main duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment, family planning and child protection.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by:
   - a. formulating Action Programs and activities for Women's and Children's Empowerment in the Strategic Plan;
   - b. establishing work networks in efforts to prevent violence; c. conducting coordination, integration, and synchronization of prevention of violence against women and children.
   - d. establishing a violence prevention system;
   - e. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the rights and protection of women and children against acts of violence;
   - f. providing critical education about the rights of women and children to the community.

Second Part Services

#### Article 16

The provision of services to victims is carried out with the principles of:

   - a. swift;
   - b. safe and comfortable;
   - c. sense of empathy;
   - d. non-discrimination;
   - e. easily accessible;
   - f. not subject to fees; and
   - g. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 17

Forms of services for victims include:

   - a. complaint, consultation, and counseling services;
   - b. assistance services;
   - c. health services;
   - d. social rehabilitation services;
   - e. legal services; and
   - f. repatriation and social reintegration services.

#### Article 18

Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Article 17 letter a include:

   - a. identification or initial recording of victims; and
   - b. consent for action to be taken (informed consent).

#### Article 19

Accompaniment services as referred to in Article 17 letter b include:

   - a. accompanying victims during the examination process and health recovery;
   - b. accompanying victims during the medicolegal process;
   - c. accompanying victims during the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and court;
   - d. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;
   - e. maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including coverage by mass media;
   - f. coordinating with other companions; and g. providing continuous handling up to the recovery stage.

#### Article 20

Health services as referred to in Article 17 letter c include:

   - a. first aid to victims;
   - b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics; and
   - c. referral to health services.

#### Article 21

Social recovery services as referred to in Article 17 letter d constitute services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation against victims and to provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength, and independence in resolving their problems, by means of:

   - a. providing spiritual guidance to victims; and
   - b. psychological recovery of victims.

#### Article 22

Legal services as referred to in Article 17 letter e to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by:

   - a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;
   - b. accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and assisting victims to comprehensively describe the violence they have experienced;
   - c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 23

1. Repatriation and reintegration services into society as referred to in Article 17 letter f aim to return victims to their families and social environment.
2. Repatriation and reintegration services into society as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with relevant government and non-government agencies and institutions.

Third Part Empowerment

### Paragraph 1

Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 24

Forms of empowerment for women victims of violence include:

   - a. job training;
   - b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and c. capital assistance.

#### Article 25

Job training as referred to in Article 24 letter a includes:

   - a. skills training;
   - b. field work practice; and
   - c. apprenticeship.

#### Article 26

Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups as referred to in Article 24 letter b include:

   - a. entrepreneurial skills training;
   - b. facilitation of the formation of joint business groups; and
   - c. assistance in business implementation.

#### Article 27

Capital assistance as referred to in Article 24 letter c includes:

   - a. assistance for work facilities and infrastructure; and
   - b. facilitation of working capital assistance

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 28

Obligations and responsibilities in the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence are a shared responsibility, namely:

   - a. Regional Government;
   - b. Village Government and Kutei Traditional Institution of Village/Sub-district c. community;
   - d. family;
   - e. parents; and
   - f. related institutions both directly and indirectly.

First Part Regional Government

#### Article 29

1. The obligations and responsibilities of regional government as referred to in Article 28 letter a, include:
   - a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government; b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
   - c. forming the P2TP2A institution;
   - d. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
   - e. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; f. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity;
   - g. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
   - h. conducting cooperation in handling acts of violence against women and children with other Regions or related institutions and parties; and
      - i. conducting evaluation of programs and activities for the protection of women and children against acts of violence.
2. SKPD having principal duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment, family planning and child protection shall coordinate, synchronize and evaluate the protection of women and children against acts of violence by involving related parties at least once every 6 (six) months.
3. In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall establish action programs and activities for the protection of women and children in 1 (one) Regional Action Plan as a basis for related SKPD in implementing the protection of women and children from acts of violence.
4. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3), constitutes part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD). (5) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (4), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 30

Regional Governments have the obligation and responsibility to:

   - a. supervise the implementation of services for victims, with minimum service standards; and
   - b. provide funds for victim protection through the APBD and/or other legitimate regional financial sources.

Second Part Village Government and Kutei Traditional Institutions of Village/Kelurahan Article 31 Village Government in the protection of women and children against acts of violence has the role of:

   - a. actively participating in preventing the occurrence of physical violence, psychological violence, sexual violence, neglect, exploitation, and/or other violence against women and children;
   - b. protecting victims;
   - c. securing perpetrators caught red-handed and evidence before handing them over to the authorities;
   - d. based on the victim's request, the Village Government shall hand over the perpetrator to the Police of the Republic of Indonesia, if there are criminal elements; and
   - e. providing emergency assistance.

#### Article 32

Kutei Desa and/or Kutei Kelurahan have obligations and responsibilities in the following matters:

   - a. actively participate in preventing the occurrence of physical violence, psychological violence, sexual violence, neglect, exploitation, and/or other violence against women and children based on customary law provisions;
   - b. carry out resolution of acts of violence against women and children if violations of customary law norms occur;
   - c. participate in conducting supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children carried out by the government and other institutions.

Third Part Society and Family

#### Article 33

The community and family have obligations and responsibilities in the following matters:

   - a. preventing the occurrence of physical violence, psychological violence, sexual violence, neglect, exploitation, and/or other violence against women and children;
   - b. reporting when violence occurs;
   - c. protecting victims;
   - d. providing security for perpetrators caught in the act; and e. providing emergency assistance.
   - f. participating in supervising the implementation of protection of women and children carried out by the government and other institutions.

#### Article 35

1. In the event of a caught red-handed situation as referred to in Article 33 letter d, the community may secure the perpetrator before handing them over to the authorities.
2. The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 28 letter b and letter e, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members;
3. The role of the community as referred to in Article 34 paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, child protection institutions, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, business entities and mass media.

# CHAPTER V

## P2TP2A

#### Article 38

1. The Regional Government in order to provide services and protection to women and children victims of violence establishes P2TP2A.
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is a functional work unit that has the duties, principal tasks, and functions of providing integrated protection to women and children from acts of violence.

#### Article 39

1. The organizational structure of P2TP2A formed by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (1) consists of: a. general chairperson or other equivalent designation;
   - b. secretary;
   - c. treasurer; and
   - d. executive chairperson who oversees the divisions.
2. The Divisions as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, at minimum include:
   - a. complaints service division;
   - b. health service division;
   - c. social recovery service division;
   - d. repatriation and reintegration into society division; and e. advocacy and legal aid service division.
3. The organizational structure of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by the Regent.

#### Article 40

The complaint service sector as referred to in Article 39 paragraph (2) letter a has the following duties:

   - a. conduct interviews and observation of the victim's condition;
   - b. prepare recommendations for follow-up services;
   - c. coordinate and refer follow-up services with related parties; and
   - d. administer the complaint process.

#### Article 41

The health service sector as referred to in Article 39 paragraph (2) letter b has the following duties:

   - a. to conduct examination, treatment, and follow-up care for victims;
   - b. to coordinate the implementation of health recovery and medicolegal matters;
   - c. to conduct medicolegal examination including the collection of evidence from victims and the preparation of visum et repertum;
   - d. to conduct supporting examinations and laboratory testing of evidence;
   - e. to conduct consultation with specialist doctors or make referrals; and f. to prepare case reports.

#### Article 42

The field of social recovery services as referred to in Article 39 paragraph (2) letter c has the following duties:

   - a. providing assistance during the case handling process; b. conducting counseling; and
   - c. facilitating the obtaining of a certificate of the victim's psychological condition.

#### Article 43

The field of repatriation and reintegration into society as referred to in Article 39 paragraph (2) letter d has the following duties:

   - a. to coordinate with relevant agencies for the repatriation of victims; b. to prepare progress reports on the process of accompanying repatriation and social rehabilitation; and
   - c. to conduct monitoring for at least three months after the victim is returned to their family.

#### Article 44

The field of legal aid services as referred to in Article 39 paragraph (2) letter e has the following duties:

   - a. to accompany and defend in every legal service process; and
   - b. to prepare reports on the progress of legal handling.

#### Article 45

Further provisions regarding the development, mechanisms and networked working relationships of P2TP2A with related parties in handling violence against women and children shall be stipulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## SAFE HOUSE

#### Article 46

1. A safe house must be provided by the Social Service to handle cases of violence against women and children that pose a high risk to the safety of victims.
2. The management of the Safe House as referred to in paragraph (1) refers to the applicable laws and regulations.
3. The financing of the management of the Safe House as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be charged to the APBD.

# CHAPTER VIII

## INFORMATION/REPORTING SYSTEM

#### Article 47

1. SKPD whose main duties and functions are in the field of women's empowerment, family planning and child protection shall implement an information/reporting system for the protection of women and children victims of acts of violence which is reported to the Regent.
2. The information/reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 48

1. Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision on the implementation of minimum service standards of P2TP2A.
2. Guidance and Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. coordination;
   - b. guidance;
   - c. education and training; and
   - d. monitoring and evaluation.
3. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a covers aspects related to planning and implementation.
4. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b covers aspects related to planning, implementation, procedures, funding, quality, control and supervision.
5. Education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c shall be conducted in accordance with laws and regulations.
6. Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d shall be conducted periodically.

# CHAPTER X

## FUNDING

#### Article 49

1. Funding for protection activities for victims carried out by the Regional Government shall be charged to the APBD and/or other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. Every SKPD related to programs and activities for the protection of women and children against acts of violence must allocate its budget.

# CHAPTER XI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 50

1. The community participates in the implementation of protection for victims.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of: a. establishing family partners at the Kelurahan/Village level; b. establishing women and children protection units within community organizations;
   - c. conducting independent socialization of women's and children's rights; d. providing first aid to victims; and
   - e. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, and mass media.

# CHAPTER XII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 51

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

##### PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

##### AGAINST ACTS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

Sociologically, acts of violence against women and children have been increasingly rising both in quantity and quality. This is in line with technological developments that can be emulated at any time by perpetrators of acts of violence, thus also increasing in quality. Culturally, the people of Kepahiang who mostly work and live on plantations, consequently their attention and responsibility toward their children are neglected. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Kepahiang Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

Juridically, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts at the Regional level, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in Kepahiang Regency.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a What is meant by "respect and fulfillment of victims' rights" is the guarantee of the fulfillment of victims' basic rights. Letter b What is meant by "justice" is a condition where every person, both men and women, are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to gain access, as well as welfare;

What is meant by "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

Letter c What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment toward victims by not making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups. Letter d What is meant by "the best interests of the victim" is all best actions concerning victims carried out by the Government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, therefore the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Letter e Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 3

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 4

What is meant by "empowerment" is the strengthening of victims of violence to be able to endeavor and work independently after they have been rehabilitated and provided with health and social recovery services. Article 5 Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 6

Letter a Sufficiently clear.

Letter b Sufficiently clear.

Letter c Sufficiently clear.

Letter d What is meant by stigma is a negative characteristic that attaches to a person's individual due to the influence of their environment.

#### Article 18

What is meant by "informed consent" is an agreement/approval of the patient regarding medical measures that will be performed by the doctor on them, after the patient receives information from the doctor concerning the medical measures that can be performed to help them accompanied by information regarding all risks that may occur.

#### Article 19

What is meant by "medicolegal" or medico-legal is related to both health and law.

#### Article 20

Letter a What is meant by "the right to have one's dignity respected as a human being" is upholding human rights. Letter b Sufficiently clear.

Letter c Sufficiently clear.

Letter d What is meant by "the right to obtain information" is the right to obtain explanations, statements, ideas, and signs that contain value, meaning, and messages, whether data, facts or explanations that can be seen, heard, and read that are presented in various packages and formats in accordance with the development of information and communication technology either electronically or non-electronically related to acts of violence.

Letter e Sufficiently clear.

Letter f What is meant by "the right to compensation" includes: economic empowerment, repatriation costs, health insurance, and education or skills training.

Letter g What is meant by "the right to social recovery" includes: access to medical services for physical and psychological recovery, legal assistance to restore civil rights, restoration of good name, and citizenship.

Letter h What is meant by "the right to complaint handling" is the availability of a special integrated service unit by officers.

Letter i Sufficiently clear Letter j What is meant by "the right to assistance" includes among others: psychologists, psychiatrists, health experts, religious counselors, advocates, and family members.
