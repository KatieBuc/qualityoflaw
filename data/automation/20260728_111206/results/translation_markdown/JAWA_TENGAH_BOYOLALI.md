##### PROTECTION AND EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN

##### I. GENERAL

The State through the government and Regional Government in accordance with their authority is responsible for respecting, protecting, and guaranteeing the human rights of every citizen including women and children without discrimination, because women and children are part of Indonesian citizens who have the same rights as others, and the rights of women and children are human rights that must be guaranteed, protected not only by the government but also by the Regional Government.

Although there are guarantees in laws and regulations that protect citizens, especially women, cases involving women continue to increase, not decrease, because cases of violence against women are like an iceberg phenomenon, namely cases reported to women's protection institutions are only a small part of the actual cases. Women victims of violence often feel hesitant or afraid to report the violence they experience, or there are other obstacles such as difficulty in accessing services and lack of information about their rights because most women victims come from poor and underprivileged families, so assistance is needed, assistance costs, and legal consultation are expensive. On the other hand, institutions that handle women's protection are still lacking, especially in areas far from the city center.

Various forms of violence, mistreatment, exploitation, and neglect of Women occurring in the Region require strategic efforts from the Regional Government to provide protection and for women.

Furthermore, in order to realize regional policies to administer government affairs that are the authority of the Region in the field of women's protection based on Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government as has been amended several times most recently by Law Number 11 of 2020 concerning Job Creation, it is necessary to formulate in a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation is a concrete manifestation of the Boyolali Regency Government in providing protection and guaranteeing the fulfillment of the human rights of Women in accordance with its duties and responsibilities.

#### Article 2

Letter a What is meant by "the principle of respect for Human Rights" is that the protection and empowerment of women is carried out as a manifestation of respect for women's rights as part of Human Rights.

Letter b What is meant by "the principle of justice and Gender Equality" is that in implementing the protection and empowerment of women it is based on justice and equality between men and women.

Letter c What is meant by "the principle of non-discrimination" is that in the protection and empowerment of women it is carried out without regard to differences whether in the dimension of region (disaster areas, conflict areas, border areas, underdeveloped areas, remote areas, and others), the dimension of age (productive age, elderly age, and others), and special dimensions (persons with disabilities, workers, and others).

Letter d What is meant by "the principle of victim protection" is that the protection and empowerment of women carried out by the Regional Government, business entities or the community is oriented towards the protection of women as victims.
