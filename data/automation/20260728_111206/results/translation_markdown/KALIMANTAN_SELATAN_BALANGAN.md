# CHAPTER I GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1 In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the Regency Region of Balangan.
2. Regional Government is the regional head as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regional Head hereinafter referred to as Regent is the Regent of Balangan Regency.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the regional people's representative institution of Balangan Regency which is positioned as an element of the Regional Government administration.
5. Office or Agency is an Office or Agency whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the field of protection and empowerment of women and children in the region. 6. Woman is an adult human of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman. 7. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb. 8. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
9. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a direct line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.
10. Parent is a biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother. 11. Protection of women and children is an effort to prevent and handle all forms of acts of violence including neglect and trafficking in persons that violate the human rights of women and children, as well as providing basic and specific services for the needs of women and children in the conditions and situations they experience. 12. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
13. Service is an activity and immediate action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
14. Empowerment of women and children is an effort to strengthen human rights, improve quality of life and increase the participation of women and children in their social interactions.
15. Victim of acts of violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
16. Assistance is an activity and action carried out by an assistant during the service process.
17. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.
18. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in the community.
19. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
20. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards. 21. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related offices, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER II PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 12 (1) Protection of women and children from acts of violence is conducted based on the principles of: a. respect for human rights; b. non-discrimination; and c. protection of victims.

2. Protection of women and children from acts of violence aims to protect, respect, guarantee the human rights of women and children, and pursue the best interests of women and children.

# CHAPTER III SCOPE

#### Article 13 (1) Every woman and child has the right to obtain protection from acts of violence.

2. Acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) comprise actions of:
   - a. physical violence;
   - b. sexual violence;
   - c. psychological violence; d. intimidation;
   - e. forced eviction;
   - f. deprivation of liberty; g. neglect; and
   - h. exploitation and human trafficking.
3. Protection of women and children from acts of violence is the joint responsibility of the Regional Government, community, family, and parents.
4. Protection of women from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (2) includes: a. prevention of acts of violence against women and children; b. services for women and children victims of acts of violence; and
   - c. empowerment of women and children victims of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER IV PREVENTION

#### Article 4 (1) Regional Government, Community, Family, and Parents are obligated to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children to avoid the impact of situations and events.

2. Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is also carried out against: a. acts of neglect; and b. indications or activities that contain the potential for occurrence of human trafficking.
3. Prevention efforts are carried out by taking into account conditions, situations, problems and forms of handling that are appropriate and directed.

#### Article 5 (1) Regional Government in accordance with its authority carries out early prevention efforts.

2. Early prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:
   - a. conducting data collection and assessment of conditions, fulfillment of rights and various aspects related to women and children in social life in the community;
   - b. mapping areas vulnerable to acts of violence based on data collection and assessment as referred to in letter a and providing solutions in the form/manner capable of minimizing vulnerability;
   - c. increasing public legal awareness through the role of community institutions/customary institutions, interfaith communication forums to provide protection for women and children;
   - d. increasing the role of mass media in providing dissemination and information on protection of women and children;
   - e. counseling or socialization of laws and regulations related to protection of women and children on a regular basis;
   - f. opening channels for public complaints, and coordination channels from the village/kelurahan, sub-district and Regional Government levels;
   - g. immediate handling of community reports.

# CHAPTER V SERVICES

#### Article 6 (1) The Regional Government provides services to women and children victims of violence and neglect.

2. Services as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:
   - a. complaints; b. health rehabilitation;
   - c. social rehabilitation;
   - d. social reintegration; and e. legal assistance and accompaniment.

#### Article 7 (1) Women and children victims of violence are placed in safe houses or places whose whereabouts are kept confidential and receive basic fulfillment in accordance with their rights.

2. Child victims of violence who are placed in safe houses are provided with care according to their needs.

# CHAPTER VI EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 8 (1) The Regional Government in accordance with its authority shall empower women and children victims of violence and neglect in the form of improving life resilience and improving economic enterprises.

2. Improving life resilience as referred to in paragraph (1) by providing guidance and assistance for mental spiritual strengthening in the form of religious knowledge.
3. Improving economic enterprises as referred to in paragraph (1) by providing:
   - a. job skills training;
   - b. productive economic enterprise training and joint business groups;
   - c. assistance in developing productive economic enterprises;
   - d. capital assistance; and e. ease of access to information and marketing promotion of productive economic production results.

#### Article 9 Child victims of violence whose parental custody rights have been revoked, their education becomes the responsibility of the Regional Government or foster parents who obtain custody rights from a court decision.

# CHAPTER VII INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 10 (1) In order to provide services to women and children victims of violence, neglect and human trafficking, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A).

2. The Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) is positioned as an independent institution.

#### Article 11 (1) The Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) has the authority to administer protection for women and children.

2. The duty of the Regional Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) is to implement protection for women and children in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 12 The membership of the Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) consists of elements from relevant agencies, vertical institutions, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, researchers, academics and religious figures, customary figures, community figures and activists for the protection of women and children in the region.

#### Article 13 (1) The Regent establishes the Management of the Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A).

2. The establishment of the Management as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Regent's Decree including the secretariat body of the management and the provision of supporting facilities for the implementation of activities.

#### Article 14 (1) The Management of the Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) consists of leadership elements and members stipulated by Regent's Decree.

2. Leadership elements include:
   - a. chairperson;
   - b. secretary; and c. treasurer.
3. Membership elements are divided into the form of:
   - a. service division
   - b. information division;
   - c. assistance division;
   - d. education and training division.

#### Article 15 (1) The Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children in carrying out its functions builds a working network with: a. Police; b. Prosecutor's Office;

   - c. Court;
   - d. Witness and Victim Protection Agency; e. Hospital/Community Health Center;
   - f. Other institutions that have similar objectives.
2. In addition to building a working network as referred to in paragraph (1), cooperation may be conducted with: a. Government;
   - b. Provincial Government;
   - c. Other Regency/City Governments; and d. Non-Governmental Institutions.
   - e. International institutions whose existence is recognized.
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), is set forth in the form of joint agreement.

#### Article 16 (1) The Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children establishes partnerships with the business sector for support in administering protection for women and children in the Regional territory.

2. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1), is in the form of:
   - a. information and opening of employment opportunities; b. provision of education and job training; c. provision of educational assistance for women and children victims of violence; and
   - d. provision of capital assistance for independent businesses.

#### Article 17 The Regent as the person responsible for the protection and empowerment of regional women and children has the authority to make changes to the management or dismissal of members if the existing management or members do not carry out their roles and functions.

# CHAPTER VIII COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 18 (1) The community can participate in providing protection and empowerment to women and children victims of violence, neglect and human trafficking.

2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of:
   - a. providing information to authorized officials or institutions;
   - b. assisting in the rescue of women and children;
   - c. providing basic and or specific needs assistance for women and children;
   - d. forming family partners or small units at the village/sub-district level that actively work together to protect the environment of women and children from acts of violence;
   - e. independently socializing the rights of women and children;
   - f. providing legal assistance and accompaniment.
3. Community participation in providing protection and empowerment of women and children shall be carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 19 (1) The Regent conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children in the region.

2. The community is authorized to actively supervise the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children in the region.
3. The authority of the community as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of:
   - a. providing input; b. submitting questions and obtaining answers from responsible regional government officials;
   - c. requesting realization and concrete activities from those assigned with duties and authority;
   - d. other matters that are constructive in nature and provide solutions.

# CHAPTER X REGIONAL ACTION PLAN

#### Article 20 (1) The Regent may establish a regional action plan related to the protection and empowerment of women and children.

2. The regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (1) is manifested in the joint commitment of all elements in the region to implement the protection and empowerment of women and children and eliminate all forms of violence, neglect and human trafficking.

# CHAPTER XI FUNDING

#### Article 21 (1) Funding for the Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children shall be charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget.

Promulgated in Paringin on January 20, 2016 (2) In addition to funds from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget, funding may come from grants or contributions from third parties that are non-binding.

3. Technically and for the operation of the Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children, it shall be placed in the budget line of the Agency determined by the Regent.
4. The allocation of funds by the Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children shall be based on the established work program.
5. Accountability for the use of funds for the implementation of protection of women and children shall be in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XIII CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 22 This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation. In order that every person may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Balangan Regency.

##### FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Violence against women and children is one form of act that violates human rights, because the right to protection from violence of a woman that has been guaranteed by the 1945 Constitution and the Law on Human Rights has been violated by the perpetrator of violence, which should be respected and enforced to enhance human dignity, welfare and justice for such women. The impact of such violence causes women or children to experience suffering both physically, psychologically, mentally, sexually, so that they need to receive protection and health rehabilitation services, social rehabilitation, repatriation and legal assistance carried out in an integrated manner by victim service providers established by the Government, Regional Government and the community.

Protection and empowerment of women and children currently becomes a focus in development. Women and children are usually made into one unit within the scope of their regulation so that it is not focused and results in ambiguity between women and children. Women can be adults or children, while children can be female or male. In the division of authority as determined in Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government, it has been focused on the respective scope of each form of handling, namely women and children do not become a unity. In that context, regionally related to women and children, regional regulations must be present concerning: a). Fulfillment of Children's Rights and Special Protection of Children, b). Gender Mainstreaming, c). Protection of Witnesses/Victims and Prevention of Human Trafficking, and d). Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children From Acts of Violence. These four regional regulations are a package that must be regulated by the Regional Government in accordance with authority.
