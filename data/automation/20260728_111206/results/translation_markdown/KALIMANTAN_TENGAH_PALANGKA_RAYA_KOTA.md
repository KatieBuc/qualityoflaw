# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by: 1. Region is the City of Palangka Raya.

2. Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Palangka Raya.
4. Regional Apparatus hereinafter abbreviated as PD is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
6. Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
7. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty by law.
8. Woman is a person who is female by sex.
9. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years who has not married, including a child who is still in the womb.
10. Community is individuals, community groups, including parents and family.
11. Prevention is all efforts to eliminate all risks and adverse impacts of violence against women and children.

- 5 -

12. Kota Layak Anak which is hereinafter abbreviated as KLA is a Community that is conducive to developing all social psychological potential of a person optimally.
13. Child Obligations are everything that must be carried out by children according to the function and role of children.
14. Forum Penanganan Korban Kekerasan Terhadap Perempuan dan Anak which is hereinafter abbreviated as FPK2PA is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is tiered at the city and sub-district levels.
15. Pusat Pelayanan Terpadu Pemberdayaan Perempuan dan Anak which is hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is an integrated service center in efforts to empower women in various fields of development, as well as protection of women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons established by local government or community-based.
16. Kelurahan Ramah Anak which is hereinafter abbreviated as KRA is the development of a kelurahan area that unites the commitment and resources of the kelurahan government involving the community and business world located in the kelurahan in order to promote, protect, fulfill and respect children's rights, which is planned consciously and sustainably.

#### Article 2

The Protection of Women and Children is organized based on the principles of: a. respect, fulfillment, protection, advancement, and enforcement of rights; and b. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:

   - a. responsive to rights;
   - b. non-discrimination;
   - c. maintaining confidentiality;
   - d. empowerment; and
   - e. participation.

##### I 11

- 6 - Article 4 The Objectives of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence include: a. ensuring the fulfillment of rights;

   - b. providing justice and equality of rights; c. providing protection and a sense of security; and d. conducting empowerment.

#### Article 5 The scope of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence includes: a. protection of Women;

   - b. protection of Children;
   - c. coordination and cooperation;
   - d. responsibility of Regional Government;
   - e. community participation;
   - f. guidance and supervision;
   - g. evaluation;
   - h. reporting; and
      - i. funding.

## Part One

Obligations and Responsibilities of Parents and/or Family

#### Article 28

Parents are obligated and responsible to: a. care for, nurture, educate, manage and protect family members;

   - b. develop family members in accordance with their abilities, talents, and interests; and c. prevent marriage at Child age.
