# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the Region of East Halmahera Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regional Government of East Halmahera Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of East Halmahera.
4. Regional Apparatus is the supporting element of the Regent and DPRD in the administration of Governmental Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Technical Regional Apparatus is the Regional Apparatus whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the affairs of Women's Empowerment and/or Child Protection.
6. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
7. Woman is a human being of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
8. Women's Empowerment is any effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social capabilities, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective capabilities.
9. Fulfillment of Children's Rights is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
10. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering either physically, sexually, psychologically, and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of a person's freedom.
11. Victim is a woman and/or child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
12. Protection is all preventive actions, services and empowerment to protect and guarantee the rights of women and children from acts of violence.
13. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
14. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will, is or has occurred.
15. Empowerment is the strengthening of women victims of violence to be able to work and work independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
16. Health Rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of victims including physical, psychological and social health so that victims can carry out their roles again properly and reasonably both in the family and in society.
17. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.
18. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of violence from the regional area to their area of origin.
19. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
20. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2PTP2A, is the Regional Technical Implementation Unit (UPTDD) of the Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children integrated into the technical Regional Apparatus of East Halmahera Regency.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The Protection of Women and Children, is implemented based on the principles of:

   - a. respect and protection of Human Rights;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. non-discrimination;
   - d. the best interests of the Child;
   - e. the right to life, survival, and development;
   - f. empowerment; and
   - g. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

This Regional Regulation aims to:

   - a. guarantee the fulfillment of the rights of women and children;
   - b. maintain the integrity of households in order to realize harmonious families;
   - c. prevent violence against women and children;
   - d. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
   - e. provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence;
   - f. provide protection to reporters and witnesses; and
   - g. carry out empowerment of women victims of violence.

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

First Section Women's Empowerment

### Paragraph 1

Women's Rights

#### Article 4

Every Woman has the right: a to live and the right to maintain her life and livelihood; b to form a family and continue descendants through lawful marriage;

c to develop herself through the fulfillment of her basic needs; d to obtain education and derive benefits from science and technology, arts and culture, for the purpose of improving the quality of her life and for the welfare of humankind;

e to advance herself in fighting for her rights collectively to build society, nation and state;

f to recognition, guarantee, protection and fair legal certainty as well as equal treatment before the law;

g to work and receive fair and proper remuneration and treatment in employment relations;

h to obtain equal opportunity in government; i to freedom of association, assembly and expression of opinion; j to protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property under her control, and the right to a sense of security and protection from threats of fear to do or not do something which is a basic right;

k to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and the right to obtain political asylum from another country; l to live in prosperity physically and spiritually, to reside and obtain a good and healthy living environment, and the right to obtain health services;

m to social security that enables her full development as a dignified human being;

n to equality of her position in law and government; o to decent work and livelihood for humanity;

p in efforts to defend the state;

q to communicate and obtain information to develop her personal and social environment, and the right to seek, obtain, possess, store, process, and convey information using all types of available channels; r to embrace her respective religion and to worship according to her religion and belief; and s to obtain education.

### Paragraph 2

Forms of Women's Empowerment

#### Article 5

1. In order to improve the position, role, and quality of women, as well as efforts to realize gender equality and justice in family life, society, nation, and state, women must be empowered.
2. The empowerment of women as referred to in paragraph (1) is directed towards obtaining opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in economic, socio-cultural, political, and legal activities.

#### Article 6 (1) Regional Governments have the obligation and responsibility in efforts to empower women.

2. Efforts to empower women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 7

The implementation of women's empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) shall be carried out through: a provision of skills and job training;

b facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups; c facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;

d facilitation and capital assistance; and e facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 8

Empowerment of women in the socio-cultural field as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) shall be implemented through: a enhancement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of education in stages according to potential to improve social status;

b enhancement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts primarily in the field of reproductive health.

c enhancement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and d facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and the development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 9

1. The implementation of women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) includes: a involvement of women in decision-making at various levels; b provision of opportunities for women to occupy public positions;

c participation in general elections; and d self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.

2. The implementation of women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 10

1. The implementation of women's empowerment in the field as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) includes: a. enhancement of awareness and knowledge in the field of law communication, information and education services; and b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.
2. The implementation of women's empowerment in the field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 11

1. To implement Women's Empowerment as referred to in Article 6, the Regional Government shall organize gender mainstreaming including: a establishing the implementation of gender mainstreaming;

b coordinating, facilitating, and mediating gender mainstreaming;

c facilitating institutional strengthening and development of gender mainstreaming mechanisms in government institutions, women's study centers, research and development institutions, non-government institutions;

d implementing gender mainstreaming related to the fields of development, education, health, economy, law, Human Rights and politics, environment and socio-culture;

e improving the quality of life of women related to the fields of development, education, health, economy, law, Human Rights and politics, environment and socio-culture;

f integrating efforts to improve the quality of life of women related to the fields of development, education, health, economy, law, Human Rights and politics, environment and socio-culture;

g coordinating the implementation of improving the quality of life of women related to the fields of development, education, health, economy, law, Human Rights and politics, environment and socio-culture;

h organizing protection of women especially protection for victims of violence, female workers, elderly women, and women with disabilities, in conflict areas and disaster-affected areas;

i facilitating the integration of protection of women especially protection for victims of violence, female workers, elderly women and women with disabilities, in conflict areas and disaster-affected areas;

j coordinating protection of women especially protection for victims of violence, female workers, elderly women and women with disabilities;

k facilitating the strengthening and development of networks of community institutions or organizations and the business sector for the implementation of gender mainstreaming;

l developing a gender-based information system; and m providing and organizing integrated or non-integrated services for women victims of violence, female workers, elderly women, and women with disabilities.

2. Further provisions regarding the forms of women's empowerment and gender mainstreaming mechanisms as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

Third Part Forms of Violence

#### Article 27

Forms of violence include:

   - a. physical violence;
   - b. psychological violence;
   - c. sexual violence;
   - d. neglect;
   - e. exploitation; and/or
   - f. other violence.

#### Article 28

Physical violence as referred to in Article 27 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or cause death.

#### Article 29

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 27 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 30

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 27 letter c is caused by:

   - a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
   - b. forced sexual intercourse;
   - c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
   - d. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and/or specific purposes.

#### Article 31

Neglect as referred to in Article 27 letter d is caused by:

   - a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of a child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
   - b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
   - c. acts of neglecting a person within their household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
   - d. acts that result in economic dependence by means of restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 32

Exploitation as referred to in Article 27 letter e is caused by:

   - a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
   - b. acts that with or without the consent of the victim include among others prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or capabilities of a person by other parties to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
   - c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

#### Article 33

Other violence as referred to in Article 27 letter f is caused by:

   - a. threat of violence includes: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, image, symbol, or body movement, whether with or without using means that causes fear or restricts a person's fundamental freedom; and
   - b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

## Part Four

Prevention of Violence against Women and Children

#### Article 34

1. Efforts to prevent violence against women and children shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government and coordinated by the technical Regional Apparatus.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by:
   - a. conducting guidance towards harmonious household integrity;
   - b. establishing work networks in efforts to prevent violence;
   - c. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
   - d. establishing a violence prevention system;
   - e. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence; and
   - f. providing critical education regarding the rights of women and children for the community.

#### Article 35

In addition to Regional Governments, prevention efforts as referred to in Article 34 paragraph (1) shall be carried out by:

   - a. family and/or closest relatives;
   - b. community;
   - c. educational institutions; and
   - d. Non-Governmental Organizations.

## Part Five

   - a. speed;
   - b. safety and comfort;
   - c. a sense of empathy;
   - d. non-discrimination;
   - e. easy accessibility;
   - f. no charges imposed; and
   - g. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 37

Forms of handling for women and children victims of violence include:

   - a. complaint services, consultation, and counseling;
   - b. assistance services;
   - c. medical rehabilitation services;
   - d. social rehabilitation services;
   - e. legal services; and
   - f. social reintegration services.

#### Article 38

Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Article 37 letter a include:

   - a. identification or initial recording of victims;
   - b. identification of Cases; and
   - c. p ri;. tujua.n dilaku kan tindakan.

#### Article 39

Assistance services as referred to in Article 37 letter b include:

   - a. accompanying victims during the examination process and health recovery;
   - b. accompanying victims during the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;
   - c. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;
   - d. protecting the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media;
   - e. providing a sense of security to victims; and
   - f. providing continuous treatment up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 40

Medical rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 37 letter c include:

   - a. first aid to victims;
   - b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics; and

#### Article 41

1. Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 37 letter d constitute services provided in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims.
2. The form of services as referred to in paragraph (1) is through social support.
3. Social support as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out through:
   - a. spiritual guidance to victims; and
   - b. psychological recovery of victims.

#### Article 42

Legal services as referred to in Article 37 letter e are to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by:

   - a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;
   - b. accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and assisting victims to comprehensively describe the violence they have experienced; and
   - c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 43

1. Social reintegration services as referred to in Article 37 letter f aim to return victims to their families and social environment.
2. Social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with:
   - a. relevant government and non-government agencies and institutions; and
   - b. village community institutions and/or local community leaders.

# CHAPTER IV

INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN (P2TP2A)

#### Article 44

1. The Regional Government in implementing the protection of women and children from acts of violence, shall establish P2TP2A as a regional technical service implementing unit (UPTDD) integrated into the Technical Regional Apparatus.
2. The implementation of protection and services for women and children victims of violence shall be carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.
3. The implementation of services for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. providing fast and free services to victims;
   - b. implementing protection and fulfillment of victims' rights to health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration and legal assistance;
   - c. cooperating with certain institutions in providing translators and volunteer assistants needed for victims;
   - d. networking with government or private hospitals for treatment and health recovery of victims as well as cooperating with witness and victim protection institutions, social protection houses or trauma centers owned by the government, community or other institutions for the health recovery of victims;
   - e. providing convenience, comfort and safety for victims;
   - f. maintaining the confidentiality of victims; and
   - g. providing fulfillment of legal assistance for victims.
4. Further provisions regarding the establishment, procedures and mechanisms for implementing P2TP2A shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 45

Every Woman and Child victim of violence receives the following rights:

   - a. right to complaint handling;
   - b. right to have their dignity and worth as human beings respected;
   - c. right to obtain protection from family, community, regional government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court;
   - d. right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
   - e. right to determine their own decisions;
   - f. right to special handling related to victim confidentiality;
   - g. right to obtain information;
   - h. right to confidentiality of identity;
      - i. right to social rehabilitation;
   - j. right to assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with statutory regulations; and
   - k. right to spiritual guidance services.

#### Article 46

Women and children victims of violence in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 45, also obtain special rights as follows:

   - a. right to survival;
   - b. right to grow and develop;
   - c. right to obtain equal protection; and
   - d. right to participate in community life.

# CHAPTER VI

## PROHIBITIONS

#### Article 47

2. Business operations as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. discotheques;
   - b. night clubs;
   - c. bars;
   - d. adult karaoke;
   - e. pubs;
   - f. massage parlors;
   - g. steam bath/sauna parlors; and
   - h. other similar business fields.

#### Article 48

Every operator of hotels, motels, lodging houses, tourism guesthouse businesses and similar business activities is prohibited from renting rooms to children without being accompanied by parents or adult family members or supervising teachers/persons in charge in the context of carrying out school activities or other activities.

#### Article 49

1. Every operator of internet service businesses, play stations and types of children's games based on other information and communication technology is obligated to manage and design child-friendly places.
2. Every business operator as referred to in paragraph (1) shall: a regulate rooms/booths with sufficient lighting and not closed off;  b not accept children wearing uniforms and/or during school hours;

c locally block sites containing elements of pornography and pornographic acts; and d limit visiting hours and supervise the use of existing facilities.

# CHAPTER VII

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 50

1. To ensure synergy, balance and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, activities, protection of Women and children, the Government conducts monitoring.
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by technical Regional Apparatus and P2TP2A.
3. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of Women and children.
4. Monitoring is conducted periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of Regional Apparatus implementing policies, activities for the protection of Women and children victims of violence.
5. Monitoring is conducted starting from planning up to the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children every year.

#### Article 51

1. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of Women and children victims of violence shall be conducted at the end of each Fiscal year or if necessary according to needs.
2. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of Women and children victims of violence shall be used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of Women and children victims of violence for the following year.
3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## FINANCING

#### Article 52

Funding for the implementation of Protection for Women and Children, is sourced from:

   - a. Regional revenue and expenditure budget; and/or
   - b. Other legitimate and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 53

1. The Regent conducts guidance and supervision over the implementation of this regional regulation.
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) includes providing implementation instructions, guidance, supervision, monitoring and evaluation of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children.

##### BABX

##### COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 54

1. In the implementation of Protection for Women and children, the community may:
   - a. establish family partners at the Kelurahan/Village level by the community;
   - b. conduct socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
   - c. provide first aid to victims; and
   - d. report to the authorized agency if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector and/or mass media.

# CHAPTER XI

## ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS

#### Article 55

1. Every person or business entity that violates the provisions as referred to in Article 47, Article 48, Article 49 shall be subject to administrative sanctions in the form of: a Written warning;

b Suspension of permit;

c Administrative fine; and d Revocation of permit.

2. Written warning as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a shall be given 3 (three) times with a grace period between one warning and another warning of at least 1 (one) month.
3. Suspension of Permit as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b shall be imposed if written warnings have been given as referred to in paragraph (2).
4. Suspension of Permit as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b may be withdrawn if the person or business entity committing the violation pays an administrative fine of Rp. 30,000,000,- (thirty million rupiah).
5. If within the period specified in the suspension of permit, the administrative fine is not paid, then revocation of permit shall be carried out.

# CHAPTER XII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 56

No later than 1 (one) year, calculated from the effective date of this Regional Regulation, the Implementing Regulations of this Regional Regulation must have been stipulated.

#### Article 57

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that every person may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of East Halmahera Regency.

##### CONCERNING

##### PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in East Halmahera so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations. Thus far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection measures at the Regional level, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation. Regional Regulations regulate protection measures for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for women and children victims of violence in East Halmahera.
