# CONCERNING

# PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

# BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

# REGENT OF TANA TORAJA,

Considering : a. that the Republic of Indonesia is a State that upholds the dignity and worth of every human being, provides guarantees for the rights and security of women and children and freedom from all forms of violence, maltreatment, exploitation, trafficking and neglect;

   - b. that women as mothers of the nation and children as the buds and future generation of the nation are creatures of God Almighty and therefore must be protected and their honor, dignity and self-worth safeguarded in a proper and proportional manner, both legally, economically, politically, socially and culturally, without distinction of ethnicity, religion, race and class;
   - c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, and letter b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children;

In View of : 1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by: Region is Tanah Toraja Regency.

Regent is the Regent of Tana Toraja.

Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the regional government and the regional house of representatives according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.

Regional Government is the regional head as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.

Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution that is positioned as an element of the Regional Government administrator.

Sub-district is a part of the regency area that is led by a sub-district head.

Lembang is another term for village which is part of the regency area that is led by the Head of Lembang;

Lembang Government is the term for village government in Tana Toraja Regency.

Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.

Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife and their children, or father and children or mother and children or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward laterally to the third degree.

Parents are biological father and/or mother or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.

Guardian is a person or body who in reality exercises custodial power as a parent over a child.

Woman is a human being who can menstruate, become pregnant, give birth and breastfeed.

Child is a person who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old, including a baby still in the womb.

Neglected child is a child whose needs are not properly fulfilled, whether physical, mental, spiritual, or social.

Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

Protection of women is all efforts made to ensure the fulfillment of women's rights as a form of respect for human rights.

Empowerment of women is every effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social capacity, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.

Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, and develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

Child with Disability is a Child who has physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory limitations for a long period of time who in interacting with the environment and community attitudes may encounter obstacles that make it difficult to participate fully and effectively based on equal rights.

Child with Excellence is a Child who has extraordinary intelligence or has special potential and/or talent not limited to intellectual ability, but also in other fields.

Adopted Child is a Child whose rights are transferred from the environment of the authority of the Family of Parents, legal Guardian, or other person responsible for the care, education, and raising of that Child into the environment of the adoptive Parents' Family based on a court decision or determination.

Foster Child is a Child who is cared for by a person or institution to be provided with guidance, maintenance, care, education, and health because the Parents or one of the Parents is unable to guarantee the proper growth and development of the Child.

Custody is the power of Parents to care for, educate, maintain, foster, protect, and develop the Child in accordance with the religion professed and in accordance with abilities, talents, and interests.

Child's Rights are part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and fulfilled by Parents, Family, community, state, government, and regional government.

Companion is a social worker who has professional competence in their field.

Violence is any act unlawfully, which results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of a person's freedom.

Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.

Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.

Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a cooperative network that carries out integrated social service functions.

Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a cooperative network that carries out integrated service functions for the protection of women and children.

Community Social Institution is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and/or other community organizations.

Family Welfare Consultation Institution hereinafter abbreviated as LK3 is an integrated social service unit that implements psychosocial problem handling for families to realize family resilience.

Regional Child Protection Commission hereinafter abbreviated as KPAD is an integrated social service unit that implements child protection services to realize the fulfillment of children's rights.

Gender mainstreaming is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of national development program policies.

Discrimination is any restriction, harassment, or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs, which results in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural fields, and other aspects of life.

Safe House (Shelter) is a shelter for women and children including families and witnesses of violence victims.

# CHAPTER II

## SCOPE

#### Article 2

The Scope of this Regional Regulation consists of: implementation of women's protection; and implementation of children's protection;

obligations and responsibilities;

institutional arrangements;

child-friendly regency;

special musrembang for women and children.

# CHAPTER III

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

The implementation of protection of women is based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia encompassing: respect for human rights;

justice and gender equality;

non-discrimination;

protection of victims;

the best interests of women;

respect for women's opinions;

legal certainty; and empowerment of women.

#### Article 4

The implementation of child protection is based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia as well as the basic principles of the convention on the rights of the child covering: non-discrimination;

the best interests of the child;

the right to life, survival, and development; and respect for the views of the child.

#### Article 5

The objectives of implementing protection for women are: to guarantee the fulfillment of women's rights;

to protect and provide a sense of security for women;

to maintain the integrity of the household in order to realize a harmonious and prosperous family;

to guarantee the fulfillment of women's rights;

to provide services to women;

to provide protection to reporters and witnesses; and to carry out empowerment for women victims of violence.

#### Article 6

The objectives of implementing child protection are: to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights;

to protect and provide a sense of security for children;

to maintain household integrity in order to realize a harmonious and prosperous family;

to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights;

to realize quality children with noble character and prosperity;

to provide services to child victims of acts of violence; and to provide protection to reporters and witnesses.

# BAB IV

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN

First Part Rights of Women

#### Article 7

The rights of women are as follows: life, to sustain life and improve their standard of living;

to live peacefully, safely, in peace, happily, prosperously both physically and spiritually;

to enjoy a good and healthy living environment;

to live in a family and continue descendants within the bonds of legal marriage based on the provisions of laws and regulations;

to obtain work in accordance with ability, appropriate conditions and fair and proper wages;

specifically for women with disabilities, the right to obtain facilities and special treatment in public services;

to play an active role in the field of politics and government in accordance with their potential and capabilities;

to obtain information and legal services;

to obtain services to increase their income; and to obtain other rights in accordance with their human dignity and based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

Second Part Women's Empowerment

#### Article 8

In order to improve the position, role, and quality of women, as well as efforts to realize equality and gender justice in family life, community life, national life, and state life, women must be empowered.

The empowerment of women as referred to in paragraph (1) is directed to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in activities: economic;

socio-cultural;

political; and legal.

Pasal 9 The implementation of women's empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2) letter a, shall be carried out through: provision of skills and job training;

facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;

facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;

facilitation of capital assistance; and facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 10

Empowerment of women in the social and cultural field as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2) letter b, is implemented through: improvement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of education in stages according to potential to improve social status;

improvement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts primarily in the field of reproductive health;

improvement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and

   - d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and traditions and development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 11

The implementation of women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2) letter c, includes: the involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;

providing opportunities for women to hold public office;

participation in general elections; and self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.

The implementation of women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 12

The implementation of women's empowerment in the field of law as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2) letter d, includes: enhancement of awareness and knowledge in the field of law through communication, information and education services; and facilitation of access and legal consultation services.

The implementation of women's empowerment in the field of law as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 13

Regional Governments have the obligation and responsibility in efforts to empower women.

Efforts to empower women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 14

1. To implement women's empowerment as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall conduct gender mainstreaming including: establishing the implementation of gender mainstreaming;

coordinating, facilitating, and mediating gender mainstreaming;

facilitating institutional strengthening and development of gender mainstreaming mechanisms in government institutions, women's study centers, research and development institutions, non-governmental institutions;

implementing gender mainstreaming related to the fields of development, education, health, economy, law, human rights and politics, environment and socio-culture;

improving the quality of life of women related to the fields of development, education, health, economy, law, human rights and politics, environment and socio-culture;

integrating efforts to improve the quality of life of women related to the fields of development, education, health, economy, law, human rights and politics, environment and socio-culture;

coordinating the implementation of improving the quality of life of women related to the fields of development, education, health, economy, law, human rights and politics, environment and socio-culture;

providing protection for women especially protection for victims of violence, female workers, elderly women, and women with disabilities, in conflict areas and disaster-affected areas;

facilitating the integration of protection for women especially for victims of violence, female workers, elderly women and women with disabilities, in conflict areas and disaster-affected areas;

coordinating the protection of women especially for victims of violence, female workers, elderly women and women with disabilities;

facilitating the strengthening and development of networks of community institutions or organizations and the business sector for the implementation of gender mainstreaming;

developing gender-based information systems; and providing and organizing integrated or non-integrated services for women victims of violence, female workers, elderly women, and women with disabilities.

2. The forms of women's empowerment and gender mainstreaming mechanisms as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in accordance with laws and regulations.

Third Part Prevention of Violence and Participation in Prevention

### Paragraph 1

Prevention of Violence

#### Article 15

Efforts to prevent violence against women shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the agency having the duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented through the following means: establishing a network in efforts to prevent violence;

conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership work patterns between Regional Government institutions and the community;

building an integrated security system in areas prone to cases of violence against women;

building complaint and service units regarding violence against women at the sub-district, village and/or Lembang level; and awareness campaigns, socialization and education to the community regarding the protection and empowerment of women.

### Paragraph 2

Participation in Prevention

#### Article 16

Participation in prevention efforts as referred to in Article 15, Regional Governments are obligated to include: family;

community; and women's advocacy institutions.

Fourth Part Protection of Women

#### Article 17

Protection of women includes: protection of the personal security of victims or witnesses;

physical and psychological threats;

confidentiality of the identity of victims and witnesses;

provision of testimony during the legal process which is regulated through protection mechanisms.

Protection for women victims also includes the provision of safe houses as temporary shelter to obtain a sense of security during the judicial process and/or outside the judicial process.

The implementation of protection and safe houses is carried out by the Regional Government.

Further provisions on the mechanism for protection of women as referred to in paragraph (2), and the implementation of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (3), shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

## IMPLEMENTATION OF CHILD PROTECTION

## Part One

Rights and Obligations of the Child

#### Article 18

The rights of children are as follows: the right to survival, growth, development and participation in a reasonable manner in accordance with human dignity and to receive protection from violence and discrimination;

the right to a name as personal identity and citizenship status;

the right to worship according to their religion, to think, and to express themselves in accordance with their level of intelligence and age, under parental guidance;

the right to know their parents, to be raised, and to be cared for by their own parents;

the right to obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual, and social needs;

the right to obtain education and teaching in the context of developing their personality and level of intelligence in accordance with their interests and talents;

specifically for children with disabilities, also have the right to obtain special education, while children who have excellence also have the right to receive special education;

the right to express and have their opinions heard, to receive, seek, and provide information in accordance with their level of intelligence and age for their self-development in accordance with the values of decency and propriety;

the right to rest and utilize leisure time, to associate with peers, to play, to recreate, and to create in accordance with their interests, talents, and level of intelligence for self-development;

children with disabilities have the right to obtain rehabilitation, social assistance and maintenance of social welfare standards;

the right to be cared for by their own parents, unless there are reasons and/or valid legal rules indicating that such separation is in the best interests of the child and is a last resort;

the right to meet directly and maintain personal relations regularly with both parents;

the right to obtain care, maintenance, education and protection for the growth and development process from both parents in accordance with their abilities, talents, and interests;

the right to obtain financial support for living from both parents;

the right to obtain protection from abuse in political activities, involvement in armed conflicts, involvement in social unrest, involvement in events containing elements of violence and involvement in warfare.

Pasal 19 Every child has the right to obtain protection from being a target of persecution, torture, or imposition of inhumane punishment.

Every child has the right to obtain freedom in accordance with the law.

Every child has the right to be protected from arrest, detention, and/or criminal acts, imprisonment of children shall only be carried out if in accordance with applicable law and can only be carried out as a last resort.

#### Article 20

A child who is detained due to a legal case, has the right to: receive humane treatment and placement separated from adults;

obtain legal assistance or other assistance effectively at every stage of applicable legal proceedings; and defend themselves and obtain justice before an objective and impartial juvenile court in a closed hearing to the public.

#### Article 21

Every child who becomes a perpetrator or victim of sexual violence or who is in conflict with the law has the right to confidentiality.

#### Article 22

In the event that due to a certain reason the parents cannot guarantee the growth and development of the child, resulting in the child becoming neglected, then the child has the right to be cared for or adopted as a foster child or adopted child by another person or institution in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 23

Every child has the obligation to: respect parents, guardians, and teachers;

love family, society, and care for friends;

love the homeland, nation and state;

perform worship in accordance with the teachings of their religion; and implement ethics and noble morals.

## Part Two

Prevention

#### Article 24

Efforts to prevent violence against children shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the agency having the duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented through the following means: establishing a network in efforts to prevent violence;

conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership work patterns between regional government institutions and the community;

building an integrated security system in areas prone to cases of violence against children;

incorporating local content material on children's rights and the elimination of violence against children as teaching material in teaching and learning activities in early childhood education institutions up to the Senior High School level;

establishing complaint and service units regarding violence against children at the sub-district, village and/or lembang levels, as well as schools;

awareness campaigns, socialization and education to the community regarding child protection.

Third Part Child Protection

#### Article 25

Every child shall receive protection from violent treatment, exploitation, trafficking, discrimination, maltreatment and neglect.

The protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is elaborated in various fields of life, among others: in the field of health;

in the field of religion;

in the field of education;

in the field of social affairs; and special protection.

#### Article 26

Protection in the field of health as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter a, is carried out by the government through the provision of health facilities and by endeavoring comprehensive health services for children, so that every child obtains the optimal degree of health from the time they are in the womb.

Comprehensive health services as referred to in paragraph (1) include promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, both for basic health services and referrals.

Comprehensive health service efforts as referred to in paragraph (2) are implemented by the Regional Government free of charge for families who are unable to afford them.

#### Article 27

Parents and family provide protection to maintain the health of children starting from examination of the child's developmental growth since in the womb.

In the event that parents and family are unable to carry out the protection as referred to in paragraph (1), then the Regional Government is obligated to fulfill it.

The Regional Government provides free assistance and services for underprivileged communities as referred to in paragraph (2), and its implementation is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Pasal 28 The Regional Government provides protection to children from attempts of organ transplantation for the purposes of other parties.

The Regional Government, parents and family protect children from acts of: removal of body organs and/or body tissue of children without regard to the child's health;

buying and selling of organs and/or body tissue of children; and health research that uses children as research objects without the permission of their parents.

#### Article 29

Every child receives protection in the religious field as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter b, to worship according to their religion.

The protection of children in embracing their religion as referred to in paragraph (1) includes the development, guidance, and practice of good religious teachings for children.

#### Article 30

Every child receives protection in the field of education as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter c, to obtain education.

The Regional Government, parents, and family must provide the widest possible opportunity to children to obtain education from an early age.

The Regional Government provides adequate facilities and infrastructure to support the teaching and learning process for children.

Non-governmental institutions may provide teaching and learning facilities and infrastructure for children, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 31

Education as referred to in Article 30 paragraph (1), is intended and directed towards: the development of the child's personality, mental, talents, and physical abilities to their fullest potential;

the development of respect for parents and fellow human beings;

the development of understanding of customary and cultural values in the place where the child grows and develops;

the preparation of the child for a responsible life; and the development of respect and love for the nation and state.

#### Article 32

Regional Governments provide protection for children with disabilities, to obtain opportunities in obtaining special education.

Children who have excellence are given the opportunity to obtain special education.

#### Article 33

Regional Governments shall provide education costs and/or free assistance or special services for children from underprivileged families, neglected children, and children living in remote areas.

#### Article 34

Protection in the field of education as referred to in Article 30 paragraph (1), applies both outside the school environment and within the school environment.

Protection outside the school environment is safeguarding and preventing children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination.

Protection within the school environment is protection from acts of violence that can be committed either by school administrators, by teachers, or by their own friends.

#### Article 35

Special services as referred to in Article 33, in addition to assistance in the form of money, school equipment, may also be in the form of procurement of school shuttle buses, the procurement of which is in accordance with the regional financial capacity.

#### Article 36

Protection of children in the Social field as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter d, includes the maintenance and care of neglected children.

The maintenance and care as referred to in paragraph (1), includes: fulfillment of basic needs consisting of clothing, food, shelter, education, health, security guarantees, freedom to worship according to their religion and equal treatment with other children to achieve social integration as optimally as possible; and fulfillment of special needs for children with disabilities.

The maintenance of neglected children as referred to in paragraph (1), can also be carried out by individuals and non-governmental organizations or community institutions.

Government institutions, and/or individuals, and/or community institutions in providing services as referred to in paragraph (2), may conduct cooperation with various related service units.

Supervision of the implementation of maintenance and care as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be carried out by regional apparatus organizations (OPD) that administer affairs in the social field in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 37

Special protection as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter e, is given specifically to children, namely: children in emergency situations;

children in conflict with the law;

children from minority and isolated groups;

children exploited economically and/or sexually;

children victims of trafficking;

children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse (napza);

children victims of kidnapping;

children victims of violence both physical and mental; and children with disabilities

#### Article 38

Children in emergency situations as referred to in Article 37 letter a, consist of: children who become refugees;

children victims of riots;

children victims of natural disasters; and children in armed conflict situations.

Special protection for children in emergency situations as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 39

Regional government and society provide special protection for children in conflict with the law as referred to in Article 34 letter b, which includes children in conflict with the law and child victims of criminal acts.

Special protection for children in conflict with the law as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented through: treatment of children in a humane manner in accordance with dignity and respect for children's rights;

provision of special accompanying officers for children;

provision of special facilities and infrastructure;

imposition of appropriate sanctions for the best interests of the child;

continuous monitoring and recording of the development of children in conflict with the law;

provision of guarantees to maintain relationships with parents or family; and protection from identity disclosure through mass media and to avoid labeling.

3. Special protection for children who become victims of criminal acts as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented through: child rehabilitation efforts;

protection efforts from identity disclosure through mass media and to avoid labeling;

provision of safety guarantees for victim witnesses;

provision of accessibility to obtain information regarding case developments.

#### Article 40

Special protection for children from minority and isolated groups as referred to in Article 37 letter c, shall be carried out through the provision of facilities and infrastructure to be able to enjoy their own culture, acknowledge and practice their own religious teachings, and use their own language.

#### Article 41

Regional Government provides special protection for children who are exploited economically and/or sexually as referred to in Article 37 letter d and child victims of violence both physical and mental as referred to in Article 37 letter h.

Special protection for exploited children and child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through: socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of children who are exploited economically and/or sexually;

monitoring, reporting, and imposition of sanctions; and involvement of various organizations, Regional Government, companies, labor unions, social institutions and the community.

#### Article 42

Special protection for children who are involved in and become victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic substances, and other addictive substances (napza) abuse as referred to in Article 37 letter f, shall be carried out through supervision, prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation efforts by the Regional Government and the community.

#### Article 43

Regional Government provides special protection for child victims of trafficking as referred to in Article 37 letter e and child victims of abduction as referred to in Article 37 letter g which is carried out through efforts of: supervision;

protection;

prevention;

care; and rehabilitation.

#### Article 44

Regional Government provides special protection for children with disabilities as referred to in Article 37 letter i, through efforts: treatment of children in a humane manner in accordance with dignity and honor, as well as respect for children's rights;

fulfillment of special needs; and obtaining equal treatment with other children to achieve social integration as optimally as possible.

# CHAPTER VI

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

General

#### Article 45

The obligation and responsibility in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence and discrimination is the shared responsibility of: Regional Government;

Lembang Government;

Community; and Parents and Family.

Second Part Regional Government

#### Article 46

The obligations and responsibilities of Regional Government as referred to in Article 45 letter a, include: establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;

conducting cooperation with other parties in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;

allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity;

fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

providing recovery services and social reintegration;

encouraging and increasing community participation; and providing human resources in the implementation of protection of women and children victims of acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination.

In the event that a child becomes neglected as referred to in Article 22, then the Regional Government is responsible and obligated to take over and provide services to the neglected child as referred to in Article 36 paragraph (2) letter a.

Community institutions, and/or individuals may submit an application to the court to become a guardian and/or foster parent for a neglected child.

#### Article 47

In order to implement the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in Article 46 paragraph (1), the Regent shall establish programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in the regional action plan as a basis for regional apparatus in implementing the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination.

The regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (1), constitutes part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD).

Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

Third Part Lembang Government

#### Article 48

The obligations and responsibilities of the Lembang Government as referred to in Article 45 letter b, include: implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination established by the Regional Government;

conducting cooperation with other parties in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination in accordance with the lembang's financial capacity;

fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; and encouraging and increasing community participation.

Fourth Part Society

#### Article 49

The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 45 letter c, are carried out by means of participating in the protection of women and children victims of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination, either individually or in groups.

The role of the community as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, women and child protection institutions, social welfare institutions, community organizations, educational institutions, mass media, and the business sector.

The role of the community in implementing the protection of women and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by means of: providing information through socialization and education regarding the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

providing input in the formulation of policies related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

reporting to the authorities if violations of acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination against women and children occur;

playing an active role in the process of rehabilitation and social reintegration for women and children victims of acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

conducting monitoring and supervision of the Protection of Women and Children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

providing facilities and infrastructure in order to support the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination;

playing an active role by eliminating negative labeling of women and children victims of acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; and providing space for women and children to be able to participate and express their opinions.

## Part Five

Parents and Family

#### Article 50

The obligations and responsibilities of parents and family as referred to in Article 45 letter d, are to safeguard and prevent the occurrence of all forms of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination both against women and against children.

The obligations and responsibilities of parents towards children as referred to in paragraph (1), are to: care for, maintain, educate, and protect children;

develop children in accordance with their abilities, talents, and interests;

prevent the occurrence of marriage at child age; and provide character education and instilling moral values in children.

Guardians and/or foster parents who have obtained a determination as referred to in Article 46 paragraph (3), must be able to fulfill the provisions as referred to in article 36 paragraph (2) letter a.

Guardians and/or foster parents who are appointed as referred to in paragraph (3) must have the same religion as that professed by the Child.

Guardians and/or foster parents as referred to in paragraph (3) are responsible for the child and must manage the property belonging to the child concerned for the best interests of the child.

Guardians and/or foster parents as referred to in paragraph (3), may represent the child to perform legal acts, both inside and outside the court for the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER VII

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 51

In the context of protection of women and children, the Regional Government may establish PPT.

The establishment and development of PPT shall be adjusted to priority needs, regional financial capacity as well as institutional and personnel capacity available in the region.

PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), may take the form of:

##### P2TP2A;

##### LK3;

KPAD; and other institutions as needed and in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 52

P2TP2A as referred to in Article 51 paragraph (3) letter a, is positioned at the regional level and sub-district level.

P2TP2A is under the coordination of OPD or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection.

P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) does not have the authority to formulate policies on women's empowerment and child protection.

P2TP2A is one form of integrated service unit, which functions as:

   - a. an information center for women and children;
   - b. a service center for women and children victims of violence; and an empowerment center for women and children.

#### Article 53

The establishment of P2TP2A is determined by Regent's Decree for the regional level and subdistrict head's decree for the subdistrict level.

P2TP2A membership may consist of elements from the Regional Government, professional organizations, academics, community leaders and other elements as needed.

Further provisions regarding P2TP2A are regulated in Regent's Regulation.

#### Article 54

In the context of handling psychosocial problems of victims, recovery of psychosocial conditions of victims and strengthening family resilience, LK3 may be established in the regions.

The procedures for establishment, main duties and functions as well as service mechanisms of LK3 shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 55

In order to provide protection and fulfillment of children's rights, KPAD may be established in the regions.

The procedures for establishment, main duties and functions as well as service mechanisms of KPAD as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## CHILD-FRIENDLY REGENCY

#### Article 56

To guarantee the fulfillment of children's rights to live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and to receive protection from violence and discrimination, concrete integrated efforts are needed among regional government, society and the business sector through the development of KLA.

Further provisions regarding the development of KLA as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be regulated by regional regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

## SPECIAL MUSRENBANG FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 57

Special musrenbang for women and children is a form of annual meeting or gathering organized specifically for women and children to explore various ideas and inputs for the fulfillment of women's and children's rights in regional development program activities.

Special musrenbang for women and children shall be implemented before the implementation of musrenbang.

Further provisions regarding the implementation mechanism of special musrenbang for women and children as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER X

## FINANCING

#### Article 58

The costs of implementing the protection of women and children are sourced from: Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget;

Lembang Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and Other legitimate and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

## PROHIBITIONS

#### Article 59

Every business operation whose activities may disrupt the growth and development of children is prohibited from accepting child visitors.

Business operations as referred to in paragraph (1), include among others: night clubs which include discotheques, bars and pubs;

adult karaoke;

massage parlors;

steam bath/sauna parlors; and other similar business fields.

#### Article 60

Every operator of hotels, motels, inns, tourism guesthouse businesses and similar business activities is prohibited from renting rooms to children without being accompanied by parents or adult family members or supervising teachers/persons in charge in the context of carrying out school activities or other activities.

#### Article 61

Every operator of internet service business, play station and types of children's games based on information and communication technology is obligated to manage and design child-friendly places.

Every business operator as referred to in paragraph (1) must: arrange rooms/booths with adequate lighting and not enclosed;

not accept children wearing uniforms and/or during school hours;

locally block sites containing elements of pornography and pornographic acts; and limit visiting hours and supervise the use of existing facilities.

# CHAPTER XII

## ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS

#### Article 62

Every person or business entity that violates the provisions as referred to in Article 59, Article 60 and Article 61, shall be subject to administrative sanctions in the form of: written warning; and revocation of permit.

Written warning as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, shall be given 3 (three) times with a grace period between one warning and another warning of at most 1 (one) month.

If after the third warning is given it is not heeded, then revocation of permit shall be carried out.

# CHAPTER XIII

## CRIMINAL SANCTIONS

#### Article 63

Every person or institution that commits acts resulting in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, coercion, or deprivation of liberty against women and children such that a violation of this regional regulation occurs, shall be subject to criminal sanctions in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

##### CONCERNING

##### PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

##### GENERAL

Women and children are creations of God who possess dignity and honor as well as human rights that need to be respected and valued.

Women are pillars of the nation and children are the buds of the next generation of the nation's ideals and struggles. That children, who will later bear great responsibility as the next generation of the nation, need to receive the widest possible opportunity to grow and develop optimally, both physically, mentally, spiritually and socially, so that children have noble character.

Women and children have a strategic role in determining the success of development programs in all aspects of state life. However, due to the still low understanding of the dignity and honor of women and children, this makes them vulnerable to becoming victims of violence and discrimination both within the family and in their environment.

Various acts of violence against women and children continue to increase from time to time. If there is no awareness to provide serious attention and handling, it will destroy household life, society, the nation and the state.

Considering this, it is time that a regulation is needed in the form of a regional regulation on the Protection of Women and Children that is capable of answering while providing legal certainty for the Protection of Women and Children from acts of violence and discrimination.

As a concrete step in the effort to provide Protection of Women and Children in Tana Toraja Regency, through the initiative of the DPRD, a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children was drafted as an implementation of various laws and regulations, including Law Number 39 Year 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection, Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 35 Year 2014 Concerning Amendments to Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection, Law Number 8 Year 2016 concerning Persons with Disabilities and Regulation of the Minister of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection Number 6 Year 2015 concerning the System for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

This Regional Regulation is intended to provide responsibility to the Regional Government starting from the prevention of acts of violence to the handling of victims of violence.

In this regional regulation, the rights of Women and Children, Women's Empowerment, Prevention, Protection of Women and Children are regulated. Also regulated are special handling of neglected children, responsibilities of regional government, responsibilities of Lembang government, up to Community, parental and Family Participation in prevention, protection and handling of Women and Children.

This regional regulation also regulates financing sourced from APBD, APBL and other non-binding sources, which is expected to guarantee the implementation of protection for Women and Children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination.
