# MAYOR OF SURAKARTA

# CENTRAL JAVA PROVINCE

# REGIONAL REGULATION OF SURAKARTA CITY NUMBER 7 YEAR 2024

# CONCERNING

# PROTECTION OF WOMEN

# BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD THE MAYOR OF SURAKARTA,

# COPY

Considering : a. that women are citizens who have human rights that must be protected, respected, maintained, and may not be ignored, reduced, or seized by anyone, therefore they need to receive protection guarantees from acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination and need to be empowered so that they can actualize their potential optimally;

   - b. that in order to realize the fulfillment of women's constitutional rights that are free from acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination, as well as to improve the quality of life of women, they need to receive protection and empowerment from the Regional Government;
   - c. that the implementation of protection of women in the Region has not been able to be carried out optimally and is accompanied by the continued occurrence of acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women, therefore a comprehensive regulation is needed to provide direction, foundation, and legal certainty;

- 2 -

   - d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women;

In View of : 1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

2. Law Number 16 Year 1950 concerning the Formation of Large City Regions Within the Province of East Java, Central Java, West Java and the Special Region of Yogyakarta (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 1950 Number 45);
3. Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as has been amended several times most recently by Law Number 6 Year 2023 concerning Stipulation of Government Regulation in Lieu of Law Number 2 Year 2022 concerning Job Creation into Law (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2023 Number 41, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6856);
4. Law Number 12 Year 2022 concerning the Criminal Act of Sexual Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2022 Number 120, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6792);
5. Law Number 11 Year 2023 concerning Central Java Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Year 2023 Number 58 Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6867);

- 3 - With Mutual Approval REGIONAL PEOPLE'S REPRESENTATIVE COUNCIL OF SURAKARTA CITY and

# MAYOR OF SURAKARTA

# DECIDES:

To Establish : REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Part One

Definitions

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the City of Surakarta.
2. Mayor is the Mayor of Surakarta.
3. Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and the Regional House of Representatives in government affairs that are the authority of the region;
5. Woman is a person of female gender starting from infants, adolescents, adults up to the elderly, who has been recognized by law.
6. Protection is all efforts to fulfill rights and provide assistance to give a sense of security to witnesses and/or victims that must be implemented by institutions in accordance with statutory regulations.

- 4 -

7. Protection of Women is all efforts made by the Regional Government, community, and/or other parties aimed at protecting women from acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination as well as providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights.
8. Empowerment of Women is an effort to obtain access and control over economic, political, social, cultural resources so that women can regulate themselves and increase self-confidence to be able to play a role and participate actively in solving problems, so as to be able to build capacity and self-concept and be free from acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination.
9. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs, which results in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, educational, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
10. Exploitation is an act with or without the consent of the victim which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.

- 5 -

11. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, mentally, psychologically, including intimidation, forced eviction, threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, neglect and obstructing the ability to enjoy all rights and freedoms.
12. Violence Against Women is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering either physically, psychologically, sexually, socially economically and discrimination, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life. any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually and psychologically including certain acts of arbitrary deprivation of freedom whether occurring in the private or public sphere.
13. Online Gender-Based Violence hereinafter abbreviated as KBGO is violence that occurs through digital media or online platforms that targets women, including: comments or messages that are insulting, demeaning, or belittling Women, messages or comments that threaten to commit physical or sexual violence against Women, disseminating victims' personal information (such as addresses, telephone numbers, or other identities) without permission with the aim of intimidating or harming victims, extortion involving threats to spread intimate images or videos of victims unless victims meet the perpetrator's demands, spreading private photos or videos of an intimate nature without the consent of the women in the content, use of technology to stalk or monitor victims continuously - 6 - often with the aim of frightening or controlling victims, and digitally altering images or videos of women for the purpose of insulting, humiliating, or sexually exploiting.
14. Economic Violence is a form of violence in which the perpetrator controls, manipulates, or restricts the victim's access to economic resources with the aim of controlling and dominating the victim, including acts of: taking full control over the victim's income, including salary, savings, or other financial assistance received by the victim, restricting or prohibiting the victim from owning or using money, so that the victim is completely dependent on the perpetrator for financial needs, prohibiting or preventing the victim from working or continuing education with the aim of limiting the victim's financial independence, using the victim's assets or property for personal gain or damaging those assets as a form of punishment, and making all financial decisions without involving the victim, so that the victim has no control over household finances.
15. Physical Violence is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causing death.
16. Psychological Violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or other psychological suffering to a person.
17. Sexual Violence is any act in the form of rape, indecent acts, forced contraception, forced sterilization, sexual torture, sexual exploitation, sexual slavery, electronic-based sexual violence, acts violating decency - 7 - that are contrary to the victim's will, forced prostitution, criminal acts of trafficking in persons intended for sexual exploitation and other forms of sexual violence that are expressly stated as criminal acts of sexual violence as regulated in the provisions of statutory regulations.
18. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband, wife, or husband, wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
19. Household is husband, wife, children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care, and representation of guardianship, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.
20. Victim is a woman who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence and life vulnerability.
21. Women's Rights is a set of rights inherent in a woman or group of people not to experience discrimination, misery or suffering either directly or indirectly committed by state apparatus or by the state or by regional government apparatus or individuals which are natural rights given by God Almighty.
22. Prevention is all efforts to prevent acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women and the recurrence of acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination against victims.

- 8 -

23. Service is all efforts and actions to fulfill the rights of women victims of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women which include complaint services, health, social rehabilitation, legal assistance and enforcement, repatriation, social reintegration, and restitution.
24. Victim Recovery is all efforts and actions to strengthen the capacity of women victims of violence, exploitation and discrimination physically, psychologically, legally, socially, politically and economically so as to obtain a dignified and prosperous life.
25. Integrated Service is the provision of integrated, multi-aspect, cross-functional and cross-sectoral services for victims, victim citizens, and/or witnesses of criminal acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination.
26. Complaint is a series of reporting actions carried out by victims and/or companions to the Integrated Service Center organizers to follow up on reports of acts of violence against victims of violence.
27. Health Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims of violence from the health disorders they suffer.
28. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring the psychological condition and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction to be able to carry out social functions normally.
29. Legal Assistance is legal services provided by companions, law enforcement officers which include providing legal consultation, exercising power, representing, accompanying, defending and taking other legal actions for victims of violence.
30. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of violence from the receiving area/area where violence occurred to the area of origin or family members.

- 9 -

31. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims of violence with family, community, institutions or other social environments that can provide protection.
32. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a technical operational implementation unit in a work unit that organizes government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection, which functions as an organizer of integrated services for women and children who experience violence, exploitation, discrimination, and other problems.
33. Temporary Shelter is a service function to provide or give access to temporary protection places that prioritize security and safety, as well as clothing, food, and supporting facilities for the needs of beneficiaries.
34. Standard Operating Procedure hereinafter abbreviated as SOP is standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from complaints/identification, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, legal services up to repatriation and social reintegration of witnesses and/or victims.

Second Part Principles

#### Article 2

The Implementation of Women's Protection is based on: a. respect for human rights; b. non-discrimination;

   - c. gender justice and gender equality; and d. benefit.

- 10 - Third Part Purpose, Objectives and Scope

#### Article 3

1. The implementation of protection for women is intended to realize the protection of women from acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination.
2. The implementation of protection for women aims to:
   - a. prevent all forms of violence, exploitation, and discrimination against women;
   - b. provide services and recovery for women victims;
   - c. fulfillment of special needs of women; and d. improve the quality of life of women. (3) The scope of regulation for the implementation of protection for women includes:
   - a. women's rights;
   - b. efforts to protect women;
   - c. obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government; d. institutions.
   - e. coordination and cooperation;
   - f. participation;
   - g. monitoring, evaluation and reporting;
   - h. awards; and
      - i. financing.

# CHAPTER II

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

## Part One

Fundamental Rights of Women

#### Article 4

1. Every woman has the right:
   - a. to life;

- 11 -

   - b. to form a family and continue lineage; c. to self-development;
   - d. to obtain justice;
   - e. to personal freedom;
   - f. to security;
   - g. to welfare; and
   - h. to participate in government.
2. The right to life as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a includes:
   - a. the right to sustain life and improve living standards;
   - b. the right to obtain tranquility, security, peace, happiness, physical and spiritual welfare; and
   - c. the right to a good and healthy environment. (3) The right to form a family and continue lineage as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, namely the right to form a family and continue lineage through lawful marriage in accordance with religion and the provisions of laws and regulations. (4) The right to self-development as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, includes:
   - a. the right to fulfillment of basic needs to grow and develop properly;
   - b. the right to protection for personal development, to obtain education, to educate oneself and improve quality of life in order to become a faithful, pious, responsible, noble, happy, and prosperous human being in accordance with human rights;
   - c. the right to develop and obtain benefits from science and technology, arts and culture in accordance with human dignity for the welfare of oneself, the nation, and humanity;

- 12 -

   - d. the right to communicate and obtain information necessary to develop oneself and one's social environment in accordance with religious, social, and legal norms;
   - e. the right to seek, obtain, possess, store, process, and convey information using all types of available means in accordance with religious, social, and legal norms;
   - f. the right to fight for the right to self-development, both personally and collectively, to build society, nation, and state in accordance with religious, social, and legal norms;
   - g. the right to perform social work in accordance with religious, social, and legal norms;
   - h. the right to participate in the planning and implementation of government policies in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
      - i. the right to organize and form organizations in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations; and
   - j. the right to conduct education and teaching in accordance with religious, social, and legal norms.
5. The right to obtain justice as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, includes:
   - a. the right to receive equal treatment without discrimination;
   - b. the right to obtain justice;
   - c. the right to submit applications, complaints, and lawsuits, whether in criminal, civil, or administrative cases; and
   - d. the right to be tried through a free and impartial judicial process, in accordance with procedural law that guarantees objective examination - 13 - by honest and fair judges to obtain a just and correct decision.
6. The right to personal freedom as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e, includes:
   - a. the right not to be enslaved or subjugated, trafficked, and all acts with similar purposes;
   - b. the right to personal integrity, both spiritual and physical;
   - c. the right not to become a research subject without one's consent; and
   - d. the right to freely express and convey opinions with due regard to religious values, morality, public order, public interest, and national unity.
7. The right to security as referred to in paragraph (1) letter f, includes:
   - a. the right to protection of oneself, family, honor, dignity, and property;
   - b. the right to recognition before the law as a human person; and
   - c. the right to be free from threats to do or not do something that violates religious, social, and legal norms.
8. The right to welfare as referred to in paragraph (1) letter g, includes:
   - a. the right to have welfare, either individually or collectively; and
   - b. the right to perform work and receive fair wages in accordance with one's work that is comparable and commensurate with human dignity in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
9. The right to participate in government as referred to in paragraph (1) letter h, includes:
   - a. the right to be elected and to vote in general elections; regional head elections, and/or - 14 - other political position elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting according to applicable laws and regulations;
   - b. the right to pursue a career in government; and c. the right to convey aspirations directly or through intermediaries in government.

Second Part Rights of Women Victims of Violence, Exploitation and Discrimination

#### Article 5

1. Every Female victim of Violence, Exploitation and Discrimination has:
   - a. the right to handling;
   - b. the right of Victims to Protection; and
   - c. the right of Victims to recovery.
2. The right to handling as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a includes:
   - a. the right to information regarding all processes and results of Handling, Protection, and Recovery; b. the right to obtain handling result documents; c. the right to legal services;
   - d. the right to psychological strengthening;
   - e. the right to health services including examination, treatment, and medical care; f. the right to services and facilities in accordance with the special needs of victims; and
   - g. the right to deletion of sexual content for cases of sexual violence using electronic media.

- 15 - (3) The right of Victims to Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b includes:

   - a. provision of information regarding rights and Protection facilities;
   - b. provision of access to information on the implementation of Protection;
   - c. protection from threats or violence by perpetrators and other parties as well as recurring violence;
   - d. protection of identity confidentiality;
   - e. protection from attitudes and behavior of law enforcement officials that demean Victims;
   - f. protection from loss of employment, employment transfer, Education, or political access; and g. protection of Victims and/or reporters from criminal prosecution or civil lawsuits regarding Sexual Violence Crimes that have been reported. (4) The right of Victims to recovery as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c includes:
   - a. medical rehabilitation;
   - b. mental and social rehabilitation;
   - c. social empowerment;
   - d. restitution and/or compensation;
   - e. social reintegration; and
   - f. obtaining skills training for economic improvement for vulnerable women.

#### Article 6

1. The forms of Violence referred to in Article 5 include:
   - a. physical violence;
   - b. psychological violence;
   - c. sexual violence;
   - d. economic violence;
   - e. exploitation; and
   - f. KBGO.

- 16 - (2) Economic Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d includes:

   - a. taking full control over the victim's income, including salary, savings, or other financial assistance received by the victim;
   - b. restricting or prohibiting the victim from owning or using money, so that the victim is completely dependent on the perpetrator for financial needs;
   - c. prohibiting or preventing the victim from working or continuing education with the aim of limiting the victim's financial independence;
   - d. using the victim's assets or property for personal gain or damaging such assets as a form of punishment; and
   - e. making all financial decisions without involving the victim, so that the victim has no control over household finances.
3. Exploitation as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e at a minimum:
   - a. exploiting women's labor by providing inadequate wages, imposing excessive working hours, or not providing safe and healthy working conditions;
   - b. exploiting women for sexual purposes, by coercion, deception, or pressure. Including human trafficking for the purpose of prostitution or pornography production; and
   - c. exploiting women unfairly in economic transactions, such as providing loans with very high interest or manipulating the market for personal gain.
4. KBGO as referred to in paragraph (1) letter f, includes:
   - a. comments or messages that are insulting, demeaning, or belittling women;

- 17 -

   - b. messages or comments that threaten to commit physical or sexual violence against women;
   - c. disseminating the victim's personal information (such as address, telephone number, or other identity) without permission with the aim of intimidating or harming the victim;
   - d. extortion involving threats to disseminate intimate images or videos of the victim unless the victim meets the perpetrator's demands;
   - e. disseminating private photos or videos of an intimate nature without the consent of the woman in such content;
   - f. the use of technology to stalk or monitor the victim continuously, often with the aim of frightening or controlling the victim; and
   - g. digitally altering images or videos of women for the purpose of insulting, humiliating, or sexually exploiting.

# CHAPTER III

## EFFORTS FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 7

The Implementation of Women's Protection includes efforts to:

   - a. prevent and protect women from acts of violence, exploitation, and discrimination in a swift, integrated, and comprehensive manner;
   - b. provide services to victims of violence, exploitation and discrimination;
   - c. empower women;
   - d. fulfill the special needs of women; and e. improve family quality.

- 18 - Second Part Prevention of Violence, Exploitation and Discrimination Article 8 (1) Regional Governments undertake integrated efforts to prevent violence, exploitation and discrimination against women in the Region.

2. Prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination against women is intended to create a safe environment for women and to reduce vulnerability to violence, exploitation and discrimination for vulnerable women groups.
3. Vulnerable women groups as referred to in paragraph (2) include:
   - a. women in disaster and social conflict situations;
   - b. women with disabilities; c. women with HIV/AIDS;
   - d. women migrant workers;
   - e. women heads of households;
   - f. domestic women workers;
   - g. home-based women workers;
   - h. elderly women;
      - i. informal women workers;
   - j. women in situations of intolerance; and k. other vulnerable women groups.
4. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) are implemented by:
   - a. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of prevention of acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination based on partnership patterns;
   - b. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women's rights;
   - c. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system for prevention of violence, exploitation and discrimination;

- 19 -

   - d. providing ease of access to obtain civil rights, education, health, employment, training and funding, welfare improvement and services;
   - e. developing communication media, information, education and public campaigns through media; f. developing educational materials and curriculum;
   - g. developing safe transportation systems and public spaces;
   - h. building early detection systems, security and integrated complaint services in industrial areas, companies, residential environments, educational environments, Islamic boarding schools and other public spaces;
      - i. providing consultation services for families, prospective brides and grooms, students, Islamic students, youth, university students and vulnerable groups;
   - j. encouraging behavioral change of parents and society;
   - k. developing policies related to regencies/cities, sub-districts, schools, Islamic boarding schools, and universities that are safe and friendly for women and integrated with regional spatial planning policies;
   - l. forming and developing cadres, communities, and groups from among youth, students, Islamic students, university students, journalists, social media influencers, educators, and religious leaders for prevention;
   - m. conducting education and advocacy to owners, managers and/or users of social media; n. developing protection systems and special support for vulnerable women groups;
   - o. conducting awareness-raising for perpetrators;

- 20 -

   - p. increasing the capacity of Regional Apparatus, government agencies, educational institutions, religious institutions, health service provider institutions, professional institutions, business sector, service institutions, Islamic boarding schools, youth organizations, legal aid organizations, media, religious leaders, community leaders and communities; and
   - q. forming cooperation networks in prevention efforts against acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination with law enforcement apparatus, government agencies, Regional Apparatus, community institutions, educational institutions, community leaders, religious institutions, health service provider institutions, professional institutions, business sector, service institutions, Islamic boarding schools, youth organizations, legal aid organizations, communities, and media.
5. Integrated prevention efforts against Violence, Exploitation and Discrimination against women as referred to in paragraph (1) are coordinated by the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection as well as governmental affairs in the field of population control and family planning.
6. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (3) letter a may involve Regional Apparatus, agencies, community institutions, educational institutions, community leaders, integrated service posts, family learning centers, religious institutions, health service provider institutions, professional institutions, business sector, service institutions, Islamic boarding schools, youth organizations, legal aid organizations, communities, and media.

- 21 -

#### Article 9

1. Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women as referred to in Article 8, shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus that administer governmental affairs in the fields of:
   - a. health;
   - b. education;
   - c. manpower;
   - d. cooperatives, small and medium enterprises and industry;
   - e. social affairs;
   - f. communication and informatics;
   - g. culture;
   - h. tourism;
      - i. environment;
   - j. transportation;
   - k. trade;
   - l. youth and sports;
   - m. politics;
   - n. public peace, order, and protection of the Community;
   - o. public works and spatial planning;
   - p. public housing and residential areas; q. women's empowerment and child protection; r. population administration and civil registration; s. population control and family planning; and
   - t. other related fields.
2. Prevention of acts of violence by Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in an integrated and continuous manner based on the Regional Action Plan coordinated by the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of Women's Protection and Empowerment.

- 22 - Third Part Services for Victims of Violence, Exploitation and Discrimination

#### Article 10

1. Services are implemented to fulfill the rights of women victims carried out with the following principles:
   - a. respect for human rights;
   - b. non-discrimination;
   - c. confidentiality;
   - d. empathy;
   - e. inclusion;
   - f. empowerment;
   - g. victim-based decisions;
   - h. honesty;
      - i. professionalism;
   - j. integration; and
   - k. sustainability.
2. The provision of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by UPTD PPA.
3. In the provision of services as referred to in paragraph (1), UPTD PPA may cooperate with:
   - a. community health centers, hospitals, and other health service facilities;
   - b. technical implementing units that handle affairs in the social sector;
   - c. detention centers, correctional institutions, and correctional centers;
   - d. police;
   - e. prosecutor's office;
   - f. courts;
   - g. technical implementing units of agencies that administer the protection of migrant workers;
   - h. provincial regional offices of ministries that administer government affairs in the field of religion;

- 23 -

   - i. regional offices and technical implementing units of Ministries that administer government affairs in the field of law and human rights;
   - j. representatives of the Witness and Victim Protection Agency in the regions;
   - k. social welfare administration institutions;
   - l. community-based service provider institutions; and
   - m. other institutions.

#### Article 11

1. Forms of services for the protection of women and women victims of violence include:
   - a. receiving reports and outreach to Victims; b. providing information about the Rights of Victims; c. facilitating the provision of health services; d. facilitating the provision of psychological strengthening services;
   - e. facilitating the provision of psychosocial services, social rehabilitation, and social reintegration;
   - f. providing legal services;
   - g. identifying economic empowerment needs;
   - h. identifying temporary shelter needs for Victims and Families of Victims that need to be immediately fulfilled;
      - i. facilitating the needs of Victims with Disabilities;
   - j. coordinating and cooperating on the fulfillment of the Rights of Victims with other Institutions; and
   - k. monitoring the fulfillment of the Rights of Victims by law enforcement apparatus during the judicial process.

- 24 - (2) Implementation of forms of services for the protection of women and women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Fourth Part Women's Empowerment

#### Article 12

1. Empowerment of Women victims of acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination shall be carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. Empowerment of Women victims of acts of violence, exploitation and discrimination as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in activities:
   - a. health;
   - b. education;
   - c. economy;
   - d. law;
   - e. employment;
   - f. politics;
   - g. governance;
   - h. social;
      - i. culture; and
   - j. environment.

#### Article 13

1. The Implementation of Women's Empowerment in the health sector as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (2) letter a shall be carried out by Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the health sector.

- 25 - (2) The form of empowerment in the health sector as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of reproductive health services and healthy, safe sexuality, free from coercion and/or violence.

3. Health services as referred to in paragraph (2) shall include:
   - a. health services during the period before pregnancy, pregnancy, childbirth and after giving birth;
   - b. pregnancy regulation, contraceptive services and sexual health;
   - c. reproductive health services; and
   - d. services and provision of adequate lactation rooms in every Regional government institution, public facilities and industries.
4. Health services as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be implemented through promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative approaches.

#### Article 14

1. Women's Empowerment in the field of education as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (2) letter b is implemented by Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of education in the form of organizing formal and non-formal education.
2. Non-formal education as referred to in paragraph (1) aims to improve women's capabilities.
3. Non-formal education as referred to in paragraph (2) includes the provision of skills and job training.

#### Article 15

The Implementation of Women's Empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (2) letter c shall be carried out by Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of micro - 26 - small and medium enterprises and other related agencies in the form of:

   - a. provision of skills and job training; b. facilitation of the formation and strengthening of productive economic business groups; and
   - c. facilitation of the development of marketing networks.

#### Article 16

The Implementation of Women's Empowerment in the field of law as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter d shall be carried out by Regional Apparatus and other related institutions in the form of:

   - a. increasing awareness and knowledge in the field of law through communication, information and education services; and
   - b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.

#### Article 17

The Implementation of Women's Empowerment in the field of employment as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (2) letter e shall be carried out by Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of labor and other related agencies in the form of:

   - a. women have equal opportunities without discrimination to obtain employment; and b. employers who employ female workers, provide access and skills training.

- 27 -

#### Article 18

1. The Implementation of Women's Empowerment in the field of politics and governance as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (2) letter f and letter g shall be carried out by Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of national unity and politics, Regional Apparatus and other related agencies in the form of:
   - a. development of women's capacity;
   - b. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions;
   - c. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;
   - d. provision of access for women to hold public office; and
   - e. participation in general elections, regional head elections and other political positions in the region.
2. The Regional Government provides guarantees to women to have the right to vote and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, and/or elections for other political positions based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
3. The Regional Government provides opportunities for women to be appointed as Regional Government officials and occupy strategic positions in regional government.
4. The Regional Government and/or Political Parties are responsible for providing political education for women.

- 28 -

#### Article 19

The Implementation of Women's Empowerment in the social field as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (2) letter h shall be carried out by Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the social field and other related agencies in the form of:

   - a. awareness efforts and self-capacity building in the social field;
   - b. provision and development of access for women to participate in the social field;

##### CONCERNING

##### PROTECTION OF WOMEN

##### I. GENERAL

Violence against women constitutes a violation of human rights, therefore it is necessary to protect their dignity and honor and to guarantee their right to life in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination. The fate of women victims of violence must be given attention by the regional government. The high number of cases of violence against women is often caused by factors that develop in society, for example low economic levels, education, environments located in the industrial sector.

Therefore, victims of violence such as this need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights. For the empowerment of victims of violence, the regional government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT) which is a generic term that can be used by various institutions that provide services for victims of violence such as the Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment (P2TP), Safe House, Social Protection House (RPS), Trauma Center Protection House (RPTC), Women's Social Protection House (RPSW), Shelter House, etc. Which specifically have the main duties and functions for the empowerment of women and children victims of violence.

The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement efforts to protect women victims of violence through: implementing protection policies for women victims of violence applied by the government, establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women victims of violence, establishing PPT, guaranteeing the implementation of ease of service to victims, seeking effectiveness and efficiency for the victim recovery process, and seeking the creation of cooperation and coordination in victim recovery efforts.
