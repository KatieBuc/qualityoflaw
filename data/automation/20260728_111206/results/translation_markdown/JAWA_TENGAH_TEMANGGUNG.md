# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Temanggung Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Temanggung.
4. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of Temanggung.
5. Regional Apparatus is the supporting element of the Regional Head and Regional House of Representatives in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling rights carried out by families, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
7. Protection of Women is all activities to guarantee and protect women and their rights, so that they can live justly, in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
8. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or hardship for women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.
10. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
11. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes or certain purposes.
12. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that results in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
13. Victim is a woman including Female Workers who work abroad and children who experience suffering and/or hardship either directly or indirectly as a result of such violence.
14. Women's Empowerment is any effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social abilities, knowledge and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.
15. Gender is the difference between men and women in roles, functions, rights, responsibilities, and behavior formed by social values, culture and customs of community groups that can change according to time and local conditions. Responsibilities and behavior formed by social values, culture and customs of community groups that can change according to time and local conditions.
16. Gender equality and justice is equal opportunity between men and women to participate in the development process, equal access in all forms of services, and have balanced social and economic status.
17. Gender Mainstreaming hereinafter abbreviated as PUG is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, budgeting, monitoring and evaluation of policies, programs is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, budgeting, monitoring and evaluation of policies, programs and development activities in the region
18. Gender Mainstreaming Working Group hereinafter abbreviated as Pokja PUG is a consultation forum for implementers and drivers of Gender Mainstreaming from various agencies/institutions in the region.
19. Integrated services is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of violence including criminal acts of human trafficking carried out jointly by related agencies or institutions as a unified implementation of health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
20. Protection of Women is any act aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
21. legal act is any act or action of a legal subject that has legal consequences and the legal consequences are indeed desired by the legal subject.
22. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.
23. Companion is a person who has the expertise to conduct counseling, therapy, and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
24. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a Complaint Center that organizes integrated services for women and children victims of violence
25. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

#### Article 2

The principles in the Empowerment and Protection of Women are:

   - a. respect for Human Rights;
   - b. gender equality;
   - c. gender justice; and
   - d. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

Empowerment and Protection of Women aims to:

   - a. guarantee the fulfillment of women's rights so they can live and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth;
   - b. provide Gender Justice and Equality, improve the quality of life of women through increasing the institutional capacity of Empowerment and Protection of Women in the regions, including the development of gender and child data systems; and
   - c. provide protection of women's rights from various forms of violence and other discriminatory treatment.

#### Article 4

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:

   - a. rights and obligations of women;
   - b. obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government;
   - c. protection of women;
   - d. empowerment of women;
   - e. protection of women victims of violence;
   - f. community participation and involvement;
   - g. monitoring, evaluation and reporting; and
   - h. funding.

#### Article 5

In order to realize the objectives as referred to in Article 3 letter a, efforts shall be made through:

   - a. providing access for women to education services, health, and other strategic fields;
   - b. encouraging the involvement of women in the development process; c. providing knowledge, skills, character values, morals and family resilience; and
   - d. encouraging programs that can increase the independence of women in the economic, political, legal, social, cultural fields and other strategic fields.

#### Article 6

In order to realize the objectives as referred to in Article 3 letter b, efforts shall be made through:

   - a. establishment, development, and capacity strengthening of institutions for the protection of women and children including service units for complaints of violence against women and children, as well as legal aid services;
   - b. improvement of the quality of human resources management;
   - c. strengthening the capacity of PUG and child institutions; and
   - d. strengthening and development of gender and child data systems.

#### Article 7

In order to realize the objectives as referred to in Article 3 letter c, the Regional Government shall make promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative efforts in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 8

Promotive efforts as referred to in Article 7 are as follows: a. strengthening coordination mechanisms and work networks of service units in efforts to handle cases of violence;

   - b. providing Communication, Information and Education materials related to prevention and handling of violence; and
   - c. conducting socialization, advocacy and social campaigns in the context of prevention and handling of violence.

#### Article 9

Preventive efforts as referred to in Article 7 are as follows:

   - a. conducting legal awareness education for the community, especially women and children;
   - b. conducting massive and sustainable movements involving the community in prevention and handling of violence;
   - c. instilling character values, morals, and family resilience; and
   - d. involving the role and participation of the community in women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 10

Curative efforts as referred to in Article 7 are as follows: a. optimizing technical service units related to complaints of violence against women and children;

   - b. providing adequate facilities and infrastructure for handling health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance; and
   - c. conducting handling of victims of crime and violence quickly, precisely and accurately by law enforcement officials.

#### Article 11

Rehabilitation efforts as referred to in Article 7 are as follows: a. providing accompanying personnel for victims of crime and violence which includes among others psychologists, psychiatrists, religious counselors/spiritual companions, lawyers, and medical personnel;

   - b. strengthening work networks and coordination in the reintegration process as well as the return of victims to their families and/or social environment.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 12

1. Every woman has the right to:
   - a. live and maintain life and improve her standard of living;
   - b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue her lineage;
   - c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life in order to become a human being who is faithful, pious, responsible, of noble character, happy, and prosperous in accordance with human rights;
   - d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;
   - e. be free from slavery or enslavement and threats;
   - f. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;
   - g. obtain welfare and a decent life;
   - h. participate in politics;
      - i. perform legal acts;
   - j. freely choose a partner in her marriage;
   - k. choose the work she likes and is also entitled to fair employment conditions; and
   - l. receive fair wages in accordance with her work that is comparable and commensurate with her human dignity (2) The right to participate in politics as referred to in paragraph (1) letter h is, women have the right to:
   - a. vote and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret balloting; and
   - b. be appointed to any government position.

Second Part Rights of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 13

Every female victim of violence has:

   - a. the right to have her dignity and worth as a human being respected; b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
   - c. the right to determine her own decisions;
   - d. the right to obtain information;
   - e. the right to confidentiality;
   - f. the right to compensation;
   - g. the right to social rehabilitation;
   - h. the right to complaint handling;
      - i. the right of the victim and her family to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and
   - j. the right to assistance.

Third Part Obligations of Women

#### Article 14

Every woman has the obligation to:

   - a. uphold her dignity and honor as a woman;
   - b. carry out her duties and responsibilities as a wife, mother, child and member of society properly and responsibly; and
   - c. comply with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 15

1. Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility for the implementation of empowerment and protection of women.
2. The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;
   - b. determination of implementation guidelines;
   - c. provision of services; and
   - d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.
3. The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 16

1. In implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and empowerment of women, Regional Governments may cooperate with the community/community institutions.
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 17

The Regent in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women shall undertake efforts to:

   - a. coordinate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities;
   - b. protect women among Regional Apparatus;
   - c. cooperate with Regencies/Cities within one Province, and cooperate among Regencies/Cities in other Provinces in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
   - d. strengthen the institutional capacity of PUG for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women;
   - e. facilitate protection services for women;
   - f. provide protection services for women;
   - g. implement affirmative action for the protection of women; and
   - h. formulate a data system for the protection of women.

#### Article 18

1. Strengthening of PUG institutional capacity as referred to in Article 17 letter d and in efforts to accelerate the institutionalization of PUG in all Regional Apparatus shall be implemented by establishing a PUG Working Group.
2. The coordinator of PUG implementation is the Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment affairs.
3. In its implementation, the PUG Working Group shall establish a Technical Team and Focal Point.
4. The implementation of PUG institutional strengthening and the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women shall be further stipulated in a Regent Regulation.

Health Protection

Second Part Employment Protection

#### Article 20

# CHAPTER VI

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 22

In order to improve the position, role, and quality of women, as well as efforts to realize equality and Gender Justice in family life, community life, national life, and state life, women must be empowered.

#### Article 23

The Empowerment of Women is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings so as to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:

   - a. economy;
   - b. social culture;
   - c. politics; and
   - d. law.

#### Article 24

The Implementation of Women's Empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 23 letter a, shall be carried out through:

   - a. provision of skills and job training;
   - b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups; c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups; d. facilitation and capital assistance; and
   - e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 25

Empowerment of women in the social and cultural field as referred to in Article 23 letter b, shall be implemented through:

   - a. improvement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of education in stages according to potential to improve social status;
   - b. improvement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts primarily in the field of reproductive health;
   - c. improvement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and
   - d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and traditions and development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 26

1. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the political field as referred to in Article 23 letter c, includes:
   - a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;
   - b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;
   - c. participation in general elections; and
   - d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions (2) The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 27

1. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the field of law as referred to in Article 22 letter d, includes:
   - a. increasing awareness and knowledge in the field of law through communication, information and education services; and
   - b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.
2. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the field of law as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 28

1. Every Victim has the right to obtain Protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage and confidential handling from individuals, groups or institutions, both Regional Government and community.
2. In the event of violence, every Victim has the right to obtain health services and assistance, both psychologically and legally, and to obtain guarantees of their rights related to their status as women.

Second Part Obligations and Efforts of Regional Government

#### Article 29

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement efforts to prevent the occurrence of Violence against Women in the form of: a. collecting data and information about victims of violence and Laws and Regulations;
   - b. conducting education on anti-Violence against Women values;
   - c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of Protection for victims of violence; d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of Protection for victims of violence; and
   - e. preparing reports on the implementation of Protection for women victims of violence.
2. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women, the Regional Government has the obligation to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
   - a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for Victims by involving community elements; and
   - b. encouraging public awareness of the importance of Protection for Victims.
3. The Regional Government in carrying out its obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.
4. To prevent the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women, the Regional Government undertakes efforts as follows:
   - a. conducting counseling and socialization regarding matters relating to Violence against Women;
   - b. conducting socialization of women's rights;
   - c. seeking to improve education for women;
   - d. opening skills courses for women to support the economic needs of the family;
   - e. creating employment opportunities for women;
   - f. providing counseling to immediately report to local authorities if Violence against Women occurs;
   - g. empowering Social Community Organizations and women's organizations to increase women's knowledge and insight;
   - h. establishing Women's Protection Complaint Posts; and i. forming networks in efforts to prevent violence.

Third Part Implementation of Protection

#### Article 30

1. The Regional Government facilitates the establishment of Women's Protection institutions.
2. The Women's Protection institution as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by forming P2TP2A.
3. Further provisions regarding P2TP2A are regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 31

The implementation of the duties and functions of P2TP2A in the Region is coordinated by the Regional Apparatus responsible for women's empowerment affairs.

#### Article 32

The provision of services to Victims shall be conducted free of charge, promptly, safely, with empathy, non-discriminatorily, easily accessible, and with a guarantee of confidentiality.

#### Article 33

1. P2TP2A may consult and coordinate with the Ministry that administers the affairs of women's empowerment and child protection by first coordinating with Regional Apparatus or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection at the Provincial level.
2. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out in the form of: a. coordination meetings with the working network;
   - b. consultation;
   - c. submission of data and information; and/or
   - d. follow-up on case handling.

Fourth Part Forms of Violence and Protection

#### Article 34

1. Forms of Violence against Women include:
   - a. physical violence;
   - b. sexual violence;
   - c. economic violence; and
   - d. psychological violence.
2. The form of physical violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, unconsciousness and or causes death.
3. The form of sexual violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, is sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
4. The form of economic violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, is the neglect of women in the form of not providing proper livelihood, care or maintenance.
5. The form of psychological violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 35

1. Every Victim of violence must receive Protection in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.
2. Protection for Victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided to Regional Residents who experience violence in the Region or outside the Region.
3. Protection for Victims of violence in addition to being provided to Regional residents as referred to in paragraph (2) shall also be provided to non-residents of the Region for violence occurring in the Region.

#### Article 36

1. Forms of Protection against Violence toward Women include: a. Medical protection;
   - b. Legal protection;
   - c. Medico legal protection (forensic medicine);
   - d. Economic protection; and
   - e. Psychological protection.
2. The form of medical protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, is in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of the Victim carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.
3. The legal form as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, is in the form of services and assistance to help the Victim in undergoing legal and judicial processes.
4. The form of medico legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, is in the form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field.
5. The form of economic protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, is in the form of services for skills and to provide economic access so that victims of violence can be independent.
6. The form of psychological protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e is in the form of psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving assistance in order to restore the traumatic condition of the Victim including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide.

#### Article 37

To provide Protection as referred to in Article 35, the Regional Government may undertake efforts to:

   - a. provide services and handling as quickly as possible to witnesses and/or Victims;
   - b. provide ease, comfort, safety, and free of charge for witnesses and/or Victims;
   - c. maintain confidentiality of witnesses and/or Victims;
   - d. guarantee justice and legal certainty for witnesses and/or Victims; e. provision of special service rooms at Police Offices;
   - f. provision of apparatus, health workers, social workers, and spiritual counselors; g. creation and development of cooperation systems and mechanisms for service programs involving parties easily accessible to Victims; h. provide Protection for companions, witnesses, families, and friends of Victims;
      - i. provide Protection for female workers/laborers in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

##### I. GENERAL

The Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia mandates Regional Governments to regulate and manage their own regions for the realization of community welfare through improvement of services, empowerment and community participation. One of the authorities of Regional Government is to make a regional policy with the aim of improving the welfare of the community in their region.

In addition, the constitution of the Republic of Indonesia has also mandated in Article 28D Paragraph (1) of the 1945 Constitution that every person has the right to recognition, guarantee, protection, and fair legal certainty as well as equal treatment before the law. Article 28B Paragraph (2) of the 1945 Constitution also states that every child has the right to survival, growth and development and has the right to protection from violence and discrimination. This shows that women should be protected by the state, especially against violence because their rights are already regulated in the constitution.

Protection carried out by the Government towards women is concretely implemented, namely by the issuance of Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women, hereinafter abbreviated as CEDAW.

The government's efforts in protecting its citizens, especially women, are also clearly implied in the provisions of Article 3 paragraph (2) of Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights which states that: "every person has the right to recognition, guarantee, protection and fair legal treatment as well as obtaining legal certainty and equal treatment before the law".

Violence and trafficking against women have been regulated in Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, and Law Number 21 of 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Criminal Act of Human Trafficking.

Therefore, it is necessary to carry out efforts to prevent and eradicate all forms of discrimination and violence against women, especially for the Temanggung Regency area. The Government of Temanggung Regency is one of the most authorized parties in carrying out women's empowerment in order to provide guarantees for justice and gender equality, through efforts to build commitment to integrate gender responsive budgets in programs and activities.

This Regional Regulation is expected to be able to encourage improvement efforts on access, control, participation and benefits for justice and gender equality as well as the physical and mental conditions of women victims of violence.
