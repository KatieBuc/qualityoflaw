# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Central Government hereinafter referred to as Government is the President of the Republic of Indonesia who holds the governmental power of the State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
2. Region is the Regency of Ogan Komering Ulu.
3. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
4. Regent is the Regent of Ogan Komering Ulu.
5. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb as referred to in Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.
6. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
7. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
8. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
9. Implementation of protection is a series of activities aimed at preventing the occurrence of violence, providing protection and recovery services and social reintegration, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation carried out by the Commission for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children, and the Integrated Service Center.
10. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
11. Violence Against Children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
12. Victim of gender-based violence is a person who due to their gender experiences physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
13. Child Victim of Violence is a child who receives harsh treatment either physically, psychologically, economically, socially, sexually, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
14. Victim Recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, whether physically, psychologically, socially, economically or sexually.
15. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the purpose of self-empowerment of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems they face.
16. Commission for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children hereinafter referred to as KPK2BGA is the Commission for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Children of Ogan Komering Ulu Regency which is established by the Regent and is a non-structural commission.
17. Integrated Services is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unified implementation, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
18. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes who can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.
19. Safe House (shelter) is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:

   - a. non-discrimination;
   - b. best interests of the victim;
   - c. gender justice and equality;
   - d. victim protection;
   - e. human survival;
   - f. survival, growth, and development of children;
   - g. respect for the opinion of children;
   - h. transparency;
      - i. integration;
   - j. not blaming the victim;
   - k. empowering;
   - l. victim confidentiality; and
   - m. decision-making in the hands of the victim.

#### Article 3

The objectives of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children are:

   - a. to prevent all forms of gender-based violence and violence against children, occurring within the household and/or community;
   - b. to provide protection;
   - c. to provide legal assistance;
   - d. to pursue recovery and social reintegration; and
   - e. to increase community participation.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

1. Every victim of gender-based violence and child victim of violence has the right to:
   - a. obtain protection for personal, family, and property security, and freedom from threats related to testimony that will be, is being, or has been given;
   - b. participate in the process of selecting and determining the form of protection and security support;
   - c. freedom from ensnaring questions;
   - d. obtain information regarding case developments and court decisions;
   - e. obtain services that are fast, appropriate, comfortable, and in accordance with needs;
   - f. recovery and social reintegration;
   - g. obtain legal assistance, psychological support, spiritual guidance, economic, social and interpreter services.
2. The rights of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 5

1. The obligations of Regional Government include:
   - a. preventing the occurrence of violence;
   - b. providing protection for victims of violence;
   - c. providing recovery services and social reintegration;
   - d. encouraging and increasing community participation;
   - e. conducting cooperation with service providers in efforts to prevent, protect and recover victims of violence.
2. To implement the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government has the authority to:
   - a. formulate policies and programs on the elimination of gender-based and child violence;
   - b. establish integrated services and KPK2BGA;
   - c. facilitate the implementation of integrated services and KPK2BGA activities;
   - d. provide facilities and infrastructure;
   - e. increase the capacity of service provider institutions;
   - f. conduct coordination and cooperation in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based and child violence;
   - g. encourage community participation;
   - h. conduct monitoring and evaluation.
3. The regional government in implementing the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

# BAB V

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

First Part Institutional

#### Article 6

In implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence, the Regional Government is assisted by:

   - a. Integrated Services; and
   - b. KPK2BGA.

Second Part Integrated Services

#### Article 7

1. Integrated services as referred to in Article 6 letter a shall be established by the Regional Government in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.
2. The establishment of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of members from regional government elements, non-governmental institutions, health service institutions, law enforcement officers, professional workers, volunteer companions, social workers, religious leaders, safe houses (shelters), and social rehabilitation centers.

#### Article 8

The integrated service duties as referred to in Article 7 are to endeavor prevention, recovery and social reintegration, provide legal protection, conduct coordination and, endeavor to increase community participation, as well as monitoring and reporting.

### Paragraph 1

Prevention Efforts

#### Article 9

Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 8 include:

   - a. conducting communication, information, and education about gender-based and child violence;
   - b. socialization of laws and regulations related to gender-based and child violence.

### Paragraph 2

Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts

#### Article 10

Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts as referred to in Article 8, include:

   - a. providing physical recovery at health service institutions; b. providing medicolegal services;
   - c. assisting the repatriation of victims;
   - d. providing temporary protection in safe houses (shelters); e. providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;
   - f. providing spiritual guidance services;
   - g. conducting family and community preparation, economic empowerment, and return to school and or other educational institutions.

### Paragraph 3

Legal Protection

#### Article 11

Legal protection as referred to in Article 8 includes:

   - a. providing protection in a safe house (shelter);
   - b. providing assistance in the legal process at the high court level; and
   - c. providing special legal protection for child victims of violence which can be carried out through the appointment of guardianship in accordance with applicable regulations.

### Paragraph 4

Coordination and Cooperation

#### Article 12

Coordination and cooperation as referred to in Article 8, includes:

   - a. conducting coordination and cooperation in handling violence cases with integrated services at the regency/city level;
   - b. conducting coordination and cooperation with institutions providing services for victims of gender-based violence and children;
   - c. conducting coordination and consultation with the Regional Government.

### Paragraph 5

Efforts to Increase Community Participation

#### Article 13

Increasing community participation as referred to in Article 8 is carried out by means of:

   - a. gender and children;
   - b. encouraging the community to actively participate in providing information and reporting the occurrence of gender-based and child violence;
   - c. fostering local wisdom in handling gender-based and child violence;
   - d. organizing the strengthening of community groups in handling gender-based and child violence; and
   - e. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to gender-based and child violence.

### Paragraph 6 Monitoring and Reporting

#### Article 14

Monitoring and reporting as referred to in Article 8 includes monitoring, documentation and reporting of cases of gender-based violence and child violence.

#### Article 15

The implementation of integrated services shall be coordinated by the Regional Apparatus Work Unit responsible for women's empowerment and child protection.

## Part Three

### KPK2BGA

#### Article 16

1. KPK2BGA as referred to in Article 6 letter b, is established by the Regional Government in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.
2. The establishment of KPK2BGA as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of members from elements of SKPD responsible for Health affairs, SKPD responsible for Social and Labor affairs, Police, community leaders, religious leaders, non-governmental organization activists, academics, and practitioners.

#### Article 17

The duties of KPK2BGA as referred to in Article 16 are:

   - a. to conduct mediation of disputes between institutions providing integrated services for gender-based and child violence;
   - b. to conduct advocacy for policies and programs for the protection of victims of gender-based and child violence;
   - c. to conduct supervision of ongoing case handling processes; and
   - d. to conduct monitoring and supervision of the implementation of integrated services for victims of gender-based and child violence.

#### Article 18

To carry out the duties as referred to in Article 17, KPK2BGA has the following functions:

   - a. preparing materials for the formulation of policies and programs for the protection of victims of gender-based violence and children;
   - b. coordinating disputes between integrated service provider institutions for victims of gender-based violence and children;
   - c. developing a protection system for victims of gender-based violence and children; and
   - d. monitoring, supervising and reporting on the protection of victims of gender-based violence and children.

#### Article 19

The implementation of KPK2BGA is facilitated by the Regional Work Unit that oversees women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 20

The procedures, requirements and establishment of Integrated Services and KPK2BGA shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

Fourth Part Cooperation

#### Article 21

1. In providing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children, regional governments may cooperate with the Government, other Regional Governments.
2. Cooperation between Regional Governments and the Government includes consultation, coordination and reporting.
3. Cooperation between Regional Governments and other Regional Governments includes coordination, advocacy, referral, repatriation, social reintegration and development of integrated service systems.

#### Article 22

The implementation of cooperation as referred to in Article 21 shall be in accordance with the provisions of the prevailing laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 23

1. The community can participate in assisting prevention efforts and handling of victims of gender-based violence and children.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is realized through:
   - a. providing information and or reporting any violence that comes to their knowledge;
   - b. providing protection for victims;
   - c. providing emergency assistance;
   - d. providing advocacy to victims and or the community regarding the handling of gender-based violence and child cases;
   - e. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection orders; and f. assisting in the repatriation and social reintegration process.

# CHAPTER VII

## PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 24

1. To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government shall conduct socialization, empowerment, and awareness-raising to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness-raising as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall undertake efforts as follows:
   - a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
   - b. opening employment opportunities for women;
   - c. building community participation and concern towards the prevention of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
   - d. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work in and/or care about women and children.

# CHAPTER VIII

## CONTROL, GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 25

1. Control, guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall be carried out by the Regent, the implementation of which shall be carried out by the Vice Regent.
2. The implementation of control, guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out on a daily basis by the SKPD responsible for women's empowerment and child protection affairs.

# CHAPTER IX

## FINANCING

#### Article 26

All activities related to the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall be charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of the Regency.

# CHAPTER X

## REPORTING

#### Article 27

1. The Head of SKPD responsible for women's empowerment and child protection affairs shall report periodically to the Regent regarding the implementation of this Regional Regulation.
2. The reporting procedures as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER XI

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 28

1. At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all implementing regulations related to the protection of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to and/or have not been replaced by new regulations.
2. The Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) of Ogan Komering Ulu Regency that was established prior to the enactment of this Regional Regulation shall remain recognized for its existence.

# CHAPTER XII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

Matters not yet regulated in this Regional Regulation insofar as concerning its implementation shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 30

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR VICTIMS OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AND CHILDREN

##### I. GENERAL

Gender-based violence and violence against children is a social phenomenon that has existed since ancient times and has become increasingly prevalent recently. In fact, gender-based violence and violence against children continues to increase, both in number and in the forms and modus operandi which are increasingly diverse. Rape, sexual harassment, trafficking of women and children, domestic violence, sexual exploitation, violence against domestic workers, pornography, exploitation of migrant workers, and neglect, will apparently continue to be encountered in daily life, both in the domestic sphere (household) and public sphere.

The causal factors of gender-based violence and violence against children are very complex and interrelated with one another. These factors include, among others, legal instruments that have not been able to provide protection to victims, the concept that women and children are family property (assets), media that lack support in reporting on violence against women and children, public services that are not yet optimal, customs that sometimes legalize violence, poverty issues, erroneous interpretations of religious teachings, all of which are wrapped in a patriarchal culture.

The handling of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children occurring in Ogan Komering Ulu Regency, in accordance with the provisions of Article 13 of Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, states that Regional Governments are obligated to establish and develop a system and mechanism of cooperation for handling violence. Furthermore, based on the provisions of Article 26 paragraph (1) letter b of Law Number 32 of 2004 concerning Regional Government, it states that the Deputy Regional Head has the duty to assist the Regional Head in coordinating the activities of vertical agencies in the region, following up on reports and/or findings from supervisory apparatus oversight, implementing women's and youth empowerment, as well as endeavoring to develop and preserve social culture and the environment. Therefore, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Violence Against Children.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a What is meant by "non-discrimination" is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnic group, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.

Letter b What is meant by "best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Letter c What is meant by "gender justice" is fair treatment given to women and men.

What is meant by "gender equality" is a condition and position that describes harmonious, compatible and balanced relations between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in access, participation, control and benefits in the implementation of development as well as enjoying the results of development in family life, as well as in community, national and state life.

Letter d What is meant by "victim protection" is providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

Letter e What is meant by "maternal survival" is ensuring that a mother does not experience death that occurs during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum, whether caused by physical or non-physical conditions.

Letter f What is meant by "growth and development" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.

What is meant by "survival" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity and the right to enjoy the highest attainable health status.

Letter g What is meant by "respect for the child's opinion" is respect for children's rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.

Letter h What is meant by "openness" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is transparent among integrated service providers and KPK2BGA.

Letter i What is meant by "integration" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is carried out by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal advocates, psychologists, clergy, social workers, police.

Letter j What is meant by "not blaming the victim" is an attitude and treatment of not blaming the victim for the occurrence of violence experienced.

Letter k What is meant by "empowering" is that every effort provided must be able to strengthen victims, whether physically, psychologically, socially or economically.

Letter l What is meant by "victim confidentiality" is every action taken to ensure victims are in a safe condition from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychological state of victims.

Letter m What is meant by "decision-making in the hands of the victim" is the victim's right to determine the best choice in resolving their problem.
