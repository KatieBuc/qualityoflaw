# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Bengkayang Regency.
2. Regional Government is Bengkayang Regency as an element of regional government administration that leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Bengkayang Regency.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the regional people's representative institution of Bengkayang Regency as an element of Regional Government administration.
5. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
6. Child's Rights are part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected and fulfilled by parents, family, community, government and regional government.
7. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb.
8. Child with Special Needs is a child with special characteristics that differ from children in general without always indicating mental, emotional or physical disability, which includes among others: blind, deaf, mentally disabled, physically disabled, emotionally disturbed, learning difficulties, behavioral disorders, gifted children, children with health disorders and disabled children.
9. Child Protection is all forms of activities to guarantee and protect -5- children so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
10. Special protection is protection given to children in emergency situations, children in conflict with the law, children from minority and isolated groups, children who are exploited economically and/or sexually, children who are trafficked, children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substances (drugs) abuse, children victims of kidnapping, sale, trafficking, children victims of violence both physical and/or mental, children with disabilities, and children victims of maltreatment and neglect.
11. Child Victim of Violence is a child who receives harsh treatment either physically, psychologically, economically, socially, sexually, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
12. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
13. Protection I is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court decisions.
14. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
15. Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate, optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
16. Implementation of protection is a series of activities aimed at: preventing the occurrence of violence, providing protection and recovery services and social reintegration, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation carried out by the Integrated Service Center.
17. Violence is any act that results in or will result in misery and suffering both physical, sexual, psychological including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.

-6-

18. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
19. Violence against Children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, economic exploitation, socially including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.
20. Domestic violence is any act against a person, especially women, that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically and/or household neglect including threats to commit acts of coercion or deprivation of freedom unlawfully within the scope of the household.
21. Prevention is direct efforts made by the Regional Government to prevent acts of violence against women and children.
22. Complaint is a notification either in writing or orally by an interested party to an authorized institution, in which the person concerned suffers losses due to the actions of others.
23. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by Professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
24. Integrated Service is a series of activities to provide protection for women and children victims of violence carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unit of implementation, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
25. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A for Protection of Women and Children is an institution providing services to victims of violence, carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unit of implementation of prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
26. Health Service is an effort that includes promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative aspects.
27. Spiritual guidance is assistance in the form of services for spiritual strengthening so that sick people can rise to be closer to God so that a sense of calm, security arises, and mental health is achieved -7-.
28. Legal assistance is legal services provided by legal assistants or advocates to carry out the process of accompanying witnesses and/or victims of violence against women and children.
29. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
30. Victim Recovery is all efforts to empower victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, whether physically, psychologically, socially, economically or sexually.
31. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for self-empowerment of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems they face.
32. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes who can provide protection and fulfill the needs of victims.
33. Safe House (shelter) is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of women against violence is conducted based on the principles of:

   - a. respect for the rights of victims;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. protection of victims;
   - d. best interests of victims; and
   - e. non-discrimination.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 4

The scope of protection for women and children victims of violence includes the rights of victims, obligations and authorities of Regional Government, implementation of protection, reporting, control, guidance, and supervision, community participation and financing.

# CHAPTER IV

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 5

1. Every woman and child victim of violence has the right to: a. obtain protection for personal security, family, and property, and be free from threats related to testimony that will be, is being, or has been given;
   - b. participate in the process of selecting and determining the form of protection and security support;
   - c. be free from ensnaring questions;
   - d. obtain information regarding case developments and court decisions;
   - e. receive services that are fast, appropriate, comfortable, and according to needs;
   - f. obtain recovery, social reintegration, continuous handling up to rehabilitation and empowerment;
   - g. obtain assistance at every level of examination and during the judicial process and out-of-court processes are carried out; and
   - h. obtain psychological assistance, spiritual guidance, economic, social and interpreter services;
2. The rights of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 6 In addition to the rights as referred to in Article 5, every victim must: a. provide true testimony to the parties -9- concerned;

   - b. become a witness if it does not endanger oneself and if there is a security guarantee for the victim

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND AUTHORITY OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 7

1. Regional Government is obligated to:
   - a. prevent the occurrence of violence;
   - b. provide protection for victims of violence;
   - c. provide recovery services and social reintegration;
   - d. encourage and increase community participation;
   - e. conduct cooperation with service providers in efforts to prevent, protect and recover victims of violence.
   - f. facilitate the establishment of P2TP2A based in Hospitals, Community Health Centers, Trauma Center Shelters, and other supporting institutions; and
   - g. supervise the implementation of services for victims, with service standards that involve the community.
2. To carry out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1), Regional Government has the authority to:
   - a. formulate policies and programs on the elimination of violence against women and children;
   - b. establish P2TP2A;
   - c. facilitate the implementation of P2TP2A activities;
   - d. provide facilities and infrastructure;
   - e. increase the capacity of service provider institutions;
   - f. conduct coordination and cooperation in the implementation of protection for victims of violence against women and children;
   - g. encourage community participation; and
   - h. conduct monitoring and evaluation.
3. Regional Government in carrying out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional -10-

#### Article 8

In implementing protection for women and child victims of violence, the Regional Government is assisted by P2TP2A. } Article 9 • (1) P2TP2A as referred to in Article 8 is established by the Regional Government in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations. (2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of elements of the Regional Government, non-governmental organizations, health service institutions, law enforcement officers, professional personnel, volunteer companions, social workers, religious leaders, safe houses (shelters), and social rehabilitation centers.

#### Article 11

P2TP2A as referred to in Article 8, has the duties to:

   - a. endeavor prevention;
   - b. endeavor recovery and social reintegration;
   - c. provide legal protection;
   - d. conduct coordination;
   - e. endeavor to increase community participation, and
   - f. monitoring and reporting.

### Paragraph 1

Prevention Efforts

#### Article 12

1. Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 11 letter a include:
   - a. establishing a prevention and handling network for violence through coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
   - b. establishing a violence prevention system, mapping of locations or -11- areas prone to violence;
   - c. organizing communication, information, and education about violence against women and children; and
   - d. socialization of laws and regulations related to violence against women and children.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in an integrated manner coordinated by the SKPD that has duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment.

#### Article 13

In addition to prevention efforts carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 12, prevention efforts must also be carried out by:

   - a. family and closest relatives;
   - b. community and business sector; and
   - c. educational institutions.

### Paragraph 2 Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts

#### Article 14

Recovery and social reintegration efforts as referred to in Article 11 letter b, include:

   - a. providing physical recovery at health service institutions;
   - b. providing medicolegal services;
   - c. assisting the repatriation of victims;
   - d. providing temporary protection in safe houses (shelters);
   - e. providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;
   - f. providing spiritual guidance services; and
   - g. preparing families and communities, economic empowerment, and return to schools and/or other educational institutions.

### Paragraph 3 Legal Protection

#### Article 15

Legal protection as referred to in Article 11 letter c includes:

   - a. providing protection in a safe house (shelter);
   - b. providing assistance in the legal process; and
   - c. providing special legal protection for child victims of violence can be carried out through the appointment of guardianship in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

-12- Paragraph 4 Coordination

#### Article 16

Coordination as referred to in Article 11 letter d, includes:

   - a. conducting coordination of violence case handling with integrated provincial services;
   - b. conducting coordination with integrated services of other regions;
   - c. conducting coordination with service provider institutions for women and child victims of violence; and
   - d. conducting coordination and consultation with Regional Government.

### Paragraph 5

Efforts to Increase Community Participation

#### Article 17

Increasing community participation as referred to in Article 11 letter e, is carried out by means of:

   - a. fostering community awareness of cases of violence against women and children;
   - b. encouraging the community to actively participate in providing information and reporting the occurrence of violence against women and children;
   - c. fostering local wisdom in handling women and children victims of violence;
   - d. organizing the strengthening of community groups in handling women and children victims of violence; and
   - e. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to violence against women and children.

### Paragraph 6

Monitoring and Reporting

#### Article 18

Monitoring and reporting as referred to in Article 11 letter f, includes monitoring, documentation, and reporting of cases of violence against women and children.

-13- Second Part Cooperation

1. In providing protection for victims of violence against women and children, the Regional Government may cooperate with the Government, Provincial Government, other regional governments and other institutions.
2. Cooperation between the Regional Government and the Government includes consultation, coordination, and reporting.
3. Cooperation with other regional governments and other institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) includes coordination, advocacy, referral, repatriation, social reintegration, and development of integrated service systems.

# CHAPTER IX

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 23

1. The community can participate in assisting prevention efforts and handling of victims of violence against women and children.

   - d. providing advocacy to victims and or the community regarding the handling of cases of violence against women and children;
   - e. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection orders; and
   - f. assisting in the process of repatriation and social reintegration.

##### I. GENERAL

As we have known together that the protection of children and women victims of violence nowadays needs to receive serious attention, particularly Bengkayang Regency in implementing the Women's Empowerment and Child Protection program, this matter is as stated in the Medium-Term Development Plan of Bengkayang Regency.

This matter is to provide understanding that every Citizen has the right to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence, especially in the Household and those who potentially become victims are generally women, likewise acts of violence and exploitation against children. Therefore it is expected particularly that women can know and understand various aspects regarding domestic violence.

As mandated in Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, it has been stated that all forms of violence, especially violence against Women and Children constitute violations of human rights and crimes against human dignity as well as forms of Discrimination that must be eliminated, so that they must receive protection from the State and or society in order to avoid and be free from violence, threats of torture or treatment that degrades the degree and dignity of humanity.

Statistically, cases of violence against women and children in Bengkayang Regency are very high on average per semester reaching 17 (seventeen) cases, this shows that -17- Women's Empowerment in Bengkayang Regency in handling cases I acts of violence against women and children must I receive very serious handling and attention.

Related to this matter the Bengkayang Regency Government needs to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection of Women And Children Victims of Violence which regulates clearly, firmly, and comprehensively to better understand and know various forms of acts of violence against women and children as well as how to seek protection.
