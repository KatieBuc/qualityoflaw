BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD
MAYOR OF PAYAKUMBUH,

Considering:
a. that the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia guarantees the welfare of every citizen, including the protection of Women and Children from all forms of violence and discrimination as mandated in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
b. that the rampant crimes and violence against women and children in society requires increased commitment and coordination from the Government, Regional Government and Society as well as all stakeholders in order to carry out handling and prevention measures;
c. that the fulfillment of the constitutional rights of women and children as well as the improvement of the quality of life of women and children, is one of the mandatory affairs that becomes the duty, authority and responsibility of the Regional Government;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children.

PROTECTION
CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1 In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is the City of Payakumbuh.
2. Regional Government is the Government of the City of Payakumbuh.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Payakumbuh.
4. Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit within the Government of the City of Payakumbuh.
5. Protection of Women is all efforts to guarantee and protect the rights of women so that they can live healthily and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and obtain protection from neglect, violence and discrimination, whether in the form of protective, preventive, repressive or post-remedial (assistance and treatment) protection.
6. Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
7. Prevention is direct efforts carried out by the Regional Government, community and Parents and/or family to prevent violations of the rights of women and children.
8. Children's Forum is a Children's Participation Forum formed with the aim of facilitating children's involvement in decision-making processes and enjoying changes relating to their lives either directly or indirectly which are implemented with the consent and willingness of all children based on awareness and understanding, in accordance with age and level of thinking maturity.
9. Child-Friendly Regency/City hereinafter abbreviated as KLA is a Regency City that has a development system based on children's rights through the integration of commitment and resources of government, community and business world that are planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights.
10. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including children still in the womb.
11. Neglected child is a child whose needs are not properly fulfilled either physically, mentally, spiritually or socially.
12. Women victims of violence are women who experience actions that result in misery or suffering physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
13. Child victims of violence are children who experience actions that result in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically or neglect that threatens life, body and degrades human dignity.
14. Trafficking in persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, kidnapping, confinement, coercion, fraud, abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
15. Complaint Handling is a series of actions carried out by integrated service providers to follow up on reports of acts of violence against women and children, submitted by victims, families and the community.
16. Social rehabilitation is services aimed at restoring and developing the abilities of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.
17. Law enforcement is action by apparatus given authority by the state to implement laws and regulations.
18. Legal aid is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocates to carry out the process of accompanying witnesses and/or violence against women and children that is gender sensitive.
19. Repatriation is an effort to return women and child victims of violence from abroad to the debarkation point/entry point or from the receiving area to the area of origin.
20. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
21. Safe house is a temporary shelter that aims to provide protection and a sense of security to victims of violence.
22. Shelter house is a forum for guidance or welfare services for street children that carries out social assistance/guidance activities, maintenance, care, education and health, skills guidance in order to ensure that children do not carry out activities on the streets so that they can grow and develop properly.
23. Spiritual guidance is an effort to restore the psyche through strengthening the religion adhered to by the victim.
24. Gender Responsive is consistent and systematic attention to the differences between women and men in society accompanied by efforts to eliminate structural and cultural barriers in achieving gender equality.
25. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
26. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife or husband, wife and their children, or father and his children, mother and her children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
27. Customs are eternal patterns of behavior passed down from generation to generation as heritage, so that they are strongly integrated with community behavior patterns.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE
Article 2
(1) The implementation of protection for women is based on the principles of: a. Best interests of women
b. Gender justice and gender equality
c. Local wisdom
d. Legal Certainty (2) The implementation of protection for children is based on the principles of: a. Non-Discrimination
b. Best interests of the child
c. Right to life, survival and development d. Respect for the views of the child
e. Legal Certainty

Article 3
The regulation of protection of women and children aims to:
a. serve as a guideline for Regional Government in planning, policy and strategy for the protection of women and children.
b. protect women and children from actions, decisions and policies that violate the rights of women and children.
c. prevent the occurrence of violations of the rights of women and children.
d. increase empowerment of women.
e. increase the role and value of local wisdom as well as the role of customs in the protection of women and children, and
f. increase the role of government and non-government institutions in efforts to protect women and children.

Article 4
The scope of regulation for the protection of women and children includes: a. Prevention;
b. Implementation of protection of women and children;
c. Handling;
d. Empowerment, and
e. Guidance and supervision;

CHAPTER III
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
Part One
Rights of Women

Article 5
Every woman has the right to:
a. Obtain protection from discriminatory actions;
b. Obtain protection from economic exploitation and sexual exploitation;
c. Obtain protection from physical, psychological, sexual violence and neglect;
d. Obtain decent work in accordance with her capabilities;
e. Obtain work leave while receiving wages/salary in accordance with statutory regulations;
f. Obtain education and teaching in all types, levels and pathways of education;
g. Obtain special protection in the implementation of her work or profession against matters that may threaten safety and or health in relation to women's reproductive function, and
h. Ownership and management of joint assets in marriage in accordance with statutory regulations.

Part Two
Rights of the Child

Article 6
Every child has the right:
a. To live, grow, develop and participate reasonably in accordance with human dignity;
b. To obtain protection from discriminatory actions;
c. To obtain protection from economic exploitation and sexual exploitation;
d. To obtain protection from physical, psychological, sexual violence and neglect;
e. To a name as personal identity and citizenship status;
f. To obtain a birth certificate free of charge;
g. To embrace a religion and worship according to the teachings of their religion with the guidance of parents/guardians;
h. To not be forced to change religion by foster parents, adoptive parents and by anyone;
i. To be recognized and know their parents, to be raised and cared for by their own parents;
j. To obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual and social needs;
k. To obtain education and teaching in accordance with their interests and talents;
l. To obtain care, education and training, to improve themselves and the ability to participate in community and state life for children with special needs;
m. To express and have their opinion heard, and
n. To rest and utilize leisure time for self-development.

PREVENTION
. First Part Regional Government

Article 13
(1) In order to prevent violations of women's rights, the Regional Government:
a. Conducts Socialization regarding the prevention of violence against women by optimizing the role of customary, cultural, religious institutions and community organizations;
b. Provides guidelines on handling acts of violence against women;
c. Involves community participation in recovery and social reintegration;
d. Develops an integrated database and information system on acts of violence against women;
e. Develops family empowerment programs;
f. Strengthens the function of community organizations working in the field of women's empowerment; and
g. Provides public facilities that are comfortable and safe for women.
(2) In order to prevent violations of children's rights, the Regional Government:
a. Undertakes efforts to prevent acts of violence against children by optimizing the role of customary, cultural, religious institutions and community organizations;
b. Develops child-friendly schools;
c. Encourages increased participation of children through the establishment of children's forums;
d. Provides guidelines on handling acts of violence against children;
e. Involves community participation in recovery services and reintegration of child victims of violence.
f. Develops an integrated database and information system on acts of violence against children;
g. Strengthens the function of community organizations working in the field of child protection; and
h. Realizes the development of a Child-Friendly City; Realizes Child-Friendly Open Public Spaces (RPTRA).

Second Part
Society

Article 14
(1) In the framework of preventing violations against women's rights, the community:
a. Monitors various conditions occurring in their environment that may give rise to violations against women's rights;
b. Strengthens the role of community organizations, religious organizations and the family environment; and
c. Prevents unregistered marriage/siri marriage (2) In the framework of preventing violations against children's rights, the community:
a. Monitors various conditions occurring in the environment that give rise to violations against children's rights;
b. Strengthens the role of community organizations, religious organizations and the family environment; and
c. Prevents the occurrence of marriage at child age.

Third Part
Parents, family and/or guardians

Article 15
(1) In order to prevent violations of children's rights, parents or guardians:
a. Provide good examples to children in accordance with religious teachings and customs;
b. Supervise children in their activities inside or outside the home; c. Provide opportunities for children to engage in dialogue and express opinions democratically; and
d. Protect children from various acts of violation of children's rights within the household.
(2) In order to prevent violations of women's rights, the family:
a. Protects women from various violations of rights within the household; and
b. Encourages the formation of a harmonious, educative and democratic family.

First Part
Implementation of Protection for Women

Paragraph 1
General

Article 16
The protection of women is organized by the Regional Government by involving the active role of families, ninik mamak, bundo kanduang, customary leaders, religious leaders, the community and social organizations in the field of Women's Protection.
Paragraph 2 Marriage

Article 17
During the marriage bond or after divorce, a woman has equal rights with the man who is her husband/former husband over all matters related to the consequences of marriage and/or divorce.

Paragraph 3
Health Sector

Article 18

Article 19
The public service provider is obligated to provide special breastfeeding facilities.
Paragraph 4 Employment Sector

Article 20
(1) Every person is prohibited from employing and involving women in bad work.
(2) Bad work as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
a. All work in the form of slavery or the like.
b. All work that exploits, provides or offers women for prostitution, pornography production, pornographic performances or gambling.
c. All work that exploits, provides, or involves women for the production and trade of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, psychotropic substances, and other addictive substances and/or
d. All work that endangers the health, safety and morals of women.
(3) Employers who employ women must maintain decency, safety and security while in the workplace;
(4) Employers must place female workers in places that are safe for women's reproductive health;
(5) Employers must fulfill the rights of female workers to receive wages in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
(6) Employers must provide special breastfeeding facilities in their companies.

Article 2 1
(1) Female workers/laborers who are less than 18 (eighteen) years old are prohibited from being employed between 23.00 WIB until 07.00 WIB.
(2) Employers are prohibited from employing pregnant female workers/laborers who according to a doctor's statement would endanger the health and safety of their pregnancy or themselves if working between 23.00 WIB until 07.00 WIB.
(3) Employers who employ female workers/laborers between 23.00 WIB until 07.00 WIB, are obligated to:
a. Provide nutritious food and drinks; and
b. Maintain decency and security while in the workplace.
(4) Employers are obligated to provide shuttle transportation for female workers/laborers who depart for and return from work between 23.00 WIB until 05.00 WIB.

(2) Safe houses aim to protect women and children victims of violence from intimidation by perpetrators and other parties.

(a) Criteria for women and children victims of violence who require safe houses include:
a. Their life is threatened
b. Receiving rejection from family or community c. Requiring intensive services but their residence is relatively far; and/or
d. Abandoned if not placed in a safe house.
(5) The provision of safe house services is carried out by gender-responsive social rehabilitation officers, consisting of:
a. Social workers
b. Volunteers
o. Security officers and
d. Community leaders (6) Further provisions regarding the provision of safe house services shall be regulated by Mayoral Regulation.

Article 49
(1) Women and children victims of violence are provided with spiritual guidance aimed at psychological recovery.
(2) Spiritual guidance as intended in paragraph (1) is implemented based on the principles of:
a. Not imposing any religion or belief; and b. Leaving the final decision in the hands of the victim/self determination) (3) Spiritual guidance is conducted by spiritual guidance officers (4) Spiritual guidance officers as intended in paragraph (3) consist of Consultants from the Advisory, Development and Preservation of Marriage Agency (BP4), Officers from the Office of Religious Affairs (KUA), Ustad/Ulama, Pastors, Ministers and other religious leaders who are gender responsive

Enforcement Services and Legal Assistance

Article 50

(2) Law enforcement and legal aid services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by prioritizing the best interests of victims.

Article 5 1
(1) Law enforcement and legal aid services are intended to provide assistance and protection to women and children victims of violence and witnesses with a gender-responsive approach so that victims obtain justice and certainty of their legal rights.
(2) Types of legal aid services include:
a. Legal aid and the reporting process and preparation of BAP.
b. Legal aid and the prosecution process at the Prosecutor's Office.
c. Legal aid and the examination process at the Court.
d. Legal aid and the court hearing decision process until there is a final legal decision; and

CONCERNING
PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
1. GENERAL
Children are a trust as well as a gift from God Almighty that we must always protect because inherent in them are dignity, honor and rights as human beings that must be upheld. The human rights of children are part of human rights included in the 1945 Constitution and the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child. From the perspective of national and state life, children are the future of the nation and the next generation of the nation's ideals, so that every child has the right to survival, to grow and develop, to participate and has the right to protection from acts of violence and discrimination as well as civil rights and freedoms.
Likewise with women, from women are expected to be born children who are healthy, strong, resilient, of noble character, potential and quality. Through the role of women in the family, society and state will be born resilient generations who will continue the ideals of the nation.
As Indonesian citizens, women also have the right to improve quality and develop themselves according to their abilities, dignity and honor.
As assets and hope of the nation and state, children need to be given protection and safeguarded from all forms of acts of violence that will hinder their growth and development. Likewise with women, women need to be protected from acts of discrimination and from acts of violence whether physical, psychological, mental, sexual and neglect. Because their physique is weaker compared to men and adults and because of the dignity of women themselves, women and children often become objects, outlets for desires, anger, economic exploitation, violence (physical, psychological and sexual), cruelty, neglect and so forth.
To realize the protection of women and children, support is needed, legislation that can guarantee its implementation.
The Child Rights Convention affirms that preventive efforts must be made so that children are protected from kidnapping, smuggling and sale. In following up on the Child Rights Convention, the government established Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.
Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights has included the Rights of the Child, the implementation of obligations and responsibilities of parents, family, society, government and the State. Child protection in all its aspects is part of National development activities, particularly in advancing national and state life.
Parents, family and society are responsible for safeguarding and maintaining these human rights in accordance with the obligations imposed by law. Likewise in the context of implementing child protection, the State, government and Regional Government are responsible for providing facilities and accessibility for children, especially in ensuring their growth and development optimally and in a directed manner.
Although the protection and rights of women and children have been regulated in national level legislation, because the fulfillment of the constitutional rights of women and children is one of the mandatory affairs that is the duty, authority and responsibility of the Regional Government, then in providing Protection to women and children, the Payakumbuh City Government needs to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children.

Paragraph (1) Sufficiently Clear Paragraph (2) Point a What is meant by Gender Responsive is that all service officers must be gender sensitive when exploring problems experienced by victims and can carry out empowerment of victims.
Point b What is meant by Non-Discrimination is that every woman and child without exception has the right to receive services related to the violence they experience, no one may be rejected or given priority over others except on the basis of certain emergency considerations.
Point c What is meant by equal and respectful relationships is that whoever the victim, the provision of services for victims must be carried out with respect for the victim without distinguishing their beliefs, values and social status.
Point d What is meant by maintaining confidentiality is that services must be provided in a place that guarantees the victim's privacy. Every piece of information revealed in the service provision process must be kept confidential and known only by people who are relevant in the provision of services. Officers must convey this principle to victims Point e What is meant by providing a sense of security and comfort is that service providers must ensure that victims are in a safe and comfortable state in telling their problems Point f What is meant by respecting individual differences is that each individual must be viewed as unique, each person has a different background, life experience so that one victim must not be compared with another victim in any matter.
Point g What is meant by not judging is that service providers must ensure that whatever the victim's condition or information that comes from the victim will not be assessed or judged.
Point h What is meant by respecting the victim's choices and decisions is that the provision of services must be carried out with the victim's consent, starting from the interview process, data recording, to the handling/actions that will be taken. Point i What is meant by being sensitive to the victim's background and condition and the use of appropriate language that is understood by the victim is that sometimes victims come from regions or economic, educational and other backgrounds that are not the same as the officers. It must be ensured that victims are served in a language understood by the victim. Recently there are increasingly many disabled/difable/disable women who become victims of violence, whether deaf, blind or those experiencing mental retardation. For this category of victims, interpreters must also be provided who, for example, can be taken from special school (SLB) teachers.
Point j What is meant by fast and simple is that the provision of services must be given immediately without unnecessary delay. Perhaps several interventions require various parties, but with the proactiveness of service officers, victims must be guaranteed to be able to go through everything with a simple process. If the victim comes on referral from another service provider, the receiving officer must first read the referral letter. Efforts must be made so that victims are not asked repeatedly about the same things related to their identity or case narrative.
Point k What is meant by empathy is that officers must apply an attitude of empathy, namely the ability to place oneself in the position of others (in this case the victim). Thus the victim feels accepted, understood and can openly tell their problems.
Point l What is meant by fulfillment of women's and children's rights is that victims are entitled to respect and full use of their rights to survive, development, protection, freedom from all forms of discrimination and to participate, as regulated in the convention on the rights of the children.
