BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF GORONTALO,

Considering : a. that Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia guarantee the right of every citizen to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence;

b. that all forms of acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination;

c. that women's empowerment and child protection are concurrent governmental affairs delegated to the regions to become the basis for implementing regional autonomy and become mandatory governmental affairs not related to basic services as referred to in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government;

d. that in order to provide regulatory strengthening, direction, and foundation in the protection of women and children victims of violence, regulation is needed related to the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

Observing : that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, letter c, and letter d, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children Against Acts of Violence;

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Gorontalo Regency.
2. Regent is the Regent of Gorontalo.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous Region.
4. Regional Governance is the administration of government affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
5. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.
6. Woman is a person who is female in gender.
7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.
8. Violence against women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.
10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly.
11. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will occur, is occurring or has occurred.
12. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
13. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will occur, is occurring or has occurred.
14. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
15. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the Region to provide services in the context of empowerment of women and children victims of acts of violence.
16. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the accompaniment process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and danger from perpetrators.
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
18. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, milk kinship, care, and guardianship who reside in that household.
19. Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner.

Article 2
Protection of Victims is based on:
a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of victims,
b. justice and gender equality,
c. non-discrimination,
d. the best interests of victims, and
e. empowerment.

Article 3
Victim Protection aims to:
a. prevent violence against women and children,

c. carry out empowerment of women victims of violence.

Pasal 4

CHAPTER II
FORMS OF VIOLENCE
Article 5
Forms of violence include:
a. physical violence,
b. psychological violence,
c. sexual violence,
d. neglect,
e. exploitation, and/or
f. other violence.

Article 6
Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a injury, wound or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causing death.

Article 7
Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 8
Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or specific purposes.

Article 9
Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of a child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care,
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care,
c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person, and/or,
d. acts that result in economic dependence by means of restricting and/or prohibiting from working properly inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Article 10
Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others, acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or capabilities of a person by other parties to obtain benefits both material and immaterial, and/or all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

Article 11
Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:
a. threat of violence which includes any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom, and coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

Il. GENERAL Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Gorontalo Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades their degree and human dignity, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

Thus far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection measures at the Regional level, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection measures for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for women and children victims of violence in Gorontalo Regency.
