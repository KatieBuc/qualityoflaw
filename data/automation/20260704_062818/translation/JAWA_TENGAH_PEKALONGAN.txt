CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Pekalongan Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
3. Agency for Community Empowerment, Women and Family Planning hereinafter referred to as BPMPKB is the Agency for Community Empowerment, Women and Family Planning of Pekalongan Regency as the institution for formulating and implementing regional policies that are specific in the field of community empowerment, women and family planning.

4. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
5. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
7. Protection against Gender is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, regional government, or other parties who know or hear that violence against men or women will or has occurred either temporarily or based on court determination.
8. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
9. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
10. Violence against children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
11. Victims of gender-based violence are people who due to their gender experience physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from State policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
12. Integrated Services are a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as a unified implementation, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and jdih.pekalongankab.go.id legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
13. Companion is a person or institution and/or body that has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
14. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a unified unit that organizes integrated services for women and children victims of violence.
15. Medical Services are facilities that provide services that are clinical in nature in the field of diagnostics, and/or inpatient care for victims.
16. Social rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial condition disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning both in the family and in society.
17. Legal Aid is the provision of legal assistance to victims seeking justice who are unable and face difficulties in the legal field outside and before the court without compensation.
18. Repatriation is the act of returning victims to their region of origin or country of origin while prioritizing protection services and fulfillment of their needs.
19. Social Reintegration is the reunification of victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
20. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers of perpetrators.
21. Service Standards are benchmarks used as guidelines for service delivery and reference for assessing service quality as obligations and promises of providers to the community in the context of quality, appropriate, easy, affordable and measurable services.
22. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct lineage upward or downward up to the third degree.
23. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES
Article 2
The protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:
a. non-discrimination;
b. best interests of the victim;
c. gender justice and equality;
d. victim protection;
e. maternal survival;
f. child survival, growth, and development;
g. respect for the child's opinion;
h. transparency;
i. integration;
j. not blaming the victim;
k. empowering;
l. victim confidentiality;
m. decision-making in the hands of the victim.

Article 3
The purpose of providing protection to victims of gender-based violence is to provide services and protection to male and female victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence that occurs within the household and/or outside the household.

CHAPTER III
OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES
Article 4
(1) The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence in the form of:
a. providing data and information about gender and child victims of violence in accordance with laws and regulations;
b. conducting education on anti-violence values based on gender and violence against children; and
c. disseminating laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection against gender-based violence and child victims of violence.
jdih.pekalongankab.go.id (2) To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government has the obligation to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
a. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving elements of SKPD, Agencies, Institutions, Community Organizations, the Community;
b. providing facilities and infrastructure;
c. increasing the capacity of service provider institutions;
d. conducting coordination and cooperation in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children;
e. conducting monitoring and evaluation.
f. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for victims.
(3) The Regional Government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

CHAPTER IV
FORMS OF VIOLENCE
Article 5
(1) Forms of Gender-Based Violence include a. Physical Violence;
b. Psychological Violence;
c. Sexual Violence;
(2) Forms of Violence against Children include a. Physical Violence;
b. Psychological Violence;
c. Sexual Violence;
d. Neglect;
e. Exploitation;
f. Maltreatment

Article 6
Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, loss of consciousness and/or causing death.

Article 7
Psychological Violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter b is caused by acts that result in jdih.pekalongankab.go.id fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to the victim.

Article 8
Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment both physical and psychological;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Article 9
Neglect as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after children as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting a person within the household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by means of restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Article 10
Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or capabilities of a person by other parties to obtain benefits either material or immaterial; and/or
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities or sexual abuse.

Article 11
Maltreatment as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) letter f is caused by:
a. any act or action whether intentional or unintentional committed by another person that makes an individual sick or disturbed in their feelings, or obtains unpleasant feelings that make someone sad, disappointed, annoyed, angry and afraid.
b. any sexual violation committed or permitted to be committed against a young person by an adult or another person legally responsible for them, including touching a child with the intention of sexual gratification or forcing a child to touch an adult, sexual intercourse, showing sexual activities to a child, pornography or allowing a child to engage in sexual intercourse that is not appropriate to their development.

CHAPTER V
RIGHTS OF VICTIMS
Article 12
Every victim of gender-based violence and children shall receive the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as a human being;
b. the right to determine their own decisions;
c. the right to obtain information;
d. the right to confidentiality of their identity;
e. the right to complaint handling;
f. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
g. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process;
h. the right to obtain repatriation efforts and social reintegration of victims of gender-based violence and children.
i. the right to social rehabilitation.

b. Empowerment of child victims of violence who are not in school is to ensure child victims of violence receive the right to continue their education, and/or receive job training with capital assistance.

CHAPTER VIII

Article 23
(1) The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children.
(2) The implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out on a daily basis by BPMPKB.

CHAPTER IX

Funding for the implementation of protection activities for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out by the Regional Government is sourced from the APBD, Provincial APBD and/or APBN as well as other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

I. GENERAL
Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.
In order to prevent and address gender-based and child violence in Pekalongan Regency so as to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for gender and child victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation. Thus far, laws and regulations governing the protection of gender and child victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts at the regional level, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation.
This Regional Regulation governs protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for gender and child victims of violence in Pekalongan Regency.

Article 2
Letter a What is meant by "non-discrimination" is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinguishing tribe, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.
Letter b What is meant by "best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.
Letter c What is meant by "gender justice" is fair treatment given to both women and men.
What is meant by "gender equality" is a condition and position that describes a harmonious, compatible and jdih.pekalongankab.go.id balanced relationship between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in access, participation, control and benefits in the implementation of development as well as enjoying the results of development in family life, as well as in community, national and state life.
Letter d What is meant by "victim protection" is providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family members, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
Letter e What is meant by "maternal survival" is ensuring that a mother does not experience death that occurs during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum, whether caused by physical or non-physical conditions Letter f.
What is meant by "growth and development" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.
What is meant by "survival" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity and the right to enjoy the highest attainable health status.
Letter g.
What is meant by "respect for the views of the child" is respect for children's rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.
Letter h What is meant by "openness" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is transparent among the integrated service providers.
Letter i What is meant by "integration" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is carried out by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal advocates, psychologists, religious counselors, social workers, police.
Letter j What is meant by "not blaming the victim" is an attitude and treatment of not blaming the victim for the occurrence of violence experienced by them.
Letter k What is meant by "empowering" is that every effort provided must be able to strengthen the victim, whether physically, psychologically, socially or economically.
jdih.pekalongankab.go.id Letter l What is meant by "victim confidentiality" is every action taken to ensure the victim is in a safe condition from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychological state of the victim.
Letter m What is meant by "decision-making in the hands of the victim" is the victim's right to determine the best choice in resolving their problem.
