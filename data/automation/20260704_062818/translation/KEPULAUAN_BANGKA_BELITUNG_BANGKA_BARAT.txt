GOVERNMENT OF WEST BANGKA REGENCY
REGIONAL REGULATION OF WEST BANGKA REGENCY
NUMBER 15 OF 2011
CONCERNING
COOPERATION IN THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD
THE REGENT OF WEST BANGKA,
Considering : a. that acts of violence against women and children constitute crimes against humanity which are violations of Human Rights;
b. that acts of violence against women and children in West Bangka Regency still frequently occur while services and protection for women and children in West Bangka Regency have not been implemented optimally;
c. that in the framework of implementing regional autonomy, the regulation and service to the community particularly for women and children victims of acts of violence is a principal duty and function of the Regency government;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in point a, point b, and point c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on Cooperation in the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Acts of Violence;
In View of : 1. Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1984 Number 29, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3277);
2. Law Number 3 of 1997 concerning Juvenile Court (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1997 Number 3, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3668);

To Establish: REGIONAL REGULATION ON COOPERATION IN THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE.

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Regional Government is the Government of West Bangka Regency.
2. Bupati is the Bupati of West Bangka.
3. Regional Apparatus of the Regency is the supporting element of the Regional Head in the administration of regional government consisting of the Regional Secretariat, DPRD Secretariat, Regional Agencies and Regional Technical Institutions.
4. Cooperation is a systematic and integrated method among protection providers in delivering services to victims of domestic violence.
5. Protection Administration is all actions that include services and assistance to victims of domestic violence.
6. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb.
7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim.
8. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes death.
10. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
11. Economic violence is any act that neglects family members in the form of not providing proper living care or maintenance.
12. Psychological violence is any act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.
13. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
14. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will occur or has occurred.
15. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
16. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is an institution providing services to victims of violence, based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical care (including medico-legal), psycho-social and legal services.
17. Community Social Institution is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and/or other Community Organizations.
18. Population is Indonesian citizens and foreigners who enter legally and reside in the territory of Indonesia.

CHAPTER II
PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES
Article 2
Cooperation in the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out based on respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity, non-discrimination, gender equality and justice as well as protection of the basic rights of women and children and the best interests of victims.

Article 3
The Cooperation in the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to provide protection and services to women from gender-based violence and the best interests of children occurring in households or in public.

CHAPTER III
INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 4
(1) The Regent through regional apparatus establishes a community service work unit that functions as an Integrated Service Center (PPT).
(2) The Integrated Service Center (PPT) as referred to in paragraph (1) is coordinated by the Regional Apparatus responsible for women's empowerment.
(3) The Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1) in implementing the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence may coordinate with other Regional Apparatus.

CHAPTER IV
HANDLING AND MECHANISM
Article 5
The Implementation of Protection for victims of acts of violence is carried out by government agencies, regional governments and social institutions in accordance with their respective duties and functions, including providing the necessary facilities to implement protection.

Article 6
Facilities as referred to in article (5) include:
a. special service rooms within the police force;
b. expert and professional personnel;
c. service centers and safe houses; and
d. other facilities and infrastructure necessary for victim recovery.

Article 7
(1) The implementation of protection for victims must pay attention to religious norms as well as the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands/other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.
(2) The mechanism for handling protection and services shall be stipulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 8
Services as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (2) include: a. health services;
b. victim assistance;
c. counseling;
d. spiritual guidance; and
e. resocialization.

Article 9
(1) Health services are provided by health workers at health facilities owned by the government, regional government, and community, including private sector by providing treatment services and health recovery for victims.
(2) Victim assistance is carried out by health workers, social workers, volunteer companions, and/or spiritual counselors by providing counseling, therapy, spiritual guidance and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims.
(3) Counseling is provided by social workers, volunteer companions, by listening empathetically and exploring problems for the psychological strengthening of victims.
(4) Spiritual guidance is provided by spiritual counselors by providing explanations regarding their rights and obligations, as well as strengthening faith and piety in accordance with the religion and beliefs they adhere to.
(5) Resocialization of victims is carried out by social agencies and social institutions so that victims can resume their social functions in society.

Article 10
In certain circumstances, health workers, social workers, accompanying volunteers and/or spiritual advisors may establish cooperation with: a. the police, to report and process perpetrators of criminal acts of domestic violence;
b. advocates, to assist victims in the judicial process; c. other law enforcement officers, to assist victims in the court hearing process;
d. the National Commission on Violence Against Women;
e. the Indonesian Child Protection Commission (KPAI); and
f. certain parties as desired for the benefit of the victim.

CHAPTER V
FORMS OF VIOLENCE AND PROTECTION
Article 11
(1) Forms of violence against Women and Children include physical, sexual, economic and psychological.
(2) Forms of physical violence are any acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or cause death.
(3) Forms of sexual violence are sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, with other persons for commercial purposes and or/ specific purposes.
(4) Forms of economic violence are neglect of family members in the form of not providing a proper living, care or maintenance.
(5) Forms of psychological violence are acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 12
(1) Every woman and child who becomes a victim of violence must receive protection in accordance with applicable provisions.
(2) Protection of women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided to residents of West Bangka Regency whether committed in West Bangka or outside West Bangka.
(3) Protection of women and children victims of violence in addition to being provided to residents of West Bangka as referred to in paragraph (2), shall also be provided to non-residents of West Bangka where committed within the territory of West Bangka Regency.

Article 13
(1) Forms of protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence include medical, legal, medicolegal (forensic medicine), economic and psychological protection.
(2) Forms of medical protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence include treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.
(3) Forms of legal protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence include services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.
(4) Forms of medicolegal protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence include medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field.
(5) Forms of psychological protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence include assistance in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims including provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in resolving problems.
(6) Forms of economic protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence include services for skills and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

CHAPTER VI
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION
Article 14
(1) The West Bangka Regency Government in implementing protection for Women and Children Victims of violence, may involve community participation.
(2) Community participation may be carried out by individuals, groups or community organizations and/or Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) that are legally established.

Article 15
(1) Social community organizations that participate in the implementation of protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 14 paragraph (2), shall be given guidance by the West Bangka Regency government through Regional Apparatus in accordance with their main duties and functions.
(2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), may be in the form of social guidance, skills guidance and operational technical guidance.

CHAPTER VII
SOURCES OF FUNDS
Article 16
(1) Sources of funds for the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence carried out by the regional apparatus of West Bangka Regency as referred to in article 4 paragraph (1) shall come from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of West Bangka Regency and/or may come from other legitimate sources.
(2) Other legitimate sources as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be supervised in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VIII
TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS
Article 17
Other matters not yet regulated in this Regional Regulation, shall be further regulated in the Regent's Regulation.

CHAPTER X
CLOSING PROVISIONS
Article 18
This Regional Regulation shall come into force from the date it is enacted.
So that everyone may know, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of West Bangka Regency.
