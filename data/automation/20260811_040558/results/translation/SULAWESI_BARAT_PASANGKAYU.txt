BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD
REGENT OF PASANGKAYU,
Considering : a. that women and children are creations of Almighty God who constitute important elements of society and the Indonesian Nation;
b. that in the life of society, women and children often experience violence or unpleasant treatment;
c. that all forms of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and natural disposition without discrimination;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children.

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
Article 1
In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is Pasangkayu Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Pasangkayu.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and DPRD in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person who is legally recognized as a Woman.
6. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a Child in the womb.
7. Protection is all service actions to fulfill rights, as well as guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence.

JDIH PASANGKAYU REGENCY
8. Protection of Women is an effort aimed at protecting Women and their rights in order to obtain a sense of security from acts of violence.
9. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and obtain protection from violence and discrimination.
10. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering, whether physical, sexual, or psychological, including neglect, threats of certain actions, coercion, or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
11. Victims of acts of violence, hereinafter referred to as victims, are individuals or groups of people who experience suffering, whether physical, mental, or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction, or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.
12. Services are activities and immediate actions carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy, and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
13. The Regional Action Plan constitutes the basis and guideline for the relevant agencies and the community in carrying out activities for the implementation of protection for Women and Children who are victims of acts of violence.
14. A Safe House is a temporary residence provided to provide protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

CHAPTER II
OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE
Article 2
The objectives of the protection of Women and Children are to:

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU
a. provide and enhance a sense of security for Women and Children;
b. help prevent acts of Violence against Women and Children;
c. help prevent exploitation activities against Women and Children;
d. provide Services to Women and Children in facing legal problems; and
e. help improve the quality of life of Women and Children.

Article 3
The scope of regulation in this Regional Regulation includes:
a. forms of Violence;
b. rights of Women and Children;
c. duties and authorities;
d. handling of acts of Violence;
e. funding;
f. guidance and supervision; and
g. community participation.

CHAPTER III
FORMS OF VIOLENCE
Article 4
Forms of violence against Women and Children consist of:
a. Physical violence;
b. Psychological violence;
c. Sexual violence;
d. neglect; and/or
e. exploitation.

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU
Article 5
Physical violence as referred to in Article 4 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Article 6
Psychological violence as referred to in Article 4 letter b is due to acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of the ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering in a person.

Article 7
Sexual violence as referred to in Article 4 letter c consists of:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse; and/or
c. forcing sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and/or specific purposes.

Article 8
Neglect as referred to in Article 4 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of the Child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after the Child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians or other parties responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting a person within the household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to the perpetrator or due to an agreement or contract, the perpetrator is obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by means of restricting and/or prohibiting from working

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU
decent work inside or outside the home so that the Victim is under that person's control.

Article 9
Exploitation as referred to in Article 4 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the consent of the Victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or forced services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/or
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other organs of the Victim to obtain benefits that are not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

CHAPTER IV
RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN
Part One
Rights of Women

Article 10
(1) Regional Governments are obligated to protect the rights of Women in the Region.
(2) The rights of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of the right to:
a. basic needs;
b. comfort and security;
c. maximum health services in facing acts of Violence;
d. assistance services, legal aid and spiritual guidance in facing acts of Violence; and
e. confidentiality regarding the problems faced.

Second Part
Rights of the Child

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU
Article 11
(1) The Regional Government is obligated to protect the rights of Children in the Region.
(2) The rights of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of the right to:
a. live, grow, develop and participate appropriately in accordance with human dignity and worth and receive guidance and Protection from Violence and discrimination;
b. worship according to their religion, think and express themselves in accordance with their level of intelligence and age under parental guidance;
c. a birth certificate obtained and/or procured by their parents in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations;
d. obtain protection from family, community and Regional Government against discriminatory treatment and exploitation both economically and socially, neglect, cruelty, violence, abuse, injustice and other mistreatment;
e. regain formal or non-formal education for Children who have dropped out of school;
f. obtain health Services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual and social needs;
g. obtain education and teaching in accordance with their abilities, interests and talents; and
h. obtain other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER V
DUTIES AND AUTHORITIES
Part One
Duties

Paragraph 1
Duties of Regional Government in the Protection of Women

Article 12
(1) Regional Governments are obligated to provide Protection for Women in the Region.
(2) Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented through the formulation of a strategic plan

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU
Long-term, medium-term, and short-term Protection of Women.
(3) The strategic formulation as referred to in paragraph (2) must be integrated with the Regional development planning documents.
(4) The Regional Government in formulating the strategic plan as referred to in paragraph (3) may involve the community, social institutions and/or other institutions that carry out activities related to the Protection of Women.

Article 13
(1) Regional Governments are obligated to undertake prevention of acts of Violence against Women.
(2) Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in the form of activities:
a. Regional Action Plan;
c. preparation of an effective information system; and
d. cooperation with institutions that have activities in the prevention of acts of Violence against Women.

Article 14
Regional Governments are obligated to assist in handling acts of Violence against Women.

Paragraph 2
Duties of Regional Government in Child Protection

Article 15
(1) Regional Governments are obligated to carry out Child Protection in the Region.
(2) Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented through the formulation of long-term, medium-term, and short-term Child Protection strategic plans.
(3) The strategic formulation as referred to in paragraph (2) must be integrated with Regional development planning documents.
(4) Regional Governments in formulating strategic plans

JDIH PASANGKAYU REGENCY
as referred to in paragraph (3) may involve the community, social institutions and/or other institutions that carry out activities related to Child Protection.

Article 16
The Regional Government provides guidance and direction for Children to embrace religion in accordance with the religion and beliefs of their parents.

Article 17
(1) Regional Governments are obligated to provide comprehensive health facilities for Children.
(2) Comprehensive health facilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of community health centers and hospitals.
(3) Comprehensive health as referred to in paragraph (1) includes promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, both for basic health services and referrals.
(4) Comprehensive health as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be provided free of charge for families who are unable to afford it or poor families.
(5) Regional Governments shall implement activities to support the provision of breast milk to infants.

Article 18
(1) Regional Governments are obligated to provide basic and secondary education for Children.
(2) In providing education as referred to in paragraph (1), Regional Governments shall provide educational cost assistance or free education for students who come from poor families and/or neglected Children.

Article 19
(1) Regional Governments are obligated to accommodate and/or care for Children who are neglected by their parents and/or family.
(2) The accommodation and/or care of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out through cooperation with institutions, social foundations, or Orphanages.

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU
Article 20
(1) The Regional Government is obligated to provide special Protection for Child Victims of riots or Victims of natural disasters.
(2) Special protection as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of fulfillment of needs:
a. basic needs consisting of food, clothing, shelter, education, health, learning and recreation, security guarantees, and equal treatment; and
b. special needs for Children with disabilities and Children experiencing psychosocial disorders.

Article 21
(1) Regional Governments are obligated to undertake prevention of efforts and acts of violence against Children.
(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out in the form of activities that include:
a. early detection and intervention;
b. socialization, seminars or workshops;
c. preparation of an effective information system; and
d. cooperation with institutions that have activities in the prevention of acts of violence against Children.

Article 22
Regional Governments are obligated to assist in handling acts of Violence against Children.

Second Part
Authority

Article 23
(1) Regional Government has the authority to:
a. shelter Victims of acts of Violence against Women and Children; and
b. shelter and/or care for neglected Children.
(2) Regional Government in exercising the authority as referred to in paragraph (1) may conduct cooperation with related parties.

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU
Article 24
(1) Related parties as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) are:
a. social community organizations, institutions or orphanages that have activities related to the protection, care, shelter, guidance, empowerment of Children and/or Women;
b. social workers or volunteers;
c. advocates; and
d. religious leaders.
(2) Cooperation as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) shall be conducted in the form of a cooperation agreement.

CHAPTER VI
HANDLING OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN
Article 25
(1) The Regional Government shall prepare medical personnel at community health centers or at hospitals specifically to handle Victims of Violence against Women and Children.
(2) Medical personnel as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be obligated to provide complete health services to Victims of Violence against Women and Children.

Article 26
(1) The Regional Government is obligated to assist in the provision of special rooms for Victims of Violence against Women and Children.
(2) The special rooms as referred to in paragraph (1) are located at the Resort Police and at every Sector Police in the region.

Article 27
(1) Social workers or volunteer personnel as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter b are tasked with accompanying Victims in facing the process of resolving acts of violence.
(2) In carrying out the duties as referred to in paragraph (1), social workers or volunteer personnel conduct activities:
a. counseling or guidance to strengthen and provide a sense of security for Victims;

b. provide information regarding the Victim's right to obtain Protection from the police and the stipulation of a Protection order from the court;
c. escort the Victim to a Safe House or alternative residence; and
d. conduct integrated coordination in providing services to the Victim with agencies or related parties needed by the Victim.

Article 28
(1) Regional Governments are obligated to prepare Safe Houses.
(2) Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (1) are used for temporary handling of Victims of Violence against Women and Children.
(3) Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (2) are provided in every Sub-district.

Article 29
(1) Advocates as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter c assist in providing legal services for Victims or provide legal consultation in the form of information regarding the rights of Victims and the judicial process;
(2) Advocates must accompany and/or represent Victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court proceedings.
(3) Information on violence from Victims that is personal in nature must be kept confidential by advocates.
(4) Advocates may coordinate with fellow law enforcers, companion volunteers, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

Article 30
Religious counselors as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter d have the duties to:
a. provide spiritual guidance or strengthening of faith and piety to Victims; and
b. provide explanation regarding the rights and obligations of Victims.

CHAPTER VII
GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION
Part One

Guidance

Article 31
(1) The Regional Government implements guidance for Women and Children.
(2) Guidance for Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted through:
   a. socialization;
   b. mentoring; and/or
   c. training.
(3) Guidance for Women and Children is conducted by the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of Women and Children empowerment.

Second Part
Supervision

Article 32
(1) Regional Governments conduct supervision in the context of Protection of Women and Children.
(2) Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through monitoring and evaluation.
(3) Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) is implemented periodically at least 2 (two) times per year.

CHAPTER VIII
COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION
Article 33
(1) The community participates in efforts to protect Women and Children.
(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in the form of:
a. providing data or information regarding Women and Children who have not yet received fulfillment of rights from the Regional Government;
b. providing information on any conditions that tend to lead to acts of violence against Women and Children to the competent authorities and/or the Regional Government; and/or

JDIH PASANGKAYU REGENCY
c. promoting the Protection of Women and Children.

CHAPTER IX
FUNDING
Article 34
(1) Funding for the implementation of Women and Child Protection shall be borne by:
a. the state revenue and expenditure budget;
b. the regional revenue and expenditure budget; and
c. other lawful and non-binding sources.
(2) Funding as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a shall be allocated in the Regional Government Work Unit that administers governmental affairs in the field of the empowerment of Women and Children.

I. GENERAL
Protection from violence that threatens the safety of Women and Children must be provided from an early stage. Protection is necessary to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. For example, Women and Children becoming Victims of abuse, neglect, eviction and mistreatment by parents or other persons. For Women and Children experiencing such problems, the Regional Government must provide a Safe House and an adequate budget.

Likewise, the role of various parties to help address violence issues faced by Women and Children must be in synergy with the programs and activities of the Regional Government. Social community organizations, volunteers, social workers, medical personnel, advocates and religious leaders must also be mobilized and can participate in providing and enhancing a sense of security for Women and Children.

For the Regional Government of Pasangkayu Regency, a concrete step in creating a legal umbrella to provide and enhance a sense of security as well as prevent acts of violence against Women and Children, is by establishing a Regional Regulation on Protection for Children and Women. Furthermore, the implementation of protection for Women and Children shall be carried out based on the principles of: justice; respect for the rights of Victims; gender justice and equality; non-discrimination; and the best interests of Victims.
