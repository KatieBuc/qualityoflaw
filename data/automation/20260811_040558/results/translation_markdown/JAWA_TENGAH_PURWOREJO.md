# REGIONAL REGULATION OF PURWOREJO REGENCY

# NUMBER 13 OF 2022

# CONCERNING

# THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

# OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

# BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

# THE REGENT OF PURWOREJO,

Considering: a. that the objective of the state is to protect the entire homeland of the Indonesian Nation by providing guarantees for security, equality, and welfare;

   - b. that women and children are still in a vulnerable position from various forms of violence, exploitation, discrimination, maltreatment and neglect so that there needs to be strategic efforts from the Regional Government together with related parties to provide systematic and comprehensive protection;
   - c. that to provide a legal foundation and guidelines in the implementation of protection of women and children in the Region, there needs to be integrated and comprehensive regulation to realize a Purworejo Regency that is friendly to women and children;
   - d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;

In View of: 1. Article 18 Paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

2. Law Number 13 of 1950 concerning the Establishment of Regency Regions within the Province of Central Java (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1950 Number 42);
3. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587), as has been amended several times, most recently by Law Number 11 of 2020 concerning Job Creation (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2020 Number 245, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 6573);

With the Mutual Approval of THE REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF PURWOREJO REGENCY and [continued in source text after "And"]

# REGENT OF PURWOREJO

# DECIDES:

To Establish: REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Purworejo Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous Region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Purworejo.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of governmental affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Technical Regional Apparatus is the Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
6. Village is a village and customary village or called by other names, hereinafter referred to as Village, is a legal community unit that has territorial boundaries authorized to regulate and manage governmental affairs, the interests of the local community based on community initiative, rights of origin, and or traditional rights that are recognized and respected in the government system of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia and located in Purworejo Regency.
7. Village Government is the Village Head or called by other names who is assisted by village apparatus as an element of Village Government administration.
8. Urban Village (Kelurahan) is part of the territory of the District as a District apparatus.
9. Female Victim is a woman who experiences suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, economically, socially, and other losses caused by violence, exploitation and/or gender-based discrimination whether occurring in the public sphere or in the sphere of personal life.
10. Child Victim is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb who experiences suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, economically, socially, and other losses caused by violence, exploitation and/or discrimination whether occurring in the public sphere or in the sphere of personal life.
11. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
12. Protection of Children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
13. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion either direct or indirect based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, tribe, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that result in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individual and collective in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural fields and other aspects of life.
14. Violence is any act of abuse of physical force with or without using means, unlawfully and causing misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of freedom unlawfully.
15. Exploitation is an action with or without the victim's consent which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or forced services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.
16. Child Maltreatment is all forms of violence against children committed by those who should be responsible and/or those who have power over children, who should be trustworthy namely parents, close family, teachers, supervisors, law enforcement officers, caregivers and companions.
17. Prevention is all efforts to prevent Discrimination, Violence, and Exploitation, as well as recurrence thereof against Women and Children.
18. Services is all efforts and actions to fulfill the rights of Female Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and neglect which include complaint Services, health, social rehabilitation, legal assistance and enforcement, repatriation, social reintegration, and restitution.
19. Assistance is all efforts and actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for self-empowerment of Female Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Child Maltreatment, and neglect of Women and Children to resolve problems faced so as to provide a sense of security and comfort and have readiness and/or ability to obtain Services and live their lives.
20. Assistant is a person or group or organization that is trusted and/or has skills and knowledge to assist Female Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and Exploitation with the aim that Female Victims and Child Victims feel safe and comfortable in accessing Services.
21. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a regional technical implementation unit established by the Regional Government in providing services for Female Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment, neglect and other problems.
22. Forum for the Protection of Violence Victims hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK is a coordination forum for Prevention, handling and empowerment of Female Victims and Child Victims of Violence and other problems whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner and established at the Regional, District, and Village levels.
23. Integrated Service Center is a non-structural institution that coordinates the implementation of an integrated Service system for Female Victims and Child Victims.
24. Social Reintegration is all efforts to strengthen the readiness and ability of Female Victims and Child Victims to regain their welfare in social, political, economic, and cultural life with dignity, including strengthening acceptance and support into the family and/or community environment for the protection and fulfillment of the rights of Female Victims and Child Victims.
25. Restitution is compensation payment given to Female Victims, Child Victims and/or victims' families by the perpetrator based on a court decision that has permanent legal force for material and/or immaterial losses suffered by Female Victims, Child Victims and/or victims' families.
26. Legal Aid Provider is a legal aid institution or community organization that provides legal aid services based on laws and regulations.
27. Business World is a commercial organization, the entire industrial environment and production of goods/services, including State-Owned Enterprises, and Regional-Owned Enterprises as well as private or entrepreneurs and their networks, who will carry out their social responsibilities.

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection of women and children is based on:

   - a. humanity;
   - b. respect and fulfillment of the rights of women and children;
   - c. non-discrimination and non-exploitation;
   - d. harmony;
   - e. shelter;
   - f. justice and gender equality;
   - g. welfare; and
   - h. proportionality.

#### Article 3

The implementation of protection for women and children is intended to provide a legal basis in implementing the protection of women and children in an integrated and comprehensive manner as well as to realize synergy between the Regional Government and the community in protecting women and children as vulnerable groups who are susceptible to becoming victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Mistreatment and/or neglect towards a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency.

#### Article 4

The implementation of protection for women and children aims to:

   - a. prevent all forms of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women;
   - b. prevent all forms of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, Mistreatment of Children, and/or neglect;
   - c. encourage the reduction of risks of various forms of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect against women and children;
   - d. provide guarantees of protection for women and children;
   - e. provide ease in accessing protection services for women and children;
   - f. realize a social life that is safe, comfortable, peaceful and free from threats directed at women and children;
   - g. create a social system that encourages respect for women and compassion for children so as to form social safeguarding as well as social protection for women and children; and
   - h. realize Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency.

#### Article 5

The scope of this regional regulation includes:

   - a. rights of women and children;
   - b. rights of victims;
   - c. duties and responsibilities;
   - d. efforts for the protection of women and children;
   - e. implementation of Women's Protection;
   - f. implementation of Child Protection;
   - g. Integrated Service Center;
   - h. role of Village/Sub-district Government;
      - i. institutional matters;
   - j. cooperation and partnership;
   - k. Women-Friendly and Child-Friendly Regency; and
   - l. data and information system for women and children;
   - m. awards; and
   - n. funding.

# CHAPTER II

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

Rights of Women

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government respects and safeguards the rights of women comprehensively based on laws and regulations.
2. Every woman has the right to:
   - a. receive equal and equitable treatment;
   - b. obtain equal and fair opportunities in various fields, including education, employment, politics, and social;
   - c. obtain complete information for legal protection and assistance;
   - d. obtain protection and defense from all forms of Discriminatory actions;
   - e. obtain protection and defense from acts of Violence;
   - f. obtain protection and defense from all forms of Exploitation; and
   - g. receive excellent and professional services.

Second Part Rights of the Child

#### Article 7

1. The Regional Government safeguards and guarantees the complete fulfillment of children's rights based on the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. Every child has the right:
   - a. to live, grow, develop, and participate reasonably in accordance with human dignity and worth;
   - b. to obtain self-identity from birth;
   - c. to worship according to their religion, to think, and to express themselves in accordance with their level of intelligence and age, under the guidance of parents or a Guardian;
   - d. to know their parents, to be raised, and to be cared for by their own parents and to receive guarantees for good survival and an outpouring of affection;
   - e. to obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual, and social needs;
   - f. to receive protection and defense from Discriminatory treatment, Violence, Exploitation, neglect, cruelty, abuse, injustice, and/or other mistreatment;
   - g. to obtain guaranteed protection from destructive influences;
   - h. to obtain protection from abuse in political activities, involvement in armed conflicts, social unrest, events containing elements of Violence, and warfare;
      - i. to obtain protection from being targets of persecution, torture, or the imposition of inhuman punishment, and (the right to) freedom in accordance with law;
   - j. to receive legal assistance and other assistance;
   - k. to obtain education and teaching in the context of developing their personality and level of intelligence according to interests and talents;
   - l. to receive protection in educational units from sexual crimes and Violence committed by educators, education personnel, fellow students, and/or other parties;
   - m. to obtain special education; and children who have excellence have the right to receive special education;
   - n. to receive a good environment and food intake for growth and development according to their age;
   - o. to think, express, state and have their opinions heard, to receive, seek, and provide information according to their level of intelligence and age for their self-development in accordance with the values of decency and propriety; and
   - p. to obtain time to rest, and to utilize free time, to socialize with children of the same age, to play, to recreate, and to create in accordance with their interests, talents, and level of intelligence for self-development.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

## Part One

Rights of Female Victims

#### Article 8

1. Regional Government is responsible for the fulfillment of the rights of Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.
2. The rights of Women Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. obtaining information related to the protection of women, victim service procedures and developments in case handling;
   - b. reporting and having their reports followed up, providing statements, and views without pressure;
   - c. participating in case handling and in efforts to eliminate Violence against women;
   - d. obtaining protection of confidentiality;
   - e. obtaining protection for the security of self, family, and property from threats;
   - f. being free from all forms of stereotypes, stigma and other discrimination;
   - g. obtaining Services and recovery needed;
   - h. obtaining restitution; and
      - i. forming and joining organizations;
3. The fulfillment of rights as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in a friendly and professional manner that is victim-oriented.

Second Part Rights of Child Victims

#### Article 9

1. Regional Governments are responsible for the fulfillment of the rights of Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect.
2. The rights of Child victims as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. obtaining legal protection and Legal Assistance;
   - b. obtaining counseling Assistance;
   - c. obtaining education guarantees;
   - d. obtaining guarantees of personal and family protection;
   - e. freedom from various forms of stigma;
   - f. guarantees of residence and living needs for survival, growth and development;
   - g. protection of personal property or wealth;
   - h. guarantees of confidentiality;
      - i. obtaining rehabilitation; and/or
   - j. obtaining restitution.

# CHAPTER IV

## DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

First

## Part General

#### Article 10

The Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus, community, Business Sector, parents and/or guardians have duties and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children so that welfare is realized both physically, spiritually, and socially.

## Part Two

Duties and Responsibilities of Regional Government

#### Article 11

1. The duties and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 10, include:
   - a. making policies to encourage Prevention efforts against various forms of threats to women and children as well as providing comprehensive protection;
   - b. implementing victim-oriented policies;
   - c. conducting data collection and data verification related to information on women and children;
   - d. conducting various forms of cooperation to enhance protection efforts for women and children;
   - e. preparing supporting facilities and infrastructure;
   - f. conducting guidance and empowerment for women;
   - g. providing safe houses for children that support development and growth as well as food intake and care; and
   - h. allocating budget according to needs and regional financial capacity;
      - i. actively involving the Village Government, community and Business Sector in conducting protection efforts for women and children; and
   - j. realizing Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Worthy Regency.
2. The duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve other related Regional Apparatus.
3. The duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out with victim orientation based on laws and regulations.

## Part Three

Duties and Responsibilities of the Community

#### Article 12

1. The duties and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 10 include:
   - a. giving attention to women's and children's issues as vulnerable groups to acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect;
   - b. preventing various acts and/or threats of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect against women and children;
   - c. providing information and/or reporting alleged acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect against women and children to the authorized institutions;
   - d. becoming part of the social safety net to protect women and children; and
   - e. participating in realizing Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency.
2. In carrying out the duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), implementation shall be in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Four

Duties and Responsibilities of the Business Sector

#### Article 13

1. The Business Sector is obligated to carry out the duties and responsibilities as referred to in Article 10 covering: a. establishing company regulations that are friendly towards female workers;
   - b. establishing policies that prevent and protect female workers from acts of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation in the work environment;
   - c. providing counseling or socialization in the work environment for Prevention, protection and reporting related to acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women;
   - d. refusing to employ underage child workers in accordance with statutory regulations;
   - e. producing products that are safe for children;
   - f. providing corporate social responsibility for women and child protection programs;
   - g. providing special rooms for breastfeeding mothers or lactation rooms;
   - h. not involving children in company activities that endanger safety and/or are contrary to statutory regulations;
      - i. reporting if there are acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect against women and children in the work environment;
   - j. providing special childcare facilities for certain companies in accordance with statutory regulations; and
   - k. participating in realizing Purworejo Regency as a Women-Friendly and Child-Appropriate Regency.
2. The Business Sector carries out the duties and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) in accordance with the provisions of statutory regulations.
3. The Business Sector that does not fulfill the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be subject to sanctions in the form of verbal warning and/or written warning.
4. In the event that the sanctions as referred to in paragraph (3) are not complied with, action may be taken to temporarily revoke the permit and may be continued with permanent permit revocation.
5. Permit revocation as referred to in paragraph (4) shall be carried out by the relevant Regional Apparatus in accordance with the provisions of statutory regulations.

Fifth Part Responsibility of Parents/Guardians

#### Article 14

The duties and responsibilities of parents/guardians as referred to in Article 10 include:

   - a. nurturing and educating children according to their intelligence based on religious values and Pancasila;
   - b. providing good and nutritious food intake in accordance with their age;
   - c. developing children according to their abilities, talents and interests;
   - d. providing protection from Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, neglect and/or injustice;
   - e. preventing marriage at a child age;
   - f. encouraging children's ability to express opinions or the expression of their thoughts as appreciation for children; and
   - g. guiding children who engage in deviant social behavior.

# CHAPTER V

## EFFORTS FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 15

1. Efforts for the protection of women and children are carried out by the Regional Government by means of:
   - a. conducting Prevention of the occurrence of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect against women and children;
   - b. conducting Prevention of trafficking of women and children;
   - c. conducting vulnerability risk management;
   - d. conducting handling and Assistance for Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect;
   - e. conducting empowerment of Women Victims and Child Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect;
   - f. conducting protection strategies for women and children; and
   - g. capacity strengthening of Regional Apparatus and Village Government in implementing protection for women and children.
2. In conducting protection efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may involve:
   - a. family;
   - b. community;
   - c. Business Sector; and
   - d. media.

# CHAPTER VI

## IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S PROTECTION

## Part One

Prevention

#### Article 16

1. Regional Government organizes the Protection of Women by conducting Prevention against the occurrence of various acts of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. conducting socialization or counseling on the protection of women;
   - b. increasing awareness, participation and public concern over the impacts and legal consequences of acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment and/or neglect through program integration for families, communities, and the Business Sector;
   - c. internalization of anti-Discrimination values, anti-Violence, and anti-Exploitation through formal education and non-formal education;
   - d. providing accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement, and social Services;
   - e. encouraging the establishment of women's forums as a platform for collective learning, support systems and cadre development;
   - f. guidance and empowerment of women to be able to be independent and defend themselves;
   - g. creating transportation and work environments that are safe and women-friendly;
   - h. building and providing digitally-based information systems and media that are easily accessible for information, protection requests and reports;
      - i. increasing the capacity of government institutions through knowledge enhancement, capability improvement and service improvement; and
   - j. conducting network building and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women.
3. In implementing Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (2), Regional Government may involve and/or cooperate with media, communities, the Business Sector, and/or educational institutions.

## Part Two

Protection

#### Article 17

1. The Regional Government provides comprehensive protection to women from various actions:
   - a. threats;
   - b. Discrimination;
   - c. Violence; and
   - d. Exploitation.
2. Every form of threatening action as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, whether carried out verbally or non-verbally, that is intentionally directed at women so as to make them feel that their life or safety is threatened, is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
3. Every form of Discrimination as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b that is carried out and leads to gender, racial, or equality-based injustice, thereby degrading the status, freedom, and opportunities of women, is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
4. Every form of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c that is carried out in the form of physical, psychological, or sexual violence, thereby causing trauma to women, is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
5. Every form of Exploitation as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d that is carried out in any form that degrades the dignity, worth, and honor of women and causes harm to women is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.

#### Article 18

Regional Governments provide protection to women by means of:

   - a. establishing social safeguards;
   - b. developing advanced referral services that are women-friendly; and
   - c. providing supporting facilities and infrastructure for the protection of women.

### Paragraph 1

Social Security

#### Article 19

1. Social safeguarding as referred to in Article 18 letter a shall be carried out by the Regional Government together with the Village/Urban Village Government, the Business Sector, and the community.
2. Social safeguarding as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be carried out by means of:
   - a. reporting if there is information on the occurrence of acts threatening Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women;
   - b. implementing protection for Women Victims of acts threatening Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation;
   - c. conducting assistance and empowerment for Women Victims of acts threatening Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation; and/or
   - d. conducting safeguarding measures against perpetrators of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against Women who potentially endanger life safety.

### Paragraph 2

Advanced Referral Services

#### Article 20

1. Advanced referral services as referred to in Article 18 letter b shall be established by the Regional Government to provide protection quickly and appropriately.
2. Advanced referral services as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be carried out by means including:
   - a. creating and implementing standard guidelines for handling complaints or reports that are excellent and women-friendly;
   - b. integrating the complaint or report system between Regional Apparatus in the fields of communication, information, public services, villages, empowerment, women, security, legal aid and enforcement;
   - c. integrating the complaint or report system based on Urban Villages (Kelurahan) and Villages (Desa);
   - d. integrating the protection and legal assistance system with each designated legal aid organization;
   - e. opening complaint or report services based on digital media and digital applications; and
   - f. integrating the needs of victims for counseling, health services, visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum, as well as other service needs.
3. Advanced referral services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided free of charge to victims in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

### Paragraph 3

Provision of Supporting Facilities and Infrastructure for the Protection of Women

#### Article 21

1. The provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in Article 18 letter c aims to maximize the sense of security, comfort and peace for Women Victims of acts of threats, Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.
2. The provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. provision of temporary safe houses;
   - b. provision of daily living needs during the process of Assistance in handling at temporary safe houses;
   - c. provision of Assistants in the field of health and counseling if needed;
   - d. conducting empowerment to increase the capacity of women in the future; and
   - e. provision of assistant personnel in the field of mental and spiritual.
3. The provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out by the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus.

## Part Three

Handling

#### Article 22

1. The handling of Women Victims and Child Victims aims to provide appropriate action in handling victims and witnesses in acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women and children.
2. The handling as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. complaints or reports;
   - b. protection needs;
   - c. physical and psychological health;
   - d. rehabilitation;
   - e. Social Reintegration;
   - f. legal assistance; and
   - g. Restitution.
3. The handling as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out by the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus.
4. Further provisions regarding legal assistance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter f, shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

### Paragraph 1

Complaint or Report

#### Article 23

1. Complaints or reports regarding acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women shall be made easily, quickly, and accurately.
2. Every person who sees or knows of acts or threats of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women shall make a complaint or report.
3. Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may be addressed to:
   - a. Village/Urban Village Government;
   - b. FPKK;
   - c. Legal Aid Providers;
   - d. health service facilities;
   - e. law enforcement officers;
   - f. Regional Apparatus implementing governmental affairs in the field of public peace and order;
   - g. non-governmental organizations; and/or
   - h. Social Media managed by related Regional Apparatus.
4. Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may be made by means of:
   - a. direct;
   - b. through telephone;
   - c. through email or direct message to social media; and/or
   - d. through letter.
5. Recipients of complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (3) shall keep confidential the identity of the reporter.

### Paragraph 2

Protection Needs

#### Article 24

1. The need for protection aims to protect the identity and personal safety of the reporter, victim and/or witness.
2. The report recipient as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (3) shall protect the reporter as well as the victim.
3. In the event that the report recipient does not have protection facilities and infrastructure, the report recipient shall coordinate with the FPKK and/or law enforcement officials who have the facilities and infrastructure.

### Paragraph 3

Physical and Psychological Health

#### Article 25

1. Physical and psychological health for Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation is intended to reduce and treat physical and psychological trauma.
2. FKPP facilitates or carries out assistance/accompaniment or handling of Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation.

### Paragraph 4

Rehabilitation

#### Article 26

1. Female Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect who experience trauma shall undergo a gradual rehabilitation process both physically, psychologically, and socially.
2. Rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus and/or the community to assist in accelerating the rehabilitation process.
3. The Regional Government shall guarantee the fulfillment of living needs of Female Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect during the rehabilitation process.

### Paragraph 5

Restitution

#### Article 27

1. The granting of Restitution shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. The amount of Restitution to be paid by the perpetrator to Women Victims of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, mistreatment, and/or neglect or their heirs shall be based on:
   - a. the result of a mediation agreement; and/or
   - b. a court decision.
3. The Regional Government through the relevant Regional Apparatus shall oversee the Restitution payment process to ensure it is appropriate and on target.

# CHAPTER VII

## IMPLEMENTATION OF CHILD PROTECTION

## Part One

Prevention

#### Article 28

1. The Regional Government creates policies and programs aimed at preventing various acts of Discrimination, Violence, Exploitation, maltreatment, and/or neglect against children.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by means of:
   - a. conducting socialization or counseling;
   - b. increasing awareness of the impact and legal consequences through program integration for families, communities, and the Business Sector;
   - c. internalization of anti-Violence, anti-Exploitation, anti-maltreatment, and anti-neglect values through formal education and non-formal education;
   - d. encouraging the establishment of children's forums as a platform for collective learning, support systems, and cadre development;
   - e. guidance and education for parents, guardians, or foster parents regarding parenting patterns in accordance with growth and development, appreciation, and outpouring of affection;
   - f. creating safe and child-friendly transportation;
   - g. building and providing digital-based information systems and media that are easily accessible for information, protection requests, and reports; and
   - h. guidance towards the capacity of families, substitute families, or care institutions in caregiving abilities oriented towards growth and development and appreciation of children.
3. In implementing Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Government may involve and/or cooperate with media, communities, the Business Sector and/or educational institutions.

## Part Two

Protection

#### Article 29

1. The Regional Government provides active and comprehensive protection to children from various acts of: a. threat;
   - b. Violence;
   - c. Exploitation;
   - d. neglect;
   - e. Child Maltreatment; and
   - f. Injustice.
2. Every form of threatening act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, whether verbal or nonverbal, that is intentionally directed at a child so as to make them feel threatened in life or safety is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
3. Every form of violent act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, whether physical, psychological, or sexual violence that causes trauma to the child is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
4. Every form of exploitative act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c that is carried out in any form and is not in accordance with the child's developmental age according to general views, legislation, or local wisdom and causes harm to the child is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
5. Every form of neglectful act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d that disregards the responsibility as a parent, guardian, or foster parent toward the child is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
6. Every form of maltreatment act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e that is carried out by exploiting closeness or abusing responsibility toward the child so as to cause physical or psychological trauma or is deemed contrary to laws and regulations is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.
7. Every form of unjust act as referred to in paragraph (1) letter f, carried out by parents toward children by discriminating or showing favoritism, as well as injustice in equality between males and females, is prohibited for any reason whatsoever.

#### Article 30

Forms of protection provided by Regional Government as referred to in Article 29 include: a. reduction of vulnerability risks;

   - b. establishing social safeguards; and/or
   - c. provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure for child protection.

### Paragraph 1

Vulnerability Risk Reduction

#### Article 31

1. The Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus or related Regional Apparatus reduces vulnerability risks with comprehensive and well-targeted vulnerability risk management.
2. Efforts to reduce vulnerability risks as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. facilitation of the implementation of vulnerability risk reduction;
   - b. facilitation of the implementation of justice in accordance with laws and regulations; and
   - c. facilitation of community capacity strengthening.
3. Facilities for the implementation of vulnerability risk reduction as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a, include:
   - a. identification and early detection of potential in the community, institutions or the Business World related to acts of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and neglect of children;
   - b. taking immediate action to mitigate vulnerability risks to children based on the results of identification and early detection;
   - c. providing guidance and assistance to parents or guardians who are identified as experiencing difficulties and/or obstacles in caring for, nurturing, and protecting children; and
   - d. providing guidance and assistance to families who are identified as experiencing difficulties and/or obstacles in building healthy and democratic relationships.
4. Facilities for the implementation of justice in accordance with laws and regulations as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b, include:
   - a. the use of diversion or mediation approaches in resolving cases of children suspected of being in conflict with the law in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
   - b. a decision on the amount of restitution that must be paid by the perpetrator to the victim in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
   - c. punishment of perpetrators of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations and justice that lives in society;
5. Facilitation of community capacity strengthening as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is carried out including:
   - a. conducting outreach and paralegal training to the community to be able to provide initial advocacy to victims;
   - b. establishing and fostering children's forums as a space for appreciating children's aspirations and support systems; and/or
   - c. providing financial support for advocacy efforts by Legal Aid Providers related to handling children as victims.

### Paragraph 2

Social Security

#### Article 32

1. The Regional Government together with the Village Government, the community, and the Business Sector shall carry out social safeguarding.
2. Social safeguarding as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. reporting if there is information on Violence, Exploitation, neglect and/or Mistreatment Against Children;
   - b. protection of child witnesses and Child Victims by separating them from perpetrators in temporary care facilities;
   - c. assistance to Child Victims; and
   - d. securing perpetrators of violence who potentially endanger the safety of life.

### Paragraph 3

Provision of Facilities and Infrastructure

#### Article 33

1. The Regional Government provides supporting facilities and infrastructure to maximize a sense of security, comfort, and peace for Child Victims.
2. The provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. provision of temporary safe houses;
   - b. provision of education, opportunities to play and good food intake, as well as daily living needs during the process of assistance in handling at temporary safe houses;
   - c. provision of assistants in the field of health and counseling if needed;
   - d. provision of assistants in the field of mental, spiritual, and religious matters; and
   - e. providing social security for vulnerable families.

#### Article 34

1. The handling of victims aims to provide appropriate action in handling victims and witnesses in acts of Violence, Exploitation, Mistreatment of Children, and/or neglect.
2. The handling of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. complaints or reports;
   - b. protection needs;
   - c. physical and psychological health;
   - d. rehabilitation;
   - e. Social Reintegration;
   - f. legal assistance; and
   - g. Restitution.
3. Further provisions regarding legal assistance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter f, shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

### Paragraph 1

Complaints and Reports

#### Article 35

1. Complaints or reports regarding acts of Violence, Exploitation, Mistreatment of Children and/or neglect shall be made easily, quickly and accurately.
2. Every person who sees or knows of any action as referred to in paragraph (1) shall make a complaint or report.
3. Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (2) may be addressed to:
   - a. Village/Urban Village Government;
   - b. FPKK;
   - c. Legal Aid Providers;
   - d. health service facilities;
   - e. law enforcement officials;
   - f. Satpol PP;
   - g. non-governmental organizations; and/or
   - h. Social Media managed by related Regional Apparatus.
4. Complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (2) may be made by means of:
   - a. direct;
   - b. by telephone;
   - c. by email or direct message to social media; and/or
   - d. by letter.
5. Recipients of complaints or reports as referred to in paragraph (3) are obligated to keep confidential the identity of the reporter.

### Paragraph 2

Protection Needs

#### Article 36

1. The need for protection aims to protect the identity and personal safety of the reporter, victim, and/or witness.
2. The report recipient as referred to in Article 35 paragraph (3) is to protect the reporter, victim, and witness.
3. If the report recipient does not have protection facilities and infrastructure, then the report recipient shall coordinate with FKPP and/or law enforcement officials who have the facilities and infrastructure.

### Paragraph 3

Physical and Psychological Health

#### Article 37

1. Physical and psychological health for Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect aims to reduce and treat physical and psychological trauma.
2. FKPP facilitates the examination and treatment of physical and psychological health for Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect.
3. FKPP facilitates the conduct of visum et repertum and visum et psikiatrikum for Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect for the purpose of criminal investigation.

### Paragraph 4

Rehabilitation

#### Article 38

1. Child Victims of acts of Violence, Exploitation, Mistreatment of Children, and/or neglect who experience trauma shall undergo rehabilitation in stages both physically, psychologically, and socially.
2. Rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus and may involve related Regional Apparatus and/or the community to assist in accelerating the rehabilitation process.
3. The Regional Government shall guarantee the living needs of Child Victims as referred to in paragraph (1) during the rehabilitation process.
4. During the rehabilitation process, the provision of food intake, education, play activities, and appreciation for children who experience trauma as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be adjusted to age and developmental needs.

### Paragraph 5

Restitution

#### Article 39

1. The granting of Restitution shall be in accordance with laws and regulations.
2. The amount of Restitution paid by the perpetrator to Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Mistreatment of Children, and/or neglect, through parents, guardians, or the Regional Government, shall be based on:
   - a. the results of a mediation agreement; and/or
   - b. a court decision.
3. The Regional Government through related Regional Apparatus shall oversee the Restitution payment process to ensure it is used for the needs of Child Victims of Violence, Exploitation, Mistreatment of Children, and/or neglect.

# CHAPTER VIII

## INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER

## Part One

Service Principles

#### Article 40

1. Services in fulfilling the rights of women and children are implemented based on the following principles:
   - a. empathy;
   - b. confidentiality;
   - c. fast and responsive;
   - d. humanity;
   - e. documented;
   - f. inclusion;
   - g. impartial;
   - h. victim-oriented;
      - i. accessible and excellent;
   - j. professional; and
   - k. sustainability.
2. The provision of services is carried out with attention to the situation and condition of the reporter, victim, and/or witness.
3. To improve the provision of services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government establishes community-based integrated women and child protection institutions at the Village/Urban Village level.

Second Part Form and Scope of Services

#### Article 41

1. The form and scope of the Integrated Service Center for realizing the protection of women and children include: a. Complaint or report and protection services; b. Health and rehabilitation services;
   - c. Legal services;
   - d. Education and empowerment services;
   - e. Economic independence services; and
   - f. Spiritual services.
2. The provision of Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided to victims and/or witnesses free of charge in accordance with the laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## ROLE OF VILLAGE/SUB-DISTRICT GOVERNMENT

#### Article 42

1. Village/Sub-district Government is responsible for implementing the protection of women and children in the Village/Sub-district environment.
2. Village/Sub-district Government may involve Village Development Non-Commissioned Officers and/or Community Security and Order Development Police to provide protection for women and children.
3. Village/Sub-district Government may establish a special institution to implement the protection of women and children.

#### Article 43

1. Forms of protection for women and children as referred to in Article 42 include:
   - a. receiving complaints or reports from residents;
   - b. providing protection to reporters, victims, and/or witnesses;
   - c. carrying out social safeguarding measures against perpetrators;
   - d. organizing mediation to resolve disputes;
   - e. imposing sanctions on village officials who commit acts of Discrimination, Violence, and/or Exploitation against women; and
   - f. imposing sanctions on village officials who commit acts of Violence, Exploitation, Maltreatment of Children, and/or neglect.
2. The Regional Government provides guidance to Village/Sub-district Governments in enhancing capacity to provide protection for women and children.
3. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of:
   - a. Assistance;
   - b. education and training; and
   - c. periodic monitoring.

# CHAPTER X

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 44

1. The Regional Government through the Technical Regional Apparatus shall carry out the protection of women and children.
2. In implementing the protection of women and children as referred to in paragraph (1), it may involve Regional Apparatus that implement governmental affairs in the fields of:
   - a. community and village empowerment;
   - b. social affairs, population control and family planning;
   - c. health;
   - d. communication and informatics;
   - e. industry, manpower, and transmigration;
   - f. law;
   - g. mental and spiritual;
   - h. population administration and civil registration; and/or
      - i. public tranquility, public order and community protection.
3. The Regional Government shall establish UPTD PPA to provide maximum Services and coordinate among Regional Apparatus in implementing the protection of women and children.

#### Article 45

1. UPTD PPA performs coordination functions with FPKK in implementing the protection of women and children.
2. The implementation of coordination functions with FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. coordinating and synchronizing prevention, services, and empowerment for Women Victims and Child Victims;
   - b. maintaining and developing FPKK in prevention, services, and empowerment of Women Victims and Child Victims in a networked manner and a referral system;
   - c. conducting education on the values of non-Discrimination, non-Violence, non-Exploitation, non-Mistreatment of Children and/or neglect towards women and children;
   - d. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of the protection of women and children;
   - e. collecting, compiling, and presenting report data on Women Victims and Child Victims;
   - f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of the protection of women and children.
3. UPTD PPA may conduct guidance and Assistance to:
   - a. Purworejo women's forum;
   - b. Purworejo children's forum;
   - c. task force for the protection of women and children;
   - d. working group for the protection of women and children;
   - e. non-governmental organizations; and/or
   - f. other organizations related to women and children.
4. Guidance and Assistance as referred to in paragraph (3) aims to encourage community participation in the implementation of the protection of women and children.

##### THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

##### I. GENERAL

Constitutionally, the purpose of the state is none other than to protect the entire homeland, advance general welfare, and also educate the life of the nation. This is clearly stated in the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. The phrase protecting the entire nation contains a philosophical as well as moral dimension, where the state without discrimination must be able to become the protector of all its people. This commitment must be manifested in various regulations and policies made by the state, so that it is no longer something that can be negotiated.

As is known, the position of women and children is very vulnerable to various kinds of violations including violations of their basic rights. Strategic steps need to be taken supported by policies, such as disseminating awareness of the importance of protection and at the same time for the fulfillment of their rights both in the family, community and at every level of education. The rights and protection of women and children are basically also mandated in the 1945 Constitution, so it has become the duty and responsibility of the state to protect them. The presence of the state in this context can be realized not only by the central government, but also by Regional Governments.

Regional Governments as representatives of the state have the most strategic position in implementing the protection of women and children. To be able to realize this, Regional Governments need to have a legal basis to be able to encourage various policies that provide protection for women and children. Here, the idea to be realized is to encourage Regional Governments to create programs or policies that are women-friendly and child-appropriate. The purpose is none other than how later in community life, the world of education, and the business world become parts that are mutually synergistic to realize the implementation of protection of women and children.

The Regional Government of Purworejo Regency needs to anticipate the emergence of complexity of problems that occur in fulfilling protection for women and children. Policies implemented must be able to provide a sense of security and comfort for women and children. In addition, policies for implementing protection of women and children must be followed by efforts to strengthen the involvement of Regional Governments, Village Governments, the Business World, educational institutions and the community. With this responsibility, it will be able to build a Society that cares for, respects, and values women and children. Efforts to empower and protect women and children in the Region require a legal umbrella that functions as a guide and direction for all parties. For this reason, based on the authority possessed by the Regional House of Representatives and the Regional Government, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children.
