# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Semarang Regency.
2. Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and assistance duties with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
3. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.
4. Regional House of Representatives of Semarang Regency hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution as an element of regional government administration.
5. Regent is the Regent of Semarang.
6. Regional Apparatus Work Unit in charge hereinafter referred to as SKPD in charge is a Regional Apparatus Work Unit that has main duties and functions in accordance with the related field.
7. Human Rights are a set of rights inherent in the nature and existence of human beings as creatures of God Almighty and constitute His gift that must be respected, upheld and protected by the state, law, Government, and every person for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity and worth.
8. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social capabilities, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective capabilities.
9. Women's Protection is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
10. Discrimination Against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex which has the effect or purpose of impairing or nullifying the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, irrespective of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women.
11. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
12. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.
13. Society is individuals, families, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.
14. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children.
15. Labor Force is every person who is capable of performing work to produce goods and/or services either to meet their own needs or for society.
16. Worker/Laborer is every person who works by receiving wages or remuneration in other forms.

# CHAPTER II

## SCOPE

#### Article 2

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes

   - a. principles and objectives;
   - b. women's rights;
   - c. obligations and responsibilities of regional government;
   - d. women's empowerment;
   - e. women's protection;
   - f. implementation of women's protection;
   - g. strategy for women's empowerment and protection;
   - h. mechanism for organizing women's empowerment and protection;
      - i. financing;
   - j. guidance and supervision;
   - k. transitional provisions; and
   - l. closing provisions.

# CHAPTER III

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

Empowerment and Protection of Women is implemented based on the principles of:

   - a. respect for human rights;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. non-discrimination; and
   - d. the best interests of women.

#### Article 4

The objectives of this Regional Regulation are:

   - a. to empower women both individually and in groups and society at large so that they are able to live more independently; and
   - b. to prevent and address the risks of violence and vulnerability of women so that their survival can be fulfilled in accordance with the principles of equality and justice.

# CHAPTER IV

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 5

1. Every woman has the right to:
   - a. live and sustain life and improve her standard of living;
   - b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue her lineage;
   - c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life in order to become a human being who is faithful, pious, responsible, of noble character, happy, and prosperous in accordance with human rights;
   - d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;
   - e. be involved in every stage of the development process;
   - f. be free from slavery or servitude and threats;
   - g. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;
   - h. obtain welfare and a decent life;
      - i. participate in politics;
   - j. perform legal acts; and
   - k. freely choose a partner in her marriage.
2. Every wife during the marriage bond has the same rights and responsibilities as her husband over all matters relating to their married life, relations with their children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
3. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to children with due regard to the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility for the empowerment and protection of women.
2. The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;
   - b. determination of implementation guidelines;
   - c. provision of services; and
   - d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.
3. The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 7

Women's empowerment is directed towards obtaining opportunities and rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:

   - a. economy;
   - b. social culture;
   - c. politics; and
   - d. law.

#### Article 8

Empowerment of women in the economic field as referred to in Article 7 letter a is implemented through:

   - a. provision of skills and job training;
   - b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;
   - c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;
   - d. facilitation and capital assistance; and
   - e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 9

Empowerment of women in the social and cultural field as referred to in Article 7 letter b shall be implemented through:

   - a. enhancement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;
   - b. enhancement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, primarily in the field of reproductive health;
   - c. enhancement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and
   - d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and traditions and development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 10

1. Empowerment of women in the political field as referred to in Article 7 letter c includes:
   - a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;
   - b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;
   - c. participation in general elections; and
   - d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.
2. Empowerment of women in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the prevailing laws and regulations.

#### Article 11

1. Empowerment of women in the field of law as referred to in Article 7 letter d includes:
   - a. increasing awareness and knowledge in the field of law through communication, information and education services;
   - b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.
2. Empowerment of women in the field of law as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

#### Article 12

1. Protection of women includes:
   - a. social protection;
   - b. economic protection;
   - c. legal protection.
2. Social protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is to ensure the survival of women in the form of social protection in order to ensure the fulfillment of decent living needs.
3. Economic protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is to prevent and address the risk of vulnerability resulting from shocks and social vulnerability of a person, family, group and/or community for the survival of women.
4. Legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c is implemented in terms of providing protection to women who become witnesses and victims of acts of violence and discrimination.

# CHAPTER VIII

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 13

The implementation of protection for women is provided to:

   - a. female workers/laborers;
   - b. elderly women;
   - c. women with disabilities;
   - d. homeless women;
   - e. female domestic workers;
   - f. female heads of household;
   - g. women who are former inmates of correctional institutions;
   - h. women victims of disasters;
      - i. female commercial sex workers; and
   - j. women victims of violence.

Second Part Women Workers/Laborers

#### Article 14

1. The implementation of protection for female workers/laborers as referred to in Article 13 letter a is to provide protection from acts of economic exploitation and cruel, inhumane treatment and to endeavor to ensure proper appreciation for the work.
2. Female workers/laborers as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection in the form of:
   - a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;
   - b. health and social security;
   - c. access to information and legal consultation services;
   - d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve status; and
   - e. protection as witnesses and victims.

Third Part Elderly Women

#### Article 15

1. The implementation of protection for elderly women as referred to in Article 13 letter b is in the framework of extending life expectancy so that they can carry out their social functions and play an active role naturally in their social life.
2. Elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection for:
   - a. basic health services to maintain and improve health status including gerontological/geriatric services;
   - b. use of facilities and accessibility of public facilities and infrastructure; and
   - c. government administrative services.

Fourth Part Women with Disabilities

#### Article 16

1. The implementation of protection for women with disabilities as referred to in Article 13 letter c is intended to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, including being free from cruel, inhuman, and degrading treatment, and being free from exploitation.
2. Women with disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection in the form of:
   - a. a guarantee of continuity and personal self-development;
   - b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, sports, arts and culture, as well as the utilization of information and communication technology;
   - c. accessibility to information, legal aid, mobility, and social services; and
   - d. early intervention efforts, including treatment and rehabilitation, to improve their functions and capacities.

Fifth Section Homeless Women

#### Article 17

1. The implementation of protection for homeless women as referred to in Article 13 letter d is in the context of providing protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.
2. Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection for:
   - a. decent housing;
   - b. health and social security;
   - c. population administration registration;
   - d. opportunities to obtain skills to improve economic status; and
   - e. access to information and legal consultation services.

Sixth Part Female Domestic Workers

#### Article 18

1. The implementation of Protection for female domestic workers as referred to in Article 13 letter e is in the framework of providing protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, cruel treatment, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation in employment relations.
2. Female domestic workers as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:
   - a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;
   - b. health and social security;
   - c. access to information and legal consultation services; and
   - d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

Seventh Part Female Head of Household

#### Article 19

1. The implementation of Protection for female heads of household as referred to in Article 13 letter f is in the context of providing protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, negative stigma and economic empowerment for female heads of household.
2. Female heads of household as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection from:
   - a. discriminatory actions and efforts to weaken the function of women as the economic support of the family;
   - b. strengthening of capacity and recognition as head of household in their environment; and
   - c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.

Eighth Part Women Former Correctional Facility Inmates

#### Article 20

1. The implementation of Protection for women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in Article 13 letter g is in order to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting themselves in community life, so that they can carry out life normally.
2. Women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:
   - a. rehabilitation and social reintegration; and
   - b. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Nine

Women Disaster Victims

#### Article 21

1. The implementation of Protection for women victims of disaster as referred to in Article 13 letter h is in the context of providing protection during disaster and post-disaster.
2. Women victims of disaster as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:
   - a. access to proper shelter;
   - b. health services;
   - c. information access services and legal consultation; and
   - d. post-disaster recovery.

Tenth Section Female Commercial Sex Workers

#### Article 22

1. The implementation of Protection for female commercial sex workers as referred to in Article 13 letter i is in the context of providing health protection and improving life skills capabilities as capital to enhance social status.
2. Protection for female commercial sex workers as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. providing life skills capabilities;
   - b. facilitating reproductive health services; and
   - c. preventing the occurrence of human trafficking.

Eleventh Part Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 23

1. The Implementation of Protection for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 13 letter j is in the context of providing legal protection and/or health recovery.
2. Protection for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. legal assistance;
   - b. assistance following the occurrence of acts of violence;
   - c. rehabilitation and social reintegration; and
   - d. health services.

# CHAPTER IX

## STRATEGY FOR WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND

## PROTECTION

#### Article 24

1. Women's empowerment and protection shall be implemented by integrating gender mainstreaming and gender-disaggregated data in an integrated manner based on applicable laws and regulations.
2. In women's empowerment and protection, a Working Group and Integrated Services shall be established as determined by Regent's Decree.
3. Women's empowerment and protection shall be coordinated by the Agency handling Women's Empowerment.

# CHAPTER X

## MECHANISM FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

## AND PROTECTION

#### Article 25

The mechanism for the implementation of women's empowerment and protection is carried out through the following stages:

   - a. formulation of policy, program planning and activities;
   - b. budgeting of programs and activities;
   - c. implementation of programs and activities; and
   - d. monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

#### Article 26

1. The formulation of policy for program and activity planning as referred to in Article 25 letter a is implemented through:
   - a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of data;
   - b. coordination and synchronization of the preparation of program and activity planning with related SKPD; and
   - c. determination of programs and activities in planning documents.
2. The formulation of policy for program and activity planning is further regulated in minimum service standards and standard operating procedures.
3. The formulation of policy for program and activity planning for the implementation of women's empowerment and protection is coordinated by the SKPD responsible for regional development planning.
4. The budgeting of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter b is implemented through general budget policy as set forth in the APBD.
5. The implementation of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter c is implemented through:
   - a. implementation of programs and activities as set forth in the SKPD budget document; and
   - b. coordination and synchronization of the implementation of programs and activities with related SKPD.
6. The implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in Article 25 letter d is implemented through:
   - a. conducting monitoring and evaluation periodically on the implementation of programs and activities that have been implemented; and
   - b. making reports periodically.

# CHAPTER XI

## FINANCING

#### Article 27

All costs for the implementation of planning policies, programs and activities related to Women's Empowerment and Protection are sourced from:

   - a. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget;
   - b. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
   - c. Other sources that are legal and non-binding

# CHAPTER XII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 28

1. The Regent or SKPD responsible for women's empowerment shall conduct guidance and supervision over the empowerment and protection of women.
2. The forms of guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) are as follows:
   - a. providing socialization to the community and business sector regarding policies on empowerment and protection of women;
   - b. providing books, leaflets, brochures regarding empowerment and protection of women, and other related issues and disseminating them to the community;
   - c. facilitating capacity building of institutions organizing empowerment and protection of women;
   - d. facilitating forums for women's participation and women's organizations;
   - e. facilitating rehabilitation and reintegration services for women victims of disasters and victims of violence;
   - f. coordinating the participation of the business sector and other institutions, including international assistance for the empowerment and protection of women; and
   - g. providing awards to the community and business sector both individually and institutionally that have made efforts in the empowerment and protection of women.
3. The form of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of monitoring and evaluation activities over the empowerment and protection of women.

# CHAPTER XIII

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all provisions of regional laws and regulations related to the Implementation of Women's Empowerment and Protection that have existed thus far, shall remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to the provisions in this Regional Regulation.

##### I. GENERAL

The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia mandates the authority of regional governments to regulate and manage their own governmental affairs directed towards accelerating the realization of public welfare through improvement of services, empowerment and community participation. The authority of Regional Government is to make regional policies to provide services, increase participation, initiative, and community empowerment aimed at improving people's welfare.

The implementation of regional autonomy must always be oriented towards improving community welfare while always paying attention to the interests and aspirations that grow in society. Therefore, governmental affairs related to basic services such as basic education, health, fulfillment of minimum living needs, basic environmental infrastructure must be fulfilled.

Human rights are basic rights that are inherently attached to human beings by nature, are universal and lasting, so they must be protected, respected, maintained, and may not be ignored, reduced, or seized by anyone. In the context of Upholding Human Rights (HAM) to guarantee justice for every individual, efforts are made to prevent all forms of unbalanced treatment or treatment deemed detrimental to other parties, whether between individuals, between groups of individuals, or between individuals as well as between government and society, including the process of marginalization of women that causes women to be vulnerable to acts of violence, discrimination and exploitation in both domestic and public spheres.

Women have equal and equivalent rights with men, however in certain discriminatory conditions, the government is obliged to implement affirmative action so that the rights of women, including women in vulnerable groups, need to receive protection so as not to experience violence and can live a decent life in accordance with the principles of humanity, equality and justice.

Empowerment of women and protection of women is implemented in order to realize gender equality and justice. To implement these affairs, efforts are needed to increase the capacity of women's human resources and strengthen gender mainstreaming institutions, through gender mainstreaming strategies and gender-disaggregated data.

The Government of Semarang Regency as the party that has the authority to regulate and protect the citizens of Semarang Regency, deems it necessary to continuously carry out and formulate regulations in order to provide guarantees for the empowerment of women and protection of women in Semarang Regency.

This Regional Regulation is expected to be able to encourage efforts to improve the physical and mental conditions of women in fulfilling their rights and life needs as part of human rights from various fields of development, increase the commitment and effectiveness of regional government in implementing policies, programs and activities as well as encourage the institutional capacity of government and society that improves the quality of life of women.

Female commercial sex workers are women who work as sex workers of their own volition and/or are prostituted. Life skills enhancement for commercial sex workers is intended to provide additional knowledge through training and skills in accordance with the talents and abilities possessed so that they can live more independently and obtain decent work to improve their standard of living and change their social status so as not to become commercial sex workers because such work is not legalized.

The protection of commercial sex workers in this Regional Regulation is not intended to legalize the existence of commercial sex workers, but to provide protection to the said commercial sex workers in various sectors.
