# BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

# THE REGENT OF EAST KOTAWARINGIN,

Considering : a. that every citizen has the right to security and freedom from all forms of violence against women and children which constitutes a violation of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination;

   - b. that violence against women and children in the region continues to increase, therefore protection efforts are required;
   - c. that laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts at the regional level, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed to ensure their implementation;
   - d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b and c above, it is necessary to regulate and stipulate by Regional Regulation of East Kotawaringin Regency concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL

## PROVISIONS

#### Article [number not provided in source text] In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:

1. Region is East Kotawaringin Regency
2. Regional Government is the Regional Government of East Kotawaringin Regency
3. Regent is the Regent of East Kotawaringin
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of East Kotawaringin Regency
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.
6. Woman is a person who is female in gender.
7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.
8. Violence against women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.
10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring in the area of East Kotawaringin Regency.
11. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the Regional Government, police, prosecution, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear about what will occur or what has occurred in violence against women.
12. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
13. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will occur, is occurring, or has occurred.
14. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has expertise in accompanying victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
15. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a functional work unit that provides integrated services for victims of violence.
16. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the accompaniment process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and danger from perpetrators.
17. Standard Operating Procedure hereinafter abbreviated as SOP is standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from complaint/identification, rehabilitation, health, social rehabilitation, legal services up to return and social reintegration of witnesses and/or victims.
18. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
19. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, foster care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.
20. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of East Kotawaringin Regency
21. Coordination Forum for Protection of Victims of Violence hereinafter abbreviated as FKPKK is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner.
22. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and children hereinafter abbreviated as PPTPPA is a community-based institution whose members are multistakeholder observers of women and children at the government and non-government levels.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The principles of implementing protection for Victims are:

   - a. Humanity;
   - b. Respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;
   - c. Justice and gender equality;
   - d. Non-discrimination;
   - e. Best interests of victims; and
   - f. Empowerment.

#### Article 3

The Objectives of Victim Protection are:

   - a. To prevent violence against women and children;
   - b. To provide services to Victims; and
   - c. To carry out empowerment and rehabilitation for victims of violence.

#### Article 4

The scope of protection for Victims includes prevention efforts, services, and rehabilitative empowerment for victims of violence in East Kotawaringin Regency.

# CHAPTER III

## VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence include:

   - a. Physical violence;
   - b. Psychological violence;
   - c. Sexual violence;
   - d. Neglect;
   - e. Exploitation; and/or
   - f. other forms of violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter (a) is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, loss of consciousness and/or cause death.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter (b) is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or suffering in a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter (c) consists of:

   - a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
   - b. forced sexual intercourse;
   - c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
   - d. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and/or specific purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:

   - a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of a child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
   - b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
   - c. acts that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
   - d. acts that result in economic dependence by means of restricting and/or prohibiting from working properly inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.
   - e. Neglect within the household.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:

   - a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
   - b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, physical exploitation, sexual exploitation, exploitation of reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or capabilities of a person by other parties to obtain benefits both material and immaterial;

and/or

   - c. all forms of utilization of the victim’s sexual organs or other body organs to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecent acts

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:

   - a. threat of violence includes: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, image, symbol, or body movement, whether with or without using means that creates fear or restricts a person's fundamental freedom; and
   - b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER IV

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 12

Every Victim obtains the following rights:

   - a. the right to have their dignity and worth as a human being respected;
   - b. the right to restoration of health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
   - c. the right to determine their own decisions;
   - d. the right to obtain information;
   - e. the right to confidentiality of their identity;
   - f. the right to compensation;
   - g. the right to social rehabilitation;
   - h. the right to complaint handling;
      - i. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or
   - j. the right to assistance.

#### Article 13

Child victims of violence, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 12, also obtain special rights, as follows:

   - a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;
   - b. the right to basic services;
   - c. the right to obtain proper education;
   - d. the right to equal protection;
   - e. the right to be free from various stigma; and/or
   - f. the right to obtain freedom in accordance with applicable norms.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

Obligations of Regional Government

#### Article 14

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement victim protection efforts, in the form of:
   - a. Establishing policies;
   - b. Forming FKPKK;
   - c. Preparing program and activity planning;
   - d. Providing facilities and infrastructure support;
   - e. Allocating budget; and
   - f. conducting guidance and supervision of the protection of women and children through related agencies.
2. In the implementation of the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall prepare a Regional Action Plan for victim protection.

#### Article 15

The Regional Government provides services for victims in the form of:

   - a. Facilitating facilities and infrastructure for P2TP2A according to its capabilities; and
   - b. Facilitating FPKK as a forum for a network for handling victims.

#### Article 16

1. Regional Governments have the obligation and responsibility to:
   - a. Supervise the implementation of services for victims, with minimum service standards; and
   - b. Provide funds for victim protection through the APBD in the form of grants to P2TP2A.
2. Funding for victim protection activities carried out by Regional Governments shall be charged to the APBD and/or other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 17

In carrying out the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 14, Article 15, and Article 16, the Regent grants authority to agencies that have duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Second Part Obligations of Society and Family

#### Article 18

The community and family have the following obligations and responsibilities:

   - a. Prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children;
   - b. Report if violence occurs;
   - c. Protect victims; and
   - d. Provide emergency assistance.

# CHAPTER VI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 19

1. The community participates in the implementation of protection for Victims.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of:
   - a. Establishing family partners at the kelurahan/village level by the community;
   - b. Establishing women and children protection units within community organizations;
   - c. Conducting independent socialization of women's and children's rights;
   - d. Providing first aid to victims; and
   - e. Reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

# CHAPTER VII

INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN (P2TP2A)

#### Article 20

1. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes a functional work unit that has the duties, main tasks, and functions of providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) has the duty to provide services and temporary protection in the form of a safe house for victims of violence.
3. In the event that P2TP2A does not have a safe house (shelter) as referred to in paragraph (2), then victims of violence shall be referred to PPT that has a safe house.

#### Article 21

1. The organizational structure of P2TP2A formed by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (1) consists of:
   - a. General Chairperson or other equivalent designation;
   - b. Secretary;
   - c. Treasurer; and
   - d. Executive Chairperson who oversees the divisions.
2. The Divisions as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, at minimum include:
   - a. Division of complaint services;
   - b. Division of health services;
   - c. Division of social rehabilitation;
   - d. Division of repatriation and social reintegration; and
   - e. Division of legal aid services.

#### Article 22

The complaint service sector as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter a has the following duties:

   - a. Conduct interviews and observation of the victim's condition;
   - b. Make recommendations for follow-up services;
   - c. Conduct coordination and referrals to follow-up services and related parties; and
   - d. Carry out administration of the complaint process.

#### Article 23

The health service sector as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter b has the following duties:

   - a. Conduct examination, treatment, and follow-up care for victims;
   - b. Coordinate the implementation of health rehabilitation and medico-legal services;
   - c. Conduct medico-legal examination including collection of evidence from victims and preparation of visum et repertum;
   - d. Conduct supporting examinations and laboratory testing of evidence;
   - e. Conduct consultation with specialist doctors or make referrals; and
   - f. Prepare case reports.

#### Article 24

The field of social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter c has the following duties:

   - a. Providing assistance during the case handling process; and
   - b. Conducting counseling.

#### Article 25

The field of repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter d has the following duties:

   - a. Coordinate with relevant agencies for the repatriation of victims;
   - b. Prepare progress reports on the assistance process for repatriation and social rehabilitation; and
   - c. Conduct monitoring for at least three months after the victim is returned to their family.

#### Article 26

The field of legal aid services as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (2) letter e has the following duties:

   - a. To accompany and defend in every legal service process; and
   - b. To prepare reports on the progress of legal handling.

#### Article 27

1. P2TP2A formed by the Regional Government must have at least three counselors.
2. Counselors as referred to in paragraph (1) include: a. Counselor in the field of law;
   - b. Counselor in the field of health; and
   - c. Counselor in the field of psychology.

# CHAPTER VIII

## VICTIM PROTECTION

## Part One

Prevention

#### Article 28

1. Efforts to prevent violence against women and children shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the agency having the principal tasks and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by:
   - a. Establishing a network in efforts to prevent violence;
   - b. Conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
   - c. Establishing a violence prevention system;
   - d. Conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence; and
   - e. Providing critical education on the rights of women and children for the community.
   - f. Imposing a curfew for children which shall be further regulated by the Regent Regulation of East Kotawaringin.

#### Article 29

In addition to prevention efforts carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 28, prevention efforts must also be carried out by:

   - a. Family and/or closest relatives;
   - b. Community; and
   - c. Educational institutions.

Second Part Services

#### Article 30

1. The provision of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.

#### Article 31

The provision of services to victims shall be carried out with:

   - a. Promptness;
   - b. Safety and comfort;
   - c. A sense of empathy;
   - d. Non-discrimination;
   - e. Easy accessibility;
   - f. No charges; and
   - g. Confidentiality is guaranteed.

#### Article 32

Forms of services for victims include:

   - a. Complaint, consultation, and counseling services;
   - b. Assistance services;
   - c. Health services;
   - d. Social rehabilitation services;
   - e. Legal services; and
   - f. Repatriation and social reintegration services.

#### Article 33

Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Article 32 letter a include:

   - a. Identification or initial recording of victims; and
   - b. Consent to carry out an action (informed consent).

#### Article 34

Accompaniment services as referred to in Article 32 letter b include:

   - a. Accompanying victims during the examination process and health recovery;
   - b. Accompanying victims during the medicolegal process;
   - c. Accompanying victims during the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and court;
   - d. Monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;
   - e. Protecting the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media;
   - f. Coordinating with other companions; and
   - g. Providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 35

Health services as referred to in Article 32 letter c include:

   - a. First aid to victims;
   - b. Treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics; and
   - c. Referral to health services.

#### Article 36

Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 32 letter d constitute services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation against victims and to provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength, and independence in resolving their problems, by means of:

   - a. Providing spiritual guidance to victims; and
   - b. Psychological recovery of victims.

#### Article 37

Legal services as referred to in Article 34 letter e to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by:

   - a. Providing legal consultation that includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;
   - b. Accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination stages in court hearings and assisting victims to fully explain the violence they have experienced;
   - c. Coordinating with law enforcement officers, volunteer companions/assistants, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 38

1. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 32 letter e aim to return victims to their families and social environment.
2. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with relevant government and non-government agencies and institutions.

Third Part Empowerment

### Paragraph 1

Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 39

Forms of empowerment for women victims of violence include:

   - a. Job training;
   - b. Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
   - c. Capital assistance.

#### Article 40

Job training as referred to in Article 39 letter a includes:

   - a. skills training;
   - b. field work practice; and
   - c. apprenticeship.

#### Article 41

Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups as referred to in Article 39 letter b include:

   - a. Entrepreneurial skills training;
   - b. Facilitation of the formation of joint business groups; and
   - c. Assistance in business implementation.

#### Article 42

Capital assistance as referred to in Article 39 letter c includes:

   - a. Assistance for work facilities and infrastructure; and
   - b. Facilitation of working capital assistance.

### Paragraph 2

Fulfillment of the Rights of Child Victims of Violence

#### Article 43

1. Regional Government, P2TP2A, and the community are obligated to fulfill the rights of child victims of violence.
2. The form of fulfillment of the rights of child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes the fulfillment of the basic rights of children in accordance with their needs.

Fourth Part Coordination of Victim Protection

#### Article 44

1. In an effort to provide and organize service handling for victims, the Regional Government establishes FPKK.
2. FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) aims to: a. Coordinate and synchronize the handling of P2TP2A services;
   - b. Maintain and develop networks and referral systems; and
   - c. Collect, compile and present reports on violence.
3. The management and membership of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by Regent's Decree.
4. The membership of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (3) is grouped into the following roles:
   - a. Health role;
   - b. Psychology role;
   - c. Legal role;
   - d. Social role; and
   - e. Economic role.

# CHAPTER IX

## INFORMATION/REPORTING SYSTEM

#### Article 45

1. The Regent implements an information/reporting system for the protection of women and children victims of violence.
2. The information/reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
3. Monthly reports, quarterly reports, and annual reports shall be submitted periodically.

# CHAPTER X

## DEVELOPMENT AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 46

1. Regional Government conducts development and supervision of the implementation of minimum service standards of P2TP2A.
2. Development and Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. Coordination;
   - b. Guidance;
   - c. Education and training; and
   - d. Monitoring and evaluation.
3. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a covers aspects related to planning and implementation.
4. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b covers aspects related to planning, implementation, procedures, funding, quality, control and supervision.
5. Education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is conducted in accordance with laws and regulations.
6. Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d is conducted periodically.

# CHAPTER XI

## OBLIGATIONS

#### Article 47

1. The Regional Government and the Community shall establish and ensure the operation of the TP2TP2A integrated service institution to protect women and child victims.
2. The Regional Government shall facilitate the establishment of service institutions.
3. The Regional Government shall encourage the community regarding the importance of services for women and children.
4. The Regional Government shall provide funds for the protection of women and children through the Regional Budget (APBD) of East Kotawaringin Regency according to capacity.
5. Every person/government administrator is obligated to provide protection to women and children who are victims of violence.

# CHAPTER XII

## SANCTIONS

#### Article 48

1. If government administrators and/or the community do not implement the provisions as referred to in Article 47, sanctions shall be imposed in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.
2. Sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of oral and written warnings up to (3) times.
3. Violations as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be in the form of imprisonment for 3 (three) months and/or fines in accordance with applicable regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation. In order that every person shall know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placement in the Regional Gazette.

##### CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE I. GENERAL Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Kotawaringin Timur Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades their degree and human dignity, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

Thus far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection measures in the Region, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection measures for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in Kotawaringin Timur Regency.

Letter a Sufficiently clear.

Letter b What is meant by "respect and fulfillment of victims' rights" is the guarantee of the fulfillment of victims' basic rights.

Letter c What is meant by "justice" is a condition where every person, both men and women, are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to gain access, as well as welfare;

What is meant by "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

Letter d What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment toward victims by not making distinctions based on age, sex, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter e What is meant by "the best interests of victims" is all best actions concerning victims carried out by the Government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, then the best interests of victims must be the primary consideration.

Letter f What is meant by "empowerment" is the strengthening of victims of violence to be able to work and earn a living independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
