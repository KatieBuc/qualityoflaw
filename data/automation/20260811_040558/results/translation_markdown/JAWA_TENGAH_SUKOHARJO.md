# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Sukoharjo Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Sukoharjo.
4. Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit that oversees women's empowerment and child protection.
5. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including children still in the womb.
6. Administration is a form of implementation of protection for women, men and child victims of violence.
7. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
8. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
9. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
10. Administration of protection is a series of activities aimed at preventing the occurrence of violence, providing protection as well as recovery services and social reintegration, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation carried out by the SKPD in charge.
11. Violence is any act that results in or can result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to victims.
12. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
13. Violence Against Children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which include but are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
14. Physical Violence is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.
15. Psychological Violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
16. Sexual Violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
17. Economic Violence is any act that neglects family members in the form of not providing a proper living, care or maintenance.
18. Victim of Gender-Based Violence is a person who due to their gender experiences physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
19. Child Victim of Violence is a child who receives harsh treatment whether physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.
20. Victim Recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, whether physically, psychologically, socially, economically or sexually.
21. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the purpose of self-strengthening of victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems they face.
22. Integrated Services is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unit of administration, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for victims of gender-based violence and children.
23. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes that can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.
24. Safe House (shelter) is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.
25. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations, and/or community organizations.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:

   - a. non-discrimination;
   - b. best interests of the victim;
   - c. gender justice and equality;
   - d. victim protection;
   - e. maternal survival;
   - f. child survival and development;
   - g. respect for the child's opinion;
   - h. transparency;
      - i. integration;
   - j. not blaming the victim;
   - k. victim confidentiality; and
   - l. decision-making in the hands of the victim.

#### Article 3

The objectives of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children are:

   - a. to prevent all forms of gender-based violence and violence against children, occurring within the household and/or community;
   - b. to provide protection;
   - c. to provide legal assistance;
   - d. to pursue recovery and social reintegration; and
   - e. to provide a sense of security to victims.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

1. Every victim of gender-based violence and child victim of violence has the right to:
   - a. obtain protection for personal security, family, and property, and be free from threats related to testimony that will be, is being, or has been given;
   - b. participate in the process of selecting and determining the form of protection and security support;
   - c. receive information regarding case developments and court decisions;
   - d. receive integrated services that are fast, appropriate, comfortable, and in accordance with needs;
   - e. recovery and social reintegration;
   - f. receive legal assistance, psychological support, spiritual guidance, economic support, social support and interpreter services;
   - g. special handling related to victim confidentiality;
   - h. receive guarantees of rights related to their status as wife/mother, husband/father, child and household member as well as community member; and
      - i. receive information about laws and regulations that protect victims.
2. The rights of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND AUTHORITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 5

1. In providing protection to victims of gender-based violence and violence against children, the Regional Government is obligated to:
   - a. implement all efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence;
   - b. provide protection for victims of violence;
   - c. provide recovery services and social reintegration for victims;
   - d. encourage and increase community participation;
   - e. cooperate with service providers in efforts to prevent, protect and recover victims of violence; and
   - f. supervise the implementation of services for victims involving the community.
2. To carry out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government has the authority to:
   - a. formulate policies and programs on the elimination of gender-based violence and violence against children;
   - b. facilitate the implementation of integrated services;
   - c. provide facilities and infrastructure;
   - d. increase the capacity of service provider institutions;
   - e. conduct coordination and cooperation in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children; and
   - f. conduct monitoring and evaluation.
3. The regional government in carrying out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

# CHAPTER V

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional

#### Article 6

In providing protection to victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence, the Regional Government is assisted by Integrated Services.

Second Part Integrated Services

#### Article 7

1. Integrated services as referred to in Article 6 shall be established by the Regional Government in accordance with the laws and regulations.
2. The establishment of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of members from elements of the regional government, non-governmental institutions, health service institutions, law enforcement officers, professional personnel, companion volunteers, social workers, clergy/spiritual counselors, safe houses (shelters), and social rehabilitation centers.
3. The Minimum Service Operational Standards for Integrated Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 8

The duties of integrated services as referred to in Article 6 and Article 7 are:

   - a. to endeavor to prevent;
   - b. recovery and social reintegration;
   - c. to provide legal protection;
   - d. to conduct coordination and cooperation;
   - e. to endeavor to increase community participation; and
   - f. monitoring and reporting.

Third Part Principles of Service

#### Article 9

In carrying out the duties as referred to in Article 8, integrated services apply the following principles:

   - a. no fees charged for underprivileged families;
   - b. fast;
   - c. safe;
   - d. empathy;
   - e. non-discrimination;
   - f. easily accessible;
   - g. confidential; and
   - h. integrated Fourth Part Prevention Efforts

#### Article 10

Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 8 letter a include:

   - a. conducting communication, information, and education regarding gender-based and child violence; and
   - b. socialization of laws and regulations related to gender-based and child violence.

Fifth Part Recovery Efforts and Social Reintegration

#### Article 11

Recovery and Social Reintegration Efforts as referred to in Article 8 letter b, include:

   - a. providing physical recovery at health service institutions;
   - b. providing medicolegal services;
   - c. assisting the repatriation of victims;
   - d. providing temporary protection in safe houses (shelters);
   - e. providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;
   - f. providing spiritual guidance services; and
   - g. conducting family and community preparation, economic empowerment, and return to school and/or other educational institutions.

Sixth Part Legal Protection

#### Article 12

Legal protection as referred to in Article 8 letter c includes:

   - a. providing protection in a safe house (shelter);
   - b. providing assistance in the legal process; and
   - c. providing special legal protection for child victims of violence can be carried out through the appointment of guardianship in accordance with applicable regulations.

Seventh Part Coordination and Cooperation

#### Article 13

Coordination and cooperation as referred to in Article 8 letter d includes:

   - a. conducting coordination and cooperation in handling cases of violence with integrated services at the regency/city level;
   - b. conducting coordination and cooperation with integrated services between regencies;
   - c. conducting coordination and cooperation with institutions providing services for victims of gender-based violence and children; and
   - d. conducting coordination and consultation with Regional Governments.

## Part Eight

Community Participation

#### Article 14

Community participation as referred to in Article 8 letter e is conducted through:

   - a. providing protection for victims;
   - b. providing emergency assistance;
   - c. providing advocacy to victims and or the community regarding the handling of gender-based and child violence cases;
   - d. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection orders;
   - e. conveying information to the authorized authorities related to gender-based and child violence cases;
   - f. fostering local wisdom in handling gender-based and child violence;
   - g. organizing strengthening of community groups in handling gender-based and child violence;
   - h. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to gender-based and child violence;
      - i. providing protection for victims;
   - j. providing emergency assistance; and
   - k. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection orders.

## Part Nine

Monitoring and Reporting

#### Article 15

Monitoring and reporting as referred to in Article 8 letter f includes monitoring, documentation and evaluation of cases of gender-based and child violence.

#### Article 16

The implementation of integrated services shall be coordinated by the relevant SKPD.

# CHAPTER VI

## COOPERATION

#### Article 17

1. In providing protection for victims of gender-based violence and children, the regional government cooperates with the Government, Provincial Government and Regency/City Government and other institutions.
2. Cooperation between the Regional Government and the Government and Provincial Government as referred to in paragraph (1) includes consultation, coordination and reporting.
3. Cooperation between the Regional Government and Regency/City as referred to in paragraph (1) includes coordination, advocacy, referral, repatriation, social reintegration, facilitation of Integrated Service System development, monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

# CHAPTER VII

## ASSISTANCE

#### Article 18

Assistance is carried out by persons or institutions that have expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and have cooperated with Integrated Services.

# CHAPTER VIII

## CONTROL, GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 19

1. Control, guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall be carried out by the Regent.
2. The implementation of control, guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out on a daily basis by the relevant SKPD.

# CHAPTER IX

## FINANCING

#### Article 20

All activities related to the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children shall be charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget as well as other lawful and non-binding receipts.

# CHAPTER X

## ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS

#### Article 21

Integrated Service Institutions that violate the service principles as referred to in Article 9 shall be subject to administrative sanctions in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 22

Matters not yet regulated in this Regional Regulation, insofar as they concern technical implementation, shall be regulated by a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 23

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that every person shall know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Sukoharjo Regency.

##### I. GENERAL.

In the 1999 National Action Plan for the Elimination of Violence Against Women, it is stated that Women as mothers of the nation and children as successors of the nation are creatures created by God Almighty, therefore they must be protected and their honor, dignity and self-respect must be safeguarded in a fair and proportional manner, both legally, economically, politically, socially and culturally, without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race and class in community, national and state life.

To realize the achievement of enforcement and fulfillment of human rights, the government is obliged to provide protection and security for women and children as outlined in a policy both at the national and regional levels.

The government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, economically and socially to prevent, suppress, reduce and eliminate all forms of violence against women and children, both under policies applicable at the national level and under policies applicable at the regional level.

Acts of violence against women and children can occur in the public sphere as well as in the domestic sphere (within the household) and can occur at any time, in peaceful situations as well as conflict. Acts of violence against women and children are actions that violate, hinder, negate and disregard the human rights of women and the rights of children.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a What is meant by "non-discrimination" is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinguishing ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.

Letter b What is meant by "best interests of the victim" is that in all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, the community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Letter c What is meant by "gender justice" is fair treatment given to both women and men.

What is meant by "gender equality" is a condition and position that describes harmonious, compatible and balanced relations between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in access, participation, control and benefits in the implementation of development as well as enjoying the results of development in family life as well as in society, nation and state.

Letter d What is meant by "victim protection" is providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

Letter e What is meant by "maternal survival" is ensuring that a mother does not die during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum, whether caused by physical or non-physical conditions.

Letter f What is meant by "child survival" is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity.

What is meant by "child growth and development" is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and to engage in recreation.

Letter g What is meant by "respect for the child's opinion" is respect for children's rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.

Letter h What is meant by "openness" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is transparent among integrated service providers.

Letter i What is meant by "integration" is that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children it is carried out by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal advocates, psychologists, religious counselors, social workers, police.

Letter j What is meant by "not blaming the victim" is an attitude and treatment of not blaming the victim for the occurrence of violence experienced by them.

Letter k What is meant by "victim confidentiality" is every action taken to ensure the victim is in a safe condition from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychology of the victim.

Letter l What is meant by "decision-making in the hands of the victim" is the victim's right to determine the best choice in resolving their problem.
