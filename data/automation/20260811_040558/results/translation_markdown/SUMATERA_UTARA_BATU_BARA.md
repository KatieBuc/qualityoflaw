# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:

1. Region is Batu Bara Regency.
2. Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the Regional Government and DPRD according to the principles of autonomy and assistance duties with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
4. Regent is the Regent of Batu Bara.
5. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution that is positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.
6. Child protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to Victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
7. Woman is an adult human of female gender and a person who is legally recognized as a Woman.
8. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a Child who is still in the womb.
9. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.
10. Violence against Women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
11. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child.
12. Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences misery and/or suffering as a result of Violence and/or threat of Violence.
13. Victim of Violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering either physically, mentally or emotionally, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of Violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.
14. Health Rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of Victims including physical, psychological, and sexual health so that Victims can carry out their roles again properly and naturally both in the family and in society.
15. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person who experiences social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions naturally.
16. Repatriation is an effort to return Victims of Violence from the Region to the region of origin.
17. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide Protection and fulfillment of needs for Victims.
18. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for witnesses and/or Victims of criminal acts and/or acts of Violence.
19. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to carry out the functions and main duties of integrated services for Women and Child Victims of Violence, including complaint services, Health Rehabilitation, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, Social Reintegration, and Legal Aid.
20. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in reports/providing handling services for complaints, health services, Social Rehabilitation, enforcement and legal aid, as well as Repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Child Victims of Violence.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES i

#### Article 2

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, is implemented based on the principles of:

   - a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of Victims;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. non-discrimination;
   - d. best interests of victims; and
   - e. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

The Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to: a. prevent all forms of violence against women and children;

   - b. protect women and provide services to women and children victims of violence; and
   - c. empower women and children victims of violence.

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

Every woman and child victim of violence has the right: a. to have their dignity respected as a human being; b. to obtain protection from family, community, Regional Government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court;

   - c. to physical, psychological and sexual health recovery in accordance with the suffering experienced by the victim of violence;
   - d. to special handling related to the confidentiality of the victim;
   - e. to assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
   - f. to spiritual guidance services; and
   - g. to determine their own decisions.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 5

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to provide protection to women and children victims of violence.
2. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children victims of violence based on laws and regulations;
   - b. establishing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence;
   - c. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence;
   - d. allocating budget in the administration of protection of women and children victims of violence;
   - e. fostering and supervising the administration of protection of women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 6

1. The community has the obligation and responsibility in providing protection to women and children victims of violence.
2. The obligation and responsibility of the community as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in the form of:
   - a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
   - b. providing protection to victims;
   - c. providing emergency assistance;
   - d. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to the competent authorities; and
   - e. participating in the handling of victims of violence.

# CHAPTER V

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 7

1. In providing protection for women and children victims of violence in the Region, the Regent may establish PPT.
2. The establishment and development of PPT shall be adjusted to the development of priority needs, and the financial capacity of the Region as well as the institutional and personnel capacity available in the Region.
3. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may accommodate institutions/work units that provide services for victims of violence such as P2TP2A, Safe Houses, Child Social Protection Houses, Trauma Center Protection Houses, Women's Social Protection Houses, Shelters and other service activities in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
4. Forms of services implemented by PPT include:
   - a. complaints;
   - b. health rehabilitation;
   - c. social rehabilitation;
   - d. legal assistance;
   - e. repatriation; and
   - f. social reintegration.
5. In providing protection for women and children victims of violence, PPT is obligated to:
   - a. provide services as quickly as possible and free of charge to Victims;
   - b. organize Protection and fulfillment of victims' rights to Health Rehabilitation, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, social reintegration and legal assistance;
   - c. cooperate with certain institutions in providing translators and volunteer assistants required for Victims;
   - d. network with government or private hospitals for treatment and health recovery of Victims and cooperate with Witness and Victim Protection institutions, social Protection houses or trauma centers owned by the government, community, or other institutions for victims' health recovery;
   - e. provide convenience, comfort, and safety for victims;
   - f. maintain confidentiality of victims;
   - g. provide fulfillment of legal assistance for victims;
   - h. in the event that integrated service delivery is carried out through networking, PPT remains responsible for the entire referral process of services required by victims.
6. The provision of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be implemented in accordance with SPM.

#### Article 8

1. Community Institutions may establish PPT.
2. In the establishment of PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), it shall be guided by the provisions of the applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

#### Article 9

MSS for the Field of Integrated Services for women and children victims of violence, includes services for:

   - a. handling complaints/reports of victims of violence against women and children;
   - b. health services for women and children victims of violence;
   - c. social rehabilitation for women and children victims of violence;
   - d. law enforcement and legal assistance for women and children victims of violence; and
   - e. repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 10

SPM as referred to in Article 9 has performance indicators comprising:

   - a. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers in integrated service units;
   - b. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at Puskesmas and Hospitals;
   - c. coverage of Social Rehabilitation services provided by trained Social Rehabilitation officers for women and children victims of violence in integrated service units;
   - d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for women and children victims of violence in integrated service units;
   - e. coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of violence against women and children;
   - f. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive legal aid services;
   - g. coverage of repatriation services for women and children victims of violence; and
   - h. coverage of social reintegration services for women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER VII

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 11

1. To ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, the Regional Government conducts monitoring.
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region.
3. Monitoring is conducted periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of Regional Work Units that implement policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.
4. Monitoring is conducted from the planning stage up to the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence for the ongoing year.

#### Article 12

1. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities on Protection of Women and Children shall be conducted at the end of each fiscal year or when necessary according to needs.
2. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities on Protection of Women and Children shall be used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities on Protection of Women and Children for the following year.
3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## REPORTING

#### Article 13

1. The Regent is responsible for preparing a report on the implementation of SPM in the Field of Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region to the Governor with a copy submitted to the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection and the Minister of Home Affairs.
2. Reporting on implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted annually.

# CHAPTER IX

## FUNDING

#### Article 14

Funding for the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, is sourced from:

   - a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
   - b. other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 15

1. The Regent conducts guidance and supervision over the implementation of SPM for Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region.
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes providing implementation instructions, guidance, supervision, monitoring and evaluation of service implementation for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

# CHAPTER XI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 16

1. In implementing Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence, the community may:
   - a. form family partners at the Kelurahan/Village level;
   - b. conduct socialization of the rights of Women and Children independently;
   - c. provide first aid to victims; and
   - d. report to the authorized agency if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

# CHAPTER XII

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 17

In the event that the PPT as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (1) has not yet been established, the existing P2TP2A shall continue to carry out the duties and functions of integrated services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region.

# CHAPTER XIII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 18

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

##### I. GENERAL

The State has the obligation to provide protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. Women and children are among vulnerable groups who tend to experience violence and therefore need to receive protection. Violence against women and children is a human rights matter, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed without discrimination.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life or body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom whether occurring in public spaces or in private life. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.

This regional regulation regulates protection efforts for women and child victims of violence particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of women and child victims of violence as well as to prevent and reduce acts of violence against women and children.
