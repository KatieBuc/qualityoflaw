# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1 In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:

1. Region is the Region of Parigi Moutong Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Parigi Moutong.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of Government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
6. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and organizations and/or community organizations.
7. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
8. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty;
9. Violence against Women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
10. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child
11. Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of Violence.
12. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that Violence against Women will or has occurred;
13. Protection of Children is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from Violence and discrimination.
14. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
15. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the Victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that Violence against the Victim will, is, or has occurred.
16. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to accompany Victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of Violence Victims.
17. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for strengthening Violence Victims to resolve the problems they face.
18. Repatriation is an effort to return Women and Child Victims of Violence to the family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.
19. Counseling is a process of providing objective and complete information, carried out systematically with interpersonal communication guidance, guidance techniques and mastery of clinical knowledge aimed at helping a person recognize their current condition, the problems being faced, and determine solutions or efforts to overcome these problems. . .
20. Rehabilitation is the recovery of Victims from psychosocial disorders and restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
21. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
22. Restitution is payment of compensation charged to the perpetrator based on a court decision that has permanent legal force for material and/or immaterial losses suffered by the victim or their heirs.
23. Technical Implementation Unit of the Women and Children Protection Service, hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is the Technical Implementation Unit of the Women and Children Protection Service of Parigi Moutong Regency.
24. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service provider institution for Victims established by the Regional Government.
25. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing complaint handling services, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance as well as repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Child Victims of Violence.
26. Social Welfare Implementation Institution hereinafter referred to as LPKS is an institution or social service place that implements social welfare administration for Children in conflict with the law, established by the ministry of social affairs.
27. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

# CHAPTER II

# CHAPTER ON WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 2

Women and children victims of violence receive rights, including:

   - a. the right to have their dignity and worth as human beings respected;
   - b. the right to recovery;
   - c. the right to determine their own decisions;
   - d. the right to obtain information;
   - e. the right to confidentiality;
   - f. the right to social rehabilitation;
   - g. the right to complaint handling that is fast, appropriate, comfortable and according to needs;
   - h. the right of Victims and their families to obtain facilitation in the judicial process;
      - i. the right to Assistance;
   - j. the right to a sense of security; and
   - k. the right to restitution.

#### Article 3

Children victims of violence in addition to receiving rights as referred to in Article 2, also receive special rights, including:

   - a. the right to survival, growth and development;
   - b. the right to basic population services;
   - c. the right to equal protection;
   - d. the right to be free from various stigma; and
   - e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER III

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General Article 14 The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of violence that occur in public or private life in the form of:

   - a. physical violence;
   - b. sexual violence;
   - c. economic violence;
   - d. social violence;
   - e. psychological violence;
   - f. domestic neglect;
   - g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and
   - h. threat of certain actions.

#### Article 5 Every person who hears, sees or knows of the occurrence of Violence as referred to in Article 4 shall make efforts to:

   - a. prevent the criminal act from taking place;
   - b. provide protection to victims;
   - c. provide emergency assistance; and
   - d. assist in the complaint process and submission of applications for protection orders.

Second Part Prevention of Acts of Violence Article 16 (1) The Regional Government conducts prevention of acts of violence against women by means of:

   - a. socializing laws and regulations;
   - b. conducting empowerment and awareness raising to families, parents and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling;
   - c. conducting efforts to increase the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
   - d. conducting efforts to build community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of women and children from violence in an integrated community-based manner;
   - e. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities, religious institutions/organizations and various non-governmental organizations in efforts to prevent violence against women and children;
   - f. establishing a violence prevention system, mapping of locations or areas prone to violence;
   - g. increasing community awareness that respects women and protects children;
   - h. establishing UPTD PPA in every Sub-district, and Women and Children Protection Service Posts at Village and Sub-district levels; and
      - i. establishing a women and children protection task force.
2. In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be implemented by:
   - a. family and/or closest relatives;
   - b. community;
   - c. vertical agencies;
   - d. law enforcement apparatus;
   - e. educational institutions;
   - f. health institutions;
   - g. religious institutions/organizations;
   - h. social community institutions;
      - i. business sector; and
   - j. other institutions.
3. Prevention of acts of violence against women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus having the main duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 7

1. Regional Governments are obligated to formulate prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 6 in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan.
2. Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

## Part Three

Integrated Services for Victims of Violence

#### Article 8

1. The Regional Government implements integrated services for Victims of Violence, through activities:
   - a. complaint services;
   - b. health services;
   - c. counseling;
   - d. psychological services;
   - e. spiritual guidance;
   - f. social rehabilitation services;
   - g. legal aid and legal assistance services;
   - h. safe house services; and/or
      - i. return and social reintegration.
2. In the event that the Regional Government does not yet have counseling personnel as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, it may request assistance from social workers, volunteer companions, and/or spiritual guides.
3. Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:
   - a. special service rooms within the police force;
   - b. expert and professional personnel;
   - c. service centers and Safe Houses; and/or
   - d. other facilities and infrastructure necessary for the recovery of victims including perpetrators.
4. The provision of services to Victims of Violence is carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with other social institutions.
5. Other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (4) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner.
6. Integrated Services for victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) through paragraph (5) are implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures.
7. Provisions regarding standard operating procedures as referred to in paragraph (6) are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 9

1. Complaint services as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (1) letter a are implemented to receive reports of acts of violence.
2. Reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may originate from:
   - a. victims;
   - b. referrals; or
   - c. outreach.

#### Article 10

1. Health services as referred to in Article 8 paragraph - (1) letter b, are implemented using health facilities owned by the Regional Government or other referral hospitals by providing treatment services and health recovery for victims.
2. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by health workers for victims in accordance with professional standards, operational procedure standards and the medical needs of victims.
3. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1), through efforts:
   - a. anamnesis of victims;
   - b. examination of victims;
   - c. providing first aid;
   - d. health recovery both physical and psychological, carrying out efforts to prevent disease and other health disorders;
   - e. counseling;
   - f. referring to more adequate health service facilities if necessary can refer to other parties with a multidisciplinary approach, including health services, medicolegal, psychosocial assistance and legal aid; and/or
   - g. providing information to the police.
4. In addition to the efforts as referred to in paragraph (3) for certain cases, health workers may provide reproductive health services in accordance with medical needs.
5. In providing services as referred to in paragraph (3) and paragraph (4), health workers must make medical records in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
6. For every medical action to be carried out by health workers there must be consent for medical action from the victim or their family in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
7. For the purposes of investigation, authorized health workers must make a visum et repertum and/or visum et repertum psichiatricum. or make a medical certificate.
8. Health workers have the right to obtain legal protection.

#### Article 11

Legal aid and protection services as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (1) letter g include:

   - a. providing protection in Rumah Aman (Safe House);
   - b. providing legal information to Victims;
   - c. providing Assistance for Victims as witnesses from the investigation process to the verdict;
   - d. providing special legal protection for child victims of violence can be carried out by appointing guardianship in accordance with laws and regulations.

#### Article 12

1. Repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (1) letter g are implemented with the following principles:
   - a. voluntary;
   - b. safe and dignified; and
   - c. respect for rights.
2. In the event that the Regional Government is not yet able to carry out repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in paragraph (1) it may request assistance from related parties.
3. Social services to administer social welfare for Children in conflict with the law are carried out by the Regional Government together with LPKS with other social institutions.
4. LPKS and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) are rehabilitation and temporary shelter places for children in conflict with the law before repatriation and social reintegration are carried out.

Fourth Part Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 13

1. The Regional Government carries out empowerment of victims of acts of violence in the form of:
   - a. job training;
   - b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
   - c. capital assistance.
2. Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by the Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.

# CHAPTER IV

## CHILD PROTECTION

## Part One

Protection of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

### Paragraph 1

Stages of Protection for Child Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 114

The stages of protection for child victims of acts of violence include:

   - a. prevention of acts of violence against children;
   - b. handling of child victims of acts of violence; and
   - c. rehabilitation of child victims of acts of violence.

### Paragraph 2

Prevention of Violence Against Children

#### Article 15

1. Prevention of acts of violence against children as referred to in Article 14 letter a is implemented by:
   - a. forming a network in efforts to prevent violence against children;
   - b. conducting coordination, integration, and synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, the private sector, and non-governmental organizations; and
   - c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of child victims of violence.
2. Prevention of violence against children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus that has the main duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 16

1. Prevention of acts of violence against children, in addition to being implemented by the Regional Apparatus as referred to in Article 14 paragraph (2), also involves the following parties:
   - a. parents, family and/or closest relatives;
   - b. community;
   - c. vertical agencies;
   - d. law enforcement apparatus;
   - e. educational institutions;
   - f. health institutions;
   - g. religious institutions/organizations;
   - h. community social institutions;
      - i. child social welfare institutions;
   - j. business sector; and
   - k. other institutions that are engaged in the field of child protection care.
2. Provisions regarding the involvement of parties as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in a Regent Regulation.

### Paragraph 3

Handling of Child Victims of Acts of Violence Article 17 The handling of child victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 14 letter b shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

### Paragraph 4 Rehabilitation of Child Victims of Acts of Violence Article 18 (1) Rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 14 letter c shall be conducted in the form of:

   - a. legal aid services;
   - b. health rehabilitation services;
   - c. social rehabilitation services;
   - d. return and social reintegration services; and/or
   - e. restitution services for child victims of violence.
2. In rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with:
   - a. vertical agencies;
   - b. provincial government;
   - c. other district/city governments;
   - d. village/urban village government;
   - e. non-governmental organizations;
   - f. private parties;
   - g. the community; and/or
   - h. families.

## Part Two

Special Protection for Children Article 19 (1) Special protection for children is provided to:

   - a. children in emergency situations;
   - b. children in conflict with the law;
   - c. children from minority and isolated groups;
   - d. children who are exploited economically and/or sexually;
   - e. children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic substances, and other addictive substances abuse;
   - f. children who are victims of pornography;
   - g. children with HIV/AIDS;
   - h. children who are victims of kidnapping, sale, and/or trafficking;
      - i. children who are victims of physical and/or psychological violence;
   - j. children who are victims of sexual crimes;
   - k. children who are victims of terrorist networks;
   - l. children with disabilities;
   - m. children who are victims of maltreatment and neglect;
   - n. children with deviant social behavior; and
   - o. children who are victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their parents' condition.
2. Regional Government in implementing the handling of special protection for children as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

##### CONCERNING

##### PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of Violence against Women and Children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in Parigi Moutong Regency so that they are protected from Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the form of regional regulations. Thus far, laws and regulations governing the protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence have not yet regulated protection efforts at the Regional level, therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation. This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of Women and Children Victims of Violence in Parigi Moutong Regency.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Self-explanatory

#### Article 2

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 3

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 4

Letter a What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, loss of consciousness and or causes death.

Letter b What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Letter c What is meant by "economic violence" is an action that restricts a person from working inside or outside the home to earn money and goods, including allowing a working person to be exploited, while someone does not fulfill the economic needs of the family.

Letter d What is meant by "social violence" is an act of a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life of a person or can cause physical damage or damage to another person's property.

Letter e What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering in a person.

Letter f What is meant by "Domestic Neglect" is:

   - a. actions that result in the non-fulfillment of;
   - b. the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
   - c. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after children as they should be;
   - d. actions that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person;
   - e. actions that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.
