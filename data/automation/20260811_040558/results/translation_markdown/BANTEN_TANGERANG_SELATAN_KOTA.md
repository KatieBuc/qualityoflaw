# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this regional regulation, what is meant by:

1. Regional Government is the Mayor, and regional apparatus as elements of the organizer of the Regional Government of South Tangerang City.
2. Mayor is the Mayor of South Tangerang.
3. Implementation is the form of execution of protection for women and children victims of violence.
4. Woman is a person who has female genitalia, can experience menstruation, pregnancy, childbirth, breastfeeding and includes a person who has obtained legal status as a Woman.
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb.
6. Violence is any act that results in or can result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim.
7. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
8. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.
9. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
10. Economic violence is any act that neglects family members in the form of not providing a proper living, care or maintenance.
11. Psychological violence is any act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
12. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
13. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
14. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
15. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an institution that provides services and protection for women and children especially those who have problems due to acts of violence, discrimination, human trafficking and other social problems in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psycho-social, public facility services and legal protection and assistance.
16. Community Social Institution is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and or other Community Organizations.
17. Population is South Tangerang Citizens and foreigners who enter legally and reside in the South Tangerang City area.
18. Service is an action that must be immediately taken to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.
19. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy in order to provide strengthening and recovery for victims of violence as determined in a decision of an authorized official.
20. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy and spiritual guidance for self-strengthening of victims of violence especially victims of domestic violence to resolve the problems they face.
21. Integrated Service Center hereinafter referred to as PPT is a unified unit that carries out integrated service functions for women and children victims of violence, based in hospitals, Community Health Centers, P2TP2A and other similar institutions that are managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psycho-social and legal services.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out based on respect and recognition of equal rights and human dignity, non-discrimination, gender equality and justice as well as protection of the basic rights of women and children and the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

The Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to provide prevention, protection, and services for women and children against gender-based violence and based on the best interests of the child, which occurs in the household or in public.

#### Article 4

The prevention activities against the occurrence of violence as referred to in Article 3, include:

   - a. Socialization;
   - b. Dissemination; and
   - c. Training.

#### Article 5

The activities of protection and services for victims as referred to in Article 3, include:

   - a. Health services;
   - b. Victim assistance;
   - c. Counseling;
   - d. Spiritual guidance;
   - e. Resocialization; and
   - f. Empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 6

Every woman and child victim of violence has the right to: a. Protection;

   - b. Obtain information;
   - c. Optimal service;
   - d. Ongoing handling up to the rehabilitation stage;
   - e. Confidential handling;
   - f. Psychological and legal assistance;
   - g. A guarantee of the rights related to their status as a family member as well as a member of society.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 7

1. Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement prevention and mitigation efforts against the occurrence of violence toward women and children.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented in the form of:
   - a. Collecting data and information about women and children victims of violence;
   - b. Conducting education about anti-violence values toward women and children;
   - c. Conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence;
   - d. Training for PPT members;
   - e. Conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.
3. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence toward women and children, Regional Government is obligated to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
   - a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving relevant community elements;
   - b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims.

#### Article 8

In carrying out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in Article 7, the Regional Government must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

# CHAPTER V

INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

2. The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), shall consist of elements from the Regional Government and elements from related institutions outside the Regional Government.
3. Further provisions regarding the PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be further regulated by Mayor Regulation.
4. The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), in implementing the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence may conduct coordination with institutions and/or organizations in South Tangerang City and other regions.

# CHAPTER VI

## HANDLING AND MECHANISM

#### Article 10

1. The implementation of Protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner in medical handling, legal protection, medicolegal, psychological and economic aspects, the implementation of which can be conducted through partnerships with social community institutions engaged in the protection of women and children.
2. The implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1), must take into account religious norms as well as the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.
3. Principles of Service and Assistance:
   - a. Fast, safe and empathetic;
   - b. Guarantee of confidentiality;
   - c. Easily accessible;
   - d. No fees charged.
4. The mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by a Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE AND PROTECTION

#### Article 11

1. Forms of violence against Women and Children include physical, sexual, economic and psychological violence.
2. Forms of physical violence are any acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, loss of consciousness and or cause death.
3. Forms of sexual violence are sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
4. Forms of economic violence are neglect of family members in the form of not providing a proper livelihood, care or maintenance.
5. Forms of psychological violence are acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering in a person.

#### Article 12

1. Every woman and child who becomes a victim of violence must receive protection in accordance with applicable provisions.
2. Protection of women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be provided to residents of South Tangerang City whether it occurs in South Tangerang City or outside South Tangerang City.
3. Protection of women and children victims of violence as an initial action shall also be provided to non-residents of South Tangerang City where the incident occurs within the territory of South Tangerang City, followed by coordination with the region of origin of such persons.

#### Article 13

1. Forms of protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence consist of medical, legal, medicolegal (forensic medicine), economic and psychological protection.
2. The form of medical protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.
3. The form of legal protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.
4. The form of medicolegal protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field.
5. The form of psychological protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of assistance in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims including provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation against victims and providing psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in resolving problems.
6. The form of economic protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of skills-training services and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

# CHAPTER VIII

# CHAPTER X

#### Article 17

3. Any person who deliberately fails to provide protection to women and children resulting in the occurrence of violence, allows violence to occur, fails to report and fails to provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as regulated in the applicable laws and regulations.

##### I. GENERAL

The State's view is based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. The State holds the view that all forms of violence, especially domestic violence, constitute violations of human rights and crimes against human dignity as well as forms of discrimination.

The State's view is based on Article 28 of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, including its amendments. Article 28G paragraph (1) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia stipulates that "Every person shall have the right to protection of his/her person, family, honor, dignity, and property under his/her control, and shall have the right to feel secure and to receive protection from the threat of fear to do or not do something which is a human right". Article 28H paragraph (2) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia stipulates that "Every person shall have the right to receive facilities and special treatment to obtain the same opportunities and benefits in order to achieve equality and justice".

Current developments show that acts of physical, psychological, sexual violence, and domestic neglect do in fact occur, thus requiring adequate legal instruments to eliminate violence, particularly domestic violence. Legal reform that favors vulnerable or subordinated groups, especially women and children, has become very necessary in connection with the many cases of violence, especially domestic violence. This also occurs in South Tangerang City. In 2010, violence cases reported to the Tangerang Police totaled 143 cases, 56 cases were recorded at the South Jakarta Metro Police, 23 cases were recorded at the Tigaraksa Police (Tangerang Regency), and in 2011, 70 cases were recorded at the South Jakarta Metro Police and 28 cases at the Tigaraksa Police (Tangerang Regency). This does not yet reflect the actual conditions in society, because cases of violence, especially those occurring within the household, are still considered shameful in the view of society, and the courage of the community to report incidents still needs to be increased. Therefore, a regional regulation on the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence needs to be owned by South Tangerang City.

The emergence of cases of violence against children and women is often caused by factors that develop within society, such as low economic levels, education, social environment, and others. Therefore, victims of such violence need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality, and upholding human rights. From the above framework, the Regional Government is responsible for taking actions, whether legal, political, economic, or social, to suppress, prevent, reduce, and eliminate all forms of violence against children and women.

In conducting guidance, development, and protection of women and children, the role of society is needed, whether through women and children protection institutions, religious institutions, non-governmental organizations, community organizations, social organizations, the business world, mass media, or educational institutions.

This Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children from violence is closely related to several other laws and regulations already in force, including, among others, Law Number 1 of 1946 concerning the Criminal Code and its Amendments, Law Number 8 of 1981 concerning the Criminal Procedure Code, Law Number 1 of 1974 concerning Marriage, Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women, Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights and most importantly Law number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence and Law number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.

This Regional Regulation, in addition to regulating matters of prevention and protection as well as recovery for victims of violence, also regulates matters of obligations for law enforcement officers, health workers, social workers, volunteer companions, or spiritual counselors to protect victims so that they are more sensitive and responsive. To carry out violence prevention, the Agency or Office that has duties and responsibilities related to the field of women's empowerment and child protection implements preventive actions, among others, organizing communication, information, and education about violence prevention, protection of women and children.

Based on these considerations, it is time to establish a Regional Regulation in South Tangerang City concerning the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, particularly Domestic Violence, which is regulated comprehensively, clearly, and firmly to protect and side with victims, while simultaneously providing education and awareness to the community and officials that all acts of violence constitute crimes against human dignity.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

The rights in this provision are intended to guarantee their life in accordance with human dignity, increase self-confidence, and the ability to participate in community, national, and state life. What is meant by discriminatory treatment includes, for example, treatment that discriminates on the basis of ethnicity, religion, race, group, sex, ethnic origin, culture and language, legal status, a child’s birth order, and physical and/or mental condition.

What is meant by “gender equality” is a condition in which women and men enjoy equal status and have the same conditions to fully realize their human rights and their potential for wholeness and continuity of life proportionally.

#### Article 3

What is meant by prevention is all activities carried out to prevent, reduce and even eliminate acts of violence, among others by organizing communication, information and education. What is meant by services and protection for women and children victims of gender-based violence is that in all service and protection actions, gender equality must be taken into account. What is meant by the best interests of the child is that in all actions concerning children carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies, and judicial bodies, the best interests of the child must be the primary consideration.

#### Article 4

Letter a What is meant by socialization is a process of instilling or transferring habits or values and rules from one generation to another generation in a group or society regarding the prevention of the occurrence of violence.

Letter b What is meant by dissemination is an activity aimed at target groups or individuals so that they obtain information, awareness arises, accept, and ultimately utilize that information.

Letter c What is meant by Training is a series of activities designed to improve the skills, knowledge, experience, or attitude changes of an individual. Training relates to the acquisition of certain skills or knowledge. Socialization, dissemination and training activities regarding violence prevention can be carried out by related Regional Work Units, related Agencies, individuals, groups or community social organizations.

#### Article 5

Letter a What is meant by health services is efforts that include promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative aspects.

Letter b What is meant by receiving psychological assistance is assistance provided by a psychologist to victims who suffer from trauma/other mental problems to restore the victim's mental condition. Meanwhile, legal assistance is assistance efforts provided by persons and/or legal aid institutions to victims at every level of examination and throughout the legal process.

Letter c What is meant by counseling is the provision of assistance by an expert or a person who is trained in such a way that the victim's psychological understanding and capacity increases in solving the problems faced.

Letter d What is meant by spiritual guidance is counseling provided by a clergyman.

Letter e What is meant by resocialization is that victims can resume life in society including the restoration of their good name.

Letter f What is meant by empowerment is making something become empowered or have power or have strength.

#### Article 6

Letter a What is meant by Protection is that victims receive protection from family, police, prosecutors, courts, advocates, social institutions or other parties, either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court.

Letter b What is meant by obtaining information is the right of victims to obtain information related to prevention, complaints, medical services and protection.

Letter c What is meant by Optimal service is service that can be provided to victims in accordance with the capacity of those providing it.

Letter d What is meant by Continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage is handling provided up to the recovery of victims in society.

Letter e What is meant by confidential handling is an effort to guarantee certainty for victims that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disseminated.

Letter f What is meant by Psychological and legal assistance is the right of victims to obtain psychological and legal assistance at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Letter g What is meant by a Guarantee of rights is the right of victims to obtain a guarantee of rights related to their status as a family member and as a member of society.
