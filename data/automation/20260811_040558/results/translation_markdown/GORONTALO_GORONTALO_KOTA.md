   - b. that all forms of acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their self-worth and dignity need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination;
   - c. that women's empowerment and child protection are concurrent governmental affairs delegated to the regions to become the basis for implementing regional autonomy and constitute mandatory governmental affairs not related to basic services as referred to in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the City of Gorontalo.
2. Mayor is the Head of the Region.
3. Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of the organizer of Regional Government who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous Region.
4. Regional Governance is the administration of government affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and co-administration tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
5. Woman is a person who is female by sex.
6. Child is a person who is not yet 18 years old, including a child who is still in the womb.
7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering, whether physical, sexual, economic, social, or psychological, to the victim.
8. Violence against women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.
10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence that occurs in the area of the City of Gorontalo.
11. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, court, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will, is, or has occurred.
12. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with the dignity and worth of humanity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
13. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is, or has occurred.
14. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
15. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the Region to provide services in the context of empowerment of women and children victims of violence.
16. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers from the perpetrator.
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct lineage upward or downward up to the third degree.
18. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.
19. Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner.

#### Article 2

Protection of Victims is based on:

   - a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of victims;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. non-discrimination;
   - d. the best interests of victims; and
   - e. empowerment.

#### Article 3

Protection of Victims aims to:

   - a. prevent violence against women and children;
   - b. provide services to Victims; and
   - c. provide empowerment to female victims of violence.

#### Article 4

The scope of protection for Victims includes prevention efforts, services and empowerment for victims of violence in Gorontalo City.

# CHAPTER 11

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence include:

   - a. physical violence;
   - b. psychological violence;
   - c. sexual violence;
   - d. neglect;
   - e. exploitation; and/or
   - f. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, gugumy a kandungan, pingstin and/or cause death.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5, letter f b, is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of the ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering in a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f c is caused by:

   - a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
   - b. forced sexual intercourse;
   - c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
   - d. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and/or specific purposes

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:

   - a. acts that result in the non-fulfillment of the needs of children in a reasonable manner, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
   - b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after children as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
   - c. acts that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
   - d. acts that result in economic dependence by means of restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter f occurs due to:

   - a. acts that economically or sexually exploit with the intention of benefiting oneself or another person;
   - b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of the physical body, sexual aspects, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues, or utilizing a person’s labor or abilities by another party to obtain benefits, whether material or immaterial; and/or
   - c. all forms of utilization of the victim’s sexual organs or other body organs to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:

   - a. threats of violence, which include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrain a person's inherent freedom; and
   - b. coercion, including: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 12

Every Victim has the right to:

   - a. be respected for their dignity and worth as a human being;
   - b. restoration of health and psychological condition from the suffering experienced by the victim;
   - c. determine their own decisions;
   - d. obtain information;
   - e. confidentiality of their identity;
   - f. compensation;
   - g. social rehabilitation;
   - h. complaint handling;
      - i. obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or
   - j. assistance.

#### Article 13

Child victims of violence, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 12, also obtain special rights, consisting of:

   - a. respect for survival, growth and development;
   - b. basic services;
   - e. equal protection;
   - d. freedom from various stigma; and/or
   - e. obtaining freedom.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

Regional Government

#### Article 14

1. The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to implement protection efforts for women and children victims of violence through:
   - a. policy determination;
   - b. preparation of program and activity planning;
   - c. establishment of P2TP2A;
   - d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure;
   - e. allocating budget; and
   - f. establishment of FPKK.
2. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government provides services for victims in the form of:
   - a. facilitating the establishment of P2TP2A;
   - b. facilitating P2TP2A facilities and infrastructure according to capacity; and
   - c. facilitating FPKK as a network forum for victim handling.

#### Article 15

The Regional Government is obliged and responsible for:

   - a. supervising the implementation of services for victims, with minimum service standards; and
   - b. providing funds for the protection of victims through the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBD) and/or other legitimate regional financial sources.

Second Part Society and Family

#### Article 16

The community and family have obligations and responsibilities which include:

   - a. preventing the occurrence of violence against women and children;
   - b. reporting if violence occurs;
   - e. protecting victims; and
   - d. providing emergency assistance.

# CHAPTER VI

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Prevention

#### Article 21

1. Efforts to prevent violence against women and children shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the regional apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by:
   - a. establishing a network in efforts to prevent violence;
   - b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
   - c. establishing a violence prevention system;
   - d. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence; and
   - e. providing critical education on the rights of women and children for the community.

#### Article 22

In addition to Regional Governments, prevention efforts as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (1) shall be carried out by:

   - a. family and/or closest relatives;
   - b. community;
   - c. educational institutions; and
   - d. Non-Governmental Organizations.

Second Part Services

#### Article 23

1. The provision of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units through a network.

#### Article 24

The provision of services to victims is carried out with:

   - a. promptly;
   - b. safe and comfortable;
   - c. a sense of empathy;
   - d. non-discrimination;
   - e. easily accessible;
   - f. no charges; and
   - g. their confidentiality is guaranteed.

#### Article 25

Forms of services for victims include:

   - a. complaint, consultation, and counseling services;
   - b. assistance services;
   - c. health services;
   - d. social rehabilitation pelayarian services;
   - e. legal services; and
   - f. repatriation and social reintegration services.

#### Article 26

Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Passil 25 huru f a include:

   - a. identification or initial recording of the victim; and
   - b. consent to carry out the action.

#### Article 27

Accompaniment services as referred to in Article 25 letter b include:

   - a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;
   - b. accompanying victims during the medicolegal process;
   - c. accompanying victims during the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and court;
   - d. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;
   - e. protecting the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media;
   - f. coordinating with other companions; and
   - g. providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 28

Health services as referred to in Article 25 letter e include:

   - a. first aid to victims;
   - b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics; and
   - e. referral to health services.

#### Article 29

1. Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 25 letter d constitute services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims.
2. Social rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:
   - a. providing spiritual guidance to victims; and
   - b. psychological recovery of victims.

#### Article 30

1. Legal services as referred to in Article 25 letter e are in the context of assisting victims in undergoing the judicial process.
2. Legal services as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:
   - a. providing legal consultation which includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;
   - b. accompanying victims at the level of investigation, prosecution, and examination in court hearings and assisting victims to completely describe the violence they have experienced;
   - c. conducting coordination with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 3 1

1. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 25 letter f aim to return victims to their families and social environment.
2. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with:
   - a. District/City Governments within the territory of Gorontalo Province; and
   - b. related agencies and institutions, both governmental and non-governmental.

#### Article 36

Fourth Part Coordination of Victim Protection

#### Article 37

1. In an effort to protect and organize the handling of services for victims, the Regional Government establishes FPKK.
2. FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of: a. Regional FPKK;
   - b. Sub-district FPKK; and
   - e. Urban Village (Kelurahan) FPKK.
3. The establishment of Regional FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a is determined by a Mayor Decree.
4. The establishment of Sub-district FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b is determined by a Camat Decree.
5. The establishment of Urban Village (Kelurahan) FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter e is determined by a Lurah Decree.
6. Further provisions regarding the organizational structure and elements of FPKK are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 38

The duties and functions of FPKK as referred to in Article 37 paragraph (1) are:

   - a. coordinating and synchronizing prevention, services, and empowerment for victims of violence against women and children;
   - b. maintaining and developing FPKK in prevention, services, and empowerment of victims of violence through networking and a referral system;
   - c. conducting education on non-discrimination values towards women and children;
   - d. disseminating laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence;
   - e. collecting, compiling, and presenting reports on data of victims of violence; and
   - f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER VII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 39

1. The community participates in the implementation of protection for victims.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of:
   - a. establishing family partners at the kelurahan level by the community;
   - b. establishing women and child protection units within community organizations;
   - c. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
   - d. providing first aid to victims; and
   - e. reporting to the competent authority if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights; therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in the City of Gorontalo so that they avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

Thus far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection measures at the Regional level; therefore institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection measures for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for women and children victims of violence in the City of Gorontalo.
