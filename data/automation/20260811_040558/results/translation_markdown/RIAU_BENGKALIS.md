   - c. that in the context of prevention and protection of women and children victims of violence, regulations are needed to guarantee the implementation efforts of protection for women and children in the Region;
   - d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children;

##### GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Bengkalis Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Government of Bengkalis Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Bengkalis.
4. Regional Apparatus is the Regional Apparatus of Bengkalis Regency as a supporting element of the Regent and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties, either temporarily or based on court determination.
7. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
8. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence.
9. Violence is any act that is against the law, with or without the use of means against physical and psychological aspects that cause danger to life, body, or result in the deprivation of a person's freedom.
10. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom arbitrarily, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
11. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.
12. Threat of violence is any act unlawfully in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom.
13. Domestic Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as KDRT, is any act against a person, especially women, that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts of coercion, or deprivation of freedom unlawfully within the scope of the household.
14. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
15. Guardian is a person or entity that in fact exercises parental authority as a Parent over a Child.
16. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
17. Society is individuals, families, groups, and/or community organizations.
18. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, milk kinship, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.
19. Non-Governmental Organization, hereinafter abbreviated as LSM, is an organization/institution formed by citizens of the Republic of Indonesia voluntarily of their own will and interested in and engaged in the field of social welfare business determined by the organization/institution as a manifestation of community participation in efforts to improve community welfare that emphasizes voluntary service.
20. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring in the Bengkalis Regency area.
21. Prevention is an effort to develop the capacity and mechanisms of the Regional Government and society in creating conditions that can prevent the occurrence of violence, maltreatment, KDRT, exploitation and/or neglect.
22. Handling is action that includes identification, rescue, rehabilitation and reintegration of women and children who are victims of acts of violence, maltreatment, KDRT, exploitation, maltreatment or neglect.
23. Repatriation is an effort to return women and child victims of violence from abroad to the debarkation point/entry point, or from the receiving area to the area of origin.
24. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will, is or has occurred.
25. Assistance is an effort made by a person or representative from an institution that has expertise in assisting victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
26. Safe house is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined standards.
27. Rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological, and social conditions in order to be able to carry out their role again properly both in the family and in society.
28. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person who experiences social dysfunction in order to be able to carry out social functions properly.
29. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
30. Legal aid is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocates to conduct the process of assisting witnesses and/or victims of violence against women and children that is gender responsive.
31. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget, hereinafter abbreviated as APBD, is the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of Bengkalis Regency.
32. Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA, is the Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children in the working area of several Sub-districts at the Office of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection of Bengkalis Regency.
33. Task Force for the Prevention and Handling of Criminal Acts of Human Trafficking is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating prevention and handling efforts for criminal acts of human trafficking.
34. Task Force for the Protection of Women and Children is a functional Work Unit that organizes integrated protection services and handling of cases of violence against children and women at the Sub-district and Village/Kelurahan levels.
35. Exploitation is an action with or without the victim's consent that includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs or unlawfully, transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of someone by another party to obtain material/immaterial benefits.
36. Human trafficking is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer or receipt of a person by threat of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
37. Empowerment is a process of developing independence and welfare of a person or society by increasing knowledge and skills to meet their life needs both economically and socially.
38. Restorative Justice is the settlement of criminal cases by involving perpetrators, victims, families of perpetrators/victims, and other related parties to jointly seek a fair settlement by emphasizing restoration to the original state, and not revenge.

Article Protection of women and children is carried out based on the principles of:

   - a. humanity;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. sheltering/protection (pengayoman);
   - d. best interests of women and children;
   - e. non-discrimination; and
   - f. empowerment.

Article This Regional Regulation is intended as:

   - a. fulfillment of the rights of children and women in the context of protection and prevention of violence for children and women in the Region; and
   - b. guidelines for the Regional Government and society to provide protection, services, medical rehabilitation, social rehabilitation and empowerment for women and children victims of acts of violence.

Article The purpose of protection of women and children in this Regional Regulation is:

   - a. to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
   - b. to eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
   - c. to protect and provide a sense of security for victims (women and children);
   - d. to provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence and to conduct rehabilitation and reintegration of women and children victims of acts of violence with the involvement of business actors;
   - e. to improve cooperation and partnerships with involvement from companies to implement empowerment and protection of women and children from various acts of violence;
   - f. to implement women's empowerment to improve the quality of life of women; and
   - g. to establish and implement policies and programs/activities for the fulfillment of children's rights.

Article The scope of implementation of protection of women and children regulated in this Regional Regulation includes:

   - a. prevention of violence against women and children;
   - b. provision of services for women and children victims of violence;
   - c. institutions;
   - d. cooperation and partnerships;
   - e. funding;
   - f. investigation; and
   - g. criminal provisions.

# CHAPTER II PREVENTION OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN

First Part Forms of Violence Against Women and Children Article Forms of violence against women and children include:

   - a. physical violence;
   - b. psychological violence;
   - c. sexual violence;
   - d. neglect;
   - e. exploitation; and/or
   - f. other violence.

Article Physical violence as referred to in Article letter constitutes an act that results in pain accompanied by injury, wounds or disability to the body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes the death of women and/or children.

Article Psychological violence as referred to in Article letter constitutes an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, a sense of helplessness and/or psychological suffering in women and/or children.

Article Sexual violence as referred to in Article letter includes:

   - a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
   - b. coercion of sexual relations in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
   - c. coercion of sexual relations with other persons for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

#### Article 10

Neglect as referred to in Article [letter] includes: a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care; b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after children as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;

   - c. acts of deliberately neglecting women and children within the household sphere, whereas according to the law applicable to him/her or due to consent or an agreement or contract, he/she is obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to such woman; and/or
   - d. acts that result in economic dependency by means of restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of such person.

#### Article 11

Exploitation as referred to in Article [letter] includes: acts of economic or sexual exploitation with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;

   - a. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, work or forced service;
   - b. slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or capabilities of a person by other parties to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/or all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, but not limited to prostitution activities or sexual abuse.

#### Article 12

Other violence as referred to in Article [letter] constitutes threats of violence and coercion.

1. Threats of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom. Coercion as referred to in paragraph (1) includes a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

## Part Two Rights of Women and Children

### Paragraph Rights of Women

#### Article 13

Every woman has the right to:

   - a. obtain protection from discriminatory actions;
   - b. obtain protection from economic exploitation and sexual exploitation;
   - c. obtain protection from physical, psychological, and sexual violence and neglect;
   - d. obtain decent work in accordance with her capabilities;
   - e. obtain work leave while receiving wages/salary in accordance with statutory regulations;
   - f. obtain education and teaching at all types, levels, and pathways of education;
   - g. receive special protection in the implementation of her work or profession against matters that may threaten safety and/or health in connection with women's reproductive function; and
   - h. ownership and management of joint assets in marriage in accordance with statutory regulations.

### Paragraph on Children's Rights

#### Article 14

Every child has the right:

   - a. to live, grow, develop and participate reasonably in accordance with human dignity; to obtain protection from discriminatory actions;
   - b. to obtain protection from economic exploitation and sexual exploitation;
   - c. to obtain protection from physical, psychological, sexual violence and neglect;
   - d. to a name as personal identity and citizenship status;
   - e. to obtain a birth certificate free of charge;
   - f. to embrace a religion and worship according to religious teachings with the guidance of parents/guardians;
   - g. not to be forced to change religion by foster parents, adoptive parents and by anyone;
      - i. to be recognized and know their parents, to be raised and cared for by their own parents;
   - j. to obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual and social needs;
   - k. to obtain education and teaching in accordance with their interests and talents;
   - l. to obtain care, education and training, to improve themselves and the ability to participate in community and state life for children with special needs;
   - m. to express and have their opinion heard; and
   - n. to rest and utilize time for self-development.

## Part Three Rights of Victims

#### Article 15

Women and children victims of acts of violence shall receive the following rights: a. the right to have their dignity and worth as human beings respected; b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim; the right to determine their own decisions; the right to obtain information; the right to confidentiality, especially regarding their identity; the right to compensation; the right to social rehabilitation; the right to complaint handling; the right to assistance; and i.

   - j. the right of victims and their families to obtain facilitation in the judicial process.

#### Article 16

Women and children victims of acts of violence, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 15, also obtain special rights, as follows:

   - a. the right to respect and full utilization for survival, growth and development;
   - b. the right to basic services;
   - c. the right to equal protection;
   - d. the right to be free from various stigma;
   - e. the right to obtain freedom;
   - f. the right to obtain empowerment; and
   - g. the right to be tried based on restorative justice.

# CHAPTER III PROVISION OF SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE Part One General

#### Article 17

The protection of women and children is a shared responsibility of:

   - a. Regional Government;
   - b. Parents, Guardians and/or Family;
   - c. Community; and
   - d. Business sector/private sector.

### Paragraph Regional Government

#### Article 18

The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government (1) as referred to in Article 17 letter a, include:

   - a. formulating and implementing programs and activities related to the protection of women and children;
   - b. implementing the protection of women and children;
   - c. supervising services for victims of violence based on minimum service standards in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations; and
   - d. allocating a budget for the protection of women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity.

In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities (2) as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government formulates programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in a regional action plan which is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government (3) as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their main duties and functions. Further provisions regarding the regional action plan (4) as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be further stipulated by a Regent's Regulation.

### Paragraph Parents, Guardians and/or Family

#### Article 19

Parents, guardians and/or family members legally have (1) full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence, report when violence occurs and protect victims. Parents and/or family members must carry out prevention (2) of all forms of violence by providing guidance and exemplary conduct in accordance with religious teachings and applicable norms. In the event that parents, guardians and/or family members as referred to in paragraph (1) do not exist or their whereabouts are unknown or due to certain reasons cannot carry out their obligations and responsibilities, then (3) the obligations and responsibilities may be transferred to the Regional Government and other family members implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

### Paragraph on the Community

#### Article 20

1. The community has the obligation and responsibility for the protection of women and children victims of violence through community participation activities in the protection of women and children.
2. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
   - b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
   - c. participating in the handling of victims of acts of violence.

### Paragraph on Business World/Private Sector

#### Article 21

The obligations and responsibilities of the business world/private sector as referred to in Article 17 letter [letter designation not provided in source text] include: a. protecting the rights of women;

   - b. participating in allocating and providing a budget for the protection and empowerment of women and children; and/or
   - c. providing wider scope of movement for women according to competence.

## Part Two

Implementation of Protection for Women and Children from Acts of Violence General Paragraph

#### Article 22

The Regional Government, in implementing the administration of protection of women and children from acts of violence, through the following activities:

   - a. prevention;
   - b. service; and
   - c. empowerment.

Prevention Paragraph

#### Article 23

Prevention as referred to in Article 22 letter a, is implemented by way of: (1)

   - a. forming a network in efforts to prevent violence against women and children;
   - b. conducting coordination, integration, and synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
   - c. establishing a system for prevention of violence against women and children;
   - d. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence;
   - e. providing knowledge about prevention and mechanisms for handling violence against women and children; and
   - f. enhancing the role and value of local wisdom as well as the role of customs in the protection of women and children.

Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in an integrated manner by Regional Apparatus whose (2) main duties and functions are in the fields of: - women's empowerment and child protection;

- education;

- health;

- social affairs and manpower;

- youth and sports; and - mental and spiritual.

Regional Apparatus in conducting prevention of acts of (3) violence against women and children as referred to in paragraph (1) may involve related agencies.

In addition to being implemented by Regional Apparatus and related agencies as referred to in paragraph (3), prevention (4) is also implemented by:

   - a. family and/or closest relatives;
   - b. community, NGOs, universities, community organizations, social organizations and religious organizations; and
   - c. business sector.

Procedures for prevention of acts of violence against women and (5) children as referred to in paragraph (1) are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

### Paragraph Services

#### Article 24

Services as referred to in Article 22 letter b, shall take into account:

   - a. service principles; and
   - b. forms of services for victims.

#### Article 25

The principles of service as referred to in Article 24 letter a, consist of:

   - a. fast;

safe and comfortable;

sense of empathy;

   - d. non-discrimination;

easily accessible;

   - e. f. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 26

Forms of services for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) of Article 24 letter b are as follows:

   - a. services for women and children victims of acts of violence, include:
1. complaint, consultation, and counseling services;
2. accompaniment services;
3. health services;
4. legal aid services;
5. repatriation and social reintegration services; and
6. social rehabilitation services.
   - b. in addition to the forms of services regulated in letter a, special services are also provided which include:
1. pick-up services; and/or
2. medical and psychological referral services.

The forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a and letter b shall be implemented by the Regional Apparatus in accordance with the minimum service standards established by the government.

Further provisions regarding the implementation of services as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 27

Complaint, consultation and counseling services as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1) letter/number include: a. identification or initial recording of victims; and b. consent for action to be taken.

#### Article 28

Assistance services as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1) letter include:

   - a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;
   - b. accompanying victims during the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office, and court;
   - c. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office, and court;
   - d. maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by the mass media;
   - e. coordinating with other assistants; and providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 29

Health services as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1) letter include:

   - a. first aid to victims by competent officers;
   - b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel, and other health services needed in the legal process;
   - c. referral to health services according to needs.

#### Article 30

1. Legal aid services as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1) letter ... are to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process.
2. Legal aid services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by:
   - a. providing legal consultation which includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;
   - b. accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination stages in court hearings and assisting victims to fully explain the violence they have experienced; and
   - c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 31

1. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1) letter/item are carried out to return victims to their families and social environment.
2. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by the Regional Government, in coordination with district/city governments within one provincial area or outside the province and/or related agencies and institutions, both governmental and non-governmental.

#### Article 32

Social rehabilitation as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1) letter/number is a service provided by companions to restore the traumatic condition of victims.

1. Social rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by means of:
   - a. providing guidance and counseling;
   - b. psychological recovery of victims;
   - c. accompanying victims in the family and community environment; and
   - d. providing Safe Houses for victims of violence who require continuous handling.
2. Special social rehabilitation for women victims of domestic violence and human trafficking is carried out by means of:
   - a. providing guidance and counseling;
   - b. psychological recovery of victims;
   - c. accompanying victims in the family and community environment;
   - d. providing training and skills education; and
   - e. providing business capital assistance.
3. Social rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by Regional Apparatus having principal tasks and functions in the fields of:
   - a. social affairs;
   - b. health;
   - c. women's empowerment and child protection;
   - d. education; and
   - e. mental and spiritual.
4. In addition to being implemented by Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (4), social rehabilitation can also be implemented by the community or social service institutions.

#### Article 33

1. The form of service as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1) shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.
2. Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes Regional Apparatus that implements duties and functions in the fields of:
   - a. women's empowerment and child protection;
   - b. social affairs;
   - c. manpower;
   - d. health;
   - e. education;
   - f. security and public order; and
   - g. mental and spiritual.
3. In implementing the duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (1), Regional Apparatus may cooperate with government agencies, the provincial government, other regency/city governments, and the community as well as the business sector.

### Paragraph Empowerment

#### Article 34

Empowerment as referred to in Article 22 letter f is carried out for women victims of violence, in the form of:

   - a. job training;
   - b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
   - c. capital assistance.

#### Article 35

Job training as referred to in Article 34 letter ... includes:

   - a. skills training;
   - b. field work practice; and
   - c. apprenticeship.

#### Article 36

Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups as referred to in Article 34 letter [specify letter] include: a. entrepreneurial skills training;

   - b. facilitation of the formation of joint business groups; and c. assistance in the implementation of business activities.

#### Article 37

Capital assistance as referred to in Article 34 letter [letter not specified in source text] includes: a. assistance for work facilities and infrastructure; and

   - b. facilitation of working capital assistance.

# CHAPTER COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 41

In order to achieve the objectives of protection of women and children as referred to in Article 4, Regional Governments may cooperate with other parties in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

The cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

   - a. exchange of data and information;
   - b. rehabilitation of victims of violence;
   - c. repatriation and social reintegration; and
   - d. provision of evidence and witnesses.

The cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be set forth in the form of a Joint Agreement and/or Cooperation Agreement in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two Partnership

#### Article 42

In addition to carrying out cooperation as referred to in (1)

#### Article 41

1. Regional Governments may partner with the business sector, NGOs/social organizations concerned with women and children in the protection of women and children from acts of violence.
2. The partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through:
   - a. provision of information on employment opportunities for women victims of acts of violence;
   - b. education and training for women and children victims of acts of violence;
   - c. educational assistance for women and children victims of acts of violence who have been withdrawn from their education;
   - d. fostering and increasing economic independence of women victims of acts of violence;
   - e. providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
   - f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities, private parties and various non-governmental organizations concerned with women and children from various forms of violence; and/or
   - g. opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children from various forms of violence up to the Village level.
3. The partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be set forth in the form of an agreement in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

## INVESTIGATION

#### Article 44

In addition to Investigator Officials of the State Police of the Republic of Indonesia, investigations into the criminal acts referred to in paragraph (1) of this Regional Regulation may be conducted by Civil Servants within the Bengkalis Regency Government whose appointment is determined in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Civil Servant Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) are authorized to:

   - a. conduct examinations of the truth of reports or information concerning criminal acts related to violence against women and children;
   - b. conduct examinations of any person and/or business entity suspected of participating in criminal acts related to violence against women and children in the business environment;
   - c. request information from any person and/or business entity in connection with incidents of criminal acts related to violence against women and children;
   - d. conduct examinations of evidence, records, or other documents concerning criminal acts related to violence against women and children;
   - e. conduct examinations at certain places suspected of containing evidence;
   - f. conduct seizure of goods and materials resulting from violations that can be used as evidence in criminal cases related to violence against women and children;
   - g. request assistance from experts in the framework of carrying out investigation duties concerning criminal acts related to violence against women and children; and
   - h. terminate the investigation if there is insufficient evidence of criminal acts related to violence against women and children.

In exercising their authority, Civil Servant Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) shall coordinate with the Indonesian National Police in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

4. Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) shall notify the commencement of the investigation and submit the results of their investigation to the Public Prosecutor through Investigator Officials of the State Police of the Republic of Indonesia in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Criminal Procedure Code.

# CHAPTER VII CRIMINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 45

Every person who intentionally commits violence against women and children as referred to in Articles ... through Article 12 shall be subject to criminal sanctions as regulated in the laws and regulations.
