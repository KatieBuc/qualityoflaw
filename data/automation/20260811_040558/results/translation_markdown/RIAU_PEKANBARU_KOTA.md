# GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the City of Pekanbaru.
2. Regional Government is the Government of the City of Pekanbaru.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Pekanbaru.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of the City of Pekanbaru.
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and DPRD in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
6. Office of Women's Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment of the City of Pekanbaru or other designation hereinafter referred to as the Office is the Regional Apparatus that carries out the duties and functions of Women's Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment affairs.
7. Head of the Office of Women's Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment of the City of Pekanbaru or other designation hereinafter referred to as the Head of Office is the Head of Apparatus whose scope of duties and responsibilities covers Women's Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment affairs.
8. Community is individuals, Families, Groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.
9. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their Child, or father and his Child, or mother and her Child, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
10. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
11. Every Person is an individual or corporation, whether in the form of a legal entity or not a legal entity.
12. Protection is all preventive actions, services and empowerment to protect and guarantee the rights of Women and Children from acts of violence.
13. Gender is a concept that refers to differences in roles and responsibilities of men and Women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
14. Gender and Child Data System is the institutionalization of the implementation of gender and Child data consisting of regulatory components, institutions, and mechanisms in the Region in the context of planning, implementation, evaluation, and reporting of results of gender-responsive and child-sensitive development policies/programs/activities.
15. Selected Data is data selected according to sex and general.
16. Gender responsive is consistent and systematic attention to the differences between Women and Men in society accompanied by efforts to eliminate structural and cultural barriers in achieving gender equality.
17. Gender sensitivity or gender awareness is the ability to identify power relationship gaps between Women and Men in the family and in the community, the impact of gender-based division of labor on Women and Men, and that the experiences, problems, needs, interests and aspirations of Women and Men are different.
18. Gender Mainstreaming in the region hereinafter referred to as PUG is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of regional development policies, programs, and activities.
19. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is a unit that organizes integrated services for victims of violence and is integrated with PPT that organizes integrated services for witnesses and/or victims of criminal acts of trafficking in persons.
20. Gender Equality hereinafter referred to as KG is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between Women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.
21. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social abilities, knowledge, and skills so that Women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.
22. Woman is a human being of female sex and a person recognized by law as a Woman.
23. Empowerment of victims of violence is the strengthening of Women victims of violence to be able to try and work independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
24. Criminal Act of Trafficking in Persons or hereinafter referred to as TPPO is any action or series of actions that meet the elements of criminal acts determined in Law Number 21 of 2007 Concerning the Eradication of Criminal Acts of Trafficking in Persons.
25. Health rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of victims including physical, psychological and social health so that victims can carry out their roles again properly and naturally in the family and in society.
26. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out social functions again properly.
27. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of violence from the regional area where the violence occurred back to the area of origin of the person concerned.
28. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
29. Integrated Service Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA is a Technical Implementation Unit established by the Regional Government in providing services for Women and Children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.
30. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a Child still in the womb.
31. Fulfillment of Children's Rights hereinafter abbreviated as PHA is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
32. Child with Disability is a Child who has physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory limitations for a long period of time who in interacting with the environment and community attitudes may encounter obstacles that make it difficult to participate fully and effectively based on equal rights.
33. Child with Excellence is a Child who has commendable character, extraordinary intelligence or has potential and/or special talents not limited to intellectual abilities, but also in other fields.
34. Children's Rights are part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and fulfilled by Parents, Family, community, state, government, and regional government.
35. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against Women and Children.
36. Safe House is a temporary residence or new residence designed under strict supervision and guard conditions for 24 (twenty-four) hours that is kept confidential in accordance with standards based on applicable provisions intended for Women and Children victims of violence.
37. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will, is or has occurred.
38. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
39. Violence is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.
40. Victim is a Woman and/or Child who experiences misery and/or suffering that occurs in the area of the City of Pekanbaru.
41. People with HIV/AIDS hereinafter referred to as ODHA are people who have been infected with HIV both at the stage of no symptoms and those who already have symptoms.
42. Standard Operating Procedure hereinafter abbreviated as SOP is a series of standardized written instructions regarding various processes of government administration, how and when it should be done, where and by whom it is done.
43. Child-Friendly City hereinafter abbreviated as KLA is a city that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of commitments and resources of the Government, Regional Government, community and business world that are planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of Children's Rights.

#### Article Protection of Women is organized with the following principles:

   - a. respect and appreciation for Human Rights;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. non-discrimination;
   - d. empowerment;
   - e. best interests of women;
   - f. protection;
   - g. continuity and sustainability;
   - h. openness;
      - i. order;
   - j. legal certainty; and
   - k. local wisdom.

#### Article Protection of Children is carried out based on the principles of:

   - a. respect and appreciation for human rights;
   - b. non-discrimination;
   - c. best interests of the Child;
   - d. respect for the Child's opinion;
   - e. the right to live, grow, and develop of the Child; and
   - f. local wisdom.

#### Article The purpose and objectives of this Regional Regulation are as the basis and guidelines for the Regional Government and stakeholders to:

   - a. fulfill the rights of Women and Children;
   - b. protect Women and Children maximally, provide a sense of security and peace for Women and Children;
   - c. prevent the occurrence of violence against Women and Children;
   - d. empower Women;
   - e. empower Women victims of violence;
   - f. provide services to Women and Children victims of violence;
   - g. provide protection to Reporters and Witnesses;
   - h. maintain household integrity so that a harmonious family is realized;
      - i. encourage the realization of quality families; and
   - k. increase the participation of non-governmental institutions to support regional efforts in providing protection to Women.

#### Article The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:

   - a. obligations and Responsibilities of the Regional Government;
   - b. rights of Women and Children;
   - c. improvement of the quality of life of Women in the Region;
   - d. protection of Women;
   - e. family quality;
   - f. fulfillment of Children's rights;
   - g. special protection of Children;
   - h. Gender and Child Data System;
      - i. funding;
   - j. participation of Parents, Family/Relatives and/or Community;
   - k. monitoring and Evaluation;
   - l. guidance and Supervision; and
   - m. prohibitions and sanctions.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

-12- Rights of Women

#### Article 10

Every Woman has the following rights:

   - a. to live in prosperous conditions physically and mentally;
   - b. to live in a family within the bonds of lawful marriage in accordance with the religious teachings she adheres to;
   - c. to obtain education and instruction in all types, levels and pathways of education;
   - d. to obtain protection from acts of discrimination;
   - e. to obtain protection from acts of economic and sexual exploitation;
   - f. to obtain protection from physical, psychological, sexual violence, and neglect;
   - g. to obtain decent work based on ability, interest and talent while upholding all matters related to her human nature;
   - h. to obtain access in advancing the business she undertakes for the purpose of achieving adequate income improvement;
      - i. to obtain work leave while receiving wages/income in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
   - j. to receive special protection in the implementation of work or profession against matters that may threaten safety and/or health in connection with Women's reproductive function;
   - k. to receive legal information and services in accordance with her needs;
   - l. to enjoy a good and healthy living environment in accordance with the standards and provisions of laws and regulations;
   - m. to own and manage joint property in lawful marriage in accordance with laws and regulations; and to have other rights inherent in accordance with her human dignity based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

Second Part Rights of the Child

#### Article 11

Every Child has the following rights:

   - a. the right to life, to grow and develop properly in accordance with human dignity, to participate in all fields of life; to obtain protection from discriminatory actions; to obtain protection from economic and sexual exploitation;
   - b. to obtain protection from physical, psychological, sexual violence and neglect;
   - c. population identity;
   - d. citizenship status;
   - f. to obtain a birth certificate;
   - g. to embrace a religion and worship according to the teachings of the religion and belief with the assistance of parental/guardian guidance;
   - h. not to be forced to change religion by foster parents, adoptive parents and by anyone;
      - i. to be recognized and to know their parents, to be raised and cared for by their own parents;
   - k. -14- to obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual, and social needs;
   - l. to obtain education and teaching in accordance with their interests and talents;
   - m. to obtain care, education, training, self-improvement and the ability to participate in community and state life for Children with physical, mental disabilities and special needs;
   - n. to express and have their opinion heard;
   - o. to rest and utilize free time for self-development outside of school hours;
   - p. to obtain other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Third Part Special Rights for Persons with Disabilities

#### Article 12

Women and children with disabilities have the right to: obtain various conveniences in carrying out

   - a. various public services; and obtain additional facilities and infrastructure in
   - b. carrying out activities in public spaces.

Second Part Women's Empowerment Paragraph Purpose of Women's Empowerment

#### Article 17

In order to enhance the role and position as well as quality of Women in family life and society, the Regional Government conducts empowerment of Women.

#### Article 18

The Empowerment of Women as referred to in Article 17 is intended so that every Woman has the ability and capacity to achieve progress in the economic, political, social, cultural, and legal fields.

### Paragraph on the Women's Empowerment System

#### Article 19

The Empowerment of Women as referred to in Article 18 shall be implemented through an empowerment system that is carried out comprehensively, inclusively and integratively.

#### Article 20

The Women's empowerment system aims to:

   - a. improve the quality of life of Women and Families;
   - b. increase the institutional capacity of Women's empowerment in the Region, including the development of its gender system; and
   - c. provide protection of Women's rights from various forms of violence and other discriminatory treatment.

#### Article 21

In order to realize the objective of improving the quality of life of Women and families as referred to in Article 20 letter [letter not specified in source text], it is carried out through:

   - a. providing access for Women to enjoy education services, health services, and other strategic fields;
   - b. encouraging the involvement of Women in the development process;
   - c. providing knowledge, skills, character values, morals, and family resilience;
   - d. encouraging programs that can increase the independence of Women in the economic, political, legal, social, cultural fields, as well as other strategic fields; and
   - e. providing opportunities and chances to every Woman to occupy various positions and posts in various fields of work, including strategic positions, as long as it is in accordance with her capacity and capability.

#### Article 22

In order to realize the objective of increasing the institutional capacity for Women's empowerment in the Region, including the development of its gender system as referred to in Article 30 letter, shall be carried out through efforts:

   - a. establishment, development and capacity strengthening of Women's protection institutions, including complaint units for violence against Women and legal aid services;
   - b. improving the quality of human resources of managers;
   - c. strengthening the institutional capacity of PUG in the Region; and
   - d. strengthening and developing gender data systems.

Paragraph Methods of Providing Protection of Women's Rights From Various Forms of Violence and Discriminatory Treatment

#### Article 23

In order to implement the objective of providing protection for Women's rights from various forms of violence and other discriminatory treatment as referred to in Article 20 letter [letter not specified in the source text], shall be carried out through:

   - a. promotive efforts;
   - b. preventive efforts;
   - c. curative efforts; and
   - d. rehabilitative efforts, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 24

Promotive efforts as referred to in Article 23 letter are carried out by:

   - a. strengthening coordination mechanisms and work networks between service units in efforts to handle cases of violence;
   - b. providing Communication, Information, and Education (IEC) materials related to the prevention and handling of violence; and
   - c. conducting socialization, advocacy, and social campaigns in the context of the prevention and handling of violence.

#### Article 25

Preventive efforts as referred to in Article 23 letter are carried out by means of:

   - a. conducting legal awareness education for the community, especially for women;
   - b. developing massive and sustainable movements involving the community in actions to prevent and handle violence;
   - c. instilling character values, morals, and family resilience; and
   - d. involving the role and participation of the community in the empowerment of Women.

#### Article 26

Curative efforts as referred to in Article 23 letter d are carried out by means of:

   - a. optimizing technical service units related to complaints of violence against Women;
   - b. providing adequate facilities and infrastructure for handling health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance; and
   - c. carrying out handling of victims of crime and violence quickly, precisely, and accurately by law enforcement officials.

#### Article 27

Rehabilitative efforts as referred to in Article 23 letter d shall be implemented by means of:

   - a. providing support personnel for victims of crime and violence, which includes, among others:
1. psychologists;
2. psychiatrists;
3. clergy/spiritual counselors;
4. advocates;
5. media personnel.
   - b. strengthening work networks and coordination in the reintegration process as well as returning victims to their families and/or social environment.

## Part Two

Prevention of Acts of Violence Against Women

#### Article 46

To anticipate and prevent acts of violence against Women from occurring, the Regional Government undertakes the following efforts:

   - a. providing various proper public facilities for Women in public spaces;
   - b. developing an integrated information system database on acts of violence against Women;
   - c. strengthening the competence of apparatus handling violence against Women;
   - d. creating various modules and guidelines on the prevention and handling of acts of violence against Women;
   - e. conducting various training and guidance for social workers operating in organizations/institutions handling violence against Women;
   - f. inviting the community to play an active role in recovery and reintegration;
   - g. strengthening the function of community organizations operating in the field of Women's empowerment;
   - h. conducting various socialization regarding prevention and anticipation of acts of violence against Women to various components of society in the Region; and
      - i. optimizing the role of religious institutions, customary institutions, culture, as well as exploring local wisdom in order to prevent and minimize violence against Women occurring both at home and outside the home.

#### Article 171

   - d. implementation of recreational activities;

   - f. avoidance of imposition of the death penalty and/or life imprisonment;

   - h. provision of justice before a Children's Court that is objective, impartial, and in hearings closed to the public;

   - n. provision of education;
   - o. provision of health services; and
   - p. provision of other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## GENDER AND CHILD DATA SYSTEM

#### Article 187

The Region organizes a Gender and Child Data System in the context of implementing PUG and integrating the rights of Children into selected data to broaden insight, as well as as input for gender analysis and the fulfillment of Children's rights.

#### Article 188

The Implementation of the Gender Data System is intended as a reference for Regions in providing and utilizing selected data for planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, evaluation and reporting of development policies/programs/activities that are gender responsive and child sensitive.

#### Article 189

The implementation of the Gender and Child Data System aims to:

   - a. strengthen and encourage institutional arrangements (regulations, institutions, mechanisms) of the data system by disaggregating by sex and general conditions in the Region, which is reliable, can be presented quickly, accurately, comprehensively, and up-to-date;
   - b. establish or strengthen coordination mechanisms among Regions, in the implementation of collection and processing of selected data; and
   - c. increase the availability and utilization of selected data for planning, implementation, monitoring, evaluation, and reporting of results of policies/programs/activities of development that is gender responsive and Child sensitive in the Region.

#### Article 190

In implementing the Gender and Child Data System as referred to in Article 187, the Region may establish or develop a selected data forum/working group.

#### Article 191

The selected data forum/working group as referred to in Article 190, has duties including:

   - a. coordinating data management units, research units, planning units, and reporting units both internally and externally in the implementation of the gender and Child data system;
   - b. encouraging implementing units/work units to collect and utilize selected data and gender and Child analysis in preparing the planning, implementation, evaluation, and reporting of gender-responsive and Child-sensitive development activities;
   - c. encouraging data management units, research units, and reporting units to document and disseminate selected data into publications and database systems that are updated regularly;
   - d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of the gender and Child data system at least (one) time in (one) year; and
   - e. reporting the results of the implementation of the gender and Child data system to the Mayor as well as the Institution

# CHAPTER XI

ROLE/PARTICIPATION OF PARENTS, FAMILY/RELATIVES AND/OR THE COMMUNITY

## Part One

Role/Participation of Parents Paragraph: Role toward Women

#### Article 198

Every parent who in their home has a Female, has a role in the following matters:

   - a. providing equal and comparable opportunities to Females in terms of obtaining access and opportunities to undergo education at all levels;
   - b. preventing every Female who is part of the family members under their leadership from receiving arbitrary, harsh, and inhumane treatment;
   - c. protecting their Female family members so they do not experience pressure and sexual harassment, physical and psychological violence that causes the Female concerned to be unable to live their life properly;
   - d. preventing a Female family member from entering into early marriage below the age limit determined by Law; and
   - e. not forcing a Female family member to marry a man/male whom she does not desire.
