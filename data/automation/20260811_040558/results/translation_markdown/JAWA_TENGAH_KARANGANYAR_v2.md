# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:

1. Region is Karanganyar Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Government of Karanganyar Regency.
3. Bupati is the Bupati of Karanganyar.
4. Regional People's Representative Council hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional People's Representative Council of Karanganyar Regency.
5. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
6. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social capabilities, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective capabilities.
7. Discrimination against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex, which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women.
8. All Forms of Discrimination against Women is all forms of Discrimination that include regional dimensions (disaster areas, conflict areas, border areas, underdeveloped areas, remote areas, and others), age dimensions (productive age, elderly, and others), and special dimensions (persons with disabilities, workers, and others).
9. Violence against Women is any action that violates, obstructs, eliminates enjoyment and disregards women's human rights.
10. Domestic Violence is any act against a woman that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically, and/or household neglect including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of freedom unlawfully within the household sphere.
11. Elimination of Violence against Women is a guarantee provided by the Regional Government to prevent the occurrence of violence, take action against perpetrators of violence and protect victims.
12. Elimination of Domestic Violence is a guarantee provided by the state to prevent the occurrence of domestic violence, take action against perpetrators of domestic violence, and protect victims of domestic violence.
13. Victim is a woman who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
14. Companion is a parent or representative from an institution that has expertise in accompanying victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
15. Temporary Protection is Protection directly provided by the police and/or social institutions or other parties, before the issuance of a Protection order determination from the court.
16. Protection Order is a determination issued by the Court to provide Protection to victims.
17. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
18. Gender Equality is equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social cultural, defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.
19. Gender Justice is a process to be fair to men and women.
20. Gender-Based Violence is any deviant action caused by an imbalance of power in relations between women and men that results in misery or suffering of women, including women, both physically, sexually and/or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom arbitrarily that occurs in the private/domestic realm and in the public realm.
21. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children's Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is an integrated service center in efforts to Empower women in various fields of development, as well as Protection of women and children from various types of Discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons, established by the Regional Government or community-based, which includes: referral centers, business consultation centers, reproductive health centers, legal consultation centers, integrated crisis centers (PKT), integrated service centers (PPT), trauma recovery centers (trauma centers), women's crisis handling centers (women crisis centers), training centers, science and technology information centers (PIPTEK), safe houses (shelters), transit houses, or other forms.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

#### Article 2

1. The Principles of Protection and Empowerment of Women are:
   - a. respect for human rights;
   - b. Gender Equality and Justice;
   - c. Protection of the rights of Victims; and
   - d. non-discriminatory.
2. The Objectives of Protection and Empowerment of Women are to:
   - a. guarantee the fulfillment of women's rights so that they can live and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth;
   - b. provide Gender Equality and Justice;
   - c. provide Protection and security for women Victims of violence and Discrimination, as well as criminal acts of trafficking in persons;
   - d. eliminate all forms of violence and Discrimination against women;
   - e. provide services to women Victims of violence, and witnesses; and
   - f. carry out recovery of women Victims of violence and increase the participation of women both individually and in groups as potential and resources in the implementation of social community life.
3. The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:
   - a. rights and obligations of Women;
   - b. obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government;
   - c. Protection of Women;
   - d. Empowerment of Women;
   - e. Protection of Women Victims of violence;
   - f. Prohibitions and Sanctions;
   - g. community participation;
   - h. Monitoring and evaluation; and
      - i. Funding.

# CHAPTER III

## RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 3

1. Every woman has the right to:
   - a. live and maintain life and improve her standard of living;
   - b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue her lineage;
   - c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life in order to become a human being who is faithful, pious, responsible, of noble character, happy, and prosperous in accordance with human rights;
   - d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without Discrimination;
   - e. be free from slavery or enslavement and threats;
   - f. obtain Protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;
   - g. obtain welfare and a decent life;
   - h. participate in politics;
      - i. perform legal acts;
   - j. be free to choose a partner in her marriage.
   - k. choose the work she likes and is also entitled to fair employment conditions;
1. receive fair wages in accordance with her work that is comparable and commensurate with her human dignity.
2. The right to participate in politics as referred to in paragraph (1) letter h is, women have the right to:
   - a. vote and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret voting; and
   - b. be appointed to every government position.
3. Every woman who is in a condition of disability, elderly age, and/or pregnant, has the right to obtain facilities and special treatment.
4. Every elderly woman, person with physical disability and/or mental disability, has the right to obtain care, education, training, and special assistance at the expense of the Regional Government to ensure a decent life in accordance with human dignity.

Second Part Rights of Women with Disabilities

#### Article 4

Every woman with disabilities, has the right to obtain:

   - a. education in all units, pathways, types, and levels of education;
   - b. employment and decent livelihood in accordance with the type and degree of disability, education, and ability;
   - c. equal treatment to play a role in development and enjoy its results;
   - d. accessibility in the context of independence;
   - e. rehabilitation, social assistance, and maintenance of social welfare standards; and
   - f. equal rights to develop talents, abilities, and social life.

Third Part Rights of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 5

Every female Victim of violence has: a. the right to have her dignity and worth as a human being respected;

   - b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the Victim;
   - c. the right to determine her own decisions;
   - d. the right to obtain information;
   - e. the right to confidentiality;
   - f. the right to compensation;
   - g. the right to social rehabilitation;
   - h. the right to complaint handling;
      - i. the right of the Victim and her family to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and j. the right to assistance.

Fourth Part Obligations of Women

#### Article 6

Every woman has the obligation to:

   - a. uphold her dignity and honor as a woman;
   - b. carry out her duties and responsibilities as a wife, mother, child and member of society properly and responsibly; and
   - c. comply with the applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 7

1. The Regional Government has obligations and responsibilities for the implementation of Women's Empowerment and Women's Protection.
2. The efforts for Women's Protection and Women's Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;
   - b. determination of implementation guidelines;
   - c. provision of services; and
   - d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.
3. The efforts for Women's Protection and Women's Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 8

1. In implementing policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women and Empowerment of women, the Regional Government may cooperate with the community/community organizations.
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 9

The Regent in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women shall undertake efforts to:

   - a. coordinate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women among Regional Apparatus;
   - b. cooperate with Regencies/Cities within one Province, and cooperate among Regencies/Cities in other Provinces in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
   - c. strengthen the institutional capacity of Gender mainstreaming for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of women;
   - d. facilitate services for the Protection of women;
   - e. provide services for the Protection of women;
   - f. implement affirmative action for the Protection of women; and
   - g. formulate a data system for the Protection of women.

# CHAPTER V

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

Health Protection

#### Article 10

1. Women have the right to obtain special Protection from matters that threaten their safety and/or health.
2. Special protection is given to women because their reproductive function is guaranteed and protected by law.
3. The Regional Government facilitates Health Protection in accordance with Regional Financial capacity.
4. The form of Health Protection for Women as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be further regulated by a Regent's Regulation.

Second Part Employment Protection

#### Article 11

1. Women have equal opportunity without Discrimination to obtain employment.
2. Equal opportunity as referred to in paragraph (1) is realized through the fulfillment of women's rights to receive equal treatment from Employers.

#### Article 12

1. Employers who employ female workers are obligated to provide Protection in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
2. Further provisions regarding the Protection that must be provided by employers to women as workers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by a Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

Protection of Elderly Women

#### Article 13

1. The Regional Government provides Protection to elderly women who are not independent, who are sick, who are economically incapable, and/or who are in a state of disability, so that they can realize and enjoy a decent standard of living.
2. Further provisions regarding the form of Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by a Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VI

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 14

In order to improve the position, role, and quality of women, as well as efforts to realize equality and Gender Justice in family life, community life, national life, and state life, women must be empowered.

#### Article 15

Women's Empowerment is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:

   - a. economy;
   - b. social culture;
   - c. politics; and
   - d. law.

#### Article 16

The Implementation of Women's Empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 15 letter a is carried out through:

   - a. provision of skills and job training;
   - b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;
   - c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;
   - d. facilitation and capital assistance; and
   - e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 17

Empowerment of women in the social and cultural field as referred to in Article 15 letter b shall be implemented through:

   - a. improvement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of education in stages according to potential to improve social status;
   - b. improvement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts primarily in the field of reproductive health;
   - c. improvement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and
   - d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and traditions and development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 18

1. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the political field as referred to in Article 15 letter c includes:
   - a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;
   - b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;
   - c. participation in general elections; and
   - d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.
2. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 19

1. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the field of law as referred to in Article 15 letter d includes:
   - a. increasing awareness and knowledge in the field of law through communication, information and education services; and
   - b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.
2. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the field of law as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 20

1. Every Victim has the right to obtain protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage, and confidential handling, provided by individuals, groups, or institutions, whether the Regional Government or the community.
2. In the event of violence, every Victim has the right to obtain health services and assistance, both psychological and legal, and to obtain guarantees for their rights related to their status as a woman.

Second Part Obligations and Efforts of Regional Government

#### Article 21

1. The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to implement efforts to prevent the occurrence of Violence against Women in the form of:
   - a. collecting data and information about victims of violence and laws and regulations;
   - b. conducting education on anti-Violence against Women values;
   - c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of Protection for victims of violence;
   - d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of Protection for victims of violence; and
   - e. preparing reports on the implementation of Protection for women victims of violence.
2. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women, the Regional Government is obligated to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
   - a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for Victims by involving community elements; and
   - b. encouraging public awareness of the importance of Protection for Victims.
3. The Regional Government in carrying out its obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians or other persons who are legally responsible for the Victim.
4. To prevent the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women, the Regional Government undertakes the following efforts:
   - a. conducting counseling and socialization regarding matters concerning Violence against Women;
   - b. conducting socialization of women's rights;
   - c. seeking to improve education for women;
   - d. opening skills courses for women to support the economic needs of the family;
   - e. creating employment opportunities for women;
   - f. providing counseling to immediately report to local authorities if Violence against Women occurs;
   - g. empowering Social Community Organizations and women's organizations to increase women's knowledge and insight;
   - h. establishing Protection for Women Complaint Posts; and
      - i. forming networks in efforts to prevent violence.

Third Part Implementation of Protection

#### Article 22

1. The Regional Government facilitates the establishment of a Women's Protection institution.
2. The Women's Protection institution as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by establishing P2TP2A.
3. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be implemented no later than 2 (two) years from the enactment of this Regional Regulation.
4. Further provisions regarding P2TP2A shall be regulated by a Regent's Regulation.

#### Article 23

1. Before the P2TP2A as referred to in Article 22 is established, the women’s protection institution shall be carried out by KP2A.
2. Further provisions regarding KP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in the Regional Regulation of Karanganyar Regency Number 5 of 2016 concerning the Implementation of Child Protection (Regional Gazette of Karanganyar Regency Year 2016 Number 54, Supplement to the Regional Gazette of Karanganyar Regency Number 57).

#### Article 24

1. The implementation of Protection for women Victims of violence is carried out in an integrated manner within the KP2A framework as referred to in

#### Article 23.

2. KP2A in handling medical, legal, medico legal, psychological and economic Protection may establish partnerships with social community institutions engaged in the Protection of women.
3. Further provisions regarding the Protection mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) stipulated by a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 25

The provision of services to Victims is carried out free of charge, quickly, safely, with empathy, non-Discriminatory, easily accessible, and with a guarantee of confidentiality.

Fourth Part Forms of Violence and Protection

#### Article 26

1. Forms of Violence against Women include: a. physical violence;
   - b. sexual violence;
   - c. economic violence;
   - d. psychological violence.
2. The form of physical violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, unconsciousness and or causes death.
3. The form of sexual violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
4. The form of economic violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c is the neglect of women in the form of not providing livelihood, care or maintenance in a proper manner.
5. The form of psychological violence as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 27

1. Every Victim of violence must receive Protection in accordance with applicable provisions.
2. Protection for Victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided to Regional Residents who experience violence in the Region or outside the Region.
3. Protection for Victims of violence, in addition to being provided to Regional residents as referred to in paragraph (2), shall also be provided to non-residents of the Region who experience violence in the Region.

#### Article 28

1. Forms of Protection against Violence against Women include:
   - a. Medical protection;
   - b. Legal protection;
   - c. Medico legal protection (forensic medicine);
   - d. Economic protection;
   - e. Psychological protection.
2. The form of medical protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of the Victim carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.
3. The form of legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is in the form of services and assistance to help Victims in undergoing legal and judicial processes.
4. The form of medico legal Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c is in the form of medical services for the purposes of evidence in the field of law.
5. The form of economic Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d is in the form of services for skills and to provide economic access so that victims of violence can be independent.
6. The form of psychological Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e is in the form of psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in solving problems, assistance in order to restore the traumatic condition of the Victim including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide.

#### Article 29

To provide Protection as referred to in Article 28, the Regional Government may undertake efforts to:

   - a. provide services and handling as quickly as possible to witnesses and/or Victims;
   - b. provide ease, comfort, safety, and free of charge for witnesses and/or Victims;
   - c. maintain confidentiality of witnesses and/or Victims;
   - d. guarantee justice and legal certainty for witnesses and/or Victims;
   - e. provision of special service rooms at Police Offices;
   - f. provision of apparatus, health workers, social workers, and spiritual counselors;
   - g. creation and development of systems and mechanisms for cooperation programs of services involving parties that are easily accessed by Victims;
   - h. provide Protection for companions, witnesses, family, and friends of Victims;
      - i. provide Protection for female workers/laborers in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## PROHIBITIONS AND SANCTIONS

#### Article 30

1. Every person and/or entity is prohibited from committing Violence against Women as referred to in

#### Article 26.

2. Every person and/or Entity in the Region is prohibited from committing Discrimination against Women, whether in words, actions, policies or regulations that are implemented.

#### Article 31

1. Violations of the provisions in Article 30 may be subject to criminal sanctions and/or administrative sanctions.
2. The imposition of criminal sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
3. Administrative sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of:
   - a. For individuals:
1. written warning;
2. administrative fine of at least Rp 10,000,000.00 (ten million rupiah) and at most Rp50,000,000.00 (fifty million rupiah).
   - b. For entities:
1. written warning;
2. temporary or permanent cessation of operational activities;
3. freezing of business permit;
4. administrative fine of at least Rp20,000,000.00 (twenty million rupiah) and at most Rp50,000,000.00 (fifty million rupiah);
5. revocation of Business Permit.
4. Administrative Fines as referred to in paragraph (3) shall constitute Regional Revenue.
5. Further provisions regarding the imposition of administrative sanctions as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (3) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 32

1. The community may participate in the implementation of Protection and Empowerment of Women.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, groups or social community organizations.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of:
   - a. cooperation;
   - b. active role in policy formulation;
   - c. complaints/reports.
4. The obligations and responsibilities of the community in the Protection of women shall be implemented through activities:
   - a. preventing the occurrence of criminal acts;
   - b. providing Protection to Victims;
   - c. providing emergency assistance;
   - d. assisting in the process of submitting applications for the stipulation of Protection; and
   - e. providing information or reporting the occurrence of criminal acts of violence to law enforcement officials.
5. Further provisions regarding community participation as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be regulated by a Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER X

## MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 33

1. To ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women, the Regional Government conducts monitoring.
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women in the Region.
3. Monitoring is conducted periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of Regional Apparatus implementing policies, programs and activities for the Protection of women.
4. Monitoring is conducted from planning to implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of women for the current year.

#### Article 34

1. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women shall be conducted at the end of each fiscal year.
2. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women shall be used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of women for the following year.
3. The evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

## FUNDING

#### Article 35

1. Funding for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of women in the Region is sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget, environmental social responsibility, and other funds that are legal and non-binding.
2. In the event that the Region receives funding assistance for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of women from the Government, it shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 36

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, the Regional Regulation of Karanganyar Regency Number 20 of 2009 concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence (Regional Gazette of Karanganyar Regency Year 2009 Number 20) insofar as it relates to the provisions on Protection of Women Victims of Violence, is revoked and declared invalid.

#### Article 37

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Karanganyar Regency.

##### REGARDING

##### PROTECTION AND EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN

##### I. GENERAL

Fundamentally, Regions are formed to improve public services and realize community welfare. Therefore, in the implementation of autonomy, regions have obligations including protecting the community, maintaining national unity, integrity and harmony, improving the quality of life of the community and realizing justice and equity.

The unequal distribution of opportunities in all areas of life, including in the fields of education and employment, can give rise to various social problems, especially for women. The reality shows that in Karanganyar Regency there are still acts of violence both physical, psychological, sexual, neglect of women in the household and even human trafficking, and this constitutes a violation of human rights.

Acts of violence and neglect are fundamentally violations of Human Rights. Therefore, to provide a sense of security, fulfillment of rights and recovery of victims, there needs to be Protection efforts for women.

Protection carried out by the Government towards women is concretely implemented, namely through the issuance of Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women (Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women), hereinafter abbreviated as CEDAW.

The government's efforts in protecting its citizens, especially women, are also clearly implied in the provisions of Article 3 paragraph (2) of Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights which states that: "every person has the right to recognition, guarantee, Protection and fair legal treatment as well as obtaining legal certainty and equal treatment before the law".

Violence and trafficking against women have been regulated in Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 of 2003 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, and Law Number 21 of 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Criminal Act of Human Trafficking. Therefore, it is the obligation of the Regional Government to protect, prevent and take action against violations of Violence against Women through its regulation in Regional Regulations, so that the community respects and honors the rights of women as they respect human rights.

In this Regional Regulation, the responsibilities and obligations of the Regional Government in the Protection of women are regulated, starting from prevention, Protection services, to recovery of women victims of various acts of violence, so that with the existence of this Regional Regulation it is hoped that various acts of Violence against Women will no longer exist in Karanganyar Regency.
