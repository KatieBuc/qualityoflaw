# REGENT OF SOUTH BANGKA,

   - b. that women are a national asset who play a very important role in the process of continuation and creation of quality generations, therefore they need to receive guarantees of protection from acts of violence and discrimination and need to be empowered so that they can actualize their potential optimally;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is South Bangka Regency.
2. Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the regional government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and assistance duties with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
4. Regent is the Regent of South Bangka.
5. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of South Bangka Regency.
6. Human Rights are a set of rights inherent in the nature and existence of human beings as creatures of God Almighty and constitute His gift that must be respected, upheld and protected by the state, law, government, and every person for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity and worth.
7. Women's empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social capacity, knowledge and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.
8. Women's protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
9. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, tribe, race, ethnicity, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs, which results in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
10. Gender is a concept that refers to roles and responsibilities as women and as men that are created and internalized in the family, society and community culture where we live including expectations, attitudes, characteristics, behavior of how to be a man and how to be a woman.
11. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.
12. Violence against women is any act that violates, hinders, eliminates enjoyment and disregards the human rights of women.
13. Disability is an individual who has physical or mental limitations.
14. Society is individuals, families, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.
15. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children.
16. Labor Force is every person who is capable of performing work to produce goods and/or services either to meet their own needs or for society.
17. Worker/laborer is every person who works by receiving wages or remuneration in other forms.

# CHAPTER III

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

Empowerment and Protection of Women, is implemented based on the principles of:

   - a. respect for human rights;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. non-discrimination; and
   - d. protection of victims.

#### Article 4

Empowerment and Protection of Women aims to: a. increase the participation of women in the development process;

   - b. improve the quality of life of women;
   - c. provide guarantees to women in the fulfillment of rights as human beings; and
   - d. provide a sense of security by increasing protection for women from various acts of violence.

# CHAPTER IV

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 5

1. Every woman has the right to:
   - a. live and sustain life and improve her standard of living;
   - b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, form a family and continue offspring;
   - c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life in order to become a human being who is faithful, pious, responsible, of noble character, happy, and prosperous in accordance with human rights;
   - d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;
   - e. be involved in every stage of the development process;
   - f. be free from slavery or enslavement and threats;
   - g. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;
   - h. obtain welfare and a decent life;
      - i. participate in politics;
   - j. perform legal acts; and
   - k. freely choose a partner in her marriage.
2. Every wife during the marriage bond has equal rights and responsibilities with her husband over all matters relating to their marital life, relations with their children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
3. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to children with due regard to the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility for the empowerment and protection of women. (2) The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
   - a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;
   - b. determination of implementation guidelines;
   - c. provision of services; and
   - d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.
3. The efforts for empowerment and protection of women as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 7

Women's empowerment is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:

   - a. economy;
   - b. social culture;
   - c. politics and government;
   - d. law;
   - e. education;
   - f. health;
   - g. employment; and
   - h. social security.

First Part Economic Field

#### Article 8

Empowerment of women in the economic field is implemented through:

   - a. provision of skills and job training;
   - b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;
   - c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;
   - d. facilitation and capital assistance; and
   - e. facilitation of marketing network development.

Second Part Social and Cultural Sector

#### Article 9

Empowerment of women in the social and cultural field is implemented through:

   - a. improvement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;
   - b. improvement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts primarily in the field of reproductive health;
   - c. improvement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and
   - d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and traditions and development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

Third Part Political and Governmental Affairs

#### Article 10

1. The empowerment of women in the field of politics and government includes:
   - a. the involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;
   - b. the provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;
   - c. participation in general elections; and
   - d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.
2. The empowerment of women in the field of politics and government as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.
3. The Regional Government provides guarantees to women to have the right to vote and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting according to the applicable laws and regulations.
4. The Regional Government provides opportunities for women to be appointed as regional government officials and occupy strategic positions in regional government; and (5) The Regional Government and/or Political Parties are responsible for providing political education for women.

Fourth Part Legal Field

#### Article 11

1. Empowerment of women in the field of law includes: a. increasing awareness and knowledge in the field of law through communication, information and education services; and
   - b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.
2. Empowerment of women in the field of law as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 12

Empowerment of women in the field of education through: a. organization of formal and nonformal education for women in South Bangka Regency.

   - b. Nonformal education aimed at improving women's capabilities.
   - c. Nonformal education as referred to in letter b, includes women's empowerment education, skills education and job training.
   - d. Formal and nonformal education as referred to in letter a, shall be regulated by a separate Regional Regulation.

Sixth Part Health Sector

#### Article 13

1. Women have the right to obtain health services in order to undergo reproductive life and sexual life that is healthy, safe, and free from coercion and/or violence.
2. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. Health Services for the Period Before Pregnancy, Pregnancy, Childbirth and After Childbirth;
   - b. Pregnancy regulation, contraceptive services and sexual health; and
   - c. Reproductive health services.
3. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented through promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative approaches.

Seventh Part Employment Sector

#### Article 14

1. Women have equal opportunity without discrimination to obtain employment.
2. Employers who employ female workers are obligated to provide protection that includes welfare, safety and health.

Eighth Part Social Security Sector

#### Article 15

1. The Regional Government is responsible for providing guidance facilities for women with social problems.
2. The Regional Government provides the right to social security for women with social problems.
3. The responsibility for providing guidance facilities for women with social problems is under the regional apparatus organization that handles women's empowerment affairs.

# CHAPTER VII

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 16

Implementation of protection for women is provided to:

   - a. female workers/laborers;
   - b. elderly women;
   - c. women with disabilities;
   - d. homeless women;
   - e. female domestic workers;
   - f. women as family economic supporters;
   - g. women who are former inmates of correctional institutions;
   - h. women victims of disasters;
      - i. female commercial sex workers; and
   - j. women victims of violence.

First Part Female Workers / Laborers

#### Article 17

1. The implementation of protection for female workers/laborers is to provide protection from acts of economic exploitation and cruel, inhumane treatment and to endeavor to ensure proper appreciation for their work.
2. Female workers/laborers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:
   - a. Recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;
   - b. Health coverage and employment social security;
   - c. Access to information and legal consultation services;
   - d. Opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve status;

Second Part Elderly Women

#### Article 18

1. Implementation of protection for elderly women in the framework of extending life expectancy so that they can carry out their social functions and play an active role naturally in their social life.
2. Elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to Protection for:
   - a. guarantee of continuity and personal self-development:
   - b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, arts and culture as well as utilization of information and communication technology:
   - c. accessibility to information, legal assistance, mobility, social services; and
   - d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their functions and capacities.

Third Part Women with Disabilities

#### Article 19

1. Implementation of Protection for women with disabilities in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination including freedom from cruel treatment, inhuman treatment, degrading human dignity and freedom from exploitation;
2. Women with disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:
   - a. guarantee of continuity and personal self-development;
   - b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, sports, arts and culture as well as utilization of information and communication technology;
   - c. accessibility to information, legal aid, mobility, social services; and
   - d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

Fourth Part Homeless Women

#### Article 20

1. The implementation of protection for homeless women in the context of providing protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.
2. Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection for:
   - a. decent housing;
   - b. health and social security;
   - c. population administration registration;
   - d. opportunities to obtain skills to improve economic status; and
   - e. access to information and legal consultation services.

Fifth Part Female Domestic Workers

#### Article 21

1. The implementation of Protection for female domestic workers is in the framework of providing protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, cruel treatment, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation in employment relationships.
2. Female domestic workers as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:
   - a. recognition of rights, wages, and decent working conditions; b. health insurance and social security for employment; c. access to information and legal consultation services; and d. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

#### Article 22

1. The implementation of protection for women as family economic pillars is in the framework of providing protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, negative stigma and economic empowerment for women as family economic pillars.
2. Women as family economic pillars as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive the right to protection from:
   - a. discriminatory actions and efforts to weaken the function of women as family economic pillars;
   - b. strengthening of capacity and recognition as family economic pillars in their environment; and
   - c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.

Seventh Part Women Former Inmates of Correctional Institutions

#### Article 23

1. The implementation of Protection for women former inmates of Correctional Institutions is intended to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting themselves in community life, so that they can carry out life normally.
2. Women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:
   - a. social rehabilitation and reintegration; and
   - b. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Eight

Women Victims of Disaster

#### Article 24

1. Protection Implementers for women victims of disaster in order to provide protection during disaster and post-disaster.
2. Women victims of disaster as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:
   - a. access to proper housing;
   - b. health services;
   - c. information access services and legal consultation; and
   - d. post-disaster recovery.

Ninth Section Female Commercial Sex Workers

#### Article 25

1. The implementation of protection for female commercial sex workers is in the context of providing health protection and enhancement of life skills capabilities as capital to improve social status.
2. Protection for female commercial sex workers as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. providing life skills capabilities;
   - b. facilitating reproductive health services; and
   - c. preventing the occurrence of human trafficking.

Tenth Part Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 26

1. Implementers of Protection for women victims of violence, in order to provide legal protection and/or health recovery.
2. Protection for women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. legal assistance;
   - b. assistance following the occurrence of acts of violence;
   - c. rehabilitation and social reintegration; and
   - d. health services.

# CHAPTER VIII

## STRATEGY FOR WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION

#### Article 27

1. Women's empowerment and protection is implemented by integrating gender mainstreaming and gender-disaggregated data in an integrated manner based on applicable laws and regulations.
2. In women's empowerment and protection, a Working Group and Integrated Services shall be formed as stipulated by Regent's Decree.
3. Women's empowerment and protection is coordinated by the Agency handling Women's Empowerment.

# CHAPTER IX

MECHANISM FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION

#### Article 28

The mechanism for implementing empowerment and protection of women is carried out through the following stages:

   - a. formulation of policy, program planning and activities;
   - b. budgeting of programs and activities;
   - c. implementation of programs and activities; and
   - d. monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

#### Article 29

1. The formulation of planning policies for programs and activities as referred to in Article 28 letter a, is implemented through:
   - a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of data;
   - b. coordination and synchronization of the preparation of planning for programs and activities with related OPD; and
   - c. determination of programs and activities in planning documents.
2. The formulation of planning policies for programs and activities is further regulated in minimum service standards and standard operating procedures.
3. The formulation of planning policies for programs and activities for the implementation of women's empowerment and protection, is coordinated by the Regional Apparatus responsible for planning.
4. The budgeting of programs and activities as referred to in Article 28 letter b, is implemented through general budget policies as set forth in the APBD.
5. The implementation of programs and activities as referred to in Article 28 letter c, is implemented through:
   - a. implementation of programs and activities as set forth in the regional apparatus budget document;
   - b. coordination and synchronization of the implementation of activity programs with related OPD.
6. The implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in Article 28 letter d, is implemented through:
   - a. conducting monitoring and evaluation periodically on the implementation of programs and activities that have been implemented;
   - b. making reports periodically; and
   - c. evaluation results as input material for the formulation of policies, programs and activities for the following year.

# CHAPTER X

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 30

1. The community may participate in the implementation of empowerment and protection of women.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be realized through:
   - a. actions to provide information and/or report violence against women to law enforcement or the authorities, or participate in handling acts of violence against women.
   - b. provision of communication, information and education media regarding empowerment and prevention of violence against women in families, communities, educational institutions and public spaces.

# CHAPTER XI

## FUNDING

#### Article 31

Funding for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the empowerment and protection of women shall be sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of South Bangka Regency and other legal and non-binding sources of funds.

# CHAPTER XII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 32

1. The Regent or OPD responsible for women's empowerment shall conduct guidance and supervision over the empowerment and protection of women;
2. The forms of guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) are as follows:
   - a. providing socialization to the community and business sector regarding policies on empowerment and protection of women;
   - b. providing books, leaflets, brochures regarding empowerment and protection of women, and other related issues and disseminating them to the community;
   - c. facilitating capacity building of institutions implementing empowerment and protection of women;
   - d. facilitating forums for women's participation and women's organizations;
   - e. facilitating rehabilitation and reintegration services for women victims of disasters and victims of violence;
   - f. coordinating the participation of the business sector and other institutions, including international assistance for empowerment and protection of women; and
   - g. providing awards to the community and business sector both individually and institutionally who have made efforts in empowerment and protection of women.
3. The form of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of monitoring and evaluation activities over the empowerment and protection of women.

# CHAPTER XIII

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 33

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, all implementing provisions related to the implementation of women's empowerment and protection, are declared to remain valid insofar as they do not conflict with the provisions in this Regional Regulation.
