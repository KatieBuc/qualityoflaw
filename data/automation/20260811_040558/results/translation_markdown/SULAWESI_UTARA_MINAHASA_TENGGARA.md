# REGENT OF SOUTHEAST MINAHASA, NORTH SULAWESI PROVINCE

REGIONAL REGULATION OF SOUTHEAST MINAHASA REGENCY NUMBER 8 OF 2016 CONCERNING THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD THE REGENT OF SOUTHEAST MINAHASA, Considering : a. that the State guarantees every person including women and children the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and the right to obtain security and freedom from all forms of violence and discrimination as guaranteed by the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

   - b. that all forms of acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their inherent nature and role without discrimination;
   - c. that violence against women and children in the Region continues to increase, so that protection efforts for women and children victims of violence are needed through institutional support and Regional Regulations that guarantee protection for women and children victims of violence;
   - d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

# CHAPTER I GENERAL PROVISIONS

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Southeast Minahasa Regency.
2. Regional Government is the administration of Government affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the Principle of Autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible Autonomy in the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
3. Regional Government is the Regional Head as an element of regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
4. Regent is the Regent of Southeast Minahasa.
5. Provincial Government is the Provincial Government of North Sulawesi.
6. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional People's Representative Institution of Southeast Minahasa Regency as an element of Regional Government administration.
7. Regional Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is a Regional Government Work Unit related to the implementation of child protection.
8. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the Regional financial plan that is discussed and approved jointly by the Regional Government and DPRD and stipulated by Regional Regulation.
9. Victims are women and children who experience suffering and/or hardship either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
10. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
11. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow and develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence, exploitation, discrimination, and neglect.
12. Special protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
13. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
14. Guardian is a person or body who in reality exercises parental authority as a parent towards a child.
15. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband, wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
16. Community is family, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.
17. Social Organization hereinafter referred to as Orsos is an institution/foundation/social association formed by the community either legally incorporated or not legally incorporated that functions as a means of community participation in implementing Social Welfare Efforts (UKS).
18. Child Protection Social House hereinafter abbreviated as RSPA is a child protection service unit for child victims of violence and child trafficking as a continuation of temporary shelter that functions to provide protection, recovery, rehabilitation, advocacy and referral.
19. Social Services for Children are physical, mental and social services aimed at helping children to grow and develop properly.
20. Social Services for Neglected Children are social services for children whose parents do not have the ability to meet the needs of children to grow and develop properly.
21. Child-friendly school is an educational institution that recognizes and respects children's rights to obtain education, health, opportunities to play and have fun, protection from violence and abuse, can express views freely, and participate in making decisions according to their capacity.
22. Social Work Profession is a profession based on a framework of science, values and technical skills and can be used as a vehicle in the implementation of social welfare efforts.
23. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a unified unit that organizes integrated services for witnesses and/or victims of criminal acts of human trafficking as well as women and children victims of violence.
24. Violence Victim Protection Forum hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK is a coordination forum for prevention, handling and empowerment of victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is networked and formed at the Regency, District and Village levels.

#### Article 2 The principles of protection for Victims are:

   - a. respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;
   - b. justice and gender equality;
   - c. non-discrimination;
   - d. best interests of victims; and
   - e. empowerment.

#### Article 3

The Objectives of Victim Protection are:

   - a. to prevent violence against women and children;
   - b. to provide services to Victims; and
   - c. to carry out empowerment of women victims of violence.

#### Article 4 The scope of protection for victims includes prevention efforts, services, empowerment and handling of victims of violence in the Region.

# CHAPTER II VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence against women and children include:

   - a. physical violence;
   - b. psychological violence;
   - c. sexual violence;
   - d. domestic neglect;
   - e. exploitation; and
   - f. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is that which results in pain, falling ill or serious injury.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of the ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering in a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c includes:

   - a. forced sexual intercourse committed against a person residing within the scope of the household and/or outside the scope of the household; and
   - b. forced sexual intercourse against a person within the scope of his/her household with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Household neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is:

   - a. the act of neglecting a person within the household scope whereas according to the applicable law for the person concerned or due to an agreement or contract he/she is obligated to provide livelihood, care or maintenance to that person.
   - b. the act of neglecting a person within the household scope, whereas according to the applicable law for him/her or due to an agreement or contract he/she is obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and
   - c. the act that results in economic dependence by means of limiting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is carried out due to:

   - a. acts that economically or sexually exploit with the intention of benefiting oneself or another person;
   - b. acts, with or without the victim’s consent, which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, subjugation, extortion, exploitation of the physical body, sexual organs, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues, or exploiting a person’s labor or abilities by another party to obtain benefits, both material and immaterial; and
   - c. all forms of exploitation of the victim’s sexual organs or other body organs to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:

   - a. threat of violence includes: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, image, symbol, or body movement, whether with or without using means that causes fear or restricts a person's fundamental freedom; and
   - b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER III RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 12

Every Victim obtains the following rights: a. the right to have their dignity respected as a human being; b. the right to restoration of health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim; c. the right to determine their own decisions; d. the right to obtain information; e. the right to confidentiality of their identity; f. the right to compensation; g. the right to social rehabilitation; h. the right to complaint handling; i. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and j. the right to assistance.

#### Article 13

Child victims of violence, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 12, also obtain special rights, as follows:

   - a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;
   - b. the right to basic services;
   - c. the right to equal protection;
   - d. the right to be free from various stigma; and
   - e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER IV OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One Regional Government

#### Article 14

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement efforts to protect victims of violence, in the form of:
   - a. establishing PPT;
   - b. establishing FPKK;
   - c. determining policies;
   - d. formulating program and activity planning;
   - e. providing facilities and infrastructure support; and
   - f. allocating budget.
2. In the implementation of the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall formulate a Regional Action Plan for the protection of victims of violence.

#### Article 15

Regional Governments provide services for victims of violence in the form of:

   - a. facilitating the establishment of PPT;
   - b. facilitating PPT facilities and infrastructure in accordance with regional financial capacity; and
   - c. facilitating FPKK as a forum for networking in handling victims of violence.

#### Article 16

Regional Governments have the obligation and responsibility to:

   - a. supervise the implementation of services for victims of violence, with minimum service standards; and
   - b. provide funds for victim protection through the APBD and/or other legitimate regional financial sources.

#### Article 17

In carrying out the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 14, Article 15 and Article 16, the Regent grants authority to agencies that have the main duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Second Part Society

#### Article 18

In addition to the obligations and responsibilities of Regional Government as referred to in Article 14, Article 15 and Article 16, the community has the following obligations and responsibilities:

   - a. prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children;
   - b. report if violence occurs;
   - c. protect victims; and
   - d. provide emergency assistance;

Third Part Family

#### Article 19

The family is obligated and responsible to implement prevention efforts and victim protection in the form of:

   - a. providing understanding about the prevention of domestic violence;
   - b. supervising every family member;
   - c. parents are prohibited from employing underage children in fulfilling the family's economic needs; and
   - d. parents must provide opportunities for school-age children to obtain adequate education.

# CHAPTER V

## VICTIM PROTECTION

#### Article 20

1. Efforts to prevent violence against women and children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the agency that has the main duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:
   - a. forming a network in efforts to prevent violence;
   - b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
   - c. establishing a violence prevention system; and
   - d. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 21

In addition to prevention efforts carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 20, prevention efforts must also be carried out by:

   - a. family and/or closest relatives;
   - b. community;
   - c. educational institutions; and
   - d. other community institutions.

# CHAPTER VI INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER

#### Article 22

1. The PPT is established by the Regional Government in the form of a Technical Implementation Unit (UPT).
2. The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is a functional work unit that has the duties, principal tasks, and functions of providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.
3. The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) has the duty to provide services and temporary protection in the form of a safe house for victims of violence.
4. In the event that the PPT does not have a safe house (shelter) as referred to in paragraph (3), then victims of violence shall be referred to a PPT that has a safe house.
5. The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be established by Regional Regulation.

#### Article 23

1. The organizational structure of PPT formed by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 22 paragraph (1) consists of:
   - a. General Chairperson:
   - b. Secretary:
   - c. Treasurer; and
   - d. Executive Chairperson who oversees the divisions.
2. The divisions as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, at minimum include:
   - a. complaint service division:
   - b. health service division:
   - c. social rehabilitation service division;
   - d. repatriation and social reintegration division; and
   - e. legal aid service division.
3. In carrying out its duties, PPT is responsible to the Regent through the Head of the agency that has the main duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 24

The complaint service sector as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter a has the following duties:

   - a. conduct interviews and observation of the victim's condition;
   - b. prepare recommendations for follow-up services;
   - c. conduct coordination and referral to follow-up services and related parties; and
   - d. carry out administration of the complaint process.

#### Article 25

The health service sector as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter b has the following duties:

   - a. conduct examination, treatment, and follow-up care for victims;
   - b. coordinate the implementation of health rehabilitation and medico-legal matters;
   - c. conduct supporting examinations and laboratory testing on evidence;
   - d. conduct consultations with specialist doctors or make referrals; and
   - e. prepare case reports.

#### Article 26

The field of social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter c has the duties of:

   - a. providing assistance during the case handling process; and
   - b. conducting counseling.

#### Article 27

The field of repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter d has the following duties:

   - a. coordinate with relevant agencies for the repatriation of victims;
   - b. prepare progress reports on the process of accompanying repatriation and social rehabilitation; and
   - c. conduct monitoring for at least three months after the victim is returned to their family.

#### Article 28

The field of legal aid services as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) letter e has the duties of:

   - a. accompanying and defending every legal service process; and
   - b. preparing reports on the progress of legal handling.

#### Article 29

1. The PPT established by the Regional Government must have at least three counselors.
2. The counselors as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
   - a. counselor in the field of law;
   - b. counselor in the field of health; and
   - c. counselor in the field of psychology.

#### Article 30

Further provisions regarding the development, mechanism, and networked working relationship of PPT shall be regulated by a Regent's Regulation.

First Part Services

#### Article 31

1. The provision of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by PPT.
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.

#### Article 32

The provision of services to victims is carried out with:

   - a. promptness;
   - b. safety and comfort;
   - c. a sense of empathy;
   - d. non-discrimination;
   - e. easy accessibility;
   - f. no charges; and
   - g. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 33

Forms of services for victims include:

   - a. complaint, consultation, and counseling services;
   - b. assistance services;
   - c. health services;
   - d. social rehabilitation services;
   - e. legal services; and
   - f. repatriation and social reintegration services.

#### Article 34

Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Article 33 letter a include:

   - a. identification or initial recording of victims; and
   - b. approval for actions to be carried out (informed consent).

#### Article 35

Accompaniment services as referred to in Article 33 letter b include:

   - a. accompanying victims during the examination process and health recovery;
   - b. accompanying victims during the medico-legal process;
   - c. accompanying victims during the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and court;
   - d. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;
   - e. protecting the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including coverage by mass media;
   - f. coordinating with other companions; and
   - g. providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 36

Health services as referred to in Article 33 letter c include:

   - a. first aid to victims;
   - b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics; and
   - c. referral to health services.

#### Article 37

Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 33 letter d constitute services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation against victims and to provide social support so that victims have self-confidence, strength, and independence in resolving their problems, by means of:

   - a. providing spiritual guidance to victims; and
   - b. psychological recovery of victims.

#### Article 38

Legal services as referred to in Article 33 letter e to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by:

   - a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;
   - b. accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and assisting victims to comprehensively describe the violence they experienced;
   - c. conducting coordination with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 39

1. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 33 letter f aim to return victims to their families and social environment.
2. Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with:
   - a. Regency/City Government within one Provincial territory; and
   - b. related government and non-government agencies and institutions.

Second

## Part Empowerment

### Paragraph 1 Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 40

Forms of empowerment of women victims of violence include:

   - a. job training;
   - b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and
   - c. capital assistance.

#### Article 41

Job training as referred to in Article 40 letter a includes:

   - a. skills training;
   - b. field work practice; and
   - c. apprenticeship.

#### Article 42

Productive economic enterprises and joint business groups as referred to in Article 40 letter b include:

   - a. entrepreneurial skills training;
   - b. facilitation of joint business group formation; and
   - c. assistance in business implementation.

#### Article 43

Capital assistance as referred to in Article 40 letter c includes:

   - a. assistance for work facilities and infrastructure; and
   - b. facilitation of working capital assistance.

### Paragraph 2 Fulfillment of the Rights of Child Victims of Violence

#### Article 44

1. Regional Government, PPT, and the community are obligated to fulfill the rights of child victims of violence.
2. The form of fulfillment of the rights of child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes the fulfillment of the basic rights of children in accordance with their needs.

Third Part Coordination of Victim Protection

#### Article 45

1. In an effort to provide and organize service handling for victims, the Regional Government establishes FPKK.
2. FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) aims to:
   - a. coordinate and synchronize the handling of PPT services;
   - b. maintain and develop networks and referral systems; and
   - c. collect, compile and present reports on violence.
3. The management and membership of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by Regent's Decree.
4. The membership of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (3) is grouped into the following roles:
   - a. health role;
   - b. psychology role;
   - c. legal role;
   - d. social role; and
   - e. economic role.
5. Further provisions regarding the establishment, main duties, and functions as well as membership of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent's Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 46

1. Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of minimum service standards at every PPT in the Region.
2. Guidance and Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:
   - a. coordination;
   - b. guidance;
   - c. education and training; and
   - d. Monitoring and evaluation.
3. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a covers aspects related to planning and implementation.
4. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b covers aspects related to planning, implementation, procedures, financing, quality, control and supervision.
5. Education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is conducted in accordance with laws and regulations.
6. Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d is conducted periodically every 6 months.

# CHAPTER VIII FUNDING

#### Article 47

Funding for victim protection activities carried out by the Regional Government shall be charged to the APBD and/or other legitimate and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 48

1. The community participates in the implementation of protection for Victims.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of:
   - a. establishing family partners at the kelurahan/village level by the community;
   - b. establishing women and children protection units within community organizations;
   - c. conducting independent socialization of women's and children's rights;
   - d. providing first aid to victims; and
   - e. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

# CHAPTER X SANCTIONS

#### Article 49

1. Every person who commits any form of violence as referred to in Article 5 shall be subject to criminal sanctions in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.
2. SKPD that do not implement the provisions of Article 14, Article 15 and Article 16, shall be subject to administrative sanctions.
3. Every person who directly witnesses violence as referred to in Article 5 and does not report it to the competent authorities shall be subject to criminal sanctions in accordance with the provisions of the applicable Laws and Regulations.

# CHAPTER XI CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 150 This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

##### CONCERNING

##### PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights; therefore, their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and natural condition, without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Southeast Minahasa Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture, or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

Thus far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts at the regional level; therefore, institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services, and empowerment for women and children victims of violence in Southeast Minahasa Regency.

##### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a What is meant by "respect and fulfillment of victims' rights" is the guarantee of the fulfillment of victims' basic rights.

Letter b What is meant by "justice" is a condition where every person, both men and women, are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to gain access, as well as welfare;

What is meant by "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

Letter c What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment toward victims without making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d What is meant by "the best interests of the victim" is all best actions concerning victims carried out by the Government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, therefore the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Letter e What is meant by "empowerment" is the strengthening of victims of violence to be able to work and operate independently after they have been restored and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
