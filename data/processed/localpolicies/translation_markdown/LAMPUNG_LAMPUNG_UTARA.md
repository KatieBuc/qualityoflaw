# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:  
1. Region is North Lampung Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the regional head as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.    
3. Regent is the Regent of North Lampung.  
4. Regional which is hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the DPRD of North Lampung Regency.  
5. Regional Apparatus Organization is an auxiliary element of the regional head and DPRD in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the region.  
6. Woman is a person who has the predicate as an adult girl, and or as a wife/mother, who by nature and law is recognized as a woman. 
7. Child is a person who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old, including a child who is still in the womb.  
8. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband, wife, or husband wife and their child, or father and his child or mother and her child, or blood relatives upward or downward up to the third degree.  
9. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.  
10. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.  
11. Violence against children is any action that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, sexually, economically, socially and psychologically.  
12. Acts of violence are women and children who experience misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.  
13. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, court, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.  
14. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
15. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.  
16. Cooperation is an activity or effort carried out by several people to achieve common goals.  
17. Forum for the Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence hereinafter abbreviated as FP2ATK is a forum established based on this Regional Regulation whose membership comes from relevant agencies and communities that care about the empowerment of women and the protection of children from acts of violence.  
18. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will, is or has occurred.  
19. Companion is a social worker who has professional competence in their field.  
20. Recovery of victims is all efforts to strengthen victims of domestic violence to be more empowered, both physically and psychologically.  
21. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TPA is a generic term that can be used by various institutions that provide services for victims of violence such as Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, Safe House, Child Social Protection House, Trauma Center Protection House, Women's Social Protection House, Shelter, or integrated service provider institutions for victims established by the Regional Government.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:  
a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of Victims of Acts of Violence;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination;  
d. the best interests of Victims of acts of violence; and  
e. Principles of the right to life, survival and development,

#### Article 3

The objectives of protecting women and children from acts of violence are to:  
a.prevent violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;  
b.eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;  
c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;  
d.provide services and Empowerment to women and children victims of violence, reporters and witnesses;  
e. facilitate and mediate domestic disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household; and  
f. Facilitate the restoration of the rights, dignity and personal values of victims.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 4

1. The Scope of this Regional Regulation consists of a. protection of women; and b. protection of children.  
2. Protection of women as referred to in Article 4 letter a includes:  
a. prevention of acts of violence;  
b. integrated services for victims of acts of violence; and  
c. empowerment of victims of acts of violence.  
3. Protection of children as referred to in Article 4 letter b includes:  
a. protection of children who are victims of acts of violence; and. 
b. special protection of children.

# CHAPTER IV

## IMPLEMENTATION

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 5

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of violence that occur in public or private life in the form of:  
a. physical violence;  
b.sexual violence;  
c. economic violence;  
d.social violence;  
e. psychological violence;  
f. domestic neglect;  
g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and  
h. threats of certain actions.

#### Article 6

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence as referred to in Article 5 is obliged to make efforts to:  
a.prevent the continuation of criminal acts;  
b.provide protection to victims of acts of violence;  
c. provide emergency assistance; and/or  
d. assist in the process of submitting an application for protection order.

## Part Two

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 7

1. The Regional Government carries out prevention as referred to in article 6 letter a by:  
a. socializing laws and regulations;  
b. providing counseling/guidance;  
c. providing education on the dangers of domestic violence;  
d. conducting seminars/workshops or the like;  
e. forming work networks in efforts to prevent violence;  
f. coordinating, integrating, synchronizing violence prevention based on partnership patterns;  
g. establishing a violence prevention system, mapping locations or areas prone to violence; and  
h. increasing public awareness in behaving in accordance with religious norms and customs.  
2. In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be carried out by:  
a.Religious institutions;  
b.family and/or closest relatives;  
c. customary leaders;  
d.customary institutions;  
e. community;  
f. educational institutions;  
g. health institutions;  
h. social community institutions; and  
i. business world and other institutions.

#### Article 8

1. Regional Governments are obliged to prepare prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (1) in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan;  
2. Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

#### Article 9

1. Regional Apparatus Organizations that administer governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment shall implement prevention as referred to in Article 8;  
2. Regional Apparatus Organizations as referred to in paragraph (1) in carrying out prevention may cooperate with vertical Agencies.

## Part Three

Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 10

1. Regional Governments shall implement integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b through activities:  
a. complaint services;  
b. health services;  
c. counseling;  
d. spiritual guidance;  
e. social rehabilitation services;  
f. legal aid and assistance services; and/or
g. repatriation and reintegration.  
2. Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:  
a. special service rooms;  
b. expert and professional personnel;  
c. service centers and safe houses; and/or  
d. other facilities and infrastructure necessary for victim recovery.  
3. The provision of services to victims of acts of violence shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A with other social institutions;  
4. P2TP2A and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner;  
5. Integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (4) shall be implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures;  
6. Provisions regarding Standard Operating Procedures as referred to in paragraph (5) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Four

Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 11

1. Empowerment of victims of acts of violence as referred to in article 5 letter c carried out by the Regional Government in the form of:  
a. job training;  
b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and  
c. capital assistance.  
2. Empowerment as referred to in paragraph 1 Apparatus in accordance with duties.

# CHAPTER V

## CHILD PROTECTION

## Part One

Protection of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 12

1. Protection of children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in article 5 letter a is the responsibility of the Regional Government. (2) Protection of child victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by:  
a. formulating policies for prevention, vulnerable risk reduction, victim handling and child data and information systems;  
b. organizing maintenance, care, and social rehabilitation of neglected children, both within and outside institutions;  
c. providing shelters, maintenance and care for neglected children; and  
d. organizing special protection for children.

#### Article 13

1. Provisions regarding prevention and services for acts of violence against women as referred to in Articles 7 through 12 shall apply mutatis mutandis to the protection of children from acts of violence;  
2. In addition to carrying out prevention as referred to in paragraph (1), also by increasing family capacity in child protection and child care skills.

## Part Two

Special Protection of Children

#### Article 14

1. Special protection of children shall be provided to:  
a. Children in emergency situations;  
b. Children in conflict with the law;  
c. Children from minority and isolated groups;  
d. Children who are exploited economically and/or sexually;  
e. Children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse;  
f. Children who are victims of pornography;  
g. Children with HIV/AIDS;  
h. Child victims of kidnapping, sale, and/or trafficking;  
i. Child victims of physical and/or psychological violence;  
j. Child victims of sexual crimes;  
k. Child victims of terrorism networks;  
l. Children with Disabilities;  
m. Child victims of abuse and neglect;  
n. Children with deviant social behavior; and  
o. victims of stigmatization from labeling related to the condition of their Parents.  
2. Regional governments in implementing handling as referred to in paragraph (1) in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## COOPERATION AND COORDINATION FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One General

#### Article 15

1. Regional governments shall carry out cooperation and coordination for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
2. Cooperation and coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus Organizations that administer governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

## Part Two

Institutional Framework for the Protection of Women and Children

#### Article 16

1. Regional Governments in carrying out cooperation and coordination for the Protection of Women and children from acts of violence, shall establish a Forum for the Care of Women and Children from Acts of Violence (FP2ATK):   
2. FP2ATK membership consists of elements:  
a. regional government;  
b. vertical agencies;  
c. educational institutions;  
d. health institutions;  
e. social institutions;  
f. labor institutions; and  
g. community organizations concerned with the protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
3. The Organizational Structure of FP2ATK as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of:  
a. policy formulation working group;  
b. socialization working group;  
c. service working group; and  
d. data, information, monitoring and evaluation working group.  
2. The establishment and term of office of FP2ATK members as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Regent Decree.

#### Article 17

FP2ATK has the duties to:  
a. provide input in policy formulation;  
b. coordinate with Regional Apparatus Organizations and other social institutions;  
c. socialize laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
d. collect and develop data and information related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
e. conduct review, monitoring, evaluation and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and  
f. provide reports, suggestions, input and considerations to the Regent.

#### Article 18

FP2ATK as referred to in article 16 paragraph (2) shall be assisted by a Secretariat at the Regional Apparatus Organization that handles women's empowerment and child protection affairs.

#### Article 19

Further provisions regarding the mechanism and working procedures of FP2ATK shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 20

1. Regional Governments in the context of services for victims of acts of violence, shall establish P2TP2A.  
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) has the duties to:  
a. provide services, including:  
1\. receive complaints/reports of violence against women and/or children, consultation, and counseling;
2\. receive and send case referrals from Regency/City Regional Governments or institutions established by Regency/City Regional Governments;  
3\. provide legal assistance and support;  
4\. health;  
5\. social rehabilitation;  
6\. legal services; and  
7\. repatriation and social reintegration.  
b. provide protection to victims of acts of violence, reporters and witnesses.  
3. The appointment of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent Decree.  
4. Regional Apparatus shall provide guidance to P2TP2A in carrying out duties as referred to in paragraph (2).

## Part Three

Cooperation

#### Article 21

1. In certain circumstances the Regional Government may cooperate on women and children from acts of violence with:  
a. other regional governments;  
b. police;  
b. advocates;  
c. health workers;  
d. social workers;  
e. volunteer companions;  
f. spiritual counselors;  
g. other law enforcers, to assist victims in the court hearing process;  
h. national commission on violence against women;  
i. Indonesian child protection commission or Child Protection Institution; and/or  
j. certain parties desired for the benefit of victims.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:  
a. socialization;  
b. provision of legal aid;  
c. health services;  
d. victim assistance;  
e. counseling;  
f. social rehabilitation;  
g. spiritual guidance; and/or  
h. repatriation and social reintegration.

## Part Four

Coordination

#### Article 22

1. The Regent coordinates the protection of women and children from acts of violence with:  
a. government;  
b. other Regency/regional government;  
c. vertical agencies;  
d. educational institutions; and  
e. community institutions.  
2. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. religious affairs;  
b. health affairs;  
b. education affairs;  
c. social affairs;  
d. legal affairs;  
e. labor affairs; and  
f. other affairs.

# CHAPTER VII

## COMMUNITY AND BUSINESS PARTICIPATION

#### Article 23

1. Every person may participate in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence; 
2. Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by:  
a. reporting acts of violence against women and/or children;  
b. providing first aid to victims of Acts of Violence;  
C. independently socializing the rights of women and children; and/or  
d. forming independent community social institutions.

#### Article 24

In the event that community social institutions implement protection for women and children from acts of violence they must coordinate with FP2ATK.

#### Article 25

1. The Regional Government encourages the business sector to participate in the protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
2. Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:a. socialization to employees within the company environment regarding the dangers of acts of violence against women and children; and  
b. assistance as a form of corporate social responsibility.

# CHAPTER VIII

## INFORMATION AND REPORTING SYSTEM

#### Article 26

1. The Regional Government prepares and compiles an information and reporting system for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
2. The Information and reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 27

1. The Regional Government implements guidance and supervision of FP2ATK and other social institutions.  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Apparatus Organization that implements women and child empowerment affairs.  
3. The results of the implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (2) must be reported to the regional government.

# CHAPTER X

## FINANCING

#### Article 28

Financing for the activities of Protection of Women and Children from acts of violence, may be sourced from;  
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and/or  
b. other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

1. In the event that FP2ATK as referred to in Article 18 has not been formed, P2TP2A its formation is guided by this Regional Regulation.
2. If FP2ATK has been formed it continues to carry out the duties and functions of services for women and children victims of acts of violence as long as it does not conflict with this Regional Regulation.

Implementing regulations of this Regional Regulation must be stipulated no later than 1 (one) year from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in North Lampung Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation to regulate protection efforts so that institutional and regulatory support is needed that can guarantee its implementation especially in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in Lampung Regency

Sufficiently clear.

Point a.

"Physical violence" means any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or causes death.

Point b.

"Sexual violence" means any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Point c.

"Economic violence" means an act that restricts a person from working inside or outside the home to earn money and goods, including allowing a working person to be exploited, while someone does not fulfill the economic needs of the family.

Point d.

"Social violence" means an act by a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life to a person or may cause physical damage or damage to another person's property.

Point e.

"Psychological violence" means an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Point f.

"Domestic Neglect" means:

1. acts that result in the failure to fulfill a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
2. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as appropriate; 3. acts of neglecting a person within the household scope, whereas according to the law applicable to him or her or due to an agreement or contract he or she is obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; 4. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Point g.

"Worker neglect" means an act by any person or legal entity that results in unfair treatment of female workers whereas according to the law applicable to him or her or due to an agreement or contract he or she is obliged to provide adequate protection from lawful acts against workers such as haphazard recruitment, unclear shelters and workplaces, arbitrary business owners.

Point h.

"Coercion or deprivation" of freedom means:

1. Coercion is any act or action of taking, bringing, inviting a child or woman by persuasion and or coercion to be married or to be employed illegally.
2. Deprivation of freedom is any act or action of taking, bringing, inviting, kidnapping a child or woman to be made a wife or employed by confining, detaining or imprisoning illegally in a room or certain place.

Point i.

"Threat of certain acts" means any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols or body movements, whether with or without using means
