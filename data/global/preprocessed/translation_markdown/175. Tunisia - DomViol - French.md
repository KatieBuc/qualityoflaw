# Organic Law No. 2017-58 of August 11, 2017, on the elimination of violence against

Organic Law No. 2017-58 of August 11, 2017, on the elimination of violence against women

In the name of the people, The Assembly of the Representatives of the People having adopted, The President of the Republic promulgates the organic law, the content of which follows:

## CHAPITRE PREMIER – General Provisions

#### Article premier – This law aims to establish measures capable of eliminating all forms

of violence against women based on discrimination between the sexes in order to ensure equality and respect for human dignity, by adopting a comprehensive approach based on combating the different forms of violence against women, through the prevention, prosecution and punishment of the perpetrators of such violence, and the protection and support of victims.

#### Art. 2 – This law concerns all forms of discrimination and violence suffered by women

based on discrimination between the sexes, whatever the perpetrators or the domain.

#### Art. 3 – For the purposes of this law, the following are understood as:

 Woman: any person of the female sex of any age,  Child: any person of male or female sex, within the meaning of the child protection code,  Violence against women: any physical, moral, sexual or economic harm against women, based on discrimination founded on sex and which results for them in injury, suffering or bodily, psychological, sexual or economic harm and also includes the threat of inflicting such harm, pressure or deprivation of rights and freedoms, whether in public or private life,  Physical violence: any harmful act or abuse affecting the integrity or physical security of the woman or her life, such as blows, kicks, injuries, pushing, disfigurement, burns, mutilation of certain body parts, confinement, torture and homicide,  Moral violence: any verbal assault, such as defamation, insult, coercion, threat, abandonment, deprivation of rights and freedoms, humiliation, neglect, mockery, belittlement and other acts or words affecting the human dignity of the woman or aimed at intimidating or dominating her,  Sexual violence: any act or word by which the perpetrator aims to subject the woman to his own sexual desires or to the sexual desires of another, by means of coercion, fraud, pressure or other means, likely to weaken or affect the will, and this, regardless of the perpetrator's relationship with the victim,  Political violence: any act or practice based on discrimination between the sexes by which the perpetrator aims to deprive the woman or prevent her from exercising any political, partisan, associative activity or any fundamental right or freedom,  Economic violence: any act or omission likely to exploit women or deprive them of economic resources, whatever their origin, such as deprivation of funds, salary or income, control of salaries or income and prohibition from working or coercion to work,  Discrimination against women: any distinction, exclusion or restriction which has the effect or purpose of impairing the recognition for women of human rights and freedoms, on the basis of complete and effective equality, in the civil, political, economic, social and cultural fields, or of compromising this recognition or the enjoyment or exercise of these rights by women, whatever the color, race, religion, thought, age, nationality, economic and social conditions, marital status, health status, language or disability.

Procedures and positive measures aimed at accelerating the establishment of equality between the two sexes are not considered discriminatory.

 Situation of vulnerability: the situation of fragility related to young or advanced age, serious illness, pregnancy, or mental or physical deficiency affecting the victim's capacity to resist the perpetrator.

The victim: the woman and the children who reside with her, who have suffered physical, moral, psychological, economic harm or have been deprived of the enjoyment of their freedoms and rights by acts, words or cases of abandonment constituting a violation of the laws in force.

#### Art. 4 – The State undertakes to support women victims of violence and the children who reside

with her in accordance with the following general principles:

 considering violence against women as a form of discrimination and a violation of human rights,  recognizing the status of victim to the woman and the children who reside with her, who have suffered violence,  respecting the victim's will to make the decision that matters to her,  respecting and guaranteeing the secrecy of the private life and personal data of the victim,  enabling equal opportunities for access to services in the different zones and regions,  providing legal advice to victims of violence and granting them legal aid,  ensuring the support of victims of violence in coordination with the competent services in order to provide them with the necessary social, health and psychological assistance and to facilitate their integration and accommodation.

#### Art. 5 – The State undertakes to develop national policies, strategic plans and joint

or sectoral programs and to take the regulations and measures necessary for their implementation with the aim of eliminating all forms of violence against women in the family space, social environment, educational, vocational training, health, cultural, sports and media environments.

## CHAPITRE II – Prevention and protection from violence against women

### Section première – Prevention of violence against women

#### Art. 6 – The State takes all necessary measures to eliminate all discriminatory practices against

women, particularly in terms of remuneration and social coverage in the different sectors, and to prohibit the economic exploitation of women and employing them in difficult, degrading working conditions or conditions detrimental to their health, safety and dignity.

#### Art. 7 – The ministries responsible for education, higher education, vocational

training, culture, health, youth, sports, children, women and religious affairs must take all necessary measures to prevent and combat violence against women in establishments under their jurisdiction, through:

 the development of didactic, educational and cultural programs aimed at eliminating and combating violence and discrimination against women, enshrining the principles of human rights and gender equality, as well as health and sexuality education,  the training of educators and personnel working in the field of education on equality, non-discrimination and combating violence in order to help them address issues of violence in the educational space,  the organization of specific training sessions in the areas of human rights, women's rights, their protection and combating violence against them, for the benefit of officials working in these fields,  taking all necessary measures to combat early school dropout, particularly among girls in all regions,  the creation of listening units, social action offices and health clubs in cooperation with interested parties,  the dissemination and consolidation of the culture of human rights education among young generations.

#### Art. 8 – The ministry responsible for health is called upon to establish integrated programs to combat

violence against women in medical and paramedical education, and to train personnel working in the field of health, at all levels, to detect, assess and prevent all forms of violence against women, as well as examination, treatment and follow-up in order to provide care for women and children residing with her, victims of violence.

It must also reserve spaces for welcoming victims of violence and provide them with psycho-health services.

#### Art. 9 – The ministry responsible for social affairs is called upon to provide adequate training to the various

social workers, including in particular social workers, in order to enable them to acquire the tools for intervention and care of women victims of violence.

The structures, care establishments, social establishments and specialized associations, under agreement with the ministry responsible for social affairs, undertake to integrate the fight against violence against women into field intervention programs, specific training programs, intervention plans, and related partnership programs, whether for awareness-raising or early detection, reporting, intervention or support for women victims of violence and children residing with them.

#### Art. 10 – The ministries of justice and interior develop integrated programs to combat

violence against women in education and training within establishments under their jurisdiction, in order to develop methods for handling complaints and cases related to violence against women.

The ministry of justice also takes all necessary measures to rehabilitate the perpetrator of the offense of violence against women and reintegrate him into the family and social environment.

#### Art. 11 – Public and private media raise awareness of the dangers of violence against

women and methods of combating and preventing such violence and ensure that personnel working in the media field are trained to address violence against women, in respect of professional ethics, human rights and equality.

Advertising and dissemination, by all media means and supports, of materials containing stereotyped images, scenes, words, or acts detrimental to the image of women, or materializing violence against them or diminishing its seriousness, are prohibited.

The independent high authority for audiovisual communication must take the measures and sanctions provided for by law to combat the violations mentioned in the preceding paragraph of this article.

#### Art. 12 – The ministry responsible for women's affairs ensures coordination between the various

actors mentioned in articles 6 to 11 of this law and the establishment of mechanisms for partnership, support and coordination with the relevant civil society organizations for the purpose of monitoring the implementation of what has been approved.

The ministry responsible for women's affairs prepares an annual report to this effect which is submitted to the presidency of the assembly of representatives of the people and to the Presidency of the government.

### Section 2 – On protection from violence against women

#### Art. 13 – Women victims of violence and children residing with her, benefit from the following rights

 appropriate legal protection to the nature of the violence exercised against her, so as to ensure her safety, physical and psychological integrity and dignity, as well as the administrative, security and judicial measures required to this effect, in respect of her specificities,  access to information and legal advice concerning the provisions governing judicial procedures and available services,  the benefit of legal aid,  fair compensation for victims of violence in the event of impossibility of execution against the person responsible for the act of violence. The State subrogates in this case the victims in the recovery of the amounts disbursed,  health and psychological follow-up, appropriate social support and where appropriate, the benefit of public and associative care, including listening,  immediate accommodation within the limits of available means.

#### Art. 14 – Any person, including one bound by professional secrecy, must alert the

competent authorities to any case of violence within the meaning of this law, as soon as they become aware of it, observe it or note its effects.

No one may be prosecuted before the courts for issuing an alert in good faith within the meaning of this law. It is prohibited for any person to disclose the identity of the person who issued the alert except with their consent or in the case where legal procedures require it.

## CHAPTER III – On offenses of violence against women

#### Art. 15

The provisions of articles 208, 226 ter, 227, 227 bis, 229, paragraph 2 of article 218, paragraph 3 of article 219, paragraph 2 of article 222 and paragraph 2 of article

228 of the penal code are repealed and replaced by the following:

#### Art. 208 (new) – The offender is punished by twenty (20) years' imprisonment, if the blows or

injuries inflicted voluntarily, but without intention to cause death, have nevertheless caused it. The penalty is life imprisonment, if:

 the victim is in a situation of fragility related to young or advanced age, serious illness, pregnancy, or mental or physical deficiency affecting their capacity to resist the perpetrator. to make their statement, to report the offense or to file a complaint, either because of their report,  there is premeditation of assault and battery,  the assault is preceded or committed with use or threat of use of a weapon,  the offense was committed by a group of persons acting as principal perpetrators or accomplices,  the assault is accompanied by an order or subject to a condition.

#### Art. 218 (paragraphe 2 nouveau) – The penalty is two (2) years of imprisonment and a fine of

two (2) thousand dinars, if:

 the offense committed is facilitated by the apparent vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator, to make their statement, to report the offense or to file a complaint, either by reason of their report, their complaint or their statement.

#### Art.219 (paragraphe 3 nouveau) – The penalty is increased to twelve (12) years of imprisonment regardless of

the rate of incapacity, if:

 the offense committed is facilitated by the apparent vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator, to make their statement, to report the offense or to file a complaint, either by reason of their report, accomplices,  the assault is preceded or committed with use or threat of use of a weapon,  the assault is accompanied by an order or subject to a condition.

#### Art. 222 (paragraphe 2 nouveau) – The penalty is doubled, if:

 the offense committed is facilitated by the apparent vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator, to make their statement, to report the offense or to file a complaint, either by reason of their report, accomplices,  the threat is accompanied by an order or subject to a condition even if this threat is only verbal.

#### Art. 226

ter (nouveau) – Is punished by two (2) years of imprisonment and a fine of five (5) thousand dinars whoever commits sexual harassment.

Is considered as sexual harassment any assault on another person by acts or gestures or words containing sexual connotations that violate their dignity or affect their modesty, with the aim of bringing them to submit to the sexual desires of the aggressor or those of another, or by exerting on them a dangerous pressure likely to weaken their ability to resist.

The penalty is doubled, if:

 the offense committed is facilitated by the apparent vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator, The limitation period for public prosecution concerning the offense of sexual harassment committed against a child runs from their majority.

#### Art. 227 (nouveau)

Is considered rape, any act of sexual penetration, whatever its nature, and the means used committed on a person of female or male sex without their consent the perpetrator of rape is punished by twenty years of imprisonment.

Consent is considered inexistent when the age of the victim is below sixteen (16) years of age.

Is punished by life imprisonment, the perpetrator of rape committed:

- 1) With violence, use or threat of use of a weapon or with the use of products, pills, narcotic or stupefying drugs.
- 2) On a child of female or male sex under the age of sixteen (16) years of age.
- 3) By incest on a child by:

 ascendants whatever the degree,  brothers and sisters,  the nephew or one of the descendants,  the father of one of the spouses, the spouse of the mother, the spouse of the father or the descendants of the other spouse,  persons one of whom is the spouse of the brother or the spouse of the sister,

4) by a person having authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by their functions,

5) by a group of persons acting as principal perpetrators or accomplices,

6) If the victim is in a situation of vulnerability due to their advanced age, or a serious illness, or a pregnancy, or a mental or physical deficiency, weakening their ability to resist the aggressor. The limitation period for public prosecution concerning the offense of rape committed on a child runs from their majority.

#### Art. 227

bis (Nouveau) – Is punished by five (5) years of imprisonment, whoever voluntarily subjects to the sexual act a child whether of female or male sex whose age is above sixteen (16) years of age, and below eighteen (18) years of age, with their consent.

The penalty is doubled in the following cases, if:

 the perpetrator is the teacher of the victim, or their servants or their doctors, accomplices,  the victim is in a situation of fragility related to advanced age, serious illness, pregnancy, or mental or physical deficiency affecting their ability to resist the perpetrator of the acts.

When the offense is committed by a child, the court applies the provisions of article 59 of the child protection code.

The limitation period for public prosecution concerning the offense of sexual act committed on a child with their consent runs from their majority.

#### Art. 228 (paragraphe 2 nouveau) – The penalty is doubled:

 if the victim is a child,  if the perpetrator is:

- an ascendant or a descendant whatever the degree,
- a brother or a sister,
- the nephew or one of their descendants,
- the son-in-law or the daughter-in-law or one of their descendants,
- the father of one of the spouses, the spouse of the mother, the spouse of the father or the descendants of the other spouse,
- persons one of whom is the spouse of the brother or the spouse of the sister,  if the perpetrator is a person having authority over the victim or abuses the authority conferred by their functions,  if the offense committed is facilitated by the apparent vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator,  if the offense is committed by a group of persons acting as principal perpetrators or accomplices.

The limitation period for public prosecution concerning the offense of indecent assault committed on a child runs from their majority.

#### Art. 16 – Are added to the penal code a paragraph 3 to article 221, a paragraph 2 to article 223, a

paragraph 2 to article 224 and article 224 bis as follows:

#### Art. 221 (paragraphe 3) – The same penalty is incurred by the perpetrator of the assault if it results in

disfigurement or partial or total mutilation of the female genital organ.

#### Art. 223 (paragraphe 2) – The penalty is doubled, if:

 the perpetrator is an ascendant or descendant of the victim, whatever the degree,  the offense committed is facilitated by the apparent vulnerability of the victim, or known by the perpetrator, to make their statement, to report the offense or to file a complaint, either by reason of their report, their complaint or their statement.

#### Art. 224 (paragraphe 2) – Incurs the same penalties provided in the preceding paragraph, whoever

habitually mistreats their spouse or a person in a situation of apparent vulnerability or known by the perpetrator, or having authority over the victim.

#### Art. 224 (bis)

Is punished by six (6) months to one year of imprisonment and a fine of one thousand dinars, whoever commits against their spouse repeated assault likely to violate the dignity of the victim, or their consideration or to alter their physical or psychological security by use of words, signals and acts.

The same penalty is incurred, if the acts are committed against one of the ex-spouses, fiancés or ex-fiancés and if the relationship between the perpetrator and the victim is the sole motive of assault.

#### Art. 17 – Is punished by a fine of five hundred (500) to one thousand dinars whoever voluntarily harasses a

woman in a public place, by any act, word or gesture likely to violate their dignity, their consideration or to affect their modesty.

#### Art. 18

Is punished by a fine of one thousand dinars whoever commits political violence. The penalty is increased to six (6) months of imprisonment in case of recidivism.

#### Art. 19 – Is punished by a fine of two (2) thousand dinars the perpetrator of violence or economic discrimination

based on sex, if it results from their act:

 the deprivation of the woman of her economic resources or the use of her income,  wage discrimination for work of equal value,  discrimination in the professional career including promotion and advancement in functions.

The penalty is doubled in case of recidivism.

#### Art. 20 – Is punished by three (3) to six (6) months of imprisonment and a fine of two (2) to five (5) thousand

dinars, whoever voluntarily and directly or indirectly hires children as domestic workers.

Incurs the same penalty provided by the preceding paragraph, whoever acts as intermediary to hire children as domestic workers.

The penalty is doubled in case of recidivism.

#### Art. 21 – Is punished by one month to two (2) years of imprisonment and a penalty of one thousand to five (5) thousand dinars

or one of these two penalties, whoever voluntarily exercises discrimination within the meaning of this law if it results from their act:

 the deprivation or restriction for the victim to benefit from their rights or to obtain a good or service,  the prohibition for the victim to exercise their activities in a normal manner,  the refusal to hire the victim, their dismissal or the sanction thereof.

## CHAPITRE IV – Procedures, services and institutions

### Section première – Procedures

#### Art. 22 – The Public Prosecutor assigns one or more of their deputies to receive

complaints relating to violence against women and to monitor the investigations relating thereto.

#### Art. 23 – Are reserved for magistrates specialized in violence against women cases,

separate spaces within the courts of first instance, at the level of the public prosecution, the examining magistracy and family justice.

#### Art. 24 – Is created within each national security and national guard station, in all

governorates, a specialized unit to investigate violence against women offenses in accordance with the provisions of this law. It must include women among its members. A special numbered register relating to these offenses is made available to this specialized unit.

#### Art. 25 – As soon as notified of a case of flagrant offense of violence against women, the agents of the

specialized unit must proceed without delay to the scene to conduct investigations after having informed the Public Prosecutor.

Is punished by one to six (6) months of imprisonment, the agent of the specialized unit investigating violence against women offenses, who voluntarily exercises pressure, or any type of coercion, on the victim in order to induce them to renounce their rights, to modify their statement or to retract.

#### Art. 26 – The specialized unit must mandatorily inform the victim of all their rights provided by

this law, including the claim of their right to protection before the family judge. The specialized unit may, upon authorization of the Public Prosecutor, and before the protection order is issued, take one of the following protection measures:

 the transfer of the victim and the children residing with them, in case of necessity, to secure locations, in coordination with the competent structures and the child protection delegate,  the transfer of the victim to receive first aid when they have suffered bodily harm,  removing the defendant from the home or prohibiting them from approaching the victim or being near their home or workplace, in case of danger threatening the victim or their children residing with them.

The protection procedures continue to take effect until the protection order is issued.

#### Art. 27 – The specialized unit establishes every six months a report on the minutes relating to violence

against women, of which it has been seized and their follow-up. Said report is submitted to the administrative and judicial supervisory authority and to the national observatory for the elimination of violence against women provided for in Article 40 of this law.

#### Art. 28 – The confrontation with the defendant may only take place with the consent of the victim of

the violence offense, unless the confrontation is the only means that guarantees them the right to be exonerated.

The victim of sexual offenses may request to be heard in the presence of a psychologist or a social worker.

#### Art. 29 – The child victim of sexual offenses must be heard in the presence of a psychologist or

a social worker. The observations of the latter are recorded in a report established for this purpose. The child victim of sexual offenses may not be heard more than once. Their hearing must be recorded in a manner to preserve voice and image.

The confrontation with the defendant in sexual offenses is prohibited when the victim is a child.

### Section 2 – The application for protection

#### Art. 30 – The family judge is seized with the examination of the application for protection following a written request

from:

 the victim in person or their representative,  the public prosecution with the victim's consent,  the child protection delegate if the victim is a child or in case of the existence of a child. The family judge may seize themselves ex officio with the examination of the granting of protection.

The seizure of the family judge with the application for protection does not preclude the victim's right to bring an action on the merits before the competent civil and criminal courts.

#### Art. 31

The application for protection includes a statement of reasons, the measures requested, their duration, and where applicable, the amount of the maintenance allowance and the housing allowance. The necessary supporting documents are attached to the application for protection.

#### Art. 32 – The family judge rules on the application for protection in accordance with the procedures provided for

in summary proceedings before the cantonal court.

The family judge receives the declarations of the parties and hears any person whose hearing is deemed useful. They may be assisted in their work by agents of the social action public services.

#### Art. 33 – The family judge may take, by virtue of the protection order, one of the following measures:

 prohibit the defendant from contacting the victim or the children residing with her, at the family home, at the workplace or place of study, at the shelter or in any place where they may be,  in case of danger threatening the victim or her children residing with her, order the defendant to leave the family home where the victim and her children reside, while allowing him/her to recover his/her personal belongings, by means of a report drawn up for this purpose, at his/her expense, by a bailiff notary,  order the defendant not to harm the private property of the victim or her children covered by the protection order, or the common property, and not to dispose of it,  designate the housing of the victim and the children residing with her, and where appropriate, order the defendant to pay housing support, unless the competent court has been seized of the matter or a judgment has been rendered to that effect,  allow the victim in person or her representative, in case of departure from the family home, to recover her personal belongings and the necessary belongings of her children, by means of a report drawn up for this purpose by a bailiff notary, at the expense of the defendant,  remove the defendant from custody or guardianship and establish procedures for visitation rights while prioritizing the best interest of the child,  determine the amount of alimony for the wife who is a victim of violence and the children, and where appropriate, the contribution of each spouse to the alimony, unless the competent court has been seized with the examination of alimony or a judgment has been rendered to that effect.

#### Art. 34

The order must provide for the duration of protection, which must not exceed, in all cases, six

(6) months.

The family judge may extend the duration of the protection order issued by him or by the court of appeal, only once for the same duration, by means of a reasoned decision subject to the same procedures provided for in articles 30, 31 and 32 of this law.

#### Art. 35

The decisions of the family judge are subject to appeal. They are not subject to cassation.

#### Art. 36

The public prosecutor executes the protection orders and those of their extension.

#### Art. 37 – Shall be punished by a maximum of six (6) months' imprisonment and a fine of one thousand dinars or

both penalties anyone who resists or prevents the execution of orders and means of protection.

#### Art. 38 – Shall be punished by one year's imprisonment and a fine of five thousand dinars anyone who

willfully violates the orders and means of protection after their execution.

### Section 3 – Services and institutions

#### Art. 39 – Persons responsible for the protection of women from violence, including judicial police

officers, child protection delegates, health personnel, women's affairs, family, social affairs, education and others, must:

 respond without delay to any request for assistance and protection, presented directly by the victim,  respond immediately to any request for assistance or protection within the meaning of article 14 of this law,  give priority to alerts concerning the commission of violence threatening the physical, sexual and psychological safety of the woman and the children residing with her,  ensure listening and examination on the occasion of receiving complaints, by meeting the parties and witnesses, including children, in separate rooms while ensuring their integrity,  inform the complainant of all her rights,  intervene, in case of loss of housing, due to violence, to ensure accommodation in protection centers for women victims of violence.

#### Art. 40 – A national observatory for the fight against violence against women is created, subject to

the supervision of the ministry responsible for women.

The observatory is responsible, in particular, for the following missions:

 detect cases of violence against women, in light of the reports and information collected, while archiving these cases and their incidences in a database created for this purpose,  ensure the monitoring of the implementation of legislation and policies, assess their effectiveness and efficiency in eliminating violence against women, and publish reports to this effect by proposing the necessary reforms,  carry out the necessary scientific and field research concerning violence against women in order to assess the required interventions and address the forms of violence as provided for in this law,  contribute to the development of national strategies, common and sectoral practical measures, and define the guiding principles for the elimination of violence against women in accordance with this law,  ensure cooperation and coordination with civil society organizations, constitutional bodies and other public organizations concerned with monitoring and controlling respect for human rights, with a view to developing and consolidating the system of rights and freedoms,  issue opinions on training and learning programs, empower all actors in the field of violence against women, propose appropriate mechanisms to develop them and ensure their monitoring, The observatory prepares an annual report on its activity, including in particular statistics on violence against women, the conditions of reception, accommodation, monitoring, support and integration of victims of violence, the follow-up to protection orders, related actions and judgments, proposals and recommendations to develop national mechanisms for the elimination of violence against women.

The report is submitted to the President of the Republic, the President of the Assembly of the Representatives of the People and the Head of Government during the first quarter of each year. Said report is made public.

The observatory may also issue press releases on its activities and programs. The administrative and financial organization of the observatory, as well as its operating procedures are established by governmental decree.

#### Art. 41

The Ministry of Women receives from all concerned ministries and public bodies, each in its respective area, reports and data relating to violence against women which it transmits to the national observatory for the prevention of violence against women.
