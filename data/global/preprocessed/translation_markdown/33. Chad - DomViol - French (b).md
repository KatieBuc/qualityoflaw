# Chad

Chad

Law No. 001/PR/2017 of May 8, 2017 on the Penal Code

## Book 1 ‐ General Provisions

### Title 1 ‐ Criminal Law

#### Chapter 1 ‐ General Principles Art.1.‐

Criminal law is to be strictly interpreted.

###### Art.2.‐ Criminal courts are not competent to interpret

administrative acts, whether regulatory or individual, and to assess their legality even if the solution of the criminal proceedings before them depends on such examination. They are required to stay the proceedings pending the decision of the administrative court on the preliminary question raised.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Application of Criminal Law in Time

###### Art.3.‐ Acts not definitively judged before the express or tacit repeal of the criminal law

cease to be punishable.

###### Art.4.‐ Any new and more lenient criminal provision applies immediately to

offenses not definitively judged on the day it enters into force.

If the new criminal provision is more rigorous, offenses committed before it enters into force continue to be judged in accordance with the more lenient provisions of the old law.

###### Art.5.‐ Any penalty or security measure immediately ceases to be executed:

• a) imposed for an act which, under the new law, no longer constitutes an offense;

• b) or which is abolished subsequent to the conviction under the new law.

#### Chapter 3 ‐ Application of Criminal Law in Space

###### Art.6.

‐ Criminal law applies to any act constituting an offense committed on national territory. National territory includes territorial lake waters, airspace as well as vessels and aircraft registered in the Republic of Chad.

However, no crew member of a foreign vessel or aircraft who commits an offense on board against another crew member, even within territorial lake waters or airspace, may be tried by the courts of the Republic unless the assistance of local authority has been requested or public order has been compromised.

###### Art.7.‐ Criminal law applies:

• a) to any offense of which one of the constituent elements was realized on its territory;

• b) to offenses against State security, counterfeiting of the State seal or national currency in circulation, even committed abroad.

However, no foreigner may be tried by the courts of the Republic for the offenses referred to in paragraph b above, unless he has been arrested on the territory of the Republic or has been extradited there.

###### Art.8.‐ Also subject to criminal law are:

• a) acts constituting complicity and attempt of these offenses carried out on the territory of the Republic with a view to committing an offense abroad, if this offense is also punished by foreign law;

• b) the same acts carried out abroad with a view to committing an offense on the territory of the Republic.

###### Art.9.‐ Criminal law applies to acts committed abroad by a Chadian or by a resident,

provided that these acts are punishable by the law of the place where they were committed and are qualified as crimes or délits by the laws of the Republic.

A citizen or resident guilty of a délit committed against a private individual may, however, only be tried by the courts of the Republic pursuant to this article upon prosecution by the Public Prosecutor seized of a complaint or official denunciation to the Government of the Republic by the Government of the country where the act was committed.

In the case provided for in this article, the penalty incurred may not exceed that fixed by foreign law.

###### Art.10.

‐ Criminal law applies to piracy, trafficking in persons, trade in persons, recruitment of children into armed forces and groups, trafficking in narcotics, trafficking in toxic waste, money laundering, cybercrime, corruption and related offenses, committed even outside the territory of the Republic.

Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph I above, a foreigner may only be tried on the territory of the Republic for the acts referred to in this article and committed abroad if he has been arrested on the territory of the Republic and has not been extradited.

In this case, only the Public Prosecutor may set the public prosecution in motion.

###### Art.11.‐ Any person found on Chadian territory and having committed abroad one of the following offenses may be prosecuted and tried on the territory of the Republic:

- a) war crimes provided for in articles 296 to 299 of this Code;
- b) crimes against humanity provided for in articles 292 to 295 of this Code;
- c) the crime of genocide provided for in articles 303 to 306 of this Code.

#### Chapter 4 ‐ Foreign Law and Criminal Sentences

###### Art.12.‐ Subject to articles 8 and 9 above, foreign criminal law has no effect before the

courts of the Republic.

###### Art.13.‐ Criminal sentences pronounced by foreign courts produce effect on

national territory only if the act is qualified as a crime or common law délit by national criminal law and subject to the regularity of the sentence, its final character and its conformity with public order.

Foreign criminal sentences:

- a) are taken into consideration for recidivism, for the granting or revocation of suspension of sentence, for the revocation of conditional release, for rehabilitation and amnesty;
- b) preclude any new prosecution for the same acts on national territory.

###### Art.14.‐ When the sentences referred to and established under the conditions provided for in the preceding article

have been pronounced against citizens or residents and have not been executed in another country, they are enforceable on national territory, unless the convicted person has benefited from conditional release, has been pardoned or amnestied, or his sentence has become time-barred. It is for the court seized under the conditions provided for in the preceding article to order the execution of the sentence.

### Title 2 ‐ Offenses, Penalties and Security Measures

#### Chapter 1 ‐ Preliminary Provisions

###### Art.15.‐ Offenses are defined by law.

Penalties and security measures are fixed by law and are only imposed for legally provided offenses.

###### Art.16.‐ Offenses are classified as crimes, délits and contraventions according to the principal penalties

that sanction them.

Offenses punishable by life imprisonment or by a custodial sentence for a fixed term whose minimum exceeds ten years are qualified as crimes.

Offenses punishable by a custodial sentence or by a fine when the custodial sentence incurred exceeds ten days but does not exceed ten years or when the minimum fine exceeds 25,000 FCFA are qualified as délits.

Offenses punishable by imprisonment not exceeding ten days or by a fine not exceeding 25,000 FCFA are qualified as contraventions.

The nature of an offense is not modified:

- a) when, as a result of the admission of an excuse or extenuating circumstances, the sentence imposed is that relating to another category of offenses;
- b) in the cases of aggravation provided for in articles 72 and 73 below.

###### Art.17.

‐ Penalties are principal, alternative, accessory or complementary.

Accessory penalties are attached to penalties pronounced for crimes and apply automatically.

Complementary penalties are left to the judge's discretion in cases provided by law.

###### Art.18.‐ The principal penalties are:

• a) life imprisonment or imprisonment for a fixed term;

• b) fine.

###### Art.19.‐ The alternative penalties are:

• a) the day-fine penalty;

• b) community service work.

###### Art.20.‐ The security measures are:

• a) confinement in a health facility;

• b) special confiscation.

#### Chapitre 2 ‐ Of the penalty of imprisonment

###### Art.21.‐ Imprisonment is a penalty involving deprivation of liberty for life or for a fixed term which is served

in a penitentiary establishment and under conditions set by texts relating to the penitentiary regime.

###### Art.22.‐ If the convicted person is not in a state of provisional detention or if a warrant of arrest or committal

is not issued against him at the hearing under the conditions provided by the rules of criminal procedure, the penalty involving deprivation of liberty may only be executed when the conviction has become final.

If a woman sentenced to a penalty involving deprivation of liberty is pregnant or has just given birth, she shall serve her sentence only three months after her delivery.

A pregnant woman or one who has just given birth, placed in provisional detention, continues until the expiration of the period referred to in the preceding paragraph to benefit from the provisional detention regime.

A husband and wife convicted, even for different offences, to a sentence of imprisonment of less than one year and not detained on the day of judgment may, upon their request, not serve their sentence simultaneously if, proving a certain common domicile, they have in their care and under their custody a child under the age of eighteen years.

###### Art.23.

‐ The sentence of imprisonment in days is calculated by twenty-four hours. The sentence of one month is thirty days.

The sentence expressed in months and years is calculated from date to date.

Subject to the provisions of Article 30 below, the starting point of the sentence is fixed:

- a) on the day the convicted person is incarcerated in execution of the conviction;
- b) in case of merger of sentences, on the day of the first incarceration in execution of one of the merged convictions.

In case of escape, the period during which the convicted person was a fugitive is excluded from the calculation of the duration of the sentence.

###### Art.24.‐ The duration of provisional detention is fully deducted from the penalty involving deprivation of

liberty pronounced.

When there has been provisional detention and the sentence pronounced is a fine, the court seized may, by way of compensation, exempt the convicted person from payment of all or part thereof.

#### Chapitre 3 ‐ Of alternative penalties

###### Art.25.‐ When a court pronounces against a person found guilty of an offence, a

firm sentence of imprisonment equal to or less than one year, it may substitute for said sentence a day-fine penalty consisting, for the convicted person, in paying a sum of money whose total amount results from the fixing, in the decision, of a daily contribution for a certain number of days.

The amount of each day-fine is determined taking into account the resources and obligations of the convicted person.

The number of day-fines is fixed having regard to the circumstances of the commission of the offence. It may not exceed one hundred and eighty days.

The day-fine penalty may not be pronounced against the accused if the latter expresses his refusal or is not present at the hearing.

The right to refuse is reminded to the person concerned by the President of the court, after the declaration of guilt and the fixing of the firm sentence.

The answer to the question asked pursuant to this paragraph is recorded in the minutes of the hearing.

In case of non-execution of the day-fine penalty, the convicted person is summoned by the Public Prosecutor or cited to appear at his request before the President of the court having pronounced the conviction, for the purpose of explaining the non-execution.

If the non-execution is due, according to the assessment of the President of the court concerned, to legitimate reasons not attributable to the convicted person, the latter is, by order, referred back to the effective execution of the penalty initially pronounced.

If the reasons invoked by the convicted person to explain the non-execution of the sentence are not recognized as founded, the President of the court concerned issues against him, by order, a committal warrant for the execution of the sentence of imprisonment initially pronounced as indicated in paragraph 1 above.

If the convicted person does not comply with the summons or citation, the President of the court concerned issues against him, by order, a warrant of arrest for the execution of the sentence of imprisonment initially pronounced as indicated in paragraph 1 above.

The orders made pursuant to paragraphs 8, 9 and 10 above are not subject to appeal.

###### Art.26.‐ When a court pronounces against a person found guilty of an offence a

firm sentence of imprisonment equal to or less than one year, it may, as a substitute, order that the convicted person shall perform, for a duration between 120 hours, that is 15 days, and 240 hours, that is 30 days, unpaid community service work for the benefit of a legal person under public law or an association authorized to implement community service work. The community service work penalty may not be pronounced against the accused if the latter suffers from an obvious physical or mental incapacity to perform such a measure, expresses his refusal or is not present at the hearing. The right to refuse is reminded to the person concerned, after the declaration of guilt and the fixing of the firm sentence.

The accused's answer to the question asked pursuant to this paragraph is recorded in the minutes of the hearing.

The community service work penalty is chosen from a list of community service work drawn up by deliberation of the competent body of the decentralized territorial authority within the jurisdiction of the competent court.

The list of community service work is filed at the registry of the courts with jurisdiction in criminal matters at the beginning of each judicial year and posted at the doors of the Courthouses and the buildings that are seats of the decentralized territorial authorities and community houses.

In case of non-execution of the community service work penalty, the convicted person is summoned by the Public Prosecutor or cited to appear at his request before the President of the court having pronounced the conviction, for the purpose of explaining the non-execution found.

If the non-execution is due, according to the assessment of the President of the court, to valid reasons not attributable to the convicted person, the latter is, by order, referred back to the effective execution of the community service work.

If the reasons invoked by the convicted person to explain the non-execution of the sentence are not recognized as valid, the President of the court issues against him, by order, a committal warrant for the execution of the sentence of imprisonment initially pronounced as indicated in paragraph 1 above.

If the convicted person does not comply with the summons or citation, the President of the court issues against him by order, a warrant of arrest for the execution of the sentence of imprisonment initially pronounced as indicated in paragraph 1 above.

The orders made pursuant to paragraphs 5, 6 and 7 above are not subject to appeal.

#### Chapter 4 ‐ Accessory penalties

###### Art.27.

‐ Civic degradation is an accessory penalty to any conviction for a crime. Civic degradation includes:

- a) removal from office and exclusion from all functions, employment or public offices;
- b) deprivation of all civic and political rights and of the right to wear a decoration;
- c) incapacity to be an assessor in a court, expert, judicial representative, witness in any act and to testify in court, except to give simple information;
- d) incapacity to be a guardian, deputy guardian, curator or legal adviser, except for one's own children and to be part of a family council
- e) deprivation of the right to carry weapons, to keep a school, to teach and to be employed in an educational establishment as a professor, teacher or supervisor;
- f) deprivation of the right to direct or manage a capital company and to be part of the board of directors of such a company.

#### Chapter 5 ‐ Complementary penalties

###### Art.28.‐ The complementary penalties are:

• residence ban:

- prohibition from the territory;
- prohibition of certain rights;
- confiscation of certain property;
- closure of an establishment;
- prohibition from exercising a profession or activity;
- publication of the conviction.

Except in cases where the pronouncement of a complementary penalty is mandatory for the judge, every decision must include a statement of reasons justifying the pronouncement of a complementary penalty.

Complementary penalties may be cumulated, except in case of incompatibility. Difficulties of execution are submitted by the Public Prosecutor to the jurisdiction that pronounced the last conviction.

###### Art.29.‐ The residence ban consists of the prohibition imposed on the convicted person from appearing in

certain places for a certain duration which cannot exceed five years for an offence and ten years for a crime.

The residence ban includes surveillance and assistance measures. Foreigners may be banned from the entire national territory.

The pronouncement of the residence ban penalty is left to the discretion of the judge, unless otherwise ordered by law. However, it cannot be pronounced as accessory to penalties of less than one year of imprisonment.

The list of prohibited places is fixed by the judgment.

The residence ban runs from the date of final or conditional release. In case of revocation of conditional release, the prohibition is suspended during the time of the new incarceration.

If no penalty of imprisonment has been pronounced or if the convicted person has benefited from a suspended sentence or if the penalty of imprisonment has expired on the day of the conviction, the prohibition runs from the day the decision became final.

Violations of the residence ban decision are punishable by fifteen days to one year of imprisonment and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 F.

###### Art.30.‐ Prohibition from the territory is that imposed on the foreign convicted person from entering and staying

in the territory of the Republic after the execution of his sentence and his expulsion. Prohibition from the national territory is pronounced in cases where it appears necessary, by reason of the nature of the offence and the personality of the convicted person, to prevent him from appearing on the national territory, when it appears that his presence involves risks of commission of new acts.

When it has a limited duration, prohibition from the territory cannot exceed five years for offences and ten years for crimes.

###### Art.31.‐ In cases of conviction for crimes specifically provided for by law, the

confiscation for the benefit of the State of all present property of the convicted person, of whatever nature, movable or immovable, or only of a portion of such property, may be pronounced, subject to rights acquired by third parties, in particular to victims of the offence. The alienation of confiscated property shall be pursued by the domains administration in the forms prescribed for the sale of State property.

Subject to the rights of third parties in good faith, the courts may pronounce in all cases the confiscation either of the corpus delicti when the property belongs to the convicted person, or of things produced by the offence, or of things that served or were intended to commit it, or of things that were given or offered to incite to commit it.

###### Art.32.‐ In case of conviction for a crime, the jurisdiction may pronounce the dissolution of the

legal person when it is established that the latter is the principal author of the offence.

###### Art.33.‐ In cases where the competent jurisdiction may order the closure of a

commercial or industrial establishment or of a professional premises having served to commit an offence, this measure entails the prohibition for the convicted person or for the third party to whom the convicted person has sold, transferred or rented the establishment or the professional premises from exercising in the same premises the same commerce, the same industry or the same profession.

###### Art.34.‐ In cases which they shall appreciate and in those specifically provided for by law, the courts

may order that their decision be published at the expense of the convicted person, for a duration of two months maximum in case of conviction for a crime or offence and of fifteen days maximum, in case of a contravention.

The decision fixes the modalities of this publication and the maximum amount of expenses to be incurred. In case of suppression, concealment or total or partial tearing down of posters ordered by the judgment of conviction, the full execution of the provisions of the judgment relating to posting shall be proceeded with again.

When the suppression, concealment or total or partial tearing down has been carried out voluntarily by the convicted person, or at his instigation or by his orders, it shall entail against him, the application of a penalty of imprisonment of one month to six months and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA.

###### Art.35.‐ Persons convicted for the same crime or for the same offence or for

connected crimes and offences subject to the same prosecution are jointly and severally liable for restitutions, damages and costs.

In case of concurrence of the fine with restitutions and damages, if the property of the convicted person is insufficient, the latter convictions shall obtain preference. The execution of convictions to fine and costs may then be pursued by means of imprisonment for debt.

###### Art.36.

‐ In case of conviction for a crime or offence, the competent jurisdiction may order, according to the nature of the offence committed by the legal person and without exclusion of other accessory penalties, either its exclusion from public procurement definitively or for a determined duration, or also prohibit it for a determined duration from making public appeal for savings or from issuing checks other than those which allow the withdrawal of funds by the enterprise itself from the drawee or those which are certified, or further prohibit it from using payment cards for a determined duration.

In matters of contraventions, in addition to the measures indicated in paragraph 1 above, the court may also pronounce the confiscation of property and other measures determined by law.

#### Chapter 6 ‐ Security Measures

###### Art.37.‐ In case of acquittal or discharge for insanity of the perpetrator of a crime or an offense

punishable by imprisonment of at least two years, the court shall order in its decision the internment of the person concerned in a special health facility, when the liberty of the person is recognized as dangerous to public order.

The medical situation of the person whose internment has been ordered shall be reviewed at least once a year.

Only the court that ordered the internment may terminate it, after opinion from the competent medical authority certifying that the liberty of the internee no longer presents any danger to public order.

###### Art.38.‐ When an alcoholic, drug addict or person suffering from a mental pathology is

convicted of a crime or offense punishable by imprisonment of at least two years, the court may order their internment in a special health facility. Internment for the reasons indicated in this article may not exceed two years for the treatment of an alcoholic or drug addict and five years for the treatment of a mentally ill person.

The court that ordered the internment may shorten the initially fixed period, after opinion from the competent medical authority certifying that the liberty of the internee no longer presents any danger to public order.

###### Art.39.‐ Objects and substances whose manufacture, possession, sale or use are illicit

shall be confiscated and destroyed even if they do not belong to the convicted person or if the prosecution has not resulted in a conviction.

The confiscation and destruction of these objects and substances shall be ordered by the conviction decision.

#### Chapter 7 ‐ Non-accumulation and Order of Execution of Sentences

###### Art.40.‐ In the case where an individual is subject to the same prosecution for several crimes or offenses

or related contraventions, only the most severe sentence incurred shall be imposed.

In the case where an individual is subject to several convictions for crimes or offenses resulting from various prosecutions, the merger of principal sentences may be ordered by the court hearing the latest prosecution. In case of accumulation of sentences, the total of the sentences imposed may not exceed the legal maximum of the sentence incurred for the most severely punished offense.

When a principal sentence has been subject to a commutation or gracious remission, the sentence resulting from the commutation or remission shall be taken into account for the accumulation of sentences. The rule of non-accumulation of sentences does not apply to two convictions where the first had become final before the commission of the acts that motivated the second.

In matters of contraventions, sentences are always accumulated, unless the court decides otherwise.

In case of commission of several offenses, sentences other than principal sentences as well as security measures are accumulated, unless the court decides otherwise.

###### Art.41.‐ Custodial sentences are executed in chronological order of the

notification of detention orders to the convicted person.

Accessory sentences are immediately applicable.

The internment of the acquitted mentally ill person is immediately applicable.

The internment of the convicted person is applicable upon expiration of the principal sentence or its suspension, in case of alcoholism, drug addiction or mental pathology.

If, during the execution of one of the measures indicated in paragraphs 3 and 4 above, the person concerned incurs, for another crime or offense, a custodial sentence, the execution of the security measure is suspended and the new sentence is served first.

#### Chapter 8 ‐ Procedures for the Execution of Imprisonment Sentences Section 1 ‐ Simple

Suspension

###### Art.42.‐ In case of conviction for a crime or offense to an imprisonment sentence of less than

or equal to five years or to a fine and if the convicted person has not been subject to a prior conviction to imprisonment, the court may order, unless otherwise provided by law and by reasoned decision, that execution of the principal sentence shall be suspended.

If during the period of five years from the day the judgment or decision became final, the convicted person has committed no crime or offense followed by conviction to imprisonment or to a more serious sentence, the conviction shall be deemed null and void.

In the contrary case, the first sentence shall be executed first without it being merged with the second and recidivism sentences shall be incurred.

The suspension does not extend to payment of trial costs and damages.

After pronouncing the conviction decision, the President of the court warns the convicted person of the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of this article and mention of this warning is made in the court record.

##### Section 2 ‐ Suspension with Probation

###### Art.43.‐ Suspension with probation may be pronounced under the same conditions as

simple suspension when the sentence is equal to or greater than six months imprisonment. Suspension with probation may also be pronounced with regard to persons previously convicted to an imprisonment sentence with simple suspension or to a firm imprisonment sentence of less than six months.

The probation period may not be less than three years nor more than five years. Subject to the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 of this article, Article 42 above is applicable to suspension with probation.

###### Art.44.

‐ The probation regime includes general obligations and, where applicable, special obligations, of supervision and assistance.

Compliance by the convicted person with the obligations attached to probation is monitored by a magistrate designated for this purpose within the court and who is assisted by probation delegates who are volunteer or remunerated.

The designation of the probation delegate is made by the magistrate in charge of monitoring, who may modify it.

The magistrate of the place of residence of the convicted person, in charge of monitoring, may at any time and by reasoned decision, suspend all or part of the special obligations or modify them without however being able to aggravate them.

###### Art.45.‐ The following general obligations are imposed by operation of law on the convicted person placed

under the probation regime:

- a) respond to summonses from the judge in charge of monitoring the application of measures taken or from the probation delegate;
- b) receive visits from the probation delegate and provide them with information or documents of a nature to allow monitoring of their means of subsistence;
- c) inform or justify to the judge in charge of monitoring the application of measures taken or to the probation delegate the reasons for changes of employment or residence;
- d) inform the probation delegate of any absence exceeding fifteen days and notify them of their return;
- e) obtain prior authorization from the judge in charge of monitoring the application of measures taken before any travel exceeding fifteen days or those planned abroad.

###### Art.46.‐ In addition to the general obligations imposed by the preceding article, the judgment or ruling may

impose on the convicted person on probation the observance of all or part of the following special obligations:

- a) establish their residence in one or more specified places;
- b) not appear in certain specified places, except with special and temporary authorization;
- c) engage in a professional activity, attend education or receive professional training;
- d) submit to control, treatment or care measures, even under hospitalization, particularly for detoxification purposes;
- e) contribute to family expenses or regularly pay maintenance allowances;
- f) repair the damage caused by the offense;
- g) not drive certain vehicles determined by the applicable license categories;
- h) not frequent certain places such as drinking establishments, racetracks, gaming houses;
- i) not place bets;
- j) abstain from any excess of alcoholic beverages;
- k) not associate with certain convicted persons, particularly co-authors or accomplices in the offense
- l) refrain from receiving or accommodating certain persons at their home.

###### Art.47.‐ The probation officer is responsible for the following missions:

• a) ensure that the convicted person respects the general and special obligations to which they are subject;

• b) encourage and support the efforts of the convicted person with a view to their social rehabilitation, particularly with regard to their family and professional reintegration;

• c) provide the convicted person with all moral assistance and, in the event that they need material assistance, refer to the designated magistrate so that aid may be provided by any welfare or social assistance organization;

• d) keep the designated magistrate regularly informed of the performance of their mission and refer to them in case of difficulties.

###### Art.48.‐ In the event that during the probation period the convicted person does not respect one of the obligations

general or special to which they are subject, the court that pronounced the conviction may order the execution of the suspended sentence.

This execution does not entail the revocation of a simple suspension previously granted.

If the suspension with probation has not been revoked pursuant to this article or Article 42 paragraph 3, the sentence is deemed null and void.

##### Section 3 ‐ Suspension with the obligation to perform community service

###### Art.49.‐ The court may, under the conditions and according to the procedures provided for in Articles 42

and 43 above, provide that the person convicted of a suspended prison sentence shall perform, during working days, for a duration ranging from 120 hours, or fifteen days, to 240 hours, or thirty days, community service for the benefit of a legal person under public law or an association authorized to implement community service.

Suspension, coupled with the obligation to perform community service, may not be ordered when the defendant suffers from an obvious physical or mental incapacity to perform such a measure, refuses it or is not present at the hearing.

The right to refuse is reminded to the interested party, after the declaration of guilt.

The response to the question asked pursuant to the above paragraph is recorded in the hearing minutes.

The procedures for implementing the obligation to perform community service are governed by the provisions of Article 26 of this Code.

###### Art.50.‐ During the period set by the court to perform community service, the

convicted person must, in addition to the obligation to perform the prescribed work, satisfy the following control measures:

- a) respond to summons from the judge responsible for monitoring the application of the measures taken or the social worker designated for this purpose;
- b) submit to the medical examination prior to the execution of the sentence, the purpose of which is to determine whether they are not suffering from a condition dangerous to other workers and to ensure that they are medically fit for the work to which it is envisaged to assign them;
- c) justify the reasons for their changes of employment or residence that impede the execution of the community service according to the established procedures;
- d) obtain prior authorization from the judge responsible for monitoring obligations for any travel that would impede the execution of the community service according to the established procedures;
- e) receive visits from the social worker designated to monitor the execution of the sentence;
- f) satisfy the particular obligations that the court has specifically imposed on them.

###### Art.51.‐ Suspension with the obligation to perform community service is governed by the

same rules as those provided for suspension with probation.

##### Section 4 ‐ Semi-detention

###### Art.52.‐ When the trial court pronounces a sentence equal to or less than one year

of imprisonment, it may decide with regard to the convicted person who justifies either the exercise of a professional activity, or their attendance at education or professional training or a traineeship or temporary employment with a view to their social integration, or their essential participation in the life of their family, or the necessity to undergo medical treatment, that the prison sentence shall be executed under the semi-detention regime.

###### Art.53.‐ The convicted person admitted to the benefit of semi-detention is required to return to

the penitentiary institution according to the procedures determined by the president of the Tribunal de Grande Instance of the place of execution of the sentence, based on the time necessary for the activity, education, professional training, traineeship, participation in family life or treatment for which they were admitted to the semi-detention regime.

###### Art.54.‐ The convicted person admitted to the benefit of semi-detention is required to remain in

the penitentiary institution where they are serving their sentence during the days when, for any reason whatsoever, their external obligations are interrupted.

In case of non-compliance with the procedures according to which they are required to return to the penitentiary institution, the convicted person is incarcerated until the end of their sentence by decision of the Public Prosecutor.

##### Section 5 ‐ Division of the correctional prison sentence

###### Art.55.‐ In case of conviction for an offense to a firm prison sentence equal to or

less than one year, the court may, for serious medical, family, professional or social reasons, decide that the imprisonment shall be, during a period not exceeding three years, executed in fractions, none of which may be less than three months.

The duration of the fractions is determined by order of the President of the Tribunal de Grande Instance of the place of execution of the sentence, the Public Prosecutor having been heard.

##### Section 6 ‐ Pardon and the effect of death

###### Art.56.‐ Pardon is the commutation or partial or total remission, conditional or not, of

sentences, security measures and obligations of probation.

###### Art.57.‐ The death of the convicted person does not prevent the execution against their property, of pecuniary

convictions nor the closure of the establishment, nor the confiscation.

### Title 3 ‐ Criminal Responsibility of Natural Persons

#### Chapter 1 ‐ General Provisions

###### Art.58.‐ A penalty may only be imposed on a criminally responsible person.

Any person who commits acts characterizing the constituent elements of an offense with the intention that such acts result in the commission of the offense is criminally responsible.

Unless otherwise provided by law, a failure to act does not entail criminal responsibility.

Unless otherwise provided by law, criminal responsibility can only exist if the conditions of paragraph 2 above are met.

However, in matters of petty offenses, criminal responsibility exists even when the act or omission is not intentional or the consequence was not intended.

###### Art.59.‐ Ignorance of the law and the motive invoked by the accused or defendant do not affect criminal responsibility.

###### Art.60.‐ An act ordered or authorized by law and performed in accordance with the law does not constitute an offense.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Causes That Eliminate or Mitigate the Criminal Responsibility of Natural Persons

###### Art.61.‐ A person who acted under the influence of a force or constraint that they could not resist is not criminally responsible.

###### Art.62.‐ Criminal responsibility cannot result from the act of a person who was suffering, at the time of the acts, from a psychological or neuropsychological disorder that completely abolished their judgment or control over their acts.

In the event that the psychological or neuropsychological disorder did not completely abolish the person's judgment or control over their acts, it constitutes a mitigating excuse of responsibility.

###### Art.63.‐ Involuntary intoxication is treated as mental illness.

###### Art.6.4.‐ A minor under thirteen years of age is not criminally responsible.

A minor from thirteen to fifteen years of age, if declared criminally responsible, may only be subject to one of the special measures provided by law, including:

- a) awarding custody to their parents, guardian, custodian or any other trustworthy person;
- b) supervised release as organized by the rules of criminal procedure;
- c) placement in a professional training or care institution;
- d) placement in a specialized institution.

A minor over fifteen years of age and under eighteen years of age declared criminally responsible benefits from the mitigating excuse of responsibility.

The age to be taken into consideration is that which the perpetrator had at the date of commission.

###### Art.65.‐ Criminal responsibility cannot result from the act of an individual subject to an imminent and otherwise unavoidable threat of death or serious injury as provided for in this Code.

However, if the act is an offense punishable by life imprisonment or if it resulted in the death or injuries referred to above, the perpetrator only benefits from the mitigating excuse. This article is not applicable to anyone who voluntarily exposed themselves to the risk of such threats.

###### Art.66.‐ The mitigating excuse is applicable:

• a) to a minor under eighteen years of age who acted under the constraint of their parents, persons having custody or customary responsibility;

• b) to employees, staff, civil servants who acted under the constraint of their hierarchical superiors.

###### Art.67.‐ Criminal responsibility cannot result from an act ordered by legitimate authority, unless the order is manifestly illegal.

###### Art.68.‐ Criminal responsibility cannot result from an act required by the immediate necessity of protecting one's life or physical integrity or that of others against an unlawful attack, provided that the defense is proportionate to the severity of the attack.

There is always just proportion between homicide and the unlawful attack that gives reason to fear death, rape or serious injury as provided for in this Code.

###### Art.69.‐ Any perpetrator of an offense immediately provoked by the unlawful act of another against themselves or, in their presence, against their spouse, their descendant or ancestor, their brother or sister, their employer or employee, the minor or incapacitated person in their custody, benefits from the mitigating excuse, if there is no disproportion between the provocation and the reaction. Homicide and injury are also excusable if they were:

- a) provoked by serious blows or violence against persons;
- b) committed by one of the spouses on their spouse or accomplice caught in the act of adultery.

###### Art.70.‐ Independently of the self-defense provided for in article 68 of this Code, criminal responsibility cannot result from harm done to property for the purpose of averting from oneself or others or from property belonging to oneself or others, a serious, imminent and otherwise unavoidable danger, provided that there is no disproportion between the harm to be averted and the measure taken to prevent it.

###### Art.71.‐ When the law provides for a mitigating excuse, the penalties are reduced as follows:

• a) if life imprisonment is incurred, the penalty is reduced to a custodial sentence of two to ten years;

• b) in the case of a felony not punishable by life imprisonment, the penalty is reduced to a custodial sentence of one to five years;

• c) in the case of a misdemeanor, the maximum custodial sentences and fines are reduced by half, and the minimum is that of article 76 paragraph 1 of this Code.

In the case of a combination of mitigating excuses and mitigating circumstances, the minimum penalty is that provided for in article 76 paragraph 1 of this Code.

#### Chapter 3 ‐ Causes That Aggravate the Criminal Responsibility of Natural Persons

Recidivism

###### Art.72.‐ Is a recidivist:

• a) anyone who, after having been convicted of a felony or misdemeanor of the same nature, commits a new offense classified as a felony or misdemeanor within a period that begins to run from the date the conviction became final and that expires five years after the execution of the sentence imposed or its prescription;

• b) anyone who, after having been convicted of a petty offense, commits a new petty offense within a period that begins to run from the date the conviction became final and that expires twelve months after the execution of the sentence imposed or its prescription.

In case of recidivism, the maximum fixed-term sentence and that of the fine provided for is doubled. The status of civil servant

###### Art.73.‐ The status of civil servant, public officer or agent in charge of a public service is an aggravating circumstance of criminal responsibility against those among them who, except in cases where the law specifically governs the penalties incurred for crimes and misdemeanors committed by them, have made themselves guilty of other crimes or misdemeanors that they were responsible for preventing or suppressing.

In the case of commission of an offense in the circumstances indicated in paragraph 1 above, the maximum penalty incurred is doubled.

#### Chapter 4 ‐ Mitigating Circumstances and Choice of Penalty

###### Art.74.‐ Mitigating circumstances may be admitted, by reasoned decision, in favor of a convicted person, except in matters where the law formally excludes them.

###### Art.75.‐ The penalties provided by law against the accused person or persons found guilty

of a crime, and in whose favor mitigating circumstances have been retained, may be reduced to ten years or five years of imprisonment if the crime is punishable by life imprisonment, to three years of deprivation of liberty if the crime is punishable by a penalty of at least fifteen years of imprisonment and to one year of deprivation of liberty in other cases.

If, in application of the provisions of paragraph 1 above, a penalty equal to or less than ten years of deprivation of liberty is pronounced, the court may impose on the convicted person a fine which cannot exceed 2,000,000 FCFA.

###### Art.76.‐ When mitigating circumstances are retained in case of misdemeanor or

petty offense, the court may reduce the penalty of deprivation of liberty incurred to the legal minimum. The same applies regarding the fine.

When the law provides only a penalty of deprivation of liberty, the court may substitute therefor a fine the maximum of which is 1,000,000 FCFA in case of misdemeanor and 25,000 FCFA in case of petty offense.

###### Art.77.‐ The penalty or measure pronounced within the limits fixed or authorized by law is

based on the circumstances of the offense, the danger it presents to public order, the personality of the convicted person and their possibilities of reclassification as well as the practical possibilities of execution.

#### Chapitre 5 ‐ On attempt

###### Art.78.‐ Constitutes an attempt and is considered as the crime or misdemeanor itself, any act

tending toward the execution of a crime or misdemeanor and demonstrating unequivocally the irrevocable intention of its perpetrator to commit the offense, if it has not been voluntarily suspended or if it failed to have its effect only by circumstances independent of the will of its perpetrator.

The attempt is punishable even when the sought purpose could not be achieved by reason of a factual circumstance unknown to the perpetrator.

The preparatory act, by itself alone, does not constitute an attempt in the penal sense of the term.

#### Chapitre 6 ‐ On co-action and complicity

###### Art.79.‐ Is a co-perpetrator whoever participates with another and in agreement with them in the commission

of an offense.

###### Art.80.‐ Shall be punished as accomplices to an action qualified as a crime or misdemeanor:

• a) those who by gifts, promises, threats, abuse of authority or power, machination or culpable artifice, shall have provoked this action or given instructions to commit it;

• b) those who shall have procured weapons, instruments or any other means which shall have served the action, knowing that they were to serve therein;

• c) those who shall have, with knowledge, aided or assisted the perpetrator or perpetrators of the offense in the acts which shall have prepared or facilitated it or in those which shall have consummated it;

• d) those who, knowing the criminal conduct of malefactors exercising brigandage or violence against the security of the State, public peace, persons or property, habitually provide them with lodging, place of retreat or meeting or information useful to their activity;

• e) those who, having knowledge of a crime or misdemeanor attempted or consummated, shall not have warned the administrative or judicial authorities, when it was still possible to prevent or limit the effects thereof, or further, to prevent the guilty parties from committing new crimes or misdemeanors;

• f) those who being able to prevent by their immediate action, without risk to themselves or to third parties, an act qualified as a crime or misdemeanor against the bodily integrity of the person, voluntarily refrain from doing so.

The accomplices to a crime or misdemeanor shall be punished as the very perpetrators of that crime or misdemeanor, except in cases where the law provides otherwise.

Whoever shall have provoked to a crime or misdemeanor shall be punished with the penalties provided for the offense even though it shall not have been committed due to the voluntary abstention of the person or persons who were to commit it or of a circumstance or quality which would be personal to them.

### Titre 4 ‐ On the criminal liability of legal persons

#### Chapitre 1 ‐ On general provisions

###### Art.81.‐ Legal persons, excluding the State and its subdivisions, are

criminally liable for offenses committed by their organs or representatives, when the latter have acted in the exercise of or on the occasion of their functions.

###### Art.82.‐ The criminal liability of legal persons does not exclude that of natural

persons who are perpetrators or accomplices to the same acts.

###### Art.83.‐ The conditions for the criminal liability of legal persons are, apart from

those defined in Article 81 above, provided by the incrimination provisions of this

Code.

#### Chapitre 2 ‐ On causes which aggravate the criminal liability of legal persons

###### Art.84.‐ The provisions on recidivism provided in Article 72 of this Code are

applicable to legal persons.

#### Chapitre 3 ‐ On the choice of penalty for legal persons

###### Art.85.‐ The penalty or measure pronounced within the limits fixed or authorized by law is

based on the circumstances of the offense, the danger that it presents to public order, the financial capacities of the legal person and, more broadly, the practical possibilities of execution.

## Livre 2 ‐ On crimes and misdemeanors against the Nation, the State and public peace

### Titre 1 ‐ On attacks on the integrity of the State

#### Chapitre 1 ‐ On Treason and espionage

##### Section 1 ‐ On Treason

###### Art.86.‐ Is guilty of the crime of treason and punished with the penalty of life imprisonment any

Chadian, any member of the military in the service of Chad, who:

- a) bears arms against Chad;
- b) maintains intelligence with a foreign power, with a view to engaging it to undertake hostilities against Chad, or provides it with the means, either by facilitating the penetration of foreign forces on the territory, or by undermining the loyalty of the armies, or in any other manner;
- c) delivers to a foreign power or to its agents, either troops, or cities, places, works, posts, stores, arsenals, materials, ammunition, buildings or navigational devices, belonging to Chad or assigned to its defense;
- d) with a view to harming the national defense, destroys or deteriorates a building, a navigational device, material, a construction or any installation whatsoever, or who, with the same purpose, introduces therein, either before or after their completion, defects of a nature to damage them or to provoke an accident;
- e) provokes members of the military to enter the service of a foreign power, facilitates the means thereof or makes enlistments for a power at war with Chad;
- f) maintains intelligence with a foreign power or with its agents with a view to favoring the undertakings of that power against Chad;
- g) hinders the movement of military material;
- h) participates in an undertaking of demoralization of the army or of the nation having as its object to harm the national defense.

###### Art.87.‐ Is also guilty of the crime of treason and punished with the penalty of life

imprisonment any Chadian who:

- a) delivers to a foreign power or to agents, in whatever form and by whatever means, information, object, document or process which must be kept secret in the interest of the national defense;
- b) secures, by whatever means, the possession of such information, object, document or process with a view to delivering it to a foreign power or to its agents;
- c) destroys or allows to be destroyed such information, object, document or process with a view to favoring a foreign power.

##### Section 2 ‐ Espionage

###### Art.88.‐ Is guilty of the crime of espionage and punished by life imprisonment any

foreigner who commits one of the acts referred to in Article 86 b, c, d, f, g and h and in Article 87 above.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Offences against national defence not qualified as treason or espionage

##### Section 1 ‐ Offences against national defence

###### Art.89.‐ Is guilty of the crime of offence against national defence and punished by life

imprisonment anyone who, with the intention of delivering them to a foreign power, gathers information, objects, documents or processes whose collection and exploitation are likely to harm national defence.

###### Art.90.‐ Is guilty of the crime of offence against national defence and punished by imprisonment

of twenty to thirty years any guardian, any custodian by function or by capacity of information, an object, document or process which must be kept secret in the interest of national defence or whose knowledge could lead to the discovery of a national defence secret, who without intention of treason or espionage:

- a) destroys, removes, allows to be destroyed or removed, reproduces or allows to be reproduced it;
- b) brings or allows to be brought to the knowledge of an unqualified person or the public.

The penalty is imprisonment of five to ten years if the guardian or custodian acted through clumsiness, imprudence, inattention, negligence or non-observance of regulations.

##### Section 2 ‐ Other offences against national defence

###### Art.91.‐ Is guilty of offence against national defence and punished by imprisonment of twenty to

thirty years anyone who, outside the cases referred to in Article 90 above, without intention of treason or espionage:

- a) secures, being without capacity, possession of information, an object, document or process which must be kept secret in the interest of national defence or whose knowledge could lead to the discovery of a national defence secret;
- b) destroys, removes, allows to be destroyed or reproduces or allows to be reproduced such information, object, document or process;
- c) brings or allows to be brought to the knowledge of an unqualified person or the public such information, object, document or process, or extends its disclosure.

###### Art.92.‐ Is guilty of offence against national defence and punished by imprisonment of twenty to

thirty years anyone who, without prior authorization from the competent authority, delivers or communicates to a person acting on behalf of a foreign power or enterprise, either an invention concerning national defence, or information, studies or manufacturing processes relating to an invention of this kind or to an industrial application concerning national defence.

Is also guilty of offence against national defence and punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article, anyone who possesses, carries, manufactures, imports firearms and/or opens a weapons depot without prior legal authorization.

###### Art.93.‐ Is guilty of offence against national defence and punished by imprisonment of one to five

years anyone who, without intention of treason or espionage, brings to the knowledge of an unqualified person or the public military information not made public by the competent authority and whose disclosure is manifestly likely to harm national defence. Are punished by the same penalties, private individuals who possess military vehicles. The complementary penalties provided for in Article 28 of this Code are furthermore applicable. The provisions of this Code and the Code of Criminal Procedure relating to suspended sentence and conditional release are not applicable to the perpetrators of the acts referred to above.

##### Section 3 ‐ Irregular presence in certain places

###### Art.94.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of three to eight years anyone who:

• a) enters under a disguise or a false name or by concealing their capacity or nationality, into a place, a work, post or arsenal, into the works, camps, bivouacs or cantonments of an army, into a building or vessel of war or merchant navigation employed for national defence, into an armed military vehicle, into an establishment or site concerning national defence;

• b) even without disguising themselves, or without concealing their name, capacity or nationality, organizes in a covert manner any means of correspondence or remote transmission likely to harm national defence;

• c) overflies Chadian territory by means of a foreign aircraft without being authorized by a diplomatic convention or permission from the Chadian authority;

• d) in a prohibited zone fixed by the military authority, executes, without its authorization, drawings, photographs, surveys or photographic operations inside or around places, works, posts or military establishments or concerning national defence;

• e) stays, in defiance of a prohibition enacted by decree, within a determined radius around fortified works or military establishments;

• f) communicates to an unqualified person or makes public information relating either to measures taken to discover and arrest the perpetrators and accomplices of crimes or offences defined in sections 1 and 2 of this chapter, or to the progress of prosecutions and investigation, or to debates before trial courts.

In peacetime, the perpetrators of the offences provided for in paragraphs c, d, e and f of paragraph 1 above are punished by imprisonment of one to five years.

##### Section 4 ‐ Intelligence with the enemy

###### Art.95.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years anyone who:

• a) by hostile acts not approved by the Government, exposes Chad to a declaration of war;

• b) by acts not approved by the Government, exposes Chadians to reprisals;

• c) maintains with the agents of a foreign power intelligence likely to harm the military or diplomatic situation of Chad or its economic interests.

###### Art.96.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of two to six years anyone who, in time of war:

• a) maintains, without authorization from the Government, correspondence or relations with subjects or agents of an enemy power;

• b) conducts, directly or through an intermediary, acts of commerce with the agents of an enemy power, in defiance of the prohibitions enacted.

###### Art.97.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years anyone who, in peacetime, with a view to

harming national defence, obstructs the circulation of military equipment or, by any means whatsoever, provokes, facilitates or organizes a violent or concerted action having as its purpose or result these obstructions.

##### Section 5 ‐ Other actions harmful to national defence

###### Art.98.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years anyone who, in peacetime, participates

knowingly in an enterprise of demoralization of the army having as its object to harm national defence.

###### Art.99.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years anyone who, in peacetime, enlists in

Chadian territory soldiers on behalf of a foreign power.

###### Art.100.

‐ In all cases provided for in this section, the guilty parties may, in addition, be sentenced to a fine of 100,000 to 10,000,000 FCFA.

#### Chapter 3 ‐ Attacks, conspiracies and other offences against the constitutional order, the integrity and

security of the national territory

##### Section 1 ‐ Offenses against the constitutional order

###### Art.101.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of twenty to thirty years anyone who commits an act constituting

an attack whose purpose is either to destroy or change the constitutional regime, or to incite citizens or inhabitants to take up arms against the authority of the State or against one another, or to undermine the integrity of the national territory.

###### Art.102.‐ Constitutes a conspiracy, the concerted and determined resolution between two or more

persons to commit one of the acts constituting a crime mentioned in the preceding article.

Conspiracy is punishable by a term of imprisonment of eight to fifteen years.

###### Art.103.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of twenty to thirty years anyone who, outside the cases provided for in

Articles 101 and 102 above, undertakes, by any means whatsoever, to undermine the integrity of the national territory.

###### Art.104.‐ In all cases provided for in Articles 101 to 103 above, the confiscation provided for in

Article 28 of this Code is ordered.

##### Section 2 ‐ Organizations against the constitutional order

###### Art.105.‐ Are punished by the penalty of life imprisonment:

• a) those who have raised or caused to be raised armed troops, recruited or enlisted, caused to be recruited or enlisted soldiers or have provided them with arms or ammunition, without order or authorization of the legitimate authority;

• b) those who, without right or legitimate reason, have assumed any military command, those who, against the order of the Government, have retained such command and commanders who have kept their army or their troops assembled after their discharge or separation has been ordered.

###### Art.106.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of twenty to thirty years any person who, being able to

deploy public force, has requisitioned or ordered, caused to be requisitioned or ordered the action or use thereof to prevent the execution of laws on military recruitment or mobilization.

##### Section 3 ‐ Offenses against State institutions

###### Art.107.‐ Is liable to the penalty of life imprisonment the attack whose purpose is to disturb

public peace by the massacre or devastation of any part of the territory. In the case provided for in this article, the confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this Code is mandatorily ordered.

###### Art.108.‐ Is punished by the penalty of life imprisonment anyone who attacks the life of the head

of State, a member of the Government or a member of the National Assembly.

##### Section 4 ‐ Armed bands

###### Art.109.‐ Is punished by the penalty of life imprisonment anyone who, with a view to disturbing the State by

one of the crimes provided for in Articles 102 and 108 above or for any other cause or even by attacking or resisting the public force acting against the perpetrators of these crimes, places himself at the head of armed bands or exercises any function or command therein.

Is punished by the penalty of life imprisonment anyone who leads the association, raises or causes to be raised, organizes or causes to be organized bands or knowingly and willfully provides or procures them with subsidies, weapons, ammunition and instruments of crime or sends supplies or, in any other manner, engages in collusion with those responsible for or commanders of the bands.

In the case provided for in this article, the confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this Code is ordered.

###### Art.110.

‐ Are punished by imprisonment of five to ten years individuals forming part of bands, without exercising any command or function therein.

###### Art.111.‐ In cases where the crimes referred to in this section do not provide for

confiscation, a fine of 50,000 to 5,000,000 FCFA may be ordered.

#### Chapter 4 ‐ Other offenses against the State

##### Section 1 ‐ Insurrectional movements

###### Art.112.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of five to ten years anyone who participates in an

insurrectional movement.

Participation in an armed insurrection and the actual use of weapons are punished by life imprisonment.

###### Art.113.‐ Are punished by a penalty of life imprisonment those who lead or organize an

insurrectional movement or who, knowingly and willfully, provide or procure for it weapons, ammunition and instruments of crime or supplies or who, in any manner whatsoever, engage in collusion with the leaders or commanders of this movement.

###### Art.114.‐ In the cases provided for in Articles 110 and 111 above, a fine of 50,000 to

5,000,000 FCFA may be ordered in addition to the penalties indicated in the said articles.

##### Section 2 ‐ Mercenarism

###### Art.115.‐ Is considered as a mercenary, any person who:

• a) is specially recruited in the country or abroad to fight in an armed conflict;

• b) in fact takes a direct part in hostilities;

• c) takes part in hostilities with a view to obtaining a personal advantage and to whom is effectively promised by a party to the conflict or on its behalf, material remuneration

• d) is neither a national of a party to the conflict, nor a resident of the territory controlled by a party to the conflict;

• e) is not a member of the armed forces of a party to the conflict;

• f) has not been sent by a State other than a party to the conflict on official mission as a member of the armed forces of the said State.

Commits the crime of mercenarism an individual, a group or an association, a representative of the State who, with the aim of opposing armed violence to the stability or territorial integrity of Chad, engages in one of the following acts:

- a) shelter, organize, finance, assist, equip, train, promote, support or employ in any way whatsoever, bands of mercenaries;
- b) enlist, engage or attempt to engage in the said bands;
- c) permit that in territories subject to its sovereignty or in any other place under its control, the activities mentioned in point (a) above develop or grant facilities for transit, transport or any other operation of the aforementioned bands.

Any natural person who commits the crime of mercenarism as defined in paragraph 1 above is sentenced to imprisonment of twenty to thirty years.

Is considered as perpetrator of mercenarism and punished by the same penalty any person who assumes command of mercenaries or gives them orders.

Mercenaries do not have combatant status and cannot benefit from prisoner of war status.

###### Art.116.‐ The mercenary is liable for both the crime of mercenarism and all related

offenses, without prejudice to all other offenses for which he could be prosecuted.

###### Art.117.‐ Legal persons may be declared criminally liable for acts of

mercenarism under the conditions defined by Articles 81 to 85 of this Code.

The penalty incurred by these legal persons is a fine of 50,000,000 to 500,000,000

FCFA.

In addition to the penalty provided for in paragraph 2 above, legal persons incur the ancillary penalties of Article 28 of this Code as well as the confiscation of all or part of their property, whatever its nature, movable or immovable, divided or undivided.

#### Chapter 5 ‐ Miscellaneous provisions

###### Art.118.‐ Subject to obligations arising from professional secrecy, any person who, in time of

war, having knowledge of plans or acts of treason, espionage or other activities liable to harm national defense, fails to report them to the military, administrative or judicial authorities as soon as he or she becomes aware of them, shall be punished by imprisonment of two to ten years.

The acts provided for in paragraph 1 of this article, when committed in peacetime, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties.

For the purposes of applying this article, the following shall be treated as accomplices and punished as such:

- a) those who provide, without constraint and with knowledge of their intentions, subsidies, means of subsistence, place of retreat or meeting to the perpetrators of attacks on the integrity of the State;
- b) those who knowingly carry correspondence of the perpetrators of such crimes or such offenses, or who knowingly facilitate, in any manner whatsoever, the search for, concealment of, transport or transmission of the object of the crime or offense.

For the purposes of applying this article, the following shall be treated as receivers and punished as such:

- a) those who knowingly conceal objects or instruments having served or intended to serve to commit the crime or offense or the objects, materials or documents obtained through the crimes or offenses;
- b) those who knowingly destroy, remove, conceal, hide or alter public or private documents liable to facilitate the investigation of the crime or offense, the discovery of evidence or the punishment of its perpetrators.

###### Art.119.‐ Any person who, before any execution or attempt of a crime

or an offense against the security of the State, gives first knowledge thereof to the administrative or judicial authorities, shall be exempt from the penalty incurred.

The penalty shall be reduced by application of the rule of mitigating excuse, if the denunciation occurs after the consummation of the attempt of the crime or offense, but before the initiation of prosecution.

The penalty shall be reduced by application of the rule of mitigating excuse with regard to the guilty party who, after the initiation of prosecution, procures the arrest of the perpetrators or accomplices of the same offense or of other offenses of the same nature or equal gravity.

Except for particular crimes that they may have personally committed, no penalty shall be pronounced against those who, having been part of an armed gang without exercising any command therein and without fulfilling any employment or function therein, withdrew at the first warning from the civil or military authorities or surrendered to these authorities.

Those who are exempt from penalty by application of this article may nevertheless be prohibited from residence and sentenced to the prohibition of certain rights, as provided in article 36 of this Code.

###### Art.120.‐ The financial retribution received by the guilty party, or the amount thereof when the

retribution could not be seized by the investigators, shall be declared acquired by the treasury by the judgment pronouncing the conviction of that person.

###### Art.121.‐ For the purposes of applying this chapter, the following shall be treated as weapons:

• a) all machines, all cutting, piercing or blunt instruments or utensils;

• b) pocket knives, simple canes and all other objects whatsoever if they have been used to kill, wound or strike.

###### Art.122.

‐ The President of the Republic may, by ordinance taken after legislative authorization, extend either for time of war or for time of peace, all or part of the provisions relating to attacks on the integrity of the State, to acts concerning it and which would be committed against the allied or friendly powers of Chad.

### Title 2 ‐ Attacks on the authority of the State and disturbances caused to the exercise of this

authority

#### Chapter 1 ‐ Gatherings Section 1 ‐

Participation in gatherings

###### Art.123.‐ The following shall be prohibited on the public highway or in a public place:

• a) any armed gathering;

• b) any unarmed gathering that could disturb public order.

A gathering is said to be armed when individuals composing it carry visible or concealed weapons or any objects whatsoever, visible or not, having served as weapons or brought with a view to serving as weapons.

###### Art.124.‐ Any unarmed person who, being part of an armed gathering, shall not have abandoned it

after the first warning, shall be punished by imprisonment of two months to one year.

The imprisonment shall be six months to two years if the unarmed person remains in an armed gathering and if it only disperses in the face of the use of force.

###### Art.125.‐ Any person who, in a gathering, during a demonstration or on the occasion of a

demonstration, during a meeting or on the occasion of a meeting, shall have been found carrying a visible or concealed weapon or any visible or concealed objects whatsoever, having served as weapons or brought with a view to serving as weapons, shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to two years.

The imprisonment shall be one year to five years in the case of a gathering dispersed by force. The penalties provided for in paragraph 2 shall be doubled in case of resistance.

###### Art.126.‐ Any direct provocation to an armed gathering shall be punished by imprisonment of

one to five years if it has been followed by effect and, if not, by imprisonment of three months to one year.

Any direct provocation to an unarmed gathering shall be punished by three months to one year of imprisonment if it has disturbed public order and, if not, by imprisonment of one to six months.

##### Section 2 ‐ Damages caused by a gathering

###### Art.127.‐ Any person who shall have continued to be part of a gathering after the third

warning made by a representative of the public authority may be sentenced to pecuniary reparation of the damages caused by that gathering.

###### Art.128.‐ Persons convicted of participation in a gathering or provocation to a

gathering shall be jointly liable for the reparation of damages caused by that gathering.

The complementary penalties of article 28 of this Code may furthermore be applied.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Rebellion and other acts of resistance to the action of administrative

and judicial authorities

##### Section 1 ‐ Rebellion

###### Art.129.

‐ Any attack, any resistance, with violence and assault, against ministerial officers, the public force, those responsible for the collection of taxes and contributions, customs officials, officers or agents of the administrative or judicial police, acting for the execution of laws, orders or ordinances of the public authority, warrants of justice or judgments, shall be qualified, according to the circumstances, as crime or offense of rebellion.

###### Art.130.‐ If the rebellion was committed without weapons or by one or two armed persons at most, the

guilty parties shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to two years.

If the rebellion was committed by more than two armed persons, the guilty parties shall be punished by imprisonment of one to four years.

In either case, a fine of 5,000 to 500,000 FCFA may be pronounced.

###### Art.131.‐ In case of rebellion with gang or gathering, the penalties provided for in paragraph I of the

preceding article shall be applicable to rebels without functions or employment in the gang, who withdrew at the first warning from the public authority, or even since, if they were seized only outside the place of rebellion and without new resistance and without weapons.

###### Art.132.‐ The prohibition of rights provided for in Article 28 of this Code may also be

pronounced in the case of the two preceding articles.

###### Art.133.‐ Any gathering of individuals for a crime or offence is deemed armed in the cases referred to in

Article 130 paragraphs 1 and 2 above.

###### Art.134.‐ Gatherings formed with or without

arms and accompanied by violence or threats against the administrative authority, police officers and agents or against the public force by prisoners under investigation, accused, convicted or subjected to civil imprisonment shall be punished as gatherings of rebels.

###### Art.135.‐ The penalty applied for rebellion to prisoners under investigation, accused or

convicted in relation to other crimes or offences, shall be served by them in the following manner:

- a) by those who, by reason of the crimes or offences that caused their detention, are or would be sentenced to a non-perpetual penalty, immediately after the expiration of that penalty;
- b) by the others, immediately after the final decision or judgment that acquitted them or dismissed them absolved of the fact for which they were detained;
- c) by those subjected to civil imprisonment, after the expiration of the constraint.

##### Section 2 ‐ Resistance to the action of authorities

###### Art.136.‐ Anyone who has

voluntarily abstained for two years from paying taxes, contributions or levies legally due shall be punished with imprisonment from three months to two years.

Public prosecution may only be initiated after formal notice has been given, either by registered letter with acknowledgment of receipt, or by official report of a sworn enforcement officer, or by writ of the enforcement agent, or at the request of the public prosecutor's office, by a judicial police officer.

###### Art.137.‐ Those who have incited refusal to pay taxes, contributions and levies shall be punished

with imprisonment from six months to five years.

###### Art.138.‐ Those who have knowingly assisted a person whom they knew to be suspected of a crime

or offence and to be sought for this reason by the justice system, to evade or attempt to evade searches shall be punished with imprisonment from one month to two years and a fine from 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA.

These provisions shall not be applicable to the spouse, to ascendants and descendants nor to relatives and in-laws up to the degree of uncle and nephew.

###### Art.139.‐ Those who, without legitimate excuse, have abstained from responding to regular summonses issued by administrative or judicial authorities, for matters within their jurisdiction, in the cases and forms provided for by law or regulation, shall be punished with imprisonment from fifteen days to three months and a fine from 25,000 to

50,000 FCFA.

###### Art.140.‐ Those who, outside the cases of rebellion and the cases provided for in the preceding articles, have

opposed, by any means whatsoever, the exercise of acts of their function by civil servants or agents holding public authority, agents of the public force or citizens charged with a public service ministry and have thus infringed or attempted to infringe public order shall be punished with imprisonment from fifteen days to three months and a fine from 25,000 to 50,000 FCFA.

###### Art.141.‐ Those who, without the prior authorization of the public officer, in the case where it is

prescribed, have caused a deceased individual to be buried, shall be punished with imprisonment from six days to six months, and a fine from 25,000 to 500,000 FCFA, without prejudice to the prosecution of crimes of which the perpetrators of this offence could be suspected in this circumstance. The same penalty shall be applied against those who have contravened, in any manner whatsoever, the law and regulations relating to the aforementioned burials.

#### Chapitre 3 ‐ Insults and violence against constitutional bodies, high authorities,

magistrates

##### Section 1 ‐ Insults to constitutional bodies

###### Art.142.‐ Without prejudice to the penalties provided for by the special legislation on offences committed

by way of the press and similar offences, insult to a member of the Government or the National Assembly or to a magistrate, in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of their functions, committed by words, writings or drawings, by gestures or by sending any objects, shall be punished with imprisonment from fifteen days to two years and a fine from 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

###### Art.143.‐ When the insult has been committed against a head of administrative district, a ministerial officer,

an agent of the public force, or a citizen charged with a public service ministry, the penalty shall be imprisonment from fifteen days to three years and a fine from 5,000 to 250,000 FCFA.

###### Art.144.

‐ Anyone who has publicly, by acts, words or writings, sought to cast discredit on a judicial act or decision, under conditions likely to undermine the authority of justice or its independence, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to six months and a fine from 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

The court may, in addition, order that its decision be posted and published under the conditions it determines, in accordance with the legal provisions relating to the publication of sentences.

The initiative for prosecution belongs to the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal.

The foregoing provisions may not, under any circumstances, be applied to purely technical commentaries, nor to acts, words or writings aimed at the review of a conviction.

###### Art.145.‐ Anyone who has published, before

the intervention of the final judicial decision, commentaries aimed at exerting pressure on the statements of witnesses or on the decision of the investigating or trial courts shall be punished with the penalties provided for in the preceding article.

The provisions of the last three paragraphs of the preceding article are also applicable.

##### Section 2 ‐ Violence against members of constitutional bodies of the State

###### Art.146.

‐ Any individual who, even without weapons and without resulting in injuries, has struck a member of the Government or the National Assembly, a magistrate, in the exercise of their functions or on the occasion of this exercise, or committed any other violence or assault against them under the same circumstances, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine from 5,000 to 50,000 FCFA.

###### Art.147.‐ Violence or assaults of the kind expressed in the preceding article directed

against a ministerial officer, an agent of the public force, or a citizen charged with a public service ministry, if they took place while they were exercising their ministry or on this occasion, shall be punished with imprisonment from one month to three years and a fine from 5,000 to

50,000 FCFA.

###### Art.143.‐ When the violence exercised against civil servants and agents as provided for

in articles 146 and 147 above has caused bloodshed, injuries or illness, the penalty shall be doubled. If death has followed, the offender shall be punished with thirty years' imprisonment.

When blows have been struck or injuries inflicted with intent to cause death and have actually caused it, the offender shall be punished with life imprisonment.

#### Chapter 4 ‐ Escapes

###### Art.149.‐ Detainees who have escaped or who have attempted to escape, by breaking prison or

by violence, shall for this sole fact be punished by imprisonment of at least six months, which may be increased up to a penalty equal to that for which they were detained, or if they were detained preventively, to that attached by law to the charge that justified the detention, without it being able, in either case, to exceed five years of imprisonment, all without prejudice to heavier penalties they may have incurred for other crimes or offenses they may have committed in their violence.

They shall serve this sentence immediately after the expiration of that which they have incurred for the crime or offense for which they were detained or immediately after the judgment or ruling that acquitted or absolved them of said crime or offense.

Shall be punished by the same penalty, which shall be served under the same conditions, any detainee transferred to a health or hospital facility who, by any means, has escaped or attempted to escape from it, and any detainee who, employed outside the prison, or beneficiary of leave permission, has taken advantage of it to escape.

###### Art.150.‐ When a detainee or prisoner of war has escaped, the chief commanders or subordinate

commanders of the force responsible for the escort or stationed at the facility, those responsible for the facilities, the guards and all other persons assigned to the conduct, transport or custody of detainees or prisoners shall be punished:

- a) in case of negligence, by imprisonment of fifteen days to two years and a fine of 25,000 to 50,000 FCFA;
- b) in case of connivance in the escape, by imprisonment of six months to five years and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

###### Art.151.‐ The penalties provided for connivance shall be incurred when the persons

referred to in the preceding article have procured or facilitated, attempted to procure or facilitate an escape, even if it has been neither consummated nor attempted and even though the preparations were carried out without the knowledge of the detainee or prisoner. Connivance may result from a simple voluntary abstention.

###### Art.152.

‐ Persons other than those referred to in Article 150 above who have procured, facilitated, attempted to procure or facilitate an escape or facilitated the flight of the escapee shall be punished by fifteen days to two years of imprisonment and a fine of

5,000 to 50,000 FCFA.

###### Art.153.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment of fifteen days to six months anyone who has, under

irregular conditions, delivered or sent or attempted to deliver or send to a detainee, in any place whatsoever, sums of money, correspondence or any objects whatsoever.

The exit or attempted irregular exit of sums of money, correspondence or any objects whatsoever, shall be punished by the same penalties.

The acts referred to in the two preceding paragraphs shall be considered as accomplished under irregular conditions if they were committed in violation of a regulation issued by the management of the penitentiary administration or approved by it.

If the offender is one of the persons designated in Article 150 above or a person authorized by their functions to approach detainees in any capacity whatsoever, the penalty for them shall be imprisonment of six months to two years.

#### Chapter 5 ‐ Disturbances caused by civil servants Section 1 ‐

Coalitions of civil servants

###### Art.154.‐ Subject to the regulations relating to the right to strike in force, any concert of

measures contrary to the laws, practiced either by the meeting of individuals or bodies holding any part of public authority, or by deputation or correspondence between them, shall be punished by imprisonment of two months to two years.

The deprivation of rights provided for in Article 28 of this Code may also be pronounced.

###### Art.155.‐ Shall be punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article, civil servants who have, by

deliberation, decided to give resignations whose object or effect would be to prevent or suspend either the administration of justice or the accomplishment of any service.

##### Section 2 ‐ Illegally prolonged exercise of public authority

###### Art.156.‐ Any civil servant or any public authority revoked, dismissed, suspended or legally prohibited,

who, after having had official knowledge of it, has continued the exercise of their functions, or who, being elective or temporary, has exercised them after having been replaced, shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to two years and a fine of 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

##### Section 3 ‐ Refusal to respond to requisitions

###### Art.157.‐ Any arms commander legally served with a requisition from the civil authority who

has refused or abstained from having the forces under his command act shall be punished by imprisonment of one to two years.

###### Art.158.‐ Witnesses and assessors who have alleged an excuse recognized as false shall be

sentenced, in addition to the fines pronounced for non-appearance, to imprisonment of fifteen days to three months.

#### Chapter 6 ‐ Encroachments on judicial functions and usurpations of titles or

functions

##### Section 1 ‐ Encroachments on judicial functions

###### Art.159.‐ Any civil servant or public agent who, without qualification, has interfered in judicial functions

by taking cognizance of private rights and interests within the jurisdiction of the courts, and who, after the claim of the parties or one of them, has nevertheless decided in the matter before the superior authority has ruled, shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to one year and a fine of 25,000 to 50,000 FCFA.

##### Section 2 ‐ Usurpation of titles or functions

###### Art.160.

‐ Anyone who, without title, has interfered in public, civil or military functions, or has performed the acts of one of these functions, shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to five years, without prejudice to the penalty for forgery, if the act bears that character.

###### Art.161.‐ Any person who has publicly worn a costume, uniform or decoration

that they were not entitled to wear, or a costume presenting with the regulatory uniforms of the army, gendarmerie or police a resemblance of a nature to deceive the public shall be punished by imprisonment of fifteen days to two years and a fine of 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

###### Art.162.‐ Shall be punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article anyone who, without fulfilling the conditions

required to claim it, has used or claimed a title attached to a legally regulated profession, an official diploma or a quality whose conditions of attribution have been fixed by the public authority.

###### Art.163.‐ Shall be punished by a fine of 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA anyone who, without right and with a view to

attributing to themselves an honorific distinction, has publicly taken an altered title or modified the name assigned to them by civil status records.

The court shall order the mention of the judgment in the margin of authentic acts or civil status records in which the title has been taken unduly or the name altered.

###### Art.164.‐ In the cases provided for by Articles 162 and 163, the court may order the

publication of the judgment.

###### Art.165.‐ Is punished by a fine of 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA any person who, in a public

or authentic act or in an administrative document intended for the public authority and except in cases where the regulations in force authorize them to subscribe to these acts or documents under a borrowed civil status, has not taken the name that is legally theirs.

### Title 3 ‐ Attacks and obstructions to public freedoms, peace and public tranquillity

#### Chapter 1 ‐ Violations and obstructions of public freedoms Section 1

‐ Violations of civic rights

###### Art.166.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to three years and a fine of

10,000 to 100,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, anyone who, on polling day or outside the legal electoral campaign period:

- a) by granting or promising a particular advantage of any nature whatsoever, influences the vote of an elector or determines him to abstain;
- b) by gifts, liberalities, favours, promises of granting public or private employment or other particular advantages made with a view to influencing the vote of one or more electors, obtains their suffrage either directly or through an intermediary;
- c) directly or through an intermediary, accepts or solicits from candidates, gifts, liberalities, favours or advantages such as those cited in point b above;
- d) for the purpose of advantaging a candidate or a list of candidates, draws up false minutes or proclaims results that he knows to be inaccurate or fraudulent.

The penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above are doubled when the vote influenced is that of an electoral college or a fraction of that college.

###### Art.167.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to three years and a fine of

10,000 to 100,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, anyone who, on polling day or outside the legal electoral campaign period:

- a) by assault or threats of any particular damage, determines an elector to abstain;
- b) on polling day, insults or exercises violence upon a polling commission or one of its members, or by assault or threat, delays or prevents electoral operations;
- c) influences the vote of an elector, by assault, violence, or threats against him, or by making him fear losing his employment, exposing his person, family or fortune to damage;
- d) by gathering, clamour or threatening demonstrations, disturbs electoral operations or infringes upon the exercise of the right or freedom to vote;
- e) on polling day, with violence, fraudulently seizes the ballot box.

###### Art.168.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to two years and a fine of

25,000 to 250,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, anyone who enters an electoral assembly with a visible weapon.

The penalties provided for in the above paragraph are doubled if the weapon is concealed.

###### Art.169.‐ Except in the case of a flagrant offence, no criminal prosecution for violation of the

provisions of this section may be instituted against a candidate before the proclamation of the results.

##### Section 2 ‐ Attacks on freedom

###### Art.170.‐ When a civil servant, public agent or government employee has

ordered or committed any arbitrary act or act violating either individual freedom or the Constitution, he shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine of

50,000 to 5,000,000 FCFA.

If, however, he justifies that he acted by order of his superiors for matters within their jurisdiction, for which hierarchical obedience was owed to them, he shall be exempt from the penalty, which shall, in that case, be applied only to the superiors who gave the order.

###### Art.171.‐ If Ministers accused of having ordered or authorized the act contrary to the

Constitution claim that the signature attributed to them was obtained by fraud, they shall be required, while terminating the act, to denounce the person they declare to be the author of the fraud, otherwise they shall be prosecuted personally.

###### Art.172.‐ Officials responsible for administrative or judicial police who have refused or

neglected to comply with a legal complaint aimed at establishing illegal and arbitrary detentions, whether in houses intended for the custody of detainees or anywhere else, and who do not justify having reported them to the higher authority, shall be punished by imprisonment from one month to one year and held liable for damages.

###### Art.173.‐ Wardens of penitentiary institutions who have received a prisoner without

a warrant or judgment, or those who have refused to present their registers to the judicial authority shall, as guilty of arbitrary detention, be punished by imprisonment from six months to two years and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

###### Art.174.

‐ Shall be punished by the penalties provided for in Article 172 above, any judicial police officer, any Attorney General or of the Republic, any deputy prosecutor, any judge who has provoked, given or signed a judgment, order or warrant aimed at the personal prosecution or accusation of either a Minister or a member of parliament without the authorizations prescribed by law, or who, except in cases of flagrant offence, has, without the same authorizations, given or signed the order or warrant to seize or arrest one or more Ministers or members of parliament.

###### Art.175.‐ Shall be punished by the penalties provided for in Article 173 above, the Attorney General or

Prosecutor of the Republic, the deputy prosecutor, the judge or public officer who has detained or caused to be detained an individual outside the places determined by the government or by the public administration.

###### Art.176.‐ When a civil servant or public officer, an administrator, an agent or an

employee of the government or police, an executor of court warrants or judgments, a commander in chief or in subordinate command of the public force has, without legitimate reason, used or committed violence against persons in the exercise of or on the occasion of the exercise of his duties, he shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

The additional penalties of Article 28 of this Code may furthermore be applied to them.

##### Section 3 ‐ Obstructions to freedoms

###### Art.177.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to three years and a fine of

50,000 to 500,000 FCFA, anyone who has used violence, assault, threats or fraudulent manoeuvres to bring about or maintain, attempt to bring about or maintain a concerted work stoppage, for the purpose of forcing the raising or lowering of wages or of infringing upon the free exercise of industry or labour.

###### Art.178.

‐ Those who, in public auctions, have obstructed or disturbed, attempted to obstruct or disturb the freedom of bids or tenders, by assault, violence or threats, whether before or during the bids or tenders, shall be punished by imprisonment from fifteen days to three months and a fine of 100,000 to

1,000,000 FCFA.

The same penalty shall be pronounced against those who, by gifts, promises or fraudulent agreements have turned away or attempted to turn away bidders, limited or attempted to limit bids or tenders, as well as against those who have received these gifts or accepted these promises. Shall be punished by the same penalty all those who, after a public auction, proceed with or participate in a re-auction without the participation of a competent ministerial officer.

###### Art.179.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment from two months to two years and a fine from 500,000

to 20,000,000 FCFA those who have, directly or through an intermediary, operated or attempted to operate the artificial increase or decrease of the price of foodstuffs or goods or public or private securities:

- a) by false or slanderous facts knowingly spread among the public, by offers thrown on the market with the intention of disturbing prices, by overbids made at the prices demanded by the sellers themselves, by any fraudulent ways or means;
- b) by exercising or attempting to exercise, either individually or by meeting or coalition, an action on the market with the aim of obtaining a gain that would not be the result of the natural interplay of supply and demand.

###### Art.180.‐ In all cases provided for by article 177 above, the court may

apply the complementary penalties provided for in article 28 of this Code. Furthermore, and notwithstanding the application of provisions on mitigating circumstances, it shall order that the judgment of conviction be published.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Offenses against public peace and tranquility Section

1 ‐ Criminal associations

###### Art.181.

‐ The formation of an association, regardless of its duration or the number of its members, or of an agreement established for the purpose of preparing or committing offenses against persons or property constitutes an offense against public peace and is constitutive, in itself, of a criminal offense.

###### Art.182.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from three to eight years anyone who has affiliated with an

association formed or has participated in an agreement for the purpose specified in the preceding article. The guilty parties shall be exempt from punishment if, before any prosecution, they have revealed to the authorities the agreement established or informed them of the existence of the association.

###### Art.183.‐ Shall be punished by the penalties provided for in article 182 above, anyone who has knowingly and

voluntarily favored the perpetrators of the acts provided for in article 181 above, by providing them with the instruments for committing the acts, means of correspondence, lodging or meeting place.

However, the provisions contained in paragraph 2 of article 182 shall apply to those guilty of the acts provided for in this article.

##### Section 2 ‐ Vagrancy and begging

###### Art.184.‐ Commits the offense of vagrancy and is punished by imprisonment from fifteen days to six

months, anyone who:

- a) not justifying either a certain domicile or means of subsistence and who, habitually exercising neither trade nor profession, is found in a public place or a place open to the public;
- b) although justifying a certain domicile, habitually derives his subsistence only from the practice or facilitation on the public highway of illicit games.

When persons convicted of vagrancy are of foreign nationality, they are expelled from the territory of the Republic upon expiration of their sentence.

###### Art.185.‐ Commits the offense of begging and is punished by imprisonment from fifteen days to six

months anyone who, having means of subsistence or being able to obtain them through work, solicits charity in a place for which there exists a public establishment organized to remedy begging.

The convicted person is, upon expiration of his sentence, taken to the begging depot.

When the acts indicated in paragraph 1 above are committed by habitual but able‐bodied beggars, in places where there does not yet exist a public establishment organized to remedy begging, the imprisonment shall be from one to three months.

The penalty shall be imprisonment from six months to two years when the beggars, even disabled:

- a) have used threats;
- b) have entered, without permission of the owner or persons of his household, either into a dwelling or into an enclosure dependent thereon;
- c) feign wounds or infirmities;
- d) beg in a group, unless it be husband and wife, father or mother and their young children, the blind person and his guide.

###### Art.186.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years, any beggar or vagrant who:

• a) has been found disguised in any manner whatsoever;

• b) has been found carrying weapons, although he has neither used them nor threatened;

• c) has been found carrying instruments suitable either to commit thefts or other offenses, or to provide him with means of entering houses.

###### Art.187.‐ Any beggar or vagrant who is found carrying one or more items

of a value exceeding 10,000 FCFA and cannot justify the origin of said items, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to two years.

###### Art.188.‐ Any beggar or vagrant who has exercised or attempted to exercise any act of violence

whatsoever against persons shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years, without prejudice to stronger penalties, if applicable, by reason of the type and circumstances of the violence.

If the beggar or vagrant who exercised or attempted violence was, moreover, in one of the circumstances expressed by article 186 above, he shall be punished by one to five years of imprisonment.

##### Section 3 ‐ Practice of witchcraft and charlatanism

###### Art.189.‐ Commit the offense of practice of witchcraft and are, without prejudice to any penalties

incurred for attacks against persons, fraud or any other crimes or offenses, punished by a penalty of one month to two years of imprisonment and a fine from 5,000 to 50,000 FCFA, those who engage in practices of divination or magic obeying no rule of logic and whose results are likely to disturb public peace, to set individuals against one another or to provoke acts of revenge consisting of attacks on life or bodily integrity or on property.

###### Art.190.‐ Commit the offense of charlatanism and are punished by a penalty of one month to two years

of imprisonment and a fine from 5,000 to 50,000 FCFA those who ordinarily exploit public credulity by promising advantages they know they cannot provide or by proposing objects they know do not possess the qualities or virtues they claim.

###### Art.191.‐ When the practices mentioned in articles 189 and 190 above have

caused either death or injuries or illnesses having resulted in incapacity for work, the penalties shall be imprisonment from five to ten years and a fine from 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

### Title 4 ‐ Corruption and related offenses

#### Chapter 1 ‐ Active and passive corruption of public officials

###### Art.192.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to ten years any public official who has, without right, at

any time, directly or indirectly, solicited or accepted offers or promises or received gifts or presents or other undue advantages for himself or for another person or entity, to do or refrain from doing an act of his functions or employment, for which no remuneration is legally due.

This penalty is accompanied by the complementary penalty of confiscation provided for in article 28 of this Code.

###### Art.193.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to ten years anyone who has offered or granted to a

public official, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, promises, gifts, presents or undue advantages, for himself or for another person or entity, so that he accomplishes or refrains from accomplishing an act of his functions or employment, just or not, but not subject to remuneration.

This penalty is accompanied by the complementary penalty of confiscation provided for in article 28 of this Code.

###### Art.194.‐ The same penalties are incurred when the perpetrators of the offenses provided for in

articles 192 and 193 above committed them in a group or participated in concealing the proceeds.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Of the misappropriation, embezzlement or any other illicit use of property by

a public official

###### Art.195.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 100,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA any public official who, in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of his duties, has embezzled or misappropriated public funds, instruments in lieu thereof, or documents, titles, acts, property, materials, commodities or any objects belonging to the State or to private individuals. When the amount of sums embezzled or misappropriated exceeds ten million francs, the penalty incurred is imprisonment of five to twenty years.

These penalties are accompanied by the additional penalty of confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this Code.

###### Art.196.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 100,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA any public official who, if he was an accountant under the terms of the texts in force or if he has been recognized as a de facto accountant, has embezzled or misappropriated funds or active instruments in lieu thereof or documents, titles, acts, property, materials, commodities or any objects belonging to the State or to private individuals that were in his hands by virtue of his duties.

When the amount of sums embezzled or misappropriated exceeds ten million francs, the penalty incurred is imprisonment of five to twenty years.

These penalties are accompanied by the additional penalty of confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this Code.

###### Art.197.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, the manager of any entity enjoying legal personality, commercial or not, who, in common agreement with any public official, has overvalued the value or the sale or rental price of a property, a service or a supply in relation to the commonly practiced price.

This penalty is accompanied by the additional penalty of confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this Code.

The public official who participated in this overvaluation is punished by the same penalties.

The manager of any entity enjoying legal personality, commercial or not, who, in common agreement with the managers of an entity having a public service mission, participated in this overvaluation shall be punished by the same penalties.

###### Art.198.

‐ Is punished by imprisonment of ten to twenty years any person who has misappropriated, embezzled or dissipated weapons, explosives or war munitions.

This penalty is mandatorily accompanied by the additional penalty of confiscation provided for in Article 31 of this Code.

###### Art.199.‐ Is punished by a fine at least equal to the value of the damage suffered by the State without

it being less than 200,000 FCFA, any person who has used, in an illicit manner, for his benefit or for the benefit of another person or any other entity, a public property.

#### Chapter 3 ‐ Of illicit enrichment and other related offenses Section 1 ‐ Of

illicit enrichment

###### Art.200.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to ten years and a fine of 100,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, any holder of State authority who cannot justify the significant increase of his patrimony in relation to his legitimate income.

The confiscation of the patrimony thus acquired is always pronounced.

###### Art.201.‐ The publication of the judgment or the ruling of conviction may be ordered at the expense of the

convicted person.

###### Art.202.‐ The competent public services may constitute themselves as civil parties in order to request

reparation of the damage caused to the public treasury.

##### Section 2 ‐ Of other related offenses

###### Art.203.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 200,000 to

5,000,000 FCFA, the act by any holder of State authority of granting in whatever form and for whatever reason, total or partial exemptions or franchises not authorized by law on public duties and taxes or levies or of delivering free of charge the products of State establishments.

###### Art.204.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 200,000 to

5,000,000 FCFA, the act by any holder of State authority of establishing or approving one or more documents of sale, receipt, delivery or service rendered to attest to the reality of an order or a service not performed or partially performed in relation to contractual commitments.

###### Art.205.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 200,000 to

5,000,000 FCFA, the act by any holder of State authority of manipulating in an abusive intent the date of taking office of public officials, of fraudulently altering writings, information or figures for the purpose of mandating, having mandated undue sums for his benefit and for the benefit of a third party.

This penalty is accompanied by the additional penalty of confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this Code.

#### Chapter 4 ‐ Of obstruction of the proper functioning of justice

###### Art.206.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, the act by anyone of resorting to physical force, threats or intimidation or any other means, of promising, offering or granting an undue advantage to obtain false testimony or prevent testimony for the presentation of evidence in a procedure related to the commission of offenses provided for in this title.

###### Art.207.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, the act by anyone of resorting to physical force, threats or intimidation or any other illicit means to prevent an agent of justice or an agent of authorized investigative or enforcement services from exercising the duties of his office in relation to the commission of one of the offenses provided for in this title.

#### Chapter 5 ‐ Of active and passive trading in influence

###### Art.208.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, anyone who offers or grants to a public official or to any other person, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, an undue advantage so that said official or said person abuses his real or supposed influence in order to obtain from an administration or a public authority an undue advantage for himself or for a third party.

###### Art.209.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, any public official or any other person who solicits, accepts, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, an undue advantage for himself or for another person in order to use his real or supposed influence, with a view to having an administration or a public authority obtain an undue advantage.

#### Chapter 6 ‐ Of abuse of functions

###### Art.210.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, any public official who has:

- 1) intentionally abused his functions or his position, by accomplishing or refraining from accomplishing, in the exercise of his duties, an act in order to obtain an undue advantage for himself or for another person;
- 2) revealed the identity or address of a whistleblower or a witness;
- 3) outside the cases where the law authorizes him to act as a whistleblower, revealed all or part of the information known to him in the context of his duties.

#### Chapter 7 ‐ Of active and passive corruption by persons not exercising a

public function

###### Art.211.‐ Is punished with imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine of

1.000.000 to 10.000.000 FCFA, the act by anyone, of promising, offering or granting promises, gifts, presents or other undue advantages, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, to a person who, without being a depositary of public authority, nor charged with a public service mission, nor vested with an elected public mandate, exercises, within the framework of a professional or social activity, a management function or work for a natural or legal person under public or private law or for any organization, for themselves or for another person, so that, in violation of their duties, they perform or refrain from performing an act.

This sentence is accompanied by the complementary penalty of confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this Code.

###### Art.212.‐ Is punished with six months to five years imprisonment and a fine of 1000.000 to

10.000.000 FCFA, the act by anyone who, without being a depositary of public authority nor charged with a public service mission, nor vested with an elected public mandate, exercises, within the framework of a professional or social activity, a management function or work for a natural or legal person under public or private law or for any organization, to solicit or accept, without right, at any time, directly or indirectly, offers or promises or to receive gifts or presents or other undue advantages for themselves or for another person, in order to perform or refrain from performing an act in violation of their duties.

This sentence is accompanied by the complementary penalty of confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this Code.

#### Chapter 8 ‐ On aggravated fraud

###### Art.213.‐ Is punished with imprisonment from three to ten years and a fine equal to triple the

value in question, anyone who commits the offense of fraud when it is carried out:

- 1) by a person who is a depositary of public authority or charged with a public service mission in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of their functions;
- 2) by a person who unduly assumes the capacity of a person who is a depositary of public authority or charged with a public service mission;
- 3) by a person who made a public appeal for the issuance of shares, bonds, vouchers, shares or any titles either of a company or of a commercial or industrial enterprise;
- 4) to the detriment of a person whose particular vulnerability, due to their age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or pregnancy, is apparent or known to its perpetrator;
- 5) in an organized gang, that is to say by a group formed or an agreement established for this purpose.

###### Art.214.‐ Is punished with imprisonment from one to five years and a fine whose maximum rate

is equal to triple the value in question, anyone who commits the offense of fraud provided for in Article 213 of this Code when the fraud is based on a permanent race between the collection of new funds and payments aimed at building confidence.

When this fraud concerns public savings appeals, it is punished with imprisonment from five to ten years and a fine equal to triple the amount extorted. This sentence is accompanied by the complementary penalty of confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this Code.

#### Chapter 9 ‐ On corruption in relation to public procurement Art.215.‐ Is

punished with imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine of 500.000 to

10.000.000 FCFA, any person who, by failure to observe the rules for awarding, executing and monitoring public procurement, obtains or causes a third party to obtain, directly or indirectly, an undue advantage.

###### Art.216.‐ Also constitute acts of corruption in relation to public procurement and

are punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 215 above:

- a) the award of a contract to a service provider without legal existence;
- b) the award of a contract with a bankrupt company, with third parties or companies in a suspect period of bankruptcy or judicial liquidation;
- c) the award of a contract with a company that does not present sufficient financial, economic and technical guarantees.

###### Art.217.‐ Constitute systematic negligence in relation to public procurement and are

punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 215 above:

- a) the repeated failure to transmit files to members of Procurement Commissions within regulatory deadlines and forms;
- b) the repeated failure to summon the independent observer for contracts falling within their competence;
- c) the summoning in disregard of deadline rules having the purpose or consequence of delaying the presence or causing the absence of members of the Commissions and the independent observer;
- d) the repeated failure to transmit documents to the body in charge of regularizing contracts within regulatory deadlines;
- e) the failure to terminate the contract in cases of termination by operation of law;
- f) the failure to transmit termination acts to the body in charge of regularizing public procurement;
- g) the non‐payment of regulatory fees;
- h) the failure to transmit documents relating to the execution of contracts to the body in charge of regulating public procurement.

###### Art.218.‐ Constitute a refusal of service due in relation to public procurement and are punished

in accordance with the provisions of Article 215 above:

- a) the refusal to sell the tender dossier or the concealment of the tender dossier;
- b) the refusal to communicate to a tenderer, after their request, the documents to which they are entitled;
- c) unjustified delays in payments;
- d) the non‐execution of prescriptions granted within the framework of collateral;
- e) the unjustified refusal of visa;
- f) the non‐compliance with deadlines for approval of statements;
- g) the unjustified refusal to release guarantees or to issue the corresponding releases within the prescribed deadlines.

###### Art.219.‐ Constitute forgery in public and authentic writings in relation to public procurement

and are punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 215 above:

- a) the change of contract stipulations without a related amendment duly adopted;
- b) the modification of the content of the final text of the contract draft in relation to the data in the tender dossier.

###### Art.220.‐ Constitutes forgery in private or commercial writing in relation to public procurement

and is punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 215 above, the provision of false documents in the tender dossier.

###### Art.221.‐ Constitute embezzlement in relation to public procurement and are punished

in accordance with the provisions of Article 215 above:

- a) the non‐application of delay penalties;
- b) the lifting of penalties without prior authorization from the body in charge of regulating the public procurement sector;
- c) the retention, without any valid reason, of a dossier.

###### Art.222.‐ Constitute favoritism in relation to public procurement and are punished

in accordance with the provisions of Article 215 above:

- a) the advance communication to a tenderer of information about the tender with the aim of favoring them in relation to their competitors;
- b) the receipt of offers after the deadline;
- c) the manipulation of an evaluation.

###### Art.223.‐ Constitute misappropriation of property or public funds in relation to public procurement

and are punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 215 above:

- a) misappropriation of operating costs of public procurement tender committees;
- b) non-remittance of fees for acquisition of tender documents;
- c) commitment of an expenditure relating to the operation of the public procurement tender committee without supporting documents;
- d) revision or updating of prices not provided for in the specifications or in violation of the provisions of said specifications;
- e) invoicing of fictitious services;
- f) recording of erroneous services;
- g) irregular affixing of visas on files tainted with irregularities;
- h) commitment of an expenditure or certification of documents without execution of services;
- i) liquidation and payment of services not performed or not in conformity with the public contract;
- j) payment of a public contract in excess of its amount and that of any amendments thereto;
- k) acceptance of services not performed or not in conformity with the public contract;
- l) certification of invoices for services not performed or not in conformity with the public contract.

#### Chapter 10 ‐ Insider Trading Offense

###### Art.224.‐ Constitutes the offense of insider trading and is punished by imprisonment of six months to two years and

a fine of 1,000,000 to 10,000,000 FCFA, the act:

- a) for directors of a commercial or industrial company and for persons having, in the course of exercising their profession or functions, privileged information on the situation or prospects of an issuer whose securities are traded on the market, of carrying out or knowingly allowing to be carried out, directly or through an intermediary, one or more transactions before the public has knowledge of such information and with the purpose of realizing an undue profit;
- b) for any person having, in the course of exercising their profession or functions, privileged information on the situation or prospects of an issuer whose securities are traded on a market, of communicating it to a third party outside the normal scope of their profession or functions and with the purpose of realizing an undue profit.

Also constitutes the offense of insider trading and is punished by the penalties provided for in paragraph I above, the act for any person having, in the course of exercising their functions, privileged information subject to professional secrecy, relating to the realization of a project by the State, a decentralized local authority or any other public law entity, of using said information to allow themselves or allow others to perform acts for their own profit so as to delay the planned project or burden it with additional charges.

The penalties are doubled if the perpetrator of the acts is a civil servant or public official within the meaning of this Code.

###### Art.225.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of two to five years and a fine of

200,000 to 2,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who, responsible by reason of their functions, for supervision of any enterprise, public corporation or concession, or for expressing opinions on their activities, collaborates or participates in any manner whatsoever, in their financing or their activity.

###### Art.226.‐ Are punished by the same penalties the actions described in the preceding article, committed

within a period of five years from the cessation of said functions, unless it concerns capital received by way of inheritance.

###### Art.227.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 200,000 to

2,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any public official who, responsible for a mission of administration even provisional of control, supervision or responsible for a mission of administration even provisional of control, supervision or advisory to a private enterprise, exercises after cessation of these functions, a corporate mandate or engages, even pursuant to a contract, in a remunerated activity for this enterprise.

#### Chapter 11 ‐ Money Laundering

###### Art.228.‐ Are punished by five to ten years of imprisonment and a fine whose maximum rate

is equal to three times the value of the property in question or the amount of the values concerned:

- 1) those who knowingly converted or transferred property with the purpose, either of concealing or disguising the illicit origin of said property or resources, or of helping any person involved in the commission of a crime or an offense to escape the legal consequences of their acts;
- 2) those who knowingly concealed or disguised the nature, origin, location, disposition, movement or actual ownership of resources, property or rights relating thereto originating from a crime or an offense;
- 3) those who, with the same purpose, knowingly acquired, possessed or used property originating from a crime or an offense.

This penalty is accompanied by the complementary penalty of confiscation provided for in Article 28 of this

Code.

###### Art.229.‐ Money laundering is punished by the penalty attached to the crime or offense that procured the property

laundered if it is more severe, when the perpetrator of the laundering had knowledge of these offenses.

###### Art.230.‐ The agreement or participation in an association with a view to committing an act

constituting money laundering and the association to commit said act, is punished by imprisonment of three to seven years and a fine equal to three times the value of the property or funds that were the subject of the laundering operations.

#### Chapter 12 ‐ Provisions Common to Corruption and Related or

Connected Offenses

###### Art.231.‐ In addition to a fine that may not be less than 1,000,000 FCFA and whose maximum rate

is equal to twice those provided for natural persons, legal persons declared criminally liable for the offenses provided for in this title incur the following penalties or one of them:

- 1) prohibition, for a period of one to five years at most, from exercising directly or indirectly one or more professional or social activities;
- 2) closure, for a period of one to five years at most, of establishments or of one or more of the establishments of the enterprise having served to commit the incriminated acts;
- 3) the penalty of confiscation on all movable or immovable property, whatever its nature, having served to commit the offense or that were intended to commit it or that are the object or the direct or indirect proceeds of the offense;
- 4) posting for a period of three to six months of all or part of the decision, or its dissemination, in the places or by the means indicated by the court.

The penalty of confiscation does not exclude the conviction of legal persons declared criminally liable for the above-mentioned offenses to pay damages to civil parties.

###### Art.232.

‐ Subject to, where applicable, provisions providing for more severe penalties, violation of one of the prohibitions pronounced as a complementary penalty in application of Article 28 of this Code shall be punished by one to three years of imprisonment and a fine of 2,000,000 to 10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties.

The complementary penalty of confiscation does not exclude the conviction of the natural person found guilty of one of the offenses provided for in this Code to pay damages to civil parties.

### Titre 5 ‐ Alterations of the truth, forgery and counterfeiting

#### Chapitre 1 ‐ Forgery and counterfeiting

##### Section 1 ‐ Forgery and counterfeiting relating to currency

###### Art.241.‐ Anyone who has counterfeited or altered either currency having legal tender in Chad, or

banknotes authorized by law or notes of the same nature issued by the treasury, or instruments issued by the treasury with its stamp or mark, anyone who has made use of such counterfeited or altered currency, notes or instruments, anyone who has participated in their issue or introduction into the territory, shall be punished by ten to twenty years' imprisonment.

###### Art.242.

‐ The counterfeiting or alteration of foreign currency, foreign treasury instruments, foreign banknotes, the issue, introduction into any country or use of such counterfeited or altered currency, instruments or notes shall be punished as if they were Chadian currency, treasury instruments or Chadian banknotes, according to the distinctions set out in this title.

However, those who, abroad, have made themselves guilty as perpetrators or accomplices of such crimes or offences may only be prosecuted in Chad under the conditions provided for in articles 7 to 12 of this Code.

###### Art.243.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to three years anyone who has colored

currency having legal tender in Chad or foreign currency with the aim of deceiving as to the nature of the metal, or has issued or introduced them into Chadian territory.

Shall be punished by the same penalty anyone who has participated in the issue or introduction of currency so colored.

###### Art.244.‐ Anyone who, having received in good faith counterfeited, altered or colored currency or

counterfeited or altered instruments or notes, has made use of them after having verified them or had them verified, shall be punished by fifteen days to six months' imprisonment and a fine equal to the sum represented by the coins thus put back into circulation.

###### Art.245.

‐ Counterfeited or altered currency, instruments and notes shall be confiscated and destroyed.

###### Art.246.‐ Persons guilty of the crimes mentioned in articles 242 and 243 above

shall be exempt from punishment, if before the completion of these crimes and before any prosecution, they have given notice and revealed the perpetrators to the constituted authorities, or if, even after prosecution has begun, they have procured the arrest of the other guilty parties.

They may nevertheless be banned from residence.

###### Art.247.‐ A fine with a minimum of 50,000 FCFA and a maximum of

500,000 FCFA shall be imposed on those guilty of the offences provided for in this section. The fine may however be increased up to one quarter of the illegitimate benefit that the forgery has procured or was intended to procure for the perpetrators of the crime or offence, their accomplices or those who have made use of the forged piece.

##### Section 2 ‐ Falsifications and counterfeiting of seals, stamps, marks and hammers and

various other forgeries

###### Art.248.‐ Those who have counterfeited or falsified the seal of the State or made use of the counterfeited

or falsified seal, shall be punished by imprisonment of ten to twenty years.

###### Art.249.‐ Those who have counterfeited or falsified, either one or more national stamps, or the

State hammers used for forestry marks, or the punches used to mark gold or silver materials, or who have made use of falsified or counterfeited papers, instruments, stamps, hammers or punches, shall be punished by imprisonment of ten to fifteen years.

###### Art.250.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment of ten to fifteen years anyone who, having unduly

procured the genuine stamps, hammers or punches, has made an application or use thereof prejudicial to the rights or interests of the State.

###### Art.251.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years and a fine of

300,000 to 3,000,000 FCFA, those who have:

- a) counterfeited marks intended to be affixed in the name of the Government on various kinds of foodstuffs or goods, or who have made use of these false marks;
- b) counterfeited the seals, stamps or marks of any authority or who have made use of counterfeited seals, stamps or marks;
- c) counterfeited letterheads or official printed matter in use in the assemblies established by the Constitution, public administrations or the various courts, who have sold, peddled or distributed them, or who have made use of papers or printed matter thus counterfeited;
- d) counterfeited or falsified postage stamps, franking impressions or reply coupons issued by the Chadian postal administration and revenue stamps, who have knowingly sold, peddled, distributed or used said counterfeited or falsified stamps, impressions or reply coupons.

###### Art.252.

‐ Anyone who, having unduly procured or printed genuine seals, hammers, marks, stamps provided for in article 250 has made or attempted to make a fraudulent application or use thereof, shall be punished by the penalties provided for in said article.

###### Art.253.‐ Shall be punished with imprisonment from one month to six months and a fine of

50,000 to 150,000 FCFA, those who:

- a) shall have manufactured, sold, hawked or distributed any printed objects or forms obtained by any process whatsoever, which, by their external form, would present with coins or banknotes having legal tender in Chad or abroad, with annuity certificates, vignettes and stamps of the postal and telecommunications service or of State agencies, shares, bonds, interests, dividend or interest coupons related thereto and generally with fiduciary securities issued by the State, territorial collectivities and public establishments as well as by companies or private enterprises, a resemblance likely to facilitate the acceptance of said objects, printed materials or forms, in lieu and place of the imitated securities;
- b) shall have manufactured, sold, hawked, distributed or used printed materials which, by their format, color, text, typographical arrangement or any other characteristic, would present, with letterheads or official printed materials in use in the assemblies instituted by the Constitution, public administrations and related jurisdictions, a resemblance likely to cause confusion in the mind of the public;
- c) shall have knowingly made use of postage stamps or revenue stamps that have already been used, as well as those who shall have, by any means, altered stamps with the aim of removing them from cancellation and thus permitting their reuse;
- d) shall have overprinted, by printing, perforation or any other means, postage stamps or other postal fiduciary securities, expired or not, with the exception of operations prescribed by the postal office for its account, as well as those who shall have sold, hawked, offered, distributed, exported, postage stamps thus overprinted;
- e) shall have counterfeited, imitated or altered vignettes, stamps, postage impressions, or reply coupons issued by the postal service of a foreign country who shall have sold, hawked or distributed said vignettes, stamps, postage impressions or reply coupons or who shall have made use of them;
- f) shall have counterfeited, imitated or altered national or foreign postal identity cards, subscription cards remaining at the post office, who shall have sold, hawked or distributed said cards or shall have made use of them.

###### Art.254.‐ The legal provisions relating to the prohibition of certain rights provided for in article

28 of this Code may be applied to persons convicted under the articles contained in sections 1 and 2 of this chapter.

##### Section 3 ‐ Forgery and use of forgery in public and authentic instruments

###### Art.255.‐ Is punished with imprisonment from fifteen to twenty years any public official or officer

who, in the exercise of his duties, commits a forgery:

- a) by false signature;
- b) by alteration of instruments, writings or signatures;
- c) by substitution of persons;
- d) by writings made or inserted in registers or other public instruments, after their preparation or closure.

###### Art.256.‐ Is punished with imprisonment from fifteen to twenty years any public official or officer

who, in drafting instruments of his ministry, shall have fraudulently misrepresented the substance or circumstances thereof, either by writing agreements other than those which would have been set down or dictated by the parties, or by recording as true facts which are false, or as acknowledged facts which were not.

###### Art.257.‐ Is punished with imprisonment from fifteen to twenty years, any person who commits a

forgery in authentic or public writing:

- a) by counterfeiting or alteration of writings or signatures;
- b) by fabrication of agreements, provisions, obligations or discharges, or by their subsequent insertion in these instruments;
- c) by addition or alteration of clauses, declarations or facts which these instruments were intended to receive and record, shall be punished with the penalty of hard labor for a fixed term.

Shall be punished with the same penalty, any military administrator or accountant who shall knowingly enter on the rolls, status statements or review statements, men, animals, materiel or days of presence beyond the actual strength.

###### Art.258.‐ Whoever shall have knowingly made use of false instruments shall be punished with the penalty

provided for the forger.

###### Art.259.‐ The complementary penalties provided for in article 28 of this Code may be

applied to persons convicted under this section.

##### Section 4 ‐ Forgeries committed in certain administrative documents, in travel

orders and certificates

###### Art.260.‐ Whoever shall have counterfeited, falsified or attempted to counterfeit, falsify or alter a

hunting permit, certificate, booklet, card, bulletin, receipt, passport, pass or other document issued by public administrations with a view to establishing a right, an identity or a qualification or to grant an authorization, shall be punished with imprisonment from three months to three years and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA.

The same penalties shall be applied to:

- a) whoever shall have made use of said documents when certain of their statements relating to the interested party have ceased to be accurate;
- b) whoever shall have made use of one of these documents when certain of their statements relating to the interested party have ceased to be accurate.

###### Art.261.‐ Whoever shall have obtained or shall have attempted to obtain improperly one of the

documents provided for in the preceding article, either by making false declarations, or by taking a false name or false qualification, or by providing false information, certificates or attestations, shall be punished with the penalties provided for in said article.

The same penalties shall be applied to whoever shall have made use of a document obtained under the conditions referred to in the preceding paragraph or established under a name other than his own.

The public official who shall have issued or caused to be issued a false document of the kind specified in this article to a person whom he knows has no right to it shall be punished with the same penalties. The complementary penalties provided for in article 28 of this Code may in addition be applied to him.

###### Art.262.

‐ Innkeepers who, knowingly, shall enter in their registers, under false or assumed names, persons lodging with them, or who, in connivance with them, shall have omitted to register them, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten days to three months.

###### Art.263.‐ Public officers who shall issue or cause to be issued a passport to a person

whom they do not know personally without having had his names and qualifications attested by two citizens known to them, shall be punished with imprisonment from one month to six months.

If the public officer, informed of the assumed name, has nevertheless issued or caused to be issued the passport under the assumed name, he shall be punished with one to five years' imprisonment.

###### Art.264.‐ Whoever manufactures a false travel order, or falsifies an originally genuine travel order,

or makes use of a manufactured or falsified travel order, is punished with imprisonment from six months to two years.

###### Art.265.‐ The penalties set forth in the preceding article are applied to any person who has

a travel order issued by the public officer under a name other than his own.

If the public officer was informed of the assumed name, when he issued the travel order, he is punished with imprisonment from one to five years.

###### Art.266.‐ Any person who, to exempt himself or free another from any public service

whatsoever, manufactures, in the name of a physician or surgeon a certificate of illness or infirmity is punished with imprisonment from one to three years.

###### Art.267.‐ Outside the case of active and passive corruption provided for in articles 192 and 193 of this

Code and without prejudice to the provisions relating to medical confidentiality, any doctor, dental surgeon, midwife or duly authorized health service agent who, in the exercise of their duties and to favor someone, falsely certifies or conceals the existence of diseases or infirmities or a state of pregnancy, or provides false information on the origin of a disease or infirmity or the cause of death, is punished by imprisonment from one to three years.

###### Art.268.‐ Whoever manufactures, under the name of a public official or officer, a certificate

of good conduct, indigence or other circumstances likely to call the benevolence of the Government or individuals upon the designated person, and to procure them a position, credits or assistance, is punished by imprisonment from six months to two years.

The same penalty is applied to:

- a) whoever falsifies a certificate of this kind, originally genuine, to attribute it to a person other than the one to whom it was originally issued;
- b) any individual who uses the certificate thus manufactured or falsified.

If this certificate is manufactured under the name of a simple private individual, the manufacture and use are punished by fifteen days to six months of imprisonment.

###### Art.269.‐ Will be punished by imprisonment from six months to two years without prejudice to

the application, where applicable, of the heavier penalties provided for by this Code and special laws, whoever:

- a) will have knowingly established an attestation or certificate stating materially inaccurate facts;
- b) will have falsified or modified in any manner whatsoever an attestation or certificate originally genuine;
- c) will have knowingly made use of an inaccurate or falsified attestation or certificate.

###### Art.270.

‐ False certificates of any nature, from which could result either injury to third parties or prejudice to the public treasury, will be punished according to the provisions contained in the first section of this chapter.

###### Art.271.‐ Whoever will have taken the name of a third party in circumstances which determined or

which could have determined the inscription of a conviction in the criminal record of the latter will be punished by six months to five years of imprisonment and from 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA in fine, without prejudice to prosecutions to be eventually exercised for forgery.

Will be punished by the same penalty whoever, by false declarations relating to the civil status of an accused person, will have knowingly been the cause of the inscription of a conviction on the criminal record of another than that accused person.

###### Art.272.‐ Whoever, by taking a false name or a false capacity, will have had an extract

from the criminal record of a third party issued to them, will be punished by one month to one year of imprisonment. Whoever, by providing imaginary identity information about themselves, will cause erroneous mentions in the criminal record will be punished by one to six months of imprisonment.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ False testimony and related offenses

###### Art.273.‐ Is guilty of false testimony in criminal or correctional matters and is punished

by imprisonment from one to ten years, whoever, testifying as a witness in a criminal or correctional procedure, affirms as false a fact which they know to be true or as true a fact contrary to reality.

False testimony is punishable by imprisonment from one month to one year and a fine of 5,000 to 100,000 FCFA, when the procedure constitutes a simple police procedure.

In case of false testimony in a civil, social, commercial or administrative jurisdictional procedure, the penalty is imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

###### Art.274.‐ Whoever, either during a procedure and in any case, or in any matter with

a view to a claim or defense in court, uses promises, offers or presents, pressure, threats, acts of violence, maneuvers or artifices to induce another to make or deliver a false deposition, declaration or attestation, will be, whether this subornation has produced its effect or not, punished by imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine of 100,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA.

The interpreter who, in criminal, correctional or civil matters, will have in bad faith distorted the substance of words or documents orally translated, will be punished by the penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above.

###### Art.275.‐ The one to whom an oath will have been deferred or referred in civil matters and who will have made a false

oath will be liable to imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA.

###### Art.276.‐ The subornation of an interpreter will be punished like the subornation of witnesses according to the

provisions of article 274 above.

###### Art.277.‐ Will be punished by imprisonment from fifteen days to two years whoever, knowing

the proof of the innocence of a person accused, charged or convicted for a crime or offense, will not have brought testimony thereof to the competent authorities.

###### Art.278.‐ Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding article, no penalty will be pronounced

against whoever will have brought their testimony belatedly but spontaneously.

###### Art.279.‐ The complementary penalties provided for in article 28 of this Code will be

applicable to the offenses provided for in this chapter.

### Title 6 ‐ Piracy

#### Chapter 1 ‐ Unlawful acts against civil aviation

###### Art.280.‐ Is guilty of an unlawful act against the security of civil aviation and is punished by

life imprisonment, anyone who commits an act contrary to the requirements of civil aviation regulations and liable to compromise or compromising the security of an aircraft in flight or on the ground, persons or property on board.

Is also guilty of an unlawful act against the security of civil aviation and punished by life imprisonment, anyone who:

- a) seizes by violence or threat of violence an aircraft in flight, in service or out of service or exercises control over it;
- b) destroys or seriously damages facilities of a national or international airport serving civil aviation;
- c) interferes with or disrupts air navigation services;
- d) destroys or seriously damages aircraft parked at a Chadian airport or in service;
- e) makes threats of any kind or commits acts of unlawful interference directed against passengers, crews, ground personnel or the public;
- f) performs against a person or property, in an airport, an act of violence which causes or is liable to cause serious injury or death to said person or the destruction of said property, or if such act compromises or is liable to compromise security in that airport;
- g) destroys or damages air navigation aids;
- h) communicates false information liable to compromise the security of an aircraft in flight;
- i) introduces or participates in the introduction on board an aircraft, of devices or substances liable to destroy or damage the aircraft or to endanger the flight;
- j) uses an aircraft in service for the purpose of causing death, or causing serious bodily injury or serious damage to property or the environment;
- k) manufactures or transports unmarked explosives, except where these are authorized military devices within the meaning of the convention on the marking of plastic explosives and sheet explosives for the purpose of detection.

Is also guilty of an unlawful act against the security of civil aviation and punished by life imprisonment, any person who, using a device, a substance or a weapon

• a) performs against a person, in an airport serving national or international civil aviation, an act of violence which causes or is liable to cause serious injury or death;

• b) destroys or seriously damages the facilities of an airport serving national or international civil aviation or aircraft which are not in service and which are located in the airport, or disrupts the services of the airport, if such act compromises or is liable to compromise security in that airport.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Unlawful acts against maritime navigation and fixed platforms

###### Art.281.‐ Is guilty of an offence against the security of maritime navigation and is punished by

life imprisonment, anyone who commits an act contrary to the requirements of regulations relating to the security of maritime navigation liable to compromise or compromising the security of a ship at sea or in a port, persons or property on board, or good order and discipline on board.

Is also guilty of an offence against the security of maritime navigation and punished by life imprisonment anyone who, unlawfully:

- a) seizes by violence or threat of violence a ship under way, in service or out of service or exercises control over it;
- b) destroys or seriously damages facilities of a national or international port serving maritime navigation;
- c) destroys or seriously damages ships stationed in a port or in service;
- d) makes threats of any kind or commits acts of unlawful interference directed against passengers, crews, ground personnel or the public;
- e) performs against a person and or property in a port, an act of violence which causes or is liable to cause serious injury or death to said person or the destruction of said property, if such act compromises or is liable to compromise security in that port;
- f) destroys or damages maritime navigation aids;
- g) communicates false information liable to compromise the security of navigation of a ship;
- h) introduces or participates in the introduction on board a ship, of devices or substances liable to destroy or damage the ship or its cargo or to endanger the navigation of the ship;
- i) manufactures or transports unmarked explosives, except where these are authorized military devices within the meaning of the convention on the marking of plastic explosives and sheet explosives for the purpose of detection.

###### Art.282.‐ Is guilty of an offence against the security of fixed platforms and is punished by

life imprisonment anyone who:

- a) seizes a platform or exercises control over it, by violence or threat of violence;
- b) destroys a platform or causes it damage liable to compromise its security;
- c) performs an act of violence against a person on board a platform, if such act is liable to compromise the security of the platform;
- d) places or causes to be placed on a platform, by any means whatsoever, a device or substance capable of destroying the platform or liable to compromise its security;
- e) causes injury or death to a person, where such facts are connected to offences provided for in points a, b, c and d above.

###### Art.283.‐ Is guilty of an offence against the security of fixed platforms and is punished by

life imprisonment, any person who:

- a) uses against or on board a ship or fixed platform, or discharges from a ship or fixed platform, radioactive materials, explosives or BCN weapons, in a manner which causes or is liable to cause death or serious damage;
- b) discharges, from a ship or fixed platform, hydrocarbons, liquefied natural gas, or other harmful or potentially dangerous substances, which are not covered by paragraph a), in a quantity or concentration, which causes or is liable to cause serious bodily or material damage;
- c) uses a ship in a manner which causes death or serious bodily or material damage;
- d) causes an act which, by its nature or context, is intended to intimidate a population or to compel a Government, an international or intergovernmental organization to perform or refrain from performing any act whatsoever.

Is punished by the same penalty, any person who threatens to commit one of the offences provided for in paragraph 1.

###### Art.284.‐ The public prosecution for offences provided for in this title is

not subject to any statute of limitations.

## Book 3 ‐ War crimes

### Title 1 ‐ War crimes resulting from grave breaches of the Geneva Conventions

of 12 August 1949

###### Art.285.‐ Is guilty of war crime and punished by life imprisonment anyone who, in time

of international armed conflict, commits the following grave breaches, when they target persons or property protected by the provisions of the Geneva Conventions of 12 August

• a) assassination;

• b) murder;

• c) torture or inhuman treatment, including biological experiments;

• d) the act of intentionally causing great suffering or seriously injuring the physical or mental integrity or health;

• e) the destruction and appropriation of property, not justified by military necessity and executed on a large scale in an unlawful and arbitrary manner;

• f) the compulsion of a prisoner of war or any other protected person to serve in the armed forces of an enemy power;

• g) the act of intentionally depriving a prisoner of war or any other protected person of their right to be tried regularly and impartially;

• h) the deportation, detention or illegal transfer of persons;

• i) the taking of hostages.

### Title 2 ‐ War crimes resulting from violations of laws and customs applicable to

international armed conflicts

###### Art.286.‐ Is guilty of a war crime and is punished with life imprisonment, whoever

commits the serious violations, hereinafter specified, of the laws and customs applicable to international armed conflicts within the established framework of international law:

- a) intentional attacks against the civilian population in general or against civilians not taking direct part in hostilities;
- b) intentional attacks against civilian objects that do not constitute military objectives;
- c) intentional attacks against personnel, installations, material, units or vehicles employed in a humanitarian aid or peacekeeping mission in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations, insofar as they are entitled to the protection that international law of armed conflicts guarantees to civilians and civilian objects;
- d) intentional attacks, knowing that they will incidentally cause loss of life and injury to civilian populations, damage to civilian objects or extensive, long-term and severe damage to the natural environment, which would be excessive in relation to the concrete and direct military advantage anticipated;
- e) intentional attacks against works or installations containing dangerous forces, knowing that such attack will cause loss of life, injury to civilians or damage to civilian objects, which would be excessive in relation to the concrete and direct military advantage anticipated, resulting in loss of life or serious injury to physical integrity or health;
- f) attacking or bombarding by any means whatsoever towns, villages, dwellings or buildings that are undefended and which do not constitute military objectives, as well as attacking unidentified localities or demilitarized zones;
- g) killing or wounding combatants who have laid down their arms or who no longer have the means to defend themselves and who have surrendered at discretion;
- h) improper use of the flag of truce, the flag or military insignia, the uniform of the enemy or the United Nations as well as the protective distinctive emblems provided for by the Geneva Conventions resulting in loss of life or serious injury;
- i) direct or indirect transfer by an occupying power of parts of its own civilian population into the territory it occupies, or the deportation or transfer within or outside the occupied territory of all or parts of the population of that territory;
- j) any unjustified and deliberate delay in the repatriation of prisoners of war or civilians, resulting in loss of life or serious injury to physical integrity or health;
- k) intentional attacks against buildings dedicated to religion, education, art, science or charitable purposes, historic monuments, hospitals and places where sick or wounded are gathered, provided that such buildings are not military objectives;
- l) the practices of apartheid and other inhuman and degrading practices based on racial discrimination, which give rise to outrages upon personal dignity, resulting in loss of life or serious injury to physical integrity or health;
- m) subjecting persons of the adverse party who have fallen into one's power to mutilations or to medical or scientific experiments of any kind, to removal of tissue or organs for transplantation which are neither justified by medical, dental or hospital treatment nor carried out in the interest of such persons, and which cause death or seriously endanger their life or health;
- n) killing or wounding treacherously individuals belonging to the hostile nation or army;
- o) declaring that no quarter will be given;
- p) destroying or seizing the property of the adverse party unless such destruction or seizure is imperatively demanded by the necessities of war;
- q) declaring the extinction, suspension or inadmissibility in court of the rights and actions of nationals of the adverse party;
- r) compelling nationals of the adverse party to take part in war operations directed against their own country, even if such nationals were in the service of said belligerent before the commencement of hostilities;
- s) pillaging a town or locality, even when taken by assault;
- t) the use of poison or poisoned weapons;
- u) employing asphyxiating, toxic or similar gases or any other similar liquid, material or device;
- v) the use of bullets which expand or flatten easily in the human body, such as bullets with a hard envelope which does not entirely cover the core or is pierced with incisions;
- w) employing weapons, projectiles, materials and methods of combat of a nature to cause superfluous injury or unnecessary suffering or to strike indiscriminately, in violation of the international law of armed conflicts, provided that such weapons, projectiles, materials and methods of combat are the subject of a comprehensive prohibition under customary law or any other relevant instrument of international humanitarian law;
- x) outrages upon personal dignity, in particular humiliating and degrading treatment;
- y) rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, enforced sterilization or any other form of sexual violence constituting a grave breach of the Geneva Conventions;
- z) utilizing the presence of a civilian or a protected person to render certain points, areas or military forces immune from military operations;
- aa) intentional attacks against buildings, material, medical units and means of transport and personnel using, in conformity with international law, the distinctive emblems provided for by the Geneva Conventions;
- bb) intentionally using starvation of civilians as a method of warfare, in particular by depriving them of objects indispensable to their survival, including by intentionally impeding relief supplies provided for by the Geneva Conventions;
- cc) conscripting or enlisting children under the age of 18 into the national armed forces or using them to participate actively in hostilities.

### Title 3 ‐ War crimes resulting from violations of common Article 3 to the

Geneva Conventions of 12 August 1949

###### Art.287.

‐ Is guilty of a war crime and is punished by life imprisonment anyone who, in the event of an armed conflict not of an international character, excluding situations of internal disturbances and tensions such as riots, isolated and sporadic acts of violence or acts of a similar nature, engages in serious violations of international Conventions, in particular common Article 3 of the Geneva Conventions, consisting of any of the acts indicated below, committed against persons who do not take a direct part in hostilities, including members of armed forces who have laid down their arms and persons who have been placed hors de combat by sickness, wounds, detention or any other cause:

- a) violence to life and person, in particular murder of all kinds, mutilation, cruel treatment and torture;
- b) outrages upon personal dignity, in particular humiliating and degrading treatment;
- c) taking of hostages;
- d) the passing of sentences and the carrying out of executions without previous judgment pronounced by a regularly constituted court, affording all the judicial guarantees which are recognized as indispensable.

### Titre 4 ‐ War crimes resulting from violations of the laws and customs applicable to

non-international armed conflicts

###### Art.288.‐ Is guilty of a war crime and is punished by life imprisonment, anyone who

engages in serious violations of the laws and customs applicable to armed conflicts not of an international character, excluding situations of internal disturbances and tensions such as riots, isolated and sporadic acts of violence or acts of a similar nature, that is to say armed conflicts which take place in a protracted manner, in the national territory, between the governmental authorities and organized armed groups or between organized armed groups among themselves, within the framework of international law, violations consisting of any of the following acts:

- a) intentional attacks against the civilian population as such or against civilians not taking direct part in hostilities;
- b) intentional attacks against buildings, material, units and means of medical transport and personnel using, in conformity with international law, the distinctive emblems of the Geneva Conventions;
- c) intentional attacks against personnel, installations, material, units or vehicles involved in a humanitarian assistance or peacekeeping mission in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations as long as they are entitled to the protection given to civilians and civilian objects under the international law of armed conflict;
- d) intentional attacks against buildings dedicated to religion, education, art, science or charitable purposes, historic monuments, hospitals and places where the sick and wounded are collected, provided they are not military objectives;
- e) pillaging a town or place, even when taken by assault;
- f) rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, enforced sterilization or any other form of sexual violence also constituting a serious violation of the Geneva Conventions;
- g) conscripting or enlisting children under the age of 18 years into armed forces or armed groups or using them to participate actively in hostilities;
- h) ordering the displacement or displacing civilian populations for reasons related to the conflict, unless the security of the said populations or imperative military reasons so demand;
- i) killing or wounding treacherously combatant adversaries;
- j) declaring that no quarter will be given;
- k) subjecting persons of another party to the conflict who have fallen into one's power to physical mutilations or to medical or scientific experiments of any kind which are neither justified by the medical, dental or hospital treatment of the person concerned nor carried out in his or her interest, and which cause death to or seriously endanger the health of such person or persons;
- l) destroying or seizing the property of an adversary unless such destruction or seizure is imperatively demanded by the necessities of the conflict;
- m) using poison or poisoned weapons;
- n) employing asphyxiating, poisonous or other gases, and all analogous liquids, materials or devices;
- o) employing bullets which expand or flatten easily in the human body, such as bullets with a hard envelope which does not entirely cover the core or is pierced with incisions.

### Titre 5 ‐ Crimes resulting from other violations of international humanitarian law

###### Art.289.‐ Is punished by life imprisonment anyone who commits, in time of armed conflict, the

following serious violations of treaties on international humanitarian law:

- a) attacking a protected cultural property or under enhanced protection;
- b) using a cultural property under enhanced protection or its immediate surroundings in support of military action;
- c) extensive destruction or appropriation of cultural property protected by international Conventions.

### Titre 6 ‐ Responsibility of military commanders and other hierarchical superiors for

the acts of their subordinates

###### Art.290.‐ The order, even if not carried out, to commit one of the offences provided for in this

book is punishable by the same penalties as the completed offence.

A military commander, a person effectively acting as a military commander or any other hierarchical superior is responsible for the offences provided for in this book, committed by subordinates placed under his or her authority and effective control, to the extent that the hierarchical superior knew or should have, in the circumstances, known that these subordinates were committing or were going to commit these offences and for which he or she did not take all necessary reasonable measures within his or her power to prevent their commission or did not take the sanctions justified by the acts committed or did not refer them to the competent authorities for the purposes of investigation.

The order of a hierarchical superior, military or civilian or of the Government cannot exonerate from his or her responsibility the perpetrator of an offence referred to in this book, unless the following three conditions are met:

- a) the perpetrator of the offence was under a legal obligation to obey orders of the Government or of the hierarchical superior concerned;
- b) the perpetrator of the offence did not know that the order was unlawful;
- c) the order was not manifestly unlawful.

For the purposes of applying the provisions of this book, the order to commit one of the offences referred to therein is always deemed to be manifestly unlawful.

###### Art.291.‐ The public prosecution relating to the offences provided for in this book as well as the penalties

pronounced are imprescriptible.

## Book 4 ‐ Crimes and Offenses against Groups of Persons and Persons

### Title 1 ‐ Crimes against Humanity

###### Art.292.

‐ Is guilty of a crime against humanity and is punished by life imprisonment anyone who, as part of a widespread or systematic attack launched against any civilian population with knowledge, commits one of the following acts:

- a) assassination;
- b) murder;
- c) extermination;
- d) mortal blows;
- e) enslavement;
- f) deportation or forcible transfer of population;
- g) imprisonment or other form of severe deprivation of physical liberty in violation of fundamental provisions of international law;
- h) torture;
- i) rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, enforced sterilization or any other form of sexual violence of comparable gravity;
- j) persecution of any group or any identifiable collectivity on political, racial, national, ethnic, cultural, religious or gender grounds, the term gender being understood as both sexes, male and female according to the context of society, or on the basis of other criteria universally recognized as impermissible under international law, in connection with any act referred to in this paragraph or any war crime or genocide;
- k) enforced disappearance of persons;
- l) the crime of apartheid;
- m) any other inhuman act of a similar character intentionally causing great suffering or serious injury to physical integrity or to physical or mental health.

For the purposes of this article, the following terms mean:

- a) "attack directed against any civilian population" means conduct involving the multiple commission of acts referred to in paragraph I above against any civilian population, pursuant to or in furtherance of a State or organizational policy to commit such attack;
- b) "extermination" means the intentional infliction of conditions of life, such as the deprivation of access to food and medicine, calculated to bring about the destruction of part of a population;
- c) "enslavement" means the exercise of any or all of the powers attaching to the right of ownership over a person, including in the course of trafficking in human beings, in particular women and children;
- d) "deportation or forcible transfer of population" means forced displacement of persons by expulsion or other coercive means from the area in which they are lawfully present, without grounds permitted under international law;
- e) "torture" means the intentional infliction of severe pain or suffering, whether physical or mental, upon a person in the custody or under the control of the accused; except that the meaning of this term does not extend to pain or suffering arising only from, inherent in or incidental to lawful sanctions;
- f) "forced pregnancy" means the unlawful confinement of a woman forcibly made pregnant, with the intent of affecting the ethnic composition of any population or committing other grave violations of international law, this definition shall not in any way be interpreted as affecting national laws relating to pregnancy;
- g) "persecution" means the intentional and severe deprivation of fundamental rights contrary to international law by reason of the identity of the group or collectivity targeted;
- h) "crime of apartheid" means inhumane acts similar to those referred to in paragraph 1 above, committed in the context of an institutionalized regime of systematic oppression and domination by one racial group over any other racial group or groups and committed with the intention of maintaining that regime;
- i) "enforced disappearance of persons" means cases where persons are arrested, detained or abducted by a State or a political organization or with the authorization, support or acquiescence of such State or organization, which then refuses to acknowledge that those persons are deprived of liberty or to reveal their fate or whereabouts, with the intention of removing them from the protection of the law for a prolonged period of time.

###### Art.293.‐ The military commander, a person effectively acting as military commander or any

other superior is responsible for the offenses provided for in this title, committed by subordinates under his or her authority and effective control, insofar as the superior knew or should have known, in the circumstances, that these subordinates were committing or were going to commit these offenses and for which he or she did not take all necessary and reasonable measures within his or her power to prevent their commission or he or she did not take the sanctions justified by the acts committed or he or she did not refer the matter to the competent authorities for the purposes of investigations.

###### Art.291.‐ The order, even if not followed by effect, to commit one of the offenses provided for in this

title, is punished by the same penalties as the completed offense.

The order of a hierarchical superior, military or civilian, or of the Government cannot exonerate the perpetrator of an offense referred to in this title from his or her responsibility, unless the following three conditions are met:

- a) the perpetrator of the offense was under a legal obligation to obey the orders of the Government or the hierarchical superior concerned;
- b) the perpetrator of the offense did not know that the order was unlawful;
- c) the order was not manifestly unlawful.

For the purposes of applying the provisions of this title, the order to commit one of the offenses referred to therein is deemed to be always manifestly unlawful.

###### Art.295.‐ Public prosecution relating to the offenses provided for in this title as well as the sentences

pronounced are not subject to any statute of limitations.

### Title 2 ‐ Genocide

###### Art.296.‐ Is guilty of genocide and is punished by life imprisonment anyone who, with

the intent to destroy, in whole or in part, a national, ethnical, racial, or religious group, or a group determined on the basis of any other arbitrary criterion, commits against members of that group one of the following acts:

- a) assassination;
- b) murder;
- e) serious bodily or mental harm;
- d) deliberately inflicting on the group conditions of life calculated to bring about its physical destruction in whole or in part;
- e) measures intended to prevent births within the group;
- f) forcible transfer of children of the group to another group.

###### Art.297.‐ The military commander, a person effectively acting as military commander or any

other superior is responsible for the offenses provided for in this title, committed by subordinates under his or her authority and effective control, insofar as the superior knew or should have known, in the circumstances, that these subordinates were committing or were going to commit these offenses and for which he or she did not take all necessary and reasonable measures within his or her power to prevent their commission or he or she did not take the sanctions justified by the acts committed or he or she did not refer the matter to the competent authorities for the purposes of investigations.

###### Art.298.‐ The order, even if not carried out, to commit one of the offenses provided for in this

title is punished with the same penalties as the completed offense.

The order of a hierarchical superior, military or civil, or of the Government cannot exonerate from responsibility the perpetrator of an offense referred to in this title, unless the following three conditions are met:

- a) the perpetrator of the offense had a legal obligation to obey the orders of the Government or the hierarchical superior concerned;
- b) the perpetrator of the offense was unaware of the illegal nature of the order;
- c) the order was not manifestly illegal.

For the purposes of applying the provisions of this title, the order to commit one of the offenses referred to therein is deemed always manifestly illegal.

###### Art.299.‐ The public action relating to the offenses provided for in this title as well as the sentences

imposed are not subject to statutory limitations.

### Titre 3 ‐ Violations of International Treaties on Weapons

###### Art.300.‐ 1°) Shall be punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of

5,000,000 to 15,000,000 FCFA or one of these two penalties only, anyone who:

- a) develops, manufactures, acquires, stockpiles or retains chemical weapons or transfers, directly or indirectly, chemical weapons to anyone;
- b) uses chemical weapons;
- c) undertakes any military preparations for the use of chemical weapons;
- d) uses riot control agents as means of warfare;
- e) possesses a chemical weapons production facility, constructs a new chemical weapons facility or modifies an existing facility to convert it into a chemical weapons production facility;
- f) on the territory of a State that is not a party to the Convention, manufactures, acquires, retains, uses or transfers (on the territory of that State) a Schedule 1 chemical;
- g) manufactures, acquires, retains, uses or transfers (on Chadian territory) a Schedule 1 chemical;
- h) exports a Schedule 1 chemical, previously imported into Chad, to a third State;
- i) illegally exports to, or illegally imports from a State not party to the Convention a Schedule 1 or 2 chemical;
- j) illegally exports a Schedule 3 chemical to a State not party to the Convention.

The sentence imposed shall be imprisonment of twenty to thirty years if the acts referred to above have resulted in death.

2°) The offenses mentioned in 1° above committed outside Chadian territory are deemed to have been committed on Chadian territory and Chadian courts have jurisdiction to try them if they are committed by a citizen of Chad, by a legal entity established under the laws of Chad or in any place under the control of the Chadian State.

3°) For the purposes of this article, the following terms mean:

"Chemical weapons": the following elements, taken together or separately:

- a) toxic chemicals and their precursors, except where intended for purposes not prohibited by the Convention, as long as the types and quantities involved are consistent with such purposes;
- b) munitions and devices specifically designed to cause death or other harm through the toxic action of toxic chemicals, defined in paragraph a, that would be released as a result of the use of such munitions and devices;
- c) any equipment specifically designed for use in direct connection with the use of munitions and devices defined in paragraph b.

"Convention": the Convention on the Prohibition of the Development, Production, Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons and on their Destruction of 13 February 1993;

"Toxic chemicals": any chemical which through its chemical action on life processes can cause death, temporary incapacitation or permanent harm to humans or animals. This includes all such chemicals, regardless of their origin, method of production, whether they are produced in facilities, in munitions or elsewhere;

"Schedule 1": Schedule 1 appearing in the Annex to the Convention;

"Schedule 2": Schedule 2 appearing in the Annex to the Convention;

"Schedule 3": Schedule 3 appearing in the Annex to the Convention.

###### Art.301.‐ 1°) Shall be punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 5,000,000

to 15,000,000 FCFA or one of these two penalties only, anyone who engages in:

- a) illicit trafficking in small arms and light weapons, their ammunition and all parts and components that can be used for their manufacture, repair and assembly;
- b) illicit manufacture of small arms and light weapons, their ammunition and all parts and components that can be used for their manufacture, repair and assembly;
- c) illicit possession and use of small arms and light weapons, their ammunition and all parts and components that can be used for their manufacture, repair and assembly
- d) falsification or illicit erasure, removal or illicit alteration of markings on small arms and light weapons, their ammunition and all parts and components that can be used for their manufacture, repair and assembly;
- e) any activity carried out in violation of an embargo on small arms and light weapons, imposed by the United Nations, the African Union, the Economic Community of Central African States or any other relevant organization.

2°) For the purposes of this article, the following terms mean:

"Small arms", individual weapons, including but not limited to: revolvers and automatic or semi-automatic pistols; rifles and carbines, submachine guns, assault rifles and light machine guns.

"Light weapons", crew-served weapons designed to be used by other persons although some may be carried and used by a single person, including but not limited to: heavy machine guns, portable removable or mounted grenade launchers, portable anti-aircraft guns, portable anti-tank guns, portable anti-aircraft missile launchers and mortars with a caliber of less than 100 mm.

### Titre 4 ‐ Attacks on Life and Bodily Integrity

#### Chapitre 1 ‐ Intentional Attacks on Life Art.302.‐

Homicide committed intentionally is qualified as murder.

The perpetrator of murder shall be punished by imprisonment of fifteen to thirty years. Murder shall entail life imprisonment, when it:

- a) has preceded, accompanied or followed another crime;
- b) has had as its object either to prepare, facilitate or execute an offense, or to facilitate the flight or ensure the impunity of the perpetrators or accomplices of that offense.

###### Art.303.

‐ Any murder committed with premeditation or by lying in wait is assassination. Any perpetrator of assassination shall be punished with the penalty of life imprisonment.

###### Art.304.‐ Shall be punished as perpetrators of assassination all criminals, whatever their

designation, who, for the execution of their crimes, employ torture or commit acts of barbarity.

###### Art.305.‐ The murder of legitimate, natural or adoptive fathers or mothers or any other legitimate

ascendant is qualified as parricide.

The perpetrator of parricide shall be punished with the penalty of life imprisonment.

###### Art.306.‐ Any attempt on the life of a person by means of substances that can cause death more

or less promptly is qualified as poisoning, in whatever manner these substances have been used or administered and whatever the consequences. The perpetrator of poisoning shall be punished with the penalty of life imprisonment.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Voluntary attacks on bodily integrity

###### Art.307.‐ Any individual who has voluntarily struck blows or caused injuries or committed

any other violence or assault on the person of another and caused his death, without intent to cause it, shall be punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA.

When there has been premeditation or lying in wait, the penalty shall be ten to twenty years' imprisonment.

When the acts have been committed on the legitimate, natural or adoptive father and mother or on any other legitimate ascendant of the offender, the penalty shall be twenty to thirty years' imprisonment.

###### Art.308.‐ Any individual who has voluntarily struck blows or caused injuries or committed

any other violence or assault on the person of another and caused mutilation, amputation or deprivation of the use of a limb, blindness, loss of an eye or other infirmity, shall be punished by a penalty of four to eight years' imprisonment and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA.

When there has been premeditation or lying in wait, the penalty shall be ten to fifteen years' imprisonment.

###### Art.309.‐ Any individual who has voluntarily struck blows or caused injuries or committed

any other violence or assault on the person of another, if it has resulted in an illness or incapacity for personal work for more than twenty days, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

When there has been premeditation or lying in wait, the penalty shall be five to ten years' imprisonment.

When the acts have been committed on the legitimate, natural or adoptive father and mother or on any other legitimate ascendant of the offender, the penalty of imprisonment shall be twenty to thirty years.

###### Art.310.‐ Any individual who has voluntarily struck blows or caused injuries or committed

any other violence or assault on the person of another, if it has resulted in an illness or incapacity for personal work equal to or less than twenty days, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

When there has been premeditation or lying in wait, the penalty shall be six months to five years' imprisonment and a fine of 50,000 to 200,000 FCFA.

When the acts have been committed on the legitimate, natural or adoptive father and mother or on any other legitimate ascendant of the offender, the penalties shall be doubled.

###### Art.311.‐ Whoever has caused another person an illness or incapacity for personal work

by voluntarily administering to him, in any manner whatsoever, substances which, while not being of a nature to cause death, are harmful to health, shall be punished by five to ten years' imprisonment and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

When the acts have been committed on the legitimate, natural or adoptive father and mother or on any other legitimate ascendant of the offender, the penalties shall be doubled.

###### Art.312.‐ For the purposes of this chapter, premeditation consists in the intent formed, before

the action, to attack the person of a specific individual, even if such intent is dependent on some circumstance or condition.

###### Art.313.‐ For the purposes of this chapter, lying in wait consists in waiting for a more or less extended period

in one or more places for an individual, either to cause his death, or to commit acts of violence against him.

###### Art.314.‐ For the application of articles 302 to 311 above, homicide, violence and assaults

are considered to be voluntary even if the intent of the offender is to strike another person.

###### Art.315.‐ Articles 307 to 310 inclusive do not apply to acts performed during

a sports activity, provided that the perpetrator has complied with the rules of that sport.

#### Chapter 3 ‐ Involuntary attacks on bodily integrity

###### Art.316.

‐ Whoever, through clumsiness, imprudence, inattention, negligence or failure to observe regulations, has involuntarily committed a homicide or has been involuntarily the cause thereof shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to two years and a fine of 100,000 to

1,000,000 FCFA.

###### Art.317.

‐ Whoever, through clumsiness, imprudence, inattention, negligence or failure to observe regulations, has caused another person an incapacity for personal work for more than six days shall be punished by imprisonment of fifteen days to one year and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA.

#### Chapter 4 ‐ Voluntary attacks on the bodily integrity of minors Art.318.‐

Whoever attacks the integrity of the genital organ of a person of the female sex by any of the following means shall be punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 10,000 to

100,000 FCFA:

- a) total or partial removal of the clitoris and or the labia minora;
- b) total removal of the labia majora and minora followed by total or partial suturing or total or partial narrowing of the vaginal opening;
- c) perforation, incision or stretching of the clitoris and or the labia, cauterization, introduction of corrosive substances or plants into the vagina to cause its narrowing or bleeding;
- d) any other process.

The penalty is imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA:

- a) if the death of the victim results therefrom;
- b) if the perpetrator habitually engages in this practice.

The court shall also order the closure of the professional premises and prohibition from practicing the profession in the event the acts are committed either in a medical establishment, or in a care center, or in the capacity of medical or paramedical personnel.

###### Art.319.‐ Whoever uses any process capable of hindering the biological development of any human organ shall be punished by imprisonment of two months to one year and a fine of

25,000 to 50,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties.

###### Art.320.‐ Those who have exposed or caused to be exposed, abandoned or caused to be abandoned a child or an incapacitated adult unable to protect himself by reason of his physical or mental condition, shall for this act alone, be sentenced to imprisonment of six months to five years and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

The penalty is imprisonment of one to ten years and a fine of 10,000 to

500,000 FCFA if the exposure or abandonment has resulted in an illness or incapacity of more than twenty days or mutilation, deprivation of the use of a limb, blindness, loss of an eye or other infirmities.

When the exposure or abandonment has caused the death of the victim, the penalty shall be imprisonment of ten to fifteen years.

#### Chapter 5 ‐ Failure to provide assistance and state of necessity

###### Art.321.‐ Whoever refrains from providing to a person in danger the assistance that, without risk to himself or to third parties, he could provide, either by his personal action or by summoning help, shall be punished by three months to two years' imprisonment.

Any person who, in the event of a calamity or public danger, has, without valid reason, refused or neglected to respond to a request for assistance or to a requisition for aid made by the competent public authority, shall be punished by the penalty provided for in paragraph 1 above.

###### Art.322.‐ There is no offense when injuries or violence are justified by the

immediate necessity of avoiding a greater harm to the victim.

### Title 4 ‐ On Torture

###### Art.323.‐ Anyone who, through torture, causes the death of another without intention to kill shall be punished by imprisonment of twenty to thirty years.

The penalty is imprisonment of ten to twenty years when the torture causes the victim permanent loss of use of all or part of a limb, an organ or a sense.

The penalty is imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 300,000 to

1,000,000 FCFA when the torture causes the victim an illness or incapacity to work exceeding thirty days.

The penalty is imprisonment of two to five years and a fine of 100,000 to

500,000 FCFA when the torture causes the victim either an illness or incapacity to work equal to or less than thirty days, or mental or moral pain or suffering. Any public official, traditional authority or any other person acting in an official capacity who tacitly or expressly consents, orders or commits other acts constituting cruel, inhuman or degrading punishment or treatment that are not acts of torture as defined in this article shall be punished by the same penalties.

For the purposes of this article, the term "torture":

- a) means any act by which severe pain or suffering, physical, mental or moral, is intentionally inflicted on a person, by a public official or traditional authority acting in an official capacity or at their instigation or with their express or tacit consent, for such purposes as obtaining from them or a third person information or a confession, punishing them for an act they or a third person has committed, or is suspected of having committed, intimidating or coercing them or intimidating or coercing a third person, or for any reason based on any form of discrimination whatsoever;
- b) does not apply to pain or suffering arising from, inherent in or incidental to lawful sanctions.

No exceptional circumstances whatsoever, whether a state of war or a threat of war, internal political instability or any other public emergency, may be invoked to justify torture.

An order from a superior or a public authority may not be invoked to justify torture. The conditions restricting the application of paragraph 1 of article 10 of this Code do not apply to torture.

###### Art.324.‐ Confiscation of property and publication of the judgment may be ordered

against public officials guilty of the offences provided for in article 323 above.

### Title 5 ‐ On Violations of Freedom and Dignity of Persons

###### Art.325.‐ Those who, without order from constituted authorities and except in cases where the law orders the arrest of suspects, have arrested, detained or confined any persons shall be punished by imprisonment of five to ten years.

Anyone who has provided a place to carry out the detention or confinement shall suffer the same penalty.

The penalty shall be reduced to imprisonment of two to five years if the perpetrators of the acts mentioned in paragraph 1 above, not yet actually prosecuted, have released the arrested, confined or detained person before the tenth day completed since the date of arrest, detention or confinement.

###### Art.326.‐ The penalty shall be imprisonment of twenty to thirty years:

• a) when an unlawful arrest has been carried out in a false uniform, under a false name, or on a false order of the public authority;

• b) if the arrested, detained or confined individual has been threatened with death;

• c) if the arrested, detained or confined persons have been subjected to physical torture;

• d) if the confinement was preceded by an abduction;

• e) if the confinement was carried out by its perpetrator with a view to obtaining payment of a ransom;

• f) or when any person seizes or detains another and threatens to kill them, injure them or continue to detain them in order to compel a State, an international or intergovernmental organization, a natural or legal person or a group of persons, to do or abstain from doing any act as an explicit or implicit condition of the release of the hostage.

###### Art.327.‐ Anyone who, to satisfy their personal interest, imposes on another work or service for which they have not offered themselves voluntarily shall be punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties.

###### Art.328.‐ Anyone who deprives a person of their freedom and exercises over them one or more of the attributes inherent in the right of ownership shall be punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 100,000 to

5,000,000 FCFA.

###### Art.329.‐ Anyone who facilitates or arranges the movement of a person in order to derive, directly or indirectly, a financial advantage or any other material advantage, whatever its nature, shall be punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 100,000 to

5,000,000 FCFA.

###### Art.330.‐ Anyone who, with a view to trafficking in a person, carries out their recruitment, transfer, harbouring or reception shall be punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 100,000 to

5,000,000 FCFA.

###### Art.331.‐ The penalties provided for in articles 327, 328, 329 and 330 above are doubled:

• a) in case of threats, use of force or other forms of coercion, abduction, fraud, deception, abuse of authority or taking advantage of a situation of vulnerability or exploitation;

• b) when the perpetrator is a person called upon, by virtue of their functions, to participate in the fight against slavery, trafficking, pledging and the trade in persons or in peacekeeping;

• c) when the offence is committed by an organized gang or by a criminal association;

• d) when the offence is committed with the use of a weapon;

• e) when the victim has suffered injuries as defined in article 307 of this Code or when they have died as a result of acts related to these facts.

###### Art.332.

‐ The perpetrators, co-perpetrators and accomplices of the offences of slavery, trafficking, pledging and trade in persons are, in addition, sentenced to the additional penalties provided for by article 28 of this Code.

###### Art.333.‐ Independently of the criminal liability of their managers, legal persons

may be declared criminally liable and sentenced to the fines provided for in articles 328 to 330 above, when the offences have been committed by said managers, acting in the exercise of or in connection with their functions.

###### Art.334.‐ The sale or purchase, whatever the form, of bones of a person deceased for more than one year constitutes trafficking in human remains.

Anyone who engages in trafficking in human remains shall be punished by imprisonment of two to five years and a fine of 5,000,000 to 10,000,000 FCFA.

###### Art.335.‐ Shall be considered a procurer and punished with imprisonment from six months to two years

and a fine from 100,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA the person who:

- a) in any manner, knowingly aids, assists or protects the prostitution of others or solicitation for the purpose of prostitution;
- b) in any form, shares in the proceeds of the prostitution of others or receives subsidies from a person habitually engaged in prostitution;
- c) knowingly living with a person habitually engaged in prostitution, cannot justify sufficient resources to enable him or her alone to provide for his or her own subsistence;
- d) hires, entices or maintains, even with consent, a person even of age, for the purpose of prostitution, or delivers that person to prostitution or debauchery;
- e) acts as intermediary, in any capacity whatsoever, between persons engaged in prostitution or debauchery and individuals who exploit or remunerate the prostitution or debauchery of others.

###### Art.336.‐ The penalty shall be imprisonment from two to five years and a fine from

100,000 to 2,000,000 FCFA in cases where:

- a) the offence was committed against a minor;
- b) the offence was accompanied by coercion, abuse of authority or rape;
- c) the perpetrator of the offence was carrying a visible or concealed weapon;
- d) the perpetrator of the offence was the spouse, father, mother or guardian of the victim, teacher or paid servant of the victim or in any capacity has authority over the victim;
- e) the perpetrator of the offence is called upon to participate, by virtue of his or her functions, in combating prostitution, protecting health or maintaining public order.

### Titre 6 ‐ Offences against the tranquility and honor of persons

#### Chapitre 1 ‐ Violations of domicile

###### Art.337.

‐ Any official of the administrative or judicial order, any officer or agent of the police, any commander or agent of the public force, who, acting in that capacity, has entered the domicile of a citizen against the latter's wishes, outside the cases provided for by law, and without the formalities it has prescribed, shall be punished with imprisonment from six days to one year and a fine from 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

Any individual who has entered the domicile of a citizen by means of threats or violence shall be punished with imprisonment from six days to six months and a fine from 25,000 to

100,000 FCFA.

#### Chapitre 2 ‐ Interference with correspondence

###### Art.338.‐ Is punished with imprisonment from fifteen days to one year and a fine from

10,000 to 100,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties anyone who, without the authorization of the addressee, suppresses or opens the correspondence of another.

This article is not applicable to spouses or to the father, mother, guardian or customary guardian with respect to unemancipated minor children for whom they have responsibility.

#### Chapitre 3 ‐ Threats

###### Art.339.

‐ Anyone who has threatened another person, by anonymous or signed writing, image, symbol or emblem, with assassination, poisoning or any other attack against persons provided for in articles 302, 308, 309 and 310 of this Code shall, whether or not the threat was made with an order to deposit a sum of money in a designated place, or to fulfill any other condition, be punished with imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

###### Art.340.‐ Anyone who has, by one of the means provided for in the preceding articles, threatened another person with

assault or violence not provided for in the preceding article, whether or not the threat was made with an order or under a condition, shall be punished with imprisonment from six days to six months and a fine from 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

If the threat was verbal, the offender shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years and a fine from 10,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

#### Chapitre 4 ‐ Sexual harassment and degrading treatment

###### Art.341.‐ Commits the offence of sexual harassment and is punished with imprisonment from six months to two

years and a fine from 100,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA, anyone who imposes on a person, repeatedly, remarks or conduct with sexual connotations that either, infringe upon that person's dignity by reason of their degrading or humiliating nature, or create against that person an intimidating, hostile or offensive situation.

Is assimilated to sexual harassment the fact, even non-repeated, of using any form of serious pressure with the real or apparent aim of obtaining an act of a sexual nature, whether it is sought for the benefit of the perpetrator or for the benefit of a third party.

The penalties are imprisonment from one to three years and a fine from 200,000 to 2,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, if the acts are committed:

- a) by a person who abuses the authority conferred by his or her functions;
- b) on a person under eighteen years of age;
- c) on a particularly vulnerable person, by reason of an illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or pregnancy, when this situation is known to the perpetrator or is apparent;
- d) on a particularly vulnerable or dependent person by reason of the precariousness of his or her economic or social situation, known to the perpetrator or apparent;
- e) by several persons acting in co-action or when one is the perpetrator and the other an accomplice.

###### Art.342.‐ Is punished with imprisonment from six months to three years and a fine from

20,000 to 200,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties anyone who:

- a) in any manner whatsoever, without infringing upon the physical integrity of his or her spouse or a person, subjects said spouse or said person to degrading, humiliating or inhuman treatment of a nature to cause psychological disturbance, trauma or mental affliction;
- b) exercises violence of the same nature on his or her spouse, concubine, a relative or a relative by marriage.

The same penalties are applicable when the acts of violence are committed on persons other than those referred to in paragraph 1 above, with a view to psychologically affecting those referred to therein.

The penalties referred to in paragraph 1 are doubled when the acts are:

- a) committed on a pregnant woman;
- b) committed in the context of widowhood rites, on a widower or on a widow;
- c) accompanied by deprivation of food, destruction or confiscation of the victim's personal effects;
- d) accompanied by deprivation of any other right or prerogative related to the victim's status.

#### Chapitre 5 ‐ False accusation

###### Art.343.‐ Anyone who has, by any means whatsoever, made a false accusation

against one or more individuals to officers of justice or of administrative or judicial police, or to any authority having the power to take action thereon or to refer the matter to the competent authority, or to the hierarchical superiors or employers of the accused, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine from

50,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

If the reported fact is subject to criminal or disciplinary sanction, proceedings may be initiated under this article either after judgment or decision of acquittal or discharge or after order or decision of no case to answer, or after dismissal of the accusation by the magistrate, official, superior authority or employer competent to give it the follow-up it was capable of receiving.

The court seised under this article shall be required to stay the proceedings if proceedings concerning the reported fact are pending.

#### Chapter 6 ‐ Defamation and insult committed otherwise than through the press

###### Art.344.‐ Is punished with a fine of 50,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA anyone who, outside the channels

provided for by texts relating to the press, whether by gestures, words or cries uttered in public or in places open to the public, damages the honor or consideration of a person by directly or indirectly imputing to them facts of which they cannot provide proof.

The truth of the imputation may be proven except:

- a) when it concerns the private life of the victim;
- b) when it refers to a fact dating back more than ten years;
- c) when it refers to a fact constituting an amnestied offense or to a fact having been the subject of a conviction otherwise erased.

Prosecution may only be initiated upon complaint by the victim or their legal representative. Until final conviction, withdrawal of the complaint stops the exercise of public prosecution. This article is applicable to defamation directed against the memory of a deceased person when the author of the defamation intended to damage the honor or consideration of the heirs, spouse or universal legatees still living.

Penalties are reduced by half if the defamation is not public.

###### Art.345.‐ Do not constitute the offense of defamation, without prejudice to

the application of legal provisions relating to the press:

- a) speeches made within the National Assembly as well as reports or any other document printed by order of the National Assembly;
- b) the account of public sessions of the National Assembly made in good faith;
- c) judicial debates, speeches delivered or writings produced before the courts;
- d) the faithful and good faith account of these debates and speeches, with the exception of defamation trials;
- e) the publication of judicial decisions, including those rendered in defamation matters;
- f) the official report made in good faith by a person duly designated to conduct an investigation and within the scope of that investigation;
- g) the imputation made in good faith by a hierarchical superior about their subordinate;
- h) information given in good faith about a person to a third party who has a personal or official interest in knowing it or who has the power to remedy an alleged injustice;
- i) criticism of a work, a performance, an opinion of any kind publicly expressed, provided that such criticism does not reflect personal animosity;
- j) historical work done in good faith.

###### Art.346.‐ Is punished with imprisonment of fifteen days to six months and a fine of

25,000 to 50,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties anyone who, outside the channels provided for by texts relating to the press, whether by gestures, words or cries uttered in places open to the public and without having been provoked, uses against a person an offensive expression, a gesture, a term of contempt or an invective that does not contain the imputation of any fact.

Prosecution may only be initiated upon complaint by the victim or their legal representative. Until final conviction, withdrawal of the complaint stops the exercise of public prosecution. This article is applicable to insult made to the memory of a deceased person.

#### Chapter 7 ‐ Extortions

###### Art.347.‐ Anyone who has extorted or attempted to extort by force, violence or coercion, the

signature or delivery of a writing, an act, a title, any document whatsoever, containing or effecting an obligation, disposition or discharge, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to ten years.

###### Art.348.

‐ Anyone who, by means of threat, written or verbal, of revelations or defamatory imputations, has extorted or attempted to extort, either the delivery of funds or securities, or the signature or delivery of the writings enumerated above, shall be punished, for blackmail, with imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 100,000 to

1,000,000 FCFA.

### Title 7 ‐ Sexual offenses

#### Chapter 1 ‐ Rape

###### Art.349.

‐ Constitutes rape, and punished with eight to fifteen years, any act of sexual penetration, of whatever nature, committed on the person of another by violence, coercion, threat or surprise.

Attempted rape is punished with the same penalty provided for in paragraph 1 above.

###### Art.350.‐ The penalty is imprisonment of ten to twenty years when the rape:

• a) has resulted in mutilation or permanent infirmity;

• b) was committed on a minor under eighteen years of age;

• c) was committed on a particularly vulnerable person due to illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or pregnancy and this condition is apparent or known to the perpetrator;

• d) was committed by an ascendant or by any other person having legal or de facto authority over the victim;

• e) was committed by a person who abuses the authority conferred upon them by their functions;

• f) was committed by several persons acting in co-action or one as perpetrator and the other as accomplice;

• g) was committed with use or threat of a weapon;

• h) was committed through contact of the victim with the perpetrator and by using, for the dissemination of messages to an undetermined public, an electronic communication network;

• i) was committed by reason of the victim's sexual orientation;

• j) was committed in conjunction with one or more other rapes committed on other victims;

• k) was committed by a person acting in a state of intoxication or under the influence of narcotic drugs;

• l) the penalty is imprisonment of fifteen to twenty years if the rape was preceded, accompanied or followed by acts of torture.

The penalty is imprisonment of twenty to thirty years when the rape has resulted in the death of the victim. In the cases provided for in points d) and e) above, the court may deprive the convicted person of parental authority and any possibility of being a guardian or curator for a period of ten years.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Other offenses against decency

###### Art.351.‐ Any exhibition likely to offend decency, made publicly or in the presence of

involuntary witnesses, is a public offense against decency and is punished with imprisonment of three months to two years and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

###### Art.352.‐ Is punished with imprisonment of one to five years, anyone who engages in sexual

relations with an animal or on a corpse.

Is punished with imprisonment of five to ten years, anyone who, using physical or psychological violence, forces a person to engage in the practices referred to in the preceding paragraph.

###### Art.353.‐ Is punished with imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA, anyone who has sexual relations with their legitimate, natural, full, consanguine or uterine brother or sister.

Is punished with the same penalty, anyone who has sexual relations with their brother-in-law or sister-in-law, their son or daughter, the son or daughter of their brother or sister, their mother or father. Penalties are doubled when the offender is assisted by one or more other persons. Is punished with imprisonment of five to ten years anyone who, using physical or psychological violence, forces a person to have sexual relations with their collaterals referred to in paragraph I of this article.

###### Art.354.‐ Is punished with imprisonment of three months to two years and a fine of

50,000 to 500,000 FCFA, anyone who has sexual relations with persons of their own sex.

### Title 8 ‐ Offences against the person or status of the child

#### Chapter 1 ‐ Infanticide

###### Art.355.‐ Infanticide is the murder or assassination of a newborn child.

The mother, principal author or accomplice to the assassination or murder of her newborn child shall be punished by imprisonment of five to ten years, without this provision being applicable to her co-authors or accomplices.

#### Chapter 2 ‐ Abortion

###### Art.356.

‐ Whoever, by food, beverages, medications, maneuvers, violence or by any other means shall have procured or attempted to procure the abortion of a pregnant or supposedly pregnant woman, whether she consented or not, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

The imprisonment shall be five to ten years and the fine 100,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA, if it is established that the offender habitually engaged in the acts referred to in the preceding paragraph.

Shall be punished by imprisonment of two months to two years and a fine of 25,000 to

50,000 FCFA, the woman who shall have procured an abortion for herself or attempted to procure one, or who shall have consented to use the means indicated or administered to her for this purpose. Doctors, midwives, dental surgeons, pharmacists, as well as medical students, pharmacy students or employees, herbalists, bandage makers, sellers of surgical instruments, nurses, masseurs, who shall have indicated, facilitated or practiced the means of procuring abortion, shall be sentenced to the penalties provided for in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this article.

The suspension for at least five years and the absolute inability to practice their profession shall, in addition, be pronounced against the offenders.

Whoever violates the prohibition to practice their profession pronounced pursuant to the preceding paragraph, shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to two years or a fine of 100,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA.

###### Art.357.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 100,000 to

2,000,000 FCFA, whoever, by violence against a pregnant woman or against the child being born, causes, even unintentionally, the death or permanent incapacity of the child.

###### Art.358.‐ The provisions of the two preceding articles are not applicable if the facts are

carried out by an authorized person and are justified by the necessity to save the mother from a serious peril to her health.

Medicalized abortion is authorized in case of sexual assault, rape, incest or when the pregnancy endangers the mental or physical health or the life of the mother or that of the fetus. The authorization to abort is issued by the Public Prosecutor after certification by a doctor of the materiality of the facts.

#### Chapter 3 ‐ Sexual offences committed against minors

###### Art.353.

‐ Commits a sexual assault and shall be punished by two to ten years of imprisonment, whoever without violence, maintains a sexual relationship or practices touching of a sexual nature on the person of a child of either sex under the age of thirteen. When the assault has been consummated or attempted with violence, it shall be punished by the penalties for rape.

###### Art.360.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment of one to three years and a fine of 100,000 to 500,000

FCFA, whoever, without violence, maintains a sexual relationship or practices touching of a sexual nature on a person of the same sex under the age of eighteen.

###### Art.361.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of two to five years and a fine of 25,000 to 200,000

FCFA, whoever commits an indecent act in the presence of a minor under the age of sixteen.

The penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above are doubled if the indecent act is committed with violence or if the perpetrator is:

- a) a person having authority over the victim or having legal custody;
- b) a civil servant or a Minister of worship;
- c) a person assisted by several others.

In all cases, the court may deprive the convicted person of parental authority and of any possibility of being a guardian or curator for a period of ten years.

###### Art.362.

‐ Constitute child pornography, the production, distribution, dissemination, importation, exportation, offer, making available, sale, obtaining or handing over to others, possession of any material representing by any means whatsoever, a child engaging in explicit sexual activities, real or simulated, or representing the sexual organs of a child.

Child pornography is punished by imprisonment of one to four years and a fine of 100,000 to 500,000 FCFA.

###### Art.363.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 25,000 to

1,000,000 FCFA, whoever incites, encourages or facilitates the debauchery of a person who has not reached the age of majority.

The penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above are doubled if the victim is under the age of sixteen. The court may furthermore pronounce additional penalties provided for in article 28 of this Code.

###### Art.364.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of fifteen days to six months and a fine of

25,000 to 100,000 FCFA, whoever, having legal or customary custody of a child under eighteen, allows them to reside in a house or establishment where prostitution is practiced or to work there or to work as an employee for a prostitute.

#### Chapter 4 ‐ Offences in relation to the status and civil status of the child

###### Art.365.‐ Those guilty of acts tending to compromise the civil status of a child, shall be punished by

imprisonment of five to ten years.

###### Art.366.‐ Those guilty of substituting one child for another shall be punished by

imprisonment of two to ten years, without prejudice to the penalties provided for forgery if applicable. If it is established that the child is not alive, nor viable, the penalty shall be six months to five years of imprisonment.

###### Art.367.

‐ Those guilty of falsely attributing a child to a woman who has not given birth, shall be punished by imprisonment of two to ten years without prejudice to the penalties provided for forgery if applicable.

###### Art.368.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 500,000 to

5,000,000 FCFA, whoever:

- a) compels by any means whatsoever, celebrates or gives their authorization to the celebration of the marriage of a person of either sex who has not yet reached the legal age of marriage or compels such person to marry;
- b) marries a person who has not yet reached the legal age of marriage.

The court may, in addition, deprive the convicted person of parental authority and of any possibility of being a guardian or curator for a period of ten years.

###### Art.369.‐ Is punished by a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA, the parent who refuses to enroll

their child in school.

Is punished by the penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above:

- a) the head of an educational establishment or training school who refuses to recruit a student on grounds of pregnancy or to reintegrate her into the school cycle after childbirth;
- b) the head of an educational establishment or training school who exercises psychological violence on a pregnant student or on the student who caused a pregnancy, excludes them from the establishment on grounds of pregnancy or compels them for this sole reason, to repeat a grade.

In case of recidivism, the perpetrator of the offence is liable to a penalty of imprisonment of one to two years.

###### Art.370.‐ Whoever shall have facilitated the recruitment or use of children in the forces or

armed groups as well as their use in wars and armed conflicts, shall be punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA.

#### Chapter 5 ‐ Abduction of Minors

###### Art.371.‐ Commits the offense of abduction of a minor and is punished by imprisonment of five to ten

years and a fine of 40,000 to 400,000 FCFA, anyone who, without fraud or violence, abducts, leads away or diverts a minor person under fifteen years of age against the wishes of those to whom legal or customary custody belongs.

The imprisonment is ten to fifteen years when the abduction was committed with violence.

###### Art.372.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 25,000 to

200,000 FCFA anyone who, by fraud or violence, abducts, leads away or diverts a person over fifteen years of age who has not yet reached civil majority, even if he believes them to be older, against the wishes of those to whom legal or customary custody belongs.

###### Art.373.‐ The acts provided for in the two preceding articles are punished by imprisonment of twenty to

thirty years:

- a) if the victim is a minor person under thirteen years of age;
- b) if the offender's purpose is to receive payment of a ransom or has received payment thereof;
- c) when the death of the minor victim results therefrom.

###### Art.374.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one month to two years and a fine of

50,000 to 500,000 FCFA, when custody of a minor has been determined by a court decision, provisional or final, the father, mother or any person who does not return this minor to those who have the right to claim him or who, even without fraud or violence, abducts him, diverts him or has him abducted or diverted from the hands of those to whom his custody has been entrusted or from the places where the latter have placed him.

###### Art.375.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of six months to one year and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000

FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, anyone who obstructs the exercise by a parent of the right of visitation granted to that parent by a court decision regarding the common child or children.

#### Chapter 6 ‐ Access of Minors to Drinking Establishments

###### Art.376.‐ Is punished by a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 FCFA, the seller of beverages

alcoholic who admits into his establishment a minor person under sixteen years of age not accompanied by a person having supervision of him and having already reached civil majority.

###### Art.377.‐ Is punished by the penalty provided for in the preceding article, the seller of beverages who sells or

offers in his establishment or in any other public place alcoholic beverages to a minor person under eighteen years of age and not accompanied by a person having supervision of him and having already reached civil majority.

###### Art.378.‐ Is punished by the same penalty, anyone who gives drink to intoxication to a person

who has not yet reached civil majority.

###### Art.379.‐ In case of recidivism, the perpetrators of the offenses provided for in articles 376 to 378 above

are punished by imprisonment of fifteen days to one month and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000

FCFA.

The court may in addition:

- a) order against the convicted seller, the closure of his establishment under the conditions provided for in article 33 of this Code;
- b) order the publication of its decision;
- c) impose the complementary penalties of article 28 of this Code.

###### Art.380.‐ Articles 376 to 378 above are not applicable to anyone who proves that he was

misled as to the age of the minor or as to the age or capacity of the person accompanying him.

#### Chapter 7 ‐ Crimes and Offenses Against the Family

###### Paragraph 1 ‐ Offenses Against the Status of Persons, Abductions, Concealment,

Suppressions, Substitution, Non-restitution of Children

###### Art.381.

‐ Those guilty of abductions, of concealment, of suppression of a child, tending to compromise his civil status, shall be punished by hard labor for a fixed term.

###### Art.382.‐ Those who, having been entrusted with a child, do not return him to the persons charged with

claiming him, shall be punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding article.

###### Paragraph 2 ‐ Offenses Against Family Authority

##### Section 1 ‐ Abductions and Diversions of Minors

###### Art.383.

‐ Anyone who, without fraud or violence, has abducted or diverted, or attempted to abduct or divert a minor of fifteen years, shall be punished by imprisonment of two to five years and a fine of 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

When a minor female thus abducted or diverted has married her abductor, the latter may be prosecuted only upon complaint of the persons who have standing to request annulment of the marriage and may be convicted only after such annulment has been pronounced.

###### Art.384.

‐ Anyone who has, by fraud or violence, abducted, or had abducted minors, or has had them led away, diverted or displaced from the places where they were placed by those to whose authority or direction they were subject or entrusted, shall undergo the penalty of imprisonment of five to ten years.

The attempt of the offense provided for in this article shall be punished as the offense.

###### Paragraph 3 ‐ Offenses Against the Integrity and Unity of the Family

##### Section 1 ‐ Adultery

###### Art.385.‐ Adultery is punished by imprisonment of two to six months and a fine of 50,000

to 500,000 FCFA.

The accomplice to adultery shall be punished by the same penalties.

###### Art.386.‐ Prosecution may be initiated only upon complaint of the offended spouse and shall be

stopped by his withdrawal.

Spouses guilty of adultery may not file complaints against each other.

###### Art.387.‐ Only the following shall be admitted as evidence of the offenses specified in the preceding article: being caught in the act

and letters, photos and other written evidence emanating from the person against whom they are opposed.

##### Section 2 ‐ Abandonment of Family

###### Art.388.‐ Shall be punished by imprisonment of one month to one year, any person who, in

disregard of an order or judgment having condemned him to pay allowances or alimony to his spouse, to his ascendants or to his descendants, has voluntarily remained more than two months without providing all of the allowances or paying the full amount of the alimony.

The failure to pay is presumed voluntary. Proof to the contrary may not be drawn from insolvency resulting from laziness, drunkenness, habitual misconduct, idleness or voluntary impoverishment of the debtor.

The competent court is that of the place of domicile or residence of the creditor.

### Title 8 ‐ Offenses Against the Confidence of Third Parties

###### Art.389.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 50,000 to

500,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, anyone who abuses a signed blank document entrusted to him to fraudulently write thereon either an obligation, disposition or discharge, or a mention likely to compromise the person or fortune of the signatory.

###### Art.390.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of three months to three years and a fine of

25,000 to 100,000 FCFA, anyone who reveals, without the authorization of the person to whom it belongs, a confidential fact that he learned or that was entrusted to him only by reason of his profession or function. Paragraph I above does not apply either to statements made to judicial authorities or judicial police authorities concerning facts likely to constitute a crime or offense, or to responses in court to any claim whatsoever.

Paragraph 2 does not apply:

- to the physician and surgeon who are always bound by professional secrecy, except within the limit of a legal requisition or an expert commission;
- to the civil servant upon written order of the Government;
- to the Minister of worship and the lawyer;
- to the notary within the limit of a legal requisition or an expert commission.

The court may, in addition, impose the complementary penalties of article 36 of this

Code.

###### Art.391.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from three months to three years and a fine from

100,000 to 5,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, whoever discloses without the authorization of the person to whom it belongs an industrial or commercial fact or process of which he became aware by reason of his employment.

The court may impose the complementary penalties of article 36 of this Code.

###### Art.392.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 100,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA any director, manager, administrator or auditor of a company who, with the aim of misleading one or more partners, shareholders or creditors, makes a false declaration or provides a false account.

The court may furthermore impose the complementary penalties of article 36 of this Code.

###### Art.393.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from three to eight years and a fine from 100,000 to 1,000,000

FCFA, whoever counterfeits or falsifies a private writing bearing obligation, disposition or discharge either in the substance, or in the signatures, dates or attestations. The penalty is imprisonment from five to ten years and a fine from 200,000 to 2,000,000 FCFA, if it concerns either:

- a) a commercial or banking writing;
- b) a document attesting to a land right;
- c) a mandate to sign one of the documents referred to in points a and b above;
- d) a will.

###### Art.394.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from three to eight years and a fine from 100,000 to 1000,000

FCFA, whoever uses a counterfeited or falsified private writing bearing obligation, disposition or discharge either in the substance, or in the signatures, dates or attestations. The penalty is imprisonment from five to ten years and a fine from 200,000 to

2,000,000 FCFA if it concerns either:

- a) a commercial or banking writing;
- b) a document attesting to a land right;
- c) the mandate to sign one of the documents referred to in points a and b;
- d) a will.

###### Art.395.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one month to one year, whoever counterfeits or falsifies a

private certificate or who issues a false certificate not otherwise punished or who uses a counterfeited, falsified or false private certificate.

The penalty provided for in paragraph 1 above is doubled in case of counterfeiting, fabrication or use of a medical certificate or a private writing not provided for in article 394 above.

## Livre 5 ‐ Offences against property

### Titre 1 ‐ Destruction

###### Art.396.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from six months to six years and a fine from

100,000 to 300,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, whoever destroys, even partially, any property belonging in whole or in part to another or subject to a charge in favor of another.

The penalty is imprisonment from two to ten years and a fine from 200,000 to 1000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, if the destruction concerns buildings, works, ships or installations.

Are punished by the penalties provided for in the preceding paragraph, those who shall have driven or let pass livestock on another person's land, sown or loaded with crops in any season whatsoever.

###### Art.397.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from six months to three years and a fine from

100,000 to 500,000 FCFA, whoever:

- a) removes or moves a boundary marker or any other sign established to mark the boundary between different properties;
- b) destroys a fence of any nature whatsoever.

###### Art.398.‐ Voluntary arson of inhabited places or places used for habitation is punished by

imprisonment from five to ten years.

If the arson has caused the death of one or more persons, the penalty shall be imprisonment from twenty to thirty years.

Voluntary arson of buildings, means of transport, all places that are neither inhabited nor used for habitation, arson of forests, woods or crops standing or cut, when they do not belong to the perpetrator of the acts, shall be punished by imprisonment from two to five years. If nevertheless the arson has caused the death of one or more persons, the penalty shall be imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

###### Art.399.‐ Voluntary destruction by means of explosive substances of inhabited places or places used for

habitation, of constructions, vehicles, public or private roads and generally of all movable or immovable objects, shall be punished by the same penalties and according to the distinctions set out in article 398 above.

The depositing, with criminal intent, on a public or private road, of an explosive device shall be assimilated to attempted murder with premeditation.

Persons guilty of the offences provided for in this article shall be exempt from penalty, if before the consummation of said offences and before any prosecution, they disclosed them and revealed the perpetrators to the constituted authorities, or if, even after prosecution has commenced, they procured the arrest of the other guilty parties.

They may nevertheless be subject to prohibition of residence.

###### Art.400.‐ The threat to commit one of the crimes listed in articles 398, 404 and 399, 405

above shall be punished as a threat of death.

### Titre 2 ‐ Fraudulent appropriation

#### Chapitre 1 ‐ Theft

###### Art.401.‐ Commits the offence of theft and is punished by six months to five years imprisonment, whoever

fraudulently misappropriates a thing that does not belong to him.

###### Art.402.‐ There is no theft by:

• a) husbands to the prejudice of their wives;

• b) wives to the prejudice of their husbands;

• c) a widower or widow as to things that had belonged to the deceased spouse;

• d) children or other descendants to the prejudice of their father or other ascendants;

• e) fathers or mothers or other ascendants to the prejudice of their children or other descendants;

• f) relatives by marriage in the same degrees, provided that the misappropriations are committed during the duration of the marriage and outside a period during which the spouses are authorized to live separately.

Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph I above, any other individual who shall have concealed or applied to his profit all or objects stolen shall be punished as guilty of receiving.

###### Art.403.‐ The penalty is five to ten years of imprisonment if:

• a) the theft was committed at night;

• b) the theft was committed by two or more persons acting together;

• c) the theft was committed with climbing over, or breaking and entering internally or externally, in an inhabited place or one used for habitation;

• d) the theft was committed using a motorised vehicle;

• e) the offenders or one of them assumed the title of a civil or military official or wore the uniform or attire of an official or alleged a false order from a civil or military authority or carried any disguise likely to give the impression of a status or capacity that is not their own;

• f) the theft was committed by a domestic servant or employee, either in the employer's house or in a house where they accompanied the employer, or by a worker or apprentice in the house, workshop or store of their employer, or by an individual who habitually works, in any capacity whatsoever, in the place where the theft was committed;

• g) a hotelier, a carrier or one of their employees stole things entrusted to them in that capacity.

The penalty is ten to fifteen years of imprisonment if:

- a) the theft was committed with two or more of the circumstances set out in paragraph 1 above;
- b) the offenders or one of them were carrying a weapon, visible or concealed;
- c) the theft was committed with violence or threats;
- d) the theft was committed using false keys.

Where the violence committed by the perpetrator of the theft has caused an incapacity to work of more than twenty days or more serious injuries or infirmities, the penalty shall be imprisonment of fifteen to twenty years.

Where the violence has caused death even without intention to cause it, the penalty shall be imprisonment of twenty to thirty years.

###### Art.404.

‐ Breaking and entering is defined as any forcing, breaking, damage, demolition, removal of walls, roofs, floors, doors, windows, locks, padlocks, or anything preventing passage and any kind of enclosure, whatever it may be.

###### Art.405.

‐ An inhabited house is any building, dwelling, lodge, hut, even mobile, which, without being currently inhabited, is intended for habitation and everything dependent on it, such as courtyards, farmyards, barns, stables, buildings enclosed therein, whatever their use, and even if they have a separate enclosure within the general enclosure or perimeter.

###### Art.406.

‐ False keys are defined as all keys that are imitated, counterfeited or altered, as well as any keys or instruments whatsoever that were not intended for opening the locks, padlocks or other fastenings to which the offender has applied them.

Whoever counterfeits or alters keys shall be sentenced to imprisonment of three months to two years and to a fine of 25,000 to 100,000 FCFA.

If the offender is a locksmith by profession, they shall be punished by imprisonment of two to five years and a fine of 25,000 FCFA to 100,000 FCFA.

The penalties provided for in paragraphs 2 and 3 above are fixed without prejudice to heavier penalties, if applicable, in the event of complicity in a crime.

###### Art.407.‐ Commits the offence of theft and is punished by six months to five years of imprisonment whoever:

• a) unlawfully appropriates energy from any motive power;

• b) appropriates a lost thing.

###### Art.408.‐ In all the cases provided for in articles 401 and 403 to 407 above, the

additional penalties provided for by the provisions of article 28 of this Code may be applied.

The attempt of the offences provided for in articles 401 and 403 is punished as the theft itself.

#### Chapitre 2 ‐ De l'escroquerie

###### Art.409.‐ Is punished by one to five years of imprisonment and a fine the maximum amount of which

is equal to three times the value handed over, whoever, either by using false names or false capacities, or by employing fraudulent manoeuvres to persuade the existence of false enterprises, an imaginary power or credit, or to give rise to the hope or fear of success or any other unreal event, has had handed over or delivered to them, or has attempted to have handed over or delivered to them, funds, movable property or obligations, dispositions, bills, promises, receipts or releases, and has, by one of these means, obtained or attempted to obtain by fraud, in whole or in part, the property of another.

###### Art.410.

‐ Is punished by the penalties sanctioning the offence of fraud, whoever takes advantage of the needs, weaknesses or passions of a person who has not yet reached the age of civil majority to have them sign any obligation, disposition or release, or any document likely to compromise the person or fortune of the signatory.

A person who is incapable and of age or in a state of notorious mental alienation is assimilated to a minor for the purposes of this article.

#### Chapitre 3 ‐ De l'abus de confiance

###### Art.411.‐ Whoever misappropriates or wastes things that had been entrusted to them only

on condition of returning or producing them, or of making a specific use or employment thereof, shall be punished by six months to five years of imprisonment and a fine of 50,000 to 2,000,000 FCFA.

Failure to repay a loan of things or money intended to be consumed does not constitute the offence of breach of trust provided for in the preceding paragraph.

###### Art.412.‐ If the breach of trust was committed by a public official or ministerial officer, the penalty shall be

imprisonment of five to ten years.

If the breach of trust was committed by a person appealing to the public to obtain, either for their own account or as director, administrator or agent of a company or commercial or industrial undertaking, the delivery of funds or securities, by way of deposit, mandate or pledge, the duration of imprisonment may be increased to ten years and the fine to

10,000,000 FCFA.

The additional penalties of article 28 of this Code may, in addition, be applied.

###### Art.413.‐ Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 2 of the preceding article, shall be punished by the penalties for

breach of trust any beneficiary of a loan granted by a public body or establishment or by an organisation covered by the guarantee of public authorities who has used the sums lent for purposes or under conditions other than those that were stipulated. The penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above shall be applied to whoever has disposed, contrary to the stipulations of the contract, of the proceeds of the operations for which the loan was granted.

#### Chapitre 4 ‐ Des autres atteintes aux biens

###### Art.414.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of fifteen days to six months and a fine of

25,000 to 50,000 FCFA whoever, being unable to pay the price thereof:

- a) has beverages or food served to them which they have consumed on the premises;
- b) occupies a room in a hotel;
- c) rents a motor vehicle;
- d) has fuel or lubricants served for a motor vehicle.

In the cases provided for in points a and b above, the duration of the supply of beverages or food or the occupation of the accommodation must not have exceeded one week.

###### Art.415.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 100,000 to

1,000,000 FCFA, the debtor pledgor who knowingly removes, misappropriates or destroys the pledged property.

###### Art.416.‐ Articles 414 and 415 above are not applicable between spouses, between

legitimate or adoptive ascendants and descendants or between natural ascendants and descendants up to the second degree if they live together or are recognised, against the widower or widow with respect to property of primary necessity having belonged to the deceased spouse.

###### Art.417.

‐ Those who, knowingly, have received things removed, diverted or obtained by means of a crime or an offense shall be punished by six months to five years' imprisonment and, in addition, by a fine which may be raised up to half the value of the received objects. In the case where the act which procured the received things constitutes a crime, the receiver shall be punished by the penalty attached by law to the crime and to the circumstances of the crime of which he had knowledge at the time of receiving.

The fine provided for in paragraph 1 above may always be pronounced.

###### Art.418.‐ Is punished by a fine of 25,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA, the lender who demands or receives

interest or other remuneration exceeding the rate fixed by law for loans of the same nature. In case of recidivism, the penalty is imprisonment of fifteen days to one year and the fine is doubled. The court may order the publication of its decision under the conditions provided for in Article 34 of this Code.

For the application of this article, the borrower is not considered as an accomplice.

### Titre 3 ‐ Infringements of intellectual property

###### Art.419.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 500,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, anyone who unduly exploits a patent for invention.

Is punished by the penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above anyone who receives, sells, exports, imports or makes use of an object resulting from the undue exploitation of a patent for invention.

In the cases provided for in paragraphs 1 and 2 above, the court orders the confiscation of the object resulting from the undue exploitation for the benefit of the owner of the patent for invention.

The court orders the publication of its decision under the conditions provided for in Article 34 of this Code.

###### Art.420.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 500,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, anyone who unduly exploits a registered design, trademark or industrial model.

Is punished by the penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above, anyone who receives, sells, exports, imports or makes use of an object resulting from the undue exploitation of a registered design, trademark or industrial model.

In the cases provided for in paragraphs 1 and 2 above, the court orders the confiscation of objects resulting from the undue exploitation of registered designs, trademarks and models, for the benefit of the injured parties.

The court orders the publication of its decision under the conditions provided for in Article 34 of this Code.

The convicted person is deprived of the right to be a voter, of the right of eligibility and of any elective or nominative mandate to the consular chambers of commerce or industry for a period of ten years.

###### Art.421.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 500,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, anyone who counterfeits a registered manufacturing or trade mark. Is punished by the penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above, anyone who receives, sells, exports, imports or makes use of an object resulting from the counterfeiting of a registered manufacturing or trade mark. Is punished by the penalties provided for in paragraph 1 above, anyone who:

- a) without counterfeiting a registered mark, has made a fraudulent imitation of it likely to deceive the buyer;
- b) has made use of a fraudulently imitated mark;
- c) has made any use of a registered mark bearing indications likely to deceive the buyer as to the nature, substantial qualities, composition or content of useful principles, species or origin of the designated object;
- d) has held without legitimate reason, products which he knew were bearing a fraudulently imitated mark;
- e) has knowingly sold, offered for sale, supplied or offered to supply products or services under such a mark.

In the cases provided for in paragraphs 1, 2 and 3 above, the court orders the confiscation for the benefit of the injured parties, of objects resulting from the counterfeiting of the manufacturing or trade mark. The court orders the publication of its decision under the conditions provided for in Article 34 of this Code.

The convicted person is deprived of the right to be a voter, of the right of eligibility and of any elective or nominative mandate to the consular chambers of commerce or industry for a period of ten years.

###### Art.422.‐ Are punished by imprisonment of five to ten years, any publication of writing, of

musical composition, of drawing, painting or of any other production printed or engraved in whole or in part in disregard of the laws relating to the property of authors, any reproduction, representation or diffusion, any exploitation by any means whatsoever of a work of the mind in violation of the rights of the author.

Is punished by the penalty provided for in paragraph I, the author of an importation or exportation of counterfeit works.

The court seized shall pronounce the confiscation provided for in Article 31 of this Code.

## Livre 6 ‐ Other crimes and offenses

#### Chapitre 1 ‐ Infringements of public health

###### Art.423.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of three months to three years and a fine of

50,000 to 500,000 FCFA, anyone who either adulterates foodstuffs for the consumption of humans or animals, beverages or medicinal substances, intended to be sold, or holds products solely suitable for carrying out this adulteration.

Is punished by the same penalty, anyone who holds for sale, these foodstuffs, beverages or medicines, either adulterated, or spoiled, or harmful to human health.

The provisions of this article are not applicable to fresh fruits and vegetables, fermented or spoiled.

The foodstuffs, beverages and medicines, if they still belong to the offender, are confiscated. If they are not used by the administration, their destruction is done at the expense of the convicted person.

The court may order the publication of the decision under the conditions provided for in Article 34 of this Code.

###### Art.424.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of three months to three years and a fine of

50,000 to 500,000 FCFA, anyone who, by his conduct, facilitates the communication of a contagious and dangerous disease.

If the facilitated contagion is dangerous for the life of animals normally intended for human consumption, the imprisonment is from one to five years.

###### Art.425.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of fifteen days to six months and a fine of

100,000 to 1,000,000 FCFA or one of these two penalties only, anyone who, by his activity:

- a) pollutes drinking water likely to be used by others;
- b) pollutes the atmosphere to the point of making it harmful to public health.

###### Art.426.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of two to five years and a fine of

1,000,000 to 10,000,000 FCFA or one of these two penalties only, any person who offers drinking water to the public without complying with the quality standards in force.

In case of recidivism, the offender incurs double the maximum of the penalties provided for in the paragraph above.

###### Art.427.

‐ Constitute trafficking in human organs, the sale or purchase of organs of the human body. Anyone who engages in trafficking in human organs is punished by imprisonment of two to five years and a fine of 5,000,000 to 10,000,000 FCFA.

###### Art.428.‐ Is punished by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 5,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or one of these two penalties only, anyone who removes an organ, tissues or cells from a living person for a therapeutic purpose without the consent of that person or of those who have custody of him.

The penalties of paragraph I above are doubled if the removal was carried out in exchange for payment in whatever form.

#### Chapitre 2 ‐ Cybercrime

##### Section 1 ‐ Attacks on computer systems

Sous‐section 1 ‐ Attacks on the confidentiality and integrity of computer systems

###### Art.429.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who accesses or attempts to fraudulently access all or part of a computer system.

Is punished by the same penalties anyone who obtains or attempts to fraudulently obtain, for himself or for others, any advantage by entering into a computer system.

###### Art.430.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who remains or attempts to fraudulently remain in all or part of a computer system. Is punished by the same penalties, any person who hinders, falsifies or attempts to hinder or falsify the operation of a computer system.

Subsection 2 - Fraudulent introduction of data into a system

###### Art.431.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who introduces or attempts to fraudulently introduce data into a computer system.

##### Section 2 - Attacks on computer data

Subsection 1 - Falsification and use of falsified data

###### Art.432.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from five to ten years and a fine from

5,000,000 to 50,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who introduces or attempts to introduce, alters or attempts to alter, deletes or attempts to delete, removes or attempts to fraudulently remove computer data, generating inauthentic data, with the intention that it be taken into account or used for legal purposes as if it were authentic, whether or not it is directly readable and intelligible.

###### Art.433.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from five to ten years and a fine from

3,000,000 to 30,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who, knowingly, makes use of data obtained under the conditions set out in Article 429 of this Code.

###### Art.434.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who intercepts or attempts to fraudulently intercept by technical means, computerized data during their non-public transmission to, from or within a computer system.

###### Art.435.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA, or by only one of these two penalties, any person who damages or attempts to damage, deletes or attempts to delete, deteriorates or attempts to deteriorate, alters or attempts to alter, modifies or attempts to fraudulently modify computer data.

###### Art.436.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who produces or manufactures a set of digitized data by the fraudulent introduction, deletion or removal of computerized data stored, processed or transmitted by a computer system, generating counterfeit data, with the intention that it be taken into account or used for legal purposes as if it were original.

###### Art.437.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who fraudulently obtains, for herself or for others, any advantage by the introduction, alteration, deletion or removal of computerized data or by any form of attack on the operation of a computer system.

###### Art.438.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who, even through negligence, carries out or causes to be carried out processing of personal data without having complied with the formalities prior to their implementation provided for by the law on personal data.

Subsection 2 - Misuse of devices

###### Art.439.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one year to three years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who produces, sells, imports, possesses, distributes, offers, transfers or makes available:

- a) a device, including a computer program, primarily designed or adapted to enable the commission of one of the offenses referred to in Articles 432 to 436 above;
- b) a password, access code or similar computer data enabling access to all or part of a computer system, with the intention that they be used to commit one or another of the offenses referred to in Articles 432 to 436 above.

The perpetrators of one of the offenses provided for in this article also incur the following additional penalties:

- a) the confiscation, according to the modalities provided for by the texts in force of any object intended or having served to commit the offense in question, with the exception of objects subject to restitution;
- b) the prohibition, under the conditions provided for by the texts in force, for a period of at least five years, from exercising a public function or a socio-professional activity, when the acts were committed in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of the functions of the incriminated person;
- c) the closure, under the conditions provided for by the texts in force, for a period of at least five years, of the establishments or of one or more of the establishments of the enterprise having served to commit the incriminated acts;
- d) the exclusion for a period of at least five years from public procurement.

Subsection 3 - Digital identity theft, association of computer criminals and complicity

###### Art.440.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who usurps the digital identity of a third party or one or more data enabling their identification, with a view to disturbing their peace or that of others or to damaging their honor, their reputation or their interests.

###### Art.441.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who participates in an association formed or an agreement established with a view to preparing or committing one or more of the offenses provided for by this chapter.

###### Art.442.‐ A person who intentionally commits an act of complicity with a view to the

perpetration of one of the offenses provided for in this chapter, with the intention that such an offense be perpetrated, commits an offense punishable by the same penalties as those provided for the principal offense.

##### Section 3 - Content-related offenses

Subsection 1 - Child pornography

###### Art.443.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who produces with a view to its distribution, attempts to produce with a view to sale, offers, makes available, distributes or attempts to distribute child pornography through a computer system.

###### Art.444.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine from 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or by only one of these two penalties, any person who obtains or provides to others, imports or causes to be imported, exports or causes to be exported child pornography through a computer system.

###### Art.445.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who intentionally possesses child pornography in a computer system or in any means of storing computer data.

Is punishable by the same penalties, any person who facilitates minors' access to images, documents, sound or a representation of a pornographic nature.

###### Art.446.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who intentionally proposes, through information and communication technologies, a meeting with a minor child, for the purpose of committing against him or her one of the offenses provided for in articles 443 to 445 above.

When the sexual proposition has been followed by material acts leading to said meeting, the perpetrator commits an aggravated offense punishable by imprisonment of five to ten years and a fine of 2,000,000 to 20,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties.

###### Art.447.‐ For the offenses described in articles 443 and 444 above, the

complementary penalties provided for in article 28 of this Code may be applied. Sub-section 2 ‐ Racist and xenophobic acts through a computer system

###### Art.448.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who creates, downloads, disseminates or makes available in any form whatsoever writings, messages, photos, drawings or any other representation of ideas or theories of a racist or xenophobic nature, through a computer system.

###### Art.449.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who commits threats through a computer system against a person by reason of his or her belonging to a group characterized by race, color, ancestry, descent or national or ethnic origin, religion, insofar as this belonging serves as a pretext for one or the other of these elements, or a group of persons distinguished by one of these characteristics.

###### Art.450.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who commits an insult through a computer system against a person by reason of his or her belonging to a group characterized by race, color, ancestry, descent or national or ethnic origin, religion, insofar as this belonging serves as a pretext for one or the other of these elements, or a group of persons distinguished by one of these characteristics.

###### Art.451.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who intentionally denies, approves or justifies acts constituting genocide or crimes against humanity through a computer system.

##### Section 4 ‐ Non-compliance with injunctions and disclosure of investigation

information

###### Art.452.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person, other than the accused, who intentionally fails without legitimate excuse or justification to comply with an injunction from judicial police officers or authorized agents.

###### Art.453.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any service provider who, having received an injunction in the context of a criminal investigation that explicitly stipulated that confidentiality must be maintained or that results from the law, intentionally without legitimate excuse or justification, discloses information relating to the investigation.

###### Art.454.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, the legal person responsible for the site used to commit the offense or any qualified person responsible for implementing the necessary technical means to ensure the prohibition of access, hosting or cutting off access to the incriminated site and who does not comply with the injunctions issued by the judge to that effect.

##### Section 5 ‐ Offenses related to cryptology

###### Art.455.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of three months to one year and a fine of

500,000 to 5,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who has not satisfied the obligation to communicate to the Public Authority in charge of cryptology a description of the technical characteristics of cryptology means in accordance with the texts relating thereto.

###### Art.456.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of three months to one year and a fine of

500,000 to 5,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who has imported a means of cryptology not exclusively providing authentication or integrity control functions without satisfying the obligation of prior declaration to the Authority in charge of cryptology.

###### Art.457.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of three months to one year and a fine of

500,000 to 5,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who has provided cryptology services without having previously obtained approval from the Public Authority in charge of cryptology.

###### Art.458.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of three months to one year and a fine of

500,000 to 5,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who has exported a means of cryptology not exclusively providing authentication or integrity control functions without having previously obtained authorization from the Public Authority in charge of cryptology.

###### Art.459.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of

1,000,000 to 10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who has made available to others a means of cryptology that has been the subject of a prohibition on use and circulation.

###### Art.460.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of

1,000,000 to 10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who uses a means of cryptology to prepare or commit a crime or an offense or to facilitate its preparation or commission.

##### Section 6 ‐ Spamming

###### Art.461.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who, intentionally and without legitimate excuse or justification:

- a) intentionally initiates the transmission of multiple electronic mails from or through a computer system;
- b) uses a protected computer system to relay or retransmit multiple electronic mails with the intent to deceive or mislead, as to the origin of these messages, the recipients or any provider of electronic mail services or Internet services;
- c) materially falsifies the information contained in the headers of electronic messages and intentionally initiates the transmission of the messages.

#### Chapter 3 ‐ Adaptation of classic offenses to information and communication technologies

##### Section 1 ‐ Offenses against property

###### Art.462.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, any person who copies or attempts to fraudulently copy computer data to the detriment of a third party.

###### Art.463.‐ Is punishable by the same penalties as those provided for fraud relating to tangible

property, any person who, either by using false names or false qualities, or by employing any fraudulent maneuvers, shall have obtained the delivery or shall have attempted to obtain the delivery of computer data and shall have, by one of these means, defrauded or attempted to defraud all or part of another's property.

###### Art.464.‐ Is punishable by the same penalties as those provided for breach of trust relating to

tangible property, any person who, having received computer data from owners, possessors or holders by way of lease, deposit, mandate, pledge, loan for use or for paid or unpaid work, shall not have, after simple notice, fulfilled their obligation to return or produce them or to make a specific use or employment thereof.

###### Art.465.‐ Is punishable by the same penalties as those provided for receiving relating to tangible

property, any person who, knowingly, shall have received, in whole or in part, computer data removed, misappropriated or obtained by means of a crime or an offense.

###### Art.466.‐ Is considered an aggravated offense and punishable by imprisonment of five to ten

years and a fine of 2,000,000 to 20,000,000 FCFA or only one of these two penalties, the act of any person who, either by using false names or false qualities, or by employing any fraudulent maneuvers, shall have had delivered or issued to them, or shall have attempted to have delivered or issued to them funds, movables or obligations, provisions, notes, promises, receipts or releases by means of a computer system of an electronic communications network and shall have, by one of these means, defrauded all or part of another's property.

##### Section 2 ‐ Offenses against national defense

###### Art.467.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of five to ten years, any Chadian citizen who:

• a) delivers to a foreign power or its agents, in whatever form or by whatever means, information, an object, document, process, digitized data or computerized file that must be kept secret in the interest of national defense;

• b) secures, by any means whatsoever, possession of such information, object, document, process, computerized data or computerized file with a view to delivering it to a foreign power or its agents;

• c) destroys or allows to be destroyed such information, object, document, process, computerized data or computerized file with a view to delivering it to a foreign power.

##### Section 3 ‐ Press offenses

###### Art.468.

‐ A person who commits a press offense, particularly defamation, public insult, or apology for a crime, by means of a public electronic communication medium, commits an offense punishable by the same penalties as those provided for press offenses committed by other means.

##### Section 4 ‐ Liability of legal persons

###### Art.469.‐ Legal persons are criminally liable for the offenses provided for in this

chapter, under the conditions set forth in articles 81 and 82 of this Code. They are furthermore held liable when the absence of supervision or control has made possible the commission of the offenses provided for in this chapter on behalf of said legal person by a natural person acting under its authority.

###### Art.470.‐ The penalties incurred by legal persons are:

• a) a fine of which the maximum rate is equal to five times that provided for natural persons in this chapter;

• b) dissolution, when the legal person was created or when it is an offense punishable, with regard to natural persons, by a term of imprisonment exceeding five years, diverted from its purpose to commit the incriminated acts;

• c) permanent or temporary prohibition for up to five years from directly or indirectly engaging in one or more professional or social activities;

• d) permanent or temporary closure for up to five years of one or more establishments of the enterprise that were used to commit the incriminated acts;

• e) permanent or temporary exclusion from public contracts for up to five years;

• f) permanent or temporary prohibition for up to five years from making public appeals for savings;

• g) prohibition, for up to five years, from issuing checks other than those allowing the withdrawal of funds by the drawer from the drawee or those that are certified or from using payment cards;

• h) confiscation of the thing that was used or intended to commit the offense or of the thing that is the product thereof;

• i) posting of the pronounced decision or dissemination thereof either through the written press or by any means of communication to the public, electronically.

#### Chapitre 4 ‐ Serious abuse or acts of cruelty towards animals Art.471.‐ Is

punishable by three months to two years imprisonment and a fine of

25,000 to 50,000 FCFA, anyone who inflicts serious abuse or commits an act of cruelty towards a domestic animal, a tamed animal or an animal held in captivity.

The court may prohibit the detention of said animal permanently or temporarily as an additional penalty.

The provisions of this article are not applicable to animal fights when an uninterrupted tradition can be established.

Is punishable only by the fine penalty provided in paragraph 1, anyone who abandons a domestic animal, a tamed animal or an animal held in captivity.

###### Art.472.‐ Are punishable by the penalties provided in article 471 paragraph I, the perpetrators of

scientific or experimental experiments or research on animals, when they are conducted in violation of laws and regulations in force.

## Livre 7 ‐ Penalties applicable to offenses provided for by the Uniform Acts of

OHADA

#### Chapitre 1 ‐ Offenses against the provisions of the Uniform Act relating to general

commercial law

###### Art.473.‐ Any person, required to complete one of the registration formalities in the Trade and

Movable Credit Register, and who has refrained from doing so, or who has completed a formality by fraud, is punishable by a fine of 50,000 to 200,000 FCFA.

Where appropriate, the court pronouncing the conviction orders the completion of the omitted formalities, the rectification of inaccurate entries and transcriptions.

###### Art.474.

‐ Any tenant-manager who has refrained from indicating at the header of their purchase orders, invoices and other documents of a financial or commercial nature, along with their registration number in the Trade and Movable Credit Register, their status as tenant-manager of the business is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 1,000,000 to

10,000,000 FCFA.

#### Chapitre 2 ‐ Offenses against the provisions of the Uniform Act relating to commercial company law

and economic interest groups

###### Art.475.

‐ Any act, by the founders, the chairman and chief executive officer, the chief executive officer, the general manager or the deputy general manager of a public limited company, of issuing shares before registration or at any time when registration is obtained by fraud or when the company is irregularly formed, is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of

1.000.000 to 10000.000 FCFA.

###### Art.476.‐ Any person who knowingly, by establishing the notarial declaration of

subscription and payment or the certificate of the depositary, has affirmed as sincere and true subscriptions that they knew to be fictitious or has declared that funds which have not been made permanently available to the company have been actually paid, is punishable by imprisonment of one to ten years and a fine of 5.000.000 to

50.000.000 FCFA.

###### Art.477.‐ Any person who has submitted to the notary or the depositary, a list of

shareholders or subscription and payment bulletins mentioning fictitious subscriptions or payments of funds which have not been made permanently available to the company, is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 2.000.000 to

20.000.000 FCFA.

###### Art.478.‐ Any person who knowingly, by simulation of subscription or payment or

by publication of subscriptions or payments that do not exist or of any other false fact, has obtained or attempted to obtain subscriptions or payments, is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 2.000.000 to

20.000.000 FCFA.

###### Art.479.‐ Any person who knowingly, to induce subscriptions or

payments has published the names of persons designated contrary to the truth as being or being intended to be attached to the company in any capacity whatsoever or who, fraudulently, has caused a contribution in kind to be assigned a valuation higher than its actual value, is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of

2.000.000 to 20.000.000 FCFA.

###### Art.480.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 2000.000 to

20.000.000 FCFA, any person who has knowingly traded:

- registered shares that have not remained in registered form until their full payment;
- shares issued as contributions in kind before the expiry of the period during which they are not negotiable;
- cash shares for which payment of one quarter of the nominal value has not been made.

###### Art.481.‐ Are punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 2000.000 to

20.000.000 FCFA, all company officers who, in the absence of inventory or by means of fraudulent inventory, have knowingly carried out, among the shareholders or partners, the distribution of fictitious dividends.

###### Art.482.‐ Are punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of 2000.000 to

20.000.000 FCFA, company officers who have knowingly, even in the absence of any distribution of dividends, published or presented to shareholders or partners, with a view to concealing the true situation of the company, summary financial statements not giving, for each financial year, a true picture of the operations of the financial year, the financial situation and that of the company's assets, at the expiry of that period.

###### Art.483.‐ Is punishable by imprisonment of six months to five years and a fine of

3.000.000 to 30.000.000 FCFA, the manager of the limited liability company, the directors, the chairman and chief executive officer, the chief executive officer, the general manager or the deputy general manager who, in bad faith, make use of the property or credit of the company in a way that they knew to be contrary to the interest of the company, for personal purposes, material or moral, or to favour another legal entity in which they had an interest, directly or indirectly.

In addition, the judge may order the prohibition from exercising the functions of manager of SARL, director, chairman and chief executive officer, chief executive officer, general manager or deputy general manager for a period which may not exceed five years.

###### Art.484.‐ Are punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of

2.000.000 to 20.000.000 FCFA, all those who, knowingly, have prevented a shareholder or partner from participating in a general meeting.

###### Art.485.‐ Are punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of

2.000.000 to 20.000.000 FCFA, the chairman of the board of directors, the chairman and chief executive officer, the chief executive officer, the general manager or the deputy general manager of a public limited company who, during a capital increase, have issued shares or share certificates:

- before the certificate of the depositary has been established;
- without the formalities prior to the capital increase having been duly completed;
- without the previously subscribed capital of the company having been fully paid up;
- without the new shares issued as contributions in kind having been fully paid up before the amendment registration in the trade and movable credit register;
- without the new shares having been paid up for at least one quarter of their nominal value at the time of subscription;
- where applicable, without the entire share premium having been paid up at the time of subscription.

Criminal sanctions are also applicable to the persons referred to in this article who have not maintained cash shares in registered form until their full payment.

###### Art.486.‐ Are punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of

2.000.000 to 20.000.000 FCFA, company officers who, during a capital increase:

- have not granted shareholders, in proportion to the amount of their shares, a preferential right to subscribe for cash shares when this right has not been removed by the general meeting and shareholders have not waived it;
- have not reserved for shareholders a period of at least twenty days, from the opening of the subscription, except when this period has been closed early;
- have not allocated the shares made available, for lack of a sufficient number of subscriptions as of right, to shareholders who have subscribed on a reducible basis for a number of shares greater than that which they could subscribe as of right in proportion to the rights they hold;
- have not reserved the rights of warrant holders.

###### Art.487.‐ Are punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of

2.000.000 to 20.000.000 FCFA, company officers who, knowingly, have given or confirmed inaccurate indications in the reports presented to the general meeting called upon to decide on the removal of the preferential subscription right.

###### Art.488.‐ Are punishable by imprisonment of one to five years and a fine of

2.000.000 to 20.000.000 FCFA, the directors, the chairman and chief executive officer, the chief executive officer, the general manager or the deputy general manager who, knowingly, have proceeded with a capital reduction:

- 1° without respecting the equality of shareholders;
- 2° without having communicated the draft capital reduction to the auditors forty-five days before the holding of the general meeting called upon to rule on the capital reduction.

###### Art.489.‐ Are punishable by imprisonment of three months to five years and a fine of

500.000 to 5.000.000 FCFA, company officers who have not initiated the appointment of the auditors of the company or have not convened them to general meetings.

### Title 5 ‐ Offenses relating to the control of companies

###### Art.490.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine of 5,000,000 to

50,000,000 FCFA, any person who, either in their own name or as a partner of an auditing firm, shall have knowingly accepted, exercised or retained the functions of statutory auditor, notwithstanding legal incompatibilities.

Furthermore, the judge may pronounce the prohibition from exercising the function of statutory auditor for a period that cannot exceed five years.

###### Art.491.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine of 5,000,000 to

50,000,000 FCFA, any statutory auditor who, either in their own name or as a partner of an auditing firm, shall have knowingly given or confirmed false information about the situation of the company or who shall not have disclosed to the public prosecutor, the criminal facts of which they became aware.

Furthermore, the judge may pronounce the prohibition from exercising the function of statutory auditor for a period that cannot exceed five years.

###### Art.492.‐ Are punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine of

2,000,000 to 20,000,000 FCFA, the company directors or any person in the service of the company who, knowingly, shall have obstructed the verifications or control of the statutory auditors or who shall have refused the communication, on site, of all documents useful for the exercise of their mission and in particular of all contracts, books, accounting documents and minutes registers.

###### Art.493.‐ Are punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine of

2,000,000 to 20,000,000 FCFA, the company directors who, knowingly, when the equity of the company becomes less than half of the share capital due to losses recorded in the summary financial statements:

- 1‐ shall not have had convened, within the four months following the approval of the summary financial statements having revealed these losses, the extraordinary general meeting for the purpose of deciding, if necessary, the early dissolution of the company;
- 2‐ shall not have filed with the registry of the court in charge of commercial matters, registered in the trade and movable credit register and published in a newspaper authorized to receive legal notices, the early dissolution of the company.

###### Art.494.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine of 2,000,000 to

20,000,000 FCFA, the liquidator of a company who, knowingly:

- shall not have, within one month from their appointment, published in a newspaper authorized to receive legal notices of the place of the registered office, the act appointing them liquidator and filed with the trade and movable credit register the decisions pronouncing the dissolution;
- shall not have convened the partners, at the end of the liquidation, to decide on the final account of the liquidation, on the discharge of their management and the release of their mandate and to note the closure of the liquidation;
- shall not have, in the cases provided for in this chapter, filed their final accounts with the registry of the court in charge of commercial matters of the place of the registered office, nor requested in court the approval thereof.

###### Art.495.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine of 2,000,000 to

20,000,000 FCFA, the liquidator who, knowingly:

- shall not have, within six months of their appointment, presented a report on the active and passive situation of the company in liquidation and on the continuation of liquidation operations, nor requested the necessary authorizations to complete them;
- shall not have, within three months of the closing of each fiscal year, prepared the summary financial statements in view of the inventory and a written report in which they account for the liquidation operations during the past fiscal year;
- shall not have allowed the partners to exercise, during the liquidation period, their right to communication of corporate documents under the same conditions as before;
- shall not have convened the partners, at least once a year, to report to them on the summary financial statements in case of continuation of the corporate operations;
- shall not have deposited in an account opened in a bank in the name of the company in liquidation, within fifteen days from the distribution decision, the sums allocated to distributions among the partners and creditors;
- shall not have deposited, in a consignment account opened in the records of the Treasury, within one year from the closure of the liquidation, the sums allocated to creditors or partners and not claimed by them.

Furthermore, the judge may pronounce the prohibition from exercising the functions of trustee for a period that cannot exceed five years.

###### Art.496.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine of 5,000,000 to

50,000,000 FCFA, any liquidator who, in bad faith:

- shall have made use of the assets or credit of the company in liquidation, in a manner that they knew to be contrary to the interest thereof, for personal purposes or to favor another legal entity in which they were interested, directly or indirectly;
- shall have transferred all or part of the assets of the company in liquidation to a person having had in the company the capacity of general partner, limited partner, manager, member of the board of directors, managing director or statutory auditor, without having obtained the unanimous consent of the partners or, failing that, the authorization of the competent jurisdiction.

Furthermore, the judge may pronounce the prohibition from exercising the function of liquidator for a period that cannot exceed five years.

###### Art.497.‐ Are punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine of

2,000,000 to 20,000,000 FCFA, the presidents, directors or managing directors of a company who shall have issued securities offered to the public:

- without a notice being inserted in a newspaper authorized to receive legal notices, prior to any publicity measure;
- without the prospectuses and circulars reproducing the statements of the notice provided for in the point above and containing the mention of the insertion of this notice in the newspaper authorized to receive legal notices with reference to the issue in which it was published;
- without the posters and advertisements in newspapers reproducing the same statements or at least an extract of these statements with reference to said notice, and indications of the issue number of the newspaper authorized to receive legal notices in which it was published;
- without the posters, prospectuses and circulars mentioning the signature of the person or representative of the company from which the offer emanates and specifying whether the securities offered are listed or not and, if so, at which stock exchange.

The same criminal sanction shall be applicable to persons who shall have served as intermediaries on the occasion of the transfer of securities without the prescriptions of this article having been respected.

#### Chapter 3 ‐ Offenses against the provisions of the Uniform Act on the organization of

security interests

###### Art.498.‐ Is punished by imprisonment from three years to five years and a fine of

500,000 to 5,000,000 FCFA, any debtor or any person who, by fraudulent maneuvers, deprives the pledgee creditor of their rights or diminishes them.

###### Art.499.‐ Is punished by a fine of 50,000 to 200,000 francs, the lessee or any person

who, by fraudulent maneuvers, deprives the lessor of their privilege totally or partially.

#### Chapter 4 ‐ Offenses against the provisions of the Uniform Act on the organization of

collective proceedings for the clearing of liabilities

###### Art.500.‐ The offense of simple bankruptcy and offenses assimilated to simple bankruptcy are punishable by imprisonment from one month to two years.

###### Art.501.‐ Any natural person in a state of cessation of payments who is in any of the following cases is guilty of simple bankruptcy:

- if they have contracted, without receiving values in exchange, commitments deemed too significant in view of their situation at the time they were contracted;
- if, with the intention of delaying the finding of cessation of their payments, they have made purchases with a view to resale below market price or if, with the same intention, they have used ruinous means to obtain funds;
- if, without legitimate excuse, they do not file with the registry of the competent court a declaration of their state of cessation of payments within thirty days;
- if their accounting is incomplete or irregularly kept or if they have not kept any accounting in accordance with accounting rules and recognized practices of the profession in view of the size of the business;
- if, having been declared twice in a state of cessation of payments within a period of five years, these proceedings have been closed for insufficiency of assets.

###### Art.502.‐ The provisions of this section are applicable:

• to natural persons who are directors of legal entities subject to collective proceedings;

• to natural persons who are permanent representatives of legal entities that are directors of the legal entities referred to in the point above.

The directors referred to in this article mean all directors in law or in fact and, in general, any person having directly or through an intermediary, administered, managed or liquidated the legal entity under the cover of or in the place and stead of its legal representatives.

###### Art.503.‐ The directors referred to in the above article who have, in such capacity and in bad faith, are punishable by the penalties for simple bankruptcy:

- consumed sums belonging to the legal entity by engaging in purely speculative operations or fictitious operations;
- with the intention of delaying the finding of cessation of payments of the legal entity, made purchases with a view to resale below market price or, with the same intention, used ruinous means to obtain funds;
- after cessation of payments of the legal entity, paid or caused to be paid a creditor to the detriment of the estate;
- caused the legal entity to contract, on behalf of others, without it receiving values in exchange, commitments deemed too significant in view of its situation at the time they were contracted;
- kept or caused to be kept or allowed to be kept irregularly or incompletely the accounting of the legal entity under the conditions provided for in this chapter;
- failed to file with the registry of the competent court, within thirty days, the declaration of the state of cessation of payments of the legal entity;
- with a view to removing all or part of their assets from the actions of the legal entity in a state of cessation of payments or from those of the partners or creditors of the legal entity, misappropriated or concealed, attempted to misappropriate or conceal a part of their property or fraudulently acknowledged themselves as debtors of sums they did not owe.

In legal entities with partners who are indefinitely and jointly liable for its debts, the legal or de facto representatives are guilty of simple bankruptcy if, without legitimate excuse, they do not file with the registry of the competent court, within thirty days, the declaration of their state of cessation of payments or if such declaration does not include the list of jointly liable partners with the indication of their names and addresses.

In addition, the judge may pronounce the prohibition from exercising the functions of SARL manager, director, chairman and chief executive officer, chief executive officer, general administrator or deputy managing director for a period that may not exceed five years.

###### Art.504.‐ The offense of fraudulent bankruptcy and offenses assimilated to fraudulent bankruptcy are punishable by imprisonment from five to ten years.

###### Art.505.‐ Any natural person referred to in this chapter who, in the event of cessation of payments, is guilty of fraudulent bankruptcy and punishable by the penalty provided for in Article 504 above:

• has concealed their accounting;

• has misappropriated or dissipated all or part of their assets;

• either in their books, or by public acts or private commitments, or in their balance sheet, has fraudulently acknowledged themselves as debtor of sums they did not owe;

• has exercised commercial activity contrary to a prohibition provided for by the Uniform Acts or by the law of each Member State;

• after cessation of payments, has paid a creditor to the detriment of the estate, has stipulated with a creditor particular advantages by reason of their vote in the deliberations of the estate or has made with a creditor a particular agreement from which would result for the latter an advantage at the expense of the debtor's assets from the day of the opening decision.

###### Art.506.‐ Also guilty of fraudulent bankruptcy and punishable by the penalties provided for in Article 504 is any natural person referred to in this chapter who, on the occasion of a judicial settlement procedure:

- has, in bad faith, presented or caused to be presented an income statement or a balance sheet or a statement of receivables and debts or an active and passive statement of privileges and securities, inaccurate or incomplete;
- has, without authorization from the President of the competent court, performed one of the acts prohibited by this chapter.

In addition, the judge may pronounce the prohibition from exercising the functions of SARL manager, director, chairman and chief executive officer, chief executive officer, general administrator or deputy managing director, as well as the prohibition from exercising civil rights and the prohibition from holding public employment, for a period that may not exceed five years.

###### Art.507.‐ The spouse, descendants, ascendants or collaterals of the debtor or their relatives by marriage who, without their knowledge, have misappropriated, diverted, or concealed assets forming part of the assets of the debtor in a state of cessation of payments are punishable by imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine of 3,000,000 to 30,000,000 CFA francs.

###### Art.508.‐ Any person making an appeal to the public to the detriment of a lessor, depositary, agent, pledgor, lender for use or contractor, any trustee of a collective proceeding who exercises a personal activity under cover of the debtor's business masking their actions, is punishable by imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine of 5,000,000 to 50,000,000 CFA francs:

- disposes of the credit or property of the debtor as their own;
- dissipates the property of the debtor;
- pursues abusively and in bad faith, in their personal interest, either directly or indirectly, a deficit-making operation of the debtor's business;
- in violation of the provisions of this chapter, acquires for their own account, directly or indirectly, the property of the debtor.

In addition, the judge may pronounce the prohibition from exercising the functions of trustee for a period that may not exceed five years.

###### Art.509.‐ Est puni d'un emprisonnement de six mois à cinq ans et d'une amende de

3.000.000 à 30.000.000 FCFA, le créancier qui a :

- stipulé avec le débiteur ou avec toutes personnes, des avantages particuliers à raison de son vote dans les délibérations de la masse ;
- fait un traité particulier duquel il résulterait en sa faveur un avantage à la charge de l'actif du débiteur à partir du jour de la décision d'ouverture de la procédure collective.

#### Chapitre 5 ‐ Des infractions aux dispositions de l'Acte Uniforme portant

organisation des procédures simplifiées de recouvrement et des voies d'exécution

###### Art.510.‐ Est puni en cas de manquement à ses obligations d'un emprisonnement de six mois à

cinq ans et d'une amende de 3000.000 à 30.000.000 FCFA, le débiteur saisi ou le tiers détenteur réputé gardien entre les mains de qui la saisie a été effectuée, si la saisie porte sur des biens corporels.

###### Art.511.‐ Est puni d'un emprisonnement de six mois à cinq ans et d'une amende de

3.000.000 à 30.000.000 FCFA, le détournement d'objets saisis dans le cadre d'une procédure de saisie‐conservatoire.

Après avoir rappelé au débiteur qu'il est tenu de lui indiquer les biens qui auraient fait l'objet d'une saisie antérieure et de lui en communiquer le procès‐verbal, l'huissier ou l'agent d'exécution dresse un procès‐verbal de saisie qui contient, à peine de nullité :

- la mention de l'autorisation de la juridiction compétente ou du titre en vertu duquel la saisie est pratiquée, Ces documents sont annexés à l'acte en original ou en copie certifiée conforme ;
- la mention, en caractères très apparents, que les biens saisis sont indisponibles, qu'ils sont placés sous la garde du débiteur ou d'un tiers désigné d'accord parties ou, à défaut par la juridiction statuant en matière d'urgence, qu'ils ne peuvent être ni aliénés ni déplacés, si ce n'est dans le cas prévu par le présent chapitre, sous peine de sanctions pénales, et que le débiteur est tenu de faire connaître la présente saisie à tout créancier qui procéderait à une nouvelle saisie sur les mêmes biens ;
- la reproduction des dispositions pénales sanctionnant le détournement d'objets saisis.

###### Art.512.‐ Est puni d'un emprisonnement de six mois à cinq ans et d'une amende de

3.000.000 à 30.000.000 FCFA, le détournement d'objets saisis dans le cadre d'une procédure de saisie‐vente.

L'huissier ou l'agent d'exécution dresse un inventaire des biens. L'acte de saisie contient, à peine de nullité :

- les noms, prénoms et domiciles du saisi et du saisissant ou, s'il s'agit de personnes morales, leurs forme, dénomination et siège social, l'élection éventuelle de domicile du saisissant ;
- la mention, en caractères très apparents, que les biens saisis sont indisponibles, qu'ils sont placés sous la garde du débiteur, qu'ils ne peuvent être ni aliénés ni déplacés, si ce n'est dans le cas prévu au présent chapitre, sous peine de sanctions pénales, et que le débiteur est tenu de faire connaître la présente saisie à tout créancier qui procéderait à une nouvelle saisie des mêmes biens ;
- la reproduction des dispositions pénales sanctionnant le détournement d'objets saisis.

###### Art.513.‐ Est puni d'un emprisonnement de six mois à cinq ans et d'une amende de

3.000.000 à 30.000.000 FCFA, le détournement d'objets saisis dans le cadre d'une saisie entre les mains d'un tiers.

Si le tiers déclare détenir des biens pour le compte du débiteur, il est dressé un inventaire qui contient, à peine de nullité :

- la référence du titre en vertu duquel la saisie est pratiquée ;
- la mention, en caractères très apparents, que les objets saisis sont indisponibles, qu'ils sont placés sous la garde du tiers, qu'ils ne peuvent être ni aliénés ni déplacés, si ce n'est dans le cas prévu au présent chapitre sous peine de sanctions pénales et que le tiers est tenu de faire connaître la présente saisie à tout créancier qui procéderait à une saisie sur les mêmes biens
- la reproduction des dispositions pénales sanctionnant le détournement d'objets saisis.

###### Art.514.‐ Est puni d'un emprisonnement de trois mois à trois ans et d'une amende de 3000.000 à

30.000.000 FCFA, le commissaire‐priseur ou tout autre auxiliaire de justice, chargé de la vente et personnellement responsable du prix des adjudications, qui aurait reçu une somme au‐dessus de l'enchère.

###### Art.515.‐ Est puni d'un emprisonnement de six mois à cinq ans et d'une amende de

3.000.000 à 30.000.000 FCFA, le détournement d'objets saisis dans le cadre d'une saisie‐ conservatoire.

Après avoir rappelé au détenteur du bien qu'il est tenu de lui indiquer si ce bien a fait l'objet d'une saisie antérieure et, le cas échéant, de lui en communiquer le procès‐verbal, l'huissier ou l'agent d'exécution dresse un acte de saisie qui contient à peine de nullité :

- les noms, prénoms et domiciles des créanciers et débiteurs ou, s'il s'agit de personnes morales, leurs forme, dénomination et siège social ;
- la mention, en caractères très apparents, que le bien saisi est placé sous la garde du détenteur qui ne peut ni l'aliéner, ni le déplacer sauf dans le cas prévu à l'article 511 ci‐dessus, sous peine de sanctions pénales et qu'il est tenu de faire connaître la saisie revendication à tout créancier qui procéderait à une saisie sur le même bien ;
- la reproduction des dispositions pénales relatives au détournement d'objets saisis.

L'huissier ou l'agent d'exécution peut photographier les biens saisis dans les conditions prescrites par le présent chapitre.

#### Chapitre 6 ‐ Des infractions aux dispositions de l'Acte Uniforme portant organisation

et harmonisation des comptabilités des entreprises

###### Art.516.‐ Sont punis d'un emprisonnement de un mois à deux ans et d'une amende de

200.000 à 2.000.000 FCFA, les entrepreneurs individuels et les dirigeants sociaux qui n'auront pas, pour chaque exercice social, dressé l'inventaire et établi les états financiers annuels ainsi que, le cas échéant, le rapport de gestion et le bilan social.

En outre, le juge peut prononcer l'interdiction d'exercer les fonctions de gérant de SARL, d'administrateur, de président directeur général, de directeur général, d'administrateur général ou d'administrateur directeur adjoint, ainsi que l'interdiction d'exercice des droits civiques et l'interdiction d'occuper un emploi public, pour une durée qui ne peut excéder cinq ans.

###### Art.517.‐ Est puni d'un emprisonnement de cinq à dix ans et d'une amende de 500.000 à

5.000.000 FCFA, tout comptable qui aura sciemment établi et communiqué des états financiers ne délivrant pas une image fidèle du patrimoine, de la situation financière et du résultat de l'exercice.

En outre, le juge peut prononcer l'interdiction d'exercer les fonctions de gérant de SARL, d'administrateur, de président directeur général, de directeur général, d'administrateur général ou d'administrateur directeur adjoint, ainsi que l'interdiction d'exercice des droits civiques et l'interdiction d'occuper un emploi public, pour une durée qui ne peut excéder cinq ans.

## Book 8 ‐ Petty police offenses

###### Art.518.‐ Shall be punished by a fine of 2,000 to 20,000 FCFA and may furthermore be punished by

imprisonment of up to fifteen days:

Intentional torts and damage:

- 1. The perpetrators or accomplices of brawls, assaults or minor violence, provided that the blows inflicted did not result in any incapacity for work;
- 2. Those who, without having been provoked, shall have uttered insults against someone other than those provided for by the press law;
- 3. Those who shall have, without necessity, committed an act of cruelty toward an animal, domestic or not;
- 4. Those who, outside the cases provided for in articles 396, 397, 398 and 471 of this Code, shall have intentionally caused damage to the movable property and domestic animals of others;
- 5. Those who shall have intentionally diverted or improperly used waters intended for irrigation by law or by regulatory provisions emanating from the administration or from distribution bodies or by custom;
- 6. Those who, outside the cases provided for in article 396 of this Code, shall have cut down, mutilated or stripped trees of which they are not the owners;
- 7. Those who shall have thrown stones or other hard objects or filth against the houses, buildings or fences of others or into gardens or enclosures;
- 8. Those who, without being authorized to do so, shall have made inscriptions or posted notices on movable or immovable property belonging to others;
- 9. Those who, without being duly authorized to do so, shall have removed from public roads, lawns, earth, stones or who, in places belonging to communes, shall have removed earth or materials, unless there exists a general custom that authorizes it.

Involuntary damage:

- 10. Those who, through clumsiness, imprudence, inattention, negligence or failure to observe regulations, shall have involuntarily been the cause of injuries, blows, illnesses or damage not resulting in an incapacity for work exceeding six days.
- 11. Those who, through clumsiness, imprudence, inattention, negligence or failure to observe regulations, shall have caused the fire of movable or immovable property belonging to others.

Disturbances to public order or peace:

- 12. The perpetrators or accomplices of noises, disturbances or insulting or nocturnal gatherings disturbing the tranquility of inhabitants;
- 13. People who make a trade of divining, prognosticating, explaining dreams; those who adorn themselves with the title of sorcerers to influence populations;
- 14. Those who, without authorization, shall have established or held in public places lottery games or other games of chance;
- 15. Those who shall have displayed or caused to be displayed in public places posters or images contrary to decency;
- 16. Those who shall have refused to accept national currency and coins that are not counterfeit or altered, for the value for which they have legal tender;
- 17. Those who shall have drafted, prepared or incited to draft or prepare petitions, letters or documents in the name of a community without being mandated by it to do so, and those who make use of these letters, petitions or documents, knowing their abusive character;
- 18. Those who, outside the cases provided for by other provisions of the law, shall have publicly worn a costume presenting a resemblance of a nature to cause confusion in the mind of the public with uniforms defined by regulatory texts;
- 19. Those who shall have refused to obey a regulatory order given by an administrative agent or member of the public force in the exercise of his functions;
- 20. Those who shall have refused, without legitimate reason, to provide information to an authority regularly authorized to obtain it or who shall have knowingly communicated false information to it.

Urban planning or road offenses:

21. Those who shall have thrown or displayed in front of their buildings things of a nature to cause harm by their fall or by unhealthy fumes;

22. Those who shall have neglected to clean streets or passages in localities where this care is left to the charge of inhabitants;

23. Those who shall have obstructed the public road by depositing or leaving there without necessity materials or any things that prevent or reduce the freedom or safety of passage;

24. Those who, in contravention of laws and regulations, shall have neglected to signal the materials stored by them or the excavations made by them on the public road;

25. Those who shall have neglected or refused to execute the regulations or orders concerning minor roads, or to obey the summons emanating from the administrative authority, to repair or demolish buildings threatening ruin;

26. Cart drivers who shall have contravened the regulations by which they are obliged to remain constantly within reach of their draft animals and their vehicles, and in a condition to guide and drive them.

Failure to supervise:

- 27. Those who shall have let roam, in an inhabited place, animals likely to harm traffic safety;
- 28. Those who shall have let roam insane persons being under their care or harmful or ferocious animals;
- 29. Those who shall have incited or shall not have restrained their dogs, when they attack or pursue passers-by, even if no harm or damage resulted therefrom.

Failure to assist:

- 30. Those who, being able to, shall have refused or neglected to perform the work or to provide the service or assistance which they shall have been required to provide, in circumstances of accident, public calamities as well as in cases of brigandage in flagrante delicto or public outcry.

Failure to observe certain obligations:

- 31. Those who shall have neglected to destroy harmful insects or animals when this care is prescribed by law or regulations;
- 32. Hotel keepers, lodging house keepers or lessors of furnished houses, who shall have neglected to register immediately in a register kept regularly, the names, titles, usual domicile, dates of entry and exit of any person who shall have slept or spent a night in their houses; those among them who shall have failed to present this register at the times determined by the regulation, or, when they shall have been required to do so, to mayors, deputies, officers or commissioners of police, or to citizens assigned to this effect.

###### Art.519.‐ Decrees containing penal provisions may provide that offenses

against the provisions they enact shall be punished by the penalties set forth in the preceding article. In case of silence of the decree and in case of offenses against all other regulations legally made by the administrative authority, the penalties set forth in the preceding article shall be applied.

## Book 9 ‐ Miscellaneous and final provisions

###### Art.520.‐ All references to the penalty of hard labor for life, either in the texts cited

in this law, or in other texts in force, are deemed to refer to the penalty of imprisonment of twenty to thirty years.

All references to the penalty of hard labor for a fixed term, either in the texts cited in this law, or in other texts in force, are deemed to refer to the penalty of imprisonment.

Any new provision of penalties provided for the repression of offenses relating to the Uniform Acts of OHADA applies in this Code.

###### Art.521.‐ This law shall come into force on the first day of the third month following the date of its

promulgation.

###### Art.522.‐ All prior provisions contrary to those of this law are repealed with the

exception of law n°034/PR/2015 of 5 August 2015 on the repression of acts of terrorism in Chad.

###### Art.523.‐ This law shall be registered and published in the Official Journal of the Republic and executed

as law of the State.
