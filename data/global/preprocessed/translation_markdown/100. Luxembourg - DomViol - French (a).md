We Henri, Grand Duke of Luxembourg, Duke of Nassau, Having heard Our Council of State;

With the assent of the Chamber of Deputies;

Having regard to the decision of the Chamber of Deputies of 15 July 2003 and that of the Council of State of 18 July 2003 stating that there is no need for a second vote;

Have ordered and order:

Art. Ier.(1) Within the framework of its missions of prevention of offenses and protection of persons, the Police, with the authorization of the State Prosecutor, expels from their home and its outbuildings persons against whom there exist indications that they are preparing to commit against a close person with whom they cohabit an offense against life or physical integrity, or that they are preparing to commit again against that person, already a victim, an offense against life or physical integrity.

Are to be considered as a close person within the meaning of this article the spouse or the person with whom the person who is the subject of an expulsion measure usually lives, his or her legitimate, adoptive or natural ascendants and descendants as well as the legitimate, adoptive or natural ascendants and descendants of the spouse or of the person with whom usually lives the person who is the subject of an expulsion measure. With regard to the descendants of the spouse or of the person with whom usually lives the person who is the subject of an expulsion measure only minor or disabled descendants are to be taken into account.

The person who is the subject of an expulsion measure may not invoke any real or personal rights in relation to the home to oppose the measure.

(2) The expulsion entails prohibition for the expelled person to enter the home and its outbuildings.

If the necessity arises for the expelled person to enter the home or its outbuildings, notwithstanding the prohibition referred to in the first paragraph, he or she may only do so in the presence of a Police officer.

(3) The Police gives the person who is the subject of the expulsion measure the possibility to take personal items of which he or she has an urgent need and to inform himself or herself about accommodation possibilities.

The person concerned communicates to the Police the address at which he or she can be contacted. If he or she cannot or will not provide an address, he or she is deemed to elect domicile at the communal administration of the place of the home, in particular for the purposes of summonses and notifications provided for in articles 1017-3 and 1017-4 of the new Code of Civil Procedure and for those of the service of a possible writ pursuant to article 1017-9 of the new Code of Civil Procedure, and the Police informs him or her thereof.

In addition, the Police has all keys giving access to the home and its outbuildings handed over and notifies the person concerned that if he or she enters or attempts to enter his or her home or its outbuildings notwithstanding the expulsion measure, he or she is exposed to the penal sanctions provided for by article 439 of the penal code.

(4) When the person concerned refuses to voluntarily comply with the expulsion measure ordered, the Police is authorized to expel him or her if necessary by force.

(5) The expulsion is the subject of a report to be drawn up immediately by the Police officers and to be transmitted to the State Prosecutor. This report mentions in particular:

- the indications at the basis of the expulsion measure;
- the day and hour of the measure ordered;
- the address provided by the expelled person allowing him or her to be contacted.

If the execution of the measure ordered had to be ensured by force, mention is made thereof in the report. The report mentions all other statements that the expelled person wishes to make.

The report is presented to the expelled person for signature. A copy is given to him or her. In case of refusal to sign, or in case of refusal to accept the delivery of the copy, mention is made of the refusal and of the reasons thereof. A copy of the report is also given to the close person to be protected. If that person, for factual or legal reasons, is not able to be given a copy, and if there is no other person able to receive the copy in lieu and place of the close person to be protected, mention is made thereof.

(6) The expulsion measure ends by operation of law at 17.00 hours on the 10th day following that of its entry into force, subject to the provisions of article 1017-2 of the new Code of Civil Procedure.

Upon expiration of the expulsion measure and in the absence of a prohibition to return to the home pronounced on the basis of articles 1017-1 and following of the new Code of Civil Procedure, the Police returns the keys to the person concerned who requests them.

## Art. II. Likewise, the Police informs a domestic violence victim assistance service of the expulsion measure

and communicates to it the address and identity of the protected person.

The expression "domestic violence victim assistance service" refers to any private or public law body whose purpose consists of assisting, guiding and advising persons who are victims of domestic violence by actively seeking contact with them.

The body must possess, for the exercise of the activities referred to above, written approval in application of the law of 8 September 1998 regulating relations between the State and bodies working in the social, family and therapeutic fields. In addition to the legal conditions provided for in article 2 of the law of 8 September 1998 mentioned above and the regulatory conditions taken in execution of that law, the body must guarantee that its activities are carried out in collaboration with the Police, the judicial authorities and other competent state authorities, taking into account the specificity of the roles respectively devolved to them, as well as in respect of the will and dignity of the protected person. Any person who, in any capacity whatsoever, participates in the activities of a domestic violence victim assistance service, obtains or receives communication of personal data in application of this article is bound by professional secrecy under the conditions and penalties of article 458 of the Penal Code.

## Art. III.

The Minister of Justice, the Police, the Public Prosecutor's Office, the domestic violence victim assistance services establish each year statistics broken down by sex, age and relationship between perpetrator and victim and indicating for each category the existence or absence of a cohabitation situation between the perpetrator and the victim, each as far as it is concerned, on the number of complaints, denunciations, expulsion measures and other types of police intervention, social interventions, prosecutions and convictions for the offenses referred to in the following articles:

327 to 330 combined with article 330-1, 372 to 377, 401 bis, 434 to 438, combined with article 438-1 and

439 paragraph 2 of the Penal Code.

## Art. IV.

A cooperation committee between professionals in the field of combating violence is created, composed of representatives of state authorities competent for the implementation of this law as well as representatives of approved domestic violence victim assistance services, responsible for centralizing and studying the statistics referred to in the previous article, examining the implementation and any practical application problems of Articles I to II of this law, Articles 1017-1 to 1017-12 of the new Code of Civil Procedure and Article 3-1 of the Code of Criminal Instruction and submitting to the Government the proposals it deems useful. A grand-ducal regulation shall determine its composition, organization and mode of operation.

Amending provisions

## Art. V. Paragraph 2 of Article 33 of the law of 31 May 1999 on the Police and the General Inspectorate of the Police is

replaced as follows:

"To this end, it ensures general surveillance and inspections in places that are legally accessible to it, exercises the powers defined by Articles I to III of the law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence, executes administrative police measures and takes material administrative police measures within its competence."

## Art. VI. The Penal Code is modified and supplemented as follows:

1°. Article 329 is replaced as follows:

"Art. 329 The threat by gestures or emblems of an attack against property, punishable by a criminal penalty, shall be punished by imprisonment from eight days to three months and a fine from 251 euros to 1,000 euros.

The threat by gestures or emblems of an attack against persons, punishable by a criminal penalty or a penalty of imprisonment of at least six months, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to one year and a fine from 251 euros to 3,000 euros."

2°. Following article 330 is inserted a new article 330-1 the content of which is as follows:

"Art. 330-1. The minimum penalties provided for by articles 327, 329 and 330 shall be increased in accordance with article 266, if the offender has committed the threat of attack against

1° the spouse or divorced spouse, the person with whom he lives or has habitually lived;

2° a legitimate or natural ascendant or his adoptive father or mother;

3° a legitimate, natural or adoptive descendant;

4° a brother or sister;

5° a legitimate or natural ascendant, the adoptive father or mother, a descendant, a brother or sister of a person referred to sub 1°;

6° a person whose particular vulnerability, due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or pregnancy, is apparent or known to their perpetrator;

7° a person who is bound to him by ties of subordination."

3°. Before the last paragraph of article 377 is inserted a new paragraph, worded as follows:

"If the victim is

1° the spouse or divorced spouse, the person with whom the offender lives or has habitually lived, 2° a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant of the offender;

3° a brother or sister;

4° a legitimate or natural ascendant, the adoptive father or mother, a descendant, a brother or sister of a person referred to sub 1°."

4°. Article 409 is replaced as follows:

"Art. 409. Shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine from 251 euros to 5,000 euros, whoever has voluntarily inflicted injuries or struck blows

1° against the spouse or divorced spouse, the person with whom he lives or has habitually lived;

2° against a legitimate or natural ascendant or his adoptive father or mother;

3° against a legitimate, natural or adoptive descendant of fourteen years or more;

4° against a brother or sister;

5° against a legitimate or natural ascendant, the adoptive father or mother, a descendant of fourteen years completed, a brother or sister of a person referred to sub 1°;

6° against a person whose particular vulnerability, due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or pregnancy, is apparent or known to their perpetrator;

7° against a person who is bound to him by ties of subordination.

When the blows or injuries have been premeditated, the penalties shall be imprisonment from one year to five years and a fine from 501 euros to 5,000 euros.

If the voluntary blows or injuries referred to in paragraph 1 have resulted in illness or personal incapacity for work, the penalties shall be imprisonment from 1 year to 5 years and a fine from 501 euros to 25,000 euros in the absence of premeditation and, otherwise, imprisonment from 5 years to 10 years and a fine from 1,000 euros to 30,000 euros.

If the voluntary blows or injuries referred to in paragraph 1 have resulted in either an apparently incurable illness, permanent personal incapacity for work, loss of absolute use of an organ, or serious mutilation, the penalties shall be imprisonment from 10 years to 15 years and a fine from 2,500 euros to 50,000 euros in the absence of premeditation and, otherwise, imprisonment from 15 years to 20 years and a fine from 3,000 euros to 50,000 euros. If the voluntary blows or injuries referred to in paragraph 1 have caused death, without intent to cause it, the offender shall be punished by imprisonment from 20 years to 30 years, in the absence of premeditation of these acts of violence, and life imprisonment, otherwise.

If the voluntary blows or injuries referred to in this article have been committed against a person with whom the offender cohabits, the court may also pronounce against the convicted person all or part of the following prohibitions:

- the prohibition from approaching the victim's dwelling beyond a distance to be determined;
- the prohibition from making contact with the victim;
- the prohibition from approaching the victim beyond a distance to be determined."

5°. Following article 438 is inserted an article 438-1 worded as follows:

"Art. 438-1. In the cases mentioned in articles 434 to 438, the minimum penalties provided for by these articles shall be increased in accordance with article 266, when the offender has committed the crime or offense against

1° his spouse or divorced spouse, the person with whom he lives or has habitually lived;

2° a legitimate or natural ascendant or his adoptive father or mother;

3° a legitimate, natural or adoptive descendant;

4° a brother or sister;

5° a legitimate or natural ascendant, the adoptive father or mother, a descendant, a brother or sister of a person referred to sub 1°;

6° a person whose particular vulnerability, due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or pregnancy, is apparent or known to their perpetrator;

7° a person who is bound to him by ties of subordination."

6°. Article 439 is supplemented by a new paragraph worded as follows:

"Shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years and a fine from 501 euros to 5,000 euros, whoever has entered or attempted to enter a house, apartment, room or dwelling inhabited by a person with whom he has cohabited, or their outbuildings, either by means of threats or violence against persons, or by means of breaking in, climbing or false keys, or even by means of keys if he acts in violation of an eviction measure governed by article I of the law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence, an interim order temporarily assigning the common dwelling to his spouse, an order prohibiting his return to the domicile in accordance with article 1017-1 or 1017-7 of the new Code of Civil Procedure."

7°. Article 448 is supplemented by a new paragraph the content of which is as follows:

"When the offender has committed the offense against

1° his spouse or divorced spouse or the person with whom he lives or has habitually lived;

2° a legitimate or natural ascendant or his adoptive father or mother;

3° a legitimate, natural or adoptive descendant;

4° a brother or sister;

5° a legitimate or natural ascendant, the adoptive father or mother, a descendant, a brother or sister of a person referred to sub 1°;

6° a person whose particular vulnerability, due to age, illness, infirmity, physical or mental deficiency or pregnancy, is apparent or known to their perpetrator;

7° a person who is bound to him by ties of subordination the minimum penalties provided for by the first paragraph shall be increased in accordance with article 266."

## Art. VII. The Code of Criminal Instruction is amended and supplemented as follows:

1°. Following the current article 3, a new article 3-1 is inserted as follows:

"Art. 3-1. Any association of national importance with legal personality and approved by the Minister of Justice may exercise the rights recognized to the civil party with respect to acts constituting an offense within the meaning of articles 375, 401 bis or 409 of the Penal Code or articles 444 (2), 453, 454, 455, 456, 457, 457-1, 457-2, 457-3 and 457-4 of the Penal Code and causing direct or indirect harm to the collective interests they have as their object to defend, even if they do not justify a material or moral interest and even if the collective interest in which they act is entirely covered by the social interest whose defense is ensured by the public prosecutor. When it concerns an offense within the meaning of articles 444 (2), 453, 454, 455, 456, 457, 457-1, 457-2, 457-3 and 457-4 of the Penal Code committed against persons considered individually or an offense within the meaning of articles 375, 401bis or 409 of the Penal Code, the association may only exercise the rights recognized to the civil party by principal action on the condition that these persons expressly declare in writing that they do not oppose it." 2°. Article 24 (5) is replaced as follows:

"(5) The State Prosecutor may, prior to his decision on the public action, decide to resort to mediation if it appears to him that such a measure is likely to ensure the repair of the damage caused to the victim, or to put an end to the disturbance resulting from the offense, or to contribute to the rehabilitation of the perpetrator of the offense. However, recourse to mediation is excluded in the presence of offenses against persons with whom the perpetrator cohabits.

The mediator is bound by professional secrecy."

## Art. VIII. The new Code of Civil Procedure is amended and supplemented as follows:

In the second part, in Book I, following title VII entitled "On judicial intervention concerning the rights of spouses", a new title VII bis entitled "On judicial intervention in cases of domestic violence" is inserted as follows:

"Title VII bis. - On judicial intervention in cases of domestic violence

Section 1: On the prohibition of return to the residence following expulsion - administrative police measure

## Art. 1017-1. In cases where one of the persons listed in the following paragraph has benefited from the protection of an

expulsion measure based on article I of the law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence, he or she may, by simple petition, request the president of the district court to order against the expelled person a prohibition of return to the residence for a maximum period of three months following the expiration of the expulsion measure, without regard to any real or personal rights of the expelled person in relation to the residence. The following are authorized to make such a request, 1° the spouse of the expelled person or the person with whom the expelled person usually lives, the legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants and descendants of the expelled person;

2° the legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants, the legitimate, natural or adoptive descendants who are minors or disabled, of the spouse of the expelled person or of the person with whom the expelled person usually lives, provided that they cohabited with the expelled person before his or her expulsion and justify that the residence meets their urgent housing needs.

The prohibition referred to in the first paragraph ends automatically as soon as a decision is made to settle the residence of the spouses during divorce proceedings.

## Art. 1017-2. The petition must be filed no later than the tenth day following the entry into force of the

expulsion measure based on article I of the aforementioned law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence. If the application has been filed in accordance with the provisions of this paragraph, the expulsion shall continue to have effect pending the order of the president to be issued.

It is filed at the registry by the interested party or by his or her agent and recorded in a special register, on unstamped paper, kept at the court registry.

The declaration contains, on pain of nullity:

1° the surnames, first names, professions of the plaintiff and defendant parties;

2° the residence in question as well as the address that the defendant party communicated to the Police in application of article I (3), paragraph 2 of the law of 8 September 2003 on domestic violence, unless he or she failed to do so, in which case the mention of the residence is sufficient;

3° the subject of the request and the statement of grounds.

A copy of the minutes drawn up at the time of expulsion is attached to the petition.

## Art. 1017-3.

The clerk summons the parties by registered mail informing them of the day, time and place of the hearing. He attaches a copy of the petition for the defendant. The summons contains, on pain of nullity, the mention of article 80 paragraph 1. A copy of the summons is also sent to the police. The hearing may be set on a public holiday or on a day usually not worked.

A ruling is made urgently on the application by order of the president.

The order pronouncing the prohibition of return to the residence is enforceable provisionally and without surety, on the original and before registration.

The order is notified through the registry. The clerk also sends a copy of the order to the Police. "Art. 1017-4. The order may be appealed within fifteen days from notification. The appeal is brought before the Court of Appeal. It is filed by depositing a reasoned petition at the registry of the district court. The date of filing is recorded by the clerk on the original petition. Within three days of the filing of the petition, the file is transmitted to the Court of Appeal. The appeal is judged according to the same procedure as at first instance.

In case of default, the order is subject to opposition within eight days from notification, which runs simultaneously with the appeal period. The opposition consists of a declaration to be made at the registry of the district court.

The judgment rendered on appeal is not subject to opposition."

## Art. 1017-5.

(1) The parties shall be required to appear in person or through a lawyer.

(2) The parties may be assisted or represented by: a lawyer, their spouse or the person with whom they usually live, their relatives or in-laws in the direct line, their relatives or in-laws in the collateral line up to and including the third degree, persons exclusively attached to their personal service or to their business, a collaborator of a service for assistance to victims of domestic violence. The representative, if not a lawyer, must justify a special power.

## Art. 1017-6. The president may, at the request of the requesting party, order penalties in the form of

periodic penalty payments.

Section 2: On various other prohibitions and injunctions in matters of violence

## Art. 1017-7.

When a person makes the continuation of common life intolerable for a close person, either because they assault or threaten to assault them or because they have towards them a behaviour that seriously affects their mental health, the president of the district court orders them, upon the request of the concerned person, to leave the dwelling and its outbuildings and prohibits them from returning before the expiration of a maximum period of three months, without regard to any real or personal rights of the defendant party in relation to the dwelling.

The following are authorized to make such a request, 1° the spouse or the person with whom the defendant person usually lives, legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants and descendants;

2° the legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants, the legitimate, natural or adoptive descendants who are minors or disabled, of the spouse or of the person with whom the defendant party usually lives, provided they justify that the dwelling satisfies their urgent housing needs and that they cohabit or have cohabited with the defendant party during the three months preceding the request.

The prohibition referred to in the first paragraph ends automatically as soon as a decision is made to settle the residence of the spouses during divorce proceedings.

## Art. 1017-8. When a person assaults or threatens to assault a close person or when they have towards them

a behaviour that seriously affects their mental health and thus makes any encounter with them intolerable, the president of the district court orders, upon the request of the concerned person, all or part of the injunctions or prohibitions listed below, provided that they do not go against fundamental and legitimate interests of the defendant party:

- the prohibition to make contact with the plaintiff party;
- the prohibition to send messages to the plaintiff party;
- the prohibition to approach the plaintiff party within a distance to be defined;
- the prohibition to establish their residence in the same neighbourhood as the plaintiff party;
- the prohibition to frequent certain places;
- the prohibition to take certain routes;
- the renewable prohibition, for a duration to be set by the President, to accommodate their child or children or to see their child or children outside a specialized structure designated by them pending any other judicial decision to be made in matters of custody and visitation rights;
- the injunction to allow the plaintiff party to enter the common dwelling to remove their personal belongings.

The following are authorized to make such a request

1° the spouse or divorced spouse, the person with whom the defendant party lives or has lived usually, legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants, legitimate, natural or adoptive descendants;

2° the legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendants, the legitimate, natural or adoptive descendants who are minors or disabled of the spouse or divorced spouse or of the person with whom the defendant party lives or has lived usually.

## Art. 1017-9. The request is brought by summons to a hearing held on the day and at the time indicated by

the president. The hearing may be held on a public holiday or a day usually not worked.

The summons is exempted from stamp and registration duties and from the formality of registration.

## Art. 1017-10.

(1) The parties shall be required to appear in person or through a lawyer.

(2) The parties may be assisted or represented by: a lawyer, their spouse or the person with whom they usually live, their relatives or in-laws in direct line, their relatives or in-laws in collateral line up to and including the third degree, persons exclusively attached to their personal service or to their business, a collaborator of a domestic violence victim assistance service. The representative, if not a lawyer, must provide proof of a special power of attorney.

## Art. 1017-11. The request is decided upon urgently.

## Art. 1017-12. The provisions of articles 938 to 940 are applicable.»
