### Title XII - Of crimes against the person

#### Chapter III - Of crimes against individual freedom

##### Section II - Of crimes against personal freedom

(1)

Anyone who, outside the cases provided for in said article, commits sexual acts with a person who, at the time of the act:

- 1) has not completed fourteen years of age (2)
- 2) has not completed sixteen years of age, when the offender is the ascendant, the parent, including adoptive parent, or their cohabitant, the guardian, or another person to whom, for reasons of care, education, instruction, supervision or custody, the minor is entrusted or who has, with the latter, a cohabitation relationship (3)

Outside the cases provided for in Article 609 bis, the ascendant, the parent, including adoptive parent, or their cohabitant, the guardian, or another person to whom, for reasons of care, education, instruction, supervision or custody, the minor is entrusted, or who has with the latter a cohabitation relationship, who, with the abuse of the powers connected to their position, commits sexual acts with a minor person who has completed sixteen years of age, is punished with imprisonment from three to six years (4)

Outside the cases provided for in the preceding paragraphs, anyone who commits sexual acts with a minor person who has completed fourteen years of age, abusing the trust earned with the minor or the authority or influence exercised over them by reason of their own quality or the office held or family, domestic, work, cohabitation or hospitality relationships, is punished with imprisonment up to four years (5)

The penalty is increased:
- 1) if the commission of sexual acts with the minor who has not completed fourteen years of age occurs in exchange for money or any other benefit, even if only promised;
- 2) if the crime is committed by multiple persons gathered together;
- 3) if the crime is committed by a person who is part of a criminal association and for the purpose of facilitating its activity;
- 4) if from the act, due to the reiteration of the conduct, serious harm results to the minor;
- 5) if from the act danger to life results for the minor (6) (7)

A minor is not punishable who, outside the cases provided for in Article 609 bis, commits sexual acts with a minor who has completed thirteen years of age, if the age difference between the subjects does not exceed four years (8)

In cases of lesser gravity the penalty is reduced by a measure not exceeding two-thirds (9)

The penalty of Article 609 ter, second paragraph, applies if the victim has not completed ten years of age.

Explanation of art. 609 quater Criminal Code

Sexual freedom is acquired, in our legal system, upon completion of the fourteenth year of age. For such reasons, the rule in question punishes anyone who commits sexual acts with a person under fourteen, with the latter's consent (the much more serious crime referred to in article

609 bis being otherwise relevant).

The same penalty applies to one who commits consensual sexual acts with a person under sixteen years of age, when the offender is a person who exercises authority over the minor. Thus, while in other cases an adult may lawfully commit sexual acts with a person over fourteen, the legislator has instead provided for punishability against those who have particular duties of care, instruction and custody towards a person under sixteen who has completed fourteen years.

With a lesser penalty is instead punished the same person in a position of supremacy or in whose charge exist duties of care, custody and instruction, if they commit sexual acts with a minor, even over sixteen, but only in the case where an abuse of the powers connected to their position is found, thereby subjugating the victim and exploiting their own position.

By way of derogation from the above, a minor who commits (consensual) sexual acts with a person under thirteen years of age is not punishable, if the age difference does not exceed three years (a sixteen-year-old can validly have sexual relations with a thirteen-year-old).

In the fourth paragraph an attenuating circumstance with special and indefinite effect is provided for, when the compression of sexual freedom is minimal, in consideration of the concrete fact and circumstances, while in the last paragraph the application of article

609 ter (aggravated sexual violence) is provided for, in the case where the passive subject is under ten years of age, equating possible consent to lack of consent, given the age of the victim and the consequent absolute impossibility of sexual self-determination.

Maxims relating to art. 609 quater Criminal Code

Cass. pen. n. 31256/2025

In matters of sexual violence against minors, an expert opinion regarding the capacity to testify of the victim is not indispensable if no pathological elements emerge that could cast doubt on said capacity, even when the minor has in the meantime reached the age of majority. Sez. III, judgment n. 31256 of 29 May 2025

Cass. pen. n. 46261/2024

The crime of sexual acts with a minor, referred to in art. 609-quater, paragraph three, Criminal Code, introduced by art. 20 of Law 23 December 2021, n. 238, being placed to protect the correct development of their sexuality, is configured in cases of abuse of authority or influence exercised and of instrumentalization or exploitation of the fiduciary relationship, correlated to the quality or office held by the acting subject or to family, domestic, work, cohabitation or hospitality relationships with the victim, whose consent is, therefore, conditioned, such that conduct of coercion and that of inducement with abuse of the condition of physical or psychological inferiority of the minor, punished pursuant to the provision of art. 609-bis Criminal Code, go beyond the perimeter of such crime. Sez. IV, judgment n. 46261 of 30 October 2024

Cass. pen. n. 43887/2024

In matters of precautionary personal measures, when there are serious indications of guilt regarding the crime referred to in art. 609-quater Criminal Code (sexual acts with a minor), the consent of the minor under fourteen is irrelevant. In the evaluation of serious indications, the judge may also consider the awareness of the investigated person of the age of the victim, even though contacts were suspended immediately after revelation of the actual age. Sez. III, judgment n. 43887 of 9 October 2024

Cass. pen. n. 41887/2023

In matters of accusatory statements made to third parties by the victim of the crime of sexual acts with a minor, of age less than ten years at the time of the facts, useful evaluation tools to verify that the declarant did not intend to please the interlocutor and adapt to their expectations include the reconstruction of the genesis of the news of the crime, the emotional reactions, the questions of the adults involved and the reasons for possible amplification over time of the narration. Sez. III, judgment n. 41887 of 30 June 2023

Cass. pen. n. 43225/2023

Respect for the operating protocols provided by the Charter of Noto in cases where it is necessary to proceed with the hearing of a minor victim of abuse does not constitute a methodological constraint that must be respected by the judge on pain of unusability of the evidence thus acquired, given that such methods merely provide suggestions aimed at guaranteeing in reality not only the reliability of the minor's statements, but also, if not above all, to protect, through the use of technical tools and also specialized competences, the psychological protection of the same on the occasion of the taking of evidence, in order to avoid the occurrence of phenomena of secondary victimization of the victim. Sez. III, judgment n. 43225 of 10 May 2023

Cass. pen. n. 26809/2023

The crime referred to in art. 609-quater Criminal Code is not necessarily characterized by physical contact between the agent and the victim, being configurable even in the case where one finds sexual satisfaction from the fact of witnessing the execution of sexual acts by the other. Sez. III, judgment n. 26809 of 4 April 2023

Cass. pen. n. 8735/2022

In matters of sexual acts with a minor, for purposes of recognizing the attenuating circumstance of lesser gravity referred to in art. 609-quater, paragraph five, Criminal Code, a global evaluation of the fact is necessary in which the means, the executive methods, the degree of coercion exercised on the victim and the physical and psychological conditions of the latter assume relevance, also in relation to age, while, for purposes of its denial, the presence of even a single element of proclaimed gravity is sufficient. (Case in which the Court deemed exempt from censure the decision of non-recognition of the reduction in face of the commission, by the accused, of sexual acts with a minor over sixteen, daughter of his own cohabitant, which were inserted in an affective and sexual relationship indicative of substantial abuse). Sez. III, judgment n. 8735 of 24 November 2022

Cass. pen. n. 2849/2022

In matters of sexual crimes, accusations coming from a minor victim of the crime referred to in art. 609-quater, especially if acquired through "hearsay" statements, impose a critical judicial examination inspired by criteria of neutrality and rigor, which requires the appropriate aid of the sciences of pedagogy, psychology and sexology, relevant in the matter, as their reliability can be vitiated by hetero-induced suggestions. (Case in which the Court deemed correct the acquittal ruling which, in overturning the conviction founded, among other things, on accusatory statements coming only from "hearsay" witnesses, had highlighted that inductive interventions on the victim, aged three years, who remained silent both during the examination conducted in pre-trial evidentiary proceedings, subject to collected expert opinion, in a non-genuine manner, with the aid of an expert, and in the trial phase and whose hearing had not been renewed in second instance, also to avoid risks of secondary victimization, could not be excluded). Sez. III, judgment n. 2849 of 27 October 2022

Cass. pen. n. 2533/2022

Suspension of execution of the sentence for the crime of sexual acts with a minor referred to in art. 609-quater Criminal Code cannot be ordered, even in the case of recognition of the special attenuating circumstance provided for by paragraph five of the same article. Sez. I, judgment n. 2533 of 13 September 2022

Cass. pen. n. 25619/2022

The crime of sexual acts with a minor referred to in art. 609-quater Criminal Code has an instantaneous nature, and not habitual or permanent, as it is perfected with the realization of the typical fact, that is with the commission of the sexual act that exhausts its offense, such that it is from the realization of the individual contested conduct, even if unified by the bond of continuation, that the prescription period must be calculated. Sez. III, judgment n. 25619 of 16 March 2022

Cass. pen. n. 3705/2021

The attempt of the crime provided for by art. 609-quater Criminal Code is configurable when, even in the absence of physical contact between the subjects involved, the conduct held by the accused presents the requirements of suitability and unequivocality of the invitation to commit sexual acts, being directed to achieve the satisfaction of the agent's instincts through the violation of the freedom of self-determination of the victim. Sez. III, judgment n. 3705 of 1 December 2021

Cass. pen. n. 7140/2020

In matters of sexual acts with a minor person aged between sixteen and eighteen years, referred to in art. 609-quater, second paragraph, Criminal Code, the notion of abuse of power postulates that the agent obtained the consent of the victim to the commission of sexual acts through the distorted use of the powers of directive and command connected to their position of supremacy. (Case relating to sexual acts committed with a minor over sixteen by a person who held, towards the said person, the qualified position of "tutor" during the conduct of a work "internship"). Sez. III, judgment n. 7140 of 18 December 2020

Cass. pen. n. 4903/2020

For purposes of integration of the crime of sexual acts with a minor aged between fourteen and sixteen years referred to in art. 609-quater, paragraph one, n. 2, Criminal Code, the title of entrustment of the minor is relevant, which determines the establishment of a fiduciary relationship that places the agent in a condition of pre-eminence and authoritativeness suitable to induce the minor to give consent to sexual acts, and not the place in which the sexual acts are consummated, which may be different from that in which the reasons of supervision and custody of the entrustment exist. (In the case the Court deemed immune from censure the recognition of responsibility for the crime in question operated by the contested judgment against the janitor of the school attended by a minor of sixteen years, for the sexual acts with her committed not only inside the school but also in the premises of a parish). Sez. IV, judgment n. 4903 of 21 January 2020

Cass. pen. n. 43538/2019

In matters of sexual acts with a minor, in the cases provided for by art. 609 quater, paragraph one, n. 2 Criminal Code, the personal qualities of the person entrusted do not affect the configurability of the fiduciary relationship with the victim, solely founded on the existence of a qualified entrustment. Sez. III, judgment n. 43538 of 24 October 2019

Cass. pen. n. 17509/2019

The conduct consisting in requesting a minor, during a telephone conversation, to commit sexual acts, to film them and to send them immediately to the interlocutor integrates the crime referred to in art. 609-quater Criminal Code, this case not being distinguished from that of the minor who commits sexual acts during a video-call or video-conversation. Sez. III, judgment n. 17509 of 24 April 2019

Cass. pen. n. 48377/2018

In matters of sexual acts with a minor, for purposes of recognition of the attenuating circumstance of lesser gravity provided for by art. 609-quater, paragraph four, Criminal Code, it is for the trial judge, with an evaluation that escapes the scrutiny of legitimacy, to determine what is the degree of compression of the legal interest, comparing among themselves the negative and positive elements, with reference to the degree of coercion exercised, to the psychophysical conditions, in relation to age and to the also psychological damage inflicted on the minor. Sez. III, judgment n. 48377 of 24 October 2018

Cass. pen. n. 29618/2018

In matters of sexual acts with minors, the possible limitation of the acts committed to the detriment of the passive subject of the crime to erotic practices not involving physical conjunction does not lead to any automatic qualification of the fact as lesser gravity, it being necessary to conduct, for purposes of recognition of the special attenuating circumstance provided for by art. 609-quater, paragraph four, Criminal Code, a comprehensive evaluation of the historical episode, of its individual elements, of the injury inflicted on the sexual freedom of the passive subject of the crime and of the intensity of the damage, also psychological, suffered by the latter. Sez. III, judgment n. 29618 of 2 July 2018

Cass. pen. n. 53135/2017

In matters of sexual acts with a minor, the cohabitation relationship required for integration of the crime referred to in art. 609-quater, paragraph one n. 2) Criminal Code, is relevant regardless of the abuse of a dominant or authoritative position over the cohabiting minor under sixteen years, element, the latter, provided instead in the case of a passive subject over sixteen, referred to in paragraph two of the same article. Sez. III, judgment n. 53135 of 22 November 2017

Cass. pen. n. 46461/2017

In matters of sexual acts with a minor, for purposes of recognition of the reduction for cases of lesser gravity referred to in art. 609-quater, fourth paragraph, Criminal Code, reference must be made to the global evaluation of the fact, in which the means, the executive methods, the degree of coercion exercised on the victim, the physical and psychological conditions of the latter, also in relation to age, assume relevance; the violent conduct held by the accused after the consummation of the crime is not, therefore, obstructive to this end, being conduct subsequent to the commission of the sexual acts as such. (In application of the principle the Supreme Court annulled with remand the judgment of the trial judges who had deemed obstructive to recognition of the attenuating circumstance the circumstance that the accused, some days after the commission of the sexual acts, had attempted to remove the minor from the mother with violence, despite the absence of physical coercion in the commission of the sexual acts and the existence of an initial consent of the victim, which became stable within a lasting relationship with the accused). Sez. III, judgment n. 46461 of 10 October 2017

Cass. pen. n. 45749/2017

For purposes of configurability of the crime referred to in art. 609-quater, paragraph one, n. 2), Criminal Code, which makes criminally relevant sexual acts with a person under sixteen where "the offender is the parent", with consequent ex officio prosecution pursuant to art. 609-septies, paragraph three, n. 2), Criminal Code, it is not necessary that the latter be the material author of the conduct, it being sufficient even only that the same holds the role of accomplice. Sez. III, judgment n. 45749 of 5 October 2017

Cass. pen. n. 34512/2017

In matters of sexual acts with a minor, for purposes of recognition of the attenuating circumstance for cases of lesser gravity, referred to in art. 609 - quater, paragraph 4, Criminal Code, a negative evaluation element is the circumstance that the sexual acts are inserted within an "amorous relationship" with the minor, such situation being indicative, on one hand, of a substantial abuse to the detriment of the victim and, on the other, of the repetition of sexual acts for a considerable period of time. Sez. III, judgment n. 34512 of 14 July 2017

Cass. pen. n. 11559/2017

The condition of entrustment in custody of the minor, required for purposes of ex officio prosecution of the crime of sexual acts with a minor, may also consist of a temporary or occasional entrustment. (In application of the principle, the S.C. deemed correct the configuration of the aggravating circumstance in a case in which the minor had been entrusted by the parents, for the weekend, to the aunt and her cohabitant, who had consummated a sexual relationship with the victim at a moment when he remained the only adult inside the dwelling). Sez. III, judgment n. 11559 of 10 March 2017

Cass. pen. n. 53672/2016

In matters of sexual crimes, the conduct to the detriment of a person under sixteen years of age, carried out by the ascendant or by another subject with this in a qualified relationship (art. 609-quater, paragraph one n. 2, Criminal Code), in exchange for the promise or giving of money or other benefit, integrates the crime provided for by art. 609 quater Criminal Code, which absorbs the less afflictive case typified in art. 600 bis, second paragraph, Criminal Code, which sanctions remunerated sexual acts with a person under eighteen years, unless the fact constitutes a more serious crime. Sez. III, judgment n. 53672 of 16 December 2016

Cass. pen. n. 47980/2016

For purposes of consummation of the crime referred to in art. 609-quater Criminal Code, the commission of a sexual act with a minor is sufficient, the physical or emotional involvement of the latter or the awareness by the latter of the offense brought to the development of their sexual personality not being necessary. (Case relating to sexual acts of self-eroticism committed by the accused while holding the niece of eleven months on his knees). Sez. III, judgment n. 47980 of 14 November 2016

Cass. pen. n. 32339/2015

The crime of child prostitution, which punishes the conduct of inducement, facilitation or exploitation of prostitution of a person under eighteen years, concurs with that of sexual acts with a minor, both for the different juridical objectivity and for the diversity of the constituent elements. (In reasoning, the Court observed that the additional and dominant element of the merchandising of the body makes the case referred to in art. 600-bis Criminal Code ontologically different from that referred to in art. 609-quater Criminal Code). Sez. III, judgment n. 32339 of 23 July 2015

Cass. pen. n. 27123/2015

The attempt of the crime of sexual acts with a minor is configurable when, even in the absence of physical contact between the subjects involved, the conduct held by the accused presents the requirements of suitability and unequivocality of the invitation to commit sexual acts, as the same is specifically directed to achieve the satisfaction of the sexual instincts of the agent through the violation of the freedom of self-determination of the victim in the sexual sphere. (Case in which the Court deemed immune from censure the contested judgment which had found the elements of the crime referred to in arts. 56 and 609 quater Criminal Procedure Code, having regard to a request directed by the father to the minor daughter to touch his genital organs, also making reference to the prior commission of sexual acts). Sez. III, judgment n. 27123 of 30 June 2015

Cass. pen. n. 24342/2015

The condition of entrustment for reasons of education and instruction provided for the crime of sexual acts with a minor (art. 609-quater, paragraph one, n. 2, Criminal Code) pertains to any fiduciary relationship, even temporary or occasional, that is established between entrustor and person entrusted. (Case in which the Court deemed that the contested judgment had correctly judged the condition of entrustment to be existing in relation to conduct carried out, for the first time, at the end of a social dinner, held among those who frequented the gym at which the same accused taught, on the occasion of an accompaniment by the latter performed upon request of the victim's mother, and then continued in the dwelling of the same, to which the minor went, expressly authorized by the parent, to receive tutoring in school subjects). Sez. III, judgment n. 24342 of 8 June 2015

Cass. pen. n. 16349/2015

The condition of entrustment of the minor, required for integration of the crime of sexual acts with a minor referred to in art. 609 quater, paragraph one, n. 2, Criminal Code, is not configurable against the driver of a route bus, in the absence of an explicit act of entrustment by the parents. (In reasoning, the Court excluded that the driver of a route bus can be deemed to have automatically in entrustment the minors on board the vehicle driven by him, since on the same the presence of such subjects is only possible, unlike what happens in the school bus, which is a means ontologically deputized to the transport of minors). Sez. III, judgment n. 16349 of 20 April 2015

Cass. pen. n. 29988/2013

In the case of consenting and remunerated sexual relations with a minor, if there has been inducement, facilitation or exploitation of prostitution, the provision of art. 600-bis, paragraph one, always applies, regardless of the age of the victim; if the activity of inducement to prostitution was lacking, the conduct is punished: a) if it concerns a person under fourteen years, pursuant to art. 609 quater; b) if the minor has an age between fourteen and sixteen years, pursuant to art. 609 bis, third paragraph; c) if aged between sixteen and eighteen years pursuant to art. 609 bis second paragraph. Sez. I, judgment n. 29988 of 12 July 2013

Cass. pen. n. 25822/2013

The crime referred to in art. 609 quater Criminal Code is not necessarily characterized by physical contact between the victim and the agent, existing also when the author of the crime finds sexual satisfaction from the fact of witnessing the execution of sexual acts by the victim. (Case of conduct perfected through telematic communication, through which the perpetrator had induced the minor victims to commit on themselves sexual acts of self-eroticism). Sez. III, judgment n. 25822 of 12 June 2013

Cass. pen. n. 18662/2013

In matters of sexual violence, the attenuating circumstance provided for by art.609 quater Criminal Code for cases of lesser gravity must be considered applicable, like the homologous one provided for by art. 609 bis, third paragraph same code, in all those cases in which - having regard to the means, to the executive methods and to the circumstances of the action - it is possible to consider that the sexual personal freedom of the victim was compressed in a not serious manner. (In the case, the attenuating circumstance was denied in consideration of the typology of sexual acts, including oral relations which had caused very painful sensations to the victim). Sez. IV, judgment n. 18662 of 26 April 2013

Cass. pen. n. 2835/2012

The condition of entrustment in custody of the minor, provided for the crime of sexual acts with a minor (art. 609 quater, paragraph one, n. 2, Criminal Code), is independent from an act of formal entrustment by the parent of the victim to the perpetrator, as it constitutes a factual datum that is independent from formal relationships between the person entrusted and the subject having custody over the minor, being able to also have a temporary and occasional character. Sez. III, judgment n. 2835 of 24 January 2012

Cass. pen. n. 46637/2011

The simple reiterated invitation, formulated towards a minor under fourteen, to the consummation of a sexual relationship, does not integrate, for unsuitability of the acts, the attempt of the crime referred to in art. 609 quater Criminal Code. Sez. III, judgment n. 46637 of 15 December 2011

Cass. pen. n. 37509/2011

The conduct of sexual acts with a minor by the parent or by another qualified person falls in any case within the hypothesis referred to in art. 609 quater n. 2 Criminal Code also when the victim is under fourteen, with consequent applicability, also in such case, of the accessory penalty of loss of parental authority. Sez. III, judgment n. 37509 of 18 October 2011

Cass. pen. n. 30548/2011

The aggravating circumstance referred to in art. 61, n. 11 Criminal Code (cohabitation) concurs with the aggravating circumstance provided for the crime of sexual acts with a minor, where committed to the detriment of a victim under ten years (art. 609 quater, paragraph five, Criminal Code), as the latter is relative to the age of the victim, while the other refers to the objective fact of the abuse of relationships deriving from cohabitation. (In the case, the Court excluded that the common aggravating circumstance could be deemed absorbed by the special one). Sez. III, judgment n. 30548 of 2 August 2011

Cass. pen. n. 35809/2010

The condition of entrustment of the minor, required for integration of the crime of sexual acts with a minor (art. 609 quater, paragraph one, n. 2, Criminal Code), is configurable against the school collaborator, as a figure assigned to tasks of reception, surveillance and supervision over students on the occasion of the momentary absence of teachers and on the occasion of their transfer in the school premises to other locations. Sez. III, judgment n. 35809 of 6 October 2010

Cass. pen. n. 27588/2010

The crime of sexual acts with a minor is configured regardless of whether or not there is consent of the victim, not only because violence is presumed by law, but also because the victim is considered immature and incapable of consciously disposing of their own body for sexual purposes. Sez. III, judgment n. 27588 of 15 July 2010

Cass. pen. n. 27272/2010

The subjective elements referred to in art. 133, second paragraph, Criminal Code, are not relevant for purposes of configurability of the hypothesis of lesser gravity of the crime of sexual violence, the mitigation of the penalty not responding to the need for adaptation to the culpability of the perpetrator and to the circumstances pertaining to their person but to the lesser harmfulness of the fact, to be related to the degree of violation of the legal interest of the sexual freedom of the victim. Sez. III, judgment n. 27272 of 14 July 2010

Cass. pen. n. 11252/2010

The attenuating circumstance of lesser gravity in the crime of sexual acts with a minor occurs if the acts committed do not involve a significant compromise of the psycho-physical integrity of the victim, with no relevance whatsoever, instead, for purposes of recognition of the attenuating circumstance, the possible consent of the same. (In reasoning the Court specified that precisely taking advantage of relationships of sympathy, confidence, affection or entrustment to have sexual relations with a minor, constitutes a pollution and corruption of their personality). Sez. III, judgment n. 11252 of 24 March 2010

Cass. pen. n. 20246/2009

The constitution as civil party "in their own right" of the parents of the minor, victim of the crime of sexual acts with a minor, is admissible, as such crime, in addition to directly harming the minor, affects the interest of the parents in the psychophysical integrity of the child and their moral sphere. Sez. III, judgment n. 20246 of 14 May 2009

Cass. pen. n. 15053/2009

The conduct consisting in inviting the victim to witness pornographic screenings accompanied by acts of exhibitionism and self-eroticism integrates the crime of corruption of a minor. (Case in which the perpetrator did not limit himself to showing himself naked to the minor but invited him to touch himself during the screening of pornographic films). Sez. III, judgment n. 15053 of 8 April 2009

Cass. pen. n.12987/2009

With regard to sexual offences, for the purposes of establishing attempted sexual acts with a minor in cases where contact between the offender and the minor occurs through remote communication, it is necessary to ascertain, on the one hand, the unequivocal intention of the agent to satisfy their own concupiscence and, on the other hand, the objective suitability of the conduct to violate the victim's freedom of sexual self-determination. (Case in which the offender had sent an SMS via mobile phone to a minor in an attempt to induce them to perform acts of self-eroticism on their own person). Section III, judgment no. 12987 of 25 March 2009

Cass. pen. n. 347/2009

With regard to sexual offences, the mitigating circumstance of the wilful contribution of the injured party is incompatible with the offence of sexual acts with a minor, since any consent by the victim does not constitute a cause or concurrent cause of the event. Section III, judgment no. 347 of 9 January 2009

Cass. pen. n. 44971/2007

With regard to the assessment of the testimony of a minor victim of the offence of sexual violence, there is no need for psychological investigation in relation to the statements of an adolescent person, whose natural maturation is connected to age, where the presence of elements such as a particular predisposition to fantasy elaboration or suggestion, such as to render the narrative doubtful, can be excluded. Section III, judgment no. 44971 of 4 December 2007

Cass. pen. n. 36389/2007

With regard to offences against individual freedom, for the purposes of establishing the offences of sexual violence (art. 609 bis c.p.), sexual acts with a minor (art. 609 quater c.p.) and corruption of a minor (art. 609 quinquies c.p.), the active or passive role assumed by the accused in the context of the relationship with the victim is irrelevant. (Case in which the accused, a catechism teacher of the victim, had argued that his homosexual tendency excluded its establishment). With regard to sexual acts with a minor, the existence of a relationship of trust for reasons of education and instruction (art. 609 quater, first paragraph, no. 2 c.p.) is assumed by the legislature as the key to interpreting a responsibility presumed per se. (Case in which the accused was the catechism teacher of the victim). Section III, judgment no. 36389 of 4 October 2007

Cass. pen. n. 35875/2007

Attempted sexual violence is established when, even in the absence of physical contact between the accused and the injured party, the conduct adopted by the former demonstrates the subjective requirement of the intention to achieve satisfaction of one's own sexual instincts and the objective requirement of suitability to violate the victim's freedom of self-determination in the sexual sphere. (In the present case, the occurrence of the hypothesis of attempt was excluded and that of the consummated offence was found in the conduct of a person who, having attracted a minor into his bedroom, had made her sit on his knees and, lifting her skirt, had touched her near the vagina). Section III, judgment no. 35875 of 1 October 2007

Cass. pen. n. 21815/2007

With regard to sexual acts with a minor under sixteen, trust for reasons of care, education, instruction, supervision or custody is established even when the active subject is not the teacher of the class in which the minor is placed, but belongs to the same school structure, since in this case too there exists between teacher and pupil that relationship of trust contemplated by the criminal law. Section III, judgment no. 21815 of 5 June 2007

Cass. pen. n. 19425/2007

Art. 609 quater c.p. (sexual acts with a minor) establishes an autonomous hypothesis of offence and not an aggravating circumstance of the offence of sexual violence provided for by the preceding art. 609 bis c.p. (Principle affirmed, in the present case, for the purposes of the operation of the maximum duration of precautionary custody provided for by the combined provisions of arts. 303, first paragraph, lett. a), no. 3, and 407, second paragraph no. 7 bis c.p.p.). Section III, judgment no. 19425 of 18 May 2007

Cass. pen. n. 16843/2007

With regard to sexual offences committed against a person under fourteen, punished by the first paragraph of art. 609 quater c.p., it must be excluded that the mere age datum entails the existence of the condition of physical or mental inferiority of the injured party provided for by the second paragraph of art. 609 bis c.p., so that, once conduct involving violence, threat or abuse of authority is excluded, the second criminal offence, which is alternative and incompatible with the first, cannot also be applied. Section III, judgment no. 16843 of 3 May 2007

Cass. pen. n. 5003/2007

With regard to offences against sexual freedom, the assessment of the content of the statements of the injured minor must contain an examination both of the witness's psychophysical aptitude to set out the events accurately, that is, to receive information, connect it with other information and express it in a complex vision, and of their psychological position in relation to the context of internal and external situations that have regulated their relations with the outside world. Section III, judgment no. 5003 of 7 February 2007

Cass. pen. n. 29730/2006

The age of the minor, if far below the limit of ten years, in addition to being an essential element of the aggravating circumstance referred to in the last paragraph of art. 609 quater c.p., is legitimately assessed by the judge also as a concrete element capable of excluding the mitigating circumstance of lesser gravity of the case provided for by art. 609 bis, third paragraph, c.p. (Case in which the age of the minor was four and a half years). Section III, judgment no. 29730 of 6 September 2006

Cass. pen. n. 3124/2006

The position of guarantee towards one's own children, established by art. 147 c.c. for the parent, entails the obligation for the latter to protect the life, safety and sexual morality of minors against attacks by others: it follows that the mother who does not prevent, and indeed consents to, the spouse sexually abusing minor children is liable for the offence referred to in art. 609 quater c.p., in participation with the perpetrator of the offence, pursuant to the second paragraph of art. 40 c.p. Section III, judgment no. 3124 of 26 January 2006

Cass. pen. n. 45286/2005

With regard to sexual offences with a minor, the hypothesis of attempt is established when, even in the absence of acts of physical contact between the accused and the injured party, the conduct adopted is objectively suitable to violate the victim's freedom of self-determination in the sexual sphere and demonstrates the subjective requirement of the intention to achieve satisfaction of one's own sexual instincts. (In the present case, the S.C. found the suitability and unequivocal nature of the acts consisting in offering on several occasions to a child, a minor under ten years of age, sweets and money with the explicit request to perform sexual acts and in the attempt to drag him into the bathroom, even in the absence of lascivious touching). Section III, judgment no. 45286 of 14 December 2005

Cass. pen. n. 29662/2004

The offence referred to in art. 609 quater c.p. (sexual acts with a minor) protects the psychophysical integrity of the minor with a view to correct development of sexual personality through absolute inviolability in the case of a minor under fourteen years of age (first paragraph no. 1) or relative with reference to specific situations of kinship or trust of the minor (first paragraph no. 2) and is established even in the absence of coercive pressures, given that in such cases facilitation of consent or impediment to refusal of the same may occur. Section III, judgment no. 29662 of 8 July 2004

Cass. pen. n. 18058/2004

With regard to offences against sexual freedom, statements made by the minor to their own parents are not usable when direct examination of the minor has been omitted, with the sole possibility that the minor's psychological balance is so fragile as to translate into a true and manifest infirmity capable of allowing recovery of the indirect testimony of the parents. Section III, judgment no. 18058 of 20 April 2004

Cass. pen. n. 15287/2004

The case referred to in art. 609 quater c.p., which criminalises sexual acts with a minor, protects the correct development of the minor's sexual personality by establishing their absolute sexual inviolability (for minors under fourteen years of age) and relative inviolability (in particular situations, for minors under sixteen years of age in relation to the active subject in a relationship of kinship, care or supervision with the minor), is established in the absence of any coercive pressure and is characterised as an offence of free form, comprising all possible forms of attack on the minor, with the exclusion of the typical acts of coercion indicated in art. 609 bis c.p., which, having the minor as recipient, rather constitute the case of aggravated sexual violence pursuant to art. 609 ter, first paragraph, no. 1 c.p. Section III, judgment no. 15287 of 30 March 2004

Cass. pen. n. 36995/2001

With regard to offences against sexual freedom, the refusal to grant generic mitigating circumstances on the basis of elements such as the assessment of the demand for unnatural relations with a very young subject and the intensity of intent demonstrated by the repetition of the action over time is legitimate. Section III, judgment no. 36995 of 15 October 2001

Cass. pen. n. 28373/2001

With regard to sexual acts with a minor, the hypothesis of attempt must be established in cases where the perpetrator does not commit an actual and concrete intrusion into the victim's sexual sphere, but limits himself to acts which, although superficial, constitute an objective manifestation of sexuality. Such characteristics of the conduct, however, do not entail the automatic application of the reduction for "lesser gravity", provided for by the last paragraph of art. 609 quater c.p., since gravity must be assessed with reference to the harmful extent that would have occurred in the case of the offence being carried to completion. (Case in which the accused had derived satisfaction from viewing the nudity of the minor and had remunerated the same with an invitation to a subsequent appointment). Section III, judgment no. 28373 of 12 July 2001

Cass. pen. n. 26930/2001

The inducement of a minor under fourteen to commit acts of lewdness on the person of the agent, already falling within the provisions referred to in the repealed art. 530, second paragraph, c.p., is still to be considered punishable pursuant to art. 609 quater, paragraph 1, no. 1, of the same code, the aforementioned art. 530 c.p. remaining applicable, as more favourable from the point of view of sanctions, if the act was committed under the force of that provision. Section III, judgment no. 26930 of 4 July 2001

Cass. pen. n. 10936/2001

With regard to offences against sexual freedom, the hypothesis provided for by the last paragraph of art. 609 quater c.p., which punishes sexual acts committed with a person under ten years of age, does not constitute an autonomous criminal case, but rather an aggravating circumstance of the offence contemplated by the first paragraph of the same provision (sexual acts with a minor), given that the last paragraph of the aforementioned article refers, for the determination of the penalty in the case where the injured party has not reached ten years of age, to the second paragraph of the preceding article 609 ter c.p., whose heading, referring to aggravating circumstances, demonstrates the legislature's intention to consider certain factual elements as presuppositions for the aggravation of the penalty and not for the configuration of autonomous offences. Moreover, among the aggravating circumstances referred to in the aforementioned art. 609 ter, the case where the criminal conduct was committed against a person under ten years of age is indicated, thus achieving full symmetry between the hypotheses referred to in arts. 609 bis (sexual violence) and 609 quater c.p. cited. With regard to offences against sexual freedom, the offence referred to in art. 609 quater (sexual acts with a minor) may be subject to the reduction for an act of lesser gravity, referred to in the third paragraph, or the aggravating circumstance of sexual acts on a minor under ten years of age, referred to in the fourth paragraph, so that, where both circumstances occur, the trial judge is called upon to make a judgment of equivalence or prevalence. Section III, judgment no. 10936 of 20 March 2001

Cass. pen. n. 2083/2000

The reduction provided for by art. 609 quater, paragraph 3, c.p. establishes a mitigating circumstance with special effect, whose recognition in concrete terms results in a different legal classification of the act. Consequently, the appeal by the Public Prosecutor against the decision issued pursuant to art. 444 c.p.p., which frames the facts with application of the aforementioned provision, must be considered admissible, it being possible to denounce by appeal to the Court of Cassation the erroneous legal classification of the act as proposed by the parties and accepted by the judge with the plea bargaining judgment. (In the present case, the Court held that the classification of the act of sexual acts with a minor as falling within the case of lesser gravity provided for by the aforementioned third paragraph, made on the basis of mere consideration of the manner of the act and not noting that in the case de quo it concerned sexual acts with a minor under ten years of age, could not be considered correct, given the provision of greater gravity pursuant to paragraph 4 of the same art. 609 quater c.p. for acts committed with a minor under ten years of age). Section III, judgment no. 2083 of 2 October 2000

Cass. pen. n. 9528/2000

With regard to sexual violence, the mitigating circumstance provided for by art. 609 quater c.p. for cases of lesser gravity must be considered applicable, like the similar one provided for by art. 609 bis, paragraph 3 of the same code, in all those cases where, having regard to the means, manner of execution and circumstances of the action, it is possible to consider that the victim's personal sexual freedom has been compressed in a non-serious manner, and implies the need for an overall assessment of the act, not limited solely to the objective components of the offence, but extended also to the subjective ones and to all the elements mentioned in art. 133 c.p. Section III, judgment no. 9528 of 7 September 2000

Cass. pen. n. 2841/1998

With the entry into force of Law 66 of 1996, the so-called presumed carnal violence, referred to in art. 519, second paragraph, c.p. is no longer established. To fill this regulatory gap, the disvalue that the legal system attributes to certain behaviours remaining unchanged, the new autonomous offence of sexual acts with a minor (art. 609 quater c.p.) was introduced, which punishes the commission, even non-violent, of sexual acts with minors under fourteen, and with the occurrence of specific conditions in the case where the minor has not yet reached sixteen years of age. Therefore, in the case of sexual acts with a person over fourteen, it is necessary to verify the existence of the specific conditions provided for by art. 609 quater. Section III, judgment no. 2841 of 5 March 1998

Cass. pen. n. 4004/1998

Attempted sexual violence referred to in the first paragraph of art. 609 quater c.p. (sexual acts with minors) is established in the case where the agent on multiple and closely-spaced occasions adopts violent attitudes (in the present case grabbing by the neck) towards a minor, at the same time inviting him each time to follow him, so that he may engage in sexual practices in his presence. The repetitiveness of the episodes and the pressing manner of the request are in the abstract revealing indices of the suitability of the action. It is, however, necessary, in order to also verify the element of unequivocal nature, to place the conduct in the concrete manner of space and time, to verify its direction towards the actual perpetration of the unlawful act and the coincidence of the concrete case with the legal one. Section III, judgment no. 4004 of 15 January 1998

Cass. pen. n. 11193/1997

With regard to offences against sexual freedom, Law no. 66 of 15 February 1996 has not decriminalised conduct carried out during the force of the previous legislation when it coincides with that provided for by the ius superveniens, so that the act previously framed in the provision of arts. 56 and 521 c.p. is referable to the notion of sexual acts referred to in art. 609 quater c.p. The unlawfulness of the conduct, the offence of sexual violence being among those against personal freedom and no longer among those against public morality, must be assessed in the light of the respect due to the human person and its aptitude to offend freedom of determination in the sexual sphere. (In the present case, the Court held that the display of the member to a girl under fourteen years of age, with an invitation to touch it, did not constitute the offence of obscene acts, but rather that of violent acts of lewdness, in the attempt phase, with exclusion of the mitigating circumstance of lesser gravity referred to in art. 3 of Law no. 66 of 15 February 1996). Section III, judgment no. 11193 of 4 December 1997
