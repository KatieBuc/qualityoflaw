##### SOLE CHAPTER

RULES FOR ITS APPLICATION

###### Art. 1º.- (Regarding space).

This Code shall apply:

1)

To crimes committed in the territory of Bolivia or in places subject to its jurisdiction.

2)

To crimes committed abroad, whose results have occurred or were to occur in the territory of Bolivia or in places subject to its jurisdiction.

3)

To crimes committed abroad by a Bolivian, provided that he is in national territory and has not been punished in the place where the offense was committed.

4)

To crimes committed abroad against the security of the State, public faith and the national economy. This provision shall extend to foreigners, if they are obtained by extradition or are found within the territory of the Republic.

5)

To crimes committed on ships, aircraft or other Bolivian means of transport in a foreign country, when they are not tried therein.

6)

To crimes committed abroad by officials in the service of the Nation, in the performance of their office or commission.

7)

To crimes that by treaty or convention the Republic has obligated itself to suppress, even when they were not committed in its territory.

###### Art. 2º.- (Foreign sentence).

In the cases provided in the preceding article, when the agent is tried in Bolivia, having already been sentenced abroad, the portion of the sentence served there shall be computed if it is of the same type and if it is of a different type, the judge shall in any case reduce the one imposed on the perpetrator.

###### Art. 3º.- (Extradition).

No person subject to the jurisdiction of Bolivian laws may be surrendered by extradition to another State, unless an international treaty or reciprocity agreement provides otherwise.

The propriety or impropriety of extradition shall be resolved by the Supreme Court. In case of reciprocity, extradition may not be carried out if the act for which it is requested does not constitute a crime according to the law of the State requesting extradition and of the one that must grant it.

###### Art. 4º.- (REGARDING TIME).-

No one may be convicted or subjected to a security measure for an act that is not expressly provided as a crime by penal law in force at the time it was committed, nor subjected to penalties or penal security measures that are not established therein.

If the law in force at the time the crime was committed is different from the one existing when the judgment is issued or from the one in force in the intervening time, the most favorable one shall always apply.

If during the service of the sentence a more lenient law is enacted, it shall be the one that applies.

Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraphs, laws enacted to govern only for a determined time shall apply to all acts committed during their validity.

### CRIME

###### Art. 8º.- (Attempt).

Whoever by suitable or unequivocal acts begins the execution of the crime and does not consummate it due to causes beyond his will, shall be punished with two-thirds of the penalty established for the consummated crime.

###### Art. 9º.- (Desistance and effective repentance).

Shall not be punished with any penalty.

1)

Whoever voluntarily desists from the commission of the crime.

2)

Whoever in the same manner prevents or contributes to preventing the result from occurring, unless the acts performed constitute crimes in themselves.

###### Art. 10º.- (Impossible crime).

If the result does not occur because the means employed are not suitable or due to impropriety of the object, the judge may only impose security measures.

###### Art. 12º.- (STATE OF NECESSITY).-

He who, in order to avoid harm to his own or another's legal interest, which cannot be overcome in any other way, commits a criminal offense, is exempt from liability when the following requirements are met:

1)

That the harm caused is not greater than that which is to be avoided, taking into account, principally, the equivalence in the quality of the legal interests involved;

2)

That the harm to be avoided is imminent or current, and significant;

3)

That the situation of necessity has not been intentionally provoked by the subject; and, 4)

That the person in need does not have, by reason of his office or position, the obligation to face the danger.

###### Art. 16º.- (ERROR).-

1) (ERROR OF FACT).- Invincible error regarding a constitutive element of the criminal offense excludes criminal liability for this offense. If the error, considering the circumstances of the act and the personal circumstances of the perpetrator, were vincible, the infraction shall be sanctioned with the penalty for the negligent offense, when the law threatens it with penalty.

Error regarding a fact that qualifies the infraction or regarding an aggravating circumstance shall prevent the application of the aggravated penalty.

An offense committed by vincible error regarding the circumstances that would have justified or excused the act shall be sanctioned as a negligent offense when the law threatens it with penalty.

2) (ERROR OF PROHIBITION).- Invincible error regarding the unlawfulness of the act constituting the criminal offense excludes criminal liability. If the error were vincible, the penalty may be mitigated pursuant to article 39.

###### Art. 28º.- (PRESTACIÓN DE TRABAJO).-

The penalty of work provision for the benefit of the community obliges the convicted person to provide their work in activities of public utility that do not violate their dignity and are in accordance with their capacity.

The work provision shall not interfere with the normal work activity of the convicted person, it shall be performed in public establishments and in associations of general interest at the times determined by the judge. It shall have a maximum duration of forty-eight weeks, and weekly it may not exceed sixteen hours, nor be less than three hours.

The work provision may only be executed with the consent of the convicted person. In the event that the convicted person does not give their consent, the sanction shall be converted into a penalty involving deprivation of liberty. For this purpose, one day of deprivation of liberty is equivalent to two weekly hours of work. This substitution shall be made only once, and once made it may not cease to be executed.

The supervisory judge must require reports on the performance of the work from the employing entity. In the event that the reports are not favorable, it shall be converted into deprivation of liberty in accordance with the preceding paragraph.

###### Art. 29º.- (DÍAS MULTA).-

The fine consists of payment to the Reparations Fund of an amount of money that shall be fixed by the judge in day-fines, based on the economic capacity of the convicted person, their daily income, their aptitude for work and their family responsibilities, considered at the time of issuing the sentence. The minimum shall be one day-fine and the maximum five hundred.

If the convicted person does not provide sufficient information about their income, assets or other bases for calculating a daily installment, then it may be assessed estimatively.

The resolution shall indicate the number of day-fines, the amount of the daily installment and the payment term.

###### Art. 30º.- (CONVERSION).-

When the penalty of fine days and custodial sentence are imposed jointly, conversion of fine days into deprivation of liberty shall not proceed. In other cases, conversion shall proceed when a solvent convicted person does not pay the fine.

Before conversion, the judge may authorize the convicted person to amortize the pecuniary penalty through provision of work. The judge may also authorize payment of the fine in installments, setting the amount and date of payments according to their economic condition, or seek to satisfy the fine by making it effective upon the assets, wages, or other income of the convicted person.

Payment of the fine at any time renders the conversion without effect, deducting the time of imprisonment that the convicted person may have served, in the established proportion.

For purposes of conversion and amortization, one day of imprisonment is equivalent to three fine days and one day of work of four hours is equivalent to one fine day.

###### Art. 36º.- (APPLICATION OF SPECIAL DISQUALIFICATION).-

Special disqualification of six months to ten years shall be imposed, after completion of the principal penalty, when the committed offense involves violation or disregard of the rights and duties corresponding to the mandate, office, employment or commission, incompetence or abuse of the professions or activities referred to in article 34 and it concerns offenses committed:

- 1) By public officials, mandataries, commissioners, in the exercise of their functions;
- 2) By physicians, lawyers, engineers, financial auditors and other professionals in the exercise of their professions; or,
- 3) By those who perform industrial, commercial or other activities.

In the foregoing cases, special disqualification is inherent to the time of completion of the custodial sentence.

The minimum special disqualification penalty shall be five years, in the following cases:

- 1) If the death of one or more persons occurs as a consequence of a serious culpable violation of the duty of care.
- 2) If the offense was committed by a public official in the exercise of their functions.

##### CHAPTER II

APPLICATION OF PENALTIES

###### Art. 37º.- (Determination of penalty).

It is incumbent upon the judge, considering the personality of the perpetrator, the greater or lesser gravity of the act, the circumstances and the consequences of the offense:

1)

To take direct knowledge of the subject, the victim and the circumstances of the act, to the extent required for each case.

2)

To determine the penalty applicable to each offense, within the legal limits.

###### Art. 38º. (Circumstances).

1)

To assess the personality of the perpetrator, the following shall be taken primarily into account:

- a) The age, education, customs and prior and subsequent conduct of the subject, the motives that drove them to commit the offense and their economic and social situation.
- b) The special conditions in which they were at the time of commission of the offense and other background and personal conditions, as well as their ties of kinship, friendship or born of other relationships, the status of the offended persons and other circumstances of a subjective nature.

Premeditation, base antisocial motive, treachery and cruelty shall also be taken into account.

2)

To assess the gravity of the act, the following shall be taken into account: nature of the action, means employed, the extent of the damage caused and the danger incurred.

###### Art. 39º.- (SPECIAL MITIGATING CIRCUMSTANCES).-

In cases where this code expressly provides for special mitigation, the procedure shall be as follows:

- 1) The penalty of thirty years' imprisonment shall be reduced to fifteen.
- 2) When the offense is punishable by a penalty of imprisonment with a minimum exceeding one year, the imposed penalty may be mitigated to the legal minimum of the imprisonment penalty scale.
- 3) When the offense is punishable by a penalty of imprisonment whose minimum is one year or a penalty of confinement with a minimum exceeding one month, the imposed penalty may be mitigated to the legal minimum of the confinement penalty scale.

The penalty may also be mitigated:

1)

When the perpetrator has acted for an honorable motive, or driven by poverty, or under the influence of serious and unjust moral suffering, or under the impression of a serious threat, or by the ascendancy of a person to whom they owe obedience or on whom they depend.

2)

When they have distinguished themselves in previous life by particularly meritorious behavior. 3)

When they have demonstrated their repentance through acts and especially by repairing the damages, to the extent that it has been possible.

###### Art. 43º.- (SANCTIONS FOR THE FOREGOING CASES).-

For the recidivist, in addition to the penalties that correspond for the crimes committed, the judge shall impose the most appropriate security measures

###### Art. 44º.- (Ideal Concurrence).

Whoever with a single action or omission violates various legal provisions that are not mutually exclusive shall be sanctioned with the penalty of the most serious crime, with the judge being able to increase the maximum by up to one fourth.

###### Art. 45º.- (Real Concurrence).

Whoever with independent purposes, with one or more actions or omissions, commits two or more crimes shall be sanctioned with the penalty of the most serious one, with the judge being able to increase the maximum by up to one half.

###### Art. 46º.- (Single Sentence).

In all cases of plurality of crimes, it is the responsibility of the judge who hears the most serious case to issue the single sentence, determining the definitive penalty for all of them, subject to the rules of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

###### Art. 70º.- ("Nulla poena sine juditio").

No one shall be sentenced to any penalty without having been heard and tried in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure.

No penalty may be executed except by virtue of a judgment issued by competent judicial authority and in compliance with a law, nor executed in a manner different from that established therein.

###### Art. 71º.- (Forfeiture).

The commission of a crime entails the loss of the instruments with which it was executed and of the effects derived from it, which shall be forfeited, unless they belonged to a non-responsible third party, who may recover them.

Forfeited instruments may be sold at public auction if they are of lawful commerce, to cover civil liability in cases of insolvency; if they are not, they shall be destroyed or rendered useless.

#### TITLE V

CIVIL LIABILITY AND

REPARATIONS FUND

##### CHAPTER I

CIVIL LIABILITY

###### Art. 87.- (Civil liability).

Every person criminally liable is also civilly liable and is obligated to repair the material and moral damages caused by the crime.

###### Art. 88.- (Preference).

Civil liability shall take precedence over payment of the fine and any other obligation that the responsible person may have incurred after the commission of the crime.

###### Art. 89.- (Exemption from Liability).

Only those who are protected by a justification cause are exempt from civil liability, except the person who caused the state of necessity.

In cases where the person responsible is not determined, the persons in whose favor the harm was prevented shall be obligated to civil liability, in proportion to the benefit obtained by each of them, and subsidiarily, the State.

###### Art. 90.- (Legal Mortgage, Seizure and Retention).

From the moment a crime is committed, the immovable property of those responsible shall be considered specially mortgaged for civil liability.

The judge may also order the seizure of movable property, and retention as the case may be.

###### Art. 91.- (Scope).

Civil liability comprises:

1)

The restitution of the property of the injured party, which shall be delivered to them even if held by a third party possessor.

2)

The reparation of the damage caused.

3)

The compensation for all harm caused to the victim, their family or a third party, the amount to be set prudentially by the judge, in the absence of full proof. Every compensation shall always include the expenses incurred by the victim for their cure, recovery and reeducation.

###### Art. 92.- (Joint Liability and Transmissibility of Obligations).

Civil liability shall be joint among all those responsible for the crime.

This obligation passes to the heirs of the responsible party and the right to demand it is transmitted to the heirs of the victim.

###### Art. 93.- (Participation in the Proceeds of the Crime).

Whoever participates for profit in the proceeds of a crime shall be obligated to compensation, up to the amount by which they have benefited.

If the responsible party or participants have acted as agents of someone or as representatives or members of a legal entity and the product or profit of the crime benefits the principal or represented party, they shall be equally obligated to compensation in the same proportion as above.

###### Art. 111.- (Espionage).

Whoever procures secret documents, objects or information of a political or military nature relating to security, means of defense or foreign relations, for purposes of espionage in favor of other countries in time of peace, that endanger the security of the State, shall incur the penalty of thirty years of imprisonment without right to pardon.

Whoever in time of war enters clandestinely, by deception or violence, into a military place or zone or is discovered in such places or in their vicinity in unjustified possession of means of espionage, shall incur deprivation of liberty from five to ten years.

###### Art. 113.- (Crimes committed by foreigners).

Foreigners residing in Bolivian territory are included in the preceding articles and shall be imposed the sanctions specified therein, except as established by treaties or by the law of nations concerning diplomatic officials.

###### Art. 114.- (Hostile acts).

Whoever without the knowledge or influence of the Government commits hostilities against a foreign power and thereby exposes the State to the serious danger of a declaration of war or to vexations or reprisals against its nationals abroad or to the rupture of diplomatic relations, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty from two to four years.

If war results from such hostilities, the penalty shall be ten years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 115.- (Revelation of secrets).

Whoever reveals secrets of a political or military nature concerning the security of the State, the means of defense or foreign relations, shall incur deprivation of liberty from one to six years.

The sanction shall be increased by one third, if the agent perpetrates this crime by abusing the function, employment or commission conferred by public authority.

###### Art. 116.- (Crime by fault).

If the revelation of the secrets mentioned in the preceding article is committed by fault of the person who is in possession by virtue of his employment or occupation, the sanction shall be imprisonment from six months to two years.

###### Art. 117.- (Infidelity in State business).

The representative or commissioner of the Government of Bolivia to negotiate a treaty, agreement or convention with another State, who departs from his instructions in a manner that may cause damage to the national interest, shall incur imprisonment from two to six years.

Whoever in time of war destroys or renders useless installations, roads, works or other means of defense, communication, transport, supply, etc., with the purpose of harming the capacity or war effort of the Nation, shall be sanctioned with thirty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 119.- (Non-compliance with contracts of military interest).

Whoever in time of war does not duly fulfill contractual obligations relating to the needs of the armed forces or national defense, shall incur imprisonment from two to six years.

###### Art. 120.- (Crimes against an allied State).

The provisions established in the preceding articles shall also apply when the acts provided for therein are committed against a power allied with Bolivia, at war against a common enemy.

###### Art. 121.- (Armed uprisings against the security and sovereignty of the State).

Those who rise up in arms with the purpose of changing the Political Constitution or the form of government established therein, deposing any of the public powers of the national government or preventing, even temporarily, the free exercise of their constitutional powers or their renewal in their legal terms, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty from five to fifteen years.

Those who organize or integrate irregular armed groups, urban or rural, under internal or external influence, to promote armed confrontations with regular or public security forces, or to commit attacks against the life and security of persons, the territorial integrity or the sovereignty of the State, shall be sanctioned with the penalty of fifteen to thirty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 122.- (Granting of extraordinary powers).

Members of Congress or those who in a popular assembly grant the Executive Power extraordinary powers, the sum of Public Power or supremacies by which the life, property and honor of Bolivians are at the mercy of the Government or any person, shall incur deprivation of liberty from two to six years.

###### Art. 123.- (Sedition).

Those who without disavowing the authority of the legally constituted Government, rise up publicly and in open hostility, to depose a public official or employee, prevent his taking office or oppose the enforcement of laws, decrees or judicial or administrative resolutions, exercise any act of hatred or revenge against the person or property of any authority or individuals or disturb or disrupt public order in any other way, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from one to three years.

Public officials who have not resisted a rebellion or sedition by all means at their disposal shall incur imprisonment from one to two years.

###### Art. 124.- (Attributing to oneself the rights of the people).

With the same penalty shall be sanctioned those who form part of an armed force or a gathering of persons that attribute to themselves the rights of the people and attempt to exercise such rights in their name.

###### Art. 125.- Common provisions for the crimes of rebellion and sedition).

In the event that rebels or seditionists submit to the first requirement of the public authority, without having caused any damage other than the momentary disturbance of order, only the promoters or leaders shall be sanctioned, to whom half of the penalty established for the crime shall be applied.

###### Art. 126.- (Conspiracy).

Whoever takes part in a conspiracy of three or more persons, to commit the crimes of rebellion or sedition, shall be sanctioned with the penalty for the crime that was attempted to be perpetrated, reduced by one half.

Participants who voluntarily desist before the execution of the proposed act and those who spontaneously prevent the commission of the crime shall be exempt from penalty.

###### Art. 127.- (Seduction of troops).

Whoever seduces troops or usurps command of them or illegally retains a political or military command, to commit a rebellion or sedition, shall be sanctioned with half of the penalty corresponding to the crime that was attempted to be perpetrated.

###### Art. 128.- (Attacks against the President and other State dignitaries).-

Whoever attacks the life or safety of the President of the Republic, Vice President, Ministers of State and President of the National Congress, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of five to ten years of deprivation of liberty.

If death results as a consequence of the attack committed, the maximum penalty that corresponds shall be applied; if serious injuries result to the victim, the penalty applicable to the act shall be increased by one third.

###### Art. 129.- (Outrage to national symbols).

Whoever publicly outrages the flag, the coat of arms or the anthem of the Nation, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to two years.

###### Art. 190.- (Counterfeiting of seals, stamped paper and stamps).

Whoever counterfeits official seals, stamped paper, official lottery tickets, postage stamps, any stamped effect or printed forms whose issuance is reserved to the authority, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty from one to six years.

The same sanction shall be imposed on whoever knowingly introduces, distributes or uses them.

###### Art. 191.- (Fraudulent printing of official seal).

Whoever fraudulently prints an authentic official seal shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one to three years.

###### Art. 192.- (Receipt in good faith).

Whoever, having received in good faith the valuables and effects indicated in Art. 190, and knowing afterwards of their falsity, introduces or puts them into circulation, shall be sanctioned with a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 193.- (Counterfeiting and improper application of marks and countersigns).

Whoever counterfeits marks, countersigns or signatures officially used to verify weights or measures, identify any object or certify its quality, quantity or content, and whoever applies them to objects other than those to which they should be applied, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to three years.

The same sanction shall be incurred by whoever performs the same acts affecting private factories or establishments.

###### Art. 194.- (Counterfeiting of tickets of public transport companies).

Whoever counterfeits or alters tickets of public or private transport companies shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one to six months or a fine of twenty to one hundred twenty days.

Whoever introduces, distributes or puts them into circulation shall incur the same sanction.

###### Art. 195.- (Counterfeiting of admission tickets).

Whoever counterfeits or alters, for profit, admission tickets or tickets that permit access to a public spectacle, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one to six months or a fine of twenty to one hundred twenty days.

###### Art. 196.- (Use of already used items).

Whoever, for the purpose of using or selling seals, stamps, marks, countersigns or other stamped effects, causes the sign indicating their use to disappear, shall be sanctioned with a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

###### Art. 197.- (Tools for counterfeiting).

Whoever manufactures, introduces into the country, keeps in their possession or negotiates materials or instruments unequivocally intended to commit any of the counterfeitings legislated in the two previous chapters, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to two years.

###### Art. 198.- (Material falsity).

Whoever inserts or causes to be inserted in a true public instrument false statements concerning a fact that the document must prove, in such a way that damage may result, shall be sanctioned with deprivation of liberty from one to six years.

###### Art. 248.- (Abandonment of family).

He who, without just cause, fails to fulfill the obligations of sustenance, housing, clothing, education and assistance inherent to parental authority, guardianship or the status of spouse or cohabitant, or abandons the family domicile or evades the fulfillment of the indicated obligations, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from six months to two years or a fine from one hundred to four hundred days.

2)

If he permits the minor to frequent gambling houses or houses of ill repute or to cohabit with a vicious person or person of ill repute.

3)

If he permits the minor to frequent shows capable of perverting him or that offend decency, or that the minor participates in performances of the same nature.

4)

If he authorizes him to reside or work in a house of prostitution.

5)

If he permits the minor to beg or serve a beggar to inspire compassion.

DEFAMATION, SLANDER AND INSULT

###### Art. 282.- (Defamation).

Whoever in a public, tendentious and repeated manner, reveals or discloses a fact, a quality or conduct capable of affecting the reputation of an individual or collective person, shall incur work service from one month to one year or a fine of twenty to two hundred forty days.

###### Art. 283.- (Slander).

Whoever by any means falsely imputes to another the commission of a crime, shall be punished with deprivation of liberty from six months to two years and a fine of one hundred to three hundred days.

###### Art. 284.- (Offense to the memory of the deceased).

Whoever offends the memory of a deceased person with defamatory expressions or slanderous imputations, shall incur the same penalties as the two preceding articles.

###### Art. 285.- (Dissemination of offenses).

Whoever disseminates or reproduces by any means the acts referred to in articles 282, 283 and 284, shall be punished as the author thereof.

###### Art. 286.- (Exception of truth).

The author of defamation and slander shall not be punishable if the imputations consist of true statements, but the accused may only prove the truth of the imputation:

1)

When it concerns offenses directed at a public official and with reference to their functions.

2)

When the complainant requests proof of the imputation, provided that such proof does not affect the rights or secrets of a third person.

###### Art. 287.- (Insult).

Whoever by any means and in a direct manner offends another in their dignity or decorum, shall incur work service from one month to one year and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

If the act provided for in Art. 283 and the insult referred to in this article are committed by means of print, typewriting or manuscript, its author shall be considered guilty of defamatory libel and punished with a fine of sixty to one hundred fifty days, without prejudice to the corresponding penalties.

###### Art. 288.- (Prohibition of proof).

Proof shall not be admitted except in the cases indicated in article 286.

###### Art. 289.- (Retraction).

The person accused of a crime against honor shall be exempt from penalty, if they retract before or at the time of providing their statement.

A second retraction on the same act shall not be admitted.

###### Art. 290.- (Reciprocal offenses).

If the offenses or imputations are reciprocal, the judge may, according to the circumstances, exempt both parties or one of them from penalty.

##### CAPITULO IX

USURY

###### Art. 360.- (Usury).

Whoever, taking advantage of the need, thoughtlessness or inexperience of a person, gives in any form, for himself or for others, values or species in exchange for interest higher than that fixed by law or other pecuniary advantages evidently disproportionate to the performance, shall be sanctioned with confinement from three months to two years and a fine of thirty to one hundred days.

The same penalty shall apply to whoever knowingly acquires, transfers or enforces a usurious credit, or to the intermediary, front man or cooperator.

###### Art. 361.- (Aggravated usury).

The sanction shall be aggravated by one half and fine of up to one hundred days:

1)

If the perpetrator were a professional or habitual usurious lender or commission agent.

2)

When any artifice or deceit has been employed to obtain the victim's consent. 3)

If the act were concealed by means of other forms of contract, even as a penalty clause that fixes interest. 4)

If the act constitutes any of the forms of compound interest.

###### Article 363

quater. (FINANCIAL CRIMES).

A natural or legal person through their legal representative commits a financial crime when, by action or omission, they engage in any of the criminal acts detailed below: : a)

Financial Intermediation without Authorization or License.

Whoever, on their own or another's behalf, directly or indirectly, conducts financial intermediation activities without prior authorization from the Financial System Supervision Authority, shall incur deprivation of liberty from five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days. b)

Improper Use of Influence for Granting Credit.

The director or directors, administration and oversight board member, executive or official of a financial intermediation entity who, with the intention of favoring themselves or the entity in any way or obtaining economic benefits for themselves or a third party, knowingly authorizes or approves the granting of credit to borrowers or borrower groups related to the entity, shall incur deprivation of liberty from three (3) to eight (8) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to three hundred (300) days.

If this activity causes harm to third parties or to the entity itself, the penalty shall be increased by half. c)

Misappropriation of Financial Funds.

Whoever, without authorization and through the use of technological means or other fraudulent maneuvers, seizes or procures the transfer of funds, whether for their own benefit or that of third parties, shall incur deprivation of liberty from five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days.

When the offense is committed by an employee of the financial entity taking advantage of their position or another's error, the penalty shall be increased by half. d)

Forging of Illicit Financial Results.

Whoever, for the purpose of procuring undue benefit, performs fraudulent maneuvers to alter the price of negotiable securities or public offerings by concealing or hiding true facts or circumstances or by affirming or suggesting false or misleading facts or circumstances, shall incur deprivation of liberty from five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days.

The penalty shall be increased by half for whoever, with the intent to obtain a benefit for themselves or a third party or to harm another market participant, causes the price to rise, fall or remain stable, or facilitates the sale or purchase of securities, by affirming or simulating false facts or circumstances or by distorting or concealing true facts or circumstances, in a manner that induces error regarding the essential characteristics of the investment. e)

Falsification of Accounting Documentation.

Whoever knowingly or with the purpose of concealing situations of illiquidity or insolvency of a financial entity or complementary financial services company, materially or ideologically falsifies the entity's financial statements, accounting entries or other financial information, shall incur deprivation of liberty from five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days. f)

Dissemination of False Financial Information.

An individual who by any means disseminates or instructs the dissemination of false information about the Bolivian financial system or its entities, which induces or provokes the massive withdrawal of deposits from one or more financial intermediation entities, incites or induces clients not to comply with acquired financial commitments, damaging or deteriorating the image and stability of a financial intermediation entity or the national financial system. Excluded from the scope of this subsection are studies, analyses and opinions of a scientific nature that, based on authentic and verifiable information, are oriented to evaluate or qualify the financial system or its actors, seeking to maximize its efficiency and development, shall incur deprivation of liberty from five (5) to ten (10) years and a fine of one hundred (100) to five hundred (500) days.
