ON MEASURES AGAINST VIOLENCE IN FAMILY RELATIONS

DECIDED:

## CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article1

Purpose (amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, amended point 2 by law no. 125/2020, dated

This law has as its purpose:

- 1. The prevention and reduction of domestic violence in all its forms, through appropriate legal measures and other necessary measures.
- 2. Guaranteeing protection with legal measures and other necessary measures for family members who are victims of domestic violence, paying special attention to women, girls, children, the elderly and persons with disabilities, subject of this law, according to its article 3.

### Article2

Object

(amended wording in letter "c" by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)

This law has as its object:

- a) the creation of a coordinated network of institutions responsible for the protection, support and rehabilitation of victims, mitigation of consequences and prevention of domestic violence;
- b) the orientation of work for the creation of structures and responsible bodies at central and local level for the support of victims and prevention of domestic violence;
- c) the strengthening of the judicial system for taking measures of protection against domestic violence; ç) ensuring/guaranteeing for victims of domestic violence quick, free of charge and simple service in accordance with the law, before the court or other competent bodies for law enforcement.

### Article3

Definitions (amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, amended point 3 by law no. 125/2020, dated In this law, except when expressly provided otherwise in its special provisions, the following terms have these meanings:

- 1. "Violence" is any action or inaction of a person toward another person, which results in harm to physical, moral, psychological, sexual, social, economic integrity.

2. "Domestic violence" is any act of violence, as defined in point 1 of this article, that occurs within the family or household unit, exercised between family members, as defined in point 7 of this article, who are or have been in a family relationship, regardless of the fact whether the abuser shares or has shared the same residence with the victim.

3. "Abuser" is any person suspected and/or sued for the exercise of violence in family relations before competent bodies. 4. "Child" is any person under the age of 18. The term "minor", used in this law, has the same meaning.

5. "Coordinated Referral Mechanism for cases of domestic violence" (hereinafter MKR) is the organized network of responsible institutions at local level for the prevention, protection, support and rehabilitation of victims of domestic violence.

6. "Household unit" is the dwelling or any environment used and adapted as such, regardless of whether it is owned or used by the family.

7. "Family members" are:

- a) the spouse or cohabitant or ex-spouse or ex-cohabiting partner;
- b) brothers, sisters, including, as the case may be, also their spouse or cohabitant, as well as nephews and nieces, if the latter are members of the household unit;
- c) ancestors and descendants in direct line, without limitations, including also cases when this relationship is created through adoption; ç) the parents of the spouse or cohabitant, including also the stepfather/stepmother, even when this relationship originates from adoption; d) relatives in direct line, including also parents, and adopted children of the spouse or cohabitant; dh) the brothers and sisters of the spouse or cohabitant; e) the children of spouses or cohabiting partners; ë) persons who are or have been in intimate relationships, not necessarily accompanied by cohabitation, enjoy the same protection; f) the guardian and the person who is in his/her care during the time that the guardianship lasts, according to the definitions of guardianship over minors and guardianship for persons with disabilities and persons who have been deprived or restricted of capacity to act according to the provisions of the Family Code.".
  - 8. "Intimate relationship" is an interpersonal relationship that includes physical or emotional intimacy.
  - 9. "Victim" is the person upon whom the violence defined in point 1 of this article has been

### exercised. 10. "Protection order" is the order issued by court decision, which provides protective measures

for the victim, children and as the case may be, measures for the rehabilitation of the abuser.

11. "Immediate protection order" is the order issued temporarily by court decision, valid until the issuance of the protection order by court decision. 12. "Order for preliminary measures of immediate protection" is the reasoned order issued according to the provisions of article 13/1 of this law, which contains immediate measures for stopping violence, which are taken by the responsible bodies for handling cases of domestic violence at the State Police, until the decision is taken by the court.

### Article3/1

Applicable legislation

(added by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)

1. This law is based on the principles enshrined in the Constitution of the Republic of Albania, in the United Nations Convention "On the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women", the Council of Europe Convention "On Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence" and in other international acts ratified by the Republic of Albania.

2. The implementation of the provisions of this law, especially measures for the protection of victims' rights, are ensured without discrimination, for any reason provided by the legislation in force for protection against discrimination.

3. The special protective measures, provided in article 10 and article 13/1 of this law, which are necessary for the prevention and protection from violence in family relations, are not considered discrimination if they are applied according to the rules and criteria provided in this law.

### Article4

Subjects

(amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)

1. The subjects protected by this law are all subjects defined in article 3, point 7, of this law, who are Albanian citizens with residence or stay in the territory of the Republic of Albania.

2. Subjects protected by this law are also foreign citizens or stateless persons who have requested international protection, according to the legislation in force for asylum in the Republic of Albania, and foreigners with regular stay for different motives, according to the legislation in force for foreigners, who are included in the category of subjects defined in article 3, point 7, of this law.

## CHAPTER II

RESPONSIBLE BODIES

### Article5

Responsible authorities

(added letter "d" by law no. 9914, dated 12.5.2008, amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)

1. The main authority responsible for the implementation of this law is the ministry responsible for issues of gender equality and fight against domestic violence.

2. Other responsible line authorities are: a) the ministry responsible for social issues; b) the ministry responsible for issues of public order and security; c) the ministry responsible for health issues; ç) the ministry responsible for justice issues; d) the ministry responsible for education issues; dh) the ministry responsible for housing issues; e) the ministry responsible for issues of employment and vocational training; ë) local government units.

3. The list of other responsible line authorities, provided in point 2 of this article, changes if one of them takes the role of the main responsible authority. In this case, this authority has also the main responsible role and covers issues in the relevant field.

### Article 6

Duties of the main responsible authority (added letters "f" and "g" by law no.9914, dated 12.5.2008, added letter "gj" by law no.10 329, dated 30.9.2010, amended letters "c", "ç", "d", "g", "gj" and added letters "h" and "i" by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)

The main responsible authority has the following duties:

- a) drafting and implementation of strategies and national programs in the field of protection and care for victims of domestic violence;
- b) financing or co-financing of projects which aim to protect and consolidate the family, as well as care for victims of domestic violence;
- c) support in establishing support structures, in cooperation with the referral mechanism, for coordination, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of policies and measures to prevent and combat all forms of violence, as well as equipping them with all necessary infrastructure, in accordance also with the principle of "reasonable accommodation", which serves to support and fulfill all needs for victims of domestic violence, including financial assistance, as well as medical and social services, according to legislation in force;
- ç) organizing trainings for prevention and treatment of domestic violence issues for social service workers who provide services at every local self-government unit, State Police structures, medical staff and for employees of licensed NGOs to provide social services. Trainings may be organized in cooperation with the Albanian School of Public Administration, with the School of Magistrates, with the Order of Psychologists and Order of Social Workers or with organizations and agencies accredited to provide trainings on issues related to domestic violence, for all responsible administrative, law enforcement and judicial authorities;
- d) coordination of work on maintaining and collecting statistical data on cases of domestic violence, including analysis, publication and annual reporting to the responsible parliamentary committee/subcommittee for gender equality issues and prevention of violence against women.

The retention and processing of data on cases of domestic violence is done in accordance with legislation in force for the protection of personal data. Their publication is done according to the rules provided in the legislation in force for official statistics.
- dh) support and supervision of the establishment of rehabilitation centers for victims of domestic violence;

- e) support and supervision of the establishment of rehabilitation centers for perpetrators of domestic violence;
- ë) licensing of various NGOs that will provide social services for victims and perpetrators;

- f) taking measures for education of the younger generation with mentalities and rules of good behavior, as well as prohibition of violence in family relations;

- g) drafts policies and oversees the taking of measures for elimination of consequences of child abuse by parents or legal guardians or, in the case when children live in families where parents or guardians abuse each other, through placement in alternative care or taking emergency protective measures, according to the provisions of legislation in force for the rights and protection of the child;
- gj) provision and supervision of the service at the National Center for Social Care for victims of domestic violence.

- h) drafting, in cooperation also with other line authorities, of procedures, standards or protocols, related to risk assessment and intervention plans in cases of domestic violence, as well as monitoring their implementation;

- i) coordination and monitoring of the establishment and functioning of social care services for victims of domestic violence, which will be provided at local self-government units.

Duties of the responsible line authorities

- d) to refer and direct the victim to other support and protection services from domestic violence.

2/1. - d) drafts special forms for processing and reporting statistical data on domestic violence, as well as publishes these data in the statistical yearbook; dh) through the responsible structures under its subordination, ensures the implementation of the obligation to prepare the medico-legal and psychiatrico-legal report by the legal medical staff, based on the request of the competent institutions and/or the victim.

3/1. The ministry responsible for education and the ministry responsible for employment and vocational training, according to the field of responsibility they cover, have these duties:

- a) drafts curricula for secondary and higher schools on the rules of conduct in the family;
- b) prepares school texts and other supplementary materials for educating pupils and/or students with mentalities of prohibiting violence in family relationships;
- c) provides programs for pre-university and vocational education of minors and young people from 18 to 21 years of age who are perpetrators, and of minors who are victims, with the aim of their rehabilitation, reintegration and resocialization.
  - 4. Local self-government units, based on the provisions of the legislation in force on local self-government, in the framework of the implementation of this law, have these duties: a) engagement in the creation of specialized social service structures for domestic violence cases, related to the manner of referring domestic violence cases, as well as monitoring the execution of protection orders and immediate protection orders;
- b) the establishment of social and rehabilitation centers for victims and the provision of services for them; coordinating work with existing centers, giving priority to centers specialized in the respective fields and emergency centers for the immediate treatment of domestic violence victims;
- c) the establishment of structures and public centers for the treatment, training and rehabilitation of perpetrators; ç) taking measures, in cooperation with law enforcement agencies, for the separation of the perpetrator from the family, with the aim of leaving the family dwelling for the use of the victim and children;
- d) taking measures for the implementation of the court decision, by the local bodies of the residence and/or stay of the victim; dh) cooperation with the probation service, for monitoring the execution of protection orders, according to the rules provided in the legislation in force on electronic monitoring of persons whose movement is restricted by court decision.

For the implementation of the obligations provided in this article, local self-government units may cooperate with each other through the conclusion of joint agreements, in accordance with the legislation in force on local self-government. 5. The ministry responsible for housing issues supports with funding local self-government units for the provision of specialized housing, according to the legislation in force on social housing.

### Article 7/1

Guarantee for protection

(added by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)

1. Any body or authority that is responsible for addressing domestic violence, to which the victim turns, guarantees protection under this law, regardless of the residence or stay of the victim.

2. This protection includes assistance for temporary shelter, transportation to a safe shelter or to the requested services, accompaniment to emergency or necessary medical services or to protection services for domestic violence victims.

### Neni8

Responsibilities of the responsible authorities (added points 7 and 8 by law no. 10 329, dated 30.9.2010, added letter "dh" of point 3 by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)

1. The responsible authorities have the duty to create special structures and appoint responsible persons for the implementation of this law. The fulfillment of this obligation is overseen by the ministry responsible for gender equality issues and the fight against domestic violence.

2. The responsible authorities have the obligation to respond to every notification made by the victim or other persons indicated in this law, in cases of violence or threat of violence or also in cases of violation of the protection order or immediate protection order. They keep the relevant report and deliver a copy to the victim or his/her companion.

3. The responsible authorities use reasonable means to protect the victim and to prevent the continuation of violence by means of:

- a) informing the victim or his/her companion about the measures to be taken according to the law in force and about the institutions to which he/she should address;
- b) informing the victim or his/her companion regarding existing social services and accompanying to the relevant centers and institutions;
- c) providing transport for the victim and his/her companion to medical or social service centers; ç) making a police officer available in cases of danger to life. dh) preparation by the social worker for domestic violence issues at the local self-government unit and the needs assessment and referral unit, in cooperation with the Coordinated Referral Mechanism for domestic violence cases, of an individual intervention plan that contains the measures and necessary services that must be taken to protect the victim from ongoing violence, provision of immediate services and minimization of the consequences of violence and of a report on the mental health and psychosocial condition of the victim.
  - 4. If there is suspicion that the abuser has threatened or committed domestic violence or has violated protection orders issued by court decision, the police authorities shall make immediate findings.
  - 5. Persons who become aware due to the duty or authority entrusted for the implementation of this law and do not act in its implementation, shall be charged with administrative and/or criminal liability, applying sanctions provided in articles 248 and 251 of the Criminal Code.
  - 6. The police authorities have the duty to record the relevant findings in a written report, as well as to initiate preliminary investigations. The victim is informed by the police of the relevant number of the reported case.
  - 7. Employees of the service and social care for victims of domestic violence, at public institutions and licensed non-profit organizations, as well as lawyers and legal representatives of victims of domestic violence maintain the confidentiality of personal data and information that the victim provides about his/her situation, except when otherwise determined in writing by the victim.
  - 8. The mechanism for coordinating work between responsible authorities for the referral of cases of violence in family relations, as well as the manner of its procedure for the support and rehabilitation of victims of violence are determined by decision of the Council of Ministers.

### Neni9

Subjects that may notify the responsible authorities (amended word in point 1 by law no. 10 329, dated 30.9.2010, replaced word in point 1 by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. In case of domestic violence, the victim may address by means of a request to the nearest police station (of the area where he/she lives or is located), the relevant local unit (commune, municipality), the health center of the area where he/she lives or is located or by request-complaint to the district court of the residence, location of himself/herself or of the abuser, to take the necessary measures.

2. Any person who observes a case of domestic violence may address by means of a request to the above authorities to undertake the necessary measures.

### Neni10/1

Rehabilitation of the abuser

(added by law nr.125/2020, date 15.10.2020)

1. In the protection order, issued by court decision, the abuser is ordered to participate in psychosocial rehabilitation programs and/or in parental skills programs, organized by public or private entities.

2. The rehabilitation program managers report to the local coordinator for the referral of domestic violence cases, the defendant's participation in the program and his progress periodically and the results of the abuser's rehabilitation. If the abuser does not participate in the program, except in cases when there are objective reasons and justified causes for non-participation, at the request of the ad-hoc meeting of the Coordinated Referral Mechanism for domestic violence cases or the subjects provided for in Article 13 of this law, the provisions of the Penal Code for actions carried out in violation of the court decision on protection orders are applied.

3. In the protection order, issued by court decision, the defendant or the abuser, as the case may be, is ordered to participate in rehabilitation programs at hospital centers, outpatient centers or community centers that provide mental health services, alcoholism services or toxicology services.

4. The rehabilitation program managers notify the local coordinator at the Coordinated Referral Mechanism for domestic violence cases of the defendant's participation in the program and his progress.

5. An abuser, sentenced by court decision to serve the sentence in penal decision execution institutions, may participate in psychosocial rehabilitation programs. Participation and cooperation in the program is taken into account in assessing the risk of the convicted person in cases of parole, reward or special leaves, visits.

### Neni11

Effects of the protection order

(word replaced in point 1 by law nr. 47/2018, date 23.7.2018)

1. Notwithstanding any other order or decision issued by the court or any other institution, the protection order containing the measures provided for in Article 10 of this law shall be issued by court decision for the cases provided for by this law.

2. The protection order or the immediate protection order, issued by court decision, or the termination of its effects does not permanently infringe property or custody rights.

## KREU IV

TRIAL FOR THE ISSUANCE OF THE PROTECTION ORDER

### Neni12

Competent body for the issuance of protection orders in cases of domestic violence

(word replaced in point 1 by law nr. 47/2018, date 23.7.2018)

1. The competent body for the issuance of protection orders in cases of domestic violence is the first instance court of general jurisdiction.

To establish the securing of protective measures referred to in Article 10 of this law, the court issues protection orders and immediate protection orders.

2. The interested party may request the court, as the case may be, in accordance with the provisions of this law, the issuance of a protection order, without first requesting the issuance of an immediate protection order.

3. After the court has issued an immediate protection order, the interested party may request the issuance of a protection order, as provided for in this law. The respective protection order serves to reconfirm the continuation of the immediate protection order, as well as secures the protective measures defined in Article 10 of this law.

During the entire period of extension of emergency measures, in the request addressed to the court for the assessment of precautionary protection measures, taken through the order for immediate protection precautionary measures issued, the police must request the court to issue a protection order, without first requesting the issuance of the immediate protection order.

4. In cases when violence is exercised against or in the presence of children, the State Police immediately takes the child under protection and simultaneously refers the case to the Child Protection Unit in the local self-government unit, according to the rules provided for in the legislation in force for the rights and protection of children.

5. Within 48 hours from the taking of measures, according to the determination of point 1 of this article, the bodies responsible for the treatment of cases of domestic violence, at the State Police, submit before the court a request for the assessment of immediate protection precautionary measures and a lawsuit for the issuance of an immediate protection order.

6. The right to request the assessment of the order for immediate protection precautionary measures before the court is also held by the victim and the subjects provided for in article 13, point 3, of this law, within 48 hours from the date of its issuance. 7. The court, 48 hours from the date of submission of the request, in a court hearing decides: a) the assessment of the protection measures imposed in the order for immediate protection precautionary measures and the issuance of the immediate protection order;

b) the annulment of the order for immediate protection precautionary measures and the interruption of its consequences, if the facts and circumstances for which it was issued are not proven.

8. The court, for the needs of the trial, may call the representative of the State Police and/or the local coordinator for the referral of cases of violence in family relationships, to appear in the court hearing to provide explanations.

### Neni14

Form of the lawsuit (added one sentence at the end of point 3 and amended point 4 by law no. 10 329, dated 30.9.2010, amended points 3, 4, 5 and added point 6 by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, replaced words in letter "b" of point 1, by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. The lawsuit for the issuance of protection orders and immediate protection orders must also contain:

- a) personal data about the family or kinship relationships between the victim and the abuser;
- b) a clear description of the facts and circumstances in which the act of domestic violence occurred, including the reasons that explain the violation of the safety, health or well-being of the victim by the defendant (abuser) provided for in article 3 points 1 and 2 of this law;
- c) the specific protective measures requested; ç) the signature of the applicant.
  - 2. The lawsuit for a protection order may be submitted at any time before the court by persons who are legitimized to request it. In cases when immediate assistance is requested, the request may be completed and submitted at the nearest police station and the police officer acts in accordance with the law "On the State Police".
  - 3. For the drafting of the lawsuit, the preparation of documents and representation in the judicial process, the applicant is assisted by a lawyer who provides secondary legal aid, in accordance with the conditions and rules provided for by the legislation in force for legal aid guaranteed by the state.
  - 4. The applicant for IPO/PO and the holder of IPPO is exempted from:
- a) payment of court fees and court expenses, in accordance with the legislation in force for legal aid guaranteed by the state;
- b) the obligation to prepay the fee for the enforcement of the order at the state judicial enforcement service, in accordance with the legislation in force for legal aid guaranteed by the state;
- c) payment for psychosocial expertise acts, sign language interpreter, medico-legal expertise, expertise for the assessment of the risk of domestic violence or any other expertise act.

After the issuance of the IPO/PO, court expenses are charged to the party that has exercised domestic violence.

5. The lawsuit is registered in a separate register, recorded on the day of its submission and drawn by lot the next day. The court conducts the trial within the deadlines provided for by this law and applies the forms of notification provided for in the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure, for the parties or other subjects. For the needs of the development of the trial, when the circumstances of the case dictate it, the court, as needed, may hold the court hearing in the absence of the parties, regardless of the confirmation of receipt of notice.

6. The court, in the decision to issue the protection order, also decides on the cases provided for in point 4 of this article.

### Article 15

Evidence in the judicial process

(amended the last sentence of point 2 and added point 2/1 by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)

1. Evidence that must be presented to the court may be: testimony, police reports, medical reports, expert reports, examinations and explanations/statements of the parties, other documents issued by employees of the social services office at local units, documents issued by legal persons (NGOs), registered according to the provisions in force.

2. When it becomes clear from the request data that police commissariats, local government bodies or health centers have written evidence related to the exercise of domestic violence, they immediately issue a certified copy (copy with seal) for this based on the request submitted by the applicant or the court itself. In cases when the applicant declares the impossibility of securing the documentation provided for in this point, as well as in any other case when the court considers that the fulfillment of the request may cause delays, which violate the essence of the victim's rights, the court may order the submission of the necessary documentation from public administration bodies that have it.

2/1 Failure to issue the documentation specified in point 2 of this article constitutes grounds for initiating disciplinary proceedings against the responsible person, based on the provisions of special legislation.

3. When it does not have all the evidence provided for in point 2 of this article, the court issues by decision the protection order, based only on the description of the circumstances and facts in which domestic violence was committed, relying on its own conviction.

### Article 16

Judgment for the issuance of the protection order (amended point 1 by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, replaced word in letter "b" of point 2, by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. The court decides on the issuance of the protection order within 15 days from the day of registration of the request.

2. During the examination of the request for a protection order, the court hears in turn the following persons:

- a) the victim, his/her legal representative or lawyer;
- b) the defendant (abuser), his/her legal representative or lawyer;
- c) the prosecutor, if he/she has submitted the request; ç) police representatives;
- d) representatives of the social services office at the unit where the mentioned person lives permanently or temporarily, when the applicant is under 18 years old, lacks capacity to act or the domestic violence exercised has an impact on the aforementioned persons; dh) employees of health centers or service and rehabilitation centers, who have assisted the victim after the exercise of violence against him/her;
- e) witnesses, whom the court considers necessary.
  - 3. In the case when the request is submitted by the police/prosecutor's office, the victim's withdrawal from the process does not result in non-judgment of the initiated case.

### Article 17

The court's decision on the issuance of the protection order (replaced word in letter "b" of point 1, removed phrase in letter "ç" of point 3, added point 6 by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. The court may issue by decision a protection order only against the abuser mentioned in the request-lawsuit. This decision contains only the protective measures described in Article 10 of this law. The court issues by decision the protection order by determining one or more of the measures specified in Article 10 of this law, if it determines that:

- a) there are reasonable grounds to believe that the abuser poses a risk for committing any act of domestic violence;
- b) the issuance of the protection order by court decision is necessary to protect the safety, health, well-being of the victim or victims, an order in which, in addition to the applicant, the court considers that other persons may also be included, with whom the applicant has family or intimate ties, which may cause the exercise of violence against these latter.
  - 2. The court cannot refuse the issuance of the protection order due to another judicial process, where one of the parties is involved.
  - 3. The final decision of the court on the issuance of the protection order, which, in addition to fulfilling the requirements of Article 310 of the Code of Civil Procedure, contains:
- a) the measure determined by the court;
- b) the duration of the measure, which cannot be longer than 12 months, but with the right of extension;
- c) notification that violation of the protection order, issued by court decision, constitutes a criminal offense within the meaning of Article 320 of the Criminal Code; ç) notification of the right to appeal against the protection order.
  - 4. The protection order, issued by court decision, is immediately notified to the abuser, who was not present during the court hearing, according to Article 316 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

The victim must be provided by the court with two copies of the original decision, so that he/she keeps one for himself/herself and makes the other available to the police when the need arises.

5. The court, within 24 hours after the issuance of the protection order by court decision, sends a copy of it to the following persons:

- a) the victim and other persons indicated in the decision;
- b) the prosecutor, if he/she has made a request;
- c) the social services office of the local units, where the victim and other persons mentioned in the decision have permanent or temporary residence; ç) the police commissariat in the area where the victim and other persons mentioned in the decision have permanent or temporary residence.
  - 6. The decision on the protection order and the immediate protection order is registered in a separate register, which is administered at each court according to the model, rules and procedures approved by decision of the High Judicial Council.

### Article 18

Judgment for the issuance of the immediate protection order (amended point 1 by law no. 10 329, dated 30.9.2010, amended letter "b" of point 2 by law no.

125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. The court decides in relation to the request for immediate protection:

- a) of a minor, within 24 hours after submission of the request;
- b) of other subjects of violence in family relations, within 48 hours after submission of the request.
  - 2. In the case of examination of the request for an immediate protection order, the court holds a hearing where the following persons are heard:
- a) the victim, his/her legal representative or lawyer;
- b) the abuser, his/her representative or lawyer;
- c) the prosecutor, if he/she participates; ç) other applicants, provided for in Article 13 of this law;
- d) witnesses, whom the court considers necessary.

### Neni20

Trial for verification of the immediate protection order

(introductory sentence amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018)

1. The court conducts the session, according to article 16 of this law, no later than 20 days from the day of issuance of the immediate protection order. At the end of the judicial process, the court:

- a) decides the annulment, as well as the termination of the effects of the immediate protection order, issued earlier by court decision, when the latter fails to be proven;
- b) refuses the issuance of the protection order by court decision, when the immediate protection order, issued earlier by court decision, fails to be proven;

### Neni21

Appeal (point 1 repealed and point 2 amended by law no. 47/2018, dated 23.7.2018, amended by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. Against the court decision for the issuance of the protection order, verification of the immediate protection order, as well as non-issuance of the immediate protection order, an appeal may be made within 5 days from the day after notification of the reasoned decision. The court of appeal renders a decision within 15 days from the day of registration of the appeal. Against the decision of the court of appeal, recourse to the High Court is not allowed. Against the decision of the court that has issued the protection order, appeal by the victim is not allowed, except in the case when the victim claims that the imposed measures do not guarantee protection.

2. The filing of the appeal does not suspend the execution of the court decision for the issuance of the immediate protection order or the protection order.

### Neni22

Circumstances for changing, terminating and continuing the protection order

(words replaced in point 1 by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

1. If circumstances have changed fundamentally, the victim (his/her representative), the abuser or the prosecutor, if he/she has participated in the proceedings, may submit a request for changing or terminating the protection order.

2. After receiving the request for changing or terminating the protection order, the court continues the examination of the request, based on article 16 of this law. At the end of the examination the court decides:

- a) leaving in force the protection order;
- b) changing the protection order if this is required by the circumstances that have changed fundamentally; or
- c) terminating it, if the criteria defined in article 10 point 1 of this law no longer apply, due to the fundamental change of circumstances.
  - 3. The submission of the request for changing or terminating the protection order does not suspend the execution of the decision for the issuance of the protection order.
  - 4. The victim or his/her authorized representative, 15 days before the end of the effects of the decision for the issuance of the protection order, may submit the request for its continuation.

If no request is submitted, the protection order is automatically terminated on the day of the end of its effects.

5. After receiving the request for continuation of the protection order, the court proceeds with the examination of the request, based on article 16 of this law. At the end of the examination the court decides:

- a) verification of the termination of the protection order on the date of the end of its effects; or
- b) continuation of the effects of the decision for the issuance of the protection order, if the criteria defined in article 17 point 1 of this law apply.

## CHAPTER V

FINAL PROVISIONS

### Article 24

Criminal proceedings

(added the final sentence of the paragraph by law no. 125/2020, dated 15.10.2020)

The issuance of the immediate protection order or the protection order by court decision does not prevent the interested parties from initiating criminal proceedings, with regard to actions or omissions that constitute criminal offenses. In every case, the police officer, to whom the victim reports for protection, refers the case to the prosecution body for the criminal prosecution of the perpetrator, according to the rules specified in the Code of Criminal Procedure.

Subordinate acts

The Council of Ministers issues subordinate acts for the implementation of this law within 3 months from its entry into force.
