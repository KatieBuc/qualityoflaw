GENERAL PART

### TÍTULO I

ON THE APPLICATION OF CRIMINAL LAW

(Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

Criminal law in time

##### Art. 2

ş - No one may be punished for an act that a subsequent law no longer considers a crime, with the execution and criminal effects of the conviction ceasing by virtue of it. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

A subsequent law that in any way favors the agent applies to previous acts, even if decided by a conviction with res judicata. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

Exceptional or temporary law (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 3

ş - An exceptional or temporary law, even after the period of its duration has elapsed or the circumstances that determined it have ceased, applies to acts committed during its effectiveness. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

Time of the crime

##### Art. 4

ş - The crime is considered committed at the moment of the action or omission, even if the moment of the result is different. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

Territoriality

##### Art. 5

ş - Brazilian law applies, without prejudice to conventions, treaties and rules of international law, to crimes committed in national territory. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

##### § 1

ş - For criminal purposes, Brazilian vessels and aircraft of a public nature or in service of the Brazilian government wherever they may be, as well as Brazilian merchant or privately-owned aircraft and vessels that are, respectively, in the corresponding airspace or on the high seas, are considered extensions of national territory. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

##### § 2

ş - Brazilian law is also applicable to crimes committed on board foreign privately-owned aircraft or vessels, when the former are landed in national territory or in flight in the corresponding airspace, and the latter are in a port or territorial sea of Brazil. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

Place of the crime (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

##### Art. 6

ş - The crime is considered committed in the place where the action or omission occurred, in whole or in part, as well as where the result was produced or should have been produced. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

Extraterritoriality (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

##### Art. 7ş - Subject to Brazilian law, even if committed abroad: (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

I - the crimes: (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

a) against the life or liberty of the President of the Republic; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

b) against the property or public faith of the Union, the Federal District, a State, a Territory, a Municipality, a public company, mixed-economy company, autarchy or foundation established by the Public Power; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

c) against public administration, by those in its service; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

d) of genocide, when the agent is Brazilian or domiciled in Brazil; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984) II - the crimes: (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

a) that, by treaty or convention, Brazil has undertaken to suppress; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

b) committed by a Brazilian; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

c) committed on Brazilian merchant or privately-owned aircraft or vessels, when in foreign territory and not tried there. (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

##### § 1

ş - In the cases of item I, the agent is punished according to Brazilian law, even if acquitted or convicted abroad. (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

##### § 2

ş - In the cases of item II, the application of Brazilian law depends on the concurrence of the following conditions: (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

a) the agent enters national territory; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

b) the act is also punishable in the country where it was committed; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

c) the crime is included among those for which Brazilian law authorizes extradition; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

d) the agent has not been acquitted abroad or has not served the sentence there; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

e) the agent has not been pardoned abroad or, for another reason, punishability is not extinguished, according to the most favorable law. (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

##### § 3

ş - Brazilian law also applies to crimes committed by a foreigner against a Brazilian outside Brazil, if, the conditions provided for in the previous paragraph being met: (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

a) extradition was not requested or was denied; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984)

b) there was a request from the Minister of Justice. (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 1984) Sentence served abroad (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 8

ş - A sentence served abroad mitigates the sentence imposed in Brazil for the same crime, when different, or is computed therein, when identical. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

Effectiveness of foreign judgment (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 9

ş - A foreign judgment, when the application of Brazilian law produces the same consequences in the case, may be ratified in Brazil to: (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

I - oblige the convicted person to repair the damage, make restitution and other civil effects; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

II - subject them to a security measure. (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

Ratification depends on: (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

a) for the effects provided for in item I, a request from the interested party; (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

b) for the other effects, the existence of an extradition treaty with the country from whose judicial authority the judgment emanated, or, in the absence of a treaty, a request from the Minister of Justice. (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

Counting of time period (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 10

The first day is included in the computation of the time period. Days, months and years are counted by the common calendar. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

Non-computable fractions of the sentence (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 11

Fractions of a day are disregarded in sentences of deprivation of liberty and in sentences restricting rights, and, in fines, fractions of a cruzeiro. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

Special legislation (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 12

The general rules of this Code apply to acts criminalized by special law, if it does not provide otherwise. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

### TÍTULO II

OF THE CRIME

Causal relationship (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 13

The result, on which the existence of the crime depends, is only imputable to the one who caused it. The action or omission without which the result would not have occurred is considered a cause. (Wording given by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

Supervening independent cause (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - The supervening of a relatively independent cause excludes imputation when, by itself, it produced the result; the prior acts, however, are imputed to the one who committed them. (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

Relevance of the omission (Included by Law No. 7,209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - Omission is criminally relevant when the person who omits had a duty and was able to act to prevent the result. The duty to act is incumbent upon those who:(Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

a) have by law an obligation of care, protection or surveillance; (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

b) have otherwise assumed the responsibility to prevent the result; (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

c) have created the risk of the occurrence of the result by their prior conduct. (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 14

A crime is said to be: (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Consummated crime (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

I - consummated, when all elements of its legal definition are present; (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Attempt (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

II - attempted, when, after execution has begun, it is not consummated due to circumstances beyond the will of the agent. (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Penalty for attempt (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

Unless otherwise provided, an attempt is punished with the penalty corresponding to the consummated crime, reduced by one to two thirds.(Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Voluntary desistance and effective repentance (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)Art. 15 - The agent who voluntarily desists from continuing the execution or prevents the result from occurring shall only be liable for acts already committed.(Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Subsequent repentance (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 16

In crimes committed without violence or serious threat to the person, if the damage is repaired or the thing restored, up until the receipt of the complaint or charge, by voluntary act of the agent, the penalty shall be reduced by one to two thirds. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Impossible crime (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 17

The attempt is not punished when, due to the absolute ineffectiveness of the means or the absolute impropriety of the object, it is impossible to consummate the crime.(Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 18

A crime is said to be: (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Intentional crime (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

I - intentional, when the agent wanted the result or assumed the risk of producing it;(Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Negligent crime (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

II - negligent, when the agent caused the result through recklessness, negligence or malpractice. (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

Except in cases expressly provided by law, no one may be punished for an act defined as a crime unless they commit it intentionally. (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Aggravation by result (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 19

For the result that especially aggravates the penalty, only the agent who has caused it at least negligently shall be liable.(Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Mistake as to elements of the offense (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 20

Mistake as to a constitutive element of the legal type of crime excludes intent, but allows punishment for a negligent crime, if provided by law. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Putative justifications (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - A person is exempt from penalty who, by mistake fully justified by the circumstances, supposes a factual situation that, if it existed, would make the action lawful. There is no exemption from penalty when the mistake derives from negligence and the act is punishable as a negligent crime.(Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Mistake caused by a third party (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - The third party who causes the mistake shall be liable for the crime. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Mistake as to the person (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 3

ş - Mistake as to the person against whom the crime is committed does not exempt from penalty. In this case, the conditions or qualities of the victim are not considered, but rather those of the person against whom the agent wanted to commit the crime. (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Mistake as to the unlawfulness of the act (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 21

Ignorance of the law is inexcusable. Mistake as to the unlawfulness of the act, if unavoidable, exempts from penalty; if avoidable, may reduce it by one sixth to one third. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

The mistake is considered avoidable if the agent acts or omits without awareness of the unlawfulness of the act, when it was possible for them, in the circumstances, to have or reach such awareness. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Irresistible coercion and hierarchical obedience (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 22

If the act is committed under irresistible coercion or in strict obedience to an order, not manifestly unlawful, from a hierarchical superior, only the author of the coercion or the order is punishable. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Exclusion of unlawfulness (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 23 - There is no crime when the agent commits the act: (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

I - in a state of necessity; (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

II - in self-defense; (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984) (Vide ADPF 779)

III - in strict compliance with legal duty or in the regular exercise of a right. (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Punishable excess (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

The agent, in any of the hypotheses of this article, shall be liable for intentional or negligent excess. (Incluído pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

State of necessity

##### Art. 24

A person is considered in a state of necessity who commits the act to save from present danger, which they did not cause by their own will, nor could otherwise avoid, their own or another's right, the sacrifice of which, in the circumstances, was not reasonable to require. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - A person who had a legal duty to face the danger cannot claim a state of necessity. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - Even if it is reasonable to require the sacrifice of the threatened right, the penalty may be reduced by one to two thirds. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Self-defense

##### Art. 25

A person is understood to be in self-defense who, using the necessary means moderately, repels unjust aggression, present or imminent, against their own or another's right. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984) (Vide ADPF 779)

###### Parágrafo único.

Subject to the requirements provided in the caput of this article, a public security agent who repels aggression or risk of aggression against a victim held hostage during the commission of crimes is also considered to be in self-defense. (Incluído pela Lei nş 13.964, de 2019) (Vide ADPF 779)

### TÍTULO III

OF CRIMINAL RESPONSIBILITY

Not criminally responsible

##### Art. 26

The agent who, due to mental illness or incomplete or delayed mental development, was, at the time of the action or omission, entirely incapable of understanding the unlawful character of the act or of determining themselves in accordance with such understanding, is exempt from penalty. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Reduction of penalty

###### Parágrafo único

The penalty may be reduced by one to two thirds, if the agent, by virtue of mental health disturbance or incomplete or delayed mental development, was not entirely capable of understanding the unlawful character of the act or of determining themselves in accordance with such understanding. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Minors under eighteen years

##### Art. 27

Minors under 18 (eighteen) years of age are criminally not responsible, being subject to the norms established in special legislation. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Emotion and passion

##### Art. 28 - Do not exclude criminal imputability: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

I - emotion or passion; (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Intoxication

II - intoxication, voluntary or culpable, by alcohol or substance of analogous effects. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - The agent is exempt from penalty who, by complete intoxication, arising from fortuitous case or force majeure, was, at the time of the action or omission, entirely incapable of understanding the illicit character of the fact or of determining himself in accordance with that understanding. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - The penalty may be reduced from one to two thirds, if the agent, by intoxication, arising from fortuitous case or force majeure, did not possess, at the time of the action or omission, the full capacity to understand the illicit character of the fact or to determine himself in accordance with that understanding. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

### TÍTULO IV

CONCURRENCE OF PERSONS

##### Art. 29

Whoever, in any way, concurs in the crime incurs the penalties assigned thereto, in the measure of his culpability. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - If the participation is of minor importance, the penalty may be reduced from one sixth to one third. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - If any of the concurring parties wished to participate in a less serious crime, the penalty for that crime shall be applied to him; this penalty shall be increased by up to half, in the event that the more serious result was foreseeable. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Non-communicable circumstances

##### Art. 30

Circumstances and conditions of a personal character do not communicate, except when elementary to the crime. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Cases of impunity

##### Art. 31

The agreement, determination or instigation and assistance, except for express provision to the contrary, are not punishable, if the crime does not, at least, come to be attempted. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

### TÍTULO V

OF PENALTIESCHAPTER I

OF TYPES OF PENALTY

##### Art. 32

The penalties are: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

I - deprivation of liberty;

II - restriction of rights;

III - fine.

SECTION I

OF PENALTIES DEPRIVING LIBERTY

Imprisonment and detention

##### Art. 33

The penalty of imprisonment shall be served in a closed, semi-open or open regime. That of detention, in a semi-open or open regime, except for the necessity of transfer to a closed regime. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - It is considered: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

a) closed regime the execution of the penalty in a maximum or medium security establishment;

b) semi-open regime the execution of the penalty in an agricultural, industrial colony or similar establishment;

c) open regime the execution of the penalty in a halfway house or adequate establishment.

##### § 2

ş - Penalties depriving liberty shall be executed in progressive form, according to the merit of the convicted person, observing the following criteria and subject to the hypotheses of transfer to a more rigorous regime: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

a) the convicted person to a penalty exceeding 8 (eight) years shall begin to serve it in a closed regime;

b) the non-recidivist convicted person, whose penalty exceeds 4 (four) years and does not exceed 8 (eight), may, from the beginning, serve it in a semi-open regime;

c) the non-recidivist convicted person, whose penalty is equal to or less than 4 (four) years, may, from the beginning, serve it in an open regime.

##### § 3

ş - The determination of the initial regime of service of the penalty shall be made with observance of the criteria provided in art. 59 of this Code.(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 4

o The convicted person for crime against public administration shall have the progression of regime of service of the penalty conditioned on the reparation of the damage caused, or the return of the product of the illicit act practiced, with legal additions. (Added by Law No. 10.763, of 12.11.2003)

Rules of the closed regime

##### Art. 34

The convicted person shall be submitted, at the beginning of service of the penalty, to a criminological classification examination for individualization of execution. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - The convicted person is subject to work in the daytime period and to isolation during nighttime rest. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - The work shall be in common within the establishment, in conformity with the aptitudes or previous occupations of the convicted person, provided they are compatible with the execution of the penalty.(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 3

ş - External work is admissible, in the closed regime, in public services or works. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Rules of the semi-open regime

##### Art. 35

The rule of art. 34 of this Code, caput, applies to the convicted person who begins service of the penalty in a semi-open regime. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - The convicted person is subject to work in common during the daytime period, in an agricultural, industrial colony or similar establishment. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - External work is admissible, as well as attendance at supplementary vocational courses, secondary or higher education. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Rules of the open regime

##### Art. 36

The open regime is based on self-discipline and sense of responsibility of the convicted person. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - The convicted person shall, outside the establishment and without surveillance, work, attend courses or exercise other authorized activity, remaining confined during the nighttime period and on days off. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - The convicted person shall be transferred from the open regime, if he practices an act defined as an intentional crime, if he frustrates the purposes of execution or if, being able, he does not pay the cumulatively applied fine. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Special regime

##### Art. 37

Women serve penalty in a proper establishment, observing the duties and rights inherent to their personal condition, as well as, where applicable, the provisions of this Chapter. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Rights of the prisoner

##### Art. 38

The prisoner retains all rights not affected by the loss of liberty, imposing on all authorities respect for his physical and moral integrity. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Work of the prisoner

##### Art. 39

The work of the prisoner shall always be remunerated, being guaranteed the benefits of Social Security. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Special legislation

##### Art. 40

Special legislation shall regulate the matter provided in arts. 38 and 39 of this Code, as well as specify the duties and rights of the prisoner, the criteria for revocation and transfer of regimes and shall establish disciplinary infractions and corresponding sanctions. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Supervening mental illness

##### Art. 41

The convicted person who suffers supervening mental illness shall be confined to a custody and psychiatric treatment hospital or, in its absence, to another adequate establishment. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Deduction

##### Art. 42

There shall be computed, in the penalty depriving liberty and in the security measure, the time of provisional imprisonment, in Brazil or abroad, that of administrative imprisonment and that of confinement in any of the establishments referred to in the preceding article. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

SECTION II

OF PENALTIES RESTRICTING RIGHTS

Penalties restricting rights

##### Art. 43.

The restrictive penalties of rights are: (Wording given by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)I - pecuniary payment; (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

II - loss of goods and assets; (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

III - weekend limitation. (Added by Law No. 7.209, of 1984)

IV - provision of service to the community or public entities; (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 25.11.1998)

V - temporary suspension of rights; (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 25.11.1998)

VI - weekend limitation. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 25.11.1998)

##### Art. 44. The restrictive penalties of rights are autonomous and replace those privative of liberty, when: (Wording given by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

I a penalty privative of liberty not exceeding four years is applied and the crime was not committed with violence or serious threat to the person or, whatever penalty is applied, if the crime was negligent;(Wording given by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

II the defendant is not a repeat offender in intentional crime; (Wording given by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

III the culpability, antecedents, social conduct and personality of the convicted person, as well as the motives and circumstances indicate that this substitution is sufficient. (Wording given by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 1o (VETOED) (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 2

o In a conviction equal to or less than one year, the substitution may be made by fine or by one restrictive penalty of rights; if greater than one year, the penalty privative of liberty may be replaced by one restrictive penalty of rights and fine or by two restrictive of rights. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 3

o If the convicted person is a repeat offender, the judge may apply the substitution, provided that, in view of a previous conviction, the measure is socially advisable and the recidivism has not occurred by virtue of the practice of the same crime. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 4

o The restrictive penalty of rights converts into privative of liberty when unjustified non-compliance with the imposed restriction occurs. In the calculation of the penalty privative of liberty to be executed, the time served of the restrictive penalty of rights shall be deducted, respecting the minimum balance of thirty days of detention or imprisonment. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 5

o If a conviction to a penalty privative of liberty for another crime supervenes, the criminal execution judge shall decide on the conversion, and may refrain from applying it if it is possible for the convicted person to serve the previous substitute penalty. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

Conversion of restrictive penalties of rightsArt. 45. In applying the substitution provided for in the previous article, one shall proceed in the form of this and of arts. 46, 47 and 48. (Wording given by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 1

o The pecuniary payment consists of payment in cash to the victim, their dependents or a public or private entity with social purpose, of an amount fixed by the judge, not less than 1 (one) minimum wage nor more than 360 (three hundred and sixty) minimum wages. The amount paid shall be deducted from the amount of any conviction in civil reparation action, if the beneficiaries coincide. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 2

o In the case of the previous paragraph, if there is acceptance by the beneficiary, the pecuniary payment may consist of a payment of another nature. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 3

o The loss of goods and assets belonging to the convicted persons shall take place, subject to special legislation, in favor of the National Penitentiary Fund, and its value shall have as ceiling whichever is greater the amount of damage caused or the proceeds obtained by the agent or by a third party, as a consequence of the commission of the crime. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 4o (VETOED) (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

Provision of services to the community or public entities

##### Art. 46.

The provision of services to the community or public entities is applicable to convictions exceeding six months of deprivation of liberty. (Wording given by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 1

o The provision of services to the community or public entities consists of the assignment of unpaid tasks to the convicted person. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 2

o The provision of service to the community shall take place in welfare entities, hospitals, schools, orphanages and other similar establishments, in community or state programs. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 3

o The tasks referred to in § 1o shall be assigned according to the aptitudes of the convicted person, and must be fulfilled at the rate of one hour of task per day of conviction, fixed so as not to harm the normal work schedule. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

##### § 4

o If the replaced penalty exceeds one year, the convicted person may serve the substitute penalty in less time (art. 55), never less than half of the fixed penalty privative of liberty. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)

Temporary suspension of rights (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 47 - The penalties of temporary suspension of rights are: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

I - prohibition of the exercise of office, function or public activity, as well as elective mandate; (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

II - prohibition of the exercise of profession, activity or occupation that depend on special qualification, license or authorization from public authorities;(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

III - suspension of authorization or license to drive a vehicle. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

IV prohibition from frequenting certain places. (Added by Law No. 9.714, of 1998)V - prohibition from enrolling in public competitive examination, assessment or exam. (Added by Law No. 12.550, of 2011)

Weekend limitation

##### Art. 48

The weekend limitation consists of the obligation to remain, on Saturdays and Sundays, for 5 (five) hours daily, in a halfway house or other suitable establishment. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único - During the stay, courses and lectures may be provided to the convicted person or educational activities assigned.(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Fine

##### Art. 49

The fine penalty consists of payment to the penitentiary fund of the amount fixed in the sentence and calculated in fine-days. It shall be, at minimum, 10 (ten) and, at maximum, 360 (three hundred and sixty) fine-days. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - The value of the fine-day shall be fixed by the judge and may not be less than one thirtieth of the highest monthly minimum wage in force at the time of the fact, nor more than 5 (five) times that wage. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - The value of the fine shall be updated, at the time of execution, by monetary correction indices. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Payment of the fine

##### Art. 50

The fine must be paid within 10 (ten) days after the sentence becomes final. At the request of the convicted person and according to the circumstances, the judge may allow payment to be made in monthly installments. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 1ş - The collection of the fine may be effected by deduction from the salary or wage of the convicted person when: (Added by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

b) applied cumulatively with restrictive penalty of rights; (Added by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

c) conditional suspension of sentence is granted. (Added by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - The deduction must not affect the resources indispensable to the support of the convicted person and their family. (Added by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Conversion of the Fine and revocation (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 51.

Once the conviction sentence has become final, the fine shall be executed before the criminal enforcement judge and shall be considered a debt of value, with the rules relating to the active debt of the Public Treasury being applicable, including with regard to the grounds for interruption and suspension of prescription. (Wording given by Law No. 13.964, of 2019)

#### CAPÍTULO III

DA APLICAÇÃO DA PENA

Setting of the penalty

##### Art. 59

The judge, taking into account the culpability, antecedents, social conduct, personality of the agent, motives, circumstances and consequences of the crime, as well as the behavior of the victim, shall establish, as necessary and sufficient for reprobation and prevention of the crime: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

I - the applicable penalties among those cominated;(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

II - the amount of applicable penalty, within the prescribed limits;(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984) III - the initial regime of compliance with the custodial penalty;(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984) IV - the substitution of the applied custodial penalty by another type of penalty, if applicable. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

Special criteria for the fine penalty

##### Art. 60

In setting the fine penalty the judge must take into account, primarily, the economic situation of the defendant. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

##### § 1

º - The fine may be increased up to triple, if the judge considers that, by virtue of the economic situation of the defendant, it is ineffective, although applied at the maximum. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

Substitute fine

##### § 2

º - The applied custodial penalty, not exceeding 6 (six) months, may be substituted by a fine, observing the criteria of items II and III of art. 44 of this Code.(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

Aggravating circumstances

##### Art. 61 - The following are circumstances that always aggravate the penalty, when they do not constitute or qualify the crime:(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

I - recidivism; (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

II - having the agent committed the crime: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

a) for a futile or base motive;

b) to facilitate or ensure the execution, concealment, impunity or advantage of another crime;

c) by betrayal, by ambush, or through dissimulation, or other resource that made difficult or impossible the defense of the offended;

d) with the use of poison, fire, explosive, torture or other insidious or cruel means, or from which common danger could result;

e) against ascendant, descendant, sibling or spouse;

f) with abuse of authority or taking advantage of domestic relations, cohabitation or hospitality, or with violence against women in the form of the specific law; (Wording given by Law No. 11.340, of 2006)

g) with abuse of power or violation of duty inherent to a position, trade, ministry or profession;

h) against a child, person over 60 (sixty) years old, infirm person or pregnant woman; (Wording given by Law No. 10.741, of 2003)

i) when the offended was under the immediate protection of the authority;

j) on the occasion of fire, shipwreck, flood or any public calamity, or particular misfortune of the offended;

l) in a state of premeditated drunkenness.

Aggravating factors in the case of participation of multiple persons

##### Art. 62 - The penalty shall be further aggravated in relation to the agent who: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

I - promotes or organizes the cooperation in the crime or directs the activity of the other agents; (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

II - coerces or induces another to the material execution of the crime; (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

III - instigates or determines someone subject to his authority or not punishable by virtue of personal condition or quality to commit the crime; (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

IV - executes the crime, or participates in it, for payment or promise of reward.(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

Recidivism

##### Art. 63

Recidivism is verified when the agent commits a new crime, after the sentence that, in the Country or abroad, has convicted him of a previous crime has become final. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

##### Art. 64 - For the purpose of recidivism: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

I - the previous conviction does not prevail if a period of time exceeding 5 (five) years has elapsed between the date of completion or extinction of the penalty and the subsequent offense, including the probation period of the suspension or conditional release, if revocation does not occur; (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

II - proper military crimes and political crimes are not considered.(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

Mitigating circumstances

##### Art. 65 - The following are circumstances that always mitigate the penalty: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

I - the agent being under 21 (twenty-one) years old at the date of the fact, or over 70 (seventy) years old at the date of the sentence; (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

II - ignorance of the law; (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984) III - having the agent:(Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of July 11, 1984)

a) committed the crime for a motive of relevant social or moral value;

b) sought, by his spontaneous will and efficiently, soon after the crime, to avoid or mitigate its consequences, or having, before the trial, repaired the damage;

c) committed the crime under coercion to which he could resist, or in compliance with an order of superior authority, or under the influence of violent emotion, provoked by an unjust act of the victim;

d) confessed spontaneously, before the authority, the authorship of the crime;

e) committed the crime under the influence of a crowd in tumult, if he did not provoke it.

##### Art. 66

The penalty may be further mitigated by reason of a relevant circumstance, prior or subsequent to the crime, even if not expressly provided for by law. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Concurrence of aggravating and mitigating circumstances

##### Art. 67

In the concurrence of aggravating and mitigating circumstances, the penalty must approach the limit indicated by the preponderant circumstances, these being understood as those resulting from the determining motives of the crime, the personality of the agent and recidivism. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Calculation of the penalty

##### Art. 68

The base penalty shall be fixed in accordance with the criterion of art. 59 of this Code; then the mitigating and aggravating circumstances shall be considered; lastly, the causes of reduction and increase. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

In the concurrence of causes of increase or reduction provided for in the special part, the judge may limit himself to a single increase or a single reduction, prevailing, however, the cause that most increases or reduces.(Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Material concurrence

##### Art. 69

When the agent, by more than one action or omission, commits two or more crimes, identical or not, the custodial sentences incurred shall be applied cumulatively. In the case of cumulative application of penalties of imprisonment and detention, the former shall be executed first. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - In the hypothesis of this article, when a custodial sentence, not suspended, has been applied to the agent for one of the crimes, the substitution referred to in art. 44 of this Code shall be inapplicable for the others. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

ş - When restrictive penalties are applied, the convicted person shall serve simultaneously those that are compatible with each other and successively the others. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Formal concurrence

##### Art. 70

When the agent, by a single action or omission, commits two or more crimes, identical or not, the most severe of the applicable penalties shall be applied to him or, if equal, only one of them, but increased, in any case, from one-sixth to one-half. The penalties shall be applied, however, cumulatively, if the action or omission is intentional and the concurrent crimes result from autonomous designs, in accordance with the provisions of the previous article.(Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

The penalty may not exceed that which would be applicable under the rule of art. 69 of this Code. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Continued crime

##### Art. 71

When the agent, by more than one action or omission, commits two or more crimes of the same kind and, by the conditions of time, place, manner of execution and other similar ones, the subsequent ones must be regarded as a continuation of the first, the penalty of only one of the crimes shall be applied to him, if identical, or the most severe, if different, increased, in any case, from one-sixth to two-thirds. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

In intentional crimes, against different victims, committed with violence or serious threat to the person, the judge may, considering the culpability, the antecedents, the social conduct and the personality of the agent, as well as the motives and circumstances, increase the penalty of only one of the crimes, if identical, or the most severe, if different, up to three times, observing the rules of the sole paragraph of art. 70 and art. 75 of this Code. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Fines in the concurrence of crimes

##### Art. 72

In the concurrence of crimes, the penalties of fine are applied distinctly and in full. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Error in execution

##### Art. 73

When, by accident or error in the use of the means of execution, the agent, instead of hitting the person he intended to offend, hits a different person, he is liable as if he had committed the crime against the former, in accordance with the provisions of § 3ş of art. 20 of this Code. In the case that the person the agent intended to offend is also hit, the rule of art. 70 of this Code applies. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Result different from that intended

##### Art. 74

Outside the cases of the previous article, when, by accident or error in the execution of the crime, a result different from that intended occurs, the agent is liable for negligence, if the fact is provided for as a negligent crime; if the intended result also occurs, the rule of art. 70 of this Code applies. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Limit of penalties

##### Art. 75.

The time of serving custodial sentences may not exceed 40 (forty) years. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 13.964, de 2019)

##### § 1

ş When the agent is sentenced to custodial sentences whose sum exceeds 40 (forty) years, they must be unified to meet the maximum limit of this article. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 13.964, de 2019)

##### § 2

ş - Upon a conviction for a fact subsequent to the beginning of the serving of the sentence, a new unification shall be made, disregarding, for that purpose, the period of sentence already served. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Concurrence of offenses

##### Art. 76

In the concurrence of offenses, the most severe penalty shall be executed first. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

#### CAPÍTULO IV

OF THE CONDITIONAL SUSPENSION OF THE SENTENCE

Requirements for suspension of sentence

##### Art. 77

The execution of the custodial sentence, not exceeding 2 (two) years, may be suspended, for 2 (two) to 4 (four) years, provided that: (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

I - the convicted person is not a recidivist in intentional crime; (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

II - the culpability, the antecedents, the social conduct and personality of the agent, as well as the motives and circumstances authorize the granting of the benefit; (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

III - The substitution provided for in art. 44 of this Code is not indicated or applicable. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - A previous conviction to a fine penalty does not prevent the granting of the benefit. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 2

o The execution of the custodial sentence, not exceeding four years, may be suspended, for four to six years, provided that the convicted person is over seventy years of age, or health reasons justify the suspension. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 9.714, de 1998)

##### Art. 78

During the suspension period, the convicted person shall be subject to observation and compliance with the conditions established by the judge. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 1

ş - In the first year of the period, the convicted person must render services to the community (art. 46) or submit to weekend limitation (art. 48). (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### § 2°

If the convicted person has repaired the damage, except for impossibility to do so, and if the circumstances of art. 59 of this Code are entirely favorable to him, the judge may replace the requirement of the previous paragraph with the following conditions, applied cumulatively: (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 9.268, de 1ş.4.1996)

a) prohibition from frequenting certain places; (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

b) prohibition from leaving the district where he resides, without authorization from the judge; (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

c) personal and mandatory appearance in court, monthly, to inform and justify his activities. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 79

The sentence may specify other conditions to which the suspension is subject, provided they are appropriate to the fact and the personal situation of the convicted person. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 80

The suspension does not extend to restrictive penalties nor to the fine. (Redaçăo dada pela Lei nş 7.209, de 11.7.1984)

Mandatory revocation

##### Art. 81

The suspension shall be revoked if, during the period, the beneficiary: (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984) I - is convicted, in an unappealable sentence, for an intentional crime; (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984) II - frustrates, although solvent, the execution of a fine or does not carry out, without justified reason, the reparation of the damage; (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

III - fails to comply with the condition of § 1ş of art. 78 of this Code. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984) Optional revocation

##### § 1

ş - The suspension may be revoked if the convicted person fails to comply with any other imposed condition or is unappealably convicted, for a negligent crime or for a misdemeanor, to a custodial sentence or restrictive of rights. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Extension of the probation period

##### § 2

ş - If the beneficiary is being prosecuted for another crime or misdemeanor, the suspension period is considered extended until the final judgment. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### § 3

ş - When revocation is optional, the judge may, instead of decreeing it, extend the probation period to the maximum, if this was not the one fixed. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Fulfillment of conditions

##### Art. 82

Once the period has expired without revocation having occurred, the custodial sentence is considered extinct. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

#### CAPÍTULO V

OF CONDITIONAL RELEASE

Requirements for conditional release

##### Art. 83

The judge may grant conditional release to a person convicted to a custodial sentence equal to or greater than 2 (two) years, provided that: (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

I - more than one third of the sentence has been served if the convicted person is not a repeat offender in intentional crime and has good background; (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

II - more than half has been served if the convicted person is a repeat offender in intentional crime; (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984) III - proven: (Wording given by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

a) good behavior during the execution of the sentence; (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

b) non-commission of serious misconduct in the last 12 (twelve) months; (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

c) good performance in the work assigned to him; and (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

d) aptitude to provide his own subsistence through honest work; (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019) IV - has repaired, except for effective impossibility to do so, the damage caused by the offense; (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

V - more than two thirds of the sentence has been served, in cases of conviction for heinous crime, practice of torture, illicit trafficking of narcotics and related drugs, trafficking in persons and terrorism, if the convict is not a specific repeat offender in crimes of this nature. (Included by Law nş 13.344, of 2016) (Vigęncia)

###### Parágrafo único

For the person convicted of an intentional crime, committed with violęncia or serious threat ŕ the person, the granting of release shall also be subject ŕ the verification of personal condiçőes that presume that the released person năo will not reoffend. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Sum of sentences

##### Art. 84

Sentences corresponding to different offenses must be added together for the purpose of release. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Specification of conditions

##### Art. 85

The sentence shall specify the conditions to which the release is subject. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Revocation of release

##### Art. 86

The release is revoked, if the released person comes to be convicted to a custodial sentence, in an unappealable sentence: (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

I - for a crime committed during the validity of the benefit; (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

II - for a previous crime, observing the provisions of art. 84 of this Code. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984) Optional revocation

##### Art. 87

The judge may also revoke the release, if the released person fails to comply with any of the obligations contained in the sentence, or is unappealably convicted, for a crime or misdemeanor, to a sentence that is not custodial.(Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Effects of revocation

##### Art. 88

Once the release is revoked, it may not be granted again, and, except when the revocation results from conviction for another crime prior ŕquele benefit, the time the convicted person was free is not deducted from the sentence. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

Extinction

##### Art. 89

The judge may not declare the sentence extinct, while the sentence in proceedings to which the released person responds, for a crime committed during the validity of the release, has not become final.(Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

##### Art. 90

If until its termination the release is not revoked, the custodial sentence is considered extinct. (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

#### CAPÍTULO VI

OF THE EFFECTS OF CONVICTION

Generic and specific effects

##### Art. 91 - The effects of conviction are: (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

I - to make certain the obligation to indemnify the damage caused by the crime; (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984) II - the forfeiture in favor of the Uniăo, subject to the right of the injured party or of a third party in good faith: (Wording given by Law nş 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

a) of the instruments of the crime, provided they consist of things whose manufacture, sale, use, carrying or possession constitutes an unlawful act;

b) of the proceeds of the crime or of any asset or value that constitutes profit obtained by the agent through the practice of the criminal act.

##### § 1

o The forfeiture of assets or values equivalent to the proceeds or profit of the crime may be decreed when these are not found or when they are located abroad. (Included by Law nş 12.694, of 2012)

##### § 2

o In the case of § 1o, the precautionary measures provided for in procedural legislation may cover equivalent assets or values of the investigated or accused person for subsequent decree of forfeiture. (Included by Law nş 12.694, of 2012)

##### Art. 91-A.

In the case of conviction for offenses ŕs which the law imposes a maximum penalty exceeding 6 (six) years of imprisonment, the forfeiture may be decreed, as product or profit of the crime, of assets corresponding ŕ the difference between the value of the convicted person's patrimony and that which is compatible with his lawful income. (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

##### § 1

ş For the purpose of the forfeiture provided for in the caput of this article, the convicted person's patrimony is understood as all assets: (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

I - of his ownership, or in relation to which he has the domain and direct or indirect benefit, on the date of the criminal offense or received subsequently; and (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

II - transferred to third parties free of charge or for a derisory consideration, from the beginning of the criminal activity. (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

##### § 2

ş The convicted person may demonstrate the non-existence of the incompatibility or the lawful origin of the patrimony. (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

##### § 3

ş The forfeiture provided for in this article must be expressly requested by the Public Prosecution, at the time of offering the indictment, with indication of the difference ascertained. (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

##### § 4

ş In the conviction sentence, the judge must declare the value of the difference ascertained and specify the assets whose forfeiture is decreed. (Included by Law nş 13.964, of 2019)

##### § 5

ş The instruments used for the commission of crimes by criminal organizations and militias shall be declared forfeited in favor of the Union or the State, depending on the Justice system where the criminal action is being processed, even if they do not endanger the safety of persons, morals or public order, nor present a serious risk of being used for the commission of new crimes. (Added by Law No. 13.964, of 2019)

##### Art. 92 - The following are also effects of conviction: (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

I - the loss of office, public function or elective mandate: (Wording given by Law No. 9.268, of 1.4.1996)

a) when a custodial sentence is imposed for a period equal to or greater than one year, in crimes committed with abuse of power or violation of duty towards the Public Administration; (Added by Law No. 9.268, of 1.4.1996)

b) when a custodial sentence is imposed for a period exceeding 4 (four) years in other cases. (Added by Law No. 9.268, of 1.4.1996)

II – incapacity for the exercise of parental authority, guardianship or curatorship in intentional crimes subject to imprisonment committed against another person equally holding the same parental authority, against son, daughter or other descendant or against ward or person under curatorship; (Wording given by Law No. 13.715, of 2018)

III - disqualification from driving a vehicle, when used as a means for the commission of an intentional crime. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

###### Parágrafo único

The effects referred to in this article are not automatic and must be declared with reasoning in the sentence. (Wording given by Law No. 7.209, of 11.7.1984)

#### CHAPTER VII

REHABILITATION

Rehabilitation

##### Art. 93

#### CAPÍTULO III

OF CRIMES AGAINST THE ADMINISTRATION OF JUSTICE

Reentry of expelled foreigner

##### Art. 338 - For a foreigner who has been expelled from the national territory to reenter it:

Penalty - imprisonment, from one to four years, without prejudice to new expulsion after serving the sentence.

False accusation

##### Art. 339.

To give cause to the institution of police inquiry, criminal investigatory procedure, judicial process, administrative disciplinary process, civil inquiry or administrative improbity action against someone, imputing to them a crime, ethical-disciplinary infraction or improper act of which one knows them innocent: (Wording given by Law nş 14.110, of 2020)

Penalty - imprisonment, from two to eight years, and fine.

##### § 1

ş - The penalty is increased by one-sixth, if the agent uses anonymity or an assumed name.

##### § 2

ş - The penalty is reduced by half, if the imputation is of the practice of a misdemeanor.

False communication of crime or misdemeanor

##### Art. 340 - To provoke the action of authority, communicating to it the occurrence of a crime or misdemeanor that one knows did not occur:

Penalty - detention, from one to six months, or fine.

False self-accusation

##### Art. 341 - To accuse oneself, before the authority, of a non-existent crime or one committed by another:

Penalty - detention, from three months to two years, or fine.

False testimony or false expert examination

##### Art. 342.

To make a false statement, or deny or conceal the truth as a witness, expert, accountant, translator or interpreter in judicial or administrative proceedings, police inquiry, or in arbitral tribunal: (Wording given by Law nş 10.268, of 28.8.2001)

Penalty - imprisonment, from 2 (two) to 4 (four) years, and fine. (Wording given by Law nş 12.850, of 2013) (Effect)§ 1o The penalties are increased by one-sixth to one-third, if the crime is committed through bribery or if committed with the purpose of obtaining evidence intended to produce effect in criminal proceedings, or in civil proceedings in which a direct or indirect public administration entity is a party.(Wording given by Law nş 10.268, of 28.8.2001)

##### § 2

o The fact ceases to be punishable if, before the sentence in the process in which the illicit act occurred, the agent retracts or declares the truth.(Wording given by Law nş 10.268, of 28.8.2001)

##### Art. 343.

To give, offer or promise money or any other advantage to a witness, expert, accountant, translator or interpreter, to make a false statement, deny or conceal the truth in testimony, expert examination, calculations, translation or interpretation: (Wording given by Law nş 10.268, of 28.8.2001)

Penalty - imprisonment, from three to four years, and fine.(Wording given by Law nş 10.268, of 28.8.2001)

###### Parágrafo único.

The penalties are increased by one-sixth to one-third, if the crime is committed with the purpose of obtaining evidence intended to produce effect in criminal proceedings or in civil proceedings in which a direct or indirect public administration entity is a party. (Wording given by Law nş 10.268, of 28.8.2001)Coercion in the course of proceedings

##### Art. 344

To use violence or serious threat, with the purpose of favoring one's own or another's interest, against authority, party, or any other person who functions or is called to intervene in judicial, police or administrative proceedings, or in arbitral tribunal:

Penalty - imprisonment, from one to four years, and fine, in addition to the penalty corresponding to the violence.

###### Parágrafo único.

The penalty is increased by 1/3 (one-third) up to half if the proceedings involve a crime against sexual dignity. (Included by Law nş 14.245, of 2021)

Arbitrary exercise of one's own rights

##### Art. 345

To take justice into one's own hands, to satisfy a claim, even if legitimate, except when the law permits it: Penalty - detention, from fifteen days to one month, or fine, in addition to the penalty corresponding to the violence.

###### Parágrafo único - If there is no use of violence, proceedings only occur upon complaint.

##### Art. 346

To remove, suppress, destroy or damage one's own property, which is in the possession of a third party by judicial determination or agreement:

Penalty - detention, from six months to two years, and fine.

Procedural fraud

##### Art. 347

To artificially alter, during the pendency of civil or administrative proceedings, the state of place, thing or person, with the purpose of misleading the judge or expert:

Penalty - detention, from three months to two years, and fine.

###### Parágrafo único

If the alteration is intended to produce effect in criminal proceedings, even if not initiated, the penalties are applied double.

Personal favoritism

##### Art. 348 - To assist the perpetrator of a crime punishable by imprisonment to evade the action of public authority:

Penalty - detention, from one to six months, and fine.

##### § 1ş - If the crime is not punishable by imprisonment:

Penalty - detention, from fifteen days to three months, and fine.

##### § 2

ş - If the person who provides assistance is an ascendant, descendant, spouse or sibling of the criminal, they are exempt from penalty.

Real favoritism

##### Art. 349 - To provide assistance to a criminal, outside of cases of co-authorship or receiving, intended to secure the proceeds of the crime:

Penalty - detention, from one to six months, and fine.

##### Art. 349-A.

To enter, promote, intermediate, assist or facilitate the entry of mobile telephone communication device, radio or similar, without legal authorization, in prison establishment. (Included by Law nş 12.012, of 2009).

Penalty: detention, from 3 (three) months to 1 (one) year. (Included by Law nş 12.012, of 2009).

Arbitrary exercise or abuse of power

##### Art. 350 - (Repealed by Law nş 13.869, of 2019) (Effect)

Escape of imprisoned person or person subject to security measure

##### Art. 351 - To promote or facilitate the escape of a person legally imprisoned or subjected to a custodial security measure:

Penalty - detention, from six months to two years.

##### § 1

ş - If the crime is committed with armed force, or by more than one person, or through breaking and entering, the penalty is imprisonment, from two to six years.

##### § 2

ş - If there is use of violence against a person, the penalty corresponding to the violence is also applied.

##### § 3

ş - The penalty is imprisonment, from one to four years, if the crime is committed by a person under whose custody or guard the prisoner or detainee is.

##### § 4

ş - In case of fault of the official responsible for custody or guard, the penalty of detention, from three months to one year, or fine, is applied.

Escape through violence against a person

##### Art. 352 - To escape or attempt to escape, the prisoner or individual subjected to a custodial security measure, using violence against a person:

Penalty - detention, from three months to one year, in addition to the penalty corresponding to the violence.

Seizure of prisoner

##### Art. 353 - To seize a prisoner, in order to mistreat him, from the power of whoever has him under custody or guard:

Penalty - imprisonment, from one to four years, in addition to the penalty corresponding to the violence.

Mutiny of prisoners

##### Art. 354 - For prisoners to mutiny, disturbing the order or discipline of the prison:

Penalty - detention, from six months to two years, in addition to the penalty corresponding to the violence.

Unfaithful representation

##### Art. 355

To betray, in the capacity of lawyer or attorney, the professional duty, harming an interest, whose representation, in court, is entrusted to him:

Penalty - detention, from six months to three years, and fine.

Simultaneous representation or tergiversation

###### Parágrafo único

The lawyer or judicial attorney who defends in the same case, simultaneously or successively, opposing parties incurs the penalty of this article.

Concealment of paper or object of evidentiary value

##### Art. 356

To render useless, totally or partially, or fail to return court records, document or object of evidentiary value, which he received in the capacity of lawyer or attorney:

Penalty - detention, from six months to three years, and fine.

Exploitation of prestige

##### Art. 357

To solicit or receive money or any other benefit, under the pretext of influencing a judge, juror, organ of the Public Ministry, court official, expert, translator, interpreter or witness:

Penalty - imprisonment, from one to five years, and fine.

###### Parágrafo único

The penalties are increased by one third, if the agent alleges or insinuates that the money or benefit is also destined for any of the persons referred to in this article.

Violence or fraud in judicial auction

##### Art. 358

To prevent, disturb or defraud a judicial auction; to remove or seek to remove a competitor or bidder, by means of violence, serious threat, fraud or offering of advantage:

Penalty - detention, from two months to one year, or fine, in addition to the penalty corresponding to the violence.

Disobedience to judicial decision on loss or suspension of right

##### Art. 359

To exercise function, activity, right, authority or duty, from which he was suspended or deprived by judicial decision:

Penalty - detention, from three months to two years, or fine.
