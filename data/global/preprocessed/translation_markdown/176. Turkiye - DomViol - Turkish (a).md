FIRST PART

Fundamental Principles, Definitions and Scope of Application

FIRST CHAPTER

Fundamental Principles and Definitions

Purpose of the Criminal Code

## Article 1- (1) The purpose of the Criminal Code is to protect individual rights and

freedoms, public order and security, the rule of law, public health and the environment, social peace, and to prevent the commission of crimes. In the Code, the fundamental principles of criminal liability and the types of crimes, penalties and security measures are regulated for the realization of this purpose. Principle of legality in crime and punishment

## Article 2- (1) No one may be punished for an act that is not explicitly defined as a crime by

law, nor may a security measure be imposed. No penalty or security measure may be imposed other than the penalties and security measures prescribed by law.

(2) Crimes and penalties may not be established by regulatory acts of the administration.

(3) Analogy may not be used in the application of provisions of laws containing crimes and penalties. Provisions containing crimes and penalties may not be interpreted broadly in a manner that leads to analogy.

Principle of justice and equality before the law

## Article 3- (1) A penalty and security measure proportionate to the gravity of the act

committed shall be imposed on the person who commits a crime.

(2) In the application of the Criminal Code, no distinction may be made among persons on the basis of race, language, religion, sect, nationality, color, sex, political or other ideas or thoughts, philosophical belief, national or social origin, birth, economic or other social status, and no privilege may be granted to any person.

Definitions

## Article 6- (1) In the application of criminal laws;

a) The term citizen means a person who was a Turkish citizen at the time of committing the act,

b) The term child means a person who has not yet reached the age of eighteen,

c) The term public official means a person who participates in the conduct of public activity through appointment or election or in any manner, permanently, for a term or temporarily,

d) The term performing judicial duties means members and judges of high courts, judicial and administrative courts, as well as public prosecutors and lawyers,2

e) The term night time means the period of time beginning one hour after sunset and continuing until one hour before sunrise,

f) The term weapon means;

1. Firearms,

2. Explosive materials,

3. Any kind of cutting, piercing or stabbing instrument made for use in attack and defense,

g) The term by means of press and publication means publications made through any kind of written, visual, audio and electronic mass media,

i) The term person who makes crime a profession means a person who has become accustomed to providing his livelihood, even partially, with gains obtained from crime,

j) The term organization member offender means a person who establishes, manages, joins a criminal organization or commits a crime on behalf of the organization together with others or alone.

SECOND CHAPTER

Scope of Application of the Law

Application in terms of time

## Article 7- (1) No one may be punished or subjected to a security measure for an act that

was not considered a crime according to the law in force at the time it was committed. No one may be punished or subjected to a security measure for an act that is not considered a crime according to the law that entered into force after it was committed. If such a penalty or security measure has been imposed, its execution and legal consequences shall automatically cease.

(2) If the provisions of the law in force at the time the crime was committed and the provisions of the laws that entered into force subsequently differ, the law more favorable to the offender shall be applied and executed.

(3) (Amended: 29/6/2005 – Law No. 5377/Art. 2) Provisions relating to the enforcement regime, except for those concerning suspension of imprisonment, conditional release and recidivism, shall be applied immediately.

(4) Temporary or time-limited laws shall continue to be applied to crimes committed during the period they were in force.

Application in terms of place

## Article 8

(1) Turkish laws shall apply to crimes committed in Turkey. A crime shall be deemed to have been committed in Turkey if the act is committed partly or wholly in Turkey or if the result occurs in Turkey.

(2) A crime shall be deemed to have been committed in Turkey when committed;

a) In Turkish land and airspace and in Turkish territorial waters,

b) On the high seas and in the airspace above it, in or with Turkish sea and air vessels,

c) In or with Turkish naval and air combat vessels,

d) On fixed platforms installed on Turkey's continental shelf or in its exclusive economic zone or against them.

Conviction in a foreign country

## Article 9- (1) A person who has been convicted in a foreign country for a crime

committed in Turkey shall be tried again in Turkey.

Official duty crimes

## Article 10- (1) A person who has undertaken an office or duty on behalf of Turkey in a

foreign country and commits a crime as a result thereof shall be tried again in Turkey, even if a conviction has been rendered against him in the foreign country for that act.

Crime committed by a citizen

## Article 11- (1) If a Turkish citizen, except for the crimes specified in Article 13, commits

in a foreign country a crime requiring imprisonment with a lower limit of not less than one year according to Turkish laws and is present in Turkey, he shall be punished according to Turkish laws on condition that no conviction has been rendered in the foreign country for this crime and that prosecution is possible in Turkey.

(2) If the crime requires imprisonment with a lower limit of less than one year, prosecution is subject to the complaint of the victim or the foreign government. In this case, the complaint must be made within six months from the date the citizen enters Turkey.

SECOND PART

Principles of Criminal Liability

FIRST CHAPTER

Personal Nature of Criminal Liability, Intent and Negligence

Personal nature of criminal liability

## Article 20

(1) Criminal liability is personal. No one can be held liable for another person's act.

(2) Criminal sanctions cannot be applied to legal entities. However, sanctions in the nature of security measures provided for in the law in connection with the offense are reserved. Intent

## Article 21

(1) The commission of an offense depends on the existence of intent. Intent is the knowing and willing realization of the elements in the legal definition of the offense.

(2) If a person commits the act despite foreseeing that the elements in the legal definition of the offense may occur, there is eventual intent. In this case, for offenses requiring aggravated life imprisonment, life imprisonment shall be imposed; for offenses requiring life imprisonment, imprisonment from twenty to twenty-five years shall be imposed; for other offenses, the base penalty shall be reduced by one-third to one-half.

Negligence

## Article 22

(1) Acts committed through negligence shall be punished in cases explicitly specified by law.

(2) Negligence is the realization of a behavior without foreseeing the consequence specified in the legal definition of the offense due to a breach of the duty of care and diligence.

(3) If the consequence occurs despite the person not desiring the foreseen consequence, there is conscious negligence; in this case, the penalty for the negligent offense shall be increased by one-third to one-half.

(4) The penalty to be imposed for an offense committed through negligence shall be determined according to the fault of the perpetrator.

(5) In offenses committed through negligence by more than one person, each person shall be liable for their own fault. Each perpetrator's penalty shall be determined separately according to their fault.

(6) If the consequence caused by the negligent act has led to the perpetrator becoming a victim to such an extent that, solely with respect to their personal and family situation, the imposition of a penalty would be rendered unnecessary, no penalty shall be imposed; in case of conscious negligence, the penalty to be imposed may be reduced from one-half to one-sixth.

Aggravated offense by result

## Article 23- (1) If an act causes a more severe or different consequence than intended, in order

for the person to be held liable for this, they must have acted at least with negligence with regard to this consequence.

SECOND CHAPTER

Grounds that Remove or Reduce Criminal Liability

Execution of law and superior's order

## Article 24- (1) A person who executes a provision of law shall not be punished.

(2) A person who executes an order given by a competent authority, the execution of which is mandatory by virtue of duty, shall not be liable.

(3) An order the subject of which constitutes an offense cannot be executed under any circumstances. Otherwise, both the executor and the person who gave the order shall be liable.

(4) In cases where the control of the lawfulness of the order is prevented by law, the person who gave the order shall be liable for its execution.

Legitimate defense and necessity

## Article 25- (1) No penalty shall be imposed on the perpetrator for acts committed

out of the necessity to repel an unlawful attack that has occurred, is occurring or whose occurrence or repetition is certain, directed against a right belonging to oneself or another, in a manner proportionate to the attack according to the circumstances at that moment.

(2) No penalty shall be imposed on the perpetrator for acts committed out of the necessity to escape or to save another from a grave and certain danger directed against a right belonging to oneself or another, which was not knowingly caused and for which there is no other means of protection, and provided that there is a proportion between the gravity of the danger and the subject matter and means used. Exercise of rights and consent of the interested party

## Article 26- (1) No penalty shall be imposed on a person who exercises their right.

(2) No penalty shall be imposed on anyone for an act committed within the framework of the declared consent of a person, in relation to a right over which the person has absolute power of disposal. Exceeding the limits

## Article 27- (1) In the event that the limits are exceeded without intent in causes that

eliminate criminal responsibility, if the act is also punishable when committed through negligence, a sentence reduced by one-sixth to one-third of the penalty prescribed by law for the negligent offense shall be imposed.

(2) If exceeding the limits in legitimate defense arises from an excusable excitement, fear or panic, no penalty shall be imposed on the perpetrator.

Coercion and violence, intimidation and threat

## Article 28- (1) No penalty shall be imposed on a person who commits a crime as a

result of coercion and violence that they cannot resist or escape from, or as a result of certain and grave intimidation or threat. In such cases, the person who uses coercion and violence, intimidation and threat shall be deemed the perpetrator of the crime. Unjust provocation

## Article 29- (1) A person who commits a crime under the influence of anger or severe

distress caused by an unjust act shall be sentenced to imprisonment from eighteen years to twenty-four years instead of aggravated life imprisonment, and from twelve years to eighteen years instead of life imprisonment. In other cases, one-quarter to three-quarters of the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced. Mistake

## Article 30- (1) A person who does not know the material elements in the legal

definition of the crime during the commission of the act does not act intentionally. Liability for negligence remains reserved in case of this mistake.

(2) A person who is mistaken about the occurrence of qualified circumstances of a crime that require more severe or less severe punishment shall benefit from this mistake.

(3) A person who falls into an unavoidable mistake regarding the occurrence of conditions relating to causes that eliminate or reduce criminal responsibility shall benefit from this mistake.

(4) (Additional paragraph: 29/6/2005 – 5377/4 art.) A person who falls into an unavoidable mistake regarding the fact that the act committed constitutes wrongfulness shall not be punished.

Young age

## Article 31- (1) Children who have not completed twelve years of age at the time of

committing the act have no criminal responsibility. No criminal prosecution may be conducted against these persons; however, security measures specific to children may be applied.

(2) (Amended: 29/6/2005 – 5377/5 art.) Those who have completed twelve years of age but have not completed fifteen years of age at the time of committing the act have no criminal responsibility if they cannot comprehend the legal meaning and consequences of the act committed or their ability to direct their behavior has not developed sufficiently. However, security measures specific to children shall be imposed on these persons. In the presence of the ability to comprehend the legal meaning and consequences of the act committed and to direct their behavior in relation to this act, if the crime requires aggravated life imprisonment, these persons shall be sentenced to imprisonment from twelve years to fifteen years; if it requires life imprisonment, from nine years to eleven years. Half of other penalties shall be reduced and in this case the imprisonment sentence to be imposed for each act cannot exceed seven years.

(3) (Amended: 29/6/2005 – 5377/5 art.) For persons who have completed fifteen years of age but have not completed eighteen years of age at the time of committing the act, if the crime requires aggravated life imprisonment, imprisonment from eighteen years to twenty-four years; if it requires life imprisonment, imprisonment from twelve years to fifteen years shall be imposed. One-third of other penalties shall be reduced and in this case the imprisonment sentence to be imposed for each act cannot exceed twelve years.

Mental illness

## Article 32- (1) No penalty shall be imposed on a person who, due to mental illness,

cannot comprehend the legal meaning and consequences of the act committed or whose ability to direct their behavior in relation to this act has significantly diminished. However, a security measure shall be imposed on these persons.

(2) For a person whose ability to direct their behavior in relation to the act committed has diminished, although not to the degree specified in the first paragraph, twenty-five years of imprisonment instead of aggravated life imprisonment, twenty years of imprisonment instead of life imprisonment shall be imposed. In other cases, the penalty to be imposed may be reduced by up to one-sixth. The sentence imposed may be applied partially or completely as a security measure specific to mental patients, provided that the duration is the same.

Deaf-muteness

## Article 33- (1) The provisions of this Code relating to children who have not

completed twelve years of age at the time of committing the act shall apply to deaf-mutes who have not completed fifteen years of age; the provisions relating to those who have completed twelve years of age but have not completed fifteen years of age shall apply to deaf-mutes who have completed fifteen years of age but have not completed eighteen years of age; the provisions relating to those who have completed fifteen years of age but have not completed eighteen years of age shall apply to deaf-mutes who have completed eighteen years of age but have not completed twenty-one years of age. Temporary causes, being under the influence of alcohol or drugs

## Article 34- (1) No penalty shall be imposed on a person who, due to a temporary

cause or under the influence of involuntarily taken alcohol or drugs, cannot comprehend the legal meaning and consequences of the act committed or whose ability to direct their behavior in relation to this act has significantly diminished.

(2) The provision of the first paragraph shall not apply to a person who commits a crime under the influence of voluntarily taken alcohol or drugs.

THIRD PART

Attempt to Commit a Crime

Attempt to commit a crime

## Article 35- (1) If a person directly commences the commission of a crime they intend

to commit through suitable acts but cannot complete it due to reasons beyond their control, they shall be held responsible for attempt.

(2) In case of attempt to commit a crime, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from thirteen years to twenty years instead of aggravated life imprisonment, from nine years to fifteen years instead of life imprisonment, according to the gravity of the damage or danger that has occurred. In other cases, one-quarter to three-quarters of the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced.

Voluntary abandonment

## Article 36- (1) If the perpetrator voluntarily abandons the acts of commission of the

crime or prevents the completion of the crime or the occurrence of the result through their own efforts, they shall not be punished for attempt; but if the completed portion itself constitutes a crime, they shall be punished only with the penalty pertaining to that crime.

FOURTH PART

Participation in Crime

Perpetration

## Article 37- (1) Each of the persons who jointly realize the act contained in the legal

definition of the crime shall be held responsible as a perpetrator.

(2) A person who uses another as an instrument in committing the crime shall also be held responsible as a perpetrator. The penalty of a person who uses those without capacity for culpability as an instrument in committing the crime shall be increased by one-third to one-half.

Instigation

## Article 38- (1) A person who instigates another to commit a crime shall be punished

with the penalty of the crime committed.

(2) In case of instigation to crime by using influence arising from ascendant and descendant relationship, the penalty of the instigator shall be increased by one-third to one-half. In case of instigation of children to crime, the existence of ascendant and descendant relationship is not required for the increase of penalty according to this paragraph provision.

(3) If the instigator is not identified, the perpetrator or other accomplice who enables their identity to be revealed may be sentenced to imprisonment from twenty years to twenty-five years instead of aggravated life imprisonment, from fifteen years to twenty years instead of life imprisonment. In other cases, a reduction of one-third may be made in the penalty to be imposed. Aiding and abetting

## Madde 39- (1) A person who aids in the commission of the crime shall be sentenced

to imprisonment from fifteen to twenty years if the committed crime requires aggravated life imprisonment; from ten to fifteen years if it requires life imprisonment. In other cases, the penalty shall be reduced by half. However, in this case, the penalty to be imposed cannot exceed eight years.

(2) In the following cases, a person shall be held responsible as an aider for the committed crime:

- a) Encouraging the commission of a crime or strengthening the decision to commit a crime or promising to provide assistance after the commission of the act.
- b) Instructing on how the crime will be committed or providing the means used in the commission of the act.
- c) Facilitating its execution by providing assistance before or during the commission of the crime.

Rule of accessoriness

## Madde 40- (1) For participation in a crime, the existence of an intentionally and

unlawfully committed act is sufficient. Every person who participates in the commission of the crime shall be punished according to their own culpable act, without regard to personal reasons preventing the punishment of the other.

(2) In special crimes, only a person having the special perpetrator qualification can be a principal. Other persons participating in the commission of these crimes shall be held responsible as inciters or aiders.

(3) To be held responsible for participation in a crime, the relevant crime must have at least reached the attempt stage.

Voluntary withdrawal in crimes committed in participation

## Madde 41

(1) In crimes committed in participation, only the accomplice who voluntarily withdraws shall benefit from the provisions of voluntary withdrawal.

(2) If the crime;

a) Has not been committed for a reason other than the effort shown by the person who voluntarily withdrew,

b) Has been committed despite all the efforts of the person who voluntarily withdrew, the provisions of voluntary withdrawal shall also be applied.

FIFTH PART

Concurrence of Crimes

Compound crime

## Madde 42- (1) A crime that is considered a single act because one constitutes an

element or aggravating reason of the other is called a compound crime. Concurrence provisions are not applied to such crimes.

Continuous crime

## Madde 43- (1) In the case of committing the same crime more than once against a

person at different times within the scope of the execution of a decision to commit a crime, one penalty shall be imposed. However, this penalty shall be increased from one-fourth to three-fourths. The basic form of a crime and its qualified forms requiring heavier or lighter penalties are considered the same crime. (Additional sentence: 29/6/2005 – 5377/6 art.) The provision of this paragraph shall also apply to crimes without a specific victim.

(2) The provision of the first paragraph shall also apply in the case of committing the same crime against more than one person with a single act.

(3) The provisions of this article shall not apply to the crimes of intentional killing, intentional injury, torture and plunder.4

4 The phrase "sexual assault, sexual abuse of children" in this paragraph was removed from the article text by Article 6 of Law No. 5377 dated 29/6/2005.

Ideal concurrence

## Madde 44- (1) A person who causes the formation of more than one different crime

with one act they commit shall be punished for the crime requiring the heaviest penalty among them.

## Article 52- (1) A judicial fine consists of the payment by the convict to the State Treasury of

FIRST CHAPTER

Genocide and Crimes Against Humanity

Genocide

## Article 76

(1) The commission of one of the following acts against members of a national, ethnic, racial or religious group, with the intent to destroy, in whole or in part, that group in execution of a plan, constitutes the crime of genocide:

- a) Intentional killing.
- b) Causing serious bodily or mental harm to persons.
- c) Forcing to live under conditions that would result in the destruction of the group, in whole or in part.
- d) Taking measures aimed at preventing births within the group.
- e) Forcibly transferring children of the group to another group.
  - (2) The perpetrator of the crime of genocide shall be sentenced to aggravated life imprisonment. However, for the crimes of intentional killing and intentional injury committed within the scope of genocide, the provisions of actual concurrence shall be applied for the number of victims determined.
  - (3) Legal entities shall also be subject to security measures for these crimes.
  - (4) There shall be no statute of limitations for these crimes.

Crimes against humanity

## Article 77

(1) The systematic commission of the following acts in line with a plan against a segment of society with political, philosophical, racial or religious motives constitutes a crime against humanity:

- a) Intentional killing.
- b) Intentional injury.
- c) Torture, torment or enslavement.
- d) Deprivation of personal liberty.
- e) Subjection to scientific experiments.
- f) Sexual assault, sexual abuse of children.
- g) Forced impregnation.
- h) Forcing into prostitution.
  - (2) In case of commission of the act in subparagraph (a) of the first paragraph, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to aggravated life imprisonment; in case of commission of acts defined in other subparagraphs, imprisonment of not less than eight years shall be imposed. However, for the crimes of intentional killing and intentional injury committed within the scope of subparagraphs (a) and (b) of the first paragraph, the provisions of actual concurrence shall be applied for the number of victims determined.
  - (3) Legal entities shall also be subject to security measures for these crimes.
  - (4) There shall be no statute of limitations for these crimes.

Organization

## Article 78- (1) A person who establishes or directs an organization for the purpose of committing

the crimes specified in the above articles shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to fifteen years. Those who become members of these organizations shall be sentenced to imprisonment from five to ten years.

(2) Legal entities shall also be subject to security measures for these crimes.

(3) There shall be no statute of limitations for these crimes.

SECOND PART

Crimes Against the Environment

Intentional pollution of the environment

## Article 181- (1) A person who intentionally releases waste or residues into the soil, water or air in violation of the technical procedures determined by the relevant laws and in a manner that will harm the environment shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years.

(2) A person who brings waste or residues into the country without authorization shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to three years.

(3) If the waste or residues exhibit persistent characteristics in the soil, water or air, the penalty to be imposed according to the above paragraphs shall be increased by two times.

(4) If the acts defined in the first and second paragraphs are committed with regard to waste or residues having characteristics that may cause the emergence of diseases that are difficult to treat in humans or animals, impairment of reproductive capacity, or alteration of the natural characteristics of animals or plants, imprisonment of not less than five years and judicial fine up to one thousand days shall be imposed.

(5) Legal entities shall be subject to security measures specific to them for the acts in the second, third and fourth paragraphs of this article.

## Article 182- (1) A person who negligently causes waste or residues to be released into the soil, water or air in a manner that will harm the environment shall be punished with a judicial fine. (2) A person who negligently causes the release into the soil, water or air of waste or residues having characteristics that may cause the emergence of diseases that are difficult to treat in humans or animals, impairment of reproductive capacity, or alteration of the natural characteristics of animals or plants shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to five years. ## Madde 247- (1) A public official who embezzles property which has been transferred to his/her possession by

and detention houses from their correspondence, visits with their visitors, participation in education and sports, vocational training and workshop work and other social and cultural activities within the framework of rehabilitation and education programs, being examined and treated by the institution physician, appointing a defense counsel or lawyer, meeting with them, going to courts or Chief Public Prosecutor's Offices, meeting with institution officials, released persons leaving the institution, those who incite convicts and arrestees to these acts, those who give instructions in this direction, those who prevent any other kind of meeting and contact opportunity that legislation grants to convicts and arrestees, shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to three years.

(2) A penalty of imprisonment from two years to four years shall be imposed on those who prevent the nutrition of convicts and arrestees. Inciting or persuading convicts and arrestees to go on hunger strike or death fast or giving them instructions in this direction shall also be deemed as prevention of nutrition.

(3) If one of the aggravated forms of the offense of intentional injury due to its consequence or death has occurred due to the prevention of nutrition, a penalty shall also be imposed according to the provisions relating to intentional injury or intentional killing offenses.

## Article 300- (1) A person who publicly degrades the Turkish Flag by tearing, burning or otherwise

FOURTH CHAPTER

Offenses Against the Security of the State

Disrupting the unity of the State and territorial integrity

## Article 302

(1) (Amended: 29/6/2005 – 5377/36 art.) Any person who commits an act aimed at placing all or part of the State territory under the sovereignty of a foreign state, or weakening the independence of the State, or disrupting its unity, or separating part of the territory under the sovereignty of the State from State administration, shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment.

(2) In the event that other offences are committed during the commission of this offence, a separate penalty shall be imposed for those offences in accordance with the relevant provisions.

(3) Security measures specific to legal entities shall be imposed on legal entities in respect of the commission of the offences defined in this article.

Cooperation with the enemy

## Article 303- (1) A citizen who accepts service in the army of a state at war with the Republic of Turkey State, or engages in armed struggle against the Republic of Turkey State on the side of the enemy state, shall be punished with life imprisonment.

(2) A citizen who assumes any command duty in the army of an enemy state shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment.

(3) In the event that other offences are committed during the commission of the offences defined in the first and second paragraphs, a separate penalty shall be imposed for those offences in accordance with the relevant provisions.

(4) No penalty shall be imposed on a citizen who is present in enemy state territory during wartime and is compelled to be taken into service in that state's army, for this reason. Incitement to war against the State

## Article 304- (1) Any person who incites foreign state officials to wage war against the Republic of Turkey State or to engage in hostile acts, or who cooperates with foreign state officials for this purpose, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to twenty years. (Repealed second sentence: 29/6/2005 – 5377/37 art.)

(2) In the application of this article, direct or indirect support of organizations formed to commit crimes against the security of the Republic of Turkey State shall be deemed as a hostile act.

(3) Security measures specific to legal entities shall be imposed on legal entities in respect of the commission of the offence defined in this article.

Obtaining benefit for engaging in activities against fundamental national interests106

## Article 305

(1) (Amended paragraph: 29/6/2005 – 5377/38 art.) A citizen or foreigner in Turkey who obtains material benefit, directly or indirectly, for himself or another from foreign persons or organizations, for the purpose of or by reason of committing acts against fundamental national interests, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three to ten years and a judicial fine of up to ten thousand days. The same penalty shall be imposed on the person who provides or promises the benefit.

(2) (Amended paragraph: 29/6/2005 – 5377/38 art.) If the act is committed during war, the penalty to be imposed shall be increased by half.

(3) If the offence is committed outside a state of war, prosecution for this reason shall be subject to the authorization of the Minister of Justice.

(4) The term fundamental national interests means independence, territorial integrity, national security and the fundamental characteristics of the Republic specified in the Constitution.

Recruiting soldiers against a foreign state

## Article 306

(1) Any person who, without authorization, recruits soldiers against a foreign state or engages in other hostile acts in a manner that would place the Turkish State in danger of war shall be sentenced to imprisonment from five to twelve years.

(2) If war occurs as a result of the act, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to life imprisonment.

(3) If the act is of a nature that would only damage political relations with a foreign state or place the Turkish State or Turkish citizens in danger of reprisal, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to imprisonment from two to eight years.

(4) If political relations are severed or reprisal occurs, a sentence of imprisonment from three to ten years shall be imposed.

(5) Prosecution of the offence set forth in this article shall be subject to the authorization of the Minister of Justice.

(6) The provisions of this article shall not apply to resistance movements for the purpose of legitimate defence against foreign state forces occupying all or part of the country's territory in an actual state of war.

Destruction of military facilities and agreement for the benefit of enemy military operations

## Article 307- (1) Any person who partially or completely destroys, or renders unusable even temporarily, land, sea and air transportation vehicles, roads, installations, depots and other military facilities belonging to or in the service of the State's armed forces, even if they are not yet completed, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six to twelve years.

(2) If the offence;

a) Has been committed for the benefit of a state at war with Turkey, 106 The title of this article was "Acts against fundamental national interests" and was changed to "Obtaining benefit for engaging in activities against fundamental national interests" by article 38 of Law No. 5377 dated 29/6/2005 and incorporated into the text.

b) Has endangered the State's war preparations or war power and capability or military operations, aggravated life imprisonment shall be imposed.

(3) If the destruction or rendering unusable has occurred as a result of the negligence of the person who holds or is obliged to protect and supervise the building, facility or equipment specified in the first paragraph, or if the commission of the offence has been facilitated for this reason, that person shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one to five years.

(4) Any person who, during wartime, reaches an agreement with a foreigner for the purpose of facilitating enemy military operations or harming the military operations of the Turkish State, to the detriment of the Turkish State, or who commits acts aimed at producing the same results even without an agreement, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

(5) If, as a result of the act defined in the fourth paragraph, enemy military operations are actually facilitated or the military operations of the Turkish State are harmed, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to aggravated life imprisonment.

(6) The same penalty shall be imposed on the foreigner who reaches an agreement with the person who commits the offences specified in the fourth and fifth paragraphs.

(7) The provisions of this article shall also apply if the acts specified in the above paragraphs are committed in Turkey to the detriment of a state with which there is an alliance or participation for war with the Turkish State.

Material and financial assistance to an enemy state

## Article 308- (1) A citizen who provides, reciprocally or gratuitously, directly or indirectly, any goods that can be used against the Republic of Turkey State in war to a state with which the Republic of Turkey State is at war shall be punished with imprisonment from five to fifteen years. This provision shall also apply to a foreigner residing in Turkey.

(2) The same penalty shall be imposed on a citizen or foreigner residing in Turkey who, during wartime, participates in borrowings made for the benefit of an enemy state or in payments for whatever reason, or facilitates transactions related thereto.

(3) A citizen or foreigner residing in Turkey who, even if commenced before the war, outside the cases specified in the first paragraph, engages in trade, directly or indirectly, with citizens of the enemy state or other persons residing in enemy state territory, wherever they may be, to the detriment of the Turkish State or in a manner that would have a positive effect on the war power of the enemy state, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from two to five years and a judicial fine of up to ten thousand days.

(4) The provisions of this article shall also apply if the acts specified in the above paragraphs are committed for the benefit of a state with which there is an alliance or participation for war with the enemy state.

PART FIVE

Offences Against the Constitutional Order and the Functioning of This Order

Violation of the Constitution

## Article 309- (1) Those who attempt to abolish the order envisaged by the Constitution of the Republic of Turkey

or to establish another order in place of this order or to prevent the actual implementation of this order by using force and violence shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment.

(2) If other crimes are committed during the commission of this crime, a sentence shall also be imposed for these crimes according to the relevant provisions.

(3) Security measures specific to legal entities shall be imposed in respect of crimes defined in this article.

Assassination and physical assault on the President

## Article 310- (1) A person who commits an assassination attempt on the President shall be punished with aggravated life

imprisonment. In the event of an attempt to commit this act, a sentence shall be imposed as if the crime had been completed.

(2) For a person who commits other physical assaults against the President, the penalty for the relevant crime shall be increased by half. However, the penalty to be imposed in this manner cannot be less than five years.

Crime against the legislative body

## Article 311- (1) Those who attempt to abolish the Grand National Assembly of Turkey by using force and violence

or to prevent the Grand National Assembly of Turkey from performing its duties partially or completely shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment.

(2) If other crimes are committed during the commission of this crime, a sentence shall also be imposed for these crimes according to the relevant provisions.

Crime against the Government

## Article 312- (1) A person who attempts to abolish the Government of the Republic of Turkey by using force and violence

or to prevent it from performing its duties partially or completely shall be sentenced to aggravated life imprisonment.

(2) If other crimes are committed during the commission of this crime, a sentence shall also be imposed for these crimes according to the relevant provisions.

Armed rebellion against the Government of the Republic of Turkey

## Article 313- (1) A person who incites the people to an armed rebellion against the Government

of the Republic of Turkey shall be sentenced to imprisonment from fifteen to twenty years. If the rebellion materializes, the person who incited it shall be sentenced to imprisonment from twenty to twenty-five years.

(2) A person who directs an armed rebellion against the Government of the Republic of Turkey shall be punished with aggravated life imprisonment. Other persons participating in the rebellion shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six to ten years.

(3) If the crimes defined in the first and second paragraphs are committed by taking advantage of the facilitation provided by the State being in a state of war, aggravated life imprisonment shall be imposed.

(4) If other crimes are committed during the commission of the crimes defined in the first and second paragraphs, a sentence shall also be imposed for these crimes according to the relevant provisions. Armed organization

## Article 314- (1) A person who establishes or directs an armed organization for the purpose of committing the crimes

contained in the fourth and fifth sections of this part shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

(2) Those who are members of the organization defined in the first paragraph shall be sentenced to imprisonment from five to ten years.

(3) Other provisions regarding the crime of establishing an organization for the purpose of committing crimes shall be applied as they are in terms of this crime.

Providing weapons

## Article 315- (1) A person who, knowing the purposes of these organizations, provides,

transports or stores weapons for these organizations by producing, purchasing or bringing them into the country for the purpose of being used in the activities of the organizations defined in the above article shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

Conspiracy to commit a crime

## Article 316- (1) If two or more persons agree in a manner determined by material facts to commit any

of the crimes contained in the fourth and fifth sections of this part with suitable means, imprisonment from three to twelve years shall be imposed according to the severity of the crimes.

(2) Those who withdraw from this conspiracy before the intended crime is committed or before an investigation is initiated due to the agreement shall not be punished.

Crimes Against National Defense

Usurpation of military commands

## Article 317- (1) Those who assume command of a military unit or navy or warship

or war air fleet or a fortress or fortified position or a military base or facility, a port or city while they are not legally authorized or appointed by the State shall be sentenced to life imprisonment.

(2) The same penalty shall be imposed on those who are legally authorized or assigned by the State as commanders of the above-mentioned places and who do not comply with the orders given by the competent authorities to abandon the command.

Dissuading the people from military service

## Article 318

(1) (Amended: 11/4/2013-6459/13 art.) Those who encourage or suggest in a manner that will cause those performing military service to desert or cause those who will join military service to give up performing this service shall be sentenced to imprisonment from six months to two years.

## Article 319- (1) Those who direct and incite soldiers or other persons serving under military

administration to disobedience to the laws or to break their oaths or to violate military discipline or their duties related to military service, and those who praise actions contrary to the laws, oaths or discipline or other duties in front of soldiers or say they approve of them shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one to three years.

(2) If the act is committed publicly, imprisonment from two to five years shall be imposed.

(3) If the act is committed during wartime, the penalty shall be increased by one fold.

Enrolling soldiers for foreign service, being enrolled

## Article 320- (1) A person who enrolls soldiers from among citizens or arms citizens within the country

to work in the service of a foreigner or foreign State or in their favor without the permission of the Government shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three to six years.

(2) If there are soldiers or those of military age among those enrolled or armed, the penalty shall be increased by one third.

(3) A person who accepts the service in the first paragraph shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one to three years.

Non-compliance with orders during wartime

## Article 321- (1) A person who knowingly acts contrary to the orders or decisions of the competent

authorities and bodies of the State during wartime shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one to six years.

Obligations during wartime

## Article 322- (1) A person who does not fulfill the obligations in a contract made with the State

or a public institution or an organization performing public services or providing public needs to do business or provide goods for the needs of the State's armed forces or the people during wartime, partially or completely, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three to ten years and a judicial fine of up to ten thousand days.

(2) If the partial or complete non-fulfillment of the obligations arises from negligence, up to three-fourths of the penalty may be reduced.

(3) If the partial or complete non-fulfillment of the obligation is caused by the main obligors and the intermediaries with whom they have a contract or their representatives, the same penalties shall be applied to them as well.

(4) The persons mentioned in the above paragraphs who commit fraud in the fulfillment of obligations during wartime shall be sentenced to imprisonment from ten to fifteen years and a judicial fine of up to ten thousand days.

Possession of documents related to State security

## Article 339- (1) A person who is caught with documents that serve to obtain information

that must remain secret for the security of the State or its internal or external political interests or matters that the competent authorities have prohibited from disclosure and that must remain secret by their nature, and for which no acceptable reason can be shown for their possession, or with anything of such nature, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from one to five years.

(2) If the act is committed during wartime, the perpetrator shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three to eight years.

EIGHTH CHAPTER

Crimes Against Relations with Foreign States

Crime against foreign head of state

## Article 340- (1) The penalty to be imposed on a person who commits a crime against the

head of one of the foreign states shall be increased by one-eighth. If the crime requires life imprisonment, aggravated life imprisonment shall be imposed.

(2) If the act is among the crimes for which investigation and prosecution are subject to complaint, investigation and prosecution are subject to the complaint of the foreign state.

Insult to foreign state flag

## Article 341- (1) A person who publicly insults the officially displayed flag of a foreign

state or other symbols of sovereignty shall be sentenced to imprisonment from three months to one year.

(2) Investigation and prosecution for this crime are subject to the complaint of the relevant state.

Crime against foreign state representatives

## Article 342- (1) Foreign state representatives permanently or temporarily assigned in the

Republic of Turkey and their diplomatic officers, or representatives of international organizations and their officials granted diplomatic privileges and immunities, shall be considered public officials with respect to crimes committed against them due to their duties; perpetrators shall be sentenced according to the relevant provisions of this Code.

(2) If the crime committed is insult, investigation and prosecution are subject to the complaint of the victim.

Reciprocity condition

## Article 343- (1) The application of the provisions set forth in this chapter is subject to the

condition of reciprocity.
