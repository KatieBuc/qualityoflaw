# REPUBLIC OF SAN MARINO

REPUBLIC OF SAN MARINO

We, the Captains Regent of the Most Serene Republic of San Marino

Having regard to Article 4 of Constitutional Law no. 185/2005 and Article 6 of Qualified Law no. 186/2005;

We promulgate and order the publication of the following ordinary law approved by the Grand and General Council in the session of 2 May 2016:

LAW OF 6 MAY 2016 no. 57

PROVISIONS FOR THE ADAPTATION OF THE SAN MARINO LEGAL SYSTEM TO

THE PROVISIONS OF THE COUNCIL OF EUROPE CONVENTION ON

PREVENTING AND COMBATING VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND DOMESTIC

VIOLENCE (ISTANBUL CONVENTION)

## Art.1

(Declarations of principle)

In compliance with the provisions of the Council of Europe Convention on preventing and combating violence against women and domestic violence opened for signature in Istanbul on 11 May 2011, the Republic of San Marino undertakes:

- 1. to inform the activities of its social and health care services, support, protection, prevention and safeguarding services in accordance with the general principles established by the Convention;
- 2. to implement effective policies aimed at promoting equality between women and men and the emancipation and self-determination of women;
- 3. to adopt a gender perspective in the design of implementing measures of the Convention and in assessing their impact.

## Art.2

(Supplementary definitions)

Article 2 of Law of 20 June 2008 no. 97 is replaced as follows:

"Art.2

(Notion of violence against women and gender-based violence, domestic violence)

Violence against a person shall mean any act of violence based on sex or gender that results in, or is likely to result in, physical, sexual, psychological or economic harm or suffering to the victim, including threats of such acts, coercion, arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.

The term gender refers to socially constructed roles, behaviours, activities and attributes that a given society considers appropriate for women and men.

Domestic violence shall mean any act of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence that occurs within the family or domestic unit or between current or former spouses or partners considered equivalent to a spouse, regardless of whether the perpetrator of the violence is or was cohabiting with the victim.

The term woman shall also include female minors under the age of eighteen.".

## Art.3

(Extension of protection and assistance measures to victims)

The protection and assistance measures referred to in Law of 20 June 2008 no. 97 shall be understood to extend to all victims of all forms of violence falling within the scope of the Convention.

## Art.4

(Assistance and recovery of victims)

Pursuant to Article 4 of Law no. 97/2008, in order to meet the specific needs for assistance and recovery of victims, legal advice, psychological support, financial assistance, accommodation, education, training and assistance in finding employment shall be ensured, where necessary.

In addition to the tasks provided for in Article 1 of Delegated Decree of 31 May 2012 no. 60, the Authority for Equal Opportunities is responsible for the organisation and coordination of the guaranteed assistance measures, through the conclusion of appropriate protocols with:

- the Order of Advocates and Notaries for the provision of assistance and advisory services also in the extrajudicial field and for information assistance on access to channels that may be used for individual and collective complaints;
- the competent structures of the ISS for the provision of psychological support services;
- the offices responsible for providing services and assistance for access to education, training and employment pathways;
- adequate reception facilities, for placement in suitable accommodation in emergency situations.

Legal assistance and advisory services shall be provided by experts registered in the list prepared by the Order of Advocates and Notaries pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 17 of Law of 20 June 2008 no. 97.

## Art.5

(Fund for financial assistance to victims of violence)

In the State budget, among the expenditure items pertaining to the Department of Institutional Affairs and Justice, a Fund for financial assistance to victims is established under the Authority for Equal Opportunities.

The Authority for Equal Opportunities is the body responsible for identifying the need to provide adequate financial assistance to victims on the basis of the availability of the Fund and in relation to the specificities of the case.

Victims of acts of violence referred to in the Convention, committed or attempted on the territory of the Republic, who are San Marino citizens, residents or staying in the territory of the Republic and who are in a state of economic difficulty, even temporary, may access the Fund. The Authority for Equal Opportunities, having ascertained the need referred to in the second paragraph of this article, shall ensure prompt disbursement by the Department of Institutional Affairs and Justice.

## Art.6

(Protection and support measures for minor victims of witnessed violence)

The measures referred to in Article 4, letters b), c), d), e) and f) of Law of 20 June 2008 no. 97, as well as the protection and support measures referred to in this law, shall be understood to extend to minors who are witnesses to any form of violence falling within the scope of the Convention.

## Art.7

(Confidentiality regarding the identity of reporting or denouncing persons)

The judicial and police authorities shall adopt, through Regulation of the Congress of State, adequate measures to ensure confidentiality regarding the identity of the natural person who has reported or denounced any act of violence falling within the scope of the Convention.

## Art.8

(Compensation for failure to adopt appropriate prevention and protection measures)

Victims of acts of violence contemplated by the Convention are granted the right to bring proceedings before the competent judicial authorities to obtain compensation for any damage resulting from the failure to adopt, through wilful misconduct or gross negligence, by the State authorities, prevention or protection measures, within their respective competences, in relation to the offences referred to in the Convention.

## Art.9

(Forced marriage)

After Article 176 of the Criminal Code the following article is inserted:

"Art.176 bis

(Forced marriage)

Anyone who forces a person to marry shall be punished with imprisonment of the third degree.

Anyone who intentionally lures by deception a person to the territory of a State other than that in which the person resides, for the purpose of forcing the person to marry, shall be punished with imprisonment of the second degree.".

## Art.10

(Female genital mutilation)

After Article 156 of the Criminal Code the following article is inserted:

"Art.156 bis

(Female genital mutilation)

Anyone who performs on another person, or forces another person to undergo, excision, infibulation or any other mutilation of all or part of the labia majora, labia minora or removal of the clitoris, shall be punished with imprisonment and disqualification of the third degree.

Outside cases of complicity, anyone who induces a person to undergo the acts referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be punished with imprisonment of the second degree.".

## Art.11

(Forced sterilisation)

After article 154 of the Penal Code the following article is inserted:

"Art.154 bis

(Forced sterilisation)

Whoever performs a surgical procedure that has the purpose and effect of permanently interrupting the reproductive capacity of a person without their prior informed consent or without them understanding the effects, is punished with imprisonment of the third degree. Imprisonment of the fourth degree combined with disqualification of the fourth degree applies if the perpetrator exercises a health profession.".

## Art.12

(Ill-treatment against family members and cohabitants, domestic violence)

Article 235 of the Penal Code is replaced by the following:

"Art.235

(Ill-treatment against family members and cohabitants, domestic violence)

Whoever uses ill-treatment against a person of the family or in any case cohabiting, or against a person who was or is bound by an emotional relationship, even not implying cohabitation, is punished with imprisonment of the second degree.

Imprisonment is of the fourth degree if from the act derives one of the aggravating events indicated in article 156 and of the fifth degree if death derives.".

## Art.13

(Discrimination, hatred or violence for racial, ethnic, national, religious reasons or related to sexual orientation and gender)

Article 179 bis of the Penal Code is replaced by the following:

"Art.179 bis

(Discrimination, hatred or violence for racial, ethnic, national, religious reasons or related to sexual orientation and gender identity)

Whoever disseminates in any way ideas based on racial or ethnic superiority or hatred, or incites to commit or commits acts of discrimination for racial, ethnic, national, religious reasons or related to sexual orientation and gender identity, or incites to commit or commits violence or acts of provocation to violence for racial, ethnic, national, religious reasons or related to sexual orientation and gender identity, is punished with imprisonment of the second degree.

For crimes aggravated by the circumstance of racial, ethnic, national or religious discrimination, or related to sexual orientation and gender, referred to in article 90, paragraph 1, point 1, proceedings are taken in any case ex officio.".

## Art.14

(Unacceptable justifications of crimes)

For crimes falling within the scope of application of the Convention, culture, uses and customs, religion, traditions or motive of honour do not constitute grounds for justification or mitigation of the sentence.

## Art.15

(Criteria of jurisdiction)

The citizen or resident on the territory of the Republic who commits outside the territory of the State the crimes contemplated by the Convention is subject to San Marino jurisdiction. The person who commits outside the territory of the State the crimes contemplated by the Convention against a San Marino citizen or resident on the territory of the Republic is subject to San Marino jurisdiction.

The provisions referred to in articles 154 bis, 156 bis and 176 bis, as introduced by this law, as well as the provisions referred to in articles 153, third paragraph n.1), 171, 172 ,172 bis of the Penal Code, also apply in the case where the facts do not constitute a crime in the legal system of the State in which they were committed, or also where the facts constitute a crime in the legal system of the State in which they were committed but the criminal proceeding can be initiated only following the report of the victim or an action brought by the State of the place where the crime was committed.

No proceedings are taken pursuant to the first and second paragraphs, when one of the conditions provided for by article 7 of the Penal Code occurs.

## Art.16

(Aggravating circumstances)

The following constitute further aggravating circumstances of the crimes contemplated by the provisions referred to in articles 154 bis, 156 bis and 176 bis, 235 as introduced or modified by this law, as well as the provisions referred to in articles 171 and 172 bis of the Penal Code when the perpetrator has committed the crime:

- a) to the detriment of a minor or in the presence of the latter;
- b) to the detriment of a person who is in conditions of infirmity or mental deficiency or state of pregnancy;
- c) with the use or with the threat of a weapon;
- d) or any other crime, correlated more than once during a certain period of time.

For abuse of family relations, constituting an aggravating circumstance provided for by article 90, point 2) of the Penal Code, also means abuse perpetrated by the former spouse or partner. In the presence of the above aggravating circumstances the judge may increase the sentence by one degree.

## Art.17

(Ex officio prosecution and withdrawal of complaint)

Article 178 of the Penal Code is replaced as follows:

"Art.178

(Ex officio prosecution and withdrawal of complaint)

For the prosecution of the misdemeanours provided for by articles 175 and 177 the complaint of the injured party is required.

For the crimes referred to in the preceding paragraph, the withdrawal of the complaint can no longer be made after the expiry of the term referred to in the third paragraph of article 7 of the Code of Criminal Procedure. For the crimes contemplated by the provisions referred to in articles 153, third paragraph n.1), 154 bis, 156 bis, 171, 172, 172 bis, 176, 176 bis, the withdrawal of the complaint by the injured party does not extinguish the crime and has no effect on the criminal proceeding possibly instituted.

In the case where the injured person is a minor, the statute of limitations of the crime, as well as the term for filing the complaint, begins to run from the attainment of the age of majority.

Ex officio proceedings are taken if the act is committed by the ascendant, by the guardian or by the adopter or by the person who has care or custody of the injured person.

Ex officio proceedings are also taken if the act is committed in conjunction with a misdemeanour prosecutable ex officio, or in case of repeated recidivism.".

## Art.18

(Resident status of victims of forced marriage)

The Permanent Council Commission for Foreign Affairs, Emigration and Immigration, at the request of the interested party and after verification of the requirements to obtain it, grants residence to the victims of the crime referred to in article 176 bis of the Penal Code who, taken to another State for the purpose of contracting a forced marriage, have lost their resident status due to the occurrence of the condition referred to in article 17, paragraph 5, letter a) of Law 28 June 2010 n.118 and subsequent amendments.

## Art.19

(Exemption from taxes for acts performed in the interest of the victim)

The exemption from taxes granted by article 17, seventh paragraph of Law n.97/2008 must be understood as extended to all acts performed in the interest of the victim of violence in the context of civil, criminal and administrative proceedings.

## Art.20

(Activities of the Authority for Equal Opportunities)

In order to ensure the effectiveness and efficacy of what is provided by Law 20 June 2008 n.97, by Delegated Decree 31 May 2012 n.60 and by this law and to recognise the full operational autonomy of the Authority for Equal Opportunities, within ninety days from the entry into force of this law a specific delegated decree shall be issued providing for the interventions necessary for this purpose.

## Art.21

(Transitional provisions)

In order to allow the preparation and implementation, in the necessary technical times, by the competent services and structures of the measures of prevention, protection, punishment and assistance guaranteed by this law in favour of the victims of the crimes of violence contemplated therein, the faculty regulated by the preceding article 8 must be understood as granted starting from the sixth month following the entry into force of this law.

## Art.22

(Entry into force)

This law enters into force on the fifth day following that of its legal publication.
