## Article 222-8

Version in force since 11/07/2025

Version in force since 11 July 2025

Amended by LAW No. 2025-623 of 9 July 2025 - art. 1

The offence defined in Article 222-7 is punishable by twenty years' criminal imprisonment when it is committed:

1° Against a minor under fifteen years of age;

2° Against a person whose particular vulnerability, due to their age, an illness, an infirmity, a physical or mental deficiency or a state of pregnancy, is apparent or known to the perpetrator;

2° bis Against a person whose state of psychological or physical subjection, within the meaning of Article 223-15-3, is known to the perpetrator;

3° Against a legitimate or natural ascendant or against adoptive parents;

4° Against a magistrate, a juror, a lawyer, a public or ministerial officer, a member or agent of the International Criminal Court, a member of the national gendarmerie, a civil servant of the national police, customs, the prison administration or any other person holding public authority, a firefighter or naval firefighter, a sworn caretaker of buildings or groups of buildings or an agent exercising on behalf of a lessor the functions of caretaking or surveillance of residential buildings pursuant to Article

L. 271-1 of the Internal Security Code, in the exercise or by reason of their functions, when the status of the victim is apparent or known to the perpetrator;

4° bis A Against a person exercising a private security activity mentioned in Articles

L. 611-1 or

L. 621-1 of the Internal Security Code in the exercise or by reason of their functions, when the status of the victim is apparent or known to the perpetrator;

4° bis Against a teacher or any member of staff working in educational establishments, against an agent of a public passenger transport network operator or any person entrusted with a public service mission, as well as against a health professional or a person exercising within a health establishment, a health centre, a health home, a birth centre, a private practice office of a health profession, a pharmacy, a home health care provider, a medical biology laboratory, a social or medico-social establishment or service, in the exercise or by reason of their functions, when the status of the victim is apparent or known to the perpetrator;

4° ter Against the spouse, ascendants or descendants in direct line or against any other person habitually living at the domicile of the persons mentioned in 4°, 4° bis A and 4° bis, by reason of the functions exercised by the latter;

5° Against a witness, a victim or a civil party, either to prevent them from reporting the facts, from filing a complaint or from testifying in court, or by reason of their report or their complaint, or because of their testimony before a national court or before the International Criminal Court;

5° bis and 5° ter (repealed)

5° quater Against a person who engages in prostitution, including occasionally, if the facts are committed in the exercise of that activity;

6° By the spouse or cohabitant of the victim or the partner linked to the victim by a civil solidarity pact; 6° bis Against a person in order to compel them to contract a marriage or to enter into a union or by reason of their refusal to contract that marriage or that union;

7° By a person holding public authority or entrusted with a public service mission in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of their functions or mission;

7° bis By a person exercising a private security activity mentioned in Articles L. 611-1 or L. 621-1 of the Internal Security Code in the exercise or on the occasion of the exercise of their functions or mission;

8° By several persons acting in the capacity of perpetrator or accomplice;

9° With premeditation or with ambush;

10° With use or threat of a weapon;

11° By a person acting in a manifest state of intoxication or under the manifest influence of narcotics.

The penalty incurred is increased to thirty years' criminal imprisonment when the offence defined in Article 222-7 is committed:

- a) Against a minor under fifteen years of age by a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant or by any other person having authority over the minor;
- b) Where a minor witnesses the facts and these are committed by the spouse or cohabitant of the victim or the partner linked to the victim by a civil solidarity pact or, if the victim is a minor, by a legitimate, natural or adoptive ascendant or by any other person having authority over the minor victim.

The first two paragraphs of Article 132-23 relating to the safety period are applicable to the offences provided for by this Article.
