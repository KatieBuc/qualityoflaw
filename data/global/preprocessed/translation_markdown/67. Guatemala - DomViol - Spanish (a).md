7) Shall encourage the media to develop appropriate dissemination guidelines and thus contribute to eradicating intrafamily violence in all its forms and, especially, to enhance respect for human dignity.

8) Shall stimulate research and compilation of statistics and relevant information on the causes, consequences and frequency of intrafamily violence, in order to evaluate state measures.

9) Shall promote, with international cooperation, the promotion of programs aimed at protecting the right to a life without violence and the exchange of ideas and experiences on the subject.

The State shall endeavor to offer treatment and rehabilitation alternatives to aggressors, taking into account, among others, their dual condition as victims and aggressors.
