"LAW ON DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Paragraph 1°. On domestic violence

## Article 1°.

Purpose of the law. The purpose of this law is to prevent, punish and eradicate domestic violence and to grant protection to its victims.

## Article 2º.

Duty of protection. It is the duty of the State to adopt appropriate measures to guarantee the life, personal integrity and safety of family members.

## Article 3º.

Prevention and Assistance. The State shall adopt policies aimed at preventing domestic violence, especially against women, older adults and children, and at providing assistance to victims. Law 20427

Among other measures, it shall implement the following: Art. 1 Nº1

a) Incorporate into study plans and programs D.O. 18.03.2010 content aimed at modifying behaviors that favor, stimulate or perpetuate domestic violence;

b) Develop training plans for public officials involved in the application of this law;

c) Develop public safety policies and programs to prevent and eradicate domestic violence;

d) Favor civil society initiatives to achieve the objectives of this law;

e) Adopt the necessary measures to comply with

Law 20066 the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women, the Convention on the Rights of the Child and other international instruments signed by the State of Chile, and

f) Create and maintain information systems and statistical records in relation to domestic violence.

## Article 4º.- It shall be the responsibility of the Ministry of

Women and Gender Equity to propose to the President of the Republic the public policies for the fulfillment of the objectives of this law. Law 20820

In coordination and collaboration with the relevant Art. 16 N° 1 public and private bodies, it shall annually formulate a D.O. 20.03.2015 national action plan.

For the purposes of the preceding paragraphs, the

Ministry of Women and Gender Equity shall have the following functions:

- a) Promote, coordinate and evaluate governmental policies against domestic violence;
- b) Recommend the adoption of legal, regulatory or other measures to prevent, punish and eradicate domestic violence;
- c) Promote the contribution of the media to eradicate violence against women and enhance respect for their dignity. ## Article 5º.

Domestic violence. Any abuse that affects the life or physical or psychological integrity of a person who has or has had the status of spouse of the offender or a cohabitation relationship with him; or is a relative by consanguinity or affinity in the entire direct line or in the collateral line up to and including the third degree, of the offender or of his spouse or current cohabitant, shall constitute domestic violence.

There shall also be domestic violence when the conduct referred to in the preceding paragraph occurs between the parents of a common child, or affects a minor, older adult or disabled person who is under the care or dependence of any of the members of the family group. Law 20427

Likewise, conduct carried out in the context of affective or Art. 1 Nº2 family relationships constitutes domestic violence, the direct object of which D.O. 18.03.2010 is to violate the economic autonomy of the woman, or the violation of property, or of the economic subsistence of the family or children, such as the repeated failure to provide support, carried out with the purpose of exercising control over her, or over her economic or property resources, generating dependence or generating impairment of such property or that of her sons and daughters. ### Paragraph 2º. On Domestic Violence within the jurisdiction

of the Family Courts

## Article 6º.- Acts of domestic violence

that do not constitute a crime shall be within the jurisdiction of the family courts and shall be subject to the procedure established in Law Nº19.968.

## Article 7°.

Situation of risk. When there is a situation of imminent risk for one or more persons of suffering abuse constituting domestic violence, even when it has not yet been carried out, the court, on the sole merit of the complaint, must adopt the corresponding protective or precautionary measures.

It shall be presumed that there is a situation of imminent risk as described in the preceding paragraph when intimidation to cause harm by the offender has occurred or when there are also, with respect to the latter, circumstances or background such as: drug addiction, alcoholism, one or more complaints for domestic violence, prior conviction for domestic violence, pending proceedings or prior convictions for felony or misdemeanor against persons or for any of the offenses established in paragraphs 5 and 6 of Title VII, of

Book Two of the Criminal Code or for violation of law

N°17.798, or psychiatric or psychological background that denotes characteristics of a violent personality. Likewise, it shall be presumed that there is a situation of imminent risk, when the accused violently opposes his refusal to accept the termination of an affective relationship that he has recently maintained with the victim. ## Article 8°.

Penalties. Abuse constituting domestic violence shall be punished, according to its severity, with a fine of half to fifteen monthly tax units for the benefit of the regional government of the domicile of the complainant or plaintiff, to be allocated to centers for the care of victims of domestic violence existing in the respective region and that are publicly or privately funded.

The convicted person must prove payment of the fine

Law 20066 within five days following the date of notification of the judgment, unless the judge, for justified reasons, extends said term for up to fifteen days.

In case of non-compliance, the court shall refer the background to the Public Prosecutor's Office, pursuant to the provisions of article 240 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Artículo 9º.

Accessory measures. In addition to what is provided in the preceding article, the judge must apply in the sentence one or more of the following accessory measures:

- a) Obligation for the offender to leave the home shared with the victim.
- b) Prohibition from approaching the victim or their LEY 20286 residence, workplace or place of study, as well as Art. 6º any other place they regularly attend or visit D.O. 15.09.2008 habitually. - c) Prohibition from carrying and possessing and, where applicable, the confiscation of firearms. This shall be reported, as appropriate, to the General Directorate of Mobilization, the Garrison Command or the respective Service Director, for the corresponding legal and regulatory purposes.
- d) Mandatory attendance at therapeutic programs or family counseling. The institutions that develop such programs shall report to the respective court on the treatment that the aggressor must follow, its commencement and termination.
- e) Obligation to report regularly to the police unit determined by the judge. Ley 20480

## Artículo 11.- Disbursements and patrimonial damages.

The sentence shall establish the obligation of the convicted person to pay the victim for disbursements and damages of a patrimonial nature that may have been caused by the execution of the act or acts constituting domestic violence that are the subject of the trial, including the replacement in money or in kind of damaged, destroyed or lost property. These damages shall be determined prudentially by the judge.

## Artículo 12.- Registry of sanctions and accessory

measures. The Civil Registry and Identification Service must keep a Special Registry of persons who have been convicted, by final judgment, as perpetrators of domestic violence, as well as of other resolutions that the law orders to be registered.

The court, once the sentence becomes final, must send an official communication to the Civil Registry, identifying the convicted person and the principal and accessory sanctions applied for the act of domestic violence, with the exception of that provided in subsection d) of Article 9°, circumstances that the aforementioned Service shall also note on the respective certificate of background. This Special Registry shall be brought to the attention of the court at its request, in cases regulated by law.

## Artículo 15.

Precautionary measures. At any stage Art. 5 N° 3 of the investigation or proceedings concerning crimes D.O. 18.11.2021 constituting domestic violence, and even before formal charges are filed, the court with criminal jurisdiction may order the precautionary measures necessary to protect the victim effectively and promptly, such as those established in Article 92 of Law No. 19,968 and those referred to in Article 7° of this law.

## Artículo 16.

Accessory measures. The accessory measures established in Article 9° shall be applied by courts with criminal jurisdiction, when the crime constitutes an act of domestic violence, without prejudice to the principal and accessory sanctions that correspond to the crime in question.

The court shall prudentially set the term of those measures, which may not be less than six months nor more than two years, considering the circumstances that justify them. Such measures may be extended, at the request of the victim, if the facts that justified them persist. In the case of subsection d) of Article 9°, the duration of the measure shall be set, and may be extended, taking into consideration the background information provided by the respective institution. ## Article 20.- Judicial representation of the victim.

In cases qualified by the National Women's Service, it may assume the legal sponsorship and representation of the adult female victim of crimes constituting intrafamily violence, if she so requests, for the purposes of the provisions of article 109 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

For compliance with the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the Service may enter into agreements with public or private entities.
