### Capitulo I

Objective, Definitions and Scope

#### Artículo 1. The provisions of this Law have as their objective the protection from the

various manifestations of domestic violence and abuse of children and adolescents, and of all persons connected with the situations described in article 3 of this Law, in accordance with the guiding principles of the Political Constitution, the Family Code and the international treaties and conventions to which the Republic of Panama is a signatory.

#### Artículo 2. For the purposes of this Law, the following terms are defined as follows:

1. Agresor o agresora. One who performs any action or omission described in the definition of violence, to the detriment of persons who are protected by this Law.

2. Cohabitar. To maintain a consensual relationship similar to that of spouses.

3. Maltrato. Offenses in fact and in word, serious or intolerable, that undermine the obligations of affection and respect that should govern relationships among persons.

4. Medida de protección. Written order issued by the competent authority, in which measures are dictated for an aggressor to refrain from engaging in or performing certain acts or conduct constituting domestic violence.

5. Relación de pareja. The relationship between spouses, ex-spouses, persons who cohabit or have cohabited, who maintain or have maintained an intimate consensual relationship and those who have procreated together a son or daughter.

6. Víctima sobreviviente. Person who suffers or has suffered physical, verbal, socio-emotional, sexual or patrimonial abuse.

7. Violencia. Any action, omission or negligent treatment committed by a person that harms the physical, psychological, sexual, patrimonial integrity or liberty of persons who are subjects of this Law.

8. Violencia doméstica. Pattern of conduct in which physical force or sexual or psychological violence, intimidation or persecution is employed against a person by his or her spouse, ex-spouse, family members or relatives with whom

G.O. 24350 he or she cohabits or has cohabited, lives or has lived under the same roof or maintains or has maintained a legally recognized relationship, or with whom he or she maintains a consensual relationship, or with a person with whom he or she has procreated at least one son or daughter, to cause physical harm to his or her person or to the person of another to cause emotional harm.

9. Violencia física. Use of force or coercion, by the aggressor, against the victim survivor to make the latter do something he or she does not wish or cease doing something he or she wishes, contrary to his or her rights.

10. Violencia patrimonial. Willful action or omission that involves damage, loss, transformation, removal, destruction, retention or misappropriation of objects, work instruments, personal documents, property, rights or other economic resources intended to satisfy the basic needs of persons included within article 3 of this Law. Unjustified limitation of access to and management of common property shall also be considered patrimonial violence.

11. Violencia sexual. Action that compels a person, through the use of force, intimidation, coercion, blackmail, bribery, manipulation, threat, use of substances or drugs or any other mechanism that affects his or her will, to participate in sexual interactions that do not necessarily in themselves constitute crimes against decency and sexual freedom. Likewise, it is considered sexual violence that the aggressor compels the victim to perform any of these acts with third parties or to witness them.

12. Violencia sicológica. Any action or omission that one person performs against another, intended to coerce, degrade or control the actions, behavior, beliefs, feelings or decisions of the persons to whom this Law is applicable. It manifests itself through intimidation, manipulation, direct or indirect threat, permanent surveillance, harassment, disparagement of personal worth, destruction of objects valued by the person, deprivation of access to food, humiliation, isolation or any other conduct that implies harm to psychological health, self-determination or personal development.

#### Artículo 3. The measures and precepts enshrined in this Law are applicable to:

1. Matrimonios,

2. Uniones de hecho.

3. Relaciones de pareja que no hayan cumplido los cinco años, cuya intención de permanencia pueda acreditarse.

4. Parentesco por consanguinidad, afinidad o adopción.

5. Hijos a hijas menores de edad no comunes que convivan o no dentro de la familia.

6. Personas que hayan procreado entre sí un hijo o una hija. Igualmente se aplicarán a las situaciones señaladas en los numerales anteriores, aun cuando hayan finalizado al momento de la agresión.

#### Article 4. Notwithstanding the initiation or continuation of the respective civil, criminal, family or

administrative proceeding, the authority, when it becomes aware of the fact, is immediately empowered, according to its competence, to apply, in favor of persons who are surviving victims of domestic violence, the following protection measures:

- 1. Order the provisional arrest of the male or female aggressor, for a term not exceeding twenty-four hours.
- 2. Order the alleged male or female aggressor to vacate the dwelling shared with the surviving victim, regardless of who is the owner of the residence.
- 3. Proceed with the search with the purpose of rescuing or immediately assisting the alleged surviving victim of the act of violence, in accordance with constitutional and legal guarantees.
- 4. Authorize the surviving victim, if so requested, to provisionally reside in a different domicile from the common one to protect him or her from future aggressions, respecting the confidentiality of the domicile.
- 5. Prohibit the introduction or maintenance of weapons in the common domicile, as well as seize them in order to guarantee that they are not used to intimidate, threaten or cause harm.
- 6. Prohibit the alleged male or female aggressor from approaching the common domicile or that where the surviving victim is located, in addition to the workplace, place of study or other place habitually frequented by him or her.
- 7. Return to the common domicile the assaulted person who has had to leave it, if so requested and, consequently, shall immediately apply the measure established in numeral 1 of this article.
- 8. Suspend the alleged male or female aggressor's custody and upbringing of his or her minor children, considering the seriousness of the acts of violence and/or the direct or indirect harm or danger to which the minors are subjected. The competent authority may grant as a first option the protective custody of the child or adolescent to the non-aggressor parent.
- 9. Suspend the visitation arrangement of the alleged male or female aggressor, considering the seriousness of the acts of violence and/or the direct or indirect harm or danger to which the minors are subjected.
- 10. Send official notes to the migration and boarding authorities, ordering the prohibition of departure from the country of the minor children of the parties.
- 11. Conduct an inventory of movable property of the household unit, to secure the common patrimony.
- 12. Grant for exclusive use to the assaulted person, the movable property necessary for the adequate functioning of the family unit.

#### Article 215

A. The person who physically, sexually, patrimonially or psychologically assaults another or harasses him or her, shall be punished with imprisonment of 1 to 3 years or with a curative security measure, consisting of a multidisciplinary therapeutic treatment program with specialized care, approved by the trial court.

Psychological assault must be proven by the forensic psychiatrist or by a forensic psychologist.

For the purposes of this Chapter, the norms contemplated in the types described are applicable to:

- 1. Marriages.
- 2. Common-law unions.
- 3. Couple relationships that have not completed five years, whose intention of permanence can be proven.
- 4. Relationship by blood, affinity or adoption.
- 5. Non-common minor children who live or do not live within the family.
- 6. Persons who have procreated a child between them.

Likewise, they shall apply to the situations indicated in the preceding numerals, even when they have ended at the time of the assault.

#### Artículo 215

B. If the conduct described in the previous article produces permanent weakening of a sense or an organ, or a mark visible to the naked eye and permanent on the face; or if inflicted on a pregnant woman hastens childbirth, the sanction shall be 2 to 4 years of prison. If the conduct described in the previous article produces incurable bodily or psychological damage, the loss of a sense, an organ or extremity; impotence or loss of capacity to procreate; permanent alteration of vision, disfigurement of the face or body for life, or permanent incapacity for work, the sanction shall be 3 to 5 years of prison.

#### Artículo 215

C. In cases of first-time female or male aggressors, the trial judge may sanction with a curative security measure, consisting of a multidisciplinary therapeutic treatment program, in accordance with article 115 of the Penal Code, duly supervised by the Department of Correction of the Ministry of Government and Justice, or with community service supervised by the competent authority within the corregimiento where they reside.

In case of non-compliance with the curative security measure or supervised community service, the judge must substitute it with the corresponding prison sentence.

#### Artículo 215

D. The person who mistreats a child or adolescent under

18 years of age, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of 2 to 6 years or with curative security measure or both.

The following conducts typify the mistreatment of minors:

- 1. Causing, allowing or making them be caused physical, mental or emotional harm, including physical injuries caused by corporal punishment.
- 2. Using them or inducing them to be used, for profit, in begging, pornography or in advertising or publicity not appropriate for their age.
- 3. Employing them in prohibited or immoral work, or that endangers their life or health.
- 4. Imposing negligent treatment and mistreatment that may affect their physical and mental health.

#### Artículo 215

E. The male or female official or the individual who has knowledge of the execution of any of the acts typified in this Title, and does not bring it to the knowledge of the authorities, shall be sanctioned with 50 to 150 days - fine. In case the commission of the crime is not proven, the male or female official or the individual shall be exempt from any legal responsibility by reason of the complaint.

Public Policies

#### Artículo 22. The Ministry of Youth, Women, Children and Family, shall have the

responsibility for monitoring, coordination, promotion and evaluation of progress in the application of this Law. Consequently, it shall present annual reports to the Executive Branch and to the Commission on Women's Affairs, Children's Rights, Youth and Family of the Legislative Assembly.

#### Artículo 23. The Ministry of Government and Justice, together with the Ministry of

Youth, Women, Children and Family, shall coordinate, promote, develop and supervise dissemination programs, through social communication media, aimed at preventing and eradicating violence; in addition, it shall promote and encourage programs for the dissemination of this Law. Likewise, it shall formulate and execute training programs for National Police personnel in order to guarantee their effective and timely intervention in cases of violence described in this Law.

#### Artículo 24. The Ministry of Health shall strengthen and train personnel at health

centers, regional and national hospitals, in the prevention and care of cases of violence established in this Law.

#### Artículo 25. All health centers, emergency rooms, medical or

hospital centers, clinics and offices, public or private, must attend to cases of violence regulated by this Law.

Those who work in these institutions may not deny medical or hospital care to surviving victims of violence, without prejudice to the fact that they may subsequently be referred to other centers for their continued care, provided that their transfer does not imply risks to their health or integrity.

#### Artículo 26. The Executive Branch, through the Ministry of Health and with the

advice of the Public Prosecutor's Office, shall regulate and design forms to register aggressions caused by the different types of violence indicated by this Law.

#### Artículo 27. El personal de salud y el personal administrativo que laboran en las

distintas instituciones de salud del país deberán documentar, mediante formularios distribuidos por el Ministerio de Salud, el historial médico, los hallazgos clínicos, el diagnostico y la incapacidad provisional del paciente o de la paciente que declare haber sido víctima sobreviviente de violencia doméstica o de maltrato al niño, niña o adolescente.

El formulario en mención, debidamente sellado y firmado, será enviado al Instituto de Medicina Legal, dentro de las cuarenta y ocho horas siguientes a la atención del paciente o de la paciente, para que el Instituto evalúe el informe médico allí contenido y prosiga con el trámite que corresponde para las sumarias que al efecto se realicen. En caso de niños, niñas y adolescentes, se remitirá al Juzgado de la Niñez y Adolescencia que corresponda.

El certificado expedido por el médico idóneo que atienda a la víctima sobreviviente deberá indicar la incapacidad síquica y física total que le corresponda.

#### Artículo 28. El Ministerio de Educación deberá incorporar contenidos orientados a

promover valores basados en los principios de la tolerancia, del respeto por las diferencias y la diversidad, así como de igualdad y equidad de género en los planes y programas de estudio de todos los niveles. Además, fomentará programas dirigidos a la resolución pacífica de conflictos, a fin de prevenir la violencia doméstica y el maltrato al niño, niña y adolescente.

#### Artículo 29. El Ministerio de Gobierno y Justicia y los municipios de la República

capacitarán a los jueces nocturnos y a los corregidores en lo concerniente a la correcta aplicación de esta Ley, con el objeto de que cada uno de éstos funcionarios, durante el ejercicio de su cargo, tengan la debida y necesaria sensibilidad, así como el conocimiento preciso para el tratamiento de los temas de violencia doméstica y maltrato al niño, niña y adolescente.

#### Artículo 30. En todos los casos, la autoridad llevará un registro de los hechos de

violencia doméstica y de maltrato al niño, niña y adolescente, en el que se consignarán las generales de los involucrados y la descripción de los hechos ocurridos, que se enviará a la Comisión Nacional de Estadística Criminal (CONADEC) del Ministerio de Gobierno y Justicia, con el objetivo de crear un banco de datos de las personas involucradas en los hechos regulados por esta Ley.

La víctima sobreviviente tendrá derecho a que se le entregue, sin costo alguno, copia autenticada del mencionado registro.

#### Artículo 31. La sociedad civil intervendrá de forma activa en la divulgación,

capacitación, coordinación y ejecución de esta Ley, junto con los diversos estamentos del Estado encargados de desarrollar las políticas públicas sobre esta materia.

#### Artículo 32. Las entidades privadas o los profesionales independientes, así como las

organizaciones no gubernamentales que brinden atención a víctimas sobrevivientes de violencia o a niños, niñas y adolescentes maltratados que califiquen como sujetos de patrocinio procesal gratuito, podrán deducir de sus declaraciones de renta el costo de esta atención.

A tal efecto, la cuenta correspondiente deberá estar debidamente documentada y aprobada por el juez de la causa.

Prestarán mérito ejecutivo, en el caso del sector privado, las cuentas presentadas como costos de atención a estas víctimas sobrevivientes.
