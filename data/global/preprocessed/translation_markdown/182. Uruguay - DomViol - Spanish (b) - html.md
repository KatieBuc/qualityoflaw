GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

## CHAPTER I - GENERAL PROVISIONS

(Purpose and scope).- The purpose of this law is to guarantee the effective enjoyment of women's right to a life free from gender-based violence. It includes women of all ages, trans women, of diverse sexual orientations, socioeconomic status, territorial belonging, belief, cultural and ethnic-racial origin or disability status, without any distinction or discrimination. Comprehensive mechanisms, measures and policies for prevention, care, protection, sanction and reparation are established.

(Declaration of public order and general interest).- The provisions of this law are of public order and general interest. The eradication of violence against women, girls, boys and adolescents is declared a priority, and the State must act with due diligence to that end.

(Interpretation and integration).- For the interpretation and integration of this law, the values, purposes, general principles of law and the provisions of the Constitution of the Republic and international Human Rights instruments shall be taken into account, in particular the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence Against Women (Convention of Belem Do Para), the International Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW), the International Convention on the Rights of the Child (CRC), the International Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD) and the Inter-American Convention on the Protection of the Rights of Older Persons. In case of conflict or doubt about the application of the provisions contained in this law, the interpretation most favorable to women in situations of gender-based violence shall prevail.

(Definition of gender-based violence against women).- Gender-based violence is a form of discrimination that affects, directly or indirectly, the life, liberty, dignity, physical, psychological, sexual, economic or patrimonial integrity, as well as the personal security of women. Gender-based violence against women is understood as any conduct, action or omission, in the public or private sphere that, based on an unequal power relationship based on gender, has as its object or result to undermine or nullify the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights or fundamental freedoms of women. This includes conduct perpetrated by the State or its agents, as well as by private institutions or individuals.

(Guiding principles and guidelines).- The following are guiding principles and guidelines for the application of this law:

- A) Priority of human rights. Actions against gender-based violence against women must prioritize the human rights of victims.
- B) State responsibility. The State is responsible for preventing, investigating and punishing gender-based violence against women, as well as protecting, caring for and providing reparation to victims in case of failure of service.
- C) Equality and non-discrimination. Any form of distinction, exclusion or restriction based on birth, nationality, ethnic-racial origin, sex, age, sexual orientation or gender identity, marital status, religion, economic, social, cultural condition, disability status, place of residence or other factors that have as their object or result, to undermine or nullify the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms of women is prohibited.
- D) Gender equality. The State, through its various forms of action, must promote the elimination of relations of domination based on sociocultural stereotypes of inferiority or subordination of women. The actions of private institutions, the community and individuals in particular must be oriented in the same direction.
- E) Comprehensiveness. Policies against violence against women must address its different dimensions, manifestations and consequences. To this end, State organs and agencies must articulate and coordinate budgetary and institutional resources.
- F) Autonomy of women. Actions against violence against women, and in particular care and reparation services, must respect and promote their own decisions and projects, overcoming tutelary and welfare interventions. In the case of girls and adolescents, their progressive autonomy must be respected according to age and maturity.
- G) Best interests of girls and adolescents. In all measures concerning girls and adolescents, their best interests must prevail, which consists of the recognition and respect of the rights inherent to their status as human persons.
- H) Quality. Actions for compliance with this law should tend to be inter and multidisciplinary, be in charge of operators specialized in the subject and have material resources to provide quality services.
- I) Citizen participation. Plans and actions against gender-based violence against women shall be developed, implemented and evaluated with the active participation of women and representative social organizations from throughout the country with impact on the subject.
- J) Transparency and accountability. The State must inform and justify to citizens the policies, actions and public services it implements to guarantee women a life free from violence.
- K) Promptness and effectiveness. The provisions of this law must be fulfilled in an effective and timely manner.

(Forms of violence).- The following are manifestations of gender-based violence, not mutually exclusive nor of others that may not be explicitly stated:

- A) Physical violence. Any action, omission or pattern of conduct that damages the bodily integrity of a woman.
- B) Psychological or emotional violence. Any action, omission or pattern of conduct aimed at disturbing, degrading or controlling the conduct, behavior, beliefs or decisions of a woman, through humiliation, intimidation, isolation or any other means that affects her psychological or emotional stability.
- C) Sexual violence. Any action that implies the violation of a woman's right to decide voluntarily about her sexual or reproductive life, through threats, coercion, use of force or intimidation, including rape within marriage and other bonding or kinship relationships, whether or not there is cohabitation, the intentional transmission of sexually transmitted infections (STIs), as well as forced prostitution and sexual trafficking.

Also sexual violence is the involvement of girls, boys and adolescents in sexual activities with an adult or with any other person who is in a position of advantage over them, whether because of their age, because of their greater physical or mental development, because of the kinship, affective or trust relationship that unites them to the boy or girl, because of their position of authority or power. Forms of sexual violence include, among others, sexual abuse, sexual exploitation and use in pornography.

D) Violence based on prejudice towards sexual orientation, gender identity or gender expression. This is violence that aims to repress and punish those who do not comply with traditional gender norms, whether because of their sexual orientation, gender identity or gender expression.

E) Economic violence. Any conduct aimed at limiting, controlling or preventing a woman's economic income, including the contumacious non-payment of maintenance obligations, with the purpose of undermining her autonomy.

F) Patrimonial violence. Any conduct aimed at affecting the free disposal of a woman's patrimony, through the subtraction, destruction, distraction, damage, loss, limitation or retention of objects, personal documents, work instruments, goods, values and patrimonial rights.

G) Symbolic violence. That exercised through messages, values, symbols, icons, images, signs and social, economic, political, cultural and religious belief impositions that transmit, reproduce and consolidate relations of domination, exclusion, inequality and discrimination, which contribute to naturalizing the subordination of women.

H) Obstetric violence. Any action, omission and pattern of conduct by health personnel in a woman's reproductive processes, that affects her autonomy to decide freely about her body or abuse of invasive techniques and procedures.

I) Labor violence. That exercised in the labor context, through acts that obstruct a woman's access to work, promotion or stability in it, such as moral harassment, sexual harassment, the requirement of requirements regarding marital status, age, physical appearance, the request for results of clinical laboratory tests, outside what is established in the applicable legal frameworks, or the reduction of the salary corresponding to the task performed due to being a woman.

J) Violence in the educational sphere. Violence exercised against a woman because of her status as such in an educational relationship, with abuse of power, including sexual harassment, that damages the self-esteem, health, integrity, freedom and security of the victim and undermines equality.

K) Street sexual harassment. Any act of a sexual nature or connotation exercised in public spaces by a person against a woman without her consent, generating discomfort, intimidation, hostility, degradation and humiliation.

L) Political violence. Any act of pressure, persecution, harassment or any type of aggression against a woman or her family, in her capacity as a candidate, elected or in the exercise of political representation, to prevent or restrict the free exercise of her position or induce her to make decisions against her will.

M) Media violence. Any publication or dissemination of messages and images through any mass media, that directly or indirectly promotes the exploitation of women or their images, injures, defames, discriminates, dishonors, humiliates or undermines the dignity of women, legitimizes unequal treatment or constructs sociocultural patterns that reproduce inequality or generate violence against women.

N) Femicidal violence. The action of extreme violence that violates the fundamental right to life and causes the death of a woman because she is one, or that of her daughters, sons or other persons in her care, with the purpose of causing her suffering or harm.

O) Domestic violence. Domestic violence constitutes any action or omission, direct or indirect, that undermines by illegitimately limiting the free exercise or enjoyment of a woman's human rights, caused by a person with whom she has or has had a kinship, marriage, dating, affective or common-law relationship.

P) Community violence. Any action or omission that, based on individual or collective acts in the community, transgresses the fundamental rights of one or more women and promotes their denigration, discrimination, marginalization or exclusion.

Q) Institutional violence. Any action or omission by any authority, official or personnel of the public sphere or private institutions, that discriminates against women or has the purpose of undermining, obstructing or impeding the enjoyment and exercise of their fundamental rights and freedoms, as well as that which obstructs women's access to policies and services intended to prevent, address, investigate, punish and eradicate the manifestations, types and modalities of violence against women provided for in this law.

R) Ethnic Racial Violence. This type of violence constitutes any physical, moral, verbal or psychological aggression, humiliating or offensive treatment, exercised against a woman by virtue of her ethnic belonging or in allusion to it; causing in the victim feelings of intimidation, shame, contempt, denigration. Whether this type of violence is exercised in public, in private, or regardless of the sphere in which it occurs.

(Rights of women victims of violence).- In addition to the rights recognized to all persons in applicable national and international legislation, every woman victim of any form of gender-based violence has the right:

- A) To respect for her dignity, privacy, autonomy as well as not to be subjected to any form of discrimination.
- B) To be respected in her sexual orientation and gender identity.
- C) To receive clear, accessible, complete, truthful, timely information, appropriate to age and sociocultural context, in relation to her rights and the mechanisms and procedures contemplated in this law and other applicable rules.
- D) To have an interpreter, language adaptation and augmentative communication as well as other necessary supports and reasonable adjustments that allow guaranteeing her rights, when she is in a situation of disability.
- E) To guarantee the confidentiality and privacy of her personal data, those of her descendants or those of any other person who is under her custody or care.
- F) To receive timely comprehensive protection and care for herself, her sons and daughters or other persons in her care, through adequate and effective services.
- G) To receive guidance, legal advice and free legal representation, depending on the socioeconomic position of the woman.

Such assistance must be immediate, specialized and comprehensive, and must include the various matters and processes required by her situation.

H) To receive specialized and comprehensive medical, psychological and psychiatric assistance for herself and her sons and daughters.

I) To respect and protection of her sexual and reproductive rights, including to exercise all rights recognized by the laws on Sexual and Reproductive Health (Law No. 18,426, of December 1, 2008) and Voluntary Termination of Pregnancy (Law No. 18,987, of October 22, 2012), whatever her nationality and even if she has not reached one year of residence in the country, provided that the acts of violence have occurred in the national territory, which constitutes an exception to Article 13 of Law No. 18,987, of October 22, 2012. (Rights of women victims of violence in administrative or judicial proceedings).- In administrative or judicial proceedings, the following rights must be guaranteed:

- A) To have efficient and accessible mechanisms to report.
- B) To communicate freely and privately with her sponsoring attorney, before, during or after the acts of the judicial or administrative process.
- C) To be heard by the judge or administrative authority, as appropriate, and obtain a timely and effective response. Her opinion must be contemplated in the decision that affects her, especially considering the context of violence and intimidation in which she may find herself.
- D) To receive immediate and preventive judicial protection, when her rights are threatened or violated.
- E) To free administrative and judicial proceedings as appropriate.
- F) To participate in proceedings related to the situation of violence that affects her, as appropriate.
- G) To attend with a companion of her trust to all judicial instances.
- H) That her testimony not be devalued based on gender stereotypes based on the inferiority or submission of women, or other factors of discrimination such as age, disability status, sexual orientation or gender identity, ethnic-racial origin, territorial belonging, beliefs or cultural identity.
- I) To receive humane treatment, taking into account her age, disability status or other conditions or circumstances that require special attention. Actions that have the purpose or result of causing suffering to direct or indirect victims of acts of violence are prohibited.
- J) To non-confrontation, including her family nucleus with the aggressor, prohibiting any form of mediation or conciliation in protection or criminal proceedings.
- K) That her informed consent be obtained prior to conducting physical examinations or other actions that affect her privacy or intimacy. In cases of sexual violence, it is her right to choose the sex of the professional or technician for such practices, who must be specialized and trained with a gender perspective.
- L) To truth, justice and reparation through a simple and quick remedy before the competent Courts. (*) (*)Notes:

(Rights of girls, boys and adolescents in administrative and judicial proceedings).- Girls, boys and adolescents, whether victims or witnesses of acts of violence, are recognized, without prejudice to the rights established by applicable rules, the right to:

- A) Be informed by their defense about their rights, the status and scope of administrative actions, deadlines and judicial resolutions in the case, in a form accessible to their age, taking into account their maturity and degree of autonomy.
- B) That their account of the reported facts be obtained by specialized technical personnel, in places suitable for that purpose and avoiding its repetition.
- C) To the maximum possible restriction of attendance at judicial or police headquarters, as well as to be questioned directly by the court or by police personnel.
- D) Be protected in their physical and emotional integrity, as well as their family and witnesses, against possible reprisals, ensuring that they do not coincide in common places with the persons reported in judicial and police spaces.
- E) In hearings, the person reported as an aggressor may not be present and the defense may not ask questions to the girl, boy or adolescent except with prior authorization from the Court and only through specialized technical personnel.
- F) Respect for the privacy of the victim and complainant family members with respect to third parties, keeping their identity and image confidential and the adoption of necessary measures to prevent their use by the media.
- G) Receive prior information accessible to their age and maturity. For the performance of examinations or other actions that affect their privacy, they may be accompanied by the trusted adult that they themselves choose. (*) (*)Notes:

## CHAPTER II - INTER-INSTITUTIONAL SYSTEM FOR RESPONSE TO GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

(Inter-institutional system).- The response system to gender-based violence against women must be comprehensive, inter-institutional and interdisciplinary, and include at a minimum: prevention actions, care services, mechanisms that guarantee effective and timely access to justice, reparation measures, the registration and organization of information, training and capacity building of operators, and evaluation and accountability.

(National Women's Institute).- The National Women's Institute is the governing body for public policies for a life free of violence for women, responsible for the promotion, design, coordination, articulation, monitoring and evaluation thereof.

In particular, it must:

- A) Ensure faithful compliance with this law.
- B) Articulate and coordinate actions with the different state areas involved, at the national, departmental and municipal levels and with university, union, business, religious sectors, organizations for the defense of women's rights, children's and adolescents' rights and others of civil society with competence in the matter.
- C) Provide mechanisms and processes to mainstream the issue in sectoral policies of the Executive Branch as well as its articulation with the Judicial Branch, the Legislative Branch and Departmental Governments.
- D) Prepare, within the framework of the National Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women, the National Plan, as well as other specific plans, programs and actions for the implementation of this law.
- E) Generate minimum standards for detection and approach to situations of violence, to ensure that actions are aimed at strengthening the autonomy of women and take into account diversity according to age, sexual orientation, gender identity, ethnic-racial origin, territorial affiliation, disability situation, beliefs, among others. For such purposes, it shall agree with state bodies or agencies on guidelines for the inclusion of gender perspective in different areas.
- F) Develop technical assistance programs for the different bodies, agencies or institutions involved, aimed at prevention, early detection, care, protection, inter-institutional articulation and the preparation of protocols for the different levels of intervention that are adapted to the diversity characteristics referred to in the previous paragraph.
- G) Provide permanent training, education and instruction on the subject to personnel of public, state, departmental and municipal bodies and agencies. Such training shall be provided in a comprehensive and specific manner according to each area, in accordance with the contents of this law.
- H) Promote training on the matter in different universities and professional associations.
- I) Promote and coordinate specialized training for legislators on violence against women.
- J) Generate quantitative and qualitative data records on gender-based violence, which include variables such as age, disability situation, ethnic-racial origin, religion, territoriality, among other dimensions of discrimination.

Measures must be adapted to guarantee the confidentiality of personal data so that the person to whom they refer is not identifiable (Law No. 18,331, of August 11, 2008).

K) Coordinate with other registries the criteria for data collection and selection on gender-based violence.

L) Formulate recommendations to public and private entities with competence in the subject matter, to guarantee women a life free of gender-based violence.

M) Evaluate compliance with public policies to guarantee women a life free of violence and report on actions and results in a public and transparent manner. (National Advisory Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women).- The National Advisory Council for the Fight against Domestic Violence, created by Law No. 17,514, of July 2, 2002, is replaced by the National Advisory Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women, with national competence and which shall have the following purposes:

- A) Advise the Executive Branch on matters within its competence.
- B) Ensure compliance with this law and its regulations.
- C) Design and submit for consideration by the Executive Branch the National Plan against Gender-Based Violence against Women as well as other specific plans, programs and actions for the implementation of this law.
- D) Supervise and monitor compliance with the National Plan against Gender-Based Violence against Women.
- E) Articulate the implementation of sectoral policies to fight gender-based violence against women.
- F) Create, support and strengthen Departmental and Municipal Commissions for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women, establishing the directives and guidelines for their operation and compliance with this law.
- G) Be consulted mandatorily in the preparation of reports that the State must produce within the framework of International Conventions ratified by the country related to the issues of gender-based violence referred to in this law.
- H) Give mandatory opinion on bills and programs whose purpose is gender-based violence against women. Failure to expressly rule within thirty days shall be understood as approval.
- I) Issue opinion regarding actions or situations relating to gender-based violence against women of which it becomes aware, communicating it to the competent authorities.
- J) Prepare an annual report on compliance with its responsibilities and on the situation of gender-based violence in the country.

This report must be presented publicly and sent to the National Coordinating Council for Gender Equality Public Policies, the National Human Rights Institution and Ombudsman's Office and the General Assembly.

(Composition of the National Advisory Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women).- The Council shall be composed as follows:

- A) One representative of the National Women's Institute of the Ministry of Social Development, who shall preside over it.
- B) One representative of the Ministry of National Defense.
- C) One representative of the Ministry of Education and Culture.
- D) One representative of the Ministry of the Interior.
- E) One representative of the Planning and Budget Office of the Presidency of the Republic.
- F) One representative of the Ministry of Public Health.
- G) One representative of the Ministry of Labor and Social Security.
- H) One representative of the Ministry of Housing, Land Management and Environment.
- I) One representative of the Judicial Branch.
- J) One representative of the General Prosecutor's Office of the Nation.
- K) One representative of the National Public Education Administration.
- L) One representative of the Social Security Bank.
- M) One representative of the Congress of Mayors.
- N) One representative of the Institute for Children and Adolescents of Uruguay.
- O) Three representatives of the Uruguayan Network against Domestic and Sexual Violence.

At meetings of the National Advisory Council, a representative of the National Human Rights Institution and Ombudsman's Office may participate with voice and without vote.

The representatives of public bodies and agencies must be of the highest ranks.

(Internal regulations).- The National Advisory Council shall issue its internal operating regulations within thirty days from the approval of this law. It may create thematic commissions for the better fulfillment of its responsibilities. The National Women's Institute shall be in charge of the technical secretariat of the National Advisory Council and shall provide the infrastructure for its meetings and those of the thematic commissions.

(Powers and duties).- The National Advisory Council may summon in consultation representatives of Ministries and other public agencies, and non-governmental organizations and private institutions fighting gender-based violence. It shall meet at least once a year with social or union organizations related to the subject matter, in order to hear the suggestions, proposals or recommendations they present, with the aim of strengthening its work. Likewise, it must provide such organizations with information on the implementation of policies to fight violence against women and statistical monitoring and evaluation reports.

(Departmental Commissions for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women).- The National Advisory Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women shall create, in each department of the country, a Departmental Commission for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women, composed of representatives of the institutions that comprise it and shall regulate its composition and operation, taking into account the particularities of each place, in consultation with local actors. The presidency and technical secretariat of the Departmental Commissions shall be in charge of the National Women's Institute, which shall provide the infrastructure for their operation.

(Responsibilities of the Departmental Commissions).- The Departmental Commissions for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women shall have the following responsibilities:

- A) Ensure compliance with this law and the National Plan for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women in the department.
- B) Implement in the territory the resolutions and directives of the National Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women.
- C) Promote the articulation of policies and actions against gender-based violence against women in the department.
- D) Advise in the department the national, departmental and municipal authorities, in articulation with the National Advisory Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women.

(Observatory on Gender-Based Violence against Women).- The Observatory on Gender-Based Violence against Women is created, intended for monitoring, collection, production, registration and permanent systematization of data and information on violence against women. It shall be in charge of an Honorary Inter-Institutional Commission formed by the Ministry of Social Development, which shall preside over it, the Ministry of the Interior and the General Prosecutor's Office of the Nation. Said Commission may summon Civil Society organizations dedicated to the fight against gender-based violence. The members of said commission shall be persons of proven experience designated by each of the Institutions. It shall function within the scope of the National Women's Institute, which shall provide the technical and administrative secretariat, as well as the necessary infrastructure for its operation. (*)

(Responsibilities of the Observatory).- The responsibilities of the Observatory on Gender-Based Violence against Women are:

- A) Collect, process, register, analyze, publish and disseminate periodic and systematic information on gender-based violence against women, taking into account sexual, racial, age and socio-economic diversity, disability situation, among other aspects that intersect with gender.
- B) Conduct studies and research on the evolution, prevalence, types and modalities of violence against women, its consequences and effects, identifying those social, cultural, economic and political factors that are associated or may constitute cause of violence.
- D) Create and maintain a documentary database, updated, of free public access, that ensures accessibility in disability situations.
- E) Systematize and disseminate good practices in the prevention and eradication of violence against women and innovative experiences.
- F) Encourage and promote the organization and periodic holding of public debates, with participation of research centers, academic institutions, civil society organizations and representatives of public and private bodies and agencies, national and international with competence in the matter, fostering the exchange of experiences and identifying relevant issues and problems for the public agenda.
- G) Request from public bodies and agencies and private institutions the information necessary for the fulfillment of its responsibilities.
- H) Articulate actions with the National Council for a Life Free of Gender-Based Violence against Women and other governmental bodies and agencies, with social organizations and with other observatories that exist at the departmental, national and international level.
- I) Enter into cooperation agreements with public or private bodies and agencies, national or international, with the purpose of developing studies and research.
- J) Conduct studies on proper compliance with the law in the administrative and judicial spheres.

## CHAPTER III - GUIDELINES FOR PUBLIC POLICIES

(Compliance and articulation of the national policy against gender-based violence).- State institutions, within the scope of their competencies, must adopt and execute programs and actions for the eradication of gender-based violence against women in accordance with applicable national and international legislation, particularly as provided in the National Plan against Gender-Based Violence against Women. (Guidelines for educational policies).- Without prejudice to the provisions of subsection 2 of article 202 and subsection 2 of article 204 of the Constitution of the Republic, the bodies and agencies responsible for educational policies at all levels (initial, primary, secondary, teacher training, tertiary, university, non-formal education) and all educational institutions, within the scope of their competencies, must:

- A) Design and implement in the educational institutions under their charge a comprehensive plan to mainstream the gender perspective in their actions, plans and programs, including the Human Rights Education Plan, to promote equality between men and women, overcome stereotypes based on the inferiority or subjugation of women and prevent, sanction, protect and repair the harm caused by violence against women. - B) Adopt measures for the effective protection of the right to education -in relation to academic performance and educational inclusion- of female students who face situations of gender-based violence. - C) Include in the minimum curricular content the gender perspective, respect and freedom in interpersonal relationships, the human right to a life free of violence, equality between men and women, the democratization of family relationships and the delegitimization of violent models. - D) Design and disseminate informative and educational materials for the prevention and early detection of gender-based violence against women, following accessibility standards for persons in situations of disability and taking into account age diversities. - E) Guide and sensitize teaching staff so that the teaching materials they use do not contain gender stereotypes or discriminatory criteria in order to foster equality of rights, opportunities and treatment between women and men of all ages. The sponsorship of books and teaching materials by educational authorities must require compliance with the established conditions. - F) Prohibit all discriminatory measures against students, teaching and non-teaching staff, based on their sexual orientation or gender identity. Trans persons, whatever their age, position, function or participation in the educational center, shall have the right to enter it with the attire characteristic of the gender with which they identify, and to manifest, without discrimination and with equal rights, their gender identity. - G) Design, test, implement and disseminate in public and private educational centers, protocols of action that allow the promotion of rights, prevention and early detection as well as the timely and responsible reporting, intervention and referral in cases of gender-based violence against women. The bodies, agencies and institutions with competence in the supervision of educational centers must ensure their compliance. Reporting mechanisms must ensure their accessibility according to age and disability situation. H) Investigate, sanction and repair gender-based violence that occurs within the institution, adopting the necessary measures to guarantee victims the rights provided in articles 7, 8 and 9 of this law. I) Provide permanent training to all personnel of educational centers on women's human rights and the prevention of gender-based violence. J) Conduct interdisciplinary research aimed at creating models for prevention, detection and intervention in the face of violence against women of all ages. K) Maintain updated records of situations of gender-based violence that are detected or that occur in educational settings, disaggregated by age, disability situation, ethnic-racial origin, sexual orientation, gender identity, beliefs, territorial affiliation among other variables, in accordance with the guidelines of the National Women's Institute, ensuring in all cases the protection and confidentiality of personal data. L) Establish as a hiring requirement for all personnel of formal education institutions, the absence of administrative or criminal records in matters of physical, psychological, sexual, domestic violence, considering it disqualifying for the teaching function. (Guidelines for health policies).- The Ministry of Public Health and all other bodies and agencies linked to health policies and institutions providing health services, within the scope of their competencies, must:

- A) Promote the right to a life free of violence against women based on gender as a priority objective of public health, mainstreaming the gender perspective and prevention actions in institutional plans, programs and actions. - B) Develop policies aimed at addressing gender-based violence as a public health problem, disseminating information about programs and services for its prevention. - C) Eradicate practices based on discriminatory stereotypes against women and adopt measures to guarantee respect for the autonomy, self-determination and human dignity of women, without distinction on grounds of age, sexual orientation or gender identity, disability situation, ethnic-racial origin, religious beliefs among other factors. - D) Ensure universal coverage and access to health care for all women in situations of gender-based violence, for prevention, reduction of risk factors, timely treatment and rehabilitation. The Ministry of Public Health shall determine the conditions that must be met by institutions providing health services, whether public or private, comprehensive or partial. - E) Guarantee that all health interventions respect the free expression of will of women, in relation to any matter affecting their autonomy, integrity or well-being. They must have the necessary information according to their communication needs, which must be of quality, non-discriminatory and understandable, including all existing options, their risks and benefits. Women may revoke their decision at any time and for any reason without this entailing any disadvantage or prejudice. - F) Guarantee that all girls, boys and adolescents have access to prophylaxis and treatment for HIV, syphilis, hepatitis and other sexually transmitted infections. Likewise, access to sexual and reproductive health services as well as education and guidance on sexual health, contraceptive methods is guaranteed without requiring prior authorization from legal representatives, as well as voluntary termination of pregnancy under the terms and conditions provided in applicable legislation. - G) Guarantee confidentiality and respect for the privacy of women of all ages in all health services. - H) Adopt measures to ensure the existence of complaint mechanisms in health services that are agile and accessible to all women, especially taking into account those who are in situations of disability, girls and adolescents, older women, women served by mental health services, as well as those hospitalized in hospital or residential centers. - I) Issue directives to ensure that all health providers develop permanent training actions for personnel (professional, technical and administrative) in relation to the prevention of gender-based violence and care and rehabilitation for affected women, incorporating the generational perspective, sexual diversity, ethnic-racial and disability situations. - J) Protocolize interventions regarding intersex persons, prohibiting unnecessary medical procedures on girls, boys and adolescents. - K) Promote records of situations of intra-institutional gender-based violence, detected or attended to in health services, in coordination with the National Women's Institute, including data on prevalence, risk factors and health repercussions of gender-based violence. - L) Promote quantitative and qualitative studies and research on the impact of gender-based violence on women's health and on care models for victims for greater effectiveness in responses. - M) Promote the approval of protocols and create spaces for attention to men who exercise violence, based on existing systematized knowledge, in order to contribute to stopping the intra-family and community transmission of violent modes of bonding and the management of gender relations, in coordination with the National Women's Institute. (Guidelines for labor and social security policies).- The Ministry of Labor and Social Security and all other bodies and agencies linked to labor and social security policies, within the scope of their competencies, must:

- A) Promote measures that guarantee the exercise of women's labor rights, both in the public and private sectors, particularly the right to equal remuneration for work of equal value, without discrimination based on sex, age, disability situation, marital status or maternity. - B) Develop awareness-raising and information actions for the prevention of gender-based violence against women in the workplace as well as promote such actions in social dialogue and collective bargaining. - C) Incorporate the gender perspective in the plans, programs and services of the Ministry of Labor and Social Security, regulating actions for the prevention, detection, investigation and punishment of gender-based violence in the workplace. - D) Implement programs for training and inclusion in work of women with restricted employment possibilities as a consequence of gender-based violence. (Guidelines for security policies).- The Ministry of the Interior and all other bodies and agencies linked to security policies, within the scope of their competencies, must:

## CHAPTER V - PROTECTION, INVESTIGATION AND PENALIZATION PROCESSES FOR GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

### SECTION I - PROVISIONS COMMON TO ALL PROCESSES

(Priority interest).- In situations of gender-based violence, the priority must be comprehensive protection of human dignity and the safety of the victim and their family environment, with special guarantee of the rights recognized in articles 7°, 8° and 9° of this law.

(Assessment of evidence).- Without prejudice to the provisions of article 140 of the General Code of Process, it must be especially taken into account that acts of violence constitute, in general, situations linked to privacy or that are carried out without the presence of third parties. The silence, lack of resistance or prior or subsequent sexual history of the victim of a sexual assault must not be assessed as demonstration of acceptance or consent of the conduct. The difference in age, economic condition, gifts, presents and other forms of compensation shall be assessed as indicators of abuse of power in situations of sexual abuse against girls, boys or adolescents. In all cases, the right and best interest of girls, boys and adolescents to give their opinion shall be respected, which must be analyzed applying the rules of sound criticism. It shall not be valid to use technical arguments to diminish the credibility of their testimonies.

### SECTION II - PROCESSES IN ADMINISTRATIVE, PUBLIC AND PRIVATE SPHERES

(Intra-institutional sphere).- Public and private organs, bodies and institutions must adopt measures for the prevention, protection, investigation and sanction of gender-based violence that occurs in the intra-institutional sphere, exercised by their personnel with respect to other officials, workers or users of the services.

(Scope of application).- For the purposes provided in the preceding article, whatever the form of gender-based violence referred to, the provisions of Law No. 18.561, of September 11, 2009, are applicable, as pertinent.

(Complaint).- Public and private organs, bodies and institutions that serve girls, boys and adolescents must implement accessible and effective complaint mechanisms. Likewise, they must ensure confidentiality, the reservation of information and especially consider situations of disability and those who are confined in public or private centers. (Communication to competent authorities).- All public and private organs, bodies and institutions that serve girls, boys or adolescents must communicate to the competent authorities situations of abuse, sexual abuse or sexual exploitation of which they have knowledge, for due protection and reparation to the victims and sanction of those responsible.

- C) Processes related to divorces, alimony, custody and visitation, suspensions, limitations or losses of parental authority in cases in which, up to two years in advance, any precautionary measure has been judicially adopted as a consequence of gender-based, domestic or sexual violence, or in which said violence is verified by any means even if the application of measures has not been requested.

### SECTION IV - PROTECTION PROCEEDINGS IN THE JUDICIAL SPHERE

(Complaint).- Any person who becomes aware of an act of gender-based violence may, by any means, give notice to the competent Court or Prosecutor's Office, which shall immediately adopt the urgent protection measures they deem pertinent in accordance with the provisions of this law. Provided that the notice is credible, the person who gave it shall not bear any type of liability. Among the first and most urgent proceedings, the court or the prosecutor's office shall ensure that the victim has legal defense secured, ordering what is necessary to that effect. The protection proceedings in the judicial sphere shall be governed by the provisions of the General Code of Procedure, insofar as they do not conflict with this law.

(Notification).- All judicial actions in matters of gender-based violence must be mandatorily notified, from the outset, to the corresponding Prosecutor.

(Hearing).- Once the complaint is received, the Court must:

- A) Adopt urgent protection measures, for the determination of which it must consider the characteristics of the facts being reported and in particular their severity and frequency, as well as any relevant background.
- B) Hold a hearing within seventy-two hours, which it must conduct personally under penalty of nullity. Prior to the holding of the hearing, the court's technical team shall submit a risk assessment report. (*) (*)Notes:

See in this regulation, article: 68.

(Duty to Appear).- The accused is obligated to appear under warning of being brought before the Court with the assistance of public force. Notwithstanding this, if he is not located or does not appear at the hearing for any reason, it shall be held in any event and the corresponding measures shall be adopted.

(Prohibition of Confrontation).- The parties must be heard separately under the most serious responsibility of the acting Judge and under no circumstances may they be present together in the same room. Effective measures must also be adopted to guarantee the victim's safety and their separate presence in the judicial building or space. In these proceedings, mediation and conciliation are prohibited.

(General Precautionary Measures).- Whenever it is proven that a fundamental human right is violated or threatened, the Court must order, ex officio, at the request of a party or the Public Prosecutor's Office, with reasoning, all measures aimed at protecting the life, physical or emotional integrity of the victim, their freedom and personal safety, as well as the economic assistance and patrimonial integrity of the victim and their family unit. Under no circumstances may reciprocal measures or measures at the expense of the victims or that restrict their rights be ordered. If the Court decides not to adopt any measure, its decision must state the grounds for such determination.

(Special Precautionary Measures).- For the fulfillment of the precautionary purpose, the Court may adopt any of the following measures, or other analogous ones, setting the corresponding term:

- A) Order the aggressor to cease acts of disturbance or intimidation that, directly or indirectly, they carry out against the victim.
- B) Prohibit the aggressor from communicating with, relating to, meeting with or engaging in any similar conduct -by themselves or through third parties- in relation to the victim, their sons and daughters and other affected persons, witnesses or complainants of the act.
- C) Prohibit, restrict or limit the presence of the aggressor at the victim's domicile or residence, workplaces, places of study or other places frequented by them or their daughters and sons or other persons in their care, and may establish monitoring mechanisms that ensure strict compliance with the measure ordered, such as technology systems for verification of presence and location of persons or other analogous ones.

In cases where the right to life or physical integrity of a victim of domestic or gender-based violence is considered to be at risk, the court may order the placement of an electronic monitoring device on adolescents. To these effects, the court must provide reasons why other types of protection are insufficient or unsuitable. (*)

D) Order the immediate return of personal objects belonging to the victim, their sons and daughters or other persons in their care.

E) Seize weapons that the aggressor may have in their possession, which shall remain in the custody of the Court, in the manner it deems pertinent.

F) Prohibit the aggressor from using, possessing or carrying firearms, notifying the competent authority to that effect.

G) Order the employer to arrange the transfer or suspension of the accused person, when the violence occurs at the victim's workplace.

H) Provide corrective measures and other measures to prevent discrimination or violence against women in the workplace or institutional setting.

I) Order medical, educational or analogous services that it deems essential, by public agencies or other responsible institutions.

J) Authorize the change of health care provider, maintaining the rights and conditions established with respect to the previous provider.

K) Order the transfer of the victim who is institutionalized in a residential, hospital or prison center to another place that ensures their fundamental rights.

L) Order the change of administrator of economic income of any nature received by women in a situation of disability or in any other situation of dependence, when the aggressor was the person performing that function.

M) Order the mandatory attendance of the aggressor to rehabilitation programs.

N) Order the removal of the aggressor from the common residence and the immediate delivery of their personal effects in the presence of the Bailiff, regardless of who holds title to the property. Likewise, a judicial inventory shall be made of the movable property that is removed and that which remains on the premises, and a transcript may be issued at the request of the parties.

O) Order the return of the victim to the domicile or residence, in the presence of the Bailiff, when they had left it due to the situation of gender-based violence.

P) Order the immediate revocation of mandates that the victim may have granted to the aggressor for the administration of common property, notifying the corresponding Registry.

Q) Prohibit the performance of acts of disposition without the written consent of the victim or judicial authorization, with respect to the property of family businesses, including the property of the family farming enterprise when the victim is the holder or collaborating spouse therein.

(Minimum Term of Special Precautionary Measures).- The minimum duration of the measures provided for in literals B) and C) of article 65 is one hundred eighty days, without prejudice to the possibility of their modification or termination. The measure of removal from the home (literal N) shall be applied with a self-executing character, not being subject to any subsequent term or condition. In case of non-compliance with the measures ordered by the Court, the aggressor shall be considered to have committed the offense provided for in article 173 of the Penal Code.

(Protection Measures).- In situations of domestic violence against a woman, the decision ordering protection measures must also resolve:

- A) Provisional alimony in favor of the woman and her sons and daughters or other persons in her care, in applicable cases.
- B) Provisional custody of daughters and sons under eighteen years of age, who under no circumstances may remain in the care of the aggressor.
- C) The suspension of the aggressor's visitation with respect to daughters and sons under eighteen years of age. These may resume once a minimum period of three months has elapsed without the repetition of acts of violence and with the aggressor having complied with the measures imposed.

Exceptionally, and if so requested by the sons or daughters and it is considered that there is no risk of violation of their rights, supervised visits may be ordered by an institution or by an adult of their confidence, who shall be responsible for their compliance under safe conditions. Under no circumstances shall visits take place during the night or at police facilities.

To such effects, compliance must be given to the provisions of article 8 of the Code of Childhood and Adolescence.

The same criteria must be followed with respect to adults declared incapacitated.

(Complementary Assessment).- If necessary, the Court ex officio, at the request of the Prosecutor or of the victim, may order a complementary assessment to that provided for in literal B) of article 61 of this law.

(Evaluation Hearing).- More than thirty days before the termination of the measures ordered, the Court must convene an evaluation hearing of the situation, in order to determine whether it is appropriate to order the continuity of the measures, their replacement by other measures or their termination. In case of non-appearance, the Judge shall order the aggressor to be brought in.

(Exemption from Counter-Security).- In proceedings for the adoption of precautionary measures, in all matters not provided for by this law, articles 313, 314 and 315 of the General Code of Procedure shall apply insofar as they are applicable. No provision of counter-security shall be required nor shall the validity of the protection measures be conditioned on the commencement of any other subsequent proceeding.

### SECTION V - FAMILY PROCEEDINGS

(Scope of application).- Family proceedings arising from situations of gender-based, domestic or sexual violence shall be governed by the provisions of the General Procedural Code and the Code of Childhood and Adolescence. In these proceedings, the context of gender-based violence must be especially taken into account in order to ensure that the resolutions adopted strengthen the human rights and autonomy of the affected persons.

(Right to name).- In cases of sexual violation that result in the birth of a boy or girl, the mother shall have the right to have the child registered in the Civil Registry with both maternal surnames and paternity recognized or declared judicially shall not entail the registration of the child with the surname of the aggressor (article 198 of the Code of Childhood and Adolescence).

### SECTION VI - CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS

(Scope of application).- Criminal proceedings processed before the Specialized Courts in Gender-Based, Domestic and Sexual Violence shall be governed by the provisions of the Criminal Procedural Code (Law No. 19,293, of December 19, 2014). The regime provided for intimidated victims and witnesses (articles 163 and 164 of said legal text) shall be applied to all victims of gender-based violence, whatever their age. The hearing shall not be public when so requested by the victim and the presence of the emotional companion shall be admitted. Whenever possible, the victim's testimony must be filmed to avoid its repetition.

(Anticipated evidence).- At the request of the victim or the Public Prosecutor's Office, the Court shall arrange for the processing of anticipated evidence (articles 213 et seq. of Law No. 19,293, of December 19, 2014, Criminal Procedural Code), without need for other grounds, and the results of the protection proceedings (Section IV of this Chapter) and the reports of the Network of Services for Women in situations of Gender-Based Violence (Chapter IV of this law) must be especially taken into account.

(Defense of the victim).- In the proceedings provided for in subsection D) of article 51, the victim may designate specialized institutions in the defense of victims' rights to appear and exercise their rights and interests on their behalf.

(Criminal action and prescription).- The criminal action with respect to the offenses provided for in articles 272, 272 bis, 272 ter, 273, 273 bis and 274 of the Penal Code is public, not requiring an instance by the offended party. The prescription of the criminal action is suspended while the victim is a minor and until having reached the age of majority they file the complaint themselves or ratify that filed by their legal representatives during their minority. If as a consequence of any of the indicated offenses the death of the minor has occurred, it shall begin to run from the day on which they would have reached the age of majority.

(Suspension of exercise of parental authority and disqualification from the exercise of public and private functions).- Persons subject to prosecution for the offenses provided for in articles 272, 272 bis, 272 ter, 273, 273 bis, and 274 of the Penal Code and in Law No. 17,815, of September 6, 2004, are suspended in the exercise of parental authority or guardianship and disqualified from the exercise of public and private functions in the educational area, health and all those that involve direct contact with girls, boys and adolescents, persons with disabilities and elderly persons in a situation of dependency, for a period of ten years if a conviction is handed down or until their dismissal or acquittal. The suspension and disqualification from the exercise of parental authority and functions established in this article shall be double that provided therein in case of recidivism. (*)

(Pecuniary sanction).- In the conviction sentence, in addition to the penalty, a patrimonial reparation for the victim shall be arranged for an amount equivalent to twelve monthly incomes of the convicted person, or failing that twelve minimum wages, without prejudice to their right to pursue the corresponding procedural channel to obtain full reparation for the damage.

(Notification to the victim).- Whenever the release of a person subject to prosecution for offenses related to gender-based, domestic or sexual violence is ordered, the competent Court must notify said resolution to the victim with a minimum advance notice of five days and arrange protection measures with respect to them for a period of no less than one hundred and eighty days.

(Disclosure of images or recordings with intimate content).- Anyone who disseminates, reveals, exhibits or transfers to third parties images or recordings of a person with intimate or sexual content, without their authorization, shall be punished with a penalty of six months imprisonment to two years penitentiary. In no case shall authorization granted by a person under eighteen years of age be considered valid. This offense is constituted even when the one who disseminates the images or recordings has participated in them. The administrators of internet sites, portals, search engines or similar who, notified of the lack of authorization, do not take down the images immediately, shall be sanctioned with the same penalty provided for in this article.

(Special aggravating circumstances).- The penalty provided for in the preceding article shall be increased from one third to one half when:

- A) The images or recordings disseminated have been obtained without the consent of the affected person.
- B) It is committed with respect to the spouse, partner or person who is or has been united by an analogous relationship of affection, even without cohabitation.
- C) The victim is under eighteen years of age.
- D) The victim is a person in a situation of disability.
- E) The acts have been committed with a lucrative purpose.

## CHAPTER VII - FINAL PROVISIONS

Law No. 17,514, of July 2, 2002, shall be applicable in situations of domestic violence with respect to male victims, including children and adolescents.

Articles 24 to 29 of Law No. 17,514, of July 2, 2002, are hereby repealed.

The administrative and judicial procedures provided for in this law as well as in Law No. 17,514, of July 2, 2002, are exempt from all national or departmental taxes.

The Supreme Court of Justice shall adopt the corresponding measures for the redistribution of competencies in compliance with articles 51 and 52 of this law.
