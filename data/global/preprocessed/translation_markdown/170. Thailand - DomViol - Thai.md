# ACT

ACT

ON PROMOTION OF FAMILY INSTITUTION DEVELOPMENT AND PROTECTION

B.E. 2562 (2019)

VAJIRALONGKORN BODINDRADEBAYAVARANGKUN

Given on the 19th Day of May B.E. 2562 (2019)

Being the 4th Year of the Present Reign

His Majesty King Vajiralongkorn Bodindradebayavarangkun is Graciously Pleased to Proclaim that: Whereas it is expedient to revise the law on the protection of persons subjected to domestic violence; This Act contains certain provisions relating to the restriction of rights and liberties of persons, in respect of which Section 26 in conjunction with Section 28, Section 32, Section 33, Section 37, and Section 38

of the Constitution of the Kingdom of Thailand so permit by virtue of the provisions of law. The grounds and necessity for the restriction of rights and liberties of persons under this Act

are to carry out operations concerning the promotion and development of families and the protection of welfare, which will be beneficial to strengthening families and developing the family institution. The enactment of this Act is in compliance with the conditions prescribed in Section 26 of the Constitution of the Kingdom of Thailand. Therefore, by and with the advice and consent of the National Legislative Assembly acting as the National Assembly, it is hereby enacted as follows:

### Section 1 This Act shall be called the "Act on Promotion of Family Institution Development and Protection, B.E. 2562 (2019)".

### Section 2 This Act shall come into force after the expiration of ninety days from the date of its publication in the Government Gazette onwards.

### Section 3 The Act on Protection of Persons Subjected to Domestic Violence, B.E. 2550 (2007), shall be repealed.

## CHAPTER 1

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Section 4 In this Act:

"domestic violence" means any act committed by a family member against another with the intention to cause, or in a manner likely to cause, danger to the life, body, mind, health, liberty, or reputation of a family member, or coercing or exercising unlawful domination over a family member to compel such family member to commit an act, omit to commit an act, or submit to an act unlawfully;

"family member" means an ascendant, descendant, spouse, former spouse, a person who cohabits or has cohabited as husband and wife without registering marriage, adopted child, as well as any person who is dependent and lives in the same household;

"State agency" means a ministry, bureau, department, or other administrative unit with the status of a department, regional administration, local administration, and other State agencies;

"State enterprise" means a State enterprise established by an Act or Royal Decree;

"Court" means the Juvenile and Family Court under the law on the Juvenile and Family Court and Juvenile and Family Case Procedure;

"Committee" means the Committee on Promotion of Family Institution Development and Protection;

"member" means a member of the Committee on Promotion of Family Institution Development and Protection;

"competent official" means a person appointed by the Minister of Social Development and Human Security to perform duties under this Act, and includes administrative officials or police under the Criminal Procedure Code;

"Centre Director" means the Director of a Family Promotion and Protection Centre;

"Director-General" means the Director-General of the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development;

"Minister" means the Minister having charge and control of this Act.

### Section 5 The Minister of Social Development and Human Security, the Minister of Interior, and the Minister of Public Health shall have charge and control of this Act and shall have the power to issue regulations and notifications for the implementation of this Act, to the extent relating to the affairs of each ministry.

The Minister of Social Development and Human Security shall have the power to appoint competent officials to perform duties under this Act.

Such regulations and notifications, upon publication in the Government Gazette, shall come into force.

## CHAPTER 2

THE COMMITTEE

### Section 6 There shall be a committee called the "Committee on Promotion of Family Institution Development and Protection", consisting of:

(1) the Prime Minister or a Deputy Prime Minister assigned by the Prime Minister, as Chairperson;

(2) the Minister of Social Development and Human Security, as Vice-Chairperson;

(3) ex officio members, namely: the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Social Development and Human Security, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Interior, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Justice, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Education, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Public Health, the Attorney-General, and the Commissioner-General of the Royal Thai Police;

(4) fifteen qualified members appointed by the Prime Minister through recruitment from persons who have acknowledged knowledge, ability and experience in the promotion and development of families, the promotion and development of children and youth, the promotion and development of quality of life of persons with disabilities, the promotion and development of quality of life of the elderly, the promotion and development of women, law, culture and religion, occupational development, educational development, public health, the protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, or other fields beneficial to the promotion, development, protection, prevention, or rehabilitation of family members, at least one person from each field.

The recruitment of qualified members under (4) shall be in accordance with the criteria, procedures, and conditions prescribed by the Minister of Social Development and Human Security in the regulations.

The Director-General shall be a member and secretary, and shall have the power to appoint not more than two officials of the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development as assistant secretaries.

### Section 7 A qualified member must have the qualifications and must not have the prohibited characteristics as follows:

(1) having Thai nationality;

(2) having an attitude and conduct appropriate for performing the functions of protecting persons subjected to domestic violence or promoting and developing the family institution;

(3) having never been examined by the National Human Rights Commission and found to have committed an act constituting a human rights violation, and having never been examined by the Child Protection Committee of Bangkok or the Provincial Child Protection Committee and found to have committed an act constituting improper treatment of a child;

(4) not being an incompetent or quasi-incompetent person;

(5) not being a bankrupt or having been a dishonest bankrupt;

(6) having never been dismissed, removed, or discharged from a State agency;

(7) having never been subject to welfare protection measures arising from the commission of domestic violence under this Act;

(8) having never been punished for committing an offence under the law on the protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, the law on child protection, the law on the Juvenile and Family Court and Juvenile and Family Case Procedure, or the law on the prevention and suppression of human trafficking;

(9) having never been imprisoned by a final judgment, except for an offence committed through negligence or a petty offence.

### Section 8 A qualified member shall hold office for a term of three years and may be reappointed, but may not hold office for more than two consecutive terms.

In the event that a qualified member vacates office before the expiration of the term, the person appointed to replace such member shall hold office for the remainder of the term of the member whom such person replaces. If the remaining term of a qualified member who vacates office before the expiration of the term is less than ninety days, the recruitment of a qualified member to replace the vacant position may not be carried out.

During the period in which a qualified member has not yet been appointed to replace a vacated position, the Committee shall consist of the remaining members.

Upon the expiration of the term under paragraph one, if qualified members have not yet been newly appointed, the qualified members who have vacated office shall remain in office to perform their duties provisionally until the newly appointed qualified members assume their duties, provided that this shall be within ninety days from the date of expiration of the term. If such period has elapsed and the qualified members who have vacated office may no longer perform their duties.

### Section 9 In addition to vacating office upon the expiration of the term, a qualified member shall vacate office upon:

(1) death;

(2) resignation;

(3) absence from more than three consecutive meetings without reasonable cause;

(4) the Committee resolving to remove such member due to disgraceful conduct, breach of duty or dishonesty in the performance of duties, or incompetence;

(5) lacking the qualifications or having the prohibited characteristics under Section 7.

### Section 10 A meeting of the Committee shall require the attendance of not less than one-half of the total number of members to constitute a quorum.

In a meeting of the Committee, if the Chairperson is not present at the meeting or is unable to perform duties, the Vice-Chairperson shall act in place of the Chairperson. If both the Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson are not present at the meeting or are unable to perform duties, the members present at the meeting shall elect one member to be the chairperson of the meeting.

A resolution of the meeting shall be by a majority of votes. Each member shall have one vote in voting. In the event of an equality of votes, the chairperson of the meeting shall have an additional casting vote.

Meetings of the Committee shall be held at least three times per year.

### มาตรา ๑๑ The Committee shall have the following duties and powers:

(๑) To prescribe policies, strategies, and master plans for the promotion and development of families and welfare protection with the approval of the Council of Ministers, as well as to prescribe programs, measures, and guidelines for the promotion and development of families and welfare protection;

(๒) To monitor and evaluate the implementation of policies, strategies, master plans, programs, measures, and guidelines for the promotion and development of families and welfare protection, as well as to report on the promotion and development of families and welfare protection to the Council of Ministers and the National Assembly for acknowledgment at least once a year;

(๓) To submit opinions and observations to the Council of Ministers regarding the enactment or amendment of laws relating to the promotion and development of families and welfare protection, as well as to submit opinions and observations regarding Thailand's role at the national and international levels in the promotion and development of families and welfare protection;

(๔) To promote the provision of support, coordination, monitoring, and assistance to government agencies, private sector, and civil society organizations that carry out the promotion and development of families and welfare protection;

(๕) To issue regulations or notifications for the implementation of this Act;

(๖) To perform other duties as prescribed by law to be the duties and powers of the Committee or as assigned by the Council of Ministers.

### มาตรา ๑๒ The Committee shall have the power to appoint sub-committees to carry out any matter assigned by the Committee.

The provisions of Section ๑๐ shall apply mutatis mutandis to meetings of sub-committees.

## หมวด ๓

Family Promotion and Protection Centers

### มาตรา ๑๓ In implementing this Act, the Provincial Office of Social Development and Human Security shall serve as the family promotion and protection center in the provincial area, and the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development shall serve as the family promotion and protection center in the Bangkok Metropolitan area, and shall have the following duties and powers:

(๑) To implement policies, strategies, master plans, programs, measures, and guidelines for the promotion and development of families and welfare protection;

(๒) To provide knowledge and understanding, counseling and advice, and mediation and conciliation in matters of family problems, as well as to receive reports, collect, and compile facts concerning acts of domestic violence for the purpose of filing petitions or statements to the court and welfare protection;

(๓) To serve as a learning center for the promotion and development of families and welfare protection, and as a coordination center with government agencies and the private sector in carrying out the promotion and development of families and welfare protection;

(๔) To carry out welfare protection, or to file petitions or statements to the court for welfare protection, as well as to implement court orders or orders of the head of the center for welfare protection;

(๕) To implement programs and guidelines for the development of the capacity of officials and personnel involved in the promotion and development of families and welfare protection, as well as to evaluate the performance of officials and personnel in the family promotion and protection center;

(๖) To monitor and report on the implementation of this Act to the Director-General and the Minister;

(๗) To provide assistance or relief to families in difficulty in accordance with regulations prescribed by the Minister;

(๘) To promote and support the mobilization of opinions and constructive exchange of knowledge among families or family representatives to lead to the formulation of policies, strategies, master plans, programs, measures, and guidelines for the promotion and development of families and welfare protection by the Committee;

(๙) To carry out any other matters relating to the promotion and development of families and welfare protection as prescribed in this Act and under other laws, or as assigned by the Committee or the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development.

### มาตรา ๑๔ The Provincial Social Development and Human Security Officer or a person assigned by the Provincial Social Development and Human Security Officer shall be the head of the center in that provincial area. In the Bangkok Metropolitan area, the Director-General or a person assigned by the Director-General shall be the head of the center to carry out matters relating to the promotion and development of families, welfare protection, or any other matters under this Act. In performing duties under this Act, the head of the center or a person assigned by the head of the center shall be a competent official under the Penal Code.

## หมวด ๔

Community Family Development Centers

### มาตรา ๑๕ Any private organization having objectives or activities relating to the promotion and development of families or welfare protection may apply for registration as a community family development center, abbreviated as "CFDC".

The qualifications of applicants for registration, the application for registration, and registration as a CFDC shall be in accordance with the criteria, procedures, and conditions prescribed by the Minister of Social Development and Human Security in a regulation.

### มาตรา ๑๖ A CFDC may submit an application for government agencies to consider providing assistance or support in financial, resource, academic, personnel development, or other aspects in carrying out the promotion and development of families or welfare protection. The submission of an application for such assistance or support shall be in accordance with the criteria, procedures, and conditions prescribed by the Minister in a regulation.

## หมวด ๕

Promotion and Development of Families

### มาตรา ๑๗ For the purpose of promoting and developing families, the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development shall coordinate with the Ministry of Interior to ensure that registrars under the law on family registration, government officials performing duties relating to the promotion and development of families, CFDCs, or private organizations having objectives in promoting and developing families have knowledge and understanding in the promotion and development of families.

In carrying out the actions under paragraph one to provide knowledge and understanding in the promotion and development of families, such knowledge shall include the following:

(๑) Knowledge concerning the relationship between husband and wife, property between husband and wife, validity of marriage, termination of marriage, parenthood and children, rights and duties of parents and children, adopted children, maintenance expenses, as well as inheritance and wills;

(๒) Knowledge concerning welfare protection, or other knowledge relating to families and the institution of the family;

(๓) Knowledge and skills in selecting a spouse, living as a couple, emotional changes and emotional control;

(๔) Knowledge in preparing for pre- and post-pregnancy, knowledge and skills in raising children;

(๕) Knowledge in occupations, job creation, income generation, savings, asset management, and access to capital sources for occupations to support families;

(๖) Knowledge of civic rights and duties, rights and welfare, and guidelines for seeking assistance from government agencies or private organizations;

(๗) Any other knowledge relating to the promotion and development of families.

In carrying out the actions under paragraph one and paragraph two, the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development shall establish cooperation and coordination among various agencies to achieve the integration of the promotion and development of the institution of the family.

### มาตรา ๑๘ In the case where any man and woman wish to register a marriage, if there is a request for advice or guidance on matters relating to the family, the registrar under the law on family registration may provide advice or guidance on matters relating to the family as requested, or coordinate with the family promotion and protection center, CFDC, or private organization having objectives in promoting and developing families to carry out such actions instead.

In the case where a married couple wishes to register a divorce, the registrar under the law on family registration may provide advice or guidance to the married couple wishing to register the divorce, or coordinate with the family promotion and protection center, CFDC, or private organization having objectives in promoting and developing families to carry out such actions instead, taking into account the voluntary consent of such married couple. In providing advice or guidance to the married couple wishing to register the divorce, consideration shall be given primarily to the welfare and future of the children, the protection, care, and education of minor children. If such married couple still wishes to register the divorce, the registrar shall proceed in accordance with the law on family registration.

### มาตรา ๑๙ In the case where there are problems or a need for knowledge and understanding concerning family matters, any person in the family may seek advice or guidance concerning family matters from the family promotion and protection center, CFDC, or government agency or private organization having objectives in promoting and developing families as appropriate. In such case, the person receiving such request shall provide appropriate advice or guidance for the benefit of promoting and developing families.

### มาตรา ๒๐ In the promotion and development of families, the Department of Women's Affairs and Family Development

shall coordinate with government agencies, state enterprises, or the private sector to provide rights, benefits, or special privileges to persons in families and announce such rights, benefits, or special privileges to the general public. In the case where any family experiences such hardship that it is unable to provide care and support for persons in the family, it may request assistance at the Family Promotion and Protection Center. The provision of assistance to families experiencing such hardship shall be in accordance with the criteria, procedures, and conditions prescribed by the Minister in regulations with the approval of the Cabinet.

### มาตรา ๒๑ Government agencies and state enterprises shall implement the policies, strategies, and

master plans for the promotion and development of families approved by the Cabinet under Section ๑๑ (๑) in order to promote and develop families for personnel under their jurisdiction. In the case where any employer or business establishment owner wishes to implement such policies, strategies, and master plans for the promotion and development of families to promote and develop families for personnel under their jurisdiction, they may request government agencies to consider providing assistance or support in terms of resources, academic matters, personnel development, or any other assistance or support. This shall be in accordance with the criteria, procedures, and conditions prescribed by the Minister in regulations upon the recommendation of the Committee.

## หมวด ๖

Welfare Protection

### มาตรา ๒๒ Welfare protection means action taken to ensure that persons in families receive

safety, have good relationships, and prevent repeated offenses, and shall include the following actions:

(๑) Protection or prevention of persons in families from being subjected to domestic violence and prevention of criminal offenses characterized as domestic violence

(๒) Promotion or support to prevent domestic violence

(๓) Mediation or action to achieve conciliation when domestic violence occurs in the family

(๔) Remedy or relief for those who have been subjected to domestic violence

(๕) Treatment and rehabilitation of persons involved in domestic violence

(๖) Follow-up care for persons in families when domestic violence occurs

### มาตรา ๒๓ Any person who witnesses or becomes aware of domestic violence shall report information

or news of such case to an official, the Social Assistance Center of the Ministry of Social Development and

Human Security, or report directly to the Family Promotion and Protection Center for welfare protection.

The report under paragraph one may be made orally, in writing, by telephone, by electronic means,

or by any other method. Such report, when made in good faith, shall be protected and shall not be subject to

civil, criminal, or administrative liability.

### มาตรา ๒๔ When a report has been made under Section ๒๓, paragraph one, the Family Promotion and Protection Center

shall verify the facts concerning the domestic violence as reported without delay in order to proceed with

welfare protection.

### มาตรา ๒๕ In the case where a complaint or accusation has been filed alleging a criminal offense which an inquiry

official considers to be characterized as domestic violence, the inquiry official shall send a copy of the record of the complaint or accusation to the Family Promotion and Protection Center located in that area without delay. The Family Promotion and Protection Center that receives such copy of the record of the complaint or accusation shall take action to provide welfare protection.

In the case where the inquiry official has not taken action under paragraph one, if the public prosecutor responsible for the case considers it to be a criminal case characterized as domestic violence, such public prosecutor shall notify the Family Promotion and Protection Center as well.

When notified under paragraph one or two, or when it appears to the Family Promotion and Protection

Center that criminal proceedings characterized as domestic violence are being conducted, in appropriate cases, the head of the Center may file a request with the court trying the criminal case during the trial proceedings to postpone judgment of that case until receiving a report on compliance with the welfare protection order of the court under Section ๒๙ or of the Family Promotion and Protection Center under Section ๓๗.

In the case where the court trying the criminal case receives a request under paragraph three and upon consideration finds that

the circumstances of the act constitute a serious danger and are not beneficial to the behavioral modification of the perpetrator of domestic violence, the court may render judgment without postponing judgment of that case.

### มาตรา ๒๖ In the case where the court trying the criminal case orders postponement of judgment,

the Family Promotion and Protection Center shall submit the welfare protection order and the results of compliance with the welfare protection

order to the court trying the criminal case without delay.

### มาตรา ๒๗ In the case where a child under eighteen years of age commits domestic violence or

is subjected to domestic violence, welfare protection shall be carried out with primary consideration for the best interests

of the child.

### มาตรา ๒๘ In the case where the Family Promotion and Protection Center deems it appropriate to provide welfare protection,

it shall have the power to file a petition for the court to issue a welfare protection order by applying the provisions of Chapter ๑๕ on Welfare Protection Case Proceedings of the Juvenile and Family Court and Juvenile and Family Case Procedure Act, B.E. ๒๕๕๓ mutatis mutandis to the consideration and issuance of welfare protection orders, and the Family Promotion and Protection Center shall be deemed an organization with the duty to protect family welfare under such Act.

### มาตรา ๒๙ The Family Promotion and Protection Center filing a petition for the court to issue a welfare protection order

may propose welfare protection measures to resolve domestic violence problems or to prevent repeated domestic violence for the court's consideration. Welfare protection measures may be any of the following actions:

(๑) Requiring the perpetrator of domestic violence to report to an official at specified intervals

(๒) Prohibiting the perpetrator of domestic violence from causing trouble or provoking in any manner, whether directly or indirectly, that would cause domestic violence

(๓) Prohibiting the perpetrator of domestic violence from consuming alcohol or any addictive substance for a specified period

(๔) Prohibiting the perpetrator of domestic violence from approaching or entering the residence of the victim of domestic violence, or requiring removal from the residence of the victim of domestic violence

(๕) Prohibiting the perpetrator of domestic violence from causing distress or annoyance to the victim of domestic violence, including threatening, stalking, tracking, photographing, telephoning, sending annoying messages by electronic media, or by any other method causing distress or annoyance, whether directly or indirectly

(๖) Requiring the perpetrator of domestic violence to receive psychosocial counseling or

treatment and rehabilitation at a medical facility or place with specialized expertise

(๗) Requiring the perpetrator of domestic violence to receive treatment and rehabilitation for alcohol addiction, gambling, any addictive substance, or any other behavior which is the cause of the violence at a medical facility or treatment center

(๘) Requiring the perpetrator of domestic violence to enter into a bond when there are reasonable grounds to believe that they may cause distress or annoyance under (๕) or cause harm resulting in danger to the victim of violence or persons in the family again, by having that person enter into a bond in an amount as the court deems appropriate that they will not cause harm again throughout the specified period, whether or not requiring bail deposit

(๙) Requiring the perpetrator of domestic violence to surrender any weapon of which they are the owner to the Family Promotion and Protection Center for custody until the behavior of the perpetrator of domestic violence changes, or if the perpetrator of domestic violence is merely in possession of such weapon, to surrender it to the Family Promotion and Protection Center, and the Family Promotion and Protection Center shall notify the owner who has ownership rights to retrieve such weapon

(๑๐) Requiring the perpetrator of domestic violence to compensate with financial assistance to relieve suffering of the victim of domestic violence or persons in families where domestic violence has occurred

(๑๑) Requiring the perpetrator of domestic violence to receive treatment, rehabilitation, or other care in addition to (๖) and (๗) as announced by the Ministry of Public Health

(๑๒) Requesting the court to order revocation of the parental authority of the perpetrator of domestic violence in whole or in part, or prohibiting the perpetrator of domestic violence from contacting that child whether directly or indirectly for a period as the court deems appropriate, in the case where the perpetrator of domestic violence has acted against a child for whom they have the duty to support and raise

(๑๓) Requesting the court to order revocation of guardianship or curatorship in the case where that person has committed domestic violence against the person under guardianship or curatorship

### มาตรา ๓๐ In the case where a petition is made to the court for a welfare protection order under the law on

the Juvenile and Family Court and Juvenile and Family Case Procedure by any person other than the Family Promotion and Protection Center, for the benefit of welfare protection, the head of the center may file a petition to the court requesting that the court issue an order appointing an official of the Family Promotion and Protection Center to provide opinions regarding welfare protection to the court before the court issues a welfare protection order. In the case where the head of the center has not filed a petition to the court but the court deems it beneficial for welfare protection, the court may issue an order for the Family Promotion and Protection Center to appoint an official of the Family Promotion and Protection Center to provide opinions regarding welfare protection to the court before the court issues a welfare protection order.

### มาตรา ๓๑ In the case where a petition is made to the court for welfare protection, if the court deems

that there is a necessity to provide protection or assistance to a victim of domestic violence or family members, the court may issue an order for the Family Promotion and Protection Center to provide protection or assistance to such persons.

### มาตรา ๓๒ In the case where the court issues a welfare protection order requiring a domestic violence perpetrator

to comply, the court may specify the commencement and termination period, provided that it shall not exceed one year, except for rehabilitation, remediation, or treatment of the physical or mental health of the domestic violence perpetrator. In the case where medical information is submitted for the court's consideration, the court may specify a period exceeding one year.

In the case where the court orders a domestic violence perpetrator to enter into a bond and there is a violation of the bond,

the provisions of Section 47 of the Criminal Code shall apply mutatis mutandis.

### มาตรา ๓๓ In the case where the court issues a welfare protection order under Section 29, the court may issue an order for

the Family Promotion and Protection Center officials to monitor and supervise the domestic violence perpetrator's compliance with the order and report to the court at such intervals as deemed appropriate, and may order the domestic violence perpetrator, victim of domestic violence, family members, or other relevant persons to appear before the court to inquire into the progress or compliance with the court's order.

### มาตรา ๓๔ Any person subject to a court's welfare protection order who intentionally violates or fails to comply

with the welfare protection order without reasonable cause, the court has the power to issue a warrant for the arrest and detention of such person until compliance with the order, but not exceeding one month. If such person is temporarily released, the court may impose conditions to be complied with during the temporary release.

### มาตรา ๓๕ In the case where it appears to the court itself, or from reports of the Family Promotion and Protection

Center officials, social workers, psychologists, or from petitions or statements of victims of domestic violence, domestic violence perpetrators, or family members that the circumstances concerning the domestic violence perpetrator or family relationships of such person have changed, the court may issue an order to terminate welfare protection before the expiration of the specified period or may issue an order to modify the welfare protection order by adding or reducing conditions or increasing or reducing the period of welfare protection.

In the case where the court issues an order to modify the welfare protection order which will have a greater impact on the domestic violence perpetrator, the court may inquire into the facts or opinions of the domestic violence perpetrator, or may require the domestic violence perpetrator to file a written statement with the court for use in the court's consideration before issuing an order to modify the welfare protection order.

### มาตรา ๓๖ In the case where domestic violence occurs and the court issues a welfare protection order

in conjunction with criminal proceedings being brought against the domestic violence perpetrator arising from domestic violence, if the domestic violence perpetrator has fully complied with the welfare protection order, the criminal offense shall have the following effects:

(๑) If the domestic violence constitutes an offense under Section 295, Section 379,

### Section 391, Section 392, Section 393, Section 397, and Section 398 of the Criminal Code,

such offense shall be a compoundable offense.

(๒) In criminal cases, the criminal court shall accept the results of compliance with the welfare protection order and take them into consideration as grounds for mercy in suspending punishment or suspending the determination of punishment under Section 56 or as grounds for mitigation of punishment under Section 78 of the Criminal Code.

(๓) Domestic violence that constitutes a criminal case where the offender committed the act because the offender was subjected to violence or subjected to wrongful acts repeatedly and continuously to the extent that it caused severe physical or mental trauma, and a statement has been filed for the consideration of the criminal court, the court may impose punishment less than that prescribed by law for such offense to any extent.

### มาตรา ๓๗ In urgent cases where delay would cause danger to the life, body, or mind

of family members and it is not possible to petition the court under Section 29 in time, the head of the center shall have the power to issue a temporary welfare protection order under Section 29 (3), (5), (6), and (9) to the extent necessary.

### มาตรา ๓๘ After a welfare protection order has been issued under Section 37, the head of the center shall

file a petition together with submitting such order to the court within forty-eight hours from the issuance of such order. If the court upon consideration approves, such welfare protection order shall continue to be in effect. In the case where the court does not approve such welfare protection order in whole or in part, or the facts or circumstances have changed, the court shall conduct an inquiry and issue an order without delay. The court may modify, amend, change, revoke such welfare protection order, or order otherwise as deemed appropriate.

### มาตรา ๓๙ When there is a notification under Section 23 or a complaint or accusation under the

Criminal Procedure Code, or the injured person files a criminal case having the nature of domestic violence, no person shall print, advertise, or disseminate to the public by any means any images, stories, or information that would cause damage to victims of domestic violence, domestic violence perpetrators, or family members.

The disclosure of information concerning victims of domestic violence, domestic violence perpetrators, or family members, in cases where it is for educational purposes or for the benefit of government or public benefit, if it is personal information, must obtain written consent from such person, and the disclosure must not specify names or identifying information indicating whose information it is if such disclosure would unreasonably invade personal privacy or would cause danger to the life, health, or safety of any person. Such disclosure shall not be permitted. Furthermore, the disclosure of such information must be carried out in accordance with the law on information.

(๓) ตรวจสอบ ประเมินสภาพปัญหาที่เกิดขึ้น รวบรวมข้อเท็จจริงและสาเหตุเกี่ยวกับการกระท า ความรุนแรงในครอบครัว รายงานความเห็นหรือผลการตรวจของผู้ประกอบวิชาชีพซึ่งเกี่ยวข้อง และ สรุปประมวลผล เพื่อให้หัวหน้าศูนย์พิจารณาออกค าสั่งคุ้มครองสวัสดิภาพ หรือเสนอต่อศาลเพื่อพิจารณา ออกค าสั่งคุ้มครองสวัสดิภาพ หรือด าเนินการอื่นใดตามพระราชบัญญัตินี้

(๔) ด าเนินการให้ผู้ถูกกระท าด้วยความรุนแรงในครอบครัวได้รับการช่วยเหลือและความคุ้มครอง ในเบื้องต้นเป็นการชั่วคราว ได้เข้ารับการตรวจรักษาตามมาตรฐานทางการแพทย์ ได้รับค าปรึกษาแนะน าจาก จิตแพทย์ นักจิตวิทยา นักสังคมสงเคราะห์ ตลอดจนไดร้ ับการเยียวยา บ าบัดฟื้นฟู หรือการช่วยเหลืออนื่ ใด ตามความเหมาะสม

(๕) ประสานงานกับเจ้าหน้าที่ฝ่ายปกครองหรือต ารวจ เจ้าหน้าที่ของสถานพยาบาล หรือ พนักงานเจ้าหน้าที่ตามกฎหมายอื่นที่เกี่ยวข้องกับการคุ้มครองสวัสดิภาพเพื่อด าเนินการตาม (๔)

(๖) ค้นเคหสถานหรือสถานที่เกิดเหตเุพื่อหาและช่วยเหลือผู้ถกู กระท าดว้ ยความรุนแรงในครอบครัว

(๗) จัดท ารายงานผลการปฏิบัติหน้าที่เสนอต่อหัวหน้าศูนย์เมื่อได้ด าเนินการเรื่องใดแล้วเสร็จ

(๘) ปฏิบัติหน้าที่อนื่ ใดเกี่ยวกบั การส่งเสริมและพัฒนาสถาบันครอบครัวหรือการคุ้มครองสวัสดภิ าพ ตามที่ศาลสั่งหรือหัวหน้าศูนย์มอบหมาย

การค้นตาม (๖) ต้องมีหมายค้น เว้นแต่มีเหตุอันควรเชื่อว่า หากเนิ่นช้ากว่าจะเอาหมายค้นมาได้ ผู้ถูกกระท าด้วยความรุนแรงในครอบครัวอาจถูกประทุษร้ายเป็นเหตุให้ได้รับอันตรายแก่ชีวิตหรือร่างกาย ให้ด าเนินการค้นได้โดยไม่ต้องมีหมายค้น แต่ต้องปฏิบัติตามประมวลกฎหมายวิธีพิจารณาความอาญา ว่าด้วยการค้น

ในการปฏิบัติหน้าที่ตาม (๑) (๔) และ (๖) ให้ผู้ซึ่งเกี่ยวข้องอ านวยความสะดวกตามสมควร

### มาตรา ๔๑ ในการปฏิบัติหน้าที่ตามพระราชบัญญัตินี้ พนักงานเจ้าหน้าที่ต้องแสดง

### มาตรา ๔๖ ผู้ใดถูกพิพากษาลงโทษจ าคุกและคดีถึงที่สุดก่อนวันที่พระราชบัญญัตินี้ใช้บังคับ

ซึ่งต้องรับโทษโดยมีเหตุตามที่บัญญัติไว้ในมาตรา ๓๖ (๓) ถ้าผู้นั้นยังไม่ได้รับโทษ หรืออยู่ในระหว่าง การรับโทษตามค าพิพากษา ผู้นั้นเอง บุคคลในครอบครัวของผู้นั้น หรือผู้ซึ่งได้รับมอบหมายจากบุคคล ดังกล่าว อาจยื่นค าร้องต่อศาลที่มีค าพิพากษาถึงที่สุด เพื่อขอให้ศาลด าเนินการไต่สวนและกา หนดโทษแก่ ผู้นั้นเสียใหม่ได้

### มาตรา ๔๗ บรรดากฎกระทรวง ระเบียบ หรือค าสั่งที่ออกตามพระราชบัญญัติคุ้มครอง

ผู้ถูกกระท าด้วยความรุนแรงในครอบครัว พ.ศ. ๒๕๕๐ ท่ีใช้บังคับอยู่ในวันก่อนวันที่พระราชบัญญัตินี้ ใช้บังคับ ให้ยังคงมีผลใช้บังคับได้ตอ่ ไปเท่าที่ไม่ขัดหรือแย้งกับพระราชบัญญตั ิน ี้ จนกว่าจะมีระเบียบหรอื ประกาศที่ออกตามพระราชบัญญัตินี้ใช้บังคับ
