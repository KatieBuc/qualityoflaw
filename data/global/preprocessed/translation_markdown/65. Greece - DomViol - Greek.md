# Law 3500/2006

Law 3500/2006

For combating domestic violence and other provisions.

### Article 1

CHAPTER A

GENERAL PROVISIONS

For the purposes of this law, the following are considered:

- 1. domestic violence, the commission of a punishable act against a family member, in accordance with Articles 6, 7, 8 and 9 of this law and Articles 299 and 311 of the Penal Code.
- 2. a. family or community consisting of spouses or parents and relatives of the first and second degree by blood or by affinity and their adopted children. b. the family includes, if they cohabit, relatives by blood or by affinity up to the fourth degree and persons whose guardian, judicial assistant or foster parent has been appointed as a member of the family, as well as any minor person cohabiting in the family. c. the provisions of this law also apply to the permanent female partner of a man or the permanent male partner of a woman and their children, common or of one of them, provided these persons cohabit, as well as to former spouses.
- 3. victim of domestic violence, every person of the preceding paragraph against whom a punishable act is committed under Articles 6, 7, 8 and 9 of this law. A victim is also the member in whose family a punishable act was committed under Articles 299 and 311 of the Penal Code, as well as the minor under paragraph 2, in whose presence one of the punishable acts of this law is committed.

### Article 2

The exercise of violence of any form between family members is prohibited.

### Article 3

CHAPTER B

CIVIL PROVISIONS

The second sentence of Article 1439 of the Civil Code is replaced.

### Article 4

In the case of exercise of physical violence against a minor as a means of discipline in the context of upbringing, Article 1532 of the Civil Code applies.

### Article 5

The monetary satisfaction under Article 932 of the Civil Code, due to moral damage to the victim for one of the acts of this law, may not be less than one thousand (1,000) euros, unless the victim himself requested a smaller amount.

### Article 6

CHAPTER C

CRIMINAL PROVISIONS

1. A family member who causes to another family member bodily harm or injury to his health, within the meaning of subparagraph a' of paragraph 1 of Article 308 of the Penal Code, or by continuous conduct causes very slight harm or injury to his health, within the meaning of subparagraph b' of the above provision, is punished by imprisonment of at least one year.

2. If the act of the first paragraph is capable of causing to the victim danger to his life or serious bodily injury, imprisonment of at least two years is imposed. If serious bodily or mental disease of the victim ensues, imprisonment up to ten years is imposed. If the perpetrator sought or knew and accepted the result of his act, he is punished by imprisonment for a term of years.

3. If the act of the first paragraph was committed against a pregnant woman or against a family member who, for any reason, is unable to resist, it is punished by imprisonment of at least two years and if the act was committed in the presence of a minor family member, it is punished by imprisonment of at least one year.

4. If the act of the first paragraph constitutes systematic infliction of intense physical pain or physical exhaustion, dangerous to health, or mental pain, capable of causing serious mental injury, especially by prolonged isolation of the victim, imprisonment for a term of years is imposed. If the victim is a minor, imprisonment for a term of years of at least ten years is imposed.

5. The provisions of the preceding paragraphs apply correspondingly when the perpetrator works for a social care provider, and his act is directed against a person who receives the services of that provider.

### Article 7

1. A family member who coerces another member using violence or threat of serious and imminent danger to an act, omission or tolerance without the victim being obliged to do so is punished by imprisonment of at least six months, regardless of whether the threatened harm is directed against the victim himself or one of his relatives within the meaning of subparagraph b' of Article 13 of the Penal Code.

2. A family member who causes terror or anxiety to another family member by threatening him with violence or other unlawful act or omission is punished by imprisonment.

### Article 8

1. Paragraph 1 of Article 336 of the Penal Code is replaced.

2. Paragraph 1 of Article 338 of the Penal Code is replaced.

### Article 9

1. A family member who offends the dignity of another family member with a particularly humiliating word or act relating to his sexual life is punished by imprisonment up to two years.

2. The act of the preceding paragraph is punished by imprisonment of at least six months up to three years if the victim is a minor.

3. The provisions of the preceding paragraphs apply correspondingly when the perpetrator works for a social care provider, and his act is directed against a person who receives the services of that provider.

### Article 10

Whoever in a case of domestic violence threatens a witness or a member of his family or exercises violence against him or bribes him, with the aim of obstructing the administration of justice, is punished by imprisonment of at least three months up to three years.

### Article 11

CHAPTER D

CRIMINAL MEDIATION

1. In misdemeanors of domestic violence, the prosecutor competent to exercise criminal prosecution examines the possibility of mediation according to the procedure of the following articles.

2. A precondition for the commencement of the criminal mediation procedure is the submission of an unconditional declaration by the person to whom the commission of the crime is attributed, that he is willing cumulatively: a) to promise that he will not commit in the future any act of domestic violence (word of honor) and that, in case of cohabitation, he agrees to remain outside the family residence for a reasonable period of time, if the victim proposes it. For this promise, a report is drawn up according to Articles 148 et seq. of the Code of Criminal Procedure. b) to attend a special counseling - therapeutic program for addressing domestic violence at a public provider, in whatever place and for whatever period of time is deemed necessary by the competent therapists. The program supervisor certifies the completion of attendance. The relevant certificate is attached to the case file. It states in detail the subject of the counseling - therapeutic program and the number of sessions attended by the person concerned. c) to remove or restore, if possible, immediately the consequences caused by the act and to pay reasonable monetary satisfaction to the victim.

3. If the victim of domestic violence is a minor, the criminal mediation is carried out on his behalf and jointly by the locally competent prosecutor for minors and the person exercising custody, provided the latter is not the same person as the alleged perpetrator of the crime. If there is no unanimity, mediation is not possible. The minor who has completed the fourteenth year of his age may, if he wishes, be present at it and be heard. The persons of the first sentence represent the minor in the criminal mediation procedure also for civil claims.

4. The provisions of this law relating to criminal mediation do not apply if the alleged perpetrator of the act of domestic violence is the guardian, judicial assistant or foster parent of the minor.

5. If the act of domestic violence constituting a misdemeanor is alleged to have been committed by a minor, Article 45A of the Code of Criminal Procedure applies.

### Article 12

1. If the procedure of Articles 417 et seq. of the Code of Criminal Procedure is initiated against the perpetrator, criminal mediation is permitted only if the court postpones the trial of the case pursuant to the provisions of Article 423 of the Code of Criminal Procedure. In this case, the relevant procedure takes place pursuant to paragraphs 3 to 6 of this Article. The court that postpones the trial of the case, pursuant to the first sentence, examines ex officio whether there is a case for imposing restrictive conditions on the perpetrator pursuant to Article 18 of this law.

2. If a preliminary investigation is conducted against the alleged perpetrator, the prosecutor, before any other action: a) may order the conduct of a medical expert examination on the alleged victim, in order to investigate the validity of the complaint regarding the commission of the act against them, b) examines himself every witness proposed, as well as family members or orders their examination by the competent investigative officers, and c) summons the person to whom the commission of the act is attributed to provide explanations to him or to the competent investigative officer under the terms of Article 31 paragraph 2 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

3. If the person providing explanations does not submit himself, or through his lawyer, the declaration concerning criminal mediation pursuant to paragraph 2 of Article 11, he is summoned to do so by the competent prosecutor. In this case, he may be given a time limit of three days to respond.

4. If the response of the person providing explanations is negative or he does not respond, criminal proceedings are initiated pursuant to the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure. If the response of the person providing explanations is positive, the prosecutor informs the victim or his lawyer of the interested party's declaration as above and, if a relevant request is submitted, the victim is given a time limit of up to three days to declare whether he accepts mediation.

5. If the victim's response is negative or he does not respond or no agreement is reached regarding the terms of case a' of paragraph 2 of Article 11, criminal proceedings are initiated pursuant to the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure. If the victim's response is positive, the prosecutor by his order places the case file in a special archive of the prosecutor's office. No appeal lies against this order.

6. If the persons to whom the commission of the act is attributed are more than one, agreement among them is required for the commencement of the criminal mediation procedure. The same applies if the alleged act concerns more than one victim. If no agreement is reached pursuant to the previous sentences, mediation is not possible.

7. The agreement of the parties to the proceedings for the commencement of the criminal mediation procedure pursuant to paragraph 2 of Article 11 of this law may also be submitted to the competent prosecutor by means of a relevant record by their lawyers.

### Article 13

1. The prosecutor's order issued following criminal mediation is registered in a special section of the criminal record certificate and is kept for a period equal to the statute of limitations for the crime to which it relates as provided by law.

2. If the interested party complies with the terms of the criminal mediation for a period of three years, then the relevant procedure is completed and the state's criminal claim for the crime concerned is extinguished.

3. The culpable non-completion of the criminal mediation as determined by the prosecutor interrupts the procedure and causes the retroactive lifting of the effects that have occurred. In this case, the prosecutor retrieves the case file from the archive, and the criminal proceedings continue pursuant to the relevant provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure, without the submission of a new request for criminal mediation being permitted any longer.

4. While the criminal mediation procedure is ongoing, the act to which it relates is pending trial. The initiation of criminal prosecution for an act for which the state's criminal claim has been extinguished due to completion of the criminal mediation procedure is inadmissible. The statute of limitations for the act is suspended until the completion of the criminal mediation procedure.

5. The refusal of one of the parties to accept mediation or the failure to complete it, for any reason, do not entail any negative substantive or procedural consequence against them in the criminal trial that follows.

6. A case c' is added to paragraph 3 of Article 574 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

### Article 14

1. The agreement of the parties for the commencement of the criminal mediation procedure is valid as a settlement regarding the monetary claims arising from the crime of domestic violence. The mere agreement of the victim spouse for the commencement of the procedure does not prevent the filing of a divorce action or the submission of an application for consensual dissolution of marriage, the progress of the trial and the dissolution of the marriage.

2. Non-compliance by the alleged perpetrator with the terms of the criminal mediation within three years from the archiving of the case gives the victim of the crime of domestic violence the right to seek, by his action, the reversal of the agreement, as regards the monetary claims. With the filing of the action for reversal, the victim's monetary claims are revived, and the amounts paid pursuant to the agreement are reclaimed pursuant to the provisions on unjust enrichment.

3. After the completion of the criminal mediation, reversal of the agreement is excluded for any reason and the reclaiming of amounts paid in compliance with it. The same effects are produced by the dissolution of the marriage between the spouses within the three-year period.

### Article 15

CHAPTER E

PROCEDURAL PROVISIONS

A sentence is added at the end of Article 735 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

### Article 16

If the acts of Articles 6, 7 and 9 of this law are directed against a minor, the commencement of the statute of limitations period is suspended until he comes of age.

### Article 17

1. Criminal prosecution for the crimes of Articles 6, 7, 9 and 10 is exercised ex officio.

2. Against the perpetrator, the procedure of Articles 417 et seq. of the Code of Criminal Procedure is applied.

### Article 18

1. In case of commission of a crime of domestic violence, it is possible, if under the specific circumstances it is deemed necessary for the protection of the physical and mental health of the victim, to impose on the accused by the competent criminal court to which he is referred for trial or by the competent examining magistrate or by the judicial council and for as long as required, the restrictive condition of his removal from the family home, his relocation, the prohibition to approach the victim's places of residence and/or work, residences of close relatives, children's educational institutions and shelters. The validity of the above restrictive condition ceases automatically after the issuance of a final judgment or the prosecutor's order archiving the case due to criminal mediation.

2. The restrictive condition imposed in accordance with the provisions of the previous paragraph may be revoked, replaced or modified by the competent judicial authority that imposed it, upon application by the accused, stating the reasons for which the revocation, replacement or modification is required. For the hearing of the application, the victim is mandatorily summoned, in accordance with the provisions in force at the time.

3. The judicial authority competent pursuant to paragraph 1 for the imposition, revocation, replacement or modification of the restrictive conditions may request, in an advisory capacity, the opinion of psychiatrists, psychologists, social workers and other scientists with special knowledge in matters of domestic violence, provided that these persons work in a public health institution.

### Article 19

1. In cases of domestic violence, family members are examined as witnesses without oath.

2. Minors in the adjudication of cases of the previous paragraph are not summoned as witnesses in the courtroom, but their testimony is read, if it exists, unless their examination is deemed necessary by the court.

### Article 20

1. In case of commission of a domestic violence crime, the competent police authorities conducting a preliminary investigation, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 243 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, are prohibited from announcing in any way the name and surname of the victim and the accused, their residence address, as well as any other data that may reveal their identity.

2. Violators of this provision are punished with imprisonment of up to two years.

### Article 21

CHAPTER VI

ASSISTANCE TO VICTIMS

1. Victims of domestic violence are entitled to moral support and necessary material assistance from legal entities of public or private law, operating specifically for these purposes under the supervision of the Ministry of Health and Social Solidarity, and from social services of local government organizations.

2. Police authorities dealing with, within the scope of their competencies, cases of domestic violence are obliged, if requested by the victim, to inform the victim and the above entities, so that the necessary assistance, as the case may be, is provided immediately.

### Article 22

Victims of domestic violence who request the taking of precautionary measures for temporary settlement of the situation, due to the specific incident, are granted the benefit of poverty with only the proof of the incident of violence, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 194 et seq. of the Code of Civil Procedure, if they are unable to pay, even temporarily, the required judicial costs.

### Article 23

1. A teacher of primary or secondary education who, during the execution of their educational work, in any way becomes aware or ascertains that a domestic violence crime has been committed against a student, informs, without delay, the director of the school unit. The director of the school unit announces, immediately, the punishable act to the competent prosecutor, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 37 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, or to the nearest police authority. Teachers and directors of private schools, as well as those responsible for Preschool Education Units of any kind, have the same obligation.

2. During the preliminary proceedings and the procedure in the courtroom, the director of the school unit, who announced the punishable act to the above competent authorities, and the teacher, who became aware of it or ascertained it, are called to be examined as witnesses, only if the information is not proven by any other means of evidence.

### Article 24

Article 342 of the Penal Code (abuse of minors for indecency) is replaced.

### Article 25

The deadlines of paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 3 and Article 4 VI of Law 3388/2005, which were extended by the joint ministerial decision no. 99583 ec./24.12.2005 (Official Gazette 1490 B') of the Ministers of Justice, Economy and Finance and Public Order, are extended respectively until the external guards are promoted to the rank of Deputy Guard and until 12.9.2007.

### Article 26

Paragraph 4 of Article 49 of Law 2721/1999, as replaced by paragraph 2 of Article 1 of Law 3388/2005 and supplemented by Article 18 of Law 3472/2006, is replaced.

### Article 27

The last sentence of paragraph 6 of Article 49 of Law 2721/1999 (Official Gazette 112 A'), as replaced by paragraph 2 of Article 1 of Law 3388/2005 (Official Gazette 225 A'), is replaced.

### Article 28

The validity of this law begins three months after its publication in the

Government Gazette.
