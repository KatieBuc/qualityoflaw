Basis and limitation of criminal law coercion

## Article 1.

Criminal offences and criminal law sanctions are prescribed only for those behaviours which violate or endanger personal freedoms and human rights and other rights and social values guaranteed and protected by the Constitution of the Republic of Croatia and international law to such an extent that their protection could not be achieved without criminal law coercion.

Principle of legality

## Article 2.

No one may be punished for an act which, before it was committed, was not established by law or international law as a criminal offence, nor may a punishment or other criminal law sanction be imposed that was not prescribed by law.

Application of criminal legislation to young persons

## Article 7.

(1) Criminal legislation shall not be applied to a child who has not reached the age of fourteen at the time of commission of the act.

(2) This Code shall apply to a person who has reached the age of fourteen but has not yet reached the age of twenty-one at the time of commission of the criminal offence, unless otherwise provided by a special law.

Time of commission of a criminal offence

## Article 8.

(1) A criminal offence is committed at the time when the perpetrator undertook the act or was obliged to undertake the act, regardless of when the consequence from the statutory description of the criminal offence occurred.

CASE LAW: Judgment

(2) If the perpetrator's activity consists of several temporally separated acts, the offence is committed on the day of the last act, and in the case of criminal offences where the act is continuous, on the day the act ceases.

CASE LAW: Decision, Judgment, Judgment

Place of commission of a criminal offence

## Article 9.

(1) A criminal offence is committed in the place where the perpetrator acted or was obliged to act and in the place where the consequence from the statutory description of the criminal offence occurred in whole or in part, or was supposed to occur according to his intent.

(2) In cases of participation, a criminal offence is committed in the place specified in paragraph 1. of this Article and in the place where any of the participants acted or was obliged to act or where, according to his intent, the consequence from the statutory description of the criminal offence was supposed to occur.

Application of criminal legislation for criminal offences committed in the territory of the Republic of Croatia

## Article 10.

The criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall apply to anyone who commits a criminal offence in its territory. Application of criminal legislation for criminal offences committed on a vessel or aircraft of the Republic of Croatia

## Article 11.

The criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall also apply to anyone who commits a criminal offence on a domestic vessel or aircraft, regardless of where the vessel or aircraft is located at the time of commission of the criminal offence.

Special provisions regarding the initiation of criminal proceedings for criminal offences committed in the territory of the Republic of Croatia, on its vessel or aircraft

When, in the case of application of the criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia according to the provisions of Article 10. and Article 11. of this Code, criminal proceedings have been finally concluded in a foreign state, criminal proceedings in the Republic of Croatia shall be initiated with the approval of the Chief State Attorney.

Application of criminal legislation for criminal offences committed outside the Republic of Croatia against its legal interest

## Article 13.

The criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall apply to anyone who commits outside its territory:

- 1. a criminal offence against the Republic of Croatia from Chapter XXXII. of this Code,
- 2. a criminal offence of counterfeiting money, securities and value tokens of the Republic of Croatia from Article 274., Article 275. and Article 276. of this Code,
- 3. a criminal offence against a Croatian state official or civil servant in connection with his office,
- 4. a criminal offence of giving false testimony from Article 305. of this Code, if the false testimony was given in proceedings before Croatian competent authorities,
- 5. criminal offences against suffrage from Chapter XXXI. of this Code,
- 6. a criminal offence from Article 193., Article 194., Article 196., Article 197. and Article 198. of this Code when committed in the protected ecological and fisheries zone, continental shelf or on the high seas.

Application of criminal legislation for criminal offences committed outside the territory of the Republic of Croatia by its nationals

(1) The criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall apply to a Croatian national and a person who has residence in the Republic of Croatia who commits outside the territory of the Republic of Croatia any other criminal offence except those covered by the provisions of Article 13. and Article 16. of this Code, if that criminal offence is punishable under the law of the state in which it was committed.

(2) The provision of paragraph 1. of this Article shall also apply when the perpetrator acquires Croatian nationality after the commission of the criminal offence.

(3) In the cases referred to in paragraphs 1. and 2. of this Article, for criminal offences from Article 115. paragraphs 3. and 4., Article 116., Article 153., Article 154., Article 158., Article 159., Article 161., Article 162., Article 163., Article 164., Article 166. and Article 169. of this Code and other criminal offences for which this is provided by an international treaty to which the Republic of Croatia is a party, the criminal legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall apply even when the criminal offence is not punishable under the law of the state in which it was committed.

(4) When Croatian nationals participate in peacekeeping operations or other international activities outside the territory of the Republic of Croatia and commit a criminal offence in such operations or activities, the application of the legislation of the Republic of Croatia shall be governed by the provisions of this Code, unless otherwise provided by an international treaty to which the Republic of Croatia is a party.

Manner of committing a criminal offense

## Članak 20.

(1) A criminal offense may be committed by action or omission.

High treason

## Article 340.

Whoever by use of force or threat of use of force or in another unlawful manner endangers the territorial integrity or constitutional order of the Republic of Croatia, shall be punished with imprisonment of at least five years.

Recognition of occupation and capitulation

## Article 341.

Whoever signs or recognizes a capitulation or whoever accepts or recognizes the occupation of the Republic of Croatia or part of its state territory, shall be punished with imprisonment of at least five years.

Preventing fight against the enemy

## Article 342.

(1) A citizen of the Republic of Croatia who during war or armed conflict in which the Republic of Croatia also participates prevents citizens of the Republic of Croatia or its allies from fighting against the enemy, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) A citizen of the Republic of Croatia within the meaning of this chapter is also a foreigner with residence in the Republic of Croatia.

Service in enemy army

## Article 343.

(1) A citizen of the Republic of Croatia who during war or armed conflict in which the Republic of Croatia participates serves in the enemy army or is in other armed groups acting in hostile combat against the Republic of Croatia or its allies, shall be punished with imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(2) The penalty from paragraph 1. of this Article shall be imposed on whoever incites, collects, takes away, or otherwise recruits citizens of the Republic of Croatia for service in the enemy army, or armed groups acting in hostile combat against the Republic of Croatia or its allies or for participation in war or armed conflict against the Republic of Croatia or its allies.

Aiding the enemy

## Article 344.

A citizen of the Republic of Croatia who during war or armed conflict in which the Republic of Croatia participates aids the enemy in implementing coercive measures against citizens or who politically or economically cooperates with the enemy, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to ten years.

Undermining the military and defense power of the state

## Članak 345. (NN 144/12)

Whoever destroys, renders unusable, or enables defensive installations, facilities, positions, armaments or other military and defense resources to fall into enemy hands, or surrenders troops to the enemy, or otherwise obstructs or endangers military and defense measures and power of the Republic of Croatia, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years. Coercion against the highest state officials of the Republic of Croatia

(2) If by the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article the life or body of a person from paragraph 1 of this Article has been endangered, or if bodily injury has been inflicted on them, or if a weapon or dangerous tool has been used, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to twelve years.

Disclosure of secret information

## Članak 347. (NN 144/12)

(3) Whoever commits the act from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article out of greed shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(4) Whoever commits the criminal offense from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article during wartime or imminent war danger shall be punished by imprisonment from three to twelve years.

(5) Whoever commits the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article through negligence shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years

## Članak 352. (NN 144/12)

(1) Whoever kills a person under international protection shall be punished by imprisonment of at least ten years or long-term imprisonment.

Abduction of a person under international protection

## Članak 353.

Attack on a person under international protection

## Članak 354.

(1) Whoever commits violence against a person under international protection or attacks their official premises, private residence or means of transport, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(2) If by the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article the death of a person from paragraph 1 of this Article has been caused, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

Threat to a person under international protection

## Članak 355.

Whoever endangers the safety of a person who is under international protection by a serious threat that they will commit a criminal offense from Articles 352 to 354 of this Act against them, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years. Violation of the reputation of a foreign state and international organization

## Članak 356.

(1) Whoever publicly exposes a foreign state, its flag, coat of arms, anthem to ridicule, contempt or gross disparagement, shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.

(2) By the punishment from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be punished whoever exposes the United Nations, the European Union, the Council of Europe, the International Red Cross or another recognized international organization to ridicule, contempt or gross disparagement.

(3) The initiation of criminal proceedings is undertaken on the basis of authorization from the State Attorney of the Republic of Croatia who may give such authorization after obtaining the consent of the state, international organization or person against whom the criminal offense was committed.

Non-execution and refusal to execute orders

## Članak 357.

(1) A military person who does not execute or refuses to execute an order of a superior in connection with service, thereby seriously endangering the service, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.

(2) If the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article has been committed during wartime or imminent threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Refusal to receive and abuse of weapons

## Članak 358.

(1) A military person who refuses to receive a weapon or refuses to use it shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.

(2) By the punishment from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be punished a military person who uses a weapon contrary to an order or rules of service, if thereby they have not committed another more serious criminal offense.

(3) If the criminal offense from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article has been committed during wartime or imminent threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(4) There is no criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article when it concerns a military person whose conscientious objection has been recognized in the prescribed procedure.

Resistance to a guard or sentries

## Article 359.

(1) A military person who resists a guard, watch or other military person in similar service, while they are performing their official duty, and a military person who does not obey their call or does not execute or refuses to execute their command, and thereby seriously endangers the service, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) If the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed during a state of war or direct threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Refusal to obey

## Article 360.

(1) A military person who together with other military persons resists the command of a superior in connection with service or does not execute it or refuses to execute it (Article 357), applies force or threat against a military person (Article 314) or attacks a military person in the performance of official duty (Article 315), shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(2) If the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed using weapons, in an organized manner or during a state of war or direct threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(3) If the criminal offense from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article caused the death of another person, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(4) If a participant from paragraph 1 of this Article obeys the command of a superior and abandons the commission of any of the criminal offenses described in paragraph 1 of this Article, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

Milder punishment for criminal offenses from Article 357 and Article 360.

## Article 361.

If the perpetrator of the criminal offense from Article 357 paragraph 1 and Article 360 paragraph 1 of this Law was provoked by unlawful or rough treatment by the military person against whom the criminal offense was committed, they may be released from punishment.

Abuse and humiliation of a subordinate or junior in service

## Article 362.

(1) A military person who in service or in connection with service abuses a subordinate or junior in service or treats them in a humiliating manner or maliciously makes their service difficult, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(3) If the criminal offense from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article caused harm to the health of the abused person or if the criminal offense from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article was committed against multiple persons, or the perpetrator persistently repeats it, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Breach of guard, internal or other similar service

## Article 363.

(1) A military person who acts contrary to regulations on guard or internal, or other similar service, and thereby seriously endangers the service, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) If the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed at a warehouse of weapons, ammunition or mine-explosive devices, or at another facility particularly important for defense or if it caused serious bodily injury or large-scale property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(3) If the criminal offense from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article was committed during a state of war or direct threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years. Submission of false reports and failure to report

## Article 364.

(1) A military person who in the performance of duty does not submit a report or in a report conceals a fact which they should not conceal or submits a report of false content and thereby endangers the service, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) If the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed during a state of war or direct threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(3) A military person who during a state of war or direct threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia does not inform a superior, senior or military command about an event that obviously requires immediate undertaking of military measures, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.

(4) For an attempt of the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article, the perpetrator shall be punished. Failure to take measures for the protection of military unit

## Article 365.

(1) A military person who does not take prescribed, commanded or other obviously necessary measures for preserving the life and health of people entrusted to them, for securing and maintaining in proper condition facilities, objects and means serving combat readiness, for proper supply of food, equipment and material to the unit entrusted to them, for safekeeping and care of official animals, or for timely and proper completion of security works or securing of facilities entrusted to them, and thereby causes danger to the life or body of people, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) If the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article caused serious bodily injury to a person or significant property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(3) If the criminal offense from paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article was committed during a state of war or direct threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(4) If the criminal offense from paragraph 2 of this Article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years, and if the criminal offense from paragraph 3 of this Article was committed through negligence by imprisonment from one to eight years.

Failure to secure during military exercises

## Article 366.

(1) Whoever during a military exercise, training or in the performance of experiments does not take prescribed, commanded or obviously necessary security or precautionary measures, and thereby causes danger to the life or body of people, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) If the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article caused serious bodily injury to a person, or significant property damage, or if the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed during a state of war or direct threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(3) If the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.

(4) If the criminal offense from paragraph 3 of this Article caused serious bodily injury to a person, or significant property damage, or if the criminal offense from paragraph 3 of this Article was committed during a state of war or direct threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years. Failure to respond to call and evasion of military service

## Article 367.

(1) Whoever during a state of war or direct threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia without justified reason does not arrive at the designated time to fulfill military obligation although they were called by individual or general call, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) Whoever calls or incites multiple persons to commit the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(3) The perpetrator of the criminal offense from paragraph 1 of this Article who voluntarily reports to the competent state body to fulfill military obligation may be released from punishment.

Evasion of military obligation by incapacitation or fraud

## Article 368.

(1) Whoever, with the aim that he or another avoids military duty or is assigned to lighter duty, injures himself or another with his consent or otherwise incapacitates himself or another for military service or any duty, or allows another to incapacitate him for military service or any duty, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) The punishment from paragraph 1. of this Article shall be imposed on whoever, with the aim that he or another avoids military duty or is assigned to lighter duty, simulates illness, uses a false document for himself or another, or acts in another fraudulent manner.

(3) Whoever commits the act from paragraph 1. of this Article in time of war or imminent threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Unlawful Exemption from Military Service

## Article 369.

Whoever, by abuse of his position or authority, causes a military person or a person subject to military duty to be exempted from duty or to be assigned to lighter duty in time of war or imminent threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

Improper and Negligent Handling of Entrusted Weapons and Military Equipment and Other Means for Defense Needs

## Article 370.

(1) Whoever improperly or negligently keeps, stores or handles entrusted weapons, military equipment and other means for defense needs, thereby causing their damage to a greater extent, destruction or loss, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) A warehouse manager of the means from paragraph 1. of this Article who does not undertake measures for their security or maintenance, thereby causing damage, destruction or loss of those means, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(3) If the criminal offense from paragraph 2. of this Article has caused considerable property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(4) If the criminal offense from paragraph 2. of this Article has been committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(5) If the criminal offense from paragraph 4. of this Article has caused the consequence from paragraph 3. of this Article, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Unauthorized Entry into Military Facilities and Making Sketches or Drawings of Military Facilities and Combat Equipment

## Article 371.

(1) Whoever in time of war or imminent threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, for the purpose of reconnaissance, enters a military facility without authorization, although he knows that access is prohibited, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) Whoever in time of war or imminent threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia makes sketches or drawings of military facilities or combat equipment without authorization, or photographs them, or records them in another manner, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Crossing Over and Surrender to the Enemy

## Article 372.

(1) A military person who during war or armed conflict crosses over to the ranks of the enemy, or the opposing side, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least five years.

(2) A military person who during war surrenders to the enemy, or the opposing side, before exhausting all possibilities of defense, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

Failure to Perform or Abandonment of Duty During Combat Operations

## Article 373.

(1) A military person who during or immediately before combat operations does not perform his duty, thereby causing harmful consequences for the military unit or for the combat situation, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(2) The punishment from paragraph 1. of this Article shall be imposed on a military person who during or immediately before combat operations does not perform his duty or arbitrarily or fraudulently abandons his duty.

Abandoning Position Contrary to Orders

## Article 374.

A military person who during or immediately before combat operations abandons a position with the unit entrusted to him contrary to orders before exhausting all possibilities of defense, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years. Abandoning Damaged Ship or Aircraft Prematurely

## Article 375.

The commander of a warship or a member of the crew of a warship or military aircraft who during war or armed conflict abandons a damaged ship or aircraft before performing his duty according to the regulations on ship service, or according to the regulations on flight and use of aircraft, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years. Leaving Undamaged Combat Equipment to the Enemy

## Article 376.

(1) A military person who allows a substantially undamaged military warehouse, ship, aircraft, tank, or other similar combat equipment to fall into the hands of the enemy, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) The punishment from paragraph 1. of this Article shall be imposed on whoever allows substantially undamaged installations or other facilities important for defense to fall into the hands of the enemy contrary to orders.

Weakening Combat Morale and Combat Situation

## Article 377.

(1) A military person who during or immediately before combat operations, by throwing away weapons or ammunition, spreading fear, creating disorder or confusion, or in another manner, weakens the combat morale of the unit, combat readiness or causes harm to the combat situation, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to twelve years.

(2) A military person who does not undertake necessary measures against a subordinate who during or immediately before combat operations spreads fear among soldiers, creates disorder or confusion in the unit, or in another manner weakens the combat morale of the unit or causes harm to the combat situation, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years. Failure to Fulfill Material Obligation

## Article 378.

(1) Whoever in time of war or imminent threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, contrary to the obligation established by law, without justified reason, does not respond or opposes the registration or inspection of personnel or the registration or inspection of material means that are on the list of material means for defense needs, or whoever during that registration or inspection gives inaccurate statements or information, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) The punishment from paragraph 1. of this Article shall be imposed on whoever in time of war or imminent threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, contrary to the obligation established by law, without justified reason, does not make available at the specified time and in the prescribed condition to the body competent for defense affairs the material means that are on the list of material means for defense needs.

Failure to Fulfill Duties in the Implementation of Mobilization

## Article 379.

(1) A military or official person who in the implementation of mobilization in time of war or imminent threat to the independence and unity of the Republic of Croatia, contrary to his duty, does not ensure the reception, deployment and accommodation or supply of mobilized personnel, facilities, vehicles, machinery, equipment and other means that are on the list of material means for defense needs, thereby causing severe harmful consequences, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) If the criminal offense from paragraph 1. of this Article has been committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years. Responsibility for Criminal Offense Committed on the Order of a Superior

## Article 380.

A military person who commits an unlawful act which fulfills the elements of a criminal offense on orders is guilty only if he knew or it was obvious that by executing the order he was committing an unlawful act. Criminal offenses from Articles 88. and 90. of this Law are always obviously unlawful.
