# OFFICIAL GAZETTE OF THE BOLIVARIAN REPUBLIC OF VENEZUELA

OFFICIAL GAZETTE OF THE BOLIVARIAN REPUBLIC OF VENEZUELA

Caracas, Monday April 23, 2007 No. 38.668

THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY

OF THE BOLIVARIAN REPUBLIC OF VENEZUELA

DECREES

the following,

ORGANIC LAW ON THE RIGHT OF WOMEN TO A LIFE

FREE OF VIOLENCE

## CHAPTER I: GENERAL PROVISIONS

Purpose

#### Article 1.- The purpose of this Law is to guarantee and promote the

right of women to a life free of violence, creating conditions to prevent, address, punish and eradicate violence against women in any of its manifestations and spheres, promoting changes in the sociocultural patterns that sustain gender inequality and power relations over women, in order to favor the construction of a just, democratic, participatory, equal and leading society.

Guiding principles

#### Article 2.- Through this Law, a comprehensive set of

measures is articulated to achieve the following purposes:

- 1. To guarantee all women the effective exercise of their enforceable rights before the organs and entities of the Public Administration, and to ensure rapid, transparent and effective access to the services established for that purpose.
- 2. To strengthen public policies for the prevention of violence against women and the eradication of gender discrimination. To this end, the Public Powers will be provided with effective instruments in the educational, labor, social services, health, advertising and media spheres.
- 3. To strengthen the current criminal and procedural framework to ensure comprehensive protection for women victims of violence from the jurisdictional instances.
- 4. To coordinate the budgetary and institutional resources of the different Public Powers to ensure the attention, prevention and eradication of acts of violence against women, as well as the appropriate punishment of those responsible for them and the implementation of socio-educational measures that prevent their recurrence.
- 5. To promote the participation and collaboration of entities, associations and organizations that act against violence toward women.
- 6. To guarantee the principle of mainstreaming of awareness, prevention, detection, security and protection measures, so that in their application the specific rights, needs and demands of all women victims of gender violence are taken into account.
- 7. To promote the specialization and awareness of professional groups that participate in the process of information, attention and protection of women victims of gender violence.
- 8. To guarantee the economic, professional, technological, scientific and any other nature resources that allow the sustainability of plans, projects, programs, actions, missions and any other initiative aimed at the prevention, punishment and eradication of violence against women and the full exercise of their rights.
- 9. To establish and strengthen security and protection measures, and precautionary measures that guarantee the rights protected in this Law and the personal, physical, emotional, labor and property protection of women victims of gender violence.

10.To establish a comprehensive system of guarantees for the exercise of the rights developed in this Law. Protected rights

#### Article 3.- This Law encompasses the protection of the following rights:

1. The right to life.

2. The protection of the dignity and physical, psychological, sexual, property and legal integrity of women victims of violence, in the public and private spheres.

3. The equality of rights between men and women.

4. The protection of women particularly vulnerable to gender-based violence.

5. The right of women victims of violence to receive full information and advice appropriate to their personal situation, through the services, agencies or offices that the Public Administration, National, State and Municipal, are obliged to create. Such information shall include the measures contemplated in this Law relating to their protection and security, and the rights and assistance provided therein, as well as that referring to the place of provision of care, emergency, support and comprehensive recovery services.

6. The others enshrined in the Constitution of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela and in all international agreements and treaties on the matter, signed by the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela, such as the Law Approving the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW) and the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women (Convention of Belem do Pará).

## CHAPTER II: ON THE GUARANTEES FOR THE EXERCISE OF RIGHTS

On the guarantees

#### Article 4.- All women regardless of their nationality,

ethnic origin, religion or any other personal, legal or social condition or circumstance, shall have access to the necessary mechanisms to enforce the rights recognized in this Law:

- 1. Information, comprehensive social assistance and legal assistance to women in situations of gender-based violence are the responsibility of the Venezuelan State.
- 2. In the case of women belonging to especially vulnerable groups, the National Women's Institute, as well as the regional and municipal institutes, must ensure that the information provided to them is offered in an accessible and understandable format, ensuring the use of Spanish and indigenous languages, other modalities or communication options, including alternative and augmentative systems. In short, the necessary means shall be arranged so that women in situations of gender-based violence who, due to their personal and social circumstances, may have greater difficulty in accessing comprehensive information, have guaranteed effective exercise of this right.
- 3. Women victims of gender-based violence have the right to social services of care, emergency, protection, support and shelter, and comprehensive recovery. Such services shall be created in each state and municipality, charged to the annual budget. The care provided by such services must be: permanent, urgent, specialized and professionally multidisciplinary and they shall be financed by the State.
- 4. The services listed in the preceding paragraph shall act in coordination and collaboration with the citizen security bodies, judges, prosecutors, health services and the National Office of the Ombudsman for Women's Rights.

Children and adolescents who are under the parental authority or care responsibility of women victims of violence shall also be entitled to comprehensive social assistance through these social services.

5. The governing body for public policies directed toward women, the regional and municipal women's institutes, as well as other organizations, associations or community forms that fight for women's rights, shall guide and evaluate the plans, projects, programs and actions that are executed, and shall issue recommendations for their improvement and effectiveness.

6. The Office of the Ombudsman, the National Women's Institute and the state, metropolitan and municipal institutes shall ensure the correct application of this Law and instruments consistent with it. It shall correspond to the National Office of the Ombudsman for Women's Rights and the state, metropolitan and municipal ombudsman's offices to ensure respect for and effective exercise of the right to justice of women victims of gender-based violence who prove insufficient resources to litigate, with these having the right to judicial and extrajudicial representation, and to be provided with the necessary legal counsel to guarantee the effectiveness of the rights enshrined herein. This right shall also apply to the legal successors in the event of the death of the assaulted woman.

7. The bar associations, medical associations, associations of psychologists, associations of nurses in the different states and metropolitan districts, must establish free comprehensive specialized advisory services for women victims of gender-based violence.

8. Female workers in situations of gender-based violence shall have the right to the reduction or reorganization of their working time, to be geographically mobilized or to change their workplace. If her condition requires a work suspension, it must be accredited with the protection order from the judge, following a report and request from the Public Prosecutor's Office, with accreditation of indications being sufficient.

9. The State shall develop public policies directed at women victims of violence who lack work, who may be inserted into training programs, missions and projects for employment, as permitted by the physical and psychological conditions in which they find themselves. If the assaulted woman has an officially recognized disability that prevents or hinders access to employment, she shall receive special attention that allows her labor insertion and training. For this purpose, programs, projects and missions shall be established. The State shall create tax exemptions for companies, cooperatives and other entities that promote employment, insertion and reinsertion in the labor and productive market of women victims of gender-based violence. priority for aid and assistance created by the Public Administration, National, State or Municipal. priority in access to housing, land, credit and technical assistance in government plans.

Obligation of the State

#### Article 5.- The State has the unavoidable obligation to adopt all

administrative, legislative, judicial and any other measures that are necessary and appropriate to ensure compliance with this Law and guarantee the human rights of women victims of violence. Participation of society

#### Article 6.- Society has the right and duty to participate in a

leading manner to achieve the full and effective validity of this Law, through community and social organizations.

Education and prevention

#### Article 7.- The State, with the active participation of society, must

guarantee permanent education and prevention programs on gender-based violence. Procedural principles

#### Article 8.- In the application and interpretation of this Law, the following

principles and procedural guarantees must be taken into account:

- 1. Free of Charge: Requests, petitions, claims and other actions related to the matters referred to in this Law, as well as certified copies issued thereof, shall be made on plain paper and without stamps. The officials of the Public Powers who intervene in any way shall process them with utmost preference and may not charge any fee or right whatsoever.
- 2. Promptness: The agencies receiving complaints, auxiliaries of the administration of justice under the terms of article 111 of the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure, and the competent courts, shall give preference to the hearing and processing of the acts provided for in this Law, without any delay, within the time periods provided therein, under warning of the administrative measure corresponding to the official who has received the complaint.
- 3. Immediacy: The judge who is to pronounce the sentence must be present at the hearing and the incorporation of the evidence from which he or she obtains conviction, except in cases where the Law permits judicial commission for the production of some evidentiary means necessary for the demonstration of the controverted facts, the results of which shall be debated at the trial hearing. The evidence contained in the file duly incorporated at the hearing shall be assessed.
- 4. Confidentiality: The officials of the agencies receiving complaints, of the care and treatment units, and of the competent courts, must maintain the confidentiality of the matters submitted for their consideration.
- 5. Orality: The proceedings shall be oral and only the written forms provided for in this Law and in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure shall be admitted.
- 6. Concentration: Once the hearing has begun, it must conclude on the same day. If this is not possible, it shall continue for the fewest consecutive days.
- 7. Public Nature: The trial shall be public, unless at the request of the woman victim of violence the court decides that it be held totally or partially in closed session, and the woman must be informed in advance and in a timely manner that she may exercise this right.
- 8. Protection of Victims: The victims of the punishable acts described herein have the right to access the specialized civil and criminal justice agencies free of charge, expeditiously, without undue delays or useless formalities, without prejudice to the rights of the accused or charged persons. The protection of the victim and the reparation of damages to which she is entitled shall also be the objective of the procedure provided herein.

Security and Protection Measures and Precautionary Measures

#### Article 9.- Security and protection measures, and precautionary measures

are those imposed by the competent authority designated in this Law, to safeguard life, protect the physical, emotional, psychological integrity and property assets of women victims of violence.

Supremacy of this Law

Article The provisions of this Law shall be of preferential application 10.- as it is an Organic Law.

Jurisdiction

Article In all crimes provided for in this Law, no special jurisdiction 11.- shall be recognized, except those expressly contained in the Constitution of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela and laws of the Republic.

Pre-eminence of the Special Procedure

Article The prosecution of the crimes dealt with in this Law shall be 12.- carried out by the special procedure provided herein, except for the special case contained in the sole paragraph of article 65, the hearing of which corresponds to the ordinary criminal courts. Intervention of interdisciplinary team

Article In the reception of complaints and in the procedural 13.- investigation of the acts dealt with in this Law, duly sensitized, aware and trained personnel in gender violence shall be used. The respective offices shall be equipped with waiting rooms for accused persons, separate from those intended for victims.

## CHAPTER III: DEFINITION AND FORMS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

Definition

Article 14.- Violence against women referred to in this Law includes any sexist act that has or may have as a result physical, sexual, psychological, emotional, occupational, economic or patrimonial harm or suffering; coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, as well as the threat of executing such acts, whether they occur in the public or private sphere.

Forms of violence

Article 15.- The following are considered forms of gender-based violence against women:

- 1. Psychological violence: It is any active or omissive conduct exercised in dishonor, discredit or disregard of personal value or dignity, humiliating and degrading treatment, constant surveillance, isolation, marginalization, negligence, abandonment, jealousy, destructive comparisons, threats and acts that lead women victims of violence to diminish their self-esteem, to harm or disturb their healthy development, to depression and even to suicide.
- 2. Harassment: It is any abusive conduct and especially behaviors, words, acts, gestures, writings or electronic messages aimed at pursuing, intimidating, blackmailing, pressuring, bothering and watching a woman that may threaten her emotional stability, dignity, prestige, physical or mental integrity, or that may endanger her employment, promotion, recognition in the workplace or outside it.
- 3. Threat: It is the verbal or behavioral announcement of the execution of physical, psychological, sexual, occupational or patrimonial harm with the purpose of intimidating the woman, both in the domestic context and outside it.
- 4. Physical violence: It is any action or omission that directly or indirectly is aimed at causing physical harm or suffering to the woman, such as: Internal or external injuries, wounds, bruises, burns, pushing or any other mistreatment that affects her physical integrity.
- 5. Domestic violence: It is any active or omissive conduct, constant or not, of use of physical force or psychological violence, intimidation, persecution or threat against the woman by the spouse, cohabitant, ex-spouse, ex-cohabitant, person with whom she maintains or maintained an affective relationship, ascendants, descendants, collateral relatives, consanguineous and by affinity.
- 6. Sexual violence: It is any conduct that threatens or violates the woman's right to decide voluntarily and freely her sexuality, understanding this not only as the sexual act, but any form of sexual contact or access, genital or non-genital, such as lewd acts, violent lewd acts, violent carnal access or rape itself.
- 7. Violent carnal access: It is a form of sexual violence, in which the man through violence or threats, forces the spouse, cohabitant, person with whom he has marital life or maintains a stable de facto union or not, to a carnal act by vaginal, anal or oral route, or introduces objects whatever their class, through any of these routes.
- 8. Forced prostitution: Forced prostitution is understood as the action of forcing a woman to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature by force or by threat of force, or by coercion such as that caused by fear of violence, intimidation, psychological oppression or abuse of power, expecting to obtain or having obtained pecuniary or other advantages or benefits, in exchange for acts of a sexual nature by the woman.
- 9. Sexual slavery: Sexual slavery is understood as the illegitimate deprivation of liberty of the woman, for her sale, purchase, loan or exchange with the obligation to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature.

10.Sexual harassment: It is the solicitation of any act or behavior of sexual content, for oneself or for a third party, or the pursuit of any type of unwanted sexual approach made by a man taking advantage of a situation of labor, teaching or similar superiority, or on the occasion of relationships derived from professional practice, and with the express or tacit threat of causing the woman harm related to the legitimate expectations that she may have in the scope of said relationship.

11.Labor violence: It is discrimination against women in work centers: public or private that obstruct their access to employment, promotion or stability therein, such as requiring requirements regarding marital status, age, physical appearance or good presence, or the request for results of clinical laboratory tests, which make the hiring, promotion or permanence of women in employment conditional. It also constitutes gender discrimination in the labor sphere to violate the right of equal pay for equal work.

12.Patrimonial and economic violence: Patrimonial and economic violence is considered any active or omissive conduct that directly or indirectly, in the public and private spheres, is aimed at causing damage to movable or immovable property to the detriment of the patrimony of women victims of violence or to common property, as well as the disturbance to the possession or ownership of their property, removal, destruction, retention or diversion of objects, personal documents, goods and valuables, patrimonial rights or economic resources intended to satisfy their needs; economic limitations aimed at controlling their income; or the deprivation of the economic means essential to live.

13.Obstetric violence: Obstetric violence is understood as the appropriation of the body and reproductive processes of women by health personnel, which is expressed in a dehumanizing treatment, in an abuse of medicalization and pathologization of natural processes, bringing with it loss of autonomy and capacity to decide freely about their bodies and sexuality, negatively impacting the quality of life of women.
14.Forced sterilization: Forced sterilization is understood as performing or intentionally causing the woman, without providing her with due information, without her voluntary and informed consent and without justified reason, a medical or surgical treatment or other act that results in her sterilization or the deprivation of her biological and reproductive capacity.

15.Media violence: Media violence is understood as the exposure, through any means of dissemination, of women, girls or adolescents, that directly or indirectly exploits, discriminates, dishonors, humiliates or threatens their dignity for economic, social or domination purposes.
16.Institutional violence: These are actions or omissions carried out by authorities, officials, professionals, personnel and agents belonging to any body, entity or public institution, whose purpose is to delay, obstruct or prevent women from having access to public policies and exercising the rights provided for in this Law to ensure them a life free of violence.
17.Symbolic violence: These are messages, values, icons, signs that transmit and reproduce relations of domination, inequality and discrimination in the social relations established between people and naturalize the subordination of women in society.

18.Trafficking of women, girls and adolescents: These are all acts that involve their recruitment or transportation within or across borders, using deception, coercion or force, for the purpose of obtaining a financial or other material benefit of an illicit nature.

19.Trade of women, girls and adolescents: It is the recruitment, transportation, transfer, harboring or reception of women, girls and adolescents, resorting to threat or use of force or other forms of coercion, abduction, fraud, deception, abuse of power or of a situation of vulnerability or the granting or receiving of payments or benefits to obtain the consent of a person having authority over women, girls or adolescents, for purposes of exploitation, such as prostitution, sexual exploitation, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, servitude or organ removal.

## CHAPTER IV: PUBLIC POLICIES FOR PREVENTION AND CARE

Definition and content

Article Public policies for prevention and care are the set of 16.- orientations and guidelines issued by competent bodies, in order to guide actions aimed at ensuring the rights and guarantees enshrined in this Law. Programs

Article Programs are an articulated set of actions developed by 17.- natural or legal persons of a public or private nature, for the purpose of preventing, detecting, monitoring, addressing and eradicating violence against women.

Co-responsibility

Article The State and society are co-responsible for the execution, 18.- monitoring and control of policies for the prevention and care of violence against women in accordance with this Law. It is the responsibility of the National Women's Institute, as the governing body, to formulate policies for the prevention and care of violence against women.

The National Executive shall provide the necessary resources to finance plans, programs, projects and actions for the prevention and care of gender-based violence, promoted by Communal Councils, women's organizations and other grassroots social organizations.

Binding nature

Article Public policies adopted pursuant to this Law are binding on 19.- all organs of the Public Administration, within their respective areas of competence.

Classification of programs

Article For the purpose of developing public policies and allowing the 20.- execution of the measures referred to in this Law, the following programs are established on an indicative basis:

- 1. Prevention: to prevent the occurrence of forms of violence against women, by raising awareness, educating and training society as a whole in human rights and gender equality.
- 2. Awareness, training, education and capacity building: to meet the awareness and training needs of those dedicated to caring for victims of violence, as well as the training and education needs of those who work with aggressors.
- 3. Support and guidance for women victims of violence and their families: to inform them, support them in making assertive decisions and accompany them in the process of developing their skills, to overcome current and future interpersonal relationships of control and submission.
- 4. Shelter: to assist women victims of gender-based violence or other members of their families who need it, in case of imminent danger or threat to their physical integrity.
- 5. Communication: for the dissemination of women's right to live free from violence.
- 6. Guidance and care for the aggressor: to promote cultural changes and encourage values of respect and equality between men and women that prevent recidivism by aggressors.
- 7. Promotion and defense: to enable women and other family members to know their right to live free from violence and the means to enforce this right.
- 8. Cultural: for the formation and respect of values and the culture of gender equality.

Powers of the National Women's Institute

Article The National Women's Institute, as the body responsible for 21.- policies and programs for the prevention and care of violence against women, shall have the following powers:

- 1. Formulate, guide, execute, coordinate and implement public policies for prevention and care to be implemented in the different organs of the National, State and Municipal Public Power, in order to establish and articulate the comprehensive protection system referred to in this Law.
- 2. Design, jointly with the ministry with competence in matters of interior and justice and the Supreme Court of Justice, training plans and programs for officials belonging to the administration of justice and the penitentiary system, and other entities involved in the treatment of acts of violence contemplated by this Law.
- 3. Design, jointly with the ministries with competence in matters of Health and Popular Participation and Social Development, plans, projects and programs for training and information for officials who carry out support activities, services and medical and psychosocial care for the appropriate treatment of women victims of violence and their families, as well as for the aggressor.
- 4. Design, jointly with the ministries with competence in matters of Education, Sports, Higher Education, Health, Participation and Social Development, Communication and Information and with any other entity responsible for educational functions, prevention and education plans, projects and programs aimed at educating for equality, extolling the values of non-violence, respect, gender equity and preparation for family life with shared rights and obligations and, in general, equality between men and women in society.
- 5. Promote the active and leading participation of public and private organizations dedicated to addressing violence against women, as well as Communal Councils and grassroots social organizations, in the definition and implementation of public policies related to the matters regulated by this Law.
- 6. Maintain a registry of organizations specialized in the matters regulated by this Law, and may enter into agreements with them for the prevention, investigation and comprehensive care of women in situations of violence and the guidance of aggressors.
- 7. Prepare the draft Regulations for this Law.
- 8. Others assigned by other laws and regulations.

Training plans, programs and projects of the Supreme Court of Justice

Article The Supreme Court of Justice, through the Executive 22.- Directorate of the Magistracy and the School of the Magistracy, shall provide what is necessary for the execution of plans, programs and projects for training in gender justice for officials of the administration of justice and all those persons involved in the treatment of the acts contemplated by this Law. Awareness, training and education shall be carried out by the Supreme Court of Justice in coordination with the National Women's Institute, and may enter into agreements with women's and gender studies areas of universities. In the proceedings provided for in this Law, judges of different instances and hierarchies, including the Supreme Court of Justice, may request the opinion of experts in gender justice.

Training plans, projects and programs by the Public Prosecutor's Office

Article The Public Prosecutor's Office shall execute special training 23.- plans, projects and programs in the prevention and care of gender-based violence, and mainstream these programs with a gender perspective, in accordance with the human rights vision enshrined in the Constitution of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela.

Powers of the ministries with competence in matters of Education and Sports

Article The ministries with competence in matters of education and 24.- sports shall incorporate into study plans, projects and programs, at all levels and modalities, content aimed at conveying to students, teachers and administrative staff the values of gender equality, respect, mutual tolerance, self-esteem, understanding, peaceful resolution of conflicts and preparation for family and civic life, with domestic rights and obligations shared between men and women and, in general, equality of conditions between men and women, boys, girls and adolescents. Likewise, the ministries with competence in matters of education and sports shall take the necessary measures to exclude from study plans, texts and support materials, all those stereotypes, criteria or values that express any type of discrimination or violence against women.

Powers of the ministry with competence in matters of Higher Education

Article The ministry with competence in matters of higher education 25.- shall develop actions to mainstream curricula with a gender perspective and shall take the necessary measures to eliminate from study plans, texts, degrees awarded, official documents and support materials used in universities, all those stereotypes, criteria or values that express any form of discrimination. Likewise, it shall take the necessary measures to ensure that universities include in their undergraduate and postgraduate programs subjects that address the issue of gender-based violence and shall promote the development of research lines on the matter.

Powers of the ministry with competence in matters of interior and justice

Article The ministry with competence in matters of interior and justice 26.- shall provide what is necessary for the execution of training plans and programs for officials directly involved in the application of this Law. Such plans and programs shall be formulated and carried out in coordination with the

National Women's Institute and must guarantee adequate treatment and assistance to women victims of violence. It shall also include in its plans special programs for the care and guidance of aggressors. It shall also establish programs aimed at guaranteeing women deprived of liberty the exercise of the rights provided for in this Law.

Powers of the ministry with competence in matters of health
Article The ministry with competence in matters of health shall execute 27.- training and information plans, jointly with the National Women's Institute, so that health personnel who carry out support activities, services and medical and psychosocial care, act appropriately in the care, investigation and prevention of the acts provided for in this Law.

Prevention programs in mass media

Article The ministry with competence in matters of infrastructure and 28.- the National Telecommunications Council, in accordance with the Law on Social Responsibility in Radio and Television, shall supervise the effective inclusion of messages and programs designed to prevent and eliminate violence against women in the programming of mass media. To this end, it may establish a minimum free airtime for radio and television stations for the transmission of messages against gender-based violence and promoting values of equality between the sexes.

Obligations of states and municipalities

Article The states and municipalities, pursuant to this Law, shall 29.- coordinate with the National Women's Institute and with the regional and municipal institutes, the policies, plans and programs to be executed for the development of the functions of prevention and care of violence against women in their respective jurisdictions.

Prevention, care and treatment units

Article The National Executive, through the governing body, shall 30.- coordinate with state and municipal bodies the establishment of specialized violence prevention units, as well as care and treatment centers for women victims. They shall also develop guidance units that will cooperate with jurisdictional bodies for the monitoring and control of measures imposed on aggressors.

Powers of the National Institute of Statistics

Article The National Institute of Statistics, jointly with the National 31.- Women's Institute, shall coordinate with the agencies of the Public Powers, censuses, statistics and any other study, permanent or not, that allows the collection of disaggregated data on violence against women in the national territory.

Shelter houses

Article The National, State and Municipal Executive, in order to make 32.- the protection of women in situations of violence more effective, with the assistance, advice and training of the National Women's Institute and the regional and municipal women's institutes, shall create in each of their dependencies shelter houses intended for housing them, in cases where remaining in the home or residence implies an imminent threat to their integrity.

## CHAPTER V: WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Attention to women victims of violence

Article The receiving agencies for complaints shall grant to the 33.- women victims of acts of violence provided for in this Law, dignified treatment with respect and support according to their condition as affected persons, seeking to facilitate to the maximum extent their participation in the proceedings in which they must intervene.

Consequently, they shall:

- 1. Advise women victims of violence on the importance of preserving evidence.
- 2. Provide assaulted women with information about the rights conferred upon them by this Law and about the governmental or non-governmental services available for their attention and treatment.
- 3. Prepare a report on those circumstances that serve to clarify the facts, which must accompany the complaint.
- 4. Any other information that the receiving agencies consider important to indicate to the woman in a situation of violence for her protection.

Labor rights

Article Female workers or civil servants who are victims of violence shall have 34.- the right, under the terms provided in the respective laws, to the reduction or reorganization of their working time, to geographical mobility, to change of workplace, to suspension of the employment relationship with job reservation, and to leave under the terms to be determined.

##### Sole Paragraph. Total or partial absences from work

motivated by the physical or psychological condition derived from gender violence suffered by female workers or civil servants, shall be considered justified when so determined by public or private health care centers, under the terms provided in the respective legislation.

Medical Certificate

Article For the purposes of certifying the physical condition of the woman victim of 35.- violence, she may present a medical certificate issued by health professionals who provide services in any public institution. If not possible, the medical certificate may be issued by a private institution; in both cases, it must be confirmed by a forensic expert, upon prior request of the Public Prosecutor's Office.

Free legal assistance

Article In those cases where the victim lacks legal 36.- assistance, she may request from the competent judge the appointment of a legal professional, who shall properly guide her and exercise the defense of her rights from the initial acts of the investigation. For such purposes, the court shall make the selection of existing lawyers, from the National Women's Rights Ombudsman's Office, from state and municipal ombudsman's offices, from bar associations of each jurisdiction, or from any public or private organization dedicated to the defense of the rights established in this Law.

Intervention in the procedure

Article The aggrieved person, the National Women's Rights

37.- Ombudsman's Office and the social organizations referred to in the sixth numeral of Article 70 of this Law, may intervene in the procedure even if they have not been constituted as complainants.

Regarding the request for simple and certified copies

Article The woman victim of violence may request before any 38.- instance simple or certified copies of all proceedings contained in the case being processed for one of the crimes typified in this Law, which shall be granted to her expeditiously, except in the case of confidentiality of proceedings referred to in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure

## CHAPTER VI: OF CRIMES

Psychological violence

Article Whoever through humiliating and demeaning treatment, offenses, 39.- isolation, permanent surveillance, destructive comparisons or constant generic threats, attempts against the emotional or psychological stability of the woman, shall be punished with imprisonment from six to eighteen months.

Harassment or pestering

Article The person who through behaviors, verbal or written 40.- expressions, or electronic messages executes acts of intimidation, blackmail, harassment or pestering that attempt against the emotional, labor, economic, family or educational stability of the woman, shall be punished with imprisonment from eight to twenty months.

Threat

Article The person who through verbal expressions, writings or 41.- electronic messages threatens a woman with causing her a serious and probable harm of physical, psychological, sexual, labor or patrimonial nature, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to twenty-two months.

If the threat or act of violence is carried out in the domicile or residence of the woman object of violence, the penalty shall be increased by one-third to one-half.

If the author of the crime is a public official belonging to any police or military body, the penalty shall be increased by one-half.

If the act is committed with bladed weapons or firearms, the imprisonment shall be from two to four years.

Physical violence

Article Whoever through the use of physical force causes damage 42.- or physical suffering to a woman, bruises, slaps, shoves or injuries of light or very light nature, shall be punished with imprisonment from six to eighteen months.

If in the execution of the crime, the victim suffers serious or very serious injuries, as provided in the Penal Code, the penalty corresponding to the injury inflicted provided in said Code shall be applied, plus an increase of one-third to one-half.

If the acts of violence referred to in this article occur in the domestic sphere, the author being the spouse, cohabitant, ex-spouse, ex-cohabitant, person with whom a relationship of affection is maintained, even without cohabitation, ascendant, descendant, collateral relative, consanguineous or affine of the victim, the penalty shall be increased by one-third to one-half.

Jurisdiction to hear the crime of injuries as provided in this article shall correspond to the courts of violence against women, according to the special procedure provided in this Law.

Sexual Violence

Article Whoever through the use of violence or threats 43.- compels a woman to consent to unwanted sexual contact that includes penetration by vaginal, anal or oral route, even through the introduction of objects of any kind through any of these routes, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

If the author of the crime is the spouse, cohabitant, ex-spouse, ex-cohabitant, person with whom the victim maintains or maintained a relationship of affection, even without cohabitation, the penalty shall be increased by one-quarter to one-third.

The same penalty increase shall apply in cases where the author is the ascendant, descendant, collateral relative, consanguineous or affine of the victim.

If the act is executed to the detriment of a girl or adolescent, the penalty shall be fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.

If the victim turns out to be a girl or adolescent, daughter of the woman with whom the author maintains a relationship in the condition of spouse, cohabitant, ex-spouse, ex-cohabitant, person with whom he maintains or maintained a relationship of affection, even without cohabitation, the penalty shall be increased by one-quarter to one-third.

Carnal act with especially vulnerable victim

Article Incurs in the crime provided in the previous article and shall 44.- be punished with a penalty of fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment, whoever executes the carnal act, even without violence or threats, in the following cases:

- 1. To the detriment of a vulnerable woman, by reason of her age or in any case under thirteen years of age.
- 2. When the author has taken advantage of his relationship of superiority or kinship with the victim, whose age is less than sixteen years.
- 3. In the case that the victim is detained or convicted and has been entrusted to the custody of the aggressor.
- 4. When it involves a victim with physical or mental disability or has been deprived of the capacity to discern by the supply of drugs or psychotropic substances.

Lascivious acts

Article Whoever through the use of violence or threats and without the 45.- intention of committing the crime referred to in article 43, compels a woman to consent to unwanted sexual contact, affecting her right to freely decide her sexuality, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to five years.

If the act is executed to the detriment of a girl or adolescent, the penalty shall be two to six years of imprisonment.

The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever executes the lascivious acts to the detriment of the girl or adolescent, even without violence or threats, taking advantage of their relationship of authority or kinship.

Forced prostitution

Article Whoever through the use of physical force, the threat of 46.- violence, psychological coercion or abuse of power, compels a woman to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature with the object of obtaining in exchange pecuniary advantages or of another nature, for their own benefit or that of a third party, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.

Sexual slavery

Article Whoever unlawfully deprives a woman of her liberty with 47.- purposes of sexually exploiting her through purchase, sale, loan, barter or other analogous negotiation, forcing her to perform one or more acts of a sexual nature, shall be punished with a penalty of fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.

Sexual harassment

Article Whoever requests from a woman an act or behavior of 48.- sexual content for himself or for a third party or seeks an unwanted sexual approach, taking advantage of a situation of labor or teaching superiority or on the occasion of relationships derived from professional practice, with the threat of causing her harm related to the legitimate expectations that she may have in the sphere of said relationship, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to three years.

Labor violence

Article The person who through the establishment of requirements 49.- referred to sex, age, physical appearance, marital status, condition of mother or not, submission to laboratory tests or of another nature to rule out state of pregnancy, obstructs or conditions the access, promotion or stability in employment of women, shall be punished with a fine of one hundred (100 T.U.) to one thousand tax units (1,000 T.U.), according to the gravity of the act.

If it is an employment policy of a public institution or State company, the sanction shall be imposed on the maximum authority thereof. In the case of private companies, franchises or transnational companies, the sanction shall be imposed on whoever exercises the maximum representation in the country.

The same sanction shall apply when through administrative, deceitful or fraudulent practices the right to legal and fair salary of the female worker or the right to equal pay for equal work is affected.

Patrimonial and economic violence

Article The spouse legally separated or the cohabitant in a situation 50.- of de facto separation duly proven, who subtracts, deteriorates, destroys, diverts, retains, orders the blocking of bank accounts or performs acts capable of affecting the community of property or the woman's own patrimony, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to three years.

The same penalty shall apply in the event that there is no legal separation, but the author has been subjected to the protection measure of exit from the home by a complaint receiving body or to a similar precautionary measure by the competent Control, Hearing and Measures Court.

In the event that the acts referred to in this article are intentionally directed to deprive the woman of the economic means indispensable for her subsistence, or prevent her from satisfying her needs and those of the family nucleus, the penalty shall be increased by one-third to one-half.

If the author of the crime referred to in this article, without being a spouse or cohabitant, maintains or maintained a relationship of affection with the woman, even without cohabitation, the penalty shall be six to twelve months of imprisonment.

Organic Code of Criminal Procedure.

Obstetric violence

Article The following shall be considered constitutive acts of obstetric violence those 51.- executed by health personnel, consisting of:

- 1. Not attending timely and effectively to obstetric emergencies.
- 2. Forcing the woman to give birth in a supine position and with legs raised, when the necessary means exist for the performance of vertical birth.
- 3. Obstructing the early attachment of the boy or girl with his or her mother, without justified medical cause, denying her the possibility of holding him or her and breastfeeding him or her immediately upon birth.
- 4. Altering the natural process of low-risk childbirth, through the use of acceleration techniques, without obtaining the voluntary, express and informed consent of the woman.
- 5. Practicing childbirth by cesarean section, when conditions exist for natural birth, without obtaining the voluntary, express and informed consent of the woman.

In such cases, the court shall impose on the person responsible, a fine of two hundred and fifty (250 T.U.) to five hundred tax units (500 T.U.), and shall send a certified copy of the definitive final conviction to the respective professional association or guild institution, for purposes of the corresponding disciplinary procedure. Forced sterilization

Article Whoever intentionally deprives the woman of her reproductive 52.- capacity, without providing her with due information, nor obtaining her express, voluntary and informed consent, when there is no duly proven medical or surgical reason that justifies it, shall be punished with a penalty of imprisonment from two to five years.

The sentencing court shall send a copy of the definitive final conviction decision to the professional association or guild institution, for purposes of the corresponding disciplinary procedure.

Institutional violence

The competent court shall send a certified copy of the definitive final conviction to the body of affiliation of the guilty party, for purposes of the corresponding disciplinary procedure.

Illicit trafficking of women, girls and adolescents

Article Whoever promotes, favors, facilitates or executes the illegal entry or 55.- exit from the country of women, girls or adolescents, using deception, coercion or force with the purpose of obtaining an illicit benefit for himself or herself or for a third party, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.

Trafficking of women, girls and adolescents

Subsistence of Protection and Security Measures Article In any case, protection measures shall subsist during 88.- the proceeding and may be substituted, modified, confirmed or revoked by the competent jurisdictional body, either ex officio or at the request of a party. The substitution, modification, confirmation or revocation of protection measures shall proceed in case of existence of evidentiary elements that determine their necessity.

Preferential application of security and protection measures and precautionary measures

Article The security and protection measures and precautionary 89.- measures established in this Law shall be of preferential application to those established in other legal provisions, without prejudice that the competent judge, ex officio, at the prosecutor's request or at the victim's request, deems the necessity to impose any of the substitute precautionary measures provided in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure with the purpose of guaranteeing the submission of the accused or defendant to the proceeding brought against him.

Procedure in case of necessity and urgency

Article The receiving agency, in cases of necessity and urgency, may 90.- request directly from the Court of Violence against Women in Functions of Control, Hearing and Measures the respective arrest order. The resolution ordering the arrest shall always be substantiated. The court must decide within twenty-four hours following the request.

Common Provisions on Protection and Security Measures

Article The Court of Violence against Women in functions of 91.- Control, Hearing and Measures, may:

- 1. Substitute, modify, confirm or revoke the protection measures imposed by the receiving agency.
- 2. Agree to those measures requested by the woman victim of violence or the Public Prosecutor.
- 3. Impose any other measure from those provided in articles 87 and 92, according to the circumstances that the case presents.

##### First Paragraph: If the urgency of the case warrants it, it

shall not be a requirement for imposing the measure, the result of the corresponding medical examination, and may be remedied with any other means of evidence that is suitable, including the presence of the woman victim of violence at the hearing.

Precautionary measures

Article The Public Prosecutor's Office may request from the Court of Violence 92.- against Women in Control, Hearing and Measures functions, or in trial functions, if applicable, the following precautionary measures:

- 1. Transitory arrest of the aggressor for up to forty-eight hours to be served in the establishment that the court agrees upon.
- 2. Order prohibiting the alleged aggressor from leaving the country, the term of which shall be set by the court according to the severity of the facts.
- 3. Prohibition on alienating and encumbering property of the marital or common-law community, up to fifty percent (50%).
- 4. Prohibition for the alleged aggressor from residing in the same municipality where the woman victim of violence has established her new residence, when there is evidence of persecution by him.
- 5. Search of the place where the acts of violence were committed.
- 6. Establish a maintenance obligation in favor of the woman victim of violence, after socioeconomic evaluation of both parties.
- 7. Impose on the alleged aggressor the obligation to attend a specialized center on gender-based violence.
- 8. Any other measure necessary for the personal, physical, psychological and patrimonial protection of the woman victim of violence.

Fifth Section: On Apprehension in Flagrante Delicto

Definition and manner of proceeding

Article Any crime provided for in this Law that is being committed 93.- or that has just been committed shall be considered flagrant. Also considered flagrant is one for which the aggressor is pursued by the police authority, by the assaulted woman, by a private individual or by public outcry, or when requests for help are made to specialized services for attention to violence against women, made through telephone calls, emails or fax, that allow establishing its commission unequivocally, or in which he is caught shortly after the act has been committed, in the same place or near the place where it was committed, with weapons, instruments or objects that in some way give reasonable grounds to presume that he is the perpetrator.

In these cases, every authority must and any private individual may apprehend the aggressor. When the apprehension is carried out by a private individual, he must immediately deliver him to the nearest authority, who in any case shall place him at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office within a period that shall not exceed twelve hours from the moment of apprehension.

It shall be understood that the act has just been committed when the victim or another person who has had knowledge of the act, appears within twenty-four hours following the commission of the punishable act before the receiving agency and states the facts of violence related to this Law. In this case, once the commission of the punishable act is known, the receiving agency or the authority having knowledge must proceed within a period not to exceed twelve hours to the place where the events occurred, shall gather the elements that prove its commission and, once the assumptions referred to in this article are verified, shall proceed to the apprehension of the alleged aggressor, who shall be placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office, according to the preceding paragraph.

The Public Prosecutor's Office, within a term not to exceed forty-eight hours from the apprehension of the alleged aggressor, must present him before the Court of Violence Against Women in Control, Hearing and Measures Functions, which, in a hearing with the parties and the victim, if she is present, shall decide whether to maintain the deprivation of liberty or substitute it with another less onerous measure.

The decision must be duly grounded and shall observe the assumptions of admissibility for deprivation of liberty contained in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure, adjusted to the nature of the crimes contained in this Law, according to the act in question and attending to the objectives of protection of the victims, without prejudice to the rights of the alleged aggressor.

Sixth Section: On the Special Procedure

Procedure

Article The prosecution of the crimes dealt with in this Law shall be 94.- followed by the special procedure stipulated herein, even in the cases of flagrante delicto provided for in the preceding article, with the exception established in the sole paragraph of article 79, for the case in which a custodial measure has been decreed against the alleged aggressor.

Forms of initiating the procedure

Article The investigation of an act that constitutes one of the 95.- crimes provided for in this Law shall be initiated ex officio, by oral or written complaint or by complaint filed before the competent jurisdictional body.

All these crimes are public action crimes; however, to initiate the investigation in the cases referred to in articles 39, 40, 41, 48, 49 and 53, the complaint of the act by the persons or institutions legitimized to file it is required.

Investigation by the Public Prosecutor's Office

Article When the Public Prosecutor's Office becomes aware of the 96.- commission of a punishable act provided for in this Law, it shall without delay order the initiation of the investigation and order that all necessary proceedings be carried out to demonstrate the commission of the punishable act, as well as the criminal responsibility of the persons identified as perpetrators or participants, immediately imposing the protection and security measures that the case warrants.

On initiation before another receiving agency

Article When the complaint or ex officio investigation is known by 97.- a receiving agency other than the Public Prosecutor's Office, it shall proceed to order the protection and security measures that the case warrants and immediately notify the corresponding Prosecutor of the Public Prosecutor's Office, so that he or she may issue the order to initiate the investigation, shall carry out all necessary proceedings to prove the commission of the punishable act, as well as the pertinent psychophysical medical examinations of the woman victim of violence.

Remission to the Public Prosecutor's Office

Article Once the protection and security measures have been ordered, as well as 98.- all necessary and urgent proceedings have been carried out, which may not exceed fifteen continuous days, the receiving agency must remit the proceedings to the Public Prosecutor's Office, to continue the investigation.

Violation of constitutional rights and guarantees

Article When one of the parties does not agree with the 99.- measure ordered by the receiving agency, it may request before the Court of Violence against Women in Control, Hearing and Measures functions, its review, which shall require the proceedings from the Public Prosecutor's Office or from the corresponding receiving agency, if applicable.

If upon receipt by the Prosecutor of the Public Prosecutor's Office of the proceedings from another receiving agency, he or she observes violation of constitutional rights and guarantees, he or she shall immediately proceed to request its review with reasons before the Control, Hearing and Measures judge; for this purpose he or she shall remit the original proceedings, leaving in the Prosecutor's Office a simple copy thereof to continue with the investigation.

Review and decision on measures

Article Within three business days following receipt 100.- of the proceedings, the Control, Hearing and Measures judge shall review the measures, and by reasoned order shall pronounce himself or herself modifying, substituting, confirming or revoking them.

Remission of the proceedings

Article On the day following publication of the decision referred to in the 101.- preceding article, the Control, Hearing and Measures Court shall remit the original proceedings to the Public Prosecutor's Office or to the corresponding receiving agency if applicable, to continue with the procedure.

End of investigation

Article Once the investigation is concluded, in accordance with the provisions of 102.- article 79 or the special case provided for in article 103 of this Law, the Public Prosecutor's Office shall proceed to issue the corresponding conclusive act.

Extraordinary extension for prosecutorial omission

Article If after all deadlines have expired, the Prosecutor of the Public 103.- Prosecutor's Office does not issue the corresponding conclusive act, the Control, Hearing and Measures judge shall notify such omission to the Superior Prosecutor, who within the following two days must commission a new Prosecutor to present the conclusions of the investigation within a period not to exceed ten continuous days from notification of the commission, without prejudice to the civil, criminal and administrative sanctions that may be applicable to the omitting Prosecutor.

Once the extraordinary extension referred to in this article has elapsed, without action by the Public Prosecutor's Office, the Control, Hearing and Measures Court shall order judicial filing, in accordance with the provisions of the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure.

On the preliminary hearing

Article Once the indictment is presented before the Court of Violence Against 104.- Women in Control, Hearing and Measures Functions, it shall set the hearing to hear the parties within the following ten business days.

Before the expiration of said period, the parties shall proceed to offer the evidence that will be presented at the oral trial hearing and raise the exceptions they deem appropriate. The court shall rule at the hearing.

At this act the accused may admit the facts, but the penalty to be imposed may only be reduced by one third.

At the conclusion of the hearing, the judge shall present with reasons his or her decision regarding the parties' submissions. In case of admitting the indictment, he or she shall issue the order to open trial and remit the proceedings to the corresponding trial court.

The order to open trial shall not be appealable.

Seventh Section: On the Oral Trial

On the oral trial

Article Upon receipt of the proceedings, the Trial Court shall set the date 105.- for the oral and public hearing, within a period that may not be less than ten business days nor more than twenty.

On the oral trial hearing

Article At the Trial Hearing only one professional judge 106.- shall act. The debate shall be oral and public, and the judge may decide to conduct it, totally or partially in closed session, upon request of the victim. The judge must inform the victim of this right before the beginning of the act. The hearing shall take place in a single day; if not possible, it shall continue on the fewest consecutive business days. It may be suspended for a maximum period of five days, only in the following cases:

- 1. Due to force majeure.
- 2. For lack of interpreter.
- 3. When the defense counsel or the Public Prosecutor's Office requests it due to the extension of the indictment.
- 4. To resolve incidental matters or the performance of some act outside the hearing room.
- 5. Any other reason considered relevant by the court.

On the decision

Article At the conclusion of the debate a record shall be made of everything that occurred, 107.- which shall be read aloud and signed by the participants.

The judge shall proceed to render judgment in the room designated for that purpose, to which the parties shall not have access at any time. The judgment shall be issued the same day, proceeding to its reading and the parties being thus notified. The original document shall be filed. The parties may request a copy of the judgment.

In case it is not possible to draft the judgment on the same day, the judge shall present to the parties the grounds thereof and read the operative part.

Publication shall take place within five business days following the pronouncement of the operative part.

On the appeal

Article Against the judgment issued at the oral hearing, an 108.- appeal shall be filed before the court that issued it and may be exercised within three business days following the date of publication of the full text of the ruling.

Formalities

Article The appeal may only be based on: 109.-

- 1. Violation of rules relating to orality, immediacy and concentration of the trial.
- 2. Lack, contradiction or manifest illogicality in the reasoning of the judgment, or when it is based on illegally obtained evidence or incorporated in violation of the principles of the oral hearing.
- 3. Breach or omission of substantial forms of acts that cause defenselessness.
- 4. Incurring in violation of the law due to non-observance or erroneous application of a legal norm.

Response to the appeal

Article Once the appeal is filed, the other parties shall respond to it within 110.- the three business days following the expiration of the period for filing it. Upon expiration of this period, the court shall remit the proceedings to the Court of Appeals for it to decide.

On the Court of Appeals

Article Upon receipt of the proceedings, the Court of Appeals shall have a 111.- period of three business days following the date of receipt to decide on the admissibility of the appeal. Once admitted, it shall set an oral hearing to be held within a period of not less than three business days nor more than five, counted from the date of admission. The order to open trial shall not be appealable.

On the hearing

Article At the hearing the judges may question the 112.- parties; they shall decide with reasons based on the evidence that is offered and is useful and pertinent. Upon conclusion of the hearing they must issue the corresponding pronouncement. When the complexity of the case warrants it, they may decide within the following five business days.

Cassation

Article The exercise of the Cassation Appeal shall be governed by the 113.- provisions of the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure.

Eighth Section: On the Jurisdictional Bodies and the Public Prosecutor's Office

Powers of the Prosecutors of the Public Prosecutor's Office

Article The powers of the Prosecutors of the Public Prosecutor's Office 114.- specialized in violence against women are:

- 1. Exercise the corresponding criminal action.
- 2. Ensure compliance with the provisions set forth in this Law.
- 3. Investigate the acts that are classified as crimes in this Law.
- 4. Request and provide evidence and participate in its production.
- 5. Direct and supervise compliance with the functions of the Investigation Police.
- 6. Request with reasons from the jurisdictional body the pertinent precautionary measures.
- 7. Request from the jurisdictional body the substitution, modification, confirmation or revocation of the protection measures ordered by the receiving agencies or of the precautionary measures it may have ordered.
- 8. Request with reasons from the jurisdictional body the definitive confiscation of the weapon seized by the receiving agency. In cases where appropriate, it shall also request the prohibition of carrying weapons.
- 9. Gather the elements of conviction leading to the preparation of the conclusive act, in whose procedures the rules set forth in the Organic Code of Criminal Procedure shall be observed.

10. Any other action provided for in the legal system.

Jurisdiction

Article It corresponds to the courts of violence against women and to 115.- the Criminal Cassation Chamber of the Supreme Court of Justice, the exercise of jurisdiction for the resolution of matters submitted to their decision, in accordance with the provisions of this

Law, the laws of judicial organization and internal regulations.

Creation of the courts of violence against women

Article The courts of violence against women are created which 116.- shall have their seat in Caracas and in each state capital, in addition to the localities determined by the Supreme

Court of Justice through the Executive Directorate of the Magistracy.

Constitution of the courts of violence against women

Article The courts of violence against women shall be organized in 117.- judicial circuits, according to what the Executive Directorate of the Magistracy determines, which may create more than one judicial circuit in the same jurisdiction, when necessary for service reasons. Their organization and operation shall be governed by the provisions established in this Law, in the corresponding organic laws and in the Internal Regulations of the Judicial Circuits.

In each judicial circuit the courts of violence against women shall be constituted in first instance by control, hearing and measures judges; trial judges and enforcement judges. In second instance they are made up of the Courts of Appeals.

Jurisdiction

Article The courts of violence against women shall have jurisdiction in the 118.- criminal order over the crimes provided for in this Law, as well as over the crime of injuries in all their classifications typified in the Criminal Code in the cases established in article 42 of this Law and in accordance with the special procedure established herein.

In the civil order, they shall have jurisdiction over all matters of a patrimonial nature.

Cassation

Article The Criminal Cassation Chamber of the Supreme Court of Justice 119.- shall hear the Cassation Appeal.

Ninth Section: On Auxiliary Services

Auxiliary services

Article The courts of violence against women shall have: 120.-

- 1. Multidisciplinary teams or the budgetary allocation for contracting them.
- 2. A work room for the multidisciplinary team.
- 3. A room for summons and notifications.

Objectives of the interdisciplinary team

Article Each Court of Violence Against Women must have 121.- a multidisciplinary team that shall be organized as an auxiliary service of an independent and impartial nature, to provide the exercise of the jurisdictional function with bio-psychosocial-legal expertise in a collegial and interdisciplinary manner. This team shall be composed of professionals in medicine, psychiatry, education, psychology, social work, law, criminology and other professions with expertise in the matter. In areas where necessary, there shall be bilingual intercultural experts in indigenous languages.

Powers of the interdisciplinary team

Article The powers of the interdisciplinary teams of the 122.- courts of violence against women are:

- 1. Issue an opinion, through comprehensive technical reports on the appropriateness of protecting the woman victim of violence, through specific precautionary measures.
- 2. Intervene as independent and impartial experts of the Justice System in judicial proceedings, conducting expert examinations through comprehensive technical reports.
- 3. Provide comprehensive advice to persons subject to precautionary measures.
- 4. Advise the judge in obtaining and assessing the opinion or testimony of children and adolescents, according to their age and degree of maturity.
- 5. Assist the courts of violence against women in the execution of judicial decisions.
- 6. Others established by law.

Equipment

Article The courts of violence against women must be equipped 123.- with the facilities, equipment and personnel necessary for the fulfillment of their functions; among other areas, they must have:

- 1. A space specifically directed to the attention of the assaulted woman, separate from that designated for the aggressor.
- 2. An appropriate space and equipment for carrying out the functions of the interdisciplinary team.

##### Sole Paragraph: The ministry with competence in matters

of interior and justice shall create within the Corps of Scientific, Penal and Criminalistic Investigations, a medical-forensic unit comprised of experts for the attention of cases of women victims of violence, which shall issue the corresponding reports and expert opinions in a timely and expeditious manner.

TRANSITORY PROVISIONS

First.- Until such time as specialized tribunals in matters of violence against women are created, the Supreme Court of Justice shall provide what is necessary for their functions to be fulfilled by ordinary criminal tribunals in control, trial and execution functions, to which exclusive competence in matters of violence against women shall be conferred by resolution of the Executive Directorate of the Magistracy, at the time of entry into force of this Law.

The Supreme Court of Justice shall take the necessary steps so that the creation of specialized tribunals in violence against women is executed within one year counted from the effective date of this Law. During said period, judges, as well as officials who are to intervene as operators of justice in matters of violence against women, shall be trained by professionals assigned to the National Women's Institute, the Ombudsman's Office, the National Women's Rights Ombudsman's Office, universities, non-governmental organizations, international organizations, and any other entity specialized in gender justice.

Second.- Until such time as units for attention and treatment of acts of violence against women are created, judges may consider reports issued by any public or private health organization when sentencing. The states and municipalities shall provide what is necessary to create and put into operation the units of attention and treatment, within the year following the entry into force of this Law. During said period they shall proceed to train the officials who will comprise them. The reports and recommendations issued by experts from non-governmental organizations specialized in the attention of acts of violence contemplated in this Law may equally be considered by judges.

Third.- Until such time as places for serving sentences for those responsible for acts of violence against women are created, the ministry with competence in the matter shall take provisions to adapt detention sites and facilitate the re-education of aggressors.

The creation of said centers must be developed within a maximum period of one year, following the entry into force of this Law. During said period, officials and all those persons who will intervene in the treatment of those convicted of the crimes provided for in this Law shall be trained.

Fourth.- Within a period of no more than one year, counted from the publication of this Law, the Nation, the states and municipalities must provide what is necessary for the creation and adaptation of the units, entities and organs provided for herein. Within the same period, the necessary regulations must be issued for the purpose of executing its provisions.

Fifth.- In accordance with article 24 of the Constitution of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela, the procedural provisions provided for in this Law shall apply from the moment they enter into force, even to proceedings that are in progress, without prejudice to the principle of non-retroactivity insofar as they favor the accused or the convicted.

Appeals already filed, the presentation of evidence already admitted, as well as terms or periods that have begun to run, shall be governed by the previous provisions.

The Public Prosecutor's Office shall provide what is necessary so that cases that are in the investigation phase are processed expeditiously and the corresponding conclusive act is presented within six months following the effective date of this Law.

Sixth.- The National Executive shall include in the annual budget laws, beginning the year immediately following the enactment of this Law, the necessary resources for the operation of the organs, entities and programs provided for herein.

Seventh.- Official and private publications of this Law must be preceded by its statement of reasons.

DEROGATORY PROVISION

Sole.- The Law on Violence against Women and the Family dated September 3, 1998, published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Venezuela No. 36,531, as well as provisions contrary to this Law, are hereby repealed.
