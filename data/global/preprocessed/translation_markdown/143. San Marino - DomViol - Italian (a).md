# REPUBLIC OF SAN MARINO

REPUBLIC OF SAN MARINO

We, the Captains Regent of the Most Serene Republic of San Marino

Having regard to Article 4 of Constitutional Law No. 185/2005 and Article 6 of Qualified Law No. 186/2005;

We promulgate and order the publication of the following ordinary law approved by the Grand and General Council in its session of 18 June 2008.

LAW OF 20 JUNE 2008 NO. 97

PREVENTION AND REPRESSION OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND

GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE

### CAPO I

GUIDING PRINCIPLES AND MEASURES FOR AWARENESS-RAISING AND PREVENTION OF

VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND GENDER-BASED VIOLENCE, INCLUDING

DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

#### Art.1

(Objective of the law)

This law aims to prevent and combat violence against women and gender-based violence, including domestic violence.

#### Art. 2

(Definition of violence against women)

Any act of violence based on sex or gender that causes or is likely to cause physical, sexual or psychological harm or suffering to the victim constitutes violence against a person, including the threat of such acts, coercion, arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether in public or private life.

#### Art. 3

(Mass media and discriminatory communications)

The mass media must promote the protection and safeguarding of equality between men and women and must avoid any discrimination between them.

It is prohibited to use, even for advertising purposes, images and expressions that are harmful to the dignity and identity of the person, or that have discriminatory content, including those containing references to the sexual orientation of the person or to gender identity. The Authority for Equal Opportunities, responsible for this matter, on its own initiative or upon written notification by anyone having an interest therein, may request the Commissioner of Law:

- a) to prohibit the dissemination of images, news, or references in breach of the prohibition set out in the second paragraph;
- b) to prohibit their continuation and to eliminate their effects; without prejudice to the right of the person portrayed or subject of the references to claim damages.

The Commissioner of Law shall issue a decree after hearing the party against whom the measure is to be enforced. In cases of particular urgency, and where the party against whom the measure is to be enforced does not have residence or headquarters in the territory of the Republic, or an authorised representative to appear in court, the Commissioner of Law may order the execution of the measure even without arranging the hearing. Anyone who fails to comply with the order issued pursuant to the preceding paragraph shall be punished pursuant to Article 366 of the Criminal Code.

An appeal to the Court of Appeal for nullity is allowed against the decree of the Commissioner of Law, but the appeal does not suspend the execution of the measure, unless the Court of Appeal decides otherwise.

The legal assistance of the Authority for Equal Opportunities in the proceedings shall be provided by the State Attorney's Office.

The acts of the proceedings are exempt from judicial tax.

#### Art. 4

(Assistance to victims of violence)

The State ensures to victims of family and sexual violence:

- a) information on the measures provided by law regarding the protection, safety and rights of assistance and relief of victims of violence;
- b) the existence of services to which the relevant social assistance responsibilities are assigned, equipped with specialised personnel, easily identifiable and accessible to victims;
- c) that the services are able to perform emergency intervention functions, including psychological support, and subsequent medium-term management of situations, also for the purpose of family reunification;
- d) the provision of measures for social support, protection, support for education, training and professional integration;
- e) in the most serious cases, where remaining in the family is harmful, the placement of victims in family-type communities for a period sufficient to implement a social reintegration project;
- f) the preparation of protection and social reintegration programmes for the victim of violence, where necessary, including meeting housing needs and maintaining the residence permit, should it expire during the proceedings, at least for the duration of the criminal trial, professional reintegration and the needs for care and support of dependent children.
- g) specific training for judges to whom the judicial proceedings referred to in this law are assigned and for the Police Forces.

The identification and organisation of services and the concrete determination of measures shall be carried out by means of a specific delegated decree to be issued within six months of the entry into force of this law.

### CAPO II

AMENDMENTS TO THE CRIMINAL CODE

#### Art. 5

(Aggravating circumstance in homicide)

In Article 150, second paragraph, of the Criminal Code, the following is inserted after number 1): "1 bis) against the spouse, a cohabiting person or a person who was or is linked by an emotional relationship, even not implying cohabitation".

#### Art. 6

(Aggravating circumstances for personal injury)

Article 156 of the Criminal Code is replaced as follows:

"When the injury has resulted in abortion, danger to life, illness cured after more than sixty days or incurable, permanent disfigurement of the face, loss or significant weakening of a sense, an organ or its functionality, or the loss of the ability to procreate, or genital mutilation, even if inflicted for religious reasons, imprisonment and disqualification of the third degree shall apply.

The same penalty applies if the victim is or was a spouse or person who is or was bound in a similar emotional relationship, even without cohabitation, or if the victim is a particularly vulnerable person living with the perpetrator".

#### Art. 7

(Reduction or maintenance in slavery or servitude)

Article 167 of the Criminal Code is amended as follows:

"Art. 167

Reduction or maintenance in slavery or servitude

Anyone who exercises over a person powers corresponding to those of property rights or anyone who reduces or maintains a person in a state of continuous subjection, forcing them to provide work or sexual services or to beg or in any case services that involve their exploitation, shall be punished with imprisonment of the fifth degree and disqualification of the fourth degree.

Reduction or maintenance in a state of slavery occurs when the conduct is carried out through violence, threat, deception, abuse of authority or taking advantage of a situation of physical or psychological inferiority or a situation of necessity, or by means of the promise or giving of sums of money or other benefits to those who have authority over the person.

The penalty is increased by one degree if the acts referred to in the first paragraph are committed against a minor under eighteen years of age or are aimed at the exploitation of prostitution or for the purpose of subjecting the victim to organ removal.".

#### Art. 8

(Trafficking in persons)

Article 168 of the Penal Code is replaced as follows:

"Trafficking in persons

Whoever commits trafficking or otherwise trades in a person who is in the conditions referred to in Article 167, or, in order to reduce or maintain a person in slavery or servitude, induces them by deception or coerces them by violence, threat, abuse of authority or exploitation of a situation of physical or mental inferiority or a situation of necessity, or by promise or giving of sums of money or other advantages to the person who has authority over them, to enter or stay or leave the territory of the State or to move within it, is punished with sixth degree imprisonment and fourth degree interdiction.

The penalty is increased by one degree if the acts referred to in the first paragraph are committed against a minor under eighteen years of age or are directed at the exploitation of prostitution or for the purpose of subjecting the victim to organ removal.".

#### Art. 9

(Abrogation of trafficking for the purpose of prostitution and placement of inducement to prostitution among crimes against personal freedom)

Article 268 of the Penal Code is abrogated.

Article 269 of the Penal Code constitutes a crime against personal freedom, and is placed in Chapter II of the Penal Code with the number Article 168 bis (inducement to prostitution).

#### Art. 10

(Aggravating circumstance in the offense of violation of sexual freedom)

The following paragraph is added to Article 172 of the Penal Code: "The penalty is increased by one degree if the act referred to in the first paragraph is committed by the spouse or cohabitant, or by one who has or has had an emotional relationship with the victim.

The penalty is increased by one degree if the act is committed against a person with disabilities".

#### Art. 11

(Group violence)

After Article 172 of the Penal Code the following is inserted:

"Art.172 bis

Group sexual violence

Group sexual violence consists in the participation, by at least two persons gathered together, in acts of violation of sexual freedom referred to in Article 171.

Participation in acts of violation of sexual freedom also exists for those who do not materially commit the violence, but who have nonetheless provided a causal contribution to the commission of the offense, consisting also in the mere presence at the place and time when the violence is perpetrated.

Whoever commits acts of group sexual violence is punished with fourth degree imprisonment and a fine.

The penalty is reduced by one degree for the participant whose work has had minimal importance in the preparation or execution of the offense, as well as for those who were determined to commit the offense when the conditions established by numbers 2 and 3 of Article 90 concur.".

#### Art. 12

(Prosecutability in offenses of violation of sexual freedom)

Article 178 of the Penal Code is replaced as follows:

"For the prosecutability of offenses provided for by Articles 171, 172, 172 bis, 173, 175, 176 and 177, a complaint by the victim is required.

The withdrawal of the complaint can no longer be made after the expiry of the term referred to in the third paragraph of Article 7 of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

In the event that the victim is a minor, the statute of limitations for the offense, as well as the term for filing the complaint, begins to run from the attainment of majority. Proceedings are ex officio if the act is committed by an ascendant, guardian or adoptive parent or by the person who has care or custody of the victim.

Proceedings are also ex officio if the act is committed in conjunction with an offense prosecutable ex officio, or in case of repeated recidivism".

#### Art. 13

(Persecutory acts – Stalking-Mobbing)

After Article 181 of the Penal Code the following is inserted:

"Art. 181 bis

Persecutory acts

Whoever repeatedly harasses or threatens a person in such a way as to cause them serious moral suffering and harm to their dignity, to the point of disturbing their normal living conditions or placing them in a state of subjection or serious physical or mental distress, or justified fear for the personal safety of themselves or others connected to them by stable emotional relationships, is punished, upon complaint by the victim, with first degree imprisonment and a fine.

When the harassment or threats referred to in the first paragraph are carried out in the workplace, in the form of systematic and repeated harassment and vexatious practices carried out by the employer or colleagues for the purpose of professionally devaluing, humiliating, isolating a worker in an attempt to induce them, after having caused them serious psycho-physical suffering, to resign, the penalty is increased by one degree.

Proceedings are upon complaint by the victim.

Proceedings are ex officio and the penalty of imprisonment is increased by one degree if the act is committed with a weapon, with violence or with a threat of particular gravity.

Proceedings are also ex officio if the act is connected with another offense for which ex officio prosecutability is provided.".

#### Art. 14

(Abduction and retention of a minor abroad)

After Article 231 of the Penal Code the following is added:

"Art. 231 bis

Abduction and retention of a minor abroad

Whoever abducts a minor from the person who has custody, regardless of the exercise of parental authority, by taking them abroad or by failing to have them return to San Marino, against the will of the same parent or guardian is punished with second degree imprisonment and a fine. If the act is committed against a minor who has reached the age of fourteen and with their consent, first degree imprisonment applies.

If the act is committed by one of the parents, second degree interdiction from parental authority also applies.".

#### Art. 15

(Ill-treatment of family members and cohabitants)

Article 235 of the Penal Code is replaced by the following:

"Ill-treatment of family members and cohabitants

Whoever inflicts ill-treatment on a family member or cohabitant in any case, or on a person subject to their authority or entrusted to them, is punished with second degree imprisonment. The imprisonment is of third degree if the act is committed against a person under the age of fourteen.

The imprisonment is of fourth degree if one of the aggravating events indicated in Article 156 derives from the act and of fifth degree if death results from it".

### CAPO III

JUDICIAL MEASURES FOR THE PROTECTION AND SECURITY OF VICTIMS

## TITOLO I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Art. 16

(Protection of the privacy of the victim)

In civil or criminal proceedings relating to violence against a person, including in the family context, the privacy and intimacy of the victim, of their personal data, of those of their children or of any other person who is under their custody must be protected.

The disclosure of the personal details and image of victims is always prohibited.

Whoever discloses or publishes data, information, news or images in violation of the foregoing prohibitions is punished with a fine of 12,000.00 euros.

#### Art. 17

(Legal Assistance)

In all civil, criminal or administrative proceedings, the victim of violence is ensured legal assistance, even outside the conditions for legal aid, when they are not objectively able to provide for their own procedural defense.

The Order of Lawyers and Notaries shall establish a list of expert members available to accept assignments in favor of victims, communicating it to the competent Social Services, Law Enforcement Forces, the Court and the Authority for Equal Opportunities.

The Order of Lawyers and Notaries shall provide permanent and specific training for those enrolled in the list, organizing interdisciplinary training courses.

The lawyer enrolled in the list may not refuse the assignment, except for serious and proven reasons.

Legal assistance is free of charge. The lawyer, however, is entitled to payment of fees at the expense of the guilty party or the person who committed the act, when the violence has been ascertained by final civil or criminal judgment or results from the acts by a specific declaration issued in adversarial proceedings by the Judge ordering the dismissal of the criminal proceedings or the civil case, or when protection orders have been issued against them.

In case of absolute necessity and urgency, legal assistance to the victim shall be provided by the Court-Appointed Lawyer, who shall promptly contact a lawyer enrolled in the list, by whom, once the urgency has ceased, they shall be replaced.

Procedural acts performed in the interest of the victim of violence are exempt from all taxes.

The State shall advance the expenses necessary for the performance of or participation in procedural acts, including judicial expert reports, necessary for the protection of the victim of violence. The State has the right of recourse against the guilty party when the violence has been ascertained by final civil or criminal judgment or results from the acts by a specific declaration issued in adversarial proceedings by the Judge ordering the dismissal of the criminal proceedings or the civil case, or when protection orders have been issued against them.

## TITOLO II

PROTECTIVE MEASURES IN CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS

#### Art. 18

(Representation of Minors in Criminal Proceedings)

Where the victim of offenses against personal freedom or of ill-treatment is a minor and the act is committed by the ascendant, the guardian, the adoptive parent, or by other family members or third parties having significant relationships with the minor or with their parents, the representation of the minor in the proceedings, for the protection of their rights, shall be entrusted to a special curator, specifically appointed by the Guardianship Judge, whom the Investigating Judge must immediately request.

If the offense referred to in the first paragraph is not prosecutable ex officio, the complaint shall be filed by the special curator, and the time limit for filing the complaint shall run from the appointment. Procedural acts detrimental to the interest of the minor in which the special curator has not participated are null and void.

For the legal assistance of the minor represented by the curator, the provisions of Article 17 of this law shall apply.

#### Art. 19

(Reporting Obligations)

Social Services, Law Enforcement Forces, and all practitioners of a health profession, both public and private, are required to report to the Commissioner of the Law exercising the functions of Civil Guardianship Judge facts of violence against women, minors or gender-based violence, of which they become aware by reason of their office or profession, even if these are offenses prosecutable upon complaint by the injured party.

Teachers of any order and level are required to promptly report to the Minors Service the facts referred to in the above paragraph of which they have become aware.

The report does not constitute a violation of official or professional secrecy; the

Commissioner of the Law shall ensure that the report and the acts of the proceedings are kept confidential.

Violation of the reporting obligation shall result in an administrative pecuniary sanction imposed by the Commissioner of the Law of 500 euros.

The Commissioner of the Law, upon receiving the report, shall instruct the Social Services to carry out all appropriate verifications; upon completion, on the basis of the specific report prepared, shall summon the victim, and, if necessary, activate the protection measures provided for by this law, instructing the competent Services.

Where the facts constitute offenses prosecutable ex officio, or, if requiring private initiative, in the event that the victim has filed a complaint, the report referred to in the first paragraph must be made to the Commissioner of the Law Investigating Judge, who shall activate the protection measures and programs, if necessary. If the victim of the crime of violence is a minor, the Investigating Judge is required to give timely notification of the news of the offense to the Guardianship Judge, for measures within their competence.

#### Art. 20

(Right to Intervene in Criminal Proceedings)

In proceedings for violence against women, minors or gender-based violence, the Authority for Equal Opportunities has the right to intervene and to join as a civil party.

For this purpose, the Investigating Judge shall give timely notification of the existence of criminal proceedings to the Authority for Equal Opportunities.

#### Art. 21

(Prohibition of Questions About the Private Life or Sexuality of Victims)

In criminal proceedings for sexual offenses, including during police investigations, questions or the formulation of expert questions about the private life or sexuality of the injured person are not permitted unless they are necessary for the reconstruction of the facts.

#### Art. 22

(Special precautionary measures in criminal proceedings)

The Investigating Judge, when proceeding for an offence against personal safety, personal freedom or family maltreatment, committed by a cohabiting person, at the request of the injured party, may order the removal of the suspect or the defendant from the family home, with an order not to return and not to access it without his/her authorization, indicating, if necessary, the visitation arrangements.

The Investigating Judge, where there are needs to protect the safety of the injured party or his/her close relatives, always at the request of the injured party, may also prescribe to the suspect or the accused not to approach certain places habitually frequented by the injured party, in particular the workplace, the domicile of the family of origin or of close relatives, unless attendance is necessary for work reasons. In this latter case, the Judge prescribes the related arrangements and may impose limitations.

The Judge, having received the request, acquires all appropriate information, and provides by reasoned decree, having heard the defendant and, if necessary, the applicant, except in cases of urgency. The Investigating Judge, at the request of the injured party, and in compliance with the adversarial principle, may also order the periodic payment of an allowance in favor of cohabiting persons who, as a result of the precautionary measure ordered, remain without adequate means. The Judge determines the amount of the allowance taking into account the circumstances and income of the obligor and establishes the methods and terms of payment. He/She may order, if necessary, that the allowance be paid directly to the beneficiary by the employer of the obligor, deducting it from the remuneration due to him/her. The payment order has the effect of an enforceable title.

The provisions referred to in the second and fourth paragraphs may be adopted also subsequently to the provision referred to in the first paragraph, provided that this has not been revoked or has not otherwise lost effect. Such measures, even if adopted subsequently, lose effect if the provision referred to in the first paragraph is revoked or otherwise loses effect. The provision referred to in the fourth paragraph, if in favor of the spouse or children, loses effect, moreover, if the provision of the Civil Judge in the judicial separation case or another provision of the Civil Judge regarding the economic-patrimonial relations between the spouses or the maintenance of the children supervenes.

The provision referred to in the fourth paragraph may be modified if the conditions of the obligor or the beneficiary change, and is revoked if cohabitation resumes.

#### Art. 23

(Psychological support for victims of violence in criminal proceedings and other protective measures in criminal investigation)

When proceeding for offences against personal safety, personal freedom or for maltreatment committed to the detriment of the person, psychological support to the victim by experts must be ensured when he/she is heard as a witness or during confrontations with the suspect or with other witnesses.

When in the proceedings for one of the offences referred to in the first paragraph a judicial and medico-legal expert examination must be carried out, the expert must preferably be chosen from professionals of the same sex as the victim.

The hearing of the injured party must take place in a manner to avoid its repetition. To this end, the Investigating Judge adopts every appropriate measure, including the video recording of the hearing.

In the case where the injured party is a minor, the Investigating Judge proceeds with the examination of the victim of the offence, in confrontation with the defendant or with witnesses, through the use of a mirror glass and an intercom system or through the use of other suitable equipment that guarantees his/her privacy; the hearing must be video recorded. The minor must always be assisted by an expert in child psychology auxiliary to the Judge.

#### Art. 24

(Protection of victims at trial)

In criminal proceedings for offences against personal safety, personal freedom or for maltreatment, the trial always takes place in closed session if the injured party is a minor, and at the request of the injured party who is of age.

There is no repetition of testimonies and confrontations if the right to defense of the defendant was guaranteed during the investigation, and, in any case, where there is video recording.

In the case where the hearing or confrontation must be repeated, the provisions referred to in Article 23 are observed. If the injured party is a minor, the repetition cannot be ordered where there is a concrete danger of worsening of the minor's conditions, which must be ascertained through judicial expert examination, in adversarial proceedings with the technical consultants of the parties.

#### Art. 25

(Probation to social services of those convicted for acts of sexual and family violence)

Probation to social services of those convicted for acts of sexual or family violence, in cases where it is permitted by law, may take place only if accompanied by the participation of the convicted person in a specific rehabilitation program.

## TITOLO III

CIVIL PROTECTIVE MEASURES

#### Art. 26

(Protection against family abuse)

When the conduct of the spouse or other cohabiting partner causes serious prejudice to the physical or moral integrity or to the freedom of the other spouse or cohabiting partner, the Judge, where the fact does not constitute an offence prosecutable ex officio or, if of private initiative, where a complaint has not been filed, upon application of a party, may adopt by decree one or more of the provisions referred to in the following article.

In any case, the conducts referred to in the first paragraph constitute just cause for removal from the family home pursuant to Article 30 of Law No. 49 of 26 April 1986.

The provisions of this title apply, insofar as compatible, also in the case where the prejudicial conduct has been carried out by another member of the family unit other than the spouse or cohabiting partner. In such case, the application is filed by the member of the family unit to the detriment of whom the prejudicial conduct is carried out.

#### Art. 27

(Protection orders against domestic abuse)

The Judge orders the spouse or cohabitant who has engaged in the prejudicial conduct to cease such conduct, and orders the removal from the family home of the spouse or cohabitant who has engaged in the prejudicial conduct, also prescribing, where necessary, that they not approach places habitually frequented by the applicant, and in particular the workplace, the domicile of the family of origin, or the domicile of other close relatives or other persons and in proximity to the places of education of the couple's children, unless they must frequent the same places for work requirements.

The Judge may also order, where necessary,

- the intervention of social services or a family mediation center, as well as associations whose statutory purpose is the support and reception of women and minors or other victims of abuse and mistreatment;

- the periodic payment of an allowance in favor of cohabiting persons who, as a result of the provisions of the first paragraph, are left without adequate means, setting the methods and terms of payment and prescribing, if necessary, that the sum be paid directly to the entitled person by the employer of the obligated party, deducting it from the salary due to them. By the same decree, the Judge, in the cases referred to in the preceding paragraphs, establishes the duration of the protection order, which runs from the day of its execution. This cannot exceed six months and may be extended, upon application of a party, only if serious reasons exist for the time strictly necessary.

By the same decree, the Judge determines the methods of implementation. Where removal from the family home is ordered, the Commissario della Legge provides for the assistance of the Police Forces and the forced removal of the recipient of the order who does not comply voluntarily. The Commissario della Legge may also indicate suitable measures to prevent subsequent violations of the provisions of the measure, including surveillance and assistance of the Police Forces.

The decree is always communicated to the Gendarmeria and to the Neuro-Psychiatric Service for the possible adoption of measures regarding weapons and ammunition.

#### Art. 28

(Procedure for protection orders against domestic abuse)

The application may also be filed by the party personally. The Judge, in such case, having received the application, appoints a defender from those enrolled in the list referred to in the second paragraph of Article 17. The Commissario della Legge, having heard the parties, proceeds in the manner deemed most appropriate to the necessary evidentiary acts, acquiring, also ex officio, all appropriate information, and issues an immediately enforceable reasoned decree.

In cases of urgency, the Judge, having obtained, where necessary, summary information, may immediately adopt the protection order, setting the hearing for the appearance of the parties before them within a period not exceeding fifteen days. At the hearing, the Judge confirms, modifies or revokes the protection order.

Against the decree by which the Judge adopts the protection order or rejects the application, pursuant to the second paragraph, or confirms, modifies or revokes the previously adopted protection order in the case referred to in the preceding paragraph, an appeal for nullity to the Civil Court of Appeal is permitted. The appeal does not suspend the execution of the protection order, unless the Court of Appeal decides otherwise.

#### Art. 29

(Sanctions)

Anyone who evades the protection order provided for by Article 27 of this law, or a measure of equal content adopted in the proceedings for personal separation of spouses or in the proceedings for dissolution or cessation of the civil effects of marriage is punished with the penalty established by Article 366 of the Criminal Code.

#### Art. 30

(Scope of application of protection orders)

The provisions of Articles 27 and 28 of this law do not apply when the prejudicial conduct is engaged in by the spouse who has filed or against whom an application for personal separation or for dissolution or cessation of the civil effects of marriage has been filed if the hearing for the appearance of the spouses referred to in Articles 110 and 127 of Law No. 49 of 26 April 1986 has been held in the relevant proceedings. In such case, the Judge may adopt protection orders in such proceedings.

The protection order adopted pursuant to Articles 27 and 28 loses effect if a decree containing temporary and urgent measures is subsequently issued in the proceedings for personal separation or dissolution or cessation of the civil effects of marriage initiated by the applicant spouse or against them.

#### Art. 31

(Suspension of parental authority)

When the act of violence is committed to the detriment of minors, and until the determination of responsibility, the Commissario della Legge may suspend the exercise of parental authority of the suspected person or of the parent who tolerated the violence.

## TITOLO IV

PREVENTIVE INTERVENTIONS BY THE POLICE FORCES

#### Art. 32

(Request for help to the Police Forces)

When the victim of violence or third parties who witnessed the fact report the violence to the Police Forces, they must intervene immediately, and in any case no later than one hour from the report, unless serious reasons exist.

The Police Forces may enter the victim's dwelling or other places or appurtenances of private property where the victim is located, even coercively; they must put an end to the harmful behavior; they must inform the victim of their rights, including the right to request protection orders; if there is fear of serious and irreparable harm, they must make an immediate report to the competent social services, unless it is an offense prosecutable ex officio or the victim has filed a complaint, in which case the report must be made to the Investigating Judge, who may adopt appropriate precautionary measures, including those indicated by this law.

The Police Forces proceed in any case to seize weapons present in the abuser's dwelling, giving notice to the Commissario della Legge and to the Command of the Gendarmeria for the activation of the procedure for suspension or revocation of the gun license or hunting license.

The obligation of immediate intervention by the Police Forces also exists in the case where they receive from anyone a report of abusive subjects who are or are about to drive vehicles while intoxicated, adopting the necessary preventive and precautionary measures. If the request for intervention concerns acts of persecution as defined by law, the Police Forces, even independently of the complaint of the injured person, are required to remove the harasser, making the reports referred to in this law.

For all interventions, a specific report must be drawn up, which must be transmitted to the Command of the Gendarmeria and to the Neuropsychiatric Service.

The data thus collected are transmitted to the Authority for Equal Opportunities and are also available to the Civil Judge requested to issue protection orders.

#### Art. 33

Article 3 of Law 25 February 2004 no.26 is amended as follows:

"Art 3

(Composition)

The Commission is appointed by the Grand and General Council at the beginning of the legislature and for its duration. It is composed of:

- eight members chosen proportionally from the groups present in the Grand and General Council;
- one member designated by the Trade Union Organizations;
- one member designated by the Council of Sammarinese Cultural Associations and Cooperatives.

The members of the Commission cannot be members of the Grand and General Council.".

#### Art. 34

(Transitional provision)

The Commission for Equal Opportunities established by Law 25 February 2004 no. 26 is replaced for the competences provided by this law by the Authority for Equal Opportunities. The Commission for Equal Opportunities in the exercise of the functions and attributions provided for in article 2 points a, g, i, j, k, l of Law 25 February 2004 no.26, from the entry into force of this law, is supported by the Authority for Equal Opportunities.

The Authority for Equal Opportunities is composed of three members appointed by the Grand

and General Council who remain in office for 4 years from among experts in legal matters, representatives of associations or non-governmental organizations active in the field of Equal Opportunities, experts in communication and psychology.

The composition of the Authority for Equal Opportunities must ensure the participation of each of the professional figures identified above.

The Authority for Equal Opportunities provides for the collection of data relating to violence against women and gender-based violence on a half-yearly basis.

The Authority for Equal Opportunities draws up an annual report that must be publicly disseminated.

#### Art. 35

(Entry into force)

This law enters into force on the fifth day following that of its legal publication.
