Promulgation of the Criminal Code

Hereby is promulgated the Criminal Code, cf. consolidated act no. 1007 of 24 October 2012, with the amendments resulting from § 2 of act no. 1242 of 18 December 2012, § 2 of act no. 1385 of 23 December 2012, § 2 of act no. 431 of 1 May 2013, § 1 of act no. 434 of 1 May 2013, § 1 of act no. 628 of 12 June 2013, § 1 of act no. 633 of 12 June 2013, § 1 of act no. 634 of 12 June 2013 and § 3 of act no. 642 of 12 June 2013. General part

1. kapitel

Introductory provisions

### § 1. Punishment may only be imposed for a circumstance whose punishability is provided by law, or which must be entirely equated with such. With regard to the legal consequences mentioned in chapter 9, a corresponding rule applies.

### § 2. The chapters 1-11 of this act apply, unless otherwise provided, to all punishable circumstances.

2. kapitel

General conditions for the application of criminal law provisions

### § 3. If the criminal legislation in force at the adjudication of an act differs from that which applied at the commission of the act, the question of punishability and punishment shall be determined according to the later law, provided that the decision shall not thereby become more severe than under the earlier law. If the lapse of the law's validity is based on external circumstances unrelated to culpability, the act shall be assessed according to the earlier law.

Stk. 2. If, outside the latter case, the punishability of an act lapses by law, the punishment imposed for such act, but not executed, also lapses. The convicted person may demand that the question of the lapse of punishment be brought before the court which heard the case at first instance by the prosecution authority. The decision is made by order.

### § 4. Whether the punishable act shall result in legal consequences of the kind mentioned in §§ 56-61, 62-70, 73 and 79, shall be determined according to the legislation in force at the adjudication of the act.

Stk. 2. Other legal consequences of the act occur, unless otherwise provided, only when this was also provided by the legislation in force at the commission of the act.

Stk. 3. The provision in § 3, stk. 2, applies correspondingly to legal consequences of the act other than punishment, if their occurrence was conditional upon the punishability of the act.

### § 5.

Where increased punishment or other legal consequences are prescribed in cases of repetition, decisions made pursuant to earlier law shall be taken into account according to their content equally with decisions under the law pursuant to which the present act is assessed.

### § 6. Under Danish criminal jurisdiction are acts which are committed

1) in the Danish state,

2) on a Danish vessel, which is located within a foreign jurisdiction, by a person who belongs to the vessel or accompanies it as a passenger, or

3) on a Danish vessel, which is located outside a jurisdiction.

### § 7. Under Danish criminal jurisdiction are acts which are committed within a foreign jurisdiction by a person who at the time of indictment has Danish nationality, is resident in the Danish state or has similar permanent residence in this country, if

1) the act is also punishable under the legislation of the place of commission (double punishability) or

2) the perpetrator also at the time of commission has the mentioned connection to this country and the act

a) comprises sexual exploitation of children, human trafficking or female circumcision or

b) is directed against someone who at the time of commission has the mentioned connection to this country.

Stk. 2. Under Danish criminal jurisdiction are also acts which are committed within a foreign jurisdiction by a person who at the time of indictment has the connection to this country mentioned in stk. 1, if acts of the kind in question may result in a punishment higher than imprisonment for 4 months.

Stk. 3. Stk. 1, no. 1, and stk. 2 apply correspondingly with regard to acts committed by a person who at the time of indictment has nationality or is resident in Finland, Iceland, Norway or Sweden, and who is present in this country.

### § 7

a. Under Danish criminal jurisdiction are acts which are committed within a foreign jurisdiction and which are directed against someone who at the time of commission has Danish nationality, is resident in the Danish state or has similar permanent residence in this country, if the act is also punishable under the legislation of the place of commission (double punishability) and under Danish law may result in a punishment of imprisonment for at least 6 years.

Stk. 2. Danish criminal jurisdiction according to stk. 1 is conditional upon the act comprising

1) intentional homicide,

2) aggravated violence, deprivation of liberty or robbery,

3) a generally dangerous crime,

4) a sexual crime or incest or

5) female circumcision.

Stk. 3. Under Danish criminal jurisdiction are also acts which are committed within a foreign jurisdiction and which are directed against someone who at the time of commission has the connection to this country mentioned in stk. 1, if acts of the kind in question may result in a punishment higher than imprisonment for 4 months.

### § 7

b. When Danish criminal jurisdiction over a legal person is conditional upon double punishability, it does not include a requirement that the legislation of the place of commission provides criminal liability for a legal person.

### § 8. Under Danish criminal jurisdiction are acts which are committed outside the Danish state, regardless of where the perpetrator belongs, when

1) the act violates the Danish state's independence, security, constitution or public authorities or an official duty towards the state,

2) the act violates interests whose legal protection in the Danish state presupposes a special connection to it,

3) the act violates an obligation which according to law the perpetrator is required to observe abroad,

4) the act violates a duty of service which the perpetrator owes to a Danish vessel,

5) the act is covered by an international provision according to which Denmark is obliged to have criminal jurisdiction, or

6) extradition of the accused for prosecution in another country is refused, and the act, insofar as it is committed within a foreign jurisdiction, is punishable under the legislation of the place of commission (double punishability) and the act under Danish law may result in a punishment of imprisonment for at least 1 year.

### § 8

a. Under Danish criminal jurisdiction are acts which are committed outside the Danish state, when acts of the kind in question are covered by the statute of the International Criminal Court, if the act is committed by a person who at the time of indictment

1) has Danish nationality, is resident in the Danish state or has similar permanent residence in this country or

2) is present in this country.

### § 8

b. Under Danish criminal jurisdiction are acts which are committed outside the Danish state, when the act is covered by § 183 a and is committed by a person who at the time of indictment

1) has Danish nationality, is resident in the Danish state or has similar permanent residence in this country or

2) is present in this country.

Stk. 2. Prosecution for acts covered by stk. 1 may also include violations of §§ 237 and 244-248, which are committed in connection with the violation of § 183 a.

### § 9.

Acts are considered to have been committed where the perpetrator was located at the time of committing the act. With regard to legal persons, acts are considered to have been committed where the act or acts that entail liability for the legal person have been committed.

Stk. 2. If the punishability of an act depends on or is affected by an occurred or intended consequence, the act is also considered to have been committed where the effect has occurred, or where the perpetrator had intent for the effect to occur.

Stk. 3. Acts of attempt and complicity are considered to have been committed in the Danish State if the perpetrator was located in this country at the time of committing the act, regardless of whether the offence is completed or is intended to be completed outside the Danish State.

Stk. 4. When a part of an offence has been committed in the Danish State, the offence in its entirety is considered to have been committed in this country.

### § 9 a.

An offence concerning text, audio or image material etc., which through acts abroad has been made generally accessible in this country through the internet or a similar system for disseminating information, is considered to have been committed in the Danish State if the material has a special connection to this country.

### § 10. When an act is prosecuted in this country, the decision on both punishment and other legal consequences of

the act shall be made in accordance with Danish legislation.

Stk. 2. When Danish criminal jurisdiction is conditional on double criminality, no more severe punishment may be imposed than provided for by the legislation at the place of the act.

### § 10 a.

A person against whom a criminal judgment has been rendered outside the Danish State cannot be prosecuted in this country for the same act when this is excluded pursuant to Denmark's international obligations concerning recognition of the legal force of criminal judgments.

Stk. 2. In other cases, a person against whom a criminal judgment has been rendered outside the Danish State cannot be prosecuted in this country for the same act if

1) the person has been definitively acquitted,

2) the imposed sanction has been served, is being served or has lapsed according to the legislation in the country of judgment, or

3) the person has been found guilty without any sanction being imposed.

Stk. 3. Stk. 2 does not apply when

1) the foreign criminal judgment concerns acts that

a) are considered to have been committed in the Danish State pursuant to § 9 or § 9 a, or

b) are covered by § 7, stk. 1, no. 2, or § 8, no. 1 or 2,

2) prosecution in this country takes place pursuant to section 985 a of the Administration of Justice Act, or

3) recognition of the foreign criminal judgment would be manifestly incompatible with Danish legal principles.

Stk. 4. In the cases mentioned in stk. 3, no. 1, prosecution in this country cannot take place when prosecution in the country of judgment has taken place at the request of the Danish prosecution authority.

### § 10 b.

If prosecution takes place against a person who has been imposed a sanction outside the Danish State for the same act, the sanction imposed in this country shall be reduced to the extent the foreign sanction has been served.

### § 11. If a person who has Danish nationality, is resident in the Danish State or has similar permanent residence

in this country, has been punished in a foreign state for an act that under Danish law may result in disqualification or forfeiture of profession or occupation or other right, disqualification thereof may take place following public prosecution in this country.

### § 12.

The application of §§ 6-11 is limited by the exceptions recognized in international law.

3. kapitel

Conditions for punishability

### § 13. Acts committed in self-defense are not punishable insofar as they have been necessary to resist

or avert a commenced or imminent unlawful attack and do not manifestly exceed what, having regard to the danger of the attack, the person of the attacker and the importance of the legal interest attacked, is justifiable.

Stk. 2. If anyone exceeds the limits of lawful self-defense, he nevertheless becomes exempt from punishment if the excess is reasonably justified by the fear or agitation caused by the attack.

Stk. 3. Corresponding rules apply to acts that are necessary to lawfully obtain compliance with lawful orders, effect a lawful apprehension or prevent the escape of a prisoner or a person subject to involuntary placement.

### § 14.

An act that would otherwise be punishable is not punished when it was necessary to avert threatening damage to person or property, and the offence could be considered to be of relatively minor importance.

### § 15. Acts committed by children under 15 years are not punished.

### § 16. Persons who at the time of the act were not criminally responsible due to insanity or conditions that

must be equated therewith are not punished. The same applies to persons who were mentally retarded to a higher degree. If the perpetrator temporarily was in a state of insanity or in a state that must be equated therewith as a result of consumption of alcohol or other intoxicants, punishment may however be imposed when special circumstances argue for it.

Stk. 2. Persons who at the time of the act were mentally retarded to a lesser degree are not punished unless special circumstances argue for imposing punishment. The same applies to persons who were in a state that must entirely be equated with mental retardation.

### §§ 17-18. (Ophævet)

### § 19. Negligence is punished for the offences referred to in this Act only when it is specifically

provided for. For other offences, the relevant penal provisions are applicable also when the offence has been committed by negligence, unless the opposite is specifically provided for.

### § 20. Where punishment or increased punishment is conditional on an intentional offence having a certain unintentional

consequence, this punishment only applies when such consequence can be attributed to the perpetrator as negligent, or he has failed to the best of his ability to avert it after becoming aware of the danger.

4. kapitel

Attempt and complicity

### § 21.

Acts that aim to promote or effect the execution of a crime are punished, when it is not completed, as attempt.

Stk. 2. The punishment prescribed for the offence may be reduced for attempt, in particular when the attempt shows little strength or firmness in the criminal intent.

Stk. 3. Unless otherwise provided, attempt is only punished when a punishment exceeding imprisonment for 4 months may be imposed for the offence.

### § 22. Attempt is not punished when the perpetrator voluntarily and not due to random obstacles to

the execution of the act or to the achievement of the objective intended thereby refrains from the implementation or prevents the completion or performs acts that would have prevented the completion if it had not, unknown to him, failed or been averted by other means.

### § 23. The penal provision given for an offence covers all who by instigation, advice

or act have contributed to the act. The punishment may be reduced for anyone who only wanted to provide less essential assistance or strengthen an already formed intent, and when the crime is not completed or an intended contribution has failed.

Stk. 2. The punishment may likewise be reduced for anyone who contributes to violation of a special duty relationship but is himself outside this.

Stk. 3. Unless otherwise provided, punishment for complicity in offences that are not punished with a punishment higher than imprisonment for 4 months may lapse when the accomplice only wanted to provide less essential assistance or strengthen an already formed intent, and when his contribution is due to negligence.

### § 24. The accomplice is not punished if he under the conditions stated in § 22 prevents the completion

or performs acts that would have prevented the completion if it had not, unknown to him, failed or been averted by other means.

5. kapitel

Criminal liability for legal persons

### § 25.

A legal person may be punished with a fine when it is provided by or pursuant to law.

### § 26.

Provisions on criminal liability for companies etc. cover, unless otherwise determined, any legal person, including public limited companies, private limited companies and cooperative societies, partnerships, associations, foundations, estates, municipalities and state authorities.

Stk. 2. Furthermore, such provisions cover sole proprietorships, insofar as these, particularly having regard to their size and organization, can be equated with the companies mentioned in paragraph 1.

### § 27. Criminal liability for a legal person presupposes that an offence has been committed within its business that can be attributed to one or more persons connected to the legal person or to the legal person as such. As regards punishment for attempt, section 21, paragraph 3, applies correspondingly. Stk. 2. State authorities and municipalities can only be punished for offences committed in the exercise of activities that correspond to or can be equated with activities carried out by private parties.

6. kapitel Penalties

### § 31. The ordinary penalties are imprisonment and fines.

### § 32. (Repealed)

### § 33.

Imprisonment is imposed for life or for a term, not less than 7 days and not more than 16 years.

Stk. 2. In cases where there is authority to increase the penalty prescribed for an offence, imprisonment of up to 20 years may however be imposed.

Stk. 3. An offender who had not reached the age of 18 when the offence was committed cannot be sentenced to life imprisonment.

Stk. 4. When imprisonment is imposed for a period shorter than 3 months, the sentence is set in days, otherwise in months and years.

### § 38.

When two-thirds of the sentence, but at least 2 months, has been served, the Minister of Justice or a person authorized by him decides whether the convicted person shall be released on probation.

Stk. 2. Release on probation may take place earlier when special circumstances warrant it and the convicted person has served half of the sentence, but at least 2 months. Decision thereon is made by the Minister of Justice or a person authorized by him with respect to specific categories of cases.

Stk. 3. Probation release cannot take place with respect to the unconditional part of a judgment under section 58, paragraph 1. Stk. 4. Release on probation presupposes that the convicted person's circumstances do not make the release inadvisable, that adequate accommodation and employment or other maintenance has been secured for him, and that he declares his willingness to comply with the conditions for release set under section 39, paragraph 2.

### § 39.

Release is conditional upon the person concerned not committing a criminal offence during the probationary period. The probationary period cannot exceed 3 years. If the remaining sentence exceeds 3 years, however, a probationary period of up to 5 years may be set.

Stk. 2. It may be set as a condition for release that the person concerned be placed under supervision for the whole or part of the probationary period. Additional conditions may be set according to the rules in section 57. Conditions concerning residence in a home, hospital or other institution are not valid for a longer period than the remaining sentence.

### § 40. If the person released on probation commits a new criminal offence during the probationary period and legal proceedings are instituted before its expiry whereby he is charged with the offence, the court makes a decision in accordance with section 61, paragraph 2, so that the remaining sentence is treated as a suspended sentence. If probation release pursuant to section 40 a, paragraph 3 or 4, has been combined with a condition of community service, the extent of community service performed must be taken into account when setting a combined sentence.

Stk. 2. If the person released on probation otherwise violates the set conditions, the Minister of Justice may

1) issue a warning,

2) change the conditions and extend the probationary period within the maximum period set in section 39, or

3) in special circumstances determine that he shall be imprisoned to serve the remaining sentence.

Stk. 3. If the person released on probation commits a criminal offence during the probationary period without charges being brought, the rules in paragraph 2 apply correspondingly. The same applies if the person released on probation is convicted abroad of a criminal offence committed during the probationary period without the question of enforcement of the remaining sentence having been addressed in connection with the conviction.

Stk. 4. Decision under paragraph 2, cf. paragraph 3, can only be made before the expiry of the probationary period.

Stk. 5. If no decision is made on enforcement of the remaining sentence under paragraph 1 or paragraph 2, cf. paragraph 3, the sentence is considered served at the time when probation release took place.

Stk. 6. If re-imprisonment is determined pursuant to paragraph 2, cf. paragraph 3, renewed probation release may take place even if the time conditions in section 38, paragraphs 1 and 2, or section 40 a, paragraph 1, are not met with respect to the remaining sentence. In a decision under the 1st sentence, account must be taken of the extent of community service that the person concerned has performed pursuant to section 40 a, paragraph 3 or 4. With regard to the probationary period after such probation release, the periods set in section 39 apply with a deduction of the time during which the person concerned was previously released on probation.

### § 40

a. When half of the sentence, but at least 2 months, has been served, the Minister of Justice or a person authorized by the Minister may, in addition to the cases mentioned in section 38, paragraph 2, decide that the convicted person shall be released on probation if consideration for law enforcement is deemed not to speak against it, and

1) the convicted person has made a special effort not to commit crime again, including by participating in treatment programs, educational programs, work training or work, or

2) the convicted person's circumstances warrant it.

Stk. 2. It is set as a condition for probation release under paragraph 1 that the convicted person be placed under supervision until the point when two-thirds of the sentence has elapsed. After this time, a condition may be set that the person concerned continues to be placed under supervision.

Stk. 3. As a condition for probation release under paragraph 1, no. 1, one or more additional conditions may be set according to the rules in section 57 and a condition that the convicted person shall perform unpaid community service.

Stk. 4. As a condition for probation release under paragraph 1, no. 2, it is set that the convicted person shall perform unpaid community service. Additional conditions may be set according to the rules in section 57.

Stk. 5. Conditions concerning community service cannot be extended beyond two-thirds of the sentence. The supervisory authority may, however, make a decision that conditions concerning community service shall have a duration beyond this time if special reasons warrant it, but not beyond the total sentence.

### § 50. Fines are paid to the state treasury.

Stk. 2. A fine may be imposed as an additional penalty to another type of punishment when the defendant has obtained or intended to obtain economic gain for himself or others through the offense.

Stk. 3. The person fined cannot demand the fine to be paid or compensated by others.

### § 51. When a fine pursuant to this Act is imposed or accepted in court, the fine is determined in

day-fines. This does not, however, apply to fines imposed as an additional penalty to another legal consequence. The number of day-fines is determined with regard to the nature of the offense and the circumstances mentioned in § 80 to a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 60. The amount of each day-fine is set at an amount corresponding to the person's average daily income, although in determining the amount, consideration should be given to the person fined's living conditions, including his assets, support obligations and other circumstances affecting the ability to pay. Stk. 2. When a fine is to be determined for an offense whereby the person has obtained or intended to obtain significant economic gain for himself or others, and the application of day-fines would result in the fine amount being set lower than is deemed reasonable with regard to the size of the profit that has been or could have been obtained through the offense, the court may, however, instead of day-fines, apply another type of fine.

Stk. 3. In determining other fines, within the limits permitted by consideration of the nature of the offense and the circumstances mentioned in § 80, particular regard shall be given to the guilty person's ability to pay and to the profit or savings obtained or intended.

Stk. 4. The police may obtain from other public authorities the information necessary for determining the fine. Furthermore, the police may from registers maintained by public authorities, including the courts, require information about the person's circumstances that are found to be of significance for determining the fine. 7. kapitel

Conditional sentences

### § 56.

If the court finds it unnecessary that punishment be executed, it is determined in the judgment that the question of sentencing is postponed and lapses after a probation period.

Stk. 2. If it must be considered more appropriate, the court may determine the sentence and decide that execution is postponed and lapses upon expiry of the probation period.

Stk. 3. The postponement is conditional upon the convicted person not committing criminal offenses during the probation period, and that he complies with the conditions that may have been stipulated pursuant to § 57. The probation period is determined by the court and generally cannot exceed 3 years. In special circumstances, however, a probation period of up to 5 years may be determined.

### § 57. The court may as a condition for the postponement determine that the convicted person for the entire probation period or a part

thereof shall be subject to supervision. Furthermore, the court may stipulate other conditions deemed appropriate, including that the convicted person

1) complies with special provisions regarding place of residence, work, education, use of leisure time or association with specific persons,

2) takes residence in a suitable home or institution; for such residence, a maximum period is determined by the judgment, which generally cannot exceed 1 year,

3) abstains from abuse of alcohol, narcotics or similar medication,

4) undergoes detoxification treatment for abuse of alcohol, narcotics or similar medication, if necessary in a hospital or special institution,

5) undergoes structured, controlled alcoholism treatment of at least one year's duration,

6) undergoes psychiatric treatment, if necessary in a hospital,

7) complies with the supervisory authority's provisions on restrictions in the disposal of income and assets and on fulfillment of financial obligations,

8) pays compensation for loss caused by the offense,

9) pursuant to the municipality's decision undergoes measures under § 52 of the Social Services Act, possibly of a more specified nature, and complies with the regulations communicated by the municipality to the person.

### § 58. If the application of unconditional imprisonment is deemed necessary, but the information about the defendant's

personal circumstances speaks in favor of application of a conditional sentence pursuant to § 56, the court may decide that a part of the deserved punishment, however at most 6 months, shall be executed, while the judgment is otherwise made conditional. Stk. 2. In connection with a conditional sentence, a fine may be imposed even if such punishment is not provided for the offense in question.

### § 59. The conditions stipulated pursuant to § 57 may later be changed or revoked by court order upon

request from the prosecution authority or the convicted person. A request from the convicted person is made to the prosecution authority, which brings the matter before the court as soon as possible. If a request from the convicted person is not granted, he cannot make a new request until half a year has elapsed from the pronouncement of the order. Stk. 2. The matters mentioned in subsection 1 are brought before the district court that has adjudicated the case at first instance, or the district court in the jurisdiction where the convicted person resides or is present. If the case has been adjudicated by a high court at first instance, the decision is made by the district court in the jurisdiction where the convicted person resides or is present.

Samfundstjeneste

### § 62. If a conditional sentence under the rules in §§ 56 and 57 is not found sufficient, the court may, if the accused

is found suitable for this, issue a conditional sentence with a condition of community service.

Subsection 2. The decision on suspension of imprisonment is made according to the rules in § 56, subsections 1 and 2. Subsection 3. The suspension is conditional upon the convicted person not committing a criminal offence during a probation period, and that he complies with the conditions laid down pursuant to § 63.

### § 63. As a condition for the suspension of imprisonment, it is determined that the convicted person shall perform unpaid

community service for a minimum of 30 and a maximum of 300 hours. The prescribed work obligation must be fulfilled within a maximum period, which is determined in relation to the number of working hours.

Subsection 2. The maximum period laid down pursuant to subsection 1 may be extended by the supervisory authority if special reasons warrant it, but not beyond the probation period. The supervisory authority's decision on extension of the maximum period is brought before the court at the request of the convicted person.

Subsection 3. In the judgment, a probation period of a maximum of 2 years is determined. It may be determined in the judgment that the probation period ceases when the maximum period for fulfillment of the work obligation has expired.

Subsection 4. The convicted person is placed under supervision during the probation period. The court may further, if found appropriate, lay down conditions as referred to in § 57. Supervision ceases, and any conditions under § 57 lapse, when the maximum period for fulfillment of the work obligation has expired, unless otherwise determined in the judgment.

### § 64. In connection with a conditional sentence with a condition of community service, unconditional

imprisonment or a fine may be imposed according to the rules in § 58. However, an imprisonment imposed pursuant to § 58, subsection 1, may not exceed 3 months.

### § 65. The provisions in § 59 on amendment or revocation of conditions apply correspondingly to the

decisions made pursuant to § 63.

### § 66. If the convicted person violates the condition of community service or other condition, the court may

### § 80. In determining the punishment, regard shall be had to uniformity in the application of the law, and

emphasis shall be placed on the seriousness of the offense and on information about the offender.

### § 81.

It shall generally be considered as an aggravating circumstance when determining the punishment,

1) that the perpetrator has previously been punished in a matter of relevance to the case,

2) that the act was committed by several persons jointly,

3) that the act was specially planned or part of extensive criminality,

4) that the perpetrator intended that the act should have considerably more serious consequences than it did,

5) that the perpetrator has shown particular recklessness,

6) that the act has its background in others' ethnic origin, faith, sexual orientation or similar,

7) that the act has its background in the injured party's lawful expressions in the public debate,

8) that the act was committed in the performance of public service or office or through abuse of position or special relationship of trust otherwise,

9) that the perpetrator has induced another to participate in the act by coercion, fraud or exploitation of that person's young age or considerable economic or personal difficulties, lack of insight, recklessness or an existing dependency relationship,

10) that the perpetrator has participated in criminality carried out by a child under 15 years of age,

11) that the perpetrator has exploited the injured party's defenceless position,

12) that the act was committed by a person serving a sentence or other criminal sanction of a deprivation of liberty character,

13) that the act was committed by a former inmate against the institution or a person employed at the institution.

Stk. 4. The limitation period for confiscation claims is 10 years.

### § 97

b. Criminal sanctions cannot be enforced after the death of the convicted person.

Stk. 2. However, the prosecution authority may bring the question of upholding a confiscation before the court that adjudicated the case in the first instance. The confiscation can only be upheld insofar as it concerns objects or amounts obtained as proceeds from the criminal act, or which correspond to such proceeds. The court may amend the decision on confiscation so that an amount is confiscated instead of objects. The court's decision is made by order.

Stk. 3. Decisions under § 164, subsection 4, and § 273, subsection 2, may be enforced after the death of the convicted person.

### § 97

c. In the same manner as for punishment, pardon may be granted for confiscation.

Special Part

Chapter 12

Crimes against the independence and security of the state

### § 98.

Any person who commits an act aimed at, by foreign assistance, by use of force or by threat thereof, bringing the Danish state or any part thereof under foreign domination or seceding any part of the state, shall be punished by imprisonment up to life.

Stk. 2. Under the provision in subsection 1, any person who, with the stated purpose, initiates more extensive sabotage, production or traffic stoppage shall also be punished, as well as any person who participates in such a measure knowing what the act aims at.

### § 99. Any person who commits an act aimed at inflicting war, occupation or other hostilities such as blockade or other coercive measures upon the Danish state or any state allied with it in case of war, or who otherwise works to have the freedom of determination of the Danish state violated by foreign assistance, shall be punished by imprisonment up to life.

Stk. 2. By occupation is understood here and elsewhere in chapters 12 and 13 foreign occupation of Danish state territory, when and as long as it is imposed upon the country by use of force or threat thereof.

### § 100. Any person who, by public statements, incites or creates evident danger of

hostile measures against the Danish state shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 6 years.

Stk. 2. Any person who, by public statements, incites or creates evident danger of foreign power interference in the affairs of the Danish state shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year.

### § 101.

Any person who, with war, occupation or other hostilities in mind, commits any act whereby assistance to the enemy is prepared shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 16 years.

### § 102. Any person who, during war or occupation, provides the enemy with assistance by advice or deed or, to further

hostile interests, weakens the combat readiness of the Danish state or its allies shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 16 years.

Stk. 2. As assistance to the enemy are thus considered the following circumstances:

- 1) Recruitment to or service in a hostile war or occupation power's armed forces or in military or police corps or similar corps or organizations operating in connection with these.
- 2) Performance of duties as a civilian official under a hostile war or occupation power's police or prison service, insofar as the duty includes participation in interrogation or guarding of prisoners.
- 3) Denunciation or similar participation in someone being arrested or subjected to arrest or assault by a hostile authority or organization or person cooperating with it.
- 4) Propaganda in favor of hostile war or occupation power, including activity as publisher, editor or managing director of a newspaper, periodical, publishing house or press agency that works to further hostile interests.
- 5) Provision of substantial economic support to further propaganda of the type mentioned under no. 4 or to a party or organization that, in an improper manner, cooperates with the hostile war or occupation power or works to further its interests.

Stk. 3. If denunciation, cf. subsection 2, no. 3, has taken place under such circumstances that the perpetrator has realized that someone was thereby subjected to imminent danger of losing life, suffering serious injury to body or health, being taken out of the country or being deprived of freedom for a longer period, or if a violation of

### §§ 245, 246 or 250 has been committed in order thereby to coerce a statement or confession or otherwise as part of

abuse of prisoners, imprisonment for life may be imposed.

### § 103.

Any person who, during war or occupation or under a threatening prospect thereof, breaches a contract concerning the measures taken by the Danish state in that connection, or who otherwise counteracts such measures, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 3 years.

Stk. 2. If the breach has taken place through gross negligence, the punishment is a fine or imprisonment for up to 4 months.

### § 104.

Any person who, in an improper manner, directly or through an intermediary, cooperates in a commercial capacity with a hostile war or occupation power shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to 8 years.
Stk. 2. Criminal liability under subsection 1 may be imposed upon anyone who has held an executive position in the enterprise in question. Criminal liability may likewise be imposed upon other persons employed in the enterprise when, in their case, particularly offensive conduct is present.

Stk. 3. When deciding whether and to what extent the conduct must be considered improper, in addition to the significance of the enterprise for the hostile war or occupation power, particular consideration shall be given to whether the person in question

1) has been active in getting a business relationship initiated, continued or expanded,

2) has, in the enemy's interest and on own initiative, carried out a restructuring of the enterprise or provided or attempted to provide greater or faster production than required,

3) has invoked the enemy's assistance vis-à-vis Danish authorities to further one's own interests,

4) has prevented or sought to prevent Danish authorities from gaining full access to learn about the enterprise's circumstances or

5) has obtained or sought to obtain unreasonable profit or other advantages that have not been reasonably justified from a business perspective.

### § 105. Any person who, during occupation, commits an act aimed at inducing the occupation power

or any organization or person cooperating with it to violate the freedom of determination of Danish authorities, or who, in an improper manner, exploits a connection with the occupation power or any organization or person cooperating with it to procure for oneself or others a special advantage shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 8 years.

### § 106. Any person who acts against the state's interest in the performance of a duty assigned to him to

negotiate or decide something with a foreign state on behalf of the state shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 16 years.

### § 107. Any person who, in the service of a foreign power or organization or for the use of persons operating

in such service, investigates or provides information about matters which, out of consideration for Danish state or social interests, must be kept secret, shall be punished, whether the information is correct or not, for espionage by imprisonment for up to 16 years.

Stk. 2. If it concerns the matters mentioned in § 109, or the act takes place during war or occupation, the punishment may increase to imprisonment for life.

### § 108.

Anyone who, without the matter falling under § 107, otherwise does something whereby foreign intelligence services are enabled or assisted in operating directly or indirectly within the territory of the Danish state, shall be punished with imprisonment for up to 6 years.

Stk. 2. If it concerns intelligence regarding military matters, or the activity takes place during war or occupation, the punishment may increase to imprisonment for 12 years.

### § 109. Anyone who discloses or passes on information about the state's secret negotiations, deliberations

or decisions in matters on which the state's security or rights in relation to foreign states depend, or which concern significant socio-economic interests vis-à-vis foreign countries, shall be punished with imprisonment for up to 12 years.

Stk. 2. If the mentioned acts are committed negligently, the punishment is a fine or imprisonment for up to 3 years.

### § 110.

Anyone who forges, destroys or disposes of any document or other object that is of importance to the state's security or rights in relation to foreign states shall be punished with imprisonment for up to 16 years.

Stk. 2. If the mentioned acts are committed negligently, the punishment is a fine or imprisonment for up to 3 years.

### § 110

a. Anyone who intentionally or negligently without proper authorisation shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment for up to 3 years

1) describes, photographs or otherwise depicts Danish non-publicly accessible military defence installations, depots, units, weapons, materiel or similar, or who reproduces or publishes such descriptions or depictions,

2) publishes provisions concerning Danish armed forces' mobilisation and other war preparedness. Stk. 2. Anyone who intentionally or negligently without proper authorisation takes photographs from aircraft over Danish state territory or publishes such unlawfully taken photographs shall be punished with a fine.

### § 110

b. Anyone who contributes to violations of neutrality against the Danish state from a foreign power's side shall be punished with imprisonment for up to 8 years.

### § 110

c. Anyone who violates provisions or prohibitions that may have been given pursuant to law for the protection of the state's defence or neutrality measures shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment for up to 4 months or under particularly aggravating circumstances with imprisonment for up to 3 years.

Stk. 2. Anyone who violates provisions or prohibitions that may have been given pursuant to law for implementation of the state's obligations as a member of the United Nations shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment for up to 4 months or under particularly aggravating circumstances with imprisonment for up to 4 years.

Stk. 3. With the same punishment as in stk. 2 shall be punished anyone who violates provisions contained in or issued pursuant to regulations adopted with authority in Article 60, 301 or 308 of the Treaty establishing the European Community or Article 215 or 352 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and which aim to wholly or partially interrupt or restrict the financial or economic connections with one or more countries outside the European Union or on corresponding sanctions against individuals, groups of persons or legal entities.

Stk. 4. If a crime as mentioned in stk. 1, 2 or 3 is committed negligently, the punishment is a fine or imprisonment for up to 2 years.

### § 110

d. If any of the crimes referred to in chapters 25, 26 and 27 is committed against a foreign head of state or the leader of a foreign diplomatic mission, the prescribed punishment may be increased by up to half, unless the matter is covered by chapter 13.

### § 110

e. Anyone who publicly insults a foreign nation, a foreign state, its flag or other recognised national symbol or the flag of the United Nations or the Council of Europe shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment for up to 2 years.

### § 110

f. The crimes referred to in this chapter are in all cases subject to public prosecution, which takes place at the order of the Minister of Justice.

- 8) Possession or use, etc. of radioactive substances under § 192 b.

Stk. 2. In the same manner shall be punished anyone who, with the intent mentioned in stk. 1, transports weapons or explosives.

Offenses against public authority, etc.

### § 119. Anyone who with violence or threat of violence assaults a person who is obliged to act pursuant

to public service or office, during the performance of the service or office or in connection therewith, or who in the same manner seeks to prevent such a person from performing a lawful official act or to compel him to perform an official act, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment for up to 8 years. Stk. 2. In the same manner shall be punished anyone who, without the circumstances falling under subsection 1, makes threats of violence, of deprivation of liberty or of accusation of a criminal or dishonorable offense against anyone who has been assigned by the state judicial authority or authority to make decisions concerning legal relations or concerning the enforcement of the state's penal authority, in connection with the performance of the service or office, or who in the same manner seeks to prevent such a person from performing a lawful official act or to compel him to perform an official act.

Stk. 3. If anyone otherwise places obstacles in the way of the performance of the service or office of the aforementioned persons, he shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year and 6 months. during the performance of his service or office or in connection therewith, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment for up to 6 months.

### § 123.

Anyone who harasses with threat of violence, or who by violence, unlawful coercion under § 260, threats under § 266 or in any other manner commits a criminal act against a person, his next of kin or others connected to him in connection with the person's expected or already given statement to the police or in court, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment for up to 8 years.

1) persons in preventive detention,

2) persons who instead of remand imprisonment are deprived of liberty in an institution, etc.,

### § 144. Anyone who, in the exercise of Danish, foreign or international public service or office,

### § 151.

### § 158.

Any person who gives false testimony before the court, including by use of telecommunication, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 4 years. The provision also applies to false testimony before a foreign court.

Stk. 3. If the false statement concerns only something which is without significance for the matter sought to be clarified, the penalty may be reduced to a fine.

### § 159.

If any person gives false testimony as an accused in a public criminal case or during questioning in cases where testimony may not be required according to law, that person shall not be punished.

Stk. 2. If false testimony is given during questioning before the court in cases where the person questioned was entitled to refuse testimony, the penalty may be reduced and, in otherwise mitigating circumstances, be waived.

### § 160. If any person is guilty of gross negligence in giving incorrect testimony in a case that

would otherwise be punishable under § 158 or § 159, subsection 2, the penalty is a fine or imprisonment for up to 4 months.

honour or in a similar solemn manner before or to a public authority, where such form is prescribed or permitted, shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 2 years.

### § 162.

Any person who otherwise gives an incorrect statement before or to a public authority concerning matters about which that person is obliged to give testimony shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 4 months.

### § 163.

Any person who otherwise, for use in legal relations concerning the public authorities, gives an incorrect statement in writing or by other readable medium or certifies something of which that person has no knowledge, shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 4 months.

### § 164. Any person who gives incorrect information to a public authority with intent that an innocent

person thereby becomes accused, convicted or subjected to criminal sanctions for a punishable offence shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 6 years.

Stk. 2. Any person who destroys, falsifies or removes evidence or produces false evidence with intent that someone thereby is accused or convicted of a punishable offence shall be punished in the same manner.

Stk. 3. Any person who commits an act as mentioned in subsections 1 and 2 with intent that he himself or another person with that person's consent thereby becomes accused, convicted or subjected to criminal sanctions for a punishable offence which he has not committed, shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year.

### § 164

a. Anyone who fails to disclose circumstances that clearly indicate that a person accused or convicted of a crime is innocent, although this could be done without exposing the person in question or their next of kin to danger to life, health or welfare or to prosecution for the crime in question, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year.

### § 165.

Anyone who reports to a public authority a criminal act that has not been committed, as well as anyone who submits false complaints to such authority, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to 6 months.

18. kapitel

Offences relating to means of payment

### § 166. Anyone who counterfeits or falsifies money in order to put it into circulation as genuine, or who for the same purpose procures counterfeit or falsified money for themselves or others, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 12 years.

Offences relating to evidence

### § 171.

Anyone who uses a false document to deceive in legal relations shall be punished for forgery of documents. Stk. 2. By a document is meant a written or electronic statement provided with designation of the issuer that appears to be intended to serve as evidence.

### § 172. The punishment for forgery of documents is a fine or imprisonment for up to 2 years.

Anyone who, in order to deprive someone of their right, destroys, removes or wholly or partially renders unusable evidence that is suitable for use as such in a legal relation, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to 2 years.

Stk. 3. If an act or omission as mentioned in subsection 1 is committed by gross negligence, the penalty shall be a fine or under aggravating circumstances imprisonment for up to 4 months.

### § 297. Anyone who disseminates false or misleading information whereby the price of goods, real property or

similar assets may be significantly affected shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year and 6 months.

Stk. 2. If an act as mentioned in subsection 1 is committed by gross negligence, the penalty shall be a fine or under aggravating circumstances imprisonment for up to 4 months.

### § 298. Anyone who, without the conditions for applying

### § 279 being present,

1) by false pretenses regarding solvency obtains for oneself or others a loan or credit with the result that financial loss is thereby inflicted,

2) by consuming prepaid remuneration renders oneself unable to deliver the counter-performance,

3) leaves without paying for accommodation, refreshment, transportation or other service in respect of which it has been a recognizable precondition that payment be made before leaving,

4) without making the stipulated payment surreptitiously gains access to a performance, exhibition or assembly or to transportation by public means of transport or to use of another publicly accessible facility.

### § 299. Anyone who, without the conditions for applying § 280 being present, in a financial matter which

the person in question is obliged to handle for another, by breach of their duty causes the latter a considerable financial loss which is not compensated before judgment in the first instance, shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year and 6 months.

Stk. 2. Anyone who, in handling another person's financial matters, in a manner contrary to duty receives, demands or has promised to oneself or others a gift or other benefit, as well as anyone who grants, promises or offers such a gift or other benefit, shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 4 years.

### § 299

a. Anyone who under particularly aggravating circumstances commits a violation of § 19 of the Marketing Practices Act shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 6 years. Particularly aggravating circumstances are considered to be especially cases where the act has caused considerable damage or has created an imminent danger thereof.

### § 299

b. Anyone who in order thereby to obtain for oneself or others unlawful gain or who otherwise under particularly aggravating circumstances commits

1) copyright infringements of a particularly serious nature, cf. § 76, subsection 2 of the Copyright Act, or unlawful import of a particularly serious nature, cf. § 77, subsection 2 of the Copyright Act,

2) trademark infringements of a particularly serious nature, cf. § 42, subsection 2 of the Trademarks Act,

3) design infringements of a particularly serious nature, cf. § 36, subsection 2 of the Designs Act,

4) patent infringements of a particularly serious nature, cf. § 57, subsection 2 of the Patents Act,

5) utility model infringements of a particularly serious nature, cf. § 54, subsection 2 of the Utility Models Act, or

6) violation of a particularly serious nature of § 91, cf.

### § 94

, subsection 2, of the Act on Radio and Television Broadcasting.

### § 299

c. Anyone who under particularly aggravating circumstances enters into a cartel agreement covered by § 23, subsection 3 of the Competition Act shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 6 years. Particularly aggravating circumstances are considered to be especially cases where the violation has been of a considerable extent or has been capable of causing considerable damage.

### § 299

d. Anyone who under particularly aggravating circumstances commits

1) violation of § 35, subsection 1, or § 39, subsection 1 of the Securities Trading Act, or

2) violation of Article 3, paragraph 1, points a or c, or Article 5 of Regulation (EU) No 1227/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2011 on wholesale energy market integrity and transparency.

Stk. 2. Particularly aggravating circumstances, cf. subsection 1, are considered to be especially cases where the offense was committed by several persons acting in concert, where considerable gain has been obtained or intended, or where a larger number of offenses have been committed.

### § 300. Shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year

1) any person who, at a time when he realizes or ought to realize that he is unable to satisfy his creditors, significantly worsens his financial position by incurring new debt or pays or provides security for significant overdue debts,

2) any person who causes his creditors significant loss through extravagant lifestyle, through gambling, through risky ventures that are not proportionate to his fortune, through highly disorderly business management or other reckless conduct,

3) any debtor and trustee who, when making the statements required during reconstruction proceedings, provides false information or is guilty of gross negligence.

### § 300

a. Shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 6 months any person who, without the conditions for applying § 279 being present, unlawfully intentionally or with gross negligence causes a person who is in error to be thereby induced to an action or omission, and thereby causes him or anyone for whom the action or omission becomes decisive a significant financial loss.

### § 300

b. Shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 6 months any person who, without the conditions for applying § 282 being present, when entering into an agreement improperly exploits the counterparty's economic difficulties or otherwise his inferior position.

### § 300

c. Shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 6 months any person who transfers a claim arising from another person's action of the nature specified in § 282, § 300 a or § 300 b, or who asserts such a claim, provided that he showed gross negligence with respect to its nature when acquiring the claim. In the same manner shall be punished any person who otherwise with gross negligence derives improper advantage from another person's action of the nature specified in § 282, § 300 a or § 300 b.

### § 301. Shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year and 6 months any person who, with intent for unauthorized

use, produces, obtains, possesses or discloses

1) information that identifies a means of payment that has been assigned to others, or

2) generated payment card numbers.

Stk. 2. If the disclosure etc. referred to in subsection 1 takes place in a wider circle or under otherwise particularly aggravating circumstances, the penalty is imprisonment for up to 6 years.

Stk. 3. The provision in subsection 1 does not apply to genuine payment cards.

### § 301

a. Shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year and 6 months any person who unlawfully obtains or discloses codes or other means of access to information systems to which access is reserved for paying users and which are protected by code or other special access restriction.

Stk. 2. If the disclosure etc. referred to in subsection 1 takes place under particularly aggravating circumstances, the penalty is imprisonment for up to 6 years. As particularly aggravating circumstances are considered in particular cases where the disclosure etc. takes place commercially, in a wider circle or under circumstances where there is a particular risk of extensive misuse.

### § 302. Shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year and 6 months, insofar as the conduct does not fall

under § 296, any person who particularly grossly violates the legislative requirements for

1) bookkeeping, including registration of transactions and preparation of accounting material,

2) storage of accounting material, including descriptions of bookkeeping and systems for storage and retrieval of material, including access codes etc. and encryption keys,

3) public authorities' access to accounting material pursuant to the legislation specifically applicable to these authorities, and

4) preparation of annual accounts or equivalent accounts.

Stk. 2. If an action or omission as referred to in subsection 1 is committed with gross negligence, the penalty is fine or imprisonment for up to 4 months.

### § 303. Any person who shows gross negligence by procuring for himself or in another similar manner receiving

things that have been acquired through a crime of enrichment shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 1 year.

### § 304. Shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 4 months any person who, in cases where a decision on a

financial matter is made by voting, obtains for himself or others access to participate unlawfully in it or to cast more votes than he is entitled to, or causes the vote to be distorted. Stk. 2. In the same manner shall be punished any person who, in votes in a bankruptcy or debt settlement estate or during reconstruction proceedings, by false pretenses influences the voting or provides, promises or offers, receives, demands or allows himself to be promised a financial advantage for voting in a particular direction or for refraining from voting.

### § 304

a. Any person who unlawfully provides, promises or offers to anyone who acts as an arbitrator in this country or abroad a gift or other advantage in order to induce that person to do or omit something in the exercise of this function shall be punished by fine or imprisonment for up to 4 years.

Stk. 2. In the same manner shall be punished any person who acts as an arbitrator in this country or abroad and who, in the exercise of this function, unlawfully receives, demands or allows himself to be promised a gift or other advantage.

### § 305.

The offences referred to in § 291, subsections 1 and 3, § 293, subsection 2, § 298 and § 299 shall be prosecuted only at the request of the aggrieved party, unless general considerations require prosecution.

Stk. 2. The offences referred to in § 294 are subject to private prosecution.
