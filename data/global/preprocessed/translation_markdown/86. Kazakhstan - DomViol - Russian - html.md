This Law defines the legal, economic, social and organizational foundations of the activities of state bodies, local self-government bodies, organizations and citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the prevention of domestic violence.

Article 1 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version)

### Статья 1.

Basic concepts used in this Law This Law uses the following basic concepts: 1) victim — an individual in respect of whom there are grounds to believe that he or she has directly suffered moral, physical and (or) property harm from domestic violence; 1-1) assistance organizations - legal entities providing victims with special social services and (or) assistance in accordance with this Law; 2) family-domestic relations - relations between spouses, former spouses, persons living or having lived together, close relatives, persons having a common child (children); 3) domestic violence — an intentional unlawful act (action or inaction) of one person in the sphere of family-domestic relations in relation to another (others), causing or containing a threat of causing physical and (or) mental suffering; 4) prevention of domestic violence — a complex of legal, economic, social and organizational measures carried out by subjects of prevention of domestic violence, aimed at protecting constitutional rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of man and citizen in the sphere of family-domestic relations, preventing and suppressing domestic violence, as well as identifying and eliminating the causes and conditions contributing to their commission; 5) subjects of prevention of domestic violence — state bodies, local self-government bodies, organizations and citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan carrying out prevention of domestic violence. See: Recommendation of the International Council under the Supreme Court of the Republic of Kazakhstan on studying international experience in sentencing for sexual and domestic violence, harassment (approved by protocol decision of the International Council under the Supreme Court of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated June 5, 2020)

### Статья 2.

Legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the prevention of domestic violence 1. The legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the prevention of domestic violence is based on the Constitution of the Republic of Kazakhstan, consists of this Law and other normative legal acts of the Republic of Kazakhstan. 2. If an international treaty ratified by the Republic of Kazakhstan establishes rules other than those contained in this Law, the rules of the international treaty shall apply.

### Статья 3.

Principles of prevention of domestic violence Prevention of domestic violence is based on the principles of: 2) guaranteeing observance of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of man and citizen; 3) inadmissibility of causing physical and (or) mental suffering to man and citizen; 4) support and preservation of the family; Subparagraph 6 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 20.04.23 No. 226-VII (entered into force on July 1, 2023) (see previous version) 6) individual approach to each man and citizen in need of special social services; 7) priority of preventive measures for prevention of domestic violence over repressive ones; 8) complexity and systematicity.

Article 4 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version)

### Статья 4.

Types of domestic violence 1. Domestic violence may be expressed in the form of physical, psychological, sexual and (or) economic violence. 2. Physical violence — intentional infliction of harm to health through the use of physical force and infliction of physical pain. 3. Psychological violence — intentional impact on a person's psyche, humiliation of honor and dignity by means of threats, insults, blackmail or coercion (inducement) to commit offenses or acts that pose a danger to life or health, as well as leading to violation of mental, physical and personal development. 4. Sexual violence — intentional unlawful action infringing on sexual inviolability or sexual freedom of a person, as well as actions of a sexual nature in relation to minors. 5. Economic violence — intentional deprivation of a person of housing, food, clothing, property, means to which he or she has a right provided by law. See: Recommendation of the International Council under the Supreme Court of the Republic of Kazakhstan on studying international experience in sentencing for sexual and domestic violence, harassment (approved by protocol decision of the International Council under the Supreme Court of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated June 5, 2020)

Special social services Paragraph 1 was amended in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 20.04.23 No. 226-VII (entered into force on July 1, 2023) (see previous version) 1. A victim recognized as a person in need of special social services in the manner established by law is provided with special social services. 2. Special social services include a guaranteed volume of special social services and paid special social services. 3. Provision of guaranteed and additional volume of special social services provided in excess of the guaranteed volume is carried out on the basis of decisions of local executive bodies of districts, cities of regional significance. Paragraph 4 is set forth in the wording of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 20.04.23 No. 226-VII (entered into force on July 1, 2023) (see previous version) 4. ## Chapter 2. Subjects of Prevention of Domestic Violence

### Article 6.

Competence of the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan The Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan: 1) develops the main directions of state policy in the field of prevention of domestic violence; 2) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see previous version) 3) ensures interaction of subjects of prevention of domestic violence and coordination of their activities; 4) exercises other powers provided for by the Constitution, laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan and acts of the President of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### Article 7.

Competence of local representative and executive bodies 1. Local representative bodies: 1) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see previous version) 2) approve and monitor the execution of local budgets with regard to expenses for the prevention of domestic violence; 3) promote the implementation by citizens and organizations of the provisions of this Law. 2. Local executive bodies: 1) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see previous version) 2) ensure interaction of subjects of prevention of domestic violence at the local level; 3) establish assistance organizations and ensure their functioning; 4) identify and maintain records of minors affected by domestic violence and dysfunctional families; Subparagraph 5 was amended in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 20.04.23 No. 226-VII (entered into force on July 1, 2023) (see previous version) 5) organize the provision of special social services to victims in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on social protection; The paragraph was supplemented with subparagraph 6 in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 24.11.21 No. 75-VII 6) ensure the analysis of the needs of students and pupils subjected to domestic violence in the provision of special social services in accordance with the standards for the provision of special social services and submit proposals to the authorized body in the field of education.

### Article 8.

Commissions on Women's Affairs and Family-Demographic Policy Commissions on Women's Affairs and Family-Demographic Policy: 1) interact with subjects of prevention of domestic violence on issues of prevention of domestic violence; 2) submit proposals to local executive bodies on the adoption of organizational and practical measures aimed at the prevention of domestic violence; 3) jointly with assistance organizations organize and conduct measures for the prevention of domestic violence and rehabilitation of victims.

### Article 9.

Competence of commissions on juvenile affairs and protection of their rights Commissions on juvenile affairs and protection of their rights: 1) interact with subjects of prevention of domestic violence on issues of prevention of domestic violence among minors; 2) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see previous version) 3) submit proposals to local executive bodies on the adoption of organizational and practical measures aimed at the prevention of domestic violence among minors; 4) jointly with assistance organizations organize and conduct measures for the prevention of domestic violence and rehabilitation of minors affected by domestic violence.

Article 10 was amended in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version)

### Article 10.

Competence of internal affairs bodies Internal affairs bodies: 1) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 03.07.13 No. 124-V (see previous version) 2) participate in the development of draft regulatory legal acts in the field of prevention of domestic violence; 3) implement measures for the prevention of domestic violence; 4) identify parents or persons substituting for them who do not fulfill or improperly fulfill obligations to raise children, committing unlawful acts against them; 5) maintain preventive records and carry out preventive control; 6) consider applications and reports on facts of domestic violence or threats of its commission with on-site visits and take measures to suppress them; 7) at the request of victims, refer them to assistance organizations or healthcare organizations; 8) conduct preventive conversations; 9) carry out the delivery of a person who committed domestic violence to internal affairs bodies; 10) issue protective orders; 11) petition the prosecutor for authorization to extend the term of a protective order; 12) carry out administrative detention; 13) petition the court to establish special requirements for the behavior of a person who committed domestic violence; 14) carry out criminal prosecution, proceedings on administrative offenses; 15) apply measures of criminal procedural coercion; 16) apply criminal law safety measures for the victim; 17) ensure the organization of special training courses on issues of prevention of domestic violence for employees of internal affairs bodies; 18) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 13.01.14 No. 159-V (see previous version)

### Article 11.

Competence of the authorized body in the field of education The authorized body in the field of education: 1) Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version) Subparagraph 2 is set out in the wording of the Law of the RK dated 24.11.21 No. 75-VII (see previous version) 2) ensures monitoring of the analysis by local executive bodies of the needs of students and pupils subjected to domestic violence in the provision of special social services in accordance with the standards for the provision of special social services; 3) participates in the development of regulatory legal acts in the field of prevention of domestic violence; 4) develops and implements in the practice of educational organizations programs and methodologies aimed at forming law-abiding behavior of students and pupils of educational organizations.

### Article 15.

Assistance Organizations 1. Assistance Organizations: 1) receive victims; 2) organize the provision of necessary psychological, pedagogical, medical, legal assistance to victims, if necessary refer them to healthcare organizations for the provision of medical assistance and further rehabilitation; 3) in accordance with their capabilities provide temporary accommodation to victims; 3-1) provide victims with special social services, and also apply on their behalf to local executive bodies of districts, cities of regional significance with an application for the provision of guaranteed and (or) additional volume of special social services provided in excess of the guaranteed volume; 4) conduct psycho-correctional programs with persons who committed domestic violence; 5) inform internal affairs bodies about facts of domestic violence or about the threat of their commission; 6) carry out legal education work on issues of domestic violence prevention; 7) carry out interaction with individuals and legal entities on issues of domestic violence prevention. Assistance provided for by subparagraphs 1), 2), 3), 4) and 5) of part one of this paragraph is provided to victims regardless of place of residence. 2. Assistance organizations are created by local executive bodies, as well as individuals and legal entities in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan. 3. Financing of the activities of assistance organizations is carried out at the expense of budgetary funds and other sources not prohibited by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

## Chapter 3. Measures for the Prevention of Domestic Violence

### Article 16.

Regulation of relations arising from the application of domestic violence prevention measures Regulation of relations arising from the application of domestic violence prevention measures is carried out in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the prevention of offenses, taking into account the features established by this chapter.

### Article 17.

Measures of individual prevention of domestic violence 1. Measures of individual prevention of domestic violence are applied for systematic targeted impact on the legal consciousness and behavior of a person who committed domestic violence, in order to prevent the commission of new offenses on his part and ensure the safety of the victim. 2. Measures of individual prevention of domestic violence are: 2) delivery to internal affairs bodies of a person who committed domestic violence, for drawing up a protocol on an administrative offense or issuing a protective order; 4) administrative detention; 5) compulsory measures of a medical nature; 6) establishment of special requirements for the behavior of the offender; 7) administrative penalty; 8) deprivation or restriction of parental rights, cancellation of adoption of a child, release and removal of guardians and trustees from the performance of their duties, early termination of the contract for the transfer of a child for upbringing to a foster parent; 9) measures of procedural coercion and security measures for victims in criminal proceedings; 10) measures taken by court sentence. 3. Measures of individual prevention of domestic violence are determined taking into account the individual characteristics of the person in respect of whom they are applied, the nature and degree of public danger of the offenses committed by him. 4. The decision to apply measures of individual prevention may be appealed by interested persons in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### Article 18.

Grounds for taking measures of individual prevention of domestic violence 1. The grounds for taking measures of individual prevention of domestic violence is one of the following circumstances: 1) receipt of an application or report from individuals and legal entities; 2) direct detection by an employee of the internal affairs body of the fact of committing domestic violence or an attempt to commit it; 3) materials received from state bodies and local self-government bodies. 2. Applications and reports on the commission of domestic violence or the threat of its commission are considered by state bodies in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### Article 19.

Preventive conversation 1. The main objectives of the preventive conversation are to identify the causes and conditions of domestic violence, explain the social and legal consequences of domestic violence and convince of the need for law-abiding behavior. 2. The preventive conversation is conducted by the subject of domestic violence prevention with a person who committed domestic violence, or in respect of whom there are grounds for taking measures of individual prevention of domestic violence. 3. The preventive conversation is conducted in the official premises of the subjects of domestic violence prevention, as well as at the place of residence, study, work or directly at the place of commission of domestic violence, and cannot last more than one hour. 4. The person with whom the preventive conversation is conducted is warned about the need to cease unlawful actions. 5. A preventive conversation with a minor is conducted in the presence of his parents, teachers or other legal representatives.

### Статья 20.

Protective order Paragraph 1 was amended in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 05.07.14 No. 236-V (see previous version); the Law of the RK dated 02.11.15 No. 388-V (entered into force on January 1, 2016) (see previous version); as revised by the Law of the RK dated 12.07.18 No. 180-VI (see previous version) 1. In order to ensure the safety of the victim and in the absence of grounds for administrative detention in the manner prescribed by the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan on Administrative Offenses, or detention in the manner prescribed by Article 128 of the Criminal Procedure Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, a protective order shall be issued by the heads of the territorial police body, the local police service of the internal affairs body, their deputies, district police inspectors, inspectors for juvenile affairs and for the protection of women from violence, taking into account the opinion of the victim, which shall be handed over for execution to the person who committed domestic violence or from whom there is a threat of its commission, against signature. In case of refusal to sign, a note to this effect shall be made in the protective order. The victim must be provided with a copy of the protective order against signature on the day of its issuance with an explanation of his or her rights, as well as the legal consequences in case of violation of the protective order by the person against whom it was issued. A copy of the protective order shall be sent to the prosecutor within twenty-four hours from the moment of its delivery to the person against whom it was issued. 2. A protective order shall be issued against a sane person who has reached the age of sixteen at the time of its issuance. 3. A protective order shall prohibit committing domestic violence, against the will of the victim, searching for, pursuing, visiting, conducting oral, telephone conversations and contacting him or her by other means, including minor and (or) incapacitated members of his or her family. 4. The protective order shall indicate: the time and place of its issuance, by whom and against whom it was issued, the place, time and circumstances of the commission or threat of commission of domestic violence, the established restrictions in relations with the victim, the legal consequences in cases of continuation of unlawful actions and violation of the protective order. 5. The period of validity of the protective order shall be thirty days from the moment of its delivery to the person against whom it was issued. The frequency of verification shall be at least once every seven calendar days. 6. Excluded in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version) 7. Violation of a protective order shall entail liability established by the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan on Administrative Offenses. 8. A person against whom a protective order has been issued shall be placed on preventive registration by the internal affairs bodies and shall be subject to preventive control.

### Статья 21.

Administrative detention 1. In order to suppress domestic violence containing the elements of an administrative offense, and if there is reason to believe that the issuance of a protective order is insufficient to ensure the safety of the victim, an official of the internal affairs bodies shall carry out administrative detention of the person who committed domestic violence, which consists in his or her temporary deprivation of freedom of action and movement with forced detention in a special facility. 2. The procedure, terms of administrative detention, rights and obligations of persons subjected to administrative detention shall be determined by the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan on Administrative Offenses.

Article 22 was amended in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 18.02.14 No. 175-V (see previous version)

### Статья 22.

Establishment of special requirements for the behavior of the offender 1. In order to ensure the safety of the victim, the court may establish special requirements for the behavior of the person who committed domestic violence. 2. Establishment of special requirements for the behavior of the offender is a measure of administrative and legal impact and shall be applied both along with the imposition of an administrative penalty and instead of it when releasing a person who committed an administrative offense from administrative liability. 3. A person against whom special requirements for behavior have been established may be prohibited from: 1) against the will of the victim, searching for, pursuing, visiting, conducting oral, telephone conversations and contacting him or her by other means, including minor and (or) incapacitated members of his or her family; 2) acquiring, storing, carrying and using firearms and other types of weapons; The paragraph was supplemented with subparagraph 3 in accordance with the Law of the RK dated 12.07.18 No. 180-VI; as revised by the Law of the RK dated 07.07.20 No. 361-VI (see previous version) 3) non-medical use of psychoactive substances. In exceptional cases, for the protection and defense of the victim and members of his or her family, the court shall have the right to also apply a measure of administrative and legal impact in the form of a prohibition for the person who committed domestic violence to reside in an individual residential house, apartment or other dwelling with the victim in case this person has other housing. 4. During the period of validity of special requirements for the behavior of the offender, he or she may be obligated to appear at the internal affairs bodies from one to four times a month for a preventive conversation. 5. The procedure for establishing special requirements for the behavior of the offender, the terms of its validity, the rights and obligations of participants in proceedings on administrative offenses shall be determined by the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan on Administrative Offenses. 6. A person against whom special requirements for behavior have been established shall be placed on preventive registration by the internal affairs bodies and shall be subject to preventive control.

### Статья 23.

Measures of procedural compulsion and security measures in criminal proceedings Paragraph 1 as revised by the Law of the RK dated 03.07.14 No. 227-V (see previous version) 1. In order to suppress domestic violence containing the elements of a criminal offense, and to ensure the safety of the victim, witness and other persons participating in criminal proceedings, members of their families and close relatives, the bodies carrying out criminal prosecution and conducting criminal proceedings shall apply measures of procedural compulsion and procedural security measures. 2. The grounds for applying measures of procedural compulsion and procedural security measures, the procedure for their application, the rights and obligations of persons participating in criminal proceedings shall be determined by the Criminal Procedure Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### Статья 24.

Supervision over compliance with legality in the field of prevention of domestic violence Supreme supervision over compliance with legality in the field of prevention of domestic violence shall be exercised by the prosecutor's offices.

### Статья 25.

Compliance with confidentiality when providing assistance to victims Information about the personal and family life of victims shall be confidential information and shall not be subject to disclosure. Disclosure of confidential information by a person who, by virtue of his or her official position, became aware of a fact of domestic violence shall entail liability established by the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### Article 26.

Procedure for Bringing this Law into Force This Law shall enter into force upon the expiration of ten calendar days after its first official publication.
