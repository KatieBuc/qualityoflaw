##### Neni10

Forceofcriminaldecisionsofforeigncourts

Criminal decisions given by foreign courts against Albanian nationals that establish the commission of a criminal offence, when not provided otherwise by bilateral or multilateral agreements, are valid in Albania within the limits of Albanian law and for the following: a) for the effect of qualification for recidivism of the person who has committed the criminal offence; b) for the execution of decisions containing supplementary punishments; c) for the execution of security measures; d) for the extinguishment of damages or other civil effects.

##### Neni11

Extradition

Extradition may be permitted only when it is expressly provided for in international agreements to which the Republic of Albania is a party. Extradition is permitted when the criminal offence that is the subject of the extradition request is provided as such simultaneously in both Albanian and foreign law.

Extraditionisnotpermitted:

- a) if the person to be extradited is an Albanian national, except in cases when the agreement provides otherwise;
- b) if the criminal offence that is the subject of the extradition request has a political or military character;
- c) when there is reason to believe that the person sought to be extradited will be persecuted, punished or sought for reasons of his political, religious, national, racial or ethnic convictions.
- d) if the person sought to be extradited has been tried by a competent Albanian court for the criminal offence for which extradition is sought.

## KREUII

CRIMINALRESPONSIBILITY

##### Neni12

Ageforcriminalresponsibility

A person who at the time of committing a crime has reached the age of fourteen years has criminal responsibility. A person who commits a criminal contravention has responsibility when he has reached the age of sixteen years.

##### Neni13

Causallink

No one has criminal responsibility when there is no causal link between his action or omission and the consequences or the possibility of their occurrence.

##### Neni14

Guilt

No one may be punished for an action or omission provided for by law as a criminal offence, if the offence was not committed with guilt.

A person who commits the offence with intent or negligence is deemed to have guilt.

##### Neni15

Intent

A criminal offence is committed with intent when the person foresees the consequences of the criminal offence and desires their occurrence or, although he foresees and does not desire them, consciously allows their occurrence.

##### Neni16

Negligence

A criminal offence is committed with negligence when the person, although he does not desire the consequences, foresees the possibility of their occurrence and with thoughtlessness hopes to avoid them, or does not foresee them, although according to the circumstances he should have and could have foreseen them.

##### Neni17

Non-responsibilityduetomentalstate

A person who at the time of committing the offence suffered from a psychic or neuropsychic disorder that completely destroyed his mental equilibrium and as a result was not able to control his actions or omissions and to understand that he committed a criminal offence has no criminal responsibility.

A person who at the time of committing the criminal offence suffered from a psychic or neuropsychic disorder that diminished his mental equilibrium to understand and fully control his actions or omissions is responsible, but this circumstance is taken into account by the court in determining the measure and type of punishment.

##### Neni18

Commissionofacriminaloffenceinastateofinтoxication

A person who has committed a criminal offence in a state of intoxication is not excluded from responsibility.

When intoxication is caused in circumstances of chance and has brought about the diminishment of mental equilibrium, this circumstance is taken into account for the mitigation of the punishment against him.

When intoxication is done with premeditation to commit the criminal offence, this circumstance is taken into account for the aggravation of the punishment.

The above rules also apply when the criminal offence is committed under the effect of narcotics or other stimulants.

##### Neni19

Necessarydefense

A person who has committed the offence being obliged to protect his life, health, rights and interests or those of another, from an unlawful, real and immediate attack with the condition that the character of the defense be in proportion to the dangerousness of the attack has no criminal responsibility. Obvious disproportion between them constitutes an exceeding of the limits of necessary defense.

##### Neni20

Extremenecessity

A person who has committed the offence out of necessity to face a real and immediate danger that threatens him, another person or property from a serious and unavoidable harm by other means, with the condition that it not be provoked by him and the harm caused not be greater than the harm averted has no criminal responsibility.

##### Neni21

Exerciseofarightorfulfillmentofaduty

A person who acts to exercise a right or fulfill duties assigned by law or by an order given by a competent person has no criminal responsibility, except in the case when the order is manifestly unlawful.

When the criminal offence was committed as a result of an unlawful order, the person who gave the order is responsible for it.

## KREUIII

ATTEMPT

##### Neni22

Meaningofattempt

A criminal offence is deemed to have remained in attempt when, although the person undertakes actions directly to commit it, the offence is interrupted and not completed due to circumstances independent of his will.

##### Neni23

Responsibilityforattempt

A person who attempts to commit a crime is responsible for it.

The court, depending on the degree of proximity of the consequence, as well as the reasons for which the crime remained in attempt, mitigates the punishment and may lower it below the minimum provided by law or determine a type of punishment milder than that provided for in the law.

##### Neni24

Withdrawalfromcommissionofacriminaloffence

A person who by his own will and in a final manner withdraws from the commission of the criminal offence, despite the possibilities he has for its commission, has no criminal responsibility.

When his earlier actions contain the elements of some other criminal offence, the person is responsible for the offence committed.

## KREUIV

COOPERATIONOFPERSONSINTHECOMMISSION

OFACRIMINALOFFENCE

##### Neni25

Meaningofcooperation

Cooperation is deemed the commission of the criminal offence by two or more persons with an agreement between them.

##### Neni26

Cooperators

Cooperators in the commission of a criminal offence are deemed: organizers, executors, instigators and aiders.

Organizers are deemed those persons who organize and direct the activity for the commission of the criminal offence.

Executors are persons who perform the actions directly for the realization of the criminal offence.

Instigators are deemed persons who incite other cooperators to commit the criminal offence.

Aiders are deemed persons who by advice, instructions, providing means, removing obstacles, giving promises to hide the cooperators, the traces or the objects that result from the criminal offence, help in its commission.

##### Neni27

Responsibilityofaccomplices

The organizers, instigators and aiders have the same responsibility as the perpetrators for the criminal offense committed by them.

In determining the punishment for accomplices, the court must take into account the degree of participation of each of them and the role they played in the commission of the criminal offense.

##### Neni28

Specialformsofcooperation (Amended by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; repealed paragraph two of point 1, amended point 2, by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)

1. A criminal organization is the highest form of cooperation, consisting of three or more persons and which is characterized by a special degree of organization, structuring, stability, duration, as well as by the purpose of committing one or more criminal offenses, in order to obtain material and non-material benefits.

2. A terrorist organization is a special form of criminal organization, composed of two or more persons, who have a cooperation stable in time, with the aim of committing acts for terrorist purposes.

3. An armed gang is a special form of cooperation which, by possessing weapons, war munitions and other necessary means, aims to commit criminal offenses, provided for in chapters V, VI and VII of the special part of this Code.

4. A structured criminal group is a special form of cooperation, consisting of three or more persons, for the commission of one or more criminal offenses, in order to obtain material and non-material benefits.

A structured criminal group for the commission of a criminal offense is not formed randomly and it is not necessary to be characterized by stable membership, division of tasks, organization and developed structuring.
5. The creation and participation in a criminal organization, terrorist organization, armed gang or structured criminal group are qualified as criminal offenses and are punished according to the provisions of the special part of this Code or other special penal provisions.

6. Members of a criminal organization, terrorist organization, armed gang or structured criminal group are responsible for all criminal offenses, committed by them, in fulfillment of the purposes of their criminal activity.

7. A member of a criminal organization, terrorist organization, armed gang or structured criminal group, benefits from exemption from punishment or its reduction, when he provides assistance, which is deemed decisive for the detection of their activity, of other accomplices, of property possessed directly or indirectly by them, as well as for the investigative activities, which are carried out against criminal organizations, terrorist organizations, armed gangs and structured criminal groups.

PENALTIES

##### Neni29

Mainpenalties

(Amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; added final paragraph by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003; repealed final paragraph by decision of the Constitutional Court no. 47, dated 26.7.2012)

For persons who have committed crimes the following main penalties are given:

1. Life imprisonment

2. Imprisonment

3. Fine

For persons who have committed penal contraventions the following main penalties are given:

1. Imprisonment

2. Fine

##### Neni30

Supplementarypenalties (Amended point 2, by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003; amended point 1 by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; added point 10 by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

For persons who have committed crimes or penal contraventions, together with the main penalty, one or several of the following supplementary penalties may also be given:

1. Prohibition of the right to exercise public functions.
2. Confiscation of the means of commission of the criminal offense and products of the criminal offense.
3. Prohibition to drive vehicles.

4. Removal of decorations, honorary titles.
5. Removal of the right to exercise an activity or profession.
6. Removal of the right to exercise leading duties with legal persons.
7. Prohibition to stay in one or several administrative units.

8. Expulsion from the territory.

9. Obligation for publication of the judicial decision.

10. Loss of parental responsibility.

The court, in special cases, when the imposition of main penalties is deemed inappropriate and when the law provides for imprisonment up to 3 years or other lighter penalties for the offense committed, may be satisfied only with the determination of a supplementary penalty.

##### Neni31

Lifeimprisonmentpenalty (Amended first paragraph, second and title by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; repealed final paragraph by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)
Life imprisonment penalty is given by court decision for the commission of a serious crime.

Life imprisonment penalty is not given to persons who at the time of commission of the crime had not reached the age of eighteen years, as well as to women.

##### Neni32

Imprisonmentpenalty

(Amended first paragraph by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Imprisonment penalty for crimes is given for a period from five days to thirty-five years.
For penal contraventions imprisonment penalty is given for a period from five days to two years.

##### Neni33

Mannerofservinglifeimprisonmentandimprisonmentpenalty

Life imprisonment penalty and imprisonment penalty are served in institutions designated specifically for this purpose.
The rules on the manner of serving penalties, the rights and duties of the convicted person shall be determined by law.
Minors serve the imprisonment penalty in separate places from adults.
Women serve the imprisonment penalty in separate institutions from men.

##### Neni34

Finepenalty (Amended third and fourth paragraphs by law no. 8175, dated 23.12.1996, added fifth paragraph, amended seventh paragraph, by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended fourth paragraph by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; repealed paragraph 8 by decision of the Constitutional Court no. 19, dated 1.6.2011; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; amended fifth paragraph by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

Fine penalty consists in payment in favor of the state of a sum of money within the limits provided by law.

Fine penalty is given to persons who commit crimes or penal contraventions.
For persons who commit crimes the fine is given from 100 thousand to 10 million lek.
For persons who commit penal contraventions, the fine is given from 50 thousand to 3 million lek.

For persons who commit crimes for motives of gaining property or securing any kind of other material benefit, the court decides according to article 36 of this Code, the confiscation of the means of commission of the criminal offense and products of the criminal offense or, in their absence, fine penalty in the amount from 100 thousand to 5 million lek.

The court gives fine penalty, after having investigated about the paying ability of the person. The paying ability is determined by the personal and property situation of the person, as well as by other circumstances related to them.
Fine penalty is paid within the deadline set in the court decision.

The court, taking into account the economic situation of the convicted person, may allow the fine to be paid in installments, determining the installments and the deadline for their payment.

When the convicted person does not pay the fine and the fine cannot be collected through compulsory execution, the court decides the replacement of the fine with imprisonment, calculating five thousand lek for one day of imprisonment.

When the convicted person cannot pay the fine on time without his fault and if the criteria, on which the fine was determined, have changed after the rendering of the decision and do not clearly justify the payment of the fine, then the convicted person may request:
a) postponement of the deadline for payment of the fine up to six months;
b) performance of work in the public interest.
If the court orders the performance of work in the public interest the rules of article 63 of this Code shall apply.

If the fine is not paid even after the postponement of the deadline or if the convicted person does not perform the work in the public interest, the court decides the replacement of the fine with imprisonment.
When the fine has been given for the commission of a crime, its replacement with imprisonment cannot exceed three years, while when it has been given for the commission of a penal contravention, the replacement cannot exceed one year of imprisonment, but always without exceeding the maximum of the imprisonment penalty provided by the respective provision.
If the penal provision does not provide for imprisonment penalty, the maximum of the imprisonment penalty is considered six months.

If the convicted person pays the fine during the execution of the imprisonment, the court revokes the decision, making the calculation according to the criterion of paragraph 8 of this article.

##### Neni35

(Reformulatedbylaw no.9275,dated16.9.2004)

Deprivation of the right to exercise public functions, for a period of no less than five years, is given mandatorily to a person who has committed a crime related to duty, by abusing public function or has committed a crime for which the court has imposed a prison sentence of no less than ten years. Deprivation of the right to exercise public functions may be given for a period from three to five years when the court has imposed a sentence from five to ten years of imprisonment, and from one to three years when a sentence of up to three years of imprisonment has been imposed.

##### Neni37

Prohibitiontodrivemotorvehicles

The prohibition to drive motor vehicles is given by the court, for a period from one to five years, against persons who have committed a criminal offense, when it is considered that it will have a preventive effect or is consistent with the nature of the offense committed.

##### Neni38

Deprivationofdecorationsandtitles

Deprivation of decorations and honorary titles is imposed on persons who have committed a criminal offense punishable by imprisonment and it is considered that holding them is not consistent with the nature of the criminal offense committed. Deprivation of decorations and honorary titles is permanent when a person is sentenced for a crime over ten years and from one to five years when sentenced up to ten years of imprisonment.

##### Neni39

Deprivationoftherightoexerciseaprofessionoranactivity

(Amendedbylaw no.144,dated2.5.2013)

Deprivation of the right to exercise a profession or an activity prohibits the convicted person from exercising a profession or activity for which a special permit, certificate, authorization or license has been given by the competent authority.

Deprivation of the right to exercise an activity or a profession is given for a period from one month to five years and is a consequence of any punishment for criminal offenses committed while abusing them, or when it is assessed that the continued exercise of the activity or profession conflicts with the legal relationship that the specific criminal offense aims to protect.

##### Neni40

Deprivationoftherightoexercisemanagerialduties

(Lastparagraphaddedbylaw no.9275,dated16.9.2004)

Deprivation of the right to exercise managerial duties at legal persons removes the convicted person's ability to exercise the duties of director, administrator, entrepreneur, liquidator, as well as any other duty that involves the capacity of representative of the legal person.

Deprivation of the right to exercise managerial duties at legal persons is a consequence of any punishment for criminal offenses and is given for a period from one month to five years when the convicted person has abused functions or has acted in violation of rules related to the duty.

When the sentence given by the court is no less than five years of imprisonment, this right may be deprived for a period from five to ten years.

##### Neni41

Prohibitiontostayinoneorseveraladministrativeunits

The prohibition to stay in one or several administrative units is given by the court for a period from one to five years when it is considered that the stay of the convicted person in these places constitutes a risk to public security.

##### Neni42

Expulsionfromtheterritory

Expulsion from the territory of the Republic of Albania is given by the court to a foreign citizen and a stateless person who commits a crime and it is considered that his further stay in the territory of the Republic of Albania should not continue in the future.

The decision is revoked by the court, at the request of the convicted person, when the foreign citizen or stateless person acquires Albanian citizenship.

##### Neni43

Publicationofthecourtdecision

Publication of the court decision is given by the court when it considers that knowledge of the content of the decision is of interest to legal and physical persons. Publication of the court decision consists of the convicted person's obligation to publish at his own expense in one or several newspapers or radio-television stations, completely or partially, the court decision, according to the order made by the court.

The date of publication and its duration are determined by the court.

Press and radio-television bodies are obliged to make the publication of the court decision sent by the court.

Publication of the court decision is not given when the disclosure of state secret is risked, the intimate life of persons is violated, or social morality is affected.

##### Neni43/a

Lossofparentalresponsibility

(Addedbylaw no.23/2012,dated1.3.2012)

Loss of parental responsibility is given by the court to a person who exercises parental responsibility when he is sentenced as author or accomplice in a criminal offense against a child or as accomplice with the child in the commission of a criminal offense.

##### Neni44

Mannerofservingsupplementarypunishments

When the court, together with a prison sentence, also gives one or several of the supplementary punishments mentioned in Article 30 of this Code, their application begins together with the serving of the main sentence.

For numbers 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of Article 30 of this Code, their application begins after serving the prison sentence. The rights violated by the supplementary punishments the convicted person cannot exercise during the period of serving the prison sentence.

##### Neni45

Applicationofcriminallawtolegalpersons

(Repealedbylaw no.8733,dated24.1.2001;addedbylaw no.9275,dated16.9.2004)

Legal persons, with the exception of state institutions, are criminally liable for criminal offenses committed on their behalf or in their benefit by their bodies and representatives.

Local government units are criminally liable only for actions committed during the exercise of their activity, which can be exercised through delegation of public services.

Criminal liability of legal persons does not exclude that of physical persons who have committed or are accomplices in the commission of the same criminal offense.

Criminal offenses and the respective punitive measures applied to legal persons, as well as the procedure for imposing and executing them, are regulated by special law.

##### Neni46

(Firstandthirdparagraphsamendedbylaw no.36/2017,dated30.3.2017)

Medical measures may be given by the court to irresponsible persons who have committed a criminal offense, while educational measures may be given to minors who are excluded from punishment or who due to age have no criminal liability.

Medicalmeasuresare:

1.Mandatoryoutpatienttreatment

2.Mandatorytreatmentinamedicalinstitution.

The court decision on medical and educational measures is revocable at any time when the circumstances for which it was given disappear, but in any case, the court is primarily obliged, after one year from the date of issuance of the decision, to review its decision.

The rules for revocation of court decisions containing medical measures are provided in the Code of Criminal Procedure. The rules for imposing or revoking medical and educational measures for minors are provided by the Juvenile Justice Code.

## CHAPTERVI

DETERMINATIONOFPUNISHMENT

##### Neni47

Mannerfordeterminingthepunishment

The court determines the punishment by respecting the provisions of the general part of this Code and the limits of punishments provided by law for the criminal offense.

In determining the punishment for the person, it takes into account the dangerousness of the criminal offense, its author, the degree of guilt, as well as mitigating and aggravating circumstances.

##### Neni48

Mitigatingcircumstances

(Addedtwoparagraphsbylawno.144,dated2.5.2013)

The following circumstances mitigate the punishment: a) When the act is committed driven by motives of positive moral and social value;

b) when the act is committed under the influence of psychological shock caused by provocation or unlawful acts of the victim or another person; c) when the act is committed under the influence of unlawful actions or instructions of a superior; ç) when the person who committed the act shows deep remorse;

d) when the person has compensated for the damage caused by the criminal offense or has actively helped to eliminate or reduce the consequences of the criminal offense; dh) when the person appears before the competent authorities after committing the criminal offense; e) when the relations between the person who committed the criminal offense and the victim are normalized. The mitigating circumstance provided in letter "a" of the first paragraph of this article does not mitigate the punishment when the criminal offense is committed in the circumstances provided in letter "j" of article 50 of this Code. The mitigating circumstance provided in letter "e" of the first paragraph of this article does not mitigate the punishment for a person who has committed a criminal offense against children or a criminal offense of domestic violence.

##### Neni49

The court, regardless of the circumstances mentioned in article 48 of this Code, may take into consideration other circumstances insofar as they justify mitigation of punishment.

##### Neni50

Aggravatingcircumstances (Changedletter"b",addedletters"h"and"i"bylawno.8733,dated24.1.2001;changedletter"dh"by lawno.9275,dated16.9.2004;addedletter"j"bylawno.9686,dated26.2.2007; addedletters"ç/1"and"e/1"andchangedletters"g"and"j"bylawno.144,dated2.5.2013;addedletter "f/1bylawno.44/2019,dated18.7.2019)

The following circumstances aggravate the punishment: a) commission of the act driven by base motives;

b) commission of the act to bring to responsibility or to hide the criminal responsibility of another or to avoid punishment for another criminal offense or to realize, or to secure for oneself or for a third party property gains, or any kind of other material benefit; c) commission of the criminal offense with cruelty and atrocity; ç) commission of a crime after being given a punishment for a crime committed earlier; ç/1) commission of the criminal offense after placing the person under electronic monitoring; d) commission of acts that aggravate or increase the consequences of the criminal offense; dh) commission of the criminal offense by abusing public office or religious service;

e) when the act is committed against children, pregnant women or persons who, for various reasons, cannot defend themselves; e/1) commission of the criminal offense during or after issuance of judicial protection orders from domestic violence; f) when the act is directed against representatives of other states; f/1. When the act is directed against elected persons and public officials, because of their duty.

g) when the act is committed taking advantage of family relations, cohabitation, friendship, hospitality; gj) commission of the criminal offense in collaboration; h) commission of the criminal offense multiple times;

i) when the act is committed using weapons, war munitions, explosive, incendiary, poisonous and radioactive substances.

j) when the act is committed driven by motives related to gender, race, color, ethnicity, language, gender identity, sexual orientation, political, religious or philosophical beliefs, health condition, genetic predisposition or disability.

##### Neni51

Punishmentforminors

(Changedbylawno.36/2017,dated30.3.2017)

For minors, who at the time of commission of the criminal offense have not reached the age of 18, the punishment cannot be more than half of the punishment provided by law for the criminal offense committed. The rules for determining punishment for minors are provided in the Code of Criminal Justice for Minors.

##### Neni52

Exemptionofminorsfrompunishment

The court, based on the low dangerousness of the criminal offense, the concrete circumstances of its commission, the previous behavior of the minor, may exempt him from punishment.

In these cases the court may decide to send the minor to an educational institution.

##### Neni52/a

Exemptionorreductionofpunishmentforjusticecollaboratorsandvictims

(Addedbylawno.144,dated2.5.2013,changedreferencebylawno.146/2020,dated17.12.2020)

The person who has promised or given a reward or other benefits, according to articles 164/a, 244, 244/a, 245, 312, 319, 319/a, 319/b, and 319/c of this Code may benefit from exemption from serving the punishment or its reduction, if he makes a report and provides assistance in the criminal proceeding for these offenses. In giving the decision, the court takes into account the time when the report was made, whether or not the consequences of the offense occurred.

The person harmed by criminal offenses related to trafficking in persons may benefit from exemption from punishment for commission of criminal offenses during the trafficking period and to the extent he was forced to commit those acts or unlawful omissions.

The person who has committed one of the criminal offenses related to trafficking in narcotics, weapons or munitions, trafficking in persons or for criminal offenses committed by a criminal organization, who cooperates and assists the criminal prosecution authorities in fighting against them or, as the case may be, in discovering other persons who commit such crimes, cannot be punished by more than half of the punishment provided for the offense committed by him. In special cases, when mitigating circumstances in his favor also concur, this person may be exempted from punishment.

##### Neni52/b

Exemption from punishment for crimes in the field of elections

(Addedbylawno.146/2020,dated17.12.2020)

The person who has committed one or more criminal offenses provided in chapter X of this Code, who cooperates and assists the criminal prosecution authorities for the investigation, as well as for the discovery of other persons, collaborators in the commission of the criminal offense or authors of other related offenses, is exempted from punishment for the criminal offense committed by him.

##### Neni53

Reductionofpunishmentbelowthelimitsprovidedbylaw

(Addedwordsbylawno.144,dated2.5.2013)

The court, in special cases, when it deems that the act and its author present low dangerousness and several mitigating circumstances are present, and no aggravating circumstance is present, has the right to determine a punishment below the minimum or a milder type of punishment than that provided by the relevant provision.

##### Neni53/a

Replacementofimprisonmentwithpaymentofasumofmoneyinfavorofthestate

(Addedbylawno.98,dated31.7.2014)

The court, in cases when expressly provided by the provision, when it deems that the criminal offense and its author present low social dangerousness and when it determines a punishment up to two years imprisonment, may decide to replace the imprisonment with payment of a sum of money in favor of the state, calculating one day of imprisonment as five thousand lekë. This replacement may be decided when requested by the author of the criminal offense.

In case the sum of money determined by the court is not paid within 10 days from the moment the decision becomes final, upon request of the prosecution, the court decides to revoke the decision for replacement of imprisonment.

##### Neni54

Acceptanceofpaymentofthefine

For penal contraventions for which, in addition to the fine, imprisonment is simultaneously provided, the court, upon request of the author of the penal contravention may accept that he pay on the spot a sum of money in favor of the state budget, equal to half of the maximum fine provided for the penal contravention in the General Part of this Code.

The request may be submitted at any stage of the trial until before giving the final decision of the first instance.

When the court does not accept the request, it gives punishment for the offense committed. Acceptance of the request is not allowed for persons who have previously been convicted also for penal contraventions.

##### Neni55

Determination of sentence for multiple criminal offenses (Paragraph two added by law no. 144/2013, dated 2.5.2013 and repealed by decision of the Constitutional Court no. 9, dated 26.2.2016; paragraph two amended by law no. 135/2015, dated 5.12.2015 and repealed by law no. 82/2016, dated 25.7.2016)

When actions or omissions contain elements of several criminal offenses, as well as when a person has committed several criminal offenses for which no decision has yet been rendered, the court first determines the sentence for each criminal offense and ultimately renders a single sentence, which consists of the most severe sentence imposed. The most severe sentence imposed may not exceed the sum total of the individually determined sentences, nor the highest limit provided for the type of sentence rendered. When the court finds that the commission of multiple criminal offenses does not demonstrate great dangerousness of the offender, it may render as a final sentence the most severe sentence it has determined for one of the criminal offenses.

The court in its final decision renders one or more of the supplementary sentences rendered separately for each individual offense.

##### Neni56

Merger of sentences

When a convicted person, before full service of the sentence, is sentenced for a criminal offense committed before the rendering of the decision, the rules of the above article apply and the portion of the served sentence is credited to the new sentence. When a convicted person, after the rendering of the decision, but before full service of the sentence, commits another criminal offense, the court merges the new sentence with the remaining portion of the previous sentence, following the rules provided for in article 55 of this Code.

##### Neni57

Calculation of pre-trial detention

(Paragraph three amended by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The time of pre-trial detention is credited to the sentence of imprisonment or fine, as well as to the obligation to perform work in the public interest as follows:

One day of pre-trial detention equals one and a half days of imprisonment.

One day of pre-trial detention equals 5,000 lekë fine.

One day of pre-trial detention equals eight hours of work in the public interest.

##KREUVII

ALTERNATIVES TO IMPRISONMENT

##### Neni58

Semi-liberty

(Amended by law no. 10023, dated 27.11.2008)

For a sentence of up to one year of imprisonment, the court, due to the convicted person's obligations for work, education, qualification or professional training, essential family responsibilities or the need for medical treatment or rehabilitation, may decide on the execution of the imprisonment sentence with semi-liberty.

The convicted person serving the sentence with semi-liberty is obligated to return to prison after having fulfilled obligations outside the prison, within the time period determined by the court. When the convicted person does not fulfill the obligations under this article, article 62 of this Code applies.

##### Neni59

Suspension of execution of imprisonment sentence and placement on probation (Amended by law no. 10023, dated 27.11.2008; paragraph three added, paragraph four amended by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

1. Due to the low dangerousness of the person, age, health or mental conditions, lifestyle and needs, especially those related to family, education or work, circumstances of commission of the criminal offense, as well as behavior after commission of the criminal offense, the court, when it renders a sentence of imprisonment of up to five years, may order that the convicted person maintain contact with the probation service and be placed on probation, suspending the execution of the sentence, on condition that during the probation period no other criminal offenses are committed. 2. The court orders that the convicted person fulfill one or more obligations provided for in article 60 of this Code.

3. For sentences rendered of up to two years of imprisonment, suspended by the court, the probation period is twice the length of the imprisonment sentence rendered by the court. 4. The probation period, with the exception of the rule provided for in paragraph 3 of this article, is from 24 months to 5 years. The probation period begins the day after the pronouncement of the court decision that rendered the decision to suspend the imprisonment sentence. The probation period may be shortened or extended depending on the manner of execution of probation and the personal conditions of the convicted person, but without violating the minimum and maximum period provided for in this paragraph.

5. If the convicted person does not maintain contact with the probation service or does not fulfill the obligations provided for in article 60, as ordered by the court, the court decides the replacement of the first sentence with another sentence, extension of the supervision period within the probation period, or revocation of the suspension of execution of the decision.

##### Neni59/a

Home detention

(Added by law no. 10023, dated 27.11.2008; paragraph two added by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

For sentences of imprisonment of up to two years or when this time is the remaining portion of the sentence, according to a decision for a longer period of imprisonment, the court may decide on the service of the sentence rendered with detention at the convicted person's home, in another private home or in a care and public health center, when the following circumstances exist: a) For pregnant women or mothers of children under 10 years of age living with them.

b) For fathers who have parental responsibility for a child living with them under 10 years of age, when the child's mother has died or is unable to care for them.

c) For persons in serious health conditions requiring continuous care from the health service outside prison. ç) For persons over 60 years of age who are incapacitated for health reasons.

d) For young persons under 21 years of age with documented health, educational, work or family responsibility needs.

The court, in the circumstances provided for in letters "a" and "b" of the first paragraph of this article, may not decide on the service of the sentence rendered with home detention for convicted persons who have committed a crime against their spouse, cohabitant or child.

The court may allow persons sentenced to home detention to leave their residence for fulfillment of essential family needs, to engage in work activities, education or training or for rehabilitation programs, for which the probation service agrees. In this case the court determines the measures to be taken by the probation service.

The court revokes home detention and replaces it with another punishment when the conditions provided for in the first paragraph of this article no longer exist. If the convicted person leaves the residence without court authorization or violates the obligations determined in the court decision, article 62 of this Code applies.

##### Neni60

Obligations of the convicted person placed on probation (Amended by law no. 10023, dated 27.11.2008; point 12 of the first paragraph amended, one sentence of the second paragraph amended and paragraph three added by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

The convicted person placed on probation may be obligated by the court to fulfill one or more of the following obligations:

1. To exercise a professional activity or to obtain education or professional training. 2. Use of wages and other income or property to fulfill financial obligations. 3. To repair the civil damage caused.

4. Prohibited from driving certain vehicles. 5. Not to exercise professional activity when the criminal offense is related to that activity. 6. Not to frequent certain places.

7. Not to frequent establishments serving alcoholic beverages.

8. To remain at one's residence at specified hours.

9. Not to associate with certain persons, mainly with convicted persons or accomplices in the criminal offense.

10. Not to possess, carry or use weapons.

11. Treatment or rehabilitation in a health institution or submission to a treatment, medical or rehabilitative program.

12. To submit to a treatment or medical or rehabilitation program with the aim of stopping the use of alcohol or narcotic substances.

In determining obligations for the convicted person, the court takes into account the age of the convicted person, mental state, lifestyle and needs, especially those related to family, education or work, motives for commission of the criminal offense, attitude after commission of the criminal offense, as well as other circumstances affecting the imposition of obligations under this article and their supervision. The obligations provided for in points 11 and 12 of paragraph 1 of this article are determined only with the consent of the convicted person.

The period for fulfillment of an obligation provided for in paragraph 1 of this article is determined by the court within the determined probation period.

##### Neni60/a

Obligationtowithdrawfromtheuseofalcoholordrugs

(Addedbylaw nr.144,dated2.5.2013;addedwordinparagraphthreebylaw nr.36/2017,dated30.3.2017)

The court, against the convicted person placed on probation, who is in a condition of chronic dependence on alcohol or narcotic substances, imposes the obligation to undergo medical treatment for withdrawal from the use of alcohol or drugs. Medical treatment for withdrawal from the use of alcohol or drugs is conducted in a specialized medical institution, according to the determination made by the Ministry of Health, based on the request of the probation service. The probation service supervises the execution of the court decision and reports immediately to the prosecutor when the convicted person does not fulfill the obligations imposed according to the provisions of Article 60 of this Code.

##### Neni61

Obligationsoftheconvictedpersonduringtheprobationperiod

(Amendedbylaw nr.10023,dated27.12.2008)

During the probation period the convicted person is obliged:

- a) to appear regularly and to continuously inform the probation service about the fulfillment of the conditions and obligations set by the court;
- b) to obtain permission from the probation service for changing residence, place of work or for frequent movement within the country.

##### Neni62

Violationofconditionsandobligationsduringtheprobationperiod

(Amendedbylaw nr.10023,dated27.11.2008)

If the convicted person, during the probation period, commits another criminal offense, the court may change the obligations imposed, replace the given sentence with another sentence, or revoke, wholly or partially, the suspension decision.

If the convicted person, during the probation period, violates the conditions or obligations imposed, the probation service reports immediately to the prosecutor.

For minor and first-time violations of the conditions or obligations imposed by the court, the prosecutor has the right to give a warning, which is registered in the personal file of the convicted person.

For serious or repeated violations, the prosecutor requests the court to change the imposed obligation, add other obligations, replace them with another sanction, or revoke the decision of suspension of the sentence and serve the remaining part of the sentence in prison.

##### Neni63

Suspensionofexecutionoftheimprisonmentdecisionandobligationtoperformworkinpublicinterest

(Amendedbylaw nr.10023,dated27.11.2008)

The court, due to the low dangerousness of the person and the circumstances of committing the criminal offense, when it has imposed a sentence of up to one year imprisonment, may decide to suspend the execution of the imprisonment decision and replace it with the obligation for the convicted person to perform work in public interest.

Work in public interest means the performance by the convicted person of work, with his consent and without compensation, in favor of the public interest or the association specified in the court decision, for a period from forty to two hundred and forty hours.

This obligation cannot be imposed when the convicted person refuses the suspension in the court hearing. Work in public interest is performed within a period of six months.

In its decision the court determines the number of working hours and the obligation for the convicted person to maintain contact with the probation service. The probation service determines the type of work to be performed, specifies the place of work performance and the days of the week when it will be performed, taking into account, as far as possible, the regular employment of the convicted person or his family obligations. The duration of work in public interest does not exceed eight hours per day.

After completion of the work, the sentence is considered as served. If the convicted person does not perform the work in public interest, does not maintain contact with the probation service or violates the conditions or other obligations imposed by the court, the prosecutor immediately informs the court. The court, in this case, decides according to Article 62 of this Code.

##### Neni64

Conditionalrelease

(Amendedbylaw nr.8733,dated24.1.2001;amendedbylaw nr.10023,dated27.11.2008; addedwordinparagraphthreebylaw nr.144,dated2.5.2013;amendedsubparagraphthreeofparagraph1andadded paragraph5bylaw nr.36/2017,dated30.3.2017)

The person sentenced to imprisonment may be released conditionally from serving the sentence only for special reasons, if through his behavior and work he shows that the purpose of his education has been achieved with the sentence served, and has served:

- no less than half of the sentence given for criminal misdemeanors;
- no less than two thirds of the sentence given for crimes with a sentence of up to five years imprisonment;
- no less than three quarters of the sentence given for crimes with sentences of more than 5 years up to the maximum provided by law, with the exception of the provision in paragraph 3 of this article. The time benefited by amnesty or pardon is not counted in the measure of the sentence served.

Conditional release is not allowed for repeat offenders for crimes committed intentionally as well as for those convicted for committing criminal offenses provided in Articles 78/a, 79/a, 79/b, 79/c or the third paragraph of Article 100.

Conditional release is revoked by the court when the convicted person for a criminal offense committed intentionally, during the conditional period, commits another intentional criminal offense, applying the provisions for the combination of sentences. The court orders the convicted person to maintain contact with the Probation Service during the probation period and to fulfill one or more of the obligations provided in Article 60 of this Code. When the convicted person does not maintain contact with the Probation Service or when he does not fulfill the obligations provided in Article 60 of this Code, ordered by the court, the latter decides to replace the first sentence with another sentence, extend the period of supervision during the probation period or revoke the decision for early conditional release.

##### Neni65

(Amendedparagraphsoneandtwobylaw nr.10023,dated27.11.2008;amendedwordinparagraphtwoandadded paragraphthreebylaw nr.36/2017,dated30.3.2017)

1. Conditional release is not allowed for the person sentenced to life imprisonment.

2. Only in exceptional cases the person sentenced to life imprisonment may be released conditionally when:

He has served no less than thirty-five years imprisonment and during the serving of the sentence has maintained exemplary behavior and it is considered that the purpose of the sentence for his education has been achieved.

3. From this rule, convicted persons for criminal offenses provided in Articles 78/a, 79/a, 79/b, 79/c and the third paragraph of Article 100 are excluded.

##### Neni65/a

Securityperiod

(Addedbylaw nr.23/2012,dated1.3.2012)

The court, in rendering the decision, may also decide to set a security period, during which the application of Article 64 of this Code is not allowed, in cases where one of these circumstances exists:

a) criminal offense, the measure of the sentence of which is more than five years;

b) the commission of the criminal offense was done with cruelty and wickedness;

c) when the offense is committed against children, pregnant women or persons who, for various reasons, cannot defend themselves;

ç) when the offense is committed by exploiting family or cohabitation relationships;

d) when the commission of the criminal offense is driven by motives related to gender, race, religion, nationality, language, political, religious or social beliefs.

The security period extends for the time period from three quarters of the sentence given by the court until the full duration of the criminal sentence.

## CHAPTER VIII

EXTINCTIONOFCRIMINALPROSECUTION,SENTENCESANDTHEIRNON-EXECUTION

##### Neni66

(Amendedbylaw nr.36/2017,dated30.3.2017)

Criminal prosecution cannot be initiated when from the commission of the offense until the moment of taking the person as a suspect have passed:

a) forty years in the case of crimes that provide for life imprisonment;

b) twenty years for crimes that provide for a sentence of no less than ten years imprisonment or other heavier sentence;

c) ten years for crimes that provide for a sentence from five to ten years imprisonment;

ç) five years for crimes that provide for a sentence of up to five years imprisonment or fine;

d) three years for criminal misdemeanors that provide for a sentence of up to two years imprisonment;

dh) two years for criminal misdemeanors that provide for a sentence with a fine.

Criminal offenses of Chapter II, Section I, Articles 76-79/c, are not subject to prescription.

##### Neni67

Non-prescriptionofcriminalprosecution

War crimes and crimes against humanity are not subject to prescription of criminal prosecution.

##### Neni68

Prescription of execution of punishment

The sentencing decision is not executed when from the date it has taken final form have passed: a) twenty years for the decision that contains a sentence of fifteen to twenty-five years imprisonment; b) ten years for the decision that contains a sentence of five to fifteen years imprisonment;

c) five years for decisions that contain imprisonment up to five years or other lighter sentences.

##### Neni69

Rehabilitation

Are considered not punished:

- a) those who have been sentenced to imprisonment for a period up to six months or with any other lighter sentence and who during two years from the date of serving the sentence have not committed other criminal offenses;
- b) those who have been sentenced to imprisonment for a period from six months to five years and who during five years from the date of serving the sentence have not committed other criminal offenses; c) those who have been sentenced to imprisonment for a period from five to ten years and who during seven years from the date of serving the sentence have not committed other criminal offenses; ç) those who have been sentenced to imprisonment for a period from ten to twenty-five years and who during ten years from the date of serving the sentence have not committed other criminal offenses.

##### Neni70

Pardon

By the act of pardon the competent body exempts wholly or partially the person from serving the sentence given by a court decision or makes the replacement of the sentence with a lighter type of sentence.

##### Neni71

Amnesty

By the act of amnesty the competent body exempts from criminal prosecution, from serving wholly or partially the sentence or makes the replacement of the sentence with a lighter type of sentence.

Amnesty extends to those criminal offenses committed up to one day before its proclamation, except when the relevant act provides otherwise.

##### Neni72

Application of provisions of the general part

The provisions of the general part of this Code are also applied for other criminal offenses provided as such by special laws.

## KREUI

CRIMES AGAINST HUMANITY

##### Neni73

Genocide

(Removed words "or death" by law no.8733, dated 24.1.2001)

The implementation of a premeditated plan that aims at the total or partial destruction of a national, ethnic, racial or religious group directed against members of the group and accompanied by the following acts, such as: intentional killing of members of the group, serious physical and mental harm, imposition of harsh living conditions that cause physical destruction, implementation of measures aimed at preventing births, as well as forced transfer of children of one group to another group, is punished by imprisonment of not less than ten years or by life imprisonment.

##### Neni74

Crimes against humanity

(Amended by law no.8733, dated 24.1.2001; amended by law no.144, dated 2.5.2013)

Killings, forced disappearance, exterminations, enslavement, internments and deportations, as well as any kind of torture or other human violence, committed according to a specific premeditated plan, or in a systematic manner, against a group of the civilian population, for political, ideological, racial, ethnic and religious motives are punished by not less than fifteen years or by life imprisonment.

##### Neni74/a

Computer dissemination of materials about genocide or crimes against humanity

(Added by law no.10023, dated 27.11.2008)

Public offering or intentional dissemination to the public, through computer systems, of materials that deny, significantly minimize, approve or justify acts that constitute genocide or crimes against humanity, is punished by imprisonment of three to six years.

##### Neni75

War crimes

(Amended by law no.8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Acts committed by various persons in time of war, such as killing, mistreatment or deportation for slave labor, as well as for any other inhuman exploitation to the detriment of the civilian population or in occupied territory, killing or mistreatment of prisoners of war, killing of hostages, destruction of private or public property, destruction of cities, communes or villages, not dictated by military necessity, is punished by deprivation of liberty of not less than fifteen years or by life imprisonment.

CRIMES AGAINST LIFE COMMITTED INTENTIONALLY

Intentional murder

Intentional murder in connection with another crime

Murder for blood feud

Murder of public officials

##### Neni79/b

Intentional murder of State Police employees, during duty or because of it, when the qualities of the victim are evident or known, is punished by imprisonment of not less than thirty years or by life imprisonment.

Murder due to family relations

Intentional murder of a person who is a spouse, ex-spouse, cohabitant or ex-cohabitant, close relative or close in-law with the perpetrator of the criminal offense, is punished by imprisonment of not less than twenty years or by life imprisonment.

##### Neni80

Providing conditions and material means to commit murder is punished by imprisonment up to five years.

##### Neni81

Murder of an infant

##### Neni82

##### Neni83

Murder committed by exceeding the limits of necessary defense

Murder committed in the conditions of exceeding the limits of necessary defense is punished by imprisonment up to seven years.

##### Neni83/a

Serious threat for revenge or blood feud

##### Neni84

Threat

Serious threat for murder or for serious injury made to a person, constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punished by fine or by imprisonment up to one year.

##### Neni84/a

Threats with racist and xenophobic motives through computer systems

(Added by law no. 10023, dated 27.11.2008)

### SEKSIONIIII

##### Neni86

(Amended by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

##### Neni87

Torture, as well as any other inhumane act, when it has resulted in mutilation, disfigurement or any permanent damage to the health of the person or death, shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to twenty years.

(Second paragraph amended by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013)

The same offense, when committed against several persons, against a person who is a spouse, ex-spouse, cohabitant or ex-cohabitant, close blood relative or close in-law of the perpetrator of the criminal offense, or when it has resulted in death, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

##### Neni88/b

(Added by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001)

Serious injury committed by exceeding the limits of necessary defense shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

##### Neni89

Intentional injury that has caused temporary incapacity for work for more than 9 days constitutes a criminal contravention and shall be punished by fine or imprisonment of up to two years.

##### Neni89/a

(Added by law no. 8204, dated 10.4.1997; amended by law no. 36/2017, dated 30.3.2017)

##### Neni93

##### Neni98

##### Neni99

##### Article 230

Acts with terrorist intent (Repealed by law 8733, dated 24.1.2001, amended by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012)

The commission of the following acts, with the intent to spread panic in the population or to compel state organs, Albanian or foreign, to carry out or not carry out a specific act, or to destroy or destabilize, in a serious manner, essential political, constitutional, economic or social structures of the Albanian state, of another state, international institution or organization, shall be punished by imprisonment of not less than fifteen years or by life imprisonment.

Acts with terrorist intent include: a) acts against persons, which may cause death or serious injury;

b) hijacking of aircraft, ships, other means of transport or fixed platforms or the unlawful exercise of control over them, by force or through the threat of use of force or by means of any other form of intimidation;

c) commission of acts of violence against a person on board an aircraft in flight, on board a ship or on board a fixed platform, when such acts may jeopardize the safety of the aircraft, the watercraft or the fixed platform; ç) destruction of an aircraft in operation, of a ship or of a fixed platform or causing such damage to the aircraft, ship or its cargo or fixed platform, that makes it impossible or endangers or may endanger the safety of the flight, navigation or the fixed platform;

d) placing, by any means, on an aircraft in service, on a ship or on a fixed platform, of a device or substance that may destroy the aircraft, ship or fixed platform or that may cause damage to the aircraft, ship or its cargo or fixed platform and that endangers or may endanger the safety of the flight, navigation of the ship or the fixed platform; dh) destruction or damage to aviation equipment or maritime navigation equipment or interference with their operation, when by such an act the safety of the aircraft or the ship may be endangered;

e) dissemination of information that is known to be false, endangering the safety of an aircraft in flight or a ship in navigation; ë) killing or abduction of an internationally protected person, as per article 9 of this Code, or any other assault against him or his freedom;

f) violent attack against the office, private residence or means of transport of the internationally protected person, as per article 9 of this Code, when by this attack his person or freedom is endangered;

g) hostage-taking or abduction of a person and threatening to kill, to injure or to continue holding him hostage; gj) taking into possession, possessing, using, transferring, alienating, disposing of or disseminating nuclear material, intentionally and without being legally authorized, when it causes or may cause death or serious damage to any person or serious damage to property; h) theft, embezzlement or obtaining through fraud of nuclear materials;

i) demanding nuclear materials, using coercion, violence or any other form of intimidation;

j) production, possession, purchase, transport or trade of explosive substances, firearms, biological, chemical or nuclear weapons, as well as scientific research for the production of weapons of mass destruction;

k) commission of acts of violence, using any device, substance or weapon, against a person at an international civil aviation airport, when such acts cause or may cause serious damage or death of persons;

l) destruction or serious damage to facilities or equipment at an international civil aviation airport or to an aircraft located at that airport that is not in flight or interruption of the services of the airport, using any device or weapon, when by this act the safety of the airport is or may be endangered; ll) dissemination, placement, discharge or detonation of explosive substances or other deadly devices, in public places, in state or government offices, public transport systems or public infrastructure, as well as distribution in the environment of hazardous substances, causing fires, floods, explosions, with the intent to cause death or serious bodily injury or with the intent to cause massive destruction of the above-mentioned places, facilities or systems, when this destruction causes or may cause major economic losses;

m) serious and large-scale destruction of public property, public infrastructure, transport system, information system or private property, endangering the lives of persons; n) causing interruption of the supply of water, electricity or any other important resource; or any other act that aims to cause death or serious injury to civilians or to any other person who does not take active part in military operations in a situation of armed conflict, committed for the purposes specified in the first paragraph of this article.

Actions that cause the interruption of an important service, a system, a public or private activity, as a result of a protest, civil disobedience, or a strike, shall not be considered acts with terrorist intent within the meaning of this article.

##### Article 230/a

Financing of terrorism (Added by law no. 9086, dated 19.6.2003; amended by law no. 23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; repealed in part insofar as it also provided for punishment by fine, as a principal punishment, alongside imprisonment, by law no. 144, dated 2.5.2013) The provision or collection of funds, by any means, directly or indirectly, with the intent that they be used or knowing that they will be used, in whole or in part: a) to commit acts with terrorist intent; b) by a terrorist organization; c) by an individual terrorist; shall be punished by not less than fifteen years of imprisonment or by life imprisonment.

The provisions of this article apply:

- a) to all funds, which include property of any kind, corporeal or incorporeal, movable or immovable, regardless of the manner of their acquisition, and legal documents or instruments of any kind, also in electronic or digital form, that indicate title or interest in such property, including bank credits, traveler's checks, bank checks, money orders, shares, securities, bonds, bank guaranteed checks, letters of credit, as well as any other similar financial instrument; b) regardless of whether the person committing one of the acts provided for in the first paragraph of this article, is located in the same state or in a different state from that in which the terrorist organization or individual terrorist is located or from the state in which the act with terrorist intent is committed or will be committed;
- c) in the case provided for by the first paragraph of this article, regardless of whether the funds were actually used to commit the act or acts, for which they were provided or collected, or whether a link can be established between the funds provided and one or more specific acts with terrorist intent.

Knowledge and intent, as per the first paragraph of this article, are inferred from objective factual circumstances.

##### Article 230/b

## CHAPTER IX

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST JUSTICE

##### Article 300

Failure to report a crime

Failure to report to the criminal prosecution authorities, to court, to public order, government or administrative authorities, a crime that is being committed or that has been committed, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to three years.

Exempted from the obligation to report are ascendants and descendants, brothers and sisters, spouse, adopter and adoptees, as well as persons who are obliged to keep a secret known by reason of duty or profession.

##### Article 301

Actions that hinder discovery of the truth

Carrying out actions to change the place of commission of the criminal offense by destroying, altering or erasing its traces or by moving, hiding, destroying, stealing, falsifying an object or a document with the aim of making difficult and preventing the discovery of the criminal offense and its perpetrator, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to three years.

##### Article 302

Support of the perpetrator of a crime

(Added second paragraph by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; added second paragraph by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)

Supplying the perpetrator of a crime with food, with other means for living or providing a dwelling, residence or in any other way with the aim of helping him escape the search, capture or arrest, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to five years.

The same offense when committed in connection with the criminal offenses provided for in articles 73, 74, 75, 79, 219, 220, 221, 230, 230/a, 230/b, 231, 232, 232/a, 234/a, 234/b, 284/a, 333 and 333/a of this Code, is punishable by imprisonment from two to seven years.

Exempted from criminal liability are ascendants and descendants, brothers and sisters, spouse, adopter and adoptees.

##### Article 303

Hiding or destruction of a corpse

Hiding or destruction of the corpse of a person, victim of murder or other violent offenses, done with the aim of helping the perpetrator of the crime escape the search, capture and arrest, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to five years.

##### Article 304

Obligation to report evidence

Failure to appear immediately to report or testify before the prosecution authorities, court or public order authorities, about known evidence that proves innocent another person who is accused or has been convicted of a criminal offense, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to five years. Exempted from the obligation to report are the perpetrator of the criminal offense, as well as persons who come to know the evidence by reason of duty or profession and are obliged not to report or testify about it.

##### Article 305

False report

Reporting the commission of a crime that is known not to have been committed or the commission of a crime by a person who is known not to have committed it, as well as, the creation of false evidence with the aim of initiating criminal prosecution, are punishable by fine or imprisonment up to five years.

##### Article 305/a

False statements before a prosecutor

(Added by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)

Whoever, during criminal investigations or proceedings, questioned by a prosecutor to provide proper information related to the investigation, provides data, orally or in writing, which he knows are, wholly or partly, false or conceals facts or evidence, is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to one year. The provisions of this article are not applicable in the case that the act was committed at any stage of criminal proceedings by a person suspected or accused of the criminal offense or by someone, who should have been exempted from the request for information or to testify, for any lawful reason, or who was not warned about the right not to testify or to answer questions.

##### Article 305/b

False statements before a judicial police officer

(Added by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)

Whoever, during investigation, questioned by a judicial police officer to provide proper information, provides data, orally or in writing, which he knows are, wholly or partly, false or conceals facts or evidence, constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to six months. The provisions of this article are not applicable in the case that the act was committed at any stage of criminal proceedings by a person suspected or accused of the criminal offense or by someone, who should have been exempted from the request for information or to testify, for any lawful reason, or who was not warned about the right not to testify or to answer questions.

##### Article 306

False testimony (Amended first paragraph by law no. 8733, dated 24.1.2001; added third paragraph, by law no. 9275, dated 16.9.2004; amended word in third paragraph by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)

False testimony given before a court constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years. When false testimony is given for the purpose of profit or any other interest given or promised, it is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to three years.

The same offense when committed in connection with the criminal offenses provided for in articles 234/a, 234/b, 284/a, 333 and 333/a of this Code, is punishable by imprisonment from two to six years.

##### Article 307

Refusal to testify

(Amended second paragraph, added last paragraph, by law no. 9686, dated 26.2.2007)

Refusal to answer questions about knowledge of the commission of a criminal offense or its perpetrator, constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to one year.

When refusal to testify is done for the purpose of profit or any other interest given or promised, it is punishable by imprisonment from one to four years. The provisions of this article are not applicable in the case that the act was committed at any stage of criminal proceedings by a person suspected or accused of the criminal offense or by someone, who should have been exempted from the request for information or to testify, for any lawful reason, or who was not warned about the right not to testify or to answer questions.

##### Article 308

Deliberate distortion of the content of a document or writing given for translation by the criminal prosecution authorities or the court or false translation before them, constitutes a criminal misdemeanor and is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to two years.

When false translation is done for the purpose of profit or any other interest given or promised, it is punishable by fine or imprisonment up to three years.

##### Neni328

Active corruption in elections (Amended by law no.23/2012, dated 1.3.2012 and by law no.89/2017, dated 22.5.2017, words removed by law no. 146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)

The offering or giving of money, material goods, promise of employment or other favors in any form, to voters or other persons related to them, for the purpose of obtaining signatures for the nomination of a candidate in elections, for voting in a certain manner, for participating or not in voting, or for engaging in illegal activities for the support of a candidate or political party, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

##### Neni328/a

Use of public function for political or electoral activities (Added by law no.23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; amended by law no.89/2017, dated 22.5.2017, words removed in the final paragraph by law no.146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)

Participation of an employee who performs a state duty in the civil service or in a non-political function in the state administration, in violation of the law, in the activities or electoral campaign of a political party or candidate in elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.

Forcing or organizing the participation in electoral activities of an electoral subject of pre-university education students by employees who perform a state duty in public education, or a duty or function in non-public education, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.

Forcing or requesting citizens, by an employee who performs a state duty, that against their will or under the threat of using administrative or disciplinary measures, to participate in electoral activities of an electoral subject, to participate or not in elections, to support or not a political party or a candidate in elections, or to vote in a certain manner, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to three years.

Use by an employee who performs a state duty, of public goods, function or state activity, or of financial or human resources, for the purpose of favoring a political party or candidate in elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to three years.

##### Neni328/b

Passive corruption in elections

(Added by law no.89/2017, dated 22.5.2017, words removed by law no.146/2020, dated 17.12.2020)

Soliciting or accepting by a voter of money, material goods, or other favors in any form, for themselves or others, for the purpose of giving a signature for the nomination of a candidate in elections, for voting in a certain manner, for participating or not in voting, or for engaging in illegal activities in support of a candidate or political party, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

##### Neni329

Threat or violence against participants in elections

(Amended by law no.23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; amended by law no.89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

A threat made to a voter to vote in a certain manner or to participate or not in voting, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.

A threat or use of violence against a commissioner, observer, counter as well as any other official charged with elections to prevent them in the performance of their duty or because of their activity in the electoral administration is punishable by imprisonment from one to four years. When this offense is committed in collaboration or more than once, it is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.

##### Neni330

Obstruction of the voter

(Amended by law no.23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; words amended by law no.89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

Obstruction of the voter from voting at their voting center, by violating voting rules, by taking or damaging their identification document, or in any other form, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

When this offense is committed more than once, against more than one voter or when committed by electoral commissioners, it is punishable by imprisonment from three to seven years.

##### Neni330/a

Abandonment of duty by members of election commissions

(Added by law no.23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; words amended by law no.89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

Abandonment of duty or refusal to perform duty by persons charged with administering the voting and counting process, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.

When the above actions are committed by taking along or destroying electoral materials, or when they have brought serious consequences for the voting process or have led to the invalidity of elections, it is punishable by imprisonment from two to seven years.

##### Neni331

Violation of the right to vote (Amended by law no.23/2012, dated 1.3.2012; repealed the part that provided for punishment with a fine, as a principal sanction, alongside punishment with imprisonment, by law no.144, dated 2.5.2013)

Intentional non-inclusion in voter lists of persons who have the right to vote or intentional registration in them of persons who do not have this right, is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years. If this offense is committed in collaboration, when it has brought serious consequences for the interests of voters or the conduct of the electoral process, it is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.

##### Neni331/a

Taking or illegal use of identification documents

(Added by law no.89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

Giving an identification document for the purpose of using it illegally for elections, guaranteeing non-participation in voting, to influence the manner of voting, or for any other illegal purpose related to elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to three years. Taking the identification document of other citizens, for the purpose of using it illegally for elections, not allowing them to vote, to influence the manner of voting, or for any other illegal purpose related to elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

##### Neni332

Abuse of military authority

(Words amended by law no.89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

Abuse of military authority by an officer or military cadre to influence voting among other military personnel under their command, by giving orders, advice or with any kind of propaganda, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from six months to three years.

##### Neni332/a

Abuse of police authority

(Added by law no.89/2017, dated 22.5.2017)

Abuse of police authority by an employee of the State Police or Prison Police to influence the support of a political party or candidate in elections, through non-exercise of function with impartiality according to law, participation in political activities of a political party or candidate in elections, by performing any action or giving orders, advice or any other kind of propaganda that favors a political party or candidate in elections, constitutes a criminal offense and is punishable by imprisonment from one to five years.

## KREUXI

CRIMINAL OFFENSES COMMITTED BY ARMED GANGS AND CRIMINAL ORGANIZATIONS

##### Neni333

Criminal organization

(Amended by law no.9275, dated 16.9.2004)

The creation, organization or direction of criminal organizations is punishable by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

Participation in a criminal organization is punishable by imprisonment from four to eight years.

If the criminal organization is armed and its members possess weapons and explosives for the purposes of fulfilling its criminal activity, even if they are hidden or kept in special places, the punishment by imprisonment is increased by one third.

When the economic activities, undertaken or controlled by members of the criminal organization, are financed wholly or partially with the proceeds of criminal offenses, the measure of punishment, according to the aforementioned paragraphs in this article, is increased by one third to one half of it.

##### Neni333/a

Structured criminal group

(Added by law no.9275, dated 16.9.2004)

The creation, organization or direction of a structured criminal group for the commission of criminal offenses is punishable by imprisonment from three to eight years.

Participation in a structured criminal group is punishable by imprisonment from two to five years.
