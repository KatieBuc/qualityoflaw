ACT

ON THE PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (ZPND)

(unofficial consolidated text No. 3)

Part One: GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Article 1

(purpose of the act)

(1) This Act defines the concept of domestic violence, determines the role and tasks of state authorities, holders of public authorisations, providers of public services and other providers of services in the field of social welfare, long-term care, health, upbringing and education, bodies of local self-government communities (hereinafter: authorities and organisations) and non-governmental organisations in dealing with domestic violence, and lays down measures for the protection of victims of domestic violence.

(2) For the prevention of domestic violence and for the protection and assistance of victims, in addition to the measures provided for by this Act, measures provided for by other acts and regulations issued on their basis shall also apply.

(3) This Act partially transposes into the legal order of the Republic of Slovenia Directive 2012/29/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 establishing minimum standards on the rights, support and protection of victims of crime, and replacing Council Framework Decision 2001/220/JHA (OJ L No. 315 of 14 November 2012, p. 57), as corrected by Corrigendum (OJ L No. 287 of 31 October 2015, p. 87).

## Article 2

(definition of family members)

(1) Family members within the meaning of this Act are:

- a spouse or cohabiting partner,
- a relative in the direct line,
- relatives in the collateral line up to and including the fourth degree,
- a person related by affinity in the direct line,
- a person related by affinity in the collateral line up to and including the second degree,
- an adopter and adoptee,
- a foster parent and a child placed in a foster family,
- a guardian and ward,
- persons who have a common child,
- persons living in a common household,
- persons in a partnership, regardless of whether they live in a common household.
  - (2) Persons referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be considered as family members within the meaning of this Act even if the relationship or community has ceased.
  - (3) A family member within the meaning of this Act shall also be a new spouse or cohabiting partner or partner in a concluded or non-concluded partnership of a family member or a child of any of the family members referred to in paragraph one of this Article.

## Article 3

(definition of domestic violence)

(1) Domestic violence (hereinafter: violence) is prohibited.

(2) Violence is any use of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence by one family member (hereinafter: perpetrator of violence) against another family member (hereinafter: victim) or neglect or stalking of the victim regardless of age, sex or any other personal circumstance of the victim or perpetrator of violence, and corporal punishment of children.

(3) Physical violence is any use of physical force or threat of the use of physical force which compels the victim to do or omit or suffer something or restricts their movement or communication and causes them pain, fear or humiliation, regardless of whether bodily injuries have occurred.

(4) Sexual violence is conduct with sexual content to which the victim does not consent, is forced into or does not understand the meaning thereof due to their stage of development, threats to use sexual violence, and public disclosure of sexual content about the victim.

(5) Psychological violence is conduct and dissemination of information by which the perpetrator of violence causes fear, humiliation, a sense of inferiority, endangerment and other mental distress to the victim, even if committed through the use of information and communication technology.

(6) Economic violence is unjustified control or restriction of the victim in disposing of income or managing property which the victim independently disposes of or manages, or unjustified restriction of disposal or management of the common property of family members, unjustified non-fulfilment of financial or property obligations towards a family member or unjustified transfer of financial or property obligations to a family member.

(7) Neglect is a form of violence when the perpetrator of violence omits due care for the victim who needs it due to illness, disability, age, developmental or other personal circumstances.

(8) Stalking is intentional repeated unwanted establishment of contact, pursuit, physical intrusion, observation, remaining in places where the victim moves or other form of unwanted intrusion into the victim's life.

Article 3.a

(prohibition of corporal punishment of children)

(1) Corporal punishment of children is prohibited.

(2) Corporal punishment of children is any physical, cruel or degrading punishment of children or any act intended to punish children which has elements of physical, psychological or sexual violence or neglect as an educational method.

## Article 4

(special protection and care)

(1) A minor family member (hereinafter: child) enjoys special protection against violence.

(2) A child is also a victim of violence if present during the perpetration of violence against another family member or lives in an environment where violence is perpetrated.

(3) Special care in dealing with violence and providing assistance to victims shall be given to elderly persons and disabled persons and persons who are unable to care for themselves due to personal circumstances.

## Article 5

(duty to act)

Authorities and organisations are obliged to carry out all procedures and measures necessary to protect the victim according to the degree of their endangerment and to protect their interests, while ensuring respect for the victim's integrity. If the victim of violence is a child, the interests and rights of the child take precedence over the interests and rights of other participants in the proceedings.

## Article 6

(duty to report)

(1) Authorities and organisations and non-governmental organisations which, in the course of their work, learn of circumstances on the basis of which it is possible to conclude that violence is being perpetrated, are obliged to immediately notify the social work centre thereof, except in the case where the victim expressly objects to this and there is no suspicion of the commission of a criminal offence prosecuted ex officio.

(2) Everyone, especially professional workers in the health sector and staff of pre-school, educational institutions and social welfare institutions, and providers of content for children in sports and cultural associations, regardless of the provisions on the protection of professional secrecy, shall immediately notify the social work centre, the police or the state prosecutor's office when they suspect that the victim of violence is a child or a person who is unable to care for themselves due to personal circumstances.

## 7. člen

(victim's companion)

(1) A victim of violence may choose a person (hereinafter: companion) who may accompany them in all procedures related to violence and in procedures where the perpetrator of violence is involved.

(2) For the presence of a companion in the procedures from the previous paragraph, it is sufficient that the victim declares before the commencement of the procedure or during the procedure that a certain person is accompanying them and that they wish for that person to be present in the procedure.

(3) A companion may be any adult person who is not treated as a perpetrator of violence in the procedure.

(4) The companion assists the victim in protecting their integrity in procedures before authorities and organizations, assists in finding solutions and provides psychological support to the victim.

(5) The authority conducting the procedure shall prohibit a person from accompanying the victim in the procedure if they do not meet the condition from the third paragraph of this Article or if there is a likelihood that the person will not be able to perform the tasks from the previous paragraph due to family or other connections with the victim or the perpetrator of violence.

## 8. člen

(victim's right to an advocate)

A victim of violence has the right to a female advocate or male advocate (hereinafter: advocate) who, in accordance with special regulations, protects the victim's interests in procedures and activities concerning them.

## 9. člen

(protection of victim's identity)

(1) Data on the basis of which it would be possible to identify the victim or their family must not be disclosed to the public regarding victims or perpetrators of violence. Disclosure of data is permissible only if an adult victim expressly consents to this and does not thereby expose children or their personal data.

(2) Parents or the child's guardian, foster parent, adoptive parent are obliged, within the framework of exercising care for the child, to protect them from exposure to the public.

9.a člen

(protection of victim's data)

Authorities and organizations and non-governmental organizations protect data on the accommodation of the victim and their children or other measures for their protection as professional secrecy. 9.b člen

(informing victims)

Authorities and organizations and non-governmental organizations shall ensure that victims receive appropriate and timely information about available support services and legal remedies in a language they understand.

Part Two: NETWORK AND TASKS OF AUTHORITIES AND ORGANIZATIONS AND NON-GOVERNMENTAL

ORGANIZATIONS

## 10. člen

(role of authorities and organizations and non-governmental organizations)

(1) Authorities and organizations and non-governmental organizations are obliged, within the framework of tasks and powers determined by laws and other regulations, to give priority treatment to cases of violence, ensure mutual notification and assistance with the aim of preventing and detecting violence, eliminating causes and providing assistance to the victim in establishing conditions for a safe life.

(2) The female minister or male minister (hereinafter: minister) responsible for the family shall specify in more detail the procedures for mutual notification and provision of assistance from the previous paragraph.

(3) Ministers responsible for the operation of the police, health organizations, social welfare and educational institutions shall determine, in agreement with the minister responsible for the family, rules and procedures that ensure coordinated operation of authorities and organizations, and which authorities and organizations from the specified areas must observe when dealing with cases of violence.

(4) Professional workers in authorities and organizations who, within the framework of the rules and procedures from the previous paragraph, perform tasks in the field of violence, shall, within the framework of continuing education, further training and qualification, undergo mandatory regular education, in particular on the prevention and detection of violent acts, prosecution, trial and enforcement of sanctions for these violent acts, on equality between women and men, on the needs and rights of victims and the prevention of secondary victimization, to the extent determined by the ministers from the previous paragraph.

(5) Responsible persons in authorities and organizations and non-governmental organizations shall ensure training for all professional workers who encounter victims or perpetrators of violence in the course of their work.

(6) Judges and state prosecutors who encounter victims or perpetrators of violence in their work shall, within the framework of continuing education, further training and qualification, undergo mandatory regular education in the areas from the fourth paragraph of this Article.

## 11. člen

(national programme)

(1) The National Assembly, on the proposal of the Government, shall adopt a resolution on the national programme for the prevention of domestic violence and violence against women (hereinafter: national programme) for a period of six years.

(2) The national programme shall be prepared by the ministry responsible for the family, in cooperation with other ministries, and shall include in particular:

- an assessment of the situation and identification of key problems in the field of prevention of domestic violence and violence against women;
- fundamental objectives, guidelines and tasks in the field of prevention of domestic violence and violence against women in the period covered by the national programme;
- holders of action plans for the implementation of guidelines and tasks from the national programme in individual areas;
- preventive activities;
- data to be collected, processed, linked, stored, analyzed and reported within the framework of official statistics activities;
- guidelines for mandatory and regular systematic education and training of professional workers of all authorities and organizations;
- provision of financial and other resources also for the operation of non-governmental organizations in the field of violence prevention;
- guidelines for work with children who are victims of sexual abuse;
- work with perpetrators of violence.

## 12. člen

(action plans)

(1) Action plans are implementing acts of the national programme that define the necessary activities for a period of two years for each individual area.

(2) Proposals for action plans based on reports on the past two-year period and any additional proposals from individual ministries shall be prepared by a working group established for this purpose by the ministry responsible for the family, and a joint action plan for the two-year period shall be submitted to the Government for adoption.

(3) Ministries shall send the report from the previous paragraph to the ministry responsible for the family no later than two months before the expiry of each action plan.

## 13. člen

(reporting on the implementation of the national programme)

The Government shall report to the National Assembly every two years on the implementation of the national programme. The report shall state the measures and activities carried out in the previous two-year period.

## 14. člen

(tasks of social work centres)

(1) A social work centre shall provide services to victims and perpetrators of violence pursuant to the law governing social welfare, with the aim of treatment being the elimination of immediate danger and concern for the victim's long-term safety, by eliminating the causes or circumstances in which violence occurs, through resolving their social and material living conditions.

(2) A social work centre shall give particularly careful treatment to cases of violence where the victim of violence is a child, especially where there is suspicion of sexual abuse of children.

(3) A social work centre may, at the request of the victim, obtain from the prison institution data on leave, any escape from serving a prison sentence and the expected date of release of a convict who perpetrated violence against the victim or their children.

(4) A social work centre may refer a perpetrator of violence to appropriate social welfare, educational, psychosocial or health programmes implemented by authorities and organizations and non-governmental organizations. The centre shall verify with the programme provider from the previous sentence the participation of the perpetrator in the programme to which they were referred. Non-attendance or irregular attendance at a programme to which the perpetrator of violence was referred constitutes an aggravating circumstance for the perpetrator of violence in court proceedings under this law.

(5) A multidisciplinary team shall be established at a social work centre for dealing with domestic violence. Participation in the team is mandatory for all those invited.

(6) The composition of the multidisciplinary team and the method of work shall be determined by the minister responsible for the family.

## 15. Article

(victim assistance plan)

(1) After examining the circumstances of the case, the Social Work Center is obliged to offer the victim forms of assistance under the law governing social welfare and to assess whether it is necessary to develop a victim assistance plan, which it develops together with the victim. The assistance plan is developed if long-term action is required to establish a safe environment for the victim or if several assistance measures are required and in other cases if it assesses that this is necessary.

(2) The assistance plan is prepared within the framework of the multidisciplinary team from the third paragraph of

Article 14 of this Act. The Social Work Center invites other authorities and organizations to participate in the team that are or will be dealing with the victim or the perpetrator of violence within the scope of their activities, as well as non-governmental organizations. Responsible persons of authorities and organizations invited to prepare the assistance plan are obliged to ensure the participation of representatives of the authorities and organizations.

(3) If the victim of violence is a child, the assistance plan shall also provide for measures for the protection of the child under regulations governing family relations.

## 16. Article

(service for coordination and assistance to victims)

(1) A service for coordination and assistance to victims is established at the Social Work Center for the purpose of providing assistance to victims of violence, implementing the intervention service, connecting the activities of authorities and organizations, and monitoring and analyzing the phenomena of domestic violence. The service for coordination and assistance to victims provides services under the law governing social welfare, urgent measures for the protection of the child's interests under the law governing family relations, and other tasks in accordance with regulations.

(2) The service for coordination and assistance to victims includes the intervention service and crisis centers.

## 17. Article

(non-governmental organizations)

(1) Non-governmental organizations in the field of treatment and protection against violence, within the framework of their programs, provide protection and psychosocial assistance to victims, organize programs for teaching perpetrators of violence about non-violent behavior in interpersonal relationships with the aim of preventing further violence and changing violent behavioral patterns, and cooperate with authorities and organizations from various fields, such as: police, state prosecutor's office, court, social work center, health organizations, educational institutions.

(2) Non-governmental organizations cooperate with authorities and organizations in dealing with individual cases of victims and perpetrators of violence. Non-governmental organizations, within the framework of the programs they implement, are also involved in the direct implementation of measures to protect the victim. 17.a Article

(funds from the budget)

Funds from the budget of the Republic of Slovenia are provided for:

- implementing tasks from the national program;
- monitoring and studying the situation in the field of violence;
- training in the field of violence, especially violence against children, including sexual abuse of children;
- financing programs for recognizing and learning positive parenting skills in the field of child-rearing;
- developing measures to prevent violence, monitoring implementation and evaluating the effectiveness of these measures by preparing proposals for improvement;
- preparing, intersectoral coordination and implementation of violence prevention programs;
- co-financing scientific research activities in the field of violence prevention.

Third Part: MEASURES TO ENSURE THE SAFETY OF THE VICTIM

## 18. Article

(protection of life and ensuring personal safety of the victim)

(1) The police protect life and ensure the personal safety of the victim in accordance with regulations governing the tasks and powers of the police.

(2) At the request of the victim, the police shall ensure the safety of the victim and their companion when entering residential and other premises in which the victim resides or has in use, so that they can take from them things necessary to ensure their basic living needs and the basic living needs of their children, as well as things needed to perform their work.

(3) It is considered that the victim, when they have requested police protection from the previous paragraph, has expressed explicit permission for police officers to enter residential and other premises to ensure their safety.

(4) The police may interrupt the implementation of the procedure from the second paragraph of this Article if the victim or their companion does not follow the police instructions on ensuring safety, or if the victim does not act with the intention of obtaining the things from the second paragraph of this Article.

(5) Taking into account the circumstances from the previous procedure and on the basis of a new request from the victim, the police may exceptionally re-implement the procedure from this Article.

(6) The police may refuse the victim's request from the second paragraph of this Article if circumstances have arisen due to which the victim does not have the right to enter residential and other premises.

## 19. Article

(court measures due to violent acts)

(1) The court may, to a perpetrator of violence who has physically injured the victim, or caused damage to their health, or otherwise unlawfully interfered with their dignity or other personality rights, in particular:

- prohibit entering the residence in which the victim lives;
- prohibit staying within a certain proximity to the residence in which the victim lives;
- prohibit staying and approaching places where the victim is usually located (for example workplace, school, kindergarten...);
- prohibit establishing contact with the victim in any way, including by means of distance communication and also through third parties;
- prohibit establishing any meeting with the victim;
- prohibit publishing personal data of the victim, documents from court and administrative files and personal records relating to the victim;
- decide on the surrender of jointly used residence to the victim in accordance with Article 21 of this Act.
  - (2) The court may also impose measures from the previous paragraph:
- if the perpetrator of violence has threatened the victim that they will injure them or otherwise unlawfully interfere with their dignity or other personality rights;
- if the perpetrator of violence has unlawfully entered the residence in which the victim lives, the premises where they work, or otherwise disturbed their peaceful possession;
- if the perpetrator of violence unlawfully harasses the victim against their explicit will, for example by stalking or using means of distance communication;
- if the perpetrator of violence unlawfully harasses the victim against their explicit will by using or publishing personal data of the victim, documents from court and administrative files and personal records relating to the victim.
  - (3) Unless this Act provides otherwise, the court shall impose a measure from the first paragraph of this Article for a maximum of 12 months. The victim may submit a proposal to extend the duration of the imposed measure before the expiry of the period for which the measure was imposed. The court may extend the duration of the measure multiple times, but each time for a maximum of 12 months, unless this Act provides otherwise.

19.a Article

(referral to programs)

(1) If the court imposes a measure from the previous Article, it may refer the perpetrator of violence to appropriate social welfare, educational, psychosocial and health programs implemented by authorities and organizations and non-governmental organizations.

(2) The list of programs and providers from the previous paragraph is maintained by the ministry responsible for the family.

## 20. Article

(measures to ensure the protection of children)

To ensure the protection of children, the court may, in addition to the measures from Article 19 of this Act:

- prohibit the crossing of the state border by the child, except with a specifically designated person, authority or organization;
- propose the withdrawal of the child's personal document in accordance with the law;
- prohibit the issuance of a personal document to the child on the basis of an application by one or both parents or a third party;
- prohibit the delivery of the child's personal document to one or both parents or a third party;
- decide on an urgent medical examination of the child or treatment of the child and on other medical procedures.

## 21. člen

(relinquishment of jointly-used dwelling)

(1) The court may order the perpetrator of violence who lives or has lived in a common household with the victim to relinquish the jointly-used dwelling to the victim for exclusive use to the extent that the perpetrator had it in use.

(2) The court shall not time-limit the duration of the measure from the previous paragraph if the perpetrator of violence is not the owner, co-owner, or joint owner of the jointly-used dwelling.

(3) If the victim and the perpetrator of violence are co-owners or joint owners of the jointly-used dwelling, if they have a building right, right of usufruct, or right of use on the land on which the jointly-used dwelling is located, or if they have jointly leased it, the court shall limit the duration of the measure from the first paragraph of this article to a maximum of 12 months. The court may extend the duration of the measure at the victim's request for a maximum of an additional 12 months.

(4) The court shall limit the duration of the measure from the first paragraph of this article to a maximum of six months if the perpetrator of violence alone or with a third person is the owner, co-owner, or joint owner of the jointly-used dwelling, or if they alone or with a third person have a building right, right of usufruct, or right of use on the land on which the dwelling is located, or if they alone or with a third person have leased it. If the victim, despite diligent effort, cannot find other suitable living space within the time limit set by the court, the court may exceptionally extend the time limit from the previous sentence at the victim's request for a maximum of an additional six months, unless this would cause a disproportionate burden on a third person.

(5) The perpetrator of violence who must relinquish the dwelling to the victim for exclusive use must refrain from all acts that could make such use more difficult or hinder it.

(6) The victim to whom the dwelling is relinquished for exclusive use shall bear the costs of regular management of the dwelling during the period of exclusive use.

## 22. člen

(housing protection in case of violence upon divorce)

(1) Upon dissolution of marriage, a spouse against whom the other spouse exercises violence or if the other spouse exercises violence against their children may demand that the other spouse relinquish to them for exclusive use the dwelling in which they live together or have lived together.

(2) The court may award the dwelling for exclusive use to the spouse who files the request from the previous paragraph, even if otherwise conditions would exist for the award of only part of the dwelling, if this is necessary to prevent violence between spouses or violence against children upon divorce.

(3) The court shall limit the duration of the measure from the previous paragraph to a maximum of six months.

At the victim's request, the court may exceptionally extend the measure for a maximum of an additional six months.

(4) The spouse to whom the dwelling is relinquished for exclusive use shall bear the costs of regular management of the dwelling during the period of exclusive use.

(5) The spouse who is obligated to relinquish the dwelling for use to the other spouse is obligated to refrain from everything that would make the use of the dwelling or part thereof more difficult or prevent it.

(6) The provisions of this article also apply to unmarried partners.

22.a člen

(court decision-making procedure on measures)

(1) The provisions of the law governing non-contentious procedure apply to the court decision-making procedure on measures (hereinafter: procedure) under this law, unless otherwise provided in this law.

(2) The district court has subject-matter jurisdiction for procedures under this law.

(3) In procedures under this law, the court where the victim has permanent or temporary residence also has territorial jurisdiction.

(4) Procedures under this law are urgent and have priority.

(5) In procedures under this law, when the court decides after a hearing has been held, it shall issue a decision within eight days.

(6) In procedures under this law, the public is excluded.

(7) In procedures under this law, the victim is exempt from payment of court fees.

(8) The court in procedures under this law decides on the costs of the procedure at its discretion.

22.b člen

(commencement of procedure under this law)

(1) The procedure under this law commences at the victim's request. The social work center may propose the commencement of the procedure with the victim's consent.

(2) When the victim is a child, the procedure under this law commences at the request of a child older than fifteen years, parents or one of the parents if they have not been deprived of parental rights, the guardian, or the social work center.

(3) When the victim is a person who has been deprived of legal capacity, the procedure under this law commences at the request of the guardian or the social work center.

(4) The request from the first paragraph of this article must be submitted no later than six months from when the victim last suffered bodily injury, or the perpetrator of violence caused them harm to health, or otherwise interfered with their dignity or other personality rights.

22.c člen

(time limits for response to the request)

The time limit for response to a request for commencement of a procedure under this law is eight days.

22.č člen

(decision on the pronouncement of a measure)

In the decision by which the court pronounces a measure under this law, it shall also pronounce a monetary penalty in the event that the perpetrator of violence does not act in accordance with the court's decision or violates the obligation from the fifth paragraph of Article 21 or the obligation from the fifth paragraph of Article 22 of this law. The monetary penalty is pronounced in accordance with the provisions of the law governing enforcement, on enforcement regarding obligations to do, allow, or refrain from something.

22.d člen

(pronouncement of measure without statement from the perpetrator of violence)

(1) When it follows from the request that there is a probability that the perpetrator of violence endangers the life or seriously endangers the health of the victim or their children, or this is necessary for the protection of the child's interests, the court may issue a reasoned decision by which it decides on the measure without sending the request to the perpetrator of violence for response.

(2) In the case from the previous paragraph, the request for commencement of the procedure is served on the perpetrator of violence together with the court's decision.

(3) An objection is allowed against the decision from the first paragraph of this article within eight days from service of the decision. The court that issued the decision decides on the objection. The objection does not stay the execution of the decision.

22.e člen

(prohibition of alternative dispute resolution)

In procedures conducted due to all forms of violence, alternative dispute resolution is excluded.

22.f člen

(appeal)

(1) An appeal is allowed against the decision by which the court decided on the request, except against the decision from the first paragraph of Article 22.d of this law, and against the decision by which it rejected the objection from the third paragraph of Article 22.d of this law, within eight days from service of the decision.

(2) The appeal does not stay the execution of the decision.

(3) The appellate court shall decide on the appeal within eight days.

22.g člen

(protection of children)

(1) The court in deciding in procedures under this law also takes into account the child's opinion if the child expressed it themselves or through a person they trust and have chosen themselves, and if they are capable of understanding its meaning and consequences.

(2) If measures under this law are pronounced for the protection of a child, the social work center monitors their implementation.

(3) The social work center also implements all other necessary measures for the protection of the child in accordance with the law governing family relations.

(4) If the court pronounces a measure from Article 19 of this law on a child who is a perpetrator of violence, the social work center implements the measures necessary for the protection of the child in a manner that respects the court's decision.

22.h člen

(notification)

(1) The court shall immediately notify the police and the social work center, as well as the preschool or educational institution attended by the child, of measures pronounced under this law.

(2) If the court pronounces a measure regarding the issuance, service, or seizure of a personal document, it shall immediately notify the ministry responsible for internal affairs.

## 23. člen

(execution of imposed measures and monetary penalties)

(1) If the perpetrator of violence violates a measure imposed by the court on the basis of the first, second, third, fourth or fifth indent of the first paragraph of Article 19 of this Act, the victim notifies the police thereof, which acts in accordance with the powers it has regarding violations of the measure prohibiting approach to a specific person, place or area, in accordance with the law governing the tasks and powers of the police. The police inform the court that imposed the measure of the established violations.

(2) A monetary penalty imposed in a court decision shall be executed by the court that imposed it, in accordance with the law governing enforcement.

(3) Enforcement in the case of vacating an apartment pursuant to Articles 21 and 22 of this Act shall be carried out by the court that imposed the measure, according to the rules of the law governing enforcement for the eviction and handover of real estate.

Četrti del: FREE LEGAL AID

## 25. člen

(free legal aid)

Unless otherwise provided in this Act, the law governing free legal aid shall apply to free legal aid for victims of violence.

## 26. člen

(conditions for granting free legal aid)

(1) A person for whom a risk assessment has been made is entitled to free legal aid under this Act, notwithstanding the provisions of the law governing free legal aid.

(2) The opinion on the risk to the person shall be provided by the competent social work centre.

## 27. člen

(scope of granting free legal aid)

Free legal aid under this Act shall be granted for proceedings conducted due to measures pursuant to Articles 19, 20, 21 and 22 of this Act and for proceedings under the law governing family relations, if this is, in the opinion of the social work centre, urgently necessary.

## 28. člen

(priority treatment)

The competent authority for free legal aid shall give priority treatment to applications submitted under this Act.

Peti del: DATA COLLECTIONS

## 29. člen

(application of provisions of the law governing personal data protection)

For the processing of personal data contained in data collections and for the protection of the information privacy of the individual, the provisions of the law governing personal data protection shall apply, unless otherwise provided by this Act for individual cases.

## 30. člen

(purpose of processing personal data)

For the purposes of assistance to the victim, treatment of the perpetrator of violence, preparation of the victim assistance plan, its implementation and monitoring, for scientific research purposes and for statistical purposes, social work centres process personal data and maintain data collections of persons they have treated as victims and as perpetrators of violence under this Act.

## 31. člen

(data collections in the field of domestic violence)

(1) Data on the victim and the perpetrator of violence and data on the type of treatment of violence shall be entered, for the purposes specified in Article 30 of this Act, in existing data collections determined by the law governing social welfare.

(2) Data may be entered in the data collections referred to in the preceding paragraph when an assessment has been adopted in accordance with the first paragraph of Article 15 of this Act that a victim assistance plan needs to be prepared.

## 32. člen

(data collection)

(1) Data contained in the data collections referred to in Article 31 of this Act shall be collected directly from the individual and from official collections maintained in the Republic of Slovenia by authorised bodies and organisations.

(2) Social work centres, when performing tasks under this Act, obtain free of charge the data they need for the purposes referred to in Article 30 of this Act and for the purposes of data collection under the law governing social welfare, from existing data collections of the following controllers:

- the Ministry of the Interior – data on the individual and family members (name and surname, gender, date of birth, citizenship data, residence data), data on concluded marriages, divorces and concluded partnerships from the central population register and data on households from other records of the Ministry of the Interior;
- the police – data on issued restraining orders;
- providers of educational activities – data on inclusion in an educational institution and data from the collection of data on children and young people who need help and counselling;
- the Ministry of Health, the National Institute of Public Health and other providers of healthcare activities – data on health status or disability;
- the Pension and Disability Insurance Institute of the Republic of Slovenia – data on insured persons and beneficiaries of pension and disability insurance rights;
- the Public Scholarship, Development, Disability and Maintenance Fund of the Republic of Slovenia – data on paid maintenance allowance;
- the Employment Service of the Republic of Slovenia – data on unemployed persons and on the date and reasons for termination of registration in the unemployment register and reasons for termination of payment of material rights;
- public social welfare institutions and concession holders in the field of social welfare – data on beneficiaries of rights under the law governing parental protection and family relations and under the law governing social welfare, data on maintenance, on placement under guardianship and on the appointment of a guardian, on placement in foster care under the law governing the provision of foster care, on inclusion in social welfare services or programmes and on treatment of domestic violence;
- employers – data on employment.
  - (3) In cases referred to in the first and second paragraphs of this Article, it is not necessary to inform the individuals to whom the data relate in advance.
  - (4) Social work centres shall collect only those data that they need for the treatment of an individual case.

## 33. člen

(data storage)

Personal data entered in the data collections referred to in Article 31 of this Act shall be stored for a maximum of ten years after entry. After this period has expired, the data shall be archived.
