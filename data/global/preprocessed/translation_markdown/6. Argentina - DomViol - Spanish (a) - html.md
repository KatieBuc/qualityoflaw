# PROTECTION AGAINST FAMILY VIOLENCE

PROTECTION AGAINST FAMILY VIOLENCE

Law No. 24.417

Enacted: December 7, 1994

Promulgated: December 28, 1994

The Senate and Chamber of Deputies of the Argentine Nation assembled in Congress, etc., enact with force of Law:

## ARTICLE 1º

Any person who suffers injuries or physical or psychological mistreatment by any member of the family group may report these facts verbally or in writing to the judge with jurisdiction in family matters and request related precautionary measures. For the purposes of this law, family group is understood as that originating from marriage or de facto unions.

## ARTICLE 2º

When the injured parties are minors or incompetent persons, elderly or disabled, the facts must be reported by their legal representatives and/or the public ministry. Social or educational assistance services, whether public or private, health professionals, and all public officials by reason of their work shall also be obligated to file the report. The minor or incompetent person may directly bring the facts to the attention of the public ministry.

## ARTICLE 3º

The judge shall require a diagnosis of family interaction conducted by experts from various disciplines to determine the physical and psychological harm suffered by the victim, the danger situation, and the social and environmental context of the family. The parties may request other technical reports.

## ARTICLE 4º — The judge may adopt, upon becoming aware of the facts giving rise to the report, the following precautionary measures:

a) Order the exclusion of the perpetrator from the dwelling where the family group resides;

b) Prohibit the perpetrator's access to the injured party's domicile as well as to their places of work or study;

c) Order the return to the domicile at the request of the person who had to leave it for reasons of personal safety, excluding the perpetrator;

d) Decree provisionally child support, custody and the right to communication with the children.

The judge shall establish the duration of the measures ordered according to the background of the case.

## ARTICLE 5º

The judge, within 48 hours of adopting the precautionary measures, shall summon the parties and the public ministry to a mediation hearing, urging them and their family group to attend educational or therapeutic programs, taking into account the report of Article 3.

## ARTICLE 6º

The regulation of this law shall provide for measures conducive to providing the accused and their family group with free psychological medical assistance.

## ARTICLE 7º

Reports filed shall be brought to the attention of the National Council for Minors and the Family in order to address the coordination of public and private services that prevent and, where applicable, overcome the causes of mistreatment, abuse and all types of violence within the family.

For the same purpose, the judge may summon public agencies and non-governmental entities dedicated to the prevention of violence and assistance to victims.

## ARTICLE 8º — The following is incorporated as the second paragraph of Article 310 of the National Criminal Procedure Code (Law 23.984):

In proceedings for any of the crimes provided for in book two, titles I, II, III, V and VI, and title V chapter I of the Penal Code committed within a cohabiting family group, even if constituted by de facto unions, and the circumstances of the case give reasonable grounds to presume that it may be repeated, the judge may order as a precautionary measure the exclusion of the accused from the home. If the accused has family support obligations and the exclusion would endanger the subsistence of the dependents, the minors' advocate shall be given intervention so that appropriate actions may be initiated.

## ARTICLE 9º

The provinces are invited to enact norms of the same nature as those provided for herein.
