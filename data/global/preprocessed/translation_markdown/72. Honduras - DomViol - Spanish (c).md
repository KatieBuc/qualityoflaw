# Equal Opportunities for Women Act

Equal Opportunities for Women Act

## TITLE I

ON THE PURPOSE AND NATURE

### CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1.- In Honduras all men and women are born free and equal in

law.

#### Article 2.-The purpose of this Act is to integrate and coordinate the actions that the

State and civil society must execute to eliminate all forms of discrimination against women and achieve equality of men and women before the law, prioritizing the areas of family, health, education, culture, media, environment, work, social security, credit, land, housing and participation in decision-making within power structures.

#### Article 3.

For the purposes of this Act, discrimination against women means any distinction, exclusion, or restriction based on sex, which has the purpose or effect of impairing or nullifying the recognition, enjoyment or exercise by women, irrespective of their marital status, on the basis of equality of men and women, of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural and civil spheres or in any other respect.

#### Article 4.-To comply with this principle of non-discrimination against women, the

following actions are established:

- 1) The State guarantees equality and equity between men and women, in the design and implementation of public policies for the execution and coordination of programs and projects; and,
- 2) Civil society must include the gender dimension in social dialogue forums to promote and encourage organizations that work for, with and on behalf of women.

#### Article 5.-The State, through its competent bodies, is obligated to

promote scientific research on equal opportunities and must create or improve the production, dissemination and evaluation of statistical records on the condition and situation of men and women and their evolution in different areas of social life, in order to periodically establish equal opportunity practices.

#### Article 6.-The State, civil society and women in particular, shall ensure that

the normative development of the principle of equal opportunities and its interpretation in the legal system is done with equity criteria, creating effective mechanisms that fully guarantee the rights of women.

#### Article 7.-The National Women's Institute (INAM) is the state agency

responsible for the coordination, promotion, development and oversight of public policy for the promotion of equal opportunities and treatment.

## TITLE II

ON THE RIGHTS TO EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES

### CHAPTER I

EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES IN THE AREA OF FAMILY

#### Article 8.-The State recognizes equal rights between men and women, in their

relationships, regardless of their marital status. Common-law union between persons legally capable of contracting marriage is recognized. The Act shall establish the conditions for it to have the effects of civil marriage. Adoption is recognized as a legal form of building a family.

#### Article 9.-The Family, as the primary sphere of socialization, transmission and

learning of identity models, behaviors, attitudes and values, must constitute a space for generating equal rights and opportunities for its members.

#### Article 10.-The State is obligated to promote within the family, through

formal and alternative non-formal education, the redistribution of family responsibilities arising from the care and reproduction of the family, to comply with equal opportunities and the proper sense of division of labor.

#### Article 11.-The State through the educational system and social media

must disseminate the concept of shared responsibility within family life, with emphasis on the needs of working mothers or female heads of household.

#### Article 12.-Women must ensure compliance with the provisions of the Family

Code regarding the personal and property rights of spouses or couples who have established common-law unions.

#### Article 13.

The State has the obligation to assist, prevent, combat, eradicate domestic and intra-family violence, giving comprehensive attention to the problem, as established in Decree No. 132-97 dated September 11, 1997, which contains the "Act Against Domestic Violence" and the Penal Code.

### CHAPTER II

EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES IN THE AREA OF HEALTH AND

ENVIRONMENT

#### Article 14.-The right to health protection is recognized.

It is the duty of everyone to participate in the promotion and preservation of personal and community health.

The State shall maintain an adequate environment to protect the health of persons.

#### Article 15.-The State through the Secretariat of State in the Office of Health

must address women's health with a comprehensive approach and establish the interrelation in the aspects of information, promotion, prevention and care considering all stages of women's lives and not only their reproductive function.

#### Article 16.-The gender approach is incorporated at the state and private levels in

physical and mental health programs and policies. To this effect, educational programs shall be developed aimed at achieving full, responsible and comprehensive sexuality and with sufficient information about sexually transmitted diseases, their prevention, symptoms and treatments.

#### Article 17.-In the training of health professionals, universities and

other educational institutions must take into account the specific needs of women, according to their psychological, physiological, social, cultural and age differences.

#### Article 18.-All women health service providers, including those

who work in the areas of kitchen and cleaning, deserve greater attention in cases of wear and fatigue from their professional or occupational practice.

#### Article 19.-Women must exercise their reproductive rights and by mutual agreement

with their partner, decide on the number of sons and daughters and the spacing of their pregnancies.

#### Article 20.-The Secretariat of State in the Office of Health shall take the

appropriate measures for the prevention or proper treatment of pregnancy in adolescents and their risk factors. Likewise, it shall take measures to prevent and address high-risk pregnancies in women of reproductive age.

#### Article 21.-Pregnant women deprived of liberty by mandate of law

shall be subject to special treatment and the State shall provide the corresponding facilities.

#### Article 22.-The State undertakes to incorporate and ensure the validity of the concept of

gender and promote the effective participation of women in the plans, programs and projects of the institutions responsible for promoting the development and conservation of the environment, taking into consideration the prevailing local conditions.

#### Article 23.-The State and civil society undertake to provide appropriate

and accessible technology that responds to the needs of women in urban and rural areas, that

INAM Compendium of Laws on Women's Rights 5 contributes to the protection of the environment and encourages their active participation in decision-making on projects in favor of their natural environment.

#### Article 24.-The State, civil society, municipal corporations, shall promote

educational programs and projects that rescue and promote the experience and wisdom of women in the management and conservation of natural resources.

#### Artículo 25.-El Estado, en coordinación con las organizaciones de la sociedad civil,

se obliga a fomentar y a estimular el diseño e implementación de proyectos con enfoque de género, sobre la producción intensiva de alimentos, con miras hacia la autosuficiencia alimentaría del país, tomando en cuenta las condiciones locales predominantes.

#### Artículo 26.-Las mujeres tendrán igual oportunidad que los hombres, para formar

parte de la fuerza de trabajo asalariada en los programas de riego y forestación, así como en otros programas y proyectos necesarios para mejorar la calidad del medio ambiente urbano y rural. La Secretaría de Estado en los Despachos de Trabajo y Seguridad Social velará por su cumplimiento.

### CAPITULO III

IGUALDAD DE OPORTUNIDADES EN LA

EDUCACIÓN, CULTURA Y COMUNICACIÓN

#### Artículo 27.-La educación es función esencial del Estado para la conservación, el

fomento y difusión de la cultura, la cual deberá proyectar sus beneficios a la sociedad sin discriminación de ninguna naturaleza.

La educación nacional será laica y se fundamentará en los principios esenciales de la democracia, inculcará y fomentará en los educandos profundos sentimientos hondureñistas y deberá vincularse directamente con el proceso de desarrollo económico y social de país.

#### Artículo 28.-El Estado garantiza la formación integral e igualitaria de mujeres y

hombres, bajo el concepto de responsabilidad solidaria, como base de la sociedad y asumirá la tarea de:

- 1) Incorporar una perspectiva no sexista a los procesos educativos de elaboración y transmisión del conocimiento, la cultura e información;
- 2) Hacer prevalecer el principio de igualdad de oportunidades y de trato en todos los niveles del sistema educativo formal, así como la educación alternativa no formal;
- 3) Garantizar la oferta educativa sin discriminación por motivos de sexo;
- 4) Eliminar de los textos usados en el sistema educativo estatal y privado, las funciones estereotipadas de hombres y mujeres y, evitar que la imagen de la mujer se siga utilizando como el único recurso para simbolizar los oficios domésticos que se realizan en el hogar;
- 5) Promover la diversificación de opciones escolares tanto intelectuales, como técnicas y científicas de hombres y mujeres y asegurar la igualdad de oportunidad en el acceso a INAM Compendio de Leyes Sobre Derechos de la Mujer 6 todas estas formas de enseñanza-aprendizaje;
- 6) Facilitar las oportunidades de trabajo de la mujer, preservando y fortaleciendo el sistema de salas cunas y guarderías infantiles; y,
- 7) Revisar y modernizar la legislación educativa, eliminando de su reglamentación la división del trabajo por sexo.

#### Artículo 29.-El Estado formará y actualizará al personal docente para optimizar su

desempeño laboral.

#### Artículo 30.-El Estado promoverá la enseñanza bilingüe en los grupos étnicos y

pueblos indígenas, respetando su identidad pluricultural y conservando la autenticidad de la lengua autóctona.

#### Artículo 31.-Las instituciones educativas estatales y privadas, en todos sus niveles,

deben eliminar la discriminación por razón de género, en aspectos tales como: Decisiones sobre administración, acceso a los cursos de capacitación, participación en actividades deportivas, asesorías y becas o bolsas de estudio académicas.

#### Artículo 32.-Las autoridades de los establecimientos educativos, están obligadas a

advertir a todo el personal que esté bajo su jurisdicción, las consecuencias legales que se derivan de abuso y acoso sexual y cuando esto ocurra, resolverlo de conformidad con la ley.

#### Artículo 33.-El Estado y las organizaciones de la sociedad civil deben brindar

oportunidades educativas a las mujeres, promover y estimularlas para que participen en programas, que las capacite en actividades que les permita incorporarse en igualdad de condiciones con los hombres, a contribuir con el desarrollo sostenible de la nación.

#### Artículo 34.-En los programas educativos de los últimos años de enseñanza básica y

media, deben incorporarse contenidos de educación en población, enfatizando los temas que se refieren a la sexualidad y reproducción, e información científica sobre prevención de embarazos y enfermedades de transmisión sexual.

#### Artículo 35.-A las estudiantes embarazadas en los centros educativos se les

concederá permiso por maternidad, sin poner en peligro la continuidad de su educación.

#### Artículo 36.-Tanto el Estado como la sociedad civil, están en la obligación de reducir

el analfabetismo del país, facilitando la incorporación de la mujer a la educación y al trabajo productivo en forma plena.

#### Artículo 37.-Los medios de comunicación social del Estado están al servicio de la

educación y la cultura. Los medios de comunicación privados, están obligados a coadyuvar en la consecución de dichos fines y, a cumplir con las programaciones que las leyes específicas les ordenan. El Estado regulará esta materia.

#### Artículo 38.-El Estado garantiza la participación la iniciativa de las mujeres en el

desarrollo cultural, respetando la diversidad de identidades, valores y experiencias.

#### Artículo 39.-El rol educativo de los medios de comunicación masiva debe contribuir

de manera significativa al cambio cultural necesario para mejorar la condición social de la mujer.

#### Artículo 40.-Corresponde al Estado, salvaguardar el patrimonio cultural y territorial

de los pueblos indígenas, elaborando programas de capacitación y sensibilización de sus comunidades, dirigidos a eliminar la discriminación hacia las mujeres dentro de los mismos.

#### Artículo 41.-El Estado y la sociedad civil debe mandar a las mujeres y a los hombres

que ocupan posiciones de poder, a que se solidaricen y trabajen por las aspiraciones de todas las mujeres de todas las etnias y estratos socio-económicos deprimidos del país.

#### Artículo 42.-En los mensajes de todo tipo difundidos por los medios de

comunicación social, debe eliminarse el uso de imágenes discriminatorias y peyorativas de las mujeres y, preferentemente se destinarán mayores espacios para informar sobre sus derechos y deberes; así como de las oportunidades y avances en todos los campos del saber humano, con énfasis en la ciencia y la tecnología. El Estado velará por el fiel cumplimiento de esta disposición.

#### Artículo 43.-Con el apoyo del Estado y la sociedad civil, la mujer está obligada a

potenciar su participación como vocera de temas públicos, en los niveles de decisión de los medios de comunicación y, como espectadora de los mismos, exigirá una programación educativa de alto nivel y la eliminación de la violencia sexista.

### CAPITULO IV

IGUALDAD DE OPORTUNIDADES EN EL

TRABAJO Y LA SEGURIDAD SOCIAL

#### Artículo 44.-La Secretaría de Estado en los Despachos de Trabajo y Seguridad

Social, exigirá que a trabajo igual corresponda salario igual, sin discriminación alguna, siempre que el puesto, la jornada y las condiciones de eficiencia y tiempo de servicio sean también iguales.

#### Artículo 45.-La mujer al igual que el hombre, tiene derecho a la seguridad de sus

medios económicos de subsistencia, en caso de incapacidad temporal y/o permanente para trabajar u obtener trabajo retribuido.

El Estado tomará todas las medidas necesarias para que el Instituto Hondureño de Seguridad Social (IHSS), cumpla eficazmente con los objetivos para el cual fue creado, revisando su cobertura actual a fin de ampliarlo en beneficio de las mujeres del área rural y urbana en todo el país a su conveniencia posible.

#### Artículo 46.-El Estado no permitirá ninguna clase de discriminación basada en el

género o en la edad que tenga el hombre o la mujer, con el fin de anular o alterar la igualdad de oportunidades o de trato en el empleo o la capacitación.

Se prohíbe a los empleadores solicitar prueba de embarazo como requisito previo para optar a un empleo.

#### Article 47.-Female workers carrying HIV/AIDS shall have the right to have their job stability respected, in accordance with the laws of the country.

#### Article 48.-Male and female employers must provide equal opportunities under similar conditions to women, in the aspects of selection, employment, work assignment and promotion, as well as in training, education and capacity building; likewise prohibiting gender discrimination in staff reductions and dismissals.

#### Article 49.-The State shall ensure that companies and other workplaces hire women and men equitably under equal conditions of work and remuneration.

#### Article 50.-Women who work in domestic service and who are not included in commercial, social and other comparable enterprises, shall be protected by the Honduran Social Security Institute (I.H.S.S.), and subject to a special regime, which shall be regulated by the Secretariat of State in the Offices of Labor and Social Security, within six (6) months from the effectiveness of this Law.

#### Article 51.-The State and social actors shall promote the effective protection of women during their pregnancy and the postnatal period, adopting strict measures aimed at eliminating discrimination in employment and ensuring their job stability and prohibiting the performance of certain types of work that affect their health.

#### Article 52.-Female and male workers in the manufacturing industry and in agro-industrial enterprises shall enjoy all the benefits and social security enjoyed by the formal sector.

#### Article 53.-To set the amount of salary for each type of work, the intensity and quality thereof, climate and living conditions and the length of service of the male or female worker within the same company must be taken into account.

#### Article 54.-A female worker who adopts a male or female minor under five (5) years of age shall be entitled to the same post-partum leave as a woman in a state of pregnancy. This leave shall begin from the day the male or female minor is delivered to her and for which she must present the corresponding judicial resolution.

#### Article 55.-Male and female employers, male and female employers, are prohibited from announcing their job offers by any means and specifying as a requirement the sex, age, religion or marital status of the person; except when due to the nature of the work or employment it requires special characteristics. In this case, the male or female employer must previously have the authorization of the Labor Inspectorate to publish said notice.

#### Article 56.-The salaried work of women outside the home must not interfere with her family responsibility; the same situation must prevail for the work of men, who are also obligated to share equally the work in the home.

#### Article 57.-The State shall ensure the effective incorporation of women into production and guarantee their participation in the preparation of national development plans, in their access to credit programs, land and technology, as well as a fair and direct form of the benefits and opportunities provided by sustainable development, such as training and education in the different areas of the economy.

#### Article 58.-The State through the Secretariat of State in the Office of Finance is obligated to design credit mechanisms and technical assistance with local resources and from international organizations, to stimulate self-management and business development of women.

#### Article 59.-Male and female employers are obligated to promote the adaptation of spaces that allow the satisfaction of basic needs in the workplace (sanitary services, cafeterias, pantries, infirmary and child care centers).

Regarding child care centers, their adaptation shall be mandatory by the employer who has in their service more than 30 female workers, counting on the contribution of parents according to their economic capacity, with the purpose of caring for male and female children under seven (7) years of age, children of male and female workers.

#### Article 60.-Sexual harassment committed by the employer or head of the State or of a private company, empowers the female worker or public servant as the case may be, to terminate the employment relationship without prior notice and without responsibility on her part, preserving the right to legal benefits and compensation as in the case of unjust dismissal. When the one who commits the harassment is a worker, he must be proceeded to immediate dismissal without responsibility for the employer or state institution.

#### Article 61.-The State and the private sector shall promote the participation and full integration of women in the business area until achieving for them sources of financing and opening of new markets, in order to maintain and increase their participation, eliminating discriminatory legal and administrative obstacles, that prevent them from enjoying real access to credit, under equal conditions through loan guarantees, technical advice and marketing development services.

#### Article 62.-The State must recognize and encourage women, both in their role as users and as agents of change in science and technology, in order to contribute to developing in them technological and business skills.

#### Article 63.-The Central Government as well as the municipal and organized civil society, shall make available the necessary resources so that through the respective agencies, women are trained for the exercise of traditional and non-traditional activities, likewise in micro, small, medium and large industry, with innovative approaches in matters of training, for which they must prepare and disseminate learning material and train female instructors.

#### Article 64.-The State and civil society shall jointly support traditional crafts, home agro-industries and small industrial and agricultural activities, carried out by women through the granting of credits, training services, marketing opportunities and technological guidance.

#### Article 65.-Production cooperatives must encourage women to actively participate in the establishment and administration of micro, small or medium enterprises, that allow them to improve their standard of living until becoming owners.

#### Article 66.-The State through the municipal government shall improve the infrastructure and management of markets, social services and transport, to raise the efficiency, security and income of women entrepreneurs and reduce their workload and risks to their health and that of their family.

#### Article 67.-It is the responsibility of the State to promote the equal presence of women at all levels of public administration, contributing to eliminating discrimination against women in access and promotion within it.

#### Article 68.-The State and civil society shall provide social coverage to all female workers, whether they are from the formal or informal sector; this principle shall be developed in the respective Regulation.

### CHAPTER V

EQUALITY OF OPPORTUNITIES IN LAND TENURE,

CREDIT AND HOUSING.

#### Article 69.-Women, like men, are recognized the right to decent and proper housing.

The State shall formulate and execute social housing programs, offering greater flexibility in terms of requirements for access to housing, in the case of women heads of family who lack the assistance of a household partner.

The Law shall regulate the ownership and rental of housing and premises, the use of urban land and their construction, in accordance with the general interest.

#### Article 70.-Credits and loans, internal or external, that the State obtains for

housing purposes, shall be regulated by a Law for the benefit of the final user of the credit, taking into account the parameters of priority and accessibility that facilitate the acquisition of housing in a rational and equitable manner.

#### Article 71.-The State shall evaluate women's housing needs, encouraging

the design and application of innovative projects that promote women's access to financing services and means. It shall repeal laws or administrative practices that restrict or limit women's possibility of acquiring housing as owners or for rent.

#### Article 72.-Women and private development organizations must

participate in housing construction and infrastructure projects, benefit from them on an equal footing with men, participate in the design and construction technology, and in the administration and maintenance of the facilities.

#### Article 73.-Family patrimony consists of urban or rural real estate

acquired by the final users of social interest programs or land titling programs that are financed directly or indirectly by the State. Said property must be registered in the

Property Registry, in the name of both spouses or of persons living under a de facto union regime, registered in the Civil Registry or even when it is not legally recognized.

In case of dissolution of the bond, the interest of minor or dependent children shall be satisfied first; once their interest is satisfied, said patrimony shall correspond in equal parts to the aforementioned spouses, which shall be liquidated in accordance with the provisions of the Family

Code.

#### Article 74.-Preference shall be given to women heads of household in obtaining

bank loans for housing and when she belongs to the peasant sector without any discrimination, she shall enjoy the benefits of the Agrarian Reform Law on equal terms with men.

### CHAPTER VI

EQUALITY OF OPPORTUNITIES IN PARTICIPATION AND

DECISION-MAKING WITHIN THE POWER STRUCTURE

#### Article 75.-The State shall guarantee participatory democracy and the full exercise of

citizenship rights for both men and women, with equal opportunities.

#### Article 76.-The State through the National Electoral Tribunal, shall guarantee that

in the internal structures of political parties, there is no discrimination that excludes or limits the participation of women.

#### Article 77.-At the state level, social organizations shall be strengthened, both at the

national, departmental, municipal and local levels, and women shall be encouraged to exercise true leadership in those instances, promoting and supervising that women are incorporated into the various Boards of Directors, progressively, until their incorporation on an equal footing is achieved.

#### Article 78.-The State has the obligation to develop training and

capacity-building programs to support the emergence and consolidation of women's organizations and to promote their active participation in social, political, economic, business, cooperative, union, religious and student organizations.

#### Article 79.-The State must incorporate women's organizations in the

instances of state, municipal and communal participation, incorporating them in the search for solutions to problems and specific interests and visions of the State, in all instances of formulation of social, national and international agreements or in the resolution of conflicts of any nature.

#### Article 80.-Civil society shall promote the equal participation of women in

the decision-making processes of community groups, non-governmental organizations, unions, cooperatives, professional associations and others, until equitable incorporation into the Boards of Directors is achieved.

#### Article 81.-The State through the respective institutions shall take effective measures

to achieve an equitable distribution of men and women in popularly elected positions, accelerating the process aimed at making effective the exercise of political rights of men and women with equal opportunities.

To achieve the effective participation of women, a base of thirty percent (30%) is established progressively, until equity between men and women is achieved, applicable with regard to leadership positions in political parties, principal and alternate deputies to the National

Congress, to the Central American Parliament, Mayors, Vice-Mayors and Councilors in eligible positions in accordance with a scale based on the results of three preceding elections.

In those departments where representation falls to a single deputy or where will and participation have not been expressed, these provisions shall not be applicable.

#### Article 82.-The State and civil society are obliged to inform the general

citizenry about the value of suffrage and the consequences that the good or bad use of it brings in a true participatory democracy.

#### Article 83.-Civil society shall demand that politicians formulate strategies and

programs that ensure a democracy where equal participation is the foundation of sustainable development and social peace.

#### Article 84.-The State shall guarantee real participation of qualified women in

high-level executive positions, in the different instances of the public sector.

#### Article 85.-The State shall promote and include in sectoral dialogue instances,

the analysis of the situation of women in any of the areas contained in this Law.

For their part, women shall develop a critical attitude towards state management and shall generate public opinion by establishing the demands that the welfare of the population requires.

#### Article 86.

Acts of discrimination shall be null and void. Authorities or private persons who violate the articles of this Law shall be sanctioned with a fine of five thousand lempiras (L.5,000.00) for the first time.

#### Article 87.-The Public Ministry through the Women's Prosecutor's Office, shall oversee and

execute the actions conducive to the proper compliance with this Law and shall have the necessary support of the National Women's Institute (INAM), the Secretariats of State and other competent bodies.

#### Article 88.-This Law repeals legal provisions whose content

discriminates or limits the opportunities of women in the use and enjoyment of the rights enshrined therein.

#### Article 89.-This law enters into force on the date of its publication in the

Official Gazette La Gaceta.

Given in the city of Tegucigalpa, municipality of the Central District, in the Session Hall of the National Congress, on the eleventh day of the month of April of the year 2000
