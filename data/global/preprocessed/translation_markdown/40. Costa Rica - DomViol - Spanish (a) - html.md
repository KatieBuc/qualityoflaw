# This Law

This Law shall regulate the application of protective measures necessary to guarantee the life, integrity and dignity of victims of domestic violence, whose guiding principle is Article 51 of the Political Constitution.

The competent authority shall ensure that aggressor persons do not use this Law against victims.

The authorities involved in the application of this Law shall provide special protection to mothers, minor persons, elderly persons and persons with any disability condition, considering the specific situations of each one.

Likewise, this Law shall protect, in particular, victims of violence in intimate partner relationships or of intrafamily sexual abuse.

(As amended by Article 1 of Law No. 8925 of February 3, 2011)

To interpret this law, the following definitions are established:

- a) Domestic violence: Action or omission, direct or indirect, exercised against a relative by consanguinity, affinity or adoption up to the third degree inclusive, by legal or de facto bond or by a guardianship, trusteeship or curatorship relationship and which produces as a consequence, the impairment of their physical, sexual, psychological or patrimonial integrity. The bond by affinity shall subsist even when the relationship that originated it has ended.
- b) Psychological violence: Action or omission intended to degrade or control the actions, behaviors, beliefs and decisions of other persons, by means of intimidation, manipulation, threat, direct or indirect, humiliation, isolation or any other conduct that implies harm to psychological health, self-determination or personal development.
- c) Physical violence: Action or omission that risks or damages the bodily integrity of a person.
- d) Sexual violence: Action that forces a person to maintain sexualized contact, physical or verbal, or to participate in other sexual interactions through the use of force, intimidation, coercion, blackmail, bribery, manipulation, threat or any other mechanism that nullifies or limits personal will.

Likewise, it shall be considered sexual violence the fact that the aggressor person forces the victim to perform any of these acts with third parties.

e) Patrimonial violence: Action or omission that implies damage, loss, transformation, subtraction, destruction, retention or diversion of objects, work instruments, personal documents, goods, valuables, rights or economic resources intended to satisfy the needs of any of the persons mentioned in subsection a) above.

f) Kinship:

Relationship of adoption, affinity or consanguinity up to the third degree inclusive, by ascending, descending or collateral line, originating from a legal, biological or de facto union bond. The bond by affinity shall subsist even when the relationship that originated it has ended.

The definitions comprised in subsections b), c), d), e) and f) shall not be restrictive.

When dealing with situations of domestic violence, the competent authority shall order any of the following protective measures:

- a) Order the alleged aggressor person to leave the common domicile immediately and, according to the particularities of the violence situation, limit them to an area distant from that of the alleged victim. Within twenty-four hours, the alleged aggressor person must inform the judicial authority of the exact address of their new residence. They shall have the same obligation each time they change residence. If they resist or fail to comply with the order, they shall be compelled by the Public Force, and proceedings shall be documented for the crime of non-compliance with a protective measure.
- b) Authorize a different domicile from the common one for the alleged victim, upon their request, to protect them from future aggressions.
- c) Order the entry into the domicile, which may proceed at any time when, due to domestic violence, the physical, sexual, patrimonial or psychological integrity of any of its inhabitants is seriously at risk. This measure shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.
- d) Prohibit the alleged aggressor person from possessing or carrying sharp or blunt weapons. Likewise, prohibit the introduction or maintenance of weapons in the dwelling when they are used to intimidate, threaten or cause harm to any of the persons cited in subsection a) of Article 2 of this Law. e) Confiscate weapons and objects used to intimidate or assault, as well as any other weapons that are in the possession of the alleged aggressor person or registered in their name, and order the cancellation of weapon carrying permits.

(As amended the preceding subsection by Article 1 of Law No. 9692 of July 9, 2019)

f) If necessary and according to the particularities of the case, the alleged aggressor person may be provisionally suspended from the exercise of custody, upbringing and education of their minor children, as well as the representation and administration of their property and the protection of elderly persons and persons with any disability condition.

g) Order the alleged aggressor person to refrain from interfering, in any way, in the exercise of custody, upbringing and education of their children, as well as in the representation and administration of their property. The same measure may be ordered in the protection and representation of elderly persons and persons with any disability condition. The foregoing, in cases where the competent authority orders the application of subsection f) of this article.

h) Provisionally suspend the right of the alleged aggressor person to visit minor children, in cases where they exercise any type of aggression.

i) Entrust protective custody to whomever the judicial authority considers suitable for that function, if such custody has been entrusted to the alleged aggressor person, when the victim is a minor person, an elderly person who cannot fend for themselves or a person with some degree of disability, in cases where the competent authority orders the application of subsection f) of this article.

j) Prohibit the alleged aggressor person from assaulting, disturbing or intimidating any member of the family group of the alleged victim of domestic violence.

k) Prohibit access by the alleged aggressor person to the domicile, permanent or temporary, of the victim and to their workplace or place of study.

Likewise, approaching said places at a reasonable distance at the discretion of the female or male judge.

l) Set a provisional maintenance obligation in favor of the alleged victim and other dependents as appropriate, in accordance with the Maintenance Pensions Law, No. 7654, even when there is no suitable document proving the degree of kinship. Once set, ex officio, proceedings shall be documented and remitted to the corresponding judicial authority.

m) Order preventive attachment, for a maximum period of three months, counted from the date on which the resolution ordering it is executed, on the family dwelling and on the property of the alleged aggressor person necessary to secure the maintenance obligation in favor of the victim and the corresponding dependents, in accordance with the law; said period may be extended by the female or male judge when the circumstances reasonably warrant it. To apply this measure, no security deposit nor payment of fees or other expenses shall be necessary.

The dwelling and household goods covered by the family patrimony regime must be specially safeguarded.

When the victim is an elderly person or has any disability condition, the alleged aggressor person must not interfere in the use and enjoyment of instruments indispensable for the victim to fend for themselves or integrate into society.

p) Order the alleged aggressor person to make monetary reparation for damages and losses caused to the victim or to property indispensable for them to continue their normal life. This includes transportation expenses, property repairs, lodging and medical expenses. The amount shall be made effective in the same proceeding through the attachment and auction of property necessary to cover the damages caused, at the discretion of the competent judicial authority.

q) Issue a protection and police assistance order directed to the public security authority of their neighborhood. The victim shall carry a copy of this order so that they may go to the nearest authority, in case of threat of aggression outside their domicile.

To apply any of the measures enumerated in this article or others that according to the particularities of the intrafamily violence situation must be adopted, the judicial authority may request the collaboration of the administrative police and the judicial police.

If one or more of these measures are not complied with in contravention of an order issued by the competent authority, the latter must document proceedings to the Public Prosecutor's Office so that prosecution may proceed for the crime of non-compliance with a protective measure.

The protective measures shall be maintained for one year, unless they are lifted or modified earlier by final judicial resolution.

It shall be the obligation of the Judicial Branch to create a registry with the names and information of persons on whom protective measures have been imposed; to this end, the offices handling the matter shall be obligated to send to the registry a copy of the resolutions that order, modify or terminate protective measures.

The registry must be consulted, necessarily, by the judge who must resolve a matter brought to his or her knowledge and that is related to the registered facts.

The information contained in this registry shall be confidential and for the exclusive use of the Judicial Branch. The entries contained in this registry shall be definitively canceled within a period of five years, counted from the last communicated resolution.

When dealing with alleged minor aggressors, the registry may not contain photographs of them; all registered information must be used with respect for the regulations that protect the rights of minors.

(Thus amended by article 1° of law N° 8925 of February 3, 2011)

The aggrieved person or whoever has requested the measures, in accordance with article 7 of this Law, may request the early lifting of the measure. The judicial authority may order this action if it deems it appropriate, after evaluation of the reports referred to in the following article 17. When the victim is a minor, the cessation of the measure, which is not requested by a representative of the National Child Welfare Agency, shall only proceed when recommended by that institution, which shall be obligated to make a pronouncement.

The lifting may also be ordered ex officio or at the request of a party, when it is evident that the law is being used contrary to its purposes. (Thus amended article 1° of law N° 8925 of February 3, 2011)

Where there are no specialized courts in domestic violence or family matters, the courts with jurisdiction to hear and order the protective measures referred to in article 3 of this Law shall be the mixed or misdemeanor courts. Those measures must also be granted by criminal courts, in cases where the aforementioned offices are unable to provide the service. In the latter case, they must immediately forward the file to the corresponding authority. If the facts described constitute a crime, they must forward certified copies to the Public Prosecutor's Office.

(Thus amended by article 1° of law N° 8925 of February 3, 2011)

## Artículo 7.-

Legitimate petitioners. The following shall be entitled to request the protective measures described in the previous chapter:

- a) Persons over twelve years of age affected by a situation of domestic violence.

When dealing with minors under twelve years of age or persons with physical or mental disabilities, the measure must be requested by their legal representative, the National Child Welfare Agency, a police authority or an adult.

- b) Public or private institutions that carry out programs for the protection of human rights and the family, when the aggrieved person so requests, is seriously ill or has a disability that prevents him or her from requesting protection or being aware of the aggression being inflicted.

- c) Adults, when the aggrieved person is unable to request them because he or she is seriously ill as a result of a situation of domestic violence.

The measures may be requested in writing or verbally, independently of any other process, whether criminal or family. The written request shall only require authentication when the person making it does not present it personally. The courts shall be empowered to conduct the proceedings by applying ex officio procedural impulse.

When there is imminent danger to the physical integrity of the persons protected by this law, the judge shall immediately issue the pertinent protective measures, in order to prevent the harm from occurring or continuing to occur.

In these cases, compliance with formalities shall not become an impediment to timely intervention.

The request for protective measures may be submitted on a form to be prepared by the institutions mentioned in paragraph b) of article 7 of this law.

The petitioner for any of the protective measures indicated in article 3 of this law must indicate:

- a) The name, surnames, qualities and neighborhood of the aggrieved person and the aggressor, if known.
- b) The facts on which it is based.
- c) The evidence, if any, on which the facts set forth in the request are based. The lack of indication of evidence shall not prevent the judicial authority from processing the request.
- d) The protective measures requested.
- e) The designation of the house or place to receive notifications.

Once the request is filed, the competent authority shall immediately order the application of any of the requested protective measures. This resolution must be notified in accordance with

## article 177 of the Code of Civil Procedure and no appeal may be filed against it.

However, without prejudice to the provisions of the preceding paragraph, the court may order, ex officio, the application of measures other than those requested.

When deemed necessary, the aggrieved person or the applicant for the measure, in accordance with the provisions of article 7 of this law, may request the competent authority to conduct a medical and psychological examination to assess the physical and psychological damages suffered.

This examination may be performed by professionals from the Department of Legal Medicine of the Judiciary or those from the Costa Rican Social Security Fund and the Ministry of Health.

In the event that the alleged aggressor requests it in writing or verbally, within five days following notification of the initial order, or that the alleged victim has a history as an aggressor, the court shall summon the parties to an oral hearing, in which the corresponding evidence shall be presented.

In any of these cases, the judicial authority shall immediately set the date and time of the hearing. This appointment must be notified to the requesting person personally, except when they have indicated a means for receiving notifications. A period of five days must elapse between this notification and the holding of the hearing.

When the victim cannot appear due to a disability or illness, prior to ruling, the judicial authority shall conduct a judicial inspection, in which act the interview shall be conducted.

In that same case, if the aggrieved person is not in a condition to attend to their own interests, the judicial authority must summon the witnesses and consider their opinion to rule.

In justified cases, the victim may request or the judicial authority may order ex officio that their appearance be made without the alleged aggressor being present, who shall be informed of what occurred once the statement is concluded and shall be given the opportunity to refer to it.

(Thus amended by article 1° of law No. 8925 of February 3, 2011)

Once the evidence is presented, the appearance shall be concluded and the court shall immediately rule whether the applied measures remain in effect or not.

The judicial authority shall rule based on the rules of rational sound criticism and, ex officio, procedural impulse shall govern; for this purpose, it shall order the evidence it deems necessary to establish the truth.

The application and interpretation of this law shall be governed by the fundamental principles of family legislation and the provisions contained in

## article 10 of the Civil Code.

The judicial authority must review the results of the execution of the measures, either through the appearance of the parties before the corresponding office, with the frequency ordered, or with the intervention of the Department of Social Work and Psychology of the Judiciary or any other state agency required for this purpose, which shall render periodic reports on the effectiveness of the measures.

The administrative police has the obligation to monitor the effective compliance with the protection measures, by all necessary means.

It is the responsibility of the public bodies that are part of the national system for the attention and prevention of domestic violence, in accordance with their competencies, to provide comprehensive support to victims of violence that allows them to improve their situation, as well as recovery and the construction of a new life project.

The

National Institute for Women (Inamu) shall provide advice to fulfill this purpose and, in addition, shall offer victims the necessary support services, legal advice and legal representation to carry out the procedures contemplated in this Law. For this last purpose, Inamu may intervene in the procedure, in order to guarantee the rights of victims and represent them legally with the same powers and attributions granted to the Public Defender in criminal matters

If the facts that motivated the protection measures constitute a crime, the judicial authority shall take the provisions it deems appropriate and shall issue testimony to the respective prosecutor's office.

The

Civil Procedural Code shall apply supplementarily in matters not addressed, where compatible and not contrary to the provisions of this law. Police authorities have the duty to intervene in situations of domestic violence, ex officio or when requested by victims or third parties. In these cases, they must:

- a) Assist the aggrieved persons even when they are inside their home.
- b) Detain the alleged aggressors and place them at the disposal of the competent authority.

In any case, the detained person must be placed at the disposal of the competent authority, within twenty-four hours, as established by

## article 37 of the Political Constitution.

c) Prepare a report on the events that occurred; for this purpose they must collect information from family members, neighbors or other persons present and record their names, characteristics and place where they can be located to summon them in a possible judicial process. d) Seize weapons and objects used to intimidate or assault, as well as any other weapons in the possession of the aggressor or registered in their name, and place them at the disposal of the respective judicial authority.

For these purposes and with the aim of protecting human life and personal integrity, in accordance with articles 21 and 23 of the Political Constitution, the police authority responding to the first call is authorized to inspect the property where the assault is taking place, in order to locate weapons to which the aggressor may have access, even if the aggressor is not present at that location.

## Article 20 bis- Cancellation of firearms carrying

permits. In cases of domestic violence situations, all firearms possessed by the aggressor or registered in their name shall be confiscated and remitted to the General Directorate of Armament of the Ministry of Public Security, for proper custody.

Once the resolution confirming the protective measures in the domestic violence proceeding has been issued, in accordance with articles 13 to 16 of this law and provided that the judicial authority determines that violent conduct occurred or that there is a risk to the victim or their family, it shall notify the Department of Arms and Explosives Control to initiate the administrative procedure for the cancellation of the firearms registration.

## Article 20

ter- Destruction of firearms. The

Department of Arms and Explosives Control, once it has received notification of the final resolution referred to in the previous article, shall immediately initiate the administrative procedure to cancel the registration of the firearms confiscated from the aggressor. Interested parties shall have the right to file ordinary remedies of revocation and appeal within three business days.

Once the cancellation resolution is final, the Department shall notify the Directorate to proceed with the destruction of the corresponding firearms.

8.- It shall ensure the research and compilation of statistics and relevant information on the causes, consequences and frequency of domestic violence, in order to evaluate state measures.

9.- It shall promote international cooperation to exchange ideas and experiences and implement programs aimed at protecting the right to a life without violence.
