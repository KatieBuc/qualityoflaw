LAW No. 1674

LAW OF DECEMBER 15, 1995

LAW OF DECEMBER 15, 1995

GONZALO SANCHEZ DE LOZADA

CONSTITUTIONAL PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC

Whereas, the Honorable National Congress has enacted the following Law:

THE H. NATIONAL CONGRESS,

DECREES:

LAW AGAINST

VIOLENCE IN THE FAMILY OR DOMESTIC

## CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1. (SCOPE).

This law establishes the State's policy against violence in the family or domestic violence, the acts that constitute violence in the family, the sanctions that correspond to the perpetrator and the measures of prevention and immediate protection to the victim.

### Article 2. (PROTECTED INTERESTS).

The legally protected interests by this law are the physical, psychological, moral and sexual integrity of each of the members of the family unit.

### Article 3. (PREVENTION).

The eradication of violence in the family constitutes a national strategy.

The State, through its specialized institutions and in coordination with civil associations and private institutions related to the matter:

- a) Will promote the incorporation in the curricular and extra-curricular teaching-learning processes, of orientations and values of respect, solidarity and self-esteem of children, youth and adults of both sexes; fostering access, use and enjoyment of citizen rights without discrimination of sex, age, culture and religion.
- b) Will promote a process of modification of socio-cultural patterns of conduct of men and women, including the design of formal and non-formal education programs appropriate to all levels of the educational process, to counteract prejudices, customs and all other types of practices based on the supposed inferiority or superiority of any of the genders or on stereotypical roles for men and women that legitimize or exacerbate violence.
- c) Will disseminate the rights and protection of women within the family as well as access to health, avoiding discrimination or acts of violence that harm or alter their health.
- d) Will sensitize the community through mass campaigns about the care that should be provided to pregnant women, avoiding all types of violence that may affect her or affect the unborn child.
- e) Will instruct health service personnel to provide good treatment and comprehensive care to victims of family violence, considering their intimacy and privacy, and avoiding the repetition of clinical examinations that affect their psychological integrity.
- f) Will coordinate joint actions of health services with comprehensive legal services to provide adequate care to victims of family violence.
- g) Will train and create awareness in justice administration personnel, police and other officials responsible for the application of this law, on measures of prevention, sanction and elimination of violence in the family.
- h) Will conduct awareness campaigns through interactive group and mass media towards the community as a whole, to strengthen the rejection of violence in the family.
- i) Will conduct communication campaigns sectorized by regions, ages and socio-economic situation, through traditional and alternative media to disseminate women's rights and the conviction that family violence is an attack on human rights.
- j) Will incorporate in the language and discourse of mass media the permanent dissemination of rejection of family violence and the full exercise of rights, through special programs, participation in interviews and regular news currents.
- k) Will disseminate the United Nations Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women and the Inter-American Convention of the Organization of American States for the Prevention, Punishment and Eradication of Violence against Women.
- l) Will disseminate the text of this law to specialized audiences, political decision-making levels, union and party leaders, and opinion leaders.
- m) Will promote the study and investigation of the causes and consequences of violence in the family and will adopt measures to promote its eradication.
- n) The National Police will deploy mobile control patrols to centers with the highest incidence of domestic violence.
- o) Will promote the establishment of temporary shelters for victims of violence and the creation of institutions for the treatment of aggressors.
- p) Will promote and support the dissemination of the Law against Violence in the Family or Domestic through the National Education System.
- q) Will insert as a curricular training subject in Military Institutes and National Police Academy the Law against Violence in the Family or Domestic.
- r) Will encourage the formation of psychological clinics for the diagnosis and therapy of victims of violence.

## CHAPTER II

VIOLENCE IN THE FAMILY OR DOMESTIC

### Article 4. (VIOLENCE IN THE FAMILY). Violence in the family or domestic is understood as physical, psychological or sexual aggression, committed by:

1) The spouse or cohabitant;

2) Ascendants, descendants, siblings, civil or affinal relatives in direct and collateral line;

3) Guardians, curators or custodians.

### Article 5. (DOMESTIC VIOLENCE).

Acts of domestic violence are considered to be aggressions committed between ex-spouses, ex-cohabitants or persons who have procreated children in common legally recognized or not, even if they have not cohabited.

### Article 6. (FORMS OF VIOLENCE). It is considered:

a) Physical violence, conducts that cause internal or external injury or any other mistreatment that affects the physical integrity of persons;

b) psychological violence, conducts that emotionally disturb the victim, harming their psychic and emotional development; and

c) sexual violence, conducts, threats or intimidation that affect the sexual integrity or sexual self-determination of the victim.

d) Likewise, acts of violence in the family are considered when parents, guardians or custodians endanger the physical or psychological integrity of minors, by abuse of corrective or disciplinary means or by imposition of excessive and inadequate work for the age or physical condition of the minor.

Likewise, acts performed against incapacitated adults are considered acts of violence in the family.

## CHAPTER III

SANCTIONS AND ALTERNATIVE MEASURES

### Article 7. (SANCTIONS).

Acts of violence in the family or domestic, covered by this law, and that do not constitute crimes typified in the Criminal Code, shall be sanctioned with penalties of fine or arrest.

### Article 8. (FINE).

The fine penalty in favor of the State shall be set by the judge up to a maximum of 20% of the national minimum wage and up to ten times that amount, according to the gravity of the acts and the economic capacity of the perpetrator.

The fine shall be paid within three days.

Non-compliance will result in the conversion of the fine into arrest, which may not exceed the maximum time set by the following article.

### Article 9. (ARREST).

The penalty of arrest consists of deprivation of liberty for a period that shall be set by the judge and that may not exceed four days, with its fulfillment possibly being deferred to weekends.

Arrest shall be served in police precincts.

### Artículo 10º. (AGRAVANTES). Las sanciones serán agravadas hasta el doble de los máximos previstos, en los siguientes casos:

### Article 10º. (AGGRAVATING CIRCUMSTANCES). Sanctions shall be aggravated up to double the maximum provided, in the following cases:

1) Cuando la víctima sea discapacitada, mayor de sesenta años o esté embarazada.

1) When the victim is disabled, over sixty years of age or is pregnant.

2) Cuando se hubieran cometido varias acciones constitutivas de violencia en la familia.

2) When several actions constituting family violence have been committed.

3) Cuando cumplida la sanción, el responsable cometa otro acto o actos constitutivos de violencia en la familia.

3) When after serving the sanction, the responsible party commits another act or acts constituting family violence.

### Artículo 11º. (MEDIDAS ALTERNATIVAS A LA EJECUCIÓN DE LA SANCIÓN).

### Article 11º. (ALTERNATIVE MEASURES TO THE EXECUTION OF THE SANCTION).

El juez podrá suspender la ejecución de la sanción, disponiendo de acuerdo a la naturaleza del hecho y la personalidad del autor, como medida alternativa terapia psicológica o prestación de trabajos comunitarios.

The judge may suspend the execution of the sanction, ordering according to the nature of the act and the personality of the perpetrator, as an alternative measure psychological therapy or performance of community work.

Estas medidas sólo podrán hacerse efectivas si mediare el consentimiento del responsable. De no prestar su consentimiento, se ejecutará la sanción impuesta.

These measures may only be made effective if there is consent from the responsible party. If consent is not given, the imposed sanction shall be executed.

Acreditado el cumplimiento de la medida, el juez declarará extinguida la sanción impuesta. En caso contrario, se ejecutará la sanción cuyo cumplimiento quedó en suspenso.

Upon proof of compliance with the measure, the judge shall declare the imposed sanction extinguished. Otherwise, the sanction whose compliance was suspended shall be executed.

### Artículo 12º. (TERAPIA PSICOLÓGICA).

### Article 12º. (PSYCHOLOGICAL THERAPY).

La terapia psicológica se llevará a cabo en consultorios privados de profesionales habilitados, con cargo al autor. Las personas de escasos recursos serán derivadas a la Secretaría de Asuntos Étnicos, de Género y Generacionales, ONAMFA o cualquier servicio social acreditado y sin fines de lucro.

Psychological therapy shall be carried out in private offices of qualified professionals, at the perpetrator's expense. Persons with limited resources shall be referred to the Secretariat of Ethnic, Gender and Generational Affairs, ONAMFA or any accredited non-profit social service.

El especialista determinará el tiempo de duración y la modalidad de la terapia psicológica e informará al juez acerca de estas circunstancias.

The specialist shall determine the duration and modality of the psychological therapy and shall inform the judge about these circumstances.

### Artículo 13º. (TRABAJOS COMUNITARIOS).

### Article 13º. (COMMUNITY WORK).

El trabajo comunitario consistirá en la prestación de trabajos en favor de la comunidad o del Estado, que se realizará fuera de los horarios habituales de trabajo y de acuerdo a la profesión, oficio u ocupación del autor.

Community work shall consist of the performance of work for the benefit of the community or the State, which shall be carried out outside regular working hours and according to the profession, trade or occupation of the perpetrator.

La duración del trabajo no podrá exceder del tiempo equivalente a cuatro días.

The duration of the work may not exceed the equivalent time of four days.

El trabajo deberá ser supervisado por la persona o autoridad designada por el juez, quien informará sobre su cumplimiento.

The work must be supervised by the person or authority designated by the judge, who shall report on its compliance.

## CAPITULO IV

## CHAPTER IV

COMPETENCIA

JURISDICTION

### Artículo 14º. (COMPETENCIA).

### Article 14º. (JURISDICTION).

El conocimiento de los hechos de violencia familiar o doméstica, comprendidos en la presente ley, será de competencia de los jueces de instrucción de familia.

Jurisdiction over acts of family or domestic violence, covered by this law, shall belong to family investigating judges.

En los lugares donde no hayan jueces de instrucción de familia serán competentes los jueces de instrucción.

In places where there are no family investigating judges, investigating judges shall have jurisdiction.

### Artículo 15º. (ACTOS DELICTIVOS).

### Article 15º. (CRIMINAL ACTS).

Los hechos de violencia que constituyan delitos tipificados en el Código Penal son de competencia exclusiva de los jueces penales.

Acts of violence that constitute crimes defined in the Penal Code are under the exclusive jurisdiction of criminal judges.

### Artículo 16º. (AUTORIDADES COMUNITARIAS).

### Article 16º. (COMMUNITY AUTHORITIES).

En las comunidades indígenas y campesinas, serán las autoridades comunitarias y naturales quienes resuelvan las controversias de violencia en la familia, de conformidad a sus costumbres y usos, siempre que no se opongan a la Constitución Política del Estado y el espíritu de la presente ley.

In indigenous and peasant communities, it shall be the community and natural authorities who resolve family violence disputes, in accordance with their customs and uses, provided they do not conflict with the Political Constitution of the State and the spirit of this law.

## CAPITULO V

## CHAPTER V

MEDIDAS CAUTELARES Y PROVISIONALES

PRECAUTIONARY AND PROVISIONAL MEASURES

### Artículo 17º. (MEDIDAS CAUTELARES).

### Article 17º. (PRECAUTIONARY MEASURES).

El juez de oficio, a petición de parte o del Ministerio Público, podrá disponer las medidas cautelares que correspondan, destinadas a garantizar la seguridad e integridad física o psicológica de la víctima. También podrá ordenar la ayuda de la fuerza pública para su cumplimiento.

The judge ex officio, at the request of a party or the Public Prosecutor's Office, may order the appropriate precautionary measures, aimed at guaranteeing the safety and physical or psychological integrity of the victim. He may also order the assistance of the police force for its enforcement.

En cualquier momento del procedimiento el juez, de oficio o a petición de parte, por resolución, podrá ampliar, modificar, sustituir o dejar sin efecto las medidas cautelares.

At any time during the proceedings the judge, ex officio or at the request of a party, by resolution, may expand, modify, substitute or revoke the precautionary measures.

### Artículo 18º. (CLASES). Son medidas cautelares:

### Article 18º. (TYPES). The following are precautionary measures:

1) Prohibir o restringir temporalmente la presencia del denunciado en el hogar conyugal.

1) To prohibit or temporarily restrict the presence of the accused in the conjugal home.

2) Ordenar la restitución de la víctima al hogar del que hubiera sido alejada con violencia.

2) To order the restitution of the victim to the home from which she had been removed with violence.

3) Autorizar a la víctima el alejamiento del hogar común y disponer la entrega inmediata de sus efectos personales.

3) To authorize the victim to leave the common home and order the immediate delivery of her personal effects.

4) Disponer la inventariación de los bienes muebles e inmuebles de propiedad de la comunidad ganancial.

4) To order the inventory of movable and immovable property owned by the community property.

5) Prohibir o limitar la concurrencia del denunciado al lugar de trabajo de la víctima.

5) To prohibit or limit the attendance of the accused at the victim's workplace.

### Artículo 19º. (TEMPORALIDAD DE LAS MEDIDAS).

### Article 19º. (TEMPORALITY OF THE MEASURES).

Las medidas cautelares enumeradas en el artículo anterior son de carácter esencialmente temporal y no podrán exceder del tiempo de duración del proceso.

The precautionary measures enumerated in the preceding article are essentially temporary in nature and may not exceed the duration of the proceedings.

### Artículo 20º. (MEDIDAS PROVISIONALES).

### Article 20º. (PROVISIONAL MEASURES).

El juez que conozca la causa podrá dictar las medidas provisionales de asistencia familiar y tenencia de hijos, que correspondan. Estas medidas tendrán vigencia sólo hasta la conclusión del proceso.

The judge hearing the case may issue the appropriate provisional measures for family support and custody of children. These measures shall be in effect only until the conclusion of the proceedings.

## CAPITULO VI

## CHAPTER VI

PROCEDIMIENTO

PROCEDURE

### Artículo 21º. (DENUNCIA).

### Article 21º. (COMPLAINT).

La denuncia podrá ser presentada en forma oral o escrita, con la asistencia de abogado patrocinante o sin ella, ante el juez competente, el Ministerio Público o la Policía Nacional.

The complaint may be filed orally or in writing, with or without the assistance of a sponsoring attorney, before the competent judge, the Public Prosecutor's Office or the National Police.

### Artículo 22º. (LEGITIMACIÓN PARA DENUNCIAR).

### Article 22º. (STANDING TO FILE A COMPLAINT).

Están legitimados para solicitar protección a favor de la víctima, denunciando hechos de violencia física o psicológica, sus parientes consanguíneos, afines o civiles, o cualquier persona que conozca estos hechos.

Blood relatives, relatives by affinity or civil relatives, or any person who knows of these facts, have standing to request protection on behalf of the victim by reporting acts of physical or psychological violence.

Los hechos de violencia sexual solamente podrán ser denunciados por la víctima, salvo que fuere menor de dieciocho años o mayor incapaz, en cuyo caso están legitimados para denunciar los sujetos señalados en el párrafo anterior.

Acts of sexual violence may only be reported by the victim, unless the victim is a minor under eighteen years of age or an incapacitated adult, in which case the subjects mentioned in the preceding paragraph have standing to report.

### Artículo 23º. (LEGITIMACIÓN PARA INTERVENIR EN EL PROCESO).

### Article 23º. (STANDING TO INTERVENE IN THE PROCEEDINGS).

En los casos de violencia física o cuando la víctima de violencia sexual o psicológica sea un menor de dieciocho años o mayor incapaz, están legitimados para ejercer la acción la víctima y el Ministerio Público.

In cases of physical violence or when the victim of sexual or psychological violence is a minor under eighteen years of age or an incapacitated adult, the victim and the Public Prosecutor's Office have standing to bring the action.

En los demás casos de violencia sexual sólo la víctima está legitimada para ejercer la acción.

In all other cases of sexual violence only the victim has standing to bring the action.

### Artículo 24º. (OBLIGATORIEDAD DE DENUNCIAR).

### Article 24º. (OBLIGATION TO REPORT).

Los trabajadores en salud de establecimientos públicos o privados que reciban o presten atención a las víctimas de violencia, están obligados a denunciar estos hechos para su respectivo procesamiento.

Health workers in public or private establishments who receive or provide care to victims of violence are obligated to report these acts for their respective prosecution.

### Artículo 25º. (DENUNCIA ANTE LA POLICÍA).

### Article 25º. (COMPLAINT BEFORE THE POLICE).

Cuando la denuncia sea presentada ante la Policía, ésta remitirá los antecedentes a conocimiento del juez competente, dentro de las 24 horas de recibida la denuncia, sin costo alguno.

When the complaint is filed with the Police, they shall remit the records to the competent judge within 24 hours of receiving the complaint, at no cost.

### Artículo 26º. (BRIGADAS DE PROTECCIÓN A LA FAMILIA).

### Article 26º. (FAMILY PROTECTION BRIGADES).

Las Brigadas de Protección a la Familia se encargarán de practicar las diligencias orientadas a la individualización de los autores y partícipes, reunir o asegurar los elementos de prueba y prestar el auxilio necesario e inmediato a la víctima.

The Family Protection Brigades shall be responsible for carrying out the procedures aimed at identifying the perpetrators and participants, gathering or securing the elements of proof and providing the necessary and immediate assistance to the victim.

Donde no existan Brigadas de Protección a la Familia, cumplirán estas funciones las autoridades policiales existentes.

Where Family Protection Brigades do not exist, the existing police authorities shall perform these functions.

### Artículo 27º. (FLAGRANCIA).

### Article 27º. (FLAGRANTE DELICTO).

En caso de flagrancia el autor podrá ser aprehendido aún sin mandamiento por cualquier persona, con el único objeto de ser conducido inmediatamente ante la autoridad competente.

In cases of flagrante delicto the perpetrator may be apprehended even without a warrant by any person, for the sole purpose of being brought immediately before the competent authority.

### Artículo 28º. (DENUNCIA ANTE EL MINISTERIO PÚBLICO).

### Article 28º. (COMPLAINT BEFORE THE PUBLIC PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE).

Cuando la denuncia sea presentada ante el Ministerio Público, el fiscal de familia o agente fiscal convocará inmediatamente al denunciado y la víctima a una audiencia de conciliación, que se realizará dentro de las 24 horas de recibida la denuncia.

When the complaint is filed with the Public Prosecutor's Office, the family prosecutor or prosecuting agent shall immediately summon the accused and the victim to a conciliation hearing, which shall take place within 24 hours of receiving the complaint.

En caso que las partes citadas no se presenten o no se produzca la conciliación, el fiscal remitirá la causa al juez competente.

In the event that the parties summoned do not appear or conciliation does not occur, the prosecutor shall refer the case to the competent judge.

A tiempo de remitir la causa, el fiscal podrá solicitar al juez las medidas cautelares que correspondan.

At the time of referring the case, the prosecutor may request the appropriate precautionary measures from the judge.

### Artículo 29º. (ADMISIÓN DE LA DENUNCIA).

### Article 29º. (ADMISSION OF THE COMPLAINT).

Recibida la denuncia, el juez al admitirla, señalará día y hora para la audiencia que tendrá lugar dentro de un plazo no mayor de 48 horas, resolverá sobre la procedencia de las medidas cautelares y dispondrá la citación del denunciado y de quien esté legitimado para ejercer la acción.

Upon receiving the complaint, the judge upon admitting it, shall set the day and time for the hearing which shall take place within a period not exceeding 48 hours, shall rule on the appropriateness of the precautionary measures and shall order the summons of the accused and of whoever has standing to bring the action.

### Artículo 30º. (CITACIÓN).

### Article 30º. (SUMMONS).

La citación al denunciado podrá efectuarse, cualquier día u hora y en el lugar donde pueda ser habido.

The summons to the accused may be made any day or time and at the place where he may be found.

La citación contendrá el motivo de la denuncia y las medidas cautelares que haya dispuesto el juez para su cumplimiento inmediato.

The summons shall contain the reason for the complaint and the precautionary measures that the judge has ordered for their immediate enforcement.

### Artículo 31º. (INCOMPARECENCIA DEL DENUNCIADO).

### Article 31º. (FAILURE TO APPEAR BY THE ACCUSED).

Cuando sin causa justificada no comparezca el denunciado, habiendo sido citado legalmente, el juez dispondrá su comparecencia con la ayuda de la fuerza pública.

When the accused does not appear without justified cause, having been legally summoned, the judge shall order his appearance with the assistance of the police force.

### Artículo 32º. (DESISTIMIENTO).

If the person entitled to bring the action does not appear, the action shall be deemed withdrawn, unless legal impediment is proven, in which case a new date and time for the hearing shall be set within the same period established in Article 29 of this law.

### Artículo 33º. (AUDIENCIA).

On the day of the hearing, the judge shall order the reading of the complaint, hear the parties, receive the evidence they offer, and propose the basis for a possible conciliation.

The accused may be assisted by a defense attorney.

If one of the parties is assisted at the hearing by a sponsoring attorney, for equity, the judge shall appoint a defender for the other.

### Artículo 34º. (PRUEBA).

All elements of evidence, legally obtained, that may lead to knowledge of the reported facts, shall be admitted as means of evidence.

Evidence shall be assessed by the judge, setting forth the reasoning on which his legal evaluation is based.

### Artículo 35º. (TESTIGOS).

Relatives or dependents of the complainant or the accused may also be witnesses, provided that their statement is voluntary.

### Artículo 36º. (RESOLUCIÓN).

The judge shall issue a resolution at the same hearing, expressing the grounds on which it is based.

The resolution, as appropriate, may:

- 1) Approve the agreements reached by the parties in the conciliation;
- 2) Declare the complaint proven when the guilt of the accused has been demonstrated.
- 3) Declare the complaint unproven.

In the event of declaring the complaint proven, the judge shall impose the corresponding sanction and order the payment of all expenses caused to the victim as a consequence of the act and the processing of the proceedings. In the same resolution, the judge may order that the sanction be suspended, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 11, 12 and 13 of this law.

### Artículo 37º. (CERTIFICADOS MÉDICOS).

Any medical certificate issued by a professional working in public health institutions shall be admitted as documentary evidence.

### Artículo 38º. (MEDIDAS PARA MEJOR PROVEER).

According to the circumstances of the case, the judge may order a psychological examination of the accused and of the family members involved in the acts of violence.

The expert report must be submitted to the judge within a period not exceeding seven working days. After this period, with or without the expert report, the judge shall issue a resolution.

### Artículo 39º. (APELACIÓN).

The parties may file an appeal verbally at the same hearing or in writing within 24 hours, before the same judge who issued the resolution.

Once the appeal has been filed, the judge shall summon the other party to respond to the appeal within the same period. Then, without further proceedings, within the following 24 hours, the proceedings must be sent to the second instance judge, under the responsibility of the clerk.

The appeal shall be granted with suspensive effect before the family district judge on duty or before the district judge in the provinces.

### Artículo 40º. (RESOLUCIÓN DE LA APELACIÓN).

Upon receipt of the proceedings, the second instance judge shall issue a resolution within the following three days, without further appeal.

### Artículo 41º. (RESERVA DEL TRAMITE).

Proceedings for acts of violence in the family or domestic sphere are absolutely confidential. The file may only be exhibited or testimonies or certificates of the documents inserted therein may be granted at the request of a legitimate party and with a judicial order.

## CAPITULO VII

DISPOSICIONES FINALES

### Artículo 42º. (INCIDENTE).

If during the processing of divorce, separation or unilateral dissolution of a free union proceedings, acts of family or domestic violence occur, the judge of the case shall hear and resolve these complaints incidentally in accordance with the procedure established in this law.

### Artículo 43º. (DELITOS DE ORDEN PUBLICO A INSTANCIA DE PARTE).

Article 7 of the Code of Criminal Procedure is modified, excluding from it the crimes of statutory rape, rape of persons over the age of puberty, indecent assault, outrage against decency and corruption of adults; which shall be considered public action crimes at the request of a party.

In the cases of this article, no case shall be brought except by accusation or complaint of the victim, his guardian or his legal representatives. However, the request of a party shall not be required when the crime is committed against a minor who has no parents, guardian or legal representatives, or when it is committed by one of the parents, guardian, legal representative or person in charge of his custody.

In all cases the name of the victim shall be kept strictly confidential.

Once the action is initiated at the request of a party, the Public Prosecutor's Office shall continue the proceedings ex officio.

### Artículo 44º. (DEROGATORIA). 

Article 276 of the Criminal Code is repealed.

### Artículo 45º. (NORMAS SUPLETORIAS).

The provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure are applicable, insofar as they are not contrary to the provisions of this law. Refer to the Executive Branch for constitutional purposes.
