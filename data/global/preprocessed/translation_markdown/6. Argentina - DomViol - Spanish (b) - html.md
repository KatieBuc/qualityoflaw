# COMPREHENSIVE WOMEN'S PROTECTION LAW

COMPREHENSIVE WOMEN'S PROTECTION LAW

Law 26.485

Comprehensive protection law to prevent, punish and eradicate violence against women in the areas where they develop their interpersonal relationships

Enacted: March 11, 2009.

De Facto Promulgated: April 1, 2009.

The Senate and Chamber of Deputies of the Argentine Nation assembled in Congress, etc. enact with force of Law:

COMPREHENSIVE PROTECTION LAW TO PREVENT, PUNISH AND ERADICATE VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN IN THE AREAS WHERE THEY DEVELOP THEIR INTERPERSONAL RELATIONSHIPS

## TITLE I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### ARTICLE 1º

Scope of application. Public Order. The provisions of this law are of public order and applicable throughout the territory of the Republic, with the exception of the procedural provisions established in Chapter II of Title III hereof.

#### ARTICLE 2º

Object. The purpose of this law is to promote and guarantee:

- a) The elimination of discrimination between women and men in all orders of life;
- b) The right of women to live a life without violence;
- c) The appropriate conditions to raise awareness and prevent, punish and eradicate discrimination and violence against women in any of its manifestations and areas;
- d) The development of inter-institutional public policies on violence against women;
- e) The removal of sociocultural patterns that promote and sustain gender inequality and power relations over women;
- f) Access to justice for women who suffer violence;
- g) Comprehensive assistance to women who suffer violence in state and private areas that carry out programmatic activities aimed at women and/or in specialized violence services.
- h) The digital rights and assets of women, as well as their development and permanence in the digital space. (Subsection incorporated by art. 1° of Law No. 27736 B.O. 23/10/2023)

#### ARTICLE 3º

Protected Rights. This law guarantees all the rights recognized by the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women, the Convention on the Rights of the Child and Law 26.061 on Comprehensive Protection of the Rights of Girls, Boys and Adolescents and, in particular, those referring to:

- a) A life without violence and without discrimination;
- b) Health, education and personal security;
- c) Physical, psychological, sexual, economic or patrimonial integrity;
- d) That their dignity, reputation and identity be respected, including in digital spaces. (Subsection substituted by art. 2° of Law No. 27736 B.O. 23/10/2023)
- e) To decide about reproductive life, number of pregnancies and when to have them, in accordance with Law 25.673 for the Creation of the National Program of Sexual Health and Responsible Procreation;
- f) Privacy, freedom of beliefs and thought;
- g) To receive appropriate information and advice;
- h) To enjoy comprehensive measures of assistance, protection and security;
- i) To enjoy free access to justice in cases covered by the scope of application of this law;
- j) Real equality of rights, opportunities and treatment between men and women;
- k) Respectful treatment of women who suffer violence, avoiding any conduct, act or omission that produces revictimization.

#### ARTICLE 4º

Definition. Violence against women is understood as any conduct, by action or omission, based on gender reasons, which, directly or indirectly, both in the public and private spheres, in the analog or digital space, based on an unequal power relationship, affects their life, freedom, dignity, physical, psychological, sexual, economic or patrimonial integrity, political participation, as well as their personal security. This includes those perpetrated by the State or its agents.

For the purposes of this law, indirect violence is considered any conduct, action, omission, disposition, criterion or discriminatory practice that places women at a disadvantage with respect to men.

(Article substituted by art. 3° of Law No. 27736 B.O. 23/10/2023)

#### ARTICLE 5º

Types. Especially included in the definition of the preceding article are the following types of violence against women:

1.- Physical: That which is used against the body of the woman producing pain, harm or risk of producing it and any other form of mistreatment or aggression that affects her physical integrity.

2.- Psychological: That which causes emotional harm and diminished self-esteem or damages and disturbs full personal development or that seeks to degrade or control their actions, behaviors, beliefs and decisions, through threat, harassment, hostility, restriction, humiliation, dishonor, discredit, manipulation, isolation. It also includes blame, constant surveillance, demand for obedience and submission, verbal coercion, persecution, insult, indifference, abandonment, excessive jealousy, blackmail, ridicule, exploitation and limitation of the right to movement or any other means that causes harm to their psychological health and self-determination.

3.- Sexual: Any action that implies the violation in all its forms, with or without genital access, of the woman's right to voluntarily decide about her sexual or reproductive life through threats, coercion, use of force or intimidation, including rape within marriage or other bonding or kinship relationships, whether or not there is cohabitation, as well as forced prostitution, exploitation, slavery, harassment, sexual abuse and trafficking of women.

4.- Economic and patrimonial: That which is directed to cause impairment of the economic or patrimonial resources of the woman, through:

- a) The disturbance of the possession, tenure or ownership of her property;
- b) The loss, theft, destruction, retention or improper diversion of objects, work instruments, personal documents, goods, securities and patrimonial rights;
- c) The limitation of economic resources intended to satisfy their needs or deprivation of the indispensable means to live a dignified life;
- d) The limitation or control of their income, as well as receiving a lower salary for equal work, within the same workplace.

5.- Symbolic: That which through stereotyped patterns, messages, values, icons or signs transmits and reproduces domination, inequality and discrimination in social relations, naturalizing the subordination of women in society.

6.- Political: That which is directed to undermine, annul, prevent, obstruct or restrict the political participation of women, violating the right to a political life free from violence and/or the right to participate in public and political affairs on equal terms with men. (Subsection incorporated by art. 3° of Law No. 27.533 B.O. 20/12/2019)

#### ARTICLE 6

Modalities. For the purposes of this law, modalities are understood as the forms in which the different types of violence against women manifest in different spheres, with the following being especially included:

- a) Domestic violence against women: that exercised against women by a member of the family group, regardless of the physical space where it occurs, that harms dignity, well-being, physical, psychological, sexual, economic or patrimonial integrity, freedom, including reproductive freedom and the right to full development of women. Family group is understood as that originating in kinship whether by consanguinity or affinity, marriage, de facto unions and couples or dating relationships. It includes current or terminated relationships, cohabitation not being a requirement;
- b) Institutional violence against women: that carried out by officials, professionals, personnel and agents belonging to any organ, entity or public institution, that has the purpose of delaying, obstructing or preventing women from having access to public policies and exercising the rights provided in this law. Also included are those exercised in political parties, unions, business organizations, sports organizations and civil society organizations;
- c) Labor violence against women: that which discriminates against women in public or private workplaces and that obstructs their access to employment, hiring, promotion, stability or permanence therein, requiring requirements regarding marital status, maternity, age, physical appearance or taking pregnancy tests. It also constitutes violence against women in the labor sphere to violate the right to equal remuneration for equal task or function. Likewise, it includes psychological harassment in a systematic manner against a particular worker with the purpose of achieving her labor exclusion;
- d) Violence against reproductive freedom: that which violates the right of women to decide freely and responsibly the number of pregnancies or the interval between births, in accordance with Law 25.673 Creating the National Program for Sexual Health and Responsible Procreation;
- e) Obstetric violence: that which health personnel exercise over the body and reproductive processes of women, expressed in dehumanized treatment, abuse of medicalization and pathologization of natural processes, in accordance with Law 25.929.
- f) Media violence against women: that publication or dissemination of stereotyped messages and images through any mass media, which directly or indirectly promotes the exploitation of women or their images, insults, defames, discriminates, dishonors, humiliates or attacks the dignity of women, as well as the use of women, adolescents and girls in pornographic messages and images, legitimizing unequal treatment or constructing sociocultural patterns that reproduce inequality or generate violence against women.
- g) Violence against women in public space: that exercised against women by one or more persons, in public places or places of public access, such as means of transportation or shopping centers, through verbal or non-verbal conduct or expressions, with sexual connotation, that affect or harm their dignity, integrity, freedom, free movement or permanence and/or generate a hostile or offensive environment. (Subsection incorporated by art. 1 of Law No. 27.501 O.G. 8/5/2019)
- h) Public-political violence against women: that which, based on gender reasons, through intimidation, harassment, dishonor, discredit, persecution, stalking and/or threats, prevents or limits the development of political life itself or access to political rights and duties, attacking the current regulations on political representation of women, and/or discouraging or diminishing the political exercise or political activity of women, and may occur in any space of public and political life, such as state institutions, voting precincts, political parties, social organizations, union associations, media, among others. (Subsection incorporated by art. 4 of Law No. 27.533 O.G. 20/12/2019)
- i) Digital or telematic violence: any conduct, action or omission against women based on their gender that is committed, instigated or aggravated, in part or in its entirety, with the assistance, use and/or appropriation of information and communication technologies, with the object of causing physical, psychological, economic, sexual or moral harm both in the private and public sphere to them or their family group.

Especially conduct that attacks their integrity, dignity, identity, reputation, freedom, and against access, permanence and activity in the digital space or that implies the obtaining, reproduction and dissemination, without consent, of real or edited digital material, intimate or of nudity, that is attributed to women, or the reproduction in the digital space of misogynistic hate speech and stereotyped sexist patterns or situations of harassment, threat, extortion, control or espionage of virtual activity, unauthorized access to electronic devices or online accounts, theft and non-consensual dissemination of personal data to the extent that they are not conduct permitted by law 25.326 and/or the one that replaces it in the future, or actions that attack the sexual integrity of women through information and communication technologies, or any cyberattack that may arise in the future and that affects the rights protected in this law.

(Subsection i) incorporated by art. 4 of Law No. 27736 O.G. 23/10/2023)

## TITLE II

PUBLIC POLICIES

### CHAPTER I

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

#### ARTICLE 7

Guiding principles. The three branches of the State, whether at the national or provincial level, shall adopt the necessary measures and shall ratify in each of their actions the unrestricted respect for the constitutional right to equality between women and men. For the fulfillment of the purposes of this law they must guarantee the following guiding principles:

- a) The elimination of discrimination and unequal power relations over women;
- b) The adoption of measures aimed at raising awareness in society, promoting values of equality and delegitimization of violence against women;
- c) Comprehensive and timely assistance to women who suffer any type of violence, ensuring their free, rapid, transparent and effective access to services created for that purpose, as well as promoting the punishment and reeducation of those who exercise violence;
- d) The adoption of the principle of mainstreaming shall be present in all measures as well as in the execution of normative provisions, articulating interinstitutionally and coordinating budgetary resources;
- e) The encouragement of cooperation and participation of civil society, engaging private entities and non-state public actors;
- f) Respect for the right to confidentiality and privacy, prohibiting the reproduction for private use or public dissemination of information related to situations of violence against women, without authorization from the person who suffers it;
- g) The guarantee of the existence and availability of economic resources that allow the fulfillment of the objectives of this law;
- h) All actions leading to make effective the principles and rights recognized by the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women.

### CHAPTER II

COMPETENT BODY

#### ARTICULO 8º

Competent Body. The National Council for Women shall be the governing body in charge of designing public policies to implement the provisions of this law.

#### ARTICULO 9º

Powers. The National Council for Women, to guarantee the achievement of the objectives of this law, must:

- a) Prepare, implement and monitor a National Action Plan for the Prevention, Assistance and Eradication of Violence against Women;
- b) Articulate and coordinate actions for compliance with this law, with the various areas involved at the national, provincial and municipal levels, and with university, trade union, business, religious spheres, organizations for the defense of women's rights and other civil society organizations with competence in the matter;
- c) Convene and establish an ad honorem Advisory Council, composed of representatives of civil society organizations and specialized academic spheres, whose function shall be to advise and recommend on courses of action and appropriate strategies to address the phenomenon of violence;
- d) Promote in the different jurisdictions the creation of comprehensive and free assistance services for women who suffer violence;
- e) Guarantee approach models aimed at empowering women who suffer violence that respect the social, political and cultural nature of the problem, not admitting models that contemplate forms of mediation or negotiation;
- f) Generate minimum standards for early detection and approach to situations of violence;
- g) Develop technical assistance programs for the different jurisdictions aimed at prevention, early detection, early assistance, re-education, inter-institutional referral and the preparation of protocols for the different levels of care;
- h) Provide permanent training, education and instruction on the subject to public officials in the field of Justice, police and security forces, and the Armed Forces, which shall be imparted in a comprehensive and specific manner according to each area of action, based on a basic module respecting the principles enshrined in this law;
- i) Coordinate with legislative spheres specialized training on violence against women and implementation of the principles and rights recognized by the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women aimed at legislators and advisors;
- j) Promote through professional colleges and associations the training of service personnel who, by reason of their activities, may intervene in cases of violence against women;
- k) Design and implement Registers of situations of violence against women in an inter-jurisdictional and inter-institutional manner, in which the basic indicators approved by all competent Ministries and Secretariats are established, independently of those determined by each area for specific purposes, and agreed upon within the framework of Federal Councils with competence in the matter;
- l) Develop, promote and coordinate with the different jurisdictions the criteria for data selection, registration modality and basic indicators disaggregated —at a minimum— by age, sex, marital status and profession or occupation of the parties, relationship between the woman who suffers violence and the man who exercises it, nature of the facts, measures adopted and their results, and sanctions imposed on the violent person. Confidentiality must be ensured in relation to the identity of women who suffer violence;
- m) Coordinate with the Judicial Branch the criteria for data selection, Registration modality and indicators that comprise it that exist in both branches, independently of those defined by each for its own purposes;
- n) Analyze and periodically disseminate statistical data and research results in order to monitor and adapt public policies through the Observatory on Violence Against Women; ñ) Design and publish a Guide to Services in coordination and permanent updating with the different jurisdictions, providing information on programs and direct assistance services;
- o) Implement a multi-support, telephone and digital service that is free and accessible, in an articulated manner with the provinces, through relevant government agencies, aimed at providing support, information and advice on existing resources in the matter of prevention of violence against women and assistance to those who suffer it, including the modality of 'violence against women in public space' known as 'street harassment'.

The information collected from the complaints made to this service must be compiled and systematized by the enforcement authority in order to prepare reliable statistics for the prevention and eradication of the various modalities of violence against women.

(Subsection o) replaced by art. 5° of Law No. 27736 O.G. 23/10/2023)

p) Establish and maintain a Register of non-governmental organizations specialized in the matter in coordination with the jurisdictions and enter into agreements for the development of preventive, control and implementation activities of assistance measures for women who suffer violence and the rehabilitation of men who exercise it;

q) Promote awareness-raising and sensitization campaigns on violence against women, informing about the rights, resources and services that the State guarantees and establishing social condemnation of all forms of violence against women. Publish dissemination materials to support the actions of the different areas;

r) Enter into agreements with public agencies and/or private institutions for any action conducive to compliance with the scope and objectives of this law;

s) Convene and put into operation the Advisory Council of civil society organizations and draft its internal operating regulations;

t) Promote network work in the community sphere, in order to develop inter-institutional and inter-sectoral care and prevention models that unify and coordinate the efforts of public and private institutions;

u) Guarantee access to specific care services for women deprived of liberty.

### CAPITULO III

BASIC GUIDELINES FOR STATE POLICIES

#### ARTICULO 10.

Technical strengthening to jurisdictions. The national State must promote and strengthen inter-institutionally the different jurisdictions for the creation and implementation of comprehensive assistance services to women who suffer violence and to persons who exercise it, and must guarantee:

1.- Education and training campaigns aimed at the community to inform, raise awareness and prevent violence against women in the spheres in which they develop their interpersonal relationships.

2.- Specialized units on violence at the first level of care that work on the prevention and assistance of acts of violence, which shall coordinate their activities according to the established standards, protocols and registries and shall have a comprehensive approach to the following activities:

- a) Interdisciplinary assistance for the evaluation, diagnosis and definition of approach strategies;
- b) Mutual help groups;
- c) Free legal assistance and representation;
- d) Coordinated care with the health area that provides medical and psychological assistance;
- e) Coordinated care with the social area that provides assistance programs aimed at promoting human development.

3.- Economic assistance programs for the self-sufficiency of women.

4.- Community support programs for the maintenance of the self-sufficiency strategy of women.

5.- Day centers for the comprehensive strengthening of women.

6.- Transit facilities for the care and shelter of women who suffer violence in cases where remaining at their home or residence implies an imminent threat to their physical, psychological or sexual integrity, or that of their family group, and which must be oriented toward immediate integration into their family, social and work environment.

7.- Re-education programs aimed at men who exercise violence.

#### ARTICULO 11.

Public policies. The national State shall implement the development of the following priority actions, promoting their articulation and coordination with the different Ministries and Secretariats of the national Executive Power, provincial and municipal jurisdictions, universities and civil society organizations with competence in the matter:

1.- Chief of Cabinet of Ministers – Cabinet Secretariat and Public Management:

- a) Promote specific policies that implement the current regulations on sexual harassment in the national public administration and guarantee the effective enforcement of the principles of non-discrimination and equality of rights, opportunities and treatment in public employment;
- b) Promote, through the Federal Council of Public Service, similar actions within the scope of provincial jurisdictions.

2.- Ministry of Social Development of the Nation:

- a) Promote policies aimed at the social and labor reconnection of women who suffer violence;
- b) Develop prioritization criteria for the inclusion of women in strengthening and social promotion plans and programs and in emergency assistance plans;
- c) Promote training and financing lines for the labor insertion of women in processes of assistance for violence;
- d) Support projects for the creation and implementation of programs for emergency care aimed at women and the care of their daughters/sons;
- e) Enter into agreements with banking entities in order to facilitate credit lines for women who suffer violence;
- f) Coordinate with the National Secretariat of Childhood, Adolescence and Family and the Federal Council of Childhood, Adolescence and Family the care criteria to be established for girls and adolescents who suffer violence.

3.- Ministry of Education of the Nation:

- a) Articulate within the framework of the Federal Council of Education the inclusion in the minimum curricular contents of the gender perspective, the exercise of tolerance, respect and freedom in interpersonal relationships, equality between the sexes, the democratization of family relationships and the enforcement of human rights and the delegitimization of violent models of conflict resolution and of "violence against women in public space" known as "street harassment"; (Subsection replaced by art. 3° of Law No. 27.501 O.G. 8/5/2019)
- b) Promote measures so that early detection of violence against women is included in teacher training plans;
- c) Recommend measures to provide for the immediate schooling of children and adolescents who are affected by a change of residence derived from a situation of violence, until the exclusion of the aggressor from the home is substantiated;
- d) Promote the incorporation of the issue of violence against women in tertiary and university curricula, both at undergraduate and postgraduate levels;
- e) Promote the review and updating of textbooks and teaching materials with the purpose of eliminating gender stereotypes and discriminatory criteria, fostering equality of rights, opportunities and treatment between women and men;
- f) Promote digital literacy programs, good practices in the use of information and communication technologies and identification of digital violence, in comprehensive sexual education classes as well as in the rest of the contents in the educational field and in teacher training. (Subsection replaced by art. 6° of Law No. 27736 O.G. 23/10/2023)
- g) The measures proposed above shall be promoted within the scope of the Federal Council of Education. (Subsection incorporated by art. 7° of Law No. 27736 O.G. 23/10/2023) 4.- Ministry of Health of the Nation:
- a) Incorporate the problem of violence against women in comprehensive women's health programs;
- b) Promote the discussion and adoption of the instruments approved by the Ministry of Health of the Nation on violence against women within the scope of the Federal Council of Health;
- c) Design specific protocols for early detection and care of all types and modalities of violence against women, primarily in the areas of primary health care, emergencies, internal medicine, obstetrics, gynecology, traumatology, pediatrics, and mental health, specifying the procedure to be followed for the care of women who suffer violence, safeguarding the privacy of the assisted person and promoting a non-sexist medical practice. The procedure must ensure the obtaining and preservation of evidentiary elements;
- d) Promote services or programs with interdisciplinary teams specialized in the prevention and care of violence against women and/or those who exercise it with the use of care and referral protocols;
- e) Promote the application of a Registry of persons assisted for situations of violence against women, which coordinates the national and provincial levels.
- f) Ensure specialized assistance for children who witness violence;
- g) Promote agreements with the Superintendence of Health Services or the body that replaces it in the future, in order to include prevention and assistance programs for violence against women in medical-care establishments, social security and prepaid medicine entities, which must incorporate them in their coverage on equal terms with other benefits;
- h) Encourage the continuing education of medical health personnel in order to improve early diagnosis and medical care with a gender perspective;
- i) Promote, within the framework of the Federal Council of Health, the monitoring and follow-up of the application of the protocols. To this end, national and provincial bodies may enter into agreements with institutions and civil society organizations.

5.- Ministry of Justice, Security and Human Rights of the Nation:

5.1. Secretariat of Justice:

- a) Promote policies to facilitate women's access to Justice through the implementation and strengthening of information and legal counseling centers. (Subsection replaced by art. 3° of Decree No. 744/2021 O.G. 29/10/2021. Effective: from the day of its publication in the OFFICIAL GAZETTE)
- b) Promote the application of agreements with Professional Associations, academic institutions and civil society organizations to provide specialized and free legal assistance;
- c) Promote the unification of criteria for the preparation of judicial reports on the danger situation of women who suffer violence;
- d) Promote articulation and cooperation between the different judicial instances involved in order to improve the effectiveness of judicial measures;
- e) Promote the development of a protocol for receiving complaints of violence against women in order to avoid unnecessary judicialization of those cases that require another type of approach;
- f) Foster instances of exchange and articulation with the Supreme Court of Justice of the Nation to encourage specific training on the subject at different levels of the Judiciary;
- g) Encourage the formation of specific training spaces for legal professionals;
- h) Promote research on the causes, nature, severity and consequences of violence against women, as well as the effectiveness of the measures applied to prevent it and repair its effects, periodically disseminating the results;
- i) Guarantee access to specific care services for women deprived of liberty.

5.2. Secretariat of Security:

- a) Encourage in police and security forces, the development of interdisciplinary services that provide support to women who suffer violence to optimize their care, referral to other services and compliance with judicial provisions;
- b) Develop within the scope of the Interior Security Council, the basic procedures for the design of specific protocols for police and security forces in order to provide adequate responses to avoid revictimization, facilitate due care, assistance and police protection to women who come to file complaints at police headquarters;
- c) Promote the articulation of police and security forces involved in the care of violence against women with governmental institutions and civil society organizations;
- d) Raise awareness and train police and security forces on the issue of violence against women within the framework of respect for human rights;
- e) Include in the training programs of police and security forces specific subjects and/or curricular contents on the human rights of women and especially on violence with a gender perspective.
- f) Urge police and security forces to act in protection of women victims of gender violence when violence occurs in public space or publicly accessible space, including the modality of "violence against women in public spaces" known as "street harassment". 6.- Ministry of Labor, Employment and Social Security of the Nation:

  - 1. Access to the job position, in matters of recruitment and selection;
  - 2. Professional career, in matters of promotion and training;
  - 3. Permanence in the job position;

- c) Raise awareness at different hierarchical levels on the issue of violence against women within the framework of respect for human rights;
- d) Include in training programs specific subjects and/or contents on the human rights of women and violence with a gender perspective.

8.- Secretariat of Media of the Nation:

- a) Promote from the National Media System the dissemination of messages and permanent awareness and sensitization campaigns aimed at the general population and particularly at women about their right to live a life free of violence;
- b) Promote in the mass media respect for the human rights of women and the treatment of violence from the gender perspective;
- c) Provide training to mass media professionals on violence against women;
- d) Encourage the elimination of sexism in information;
- e) Promote, as a matter of corporate social responsibility, the dissemination of advertising campaigns to prevent and eradicate violence against women.

### CAPITULO IV

OBSERVATORY OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

#### ARTICULO 12.

Creation. The Observatory of Violence against Women is hereby created within the scope of the National Women's Council, intended for the monitoring, collection, production, registration and systematization of data and information on violence against women.

#### ARTICULO 13.

Mission. The Observatory shall have as its mission the development of a permanent information system that provides inputs for the design, implementation and management of public policies aimed at the prevention and eradication of violence against women.

#### ARTICULO 14.

Functions. The functions of the Observatory of Violence against Women shall be:

- a) To collect, process, register, analyze, publish and disseminate periodic and systematic information that is diachronically and synchronically comparable on violence against women;
- b) To promote the development of studies and research on the evolution, prevalence, types and modalities of violence against women, its consequences and effects, identifying those social, cultural, economic and political factors that are in some way associated with or may constitute a cause of violence;
- c) To incorporate the results of its research and studies in the reports that the national State submits to regional and international bodies on matters of violence against women;
- d) To enter into cooperation agreements with public or private bodies, national or international, with the purpose of articulating in an interdisciplinary manner the development of studies and research;
- e) To create an information network and disseminate to the public the data collected, studies and activities of the Observatory, through its own web page or linked to the portal of the National Women's Council. To create and maintain a documentation base permanently updated and open to the public;
- f) To examine good practices in the prevention and eradication of violence against women and innovative experiences in the matter and disseminate them for the purpose of being adopted by those national, provincial or municipal bodies and institutions that deem appropriate;
- g) To coordinate actions with governmental bodies with competence in matters of women's human rights for the purpose of monitoring the implementation of policies for the prevention and eradication of violence against women, to evaluate their impact and prepare proposals for actions or reforms;
- h) To encourage and promote the organization and periodic holding of public debates, with participation of research centers, academic institutions, civil society organizations and representatives of public and private bodies, national and international, with competence in the matter, encouraging the exchange of experiences and identifying relevant issues and problems for the public agenda;
- i) To provide training, advice and technical support to public and private bodies for the implementation of the Registries and protocols;
- j) To coordinate the actions of the Observatory of Violence against Women with other Observatories that exist at the provincial, national and international level;
- k) To publish the annual report on the activities developed, which must contain information on the studies and research conducted and proposals for institutional or normative reforms. It shall be disseminated to the public and submitted to the authorities with competence in the matter so that they may adopt the appropriate measures.

#### ARTICULO 15.

Composition. The Observatory of Violence against Women shall be composed of:

- a) A person designated by the Presidency of the National Women's Council, who shall exercise the Directorship of the Observatory, and must have accredited training in social research and human rights;
- b) An interdisciplinary team qualified in the matter.

## TITULO III

PROCEDURES

### CAPITULO I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### ARTICULO 16.

Minimum rights and guarantees of judicial and administrative procedures. State bodies must guarantee women, in any judicial or administrative proceeding, in addition to all the rights recognized in the National Constitution, the International Human Rights Treaties ratified by the Argentine Nation, this law and the laws enacted in consequence thereof, the following rights and guarantees:

- a) To the free of charge nature of all proceedings and instances in the course of judicial actions, and to access to available public resources for the production of evidence, in particular for the performance of computer expert reports and preferably specialized legal representation. (Subsection replaced by art. 8 of Law No. 27736 O.G. 23/10/2023)
- b) To obtain a timely and effective response;
- c) To be heard personally by the judge and by the competent administrative authority;
- d) That her opinion be taken into account when reaching a decision that affects her;
- e) To receive urgent and preventive judicial protection when any of the rights set forth in article 3 of this law are threatened or violated;
- f) To the protection of her privacy, guaranteeing the confidentiality of the proceedings;
- g) To participate in the procedure by receiving information on the status of the case;
- h) To receive humane treatment, avoiding revictimization;
- i) To evidentiary breadth to prove the reported facts, taking into account the special circumstances in which acts of violence occur and who their natural witnesses are;
- j) To oppose the performance of inspections on her body outside the strict framework of the judicial order. In case of consenting to them and in judicial expert examinations, she has the right to be accompanied by someone she trusts and to have them performed by specialized professional staff trained with a gender perspective;
- k) To have efficient mechanisms to report officials for non-compliance with established deadlines and other irregularities.
- l) To the diligent and expeditious safeguarding of evidence on digital media by specialized investigative bodies or corresponding public agencies. (Subsection incorporated by art. 9 of Law No. 27736 O.G. 23/10/2023)

#### ARTICULO 17.

Administrative Procedures. Local jurisdictions may establish the procedures prior to or subsequent to the judicial instance for compliance with this law, which shall be applied by municipalities, communes, development commissions, boards, delegations of the Provincial Women's Councils or decentralized areas, justices of the peace or bodies they deem appropriate.

#### ARTICULO 18.

Complaint. Persons working in assistance, social, educational and health services, in the public or private sphere, who by reason of or in the course of their duties become aware of an act of violence against women under the terms of this law, shall be obliged to file complaints, as appropriate, even in those cases where the act does not constitute a crime.

### CAPITULO II

PROCEDURE

#### ARTICULO 19.

Scope of application. Local jurisdictions, within the scope of their competences, shall enact their procedural rules or adhere to the procedural regime provided for in this law.

#### ARTICULO 20.

Characteristics of the procedure. The procedure shall be free of charge and summary.

#### ARTICULO 21.

Filing of the complaint. The filing of the complaint for violence against women may be made before any judge of any jurisdiction and instance or before the Public Prosecutor's Office, orally or in writing.

The identity of the complainant shall be kept confidential.

#### ARTICULO 22.

Jurisdiction. The judge who has jurisdiction by reason of subject matter according to the types and modalities of violence involved shall hear the case.

Even in case of lack of jurisdiction, the intervening judge may order the preventive measures deemed pertinent.

#### ARTICULO 23.

Police statement. In the event that upon going to a police service only a statement is made and from it the possible existence of violence against women arises, it shall be referred to the competent judicial authority within TWENTY-FOUR (24) hours.

#### ARTICULO 24.

Persons who may file the complaint. Complaints may be filed:

- a) By the woman who considers herself affected or her legal representative without any restriction;
- b) The girl or adolescent directly or through her legal representatives in accordance with the provisions of Law 26.061 on Comprehensive Protection of the Rights of Girls, Boys and Adolescents;
- c) Any person when the affected woman has a disability, or due to her physical or psychological condition could not file it;
- d) In cases of sexual violence, the woman who has suffered it is the only one with standing to file the complaint. When it is filed by a third party, the woman shall be summoned to ratify or rectify it within TWENTY-FOUR (24) hours. The competent judicial authority shall take the necessary precautions to prevent the case from becoming public.
- e) The criminal complaint shall be mandatory for any person who works in assistance, social, educational and health services, in the public or private sphere, who by reason of or on occasion of their duties becomes aware that a woman suffers violence provided that the facts could constitute a crime.

#### ARTICULO 25.

Protective assistance. At any stage of the process the presence of a companion shall be admitted as ad honorem protective aid, provided that the woman who suffers violence requests it and with the sole purpose of preserving her physical and psychological health.

#### ARTICULO 26. — Urgent preventive measures.

a) During any stage of the process the intervening judge may, ex officio or at the request of a party, order one or more of the following preventive measures according to the types and modalities of violence against women defined in articles 5 and 6 of this law:

- a. 1. Order the prohibition of approach by the alleged aggressor to the place of residence, work, study, recreation or to the places of habitual attendance of the woman who suffers violence; a.2. Order the alleged aggressor to cease acts of disturbance or intimidation that, directly or indirectly, he/she carries out towards the woman, both in the analog space and in the digital space. (Paragraph replaced by art. 10 of Law No. 27736 O.G. 23/10/2023) a.3. Order the immediate restitution of personal effects to the petitioning party, if she has been deprived of them; a.4. Prohibit the alleged aggressor from purchasing and possessing weapons, and order the seizure of those in his/her possession; a.5. Provide measures conducive to providing those who suffer or exercise violence, when so required, medical or psychological assistance, through public agencies and civil society organizations with specialized training in the prevention and care of violence against women; a.6. Order security measures at the woman's home; a.7. Order any other measure necessary to guarantee the safety of the woman who suffers violence, stop the situation of violence and prevent the repetition of any act of disturbance or intimidation, aggression and mistreatment by the aggressor towards the woman. a.8. Order the prohibition of contact by the alleged aggressor towards the woman who suffers violence through any information and communication technology, instant messaging application or digital communication channel. (Paragraph incorporated by art. 11 of Law No. 27736 O.G. 23/10/2023) a.9. Order by reasoned order, to digital platform companies, social networks, or electronic pages, in writing or electronically, the deletion of content that constitutes an exercise of digital or telematic violence defined in this law, the specific URL of the content whose removal is ordered must be identified in the order. For purposes of notification of the measure of this paragraph, article 122 of law 19.550 may be applied.

The intervening authority in the case must request from digital platform companies, social networks, or electronic pages, the securing of computer data relating to traffic, subscribers and content of the deleted material, that are in their possession or are under their control, for the substantive actions that correspond, for a period of ninety (90) days that may be renewed once for the same period at the request of the interested party. The execution of said procedure must be ordered to be kept secret while the securing order lasts.

The authority may, at the request of a party and solely for the investigation of the substantive actions that correspond, request from those required that they reveal the computer data of subscribers that are in their possession or are under their control and also those relating to traffic and content of the deleted material by reasoned order according to the internal cooperation mechanisms and/or procedures provided for within the framework of current international cooperation rules and treaties.

(Paragraph a.9. incorporated by art. 12 of Law No. 27736 O.G. 23/10/2023)

b) Without prejudice to the measures established in paragraph a) of this article, in cases of the modality of domestic violence against women, the judge may order the following urgent preventive measures: b.1. Prohibit the alleged aggressor from alienating, disposing of, destroying, concealing or transferring community property of the conjugal partnership or the common property of the cohabiting couple; b.2. Order the exclusion of the aggressor from the common residence, regardless of its ownership; b.3. Decide the return to the home of the woman if she had left, after exclusion of the alleged aggressor from the dwelling; b.4. Order the police force to accompany the woman who suffers violence to her home to remove her personal effects; b.5. In case it concerns a couple with children, a provisional alimony quota shall be set, if applicable, in accordance with the background in the case and according to the rules governing the matter; b.6. In case the victim is a minor, the judge, by reasoned resolution and taking into account the opinion and the right to be heard of the girl or adolescent, may grant custody to a member of her family group, by consanguinity or affinity, or to other members of the extended family or the community. b.7. Order the provisional suspension of the visitation regime; b.8. Order the alleged aggressor to refrain from interfering, in any way, in the exercise of custody, upbringing and education of the children; b.9. Order the inventory of the community property of the conjugal partnership and the separate property of those who exercise and suffer violence. In cases of cohabiting couples the inventory of the assets of each one shall be ordered; b.10. Grant exclusive use to the woman who suffers violence, for the period deemed convenient, of the furniture of the house.

#### ARTICULO 27.

Powers of the judge. The judge may issue more than one measure at a time, determining their duration according to the circumstances of the case, and must establish a maximum period of duration for them, by reasoned order.

#### ARTICULO 28.

Hearing. The intervening judge shall set a hearing, which must be personally conducted under penalty of nullity, within FORTY-EIGHT (48) hours of ordering the measures of article 26, or if none of them were adopted, from the moment he/she became aware of the complaint.

The alleged aggressor shall be obligated to appear under warning of being brought before the court with the assistance of the public force.

In said hearing, he/she shall hear the parties separately under penalty of nullity, and shall order the measures deemed pertinent.

If the victim of violence is a girl or adolescent, the provisions of Law 26.061 on Comprehensive Protection of the Rights of Girls, Boys and Adolescents must be considered.

Mediation or conciliation hearings are prohibited.

#### ARTICULO 29.

Reports. Whenever possible, the intervening judge may request a report made by an interdisciplinary team to determine the physical, psychological, economic or other damages suffered by the woman and the dangerous situation in which she finds herself.

Said report shall be submitted within a period of FORTY-EIGHT (48) hours, so that other measures may be applied, or some of those mentioned in article 26 may be interrupted or terminated.

The intervening judge may also consider reports prepared by interdisciplinary teams of the public administration regarding the physical, psychological, economic or other damages suffered by the woman and the dangerous situation, avoiding the production of new reports that revictimize her.

He/she may also consider reports from professionals of civil society organizations skilled in the treatment of violence against women.

#### ARTICULO 30.

Evidence, principles and measures. The judge shall have broad powers to order and advance the process, being able to arrange the measures necessary to investigate the events, locate the whereabouts of the alleged aggressor, and protect those at risk of suffering new acts of violence, the principle of obtaining material truth being in force.

#### ARTICULO 31.

Resolutions. The principle of broad evidentiary freedom shall apply to prove the reported facts, the evidence offered being evaluated according to the principle of sound judgment. Presumptions that contribute to the demonstration of the facts shall be considered, provided they are serious, precise and concordant indications.

#### ARTICULO 32.

Sanctions. In case of non-compliance with the ordered measures, the judge may evaluate the advisability of modifying them, being able to expand them or order others.

In the face of new non-compliance and without prejudice to the corresponding civil or criminal responsibilities, the Judge must apply one or more of the following sanctions:

- a) Warning or call to attention for the act committed;
- b) Communication of the acts of violence to the agency, institution, union, professional association or workplace of the aggressor;
- c) Mandatory attendance of the aggressor to reflective, educational or therapeutic programs aimed at modifying violent behaviors.

Likewise, when the non-compliance constitutes disobedience or another crime, the judge must bring the fact to the attention of the judge with jurisdiction in criminal matters.

#### ARTICULO 33.

Appeal. Resolutions that grant, reject, interrupt, modify or order the cessation of any of the urgent preventive measures or impose sanctions, shall be appealable within a period of THREE (3) working days.

The appeal against resolutions that grant urgent preventive measures shall be granted in relation and with devolutive effect.

The appeal against resolutions that order the interruption or cessation of such measures shall be granted in relation and with suspensive effect.

#### ARTICULO 34.

Follow-up. During the processing of the case, for the time deemed appropriate, the judge must monitor the effectiveness of the measures and decisions adopted, either through the appearance of the parties before the court, with the frequency ordered, and/or through the intervention of the interdisciplinary team, who shall prepare periodic reports on the situation.

#### ARTICULO 35.

Reparation. The injured party may claim civil reparation for damages and losses, according to the common rules governing the matter.

#### ARTICULO 36.

Obligations of officials. Police, judicial officials, health agents, and any other public official to whom affected women turn, have the obligation to inform about:

- a) The rights that legislation confers on women who suffer violence, and about governmental services available for their care;
- b) How and where to proceed to be assisted in the process;
- c) How to preserve evidence.

#### ARTICULO 37.

Records. The Supreme Court of Justice of the Nation shall keep sociodemographic records of complaints filed regarding acts of violence provided for in this law, specifying, at a minimum, age, marital status, profession or occupation of the woman who suffers violence, as well as of the aggressor; relationship with the aggressor, nature of the facts, measures adopted and their results, as well as sanctions imposed on the aggressor.

The courts intervening in cases of violence provided for in this law must annually submit the relevant information for said registry.

Access to the records requires justified reasons and prior judicial authorization, guaranteeing the confidentiality of the identity of the parties.

The Supreme Court of Justice of the Nation shall prepare publicly accessible statistics that allow knowledge of, at a minimum, the characteristics of those who exercise or suffer violence and its modalities, relationship between the parties, type of measures adopted and their results, and type and number of sanctions applied.

#### ARTICULO 38.

Collaboration of public or private organizations. The judge may request or accept as amicus curiae the collaboration of public or private organizations or entities dedicated to the protection of women's rights.

#### ARTICULO 39.

Exemption from charges. Proceedings based on this law shall be exempt from payment of stamps, fees, deposits and any other tax, without prejudice to the provisions of article 68 of the Civil and Commercial Procedural Code of the Nation regarding costs.

#### ARTICULO 40.

Supplementary rules. The corresponding procedural regimes shall apply supplementarily, according to the types and modalities of violence reported.

## TITULO IV

FINAL PROVISIONS

#### ARTICULO 41.

In no case shall the conducts, acts or omissions provided for in this law entail the creation of new criminal types, nor the modification or repeal of existing ones.

#### ARTICULO 42.

Law 24.417 on Protection against Family Violence shall apply in those cases of domestic violence not provided for in this law.

#### ARTICULO 43.

The appropriations necessary for compliance with this law shall be provided annually in the General Budget Law of the National Administration.

#### ARTICULO 44.

The law shall enter into force upon its publication in the Official Bulletin of the Nation.
