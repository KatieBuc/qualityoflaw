### CHAPTER II SECOND PART

DEFINITIONS DEFINITIONS

#### Article 3.

Definitions. For the purposes of this law, the following shall be understood as:

- a. Access to information: It is the right of women victims of violence to receive complete information and adequate counseling for their personal situation, through the services, agencies or offices that may be made available by the competent institutions, both public and private. Such information shall include the measures contemplated in this law, relating to their protection and security, and the rights and aid provided therein, as well as the reference to the place of provision of care, emergency, support and comprehensive recovery services.
- b. Private sphere: Comprises domestic interpersonal relationships, family relationships or relationships of trust within which acts of violence are committed against women, when the aggressor is the spouse, ex-spouse, cohabitant or ex-cohabitant, with whom the victim has procreated or not, the aggressor is the boyfriend or ex-boyfriend, or relative of the victim.
- c. Also included in this sphere are relationships between the spouse, ex-spouse, cohabitant or ex-cohabitant, boyfriend or ex-boyfriend of a woman with her daughters.
- d. Public sphere: Comprises interpersonal relationships that take place in the community and that include the social, labor, educational, religious sphere or any other type of relationship that is not included in the private sphere.
- e. Comprehensive assistance: Women victims of violence, their daughters and sons, have the right to social services of care, emergency, support, shelter, as well as recovery. Multidisciplinary care shall especially involve:
  - 1. Medical and psychological care.
  - 2. Social support.
  - 3. Legal follow-up of claims for women's rights.
  - 4. Support for training and labor insertion.
  - 5. Assistance of an interpreter.
- e. Femicide: Violent death of a woman, caused in the context of unequal power relations between men and women, in exercise of gender power against women.
- f. Misogyny: Hatred, contempt or underestimation of women for the sole fact of being women.
- g. Power relations: Manifestations of control or domination that lead to the submission of women and to discrimination against them.
- h. Reparation to the victim: Reparation shall be understood as the set of measures tending to approximate the victim's situation to the state in which she would have been had the criminal act not occurred.
- i. Reparation must be characterized by its integrality and shall comprise, in addition to compensation of an economic nature, all those measures tending to provide the victim with medical, psychological, moral and social reparation.
- j. Victim: A woman of any age who suffers any type of violence.
- k. Violence against women: Any action or omission based on belonging to the female sex that results in immediate or subsequent harm, physical, sexual, economic or psychological suffering for the woman, as well as threats of such acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether it occurs in the public or private sphere.
- l. Economic violence: Actions or omissions that affect the use, enjoyment, availability or accessibility of a woman to material goods that belong to her by right, by marital bond or common-law union, by capacity or by inheritance, causing her deterioration, damage, transformation, subtraction, destruction, retention or loss of objects or material goods of her own or of the family group, as well as the retention of work instruments, personal documents, goods, valuables, rights or economic resources.
- m. Physical violence: Actions of aggression in which direct corporal force is used or by means of any object, weapon or substance with which damage, physical suffering, injuries or illness is caused to a woman.
- n. Psychological or emotional violence: Actions that can produce psychological or emotional damage or suffering to a woman, her daughters or her sons, as well as actions, threats or violence against daughters, sons or other family members up to the fourth degree of consanguinity and second degree of affinity of the victim, in both cases with the purpose of intimidating her, undermining her self-esteem or controlling her, who subjected to that emotional climate may suffer a progressive psychological weakening with depressive conditions.

o. Sexual violence: Actions of physical or psychological violence whose purpose is to violate the freedom and sexual integrity of women, including sexual humiliation, forced prostitution and the denial of the right to use family planning methods, whether natural or artificial, or to adopt protective measures against sexually transmitted diseases.

#### Artículo 8.

Economic violence. Commits the crime of economic violence against women whoever, within the public or private sphere, engages in conduct comprised in any of the following circumstances:

Diminishes, limits or restricts the free disposal of her assets or property rights or labor rights.

Obliges the woman to sign documents that affect, limit, restrict her patrimony or place it at risk; or that exempt from economic, criminal, civil or any other kind of responsibility.

Destroys or hides documents justifying ownership or personal identification, or goods, personal objects, work instruments that are indispensable for carrying out her habitual activities.

Subjects the will of the woman through economic abuse by not covering the basic needs of her and her daughters and sons.

Exercises psychological, sexual or physical violence against the woman, with the purpose of controlling the income or flow of monetary resources that enter the household.

The person responsible for this crime shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of five to eight years, without prejudice to the facts constituting other crimes stipulated in ordinary laws.

#### Artículo 9.

Prohibition of justification grounds. In crimes classified against women, customs or cultural or religious traditions may not be invoked as grounds for justification or exculpation to perpetrate, inflict, consent to, promote, instigate or tolerate violence against women.

With the sole complaint of the act of violence in the private sphere, the jurisdictional body that hears it must dictate the security measures referred to in article 7 of the Law to Prevent, Sanction and Eradicate Intrafamily Violence, being applicable to women who are victims of the crimes established in the present law, even when the aggressor is not their relative.

#### Artículo 10.

Aggravating circumstances. The circumstances that aggravate violence against women must be analyzed according to the following:

- a) In relation to the personal circumstances of the person who aggresses.
- b) In relation to the personal circumstances of the victim.
- c) In relation to the power relations existing between the victim and the person who aggresses.
- d) In relation to the context of the violent act and the harm produced to the victim.
- e) In relation to the means and mechanisms used to perpetrate the act and the harm produced.

### CHAPTER V

REPARATIONS

#### Artículo 11.

Compensation to the victim. The reparation to the victim shall be proportional to the harm caused and to the degree of culpability of the perpetrator of the crime. In no case shall it imply an unjust enrichment of the victim.

The compensation may be decreed by the justice bodies that hear the specific case.

When the victim has died, the right to reparation extends to their successors, according to what is established in the Civil Code and Criminal Procedure Code.

#### Artículo 12.

Responsibility of the State. In compliance with what is established in the Political Constitution of the Republic of Guatemala and in the international conventions and treaties on human rights accepted and ratified by the State of Guatemala, the State shall be jointly and severally liable for the action or omission incurred by female or male public officials who obstruct, delay or deny the fulfillment of the sanctions provided in the present law, being able to exercise against them the action of recourse if convicted, without prejudice to administrative or civil responsibilities.

### CHAPTER VI

OBLIGATIONS OF THE STATE

#### Article 13.

Rights of the victim. It is the obligation of the State to guarantee to the woman who becomes a victim of any form of violence, the following rights:

- a) Access to information.
- b) Comprehensive assistance. Officials who without justified cause deny or delay the provision of information or comprehensive assistance to the detriment of the process or the victim, shall be subject to labor and administrative measures and sanctions, without prejudice to civil or criminal liabilities as the case may be.

#### Article 14.

Strengthening of agencies responsible for criminal investigation. For compliance with the provisions contained in this law, the Public Prosecutor's Office must create the Prosecutor's Office for Crimes against the Life and Physical Integrity of Women, specialized in the investigation of the crimes created by this law, with the budgetary, physical, material, scientific and human resources that allow it to fulfill its purposes.

#### Article 15.

Creation of specialized jurisdictional bodies. The Supreme Court of Justice shall implement specialized jurisdictional bodies that must have jurisdiction over the crimes established in this law, organizing their operation on a twenty-four (24) hour basis, without prejudice to the jurisdiction attributed to criminal courts.

#### Article 16.

Comprehensive Support Centers for Women Survivors of Violence. It is the obligation of the State to guarantee access, relevance, quality and financial, human and material resources for the operation of the Comprehensive Support Centers for Women Survivors of Violence. It shall be the National Coordinator for the Prevention of Domestic Violence and Violence against Women - CONAPREVI - who shall promote their creation and provide accompaniment, advice and monitoring to specialized women's organizations that administer them.

#### Article 17.

Institutional strengthening. The National Coordinator for the Prevention of Domestic Violence and Violence against Women - CONAPREVI - is the coordinating, advisory and promoting entity for public policies related to reducing domestic violence and violence against women.

It is the State's responsibility to strengthen and institutionalize the already created instances for addressing the social problem of violence against women, to ensure their sustainability, among them: CONAPREVI, the Office of the Indigenous Women's Defender - DEMI -, the Presidential Secretariat for Women - SEPREM -, as well as the free legal assistance service to victims provided by the Institute of Public Criminal Defense. Likewise, the strengthening of other non-governmental organizations in the same vein shall be guaranteed.

#### Article 18.

Training for State officials. Within the framework of the implementation of the National Plan for the Prevention and Eradication of Domestic Violence and Violence against Women - PLANOVI -, the National Coordinator for the Prevention of Domestic Violence and Violence against Women - CONAPREVI - and other non-governmental organizations, are responsible for advising, following up and monitoring training and capacity-building processes on violence against women with ethnic-cultural relevance directed at public officials, with special emphasis on justice operators.

#### Article 19.

Legal assistance to the victim. The State has the obligation to provide free legal assistance to the victim or her family members, and must provide them with the services of a public defender, to guarantee the effective exercise of their rights.

#### Artículo 20.

National Information System on Violence Against Women. The National Statistics Institute -INE- is obligated to generate, with the information that must be provided by the Judicial Branch, Public Prosecutor's Office, Attorney General's Office, Human Rights Ombudsman's Office, National Civil Police, Public Criminal Defense Institute, Popular Legal Clinics and any other institution with knowledge of crimes contemplated in this law, indicators and statistical information, and must create a National Information System on Violence Against Women. The referred entities must implement appropriate mechanisms, according to their internal regulations, for the fulfillment of this obligation.

Appropriate mechanisms must be established according to the information that needs to be provided, in order to comply with the requirements established in this article.

#### Artículo 21.

Budget Allocations: The Ministry of Public Finance must allocate resources within the State Revenue and Expenditure Budget for the fulfillment of this law, in relation to the following aspects:

- a) Creation of the Prosecutor's Office for Crimes against the Life and Physical Integrity of Women.
- b) Strengthening of the National Institute of Forensic Sciences -INACIF-.
- c) Creation of specialized jurisdictional bodies for the prosecution of crimes against the life and physical integrity of women.
- d) Strengthening and adequate functioning of the National Coordinator for the Prevention of Domestic Violence and Violence Against Women -CONAPREVI-.
- e) Implementation of the National Plan for the Prevention and Eradication of Domestic Violence and Violence Against Women -PLANOVI-.
- f) Strengthening of the protection service for procedural subjects and persons linked to the administration of criminal justice.
- g) Strengthening of the Public Criminal Defense Institute for the provision of free legal assistance services.
