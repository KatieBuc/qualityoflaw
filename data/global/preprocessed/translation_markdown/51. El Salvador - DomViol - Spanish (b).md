DECRETO Nº 520

THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF THE REPUBLIC OF EL SALVADOR,

CONSIDERING:

I.- That the Constitution recognizes the human person as the origin and end of State activity, which is organized for the attainment of justice, legal security and the common good. Consequently, it is the State's obligation to ensure for the inhabitants of the Republic the enjoyment of freedom, health, culture, economic welfare and social justice. Likewise, Article

144, establishes that international treaties entered into by El Salvador with other States or with international organizations, constitute Laws of the

Republic.

II.- That through Legislative Decree No. 430, dated August 23, 1995, published in the Official Gazette No. 154, Volume No. 328, of that same date, the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate

Violence against Women, "Convention of Belem do Para", was ratified, which establishes the obligation for States parties to include in their domestic legislation criminal, civil and administrative norms; as well as, those of another nature that are necessary to prevent, punish and eradicate violence against women.

III.- That it is necessary to have legislation that adequately regulates the policy of detection, prevention, care, protection, reparation and punishment, for the eradication of all forms of violence against women and the respect of their human rights as an obligation of the State; the introduction of a legal instrument that adequately guides public and private actions in favor of women, and that guarantees a better quality of life and an advancement in their capacities in a comprehensive manner, becomes indispensable.

IV.- That violations of human rights arising from the different forms of violence that affect life, integrity and citizen security, have a differentiated impact according to the gender of the victims; since any aggression perpetrated against a woman is directly linked to the unequal distribution of power and to the asymmetric relationships between women and men in society.

V.- That the inequalities of power between men and women perpetuated through violence, do not allow women to fully exercise their rights in the social, political, economic, cultural and family fields, denying them access to a life free of violence, which constitutes a violation of their human rights and fundamental freedoms; for which reason it is necessary to legislate in a comprehensive manner through measures that include the detection, prevention, care, protection, reparation and punishment of violence against women in any of its manifestations.

COMPREHENSIVE SPECIAL LAW FOR A LIFE FREE OF VIOLENCE FOR WOMEN

## Title I

Guarantee and Application of the Law

### Chapter I

Preliminary Provisions

#### Article 1.- Object of the Law

The purpose of this Law is to establish, recognize and guarantee the right of women to a life free of violence, through Public Policies oriented to the detection, prevention, care, protection, reparation and punishment of violence against women; in order to protect their right to life, physical and moral integrity, freedom, non-discrimination, dignity, effective protection, personal security, real equality and equity.

#### Article 2.- Right of Women to a Life Free of Violence

The right of women to a life free of violence includes being free from all forms of discrimination, being valued and educated free from stereotyped patterns of behavior, social and cultural practices based on concepts of inferiority or subordination.

Likewise, it refers to the enjoyment, exercise and protection of human rights and freedoms enshrined in the Constitution and in the National and International Instruments on the subject in force, including the right to:

- 1. That their life and their physical, psychological and moral integrity be respected.
- 2. That the inherent dignity of their person be respected and protection be provided to their family.
- 3. Freedom and personal security.
- 4. Not be subjected to torture or degrading treatment.
- 5. Equal protection before the Law and of the Law.
- 6. A simple and prompt remedy before competent tribunals that protect them against acts that violate their rights.
- 7. Freedom of association.
- 8. Profess religion and beliefs.
- 9. Participate in public affairs including public offices.

#### Article 3.- Scope of Application

This Law shall apply for the benefit of women who are in the national territory, whether they are nationals or not, or who having the status of Salvadorans, are outside the national territory, provided that the actions or omissions addressed by this Law can be prosecuted based on extraterritoriality parameters.

#### Article 4.- Guiding Principles

The guiding principles of this Law are:

- a) Specialization: It is the right to differentiated and specialized attention, according to the specific needs and circumstances of women and especially, those who are in conditions of vulnerability or risk.
- b) FAVORABILITY: IN CASE OF CONFLICT OR DOUBT REGARDING THE APPLICATION OF THE PROVISIONS CONTAINED IN THIS LAW, THE MOST FAVORABLE TO WOMEN FACING VIOLENCE SHALL PREVAIL, EXCEPT IN THOSE PROVISIONS THAT INDICE LEGISLATIVO REFER TO THE ESTABLISHMENT OF FACTS OR THE ASSESSMENT OF EVIDENCE. (1)
- c) Integrality: It refers to the coordination and articulation of State Institutions for the eradication of violence against women.
- d) Intersectoriality: It is the principle that supports the articulation of programs, actions and resources of the different sectors and actors at national and local level, for the detection, prevention, care, protection and sanction, as well as for the reparation of harm to victims.
- e) Secularity: It refers to the fact that no custom, tradition, or religious consideration may be invoked to justify violence against women.
- f) Absolute priority: It refers to respect for the right of women to a life free of violence, in any sphere.

#### Article 5.- Rights Holders

This Law shall apply for the benefit of women, without distinction of age, who are in the national territory; to this end, all forms of discrimination are prohibited, understood as any distinction, exclusion, restriction or arbitrary differentiation based on sex, age, sexual identity, family status, rural or urban origin, ethnic origin, economic condition, nationality, religion or beliefs, physical, mental or sensory disability, or any analogous cause, whether it comes from the State, its agents or private individuals.

#### Article 6.- Obligated Parties

For purposes of this Law, obligated parties are any natural or legal person who is or acts in Salvadoran territory, who must comply with and enforce the provisions of this Law, whatever their nationality, domicile or residence.

#### Article 7.- Power or Trust Relations

For the application and interpretation of this Law, it is presumed that the types and modalities of violence contemplated in this Law originate from the unequal power or trust relationship; in which women are in a position of disadvantage with respect to men, consisting of:

- a) Power relations: They are characterized by asymmetry, domination and control of one or more persons over another or others.
- b) Trust relations: They are those based on the assumptions of loyalty, credibility, honesty and security established between two or more persons.

The inequality in power or trust relations may subsist, even when the bond that originated them has ended, regardless of the sphere in which they took place.

#### Article 8.- Definitions

For purposes of this Law, the following shall be understood as:

- a) Comprehensive Care: All actions to detect, care for, protect and restore the rights of women who face any type of violence; for which, the State must allocate the necessary and appropriate human, logistical and financial resources to establish specialized services that guarantee the restitution of rights and the elimination of subsequent risks or harm.
- b) Workplace Harassment: It is the action of physical or psychological hostility that, in a systematic and recurrent manner, is exercised against a woman because she is a woman in the workplace, with the purpose of isolating, intimidating or destroying the communication networks of the person facing these acts, damaging her reputation, discrediting the work performed or disturbing or obstructing the exercise of her duties.
- c) Unlearning: It is the process by which a person or group of persons, deconstructs or invalidates what has been learned by considering it subject to questioning or inappropriate for their own development and that of the community to which they belong.
- d) Misogyny: Behaviors of hatred, implicit or explicit, against everything related to the feminine such as rejection, aversion and contempt against women.
- e) Aggressor: The person who exercises any of the types of violence against women, in an unequal power relationship and in any of its modalities.
- f) Prevention: Norms and policies to reduce violence against women by intervening from the identified causes thereof, and whose objective is to avoid its reproduction and reduce the probability of the problem appearing; therefore, they are aimed at transforming the risk environment and strengthening the skills and conditions of persons and communities for its eradication, ensuring rapid and effective identification, as well as the reduction of impacts and sequelae when the problem occurs and recidivism.
- g) Sexist Advertising: Any form of advertising that transmits values, roles, stereotypes, attitudes, feminine and masculine behaviors, verbal and non-verbal language, that promote discrimination, subordination, violence and misogyny.
- h) Relearning: It is the process through which persons assimilate knowledge or behavior after its androcentric deconstruction, from a critical and non-traditional vision as a product of the new relationships established with their natural social environment.
- i) Re-victimization: Actions that have the purpose or result of causing suffering to direct or indirect victims of acts of violence contemplated or not in this Law, through actions or omissions such as: rejection, indolence, indifference, disqualification, minimization of facts, unjustified delay in processes, INDICE LEGISLATIVO lack of credibility, blaming, lack of protection, denial and unjustified lack of effective assistance.
- j) Sexism: Any discrimination based on sexual difference that affects all relationships between human beings and encompasses all daily dimensions of private or public life that defines feelings, conceptions, attitudes and actions.
- k) Violence against Women: Any action based on their gender, that causes death, harm or physical, sexual or psychological suffering to women both in the public and private spheres.
- l) Direct Victim: Refers to any woman whose right to live free of violence is violated, regardless of whether the aggressor is reported, individualized, apprehended, prosecuted or convicted.
- m) Indirect Victim: Any person whose right to live a life free of violence is violated or who suffers harm when intervening to assist the direct victim or prevent their victimization, regardless of the type of relationship that exists between them.

#### Article 9.- Types of Violence

For the purposes of this Law, the following are considered types of violence:

- a) Economic Violence: It is any action or omission by the aggressor that affects the economic survival of the woman, which manifests itself through acts aimed at limiting, controlling or preventing the income of her economic earnings.
- b) Femicidal Violence: It is the extreme form of gender violence against women, product of the violation of their human rights, in the public and private spheres, formed by the set of misogynistic conducts that lead to social or State impunity, which may culminate in femicide and other forms of violent death of women.
- c) Physical Violence: It is any conduct that directly or indirectly is directed at causing harm or physical suffering to the woman, with result or risk of producing physical injury or damage, exercised by whoever is or has been her spouse or by whoever is or has been linked to her by an analogous relationship of affection, even without cohabitation. Likewise, acts exercised by the aggressor in her family, social or work environment shall be considered acts of physical violence against the woman.
- d) Psychological and Emotional Violence: It is any direct or indirect conduct that causes emotional harm, diminishes self-esteem, harms or disturbs the healthy development of the woman; whether this conduct is verbal or non-verbal, that produces in the woman devaluation or suffering, through threats, demands for obedience or submission, coercion, guilt, or limitations of her sphere of freedom, and any alteration in her health that triggers the distortion of the concept of herself, of her value as a person, of her vision of the world or of her own affective capacities, exercised in INDICE LEGISLATIVO any type of relationship.
- e) Patrimonial Violence: They are actions, omissions or conducts that affect the free disposition of the woman's patrimony; including damages to common or own property through the transformation, removal, destruction, diversion, damage, loss, limitation, retention of objects, personal documents, goods, securities and patrimonial rights. Consequently, acts of fraudulent conveyance, simulation of alienation of movable or immovable property shall be null; whatever the patrimonial regime of the marriage may be, including that of non-marital unions.
- f) Sexual Violence: It is any conduct that threatens or violates the woman's right to voluntarily decide her sexual life, which includes not only the sexual act but any form of sexual contact or access, genital or non-genital, regardless of whether or not the aggressor has a marital, couple, social, work, affective or kinship relationship with the victim woman.
- g) Symbolic Violence: They are messages, values, icons or signs that transmit and reproduce relations of domination, inequality and discrimination in the social relations established between people and naturalize the subordination of women in society.

#### Article 10.- Modalities of Violence

For the purposes of this Law, the following are considered modalities of Violence:

- a) Community Violence: Any abusive action or omission that from individual or collective acts transgresses the fundamental rights of women and promotes their denigration, discrimination, marginalization or exclusion.
- b) Institutional Violence: It is any abusive action or omission by any public servant that discriminates or has the purpose of delaying, hindering or preventing the enjoyment and exercise of the fundamental rights and freedoms of women; as well as that which intends to hinder or hinders women's access to the enjoyment of public policies aimed at preventing, attending to, investigating, sanctioning and eradicating the manifestations, types and modalities of violence conceptualized in this Law.
- c) Labor Violence: They are actions or omissions against women, exercised repeatedly and maintained over time in public or private workplaces, that constitute physical or psychological aggressions against their integrity, personal and professional dignity, that hinder their access to employment, promotion or stability in the same, or that violate the right to equal pay for equal work.

#### Article 11.- Interpretation

This Law shall be interpreted and applied in accordance with the provisions of the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Punish and Eradicate Violence against Women, the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, the Convention on the Rights of the

### Chapter II

Rectorship

#### Article 12.- Governing Institution and its Purpose

The Salvadoran Institute for the Development of Women is the governing institution of this Law; and its purpose is:

- a) To ensure, monitor and guarantee the comprehensive compliance and execution of the Law.
- b) To coordinate the joint actions of public administration institutions for the compliance with the National Policy for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence.
- c) To formulate Public Policies for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence, to the State Bodies, Autonomous and Municipal Institutions.
- d) To convene in a consultative or coordinating capacity civil society organizations, universities, international and cooperation organizations.

#### Article 13.- Functions and Powers of the Salvadoran Institute for the Development of Women

In this Law the Salvadoran Institute for the Development of Women has the following powers:

- a) To prepare a framework policy that shall be the reference for the design of the public policies referred to in this Law.
- b) To present proposals to State institutions for Public Policies for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence.
- c) To approve, modify, monitor, evaluate and ensure compliance with the National Policy for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence, which is defined in this Law.
- d) To define strategies and manage in the event of a national or local emergency situation, in order to prevent and detect acts of violence against women.
- e) To render an annual report to the Legislative Body on the state and situation of violence against women in accordance with this Law and with the international commitments acquired in this matter.

#### Article 14.- Specialized Technical Commission

To guarantee the operationalization of this Law and that of the Public Policies for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence, the Specialized Technical Commission is created, whose coordination will be the responsibility of the Salvadoran Institute for Women's Development and will be composed of one representative from each institution that is part of the board of directors of said Institute, as well as one representative from the following institutions:

- a) Judicial Branch.
- b) Ministry of Finance.
- c) Ministry of Governance.
- d) Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
- e) Ministry of Economy.
- f) One person designated by the Presidency of the Republic.
- g) Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock.

#### Article 15.- Members of the Specialized Technical Commission

To be a member of the Specialized Technical Commission, the representatives of each of the institutions must meet the following profile:

- a) Demonstrable honorability.
- b) Not having been convicted of crimes in the last ten years.
- c) Specialization in matters of women's rights.
- d) Sensitivity in the respect and compliance with women's human rights.

The Functions of the Technical Commission shall be established based on work instructions formulated by the institutions that comprise it and must be in accordance with the National Policy for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence.

### Chapter III

National Policy for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence

#### Article 16.- National Policy for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence

The National Policy for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence, hereinafter National Policy, is the set of objectives and strategies of a public nature that has as its purpose to guarantee women's right to a life free of violence, through its prevention, detection, attention and protection. Its Action Plan will have a period of five years.

#### Article 17.- Contents of the National Policy for Women's Access to a Life Free

of Violence

The National Policy must contain programs of:

- a) Detection, which have as their purpose the early identification and targeting of the factors that originate acts of violence against women both in the public and private spheres, establishing detection models according to the types and modalities of violence contemplated in this Law.
- b) Prevention, which have as their purpose to prevent violence against women in any of its types and modalities, from the unlearning of conventional models that have historically been attributed to the image and concept of women, and the relearning of new models based on principles of equality, equity, diversity and democracy.
- c) Attention, which have as their purpose to attend to, protect and reestablish, in an expeditious and effective manner, the rights of direct and indirect victims of any type of violence exercised against women, both in the public and private spheres.
- d) Protection, which have as their purpose to attend to and favor in a comprehensive manner the rights of women victims of violence, whether or not they are in a situation of risk.
- e) Eradication of violence against women, which have as their purpose the destructuring of practices, conduct, norms and social and cultural customs that are detrimental to the identity, dignity and physical and emotional integrity of women, or that place them in conditions of vulnerability.
- f) Citizen security, through the design of strategies that promote safe public spaces for women, the creation of maps of territorial violence location, national and local citizen networks, as well as institutions that actively participate in the detection and prevention of violence against women.
- g) Training and capacity building, which facilitates labor insertion and income generation for women facing acts of violence.

h) Development of studies and research on violence against women at the national level.

Likewise, the National Policy, for its compliance and implementation, must contain programs of sensitization, knowledge and specialization for service personnel for the detection, prevention, attention and protection of cases of violence against women, as well as Protocols of Action and Coordination with the different State Institutions.

#### Article 19.- Citizen Participation

The mechanisms of citizen participation and representation at the national and local level must include within their regulations or rules, actions to eradicate violence against women in coherence with the National Policy.

#### Article 20.- Responsibilities in the Educational Sphere

The Ministry of Education through educational teaching-learning programs and processes, formal and non-formal, at the levels of education: preschool, basic, middle, higher and non-university, will include within the obligation it has to plan and regulate in a comprehensive manner the training of educators, as well as in curricular and extracurricular activities, the promotion of women's right to live free of violence and discrimination, as well as the dissemination of measures aimed at the prevention and eradication of any type of violence against women, fostering for this purpose relationships of respect, equality and promotion of women's human rights.

Likewise, they must eliminate from all educational programs the regulations, rules and materials that directly or indirectly promote any form of violence against women, the patterns of conduct, prejudices and stereotyped customs that promote, legitimize, naturalize, make invisible and justify violence against women, for which the Ministry of Education must guarantee that the contents of all materials circulating within the educational system comply with what is established in this Law.

#### Article 21.- Higher Education

#### Artículo 23.- Responsibilities of the Ministry of Public Health and Social Assistance

The Ministry of Public Health and Social Assistance shall be responsible for:

- a) Guaranteeing specific measures in the field of public health services for the prevention, early detection, attention and intervention in cases of violence against women.

### Chapter V

Of the Municipal Councils

#### Article 29.- Municipal Councils

The Municipal Councils, for the application of this Law, in accordance with the powers and attributions conferred by the Municipal Code, shall develop actions consistent with this Law and with the National Policy, such as:

- 1) Prepare every three years, the Municipal Plan for the Prevention and Care of Violence against Women, which shall comply with the provisions of the National Policy for Women's Access to a Life Free of Violence.
- 2) Convene and articulate local institutions and organizations, to generate coordination actions, exchange of information and collaboration for the fulfillment of their Municipal Plan.
- 3) Establish within their budget an earmarked item for the execution of their Municipal Plan and render an annual report thereon, to the citizens of their municipalities and to the Salvadoran Institute for the Development of Women.
- 4) Submit to the Ministry of Justice and Public Security, the data and statistics on cases of violence against women of which they have knowledge.

INDICE LEGISLATIVO

The Municipal Councils may not mediate or conciliate any type or modality of violence against women.

### Chapter VI

National System of Data, Statistics and Information on Violence Against Women

#### Article 30.- National System of Data and Statistics

The Ministry of Justice and Public Security shall be responsible for managing the National System of Data, Statistics and Information on violence against women, hereinafter National System of Data and Statistics; which shall coordinate with the General Directorate of Statistics and Censuses. Said Directorate shall be in charge of requesting and receiving information from other institutions that possess and process data, statistics or information on acts of violence against women.

The reports of said System shall contain:

- 1. System of indicators.
- 2. Evaluation of the impact of the policies that are developed for the eradication of any type of violence against women, and of the actions that are implemented, to guarantee comprehensive care to those who have faced it.
- 3. Data according to geographic location of occurrence of the act or acts; as well as the territorial origin, age, occupation, family status and level of education of the women who have faced acts of violence and of the aggressor.
- 4. Data on the acts attended to, such as types, scopes and modalities of violence against women, frequency, types of weapons or means used to execute the violence, measures granted and the history of the judicial process.
- 5. Effects caused by violence against women.
- 6. Data relating to the number of women who have faced acts of violence attended to in hospital, educational centers and services, work centers and recurrence from different sectors of the economy.
- 7. Referrals made to other instances.
- 8. Resources expended for the care of women who have faced acts of violence.
- 9. Others that are considered necessary.

The Ministry of Justice and Public Security shall annually publish the results of the systematization of data on acts of violence against women, through the presentation of reports in print and electronic media, which shall be available upon request by any natural or legal person who so requires.

INDICE LEGISLATIVO

#### Article 31.- Purpose and Formation of the National System of Data, Statistics and

Information on Violence Against Women

The purpose of the National System of Data and Statistics shall be to guarantee the national database on acts of violence against women, for which it shall collect and standardize the statistical data and information provided, for which purpose the Ministry of Justice and Public Security in coordination with the General Directorate of Statistics and Censuses, shall have the obligation to request relevant information from the corresponding Institutions; as well as to provide monthly the information that is requested.

#### Article 32.- Report on Indicators of Violence Against Women

The Institute of Legal Medicine shall annually present diagnostic indicators based on the expert examinations performed that shall include:

- a) The prevalence of cases of Feminicide.
- b) The effects of physical, psychological and sexual violence on women who face acts of violence.
- c) The effects of exposure to violence and of the aggressions suffered by the sons, daughters, girls, boys or adolescents, in the care of the woman who faces acts of violence.
- d) Assessment of the incidence, objective dangerousness and risk of recidivism of the aggressor.

### Chapter VII

Budget, Finances and Special Fund

#### Article 33.- Budget

The resources to finance this Law shall be the following:

- a) The allocations from the items of the General Budget of the Nation, which must be allocated each year or those resources earmarked for violence against women in any of its forms, to each of the public entities empowered by this Law.
- b) Those special funds designated for women victims of violence.
- c) National and international donations.
- d) Regional or international cooperation.
- e) Other sources of national or international financing.

INDICE LEGISLATIVO

#### Article 34.- Financing for the Application of this Law

The State through the Ministry of Finance, must guarantee for the execution of this Law the allocation of earmarked budget items in the General Budget of the Nation for each year, to each of the public institutions empowered in this Law for its application.

#### Article 35.- Special Fund for Women Victims of Violence

The funds obtained from economic sanctions imposed for violations committed to this law, shall be deposited into the General Fund of the Nation; and the Ministry of Finance, must transfer them entirely to finance those projects referred to in this Law.

#### Article 36.- Oversight of Funds

The Court of Accounts of the Republic shall be responsible for the subsequent oversight of the correct use of the funds allocated for the execution of this Law.

### Chapter VIII

Protection of Housing

#### Article 37.- Social Assistance and Subsidies

The social assistance or subsidies shall be compatible with any of those provided for in the laws in force with social programs; and shall come from the Special Fund for women victims of violence.

#### Article 38.- Access to Social Housing for Women

Women subject to this Law shall be considered priority groups in access to protected social housing and programs, under the terms determined by the legislation in force, assessing their circumstances and the context of lack of protection and vulnerability.

#### Article 39.- Protection of the Use of Rented Housing

In cases and acts of violence against women by their partner, and the latter rents the dwelling, the woman may continue with the use thereof by court order through the corresponding protection measure. The foregoing does not exempt from payment of the rent, to which the aggressor must be condemned.

Said measure shall be notified to the aggressor and to the landlord, so that the woman may use the dwelling for a maximum period of ninety days from the notification of the corresponding judicial resolution, accompanied by the copy of said judicial resolution or the part thereof, that affects the use of the dwelling to the landlord.

#### Article 40.- Access to Housing

The Ministry of Public Works, through the Vice Ministry of Housing and Urban Development, the Social Housing Fund (FSV), and the National Popular Housing Fund (FONAVIPO), must

INDICE LEGISLATIVO

develop a Housing Policy that progressively incorporates a reserve of specific housing for women who face acts of violence, and who are in total lack of protection and high-risk conditions. Priority shall be given to elderly women and women with disabilities.

#### Article 41.- Supervised Housing

Supervised housing consists of temporary housing spaces under the figure of housing in public protection for women who are in cycles of violence and who have established said situation.

The temporary housing spaces shall be guaranteed by the State; for which purpose, it must issue a Regulation that regulates the procedure so that women who establish the situation of violence can have access to supervised housing.

#### Article 42.- Certification of Complaint

The Institutions obligated by this Law shall guarantee to women who face acts of violence, the right to obtain the certification of complaint, which must be issued within the term established by Law.

The official who fails to comply with this obligation shall incur a penalty equivalent to ten minimum wages established for commerce and service workers in force, without prejudice to the corresponding criminal liability.

#### Article 43.- Establishment of the Situation of Violence

In cases where they require it, or where the establishment of the situation of violence against women is required for the recognition of their rights, this shall be accredited, without prejudice to what is established for each case, through:

- 1. Certification of judicial resolution for any type and form of violence.
- 2. Certification that accredits specialized care, by a competent public agency in matters of violence.

## Title II

Offenses and Sanctions

### Chapter I

Offenses and Sanctions

#### Article 44.- Public Action Offenses

All offenses contemplated in this chapter are public action offenses.

INDICE LEGISLATIVO

#### Article 45.- Feminicide

Whoever causes the death of a woman with motives of hatred or contempt for her condition as a woman, shall be punished with imprisonment of twenty to thirty-five years.

It is considered that hatred or contempt for the condition of woman exists when any of the following circumstances occur:

- a) That the death was preceded by some incident of violence committed by the perpetrator against the woman, regardless of whether the act was reported or not by the victim.
- b) That the perpetrator had taken advantage of any condition of risk or physical or psychological vulnerability in which the woman victim was.
- c) That the perpetrator had taken advantage of the superiority generated by unequal power relations based on gender.
- d) That prior to the death of the woman the perpetrator had committed against her any conduct classified as an offense against sexual freedom.
- e) Death preceded by reason of mutilation.

#### Article 46.- Aggravated Feminicide

The offense of feminicide shall be punished with a sentence of thirty to fifty years of imprisonment, in the following cases:

- a) If committed by a public or municipal official or employee, public authority or agent of authority.
- b) If committed by two or more persons.
- c) If committed in front of any relative of the victim.
- d) When the victim is under eighteen years of age, elderly or suffers from physical or mental disability.
- e) If the perpetrator took advantage of the superiority originating from relationships of trust, friendship, domestic, educational or work relationships.

#### Article 47.- Obstruction of Access to Justice

Whoever in the exercise of a public function promotes, furthers or tolerates, impunity or obstructs the investigation, prosecution and punishment of the offenses established in this Law, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to four years and disqualification from the public function performed for the same period.

INDICE LEGISLATIVO

#### Artículo 48.- Feminicidal Suicide by Inducement or Assistance

Whoever induces a woman to commit suicide or provides her with assistance to commit it, taking advantage of any of the following circumstances, shall be punished with imprisonment of five to seven years:

- a) That any of the types or modalities of violence contemplated in this Law or in any other Law precedes it.
- b) That the accused took advantage of any risk situation or physical or psychological condition in which the victim was found, due to any of the types or modalities of violence contemplated in this Law or in any other Law having been exercised against her.
- c) That the inducer took advantage of the superiority generated by pre-existing or existing relationships between him and the victim.

#### Artículo 49.- Inducement, Promotion and Favoring of Sexual or Erotic Acts by Computer

or Electronic Means

Whoever individually, collectively or in an organized manner publishes, distributes, sends, promotes, facilitates, administers, finances or organizes, in any way the use of women over eighteen years of age, without their consent in sexual or erotic acts, using computer or electronic means, shall be punished with imprisonment of five to ten years.

#### Artículo 50.- Illegal Dissemination of Information

Whoever publishes, shares, sends or distributes personal information that damages the honor, personal and family privacy, and the image of the woman without her consent, shall be punished with a penalty of one to three years.

#### Artículo 51.- Dissemination of Pornography

Whoever publishes, shares, sends or distributes pornographic material by any computer or electronic means in which the image or identity of the woman is used without her consent, shall be punished with a penalty of three to five years.

#### Artículo 52.- Favoring Non-Compliance with Economic Assistance Duties

Whoever being obliged to report on the income of those who must comply with economic assistance duties, conceals or gives false or late information, or fails to comply with an order from a judicial or administrative authority, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years, and a fine equivalent to thirty minimum wages in commerce and services.

#### Artículo 53.- Patrimonial Subtraction

Whoever subtracts any property or asset from the possession or patrimony of a woman with whom he maintains a relationship of kinship, marriage or cohabitation without her consent, shall be punished with imprisonment

INDICE LEGISLATIVO

of two to four years.

#### Artículo 54.- Subtraction of Profits from Family Economic Activities

Whoever subtracts the profits or income derived from a family economic activity, or disposes of them for his personal benefit and to the detriment of the rights of a woman with whom he maintains a relationship of kinship, marriage or cohabitation, declared or not, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to six years.

#### Artículo 55.- Expressions of Violence Against Women

Whoever engages in any of the following conducts shall be punished with a fine of two to twenty-five minimum wages in commerce and services:

- a) Elaborate, publish, disseminate or transmit by any means, images or visual, audiovisual, multimedia messages or computer platforms with content of hatred or contempt towards women.
- b) Use verbal or non-verbal expressions relating to the exercise of parental authority that are intended to intimidate women.
- c) Mock, discredit, degrade or isolate women within their work, educational, community environments, spaces for political or civic participation, institutional or other similar environments as a form of expression of discrimination in accordance with this Law.
- d) Prevent, limit or obstruct the participation of women in any process of academic training, political participation, labor insertion or health care.
- e) Expose women to imminent risk to their physical or emotional integrity.
- f) Display or share pornography of persons of legal age in public, work and community spaces.

#### 1) Article 57.- Procedural Guarantees for Women Facing Acts of Violence

Women who face acts of violence shall be guaranteed:

- a) That their intimacy and privacy be preserved at all times. Consequently, their sexual life may not be exposed directly or indirectly to justify, minimize, or relativize the harm caused.
- b) That they be provided with a copy of the prosecutorial request, the administrative complaint, the legal medical examination, and any other document of interest to the woman facing acts of violence; as well as being treated with dignity and respect, especially by the parties involved in the process.
- c) To be attended, to the extent possible, by persons of the same sex who are experts and trained in victims' rights, women's human rights, gender perspective, and prevention of gender-based violence, in accessible places that guarantee privacy, security, and comfort.
- d) Not to be discriminated against on the basis of their sexual history or for any other reason.
- e) That their privacy be duly protected and total or partial confidentiality of the file be applied to prevent the disclosure of information that could lead to their identification or that of their family members, maintaining the confidentiality of information about their residence, telephone, place of work or study, among other aspects. Such protection includes their family and close relatives.
- f) TO BE INFORMED AND NOTIFIED IN A TIMELY AND TRUTHFUL MANNER OF THE ACTIONS BEING TAKEN THROUGHOUT THE JUDICIAL OR ADMINISTRATIVE PROCESS, AS WELL AS THE PERTINENT REMEDIES AND ASSISTANCE SERVICES. (1)
- g) To receive comprehensive, adequate, and timely assistance, which may exceed the duration of the administrative or judicial process, regardless of the outcome.
- h) To receive medical care, adequate and specialized treatment, in cases that warrant it. As well as the use of the Protocol for care in cases of sexual violence to prevent Sexually Transmitted Infections and the Technical Guide for Family Planning Care.
- i) To designate a companion throughout the judicial or administrative process.
- j) Not to be coerced for statements made during the process.
