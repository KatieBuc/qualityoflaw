The prevention and combating of domestic violence are part of the national policy for the protection and support of the family and represent an important public health issue. For the consolidation, protection and support of the family, for ensuring compliance with the fundamental principles of legislation on the family, recognizing that the achievement of gender equality between women and men is a key element in the prevention of violence against women, that domestic violence disproportionately affects women, but also that children and men can also be victims of violence, the Parliament adopts this organic law.

## Chapter I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1. Object of the law

This law establishes the legal and organizational bases of the activity for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, the authorities and institutions empowered with functions of prevention and combating of domestic violence, the mechanism for reporting and resolving cases of violence.

### Article 2. Main notions

For the purposes of this law, the following notions mean: domestic violence – acts of physical, sexual, psychological, spiritual or economic violence, with the exception of actions of self-defense or defense of another person, including the threat of such acts, committed by a family member against another member of the same family, which caused material or moral harm to the victim; physical violence - intentional harm to bodily integrity or health through beating, pushing, throwing, hair pulling, pricking, cutting, burning, strangulation, biting, in any form and of any intensity, through poisoning, intoxication, other actions with similar effect; sexual violence - any violence of a sexual nature or any illegal sexual conduct within the family or in other interpersonal relationships, such as marital rape, prohibition of the use of contraceptive methods, sexual harassment; any unwanted, imposed sexual conduct; forced prostitution; any illegal sexual behavior in relation to a minor family member, including through caressing, kissing, posing of the child and through other unwanted touches of a sexual nature; other actions with similar effect; psychological violence – imposition of will or personal control; causing states of tension and psychological suffering through offenses, ridicule, abuse, insult, nicknaming, blackmail, demonstrative destruction of objects, verbal threats, ostentatious display of weapons or beating of domestic animals, neglect; interference in personal life; acts of jealousy; imposition of isolation through detention, including in the family home; isolation from family, community, friends; prohibition and/or creation of impediments in professional achievement or prohibition and/or creation of impediments in the completion of the educational program in the educational institution; persecution through contacting or attempting to contact by any means or through another person the victim who has been caused a state of anxiety, fear for their own safety or that of close relatives, being forced to modify their life conduct; dispossession of identity documents; intentional deprivation of access to information; other actions with similar effect;

[Art.2 notion as worded by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force spiritual violence - underestimation or diminution of the importance of satisfying moral-spiritual needs through prohibition, limitation, ridicule, penalization of the aspirations of family members, through prohibition, limitation, ridicule or punishment of access to cultural, ethnic, linguistic or religious values; imposition of a personally unacceptable value system; other actions with similar effect or with similar repercussions; economic violence - deprivation of economic means, including deprivation of primary means of subsistence, such as food, medicines, basic necessities; abuse of various situations of superiority to steal the person's goods; prohibition of the right to own, use and dispose of common goods; inequitable control over common goods and resources; refusal to support the family; imposition of heavy and harmful work to the detriment of health, including of a minor family member; other actions with similar effect; violence against women – acts of gender-based violence that cause or may cause women physical, sexual or psychological suffering, including the threat of such acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, committed in the public or private sphere;

[Art.2 notion introduced by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force adult victim – adult person who has been subjected to acts of domestic violence, in cohabitation;

[Art.2 notion introduced by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force child victim – person who has not reached the age of 18 years, who has been subjected to or has witnessed acts of domestic violence, in cohabitation;

[Art.2 notion introduced by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force moral harm – causing physical and/or psychological suffering, which leads to humiliation, fear, degradation, inability to defend against violence, feelings of frustration and other consequences of a similar nature; material harm - material damages, susceptible to financial/pecuniary evaluation or estimation, resulting from any act of domestic violence, in cohabitation, as well as the costs for the investigation of cases of domestic violence; aggressor - person who commits acts of domestic violence, in cohabitation. In case of complaints from several family members, with reciprocal accusations of committing acts of violence, the aggressor will be identified taking into consideration the following circumstances: which of the subjects involved acted in defense of themselves or another person; the probability of critical situations occurring for each person; the manner of action in the violence scene and the severity of injuries caused by each person; previous complaints of domestic violence, other circumstances that indicate the initiator of acts of violence;

### Articolul 3. Subjects of domestic violence

(1) Subjects of domestic violence are the aggressor and the victim who are members of the same family.

(2) Family members, within the meaning of this law, are:

- a) in the condition of cohabitation: persons in marriage, in divorce, under guardianship and trusteeship, in respect of whom a judicial protection measure has been instituted, relatives, their in-laws, spouses of relatives, persons in relationships similar to those between spouses (cohabitation) or between parents and children;

[Art.3 al.(2), lit.a) modificată prin LP113 din 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; în vigoare 07.09.20]

b) in the condition of separate residence: persons in marriage, in divorce, relatives, their in-laws, adopted children, persons under trusteeship, in respect of whom a judicial protection measure has been instituted, persons who are or have been in relationships similar to those between spouses

(cohabitation).

[Art.3 al.(2), lit.b) modificată prin LP113 din 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; în vigoare 07.09.20]

### Articolul 4. Legislative framework

(1) The legislation regarding the prevention and combating of domestic violence consists of the Constitution of the Republic of Moldova, this law, other normative acts, and international treaties to which the Republic of Moldova is a party.

(2) If an international treaty to which the Republic of Moldova is a party establishes other norms than this law, the norms of the international treaty shall apply.

### Articolul 5. Basic principles of prevention and combating of domestic violence

The prevention and combating of domestic violence is carried out on the following principles:

- a) legality;
- b) equality and non-discrimination;
- c) confidentiality;
- d) access to justice;
- e) protection and security of the victim;
- f) cooperation of public administration authorities with civil society and international organizations.

### Articolul 6. Scope of the law

The provisions of this law apply equally to citizens of the Republic of Moldova, foreign citizens and stateless persons residing on its territory.

## Chapter III

MECHANISM FOR RESOLVING ACTS

OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 11. Right to protection of the victim

(1) The victim is guaranteed the defense of legitimate rights and interests.

(2) Persons in positions of responsibility, other persons who know of the existence of a danger to the life and health of a potential victim must communicate this fact to the authorities empowered with functions of preventing and combating domestic violence.

(21) The victim has the right to assistance for physical, psychological and social recovery through special medical, psychological, legal and social actions. The provision of protection and assistance services is not conditioned on the victim's desire to make statements and participate in prosecution processes against the aggressor. The right to private life and confidentiality of information regarding the victim are guaranteed.

(3) Authorities empowered with functions of preventing and combating domestic violence are obliged to react promptly to any notification and to inform victims about their rights, about the authorities and institutions with functions of preventing and combating domestic violence; about the type of services and organizations they can turn to for help; about the assistance available to them; where and how they can file a complaint; about the procedure that follows after filing a complaint and their role in such procedures; how they can obtain protection; to what extent and under what conditions they have access to counseling or legal assistance; whether there is a danger to their life or health in case of release of a detained or convicted person; whether the protection order has been cancelled.

(4) The victim of domestic violence has the right to demand from the aggressor reparation, under the conditions of the law, of the material and moral damage caused by acts of violence, and from the state – reparation of material and moral damage as a result of failure to provide adequate assistance and protection.

(41) The victim of domestic violence who has suffered serious injury to bodily integrity or health benefits from financial compensation from the state for the damage caused by the crime, under the conditions established by legislation.

(5) The victim has the right to free primary and qualified legal assistance in accordance with the legislation on state-guaranteed legal assistance.

(6) The victim is provided medical assistance by health care institutions in accordance with the Law on Compulsory Health Care Insurance. The bodily examination of the victim and the completion of forensic medical reports on the severity of injury to bodily integrity or health are performed free of charge in cases provided by law.

(7) The private life and identity of the victim are protected. The recording, storage and use of personal data concerning the victim are carried out in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Protection of Personal Data.

### Article 12. Notification of cases of domestic violence

(1) The victim has the right to report any case of domestic violence and to request protection.

(2) Representatives of authorities empowered with functions of preventing and combating domestic violence are prohibited from undertaking actions aimed at discouraging the victim from reporting acts of violence to which they are subjected.

(3) Any person who witnesses acts of domestic violence or who has reasonable suspicion that such acts have been committed or that acts of violence are possible has the right to communicate this to authorities empowered with functions of preventing and combating domestic violence.

(4) Persons in positions of responsibility and professionals who are required to ensure confidentiality are obliged to report to competent authorities about acts of domestic violence that endanger the life or health of the victim or about the imminent danger of such acts of violence occurring. In other cases, reporting will be carried out only with the victim's consent.

(41) Reporting cases of violence against children, including reasonable suspicion of violence against children, is mandatory and does not need to be carried out with the victim's consent. [Art.12 para.(41) introduced by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force

(5) The police are obliged to respond immediately to communications about cases of domestic violence and not to underestimate the importance of actions to counteract any forms of domestic violence.

### Article 121. Issuance of the emergency restraining order

(1) In case of establishment at the scene, following risk assessment, of circumstances from which a reasonable suspicion results that acts of domestic violence have been committed and/or there persists an imminent danger of repetition or commission of violent actions, the police body is obliged to immediately order the issuance of the emergency restraining order against the aggressor to remove the crisis situation and, concomitantly, undertake the necessary actions to establish the commission of the domestic violence offense.

(11) The police body has the right to apply against the aggressor one or more of the following measures:

- a) obligation to temporarily leave the common dwelling or to stay away from the victim's dwelling;
- b) prohibition from approaching the victim, respecting the distance that would ensure their security and excluding any visual contact with the victim and/or children;
- c) prohibition of any contact, including by telephone or by any other means of communication, with the victim and/or children;
- d) prohibition from keeping and carrying weapons.

[Art.121 para.(11) introduced by LP113 of 09.07.20, MO199-204/07.08.20 art.400; in force

(2) The emergency restraining order is issued for a period of up to 10 days and is immediately put into effect, the aggressor and the victim (in the case of children – the legal representative of the victim) being informed about the applied restrictions, the rights and obligations incumbent upon them and about the liability for non-execution of the requirements of the restraining order.

(3) Supervision of the fulfillment of the measures established in the emergency restraining order is exercised by the employees of the police station or police sector in the locality in the manner provided by law.

(4) The aggressor has the right to challenge the emergency restraining order in court under the conditions of administrative litigation. Filing the application does not suspend the action of the restraining order.

(5) The victim has the right, during the period of action of the emergency restraining order, to request, under the conditions of the law, the issuance of the protection order. The action of the emergency restraining order, in this case, is extended by right and ceases upon the implementation of the protection measures established by the court.

### Article 13. Filing the application

(1) The application regarding the commission of acts of domestic violence is filed:

- a) at the police bodies;
- b) in court;
- c) at the social assistance and family and child protection body;
- d) at the local public administration authority.
  - (2) The application is filed at the place:
- a) of domicile of the victim;
- b) of temporary residence of the victim if they left the domicile to avoid the continuation of direct violence;
- c) of residence of the aggressor;
- d) where the victim sought assistance;
- e) where the act of violence took place.
  - (21) The application for the issuance of the protection order is filed by the victim personally or through a representative. In case of impossibility of filing the application by the victim due to health, age, other well-founded reasons, at their request, the application for the issuance of the protection order may be filed, in the interests of the victim, by the police body, the social assistance body or by the prosecutor. The application for the issuance of the protection order in the interests of the child or of the person in respect of whom a judicial guardianship measure is instituted may be filed by the local guardianship authority or by the prosecutor even in the absence of a request from the victim or their legal representative.
  - (3) For filing in court the application for issuance of the protection order, no state fee is paid.

### Article 14. Manner of examining the application

(1) The application filed with the police body is examined according to the procedure provided by the legislation in force.

(2) The application or request for the issuance of the protection order filed in court is examined according to the Code of Civil Procedure, the Code of Criminal Procedure.

(3) The application filed with any authority empowered with functions of prevention and combating of domestic violence is redirected according to competence within one working day.

### Article 15. Protection Measures

- b) obligation to stay away from the victim's location, at a distance that would ensure their safety, also excluding any visual contact with the victim or their children, with other persons dependent on them;

e) obligation, until the cessation of protection measures, to contribute to the maintenance of the children they have in common with the victim;

### Article 151. Revocation of Protection Measures

(1) At the substantiated request of the victim, the court may revoke the established protection measures before the deadline.

(2) The revocation may be ordered if the following conditions are cumulatively met:

- a) the aggressor has respected the established prohibitions and obligations;
- b) the aggressor has undergone/is undergoing the counseling or treatment that was established for them.
  - (3) The court shall examine whether the victim's will is freely expressed, ensuring that they have had real access to assistance and protection.
  - (4) The revocation request shall be resolved with the summoning of the parties and the representative of the police who exercised the supervision of the execution of the protection order whose revocation is requested.

### Article 152. Supervision of the Execution of the Emergency Restraining Order and the

Protection Order

(1) The supervision of the execution of protection measures for victims of domestic violence, imposed through the emergency restraining order and through the protection order, is within the competence of the police.

(2) The police shall inform and explain to the aggressor the established victim protection measures.

(3) In case the aggressor must leave the family dwelling, the police must require them to immediately hand over all keys to the dwelling, which are subsequently transmitted to the victim or are taken for temporary custody at the police authority. Upon leaving the dwelling, the aggressor may take, under police supervision, only strictly necessary personal use items (clothes, documents, hygiene items).

(4) If the aggressor refuses to voluntarily leave the dwelling, the police authority has the right to undertake the necessary measures, under the conditions of the law, to overcome the resistance opposed to legal requirements.

(5) During the period of validity of the victim protection measures, the police authority shall ensure the carrying out of unannounced visits to the victim's dwelling, as well as immediate intervention upon any notification about non-compliance or attempted violation of the protection measures by the aggressor.

(6) The refusal or evasion of the aggressor from executing the requirements of the emergency restraining order/protection order shall entail liability according to the law.

### Article 153. Assessment and Management of Risks

of Recurrence/Commission of Acts of Domestic Violence

(1) The process of assessment and management of risks of recurrence/commission of acts of domestic violence is intended for response with immediate and adequate measures aimed at ensuring the protection of the victim or potential victim following the actions or potential actions of the aggressor.

(2) The organization and conduct of the assessment and management of the risk of recurrence/commission of acts of domestic violence take place in the manner and conditions established by the internal procedures of the competent authority and/or by joint orders.

### Article 16. Financing

(1) The activities for implementing this law shall be financed from the state budget, from the budgets of administrative-territorial units, within the limit of financial means established annually, from other sources not prohibited by law.

(2) The financing of centers/services for assistance and protection of victims of domestic violence and their children and of centers/services for assistance and counseling for family aggressors shall be carried out from allocations provided in the state budget, from donations, grants, from other sources, according to the legislation in force. In case of limited resources, priority shall be given to the allocation of funds for supporting centers/services for assistance and protection of victims of domestic violence.

### Article 17. Sanctions

(2) In case of committing acts of domestic violence that meet the constituent elements of a crime, liability arises under the conditions of the law, regardless of the establishment of protective measures

(3) Non-compliance with this law by persons with responsibility functions and professionals in the field is a breach of labor discipline and entails disciplinary liability in accordance with the legislation.

## Chapter IV

FINAL PROVISIONS
