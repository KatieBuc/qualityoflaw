## Article 1.

This Act prescribes the rights of victims of domestic violence, the circle of persons to whom the Act applies, determines the forms of domestic violence, misdemeanour sanctions for protection from domestic violence, the collection of data on the application of the Act, the establishment of the Commission for Monitoring and Improving the Work of Criminal and Misdemeanour Procedure Bodies and the Enforcement of Sanctions Related to Protection from Domestic Violence, and misdemeanour provisions.

5. Protocol on Procedure in Cases of Sexual Violence

## Article 2.

This Act transposes into the legal order of the Republic of Croatia Directive 2012/29/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 establishing minimum standards on the rights, support and protection of victims of crime, and replacing Council Framework Decision 2001/220/JHA (OJ L 315, 14.11.2012.).

## Article 3.

The provisions of the law governing misdemeanours and criminal procedure shall apply accordingly, unless otherwise prescribed by this Act.

## Article 4.

All bodies acting in cases of domestic violence are obliged to act urgently and all proceedings initiated under this Act are urgent.

## Article 5.

(1) All bodies acting in cases of domestic violence are obliged to act with particular consideration towards the victim of domestic violence and to care for the victim's rights in an appropriate manner when undertaking actions.

(2) All bodies acting in cases of domestic violence are obliged to act with particular consideration towards a child victim of domestic violence, taking into account his or her age, personality and personal and family circumstances, in order to avoid harmful consequences for the upbringing and development of the child. When acting towards a child victim of domestic violence, the competent bodies shall be primarily guided by the best interests of the child.

(3) Bodies acting in cases of domestic violence are obliged to respect the dignity of a person with a disability and an elderly person as a victim of violence and to respect the particularities arising from their disability or age.

## Article 6.

(1) A victim of domestic violence has the following rights:

- 1. the right to access services for the support of victims of domestic violence
- 2. the right to effective psychological and other professional assistance and support from bodies, organisations or institutions for the assistance of victims of domestic violence
- 3. the right to protection from intimidation and retaliation
- 4. the right to protection of dignity during the examination of the victim as a witness
- 5. the right to be accompanied by a person of trust during all actions in which they participate
- 6. the right to be informed, upon their request, without undue delay, of the lifting of detention or escape of the accused and the annulment of decisions imposing protective measures and the lifting of precautionary measures that have been determined for their protection or the release of the convicted person from serving a prison sentence
- 7. the right to confidentiality of data whose disclosure could endanger their safety or the safety of persons referred to in Article 8 paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Act and the right to request the exclusion of the public in court proceedings
- 8. the right to legal representation in proceedings
- 9. the right, upon their own request, to be informed of actions taken following the report and of the outcome of the proceedings
- 10. the right to be examined without unjustified delay after filing a report, the right to request to be examined in court proceedings and the right to have further examinations conducted only to the extent necessary for the purposes of the misdemeanour proceedings
- 11. the right to be examined at the police station by a person of the same sex
- 12. the right to avoid contact with the perpetrator before and during the proceedings, unless the misdemeanour proceedings require such contact
- 13. the right to temporary accommodation in an appropriate institution in accordance with a special law
- 14. the right to police protection and security, by court order, for the purpose of unhindered collection of personal belongings when leaving the common household
- 15. other rights prescribed by the law governing criminal procedure, except those rights which by their nature can only be held by a victim of a criminal offence.
  - (2) Bodies acting in cases of domestic violence are obliged to inform the victim, in a manner understandable to them, of all the rights they have in accordance with the provisions of this Act and the law governing criminal procedure, already during the first action in which the victim participates.
  - (3) The bodies referred to in paragraph 2 of this Article are obliged to ensure that the victim has understood the information given about their rights.
  - (4) In cases where it is necessary to examine a child victim of domestic violence, the bodies referred to in paragraph 2 of this Article are obliged to examine the child victim by applying the provisions of the law governing criminal procedure on the special method of examining children.
  - (5) If a child is a victim of domestic violence and the child's interests are contrary to the interests of the parents, the competent body shall call upon the competent social welfare body to appoint a special guardian. The special guardian is authorised to make all statements and take all actions that the victim is authorised to make. By way of exception, a child victim of domestic violence who has reached the age of 16 may independently make statements and take actions in the proceedings.
  - (6) If the victim of domestic violence is a child, the notifications referred to in paragraph 1 points 6 and 9 of this Article shall be given ex officio.

## Article 7.

(1) Health care workers, employees in social welfare institutions, persons employed in educational institutions, professionals employed in religious institutions, humanitarian organisations or civil society organisations and all other professional persons who come into contact with victims of domestic violence in their work are obliged to report to the police or the State Attorney's Office the commission of domestic violence of which they became aware in the performance of their duties.

(2) Bodies acting in cases of domestic violence are obliged to inform the competent social welfare centre without delay of the facts and circumstances that contributed to or favoured the commission of domestic violence, in order to take measures within the competence of the social welfare centre.

## Article 8.

(1) The persons to whom this Act applies are: spouse, extramarital partner, life partner, informal life partner, their common children and the children of each of them, relatives by blood in the direct line, relatives in the collateral line up to and including the third degree, relatives by affinity up to and including the second degree, adoptive parent and adoptee.

(2) The provisions of this Act also apply to a former spouse, former extramarital partner, former life partner, former informal life partner, current or former partner in an intimate relationship, persons who have a common child and persons living in a common household.

(3) Persons with disabilities and elderly persons referred to in paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article as victims of domestic violence enjoy special protection under this Act.

(4) An extramarital partner is a person living in an extramarital union that is of a more permanent nature or that lasts for a shorter period of time if a common child was born in it.

(5) An informal life partner within the meaning of this Act is a person living in a same-sex union that is of a more permanent nature.

(6) A child is a person who has not reached the age of eighteen.

(7) A victim of domestic violence is a person who, as a result of the commission of domestic violence, suffers physical or psychological consequences, property damage or a substantial violation of fundamental rights and freedoms.

(8) A person of trust is the legal representative or another adult person chosen by the victim of domestic violence, unless proposed or called as a witness.

(9) A person with a disability is a person who has long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairments which in interaction with various barriers may hinder their full and effective participation in society on an equal basis with others.

(10) An elderly person is a person aged 65 years or older.

## Article 9.

Terms used in this Act that have gender meaning are used neutrally and apply equally to the male and female sex.

## Article 10. (NN 126/19)

Domestic violence is:

- 1. application of physical force as a result of which no bodily injury occurred
- 2. corporal punishment or other forms of degrading treatment of children
- 3. psychological violence that has caused injury to dignity or distress in the victim
- 4. sexual harassment
- 5. economic violence as prohibition or prevention of using joint or personal property, disposing of personal income or property acquired through personal work or inheritance, prevention of employment, withholding funds for maintaining the joint household and for child care
- 6. neglect of the needs of a person with disability or an elderly person that leads to their distress or offends their dignity and thereby causes them physical or mental suffering.

Types and purpose of misdemeanor sanctions for protection from domestic violence

## Article 11.

(1) Misdemeanor sanctions for protection from domestic violence are protective measures, fines, imprisonment and other misdemeanor sanctions prescribed by the law governing misdemeanors.

(2) The purpose of misdemeanor sanctions is the protection of family members exposed to violence, respect for the legal system, and prevention of repeated commission of domestic violence through appropriate sanctioning of the perpetrator of the misdemeanor.

Protective measures

## Article 12.

(1) The purpose of protective measures is to prevent domestic violence, ensure protection of the health and safety of the victim of domestic violence, and eliminate circumstances that favor or have an encouraging effect on the commission of a new misdemeanor, and they are imposed in order to eliminate the endangerment of the victim of domestic violence.

(2) Protective measures may be imposed independently and without imposing a penalty or other misdemeanor sanction.

(3) Protective measures may be imposed ex officio, on the proposal of an authorized prosecutor, the victim, or the social welfare center.

(4) On the proposal of the victim or another authorized prosecutor, the court may, even before the expiration of the time for which the protective measure was imposed, review the justification for the further duration of the imposed protective measure and, if necessary, may replace the imposed protective measure with another or revoke it.

Types of protective measures

## Article 13.

The court may impose on the perpetrator of domestic violence, in addition to the protective measures prescribed by the Misdemeanor Act, the following protective measures:

- 1. mandatory psychosocial treatment
- 2. prohibition of approaching, harassing or stalking the victim of domestic violence
- 3. removal from the joint household
- 4. mandatory treatment for addiction.

## Article 14.

(1) The protective measures from Article 13, items 2 and 3 of this Act may be imposed by the court before initiating misdemeanor proceedings on the proposal of the victim or another authorized prosecutor if there is a direct danger to the safety of the victim or members of their family or a member of the joint household.

(2) The decision from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be made by the court without delay, and at the latest within twenty-four hours from the submission of the proposal. The court shall make the decision after hearing the victim and the person against whom the imposition of the protective measure is requested. An appeal does not postpone the execution of the decision.

(3) The decision from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be delivered by the court without delay to the competent police station for the implementation of the imposed protective measure.

(4) The decision from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be revoked by the court if the victim or another authorized prosecutor does not submit an indictment proposal within eight days from the date of the decision, of which they are obliged to warn the victim. The court shall inform the police without delay of the revocation of the protective measure.

Mandatory psychosocial treatment

## Article 15.

(1) The protective measure of mandatory psychosocial treatment may be imposed on the perpetrator of domestic violence in order to eliminate their violent behavior or if there is a danger that they could repeat domestic violence.

(2) The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article may be determined for a duration of at least six months.

(3) The minister responsible for judicial affairs shall prescribe by regulation the manner and place of implementation of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article.

Prohibition of approaching, harassing or stalking the victim

## Article 16.

(1) The protective measure of prohibition of approaching, harassing or stalking the victim may be imposed on the perpetrator of domestic violence if there is a danger that the perpetrator could repeat domestic violence against that person.

(2) In the decision by which the court imposes the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article, places or areas and the distance below which the perpetrator must not approach the victim of domestic violence shall be determined, or harassment or stalking of the victim shall be prohibited.

(3) The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article may not be shorter than one month nor longer than two years.

(4) The decision by which the court imposes the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be delivered by the court without delay to the competent police station for monitoring the implementation of the imposed protective measure.

(5) The minister responsible for internal affairs shall prescribe by regulation the manner of implementation of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article. Removal from the joint household

## Article 17.

(1) The protective measure of removal from the joint household may be imposed on the perpetrator of domestic violence who committed the violence against a family member with whom they live in an apartment, house or some other residential space that constitutes a joint household if there is a danger that they could repeat domestic violence.

(2) The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article may not be shorter than one month nor longer than two years.

(3) The decision by which the court imposes the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be delivered by the court without delay to the competent police station for monitoring the implementation of the imposed protective measure.

(4) The minister responsible for internal affairs shall prescribe by regulation the manner of implementation of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article. Mandatory treatment for addiction

## Article 18.

(1) The protective measure of mandatory treatment for addiction may be imposed on the perpetrator of domestic violence who committed the violence under the influence of addiction to alcohol, drugs or another type of addiction if there is a danger that they will repeat domestic violence due to that addiction.

(2) The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article is determined for a duration that may not be longer than one year.

(3) The minister responsible for health affairs shall prescribe by regulation the manner of implementation of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article.

Compliance with protective measures

## Article 19.

(1) The perpetrator of domestic violence is obliged to comply with the imposed protective measure.

(2) Persons from Article 7, paragraph 1 of this Act who, in the performance of their duties, learn that the perpetrator of domestic violence is not complying with the imposed protective measure are obliged to inform the police thereof.

## Article 20. (NN 126/19)

(1) The ministry responsible for judicial affairs, the ministry responsible for social welfare affairs, the ministry responsible for internal affairs, the ministry responsible for health affairs, and the ministry responsible for education affairs are obliged to collect data on the application of this Act and the Criminal Code and, based on the collected data, prepare annual reports from their scope of activities and submit them to the Commission from Article 21 of this Act by the end of March of the current year for the previous year.

(2) The minister responsible for judicial affairs shall prescribe by regulation the manner of collection, processing and delivery of statistical data and reports compiled based on the collected data.

## Članak 21. (NN 126/19)

(1) The Commission for Monitoring and Improving the Work of Bodies in Criminal and Misdemeanor Proceedings and the Execution of Sanctions Related to Protection from Domestic Violence (hereinafter: the Commission) shall be established at the ministry responsible for judicial affairs.

(2) The Commission shall have 11 members who shall be elected from among judges, state attorneys, lawyers, civil servants of the ministry responsible for internal affairs, civil servants of the ministry responsible for judicial affairs, civil servants of the ministry responsible for social welfare, civil servants of the ministry responsible for health, civil servants of the ministry responsible for education, and representatives of civil society.

(3) The Commission shall collect reports from competent bodies, in accordance with Article 20, paragraph 1 of this Act, review the submitted reports and monitor the situation based on them. Based on the collected reports and other necessary data, the Commission shall prepare an annual report on the implementation of this Act and provide proposals and opinions in relation to the implementation of this Act and the Criminal Code.

(4) The Commission shall submit the annual report to the ministry responsible for judicial affairs, and it shall then be published on the website of the ministry responsible for judicial affairs by the end of June of the current year for the previous year.

(5) The president and members of the Commission shall be appointed by decision of the minister responsible for judicial affairs for a term of four years.

(6) The minister responsible for judicial affairs shall adopt the rules of procedure for the work of the Commission.

(7) The coordination of the work of the Commission and administrative tasks for the Commission shall be carried out by the ministry responsible for judicial affairs.

## Članak 22. (NN 126/19, 114/22)

(1) Whoever commits violence from Article 10 of this Act shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine in the amount of at least 260.00 euros or imprisonment of up to 90 days.

(2) Whoever repeats the violence from paragraph 1 of this Article shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine in the amount of at least 790.00 euros or imprisonment for a term of at least 30 days.

(3) Whoever commits violence from Article 10 of this Act in the presence of a child or a person with a disability or an elderly person shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine in the amount of at least 920.00 euros or imprisonment for a term of at least 45 days.

(4) Whoever repeats domestic violence from paragraph 3 of this Article shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine in the amount of at least 1060.00 euros or imprisonment for a term of at least 60 days.

(5) If the violence from paragraph 1 of this Article was committed against a child or a person with a disability or an elderly person, the perpetrator shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine in the amount of at least 1590.00 euros or imprisonment for a term of at least 70 days.

(6) Whoever repeats domestic violence from paragraph 5 of this Article shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine in the amount of at least 2250.00 euros or imprisonment for a term of at least 80 days.

## Članak 23. (NN 126/19, 114/22)

The persons from Article 7, paragraph 1 of this Act who do not report to the police or the state attorney's office the commission of domestic violence of which they became aware in the performance of their duties shall be punished with a fine in the amount of at least 390.00 euros to 1320.00 euros.

## Članak 24. (NN 114/22)

A perpetrator of domestic violence who does not act in accordance with the imposed protective measure shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine in the amount of at least 390.00 euros or imprisonment of at least ten days.
