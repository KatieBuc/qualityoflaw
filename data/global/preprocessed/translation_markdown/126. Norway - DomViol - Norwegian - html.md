### § 271.

Assault

A person who exercises violence against another person or otherwise physically assaults him shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 1 year.

An assault may be made exempt from punishment if a. it has been retaliated with an assault or bodily harm, or b. it retaliates a preceding assault, bodily harm or particularly provocative statement.

Share section

### § 272.

Aggravated assault

Aggravated assault shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 years. When deciding whether the assault is aggravated, particular weight shall be attached to whether it has resulted in severe pain, injury or death, and otherwise whether it a. has occurred without provocation and has the character of an attack, b. has been committed against a defenceless person, c. has the character of abuse, d. has been committed by several persons jointly, e. is motivated by the injured party's skin colour, national or ethnic origin, religion, belief, sexual orientation, gender identity or gender expression or reduced functional ability, or f. has been committed by use of a knife or other particularly dangerous instrument.

§ 271 second paragraph applies correspondingly.

### § 273.

Bodily harm A person who injures another person's body or health, renders another person physically powerless or induces unconsciousness or a similar condition in another person shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 years.

### § 274.

Serious bodily harm

Serious bodily harm is punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years. When determining whether the bodily harm is serious, particular weight shall be given to whether it has resulted in incurable disfigurement or injury, illness or incapacity for work of some duration or severe pain, significant injury or death, and furthermore whether it a. has occurred without provocation and has the character of an assault, b. is committed against a defenceless person, c. has the character of abuse, d. is committed by several persons jointly, e. is motivated by the injured party's skin colour, national or ethnic origin, religion, belief, sexual orientation, gender identity or gender expression or reduced functional ability, or f. is committed by use of a knife or other particularly dangerous instrument.

Any person who causes significant injury to another person's body or health is punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 15 years. (entered into force 22 Apr 2016 pursuant to decree 22 Apr 2016 No. 407

### § 283.

Aggravated abuse in close relationships

Aggravated abuse in close relationships is punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 15 years. When determining whether the abuse is aggravated, particular weight shall be given to whether it has resulted in significant injury or death, and furthermore a. its duration, b. whether it is carried out in a particularly painful manner, or has resulted in significant pain, or c. whether it is committed against a defenceless person.

### § 284.

Genital mutilation

Any person who performs an intervention on a woman's genitals that damages the genitals or inflicts permanent changes on them is punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 years. Reconstruction of genital mutilation is punishable in the same manner.

Consent does not exempt from punishment.

### § 286.

Violence against particularly vulnerable occupational groups

Any person who by violence seeks to influence the professional practice of a person from a particularly vulnerable occupational group is punishable by a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years.

Particularly vulnerable occupational group means a. health personnel who provide medically justified health care, and fire and rescue personnel who provide services in operations, b. persons who conduct publicly available passenger transport, such as for example railway, subway, tram, bus, aircraft, taxi or ferry, and c. persons who have responsibility for education in primary school or upper secondary school.

### § 287.

Neglect of duty to render assistance

Any person who fails a. to the best of his or her ability to assist a person who is in obvious danger of losing his or her life or suffering significant injury to body or health, or b. by reporting or otherwise to the best of his or her ability to avert fire, flood, explosion or similar accident that entails danger to human life or significant injury to body and health,

is punishable by a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months.

Breach of the duty to render assistance pursuant to the first paragraph is not punishable when the duty could not be fulfilled without exposing oneself or others to particular danger or sacrifice.

### § 288.

Abandonment in a helpless condition, etc.

Any person who abandons another person in a helpless condition with danger to life, body or health is punishable by imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years. Any person who fails to provide assistance to a person in a helpless condition for whom he or she has a duty to care is punishable in the same manner.
