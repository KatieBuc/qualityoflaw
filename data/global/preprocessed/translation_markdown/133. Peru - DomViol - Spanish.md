SUBSTANTIVE PROVISIONS FOR THE PREVENTION, PUNISHMENT AND ERADICATION OF

VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND MEMBERS OF THE FAMILY GROUP

### CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1. Object of the Law

This Law has the object to prevent, eradicate and punish all forms of violence occurring in the public or private sphere against women on the basis of their status as such, and against members of the family group; especially when they are in a situation of vulnerability, due to age or physical condition such as girls, boys, adolescents, older adults and persons with disabilities. To this end, it establishes mechanisms, measures and comprehensive policies for prevention, care and protection of victims as well as reparation for the damage caused; and provides for the prosecution, punishment and reeducation of convicted aggressors with the purpose of guaranteeing women and the family group a life free from violence ensuring the full exercise of their rights.

#### Article 2. Guiding principles

In the interpretation and application of this Law, and in general, in all measures adopted by the State through its public powers and institutions, as well as in the action of society, the following principles are considered preferentially:

- 1. Principle of equality and non-discrimination Peruvian Legal Information System Ministry of Justice Equality between women and men is guaranteed. All forms of discrimination are prohibited.

Discrimination is understood as any type of distinction, exclusion or restriction, based on sex, whose purpose or result is to undermine or nullify the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of the rights of persons.

2. Principle of the best interests of the child

In all measures concerning girls and boys adopted by public or private social welfare institutions, courts, administrative authorities or legislative bodies, the best interests of the child must be a primary consideration.

3. Principle of due diligence

The State adopts without delay all policies aimed at preventing, punishing and eradicating all forms of violence against women and members of the family group. Corresponding sanctions must be imposed on authorities who fail to comply with this principle.

4. Principle of immediate and timely intervention

Justice operators and the National Police of Peru, in the event of an act or threat of violence, must act in a timely manner, without delay for procedural, formal or other reasons, ordering the exercise of protection measures provided for in the law and other regulations, with the purpose of effectively attending to the victim.

5. Principle of simplicity and orality

All processes for violence against women and members of the family group are developed considering the minimum of formalism, in friendly spaces for the alleged victims, encouraging them to trust the system and cooperate with it for an adequate punishment of the aggressor and the restitution of their violated rights.

6. Principle of reasonableness and proportionality

The prosecutor or judge in charge of any violence process must weigh the proportionality between the potential harm caused and the protection and rehabilitation measures to be adopted. To this end, a reasonableness judgment must be made according to the circumstances of the case, issuing decisions that allow effective protection of the life, health and dignity of the victims. The adoption of these measures is adapted to the phases of the cycle of violence and to the various typologies presented by violence against women and members of the family group.

#### Article 3. Approaches

Operators, when applying this Law, consider the following approaches:

- 1. Gender approach Recognizes the existence of asymmetric circumstances in the relationship between men and women, built on the basis of gender differences that constitute one of the main causes of violence against women. This approach must guide the design of intervention strategies aimed at achieving equal opportunities between men and women.
- 2. Comprehensive approach Recognizes that multiple causes and factors converge in violence against women, which are present in different spheres, at the individual, family, community and structural levels. Therefore, it is necessary to establish interventions at the different levels in which people operate and from different disciplines.
- 3. Intercultural approach Recognizes the need for dialogue among the different cultures that are integrated in Peruvian Legal Information System Ministry of Justice Peruvian society, in a manner that allows recovering, from the various cultural contexts, all those expressions that are based on respect for the other person. This approach does not admit accepting discriminatory cultural practices that tolerate violence or hinder the enjoyment of equal rights between persons of different genders.
- 4. Human rights approach Recognizes that the main objective of any intervention under this Law must be the realization of human rights, identifying the rights holders and what they are entitled to according to their particular needs; likewise identifying the obligated parties or duty bearers and the obligations that correspond to them. It seeks to strengthen the capacity of rights holders to claim them and of duty bearers to fulfill their obligations.
- 5. Intersectionality approach Recognizes that women's experience of violence is influenced by factors and identities such as their ethnicity, color, religion; political or other opinion; national or social origin, patrimony; marital status, sexual orientation, HIV-positive status, immigrant or refugee status, age or disability; and, where appropriate, includes measures aimed at specific groups of women.
- 6. Generational approach Recognizes that it is necessary to identify power relations between different ages of life and their connections to improve living conditions or common development. It considers that childhood, youth, adulthood and old age must have a connection, since together they are contributing to a common history and must be strengthened generationally. It presents long-term contributions considering the different generations and placing importance on building co-responsibilities among them.

#### Article 4. Scope of Application of the Law

The provisions of this Law apply to all types of violence against women on the basis of their status as such and against members of the family group.

#### Article 5. Definition of Violence Against Women

Violence against women is any action or conduct that causes them death, harm or physical, sexual or psychological suffering on the basis of their status as such, both in the public and private spheres.

Violence against women is understood to include:

- a. That which takes place within the family or domestic unit or in any other interpersonal relationship, whether the aggressor shares or has shared the same domicile as the woman.

This includes, among others, rape, physical or psychological abuse and sexual abuse.

b. That which takes place in the community, is perpetrated by any person and includes, among others, rape, sexual abuse, torture, trafficking in persons, forced prostitution, kidnapping and sexual harassment in the workplace, as well as in educational institutions, health establishments or any other place.

c. That which is perpetrated or tolerated by State agents, wherever it occurs. Peruvian System of Legal Information Ministry of Justice

#### Article 6. Definition of Violence Against Members of the Family Group

Violence against any member of the family group is any action or conduct that causes them death, harm or physical, sexual or psychological suffering and that occurs in the context of a relationship of responsibility, trust or power, on the part of one member to another of the family group. Special consideration is given to girls, boys, adolescents, older adults and persons with disabilities.

#### Article 7. Subjects of Protection of the Law

The subjects of protection of the Law are: a . Women throughout their life cycle: girl, adolescent, young woman, adult and older adult. b . Members of the family group. This is understood to include spouses, former spouses, cohabitants, former cohabitants; stepfathers, stepmothers; ascendants and descendants; collateral relatives of spouses and cohabitants up to the fourth degree of consanguinity and second degree of affinity; and those who, without having any of the aforementioned conditions, live in the same household, provided there are no contractual or employment relationships; and those who have procreated children in common, regardless of whether they cohabit or not, at the time the violence occurs.(*)

#### Article 8. Types of violence

The types of violence against women and members of the family group are: a) Physical violence. It is the action or conduct that causes damage to bodily integrity or health. It includes mistreatment by negligence, neglect or deprivation of basic needs, which have caused physical harm or may cause it, regardless of the time required for recovery. b) Psychological violence. It is the action or conduct, tending to control or isolate the person against their will, to humiliate or shame them and that may cause psychological harm. Psychological harm is the affectation or alteration of some of the mental functions or capacities of the person, produced by a fact or a set of situations of violence, which determines a temporary or permanent, reversible or irreversible impairment of the previous integral functioning. c) Sexual violence. These are actions of a sexual nature that are committed against a person without their consent or under coercion. They include acts that do not involve penetration or any physical contact. Likewise, Sistema Peruano de Información Jurídica Ministerio de Justicia such are considered the exposure to pornographic material and that violate the right of persons to voluntarily decide about their sexual or reproductive life, through threats, coercion, use of force or intimidation. d) Economic or patrimonial violence. It is the action or omission that is directed to cause a detriment in the economic or patrimonial resources of any person, through:

1. the disturbance of the possession, holding or ownership of their property;

2. the loss, removal, destruction, retention or improper appropriation of objects, work instruments, personal documents, property, values and patrimonial rights;

3. the limitation of economic resources intended to satisfy their needs or deprivation of the indispensable means to live a dignified life; as well as the evasion of compliance with their maintenance obligations;

4. the limitation or control of their income, as well as the perception of a lower salary for equal work, within the same workplace. (*)

### CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND THE FAMILY GROUP

#### Article 9. Right to a life free of violence

Women and members of the family group have the right to a life free of violence, to be valued and educated, to be free from all forms of discrimination, stigmatization and stereotyped patterns of behavior, social and cultural practices based on concepts of inferiority and subordination.

#### Article 11. Labor rights

The male or female worker who is a victim of the violence referred to in this Law has the following rights:

- a. Not to suffer dismissal for reasons related to said acts of violence.
- b. To change of workplace insofar as possible and without detriment to their remuneration and category rights. The same applies to work schedule, where pertinent.

Peruvian System of Legal Information Ministry of Justice

c. To justification of absences and tardiness to the workplace derived from said acts of violence. These absences cannot exceed five working days in a period of thirty calendar days or more than fifteen working days in a period of one hundred eighty calendar days. For this purpose, the complaint filed with the police department or with the Public Prosecutor's Office is considered justifying documentation.

d. To suspension of the employment relationship. The judge in charge of the proceeding may, at the request of the victim and considering the gravity of the situation, grant up to a maximum of five consecutive months of suspension of the employment relationship without pay.

The reinstatement of the male or female worker to their workplace must be carried out under the same conditions existing at the time of suspension of the employment relationship.

#### Article 12. Rights in the field of education

The person victim of the violence referred to in this Law has, among others, the following rights:

- a. To change of place and schedule of studies without detriment to their rights.
- b. To justification of absences and tardiness derived from acts of violence. These absences or tardiness cannot exceed five days in a period of thirty calendar days or more than fifteen days in a period of one hundred eighty calendar days.
- c. To specialized attention in the educational field for the aftereffects of violence, so that the educational service responds to their needs without diminishing its quality.

It is the State's obligation to formulate specific measures to favor the permanence of victims in the educational field and, if applicable, favor their reinsertion therein.

#### Article 27. Services for promotion, prevention and recovery of victims of violence

The protection of women and members of the family group against acts of violence is in the public interest. The State is responsible for promoting prevention against such acts and the recovery of victims.

#### Article 29. Implementation and Registration of Temporary Shelter Homes

The creation of temporary shelter homes is a permanent policy of the State.

The Ministry of Women and Vulnerable Populations implements and administers the registry of temporary shelter homes that comply with quality standards in service delivery. ### CHAPTER II

REEDUCATION OF AGGRESSORS

#### Article 30. Reeducation of Aggressors

It is State policy to create treatment services that contribute to the reeducation of aggressors who have committed acts of violence against women and members of the family group so that the aggressor stops all types of violence against them.

#### Article 31. Penitentiary Treatment for the Social Reintegration of Aggressors

Deprived of Liberty

The National Penitentiary Institute incorporates the axis of prevention of violence against women and members of the family group within the various penitentiary treatment programs aimed at the prison population.

Those sentenced to effective deprivation of liberty for crimes related to violence against women and members of the family group, following evaluation, must undergo multidisciplinary and differentiated reeducation treatment, taking into account the approaches set forth in this Law in order to facilitate their social reintegration. Compliance with treatment is a mandatory requirement for the granting of penitentiary benefits, pardon and commutation of sentence where applicable, in accordance with the current legal framework, which cannot be granted without the corresponding psychological and social report ruling on the evolution of the differentiated treatment.

The Ministry of Women and Vulnerable Populations provides technical assistance for the design of the reeducation program.

#### Article 32. Treatment for Aggressors in the Community

In proceedings for crimes related to acts of violence against women and members of the family group, the judge may impose on the aggressor psychosocial, psychiatric or self-help group treatment specialized in violence through attendance at therapies on violence against women and members of the family group, using the various programs developed by family protection institutions. This measure may be applied from the beginning of the procedure. It is the obligation of local governments to implement, in coordination with the Ministry of Women and Vulnerable Populations, care and intervention services for men and aggressors. In proceedings for crimes related to acts of violence against women and members of the family group, criminal courts must rule in the conviction on specialized treatment for the aggressor who does not serve an effective custodial sentence. Submission to a treatment service for the reeducation of aggressors in public or private institutions as ordered by the court is considered a rule of conduct, without prejudice to the applicable criminal sanction.

## TITLE IV

NATIONAL SYSTEM FOR THE PREVENTION, PUNISHMENT AND ERADICATION OF VIOLENCE

AGAINST WOMEN AND MEMBERS OF THE FAMILY GROUP

#### Article 33. Creation, Purpose and Competence of the System

The National System for the Prevention, Punishment and Eradication of Violence against Women and Members of the Family Group is hereby created, in order to coordinate, plan, organize and execute articulated, integrated and complementary actions for State action in the prevention, care, protection and reparation of the victim, the punishment and reeducation of the aggressor, for the purpose of achieving the eradication of violence against women and members of the family group. It is a functional system.

#### Article 34. Members of the System

The National System for the Prevention, Punishment and Eradication of Violence against Women and Members of the Family Group is composed of the entities that make up the high-level multisectoral commission, which has a technical secretariat, and the regional, provincial and district coordination bodies to eradicate violence against women and members of the family group.

#### Article 35. High-Level Multisectoral Commission

The High-Level Multisectoral Commission is hereby established for the purpose of directing the National System for the Prevention, Punishment and Eradication of Violence against Women and Members of the Family Group and formulating the guidelines and evaluation of the provisions established in this regulation.

The Commission is chaired by the head or the senior management representative of the Ministry of Women and Vulnerable Populations and is comprised of the heads or senior management representatives of the institutions to be determined in the regulations of this Law.

The Ministry of Women and Vulnerable Populations is the governing body in matters of prevention, protection and care of violence against women and members of the family group and is responsible for the coordination, articulation and monitoring of the effective application and compliance with this Law.

The General Directorate against Gender Violence of the aforementioned ministry is constituted as the technical secretariat of the Commission, which convenes specialists from different sectors and representatives of civil society for the purpose of establishing a national working group.

The regulations of this Law regulate the functioning of the Commission.

FINAL COMPLEMENTARY PROVISIONS

FIRST. Regulation

The regulation of this Law shall be issued by the Executive Branch within a period not exceeding ninety calendar days from its entry into force. For this purpose, a commission consisting of the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights, the Ministry of Women and Vulnerable Populations, the Ministry of the Interior, the Judicial Branch and the Public Prosecutor's Office is convened.

SECOND. Normative prevalence

The provisions of this Law prevail over other general or special rules that oppose them. The rights that this Law recognizes to victims of violence against women and against members of the family group are non-waivable.

#### Artículo 121-A.

Aggravated forms. Serious injuries when the victim is a minor, elderly or person with disability

In the cases provided for in the first part of article 121, when the victim is a minor, over sixty-five years of age or suffers from physical or mental disability and the agent takes advantage of such condition, a custodial sentence of not less than six nor more than twelve years is imposed. #### Artículo 121-B.

Aggravated forms. Serious injuries due to violence against women and their family environment

In the cases provided for in the first part of article 121, a custodial sentence of not less than six nor more than twelve years is imposed when the victim:

- 1. Is a woman and is injured because of her condition as such in any of the contexts provided for in the first paragraph of article 108-B.
- 2. Is an ascendant, descendant, natural or adoptive, spouse or cohabitant of the agent.
- 3. Is dependent or subordinate.

When the victim dies as a consequence of the injury and the agent could have foreseen that result, the sentence shall be not less than twelve nor more than fifteen years.(*)

#### Artículo 122. Minor injuries

1. Whoever causes another injuries to the body or health that require more than ten and less than thirty days of care or rest, or a moderate level of psychological damage, according to professional prescription, shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not less than two nor more than five years.

2. The sentence shall be a custodial sentence of not less than six nor more than twelve years if the victim dies as a consequence of the injury provided for in paragraph 1 and the agent could have foreseen that result.

3. The sentence shall be a custodial sentence of not less than three nor more than six years if the victim:

- a. Is a member of the National Police of Peru or the Armed Forces, a magistrate of the Judicial Branch, the Public Prosecutor's Office or the Constitutional Court or an authority elected by popular mandate or a public official or servant and is injured in the exercise of his or her official functions or as a consequence thereof.
- b. Is a minor, over sixty-five years of age or suffers from physical or mental disability and the agent takes advantage of such condition.

c. Is a woman and is injured because of her condition as such, in any of the contexts provided for in the first paragraph of article 108-B.

d. Is an ascendant, descendant, natural or adoptive, spouse or cohabitant of the agent.

e. Is dependent or subordinate in any form to the agent.

4. The custodial sentence shall be not less than eight nor more than fourteen years if the victim dies as a consequence of the injury referred to in paragraph 3 and the agent could have foreseen that result.

#### Artículo 377. Omission, refusal or delay of functional acts

The public official who illegally omits, refuses or delays any act of his office shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not more than two years and with thirty to sixty days-fine. When the omission, refusal or delay of functional acts refers to a request for personal guarantees or case of family violence, the sentence shall be a custodial sentence of not less than two nor more than five years.

#### Artículo 378. Denial or deficient police support

The police officer who refuses, omits or delays, without justified cause, the provision of assistance legally required by the competent civil authority, shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not more than two years.

If the provision of assistance is required by a private individual in a situation of danger, the sentence shall be not less than two nor more than four years.

The sentence provided for in the second paragraph shall be imposed if the provision of assistance refers to a request for personal guarantees or a case of family violence".

"Artículo 46-E. Qualified aggravating circumstance due to abuse of kinship

The sentence is increased by up to one third above the maximum legal limit set for the crime when the agent has taken advantage of his or her status as ascendant or descendant, natural or adoptive, stepfather or stepmother, spouse or cohabitant of the victim.
