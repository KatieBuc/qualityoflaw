###### Paragraph VI
Exercise of public authority unlawfully anticipated or prolonged

###### Article 176

Any public official who shall have entered upon the exercise of his functions without having taken an oath, may be prosecuted and shall be punished with a fine from 25,000 to 50,000 francs.

Article 177

Any public official who shall have continued the exercise of his functions beyond the term set for those functions, or who being elective or temporary, shall have exercised them after having been replaced, shall be punished with imprisonment of at least six months and not more than two years and a fine from 25,000 to 100,000 francs. He shall be disqualified from the exercise of any public function for at least five years and not more than ten years, from the day he shall have served his sentence: all without prejudice to the heavier penalties against officers or military commanders by article 76 of this Code.
