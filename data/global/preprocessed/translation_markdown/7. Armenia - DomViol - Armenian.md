LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA ON PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, PROTECTION OF PERSONS SUBJECTED TO DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, AND RESTORATION OF SOLIDARITY IN THE FAMILY

THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA

L A W

ON PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, PROTECTION OF PERSONS SUBJECTED TO DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, AND RESTORATION OF SOLIDARITY IN THE FAMILY

C H A P T E R 1

G E N E R A L P R O V I S I O N S

The National Assembly of the Republic of Armenia, with the aim of ensuring special protection of the family as a natural and fundamental cell of society, forming the necessary legal mechanisms for the prevention of domestic violence, safety and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, ensuring their rights and legitimate interests, ensuring the safety of family members and promoting the restoration of solidarity in the family, regulating the activities of responsible bodies in the direction of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, ensuring legal grounds for the activities of responsible bodies for providing psychological, material and social support to persons subjected to domestic violence and their social rehabilitation, adopts this Law.

Article 1. Subject matter of regulation of the Law

## 1.

This Law regulates the organizational and legal grounds for the prevention of domestic violence and the protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, defines the concept of domestic violence, the powers of competent bodies in the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence, the types of protection measures, the grounds for their application, the reconciliation of persons subjected to domestic violence and persons who committed domestic violence, http://www.arlis.am/DocumentView.aspx?DocID=118672 2/13 the peculiarities of legal protection of information concerning persons subjected to domestic violence.

Article 2. Principles of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence

## 1.

State and local self-government bodies, their officials and public organizations in carrying out their activities in the field of prevention of domestic violence and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence are guided by:

1) the priority of ensuring the safety and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence,

2) support of the family as a natural and fundamental cell of society, strengthening of traditional values in the family and restoration of solidarity in the family,

3) the priority of the best interests of the child,

4) ensuring equality and prohibition of discrimination,

5) accessibility of justice,

6) non-interference with information concerning private and family life,

7) the principles of cooperation between state, local self-government bodies and civil society.

Article 3. Domestic violence and its types

## 1.

Domestic violence is considered to be a violent act of physical, sexual, psychological or economic nature defined by this Law, as well as neglect, committed between family members.

- 2. The types of domestic violence are:
  - 1) physical violence: beating and other violent actions provided for by the Criminal Code of the Republic of Armenia, intentional infliction of harm to health, illegal deprivation of liberty, intentional infliction of severe physical pain;
  - 2) sexual violence: crimes against sexual inviolability and sexual freedom provided for by the Criminal Code of the Republic of Armenia;
  - 3) psychological violence: intentional infliction of severe mental suffering, including a real threat of committing physical, sexual or economic violence, intentional periodic commission of such actions that arouse in a person reasonable fear about danger threatening the personal safety of himself or a family member, periodic humiliation of dignity, extreme social isolation, coercion to artificial termination of pregnancy;
  - 4) economic violence: deprivation of means necessary for existence (food, clothing, housing, medicine) for the purpose of creating forced material dependence on or controlling a person, unlawful restriction of the rights to dispose of, possess and use property belonging by right of ownership or common ownership, restriction of a person's rights to freedom of education or choice of employment;
  - 5) neglect: intentional failure by a parent or legal representative to satisfy the minimum conditions necessary for the child's living (food, clothing, housing, medical care and service, education), and intentional failure by able-bodied adult children to satisfy the minimum conditions necessary for the living of their disabled and needy parents (food, clothing, housing, medical care and service), if the parent or legal representative or able-bodied adult children possess appropriate information and capabilities, and if the relevant services are available to them.

Article 4. Basic concepts of the Law

## 1. The following basic concepts are used in this Law.

1) family members: a. regardless of the circumstance of cohabitation: the spouse (including a person in a de facto marital relationship), the former spouse, the parent, including the step-parent, the adoptive parent, the foster parent, the child (also adopted, step, foster child), the spouse of the adoptive parent, the spouse's parents, http://www.arlis.am/DocumentView.aspx?DocID=118672 3/13 the former spouse's parents, b. cohabiting grandmother, grandfather, sister and brother (also half-blood siblings by mother or father), the spouse's sister, the spouse's brother, as well as for the spouse's parents, sister and brother: the son-in-law and daughter-in-law.

2) support center: a state organization providing social services as envisaged by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Social Assistance".

3) shelter (care center for persons subjected to domestic violence): a safe temporary accommodation provided to persons subjected to domestic violence and persons under their care in cases prescribed by this Law, where they receive social services envisaged by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Social Assistance".

4) person who committed domestic violence: a natural person against whom a warning, urgent intervention or protection decision has been issued in the manner prescribed by this Law.

5) person subjected to domestic violence: a natural person who, according to a warning, urgent intervention, protection decision or a court judgment having entered into legal force, has suffered from domestic violence.

6) person under care: a child, elderly or person with disability who lives with a person subjected to domestic violence and whom the latter permanently cares for.

7) authorized police officer: an officer of a police subdivision specialized in the field of domestic violence prevention and protection of persons subjected to domestic violence.

8) rehabilitation program: a comprehensive program of measures aimed at preventing repeated violence by the person who committed violence through the provision of psychological and social support, health restoration, and liberation from harmful habits (alcoholism, drug addiction, substance abuse, gambling addiction).

## 1.

If one family member has used violence against another, and there is a reasonable presumption about the imminent threat of repetition or continuation of violence, then for the purpose of ensuring the life and health of the other family member, the authorized police officer shall immediately issue an immediate intervention decision. An immediate intervention decision may also be issued in the case when, after receiving a warning, within one year, a person has committed an act of violence that does not contain the elements of a criminal offense.

- 2. The term of validity of the immediate intervention decision may not exceed twenty days. If during the validity of the immediate decision the court is examining an application for a protective order, then it remains in force until a decision is made by the court.
- 3. The following restrictions may be applied by the immediate intervention decision:
  - 1) force the person who committed violence in the family to immediately leave the residence area of the victim of domestic violence and prohibit his or her return there until the expiration of the term established by the decision;
  - 2) prohibit the person who committed violence in the family from visiting the victim of domestic violence who does not reside in the same residential area with him or her, and if necessary, also persons under his or her care, at their places of work, study, rest, residence or other places;
  - 3) prohibit the person who committed violence in the family from approaching the victim of domestic violence (if necessary, also persons under his or her care) closer than a distance that would reasonably cause in the victim of domestic violence a justified fear about the danger threatening his or her personal safety. When applying this restriction, the decision shall specify the specific measure of distance;
  - 4) temporarily confiscate the weapon in the possession of the person who committed violence until the expiration of the term established by the decision. When applying this restriction, if there is a weapon in the possession of the person who committed violence in the family at the moment of notification about the decision, he or she shall immediately hand it over to the police officer who issued the decision;
  - 5) prohibit the person who committed violence in the family from communicating by telephone, correspondence or other means of communication with the victim of domestic violence, and if necessary, also with persons under his or her care.
- 4. The restrictions provided for by part 3 of this article may be applied separately or in combination, indicating the same or different deadlines for their execution. When choosing the means of restriction, the decision-maker shall be guided by the principles established by this law, ensuring the proportionality of the restriction.
- 5. If the person under imminent threat is a minor or incapacitated person, then the authorized police officer who issued the decision shall immediately send a copy of that decision and the corresponding record to the guardianship and trusteeship authority, which, assessing the situation, shall take the actions provided for by law and its charter.
- 6. If the immediate intervention decision is applied against the sole legal representative living together with a minor or incapacitated person, then the temporary care of children and incapacitated persons shall be organized by the guardianship and trusteeship authority, after receiving a copy of the decision, within the shortest possible time, but not more than 24 hours, in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Armenia, being guided by their interests.
- 7. The reasoned decision of the authorized police officer on applying the immediate intervention decision shall enter into force from the moment of notifying the person who committed violence in the family about it. A copy thereof shall be handed to the person who committed violence in the family with a signature, in person, in case of his or her absence at the scene, the content of the decision shall be explained to him or her by means of telephone communication, and in case of impossibility thereof, a copy of the decision shall be sent to his or her registered address by official email or registered mail.
- 8. When applying the restriction provided for by point 1 of part 3 of this article, the authorized police officer issuing the immediate intervention decision shall not leave until the person who committed violence in the family collects his or her belongings and leaves the residence of the victim of domestic violence.

The person who committed violence in the family may collect his or her personal belongings from that place after the decision enters into force only once, accompanied by a police officer.

- 9. The authorized police officer issuing the immediate intervention decision shall immediately submit a copy of the decision and the corresponding record to the head of his or her subdivision.
- 10. The victim of domestic violence is obliged to refrain from performing actions aimed at non-fulfillment by the person who committed violence in the family of the obligations imposed on him or her by the immediate intervention decision. In case of deliberately and repeatedly performing such actions by the victim of domestic violence, the immediate intervention decision may be revoked by the authorized police officer who issued it. The immediate intervention decision shall also be revoked by the same person in cases of concluding reconciliation as a result of the reconciliation process provided for by this law in the family, as well as in case of issuing a protective order by the court before the expiration of its term.
- 11. Supervision over the execution of the immediate intervention decision by the person who committed violence in the family is carried out by the police.
- 12. A copy of the immediate intervention decision shall be sent by the authorized police officer who issued it to the support center at the place of permanent residence of the victim of domestic violence.
- 13. The restriction provided for by point 3 of part 3 of this article shall not apply during a meeting organized between the victim of domestic violence and the person who committed violence in the family within the framework of the reconciliation process provided for by this law, with the permission of the support center, in the territory of the center or in the presence of the relevant specialist of the center.
- 14. Non-execution of the requirements of the immediate intervention decision by the person who committed violence in the family shall give rise to liability provided for by law.
- 15. The immediate intervention decision shall indicate the term and body for appealing it, including the court to which the decision may be appealed, as well as the means of liability provided for by law for violating it. The immediate intervention decision may be appealed hierarchically, within a five-day period after notifying the person who committed violence in the family about it, as well as in a judicial manner, within the terms established by the Administrative Procedure Code of the Republic of Armenia. Filing a complaint against the decision shall not suspend the execution of the decision. The administrative complaint shall be examined and a decision thereon shall be made within a five-day period after receiving it.

Article 8. Protective order

## 1.

A person subjected to or allegedly subjected to domestic violence, or with their consent, a support center, may apply to the court for a protection order. If the person subjected to domestic violence is a minor or a person recognized as incapacitated or with limited capacity by court order, their close relatives, legal representative, and the guardianship and trusteeship body may apply to the court for a protection order.

- 2. The existence of an urgent intervention order does not exclude the possibility of applying for a protection order.
- 3. A protection order is issued for the purpose of protecting the person subjected to domestic violence and persons under their care and preventing new manifestations of domestic violence.
- 4. A protection order is issued for a period of up to 6 months and may be extended twice by a reasoned court decision based on an appropriate application justifying its necessity, each time for a maximum period of up to 3 months.
- 5. A protection order may impose the following restrictions:
  - 1) require the person who committed domestic violence to immediately leave the place of residence of the person subjected to domestic violence and prohibit their return there until the expiration of the period established by the order.

When setting the period for this restriction, the court takes into account the possibility and appropriateness of relocating the person subjected to domestic violence and persons under their care to a shelter, the availability of other places of residence for the person who committed domestic violence.

2) prohibit the person who committed domestic violence from visiting the person subjected to domestic violence who does not reside in the same residential space, and if necessary, also persons under their care, at their places of work, study, recreation, residence or other places.

3) prohibit the person who committed domestic violence from approaching the person subjected to domestic violence (if necessary, also persons under their care) closer than a distance that would reasonably cause fear in the person subjected to domestic violence of danger threatening their personal safety. http://www.arlis.am/DocumentView.aspx?DocID=118672 6/13 When applying this restriction, the order establishes a specific distance measurement.

4) require the weapon under the possession of the person who committed violence to be deposited with the police until the expiration of the period established by the order.

5) impose an obligation on the person who committed domestic violence to share the care expenses of their joint minor or adult incapacitated children, as well as persons under their joint care, in the amount payable for alimony obligations established by the Family Code of the Republic of Armenia. The court may impose an obligation on the person who committed domestic violence to cover other necessary expenses of the person subjected to domestic violence that arose as a result of the violence committed.

6) if necessary, prohibit visits with children.

7) prohibit the person who committed domestic violence from communicating by telephone, correspondence or other means of communication with the person subjected to domestic violence, and if necessary, also with persons under their care.

8) impose an obligation on the person who committed domestic violence to undergo a rehabilitation program.

- 6. The restrictions provided for in part 5 of this article may be applied separately or in combination, indicating the same or different periods for their execution.
- 7. The requirements for protection order applications, the procedure for their examination, the acts adopted by the court, the procedure for appealing them, and the grounds for termination of the protection order are provided for by the Civil Procedure Code of the Republic of Armenia. The grounds and procedure for providing a public defender for judicial representation of a person subjected to domestic violence are provided for by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Advocacy".
- 8. If the person subjected to domestic violence is a minor or incapacitated person, and the protection order is applied against the only legal representative (or representatives) living together with them, then the court instructs the guardianship and trusteeship body to resolve the issue of temporary care of the child or incapacitated person in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Armenia, guided by their interests.
- 9. The police exercises supervision over the execution of the protection order by the person who committed domestic violence. When applying the restriction provided for in point 1 of part 5 of this article, the person who committed domestic violence may collect their belongings from the place of residence of the person subjected to domestic violence once every two months, accompanied by a police officer. When applying the restriction provided for in point 4 of part 5 of this article, if at the time of the decision the person who committed domestic violence has a weapon in their possession, they shall immediately, but not later than within 10 hours, hand it over to the police. The Compulsory Enforcement Service of Judicial Acts of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Armenia ensures the execution of the obligation provided for in point 5 of part 5 of this article, and the Authorized Body monitors the execution of the obligation provided for in point 8 of the same part.
- 10. The restriction provided for in point 3 of part 5 of this article does not apply during a meeting organized between the person subjected to domestic violence and the person who committed domestic violence within the framework of the reconciliation process provided for by this law, with the permission of the support center at the center or in the presence of a relevant specialist of the center.
- 11. Failure to comply with the requirements of the protection order by the person who committed domestic violence entails liability provided for by law.

## 1.

The state takes necessary measures to ensure the availability and operation of shelters. A shelter is established by the Authorized body, a non-profit organization established by a local self-government body, or other non-profit legal entities.

- 2. The Authorized body may conclude a contract with non-profit legal entities regarding the establishment of a shelter by them or the support of the operation of an already established shelter, which must also contain the procedure for the Authorized body to exercise supervision over the operation of that shelter, including over the use of financial resources provided. The location for establishing a new shelter is determined by the Authorized body under this law, based on the needs assessment conclusions provided by the territorial bodies providing social services provided by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Social Assistance". The non-profit legal entity to be financed is selected through a competition held in the manner established by the legislation of the Republic of Armenia.
- 3. In order to establish a shelter, the relevant organizations must obtain a certificate provided by the Authorized body, the procedure for granting which is established by the Government.

The Government may provide support to non-profit legal entities for creating a shelter and ensuring its operation in the manner established by law.

- 4. The shelter must be provided with the necessary conditions of communal, sanitary-hygienic and fire safety and an appropriate security system meeting the requirements established by the legislation of the Republic of Armenia, and for children also with care and education conditions, and comply with other criteria established by the Government of the Republic of Armenia.
- 5. Victims of domestic violence, upon their application, together with persons under their care (hereinafter: beneficiaries) may be placed in a shelter if there is a reasonable assumption of the danger of repetition or continuation of violence. Children cannot be placed in a shelter without at least one parent or other legal representative. A decision on placing a person in a shelter may be made by:
  - 1) the police in cases of danger threatening life and health.
  - 2) the support center upon receiving a conclusion from the territorial body providing social services provided by the Law of the Republic of Armenia "On Social Assistance".

The decision on placing a person in a shelter must be reasoned.

- 6. The shelter:
  - 1) provides beneficiaries with immediate secure living space, psychological and legal http://www.arlis.am/DocumentView.aspx?DocID=118672 12/13 assistance, social support, involving relevant specialists when necessary, and also provides food and clothing, and for children also space with furniture and supplies necessary for preparation of lessons.
  - 2) when necessary and with the consent of the beneficiaries, organizes their application to healthcare institutions to receive medical assistance and services.
- 7. Beneficiaries are placed in the shelter for no more than 12 months. The duration of stay in the shelter is determined by the shelter, upon the recommendation of specialists providing appropriate assistance to the beneficiaries, taking into account the presence or absence of threats to their safety, their ability to live independently or together with another person, and other relevant circumstances, with the consent of the beneficiaries to remain in the shelter being a mandatory condition.
