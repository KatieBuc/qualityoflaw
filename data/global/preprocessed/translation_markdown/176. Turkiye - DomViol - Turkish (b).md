# PROTECTION OF THE FAMILY AND AGAINST VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

PROTECTION OF THE FAMILY AND AGAINST VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

LAW ON PREVENTION

Law Number: 6284

Date of Adoption: 8/3/2012

Published in the Official Gazette: Date: 20/3/2012 Number: 28239

Published in Düstur: Series: 5 Volume: 52

PART ONE

Purpose, Scope, Basic Principles and Definitions

Purpose, scope and basic principles

## ARTICLE 1 – (1) The purpose of this Law is to regulate the procedures and principles

regarding the measures to be taken for the protection of women, children, family members subjected to violence or at risk of being subjected to violence and victims of unilateral persistent stalking, and for the prevention of violence against these persons.

(2) In the implementation of this Law and the provision of necessary services, the following basic principles shall be adhered to:

- a) The Constitution of the Republic of Turkey and international conventions to which Turkey is a party, in particular the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence, and other legal regulations in force shall be taken as basis.
- b) A procedure based on fundamental human rights, sensitive to gender equality between women and men, in accordance with the principle of social state, fair, effective and expeditious shall be followed in the provision of support and services to be given to victims of violence.
- c) Protective measures taken for the victim of violence and the perpetrator of violence shall be executed in a manner befitting human dignity. ç) Within the scope of this Law, special measures that prevent gender-based violence against women and protect women from gender-based violence cannot be interpreted as discrimination.

Definitions

## ARTICLE 2 – (1) In this Law:

a) Ministry: Refers to the Ministry of Family and Social Policies,

b) Domestic violence: Refers to all forms of physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence occurring among family members or in the family or household or among other persons considered as family members, even if the victim of violence and the perpetrator of violence do not share the same household,

c) Judge: Refers to the family court judge, ç) Violence against women: Refers to all kinds of attitudes and behaviors that are applied to women solely because they are women or that affect women, which constitute gender-based discrimination and violate women's human rights and are defined as violence in this Law,

d) Violence: Refers to all kinds of physical, sexual, psychological, verbal or economic attitudes and behaviors occurring in social, public or private sphere, including actions that result or are likely to result in physical, sexual, psychological or economic harm or suffering to a person, threats and coercion to this end, or arbitrary restriction of freedom,

e) Victim of violence: Refers to a person directly or indirectly subjected to or at risk of being subjected to attitudes and behaviors defined as violence in this Law, and persons affected by or at risk of being affected by violence,

f) Violence prevention and monitoring centers: Refers to centers that provide support and monitoring services for the prevention of violence and the effective implementation of protective and preventive measures, and that operate on a twenty-four hours seven days basis,

g) Perpetrator of violence: Refers to persons who commit or are at risk of committing attitudes and behaviors defined as violence in this Law, ğ) Protective measure decision: Refers to protective measure decisions to be given by judges, law enforcement officers and provincial governors, upon request or ex officio, regarding victims of violence and perpetrators of violence within the scope of this Law.

PART TWO

Provisions on Protective and Preventive Measures

Protective measure decisions to be given by the provincial governor

## ARTICLE 3 – (1) With regard to persons protected under this Law, one, several or

similar measures deemed appropriate among the following measures may be decided by the provincial governor:

- a) Providing suitable accommodation for oneself and, if necessary, the children accompanying, at the current location or at another location.
- b) Providing temporary financial assistance, without prejudice to assistance to be made under other laws.
- c) Providing guidance and counselling services in psychological, vocational, legal and social aspects. ç) In case of life-threatening danger, taking under temporary protection upon the request of the person concerned or ex officio.
- d) If necessary, if the protected person has children, to support their participation in working life, limited to four months, or two months if the person is working, not exceeding half of the monthly net minimum wage determined each year for those over sixteen years of age, and to be met from the relevant appropriation of the Ministry's budget subject to documentation, providing nursery facilities.
  - (2) In cases where delay is prejudicial, the measures specified in subparagraphs (a) and (ç) of the first paragraph may also be taken by the relevant law enforcement chiefs. The law enforcement chief shall submit the documents to the approval of the provincial governor at the latest within the first working day following the date of the decision. Measures not approved by the provincial governor within forty-eight hours shall lapse automatically.

Protective measure decisions to be given by the judge

## ARTICLE 4 – (1) With regard to persons protected under this Law, one, several or

similar measures deemed appropriate among the following protective measures may be decided by the judge:

- a) Change of workplace.
- b) In case the person is married, determination of a separate place of residence from the common place of residence.
- c) In the presence of the conditions in the Turkish Civil Code No. 4721 dated 22/11/2001 and upon the request of the protected person, placing a family home annotation on the land registry. ç) In case of a life-threatening danger for the protected person and if it is understood that other measures will not be sufficient to prevent this danger, and based on the informed consent of the person concerned, changing the identity and other relevant information and documents in accordance with the provisions of the Witness Protection Law No. 5726 dated 27/12/2007.

Preventive measure decisions to be given by the judge

## ARTICLE 5 – (1) Regarding those who apply violence, a decision may be made by the judge for one, several, or similar measures deemed appropriate from the following preventive measures:

- a) Not to engage in words or behavior involving threats of violence, insult, humiliation or degradation towards the victim of violence.
- b) Immediate removal from the shared residence or the place where they are located and allocation of the shared residence to the protected person.
- c) Not to approach the protected persons, the residence, school and workplace where these persons are located. ç) If there is a previously issued decision on establishing personal relationship with children, that the personal relationship be conducted in the presence of an escort, limitation of the personal relationship or its complete removal.
- d) Not to approach the relatives of the protected person, their witnesses and, without prejudice to cases concerning establishing personal relationship, their children, even if the protected person has not been subjected to violence, if deemed necessary.
- e) Not to damage the personal belongings and household items of the protected person.
- f) Not to disturb the protected person by means of communication or otherwise.
- g) To surrender to law enforcement any weapons that are legally permitted to be kept or carried. ğ) To surrender to their institution the weapon in their possession due to their duty, even if they perform a public duty that requires carrying a weapon.
- h) Not to use alcohol or narcotics or stimulants in places where the protected persons are located, or not to approach the protected persons and the places where they are located while under the influence of these substances, and in case of addiction, ensuring their examination and treatment, including hospitalization. ı) To apply to a health institution for examination or treatment and ensuring their treatment.
  - (2) In cases where delay is harmful, the measures set forth in subparagraphs (a), (b), (c) and (d) of the first paragraph may also be taken by the relevant law enforcement chiefs. The law enforcement chief submits the documents for the judge's approval at the latest within the first business day following the date the decision was taken. Measures not approved by the judge within twenty-four hours are automatically lifted.
  - (3) Together with the measures specified in this Law, the judge is authorized to decide on protective and supportive measures set forth in the Child Protection Law No. 5395 dated 3/7/2005 and on matters of custody, guardianship, alimony and establishing personal relationship according to the provisions of Law No. 4721.
  - (4) If the person applying violence is also the person who provides for or contributes to the family's livelihood, the judge may order provisional alimony even if not requested, taking into account the living standard of the victim of violence, provided that alimony has not been ordered according to the provisions of Law No. 4721.

Provisions reserved for crimes

## ARTICLE 6 – (1) Due to the person's possession of a weapon, use of narcotics or stimulants constituting a crime, or due to their act constituting another crime;

a) The legal provisions regarding protective measures or probation measures during the investigation and prosecution phase,

b) In case of conviction, the legal provisions regarding the execution of sentences or security measures and probation measures that may be applied within this framework, are reserved.

Notification

## ARTICLE 7 – (1) In case of violence or the presence of a threat of violence, anyone may report this situation to official authorities or bodies. Public officials who receive the notification are obliged to fulfill their duties within the scope of this Law without delay and to inform the authorities regarding other measures that need to be implemented.

Issuance, service and confidentiality of protective measure decision

## ARTICLE 8 – (1) The protective measure decision is issued upon the request of the concerned party, the application of the Ministry or law enforcement officials or the public prosecutor. Protective measure decisions may be requested from the most quickly and easily accessible local judge, civil governor or law enforcement unit.

(2) The protective measure decision may be issued for a maximum of six months for the first time. However, in cases where it is understood that the violence or threat of violence will continue, a decision may be made ex officio, upon the request of the protected person or the Ministry or law enforcement officials, to change the duration or form of the measures, to lift these measures or to continue them as is.

(3) For a protective measure decision to be issued, no evidence or document is sought regarding the application of violence. The preventive measure decision is issued without delay. The issuance of this decision cannot be delayed in a manner that could jeopardize the achievement of the purpose of this Law.

(4) The protective measure decision is communicated or served to the protected person and the person applying violence. The decision regarding the rejection of the protective measure request is served only to the protected person. In cases where delay is harmful, the protective measure decision issued by the relevant law enforcement unit is immediately served to the person applying violence with a report.

(5) In the communication and service procedures of the protective measure decision, a warning is given that coercive imprisonment will be applied against the person applying violence in case of violation of the protective measure decision.

(6) If deemed necessary, together with the protective measure decision, upon request or ex officio, the identity information or information that could reveal the identity and addresses of the protected person and other family members, as well as other information that is important for the effectiveness of the protection, are kept confidential in all official records. A separate address is determined for notifications to be made. Provisions of the Turkish Penal Code No. 5237 dated 26/9/2004 apply to persons who unlawfully give, disclose or reveal this information to others.

(7) Upon request, the delivery of personal belongings and documents to the concerned parties is provided through law enforcement. Objection

## ARTICLE 9 – (1) Decisions issued according to the provisions of this Law may be objected to the family court by the concerned parties within two weeks from the date of communication or service.

(2) Upon objection to protective measure decisions issued by the judge, the file is sent without delay to the next numbered chamber if there is more than one chamber of the family court in that location, to the first chamber for the last numbered chamber, to the civil court of first instance if there is only one chamber of the family court in that location, or to the nearest civil court of first instance if the family court judge and the civil court of first instance judge are the same judge.

(3) The objection authority issues its decision within one week. Decisions issued upon objection are final. Notification and implementation of protective measure decisions

## ARTICLE 10 – (1) Measure decisions taken in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall be notified to the relevant

provincial and district directorates of the Ministry and, depending on the nature of the decision rendered, to the Chief Public Prosecutor's Office or to the law enforcement by the most expeditious means.

(2) Applications made to the relevant authorities within the scope of this Law and decisions regarding the acceptance or rejection of these applications shall be immediately notified by the authority to which the application is made to the relevant provincial and district directorates of the Ministry.

(3) The law enforcement unit of the place of residence or location of the persons subject to protective or preventive measure decisions or the place where the measure is to be implemented shall be responsible and authorized for the implementation of the protective measure decision regarding the temporary protection of the protected person and the preventive measure decisions issued against the perpetrator of violence.

(4) In cases where the measure decision is issued and implemented by the law enforcement chief or where the protected person is at the law enforcement facility, the person shall be promptly transferred by the law enforcement units to the relevant provincial or district directorates of the Ministry; in case this is not possible, temporary accommodation shall be provided to them and their accompanying persons, with expenses to be covered from the relevant appropriation of the Ministry's budget.

(5) The failure to communicate or notify the measure decision to the concerned parties shall not constitute an obstacle to the implementation of the decision.

(6) Persons for whom accommodation has been decided shall be placed in locations belonging to the Ministry or under the Ministry's supervision and control. In cases where accommodation facilities are insufficient, protected persons may be temporarily accommodated in social facilities, dormitories or similar places belonging to public institutions and organizations upon the request of the civil governor, or in urgent cases, of the law enforcement or the Ministry.

(7) The measure decision regarding the change of workplace shall be implemented by the competent authority or person in accordance with the provisions of the relevant legislation to which the person is subject.

Law enforcement duties

## ARTICLE 11 – (1) Law enforcement duties shall be fulfilled in the central and provincial organization of the law enforcement by

sufficient personnel who have been trained in the human rights of children and women and gender equality and who have been designated by the relevant law enforcement units, in relation to the services specified in this Law.

Monitoring by technical methods

## ARTICLE 12 – (1) In the implementation of measure decisions issued in accordance with the provisions of this Law,

technical tools and methods may be used with a judge's decision. However, in this manner, the audio and video of persons cannot be listened to, monitored or recorded.

(2) The procedures and principles regarding monitoring with technical tools and methods shall be regulated by regulation. Violation of measure decisions

## ARTICLE 13 – (1) The perpetrator of violence against whom a measure decision has been issued in accordance with the provisions of this Law,

shall, in case of acting contrary to the requirements of this decision, be subject to coercive detention from three days to ten days by judge's decision according to the nature of the violated measure and the severity of the violation, even if the act constitutes a crime.

(2) For each repetition of violation of the requirements of the measure decision, the period of coercive detention shall be from fifteen days to thirty days according to the nature of the violated measure and the severity of the violation. However, the total period of coercive detention cannot exceed six months.

(3) Decisions regarding coercive detention shall be implemented by the Chief Public Prosecutor's Office. These decisions shall be notified to the relevant provincial and district directorates of the Ministry.

PART THREE

Establishment of Centers, Support Services and Inter-Institutional Coordination

Establishment of violence prevention and monitoring centers

## ARTICLE 14 – (1) The Ministry shall establish violence prevention and monitoring centers where necessary expert personnel are employed and preferably female

personnel are employed, that provide support and monitoring services for the prevention of violence and the effective implementation of protective and preventive measures, that operate on a seven days twenty-four hours basis, and whose working procedures and principles are determined by regulation.

(2) In the established centers, monitoring activities are conducted and support services are provided for the prevention of violence and the effective implementation of protective and preventive measures. Support services

## ARTICLE 15 – (1) Support services to be provided by violence prevention and monitoring centers in terms of preventing violence and monitoring the effective implementation of measure decisions

issued within the scope of this Law are as follows:

- a) To establish an information bank by collecting data regarding the issuance and implementation of protective and preventive measure decisions and coercive detention, to keep a register of measure decisions.
- b) To coordinate accommodation, temporary financial assistance, health, legal aid services and other services provided to the protected person.
- c) To make applications for the issuance and implementation of measure decisions in necessary cases. ç) To prepare and implement programs at individual and social scale aimed at ending violence within the scope of this Law.
- d) To ensure the expansion of the call center established within the Ministry in accordance with the purpose of this Law and the monitoring of applications made.
- e) To cooperate with relevant non-governmental organizations working to end violence within the scope of this Law.
  - (2) Support services to be provided by violence prevention and monitoring centers regarding protected persons are as follows:
- a) To provide guidance to the person on their rights, institutions from which they can receive support, job placement and similar matters and to conduct activities for their participation in vocational training courses.
- b) To make suggestions and provide assistance towards achieving the purpose intended by the measure decision issued.
- c) To monitor the results of the implementation of measure decisions and their effects on persons. ç) To provide assistance and counseling in resolving psycho-social and economic problems.
- d) Upon the request of the judge; to prepare and submit a detailed social investigation report about the person's past, family, environment, education, personal, social, economic and psychological status.
- e) To prepare a report on the results of the implementation of measures and their effects on the concerned parties, if requested by the relevant authority.
- f) To provide necessary guidance on the provision of financial support in accordance with the provisions of the Law No. 3294 on Encouraging Social Solidarity and Assistance dated 29/5/1986.
  - (3) Support services to be provided by violence prevention and monitoring centers regarding the perpetrator of violence are as follows:
- a) Upon the request of the judge; to prepare and submit a detailed social investigation report about the person's past, family, environment, education, personal, social, economic and psychological status and the risk they pose to other persons and society.
- b) To prepare a report on the results of the implementation of measures and their effects on the concerned parties, if requested by the relevant authority or office.
- c) In an encouraging, enlightening and guiding manner, to conduct activities for the person's;
  - 1) Participation in education and rehabilitation programs aimed at changing attitudes and behaviors by providing awareness of anger control, coping with stress, and preventing violence,
  - 2) Examination or treatment at a health institution in case of alcohol, drug, volatile or stimulant substance addiction or mental disorder,
  - 3) Participation in vocational training courses.
    - (4) Services for the victim of violence and the perpetrator of violence shall be provided in different units except in mandatory cases.

Inter-institutional coordination and training

## ARTICLE 16 – (1) Inter-institutional coordination in the implementation of the provisions of this Law shall be carried out by the Ministry.

(2) Public institutions and organizations as well as other natural and legal persons are obliged to cooperate and provide assistance in matters falling within their scope of duty with regard to the implementation of this Law and to promptly execute the preventive measure decisions taken. Natural and legal persons shall be encouraged to support the Ministry's work and to carry out joint work within the scope of this Law.

(3) The Turkish Radio and Television Corporation as well as private television organizations and radio stations broadcasting nationally, regionally and locally are obliged to broadcast informational materials prepared by or commissioned by the Ministry on the participation of women in working life, mechanisms to combat violence especially concerning women and children, and similar policies, for at least ninety minutes per month. These broadcasts shall be made between 08.00-22.00 hours with a minimum of thirty minutes being between 17.00-22.00 hours, and copies of the broadcasts shall be regularly delivered to the Radio and Television Supreme Council every month. Broadcasts made outside these hours shall not be included in the monthly ninety-minute period. These periods shall be supervised by the Radio and Television Supreme Council. Informational materials to be broadcast on television organizations and radio stations shall be prepared by the Ministry units with the opinions of universities, relevant professional organizations and non-governmental organizations also being obtained.

(4) During the performance of the duties stipulated in this Law, personnel of public institutions and organizations shall assist the Ministry officials.

(5) All public institutions and organizations as well as professional organizations having the nature of a public institution shall ensure the participation of their personnel and members in training programs on women's human rights and gender equality, which will be prepared and coordinated by the Ministry for the purpose of effective implementation of this Law.

(6) Courses on education regarding women's human rights and gender equality shall be included in primary and secondary education curricula.

PART FOUR

Financial Provisions

Provision of temporary financial assistance

## ARTICLE 17 – (1) In the event that a decision is made to provide temporary financial assistance pursuant to the provisions of this Law, daily payment shall be made up to one thirtieth of the monthly net minimum wage amount determined each year for those over sixteen years of age. In the event that there is more than one protected person, an additional payment shall be made at a rate of twenty percent of this amount for each additional person. However, the amount to be paid may in no way exceed one and a half times the determined daily payment amount. In the event that shelter is provided to the protected persons, the amounts determined in this paragraph shall be applied reduced by fifty percent.

(2) These payments shall be covered from the appropriation allocated for temporary financial assistance in the Ministry budget. The payments made shall be collected from the perpetrator of violence within one month from the notification date. Those that cannot be collected in this manner shall be pursued and collected by the relevant tax office in accordance with the provisions of the Law No. 6183 dated 21/7/1953 on the Procedure for Collection of Public Receivables.

(3) In the event that it is determined that the protected person has made a statement contrary to the truth, the assistance provided shall be collected from this person in accordance with the provisions of Law No. 6183.

Alimony

## ARTICLE 18 – (1) In the event that a decision is made for alimony pursuant to the provisions of this Law, a copy of the decision shall be sent ex officio to the execution office of the place of residence of the alimony creditor or debtor.

(2) In the event that the person obliged to pay alimony has a connection with the Social Security Institution, the alimony shall be collected by the execution office from the relevant person's pension, salary or wage without requiring an application by the protected person. Postal expenses relating to the collection transactions of alimony by the execution offices shall be covered from the flagrante delicto appropriation of the Chief Public Prosecutor's Office.

Health expenses

## ARTICLE 19 – (1) Those persons regarding whom a protective measure decision has been made pursuant to the provisions of this Law and who are not general health insurance holders and who do not fall within the scope of persons whom a general health insurance holder is obliged to support, or who cannot actually benefit from general health insurance due to general health insurance premium debt, or who do not have the right to benefit from treatment assistance pursuant to the provisions of other legislation; for the duration of these situations, shall be deemed general health insurance holders within the scope of sub-item (1) of item (c) of the first paragraph of Article 60 of the Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 dated 31/5/2006, without being subjected to an income test.

(2) In the event that a person regarding whom a preventive measure decision has been made pursuant to the provisions of this Law is also decided to require rehabilitation or treatment, expenses for rehabilitation services not covered by general health insurance as well as expenses for other health services to be provided within the scope of rehabilitation services shall be covered from the relevant appropriations of the Ministry budget.

Exemption from fees and expenses, taxes and participation in lawsuits

## ARTICLE 20 – (1) No expense shall be collected under any name whatsoever such as litigation costs, fees, postal expenses and the like for applications within the scope of this Law and for transactions made for the execution and enforcement of decisions rendered. Payments made pursuant to Article 17 of this Law are exempt from income tax and inheritance and transfer tax, and documents issued for these payments are exempt from stamp tax.

(2) The Ministry may, when it deems necessary, participate in all types of administrative, criminal, and civil lawsuits and non-contentious proceedings filed due to violence or the threat of violence applied against women, children and family members.

PART FIVE

Miscellaneous and Final Provisions

Positions1

## ARTICLE 21 – (1) The positions contained in the attached list have been created and added to the section pertaining to the Ministry of Family and Social Policies of annex (I) of the Decree Having the Force of Law No. 190 dated 13/12/1983 on General Positions and Procedure.

Regulation

## ARTICLE 22 – (1) The procedures and principles regarding the implementation of this Law shall be regulated by regulations prepared by the Ministry within six months, with the opinions of the Ministries of Justice, Interior, Finance, National Education and Health being obtained.

Repealed provisions and references

## ARTICLE 23 – (1) The Law No. 4320 dated 14/1/1998 on the Protection of the Family has been repealed.

(2) References to Law No. 4320 in the legislation shall be deemed to have been made to this Law.

(3) The implementation of decisions rendered pursuant to the provisions of Law No. 4320 before the entry into force of this Law shall continue.

Violence prevention and monitoring centers becoming operational

PROVISIONAL ARTICLE 1 – (1) The violence prevention and monitoring centers whose establishment is envisaged in Article 14 of this Law shall be established for pilot implementation in provinces to be determined by the Ministry within two years from the date of entry into force of the Law. Until their establishment is completed, which units of the Ministry shall perform the duties of the centers shall be determined by the Ministry.

Entry into force

## ARTICLE 24 – (1) This Law enters into force on the date of its publication.

Execution

## ARTICLE 25 – (1) The provisions of this Law shall be executed by the Council of Ministers.
