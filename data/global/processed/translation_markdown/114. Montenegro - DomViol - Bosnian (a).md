# Law on Protection from Domestic Violence

Law on Protection from Domestic Violence

The Law was published in the "Official Gazette of MN", No. 46/2010.

See: Art. 152 of the Law - 40/2011-1.

I. BASIC PROVISIONS

Subject of the law

## Article 1

Protection from domestic violence (hereinafter: protection) is exercised in accordance with this law.

Domestic violence

## Article 2

Domestic violence (hereinafter: violence), within the meaning of this law, is an act or omission by a family member that endangers the physical, psychological, sexual or economic integrity, mental health and peace of another family member, regardless of the place where it was committed. Family members

## Article 3

Family members, within the meaning of this law, are considered to be:

- 1) spouses or former spouses, their children and children of each of them;
- 2) common-law partners or former common-law partners, regardless of the duration of the common-law union, their children and children of each of them;
- 3) blood relatives and relatives by complete adoption in direct line without limitation, and in collateral line up to and including the fourth degree;
- 4) relatives by incomplete adoption;
- 5) relatives by affinity up to and including the second degree in marriage or common-law union;
- 6) persons living in the same family household, regardless of kinship;
- 7) persons who have a common child or a child has been conceived.

Right to assistance and protection

## Article 4

A victim of violence (hereinafter: victim) has the right to psychosocial and legal assistance and social and medical protection, in accordance with the law.

Protection of the victim is also ensured by imposing protective measures.

Special assistance and protection is enjoyed by a victim who is a child, elderly person, person with disability and person who is not able to take care of themselves, in accordance with the law.

Institutions dealing with protection

## Article 5

The administrative body responsible for police affairs (hereinafter: police), the body for misdemeanors, the State Prosecutor's Office, social work center or other institution of social and child protection, health institution, as well as other bodies and institutions dealing with protection are obliged to provide, within their powers, complete and coordinated protection that is necessary for the protection of the victim depending on the degree of their endangerment.

A non-governmental organization, other legal and natural person may provide protection in accordance with the law. The bodies and institutions from paragraph 1 of this Article are obliged to give priority, in accordance with the law, to resolving cases of violence and to ensure mutual notification and assistance in order to prevent and detect violence, eliminate causes and provide assistance to the victim in establishing conditions for a safe life.

Urgency of proceedings

## Article 6

In proceedings relating to protection, the bodies and institutions from Article 5 paragraph 1 of this Law are obliged to act urgently, taking into account that the interest and welfare of the victim, especially if the victim is a child, elderly person, person with disability and person who is not able to take care of themselves, are a priority in those proceedings.

Use of gender-sensitive language

## Article 7

Terms used in this Law for natural persons in the masculine gender imply the same terms in the feminine gender.

II. PROTECTION OF THE VICTIM

1. Forms and methods of providing assistance and protection

Forms of violence

## Article 8

peace of another family member is particularly considered if a family member:

- 1) uses physical force, regardless of whether bodily injury to another family member has occurred;
- 2) threatens with an attack or causes danger that may produce a feeling of personal insecurity or
- 3) verbally attacks, swears, calls by a derogatory name and in other ways insults another family member;
- 4) restricts the freedom of another family member to communicate with third parties;
- 5) exhausts by work, denies sleep and other type of rest, threatens with eviction from the apartment and taking away the children;
- 6) sexually harasses another family member;
- 7) stalks and in other ways grossly harasses another family member;
- 8) damages or destroys common property or property of another family member or attempts to do so;
- 9) denies basic means of subsistence to another family member;
- 10) by insolent behavior endangers the family peace of a family member with whom they do not live in a family community. peace of another family member is also considered if a family member does not take sufficient care of:
- 1) nutrition, hygiene, clothing, medical protection or regular school attendance or does not prevent the child from harmful association, vagrancy, begging or theft or in other ways to a greater extent neglects the upbringing and education of the child;
- 2) nutrition, hygiene, clothing or medical protection of another family member for whom they are obliged to care, and who needs special assistance due to illness, disability, old age or other personal characteristics because of which they are not able to take care of themselves.

Concealment of a family member with special needs is considered a more severe form of domestic violence.

Duty to report violence

## Article 9

State bodies, other bodies, health, educational and other institutions are obliged to report to the police acts of violence of which they become aware in the performance of duties within their competence or activity.

A report on committed violence must be submitted to the police by the responsible person in the body or institution from paragraph 1 of this Article, as well as by a health and social worker, teacher, educator and other person who becomes aware of the committed violence in the performance of their duties.

The body for misdemeanors and the police are obliged to inform the social work center about the reported violence.

Emergency intervention

## Article 10

After becoming aware of violence, the police shall without delay take actions and measures aimed at protecting the victim, in accordance with this Law and laws regulating the work and powers of the police, misdemeanor proceedings, criminal proceedings and witness protection.

The social work center, or other institution of social and child protection, health institution, as well as other bodies and institutions dealing with protection are obliged to, without delay, provide protection and assistance to the victim in accordance with their competences.

The bodies and institutions from paragraph 2 of this Article are obliged to take care of all the needs of the victim and enable them access to all forms of assistance and protection.

Plan for assistance to the victim

## Article 11

The social work center forms an expert team composed of representatives of that institution, bodies and services of local administration, police, non-governmental organizations and experts dealing with family issues, in order to determine the plan for assistance to the victim and coordinate activities in the process of assisting the victim, in accordance with their needs and choice.

The plan for assistance to the victim specifically contains measures that need to be taken in accordance with the law regulating social and child protection. If the victim is a child, the plan for assistance to the victim also contains measures for the protection of the child in accordance with the law regulating family relations.

For the purpose of implementing activities from paragraph 1 of this Article, the expert team may also be formed by another body, institution or organization dealing with protection.

Social protection

## Article 12

Social protection of the victim includes material and non-material assistance, accommodation and social work services, in accordance with the law regulating social and child protection. Social work centers are obliged to establish precise records of children and persons with special needs in their territory.

For the purpose of protection from violence against these persons, the centers are obliged to form special teams that will, at least once a month, visit families in which children and persons with special needs are accommodated or live, monitor the treatment of these persons and, based on the established situation, prepare a written report.

Social work centers are obliged to, at least once every six months, submit a written report to the ministry responsible for social welfare affairs on the degree of implementation of social protection of children and persons with special needs.

Legal assistance

## Article 13

The victim has the right to free legal assistance, in accordance with a special law.

Security of the victim

## Član 14

A police officer is obliged to accompany the victim to the apartment or other residential space when taking personal belongings that are necessary for their everyday life, unless the victim explicitly objects to this.

The action from paragraph 1 of this article may also be carried out in order to provide temporary accommodation and care for the victim.

Confidentiality of proceedings

## Član 15

The bodies, institutions, organizations and other legal and natural persons from Article 5 of this law are obliged to maintain the confidentiality of information and ensure the protection of personal data, in accordance with the law. Information on the basis of which the victim or a family member of the victim can be identified may not be publicly disclosed about the victim or the family member who committed the violence (hereinafter: the perpetrator of violence), unless an adult victim explicitly consents to this.

Confidential person

## Član 16

The victim may choose a person who will be present at all procedures and actions related to protection (hereinafter: confidential person).

The confidential person may be a family member, a person from a body, institution, non-governmental organization and other legal entity, or another person in whom the victim has confidence.

The confidential person may not be the perpetrator of violence.

The victim may choose a confidential person before or during the procedure and undertaking of actions related to protection. Competent bodies are obliged to enable the presence of a confidential person in all procedures and actions in which the victim is involved, and which are related to family relations. Multidisciplinary team and principle of cooperation

## Član 17

For the purpose of organizing, monitoring and improving coordinated and effective protection, a body or institution from Article 5 paragraph 1 of this law may form a team of experts from the fields of social and child protection, health protection, justice, police protection, protection of human rights and freedoms, as well as representatives of non-governmental organizations dealing with protection.

Protection strategy

## Član 18

Protection is implemented in accordance with the strategy for protection against domestic violence (hereinafter:

Strategy), which contains:

- 1) assessment of the situation and identification of key problems in social and other protection;
- 2) objectives and measures for improving social and other protection, and in particular in relation to: raising the level of citizens' awareness about the problem of violence and forming attitudes about the unacceptability of violence; development of programs for violence prevention; family support in violence prevention; further development of the normative framework in the field of protection; strengthening cooperation of bodies, institutions, organizations and other legal and natural persons dealing with protection; acquiring new knowledge and skills of all those dealing with protection; improvement of the system for collecting and analyzing data and reporting on cases of violence.

Activities for implementing the objectives and measures from paragraph 1 item 2 of this article are determined by an action plan for implementing the Strategy.

The Strategy and the action plan for implementing the Strategy are adopted by the Government of Montenegro.

2. Zaštitne mjere

Purpose of protective measures

## Član 19

Protective measures are imposed in order to prevent and suppress violence, eliminate the consequences of committed violence and undertake effective measures for re-education of the perpetrator of violence and elimination of circumstances that favor or encourage the commission of new violence.

Types of protective measures

## Član 20

One or more protective measures may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence:

- 1) removal from the apartment or other residential space (hereinafter: removal from the apartment);
- 2) prohibition of approach;
- 3) prohibition of harassment and stalking;
- 4) mandatory treatment for addiction;
- 5) mandatory psychosocial treatment.

The bodies and institutions from Article 5 paragraph 1 of this law are obliged to provide the perpetrator of violence with appropriate information about their rights.

Removal from the apartment

## Član 21

Removal from the apartment may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence who lives with the victim in an apartment or other residential space, regardless of the ownership and other rights of the perpetrator of violence and the victim to the apartment or other residential space, if there is a danger that they will commit violence again.

The perpetrator of violence to whom removal from the apartment has been imposed is obliged to immediately leave the apartment or other residential space.

Removal from the apartment is imposed for a period that may not be shorter than thirty days nor longer than six months.

Prohibition of approach

## Član 22

Prohibition of approach to the victim may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence if there is a danger that they will commit violence again or if the presence of the perpetrator of violence in the vicinity of the victim would create a high degree of mental suffering in the victim which, for a shorter or longer period of time, prevents the normal mental activities of the victim. The body for misdemeanors is obliged to determine in the decision on the prohibition of approach the place or area in which the perpetrator of violence may not approach the victim.

Prohibition of approach is imposed for a period that may not be shorter than thirty days nor longer than one year.

Prohibition of harassment and stalking

## Član 23

Prohibition of harassment and stalking of the victim may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence if there is a danger that they will commit violence again.

The protective measure from paragraph 1 of this article is imposed for a period that may not be shorter than thirty days nor longer than one year.

Mandatory treatment for addiction

## Član 24

Mandatory treatment for addiction may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence who commits violence as a result of addiction to alcohol, narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances, and there is a danger that they will commit violence again due to that addiction.

The protective measure from paragraph 1 of this article lasts as long as there is a need for treatment, but not longer than one year.

Mandatory psychosocial treatment

## Član 25

Mandatory psychosocial treatment may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence in order to eliminate the causes of violent behavior and re-education, that is, in order to reduce and eliminate the danger that they will commit violence again.

The protective measure from paragraph 1 of this article lasts until the cessation of the reason for which it was imposed, but not longer than six months.

The detailed manner of determining and implementing the protective measure from paragraph 1 of this article is prescribed by the ministry responsible for social welfare affairs.

3. Postupak za određivanje zaštitnih mjera

Imposing a protective measure

## Član 26

A protective measure may be imposed in addition to a penalty or as an independent sanction.

The body for misdemeanors may impose one or more protective measures on the perpetrator of violence when the conditions for their imposition prescribed by this law exist.

The body for misdemeanors may decide to extend the duration of the protective measures from Articles 21 to 25 of this law if the reasons for which they were determined have not ceased, but not longer than two years. Applicants

## Član 27

An application for determining a protective measure (hereinafter: application) may be submitted by the victim or their representative, the social work center, that is, another institution of social and child protection, the police or the state prosecutor.

The body for misdemeanors may also determine a protective measure ex officio.

Naredba

## Article 28

A police officer may, in order to eliminate danger to the physical integrity of the victim, order the perpetrator of violence to be removed or prohibited from returning to an apartment or other residential space, which may not last longer than three days.

A written order on removal or prohibition from returning to an apartment or other residential space must be delivered by the police officer to the perpetrator of violence and the victim immediately, and at the latest within two hours, in the presence of an adult person who may be another police officer, but who is not a family member. The written order from paragraph 2 of this article must contain: the day and hour of removal or prohibition from returning to the apartment or other residential space; the boundaries of the area in which the perpetrator of violence may not move, stay or approach the victim; and the address of residence of the perpetrator of violence for the duration of the removal or prohibition from returning.

When leaving the apartment or other residential space, the perpetrator of violence has the right to take his personal belongings necessary for daily life and the obligation to hand over the keys to the apartment or other residential space to the police officer.

The police officer shall attach the written order from paragraph 2 of this article to the official report on the event and shall immediately, and at the latest within 12 hours, notify the misdemeanor authority and the social work center.

The misdemeanor authority shall, within the time limit from paragraph 1 of this article, decide whether to impose a protective measure. The more detailed content and appearance of the written order form from paragraph 2 of this article and the manner of conducting actions in the application of the provisions of this article shall be prescribed by the ministry responsible for internal affairs. Imposition of protective measure before and during proceedings

## Article 29

If it assesses that it is necessary to protect the victim without delay, the misdemeanor authority may impose a protective measure before initiating and during proceedings, at the latest within 48 hours from the time of receipt of the request.

The misdemeanor authority may request the social work center or other social and child protection institution to provide assistance in obtaining necessary evidence and to express its opinion on the appropriateness of the requested protective measure.

If the request was submitted before the initiation of proceedings, and the applicant of that request does not submit a request for initiation of proceedings within five days, the misdemeanor authority shall revoke the imposed protective measure.

The misdemeanor authority is obliged to warn the applicant of the consequences of failure to submit the request from paragraph 3 of this article.

Appeal

## Article 30

An appeal against the decision on determination of a protective measure may be filed within three days from the day of delivery of the decision.

The appeal is decided upon by the second-instance authority within three days from the day of receipt of the appeal.

The appeal does not postpone the execution of the decision on determination of a protective measure.

Extension, termination and replacement of protective measure imposed before and during proceedings

## Article 31

A protective measure imposed before and during proceedings may last until the reasons for which the measure was determined cease, and at the longest until the completion of the proceedings.

Before the completion of the proceedings, the misdemeanor authority may replace the protective measure imposed before and during proceedings with another protective measure.

Obligation to act in accordance with protective measure

## Article 32

The perpetrator of violence is obliged to act in accordance with the imposed protective measure.

A person who, in the scope of his work, learns that the perpetrator of violence is not acting in accordance with the imposed protective measure is obliged to notify the misdemeanor authority, the social work center, or other social and child protection institution, the police or the state prosecutor. Execution of decision

## Article 33

The misdemeanor authority is obliged to deliver the decision on the imposed protective measure immediately to the authority or institution responsible for carrying out the execution, and at the latest within three days from the day of adoption of the decision.

The decision on the imposed protective measure from Articles 21, 22 and 23 of this law shall be delivered for execution to the police.

The decision on the imposed protective measure from Articles 24 and 25 of this law shall be delivered to the authority, or institution that executes the protective measure in accordance with the law governing the treatment and rehabilitation of persons dependent on psychoactive substances (alcoholics and drug addicts) and persons with other behavioral disorders.

The more detailed manner of execution of the decision on the imposed protective measure from Articles 21, 22 and 23 of this law shall be prescribed by the ministry responsible for internal affairs, and the more detailed manner of execution of the decision on the imposed protective measure from Articles 24 and 25 of this law shall be prescribed by the ministry responsible for health affairs. Delivery of decision and records

## Article 34

The misdemeanor authority is obliged to deliver the decision on the imposed protective measure to the social work center in whose territory the victim and the perpetrator of violence have residence or place of stay. Records on reported cases of violence, victims, perpetrators of violence, imposed protective measures, as well as other protection and assistance measures shall be kept by the authorities and institutions from Article 5 paragraph 1 of this law, in accordance with their competences, and shall be submitted to the ministry responsible for the protection of human and minority rights.

Applicable application of law

## Article 35

The provisions of laws governing the work and powers of the police, misdemeanor procedure, criminal procedure, criminal sanctions and their application and execution shall apply to the procedure for determination and implementation of protective measures, unless otherwise regulated by this law.

III. PENAL PROVISIONS

Misdemeanor liability of family member

## Article 36

A fine of 150 euros or imprisonment of at least ten days shall be imposed for a misdemeanor on a family member if he:

- 1) uses physical force, regardless of whether bodily injury of another family member has occurred;
- 2) threatens with attack or causes danger that may cause a feeling of personal insecurity or
- 3) verbally attacks, swears at, calls by derogatory name and in another manner insults another family member;
- 4) restricts another family member's freedom to communicate with third parties;
- 5) exhausts by work, denies sleep and other rest, threatens with eviction from the apartment and taking away of children;
- 6) sexually harasses another family member;
- 7) stalks and in another manner grossly harasses another family member;
- 8) damages or destroys joint property or property of another family member or attempts to do so;
- 9) denies basic means of subsistence to another family member;
- 10) by insolent behavior endangers the family peace of a family member with whom he does not live in a family community (Article 8 paragraph 1).

A fine of at least 250 euros or imprisonment of at least twenty days shall be imposed for a misdemeanor on an adult family member if he commits violence from paragraph 1 of this article in the presence of a child.

A fine of at least 500 euros or imprisonment of at least thirty days shall be imposed for a misdemeanor on a family member who commits violence from paragraph 1 of this article if the victim is a child. A fine of at least 1,000 euros or imprisonment of at least 60 days shall be imposed for a misdemeanor on a family member who conceals a family member with special needs (Article 8 paragraph 3). + See:

- 2) objectives and measures for improving social and other protection, particularly in relation to: raising the level of public awareness of the problem of violence and forming attitudes about the unacceptability of violence; development of violence prevention programs; support to the family in violence prevention; further development of the normative framework in the field of protection; strengthening cooperation of bodies, institutions, organizations and other legal entities and natural persons dealing with protection; acquisition of new knowledge and skills by all those dealing with protection; improvement of the system for collecting and analyzing data and reporting on cases of violence.

The activities for implementing the objectives and measures referred to in paragraph 1 item 2 of this Article shall be determined by an action plan for the implementation of the Strategy.

The Strategy and the action plan for the implementation of the Strategy shall be adopted by the Government of Montenegro.

2. Protective measures

Purpose of protective measures

## Article 19

Protective measures shall be imposed for the purpose of preventing and suppressing violence, eliminating the consequences of committed violence and undertaking effective measures for the re-education of the perpetrator of violence and eliminating circumstances that favor or encourage the commission of new violence.

Types of protective measures

## Article 20

One or more protective measures may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence:

- 1) removal from the apartment or other dwelling space (hereinafter: removal from the apartment);
- 2) prohibition of approach;
- 3) prohibition of harassment and stalking;
- 4) mandatory treatment for addiction;
- 5) mandatory psychosocial treatment.

The bodies and institutions referred to in Article 5 paragraph 1 of this Law shall be obliged to provide the perpetrator of violence with appropriate information about their rights.

Removal from the apartment

## Article 21

Removal from the apartment may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence who lives with the victim in an apartment or other dwelling space, regardless of the ownership and other rights of the perpetrator of violence and the victim to the apartment or other dwelling space, if there is a danger that they will commit violence again.

The perpetrator of violence to whom removal from the apartment has been imposed shall be obliged to immediately leave the apartment or other dwelling space.

Removal from the apartment shall be imposed for a duration that cannot be shorter than thirty days nor longer than six months.

Prohibition of approach

## Article 22

Prohibition of approach to the victim may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence if there is a danger that they will commit violence again or if the presence of the perpetrator of violence in the vicinity of the victim would create in the victim a high degree of mental suffering which, for a shorter or longer time, prevents the victim's normal mental activities. The body for misdemeanors shall be obliged to determine in the decision on prohibition of approach the place or area in which the perpetrator of violence may not approach the victim.

Prohibition of approach shall be imposed for a duration that cannot be shorter than thirty days nor longer than one year.

Prohibition of harassment and stalking

## Article 23

Prohibition of harassment and stalking of the victim may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence if there is a danger that they will commit violence again.

The protective measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be imposed for a duration that cannot be shorter than thirty days nor longer than one year.

Mandatory treatment for addiction

## Article 24

Mandatory treatment for addiction may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence who commits violence as a result of addiction to alcohol, narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances, and there is a danger that they will commit violence again due to that addiction.

The protective measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall last as long as there is a need for treatment, but not longer than one year.

Mandatory psychosocial treatment

## Article 25

Mandatory psychosocial treatment may be imposed on the perpetrator of violence for the purpose of eliminating the causes of violent behavior and re-education, that is, with the aim of reducing and eliminating the danger that they will commit violence again.

The protective measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall last until the cessation of the reasons for which it was imposed, but not longer than six months.

The more detailed manner of determining and implementing the protective measure referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be prescribed by the ministry responsible for social welfare affairs.

3. Procedure for determining protective measures

Imposition of protective measure

## Član 26

A protective measure may be imposed together with a penalty or as an independent sanction.

The misdemeanor authority may impose on the perpetrator of violence one or more protective measures when conditions for their imposition prescribed by this law exist.

The misdemeanor authority may decide to extend the duration of protective measures from Articles 21 to 25 of this law if the reasons for which they were determined have not ceased, but not longer than two years. Applicants

## Član 27

A request for determination of a protective measure (hereinafter: request) may be submitted by the victim or their representative, social welfare center, or other social and child protection institution, police, or state prosecutor.

The misdemeanor authority may also determine a protective measure ex officio.

Order

## Član 28

A police officer may, in order to remove danger to the physical integrity of the victim, order the perpetrator of violence removal or prohibition of return to the apartment or other residential space, which cannot last longer than three days.

A written order on removal or prohibition of return to the apartment or other residential space must be handed by the police officer to the perpetrator of violence and the victim immediately, and at the latest within two hours, in the presence of an adult person who may be another police officer, and who is not a family member. The written order from paragraph 2 of this Article must contain: the day and time of removal or prohibition of return to the apartment or other residential space; the boundaries of the area in which the perpetrator of violence may not move, stay or approach the victim and the address of residence of the perpetrator of violence during the duration of removal or prohibition of return.

When leaving the apartment or other residential space, the perpetrator of violence has the right to take their personal belongings necessary for daily life and the obligation to hand over to the police officer the keys to the apartment or other residential space.

The police officer attaches the written order from paragraph 2 of this Article to the official report on the event and immediately, and at the latest within 12 hours, notifies the misdemeanor authority and the social welfare center.

The misdemeanor authority shall, within the time limit from paragraph 1 of this Article, decide whether to impose a protective measure. The detailed content and form of the written order from paragraph 2 of this Article and the manner of carrying out actions in the application of the provisions of this Article shall be prescribed by the ministry responsible for internal affairs. Imposition of protective measures before and during proceedings

## Član 29

If it assesses that it is necessary to protect the victim without delay, the misdemeanor authority may impose a protective measure before initiation and during proceedings, at the latest within 48 hours from the time of receipt of the request.

The misdemeanor authority may request from the social welfare center or other social and child protection institution to provide assistance in obtaining necessary evidence and to present its opinion on the appropriateness of the requested protective measure.

If the request is submitted before initiation of proceedings, and the applicant does not submit a request for initiation of proceedings within five days, the misdemeanor authority shall revoke the imposed protective measure.

The misdemeanor authority is obliged to warn the applicant of the consequences of failure to submit the request from paragraph 3 of this Article.

Appeal

## Član 30

An appeal may be filed against the decision on determination of a protective measure within three days from the day of delivery of the decision.

The second-instance authority decides on the appeal within three days from the day of receipt of the appeal.

An appeal does not postpone the execution of the decision on determination of a protective measure.

Extension, termination and replacement of protective measures imposed before and during proceedings

## Član 31

A protective measure imposed before and during proceedings may last until the reasons for which the measure was determined cease, and at the longest until the completion of proceedings.

Before completion of proceedings, the misdemeanor authority may replace a protective measure imposed before and during proceedings with another protective measure.

Obligation to act in accordance with protective measure

## Član 32

The perpetrator of violence is obliged to act in accordance with the imposed protective measure.

A person who, in the scope of their work, learns that the perpetrator of violence is not acting in accordance with the imposed protective measure is obliged to inform the misdemeanor authority, social welfare center, or other social and child protection institution, police or state prosecutor. Execution of decision

## Član 33

The misdemeanor authority is obliged to deliver the decision on imposed protective measure immediately to the authority or institution responsible for carrying out execution, and at the latest within three days from the day of adoption of the decision.

The decision on imposed protective measure from Articles 21, 22 and 23 of this law is delivered for execution to the police.

The decision on imposed protective measure from Articles 24 and 25 of this law is delivered to the authority, or institution that executes the protective measure in accordance with the law governing treatment and rehabilitation of persons dependent on psychoactive substances (alcoholics and drug addicts) and persons with other behavioral disorders.

The detailed manner of execution of the decision on imposed protective measure from Articles 21, 22 and 23 of this law is prescribed by the ministry responsible for internal affairs, and the detailed manner of execution of the decision on imposed protective measure from Articles 24 and 25 of this law is prescribed by the ministry responsible for health affairs. Delivery of decision and records

## Član 34

The misdemeanor authority is obliged to deliver the decision on imposed protective measure to the social welfare center in whose territory the victim and perpetrator of violence have residence or domicile. Records on reported cases of violence, victims, perpetrators of violence, imposed protective measures, as well as other protection and assistance measures are kept by authorities and institutions from Article 5 paragraph 1 of this law, in accordance with their competences and deliver them to the ministry responsible for protection of human and minority rights.

Appropriate application of law

## Član 35

The provisions of laws governing the work and powers of police, misdemeanor proceedings, criminal proceedings, criminal sanctions and their application and execution apply to the procedure for determining and implementing protective measures, unless otherwise regulated by this law.

III. PENAL PROVISIONS

Misdemeanor liability of family member

## Član 36

A family member shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine of at least three times the amount of the minimum wage in Montenegro or imprisonment of at least ten days, if:

- 1) they use physical force, regardless of whether bodily injury to another family member has occurred;
- 2) they threaten with attack or cause danger that may cause a feeling of personal insecurity or
- 3) they verbally attack, curse, call by derogatory names and in other ways insult another family member;
- 4) they restrict another family member's freedom to communicate with third parties;
- 5) they exhaust with work, deny sleep and other rest, threaten with eviction from the apartment and taking away children;
- 6) they sexually harass another family member;
- 7) they stalk and in other ways grossly harass another family member;
- 8) they damage or destroy common property or property of another family member or attempt to do so;
- 9) they deny basic means of subsistence to another family member;
- 10) they endanger by insolent behavior the family peace of a family member with whom they do not live in a family community (Article 8 paragraph 1).

An adult family member shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine of at least five times the amount of the minimum wage in Montenegro or imprisonment of at least twenty days if they commit violence from paragraph 1 of this Article in the presence of a child.

A family member who commits violence from paragraph 1 of this Article if the victim is a child shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine of at least ten times the amount of the minimum wage in Montenegro or imprisonment of at least thirty days.

A family member who conceals a family member with special needs (Article 8 paragraph 3) shall be punished for a misdemeanor with a fine of at least twenty times the amount of the minimum wage in Montenegro or imprisonment of at least 60 days.

Misdemeanor liability for neglect

## Član 37

A family member who does not take sufficient care shall be punished for a misdemeanor by a fine of at least five times the minimum wage in Montenegro or imprisonment of at least ten days:

- 1) of nutrition, hygiene, clothing, medical care or regular school attendance or does not prevent the child from harmful association, vagrancy, begging or theft, or otherwise to a greater extent neglects the upbringing and education of the child (Article 8, paragraph 2, item 1);
- 2) of nutrition, hygiene, clothing or medical care of another family member whom they are obliged to care for, and who needs special assistance due to illness, disability, old age or other personal characteristics due to which they are not able to take care of themselves (Article 8, paragraph 2, item 2).

Liability for failure to comply with an order

## Član 38

The perpetrator of violence who does not comply with the police officer's order to remove or prohibit return to the apartment or other living space shall be punished for a misdemeanor by a fine of at least fifteen times the minimum wage in Montenegro or imprisonment of at least forty days (Article 28, paragraphs 1 and 2).

Misdemeanor liability of another person

## Član 39

A fine of two to ten times the minimum wage in Montenegro shall be imposed for a misdemeanor on:

- 1) a responsible person in a state body, other body, health, educational and other institution, health and social worker, teacher, educator and other person who does not report violence committed to the police of which they become aware in the performance of their duties (Article 9, paragraph 2);
- 2) a person who in the course of their work learns that the perpetrator of violence does not act in accordance with the imposed protective measure and does not notify the misdemeanor authority, social welfare center or other institution of social and child protection, the police or the state prosecutor (Article 32, paragraph 2).
