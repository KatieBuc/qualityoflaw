###### Article 11.- Place of the act

1º The act shall be deemed to have been committed in all places where the perpetrator or participant executed the action or, in case of omission, should have executed it; or where the result provided for in the law has occurred or where it should have occurred according to the perpetrator's representation.

2º The participant151 shall be considered to have committed the act also in the place where the perpetrator would have committed it.

3° Paraguayan law shall be applicable to the participant in an act committed abroad, when the latter has acted in the national territory, even if the act lacked criminal sanction under the law in force in the place where it was committed.

###### Article 12.- Application to minors

This Code shall apply to acts committed by minors, unless the legislation on minor offenders provides otherwise152.

###### Article 23.- Mental Disorder

1º One who, at the time of the action or omission, due to mental disorder, incomplete or retarded psychological development, or serious disturbance of consciousness, was incapable of knowing the unlawfulness of the act or of determining himself in accordance with such knowledge is not culpable191.

###### Article 24.- Excess due to Confusion or Terror

One who commits an unlawful act by exceeding, due to confusion or terror, the limits of legitimate defense or of a justifying state of necessity, shall be exempted from penalty193.

###### Article 25.- Impossibility to Require Other Conduct

One who commits an unlawful act to repel or avert from himself, from a relative194 or from another person close to him, a present danger to his life, physical integrity or liberty, shall be exempted from penalty when, considering all the circumstances, no other conduct could be required of him. If other conduct could have been required, the penalty may be mitigated in accordance with article 67195.

###### Article 26.- Acts that Constitute It

There is an attempt when the perpetrator executes the decision to commit a punishable act by means of acts which, taking into account his representation of the act, are immediately prior to the consummation of the legal type196.

###### Article 27.- Punishability of the Attempt

1º The attempt of crimes197 is punishable; the attempt of offenses198 is punishable only in cases expressly provided for by law.

2º To the attempt are applicable the penal frameworks provided for consummated punishable acts.

3º When the perpetrator has not yet performed all the acts which, according to his representation of the act, are necessary to achieve its consummation, the penalty shall be mitigated in accordance with article 67.

##### CHAPTER III

PLURALITY OF PARTICIPANTS

###### Article 29.- Authorship

1º One who commits the act acting by himself or making use of another for this purpose shall be punished as an author.

2º One who acts in agreement with another in such a manner that, through his contribution to the act, he shares with the other the control over its execution shall also be punished as an author201.

###### Article 30.- Instigation

One who induces another to commit a willful unlawful act shall be punished as an instigator. The penalty shall be that provided for the author202.

###### Article 52.- Fine Penalty
