LAW

ON PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

("Official Gazette of RS", No. 94/2016 and 10/2023 - other law)

I INTRODUCTORY PROVISIONS

Subject of the Law

## Article 1

This law regulates the prevention of domestic violence and the actions of state authorities and institutions in preventing domestic violence and providing protection and support to victims of domestic violence.

This law does not apply to minors who commit domestic violence.

Purpose of the Law

## Article 2

The purpose of this law is to regulate in a general and uniform manner the organization and actions of state authorities and institutions and thereby enable effective prevention of domestic violence and urgent, timely and effective protection and support to victims of domestic violence. Prevention of domestic violence, imminent danger of domestic violence, domestic violence

## Article 3

Prevention of domestic violence consists of a set of measures for detecting whether there is an imminent danger of domestic violence and a set of measures applied when imminent danger has been detected.

Imminent danger of domestic violence exists when the behavior of a potential perpetrator and other circumstances indicate that he or she is prepared to commit or repeat domestic violence for the first time in the immediately foreseeable future.

Domestic violence, within the meaning of this law, is an act of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence by a perpetrator against a person with whom the perpetrator is in a current or former marital or extramarital or partnership relationship, or against a person with whom he or she is a blood relative in direct line, and in collateral line up to the second degree, or with whom he or she is related by affinity up to the second degree, or whose adopter, adoptee, foster child or foster parent he or she is, or against another person with whom he or she lives or has lived in a common household. Application of this law to certain criminal offenses

## Article 4

This law also applies to cooperation in the prevention of domestic violence (Articles 24-27) in criminal proceedings for criminal offenses:

- 1) stalking (Article 138a of the Criminal Code);
- 2) rape (Article 178. of the Criminal Code);
- 3) sexual intercourse with a helpless person (Article 179. of the Criminal Code);
- 4) sexual intercourse with a child (Article 180. of the Criminal Code);
- 5) sexual intercourse by abuse of position (Article 181. of the Criminal Code);
- 6) prohibited sexual acts (Article 182. of the Criminal Code);
- 7) sexual harassment (Article 182a of the Criminal Code);
- 8) pimping and enabling sexual intercourse (Article 183. of the Criminal Code);
- 9) mediation in prostitution (Article 184. of the Criminal Code);
- 10) showing, obtaining and possessing pornographic material and exploitation of minors for pornography (Article
  - 185. of the Criminal Code);
- 11) inducing a child to witness sexual acts (Article 185a of the Criminal Code);
- 12) neglect and abuse of a minor (Article 193. of the Criminal Code);
- 13) domestic violence (Article 194. of the Criminal Code);
- 14) failure to provide maintenance (Article 195. of the Criminal Code);
- 15) breach of family obligations (Article 196. of the Criminal Code);
- 16) incest (Article 197. of the Criminal Code);
- 17) human trafficking (Article 388. of the Criminal Code);
- 18) other criminal offenses, if the criminal offense is a consequence of domestic violence.

This law also applies to the provision of protection and support to victims of criminal offenses referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article (hereinafter: criminal offenses specified by this law).

Application of other regulations

## Article 5

Unless otherwise specified by this law, the prevention of domestic violence, in proceedings against perpetrators of criminal offenses specified by this law, and the provision of protection and support to victims of domestic violence and victims of criminal offenses specified by this law shall be governed by the Criminal Code, the Code of Criminal Procedure, the Law on Civil Procedure, the Family Law and the Law on Police.

II COMPETENT AUTHORITIES AND INSTITUTIONS

## Article 7

The police, public prosecutor's offices, courts of general jurisdiction and misdemeanor courts, as competent state authorities, and centers for social work, as institutions, are responsible for the prevention of domestic violence and the provision of protection and support to victims of domestic violence and victims of criminal offenses specified by this law.

In addition to the competent state authorities and centers for social work, other institutions in the field of child, social protection, education, upbringing and health (hereinafter: state authorities and institutions competent for the application of this law), as well as bodies for gender equality at the level of local self-governments, participate in the prevention of domestic violence through the provision of assistance and notification of violence, as well as the provision of support to victims of violence.

Support to victims of domestic violence and victims of criminal offenses specified by this law may also be provided by other legal entities and natural persons and associations.

Police

## Article 8

The head of the regional police directorate designates police officers who have completed specialized training to prevent domestic violence and provide protection to victims of violence (hereinafter: competent police officer).

Public Prosecutor's Office

## Article 9

In each public prosecutor's office, except those of special jurisdiction, the public prosecutor designates deputy public prosecutors who have completed specialized training to exercise the competences of the public prosecutor's office in the prevention of domestic violence and the prosecution of perpetrators of criminal offenses specified by this law.

Courts

## Article 10

The president of each court of general jurisdiction and misdemeanor court designates judges who have completed specialized training to adjudicate cases of prevention of domestic violence and criminal offenses specified by this law.

Cases of prevention of domestic violence are considered to be proceedings for the extension of urgent measures, proceedings for the determination of protection measures against domestic violence provided for by the Family Law (hereinafter: protection measures against domestic violence) and misdemeanor proceedings for misdemeanors provided for by this law (Article 36).

Center for Social Work

## Article 11

The head of each center for social work designates a team of experts among the employees of the center to assist in the prevention of domestic violence and provide support to victims of violence.

III PROCEDURE

General Rules

## Article 12

State authorities and institutions competent for the application of this law are obliged to quickly, effectively and in a coordinated manner prevent domestic violence and the commission of criminal offenses specified by this law and to provide the victim with protection, legal assistance and psychosocial and other support for his or her recovery, empowerment and independence.

a) Prevention of domestic violence

Reporting and recognition of domestic violence

## Article 13

Everyone must report without delay to the police or the public prosecutor domestic violence or imminent danger thereof. State and other authorities, organizations and institutions are obliged to immediately report to the police or the public prosecutor any knowledge of domestic violence or imminent danger thereof.

The competent state authorities and centers for social work (Articles 8-11) are obliged to recognize domestic violence or the danger thereof within the scope of their regular duties.

Recognition may result from the examination of a report submitted by the victim of violence to anyone, the observation of traces of physical or other violence on the victim and other circumstances indicating the existence of domestic violence or imminent danger thereof. A public prosecutor to whom violence or imminent danger thereof has been reported is obliged to immediately forward the report to police officers so that they may inform the competent police officer thereof (Article 14, paragraph 1).

Actions of police officers

## Član 14

Police officers are obliged to immediately notify the competent police officer of any domestic violence or immediate danger thereof, regardless of how they became aware of it, and have the right to, independently or at the request of the competent police officer, bring the potential perpetrator to the competent organizational unit of the police for the purpose of conducting proceedings.

Detention in the competent organizational unit of the police for the purpose of conducting proceedings may last a maximum of eight hours. During the proceedings in the competent organizational unit of the police, the potential perpetrator must be instructed and enabled to contact and use the services of a defense counsel and legal assistance in accordance with the Constitution and laws of the Republic of Serbia.

Procedure of the competent police officer

## Član 15

The competent police officer must provide the potential perpetrator who has been brought to the competent organizational unit of the police with the opportunity to state their position on all relevant facts, collect necessary information from other police officers, immediately assess the risk of immediate danger of domestic violence (hereinafter: risk assessment) and, under the conditions determined by this law (Article 17, paragraph 1), impose an emergency measure to prevent domestic violence (hereinafter: emergency measure). If the potential perpetrator has not been brought to the competent organizational unit of the police, the competent police officer assesses the risk immediately upon receiving notification from police officers about violence or immediate danger thereof.

Before completing the risk assessment, the competent police officer may, if necessary, request the opinion of the social welfare center. Risk assessment

## Član 16

The risk assessment is based on available information and takes place as soon as possible.

When assessing the risk, particular consideration is given to whether the potential perpetrator has previously or immediately before the risk assessment committed domestic violence and whether they are ready to repeat it, whether they have threatened murder or suicide, whether they possess weapons, whether they are mentally ill or abuse psychoactive substances, whether there is a conflict over custody of a child or over the manner of maintaining personal relations between the child and the parent who is the potential perpetrator, whether an emergency measure has been imposed on the potential perpetrator or a protection measure against domestic violence has been determined, whether the victim experiences fear and how they assess the risk of violence.

The competent police officer immediately submits all available information about domestic violence or immediate danger thereof and the risk assessment - if it indicates an immediate danger of violence - to the basic public prosecutor in whose area the victim's place of residence or temporary residence is located, to the social welfare center and to the coordination and cooperation group.

If the competent police officer establishes the existence of a danger that is not immediate, all available information about domestic violence or danger thereof and their risk assessment is submitted to the basic public prosecutor and the social welfare center. Emergency measures

## Član 17

If, after the risk assessment, they establish an immediate danger of domestic violence, the competent police officer issues an order imposing an emergency measure on the perpetrator who has been brought to the competent organizational unit of the police (Article 15, paragraph 1). Emergency measures are: a measure of temporary removal of the perpetrator from the dwelling and a measure of temporary prohibition for the perpetrator to contact and approach the victim of violence.

Both emergency measures may be imposed by the order.

The order contains: the name of the authority issuing it, data about the person to whom the emergency measure is imposed, the type of emergency measure being imposed and its duration, the day and hour of imposition of the emergency measure, and the obligation of the person to whom the emergency measure has been imposed to report to the police officer who imposed it upon its expiration.

The order is delivered to the person to whom the emergency measure has been imposed. If they refuse to accept the order, the competent police officer composes a note about it, whereby the order is considered delivered.

The competent police officer submits the order, immediately after its delivery, to the basic public prosecutor in whose area the victim's place of residence or temporary residence is located, to the social welfare center and to the coordination and cooperation group, and the victim of violence is notified in writing about the type of emergency measure that has been imposed.

Procedure of the public prosecutor

## Član 18

After receiving the notification, risk assessment and order, the basic public prosecutor studies the notifications and evaluates the risk assessment of the competent police officer.

If, after that, they establish an immediate danger of domestic violence, they are obliged to submit to the court a motion to extend the emergency measure, within 24 hours from the hour of delivery of the order to the person to whom the emergency measure was imposed.

Along with the motion, the basic public prosecutor submits to the court the risk assessment of the competent police officer, their evaluation of their risk assessment, and other evidence indicating an immediate danger of domestic violence.

Procedure of the first instance court

## Član 19

The motion to extend the emergency measure is submitted to the basic court in whose area the victim's place of residence or temporary residence is located, and the motion is decided by a single judge.

The court extends the emergency measure if, after evaluating the risk assessment of the competent police officer, evaluating the risk assessment made by the basic public prosecutor, assessing the attached evidence and allegations from the motion of the basic public prosecutor, and assessing the statement of the person to whom the emergency measure was imposed, it establishes an immediate danger of domestic violence; otherwise it rejects the motion as unfounded.

The decision on the motion is issued without holding a hearing, within 24 hours from receipt of the motion to extend the emergency measure. Appeal against the decision of the basic court

## Član 20

The basic public prosecutor and the person to whom the emergency measure was imposed may file an appeal against the decision of the basic court to the higher court, within three days from the day of receipt of the decision, through the basic court that issued the decision.

The basic court is obliged to forward the appeal and all case files to the higher court within 12 hours from receipt of the appeal.

The appeal is decided by a panel of the higher court consisting of three judges, within three days from the day it received the appeal from the basic court. The higher court may reject the appeal and uphold the decision of the basic court or accept the appeal and modify the decision of the basic court. It may not annul the decision of the basic court and return the case to the basic court for retrial.

The appeal does not stay the execution of the decision of the basic court.

The law governing civil procedure applies mutatis mutandis to the procedure for deciding on the extension of an emergency measure, unless otherwise determined by this law.

Duration of emergency measures

## Član 21

An emergency measure imposed by the competent police officer lasts 48 hours from delivery of the order.

The court may extend the emergency measure for an additional 30 days.

If the emergency measure of temporary removal of the perpetrator from the dwelling is extended, the person to whom it was imposed may, accompanied by police officers, take necessary personal belongings from the dwelling.

b) Special provisions on criminal procedure

Obligation to report a criminal offense

## Član 22

A person who has knowledge of a committed criminal offense determined by this law is obliged to report it to the police or public prosecutor.

Urgency in deciding on measures to ensure the presence of the accused

## Član 23

In criminal proceedings conducted for criminal offenses determined by this law, the court is obliged to decide within 24 hours on the motion of the public prosecutor for determining the measure of prohibition of approach, meeting or communication with a certain person and visiting certain places, the measure of prohibition of leaving the place of temporary residence, and the measure of prohibition of leaving the dwelling.

IV COOPERATION IN PREVENTING DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

Persons designated for liaison

## Član 24

Liaison persons are appointed in the police administration, basic and higher public prosecutor's office, basic and higher court, and social work center.

They are appointed by the head of the police administration, the public prosecutor, the president of the court, and the head of the social work center, from among competent police officers and judges and deputy public prosecutors who have completed specialized training, and employees of the social work center.

Liaison persons exchange on a daily basis information and data relevant for the prevention of domestic violence, detection, prosecution and trial for criminal offenses prescribed by this law, and for the provision of protection and support to victims of domestic violence and victims of criminal offenses prescribed by this law.

The minister responsible for internal affairs, the minister responsible for justice affairs, and the minister responsible for family protection affairs shall jointly prescribe the manner of exchanging information and data between liaison persons.

Grupa za koordinaciju i saradnju

## Član 25

A coordination and cooperation group shall be formed in the territory of each basic public prosecutor's office.

It considers each case of domestic violence that has not been concluded by a final court decision in civil or criminal proceedings, cases where protection and support should be provided to victims of domestic violence and victims of criminal offenses under this law, prepares an individual plan for protection and support of the victim, and proposes measures to the competent public prosecutor's office for the conclusion of court proceedings.

The coordination and cooperation group holds meetings at least once every 15 days, and keeps minutes of the course of the meeting. Representatives of educational, upbringing and health institutions and the National Employment Service, representatives of other legal entities and associations, and individuals who provide protection and support to victims may attend the meetings as needed. The coordination and cooperation group adopts rules of procedure which further regulate its manner of work and decision-making. Sastav grupe za koordinaciju i saradnju

## Član 26

The coordination and cooperation group consists of representatives of basic public prosecutor's offices, police administrations, and social work centers, from the territory for which the group is formed.

Members of the coordination and cooperation group are appointed by the heads of the bodies, from among deputy basic public prosecutors who have completed specialized training and competent police officers and employees of social work centers who work on cases of domestic violence.

The coordination and cooperation group is chaired by a member of the group from among deputy basic public prosecutors.

If a higher public prosecutor's office is competent for the prosecution of perpetrators of criminal offenses prescribed by this law, the higher public prosecutor shall appoint his or her deputy, who has completed specialized training, to participate in the work of the group and to chair it.

Pravilnik o saradnji

## Član 27

The minister responsible for justice affairs, the minister responsible for internal affairs, and the minister responsible for family protection affairs shall jointly adopt a rulebook on cooperation, which further regulates mutual rights.

The rulebook on cooperation determines the obligations and cooperation of state bodies and institutions competent for the implementation of this law in the prevention of domestic violence and the provision of protection and support to victims of domestic violence and victims of criminal offenses prescribed by this law.

V OBUKA

Specijalizovana obuka

## Član 28

Competent police officers and public prosecutors, deputy public prosecutors, and judges who apply this law are obliged to complete specialized training according to a program adopted by the Judicial Academy.

Specialized training is conducted by the Judicial Academy for public prosecutors, deputy public prosecutors, and judges, in cooperation with other professional institutions and organizations, and for police officers, specialized training is conducted by the Criminal Police Academy.

Upon completion of specialized training, the Judicial Academy and the Criminal Police Academy issue certificates of completed training to the trainees.

The issuance and form of the certificate shall be further regulated by an act of the Judicial Academy and the Criminal Police Academy.

VI ZAŠTITA I PODRŠKA ŽRTVAMA NASILJA U PORODICI I ŽRTVAMA KRIVIČNIH

DELA ODREĐENIH OVIM ZAKONOM

Pravo na obaveštenje

## Član 29

State bodies and institutions competent for the implementation of this law are obliged to provide, at the first contact with a victim of domestic violence or a victim of a criminal offense under this law, complete information to the victim about the bodies, legal entities, and associations that provide protection and support to the victim, in a manner and language that the victim of violence understands.

Pravo na besplatnu pravnu pomoć

## Član 30

A victim of domestic violence and a victim of a criminal offense under this law has the right to free legal aid, in accordance with a special law.

Individualni plan zaštite i podrške žrtvi

## Član 31

Upon receipt of a risk assessment establishing an immediate danger of domestic violence, the coordination and cooperation group prepares an individual plan for protection and support of the victim, which contains comprehensive and effective measures of protection and support for the victim, as well as for other family members who need support.

The victim also participates in the preparation of the individual plan for protection and support of the victim, if he or she wishes and if his or her emotional and physical condition permits.

Protection measures must provide safety to the victim, stop the violence, prevent its recurrence, and protect the victim's rights, while support measures must enable the victim to be provided with psychosocial and other support for his or her recovery, empowerment, and independence. The individual plan for protection and support of the victim determines the executors of specific measures and deadlines for their implementation, as well as a plan for monitoring and assessing the effectiveness of the planned and implemented measures.

An individual plan for protection and support of the victim is also prepared for victims of criminal offenses under this law.

VII EVIDENCIJA PODATAKA O SLUČAJEVIMA NASILJA U PORODICI

Evidencije

## Article 32

The competent police administration maintains records of reported cases of domestic violence and of the imposition and enforcement of urgent measures and enforcement of protective measures against domestic violence.

The records of the police administration contain:

- 1) data on reported cases of domestic violence (participants in the event, time, place, collected statements, circumstances of the case, data on the possible victim, etc.);
- 2) data on the reported possible perpetrator (name, surname, unique citizen registration number, address of residence or stay, data on previously imposed protective measures against domestic violence);
- 3) data on risk assessment and names of authorities to which the risk assessment was submitted;
- 4) data on the imposition of urgent measures (date and number of the order imposing urgent measures, their duration and the time of commencement of their duration);
- 5) data on the extension and enforcement of urgent measures (number and date of the decision of the basic court on the extension of urgent measures, data on the enforcement of urgent measures);
- 6) data on the enforcement of protective measures against domestic violence.

The basic court maintains records of its decisions on proposals for extension of urgent measures and on imposed protective measures against domestic violence.

The records of the basic court on proposals for extension of urgent measures contain:

- 1) data on the person for whom the urgent measure was extended (name, surname, unique citizen registration number, address of residence or stay, data on previously imposed protective measures against domestic violence);
- 2) number and date of the decision by which the urgent measure was extended;
- 3) number and date of the decision by which the proposal to extend the urgent measure was rejected;
- 4) data on the appeal against the decision made on the proposal for extension of urgent measures;
- 5) data on the decision made on the appeal.

The records of the basic court on imposed protective measures against domestic violence contain:

- 1) data on the person for whom a protective measure against domestic violence was imposed (name, surname, unique citizen registration number, address of residence or stay, data on previously imposed protective measures against domestic violence);
- 2) data on the court's decision on imposing protective measures against domestic violence (number and date of the decision, type of measure imposed and its duration);
- 3) data on the appeal against the court's decision on imposing protective measures against domestic violence;
- 4) data on the decision made on the appeal;
- 5) data on the extension or termination of protective measures against domestic violence.

The basic public prosecutor's office maintains records of proposals for extension of urgent measures and requests for imposing protective measures against domestic violence.

The records of the basic public prosecutor's office contain:

- 1) data on the person for whom extension of urgent measures was proposed (name, surname, unique citizen registration number, address of residence or stay, data on previously imposed protective measures against domestic violence);
- 2) data on the extension of urgent measures (date and number of the proposal for extension of urgent measures, name of the court to which the extension of urgent measures was proposed, court decision on the public prosecutor's proposal, data on the decision on the appeal);
- 3) data on filing a lawsuit for imposing protective measures against domestic violence;
- 4) type of protective measure against domestic violence whose imposition is requested;
- 5) data on the court's decision regarding the lawsuit for imposing a protective measure against domestic violence (number and date of the decision, type of measure imposed and duration of the measure);
- 6) data on the extension and termination of protective measures against domestic violence.

The competent social welfare centre maintains records of the implementation of individual protection and support plans for the victim.

The records of the social welfare centre contain:

- 1) name, surname, unique citizen registration number and address of residence or stay of the victim;
- 2) data on the individual protection and support plan for the victim;
- 3) data on planned victim protection measures;
- 4) data on planned victim support measures;
- 5) data on the executors of specific protection and support measures and deadlines for their undertaking;
- 6) data on the plan for monitoring and assessing the effectiveness of planned and undertaken measures.

The records of police administrations, basic courts, basic public prosecutor's offices and social welfare centres are maintained in electronic form and constitute the Central Records of Domestic Violence Cases (hereinafter: Central Records), maintained by the Republic Public Prosecutor's Office.

Data may be entered into the Central Records only with the use of appropriate protected access codes. Data are kept in the records and in the Central Records for ten years and are then deleted.

Access to records

## Article 33

Access to data from the Central Records is permitted only for the purpose of exercising the competences provided for by this law and with the use of protected access codes.

The deputy public prosecutor who exercises the competences of the public prosecutor's office in preventing domestic violence and prosecuting perpetrators of criminal offences determined by this law (Article 9) has the right to access all data from the Central Records.

The competent police officer has the right to access the Central Records only in the part containing data from the records of regional police administrations and competent social welfare centres, competent courts only in the part containing data from the records maintained by basic courts, and competent social welfare centres - only in the part containing data from the records maintained by social welfare centres.

Protection of personal data

## Article 34

State authorities and institutions competent for the implementation of this law are obliged to protect personal data, in accordance with the law governing the protection of personal data.

The consent of the person to whom the data relate is not required for the collection of data contained in the records.

VIII MONITORING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE LAW

Council for Combating Domestic Violence

## Article 35

The Government establishes the Council for Combating Domestic Violence (hereinafter: Council), which monitors the implementation of this law and improves the coordination and effectiveness of prevention of domestic violence and protection from domestic violence.

The members of the Council consist of representatives of state authorities and institutions competent for the implementation of this law.

The Council may, as necessary, include in its work representatives of scientific and other professional institutions and associations whose activities are related to protection from domestic violence.

The composition, manner of work and decision-making of the Council shall be regulated in more detail by the Government's act on establishing the Council.

IX PENAL PROVISIONS

Misdemeanours

## Article 36

A person who violates an urgent measure imposed or extended on them shall be punished by imprisonment of up to 60 days for a misdemeanour. A responsible person in a state or other body, organization or institution who does not immediately report to the police or public prosecutor or does not respond to a report or obstructs the reporting or response to any knowledge of domestic violence or immediate danger thereof (Article 13, paragraph 2) shall be punished by a fine of 50,000 dinars to 150,000 dinars for a misdemeanour. A conviction for the misdemeanour referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article may be enforced before it becomes final, in accordance with the Law on Misdemeanours.

X TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

Deadline for adopting implementing regulations

## Article 37

The implementing regulations provided for by this law shall be adopted within three months from the entry into force of this law, except for the acts adopted by coordination and cooperation groups, which shall be adopted within 30 days from the date of their establishment. Deadline for establishing coordination and cooperation groups and the Council and appointing liaison persons

## Article 38

By the date of commencement of application of this law, coordination and cooperation groups and the Council shall be established and liaison persons shall be appointed.

Final provision

## Article 39

This law enters into force on the eighth day from the date of publication in the "Official Gazette of the Republic of Serbia", and shall be applied from

1 June 2017.
