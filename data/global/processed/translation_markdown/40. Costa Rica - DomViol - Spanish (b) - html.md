CRIMINALIZATION OF VIOLENCE AGAINST

TITLE I

GENERAL PART

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1

Purposes. The purpose of this law is to protect the rights of women victims of violence and to sanction forms of physical, psychological, sexual and patrimonial violence perpetrated against them, as a discriminatory practice based on gender, specifically in a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabiting, non-cohabiting, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, in fulfillment of the obligations contracted by the State in the Convention for the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, Law 6968, of October 2, 1984, as well as in the Inter-American Convention to Prevent, Sanction and Eradicate Violence against Women, Law 7499, of May 2, (As amended by

### article 1° of law N° 9975 of May 14, 2021)

### Article 2- Scope of application.

This law shall apply when the conducts classified as crimes are directed against a woman, in the context of or on the occasion of a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabiting, non-cohabiting, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty.

The scope of application of this law is extended to the cases contemplated in article 21 bis, denominated

"Femicide in other contexts", as well as to what is established in the second paragraph of articles 22 "Mistreatment", 23 "Restriction of freedom of movement" and 27 "Threats against a woman" of this law.

(The preceding paragraph was thus added by article 1° of the Law to establish extended femicide, N° 10022 of August 23, 2021)

(As amended by

### article 1° of law N° 9975 of May 14, 2021)

The sources of interpretation of this Law are all international human rights instruments in force in the country, which have a value similar to the Political Constitution, which, to the extent that they grant greater rights and guarantees to persons, prevail over the Political Constitution. In particular, the following shall be sources of interpretation of this Law:

- a) The Convention for the elimination of all forms of discrimination against women, Law Nº 6968, of October 2, 1984.
- b) The Inter-American Convention to prevent, sanction and eradicate violence against women, Law Nº 7499, of May 2, 1995.

### ARTICLE 5.- Obligations of

persons in public service

Those who, in the exercise of their functions, are obliged to know of situations of violence against women, in any of its forms, or to resolve them, must act promptly and effectively, respecting both the procedures and the human rights of the affected women; otherwise, they may incur in the crime of breach of duties.

A person who, in the exercise of a public function, files a formal complaint of any of the public action crimes contained in this Law shall not incur in a crime, even if the accused is not convicted, except when the crimes of slander and slanderous denunciation are configured.

### Article 7.

Protection of victims during the process. To protect the victims, the protection measures contemplated in Law N° 7586, Law against Domestic Violence, of April 10, 1996, may be requested from the beginning of the judicial investigation, as well as the necessary precautionary measures provided in Law Nº 7594, Criminal Procedural Code, of April 10, 1996.

Likewise, the judge may order the accused person to use the electronic device without prejudice to linking with the victim, in order to guarantee their protection. (As amended by article 7° of law

N° 9271 of September 30, 2014, "Electronic monitoring mechanisms in criminal matters")

### ARTICLE 8.- General

aggravating circumstances of the crime

The following shall be general aggravating circumstances of the punishable conducts described in this Law, with the exception of the crime of femicide, and provided they are not constitutive of the type, to perpetrate the act:

- a) Against a woman who has a sensory, physical or mental disability, total or partial, temporary or permanent.
- b) Against a woman over sixty-five years of age.
- c) Against a woman in a state of pregnancy or during the three months following childbirth.
- d) In the presence of the minor children of the victim or of the perpetrator of the crime.
- e) With the concurrence of other persons, with force against things or through the use of weapons.
- f) With treachery or cruelty.
- g) For price, reward, remunerative promise or advantage of any other nature.
- h) With the use of a high degree of scientific, professional or technological knowledge of the perpetrator in the commission of the crime.
- i) With the use of animals.

The judge who imposes the sentence shall increase by up to one third the sentence indicated for the corresponding crime, when one or several aggravating circumstances concur.

The principal penalty for the crimes recorded in this Law shall be imprisonment. The judge may opt for alternative sentences, if this does not place the life or integrity of the victim at risk or if the victim is harmed in the exercise of other rights. For this purpose, the trial court, prior to replacing the prison sentence, must order another complete psychological and psychiatric examination, if it considers it necessary; in addition, it must hear the victim's opinion. In case of replacement by discount of half of the sentence, the sentence execution judge must hear the victim previously, if the victim is locatable.

### ARTICLE 11.- Imposition and

replacement of alternative sentences

When a person who is a first-time offender in matters of violence against women is imposed a prison sentence of less than three years, said sentence, in accordance with article 9º of this Law, may be replaced by two alternative sentences of those indicated in this Law; one of them shall necessarily be the sentence of compliance with instructions, except when the sentence of banishment is applied.

Also, at the request of the convicted person, alternative sentences may be applied, when said person is a first-time offender in matters of violence against women, has been imposed a sentence exceeding three years, and has served at least half of it. The alternative sentence may not exceed the amount of the principal sentence imposed.

The weekend detention sentence shall consist of a limitation of freedom of movement and shall be served in a penitentiary center or in a rehabilitation center for periods corresponding to the weekends, with a minimum duration of twenty-four hours and a maximum of forty-eight hours per week.

### ARTICLE 13.- Penalty of

provision of public utility services

The penalty of provision of public utility services shall consist of the convicted person providing service at the places and times determined by the judge, in favor of public welfare establishments or community utility organizations, or social organizations, under the control of the authorities of said centers, in such a way that it does not violate the human rights of the convicted person, does not disturb their work activity, nor put the victim or third parties at risk. The periods for compliance with this penalty shall be eight to sixteen hours per week.

The non-compliance with an alternative penalty shall authorize the sentencing enforcement judge to revoke it and order that the prison sentence be applied to the convicted person for the remaining time of the sentence.

Upon commission of a new crime, the judge shall have the power to revoke the alternative penalty, if the person is subsequently sentenced in other criminal cases for violence against women.

Accessory penalties shall be applied together with the prison sentence or alternative penalties. The replacement of the main penalty with alternatives shall not affect compliance with the accessory penalty. The foregoing shall be carried out while respecting, at all times, the right of the accused to due process of law in criminal matters.

### ARTICLE 16.- Penalty of compliance

with instructions

The penalty of compliance with instructions shall consist of submission to a conduct plan in freedom, which shall be established by the judge who issues the sentence or by the sentencing enforcement judge and may contain the following instructions:

- a) Subject the person to an addiction treatment program for the control of alcohol consumption, narcotic substances, psychotropic substances or narcotic drugs, when such addiction is related to the sanctioned conduct or its circumstances.
- b) Subject the person to a specialized program for offenders, oriented to the control of violent behaviors and comprehensive psychological and psychiatric treatments.
- c) Prohibition of residence: this penalty consists of the prohibition to reside in a certain place and to go to it or transit through it without judicial authorization. The judge shall determine the place, which may be a neighborhood, a district, a canton or a province, taking into account the need to protect the victims. This instruction may in no case take the form of a punishment of banishment.
- d) Limitation on use of weapons: it shall consist of the prohibition of obtaining permits for possession, registration and carrying of weapons of any type. The final judgment imposing this penalty must be communicated to the National Arsenal of the Ministry of Public Security, which shall keep a file of such judgments, in order to consider any application for registration or carrying of firearms made by the sentenced person.

For the purposes of paragraphs a) and b) of this article, the National Institute of Women and the Ministry of Justice shall send each year, to the Supreme Court of Justice, the list of accredited institutions, public and private, to which the competent judicial authority may refer for compliance with this penalty. The expenses incurred for this treatment shall be borne by the State, unless the convicted person has sufficient resources to pay for them.

The penalty of disqualification shall produce the suspension or restriction to exercise one or more of the rights indicated in this article. In a reasoned judgment, the judge shall apply the pertinent penalties, according to the crime committed.

The penalty of disqualification shall consist of:

- a) Impediment to exercise public office, including those of popular election, the profession, trade or activity on the occasion of whose performance the crime was committed.
- b) Impediment to exercise guardianship, curatorship or judicial administration of property, when the crime has been committed taking advantage of these legal situations.

The penalty of disqualification may not be less than one year nor more than twelve years.

The replacement of the main penalty shall not affect compliance with the penalty of disqualification.

The person sentenced to the penalty of disqualification may be rehabilitated when half of the term has elapsed, if they have not violated the disqualification and if they have repaired the damage to the satisfaction of the victim.

When the disqualification has meant the loss of a public office, the rehabilitation shall not entail reinstatement in that office.

When a foreign person is imposed a prison sentence of five years or less, in judgment or during its execution, it may be replaced by the obligation to immediately leave the national territory and not to re-enter it for twice the time of the sentence. This penalty shall not be applied when it seriously harms the patrimonial interests of the victim nor when it makes compliance with family duties impossible. Re-entry to the country shall imply the revocation of the replacement, without prejudice to other responsibilities. For migratory control, the General Directorate of Migration and Foreigners shall keep a special index of this type of convicted persons.

### ARTICLE 20.-

Institutional responsibilities in the execution of alternative penalties The Ministry of Public Security shall assist the Judicial Branch and the Ministry of Justice and Grace in the formulation and operationalization of a system for the execution of the alternative penalties contemplated

### Article 21

Femicide. A prison sentence of twenty to thirty-five years shall be imposed on anyone who causes the death of a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or bond of partnership, whether marital, de facto union, courtship, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or similar, even if there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty.

(Thus reformed by

### article 1 of law No. 9975 of May 14, 2021)

### Article 21

bis- Femicide in other contexts. A prison sentence of twenty to thirty-five years shall be imposed on anyone who causes the death of a woman of legal age or minor, when one of the following circumstances is present:

- a) When the perpetrator has taken advantage of a relationship or bond of trust, friendship, kinship, authority or a power relationship that they had with the woman victim or it occurs within family relationships of consanguinity or affinity up to the third degree, whether or not they share or have shared the same domicile.
- b) When the perpetrator has a history of violence perpetrated against the woman victim, in the family, work, educational, community or religious sphere, even when the facts have not been previously reported.
- c) When the perpetrator is a sexual exploiter client, trafficker or pimp of the woman victim.
- d) When the woman victim had refused to establish or reestablish, with the perpetrator, a permanent or casual relationship or partnership bond, or to have any type of sexual contact.
- e) When the perpetrator commits the act to prepare, facilitate, consummate or conceal a sexual crime.
- f) When the perpetrator has committed the act using the woman victim as an act of revenge, reprisal or debt collection in organized drug trafficking crimes or other related crimes.
- g) When the perpetrator has committed the act by reason of the participation, position or political activity of the woman victim.

(Thus added by article 1 of the Law to establish extended femicide, No. 10022 of August 23, 2021)

### Artículo 22

Mistreatment. Whoever by any means strikes or physically abuses a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, without incapacitating her for her usual occupations, shall be imposed a prison sentence of three months to one year.

The same penalty shall be imposed when the acts of mistreatment are committed in any of the situations established in

### artículo 21 bis de esta ley.

(Thus added the preceding paragraph by article

3° of the Law to establish expanded femicide, N° 10022 of August 23, 2021)

If the action results in incapacity for her usual work for less than five days, a prison sentence of six months to one year shall be imposed, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty.

Whoever causes physical damage or damage to the health of a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty that produces incapacity for her usual occupations for a time greater than five days and up to one month, shall be imposed a prison sentence of eight months to two years.

(Thus reformed by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 23- Restriction on

freedom of movement. Whoever, without profit motive, deprives or restricts the freedom of movement of a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be punished with a prison sentence of two to ten years. The same penalty shall be imposed when the conducts are committed in any of the situations established in article 21 bis of this law.

(Thus added the preceding paragraph by article 3° of the Law to establish expanded femicide, N° 10022 of August 23, 2021)

(Thus reformed by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 25- Offenses to

dignity. Whoever verbally offends the dignity or decorum of a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be punished with a prison sentence of six months to two years.

(Thus reformed by article 1° of the law N° 9975 of May 14, 2021)

### Artículo 26- Restriction on

self-determination. A prison sentence of two to four years shall be imposed on whoever, through the use of threats, violence, intimidation, blackmail, persecution or harassment, forces a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, to do, cease to do or tolerate something to which she is not obligated.

(Thus reformed by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 27- Threats against a

woman. Whoever threatens a woman, her family or a third person intimately linked, with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be punished with a prison sentence of six months to two years.

The same penalty shall be imposed when the acts of threats are committed in any of the situations established in

### artículo 21 bis de esta ley.

(Thus added the preceding paragraph by article 3° of the Law to establish expanded femicide, N° 10022 of August 23, 2021)

(Thus reformed by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 29- Rape against a

woman. Whoever inserts the penis, orally, anally or vaginally, into a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, against her will, shall be punished with a prison sentence of twelve to eighteen years.

The same penalty shall be applied to whoever inserts any object, animal or body part, vaginally or anally, to whoever forces the victim to insert, anally or vaginally, any body part or object into the perpetrator or into herself.

(Thus reformed by article

1° of the law N° 9975 of May 14, 2021)

### Artículo 30- Abusive sexual

conduct. A penalty of imprisonment of three to six years shall be imposed on whoever forces a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, to endure during sexual relations acts that cause her pain or humiliation, to perform or view acts of exhibitionism, to view or listen to pornographic material or to view or listen to acts with sexual content. (Thus reformed by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 31- Sexual exploitation

of a woman. Whoever forces a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, to have sexual relations with third parties, without profit motive, shall be punished with a prison sentence of two to five years.

(Thus reformed by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### ARTÍCULO 32.- Aggravated forms

of sexual violence

The penalty for the crimes referred to in the three preceding articles shall be increased by up to one third, if any of the following consequences result from the commission of the act:

- a) Pregnancy of the victim.
- b) Transmission of a sexually transmitted disease to the victim.

### Artículo 34- Patrimonial

misappropriation. Whoever illegitimately misappropriates any property or asset from the possession or patrimony of a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether marital, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar relationship, even when there is divorce, separation or breakup, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be punished with a prison sentence of six months to three years.

(Thus reformed by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 35

Patrimonial damage. The person who, to the detriment of a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether matrimonial, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar, even when divorce, separation or rupture intervenes, destroys, renders useless, causes to disappear or damages a property owned, possessed or held by her, or a property that is susceptible to being joint property, shall be punished with a prison sentence of three months to two years, provided that it does not constitute another more severely punished crime.

(As amended by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 36- Limitation on the

exercise of the right to property. Shall be punished with a prison sentence of eight months to three years, the person who prevents, limits or prohibits the use, enjoyment, administration, transformation, alienation or disposition of one or more properties that form part of the patrimony of the woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether matrimonial, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar, even when divorce, separation or rupture intervenes, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty.

(As amended by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 37- Fraud of simulation

concerning properties susceptible to being joint property. The person who simulates the execution of an act, contract, procedure, legal or judicial writing, concerning properties susceptible to being joint property, to the detriment of the rights of a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether matrimonial, de facto union or cohabitation, even when divorce, separation or rupture intervenes, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be imposed a prison sentence of two months to three years, if the amount defrauded does not exceed ten times the base salary and, with imprisonment of six months to ten years, if the amount defrauded exceeds ten times the base salary.

(As amended by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 38- Diversion of the

profits of family economic activities. Shall be punished with a prison sentence of six months to one year, the person who unilaterally extracts the profits derived from a family economic activity or disposes of them for their exclusive personal benefit and to the detriment of the rights of a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether matrimonial, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar, even when divorce, separation or rupture intervenes, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty. (As amended by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### Artículo 39- Economic

exploitation of women. The person who, through the use of force, intimidation or coercion, has themselves maintained, totally or partially, by a woman with whom they maintain or have maintained a relationship or partnership bond, whether matrimonial, de facto union, dating, cohabitation, non-cohabitation, casual or other similar, even when divorce, separation or rupture intervenes, provided that the conduct does not constitute a more serious crime or one provided with a greater penalty, shall be punished with a prison sentence of six months to three years.

(As amended by

### artículo 1° de la ley N° 9975 del 14 de mayo del 2021)

### ARTÍCULO 41.- Obstruction of access to justice

The person who, in the exercise of a public function, promotes, by an illicit means, impunity or obstructs the police, judicial or administrative investigation for actions of physical, sexual, psychological or patrimonial violence, committed to the detriment of a woman, shall be punished with a prison sentence of three months to three years and disqualification for a period of one to four years from the exercise of public function. The penalty of disqualification for the crime of breach of duties shall be two to six years, if the breach occurs in a situation of risk to the personal integrity or economic need of the woman victim.

### ARTÍCULO 43.- Breach of

a protective measure

Shall be punished with a prison sentence of six months to two years, whoever breaches a protective measure issued by a competent authority, within a domestic violence proceeding in application of the Law against Domestic Violence.

### ARTÍCULO 44.- Application of the

general part of the Criminal Code

For the purposes of this Law, the provisions of the general part of the Criminal Code shall apply, in accordance with the purposes provided in article 1º of this Law. Subsection d) is added to article 239 of the Criminal Procedural Code, the text of which shall read: "Article 239.- Grounds for preventive detention

d) There exists danger to the victim, the complainant or the witness. When the victim is in a situation of risk, the judge shall take into account the need to order this measure, especially in the framework of the investigation of crimes attributable to a person with whom the victim maintains or has maintained a relationship of marriage, in a declared or undeclared de facto union."

### ARTÍCULO 46.- Amendment of the

Law against Domestic Violence

The final paragraph of article 3º of the Law against Domestic Violence is amended. The text shall read:

"Article 3.- Protective measures

If one or more of these measures are breached in contravention of an order issued by the competent judicial authority, it must provide certified copies to the corresponding prosecutor's office, so that the investigation for the crime of breach of a protective measure may be initiated."

In a period of three months, counted from the entry into force of this Law, public institutions and private organizations interested in developing specialized care programs for offenders, according to article 18 of this Law, must manage their accreditation before the National Institute for Women. Takes effect from its publication.
