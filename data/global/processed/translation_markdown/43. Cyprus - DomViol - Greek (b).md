The Prevention and Combating of Violence against Women and Domestic Violence and Related Matters Law of 2021 is hereby published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Cyprus in accordance with Article 52 of the Constitution.

Number 115(I) of 2021

LAW PROVIDING FOR THE PREVENTION AND COMBATING OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN

AND DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, AS WELL AS FOR RELATED MATTERS

Preamble. WHEREAS all forms of violence against women, including domestic violence, constitute a serious violation of human rights, AND WHEREAS the Republic of Cyprus has ratified the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence, by Law No. 14(III) of 2017, AND WHEREAS according to the Convention, the contracting states are encouraged to take measures to protect all victims of violence against women and to upgrade their legislative framework to address violence against women and to eliminate gender discrimination, AND WHEREAS violence against women constitutes a violation of human rights and a form of discrimination against women, and includes all acts of gender-based violence, which has or is likely to result in physical, sexual, psychological or economic harm or suffering to women, as well as threats to commit such acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether in public or private life, AND WHEREAS the implementation of de jure and de facto equality between women and men is a key element for the prevention and combating of violence against women, AND WHEREAS, as recognised by the United Nations Committee on the Elimination of

Discrimination against Women in its General Recommendation No. 28, of 16 December 2019, concerning the core obligations of States parties, in the context of article 2 of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, which was ratified by Law No. 78 of 1985, discrimination based on sex constitutes a violation of human rights and an obstacle to the enjoyment of human rights and fundamental freedoms, AND WHEREAS violence against women constitutes a manifestation of historically unequal power relations between women and men, which have led to domination and discrimination against women by men and to the impediment of the full advancement of women, AND WHEREAS the nature of violence exercised against women as gender-based violence constitutes one of the social mechanisms by which women are placed in a subordinate position compared to men, AND WHEREAS women and young girls are often exposed to aggravated forms of violence, such as domestic violence, sexual violence, sexual harassment, psychological violence, economic violence, rape, stalking, dissemination of pornographic or sexual material, forced marriage, crimes committed in the name of so-called "honour" and female genital mutilation, which constitute serious violations of the human rights of women and girls, as well as a major obstacle to the achievement of equality between women and men, AND WHEREAS there are continuing violations of human rights during armed conflicts, which affect civilians, especially women in the form of widespread or systematic rape and sexual violence and the possibility of increased gender-based violence, both during and after conflicts, AND WHEREAS women and girls are exposed to a greater risk of gender-based violence than men and boys, AND WHEREAS domestic violence affects women disproportionately in relation to men, without disregarding that men may also be victims of domestic violence, AND WHEREAS children, when they are witnesses of violence in the family, are also victims of domestic violence, AND WHEREAS, the establishment or adoption of special measures which are deemed necessary for the prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence are not considered discriminatory treatment, in accordance with the provisions of the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence, AND WHEREAS for the purposes of better implementation of the provisions of the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence (Ratification) Law, and the United Nations Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (Ratification) Law, the establishment of specific legislative measures is deemed necessary, AND WHEREAS the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence is applied in conjunction with the Laws set out in the Second Schedule of the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence (Ratification) Law, AND WHEREAS relevant fundamental principles of human rights which the Republic is obliged to implement, arise from the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights and the International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights of the United Nations, which were ratified by Law No. 14 of 1969, the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW) of the United Nations, and its Optional Protocol which was ratified by Law No. 1 (III) of 2002, as well as the General Recommendation No. 19 of the CEDAW Committee on Violence against Women, the Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action adopted in Beijing on 15 September 1995 by the Fourth United Nations World Conference on Women (Action for Equality, Development and Peace), the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child, which was ratified by Law No. 243 of 1990 and its Optional Protocols which were ratified by Laws No. 6 (III) of 2006 and 13 (III) of 2017 and the United Nations Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, which was ratified by Law No. 8 (III) of 2011, For all these reasons, the House of Representatives votes as follows:

Short 1. This Law shall be cited as the Prevention and Combating of Violence against Women and title. Domestic Violence and Related Matters Law of 2021.

Purpose of the 3. The purpose of this Law is the definition of offences of violence against women and domestic present Law. violence, their prevention, suppression and combating, the protection of women from all forms of violence and the elimination of violence against women and domestic violence, the promotion of substantive equality between women and men, the design of a comprehensive framework, policies and measures for the protection and support of all victims and the provision of support and assistance to organisations and involved services towards effective cooperation, so as to adopt an integrated approach for the elimination of violence against women and domestic violence.

Principles 4.-(1) The provisions of this Law and the measures for the protection and promotion of the rights of regarding the victims of violence against women and domestic violence are applied by every involved service and rights of victims non-governmental organisation without any discrimination based on sex, gender, race, colour, language, of violence religion, political or other opinion, national or social origin, association with a national minority, property, against women birth, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, health status, disability, marital status, migration or refugee and domestic status or other status. violence on which the application of the provisions of the present Law is based.

(2) Child victims are treated by every involved service and non-governmental organisation in accordance with the principles and provisions of the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child and other international standards regarding the treatment of child victims of criminal offences, safeguarding the interests of women who have not attained the eighteenth (18th) year of their age and taking into account their age and degree of psychological and physical development or maturity, as well as their vulnerable situation.

Offences of 5. The following offences are considered as offences of violence against women: violence against women.

Schedule. Exercise of 6. A person who, by their conduct which is expressed through coercion, pressure, derogatory psychological comments, insult or threats seriously impairs the psychological integrity of a woman or causes her real violence. fear, is guilty of an offence and, upon conviction, is liable to imprisonment not exceeding five (5) years or to a fine not exceeding ten thousand euros (€10,000) or to both such penalties.

Sexual 7. A person who engages in unwanted conduct of a sexual nature towards a woman which is harassment. expressed in words or acts and has the purpose or effect of violating the dignity of the woman, especially when through this conduct that person creates an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating or offensive environment towards that woman, is guilty of an offence and, upon conviction, is liable to imprisonment not exceeding five (5) years or to a fine not exceeding ten thousand euros (€10,000) or to both such penalties.

Economic 8. The spouse or partner of a woman financially dependent on them, who deprives her of the violence. necessary economic means for living including maintenance, medical care, clothing and shelter, with the purpose of causing her physical and/or psychological harm and/or with the purpose of forcing her to commit any act or omission and/or recklessly, disregarding whether they will cause her physical or psychological harm, is guilty of an offence and, upon conviction, is liable to imprisonment not exceeding five (5) years or to a fine not exceeding ten thousand euros (€10,000) or to both such penalties.

(2) A person who blackmails or threatens a woman that without her consent they will send, distribute, publish, disseminate, circulate, reproduce or transmit by any electronic, digital, printed or other means of any nature, material with pornographic or sexual content depicting her, is guilty of a felony and, upon conviction, is liable to imprisonment not exceeding fourteen (14) years.

Liability of 12.-(1) A legal person is liable for the commission of an offence of violence against women when it legal persons. is committed by a person acting, either individually or as a member of an organ of that legal person, who holds in it a position based on- (a) power of representation of the legal person, or (b) authority to take decisions on behalf of the legal person, or (c) authority to exercise control within the legal person.

(3) The liability of a legal person provided for in subsections (1) and (2) does not preclude the criminal prosecution of a natural person who acts as a perpetrator, instigator or accomplice in an offence of violence against women.
