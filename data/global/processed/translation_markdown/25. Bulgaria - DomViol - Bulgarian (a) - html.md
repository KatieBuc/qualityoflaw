LAW ON PROTECTION AGAINST DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (TITLE AMD. - SG, NO. 102 OF 2009, IN FORCE FROM 22.12.2009)

Promulgated SG No. 27 of 29 March 2005, amd. SG No. 82 of 10 October 2006, amd. SG No. 102 of 22 December 2009, amd. SG No. 99 of 17 December 2010, suppl. SG No. 50 of 3 July 2015, amd. SG No. 24 of 22 March 2019, amd. SG No. 101 of 27 December 2019, amd. and suppl. SG No. 66 of 1 August 2023, amd. and suppl. SG No. 69 of 11 August 2023.

Project: 354-01-22/17.04.2003

Chapter One.

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Art. 1.

(1) (Amd. - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) This law regulates:

- 1. the rights of persons who have suffered domestic violence or are at risk;
- 2. the measures for protection against domestic violence;
- 3. the bodies and mechanisms for implementing the state policy for prevention and protection against domestic violence and the interaction between them;
- 4. the programs for prevention and specialized services for providing protection, assistance and support to persons who have suffered domestic violence or are at risk;
- 5. the proceedings for imposing measures for protection against domestic violence;
- 6. the measures for implementing Regulation (EU) No 606/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 June 2013 on mutual recognition of protection measures in civil matters (OJ, L 181/4 of 29 June 2013), hereinafter referred to as "Regulation (EU) No 606/2013".
  - (2) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The State pursues a consistent policy in the field of domestic violence through coordinating, implementing, monitoring and evaluating policies and measures for preventing and protecting against domestic violence.
  - (3) (Suppl. - SG, No. 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, former para. 2, amd. - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The measures under this law do not exclude the civil, administrative-penal and criminal liability of the perpetrator.

Art. 1a. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The aim of this law is to provide quick and effective protection and to ensure assistance and support to persons who have suffered domestic violence or are at risk, and to exercise a preventive and deterrent effect on the perpetrator of violence.

Art. 2. (Amd. - SG, No. 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009) (1) (Amd. - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024, suppl. - SG, No. 69 of 2023) Domestic violence is any act of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence, as well as an attempt at such violence, forced restriction of private life, personal freedom and personal rights, committed against persons who are in kinship, who are or have been in a family relationship or in de facto marital cohabitation or in an intimate relationship.

(2) (Amd. - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) Any domestic violence committed in the presence of a child is also considered psychological violence against the child.

(3) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) The acts under para. 1 and 2 may be committed through action or omission.

#### Art. 3. Protection under this law may be sought by any person who has suffered domestic violence committed by:

1. a spouse or former spouse;

2. (suppl. - SG, No. 69 of 2023) a person with whom they are or have been in de facto marital cohabitation or in an intimate relationship;

3. a person with whom they have a child;

4. an ascendant;

5. a descendant;

6. (amd. - SG, No. 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009) a person with whom they are related in the collateral line up to and including the fourth degree;

7. (amd. - SG, No. 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009) a person with whom they are or have been related by affinity up to and including the third degree;

8. (amd. - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) a guardian, trustee, person entrusted with care of the child, or person to whom the child is entrusted;

9. (new - SG, No. 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, amd. - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024, amd. - SG, No. 69 of 2023) a person who is a relative in the direct or collateral line up to and including the fourth degree, of the person with whom they are or have been in de facto marital cohabitation or in an intimate relationship;

10. (new - SG, No. 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, suppl. - SG, No. 69 of 2023) a person with whom the parent is or has been in de facto marital cohabitation or in an intimate relationship;

11. (new - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) a person who is a spouse or former spouse of the parent.

#### Art. 4.

(1) In case of domestic violence, the victim has the right to turn to the court for protection.

(2) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) At the request of the victim, state and municipal authorities, medical institutions and legal entities which carry out activities for prevention and protection against domestic violence are obliged within 24 hours to forward the application to the respective district court for initiating proceedings for issuing a protection order.

(3) (Amd. - SG, No. 82 of 2006, amd. - SG, No. 102 of 2009, in force from 22.12.2009, former para. 2 - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) In cases where there is evidence of danger to the life or health of the victim, they may also submit an application to the authorities of the Ministry of Interior for taking measures pursuant to the Law on the Ministry of Interior.

(4) (Former para. 3 - SG, No. 66 of 2023, in force from 01.01.2024) At the request of the victim, every doctor is obliged to issue a document in which to certify in writing the injuries or traces of violence ascertained by them.

#### Art. 5.

(1) (Amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009) The measures for protection from domestic violence are:

- 1. obligating the perpetrator to refrain from committing domestic violence;
- 2. removal of the perpetrator from the jointly inhabited dwelling for the term determined by the court;
- 3. (supplemented - SG, issue 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, supplemented - SG, issue 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) prohibition for the perpetrator to approach the victim, the dwelling, the workplace and the places for social contacts and leisure of the victim under conditions and for a term determined by the court;
- 4. (new - SG, issue 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) prohibition for the perpetrator to make contact with the victim in any form whatsoever, including by telephone, through electronic or regular mail and fax, as well as through any other means and systems of communication under conditions and for a term determined by the court;
- 5. (previous item 4, supplemented - SG, issue 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) temporary determination of the child's residence with the victim parent or with the parent who has not committed the violence, under conditions and for a term determined by the court, if this does not contradict the interests of the child;
- 6. (previous item 5, supplemented - SG, issue 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) obligating the perpetrator of the violence to attend specialized programs for overcoming aggression and managing anger;
- 7. (previous item 6, amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) directing the victims to specialized services for protection, assistance and support or recovery programs;
- 8. (new - SG, issue 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) directing the victimized children to specialized services for protection, assistance and support of children, victims or witnesses of violence.
  - (2) (Amended - SG, issue 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended and supplemented - SG, issue 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) The measures under para. 1, items 2 - 5 are imposed for a term of three to 18 months with deduction of the term of validity of the measures under an issued order for immediate protection under Art. 18 and 19.
  - (3) (New - SG, issue 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) In proceedings under this law the court makes a mandatory assessment for imposing the measure under para. 1, item 5, when the victim and the perpetrator have a child.
  - (4) (Previous para. 3 - SG, issue 102 of 2009, effective 22.12.2009, amended - SG, issue 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) The court may rule as a protective measure that the regime of personal relations, determined according to the procedure of the Family Code, between the child and the parent, perpetrator of domestic violence, be exercised for the term under para. 2 in a protected environment, provided in a specialized service under this law or provided by a provider of social service at the current address of the child or at its place of residence, under the supervision of a psychologist or social worker.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR PREVENTION AND PROTECTION FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (NEW - SG, NO. 66 OF 2023, EFFECTIVE FROM 01.08.2023) Art. 6a. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.08.2023) (1) A National Council for Prevention and Protection from Domestic Violence, hereinafter referred to as "the National Council", shall be established at the Council of Ministers.

(2) The National Council is a specialized permanent collective and advisory body for the implementation of state policy on prevention and protection from domestic violence through coordination, monitoring and evaluation of policies and measures for prevention and protection from domestic violence.

(3) The Chairman of the National Council shall be a Deputy Prime Minister designated by the Council of Ministers. The Deputy Chairman of the National Council shall be the Minister of Justice.

(4) The composition of the National Council shall include the Minister of Interior, the Minister of Labour and Social Policy, the Minister of Health, the Minister of Education and Science, the Minister of Youth and Sports, a representative of the Supreme Court of Cassation, the Chairman of the State Agency for Child Protection, the Executive Director of the Social Assistance Agency, the Executive Director of the National Association of Municipalities in the Republic of Bulgaria.

(5) The composition of the National Council shall include representatives of non-profit legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection from domestic violence. The organizations that represent these members of the Council and related persons may not participate with projects for funding under the procedure of Art. 6e, para. 6.

(6) In the meetings of the National Council, upon invitation of the Chairman, the Ombudsman of the Republic of Bulgaria, Members of Parliament, representatives of the judicial authorities, representatives of academic circles, representatives of legal entities and international organizations with representations in the country that carry out activities for prevention and protection from domestic violence, heads of other state bodies, representatives of local self-government and local administration bodies, professional and civil organizations, other officials and experts may participate.

(7) The administrative-technical, organizational, expert and financial servicing of the National Council shall be carried out by the administration of the Council of Ministers.

(8) The Council of Ministers shall adopt rules for the structure, organization and activity of the National Council.

(9) The conditions and procedure for the selection of the non-profit legal entities under para. 5 shall be determined in the rules under para. 8. Art. 6b. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.08.2023) (1) The National Council:

- 1. determines the priorities in policies in the field of prevention and protection from domestic violence;
- 2. carries out coordination, monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of policies and measures in the field of prevention and protection from domestic violence;
- 3. develops and updates a coordination mechanism for assistance and support to persons affected by domestic violence, and presents it for adoption by the Council of Ministers, monitors its effective functioning and gives instructions and recommendations on its application;
- 4. develops a national programme for prevention and protection from domestic violence based on the identified priority activities for funding and submits it for adoption by the Council of Ministers, making proposals for its updating when necessary;
- 5. requests and receives information from state bodies, legal entities and others that are related to prevention and protection from domestic violence;
- 6. creates and maintains a national information system for cases of domestic violence committed on the territory of the country, or in relation to Bulgarian citizens abroad who have sought help and support, and a list of legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection from domestic violence under the procedure of this law, in compliance with the requirements of the Personal Data Protection Act;
- 7. summarizes and analyzes the results of the implementation of policies in the field of prevention and protection from domestic violence and proposes measures to increase their effectiveness;
- 8. publishes and disseminates statistical information related to domestic violence in compliance with the requirements of the Statistics Act and the Personal Data Protection Act;
- 9. organizes the conduct of information, awareness-raising and training campaigns on issues related to domestic violence;
- 10. develops and updates draft strategies, programmes and plans in the field of prevention and protection from domestic violence and monitors and reports on their implementation;
- 11. gives opinions and makes proposals for amendments to existing and for the adoption of new normative acts in the field of prevention and protection from domestic violence;
- 12. develops draft normative acts related to prevention and protection from domestic violence and submits them to the Council of Ministers through the Chairman of the National Council;
- 13. approves methodological instructions and participates jointly with other representatives of organizations, institutions and judicial bodies in the development of documents related to the risk assessment under this law;
- 14. carries out international cooperation with international organizations and institutions with activities in the field of prevention and protection from domestic violence, exchanging information and good practices;
- 15. approves the procedures for selection of activities for prevention of domestic violence and specialized services for protection from domestic violence, financed under the procedure of Art. 6e, para. 6, and exercises control over the fulfilment of the conditions under the funding agreement;
- 16. approves training programmes for employees to perform functions in connection with prevention and protection from domestic violence;
- 17. maintains and develops a national helpline for assistance to persons affected by domestic violence and at risk;
- 18. monitors and evaluates the impact of measures for prevention and protection from domestic violence.
  - (2) The Chairman of the National Council shall present an annual report on its activities to the Council of Ministers.

Chapter One "b".

INTERACTION OF THE NATIONAL COUNCIL WITH BODIES AND ORGANIZATIONS (NEW - SG, NO. 66 OF 2023, EFFECTIVE FROM 01.01.2024)

Art. 6c. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) (1) For the implementation of activities for prevention and protection from domestic violence, the National Council shall interact with the executive authorities, the judicial authorities, the local self-government and local administration bodies and the legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection from domestic violence.

(2) The interaction under para. 1 shall be carried out through:

- 1. exchange of information on identified cases of violence, risk assessment, referral to competent authorities and/or organizations, determination of protection and support measures, applications for protection from domestic violence and regarding the implementation of measures and/or
- 2. carrying out joint actions in the prevention and protection from domestic violence.

Art. 6d. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) (1) The interaction for protection, assistance and support of persons affected by domestic violence shall be carried out under the conditions and according to the procedure of a coordination mechanism for assistance and support to persons affected by domestic violence.

(2) The coordination mechanism under para. 1 regulates the basic procedures for interaction between the executive authorities, the local self-government and local administration bodies and the legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection from domestic violence under the procedure of this law. The interaction shall be carried out at the following stages:

- 1. notification and referral: a) identification of cases of violence; b) risk assessment from the point of view of the life and health of the victim with the aim of adequate planning of protection measures and for a coordinated response; c) referral for the use of specialized services;
- 2. determination of protection measures in case of: a) direct and immediate danger from domestic violence; b) risk;
- 3. measures for long-term support after cessation of violence: a) psycho-social counselling and psychotherapeutic work; b) development of skills for realization on the labour market and for realization in the social environment; c) social counselling, advocacy and active social work.

Chapter One "c".

NATIONAL INFORMATION SYSTEM (NEW - SG, NO. 66 OF 2023, EFFECTIVE FROM 03.08.2024)

Art. 6e. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 03.08.2024) (1) The National Council shall create and maintain a National Information System for Prevention and Protection from Domestic Violence, in which data on cases of domestic violence committed on the territory of the country or in relation to Bulgarian citizens abroad who have sought help and support shall be stored, hereinafter referred to as the "National Information System".

(2) The National Information System shall contain data on:

- 1. acts of domestic violence;
- 2. affected persons, perpetrators and the relationships between them;
- 3. the type of violence - physical, psychological, sexual, economic, restriction of rights and others;
- 4. the duration of the violence;
- 5. the harm caused to the affected person;
- 6. the history of the violence;
- 7. applications submitted for issuing a protection order, an immediate protection order or requests of the prosecutor under the procedure of Art. 8, para. 2;
- 8. measures imposed under this law, the Ministry of Interior Act, the Child Protection Act and the social services provided under the procedure of the Social Services Act in relation to persons affected by domestic violence;
- 9. legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection from domestic violence under the procedure of this law;
- 10. services used under the procedure of this law.
  - (3) The National Council shall ensure the registration, storage and maintenance in an up-to-date state of the data under para. 2.
  - (4) The information subject to entry in the National Information System, the procedure for its collection and provision, the organization and functioning of the system and the level of access shall be determined by the implementing regulations of the law.
  - (5) The executive authorities, the judicial authorities, the local self-government and local administration bodies and the legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection from domestic violence under the procedure of this law shall provide the National Council with the necessary data under para. 2 according to a procedure determined in the implementing regulations of the law.
  - (6) Officials from the Ministry of Interior, the Social Assistance Agency, the State Agency for Child Protection and the judicial authorities shall have the right of access to the data in the National Information System.
  - (7) Personal data in the system under para. 1 shall be stored for a period of no more than twenty years.
  - (8) For the needs of the system under para. 1, the National Council shall have the right of access to data on perpetrators of domestic violence in the Unified Information System for Combating Crime under Art. 378 of the Judicial System Act according to a procedure determined by the implementing regulations of this law.

Chapter One "d".

PROGRAMMES FOR PREVENTION AND SPECIALIZED SERVICES FOR PROTECTION FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE (NEW - SG, NO. 66 OF 2023, EFFECTIVE FROM 01.01.2024)

Chapter One "d".

PROGRAMMES FOR PREVENTION AND SPECIALIZED SERVICES FOR PROTECTION FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

Art. 6f. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) (1) The State shall create conditions for the implementation of programmes for prevention of domestic violence and specialized services for protection, assistance and support to persons affected by domestic violence and at risk.

(2) The executive authorities, the National Institute of Justice and the local self-government and local administration bodies shall organize training for persons who carry out activities under this law.

(3) The executive authorities, the local self-government and local administration bodies and the legal entities that carry out activities for prevention and protection of persons affected by domestic violence or at risk, under the procedure of this law, shall develop, organize the implementation and implement the programmes and services provided for in Art. 5, para. 1, items 6 - 8.

(4) The Council of Ministers shall adopt a three-year national programme for prevention and protection from domestic violence by 31 March of the relevant year.

(5) The funds for financing the implementation of the obligations under the national programme under para. 4 shall be determined on the basis of medium-term three-year financial forecasts prepared by the respective executive authorities having commitments to the implementation of activities from the programme.

(6) By the law on the state budget of the Republic of Bulgaria for the relevant year, funds shall be allocated under the budget of the Council of Ministers for financing projects of the persons under para. 3, provided that they carry out activities under this law, for the development and implementation of:

- 1. programmes for prevention of domestic violence regarding: a) work with the judicial authorities; b) work with the authorities of the Ministry of Interior, including training of police officers and other law enforcement officials to identify cases of domestic violence, on protocols for response and application of relevant legislation; the programme shall be adopted by the Ministry of Interior; c) training in educational institutions for the prevention of domestic violence and the promotion of gender equality and respect; the programme shall be adopted by the Ministry of Education and Science; d) training of persons who carry out prevention and protection under the law; e) monitoring of the application of the law; f) preparation and conduct of information campaigns, seminars and conferences;
- 2. specialized services for providing protection, assistance and support to persons affected by domestic violence or at risk, which include: a) a national helpline for assistance to persons affected by domestic violence or at risk; b) social, psychological and legal counselling and specialized programmes for recovery and/or protection of persons or children, victims of domestic violence or witnesses, in a counselling centre; c) social, psychological and legal counselling and specialized programmes for recovery and/or protection of persons or children, victims of domestic violence or witnesses, in a shelter;
- 3. specialized programmes for overcoming aggression and anger management for perpetrators of domestic violence, which include social and psychological counselling.
  - (7) The procedures for selection of legal entities that carry out prevention of domestic violence under the procedure of this law, the conditions and procedure for financing, reporting and control of prevention activities shall be determined by the implementing regulations of the law in compliance with the state aid rules.

Art. 6g. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) (1) To a person affected by domestic violence, the following specialized services shall be provided independently or as a package:

- 1. a national helpline for assistance to persons affected by domestic violence or at risk;
- 2. a counselling centre for persons affected by domestic violence;
- 3. a shelter.
  - (2) The specialized services under para. 1 shall be provided according to the wish and personal choice of persons affected by domestic violence or at risk, and shall be free of charge. The services shall be provided regardless of whether the victims have expressed consent to cooperate in the detection of the perpetrator of domestic violence.
  - (3) The use of a specialized service under para. 1 does not exclude the use of other services.

Art. 6h. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) To a person who has committed domestic violence, a specialized programme for overcoming aggression and anger management shall be provided. The programme may be part of a package of specialized services or may be provided independently.

Art. 6i. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) When using a specialized service or programme under the procedure of this law, the persons under Art. 6g and 6h shall be provided with appropriate and timely information in a language understandable to them.

Art. 6j. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) The conditions, procedure and minimum standards for the provision of specialized services and programmes for protection from domestic violence under the procedure of this law, as well as their financing and control, shall be regulated by the implementing regulations of the law.

### Section I.

General provisions

#### Art. 7.

(Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) The district court in the region where the injured person actually resides at the time of filing the application is competent to impose a protection measure.

#### Art. 8.

(1) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009, previous text of Art. 8, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) Proceedings for issuing a protection order may be instituted upon application by:

- 1. (supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) the injured person, if they have reached 14 years of age or are placed under full or limited interdiction;
- 2. a brother, sister or person who is related in a direct line to the injured person;
- 3. (supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) the guardian or trustee of the injured person, the person to whom care for the child has been entrusted, or the person to whom the child has been entrusted;
- 4. (amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) the director of the "Social Assistance" directorate, when the injured person: a) is a minor or underage; b) is in a helpless condition as a result of serious disability, illness or old age or is placed under interdiction.
  - (2) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) When the injured person cannot defend themselves due to a helpless condition or dependence on the perpetrator, the proceedings may also be instituted at the request of the prosecutor.
  - (3) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) When the injured person is a minor, is placed under interdiction or is in a helpless condition as a result of serious disability, illness or old age, the court may decide to provide legal aid under the procedure of the Legal Aid Act.

#### Art. 9.

(1) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009) The application is in writing and contains:

- 1. (amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) the names, uniform civil number or other identification number and date of birth of the applicant; in cases where the person resides short-term on the territory of the country - also an address at which they may be summoned; at the request of the applicant, their summons may be carried out by telephone, electronic address for service, telex, fax or telegram;
- 2. (amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) the names and address at which the perpetrator may be summoned, as well as the telephone number;
- 3. data on the family, kinship or de facto relationship between the injured person and the perpetrator;
- 4. (amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009) the date, place, manner and other facts and circumstances of the committed domestic violence;
- 5. signature.
  - (2) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) In the cases under Art. 8, para. 1, items 2 - 4 and para. 2, the court ex officio constitutes the injured person as a party.
  - (3) (Amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) A declaration by the applicant concerning the committed violence may also be attached to the application under Art. 8, para. 1, item 1.
  - (4) (Amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) The court ex officio requests for the parties certificates of criminal record and of imposed measures under this law and certificates whether they are registered on psychiatric record.
  - (5) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) The court also performs an inquiry into the family and/or kinship relationship between the parties.
  - (6) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) The court may also request ex officio other inquiries for the purposes of the proceedings.

#### Art. 10.

(1) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.08.2023) The application shall be filed within three months from the act of domestic violence, unless this is objectively impossible, but not later than 6 months.

(2) (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.08.2023) The time limit under para. 1 is not considered missed when the application to the court has been filed through the municipality, the mayoralty or the bodies of the Ministry of Interior.

(3) (Repealed, amended and supplemented - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009, previous para. 2 - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.08.2023) The application is registered in a special register with separate sequential numbering (index) of cases and is distributed on the day of receipt.

#### Art. 11.

(1) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009, supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) Upon filing an application under Art. 8, para. 1, no state fee is paid.

(2) Upon issuing the order, the court assigns the state fee and the costs of the case to the perpetrator of the domestic violence.

(3) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) Upon refusal to issue an order or upon revocation of the order, the state fee and the costs of the case are paid by the applicant, except when the application is for the protection of persons who have not reached 18 years of age, as well as persons who are in a helpless condition as a result of serious disability, illness or old age or are placed under interdiction.

Art. 11a. (New - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) When the proceedings are instituted at the request of the prosecutor, Art. 9 - 11 apply.

### Section II.

Consideration of the case

#### Art. 12.

(1) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009) On the day of receipt of the application, the court schedules a public hearing within a period of not later than one month, and together with the summons and the copy of the application with the attachments, notifies the respondent of their obligation to present evidence.

(2) (Amended - SG, No. 102 of 2009, effective from 22.12.2009, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) In the cases under Art. 8, para. 1, items 2 - 4 and para. 2, the injured person is also summoned.

(3) Where necessary, the summons is served with the assistance of the police authorities or the mayor.

#### Art. 13.

(1) (Supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) In proceedings for issuing a protection order, the means of evidence under the Code of Civil Procedure are admissible, and the court collects evidence on its own initiative as well.

(2) Means of evidence in the proceedings under para. 1 may also be:

- 1. (supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) the protocols, reports, risk assessment and other acts issued by the "Social Assistance" directorates, by the Ministry of Interior, by doctors, as well as by psychologists who have counseled the injured person;
- 2. (amended - SG, No. 24 of 2019, effective from 01.07.2020, amended regarding entry into force - SG, No. 101 of 2019, amended - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) the documents issued by providers of specialized services for protection from domestic violence.;
- 3. the declaration under Art. 9, para. 3.
  - (3) (Supplemented - SG, No. 66 of 2023, effective from 01.01.2024) When there is no other evidence for specific facts and circumstances, the court issues a protection order solely on the basis of the attached declaration under Art. 9, para. 3.

#### Art. 14.

Supplementary Provisions

#### § 1.

(New - SG No. 66 of 2023, effective 01.01.2024) Within the meaning of this law:

- 1. "National helpline for victims of domestic violence or persons at risk with a single harmonised telephone number" is a free telephone line with national coverage and a single harmonised telephone number for reporting cases of domestic violence, informing, counselling, referring victims of domestic violence or persons at risk, while observing confidentiality and anonymity for the persons. The telephone line is accessible 24 hours a day, seven days a week, on all days of the year.
- 2. "Counselling centre for victims of domestic violence" is a standalone service or complex of interdisciplinary specialized services for adults and/or children who are victims of domestic violence, providing psychological, social and legal assistance and aimed at ensuring support for victims simultaneously with or after using other specialized services.
- 3. "Sheltered housing" is a specialized service for the protection of adults and their children who are victims of domestic violence, consisting of temporary accommodation in a safe environment.
- 4. "Related persons" within the meaning of Art. 6a, para. 5 are: a) persons in whose management body the same legal or natural person participates; b) persons in whose supreme body the same legal or natural person participates, which can impose or prevent the taking of decisions; c) persons, one of which is a member of the National Council and whose representative in the National Council is in hierarchical dependence on the other by virtue of an employment relationship; d) persons, one of which is a member of the National Council and has received remuneration from the other by virtue of a civil legal relationship in a total amount exceeding BGN 10,000 during the last twelve months from the date of their election as a member of the National Council; e) persons, one of which is a member of the National Council and has received a donation and/or grant funding from the other in a total amount exceeding BGN 10,000 during the last twelve months from the date of their election as a member of the National Council; f) persons, one of which exercises control over the other; g) persons whose activity is controlled by a third party or by its subsidiary legal entity.
- 5. "Control" within the meaning of items e) and g) of point 4 exists when the controlling party: a) holds, directly or indirectly or by virtue of an agreement with another person, more than half of the votes in the supreme body of another person, or b) has the ability to determine, directly or indirectly, more than half of the members of the management or supreme body of another person, or c) can in another way exercise decisive influence over decision-making in relation to the activities of the legal entity.
- 6. (new - SG No. 69 of 2023) "Intimate relationship" represents a set of voluntary and lasting personal, intimate and sexual relations between two natural persons of male and female gender, regardless of whether they share one household and whose origin, content and termination are not subject to legal regulation by another law. Lasting within the meaning of the previous sentence are relations with a duration of at least 60 days.
