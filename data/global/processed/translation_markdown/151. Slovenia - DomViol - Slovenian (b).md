CRIMINAL CODE

(KZ-1)

(unofficial consolidated text No. 12)

GENERAL PART

First Chapter

FUNDAMENTAL PROVISIONS

Enforcement of criminal liability

1st Article

(1) Criminal liability in the Republic of Slovenia may be enforced in compliance with constitutionally guaranteed human rights and fundamental freedoms in a democratic order and on the principles of the rule of law.

(2) Under this code, criminal liability is enforced by punishing adult persons for committed criminal offences on the basis of established guilt.

(3) Criminal liability is not enforced against a person whose guilt is excluded because they were not sane at the time of commission. Measures provided for in this code may be imposed on such a person for an act that has the characteristics of a criminal offence and is contrary to law (unlawful act). No criminal offence and no penalty without law

## 2nd Article

No one may be sentenced to a penalty or other criminal sanction for an act which the law has not defined as a criminal offence before it was committed and for which a penalty or other criminal sanction has not been prescribed by law.

System of criminal sanctions

3rd Article

(1) Criminal sanctions are: penalties, warning sanctions and security measures.

(2) For a criminal offence, a penalty is always prescribed by law, which is imposed on the perpetrator (female or male perpetrator; hereinafter: perpetrator) in proportion to the gravity of the act and their guilt. Under the conditions specified in the general part of this code, warning sanctions may be imposed on the perpetrator instead of a penalty, and security measures in addition to a penalty or warning sanction.

(3) Under the conditions specified in this code, confiscation of pecuniary gain and publication of the judgment are also imposed on the perpetrator for a committed criminal offence.

(4) Where regulations provide that, by reason of the conviction itself for a criminal offence, any rights shall be withdrawn or restricted from the convicted person (female or male convicted person; hereinafter: convicted person) in addition to the imposed criminal sanctions, the prohibition from the preceding article shall also apply to such legal consequences.

(5) Under the conditions specified in this code, security measures, confiscation of pecuniary gain and publication of the judgment may be imposed on a perpetrator who is not guilty because they were not sane at the time of committing the unlawful act. Provisions on the legal consequences of conviction also apply to such perpetrators. Second Chapter

APPLICABILITY OF THE CRIMINAL CODE

1. Personal applicability

Equal applicability of the criminal law

4th Article

(1) Unless exceptionally provided otherwise in this code, the criminal code applies equally to all adult persons, regardless of whether they are citizens (female or male citizens; hereinafter: citizen) of the Republic of Slovenia or foreign nationals (female or male foreign nationals; hereinafter: foreign national).

(2) A criminal law provision which exceptionally applies only to citizens of the Republic of Slovenia shall not be applied to citizens of other Member States of the European Union and other foreign nationals.

(3) Where a criminal law provision exceptionally applies only to foreign nationals, it may also provide when citizens of other Member States of the European Union are not considered foreign nationals. Special personal applicability

5th Article

(1) Where the law provides that only persons with special characteristics, rights or positions are punished for a criminal offence, the criminal law applies equally to all such persons.

(2) Special criminal laws provide for the criminal liability of minors (female or male minors; hereinafter: minor) and legal persons.

(3) A special law providing for the criminal liability of minors may also provide that persons who were already adults at the time of committing the criminal offence but were not yet twenty-one years old (younger adults, female or male) may be imposed criminal sanctions for minors instead of penalties, depending on their personal development.

Exclusion of personal applicability

6th Article

(1) The criminal law does not apply to acts of persons whose criminal liability is excluded due to immunity under constitutional provisions or rules of international law.

(2) Where the law provides that the perpetrator is prosecuted for a criminal offence upon a motion or private prosecution, such provision shall not be applied against a person against whom the injured party (female or male injured party; hereinafter: injured party) has not submitted a motion or has not filed a private prosecution or has withdrawn it.

2. Temporal applicability

Application of a subsequent law more lenient for the perpetrator

7th Article

(1) For the perpetrator of a criminal offence, the law that was in force at the time of committing the criminal offence applies.

(2) If the law changes (once or several times) after the commission of the criminal offence, the law that is more lenient for the perpetrator applies.

Temporally limited laws

## 8th Article

If a law or other regulation to which the criminal law refers is valid only for a specified time, it applies to a criminal offence committed during the time of its validity also after the expiry of that time, unless otherwise provided.

Concurrent applicability of the general part of this code and other criminal laws

9th Article

(1) The provisions of the general part of this code also apply to criminal offences defined by other laws or ratified and published international treaties or acts of the European Union, unless otherwise provided by such acts.

(2) Where laws that provide for criminal liability for minors, legal persons or other special types of perpetrators or acts (special criminal laws) refer in their general provisions to the concurrent application of the general part of this code, the last valid code applies.

3. Territorial applicability

Applicability of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia to anyone who commits a criminal offence on its territory

10th Article

(1) The criminal law of the Republic of Slovenia applies to anyone who commits a criminal offence on the territory of the Republic of Slovenia.

(2) The criminal law of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to anyone who commits a criminal offence on a domestic vessel, regardless of where the vessel was at the time of committing the act.

(3) The criminal law of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to anyone who commits a criminal offence on a domestic civil aircraft in flight or on a state aircraft, regardless of where the aircraft was at the time of committing the act.

Applicability of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia to certain criminal offences committed abroad

## Article 11

The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia applies to anyone who commits abroad

- a criminal offense from Article 243 of this Code and criminal offenses from Articles 332, 333 and 334 of this Code, if committed in the protective ecological zone or on the continental shelf of the Republic of Slovenia, and

- criminal offenses from Article 108 and Articles 348 to 360 of this Code.

Validity of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia for a citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who commits a criminal offense abroad

## Article 12

The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia applies to a citizen of the Republic of Slovenia, even if they commit abroad any other criminal offense in addition to the criminal offenses listed in the previous article.

Validity of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia for a foreigner who commits a criminal offense abroad

Article 13

(1) The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to a foreigner who commits outside the Republic of Slovenia, against it or its citizen, a criminal offense, even though these are not criminal offenses from

Article 11 of this Code.

(2) The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to a foreigner who commits against a foreign state or against a foreigner abroad a criminal offense, if they are caught on the territory of the Republic of Slovenia and are not extradited to a foreign state. In such a case, the court may not impose a more severe penalty than that prescribed by the law of the state in which the criminal offense was committed.

(3) The Criminal Code of the Republic of Slovenia also applies to anyone who commits abroad any other criminal offense which, under an international treaty or under general legal principles recognized by the international community, is prosecuted in all states, regardless of where it was committed. Special conditions for prosecution

Article 14

(1) If, in the cases from Article 10 and the first indent of Article 11 of this Code, criminal proceedings have been initiated or concluded abroad, the perpetrator is prosecuted in the Republic of Slovenia with the permission of the Minister of Justice with an indication of the conditions under which prosecution does not violate the prohibition of double jeopardy.

(2) In the cases from Article 12 and the first and second paragraphs of Article 13 of this Code, the perpetrator is not prosecuted:

- 1) if the sentence for which they were convicted abroad has been fully served or if it was determined in accordance with an international treaty that they will serve the sentence imposed abroad in the Republic of Slovenia;
- 2) if they were acquitted abroad by a final judgment or the sentence was remitted or the execution of the sentence has become time-barred;
- 3) if the criminal offense under foreign law is prosecuted at the request of the injured party, but such request has not been filed or has been withdrawn.
  - (3) In the cases from Article 12 and the first and second paragraphs of Article 13 of this Code, the perpetrator is prosecuted only if the act is also punishable under the law of the state in which it was committed.
  - (4) If in the case from Article 12 of this Code the act is not punishable under the law of the state in which it was committed, but this act, committed against the Republic of Slovenia or its citizen, the perpetrator may be prosecuted only with the permission of the Minister of Justice.
  - (5) In all other cases except the cases from the second indent of Article 11, the third paragraph of Article 13 and the fourth paragraph of this article of this Code, when the act is committed abroad, but in the state in which it was committed it is not punishable, the perpetrator may be prosecuted with the permission of the Minister of Justice, if the act was, at the time it was committed, considered a criminal offense under general legal principles recognized by the international community.
  - (6) In the case from Article 10 of this Code, the prosecution of a foreigner may, under the conditions prescribed by law, be transferred to a foreign state.
  - (7) In the cases from the third paragraph of Article 13 of this Code, the perpetrator is prosecuted only with the permission of the Minister of Justice.

Crediting deprivation of liberty abroad

## Article 15 V

Detention ordered abroad and any deprivation of liberty during extradition proceedings shall be credited towards the sentence from conviction before a domestic court, and likewise the sentence already served from a foreign court's judgment shall be credited, if it becomes known later. If the sentences are not of the same type, the court shall decide how it should be credited. If the convict has served in total more sentences than were imposed on them in the conviction before the domestic court, the excess is considered unjustly served.

3.a Prosecution of criminal offenses against minors

Manner of prosecution of perpetrators of criminal offenses committed against minors

Article 15.a

In cases where a criminal offense from the chapters against life and body, against human rights and freedoms, against sexual integrity or another criminal offense from this Code with signs of violence is committed against a minor, the provisions of this Code on filing a motion or private action do not apply with regard to the manner of criminal prosecution and the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio.

Third Chapter

GENERAL PROVISIONS ON CRIMINAL OFFENSES

1. Criminal offense and perpetrator

Criminal offense

## Article 16

A criminal offense is a human unlawful act which the law, for the necessary protection of legal values, designates as a criminal offense and simultaneously determines its elements and the punishment for the guilty perpetrator.

Manner of commission of a criminal offense

Article 17

(1) A criminal offense may be committed by commission or omission.

(2) A criminal offense may be committed by omission only if the perpetrator omitted an act they were obliged to perform.

(3) A criminal offense which the law does not designate as an omissive criminal offense may also be committed by omission if the perpetrator does not prevent the prohibited consequence. In such a case, the perpetrator is punished for omission only if they were obliged to prevent the occurrence of the prohibited consequence and if the omission is of equal significance for the occurrence of such consequence as the commission. Time of commission of a criminal offense

## Article 18

A criminal offense is committed at the time when the perpetrator acted or should have acted, regardless of when the consequence occurs.

Place of commission of a criminal offense

Article 19

(1) A criminal offense is committed in the place where the perpetrator acted or should have acted, as well as in the place where the prohibited consequence occurred.

(2) An attempt of a criminal offense is considered to have been committed in the place where the perpetrator acted, as well as in the place where, according to their intent, the prohibited consequence was to occur or could have occurred.

Perpetrator and co-perpetrator

Article 20

(1) The perpetrator of a criminal offense is anyone who commits it personally or by exploiting and directing the conduct of another (indirect perpetrator).

(2) The perpetrator of a criminal offense is also anyone who commits a criminal offense together with another, by consciously participating in the commission or otherwise decisively contributing to the commission (co-perpetrator).

Limitations on criminal liability according to the age of perpetrators

## Article 21

Whoever committed an unlawful act when they were not yet fourteen years old (child) cannot be a perpetrator of a criminal offense.

Self-defense

Article 22

(1) An act committed in self-defense is not unlawful.

(2) Self-defense is a defense that is necessarily required for the perpetrator to avert from themselves or another person a simultaneous unlawful attack.

(3) A perpetrator who has exceeded the limits of self-defense may be punished more leniently; if they exceeded self-defense due to strong agitation or fear caused by the attack, the punishment may also be remitted.

Duress

## Article 23

An act committed under the influence of force which the perpetrator could not resist is not a criminal offense.

2. Guilt and punishability of perpetrators

Guilt

## Article 24

A perpetrator is guilty if, at the time of committing the criminal offense, they were of sound mind and acted with intent or negligence, while being aware or should and could have been aware that they were acting contrary to law, and if there are no grounds that exclude guilt. Intent

## Article 25

A criminal offense is committed with intent if the perpetrator was aware of their act and wanted to commit it (direct intent), or if they were aware that they might commit the act and consented to it (eventual intent).

Negligence

## 26. člen

A criminal act is committed through negligence if the perpetrator did not act with the required care, even though he was aware that he could commit the act, but recklessly thought that it would not happen or that he could prevent it (conscious negligence), or if he was not aware that he could commit the act, but should and could have been aware of it according to the circumstances and his personal characteristics (unconscious negligence).

Punishability of negligence

27. člen

(1) For a criminal act committed through negligence, the perpetrator is punished only if the law so provides.

(2) The court may remit the punishment to a perpetrator who committed a criminal act through negligence if the consequences of the act affect the perpetrator to such an extent that punishment in such a case would obviously not be justified.

Liability for a more serious consequence

## 28. člen

If a more serious consequence has resulted from the criminal act, for which the law prescribes a more severe penalty, this penalty may be imposed if the perpetrator acted negligently with regard to that consequence. Insanity

29. člen

(1) Whoever was not of sound mind at the time of committing an unlawful act is not guilty.

(2) A perpetrator who, at the time of committing an unlawful act, was unable to understand the meaning of his act or was unable to control his conduct due to mental disorder or mental underdevelopment, is not of sound mind.

(3) A perpetrator of a criminal act whose ability to understand the meaning of his act or ability to control his conduct was substantially diminished due to any condition from the previous paragraph or due to any other permanent and severe mental disturbance, may be punished more leniently.

(4) Guilty is the perpetrator of a criminal act who caused his own insanity through the use of alcohol, drugs, other psychoactive substances or in any other way, if his guilt, as determined by law for that act, was present before the criminal act.

Mistake of fact

30. člen

(1) Whoever commits an act that the law defines as an intentional criminal act under a mistake of fact is not guilty.

(2) A criminal act is committed under a mistake of fact if the perpetrator was not aware at the time of commission of circumstances that the law defines as elements of a criminal act, or mistakenly thought that the circumstances were such that the act would be permissible.

(3) For a criminal act committed through negligence, the guilt of the perpetrator cannot be excluded if he was mistaken about circumstances of which he should and could have been aware within the limits of the required care.

Mistake of law

31. člen

(1) A perpetrator of a criminal act who, for justified reasons, did not know that the act was contrary to law, is not guilty.

(2) There are no justified reasons from the first paragraph of this article if the perpetrator did not know of legal rules with which he could have become acquainted under the same conditions as others in his wider environment, or if he should have known special legal rules due to his work, role or other position.

(3) If the perpetrator committed a criminal act under a mistake of law that could have been avoided, he may be punished more leniently.

Necessity

32. člen

(1) An act committed in order for the perpetrator to avert from himself or another a simultaneous danger that could not be averted otherwise is not unlawful if the evil caused is less than the evil threatened (justified necessity).

(2) Not guilty is the perpetrator who commits an unlawful act in order to avert from himself or another a simultaneous danger not caused by him to life, bodily integrity or personal freedom, which could not be averted otherwise, if the evil caused was not disproportionately greater than the evil threatened, and if the perpetrator was not obliged to expose himself to the danger (excusable necessity).

(3) A perpetrator who acted under an avoidable mistake that the circumstances of necessity from the previous paragraph existed is liable for a criminal act of negligence if the law provides for the punishability of negligence for that act.

(4) If in the case from the second paragraph of this article the perpetrator himself caused the danger, but through negligence, or if in the case from the first or second paragraph of this article the perpetrator exceeded the limits of necessity, he may be punished more leniently, but if he exceeded the limits of necessity in particularly mitigating circumstances, the punishment may be remitted.

Exclusion of punishability

33. člen

(1) The law may provide that due to personal relations, characteristics or special circumstances, the perpetrator of an unlawful act is not punished.

(2) Exclusion from prosecution due to non-punishability is not an obstacle to imposing a different legal liability on the perpetrator for the act committed.

3. Attempt of a criminal act

Attempt

34. člen

(1) Whoever has begun an intentional criminal act but has not completed it is punished for attempt if it is a criminal act for which a sentence of three years of imprisonment or a more severe penalty may be imposed under the law; for attempt of other criminal acts only if the law expressly prescribes that the attempt is also punishable.

(2) The perpetrator is punished for attempt within the limits of the penalty prescribed for the criminal act, but may also be punished more leniently.

Impossible attempt

## 35. člen

A perpetrator who attempts to commit a criminal act with an impossible means or against an impossible object may have the punishment remitted.

Voluntary withdrawal

36. člen

(1) A perpetrator who has attempted to commit a criminal act but has voluntarily withdrawn from its commission may have the punishment remitted.

(2) If the perpetrator voluntarily withdraws from the commission of a criminal act, he is punished for those acts that constitute some other independent criminal act.

(3) A perpetrator may have the punishment remitted if he sincerely and appropriately endeavored to prevent the consequence, even if it subsequently did not occur for another reason.

4. Participation in a criminal act

Participant

36.a člen

The provisions of this code that apply to the perpetrator also apply to a female participant or male participant (hereinafter: participant) who participates in a criminal act in the context of instigation or aiding, unless the law provides otherwise.

Instigation

37. člen

(1) Whoever intentionally instigates another to commit a criminal act is punished as if he had committed it himself.

(2) Whoever intentionally instigates another to commit a criminal act for which a sentence of three years of imprisonment or a more severe penalty may be imposed under the law is punished as for attempt of a criminal act, even if there was no attempt to commit the act.

Aiding

38. člen

(1) Whoever intentionally aids the perpetrator in an intentional criminal act is punished as if he had committed it himself, but may also be punished more leniently.

(2) Aiding in the commission of a criminal act includes in particular: if someone gives the perpetrator advice or instructions on how to commit the criminal act, if he makes available to him the means or removes obstacles to the commission, if he promises in advance that he will conceal the criminal act, the perpetrator, the means by which the criminal act will be committed, the traces of the criminal act, objects created by the criminal act or the pecuniary benefit obtained by the criminal act.

Punishment of instigator and aider for attempt

## 39. člen

If a criminal offense remains at an attempt, the female instigator or male instigator (hereinafter: instigator) and the female accomplice or male accomplice (hereinafter: accomplice) shall be punished as for an attempt.

Limits of punishability of perpetrators and participants

40. člen

(1) The perpetrator and co-perpetrator shall be punished for criminal offenses within the limits of their intent or negligence, and the instigator and accomplice within the limits of their intent.

(2) The instigator or accomplice may be excused from punishment if he or she voluntarily prevented the criminal offense.

(3) The same applies if the instigator or accomplice sincerely and appropriately endeavored to prevent the consequence, even if it subsequently did not occur for another reason.

(4) Personal relations, characteristics and special circumstances by reason of which the law excludes guilt or punishability or permits dismissal, reduction or increase of punishment, may only be taken into account with respect to that perpetrator or participant in whom such relations, characteristics and circumstances are established.

Liability of members and leaders of a criminal organization

41. člen

(1) A more severe penalty prescribed for a criminal offense committed in a criminal organization shall be imposed on a female member or male member (hereinafter: member) of an association of at least three persons, if he or she commits a criminal offense for the purpose of carrying out the criminal plan of this organization in connection with at least one more member as a co-perpetrator or participant.

(2) In the case referred to in paragraph one of this Article, the leader of the organization who led the execution of the criminal plan or disposed of the unlawfully obtained property benefit of this organization upon commission of a criminal offense arising from this criminal plan shall also be punished in the same manner as the perpetrator, regardless of whether he or she directly participated in its execution as a perpetrator or participant pursuant to Articles 20 or 37 and 38 of this Code.

5. Punishability of legal entities

Liability of legal entities for criminal offenses

42. člen

(1) Criminal liability shall be enforced against a legal entity for a criminal offense committed by a perpetrator in the name, on account of or for the benefit of the legal entity and it is stated in the law governing the liability of legal entities for criminal offenses that the legal entity is liable therefor.

(2) Criminal liability of legal entities does not exclude the liability of natural persons as perpetrators, instigators or accomplices in the same criminal offense.

(3) The law governing the liability of legal entities for criminal offenses determines the conditions for criminal liability of legal entities, punishments, warning sanctions or security measures and legal consequences of conviction for legal entities.

Fourth Chapter

PUNISHMENTS

1. Types of punishments and conditions for their imposition

Types of punishments

## 43. člen

For criminal offenses, the following penalties may be imposed on guilty perpetrators:

- imprisonment;
- monetary penalty;
- prohibition of driving a motor vehicle;
- expulsion of a foreigner from the country.

Glavne in stranske kazni

44. člen

(1) Imprisonment may be imposed only as a principal penalty.

(2) A monetary penalty may be imposed as both a principal and an accessory penalty.

(3) The prohibition of driving a motor vehicle and the expulsion of a foreigner from the country may be imposed only as an accessory penalty together with a penalty of imprisonment, a monetary penalty or a suspended sentence.

(4) In addition to the principal penalty, one or both accessory penalties may be imposed.

Zakonitost pri izrekanju kazni

45. člen

(1) The perpetrator of a criminal offense shall be imposed the penalty prescribed for the committed criminal offense; a milder penalty than the prescribed one may be imposed only under the conditions determined by this code.

(2) For criminal offenses committed out of greed, a monetary penalty may be imposed as an accessory penalty even when it is not prescribed by law or when the law prescribes that the perpetrator will be punished by imprisonment or a monetary penalty, but the court imposes imprisonment as the principal penalty.

Namen kaznovanja

45.a člen

By punishment under the provisions of this code, the state protects the fundamental values and principles of the legal order, establishes awareness in the perpetrator of the criminal offense and others of the inadmissibility of committing criminal offenses, and primarily, while respecting the human dignity and personality of the perpetrator of the criminal offense, enables the perpetrator to achieve dignified integration into the common social environment through an appropriate sanction.

Zapor

46. člen

(1) Imprisonment may not be shorter than one month and not longer than thirty years.

(2) For criminal offenses of genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes and aggression, and under the conditions of point 1 of the second paragraph of Article 53 of this code for two or more criminal offenses under the fifth paragraph of Article 108, under Article 116, under Article 352, under the second paragraph of Article 360, under the fourth paragraph of Article 371 and under the third paragraph of Article 373, a penalty of life imprisonment may be imposed.

(3) A penalty of imprisonment of up to two years shall be prescribed without a minimum measure of this penalty.

(4) Imprisonment is imposed in whole years and whole months, and up to six months also in whole days.

Denarna kazen

47. člen

(1) A monetary penalty is imposed by multiplying a certain number of daily amounts that the perpetrator must pay, as determined in the judgment, by the amount of the daily sum, which the court determines according to the financial circumstances of the perpetrator.

(2) The number of daily amounts cannot be less than ten and not more than three hundred and sixty, and for criminal offenses committed out of greed, not more than one thousand five hundred. The court determines it taking into account the general rules on the determination of penalties, except for circumstances relating to the financial circumstances of the perpetrator.

(3) The court determines the amount of the daily sum according to the financial circumstances of the perpetrator on the basis of data on his earnings, other income, the value of his property, the average costs of his subsistence and his family obligations. The daily amount cannot be higher than 1,000 euros.

(4) When determining the amount of the daily sum, the court takes into account data that are not older than one year at the time of imposing the penalty. If the court cannot obtain data for determining the amount of the daily sum or if their acquisition would be associated with disproportionate difficulties or delay, or if a penal order is being issued, the amount of the daily sum is determined on the basis of the data available to it and other established circumstances regarding the financial circumstances of the perpetrator.

(5) In the judgment, the court determines the time limit for payment of the monetary penalty, which may not be shorter than fifteen days and not longer than three months. In justified cases, the court may, until the expiry of the time limit for payment, allow the convicted person to pay the monetary penalty in installments, whereby the time limit for payment may not be longer than two years. If the perpetrator delays the payment of an individual installment, the court may order immediate payment by decision within a time limit that may not be longer than three months.

Prepoved vožnje motornega vozila

48. člen

(1) The perpetrator of a criminal offense who has committed a criminal offense against the safety of public traffic as a female driver or male driver (hereinafter: driver) of a motor vehicle may be imposed by the court a prohibition of driving a motor vehicle of a certain type or category.

(2) The court determines the duration of the penalty from the previous paragraph, which may not be shorter than six months and not longer than two years, counting from the day the judgment becomes final. Time spent in prison or in a health institution for treatment and care is not included in the duration of this penalty.

(3) If the penalty under the first paragraph of this article is imposed on a person who has a foreign driving license for a motor vehicle, this penalty includes the prohibition of using this license on the territory of the Republic of Slovenia.

(4) The law may determine the mandatory imposition of the prohibition of driving a motor vehicle.

(5) The court shall not impose or execute the prohibition of driving a motor vehicle if it has imposed on the driver of the motor vehicle a security measure of withdrawal of the driving license.

Izgon tujca iz države

48.a člen

(1) The court may impose on a foreigner expulsion from the territory of the Republic of Slovenia for a period of one to five years if it has imposed a penalty for a criminal offense for which a penalty of more than two years of imprisonment may be imposed, and if, based on his personal circumstances, there is a serious threat to public order or public safety.

(2) The duration of expulsion is counted from the day the judgment becomes final. Time spent in prison is not included in the duration of this penalty.

(3) If the penalty of expulsion of a foreigner from the country is executed more than two years after the judgment imposing the expulsion becomes final, the court reassesses before expulsion whether the personal circumstances that were the basis for the decision on expulsion are still present.

2. Odmera kazni

Splošna pravila za odmero kazni

49. člen

(1) The court shall determine the penalty for the perpetrator of a criminal offense within the limits prescribed by law for that offense, taking into account the gravity of the committed offense and the perpetrator's guilt.

(2) In doing so, the court takes into account all circumstances that affect whether the penalty should be lower or higher (mitigating and aggravating circumstances), in particular: the degree of the perpetrator's guilt, the motives from which the offense was committed, the degree of endangerment or violation of the protected legal value, the circumstances in which the offense was committed, the perpetrator's previous life, his personal and financial circumstances, his behavior after the committed offense, in particular whether he compensated for the damage caused by the criminal offense, and other circumstances relating to the perpetrator's personality and the expected effect of the penalty on the perpetrator's future life in the social environment.

(3) If the motive for committing the criminal offense was the victim's national, racial, religious or ethnic affiliation, his gender, skin color, origin, financial status, education, social position, political or other conviction, disability, sexual orientation or any other personal circumstance, this shall be taken into account as an aggravating circumstance.

(4) When determining the penalty for a perpetrator who has committed a criminal offense after having already been convicted by a final judgment or after having served the penalty or after it has expired by limitation or after it has been remitted (recidivism), the court takes into account in particular whether the previous offense was of the same type as the new one, whether both offenses were committed from the same motives, and how much time has elapsed since the previous conviction or since the served, remitted or time-barred penalty.

Omilitev kazni

## 50. člen

The court may impose a punishment on the perpetrator below the limit prescribed by law, or may apply a milder type of punishment:

- if the law provides that the perpetrator may be punished more mildly;
- if it establishes special mitigating circumstances that justify the imposition of a mitigated punishment.

Limits of mitigation of imprisonment sentence

51. člen

(1) When the conditions for mitigation of punishment from the previous article are met, the court mitigates it within these limits:

- 1) if fifteen years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum punishment for the criminal offense, it may mitigate it down to ten years of imprisonment;
- 2) if three or more years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum punishment for the criminal offense, it may mitigate it down to one year of imprisonment;
- 3) if one year of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum punishment for the criminal offense, it may mitigate it down to three months of imprisonment;
- 4) if less than one year of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum punishment for the criminal offense, it may mitigate it down to one month of imprisonment;
- 5) if imprisonment is prescribed for the criminal offense and no minimum punishment is specified, it may impose a fine instead of imprisonment.
  - (2) For a perpetrator who, under the law governing criminal procedure, admits guilt when first making a statement on the indictment in which mitigation of punishment is proposed for such a case, or admits it in an agreement with the state prosecutor, the court mitigates the punishment within these limits:
- 1) if ten or more years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum punishment for the criminal offense, it may mitigate it down to three years of imprisonment;
- 2) if three to ten years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum punishment for the criminal offense, it may mitigate it down to three months of imprisonment;
- 3) if less than three years of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum punishment for the criminal offense, it may mitigate it down to one month of imprisonment;
- 4) if less than one year of imprisonment is prescribed as the minimum punishment for the criminal offense, it may impose a fine instead of imprisonment.

Remission of punishment

52. člen

(1) The court may remit the punishment of the perpetrator of a criminal offense when the law specifically provides so.

(2) When the court has the right to remit the punishment of the perpetrator of a criminal offense, it may mitigate it without the limitations prescribed for mitigation of punishment.

Concurrence of criminal offenses

53. člen

(1) If the perpetrator has committed two or more criminal offenses by one act or several acts, for which he is tried simultaneously, the court first determines the punishment for each individual criminal offense, and then imposes a single punishment for all these criminal offenses.

(2) The court imposes a single punishment according to the following rules:

- 1) if it has determined a sentence of life imprisonment for any criminal offense in concurrence, or if it has determined a sentence of thirty years of imprisonment for two or more criminal offenses from the second paragraph of Article 46 of this Code in concurrence, it imposes a single sentence of life imprisonment;
- 2) if it has determined a sentence of thirty years of imprisonment for any criminal offense in concurrence and it is not a case from point 1), it imposes only that punishment;
- 3) if it has determined imprisonment sentences for criminal offenses in concurrence, the single punishment must be greater than each individual determined punishment, but must not reach the sum of individual punishments and must not exceed thirty years of imprisonment;
- 4) if imprisonment sentences of up to one year are prescribed for all criminal offenses in concurrence, the single punishment must not be longer than five years of imprisonment;
- 5) if it has determined only fines for criminal offenses in concurrence, it increases the highest determined fine, which must not exceed the sum of determined fines and must not exceed three hundred and sixty daily amounts, or if one or more criminal offenses were committed out of greed, must not exceed one thousand five hundred daily amounts;
- 6) if it has determined imprisonment for some criminal offenses in concurrence, and fines for other criminal offenses, it imposes one imprisonment sentence and one fine according to points 2), 3),
- 4) and 5) of this paragraph;
- 7) if it has determined several accessory punishments of the same type for criminal offenses in concurrence, it imposes a single accessory punishment such that it must not reach their sum and must not exceed the highest general limit of punishment.
  - (3) The court imposes an accessory punishment even if it was determined for only one criminal offense in concurrence; however, if it has determined several fines, it imposes a single fine according to point 5) of the second paragraph of this article.

Continuing criminal offense

54. člen

(1) Whoever, out of greed or harmful motives, simultaneously or successively commits or attempts to commit two or more identical or similar property criminal offenses that, considering the place, manner or other identical circumstances, constitute a unified activity, commits a continuing criminal offense.

(2) For a continuing criminal offense, the provisions on concurrence from the previous article of this Code are not applied, but the perpetrator is imposed one punishment for all criminal offenses together within the limits of the punishment prescribed for the most serious criminal offense.

(3) A perpetrator who, through the activity under the first paragraph of this article, together acquires a greater or large property benefit or causes greater or large property damage, for which a more severe punishment is prescribed for the criminal offense, is imposed this more severe punishment under the second paragraph of this article if his intention was to acquire such benefit or cause such damage through simultaneously or successively committed criminal offenses.

Sentencing of a convict

55. člen

(1) If the court tries a convict for a criminal offense he committed before he began serving the punishment from a previous conviction, or for a criminal offense he committed while serving imprisonment, it imposes a single punishment for all criminal offenses according to Article 53; in doing so, it takes into account that the previously imposed punishment has already been determined. The punishment or part of the punishment that the convict has already served is credited toward the imposed imprisonment sentence.

(2) For a criminal offense committed while serving imprisonment, the court imposes a punishment on the perpetrator regardless of previously imposed punishments if the unserved part of the previously imposed punishment would be disproportionately small when applying the provisions of Article 53.

Crediting of detention and previous punishment

56. člen

(1) Time served in detention, as well as any deprivation of liberty in connection with a criminal offense, is credited toward the imposed imprisonment sentence and fine.

(2) If criminal proceedings are instituted for several criminal offenses and detention is not ordered for each of them, the time served in detention is credited toward the imposed imprisonment sentence and fine for the criminal offense for which the accused is convicted.

(3) A fine, deprivation of liberty or any other sanction that the convict has served or paid for a misdemeanor, as well as punishment or disciplinary deprivation of liberty that he has served for violation of military discipline, is credited toward the punishment imposed for a criminal offense whose elements also have the elements of a misdemeanor or violation of military discipline.

(4) One day of detention, one day of deprivation of liberty, one day of imprisonment and two daily amounts of a fine, as well as 42 euros of a fine imposed for a misdemeanor, are equated in every crediting. Fifth Chapter

WARNING SANCTIONS

1. Conditional sentence

General provisions on conditional sentence

57. člen

(1) The court may, under the conditions specified in this Code, impose a conditional sentence on the perpetrator of a criminal offense instead of punishment.

(2) With a conditional sentence, the court determines a punishment for the perpetrator of a criminal offense, which will not be imposed if the convict does not commit a new criminal offense during the time determined by the court, which must not be less than one year and not more than five years (probationary period).

(3) In a conditional sentence, the court may determine that the specified punishment will also be imposed if the convict does not return the property benefit obtained through the criminal offense within the specified time limit, does not compensate for the damage caused by the criminal offense, or does not fulfill other obligations provided for in criminal law provisions. The time limit for fulfilling these obligations is determined by the court within the limits of the probationary period.

(4) Security measures imposed along with a conditional sentence are enforced.

Imposition of conditional sentence

58. člen

(1) A conditional sentence may be imposed if the court has determined an imprisonment sentence of up to two years or a fine for the perpetrator.

(2) A conditional sentence may not be imposed for criminal offenses for which imprisonment of at least three years is prescribed.

(3) The court imposes a conditional sentence if, considering the perpetrator's personality, his previous life, his behavior after committing the criminal offense, the degree of guilt and other circumstances in which the act was committed, it concludes that it is possible to expect that he will not repeat criminal offenses.

(4) If the court determines accessory punishments in a conditional sentence, it may impose that all or individual accessory punishments be enforced.

(5) For a perpetrator who, under the law governing criminal procedure, admits guilt when first making a statement on the indictment in which the imposition of a conditional sentence is proposed for such a case, or admits it in an agreement with the state prosecutor, the court may impose a conditional sentence even for criminal offenses for which imprisonment of at least five years is prescribed, determine an imprisonment sentence of up to five years, and a probationary period of up to ten years.

Revocation of conditional sentence due to a new criminal offense

59. člen

(1) The court revokes the conditional sentence if the convict commits one or more criminal offenses during the probationary period for which it has imposed an imprisonment sentence of two years or a longer sentence.

(2) If the convict commits one or more criminal offenses during the probationary period for which the court has determined an imprisonment sentence of less than two years or a fine, the court decides whether to revoke the conditional sentence, when it assesses all circumstances relating to the committed criminal offenses and the perpetrator, especially the similarity of the committed criminal offenses, their significance and the motives from which they were committed. In doing so, it is bound by the prohibition that it may not impose a conditional sentence if it is necessary to impose a punishment of more than two years of imprisonment for the criminal offenses established in the conditional sentence and for the new criminal offenses (first paragraph of the previous article), and in cases of admissions of guilt and plea agreements, more than five years of imprisonment (fifth paragraph of the previous article).

(3) If the court revokes the conditional sentence, it imposes a single punishment for the previously committed and new criminal offense according to Article 53 of this Code; in doing so, it takes into account that the punishment from the revoked conditional sentence has already been determined.

(4) If the court does not revoke the conditional sentence, it may impose a conditional sentence or punishment for the new criminal offense. If it concludes that a conditional sentence must also be imposed for the new criminal offense, it determines a single punishment for the previously committed and new criminal offense according to Article 53 of this Code and determines a new probationary period, which must not be shorter than one year and not longer than five years, and in cases of admissions of guilt and plea agreements not longer than ten years, and is counted from the finality of the new judgment. For a convict who is imposed imprisonment for a new criminal offense, the time of serving this punishment is not counted in the probationary period determined by the conditional sentence for the previous criminal offense.

Revocation of conditional sentence due to a previously committed criminal offense

60. člen

(1) The court revokes the conditional sentence if, after its imposition, it establishes that the convict committed a criminal offense before being conditionally sentenced, and if it considers that there would be no basis for a conditional sentence if this act had been known. In such a case, it acts according to the third paragraph of the previous article.

(2) If the court does not revoke the conditional sentence, it acts according to the fourth paragraph of the previous article.

Revocation of conditional sentence due to non-fulfillment of imposed obligations

61. člen

(1) If the convict was imposed to fulfill any obligation from the third paragraph of Article 57 of this Code with the conditional sentence but has not fulfilled it within the time limit specified in the judgment, the court may, within the limits of the probationary period, extend the time limit for fulfillment of the obligation or revoke the conditional sentence and impose the punishment that was determined in the conditional sentence. If it concludes that the convict cannot fulfill the imposed obligation for justified reasons, the court may release him from its fulfillment or replace it with another appropriate obligation specified in the law.

(2) If personal bankruptcy proceedings against the convict have started during the time limit for fulfillment of the obligation from the third paragraph of Article 57 of this Code, which was specified in the judgment, the course of the time limit for fulfillment of this obligation is interrupted for the duration of personal bankruptcy, and the time limit in which the court may make decisions from the previous paragraph is extended beyond the limit of the determined probationary period for the duration of such interruption, notwithstanding the previous paragraph.

Time limits for revocation of conditional sentence

62. člen

(1) A conditional sentence may be revoked during the probationary period. If the convict commits a criminal offense during this time that results in revocation of the conditional sentence, but this is established by judgment only after the expiry of the probationary period, the conditional sentence may be revoked at the latest within one year after the expiry of the probationary period.

(2) If the convict does not fulfill the obligation from the third paragraph of Article 57 of this Code within the given time limit, the court may revoke the conditional sentence and impose the punishment that was determined in the conditional sentence at the latest within one year after the expiry of the probationary period or after the expiry of the time limit from the second paragraph of the previous article.

2. Conditional sentence with protective supervision

Protective supervision

63. člen

(1) Under the conditions specified in this Code, the court may determine that the perpetrator who has been imposed a conditional sentence will be under protective supervision for a specified time during the probationary period.

(2) Protective supervision includes assistance, supervision or protection determined by law.

Conditions for imposition of protective supervision

64. člen

(1) The court imposes a measure of protective supervision if it concludes that the perpetrator needs some form of such supervision (second paragraph of the previous article) during the probationary period. The court imposes this measure for a specified time within the limits of the probationary period determined by the conditional sentence.

(2) If the court establishes that protective supervision is no longer necessary, it may stop the implementation of this measure before the expiry of the probationary period.

Selection of instructions

65. člen

(1) When imposing a measure of protective supervision, the court may also order one or more instructions that the convict must follow.

(2) When selecting instructions, the court takes into account especially the perpetrator's age, his mental characteristics, the motives from which he committed the act, personal circumstances, his previous life, the circumstances in which he committed the act, and his behavior after committing the criminal offense. It must particularly ensure that the selection of instructions will not violate the convict's human dignity or cause him special difficulties.

(3) The court's instructions may include these tasks:

- 1) treatment in an appropriate health institution, with consent also treatment for alcohol or drug addiction;
- 2) attending an appropriate vocational, psychological or other counseling center;
- 3) vocational training or accepting employment that corresponds to the convict's health, abilities and inclination;
- 4) use of income in accordance with maintenance obligations;
- 5) prohibition of association with certain persons;
- 6) prohibition of establishing direct or indirect contacts with one or more specified persons, including the use of electronic communication means;
- 7) prohibition of approaching the victim or any other person;
- 8) prohibition of access to individual places;
- 9) inclusion in one or more social care services of the social work center.
  - (4) During the probationary period, the court may change or revoke instructions ex officio, at the proposal of the counselor or the convict.

Activity of the counselor

66. člen

(1) Protective supervision is carried out by a counselor appointed by the authority responsible for probation.

(2) The counselor provides assistance and protection to the convict in accordance with the law governing probation and supervises the fulfillment of instructions imposed on the convict by the court. Consequences of non-fulfillment of instructions

67. člen

(1) If the convict does not fulfill the instructions during the probationary period, avoids contacts with the counselor or otherwise violates the personal plan prepared in accordance with the law governing probation, the court may warn him, change the instructions, extend the protective supervision beyond the limit of the determined probationary period for the duration of non-fulfillment of instructions, avoidance of contacts or other violation of the personal plan, or revoke the conditional sentence and impose the punishment that was determined in the conditional sentence.

(2) If the convict also does not fulfill the instructions during the extended protective supervision from the previous paragraph, avoids contacts with the counselor or otherwise violates the personal plan prepared in accordance with the law governing probation, the court may revoke the conditional sentence and impose the punishment that was determined in the conditional sentence.

3. Judicial admonition

Conditions for imposition of judicial admonition

68. člen

(1) A judicial admonition may be imposed for criminal offenses for which a fine or imprisonment of up to one year is prescribed, if they were committed in such mitigating circumstances that make them particularly minor.

(2) For specified criminal offenses, a judicial admonition may be imposed under conditions specified by law, even when imprisonment of up to three years is prescribed for them.

(3) The court may impose a judicial admonition for several criminal offenses committed in concurrence if the conditions from the first or second paragraph of this article are met for each of these acts.

(4) When deciding whether to impose a judicial admonition, the court takes into account the perpetrator's personality, his previous life, his behavior after committing the criminal offense, the degree of guilt and other circumstances in which the act was committed.

(5) For a perpetrator who, under the law governing criminal procedure, admits guilt when first making a statement on the indictment in which the imposition of a judicial admonition is proposed for such a case, or admits it in an agreement with the state prosecutor, the court may impose a judicial admonition even for criminal offenses for which imprisonment of up to three years is prescribed.

Sixth Chapter

SECURITY MEASURES

Types of security measures

## 69. člen

The following security measures may be imposed on perpetrators of criminal offences:

- 1) mandatory psychiatric treatment and custody in a health institution;
- 2) mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty;
- 3) prohibition from practising a profession;
- 4) prohibition of approaching or communicating with the victim;
- 5) revocation of driving licence;
- 6) confiscation of objects.

Imposition of security measures

70. člen

(1) The court may impose on the perpetrator of a criminal offence one or more security measures when the conditions stipulated by this Code are met.

(2) When imposing a security measure, the court shall take into account, according to the principle of proportionality, the gravity of the criminal offence committed and the acts which the court has reasonable grounds to believe the perpetrator might commit if the security measure were not imposed.

(3) Mandatory psychiatric treatment and custody in a health institution and mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty shall be imposed independently on a non-accountable perpetrator of an unlawful act if the safety of people cannot otherwise be ensured. In addition to these two measures, the court may also impose a prohibition from practising a profession, revocation of driving licence and confiscation of objects.

(4) Revocation of driving licence and confiscation of objects may be imposed if the perpetrator has been sentenced to a penalty, conditional sentence, judicial admonition or has been remitted the penalty.

(5) Prohibition from practising a profession may be imposed if the perpetrator has been sentenced to imprisonment or if imprisonment has been determined in a conditional sentence.

(6) Prohibition of approaching or communicating with the victim may be imposed if the perpetrator has been sentenced to a penalty, warning sanction or has been remitted the penalty.

Mandatory psychiatric treatment and custody in a health institution

70.a člen

(1) The court shall impose mandatory psychiatric treatment and custody in a health institution on a perpetrator who has committed an unlawful act in a state of non-accountability or a criminal offence in a state of substantially diminished accountability and for which a sentence of imprisonment of one year or more may be imposed, if, based on the gravity of the act committed and the degree of the perpetrator's mental disorder, it determines that at liberty the perpetrator could commit a serious criminal offence against life, body, sexual integrity or property and that such danger can only be eliminated by treatment and custody in a forensic psychiatric department of a health institution that meets the special security conditions determined by law.

(2) The court shall terminate the measure from the preceding paragraph or replace it with the measure from

Article 70.b of this Code when it determines that treatment and custody in a health institution are no longer necessary. After six months have elapsed, the court shall decide anew each time whether further treatment and custody in the health institution are still necessary.

(3) The measure from the first paragraph of this Article shall last for a non-accountable perpetrator for a maximum of five years.

(4) If a person commits a criminal offence during the serving of a sentence for which the court imposes a security measure pursuant to the first paragraph of this Article, the serving of the sentence shall be interrupted for the duration of the execution of the security measure.

(5) For a perpetrator who has committed a criminal offence in a state of substantially diminished accountability and has been sentenced to imprisonment, the execution of the measure from the first paragraph of this Article shall be terminated when the perpetrator has spent in the health institution the time for which the sentence was imposed. The time spent in the health institution shall be counted towards the time of serving the sentence. If this time is shorter than the imposed sentence, the court may order that the convict must serve the remainder of the sentence or that the convict should be released on parole. When deciding on the parole of the convict, the court shall take into account in particular the success of treatment, the convict's state of health, the time spent in the health institution and the remainder of the sentence not served.

Mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty

70.b člen

(1) The court shall impose mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty on a perpetrator who has committed an unlawful act in a state of non-accountability if it determines that this is necessary and sufficient to prevent the perpetrator from repeating more serious criminal offences.

(2) The court may also impose mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty on a perpetrator whose accountability is substantially diminished and who has been released on parole pursuant to the fifth paragraph of the preceding Article.

(3) Mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty may last for a maximum of two years. After six months have elapsed, the court shall decide anew each time whether mandatory treatment at liberty is still necessary.

(4) If the perpetrator refuses treatment at liberty, or if the perpetrator arbitrarily abandons treatment, or if treatment is not successful, the court may, under the conditions of the first paragraph of the preceding Article, order that this measure be executed in a health institution. In such a case as well, this measure may last for a maximum of two years in total. After six months have elapsed, the court shall decide again whether this measure is still necessary.

Prohibition from practising a profession

71. člen

(1) The court may prohibit the perpetrator of a criminal offence from practising a profession, independent activity or any duty if the perpetrator has abused that profession, position, activity or duty for the commission of a criminal offence and if the court has reasonable grounds to believe that it would be dangerous if the perpetrator were to continue practising such activity.

(2) The court shall determine how long the measure from the preceding paragraph is to last; it may not last less than one year and not more than five years, counted from the finality of the decision, whereby the time spent in prison or in a health institution for treatment and custody shall not be counted towards the duration of this measure.

(3) When imposing a conditional sentence, the court may determine that the sentence shall be revoked if the perpetrator violates the prohibition from practising a profession.

(4) The court may decide that this security measure shall cease if two years have elapsed from the commencement of its execution. The court shall decide on this at the request of the convict if it determines that the reasons for imposing this measure have ceased.

Prohibition of approaching or communicating with the victim of a criminal offence

71.a člen

(1) The court may impose on the perpetrator of a criminal offence against life and body, against honour and reputation, against sexual integrity, against human health or another criminal offence with elements of violence a prohibition of approaching or communicating with the victim of any of these criminal offences for a period from one month to three years.

(2) Within the framework of the measure from the preceding paragraph, the court may determine:

- prohibition of approaching the victim or the victim's close relatives,
- prohibition of establishing direct or indirect contact or communicating with the victim or the victim's close relatives, including by means of electronic communications,
- prohibition of access to individual places where the victim or the victim's close relatives regularly move, or
- prohibition of associating with the victim or the victim's close relatives.
  - (3) The court may impose this measure if the perpetrator has, through the criminal offence, harmed the physical or mental integrity of the victim and if it assesses that the perpetrator's contact or communication with the victim or the victim's close relatives would constitute a danger of repetition of the same type or commission of another criminal offence against the victim or the victim's close relatives. The court shall select one or more methods of execution of the measure from the preceding paragraph and shall in particular precisely define the persons to whom the measure applies, and the relationship between the perpetrator and the victim or the victim's close relative, appropriate distances of the perpetrator from the victim or the victim's close relative or places that must be respected, define individual methods of prohibited forms of communication or determine prohibited forms of association.
  - (4) When imposing a conditional sentence, the court may decide that the sentence shall be revoked if the perpetrator violates the prohibition of approaching or communicating with the victim or the victim's close relative.
  - (5) The duration of the prohibition of approaching or communicating shall be counted from the finality of the judgment. The time spent in prison or in a health institution for treatment and custody shall not be counted towards the duration of this measure.
  - (6) The court may decide that this security measure shall cease if six months have elapsed from the commencement of execution. The court shall decide on this at the request of the convict if it determines that the reasons for imposing this measure have ceased.

Revocation of driving licence

72. člen

(1) The court may revoke the driving licence of the perpetrator of a criminal offence against the safety of public traffic for particular types of motor vehicles and may determine that a new licence shall not be issued for one to five years. If the perpetrator does not have a driving licence, the court shall impose that a licence shall not be issued.

(2) The court may impose this measure if it finds that the perpetrator's further participation in public traffic would constitute a danger to public traffic due to the perpetrator's behaviour, personal characteristics or inability to safely operate motor vehicles.

(3) The driving licence shall cease to be valid upon the finality of the court decision. The time spent in prison or in a health institution for treatment and custody shall not be counted towards the duration of this measure.

(4) After the expiry of the time determined by the court within the minimum and maximum duration of this measure, the perpetrator may obtain a new driving licence under the general conditions prescribed for obtaining particular types of driving licences.

(5) The court may decide that this security measure shall cease and that the perpetrator may obtain a new driving licence if two years have elapsed from the commencement of its execution. The court shall decide on this at the request of the convict if it determines that the reasons for imposing this measure have ceased.

Confiscation of objects

73. člen

(1) Objects that have been used or intended for a criminal offence or have originated from a criminal offence may be taken if they are the property of the perpetrator.

(2) Objects from the preceding paragraph may be taken even when they are not the property of the perpetrator if general safety or moral reasons so require, but the right of others to claim compensation from the perpetrator is not thereby affected.

(3) Mandatory confiscation of objects may be determined by law, even if they are not the property of the perpetrator.

Chapter Seven

CONFISCATION OF PECUNIARY BENEFIT ACQUIRED THROUGH A CRIMINAL OFFENCE

Grounds for confiscation of pecuniary benefit

74. člen

(1) No one may retain a pecuniary benefit that has been acquired through a criminal offence or due to it.

(2) The benefit from the preceding paragraph of this Article shall be confiscated by a court decision by which a criminal offence has been established under the conditions determined in this Code.

Method of confiscation of pecuniary benefit

75. člen

(1) Money, valuables and any other pecuniary benefit that has been acquired through a criminal offence or due to it shall be confiscated from the perpetrator or other recipient of the benefit (hereinafter: recipient), and if they cannot be confiscated, property corresponding to the pecuniary benefit shall be confiscated.

(2) If the pecuniary benefit or property corresponding to the pecuniary benefit cannot be confiscated from the perpetrator or other recipient of the benefit, the perpetrator or recipient shall be ordered to pay a monetary amount corresponding to that pecuniary benefit. The court may, in justified cases, permit that the monetary amount corresponding to the pecuniary benefit may also be paid in instalments, provided that the payment period shall not be longer than two years.

(3) Pecuniary benefit acquired through a criminal offence or due to it may also be confiscated from those to whom it has been transferred without payment or for payment that does not correspond to the actual value, if they knew or could have known that it was acquired through a criminal offence or due to it.

(4) If pecuniary benefit acquired through a criminal offence or due to it has been transferred to close relatives of the perpetrator of the criminal offence (relationships from Article 224 of this Code), or if, for the purpose of preventing confiscation of the pecuniary benefit under the first paragraph of this Article, any other property of the perpetrator has been transferred to these persons, it shall be confiscated from them, unless they prove that they have paid the actual value for it.

(5) If pecuniary benefit has been acquired by several persons jointly, the share acquired by each person shall be confiscated; if this share cannot be determined more precisely, it shall be determined by the court taking into account all the circumstances of the case.

Protection of the injured party

76. člen

(1) If a property claim has been awarded to the injured party in criminal proceedings, the court shall impose confiscation of pecuniary benefit when it exceeds the property claim awarded to the injured party.

(2) An injured party who has been referred to litigation by the court in criminal proceedings regarding the property claim may request payment from the confiscated value if the injured party commences litigation within six months from the finality of the decision by which the injured party was referred to litigation and if the injured party requests payment from the confiscated value within three months from the finality of the decision by which the injured party's claim was established.

(3) An injured party who did not file a property claim in criminal proceedings may request payment from the confiscated value if litigation to establish the claim was commenced within three months from the day the injured party learned of the decision by which the pecuniary benefit was confiscated, but no later than two years from the finality of that decision, and if within three months from the finality of the decision by which the injured party's claim was established, the injured party requests payment from the confiscated value.

Confiscation of pecuniary benefit from a legal person

## 77. člen

If property benefit has been obtained by or through a criminal offense to a legal entity, such benefit shall be confiscated from it. Property benefit or property corresponding to the property benefit shall also be confiscated from a legal entity if persons referred to in the first paragraph of Article 75 of this Code transferred it to the legal entity gratuitously or for payment that does not correspond to the actual value.

Confiscation of property for criminal offenses committed in a criminal organization

77.a člen

(1) Under the provisions of this chapter, property benefit or property obtained by or through a criminal offense or which is possessed by a criminal organization shall also be confiscated.

(2) From the perpetrator who committed a criminal offense in a criminal organization, property that the court determines originates from criminal activities committed in that organization shall also be confiscated as property benefit obtained through a criminal offense.

Confiscation of property obtained in connection with a criminal offense

77.b člen

Under the provisions of this chapter, property that the perpetrator or other persons from whom property is confiscated use for their exclusive or predominant benefit with the consent of persons to whom the property belongs shall also be confiscated as property obtained in connection with or through a criminal offense, if those persons knew or should have known that the property was obtained by or through a criminal offense or that a criminal offense was prepared, committed or concealed with it, or that it was obtained with the intention of being used for a criminal offense.

Presumption of gratuitousness

77.c člen

(1) Regardless of the legal basis of the transfer, it shall be presumed that property benefit or property that is the subject of confiscation under the provisions of this chapter was transferred gratuitously or for payment that does not correspond to the actual value if the perpetrator or his close relatives (relationships under Article 224 of this Code) directly or indirectly transferred it to a company or other legal entity that is majority-owned by them or in which they have the right to exercise predominant influence or control.

(2) Property benefit or property shall not be confiscated from the company or entity referred to in the previous paragraph if it proves that it paid the actual value for it.

Chapter Eight

LEGAL CONSEQUENCES OF CONVICTION

Arising of legal consequences of conviction

78. člen

(1) Convictions for individual criminal offenses or individual sentences may result in the termination or loss of individual rights or prohibition of acquisition of individual rights as a legal consequence.

(2) Legal consequences of conviction cannot arise if a fine, suspended sentence or judicial admonition was imposed on the perpetrator for the criminal offense or if the sentence was remitted.

(3) Legal consequences of conviction may be prescribed only by law and arise by operation of the law by which they are prescribed.

(4) Only those legal consequences of conviction that were determined by law at the time of commission of the criminal offense may be applied to the perpetrator.

Types of legal consequences of conviction

79. člen

(1) A legal consequence of conviction relating to the termination or loss of individual rights is the termination of performance of certain public functions or powers of an official person or termination of employment relationship or loss of a foreigner's right to reside in the Republic of Slovenia.

(2) Legal consequences consisting of prohibition of acquisition of individual rights are:

- 1) prohibition of performance of individual public functions or powers of an official person;
- 2) prohibition of acquisition of an individual profession or conclusion of an employment contract;
- 3) prohibition of acquisition of individual permits or approvals issued by state authorities through their decision.

Commencement and duration of legal consequences of conviction

80. člen

(1) Legal consequences take effect from the day the conviction becomes final.

(2) Legal consequences of conviction consisting of prohibition of acquisition of individual rights may last a maximum of five years from the date the sentence was served, remitted or became time-barred, unless a shorter or longer period is prescribed by law for individual legal consequences.

(3) After the expiry of two years from the date the sentence was served, became time-barred or was remitted, the court may, at the request of the convict, order the termination of the legal consequence of conviction relating to the prohibition of acquisition of an individual right.

(4) When assessing whether to order the termination of the legal consequence of conviction, the court shall consider the conduct of the convict after conviction, whether he compensated for the damage caused by the criminal offense, and also other circumstances indicating that the termination of the legal consequence of conviction is justified.

(5) The termination of legal consequences of conviction does not affect those rights of others that are based on the conviction.

(6) Legal consequences cease with the expungement of the conviction.

Chapter Nine

REHABILITATION, EXPUNGEMENT OF CONVICTION AND CONDITIONS FOR PROVIDING DATA FROM CRIMINAL RECORDS

Legal status of convict after serving sentence

81. člen

(1) After serving, paying or otherwise executing in accordance with law, remitting or time-barring of the sentence, former convicts have all rights provided for in the constitution, laws and other regulations, and may acquire all rights, except those limited to them due to an imposed security measure or due to legal consequences of conviction that have arisen.

(2) The previous paragraph also applies to those on parole.

Statutory rehabilitation and expungement of conviction

82. člen

(1) By statutory rehabilitation, the conviction is expunged from the criminal record and all its legal consequences cease, and the convict is considered unconvicted.

(2) A final decision is considered a conviction, as well as amendments to that decision by amnesty or pardon.

(3) A conviction shall be expunged from the criminal record within a specified period from the time the sentence was executed, became time-barred or was remitted, if the convict does not commit a new criminal offense during that period.

(4) The periods referred to in the previous paragraph are:

- 1) for a conviction by which a judicial admonition was imposed on the perpetrator, and for a conviction by which the sentence was remitted – one year from the finality of the court decision;
- 2) for a suspended sentence – one year from the expiry of the probationary period or the period under the second paragraph of Article 61 or Article 67 of this Code;
- 3) for a conviction to a fine, accessory sentence or imprisonment sentence of up to one year – three years;
- 4) for a conviction to an imprisonment sentence of more than one year to three years – five years;
- 5) for a conviction to an imprisonment sentence of more than three years to five years – eight years;
- 6) for a conviction to an imprisonment sentence of more than five to ten years – ten years;
- 7) for a conviction to an imprisonment sentence of more than ten to fifteen years – fifteen years.
  - (5) A conviction to an imprisonment sentence of more than fifteen years shall not be expunged.
  - (6) A conviction cannot be expunged from the criminal record as long as security measures are in effect.
  - (7) A security measure of compulsory psychiatric treatment and custody in a health institution and compulsory psychiatric treatment at liberty, which was imposed independently, shall be expunged within three years from the time it ceased to be executed or the execution became time-barred.

Judicial rehabilitation

## 83. člen

The court may, at the request of the convicted person, decide that the conviction be deleted from the criminal record and that the convicted person be considered unconvicted if half of the statutory period has elapsed after which the conviction is deleted, provided that the convicted person has not committed a new criminal offense during this time. When deciding on deletion, the court takes into account the behavior of the convicted person after serving the sentence, the nature of the criminal offense, and other circumstances relevant to the deletion of the conviction. Provision of data from the criminal record

84. člen

(1) Data on pronounced judgments are kept in criminal records. The scope of data and the provision of data from criminal records before deletion are determined by law.

(2) At the request of kindergartens, guardians of preschool children, schools, other institutions, establishments or associations to which children or minors are entrusted for learning, education, care or custody, data from criminal records are also provided for deleted convictions for criminal offenses under Article 170, Article 171, Article 172, Article 173, Article 173.a, the second paragraph of Article 174 committed against a minor, the second paragraph of Article 175 and Article 176 of this code, as well as for deleted convictions for comparable criminal offenses pronounced against citizens of the Republic of Slovenia by foreign courts, for the purpose of verifying the fulfillment of conditions for their employee or other person who performs or will perform work for them.

(3) The law determines that convictions for criminal offenses from the previous paragraph are entered in the register of deleted convictions for criminal offenses against sexual integrity and prescribes the conditions, limitations and procedure for providing data on such convictions; in cases not covered by the previous paragraph, the conviction is considered deleted despite being retained in this register

(first paragraph of this Article).

Deseto poglavje

TEMELJNE DOLOČBE O IZVRŠEVANJU KAZENSKIH SANKCIJ

Položaj obsojenca na prestajanju kazni zapora

85. člen

(1) The rights guaranteed by the constitution and laws of those against whom criminal sanctions are executed may be withdrawn or restricted in accordance with law only to the extent necessary to execute an individual criminal sanction.

(2) A person against whom a criminal sanction is being executed must not be tortured, nor may they be treated in a cruel, inhuman or degrading manner. If they have been treated in such a manner, judicial protection must be guaranteed to them.

(3) Convicted persons must be treated humanely, their personal dignity must be respected, and their physical and mental integrity must be protected.

(4) Convicted persons must be provided with appropriate health care, including, with their consent, treatment for alcohol or drug addiction.

Način izvršitve kazni zapora

86. člen

(1) Convicted persons serve prison sentences in institutions for serving sentences (hereinafter: institution), which are determined by law.

(2) Convicted persons are classified into institutions according to the extent to which their freedom must be restricted.

(3) If a convicted person has been sentenced to imprisonment of up to five years, the court may order that it be served in an open or semi-open institution or department; if they have been sentenced to imprisonment of up to eight years, the court may order that it be served in a semi-open institution or department.

(4) A prison sentence of up to three years, except for a criminal offense against sexual integrity, may also be executed for a convicted person who meets the conditions specified in the law governing the execution of criminal sanctions in such a way that the convicted person continues to work or study and reside at home during the serving of the prison sentence, except on free days, usually at the end of the week, when they must be in the institution. More detailed conditions of execution are determined by the institution.

(5) A prison sentence of up to nine months may also be executed by house arrest if, given the danger posed by the convicted person, the possibility of repetition of the act, and the personal, family and professional circumstances of the convicted person at the time of execution of the sentence, there is no need to serve the sentence in an institution, or if, due to illness, disability or old age of the convicted person, the sentence must and can be executed in an appropriate public institution.

(6) During the serving of the sentence, the convicted person may not leave the building or part of the building where house arrest is being executed, unless the court exceptionally permits this for a specified time when it is inevitably necessary for the convicted person to secure the most essential necessities of life or medical assistance, or to perform work. In the judgment by which it determined that the sentence be executed by house arrest, the court also determines protective supervision according to the provisions of this code on conditional sentence, and may also determine one or more instructions. The court may restrict or prohibit the convicted person's contacts with persons who do not reside with them or who do not treat or care for them, and may specify in more detail the manner of execution of house arrest.

(7) If the convicted person leaves the building or part of the building where house arrest is being executed without the permission of the court, or does so outside the permitted time, or does not comply with the restriction or prohibition of contacts, or violates other rules established by the court regarding the execution of house arrest, the court may decide by order that the remainder of the pronounced sentence be executed in an institution.

(8) A prison sentence of up to two years, except for a criminal offense against sexual integrity, may also be executed in such a way that the convicted person, instead of a prison sentence, performs community service work within a period of up to three years from the enforceability of the judgment. The scope of work is determined so that one day of imprisonment is replaced by two hours of work. When determining work for the convicted person within the framework of available work at implementing organizations, the authority responsible for execution takes into account the convicted person's professional knowledge and abilities, and may also take into account those essential interests regarding urgent family, educational or professional obligations, the disregard of which would cause difficult to repair or irreparable damage to the fulfillment of these obligations. Community service work is performed without remuneration.

(9) When deciding on the execution of a prison sentence in the manner referred to in the previous paragraph, the court takes into account in particular the behavior of the convicted person at the time of the decision, the danger of repetition of the act at liberty, the possibility and ability to perform suitable work, and the personal and family circumstances of the convicted person at the time of the anticipated execution of the sentence.

(10) In the judgment in which it determined that the sentence be executed by community service work, the court also determines protective supervision according to the provisions of this code on conditional sentence, and may also determine one or more instructions.

(11) If the convicted person wholly or partially fails to fulfill tasks within the framework of community service work or substantially fails to fulfill instructions within the framework of protective supervision, or avoids contact with the counselor, or otherwise violates obligations from community service work, the court decides by order that the pronounced prison sentence be executed to the extent of the unperformed work.

(12) On the admissibility of execution of a prison sentence in the manners specified in this Article, the court decides on the proposal of the defendant by judgment by which it pronounces a prison sentence, or on the proposal of the convicted person by a special order.

(13) On the admissibility of execution of a prison sentence in the manners specified in this Article, for a defendant who, according to the law governing criminal procedure, admits guilt when making their first statement on the indictment in which a method of execution of the sentence is proposed for such case, or admits it in an agreement with the state prosecutor, the court decides in accordance with this proposal or agreement.

Način izvršitve denarne kazni

87. člen

(1) If the fine cannot even be enforced by compulsion, the court executes it by determining one day of imprisonment for every two commenced daily amounts of the fine, whereby the imprisonment may not be longer than six months.

(2) If the convicted person pays only part of the fine, the remainder is proportionally converted into imprisonment for them; if they pay the remainder as well, the execution of the prison sentence is suspended.

(3) After the death of the convicted person, the fine is not executed.

(4) A fine of up to three hundred and sixty daily amounts may also be executed in such a way that the convicted person, within a period of up to one year, instead of paying the fine, performs community service work without remuneration. The scope of work is determined so that one daily amount is replaced by one hour of work.

(5) Regarding issues not regulated in the previous paragraph, the provisions of the eighth to thirteenth paragraphs of Article 86 of this code apply mutatis mutandis.

Pogojni odpust

88. člen

(1) A convicted person who has served half of the prison sentence may be released from serving the sentence on the condition that they do not commit a new criminal offense until the expiry of the time for which the sentence was pronounced.

(2) A convicted person who has been sentenced by the court to imprisonment of more than fifteen years may be conditionally released from serving the sentence when they have served three-quarters of the sentence.

(3) A convicted person who has been sentenced by the court to life imprisonment may be conditionally released from serving the sentence when they have served twenty-five years of imprisonment.

(4) The authority that decides on conditional release and the procedure for deciding on conditional release are determined by law.

(5) A convicted person may be conditionally released if it can be reasonably expected that they will not repeat the criminal offense. When assessing whether the convicted person should be conditionally released, consideration is given primarily to recidivism, any criminal proceedings pending against the convicted person for criminal offenses committed before the commencement of the prison sentence, the convicted person's attitude toward the criminal offense committed and the victim, their behavior during the serving of the sentence, success in treating addiction, and conditions for integration into life at liberty.

(6) Exceptionally, a convicted person who has served only one-third of the sentence may also be conditionally released if the condition from the fifth paragraph of this Article is fulfilled and if special circumstances relating to the convicted person's personality indicate that they will not repeat the criminal offense.

(7) The authority deciding on conditional release may place the convicted person who will be conditionally released under protective supervision. Protective supervision is carried out by a counselor who has the same tasks as in the case of a conditional sentence with protective supervision.

(8) The authority deciding on conditional release may impose the following tasks on the convicted person who has been conditionally released:

- 1) treatment in an appropriate health institution, with their consent also treatment for alcohol or drug addiction;
- 2) attendance at an appropriate vocational, psychological or other counseling center;
- 3) vocational training or acceptance of employment that corresponds to the convicted person's health, abilities or inclinations;
- 4) expenditure of income in accordance with maintenance obligations;
- 5) prohibition of association with certain persons;
- 6) prohibition of establishing direct or indirect contact with one or more specified persons, including the use of electronic means of communication;
- 7) prohibition of approaching the victim or any other person;
- 8) prohibition of access to particular places.
  - (9) The provisions of this Article also apply to conditional release from house arrest. When assessing whether the convicted person should be conditionally released, for the period of house arrest, compliance with restrictions in its execution is taken into account instead of behavior during the serving of the sentence.

Preklic pogojnega odpusta

89. člen

(1) The court revokes conditional release if the convicted person on conditional release commits one or more criminal offenses for which it has pronounced a prison sentence of more than one year.

(2) The court may revoke conditional release if the conditionally released person commits one or more criminal offenses for which it has pronounced a prison sentence of up to one year. When assessing whether to revoke conditional release, the court takes into account in particular the relatedness of the criminal offenses committed, their significance, the motives from which they were committed, and other circumstances indicating that the revocation of conditional release is justified. The court may also revoke conditional release if the conditionally released person does not perform the tasks imposed on them by the authority deciding on conditional release.

(3) When the court revokes conditional release, it pronounces a sentence according to Article 53 and the second paragraph of Article 55 of this code; in doing so, it takes as determined that part of the previously pronounced sentence which the convicted person has not yet served.

(4) The first, second and third paragraphs of this Article also apply when the court convicts the conditionally released person for an act they committed before being conditionally released.

(5) If the conditionally released person is sentenced to a prison sentence of up to one year and the court does not revoke conditional release, the conditional release is extended for the time the convicted person served the prison sentence.

(6) If the convicted person commits a criminal offense during conditional release that results in the revocation of conditional release, and this is established by judgment only after its expiry, conditional release may be revoked no later than one year from the time when the period of conditional release expired.

Enajsto poglavje

ZASTARANJE

Zastaranje kazenskega pregona

90. člen

(1) Unless otherwise provided in this code, criminal prosecution is no longer permitted if the following has elapsed:

- 1) fifty years from the commission of a criminal offense for which imprisonment of thirty years may be pronounced by law, unless it is a non-prescriptive criminal offense;
- 2) thirty years from the commission of a criminal offense for which imprisonment of more than ten years may be pronounced by law;
- 3) twenty years from the commission of a criminal offense for which imprisonment of more than five years may be pronounced by law;
- 4) ten years from the commission of a criminal offense for which imprisonment of more than one year may be pronounced by law;
- 5) six years from the commission of a criminal offense for which imprisonment of up to one year or a fine may be pronounced by law.
  - (2) If multiple penalties are prescribed for a criminal offense, the prescription period is determined according to the most severe prescribed penalty.
  - (3) Notwithstanding the first paragraph of this Article, for the criminal offense of enslavement and the criminal offense of human trafficking, as well as for criminal offenses against sexual integrity and criminal offenses against marriage, family and children committed against a minor, the period for prescription of criminal prosecution begins to run from the majority of the victim.

Tek in pretrganje zastaranja kazenskega pregona in roka za novo sojenje

91. člen

(1) The prescription of criminal prosecution begins on the day when the criminal offense was committed.

(2) If a final judgment is annulled in proceedings with an extraordinary legal remedy or with a constitutional appeal, the period for a new trial is five years from the annulment of the final judgment.

(3) Prescription and the period for a new trial do not run during the time when prosecution may not be initiated or continued by law, or when the perpetrator is unreachable for state authorities.

(4) Prescription and the period for a new trial are interrupted if the perpetrator, during the time when the prescription period or the period for a new trial is running, commits an equally serious or more serious criminal offense; after the interruption, prescription or the period for a new trial begins to run again.

Zastaranje izvršitve kazni

## Article 92

Unless otherwise provided in this code, an imposed sentence may no longer be executed when the following period has elapsed:

- 1) fifty years from a sentence of thirty years of imprisonment, unless it is a non-prescriptible criminal offense;
- 2) thirty years from a sentence of imprisonment exceeding ten years;
- 3) twenty years from a sentence of imprisonment exceeding five years;
- 4) ten years from a sentence of imprisonment exceeding one year;
- 5) six years from a sentence of imprisonment up to one year or a fine.

Limitation period for execution of accessory penalties and security measures

Article 93

(1) Execution of a fine as an accessory penalty is statute-barred when four years have elapsed from the finality of the judgment imposing this penalty.

(2) Execution of a prohibition from driving a motor vehicle as an accessory penalty is statute-barred when the principal penalty is statute-barred.

(3) Execution of the security measures of mandatory psychiatric treatment and custody in a health institution, mandatory psychiatric treatment at liberty prohibition of approaching or communicating with the victim, and, revocation of driving license and confiscation of objects is statute-barred when six years have elapsed from the finality of the decision imposing these measures.

(4) Execution of the security measure of prohibition from practicing a profession is statute-barred when the time for which the measure was imposed has elapsed.

Commencement and interruption of limitation period

Article 94

(1) The limitation period for execution of a sentence commences on the day the judgment becomes final; if a suspended sentence is revoked, on the day the decision on revocation becomes final.

(2) The limitation period does not run during the time when execution of the sentence is prohibited by law.

(3) The limitation period for execution of a sentence does not run during the time when the convicted person has not responded to a summons to serve the sentence or commencement of the sentence cannot be ensured because the person is a fugitive, is in hiding or is otherwise evading execution of the sentence or is otherwise unavailable to state authorities, or when the commencement of execution of the sentence has been postponed in accordance with law.

(4) The limitation period for execution of a sentence of imprisonment ceases to run on the day of commencement of the sentence. If the convicted person escapes from serving the sentence, execution of the remainder of the sentence is not statute-barred.

(5) The second and third paragraphs of this Article also apply to limitation periods for security measures. Non-prescriptibility of criminal offenses and special limitation period

Article 95

(1) Criminal prosecution and execution of sentence are not statute-barred for criminal offenses for which a sentence of life imprisonment may be imposed under this code, for criminal offenses under Articles 100 to 105 of this code, and also not for those criminal offenses for which limitation is not possible under international treaties.

(2) Execution of a sentence of life imprisonment is not statute-barred.

(3) Criminal prosecution for criminal offenses under Articles 170 to 173, and the second paragraph of Article 174, the second paragraph of Article 175 and the second paragraph of Article 176 of this code is no longer permitted if a period has elapsed since the commission of the criminal offense equal to three times the period determined for limitation of criminal prosecution by the first paragraph of Article 90 of this code.

Chapter Twelve

AMNESTY AND PARDON

Amnesty

## Article 96

By amnesty, persons who are granted it are relieved of prosecution, execution of sentence is remitted in whole or in part, an imposed sentence is commuted to a more lenient one, a conviction is expunged or a legal consequence of conviction is removed.

Pardon

## 97. člen

By pardon, prosecution is remitted by name to an individual person, or the execution of a sentence is completely or partially remitted, the imposed sentence is changed to a more lenient one or to a suspended sentence, or a conviction is deleted, or the legal consequence of conviction is abolished or shortened in duration. Application of the general part in amnesty and pardon

98. člen

(1) If a provision on criminal sanction is changed by amnesty or pardon, the general part of this code applies.

(2) If a sentence of life imprisonment is imposed, by pardon or on the basis of amnesty, a sentence of imprisonment from twenty-five to thirty years is imposed.

(3) By amnesty or pardon, those rights of others that are based on conviction are not affected.

Thirteenth Chapter

Meaning of terms in this code

99. člen

(1) An official person under this code may be:

- 1) a member of the National Assembly, a member of the National Council and a member of a local or regional representative body;
- 2) a judge of the Constitutional Court, a judge, a lay judge, a state prosecutor or a senior state attorney;
- 3) a person who performs official tasks at a state body or a body of a self-governing local community or at another legal person under public law, or has an official function with managerial powers and responsibilities;
- 4) another person who performs individual official tasks on the basis of authorizations given to them by law or by regulations issued on the basis of law (public authorization) or on the basis of an arbitration contract concluded under law;
- 5) a military person who is defined as such by special regulations, in the case of special criminal offences in which an official person is mentioned, but which are not defined as criminal offences against military duty;
- 6) a person who in a foreign state at any level performs a legislative, executive or judicial task or other official duty and in substance fulfils the conditions from points 1, 2 or 3 of this paragraph;
- 7) a person to whom an international public organization gives the position of an official person;
- 8) a person who performs judicial, prosecutorial or other official duty or task at an international court.
  - (2) A military person under this code is considered to be: a soldier, officer, non-commissioned officer and military employee who professionally performs military service, a soldier serving mandatory or voluntary military service and a person liable for mandatory or contractual reserve when performing military service.
  - (3) Elections and voting under this code are considered to be elections of the President of the Republic, members of the National Assembly, members of the National Council, members of the European Parliament, local or regional elections and legislative referendum and other referendums prescribed by the constitution.
  - (4) An extramarital union under this code is considered to be a long-lasting life partnership of a man and a woman who have not entered into marriage.
  - (5) A document is any writing, data carrier or other object suitable and intended for proof of a fact that has value for legal relations.
  - (6) A movable thing under this code is also considered to be any acquired or collected energy for light, heat, radiation, propulsion, movement or transmission of voice, image or text at a distance, and virtual currency.
  - (7) Force is also considered to be the use of hypnosis, drugs or other special means for someone to become unconscious or incapable of resistance against their will.
  - (8) A motor vehicle is considered to be any motor-powered means of transport in land, water and air traffic.
  - (9) Pecuniary benefit, damage or value is considered to be the amount at the time of commission of the criminal offence, which
- 1) in the case of small does not exceed 500 euros;
- 2) in the case of large exceeds 5000 euros;
- 3) in the case of great exceeds 50,000 euros.
  - (10) Economic activity under this code is considered to be:
- 1) any activity carried out for payment on the market;
- 2) any activity carried out professionally or in an organized manner for agreed or prescribed payment.
  - (11) Performance of economic activity or economic operations under this code is considered to be:
- 1) implementation, management, decision-making, representation, direction and supervision within the framework of activities from paragraph ten of this article;
- 2) management of real estate, movables, cash, income, receivables, capital investments, other forms of financial assets and other assets of legal persons under public or private law, disposal of these assets and supervision over them.
  - (12) A large quantity of counterfeit money, vouchers and securities under this code is considered to be a nominal value exceeding 50,000 euros.
  - (13) A commodity spot contract under this code is considered to be a contract for the supply of goods, except for wholesale energy products, which is traded on the spot market and which is delivered immediately upon settlement of the transaction, and a contract for the supply of goods that is not a financial instrument, including a physically settled forward contract.

SPECIAL PART

Fourteenth Chapter

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST HUMANITY

Genocide

100. člen

(1) Whoever orders or commits, with the intention of destroying in whole or in part a national, ethnic, racial or religious group, the following acts:

- killing members of such a group,
- causing serious bodily injury or impairment of mental health to members of such a group,
- deliberately subjecting such a group to living conditions intended to bring about its complete or partial physical destruction,
- implementing measures to prevent births in such a group,
- forcibly transferring children of one group to another group, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.
  - (2) The same punishment shall be imposed on whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph against any group for reasons from point eight of Article 101.

Crimes against humanity

## 101. člen

Whoever orders or commits acts that are part of a widespread or systematic attack against a civilian population with the knowledge of the perpetrator that this is such an attack:

- murder;
- extermination, which means creating such living conditions, including restricting access to food and medicine, that are intended to lead to the partial destruction of the population;
- enslavement, which means exercising any or all of the powers arising from ownership rights over a person and also includes exercising such powers in trafficking in persons, especially women and children;
- deportation or forcible transfer of population, which means the forced removal of persons by expulsion or other coercive acts from the area in which they lawfully reside, without grounds permitted under international law;
- imprisonment or other severe deprivation of liberty in violation of fundamental rules of international law;
- torture, which means the intentional infliction of severe pain, physical or mental suffering on a person in the custody of the perpetrator, whereby torture does not include pain or suffering arising solely from the execution of lawful sanctions or associated with them;
- rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, which means the unlawful detention of a woman who has been impregnated by force, with the intent to affect the ethnic composition of any population or to commit other serious violations of international law, forced sterilization, or any other form of sexual violence of comparable gravity;
- persecution, which means the intentional or severe deprivation of fundamental rights contrary to international law on the basis of an identifiable characteristic of a group or community, namely on political, racial, national, cultural, religious grounds, grounds related to gender, or other grounds universally recognized as impermissible under international law, where such persecution is connected with the criminal offences under this Article and under Articles 100, 102 and 103;
- enforced disappearance of persons, which means the arrest, detention, abduction or any other form of deprivation of liberty of a person by order of the state or a political organization or with its authorization, support or consent, which then does not acknowledge such deprivation of liberty or refuses to provide information about the fate of these persons or their whereabouts, thereby depriving these persons of legal protection;
- apartheid, which means inhumane acts similar to those listed in this Article, committed within the framework of an institutionalized regime of systematic oppression and domination of one racial group over any other racial group or groups with the intent to maintain such a regime;
- other similar inhumane acts by which great suffering or serious bodily injury or impairment of mental or physical health is intentionally caused, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

War crimes

## Article 102

Whoever orders or commits war crimes, especially if committed as part of a plan or policy or as part of large-scale commission of such crimes, which are:

- 1) grave breaches of the Geneva Conventions of 12 August 1949 (Act on Notification of Succession Concerning Council of Europe Conventions, Geneva Conventions and Additional Protocols on the Protection of War Victims and International Agreements in the Field of Arms Control for which the Three Main Nuclear Powers are Depositaries (Official Gazette of the Republic of Slovenia, No. 14/1992)), namely any of the following acts against persons or property protected under the relevant Geneva Conventions:
  - wilful killing;
  - torture or inhuman treatment, including biological experiments;
  - wilfully causing great suffering or serious injury to body or health;
  - extensive destruction or appropriation of property not justified by military necessity and carried out unlawfully and wantonly;
  - compelling a prisoner of war or other protected person to serve in the forces of a hostile power;
  - wilfully depriving a prisoner of war or other protected person of the rights of fair and regular trial;
  - unlawful deportation or unlawful confinement;
  - taking of hostages;
- 2) other violations of the laws and customs applicable in international armed conflicts under applicable international law, namely any of the following acts:
  - intentionally directing attacks against the civilian population as such or against individual civilians not taking direct part in hostilities;
  - intentionally directing attacks against civilian objects, that is, objects which are not military objectives;
  - intentionally directing attacks against personnel, installations and equipment, material, units or vehicles involved in a humanitarian assistance or peacekeeping mission in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations, as long as they are entitled to the protection given to civilians or civilian objects under the international law of armed conflict;
  - intentionally launching an attack in the knowledge that such attack will cause incidental loss of life or injury to civilians or civilian objects or widespread, long-term and severe damage to the natural environment which would be excessive in relation to the concrete and direct overall military advantage anticipated;
  - attacking or bombarding, by whatever means, undefended towns, villages, dwellings or buildings which are not military objectives;
  - killing or wounding a combatant who, having laid down his arms or having no longer means of defence, has surrendered at discretion;
  - making improper use of a flag of truce or of the flag or military insignia or uniform of the enemy or of the United Nations, emblems or flag of the Red Cross or corresponding emblems and other distinctive signs under the Geneva Conventions or markings of cultural property under the Hague Convention (Convention for the Protection of Cultural Property in the Event of Armed Conflict with Regulations for its Execution (Official Gazette of the FPRY – International Treaties, No. 4/56) and Second Protocol to the Hague Convention of 1954 for the Protection of Cultural Property in the Event of Armed Conflict (Official Gazette of the Republic of Slovenia, No. 22/2003)), resulting in death or serious personal injury;
  - the transfer, directly or indirectly, by the occupying power of parts of its own civilian population into the territory it occupies, or the deportation or transfer of all or parts of the population of the occupied territory within or outside this territory;
  - intentionally directing attacks against buildings dedicated to religion, education, art, science or charitable purposes, cultural or historical monuments, cultural property bearing special distinctive marking, natural landmarks, hospitals and places where the sick and wounded are collected, provided they are not military objectives;
  - subjecting persons who are in the power of an adverse party to physical mutilation or to medical or scientific experiments of any kind which are neither justified by the medical, dental or hospital treatment of the person concerned nor carried out in his or her interest, and which cause death to or seriously endanger the health of such person or persons;
  - killing or wounding treacherously individuals belonging to the hostile nation or army;
  - unlawfully taking things from the dead and wounded on the battlefield;
  - declaring that no quarter will be given;
  - destroying or seizing the enemy's property unless such destruction or seizure be imperatively demanded by the necessities of war;
  - declaring abolished, suspended or inadmissible in a court of law the rights and actions of nationals of the hostile party;
  - compelling the nationals of the hostile party to take part in the operations of war directed against their own country, even if they were in the service of the belligerent before the commencement of the war;
  - pillaging a town or place, even when taken by assault;
  - employing poison or poisoned weapons;
  - employing asphyxiating, poisonous or other gases, and all analogous liquids, materials or devices;
  - employing bullets which expand or flatten easily in the human body, such as bullets with a hard envelope which does not entirely cover the core or is pierced with incisions;
  - employing weapons, projectiles and material and methods of warfare which are of a nature to cause superfluous injury or unnecessary suffering or which are inherently indiscriminate in violation of the international law of armed conflict, provided that such weapons, projectiles and material and methods of warfare are the subject of a comprehensive prohibition;
  - committing outrages upon personal dignity, in particular humiliating and degrading treatment;
  - rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, as defined as the unlawful confinement of a woman forcibly made pregnant, with the intent of affecting the ethnic composition of any population or carrying out other grave violations of international law, enforced sterilization, or any other form of sexual violence also constituting a grave breach of the Geneva Conventions;
  - utilizing the presence of a civilian or other protected person to render certain points, areas or military forces immune from military operations;
  - using cultural property under enhanced protection or their immediate surroundings in support of military action;
  - intentionally directing attacks against buildings, material, medical units and transport, and personnel using the distinctive emblems of the Geneva Conventions;
  - intentionally using starvation of civilians as a method of warfare by depriving them of objects indispensable to their survival, including wilfully impeding relief supplies as provided for under the Geneva Conventions;
  - conscripting or enlisting children under the age of fifteen years into the national armed forces or using them to participate actively in hostilities;
- 3) in the case of an armed conflict not of an international character, which does not consist of situations of internal disturbances and tensions such as riots, isolated and sporadic acts of violence or other acts of a similar nature, serious violations of article 3 common to the four Geneva Conventions of
  - 12 August 1949, namely, any of the following acts committed against persons taking no active part in the hostilities, including members of armed forces who have laid down their arms and those placed hors de combat by sickness, wounds, detention or any other cause:
    - violence to life and person, in particular murder of all kinds, mutilation, cruel treatment and torture;
    - committing outrages upon personal dignity, in particular humiliating and degrading treatment;
    - taking of hostages;
    - the passing of sentences and the carrying out of executions without previous judgement pronounced by a regularly constituted court, affording all judicial guarantees which are generally recognized as indispensable;
- 4) other serious violations of the laws and customs applicable in armed conflicts not of an international character, under applicable international law, namely any of the following acts:
  - intentionally directing attacks against the civilian population as such or against individual civilians not taking direct part in hostilities;
  - intentionally directing attacks against buildings, material, medical units and transport, and personnel using the distinctive emblems of the Geneva Conventions in conformity with international law;
  - intentionally directing attacks against personnel, installations, material, units or vehicles involved in a humanitarian assistance or peacekeeping mission in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations (Act on Notification of Succession Concerning Conventions for which the Government of the USA is the Depositary, Hague Conventions and Conventions on Intellectual Property (Official Gazette of the Republic of Slovenia, No. 24/1992)), as long as they are entitled to the protection given to civilians or civilian objects under the international law of armed conflict;
  - intentionally directing attacks against buildings dedicated to religion, education, art, science or charitable purposes, cultural monuments, hospitals and places where the sick and wounded are collected, provided they are not military objectives;
  - pillaging a town or place, even when taken by assault;
  - rape, sexual slavery, enforced prostitution, forced pregnancy, as defined as the unlawful confinement of a woman forcibly made pregnant, with the intent of affecting the ethnic composition of any population, enforced sterilization, and any other form of sexual violence also constituting a serious violation of article 3 common to the four Geneva Conventions;
  - conscripting or enlisting children under the age of fifteen years into armed forces or groups or using them to participate actively in hostilities;
  - ordering the displacement of the civilian population for reasons related to the conflict, unless the security of the civilians involved or imperative military reasons so demand;
  - killing or wounding treacherously a combatant of the adverse party;
  - declaring that no quarter will be given;
  - subjecting persons who are in the power of another party to the conflict to physical mutilation or to medical or scientific experiments of any kind which are neither justified by the medical, dental or hospital treatment of the person concerned nor carried out in his or her interest, and which cause death to or seriously endanger the health of such person or persons;
  - destroying or seizing the property of an adversary unless such destruction or seizure be imperatively demanded by the necessities of the conflict;
  - employing poison or poisoned weapons;
  - employing asphyxiating, poisonous or other gases, and all analogous liquids, materials or devices;
  - employing bullets which expand or flatten easily in the human body, such as bullets with a hard envelope which does not entirely cover the core or is pierced with incisions, shall be punished by imprisonment for at least fifteen years.

Aggression

Article 103

(1) An official or other person in a position of effective control or capacity to direct political or military action of a state, who plans, prepares, initiates or executes an act of aggression which, by its character, gravity and scale, constitutes a manifest violation of the Charter of the United Nations, shall be punished by imprisonment for at least fifteen years.

(2) For the purpose of this provision, "act of aggression" means the use of armed force directed against the sovereignty, territorial integrity or political independence of another state, or in any other manner inconsistent with the Charter of the United Nations. The following acts, regardless of a declaration of war, shall constitute aggression:

- 1) the invasion or armed attack on the territory, sea, aircraft, ports or vessels of another state, with temporary or permanent military occupation or forcible annexation of the territory of that state or part thereof;
- 2) bombardment or the use of any weapons against the territory of another state;
- 3) the blockade of the ports or coasts of another state;
- 4) the use of armed forces which are within the territory of another state with its consent, in contravention of the conditions provided for in such consent, or the retention of such forces beyond the time provided for in such consent;
- 5) allowing another state to use the territory of the Republic of Slovenia, which is at the disposal of that state, to perpetrate an act of aggression against another state;
- 6) the sending of armed bands, mercenaries or paramilitary units with the intent of carrying out such acts of gravity as to amount to the acts listed above.

Responsibility of military commanders and other superiors

Article 104

(1) A military commander for the criminal offences referred to in Articles 100 to 103 or paragraph three of Article 134 of this Code, committed by forces under his or her effective command and control, as a result of his or her failure to exercise control properly over such forces and failure to take all appropriate and necessary measures within his or her power to prevent or repress their commission or to submit the matter to the competent authorities for investigation and prosecution, although he or she knew that his or her forces committed or were about to commit such criminal offences in the circumstances, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to eight years.

(2) For the acts referred to in the preceding paragraph, a person effectively acting as a military commander, or a person effectively exercising executive authority and control in a civilian organization or enterprise, shall be punished in the same manner.

(3) For the acts referred to in paragraphs one and two of this Article, a military commander or a person effectively acting as a military commander, or a person effectively exercising executive authority and control in a civilian organization or enterprise, who should have known or could have known that his or her forces or other subordinates committed or were about to commit the criminal offences referred to in Articles 100 to 103 or paragraph three of Article 134 of this Code in the circumstances, shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years.

Association and incitement to genocide, crimes against humanity or aggression

Article 105

(1) Whoever establishes a criminal association for the commission of the criminal offences referred to in Articles 100 to

103 of this Code shall be punished by imprisonment of one to ten years.

(2) Whoever becomes a member of the association referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years.

(3) The perpetrator of the criminal offence referred to in paragraph one or two of this Article who prevents the commission of the criminal offences specified in paragraph one or reports them in time shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years, but the penalty may also be remitted.

(4) Whoever calls for or incites the direct commission of the criminal offences referred to in Articles 100 to 103 shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years.

Recruitment of military mercenaries and persons under eighteen years of age

Article 106

(1) Whoever, during war, armed conflict or occupation, or whoever in the execution of or in support of a policy of any state or organization as part of a large-scale systematic attack, orders the recruitment or recruits persons who have not yet reached the age of eighteen years into national or other armed forces and uses them or orders their use to participate actively in hostilities, shall be punished by imprisonment of ten to fifteen years.

(2) Whoever recruits, trains or finances the training of military mercenaries shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Unjustified delay in repatriation of prisoners of war or civilians

## 107. člen

Whoever, in violation of the rules of international law, orders or personally delays the repatriation of prisoners of war or civilian persons after the end of war or armed conflict, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Terrorism

108. člen

(1) Whoever, with the intent to destroy or seriously endanger the constitutional, economic, social or political foundations of the Republic of Slovenia or another state or international organization, to seriously intimidate the population, or to compel the government of the Republic of Slovenia or another state or international organization to do or refrain from doing something, commits or threatens to commit one or more of the following acts:

- attack on life and body or on human rights and freedoms,
- abduction or taking of hostages,
- substantial destruction of state or public facilities or representations of foreign states, transportation system, infrastructure, public place or private property,
- unauthorized interference with an information system that seriously disrupts or interrupts its operation by entering, transmitting, damaging, deleting or altering data or by preventing or disabling access to data, and which causes serious damage or was committed using a computer program, passwords or access codes designed or adapted for the purpose of committing the act, or unauthorized interference with an information system of critical infrastructure that seriously disrupts or interrupts its operation by entering, transmitting, damaging, deleting or altering data or preventing or disabling access to data,
- unauthorized deletion, damage or alteration of data in an information system of critical infrastructure or prevention or disabling of access to such data,
- seizure or taking control of a fixed platform in the continental shelf using force or threat or other means of intimidation, or violent conduct against a person on the platform if such act endangers its safety, or destruction of a fixed platform in the continental shelf or causing damage to it that may endanger its safety, or placement of a device to destroy the fixed platform or endanger safety on it, or injury or causing death of a person in connection with any of the aforementioned acts,
- hijacking of aircraft, vessel, means of cargo transport or public transport,
- production, possession, acquisition, transport, supply or use of weapons, explosives, nuclear, biological or chemical weapons,
- research and development of nuclear, biological or chemical weapons,
- endangering safety by releasing dangerous substances or causing fires, floods or explosions,
- disruption or interruption of supply of water, electricity or other natural resources essential for human life, which may endanger human life, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.
  - (2) Whoever seeks to achieve the purpose from the previous paragraph by using or threatening to use nuclear or other radioactive substance or device, by damaging a nuclear facility so that it releases radioactive substance or enables its release, or by threat or use of force demands nuclear or other radioactive substance, device or nuclear facility, shall be punished by imprisonment up to fifteen years.
  - (3) Whoever prepares or assists in preparing the criminal offences from the previous paragraphs by unlawfully obtaining or providing material resources necessary for the commission of these criminal offences, or by extortion prepares another person to participate in these criminal offences, or forges official or public documents necessary for the commission of these criminal offences, or uses such documents, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.
  - (4) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from eight to fifteen years.
  - (5) If the perpetrator, in committing the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article, intentionally takes the life of one or more persons, he shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.
  - (6) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article is committed in a criminal organization or group that has the intent to commit the criminal offences (hereinafter terrorist criminal organization or group) specified in these paragraphs, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from eight to fifteen years.
  - (7) Whoever participates in a terrorist criminal organization or group that has the intent to commit criminal offences from the first, second, fourth or fifth paragraph of this Article, shall be punished by imprisonment up to eight years.
  - (8) Whoever establishes or leads a criminal organization from the previous paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Travel abroad for the purpose of terrorism

108.a člen

(1) Whoever travels abroad with the intent to commit acts from Article 108 of this Code shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(2) Whoever travels abroad with the intent to commit acts from the second or third paragraph of Article 111 of this Code shall be punished by imprisonment up to eight years.

(3) Whoever, knowing that it is for the purpose from the first or second paragraph of this Article, organizes or otherwise enables travel from the first or second paragraph of this Article, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Financing of terrorism

109. člen

(1) Whoever provides or collects money or property with the intent or knowing that it will be used in part or in whole for the commission of an act from Articles 108, 108.a, 110, 111, 307, 329, 330, 352, 353, 354, 355, 371 and 373 of this Code, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) The perpetrator of the act from the previous paragraph shall be punished equally even if the money or property provided or collected with intent was not actually used for the commission of the criminal offences specified in the previous paragraph.

(3) The same punishment applies to whoever provides or collects money or property with the intent or knowing that it will be used in part or in whole by a terrorist, terrorist criminal organization or group for the commission of terrorist criminal offences.

(4) If the act from the previous paragraphs was committed in a terrorist criminal organization or group for the commission of terrorist criminal offences, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(5) The money and property from the previous paragraphs shall be confiscated.

Incitement and public glorification of terrorist acts

110. člen

(1) Whoever incites the commission of criminal offences from Article 108 of this Code by disseminating messages or making them available to other persons in any other way and thereby causes a danger for the commission of one or more such criminal offences, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever directly or indirectly publicly glorifies or advocates criminal offences from Article 108 or a criminal offence from the previous paragraph by disseminating messages or making them available to the public and thereby causes a danger for the commission of one or more such acts.

(3) Prosecution for criminal offences from the previous paragraphs shall be initiated with the permission of the Minister of Justice.

Recruitment and training for terrorism

111. člen

(1) Whoever recruits for terrorism by encouraging another person to commit a criminal offence from Article 108 of this Code or to participate in ordering such a terrorist criminal offence or to join a terrorist criminal organization or group for the commission of terrorist criminal offences committed by that criminal organization or group, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever trains others in the production and use of explosives, firearms or other weapons, harmful or dangerous substances or in other special methods and techniques with the intent to commit or participate in the commission of a criminal offence from Article 108 of this Code.

(3) The same punishment applies to whoever, through active participation, receives training in the production and use of explosives, firearms or other weapons, harmful or dangerous substances or in other special methods and techniques with the intent to commit or participate in the commission of a criminal offence from Article 108 of this Code.

Placing in slavery relationship

112. člen

(1) Whoever, in violation of the rules of international law, places another person in slavery or a similar relationship or keeps them in such a relationship, buys, sells, hands over to another person or mediates in the purchase, sale or handover of such a person or incites another to sell their freedom or the freedom of a person they support or care for, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) Whoever transports persons in slavery or a similar relationship from one state to another shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(3) Whoever commits an act from the first or second paragraph of this Article against a minor shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

Trafficking in human beings

113. člen

(1) Whoever, for the purpose of exploitation of prostitution or other forms of sexual abuse, forced labor, begging, slavery or a similar relationship, servitude, commission of criminal offences or trade in organs, human tissues or blood, buys, takes over, accommodates, transports, sells, hands over or otherwise disposes of another person, or recruits such a person, exchanges or transfers control over them or mediates in these actions, shall, regardless of any possible consent of that person, be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed against a minor or by force, threat, deception, abduction or abuse of a subordinate or dependent position, or by giving or receiving payments or benefits to obtain the consent of a person who has control over another person, or with the intent to force pregnancy or artificial insemination, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(3) Whoever, with the intent to commit an act from the first or second paragraph of this Article, withholds, takes away, hides, damages or destroys a public document with which the identity of a victim of trafficking in human beings is proven, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years and a fine.

(4) Whoever knows that a person is a victim of trafficking in human beings but uses their services that are the result of exploitation of that person as described in the first and second paragraphs of this Article, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years and a fine.

(5) Whoever commits an act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article as a member of a criminal organization for the commission of such acts or if a large property benefit was obtained by that act, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years and a fine.

(6) The same as in the previous paragraph shall be punished by an official or public employee who commits an act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article in the performance of their duties.

Prohibited creation of living beings

114. člen

(1) Whoever produces or participates in the production or attempts to produce or cross-breed humans or other species, which is harmful to humanity and prohibited by regulations and international law, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

(2) If the acts from the previous paragraph involve the creation of a human being that is genetically identical to another living or dead human being, the creation of human embryos for research, industrial or commercial purposes, or the replacement of significant human body parts or organs, which is not permitted by regulations and international law, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

(3) Whoever performs genetic tests that can predict hereditary diseases or enable determination of gene carrier status responsible for a disease, or reveal genetic predisposition or susceptibility to a disease, but these tests are not performed exclusively for health purposes or as health research for health purposes, or whoever omits appropriate genetic counseling when performing tests or, in violation of regulations, conducts scientific research in the field of biology and medicine that is prohibited by regulations and international law, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to three years.

(4) The same as in the previous paragraph shall be punished whoever, in research on human embryos, endangers the integrity or life of a human embryo.

(5) Whoever enables acts from the first or second paragraph of this Article by financing, by providing facilities, tools or substances for the production of living beings or their cross-breeding, by recruiting collaborators or by otherwise organizing production, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years.

Chapter Fifteen

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST LIFE AND BODY

Homicide

115. člen

(1) Whoever takes the life of another shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed by two or more persons who have joined together to commit homicide, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

Murder

## 116. člen

Whosoever murders someone by taking their life

1) in a gruesome or treacherous manner;

2) because of action in official acts of protecting public safety or in pre-trial proceedings or because of decisions of state prosecutors or because of proceedings and decisions of judges or because of a denunciation or testimony in court proceedings;

3) because of violation of equality;

4) out of bloodlust, out of greed, in order to commit or conceal another criminal offense, out of ruthless revenge or out of other base motives;

5) by an act committed in a criminal association for the commission of such acts, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Killing in the heat of passion

## 117. člen

Whosoever kills someone in the heat of passion because, through no fault of their own, they were greatly provoked by an attack or severe insults, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years. Causing death by negligence

## 118. člen

Whosoever causes the death of another by negligence shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Infanticide

## 119. člen

A mother who takes the life of her child during childbirth or immediately after it, while still under its influence, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Incitement to suicide and assistance in suicide

120. člen

(1) Whosoever intentionally incites someone to suicide or assists them in it and the person commits it, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) Whosoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph against a minor who has already reached the age of fourteen, or against a person whose ability to understand the meaning of their act or to control their conduct was substantially diminished, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(3) If the act from the first paragraph of this Article is committed against a minor who has not yet reached the age of fourteen, or against a person who was unable to understand the meaning of their act or to control their conduct, the perpetrator shall be punished as for manslaughter or murder.

(4) Whosoever treats in a cruel or inhumane manner someone who is subordinate to or dependent on them, and due to such treatment the person commits suicide, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(5) Whosoever assists someone in suicide and the person commits it, but where special mitigating circumstances exist, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(6) If someone has only attempted suicide as a result of any act from the preceding paragraphs, the court may punish the perpetrator more leniently.

Unlawful intervention in pregnancy

121. člen

(1) Whosoever, in contravention of the health conditions and manner of artificial termination of pregnancy determined by law, terminates the pregnancy of a pregnant woman with her consent, begins to terminate it or assists her in terminating the pregnancy, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) Whosoever terminates or begins to terminate the pregnancy of a pregnant woman without her consent shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(3) Whosoever, by using methods of medically assisted fertilization, influences the selection of the sex of the future child, except if this is to avoid a severe hereditary disease linked to sex, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(4) In the same manner as in the preceding paragraph of this Article, whosoever unlawfully performs a procedure of biomedically assisted fertilization for the purpose of surrogate motherhood shall be punished.

(5) In the same manner as in the third paragraph of this Article, whosoever trades in sperm cells, unfertilized egg cells and early human embryos shall be punished.

(6) If the act from the preceding paragraphs results in particularly severe bodily injury to the woman, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(7) If the woman dies as a result of the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

Minor bodily injury

122. člen

(1) Whosoever injures someone bodily in such a way that a part of their body or an organ was temporarily impaired or weakened as a result, or the injured person's capacity for work was temporarily diminished or their appearance was affected or their health was temporarily impaired, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the injury from the preceding paragraph was inflicted with a weapon, a dangerous tool, another means or in such a manner that the body can be severely injured or health severely impaired, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(3) The court may impose a judicial admonition on the perpetrator of the act from the preceding paragraph, especially if the perpetrator was provoked by indecent or cruel behavior of the injured person.

(4) Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this Article shall be instituted upon a motion.

Severe bodily injury

123. člen

(1) Whosoever injures someone bodily or inflicts such harm to their health that the life of the injured person could be endangered as a result, or a part of their body or an organ is destroyed or permanently and substantially weakened or an important part of the body or an important organ is temporarily and substantially weakened or the injured person is thereby temporarily incapable of any work or their capacity for work is permanently diminished or was temporarily considerably diminished or they were temporarily disfigured or their health was temporarily severely or permanently to a lesser extent impaired, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) If the injured person dies as a result of the injury from the preceding paragraph, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(3) Whosoever commits the act from the first paragraph of this Article by negligence shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(4) If the perpetrator committed the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article in the heat of passion because the injured person, through no fault of their own, greatly provoked them by an attack or severe insults, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Particularly severe bodily injury

124. člen

(1) Whosoever injures someone so severely bodily or inflicts such severe harm to their health that the life of the injured person was endangered as a result, or an important part of their body or an important organ is destroyed or permanently and very severely weakened or the injured person thereby became permanently incapable of any work or remained disfigured or their health was permanently severely impaired, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) If the injured person dies as a result of the injury from the preceding paragraph, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(3) Whosoever commits the act from the first paragraph of this Article by negligence shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(4) If the perpetrator committed the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article in the heat of passion because the injured person, through no fault of their own, greatly provoked them by an attack or severe insults, they shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Exclusion of criminal offense in case of bodily injury with the consent of the injured person

125. člen

(1) Causing minor bodily injury (Article 122) is not unlawful if the injured person consented to it. In this case, the consent of a minor or incapacitated person is taken into account if, in accordance with the law and in the interest of their health, it is given by the person who represents such persons.

(2) Intentional causing of severe (Article 123) or particularly severe (Article 124) bodily injury is not unlawful if the injured person consented to it and the interests of another person were not affected or any common legal value endangered.

(3) Notwithstanding the preceding paragraph, intentional causing of severe or particularly severe bodily injury in treatment or medical activity is not unlawful if consent was given in the form and under the conditions determined by law.

Participation in a brawl

## 126. člen

Whosoever participates in a brawl in which someone is killed or severely bodily injured shall be punished for the participation alone by imprisonment of up to one year.

Endangerment with a dangerous tool in a brawl or quarrel

127. člen

(1) Whosoever, in a brawl or quarrel, reaches for a weapon, dangerous tool or any other means with which the body can be severely injured or health severely impaired, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to six months.

(2) Prosecution shall be instituted upon a motion.

Causing danger

## 128. člen

Whosoever leaves someone without assistance in mortal danger which they themselves caused shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Abandonment of a helpless person

## Article 129

Whoever leaves a person who has been entrusted to them or for whom they must otherwise care, without assistance in circumstances that are dangerous for life or health, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Failure to Render Assistance

## 130. člen

Whoever fails to assist a person who is in immediate mortal danger, although they could do so without danger to themselves or to another person, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year. Sixteenth Chapter

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST HUMAN RIGHTS AND FREEDOMS

Violation of Equality

131. člen

(1) Whoever, due to difference in nationality, race, colour, religion, ethnic origin, sex, language, political or other belief, sexual orientation, financial status, birth, genetic heritage, education, social status or any other circumstance, deprives anyone of any human right or fundamental freedom recognised by the international community or determined by the constitution or law, or restricts such right or freedom, or whoever, on the basis of such differentiation, grants anyone any special right or privilege, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Whoever persecutes an individual or organisation for advocating the equality of people shall be punished in the same manner.

(3) If the act referred to in paragraph one or two of this Article is committed by an official person by abuse of official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years. Coercion

132. člen

(1) Whoever by force or serious threat coerces another to do or omit something or to suffer something, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Prosecution shall commence upon proposal.

Forced Marriage or Establishment of Similar Union

132.a člen

(1) Whoever by force or threat of use of force or by abuse of another's subordinate or dependent position coerces another into marriage or into establishing a similar union that is, in accordance with law, equated with marriage in certain legal consequences, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) Whoever commits the act referred to in the preceding paragraph against a minor or a feeble person shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Unlawful Deprivation of Liberty

133. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully imprisons another, keeps them imprisoned or in any other way restricts their freedom of movement, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person by abuse of their position or powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(3) Attempt of the act referred to in paragraph one of this Article is punishable.

(4) Whoever unlawfully deprives another of liberty for more than one week or does so in a cruel manner, shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to five years.

Abduction and Enforced Disappearance

134. člen

(1) Whoever abducts another with the intention of coercing them or another person to do, omit or suffer something, or whoever in connection with the abduction coerces the abducted person or another person to do, omit or suffer something, shall be punished by imprisonment of six months to five years.

(2) Whoever commits the act referred to in the preceding paragraph against a minor or threatens the abducted person with murder or with serious bodily injury, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to ten years.

(3) Whoever arrests, detains, abducts or otherwise deprives another of liberty by order of a state or political organisation or with its authorisation, support or consent, and subsequently refuses to acknowledge such deprivation of liberty or refuses to provide information about the fate of such person or their whereabouts, thereby withdrawing legal protection from such person, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to eight years.

(4) The perpetrator of acts referred to in paragraph one or two of this Article who voluntarily releases the abducted person before their demand for which they abducted them is fulfilled, may be punished more leniently or their punishment may be remitted.

Stalking

134.a člen

(1) Whoever stalks another person or their close relative by repeated observation, following or intrusive attempts to establish direct contact or contact via electronic communication means and thereby causes fear or endangerment in them or their close relative, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) If the stalked person is a minor or a feeble person, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to three years.

(3) Prosecution for the act referred to in paragraphs one and two of this Article shall commence upon proposal.

Threat

135. člen

(1) Whoever, in order to intimidate or harass them, seriously threatens another that they will attack their life or body or freedom or destroy their property of great value, or that they will commit these acts against their close relative, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to six months.

(2) Whoever commits the act referred to in the preceding paragraph against two or more persons or with brutal conduct or with a weapon, dangerous tool, other means or in such a manner that the body may be seriously injured or health seriously impaired, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(3) Prosecution for the act referred to in paragraphs one and two of this Article shall commence upon proposal.

Torture

135.a člen

(1) Whoever intentionally inflicts severe pain or suffering, whether physical or mental, on another person in order to obtain information or a confession from them or a third person, to punish them for an act committed or suspected to have been committed by them or a third person, to intimidate them or exert pressure on them or to intimidate a third person or exert pressure on them, or for any other reason based on any form of violation of equality, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to ten years.

(2) If the pain or suffering referred to in the preceding paragraph is inflicted or caused by an official person or anyone else acting with official status or authorisation or at the instigation of such person or with their express or tacit consent, they shall be punished by imprisonment of three to twelve years. Unauthorised Personal Search

136. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully searches another person or things that they have on their person or with them, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person by abuse of official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(3) Attempt of acts referred to in paragraphs one and two of this Article is punishable.

(4) Prosecution for the act referred to in paragraph one of this Article shall commence upon proposal.

Unauthorised Eavesdropping and Audio Recording

137. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully eavesdrops on or audio records a conversation or statement not intended for them using special devices, or whoever directly transmits such conversation or statement to a third person or plays such recording to them or in any other way enables them to become directly acquainted with it, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Whoever audio records another's confidential statement intended for them without their consent with the intention of abusing such statement, or whoever directly transmits such statement to a third person or plays such recording to them or in any other way enables them to become directly acquainted with it, shall be punished in the same manner.

(3) If the act referred to in paragraph one or two of this Article is committed by an official person by abuse of official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to five years.

(4) Prosecution for the act referred to in paragraph one of this Article shall commence upon proposal, and for the act referred to in paragraph two by private action.

Unauthorised Image Recording

138. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully records or makes an image recording of another person or their premises without their consent and thereby substantially interferes with their privacy, or whoever directly transmits such recording to a third person or displays such recording to them or in any other way enables them to become directly acquainted with it, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person by abuse of official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to five years.

(3) Prosecution for the act referred to in paragraph one of this Article shall commence upon proposal.

Violation of Secrecy of Communications

139. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully opens another's letter, telegram or any other sealed writing or package, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to six months.

(2) The following shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year:

- 1) whoever, by using technical or chemical means, without opening another's letter, telegram or any other sealed package, unlawfully becomes acquainted with its contents;
- 2) whoever, by using technical means, unlawfully becomes acquainted with a message being transmitted by telephone or any other electronic communication means;
- 3) whoever unlawfully opens a sealed object protecting a message and unlawfully becomes acquainted with the message therein.
  - (3) Whoever, by any of the acts referred to in paragraphs one and two of this Article, enables another person to become directly acquainted with the contents of a message or package, shall be punished in the same manner as in the preceding paragraph.
  - (4) Whoever unlawfully withholds, conceals, destroys or hands over to another person another's letter, telegram or any other package before the recipient has become acquainted with its contents, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.
  - (5) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraphs of this Article is committed by an official person by abuse of official position or official powers, or by a postal worker or other worker entrusted with receiving, transferring or delivering other people's letters, telegrams or any other writings or packages, they shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to five years.
  - (6) Prosecution for acts referred to in paragraphs one to four of this Article shall commence upon proposal.

Unauthorised Publication of Private Writings

140. člen

(1) Whoever, without permission of an authorised person, when such permission is required, publishes a diary, letter or any other private writing, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Prosecution shall commence by private action.

Violation of Inviolability of Dwelling

141. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully enters another's dwelling or enclosed premises, or whoever, at the request of the authorised person, does not remove themselves from there or in any other way prevents them from using it, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Whoever unlawfully searches the dwelling or premises referred to in paragraph one of this Article shall be punished in the same manner.

(3) If the act referred to in paragraph one or two of this Article is committed by an official person by abuse of official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to five years.

(4) Attempt of acts referred to in paragraphs one, two and three of this Article is punishable.

(5) If the court imposes a suspended sentence, it may order the perpetrator to vacate the dwelling or premises within a specified period.

(6) Prosecution for acts referred to in paragraphs one and two of this Article shall commence upon proposal.

Unauthorised Disclosure of Professional Secrecy

142. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully discloses a secret they learned of as a defence lawyer, attorney, doctor, priest, social worker, psychologist or as any other person in the course of their profession, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) No punishment shall be imposed for the act referred to in the preceding paragraph on whoever discloses a secret for the sake of the general interest or legitimate public interest or for the sake of another person's interest, if such interest is greater than the preservation of the secrecy or if the law provides for release from the duty of preserving the secrecy.

(3) Prosecution shall commence by private action.

Abuse of Personal Data

143. člen

(1) Whoever, without a basis in law or in the personal consent of the individual to whom the personal data relate, submits for public publication or publicly publishes personal data that are being processed on the basis of law or personal consent of the individual, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Whoever breaks into or enters without authorisation a computerised database with the intention of obtaining any personal data for themselves or another person shall be punished in the same manner.

(3) Whoever on the internet or otherwise publicly publishes or enables another person to publish personal data of victims of criminal offences, victims of violations of rights or freedoms, protected witnesses, found in court files of judicial proceedings where the presence of the public or identification of victims or protected witnesses and personal records about them in connection with the judicial proceedings is not permitted by law or by court decision, on the basis of which these persons can be determined or are determinable, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(4) Whoever, by assuming the identity of another person or by processing their personal data, exploits their rights, obtains pecuniary or non-pecuniary benefit at their expense or injures their personal dignity, shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to three years.

(5) Whoever commits the act referred to in paragraph one of this Article by submitting for public publication or publicly publishing sensitive personal data, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(6) Whoever publicly publishes recordings or messages of another person with sexual content without the consent of that person and thereby seriously injures their privacy, shall be punished by imprisonment of three months to three years.

(7) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraphs of this Article is committed by an official person by abuse of official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(8) Prosecution under paragraphs four and six of this Article shall commence upon proposal.

Violation of the Right to Legal Remedy or Petition

144. člen

(1) Whoever, in the course of their work, prevents another person from exercising their right to appeal, objection or any other legal remedy, to a submission or proposal, or from making political or other initiatives of general importance, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person by abuse of their position or powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Prevention or Obstruction of Public Assembly

145. člen

(1) Whoever by force, serious threat, deception or in any other unlawful manner prevents or obstructs the convening or course of a peaceful public assembly, shall be punished by a fine or by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the act referred to in paragraph one of this Article is committed by an official person by abuse of their official position or official powers, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Prevention of Printing and Broadcasting

## 146. člen

Whoever unlawfully prevents the printing, sale or distribution of a newspaper, book or other printed matter or the broadcasting of a radio or television programme, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Violation of Moral Copyright

147. člen

(1) Whoever publishes, displays, performs or broadcasts another's copyrighted work or part thereof under their own name or the name of another person, or permits this to be done, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Whoever defaces, mutilates or otherwise unlawfully interferes with another's copyrighted work, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

(3) Prosecution shall be instituted upon a motion.

Violation of Material Copyright

148. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully uses one or more copyrighted works or copies thereof whose total market value represents a substantial property value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) If the market value of the copyrighted works from the preceding paragraph represents a large property value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(3) If a large unlawful property gain was acquired through the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article and the perpetrator intended to acquire such property gain for themselves or another person, they shall be punished by imprisonment of one to eight years.

(4) Copies of copyrighted works and devices for their reproduction shall be confiscated.

(5) In determining the property value under the provisions of this Article and Article 149 of this Code, the benefit from the unlawful use of material copyright or the unlawful reproduction, making available to the public, distribution or rental of related rights for profit shall be taken into account.

Violation of Related Rights

149. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully reproduces, makes available to the public, distributes or rents out one or more performances, phonograms, videograms, broadcasts or databases whose total market value represents a substantial property value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) Whoever unlawfully reproduces, makes available to the public, distributes or rents out one or more performances, phonograms, videograms, broadcasts or databases whose total market value represents a large property value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(3) If a large unlawful property gain was acquired through the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article and the perpetrator intended to acquire such property gain for themselves or another person, they shall be punished by imprisonment of one to eight years.

(4) Copies of performances, phonograms, videograms, broadcasts or databases and devices for their reproduction shall be confiscated.

Chapter Seventeen

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST ELECTORAL RIGHTS AND ELECTIONS

Violation of Electoral Rights

## 150. člen

An official person who, with the intention of preventing someone from exercising their electoral right, unlawfully fails to register them in the electoral register or deletes them from it, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Violation of Free Decision of Voters

151. člen

(1) Whoever by force, serious threat, bribery, deception or other unlawful means compels someone or influences them to vote or not vote in elections or voting, or not to vote validly, or to vote for or against a particular proposal, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person in the performance of their duty in connection with elections or voting, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(3) The bribe given shall be confiscated.

Abuse of Electoral Rights

## 152. člen

Whoever votes in elections or voting in place of another person under their name or votes more than once in the same voting, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Violation of Free Determination

## 153. člen

Whoever calls upon a voter in elections or voting to account for their vote or demands that they say how they voted or why they did not vote, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Destruction or Forgery of Electoral Documents

154. člen

(1) Whoever in elections or voting destroys, damages, conceals or forges any document concerning elections or voting or any object that is evidence for determining the outcome of elections or voting, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed by an official person in the performance of their duty in connection with elections or voting, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years. Forgery of Electoral Results

## 155. člen

An official who, in elections or voting, changes the number of votes cast by adding or removing ballots or votes during counting or announces the result of the election or voting that does not match the voting conducted, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years. Violation of voting secrecy

156. člen

(1) Whoever in any way violates the secrecy of voting in elections or voting shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed by an official in the performance of their duties in connection with elections or voting, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years. Accepting bribes in elections

157. člen

(1) Whoever, in order not to vote in elections or voting or to vote for or against a specific proposal or not to vote validly, demands or accepts a reward, gift or any other pecuniary or non-pecuniary benefit for themselves or for another, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) The accepted reward, gift or other pecuniary benefit shall be confiscated.

Chapter Eighteen

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST HONOR AND REPUTATION

Insult

158. člen

(1) Whoever insults someone shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three months.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed through print, radio, television or other means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

(3) A person shall not be punished if they express themselves insultingly about someone in a scientific, literary or artistic work, in serious criticism, in the performance of official duty, journalistic profession, political or other social activity, defense of any right or protection of legitimate interests, if it is evident from the manner of expression or from other circumstances that they did not do so with the intent to demean.

(4) If the insulted person has returned the insult, the court may punish both parties or one of them or remit the punishment.

Defamation

159. člen

(1) Whoever asserts or spreads something untrue about someone that may harm their honor or reputation, even though they know that what they are asserting or spreading is untrue, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed through print, radio, television or other means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(3) If what is falsely asserted or spread is of such nature that it has serious consequences for the injured party, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Offensive accusation

160. člen

(1) Whoever asserts or spreads something about someone that may harm their honor or reputation shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three months.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed through print, radio, television or other means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

(3) If what is asserted or spread is of such nature that it has serious consequences for the injured party, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) If they prove the truthfulness of their assertion or if they prove that they had reasonable grounds to believe in the truthfulness of what they asserted or spread, the perpetrator shall not be punished for offensive accusation, but may be punished for insult (Article 158) or imputation of a criminal offense with the intent to demean (Article 162).

(5) If someone asserts or spreads that someone has committed a criminal offense for which the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio, the truth that the injured party committed the criminal offense may be proven only by a final judgment, or by other evidence only if prosecution or trial is not possible or not permitted.

(6) If the offensive accusation that the injured party committed a criminal offense for which the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio was committed in the circumstances of the third paragraph

of Article 158 of this Code, the perpetrator shall not be punished for offensive accusation, even though there is no final judgment, if they prove that they had reasonable grounds to believe in the truthfulness of what they asserted or spread.

Spreading private information

161. člen

(1) Whoever asserts or spreads something from the personal or family life of a person that may harm their reputation shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three months.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed through print, radio, television or other means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

(3) If what someone asserts or spreads is of such nature that it has serious consequences for the injured party, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) The truthfulness or untruthfulness of what is asserted or spread from the personal or family life of another cannot be proven, except in cases under the fifth paragraph of this Article.

(5) Whoever asserts or spreads something from the personal or family life of another in the performance of official duty, political or other social activity, in defense of any right or protection of legitimate interests, shall not be punished if they prove the truthfulness of their assertion or if they prove that they had reasonable grounds to believe in the truthfulness of what they asserted or spread. Imputation of a criminal offense with the intent to demean

162. člen

(1) Whoever with the intent to demean imputes to someone that they have committed a criminal offense or that they were convicted of a criminal offense or tells this to someone with the same intent shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three months.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed through print, radio, television or other means of public communication or on websites or at a public gathering, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

Disparagement of the Republic of Slovenia

163. člen

(1) Whoever publicly commits an act from Articles 158 to 162 of this Code against the Republic of Slovenia or the President of the Republic in connection with the performance of their duties shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever publicly disparages the flag, coat of arms or anthem of the Republic

of Slovenia.

Disparagement of a foreign state or international organization

164. člen

(1) Whoever publicly commits an act from Articles 158 to 162 of this Code against a foreign state, the leader of a foreign state or a diplomatic representative of a foreign state in the Republic of Slovenia or whoever publicly disparages the flag, coat of arms or anthem of a foreign state shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever commits the act from the previous paragraph against an international organization recognized by the Republic of Slovenia, or against its representative or its symbols.

Disparagement of the Slovenian nation or national communities

## Article 165

Whoever publicly commits an act referred to in Articles 158 to 160 of this Code against the Slovenian nation or against the Italian or Hungarian national community or against the Roma community living in the Republic of Slovenia, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Public publication of criminal offenses against honor and good name

Article 166

(1) The responsible editor or the person who replaced them, for criminal offenses referred to in

Articles 158 to 165 of this Code, which are committed by public publication of these acts in newspapers and magazines, radio and television programs, electronic publications, on teletext or in other forms of daily or periodical publications or on websites, shall be punished within the limits of the penalty prescribed for the criminal offense, under one of the following conditions:

- 1) if the author remained unknown until the end of the main hearing before the first instance court;
- 2) if the information was published without the author's consent;
- 3) if, at the time the information was published, there were factual or legal obstacles to the prosecution of the author, which still persist.
  - (2) Under the conditions of the previous paragraph, the publisher or printer shall be punished equally if the public publication of the criminal offenses referred to in Articles 158 to 165 of this Code was committed through a non-periodical printed publication, and the producer, if it was committed through a gramophone record, compact disc, film, DVD and other video media, audio or similar media intended for a wider circle of people.
  - (3) The responsible editor or the person who replaced them shall not be punished for the act referred to in the first paragraph of this article if it was a live broadcast that they could not prevent or a publication on websites that allow users to publish content in real time or without prior supervision.

Remission of penalty for criminal offenses under Articles 158 to 162

## Article 167

If the perpetrator of a criminal offense referred to in Articles 158 to 162 and 166 of this Code was provoked by indecent or cruel conduct of the injured party or apologizes to the injured party before the court or retracts before the court what they claimed or spread, the court may remit the penalty.

Special provisions on prosecution

Article 168

(1) Prosecution for criminal offenses referred to in Articles 158 to 162 and 166 of this Code shall be initiated by private action.

(2) If the acts referred to in Articles 158 to 162 and 166 of this Code were committed against a state body or a municipal or regional body or against an official or military person in connection with the performance of their service in that body, prosecution shall be initiated upon a motion, except for official persons referred to in point 1 of the first paragraph of Article 99, functionaries of the Government of the Republic of Slovenia and functionaries of self-governing local communities, where prosecution shall be initiated by private action.

(3) Prosecution for the criminal offense referred to in Article 164 of this Code shall be initiated with the permission of the Minister of Justice.

(4) If the acts referred to in Articles 158 to 162 and 166 of this Code were committed against a deceased person, prosecution shall be initiated by private action of the spouse, person with whom the deceased lived in extramarital cohabitation, partner from a registered same-sex partnership, their children or adoptees, parents or adopters, brothers or sisters.

Publication of judgment

## 169. člen

At the conviction for criminal offenses from Articles 158 to 165 of this code, committed through press, radio, television or other means of public information or on websites, the court may, at the request of the injured party, decide that the judgment be published at the expense of the convicted person in the same manner as the act was committed, in whole or in extract.

Nineteenth Chapter

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST SEXUAL INVIOLABILITY

Rape

170. člen

(1) Whoever, without the consent of another person, achieves that this person engages in sexual intercourse or equivalent sexual act, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) Consent from the previous paragraph of this article is given if the person, according to their externally perceptible, unambiguous and free will, consented to sexual intercourse or equivalent sexual act and was capable of making such a decision.

(3) Whoever forces another person to sexual intercourse or equivalent sexual act by using force or threatening with immediate attack on life or body, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(4) If the act from the previous paragraph was committed in a cruel or particularly humiliating manner, or if the act was committed by several persons consecutively or against convicts or other persons deprived of liberty, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(5) Whoever forces another person to sexual intercourse or equivalent sexual act by threatening to reveal about them or their relatives something that would harm their honor or good name, or that they will cause great property damage to them or their relatives, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(6) If the acts from the first, third or fifth paragraph of this article were committed against a person with whom the perpetrator lives in marital, extramarital or registered same-sex partnership, prosecution shall be initiated upon proposal.

Sexual Violence

171. člen

(1) Whoever, without the consent of another person, achieves that this person commits or suffers any sexual act not covered by the previous article, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

(2) Consent from the previous paragraph is given if the person, according to their externally perceptible, unambiguous and free will, consented to the sexual act from the previous paragraph and was capable of making such a decision.

(3) Whoever uses force or threatens another person with immediate attack on life or body and thus forces them to commit or suffer any sexual act from the first paragraph of this article, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to ten years.

(4) If the act from the previous paragraph was committed in a cruel or particularly humiliating manner, or if the act was committed by several persons consecutively or against convicts or other persons deprived of liberty, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(5) Whoever forces another person to commit or suffer any sexual act from the first paragraph of this article by threatening to reveal about them or their relatives something that would harm their honor or good name, or that they will cause great property damage to them or their relatives, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

(6) If the acts from the first, third or fifth paragraph of this article were committed against a person with whom the perpetrator lives in marital, extramarital or registered same-sex partnership, prosecution shall be initiated upon proposal.

Sexual Abuse of a Vulnerable Person

172. člen

(1) Whoever engages in sexual intercourse or commits any other sexual act with another person without their consent, by abusing their mental illness, temporary mental disorder or more severe mental underdevelopment, due to which they are unable to give consent, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(2) Consent from the previous paragraph is given if the person, according to their externally perceptible, unambiguous and free will, consented to sexual intercourse or any other sexual act and was capable of making such a decision.

(3) Whoever, in the circumstances from the first paragraph of this article, otherwise violates the sexual inviolability of a vulnerable person, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

Sexual Assault on a Person under Fifteen Years of Age

173. člen

(1) Whoever engages in sexual intercourse or commits any other sexual act with a person of the other or same sex who is not yet fifteen years old, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to eight years.

(2) Whoever commits the act from the previous paragraph with a vulnerable person who is not yet fifteen years old, or by using force or threatening with immediate attack on life or body, or achieves in such manner the commission of the act with another person, shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

(3) A teacher, educator, guardian, adoptive parent, parent, priest, doctor or other person who, by abusing their position, engages in sexual intercourse or commits any other sexual act with a person who is not yet fifteen years old and is entrusted to them for teaching, education, medical treatment, care or custody, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years.

(4) Whoever, in the circumstances from the first, second and third paragraph of this article, otherwise violates the sexual inviolability of a person who is not yet fifteen years old, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

(5) The act from the first paragraph of this article is not unlawful if it was committed with a person of comparable age and if it corresponds to the level of their mental and physical maturity. Solicitation of Persons under Fifteen Years of Age for Sexual Purposes

173.a člen

(1) Whoever solicits a person younger than fifteen years through information or communication technologies for a meeting with the intention of committing against them a criminal offense from the first paragraph of Article 173 of this code or for the production of pictures, audiovisual or other materials of pornographic or other sexual content, and the solicitation was followed by concrete acts to realize the meeting, shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.

(2) The act from the previous paragraph is not unlawful if it was committed for the purpose of realizing the act from the first paragraph of Article 173 and under the conditions from the fifth paragraph of Article 173 of this code.

Violation of Sexual Inviolability by Abuse of Position

174. člen

(1) Whoever abuses their position and thus induces a person of the other or same sex who is subordinate to them or dependent on them to engage in sexual intercourse or to commit or suffer any other sexual act, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

(2) A teacher, educator, guardian, adoptive parent, parent or other person who, by abusing their position, engages in sexual intercourse or commits any other sexual act with a person over fifteen years old who is entrusted to them for teaching, education, care and custody, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

Abuse of Prostitution

175. člen

(1) Whoever, for the purpose of exploitation, participates in the prostitution of another person or whoever, by force, threat or deception, induces, recruits or encourages another person to prostitution, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.

(2) Whoever, for the purpose of exploitation, participates in the prostitution of a minor or resorts to the prostitution of a minor, or whoever, by force, threat, deception, recruitment or solicitation, induces, recruits or encourages a minor to prostitution, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(3) If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this article were committed against several persons or within a criminal organization, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

Display, Production, Possession and Distribution of Pornographic Material

176. člen

(1) Whoever sells to, displays to or by public exhibition or otherwise makes accessible to a person younger than fifteen years writings, pictures, audiovisual or other objects of pornographic content, or shows them a pornographic or other sexual performance, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to two years.

(2) Whoever, by force, threat, deception, exceeding or abuse of powers, recruitment, solicitation or for the purpose of exploitation, induces, recruits or encourages a minor for the production of pictures, audiovisual or other materials of pornographic or other sexual content, for participation in a pornographic or other sexual performance, or whoever knowingly attends such a performance, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.

(3) The same as in the previous paragraph shall be punished whoever, for themselves or another, acquires, produces, distributes, sells, imports, exports or otherwise offers pornographic or other sexual material involving minors or their realistic images, or whoever possesses such material, obtains access or intentionally accesses such material by means of information or communication technologies, or reveals the identity of a minor in such material.

(4) If the act from the second or third paragraph of this article was committed in a criminal organization for the commission of such criminal offenses, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(5) The act from the third paragraph of this article, in the part that constitutes acquisition, production, possession or obtaining access by means of information or communication technologies to pornographic or other sexual material, is not unlawful if it was committed among minors of comparable age who agreed to the act, corresponds to the level of their mental and physical maturity and depicts such persons.

(6) Pornographic or other sexual material from the second, third and fourth paragraph of this article shall be seized or its use appropriately prevented.

Twentieth Chapter

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST HUMAN HEALTH

Transmission of Contagious Diseases

177. člen

(1) Whoever does not comply with regulations or orders by which the competent authority orders examination, disinfection, isolation of patients or any other measures to suppress or prevent contagious diseases in humans and thereby causes a contagious disease to spread, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to one year.

(2) The same shall be punished whoever does not comply with regulations or orders by which the competent authorities prescribe measures to suppress or prevent infectious diseases in animals that can be transmitted to humans, and thereby causes an infectious disease to be transmitted to humans.

(3) Whoever commits the act from the first or second paragraph of this article by negligence, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to six months.

(4) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph by imprisonment up to eight years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment up to five years. Failure to Provide Medical Assistance

178. člen

(1) A doctor or other health worker who, contrary to their professional duty, does not provide assistance to a patient or someone else who is in danger for their life, shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.

(2) The act from the previous paragraph is not unlawful if the doctor omits a certain method of treatment, procedure or medical process at the express written request of a patient or another person who is capable of making decisions about themselves and refuses assistance even after being informed about the necessity of assistance and about possible consequences of refusal, and even after the doctor has again tried to persuade them to change their decision.

Negligent Treatment and Provision of Healing Services

179. člen

(1) A doctor who, in the provision of medical services, acts negligently contrary to the rules of medical science and profession and thereby causes someone's health to deteriorate significantly, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.

(2) The same shall be punished

(a) a health worker who, in their health services, acts negligently contrary to the rules of the profession and thereby causes someone's health to deteriorate significantly, or

(b) a healer who, in the provision of permitted healing services, negligently selects and applies inappropriately a healing system or healing method and thereby causes someone's health to deteriorate significantly.

(3) If due to the act from the first or second paragraph someone dies, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

Quackery

180. člen

(1) Whoever engages in treatment or provision of healing services, although they do not have the prescribed qualification, and thereby turns a patient away from timely seeking medical help, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) The perpetrator of the act from the previous paragraph who causes significant damage to the health of a person who did not consent to the treatment procedures or was not capable of consenting, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.

(3) If the act from the first paragraph results in the death of the patient, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(4) Devices intended for or used in treatment according to the first paragraph of this article shall be confiscated.

Unauthorized Transplantation of Parts of the Human Body and Alteration of the Human Genome

181. člen

(1) Whoever, contrary to regulations in the field of procurement and transplantation of parts of the human body for treatment purposes, removes from a living or dead person a part of the human body for transplantation or other purposes, or whoever transplants a part of the human body to someone contrary to these regulations, or whoever transplants or uses for another purpose a part of the human body knowing that it was removed contrary to regulations in the field of procurement and transplantation of parts of the human body for treatment purposes, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) The punishment from the previous paragraph shall be imposed on whoever unlawfully removes reproductive cells, blood or blood components, unlawfully handles them or violates the anonymity of the donor.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article is committed by a person by abuse of their position, or if the act was committed in a criminal organization for the commission of such acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.

(4) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in particularly severe bodily injury or corresponding impairment of health of one or more persons, or if the act was committed against a minor or other vulnerable person, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(5) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.

(6) Whoever attempts or carries out an intervention whose purpose is to alter the human genome and is not performed for preventive, diagnostic or therapeutic purposes, or whose goal is to introduce changes into the genome of descendants, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

Trafficking in Parts of the Human Body, Reproductive Cells, Blood and Blood Components

181.a člen

(1) Whoever, in order to obtain payment or other property benefit for themselves or someone else, advertises the availability of or need for parts of the human body or solicits or recruits someone to be a donor or recipient of parts of the human body, or otherwise mediates in the giving of body parts of a living or deceased person for transplantation, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

(2) The same as in the previous paragraph shall be punished whoever, in order to obtain payment or other property benefit for themselves or someone else, advertises the availability of or need for reproductive cells, blood or blood components or solicits or recruits someone to be a donor or recipient of reproductive cells, blood or blood components, or otherwise mediates in the giving of reproductive cells, blood or blood components.

(3) The same as in the first paragraph of this article shall be punished whoever removes or obtains a removed part of the human body, reproductive cells, blood or blood components for which the donor receives payment or other property benefit, whoever unlawfully disposes of a removed part of the human body, reproductive cells, blood or blood components, or whoever uses the human body, its parts, reproductive cells, blood or blood components with the intention of obtaining property benefit for themselves or someone else.

(4) The punishment from the first paragraph of this article shall be imposed on whoever prepares, preserves, stores, transports, transfers, receives, imports or exports parts of the human body, reproductive cells, blood or blood components that were removed contrary to regulations in the field of procurement and transplantation of parts of the human body, reproductive cells, blood or blood components for treatment purposes.

(5) If the act from the first, second, third or fourth paragraph of this article is committed by a person by abuse of their position, or if the act was committed in a criminal organization for the commission of such acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.

(6) If the act from the first, second, third or fifth paragraph of this article results in particularly severe bodily injury or corresponding impairment of health of one or more persons, or if the act was committed against a minor or other vulnerable person, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(7) If the act from the first, second, third or fifth paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years. Negligent Provision of Pharmaceutical Services

## 182. člen

A pharmacist or other person authorized to dispense medicines who, through negligence, fails to prepare a medicine in the prescribed ratio or quantity, or dispenses another medicine or substance instead of the prescribed or requested medicine, or otherwise acts contrary to the rules of science and profession in the preparation or dispensing of medicines and thereby causes a significant deterioration in someone's health, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Production and trade in harmful means for treatment

183. člen

(1) Whoever produces, prepares, manufactures, sells or offers for sale, or purchases or stores for the purpose of sale or placing on the market, or acts as intermediary in the sale or purchase, imports or exports, brings in or takes out, or otherwise places on the market medicines, medicinal substances, auxiliary substances, medical devices or their parts or accessories, or other means for treatment that are harmful to health, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever acquires, prepares, manufactures, processes, sells or offers for sale, or purchases or stores for the purpose of sale or placing on the market, or acts as intermediary in the sale or purchase, imports or exports, brings in or takes out, or otherwise places on the market infected blood or other tissue or substances for treatment made therefrom.

(3) Whoever commits an act from the first or second paragraph of this article through negligence shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in serious or particularly serious bodily injury or corresponding impairment of health of one or more persons, or if the act is committed by a person who has abused the trust given, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph of this article by imprisonment from one to ten years, and for the act from the preceding paragraph by imprisonment of up to five years.

(5) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph by imprisonment from two to fifteen years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(6) Harmful medicines, medicinal substances, auxiliary substances, medical devices, their parts, accessories or other means for treatment, infected blood, other tissue or substances for treatment made therefrom shall be seized.

Production and trade in counterfeit means for treatment, medicines without marketing authorization, or medical devices that do not meet conformity requirements

183.a člen

(1) Whoever produces, prepares, manufactures, sells or offers for sale, or purchases or stores for the purpose of sale or placing on the market, or acts as intermediary in the sale or purchase, imports or exports, brings in or takes out, or otherwise places on the market medicines, medicinal substances, auxiliary substances, medical devices or their parts or accessories, or other means for treatment that are counterfeit, and if a more serious criminal offense under this code is not established, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever commits an act from the preceding paragraph with medicines that do not have marketing authorization or with medical devices that do not meet conformity requirements.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article is committed by a person who has abused the trust given, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.

(4) Medicines, medicinal substances, auxiliary substances, medical devices, their parts, accessories or other means for treatment from the first and second paragraph of this article shall be seized.

Production and trade in foodstuffs and other products harmful to health

184. člen

(1) Whoever produces, sells or otherwise places on the market foodstuffs that are harmful to human health and thereby causes danger to human life or health shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever produces, sells or otherwise places on the market personal care products, children's toys or similar products for mass consumption that are harmful to health.

(3) Whoever commits an act from the first or second paragraph of this article through negligence shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in serious or particularly serious bodily injury or corresponding impairment of health of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph by imprisonment of up to eight years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment of up to five years.

(5) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(6) Harmful foodstuffs and other products shall be seized.

Unconscientious inspection of meat for consumption

185. člen

(1) A veterinarian or other authorized person who acts unconscientiously in the inspection of slaughter animals or meat intended for consumption, or fails to perform the inspection despite regulations, and thereby enables meat harmful to human health to be placed on the market, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph is committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

Unauthorized production and trade in illicit drugs, prohibited substances and methods in sport, and precursors for the manufacture of illicit drugs

186. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully produces, processes, sells or offers for sale, or purchases, stores or transports for the purpose of sale or placing on the market, or acts as intermediary in the sale or purchase, or otherwise unlawfully places on the market plants or substances classified as illicit drugs or prohibited substances in sport, or precursors used for the manufacture of illicit drugs, or means or components used for methods classified as prohibited in sport, or methods classified as prohibited in sport, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) Whoever sells, offers for sale or distributes free of charge an illicit drug, or a prohibited substance in sport, or a precursor for the manufacture of illicit drugs, or whoever sells, offers or administers prohibited methods in sport to a minor, a mentally ill person, a person with temporary mental disorder, severe mental retardation or a person undergoing addiction treatment or rehabilitation, or if they commit the act in educational or training institutions or in their immediate vicinity, in prisons, in military units, in public establishments or at public events, or commits the act from the first paragraph as a public official, priest, doctor, social worker, teacher or educator while exploiting their position, or whoever uses minors to commit the aforementioned act, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph is committed within a criminal organization for the commission of such acts, or if the perpetrator of this act has organized a network of traffickers or intermediaries, they shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

(4) Whoever without authorization manufactures, procures, possesses or makes available equipment, substances or precursors, knowing that they are intended for the manufacture of illicit drugs or prohibited substances in sport or for the use of prohibited methods in sport, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(5) Illicit drugs, prohibited substances in sport, means or components enabling the use of prohibited methods in sport, and means for their manufacture shall be seized. Means of transport used for the transport and storage of drugs, prohibited substances in sport or means enabling the use of prohibited methods in sport shall be confiscated if they have specially adapted spaces for the transport and storage of drugs or prohibited substances and methods in sport, or if their owner knew or could have known that they would be used for such purpose.

Enabling the use or application of illicit drugs or prohibited substances or methods in sport

187. člen

(1) Whoever incites another to use illicit drugs or prohibited substances in sport or to apply prohibited methods in sport, or gives them to them for use or application by themselves or another person, or whoever makes premises available for the use or application of illicit drugs, prohibited substances or methods in sport, or otherwise enables another person to use or apply illicit drugs, prohibited substances or methods in sport, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.

(2) Whoever commits the act from the first paragraph against multiple persons, against a minor, a mentally ill person, a person with temporary mental disorder, severe mental retardation or a person undergoing addiction treatment or rehabilitation, or if they commit the act in educational or training institutions or in their immediate vicinity, in prisons, in military units, in public establishments or at public events, or commits the act from the first paragraph as a public official, priest, doctor, social worker, teacher or educator while exploiting their position, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

(3) Illicit drugs, prohibited substances in sport and paraphernalia for their use, as well as means and components enabling the use of prohibited methods in sport, shall be seized.

(4) The act from the first and second paragraph of this article is not unlawful if the perpetrator acts according to a program of addiction treatment or controlled drug use that is approved in accordance with the law and is implemented within or under the supervision of public health care.

Chapter Twenty-One

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST MARRIAGE, FAMILY AND CHILDREN

Bigamy

188. člen

(1) Whoever enters into a new marriage although already married shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) The same punishment shall also apply to whoever enters into a marriage with a person whom they know to be married.

(3) If the previous marriage has ceased or has been annulled, prosecution shall not be initiated, and if it has been initiated, it shall be discontinued.

Change of family status

## Article 189

Whoever substitutes or switches a child or otherwise changes their kinship status shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

Abduction of a Minor

Article 190

(1) Whoever unlawfully removes a minor from a parent, adoptive parent, guardian, institution or person to whom they are entrusted, or detains them and prevents the person entitled to them from having them, or whoever maliciously prevents the enforcement of an enforceable decision concerning a minor, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) If the act has caused deterioration of the minor's mental or physical health or has endangered their development, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.

(3) If the court imposes a suspended sentence, it may order the offender to hand over the minor to the person entitled or to enable the enforcement of the enforceable decision concerning the minor.

(4) If the offender of the act from paragraph one of this article has voluntarily handed over the minor to the person entitled or has enabled the enforcement of the enforceable decision, the penalty may be remitted.

Domestic Violence

Article 191

(1) Whoever within a family community treats another person rudely, beats them or otherwise treats them painfully or humiliatingly, drives them from the common residence by threatening with a direct attack on life or body or restricts their freedom of movement, stalks them, forces them to work or refrain from work, or in any other way, by violently restricting their equal rights, brings them into a subordinate position in the community, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.

(2) The same punishment shall be imposed on whoever commits the act from the previous paragraph in any other permanent life community.

(3) If the act from paragraph one was committed against a person with whom the offender lived in a family or other permanent community that has dissolved, but the act is connected with that community, the offender shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

Neglect of a Minor and Cruel Treatment

Article 192

(1) Parents, a guardian, a foster parent or another person who seriously violates their duties towards a minor shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) Parents, a guardian, a foster parent or another person who forces a minor to excessive work or to work that is not appropriate to their age or to refrain from work, or out of greed leads them to begging or other acts that are harmful to their development, or treats them cruelly or tortures them, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.

Violation of Family Obligations

Article 193

(1) Whoever seriously neglects family obligations they have under the law and thereby leaves a family member who cannot take care of themselves in a difficult position, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.

(2) If the court imposes a suspended sentence, it may order the offender to regularly fulfill their duties of care, upbringing and maintenance.

Non-payment of Maintenance

Article 194

(1) Whoever does not provide maintenance for a person whom they are obliged to maintain by law and for whom the amount of their maintenance obligation is determined by an enforceable title, although they would be able to, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) If due to the act from the previous paragraph the maintenance of the entitled person is endangered or could be endangered, or if the offender evades providing maintenance, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(3) If the court imposes a suspended sentence, it may order the offender of the act from paragraph one or two of this article to regularly pay maintenance, and may also order them to settle overdue maintenance or other awarded obligations arising from maintenance. Incest

## 195. člen

A person who has sexual intercourse with a minor blood relative in direct line or with a minor brother or sister shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Dvaindvajseto poglavje

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST EMPLOYMENT RELATIONSHIP AND SOCIAL SECURITY

Violation of fundamental rights of workers

196. člen

(1) Whoever fails to act in accordance with regulations on the conclusion of employment contracts and on termination of employment relationship, salary and other benefits from employment relationship, working time, breaks, rest, annual leave or absence from work, protection of women, youth and persons with disabilities, protection of workers due to pregnancy and parenthood, protection of older workers, prohibition of overtime or night work, or payment of prescribed contributions, and thereby deprives one or more workers or job seekers of the rights to which they are entitled, or limits them, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years and a fine.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph violated the rights of at least twenty workers or if the rights of one or more workers were violated over a period of at least two years, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years and a fine.

(3) The penalty from the first paragraph of this article shall be imposed on whoever conditions the conclusion of an employment contract on the requirement that a female worker will not become pregnant or give birth to a child during employment, or requires a statement from a female worker during employment that she will resign from employment or accept termination of employment by mutual agreement in such case.

Harassment in the workplace

197. člen

(1) Whoever at the workplace or in connection with work, through sexual harassment, psychological violence, bullying or unequal treatment, causes humiliation or intimidation to another employee, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph results in psychological, psychosomatic or physical illness or reduction of work performance of an employee, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Violation of rights in employment or unemployment

198. člen

(1) Whoever denies or limits someone's right to free employment under such conditions as determined by regulations, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever deliberately fails to act in accordance with regulations on the rights of unemployed persons and thereby deprives an unemployed person of a right to which they are entitled or limits it. Illegal employment

199. člen

(1) Whoever, contrary to regulations, employs two or more workers and fails to register them for appropriate insurance, or employs two or more foreigners or stateless persons without appropriate work permits, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Whoever consecutively or permanently or in significant numbers employs foreigners who are not citizens of a European Union member state and illegally reside on the territory of the Republic of Slovenia, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article was committed by employing workers who are not qualified to perform work with special powers or with the right to interfere with the physical or mental integrity of an individual, or under particularly exploitative working conditions, or by exploiting a victim of human trafficking, or by employing a minor, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Violation of rights to participate in management and violation of union rights

200. člen

(1) Whoever, by violating regulations or general acts, prevents or makes it impossible for workers to exercise the rights to participate in management, or abuses these rights or hinders their exercise, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever, by violating regulations or general acts, prevents or makes it impossible for workers to freely associate and engage in union activities, or hinders the exercise of union rights or takes over a union.

Endangering safety at work

201. člen

(1) Whoever, in a mine, factory, construction site or any other workplace, destroys, damages or removes safety devices and thereby causes danger to human life, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) A person who is responsible for safety and health at work in mines, factories, workshops, construction sites or other workplaces and fails to install safety devices or fails to ensure their proper condition or fails to ensure their operation when necessary, or otherwise fails to act in accordance with regulations or technical rules on safety measures and thereby causes danger to human life, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph of this article by imprisonment of up to five years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment of up to three years.

(5) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first or second paragraph by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment from one to eight years.

Violation of rights from social insurance

## 202. člen

Whoever does not act in accordance with regulations on social insurance and thereby deprives someone of a right that belongs to them, or limits it, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Abuse of rights from social insurance

203. člen

(1) Whoever feigns or causes themselves an illness or incapacity for work and thereby achieves that a right is granted to them on the basis of social insurance that would not belong to them, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Whoever submits false documents to the competent authority or makes a false statement and thereby deceives it to issue a decision on pension, disability benefit or cash social supplement, even though the conditions for issuing such a decision are not met, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to two years. Twenty-third chapter

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST PROPERTY

Theft

204. člen

(1) Whoever takes another's movable property from someone in order to unlawfully appropriate it, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) If the value of the stolen property is small and the perpetrator intended to appropriate property of such value, they shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(3) Prosecution for the offence from the previous paragraph shall be initiated upon proposal.

(4) If the perpetrator returned the stolen property to the injured party before learning that criminal proceedings have been initiated, the punishment may be remitted.

Grand theft

205. člen

(1) The perpetrator of theft from the first paragraph of the previous article shall be punished with imprisonment of up to five years:

- 1) if they committed the theft by breaking in, intruding or otherwise overcoming major obstacles to enter a closed building, room, safe, cabinet or other closed spaces;
- 2) if the theft was committed by two or more persons who joined together to steal;
- 3) if they committed the theft in a particularly bold manner;
- 4) if they had on them some weapon or dangerous instrument for attack or defence;
- 5) if they committed the theft during a fire, flood or similar natural disaster;
- 6) if they committed the theft by exploiting the helplessness or misfortune of another.
  - (2) The perpetrator of theft shall be punished equally if the stolen property is of special cultural significance or a natural treasure or if the stolen property is of great value and the perpetrator intended to appropriate such property or property of such value.
  - (3) If property of special cultural significance or property of great value was obtained by the offence from the first paragraph of this article and the perpetrator intended to appropriate such property or property of such value or if the offence from the second paragraph of this article was committed in a criminal association, they shall be punished with imprisonment from one to eight years.

Robbery

206. člen

(1) Whoever takes another's movable property in order to unlawfully appropriate it by using force against a person or threatening them with an immediate attack on life or body, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) If the robbery was committed by two or more persons who joined together to rob, or if the value of the stolen property is great and the perpetrator intended to appropriate property of such value, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(3) If the offence from the first or second paragraph of this article was committed in a criminal association, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

Robbery theft

207. člen

(1) Whoever is caught in the act of theft and therefore, in order to keep the stolen property, uses force against someone or threatens them with an immediate attack on life or body, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) If the value of the stolen property is great and the perpetrator intended to appropriate property of such value, they shall be punished with imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

Misappropriation

208. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully appropriates another's movable property that was entrusted to them, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) If the value of the misappropriated property is small and the perpetrator intended to appropriate property of such value, they shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

(3) If the offence from the first paragraph of this article is committed by a guardian, they shall be punished with imprisonment of up to three years.

(4) If the misappropriated property is of special cultural significance or a natural treasure or if the misappropriated property is of great value and the perpetrator intended to appropriate such property or property of such value, they shall be punished with imprisonment of up to five years.

(5) Whoever unlawfully appropriates another's movable property that they found or came upon by chance, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(6) Prosecution for the offence from the first, second and fifth paragraphs of this article shall be initiated upon proposal.

Embezzlement and unauthorized use of another's property

209. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully appropriates money, movable property or another part of another's property that was entrusted to them in connection with employment or in performing economic, financial or business activity or in performing the duties of a guardian or was left to them as an official person in service, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) If an official person commits the offence from the previous paragraph against another's property that is accessible to them during the search of a dwelling, premises or persons, during enforcement in judicial or administrative proceedings or in connection with tasks of protecting persons or property, they shall be punished with imprisonment of up to five years.

(3) If the offence from the first paragraph of this article concerns property of small value and the perpetrator intended to appropriate such value, they shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) If the offence from the first or second paragraph of this article concerns property of great value and the perpetrator intended to obtain such pecuniary benefit, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from one to eight years.

(5) If the perpetrator unauthorized uses the entrusted or accessible property from the first or second paragraph of this article, they shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to three years.

Taking of a motor vehicle

210. člen

(1) Whoever takes another's motor vehicle with the intention of unlawfully using it for driving, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) Attempt is punishable.

(3) If the perpetrator of the criminal offence from the first paragraph of this article destroys the motor vehicle, renders it unusable, or abandons it in an unknown location, they shall be punished with imprisonment of up to three years.

Fraud

211. člen

(1) Whoever, in order to obtain for themselves or another an unlawful pecuniary benefit, induces someone into error by false representation or concealment of factual circumstances or leaves them in error and thereby induces them to do or omit something to the detriment of their own or another's property, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) Whoever with the intention from the previous paragraph of this article in insurance upon conclusion of a contract provides false information or conceals important information, concludes prohibited double insurance or concludes an insurance contract after the insured event or damage has already occurred, or falsely represents the damage event, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to one year.

(3) If the fraud was committed by two or more persons who joined together to commit fraud, or if the perpetrator caused great pecuniary damage by the offence from the first paragraph of this article, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from one to eight years.

(4) If the offence from the first or third paragraph of this article was committed in a criminal association, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from one to ten years.

(5) If the offence from the first paragraph of this article caused small pecuniary damage and the perpetrator intended to obtain a small pecuniary benefit, they shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(6) Whoever, in order to damage another, induces someone into error by false representation or concealment of factual circumstances or leaves them in error and thereby induces them to do or omit something to the detriment of their own or another's property, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(7) Prosecution for the offence from the fifth and sixth paragraphs of this article shall be initiated upon proposal.

Organizing money chains, illegal organization of games of chance and illegal fixing of results of sports competitions

212. člen

(1) Whoever organizes, participates or assists in organizing or carrying out money chains, in which participants pay certain monetary amounts to organizers or other participants who joined the game or activity before them and expect payment of certain monetary amounts from participants who are to join such game or activity after them, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) Equally shall be punished whoever, in order to obtain for themselves or another a greater unlawful pecuniary benefit, organizes, participates or assists in organizing games of chance for which no permission or concession was issued by the competent authority.

(3) Equally as in the first paragraph of this article shall be punished a person who, in order to obtain for themselves or another a pecuniary or some other benefit, agrees to an unauthorized change of the result or course of an official sports competition or does or omits something for such a change.

(4) If by the offences from the first or third paragraph of this article they themselves or another obtained a greater pecuniary benefit or caused another greater pecuniary damage, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment of up to five years.

(5) If by the offences from the first, second or third paragraph of this article they themselves or another obtained a great pecuniary benefit or caused another great pecuniary damage, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from one to eight years.

Extortion

213. člen

(1) Whoever, in order to obtain for themselves or another an unlawful pecuniary benefit, by force or serious threat compels someone to do or omit something to the detriment of their own or another's property, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) Equally shall be punished whoever, in order to obtain for themselves or another an unlawful pecuniary benefit, threatens someone that they will reveal something about them or their relatives that would harm their honour or good name, and thereby compels them to do or omit something to the detriment of their own or another's property.

(3) If the offence from the first or second paragraph of this article is committed by two or more persons or if it is committed using a weapon or dangerous instrument or in a particularly brutal and humiliating manner, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from one to eight years.

(4) If the offence from the previous paragraphs was committed in a criminal association, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from one to ten years.

Usury

## 214. člen

Whoever accepts or secures for themselves or for another person a manifestly disproportionate property benefit for a service rendered to someone by exploiting their poor financial situation, severe housing conditions, distress, insufficient experience or recklessness, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years and a fine.

Breach of Trust

215. člen

(1) Whoever represents the property interests of any person or manages their property, and knowingly fails to fulfill their obligation to act in their interest or acts to their detriment, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the perpetrator in committing the act from the preceding paragraph abuses the authorization to obtain a property benefit for themselves or for another person, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(3) If the act from the second paragraph of this Article is committed by an attorney, notary, guardian, executor or bankruptcy administrator, or if the perpetrator obtains a large property benefit, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Abuse of Enforcement

216. člen

(1) Whoever in enforcement unlawfully collects more than someone is obliged to pay, or appropriates a mistaken overpayment of a debt, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever in enforcement defrauds the creditor of their claim by agreement with auction participants.

(3) Attempt is punishable.

Concealment

217. člen

(1) Whoever purchases, receives in pledge, otherwise acquires, conceals or disposes of movable or immovable property, knowing that it was obtained through a criminal offense, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) Whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph, but should and could have known that the property was obtained through a criminal offense, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article was committed by two or more persons who have joined together for the purpose of concealment, or if the value of the property from the first or second paragraph of this Article is greater, or if the property is of special cultural significance or a natural value, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment of up to three years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment of up to two years.

(4) If the concealed property was obtained through a criminal offense for which the perpetrator is prosecuted by private action or motion, the perpetrator of the acts from the first and second paragraphs of this Article shall be prosecuted by private action or motion respectively.

(5) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article was committed in a criminal organization for the execution of criminal offenses, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years. Unlawful Possession and Trade in Items of Special Cultural Significance or Natural Values

218. člen

(1) Whoever takes abroad without authorization from the competent authority, or brings from abroad contrary to international law, an item of special cultural significance or a natural value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever markets or otherwise offers for sale items from the preceding paragraph, knowing that they were obtained unlawfully, or whoever unlawfully possesses an item of special cultural significance or a natural value.

(3) If the item from the first or second paragraph of this Article is of great or exceptional cultural significance, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Damage, Removal or Destruction of Items of Special Cultural Significance or Natural Values

219. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully damages, removes or destroys an item of special cultural significance, a natural value or other protected natural wealth, or an item that is a public good, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) If the damaged or destroyed item is a cultural monument or natural value of great or exceptional importance for the Republic of Slovenia, or if the damage caused is great, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to eight years.

Damage to Another's Property

220. člen

(1) Whoever damages, destroys or renders unusable another's property, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) If the damage caused is great, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(3) Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this Article shall be initiated on motion.

Attack on Information System

221. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully enters or intrudes into an information system, or whoever unlawfully intercepts data during a non-public transmission to or from an information system, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) Whoever unlawfully uses, modifies, copies, transmits, destroys data in an information system, or unlawfully enters any data into an information system, obstructs transmission or access to data, or obstructs the operation of an information system, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(3) If by the act from the preceding paragraph using the devices from the third paragraph of Article 306 of this Code the operation was obstructed or three or more information systems were otherwise affected, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to four years.

(4) If by the act from the second paragraph of this Article great damage was caused, or if the act was committed in a criminal organization or against an information system of a critical infrastructure operator or against an information system of an obligated party under the law governing information security, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.

Arson

222. člen

(1) Whoever sets fire to another's house or other building intended for residence, economic building or business building, building or other immovable property in public use, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(2) If the items from the preceding paragraph are the property of the person who set the fire, but they acted from malicious or other base motives, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(3) If the act from the first paragraph of this Article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(4) If the act from the first paragraph of this Article was committed by two or more persons, or if the burned item is of special cultural significance, a natural value or of great value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Damaging Another's Rights

223. člen

(1) Whoever, in order to prevent someone from asserting a real right, alienates, destroys, damages or takes their own property on which that person has a pledge right or a usufruct right, and thereby causes them damage, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever, in order to prevent payment to a creditor, during forced execution destroys, damages, alienates or hides parts of their property and thereby causes damage to the creditor.

(3) Prosecution for the act from the first and second paragraphs of this Article shall be initiated on motion.

Prosecution When the Perpetrator is in a Close Relationship with the Injured Party

## Article 224

For criminal offenses under Articles 204, 205, first, second, fourth and fifth paragraphs of 208, 210, 211, first and second paragraphs of 215 and 220 of this Code, which have been committed against a spouse or a person with whom one lives in an extramarital partnership, or a partner from a registered same-sex partnership, a blood relative in the direct line, a brother or sister or other blood relative in the collateral line up to and including the third degree, a relative by marriage up to and including the second degree, an adopter or adoptee, a foster parent or foster child, or against other persons with whom the perpetrator lives in a common household, prosecution shall be initiated by private action.

Chapter Twenty-Four

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST THE ECONOMY

Unlawful Restriction of Competition

Article 225

(1) Whoever, in the course of economic activity, in violation of regulations governing the protection of competition, infringes the prohibition of restrictive agreements between undertakings, abuses a dominant position of one or more undertakings, or creates a prohibited concentration of undertakings, thereby preventing or significantly impeding or distorting competition in the Republic of Slovenia or in the market of the European Union or a substantial part thereof, or significantly affecting trade between Member States, resulting in a large pecuniary benefit for that undertaking or those undertakings or a large pecuniary loss for another undertaking, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) A perpetrator of a criminal offense of violation of the prohibition of restrictive agreements from the preceding paragraph, who reported the act before it was discovered or before learning that it was discovered and cooperated in its investigation and elimination of consequences and did not coerce others to participate in the restriction of competition, nor coerce them to continue to participate in it, may have the sentence remitted.

Damaging Creditors by Fraud or Dishonest Business Conduct

Article 226

(1) Whoever knows that they themselves or another person as debtor is insolvent, yet prevents or reduces the possibility of settling obligations to creditors by:

- 1. concealing, damaging, removing, fictitiously, gratuitously or for an unreasonably low price alienating or destroying property or a part thereof;
- 2. concluding a fictitious debt contract or acknowledging a false claim;
- 3. concealing, destroying, altering or keeping business books, documents or reports in such a way that the actual financial condition, solvency or capital adequacy of the debtor cannot be determined in a timely or correct manner, or failing to keep business books, documents or reports at all;
- 4. in another fraudulent manner fictitiously or actually worsening their financial condition or the financial condition of another debtor,

shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

- (2) Whoever knows that they themselves or another person as debtor is insolvent, yet fictitiously or actually worsens their financial condition or the financial condition of another debtor by:
- 1. wastefully spending resources, incurring disproportionate debt, failing to timely secure and enforce claims or concluding speculative or harmful transactions;
- 2. unnecessarily transferring to other persons, encumbering or otherwise reducing the value of their property or the property or enterprise that they manage;
- 3. failing to fulfill legal obligations upon the occurrence of insolvency or violating fundamental rules in insolvency proceedings;
- 4. otherwise manifestly violating their duties in conducting business activity or financial operations,

shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

- (3) If the acts under the first or second paragraph of this Article resulted in a large pecuniary loss, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

Giving Preference to Creditors

Article 227

(1) Whoever knows that they themselves or another person as debtor is insolvent, yet pays a debt or otherwise intentionally places any creditor in a more favorable position and thereby causes pecuniary loss to other creditors, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) If the act under the preceding paragraph caused a large pecuniary loss, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

Business Fraud

Article 228

(1) Whoever, in the course of economic activity, in concluding or performing a contract or transaction, deceives another by representing that obligations will be fulfilled, or by concealing that obligations will not or cannot be fulfilled, and through partial or complete non-performance of obligations thereby gains a pecuniary benefit or causes pecuniary loss to the other party or someone else, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) If the act under the preceding paragraph resulted in a large pecuniary benefit or a large pecuniary loss, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(3) If the act under the first paragraph of this Article resulted in a small pecuniary benefit or a small pecuniary loss, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Fraud Against the European Union

Article 229

(1) Whoever avoids expenditure by using or submitting false, incorrect or incomplete statements or documents or failing to disclose information and thereby causes loss to the general budget of the European Union, a budget administered by the European Union, or a budget administered on behalf of the European Union, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever misappropriates, unlawfully retains or inappropriately uses resources from the general budget of the European Union, a budget administered by the European Union, or a budget administered on behalf of the European Union.

(3) The same punishment shall apply to whoever obtains resources from the budgets under the first and second paragraphs of this Article by using or submitting false, incorrect or incomplete statements or documents or failing to disclose information.

(4) If the act under the preceding paragraphs resulted in a large pecuniary benefit or caused a large pecuniary loss, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(5) The penalties under the preceding paragraphs of this Article shall apply to directors of undertakings or other persons authorized to make decisions or supervise in undertakings, if they enable or fail to prevent criminal offenses by perpetrators from the preceding paragraphs who are subordinate to them and act on behalf of the undertaking.

Deception in Obtaining and Using Loans or Benefits

Article 230

(1) Whoever obtains for themselves or for another a loan, investment resources, a subsidy or any other benefit for conducting economic activity, even though they do not meet the required conditions, by submitting to the lender or to another person competent to grant or approve such a benefit false or incomplete information about financial condition, balance sheets, profit or loss or other information relevant to the approval of the loan or benefit, or by concealing such information, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) Whoever deceives the person who granted or approved the loan or benefit under the preceding paragraph regarding the purpose of use or conceals from them that they used them for purposes other than those approved or agreed upon, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to two years.

Deception in Trading Securities

Article 231

(1) Whoever, for the purpose of trading in shares, other securities or other financial instruments, falsely represents the financial condition, information about profit or loss, or other information in a prospectus, in the publication of an annual report or in another manner that materially affects their value, and thereby induces one or more persons to purchase, sell or otherwise transact in them, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) If the act under the preceding paragraph involved securities or other financial instruments of high value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Deception of Buyers

Article 232

(1) Whoever, in order to deceive buyers, distributes on a large scale products with a label containing information that does not correspond to the content, type, origin or quality of the goods, or distributes products that are not as heavy or of such quality as is normally assumed, or distributes products without a label about the content, type, origin, quality or duration of the product, if such a label is prescribed, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever concludes contracts with false statements about the delivery deadline or manner of performance of obligations as an essential component of the contract.

(3) Whoever, in order to deceive buyers or users of services, falsely announces that the price of goods has been reduced or that goods are being sold at a discount or that prices will be increased or uses any other false advertisement, shall be punished by a fine.

Unauthorized Use of Another's Mark or Design

Article 233

(1) Whoever, in business operations, unlawfully uses another's business name, trademark, geographical indication or other special designation for goods or services or uses an essential part of that designation as their own business name, trademark or other sign for designating goods or services, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever, in business operations, unlawfully uses another's design.

(3) Objects under the first and second paragraphs of this Article and tools and devices used for their manufacture shall be seized.

Unauthorized Use of Another's Invention or Topography

Article 234

(1) Whoever, in business operations, unlawfully uses another's invention protected by a patent or supplementary protection certificate, or a registered topography of a semiconductor integrated circuit, or a new plant variety protected by plant breeders' rights, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) Products manufactured based on the unauthorized use under the preceding paragraph shall be seized.

Forgery or Destruction of Business Documents

Article 235

(1) Whoever, in business books, files or other business documents and records that must be kept according to law or pursuant to other regulations issued on the basis of law and are important for business transactions with other legal or natural persons or are intended for decisions in connection with economic or financial activity or as a basis for tax control, enters false information or fails to enter any material information or with their signature certifies such a book, document or file with false content or enables the preparation of a book, document or file with false content, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever uses a false business book, document or file as genuine or whoever destroys, conceals, considerably damages or otherwise renders unusable business books, documents or files under the preceding paragraph.

(3) The attempt of acts under the first and second paragraphs of this Article is punishable.

Disclosure and Unauthorized Obtaining of Business Secrets

Article 236

(1) Whoever unlawfully, in violation of their duties regarding the protection of business secrets, communicates or hands over to someone information that constitutes a business secret, or otherwise enables them to obtain it, or collects it with the intent to hand it over to an unauthorized person, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever, with the intent to use it unlawfully, unlawfully obtains information protected as a business secret.

(3) If the information under the first or second paragraph of this Article is particularly important, if someone hands over such information so that someone takes it abroad, or if the act was committed out of greed, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(4) If the act under the first or third paragraph of this Article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(5) Business secrets shall mean documents and information that have been declared as industrial, banking or other business secrets by law, statute, rules or other general act or order of a competent body or other authorized person and are so important that their disclosure has manifestly resulted or could result in serious harmful consequences.

Abuse of Information System

Article 237

(1) Whoever, in business operations, unlawfully enters or intrudes into an information system or unlawfully uses it by using, modifying, copying, transmitting, destroying or entering any data into the information system, obstructs transmission or access to data or obstructs the operation of the information system or unlawfully intercepts data during non-public transmission to an information system, in order to gain for themselves or for another an unlawful pecuniary benefit or to cause pecuniary loss to another, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) If the act under the preceding paragraph resulted in a large pecuniary benefit or caused a large pecuniary loss and the perpetrator intended to gain for themselves or for another such pecuniary benefit or to cause such pecuniary loss to another, or if it was committed in a criminal organization or against the information system of a critical infrastructure operator or against the information system of an obligated entity under the law governing information security, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.

Abuse of Insider Information

Article 238

(1) Whoever obtains inside information that could materially affect the price of a security or other financial instrument admitted to an organized market or other trading venue in the Republic of Slovenia or in at least one Member State of the European Union or for which an application for admission to such market has been submitted, regardless of whether the transaction was concluded on that market or outside it, in connection with their position with the issuer of the security or ownership interest in the capital of the issuer of the security, their employment or in the conduct of activities, and exploits it for themselves or for another for the direct or indirect acquisition or disposal of that security or other financial instrument or for cancellation or modification of a trading order, if the order was submitted before the person had inside information, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to a person who communicates inside information to an unauthorized person or on the basis of inside information recommends to a third person the direct or indirect acquisition or disposal of that security or other financial instrument.

(3) The same punishment as in the first paragraph shall apply to a person who unlawfully obtains inside information and exploits it for the direct or indirect acquisition or disposal of that security or other financial instrument for themselves or for another.

(4) The same punishment as in the first paragraph of this Article shall apply to a person who commits the act under the first paragraph of this Article with securities or other financial instruments on the over-the-counter market, if their price or value depends on or is affected by the price or value of financial instruments on the markets under the first paragraph of this Article.

(5) If the acts under the preceding paragraphs involved securities or other financial instruments of high value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Abuse of Financial Instruments Market

Article 239

(1) Whoever, in order to gain for themselves or for another an unlawful pecuniary benefit, abuses the financial instruments market through prohibited conduct, by:

- 1) concluding a transaction, issuing a trading order or otherwise acting in such a way as to give market participants a false or misleading impression regarding the supply, demand or price of a financial instrument or related commodity spot contract, or thereby secures for one or more related persons the price of one or more financial instruments or related commodity spot contracts at an abnormal or artificial level;
- 2) using fictitious means or other forms of fraudulent conduct in concluding a transaction or issuing a trading order;
- 3) spreading false or misleading information about financial instruments or related commodity spot contracts, spreading rumors and false and misleading news through the media, the internet or in another similar manner with the same purpose;
- 4) transmitting false or misleading information or providing false or misleading input data or otherwise acting so as to manipulate the calculation of a benchmark,

shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

- (2) If the act under the preceding paragraph resulted in a large pecuniary benefit or caused a large pecuniary loss and the perpetrator intended to gain for themselves or for another such pecuniary benefit or to cause such pecuniary loss to another, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Abuse of Position or Trust in Business Activity

Article 240

(1) Whoever, in conducting business activity, abuses their position or trust given, exceeds rights or omits duties that they have on the basis of law, other regulation, act of a legal entity or legal transaction regarding the disposal of another's property or benefits, their management or representation, and thereby gains for themselves or for another an unlawful pecuniary benefit or causes pecuniary loss, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) If the act under the preceding paragraph resulted in a large pecuniary benefit or caused a large pecuniary loss and the perpetrator intended to gain for themselves or for another such pecuniary benefit or to cause such pecuniary loss to another, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(3) If the perpetrator gained for themselves or for another a non-pecuniary benefit through the act under the first paragraph of this Article, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Unlawful Acceptance of Gifts

Article 241

(1) Whoever, in conducting business activity, demands or accepts for themselves or for another an unlawful reward, gift or any other benefit or promise or offer of such a benefit in order to neglect the interests of their organization or another natural person or to cause it loss for the purpose of obtaining or retaining business or other unlawful benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to six years and a fine.

(2) A perpetrator of the act under the preceding paragraph, who demands or accepts an unlawful reward, gift or any other benefit or promise or offer of such a benefit for themselves or for another as a quid pro quo for obtaining or retaining business or other benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years and a fine.

(3) A perpetrator of the act under the first paragraph of this Article, who after concluding a transaction or providing a service or obtaining another unlawful benefit demands or accepts for themselves or for another an unlawful reward, gift or any other benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to four years and a fine.

(4) The accepted reward, gift or any other benefit shall be seized.

Unlawful Giving of Gifts

Article 242

(1) Whoever promises, offers or gives to a person conducting business activity an unlawful reward, gift or any other benefit for them or for another in order to gain for themselves or for another any unjustified advantage in obtaining or retaining business or other unlawful benefit under the first paragraph of Article 241, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to six years and a fine.

(2) Whoever promises, offers or gives to a person conducting business activity an unlawful reward, gift or any other benefit for them or for another as a quid pro quo for obtaining or retaining business or other benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to four years and a fine.

(3) A perpetrator under the preceding paragraphs, who gave an unlawful reward, gift or any other benefit upon demand, yet reported the act before it was discovered or before learning that it was discovered, may have the sentence remitted if this does not conflict with the rules of international law.

(4) The given reward, gift or any other benefit shall be seized, and in the case under the preceding paragraph may be returned to the person who gave it.

Counterfeiting of Money

Article 243

(1) Whoever counterfeits money in order to put it into circulation as genuine, or whoever alters genuine money in order to put it into circulation, or whoever puts such counterfeit money into circulation, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever imports, exports, transports, receives or otherwise procures counterfeit money in order to put it into circulation as genuine.

(3) The same punishment shall apply to whoever manufactures money with lawful devices or materials of the body competent for issuing money in violation of the rights or conditions under which competent bodies may issue money.

(4) If the act under the first or second paragraph of this Article involves a large quantity or large pecuniary value of counterfeit money, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(5) Whoever puts into circulation counterfeit money that they received as genuine, or whoever knows that money has been counterfeited or that such money has been put into circulation yet fails to report it, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

(6) Counterfeit money shall be seized.

(7) Money means coined or paper money that is in circulation or is intended for circulation on the basis of law in the Republic of Slovenia or another state.

Counterfeiting and Use of Counterfeit Stamps or Securities

Article 244

(1) Whoever counterfeits stamps or postage stamps or other stamps, or whoever alters any such genuine stamp in order to use it as genuine or to give it to another for use, or whoever uses counterfeit stamps as genuine or procures them with such intent, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) Whoever counterfeits securities or whoever alters any such genuine security in order to use it as genuine or to give it to another for use, or whoever uses such counterfeit securities as genuine or procures them with such intent, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(3) If the act under the preceding paragraphs involves a large quantity of stamps or securities, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(4) Whoever removes a mark with which stamps under the first paragraph of this Article are cancelled, or whoever otherwise attempts to make these stamps appear as if they had not been used, or whoever uses such already used stamps again or sells them as if they were valid, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(5) Counterfeit stamps and counterfeit securities shall be seized.

(6) Stamps under this Code mean stamps and other stamps issued and in circulation on the basis of the law of the Republic of Slovenia, as well as foreign stamps.

Money Laundering

Article 245

(1) Whoever accepts, exchanges, stores, disposes of, uses in economic activity or in another manner prescribed by the law on the prevention of money laundering, money or property that they know was obtained through a criminal offense, thereby concealing or attempting to conceal its origin through laundering, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever commits the act under the preceding paragraph, and is at the same time the perpetrator or participant in the criminal offense through which the money or property under the preceding paragraph was obtained.

(3) If the money or property under the first or second paragraph of this Article is of high value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to eight years and a fine.

(4) If the act under the preceding paragraphs was committed in a criminal organization for the execution of such acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine.

(5) Whoever should have and could have known that the money or property was obtained through a criminal offense, yet commits the act under the first or third paragraph of this Article, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(6) Money and property under the preceding paragraphs shall be seized.

Abuse of Non-Cash Payment Instrument

Article 246

(1) Whoever, by abusing a check, credit or payment card or other non-cash payment instrument that they are authorized to use, obliges the bank or other issuer to pay an amount, contrary to the agreement on the use of that instrument, which they know has no coverage in the account, and thereby gains a pecuniary benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) Whoever uses a check, credit or payment card or other non-cash payment instrument in its original form that they are not authorized to use and was obtained in an unlawful manner, and thereby gains for themselves or for another an unlawful pecuniary benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(3) Whoever, with the intent of unlawful use, possesses, purchases or otherwise procures for themselves or for another, transfers, imports, exports, sells or otherwise distributes a non-cash payment instrument obtained in an unlawful manner, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) If the act under the first, second or third paragraph of this Article resulted in a larger pecuniary benefit, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(5) If the act under the first, second or third paragraph of this Article resulted in a large pecuniary benefit or if it was committed in a criminal organization, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(6) Prosecution for the act under the first paragraph of this Article shall be initiated upon application.

Use of Counterfeit Non-Cash Payment Instrument

Article 247

(1) Whoever installs on an ATM or card payment machine a device for copying data from payment, credit or other cards or other non-cash payment instruments or obtains their data for themselves or for another on the entire internet or in another manner or counterfeits a non-cash payment instrument, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever uses a counterfeit payment, credit or other card or other non-cash payment instrument or their data and thereby gains a pecuniary benefit.

(3) Whoever, with the intent of unlawful use, possesses, purchases, transfers, imports, exports, sells or otherwise distributes a counterfeit non-cash payment instrument or its data, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) If the act under the first, second or third paragraph of this Article resulted in a large pecuniary benefit or if it was committed in a criminal organization, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

Manufacture, Acquisition and Disposal of Counterfeiting Devices

Article 248

(1) Whoever manufactures, stores, transfers, procures, sells or gives for use devices for counterfeiting money, stamps, securities or protective elements intended for protection against counterfeiting, or a device for copying the record of a credit, payment or other card or other non-cash payment instrument, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) Devices for counterfeiting shall be seized.

(3) The attempt of the criminal offense under the first paragraph of this Article is punishable.

Tax Evasion

Article 249

(1) Whoever, through one or more acts, in order that they themselves or another completely or partially evade the payment of taxes, contributions or other prescribed obligations of natural or legal persons or unlawfully obtain a completely or partially refunded tax in the Republic of Slovenia or other Member States of the European Union, gives false information about income obtained, costs, items, goods or other circumstances that affect the determination of taxes and other prescribed obligations, or otherwise deceives the body competent for assessment or supervision of calculation and payment of these obligations, and the total amount of unsettled obligations or obligations that they evaded, or the tax that was unlawfully refunded to them, regardless of the type of obligation or tax in a period of no more than twelve consecutive months, reaches a large pecuniary value, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever, with the intent under the preceding paragraph, once or several times fails to report income obtained or other circumstances that affect the determination of taxes, contributions or other prescribed obligations of natural or legal persons, when reporting is mandatory, and the total amount of unsettled obligations or obligations that they evaded, regardless of the type of obligation in a period of no more than twelve consecutive months, reaches a large pecuniary value.

(3) Whoever, with the intent to prevent the determination of the actual tax obligation, upon the request of the competent tax authority fails to provide information, fails to keep or submit business books and records that they are obliged to keep, or the books and records are substantively incorrect, or fails to provide explanations in connection with the subject of tax supervision or obstructs tax supervision, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(4) If the act under the first or second paragraph of this Article was committed in a criminal organization, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to twelve years.

Smuggling

Article 250

(1) Whoever transports, while evading customs control measures, across the customs line of the European Union goods of large pecuniary value, or transports them using force or threat, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years and a fine.

(2) Whoever engages in transporting goods into the customs territory of the European Union while evading customs control measures, transports it within it, provides hiding places or warehouses, offers it or achieves its sale, when the goods together reach a larger pecuniary value, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years and a fine.

(3) The penalty under the preceding paragraph shall apply to an official who, by abusing their official position or rights, enables smuggling of goods into the customs territory of the European Union or transportation within it.

(4) If the perpetrator through the acts under the second or third paragraph of this Article gains for themselves or for another a large pecuniary benefit or causes danger to human life or health or supports terrorist activity or commits such acts as a member of a criminal organization, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years and a fine.

(5) Whoever procures or collects smuggled goods of large pecuniary value for transport into the customs territory of the European Union, provides counterfeit documents or transport to and within the customs territory of the European Union or otherwise organizes concealment, storage or sale of smuggled goods, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to twelve years and a fine.

(6) The preceding paragraphs shall also apply to criminal offenses committed abroad, if the state in which they were committed has, like the Republic of Slovenia, accepted a common international legal obligation to prevent such criminal offenses regardless of where they were committed, and has correspondingly defined the acts in its law as criminal offenses.

(7) The value of goods under this Article shall be determined by their market value in the territory of the Republic of Slovenia.

(8) Smuggled goods shall be seized.

Chapter Twenty-Five

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST LEGAL TRANSACTIONS

Forgery of Documents

Article 251

(1) Whoever forges a document or alters a genuine document in order that such a document be used as genuine, or whoever uses a forged or altered document as genuine, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) The attempt is punishable.

(3) Whoever forges a public document, a will, a public or official book or any other book that must be kept on the basis of law, alters such a genuine document or whoever keeps such a forged or altered document for use or uses it as genuine, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Certification of False Content

Article 253

(2) The same punishment shall also apply to whoever uses a public document, record, book or business document under the preceding paragraph, even though they know that it is false.

Abuse of official position or official rights

257. člen

(1) An official person or public employee who abuses their position or exceeds the limits of official rights or fails to perform official duty and thereby obtains any non-property benefit for themselves or another person or causes damage to someone, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years. (2) If the perpetrator by the act from the preceding paragraph causes greater damage or more seriously violates the rights of another, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years. (3) An official person or public employee who abuses their official position or exceeds the limits of official rights or fails to perform official duty and thereby obtains unlawful property benefit for themselves or another person, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years. (4) The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall apply to the perpetrator from the preceding paragraph who exploits their position or influence for unlawful increase of their own property in greater value. Damage to public funds

(1) An official person, public employee or other authorized person of a user of public funds who, in ordering, obtaining, managing these funds or disposing of them, knowingly violates regulations, omits due supervision or in any other way causes or enables unlawful or inappropriate use of public funds, although they foresee or should and could have foreseen that greater property damage may thereby arise to public funds, and such damage indeed arises, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years and by a fine. Negligent work in service

## Article 258

An official or public employee who knowingly violates laws or other regulations, neglects their supervision duties or otherwise manifestly acts negligently in service, even though they foresee or should have and could have foreseen that this may result in a serious violation of another's rights or damage to public goods or pecuniary damage, and such violation or greater damage does occur, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to three years.

Forgery or destruction of official documents, books, files or archival material

Article 259

(1) An official who enters false data into an official document, book or file, or fails to enter any important data, or with their signature or seal certifies an official document, book or file with false content, or with their signature or official seal enables the creation of an official document, book or file with false content, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) An official who uses an official document, book or file with false content in service as genuine, or who destroys, conceals, considerably damages or otherwise renders unusable an official document, book or file, shall be punished in the same manner.

(3) Whoever unlawfully alienates, destroys or conceals archival material, or renders it unusable, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to three years.

Disclosure of classified information

Article 260

(1) An official or other person who, contrary to their duty to protect classified information, communicates or hands over classified information to someone or otherwise enables them to obtain it, or collects such information in order to hand it over to an unauthorized person, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) Whoever unlawfully obtains classified information in order to use it without authorization, as well as whoever publicly discloses such information without permission, shall be punished in the same manner.

(3) A person who fulfills the elements of the criminal offense from the first paragraph of this article shall not be punished if the classified information reveals an unlawful interference with human rights or fundamental freedoms, other constitutional or statutory rights, serious abuse of authority or powers, or other serious irregularities in the exercise of authority, public powers or performance of public service, and the act was not committed out of self-interest and does not endanger people's lives or have serious or irreparable harmful consequences for the security or legally protected interests of the Republic of Slovenia.

(4) Notwithstanding the provision of the second paragraph of this article, whoever publicly discloses, obtains, transmits or possesses classified information with the intention of disclosure to the public shall not be punished if, according to the circumstances of the case, the public interest in disclosing the classified information prevails over the public interest in maintaining its secrecy, and if the act does not directly endanger the life of one or more persons.

(5) If the act from the first paragraph of this article was committed out of self-interest or if the publication directly endangered people's lives or if the publication had serious or irreparable harmful consequences for the security or legally protected interests of the Republic of Slovenia, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to eight years.

(6) If the act from the first paragraph of this article was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

Taking bribes

Article 261

(1) An official or public employee who, for themselves or for someone else, demands or accepts a reward, gift or any other benefit or promise or offer of such benefit, in order to perform, within the scope of their official powers, an official act that they should not perform, or in order not to perform an act that they should or could perform, or to otherwise abuse their position, or whoever mediates in such bribery, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years and a fine.

(2) An official or public employee who, for themselves or for someone else, demands or accepts a reward, gift or any other benefit or promise or offer of such benefit, in order to perform, within the scope of their official powers, an official act that they should or could perform anyway, or in order not to perform an act that they should not perform anyway, or to otherwise use their position, or whoever mediates in such bribery of an official, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years and a fine.

(3) An official or public employee who, after performing or not performing an official act from the previous paragraphs, demands or accepts in connection therewith a reward, gift or any other benefit, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to four years and a fine.

(4) The accepted reward, gift and any other benefit shall be seized.

Giving bribes

Article 262

(1) Whoever promises, offers or gives a reward, gift or any other benefit to an official or public employee for them or for someone else, in order for them to perform, within the scope of their official powers, an official act that they should not perform, or in order not to perform an act that they should or could perform, or to otherwise abuse their position, or whoever mediates in such bribery, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to six years and a fine.

(2) Whoever promises, offers or gives a reward, gift or any other benefit to an official or public employee for them or for someone else, in order for them to perform, within the scope of their official powers, an official act that they should or could perform anyway, or in order not to perform an act that they should not perform anyway, or to otherwise use their position, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to four years and a fine.

(3) A perpetrator of the criminal offense from the previous paragraphs who gave a reward, gift or any other benefit at the request of an official or public employee, and reported the act before it was discovered or before they learned that it was discovered, may be exempted from punishment if this does not conflict with the rules of international law.

Accepting benefits for illegal mediation

Article 263

(1) Whoever, for themselves or for someone else, demands or accepts a reward, gift or any other benefit or promise or offer of such benefit, in order to exploit their position or their real or alleged influence and mediate for the performance or non-performance of any official act, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to four years and a fine.

(2) Whoever exploits their position or their real or alleged influence and mediates for the performance of an official act that should not be performed, or for the non-performance of an official act that should or could be performed, shall be punished in the same manner.

(3) If the perpetrator, for mediation from the previous paragraph, before or after the mediation, accepts for themselves or for someone else a reward, gift or any other benefit, they shall be punished by imprisonment from one to six years and a fine.

(4) The accepted reward, gift or any other benefit shall be seized.

Giving gifts for illegal mediation

Article 264

(1) Whoever promises, offers or gives to another person a reward, gift or any other benefit for them or for someone else, in order for them to exploit their position or their real or alleged influence and mediate for the performance or non-performance of any official act, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to four years and a fine.

(2) Whoever promises, offers or gives to another person a reward, gift or any other benefit for them or for someone else, in order for them to exploit their position or their real or alleged influence and mediate for the performance of an official act that should not be performed, or for the non-performance of an official act that should or could be performed, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to six years and a fine.

(3) A perpetrator of the criminal offense from the previous paragraphs who gave a reward, gift or any other benefit at the request of the person who illegally mediated, and reported the act before it was discovered or before they learned that it was discovered, may be exempted from punishment.

Chapter Twenty-Seven

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST MILITARY SERVICE

Failure to carry out an order and refusal of obedience

269. člen

(1) A military person who does not carry out an order of a superior military person in connection with service or refuses obedience, and as a result danger arises to the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment up to two years.

(2) A military person who resists a guard, watch, patrol, duty officer or other military person when, in the performance of their military duty, they ensure safety and order in a military unit, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to one year.

Resistance to a superior

270. člen

(1) A military person who, together with other military persons, resists an order of a superior military person in connection with service or refuses to carry it out or refuses to perform their duty, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.

(2) A military person who uses weapons in committing the act from the preceding paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(3) With the penalty from the preceding paragraph shall be punished whoever organized the act from paragraph one of this Article, as well as a military superior who participated in such an act. Refusal to receive or use weapons

## 271. člen

A military person who, contrary to regulations and without justified reason, refuses to receive weapons or refuses to use them according to an order or rules of service, shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.

False oral reports and false reports

## 272. člen

A military person who, in the performance of service, makes a false oral report or gives a false report or conceals in it any fact that should not be concealed, and thereby endangers the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to one year.

Unauthorized absence and desertion from the military

273. člen

(1) A military person who unauthorizedly leaves their unit or service during the performance of an important task or during heightened combat readiness of the unit, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment up to one year.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to a military person who knows of the circumstances from paragraph one of this Article but does not return from authorized absence from the unit or service, and a military person who unauthorizedly leaves their unit or service for more than ten days.

(3) A military person who hides in order to avoid service in the military shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.

(4) With the penalty from the preceding paragraph shall be punished a military person who leaves the country and remains abroad in order to avoid service in the military.

Mistreatment of a subordinate

274. člen

(1) A superior military person who, in service or in connection with service, mistreats a subordinate or treats them in a manner that violates their human dignity, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.

(2) If a superior military person commits the act from the preceding paragraph against several persons, they shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

Violation of regulations on guard duty

275. člen

(1) A military person who does not act in accordance with regulations on guard duty, patrol, duty service or other service for maintaining safety and order in a military unit, command or institution or for military protection of facilities or areas, and thereby causes danger to the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.

(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.

(3) If the acts from paragraph one or two of this Article result in serious bodily injury of one or more persons or great property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from paragraph one by imprisonment from one to five years, and for the act from paragraph two by imprisonment up to three years.

(4) If the acts from paragraph one or two of this Article result in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from paragraph one by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from paragraph two by imprisonment from one to eight years. Failure to take measures for the safety of people and property in a military unit

276. člen

(1) A commanding military person who does not take prescribed or ordered measures for the safety of the life and health of people entrusted to them, for the protection and proper maintenance of facilities, items and means for combat readiness, for the essential supply of the unit entrusted to them with food, equipment or means, or does not take action that must be taken to ensure that protective works are performed timely and properly, and thereby endangers the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.

(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.

(3) If the acts from paragraph one or two of this Article result in serious bodily injury of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from paragraph one by imprisonment from one to five years, and for the act from paragraph two by imprisonment up to three years.

(4) If the acts from paragraph one or two of this Article result in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from paragraph one by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from paragraph two by imprisonment from one to eight years. Failure to take protective measures during military exercises

277. člen

(1) A military person who, during an exercise, instruction or trials, does not take prescribed or ordered safety or precautionary measures and thereby endangers the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment up to three years.

(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment up to one year.

(3) If the acts from paragraph one or two of this Article result in serious bodily injury of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from paragraph one by imprisonment from one to five years, and for the act from paragraph two by imprisonment up to three years.

(4) If the acts from paragraph one or two of this Article result in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from paragraph one by imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from paragraph two by imprisonment from one to eight years. Liability for a criminal offense committed on the order of a superior

## 278. člen

A subordinate shall not be punished if they commit a criminal offense on the order or command of a superior military person and this order or command relates to military duty, unless it concerns a war crime or some other serious criminal offense, or if they knew that the execution of the order or command constitutes a criminal offense.

More lenient punishment due to provocation

## 279. člen

If the perpetrator of a criminal offense from Article 269, first paragraph of Article 270 or first paragraph of Article 273 of this code was provoked by unlawful or cruel treatment by a military person, they may be punished more leniently or the penalty may be remitted.

Osemindvajseto poglavje

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST JUSTICE

Failure to Report that a Criminal Offense is Being Prepared

280. člen

(1) Whoever knows that a criminal offense is being prepared, for which the law prescribes a penalty of three or more years of imprisonment, and does not report it at a time when it could still be prevented, and the act was attempted or completed, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph concerns a criminal offense for which the law prescribes a penalty of at least fifteen years of imprisonment or life imprisonment, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(3) Whoever fails to report that a criminal offense from the first paragraph of this article is being prepared shall not be punished if the perpetrator is a spouse, a person with whom they live in an extramarital relationship or registered same-sex partnership, a blood relative in a direct line, a brother or sister, an adoptive parent or adoptee. If any of the persons listed in this paragraph is not punished for failure to report from the first paragraph of this article, neither their spouse nor the person with whom they live in an extramarital relationship nor a partner from a registered same-sex partnership shall be punished for failure to report.

Failure to Report a Criminal Offense or Perpetrator

281. člen

(1) Whoever knows of a perpetrator of a criminal offense for which the law prescribes a penalty of at least fifteen years of imprisonment or life imprisonment, or whoever only knows that such an act was committed, and does not report it, although such a report is necessary for the perpetrator or the act to be discovered in time, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) An official who knowingly fails to report a criminal offense of which they learned in the performance of their duties, if the law prescribes a penalty of three or more years of imprisonment for it, and the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(3) Whoever fails to report shall not be punished if the perpetrator is a spouse, a person with whom they live in an extramarital relationship or registered same-sex partnership, a blood relative in a direct line, a brother or sister, an adoptive parent or adoptee, or if they are the defense counsel, physician or confessor of the perpetrator. Since any of the persons from this paragraph except the defense counsel, physician or confessor is not punished for failure to report from the first paragraph of this article, neither their spouse nor the person with whom they live in an extramarital relationship nor a partner from a registered same-sex partnership shall be punished for failure to report.

Assistance to Perpetrator After Commission of Criminal Offense

282. člen

(1) Whoever conceals the perpetrator of a criminal offense that is prosecuted ex officio, or helps them by concealing tools, traces or in any other way to avoid discovery, or whoever conceals a convict or does anything else to prevent the execution of a sentence or imposed security measure or to prevent the use of an educational measure of placement in an educational institution or reformatory, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) If it concerns assistance to the perpetrator of a criminal offense from the previous paragraph, for which a penalty of imprisonment of at least fifteen years or life imprisonment is prescribed, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.

(3) The penalty for the act from the first paragraph of this article may not be more severe either in type or in amount than the penalty prescribed for the criminal offense of the person whom the perpetrator helped.

(4) For the act from the first or second paragraph of this article, the perpetrator's spouse, a person with whom they live in an extramarital relationship or in a registered same-sex partnership, or a blood relative in a direct line, a brother or sister, an adoptive parent or adoptee shall not be punished. Since any of the persons from this paragraph is not punished for the criminal offense from the first or second paragraph, neither their spouse nor the person with whom they live in an extramarital relationship nor a partner from a registered same-sex partnership shall be punished for assisting the perpetrator in the commission of a criminal offense from the first or second paragraph of this article.

(5) If the prosecution of the perpetrator of a criminal offense begins upon a motion, the perpetrator of the criminal offense from the first paragraph of this article shall also be prosecuted upon a motion.

False Accusation

283. člen

(1) Whoever reports that someone has committed a criminal offense that is prosecuted ex officio, knowing that they did not commit it, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever plants evidence of a criminal offense or in any other way causes criminal proceedings to be initiated for a criminal offense for which the perpetrator is prosecuted ex officio, against someone who they know did not commit it.

(3) Whoever reports themselves as having committed a criminal offense that is prosecuted ex officio, although they did not commit it, shall be punished by a fine.

(4) The same punishment as in the previous paragraph applies to whoever reports that a criminal offense that is prosecuted ex officio was committed, although they know that no such act was committed, and thereby causes state authorities to begin taking action.

(5) If an official commits an act from the first or second paragraph of this article by abusing their official position, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

False Testimony

284. člen

(1) A witness, expert, appraiser, translator or interpreter who gives false testimony before a court, in misdemeanor proceedings, parliamentary inquiry, for determining violations of work obligations or other violations of work discipline, in other disciplinary or administrative proceedings, an expert or appraiser who gives a false written opinion, or a translator who gives a false written translation, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) The same punishment applies to a party who has given false testimony when proving by hearing parties in civil, non-contentious, enforcement or administrative proceedings, and the court or other competent authority based its decision in such proceedings on this testimony.

(3) For false testimony in criminal proceedings, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.

(4) If the act from the previous paragraph has particularly severe consequences for the accused, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(5) If the perpetrator voluntarily retracts their false testimony before a final decision is issued, they shall be punished by a fine, but the penalty may also be remitted.

Prevention of Evidence

285. člen

(1) Whoever, in order to prevent or hinder the presentation of evidence, conceals, destroys or damages someone else's document or other item proposed for evidence, or renders it partially or completely unusable, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever, with the intention from the previous paragraph, removes, destroys, damages, moves or relocates a boundary marker, surveying mark or any mark indicating ownership rights to real estate or the right to use water, or whoever with the same intention places such a mark incorrectly.

(3) Whoever, in order to prevent or hinder the presentation of evidence in criminal proceedings, submits evidence that they know to be false or forged, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.

Obstruction of Judicial and Other State Authorities

286. člen

(1) Whoever, with the intention of influencing testimony or the presentation of evidence in judicial or administrative proceedings or in parliamentary inquiry proceedings or in proceedings before the Commission for the Prevention of Corruption, or whoever with the intention of influencing the collection of information in pre-trial proceedings, uses physical force, threat or intimidation against another, or offers or gives them unlawful benefits, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever, with the intention of influencing the performance of official duties of officials in the judiciary, in detection and prosecution authorities in connection with criminal proceedings, uses physical force, threat or intimidation against an official.

Violation of Confidentiality of Proceedings

287. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully discloses what they learned in proceedings before the main hearing or at a hearing before a court, at an oral hearing in administrative proceedings or misdemeanor proceedings or parliamentary inquiry proceedings or in proceedings before the Commission for the Prevention of Corruption, which the law determines may not be published, or which was decided by a decision of the competent authority or court to remain secret, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) Whoever publishes personal data of a minor who is a participant in judicial, administrative or any other proceedings, or publishes other information based on which their identity could be identified, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to 3 years.

(3) Whoever discloses the identity or individual personal or other data that could lead to the disclosure of the identity of a protected witness, endangered person or person with a modified identity, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(4) The penalty from the previous paragraph applies to whoever discloses the identity or individual personal or other data that could lead to the disclosure of the identity of a protected whistleblower of corruption, if this disclosure causes more severe consequences for the whistleblower.

(5) If an official commits an act from the third or fourth paragraph of this article, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.

Unlawful, Biased and Unjust Adjudication

288. člen

(1) A judge who, in conducting judicial proceedings or issuing a judicial decision, knowingly violates the law or distorts the law in order to harm a party in the proceedings or give them an unjustified advantage, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) The same punishment applies to a judge who, with the intention from the previous paragraph of this article, bases a judicial decision on facts that they know do not exist or are falsely planted with false or inadmissible evidence.

Prevention of Return to Work

## 289. člen

Whoever knowingly fails to act in accordance with a final court decision by which it was decided on the return of a worker to work, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year. Violation of the prohibition on practicing a profession

## 290. člen

Whoever enables another person to practice a profession, activity or duty, even though they know that a security or protective measure prohibiting the practice of a profession has been imposed on them by a final court decision or that such a prohibition arose as a legal consequence of a conviction, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Escape of a person deprived of liberty

## 291. člen

Whoever escapes from prison or detention by force or by threatening to directly attack life or body, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Resistance of persons deprived of liberty

292. člen

(1) Whoever participates in a group of persons deprived of liberty who have gathered to free themselves by force or to jointly attack persons to whose supervision they are entrusted, or to force them by force or threat to directly use force to do or omit something that is contrary to their duty, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) The perpetrator of the act from the previous paragraph who has used force or threat shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Enabling escape of a person deprived of liberty

293. člen

(1) Whoever by force, threat, deception or in any other way enables the escape of someone who is serving a sentence in prison, or someone who is detained, or who is undergoing a security measure of mandatory psychiatric treatment and care in a health institution, or a juvenile who is in a correctional home, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed in an organized group, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Twenty-ninth chapter

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST PUBLIC ORDER AND PEACE

Criminal association

294. člen

(1) Whoever participates in a criminal association that has the purpose of committing a criminal offence for which a sentence of more than three years of imprisonment or life imprisonment may be imposed, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to five years.

(2) Whoever establishes or leads an association from the previous paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.

(3) The perpetrator of a criminal offence from the previous paragraphs who prevents the further commission of these acts or discloses information that is relevant for the investigation and proof of criminal offences already committed shall have the sentence for these acts mitigated in accordance with Article 51 of this Code. Agreement for a criminal offence

## 295. člen

Whoever agrees with someone that they will commit a criminal offense for which a sentence of five years imprisonment or a more severe penalty may be imposed, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.

Hooliganism

296. člen

(1) Whoever treats another person roughly, beats them or otherwise painfully or degradingly punishes them, drives them away by force or threat of immediate attack on life or body or restricts their freedom of movement, forces them to work or to refrain from work, or by otherwise violently restricting their equal rights places them in a subordinate position, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed by two or more persons, or if several persons have been seriously humiliated, or if the perpetrator lightly injures another person physically, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(3) The same punishment as in the previous paragraph shall apply to whoever causes violence or endangers the safety of others at sports events or in connection with such events.

Public Incitement to Hatred, Violence or Intolerance

297. člen

(1) Whoever publicly incites or stirs up hatred, violence or intolerance based on national, racial, religious or ethnic affiliation, sex, skin color, origin, property status, education, social position, political or other conviction, disability, sexual orientation or any other personal circumstance, and the act is committed in a manner that may endanger or disturb public order and peace, or by using threats, insults or offenses, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever, in the manner from the previous paragraph, publicly disseminates ideas about the superiority of one race over another or provides any assistance in racist activities or denies, diminishes the significance of, approves, justifies, ridicules or advocates genocide, the holocaust, crimes against humanity, war crimes, aggression or other criminal offenses against humanity as defined in the legal order of the Republic of Slovenia.

(3) If the act from the previous paragraphs is committed by publication in the media or on websites, the penalty from the first or second paragraph of this article shall also be imposed on the responsible editor or the person who replaced them, unless it was a live broadcast that could not be prevented or a publication on websites that allow users to publish content in real time or without prior supervision.

(4) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article is committed by coercion, rough treatment, endangering safety, desecration of ethnic, nationality, national or religious symbols, damaging property of others, desecration of monuments, memorials or graves, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(5) If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this article are committed by an official person by abuse of official position or rights, they shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.

(6) Means and objects with messages from the first and second paragraphs of this article, as well as tools intended for their production, reproduction and distribution, shall be confiscated or their use shall be appropriately prevented.

Participation in a Group that Commits a Criminal Offense

298. člen

(1) Whoever participates in a group that by joint action commits any violence against people, destroys or damages property of greater value, or attempts to commit such a criminal offense, shall be punished for mere participation by imprisonment for up to two years.

(2) If, during the action of the group from the previous paragraph, someone was killed or seriously physically injured, the group participant shall be punished for mere participation by imprisonment for up to three years.

(3) The leader of the group that committed the act from the first or second paragraph of this article shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Prevention of Official Act or Revenge against Official Person

299. člen

(1) Whoever by force or threat of immediate use of force prevents or attempts to prevent an official person from performing an official act that they intended to perform within the scope of their powers, or in the same manner forces them to perform an official act, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to two years.

(2) If the perpetrator, when committing the act from the first paragraph of this article, seriously provokes the official person, treats them roughly, or inflicts a light bodily injury on them, or threatens them with the use of a weapon or dangerous object or means, they shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(3) Whoever commits the act from the first or second paragraph of this article against an official person when they are performing police or state security tasks, pursuing the perpetrator of a criminal offense, or guarding someone who has been deprived of liberty, or performing acts of criminal prosecution, acts of administrative inspection supervision, conducting an investigation or adjudicating in criminal proceedings, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to five years.

(4) The same punishment as in the previous paragraph shall apply to whoever takes revenge against an official person who performs or has performed acts in misdemeanor or criminal prosecution proceedings, police tasks, acts of administrative inspection supervision, conducts or has conducted an investigation, or adjudicates or has adjudicated in criminal proceedings, for acts performed by them or another official person within the scope of their powers, in such a way that the life, body, personal safety or property of the official person or their close relatives is endangered.

(5) If the perpetrator of the act from the first to fourth paragraphs of this article was provoked by unlawful conduct of the official person, they shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to six months, but the penalty may be remitted.

Attack on Official Person when Performing Security Tasks

300. člen

(1) Whoever attacks or seriously threatens to attack an official person or another person whom they know is assisting the official person, when they are performing police or state security tasks or protecting public order, or a person performing tasks related to the execution of criminal sanctions, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to three years.

(2) If the perpetrator, when committing the act from the previous paragraph, threatens the official person or another person whom they know is assisting them with a weapon or dangerous object or means, or treats them roughly or lightly injures them physically, they shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(3) The penalty from the previous paragraph shall apply to whoever commits the act from the first paragraph of this article against two or more persons.

Participation in a Group that Prevents an Official Person from Performing an Official Act

301. člen

(1) Whoever participates in a group that by joint action prevents or attempts to prevent an official person from performing an official act or in the same manner forces them to perform an official act, shall be punished for mere participation by imprisonment from three months to two years.

(2) The leader of the group that committed the act from the previous paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to three years.

Incitement to Resistance

302. člen

(1) Whoever incites others to violent resistance to lawful decisions or measures of state authorities or against an official person performing an official act shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) If, due to the act from the previous paragraph, a decision or measure of a state authority was not executed or the execution was greatly impeded, or if this act was committed by a group leader, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

Removal or Damage of Official Seal or Sign

## 303. člen

Whoever removes or damages an official seal or sign that an authorized official person has used to secure objects or premises, or whoever enters such a premise without removing or damaging the seal or sign, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

Removal or destruction of official seal or official documents

304. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully takes, conceals, destroys or damages or otherwise renders unusable an official seal, official book, file or document that belongs to or is with a state authority, company, other legal entity or individual exercising public authority, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) Attempt is punishable.

False representation as an official or military person

305. člen

(1) Whoever, in order to obtain some benefit for themselves or another or to cause harm to someone, falsely represents themselves as an official or military person or unlawfully wears insignia of an official or military person, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever performs any act that only an official or military person is authorized to perform.

Manufacturing and obtaining weapons and devices intended for a criminal offense

306. člen

(1) Whoever manufactures or obtains or keeps weapons, explosives or devices with which they can be made, or poisons, knowing that they are intended for a criminal offense, or enables someone to obtain them, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) Whoever makes or hands over to someone a counterfeit key, lock pick or any other burglary device, even though they know it is intended for a criminal offense, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(3) The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall apply to whoever, with the intention of committing a criminal offense, possesses, manufactures, sells, makes available for use, imports, exports or otherwise provides devices for intrusion, unauthorized access or interference with an information system or part thereof or for unauthorized interference with data in an information system or for their interception.

Unauthorized production and trafficking of weapons or explosives

307. člen

(1) Whoever unlawfully assembles, manufactures, offers, sells, exchanges, delivers, imports, exports, brings into the country or takes out of it firearms, chemical, biological or nuclear weapons, ammunition or explosives or military weapons and military equipment, the trafficking, acquisition and possession of which are not permitted or are restricted for individuals, legal entities and entrepreneurs, or acts as an intermediary therein, or whoever unlawfully obtains or keeps such weapons, ammunition or explosives, except for firearms or ammunition for which a weapons permit can be issued, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph involves weapons, ammunition or explosives or any other warfare means in large quantity or value or poses a danger or if the act was committed in a criminal organization, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(3) If the act from the first paragraph of this article involves a single firearm or a small quantity of ammunition for such a weapon or if the perpetrator obtains or keeps firearms or ammunition for which a weapons permit can be issued with the intention of unauthorized sale, or if they keep it in large quantity or value, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall apply to whoever counterfeits or unlawfully destroys, removes or alters markings on firearms.

(5) Whoever unlawfully manufactures, obtains, offers, sells, exchanges, sends, delivers, imports, exports, brings into the country or takes out of it component or spare parts of weapons, ammunition, explosives, explosive devices and explosive weapons or military weapons and military equipment, substances, components, software or technology, knowing that they will be used for the production or operation of the objects from the preceding paragraphs, keeps them for this purpose or acts as an intermediary therein, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Prohibited crossing of border or territory of the state

308. člen

(1) Whoever crosses the state border of the Republic of Slovenia by force or enters its territory unlawfully while armed, shall be punished by imprisonment from three months to three years or by a fine.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to a foreigner who does not have permission to reside in the Republic of Slovenia if they stay on its territory in the manner from the preceding paragraph or resist lawful removal from it.

(3) Whoever engages in unlawfully bringing onto its territory foreigners who do not have permission to enter or reside in the Republic of Slovenia, transporting them across it or helping them hide or whoever unlawfully brings one or more such foreigners across the border or territory of the state, transports them across it or enables unlawful residence on it with the intention of obtaining pecuniary gain for themselves or for someone else, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years and a fine.

(4) The punishment from the preceding paragraph shall apply to an official person who enables a foreigner to unlawfully enter the territory of the Republic of Slovenia or to unlawfully reside on it.

(5) Whoever recruits or gathers people for illegal migration, procures for them forged documents or transportation or otherwise organizes illegal migration or organizes migration by deceiving competent authorities regarding the true purpose of entry to the territory of the Republic of Slovenia, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to ten years and a fine.

(6) If the perpetrator by acts from the third, fourth or fifth paragraph of this article obtains for themselves or someone else a disproportionate pecuniary gain or procures illegal labor force or exposes someone to danger to life or health or commits such acts as a member of a criminal organization, they shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years and a fine.

(7) The preceding paragraphs shall also apply to criminal offenses committed abroad if the state in which they were committed has, like the Republic of Slovenia, accepted a common international legal obligation to prevent such criminal offenses regardless of where they are committed and has correspondingly defined the acts in its law as criminal offenses. If the criminal offense was committed in the territory of the European Union, citizens of its member states shall not be considered foreigners in the application of the second, third, fourth and fifth paragraphs of this article.

(8) The means of transport used for transporting one or more persons who are crossing the border or territory of the state in a prohibited manner shall be confiscated if it is the perpetrator's property. If the means of transport is not the perpetrator's property, in addition to the cases from the second paragraph of Article 73 of this code, it shall also be confiscated if it is specially adapted, modified or adjusted for transporting such persons, or if the owner or person who handed over the means of transport knew or could have known that the vehicle would be used for such a purpose.

Abuse of distress and danger signals

309. člen

(1) Whoever abuses a distress signal or danger signal or unjustifiably calls for help or falsely reports that danger is imminent and thereby causes state authorities or other competent organizations to act without necessity or to unjustifiably use the resources of the protection, rescue and assistance system, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) If by the act from the preceding paragraph the work of judicial or other state authorities was prevented or obstructed and thereby the performance of an official act was prevented or greater pecuniary damage was caused, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Self-help

310. člen

(1) Whoever arbitrarily takes their own right or a right which they believe belongs to them, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

(2) Whoever arbitrarily takes their own right or a right which they believe belongs to them, by use of force or serious threat of attack on life or body, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(3) The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall apply to whoever commits the act from the preceding paragraph for another.

(4) Whoever by an act from the second paragraph of this article collects a debt for themselves or someone else, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(5) Prosecution for the act from the first paragraph of this article shall be initiated upon private action, and for the acts from the second and third paragraphs upon a motion.

Obstruction of religious ceremonies

## 311. člen

Whoever obstructs, prevents or disturbs a religious ceremony or whoever behaves offensively in a place designated for this purpose, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year. Obstruction of funerals and desecration of graves

312. člen

(1) Whoever obstructs, prevents or disturbs a funeral, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) The same punishment shall apply to whoever unlawfully digs up or tears open a grave or any other place where the deceased are buried, or otherwise seriously desecrates it.

(3) If the acts from the previous paragraphs are committed by two or more persons or if two or more graves have been desecrated, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Desecration of corpse

## 313. člen

Whoever unlawfully hides, removes, damages, destroys or otherwise desecrates a corpse, part of a corpse or mortal remains, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Thirtieth chapter

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST THE GENERAL SAFETY OF PEOPLE AND PROPERTY

Causing general danger

314. člen

(1) Whoever, by fire, flood, explosion, poison or poisonous gas, ionising radiation, motor power, electrical or any other energy or any other generally dangerous act or means or by omission of an act that should have been performed to ensure the general safety of people and property, causes danger to human life or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) Whoever, with explosives or other dangerous act or means, with the intent of extortion, intimidation, coercion to commit or omit some other act, revenge or obtaining property or non-property benefit for himself or someone else, plans, attempts or carries out a dangerous act in which the safety of people or property of greater value is or would be endangered, shall be punished by imprisonment of one to eight years.

(3) Whoever commits the act from the first paragraph by negligence, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons or great property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first and second paragraph by imprisonment of up to ten years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment of up to five years.

(5) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment of one to fifteen years, for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment of at least fifteen years, and for the act from the third paragraph by imprisonment of up to eight years.

Causing danger in construction activity

315. člen

(1) A person responsible for planning or supervising plans for the preparation or management of building or construction works, who acts contrary to regulations or generally recognised technical rules and thereby causes danger to human life or property of great value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons or great property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment of one to five years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment of up to three years.

(4) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph by imprisonment of one to twelve years, and for the act from the second paragraph by imprisonment of up to eight years.

Endangering the environment with noise or light

317. člen

(1) Whoever, by violating regulations, causes excessive noise or excessively strong lighting, which may cause more serious harm to human health, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Damaging or destroying public facilities

318. člen

(1) Whoever destroys, damages or removes electrical lines, gas pipelines, water pipelines, heating pipelines, oil pipelines, telecommunication facilities, submarine cables, sewage facilities and environmental protection facilities or other similar public facilities and thereby causes disruptions in the supply of the population or the economy, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph is committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Transport or carrying of explosives or dangerous substances contrary to regulations

## 319. člen

Whoever, contrary to regulations on traffic in explosives or easily flammable substances or other dangerous substances or dangerous waste, transports or hands over such substances for transport by public means of transport or carries them himself when using public means of transport, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

Abuse of telecommunication signals

## 320. člen

Whoever knowingly and without necessity sends out an internationally agreed signal for help or a signal that he is in danger, or whoever deceives by a telecommunication signal that safety is given, or whoever otherwise abuses an internationally agreed telecommunication signal, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year. Failure to avert danger

321. člen

(1) Whoever does not notify in time the competent authority or organisation or otherwise does not do everything necessary to avert fire, flood, explosion, traffic accident, ecological accident or any other danger to human life or excessive environmental pollution or property of greater value, although he could do so without danger to himself or someone else, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Whoever prevents someone by force, threat or deception from doing what is necessary to avert danger to human life, the environment or property of greater value, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

Refusal to cooperate in averting general danger

## Article 322

Whoever, contrary to an order of a competent authority or organization, without justified reason does not participate in averting a general danger or eliminating consequences, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to six months.

Chapter Thirty-One

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST PUBLIC TRAFFIC SAFETY

Causing a traffic accident by negligence

Article 323

(1) A traffic participant who, by violating regulations on road traffic safety through negligence, causes a traffic accident in which any person was seriously bodily injured, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment of up to eight years and prohibition from driving a motor vehicle.

(3) A perpetrator who did not have the right to drive the motor vehicle with which the criminal offense from the first or second paragraph of this article was committed, shall have that motor vehicle confiscated. A motor vehicle that is the property of another person shall be confiscated if that person enabled, allowed or permitted driving to the perpetrator, knowing or should have known that they did not have the right to drive. Dangerous driving in road traffic

Article 324

(1) A driver of a motor vehicle who in road traffic causes direct danger to the life or body of any person by:

1) driving under the influence of alcohol with more than 1.10 grams of alcohol per kilogram of blood or more than 0.52 milligrams of alcohol per liter of exhaled air or driving under the influence of prohibited drugs, psychoactive medicines or other psychoactive substances, or

2) exceeding the speed by more than 30 km/h from the permitted speed in a pedestrian zone, in a traffic calming zone or in a speed limit zone, or by more than 50 km/h from the permitted speed on the road, or

3) drives recklessly or carelessly or drives a motor vehicle that they have no right to drive, and in doing so:

- does not observe the rules on overtaking, right of way or safety distance, or
- on a road that has two or more marked lanes for driving in one direction, drives on the directional roadway intended for driving in the opposite direction, turns around or drives in reverse or attempts to drive in such a manner, or
- does not adjust the driving speed on non-visible sections of the road, at intersections, before or at pedestrian crossings, bicycle paths or crossings, crossings of the road over a railway line, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to three years.
  - (2) Whoever commits the act from the previous paragraph by negligence, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.
  - (3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article results in a traffic accident with minor bodily injury to one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph with a fine or imprisonment from three months to three years and prohibition from driving a motor vehicle, and for the act from the second paragraph of this article with a fine or imprisonment of up to two years.
  - (4) If the act from the first paragraph of this article results in a traffic accident with serious bodily injury to one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment of up to five years and prohibition from driving a motor vehicle.
  - (5) If the act from the first paragraph of this article results in a traffic accident with the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from one to twelve years and prohibition from driving a motor vehicle.
  - (6) A perpetrator who did not have the right to drive the motor vehicle with which the criminal offense from the first, second, third, fourth or fifth paragraph of this article was committed, shall have that motor vehicle confiscated. A motor vehicle that is the property of another person shall be confiscated if that person enabled, allowed or permitted driving to the perpetrator, knowing or should have known that they did not have the right to drive.

Endangering special types of public traffic

Article 325

(1) Whoever, by violating regulations on the safety of railway, maritime, air traffic or cable car traffic or public passenger transport in road traffic by violating regulations on road traffic safety through negligence, causes an accident, shall be punished with a fine or imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph results in serious bodily injury to any person, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment of up to five years and prohibition from driving a motor vehicle.

(3) If the act from the first paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment of up to eight years and prohibition from driving a motor vehicle. Endangering public traffic with a dangerous act or means

Article 326

(1) Whoever destroys or damages traffic facilities, means of transport, traffic signs and signalling devices, traffic safety devices, gives false signals or signs, places obstacles on traffic routes or acts in another similar manner and thereby causes danger to the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph was committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment of up to one year.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons or great property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph with imprisonment of up to five years, and for the act from the second paragraph with imprisonment of up to three years.

(4) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first paragraph with imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the second paragraph with imprisonment of up to eight years.

Failure to supervise in public traffic

Article 327

(1) A person entrusted with supervision over the maintenance of roads, bridges, means of transport or public traffic, or with the management or direction of driving, who by violating their duty causes danger to the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) A person who gives an order to drive, although knowing that the driver is unable to drive the vehicle due to fatigue or for any other reason or that the means of transport is not in proper condition, and thereby causes danger to the life of people or property of great value, shall be punished in the same manner.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article was committed by negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment of up to one year.

(4) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in serious bodily injury to one or more persons or great property damage, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first and second paragraph with imprisonment of up to five years, and for the act from the third paragraph with imprisonment of up to three years.

(5) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act from the first and second paragraph with imprisonment from one to twelve years, and for the act from the third paragraph with imprisonment of up to eight years.

Leaving an injured person in a traffic accident without assistance

Article 328

(1) A driver of a motor vehicle or other means of transport who leaves without assistance someone who was injured by that means of transport or because of it, shall be punished with imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) If the failure to provide assistance results in serious bodily injury or death of the injured person, the perpetrator shall be punished with imprisonment from three months to five years.

Hijacking of aircraft or vessel

## 329. člen

Whoever seizes control of an aircraft in flight or a vessel at sea by force or serious threat to use force shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.

Attack on aviation safety

330. člen

(1) Whoever endangers the flight of an aircraft by placing or bringing into an aircraft explosives or other similar devices or substances, destroys or damages navigation equipment on an aircraft or causes any other damage on it, or provides false information in connection with the flight, or by attacking the crew of a civilian aircraft restricts or diminishes the ability of crew members to perform their duties, or by violating safety instructions that are clearly marked or communicated to passengers by the commander of the aircraft or other crew members, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever endangers safety at an airport by using force or threatening to use force to compel airport personnel to interrupt operations related to aircraft flights, or by using explosives or other similar devices or substances to destroy or damage an aircraft or equipment intended for aviation safety.

(3) If the act referred to in paragraph one or two of this Article results in the death of one or more persons or the destruction of an aircraft or airport, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

Destruction and removal of signs intended for aviation safety

## 331. člen

Whoever destroys, damages or removes a sign intended for aviation safety shall be punished by imprisonment up to two years.

Thirty-second chapter

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST THE ENVIRONMENT, SPACE AND NATURAL RESOURCES

Pollution and destruction of the environment

332. člen

(1) Whoever, in violation of regulations:

- 1) by discharges, emissions or inputs of quantities of substances or ionising radiation into the air, soil or water, endangers the life of one or more persons or causes a risk of serious bodily injury or actual damage to the quality of air, soil or water or to animals or plants;
- 2) collects, transports, processes or disposes of waste or supervises such procedures or activities after the cessation of operation of a waste disposal facility, or trades in waste or brokers it in such a way as to endanger the life of one or more persons or cause a risk of serious bodily injury or actual damage to the quality of air, soil or water or to animals or plants;
- 3) from point 35 of Article 2 of Regulation (EC) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2006 on shipments of waste, ships non-negligible quantities of waste in one shipment or in several shipments that appear to be linked;
- 4) operates a facility in which a dangerous activity is carried out or in which hazardous substances or preparations are stored, which could cause the death of one or more persons or serious bodily injury or actual damage to the quality of air, soil or water or to animals or plants outside the facility;
- 5) causes a risk of significant deterioration of a habitat within a protected area;
- 6) produces, imports, exports, places on the market or uses substances that deplete the ozone layer, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.
  - (2) If the act referred to in points 1), 2), 4) or 5) of the preceding paragraph results in serious bodily injury or actual damage to the quality of air, soil or water or to animals or plants, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment up to eight years.
  - (3) If the act referred to in points 1), 2) or 4) of paragraph one of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.
  - (4) If the act was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in paragraph one of this Article by a fine or imprisonment up to two years, for the act referred to in paragraph two of this Article by imprisonment up to three years, and for the act referred to in paragraph three of this Article by imprisonment from one to eight years.
  - (5) If the act referred to in paragraph one, two or three of this Article was committed within a criminal organisation for the commission of these acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

Pollution of the sea and waters from vessels

333. člen

(1) Whoever, in violation of regulations, discharges or orders the discharge of oil, chemicals or other pollutants from vessels into the sea, lake or river waters and thereby pollutes the sea, waters or coasts, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever pollutes through repeated discharges of a smaller scale which in individual cases do not cause deterioration of the quality of the sea, lake or river water, but in the case of repeated discharges do cause such deterioration.

(3) If the act referred to in paragraph one or two of this Article results in deterioration of human health or irreparable damage or destruction of waters or the coast, animal or plant life, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(4) If the act referred to in paragraph one or two of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from three to twelve years.

(5) If the act was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in paragraph one or two of this Article by imprisonment up to three years, for the act referred to in paragraph three of this Article by imprisonment from six months to five years, and for the act referred to in paragraph four of this Article by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(6) If the act referred to in paragraph one, two, three or four of this Article was committed within a criminal organisation for the commission of these acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

Unlawful handling of nuclear or other dangerous radioactive materials

334. člen

(1) Whoever, contrary to regulations or technical rules on safety measures, receives, possesses, uses, transfers, modifies, disposes of, discards, enables the acquisition of, distributes, produces, processes, stores, transports, imports, exports or removes nuclear materials or other dangerous radioactive materials, which could cause death or serious bodily injury or great damage to property or great actual damage to the environment, shall be punished by imprisonment up to five years.

(2) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph results in serious bodily injury or great damage to property or great actual damage to the environment, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to eight years.

(3) If the act referred to in paragraph one of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

(4) Whoever obtains nuclear materials by theft, robbery, misappropriation, embezzlement, fraud, abuse of position or authority, threat or use of force or other means of intimidation, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(5) Whoever, by an act directed against a nuclear facility or an act that interferes with the operation of a nuclear facility, causes serious bodily injury or death of one or more persons, great damage to property or great actual damage to the environment due to exposure to radiation or release of radioactive substances, or whoever causes a risk of any of the aforementioned consequences, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.

(6) Whoever threatens to use nuclear materials in such a way as to cause serious bodily injury or death of one or more persons, great damage to property or great actual damage to the environment, or to commit an act referred to in paragraph four or five of this Article in order to compel any natural or legal person to commit or omit any act, shall be punished by imprisonment up to fifteen years.

(7) If the act was committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished for the act referred to in paragraph one of this Article by imprisonment up to three years, for the act referred to in paragraph two of this Article by imprisonment from six months to five years, and for the act referred to in paragraph three of this Article by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(8) If the act referred to in paragraph one, two, three, four, five or six of this Article was committed within a criminal association for the commission of these acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.

Unlawful occupation of real property

338. člen

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever occupies another's land with the intention of using it for construction.

Destruction of plantations with harmful substances

## 339. člen

Whoever causes the destruction of another's plants, fruit trees or other plantations with harmful substances and thereby causes greater damage to these plantations, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to two years.

Destruction of forests

340. člen

(1) Whoever knowingly and contrary to regulations or orders of competent authorities clears or clear-cuts forests on a larger scale or otherwise destroys them, and the elements of any other criminal offence are not present, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) Whoever commits the act from the previous paragraph in a protective forest or a forest with a special purpose, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

Torture of animals

341. člen

(1) Whoever treats an animal cruelly or causes it suffering unnecessarily, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) Whoever engages in organizing animal fights or betting in connection with animal fights or engages in breeding and raising animals with the intention of using them for animal fights, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years and a fine.

(3) Whoever tortures multiple animals by the act from the first paragraph of this article or permanently mutilates a tortured animal or causes its death in a cruel manner, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(4) If due to the act from the second paragraph of this article an animal is permanently seriously mutilated or dies, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years and a fine.

Illegal hunting

342. člen

(1) Whoever hunts without permission or otherwise unlawfully and kills or injures a wild animal or catches it alive, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to six months.

(2) If the act from the previous paragraph concerns game of greater value or game important according to regulations on hunting, or the act is committed during the protection period or in a group, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.

(3) Whoever hunts endangered or rare species of game whose hunting is prohibited, whoever hunts without special permission game of a particular species for which such permission is required, whoever hunts by methods or with means by which game is mass killed or whoever hunts with the aid of a motor vehicle or spotlight, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to two years.

Illegal fishing

## 343. člen

Whoever catches fish with explosives, electricity, poison, narcotic substances, and thereby causes the death of fish, or catches them in a manner that is harmful to their reproduction, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.

Illegal dealing with protected wild animal and plant species

344. člen

(1) Whoever illegally possesses, takes, damages, kills, exports, imports, removes, brings in or trades in protected wild animal and plant species or their parts or products made from them, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to three years.

(2) If the object from the previous paragraph is of great or exceptional nature conservation importance or if the act from the previous paragraph is committed in a criminal organization for carrying out such acts, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Transmission of contagious diseases in animals and plants

345. člen

(1) Whoever during an epidemic of any contagious animal disease which can endanger livestock farming throughout the entire territory of the country, fails to act according to regulations which prescribe measures for suppressing or preventing the disease, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) The same punishment applies to whoever during the existence of danger from a disease or pests which can endanger the plant world throughout the entire territory of the country, fails to act according to regulations which prescribe measures for suppressing or preventing the disease or pests.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this article results in the spread of a contagious disease or pests on a larger scale, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.

(4) If the act from the first, second or third paragraph of this article is committed through negligence, the perpetrator shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to six months.

Production of harmful medicines for treating animals

346. člen

(1) Whoever produces for sale or distributes substances as medicines for treating or preventing contagious diseases in animals which are dangerous to their life or health, and as a result animals of greater value or multiple animals have died or contagious diseases have spread, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.

(2) The substances from the previous paragraph of this article shall be seized.

Negligent veterinary assistance

## 347. člen

A veterinarian or other veterinary worker who in performing veterinary activity through negligence acts in obvious contradiction to the rules of veterinary science and profession and thereby causes the death of animals of greater value, shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment for up to one year.

Thirty-third chapter

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST THE SOVEREIGNTY OF THE REPUBLIC OF SLOVENIA AND ITS

DEMOCRATIC CONSTITUTIONAL ORDER

High treason

## 348. člen

Whoever by force or threat to use force endangers the existence of the Republic of Slovenia or thereby attempts to change its constitutional order or overthrow the highest state authorities, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Attack on territorial integrity

## 349. člen

Whoever attempts by force or threat to use force to secede part of the territory of the Republic of Slovenia or annex part of this territory to another state shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Attack on State Independence

## 350. člen

A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who attempts to bring the Republic of Slovenia into subordination or dependence with respect to another state shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Violation of Territorial Integrity

## 351. člen

Whoever invades the territory of the Republic of Slovenia in violation of its territorial integrity shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years. Assassination of the President of the Republic

## 352. člen

Whoever, in order to endanger the constitutional order or security of the Republic of Slovenia, takes the life of the President of the Republic of Slovenia or the person substituting for them, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Violence against the Highest State Representatives

## 353. člen

Whoever, in order to endanger the performance of tasks in a democratic order or the security of the Republic of Slovenia, abducts any of the highest state representatives, a member of parliament, a member of the National Council, a member of government, a judge of the Constitutional or Supreme Court, or commits any other violence against them or their close relatives, or attacks their official premises, private residence or means of transport, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years. Violence against Representatives of Foreign States or International Organizations

## 354. člen

Whoever, in order to endanger the constitutional order or security of the Republic of Slovenia or harm a foreign state, abducts a representative of a foreign state or international organization, or commits any other violence against them or their close relatives, or attacks their official premises, private residence or means of transport, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

Armed Rebellion

355. člen

(1) Whoever organizes or leads an armed rebellion in order to endanger the existence of the Republic of Slovenia, change its constitutional order or overthrow its highest state bodies, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

(2) Whoever participates in an armed rebellion referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

Diversion

## 356. člen

Whoever, in order to endanger the constitutional order or security of the Republic of Slovenia, demolishes, sets fire to or otherwise destroys or damages any economic facility, means of transport or transport installation, installation intended for a communications system, public installation for water or energy transmission, or any other facility important for the safety of people, supply of the population or the economy, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Sabotage

357. člen

(1) Whoever, in order to endanger the constitutional order or security of the Republic of Slovenia, covertly, treacherously or in another similar manner causes great damage in the performance of their work duties to a state body or organization in which they work, or to another state body or organization, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) If the act referred to in the preceding paragraph is committed at a nuclear facility, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from one to twelve years.

Espionage

358. člen

(1) Whoever serves a foreign state or foreign organization or its agent by collecting confidential military, economic or official data or documents, or communicates or delivers them to it, or enables it to obtain them, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to eight years.

(2) Whoever, to the detriment of the Republic of Slovenia, establishes or leads an intelligence service for a foreign state or foreign organization, shall be punished by imprisonment from three to fifteen years.

(3) Whoever joins a foreign intelligence service referred to in the preceding paragraph or supports its work shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Incitement to Violent Change of Constitutional Order

359. člen

(1) Whoever, in order to endanger the existence, constitutional order or security of the Republic

of Slovenia, calls for or incites the direct commission of criminal offences under Articles 348 to 357 of this Code, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to five years.

(2) Whoever commits the act referred to in the preceding paragraph with financial or other assistance from abroad shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(3) Whoever produces or reproduces material that calls for or incites the commission of criminal offences referred to in the first paragraph of this Article, in order that they or another person disseminate it, or whoever for the same purpose stores a large quantity of such material or disseminates it, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

Punishment for the Most Serious Forms of Criminal Offences

360. člen

(1) For a criminal offence under Articles 348 to 351 and 354 and 357 of this Code that results in the death of one or more persons, serious violence or great devastation, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

(2) If the perpetrator intentionally takes the life of one or more persons in committing the acts referred to in the preceding paragraph, they shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Chapter Thirty-Four

CRIMINAL OFFENCES AGAINST THE DEFENCE CAPACITY OF THE STATE

Evasion of Defence Duties

361. člen

(1) Whoever, when a state of emergency or war is declared, hides or fails to respond to a summons to fulfill a defence duty shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to one year.

(2) Whoever, in order to evade defence duty during a declared state of emergency or war as incapable of such duty, feigns illness, uses a forged document or otherwise deceives the competent authority, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(3) The same punishment as in the preceding paragraph shall be imposed on whoever commits the act referred to in the preceding paragraph by injuring themselves or otherwise rendering themselves incapable of defence duty, alone or with the help of another.

Evasion of Defence Duties by Deception

362. člen

(1) Whoever, when a state of emergency or war is declared, feigns illness, uses a forged document or otherwise deceives the competent authority in order to evade military service or other defence duty, and thus induces it to declare them incapable of performing military service or military duty, shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(2) The same punishment shall be imposed on whoever, in order to evade the service or duty referred to in the preceding paragraph or in order to be assigned to lighter service or duty, injures themselves or otherwise renders themselves incapable, or allows another to render them incapable.

(3) Whoever physically injures another with or without their consent, or otherwise renders them incapable, in order that they evade military service or other military obligation, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Force against a Military Person in the Performance of Military Duty

363. člen

(1) Whoever by force or threat to directly use force prevents a military person from performing military duty or compels them in the same manner to perform military duty shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) An attempt is punishable.

(3) If the perpetrator, in committing the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article, insults a military person, ill-treats them or inflicts a minor bodily injury on them or threatens them with the use of a weapon, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(4) If the perpetrator of the act referred to in the first to third paragraphs of this Article was provoked by unlawful or cruel conduct of a military person, they shall be punished by a fine or imprisonment of up to six months, but the punishment may also be remitted.

Attack on a Military Person Performing Service

364. člen

(1) Whoever attacks or seriously threatens to attack a military person performing service shall be punished by imprisonment of up to two years.

(2) If the perpetrator in the act referred to in the preceding paragraph threatens a military person with a weapon, ill-treats them or inflicts a minor bodily injury on them, they shall be punished by imprisonment of up to three years.

(3) The punishment referred to in the preceding paragraph shall be imposed on whoever commits the act referred to in the first paragraph of this Article against two or more military persons.

Negligent Handling of Defence Resources

## 365. člen

Whoever acts contrary to regulations concerning weapons, ammunition, explosives, combat devices or other means intended for the defense of the state, which are entrusted to them for storage, repair, maintenance or use, although they foresee or should and could foresee that the means may be lost, destroyed or damaged, and thereby causes great material damage, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to two years.

Undermining Defense Measures

## 366. člen

Whoever destroys defense installations, defense facilities, positions, weapons or other defense means, renders them unusable or otherwise obstructs or abandons defense measures of the state, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Preventing Combat Against the Enemy

## 367. člen

A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who, during war or armed conflict, prevents citizens of the Republic of Slovenia or citizens of its allies from fighting against the enemy, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Service in the Enemy's Army

## 368. člen

A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who, during war or armed conflict, serves in the enemy's army or in other enemy armed forces or participates in war or armed conflict as a combatant against the Republic of Slovenia or its allies, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to ten years.

Recruiting for a Foreign Army

## 369. člen

Whoever recruits citizens of the Republic of Slovenia or citizens of other states or stateless persons for service in the enemy's army or other enemy armed forces or for participation in war or armed conflict against the Republic of Slovenia or its allies, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

Aiding the Enemy

370. člen

(1) A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who, during war, aids the enemy in the seizure of movable property, taking of food or other goods or in any other measures against the population, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to five years.

(2) A citizen of the Republic of Slovenia who, during war, politically or economically collaborates with the enemy, shall be punished in the same manner.

Chapter Thirty-Five

CRIMINAL OFFENSES AGAINST INTERNATIONAL LAW

Endangering Persons Under International Protection

371. člen

(1) Whoever endangers the safety of a person who is under international legal protection by making a serious threat to attack them, their official premises, private residence or means of transport, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) Whoever abducts a person who is under international legal protection or commits any other violence against them or attacks their official premises, private residence or means of transport, shall be punished by imprisonment of at least one year.

(3) If the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

(4) If the perpetrator, in committing the act from the first or second paragraph of this Article, intentionally takes the life of one or more persons, they shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years. Violation of Parliamentary Rights

## 372. člen

Whoever, contrary to the rules of international law, during war or armed conflict, insults a parliamentary envoy or their escort, treats them harshly, detains them or prevents their return or otherwise violates their inviolability, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

Taking of Hostages

373. člen

(1) Whoever abducts any person and threatens to kill, injure or hold them as a hostage in order to compel any state or international organization to do or not do something as an express or implied condition for the release of the hostage, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to fifteen years.

(2) If the act from the preceding paragraph results in the death of one or more persons, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

(3) If the perpetrator, in committing the act from the first paragraph of this Article, intentionally takes the life of one or more persons, they shall be punished by imprisonment of at least fifteen years.

Piracy

374. člen

(1) A crew member or passenger on a vessel or aircraft, except a military ship or aircraft and a public ship or aircraft, who, in violation of the rules of international law, with the intent to obtain for themselves or another a material or non-material benefit or to severely harm another, on the high seas or in a place not under the authority of any state, commits an unlawful act of violence, detention or any plundering against another vessel or aircraft, persons or things on them, shall be punished by imprisonment from one to ten years.

(2) Piracy from the preceding paragraph shall also include an act committed by a crew member of a military or public ship or aircraft that has unlawfully mutinied and taken control of the ship or aircraft.

(3) If the acts from the first or second paragraph of this Article result in the death of one or more persons or great material damage, the perpetrator shall be punished by imprisonment from five to fifteen years.

Violation of Restrictive Measures

374.a člen

(1) Whoever, contrary to the restrictions determined in regulations on the implementation of restrictive measures adopted on the basis of legal acts and decisions of international organizations, or restrictions that are directly applicable in the Republic of Slovenia in accordance with the legal regulations of international organizations, offers, sells, transfers, exchanges, delivers, imports, exports, brings into the country or takes out of the country goods, technology, money or property or intermediates or enables access to such goods, technology, money or property or to benefits from them or fails to prevent this, or whoever unlawfully acquires or stores such goods, technology, money or property and thereby obtains great material benefit, shall be punished by imprisonment from six months to five years.

(2) The goods, technology, money and property from the preceding paragraph shall be confiscated.
