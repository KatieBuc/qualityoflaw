# LAW ON PROTECTION FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

LAW ON PROTECTION FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

OF THE REPUBLIC OF SRPSKA

("Official Gazette of the Republic of Srpska", No. 102/2012, 108/2013, 82/2015 and 84/2019)

I - BASIC PROVISIONS

## Article 1

This law regulates the protection from violence in the family or family community (hereinafter: domestic violence), persons who are considered members of the family or family community within the meaning of this law, subjects of protection and the procedure for protecting victims of domestic violence, the establishment of the Council for Combating Domestic Violence, measures for protecting victims of domestic violence and other issues of importance for protection from domestic violence.

## Article 2

The basic goal of this law is the protection of victims of domestic violence by preventing and combating domestic violence, which violates fundamental human rights and freedoms guaranteed by the constitution and laws.

## Article 3

Protection from domestic violence is achieved through the application of this law and other regulations governing the realization of the rights of victims of domestic violence.

## Article 4

(1) For the purpose of protection from domestic violence, the National Assembly of the Republic of Srpska adopts the Strategy for Combating Domestic Violence of the Republic of Srpska (hereinafter: the Strategy), on the proposal of the Government of the Republic of Srpska (hereinafter: the Government).

(2) Annual action plans are adopted by the Government on the proposal of the Ministry of Family, Youth and Sports

(hereinafter: the Ministry).

(3) The Strategy is adopted for a period of at least five years.

## Article 5

Grammatical expressions used in this law to designate the male or female gender include both sexes.

II - ACTS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE, VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND SUBJECTS

OF PROTECTION

## Article 6

(1) Domestic violence, within the meaning of this law, exists if there is a basis for suspicion that a member of the family or family community has committed acts of physical, sexual, psychological and/or economic violence, as well as threats that cause fear of physical, sexual, psychological and/or economic harm to another member of the family or family community.

(2) Acts of domestic violence, within the meaning of paragraph 1. of this Article, are:

- a) application of force to the physical or mental integrity of a member of the family or family community,
- b) conduct that may cause or create a danger of causing physical or mental pain or suffering to a member of the family or family community,
- v) causing fear or personal endangerment or violation of the dignity of a member of the family or family community by blackmail or other coercion,
- g) verbal attack, insult, cursing, calling derogatory names, and other forms of gross harassment of a member of the family or family community,
- d) sexual violence, đ) preventing access to health care and treatment,
- e) stalking and all other similar forms of harassment of a member of the family or family community, ž) intentional damage or destruction of property of a member of the family or family community, joint property, or property in possession,
- z) denying or depriving the right to economic independence by prohibiting work or keeping a member of the family or family community in a relationship of dependence or subordination,
- i) raising children in a manner of humiliating treatment,
- j) taking away children or evicting from the apartment a member of the family or family community,
- k) exhaustion by work, starvation, deprivation of sleep or necessary rest of a member of the family or family community,
- l) non-compliance with the decision of the competent authority establishing personal contact of children with parents, lj) withholding a travel document or any other document of a member of the family or family community,
- m) forced isolation or restriction of freedom of movement of a member of the family or family community and
- n) any other act that constitutes violence in the family or family community.
  - (3) Acts of domestic violence also include threats of acts referred to in paragraph 2. of this Article.

## Article 7

Members of the family or family community, within the meaning of this law, are considered to be:

- a) spouses or former spouses and their children and the children of each of them,
- b) extramarital partners or former extramarital partners, their children or the children of each of them,
- v) relatives by affinity up to the second degree regardless of the fact that the marital union has ceased,
- g) parents of current and former marital and extramarital partners,
- d) relatives from full adoption in the direct line without limitation, and in the collateral line up to the fourth degree, as well as relatives from incomplete adoption, đ) persons bound by a guardianship relationship,
- e) persons who live or have lived in the same family household, regardless of kinship, ž) persons who have a common child or a child has been conceived, although they have never lived in the same family household,
- z) persons who were or are still in an emotional or intimate relationship with each other, regardless of whether the perpetrator shares or has shared a household with the victim.

## Article 8

(1) A victim of domestic violence has the right to psychosocial assistance and social and medical protection in accordance with laws in the field of health and social protection.

(2) Special assistance and protection in accordance with this law is enjoyed by a victim who is:

- a) a child,
- b) an elderly person,
- v) a person with a disability and
- g) a person under guardianship.
  - (3) A child is also a victim if it was present during the commission of violence against another family member, even though the acts of violence were not directed at it.

## Article 9

(1) Protection, assistance and support to victims of domestic violence shall be provided by members of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (hereinafter: the police), the prosecutor's office, social work centers, i.e. social protection services, health and educational institutions and the competent court (hereinafter: subjects of protection).

(2) Subjects of protection are obliged to act in accordance with the provisions of this law and to provide protection, support and assistance to victims of domestic violence and to prevent the recurrence of violence regardless of whether criminal or misdemeanor proceedings have been initiated against the perpetrator.

## Article 10

(1) Victims of domestic violence have the right to access all subjects of protection and are exempt from all costs of proceedings.

(2) Subjects of protection are obliged at the first contact to inform the victim, in a manner understandable to it, of all rights it has in accordance with this and other regulations and of institutions, bodies, legal entities and organizations that provide assistance, support and protection.

(3) In proceedings for the realization of their rights and protection, victims have the right to free legal aid in accordance with regulations governing the field of free legal aid.

III - PROCEDURE FOR ACHIEVING PROTECTION OF VICTIMS OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## Article 11

(1) Subjects of protection are obliged without delay to ensure urgent resolution of cases of domestic violence, taking into account that the interest and welfare of the victim are a priority in such proceedings, especially if the victim is a child, an elderly person, a person with a disability and a person under guardianship.

(2) Subjects of protection in the process of protection, assistance and support to victims of domestic violence cooperate in the best interest of the victim.

(3) In the area of the local self-government unit, a protocol on conduct is concluded and a group for coordination and cooperation is formed from representatives of all institutions, bodies and organizations that provide protection, assistance and support to victims of domestic violence.

(4) Based on annual reports, the Ministry monitors and analyzes the implementation of the protocol referred to in paragraph 3. of this Article.

(5) The coordination and cooperation group referred to in paragraph 3. of this Article shall be formed within 30 days from the date of entry into force of this law.

## Article 12

(1) Family members, subjects of protection, as well as employees in educational, social and health institutions, as well as every other citizen, are obliged to report to the police immediately upon learning that domestic violence has been committed or that there is a basis for suspicion that domestic violence has been committed.

(2) After receiving a report that domestic violence has been committed, the competent police officer is obliged to immediately inform the social welfare centre, which will immediately provide direct social protection and psychosocial assistance to the victim, take other measures within its competence, and prepare an official report on this.

(3) The health institution will enable the victim to have a free medical examination in order to determine the existence of injuries to physical or mental integrity.

(4) The competent police officer immediately notifies the competent public prosecutor of the committed domestic violence and, together with the report, submits the collected evidence and risk assessment.

(5) The competent public prosecutor, after completion of the investigation, in written form, notifies the competent police officer of the filing of an indictment against the suspect or of the suspension of the investigation, as well as of the reasons for the suspension of the investigation.

(6) The competent police officer also submits the notification and risk assessment to the social welfare centre, in addition to the competent public prosecutor.

## Article 12a

(1) A risk assessment is carried out for every report of domestic violence.

(2) The competent police officer carries out the risk assessment based on collected information on:

- a) whether the perpetrator has previously or immediately before the risk assessment committed domestic violence or some other form of violence and whether he is ready to repeat it,
- b) whether he has threatened murder or suicide,
- v) whether he possesses weapons,
- g) whether he is mentally ill or abuses psychoactive substances,
- d) whether there is a dispute over custody of the child or over the manner of maintaining personal contact between the child and the parent who is the perpetrator, đ) whether the perpetrator has been issued an emergency measure or a protection measure against domestic violence,
- e) whether the victim experiences fear and how she assesses the risk of repeated violence and ž) other facts and circumstances relevant to the risk assessment.
  - (3) The Minister of Internal Affairs adopts the Rulebook on the procedure and manner of conducting risk assessment.

## Article 13

(1) In order to eliminate immediate danger to physical and mental integrity, to prevent the recurrence of violence and to guarantee the safety of the victim, emergency protection measures may be imposed on the perpetrator of domestic violence before the initiation of proceedings or during the proceedings.

(2) Emergency protection measures are imposed by the competent court in misdemeanor proceedings

(3) A proposal for the imposition of an emergency protection measure is submitted by the competent police officer, the social welfare centre or the victim of domestic violence or family community, and they are imposed no later than 24 hours from the receipt of the proposal, i.e. the bringing of the perpetrator of domestic violence before the court.

(4) Emergency protection measures are:

- a) removal of the perpetrator of violence from the apartment, house or other residential space and/or
- b) prohibition of approach and contact of the perpetrator of violence with the victim of domestic violence.

## Article 14

(1) Emergency protection measures are imposed for a duration that cannot be longer than 30 days.

(2) Emergency protection measures from paragraph 1 of this Article are imposed by the judge of the misdemeanor department of the competent basic court by decision, against which an appeal can be filed within three days from the day of imposition.

(3) An appeal against the court's decision on the imposed emergency protection measure does not postpone its execution.

(4) The appeal is decided by the council of the competent basic court that made the decision within 48 hours from the receipt of the appeal.

(5) When leaving the apartment, house or other living space, the perpetrator of domestic violence has the right to take his personal belongings necessary for daily life and the obligation to hand over the keys to the apartment, house or other living space.

(6) The court that imposed it delivers the decision on its imposition to the competent organizational unit of the Ministry of Internal Affairs for implementation and examines its implementation and the justification of the duration for the period for which it was imposed.

(7) The manner of implementing emergency protection measures is regulated by the rulebook from Article 26, paragraph 3 of this law.

2. Manner of sheltering victims of domestic violence

## Article 15

(1) The social welfare centre, i.e. the social protection service with the assistance of the police may, with the prior consent of the victim of domestic violence, in order to ensure physical protection and realization of the rights and interests of the victim, adopt a decision on temporary sheltering of the victim in a safe house.

(2) The victim of domestic violence, due to the suffered violence, fear and distress, in order to ensure physical protection and realization of their rights and interests, as well as prevention of recurrence of violence, has the right to submit to the competent social welfare centre, i.e. the social protection service, a request for the use of a special support measure - temporary sheltering in a safe house.

(3) A safe house represents a special support measure which provides safe accommodation and assistance to victims of domestic violence, which can be implemented by a legal entity.

(4) For the protection and security of the victim, the police will escort the victim to the house, apartment or other residential space so that she can take her own and the personal belongings of other persons who left that space with her, which are necessary to meet daily needs.

(5) The measure from paragraph 1 of this Article may be determined for the duration necessary to achieve the purpose of the measure, provided that it cannot last longer than six months.

(6) In justified cases, the duration of the measure from paragraph 1 of this Article may be extended for another six months, i.e. until the completion of the proceedings and execution of the decision by which a protective measure is imposed on the perpetrator of violence.

## Article 16

(1) The safe house from Article 15 of this law must meet the established standards in terms of space, equipment and staff.

(2) The fulfillment of the standards from paragraph 1 of this Article is determined by a commission, which is appointed by the Minister of Family, Youth and Sports (hereinafter: the Minister).

(3) Based on the proposal of the commission, the Minister adopts a decision on the fulfillment of the standards from paragraph 1 of this Article.

(4) The decision from paragraph 3 of this Article is subject to revision after the expiry of a period of two years from the date of receipt of the decision.

(5) The Ministry ex officio takes into account the expiry of the period from paragraph 4 of this Article.

(6) The decision from paragraph 3 of this Article is final and an appeal against it is not permitted, but an administrative dispute can be initiated before the competent court.

(7) The Minister adopts a rulebook on standards for the implementation of a safe house.

## Article 17

(1) Based on the decision from Article 16, paragraph 3 of this law, safe houses are entered in the Register of Safe Houses, which is maintained by the Ministry.

(2) The Minister adopts a rulebook on the content and manner of maintaining the Register of Safe Houses.

## Article 18

Funds for temporary sheltering and accommodation of victims of domestic violence in safe houses are provided from the budget of the Republic of Srpska (hereinafter: the Republic budget) in the amount of 70%, and from the budget of local self-government units in the amount of 30% of the determined price of accommodation of the victim.

## Član 21

(1) Centar za socijalni rad obrazuje stručni tim od predstavnika te ustanove, organa i službi jedinice lokalne samouprave, policije, nevladinih organizacija i stručnjaka koji se bave pitanjima porodice i nasilja u porodici radi utvrđivanja plana pomoći žrtvi i koordinacije aktivnosti u procesu pomoći žrtvi, u skladu sa njenim potrebama i izborom.

(2) Plan pomoći žrtvi posebno sadrži mjere koje je potrebno preduzeti u skladu sa zakonom kojim se uređuju socijalna, zdravstvena i dječija zaštita.

(3) Ako je žrtva dijete, plan pomoći žrtvi sadrži i mjere za zaštitu djeteta u skladu sa zakonom kojim se uređuju porodični odnosi i propisima kojima se uređuje zaštita prava djece.

## Član 21a

(1) Žrtva može izabrati, prije ili u toku postupka, lice od povjerenja koje će biti prisutno tokom jednog ili više postupaka ili radnji u vezi sa zaštitom od nasilja u porodici.

(2) Lice od povjerenja može biti bilo koje punoljetno lice osim učinioca nasilja.

(3) Nadležni organi su obavezni da omoguće prisustvo lica od povjerenja u svim postupcima i radnjama u koje je uključena žrtva, u skladu sa propisima iz svoje nadležnosti.

(4) Žrtva nasilja ima pravo na punomoćnika u postupku.

## Član 22

O žrtvi ili učiniocu nasilja u porodici ne smije se javno objaviti informacija na osnovu koje se može prepoznati žrtva ili član porodice žrtve, osim ako je punoljetna žrtva s tim izričito saglasna.

IV - PROTECTIVE MEASURES

## Član 23

(1) Mjere za zaštitu žrtve od nasilja u porodici su zaštitne mjere.

(2) Svrha zaštitnih mjera jeste da se osigura nužna zaštita i bezbjednost žrtve, te otklone stanja ili uslovi koji mogu biti od uticaja ili mogu pogodovati ili podsticajno djelovati da učinilac ubuduće vrši radnje nasilja u porodici.

(3) Zaštitne mjere su:

- a) udaljenje iz stana, kuće ili nekog drugog stambenog prostora,
- b) zabrana približavanja žrtvi nasilja u porodici ili porodičnoj zajednici (u daljem tekstu: zabrana približavanja žrtvi),
- v) zabrana uznemiravanja ili uhođenja žrtve nasilja u porodici ili porodičnoj zajednici (u daljem tekstu: zabrana uznemiravanja ili uhođenja žrtve),
- g) obavezan psihosocijalni tretman i
- d) obavezno liječenje od zavisnosti.
  - (4) Zaštitne mjere izriče nadležni sud u prekršajnom postupku, a na prijedlog nadležnog policijskog službenika, centra za socijalni rad ili žrtve nasilja u porodici.
  - (5) Sud je dužan izreći zaštitne mjere u roku od 30 dana od dana podnošenja Prijedloga za izricanje zaštitne mjere.

## Član 23a

Zaštitne mjere iz člana 23. stav 3. ovog zakona izriču se samostalno i predmet su direktnog izvršenja organa nadležnih za njihovo sprovođenje, u skladu sa odredbama ovog zakona.

(deleted)

## Član 24

(1) Zaštitna mjera - udaljenje iz stana, kuće ili nekog drugog stambenog prostora izriče se učiniocu nasilja ako je izvršio nasilje prema članu porodice sa kojim živi u stanu, kući ili nekom drugom stambenom prostoru ako nadležni sud ocijeni da postoji opasnost da bi bez sprovođenja ove mjere učinilac nasilja u porodici mogao ponovo počiniti nasilje.

(2) Lice kome je izrečena mjera iz stava 1. ovog člana dužno je, bez odlaganja, napustiti stan, kuću ili neki drugi stambeni prostor u prisustvu policijskog službenika.

(3) Mjera iz stava 1. ovog člana određuje se u trajanju koje ne može biti kraće od 30 dana niti duže od šest mjeseci.

## Član 25

(1) Zaštitna mjera - zabrana približavanja žrtvi izriče se učiniocu nasilja ako postoji opasnost da bi ponovo mogao izvršiti nasilje ili ako bi prisustvo učinioca nasilja u porodici u blizini žrtve stvaralo žrtvi visok stepen duševne patnje koja onemogućava normalne psihičke aktivnosti žrtve.

(2) U rješenju kojim sud izriče zaštitnu mjeru - zabrana približavanja žrtvi sud će odrediti mjesta ili područja i udaljenost od najmanje 200 metara na kojoj se učinilac nasilja u porodici ne smije približiti žrtvi.

(3) Mjera iz stava 1. ovog člana određuje se u trajanju koje ne može biti kraće od 30 dana niti duže od jedne godine.

## Član 26

(1) Zaštitna mjera - zabrana uznemiravanja ili uhođenja žrtve izriče se učiniocu nasilja koji je nasilje izvršio uznemiravanjem ili uhođenjem, a postoji opasnost da bi mogao ponoviti uznemiravanje ili uhođenje žrtve.

(2) Mjera iz stava 1. ovog člana određuje se u trajanju koje ne može biti kraće od 30 dana niti duže od jedne godine.

(3) Ministar unutrašnjih poslova donosi pravilnik o načinu sprovođenja hitnih i zaštitnih mjera koje su u nadležnosti Ministarstva unutrašnjih poslova, a koje su propisane u čl. 13, 24, 25. i 26. ovog zakona.

## Član 27

(1) Zaštitna mjera - obavezan psihosocijalni tretman izriče se učiniocu nasilja radi otklanjanja uzroka nasilničkog ponašanja i prevaspitanja, odnosno u cilju smanjivanja i otklanjanja opasnosti ponavljanja učinjenog nasilja.

(2) Mjera iz stava 1. ovog člana može trajati do prestanka razloga zbog kojeg je određena, ali ne može trajati duže od jedne godine.

(3) Ministar zdravlja i socijalne zaštite donosi pravilnik o načinu sprovođenja zaštitne mjere - obavezan psihosocijalni tretman.

## Član 28

(1) Zaštitna mjera - obavezno liječenje od zavisnosti izriče se učiniocu nasilja koji je nasilje izvršio pod uticajem zavisnosti od alkohola, opojnih droga i drugih psihotropnih supstanci, a postoji opasnost da zbog te zavisnosti ponovo učini nasilje.

(2) Mjera iz stava 1. ovog člana određuje se u trajanju koje je potrebno za liječenje, zasnovanom na mišljenju specijalizovanih stručnjaka zaduženih za liječenje, ali ne može trajati duže od godinu dana.

(3) Ministar zdravlja i socijalne zaštite donosi pravilnik o načinu sprovođenja zaštitne mjere - obavezno liječenje od zavisnosti.

## Član 29

(1) Nadležni sud može učiniocu nasilja izreći jednu ili više zaštitnih mjera kad postoje uslovi za njihovo izricanje propisani ovim zakonom.

(2) Nadležni sud može zatražiti od centra za socijalni rad:

- a) da mu pruži pomoć u pribavljanju potrebnih dokaza i
- b) da dostavi svoje mišljenje o svrsishodnosti tražene zaštitne mjere.

## Article 30

(1) Against the decision on imposing a protective measure, an appeal may be filed with the competent court within three days from the day of delivery of the decision.

(2) The competent district court decides on the appeal within three days from the day of receiving the appeal.

(3) The appeal does not postpone the execution of the decision on imposing a protective measure.

## Article 31

(1) The perpetrator of domestic violence is obliged to act in accordance with the imposed protective measure.

(2) A protection entity that, within the scope of its work, learns that the perpetrator of domestic violence is not acting in accordance with the imposed protective measure is obliged to inform the competent court and the social welfare center about this.

## Article 32

(1) The competent court is obliged to deliver the decision on the imposed protective measure to the body or institution responsible for implementing the execution within three days from the day of issuing the decision.

(2) The decision on the imposed protective measure from Articles 24, 25 and 26 of this law is delivered to the police for execution.

(3) The decision on the imposed protective measure from Articles 27 and 28 of this law is delivered to the social welfare center and the body, i.e. institution that executes the protective measure in accordance with the law regulating the treatment and rehabilitation of persons addicted to alcohol, narcotic drugs and other psychotropic substances.

## Article 33

(1) When imposing protective measures under this law, consideration will be given to the purpose, severity, duration of the imposed measure and its effectiveness.

(2) The bodies responsible for implementing protective measures are obliged to monitor their execution and report to the court about it, propose termination, extension or replacement with another measure.

(3) The body responsible for implementing the protective measure is obliged to submit a report to the court on the execution of protective measures no later than within six months from the day of imposing the protective measure, and if necessary, earlier.

V - RECORDS AND TRAINING

## Article 34

(1) Protection entities and other bodies authorized to act under this law are obliged to keep records of actions taken under this law and data on the number of initiated and completed proceedings and other measures taken, and to submit reports on this to the Ministry.

(2) The Ministry collects, processes and records data on domestic violence.

(3) The Minister adopts a rulebook on the content of records and reports on domestic violence from paragraph 1 of this Article.

## Article 35

The Center for Education of Judges and Prosecutors of Republika Srpska is responsible for acquiring special knowledge and continuous professional training and advancement of judges and prosecutors in the field of domestic violence.

VI - COUNCIL FOR COMBATING DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

## Article 36

For the purpose of monitoring and evaluating the implementation of policies and measures for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, and improving coordinated and effective action in the field of domestic violence, the Government establishes a Council for Combating Domestic Violence and Family Violence (hereinafter: the Council).

## Article 37

(1) Members of the Council are appointed and dismissed by the Government, upon the proposal of the Ministry.

(2) The Council has nine members, consisting of representatives of competent ministries and other republican bodies, public institutions and professional services.

(3) The Council may, as needed, include in its work representatives of institutions, as well as non-governmental organizations, and experts from the academic and research community in areas relevant to improving policies in the field of domestic violence.

## Article 38

(1) The mandate of Council members lasts four years.

(2) The Council elects a president from among its members.

(3) The work of Council members is voluntary and unpaid.

(4) The procedure for election and appointment of new Council members, due to replacement or resignation of previous members, is identical to the procedure and manner of election and appointment of the members being replaced.

## Article 39

The Council performs the following tasks within its competence:

- a) monitors the implementation and evaluates policies and measures for the prevention and combating of domestic violence, and in that regard provides recommendations and opinions,
- b) provides recommendations and opinions to the Ministry in the process of proposing and adopting legal regulations related to domestic violence,
- v) creates and participates in the implementation of scientific, professional, research and other projects in the field of domestic violence,
- g) participates in the activities of social partners in working on the prevention and combating of domestic violence and
- d) considers other issues relevant to the prevention and combating of domestic violence.

## Article 40

(1) Administrative and technical tasks for the Council are performed by the Ministry.

(2) The work and functioning of the Council is regulated by the Rules of Procedure of the Council.

(3) The Rules of Procedure from paragraph 2 of this Article are adopted by the Council by a majority vote of the total number of members.

VII - SUPERVISION AND PENAL PROVISIONS

## Article 41

(1) Supervision over the implementation of this law is performed by the Ministry.

(2) The Gender Center monitors the application of this law from the aspect of fulfilling obligations from domestic and international standards for combating and preventing violence against women and gender-based violence and standards for gender equality.

## Article 42

(1) A fine from 1,000 KM to 3,000 KM will be imposed for a misdemeanor on an employee in an educational, social and health institution who does not report domestic violence.

(2) A fine from 300 KM to 900 KM will be imposed for a misdemeanor on a citizen who does not report domestic violence.

(3) A fine from 300 KM to 900 KM will be imposed for a misdemeanor on a family member or family community member who does not report domestic violence committed against a child, except in cases where they themselves are a victim of domestic violence.

(4) A fine from 3,000 KM to 5,000 KM will be imposed for a misdemeanor on protection entities and other bodies authorized to act under this law that do not act in accordance with Article 34 paragraph 1 of this law.

## Article 42a

A police officer who does not act in accordance with the obligations under this law upon reporting domestic violence will be held responsible in accordance with the law.

## Article 43

Anyone who violates the urgent protection measures and protective measures imposed by the court through the application of this law will be punished in accordance with the provisions of the law prescribing criminal offenses.

## Article 43a

A legal entity that implements a special support measure that does not act in accordance with Article 20 paragraph 4 of the Law will be fined from 3,000 KM to 7,000 KM.

VIII - TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

## Article 44

Legal entities that operate a safe house are obliged to meet the standards from Article 16 of this law within six months from the day of entry into force of the rulebook on standards for the operation of a safe house.

## Article 45

(1) The Minister will, within six months from the day of entry into force of this law, adopt:

- a) a rulebook on standards for the operation of a safe house (Article 16 paragraph 7),
- b) a rulebook on the content and manner of maintaining the Register of Safe Houses (Article 17 paragraph 2),
- v) a rulebook on the manner of allocating funds to safe houses (Article 19 paragraph 5) and
- g) a rulebook on the content of records and reports on domestic violence (Article 34 paragraph 3).
  - (2) The Minister of Internal Affairs will, within six months from the day of entry into force of this law, adopt a rulebook on the manner of implementing protective measures that are within the competence of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (Article 26 paragraph 3).
  - (3) The Minister of Health and Social Welfare will, within six months from the day of entry into force of this law, adopt:
- a) a rulebook on the manner and place of implementing the protective measure - mandatory psychosocial treatment (Article
  - 27 paragraph 3) and
- b) a rulebook on the manner and place of implementing the protective measure - mandatory treatment for addiction (Article
  - 28 paragraph 3).

## Article 45a

The Minister of Internal Affairs will, within six months from the day of entry into force of this law, adopt a Rulebook on the procedure and manner of conducting risk assessment (Article 12a paragraph 3).

## Article 46

Until the adoption of regulations from Article 45 of this law, by-laws adopted on the basis of the Law on Protection from Domestic Violence ("Official Gazette of Republika Srpska", Nos. 118/05 and 17/08) will be applied if they are not contrary to the provisions of this law.

## Article 47

Upon the entry into force of this law, the Law on Protection from Domestic Violence ("Official Gazette of Republika Srpska", Nos. 118/05 and 17/08) ceases to be valid.

## Article 48

This law enters into force on the eighth day from the day of publication in the "Official Gazette of the Republic of

Srpska".
