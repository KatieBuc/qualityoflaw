# PENAL CODE OF HONDURAS

PENAL CODE OF HONDURAS

DECREE NUMBER 144-83

THE NATIONAL CONGRESS,

DECREES:

The following:

PENAL CODE

FIRST BOOK

GENERAL PART

#### TITLE I

APPLICATION OF CRIMINAL LAW

###### ARTICLE 1. No one shall be punished for offenses that are not determined in a law

prior to the commission of a crime.

###### ARTICLE 2. No penalties or security measures other than those

previously established by Law shall be imposed.

###### ARTICLE 2-A. Actions or omissions susceptible to being classified according to

two or more provisions of this Code shall be sanctioned observing the following rules:

- 1) The special provision shall be applied with preference to the general provision;
- 2) The subsidiary provision shall be applied in default of the principal provision when such subsidiarity is expressly declared or it appears in tacit form; and,
- 3) The complex criminal provision shall absorb those that sanction the offenses consumed therein.

###### ARTICLE 2-B. Every person to whom a crime or misdemeanor is attributed has the right to be

treated with the respect due to the dignity inherent to the human being. Consequently, penalties or security measures that imply inhuman or degrading treatment may not be imposed.

###### ARTICLE 2-C. No penalty or security measure may be imposed if the action or

omission does not injure or effectively endanger a legal interest protected by criminal law.

###### ARTICLE 2-D. Penalties and security measures shall only be imposed when they are

necessary and in a manner proportional to the gravity of the act committed.

In no case may a security measure be imposed unless it is as a consequence of an action or omission described as a crime by criminal law.

###### ARTICLE 3. Honduran Criminal Law shall apply to any person who commits a punishable act

in the national territory and other places subject to the jurisdiction of Honduras, except for the exceptions stipulated in International Law.

###### ARTICLE 4. Honduran Criminal Law shall apply to those who have committed abroad

crimes against public health, public faith, the economy or the exterior or interior security of the State, regulated in titles V, IX, X, XI and XII of the Second Book of this Code. It shall likewise apply when a Honduran public official or employee has committed abroad crimes against the national public administration regulated in Title XIII of the aforementioned Second Book.

###### ARTICLE 5. Honduran courts shall likewise have jurisdiction over crimes committed

abroad when the accused is in Honduras and any of the following situations occurs:

- 1) When he has not been tried for the crime perpetrated aboard a Honduran vessel or aircraft, merchant or private, or when having been tried he has escaped and has not served all or part of the sentence;
- 2) If the accused being Honduran, his extradition is requested by the State in whose territory the punishable act was committed;
- 3) If the person responsible for the crime is an official of the Government of Honduras and enjoys diplomatic or official immunity;
- 4) If the person responsible for the crime committed against a Honduran has not been tried in the country in which it was perpetrated nor has his extradition been requested or when having been tried he has escaped and failed to serve all or part of the sentence; and,
- 5) When in accordance with international agreements of which Honduras is a party, the crime is subject to Honduran criminal law for reasons other than those mentioned in the preceding numerals or seriously injures universally recognized human rights. Preference shall be given, however, to the claim of the State in whose territory the punishable act was committed provided that it asserts it before the respective criminal action is instituted in the competent Honduran court.

###### ARTICLE 6. Foreign criminal sentences concerning the crimes referred to in articles 3 and 4 above

shall not have the value of res judicata. However, the sentence that the convict has served, totally or partially, by virtue of any of said sentences, or those issued in relation to numerals 1), 2) and 4) of article 5, above, shall be deducted from that imposed upon him in accordance with Honduran law if both are of similar nature; if they are not, the new sentence shall be prudently mitigated.

###### ARTICLE 7. With the exception of the cases stated in the preceding article, the foreign

criminal sentence of acquittal shall have the value of res judicata for all legal purposes. The conviction shall have it to determine the recidivism or habitual nature of the convict, and for the civil effects of the sentence which shall be governed by Honduran Law.

###### ARTICLE 8. Honduran Criminal Law shall not apply to foreign Heads of State

who are in the national territory nor to diplomatic agents and other persons who, according to International Law, enjoy immunity.

###### ARTICLE 9. Criminal laws have retroactive effect insofar as they favor the

defendant, even if upon their entry into force a final sentence has been rendered and the convict is serving the sentence.

###### ARTICLE 10. In no case shall the extradition of Hondurans who

having committed a crime abroad are found in the national territory be granted. The extradition of foreigners may only be granted by virtue of Law or Treaty, for common crimes that merit a penalty of not less than one year of deprivation of liberty; and never for political crimes, even if a common crime results from them.

###### ARTICLE 11. Judicial authorities may not create any type of criminal

offenses.

###### ARTICLE 12. The general provisions of this Code shall apply to all

crimes punished by special laws, insofar as the latter do not provide otherwise.

#### TITLE II

THE CRIME

###### ARTICLE 13. A crime may be committed by action or by omission and must necessarily

be intentional or negligent.

A crime is intentional, when the result corresponds to the intention that was had upon executing it or when the author knows, or is obliged to know, that as a consequence of the action or omission there exists the possibility that a harmful effect constituting a crime will be produced, notwithstanding which he executes the act and accepts, therefore, the consequences that derive from it.

A crime is negligent when it is the result of recklessness, lack of skill or negligence or when it is the product of the non-observance of a law, a regulation or orders, resolutions or duties, considering the circumstances and personal situation of the offender. A negligent crime shall only be punishable in cases expressly determined by law.

In no case may the penalty for a negligent crime be greater than that corresponding to that same crime if it had been committed intentionally; when this occurs, the penalty for the intentional crime reduced by one-sixth shall be imposed.

###### ARTICLE 13-A. For criminal purposes, political crimes are considered to be those included

in Chapters I, II and III of Title XI and II, V, VI and VII of Title XII of the Second Book of the Penal Code.

Common crimes connected with political crimes are those that have a direct or immediate relation to a political crime or are a natural and frequent means of preparing, carrying out or favoring it.

###### ARTICLE 14. A crime is consummated when all the elements of its

legal typification concur therein.

###### ARTICLE 15. There is an attempt when, with the intention of committing a specific crime,

unequivocal acts of execution are carried out and it is not consummated due to causes beyond the will of the agent.

###### ARTICLE 16. If the attempt is made with inadequate means or upon improper objects,

the penalty may be mitigated or the act declared not punishable, according to the dangerousness revealed by its author.

###### ARTICULO 17. Conspiracy and proposal to commit a crime are only punishable

in cases where the law expressly declares it.

Conspiracy exists when two or more persons agree to the execution of the crime.

Proposal is configured when one who has resolved to commit a crime proposes its execution to another or other persons.

###### ARTICULO 18. The crime is considered committed at the moment of the action or omission,

even when the moment of the result is different.

###### ARTICULO 19. The crime is considered committed:

1) In the place where the criminal activity of authors or participants was carried out, in whole or in part.

2) In the place where the result occurred or should have occurred.

3) In omissive crimes, in the place where the omitted action should have been executed.

###### ARTICULO 20. To one who by error or by any other accident commits a crime to the

detriment of a person other than the one against whom he has directed his action, the crime shall be imputed, but not the aggravating circumstances that proceed from the offended party or from links with him. The attenuating circumstances that would arise from the fact if it had been perpetrated to the harm of the other person shall be appreciated in his favor.

###### ARTICULO 21. There is no crime if, on the occasion of performing a lawful act with due

diligence, the author causes harm by mere accident.

#### TITULO III

CAUSES THAT EXEMPT FROM LIABILITY

###### ARTICULO 22. The causes exempting from criminal liability are of three classes,

namely:

- 1) Causes of non-imputability.
- 2) Causes of justification.
- 3) Causes of non-culpability.

##### CAPITULO I

CAUSES OF NON-IMPUTABILITY

###### ARTICULO 23. Not imputable:

1) The minor under twelve (12) years. Both this one and the one over that age but under eighteen (18) years shall be subject to a special law; and,

2) One who at the moment of the action or omission suffers from psychosis, severe mental retardation or transitory psychosis and lacks, therefore, the capacity to understand the illicit character of the act or to determine himself in accordance with that understanding, unless the transitory mental disorder has been provoked by the agent willfully or negligently.

##### CAPITULO II

CAUSES OF JUSTIFICATION

###### ARTICULO 24. Is exempt from criminal liability:

1) One who acts in defense of his person or rights provided that the following circumstances concur:

- a) Unlawful aggression;
- b) Rational necessity of the means employed to prevent or repel it; and,
- c) Lack of sufficient provocation on the part of the one who defends himself.

It shall be understood that the first two circumstances concur with respect to one who during the night rejects the climbing or breaking of the fences, walls or entrances of an inhabited house or apartment, or of its dependencies, or employs violence against the individual foreign to it who is surprised within the indicated places. If the acts occur during the day, it shall only be understood that unlawful aggression concurs.

2) One who acts in defense of the person or rights of his spouse or of his relatives up to the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity, or of relatives by adoption in the same degrees, provided that the circumstances provided for in the preceding literals a) and b) concur, and that, in case sufficient provocation by the defended party has preceded, the defender has not participated in it;

3) One who acts in defense of the person or rights of a stranger, provided that the circumstances provided for in the preceding numeral concur and that the defender is not impelled by revenge, resentment or other illegitimate motive;

4) One who has committed an act compelled by the necessity of saving himself or saving another or others from a danger not caused by him voluntarily nor avoidable in another manner, provided that the act is proportionate to the danger.

This exemption extends to one who has caused damage to another's property, if the following conditions concur:

- a) Reality of the harm that is sought to be avoided;
- b) That said harm be greater than that caused to avoid it; and,
- c) That there be no other practicable and less harmful means to prevent it.

One who had the duty to face the danger cannot allege state of necessity.

5) One who acts in fulfillment of a duty or in the legitimate exercise of a right, office or position.

It shall be understood that this last circumstance exists with respect to the authority or its agents and the persons who concur in its assistance, who in case of disobedience or resistance or to prevent the escape of a delinquent employ proportionate means of repression, provided that formal warning precedes;

6) One who executes an act by legitimate obedience, provided that:

- a) The order emanates from competent authority;
- b) The agent has the obligation to comply with it; and,
- c) The action or omission ordered does not violate or restrict the exercise of the rights and guarantees enshrined in the Constitution of the Republic and in the international treaties on human rights to which Honduras is a party.

##### CAPITULO III

CAUSES OF NON-CULPABILITY

###### ARTICULO 25. Neither do they incur criminal liability:

1) One who acts impelled by irresistible physical force or insurmountable fear.

2) One who incurs in some omission, being impeded by legitimate or insurmountable cause.

3) One who in the rational belief that there exists an unjust aggression against his person, reacts against the supposed aggressor, provided that the reaction is proportionate to the supposed risk.

#### TITULO IV

CIRCUMSTANCES THAT MODIFY CRIMINAL LIABILITY

##### CAPITULO I

ATTENUATING CIRCUMSTANCES

###### ARTICULO 26. Attenuating circumstances are:

1) Those expressed in the preceding Title, when not all the necessary requisites to exempt from liability in their respective cases concur.

2) The guilty party being under twenty-one years and over seventy.

3) Executing the act in a state of intoxication, when this is not habitual or subsequent to the plan to commit the crime, provided that these situations are scientifically proven.

4) Having immediately preceded, on the part of the offended party, provocation or threat proportionate to the crime.

5) Having executed the act in proximate vindication of a grave offense, caused to the author of the crime, to his spouse or person with whom he leads a marital life, his ascendants, descendants, siblings or relatives by affinity up to the second degree.

6) Acting by stimuli so powerful that they have naturally produced outburst or obsession.

7) The guilty party having sought, with effective means, to repair the harm caused or prevent its pernicious consequences.

8) If, being able to elude the action of justice by flight or other suitable means, the accused has presented himself voluntarily to the competent authority.

9) There being no other direct proof in the process than the confession of the accused.

10) Having proceeded impelled by collective or tumultuous suggestion, provided that the guilty party has not provoked it nor acted in it as director of the group.

11) The woman having acted under the influence of physiological disorders proper to her sex.

12) Having acted by noble, altruistic or merciful motives.

13) The delinquent not having had the intention to cause harm of as much gravity as that which he produced.

14) Any other circumstance analogous to the preceding ones.

##### CAPITULO II

AGGRAVATING CIRCUMSTANCES

###### ARTICLE 27. Aggravating circumstances are:

1) Acting for trivial or abject motives.

2) Executing the crime with treachery.

There is treachery when the guilty party commits any of the crimes against life and bodily integrity, employing means, modes or forms in the execution that tend directly and especially to ensure it, without risk to his person that may arise from the defense that the victim could make.

3) Committing the crime by means of price, reward or remunerative promise.

4) Executing it by means of flooding, fire, poisons, explosion, derailment, beaching or damage to a vessel or other device that may cause great havoc.

5) Deliberately increasing the seriousness of the crime, causing other harm unnecessary for its execution.

6) Acting with known premeditation or employing cunning, fraud or disguise

7) Abusing superiority or employing means that weaken the defense.

8) Acting with abuse of trust.

9) Taking advantage of the public character held by the guilty party.

10) Employing means or causing circumstances to concur that add ignominy to the proper effects of the act.

11) Committing the crime on the occasion of fire, shipwreck, or other calamity or misfortune.

12) Executing it with the assistance of armed people or persons who ensure or provide impunity.

13) Executing the act in an uninhabited place or taking advantage of the darkness of night.

The Courts may not take this circumstance into consideration according to the nature and incidents of the crime.

14) Committing the act with contempt or offense to public authority, or in its presence, or where it exercises its functions.

15) Executing it in a place that deserves respect or reverence or in the dwelling of the victim, when the latter has not provoked the event.

16) Executing the act with offense or contempt of the respect that the victim deserves by reason of dignity, age or sex.

17) Executing it with scaling.

18) Executing the act with breaking of wall, ceiling or pavement, or with fracture of doors or windows.

19) Executing it in a gang.

20) Deliberately getting drunk or intoxicated to prepare or execute the crime, provided that this situation is scientifically proven.

21) Committing it using an automobile, vessel or aircraft, or any other similar means of sufficient efficacy to ensure the aggression or the escape.

22) Executing the crime by previously interrupting the means of communication, or after cutting or interrupting the exterior or interior electrical service, the elevator service at the place of the event of any of those that the guilty party is to use.

23) The aggrieved party and the offender being linked by marriage or common-law union, or being ascendants and descendants to each other by consanguinity, or collaterals up to the fourth degree; by ties of adoption, or by affinity up to the second degree.

This circumstance may not be taken into consideration by the Courts, or be assessed as a mitigating factor, according to the nature, incidents and effects of the crime.

24) The violation of special duties that relationships of respect, friendship, gratitude, dependence or hospitality impose on the accused with respect to the victim.

25) Being a repeat offender.

26) Taking advantage of non-imputable subjects for the commission of the crime.

##### CHAPTER III

RECIDIVISM AND HABITUAL OFFENDING

###### ARTICLE 28. A repeat offender is one who commits a new crime before the expiration of

five years from the conviction for a previous crime committed in the country or abroad, whether the sentence has been served or not. The time in which the agent remains deprived of liberty, by preventive detention or by the sentence, shall not be computed in the indicated period of five years.

###### ARTICLE 29. A habitual offender is considered to be one who, having been convicted of

two or more previous crimes, committed in the country or abroad, whether the sentences have been served or not, manifests a definite tendency toward crime, in the opinion of the court, by the kind of life led, inclination to idleness, inferiority of the environment in which he acts, relationships he cultivates, motives of the crime, and other antecedents of a similar nature.

###### ARTICLE 30.

There is no recidivism or habitual offending between intentional and negligent crime; between common and military crime; between common and political crime; between military and political crime; and, between crime and misdemeanor.

#### TITLE V

CONCURRENCE OF OFFENDERS AND CRIMES

##### CHAPTER I

PARTICIPATION IN THE CRIME

###### ARTICLE 31. The perpetrators and accomplices are criminally responsible for the crime.

###### ARTICLE 32. Perpetrators are considered to be those who take direct part in the execution of the

act, those who force or directly induce others to execute it and those who cooperate in the execution of the act by an act without which it would not have been carried out.

In crimes by omission, perpetrators are those who fail to do what the law commands, cause the omission or cooperate in it.

###### ARTICLE 33. Accomplices are those who, not being included in the Article

above, cooperate in the execution of the act by prior or simultaneous acts. If from the particular circumstances of the proceedings it should result that the accused of complicity only wished to cooperate in an act less serious than that committed by the perpetrator, the penalty shall be applied to the accomplice only by reason of the act he intended to execute.

###### ARTICLE 34. When dealing with crimes committed by a crowd,

the following provisions shall apply:

- 1) If the purpose of the gathering was to commit certain crimes, all those who have materially participated in their execution shall be liable as perpetrators, as well as those who, without having had material participation, assume the character of directors.
- 2) If the purpose of the gathering was not to commit crimes and these are committed afterwards by impulse of the crowd in tumult, all those who have materially participated in the execution shall be liable as accomplices; and as perpetrators, those who have the character of instigators, whether or not they have had material participation in the execution of the criminal acts. Those who participate in the crowd and are not perpetrators or accomplices shall be exempt from punishment.

###### ARTICLE 34-A. For crimes committed in the name and on behalf of a legal

person, the legal representatives thereof who have made the unlawful act or omission possible shall be personally liable.

Civil liability, however, shall fall on the legal person.

##### CHAPTER II

CONCURRENCE OF CRIMES

###### ARTICLE 35. A person guilty of two or more crimes shall be imposed all the penalties

corresponding to the various offenses.

The convicted person shall serve his sentences simultaneously, if possible. When this is not possible, or if any of the sentences thereby becomes illusory, he shall serve them successively, beginning with the greatest, that is, those designated for the most serious crimes. However, the duration of the accumulated sentences for several crimes shall not exceed thirty years (sic).

###### ARTICLE 36. The provisions of the preceding article shall not apply in the case that

a single act constitutes two or more crimes or when one of them is a necessary means to commit the other.

In these cases, the penalty corresponding to the crime that has the greater sanction designated shall be imposed, increased by one fourth.

###### ARTICULO 37. Cuando se cometa un mismo delito contra la propiedad dos o más veces,

bien sea en un solo momento o en momentos diversos, mediante acciones u omisiones ejecutadas en cumplimiento de un plan preconcebido o aprovechando idénticas o similares circunstancias, dichos delitos se considerarán como un (sic) solo continuado. En tal situación se aplicará al agente la pena más grave, aumentada en dos tercios. En caso de duda, se tendrá por más grave la pena que tenga señalado el máximo más alto. Lo dispuesto en el párrafo anterior no se aplicará si le resulta más favorable al reo la imposición de todas las penas aplicables a los delitos concurrentes.

Lo prescrito en la última parte del segundo párrafo de este Artículo y en el párrafo anterior, será aplicable a lo estatuido en el artículo 36.

#### TITULO VI

PENALTIES

##### CAPITULO I

CLASSES OF PENALTIES

###### ARTICULO 38. Las penas se dividen en principales y accesorias:

Principal penalties are: imprisonment, confinement, fine, absolute disqualification and special disqualification.

Accessory penalties are: civil interdiction and confiscation.

Absolute or special disqualification shall be imposed as an accessory penalty to imprisonment, provided that the law does not impose it as a principal penalty for a particular offense.

##### CAPITULO II

DURATION, NATURE AND EFFECTS OF PENALTIES

###### ARTICULO 39. La pena de reclusión sujeta al reo a trabajar por el tiempo de la condena

in public works or in labor within the establishment, in accordance with the law that regulates the penitentiary system.

###### ARTICULO 40. Cuando la reclusión excediere de tres años, se cumplirá en una

national penitentiary; and when it does not exceed said period, it must be served in departmental or sectional jails, without prejudice to what the following article provides.

###### ARTICULO 41. El Poder Ejecutivo, siempre que lo crea conveniente por razón de mayor

security or for any other reason may, with knowledge of the Supreme Court of Justice, order that convicts sentenced to imprisonment who are serving their sentences in other jails be transferred to a penitentiary.

###### ARTICULO 42. Si la reclusión no excede de seis meses, podrán ser detenidas en sus

own homes women of good reputation and persons over seventy years of age or infirm. The same treatment shall be given to these persons if the applicable penalty were confinement.

###### ARTICULO 43. Las mujeres y los varones menores de veintiún años y mayores de

eighteen shall serve the penalty of imprisonment in special establishments; and, if there are none, in separate and independent sections (sic), where they shall perform work appropriate to their condition.

###### ARTICULO 44. Quedan exentos de la obligación de realizar los trabajos de la manera

set forth in Article 39:

- 1) Convicts who have reached seventy years of age.
- 2) Convicts who have a physical impediment or suffer from an illness that makes work impossible or dangerous for them, in accordance with the corresponding medical opinion.

###### ARTICULO 45. Cuando los centros penales no estén convenientemente acondicionados,

the custodial sentence of women who are pregnant shall not be executed until six (6) months after childbirth has occurred. If the infant dies, the custodial sentence shall begin to be served four (4) weeks after childbirth or miscarriage. In these cases, and in the case of preventive detention, the provisions of

###### artículo 42.

###### ARTICULO 46. A los reos por delitos políticos se les mantendrá separados de los reos

for common offenses.

###### ARTICULO 47. La pena de prisión sujeta al penado a la privación de su libertad en

local jails, with the obligation to work in labor within the establishment.

###### ARTICULO 48. La inhabilitación absoluta se entiende para cargos u oficios públicos,

political rights and licensed professions during the duration of the sentence and produces:

- 1) The deprivation of all public offices or positions and exercise of licensed professions that the convicted person holds, even when the offices are by popular election.
- 2) The deprivation of all political rights and the incapacity to obtain them.
- 3) The incapacity to obtain the mentioned public offices or positions, professions and rights.

###### ARTICULO 49. La pena de inhabilitación especial se entiende para un determinado cargo

or public position, political right or licensed profession for the duration of the sentence and produces:

- 1) The deprivation of the office, position, right or exercise of the profession to which it applies.
- 2) The incapacity to obtain said office, position, right, profession or other analogous ones.

###### ARTICULO 50. (DEROGADO)

###### ARTICULO 51. La pena de multa obliga al reo a pagar al Estado, la suma de dinero que

this Code or special laws determine or that the judge sets in each case within the legal limits, taking into account the economic capacity of the convicted person and the severity of the damage caused by the offense.

Fines shall be made effective at the General Treasury of the Republic or at the Institution of the National Financial System that the Secretariat of State in the Office of Finance has authorized for such purpose.

###### ARTICULO 52. Previo otorgamiento de caución real o personal, podrá autorizarse el pago

of the fine in installments, the amount and payment date of which shall be set by the judge, taking into account the economic conditions of the sentenced person.

###### ARTICULO 53. Si no se paga total o parcialmente la multa penal, ya sea en forma

voluntary form or by way of compulsion, it shall be commuted to confinement at a rate of one (1) day for every five Lempiras (L.5.00) when it corresponds to a petty offense, or to imprisonment at a rate of one (1) day for every ten Lempiras (L.10.00) per day when it corresponds to a crime.

The confinement commuted according to the preceding paragraph may not exceed six (6) months and imprisonment five (5) years. The convicted person, however, may pay the fine that has been imposed on him, in which case his release shall be decreed. The value corresponding to the time that the convict has been detained shall be deducted from the fine, at a rate of five Lempiras (L.5.00) per day when the fine corresponds to a petty offense and ten Lempiras (L.10.00) per day when it corresponds to a crime.

The imprisonment and confinement to which this Article refers shall not give rise to the application of accessory penalties nor to the provision of services in public works or other kinds of labor inside or outside the penal establishment.

Payment of the maximum fine shall extinguish any liability for the quantifiable excess thereof.

###### ARTICULO 54. La interdicción civil consiste en la suspensión de los derechos de patria

authority, guardianship, custody and administration of property; but the interdicted person may dispose of his own by will.

###### ARTICULO 55. El comiso consiste en la pérdida de los efectos que provengan de un

crime or petty offense, and of the instruments with which it is executed, unless they belong to a third party not responsible for the act.

###### ARTICULO 56. En todos los casos que procediere imponer el pago de costas, se

procedural and personal costs shall be included and, in addition, the expenses caused by the trial and not included in the costs, which shall be assessed in the same manner as those.

###### ARTICULO 57. Si los bienes del culpable no fueren bastantes para cubrir las

pecuniary liabilities, these shall be satisfied in the following order:

- 1) Procedural and personal costs.
- 2) The expenses caused by the trial.
- 3) Compensation for damages and losses.
- 4) The fine.

In case of insolvency proceedings or bankruptcy, these claims shall be ranked being considered as one (sic) among those that do not enjoy preference.

###### ARTICLE 58. Sentences of imprisonment and incarceration shall begin to be counted from the day

on which the convict is apprehended, discounting the time that they remain released. Absolute and special disqualification, as principal penalties, shall be counted from the declaration of guilt or of having been declared with grounds for prosecution.

###### ARTICLE 59. The following shall not be considered as penalties:

1) Detention and preventive imprisonment of the accused.

2) The suspension from employment or public office agreed upon during the proceeding or to institute it.

3) Fines and other corrections that, in the exercise of governmental or disciplinary powers, superiors impose on their subordinates or administered persons.

4) Deprivations of rights (sic) and reparations that civil laws establish in criminal form.

###### ARTICLE 60. For the purposes of this Code, days shall be counted as twenty-four

consecutive hours, and months and years from date to date.

###### ARTICLE 61. Only the following shall be commutable by right:

1) Imprisonment at the rate of five Lempiras (L.5.00) per day; and,

2) Incarceration of up to five (5) years, at the rate of ten Lempiras (L.10.00) per day. Commutations shall be paid in cash at the General Treasury of the Republic or at the institution of the national financial system that the Secretariat of State for Finance has authorized for such purpose.

Commutation shall not proceed in case of recidivism.

##### CHAPTER III

PENALTIES THAT CARRY WITH THEM OTHER ACCESSORY PENALTIES

###### ARTICLE 62. Incarceration for more than five years carries as accessory penalties the

absolute disqualification for the duration of the sentence and civil interdiction.

###### ARTICLE 63. The penalty of incarceration that does not exceed five years carries as

accessory penalties those of special disqualification and civil interdiction.

###### ARTICLE 64. The conviction sentence shall include confiscation and payment of costs

in cases where it is applicable.

##### CHAPTER IV

APPLICATION OF PENALTIES

###### ARTICLE 65. Whenever the law generally establishes the penalty for an offense, it

shall be understood that it is imposed on the perpetrator of a consummated offense.

###### ARTICLE 66. The perpetrator of an attempt and the accomplice of a consummated offense, shall

be sanctioned with the penalty applicable to the perpetrator of the consummated offense reduced by one-third. In the case that the offense is punishable by life imprisonment, they shall be given twenty (20) to thirty (30) years of incarceration.

###### ARTICLE 67. The accomplice to an attempt shall have four to five sixths of the

penalty applicable to the perpetrator of a consummated offense reduced.

In the case that the offense is punishable by life imprisonment, the accomplice to an attempt shall be given the penalty of twenty (20) to thirty (30) years of incarceration.

###### ARTICLE 68. When this Code provides that a penalty be increased or decreased by

a specific quota or fraction, the maximum and minimum shall be increased or decreased, as the case may be, in the corresponding proportion, thus establishing the new penalty, within whose limits its application shall be graduated according to the following Article.

###### ARTICLE 69. The judge shall determine in the sentence the penalty applicable to the accused within

the maximum and minimum established by law for each offense and the circumstances in which it was committed. To this end, they shall take into account their personal background, their greater or lesser dangerousness, the mitigating and aggravating circumstances that have occurred in the act assessed both by their number and, above all, by their magnitude and importance, and the greater or lesser extent of the harms produced by the offense, particularly those of an economic nature.

In the reasoning of the sentence, the judge shall expressly state the circumstances indicated in the preceding paragraph that they have taken into account to determine the extent of the penalty.

##### CHAPTER V

CONDITIONAL SUSPENSION OF EXECUTION OF THE PENALTY

###### ARTICLE 70. In the conviction sentence, the courts may suspend the

execution of the penalty, for a probation period of five years, in the case of offenses, and two years, in the case of misdemeanors, if the following requirements are met:

- 1) That the sentence consist of deprivation of liberty not exceeding three years.
- 2) That the accused has not been previously convicted of an offense or misdemeanor.
- 3) That the nature or modalities of the criminal act charged, the character or background of the convict and the motives that drove them to commit the offense, duly investigated, lead the Judge to the conviction that the agent is not dangerous and may presume, consequently, that they will not reoffend.

###### ARTICLE 71. The benefit established in the preceding Article shall not be granted to

those who, according to the rules given in this Code, must be subjected to security measures.

###### ARTICLE 72. The conditional suspension of execution of the penalty shall not extend to

accessory penalties and other effects of the conviction. Nor shall it exempt from civil obligations arising from the offense.

###### ARTICLE 73. The judge of the case shall make to the convict, personally, the necessary

warnings about the nature of the benefit granted and the reasons that may cause its termination, which shall be recorded in the file by certificate.

###### ARTICLE 74. The conditionally suspended penalty shall be enforced, when the

convicted person within the established time periods:

- 1) Reoffends or fails to fulfill the civil obligations arising from the offense or misdemeanor.
- 2) Is imposed another conviction for an offense or misdemeanor committed prior to that which was the subject of the penalty suspension.

###### ARTICLE 75. If during the probation period, the offender does not incur the

acts dealt with in the preceding article, the conviction shall be deemed extinguished by resolution of the sentencing Court.

##### CHAPTER VI

CONDITIONAL RELEASE

###### ARTICLE 76. The Court of first instance that heard the case, may grant

conditional release to the convict who has served more than half of the sentence, in cases of conviction to incarceration exceeding three years and not exceeding twelve; or who has served three-quarters of the sentence, when it exceeds twelve years, and in addition, in both cases, the following circumstances concur:

- 1) That the convict has not been definitively convicted previously, for another willful offense.
- 2) Having observed good conduct during their stay in the penal establishment and having acquired habits of work, order and morality, that demonstrate their repentance and purpose of reform.
- 3) That they have restored the thing and repaired the damage in cases of offenses against property and fulfilled the other civil obligations arising from the offense, or demonstrate their economic inability to satisfy the latter.

###### ARTICLE 77.

In the resolution granting the benefit of conditional release, the Judge may impose any of the security measures indicated in subsections d),

e) and f) of Article 83.

###### ARTICLE 78. The probation period to which the person enjoying the benefit of

conditional release shall be subject will be equal to that which remains to complete the imposed sentence.

If during the probation period they commit a new offense or violate the imposed security measures, the conditional release shall be revoked and the part of the sentence that remains to be served shall be enforced, without counting in it the time that they have remained at liberty.

###### ARTICLE 79. Once the probation period has elapsed without the beneficiary having incurred

the acts that give rise to the revocation of conditional release, the penalty shall be deemed extinguished.

#### TITLE VII

SECURITY MEASURES

###### ARTICLE 80. Security measures shall not be decreed without legal provision that

authorizes them, or outside the cases that the law determines.

###### ARTICULO 81. Security measures may be decreed by the Judge in the sentence,

whether conviction or acquittal, but at any moment of the process, before the ruling, said official may order, on a provisional basis, the internment of the non-imputable person covered in numeral 2 of Article 23, in the corresponding establishment.

###### ARTICULO 82. Unless otherwise provided by law, security measures shall be applied

indefinitely. At any time judges may reform or revoke their resolutions in this regard, if the state of dangerousness of the accused is modified or ceases.

###### ARTICULO 83. The security measures that may be applied are the following:

1) Internment in psychiatric establishment.

2) Internment in work institution or penal farm.

3) Internment in reeducational or special treatment establishment.

4) Supervised liberty.

5) Prohibition to reside in a determined place.

6) Prohibition to attend certain places.

7) Good conduct bond.

8) Expulsion of foreigners.

When the measures comprised in numerals 4), 5) and 7) are applied, the sanctioned person shall be obligated to declare before the Judge who knows of the matter, his current domicile and inform the changes that said domicile may have.

###### ARTICULO 84. Judges who declare defendants exempt from penalty, in the cases

of numerals 2 and 3 of Article 23, shall order their internment in a psychiatric establishment, for at least one year.

###### ARTICULO 85. They may also order, after the penalty has been served if they still

deem the offender dangerous, that the deaf-mute or the person suffering from mental abnormality which does not result in absolute non-imputability, be interned in an educational or special treatment establishment.

###### ARTICULO 86. The delinquents referred to in Article 29 shall be subjected, according to the

degree of dangerousness they demonstrate, to the work regime that corresponds to the institutions mentioned in numeral 2 of Article 83; internment that shall be decreed when, once the sentence is served, the Judge deems that the penalty has been ineffective regarding the rehabilitation of the delinquent.

###### ARTICULO 87. In the cases of Article 16, the Judge shall subject the accused, according

to their degree of dangerousness, to special work regime in one of the institutions mentioned in numeral 3 of Article 83.

###### ARTICULO 88. Where there is no adequate establishment, the internment measure

according to its nature, shall be complied with in an annex or special section of a penal establishment.

###### ARTICULO 89. The internment measure shall not cease, except by virtue of judicial resolution

issued with hearing of the Public Ministry, and prior medical opinion, demonstrating that the defendant can be subjected to supervised liberty without danger of causing harm.

###### ARTICULO 90. Supervised liberty while the causes that motivated it last, shall consist

for the mentally ill, drug addicts or habitual drunkards, in entrusting them to the care of their family or a guardian, under the immediate inspection of the competent authority, with the obligation to subject them to medical treatment and to report periodically to the respective judge.

In the other cases the surveillance shall correspond to the judicial police in the manner that the judge provides.

When applying this measure, the judge shall prescribe the rules of behavior destined to avoid new infractions of the penal law. The judicial police shall be organized and shall be under the dependence of the Supreme Court of Justice.

###### ARTICULO 91.

When circumstances require it, the judge may, at his prudent discretion, impose on the convicted person who has served a penalty or a security measure, the prohibition to reside in a determined place, for at least one year.

###### ARTICULO 92. The prohibition to attend certain places shall be imposed on the

person convicted of crime under the influence of alcohol or toxic, enervating, or stupefacient drugs. These prohibitions shall last at least one year and their contravention shall oblige that it be substituted by supervised liberty.

###### ARTICULO 93. The good conduct bond consists in the personal, mortgage,

pawn or depository guarantee, provided to the satisfaction of the judge and for the term indicated la (sic) sentence, that the dangerous subject will not perpetrate new punishable acts and that he will comply with the conduct norms imposed on him during a probation period, which shall not be less than one year nor more than five.

This measure shall be applied in all cases in which the judge deems it opportune, especially to the authors of danger crimes, without prejudice to the penalty when applicable. The bond shall be made effective in favor of the Treasury when the person subjected to this measure violates the imposed conduct norms; otherwise, at the end of the term, the restitution of the deposited sum, the extinction of the bond or the cancellation of the pawn or mortgage obligation that has been constituted shall be ordered.

###### ARTICULO 94. The judge who imposes a penalty of more than three years of imprisonment on a

foreigner, or when the latter is a repeat offender, whatever the penalty may be, may decree his expulsion from the national territory, in accordance with the law, which shall be executed once the penalty is served.

###### ARTICULO 95. The imposition of security measures shall not prevent the administrative

expulsion of the foreigner in the cases provided by Law.

#### TITULO VIII

EXTINCTION OF PENAL RESPONSIBILITY AND ITS EFFECTS

###### ARTICULO 96. Penal responsibility is extinguished:

1. By the death of the defendant;

2. By the fulfillment of the sentence, which produces by right the rehabilitation of the convicted person;

3. By amnesty, which extinguishes the penalty and completely all its legal effects, except as provided in Article 103;

4. By pardon, which only extinguishes the principal penalty when it does not include the accessory ones; but does not favor the pardoned person regarding recidivism and other effects of the penalties that the laws expressly determine.

The pardoned person may not inhabit, without the consent of the offended party, the domicile where the latter lives, or, in his absence, his spouse, ascendants, descendants and siblings. This prohibition shall last for all the time that, had he not been pardoned, the sentence should last;

5. By the express pardon of the offended party or of whoever has his legal representation in crimes that are only prosecutable by virtue of complaint or denunciation of the aggrieved party. The provisions of this paragraph shall not be applicable to those responsible for the crime of rape nor to those responsible for crimes or misdemeanors committed to the detriment of boys or girls.

In crimes or misdemeanors committed against incapable persons not included in the last part of the preceding paragraph, judges or tribunals shall reject the efficacy of the pardon granted by the representatives of those persons if there are rational motives to think that it was granted considering pecuniary or direct personal interests of the respective representative. In such case, they shall order the continuation of the procedure with intervention of the Public Ministry or the fulfillment of the sentence.

To reject the pardon referred to in the preceding paragraph, the judge or tribunal must hear the representative of the incapable person.

6. By the prescription of the penal action; and,

7. By the prescription of the penalty.

Notwithstanding, the provisions in numerals 6) and 7) of this Article, the penal action and the penalty are imprescriptible in those crimes for which deprivation of liberty for life is applicable or which is established as its maximum limit.

###### ARTICLE 97. The criminal action prescribes:

1) Upon the passage of a period equal to the maximum duration of the sanction established for the crime, increased by half, if it were imprisonment.

However, the prescription period shall not be, in any case, less than two (2) years;

2) At five (5) years when dealing with an act for which disqualification is established as the principal penalty.

3) In three (3) years, when a fine is imposed as the principal penalty; and

4) At six (6) months, if dealing with misdemeanors.

The preceding rules are understood without prejudice to those established by the Constitution of the Republic.

###### ARTICLE 98. The prescription of the criminal action shall begin to run from the day on

which the offense was committed; and in continuing crimes, from the day on which the last act was executed or the last action was performed.

In the case of attempt, the prescription shall be counted from the day on which its execution was suspended.

The prescription of the criminal action in the crime of bankruptcy shall run from the day on which the declaration of fraudulent or culpable insolvency became final.

###### ARTICLE 99. The prescription of the criminal action shall be interrupted from the moment

proceedings are initiated against the guilty party, the prescription period running anew from the moment its prosecution is paralyzed for any circumstance.

###### ARTICLE 100. Penalties imposed by final judgment prescribe in the terms indicated in

Article 97.

The time of this prescription shall be counted from the date on which the judgment becomes final, or from the day of the breach of sentence, as applicable.

###### ARTICLE 101. The prescription of the penalty shall be interrupted, rendering the elapsed time

without effect, when the offender commits another crime, without prejudice to it beginning to run anew.

###### ARTICLE 102. The exercise of the action to claim civil liabilities derived from the crime does

not interrupt its prescription, that of the criminal action or of the penalty.

###### ARTICLE 103. Amnesty and pardon do not extinguish the right to compensation for damage

caused by the crime.

###### ARTICLE 104. When the offender appears or is apprehended after half the time necessary

for the criminal action or penalty to prescribe has elapsed, the Judge must take into account said period to make a reduction of one third to one half in the penalty that corresponds to be applied or in that imposed by the judgment.

This reduction shall not apply to prescriptions that do not exceed one year.

#### TITLE IX

CIVIL LIABILITY

###### ARTICLE 105. Anyone who incurs criminal liability for a crime or misdemeanor is also

liable civilly.

###### ARTICLE 106. The exemption from criminal liability declared in numerals 2 and 3 of

Article 23, in numeral 2 of Article 24 and in numeral 1 of Article 25, does not include civil liability, which shall be made effective subject to the following rules:

- 1) In cases of non-imputability mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the mentally ill and mentally deficient or deaf-mutes are liable with their property for the damages they cause. If they are insolvent, those who have them under their authority or legal custody shall be subsidiarily liable, unless they demonstrate their lack of culpability.

In both situations there shall be entitlement to the benefit of competence.

2) In the case of numeral 2 of Article 24, the persons in whose favor the harm has been prevented are liable in proportion to the benefit they have derived.

The courts shall determine, according to their prudent discretion, the proportional share for which each interested party must be liable.

3) In the case of numeral 1 of Article 25, those who have caused the fear or exercised the force shall be liable.

###### ARTICLE 107. Civil liability includes:

1) Restitution.

2) Reparation of material and moral damages.

3) Compensation for losses.

###### ARTICLE 108. Restitution must be made of the same thing, whenever possible, with

payment for deterioration or impairment at the judgment of the Court and even if the thing is in the possession of a third party who has acquired it legally, saving their right to recover against the appropriate party. This last provision is not applicable to a third party who has acquired the thing in the manner and with the requirements established by law to make it non-recoverable.

###### ARTICLE 109. Reparation of material damage shall be made through monetary

compensation that shall be fixed by valuing the extent of all patrimonial damages caused by the punishable act or omission, considering the price of the thing, and whenever possible, the sentimental value it has had for the aggrieved party; and it shall only be required when there is no place for restitution.

###### ARTICLE 110. Reparation of moral damage, in offenses against honor, dignity or honesty,

or in other cases of damages to interests of a moral nature, shall consist of monetary compensation that the Judge shall determine prudently according to the circumstances of the offense, the conditions of the offended person and the nature and consequences that have occurred or are possible from the injury suffered.

###### ARTICLE 111. Compensation for losses shall include, not only those that have been caused

to the offended party, but also those that have been inflicted, by reason of the crime, on their family or on a third party. The Court shall regulate the amount of this compensation in the same terms established in Articles 109 and 110 for the reparation of damage.

###### ARTICLE 112. The obligation to restitute, repair damages and compensate for losses is

transmitted to the heirs of the responsible party.

The action to request restitution, reparation and compensation is likewise transmitted to the heirs of the injured party.

###### ARTICLE 113. In case there are two or more persons who must respond civilly for a crime,

the judgment must indicate the share of liability of each one. The principals and accomplices, however, each within their respective class, shall be jointly and severally liable among themselves for their shares and subsidiarily for those corresponding to those of the other class.

Subsidiary liability shall be made effective, first on the property of the principals and then on that of the accomplices.

Both in cases in which joint and several liability as well as subsidiary liability is made effective, the right to recover of the one who has paid for another shall remain preserved.

###### ARTICLE 114. Whoever, without having participated in any crime, has obtained from it

any economic benefit, is obligated to return the amount by which they have profited.

###### ARTICLE 115. Civil liability for crime is extinguished according to the modes established

by the Civil Code with respect to obligations of this nature.

#### TITLE I

### CRIMES AGAINST LIFE AND BODILY INTEGRITY

##### CHAPTER I

HOMICIDE

###### ARTICLE 116. Whoever causes the death of a person without the circumstances mentioned

in the following Articles of this Chapter being present, commits the crime of simple homicide, and shall incur the penalty of fifteen (15) to twenty (20) years of imprisonment.

The penalty shall be twenty (20) to thirty (30) years of imprisonment, when the victim of the crime is a judicial or police authority, member of the Public Ministry, official or employee of Penal Centers, when the crime is committed on the occasion of or in the exercise of their position or function.

###### ARTICLE 117. Is guilty of murder, whoever causes the death of a person executing it with the

concurrence of any of the following circumstances:

- 1) Treachery;
- 2) With known premeditation;
- 3) By means of flooding, fire, poisoning, explosion, derailment, overturning, stranding or shipwreck or other device that may cause great havoc, provided there is malice and intentionality; and,
- 4) With cruelty, deliberately and inhumanly increasing the pain of the victim.

The penalty for murder shall be twenty (20) to thirty (30) years of imprisonment and when committed by payment, reward or promise of remuneration, or accompanied by robbery or rape, the penalty shall be thirty (30) years to lifelong deprivation of liberty.

###### ARTICLE 118. Is guilty of parricide, whoever causes the death of any of their ascendants or

descendants, their spouse or the person with whom they live as a couple, and shall suffer the penalty of thirty (30) to forty (40) years of imprisonment.

###### ARTICLE 119. If death has been produced while several persons were fighting among

themselves, confusedly and tumultuously without being able to determine the cause of the injuries of mortal effect, there shall be imposed, on all those who have exercised violence upon the victim, three to six years of imprisonment.

###### ARTICLE 120. Whoever with the purpose of causing injuries to a person produces their

death when the means employed should not reasonably have caused it, shall be sanctioned with the penalty applicable to simple homicide reduced by one third.

###### ARTICLE 121. The perpetrator of a culpable homicide shall be punished with three (3) to five (5)

years.

If the perpetrator had committed the act as a consequence of being in a state of intoxication or having consumed drugs prohibited by law, they shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years.

###### ARTICLE 122. (REPEALED)

###### ARTICLE 123. The mother who to conceal her dishonor causes the death of her child who has not

completed three (3) days since birth, shall be sanctioned with six (6) to nine (9) years of imprisonment.

###### ARTICLE 124. Whoever attempts suicide, shall be imposed a security measure

consisting of adequate psychiatric treatment.

###### ARTICLE 125.

Whoever induces another to commit suicide or provides assistance to do so, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to six years, if the suicide is consummated. In the event that the suicide is not consummated, the collaborator in the attempt thereof shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one to three years.

##### CHAPTER II

ABORTION

###### ARTICLE 126. Abortion is the death of a human being at any moment of

pregnancy or during childbirth. Whoever intentionally causes an abortion shall be punished:

- 1. With three (3) to six (6) years of imprisonment if the woman had consented;
- 2. With six (6) to eight (8) years of imprisonment if the agent acts without the consent of the mother and without employing violence or intimidation;
- 3. With eight (8) to ten (10) years of imprisonment if the agent employs violence, intimidation or deceit.

###### ARTICLE 127. The penalties indicated in the preceding article and a fine

of fifteen thousand (L.15,000.00) to thirty thousand (L.30,000.00) Lempiras shall be imposed on the physician who, abusing their profession, causes or cooperates in abortion.

The same sanctions shall apply to medical practitioners, paramedics, nurses, midwives who commit or participate in the commission of abortion.

###### ARTICLE 128. The woman who produces her abortion or consents that another person

cause it, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

###### ARTICLE 131. (REPEALED)

###### ARTICLE 132. Whoever by acts of violence causes abortion without the purpose of

causing it, being aware of the pregnancy state of the victim, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of four (4) to six (6) years.

##### CHAPTER III

INJURIES

###### ARTICLE 133. Commits the crime of injuries whoever causes harm that affects the body or

the physical or mental health of another person.

###### ARTICLE 133-A. Whoever purposely castrates, sterilizes by deceit or by violent

means or leaves another person blind, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six (6) to ten

(10) years.

###### ARTICLE 134. The mutilation of a main limb or organ of a human being,

executed purposely, shall be punished with imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years; if it were of a non-main limb or organ, with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

###### ARTICLE 135. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment:

1) Of four (4) to eight (8) years whoever causes another person an injury that produces a mental or physical illness, certain or incurable or that permanently incapacitates them for work or causes the loss of a sense;

2) Of four (4) to seven (7) years whoever causes another person an injury that causes the loss or use of a main organ or limb, of speech or of the capacity to procreate or conceive; and,

3) Of three (3) to six (6) years if the injury produces permanent deterioration of health, of a sense, of a main organ or limb, or if it causes a permanent problem to make normal use of speech, or if it incapacitates the victim for work for more than thirty (30) days or causes a permanent deformity on the face.

###### ARTICLE 136. Shall be punished with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years whoever produces an

injury in which none of the harmful circumstances referred to in the three preceding articles concurs but which causes illness or incapacity for work for a term of not less than ten (10) days nor more than thirty (30), or that produces the loss, disablement or weakening of a non-main limb or organ or leaves a visible and permanent scar on the face.

###### ARTICLE 137. In the case of injuries caused in a tumultuous fight, without being able to

determine the author or authors thereof, there shall be applied to all those who have exercised violence on the victim a penalty reduced by one third of that indicated by law for the injuries inflicted.

###### ARTICLE 138. Culpable injuries shall be sanctioned with a penalty equal to half of

that corresponding to the intentional injury.

If the perpetrator had committed the act as a consequence of being in a state of intoxication or having consumed drugs prohibited by law, they shall be punished with two thirds of the penalty applicable to the corresponding intentional injury.

##### CHAPTER IV

ABANDONMENT OF CHILDREN AND HELPLESS PERSONS

###### ARTICLE 139. Whoever abandons a child under twelve years of age, or a person

incapable of fending for themselves, due to mental or bodily illness or old age, who is under their care or custody, shall be punished with one to three years of imprisonment. If as a consequence of the abandonment results the death of the abandoned person, or their life has been placed in serious danger, or serious injury or illness has been caused to them, the sanction shall be three to six years of imprisonment, if the act does not constitute a crime of greater gravity.

#### TITLE II

### CRIMES AGAINST SEXUAL FREEDOM AND DECENCY

##### CHAPTER I

RAPE, STATUTORY RAPE, OUTRAGE AGAINST MODESTY, ABDUCTION

###### ARTICULO 140. Carnal access with a person of either sex by means of violence or

threat of causing the victim, their spouse or domestic partner, or one of their relatives within the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity, a serious and imminent harm, constitutes the crime of rape.

Special cases of rape are carnal access with a person of either sex when any of the following circumstances exist:

- 1) That the victim is younger than fourteen (14) and older than twelve (12) years;
- 2) That the victim is deprived of reason or will or when for any cause cannot offer resistance.

The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever intentionally drugs or intoxicates a person for the purpose of raping them;

3) When the perpetrator is in charge of the guard or custody of the victim and takes advantage of their position of authority to have access to them; and,

4) When the offender impersonates another person.

The perpetrator of the crime of rape shall be punished with imprisonment of nine (9) to thirteen (13) years. If the victim is younger than twelve (12) or older than seventy (70) years or if the rape is committed by more than one person, or by a recidivist, the penalty shall be fifteen (15) to twenty (20) years.

The penalty referred to in the preceding paragraph shall also apply to those who knowingly being carriers of Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome/Human Immunodeficiency Virus (AIDS/HIV) or an incurable contagious disease, commit rape.

For the purposes of this Article, carnal access shall be understood as that which occurs vaginally, anally or orally.

###### ARTICULO 141. Whoever, taking advantage of the conditions or employing the means indicated

in the preceding Article, makes another person a victim of acts of lewdness other than carnal access, shall be punished with three (3) to five (5) years of imprisonment.

When the acts of lewdness consist of the introduction of objects or instruments of any nature into the sexual organs or other natural or artificial orifices that simulate the sexual organs of the victim's body, the offender shall be punished with nine (9) to thirteen (13) years of imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 142. Statutory rape of a woman older than fourteen (14) but younger than eighteen

(18) years, taking advantage of trust, hierarchy or authority, shall be punished with six (6) to eight (8) years of imprisonment.

When statutory rape is committed by means of deceit, it shall be punished with a penalty of four (4) to six (6) years of imprisonment.

Any other indecent abuse committed when any of the circumstances provided for in this Article exists shall be punished with a penalty of two (2) to four (4) years of imprisonment.

The penalties applicable to the indecent abuses referred to in this article and the first paragraph of the preceding one shall be increased by one third if the offended person is younger than fourteen (14) years even if they consented to the act, or if being older than that age the victim suffers from mental illness or incomplete or delayed psychic development or is deprived of reason or will or when for any cause cannot offer resistance.

###### ARTICULO 143. Whoever in a public place or a place exposed to the public

executes obscene acts or utters expressions of an analogous character commits outrage to decency. This crime shall be punished with a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand (L.10,000.00) Lempiras. Whoever publicly offers obscene theatrical, televised, cinematographic or circus shows or transmits them by radio or other analogous means, or makes, distributes or sells publications of identical character, shall be punished with a fine equal to twice the previous one.

###### ARTICULO 144. Whoever with dishonest aims and by means of force, intimidation or deceit

abducts or retains a person older than eighteen (18) years, shall incur the crime of abduction and shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

###### ARTICULO 145. The abduction of a person younger than eighteen (18) years shall be punished

with the penalty provided for in the preceding Article, increased by one third.

###### ARTICULO 147. (REPEALED)

###### ARTICULO 147-A. Whoever, taking advantage of a situation of hierarchical superiority in employment,

administrative, teaching or analogous matters, causes the victim job instability, disqualification in the performance of their work or for job promotions, or prevents them from accessing a job position; as retaliation for the rejection of indecent acts carried out through insinuations or requests for sexual favors for themselves or for a third party, shall incur the crime of sexual harassment and shall be punished with a penalty of imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years or special disqualification for the same period, when applicable, provided that the insinuations or request for sexual favors have been rejected before the person making them or have been timely brought to the knowledge of the hierarchical labor authority or the union to which the victim is affiliated.

###### ARTICULO 151. In cases of statutory rape or abduction, the offender shall be exempt from all

- 1) Provide support to the offended person and to those conceived as a consequence of the sexual relation, as applicable; and

#### TITULO III

### CRIMES AGAINST HONOR

##### CAPITULO I

SLANDER, INSULT AND DEFAMATION

###### ARTICULO 155. Slander or false imputation of a crime that gives rise to ex officio proceedings, shall be punished with imprisonment from two (2) to three (3) years. If the slandered party requests it, the operative part of the judgment declaring the slander shall be published in one of the newspapers with the largest circulation in the country, at the expense of the defendant.

###### ARTICULO 156. The person accused of the crime of slander shall be exempt from all punishment by proving the criminal act that he had imputed.

###### ARTICULO 157. Whoever utters an expression or executes an action in dishonor, discredit or contempt of another person shall be punished for insult with imprisonment from one (1) to two (2) years.

###### ARTICULO 158. The accused of insult shall not be admitted proof of the truth of the imputation, except when the offended party is a public official or employee and it concerns facts relating to the exercise of his office. In this case the accused shall be acquitted if he proves the imputation to be true.

###### ARTICULO 159. When the insults are reciprocal, the Judge may according to the circumstances, declare them not punishable with respect to both parties or one of them. The adjudicator shall have the same power when the insults are uttered in a state of anger, caused by the unjust act of another and immediately after becoming aware of it.

###### ARTICULO 160. Defamation is committed, and the guilty party shall be imposed the penalty for slander or insult, as appropriate, increased by one third, when the imputations constituting insult or slander are made in a form or by means of dissemination that may incite hatred or public contempt against the offended party.

###### ARTICULO 161. Whoever publishes or reproduces, by any means, insults or slanders inflicted by another, shall be punished as the author of the insults or slanders in question.

##### CAPITULO II

COMMON PROVISIONS

###### ARTICULO 162. The crime of slander, insult or defamation is committed not only manifestly, but also by means of allegories, caricatures, emblems or allusions.

###### ARTICULO 163. There shall be no grounds to proceed with the case for insult, slander or defamation:

1) If the accused publicly retracts before answering the complaint or in the act of doing so and the offended party accepts the retraction.

2) If in the case of concealed or equivocal slander or insults, the accused gives satisfactory explanations before answering the complaint or at the time of doing so.

###### ARTICULO 164. The owners, managers or directors of media outlets are obligated to exhibit the signature covering the original writing, or the magnetic tape or film containing the recordings or images, in whose publications slander, insult or defamation has occurred; and failing to do so, they shall be responsible for the crime in question.

###### ARTICULO 165. If the offended party requests it, the directors and failing that, the owners or managers of the media outlets in which the slander, insult or defamation has been made public, shall insert in them the retraction, satisfactory explanation or condemnatory sentence, within three (3) days following the date of its receipt or the term that the court has set.

The violation of this rule, after a second requirement of equal term, shall be sanctioned with a fine from fifteen thousand (L.15,000.00) to thirty thousand (L.30,000.00) Lempiras, without prejudice to the respective publication.

###### ARTICULO 166. The crimes of slander, insult or defamation can only be prosecuted by virtue of a complaint by the aggrieved party, except when the offense is directed against public authority or State institutions, and, in general, if they constitute another crime specially punished in this Code.

For this purpose, the heads of friendly or allied nations, their diplomatic representatives and others who, according to International Law, should be included in this provision are also deemed to be authority. In these cases, proceedings may only be undertaken at the request of the Executive Power.

###### ARTICULO 167. No one may bring an action for slander or insult caused in a trial without prior authorization from the Judge or Court hearing it.

###### ARTICULO 168. If the offended party dies before the expiration of the term set for the statute of limitations of the action, or the crime has been committed against the memory of a deceased person, the complaint may be filed by the spouse or any of the ascendants, descendants and siblings of the deceased or heirs thereof.

###### ARTICULO 169. The pardon of the offended party extinguishes the crimes of slander, insult and defamation against private individuals, or the penalty as the case may be.

#### TITULO IV

### CRIMES AGAINST CIVIL STATUS AND THE ORDER OF THE FAMILY

##### CAPITULO I

SUPPOSITION OF BIRTHS AND USURPATION OF CIVIL STATUS

###### ARTICULO 170. The supposition of births and the substitution of one child for another, shall be punished with a penalty of three (3) to six (6) years of imprisonment. The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever:

- 1) Hides or exposes a child with the intention of making him lose his civil status;
- 2) Reports or causes to be falsely registered in the National Registry of Persons the birth, death or any other fact that alters the civil status of a person or knowingly takes advantage of the false registration; and
- 3) Usurps the civil status of another person.

##### CAPITULO II

CELEBRATION OF ILLEGAL MARRIAGES

###### ARTICULO 171. The person who contracts a second or subsequent marriage without the previous one being legitimately dissolved, shall be punished with the penalty of two to five years of imprisonment. The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever being single contracts marriage knowingly, with a married person.

###### ARTICULO 172. Shall incur the penalty of imprisonment from one to four years:

1) Whoever deceiving a person, simulates marriage with him or her.

2) Whoever contracts marriage knowing that he has a non-dispensable diriment impediment.

###### ARTICULO 173. Officials who authorize marriages prohibited by law, knowingly, or without the concurrence of any of the requirements for its existence or validity, shall be sanctioned with a fine from fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) and special disqualification from four (4) to six (6) years.

###### ARTICULO 174. In all cases of this Chapter the fraudulent contracting party shall be condemned to indemnify according to his ability the other contracting party, if the latter has contracted marriage in good faith.

###### ARTICULO 175. The fine provided in Article 173, preceding, shall be applicable to ministers of any religion who authorize religious marriage without the celebration of civil marriage taking place.

##### CAPITULO III

INCEST

###### ARTICULO 176. Carnal access between ascendants and descendants or between siblings shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years.

Whoever commits incest with a descendant or sibling under eighteen (18) years of age shall be punished with four (4) to seven (7) years of imprisonment. Said minors shall not incur any penalty, but shall be subject to the protective measures that special laws determine.

In the crime of incest proceedings shall be by virtue of a complaint by the offended party or his legal representative if he is absolutely or relatively incapable. To this crime shall be applied the provisions of numerals 1, 2 and 3 of the second paragraph of Article 152, preceding.

##### CAPITULO IV

DENIAL OF FAMILY ASSISTANCE

###### ARTICULO 177. Quien estando obligado legalmente, o en virtud de sentencia firme,

después de haber sido requerido fehacientemente, dejare sin justa causa de proveer a la subsistencia del cónyuge, de los hijos menores de veintiún años o del pupilo bajo su guarda, será sancionado con reclusión de uno a tres años.

En la misma pena incurrirá quien en iguales circunstancias, dejare de proporcionar los recursos necesarios a sus ascendientes o descendientes que se encuentren inválidos, enfermos o por cualquier causa incapacitados para el trabajo.

###### ARTICULO 178. Quien para eludir el cumplimiento de la obligación alimentaria, se coloca

en situación de insolvencia, traspasa sus bienes a terceras personas, renuncia a su trabajo, simula obligaciones o emplea cualquier otro medio fraudulento, será sancionado con reclusión de dos (2) a cinco (5) años.

###### ARTICULO 179. Las sanciones establecidas en el presente Capítulo no exonerarán al

indiciado de sus obligaciones alimentarias.

##### CAPITULO V

DE LA VIOLENCIA INTRAFAMILIAR

###### ARTICULO 179-A. Quien emplee fuerza, intimidación o haga objeto de persecución a su

cónyuge o ex cónyuge, a la persona con quien conviva o haya mantenido una relación concubinaria o a aquella con quien haya procreado un hijo, con la finalidad de causarle daño físico o emocional o para dañar sus bienes, será sancionado con reclusión de uno

(1) a tres (3) años, sin perjuicio de la pena que corresponda a las lesiones o daños causados. La misma pena se aplicará cuando la violencia se ejerza sobre los hijos comunes o sobre los hijos de las personas mencionadas que se hallen sujetos a patria potestad, o sobre el menor o incapaz sometido a tutela o curatela o sobre los ascendientes.

###### ARTICULO 179-B. Será sancionado con reclusión de dos (2) a cuatro (4) años quien

haga objeto de malos tratamientos de obra a su cónyuge, ex cónyuge, concubina o ex concubina o a la persona con quien haya procreado un hijo, en cualquiera de las circunstancias siguientes:

- a) Penetre en la morada de la persona o en el lugar en que esté albergada o depositada para consumar el hecho;
- b) Le infiera grave daño corporal;
- c) Realice la acción con arma mortífera aunque no haya actuado con la intención de matar o mutilar;
- d) Actúe en presencia de menores de edad;
- e) Induce, incita u obliga a la persona a consumir drogas, estupefaciente u otras sustancias sicotrópicas o embriagantes;
- f) Hace también objeto de malos tratos a un menor de edad; y
- g) Utilice como pretexto para restringir su libertad que la víctima padece de una enfermedad o de defecto mental.

Lo dispuesto en este Artículo se entenderá sin perjuicio de la pena que corresponda a los otros delitos en que incurra.

###### ARTICULO 179-C. No obstante lo establecido en el artículo 15 del Código de

Procedimientos Penales, los delitos contemplados en el presente Capítulo y en el anterior serán de acción pública.

#### TITULO V

### DELITOS CONTRA LA SALUD PUBLICA

###### ARTICULO 180. A quien dolosamente propagare una enfermedad peligrosa o causare

una epidemia mediante la difusión de gérmenes patógenos, se impondrá reclusión de tres a seis años.

###### ARTICULO 181. Quien a sabiendas distribuya o venda productos alimenticios,

medicamentos o sustancias consumibles en general, aún sin valor nutritivo, que representan riesgo para la salud o puedan causar enfermedades por su contaminación, indebida elaboración o mala higiene, será sancionado dependiendo de la gravedad de la lesión, con reclusión de dos (2) a cinco (5) años más una multa de hasta veinte (20) veces el valor del producto sin perjuicio de las sanciones que correspondan a otros delitos cometidos como consecuencia de las mismas acciones.

###### ARTICULO 181-A. Quien contamine la totalidad o parte del territorio nacional, incluyendo

las aguas, con desechos, desperdicios, basuras o sustancias traídas del extranjero que produzcan o sean susceptibles de producir daños a la salud de las personas o al ecosistema, será sancionado con reclusión de seis (6) a doce (12) años y multa de cien mil lempiras (L.100,000.00) a quinientos mil lempiras (L.500,000.00).

###### ARTICULO 181-B. Las penas establecidas en el Artículo anterior se impondrán también a

quien dentro o fuera del país promueva o de cualquier manera gestione la introducción al territorio nacional de desechos, desperdicios, basuras o sustancias que provoquen o sean susceptibles de provocar contaminación al medio ambiente o daño a la salud de las personas.

###### ARTICULO 182. Quien elabore sustancias alimenticias o terapéuticas en forma peligrosa

para la salud, será sancionado con reclusión de uno a cinco años.

En la misma pena incurrirá quien a sabiendas, importe, distribuya, venda, exporte o mantenga en depósito para su distribución o expendio, sustancias nocivas a la salud o alimentos falsificados, adulterados, deteriorados, contaminados o vencidos.

###### ARTICULO 182-A. Será penado con reclusión de uno (1) a seis (6) años el que sin estar

legalmente autorizado al efecto expenda sustancias, estupefacientes, enervantes o análogas de regulación estrictamente farmacéutica, expenda o sirva de intermediario en la compra a menores de veintiún (21) años de productos que contengan compuestos orgánicos como cementos plásticos, adelgazadores de pinturas y todo tipo de pegamentos que al ser inhalados produzcan hábito, acostumbramiento o dependencia, tanto psíquica como psicofísica.

###### ARTICULO 183. Será sancionado con pena de uno a tres años de reclusión quien,

ejerciendo el comercio de medicamentos, debidamente autorizado por la ley, los expendiere sin prescripción facultativa, cuando ésta fuere necesaria, o en desacuerdo con ella, o los suministrare en especie, cantidad o calidad diferente a la prescrita por el facultativo.

Igual sanción se aplicará a quien, en el mismo caso, vendiere sustancias medicinales a sabiendas de que han perdido sus propiedades terapéuticas.

###### ARTICULO 184. Si de los delitos configurados en los cuatro artículos precedentes

resultare la muerte de alguna persona, se sancionará al responsable con la pena del homicidio simple o la del homicidio calificado, según las circunstancias concurrentes en el hecho.

###### ARTICULO 185. En todo caso, las sustancias alimenticias o medicinales nocivas serán

decomisadas, y remitidas a la Secretaría de Salud Pública, para los efectos legales correspondientes.

###### ARTICULO 186. Será penado con reclusión de seis meses a dos años quien infrinja las

medidas adoptadas por la autoridad sanitaria con el fin de impedir la introducción o propagación de una epidemia, o de una epizootia susceptible de afectar a los seres humanos.

La pena se aumentará en una cuarta parte cuando el autor fuere funcionario o empleado de sanidad, médico, farmacéutico u odontólogo, o ejerciere alguna de las actividades auxiliares de estas profesiones.

###### ARTICULO 187. Se impondrá reclusión de uno a tres años a quien corrompiere o

ensuciare fuente, pozo o río cuya agua sirva de bebida, tornándola nociva para la salud.

###### ARTICULO 188. El médico o profesional que, habiendo intervenido en el diagnóstico o

tratamiento de una enfermedad transmisible, omitiere la notificación que previene el Código Sanitario, incurrirá en reclusión de tres meses a un año y multa de cien a trescientos lempiras.

###### ARTICULO 189. Quien sin título ni autorización para el ejercicio de profesiones

relacionadas con la salud, o excediéndose los límites de su autorización, anunciare, prescribiere, administrare o aplicare cualquier medio químico destinado al tratamiento de enfermedades de las personas, aún en forma gratuita, será penado con uno a dos años de reclusión.

###### ARTICLE 190. Whoever practices inhumation, exhumation or transfer of cadavers or

human remains in contravention of the pertinent legal or regulatory provisions, shall be sanctioned with a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras

(L.10,000.00).

###### ARTICLE 191. Crimes against public health that are committed in a negligent manner

shall be sanctioned with the corresponding penalty, reduced by two thirds.

#### TITLE VI

### CRIMES AGAINST FREEDOM AND SECURITY

##### CHAPTER I

KIDNAPPING AND ILLEGAL DETENTIONS

###### ARTICLE 192. Whoever kidnaps a person to obtain from them or from a third party,

as the price of their freedom, money, goods, titles or documents that produce benefit or utility in favor of the kidnapper or of another person that they designate, shall be sanctioned as follows:

- 1) With a penalty of deprivation of liberty for thirty (30) years to deprivation of liberty for life if they cause or give rise to the death of the kidnapped person, even if they do not achieve their objective;
- 2) From thirty (30) to forty (40) years, if the kidnapped person dies as a result of the rescue process;
- 3) From twenty (20) to thirty (30) years, if they release the person having collected the price demanded; and,
- 4) From five (5) to ten (10) years, if they desist by releasing them and have not obtained the price demanded.

If as a result of the liberation members of the authority die, the provisions of numeral 2) of this Article shall apply. If for the same reason injuries are produced to members of the authority, the penalties shall be increased by one third.

The same penalties referred to in this Article shall be applicable to whoever kidnaps or retains a person with the purpose that they do or refrain from doing something for publicity purposes of a political nature.

###### ARTICLE 193. Whoever outside the cases provided for in the preceding article unjustly

deprives another of their liberty, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

###### ARTICLE 194. Insofar as applicable, the penalties indicated in Articles 192,

193 and 195 shall be increased by one third when the following circumstances concur:

- 1) Threat or cruel treatment of the kidnapped person;
- 2) Deprivation of liberty for more than twenty-four (24) hours; and
- 3) Application of drugs or any other means that weakens or annuls the will of the kidnapped person.

###### ARTICLE 195. Whoever traffics with Hondurans or persons of any nationality or

origin, conducting them or having them conducted through the national territory, to introduce them illegally to another State for any purpose, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six

(6) to nine (9) years.

The sanction shall be increased by one third when those responsible for the crime are public employees or officials.

If as a consequence of the commission of this crime the passive subjects suffer deprivations of liberty abroad, are victims of crimes of any order or die from violent causes, even if accidental, the penalty referred to in the first paragraph shall be increased by two thirds.

###### ARTICLE 196. (REPEALED)

###### ARTICLE 196-A. Hondurans who participate or collaborate in Honduras with

foreign organizations or authorities in the illegal detention of fellow nationals so that they are tried abroad for crimes committed within or outside the national territory, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of nine (9) to twelve (12) years. If they are public officials, the penalty shall be twelve (12) to fifteen (15) years, plus absolute disqualification for double the time that the imprisonment lasts.

##### CHAPTER II

ABDUCTION OF MINORS

###### ARTICLE 197. The abduction of a minor under twelve (12) years by persons other than

their parents shall be punished with imprisonment of six (6) to eight (8) years. In the same case, the abduction of a person over twelve (12) years and under eighteen (18) years shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of four (4) to six (6) years.

###### ARTICLE 198. The same penalty established in the preceding article shall be incurred by whoever,

being in charge of the person of a minor, does not present them to their parents or guardians, nor gives a satisfactory explanation regarding their disappearance.

###### ARTICLE 199. Whoever induces a minor under eighteen (18) years to abandon the

house of their parents, guardians or those in charge of their care, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years.

###### ARTICLE 200. Whoever having in their charge the upbringing of a minor under eighteen (18)

years delivers them to a public establishment or to another person without the consent of the one who had entrusted them, or of the authority, in default thereof, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years.

##### CHAPTER III

COMMON PROVISION TO THE TWO PRECEDING CHAPTERS

###### ARTICLE 201. Whoever kidnaps any person with motives other than those indicated in

Article 192 shall be punished with a penalty of twenty (20) to thirty (30) years if the person is found unharmed; when they do not give account of their whereabouts or do not prove having set them free, the penalty shall be thirty (30) to forty (40) years.

##### CHAPTER IV

TRESPASS OF DWELLING

###### ARTICLE 202. The private individual who enters the dwelling of another against the will of its

occupant or having entered with the express or tacit consent thereof, remains there despite having been warned to leave it, shall be sanctioned with three months to one year of imprisonment.

If the foregoing acts are executed with violence or intimidation or simulation of authority, the penalty shall be one to three years of imprisonment.

###### ARTICLE 203. The agent of authority, official or public employee who enters

a house without complying with the requirements prescribed by law, shall be sanctioned with two (2) to five (5) years of imprisonment and special disqualification for a time equal to double that applied to the imprisonment.

###### ARTICLE 204. The provision of the first paragraph of Article 202 is not applicable to

whoever enters the dwelling of another to avoid serious harm to themselves, to the occupants or to a third party, nor to whoever does so to provide some service to humanity or when required by the judicial authority.

###### ARTICLE 205. The provisions of this Chapter do not apply with respect to cafes,

bars and other establishments serving the public while they are open.

##### CHAPTER V

COERCION AND THREATS

###### ARTICLE 206. Whoever without being legitimately authorized prevents another with violence

from doing what the law does not prohibit, or compels them to execute what they do not wish, whether just or unjust, shall suffer imprisonment of three months to two years.

###### ARTICLE 207.

The private individual who threatens another with causing harm to them or their family, in their person, honor or property, whether it constitutes a crime or not, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to two years, and in addition, with the security measures that the Judge determines.

###### ARTICLE 208. Whoever with violence seizes a thing belonging to their

debtor to obtain payment with it, shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment of three months to one year.

###### ARTICLE 209. The agent of authority, official or public employee who to

obtain the confession of being responsible for a certain crime or with another similar purpose threatens with physical or moral violence any person, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years, and absolute disqualification for a time equal to double that applied to the imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 209-A. Commits torture the employee or public official, including those of

penitentiary institutions or centers for the protection of minors who, abusing their position and for the purpose of obtaining a confession or information from any person or punishing them for any act they have committed or are suspected of having committed, subjects them to conditions or procedures that by their nature, duration or other circumstances cause them physical or mental suffering, the suppression or diminution of their faculties of knowledge, discernment or decision, or that in any other way violate their moral integrity. The guilty party of torture shall be punished with imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years if the damage is serious, and five (5) to ten (10) years if it is not, plus absolute disqualification for double the duration of the imprisonment. The foregoing penalties shall be understood without prejudice to those that may be applicable to injuries or damage to life, bodily integrity, health, sexual freedom or property of the victim or a third party.

When the crime of torture is committed by private individuals, the penalties provided in the first paragraph of this article shall be reduced by one third.

##### CAPITULO VI

### CRIMES AGAINST FREEDOM OF WORSHIP, RELIGIOUS SENTIMENT

AND RESPECT FOR THE DECEASED

###### ARTICULO 210. Whoever by means of violence compels another to perform a religious act or

prevents them from participating in a ceremony of the same nature, shall be punished with imprisonment of three months to one year.

###### ARTICULO 211. The same penalty as established in the previous article shall be incurred by whoever

interrupts or prevents, without justified cause, the celebration of a ceremony or religious function of any worship permitted in the nation.

###### ARTICULO 212. Whoever causes damage to objects intended for worship or to the symbols

of any legally permitted religion, or publicly offends such worship or its members by reason of their investiture, shall incur imprisonment of three months to one year.

###### ARTICULO 213. Whoever violates a grave, sepulcher or funeral home, or in any other

serious manner desecrates a human corpse or its remains, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years.

##### CAPITULO VII

VIOLATION AND DISCLOSURE OF SECRETS

###### ARTICULO 214. Whoever without due judicial authorization, with any purpose,

seizes the papers or correspondence of another, intercepts or causes to be intercepted their telephone, telegraph, electronic media or computer communications, facsimiles or of any other nature, including electronic ones, shall be sanctioned with six (6) to eight (8) if they are a private individual and from eight (8) to twelve (12) years if they are a public official or employee.

###### ARTICULO 215. Whoever reveals without just cause or uses for their own benefit or that of another a

secret of which they became aware by reason of their trade, employment, profession or art and thereby causes harm to someone, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

##### CAPITULO VII

### CRIMES AGAINST POLITICAL FREEDOM

###### ARTICULO 216. Whoever outside the cases provided for in the respective special laws,

by means of violence or threats prevents or restricts the exercise of any political right, shall be sanctioned with six months to two years of imprisonment.

#### TITULO VII

### CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY

##### CAPITULO I

ROBBERY

###### ARTICULO 217. Commits the crime of robbery whoever seizes movable property of others,

including animals, by employing violence or intimidation against persons or force against things.

Equivalent to violence against persons is the act of snatching by surprise from the victim the thing they carry with them or the use of means that weaken or nullify their resistance.

###### ARTICULO 218. The guilty party of robbery shall be punished with five (5) to nine (9) years of

imprisonment.

To the guilty party of the crime of robbery of land motor vehicles, aircraft, ships and other similar goods, a penalty of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years of imprisonment shall be applied.

The penalty applicable to the crime of robbery of land motor vehicles, aircraft, ships and other similar goods, a penalty of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years of imprisonment shall be applied.

The penalty applicable to the robbery of large livestock shall be seven (7) to ten (10) years of imprisonment. The robbery of small livestock shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to seven (7) years. An aggravating circumstance of this crime is the robbery of three (3) or more heads of large or small livestock.

###### ARTICULO 219. The penalties indicated in the previous Article shall be increased by one third

when the robbery is committed:

- 1) In an uninhabited place or in a gang;
- 2) With the wrongdoers carrying weapons, in an inhabited house or in a public office, in a building intended for religious worship, in a public or private educational or cultural center, in a commercial, banking or social assistance establishment, provided that any of the following circumstances are present:
  - a) Scaling;
  - b) Breaking of walls, roofs, floors or pavements or breaking of doors or windows;
  - c) Using false keys, lock picks or other similar instruments. The legitimate key stolen from the owner shall be considered a false key; and
  - d) Breaking of cabinets, safes or other kinds of sealed or closed furniture or objects or by theft of the same to be opened or violated outside the place of robbery.

The increase referred to in the first paragraph shall also be made when any of the circumstances mentioned in Article 225 are present.

###### ARTICULO 220. An inhabited house is considered to be any shelter that constitutes the dwelling of

one or more persons, even if they are accidentally absent from it when the robbery takes place.

Dependencies of an inhabited house, or of the establishments listed in paragraph 2 of the previous article, are considered to be corrals, warehouses, granaries, haysheds, garages, stables and other departments or enclosed sites adjacent to the building, and in interior communication with it and with which they form a single whole.

###### ARTICULO 221. Whoever has in their possession lock picks or other instruments specially intended

to execute the crime of robbery, and does not give sufficient account of their acquisition or conservation, shall be punished with imprisonment of three months to one year. The same penalty shall be incurred by those who for the same purpose manufacture such instruments. If they are locksmiths, they shall be sentenced to one to two years of imprisonment.

##### CAPITULO II

EXTORTION AND BLACKMAIL

###### ARTICULO 222. Shall incur imprisonment of three to nine years:

1) Whoever by means of violence or threats, compels someone to do or refrain from doing something, in order to obtain for themselves or for others an unjust benefit.

2) Whoever to defraud another compels them with violence or intimidation to sign, grant, deliver or destroy a public deed or any other public or private document.

3) Whoever with threats of imputations against honor or prestige, or of violation or disclosure of secrets with harm in either case to the offended party, their family or the entity in whose management they intervene or have interest, demands the delivery of a sum of money, reward or effects.

##### CAPITULO III

THEFT

###### ARTICULO 223. The following commit the crime of theft:

1) Whoever without the owner's consent takes movable property belonging to others, including animals, without violence or intimidation against persons or force against things;

2) Whoever finding a lost thing does not deliver it to the authority or to its owner if they know who it is and takes possession of it with intent of ownership; and,

3) Whoever subtracts or uses the fruits or objects of the damage that they had caused, except for the cases provided in Book Three.

Electrical energy, the radioelectric spectrum and other classes of waves or energy in telephone, television, facsimile, computer systems or any others that have economic value are equated to movable property.

###### ARTICULO 224. Theft shall be punished with two (2) to five (5) years of imprisonment if the

value of the stolen thing does not exceed five thousand Lempiras (L.5,000.00) and with four (4) to seven (7) years if it exceeds said amount.

The theft of land motor vehicles, aircraft, ships and other similar goods shall be punished with imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years.

###### ARTICULO 225. The applicable penalties according to the previous article shall be increased by one

third if the act is committed:

- 1) By a domestic employee or involving serious abuse of trust.
- 2) Taking advantage of a public or private calamity or a common danger.
- 3) At night, or if, to execute it, the agent remained surreptitiously in a building or place intended for habitation.
- 4) With the cooperation of two or more persons or by one person who pretends to be an agent of authority, or employee of a public service.
- 5) In the luggage of travelers, in any type of vehicles, in stations, docks, hotels, or in establishments where food or beverages are served.
- 6) In houses intended for worship or public ornamental use or when it concerns funerary monuments.
- 7) In objects of scientific, artistic or cultural value that are found in museums or other public establishments or that belong to the national historical heritage, even if they had remained hidden or undiscovered.

###### ARTICULO 226. The theft of large livestock shall be punished with imprisonment of six (6) to

nine (9) years if the value of what is stolen exceeds five thousand lempiras (L.5,000.00) and four (4) to seven (7) years if it does not exceed said value.

The theft of small livestock shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to six (6) years, if the value of what is stolen exceeds five thousand Lempiras (L.5,000.00), and two (2) to five (5) years if it does not exceed said value.

The theft of three (3) or more heads of large or small livestock constitutes an aggravating circumstance of this offense.

##### CAPITULO IV

USURPATION

###### ARTICULO 227. Whoever usurps immovable property or a real right shall be punished with

imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years, without prejudice that as soon as the corresponding right is proven in the proceedings, the judge hearing the case orders the eviction of the property in question or the restoration of the usurped right.

###### ARTICULO 228. Whoever alters

boundaries or landmarks of towns, or properties, or any kind of markers intended to fix the limits of contiguous estates shall incur the same penalties as the previous article.

###### ARTICULO 229. Whoever outside the mentioned cases disturbs with violence or

threats against persons, the peaceful possession of immovable property, shall be punished with three months to one year of imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 230. Whoever without proper authorization and to the detriment of another dams, diverts or

detains the waters of a river, creek, stream, canal or spring, or usurps any right related to the course of such waters, shall be punished with imprisonment of one

(1) to three (3) years, without prejudice to destroying the unlawfully made works.

###### ARTICULO 231. Whoever occupies the land or space corresponding to the right of way,

highway, street, garden, park, green area, promenade or other places of public use or domain or any other real property of the State or municipalities, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years, without prejudice to vacating the occupied land or space. When the occupied property is a beach the penalty shall be increased by two thirds.

###### ARTICULO 232. (REPEALED)

##### CAPITULO V

PUNISHABLE INSOLVENCY

###### ARTICULO 233. The merchant declared in fraudulent bankruptcy according to the Code of

Commerce, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to six years and special disqualification of three to seven years.

###### ARTICULO 234. The merchant declared in culpable bankruptcy for any of the causes included in the Commercial Code shall incur imprisonment of six months to two years and special disqualification

of one to three years.

###### ARTICULO 235. Those who execute any of the following acts shall be punished as accomplices to the crime of fraudulent bankruptcy

:

- 1) Conspire with the bankrupt to simulate credits against them or to increase them, alter their nature or maturity date in order to take precedence in ranking, to the detriment of other creditors, even if this occurs before the declaration of bankruptcy.
- 2) Have assisted the bankrupt in the removal, subtraction or concealment of their assets.
- 3) Conceal from the bankruptcy administrators the existence of assets that, belonging to it, are in the possession of the guilty party, or deliver them to the bankrupt and not to said administrators.
- 4) Make private arrangements with the bankrupt to the detriment of other creditors.

###### ARTICULO 236. When a commercial enterprise is declared bankrupt, any director

or administrator thereof who has cooperated in the execution of any of the unlawful acts that motivated it, shall be punished with the penalty of the fraudulent or culpable bankrupt, as applicable.

###### ARTICULO 237. Whoever, to evade payment of their obligations, alienates or encumbers their assets without notifying their creditors in authentic form, with at least fifteen days in advance; or conceals their assets, simulates alienations or credits, or moves abroad without leaving a person to represent them, or sufficient assets to answer for the payment of their debts, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to six years, if they are a merchant,

and two to five, if they are not.

###### ARTICULO 238. The non-merchant debtor whose insolvency is the result, in whole or in part, of any of the following acts shall incur imprisonment of one to two years

:

- 1) Having made excessive domestic or personal expenses, disproportionate in relation to their fortune, considering the circumstances of their rank and family.
- 2) Having suffered, in any kind of game, losses that exceeded what a good family father would risk by way of recreation in entertainment of this kind.
- 3) Having had losses in substantial bets, simulated purchases and sales and other speculation operations, whose success depends exclusively on chance.
- 4) Having alienated, with notable depreciation, goods whose price is owed.
- 5) Delay their filing for bankruptcy proceedings, when their liabilities are twenty-five percent greater in relation to their assets.

###### ARTICULO 239. Those who execute with respect to the debtor any of the acts listed in Article 235 shall be punished as accomplices to the crime provided in the preceding article

.

##### CAPITULO VI

FRAUD AND OTHER DECEPTIONS

###### ARTICULO 240. Whoever with a false name, false titles commits the crime of fraud

, simulated influence or quality, abuse of trust, pretending to be the owner of assets, credits, enterprises or businesses or using any artifice, cunning or deceit, induces another into error, defrauding them for their own or another's benefit.

###### ARTICLE 241. The crime of fraud shall be punished:

1) With two (2) to five (5) years of imprisonment when the value of what was defrauded does not exceed five thousand lempiras (L.5,000.00);

2) With four (4) to seven (7) years of imprisonment if the amount exceeds five thousand lempiras (L.5,000.00) and does not exceed one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00); and

3) With six (6) to nine (9) years of imprisonment if the amount exceeds one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) Lempiras.

The agent shall also be punished with a fine equal to ten percent (10%) of the defrauded value. The foregoing shall be understood without prejudice to the provisions of Articles 36, 37 and 53, when applicable.

###### ARTICLE 242. Shall incur the penalties of the preceding article:

1) Whoever defrauds another in the substance, quality or quantity of things delivered to them by virtue of contract or obligatory title.

2) Whoever, in order to defraud someone, causes them to sign, destroy or mutilate, through deception, any document.

3) Whoever commits any fraud by abuse of another's signature in blank, executing with it any document to the detriment of the same person or a third party.

4) Whoever, to the detriment of another, grants simulated contracts or false receipts.

5) The commission agent, agent, administrator or representative who commits fraud by altering in their accounts the prices or conditions of contracts, assuming expenses or exaggerating those they have made.

6) Whoever defrauds through the concealment, substitution or mutilation of any proceeding, file, document or other important paper.

7) Whoever in gambling uses artifice or deception to secure luck.

8) Whoever to the detriment of another appropriates or misapplies money, effects or any other movable thing that they have received in deposit, commission or administration, or by any other title that entails an obligation to deliver or return it, or denies having received it.

9) Whoever sells or encumbers, as free, property that is in litigation, seized or encumbered, and whoever sells, encumbers or leases, property of others as their own.

10) Whoever defrauds another under the pretext of supposed remuneration, gratification or gift to judges or other public employees, without prejudice to the action for slander that corresponds to them.

11) Whoever, in order to obtain the price of insurance, or any undue benefit, destroys, damages or conceals the insured thing or any other of their property.

12) Whoever, with the same purpose, causes themselves or through a third party, a personal injury or aggravates one caused by an accident; and

13) Whoever, abusing the needs, inexperience, or passions of a minor, or the state of illness or mental deficiency of a person, causes them to sign a document or execute any other act that has legal effect.

###### ARTICLE 243. Whoever defrauds or harms another using any deception that is not

included in the preceding Articles of this Chapter, shall be punished with a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras (L.10,000.00). In case of recidivism the penalty shall be equal to double the previous one.

##### CHAPTER VII

USURY AND PROFITEERING

###### ARTICLE 244. Commits the crime of usury the lender who demands from their debtors in

any form, an interest rate greater than the average interest rate in effect in the National Financial System on the date of the loan contract, increased by six points, even when said interest is concealed or disguised in any manner or given another name. Shall be considered as loans, those made by persons registered as non-banking lenders or who are recognized as such by notoriety even when the obligation is formalized through a negotiable instrument or any other document. The crime of usury shall be punished with imprisonment of four (4) to six (6) years, plus a fine equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the credit amount.

###### ARTICLE 245. In the same penalty as the preceding article, shall incur whoever taking advantage of the

urgent need, recklessness or inexperience of a person, causes them to give or propose in any form, for themselves or for another, pecuniary advantage exceeding thirty percent of their performance, or grants a guarantee of an extortionate nature.

###### ARTICLE 246. Whoever habitually lends money with or without guarantee and is not

registered in the corresponding official registry or does not keep accounting books, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of fifty thousand

(L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00).

###### ARTICLE 247. Whoever increases the prices of goods or public services

above those set by the competent authorities in accordance with the Law, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of twenty thousand (L.20,000.00) to fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) Lempiras.

##### CHAPTER VIII

### CRIMES AGAINST SPECIAL PROPERTIES

###### ARTICLE 248. Whoever violates the rights of authors of literary, scientific or

artistic works and others protected by the Copyright and Related Rights Law, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years, plus a fine of fifty thousand

(L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00).

###### ARTICLE 248-A. With the same penalties as Article 248 shall be punished

natural or legal persons who without authorization from the respective holders of copyright or related rights use for commercial purposes, television signals transmitted by satellite or reproduce or project videos, films or other similar works that, by their nature, are or should be protected by the Law on the Matter.

With the same penalties shall be punished those who use radio spectrum frequencies without authorization from the corresponding authority.

###### ARTICLE 249. In the same penalty as the preceding Article 248, shall incur whoever manufactures or

offers for sale articles that by their name, brand, packaging, presentation or appearance may be confused with similar products patented or registered in the name of another.

###### ARTICLE 250. (REPEALED)

###### ARTICLE 251. With the penalties provided in the preceding Article 248, shall be

punished those who:

- 1) Falsify, imitate or fraudulently use any of the figures or legal assets protected by the Industrial Property Law;
- 2) With knowledge that said figures or legal assets are falsified, negotiate them in any form; and
- 3) Knowingly market goods, articles or products protected with the indicated figures or legal assets that are falsified, imitated or fraudulently used.

###### ARTICLE 252. Whoever uses or permits the use of the elements referred to in the

preceding article, contravening morality, public order, good customs, or that ridicule persons or offend religious sentiments, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years.

###### ARTICLE 253. Shall be punished with the penalties provided in the preceding Article 248,

whoever commits any of the infractions provided in Title VI of the Industrial Property Law that are not included in the preceding Articles of this Chapter.

##### CHAPTER IX

DAMAGES

###### ARTICLE 254. Imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years shall be imposed on whoever destroys,

renders useless, makes disappear or in any way, damages movable or immovable things or animals belonging to another, provided that the act does not constitute a crime of those provided in the following chapter.

The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever by any means destroys, alters, renders useless or in any other way damages data, programs or electronic documents belonging to others, contained in networks, media or computer systems.

###### ARTICULO 255. Shall be punished with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years whoever causes

any of the damages referred to in the preceding Article:

- 1) With the purpose of preventing the free exercise of authority or in revenge for its decisions, whether the crime is committed against public officials or employees or against private individuals who, as witnesses or in any other manner, have contributed or may contribute to the enforcement of the laws;
- 2) By any means that produces infection or contagion in animals or plants;
- 3) Using poisonous, corrosive, explosive or flammable substances;
- 4) In a gang or in an uninhabited place;
- 5) On objects of scientific, historical, welfare, educational, cultural or artistic interest or in laboratories, archives, libraries, museums, monuments or on property of social utility;
- 6) On means or routes of communication or transit, bridges, canals, parks, promenades or other property of the State or national property for public use;
- 7) Through the destruction of property to the detriment of a creditor; and
- 8) Destroying forests or large plantations.

Negligent damages shall be punished with a penalty equal to half of that corresponding to intentional damage. If the perpetrator had committed the act as a consequence of having consumed alcohol or drugs, he shall be punished with two-thirds of the penalty applicable to the corresponding intentional damage.

##### CAPITULO X

FIRE AND OTHER HAVOC

###### ARTICULO 256. Whoever causes a fire, endangering the life, bodily integrity

or property of another, shall incur imprisonment from three to six years. The penalty shall be six to twelve years if the fire is committed:

- 1) With intent to profit, for one's own benefit or that of another.
- 2) In a building, farmhouse, hut or shelter that is inhabited or intended for habitation.
- 3) In a public building or one intended for public use or for social welfare or cultural work.
- 4) On a vessel, aircraft, convoy or collective transport vehicles.
- 5) In an airport, railway station or motor vehicles.
- 6) In a shipyard, factory or workshop.
- 7) In a depot of explosive or flammable substances.
- 8) In an oil well or mine gallery.
- 9) In a sown field, pasture field or forest.

###### ARTICULO 257. Those who cause havoc shall incur imprisonment from six to twelve years

by means of the collapse of a building, flood, explosion, and in general, by any agent or means of destruction as powerful as those mentioned.

The same penalty shall apply to those who, in the act of a fire, flood, shipwreck or any other disaster occurring, remove, conceal or render useless materials, instruments or other means intended to prevent such disasters.

###### ARTICULO 258. If the fire or havoc results in the death of a person, the penalty

shall be ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 259. Whoever causes a fire or other havoc in a negligent manner shall be

punished with imprisonment from six months to two years.

If death of any person results from the omission or negligent act, the imprisonment shall be two to five years.

##### CAPITULO XI

GAMBLING

###### ARTICULO 260. The owners and administrators of casinos, gambling houses or

houses of chance, betting or games of chance not legally authorized, shall be punished with imprisonment from three

(3) to seven (7) years, plus a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00), without prejudice to the definitive closure of the business.

Those who gamble in said houses shall be punished with imprisonment from one (1) to three (3) years, plus a fine equal to half of the foregoing.

###### ARTICULO 261. The producers and vendors of lottery tickets not legally authorized

and those who conduct raffles that have also not been legally authorized, shall be punished with imprisonment from two (2) to four (4) years plus a fine of ten thousand (L.10,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras (L.50,000.00). Raffles conducted for charitable, educational or promotional purposes for the arts or sports by centers or establishments dedicated to these activities are excepted.

Those who use fraudulent means in gambling or raffles shall be punished as swindlers.

###### ARTICULO 262.

The money or effects and the instruments and utensils intended for gambling or raffles shall be forfeited.

##### CAPITULO XII

GENERAL PROVISIONS

###### ARTICULO 263. (REPEALED)

###### ARTICULO 264. The following are exempt from criminal liability and subject only to civil liability

for thefts, frauds or damages that they reciprocally cause each other:

- 1) Spouses, the man and woman who live together as a couple and ascendants or descendants by blood or affinity.
- 2) The widower or widow, with respect to things belonging to their deceased spouse, as long as they have not passed into the possession of another.
- 3) Brothers and sisters and brothers-in-law and sisters-in-law, if they live under the same roof.

The exemption of this article does not apply to strangers who participate in the crime.

#### TITULO VIII

### CRIMES AGAINST MEANS OF COMMUNICATION

AND OTHER PUBLIC SERVICES

###### ARTICULO 265. Shall be punished with imprisonment from two to five years, whoever prevents or

disrupts railway service in any of the following ways:

- 1) Destroying, damaging or disrupting, totally or partially, railway line, rolling stock or traction equipment, works or installation.
- 2) Placing obstacles on the track.
- 3) Transmitting false notice about the movement of railway vehicles.
- 4) Performing any other act from which disaster may result.

###### ARTICULO 266.

If disaster results from the acts referred to in the preceding Article, the penalty shall be imprisonment from four to twelve years.

###### ARTICULO 267. Whoever endangers a vessel or aircraft, one's own or another's, or

performs any act tending to prevent or hinder water or air navigation, shall be punished with imprisonment from four to twelve years.

###### ARTICULO 268. If the act referred to in the preceding article results in shipwreck or

grounding of a vessel, or the crash or destruction of an aircraft, the penalty shall be imprisonment from four to twelve years.

###### ARTICULO 269. Whoever exposes another means of public transport to danger, prevents or

hinders its operation, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to two years. If disaster results from the act, the penalty shall be imprisonment from four to twelve years.

###### ARTICULO 270. In any of the crimes provided for in the preceding Articles of

this Title, if death or injuries of those included in Article 135 result from the act, the penalty shall be imprisonment from six to fifteen years, unless the crime committed merits a greater penalty according to other provisions of this Code.

If the act is negligent it shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to four years, this penalty being increased by half if death or injuries mentioned in the preceding paragraph result.

###### ARTICULO 271. Whoever destroys or damages the postal, telegraph, telephone,

electrical, radio service or other means serving telecommunications, shall be punished with imprisonment from six (6) to nine (9) years.

Shall be punished with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years whoever obstructs or interrupts the services referred to in the preceding paragraph or prevents or hinders their restoration.

###### ARTICULO 272. Shall incur imprisonment from two to six years whoever assaults a

carrier of public correspondence to intercept or detain it, or to seize it or in any way render it useless.

###### ARTICULO 273. Imprisonment from six months to two years shall be imposed on whoever by any

means prejudices the safety or normal operation of an establishment or installation intended to distribute water, light, energy, heat or other goods for public use to the public.

If prejudice results from the act, not only to the establishment or installation, but also to the public service, the imprisonment shall be two to six years.

#### TITULO IX

### CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC FAITH

##### CAPITULO I

COUNTERFEITING OF CURRENCY, BANK NOTES

AND SECURITIES

###### ARTICULO 274. Shall incur imprisonment of three to twelve years:

1) Whoever counterfeits or alters national or foreign currency inside or outside the country, that has legal or commercial tender.

2) Whoever knowingly introduces into the country counterfeit or altered currency that imitates that which has legal tender in the Republic.

3) Whoever, knowingly, acquires or receives counterfeit or altered currency and puts it into circulation in any manner.

###### ARTICULO 275. Whoever clips legitimate currency or knowingly introduces into the country

clipped currency or puts it into circulation, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of five

(5) to eight (8) years.

###### ARTICULO 276. Whoever having received in good faith false, altered or

clipped currency puts it into circulation knowing that it is illegitimate, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years.

###### ARTICULO 277. For the purposes of the three preceding articles, the counterfeiting or alteration of legally authorized bank notes, bonds and coupons of the national or municipal public debt, drafts or warrants of the State or Municipal treasury, and bonds, certificates or shares issued by banks or companies authorized to do so, shall be equivalent to

the counterfeiting or alteration of currency.

###### ARTICULO 278. The counterfeiting, clipping or alteration of foreign currency or notes

that do not have legal tender in the Republic or of securities, public debt bonds and other foreign credit documents referred to in the preceding Article, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of four (4) to six (6) years in the case of Article 274; three (3) to five (5) years in the case of Article 275 and two (2) to four (4) years in the case of Article 276.

###### ARTICULO 279. The public official or employee or the director, manager or administrator

of a bank or company who authorizes the manufacture or issuance of currency or bank notes or any bonds, certificates or bearer shares in amounts exceeding those authorized or under conditions different from those agreed upon or prescribed for the case, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years, plus a fine equal to triple the value of the operation in question.

The public officials or employees and others to whom this Article refers shall also be sanctioned with absolute disqualification from eight (8) to ten (10) years.

##### CAPITULO II

COUNTERFEITING OF SEALS, STAMPED PAPER

AND OTHER OFFICIAL EFFECTS

###### ARTICULO 280. Shall be punished with imprisonment of one to five years:

1) Whoever counterfeits official seals.

2) Whoever counterfeits stamped paper, postage stamps, telegraph stamps, revenue stamps or any other class of sealed or stamped effects, whose issuance is reserved to the State or its institutions, that have as their purpose the collection of taxes, fees or services. In these cases, as in those covered in the following articles, the fraudulent printing or use of the true seal shall be considered counterfeiting.

3) Whoever sells counterfeit fiscal stamps or under conditions that make their use and circulation illicit.

###### ARTICULO 281. Imprisonment of six months to three years shall be imposed:

1) On whoever counterfeits marks, stamps or signatures that are used in public offices to verify weights or measures or identify any object.

2) On whoever applies seals, marks or legitimate stamps of official use, to objects, works or articles different from those to which they should be applied.

###### ARTICULO 282. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years whoever makes

disappear from the seals, stamps, marks or stamps of official use referred to in the preceding Articles, the sign or mark placed by the authorities to denote that it has already been used or that it has been rendered useless for its circulation or sale.

The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever, knowingly, puts up for sale the adulterated seals, stamps, marks or stamps or uses them or delivers them to another person so that they may be used or gives rise to their being able to be used.

###### ARTICULO 283. When the person responsible for any of the crimes included in the

preceding articles is a public official or employee and commits the act by abusing their position, absolute disqualification from two to ten years shall also be imposed.

##### CAPITULO III

FALSIFICATION OF DOCUMENTS IN GENERAL

###### ARTICULO 284. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three to nine years, whoever makes in

whole or in part a false public document or alters a true one, in such a way that harm may result, by executing any of the following acts:

- 1) Counterfeiting or forging handwriting, signature or rubric.
- 2) Assuming in an act the intervention of persons who have not had it.
- 3) Attributing to those who have intervened in it, declarations or statements different from those they have made.
- 4) Failing to tell the truth in the narration of the facts.
- 5) Altering the true dates and amounts.
- 6) Making in a true document any alteration or insertion that changes its meaning.
- 7) Giving a reliable copy of a supposed document or stating in it something contrary or different from what the true original contains.
- 8) Improperly inserting any writing in an official protocol, register or book.
- 9) Destroying, mutilating, suppressing or concealing a document.

The religious minister who commits any of the acts included in the preceding numerals, with respect to ecclesiastical records or documents that may produce effects on the status of persons or on the civil order, shall also incur the penalty indicated in the first paragraph of this article.

###### ARTICULO 285. Whoever to the detriment of a third party or with intent to cause it commits in

a private document any of the falsifications designated in the preceding article, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to three years.

###### ARTICULO 286. The official or employee of the telecommunications service who

suppresses or falsifies a telegraphic dispatch, shall incur the penalty of imprisonment of one to two years.

Whoever makes use of the false dispatch with intent to profit or intent to harm another, shall be punished as the author of the falsification.

###### ARTICULO 287. Whoever counterfeits lottery tickets or those used

for raffles or by transport companies, public shows or other similar entities and puts them into circulation or takes advantage of them personally or benefits another person, and whoever knowingly puts them into circulation knowing they are counterfeit, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years, plus a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand lempiras (L.200,000.00).

###### ARTICULO 288. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of ten

thousand (L.10,000.00) to twenty thousand lempiras (L.20,000.00) the physician who issues a certificate or attestation that contains false information about the existence or non-existence, present or past, of any illness or injury, even if no harm results from it.

The penalty shall be doubled when as a consequence of the false certificate or attestation a healthy person is confined or held in a psychiatric treatment center, hospital or other similar establishment; it serves to obtain undue benefits from social security or other equivalent services or to achieve the release of a person from prison or evade the action of justice. The same penalty shall be applied to whoever requests the false certificate or attestation.

###### ARTICULO 289. Whoever, knowingly, makes use of a document false in whole or in

part shall be sanctioned as if they were the author of the falsification.

###### ARTICLE 290. When any of the offenses provided for in this Chapter is committed

by a public official or employee with abuse of their position, the guilty party shall suffer, in addition, absolute disqualification from two to ten years.

###### ARTICLE 291.

Whoever manufactures, imports or keeps in their possession machinery or equipment, instruments or materials intended to commit any of the forgeries set forth in this Title shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years.

##### CHAPTER IV

USURPATION OF FUNCTIONS AND TITLES AND IMPROPER USE

OF NAMES, UNIFORMS, INSIGNIA AND DECORATIONS

###### ARTICLE 292. Whoever without title or legitimate cause exercises acts proper to an

authority or official attributing official character to themselves, shall be sanctioned with two to three years of imprisonment.

###### ARTICLE 293. Whoever improperly claims academic title or degree, practices a

profession or maintains an office, office, clinic, law office, laboratory or premises for the purpose of offering academic, professional or technical services without having the legally required authorization from a public or private entity, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three

(3) to six (6) years.

###### ARTICLE 294. Shall incur a fine of ten thousand (L.10,000.00) to fifty thousand Lempiras

(L.50,000.00):

- 1) Whoever usurps the functions proper to the minister of a religious cult that has proselytes in Honduras;
- 2) The public official who, abusing their position, attributes to any person a title, rank or name that does not belong to them;
- 3) Whoever publicly or improperly uses a uniform proper to a public position they do not hold or insignia or decorations that have not been legally granted to them.

Whoever publicly uses an assumed name for the purpose of concealing a crime, evading a penalty or causing any harm, shall be imposed imprisonment from two (2) to five (5) years.

#### TITLE X

### OFFENSES AGAINST THE ECONOMY

###### ARTICLE 295. Whoever maliciously and as the sole consequence of their acts endangers

the national economy or public credit, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three

(3) to six (6) years and a fine of twenty thousand (L.20,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras

(L.50,000.00).

###### ARTICLE 296. Whoever destroys raw materials, agricultural or industrial products or

instruments of production for the purpose of causing damage to the country's economy or to consumers, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from two (2) to five (5) years and a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand lempiras (L.10,000.00). If the damage is serious, the indicated penalties shall be doubled.

###### ARTICLE 297. Whoever fraudulently causes in the market or in trade exchanges

or stock exchanges an increase or decrease of wages or the price of foodstuffs, goods, merchandise, shares, securities, currencies or other effects, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of twenty-five thousand

(L.25,000.00) to fifty thousand Lempiras (L.50,000.00).

If the offense is committed by public officials or employees, exchange agents or stockbrokers or trade brokers, the above penalties shall be increased by one third.

###### ARTICLE 298. (REPEALED)

###### ARTICLE 299. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of

fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00) whoever:

- 1) Hoards articles used in the construction industry for the purpose of provoking a rise in prices;
- 2) With improper acts or procedures obstructs free competition in the production and marketing of merchandise or the practice of public bids or auctions;
- 3) Executes acts of unfair competition, according to the rules established by the Commercial Code, other special laws or international agreements;
- 4) Exports staple goods or basic raw materials, without permission from the competent authority when required, if this produces scarcity or high prices.

The public official who, knowing that scarcity or high prices of certain staple goods or basic raw materials may occur, grants the permit and as a consequence thereof the scarcity or high prices effectively occurs, shall incur the same penalty;

5) Being duly authorized by the Central Bank of Honduras to conduct transactions in foreign currency, conducts them in contravention of the legal provisions or those issued by it;

6) Not being part of the institutions of the National Financial System or even being part of such System, performs operations for which it is not authorized by law; excepting those foundations or non-profit organizations that strengthen micro and small enterprises; and,

7) Whoever refuses to comply with the pecuniary sanctions established by the Law on Income of Foreign Exchange Derived from Exports contained in Decree 108-90 of September 20, 1990 and its amendments. In addition to the sanction contemplated in this Article, the violators in cases where applicable shall be subject to confiscation of the objects or effects of the crime. The corresponding values shall enter the General Treasury of the Republic or the Institution of the National Financial System that the Secretariat of State in the Office of Finance has authorized for such purpose.

###### ARTICLE 301. (REPEALED)

#### TITLE XI

### OFFENSES AGAINST THE EXISTENCE AND SECURITY OF THE STATE

##### CHAPTER I

TREASON

###### ARTICLE 302. Commits the offense of treason and shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment,

from fifteen to twenty years, whoever executes acts that tend directly to undermine the territorial integrity of the Republic, subject it totally or partially to foreign dominion, compromise its sovereignty or attempt against the unity of the State.

###### ARTICLE 303. The Honduran, even if having lost their nationality, or the foreigner who

owes obedience to Honduras by reason of their public employment or function, who takes part in an act of military hostility against the nation, or places themselves at the service of the foreign enemy in the case of armed conflict, shall incur ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.

If it concerns a foreigner who does not owe special obedience to the country by reason of their public employment or function, the sanction shall be reduced by half.

###### ARTICLE 304. In the case referred to in the preceding article, the penalty shall be fifteen to

twenty years of imprisonment if as a consequence of the services rendered to the enemy, any part of the national territory, body of troops, depot of war material, victuals and foodstuffs, or any other element indispensable for the defense of the State falls into their power, or if the arms of the Republic suffer defeat.

###### ARTICLE 305. The Honduran, even if having lost their nationality, or the foreigner who

owes obedience to Honduras by reason of their public employment or function, who with the purpose of provoking against Honduras aggression or hostilities from one or other nations, executes acts that tend directly to that end, shall suffer imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

If aggression or hostilities from a foreign State or States effectively occurs, the penalty shall be fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.

The final paragraph of Article 303 shall be applicable where appropriate.

###### ARTICLE 306. Whoever entrusted by the Honduran Government to deal with matters of

State with a foreign government or with persons or groups from another country, acts disloyally in the exercise of their mandate, shall incur five to ten years of imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 307. Whoever reveals political, diplomatic or military secrets relating to

the security of the State, whether by communicating or publishing documents, drawings, plans or other data relating to military materiel, fortifications or operations or any other matter essential to the defense of national interests, or by otherwise facilitating their disclosure, shall incur imprisonment of four (4) to seven (7) years, a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to five hundred thousand Lempiras (L.500,000.00), plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

If the secrets are revealed to a foreign government or to its agents or subjects, the imprisonment shall be five (5) to eight (8) years, the fine equal to double the foregoing and the disqualification equal to double the duration of the imprisonment sentence. If the revelation of secrets is made to a State that is at war with Honduras, or to its agents or subjects, the penalty shall be ten (10) to fifteen (15) years of imprisonment, the fine equal to triple that established in the first paragraph of this Article and absolute disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment.

The foregoing penalties shall be increased by one third if the offender had knowledge of the secrets by virtue of his character as a public official or employee or had used violence or fraud to obtain such knowledge.

###### ARTICULO 308. Whoever without being duly authorized surveys plans of

fortifications, barracks, ships, arsenals, roads or military works intended for the defense of the country or whoever for such purpose enters clandestinely or fraudulently into places whose access is prohibited by military authorities, shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand lempiras

(L.200,00.00).

###### ARTICULO 309. Whoever destroys or removes the boundary markers, buoys or signs that mark the

national borders shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand Lempiras (L.200,000.00).

If as a consequence of the destruction or removal of the boundary markers, buoys or border signs Honduras becomes involved in a conflict, or if such destruction or removal occurs during a war with a bordering State, the foregoing penalties shall be doubled.

###### ARTICULO 310. Whoever during an armed conflict with a foreign country induces

Honduran troops or officers to desert or to serve the enemy, or puts into practice any other means to achieve that end, shall be punished with imprisonment of six (6) to ten

(10) years, a fine of two hundred thousand (L.200,000.00) to five hundred thousand Lempiras (L.500,000.00) and absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 310-A. The crimes of treason against the Homeland typified in Articles 2 and 19 of

the Constitution of the Republic shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment of fifteen

(15) to twenty (20) years.

###### ARTICULO 310-B. Honduran nationality may only be acquired in the manner prescribed

by the Constitution of the Republic and International Conventions or Treaties. Whoever grants it following different procedures shall commit the crime of treason against the homeland and shall be punished with the penalty provided in the preceding Article.

###### ARTICULO 311. The attempt to commit any of the crimes included in the

preceding Articles shall be punished as if it were a consummated crime. The conspiracy to commit any of said crimes shall be punished with the penalty for the consummated crime reduced by two thirds. The proposal shall be punished with the same penalty reduced by five sixths. Desistance from the conspiracy or proposal exempts from all penalty if notice is given to the authority or its circumstances are brought to its knowledge before having begun the execution of the crime.

If the offender effectively achieves the intended purpose when the consummation of the crime does not require that said purpose be achieved, the corresponding penalty shall be increased by one third.

##### CAPITULO II

### CRIMES THAT COMPROMISE THE PEACE, EXTERNAL SECURITY

OR THE DIGNITY OF THE NATION

###### ARTICULO 312. Whoever commits any of the crimes provided for in the

preceding chapter, against a State allied with Honduras in war or armed conflict against a common enemy, shall incur the respective penalty reduced by one third.

###### ARTICULO 313. Whoever provokes the rupture of Honduras's relations with another

State giving rise to the imminence of a warlike conflict or to Hondurans suffering vexations or reprisals in their persons or property, shall be punished with imprisonment of four (4) to seven (7) years, a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to three hundred thousand Lempiras (L.300,000.00) and absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment. If war results from the rupture of relations, the penalty shall be eight (8) to twelve (12) years, a fine equal to double the foregoing and absolute disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 314. Whoever violates the truces or armistices agreed between the Republic and

another State or between the armed forces of both countries, or safe-conducts duly issued, and whoever impedes or disturbs the fulfillment of a treaty or agreement concluded with another State, shall be subject to imprisonment of one to three years.

###### ARTICULO 315. Whoever outrages any of the national symbols shall be punished with

imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years, a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand Lempiras (L.100,000.00) and special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 316. Whoever violates the immunity of a foreign Head of State or of a

diplomatic agent accredited to the government of Honduras shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years.

###### ARTICULO 317. Whoever recruits

troops in Honduras for the service of a foreign nation, whatever the object intended, shall be punished with three to six years of imprisonment.

The same penalty shall be incurred by those who use the national territory to invade or attack another nation.

##### CAPITULO III

### CRIMES AGAINST THE LAW OF NATIONS

###### ARTICULO 318. Whoever kills a foreign Head of State or Head of Government,

who is in Honduras in an official capacity, shall be punished with imprisonment of thirty

(30) to life deprivation of liberty.

###### ARTICULO 319. Whoever, with the purpose of destroying totally or partially a national, ethnic or religious group, commits any of the following acts, shall be punished with imprisonment of sixteen (16) to twenty (20) years plus

absolute disqualification for an equal time:

- 1) Killing any member of the group;
- 2) Seriously injuring the physical or mental integrity of any of the members of the group;
- 3) Subjecting the group to conditions of existence likely to produce its physical destruction or to cause it serious moral harm;
- 4) Adopting measures aimed at preventing the birth of children within the group; or
- 5) Compulsorily transferring minors under eighteen (18) years of age from one group to another.

The imprisonment shall not be less than twenty (20) years when those responsible for the crime of genocide are civil or military public officials or employees.

The proposal and the conspiracy shall be punished with imprisonment of eight (8) to twelve (12) years; direct incitement shall be punished with the penalty applicable to the perpetrator and indirect incitement shall be punished with imprisonment of five (5) to eight (8) years.

###### ARTICULO 320. Shall be punished as a pirate, with imprisonment of five (5) to ten (10)

years:

- 1) Whoever without political purposes violently seizes or under threat aircraft or public transport vehicles; and
- 2) Whoever in the territorial sea or in the rivers of the Republic seizes a ship or practices acts of violence or depredation against the vessel or against the persons or things found therein.

The provisions of this article shall be understood without prejudice to the offenses that result against life, bodily integrity, honesty, property and freedom, which shall be punished separately.

###### ARTICULO 321. Shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of

thirty thousand (L.30,000.00) to fifty thousand Lempiras (L.50,000.00) whoever discriminates against another person on grounds of sex, race, age, class, religion, party or political affiliation, suffering from any disability and any other harmful to human dignity. If the responsible party is a foreigner, he shall be expelled from the national territory once the sentence has been served.

#### TITULO XII

### CRIMES AGAINST THE INTERNAL SECURITY OF THE STATE

##### CAPITULO I

### CRIMES AGAINST HIGH OFFICIALS OF THE STATE

###### ARTICULO 322. Whoever causes the death of the President of a Branch of the State, shall be

punished with thirty (30) years to life imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 323. Whoever offends the President of the Republic in his bodily integrity

or his freedom shall be punished with eight to twelve years of imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 324. Conspiracy to commit any of the crimes included in the

preceding articles shall be punished with two to six years of imprisonment; and the proposal, with one to three years.

###### ARTICULO 325. The crimes dealt with in the three preceding articles committed

against the Secretaries of State, Deputies to the National Congress and Justices of the Supreme Court of Justice, shall be punished respectively with the penalties indicated in said articles, reduced by one-fifth.

###### ARTICULO 326. Those who violently or with intimidation invade the place where the

National Congress, the Supreme Court of Justice or the Council of Ministers is meeting, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years.

###### ARTICULO 327. Shall incur imprisonment of six months to two years.

1) Those who violently or with intimidation invade the premises where the office of a Secretary of State is constituted.

2) Those who restrict or in any way place obstacles to the freedom of the Ministers meeting in Council.

3) Those who employ force or serious intimidation, to prevent a Minister from attending his office or the Council of Ministers.

##### CAPITULO II

### CRIMES AGAINST THE FORM OF GOVERNMENT

###### ARTICULO 328. Commit crimes against the form of Government and shall be punished with

imprisonment of six to twelve years, those who execute acts directly aimed at achieving by force, or outside legal channels, any of the following purposes:

- 1) Replace the republican, democratic and representative Government with any other form of Government.
- 2) Alter the constitution of any of the Branches of the State, Legislative, Executive or Judicial, or attack their independence.
- 3) Deprive in whole or in part the Congress, the Executive Branch or the Supreme Court of Justice, of the prerogatives and powers attributed to them by the Constitution.
- 4) Vary the legitimate order of succession to the Presidency, or deprive the successor of the President of the powers granted to him by the Constitution.
- 5) Deprive the Council of Ministers or the person in charge of the Executive Branch, of the power to provisionally govern the State in the cases provided for in the Constitution.

###### ARTICULO 329. When the perpetrators of these crimes are officials they shall be

punished, in addition, with absolute disqualification of six to twelve years.

###### ARTICULO 330. Shall be punished with imprisonment of six to ten years whoever having

exercised under any title the Presidency of the Republic, promotes or executes acts violating the constitutional article that prohibits him from exercising again the Presidency of the Republic or performing again said position under any title.

The same penalty shall be incurred by those who directly support him or propose to reform said article.

When the perpetrators of these crimes are officials they shall be punished in addition with absolute disqualification for ten years counted from the date of the violation or its attempted reform.

##### CAPITULO III

### CRIMES COMMITTED BY PRIVATE INDIVIDUALS, EXCEEDING IN THE

EXERCISE OF THE RIGHTS GUARANTEED TO THEM BY THE CONSTITUTION

###### ARTICULO 331. Shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years and a fine of

thirty thousand Lempiras (L.30,000.00) to sixty thousand Lempiras (L.60,000.00), those who unlawfully convene or direct any meeting or demonstration. All those meetings attended by persons with weapons, explosive devices or blunt objects or otherwise dangerous, for the purpose of committing a crime, shall have the character of unlawful.

Those attending an unlawful meeting or demonstration who carry weapons, devices, explosives or blunt objects or otherwise dangerous, shall be punished with the same penalty as those who convene or direct them. Mere attendees shall be punished with half of the previous penalties.

Persons who on the occasion of the holding of an unlawful meeting or demonstration perform acts of violence against the authority, its agents, persons or public or private properties, shall be punished with the penalty provided in the first paragraph of this Article, without prejudice to those corresponding to the other crimes committed. Those who on their own initiative attend a lawful meeting or demonstration, carrying weapons, explosive devices or blunt objects or otherwise dangerous for the purpose of committing a crime, shall be punished with the same penalty applicable to those who convene or direct an unlawful meeting or demonstration.

###### ARTICULO 332. Shall be punished with three (3) to six (6) years of imprisonment and a fine of one

hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand Lempiras (L.200,000.00) the founders, ringleaders or leaders of gangs or unlawful groups. The other members shall be punished with the same penalties reduced by one-third.

Shall be punished with imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras (L.10,000.00) and confiscation whoever without authorization or corresponding permit is found carrying national or war weapons. Whoever carries a commercial weapon without the due permit shall have it confiscated. The confiscated weapons shall be delivered to the national police after an inventory drawn up for that purpose.

##### CAPITULO IV

### CRIMES COMMITTED BY OFFICIALS AGAINST THE EXERCISE OF

THE RIGHTS GUARANTEED BY THE CONSTITUTION

###### ARTICULO 333. The penalty of imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years and a fine of

fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) shall be applied to the public official or employee who:

- 1) Detains or illegally holds incommunicado a person or does not immediately comply with the writ of habeas corpus issued by competent authority;
- 2) Does not timely order the release of a detainee when legally appropriate or who retains him after having received the order of release of the same;
- 3) Makes victims of vexations or illegal duress the persons entrusted to his custody;
- 4) Does not process or resolve within the legal terms a petition for habeas corpus or amparo or by any means obstructs its processing; and,
- 5) Orders, executes or consents to the expatriation of a Honduran.

###### ARTICLE 334. Officials or public employees who perform any of the following acts shall be sanctioned with the penalties provided in the preceding Article:

- 1) Retain, conceal, destroy or violate postal, telegraphic, facsimile or any other type of correspondence;
- 2) Illegally restrict the freedom of movement of a person within the national territory;
- 3) Force a person to change domicile, except by sentence of a competent judge;
- 4) Force a private individual to provide their personal services without legal remuneration, except by judicial sentence;
- 5) Force a person to be part of an association dedicated to the execution of illicit acts.
- 6) Prevent or suspend a legally authorized meeting;
- 7) Prevent or hinder the free circulation of a printed material whose authors or publishers have complied with the legal requirements for its publication and sale;
- 8) Deprive a person of their books and publications of a scientific, philosophical, political or artistic nature or any other printed materials with the intention of restricting or preventing the free circulation of ideas; and,
- 9) Impose on prisoners or sentenced persons sanctions, deprivations or regulations not provided for or authorized in the laws or that exceed them.

##### CHAPTER V

TERRORISM

###### ARTICLE 335. Those who, with political purposes, attack the security of the State by executing any of the following acts commit the crime of terrorism:

- 1) Being part of the crew of a civil or military aircraft, national or foreign, that is flying over Honduran territory or whose destination is an airport of the Republic, or that is located at one of these, rise up against the commander or captain thereof, taking control or seizing its cargo;
- 2) Assault and seize a civil or military aircraft, national or foreign, diverting it from its destination, taking it from the place where it is located or retaining it against the wishes of its owner, commander or captain or forcing any of them to execute acts against their will;
- 3) Manufacture, possess, trade or transport without proper legal authorization, firearms, explosives, detonators, flammable products and communication equipment, clothing, uniforms and any other material or equipment for the exclusive use of the Armed Forces of Honduras;
- 4) Perform acts that have the purpose of damaging, deteriorating, harming or destroying the physical facilities of a productive enterprise or sabotaging its operation;
- 5) Plan, organize, coordinate or participate in the kidnapping or illegal detention of one or more persons;
- 6) Form bands, gangs or armed groups that invade or assault towns, hospitals, health centers, banking, financial or insurance institutions, shopping or work centers, temples and other similar places or take or obstruct highways or other public roads;
- 7) Form bands, gangs or armed groups that exert violence on persons or seize by means of threats property of any kind or force their owners, possessors, administrators or guardians to deliver them or collect periodic sums of money or valuables under the pretext of guaranteeing, defending or ensuring respect for the life or rights of one or more persons;
- 8) Damage the property of others through the use of bombs, explosives, chemical or flammable substances or other similar means;
- 9) Force another person to deliver, send, deposit or place at their disposal goods, money or other valuable objects, or documents capable of producing legal effects, by means of threats or violence or by impersonating a public authority or simulating an order thereof; and,
- 10) Force another person, by any of the means provided in the preceding paragraph, to sign or destroy documents or securities in their possession.

The crime of terrorism shall be punished with imprisonment of fifteen (15) to twenty (20) years and absolute disqualification for the same period. The attempt, in the cases referred to in paragraphs 1) and 2) of this Article, shall be punished with the indicated penalty reduced by one quarter.

##### CHAPTER VI

REBELLION

###### ARTICLE 336. Those who rise up in arms to overthrow the legally constituted government or to change or suspend in whole or in part the existing constitutional regime with regard to the formation, operation or renewal of public powers are guilty of rebellion.

The promoters, leaders or ringleaders of the crime of rebellion shall be punished with imprisonment of ten (10) to fifteen (15) years, a fine of one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to two hundred thousand Lempiras (L.200,000.00) and absolute disqualification for the duration of the imprisonment.

Those who, by designation of the rebels, perform administrative or jurisdictional functions shall suffer the same penalties reduced by one third.

The mere executors of the rebellion shall incur imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years, plus a fine of thirty thousand (L.30,000.00) to fifty thousand Lempiras (L.50,000.00), if there has been combat between the rebel force and the public force loyal to the government or the former has caused damage to private property, the State or its institutions, if it has destroyed or interrupted any public service, exercised serious violence against persons, or demanded contributions or diverted public funds from their legitimate investment; and the penalty of imprisonment of three (3) to five (5) years, plus a fine of ten thousand (L.10,000.00) to thirty thousand Lempiras (L.30,000.00) when such circumstances do not occur.

The proposal and conspiracy to commit the crime of rebellion shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years, plus a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras (L.10,000.00).

##### CHAPTER VII

SEDITION

###### ARTICLE 337. Those who, without being included in the crime of rebellion, rise up publicly and tumultuously to achieve by force or outside legal channels any of the following purposes are guilty of sedition:

- 1) Prevent the holding of elections for national, departmental or municipal authorities;
- 2) Prevent legitimately elected or appointed officials from taking office;
- 3) Prevent any authority from the free exercise of its functions or the fulfillment of its resolutions;
- 4) Prevent the approval, sanction, promulgation, publication or execution of any law;
- 5) Carry out any act of hatred or revenge against individuals or against State servants or against their property with political or social purpose; or
- 6) Break into penal centers or attack custodians of prisoners, either to rescue or to mistreat them.

Those guilty of sedition shall be punished with imprisonment of five (5) to ten (10) years, a fine of fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) and absolute disqualification for the duration of the imprisonment, if they act as instigators, ringleaders or leaders, and with imprisonment of three (3) to six (6) years and a fine of twenty-five thousand (L.25,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras (L.50,000.00) if they are mere executors.

###### ARTICLE 338. The proposal and conspiracy to commit the crime of sedition shall be punished with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years and a fine of three thousand (L.3,000.00) to six thousand lempiras (L.6,000.00).

##### CHAPTER VIII

PROVISIONS COMMON TO THE TWO PRECEDING CHAPTERS

###### ARTICLE 339. When rebels or seditionists dissolve or submit to the demand made to them by the legitimate authority for that purpose, the mere executors of any of those crimes are exempt from penalty, if they are not public employees.

###### ARTICULO 340. Those who publicly incite formally or directly to a rebellion or

sedition, communicate instructions or indicate the means to consummate said crimes, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years and a fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand lempiras (L.10,000.00) when these have not been attempted or consummated.

###### ARTICULO 341. Persons who, whether or not participating in a rebellion or sedition or who

on account of it, commit other special crimes defined in this Code, shall be sanctioned in accordance with the provisions established in the corresponding chapters.

###### ARTICULO 342. When it is not possible to discover the perpetrators, the principal leaders

of the rebellion or sedition shall be punished as such.

##### CAPITULO IX.

ATTACK

###### ARTICULO 343. Commit an attack:

1) Those who, without publicly rising up, use force or intimidation for any of the purposes indicated in the crimes of rebellion and sedition.

2) Those who assault the authority or its agents, or use force against them, or seriously intimidate them, or offer also serious resistance to them, while they are performing the functions of their positions or on occasion of them.

###### ARTICULO 344. The attacks covered in the preceding article shall be punished

with imprisonment of one to three years, provided that any of the following circumstances occur:

- 1) If the aggression is carried out with armed force.
- 2) If the accused are officials.
- 3) If they lay hands on the authority.
- 4) If as a consequence of coercion, the authority has acceded to the demands of the accused.

#### TITULO XIII

### CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION

##### CAPITULO I

CONTEMPT

###### ARTICULO 345. Anyone who threatens, slanders, injures, insults or in any other manner

offends the dignity of a public authority on occasion of the exercise of their functions, whether by act, word or in writing, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two (2) to four (4) years.

If the offended party is the President of the Republic or any of the high officials referred to in Article 325 of this Code, the imprisonment shall be two (2) to five (5) years.

##### CAPITULO II

DISOBEDIENCE

###### ARTICULO 346. Anyone who disobeys an authority by openly refusing to give due

compliance to judgments, resolutions or orders issued within the limits of their competence and clothed with legal formalities, shall be punished with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years.

###### ARTICULO 347. A foreigner who, legally expelled from Honduras, enters the national

territory again violating the corresponding order, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six (6) months to three (3) years and upon completion of the sentence shall be expelled from the country.

###### ARTICULO 348. Anyone who refuses or declines to perform a public office of popular

election without justified cause or after such cause has been dismissed by the competent authority, shall incur a fine of ten thousand (L.10,000.00) to thirty thousand lempiras

(L.30,000.00).

###### ARTICULO 348-A. The penalty provided in Article 348, above, plus imprisonment of six (6)

months to two (2) years, shall be imposed on anyone who, legally summoned as an expert or witness, abstains without just cause from providing the respective declaration or from appearing before the competent judge.

##### CAPITULO III

ABUSE OF AUTHORITY AND VIOLATION

OF THE DUTIES OF OFFICIALS

###### ARTICULO 349. The official or public employee who shall be punished with imprisonment

of three (3) to six (6) years and special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment:

- 1) Refuses to give due compliance to orders, judgments, rulings, resolutions, agreements or decrees issued by judicial or administrative authorities within the limits of their respective competences and with legal formalities;
- 2) Issues or executes orders, judgments, rulings, resolutions, agreements or decrees contrary to the Constitution of the Republic or to the laws or abstains from complying with the provisions of any of said legal ordinances;
- 3) Omits, refuses or delays any act that must be executed in accordance with the duties of their position;
- 4) When required by competent authority does not provide due cooperation for the effective administration of justice or another public service. When the lack of cooperation consists of failing to comply out of malice or negligence with an arrest order issued by competent authorities, the penalty shall be increased by one third; or
- 5) Reveals or facilitates the revelation of a fact of which they have knowledge by reason of their position and which must remain secret. When the revelation is not of serious significance, the penalty shall be reduced by one sixth.

The same penalty shall be applied to the officer or agent of the public force who refuses, omits or delays, without justified cause, the provision of assistance legally required by competent authority.

###### ARTICULO 350. Anyone who begins to perform a public office or employment without having

rendered the bond required by law or without having made the sworn declaration of assets ordered by the Law Against Illicit Enrichment of Public Servants or without having taken the corresponding legal oath, shall incur a fine equal to triple the monthly salary of the corresponding employee or official, without prejudice to the obligation to fulfill the legal requirements.

The same sanction shall be incurred by the official or public employee who has made it possible for a subordinate to begin performing the office or employment before having fulfilled any of the requirements indicated in the preceding paragraph.

###### ARTICULO 350-A. Anyone who continues performing an employment, commission or public office

after they should cease in it in accordance with the law, shall be sanctioned with a fine of fifteen thousand (L.15,000.00) to twenty thousand lempiras (L.20,000.00) and special disqualification of one (1) year to two (2) years.

###### ARTICULO 351. The person responsible for any of the crimes referred to in the two

preceding Articles who has received salaries, wages, rights or emoluments by reason of the office or employment, whether before being able to perform it or after having been legally suspended, must restore to the Treasury the sums received. If they do not do so, the restitution shall be commuted for imprisonment in the manner established for the fine.

###### ARTICULO 352. The official or public employee who abandons their position without their

resignation having been accepted, shall be sanctioned with a fine equal to the last three monthly salaries they earned and with special disqualification for two (2) to four (4) years, if the abandonment causes damages to the State. If it does not cause such damages, the penalty shall be special disqualification of one (1) to two (2) years.

If the official or public employee abandons the position in order not to prosecute or punish any of the crimes against the existence and security of the State or against the internal security of the State, they shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of four (4) to seven (7) years, plus special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment. If the purpose sought is not to prosecute or punish any other crime, they shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one (1) to three (3) years, plus special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment. The provisions of this Article shall not be applicable if within thirty (30) days following the date of submission of the resignation it has not been accepted or rejected.

###### ARTICULO 353. A fine of five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand Lempiras

(L.10,000.00) and special disqualification of three (3) to six (6) years shall be imposed on the official or employee who, knowingly, detains, prosecutes or judges a public official who enjoys immunity without having previously exhausted the procedures established by law.

###### ARTICULO 354. The public official or employee who usurps functions proper to another

position shall be punished with imprisonment from two (2) to five (5) years, plus a fine from five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand lempiras (L.10,000.00) and special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 355. The official who, legally required to recuse himself, continues acting

before the question of jurisdiction is resolved, shall be punished with a fine from five thousand (L.5,000.00) to ten thousand lempiras (L.10,000.00).

###### ARTICULO 356. The public official or employee, civil or military, who directs orders or

demands or in any way interferes in cases, matters or business that are within the exclusive jurisdiction of a judicial authority, shall be punished with imprisonment from three

(3) to six (6) years and a fine equal to triple the last salary received by the corresponding public official or employee, civil or military.

###### ARTICULO 357. The public official or employee who, knowingly, proposes or appoints

to a public office or position persons who do not meet the requirements established by Law, shall be punished with a fine from twenty-five thousand (L.25,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras (L.50,000.00) and special disqualification from one (1) to three (3) years.

Those cases in which the appointment is interim in nature and is made based on special laws are excepted.

##### CAPITULO IV

VIOLATION OF SEALS AND DOCUMENTS

###### ARTICULO 358. Whoever violates the seals placed by a public official or employee to ensure the preservation, identity or privacy of the contents of a thing shall be punished with imprisonment from one (1) to three (3) years, plus a fine from

fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to seventy-five thousand lempiras (L.75,000.00).

If the perpetrator were a public official or employee and had committed the act with abuse of his position, in addition to the penalty provided in the preceding paragraph he shall be punished with special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

If the act had been committed through the fault of a public official or employee, he shall be punished with a fine from twenty-five thousand (L.25,000.00) to fifty thousand lempiras

(L.50,000.00).

###### ARTICULO 358-A. Whoever removes, conceals, destroys or renders useless records or documents

entrusted to the custody of a public official or another person in the interest of public service or objects intended to serve as evidence before a competent authority, shall be punished with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years.

If the perpetrator of the act were the depositary himself, in addition to the preceding penalty he shall be subject to absolute disqualification for double the time of the sentence. If the act is committed through the fault of the depositary, he shall be subject to a fine from ten thousand

(L10,000.00) to thirty thousand lempiras (L.30,000.00).

###### ARTICULO 359. The public official or employee who, not being included in the

preceding Article, opens or consents to the opening, without proper authorization, of closed or sealed papers or documents whose custody had been entrusted to him, shall be punished with imprisonment from one (1) to three (3) years.

###### ARTICULO 360. The penalties indicated in the three preceding articles are also applicable

to ministers of any religion and to private individuals accidentally entrusted with the dispatch or custody of documents or papers by commission of the Government, or of officials to whom those had been entrusted by reason of their position.

##### CAPITULO V

BRIBERY

###### ARTICULO 361. The public official or employee who solicits, receives or accepts, by himself or

through another person, gifts, presents, offers, promises or any other undue advantage to execute an act contrary to his duties that constitutes a crime, shall be punished with imprisonment from five (5) to seven (7) years, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment, without prejudice to the penalty applicable to the crime committed by reason of the gift or promise.

###### ARTICULO 362. The public official who solicits, receives or accepts, directly or

indirectly, gifts, presents, offers, promises or any other undue advantage to perform an unjust act not constituting a crime related to the exercise of his position, shall be punished with imprisonment from two (2) to five (5) years. If said act is not consummated, the offender shall be punished with imprisonment from one (1) to three (3) years. In both cases absolute disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment shall be applied.

###### ARTICULO 363. When the gift or present solicited, received or promised has as its object

that the public official or employee abstain from executing an act that he should perform in fulfillment of his legal obligations, the penalty shall be imprisonment from two

(2) to five (5) years plus special disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 364. The provisions of the three preceding articles shall apply to

arbitrators, mediators, experts or any persons who perform a public function.

###### ARTICULO 365. The public official or employee who accepts a gift or benefit of

any kind from someone who has a matter submitted to his knowledge, shall be punished with imprisonment from one (1) to four (4) years, plus special disqualification equal to double the time of the imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 366. Whoever with gifts, presents, offers or promises corrupts or

attempts to corrupt a public official or employee, shall be punished with the same penalties applicable to passive bribery, increased by two thirds.

###### ARTICULO 367. (REPEALED)

###### ARTICULO 368. The gifts or presents referred to in the preceding Articles shall be

confiscated and publicly delivered to the municipal corporation having jurisdiction in the place where the crime was committed for works of social interest.

###### ARTICULO 369. The judge who accepts a gift, a present, a promise or a loan

to render, delay or abstain from rendering an order, resolution or ruling in any matter of which he is cognizant, shall be punished with imprisonment from five (5) to seven (7) years, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment. The penalties provided in this Article and in the preceding ones of this Chapter shall be applicable, as the case may be, to whoever being a member of a collegiate body or tribunal, votes in a certain direction due to bribery.

##### CAPITULO V-A

###### ARTICULO 369-A. The public official or employee who influences another public official or

employee by taking advantage of the powers of his position or any other situation derived from his personal or hierarchical relationship with the latter or with another official or employee to obtain a resolution that may generate directly or indirectly an economic benefit or of any other nature for himself or for a third party, shall incur the penalties of imprisonment from four (4) to seven (7) years, plus a fine from one hundred thousand (L.100,000.00) to one hundred fifty thousand Lempiras (L.150,000.00) and absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment. If he obtains the benefit sought, the imprisonment shall be from six (6) to nine (9) years, the fine equal to double the benefit obtained and the disqualification from five (5) to eight (8) years.

###### ARTICULO 369-B. The private individual who influences a public official or employee

by taking advantage of any situation derived from his personal relationship with the latter or with another public official or employee to obtain a resolution that may generate, directly or indirectly, an economic benefit or of any other nature for himself or for a third party, shall be punished, as the case may be, with the penalties of imprisonment and fine established in the preceding Article.

###### ARTICULO 369-C. Those who, offering to carry out the conducts described in the

preceding Articles, solicit from third parties gifts, presents or any other remuneration or gratuity, or accept an offer or promise, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years plus a fine from fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00).

In any of the cases referred to in this Article, the judicial authority shall also impose the suspension of activities of the natural or legal person, organization or office and the closure of their establishments or offices open to the public for a term of two (2) to four (4) years.

###### ARTICULO 369-D. In the cases provided for in this Chapter and in the preceding one, the gifts,

presents, commissions or presents shall be confiscated.

##### CAPITULO VI

MISAPPROPRIATION OF PUBLIC FUNDS

###### ARTICULO 370. The public official or employee who misappropriates funds, goods or

effects whose administration, collection or custody has been entrusted to them by reason of their office, or who without having been entrusted with them intervenes in such acts for any reason, shall be punished with imprisonment from two (2) to five (5) years if the value thereof does not exceed one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00) and from six (6) to twelve (12) years if it exceeds said amount, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

The provisions of this Article shall also be applicable to the directors of unions, peasant associative enterprises, cooperatives, patronages, charitable or sports associations and, in general, to all other similar civil entities.

###### ARTICULO 371. The public official or employee and the directors of the associations

referred to in the preceding Article who negligently allow another person to misappropriate the funds, goods or effects referred to in the same provision, shall be sanctioned with a fine from fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) and special disqualification from two (2) to four (4) years.

###### ARTICULO 372. The public official or employee who allocates the funds, goods or

effects that they administer to a purpose different from that which corresponds to them and if thereby does not cause damage to the patrimonial interests of the State, shall be sanctioned with a fine from fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to one hundred thousand lempiras (L.100,000.00) and special disqualification from three (3) to five (5) years.

If damage is caused to said interests or a public service is hindered, the fine shall be equal to one hundred percent (100%) of the value of the damage caused or the expenses that the State must incur to normalize the corresponding public service, plus absolute disqualification from five (5) to eight (8) years. In no case shall the fine be less than that indicated in the preceding paragraph.

###### ARTICULO 373. The public official or employee who, having available funds, unjustifiably

delays a legally demandable payment, shall be sanctioned in the manner provided in the first paragraph of the preceding Article.

The same sanction shall be imposed on the public official or employee who, legally required, refuses to deliver a sum of money or the funds, goods or effects that are under their administration or custody.

###### ARTICULO 373-A. The provisions of this Chapter shall be applicable to those who are in

charge for any reason of the management of departmental or municipal funds, revenues or effects or those belonging to an educational or charitable institution, as well as to the administrators or depositaries of funds seized, sequestered or deposited by a public authority even if they belong to private individuals.

##### CAPITULO VII

DEALINGS INCOMPATIBLE WITH THE EXERCISE

OF PUBLIC FUNCTIONS

###### ARTICULO 374. The public official or employee who directly or through another

person, or through simulated acts, takes an interest, with intent of personal profit, in any contract or operation in which they are participating by reason of their office, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years and absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall be applicable to private experts and accountants who have participated in the appraisal, partition or adjudication of property and to guardians or curators and to the trustees of a bankruptcy.

###### ARTICULO 375. The sanction established in the preceding article shall be applied to the

official or employee who, with purpose of profit, interposes their influence to obtain a resolution from any authority, or opinion that must be rendered before it.

##### CAPITULO VIII

FRAUD AND ILLEGAL EXACTIONS

###### ARTICULO 376. The public official or employee who by reason of their office participates

in any legal act in which the State has an interest and conspires with any of the interested parties to defraud the Treasury or with that same purpose takes advantage of their position to favor a third party or to facilitate their personal participation, direct or indirect, in the benefits that such matters may produce or uses any other artifice with the same purpose, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from six (6) to nine (9) years, plus absolute disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 377. The public official or employee who demands payment of a tax or

tribute, contribution or fee knowing that it is illegal or who, it being legal, employs vexatious or burdensome means for its collection or charge or falsely invokes a judicial order or other legitimate authorization, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to five (5) years, plus special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

The public official or employee who uses for their own benefit or that of third parties the exactions referred to in the preceding paragraph, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from four

(4) to eight (8) years, plus absolute disqualification for a time equal to double that of the imprisonment.

##### CAPITULO IX

MALFEASANCE

###### ARTICULO 378. The judge who with malice or awareness of the injustice renders a

judgment contrary to law to favor or harm a defendant in criminal matters, shall incur imprisonment from three (3) to nine (9) years, plus special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment.

###### ARTICULO 379. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to five (5) years:

1) The judge who with malice and true awareness of its injustice renders a judgment contrary to law in a non-criminal trial; and,

2) The official who with malice or awareness of the injustice renders a resolution, agreement or decree contrary to law in purely administrative matters.

###### ARTICULO 380. Shall incur the penalty of special disqualification from three (3) to five (5)

years:

- 1) The Judge who refuses to rule on the pretext of obscurity, insufficiency or silence of the law;
- 2) The Judge who through negligence or inexcusable ignorance renders a manifestly illegal judgment; and,
- 3) The administrative official who through negligence or inexcusable ignorance renders a manifestly illegal agreement or resolution, in a merely administrative matter.

###### ARTICULO 381. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from two (2) to four (4) years, plus

special disqualification for double the time of the imprisonment, the notary, attorney, licentiate in legal and social sciences or procurator who through abuse in the performance of their mandate or through negligence or inexcusable ignorance harms their client or discloses their secrets that they have learned due to the exercise of their profession.

###### ARTICULO 382. The legal professional or Procurator who has acted or is

acting as representative of a person and represents without their consent the opposing party or advises them in the same matter, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to five (5) years.

##### CAPITULO X

DENIAL AND DELAY OF JUSTICE

###### ARTICULO 383. A Judge who does not proceed with a petition, lawsuit, charge, complaint or report submitted in legal form or who maliciously or irresponsibly delays the administration of justice, shall be sanctioned with special disqualification from three (3) to six (6) years.

###### ARTICULO 384. A public official or employee who, failing in the obligations of their office, does not adopt the necessary measures to achieve the detention and prosecution of a suspected offender, shall be sanctioned with a fine from thirty thousand (L.30,000.00) to sixty thousand Lempiras (L.60,000.00) and special disqualification from three (3) to six (6) years.

##### CAPITULO XI

FALSE TESTIMONY, FALSE ACCUSATION AND FALSE REPORT

###### ARTICULO 385.

A witness, expert or interpreter who in their deposition, report, translation or interpretation, made before a competent authority, totally or partially falsifies the truth or conceals it, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to five (5) years.

The sanction shall be from three (3) to six (6) years, if the false testimony is committed in a criminal case to the detriment of the accused.

In all cases, absolute disqualification from three (3) to eight (8) years shall also be imposed.

###### ARTICULO 386. Whoever knowingly presents false witnesses in court shall incur the respective penalties of the preceding article.

###### ARTICULO 387. The crime of false accusation or false report is committed when acts are imputed to a person that, if true, would constitute crimes giving rise to ex officio proceedings, if the imputation is made before an administrative or judicial official who by reason of their office must proceed to investigate or punish it. However, proceedings may not be instituted against the reporter or accuser except after the case has been dismissed or an acquittal has been pronounced.

False accusation or false report shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from three (3) to six (6) years.

##### CAPITULO XII

CONCEALMENT

###### ARTICULO 388. Whoever, without prior agreement with the perpetrators or accomplices of a crime, but with sufficient grounds to suppose its commission, shall incur imprisonment from three (3) to five (5) years:

- 1) Hides the offender or facilitates their escape to avoid prosecution;
- 2) Procures the disappearance of evidence of the crime;
- 3) Keeps, hides, buys, sells or receives as pledge or barter the effects or instruments of the crime;
- 4) Denies the authority, without justified cause, permission to enter their domicile to apprehend the offender who is found therein;
- 5) Fails to communicate to the authority information they may have about the commission of a crime when obligated to do so by their profession or employment. In this case, in addition to the established sanction, special disqualification for double the duration of the imprisonment shall be imposed; or
- 6) Assists the perpetrators or accomplices to benefit from the product or price of objects originating from the crime or personally takes advantage of the aforementioned product or price.

When the concealment is executed with intent to profit, the penalty shall be increased by one third. If the concealer executes the acts referred to in this Article habitually, the penalty shall be increased by fifty percent (50%).

###### ARTICULO 389. Those who conceal their spouse or the person with whom they live in marital union, or their relatives within the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity, shall not be sanctioned, unless they have taken advantage themselves or have assisted the offenders to take advantage of the effects of the crime.

##### CAPITULO XIII

ESCAPE

###### ARTICULO 390. Whoever escapes while legally detained or convicted shall be sanctioned, in addition, with imprisonment from five (5) to ten (10) years. If the escape is effected by means of intimidation or violence against persons or force against things, the penalty shall be increased by one third, without prejudice to the sanction that must be applied to the crimes arising from the intimidation, violence or force.

###### ARTICULO 391. Whoever collaborates in the escape of a detainee or convicted person shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from five (5) to ten (10) years. If the person who has facilitated the escape is a public servant, in addition to the increase by one third in the penalty due to such status, special disqualification for double the time established for the imprisonment shall be imposed.

However, criminal liability is exempted if the person who collaborates in the escape of a detainee or convicted person is their spouse or person with whom they live in marital union, or their relatives within the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity, of the person who has escaped, excepting public servants.

###### ARTICULO 392. The custodian through whose fault the escape of a detainee or convicted person has occurred shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from one (1) to three (3) years. If the escape is through fault of the warden or other penitentiary official, the imprisonment shall be from three (3) to six (6) years.

If the escape occurs through intent of the custodian, warden or other penitentiary official or employee, they shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from four (4) to nine (9) years, a fine from fifty thousand (L.50,000.00) to seventy thousand lempiras (L.70,000.00), plus special disqualification for double the duration of the imprisonment.

##### CAPITULO XIV

GENERAL PROVISION

###### ARTICULO 393. For all purposes of this Code, any natural person who, by provision of law or by appointment of competent authority, participates in the exercise of public functions or holds a public office or employment, shall be considered a public official or employee.

Municipal mayors and councilors shall also be considered public officials.

LIBRO TERCERO

MISDEMEANORS

#### TITULO I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

###### ARTICULO 394. The provisions contained in Book One of this Code are applicable to misdemeanors, as pertinent, with the following modifications:

- 1) Only misdemeanors committed in the national territory are punishable.
- 2) Only consummated misdemeanors are punishable.
- 3) Only the perpetrators are liable for misdemeanors.
- 4) Conditional suspension of sentence shall be applicable to imprisonment imposed for misdemeanors, during a probationary period of two years.
- 5) The security measures established in Article 83 of this Code may be applied to perpetrators of misdemeanors, but shall in no case exceed one year.
- 6) Recidivism in misdemeanors may not be considered after one year has elapsed from the date of the conviction.
- 7) Confiscation of the instruments and effects of misdemeanors shall be decreed by the courts at their prudent discretion according to the circumstances.

###### ARTICULO 395. The judge may exempt from penalty in the sentence whoever commits a misdemeanor for the first time, when due to the minor nature of the act, the determining motives and the minimal dangerousness of the subject, there exist probabilities that they will not offend again, without prejudice to the corresponding civil liability.

#### TITULO II

MISDEMEANORS AGAINST PERSONS

###### ARTICULO 396. Shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from thirty to ninety days:

1) Whoever causes another injuries that produce illness or incapacity for work for up to ten days.

2) Whoever finding an abandoned or lost child under twelve years of age, does not notify their family or the authority, or fails to take them to a safe place within forty-eight hours following the finding.

3) Whoever in a tumultuous brawl has exercised any violence against the person of the victim, provided that no more injuries than those mentioned in the first numeral of this Article have been inflicted upon them, and it is not established who was the perpetrator.

4) Parents or legal guardians, whose means permit it, who do not provide the necessary means to educate their children while they are minors.

###### ARTICULO 397. Will incur the penalty indicated in the previous article, if the act is not

included in Book Two of this Code.

1) Whoever does not help or aid a person found in an unpopulated area, injured or in danger of perishing, when they could do so without risk or detriment to themselves.

2) Whoever verbally, in the heat of anger, threatens another with causing them harm that constitutes a crime, and by their subsequent acts demonstrates that they do not persist in the idea signified by their threat.

3) Whoever causes another unjust coercion or humiliation.

4) Spouses who scandalize with their domestic disputes.

What is said of spouses applies to the man and woman who live as a married couple. 5)Whoever threatens another with a weapon, or draws it in a fight except in lawful self-defense.

###### ARTICULO 398. Will suffer imprisonment from fifteen to sixty days.

1) Whoever causes injuries that do not prevent the offended party from engaging in their usual work.

2) Whoever mistreats their wife or the woman with whom they live as a married couple, when it does not produce injury to her.

3) Whoever stones a person or throws objects or substances at them without causing harm.

4) Whoever attacks a pregnant woman, without causing harm, when the pregnancy is evident, or their condition is known to them.

5) Whoever strikes or physically mistreats a person without causing injury.

6) Whoever verbally threatens another with causing them harm that does not constitute a crime.

7) Parents or guardians who exceed in the correction of their children or wards; provided they do not cause them injuries.

8) Guardians who abandon their wards exposing them to corruption or not providing them with assistance and education.

###### ARTICULO 399. Will be punished with imprisonment from ten to thirty days:

1) The woman who physically or verbally mistreats her husband or marital life companion, without causing him injury.

2) Whoever has themselves accompanied by minors under eighteen years of age in vagrancy or begging, or makes them work in violation of labor laws.

3) Whoever slightly injures another, if claimed by the offended party, whose pardon will extinguish the penalty. Articles 159 and 163 are applicable, as the case may be.

4) Whoever, required by another or others to prevent harm, fails to provide the aid requested, if no prejudice would result.

#### TITULO III

OFFENSES AGAINST PROPERTY

###### ARTICULO 400. Will be sanctioned with imprisonment from thirty to ninety days:

1) Whoever commits theft of movable property whose value does not exceed ten lempiras.

2) The perpetrator of fraud who causes patrimonial damage not greater than ten lempiras.

3) Whoever, finding a lost thing, does not deliver it to the authority or to its owner if they know who it is and appropriates it with intent of profit, when its value does not exceed ten lempiras.

4) Whoever for interest or profit interprets dreams, makes predictions or divinations, or abuses public credulity in another similar manner.

5) Whoever fraudulently obtains a service or provision knowing that they cannot pay for them, if their value does not exceed ten lempiras.

6) Whoever acquires objects of suspicious origin, purchased from a minor or from a person who can be presumed not to be their legitimate owner.

7) Whoever destroys, diminishes or damages another's property causing damage that does not exceed ten lempiras.

###### ARTICULO 401. Will be sanctioned with imprisonment from one to twenty days:

1) Whoever enters another's estate or field to pick fruit and eat it on the spot.

2) Whoever in the same manner picks fruit, crops and other forest products to feed them on the spot to horses or livestock, if the value does not exceed ten lempiras.

3) Whoever without the owner's permission enters another's estate or field, before having completely harvested the crops, to take advantage of gleaning and other remains thereof.

4) Whoever enters another's enclosed estate, if the prohibition to enter is manifest.

5) Whoever enters to hunt or fish in an enclosed estate or prohibited field without the owner's permission.

6) Whoever for any reason or pretext crosses plantings or sown fields without the owner's permission.

7) Whoever carrying vehicles of any kind, horses or harmful animals, commits any of the excesses provided for in the two preceding subsections, if by reason of the damage they do not deserve a greater penalty.

8) Whoever destroys or wrecks a hut, shelter, hedges, fences, enclosures or other defenses of properties, if the act does not deserve a greater penalty.

9) Whoever causes damage by throwing stones, materials or projectiles of any kind, if the act does not deserve a greater penalty.

###### ARTICULO 402. Will be sanctioned with a fine from five hundred (L.500.00) to nine hundred

Lempiras (L.900.00), the owner of livestock that enter another's estate or enclosed field and cause damage. If they do not cause damage, the fine will be from two hundred (L.200.00) to five hundred lempiras (L.500.00).

###### ARTICULO 403. If the livestock were introduced on purpose, or by abandonment or

negligence of the owners or those in charge, in addition to them paying the fine last expressed, they will suffer in their respective cases, imprisonment from thirty to ninety days, unless the act constitutes a crime.

###### ARTICULO 404. Will be sanctioned with the penalty of imprisonment from thirty to ninety days whoever

executes a fire of any kind that is not punished in Book Two of this

Code.

###### ARTICULO 405. Will be sanctioned with a fine from one hundred (L.100.00) to four hundred lempiras

(L.400.00):

- 1) Whoever violates the regulations or ordinances on burning stubble or forest goods or products; and,
- 2) Whoever violates the ordinances on hunting and fishing.

###### ARTICULO 406. Whoever causes damage whose value does not exceed five hundred lempiras

(L.500.00), will be sanctioned with a fine equal to double the respective value.

If the perpetrator of the damage removes or uses the objects or fruits of the damage caused, in addition to the fine provided in the preceding paragraph will be sanctioned with imprisonment from twenty (20) to thirty

(30) days.

###### ARTICULO 407. (REPEALED)

###### ARTICULO 408. Whoever taking advantage of waters that belong to another or diverting them from

their course, causes damage whose amount does not exceed ten lempiras, will incur imprisonment from five to twenty days.

###### ARTICULO 409. Whoever intentionally, through negligence or carelessness causes damage

of any kind not punished in this book or in the previous one, will be punished with a fine of one hundred

(L.100.00) to two hundred lempiras (L.200.00).

#### TITULO IV

OFFENSES AGAINST GOOD MORALS

###### ARTICULO 410. Will be punished with imprisonment from sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine

from seven hundred (L.700.00) to one thousand Lempiras (L.1,000.00):

- 1) Whoever offends modesty in a public manner;
- 2) Whoever exhibits themselves naked offending public decency;
- 3) Whoever gets intoxicated and causes scandal or endangers their own safety or that of others. If the intoxication is habitual, the judge may apply the security measure they consider adequate; and,
- 4) Whoever in public places addresses a woman in a vulgar manner or with disrespectful phrases or propositions; or bothers her with acts or attitudes offensive to modesty.

#### TITULO V

OFFENSES AGAINST GENERAL INTERESTS

AND REGIME OF THE SETTLEMENTS

###### ARTICULO 411. Shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to thirty days:

1) Whoever fails to fulfill the responsibility over persons whom the law has placed under their supervision.

2) Whoever having received counterfeit currency in good faith and after ascertaining its falsity causes it to circulate in an amount not exceeding ten lempiras.

3) Owners or managers of establishments who dispense or serve adulterated beverages or foodstuffs, harmful to health, or do not observe in the use and preservation of utensils intended for service, the established rules or customary precautions when the act does not constitute a crime.

4) Whoever violates the sanitary provisions issued by authority regarding the transportation of corpses and burials, in cases not provided for in Book Two of this Code.

5) Whoever, with acts that do not constitute a crime, desecrates corpses, cemeteries or burial places.

###### ARTICULO 412. Shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine

of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00), the physician or medical student, dentist or dental student, pharmacist or chemistry and pharmacy student, paramedic, nurse or midwife who, having provided assistance to a person regarding whom it may be suspected that they have participated in the commission of a crime, does not immediately report to the competent authority.

###### ARTICULO 413. Shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine

of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00) whoever violates the regulations or ordinances on the production and custody of flammable or corrosive materials or chemical products that may cause havoc.

###### ARTICULO 414. In the manner provided in the preceding Article shall be punished whoever:

1) Violating the orders of the authority does not carry out the repair or demolition of dilapidated, unhealthy or unsightly buildings;

2) Throws into the street or public place water, stones or other objects that may cause harm to persons or things, if the act does not have a greater penalty assigned in the preceding book;

3) Having left rubble, materials or other objects, or made pits, ditches or excavations in a place of public transit, does not place warning signs or protective barriers or does not alert passersby to the existence of the danger;

4) On balconies, windows, parapets or other exterior points of buildings places or suspends objects that, in case of falling, may cause harm to passersby or neighbors;

5) In violation of traffic or police provisions drives animals or vehicles of any kind in places where there is a concentration of people; and,

6) Drives or leaves in the public thoroughfare a draft, pack or racing animal, or any other animal, without having taken sufficient precautions to prevent it from causing harm.

#### TITULO VI

OFFENSES RELATING TO THE EXPRESSION OF THOUGHT

###### ARTICULO 415. Shall incur imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine of

eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand Lempiras (L.1,000.00), whoever:

- 1) Disseminates false news from which may result some danger to public order or damage to the interests or credit of the State, if the act does not merit a greater penalty; and,
- 2) Without committing a crime, publicly incites disobedience of the laws or of the constituted authorities, makes the apology of actions or omissions constituting a crime or offends morality, good customs or public decency.

#### TITULO VII

OFFENSES AGAINST PUBLIC ORDER

###### ARTICULO 416.

Whoever causes damage or deterioration to streets, parks, gardens or promenades, to public lighting, to aqueducts or sewers, to telephone or telegraph services; to bridges or other infrastructure works, damages or steals road signs or ornamental objects, artistic objects, historical monuments, archaeological or of public utility or recreation, even when they belong to private individuals, shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00), if the act by its gravity does not constitute a crime.

###### ARTICULO 417. Shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine

of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand lempiras (L.1,000.00) whoever:

- 1) With words, shouts, loud or repeated whistles or by means of sound instruments or in any other manner disturbs public order, the normal functioning of a court of justice or other state office or the peaceful development of a meeting or demonstration or other similar public act, if the act by its gravity does not constitute a crime;
- 2) Announces disasters, accidents or nonexistent dangers and thereby causes public alarm.

###### ARTICULO 418. Shall be punished with imprisonment of sixty (60) to ninety (90) days and a fine

of eight hundred (L.800.00) to one thousand (L.1,000.00) Lempiras, whoever:

- 1) By means of prints or engravings or in any other manner offends morality and good customs;
- 2) Fails to show respect and consideration due to a public authority or fails to observe a provision legally issued for reasons of justice, public safety, public order or hygiene;
- 3) Offends or disobeys agents of authority when they act in the exercise of their functions;
- 4) Does not provide to the public authority the assistance it requests in case of crime, fire, shipwreck, accident, flood or other calamity, being able to do so without harm or personal risk;
- 5) With noises or commotions or by means of sound instruments or acoustic signals disturbs the occupations or rest of persons; and,
- 6) In a public place or place open to the public or by means of telephone, doorbells or other sound means, by impertinence or for another reprehensible reason, causes annoyances or displeasures to a person.

The provisions of this Article shall not apply if by gravity the act is constitutive of a crime.

###### ARTICULO 419. In the same penalty as that established in the preceding article shall incur whoever

conceals their true name, status or domicile from the authority or official who asks them by reason of their office.

###### ARTICULO 420. In the same sanction of Article 413, shall incur whoever exercises without title

acts of a profession that requires it, when the act does not have a greater penalty assigned in this Code or in other laws.

FINAL TITLE

##### SOLE CHAPTER

TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

###### ARTICULO 421. If in accordance with the present Code the acts that are being judged or

on which no sentence has been rendered, do not constitute a crime or offense, the courts shall issue dismissal or the imposed penalty shall cease to be executed.

###### ARTICULO 422. The penalties of confinement, relegation, banishment and exile applied

in accordance with the Penal Code of 1906, other Codes and other special laws, shall be replaced by imprisonment without any modification with respect to their duration, unless the sanctions contemplated in the present Code are more lenient, in which case these shall be applicable; likewise, the penalty of suspension shall be converted ex officio into that of special disqualification.

###### ARTICULO 423. The institutions of "Conditional Suspension of the Execution of the

Sentence" and "Conditional Release", shall be applicable to those who have been convicted by virtue of previous laws.

###### ARTICULO 424. The terms established in this Code for the statute of limitations of

the crime, of the criminal action and of the penalty shall apply to those who have violated previous criminal laws, provided that said terms are more favorable.

###### ARTICULO 425. Only persons responsible for crimes, whose maximum penalty does not

exceed five years, may be heard in liberty during the process, if they provide the respective bond in accordance with the law.

###### ARTICLE 426. This Code shall enter into force one year after its

publication in the Official Gazette "La Gaceta", and from that date the Penal Code decreed on the eighth of February nineteen hundred and six, its amendments and other provisions that oppose it shall be repealed.
