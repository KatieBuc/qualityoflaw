# REPUBLIC OF LITHUANIA

REPUBLIC OF LITHUANIA

LAW ON PROTECTION FROM VIOLENCE IN THE CLOSE ENVIRONMENT

May 26, 2011 No. XI-1425 Vilnius

FIRST CHAPTER

GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Article 1. Purpose of the Law

1. This Law aims to protect persons from violence in the close environment, which due to its harm to society is classified among acts of public significance, to respond quickly to emerging threats, to implement prevention measures, to apply protection measures and to provide appropriate assistance.

2. Violence in the close environment is a violation of human rights and freedoms. This Law defines the concept of violence in the close environment, establishes the rights and responsibilities of subjects of violence in the close environment, the implementation of prevention measures, the provision of assistance in cases of violence in the close environment and the application of protection measures to persons who have suffered violence. Protection of children from violence is regulated in this Law and other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

## Article 2. Basic concepts of this Law

1. Close environment – an environment consisting of persons linked or previously linked by marital, partnership, affinity or other close ties, as well as persons living together and maintaining a common household.

2. Non-governmental organization – a public legal entity independent of state and municipal institutions, operating on a voluntary basis for the benefit of society or a group thereof, whose purpose is not the pursuit of profit or political power or only the implementation of religious objectives.

3. Assistance to persons who have suffered violence in the close environment (hereinafter – assistance) – psychologist, legal, social, health care, education, accommodation and other necessary services provided by state, municipal institutions and non-governmental organizations to persons who have suffered violence in the close environment.

4. Person who has suffered violence in the close environment (hereinafter – person who has suffered violence) – a person against whom violence in the close environment has been used, as well as a child who has become a witness to violence in the close environment or is living in an environment where violence has occurred.

5. Violence – intentional physical, mental, sexual, economic or other impact on a person by action or inaction, as a result of which the person suffers physical, material or non-material damage.

6. Perpetrator of violence – a person who commits violence in the close environment.

7. Subject of violence in the close environment – a person who has suffered violence or a perpetrator of violence.

8. Specialized comprehensive assistance to persons who have suffered violence (hereinafter – specialized comprehensive assistance) – comprehensive assistance provided by specialized assistance centers in cases of violence in the close environment to overcome a critical situation, specialized psychologist, legal and other assistance.

9. Specialized assistance center – an institution performing public functions, providing specialized comprehensive assistance to persons who have suffered violence.

10. Other concepts of this Law are understood as they are defined in other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

## Article 3. Principles of protection from violence in the close environment

Protection from violence in the close environment is implemented in accordance with the following principles: cooperation, participation, comprehensiveness, accessibility and quality, solidarity, appropriateness, coordination, legality, protection of individual rights and freedoms, proportionality, humanity, justice, efficiency, objectivity and effectiveness.

## Article 4. Prevention of violence in the close environment

1. State and municipal institutions, establishments and non-governmental organizations, according to their competence, develop and implement measures aimed at preventing violence in the close environment, and implement the requirements of this and other legal acts.

2. Prevention measures are implemented according to programs prepared by the Government of the Republic of Lithuania (hereinafter

– the Government) and institutions authorized by it and municipalities, financed from state and municipal budgets and international programs. The Government finances preventive activities carried out by non-governmental organizations.

3. State and municipal institutions, establishments and non-governmental organizations, in order to prevent violence in the close environment, according to their competence, take the following prevention measures:

- 1) organize public education and information campaigns encouraging non-tolerance of violence;
- 2) organize training and qualification courses for judges, prosecutors, police officers, specialists and other persons working in the field of prevention of violence in the close environment and assistance, according to programs prepared by the Government and institutions authorized by it and municipalities, financed from state and municipal budgets and international programs;
- 3) organize public legal education on acts that are considered violence in the close environment, the legal consequences of these acts and the inevitability of liability for their commission;
- 4) organize research, collection of statistical data on violence in the close environment and analysis of this data;
- 5) prepare courses teaching peaceful ways of resolving conflicts arising in the close environment;
- 6) do not issue permits to keep (carry) weapons or revoke existing permits;
- 7) take other prevention measures.

SECOND CHAPTER

PROTECTION OF PERSON WHO HAS SUFFERED VIOLENCE

## Article 5. Measures to ensure protection of person who has suffered violence

1. If a fact of violence in the close environment is established, the following measures to ensure protection of the person who has suffered violence are assigned:

- 1) obligation for the perpetrator of violence to temporarily move out of the place of residence, if he/she lives with the person who has suffered violence;
- 2) obligation for the perpetrator of violence not to approach, not to communicate, not to seek contact with the person who has suffered violence.
  - 2. The measures established in paragraphs 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of this Article are assigned until the end of the examination of the case, if the pre-trial investigation judge does not assign the preventive measures established in the Code of Criminal Procedure of the Republic of Lithuania – detention or the obligation to live separately from the victim. These measures are assigned by the pre-trial investigation judge by ruling no later than within 48 hours.
  - 3. The measures established in paragraphs 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of this Article are assigned together with the punishment, except in cases where the convicted person is sentenced to arrest or deprivation of liberty.
  - 4. The measures established in paragraphs 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of this Article are not assigned when the perpetrator of violence is a minor.
  - 5. When assigning the measures established in paragraphs 1 and 2 of paragraph 1 of this Article, if the person who has suffered violence or the perpetrator of violence cannot remain without supervision due to their physical or mental deficiencies or characteristics, the issue of supervision of these persons is resolved in the manner established by other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

## Article 6. Application of measures to ensure protection of person who has suffered violence

1. A police officer, having recorded an incident of violence in the close environment, without delay takes measures to protect the person who has suffered violence and, taking into account the established circumstances, initiates a pre-trial investigation, and informs the prosecutor if the prosecutor's request is necessary to initiate the pre-trial investigation.

2. An accelerated process is applied to procedural actions when the circumstances of the violence used in the close environment are clear, and the criminal case for the commission of that act must be examined in the district court.

## 7 straipsnis. Functions of Police Officers

1. Upon receiving a report about violence in a close environment, police officers, upon arriving at the scene of the incident or being witnesses to that incident themselves, record data about the fact of violence in a close environment and initiate a pre-trial investigation. A person who has experienced violence does not file a complaint.

2. Police officers organize the provision of assistance in the procedure established in paragraphs 1 and 3 of Article 9 of this Law.

3. When an obligation is applied for the perpetrator to temporarily move out from the place of residence, if he/she lives together with the person who has experienced violence, police officers immediately ensure the removal of the perpetrator. The procedure for eviction of the perpetrator is established by an institution authorized by the Government.

4. Police officers notify the specialized assistance center about the court-imposed obligation for the perpetrator to temporarily move out from the place of residence without delay, but no later than the next working day after the decision is made. In those cases where, when applying the obligation for the perpetrator to move out, a minor is left without supervision, police officers also notify the child rights protection department about this.

5. Police officers monitor how the court decision is implemented. The monitoring is carried out in the procedure established by an institution authorized by the Government.

THIRD SECTION

MANAGEMENT AND ORGANIZATION OF ASSISTANCE

## 8 straipsnis. Management of Assistance

1. The management of assistance includes planning, administration, organization of assistance measures, allocation of responsibilities and ensuring financing.

2. The Government approves a long-term state assistance provision and financing program prepared by an institution authorized by the Government, which is financed from state and municipal budget funds and international programs.

3. Institutions authorized by the Government are responsible for the preparation of a specialized assistance centers program. Specialized assistance centers are established giving priority to non-governmental organizations.

4. Specialized assistance centers provide specialized comprehensive assistance.

5. Municipal institutions and non-governmental organizations provide and implement long-term assistance services in the procedure established by laws.

6. The provision of assistance is supervised by responsible institutions.

## 9 straipsnis. Organization of Assistance

1. Police officers, upon arriving at the scene of a violence in a close environment incident, inform the person who has experienced violence about the possibilities of receiving assistance and, with his/her knowledge, report the incident to the specialized assistance center.

2. The specialized assistance center, upon receiving a report from police officers, immediately contacts the person who has experienced violence and offers him/her specialized comprehensive assistance.

3. Police officers immediately, but no later than the next working day, notify the child rights protection department if a minor has experienced violence in a close environment, was a witness to it, lives in an environment where violence occurred, or if the suspected perpetrator is a minor.

FOURTH SECTION

RIGHTS OF SUBJECTS OF VIOLENCE IN A CLOSE ENVIRONMENT

## 10 straipsnis. Rights of a Person Who Has Experienced Violence

1. A person who has experienced violence has the right:

- 1) to receive information from a police officer and from the specialized assistance center about institutions providing assistance under this Law;
- 2) to receive specialized comprehensive assistance provided for persons who have experienced violence;
- 3) to apply for and receive assistance in the procedure established by this Law and other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.
  - 2. The rights established in paragraph 1 of this Article also apply when police officers have not been contacted regarding violence in a close environment.

## 11 straipsnis. Rights of the Perpetrator

A suspected perpetrator has the right:

- 1) to receive information from a police officer about institutions providing accommodation services when an obligation to move out from the place of residence is applied;
- 2) to provide explanations regarding violence in a close environment;
- 3) to apply for and receive assistance in the procedure established by other legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

FIFTH SECTION

RESPONSIBILITY FOR VIOLENCE IN A CLOSE ENVIRONMENT

## 12 straipsnis. Responsibility for Violence in a Close Environment

1. Violence entails criminal liability.

2. A perpetrator who has caused damage to human health, property, environment, as well as caused non-pecuniary damage, must compensate it to the person who has experienced violence in the procedure established by legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

## 13 straipsnis. Responsibility for False Report

A person who has falsely reported about violence in a close environment or abused the rights of a person who has experienced violence is liable in the procedure established by legal acts of the Republic of Lithuania.

SIXTH SECTION

FINAL PROVISIONS

## 14 straipsnis. Entry into Force and Application of the Law

1. This Law, except for Article 15, enters into force 6 months after its publication in the publication "Valstybės žinios".

2. The provision of paragraph 4 of Article 8 of this Law is applied after the Government or an institution authorized by it approves the specialized assistance centers program within the time limits specified in paragraph 3 of Article 15 of this Law.

## 15 straipsnis. Proposals to the Government

1. The Government, within 3 months from the publication of this Law in the publication "Valstybės žinios", submits to the Seimas of the Republic of Lithuania draft amendments to laws related to the implementation of this Law.

2. The Government or an institution authorized by it adopts implementing legal acts of this Law before this Law enters into force.

3. The Government or an institution authorized by it, within 6 months from the publication of this Law in the publication "Valstybės žinios", approves the specialized assistance centers program. I promulgate this Law adopted by the Seimas of the Republic of Lithuania.
