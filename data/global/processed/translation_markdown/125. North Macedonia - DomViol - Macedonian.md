I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

Subject of the law

## Article 1

This law regulates the conduct of institutions with due diligence in taking measures for the prevention of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, the conduct of institutions for the protection of women from gender-based violence and domestic violence, the mutual coordination of institutions and organizations, services for the protection of victims, and the collection of data on gender-based violence against women and domestic violence.

Purpose of the law

## Article 2

The purpose of the law is the prevention and combating of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, effective protection of victims from any form of gender-based violence against women as well as victims of domestic violence with respect for fundamental human freedoms and rights guaranteed by the Constitution of the Republic of North Macedonia and international treaties ratified in accordance with the Constitution of 1 of 34

the Republic of North Macedonia (hereinafter: ratified international treaties). The purpose of the law is based on the principle of equality and elimination of stereotypes about gender roles.

Definitions

## Article 3

Individual terms used in this law have the following meaning:

- 1) "Violence against women" is a violation of human rights, discrimination against women and denotes all acts of gender-based violence that result in or are likely to result in physical, sexual, psychological or economic injury or suffering to women, including direct and indirect threats and intimidation for such acts, coercion, arbitrary restriction and/or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life;
- 2) "Gender-based violence against women" is violence directed against a woman because she is a woman or which disproportionately affects her. Gender-based violence against women encompasses the causes and result of the unequal power relationship between women and men as a result of a social, not an individual problem. Gender-based violence against women also refers to violence against women defined in points 1) and 3) of this article;
- 3) "Domestic violence" is maltreatment, insult, endangering safety, bodily injury, sexual or other psychological, physical or economic violence that causes a feeling of insecurity, endangerment or fear, including threats of such acts, against a spouse, parents or children or other persons living in a marital or extramarital union or common household, as well as against a current or former spouse or extramarital partner or persons who have a common child or are in close personal relationships, regardless of whether the perpetrator shares or shared the same residence with the victim or not;
- 4) "Close personal relationships" are personal relationships between persons who are or have been in partner relationships, regardless of whether the perpetrator shares or shared the same residence with the victim or not;
- 5) "Intimate partner violence" is behavior of a current or former spouse or partner that causes physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence;
- 6) "Sex" refers to the physical characteristics of an individual (reproductive system, chromosomes, hormones) according to which the sex that is assigned to individuals at birth is designated, based on a combination of bodily characteristics and internal reproductive organs;
- 7) "Gender" refers to socially constructed roles, behaviors, activities and attributes that a given society considers appropriate for women and men;
- 8) "Transgender person" is a person who does not identify by gender with the sex assigned at birth;
- 9) "Victim" is every natural person who is exposed to violence specified in points 1), 2), 3) and 5) of this article, on the territory of the Republic of North Macedonia;
- 10) "Perpetrator" is every natural and legal person who has committed or has not undertaken one of the acts from points 1), 2), 3) and 5 of this article;
- 11) "Woman" includes girls under the age of 18;
- 12) "Non-refoulement" is a principle based on which the state undertakes not to expel or in any way return women and children asylum seekers, persons with refugee status or persons under subsidiary protection to the borders of a state where 2 of 34 there exists an immediate danger to life and body, from violence against women or domestic violence or their freedom would be in danger due to their race, religion, nationality, membership in a particular social group or political belief or where they would be subject to torture, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment;
- 13) "Child" is every person under the age of 18;
- 14) "Physical violence" is any act of use of physical force or action that harms the health and bodily and psychological integrity of the victim.
- 15) "Psychological violence" is any behavior using coercion, intimidation or threat that causes a feeling of fear, endangerment, disturbance or injury to the dignity and psychological integrity of the victim;
- 16) "Stalking" is any intentional behavior of repeated threatening directed at another person that causes the person to fear for their safety;
- 17) "Economic violence" is any act of restricting or preventing the realization and/or disposal of personal income and financial resources, for maintaining the common household and for care of the child, thereby causing economic dependence of the victim;
- 18) "Sexual violence and rape" is any act of a sexual nature, with any body part or object, on the body of another person without his/her consent.

Sexual violence also exists when another person is induced or coerced to engage in acts of a sexual nature with a third person without his consent. Consent must be expressed voluntarily as a result of the free will of the person, assessed in the context of the circumstances;

19) "Sexual harassment" is any verbal, non-verbal or physical behavior of a sexual nature that has the purpose or consequence of violating the dignity of a person, especially when a threatening, hostile, degrading, humiliating or offensive environment is created;

20) "Sexual harassment via the internet" is any verbal, non-verbal or other conduct of a sexual nature, that has the purpose or consequence, of violating dignity or creating a threatening, hostile, humiliating or intimidating environment, access or practice, through electronic means of communication;

21) "Forced marriage" is forcing, enticing, misleading, threatening or otherwise coercing an adult or child to enter into marriage or extramarital union;

22) "Female genital mutilation" is the cutting, infibulation or any other mutilation in whole or in part of the labia majora, labia minora or clitoris of a woman;

23) "Forced abortion and forced sterilization" is termination of pregnancy on a woman without her being informed beforehand and giving written consent, as well as performing an operation on a woman without her being informed beforehand and giving written consent, i.e. understanding of the procedure whose purpose or consequence is to terminate her capacity for natural reproduction;

24) "Trafficking in women" is an act whereby through force, serious threat the victim is misled or other forms of coercion, abduction, fraud, abuse of one's position or the state of pregnancy, helplessness or physical or mental incapacity of another or by giving or receiving money or other benefit for obtaining the consent of a person who has control over another person or otherwise recruits, transports, transfers, buys, sells, shelters or receives persons for the purpose of exploitation through prostitution or other forms of sexual exploitation, pornography, forced 3 of 34 labor or servitude, slavery, forced marriages, forced pregnancy, illegal adoption or similar relationship, begging or exploitation for legally prohibited activity or inadmissible transplantation of parts of the human body and

25) "Coercive control over women" is abuse of power, use of threats, force or other forms of coercion, deception or misrepresentation with the aim of controlling the behavior and life of women.

Due diligence

## Article 4

(1) The state authorities, legal entities exercising public powers established by law, officials, employees and other authorized persons performing work on behalf of the state have an obligation to refrain from participating in acts of violence and to take all measures and activities to prevent any act of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence.

(2) The subjects from paragraph (1) of this article have an obligation to take all appropriate legislative, administrative, judicial and other measures to prevent, protect, investigate, punish and provide fair compensation to victims or restoration to the original position for acts of violence committed by natural or legal persons.

(3) Officials in institutions performing activities in the field of social protection, internal affairs, health, child protection and education, as well as the competent courts and public prosecutor's offices in proceedings for gender-based violence against women and domestic violence are obliged to act urgently, with due regard to the interests and needs of the victim.

II. BASIC PRINCIPLES

Non-discrimination

## Article 5

(1) Gender-based violence against women is discrimination against women that restricts them from enjoying rights and freedoms on an equal basis with men.

(2) The measures, activities and services under this law are undertaken with the aim of implementing the principle of equality, eliminating stereotypes about gender roles, prevention and protection from all forms of discrimination against women victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence.

(3) The measures, activities and services for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence are implemented without discrimination on the basis of race, skin color, origin, national or ethnic affiliation, sex, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, membership in a marginalized group, language, citizenship, social origin, education, religion or religious belief, political belief, other belief, disability, age, family or marital status, property status, health condition, personal characteristic and social status or any other basis.

(4) The measures, activities and services under this law, which are undertaken for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and domestic violence are not considered discrimination.

Prohibition of victimization

## Article 6

Any act undertaken with the aim of intentionally or unintentionally punishing or placing in a less favorable position a person who has taken actions for protection from gender-based violence and domestic violence, or if the person has reported violence, initiated a procedure for protection from gender-based violence and domestic violence, testified during the procedure or otherwise participated in a procedure for protection from gender-based violence and domestic violence, is prohibited.

Vulnerable women

## Article 7

The subjects from Articles 11 and 24 of this law are obliged when taking measures and activities to take into account the consequences that gender-based violence has on vulnerable categories of women, especially on pregnant women, women with children and children with disabilities, single mothers, women with disabilities, women from rural areas, women who use drugs, sex workers, migrants, refugees, asylum seekers, stateless women, lesbians, bisexuals and transgender people, women living with HIV, homeless women, women victims of human trafficking, elderly women, financially insecure women and others.

Reasonable accommodation

## Article 8

The measures, activities and services for victims of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence should be appropriately adapted to the specific needs of women with disabilities.

Principle of gender-responsive policies

## Article 9

(1) The measures, activities and services for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and domestic violence should be gender-responsive and recognize the characteristics and differences that arise from the gender roles of women and men, so that the results of the measures, activities and services can contribute to gender equality.

(2) Gender-responsive policies include the gender perspective as a criterion that is integrated in all phases of the policy-making process, assessment of situations, implementation and evaluation of policies arising from this law.

Principle of empowerment of women who suffer violence

## Article 10

(1) The measures, activities and services for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and domestic violence are provided in a manner that enables strengthening of the potential and capacities of the victim for independent life organization and self-help in the future.

5 of 34

(2) The victim has the right to participate in the assessment of her condition and needs, to be informed about the measures, activities and services for protection and to participate in the selection of services and measures for her assistance, support and protection.

(3) Without the consent of the victim, or her legal representative, measures, activities and services for protection cannot be undertaken, except when there is immediate danger to her life and body.

III. COMPETENT INSTITUTIONS AND INTEGRATED POLICIES

Competence for implementation of the law

## Article 11

(1) Competent for the implementation of this law are all state administration bodies, courts, public prosecutor's office, local self-government units and legal entities exercising public powers established by law.

(2) The subjects from paragraph (1) of this article are obliged to ensure that the work within their competence is performed by professional persons trained for the implementation of the work specified by this law.

Institutional and multi-sectoral cooperation

## Article 12

(1) The subjects from Article 11 of this law are obliged to cooperate with each other for the achievement of the objectives of this law by preparing a protocol for mutual cooperation.

(2) The local self-government units with institutions and associations prepare a protocol for mutual cooperation for taking measures for prevention, prevention and protection from gender-based violence against women and domestic violence in the local community.

Assembly of the Republic of North Macedonia

## Article 13

(1) The Assembly of the Republic of North Macedonia, upon proposal by the Government of the Republic of North Macedonia, adopts a National Strategy for Prevention and Protection from Gender-Based Violence against Women and Domestic Violence (hereinafter: the Strategy).

(2) The Strategy is adopted for a period of eight years and is implemented through two national action plans lasting four years each and annual operational plans at the level of the competent institutions.

(3) The Assembly monitors the implementation of the Strategy and the spending of funds from the Budget from the annual operational plans through the annual report of the National Coordinating Body from Article 15 of this law.

Government of the Republic of North Macedonia

## Article 14

The Government of the Republic of North Macedonia (hereinafter: the Government), within its competences, takes measures and activities for prevention and protection of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence through inclusion of the principle of equality and elimination of stereotypes about gender roles in the proposals 6 of 34 for laws and other regulations, organizing and financing national campaigns to raise awareness of all forms of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence and other.

National Coordinating Body for Implementation of the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence against Women and Domestic Violence

## Article 15

(1) The Government establishes a National Coordination Body for the implementation of the Council of Europe Convention on preventing and combating violence against women and domestic violence (hereinafter: National Coordination Body), with a mandate of five years.

(2) The National Coordination Body referred to in paragraph (1) of this Article is composed of one representative from the General Secretariat of the Government, three senior officials from the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy, a member and deputy member from the Ministry of Health, a member and deputy member from the Ministry of Justice, a member and deputy member from the Ministry of Education and Science, a member and deputy member from the Ministry of Internal Affairs, judges and public prosecutors, a representative of local self-government units and a representative of associations, trade unions and employers' associations, one representative each from the Commission for Equal Opportunities for Women and Men and from the Women MPs Club of the Assembly of the Republic of North Macedonia.

(3) The Judicial Council of the Republic of North Macedonia proposes the representative of judges as a member of the National Coordination Body.

(4) The Council of Public Prosecutors of the Republic of North Macedonia proposes the representative of public prosecutors as a member of the National Coordination Body.

(5) The Association of Local Self-Government Units proposes the representative of local self-government units as a member of the National Coordination Body.

(6) The Government announces a public call for the selection of three members of the National Coordination Body from among associations, who must have at least five years of work experience in the field of violence against women and domestic violence.

(7) The manner of work of the National Coordination Body is regulated by rules of procedure.

(8) The National Coordination Body is chaired by the Minister of Labor and Social Policy.

(9) Administrative and professional tasks for the needs of the National Coordination Body are performed by the Ministry of Labor and Social Policy.

IV. СОБИРАЊЕ И ЗАШТИТА НА ПОДАТОЦИ

Собирање на податоци

## Article 28

(1) The subjects from articles 11 and 24 of this law collect statistical and administrative data on the state of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence, based on sex, gender, age, community affiliation, residence and other data determined by this or another law, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Protection of Personal Data.

(2) The Ministry of Labour and Social Policy on a daily basis using the national platform for interoperability – Macedonian Information Highway (hereinafter: MIM), takes over the data from the subjects determined in paragraph

(1) of this article who are users of MIM, in accordance with the regulations in the field of electronic governance and electronic services.

13 of 34

(3) The subjects from paragraph (1) of this article who are not users of MIM and do not have the possibility for direct exchange of data through MIM are obliged to develop web services on an appropriate platform that meets the conditions determined by the regulations in the field of electronic governance and electronic services, which will contain the requested data from the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy.

(4) The Ministry of Labour and Social Policy, using the previously collected data from the subjects from paragraph (1) of this article, performs analysis and statistical processing of the same in its system for social rights and services. The analyses and reports that do not contain data protected by the Law on Protection of Personal Data, the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy publishes them on an annual basis in a form and content determined by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy.

(5) The data from paragraph (1) of this article that do not contain personal data, the competent institutions are obliged to publicly publish in processed form on their web pages, as well as in the Central Catalogue of Public Sector Data in accordance with the Law on Use of Public Sector Data.

(6) The form, content and manner of integrated collection of data from paragraph

(1) of this article, entry of data into the system for social rights and services from paragraph (4) of this article and the content of data from paragraph (5) of this article, are prescribed by the Minister of Labour and Social Policy in agreement with the Minister of Internal Affairs, the Minister of Health and the Minister of Justice.

VIII. URGENT AND TEMPORARY PROTECTION MEASURES

Urgent measure

## Article 57

(1) For the purpose of removing immediate and serious danger to the life and physical and psychological integrity of the victim and members of their family, an urgent protection measure is imposed, namely removal of the perpetrator from the home and prohibition of approaching the home, upon proposal by the Ministry of Internal Affairs, without the consent of the victim.

(2) The proposal for imposing an urgent protection measure, the Ministry of Internal Affairs submits to the competent court after the risk assessment made pursuant to Article 50 paragraph (2) of this law and the police report from Article 49 paragraph (7) of this law.

(3) The urgent protection measure from paragraph (1) of this article is imposed for a duration of at least ten and at most up to 30 days.

Temporary protection measures

## Article 58

(1) For the purpose of stopping violence, removing the consequences of committed violence and undertaking effective measures towards the perpetrator of violence, in order to eliminate the causes for repeated commission of violence, the court may impose on the perpetrator of gender-based violence against women and victims of family violence the following temporary protection measures:

- 1) prohibition to threaten to commit violence;
- 2) prohibition to mistreat, harass, telephone, contact or otherwise directly or indirectly communicate with the victim;
- 3) prohibition to approach at a distance less than 100 meters to the residence, school, workplace or specific place regularly visited by the victim;
- 4) removal from the home regardless of ownership for a duration of ten to 30 days;
- 5) prohibition to possess firearm or other weapon or the same to be confiscated;
- 6) obligation to return the items necessary for meeting the daily needs of the victim and family;
- 7) mandatory legal maintenance of the family;
- 8) mandatory attendance of counseling center for perpetrators of violence against women or family violence;
- 9) mandatory treatment of the perpetrator if they use alcohol, drugs and other psychotropic substances or have a mental illness;
- 10) mandatory compensation by the perpetrator of the medical and other costs incurred from the violence, and
- 11) any other measure which the court deems necessary to ensure safety and well-being of the victim and other family members.
  - (2) In procedures in which the court decides on temporary protection measures, the provisions of the Law on Civil Procedure apply, unless otherwise regulated by this law.

- 1) to establish the liability of the defendant for failure to act with due diligence established by this law;
- 2) to award compensation for damages to the victim due to failure to act with due diligence by the defendant and
- 3) to oblige the defendant to take actions to protect the victim in accordance with the provisions and deadlines established by this law.
  - (3) In the proceedings, the provisions of the Law on Civil Procedure apply accordingly, unless otherwise provided by this law.

Local jurisdiction

## Article 84

In all civil proceedings for protection of victims of gender-based violence and domestic violence, in addition to the court with general local jurisdiction, the court in whose area is the residence, registered domicile of the plaintiff or the seat of the shelter center where the victim is accommodated is locally competent.

Burden of proof

## Article 85

The plaintiff who claims that in accordance with the provisions of this law the principle of due diligence has been violated is obliged to present the facts that make the claim probable and then the burden of proof passes to the defendant to prove that he acted with due diligence.

Amicable settlement of disputes

## Article 86

In proceedings relating to the protection of victims, the court cannot refer to mandatory amicable settlement of the dispute.

Compensation of victims

## Article 87

(1) Victims have the right to compensation for material and non-material damage in accordance with the law.

(2) A victim who cannot collect his/her property claim awarded by a final court judgment from the perpetrator, shall be able to request that it be paid from the state fund in accordance with the law.

(3) Victims are exempted from paying all costs of proceedings for compensation of damages until the final collection of the property claim in accordance with the law.

XII. MISDEMEANOR PROVISIONS

## Article 101

(1) A fine in the amount of 800 to 1,000 euros in denar equivalent shall be imposed for a misdemeanor of a legal entity that fails to act in accordance with articles 41, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49, 50, 51, 54 and 55 paragraphs (2) and (4), 57 paragraph (1), 59 paragraphs (1), (3), (5) and (6), 73 paragraph (1), 75, 78, 79 and 80 paragraphs (1) and (2) of this law.

(2) A fine in the amount of 100 to 500 euros in denar equivalent shall be imposed for the misdemeanors from paragraph (1) of this article, on the responsible person in the legal entity.

(3) A fine in the amount of 50 to 250 euros in denar equivalent shall be imposed for the misdemeanors from paragraph (1) of this article on the official person.

## Член 102

(1) For the misdemeanors established in Article 101 of this law, the competent inspector, before submitting a request to initiate misdemeanor proceedings, is obliged to offer the responsible person or their authorized person a settlement procedure by issuing a misdemeanor payment order in accordance with the Law on Misdemeanors.

32 од 34

(2) When the perpetrator of the misdemeanor agrees with the settlement procedure, the inspector draws up a record and issues a misdemeanor payment order to the responsible person or their authorized person.

(3) The record and the payment order are signed by the inspector and the perpetrator of the misdemeanor. Upon receipt of the misdemeanor payment order, the perpetrator is obliged to pay the fine imposed in the misdemeanor payment order, within eight days from the day of receipt of the misdemeanor payment order.

(4) When a legal entity appears as the perpetrator of a misdemeanor, the record and the misdemeanor payment order are signed by the responsible person or their authorized person.

(5) The perpetrator who pays the fine within the deadline from paragraph (3) of this article will pay only half of the imposed fine, which right is instructed in the legal instruction.

(6) If the perpetrator does not pay the fine within the deadline from paragraph (3) of this article, the inspector will submit a request to initiate misdemeanor proceedings to the competent authority.

(7) In the settlement procedure, the fine in the misdemeanor payment order is imposed in a fixed amount prescribed by law, and if the fine is prescribed in a range, the minimum prescribed amount for the misdemeanor is imposed.

(8) In the procedure that ends with payment of a misdemeanor payment order, no procedural costs are paid.

(9) If the perpetrator does not agree to be issued a misdemeanor payment order, this will be noted in the record of the established misdemeanor, and the inspector submits a request to initiate misdemeanor proceedings before the competent court.

## Член 103

The determination of the amount of the fine for legal entities is carried out in accordance with the Law on Misdemeanors.

## Член 104

For the misdemeanors from Article 101 of this law, the misdemeanor proceedings are conducted and misdemeanor sanctions are imposed by the competent court.
