Annex to the announcement of the Marshal of the Sejm of the Republic of

Poland of January 9, 2020 (item 218)

ACT

of July 29, 2005 on counteracting domestic violence

Recognizing that domestic violence violates fundamental human rights, including the right to life and health and respect for personal dignity, and that public authorities have the duty to ensure equal treatment of all citizens and respect for their rights and freedoms, and in order to increase the effectiveness of counteracting domestic violence, the following is hereby enacted:

## Art. 1. The Act defines:

1) tasks in the scope of counteracting domestic violence;

2) principles of conduct towards persons affected by domestic violence;

3) principles of conduct towards persons committing domestic violence.

## Art. 2. Wherever the Act refers to:

1) family member – this shall mean a closest person within the meaning of Article 115 § 11 of the Act of June 6, 1997 – the Penal Code (Journal of Laws of 2019, items 1950 and 2128), as well as another person cohabiting or sharing a household;

2) domestic violence – this shall mean a single or repeated intentional act or omission violating the rights or personal interests of the persons referred to in point 1, in particular exposing such persons to the danger of loss of life, health, violating their dignity, bodily integrity, freedom, including sexual freedom, causing damage to their physical or mental health, as well as causing suffering and moral harm to persons affected by violence.

## Art. 3.

1. A person affected by domestic violence shall be provided with free assistance, in particular in the form of:

- 1) medical, psychological, legal, social, vocational and family counseling;
- 2) crisis intervention and support;
- 3) protection against further harm, by preventing persons committing violence from using a dwelling shared with other family members and prohibiting contact with and approaching the injured person;
- 4) providing a person affected by domestic violence with safe shelter in a specialized support center for victims of domestic violence;
- 5) medical examination in order to determine the causes and type of bodily injuries related to the use of domestic violence and issuing a medical certificate in this regard;
- 6) providing a person affected by domestic violence who has no legal title to the premises jointly occupied with the perpetrator of violence with assistance in obtaining housing.
  - 2. The minister competent for health matters shall specify, by means of a regulation, the model medical certificate on the causes and type of bodily injuries related to the use of domestic violence, taking into account the usefulness of the certificate for the legal protection of a person affected by domestic violence.

## Art. 4. Towards persons committing domestic violence, measures provided for in this Act shall be applied aimed at

preventing their contact with injured persons and applying corrective and educational interventions.

## Art. 5. The minister competent for social security matters shall specify, by means of a regulation:

1) the standard of basic services provided by specialized support centers for victims of domestic violence,

2) the qualifications of persons employed in specialized support centers for victims of domestic violence,

3) detailed directions for conducting corrective and educational interventions towards persons committing domestic violence,

4) the qualifications of persons conducting corrective and educational interventions

– taking into account the need to adapt the scope of assistance to the situation and needs of persons affected by domestic violence, as well as the effectiveness of services provided by specialized support centers for victims of domestic violence and the effectiveness of corrective and educational interventions towards persons committing domestic violence.

## Art. 6.

1. Tasks in the field of preventing domestic violence are carried out by government administration bodies and local government units according to the principles set out in the provisions of the Act of 12 March 2004 on social assistance (Journal of Laws of 2019, items 1507, 1622, 1690, 1818 and 2473) or the Act of 26 October 1982 on upbringing in sobriety and counteracting alcoholism (Journal of Laws of 2019, item 2277), unless the provisions of this Act provide otherwise.

2. The municipality's own tasks include in particular the creation of a municipal system for preventing domestic violence, including:

- 1) development and implementation of a municipal programme for preventing domestic violence and protecting victims of domestic violence;
- 2) providing counselling and intervention in the field of preventing domestic violence, in particular through educational activities aimed at strengthening the caring and educational competences of parents in families at risk of domestic violence;
- 3) ensuring places in support centres for persons affected by domestic violence;
- 4) creating interdisciplinary teams.
  - 3. The district's own tasks include in particular:
- 1) development and implementation of a district programme for preventing domestic violence and protecting victims of domestic violence;
- 2) development and implementation of programmes aimed at preventive actions intended to provide specialist assistance, particularly in the field of promoting and implementing proper educational methods for children in families at risk of domestic violence;
- 3) ensuring places in support centres for persons affected by domestic violence;
- 4) ensuring places in crisis intervention centres for persons affected by domestic violence.
  - 4. Tasks within the scope of government administration carried out by the district include in particular:
- 1) creation and operation of specialised support centres for victims of domestic violence;
- 2) development and implementation of correctional and educational intervention programmes for persons using domestic violence.
  - 5. Funds for the implementation and service of the tasks referred to in paragraph 4 are provided by the state budget.
  - 6. The voivodeship self-government's own tasks include in particular:
- 1) development and implementation of a voivodeship programme for preventing domestic violence;
- 2) inspiring and promoting new solutions in the field of preventing domestic violence;
- 3) development of framework programmes for the protection of victims of domestic violence and framework programmes of correctional and educational interventions for persons using domestic violence;
- 4) organising training for persons carrying out tasks related to preventing domestic violence.

## Art. 6a. Persons managing specialised support centres for victims of domestic violence must meet the

qualification requirements specified in Article 122 of the Act of 12 March 2004 on social assistance.

## Art. 7.

1. The voivode's tasks include in particular:

- 1) developing instructional materials, recommendations, intervention procedures in crisis situations related to domestic violence for persons carrying out these tasks;
- 2) monitoring the phenomenon of domestic violence;
- 3) appointing and dismissing the Voivodeship Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for Preventing Domestic Violence;
- 4) monitoring the implementation of the National Programme for Preventing Domestic Violence with the assistance of the Voivodeship Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for Preventing Domestic Violence;
- 5) supervision over the implementation of tasks in the field of preventing domestic violence carried out by municipal, district and voivodeship self-government;
- 6) control of the implementation of tasks in the field of preventing domestic violence performed by non-public entities on the basis of contracts with government administration and local government bodies.
  - 2. The provisions of Articles 126–133 of the Act of 12 March 2004 on social assistance shall apply accordingly to the supervision and control referred to in paragraph 1 points 5 and 6.
  - 3. The minister competent for social security shall determine, by regulation:
- 1) the organisation and procedure for conducting supervision and control, the qualifications of inspectors authorised to perform supervisory and control activities, as well as the template for the identity card authorising the performance of supervisory and control activities,
- 2) the qualifications of the Voivodeship Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for Preventing Domestic Violence
  - taking into account the need to ensure an appropriate level of task performance.

## Art. 8. The tasks of the minister competent for social security include in particular:

1) commissioning and financing research, expert opinions and analyses concerning the phenomenon of domestic violence;

2) conducting activities promoting the raising of public awareness regarding the causes and effects of domestic violence;

3) appointing and dismissing the National Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Programme for Preventing Domestic Violence with the rank of secretary or undersecretary of state in the office serving the minister competent for social security;

4) monitoring the implementation of the National Programme for Preventing Domestic Violence with the assistance of the coordinator referred to in point 3;

5) developing and issuing at least once every two years guidelines for conducting training in the field of preventing domestic violence;

6) developing and financing protective programmes in the field of preventing domestic violence;

7) financially supporting programmes in the field of preventing domestic violence implemented by:

- a) local government units,
- b) non-governmental organisations working to prevent domestic violence,
- c) organisational units operating on the basis of provisions on the State's relationship with the Catholic Church in the Republic of Poland, the State's relationship with other churches and religious associations, and on guarantees of freedom of conscience and religion, if their statutory objectives include conducting activities in the field of preventing domestic violence.

## Art. 8a. The tasks of the Prosecutor General include developing and issuing at least once every two years gui-

delines concerning the principles of conduct of general organisational units of the prosecutor's office in the field of preventing domestic violence.

## Art. 9.

1. Government administration and local government bodies cooperate with non-governmental organisations and churches and religious associations in the field of providing assistance to persons affected by violence, influencing persons using violence, and raising public awareness about the causes and effects of domestic violence.

2. Government administration or local government bodies may commission the performance of tasks specified in the Act in the manner provided for in the Act of 24 April 2003 on public benefit activity and volunteer work (Journal of Laws of 2019, items 688, 1570 and 2020).

## Art. 9a.

1. The municipality undertakes actions to prevent domestic violence, in particular within the framework of work in an interdisciplinary team.

2. The interdisciplinary team is established by the mayor, town mayor or city president.

3. The interdisciplinary team consists of representatives of:

- 1) organizational units of social assistance;
- 2) the municipal commission for solving alcohol problems;
- 3) the Police;
- 4) education;
- 5) healthcare;
- 6) non-governmental organizations.
  - 4. The interdisciplinary team also includes court probation officers.
  - 5. The interdisciplinary team may also include prosecutors and representatives of entities other than those specified in paragraph 3, operating to prevent domestic violence.
  - 6. The chairman of the interdisciplinary team is elected at the first meeting of the team from among its members.
  - 7. Meetings of the interdisciplinary team take place depending on needs, but not less frequently than once every three months.
  - 8. The interdisciplinary team operates on the basis of agreements concluded between the mayor, town mayor or city president and the entities referred to in paragraphs 3 or 5.

9.1) Organizational and technical support for the interdisciplinary team is provided by the social assistance center, and in the case of transformation of the social assistance center into a social services center under the provisions of the Act of July 19, 2019 on the provision of social services by the social services center (Journal of Laws item 1818) – the social services center.

10. The interdisciplinary team may create working groups to solve problems related to the occurrence of domestic violence in individual cases.

11. Working groups consist of representatives of:

- 1) organizational units of social assistance;
- 2) the municipal commission for solving alcohol problems;
- 3) the Police;
- 4) education;
- 5) healthcare.
  - 12. Working groups may also include court probation officers, as well as representatives of other entities, specialists in the field of preventing domestic violence.
  - 13. Members of the interdisciplinary team and working groups perform tasks within the framework of official or professional duties.
  - 14. Work within working groups is carried out depending on needs reported by the interdisciplinary team or resulting from problems occurring in individual cases.
  - 15. The municipal council shall determine, by resolution, the mode and manner of appointing and dismissing members of the interdisciplinary team and detailed conditions for its functioning.
- 1) As worded by Article 68 of the Act of July 19, 2019 on the provision of social services by the social services center (Journal of Laws item 1818), which entered into force on January 1, 2020.

## Art. 9b.

1. The interdisciplinary team implements actions specified in the municipal program for preventing domestic violence and protecting victims of domestic violence.

2. The task of the interdisciplinary team is to integrate and coordinate the actions of the entities referred to in Article 9a paragraphs 3 and 5, and specialists in the field of preventing domestic violence, in particular by:

- 1) diagnosing the problem of domestic violence;
- 2) undertaking actions in an environment at risk of domestic violence aimed at preventing this phenomenon;
- 3) initiating intervention in an environment affected by domestic violence;
- 4) disseminating information about institutions, persons and possibilities of providing assistance in the local environment;
- 5) initiating actions towards persons using domestic violence.
  - 3. The tasks of working groups include, in particular:
- 1) developing and implementing an assistance plan in individual cases of domestic violence;
- 2) monitoring the situation of families where violence occurs and families at risk of violence;
- 3) documenting actions taken towards families where violence occurs and the effects of these actions.

## Art. 9d.

1. Undertaking intervention in the environment with regard to a family affected by violence takes place based on the "Blue Cards" procedure and does not require the consent of the person affected by domestic violence.

2. The "Blue Cards" procedure includes all activities undertaken and carried out by representatives of organisational units of social welfare, municipal committees for solving alcohol problems, the Police, education and healthcare, in connection with justified suspicion of occurrence of domestic violence.

3. Representatives of the entities referred to in paragraph 2 implement the "Blue Cards" procedure based on the principle of cooperation and provide information on actions taken to the chairperson of the interdisciplinary team.

4. Initiation of the "Blue Cards" procedure takes place by completing the "Blue Card" form in the event of suspicion, in the course of official or professional activities, of the use of violence against family members or as a result of a notification made by a family member or by a person who is a witness to domestic violence.

5. The Council of Ministers shall specify, by way of regulation, the "Blue Cards" procedure and templates of "Blue Card" forms completed by representatives of entities implementing the "Blue Cards" procedure, having regard to the effectiveness of actions towards persons affected by domestic violence and the welfare of these persons.

## Art. 10.

1. In order to create conditions for effective counteracting of domestic violence, the Council of Ministers shall adopt the National Program for Counteracting Domestic Violence, specifying detailed actions in the scope of:

- 1) ensuring protection and providing assistance to persons affected by domestic violence;
- 2) correctional and educational interventions towards persons using domestic violence;
- 3) raising social awareness about the causes and effects of domestic violence and promoting educational methods without the use of violence;
- 4) disseminating information about the possibilities and forms of providing assistance to both persons affected by violence and those using violence in the family.
  - 2. The National Program for Counteracting Domestic Violence at the central level shall be implemented by the coordinator referred to in Art. 8 point 3.
  - 3. The National Program for Counteracting Domestic Violence at the voivodeship level shall be implemented by the coordinator referred to in Art. 7 sec. 1 point 3.

## Art. 10a.

1. A Monitoring Team for Counteracting Domestic Violence is hereby established, hereinafter referred to as the "Team", as an advisory and consultative body of the minister competent for social security matters.

2. The term of office of the Team lasts 3 years.

3. The tasks of the Team include in particular:

- 1) initiating and supporting activities aimed at counteracting domestic violence;
- 2) monitoring activities in the field of counteracting domestic violence;
- 3) expressing opinions on matters concerning the application of the Act and initiating amendments to provisions in the field of counteracting domestic violence;
- 4) expressing opinions in case of disputes between public administration authorities and non-governmental organizations implementing tasks in the field of counteracting domestic violence;
- 5) expressing opinions on public tasks in the area of counteracting domestic violence and on entrusting these tasks for implementation by the entities listed in Art. 9 sec. 1;
- 6) developing standards for assistance to victims of domestic violence and work with persons using domestic violence;
- 7) creating, in cooperation with the entities listed in Art. 9 sec. 1, mechanisms for informing about standards for providing assistance to victims of domestic violence and work with persons using domestic violence;
- 8) disseminating the results of monitoring activities in the field of counteracting domestic violence.

## Art. 10b.

1. The minister competent for social security matters shall appoint to the Team:

- 1) the National Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Program for Counteracting Domestic Violence;
- 2) seven representatives of government administration authorities and entities subordinate to them or supervised by them, appointed from among persons nominated by these authorities and persons managing these entities;
- 3) five representatives of local government units appointed from among persons nominated by the local government side in the Joint Commission of the Government and Local Government;
- 4) ten representatives of non-governmental organizations, associations and agreements of non-governmental organizations, and churches and religious associations appointed from among persons nominated by these entities.
  - 2. The minister competent for social security matters shall dismiss a member of the Team before the end of the term of office:
- 1) at his or her request;
- 2) at the request of the entity which he or she represents;
- 3) in case of conviction by a final judgment for an intentional crime or intentional fiscal crime.

## Art. 10c.

1. The Chairperson of the Team is the National Coordinator for the Implementation of the National Program for Counteracting Domestic Violence.

2. Meetings of the Team shall be convened by the Chairperson of the Team or at the request of at least one quarter of the number of members of the Team, but not less frequently than once every six months.

3. Members of the Team shall benefit from leave from work in order to participate in meetings of the Team and shall be entitled to reimbursement of travel costs from the state budget from the part administered by the minister competent for social security matters.

## Art. 10d. Government administration authorities and state organizational entities subordinate to them shall, at the request of the Chairperson of the Team, provide comprehensive assistance to the Team in performing its tasks, and in particular shall present necessary information or documents.

## Art. 10e. Administrative and office support for the Team shall be provided by the office serving the minister competent for social security matters.

## Art. 10f. The minister competent for social security matters shall specify, by way of regulation, the procedure for appointing members of the Team and the organization and mode of operation of the Team, as well as the rules for participation in its work, taking into account the need to ensure an appropriate level of performance of tasks by the Team.

## Art. 11. The Council of Ministers shall submit annually to the Sejm and Senate of the Republic of Poland, by September 30, a report on the implementation of the National Program for Counteracting Domestic Violence.

## Art. 11a.

1. If a family member jointly occupying a dwelling, by his or her behavior consisting in the use of domestic violence, makes living together particularly burdensome, the person affected by violence may demand that the court order him or her to vacate the dwelling.

2. The court shall hear the case under the provisions of the Act of 17 November 1964 – Code of Civil Procedure (Journal of Laws of 2019, item 1460, as amended4)) on non-contentious proceedings. The ruling shall be issued after conducting a hearing, which should take place within one month from the date of receipt of the application. It becomes enforceable upon its announcement and may be changed or revoked in case of change of circumstances.

3. To the performance of the obligation adjudicated on the basis of sec. 1, the provisions on enforcement of the obligation to vacate premises serving to satisfy the housing needs of the debtor shall apply accordingly.

## Art. 12.

1. Persons who, in connection with the performance of their official or professional duties, have formed a suspicion of the commission of an ex officio prosecuted crime involving the use of domestic violence, shall immediately notify the Police or the prosecutor thereof.

2. Persons who witness domestic violence should notify the Police, the prosecutor or another entity acting to counteract domestic violence thereof.

4) Amendments to the consolidated text of the said Act were published in the Journal of Laws of 2019, items 1469, 1495, 1649, 1655, 1798, 1802, 1818, 2070, 2089, 2128 and 2217.

## Art. 12a.

1. In case of direct threat to the life or health of a child in connection with domestic violence, a social worker performing official duties has the right to remove the child from the family and place them with another non-cohabiting closest person, within the meaning of Art. 115 § 11 of the Act of 6 June 1997 – Criminal Code, in a foster family or in a care and educational institution.

2. The procedure for placing children in a foster family or in a care and educational institution is regulated by the provisions of the Act of 9 June 2011 on supporting the family and the foster care system (Journal of Laws of 2019, items 1111, 924 and 1818).

3. The decision referred to in para. 1 is made by the social worker jointly with a Police officer, as well as with a physician, or medical rescuer, or nurse. The provisions of Art. 59810, Art. 59811 § 3 and Art. 59812 § 1 first sentence of the Act of 17 November 1964 – Code of Civil Procedure shall apply accordingly.

4. The social worker has the obligation to immediately notify the guardianship court, no later than within

24 hours, of the removal of the child from the family and their placement with a non-cohabiting closest person, in a foster family or in a care and educational institution.

5. To the placement of a child with a non-cohabiting closest person, the provisions concerning the placement of a child in a foster family or in a care and educational institution shall apply accordingly.

6. The minister competent for internal affairs, in consultation with the minister competent for social security, shall determine, by regulation, the procedure for Police actions when performing the activities referred to in para. 1 and 3, as well as the method of documenting the activities carried out by the Police, taking into account the need to provide effective assistance to children.

## Art. 12b.

1. Parents, legal guardians or de facto guardians have the right to appeal to the guardianship court against the removal of the child referred to in Art. 12a. In the appeal, one may request an examination of the justification and legality of the child's removal and the correctness of its execution.

2. The appeal may be submitted through the social worker or Police officer who carried out the removal of the child. In such case, the appeal shall be immediately forwarded to the guardianship court.

3. The court shall examine the appeal immediately, but no later than within 24 hours. In case of finding the removal of the child unjustified or illegal, the court shall order the immediate return of the child to the parents, legal guardians or de facto guardians from whom the child was removed.

4. In case of finding that the removal of the child was unjustified, illegal or incorrect, the court shall notify the superiors of the persons who carried out the removal.

## Art. 12c. About the right to file an appeal, together with an indication of the guardianship court having local jurisdiction to

examine it, the social worker or Police officer shall instruct the parents, legal guardians or de facto guardians of the child. This instruction shall be handed over in writing.

## Art. 12d.

1. In the case where a professional probation officer's motion for ordering the execution of a conditionally suspended sentence of imprisonment or revocation of conditional early release concerns a person convicted of a crime committed with the use of violence or unlawful threat against a family member, who during the probation period grossly violated the legal order by again using violence or unlawful threat against a family member, the court competent to examine the motion shall order the detention and forcible bringing of the convicted person to the hearing on the examination of the motion.

2. The detained person, upon their request, shall be immediately enabled to establish contact with a lawyer in an available form, as well as to have a direct conversation with them.

3. The court's ruling on ordering the execution of the sentence or on the revocation of conditional early release shall be executed from the moment of its issuance; the court that issued the ruling or the court competent to examine the appeal may suspend the execution of the ruling.
