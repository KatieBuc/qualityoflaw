PENAL CODE

PRELIMINARY TITLE

ON PENAL GUARANTEES AND THE APPLICATION OF PENAL LAW

###### Artículo 1 Principle of legality

No person may be convicted for an action or omission that is not provided as a crime or offense by penal law prior to its commission. Security measures and accessory consequences may only be applied when the prerequisites previously established by law are met.

No crime or offense shall be sanctioned with a penalty, security measure or accessory consequence that is not provided for by the law prior to its commission.

Under no motive or circumstance may indeterminate penalties or accessory consequences be imposed.

Penal laws, insofar as they establish or aggravate penal responsibility, shall not be applied to cases other than those expressly included in them. Under no circumstances may the Public Administration impose measures or sanctions that imply deprivation of liberty.

###### Art. 2 Principle of non-retroactivity

Penal law does not have retroactive effect, except when it favors the accused. If after the commission of a crime or offense, a new law enters into force, in the particular case being judged, the one most favorable to the accused shall be applied. This principle also applies to convicted persons who are pending full or partial completion of the sentence.

Acts committed under the validity of a temporary law shall be judged according to it, unless the subsequent law unequivocally shows otherwise.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 3 Law issued before completion of sentence

If the entry into force of a new law occurs before completion of the sentence and is favorable to the convicted person, the competent Judge or Court must modify the sentence in accordance with it regarding the penalty or security measure.

If the conviction was based on an act considered as a crime or offense by the prior law and the new law does not sanction it as such, the competent Judge or Court must order the immediate release of the accused or convicted person. In case of doubt regarding the determination of the most favorable law, the convicted person shall be heard.

###### Art. 4 Principle of human dignity

The State guarantees that every person charged with a crime or penal offense has the right to be treated with the respect due to the inherent dignity of the human being. Penalties or security measures that imply torture, inhuman, cruel, defamatory or degrading procedures or treatment may not be imposed.

###### Art. 5 Principle of recognition and protection of the victim

The State guarantees every person who has been a victim of a crime or penal offense the recognition and protection of their rights and guarantees, including being treated by criminal justice with the respect due to the inherent dignity of the human being.

###### Art. 6 Jurisdictional and execution guarantee

No penalty or security measure may be executed except by virtue of a final sentence issued by the competent courts of justice, in accordance with procedural laws.

Nor may a penalty or security measure be executed in any form other than that prescribed by law and the regulations that develop it. The execution of the penalty or security measure shall be carried out under the control of the competent judges and courts, in accordance with the law and its regulations.

###### Art. 7 Principle of harm

Only conduct that damages or significantly endangers a legal interest protected by penal law may be sanctioned.

###### Art. 8 Principles of personal responsibility and humanity

A person is only responsible for their own acts. The penalty does not extend beyond the person of the convicted.

No penalty or penalties shall be imposed that, individually or collectively, last more than thirty years.

Law No. 641

This rule is also applicable to security measures.

###### Art. 9 Principles of subjective responsibility and culpability

A penalty or security measure shall only be imposed if the action or omission has been committed with intent or negligence. Consequently, objective liability for the result is prohibited.

There is no penalty without culpability. The penalty may not exceed that which is proportionate to the degree of culpability with respect to the crime; consequently, the penalty shall be adjusted according to the lesser culpability.

###### Art. 10 Extensive interpretation and analogical application

Extensive interpretation and analogical application are prohibited in penal matters for:

- a) Creating crimes, offenses, aggravating circumstances of responsibility, sanctions or security measures and accessory consequences not provided for in the law;
- b) Expanding the limits of legal conditions that permit the application of a sanction, security measure and accessory consequence;
- c) Expanding the limits of sanctions, security measures and accessory consequences legally provided for.

On the contrary, provisions that favor the accused may be applied analogically.

###### Art. 11 Apparent concurrence of laws

Acts susceptible to being qualified according to two or more provisions of this Code, and not included in articles 84 and 85, shall be sanctioned in accordance with the following rules:

- a) The special rule prevails over the general;
- b) The subsidiary provision shall only be applied in the absence of the principal one, both when such subsidiarity is expressly declared and when it is tacitly deducible.
- c) The complex provision or the provision whose infraction normally implies that of another less serious sanction shall absorb those that punish the infractions subsumed in it;
- d) When application of any of the three preceding rules is not possible, the penal provision that sanctions most severely shall exclude those that punish with a lesser penalty.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 12 Time and place of commission of the crime

The punishable act is considered committed at the moment of the action or omission, even when the time of the result is different. However, for purposes of prescription, in crimes of result the act is considered committed at the moment the result occurs.

The punishable act is considered committed both in the place where the criminal activity of the perpetrators and participants took place, wholly or partially, and in the place where the result or its effects occurred or should have occurred. In pure crimes of omission, the act is considered committed where the omitted action should have taken place.

###### Art. 13

Application of penal law. Principle of territoriality

Nicaraguan penal laws are applicable to crimes and offenses committed in Nicaraguan territory, except for the exceptions established in international instruments ratified by Nicaragua.

Nicaraguan penal law is also applicable to acts committed on vessels, aircraft and ships flying the Nicaraguan flag.

###### Art. 14 Personal principle

Nicaraguan penal laws are applicable to acts provided therein as crimes, even if committed outside the territory, provided that those penally responsible are Nicaraguan or foreigners who have acquired Nicaraguan nationality after the commission of the act and the following requirements are met:

- a) That the act be punishable in the place of execution;
- b) That the victim, offended or aggrieved party or the representation of the State file an accusation before the Nicaraguan courts or tribunals;
- c) That the offender has not been acquitted, amnestied or pardoned or has not served the sentence abroad. If only partially served, it shall be taken into account to proportionally reduce what corresponds. In the case of pardon, it must meet the requirements of the special law.

###### Art. 15 Real Principle or Protection of Interests

Nicaraguan criminal laws are applicable to Nicaraguans or foreigners who have committed outside the national territory any of the following offenses:

- a) Offenses against the internal and external security of the State;

Law No. 641

b) Those of falsification of signature or official seals or other falsifications that harm the credit or interests of the State;

c) The falsification of coins, securities or negotiable instruments or bank notes whose issuance is authorized by law;

d) Those committed in the exercise of their functions by Nicaraguan authorities, officials and public employees residing abroad or accredited in diplomatic missions and offenses against the Nicaraguan public administration and against its officials.

e) Those committed in Nicaragua's diplomatic missions abroad. For all the cases expressed in this article, paragraph c) contained in article 14 applies.

###### Art. 16 Principle of Universality

Nicaraguan criminal laws shall also be applicable to Nicaraguans or foreigners who have committed outside the national territory any of the following offenses:

- a) Terrorism;
- b) Piracy;
- c) Slavery and slave trade;
- d) Offenses against the international order;
- e) Falsification of foreign currency and trafficking in such false currency;
- f) Offenses of migrant smuggling and Trafficking in persons for purposes of slavery or sexual exploitation and labor exploitation;
- g) Offenses of international trafficking in persons;
- h) Offenses of trafficking and extraction of human organs and tissues;
- i) Offenses of trafficking in cultural historical heritage;
- j) Offenses related to narcotics, psychotropics and other controlled substances;
- k) Offenses of international trafficking in vehicles; and
- l) Money laundering, property or assets;
- m) Sexual offenses against children and adolescents; and,
- n) Any other offense that may be prosecuted in Nicaragua, in accordance with the international instruments ratified by the country.

For all the cases expressed in this article, paragraph c) contained in article 14 applies.

###### Art. 17 Extradition

Extradition shall take place under the terms and conditions established by the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua, the international instruments ratified by the State of Nicaragua and the provisions contained in this Code.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 18 Requirements for Extradition

For extradition to proceed, it is necessary that:

- a) The act that motivates it constitutes an offense in the requesting State and also in Nicaragua;
- b) The criminal action or the penalty has not prescribed in either of the two countries;
- c) The person claimed is not subject to trial nor has been tried for the same act by the courts of the Republic;
- d) It does not involve a political offense or common offense connected with it, according to Nicaraguan classification;
- e) The offense prosecuted is punished by Nicaraguan law with a penalty of no less than one year of deprivation of liberty;
- f) The requesting State guarantees that the person claimed will not appear before an exceptional court or tribunal, will not be executed or subjected to penalties that violate their bodily integrity or to inhuman or degrading treatment;
- g) The person claimed has not been granted the status of asylee or political refugee;
- h) The person claimed is not being tried or has been convicted of offenses committed in Nicaragua, prior to the extradition request.

Nevertheless, if declared not guilty or has served the sentence, extradition may be decreed;

i) The offense has been committed in the territory of the requesting State or produced its effects therein.

###### Art. 19 Principle of Non-Surrender of Nationals

The State of Nicaragua may under no circumstances surrender Nicaraguans to another State.

Nor may the person who at the time of the commission of the punishable act had Nicaraguan nationality be surrendered.

In both cases, if extradition is requested, the State of Nicaragua must try them for the common offense committed. If the person required has served abroad part of the penalty or security measure imposed, they shall be credited by the Judge.

###### Art. 20 Special Criminal Laws

The provisions of the Preliminary Title and First Book of this Code shall apply to offenses and infractions that are punished by special laws. The remaining provisions of this code shall apply as supplementary provisions for matters not expressly provided for by those laws.

Law No. 641

Offenses and infractions committed by members of indigenous peoples and ethnic communities of the Atlantic Coast within them and among community members, whose penalty does not exceed five years of imprisonment, shall be tried in accordance with customary law, which in no case may contradict the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua. Nevertheless, the right of the victim to choose the state justice system at the very beginning of the prosecution and with absolute respect for the prohibition of multiple criminal prosecution remains safeguarded.

#### TITLE I

CRIMINAL INFRACTION

##### CHAPTER I

### OFFENSES AND INFRACTIONS

###### Art. 21 Offenses and Infractions

Offenses or infractions are intentional or negligent actions or omissions classified and punished in this Code or in special laws.

###### Art. 22 Intentional and Negligent Offenses and Infractions

When the law typifies a conduct, it does so as intentional, unless it expressly establishes liability for negligence.

###### Art. 23 Omission and Commission by Omission

Offenses or infractions may be committed by action or omission. Those that consist in the production of a result may be understood as committed by omission only when the failure to prevent it violates a special legal duty of the perpetrator and is equivalent, according to the strict sense of the law, to causing the result.

In those omissions that, despite their perpetrator violating a special legal duty, do not amount to the active causation of the result, an aggravated penalty of up to twice the maximum limit of that of the omissive offense shall be imposed. Nevertheless, said penalty may not in any case exceed the minimum limit of the penalty assigned to the result offense that would correspond to apply if there were commission by omission.

###### Art. 24 Classification of Punishable Acts by Their Seriousness

a) Serious offenses, infractions that the law punishes with serious penalty;

b) Less serious offenses, infractions that the law punishes with less serious penalty;

c) Infractions, violations that the law punishes with minor penalty. Law No. 641

When the penalty, by its extent, may be included simultaneously among those mentioned in the first two numerals of this article, the offense shall be considered, in any case, as serious.

###### Art. 25 Mistake of Fact

Invincible mistake about a fact constituting the criminal infraction excludes criminal liability. If the mistake were surmountable, the infraction shall be punished, as the case may be, as negligent.

Mistake about a fact that qualifies the infraction or about an aggravating circumstance shall prevent the appreciation of the qualifying or aggravating circumstance.

###### Art. 26 Mistake of Law

Invincible mistake about the unlawfulness of the act constituting the criminal infraction excludes criminal liability.

If the mistake about the prohibition of the act were surmountable, a mitigated penalty shall be imposed whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of the penalty provided by law for the offense or infraction in question and whose minimum limit may be half or one-fourth thereof.

###### Art. 27 Consummated offense, frustrated offense and attempt

The following are punishable: the consummated offense, the frustrated offense and the attempt of an offense. Misdemeanors, except those against persons and property, shall be punished only when they have been consummated.

###### Art. 28 Consummation, frustration and attempt

a) It is considered consummated when the perpetrator carries out all the constituent elements of the offense in question.

b) There is frustration when the person, with the intent to commit an offense, performs all the acts of execution that objectively should produce the result, and nevertheless it does not occur due to causes independent of or beyond the will of the subject.

c) There is attempt when the subject, with the intent to commit an offense, commences its execution directly by external acts, but only carries out part of the acts that objectively can produce consummation, for any cause other than his own voluntary desistance.

###### Art. 29 Desistance

The person who effectively desists from the execution or consummation of the offense by his own will shall be exempt from criminal liability for the attempt or frustration. However, he shall be criminally liable for acts of execution that by themselves already constitute an offense.

Law No. 641

If several subjects participate in the act, only the one or those who voluntarily desist from execution and prevent or seriously attempt to prevent consummation shall be exempt from criminal liability.

The exemption provided in the preceding paragraphs shall not extend to the liability that may exist if the acts already carried out were by themselves constitutive of another offense or misdemeanor.

###### Art. 30 Impossible offense

The attempt or frustration shall not be punished when the consummation of the offense is absolutely impossible.

###### Art. 31 Conspiracy and proposition

There is conspiracy when two or more persons agree to the execution of an offense and resolve to execute it.

There is proposition when one who has resolved to commit an offense invites another or other persons to execute it.

Conspiracy and proposition to commit an offense shall only be punished in the special cases expressly provided in the law.

###### Art. 32 Provocation, apology and inducement

Provocation exists when, directly or indirectly, but by means adequate for its effectiveness, incitement is made to the commission of an offense.

One who, before a gathering of persons, glorifies a crime or exalts its perpetrator and participants, commits, for the purposes of this Code, apology. Apology shall only be criminal as a form of provocation if by its nature and circumstances it constitutes, with the requirements of the preceding paragraph, an incitement to commit an offense. The exercise of freedom of thought, expression and the right to information that does not contravene constitutional precepts and principles and special laws shall not be considered apology.

Provocation shall be punished exclusively in the cases in which the law so provides. If the provocation has been followed by partial or total execution of the offense, it shall be punished as inducement.

##### CHAPTER II

CAUSES THAT EXEMPT FROM CRIMINAL LIABILITY

###### Art. 33 Minority of age

When a person under eighteen years of age commits an offense or misdemeanor, none of the penalties, measures or accessory consequences provided in this Code shall be applied to him; but if he is an adolescent, he may be held responsible in accordance with the provisions of BOOK THREE, SPECIALIZED CRIMINAL JUSTICE SYSTEM of Law No. 641

CODE ON CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS.

###### Art. 34 Exemptions from criminal liability

The following is exempt from criminal liability:

- 1. At the time of committing the criminal offense, due to any permanent or transitory mental alteration, is unable to understand the unlawfulness of the act or to act in accordance with that understanding. Transitory mental disorder shall not exempt from punishment when it has been provoked by the subject with the purpose of committing the offense or he had foreseen or should have foreseen its commission.
- 2. At the time of committing the criminal offense is in a state of disturbance that prevents him from appreciating and understanding the unlawfulness of the act or acting in accordance with that understanding, provided that the state of disturbance has not been sought with the purpose of committing an offense or he had foreseen or should have foreseen its commission.
- 3. By suffering alterations in perception from birth or from infancy, has seriously altered awareness of reality.
- 4. Acts in legitimate defense of one's own or another's person or rights, provided that the following requirements concur.
  - a) Unlawful aggression; in case of defense of property, an attack against them that constitutes an offense and places them in serious danger of imminent damage or loss shall be considered unlawful aggression. In case of unlawful aggression against the dwelling and its appurtenances, unlawful entry into one or the other shall be considered;
  - b) Rational necessity of the means employed to prevent or repel the aggression;
  - c) Lack of sufficient provocation on the part of the defender.
- 5. In a state of necessity, injures or endangers a legal interest or breaches a duty, provided that the following requirements concur, that:
  - a) The harm caused is not greater than that which is sought to be avoided,
  - b) The situation of necessity has not been intentionally provoked by the subject;
  - c) The person in need does not have, by reason of his office or position, an obligation to sacrifice himself.
- 6. Acts impelled by insurmountable fear.
- 7. Acts in fulfillment of a legal duty or in the legitimate exercise of a right, office or position. In the case of the National Police, the use of force Law No. 641 and weapons shall be regulated by the respective law.
- 8. Acts or fails to act compelled by absolute external force.
- 9. On the occasion of carrying out lawful or unlawful conduct causes harm by mere accident, without intent or negligence.
- 10. Performs an action or omission in circumstances in which it is not rationally possible to require of him conduct different from that which he performed.
- 11. Acts by virtue of obedience. Due obedience is understood when the following requirements concur:
  - a) That the order emanates from competent authority to issue it and is clothed with the formalities required by law;
  - b) That the agent is hierarchically subordinate to the one who issues the order; and,
  - c) That the order does not have the character of an evident punishable offense.

In the cases of the first three numerals of this article, the security measures provided in this Code shall be applied, if applicable.

##### CHAPTER III

CIRCUMSTANCES THAT MITIGATE CRIMINAL LIABILITY

###### Art. 35 Mitigating circumstances

The following are mitigating circumstances:

- 1. Incomplete exemptions. The grounds stated in the previous chapter, when not all the requirements necessary to exempt from criminal liability in their respective cases are met.
- 2. Psychological impairment due to disturbance. Acting by the offender due to a disturbance that does not fall under the exemption established in paragraph 2 of Article 34.
- 3. Spontaneous statement. Having accepted the facts in the first statement before a competent Judge or Court.
- 4. State of passion. Acting due to causes or stimuli so powerful that they have produced passion or obsession.
- 5. Reduction or reparation of harm. When the offender proceeds to repair the harm caused to the victim, or reduces its effects, at any time during the proceedings prior to the oral trial.
- 6. Discernment and education. When the offender has limited discernment or such limited education that he or she cannot read or write. For both cases it is understood that the agent indispensably needed Law No. 641 the indicated conditions to fully appreciate the imputed act.
- 7. Minority age. The perpetrator being over eighteen years of age and under twenty-one years of age.
- 8. Natural punishment. When the accused has suffered serious physical or moral harm as a consequence of the act imputed to him or her.

Any other circumstance of the same nature, which in the judgment of the Court should be appreciated by its analogy with the foregoing or by particular personal conditions of the active subject of the crime or his or her environment.

##### CHAPTER IV

CIRCUMSTANCES THAT AGGRAVATE CRIMINAL LIABILITY

###### Art. 36 Aggravating circumstances

The following are aggravating circumstances:

- 1. Treachery. There is treachery when the offender commits any of the crimes against life and physical integrity and personal safety using in the execution means, methods or forms that tend directly or especially to ensure it, without the risk to his or her person that could arise from the defense by the victim. Likewise, anyone who takes advantage of the circumstances of defenselessness in which the victim finds himself or herself at the time of the attack shall act with treachery.
- 2. Abuse of superiority. When the act is executed by means of disguise or deceit, with abuse of superiority or taking advantage of circumstances of place, time or assistance from other persons that weaken the defense of the victim or facilitate the impunity of the offender.
- 3. Motive of economic interest. When the act is executed for a price, reward or remunerative promise.
- 4. Fire, poison, explosion. When the act is executed on the occasion of or by means of flood, fire, poison, explosion, shipwreck, aviation accident, purposely caused damage, railway derailment, disturbance of public order or use of any device that may cause great havoc.
- 5. Discrimination. When the crime is committed on racial grounds, or other kind of discrimination related to the ideology or political choice, religion or beliefs of the victim; ethnicity, race or nation to which he or she belongs; sex or sexual orientation; or illness or disability he or she suffers from.
- 6. Cruelty. Deliberately and inhumanly increasing the suffering of the victim and causing him or her unnecessary suffering for the execution of the crime.
- 7. Breach of trust. When to execute the act one takes advantage of the Law No. 641 position attained as a consequence of the trust placed by the victim or injured party, in violation of the principles of loyalty and fidelity derived from bonds of friendship, kinship or service.
- 8. Taking advantage. Taking advantage of the status of public official or public employee that the offender has or taking advantage of a management position or employment held in an enterprise providing a public service.
- 9. Recidivism. A recidivist is one who, having been convicted by final judgment in the last five years for an intentional crime, commits another intentional crime included within the same Title.
- 10. Persons protected by international law. Persons who are recognized as having this status by virtue of international instruments ratified by Nicaragua.
- 11. Taking advantage based on gender. When the act carried out is executed by taking advantage of a relationship of dependence, authority or affinity, to cause harm to another person by reason of his or her sex; whether those relationships derive from marriage, stable de facto union or other relationship of affinity or employment, even when the relationship has ceased.

The increase of the penalty may not exceed, for any reason, the maximum established for the crime committed.

##### CHAPTER V

MIXED CIRCUMSTANCE: MITIGATING OR AGGRAVATING

###### Art. 37 Kinship

It is a circumstance that may be mitigating or aggravating, depending on the nature, motives and effects of the crime, that the victim or injured party be the spouse or partner in a stable de facto union of the offender, as well as his or her relatives within the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity.

##### CHAPTER VI

GENERAL PROVISIONS

###### Art. 38 Authority, public official and public employee

For the purposes of this Code, authority, public official and public employee shall be deemed to be anyone who, by immediate provision of law or by direct or indirect election or by appointment, commission of competent authority or contractual relationship, participates in the exercise of public functions, including members of the Army of Nicaragua and the National Police or any other agent of authority. Public function shall be understood as any temporary or permanent activity, remunerated or honorary, performed by a natural person in the name of the State or at the service of the State or its entities and enterprises, at any of its hierarchical levels.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 39 Document

For the purposes of this Code, a document is considered to be any product of a human act, perceptible to the senses, that expresses or incorporates data, facts or narratives, that serve as indirect and representative historical evidence of a fact of legal relevance.

###### Art. 40 Incapacitated persons or persons with disabilities

For the purposes of this Code, any person is considered incapacitated, whether or not his or her incapacity has been declared, who suffers from a persistent illness that prevents him or her from governing his or her person or property by himself or herself.

#### TITLE II

PERSONS CRIMINALLY LIABLE FOR CRIMES AND MISDEMEANORS

##### SOLE CHAPTER

PERSONS CRIMINALLY LIABLE FOR CRIMES AND MISDEMEANORS

###### Art. 41 Criminal liability

Perpetrators and participants are criminally liable for crimes and misdemeanors. Perpetrators may be direct, intellectual, mediate or co-perpetrators. Participants are inducers, necessary cooperators and accomplices. The liability of the participant shall in all cases be accessory with respect to the act executed by the perpetrator. In crimes that require a specific quality in the perpetrator that implies a special duty, the participant, in whom said quality does not exist, shall be liable with a mitigated penalty whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of the penalty corresponding to the perpetrator and whose minimum limit shall be half thereof.

###### Art. 42 Direct perpetrators, intellectual perpetrators, co-perpetrators or mediate perpetrators

Direct perpetrators are those who carry out the typical act by themselves; intellectual perpetrators are those who, without directly intervening in the execution of the act, plan, organize and direct its execution; co-perpetrators are those who jointly commit the crime, and mediate perpetrators are those who commit the crime through another person who acts as an instrument.

###### Art. 43 Inducers and necessary cooperators

For purposes of punishment, those who directly induce another or others to execute the act and those who cooperate in its execution with an act without which it would not have been carried out shall be considered as perpetrators.

###### Art. 44 Accomplices

Accomplices are those who willfully provide any assistance prior to or simultaneous with the execution of the act, provided they are not included in the two preceding articles.

###### Art. 45 Acting on behalf of another

Law No. 641

The person who, acting as a director, de facto or de jure administrator or organ of a legal person or on behalf of or in legal or voluntary representation of another, performs an act that, except for the capacity of the perpetrator, is subsumable under the provision corresponding to a crime or misdemeanor, shall be personally liable in accordance therewith, even if the conditions, qualities or relationships that the corresponding crime or misdemeanor requires to be an active subject do not concur in them, if such circumstances exist in the entity or person on whose behalf or representation they act.

#### TITLE III

PENALTIES

##### CHAPTER I

PENALTIES, THEIR CLASSES AND EFFECTS. PENAL GUARANTEE

###### Art. 46 Penalties

Penalties have a re-educational character. The penalties that may be imposed in accordance with this Code, either as principal or as accessory, are deprivation of liberty, deprivation of other rights and fines.

###### Art. 47 Classification by their character

Penalties are classified as principal and accessory:

Principal penalties are:

- a) Imprisonment;
- b) Deprivation of other rights;
- c) Day-fines;
- d) Fines.

Accessory penalties are those that by their nature or by provision of law are attached to other principal penalties, these being:

- a) Deprivation of other rights;
- b) Day-fines;
- c) Fines.

The imposition of any of these penalties must be specified expressly and with reasons in the corresponding judgment.

###### Art. 48 Duration of accessory penalties

Accessory penalties shall have the duration that the principal penalty respectively has, unless the law provides otherwise.

###### Art. 49 Classification of penalties by their severity

Penalties are classified as serious, less serious and minor.

a) Serious penalties are: penalties of imprisonment and disqualification that are sanctioned at their maximum limit with a penalty of five or more years of imprisonment. Law No. 641

b) Less serious penalties are: penalties of imprisonment and disqualification from six months to five years; deprivation of the right to drive motorized vehicles and of the right to possession and carrying of weapons and to reside in a specific place, exceeding one year; proportional fines; fines exceeding ninety days; and community service work exceeding thirty days.

c) Minor penalties are: deprivation of the right to drive motor vehicles or of the right to possession and carrying of weapons and deprivation of the right to reside in a specific place of up to one year; fines of up to ninety days; and community service work of up to thirty days.

Subsidiary personal liability for failure to pay fines shall be of a less serious or minor nature, according to that corresponding to the penalty it replaces.

###### Art. 50 Non-consideration as penalties

The following shall not be considered penalties:

- a) Detention and pre-trial detention and other precautionary measures of a criminal procedural nature.
- b) Fines and other corrections that, in the exercise of governmental or disciplinary powers, are imposed on subordinates or those under administration.
- c) Deprivations of rights and restorative sanctions established by civil or administrative laws.

###### Art. 51 Penalties involving deprivation of liberty

Penalties involving deprivation of liberty are: imprisonment and deprivation of liberty in cases of non-compliance with community service work and failure to pay fines.

###### Art. 52 Penalty of imprisonment

The penalty of imprisonment shall have a minimum duration of six months and a maximum of thirty years. This must be served in the penitentiary establishments designated for such purpose.

Its fulfillment, as well as the penitentiary benefits that entail shortening of the sentence, shall be in accordance with the provisions of the laws and this Code.

###### Art. 53 Calculation of the penalty of imprisonment

When the offender is imprisoned, the duration of principal or accessory penalties shall begin to be calculated from the day on which the conviction has become final.

Law No. 641

When the offender is not imprisoned, the duration of principal or accessory penalties shall begin to be counted from when they enter the appropriate establishment for their fulfillment.

All of this, without prejudice to the crediting of the time of deprivation of liberty suffered preventively during the processing of the proceedings.

###### Art. 54 Penalties involving deprivation of other rights

Penalties involving deprivation of other rights are:

- a) Absolute disqualification.
- b) Special disqualification.
- c) Deprivation of the right to drive motor vehicles.
- d) Deprivation of the right to possession and carrying of weapons.
- e) Deprivation of the right to reside in certain places or to go to them.
- f) Community service work.

###### Art. 55 Absolute disqualification

The penalty of absolute disqualification shall have a duration of six to twenty years and consists of the loss of public office or employment, even if it comes from a popular election, deprivation of all public honors, as well as the incapacity to obtain any other honor, office or public employment and the loss of the right to vote and be elected during the term of the sentence.

###### Art. 56 Special disqualification

The penalty of special disqualification may consist of:

- a) Deprivation of the right to practice a profession, trade, industry or commerce or any other activity.

This penalty shall be applied whenever the crime is committed by abusing the profession, trade or activity or involves a serious or repeated violation of the duty of care or skill required by the profession, trade or activity. Special disqualification may consist of the prohibition to engage in commerce or to be part of the organs of a legal person, when the crime was committed in the exercise of commercial activity or involves the violation of good faith in business.

b) Deprivation of the right to exercise passive suffrage or to be elected to public office.

The duration of the special disqualification referred to in subsections a) and b), deprives the convicted person of the power to exercise the rights indicated during the term of the sentence, unless the law provides otherwise.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 57 Disqualification from holding employment or public office

The penalty of special disqualification from holding employment or public office produces the deprivation of the employment or office to which it applies and of the honors attached to it for a period of six months to twenty years. It also produces the incapacity to obtain the same or similar ones during the term of the sentence. The judgment must specify the employments, offices or honors to which this disqualification applies.

###### Art. 58 Special disqualification for the exercise of rights derived from the

mother-child, father-child relationship, guardianship or custody

Special disqualification for the exercise of rights derived from the mother-child, father-child relationship, guardianship or custody, consists of the deprivation of the convicted person from exercising these rights during the term of the sentence, unless the law provides otherwise.

###### Art. 59 Deprivation of the right to drive and to carry weapons

The penalty of deprivation of the right to drive motor vehicles and the deprivation of the right to possession and carrying of weapons, revokes the required authorization or license, prohibits their new obtainment and the exercise of such activities from three months to ten years.

###### Art. 60 Deprivation of the right to reside in a certain place or to approach or

communicate with certain persons

The deprivation of the right to reside in certain places or go to them shall last from one month to five years and this prevents the convicted person from returning to the place where he committed the crime, or to the place where the victim or his family resides, if they are different.

The prohibition to approach the victim, his family members or other persons determined by the Judge or Court, prevents the convicted person from approaching them in any place where they may be found, as well as approaching their home, their places of work or any other place they regularly frequent.

The prohibition to communicate with the victim, his family members or other persons determined by the Judge or Court, prevents the convicted person from establishing written, verbal or visual contact with them, whatever the means employed. The deprivation of this right shall last from three months to ten years.

###### Art. 61 Performance of work for the benefit of the community

Work for the benefit of the community may not be imposed without the consent of the convicted person and consists of providing his unpaid cooperation in certain physical or intellectual activities of social utility. It must also be observed that they do not violate the dignity of the convicted person and its duration may be from one day to one year.

The daily workday of this work may not exceed eight hours and shall be

Law No. 641 carried out in public or private establishments of social utility, at places and times determined by the corresponding Judge or court, and under the control of its authorities, so that it is appropriate to his capacity. For purposes of calculation, it shall be understood that months are of thirty days and the year of three hundred and sixty.

Work for the benefit of the community shall be facilitated by the municipal administration, public entity or association of general interest in which the services are provided.

###### Art. 62 Non-compliance with work for the benefit of the community

If the convicted person incurs three unjustified absences from work, the corresponding Judge shall order that the sentence be executed uninterruptedly until the sentence is served, computing in such case, one day of deprivation of liberty for every two daily eight-hour workdays that he does not complete. The Judge shall proceed in the same manner when the penalty of community work has been imposed as a principal penalty.

###### Art. 63 Circumstances of execution

The other circumstances of execution of the penalty shall be established by regulation in accordance with the provisions of the penitentiary legislation, whose provisions shall be applied supplementarily in matters not expressly provided for in this Code.

###### Art. 64 Penalty of fine days

The penalty of fine days shall consist of the payment of a sum of money to be fixed in fine days. Its minimum limit shall be ten days and its maximum limit shall be one thousand days. This maximum limit shall not apply when the fine is imposed as a substitute for another penalty.

The judges and courts shall fix the number of fine days to be imposed within the limits established for each crime or misdemeanor, taking into account the seriousness of the act, the circumstances of manner, time and place, as well as the characteristics of the perpetrator that are directly related to the criminal conduct.

The sum of money corresponding to each fine day shall be fixed by the judges and courts, in a reasoned judgment, according to the economic situation of the accused, taking into account all his daily income and the reasonable expenses to meet his needs and those of his family. One fine day shall be calculated on the basis of one-third of the daily income of the convicted person. In case that income cannot be determined, the minimum wage of the industrial sector shall be taken as a basis. It shall be up to the parties to demonstrate to the Judge the true economic situation of the accused.

The fine shall be served by paying the indicated amount for the benefit of the Penitentiary System for quality of life, infrastructure and treatment programs Law No. 641 for the prison population.

For purposes of application of this Code, the salary to be considered shall be the one in force at the time the crime or misdemeanor is committed.

The convicted person must cover the total amount of the fine within thirty days after the judgment has become final, however, at the request of the interested party, even after the judgment has been issued, the Judge or Court may authorize a longer term, or payment in installments or successive quotas, taking into account the economic situation of the obligor. These benefits may be modified, and even revoked, in case of significant variations in his economic condition.

If the convicted person has his own property, the Judge or Court may require that a guarantee be granted on it; in case it does not cover the fine within the corresponding term, the creditor of the unfulfilled obligation shall seek its judicial enforcement.

From the imposed fine penalty shall be deducted the proportional part that he has satisfied with another penalty or with any precautionary measure of a personal nature.

###### Art. 65

Liability for non-payment of fine days and fine. Commutation

If voluntarily, by way of constraint or for lack of economic capacity, the convicted person does not satisfy the fine imposed by the Judge or Court, he shall be subject to two hours of work for the benefit of the community for one unsatisfied fine day.

In case the convicted person fails to comply with or does not accept the commutation established in the preceding paragraph, the custodial penalty shall be imposed at the rate of one day of imprisonment for every eight hours of work for the benefit of the community not performed.

When the fine penalty is expressed in liquid, determined or determinable amounts, in this Code or special laws, the fine shall be commuted at the rate of one day of imprisonment for the equivalent of one month of the minimum wage of the industrial sector not performed.

###### Art. 66 On accessory penalties

Disqualification penalties are accessory in cases where, without specifically imposing them, the law declares that other penalties carry them.

The penalty of imprisonment equal to or greater than ten years shall carry absolute disqualification for the duration of the sentence, unless it is already provided for as a principal penalty for the case in question.

In sentences of imprisonment of up to ten years, judges or courts may Law No. 641 impose, taking into account the seriousness of the crime, as accessory penalties any of the following: special disqualification for the right of passive suffrage for the duration of the sentence or special disqualification for public employment or position, profession, trade, industry, commerce or any other related activity, if these had a direct relationship with the crime committed, this connection having to be expressly determined in the judgment, unless it is already provided for as a principal penalty for the case in question.

The penalties established in this article may also be imposed for a period of time not exceeding three months, for the commission of an infraction classified as a misdemeanor against persons.

###### Art. 67 Prohibition of presence

Judges or courts, considering the severity of the facts or the danger represented by the offender, may order in their sentences, within the period of time determined by them, the imposition of one or more of the following prohibitions, which in no case shall exceed the duration of the penalty imposed as principal.

a) Prohibition of approaching or communicating with the victim, their family members or other persons determined by the Judge or Court; or

b) Prohibition of returning to the place where the crime was committed or of going to the place where the victim or their family resides, if they are different.

The prohibitions established in this article may also be imposed for a period not exceeding three months for the commission of an offense classified as a misdemeanor against persons.

###### Art. 68 Credit for term of preventive detention

The time of deprivation of liberty or arrest suffered preventively during the course of criminal proceedings shall be credited in its entirety for the fulfillment of the sentence or sentences imposed in the case in which such deprivation was ordered or, failing that, of those that could be imposed against the accused in others, provided that they relate to acts prior to entering prison. Likewise, the deprivations of rights ordered as precautionary measures shall be credited in their entirety for the fulfillment of the imposed sentence.

###### Art. 69 Different nature

When the precautionary measures suffered and the penalty imposed are of different nature, the Judge or Court shall order that the imposed penalty be considered executed in the part deemed compensated.

###### Art. 70 Suspension of custodial sentence

When, after a final sentence has been pronounced, a lasting situation of severe mental disorder is found in the convicted person, Law No. 641 occurring in prison, that prevents them from understanding the meaning of the sentence, or if they suffer from another serious or terminal illness, upon opinion issued by the Institute of Legal Medicine, the execution of the custodial sentence imposed shall be suspended, with the Judge or Court guaranteeing that they receive the necessary medical care.

Once the health of the convicted person is restored, they shall serve the sentence if the penalty has not prescribed.

##### CAPÍTULO II

APPLICATION OF PENALTIES

###### Art. 71 Guarantee of execution

No penalty may be executed except by virtue of an executed sentence.

###### Art. 72 Penalty for perpetrators, inciters and necessary cooperators

When the law establishes a penalty, it is understood to impose it on the perpetrators of the completed offense.

Inciters and necessary cooperators shall be imposed the same penalty as the perpetrators of the completed crime or, as the case may be, that provided for the perpetrators of frustrated or attempted crime.

###### Art. 73 Penalty for frustrated attempt

The perpetrator of a frustrated crime, taking into account the severity of the act and the culpability of the subject, at the discretion of the Judge, shall be imposed a mitigated penalty whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of the penalty deserved by the completed crime and whose minimum limit shall be half of the latter.

###### Art. 74 Penalty for attempt

The perpetrator of an attempt shall be imposed, at the discretion of the Judge, who must take into account the severity of the act and the culpability of the subject, a mitigated penalty, whose maximum shall be half of the lower limit of the penalty established for the perpetrator of the completed crime and whose minimum shall be half of the latter.

###### Art. 75 Penalty for accomplices

An accomplice to a completed, frustrated or attempted crime, taking into account the severity of the act and the participation of the subject, at the discretion of the Judge, shall be imposed a mitigated penalty whose maximum shall be the lower limit of the penalty deserved by the perpetrator of the crime and whose minimum limit shall be half of the latter.

###### Art. 76 Inapplicability

The foregoing rules shall not apply in cases where the frustrated attempt, the attempt or complicity are specially penalized by law. Law No. 641

###### Art. 77 Communicability of circumstances

Aggravating or mitigating circumstances consisting in the moral disposition of the offender, in their particular relations with the offended party or in another personal cause, shall serve to aggravate or mitigate the responsibility only of those in whom they concur.

Those consisting in the material execution of the act or in the means employed to carry it out, shall serve only to aggravate or mitigate the responsibility of those who had knowledge of them at the time of the action or of their cooperation in the crime.

###### Art. 78 Rules for the application of penalties

Judges and courts shall determine the penalty within the maximum and minimum established by law for the crime or misdemeanor, taking into consideration the following rules:

- a) If there are no aggravating and mitigating circumstances or when both concur, the personal circumstances of the offender and the greater or lesser severity of the act shall be taken into account.
- b) If there are only aggravating circumstances, the median penalty up to its upper limit shall be applied, unless discouraged by the personal circumstances of the subject.
- c) If only some mitigating circumstance concurs, the penalty shall be imposed in its lower half.
- d) If several mitigating circumstances or a single highly qualified one concur, a mitigated penalty may be imposed, whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of the penalty provided by law for the crime or misdemeanor in question, and whose minimum limit may be half or a quarter of the latter, taking into account in setting its extension, the nature and number of the mitigating circumstances.

Judges and courts must, under penalty of nullity, reason or justify in the grounds of the sentence the application of the penalty.

###### Art. 79 Inapplicability of the rules

The rules of the preceding article shall not apply to specific aggravating or mitigating circumstances that the law has taken into account in describing or sanctioning an offense, nor to those that are so inherent to the crime that without their concurrence it could not be committed.

###### Art. 80 Lower penalty for incomplete exemptions

When not all the necessary requirements to establish the exemption for permanent or transitory psychic alteration concur, the Judges or Courts shall impose a mitigated penalty whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of the penalty provided by law for the crime or misdemeanor in question, and whose minimum limit may be half or a quarter of the latter, and to apply it to the extent they deem appropriate, they shall consider the number and quantity of the Law No. 641 requirements that are lacking or concur, the personal circumstances of the perpetrator and, where appropriate, the rest of the mitigating or aggravating circumstances.

###### Art. 81 Penalty above and below maximum and minimum limits

The determination of penalties must be established between the maximum and minimum that the law sets for the offense or misdemeanor. Judges in the judgment have the obligation to express the reasons on which they based the imposition. The penalty may never be greater than the maximum nor less than the minimum set by law, except in the cases mentioned in the following paragraphs.

When in application of a legal penalty it is appropriate to impose a penalty below the minimum limit of the corresponding penalty, judges or courts shall not be limited by the minimum amounts set by law for each class of penalty, but may reduce them in the manner resulting from the application of the corresponding rule.

Nevertheless, when by application of said rules it is appropriate to impose a prison sentence of less than six months, it shall in all cases be substituted in accordance with the provisions of Article 94, without prejudice to the suspension of execution of the penalty in cases where appropriate.

When in application of the conditional agreement the accused or defendant collaborates effectively with the administration of justice, the Judge shall set the agreed penalty, which in no case may be less than half of the minimum limit of the offense or offenses in question.

When in application of any legal rule, it is appropriate to impose a higher penalty that exceeds the maximum limits set for each penalty in this Code, the following shall be considered as immediately higher:

- a) If the penalty determined were imprisonment, the same penalty, with the clause that its maximum duration shall be thirty years;
- b) If it were absolute or special disqualification, the same penalty, with the clause that its maximum duration shall be twenty-five years;
- c) In the case of deprivation of other rights, the same penalties, with the clause that their maximum duration shall be fifteen years;
- d) If it were community service work, the same penalty, with the clause that its maximum duration shall be one and a half years; and
- e) If it were a fine, the same penalty, with the clause that its maximum duration shall be one thousand five hundred days.

###### Art. 82 Real concurrence

The person responsible for two or more offenses or misdemeanors shall have all Ley No. 641 the penalties corresponding to the various infractions imposed for their simultaneous execution, if possible by their nature and effects.

When all or some of the penalties corresponding to the various infractions cannot be served simultaneously by the convicted person, the order of their respective severity shall be followed for their successive execution, insofar as possible.

Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraphs, the maximum effective execution of the sentence may not exceed triple the time of the most serious penalty imposed, declaring those exceeding said maximum extinguished, which, in no case, may be more than thirty years of imprisonment, twenty-five years of absolute or special disqualification, one thousand five hundred days fine and one and a half years of community service work days.

###### Art. 83 Continuous offense

Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding article, whoever, in execution of a preconceived plan or taking advantage of an identical occasion, or performs a plurality of actions or omissions that offend one or more subjects and violate the same criminal provision or provisions of the same or similar nature, shall be punished as the author of a continuous offense or misdemeanor, with the penalty set for the most serious infraction, which shall be imposed in its upper half.

If they are infractions against property, the penalty shall be imposed and the total damage caused shall be taken into account. In these infractions the Judge or Court shall impose, with reasoning, an aggravated penalty up to twice the maximum limit of the corresponding penalty, to the extent deemed appropriate, if the act were notably serious and had harmed a generality of persons.

The provisions of the preceding paragraphs do not apply to infractions against eminently personal interests.

###### Art. 84 Real and medial concurrence

The provisions for real concurrence and continuous offense are not applicable in the case of ideal concurrence in which a single act constitutes two or more infractions; or in the case of medial concurrence, when one of them is a necessary means to commit the other.

###### Art. 85 Penalty for ideal and medial concurrence

For ideal and medial concurrence, the penalty provided for the most serious infraction in its upper half shall be applied, without exceeding that which would represent the sum of what would correspond to apply if the infractions were penalized separately.

###### Art. 86 Express consideration

Whenever judges or courts impose a penalty that carries with it Ley No. 641 other accessory penalties, they shall also expressly condemn the defendant to the latter.

##### CAPÍTULO III

FORMAS SUSTITUTIVAS DE LA EJECUCIÓN DE LAS PENAS PRIVATIVAS DE LIBERTAD

###### Art. 87 Suspension of prison sentence

Sentencing judges or courts may suspend the execution of custodial sentences of up to five years by reasoned decision; for this they shall primarily consider the criminal dangerousness of the subject.

The suspension period shall be for a probation period of two to five years for custodial sentences of up to five years, and from three months to two years for minor penalties and shall be set by the judges or courts, after hearing the parties, taking into account the personal circumstances of the offender, the characteristics of the act and the duration of the penalty.

The suspension of execution of the penalty shall not extend to civil liability arising from the offense or misdemeanor punished.

Sentencing judges and courts may grant suspension of the imposed prison sentence without being subject to any requirement in the case that the convicted person suffers from a very serious illness with incurable suffering, unless at the time of commission of the offense he or she already had another penalty suspended for the same reason.

###### Art. 88 Conditions for suspension of execution of penalties

The following shall be necessary conditions to suspend the execution of the penalty:

- a) That the convicted person has offended for the first time. For this purpose, previous convictions for negligent offenses nor criminal records that have been or should be cancelled in accordance with the provisions of this Code shall not be taken into account.
- b) That the penalty imposed, or the sum of those imposed in the same judgment, does not exceed five years of imprisonment.
- c) That the civil liabilities that have arisen have been satisfied or guaranteed, unless the sentencing Judge or Court, after hearing the parties and the Public Prosecutor, declares the total or partial impossibility of the convicted person to meet them.
- d) In case of very serious and incurable illness, the opinion of a physician designated by the Institute of Legal Medicine shall be required.

Ley No. 641

###### Art. 89 Final judgment

Once the judgment is final and the requirements established in the preceding article are accredited, the judges or courts shall rule with the utmost urgency on the granting or not of the suspension of execution of the penalty.

###### Art. 90 Suspension of execution

The suspension of the execution of the sentence shall always be conditioned on the convicted person not committing a crime within the period set by the Judge or Court. In the event that the suspended sentence was imprisonment, the sentencing Judge or Court, if it deems it necessary, may also condition the suspension on compliance with the obligations or duties that it has established from among the following:

- a) Prohibition from going to certain places;
- b) Prohibition from leaving without authorization from the Judge or Court the place where they reside;
- c) Compliance with other duties that the Judge or Court deems appropriate for the social rehabilitation of the convicted person, with their prior consent, provided that they do not violate their dignity as a person; or
- d) Subjection to the supervision of the authority, which shall require the convicted person to appear personally on a periodic basis to report on their activities and justify them.

###### Art. 91 Revocation of the suspension of the sentence

The Judge or Court may revoke the suspension of the execution of the sentence when the subject commits a crime during the established suspension period.

If the subject violates the imposed obligations or duties during the suspension period, the Judge or Court may, after hearing the parties, depending on the case:

- a) Extend the suspension period, which in no case may exceed five years.
- b) Revoke the suspension of the execution of the sentence, if the non-compliance is repeated.

###### Art. 92 Revocation

Once the suspension is revoked, the execution of the sentence shall be ordered.

###### Art. 93 Suspension in crimes prosecutable at the request of a party

In crimes that can only be prosecuted upon complaint or accusation by the offended party, the judges and courts shall hear them and, where applicable, their representative, before granting the benefits of the suspension of the execution of the sentence.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 94 Substitution of the prison sentence

Judges or courts may substitute, after hearing the parties, in the same sentence, or subsequently by reasoned order, before beginning its execution, prison sentences not exceeding one year with a fine, even if the law does not provide for this penalty for the crime in question, when the personal circumstances of the convicted person, the nature of the act, their conduct and, in particular, the effort to repair the damage caused so advise, provided that they are not habitual offenders. Each day of imprisonment shall be substituted by two fine-days. Each fine-day must be imposed based on article 64. Such prison sentences of less than one year may also be substituted, taking into account the circumstances of the convicted person and the act, by community service, substituting each day of imprisonment for one day of work. In these cases the Judge or Court may also impose on the convicted person the observance of one or more of the obligations or duties provided for in

###### article 90 of this Code.

Judges or courts may exceptionally substitute prison sentences not exceeding two years for non-repeat offenders, when from the circumstances of the act and the offender, it can be inferred that their fulfillment would frustrate their purposes of prevention and social reintegration. In these cases, the substitution shall be carried out with the same requirements and under the same conversion terms established in the previous paragraph.

In the event of breach or non-compliance, in whole or in part, with the substitute sentence, the imposed prison sentence shall be executed with deduction, where applicable, of the portion of time that has been served, in accordance with the conversion rules respectively established in the preceding sections. In no case may sentences that are substitutes for others be substituted.

###### Art. 95 Expulsion

Prison sentences of less than five years, imposed on a foreigner with illegal entry or stay in Nicaragua, may be substituted by their expulsion from the national territory at the request of the Public Prosecutor's Office.

The expelled foreigner may not return to Nicaragua for a period not less than double the sentence imposed for the crime committed, counted from the date of their expulsion. If they return, they shall serve the sentences that have been substituted.

###### Art. 96 Conditional release

Conditional release is established for prison sentences exceeding five years, for those sentenced persons in whom the following circumstances concur:

- a) That they have served two-thirds of the imposed sentence;
- b) That they have observed good conduct and there exists, with respect to them, Law No. 641 an individualized and favorable prognosis of social reintegration, issued by the penitentiary authorities.

The probation period for conditional release shall include the time remaining for the completion of the sentence and during it, the convicted person shall be subject to the supervision of the authority, which shall require the convicted person to appear personally on a periodic basis.

If during the probation period the convicted person commits a new crime or violates the duties that have been imposed on them, their conditional release shall be revoked and the remainder of the sentence that they have failed to serve shall be enforced.

###### Art. 97 Extraordinary conditional release

Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding articles, convicted persons who have reached the age of seventy years or reach it during the execution of the sentence, and meet the requirements established in the previous article, except for the completion of two-thirds of the sentence, may obtain the grant of conditional release.

Conditional release shall also proceed when, according to a forensic medical report, it concerns very seriously ill patients, with incurable and terminal ailments.

#### TITLE IV

SECURITY MEASURES

##### CHAPTER I

SECURITY MEASURES IN GENERAL

###### Art. 98 Security measures

Security measures are based on the criminal dangerousness of the subject to whom they are imposed, manifested in the commission of an act provided for as a crime. Security measures shall be applied exclusively by the Judge or Court in a sentence, to persons who are in the situations provided for in the following chapter of this Title, provided that these circumstances concur:

- a) That the subject has committed an act provided for as a crime, according to a final judgment;
- b) That from the act and the personal circumstances of the subject a prognosis of future behavior can be deduced that reveals the probability of committing new crimes.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 99 Proportionality and necessity

Security measures cannot be more onerous than the penalty abstractly applicable to the act committed, nor exceed the limit of what is necessary to prevent the dangerousness of the subject. In any case they must be proportionate to the criminal dangerousness of the subject and to the gravity of the act committed and of those that they are likely to commit.

For such purposes, the Judge or Court shall establish in the sentence, with reasoning, the maximum limit of duration. In any case, when the penalty that could have been imposed for the crime committed was not custodial, the Judge or Court may only impose one or more of the non-custodial measures.

Custodial security measures may not have a longer duration than the maximum limit of the penalty established by law for the crime committed.

The Judge or Court shall decree the cessation of the measures as soon as the criminal dangerousness of the subject disappears in accordance with the corresponding expert reports.

###### Art. 100 Classification

The security measures that may be imposed in accordance with this Code are deprivations of liberty and deprivations of other rights.

Are measures depriving of liberty:

- a) Internment in a psychiatric center;
- b) Internment in a rehabilitation center;
- c) Internment in a special educational center; and,
- d) Internment in a social therapy center.

Are non-custodial measures:

- a) Subjection to the surveillance of the authority or supervised release, which shall require the convicted person to appear personally on a periodic basis;
- b) Prohibition from staying and residing in certain places;
- c) Deprivation of the right to drive motor vehicles;
- d) Deprivation of the license or permit to carry weapons;
- e) Professional disqualification; and,
- f) Expulsion from the national territory of foreigners not legally resident in Nicaragua.

The others provided for in this Code.

###### Art. 101 Concurrence of penalties and security measures

In the case of concurrence of penalties and custodial security measures, the Judge or Court shall order the enforcement of the measure, which shall be credited toward the penalty. Once the security measure is lifted, the Judge or Court Law No. 641 may, if the execution of the penalty would endanger the effects achieved through it, suspend the enforcement of the remainder of the penalty for a period not exceeding its duration or apply any of the measures provided for in this Code.

###### Art. 102 Breach

The breach of a custodial security measure shall result in the reentry of the subject into the same center from which he or she has escaped or into another corresponding to his or her condition.

If other measures are involved, the Judge or Court may agree to substitute the breached measure with that of internment, if this is provided for the case in question and if the breach demonstrates its necessity.

##### CAPÍTULO II

APPLICATION OF SECURITY MEASURES

###### Art. 103 Of custodial measures

A subject who is declared exempt from criminal liability pursuant to subsection 1 of Article 34 may be subject, if necessary, to the measure of internment for medical treatment or special education in an establishment appropriate to the type of anomaly or mental disorder found.

Alternatively, the Judge or Court may apply any other of the security measures indicated in Article 100 of this Code.

The person subject to these measures may not leave the establishment without authorization from the sentencing Judge or Court. The foregoing provision is applicable to the security measures provided for in the following two articles.

###### Art. 104 Internment for rehabilitation

Those exempt from criminal liability pursuant to subsection 2 of Article 34 shall be subject, if necessary, to the measure of internment in a duly accredited or approved public or private rehabilitation center.

###### Art. 105 Internment in an educational center

Those who are declared exempt from criminal liability pursuant to subsection 3 of Article 34 of this Code may be subject, if necessary, to the measure of internment in a special educational center.

###### Art. 106 Incomplete exemption

In cases of incomplete exemption in relation to subsections 1, 2 and 3 of Article 34, the Judge or Court may impose, in accordance with the provisions of Article 99, in addition to the corresponding penalty, the measures provided for in Articles 103, 104 and 105. However, the measure of internment shall only be applicable when the penalty imposed is custodial.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 107 Of measures depriving of other rights

In the cases provided for in Articles 103 to 105 of this Code, the Judge or Court may reasonably order, from the beginning or during the execution of the sentence, the imposition of compliance with one or more of the following measures, for a period not exceeding five years:

- a) Submission to outpatient treatment in medical centers;
- b) Obligation to reside in a specified place;
- c) Prohibition from residing in the place or territory designated. In this case, the subject shall be obligated to declare the domicile chosen and any changes that occur;
- d) Prohibition from going to certain places or visiting establishments serving alcoholic beverages;
- e) Family custody; the person subject to family custody shall be subject to the care and supervision of the designated family member who accepts the custody, who shall exercise it in relation to the supervisory Judge and without prejudice to the educational or work activities of the person in custody;
- f) Submission to programs of a training, cultural, educational, professional, sex education or similar nature.

For a period of up to ten years:

- a) Deprivation of the license or permit to carry weapons;
- b) Deprivation of the right to drive motor vehicles.

###### Art. 108 Foreigner with illegal entry or stay

If the subject is a foreigner with illegal entry or stay in Nicaragua, the Judge or Court may order, after hearing him or her, expulsion from the national territory as a substitute for the custodial security measures applicable to him or her.

The person subject to this measure may not reenter Nicaragua for a period not less than twice the duration of the security measure that would be applicable, without exceeding ten years.

###### Art. 109 Habitual offenders

Habitual offenders responsible for crimes punishable by imprisonment with a minimum limit exceeding six years shall be imposed by the Judge or Court, in addition to the corresponding penalty, a social therapy treatment for their reintegration for the period of their sentence.

For the purposes of this article, a habitual offender is considered to be one who has been convicted of three or more crimes that, not having been canceled in the registry, make presumable his or her inclination to commit crimes, according to express declaration by the Judge or Court, after the necessary expert reports. Law No. 641

###### Art. 110 Internment of women

When the crime has been committed by women, they must be interned in prisons intended exclusively for them, or in sections of penal establishments duly separated from the cells of men. These prisons and sections must be managed by female prison officials, in accordance with the law and regulations on the matter.

##### CAPÍTULO III

URGENT PROTECTION MEASURES

###### Art. 111 Emergency protection measures for the victim of intrafamily or domestic violence

When the action or omission has been committed by a family member up to the fourth degree of consanguinity or second degree of affinity within the cohabiting family or in a stable de facto union, the judicial authority of the place where the event occurred, at the request of a party, may apply, depending on the case, the following protection measures:

- a) Order the immediate abandonment of the home by the accused or defendant and, taking into account the will of the victim, reinstate her to the home from which she was removed with violence or intimidation;
- b) Prohibit or restrict the presence of the denounced person at the residence of the offended party within a minimum radius of one hundred fifty meters;
- c) Order the reinstatement of the offended person to the home from which they were removed with violence or intimidation;
- d) Prohibit or limit the presence of the denounced person at the workplace of the offended party within a minimum radius of one hundred fifty meters;
- e) Guarantee the offended person medical, psychological or psychiatric care in case it is necessary. The same care shall be provided, if necessary, to the denounced person for their rehabilitation and to prevent recidivism;
- f) Order the bio-psycho-social examination of minors involved in acts of domestic or intrafamily violence and provide them with due care;
- g) In case of reports of child abuse, the corresponding authority shall be requested to intervene through specialized agencies that conduct the investigation and provide support, protection, advice, counseling and respective follow-up;
- h) The denounced person must provide sufficient guarantees as determined by the Judge to compensate for possible damages caused to the offended person;

Law No. 641

i) In case the victim is a minor or a person with disabilities, the competent judicial authority may provisionally entrust protective custody to whomever they consider suitable for such function, if it was entrusted to the aggressor;

j) Prohibit all forms of harassment that disturb the tranquility of the offended party including electromagnetic or other means;

k) Order the confiscation of weapons from the denounced person.

In the case of indigenous peoples of the Atlantic Coast, the measures shall be applied by the communal authority in accordance with customary law and current laws.

The Judge or Court may order the protection measures referred to in the preceding paragraphs upon becoming aware of the crimes. For their enforcement, they may request the assistance of the National Police.

The National Police and the Public Prosecutor's Office in the investigative stage shall request from the judicial authority the application of the protection measures referred to in this article in a preventive manner, for a period not exceeding ten days.

When the Public Prosecutor's Office decides not to exercise criminal action, the victim or their representative may request the criminal judge to apply or maintain the protection measures for the period it takes to resolve the respective appeals.

In case of non-compliance by the accused with the protection measures ordered by the judge, the judge shall proceed to apply a more severe measure, at the request of a party.

#### TITLE V

OTHER ACCESSORY CONSEQUENCES OF THE CRIME

##### SOLE CHAPTER

CONFISCATION AND OTHER CONSEQUENCES

###### Art. 112 Confiscation

Any penalty imposed for an intentional or negligent crime or misdemeanor shall carry with it the loss of the effects derived from them or of assets acquired with the value of said effects, of the instruments with which it was executed or had been intended for its execution, or of the profits arising from the criminal offense, whatever transformations they may undergo. Both shall be confiscated, unless they belong to a third party in good faith not responsible for the crime and who has acquired them legally.

Law No. 641

The confiscated effects, instruments or profits shall be sold if they are of lawful commerce, applying their proceeds to cover the civil liabilities of the convicted person; if they are not, they shall be given the corresponding destination and, failing that, shall be rendered unusable. When they are firearms or weapons of war, they shall be placed at the disposal of the National Police or the Army of Nicaragua, as appropriate.

When the referred effects and instruments are of lawful commerce and their value is not proportionate to the nature or gravity of the criminal offense, or if the civil liabilities are completely satisfied, the Judge or Court may not decree the confiscation, or decree it partially.

###### Art. 113 Accessory consequences that fall upon the legal entity

When the criminal act is committed within the scope of a legal entity or for its benefit, the Judge or Court, after hearing the parties or their legal representatives, may impose, with reasoning and when necessary in the specific case, one or more of the following accessory consequences:

- a) The intervention of the company to safeguard the rights of workers or creditors for the necessary time and not exceeding a maximum period of five years;
- b) Closure of the company, its premises or establishments, on a temporary or permanent basis. Temporary closure may not exceed five years;
- c) Dissolution of the company, association or foundation;
- d) Suspension of the activities of the company, enterprise, foundation or association for a period that may not exceed five years;
- e) Prohibition from carrying out in the future activities, commercial operations or businesses of the kind of those in whose exercise the crime was committed, favored or concealed. This prohibition may be temporary or permanent. If it is temporary, the prohibition period may not exceed five years.

The temporary closure provided for in paragraph b) and the suspension indicated in paragraph d) of the preceding paragraph may also be agreed upon by the Judge during the proceedings of the case.

The accessory consequences provided for in this article shall be oriented in their imposition and enforcement to prevent the continuity of criminal activity and its effects.

Law No. 641

#### TITLE VI

CIVIL LIABILITY DERIVED FROM CRIMES AND MISDEMEANORS

##### CHAPTER I

CIVIL LIABILITY

###### Art. 114 Civil liability

The execution of an act described by law as a crime or misdemeanor obliges to repair, in the terms provided by law, the damages and losses caused by it. The procedure to determine civil liability in criminal proceedings is that provided by the Code of Criminal Procedure. The injured party may opt, in any case, to demand civil liability before the civil jurisdiction.

###### Art. 115 Scope

The liability established in the preceding article includes:

- a) Restitution;
- b) Reparation of material or moral damages; or
- c) Compensation for losses.

###### Art. 116 Restitution

The same property must be returned, whenever possible, with payment for the deterioration and impairments determined by the Judge or Court. Restitution shall take place even if the property is in the possession of a third party, preserving their right of recourse against whoever is appropriate and, where applicable, the right to be compensated by the person civilly liable for the crime or misdemeanor.

This provision is not applicable when the third party has acquired the property in the manner and with the requirements established by law to make it non-recoverable.

###### Art. 117 Reparation of Damage

The reparation of material or moral damages shall consist of the obligation to give, to do or not to do that the Judge or Court shall establish, in consideration of its nature and the personal and financial conditions of the guilty party, and shall determine whether they must be fulfilled by him personally or may be executed at his expense.

###### Art. 118 Indemnifications

The indemnification for material and moral damages shall include not only those caused to the aggrieved party, but also those caused to his family members or third parties.

###### Art. 119 Moderation

If the victim had contributed with his conduct to the production of the damage or loss suffered, the judges or courts may moderate the amount of his reparation or indemnification.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 120 Determination

The judges and courts, upon declaring the existence of civil liability, following the procedure established in the Criminal Procedure Code, shall establish with reasoning in their resolutions the bases on which they ground the amount of damages and indemnifications.

##### CHAPTER II

CIVILLY LIABLE PERSONS

###### Art. 121 Civil Liability

Every person criminally liable for a crime or misdemeanor is also civilly liable if damages or losses arise from the act. If there are two or more persons liable for a crime or misdemeanor, the judges or courts shall determine the share for which each must respond.

The perpetrators, inciters, necessary cooperators and accomplices, each within their respective class, shall be jointly and severally liable among themselves for their shares, and subsidiarily for those corresponding to the other liable parties.

Subsidiary liability shall be enforced: first, against the property of the perpetrators, inciters, necessary cooperators, and then, against that of the accomplices.

Both in cases where joint and several liability is enforced as well as subsidiary liability, the right of recourse of the one who has paid against the others for the shares corresponding to each shall be preserved.

###### Art. 122 Insurers

Insurers who assume the risk of pecuniary liabilities arising from the use or economic exploitation of any property, company, industry or activity, when, as a consequence of an act provided for in this Code, the event that determines the insured risk occurs, shall be directly civilly liable up to the limit of the indemnification legally established or contractually agreed upon, without prejudice to the right of recourse against the appropriate party.

###### Art. 123 Exemptions without Exception of Civil Liability

The exemption from criminal liability declared in numerals 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 of article 34, does not include that of civil liability, which shall be enforced according to the following rules:

- a) In the cases of numerals 1 and 3 of article 34, those who have them under their authority or legal or de facto custody are also liable for the acts executed by those declared exempt from criminal liability, provided that there has been negligence on their part and without prejudice to the direct civil liability that may correspond to the Law No. 641 imputable parties. The judges or courts shall equitably determine the extent to which each of said subjects must respond with their property.
- b) Persons who act in accordance with numeral 2 of article 34 are equally liable.
- c) In the case of numeral 5 of article 34, the persons for whose benefit the harm has been averted shall be directly civilly liable, in proportion to the damage that has been avoided to them, if it is estimable or, otherwise, in the amount that the Judge or Court establishes according to his prudent discretion.

When the shares for which the interested party must respond are not equitably assignable by the Judge or Court, not even by approximation, or when the liability extends to the Public Administration or to the majority of a population and, in any case, whenever the damage has been caused with the acquiescence of the authority or its agents, the indemnification shall be agreed, where applicable, in the manner established by special laws and regulations.

d) In the case of numeral 6 of article 34, those who have caused the fear shall be primarily liable, and in their absence, those who have executed the act.

e) In the case of the second paragraph of article 26, the perpetrators of the act shall be civilly liable.

In all the foregoing cases, the Judge or Court that issues a judgment of non-guilt upon finding the concurrence of any of the cited causes of exemption, shall proceed to establish the civil liabilities, unless there has been an express reservation of the actions to claim them in the appropriate venue.

###### Art. 124 Claim

The civil liability referred to in the preceding article may be claimed in criminal proceedings in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Code or in civil proceedings, according to the laws of the matter.

###### Art. 125 Co-liability

The following are also civilly liable, in default of those who are criminally liable:

- a) Natural or legal persons, in cases of crimes or misdemeanors committed in establishments of which they are owners, when those who direct or manage them, or their dependents or employees, have willfully violated laws, administrative regulations or provisions of authority that are related to the punishable act committed, in such a way that it would not have occurred without said violation;

Law No. 641

b) Natural or legal persons engaged in any type of industry or commerce, for crimes or misdemeanors committed by their employees or dependents, representatives or agents in the performance of their obligations or services; and

c) Natural or legal persons who are owners of motor vehicles, vessels or aircraft for the transport of persons or goods capable of creating risks for third parties, for crimes or misdemeanors committed in the use thereof by their employees, dependents or representatives or authorized persons, when the act occurs due to lack of foresight, negligence or imprudence of the owner.

###### Art. 126 Patrimonial Liability of the State

The State is patrimonially liable for damages and losses caused by those criminally liable for willful or negligent crimes committed by authority, public official or employee in the exercise of their positions or functions, provided that in the criminal proceedings it is determined that the injury is a direct consequence of abuse, negligence or omission in their performance, except in cases of force majeure.

If in the criminal proceedings civil liability is demanded from the authority, its agents and contractors, officials or public employees, the claim must be directed simultaneously against the administration or public entity presumably subsidiarily civilly liable.

The injured party may opt, in any case, to demand the liability of the State before the contentious administrative jurisdiction, without there being, in any case, a duplication of indemnification.

In turn, the State may bring an action for recourse against the authority, official or public employee who caused the injury.

###### Art. 127 Limitation

The person who by lucrative title participates in the effects of a crime or misdemeanor, is obliged to the restitution of the thing or to the compensation of the damage up to the amount of his participation.

##### CHAPTER III

FULFILLMENT OF CIVIL LIABILITY AND OTHER PECUNIARY LIABILITIES

###### Art. 128 Fulfillment

When the property of the civilly liable party is not sufficient to satisfy at once all the pecuniary liabilities, the Judge or Court, after hearing the parties, may divide its payment into installments, and shall determine according to his prudent discretion and in consideration of the needs of the injured party and the economic possibilities of the liable party, the period and amount of the installments.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 129 Order

Payments made by the convict or the subsidiary civil party liable shall be allocated in the following order:

- a) To the reparation of damage and compensation for harm caused to the victim or injured party;
- b) To compensation to the State for the amount of expenses that have been made on its behalf in the case;
- c) To the fine.

#### TITLE VII

EXTINCTION OF CRIMINAL LIABILITY AND ITS EFFECTS

##### CHAPTER I

CAUSES THAT EXTINGUISH CRIMINAL LIABILITY

###### Art. 130 Extinction

Criminal liability is extinguished by:

- a) The death of the accused, charged, or convicted person;
- b) Fulfillment of the sentence;
- c) Pardon, whose effect is limited to the total or partial extinction of the sentence, shall be determined in each case by the National Assembly. Those convicted of crimes against the international order are excluded from this benefit;
- d) Amnesty, which completely extinguishes principal and accessory penalties and all their effects. Those convicted of crimes against the international order are excluded from this benefit;
- e) Forgiveness by the injured party, when the law so provides;
- f) Prescription of the criminal action;
- g) Prescription of the penalty;
- h) The final application of one of the manifestations of the principle of opportunity; and
- i) Other cases expressly indicated by law.

In cases where the law permits it, forgiveness by the injured party may be granted at any time during the proceedings and the execution of the sentence. In crimes or offenses against girls, boys, adolescents under eighteen years of age or persons with disabilities, the judges or courts, having heard the Public Prosecutor's Office, may reject the effectiveness of the forgiveness granted by their representatives, and order the fulfillment of the sentence or the continuation of the proceedings.

Law No. 641

To reject the forgiveness referred to in the preceding paragraph, the Judge or Court must hear again the representative of the children, adolescents or persons with disabilities.

###### Art. 131 Prescription of criminal action

Criminal action prescribes:

- a) After twenty years, when the maximum penalty indicated for the crime is imprisonment of fifteen or more years;
- b) After fifteen years, when the maximum penalty indicated by law is imprisonment of more than ten years and less than fifteen years; after ten years, when the maximum penalty indicated by law is imprisonment of more than five and less than ten years;
- c) After five years, the remaining serious crimes;
- d) After three years, less serious crimes;
- e) Crimes of slander and insult prescribe after thirty days.

Misdemeanors prescribe after three months.

When the penalty indicated by law is compound, the one that requires the longest time for prescription shall be used for the application of the penalties included in this article.

Criminal action for the crimes indicated in article 16 of this Code shall not prescribe in any case.

When dealing with crimes committed by authority, official or public employee on the occasion of the exercise of their functions, the prescription period for criminal action shall be interrupted while the person enjoys immunity or evades justice.

###### Art. 132 Computation of periods

The periods provided for in the preceding article shall be computed from the day on which the punishable offense was committed. In cases of continuing crime and permanent crime, such periods shall be computed respectively, from the day on which the last offense was committed and from when the unlawful situation was eliminated. Prescription shall be interrupted, rendering the elapsed time without effect, when the procedure is directed against the guilty party, and the prescription period shall begin to run again from when the procedure is paralyzed or ends without conviction, without prejudice to the other grounds established by the Criminal Procedure Code.

In the event that criminal action is not timely exercised in crimes against sexual freedom and integrity, committed to the detriment of boys, girls or adolescents, the prescription period for criminal action shall begin from the day on which the injured party reaches the age of majority.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 133 Prescription of penalties

Penalties imposed by final judgment prescribe:

- a) After twenty-five years, those of imprisonment of fifteen or more years;
- b) After twenty years, those of disqualification for more than ten years and those of imprisonment for more than ten and less than fifteen years;
- c) After fifteen years, those of disqualification for more than six and less than ten years and those of imprisonment for more than five and less than ten years;
- d) After ten years, the remaining serious penalties;
- e) After five years, less serious penalties.
- f) After one year, minor penalties and misdemeanors.

Penalties imposed for the crimes indicated in article 16 of this Code shall not prescribe in any case.

###### Art. 134 Computation

The time for prescription of the penalty shall be computed from the date of the final judgment, or from the breach of the sentence, if it began to be served; or, from when the conditional execution sentence or conditional release is revoked.

###### Art. 135 Prescription of security measures

Security measures shall prescribe after ten years, if they are deprivations of liberty exceeding three years, and after five years if they are deprivations of liberty equal to or less than three years or have other content.

The time for prescription shall be computed from the day on which the resolution imposing the measure became final or, in case of successive compliance, from when it should have begun to be served.

If compliance with a security measure is subsequent to that of a penalty, the period shall be computed from the extinction of the latter.

##### CHAPTER II

ON THE CANCELLATION OF CRIMINAL RECORDS

###### Art. 136 Cancellation of criminal records

Convicted persons who have extinguished their criminal liability have the right to obtain from the respective authority, ex officio or at the request of a party, the cancellation of their criminal records, upon prior report from the sentencing Judge or Court.

For the recognition of this right, the following shall be indispensable requirements:

- a) Having satisfied the civil liabilities arising from the offense, except in cases of insolvency declared by the Judge Law No. 641 or sentencing Court, unless the convicted person has come into better fortune.

Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraph, in the case provided for in

###### article 129 it shall be sufficient that the defendant is current with the payments

in installments that have been indicated by the Judge or Court and provides, in the judgment of the latter, sufficient guarantee with respect to the deferred amount.

b) Having elapsed, without the guilty party reoffending, the following periods: six months for minor penalties; two years for penalties not exceeding twelve months and those imposed for negligent crimes; three years for the remaining less serious penalties; and five for serious penalties.

These periods shall be counted from the day following that on which the penalty was extinguished, including the assumption that the conditional sentence is revoked.

Criminal record entries shall not be public. During their validity, certifications shall only be issued in the cases and with the limitations and guarantees provided by law. In any case, those requested by judges or courts shall be issued, whether or not they refer to cancelled entries, expressly stating, if applicable, this latter circumstance.

In cases where, despite the requirements established in this article for cancellation being met, it has not occurred, the Judge or Court, having verified such circumstances ex officio or at the request of the interested party, shall order the cancellation and shall not take into account said records.

###### Art. 137 Cancellation of annotations of security measures

The annotations of security measures imposed in accordance with the provisions of this Code or other criminal laws shall be cancelled once the respective measure has been fulfilled or prescribed; in the meantime, they shall only appear in certifications issued by the respective authority for judges or courts or administrative authorities, in cases established by law.

BOOK TWO

ON CRIMES AND THEIR PENALTIES

#### TITLE I

### CRIMES AGAINST LIFE, PHYSICAL INTEGRITY AND PERSONAL SECURITY

##### CHAPTER I

### CRIMES AGAINST LIFE

Law No. 641

###### Art. 138 Homicide

Whoever deprives another of life shall be punished with a penalty of ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 139 Parricide

Whoever, knowing the bond that unites them, deprives of life their ascendant, descendant, sibling, spouse or partner in stable de facto union, shall be punished with a penalty of fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.

If any of the circumstances of murder concur, the penalty shall be twenty to twenty-five years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 140 Murder

Whoever deprives another of life with any of the following circumstances concurring:

- a) Treachery;
- b) Cruelty;
- c) Price, reward or remunerative promise.

Shall be imposed a penalty of fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.

When two or more of the circumstances indicated in this article concur, the person responsible for murder shall be punished with imprisonment of twenty to thirty years.

###### Art. 141 Negligent homicide

Whoever causes a homicide by reckless negligence, understood as such the violation of elementary standards of care, shall be punished with the penalty of one to four years of imprisonment.

Whoever causes a homicide by reckless negligence under the effects of pharmaceuticals, toxic drugs, narcotics, psychotropic substances or alcoholic beverages shall be punished with imprisonment of four to eight years.

In addition to the penalty indicated in this article, special disqualification for the period of the sentence shall be imposed when death is produced on the occasion of the exercise of a profession or trade; deprivation of the right to drive or obtain a license when it is produced through the driving of a motor vehicle, or deprivation of the right to possession and carrying of weapons, when it is produced through the use thereof.

###### Art. 142 Inducement or assistance to suicide

Whoever induces another to suicide, shall be punished with a penalty of two to six years of imprisonment.

Whoever cooperates with necessary and direct acts to the suicide of another, shall be punished with the penalty of two to six years of imprisonment.

Law No. 641

Whoever provides any prior or simultaneous assistance in the execution of suicide, provided that it is not the conduct provided for in the previous paragraph, shall be punished with a penalty of imprisonment of six months to two years.

Whoever causes the death of another at their express request due to an incurable disease or unbearable suffering, shall be punished with a penalty of two to six years of imprisonment.

##### CHAPTER II

ABORTION, GENETIC MANIPULATIONS AND INJURIES TO THE UNBORN

###### Art. 143 Abortion

Whoever causes an abortion with the consent of the woman shall be punished with a penalty of one to three years of imprisonment. If it is a medical or health professional, the principal penalty shall simultaneously contain the penalty of special disqualification of two to five years to practice medicine or health trade. The woman who intentionally causes her own abortion or consents to another person performing it, shall be imposed a penalty of one to two years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 144 Abortion without consent

Whoever intentionally causes an abortion without the consent of the woman, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to six years. If it is a health professional, the principal penalty shall simultaneously contain the penalty of special disqualification of four to seven years to practice medicine or health trade. If the abortion is performed with violence, intimidation or deceit, it shall be punished with a penalty of six to eight years of imprisonment. If it is a health professional, the principal penalty shall simultaneously contain the penalty of special disqualification of five to ten years to practice medicine or health trade.

###### Art. 145 Negligent abortion

Whoever by reckless negligence causes an abortion to a woman, shall be punished with a penalty of six months to one year of imprisonment; if the act is produced on the occasion of the exercise of the health profession, the penalty of special disqualification of one to four years shall also be imposed. The pregnant woman shall not be punished under this precept.

###### Art. 146 Genetic manipulation and cell cloning

Whoever alters the type of vital structure or genotype by manipulation of human genes, for reasons other than therapeutic, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years.

Whoever experiments or manipulates genetic material that enables the creation of human hybrids or cloning, shall be punished with a penalty of three to six Law No. 641 years of imprisonment. With the same penalty shall be punished those who experiment or manipulate human genetic material for purposes of race selection.

Whoever artificially fertilizes human eggs for any purpose other than human procreation, shall be punished with a penalty of imprisonment of two to five years. In all cases described in the preceding numerals, in addition to the penalty of imprisonment, special disqualification of four to seven years to practice a profession or trade related to health shall be imposed.

###### Art. 147 Genetic manipulation for production of biological weapons

Whoever uses genetic engineering for the production of biological or exterminating weapons of the human species, shall be punished with a penalty of fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period to exercise employment or public office, profession or trade.

###### Art. 148 Injuries to the unborn

Whoever, by any means or procedure, causes in the unborn an injury or disease that seriously harms their normal development, or causes in the same a serious and permanent physical or psychological injury, shall be punished with a penalty of imprisonment of two to five years and special disqualification to exercise any health profession, or to provide services of any kind in clinics, establishments or gynecological offices, public or private, for a period of two to eight years.

###### Art. 149 Negligent injuries to the unborn

Whoever by reckless negligence causes in the unborn the injuries described in the previous article, shall be punished with a penalty of one to two years of imprisonment and special disqualification of two to five years to exercise any medical or health profession, or to provide services of any kind in public or private gynecological clinics, establishments or offices, for a period of one to five years. The pregnant woman shall not be punished under this precept.

##### CHAPTER III

INJURIES AND TUMULTUOUS AFFRAY

###### Art. 150 Injuries

For purposes of this Code, the concept of injury includes wounds, bruises, abrasions, fractures, dislocations, burns and any alteration in health and any other damage to the physical or psychological integrity of persons, provided they are produced by an external cause.

###### Art. 151 Minor injuries

Whoever causes another person an injury to their physical or psychological integrity that objectively requires for their healing, in addition to first medical assistance, medical treatment, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to one year.

Law No. 641

If the injury, in addition, requires surgical intervention, the penalty shall be imprisonment of six months to two years.

###### Art. 152 Lesiones graves

If the injury produces a persistent impairment of health or physical or mental integrity of a sense, organ, limb or function, has endangered life or leaves a visible and permanent scar on the face, it shall be punished with imprisonment of two to five years.

If the injury leaves a visible and permanent scar on any other part of the body, on a person who by profession, sex, occupation or custom usually leaves uncovered, it shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years. When the serious injury is produced using weapons, instruments, objects, means, methods or forms specifically dangerous to the life, physical or mental health of the injured person, imprisonment of three to six years shall be imposed.

###### Art. 153 Lesiones gravísimas

Whoever causes another person, by any means or procedure, the loss or uselessness of a principal organ or limb or of a sense, impotence, sterility, a serious deformity or a serious somatic or mental illness, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to ten years.

###### Art. 154 Lesiones imprudentes

Whoever by reckless negligence causes any of the injuries provided for in the preceding articles, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to one year, if they are minor injuries; nine months to two years, for serious injuries, and one to three years, for very serious injuries.

When the acts referred to in this article have been committed using a motor vehicle or a firearm, the penalty of deprivation of the right to drive motor vehicles or the right to possess and carry weapons for a period of one to three years shall also be imposed.

When the injuries are committed by professional negligence, the penalty of special disqualification for the exercise of the profession, occupation or position for a period of one to three years shall also be imposed.

###### Art. 155 Violencia doméstica o intrafamiliar

Whoever exercises any type of force, violence or physical or psychological intimidation against whoever is or has been their spouse or cohabitant in a stable de facto union or against the person to whom they are or have been stably linked by an affective relationship, or against their own daughters and sons, of the spouse or of the cohabitant outside the cases of the right of disciplinary correction, or against ascendants or disabled persons who live with him or her, or who are subject to the parental authority, guardianship or custody of one or the other Law No. 641 and as a consequence of the performance of the acts previously indicated, the following are caused:

- a) lesiones leves, la pena será de uno a dos años de prisión;
- b) lesiones graves, la pena será de tres a siete años de prisión y,
- c) lesiones gravísimas, la pena será de cinco a doce años de prisión.

In addition to the penalties of imprisonment previously indicated, those responsible for intrafamily violence shall be imposed special disqualification for the same period of the rights derived from the relationship, mother, father and children, guardianship or custody.

###### Art. 156 Contagio provocado

Whoever knowingly suffering from a sexually transmitted disease or any other serious infectious disease, performs on another person acts that involve danger of transmission or contagion of such disease, thereby endangering their health, physical integrity or life, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years. If contagion occurs, the penalty shall be one to four years of imprisonment.

If the contagion produces death, the corresponding criminal offense shall apply.

###### Art. 157 Eximentes por consentimiento

Injuries performed on the body of another person with their valid, free, conscious, spontaneous and expressly given consent shall not be punishable, when these take place for the purpose of benefiting their health or that of a third party or improving their physical appearance, unless the consent has been obtained vitiatedly or the grantor is a minor or incapable person, or the injuries were caused by professional negligence.

###### Art. 158 Riña tumultuaria

Those who fight among themselves attacking tumultuously and using means or instruments that endanger the life or integrity of persons, shall be punished for their participation in the affray with imprisonment of six months to one year. It shall be considered a tumultuous affray when more than three persons confront each other.

##### CAPÍTULO IV

EXPOSICIÓN DE PERSONAS AL PELIGRO

###### Art. 159 Exposición y abandono de personas

Whoever exposes to danger the life or integrity of any person, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years.

Whoever exposes to danger the life or integrity of a child or person incapable of fending for themselves, abandons them or places them in a situation of helplessness, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years.

Ley No. 641

If the perpetrator were the legal guardian of the child or person incapable of fending for themselves, the penalty shall be two to four years of imprisonment and special disqualification of two to six years from the rights derived from the mother, father and children relationship, guardianship or custody.

###### Art. 160 Omisión de auxilio

Whoever fails to provide necessary assistance to a helpless person in manifest and serious danger, when they have the possibility of doing so without personal risk or risk to third parties, shall be punished with one hundred to five hundred days fine. Whoever finds a lost or abandoned child or incapable person whose life is in imminent danger and fails to provide necessary assistance having the possibility of doing so without personal risk or risk to third parties, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to four years and one hundred to five hundred days fine.

If the victim, indicated in the preceding paragraphs, were so by accident fortuitously caused by the one who failed to render assistance, the penalty shall be imprisonment of three to six months and if the accident were due to negligence, that of imprisonment of six months to one year.

If the perpetrator of the offenses indicated in the preceding paragraphs were the legal guardian of the victim, the penalty of special disqualification of six months to three years from the rights derived from the mother, father and children relationship, guardianship or custody shall also be imposed.

Whoever denies healthcare or abandons healthcare services, when from the denial or abandonment serious risk to the health of persons derives, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to four years and special disqualification of three to five years.

###### Art. 161

Utilización de niños, niñas, adolescentes, discapacitados o personas de la tercera edad para mendicidad

Whoever uses persons with disabilities, children, adolescents or elderly persons to practice begging, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years.

If the perpetrator of this offense were the legal guardian, special disqualification of one to four years to exercise the rights derived from the mother, father and children relationship, guardianship or custody shall also be imposed.

###### Art. 162 Provocación, conspiración y proposición

Provocation, conspiracy and proposal to commit the offenses of homicide, parricide, murder, genetic manipulation and cell cloning, genetic manipulation for production of biological weapons, lesiones leves, lesiones graves and lesiones gravísimas, provided for in the preceding chapters, shall be punished with a penalty whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of Law No. 641 the penalty provided for in the law, for the offense in question and whose minimum limit shall be half of that.

#### TÍTULO II

### DELITOS CONTRA LA LIBERTAD

##### CAPÍTULO I

SECUESTRO Y DETENCIONES ILEGALES

###### Art. 163 Secuestro simple

Whoever abducts, detains or conceals a person against their will, shall incur imprisonment of three to six years and one hundred to three hundred days fine.

If the perpetrator of this offense sets free the kidnapped person within the first forty-eight hours of their deprivation of liberty, they shall be imposed a penalty of imprisonment of one to three years.

###### Art. 164 Extortive Kidnapping

Whoever kidnaps a person with the purpose of demanding for their freedom a benefit, ransom or any utility, or to have something done or omitted, or for publicity or political purposes, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of five to ten years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 165 Aggravating Circumstances

The penalties established for extortive kidnapping shall be ten to twelve years of imprisonment, if any of the following circumstances are present:

- a) That the victim is a person with a disability or is seriously ill in such a way that places them in a notorious state of defenselessness, a minor under thirteen years of age, a pregnant woman or a person over sixty-five years of age.
- b) That the deprivation of liberty is prolonged for more than ten days;
- c) That the crime is committed by an authority, official or public employee taking advantage of the exercise of their position;
- d) If the crime is committed by taking advantage of the trust placed by the victim in the author or in any of the co-participants;
- e) That the act is committed by pretending to be an official or public employee;
- f) That the victim is an authority, official or public employee or that the act is perpetrated with the purpose of obtaining from them some benefit or advantage in connection with the performance of their position; or
- g) When the kidnapper gives no account of the kidnapped person. In this case the penalty shall be twelve years of imprisonment.

Ley No. 641

###### Art. 166 Illegal Detention and Concealment of Detainee

Whoever orders or executes the detention of someone without a judicial order or order from a competent authority, except in the case of flagrante delicto, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of imprisonment of one to two years and special disqualification of two to four years from exercising a position or public function. The person in charge of a detention center who admits the illegally detained person shall incur the same penalty.

The same sanction shall apply to the authority, official or public employee who does not obey the order of release issued by a competent Judge and to the private individual, official or public employee who does not place a detainee at the disposal of the competent authority within the time limits established by law. The authorities who order and those who execute the concealment of a detainee shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two to four years and with special disqualification from exercising the position or public employment of four to six years, as applicable.

##### CHAPTER II

### CRIMES AGAINST FREEDOM AND SEXUAL INTEGRITY

###### Art. 167 Rape

Whoever has carnal access or causes access to themselves or introduces into the victim or forces them to introduce a finger, object or instrument for sexual purposes, vaginally, anally or orally, using force, violence, intimidation or any other means that deprives the victim of will, reason or sense, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of eight to twelve years of imprisonment.

Persons of either sex may be perpetrators or victims of this crime.

###### Art. 168 Rape of Minors Under Fourteen Years of Age

Whoever has carnal access or causes access with or by a person under fourteen years of age or whoever for sexual purposes introduces into them or forces them to introduce a finger, object or instrument vaginally, anally or orally, with or without their consent, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of twelve to fifteen years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 169 Aggravated Rape

The penalty of twelve to fifteen years of imprisonment shall be imposed when:

- a) The perpetrator commits the crime by taking advantage of a relationship of superiority, authority, kinship, dependence or trust with the victim, or of permanently sharing the family home with them;
- b) The rape is committed with the participation of two or more persons;
- c) When the victim is especially vulnerable due to illness or physical or psychological disability to resist, or is a pregnant person or over sixty-five years of age; or
- d) Serious harm to the health of the victim results.

Ley No. 641

If two or more of the circumstances provided in this article concur, the maximum penalty shall be imposed.

###### Art. 170 Statutory Rape

Whoever being married or in a stable common-law union or being of legal age, without violence or intimidation, carnally accesses or causes access by a person over fourteen and under sixteen years of age, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of two to four years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 171 Aggravated Statutory Rape

When statutory rape is committed by someone who is in charge of the education or spiritual guidance, custody or care of the victim or by a person who maintains with them a relationship of authority, dependence or familiarity or permanently shares the family home with them, the penalty of imprisonment of five to ten years shall be imposed.

###### Art. 172 Sexual Abuse

Whoever performs lascivious acts or lewd touching on another person, without their consent, or forces them to perform it, using force, intimidation or any other means that deprives them of will, reason or sense, or taking advantage of their state of incapacity to resist, without reaching carnal access or other conduct provided for in the crime of rape, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of imprisonment of five to seven years.

When in the commission of the crime any of the circumstances of aggravated rape occurs, the penalty shall be seven to twelve years of imprisonment. If two or more of such circumstances concur or the victim is a girl, boy, or adolescent, the maximum penalty shall be imposed.

In none of the cases is value given to the consent of the victim when the victim is under fourteen years of age, or a person with a disability or mental illness.

###### Art. 173 Incest

Imprisonment of one to three years shall be imposed on whoever, knowing the consanguineous relationships that bind them and by consent, has carnal access with an ascendant, descendant, or collateral within the second degree of consanguinity over eighteen years of age. The foregoing, without prejudice to the penalty that may be imposed for the commission of other crimes.

In this case the pardon of the offended party extinguishes the exercise of criminal action.

###### Art. 174 Sexual Harassment

Whoever repeatedly or by using their position of power, authority or superiority demands, requests for themselves or for a third party, any sexual act in exchange for promises, explicit or implicit, of preferential treatment, or Ley No. 641 threats relating to the current or future situation of the victim, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years.

When the victim is a person under eighteen years of age, the penalty shall be three to five years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 175 Sexual exploitation, pornography and sexual act with adolescents through payment

Whoever induces, facilitates, promotes or uses for sexual or erotic purposes a person under sixteen years of age or disabled person, making them witness or participate in a public or private behavior or show, even if the victim consents to witnessing that behavior or participating in it, shall be punished by five to seven years of imprisonment and four to six years of imprisonment shall be imposed when the victim is older than sixteen and younger than eighteen years of age.

Whoever promotes, finances, manufactures, reproduces, publishes, markets, imports, exports, disseminates, distributes material for purposes of sexual exploitation, by any means whether direct, mechanical, digital, audiovisual, or with computer, electronic or other type of support, the image or voice of a person under eighteen years of age in sexual or erotic activity, real or simulated, explicit or implicit, or the representation of their genitals for sexual purposes, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of five to seven years of imprisonment and one hundred fifty to five hundred days fine.

Whoever for purposes of sexual exploitation, possesses pornographic or erotic material in the terms expressed in the preceding paragraph, shall be punished with the penalty of one to two years of imprisonment.

Whoever performs a sexual or erotic act with a person older than fourteen years and younger than eighteen years of age of any sex, paying or promising to pay or give in exchange economic advantage or of any nature, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of imprisonment of five to seven years.

###### Art. 176 Specific aggravating circumstances in case of sexual exploitation, pornography and sexual act with adolescents through payment

The penalty shall be six to eight years of imprisonment when:

- a) The act is executed for profit purposes;
- b) The author or authors are part of an organized group to commit crimes of a sexual nature, unless the crime of organized crime concurs;
- c) There is deceit, violence, abuse of authority or any means of intimidation or coercion; or Law No. 641
- d) The author commits the crime taking advantage of a relationship of superiority, authority, kinship, dependency or trust with the victim, or of permanently sharing the family home with her.

If two or more of the provided circumstances concur, the penalty to be imposed shall be seven to nine years of imprisonment. The maximum penalty shall be imposed when the victim is a person with disability or under fourteen years of age.

###### Art. 177 Promotion of Tourism for purposes of sexual exploitation

Those who within or outside the national territory, individually or through tour operators, advertising campaigns, reproduction of texts and images, promote the country as a sexual tourist attraction or destination, using persons under eighteen years of age, shall be sanctioned with the penalty of five to seven years of imprisonment and one hundred fifty to five hundred days fine.

###### Art. 178 Procuring

Whoever induces, promotes, facilitates or favors the sexual exploitation, pornography and remunerated sexual act of a person of any sex, maintains them in it or recruits them for that purpose, shall be punished with imprisonment of four to six years and one hundred to three hundred days fine.

###### Art. 179 Aggravated procuring

The penalty shall be six to eight years of imprisonment and three hundred to six hundred days fine when:

- a) The victim is under eighteen years of age or with disability;
- b) There is profit motive;
- c) There is deceit, violence, abuse of authority or any means of intimidation or coercion;
- d) The author commits the crime taking advantage of a relationship of superiority, authority, kinship, dependency or trust with the victim, or of permanently sharing the family home with her.

###### Art. 180 Pandering

Whoever by means of threats or coercions, has himself maintained economically, even partially, by a person who performs sexual acts through payment, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to five years and sixty to two hundred days fine.

If the victim were under eighteen years of age or with disability, the sanction shall be five to seven years of imprisonment and two hundred to four hundred days fine. The same penalty shall apply when the author is united in marriage or in a stable de facto union with the victim.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 181 Restriction of mediation and other benefits

When the sexual crime is committed against children and adolescents, there shall be no place for the mediation procedure, nor any benefit of suspension of sentence.

###### Art. 182 Trafficking of persons for purposes of slavery, sexual exploitation or adoption

Whoever in exercise of power or using threats, offers, deceits, promotes, facilitates, induces or executes the capture, recruitment, hiring, transport, transfer, retention, reception or harboring of persons, for purposes of slavery, sexual exploitation or adoption, so that it is exercised within or outside the national territory, even with the consent of the victim, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of imprisonment of seven to ten years.

If the victim is a person under eighteen years of age, or person with disability, or the act were committed by a family member, guardian or person in charge of education, guard or custody, spiritual guide or permanently shares the family home of the victim, or there is a relationship of trust, the penalty shall be ten to twelve years of imprisonment.

Whoever sells, offers, delivers, transfers or accepts a girl, boy, or adolescent in which there is or is not payment or reward for purposes of sexual exploitation, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of eight to twelve years of imprisonment. The same penalty shall apply to whoever offers, possesses, acquires or accepts the sale of a girl, boy or adolescent for purposes of illegal adoption.

###### Art. 183 Common provisions

When the author of aggravated rape, aggravated statutory rape, sexual abuse, sexual exploitation, sexual acts with adolescents through payment and pornography, promotion of tourism for purposes of sexual exploitation, aggravated procuring, pandering or trafficking of persons for purposes of slavery or sexual exploitation is the father, mother or legal guardian responsible for the care of the victim, the penalty of special disqualification shall also be imposed for the period established for the prison sentence of the rights derived from the mother-child, father-child relationship, guardianship or custody.

The provocation, conspiracy and proposal to commit the crimes of sexual exploitation, sexual acts with adolescents through payment and pornography, promotion of tourism for purposes of sexual exploitation, procuring, pandering or trafficking of persons for purposes of slavery or sexual exploitation, provided in the preceding chapters, shall be punished with an attenuated penalty whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of the penalty provided in the law for the crime in question and whose minimum limit shall be half of that.

##### CHAPTER III

### CRIMES AGAINST FREEDOM OF ACTION

###### Art. 184 Threats

Whoever threatens another with causing him, his family or other persons with whom he is intimately linked, a harm that constitutes a crime and that by its nature appears credible, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of imprisonment of six months to one year.

If the threat consists of causing a harm that does not constitute a crime, it shall be sanctioned with a penalty of one hundred to two hundred days fine.

###### Art. 185 Blackmail

Whoever with threats of imputations against honor or prestige, violation or disclosure of secrets, with prejudice in either case for the offended party, his family or the entity he represents or in which he has interest, compels another to do or not do something, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two to four years and one hundred to two hundred days fine.

###### Art. 186 Threat with weapons

Whoever threatens another person with a bladed or firearm weapon or object capable of causing harm to physical integrity or health, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years and a fine of one hundred to two hundred days.

###### Art. 187 Coercion and displacement

Whoever by means of violence or intimidation compels another to do, not to do, or to tolerate something to which he is not obligated, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to four years and a fine of one hundred to two hundred days.

If the coercion consists of forcing a person to change their domicile or residence or to abandon their dwelling in a permanent or transitory manner, the penalty shall be from three to five years of imprisonment and a fine of two hundred to five hundred days. If the displacement affects a group of more than ten persons, the penalty shall be from four to six years of imprisonment and a fine of five hundred to one thousand days. If the coercion prevents the exercise of an individual right enshrined in the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua, the sanction shall be from two to four years of imprisonment. This provision shall apply only in the absence of any other provision that punishes the same conduct with a higher penalty.

###### Art. 188 Insemination without consent

Whoever, without the woman's consent, procures her pregnancy using medical or chemical techniques of artificial insemination, shall be punished with imprisonment from three to five years. If pregnancy results, imprisonment from four to six years shall be imposed. In both cases, when health professionals are involved, a penalty of special disqualification from five to ten years shall also be imposed. Law No. 641

###### Art. 189 Fraudulent insemination

Whoever fraudulently alters the agreed conditions for performing an artificial insemination, or obtains consent through deceit or false promises, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to four years and special disqualification from one to five years from exercising the profession or occupation in the exercise of which they committed the offense.

Whoever prevents or disturbs the exercise of religious worship or ceremonies, shall be punished with a fine of one hundred to two hundred days.

### OFFENSES AGAINST PRIVATE LIFE

###### Art. 192 Unlawful opening or interception of communications

Whoever unlawfully opens, intercepts or by any other means becomes aware of the content of a letter, a sealed document or a telegraphic, telematic, electronic or other nature dispatch not addressed to them, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years.

If they also disseminate or reveal the content of the communications indicated in the preceding paragraph, imprisonment from one to three years shall be imposed.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 193 Removal, diversion or destruction of communications

Whoever without becoming aware of its content, unlawfully seizes, destroys or diverts from its destination a communication not addressed to them, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to one year.

Whoever knowing or presuming the content of the communication performs the conduct provided in the preceding paragraph, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to two years.

###### Art. 194 Unlawful interception of others' communications

Whoever unlawfully records others' words or conversations, not intended for the public, or whoever by technical procedures listens to private or telephone communications not addressed to them, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to two years.

###### Art. 195 Disclosure

Whoever being legitimately in possession of a communication, documents or recordings of a private nature, makes them public without due authorization, even if they were addressed to them, shall be punished with a fine of sixty to one hundred eighty days.

###### Art. 196 Violation of professional secrecy

Whoever by reason of their investiture, occupation, position, employment, profession or art, has knowledge of a secret whose disclosure may cause harm, and reveals it without legitimate justification, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to three years and special disqualification from two to five years from exercising the position, profession or occupation in question.

###### Art. 197 Prohibited registries

Whoever without legal authorization promotes, facilitates, authorizes, finances, creates or commercializes a database or computer registry with data that may affect natural or legal persons, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to four years and a fine of three hundred to five hundred days.

###### Art. 198 Unauthorized access and use of information

Whoever, without due authorization, uses another's computer records, or accesses, by any means, their database or electronic files, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to two years, and a fine of two hundred to five hundred days.

###### Art. 199 Aggravation for abuse of function or position

The authority, official or public employee who outside the cases authorized by law and taking advantage of their position or function performs any of the conducts established in this chapter, shall be imposed a penalty of three to six years of imprisonment and disqualification from exercising the public position or employment for the same period.

##### CAPÍTULO II

### OFFENSES AGAINST THE INVIOLABILITY OF DOMICILE

###### Art. 200 Violation of domicile

Whoever enters or remains in another's dwelling, in its dependencies or in a premises inhabited by another, whether against the express or presumed will of whoever has the right to exclude them, whether clandestinely or by deceit, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to three years.

In the case of the preceding paragraph, the penalty shall be from two to four years of imprisonment if the act was committed by two or more persons or with force on things or climbing, and from three to five years if committed with violence or intimidation against persons or with display of weapons.

If the unlawful entry or remaining occurs in the domicile of a legal person, public or private, professional office or private office, the penalty shall be from six months to one year, and if any of the circumstances of the preceding paragraph occurs, the penalty corresponding to it shall be imposed, reduced by half.

###### Art. 201 Unlawful search

The authority, official or public employee who searches a domicile without the formalities prescribed by law, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of three to five years of imprisonment and disqualification for the same period.

Searches carried out in cases expressly provided for in the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua and in the law are excluded from this provision.

###### Art. 202 Calumny

If the calumny is disseminated with publicity, they shall be sanctioned with a penalty of one hundred twenty to three hundred days fine.

###### Art. 203 Insult

Whoever, by expression or action, injures the dignity of another person by diminishing their fame, image, reputation, honor or attacking their self-esteem, shall be punished with a penalty of one hundred to two hundred day-fines.

Law No. 641

If the insults are disseminated publicly, they shall be punished with a penalty of two hundred to three hundred day-fines.

###### Art. 204 Exclusion from criminal liability

The offense of insult does not exist when:

- a) The imputation is true and is linked to the defense of a current public interest;
- b) The information about newsworthy facts has been carried out in accordance with journalistic ethics;
- c) It involves unfavorable judgments of political, literary, artistic, historical, scientific or professional criticism, without offensive purpose;
- d) The expressions are directed against public officials or employees regarding true facts concerning the exercise of their positions;
- e) It involves an unfavorable opinion expressed in fulfillment of a duty or exercising a right, provided that the manner of proceeding or the lack of discretion, when there should have been any, do not demonstrate an offensive purpose;
- f) The offenses contained in the writings submitted or in the statements or speeches made by litigants, attorneys or defenders before the Courts, and concerning the subject matter of the trial.

These shall be subject only to the corresponding disciplinary sanctions.

###### Art. 205 Unauthorized dissemination of images of a deceased person

Whoever disseminates, by any means, images of a deceased person without the authorization of their spouse, father, mother, sons and daughters, or brothers and sisters, with unwholesome interest that increases the pain generated by their death, shall be punished with a penalty of one hundred to three hundred day-fines.

##### CAPÍTULO III

COMMON PROVISIONS

###### Art. 206 Aggravating circumstance

When defamation or insult is committed for a price, reward or promise of remuneration, an aggravated penalty shall be imposed that shall have as its lower limit the maximum limit of the penalty for the offense of defamation or insult in question and as its upper limit, the latter increased by half.

###### Art. 207 Retraction

The retraction made by the defendant in the offenses of defamation and insult extinguishes the criminal action, provided that the offended party accepts it. Law No. 641

The Judge at the request of the victim and at the expense of the defendant shall order the publication of the retraction, of the dismissal judgment or the record of the mediation proceeding in which the retraction appears or of a summary thereof in a written media outlet.

If the defamation or insult was disseminated through a media outlet, the Judge at the request of the victim and at the expense of the defendant, shall order the publication referred to in the previous paragraph, in the same outlet or one of similar coverage, in the same form, space, place and proportion in which it was published.

###### Art. 208 Forgiveness of the offended party

At any time before the final judgment, the defendant for defamation or insult shall be exempt from criminal liability by the forgiveness of the offended person or their legal representative, specially empowered to forgive.

###### Art. 209 Offense to the memory of a deceased person

Whoever offends the memory of a deceased person with insulting or defamatory expressions, shall be punished with a penalty of one hundred to two hundred day-fines. The right to file a complaint for this offense includes the spouse, father, mother, sons and daughters, or brothers and sisters.

###### Art. 210 Illegal marriage

Whoever contracts a subsequent civil marriage, without the previous one being legally dissolved or annulled, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to four years and a penalty of one hundred to two hundred day-fines.

The same penalty of the previous article shall be suffered by whoever deceives another person, simulating the celebration of a marriage with them.

###### Art. 212 Illegal celebration of marriage

###### Art. 214 Simulation of childbirth and alteration of filiation

Whoever with the intention of modifying or altering filiation shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years:

- b) Conceals a son or daughter or other descendant or any boy or girl, even if not linked to them by filiation or kinship, or,
- c) Delivers a boy or girl to another person evading the legal procedures of custody or adoption, provided that it does not constitute the offense of human trafficking.

###### Art. 215 Substitution of girl or boy

Whoever substitutes a girl or boy for another, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to five years.

The substitution of girl or boy occurring in hospitals, health centers, public or private medical clinics, due to lack of due care by those responsible for their identification and custody, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to two years.

###### Art. 216 Aggravating circumstances

When the aforementioned conducts are carried out by an educator, professional Law No. 641 medical or health professional, public official or employee in the exercise of their position, or notary public in the exercise of their profession, there shall be imposed, in addition to the penalty indicated, that of special disqualification from public employment or position, profession or office for the same period of the principal penalty imposed.

###### Art. 217 Failure to Fulfill Maintenance Obligations

A penalty of imprisonment from six months to two years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise rights derived from the father, mother and children relationship, custody or guardianship shall be imposed on:

- a) Whoever, being obligated to provide maintenance in accordance with the law on the matter, with a provisional or final resolution or contractual obligation, or by agreement before any body or institution, deliberately fails to provide it;
- b) Whoever, being obligated to care for or educate another person, fails to fulfill or neglects such duties, in such a way that the latter is in a situation of material or moral abandonment.

The penalty shall be two to three years of imprisonment, when the perpetrator knowingly of his maintenance obligation places himself in a state in which it is impossible for him to fulfill his maintenance duty or for having used any fraudulent means to conceal his assets, or having resigned or abandoned his work in order to evade his responsibility.

Whoever fails to fulfill the maintenance duty for having transferred his assets to third parties in the period of twelve months prior to the filing of the judicial proceeding for collection of the maintenance duty, during the judicial proceeding for maintenance collection and up to six months after the issuance of the final favorable resolution of the existence of the maintenance credit or the duty to satisfy it shall also incur this offense.

The person who pays the maintenance owed, reasonably guarantees the subsequent fulfillment of his obligations or adequately guarantees the care and education of the person in his charge shall be exempt from the imposed penalty.

The employer who fails to withhold the amounts from the salary of the maintenance debtor ordered by the Judge or conceals information in relation to salaries or other aspects of interest for the establishment of the amount that must be paid to fulfill the maintenance duty, that has been requested by the jurisdictional authority, shall be liable for disobedience to authority. For the purposes of this article, Law No. 641 shall also be understood as maintenance debtors children in relation to their parents, when they are obligated to provide maintenance, as well as siblings with respect to their incapacitated sibling.

##### CHAPTER I

THEFT

###### Art. 219 Simple theft

Whoever unlawfully takes possession of movable property wholly or partially belonging to another shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years and ninety to one hundred twenty days fine, provided that the value of the stolen thing is greater than the sum resulting from two monthly minimum wages of the industrial sector.

###### Art. 220 Aggravated theft

Theft shall be punished with a penalty of imprisonment from two to five years and one hundred twenty to three hundred days fine in the following cases, when:

- a) It is committed with abuse of trust or with the aid of a domestic servant or employee of the offended party or using a false name, or simulating authority, an order thereof or a representation that one does not have;
- b) It involves luggage or valuables of a traveler, in any type of vehicle or in the parking lots or terminals of transportation companies, air or land customs. The same shall apply if the theft occurs in correspondence or goods sent by mail, customs facilities and the like;
- c) It involves supplies, machines or work instruments;
- d) It is committed taking advantage of the facilities arising from a disaster, a public disturbance or a particular misfortune of the victim;
- e) It involves objects of scientific, security or religious value, when by the place where they are located they are intended for the service, utility or reverence of an indeterminate number of persons, or entrusted to public confidence;

Law No. 641

f) It involves cultural property, defined as such by law;

g) It is of things that form part of the installation of a public service and are entrusted to public confidence;

h) It has been carried out by abusing the personal circumstances of the victim;

i) The amount of what was stolen exceeds ten minimum wages using the industrial sector as a parameter; or

j) The perpetrator is a repeat offender of theft, simple or aggravated.

When two or more of the circumstances described in the preceding paragraph concur, the penalty of imprisonment shall be four to six years and two hundred to three hundred days fine.

###### Art. 221 Theft for use

Whoever, without any right, takes without violence movable property wholly or partially belonging to another with the sole purpose of using it and returns it within the following forty-eight hours, shall be punished with ninety to three hundred days fine, provided that the value of the stolen thing is greater than the sum resulting from two monthly minimum wages of the industrial sector. If restitution is not made within the specified period, the act shall be punished as theft, simple or aggravated.

##### CHAPTER II

ROBBERY

###### Art. 223 Robbery with Force upon Things

Whoever unlawfully takes possession of a movable thing wholly or partially belonging to another with use of force upon things shall be punished with imprisonment from two to five years and one hundred twenty to three hundred days fine.

It shall be understood that there is force upon things when the act is executed under any of the following circumstances:

- a) Breaking, fracture, boring or perforation of wall, partition, fence, door, window, roof or floor;
- b) Fracture of wardrobes, chests, trunks, filing cabinets or other kinds of furniture or closed or sealed objects, or forcing of their locks or hinges;
- c) Disabling of specific alarm or security systems; or
- d) Use of skeleton key, false key or other similar instrument or the true key obtained illicitly. Magnetic or perforated cards, and any other control or electronic opening instrument are also considered keys.

###### Art. 224 Robbery with Violence or Intimidation upon Persons

Whoever unlawfully takes possession of a movable thing wholly or partially belonging to another making use of violence or intimidation upon persons, shall be punished with imprisonment from three to six years.

These penalties shall apply when the violence or intimidation takes place before the act to facilitate it, in the act of committing it or immediately after, without prejudice to those that might correspond to the acts of violence practiced by reason or occasion of the robbery.

Apart from cases of violence that might occur, it shall be deemed to exist when the act is executed by snatching by surprise a thing that the victim carried with them or using hypnotic or narcotic means.

###### Art. 225 Aggravated Robbery

The penalty of imprisonment from three to six years shall be imposed, when robbery with force upon things is committed:

- a) By two or more persons;
- b) Under any of the circumstances established in subsections b), d), e) or
- i) for the crime of aggravated theft; or,
- c) In an inhabited place or its dependencies with presence of persons.

Ley No. 641

The penalty of imprisonment shall be four to seven years, when robbery with violence or intimidation upon persons is committed:

- a) By two or more persons;
- b) At night, in an unpopulated, solitary place or in a dwelling house;
- c) With weapons or other equally dangerous means to commit the crime; or
- d) Under any of the circumstances established in subsections b), d), e), f), g) or i) of the article on aggravated theft.

The penalty of imprisonment in its upper half shall be applied, when two or more of the circumstances described in the preceding subsections concur.

###### Art. 226 Receiving Stolen Property

Whoever purchases, receives or conceals goods or valuables originating from a crime, knowing their illicit origin, shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment from one to three years or fifty to three hundred days fine or community service from ninety to three hundred days of two hours daily.

If the crime were committed by a merchant or intermediary in the financial sector, authority, official or public employee in the exercise of their functions in a commercial establishment, or to traffic with the proceeds of the crime, the penalty shall be imprisonment from two to five years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise the public employment or position, profession, trade, industry or commerce.

The penalties established in the preceding paragraphs shall be increased by one-third in their minimum and maximum limits when goods or valuables are received whose absence has caused interruption in basic services, affects the economy or national security.

##### CHAPTER III

ILLICIT TRAFFICKING IN VEHICLES

###### Art. 227 Illicit Trafficking in Vehicles

Whoever traffics, imports, exports, transports, stores or commercializes stolen or robbed vehicles or that have been the object of misappropriation or wrongful retention, nationally or internationally, or parts thereof, shall be punished with a penalty of three to five years of imprisonment.

The penalty shall be four to seven years of imprisonment if the act were committed by a member of an organized criminal group or national or international gang. The penalty shall be four to eight years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period if the act were carried out by an authority, official or Ley No. 641 public employee.

##### CHAPTER IV

EXTORTION

###### Art. 228 Extortion

Whoever with the purpose of obtaining an illicit benefit, compels another with violence or intimidation to perform or omit a legal act or transaction, to the detriment of their patrimony or that of a third party, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to five years.

##### CHAPTER V

FRAUD

###### Art. 229 Fraud

Whoever, with the purpose of obtaining an illicit benefit for himself or a third party, by means of artifice or deceit, induces or maintains another person in error so that he performs a total or partial disposition over his own or another's patrimony, provided that the value of the patrimonial damage exceeds the sum equivalent to two minimum monthly salaries of the industrial sector, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to four years and ninety to three hundred days' fine.

The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever, with the purpose of obtaining an illicit benefit, achieves the non-consensual transfer of any patrimonial asset to the detriment of a third party, by means of the manipulation of computer records or computer programs or the use of another similar artifice.

###### Art. 230 Aggravated fraud

Fraud shall be punished with imprisonment of three to six years and three hundred to five hundred days' fine, in the following cases:

- a) When its object consists of housing or land destined for the construction thereof or other goods of recognized social utility;
- b) When it is committed with abuse of the personal relations existing between the victim and the swindler, or the latter takes advantage of his business or professional credibility;
- c) When it affects goods that form part of the historical, cultural or scientific patrimony of the nation;
- d) When it is carried out by an agent or administrator of a company that obtains, totally or partially, its resources from public savings, or by one who, personally or through a registered or unregistered entity, of any nature, has obtained his resources totally or partially from public savings;
- e) When the value of the swindled amount and the extent of the damage place Law No. 641 the victim or his family in a serious deterioration of their standard of living;
- f) When it is committed by using one's own or another's credit or debit card, or with abuse of a blank signature; or,
- g) When it is carried out by means of a check, promissory note, blank bill of exchange or fictitious exchange transaction.

###### Art. 231 Insurance fraud

Whoever, with the purpose of achieving for himself or for another the undue collection of insurance or other illegal benefit, destroys, damages, simulates the disappearance or causes the disappearance of an insured thing, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and ninety to one hundred fifty days' fine. If he achieves his purpose, the penalty shall be three to five years and one hundred fifty to three hundred days' fine. The same penalty shall apply to the insured person who with the same purpose inflicts or simulates an injury or aggravates the consequences of injuries suffered in a misfortune or to whoever simulates the disappearance of a person.

###### Art. 232 Issuance of checks without funds

Whoever issues a check in any of the following circumstances shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to one year or thirty to three hundred days' fine, if the act does not constitute the crime of aggravated fraud:

- a) Without having provision of funds, except where there is express authorization from the bank to pay the overdraft;
- b) If he gives a stop-payment order, outside the cases in which the law authorizes it;
- c) Knowing that at the time of its presentation it cannot be legally paid.

For the criminal action to take place, it shall be necessary that the check be rejected by the corresponding bank or credit institution, that the drawer has been personally informed of the non-payment, by means of a notarial certificate, and that he does not pay the amount of the check plus the corresponding legal surcharges, within five calendar days following the notarial notification.

Payment of the check plus the corresponding legal surcharges, before the judgment of first instance, shall extinguish the criminal action.

The issuance or transfer of a postdated check or delivered as guarantee shall not give rise to criminal action.

In no case shall the amount of the days' fine penalty be less than the amount of the check plus the corresponding legal surcharges.

###### Art. 233 Estelionato

Imprisonment of one to four years and ninety to three hundred days' fine shall be imposed on whoever:

- b) Sells, encumbers or leases as his own another's property;
- c) Sells, encumbers or leases the same property unlawfully to different persons;
- d) By means of any legal act other than alienation, whether by concealing, damaging or removing property, makes impossible, uncertain or litigious the right or the fulfillment of an obligation agreed with another, for a price or as guarantee; and,
- e) Damages or renders useless to the detriment of a third party, a movable property of his own or removes it from whoever legally has it in his possession.

###### Art. 234 Fraud in the delivery of things

Whoever deceives as to the substance, quality or quantity of the things he must make or deliver, or of the materials he must use to carry out a work that has been entrusted to him, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and ninety to one hundred fifty days' fine, when what is defrauded is greater than two minimum salaries of the industrial sector.

The imprisonment penalty shall be three to five years and one hundred fifty to three hundred days' fine when it concerns products for mass consumption or distribution, basic or essential products, objects of artistic value or others subject to official control.

If the value of the things to be made or delivered, or the materials to be used is greater than twenty minimum salaries of the industrial sector, the penalty shall be three to seven years of imprisonment. The same penalty shall apply when it concerns housing or public works.

###### Art. 235 Fraud by simulation

The same penalty as for the crime of fraud shall be imposed on whoever:

- a) With simulation contracts, prepares writings or performs acts to the detriment of another and to obtain any undue benefit;
- b) Constitutes himself as debtor or guarantor and performs acts with the purpose of evading payment of the guarantee or the debt;

c) To the detriment of another's patrimony, carries out a simulation of litigation, proceedings or use of other procedural fraud.

###### Art. 236 Unlawful use of electric power, water and

Telecommunications

Whoever by means of an illegal connection or having altered the control and measurement systems, obtains or uses for himself or for a third party, the services of water, electricity, telecommunications or other public services, to the detriment of the supplying company or other users, for a monthly amount equal to or greater than three monthly salaries of the industrial sector, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and one hundred to three hundred days' fine.

##### CHAPTER VI

FRAUDULENT ADMINISTRATION AND MISAPPROPRIATION

###### Art. 237 Fraudulent administration

The de facto or de jure administrator of another's property who damages its owner by altering in his accounts the prices or conditions of contracts, assuming operations or expenses or exaggerating those he has made, concealing or retaining assets or using them abusively or unduly, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to four years.

###### Art. 238 Unlawful appropriation and retention

The penalties provided for the crime of fraud shall apply to whoever, having under his power or custody a movable property, patrimonial asset or value belonging to another, exceeding the sum equivalent to two minimum salaries of the industrial sector, by virtue of a title that produces an obligation to deliver or return, appropriates it or does not deliver or restore it in due time, under the pre-established conditions, to the detriment of another.

If there has been no appropriation, but unlawful use of the thing to the detriment of a third party, the penalty shall be six months to one year of imprisonment.

###### Art. 239 Irregular Appropriation

Shall be punished with thirty to one hundred eighty days fine or community service work of thirty to one hundred fifty days of two hours daily:

- a) Whoever appropriates a lost item belonging to another without complying with the requirements prescribed by Law;
- b) Whoever appropriates an item belonging to another which came into their possession as a consequence of an error or fortuitous event; and,
- c) Whoever appropriates all or part of a discovered treasure, without delivering the portion corresponding to the property owner, in accordance with the Law.

##### CHAPTER VII

ON USURPATION

###### Art. 240 Usurpation of Private Domain

A penalty of six months to three years of imprisonment shall be imposed on whoever:

- a) Through violence, intimidation, deceit or breach of trust dispossesses another, totally or partially, of the possession or holding of a property or of the exercise of a real right constituted over it, whether the dispossession occurs by invading the property, remaining in it or expelling the occupants;
- b) In order to seize all or part of a property, alters its boundaries or limits; or
- c) Through violence or intimidation, disturbs the possession or holding of a property.

###### Art. 241 Usurpation of Public or Communal Domain

Shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years, whoever:

- a) Without authorization, permanently occupies land or space corresponding to streets, roads, gardens, parks, promenades or other places of public domain, or vacant lands or any other real property of the State, of the Autonomous Regions of the Atlantic Coast or of the Municipalities;
- b) Without being legally authorized, exploits veins, deposits, layers and other mineral deposits;
- c) Making use of concessions granted by law for a specific purpose, has taken possession of land and uses it for a purpose different from that authorized, or, after exploiting the respective forest, abandons said land, without fulfilling the commitments acquired in relation to said exploitation;
- d) Occupies communal lands or lands belonging to indigenous communities;
- e) Whoever by acts of force restricts, limits or prevents passage through public roads that constitute access to a property, hamlet, community, town, lake, maritime or river shores. Without prejudice to what may be appropriate, the Judge shall order immediate opening.

###### Art. 242 Usurpation of Waters

A penalty of imprisonment of six months to two years or community service work of forty-five to two hundred days of two hours daily shall be imposed on whoever:

##### CHAPTER VIII

ON DAMAGES

###### Art. 243 Damage

Whoever destroys, renders useless, makes disappear or in any way damages movable or immovable property, totally or partially belonging to another, provided that the act does not constitute another more severely punished crime, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of six months to two years or ninety to three hundred days fine or community service work of forty-five to two hundred days of two hours daily, taking into account the economic condition of the victim and the amount of damage, if this exceeds two monthly minimum wages of the industrial sector.

###### Art. 244 Aggravated Damage

Imprisonment of two to three years shall be imposed when the damage:

- a) Is committed to prevent the free exercise of authority or in revenge for its decisions;
- b) Is caused to archives, registers, libraries, museums or other things of scientific, artistic, cultural, historical or religious value; to public use property, commemorative signs or monuments, tombs and other constructions in cemeteries;
- c) Falls upon means or routes of communication or transit, upon bridges or canals, upon production plants or conduits of water, electricity or energy substances;
- d) S e ejecute con violencia en las personas o con intimidación;
- e) Leaves the victim in a serious economic situation;
- f) Falls upon works, establishments or military or police facilities, means of transport or military transmission, war material, supplies or other means or resources assigned to the service of the Army of Nicaragua or the National Police;
- g) Produces infection or contagion in plants or animals;
- h) Is perpetrated by three or more persons; or,

If the damage occurs to a dwelling or residence, the penalty shall be three to five years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 245 Destruction of Computer Records

Whoever destroys, erases or in any way renders useless computer records, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to two years or ninety to three hundred days fine. The penalty shall be increased to three to five years, when it involves information necessary for the provision of a public service or an official record.

###### Art. 246 Use of Destructive Programs

Whoever, with the intention of causing damage, acquires, distributes or puts into circulation destructive computer programs or instructions, that may cause harm to records, programs or computer equipment, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and three hundred to five hundred days fine.

##### CHAPTER IX

### CRIMES AGAINST COPYRIGHT AND RELATED RIGHTS

###### Art. 247 Unauthorized Exercise of Copyright and Related Rights

Shall be sanctioned with ninety to one hundred fifty days fine or imprisonment of six months to two years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise a position, profession, trade, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct, whoever contravening the law on the matter, and with the purpose of obtaining an economic benefit for themselves or for a third party, performs any of the following acts without written authorization from the right holder:

- a) The translation, arrangement, or other transformation of the work;
- b) The public communication of a work or phonogram by any form, means or procedure, wholly or partially;
- c) The retransmission, by any wired or wireless means of a broadcasting emission;
- d) The reproduction of a greater number of copies than that established in the contract;
- e) Distributing or communicating the work after the contract has ended;
- f) The false attribution of authorship of a work;
- g) The performance of any act that evades or attempts to evade a Ley No. 641 technological measure implemented by the right holder to prevent unauthorized use of a work or phonogram;
- h) The manufacture, importation, distribution and commercialization, or whoever provides mechanisms, devices, products or components, or offers installation services to evade technological measures set forth in the preceding paragraph;
- i) The alteration, suppression of information on rights management; and
- j) The importation, distribution, commercialization, leasing or any other form of distribution of works or phonograms whose information on rights management has been suppressed or altered.

###### Art. 248 Unlawful Reproduction

Whoever, in violation of the applicable Law and with the purpose of obtaining an economic benefit for himself or for a third party, performs any of the following acts without written authorization from the rights holder, shall be punished with a fine of three hundred to five hundred days or imprisonment of one to three years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise any position, profession, trade, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct:

- a) The reproduction, total or partial, of a work or phonogram by any means, form or procedure;
- b) The distribution of copies of a work or phonogram by means of sale, rental, public lending, importation, exportation or any other form of distribution;
- c) The fixation of the performance of a performing artist; and
- d) The fixation of a protected broadcast for its subsequent reproduction or distribution.

###### Art. 249 Crimes against protected satellite signals

Whoever, in violation of the applicable law and with the purpose of obtaining an economic benefit for himself or for a third party, performs any of the following acts without written authorization from the rights holder:

- a) The retransmission or distribution to the public of a program-carrying signal, whether by wire or wireless means or other similar means or procedure;
- b) The decoding of an encoded program-carrying signal;
- c) The fixation or reproduction of broadcasts;

Ley No. 641

d) The manufacture, assembly, modification, importation, exportation, sale, installation, maintenance, rental or any other form of distribution or commercialization of devices or systems used to decode an encoded program-carrying signal.

Whoever engages in any of the aforementioned conducts shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years or a fine of three hundred to five hundred days and special disqualification for the same period to exercise any position, profession, trade, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct.

###### Art. 250 Protection of computer programs

Whoever, in violation of the applicable law, manufactures, distributes or sells mechanisms or systems that permit or facilitate the unauthorized suppression of technical devices that have been used to prevent the reproduction of computer programs, shall be punished with a fine of three hundred to five hundred days or imprisonment of one to three years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise any position, profession, trade, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct.

###### Art. 251 Aggravating and mitigating circumstances

The penalties provided in the preceding articles shall be increased by one third in their minimum and maximum limits, when they apply to a work not intended for disclosure or when deformation, mutilation or other modification is carried out, which affects or endangers the decorum or reputation of persons.

The penalties provided in the preceding articles shall be reduced by one third if they are carried out without the purpose of obtaining an economic benefit, for oneself or for a third party.

###### Art. 252 Fraud regarding patent, utility model or industrial design

Whoever, in violation of the applicable law and without written authorization from the rights holder, performs any of the following acts, shall be punished with a fine of ninety to three hundred days or imprisonment of one to two years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise any position, profession, trade, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct:

- a) Makes products that are not so appear as patented products, protected by utility model or industrial design;
- b) Without being the holder of a patent, utility model or industrial design or without already enjoying these privileges, invokes them before a third party as if enjoying them.

###### Art. 253 Violation of the rights of patent, utility model or industrial

design.

Whoever, in violation of the applicable law and without written authorization from the rights holder, performs any of the following acts, shall be punished with a fine of three hundred to five hundred days or imprisonment of one to three years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise any position, profession, trade, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct:

- a) The manufacture of a product protected by a patent or utility model, or a protected industrial design;
- b) The use of a patented process for the manufacture of products obtained directly from the patented process;
- c) The sale, distribution, importation, exportation or storage of a product protected by a patent or obtained by a patented process, knowing that they were manufactured or produced; and,
- d) The sale, distribution, importation, exportation or storage of a product protected by a utility model, or that incorporates a protected industrial design, knowing that they were manufactured or produced.

###### Art. 254 Crimes against the breeder's right

Whoever, in violation of the applicable law and without written authorization from the rights holder, produces, reproduces, prepares for the purposes of reproduction or multiplication, commercializes, exports, imports or donates reproduction or multiplication material of the protected plant variety, shall be punished with a fine of three hundred to five hundred days or imprisonment of one to three years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise any position, profession, trade, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct.

###### Art. 255 Unlawful commercial use of trademarks and other distinctive signs

Whoever, in violation of the applicable law and without written authorization from the rights holder, performs any of the following acts, shall be punished with a fine of three hundred to five hundred days or imprisonment of one to three years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise any profession, trade, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct:

- a) The manufacture, sale, storage, distribution, importation, exportation of products or services that bear a registered trademark or distinctive sign or a servile copy or imitation thereof, as well as the modification thereof, if the trademark or distinctive sign is used in relation to the products or services distinguished by the protected sign;
- b) The manufacture, reproduction, sale, storage or distribution of Ley No. 641 labels, containers, wrappers, packaging or other similar materials that reproduce or contain a registered trademark or distinctive sign;
- c) The use for commercial purposes of containers, wrappers or packaging that bear a registered trademark or distinctive sign with the purpose of giving the appearance that they contain the original product; and
- d) The manufacture, sale, storage or distribution of products that bear a false geographical indication or designation of origin even when the true origin of the product is indicated or is used accompanied by expressions such as "type", "kind", "style", "imitation" or other similar expressions.

###### Art. 256 Violation of rights derived from ownership of layout designs or topographies of integrated circuits

Shall be punished with three hundred to five hundred days-fine or imprisonment from one to three years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise position, profession, trade, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct, whoever contravening the law on the matter and without written authorization of the right holder, performs any of the following acts:

- a) Reproduces by incorporation into an integrated circuit, or in any other way, a protected layout design, in whole or in any of its parts that are considered original.
- b) Imports, exports, sells, distributes, stores, a protected layout design, an integrated circuit incorporating that design, or an article containing an integrated circuit that in turn incorporates the protected design.

###### Art. 257 Publication of judgments

###### Art. 262 Evasion of civil liability derived from crime

The person responsible for any criminal act who, after its commission and with the purpose of evading compliance with civil liabilities arising from the criminal offense, performs acts of patrimonial disposition or contracts obligations that diminish their assets to the degree of becoming insolvent, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to five years.

##### CAPÍTULO XII

OF USURY

###### Art. 263 Usury

Whoever in exchange for a loan or other legal obligation, in any form, for themselves or for others, charges interest or other pecuniary advantages or has receipts or guarantees granted in excess of the interest rates established in the Law regulating loans between individuals, in the Law for the promotion and regulation of credit card use and other laws on the matter, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to four years and one hundred to one thousand days-fine.

The same penalty shall be applied to whoever acquires, transfers or enforces a usurious credit and to whoever demands from their debtors, in any form, an interest rate higher than that indicated in the previous paragraph, even when the interest is concealed or disguised under other denominations, and to whoever capitalizes interest for the purpose of charging interest on interest.

The penalty shall be five hundred to one thousand days-fine and two to five years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period to exercise position, profession, commerce or trade, if the perpetrator is a habitual lender.

Whoever offers to the public bonds, shares or other type of obligations of commercial companies, concealing or hiding true facts or circumstances, or affirming false facts or circumstances, that may cause damage to a third party, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years and special disqualification for the same period of the sentence to exercise position, profession, trade, industry or commerce.

###### Art. 265 Publication and authorization of false balance sheets

Whoever, with the intent to obtain a benefit for themselves or for a third party or to harm another, causes the price of securities tradable on the stock exchange to rise, fall or remain stable, by means of affirming or simulating false facts or circumstances or the distortion or concealment of true facts or circumstances, in such a way as to mislead regarding the essential characteristics or promising prospects of the investment or the issues, shall be punished with a penalty of three to five years of imprisonment, and special disqualification for the same period to exercise a position, profession, trade, industry or commerce.

###### Art. 271 Billing Fraud

Whoever, to the detriment of the consumer and by any means, alters invoices through the establishment of higher amounts for products or services, includes in them improper billing items or alters the legally or contractually established values, or the instruments for measuring weights or measures to increase information with the same object or effect; shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years and three hundred to five hundred days fine.

The penalty shall be increased by one third in its minimum and maximum limits when the conduct concerns staple goods or public services.

###### Art. 272 Misleading Advertising

Whoever by any advertising medium makes misleading statements about the nature, composition, origin, virtues or substantial qualities, discounts, conditions of the offer, prizes or awards received for the products or services advertised, capable in themselves of misleading Law No. 641 the consumer or harming a competitor, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise a profession, trade, industry or commerce, or three hundred to five hundred days fine. The fine shall be increased to double in its minimum and maximum limits when it involves advertising related to food products, medicines or those destined for child consumption or use.

###### Art. 273 Anti-competitive Practices

Whoever, in violation of the law on the matter, by means of agreements prevents, hinders or restricts free competition, endangering the economic stability of the country, or where the anti-competitive practice concerns goods, products or services of basic necessity through any of the following practices, shall be punished with six hundred to one thousand days fine and imprisonment of two to six years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise a profession, trade, industry or commerce:

- a) The direct or indirect imposition of prices or other conditions of purchase or sale of goods or services, exchange information with the same object or effect;
- b) The imposition of limitations or restrictions on the production, processing, distribution and marketing of goods or services;
- c) The division of markets, supply areas, sources of supply or customers;
- d) Preventing, hindering or obstructing other economic agents from entering or remaining in a market or excluding them from it.

###### Art. 274 Unfair Competition

Whoever by means of acts of denigration, fraudulent inducement or comparison, attempts to divert, for their own benefit or that of a third party, the clientele of an industrial or commercial establishment, to the detriment of a competitor or consumer, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years or three hundred to six hundred days fine.

###### Art. 275 Misappropriation of Trade Secrets

Whoever, for their own benefit or that of a third party, takes possession by any means of information, data, written or electronic documents, computer records or other media or objects containing a trade secret, without authorization from its legitimate possessor or the authorized user, shall be punished with a penalty of imprisonment of two to four years or three hundred to six hundred days fine.

The provisions of this article shall be understood without prejudice to the penalties that may apply for acts of misappropriation or damages Law No. 641 caused.

###### Art. 276 Disclosure of Trade Secrets

Whoever having, legally or contractually, the obligation to maintain confidentiality, illegitimately disseminates, communicates, divulges, reveals or exploits a trade secret, shall be punished with a penalty of two to five years of imprisonment or five hundred to seven hundred days fine.

###### Art. 277 Improper Use of Trade Secrets

Whoever, with knowledge of its illicit origin, and without having participated in the misappropriation of the trade secret, carries out any of the conducts described in the two preceding articles, shall be punished with the penalties of imprisonment of six months to two years and three hundred to six hundred days fine.

##### CAPÍTULO XIV

CORPORATE CRIMES

###### Art. 278 Abusive Management

The director, manager, supervisor, auditor, legal representative, de facto or de jure administrator, or partner of a commercial or civil entity, with or without profit motive, who adopts or contributes to making any abusive decision or agreement for their own benefit or that of third parties, causing serious harm to the company or entity, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and special disqualification for the same period from exercising a profession, trade, industry or commerce.

The same penalty shall be imposed when the persons mentioned in the preceding paragraph alter accounts or financial information with the purpose of presenting a distorted situation in a manner capable of causing economic harm to the entity, to any of the partners or third parties.

###### Art. 279 Authorization of Improper Acts

The director, manager, supervisor, auditor or legal representative de jure or de facto, of a company formed or in formation who, knowingly, lends their cooperation or consent to the performance of acts contrary to law or to the bylaws, from which some harm may arise to their principal or the public, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years and special disqualification for the same period from exercising office, profession, trade, industry or commerce.

The penalty may be increased up to double in its minimum and maximum limits, when the crime is executed through an entity that makes a public offering of securities.

Ley No. 641

##### Capítulo XV

Of crimes against the banking and financial system

###### Art. 280 Crimes against the banking and financial system

The partner, director, manager, administrator, supervisor, external or internal auditor, legal representative, official or employee of banks, non-bank financial institutions and financial groups, supervised by the Superintendency of Banks and Other Financial Institutions, who knowingly or having reason to know, directly or indirectly performs acts or operations, which, with abuse of their own functions, cause serious patrimonial harm to depositors, their clients, creditors, partners of their respective entity, to the stability of the Banking and Financial System or to the State, shall be punished with the penalty of six to eight years of imprisonment and special disqualification from exercising the profession, trade, industry or commerce for the same period and from three hundred to one thousand days fine.

They shall be punished with a penalty of five to seven years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period from exercising positions in the Financial System and from three hundred to one thousand days fine, whoever knowingly or having reason to know, conceals, alters, disfigures, distorts or destroys information, data or records of financial statements, minute books, accounting books, accounts, correspondence or other documents belonging to the institution, with the purpose of causing harm to it, or with the intention of avoiding or hindering the supervisory work of the Superintendency of Banks and Other Financial Institutions or trying to prevent the patrimonial reality of the institution from being known or the origin of the invested capital from being truthfully identified. A penalty of three hundred to five hundred days fine shall be imposed on whoever prevents or denies one or more partners from obtaining truthful information about the real patrimonial state of the business and of the financial statements.

A penalty of three to five years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period from exercising positions in the Financial System and from four hundred to one thousand days fine, shall be imposed on the partner, director and any official of the entities supervised by the Superintendency of Banks and Other Financial Institutions who by their functions or position, authorizes and approves distribution of profits that are fictitious or that are not authorized or have been reasonably objected to by the Superintendent in accordance with the Law on the matter or that have not been effectively received, except in the case of distribution of accounting profits intended to be capitalized, subject to non-objection by the Superintendency of Banks and Other Financial Institutions.

A penalty of four to six years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period from exercising positions in the Financial System and from three hundred to six hundred days fine shall be imposed on the person who, with or without the participation of partners, directors and any official of the entities supervised by Ley No. 641 the Superintendency of Banks and Other Financial Institutions, performs fraudulent acts that seriously endanger the solvency, liquidity and stability of banking and non-bank financial entities, disseminates by any means of mass social communication, unfounded rumors or unauthorized information, about the solvency, liquidity, and risks inherent to the Financial System, that threaten the stability and operation of any of the banking and non-bank financial entities.

If the facts indicated in the preceding paragraphs lead to the forced liquidation of one or more supervised entities, or seriously damage the National Financial System or the economy of the Nation, the penalty shall be ten to fifteen years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period from exercising positions in the Financial System and from eight hundred to one thousand days fine.

##### CAPÍTULO XVI

### CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC TRUST

###### Art. 281 Fraud in contests and other public acts

Whoever conspires with another to alter the price or other conditions in an auction, price contest, public auction or public bidding or solicits or receives payment, pays or makes promise of payment to participate or not participate in an auction, price contest, public auction or public bidding shall be punished with the penalty of two to four years of imprisonment and special disqualification from exercising the activity related to the criminal activity for the same period. A penalty of three to five years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period from exercising the activity related to the criminal activity shall be imposed on whoever prevents or attempts to prevent the participation of another bidder or participant or tenderer by means of violence, intimidation or deceit, or, disseminates false or distorted news in any of the acts indicated in the preceding paragraph to obtain profit for themselves or third parties.

##### CAPÍTULO XVII

MONEY LAUNDERING, PROPERTY OR ASSETS

###### Art. 282 Money, assets or property laundering

Anyone who knowingly or having reason to know, by themselves or through an intermediary, carries out any of the following activities:

- a) Acquires, uses, converts, conceals, moves, secures, holds in custody, administers, collects, safeguards, intermediates, sells, encumbers, donates, simulates or extinguishes obligations, invests, deposits or transfers money, assets or property, original or substitute, originating from illicit activities or any other act with the purpose of hiding or concealing their illicit origin, whether in a single act or through the repetition of interconnected acts, regardless of whether any of these occurred within or outside the country;

Ley No. 641

b) Prevents in any way the actual determination of the nature, origin, source or connection of money, assets, property, securities or interests generated from illicit activities; or advises, manages, finances, organizes fictitious companies and businesses or performs acts with the purpose of hiding or concealing their illicit origin, whether in a single act or through the repetition of interconnected acts, regardless of whether they occurred within or outside the country;

c) Provides false or incomplete information to, or from banking or non-banking financial entities, insurance, stock market, currency exchange, remittance, commercial or any other type of entity with the purpose of contracting services, opening accounts, making deposits, obtaining credits, carrying out transactions or business of assets, property or other resources, when these originate from or have been obtained from some illicit activity with the purpose of hiding or concealing their illicit origin;

d) Facilitates or lends their identification data or the name or business name of the company, business or any other legal entity of which they are a partner or shareholder or with which they have any connection, whether or not legally constituted, regardless of its line of business, for the commission of the crime of money, assets or property laundering or carries out any other front activity;

e) Brings into or takes out of the national territory assets or property originating from illicit activities using customs or immigration posts: land, maritime or air or any other point in the country;

f) Seriously fails to comply with the duties of their position to facilitate the conducts described in the preceding paragraphs.

The foregoing conducts constitute this crime when they have as a predicate illicit activity those that are punished at their upper maximum limit with a penalty of five or more years of imprisonment.

The crime of money, assets or property laundering is autonomous with respect to its predicate crime and shall be prevented, investigated, prosecuted, ruled upon or sentenced by the competent authorities as such, in relation to the illicit activities from which it may originate, for which a prior criminal proceeding in relation to the predicate illicit activity shall not be required. For its prosecution it shall be sufficient to demonstrate its connection with that from which it originates.

These conducts shall be punished with a penalty of five to seven years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period for the exercise of the profession, trade or position, and a fine of one to three times the value of the money, assets or property in question.

Ley No. 641

###### Art. 283 Aggravating circumstances.

When the illicit activities that precede the crimes established in this Chapter are linked to or derive from crimes related to narcotics, psychotropic substances and other controlled substances or from other crimes that have been committed by a member of an organized criminal group or national or international gang, unless the crime of organized crime is present, a fine of three to six times the value of the money, assets or property in question and imprisonment of seven to fifteen years and disqualification for the same period to exercise the profession, position or trade shall be imposed.

The same penalty shall be imposed on anyone who knowingly or having reason to know, receives or uses money, assets or property or any financial resource originating from any illicit act provided for in the preceding article for the financing of political activities.

The imprisonment penalties provided for in this Chapter shall be increased by up to one third when the foregoing crimes are committed by a director, partner, representative or employee of a legal entity or public official, authority or public employee.

#### TITLE VII

### CRIMES OF FALSIFICATION

##### CHAPTER I

FALSIFICATION OF DOCUMENTS

###### Art. 284 Material falsification

Anyone who makes in whole or in part a false document or alters a genuine one, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to four years, if it is a public document or instrument, and with imprisonment of six months to two years if it is a private document.

###### Art. 285 Ideological falsity

The penalties provided for the material falsification of a public or private instrument or document are applicable to anyone who inserts or causes to be inserted in a public document or instrument false statements concerning a fact that the document must prove.

###### Art. 286 Suppression, concealment and destruction of documents

Anyone who suppresses, conceals or destroys, in whole or in part, a public or private document shall be punished with the penalties provided for the crime of material falsification, in the respective cases.

###### Art. 287 Equivalent documents

Anyone who falsifies in whole or in part, suppresses, conceals or destroys a sealed will, a check, a bill of Ley No. 641 exchange, shares or other documents or credit instruments transferable by endorsement or to bearer shall be punished with the penalties provided for the falsification or alteration of public documents or instruments.

###### Art. 288 Falsity in medical certificates

A physician who issues a materially or ideologically false certificate concerning the existence or non-existence, present or past, of any disease or injury shall be punished with imprisonment of two to four years and special disqualification for the same period.

The penalty shall be two to five years of imprisonment and special disqualification for an equal period to exercise the profession, if the false certificate has any of the following purposes:

- a) That a healthy person be confined in a psychiatric hospital or other health facility;
- b) That an accused person evade a precautionary measure in a criminal case; or,
- c) That a convicted person evade the imposed sanctions.

###### Art. 289 Use of false document

A penalty of imprisonment of six months to three years and fifty to two hundred days fine shall be imposed on anyone who makes use of a false or altered document.

###### Art. 290 Aggravating circumstance

The penalties provided for in this Chapter shall be increased by up to one third when the foregoing crimes are committed by an authority, public official or public employee in the exercise of their functions or by reason of their position.

##### CHAPTER II

FALSIFICATION OF CURRENCY, SECURITIES AND STAMPED EFFECTS

###### Art. 291 Counterfeiting of currency

Anyone who manufactures, brings in, possesses, sells or distributes counterfeit national or foreign currency shall be punished with imprisonment of three to six years and six hundred to one thousand days fine.

The same penalty shall apply to those who falsify or alter negotiable securities or instruments issued by the State and to the good faith acquirer of these securities who, after their acquisition, becomes aware of their falsity and puts them into circulation, transferring them under any title.

For the purposes of this article, currency means metallic coin and paper money in legal circulation, national or foreign; credit cards, debit cards, traveler's checks, electronic entries in accounts, national or municipal debt securities, national treasury bonds or bills and treasury bonds bills issued by a foreign government and any Ley No. 641 other form of currency established in our legislation.

###### Art. 292 Falsification of postal stamps or fiscal stamps

Whoever falsifies postal stamps or fiscal stamps, or stamped paper, national postal stamps, or any kind of stamped effects whose issuance is reserved by law, or with knowledge of their falsity exports them, introduces them into the national territory, distributes them or uses them, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years. The same penalty shall apply when the action involves authorized lottery tickets.

For the purposes of this article and the following ones, the fraudulent printing of a genuine stamp shall be considered falsification.

###### Art. 293 Falsification of signs and marks

Whoever shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years:

- a) Falsifies marks, passwords or signatures officially used to verify weights or measures, identify any object or livestock or certify their quality, quantity or content, date, expiration, sanitary registration and whoever applies them to objects other than those to which they should be applied;
- b) Falsifies bonds, tickets, bills, vouchers, receipts, coupons of public or private service entities; or,
- c) Falsifies, alters or suppresses the individualizing numbering of an object, registered in accordance with the law for security or fiscal reasons.

###### Art. 294 Fraudulent restoration of stamps

Whoever causes to disappear, in any of the stamps, fiscal stamps, marks or passwords referred to in the preceding articles, the sign indicating that it has served or has been used for the purpose of its issuance, with the purpose of reusing it or selling it, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years or ninety to one hundred fifty days fine.

If the fraudulent restoration of stamps occurs in food or medicinal products, the penalty shall be two to four years of imprisonment.

The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever, knowingly, uses, causes to be used or offers for sale the invalidated effects referred to in the preceding paragraph.

###### Art. 295 Possession of falsification instruments

Whoever manufactures, introduces into the country or keeps in their possession materials or instruments intended to commit any of the falsifications provided for in this Title, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to one year or ninety to three hundred days fine.

USURPATION OF PUBLIC FUNCTIONS, PROFESSIONAL TITLES AND

###### Art. 296 Usurpation of public functions

Whoever shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and special disqualification for the same period for the exercise of public office:

- b) After having ceased by operation of law in the performance of a public office or after having received from the competent authority official communication of the resolution that ordered the cessation or suspension of their functions and having made the official transfer or refusing to do so, continues exercising them;
- c) Usurps functions corresponding to another office or body.

###### Art. 297 Improper use of emblems, uniforms or equipment of the Army of

Nicaragua and of the National Police

Whoever improperly and publicly uses uniforms, insignia, official emblems or other equipment that simulate their bearer's membership in the Army of Nicaragua or the National Police, with the purpose of usurping the authority of these institutions, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years or fifty to two hundred days fine.

###### Art. 298 Illegal exercise of profession and usurpation of title

Whoever exercises acts proper to a profession whose exercise mandatorily requires possession of the academic title issued or recognized in Nicaragua and authorization in accordance with current legislation, shall incur the penalty of one hundred fifty to three hundred days fine.

Whoever having a professional title and being suspended in the exercise of their profession, exercises it, shall be imposed the penalty of ninety to one hundred fifty days fine and special disqualification to exercise the profession from one to three years.

#### TÍTULO VIII

### CRIMES AGAINST THE CULTURAL HERITAGE OF THE NATION

##### CAPÍTULO ÚNICO

###### Art. 299 Illegal trafficking of cultural heritage

Whoever traffics, transports, stores, commercializes or extracts from the country cultural property, or in any way transfers property described in the Law for the Protection of the Cultural Heritage of the Nation without authorization from the competent body Ley No. 641, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to five years and five hundred to eight hundred days fine.

If the cultural, artistic or scientific work by its nature, quality and importance is considered irreplaceable or forms part of the cultural heritage of other States or forms part of the cultural heritage of humanity, the penalty of four to six years of imprisonment and six hundred to one thousand days fine shall be imposed.

For the purposes of this article, whoever acquires stolen or robbed cultural property is considered the author of the crimes indicated in the preceding paragraphs.

###### Art. 300 Exemption from penalty

If before the start of the oral and public trial hearing the stolen cultural, scientific or artistic property is delivered to the Nicaraguan Institute of Culture, official museum, municipal mayor's office or university, exemption from penalty shall be granted.

###### Art. 301 Demolition or serious alteration of buildings of historical interest,

artistic, cultural or monumental

Whoever in contravention of laws, regulations or urban planning regulations, demolishes or seriously alters a building or an urban or rural complex protected for its historical, artistic, cultural or monumental interest, shall be punished with the penalty of six months to three years of imprisonment.

Whoever being obligated to conserve a building or urban or rural complex indicated in the preceding paragraph, unjustifiably fails to maintain it or does not allow its maintenance, shall be imposed a penalty between fifty and two hundred days fine.

###### Art. 302 Crimes against cultural heritage committed by officials

When the acts described in the preceding articles are committed by an authority, public official or employee, in addition to the applicable penalty for each of the crimes, the author shall be imposed absolute disqualification to exercise public office for a period of six to twelve years.

#### TÍTULO IX

### CRIMES AGAINST THE PUBLIC TREASURY AND SOCIAL SECURITY

##### CAPÍTULO I

### TAX CRIMES

###### Art. 303 Tax fraud

Whoever totally or partially evades the payment of a fiscal obligation shall be punished with the penalty of six months to three years of imprisonment and a fine equivalent to double the amount defrauded or attempted to be defrauded provided that the amount exceeds the sum of ten minimum wages of the industrial sector and does not exceed twenty-five, when:

Ley No. 641

a) Illegally benefits from a tax incentive or tax refund;

b) Omits the duty to file a tax return or provides false information;

c) Totally or partially conceals the reality of their business regarding production of goods and services or amounts of sales;

d) Fails to comply with the duty to issue invoices for sales made or receipts for professional services rendered; and

e) Omits the payment of taxes by means of stamps or fiscal stamps on documents determined by law.

When the amount defrauded or attempted to be defrauded exceeds the equivalent of twenty-five minimum wages of the industrial sector, the crime of tax fraud shall be punished with imprisonment of three to five years and a fine equivalent to double the amount defrauded or attempted to be defrauded.

###### Art. 304 Determination for tax fraud

For the determination of the penalties referred to in the preceding article, only the amount defrauded or attempted to be defrauded within the same fiscal period shall be taken into account, even when involving different tax obligations and various actions or omissions provided for in said article.

###### Art. 305 Misappropriation of tax withholding

The tax withholding agent authorized by the corresponding law who, through fraudulent alteration or concealment, fails to remit the withheld tax, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and a fine of one to three times the value of the amount not remitted.

###### Art. 306 Fraud against regional and municipal treasuries

The provisions of the two preceding articles shall be applicable, as pertinent, to the case of tax obligations established by law in favor of the Autonomous Regions of the Atlantic Coast and the Municipalities of the country.

###### Art. 307 Customs fraud

Whoever, with the purpose of evading totally or partially the payment of duties and taxes on the importation or exportation of goods and merchandise whose value in córdobas exceeds an amount equivalent to one hundred thousand Central American pesos, performs any act tending to defraud the application of the established tax charges, shall be punished with a penalty of three to six years of imprisonment and a fine equivalent to twice the value of the goods or merchandise involved.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 308 Smuggling

Whoever, with the purpose of evading totally or partially the payment of duties and taxes on the importation or exportation of goods and merchandise whose value in córdobas exceeds an amount equivalent to one hundred thousand Central American pesos, introduces, disposes of, maintains or illegally removes them from the national territory, shall be punished with a penalty of four to eight years of imprisonment and a fine equivalent to twice the value of the goods or merchandise involved.

###### Art. 309 Common provisions

The fines established in the preceding articles shall be paid without prejudice to the payment of the respective charges.

When these crimes are committed by authority, public officials or employees, the penalty of absolute disqualification for a period of six to ten years shall also be imposed, and if they are committed by other offenders, special disqualification of six months to three years shall be imposed to exercise profession, trade, industry, commerce or right related to the criminal activity.

When the act is committed by a director, partner participant or employee of a legal person for its benefit, in addition to the criminal liabilities incurred by the perpetrators and other participants, the legal person shall be subject to the fines and administrative and civil liabilities they have incurred. In case of recidivism, the judicial authority may order the dissolution and liquidation of the legal entity. Whoever, before the final judgment, fully settles the tax obligations shall be exempt from the corresponding penalty for the foregoing crimes, without prejudice to the corresponding administrative sanctions. This provision does not apply to the authority, official or public employee or to the repeat offender for this crime.

###### Art. 310 Accounting violations

Anyone who, being required by law to keep commercial accounting, books or tax records to satisfy obligations with the Treasury, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years and ninety to one hundred twenty days fine:

- a) Keeps two or more similar books with different entries or data, even when involving auxiliary or unauthorized books, to record accounting, tax or corporate operations;
- b) Conceals, destroys, orders or permits the destruction, totally or partially, of the accounting books required by commercial laws or tax provisions, rendering them illegible; or,
- c) Substitutes or alters the foliated pages in the books referred to in Law No. 641 paragraph b).

In the event that the beneficiary of the accounting violation is a legal person, the natural persons involved shall be liable for it.

In case of recidivism for this crime, the penalty shall be applied at its upper maximum limit and a penalty of community service of thirty to one hundred days, for a period of no less than two hours daily.

##### CHAPTER II

### CRIMES RELATED TO PUBLIC EXPENDITURE

###### Art. 311 Fraud in state subsidies, concessions or benefits

Whoever, through fraudulent alterations in accounting statements, concealment of the company's financial situation or false declarations under promise of law, obtains a state subsidy, concession or benefit exceeding the sum equivalent to twenty monthly minimum wages in the industrial sector, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and three hundred to six hundred days fine.

###### Art. 312 Misuse of state subsidies, concessions or benefits

The same penalty of the preceding article shall be imposed on the holder of a state subsidy, concession or benefit exceeding the sum equivalent to twenty monthly minimum wages in the industrial sector who uses or applies the benefit, concession or subsidy distorting its purpose or allocates it to activities or purposes other than those provided for in the rationale of the granting act.

##### CHAPTER III

### CRIMES AGAINST SOCIAL SECURITY

###### Art. 313 Social security fraud

The employer who:

- a) Having deducted and withheld from their workers the social security labor quota, fails to remit it to the corresponding institution, shall be imposed a penalty of four to eight years of imprisonment; or,
- b) Through fraudulent maneuver fails to remit the due employer contribution to social security, shall be imposed a penalty of one to four years of imprisonment.

When the activities described above are carried out in the sphere of national, regional or municipal public administration, in addition to the penalties indicated, absolute disqualification of the responsible official for six to ten years shall be imposed.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 314 Common Provisions.

Whoever, before final judgment, settles the corresponding obligations with social security shall be exempt from penalty, when the contributions due do not exceed three continuous months, without prejudice to the corresponding administrative, civil and labor liabilities. The penalty shall be reduced by one third when the perpetrator of the crime, before the final judgment, fully settles the obligations with social security, without prejudice to the corresponding administrative, civil and labor sanctions.

The authority, official or public employee or the repeat offender for this crime are excepted from these provisions.

#### TÍTULO X

### CRIMES AGAINST LABOR RIGHTS

##### SOLE CHAPTER

###### Art. 315 Discrimination, servitude, exploitation

Whoever discriminates in employment on grounds of birth, nationality, political affiliation, race, ethnic origin, sexual orientation, gender, religion, opinion, economic position, disability, physical condition, or any other social condition, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to one year and ninety to one hundred fifty days fine.

Whoever subjects, reduces or maintains another person in slavery or conditions similar to slavery, forced or compulsory labor, regime of servitude or any other situation against human dignity, in labor activity, shall be punished with imprisonment from five to eight years.

A penalty of imprisonment from five to eight years and one hundred fifty to three hundred days fine shall be imposed on those who traffic persons, for the purpose of subjecting them to labor exploitation activities, as well as forced recruitment to participate in armed conflicts.

The penalty for the crimes indicated in the preceding paragraphs shall be aggravated up to half of the maximum limit of the crime in question, when they are committed:

- a) To the detriment of boys or girls; or
- b) By means of violence or intimidation.

If both circumstances concur, the penalty shall be aggravated up to three-quarters of the maximum limit of the respective crime.

Ley No. 641

Whoever hires for employment a person under eighteen years of age outside the cases authorized by law for purposes of labor exploitation, shall be punished with a penalty of two to four years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 316 Reprisal

Whoever, in reprisal for the exercise of a labor right recognized in the Constitution, international instruments, laws, regulations or collective agreements, terminates the labor relationship or modifies it to the detriment of the worker, shall be punished with ninety to three hundred days fine.

The same penalty shall apply to the employer, manager or administrator who finances or promotes organizations intended to restrict or impede the full freedom and union autonomy contemplated in the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua, international instruments, laws, regulations or collective agreements.

###### Art. 317 Workplace safety

The employer, manager or administrator who, disregarding the firm indications or recommendations issued by competent authority relating to safety and hygiene at work, does not adopt the necessary measures to avoid danger to the life and health of workers or third parties, shall be punished with two to four years of imprisonment or three hundred to six hundred days fine.

Whoever employs or allows persons under eighteen years of age to perform work in unhealthy places and at risk to their life, health, physical, mental or moral integrity, such as work in mines, underground, garbage dumps, nighttime entertainment centers, those that involve handling dangerous machinery, equipment and tools, manual transport of heavy loads, toxic and psychotropic objects and substances, and nighttime work in general or in any other task contemplated as hazardous child labor, according to the corresponding regulations, shall be punished with a penalty of three to six years of imprisonment and four hundred to seven hundred days fine.

###### Art. 318 Trafficking of illegal migrants

Whoever with the purpose of trafficking persons, enters, facilitates the exit or stay, transports, hires or harbors illegal migrants knowing their condition, shall be punished with five to eight years of imprisonment and five hundred to one thousand days fine.

If the crime is committed through negligence, the penalty to be imposed shall be three to five years of imprisonment.

Ley No. 641

#### TÍTULO XII

### CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC SAFETY

##### CAPÍTULO I

FIRE AND OTHER HAVOC

###### Art. 319 Fire

Whoever sets fire to movable property with danger to the safety of persons, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to four years.

If the fire occurs on immovable property, the penalty shall be two to five years of imprisonment.

If the immovable property referred to in the preceding paragraph is a dwelling, educational center, public building, or places intended for religious worship or entertainment, at times when they are crowded, the penalty shall be four to eight years of imprisonment, without prejudice to the liability generated by other crimes produced on occasion of the fire.

###### Art. 320 Havoc

Whoever causes damage of large proportions that entails a danger to the life or integrity of persons or public or private property assets by means of explosion, flooding, landslide, collapse of a building or by any other powerful means of destruction, shall be punished with a penalty of imprisonment from five to eight years.

###### Art. 321 Rendering civil defense works useless

Whoever damages or renders useless dikes or other works intended for civil defense, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to four years.

The same penalty shall apply to whoever, in order to impede or hinder civil defense tasks against a disaster, removes, hides or renders useless materials, instruments or other means intended for civil defense.

The penalty shall be increased by one-third at its minimum and maximum limits when the crime is committed by an authority, official or public employee.

###### Art. 322 Failure to observe safety rules

Whoever in the manufacture, handling, transport, possession or commercialization of explosives, flammable or corrosive substances, toxic, asphyxiating, nuclear materials, radioactive elements or organisms, or any Ley No. 641 other matter, apparatus or devices that can cause havoc, violates the established safety standards, putting in concrete danger the life, physical integrity or health of persons, or the environment, shall be punished with a penalty of six months to two years of imprisonment and special disqualification from one to three years to exercise profession, industry, commerce or right related to the criminal activity.

###### Art. 323 Negligent disaster

Whoever through negligence causes any of the conducts provided in the preceding articles, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to one year or ninety to one hundred fifty days fine.

CONDUCTORS

###### Art. 324 Danger to means of transport

Whoever executes any act that endangers the safety of a vessel, floating construction, air or land motor transport, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to four years.

If the act executed causes shipwreck, stranding or air or land disaster, the penalty shall be four to six years of imprisonment.

The preceding provisions shall apply even if the action falls on one's own property, if from the act danger to life, physical integrity or health of persons is derived and without prejudice to the penalties that may correspond for other results.

###### Art. 325 Attacks against plants or energy conductors

Whoever endangers life, physical integrity or health in any of the following ways shall be punished with imprisonment from two to four years:

- a) Attacking works or installations intended for the production or transmission of electrical energy or energy substances;
- b) Attacking the safety of energy conducting means;
- c) Avoiding or preventing the repair of damage to works or installations referred to in subparagraph a), or the restoration of interrupted energy conductors.

If devastation or disaster results from such acts, the penalty shall be four to six years of imprisonment.

The acts provided for in this article shall also be punishable by imprisonment from four to six years when they are executed with the purpose of preventing or Law No. 641 hindering defense or rescue tasks against a disaster occurring against electrical energy works or installations or energy substances.

###### Art. 326 Driving or operation while intoxicated or under the effects of

pharmaceuticals, drugs and other controlled psychotropic substances or alcoholic beverages

Whoever drives or operates any type of air, land or water vehicle dedicated to collective transport or public service or heavy construction, agricultural or industrial vehicle, under the influence of pharmaceuticals, toxic drugs, narcotics, psychotropic substances or alcoholic beverages in such a way as to diminish their faculties, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to three years and deprivation of the right to drive or operate vehicles for the same period.

In case of recidivism, in addition to the corresponding prison sentence, deprivation of the right to drive or operate vehicles shall be imposed for up to a period of ten years.

###### Art. 327 Obstruction of public services

Whoever creates a dangerous situation, preventing or seriously obstructing the normal functioning of transport by land, water and air, or that of public services for the provision of drinking water, electricity or other energy substances, or telecommunications, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years.

##### CHAPTER IV

###### Art. 328 Piracy

Whoever does the following shall be punished with imprisonment from five to eight years:

- a) Commits at sea, in the air, on lakes or navigable rivers, any act of depredation or violence against a vessel of any draft or aircraft, or against persons or things found therein;
- b) Being inside, seizes any vessel, aircraft or what belongs to its cargo, luggage or gear, by means of deceit or violence committed against its commander or person in charge of it or its crew;
- c) In connivance with pirates, delivers or facilitates the seizure of a vessel or aircraft, its cargo, goods or persons found therein Law No. 641;
- d) With threats or violence, opposes or prevents the commander or crew from defending the vessel or aircraft attacked by pirates;
- e) Knowingly and on their own or another's behalf, equips a vessel or aircraft intended for piracy;
- f) Traffics with pirates or supplies them with aid from the territory of the Republic; or
- g) Through violence or intimidation, prevents a vessel or aircraft from continuing its course or journey, diverting it from its route or unlawfully detaining it.

###### Art. 330 Production and commercialization of harmful substances or substances of restricted use

Whoever, without being authorized by the competent body, produces, dispatches, supplies or commercializes substances harmful to health or of restricted use, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to three years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise, as the case may be, a profession, occupation, industry or commerce related to the criminal conduct.

###### Art. 331 Failure to comply with prescribed formalities

Whoever, being authorized for the traffic of substances or products referred to in the previous article, supplies or dispatches them without complying with the formalities provided for in the respective laws and regulations, endangering the life, physical integrity and health of persons, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years and special disqualification, as the case may be, to exercise profession, occupation, industry, commerce or right related to the criminal conduct for the same period.

Whoever knowingly supplies, imports, distributes or commercializes adulterated, expired or deteriorated medicines, or fails to comply with technical requirements relating to their storage, composition, stability and efficacy or substitutes one for another, endangering the life, physical integrity or health of persons, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to two years and special disqualification from one to three years for the same period to exercise profession, occupation, industry, commerce or right related to the conduct.

###### Art. 333 Production and commercialization of unauthorized pharmaceuticals

Whoever introduces, sells, produces, stores, supplies, commercializes or prescribes pharmaceuticals without the proper health registration of Nicaragua, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years, and special disqualification for the same period to exercise profession, occupation, industry or commerce related to the conduct, as the case may be.

###### Art. 334 Adulteration of medicines

Whoever alters the quantity, dosage or original composition of a medicine with respect to what is authorized and declared on its label, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to four years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise, profession, occupation, industry or commerce related to the conduct, as the case may be.

###### Art. 335 Simulation of pharmaceuticals

Whoever with the purpose of commercializing or using in any manner, imitates or simulates medicines giving them the appearance of genuine ones, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to four years and special disqualification for the same period to exercise profession, occupation, industry or commerce related to the conduct, as the case may be.

The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever, knowing the alteration of the substance and with the purpose of selling them or destining them for use by other persons, has in storage, announces, advertises, offers, exhibits, sells, facilitates or uses in any way the adulterated or simulated medicines or substances, endangering the life or health of persons.

When the crimes indicated in this article are committed by pharmacists or technical directors of legally authorized laboratories in whose name or representation they act, the penalty of three to five years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period to exercise profession, occupation, industry or commerce related to the conduct shall be imposed.

###### Art. 336 Adulteration of food

Whoever uses in food, substances or beverages intended for human consumption additives or other unauthorized agents capable of causing harm to people's health, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years and disqualification for the same period to practice a profession, trade, Law No. 641 industry, or business related to the conduct, as the case may be.

###### Art. 337 Marketing of expired food, in bad condition or without sanitary authorization

Whoever introduces, exports, distributes or markets packaged food without sanitary registration, expired or in bad condition, shall be punished with a penalty of six months to three years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 338 Contamination and sale of meat unfit for consumption

A penalty of one to three years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period to practice a profession, trade, industry or business related to the conduct, as the case may be, shall be imposed on whoever:

- a) Administers to animals whose meat or products are intended for human consumption, additives or substances that are not authorized, prohibited or in doses higher than permitted, which create risk to the life or health of people;
- b) Slaughters animals and destines their products for human consumption knowing that they have been administered the substances mentioned in the previous subsection, or that they are carriers of diseases capable of producing alterations in people's health; or
- c) Sells animals or products derived from animals, for human consumption, with knowledge that they are carriers of transmissible diseases capable of altering health.

The public official or public employee who authorizes the slaughter, sale or marketing of animals or products derived from animals, under the conditions described above, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to four years and special disqualification for the same period to hold office, profession or trade.

###### Art. 339 Slaughter of animals without sanitary control

Whoever slaughters animals for marketing without due authorization and sanitary supervision, causing risk to the life or health of people, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years and special disqualification for the same period to practice a profession, trade, industry or business related to the conduct, as the case may be.

###### Art. 340 Poisoning of water and food

Whoever poisons or contaminates water or food, intended for human consumption, with serious risk to health, shall be punished with imprisonment of five to eight years.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 341 Irrigation with contaminated or wastewater

Whoever knowingly irrigates with contaminated or untreated wastewater any type of crop intended for human or animal consumption, shall be punished with the penalty of six months to two years of imprisonment or fifty to two hundred days fine.

###### Art. 342 Contamination by blood transfusion

Whoever knowingly and on the occasion of a blood transfusion or one of its derivatives or in the preparatory process to carry out this activity, contaminates the recipient person with some disease or condition transmissible by this route, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to five years and disqualification for the same period to practice a profession or trade related to the conduct.

When the previous conducts produce an incurable disease, the penalties shall be increased by one third, in their minimum and maximum limits. Whoever, knowingly, applies to a recipient person a type of blood that is not compatible with their blood type, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to five years and disqualification for the same period to practice a profession or trade related to the conduct.

###### Art. 343 Liability for negligence

When any of the acts provided for in the previous articles of this Chapter is committed through reckless negligence, a reduced penalty shall be imposed, whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of the penalty that the crime in question deserves and its minimum limit one third thereof.

###### Art. 344 Illegal practice of medicine or related professions

Imprisonment of one to four years and ninety to three hundred days fine shall be imposed on whoever:

- a) Without a degree or authorization for the practice of the medical profession or related professions, announces, prescribes, administers or habitually applies any real or supposed means intended for the diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, therapy or prevention of diseases in people, even if done free of charge; or
- b) Having a degree or authorization for the practice of medicine or related professions, lends their name to another who lacks them so that they may practice the acts referred to in the previous subsection. In addition, special disqualification of one to four years shall be imposed to practice a profession or trade related to the conduct.

Uses and customs in traditional healing matters, alternative therapies whose effectiveness is proven, and those attributable to acts of faith that do not threaten the life or integrity of people are not punishable. Law No. 641

###### Art. 345 Experiments on human beings

Whoever conducts medical, chemical, biochemical, physiological or psychological experiments on a person, without the person's consent, and without authorization from the competent body, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to four years and special disqualification of six to eight years to practice a profession or trade related to the conduct.

If the experiments had been carried out on a group of more than ten people, imprisonment of four to six years shall be imposed and the indicated special disqualification penalty shall have a duration of eight to ten years.

If the experiment had consisted of the free distribution or public sale of drugs or medicinal substances, not scientifically proven, or whose main or secondary effects are unknown, imprisonment of six to ten years and special disqualification of ten to fifteen years to practice a profession or trade related to the conduct shall be imposed. The same penalties shall apply to the public official who authorizes the distribution to the public of drugs or medicinal substances under those circumstances.

If the experiment is likely to put people's health at risk, the penalties provided for in the previous paragraphs shall be increased by one third.

###### Art. 346 Trafficking and extraction of human organs and tissues

Whoever without the corresponding authorization imports, exports, traffics or extracts human organs or tissues, shall be punished with imprisonment of five to ten years and special disqualification for the same period for the practice of the profession, trade or activity, related to the conduct. If the human organs or tissues came from a minor under eighteen years of age, the penalty shall be six to twelve years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period. The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever traffics in gametes, zygotes or human embryos, obtained in any manner or under any title.

The previous penalties shall be imposed without prejudice to those that correspond in case of crimes against life or physical integrity.

###### Art. 347 Aggravating circumstances

When the conducts indicated in this Chapter are carried out by an authority, public official or public employee in the exercise of their office, the indicated penalties shall be increased by one third.

###### Art. 348 Illicit financing of narcotics, psychotropics and controlled substances

Whoever illicitly finances the planting, cultivation, harvesting, storage, trafficking, elaboration, manufacturing, transportation or commercialization of narcotics, psychotropics and controlled substances or the seeds or plants from which they are extracted, or the illicit use of precursors, chemicals, solvents or other agents necessary for their obtainment, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to twenty-five years and a proportional fine of five to ten times the value of what was financed.

The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever finances to lease, construct or purchase movable and immovable property, and infrastructure in general, for the same purpose.

###### Art. 349 Production of narcotics, psychotropics and other controlled substances

Whoever with the purpose of exploitation or commerce, illicitly plants, cultivates or harvests plants or seeds from which the controlled substances described in the previous article can be obtained, shall be punished with imprisonment from five to ten years and from one hundred to one thousand days fine.

###### Art. 350 Production, possession or illicit trafficking of precursors

Whoever illicitly introduces into the country or extracts from it, re-exports, transports, diverts, manufactures, stores, commercializes or has in their possession precursors, chemicals, solvents or other necessary agents with the purpose of using them for the processing of narcotics, psychotropics or controlled substances, shall be punished with imprisonment from five to ten years and a fine of one to ten times the market value of the precursors.

###### Art. 351 Industrialization or illegal processing of narcotics, psychotropics and other controlled substances

Whoever illicitly, through any procedure, whether in an industrial or artisanal manner extracts, elaborates, transforms or modifies raw material to obtain narcotics, psychotropics or controlled substances shall be punished with imprisonment from five to twenty years and from one hundred to one thousand days fine.

###### Art. 352 Illegal transport of narcotics, psychotropics and other controlled substances

Whoever by themselves or through an intermediary person, transports narcotics, psychotropics or controlled substances, shall be punished with imprisonment from five to fifteen years and from three hundred to one thousand days fine.

The penalty of four to eight years of imprisonment shall be imposed on whoever with the same purpose, prepares, hides, guides, guards, or conditions the necessary means to carry out the conduct established in the previous paragraph.

Ley No. 641

When the transport is international, the penalty to be imposed shall be from ten to twenty years of imprisonment, and from five hundred to one thousand days fine.

For the purposes of this article, any means shall be understood as means for transport whether it be terrestrial, aerial, maritime, fluvial, or any other object that can be used for the intended purpose.

###### Art. 353 Carrying of narcotics, psychotropics and other controlled substances

Whoever carries on their body, attached to it or hidden in their clothing, narcotics, psychotropics or other controlled substances, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to eight years.

###### Art. 354 Construction or facilitation of runways or landing sites

Whoever knowingly constructs, has constructed or allows to be constructed or facilitates their construction or the use of runways or landing sites, to be used in the trafficking, transport or carrying of narcotics, psychotropics or controlled substances, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to twenty years of imprisonment and from three hundred to eight hundred days fine.

###### Art. 355 Storage of narcotics, psychotropics or other controlled substances

Whoever knowingly or having a duty to know, without being legally authorized, by themselves or through an intermediary person, keeps, guards, hides or stores narcotics, psychotropics or controlled substances, shall be punished with the penalty of imprisonment from five to fifteen years and from one hundred to one thousand days fine.

###### Art. 356 Promotion or encouragement for the consumption of psychotropic narcotics or other controlled substances

Whoever by any means advertises, incites or induces others to consume narcotics, psychotropics or other controlled substances or offers or gives them as a gift, shall be punished with the penalty of five to ten years of imprisonment and from one hundred to five hundred days fine.

###### Art. 357 Supply of products containing aromatic hydrocarbons or similar substances

Whoever knowingly or having a duty to know, supplies to any person products containing aromatic hydrocarbons or similar substances that produce toxic effects, for the purpose of inhalation, shall be punished with a penalty of five to ten years of imprisonment and from one hundred to five hundred days fine.

###### Art. 358 Possession or holding of narcotics, psychotropics and other controlled substances

Whoever is found in possession or is demonstrated to have the holding of narcotics, psychotropics and other controlled substances, in quantities exceeding five grams and less than twenty grams if it is marijuana, and exceeding one gram and less than five grams if it is Ley No. 641 cocaine or any other controlled substance, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to three years and from fifty to one hundred days fine.

If the seized substances exceed the limits of twenty grams in the case of marijuana and five grams in the case of cocaine or any other controlled substance, the penalty to be imposed shall be from three to eight years of imprisonment and from one hundred to three hundred days fine.

###### Art. 359 Trafficking of narcotics, psychotropics and controlled substances

Whoever illicitly, distributes, sells, exchanges, dispenses, offers for sale or in any other way commercializes narcotics, psychotropics or controlled substances, shall be punished with imprisonment from five to fifteen years and from three hundred to eight hundred days fine.

The same penalty shall be imposed on owners, administrators or any employee or whoever provides services to the establishment, who knowing or having a duty to know and in a direct or indirect manner, permits or facilitates the previous conduct in their respective premises.

When the trafficking of these substances is carried out at an international level, introducing them, extracting them or in transit through the national territory, the penalty of ten to twenty years of imprisonment and from five hundred to one thousand days fine shall be imposed.

###### Art. 360 Provocation, proposition and conspiracy

The proposition, provocation or conspiracy expressed by any means, to commit any of the illicit acts established in this chapter, shall be punished with a penalty equivalent to one third of the penalty established for the crime that is proposed, provoked or conspired.

###### Art. 361 Common provisions

Whoever with knowledge of the cause facilitates properties of any type to store, elaborate, manufacture, cultivate or transform narcotics, psychotropics or controlled substances or facilitates means for their transport, shall be considered a co-author of the previous crimes, and shall be punished with the corresponding penalty of the crime in question.

For purposes of this chapter, the narcotics, psychotropics, controlled substances and precursors to which the previous articles refer, are those contained in the Law on the matter and those defined by the Ministry of Health.

###### Art. 362 Aggravating Circumstances

The minimum and maximum limits of the penalties established in this Chapter shall be increased by one third when:

- a) The crime is committed against persons under eighteen years of age or incapacitated persons;

###### Art. 365 Soil and Subsoil Contamination

Whoever, directly or indirectly, without due authorization from the competent authority, and in contravention of the respective technical standards, discharges, deposits or infiltrates or permits the discharge, deposit or infiltration of wastewater, liquids or chemical or biochemical materials, toxic waste or contaminants in soils or subsoils, with danger or damage to health, natural resources, biodiversity, water quality or ecosystems in general, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to five years and from one hundred to one thousand days-fine.

The penalties established in this article shall be reduced by one third at their minimum and maximum limits, when the crime is committed by reckless imprudence.

###### Art. 366 Water Contamination

Whoever, directly or indirectly, without due authorization from the competent authority and in contravention of the respective technical standards, discharges, deposits or infiltrates or permits the discharge, deposit or infiltration of wastewater, liquids or chemical or biochemical materials, toxic waste or contaminants in marine waters, rivers, basins and other water reservoirs or currents with danger or damage to health, natural resources, biodiversity, water quality or ecosystems in general, shall be punished with imprisonment from two to five years and from one hundred to one thousand days-fine. The penalty of four to seven years of imprisonment shall be imposed when, for the purpose of concealing water contamination, greater volumes of water are used than those generated by wastewater discharges, thus contravening the technical standards that establish the particular conditions of discharges in environmental matters.

Ley No. 641

The penalties established in this article shall be reduced by one third at their minimum and maximum limits, when the crime is committed by reckless imprudence.

###### Art. 367 Atmospheric Contamination

Whoever without due authorization from the competent authority and in contravention of the respective technical standards, through the use or carrying out of burning of solid and liquid, chemical or biochemical or toxic materials, generates or discharges point or continuous emissions that contaminate the atmosphere and air with gases, smoke, dust or contaminants with serious damage to the health of persons, natural resources, biodiversity or ecosystems shall be punished with imprisonment from three to five years and from one hundred to one thousand days-fine.

The penalties established in this article shall be reduced by one third at their minimum and maximum limits, when the crime is committed by reckless imprudence.

###### Art. 368 Transport of Toxic, Hazardous or Contaminating Materials and Waste

The penalties established in this article shall be reduced by one third at their minimum and maximum limits, when the crime is committed by reckless imprudence.

###### Art. 369 Storage or Handling of Toxic, Hazardous,

Explosive, Radioactive or Contaminating Substances

Whoever without complying with the measures and precautions established in the current legislation in a manner that endangers or damages the life or health of the population or the environment or natural resources; stores, distributes, markets, handles or uses gasoline, diesel, kerosene or other petroleum derivatives, butane gas, insecticides, fertilizers, pesticides or any other agrochemical, toxic, hazardous, explosive, radioactive or contaminating substances, shall be punished with one hundred to one thousand days-fine and imprisonment from three to five years and special disqualification for the same period from exercising a trade, art, profession or commercial or industrial activity.

The penalties established in this article shall be reduced by one third at their minimum and maximum limits, when the crime is committed by reckless imprudence.

##### CHAPTER III

### OFFENSES AGAINST NATURAL RESOURCES

###### Art. 373 Illegal exploitation of natural resources

Whoever, without authorization from the competent authority or exceeding what is authorized, exploits, conceals, trades, exploits, transports, traffics or benefits from specimens, products or parts of fauna resources, forest, floristic, hydrobiological, genetic resources and mineral substances, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to two years of imprisonment and from one hundred to three hundred days fine.

###### Art. 374 Diversion and illicit exploitation of waters

Whoever, without authorization from the competent authority or exceeding what is authorized, constructs dikes, retaining walls, drills, obstructs, retains, exploits, diverts or causes to diminish the free course of waters from rivers, streams or other natural drainage channels or from the subsoil, or in management zones, closed zones or natural reserves in a permanent manner, directly affecting ecosystems, the health of the population or economic activities, shall be punished with a penalty of one to three years of imprisonment and from one hundred to five hundred days fine.

###### Art. 375 Fishing during closed season

Whoever fishes or carries out activities of extraction, collection, capture, trade or transport of hydrobiological resources, in prohibited areas or during closed season, shall be punished with imprisonment from one to two years.

The minimum and maximum limits of the penalty of the preceding paragraph shall be increased by double, if in committing the act unauthorized or prohibited gear is used by the competent authority, or specimens declared threatened or in danger of extinction are captured or extracted in accordance with

Law No. 641 national legislation and the international instruments to which Nicaragua is a party, or that do not comply with the minimum sizes and weights for capture established by the competent authority.

Whoever captures or extracts specimens of hydrobiological resources that do not comply with the minimum sizes and weights established in the corresponding laws, even if it is not during closed season, shall be punished with a penalty of one to two years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 376 Transfer of catch or discarding on the high seas

Whoever transfers fishing products on the high seas or does not land them in Nicaraguan ports shall be punished with three to five years of imprisonment. With the same penalty shall be punished whoever carries out massive discarding of fishing products to the sea or captures sharks in continental, maritime, lacustrine waters or any other body of water, solely to cut off their fins or tail.

In the cases of the preceding paragraphs, in the conviction sentence, the Judge shall order the definitive cancellation of the license granted for the fishing activities on the occasion of which the offense was committed.

###### Art. 377 Fishing without conservation devices

Whoever, authorized for fishing, carries out fishing activities without having installed on their vessels the conservation and protection devices for species established by national legislation and the international instruments to which the State is a party, shall be punished with two to four years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 378 Fishing with explosives or other destructive form of fishing

Whoever fishes with explosive elements, poisons or carries out fishing activities with methods that allow the indiscriminate destruction of species, as well as the use of trammel nets in inlets or natural reefs shall be punished with two to four years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 379 Fishing with unauthorized foreign flag

Whoever carries out fishing activities with industrial or artisanal vessels of foreign flag without due authorization shall be punished with three to five years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 380 Hunting of animals in danger of extinction

Whoever hunts animals that have been declared in danger of extinction by international instruments ratified by the State, or defined as such by law or by administrative provision, shall be punished with a penalty of one to four years of imprisonment and from five hundred to one thousand days fine.

The minimum and maximum limits of the penalty provided in the preceding paragraph shall be Law No. 641 increased by double, if the activity is carried out in protected areas.

If the hunting is carried out on species of animals that are not in danger of extinction, but without the permission of the competent authority or in protected areas, a fine of one hundred to four hundred days shall be imposed.

###### Art. 381 Commercialization of fauna and flora

Whoever without authorization from the competent authority, commercializes or sells species of wild fauna or flora that are not catalogued by law or administrative provision as endangered species or restricted for commercialization, shall be sanctioned with fifty to one hundred days fine. Excepted from the previous paragraph is fishing or hunting for rational self-consumption, when it does not involve species or subspecies on the way to extinction or is not carried out in national, ecological or municipal parks and wildlife refuges.

###### Art. 382 Aggravating circumstance

The penalties indicated in the two previous articles shall be increased by one third in their minimum and maximum limits when the hunting or commercialization of species is destined for international traffic or trade.

###### Art. 383 Forest fires

Whoever causes a forest fire or incites others to carry out a forest fire, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of two to four years of imprisonment and five hundred to one thousand days fine.

Whoever, being authorized by competent authority and due to their imprudence, carries out agricultural burns that cause damage outside the areas designated to carry out such burning, shall be sanctioned with fifty to two hundred days fine.

Whoever without authorization from competent authority carries out agricultural burns and causes damage in forest areas shall be sanctioned with the penalties provided in the first paragraph, whose greater and lesser extremes shall be increased to double. Controlled burns authorized by the competent authority, nor damages produced as a consequence of a fortuitous or unexpected situation shall not constitute an offense.

###### Art. 384 Cutting, exploitation and forest ban

Whoever without corresponding authorization, destroys, removes totally or partially, trees or plants on state, vacant, communal lands, private property and public roads, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of six months to two years of imprisonment and two hundred to five hundred days fine.

Whoever without corresponding authorization, clear-cuts trees on Ley No. 641 lands defined as forest, or of forest vocation, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of two to five years of imprisonment and two hundred to five hundred days fine. Whoever authorizes clear-cutting in areas defined as forest or of forest vocation to change the vocation of land use, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of three to seven years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period from holding employment or public office.

If the activities described in the previous paragraphs are carried out in protected areas, the penalty shall be four to ten years of imprisonment and five hundred to one thousand days fine.

Exploitation carried out for purposes of domestic use or consumption, in accordance with the legislation on the matter, shall not constitute an offense.

Whoever cuts species under ban, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three to seven years.

###### Art. 385 Cutting on slopes and watersheds

Whoever, even if they are the owner, deforests, cuts or destroys trees or shrubs, in areas designated for the protection of watersheds or natural springs or slopes determined by the law on the matter, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of two to five years of imprisonment and five hundred to one thousand days fine.

###### Art. 386 Illegal cutting, transport and commercialization of timber

Whoever cuts, transports or commercializes forest resources without the respective permit from the competent authority, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of two to four years of imprisonment and five hundred to eight hundred days fine.

###### Art. 387 Cutting or pruning of trees in urban areas

Whoever cuts or destructively prunes one or more trees on the sides of highways, avenues, streets or boulevards, electrical or telecommunications line easements, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of six months to four years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 388 Non-compliance with Environmental Impact Study

Whoever deforests, cuts or destroys, removes totally or partially herbaceous vegetation, or trees, without complying, when applicable, with the Environmental Impact Studies (EIA) and the technical and environmental regulations established by the competent authority, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of two to four years and two hundred to five hundred days fine.

###### Art. 389 Restitution, reparation and compensation for environmental damage

In the case of the offenses contemplated in this Title, the Judge must order at the expense of the author or authors of the act and in accordance with the principle of proportionality any of the following measures in order of priority: Ley No. 641

a) Restitution to the state prior to the commission of the punishable act:

- b) Reparation of the environmental damage caused; and
- c) Total compensation for the environmental damage produced.

If the offenses were carried out through a legal entity, the accessory consequences that fall on the legal entity provided in this Code shall also be applied.

###### Art. 390 Introduction of invasive species, biological or biochemical agents

Whoever without authorization, introduces, uses or propagates in the country invasive flora and fauna species, biological or biochemical agents capable of significantly altering animal or plant populations or endangering their existence, in addition to causing damage to the ecosystem and biodiversity, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of one to three years of imprisonment and a fine of five hundred to one thousand days.

##### CAPÍTULO IV

ANIMAL CRUELTY

###### Art. 391 Physical harm or cruelty to animals

Whoever mistreats, subjects to cruel treatment or is cruel to an animal of any species, whether domestic or not, and regardless of their use or purpose, even if they are their property, causing physical harm by blows, punishments or manifestly excessive work that leads them to suffer impediments or causes damage to their health, stress or death, shall be sanctioned with fifty to two hundred days fine or community service work of ten to twenty days for a period of no less than two hours daily.

Whoever carries out violent spectacles between animals, whether in public or private places shall be sanctioned with imprisonment of three to six months. If the spectacle is carried out for profit, a penalty of six months to two years of imprisonment shall be imposed.

Excepted from the previous provisions are spectacles or games of popular tradition, such as cockfights and bullfights.

#### TÍTULO XVI

### OFFENSES AGAINST PUBLIC TRANQUILITY

##### CAPÍTULO I

ASSOCIATION TO COMMIT CRIMES AND ORGANIZED CRIME

###### Art. 392 Association to commit crimes

Whoever forms part of an association of two or more persons, organized with the permanent purpose of committing or favoring less serious offenses, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of imprisonment of six months to one year.

Ley No. 641

The leaders and promoters of an illicit association, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of one to two years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 393 Organized Crime

Whoever forms part of an organized criminal group or structured national or international gang, of two or more persons, that exists for a certain time and acts in concert with the purpose of obtaining directly or indirectly, an economic benefit or of any kind, with the purpose of committing one or more serious offenses, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of five to seven years of imprisonment.

The penalty shall be increased in its minimum and maximum extremes:

- a) By one third, if the author holds a position of superiority in relation to the rest of the persons involved in the criminal organization, or if the offense is carried out totally or partially at an international level.
- b) To double if the offense committed is sanctioned with a penalty equal to or greater than fifteen years of imprisonment.

Provocation, conspiracy and proposition to commit the offense, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of one to five years of imprisonment.

##### CAPÍTULO II

TERRORISM

###### Art. 394 Terrorism

Anyone who, acting in service of or collaboration with gangs, organizations or armed groups, using explosives, toxic substances, weapons, fires, flooding, or any other act of mass destruction, carries out acts against persons, property, public services and means of transport, as a means to produce alarm, fear or terror in the population, in a group or sector thereof, alter the constitutional order, seriously alter public order or cause panic in the country, shall be punished with a penalty of fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 395 Financing of Terrorism

Anyone who generates, collects, obtains, channels, deposits, transfers, moves, secures, administers, safeguards, intermediates, lends, provides, delivers funds or assets from lawful or unlawful sources to be used in the commission of any terrorist act or deed described in the preceding article, or in any other way finances them or finances a terrorist organization without participating in its execution or it is not consummated, shall be punished with a penalty of fifteen to twenty years of imprisonment.

Law No. 641

The penalty shall be increased by one third in its minimum and maximum limits, when the crime is committed through the financial system or by a partner, director, manager, administrator, watchman, external or internal auditor, representative or employee of a public entity or by an authority, official or public employee.

###### Art. 396 Hostage-taking

Anyone who deprives one or more persons of their freedom and holds them against their will for terrorist purposes, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.

If as a consequence of the facts described above, the death or injuries of one or more persons occur, they shall be punished in addition to the penalty described above, with the penalty of the corresponding crime.

###### Art. 397 Specific aggravating circumstance

The minimum and maximum limits of the penalties established in the preceding chapters shall be increased by one third, when the following circumstances concur:

- a) That the crime is committed in an educational institution, health center, or in their vicinity or in other places, to which schoolchildren, students and citizens go to carry out educational, sports, social activities or on property that is part of the archaeological, historical and artistic heritage of the country;
- b) That girls, boys and adolescents are used or victimized, for the commission of these crimes.

###### Art. 398 Incitement, proposal and conspiracy to commit terrorist acts

The incitement, proposal and conspiracy to commit terrorist acts shall be punished with a penalty whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of the respective penalty of the crime in question and whose minimum limit shall be half of this.

##### CHAPTER III

### CRIMES AGAINST THE SECURITY OF VESSELS AND PORT FACILITIES

###### Art. 399 Crimes Against the Security of Civil Aviation

Anyone who on board an aircraft in flight, by means of violence, threat of violence or any other form of intimidation seizes an aircraft, exercises control over it or executes any act that endangers the security of the aircraft or of the persons or property transported therein, shall be punished with a penalty of four to eight years of imprisonment. The same penalty shall be imposed on anyone who:

Law No. 641

a) Destroys an aircraft on the ground that is in service or causes damage that renders it incapable of flight or that by its nature constitutes a danger to the security of the aircraft in flight;

b) Places or causes to be placed on an aircraft in service, by any means, a device or substance capable of destroying it or of causing damage that renders it incapable of flight or that by its nature constitutes a danger to the security of the aircraft in flight;

c) Destroys or damages airport facilities or air navigation services or disrupts their operation, if such acts by their nature constitute a danger to the security of the aircraft in flight;

d) Knowingly communicates false information, thereby endangering the security of an aircraft in flight.

###### Art. 400 Crimes against navigation and port security

Anyone who affects navigation or port security, by performing any of the following acts, shall be punished with a penalty of four to eight years of imprisonment:

- a) Destroys a vessel in port that is in service or causes damage that renders it incapable of navigation or that by its nature constitutes a danger to the security of the vessel.
- b) Places or causes to be placed on a vessel in service by any means, a device or substance capable of destroying it or of causing damage, that renders it incapable of navigating or that by its nature constitutes a danger to the security of the vessel.
- c) Destroys or damages port facilities or water navigation services or disrupts the operation of the port, if such acts by their nature constitute a danger to the security of the vessel or port services.
- d) Knowingly communicates false information thereby endangering the security of a vessel.

##### CHAPTER IV

### CRIMES AGAINST THE CONTROL AND REGULATION OF WEAPONS, AMMUNITION, EXPLOSIVES AND OTHER DANGEROUS MATERIALS

###### Art. 401 Illegal carrying or possession of firearms or ammunition

Anyone who sells, carries, possesses or facilitates the carrying or use of a firearm or ammunition, without having the respective license or authorization, shall be punished with a penalty of six months to one year of imprisonment and fifty to one hundred days fine. Law No. 641

###### Art. 402 Illicit trafficking of weapons

Anyone who imports, exports, transports, delivers or transfers firearms, ammunition and their accessories, from outside or through the national territory, in contravention of the provisions of the respective legislation, shall be punished with a penalty of two to six years of imprisonment and two hundred to five hundred days fine. A penalty of six months to two years shall be imposed on anyone who, for the same purpose, prepares, conceals or conditions the necessary means to carry out the conducts established in the preceding paragraph.

###### Art. 403 Alteration of technical characteristics of firearms

Anyone who alters, eliminates or modifies the system or technical mechanisms, manufacturer's mark, serial number, model, type, barrel change or caliber of a firearm, without due and prior authorization from the competent authority, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and one hundred to two hundred days fine.

###### Art. 404 Manufacture, trafficking, possession and use of restricted weapons, substances or explosive devices

Anyone who without authorization or license, transports, manufactures, trades, imports, or exports from the national territory, possesses or stores restricted weapons, according to national legislation; automatic or semi-automatic weapons for military use or substances or explosive devices, shall be punished with a penalty of four to eight years of imprisonment and two hundred to five hundred days fine.

The same penalty shall be imposed on anyone who for criminal purposes manufactures firearms in an artisanal manner that simulate or reach the capacity of authorized weapons.

###### Art. 405 Trafficking, stockpiling or storage of prohibited weapons

Anyone who imports, exports, transports, possesses, delivers, intermediates, stockpiles, stores, distributes, transfers, from outside or through the national territory, prohibited weapons, according to national legislation shall be punished with imprisonment of eight to twelve years and two hundred to five hundred days fine.

###### Art. 406 Construction or provision of landing strips

Whoever constructs, or facilitates the use of landing strips or docking sites, to be used in the transportation of explosive, bacteriological, chemical substances, weapons of mass destruction, goods or money from illicit trafficking of weapons, munitions, explosives, other related materials and connected activities, shall be punished with imprisonment of eight to twelve years and a fine of three hundred to seven hundred days

###### Art. 407 Delivery of weapons or dangerous substances to persons who cannot

handle them

Whoever sells or entrusts weapons, explosive materials or poisonous or Law No. 641 corrosive substances to a minor under sixteen years of age or to any person who does not have the physical, mental, civil or legal capacity in such a way that represents a serious danger to himself or a third party, shall be punished with a penalty of one to two years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 408 Common provision

In addition to the penalties provided in this chapter, a penalty of special disqualification of six months to three years shall be imposed, to exercise commerce, profession or industry and the deprivation of the right to possession and carrying of weapons.

The penalties shall be increased by one third, when the crimes of this Chapter are committed by an authority, official or public employee, related to any of the activities provided, in addition to absolute disqualification for the same period.

#### TÍTULO XVII

### CRIMES AGAINST STATE SECURITY

##### CAPÍTULO I

ACTS OF TREASON

###### Art. 409 Treason

The Nicaraguan who in an international armed conflict takes up arms against Nicaragua or joins its enemies providing them with aid, collaboration or facilitates the advance or progress of enemy forces or hinders the defense of the State, shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to fifteen years and absolute disqualification, for the performance of function, employment or public office for the same period.

The same penalties shall be imposed on whoever induces a foreign State to declare war on Nicaragua or conspires with it for the same purpose. If the perpetrator is an authority, official or public employee, the previous penalties shall be increased by one third.

###### Art. 410 Undermining national integrity

Whoever carries out acts that tend to undermine or fracture the territorial integrity of Nicaragua, to subject it in whole or in part to foreign domination, to affect its nature as a sovereign and independent State, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to fifteen years of imprisonment and absolute disqualification, for the same period for the performance of function, employment or public office, except as provided in the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua.

###### Art. 411 Treason committed by foreigners

The preceding provisions are applicable to foreigners residing in Nicaraguan territory, except as established by treaties ratified by Law No. 641

Nicaragua or by international law concerning diplomatic officials and nationals of countries in conflict.

###### Art. 412 Provocation, proposal and conspiracy

Provocation, proposal and conspiracy to commit any of the acts provided in this Chapter, shall be punished with a penalty whose maximum limit shall be the lower end of the respective penalty and whose minimum limit shall be half of it.

##### CAPÍTULO II

### CRIMES THAT COMPROMISE PEACE

###### Art. 413 Hostile acts

Whoever with illegal acts or that are not duly authorized by the national government, in accordance with national legislation, provokes or places in imminent danger a declaration of war against Nicaragua, or exposes its inhabitants to experience harassment or reprisals against their persons or property, or endangers the peaceful relations of the Nicaraguan government with a foreign government, shall be punished with a penalty of two to six years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 414 Violation of immunity

Whoever violates the immunity of a head of state, head of government, or any person protected by immunity in accordance with international treaties ratified by Nicaragua, shall be punished with a penalty of six months to three years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 415 Violation of state secrets

Whoever improperly obtains, uses or reveals state secrets relating to national security, national defense, foreign relations of the State, determined as reserved information in accordance with the law on the matter, and that endangers national security or peaceful relations with other countries, shall be punished with a penalty of three to eight years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 416 Imprudent revelation of state secrets

Whoever by reckless imprudence allows the secrets mentioned in the preceding article to become known, which he possesses by virtue of his employment, occupation or an official contract, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to two years.

###### Art. 417 Intrusion

Whoever improperly for espionage purposes commits any of the following acts shall be punished with a penalty of four to eight years of imprisonment:

- a) Draws up or reproduces plans or documentation referring to zones, installations or military materials that are of restricted access and Law No. 641 whose knowledge is protected and reserved by information legally classified as reserved;
- b) For purposes contrary to the security of the State, takes, traces or reproduces images of fortifications, ships, establishments, roads or military works;
- c) Enters into computer programs relating to national security or national defense; or
- d) Has in his possession objects or information legally classified as reserved, relating to security, national defense or foreign relations of the State.

###### Art. 418 Diplomatic infidelity

Whoever, officially designated by the Nicaraguan government to direct a negotiation with a person or group of persons from another country, with a foreign state or an international organization, acts outside the instructions received to the detriment of the interests of Nicaragua, shall be punished with a penalty of five to eight years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period to exercise function, employment, profession, occupation or public office.

###### Art. 419 Violation of contracts of military interest

Whoever, when the State is at war, does not duly comply with contractual obligations relating to the needs of the armed forces, shall be punished with imprisonment of four to eight years. If the breach was imprudent, the penalty shall be imprisonment of two to four years.

#### TÍTULO XVIII

### CRIMES AGAINST THE POLITICAL CONSTITUTION OF THE REPUBLIC OF NICARAGUA

##### CAPÍTULO I

CRIMES AGAINST THE CONSTITUTIONAL ORDER

###### Art. 420 Rebellion

Whoever takes up arms to achieve any of the following purposes shall be punished with a penalty of five to seven years of imprisonment:

- a) Modify, suspend or repeal the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua in whole or in part.
- b) Substitute any Power of the State or prevent the free exercise of its functions.
- c) Substitute the President of the Republic or the government cabinet or prevent the free exercise of their functions.

Law No. 641

d) Withdraw the nation in whole or in part from the obedience of the constituted government.

The inducers, promoters, leaders of the rebellion, shall be punished with a penalty of eight to ten years of imprisonment.

The subordinates with commands, shall be punished with a penalty of six to eight years of imprisonment

###### Art. 421 Riot

Those who, without being included in the crime of rebellion, publicly rise up with violence to prevent the enforcement of laws or resolutions of authorities, officials or public employees; force them to take a measure or grant some concession, or prevent the exercise of public functions, provoking serious disturbance of public order, shall be punished with a penalty of six months to two years of imprisonment and special disqualification from exercising employment or public office for the same period.

The inducers, promoters and leaders of the riot shall be sanctioned with a penalty of three to five years of imprisonment and special disqualification from exercising employment or public office for the same period.

Subordinates with command shall be punished with a penalty of one to three years of imprisonment and special disqualification from exercising employment or public office for the same period.

###### Art. 422 Special Aggravation

If the conducts provided for in the crimes of rebellion or riot have been carried out by an authority, official or public employee, the penalties shall be increased by one third at their minimum and maximum extremes and absolute disqualification shall be imposed for the same period. In the case of rebellion, in addition, the impediments indicated for this purpose by the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua shall be applied.

###### Art. 423 Desistance

No crime shall exist when the rebels or rioters submit to legitimate authority or dissolve, before it makes summons to them or as a consequence of it, without having caused any other harm than the momentary disturbance of order. In this case, only the inducers, promoters and leaders of the crime of rebellion shall be punishable, who shall be sanctioned with one third of the penalty established for the crime.

###### Art. 424 Provocation, Proposal and Conspiracy

The provocation, proposal and conspiracy to commit the crimes of rebellion or riot shall be sanctioned with a penalty whose maximum limit shall be the lower limit of the respective penalty of the crime in question, and whose minimum limit shall be half of this.

Ley No. 641

###### Art. 425 Seduction, usurpation and illegal retention of command

Whoever personally seduces members of the armed or police forces to remove them from their military or police command, usurps or illegally retains military or police command, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of five to seven years of imprisonment and special disqualification from exercising employment, office, profession or trade for the same period.

###### Art. 426 Breach of duty of resistance

The authorities, officials or public employees who, being in charge of maintaining public order, do not confront rebellion or riot with the means at their disposal and with due opportunity, shall be considered as accomplices to the crime in question.

##### CAPÍTULO II

OF CRIMES AGAINST CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS AND GUARANTEES

###### Art. 427 Discrimination

Whoever prevents or hinders another from exercising a right or power provided for in the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua, laws, regulations and other provisions, for any economic, social, religious, political, personal or other reason or condition, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of imprisonment of six months to one year or three hundred to six hundred days fine.

###### Art. 428 Promotion of discrimination

Whoever publicly promotes the performance of acts of discrimination, as indicated in the previous article, shall be punished with one hundred to five hundred days fine.

###### Art. 429 Crimes against freedom of expression and information

Whoever prevents, through violence or intimidation, the exercise of freedom of expression, the right to inform and be informed, the free circulation of a book, magazine, newspaper, tapes reproducing voice or image, or any other means of emission and dissemination of thought, shall be sanctioned with three to five years of imprisonment and special disqualification from exercising the profession or trade related to the criminal activity for the same period.

If the conduct previously described were carried out through bribery or deceit, a penalty of six months to two years of imprisonment and special disqualification from exercising the profession or trade related to the criminal activity for the same period shall be imposed.

###### Art. 430 Obstacle to the assistance of counsel or the rights of the accused,

defendant or convicted person

The authority, official or public employee who prevents or obstructs the right to legal assistance of the accused, defendant, convicted person, seeks or favors their waiver of such assistance or does not inform them immediately or comprehensibly of their rights and the reasons for their Ley No. 641 detention, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of one hundred to three hundred days fine and special disqualification for one to three years from exercising a profession, employment or public office.

###### Art. 431 Suspension of Constitutional Guarantees

The authority, official or public employee who, outside the cases provided for in the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua, totally or partially suspends, in part or throughout the national territory, rights, freedoms or guarantees established therein, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of five to ten years of imprisonment.

If the suspension affects rights, freedoms, or guarantees that cannot be suspended according to the constitutional norm, the penalty shall be seven to fifteen years of imprisonment.

In both cases, absolute disqualification from exercising office or public employment shall be imposed for the same period as the prison sentence imposed.

#### TÍTULO XIX

### CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION

##### CAPÍTULO I

OF ABUSES OF AUTHORITY

###### Art. 432 Abuse of authority or functions

The authority, official or public employee who, with abuse of their office or function, orders or commits any act contrary to the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua, laws or regulations to the detriment of the rights of any person, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of six months to two years of imprisonment and disqualification from exercising office or public employment of six months to four years.

###### Art. 433 Breach of duties

The authority, official or public employee who, without justified cause, omits, refuses or delays any act due and proper to their function, to the detriment of any person, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of six months to one year of imprisonment and disqualification from exercising office or public employment for the same period.

###### Art. 434 Requisition of force against legitimate acts

The authority, official or public employee who, abusing their office, requests the assistance of the National Police or the Army of Nicaragua to prevent the execution of legal provisions or orders of the authority or of judicial sentences or resolutions, shall be punished with special disqualification of three to six years from exercising office or public function.

Ley No. 641

###### Art. 435 Abandonment of public functions

The authority, official or public employee who unjustifiably abandons their functions, causing damage to public service, shall be subject to one hundred to five hundred days fine or ten days of work for the benefit of the community of two hours daily. The exercise of the right to strike in accordance with the law is excepted from this provision.

###### Art. 436 Illegal appointment

The authority, official or public employee who, in the exercise of their competence and knowingly of its illegality, appoints or gives possession for the exercise of a certain public office to any person without the legally established requirements being met, shall be punished with one hundred to three hundred days fine and special disqualification of one to four years from exercising employment or public office.

The same penalties shall be imposed on whoever, knowingly of its illegality, accepts the appointment or taking of possession.

##### CAPÍTULO II

OF DISOBEDIENCE AND DENIAL OF ASSISTANCE

###### Art. 437 Denial of assistance

The authority, official or public employee who, when required in the exercise of their competence, does not provide the assistance legally required by competent authority, shall be punished with a penalty of special disqualification from one to four years for public employment or office.

The authority, official or public employee who, when required by an individual to provide the assistance to which they are obligated by reason of their office to prevent a crime, shall be punished according to the gravity of the same with the penalty of special disqualification for the exercise of public office or employment from six months to two years.

###### Art. 438 Disobedience of authority, official or public employee

Authorities, officials or public employees who openly refuse to duly comply with judicial resolutions, decisions or orders of competent authority, issued within the scope of their respective competence and vested with legal formalities, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of ninety to one hundred fifty days fine and special disqualification to exercise public employment or office for a period of six months to two years.

Authorities, officials or public employees shall not incur criminal liability for not complying with a mandate that constitutes a clear and manifest infringement of a constitutional or legal provision.

Ley No. 641

###### Art. 439 Non-appearance before National Assembly

The official, authority or public employee who, having been duly summoned by the National Assembly to appear in matters within its competence, and without just cause, refuses to appear or omits, conceals or alters required information, shall be sanctioned with six months to one year imprisonment and disqualification to exercise public office or employment for the same period.

The same conduct shall be incurred by one who, having a legal or contractual relationship with state institutions or who has in their possession documents or information on the matter being investigated or of public interest, shall be sanctioned with fifty to one hundred fifty days fine.

##### CAPÍTULO III

OF INFIDELITY IN THE CUSTODY OF DOCUMENTS

AND VIOLATION OF SECRETS

###### Art. 440 Improper access to documents or reserved public information

The authority, official or public employee who accesses or allows access to documents or public information whose access is reserved in accordance with the law of the matter, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of one to three years imprisonment and disqualification from two to four years to exercise public employment or office.

###### Art. 441 Revelation, disclosure and exploitation of information

The authority, official or public employee who reveals or discloses information or documents declared as reserved public information or private information in accordance with the law of the matter, shall be punished with three to five years imprisonment and disqualification to exercise public employment or office for the same period.

If the author is in charge of the custody of the information or document, the penalty to be imposed shall be four to eight years imprisonment and disqualification to exercise public employment or office for the same period.

The individual who, taking advantage of reserved public information or private information revealed by the authority, official or public employee under the conditions indicated in the preceding paragraphs and obtains profit or benefit for themselves or for a third party, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of three to five years imprisonment.

###### Art. 442 Negligent facilitation

The authority, official or public employee who through reckless imprudence gives rise to the conducts described in this Chapter, shall be sanctioned with the penalty of disqualification to exercise public employment or office from six months to two years.

Ley No. 641

##### CAPÍTULO IV

### CRIMES AGAINST ACCESS TO PUBLIC INFORMATION

###### Art. 443 Denial of Access to Public Information

The authority, official or public employee who, outside the cases established by law, denies or prevents access to required public information, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of six months to two years imprisonment, and disqualification from one to two years for the exercise of public employment or office.

###### Art. 444 Violation of informational self-determination

The authority, official or public employee who discloses private information or refuses to rectify, update, delete false information about a person contained in archives, files, databases, or public registries, shall be sanctioned with imprisonment from six months to two years, and disqualification from one to two years to exercise public employment or office

##### CAPÍTULO V

### OF BRIBERY

###### Art. 445 Bribery committed by authority, official or public employee

The authority, official or public employee who requires or accepts, by themselves or through third parties, a gift, money, favors, promises or advantages, or any object of pecuniary value for themselves or for another person or entity, in exchange for the performance, delay, expediting or omission of any act in the exercise of their public functions, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of four to six years imprisonment and disqualification for the same period, to exercise public employment or office.

###### Art. 446 Bribery committed by individual

Whoever, by themselves or through third parties, offers or grants to an authority, official or public employee, a gift, money, favors, promises, advantages or any object of pecuniary value, for that authority, official or public employee or for another person or entity, in exchange for the performance, delay, expediting or omission of any act in the exercise of their functions, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of three to six years imprisonment and three hundred to five hundred days fine

###### Art. 447 Requirement or acceptance of undue advantages for an act performed

or omitted

The authority, official or public employee who requires or accepts for themselves or for a third party a gift, money or any other undue advantage, for themselves or for another person or entity, for an act performed or omitted, in their capacity as authority, official or public employee, shall be punished with four to six years imprisonment.

The same penalty shall apply to whoever, by themselves or through third parties, offers or grants to an authority or official or public employee, a gift, money, favors, Ley No. 641 promises, advantages or any object of pecuniary value, for that authority, official or public employee or for another person or entity, in exchange for an act performed or omitted in the exercise of their functions.

If the gift or advantage is required or accepted under the sole circumstance of the condition of their function, the penalty to be imposed shall be two to five years imprisonment.

###### Art. 448 Illicit enrichment

The authority, official or public employee, who without incurring in a more severely punished crime, obtains an increase in their assets with significant excess, with respect to their legitimate income, during the exercise of their functions and who cannot reasonably justify its origin, when required by the competent body indicated in the law, shall be sanctioned with three to six years imprisonment and disqualification for the same period to exercise public office or employment.

###### Art. 449 International bribery

A non-resident foreigner who offers, promises, grants or confers to a national authority, official or public employee, or a national or resident foreigner, who engages in the same conduct, with respect to officials of another state or of an international organization or entity, directly or through an intermediary person or entity, any object of pecuniary value or other benefits, such as gifts, money, favors, promises or advantages, in exchange for the authority, official or public employee, having performed or omitted, or to perform or omit any act in the exercise of their functions, related to an economic or commercial activity of an international nature, shall be punished with four to eight years of imprisonment and from five hundred to one thousand days fine. The authority, official or public employee who requests or accepts from a non-resident foreigner, directly or indirectly, a gift, money, favors, promises or advantages or any object of pecuniary value, for themselves or for another person or entity, in exchange for performing or omitting or for having performed or omitted any act, in the exercise of their functions, related to an economic or commercial activity of an international nature, shall be punished with four to eight years of imprisonment and disqualification for the same period to hold office or public employment.

##### CAPÍTULO VI

TRAFFICKING IN INFLUENCE

###### Art. 450 Trafficking in influence

The authority, official or public employee, who by themselves or through another person or acting as an intermediary, influences another authority, official or public employee, of equal, lower or higher rank, taking advantage of the exercise of the powers of their office or any other situation derived from their personal or hierarchical relationship with them or with another authority, official or Ley No. 641 public employee or abusing their real or supposed influence to obtain an undue advantage or benefit, which may generate directly or indirectly a profit, economic or of any other nature, for themselves or for third parties, shall be punished with the penalty of four to six years of imprisonment and absolute disqualification for the same period.

The private individual who influences an authority, official or public employee and takes advantage of any situation derived from their personal relationship with them or with another authority, official or public employee to obtain an undue advantage or benefit that may generate directly or indirectly an economic profit of any other nature for themselves or for a third party, shall be punished with the penalty of three to six years of imprisonment.

##### CAPÍTULO VII

EMBEZZLEMENT

###### Art. 451 Embezzlement

The authority, official or public employee who subtracts, appropriates, misapplies or consents, that another subtracts, appropriates or misapplies goods, funds, securities or public effects, whose administration, possession or custody have been entrusted to them by reason of their position in functions in the public administration, organs, dependencies, deconcentrated, decentralized, autonomous entities or State enterprises of the Municipality and of the Autonomous Regions, to obtain for themselves or for a third party a benefit, shall be punished with imprisonment of four to ten years, and absolute disqualification for the same period.

If the goods, funds, securities or effects subtracted, appropriated or misapplied had been declared of cultural, paleontological, historical, artistic, archaeological value, or if they are effects destined to alleviate some public calamity, the penalty of six to twelve years of imprisonment and absolute disqualification for the same period shall be imposed.

These provisions shall also be applicable to the administrators and depositaries of goods, funds, securities or effects that have been delivered by competent authority, even if they belong to private individuals.

For the purposes of this Chapter and the following one, public goods, funds or effects shall be understood as all goods, movable and immovable, tangible or intangible, corporeal and incorporeal, funds, securities assets and other rights that belong to the State or its institutions, whatever the source from which they originate and the documents or legal instruments that accredit, attempt to steal or refer to the ownership or other rights over said assets.

Ley No. 641

##### CAPÍTULO VIII

MISAPPROPRIATION OF PUBLIC FUNDS

###### Art. 452 Misappropriation of public funds

The authority, official or public employee who gives a different destination from that designated by law, for public funds, movable or immovable property, money or securities belonging to any public administration, organs, dependencies, deconcentrated, decentralized, autonomous entities or State enterprises, of the Municipality and of the Autonomous Regions or organisms dependent on some of them, whose administration, possession or custody have been entrusted to them by reason of their position or functions in the public administration, shall be punished with a penalty of two to five years of imprisonment and disqualification for the same period, to hold employment or public office.

###### Art. 453 Use of human resources of the Public Administration

The authority, official or public employee who takes advantage of or permits another to take advantage of or who gives for private use, for their own benefit or that of a third party, human resources in the service or person under custody of the administration or state, regional or municipal entity or of decentralized, deconcentrated or autonomous entities, to which they have had access by reason of or on the occasion of the function performed, shall incur the penalty of two to four years of imprisonment and disqualification for the same period to hold office or public employment.

##### CAPÍTULO IX

FRAUD AND EXACTIONS

###### Art. 454 Fraud

The authority, official or public employee who in contracts, supplies, bidding, price competitions, auctions, or any other operation in which they intervene by reason of their office or special commission, defrauds or consents to defrauding the public administration, organs, dependencies, deconcentrated, decentralized, autonomous entities or State enterprises, of the municipality and of the Autonomous Regions, shall be punished with a penalty of five to ten years of imprisonment and disqualification for the same period to hold office or public employment.

###### Art. 455 Exactions

The authority, official or public employee who abusing their office demands or causes to be paid rights, tariffs, fees, taxes, contributions, rates or levies that are non-existent or in a greater amount than that designated by law, shall be punished, without prejudice to the reimbursements to which they are obliged, with two to six years of imprisonment, and disqualification for the same period to hold office or public employment.

Ley No. 641

##### CAPÍTULO X

NEGOTIATIONS PROHIBITED TO PUBLIC OFFICIALS OR EMPLOYEES

AND ABUSES IN THE EXERCISE OF THEIR FUNCTION

###### Art. 456 Incompatible professional activity

The authority, official or public employee who, outside the cases admitted in the laws or regulations, performs by themselves or through an intermediary person, a professional or advisory activity permanent or incidental under the dependence or in the service of private entities or individuals, in matters in which they must intervene or in which they have intervened by reason of their office and functions, shall be punished with a penalty of two to four years of imprisonment and disqualification for the same period to hold office or public employment.

###### Art. 457 Business Incompatible with the Position

The authority, official or public employee who, openly or covertly or in any other manner, takes for themselves in whole or in part, property or assets in whose auction, lease, adjudication, embargo, sequestration, judicial participation, deposit or administration, they intervene by reason of their position or office or participates in any negotiation or speculation for profit or personal interest over the same properties or assets, or over anything in which they have official intervention, shall be sanctioned with a penalty of four to six years of prison and one hundred to three hundred days of fine.

###### Art. 458 Use of Reserved Information

The authority, official or public employee who makes use of any type of reserved information of which they have had knowledge by reason of or on occasion of the function performed with intent to obtain an economic benefit for themselves or for a third party, shall incur the penalty of two to six years of prison and disqualification for the same period to exercise employment or public office. If the economic benefit pursued is actually obtained, the penalty shall be imposed in its upper half.

If serious damage results to the public cause or to a third party, the penalty shall be prison of three to seven years, prison and disqualification for the same period to exercise employment or public office.

###### Art. 459 Benefited Third Party

Whoever obtains a benefit derived from the commission of the criminal conducts established in this Title, shall be sanctioned with the same penalty of the crime committed by the authority, official or public employee.

Law No. 641

#### TITLE XX

### CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC ORDER

##### SOLE CHAPTER

###### Art. 460 Obstruction of Functions

Whoever employs intimidation or violence to prevent or obstruct an authority, official or public employee from fulfilling an act proper to their functions, shall be sanctioned with prison of one to four years.

The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever intimidates or exercises violence against the person who provides assistance to the authority, official or public employee in the exercise of a legitimate act of their functions, at their request or by virtue of a legal duty or in apprehension of a person in flagrante delicto.

###### Art. 461 Aggravating Circumstances

In the case of the preceding article the penalty shall be aggravated by one third in its minimum and maximum extremes.

a) If the act was committed with a firearm;

b) If the act was committed with the participation of two or more persons;

c) If the perpetrator took advantage of their condition as authority, official, or public employee.

###### Art. 462 Disobedience or Contempt of Authority

Whoever disobeys a judicial resolution or one emanating from the Public Prosecutor's Office, except in the case of their own detention, shall be penalized with six months to one year of prison or fifty to one hundred fifty days fine.

There shall be no crime when the disobeyed resolution is voluntarily or by requirement of authority subsequently complied with.

#### TITLE XXI

### CRIMES AGAINST THE ADMINISTRATION OF JUSTICE

##### CHAPTER I

OF PERVERSION OF JUSTICE AND PROFESSIONAL DISLOYALTY

###### Art. 463 Perversion of Justice

Prison of five to seven years and absolute disqualification for the same period shall be imposed on the Judge or Magistrate who incurs in any of the following conducts:

- a) Issues a resolution against the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua or express law;
- b) Bases the resolution on a false fact;
- c) Hears a case that they sponsored as a lawyer;
- d) Advises or counsels the parties or their lawyers who litigate in cases Law No. 641 pending in their office;
- e) During the processing of the case becomes involved in business or sentimentally with any of the parties or their relatives within the second degree of consanguinity.

###### Art. 464 Denial of Justice

The judge or magistrate who refuses to resolve, without having legal cause, or under the pretext of obscurity, insufficiency or silence of the law, shall be penalized with three hundred to six hundred days fine and special disqualification to exercise employment or public office for two to six years.

###### Art. 465 Malicious Delay

The judge or magistrate who maliciously delays the administration of justice, shall be penalized with three hundred to six hundred days fine and special disqualification for two to six years. The delay shall be understood as malicious when it is provoked to affect the interests of any of the parties.

If the same conduct described in the preceding paragraph is carried out by the prosecutor, procurator, secretary or judicial employee, the preceding penalties shall be reduced by half.

###### Art. 466 Unfaithful Representation

The lawyer who deliberately harms the interests that have been entrusted to them, shall be penalized with three hundred to six hundred days fine and special disqualification for two to six years.

If the preceding conduct is carried out through reckless imprudence, it shall be sanctioned with special disqualification for the exercise of law practice for six months to two years.

Shall be sanctioned with the same penalties indicated in the preceding paragraph:

- a) the lawyer who, having advised, defended or represented a person, advises, defends or represents in the same matter someone who has contradictory interests; or
- b) the lawyer who destroys, renders useless or conceals documents or information to which they have had access by reason of their profession, with prejudice to the interests of the party they represent, assist or advise.

###### Art. 467 Equivalent Subjects

The provisions of the preceding article shall be applicable to prosecutors, procurators, public defenders, assistants, secretaries, technical consultants or expert witnesses for a party, arbitrators or mediators.

Law No. 641

##### CHAPTER II

OF THE OMISSION OF THE DUTY TO PROSECUTE CRIMES

AND TO PROMOTE THEIR PROSECUTION

###### Art. 468 Omission of the Duty to Prosecute Crimes

The authority, official or public employee who, failing in the obligation of their position, illegally ceases to promote or continue the prosecution of crimes of which they have notice, shall be sanctioned with two hundred to five hundred days fine and special disqualification to exercise employment or public office for one to three years.

###### Art. 469 Omission of the Duty to Prevent Crimes

Whoever with their immediate intervention, without risk to themselves or others, and with capacity to do so does not prevent the commission of a crime that affects persons in their life, integrity, health, liberty or sexual liberty, shall be penalized with two hundred to five hundred days fine if the crime is against life, and one hundred to five hundred days fine in other cases.

The same penalties shall be incurred by whoever, being able to do so, does not go to the authority or its agents so that they prevent a crime of those provided in the preceding paragraph and of whose imminent or current commission they have notice.

##### CHAPTER III

OF CONCEALMENT

###### Art. 470 Concealment

Shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years, whoever, with knowledge of the commission of a crime and without having participated in it as author or participant, intervenes after its execution in any of the following ways:

- a) Assists the authors or participants to benefit from the profit, product or price of the crime;
- b) Conceals, alters or renders useless the effects or instruments of a crime to prevent its discovery;
- c) Helps the alleged perpetrators of a crime to evade investigation by the authority or its agents, or to escape their search or capture. In this case, the spouse or partner in a stable de facto union, ascendants, descendants, brothers or sisters shall be exempt from criminal liability.

In no case may a prison sentence exceeding that assigned to the concealed crime be imposed. If the latter were punished with a penalty of another nature, the prison sentence shall be replaced by a fine of ninety to three hundred days, unless the concealed crime has an equal or lesser penalty assigned to Law No. 641 it, in which case half of the minimum extreme of the penalty applied to the principal crime shall be imposed on the guilty party.

###### Art. 471 Specific aggravating circumstances

The penalties established in the preceding article shall be increased by one third in their minimum and maximum limits when:

- a) The concealed crime is a serious crime;
- b) The author is an authority, public official or public employee with abuse of their public functions. In this case, in addition to the penalties imposed, disqualification from holding public office for three to five years shall be imposed.

The provisions of this Chapter shall apply even when the author of the concealed act is exempt from criminal liability.

##### CHAPTER IV

OF FALSE ACCUSATION AND REPORT

###### Art. 472 False accusation and report

Whoever, with knowledge of its falsity, reports to a competent authority or accuses any person of facts that if true would constitute a crime, shall be punished with a penalty of:

- a) Imprisonment of six months to two years and three hundred to six hundred days fine, if a serious crime is imputed; or
- b) Imprisonment of six months to one year and ninety to three hundred days fine if a less serious crime is imputed.

The penalty shall be three to eight years of imprisonment, if the conviction of the innocent person results.

No proceedings may be brought against the complainant or private accuser, except after a judgment of not guilty or final dismissal order, or also final order rejecting the accusation for lack of merit issued by a competent judge. Without prejudice to the corresponding responsibilities, members of the Public Prosecutor's Office in the exercise of public criminal action shall not incur this crime.

###### Art. 473 Simulation of crimes

Whoever before a competent authority simulates being responsible for or victim of a crime or misdemeanor or reports a non-existent one and provokes investigative or procedural actions shall be imposed a fine of one hundred to two hundred days.

##### Chapter V

Of perjury and false testimony

###### Art. 474 Perjury

Whoever fails to tell the truth regarding their own facts when the obligation to tell it is imposed under legal promise in a judicial case, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years or three hundred to six hundred days fine.

###### Art. 475 False testimony

Whoever, when giving testimony or statement in a judicial or administrative case, conceals or distorts true facts or simulates or affirms false facts, totally or partially, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to five years.

If the false testimony is given against the accused or defendant in a criminal case, the prison sentence shall be five to seven years. If as a consequence of the false testimony a conviction has been issued, a penalty of six to eight years of imprisonment shall be imposed.

The preceding penalties shall be increased by one third in their minimum and maximum limits, when the falsity is committed through bribery.

In no case may a prison sentence exceeding that assigned to the accused crime be imposed. If the latter were punished with a penalty of another nature, the prison sentence shall be replaced by a fine of ninety to three hundred days, unless the accused crime has an equal or lesser penalty assigned to it, in which case half of the minimum extreme of the penalty applied to the principal crime shall be imposed on the guilty party.

###### Art. 476 Falsity in expert opinion, interpretation or translation

The penalties of the preceding article shall also be imposed on experts, interpreters or translators who conceal or distort true facts or simulate or affirm false facts, totally or partially. In addition, the penalty of special disqualification for four to six years to exercise the profession, trade, employment or public office in question shall be imposed on them.

The preceding penalty shall be increased by one third in its minimum and maximum limits, when the falsity is committed through bribery.

###### Art. 477 Offering and exchange of witnesses, experts, interpreters or translators

Whoever knowingly has offered or exchanged a witness, expert, interpreter or translator, who has incurred falsity in their statement, report or translation in a judicial or administrative case, shall be punished with a penalty of two to four years of imprisonment and special disqualification for the same period to exercise the employment, trade or public office in question.

The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever consciously presents false documents or evidence in court.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 478 Bribery of witnesses, experts, interpreters or translators

Whoever offers or promises a gift or any other advantage to one of the persons referred to in the preceding article, so that they commit false testimony, if the offer or promise is not accepted or, being accepted, the falsity is not committed, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years.

If the falsity is committed, the corresponding penalties shall be applied to the briber, to whoever provided or concealed false information.

###### Art. 479 Retraction

The penalties shall be reduced by two thirds in cases of the crime of false testimony when the witness, expert, interpreter or translator, having given false testimony, report or translation, retracts before the Judge and states the truth, for it to take effect before a judgment is issued in the proceeding where they gave the statement, report or translation.

If the retraction takes place after the judgment is issued, the penalty shall be reduced by half.

##### CHAPTER VI

OF OBSTRUCTION OF JUSTICE

###### Art. 480 Obstruction of justice

Whoever, legally summoned to appear before a Judge or Court in a criminal case, fails to appear or absents themselves without just cause, and causes the suspension of the procedural act, shall be punished with ninety to three hundred days fine. Whoever, personally or through another person, has impeded or obstructed the appearance or facilitated the absence of the summoned person, shall be punished with one hundred to three hundred days fine. The fine penalty shall be increased by one third in its minimum and maximum extremes, when the perpetrator is a lawyer, representative, advisor or assistant of one of the parties in an investigation or trial proceeding.

A penalty of two to seven years of imprisonment shall be imposed if, in order to impede or obstruct the appearance or facilitate the absence, violence or intimidation is used, without prejudice to the penalties corresponding to the acts of violence or intimidation exercised.

If the failure to appear or the absence causes the interruption or consequent annulment of the trial hearing in a criminal case, the penalty shall be two hundred to five hundred days fine.

If the suspension of the procedural act is caused by the absence without just cause of the prosecutor, defense counsel or attorney, in addition to the days fine penalty indicated in the previous paragraph, special disqualification to hold Law No. 641 employment, public office or professional practice shall be imposed for a period of three months to one year.

If the person responsible for the suspension of the procedural act due to their failure to appear without just cause is the magistrate, judge or judicial secretary, in addition to the days fine penalty indicated in the first paragraph, special disqualification to hold employment, public office or professional practice shall be imposed for a period of six months to two years.

###### Art. 481 Undue influence in the proceedings

Whoever with violence or intimidation attempts to influence or influences directly or indirectly a complainant, party or accused, lawyer, prosecutor, attorney, expert, interpreter, translator or witness, in a proceeding, so that they alter their statement, testimony, opinion, interpretation, translation, defense or action, in a judicial proceeding, shall be punished with imprisonment of four to eight years.

The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever carries out any act against the life, integrity, freedom, sexual freedom or property, in retaliation against the persons mentioned in the previous paragraph, for their actions in the judicial proceeding. The penalties indicated in the previous paragraphs shall be imposed without prejudice to those corresponding to the acts of violence or intimidation exercised.

##### CAPÍTULO VII

DE LA FACILITACIÓN PARA LA EVASIÓN

Y EL QUEBRANTAMIENTO DE CONDENA

###### Art. 482 Facilitation of escape

The authority, official or public employee who procures, permits or facilitates the escape of a person legally detained or a convicted person, shall be punished with imprisonment of one to three years and special disqualification to hold employment or public office of two to six years.

A private person who provides the means for the escape of a person legally detained or the breach of sentence by a convicted person, shall be imposed a penalty of one to two years of imprisonment.

If violence or intimidation against persons, force against things or bribery is used to facilitate the escape, the penalty shall be five to seven years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 483 Breach of sentence

Whoever breaches their sentence, security measure, imprisonment, precautionary measure or custody, shall be punished with a penalty of imprisonment of six months to two years if they were deprived of liberty and ninety to three hundred days fine in other cases.

Ley No. 641

#### TÍTULO XXII

### DELITOS CONTRA EL ORDEN INTERNACIONAL

##### CAPÍTULO I

GENOCIDIO

###### Art. 484 Genocidio

Twenty to twenty-five years of imprisonment shall be imposed on whoever, with the purpose of destroying totally or partially a certain group of persons, by reason of their nationality, ethnic group or race, religious belief or political ideology, carries out any of the following acts:

- a) Cause the death of one or more members of the group;
- b) Seriously injure the physical or mental integrity, or attack the freedom, or sexual integrity of one or more members of the group;
- c) Subject one or more members of the group to conditions of existence that bring about their physical destruction, total or partial;
- d) Carry out forced displacements of the group or its members or impose measures intended to prevent their reproduction, hinder births within the group or forcibly transfer children or adolescents of the group to another different group.

###### Art. 485 Provocation, proposal and conspiracy

Provocation, proposal and conspiracy to commit genocide shall be punished with a penalty of ten to fifteen years of imprisonment.

##### CAPÍTULO II

### DELITOS DE LESA HUMANIDAD

###### Art. 486 Tortura

Whoever subjects another person to any type of physical or psychological torture for purposes of criminal investigation, as an intimidating means, personal punishment, preventive measure, penalty or any other purpose, shall be punished with a penalty of seven to ten years of imprisonment.

The authority, official or public employee who carries out any of the conduct described in the previous paragraph shall be imposed, in addition to the imprisonment penalty, that of absolute disqualification of eight to twelve years.

The authority, official or public employee who does not prevent the commission of any of the acts typified in the previous paragraphs, when they have knowledge and competence to do so, shall be punished with a penalty of five to seven years of imprisonment and special disqualification to hold employment or public office of five to nine years. The same penalty shall be imposed on the authority, official or public employee who, having knowledge of the commission of any of the acts indicated in the previous paragraphs and lacking competence, fails to report the act to the competent authority within forty-eight hours, from the moment they learned of them. Ley No. 641

For the purposes of this Code, torture shall be understood as intentionally causing serious pain or suffering, whether physical or psychological, to a person who the accused has under their custody or control, however pain or suffering arising solely from lawful sanctions or that are a normal or incidental consequence of them shall not be understood as torture.

###### Art. 487 Apartheid

Whoever commits against a person an inhumane act with the intention of maintaining an institutionalized regime of systematic oppression and domination of one racial group over one or more racial groups, shall be punished with ten to twenty years of imprisonment.

###### Art. 488 Forced disappearance of persons

The authority, official, public employee or agent of authority who detains a person legally or illegally and does not give reasons about their whereabouts, shall be punished with a penalty of four to eight years of imprisonment and absolute disqualification from office or public employment of six to ten years.

##### CAPÍTULO III

### DELITOS CONTRA LAS PERSONAS Y BIENES PROTEGIDOS EN CONFLICTO ARMADO

###### Art. 489 Ataque a personas protegidas

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, attacks protected persons endangering their lives, or causing them serious suffering, shall be punished with a penalty of five to ten years of imprisonment. For the purposes of this Code, protected persons shall be understood as the wounded, sick or shipwrecked, health or religious personnel, combatants who have laid down their arms, prisoners of war and persons detained during the armed conflict, civilian persons and civilian population, according to the international instruments on the matter that Nicaragua has ratified.

###### Art. 490 Attacks against personal dignity

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, commits attacks against the personal dignity of a protected person, especially through humiliating or degrading treatment, shall be punished with imprisonment of six to eight years.

###### Art. 491 Apartheid and other inhuman and degrading practices based on racial discrimination

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, commits against a protected person an inhuman act with the intention of maintaining an institutionalized regime of systematic oppression and domination of one racial group over one or more racial groups, shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to twenty years.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 492 Intentional homicide

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, intentionally causes the death of a protected person shall be punished with imprisonment of fifteen to twenty-five years.

###### Art. 493 Causing starvation with risk to life

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, intentionally causes starvation with risk to life to the civilian population as a method of warfare, depriving it of objects indispensable to its survival, shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to fifteen years.

###### Art. 494 Sexual crimes

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, commits acts of rape, sexual slavery, forced prostitution, sexual exploitation, forced pregnancy, forced sterilization or any other form of sexual violence against a protected person, shall be punished with imprisonment of fifteen to twenty years.

###### Art. 495 Biological experiments

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, carries out biological experiments that violate the physical or mental integrity of protected persons, shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to twenty years.

###### Art. 496 Harmful medical acts

Whoever, on the occasion of an internal or international armed conflict, intentionally carries out medical actions or omissions that are not justified by the state of health of the protected persons or that are not in conformity with the generally accepted rules of medicine and that cause harm to the health and physical or mental integrity, including physical mutilations, medical or scientific experiments, the removal of tissues or organs for transplants, shall be punished with imprisonment of five to ten years.

If the offense is committed by medical health personnel, the penalty of the preceding paragraph shall be increased by one third and, in addition, special disqualification for the same period to exercise the trade or profession in question shall be imposed.

###### Art. 497 Indiscriminate attack on civilian population

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, carries out an indiscriminate attack that affects the civilian population, knowing that such attack will cause deaths or injuries among said population or civilian damage, excessive in relation to the concrete and direct military advantage anticipated, shall be punished with imprisonment of fifteen to twenty-five years.

For the purposes of this Code, indiscriminate attack shall be understood as any Law No. 641 attack that is not directed against a specific military objective, as well as where methods or means of combat have been employed that cannot be directed against a specific military objective and whose effects cannot be limited in accordance with the provisions of International Humanitarian Law.

###### Art. 498 Attacks against unequivocal acts of surrender

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, knowing that there have been unequivocal acts of surrender by the adversary, continues attacking persons hors de combat with the purpose of leaving no survivors, of finishing off the wounded and sick or abandoning them or other types of acts of barbarity, shall be punished with imprisonment of fifteen to twenty-five years.

###### Art. 499 Violation of truce

Whoever violates a truce or armistice agreed between Nicaragua and an adversary State, or between national or foreign belligerent forces, shall be punished with imprisonment of five to ten years.

###### Art. 500 Improper use of emblems and insignia

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, with the purpose of harming or attacking the adversary, simulates the status of protected person or uses protective signs such as the Red Cross or the Red Crescent, the flag of the United Nations or of international organizations, of truce or surrender; flags, uniforms or insignia of the enemy or of neutral countries or of military or police detachments of the United Nations, or other protective signs contemplated in international treaties ratified by Nicaragua, shall be punished with imprisonment of six months to three years.

###### Art. 501 Taking of hostages

Whoever, serving exclusively the purposes of the armed conflict itself, whether of an international or internal nature, deprives protected persons of their liberty, with the object of requesting any conduct from the other party that benefits them as a condition for respecting their security and integrity, or releasing those detained, shall be punished with imprisonment of ten to fifteen years.

###### Art. 502 Unjustified delay of repatriation

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, unjustifiably delays the repatriation of protected persons, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to seven years.

###### Art. 503 Illegal deportation or transfer

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, deports or illegally transfers a protected person, in particular when transferring to occupied territory population of the occupying power, or deports or transfers Law No. 641 within or outside the occupied territory all or part of the population of that territory, or orders the displacement of the civilian population unless required by the security of the civilians concerned or for imperative military reasons, shall be punished with imprisonment of five to twelve years.

###### Art. 504 Illegal detention of protected persons

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, illegally deprives a protected person of their liberty shall be punished with imprisonment of five to eight years.

###### Art. 505 Non-compliance with due process

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, deprives a protected person of the possibility of being tried in accordance with the guarantees of due process established in the Political Constitution of the Republic of Nicaragua, shall be punished with imprisonment of five to eight years.

###### Art. 506 Omission and obstruction of relief and humanitarian assistance measures

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, omits relief and humanitarian assistance measures in favor of protected persons, being obliged to provide them, shall be punished with imprisonment of three to six years.

Likewise, whoever, under the same circumstances, obstructs or prevents medical, health and relief personnel or the civilian population from carrying out the health and humanitarian tasks that, in accordance with the norms of International Humanitarian Law, they may or must carry out, shall be punished with imprisonment of four to eight years.

###### Art. 507 Attack on protected property

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, attacks property of a civilian nature, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to five years. If the action falls upon property indispensable to the survival of the civilian population, the penalty to be imposed shall be imprisonment of five to twelve years.

###### Art. 508 Use of human shields

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, uses the presence of a protected person to shield certain points, zones or military forces from military operations, shall be punished with imprisonment from seven to fifteen years.

###### Art. 509 Recruitment of children

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, recruits or enlists persons under eighteen years of age in the armed forces, or uses them to participate actively in hostilities, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

Ley No. 641

###### Art. 510 Pillage and attack on towns, villages or squares

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, attacks or pillages a town, village or square, even when taken by assault and that are not defended, shall be punished with imprisonment from four to eight years.

###### Art. 511 Attack on installations containing dangerous forces

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, carries out an attack against works or installations containing dangerous forces knowing that such attack will cause deaths or injuries among the civilian population or damage to civilian property, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

For the purposes of this Code, dangerous forces shall be understood as those which, when released, may cause significant losses to the civilian population, such as water contained in dams or dykes, nuclear energy generated in power plants, toxic deposits, among others.

###### Art. 512 Attack on undefended localities

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, carries out an attack on undefended localities, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

An undefended locality shall be understood as any inhabited place, by prior agreement, that is in the proximity of or within a zone where armed forces are in contact and that is open to occupation by an adverse party. Such locality must not have presence of combatants, weapons and mobile military equipment, nor shall there exist any activity in support of military operations, nor shall the authorities or the population commit acts of hostility, this prohibition including the hostile use of fixed military installations or establishments.

###### Art. 513 Attack on demilitarized zones

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, carries out an attack on demilitarized zones, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

Demilitarized zones shall be understood as those zones to which, by verbal or written agreement, the status of demilitarized zone has been conferred. Such demilitarized zone must not have presence of combatants, weapons and mobile military equipment and all activity related to the military effort must have ceased, nor shall the authorities or the population commit acts of hostility, this prohibition including the hostile use of fixed military installations or establishments.

Ley No. 641

###### Art. 514 Declaration that there be no survivors

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, orders an attack or makes a declaration to the effect that there be no survivors, shall be punished with imprisonment from ten to fifteen years.

###### Art. 515 Compelling to serve in enemy forces

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, compels a prisoner of war or another protected person to serve in the armed forces of the adverse party, shall be punished with imprisonment from five to eight years.

###### Art. 516 Destruction or appropriation of property

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, destroys or appropriates property, on a large scale and arbitrarily, without justification by military necessity, shall be punished with imprisonment from four to eight years.

###### Art. 517 Attacks against peacekeeping or humanitarian assistance

mission

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, intentionally directs attacks against personnel, installations, material, units or vehicles participating in a peacekeeping or humanitarian assistance mission in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations, provided they are entitled to the protection granted to civilians or civilian objects in accordance with international humanitarian law, shall be punished with imprisonment from seven to ten years.

###### Art. 518 Destruction of medical property and installations

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, attacks or destroys ambulances and medical transports, field hospitals and hospitals, depots of relief supplies, medical convoys, property destined for assistance and relief for the civilian population and for other protected persons and against personnel using the distinctive emblems of the Geneva Conventions in accordance with international law, shall be punished with imprisonment from five to ten years.

###### Art. 519 Destruction of cultural property

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, attacks, destroys, removes, pillages, steals, improperly uses, commits acts of vandalism against or renders useless cultural property or places of worship, or those to which protection has been conferred by virtue of special agreements, or cultural property under enhanced protection, or buildings dedicated to education, science or charity, shall be punished with imprisonment from four to eight years. Ley No. 641

Shall be punished with the same penalty whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, uses cultural property under enhanced protection or its immediate surroundings in support of military actions.

Cultural property shall be understood as movable and immovable property, such as: monuments of architecture, art or history, religious or secular, archaeological sites, monumental centers comprising a considerable number of cultural property, works of art, manuscripts, books and other objects of historical or artistic interest, as well as buildings whose main purpose is to preserve or display the cultural property described, such as museums, libraries, archive repositories and shelters intended for the protection of cultural property in case of armed conflict.

###### Art. 520 Destruction of the environment

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, launches an intentional attack that causes extensive, lasting and severe damage to natural resources and the natural environment, which would be manifestly excessive in relation to the concrete and direct military advantage, shall be punished with imprisonment from five to ten years.

###### Art. 521 Prohibited weapons and methods of combat

Whoever, on the occasion of an international or internal armed conflict, uses prohibited means and methods of warfare intended to cause unnecessary suffering or losses or superfluous harm, shall be punished with a sentence of five to ten years of imprisonment.

For the purposes of this article, prohibited means of warfare are:

- a) Poison or poisoned weapons;
- b) Asphyxiating, toxic or similar gases or any analogous liquids, materials or devices;
- c) Chemical, biological, reactive or atomic weapons;
- d) Bullets which expand or flatten easily in the human body, such as bullets with a hard jacket which does not entirely cover the core or is pierced with incisions;
- e) Weapons the primary effect of which is to injure by fragments which cannot be detected by X-rays in the human body;
- f) Mines, booby-traps and other similar devices.

For the purposes of this article, mines, booby-traps and other similar devices are:

Law No. 641

Mines, booby-traps and other devices fitted with a mechanism or device specifically designed to detonate the munition by the presence of commonly available mine detectors as a result of their magnetic or other non-contact influence during normal use in detection operations, prohibited by the Protocol on Prohibitions or Restrictions on the Use of Mines, Booby-Traps and Other Devices as amended on May 3, 1996. Self-deactivating mines fitted with an anti-handling device designed so that the anti-handling device can function after the mine has ceased to be capable of functioning, prohibited by the Protocol on Prohibitions or Restrictions on the Use of Mines, Booby-Traps and Other Devices as amended on May 3, 1996.

Booby-traps and other devices which are in any way attached to or associated with:

- a) Internationally recognized protective emblems, signs or signals;
- b) Sick, wounded or dead persons;
- c) Burial sites, crematoriums or cemeteries;
- d) Medical facilities, equipment, supplies or transports;
- e) Toys or other portable objects or products especially designed for the feeding, health, hygiene, clothing or education of children;
- f) Food or drink;
- g) Kitchen utensils or appliances, except in military establishments, military locations or military supply depots;
- h) Objects of a clearly religious character;
- i) Historic monuments, works of art or places of worship which constitute the cultural or spiritual heritage of peoples;
- j) Living or dead animals; prohibited by the Protocol on Prohibitions or Restrictions on the Use of Mines, Booby-Traps and Other Devices as amended on May 3, 1996;

Booby-traps or other devices in the form of apparently harmless portable objects which are specifically designed and constructed to contain explosive material, prohibited by Protocol II on Prohibitions or Restrictions on the Use of Mines, Booby-Traps and Other Devices as amended on May 3, 1996.

###### Art. 522 Responsibility of the superior

The superior who exercises authority over his subordinates, in an international or internal armed conflict, when crimes have been committed by forces under his effective command and control, or his effective authority and control, by reason of not having exercised appropriate control over his subordinates, shall be punished with the same penalty as that prescribed for the offenses described in this title, when:

Law No. 641

a) He had known or, in view of the circumstances at the time, should have known that the forces or subordinates were committing such crimes or were about to commit them; and

b) He had not taken all necessary and reasonable measures within his power to prevent or repress their commission or to submit the matter to the competent authorities for investigation and prosecution.

#### TITLE I

OFFENSES AGAINST PERSONS

##### SOLE CHAPTER

###### Art. 523 Assaults against persons

A penalty of ten to forty-five days fine, or community service work of fifteen to forty-five days of two hours per day, shall be imposed on whoever:

- a) Causes another an injury that does not require medical treatment beyond initial medical assistance;
- b) Mistreats or strikes another person, throws stones or similar objects at them, or in any way physically assaults them, not requiring medical treatment, or,
- c) Maliciously releases or incites a dog or other animal against a person.

###### Art. 524 Mass assaults

Whoever incites the physical assault of a person or group of persons against other persons or public or private property, shall be punished with fifteen to sixty days fine, or community service work of twenty to sixty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 525 Discharge of firearms

Whoever, in a populated or frequented place, discharges a firearm and with danger to persons or property, shall be punished with ten to forty-five days fine or with community service work of fifteen to forty-five days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 526 Presence of children and adolescents in places intended for

adults

A penalty of ten to forty-five days fine, or community service work of fifteen to forty-five days of two hours per day, shall be imposed on whoever, having the duty to prevent it as owner or operator, administrator, security guard,

Law No. 641 tolerates the entry or presence of a child or adolescent in a place intended exclusively for the presence of adults.

###### Art. 527 Negligence in the supervision of persons with mental illness

The person in charge of a person declared to be in a state of interdiction or with evident lack of volitional and cognitive capacity who represents a danger to himself or others, who neglects his supervision or does not notify the authority when the person with mental illness escapes from his custody, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

#### TITLE II

OFFENSES AGAINST PUBLIC ORDER AND TRANQUILITY

##### CHAPTER I

DISOBEDIENCE TO AUTHORITY

###### Art. 528 Disobedience to authority

Whoever disobeys instructions, resolutions or recommendations of an authority, public official or public employee, in the exercise of their functions, shall be imposed a penalty of ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 529 Disobedience of auxiliaries in proceedings

Whoever, having been legally summoned and having the obligation to appear as a witness, expert or interpreter, unjustifiably fails to comply with the summons of the authority, shall be imposed fifteen to sixty days fine, or community service work of twenty to sixty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 530 Destruction of official seals

Whoever violates, tears off, destroys or in any other way renders useless the seals affixed by the authority for police, judicial or fiscal purposes, shall be imposed ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 531 Denial of assistance to authority, public official or public employee

Whoever does not provide the authority, public official or public employee with the assistance requested or does not provide the information requested or provides false information in the event of an earthquake, fire, flood, shipwreck or other calamity or disaster, being able to do so without serious detriment to himself, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to forty-five days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 532 Obstruction of authority, public official or employee

Whoever, without assaulting an authority, public official or employee or the person who Law No. 641 provides assistance at the request of the former by virtue of a legal obligation, obstructs or hinders in any way the performance of an act proper to their functions, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 533 Refusal to identify oneself

Whoever, when required or questioned by the authority in the exercise of their functions and within their scope of competence, refuses to present their identity card, passport or residence permit or refuses to give their name, occupation or profession, marital status, nationality, place of birth, domicile and other personal data, or gives false information, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

##### CAPÍTULO II

DISTURBANCES OF PUBLIC PEACE

###### Art. 534 Disturbance by noise

Whoever, using sound, electronic or acoustic means of any nature, such as loudspeakers, radios, sound systems, alarms, whistles, industrial machinery, plants or equipment of any nature and purpose, musical instruments and microphones, among others, whether on public roads, in premises, in populated centers, residential areas or popular housing of any kind, near hospitals, clinics, schools or colleges, public offices, among others; produces sounds at decibel levels higher than those established by the competent authority and the standards and recommendations issued by the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), and that cause harm to health or disturb the tranquility and daytime and nighttime rest of citizens, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day, and in addition the suspension, cancellation or closure of the activities that generate the noise or disturbance.

Activities such as mass evangelistic campaigns carried out outdoors in plazas, parks and streets shall require the corresponding authorization. Exempt are the activities of religious congregations within their temples, such as worship services, daytime congregational fasts and nighttime vigils. Likewise, exempt are those that have established acoustic protection systems that prevent the emission of sounds, music or noises, outside of premises duly adapted for such purposes and that have the corresponding authorization and within the permitted hours.

For purposes of this article, the following sound intensity scales shall be considered.

Law No. 641

a) For bedrooms in dwellings thirty decibels for continuous noise and forty-five for single noise events. During the night outside sound levels must not exceed forty-five decibels at one meter from house facades;

b) In schools, colleges and preschool centers the background sound level must not be higher than thirty-five decibels during classes;

c) In hospitals during the night it must not exceed forty decibels and during the day the guideline value indoors is thirty decibels; and

d) At ceremonies, festivals and recreational events the sound must be below one hundred ten decibels.

The decibel is the unit of measurement on a logarithmic scale used to express the intensity of a sound.

###### Art. 535 False call to police, firefighters or emergency services

Whoever by unjustified alarm or summons causes a dispatch of the police, a fire truck or an ambulance, shall be imposed a penalty of ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 536 Unjustified alarm to the community

Whoever unjustifiably alarms the community with news of a public or private calamity or misfortune, or raises the alarm of fire without reason to do so, shall be imposed a penalty of ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

##### CAPÍTULO III

SCANDALOUS ACTS IN PUBLIC FORM

###### Art. 537 Public scandal

Whoever causes scandal or disturbs the tranquility of persons shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 538 Improper sale of alcoholic beverages

The owner, manager or customer service personnel of any commercial establishment that serves, sells or facilitates the consumption of alcoholic beverages to minors under eighteen years of age shall be imposed ten to sixty days fine or community service work of ten to sixty days of two hours per day.

The same penalty shall apply to whoever sells for consumption on site, permits consumption or consumes alcoholic beverages at a place of sale or distribution of hydrocarbons or their derivatives.

Law No. 641

###### Art. 539 Harassment

Whoever harasses another person with importunities in fact, word or writing shall be imposed ten to fifteen days fine, or community service work of five to twenty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 540 Exhibitionism

Whoever shows themselves naked or exhibits their genital organs in public places shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

###### Art. 541 Sexual acts in public form

Whoever performs sexual acts in public form shall be imposed ten to twenty days fine, or community service work of ten to twenty days of two hours per day.

#### TÍTULO III

OFFENSES AGAINST PUBLIC OR COMMON SECURITY

##### CAPÍTULO I

TRAFFIC SECURITY

###### Art. 542 Omission in the placement of warning signs

Whoever omits to place the signs or notices ordered by law, regulations or authority to warn persons in a place of public transit, or removes said notices or signs, or turns off a light placed as a signal, shall be punished with fifteen to sixty days fine, or community service work of twenty to sixty days of two hours per day.

When the person who incurs in this conduct is the authority, official or public employee responsible for signage, the penalty shall be thirty to one hundred twenty days, without the possibility of substitution by community service work.

###### Art. 543 Disabling of traffic signals

Whoever alters, disables, removes, destroys, stains or in any way affects a traffic signal or sign intended to guide the circulation of vehicles or pedestrians or to warn of a danger, shall be punished with fifteen to sixty days fine, or community service work of twenty to sixty days of two hours per day, without prejudice to the other responsibilities that correspond to them.

###### Art. 544 Reckless crossing of public road

Whoever with risk to themselves or others recklessly crosses a street or highway shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine or community service work of ten to thirty days of two hours per day.

Law No. 641

##### CAPÍTULO II

SECURITY OF CONSTRUCTIONS AND BUILDINGS

###### Art. 545 Omission in the placement of construction or building signs

Whoever fails to place signs or notices ordered by law, regulations or authority, to indicate the risk of collapse or other threats to the safety of persons in buildings, yards, streets or lands of any nature, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service of ten to thirty sessions of two hours daily. The same penalty shall be imposed on whoever removes, conceals or destroys such notices or signs.

###### Art. 546 Negligence in the repair or demolition of a ruinous construction or work

Whoever, being obligated, omits or delays the repair or demolition of a ruinous construction or work, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service of ten to thirty sessions of two hours daily.

###### Art. 547 Omission of security measures in defense of persons

The person responsible for the construction or demolition of a work who fails to take adequate security measures in defense of persons or property, shall be punished with a penalty of ten to forty-five days fine, or community service of fifteen to forty-five sessions of two hours daily.

#### TÍTULO IV

OFFENSES AGAINST PROPERTY

##### CAPÍTULO ÚNICO

###### Art. 548 Theft, fraud or misappropriation of lesser amount

Whoever commits theft, fraud or misappropriation, fraud in the delivery of things, of an amount not exceeding the sum resulting from two minimum wages of the industrial sector, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service of ten to thirty sessions of two hours daily.

###### Art. 549 Minor customs fraud and smuggling

Whoever totally or partially evades payment of duties and taxes on the import or export of goods and merchandise whose value is greater than five thousand and less than one hundred thousand Central American pesos, whether customs fraud or smuggling, shall be punished with a fine equivalent to twice the value of the goods or merchandise imported or exported. Law No. 641

###### Art. 550 Minor tax fraud

Whoever totally or partially evades payment of a tax obligation of less than ten minimum wages of the industrial sector, shall be punished with a fine equivalent to twice the value defrauded or attempted to be defrauded.

###### Art. 551 Harmful entry to another's property

Anyone who crosses planted land or plantations on another's property without justified reason and causes any damage that does not constitute a crime, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service of ten to twenty sessions of two hours daily.

###### Art. 552 Minor damages

Whoever destroys, renders useless, causes to disappear or in any way damages movable or immovable property, totally or partially belonging to another, if the amount does not exceed two monthly minimum wages of the industrial sector, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine or community service of ten to thirty sessions of two hours daily.

The same penalty shall be imposed on the owner or person in charge of livestock or domestic animals who, through carelessness or negligence, causes damage to another's property in the amount indicated.

OFFENSES AGAINST THE ENVIRONMENT

###### Art. 553 Contamination of water resources and protected areas

Whoever throws garbage or waste of any nature into storm water channels, streams, rivers, lakes, lagoons, estuaries, ravines, beaches, seas or any other place not designated by the authority for that purpose, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine or community service of ten to thirty sessions of two hours daily, if the act does not constitute a crime. If the conduct is carried out in a protected area, one hundred to two hundred days fine and community service of one hundred to two hundred sessions of two hours daily shall be imposed.

###### Art. 554 Mistreatment of trees or shrubs

Whoever affixes signs, posters, leaflets to trees or shrubs located in public places, or applies paint or any substance not intended for their preservation or ornamentation, or in any other way mistreats them, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service of ten to thirty sessions of two hours daily.

##### CAPÍTULO ÚNICO

###### Art. 555 Throwing garbage and sewage in public places

Whoever throws, tosses or dumps plastic bags, papers, sewage or garbage of any kind on public roads, squares, parks or other places of public access, shall be punished with ten to thirty days fine, or community service of ten to thirty sessions of two hours daily.

The same penalty shall be incurred by whoever fails to place and maintain an adequate container for users to deposit garbage, in collective and selective public transport vehicles.

###### Art. 556 Graffiti

Whoever, without the owner's authorization, makes graffiti or posts posters or leaflets on walls, walls, doors or windows of public or private buildings, shall be punished with ten to twenty days fine, or community service of ten to twenty days of two hours daily.

If the acts described above are performed on property defined as cultural and historical heritage by the law on the matter, they shall be punished with twenty to sixty days fine, or community service of ten to thirty days of two hours daily.

###### Art. 557 Destruction of gardens

Whoever destroys or removes plants, flowers or ornamental objects or objects for public use in parks and public gardens, shall be punished with twenty to forty days fine, or community service of ten to thirty sessions of two hours daily.

If the conduct is carried out in a protected area, one hundred to two hundred days fine and community service of one hundred to two hundred sessions of two hours daily shall be imposed.

###### Art. 558 Disrespect and negligence in the provision of a public service

Twenty to fifty days fine and community service of twenty to fifty sessions of two hours daily shall be imposed on the authority, official or public employee who, in the exercise of his function or employment, or on the occasion thereof:

Law No. 641

b) Delays proceedings unjustifiably, or imposes or subordinates them to conditions not provided for;

c) Negligently informs about the requirements or conditions necessary to carry out a procedure; or

###### Art. 559 Omission of indication of drug dependence risk

Whoever, being legally obligated, fails to indicate on the labels of pharmaceutical products the risks of drug dependence that their use implies, shall be punished with one hundred to two hundred days fine and special disqualification from practicing a profession, trade or industry related to the activity for three to twelve months.

###### Art. 560 Possession of controlled substances in quantity greater than

authorized

Whoever has in stock dependency-producing medications in a quantity greater than that authorized by the Ministry of Health, shall be punished with one hundred to two hundred days fine, and special disqualification from practicing the profession, industry or trade related to the criminal activity for a period of three months to one year.

###### Art. 561 Minor possession of narcotics, psychotropic substances or controlled substances

Whoever is found in possession of or demonstrated to have custody of narcotics, psychotropic substances or other controlled substances, in quantities less than five grams of marijuana or one gram, in the case of cocaine or any other controlled substance, shall be sanctioned with seventy to one hundred days-fine and community service of thirty to sixty days of two hours daily.

###### Art. 562 Criterion for application of criminal infractions

The provisions contained in the articles of this Third Book shall be applied only when the act does not constitute a crime.

BOOK FOUR

ADDITIONAL, DEROGATORY, TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

##### CHAPTER I

ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS

###### Art. 563 Prior mediation in criminal infractions

To file an accusation for criminal infractions, the prior mediation procedure must be exhausted, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code, which may be carried out before lawyers and public notaries, public defenders, mediators, rural judicial facilitators, promoters or justice facilitators of civil society organizations, mediation centers, university and popular law offices, Human Rights organizations, and any institution or organism with capacity to intermediate between the parties in conflict.

Mediation in criminal infractions has a restorative purpose. The accused and the victim shall intervene in it and, when appropriate, other persons or members of the affected community, the latter as interested third parties, and they shall participate jointly in the resolution and follow-up of matters arising from the act.

The right to accuse in infractions shall be exercised indistinctly by the directly offended party, by any competent administrative authority by reason of subject matter, or in its absence, by a representative of the National Police.

Without prejudice to the legality and validity control that corresponds, the judicial authorities shall facilitate the application of mediation at any stage of the process, including the execution phase.

###### Art. 564 Exercise of criminal action by the victim in less serious crimes

Without prejudice to the power of the Public Prosecutor's Office to exercise criminal action ex officio in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code, in less serious crimes, the victim may exercise it directly before the competent court, without the need to exhaust the administrative process; when there is a detainee, the action may be exercised within forty-eight hours from the beginning of detention. In this case, the National Police and the Public Prosecutor's Office shall provide facilities to the victim or their representative to formulate the accusation.

Once the accusation is admitted, the judge shall remit a copy of it to the Public Prosecutor's Office, which may intervene at any moment of the process to assist in the action exercised by the victim of less serious crimes of public action.

###### Art. 565 Technical judge

Trials for crimes of domestic or Law No. 641 intrafamily violence, cattle rustling, extortive kidnapping and organized crime shall be conducted with a technical judge. This provision is also applicable to crimes contained in the following chapters: crimes against sexual freedom and integrity; money, property or asset laundering; crimes related to narcotics, psychotropic substances and other controlled substances; terrorism; bribery; influence peddling; embezzlement; misappropriation of public funds, fraud and extortion.

###### Art. 567 Transitory Provisions

The transitory regime of this Code shall be governed by the following rules:

1. Crimes and misdemeanors committed prior to the entry into force of this Code shall be judged in accordance with the Penal Code of 1974, the laws amending it and other special laws containing crimes and criminal misdemeanors.

Law No. 641

2. Once this Code enters into force, its provisions shall have retroactive effect, insofar as they favor the accused or sentenced person. Judges may proceed ex officio or at the request of a party to rectify sentences that have been issued before the entry into force of this Code, applying the most favorable provision.

3. For the determination of the most favorable law, one must take into account, in addition to the typical elements and the penalty that would correspond to the act, the aggravating or mitigating circumstances, generic or specific, the penalty corresponding to the concurrence of crimes and the causes of exclusion of criminal liability, if any. In addition, the penitentiary benefits that may correspond in each case shall be considered.

4. For the assessment of recidivism as an aggravating circumstance, those crimes provided for in the repealed Penal Code that harm or endanger the same legal interest shall be understood to be included within the same Title.

5. For the purposes of criminal proceedings, the penalty shall be homologated as follows:

a) Penalties more than correctional shall correspond to serious penalty;

b) Correctional penalties shall correspond to less serious penalty.

6. The denomination of the monthly minimum wage of the industrial sector, contained in this Code, corresponds to the amount equivalent to the monthly salary that appears in the job listing of the industrial sector in accordance with the law.

Modifications made to the monthly minimum wage of the industrial sector shall not be considered as a variation to the criminal type, for the purposes of the principle of retroactive application of the most favorable criminal law.

7. The statute of limitations for criminal action in cases pending in the courts at the time the new Penal Code enters into force shall be governed by the provisions of the Penal Code of 1974 and the Code of Criminal Procedure.

8. For the purposes of this Code, the crime of murder contemplated in

###### article 140 is equated to the heinous murder contained in article 135

of the Penal Code of 1974 that is repealed. Consequently, those accused of the crime of heinous murder according to the repealed Penal Code shall continue to be prosecuted for the crime of murder and those convicted of the same crime shall have their sentence reviewed in accordance with the penalty for murder in this Penal Code.

Law No. 641

9. Those accused or sentenced for the crime of infanticide established in article 136 of the repealed Code shall be tried for the crime of aggravated homicide under the circumstance contemplated in article 36 numeral 2 of this Code, and in cases of conviction for this crime, it must be reviewed in accordance with this provision.

10. The provisions of the Penal Code of 1974 and its amendments, and those of the Code of Criminal Procedure in which reference is made to serious insults, correspond to the crime of insults in accordance with this Code.

11. In those provisions in force that refer to the crime of contempt, it shall be understood to refer to the misdemeanor or crime that corresponds according to this Penal Code.
