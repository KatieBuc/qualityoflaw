LAW ON PREVENTION AND PROTECTION FROM DOMESTIC VIOLENCE VIOLENCE

## CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1

Purpose

This law aims to prevent and address violence against women, domestic violence and gender-based violence, to protect and contribute to the elimination of all forms of discrimination against women, the promotion of gender equality and empowerment of women, as well as to build the necessary mechanisms for providing support to victims of all forms of violence regulated by this law.

### Article 2

Scope

1. The provisions of this law are mandatory for all public and private institutions, individuals, civil society and other responsible professionals, to provide assistance, support, protection and preservation of the dignity of victims of all forms of violence, according to the provisions of this Law, other legislation in force in the Republic of Kosovo, as well as key international conventions and instruments directly applicable in the domestic legal system.

2. This law applies in the civil and administrative justice system, and where applicable, as an additional measure in the criminal justice system to complement protection from the forms of violence covered by this law.

1.1."Family Relationship" - relationship between persons who are or have been:

"Political Violence" - political violence means actions, behaviors and/or physical, psychological, sexual aggressions committed by a person or group of people, directly or through third parties, against women candidates, elected, appointed or in exercise of political-public function; or against her family, to shorten, suspend, prevent or limit the exercise of her duty or to incite or force her to carry out, against her will, an action or not to act, in the performance of duties or in the exercise of their rights;

1.7. "Gender"- means the socially constructed roles, behaviors, activities and attributes that a particular society considers appropriate for women and men;

1.8."Psychological violence"- includes continuous control, verbal abuse, isolation, humiliation or emotional abuse through cursing, insulting, blackmail, intimidation, following and continuous surveillance, concealment of information, disinformation and threatening behavior, degrading treatment, threats or similar forms of control or psychological pressure, putting a person in reasonable fear of the use of violence against him or persons in family relationship, threat to damage property or keeping under continuous emotional distress;

## CHAPTER II

FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES

### Article 4

Non-discrimination

1. Domestic violence, violence against women and gender-based violence is a violation of human rights and freedoms, and constitutes discrimination that restricts them from enjoying rights and freedoms on an equal basis with men.

2. The implementation of the provisions of this law by the parties, especially measures for the protection of victims, are ensured without discrimination on the basis of gender, race, color, religion, political or other opinion, national or social origin, belonging to a national minority, property, birth, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, health status, disability, marital status, migrant or refugee status or other status.

3. The measures, activities and services of this law shall be undertaken to implement the principle of equality, to eliminate stereotypes related to gender roles, prevention and protection from all forms of discrimination against women, victims of gender-based violence and victims of domestic violence.

### Article 5

Priority treatment

1. State institutions, legal persons performing public authorizations determined by law, officials, officers and other authorized persons performing work on behalf of the state, have the obligation to treat with priority all measures and activities to prevent any effect of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence.

2. The subjects from paragraph 1 of this article are obliged to undertake with priority all appropriate legislative, administrative, judicial and other measures, to prevent, protect, investigate, punish and ensure fair compensation for victims including priority division of joint property for victims of domestic violence. 3.Official persons in institutions conducting activities in the field of social protection, internal affairs, health, child protection and education, as well as competent courts in civil, administrative and criminal proceedings for domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against women are obliged to act urgently, with increased priority towards the interests and needs of the victim.

4. Priority treatment also implies increased care in undertaking necessary measures for risk assessment, by all relevant authorities, with the aim of preventing the repetition of violence.

### Article 6

Prohibition of victimization

Any action taken or omission with the purpose or without intent to punish or place in a less favorable position a person who has taken actions to protect themselves from domestic violence, gender-based violence and, respectively if the person has reported violence, has initiated a protection procedure, or has testified during the procedure or otherwise, has participated in a protection procedure according to the provisions of this law and international conventions, applicable in the internal legal system, is prohibited.

### Article 7

Appropriateness of actions

1.Public institutions which bear obligations under this law to undertake measures, activities and services for victims of gender-based violence against women and domestic violence must adapt them to the specific needs of vulnerable victims.

2. Measures, activities and services for prevention and protection of women from gender-based violence and domestic violence are provided in a manner that enables the empowerment of the potential and capacities of victims for independent organization of life in the future.

3.The victim and/or legal representative has the right to participate in the assessment of the situation and needs, to be informed about measures, activities and services for protection and to participate in the selection of services and measures for assistance, support and protection.

4.Without the consent of the victim, respectively their legal representative, measures, activities and services for protection cannot be undertaken, except when there is a direct risk to their life and body, including child victims of domestic violence.

### Article 8

Gender mainstreaming

Public institutions are obliged to include the gender perspective as a criterion which is integrated in all phases of the policy-making process, assessment of situations, implementation and evaluation of policies arising from this law.

Competence for implementation of the law

1.Competent for the implementation of this law are all state administration bodies, courts, prosecutors, police, social work centers, victim advocates, local self-government units and legal persons who perform public authorizations determined by law. 2.The subjects from paragraph 1 of this article, are obliged to ensure that the work within their competence is performed by professional persons, trained for the implementation of the work determined by this law.

### Article 12

National Strategy for prevention, protection and treatment from Domestic Violence, violence against women

1. The Government adopts the National Strategy for Prevention and Protection from Domestic Violence and the action plan.

2. The National Coordinator leads the drafting process and submits to the Government for adoption the National Strategy for Protection from Domestic Violence.

3. The national strategy defines the institutional duties and responsibilities for the implementation of policies aimed at prevention, protection, coordination, prosecution, resocialization and reintegration, all institutions are obliged to fulfill the duties and responsibilities arising from the action plan of the strategy.

4. The drafting of the National Strategy for Protection from Domestic Violence and the action plan is done in accordance with the requirements set out in this law and the Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence Against Women and Domestic Violence.

### Article 13

National Coordinator for protection from domestic violence, violence against women and gender-based violence

1. By decision of the Prime Minister, the minister responsible for justice is appointed as National Coordinator against domestic violence, violence against women and gender-based violence. 2. The National Coordinator for Protection from Domestic Violence, violence against women and gender-based violence is the highest institutional authority for coordination, co-ordination, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of policies and measures for the prevention and combating of all forms of violence covered by this Law, the Council of Europe Convention on preventing and combating violence against women and domestic violence and the National Strategy of the Republic of Kosovo.

3. The duties and responsibilities of the National Coordinator are as follows:

3.1. Leading the process of drafting and monitoring the implementation of the national strategy and the Action Plan for protection from Domestic Violence, violence against women;

3.2. Coordination, co-ordination, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of policies and measures for the prevention and combating of all forms of violence covered by this Law and the Council of Europe Convention on preventing and combating violence against women and domestic violence;

3.3. Chairing and representing the Inter-Ministerial Coordinating Group for protection from

Domestic violence, violence against women;

3.4. Is responsible for reporting before the government, the parliament at least once a year;

3.5. Leading the process of capacity building of coordinating mechanisms for protection from domestic violence and violence against women, in cooperation with the Agency for Gender Equality.

4. The duties and responsibilities as well as the organization and functioning of the Office of the National Coordinator for Protection from Domestic Violence and the functioning of the inter-ministerial coordinating group is regulated by a sub-legal act which is proposed by the ministry responsible for justice and approved by the Government. 3.2. The responsible party poses a direct and immediate threat to the safety, health and well-being of the protected party or of the person with whom the protected party has a family relationship, who must be protected by means of a protection order and,

3.3. The issuance of the emergency temporary protection order is necessary to ensure the health or well-being of the protected party or of the person with whom the protected party has a family relationship.

4. A request for an emergency temporary protection order may also be submitted to the Kosovo Police by NGOs that have reliable information about the violence committed and that are familiar with the victim.

### Neni 54

Violation of Protection Orders

1. Violation in whole or in part of the protection order, the emergency protection order or the emergency temporary protection order constitutes a criminal offense sanctioned according to the Criminal Code of the Republic of Kosovo.

2.Continuous repetition of the violation in whole or in part of the protection order, the emergency protection order or the emergency temporary protection order is considered an aggravating circumstance for the perpetrator of the offense.

3. Violation of the protection order, the emergency protection order or the emergency temporary protection order is prosecuted ex officio.

4. The Kosovo Police regularly monitors and implements protection orders issued by the competent authority, depending on the degree of risk in accordance with the ongoing assessment of the risk of repetition of violence.

5.The Kosovo Police executes protection orders issued by the competent authority.

### Neni 55

Prohibition of Application of Alternative Procedures

The application of alternative procedures in criminal and administrative proceedings for the resolution of cases of domestic violence, violence against women and gender-based violence is prohibited, but not limited to mediation and reconciliation.

### Neni 56

Orders Issued by Other Countries

1. A protection order issued by another state is registered and enters into force by the courts of Kosovo and is enforced by the respective institutions as if it were issued in accordance with this law.

2.The court performs the registration of the protection order issued by another state based on the following documents:

2.1. A certified copy of the protection order, and/or;

2.2. A statement signed by an official of the court of the other state from which the order was issued, through which it is verified that the issued order is still in force on the day of certification.

3. The court retains the right to keep a certified copy of the protection order for registration purposes.

4.The court is obliged to send a copy of the protection order to the police and all other relevant institutions within 24 hours.

### Article 57

Non-recognition of protection order issued by other states

1. Courts may decide not to recognize a protection order issued by another country in cases where:

1.1. At the time of submission of the request to the court of Kosovo, the protection order was not in full force in the state where it was issued, or

1.2. At the time of submission of the request to the courts of Kosovo, the term of the protection order has expired;

1.3. The protective measures included in the protection order issued by the other state are not provided for in the legislation in force of Kosovo.

### Article 58

Limitation of issuance of protection orders against persons incited to commit violence

1. With the exception of the existence of special circumstances which, according to the court, make the issuance of a protection order necessary, protection orders are not issued against children under 14 years of age, elderly persons in mental health conditions that limit the possibility of reasonable judgment, and persons with limited capacity who have been incited to commit violence.

2. In case of issuance of protection orders against the categories specified in paragraph 1 of this article, the Court takes into consideration their well-being and decides on:

2.1. Appointment of legal aid to represent them during court proceedings;

2.2. Appointment of guardianship and appropriate care from service providers according to the needs of the responsible party belonging to the groups mentioned in paragraph 1 of this article.

### Article 59

Relations of the victim and the protected party with employers

1.Victims of domestic violence and protected parties have the right to receive 15 calendar days of paid leave to address health, legal, residence relocation issues for themselves or for persons dependent on him/her.

2. Victims of domestic violence have the right to request up to one year of unpaid leave to address health problems that are a direct consequence of the violence experienced. 3.Employers have the right to request from their employees, victims of domestic violence, supporting documents for days spent addressing health, legal issues, relocation of residence, etc.

4. Victims are guaranteed retention of the workplace without pay for up to one year, only if the employee/victim of domestic violence has been employed at the workplace for not less than 12 months.

### Article 60

Protective Measures for Asylum Seekers, Immigrants and Foreign Workers Victims of domestic violence and against women

1. The provisions of this law apply without prejudice and equally to asylum seekers, immigrants and foreign workers, and members of their families who are in the territory of the Republic of Kosovo.

2. In case the protected party has obtained a residence permit or temporary residence or a request for granting asylum status in the territory of the Republic of Kosovo through the application of the responsible party, the competent authorities examine the application for residence permit of the protected party separately from the application or case of the responsible party.

### Article 61

Obligation to collect and register data

1. The relevant Ministry in the field of justice creates and maintains the electronic system for information management - registration of cases of domestic violence and violence against women which is supervised and maintained by the Office of the National Coordinator for Protection from Domestic Violence and Violence against Women within the Ministry responsible for the field of justice.

2. The National Coordinator for Protection from Domestic Violence and the Coordinating Group decides on the creation, modification, completion and expansion of the integrated data system. 3.All institutions that provide services in cases of domestic violence have the obligation to register data within 24 hours in the database for registration of cases of domestic violence in the Republic of Kosovo.

4. The relevant Ministry for the field of justice issues an administrative instruction which determines the manner of maintenance, security and use of the system and the issuance of public reports while ensuring confidentiality for the victim.

## CHAPTER V

TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

### Article 62

Implementation

1.Six (6) months after the entry into force of this law, the Government of the Republic of Kosovo issues the sub-legal acts arising from this law.

2. For cases for which court proceedings have commenced before the entry into force of this law, the provisions of Law No. 03/L-182 on Protection from Domestic Violence shall apply.

3.For all procedural matters that are not regulated by this Law, the provisions of the Law on Contentious Procedure apply and are applied accordingly.

### Article 63

Repeal

With the entry into force of this law, Law No. 03/L-182 on Protection from Domestic Violence is repealed.

### Article 64

Entry into force

This law enters into force fifteen (15) days after publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo.
