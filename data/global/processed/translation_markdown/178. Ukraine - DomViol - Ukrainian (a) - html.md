On Prevention and Counteraction to Domestic Violence

This Law defines the organizational and legal framework for preventing and counteracting domestic violence, the main directions for implementing state policy in the field of preventing and counteracting domestic violence, aimed at protecting the rights and interests of persons who have suffered from such violence.

1. In this Law, the terms are used in the following meaning:

- 1) child offender - a person who has not reached 18 years of age and has committed domestic violence in any form;
- 2) child who suffered from domestic violence (hereinafter - affected child) - a person who has not reached 18 years of age and has suffered domestic violence in any form or has become a witness (eyewitness) to such violence;
- 3) domestic violence - acts (actions or omissions) of physical, sexual, psychological or economic violence committed in the family or within the place of residence, or between relatives, or between former or current spouses, or between other persons who live together (lived together) as one family but are not (were not) in family relations or in marriage with each other, regardless of whether the person who committed domestic violence lives (lived) in the same place as the affected person, as well as threats to commit such acts;
- 4) economic violence - a form of domestic violence that includes intentional deprivation of housing, food, clothing, other property, funds or documents or the possibility to use them, leaving without care or custody, obstruction in receiving necessary services for treatment or rehabilitation, prohibition to work, coercion to work, prohibition to study and other offenses of an economic nature;
- 5) prevention of domestic violence - a system of measures carried out by executive authorities, local self-government bodies, enterprises, institutions and organizations, as well as citizens of Ukraine, foreigners and stateless persons who are in Ukraine on legal grounds, and aimed at raising public awareness of the forms, causes and consequences of domestic violence, forming an intolerant attitude towards a violent model of behavior in private relations, a caring attitude towards affected persons, especially affected children, eradicating discriminatory ideas about the social roles and responsibilities of women and men, as well as any customs and traditions based on them;
- 6) offender - a person who has committed domestic violence in any form;
- 7) restrictive order against an offender - a measure established by court order for temporary restriction of rights or imposition of obligations on a person who has committed domestic violence, aimed at ensuring the safety of the affected person;
- 8) person who suffered from domestic violence (hereinafter - affected person) - a person who has suffered domestic violence in any form;
- 9) risk assessment - assessment of the likelihood of continuation or repeated commission of domestic violence, the occurrence of serious or especially serious consequences of its commission, as well as the death of the affected person;
- 10) program for the offender - a set of measures formed on the basis of risk assessment results and aimed at changing the violent behavior of the offender, forming in them a new, non-aggressive psychological model of behavior in private relations, a responsible attitude towards their actions and their consequences, including child rearing, and eradicating discriminatory ideas about the social roles and responsibilities of women and men;
- 11) program for the affected person - a set of measures aimed at eliminating emotional dependence, lack of self-confidence and forming in the affected person the ability to defend their own dignity, protect their rights in private relations, including with the help of authorized state authorities, local self-government bodies;
- 12) counteraction to domestic violence - a system of measures carried out by executive authorities, local self-government bodies, enterprises, institutions and organizations, as well as citizens of Ukraine, foreigners and stateless persons who are in Ukraine on legal grounds, and aimed at stopping domestic violence, providing assistance and protection to the affected person, compensating them for the damage caused, as well as proper investigation of cases of domestic violence, bringing offenders to justice and changing their behavior;
- 13) preventive registration - implementation by authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine of organizational and practical measures to control the behavior of the offender in order to prevent repeated commission of domestic violence, to ensure compliance with temporary restrictions on their rights and fulfillment of obligations imposed on them in connection with the commission of domestic violence;
- 14) psychological violence - a form of domestic violence that includes verbal insults, threats, including against third parties, humiliation, harassment, intimidation, other acts aimed at restricting the expression of will of a person, control in the reproductive sphere, if such actions or omissions caused the affected person to fear for their safety or the safety of third parties, caused emotional insecurity, inability to defend themselves or caused harm to the mental health of the person;
- 15) sexual violence - a form of domestic violence that includes any acts of a sexual nature committed against an adult person without their consent or against a child regardless of their consent, or in the presence of a child, coercion to an act of a sexual nature with a third party, as well as other offenses against sexual freedom or sexual integrity of a person, including those committed against a child or in their presence;
- 16) urgent prohibition order against an offender - a special measure of counteraction to domestic violence, applied by authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine as a response to the fact of domestic violence and aimed at immediate cessation of domestic violence, elimination of danger to the life and health of affected persons and prevention of continuation or repeated commission of such violence;
- 17) physical violence - a form of domestic violence that includes slaps, blows, pushing, pinching, whipping, biting, as well as unlawful deprivation of liberty, beating, torture, infliction of bodily injuries of varying degrees of severity, leaving in danger, failure to provide assistance to a person who is in a life-threatening condition, causing death, commission of other violent offenses.

### Article 2. Legislation on prevention and counteraction to domestic violence

1. Legislation on prevention and counteraction to domestic violence consists of the Constitution of Ukraine, international treaties consented to be binding by the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine, this Law and other normative legal acts regarding prevention of violence.

### Article 3. Scope of Application of the Legislation on Prevention and Counteraction to Domestic Violence

1. The subject matter of this Law is legal relations arising in the process of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence.

2. The effect of the legislation on prevention and counteraction to domestic violence, regardless of the fact of cohabitation, extends to the following persons:

- 4) mother (father) or children of one of the spouses (former spouse) and the other of the spouses (former spouse);
- 5) persons who cohabit (cohabited) as one family but are not (were not) married to each other, their parents and children;
- 6) persons who have a common child (children);
- 7) parents (mother, father) and child (children);
- 8) grandfather (grandmother) and grandson (granddaughter);
- 9) great-grandfather (great-grandmother) and great-grandson (great-granddaughter);
- 10) stepfather (stepmother) and stepson (stepdaughter);
- 12) other relatives: uncle (aunt) and nephew (niece), first cousins, great-uncle (great-aunt) and great-nephew (great-niece);
- 13) children of spouses, former spouses, fiancés, persons who have a common child (children), who are not common or adopted;
- 14) guardians, custodians, their children and persons who are (were) under guardianship, custody;
- 15) foster parents, parent-educators, patronage educators, their children and foster children, ward children, children who reside (resided) in the family of a patronage educator.
  - 3. The effect of the legislation on prevention and counteraction to domestic violence also extends to other relatives, other persons who are connected by common household, have mutual rights and obligations, subject to cohabitation, as well as to entities that implement measures in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence.

### Article 4. Basic Principles of Prevention and Counteraction to Domestic Violence

1. Activities aimed at prevention and counteraction to domestic violence are based on the following principles:

- 1) guaranteeing affected persons safety and fundamental human and civil rights and freedoms, in particular the right to life, liberty and security of person, to respect for private and family life, to a fair trial, to legal aid, taking into account the case law of the European Court of Human Rights;

2) due attention to every fact of domestic violence during the implementation of measures in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence;

3) taking into account the disproportionate impact of domestic violence on women and men, children and adults, adherence to the principle of ensuring equal rights and opportunities for women and men during the implementation of measures in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence;

4) recognition of the public danger of domestic violence and ensuring intolerance towards any manifestations of domestic violence;

5) respect and impartial and caring attitude towards affected persons by entities that implement measures in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence, ensuring the priority of rights, legitimate interests and safety of affected persons during the implementation of measures in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence;

6) confidentiality of information about affected persons and persons who reported the commission of domestic violence;

7) voluntary receipt of assistance by affected persons, except for children and incapacitated persons;

8) taking into account the special needs and interests of affected persons, in particular persons with disabilities, pregnant women, children, incapacitated persons, elderly persons;

9) effective interaction of entities that implement measures in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence with public associations, non-governmental organizations, media and other interested persons.

2. In case the affected person is a child, any actions taken in relation to such child are based on the principles defined by the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, the Council of Europe Convention on the Protection of Children against Sexual Exploitation and Sexual Abuse, the European Convention on the Exercise of Children's Rights and legislative acts of Ukraine in the field of protection of children's rights.

3. Measures in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence are implemented without discrimination on any grounds.

4. Customs, religious beliefs, religion, traditions cannot be considered as justification for any forms of domestic violence provided for by this Law, or be grounds for releasing the offender from liability.

### Article 5. Main Directions of Implementation of State Policy in the Field of Prevention and Counteraction to Domestic Violence

1. State policy in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence is aimed at ensuring a comprehensive integrated approach to overcoming domestic violence, providing comprehensive assistance to affected persons and affirming the non-violent nature of private relations.

2. The main directions of implementation of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence are:

- 1) prevention of domestic violence;
- 2) effective response to facts of domestic violence by introducing a mechanism of interaction of entities that implement measures in the field of prevention and counteraction to domestic violence;
- 3) provision of assistance and protection to affected persons, ensuring compensation for harm caused by domestic violence;
- 4) proper investigation of facts of domestic violence, bringing offenders to liability provided for by law and changing their behavior.

### Article 6. Entities carrying out measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence

1. Entities carrying out measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence are:

- 1) specially authorized bodies in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 2) other bodies and institutions entrusted with functions of carrying out measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 3) general and specialized support services for victims;
- 4) citizens of Ukraine, foreigners and stateless persons who are in Ukraine on legal grounds.
  - 2. Specially authorized bodies in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence are:
- 1) the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 2) the central executive body that implements state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 3) the Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, local state administrations, including their structural subdivisions, whose powers include carrying out measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 4) village, settlement, city, district in cities (if established) councils, their executive bodies, whose powers include carrying out measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
  - 3. Other bodies and institutions entrusted with functions of carrying out measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence include:
- 2) authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine bodies;
- 3) education management bodies, educational institutions, establishments and organizations of the education system;
- 4) health care bodies, health care institutions and establishments;
- 5) centers for provision of free legal aid;

## Section II

ENTITIES CARRYING OUT MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF PREVENTION AND COUNTERACTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 7.

Powers of the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, and the central executive body that implements state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence

1. The powers of the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence include:

- 1) formation of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 2) regulatory and legal regulation in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 3) coordination of activities of entities carrying out measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 4) ensuring the development and approval of standard programs for victims, as well as methodological recommendations for their implementation;
- 5) ensuring the development and approval of standard programs for offenders, as well as methodological recommendations for their implementation, including taking into account the age, health status, gender of the offender;
- 6) providing methodological support to local state administrations and local self-government bodies in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 7) approval of standards for provision of social services to victims and methodology for determining the needs of territorial communities in establishing specialized support services for victims;
- 8) implementation of international cooperation in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
  - 2. The powers of the central executive body that implements state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence include:
- 1) implementation of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 2) ensuring the establishment and functioning of the Unified State Register of cases of domestic violence and gender-based violence, exercising the powers of the holder of this register;
- 3) ensuring the functioning of support services for victims and exercising control over their activities;
- 4) coordination of training of specialists representing entities carrying out measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 5) organization and conduct of nationwide sociological, legal, psychological and pedagogical and other studies on domestic violence, its causes and consequences;
- 6) collection, analysis and dissemination in accordance with legislation of information on domestic violence, improvement of the system of indicators in forms of state statistical reporting on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 7) ensuring the monitoring of the effectiveness of legislation on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, practice of its application, measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, provision of methodological and practical assistance to entities carrying out measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 8) preparation and publication of an annual report on the state of implementation of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 9) maintaining records of data on activities of general and specialized support services for victims at the national level.

### Article 8.

Powers of the Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, local state administrations and local self-government bodies in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence

1. The powers of the Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, regional, Kyiv and Sevastopol city state administrations in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence include:

- 1) ensuring the implementation of state policy in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence at the regional level, in particular by exercising constant control over the timeliness and appropriateness of measures taken in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence;
- 2) ensuring the development, approval and implementation of regional programs in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence;
- 3) ensuring coordination of activities of entities carrying out measures in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence, and their interaction at the regional level;
- 4) maintaining records on entities carrying out measures in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence at the regional level;
- 5) ensuring the training of specialists whose competence includes issues of preventing and combating domestic violence, including specialists who implement programs for offenders;
- 6) ensuring the preparation of methodological recommendations on organizing and ensuring activities and improving the qualifications of specialists whose competence includes issues of preventing and combating domestic violence;
- 7) ensuring the implementation of information and educational activities (including the preparation and dissemination of relevant materials) on the forms, causes and consequences of domestic violence, measures in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence, forming citizens' intolerant attitude towards violent behavior patterns in private relationships;
- 8) organizing and/or conducting regional sociological, psychological-pedagogical and other studies on the forms, causes and consequences of domestic violence;
- 9) monitoring the status of fulfillment by entities carrying out measures in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence of assigned tasks in the process of implementing state policy in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence at the regional level, providing them with methodological and practical assistance, clarifying problematic issues in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence and taking exhaustive measures to resolve them;
- 10) reporting to the central executive body that implements state policy in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this field in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence.
  - 2. The powers of district, district in the cities of Kyiv and Sevastopol state administrations and executive bodies of rural, settlement, city, district in cities (if they are established) councils in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence include:
- 1) ensuring the implementation of state policy in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence in the territory of the respective administrative-territorial unit;
- 2) ensuring the provision of social services in the territory of the respective administrative-territorial unit, including through social procurement in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence;
- 3) determining the need for establishing specialized support services for victims, ensuring their establishment and functioning, exercising control over their activities;
- 4) participation in the training of specialists whose competence includes issues of preventing and combating domestic violence, including specialists who implement programs for offenders;
- 5) receiving and reviewing applications and notifications of domestic violence, ensuring the application of measures to stop it, providing assistance to victims;
- 6) ensuring coordination of activities of entities carrying out measures in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence, and their interaction in the territory of the respective administrative-territorial unit;
- 7) informing victims about rights, measures and social services they can use;
- 8) collecting, analyzing and disseminating information on domestic violence in the territory of the respective administrative-territorial unit in accordance with the law;
- 9) reporting to the central executive body that implements state policy in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this field in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence;
- 10) exercising the powers of guardianship and custody authority.
  - 3. The specifics of exercising by district state administrations and executive bodies of rural, settlement, city (cities of district subordination) councils the powers provided for in part two of this Article shall be determined by the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine.

The specifics of exercising by executive bodies of district in city councils the powers provided for in part two of this Article shall be determined by the city council of the respective city.

4. The structural units of local state administrations whose powers include implementing measures in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence are structural units on family matters.

The executive bodies of rural, settlement, city, district in cities (if they are established) councils whose powers include implementing measures in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence are executive bodies on family matters.

### Article 9. Powers of guardianship and custody bodies, children's services in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence

1. The powers of guardianship and custody bodies in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence concerning a child and involving a child include:

- 1) protection of the rights and interests of the affected child, child-abuser, including by applying to court, representing the rights and interests of the child in court when considering matters related to the commission of acts of domestic violence, in particular regarding the issuance of a restraining order;
- 2) direct provision of assistance and protection to affected children, child-abusers who have the status of orphans and children deprived of parental care;
- 3) consideration, in the procedure established by the Family Code of Ukraine, of the issue of expediency of taking the child away or deprivation of parental rights concerning the child, if the child's abusers are parents (adoptive parents) or one of them;
- 4) consideration, in the procedure established by law, of issues regarding the expediency of taking the child away from a guardian (custodian), foster parents, parent-educators, regarding the release of a person from the duties of guardian (custodian) of the child, termination of the agreement on patronage over the child, cancellation of the decision on placing the child in a family-type children's home or in a foster family in case of commission of domestic violence concerning the child or involving the child;
- 5) granting consent to receiving social services by the affected child, child-abuser, if the parents, other legal representatives of the child are abusers or evade the protection of the rights and interests of the child;
- 6) granting consent to entering personal data about the child who reported the commission of violence or is an affected person into the Unified State Register of Cases of Domestic Violence and Gender-Based Violence, if the parents, other legal representatives of the child are abusers or evade the protection of the rights and interests of the child;
- 7) placement of the child in the family of a patronage educator in case of impossibility of the child's living with their parents, other legal representatives in connection with the commission of domestic violence concerning this child or involving them.
  - 2. The powers of children's services in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence concerning children include:
- 1) development and implementation of measures to protect the rights and legitimate interests of the affected child;
- 2) development and implementation of measures to protect the rights and legitimate interests of the child-abuser;
- 3) reception and consideration of applications and reports on domestic violence concerning children and involving children, including reports received by the call center on preventing and combating domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children;
- 4) informing the affected child, their parents, other legal representatives, if they are not abusers of the child, about the rights, measures and services they can use;
- 5) informing the child-abuser, their parents, other legal representatives about the rights of the child, measures and services they can use;
- 6) placement of the child in a center for social and psychological rehabilitation of children, shelter for children of the children's service, other institutions for children regardless of form of ownership and subordination, in which appropriate conditions for living, upbringing, education and rehabilitation of the child are created according to their needs, as well as exercising control over the conditions of their stay and providing assistance in case of impossibility of the child's living with their parents, other legal representatives in connection with the commission of domestic violence concerning this child or involving them;
- 7) conducting preventive work with parents, other legal representatives of the child on preventing domestic violence concerning children and involving children;
- 8) raising before executive authorities and local self-government bodies the issue of bringing to liability in accordance with the law of officials in case of non-performance or improper performance by them of duties during the detection of facts of domestic violence, work with the affected child, child-abuser;
- 9) interaction with other subjects implementing measures in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence, in accordance with Article 15 of this Law;
- 10) implementation of other measures provided by law in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence concerning children or involving children;
- 11) reporting to the central executive authority implementing state policy in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this field in the procedure determined by the central executive authority ensuring the formation of state policy in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence.
  - 3. The powers of guardianship and custody bodies in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence concerning incapacitated persons and persons with limited legal capacity include:
- 1) resolving the issue of receiving social services by the affected person who is an incapacitated person, in case a legal representative has not been appointed for such person or the legal representative is an abuser or evades the protection of the rights and interests of such person;
- 2) granting consent to entering personal data about the incapacitated person who reported the commission of violence or is an affected person into the Unified State Register of Cases of Domestic Violence and Gender-Based Violence, if the legal representative of such person is an abuser or evades the protection of the rights and interests of the incapacitated person;
- 3) placement of the affected person who is an incapacitated person in a social protection institution in case, if in connection with the commission of domestic violence the living of such person in the family or with a guardian poses a threat to their life and health;
- 4) taking measures to provide the incapacitated person who is an abuser with psychiatric assistance on a compulsory basis in case, if in connection with the commission of domestic violence the living of such person in the family or with a guardian poses a threat to the life and health of family members or the guardian;
- 5) raising before the court the issue of release in the procedure established by law from the powers of guardian or custodian in case of commission by them of domestic violence concerning an incapacitated person or a person with limited legal capacity;
- 6) protection of rights and representation of interests of the affected person who is an incapacitated person, in state authorities, local self-government bodies or in court (if necessary);
- 7) raising before executive authorities and local self-government bodies the issue of bringing in the procedure established by law to liability of officials in case of non-performance or improper performance by them of duties during the detection and work with affected persons who are incapacitated persons or persons with limited legal capacity;
- 8) implementation of other measures provided by law in the field of preventing and combating domestic violence concerning incapacitated persons and persons with limited legal capacity, or involving them.

### Article 10. Powers of authorized subdivisions of the National Police of Ukraine in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence

1. The powers of authorized subdivisions of the National Police of Ukraine in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence include:

- 1) detection of facts of domestic violence and timely response to them;
- 2) reception and consideration of applications and notifications about commission of domestic violence, including consideration of notifications received by the call center on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children, taking measures to stop it and providing assistance to victims taking into account the results of risk assessment in the procedure determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, jointly with the National Police of Ukraine;
- 3) informing victims about their rights, measures and social services they can use;
- 4) issuance of urgent restraining orders against perpetrators;
- 5) registration of perpetrators for preventive monitoring and conducting preventive work with them in the procedure determined by law;
- 6) exercising control over the execution by perpetrators of special measures to counteract domestic violence during the period of their validity;
- 7) cancellation of permits for the right to purchase, store, carry weapons and ammunition to their owners in case of commission of domestic violence by them, as well as seizure of weapons and ammunition in the procedure determined by law;
- 8) cooperation with other subjects implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, in accordance with Article 15 of this Law;
- 9) reporting to the central executive body implementing state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the procedure determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
  - 2. Authorized subdivisions of the National Police of Ukraine exercise powers in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence taking into account international standards of response of law enforcement bodies to cases of domestic violence and risk assessment.

Police officers may enter a person's dwelling without a reasoned court decision in urgent cases related to stopping an ongoing act of domestic violence, in case of immediate danger to the life or health of the victim.

### Article 11. Powers of educational authorities, educational institutions and establishments of the education system in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence

1. The powers of educational authorities in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence according to their competence include:

- 1) ensuring training of specialists of appropriate qualification and field of knowledge for the purpose of proper performance by them of functions on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 2) ensuring implementation in the educational process at all educational levels, including inclusion in curricula and plans, of issues of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 3) ensuring inclusion in educational and professional programs when establishing state standards of education of issues of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 4) participation in training (retraining, professional development) of specialists representing subjects implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 5) ensuring inclusion in educational and upbringing programs of issues of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 6) methodological support of educational institutions on issues of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 7) reporting to the central executive body implementing state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the procedure determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
  - 2. Educational institutions and establishments of the education system when implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence:
- 1) conduct educational work with participants of the educational process on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 2) notify no later than one day the children's service, authorized subdivisions of the National Police of Ukraine in case of detection of facts of domestic violence against children or receipt of relevant applications or notifications;
- 3) conduct informational and educational activities with participants of the educational process on issues of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, including concerning children and with participation of children, paying special attention to forming a caring attitude of students towards affected children, awareness of the need for immediate informing teachers about cases of domestic violence that became known to them, reporting such cases to the call center on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children;
- 4) organize the work of a practical psychologist and/or social pedagogue with affected children;
- 5) cooperate with other subjects implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, in accordance with Article 15 of this Law;
- 6) report to the central executive body implementing state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the procedure determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.

### Article 12. Powers of bodies, institutions and establishments of health care in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence

1. The powers of health care bodies in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence include:

- 1) development and approval of the standard for providing medical assistance to victims or persons who have likely suffered from domestic violence;
- 2) approval of the procedure for conducting and documenting the results of medical examination of victims;
- 3) methodological support of institutions and establishments of health care on issues of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence;
- 4) reporting to the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.
  - 2. Institutions and establishments of health care during the implementation of measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence:
- 1) notify authorized units of bodies of the National Police of Ukraine about the detection of injuries that could have occurred as a result of committing domestic violence, and in case of detection of injuries in a child - also the service for children's affairs;
- 2) in case of detection of bodily injuries, ensure in the established manner the conduct of medical examination of victims;
- 3) in case of detection of injuries of a sexual nature, refer victims for HIV testing;
- 4) provide medical assistance to victims taking into account individual needs;
- 5) inform victims about measures and social services they can use;
- 6) interact with other subjects that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence in accordance with Article 15 of this Law;
- 7) report to the central executive body that implements state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence on the results of exercising powers in this sphere in the manner determined by the central executive body that ensures the formation of state policy in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.

### Article 16. Unified State Register of Cases of Domestic Violence and Gender-Based Violence

1. The Unified State Register of Cases of Domestic Violence and Gender-Based Violence (hereinafter - the Register) is an automated information and communication system designed for the collection, registration, accumulation, storage, adaptation, modification, renewal, use, distribution (dissemination, implementation, transfer), depersonalization and destruction of data defined by this Law on cases of domestic violence and gender-based violence.

{Part one of Article 16 as amended in accordance with Law No. 2801-IX of 01.12.2022}

1) protection of vital interests of victims, including child victims;

2) prevention of repeated cases of domestic violence and gender-based violence;

3) ensuring the implementation of measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence and in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence;

4) provision of comprehensive and timely assistance to victims by entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence and in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence;

5) registration of cases of domestic violence and gender-based violence;

6) coordination of activities of entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence and in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence.

3. The Register contains information (separately for each case of violence) about:

- 1) the employee of an entity implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, or an entity implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence, who entered information about a case of violence into the Register or corrected it (date of entry; surname, first name, patronymic; position; contact telephone number; name and address of the relevant entity);
- 2) the person who reported the commission of violence (surname, first name, patronymic; day, month, year of birth; place of residence; contact telephone number) - with consent provided in the manner determined by part four of this Article;
- 3) the victim (surname, first name, patronymic; day, month, year of birth; gender; citizenship; place of residence; place of study and/or place of work; contact telephone number) - with consent provided in the manner determined by part four of this Article;
- 4) the perpetrator (surname, first name, patronymic; day, month, year of birth; gender; citizenship; place of residence; place of study and/or place of work; contact telephone number) and about the nature of the relationship between the perpetrator and the victim, including in accordance with Article 3 of this Law;
- 5) the case of domestic violence, gender-based violence (date of commission; place of commission; form of domestic violence; type of harm or suffering inflicted as a result of gender-based violence; brief description);
- 6) the needs of the victim;
- 7) entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, or entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence, in connection with the detection of a case of violence (name; powers; location and contact telephone number (except for specialized support services);
- 8) exhaustive list of measures taken in connection with the detection of a case of violence, and their result.
  - 4. Consent to the entry of personal data about the person who reported the commission of violence, or about the victim is provided by the relevant person in compliance with the requirements of the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection".

In case the specified person is a child, such consent is provided by their parents or other legal representatives, and if the parents (other legal representatives of the child) are perpetrators or evade the protection of the rights and interests of the child, - by the guardianship and trusteeship authority. In case the specified person is incapacitated, such consent is provided by their legal representative, and if their legal representative is a perpetrator or evades the protection of the rights and interests of the incapacitated person, - by the guardianship and trusteeship authority.

5. Personal data about the person who reported the commission of violence is stored in the Register for one year from the date of receipt of the relevant information about the commission of violence.

Personal data about the victim is stored in the Register for three years from the date of commission of domestic violence or gender-based violence.

Personal data about the perpetrator is stored in the Register:

- 1) in the absence of criminal proceedings against the perpetrator in connection with the commission of domestic violence, gender-based violence or other forms of violence against the victim, a court decision on the application of a restrictive order or the imposition of an administrative penalty, a court verdict on recognition of the perpetrator as guilty of committing domestic violence or gender-based violence or other forms of violence against the victim, that have entered into legal force, - for one year from the date of receipt of the relevant information about the commission of violence;
- 2) in case of an acquittal verdict of the court that has entered into legal force, - until the entry into force of the relevant court decision;
- 3) in case of initiation of criminal proceedings against the perpetrator in connection with the commission of domestic violence or gender-based violence or other forms of violence against the victim - during the period of investigation of criminal proceedings;
- 4) in case of adoption of a court decision that has entered into legal force, on the application of a restrictive order or the imposition of an administrative penalty - for three years from the date of entry into force of the relevant court decision;
- 5) in case of issuance of a court verdict that has entered into legal force, on recognition of the perpetrator as guilty of committing domestic violence or gender-based violence or other forms of violence against the victim with serving a sentence - for ten years from the date of expiration of the sentence;
- 6) in case of issuance of a court verdict that has entered into legal force, on recognition of the perpetrator as guilty of committing domestic violence or gender-based violence or other forms of violence against the victim without serving a sentence - for ten years from the date of entry into force of the relevant court decision.

In case of refutation of the fact of commission of violence, as well as after the expiration of the period of storage of personal data in the Register, such data shall be deleted or destroyed in the manner established by law.

6. Formation of the Register is carried out separately for each case of domestic violence, gender-based violence by entering the relevant information into it.

Information into the Register (except for information about the perpetrator) is entered by all entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, or entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence (except for citizens of Ukraine, foreigners and stateless persons who are in Ukraine on legal grounds).

Information about the perpetrator is entered into the Register:

- 1) by responsible structural units of local state administrations, whose powers include the implementation of measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence or in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence;
- 2) by responsible executive bodies of village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their creation) councils, whose powers include the implementation of measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence or in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence;
- 3) by authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine.

Information about the perpetrator is entered into the Register in case of reasonable suspicion of their commission of violence, in particular after confirmation of such fact by conducting a verification of the relevant information in accordance with the procedure for interaction of entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, and/or the procedure for interaction of entities implementing measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence, approved by the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine.

7. Access to the Register is granted:

- 1) to employees of specially authorized bodies in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, the specially authorized central executive body on ensuring equal rights and opportunities for women and men, whose official duties include prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, gender-based violence;
- 2) to authorized persons (coordinators) on ensuring equal rights and opportunities for women and men, prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence of local state administrations and village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their creation) councils;
- 3) to employees of responsible structural units of local state administrations and responsible executive bodies of village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their creation) councils on prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence;
- 4) to employees of authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine;
- 5) to employees of children's services - in respect of cases of violence in which the perpetrators or victims are children.

Access of relevant employees to the Register is carried out using a qualified electronic signature or an advanced electronic signature based on a qualified electronic signature certificate and compatible with the software of the Register, or using electronic identification means with a medium or high level of trust in accordance with the requirements of the Law of Ukraine "On Electronic Identification and Electronic Trust Services".

{Paragraph seven of part seven of Article 16 as amended by Law No. 2801-IX of 01.12.2022}

8. The holder of the Register is the central executive body that implements state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.

9. The administrator of the Register is a state enterprise designated by the central executive body that implements state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, which belongs to the sphere of its management, implements measures for the creation, implementation and support of the software of the Register, is responsible for its technical and technological support, preservation and protection of the Register data, implements technical and technological measures for granting, blocking and revoking access to the Register, organizes and conducts training on working with the Register.

10. The totality of information about individuals (personal data) contained in the Register is restricted access information. Processing of this information is carried out in compliance with the requirements of the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection".

11. The procedure for formation, maintenance and access to the Register is determined by the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine.

## Section III

PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 18. Tasks in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence

1. Tasks in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence include:

- 1) determining the state, causes and prerequisites of the spread of domestic violence;
- 2) raising the level of awareness of the population about forms, manifestations, causes and consequences of domestic violence;
- 3) promoting society's understanding of the nature of domestic violence, its disproportionate impact on women and men, particularly on persons with disabilities, pregnant women, children, incapacitated persons, elderly persons;
- 4) forming in society an intolerant attitude towards violent models of behavior, a caring attitude towards victims, especially child victims, awareness of domestic violence as a violation of human rights;
- 5) eradicating discriminatory notions about social roles and duties of women and men, as well as any customs and traditions based on them;
- 6) encouraging all members of society, especially men and boys, to actively contribute to the prevention of domestic violence.

### Article 19. Measures in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence

1. In fulfilling tasks in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence, subjects implementing measures in the sphere of prevention of and countering domestic violence ensure:

- 1) studying the situation and collecting sex-disaggregated statistical data on facts of domestic violence;
- 2) organizing and conducting sectoral and cross-sectoral research on the state, causes and prerequisites of the spread of domestic violence, the effectiveness of legislation in the sphere of prevention of and countering domestic violence and the practice of its application;
- 3) organizing and conducting among the population, including among children and youth, information campaigns on prevention of and countering domestic violence, explaining its forms, manifestations and consequences;
- 4) developing and implementing in educational institutions educational and upbringing programs on issues of prevention of and countering domestic violence, including with regard to children;
- 5) engaging media in conducting educational campaigns aimed at fulfilling tasks in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence, in particular forming a caring attitude towards victims, especially child victims, awareness of the need for immediate reporting of cases of domestic violence, in particular to the call center on prevention of and countering domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children;
- 6) organizing and conducting joint and specialized trainings and seminars for specialists who work in the sphere of prevention of and countering domestic violence, as well as for law enforcement officers and judges.
  - 2. Subjects implementing measures in the sphere of prevention of domestic violence take into account the nature of domestic violence, its disproportionate impact on women and men, particularly on persons with disabilities, pregnant women, children, incapacitated persons, elderly persons, the need for support and protection of victims, the consequences of domestic violence leading to violation of human rights.
  - 3. Citizens of Ukraine, foreigners and stateless persons who are in Ukraine on legal grounds, who became aware of the commission of domestic violence, in particular if children have become or may become victims, are obliged to immediately report this to district, district in the cities of Kyiv and Sevastopol state administrations, executive bodies of village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their establishment) councils, authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine or to the round-the-clock call center on prevention of and countering domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children.

## Section IV

PROVIDING ASSISTANCE AND PROTECTION TO VICTIMS

### Стаття 20. Надання допомоги та захисту постраждалим особам

1. Суб'єкти, що здійснюють заходи у сфері запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству, відповідно до компетенції забезпечують надання дієвої допомоги та захисту постраждалим особам з урахуванням основних засад запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству, визначених статтею 4 цього Закону.

2. Надання допомоги та захисту постраждалим особам здійснюється за такими напрямами:

- 1) надання постраждалим особам інформації про їхні права та можливості реалізації таких прав зрозумілою їм мовою або через перекладача чи залучену третю особу, яка володіє мовою, зрозумілою постраждалим особам;
- 2) забезпечення доступу до загальних та спеціалізованих служб підтримки постраждалих осіб для отримання соціальних послуг медичної, соціальної, психологічної допомоги;
- 3) надання у разі потреби тимчасового притулку для безпечного розміщення постраждалих осіб;
- 4) забезпечення постраждалим особам доступу до правосуддя та інших механізмів юридичного захисту, у тому числі шляхом надання безоплатної правничої допомоги у порядку, встановленому Законом України "Про безоплатну правничу допомогу";

{Пункт 4 частини другої статті 20 в редакції Закону № 3022-IX від 10.04.2023}

5) утворення цілодобового безоплатного кол-центру з питань запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству, насильству за ознакою статі та насильству стосовно дітей для забезпечення невідкладного реагування на випадки домашнього насильства, надання консультацій щодо всіх форм домашнього насильства, визначених статтею 1 цього Закону, абонентам анонімно або з належним дотриманням правового режиму інформації з обмеженим доступом.

3. Під час надання допомоги та захисту постраждалим особам враховуються вік, стан здоров'я, стать, релігійні переконання, етнічне походження, спеціальні потреби таких осіб.

4. Допомога постраждалим особам надається за місцем звернення.

5. Надання допомоги постраждалим особам не залежить від звернення таких осіб до правоохоронних органів чи суду, від їх участі у кримінальному або цивільному провадженні.

6. Питання надання допомоги постраждалим недієздатним особам вирішують їхні опікуни, а якщо опікуни є кривдниками постраждалих осіб або ухиляються від захисту їхніх прав та інтересів, - органи опіки та піклування.

7. Питання надання допомоги постраждалим дітям вирішують їхні батьки, інші законні представники дитини, а якщо батьки, інші законні представники дитини є кривдниками дитини або ухиляються від захисту прав та інтересів дитини, - органи опіки та піклування, у тому числі за зверненням родичів дитини (баби, діда, повнолітніх брата, сестри), мачухи або вітчима дитини, якщо вони не є кривдниками.

### Стаття 21. Права постраждалих осіб

1. Постраждала особа має право на:

- 1) дієвий, ефективний та невідкладний захист в усіх випадках домашнього насильства, недопущення повторних випадків домашнього насильства;
- 2) звернення особисто або через свого представника до суб'єктів, що здійснюють заходи у сфері запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству;
- 3) отримання повної та вичерпної інформації від суб'єктів, що здійснюють заходи у сфері запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству, про свої права і соціальні послуги, медичну, соціальну, психологічну допомогу, якими вона може скористатися;
- 4) безоплатне отримання відповідно до законодавства соціальних послуг, медичної, соціальної та психологічної допомоги відповідно до її потреб;
- 5) безоплатну правничу допомогу у порядку, встановленому Законом України "Про безоплатну правничу допомогу";

{Пункт 5 частини першої статті 21 в редакції Закону № 3022-IX від 10.04.2023}

6) повагу до честі та гідності, уважне та гуманне ставлення з боку суб'єктів, що здійснюють заходи у сфері запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству;

7) конфіденційність інформації особистого характеру, яка стала відома суб'єктам, що здійснюють заходи у сфері запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству, під час роботи з постраждалою особою, та захист персональних даних;

8) вибір спеціаліста за статтю (за можливості);

9) відшкодування кривдниками завданих матеріальних збитків і шкоди, заподіяної фізичному та психічному здоров'ю, у порядку, визначеному законодавством;

10) звернення до правоохоронних органів і суду з метою притягнення кривдників до відповідальності, застосування до них спеціальних заходів щодо протидії домашньому насильству;

11) своєчасне отримання інформації про остаточні рішення суду та процесуальні рішення правоохоронних органів, пов'язані з розглядом факту вчинення стосовно неї домашнього насильства, у тому числі пов'язані з ізоляцією кривдника або його звільненням;

12) інші права, передбачені законодавством у сфері запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству, а також міжнародними договорами, згоду на обов'язковість яких надано Верховною Радою України.

### Стаття 22. Права постраждалої дитини

1. Постраждала дитина має всі права постраждалої особи, реалізація яких забезпечується з урахуванням найкращих інтересів дитини, її віку, статі, стану здоров'я, інтелектуального та фізичного розвитку.

Звернення та повідомлення про вчинення домашнього насильства стосовно дітей приймаються і розглядаються згідно з порядком, затвердженим Кабінетом Міністрів України.

2. Якщо у зв'язку із вчиненням домашнього насильства стосовно дитини вона не може проживати із своїми батьками, іншими законними представниками, на час подолання причин і наслідків домашнього насильства дитина може бути влаштована до родичів, у сім'ю патронатного вихователя, до центру соціально-психологічної реабілітації дітей, притулку для дітей служб у справах дітей, інших установ для дітей незалежно від форми власності та підпорядкування, в яких створені належні умови для проживання, виховання, навчання та реабілітації дитини відповідно до її потреб.

3. Захист прав та інтересів постраждалої дитини, у тому числі звернення до суду, представництво її прав та інтересів у суді, крім батьків та інших законних представників дитини, можуть здійснювати родичі дитини (баба, дід, повнолітні брат, сестра), мачуха або вітчим дитини, якщо вони не є кривдниками дитини, а також орган опіки та піклування.

4. Під час розгляду судом та/або органом опіки та піклування спорів щодо участі одного з батьків у вихованні дитини, визначення місця проживання дитини, відібрання дитини, позбавлення та поновлення батьківських прав, побачення з дитиною матері, батька дитини, які позбавлені батьківських прав, відібрання дитини від особи, яка тримає її у себе не на законних підставах або не на основі рішення суду, обов'язково беруться до уваги факти вчинення домашнього насильства стосовно дитини або за її присутності.

### Стаття 23. Виконання програм для постраждалих осіб

1. Суб'єктами, відповідальними за виконання програм для постраждалих осіб, є місцеві державні адміністрації та органи місцевого самоврядування.

2. Суб'єкт, відповідальний за виконання програм для постраждалих осіб, організовує та забезпечує проходження такими особами зазначених програм за їхньою згодою.

3. Програми для постраждалих осіб виконуються фахівцями, які пройшли підготовку, перепідготовку, підвищення кваліфікації та представляють суб'єктів, що здійснюють заходи у сфері запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству, а також суб'єктами, визначеними статтею 17 цього Закону, в порядку, визначеному центральним органом виконавчої влади, що забезпечує формування державної політики у сфері запобігання та протидії домашньому насильству.

## Розділ V

СПЕЦІАЛЬНІ ЗАХОДИ ЩОДО ПРОТИДІЇ ДОМАШНЬОМУ НАСИЛЬСТВУ

### Стаття 24. Спеціальні заходи щодо протидії домашньому насильству

1. До спеціальних заходів щодо протидії домашньому насильству належать:

- 1) терміновий заборонний припис стосовно кривдника;
- 2) обмежувальний припис стосовно кривдника;
- 3) взяття на профілактичний облік кривдника та проведення з ним профілактичної роботи;
- 4) направлення кривдника на проходження програми для кривдників.

### Article 25. Urgent Restraining Order against the Perpetrator

1. An urgent restraining order is issued against the perpetrator by authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine in case of an immediate threat to the life or health of the victim with the aim of immediately stopping domestic violence, preventing its continuation or repeated commission.

2. An urgent restraining order may contain the following measures:

- 1) obligation to leave the place of residence (stay) of the victim;
- 2) prohibition to enter and stay at the place of residence (stay) of the victim;
- 3) prohibition to contact the victim in any way.
  - 3. When deciding on issuing an urgent restraining order, priority is given to the safety of the victim. This requirement also applies to the place of joint residence (stay) of the victim and the perpetrator regardless of their property rights to the respective residential premises.

Officers of the authorized unit of the National Police of Ukraine may apply police coercive measures in the manner established by law for eviction from residential premises of the perpetrator if the urgent restraining order provides for an obligation to leave the place of residence (stay) of the victim and the perpetrator refuses to leave it voluntarily.

4. An urgent restraining order is issued upon the application of the victim, as well as on the initiative of an officer of the authorized unit of the National Police of Ukraine based on the results of risk assessment.

5. An urgent restraining order is issued for a term of up to 10 days.

6. An urgent restraining order is served on the perpetrator, and its copy - on the victim or their representative.

7. The effect of an urgent restraining order ceases in case of application by a court of an administrative penalty in the form of administrative arrest or choosing a preventive measure in the form of detention in custody in criminal proceedings against the perpetrator.

8. The perpetrator against whom an urgent restraining order has been issued, according to which they must leave the place of joint residence (stay) with the victim, is obliged to inform the authorized unit of the National Police of Ukraine at the place of commission of domestic violence about the place of their temporary stay.

9. A person against whom an urgent restraining order has been issued may appeal it to a court in the general procedure provided for appealing decisions, actions or omissions of officers of authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine.

10. An urgent restraining order may not contain measures provided for in points 1 and 2 of part two of this Article if the perpetrator is a person who has not reached the age of eighteen on the day of issuing the order and has a common place of residence (stay) with the victim.

11. An urgent restraining order is issued in the procedure approved by the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine.

### Article 26. Restrictive Order against the Perpetrator

1. The right to apply to a court with an application for issuing a restrictive order against the perpetrator is held by:

- 1) the victim or their representative;
- 2) in case of commission of domestic violence against a child - parents or other legal representatives of the child, relatives of the child (grandmother, grandfather, adult brother, sister), stepmother or stepfather of the child, as well as the guardianship and custody authority;
- 3) in case of commission of domestic violence against an incapacitated person - guardian, guardianship and custody authority.
  - 2. A restrictive order determines one or several of the following measures of temporary restriction of the perpetrator's rights or imposition of obligations on them:
- 1) prohibition to stay at the place of joint residence (stay) with the victim;
- 2) removal of obstacles to the use of property that is an object of joint common property right or personal private property of the victim;
- 3) limitation of communication with the victim child;
- 4) prohibition to approach at a specified distance to the place of residence (stay), study, work, other places frequently visited by the victim;
- 5) prohibition to search personally and through third persons for the victim if they are of their own will in a place unknown to the perpetrator, to persecute them and to communicate with them in any way;
- 6) prohibition to conduct correspondence, telephone conversations with the victim or to contact them through other means of communication personally and through third persons.
  - 3. The decision on issuing a restrictive order or on refusal to issue a restrictive order is made based on risk assessment.
  - 4. A restrictive order is issued for a term from one to six months.
  - 5. Upon the application of persons specified in part one of this Article, based on risk assessment, a restrictive order may be extended by a court for a term not exceeding six months after the expiration of the term established by the court decision in accordance with part four of this Article.
  - 6. On issuing a restrictive order against the perpetrator, the judge within the time limit established by law informs the authorized units of the National Police of Ukraine at the place of residence (stay) of the victim for placing the perpetrator on preventive registration, as well as rayon, rayon in the cities of Kyiv and Sevastopol state administrations and executive bodies of village, settlement, city, rayon in cities (in case of their creation) councils at the place of residence (stay) of the victim.
  - 7. A restrictive order may not contain measures restricting the right of residence or stay of the perpetrator at the place of their permanent residence (stay) if the perpetrator is a person who has not reached the age of eighteen on the day of issuing such order.
  - 8. The procedure for issuing a restrictive order by a court is determined by the Civil Procedural Code of Ukraine.
  - 9. The victim may demand from the perpetrator compensation for their expenses for treatment, obtaining consultations or for rent of housing that they rent (rented) with the aim of preventing commission of domestic violence against them, as well as periodic expenses for their maintenance, maintenance of children or other family members who are (were) dependent on the perpetrator, in the procedure provided for by legislation.
  - 10. In case of initiation of criminal proceedings in connection with commission of domestic violence, the list of measures for temporary restriction of rights or imposition of obligations on a person who is suspected, accused of committing a criminal offense related to domestic violence, or recognized guilty of its commission, as well as the procedure for application of such measures are determined by the Criminal Code of Ukraine and the Criminal Procedural Code of Ukraine.

### Article 27. Placement on Preventive Registration of Perpetrators and Conducting Preventive Work with Them

1. The authorized unit of the National Police of Ukraine places the perpetrator on preventive registration from the moment of detection of the fact of commission of domestic violence by them for the term established by legislation and conducts preventive work with them.

2. Removal of the perpetrator from preventive registration is carried out by the authorized unit of the National Police of Ukraine that placed them on preventive registration, automatically after completion of the established term, unless otherwise provided by legislation.

3. The procedure for placement on preventive registration, conducting preventive work and removal from preventive registration of the perpetrator is approved by the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine.

### Стаття 28. Implementation of Programs for Offenders

1. The entities responsible for the implementation of programs for offenders are local state administrations and local self-government bodies.

2. The entity responsible for the implementation of programs for offenders organizes and ensures the completion of such programs by offenders.

3. The implementation of programs for offenders in relation to child offenders is carried out taking into account the age and psychological characteristics of children.

4. In order to prevent the repeated commission of domestic violence and ensure the implementation of the program for the offender, a child offender may be temporarily placed with relatives, in the family of a foster educator, or in an institution for children regardless of the form of ownership and subordination, where appropriate conditions are created for the child's residence, upbringing, education and rehabilitation in accordance with their needs.

5. The implementation of programs for offenders is ensured by specialists who have undergone appropriate training.

6. An offender may be referred by the court to complete a program for offenders for a period from three months to one year in cases provided for by law.

7. An offender must have the opportunity to attend a program for offenders on their own initiative on a voluntary basis.

8. In case of failure of the offender to appear for the completion of the program for offenders or evasion from completing the program without valid reasons, the entities responsible for the implementation of programs for offenders provide within three working days a written notification thereof to the authorized unit of the National Police of Ukraine for taking measures.

9. Holding an offender liable for failure to complete a program for offenders does not release them from the obligation to complete such a program.

10. In case of bringing an offender, including a child offender, to criminal liability, the court may impose on them the obligation to complete a probation program in accordance with point 4 of part two of Article 76 of the Criminal Code of Ukraine.

## Section VI

RESPONSIBILITY IN THE FIELD OF PREVENTION AND COUNTERACTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 29. Responsibility for Committing Domestic Violence

1. An offender who has violated the requirements of special measures to counteract domestic violence bears responsibility in accordance with the law.

### Article 30. Compensation for Material Damages and Moral Harm Caused as a Result of Committing Domestic Violence

1. For a person who has the right to compensation for material damages and moral harm caused as a result of domestic violence, moral harm is compensated regardless of the material damages subject to compensation and is not related to their amount.

2. The procedure for compensation for material damages and moral harm caused as a result of domestic violence is determined by the Civil Code of Ukraine and other legislative acts.

### Article 31. Responsibility of Officials for Non-Compliance with the Requirements of Legislation in the Field of Prevention and Counteraction of Domestic Violence

1. Officials and officers recognized as guilty of violating the requirements of legislation in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence bear responsibility in accordance with the law.

2. The victim and the offender have the right to appeal the decisions, actions or inaction of entities that implement measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, in the manner established by law.

## Section VII

FINANCIAL SUPPORT OF MEASURES IN THE FIELD OF PREVENTION AND COUNTERACTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 32. Financing of Costs Related to Prevention and Counteraction of Domestic Violence

1. Financial support of measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, provision of assistance to victims, as well as the creation and ensuring of the functioning of the Unified State Register of Cases of Domestic Violence and Gender-Based Violence is carried out at the expense of state and local budgets, funds of enterprises, institutions and organizations, trade unions, voluntary contributions of legal entities and individuals, and other sources not prohibited by law.

2. Financial support of the activities of entities that implement measures in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, of state and communal forms of ownership, is carried out at the expense of the relevant budget and other sources not prohibited by law.

3. The maintenance of specialized support services for victims that do not belong to state and communal forms of ownership, ensuring the development of their material base, and their provision of social services are carried out at the expense of funds of their founders and other funds not prohibited by law, including under the conditions of social procurement.

## Section VIII

CONTROL OVER COMPLIANCE WITH LEGISLATION IN THE FIELD OF PREVENTION AND COUNTERACTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

### Article 33. Control in the Field of Prevention and Counteraction of Domestic Violence

1. The Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine exercises parliamentary control in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence within the powers and in the manner determined by the Constitution of Ukraine.

2. Other state authorities exercise control in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence within the powers and in the manner determined by the Constitution and laws of Ukraine.

### Article 34. Public Control over Compliance with Legislation in the Field of Prevention and Counteraction of Domestic Violence

1. Public associations, members or authorized representatives of such associations, in compliance with the legal regime of information with limited access, may exercise public control over the implementation of measures and the realization of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence.

2. Reports based on the results of public control are brought to the attention of specially authorized bodies in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence and are taken into account by them when determining priorities and main directions of state policy in the field of prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, as well as during the development of relevant regional programs.

### Article 21-3. Provision of Assistance and Protection to Victims

Subjects that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence, in accordance with their competence, ensure the provision of assistance and protection to persons who have suffered from such violence.

The provision of assistance and protection to victims is carried out in the following areas: providing the victim with information about their rights and opportunities for their realization in a language understandable to them or through an interpreter or an involved third party who speaks a language understandable to the victim; ensuring victims' access to specialized support services for victims and receiving social services they provide; providing, if necessary, temporary shelter for safe accommodation of victims, especially women with children; ensuring victims' access to justice and other legal protection mechanisms, including by providing free legal aid in the manner established by the Law of Ukraine "On Free Legal Aid"; establishment of a round-the-clock free call center on prevention and counteraction of domestic violence, gender-based violence and violence against children to ensure immediate response by authorized units of the bodies of the National Police of Ukraine, other state authorities and local self-government bodies defined by this Law, to cases of gender-based violence, providing consultations on gender-based violence to subscribers anonymously or with due regard for confidentiality requirements and personal data protection; providing qualified consultations in case of commission of gender-based violence with compliance with the legal regime of information with restricted access.

Assistance to victims is provided at the place of application.

The provision of assistance to a victim does not depend on such person's application to law enforcement agencies or to the court and on their participation in criminal or civil proceedings.

### Article 21-4. Rights of Victims

Victims have the right to: effective, efficient and immediate protection in case of commission of violence, prevention of repeated cases of violence; personal application or through their representative to subjects that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence; receiving full and comprehensive information from subjects that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence regarding their rights, measures and social services they can use; free receipt in accordance with the law of medical, social and psychological assistance, other social services; free legal aid in the manner established by the Law of Ukraine "On Free Legal Aid"; respect for honor and dignity, attentive and humane treatment by subjects that implement measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence; confidentiality of information of a personal nature that became known during work with such persons; application to the court with a petition for issuing a restrictive order against the perpetrator in the manner prescribed by law; other rights provided by legislation in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence.

### Article 21-5. Restrictive Order Against the Perpetrator

A victim or their representative, and in case of commission of gender-based violence against a child - parents or other legal representatives of the child, relatives of the child (grandmother, grandfather, adult brother, sister), stepmother or stepfather of the child, as well as the guardianship and trusteeship authority have the right to apply to the court with a petition for issuing a restrictive order against the perpetrator.

A restrictive order establishes one or several of the following measures of temporary restriction of the perpetrator's rights or imposition of the following obligations on them: prohibition to stay at the place of joint residence (stay) with the victim; removal of obstacles to the use of property that is an object of joint common property or personal private property of the victim; restriction of communication with the victim child; prohibition to approach a specified distance to the place of residence (stay), study, work, other places frequently visited by the victim; prohibition personally and through third parties to search for the victim if they, by their own will, stay at a place unknown to the perpetrator, to pursue them and to communicate with them in any way; prohibition to conduct correspondence, telephone conversations with the victim or to contact them through other means of communication personally and through third parties.

A decision on issuing a restrictive order or on refusing to issue a restrictive order is made on the basis of risk assessment.

A restrictive order is issued for a period from one to six months.

Upon application by the persons defined in part one of this Article, on the basis of risk assessment, a restrictive order may be extended by the court for a period of no more than six months after the expiration of the period established by the court decision in accordance with part four of this Article.

On issuing a restrictive order against the perpetrator, the judge within the period established by law informs the authorized units of the bodies of the National Police of Ukraine at the place of residence (stay) of the victim for placing the perpetrator on preventive monitoring, as well as district, district in the cities of Kyiv and Sevastopol state administrations and executive bodies of village, settlement, city, district in cities (in case of their establishment) councils at the place of residence (stay) of the victim.

A restrictive order may not contain measures that restrict the right of residence or stay of the perpetrator at the place of their permanent residence (stay) if the perpetrator is a person who has not reached the age of eighteen on the day of issuing such order.

The procedure for issuing a restrictive order by the court is determined by the Civil Procedure Code of Ukraine.

A victim may demand from the perpetrator compensation for their expenses for treatment, receiving consultations or for rent of housing they rent (rented) in order to prevent commission of domestic violence against them, as well as periodic expenses for their maintenance, maintenance of children or other family members who are (were) supported by the perpetrator, in the manner prescribed by legislation.

In case of initiation of criminal proceedings in connection with commission of gender-based violence, the list of measures for temporary restriction of rights or imposition of obligations on a person who is suspected, accused of committing a criminal offense related to domestic violence, or found guilty of its commission, as well as the procedure for applying such measures are determined by the Criminal Code of Ukraine and the Criminal Procedure Code of Ukraine.

### Article 21-6. Registration of the Perpetrator for Preventive Monitoring and Conducting Preventive Work with Them

The procedure for placing on preventive monitoring, conducting preventive work and removing from preventive monitoring of the perpetrator is approved by the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Ukraine.

### Стаття 21-7. Interaction of Entities Implementing Measures in the Sphere of Prevention and Counteraction of Gender-Based Violence

The interaction of entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence provides for: mutual information no later than one day about detected facts of gender-based violence in compliance with the legal regime of information with restricted access; response to facts of commission of gender-based violence in accordance with competence and taking into account risk assessment threatening the victim; coordination of response measures to cases of gender-based violence and provision of effective assistance to victims, which are carried out by different entities; development and implementation in accordance with competence of programs for perpetrators; development of programs for prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence in accordance with competence; organization of implementation of measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence in accordance with competence; exchange of experience in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence; coordinated interdepartmental training of specialists who represent entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence; monitoring of compliance with requirements of legislation in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence by entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence, submission of proposals for improvement of legislation and practice of its application.

The procedure for interaction of entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence is approved by the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine.

Entities implementing measures in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence report to the specially authorized central executive authority on issues of ensuring equal rights and opportunities of women and men on the results of exercise of powers in the sphere of prevention and counteraction of gender-based violence in the manner determined by the specially authorized central executive authority on issues of ensuring equal rights and opportunities of women and men.
