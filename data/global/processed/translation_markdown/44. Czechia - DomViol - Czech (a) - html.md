  - (1) If the conduct of the party against whom the application is directed seriously threatens the life, health, freedom or human dignity of the applicant, the presiding judge may, by preliminary injunction, impose upon the party against whom the application is directed, in particular, the obligation to
    - a) temporarily vacate the flat or house jointly occupied with the applicant (hereinafter referred to as "joint dwelling"), as well as its immediate vicinity, or not to enter it,
    - b) refrain from meeting with the applicant and making contact with him or her.
  - (2) Exceptions arising from the fulfilment of obligations imposed by the preliminary injunction on the party against whom the application is directed shall be determined by the court, taking into account his or her legitimate interests.
  - (3) A preliminary injunction pursuant to paragraph 1 shall remain in force for one month from its enforceability. Where a decision on an application for a preliminary injunction pursuant to paragraph 1 was preceded by a decision of the Police of the Czech Republic pursuant to a special legal regulation33c), this period shall commence on the day following the day on which the period laid down in that special legal regulation expired. In the case of an application to extend the preliminary injunction pursuant to paragraph 4, the period shall not expire before the court has ruled on such extension.
  - (4) If proceedings on the merits have been initiated before the expiry of the period referred to in paragraph 3, the presiding judge may, on the applicant's application, decide to extend the duration of the preliminary injunction; he or she shall take into account the duration of the state of threat to the applicant pursuant to paragraph 1, the content and reasons of the application for the initiation of proceedings on the merits, the property or other circumstances of the parties, including ownership and other relations to the joint dwelling to which the preliminary injunction relates, and other relevant circumstances. However, the preliminary injunction shall expire at the latest upon the expiry of one year from the moment of its issuance.
  - (5) A decision on an application for a preliminary injunction pursuant to paragraph 1 is not conditional upon a prior decision of the Police of the Czech Republic pursuant to a special legal regulation.

13. After § 273a, a new § 273b is inserted, which, including the heading, reads as follows:

Enforcement of a decision on expulsion from joint dwelling and non-establishment of contacts with the entitled person

(1) If the court has, by way of a preliminary injunction, ordered the obligor to temporarily vacate the joint dwelling, as well as its immediate vicinity, or to refrain from meeting with a person designated by the court in the decision and from establishing contacts with that person (§ 76b), it shall also ensure the immediate enforcement of that decision.

(2) The enforcement of the decision shall be carried out by the court, in cooperation with the competent state authorities, expelling the obligor from the joint dwelling, taking away from him or her all keys to the joint dwelling which the obligor holds, and, where appropriate, prohibiting him or her from meeting with the designated person or otherwise contacting that person. At the same time, the court shall provide the obligor with the opportunity to immediately collect from the joint dwelling, at the time of enforcement of the decision, his or her personal valuables and documents, as well as items serving his or her personal needs; during the duration of the preliminary injunction pursuant to § 76b, the obligor shall also be allowed to collect items necessary for the performance of his or her business activity or profession.

(3) If the obligor is not present at the time of enforcement of the decision, the court shall, upon his or her request, provide him or her during the period referred to in § 76b(4) with the opportunity to collect from the joint dwelling the items referred to in paragraph 2. The court shall notify the obligor of this right by a notice left on the door of the joint dwelling.

(4) The court competent to enforce the decision referred to in paragraph 1 is the court which issued the preliminary injunction.". PART THREErepealed
