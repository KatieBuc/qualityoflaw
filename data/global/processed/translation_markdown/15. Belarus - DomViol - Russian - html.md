## CHAPTER 1

GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1. Basic terms and their definitions used in this Law

This Law uses the following basic terms and their definitions: close relatives – parents, children, adopters, adopted children, siblings, grandfather, grandmother, grandchildren, as well as spouse; actions of a psychological nature – impact on the psyche of a citizen of the Republic of Belarus, a foreign citizen and a stateless person (hereinafter, unless otherwise defined, – citizen) by means of threat, humiliation of honor and dignity, commission of other immoral actions that objectively give the citizen reason to fear for his safety or the safety of persons close to him; actions of a sexual nature – encroachment on sexual freedom or sexual inviolability; actions of a physical nature – infliction of bodily injury, pain, torment, battery; domestic violence – intentional unlawful or immoral actions of a physical, psychological or sexual nature by close relatives, former spouses, citizens having a common child (children), or other citizens who live (lived) together and maintain (maintained) a common household, in relation to each other, causing physical and (or) mental suffering; individual prevention of offenses – activities of subjects of prevention of offenses to provide corrective influence on a citizen in order to prevent the commission of offenses, carried out in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation; general prevention of offenses – activities of subjects of prevention of offenses to identify the causes of offenses and conditions contributing to their commission, and to take measures to eliminate them, influence social processes and phenomena in order to prevent unlawful behavior of citizens, carried out in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation; victim of domestic violence – a citizen to whom physical and (or) mental suffering has been caused as a result of domestic violence; offense – unlawful culpable action (inaction) for the commission of which criminal or administrative liability is provided; prevention of offenses – activities involving the application of measures of general and (or) individual prevention of offenses by subjects of prevention of offenses in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation; preventive measure – a set of actions of a subject of prevention of offenses, organized and carried out by it within the framework of general and (or) individual prevention of offenses.

Definitions of other terms are contained in separate articles of this Law.

### Article 2. Scope of this Law

This Law regulates public relations arising in the implementation of prevention of offenses, unless otherwise provided by part two of this article.

Prevention of offenses in the conduct of criminal and administrative proceedings, execution of punishment and other measures of criminal liability is carried out in accordance with other legislative acts.

Prevention of offenses committed by minors is carried out in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Belarus of May 31, 2003 No. 200-Z "On the Fundamentals of the System for the Prevention of Child Neglect and Offenses by Minors."

Citizens participate in activities for the prevention of offenses in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Belarus of June 26, 2003 No. 214-Z "On the Participation of Citizens in the Protection of Public Order," this Law and other acts of legislation.

### Article 3. Legal regulation of relations in the sphere of prevention of offenses

Relations in the sphere of prevention of offenses are regulated by legislation in the sphere of prevention of offenses, as well as by international treaties of the Republic of Belarus.

Legislation in the sphere of prevention of offenses is based on the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus and consists of this Law and other acts of legislation.

If an international treaty of the Republic of Belarus establishes rules other than those contained in this Law, the rules of the international treaty shall apply.

### Article 4. Basic principles of activities of subjects of prevention of offenses in the sphere of prevention of offenses

The activities of subjects of prevention of offenses in the sphere of prevention of offenses are based on the principles of: legality; humanism; planning and consistency; implementation of individual prevention of offenses taking into account the personality of the citizen; protection and observance of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of citizens, rights and legitimate interests of organizations; coordination of actions and operational interaction of subjects of prevention of offenses; responsibility of officials of subjects of prevention of offenses for violation of legislation in the sphere of prevention of offenses; preservation of traditional family values; respect for private life; inadmissibility of considering customs, beliefs, traditions as justification for offenses; priority of preventive measures over measures of liability.

### Article 5. Subjects of Prevention of Offenses

The subjects of prevention of offenses are: internal affairs bodies; prosecutor's offices; state security bodies; border service bodies; customs authorities; state protection body; bodies of the State Control Committee; bodies and divisions for emergency situations;

Armed Forces of the Republic of Belarus (hereinafter – Armed Forces), internal troops of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (hereinafter – internal troops), other state military formations and paramilitary organizations; local executive and administrative bodies;

Ministry of Health, structural subdivisions of regional executive committees, Minsk City Executive Committee, exercising state authority powers in the sphere of health care, health care management bodies of other republican state administration bodies and other organizations subordinate to the Government of the Republic of Belarus, and state health care organizations subordinate to them (hereinafter – health care management bodies); state health care organizations;

Ministry of Education, structural subdivisions of regional executive committees, Minsk City Executive Committee, city, district executive committees, local administrations of districts in cities, exercising state authority powers in the sphere of education (hereinafter – education management bodies); educational institutions;

Ministry of Labor and Social Protection, structural subdivisions of regional executive committees, Minsk City Executive Committee, city, district executive committees, exercising state authority powers in the sphere of labor, employment and social protection, structural subdivisions of local administrations of districts in cities, exercising state authority powers in the sphere of social protection (hereinafter – bodies for labor, employment and social protection); social service institutions;

Ministry of Justice, structural subdivisions of regional executive committees, Minsk City Executive Committee, exercising state authority powers in the sphere of justice;

State Inspectorate for Protection of Fauna and Flora under the President of the Republic of Belarus; legal entities assigned the functions of editorial offices of state mass media; councils of public law and order protection posts; voluntary people's guards; public associations and other organizations (hereinafter – other organizations).

### Article 6. Coordination of Activities for Prevention of Offenses

Coordination of activities for prevention of offenses within the limits of competence is carried out by the General Prosecutor and lower prosecutors of territorial and transport prosecutor's offices, including through organizing the work of coordination meetings on combating crime and corruption.

## CHAPTER 2

POWERS OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF BELARUS, STATE BODIES, OTHER ORGANIZATIONS IN THE SPHERE OF PREVENTION OF OFFENSES

### Article 7. Powers of the President of the Republic of Belarus in the Sphere of Prevention of Offenses

The President of the Republic of Belarus in the sphere of prevention of offenses: determines state policy; exercises control over the activities of subjects of prevention of offenses subordinate (accountable) to the President of the Republic of Belarus; exercises other powers in accordance with the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus, this Law and other legislative acts.

### Article 8. Powers of the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus in the Sphere of Prevention of Offenses

The Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus in the sphere of prevention of offenses: implements state policy; exercises control over the activities of subjects of prevention of offenses subordinate to the Government of the Republic of Belarus; develops jointly with the General Prosecutor's Office comprehensive plans to combat crime and corruption and submits them for approval to the republican coordination meeting on combating crime and corruption; exercises other powers in accordance with the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus, this Law, other laws and acts of the President of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 9. Powers of Subjects of Prevention of Offenses in the Sphere of Prevention of Offenses

Subjects of prevention of offenses in the sphere of prevention of offenses within the limits of their competence in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation: participate in the implementation of state policy; identify causes of offenses and conditions conducive to their commission, and take measures to eliminate them; develop and conduct preventive measures; participate in the implementation of comprehensive plans to combat crime and corruption and regional comprehensive plans for prevention of offenses; provide in accordance with legislation information on preventive measures being conducted and conducted; place in public places, buildings (premises) of organizations, state mass media, including those distributed using the global computer network Internet, on official websites of subjects of prevention of offenses information on the formation of law-abiding behavior, healthy lifestyle, skills to ensure personal and property security of citizens; conduct systematic monitoring of legislation and submit proposals for its improvement in the established manner; exercise other powers provided for by this Law and other acts of legislation.

Organizations in the sphere of prevention of offenses conduct preventive measures to prevent offenses among employees of these organizations, including for the purpose of ensuring safe working conditions and preventing emergency situations of natural and technogenic character (hereinafter – emergency situations) on territories and facilities belonging to (subordinate to) them, provide on the basis of requests from other subjects of prevention of offenses space on information stands for placing information on the formation of law-abiding behavior, healthy lifestyle, skills to ensure personal and property security of citizens, exercise other powers provided for by this Law and other acts of legislation.

Legal entities assigned the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, in the sphere of prevention of offenses within the limits of their competence in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation: promote the participation of citizens and organizations in activities for prevention of offenses; disseminate information on the activities of subjects of prevention of offenses, analytical and other materials on prevention of offenses, protection of rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of citizens, rights and legitimate interests of organizations, including demonstrating through specific examples the inevitability of punishment for committed crimes; carry out activities to place (disseminate) social advertising, and also inform citizens about forms and methods of self-protection from criminal encroachments; exercise other powers provided for by this Law and other acts of legislation.

## CHAPTER 3

GENERAL PREVENTION OF OFFENSES

### Article 10. Measures of General Prevention of Offenses

Measures of general prevention of offenses are: development and approval of regional comprehensive plans for prevention of offenses and conduct of preventive measures; legal education of citizens; submission of representations, issuance of instructions; other measures provided for by this Law and other legislative acts.

### Статья 11. Regional Comprehensive Plans for the Prevention of Offenses

Based on the provisions of this Law, comprehensive plans for combating crime and corruption, and proposals of subjects of prevention of offenses, regional comprehensive plans for the prevention of offenses are annually developed and approved by local executive and administrative bodies of regional and basic territorial levels, which are implemented by subjects of prevention of offenses within their competence in the territory of the respective administrative-territorial units.

Regional comprehensive plans for the prevention of offenses must ensure the implementation of preventive measures for the prevention of: crimes against the state and the order of exercise of power and administration; offenses creating conditions for corruption and corruption offenses; offenses against life and health, public order and public morality; offenses contributing to the occurrence of emergency situations and deaths from them; offenses against property and the order of exercise of economic activity; domestic violence; offenses committed by citizens in a state of alcoholic intoxication or in a state caused by the consumption of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, toxic or other intoxicating substances; offenses committed by citizens released from correctional institutions, open-type correctional institutions, detention houses, as well as citizens who have returned from special educational institutions and special treatment and educational institutions; other offenses.

The respective coordination meetings for combating crime and corruption coordinate regional comprehensive plans for the prevention of offenses before their approval by local executive and administrative bodies of regional and basic territorial levels, and also monitor the implementation of preventive measures provided for therein.

### Статья 12. Basic Preventive Measures for the Prevention of Crimes Against the State and the Order of Exercise of Power and Administration

Bodies of internal affairs, bodies of state security, bodies of the border service, the body of state protection, internal troops, other state military formations and paramilitary organizations, within their competence, conduct preventive measures to prevent the creation and activities of illegal armed formations, organized criminal groups and criminal organizations, to prevent extremist activity, organization of illegal migration and other crimes against the state and the order of exercise of power and administration.

The body of state protection, bodies of internal affairs, bodies of state security, the Armed Forces, bodies of the border service, bodies and subdivisions for emergency situations, internal troops, within their competence, conduct preventive measures to ensure the security of persons subject to state protection in places of their stay, to eliminate circumstances impeding the implementation of state protection, and to prevent other crimes against the state and the order of exercise of power and administration.

State bodies not specified in parts one and two of this Article, other state organizations, within their competence, conduct preventive measures for the prevention of crimes against the state and the order of exercise of power and administration.

### Статья 13. Basic Preventive Measures for the Prevention of Offenses Creating Conditions for Corruption and Corruption Offenses

Bodies of the prosecutor's office, bodies of internal affairs, bodies of state security, bodies of the State Control Committee, other state bodies, other state organizations, within their competence, take measures to identify and suppress offenses creating conditions for corruption and corruption offenses, to eliminate their consequences, as well as the prerequisites and causes of corruption.

Local executive and administrative bodies, other state bodies, other state organizations plan and conduct preventive measures to counter corruption, including exercising control over the selection and placement of personnel, systematic work on compliance with restrictions and special requirements aimed at ensuring financial control over state officials.

State bodies, other state organizations, in cooperation with legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, inform citizens about the results of combating corruption and promote rejection of manifestations of corruption.

### Article 14. Basic Preventive Measures for the Prevention of Offenses Against Life and Health, Public Order and Public Morality

Internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, border service bodies, customs authorities within their competence identify and eliminate causes and conditions contributing to illegal circulation of weapons, ammunition, explosives. These state bodies, in cooperation with legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, explain to citizens the necessity of voluntary surrender of illegally stored weapons, ammunition, explosives.

Internal affairs bodies, border service bodies, customs authorities within their competence identify owners of weapons who have been subject to administrative penalties for committing administrative offenses that constitute grounds for annulment of permits for storage and carrying of weapons.

Armed Forces, state security bodies, border service bodies, internal troops, other state military formations and paramilitary organizations within their competence conduct preventive measures to ensure the safety of storage of weapons, ammunition, explosives and other means of destruction.

Internal affairs bodies in cooperation with educational institutions, legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, explain to citizens the possible negative consequences of employment abroad without the participation of legal entities or individual entrepreneurs having a license to carry out activities related to employment outside the Republic of Belarus.

Internal affairs bodies, divisions and organizations of the Ministry of Transport and Communications, local executive and administrative bodies within their competence take organizational and other measures ensuring the safety of passenger transportation.

Internal affairs bodies in cooperation with educational institutions, legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, organize explanatory work on the prevention of knowingly false reports of danger.

Local executive and administrative bodies take measures to organize proper lighting of streets and yard territories of settlements.

Local executive and administrative bodies, upon proposal of internal affairs bodies, take measures to equip public places of mass presence of citizens, public places where offenses are most frequently committed, with video surveillance systems and emergency communication devices controlled by internal affairs bodies.

Local executive and administrative bodies take measures to ensure communication of route vehicles in the city of Minsk and regional centers with the intersection control center of the automated traffic management system.

Local executive and administrative bodies take organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access of citizens to vacant houses, buildings, structures, basements and attics of residential buildings, buildings and structures.

Internal affairs bodies in cooperation with organizations carrying out operation of housing stock and (or) providing housing and communal services, organize appropriate training of employees of organizations carrying out operation of housing stock and (or) providing housing and communal services to assist internal affairs bodies in maintaining public order in yard territories.

Internal affairs bodies in cooperation with legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, carry out informing citizens about methods and means of ensuring personal safety.

### Article 15. Basic Preventive Measures for the Prevention of Offenses Contributing to the Occurrence of Emergency Situations and Loss of Life from Them

Local executive and administrative bodies in cooperation with emergency situation bodies and divisions, health administration bodies, state health organizations, labor, employment and social protection bodies, social service institutions, organizations carrying out operation of housing stock and (or) providing housing and communal services, and educational institutions conduct preventive measures aimed at preventing fires and other emergency situations and loss of life from them, targeted inspections of the condition of residential premises of single and solitary living elderly citizens and disabled persons, large families and families in which children are in socially dangerous situation, organize inspections of the condition of residential premises of other categories of citizens in the course of fire safety inspections of residential buildings and take necessary measures to bring housing stock to fire-safe condition, conduct briefings on fire safety measures in everyday life, take measures in the established order to provide targeted social assistance to citizens to ensure safe living conditions.

Emergency situation bodies and divisions organize informing the population about the state of fire safety, provide assistance to republican state administration bodies, local executive and administrative bodies, other organizations in training citizens in fire safety measures in everyday life and at work, provide assistance to volunteer fire formations in organizing fire prevention work.

State supervision bodies in the field of industrial, nuclear and radiation safety, local executive and administrative bodies, other authorized state bodies and other organizations conduct preventive measures to ensure industrial, nuclear and radiation safety.

State energy supervision bodies conduct targeted inspections of the technical condition of electrical and heat-using installations, conditions of their operation in residential premises of citizens, take measures to identify and eliminate causes and conditions contributing to violation of rules for installation and technical operation of electrical and heat-using installations, safety rules during their operation, rules for use of electrical and thermal energy by consumers of electrical and thermal energy.

State gas supervision bodies conduct targeted inspections of the technical condition of gas-using equipment (installations) of gas consumers, conditions of their operation in residential premises of citizens, take measures to identify and eliminate causes and conditions contributing to violation of rules for installation and technical operation of gas-using equipment (installations), safety rules during their operation by gas consumers.

State bodies and other organizations carrying out activities for the development and implementation of fire-resistant materials, within their competence take measures for the development and implementation of fire-resistant materials in construction and everyday life.

### Article 16. Basic Preventive Measures for the Prevention of Offenses Against Property and the Procedure for Carrying Out Economic Activities

Internal affairs bodies identify the causes and conditions contributing to the commission of thefts of property of citizens and organizations, within their competence take measures to eliminate these causes and conditions, and ensure the safety of commodity and material assets.

Bodies of the State Control Committee, other state bodies, other state organizations conducting inspections of financial and economic activities of organizations and individual entrepreneurs, conduct in the established procedure inspections of the expenditure of budgetary funds, as well as preventive measures to prevent offenses in the real sector of the economy and the social sphere.

Bodies of the State Control Committee take measures to improve the methodology for conducting inspections of financial and economic activities of organizations and individual entrepreneurs.

Internal affairs bodies, in cooperation with organizations operating the housing stock and (or) providing housing and communal services, legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, carry out informing of organizations and citizens about the necessity of equipping premises with security and safety means, entrance doors to building entrances with intercoms, code locks and video surveillance means.

Local executive and administrative bodies take measures for the construction of guarded vehicle parking lots and the creation of garage construction cooperatives, the equipping and maintenance in technically sound condition of electrical lighting systems in garage complexes and at vehicle parking lots, the allocation in residential complexes during their design and construction of places for garages and parking lots for citizens' vehicles at the rate of one vehicle per one apartment.

### Article 17. Main Preventive Measures for the Prevention of Domestic Violence

Local executive and administrative bodies, internal affairs bodies, prosecutor's offices, bodies for labor, employment and social protection, social service institutions, health care management bodies, state health care organizations, education management bodies, educational institutions in the sphere of preventing domestic violence within their competence: take measures to form a negative attitude of society towards domestic violence, to identify and eliminate the causes and conditions contributing to it; conduct information and educational work on the prevention of domestic violence; ensure training, retraining and advanced training of specialists of subjects of crime prevention, including in the field of interdepartmental cooperation; organize the implementation (implement) of educational programs of training courses (lectures, thematic seminars, practical classes, trainings, officer courses and other types of training courses) for specialists of subjects of crime prevention; inform victims of domestic violence about organizations where they can be provided with assistance; conduct sectoral and intersectoral studies of the state, causes and conditions of the spread of domestic violence, the effectiveness of legislation and the practice of its application; provide assistance in conducting a free "hotline" on the issue of counseling victims of domestic violence. Local executive and administrative bodies in the sphere of preventing domestic violence: ensure the planning and systematic nature of preventive measures to prevent domestic violence, take measures to study and comprehensively respond to citizens' requests taking into account local characteristics; create interdepartmental councils to assist victims of domestic violence in the relevant territory, which determine the procedure for implementing a set of measures to assist victims of domestic violence within the framework of interdepartmental cooperation protocols in accordance with the model regulation on the interdepartmental council for assistance to victims of domestic violence, approved by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus, and the model form of the interdepartmental cooperation protocol established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus; if necessary, protect the rights of a victim of domestic violence recognized by the court as incapacitated or with limited capacity, and represent his interests in state bodies or in court; promote the development of cooperation with other organizations and citizens in activities to prevent domestic violence; make decisions on the establishment of state organizations, their structural subdivisions for the round-the-clock provision of temporary shelter services to victims of domestic violence, provision of social, psychological and other assistance to them, and also take, if necessary, in the manner established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus, measures to provide temporary accommodation for citizens against whom a protective order has been applied; organize within their competence the conduct of correctional programs; carry out accounting, generalization, systematization and analysis of data on the provision of assistance to victims of domestic violence, on measures taken against citizens who have committed domestic violence.

Internal affairs bodies in the sphere of preventing domestic violence: receive and consider applications and reports on the commission of domestic violence, take measures to suppress it, prepare materials for making decisions in the established manner to bring citizens who have committed domestic violence to administrative or criminal liability, to recognize them as having limited capacity; organize preventive measures to prevent domestic violence; inform guardianship and trusteeship bodies about the identification of facts of domestic violence against minors, citizens recognized by the court as incapacitated or with limited capacity; organize within their competence the conduct of correctional programs; carry out accounting, generalization, systematization and analysis of information on facts of domestic violence.

Prosecutor's offices in the sphere of preventing domestic violence: participate in the conduct of preventive measures to prevent domestic violence; inform guardianship and trusteeship bodies about the identification of facts of domestic violence against minors, citizens recognized by the court as incapacitated or with limited capacity.

Bodies for labor, employment and social protection in the sphere of preventing domestic violence provide methodological support for the activities of social service institutions on issues of preventing domestic violence and providing assistance to victims of domestic violence.

Social service institutions in the sphere of preventing domestic violence within their competence provide social services to victims of domestic violence in the manner and under the conditions provided for by legislation, assistance in obtaining social assistance, social guarantees and benefits, other types of assistance provided for by legislation.

Health care management bodies in the sphere of preventing domestic violence provide methodological support for the activities of state health care organizations on issues of preventing domestic violence and providing assistance to victims of domestic violence.

Education management bodies in the sphere of preventing domestic violence provide methodological support for the activities of educational institutions on issues of preventing domestic violence and providing assistance to minors.

Bodies for labor, employment and social protection, social service institutions, health care management bodies, state health care organizations, education management bodies, educational institutions in the sphere of preventing domestic violence within their competence: inform internal affairs bodies and guardianship and trusteeship bodies about the identification of circumstances indicating domestic violence committed primarily against minors, citizens recognized by the court as incapacitated or with limited capacity; participate in the conduct of correctional programs, preventive measures to prevent domestic violence; carry out accounting, generalization, systematization and analysis of data on the provision of assistance or social services to victims of domestic violence.

Other organizations in the sphere of preventing domestic violence have the right in accordance with legislation and their charters: to participate in the conduct of correctional programs, preventive measures to prevent domestic violence; to provide temporary shelter services to victims of domestic violence; to provide social, psychological, legal and other assistance to victims of domestic violence; to organize a free "hotline" on the issue of counseling victims of domestic violence; to conduct other preventive measures to prevent domestic violence.

### Article 18.

Basic preventive measures for the prevention of offenses committed by citizens under the influence of alcohol or in a state caused by the consumption of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, toxic or other intoxicating substances

Internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, border service bodies, customs authorities, state healthcare organizations, within their competence, identify the causes and conditions conducive to the illegal circulation of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their precursors and analogues. These state bodies and state organizations, in cooperation with legal entities entrusted with the functions of editorial offices of state mass media, explain to citizens the need for voluntary surrender of illegally stored narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their precursors and analogues.

Local executive and administrative bodies, in cooperation with internal affairs bodies, healthcare management bodies, state healthcare organizations, education management bodies, educational institutions, and other organizations, ensure the conduct of information and educational work on preventing the distribution and consumption of alcoholic beverages, low-alcohol beverages and beer, and the consumption of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, toxic or other intoxicating substances.

Local executive and administrative bodies, internal affairs bodies, and educational institutions, within their competence, conduct preventive measures with students to prevent offenses committed under the influence of alcohol or in a state caused by the consumption of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, toxic or other intoxicating substances.

State healthcare organizations, in the established manner, ensure the provision of medical assistance to citizens suffering from mental and (or) behavioral disorders caused by alcohol consumption, consumption of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, toxic or other intoxicating substances.

Internal affairs bodies, state security bodies, border service bodies, and customs authorities, within their competence, identify places of illegal production and storage of alcoholic products, non-food alcohol-containing products, ethyl alcohol, narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their precursors and analogues.

Internal affairs bodies, within their competence, prepare materials and submit applications to court for sending citizens to medical-labor preventoria in the established manner, identify citizens consuming narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues, for sending them in the established manner to state healthcare organizations for the provision of medical assistance.

Local executive and administrative bodies, in the manner established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus, coordinate with internal affairs bodies the operating hours of trade facilities and public catering facilities that sell alcoholic beverages, as well as the sale of low-alcohol beverages and beer at night.

Bodies for labor, employment and social protection, in the manner established by legislation on employment of the population, provide assistance to citizens who have ceased their stay in medical-labor preventoria, as well as to citizens to whom compulsory safety and treatment measures were applied by court decision, in selecting work and employment.

### Article 19.

Basic preventive measures for the prevention of offenses committed by citizens released from correctional institutions, open-type correctional institutions, arrest houses, as well as citizens who returned from special educational institutions and special medical-educational institutions

Internal affairs bodies ensure control over the arrival of citizens released from correctional institutions, open-type correctional institutions, arrest houses, at their chosen place of residence.

Local executive and administrative bodies take measures to provide housing and employment for citizens released from correctional institutions, open-type correctional institutions, arrest houses, as well as citizens who returned from special educational institutions and special medical-educational institutions.

Local executive and administrative bodies, in the manner established by legislation, provide support to organizations and individual entrepreneurs that provide jobs for the employment of citizens released from correctional institutions, open-type correctional institutions, arrest houses.

Organizations and individual entrepreneurs that provide jobs for the employment of citizens released from correctional institutions have the right to partial compensation of labor costs for such persons at the expense of funds allocated for financing measures to ensure employment of the population, and other sources not prohibited by legislation.

The procedure for organizing and financing measures for the employment of citizens released from correctional institutions, including partial compensation of labor costs for such persons, is determined by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 20. Legal education of citizens

Legal education of citizens is the formation and enhancement of the level of legal consciousness and legal culture of citizens, carried out by subjects of offense prevention within their competence.

Legal education of citizens is carried out by: holding conferences, round tables, seminars, lectures and presentations on issues of offense prevention; posting in public places, buildings (premises) of organizations, state mass media, including those distributed using the global computer network Internet, on the official websites of subjects of offense prevention, information on the formation of law-abiding behavior, healthy lifestyles, skills for ensuring personal and property security of citizens; in other forms in accordance with legislative acts.

### Article 21. Representation on the Elimination of Causes and Conditions Contributing to the Commission of Offenses, and Instruction on the Elimination of Violations of Legislation

A representation on the elimination of causes and conditions contributing to the commission of offenses (hereinafter referred to as representation) is a written demand of an internal affairs body, prosecutor's office, state security body, border service body, customs body, state protection body, financial investigations body of the State Control Committee, emergency situations body and unit on the elimination of causes and conditions contributing to the commission of offenses.

A representation is submitted by the head of the offense prevention entity specified in part one of this Article, or by his deputy, to an official, organization or individual entrepreneur who are authorized to take measures to eliminate causes and conditions contributing to the commission of offenses.

A representation is subject to immediate consideration with the adoption of necessary measures to fulfill the requirements contained therein. The relevant organization, official, individual entrepreneur to whom the representation was submitted shall inform the offense prevention entity that submitted the representation in writing within one month from the date of its receipt about the measures taken.

When a representation is considered by a collegial body, the offense prevention entity that submitted it is notified in advance of the date, time and place of consideration of the representation. The offense prevention entity has the right to send its representative to participate in the consideration of the representation.

An instruction on the elimination of violations of legislation (hereinafter referred to as instruction) is a written demand of an internal affairs body, prosecutor's office, state security body, border service body, customs body, state protection body, financial investigations body of the State Control Committee, emergency situations body and unit on the immediate elimination of a violation of legislation that is of an obvious nature and may cause substantial harm to the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of citizens, including individual entrepreneurs, rights and legitimate interests of organizations, state or public interests, if it is not immediately eliminated.

An instruction is sent by the head of the offense prevention entity specified in part five of this Article, or by his deputy, to an official, organization or individual entrepreneur who committed the violation of legislation, or to a superior state body, official, other organization authorized to eliminate such violation. An instruction must contain an indication of the legislative act that was violated, the nature of the offense and specific proposals on measures to eliminate it.

An instruction is subject to immediate execution. The offense prevention entity that issued the instruction is notified without delay about the results of execution of the instruction.

When inspections are conducted by controlling (supervisory) bodies, instructions are issued by these bodies in accordance with legislative acts on control (supervisory) activities.

The offense prevention entities specified in parts one and five of this Article control the actual fulfillment of the requirements of representations submitted by them and instructions issued by them.

### Article 22. Other Measures of General Prevention of Offenses

Other measures of general prevention of offenses include: informing about preventive measures being conducted and conducted; organizing public opinion polls on the activities of offense prevention entities and covering their results in state mass media, including those distributed using the global computer network Internet, on the official websites of offense prevention entities; ensuring incentives for citizens for providing reliable information about offenses being prepared or committed; conducting criminological expertise of draft legal acts and legal acts in the manner established by the President of the Republic of Belarus; other measures provided for by legislative acts.

## CHAPTER 4

INDIVIDUAL PREVENTION OF OFFENSES

### Article 23. Measures of Individual Prevention of Offenses

Measures of individual prevention of offenses are: preventive conversation; official warning; preventive registration; protective instruction; correctional program; other measures provided for by legislative acts.

### Article 24. Preventive Conversation

A preventive conversation is an oral explanation to a citizen of the public danger of preparing and committing offenses, the legal consequences arising as a result of committing offenses, as well as persuading the citizen of the inadmissibility of committing them.

A preventive conversation is conducted with a citizen: released from a correctional institution, open-type correctional institution, arrest house and who has arrived at the chosen place of residence; whose behavior in public places, at the place of residence, work, study or lifestyle led to the commission of an offense or gives grounds to believe in the possibility of his commission of an offense.

### Article 25. Procedure for Conducting a Preventive Conversation

With a citizen released from a correctional institution, open-type correctional institution, arrest house, a preventive conversation is conducted by an official of the internal affairs body within ten days after the arrival of this citizen at the chosen place of residence.

A citizen released from a correctional institution, open-type correctional institution, arrest house and who has arrived at the chosen place of residence is summoned by the internal affairs body to conduct a preventive conversation to the premises of the internal affairs body. The summons is carried out in writing, by telephone message or telegram, SMS message, using facsimile communication, the global computer network Internet, including email, as well as other means of communication that ensure recording of the summons. A preventive conversation with the specified citizen may be conducted at his place of residence or place of stay, study, work or at another place determined by an official of the internal affairs body if this citizen did not appear upon summons.

A preventive conversation with a citizen whose behavior in public places, at the place of residence, work, study or lifestyle led to the commission of an offense or gives grounds to believe in the possibility of his commission of an offense is conducted by an official of the offense prevention entity.

When conducting a preventive conversation, an official of the offense prevention entity explains to the citizen his rights and obligations provided for by Article 32 of this Law.

The duration of conducting a preventive conversation is determined by an official of the offense prevention entity individually, as a rule, within one hour.

During the conduct of a preventive conversation, demonstration of visual materials and video clips may be carried out.

### Article 26. Official Warning

An official warning is a written explanation to a citizen about the inadmissibility of preparing or committing offenses for the purpose of preventing the commission of offenses by them.

An official warning is issued to a citizen in respect of whom information has been received about the commission of acts that may create a threat to national security, cause harm to state or public interests, rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of other citizens, or lead to the commission of a crime.

The court, the body conducting the administrative process, the criminal prosecution body shall within five days send to the state security bodies, border service bodies, customs bodies, taking into account their competence, the information provided for in part two of this article.

### Article 27. Procedure for Issuing and Announcing an Official Warning

The head of the internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs body or their deputy, an official of the subject of offense prevention considering a case of an administrative offense, within ten days after receiving information about the commission of acts that may create a threat to national security, cause harm to state or public interests, rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of other citizens, or lead to the commission of a crime, in respect of citizens specified in part two of Article 26 of this Law, shall issue an official warning to such citizens.

A citizen in respect of whom an official warning has been issued is summoned by an official of the internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs body to announce the official warning at the premises of the relevant subject of offense prevention. The summons is carried out in written form, by telephone message or telegram, SMS message, using facsimile communication, the global computer network Internet, including email, as well as other means of communication ensuring the recording of the summons. An official warning may be announced to the citizen in respect of whom it has been issued at their place of residence or place of stay, study, work or in another place determined by an official of the internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs body, if this citizen did not appear upon summons.

An official of the internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, customs body announces the official warning to the citizen in respect of whom it has been issued, hands them a copy of the official warning and explains their rights and obligations provided for in Article 32 of this Law. Upon receipt of a copy of the official warning and explanation of rights and obligations, a corresponding entry is made in the official warning by the citizen in respect of whom the official warning has been issued and by the official who handed over the copy of the official warning and explained the rights and obligations. In case the citizen in respect of whom an official warning has been issued refused to sign the official warning, the official who handed over the copy of the official warning makes an entry about this in the official warning.

The form of the official warning is established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 28. Preventive Registration

Preventive registration is the observation of the behavior of a citizen in respect of whom a decision has been made to implement preventive registration, for the purpose of preventing the preparation or commission of offenses on their part and exerting preventive influence on them.

Preventive registration is carried out in respect of a citizen: who has committed domestic violence and in connection with this has been brought to administrative responsibility for intentional infliction of bodily injury and other violent actions or violation of a protective order; who has committed domestic violence and in respect of whom in connection with this a check is being conducted in accordance with the requirements of criminal procedure legislation or a criminal case has been initiated concerning a crime against life and health, sexual inviolability or sexual freedom, or personal freedom, honor and dignity; who has fully served the primary and additional sentence, as well as in respect of whom the application of compulsory educational measures has been terminated in connection with the convict reaching the age of majority, with the exception of citizens over whom preventive supervision has been established or in respect of whom preventive observation is being carried out; in respect of whom information has been received about the commission of acts that may create a threat to national security, cause harm to state or public interests, rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of other citizens, or lead to the commission of a crime.

The court, bodies and institutions executing punishment and other measures of criminal responsibility shall send documents on release from serving punishment, on termination of application of compulsory educational measures, on payment of a fine by citizens specified in paragraph four of part two of this article, on the day of release of these citizens (ten days before their release – in case of release upon expiration of the term of punishment imposed by court sentence), termination of application of compulsory educational measures, payment of a fine to the internal affairs bodies at the place of residence chosen by them.

### Article 29. Procedure for Implementing Preventive Registration

Preventive registration is carried out in relation to citizens specified in: paragraphs two through four of part two of Article 28 of this Law – by internal affairs bodies; paragraph five of part two of Article 28 of this Law – by state security bodies, border service bodies, customs authorities in accordance with their competence.

The decision to implement preventive registration is made by the head of the offense prevention entity or their deputy within ten days from the moment the grounds specified in part two of Article 28 of this Law arise.

A citizen in respect of whom a decision to implement preventive registration has been made is summoned by an official of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, or customs authority to the premises of the relevant offense prevention entity for the purpose of handing over a copy of this decision. The summons is carried out in written form, by telephone message or telegram, SMS message, using facsimile communication, the global computer network Internet, including email, as well as other means of communication that ensure the recording of the summons.

An official of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, or customs authority announces the decision to implement preventive registration to the citizen in respect of whom it has been made, hands over to them a copy of this decision and explains their rights and obligations provided for by Article 32 of this Law. If the citizen did not appear upon summons, a copy of the specified decision may be handed over to this citizen at their place of residence or place of stay, study, work, or in another place determined by an official of an internal affairs body, state security body, border service body, or customs authority. A corresponding record of receipt of a copy of the decision to implement preventive registration and explanation of rights and obligations is made in this decision by the citizen in respect of whom it has been made and by the official who handed over the copy of the named decision and explained the rights and obligations. In the event that the citizen refused to sign the decision to implement preventive registration, the official who handed over its copy makes a record of this in the specified decision.

The decision to implement preventive registration enters into force: from the moment of handing over a copy of this decision to the citizen in respect of whom it has been made – upon the arising of grounds specified in paragraphs two, three and five of part two of Article 28 of this Law; from the moment of making such a decision – upon the arising of grounds specified in paragraph four of part two of Article 28 of this Law.

On the day the decision to implement preventive registration enters into force, a preventive case file is opened by the offense prevention entity. The preventive case file is maintained in electronic form, and in the absence of such a possibility – in printed form.

An official of an internal affairs body, border service body, or customs authority: visits the citizen in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out at their place of residence or place of stay, study, work, or in another place determined by an official of the relevant offense prevention entity, once per month, unless a different frequency of visits is determined by the head of the relevant offense prevention entity or their deputy; determines the place and time of conducting preventive measures, ensures the participation in them of the citizen in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out.

A citizen in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out appears upon summons by an official of an internal affairs body, border service body, or customs authority and participates in preventive measures. Preventive measures are conducted once per month, unless a different frequency is determined by the head of the relevant offense prevention entity or their deputy, in the form of lectures, demonstration of films of preventive orientation, including about the consideration of criminal cases, civil cases on recognition of citizens as having limited legal capacity, on removal of a child without deprivation of parental rights or on deprivation of parental rights, referral to a medical-labor dispensary, consideration of administrative offense cases, in other forms provided for by legislation on the prevention of offenses. Preventive measures are conducted at a time when the citizen in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out is not engaged in work or study. The duration of a preventive measure is determined by an official of an internal affairs body, border service body, or customs authority individually within the limit of one hour.

Preventive measures specified in parts seven and eight of this Article are not conducted during periods when citizens in respect of whom preventive registration is being carried out are held in custody, serving administrative arrest, staying in medical-labor dispensaries, traveling outside the Republic of Belarus, or receiving medical care in inpatient settings.

The procedure for conducting preventive measures with the participation of citizens in respect of whom preventive registration is carried out by state security bodies is determined by the State Security Committee.

The forms of decisions on implementation of preventive registration and on its termination, the form of a preventive case file are established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 30. Termination of Implementation of Preventive Registration

Implementation of preventive registration of citizens specified in paragraphs two and three of part two of Article 28 of this Law is terminated upon expiration of one year from the day of detection of the last instance of domestic violence.

Implementation of preventive registration of citizens specified in paragraph four of part two of Article 28 of this Law is terminated from the moment of expungement or removal of a criminal record.

Implementation of preventive registration of citizens specified in paragraph five of part two of Article 28 of this Law is terminated in the event of persons' refusal to commit acts that served as the basis for implementation of preventive registration, as well as due to elimination of a threat to national security, causing harm to state or public interests, rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of other citizens.

Implementation of preventive registration is terminated before the occurrence of the time periods specified in parts one and two of this Article in the event of: termination of an administrative offense case against a citizen that served as the basis for implementation of preventive registration; termination of preliminary investigation in a criminal case or criminal prosecution, or making a decision to terminate verification based on a statement or report about a crime, refusal to initiate a criminal case, which served as the basis for implementation of preventive registration; entry into legal force of a court sentence on conviction of a citizen to punishments specified in clauses 1, 3–10 of part 1 of Article 48 of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Belarus, or other measures of criminal liability in accordance with Articles 77–79 of the Criminal Code of the Republic of Belarus; conscription of a citizen for military service; expulsion or deportation of a citizen from the Republic of Belarus; death of a citizen.

A citizen in respect of whom a decision to terminate implementation of preventive registration has been made is notified of this in writing by an official of the relevant offense prevention entity within three days after making this decision, with the exception of cases specified in paragraphs four through seven of part four of this Article.

### Article 31. Protective Order

A protective order is the establishment of temporary prohibitions on performing certain actions and obligations for a citizen who has committed domestic violence, for the protection of the life and health of a victim of domestic violence.

A protective order may be applied to a citizen who has committed domestic violence and in respect of whom in connection therewith: preparation is being carried out for the consideration of a case on an administrative offense of intentional infliction of bodily injury and other violent actions or violation of a protective order; an investigation is being conducted in accordance with the requirements of criminal procedure legislation or a criminal case has been initiated regarding a crime against life and health, sexual integrity or sexual freedom, or personal freedom, honor and dignity.

By a protective order, a citizen in respect of whom it has been applied may be prohibited from: attempting to ascertain the whereabouts of the victim (victims) of domestic violence, if this victim (these victims) is (are) in a place unknown to the citizen who committed the domestic violence; visiting the locations of the victim (victims) of domestic violence, if this victim (these victims) is (are) temporarily located outside the common place of residence or place of stay; communicating with the victim (victims) of domestic violence, including by telephone, using the global computer network Internet; disposing of common joint property with the victim (victims) of domestic violence.

By a protective order, a citizen in respect of whom it has been applied may be imposed with an obligation to temporarily vacate the residential premises shared with the victim (victims) of domestic violence.

A protective order is applied or extended by an authorized official of the internal affairs body with the written consent of an adult victim (victims) of domestic violence, taking into account an assessment of the likelihood of continuation or repeated commission of domestic violence, the occurrence of serious or particularly serious consequences of its commission, including the death of the victim of domestic violence (hereinafter – the risk of domestic violence).

The procedure for assessing the risk of domestic violence is determined by the Ministry of Internal Affairs in agreement with the General Prosecutor's Office. In the absence of written consent of an adult victim (victims) of domestic violence, if this victim (these victims) is (are) dependent on the citizen who committed the domestic violence, or for other reasons is (are) unable to independently protect their rights and legitimate interests, and when there is a high level of risk of domestic violence, a protective order is applied by the head of the internal affairs body or their deputy in agreement with the relevant prosecutor.

A protective order is immediately announced to the citizen in respect of whom it has been applied, with the delivery of a copy of the protective order and an explanation of their rights and obligations provided for by Article 32 of this Law. A corresponding entry regarding the receipt of a copy of the protective order and explanation of rights and obligations is made in the protective order by this citizen and by the official who delivered the copy of the protective order and explained the rights and obligations. In the event that the citizen refused to sign the protective order, the official who delivered the copy of the protective order makes an entry to this effect in the protective order.

A protective order enters into force from the moment of its announcement to the citizen in respect of whom it has been applied. A citizen in respect of whom a protective order has been applied with the establishment of the obligation specified in part four of this Article is provided with the opportunity to take only personal belongings with them. The specified citizen is obliged to inform the official of the internal affairs body of their place of temporary stay, hand over the keys to the residential premises shared with the victim (victims) of domestic violence for transfer to the victim (one of the victims) of domestic violence.

Within the next working day from the moment of announcement of the protective order to the citizen in respect of whom it has been applied, copies of the protective order are sent (provided) to the adult victim (victims) of domestic violence, as well as to the relevant prosecutor, to the relevant local executive and administrative body for conducting, if necessary, preventive measures to prevent domestic violence in accordance with this Law and other acts of legislation.

The prohibitions and obligation specified in parts three and four of this Article are established for a period of up to fifteen days from the moment of announcement of the protective order to the citizen in respect of whom it has been applied.

A decision by the head of the internal affairs body or their deputy to extend the protective order for a period of up to thirty days is agreed upon with the relevant prosecutor. The decision to extend the protective order must set out the grounds and reasons for such extension. Within the next working day from the moment of agreement on the decision to extend the protective order, a copy thereof is sent (provided) to the citizen in respect of whom the protective order has been applied, to the place of temporary stay indicated by them, which constitutes proper notification, as well as to the adult victim (victims) of domestic violence, to the relevant local executive and administrative body.

Control over the compliance by the citizen in respect of whom a protective order has been applied with the established prohibitions and obligation, monitoring of their behavior at the place of temporary stay are ensured by the internal affairs body.

The effect of a protective order is terminated: upon expiration of the period for which it has been applied or extended; in the event of termination in respect of the citizen of the case on an administrative offense that served as the basis for the application of the protective order; in the event of termination of preliminary investigation in a criminal case or criminal prosecution, or adoption of a decision to terminate the investigation on an application or report of a crime, to refuse to initiate a criminal case, that served as the basis for the application of the protective order.

A decision to terminate a protective order may be made by the head of the internal affairs body or their deputy upon application of an adult victim (victims) of domestic violence.

The form of a protective order and the form of a decision to extend a protective order are established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 311. Correctional Program

A correctional program is a set of measures for providing psychological assistance to a citizen who has committed domestic violence, aimed at correcting (adjusting) the citizen's behavior, developing their skills in non-violent communication, and preventing the recurrence of domestic violence.

The basis for conducting a correctional program is a recommendation from an official of a crime prevention entity. The correctional program is conducted with the consent of the citizen who committed domestic violence.

The conduct of a correctional program is ensured in accordance with legislation in the field of providing psychological assistance and is carried out on a gratuitous basis.

Local executive and administrative bodies, medical-labor preventive facilities, institutions of the penitentiary system of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, and other state organizations, when carrying out activities to provide psychological assistance within their competence, determine the state organization, other organization, or psychologist responsible for conducting the correctional program, other crime prevention entities involved in providing psychological assistance, and inform the citizen who committed domestic violence thereof.

The state organization, other organization, or psychologist responsible for conducting the correctional program, after direct application by the citizen who committed domestic violence, shall draw up a document defining, taking into account the personality of this citizen and available information about their unlawful behavior, a set of measures for providing psychological assistance, the place, time, sequence and terms of their implementation, as well as the type, form and method of providing psychological assistance.

With the consent of the citizen who committed domestic violence and the adult victim of domestic violence, psychological assistance may be provided with the participation of the latter.

To improve the effectiveness of providing psychological assistance, internal affairs bodies and other crime prevention entities, within their competence, transfer to the state organization, other organization, or psychologist responsible for conducting the correctional program, with the consent of the citizen who committed domestic violence, information about offenses committed by them and other personal data thereof.

### Article 312. Consent for Transfer of Information about Domestic Violence

Consent for transfer of information about domestic violence is a free, unambiguous, informed expression of the will of a victim of domestic violence regarding the transfer of information about domestic violence committed against them and other personal data thereof, necessary for protecting the victim of domestic violence and providing them with assistance and social services provided by legislation by the relevant crime prevention entities.

Consent for transfer of information about domestic violence shall be sent, within the next business day from the moment of its receipt, by the entity conducting preventive measures to prevent domestic violence to crime prevention entities according to their competence.

The presence of consent for transfer of information about domestic violence or direct application by a victim of domestic violence to a crime prevention entity shall constitute grounds for protecting the victim of domestic violence and providing them, at the initiative of the relevant crime prevention entity, with assistance and social services.

The form of consent for transfer of information about domestic violence shall be established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 313. Registry of Information on Facts of Domestic Violence

The registry of information on facts of domestic violence is a state information system intended for collecting, processing, registering, accumulating, storing and using information on facts of domestic violence.

The functioning of the registry of information on facts of domestic violence is ensured by the Ministry of Internal Affairs, which is its owner.

The Ministry of Internal Affairs shall ensure: formation of the registry of information on facts of domestic violence; safety and protection of information on facts of domestic violence; compliance with the procedure for providing stored information on facts of domestic violence. Other crime prevention entities subordinate to the Government of the Republic of Belarus that conduct preventive measures to prevent domestic violence shall, within their competence, ensure the registration and transfer of information on facts of domestic violence to internal affairs bodies.

When registering information on facts of domestic violence, the following shall be recorded: the name of the crime prevention entity, information about the official, date and basis for entering information about a fact of domestic violence into the registry of information on facts of domestic violence; place, date, time and circumstances of commission of domestic violence; surname, given name, patronymic (if any), place of residence and contact telephone number of the citizen who reported the fact of domestic violence; identification number (if available), surname, given name, patronymic (if any), day, month, year and place of birth, education, place of work (study) and position of employee (profession of worker), place of residence and contact telephone number of the victim of domestic violence; identification number (if available), surname, given name, patronymic (if any), day, month, year and place of birth, education, place of work (study) and position of employee (profession of worker), place of residence and contact telephone number of the citizen who committed domestic violence; data on provision of assistance to the victim of domestic violence; measures taken against the citizen who committed domestic violence and their result. For the purposes of forming the registry of information on facts of domestic violence and effective application of measures for individual prevention of offenses, the processing of personal data shall be carried out by officials of crime prevention entities that conduct preventive measures to prevent domestic violence, without the consent of the personal data subject, in compliance with the requirements of legislation on personal data.

Personal data placed in the registry of information on facts of domestic violence shall be stored for five years. Upon expiration of this period, personal data placed in the registry of information on facts of domestic violence shall be deleted.

Issues of functioning of the registry of information on facts of domestic violence not regulated by this Law and other legislative acts shall be regulated in the manner established by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 32. Rights and obligations of citizens in respect of whom individual prevention of offenses is carried out

Citizens in respect of whom individual prevention of offenses is carried out have the right to: know the grounds for applying measures of individual prevention of offenses to them; familiarize themselves with the materials of preventive cases opened in respect of them; exercise other rights provided by this Law and other legislative acts.

Citizens in respect of whom individual prevention of offenses is carried out are obliged to: timely appear upon summons of officials of authorized subjects of prevention of offenses; participate in preventive measures conducted by officials of authorized subjects of prevention of offenses; receive copies of official warnings issued in respect of them; receive copies of decisions on carrying out preventive registration adopted in respect of them; receive protective orders issued in respect of them, not violate prohibitions, fulfill obligations contained in these protective orders; fulfill other obligations provided by this Law and other legislative acts.

Restriction of rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of citizens in respect of whom individual prevention of offenses is carried out, including restriction of rights to secrecy of correspondence, telephone and other communications, free movement and choice of place of residence, inviolability of dwelling and other lawful possessions of citizens, is not permitted unless otherwise provided by this Law and other legislative acts.

### Article 321. Rights of victims of domestic violence and other offenses

Victims of domestic violence and other offenses, regardless of registration at place of residence and consent to transfer information about domestic violence, have the right in accordance with legislation to receive: protection and prompt response in all cases of offenses committed against them; complete and comprehensive information about types of assistance provided within the competence of state bodies and other organizations; free of charge psychological assistance, social services in the form of emergency social services, including temporary shelter services; free medical and legal assistance; access to education, including for minor children residing with them. Protection of a victim of domestic violence and other offenses, provision of assistance and social services to them do not depend on their readiness to file a complaint about bringing a citizen who committed domestic violence or another person who committed an offense to criminal or administrative liability or to testify against them.

The rights of minor victims of domestic violence and other offenses are protected taking into account the interests of the child, their age, gender, state of health, intellectual and physical development. The exercise of protection of rights and legitimate interests of minors is entrusted to their parents and other legal representatives, and in the case of their absence or commission by them of domestic violence against minors – to other relatives or guardianship and trusteeship authorities.

## CHAPTER 5

OTHER ISSUES OF ACTIVITIES ON PREVENTION OF OFFENSES

### Article 33. Liability for violation of legislation in the sphere of prevention of offenses

Violation of legislation in the sphere of prevention of offenses entails liability established by legislative acts.

### Article 34. Procedure for appealing decisions and actions (inaction) of officials of subjects of prevention of offenses

Citizens who believe that their rights, freedoms and legitimate interests have been infringed, as well as organizations that believe that their rights and legitimate interests have been infringed by decisions and actions (inaction) of subjects of prevention of offenses, their officials, have the right to appeal these decisions and actions (inaction) in the established order to a higher state body (higher official), and thereafter – to the prosecutor or to court.

### Article 35. Control and supervision in the sphere of prevention of offenses

Control over the activities of subjects of prevention of offenses is exercised by higher state bodies and officials.

Supervision over the precise and uniform enforcement of legislation in the sphere of prevention of offenses is exercised by the Prosecutor General and prosecutors subordinate to him.

### Article 36. Financing and material and technical support of activities on prevention of offenses

Financing and material and technical support of activities on prevention of offenses are carried out at the expense of funds of the republican and local budgets, as well as other sources not prohibited by legislation.

FINAL PROVISIONS

### Article 37. Amendments to the law

To introduce into the Law of the Republic of Belarus of 26 June 2003 "On participation of citizens in the protection of public order" (National Register of Legal Acts of the Republic of Belarus, 2003, No. 74, 2/963; 2010, No. 5, 2/1630) the following amendments: in article 4: paragraph three after the words "prosecutor's office," and "service of the Republic of Belarus" to supplement respectively with the words "Investigative Committee of the Republic of Belarus, bodies" and ", other state military formations and paramilitary organizations"; paragraph four to supplement with the words ", other state military formations and paramilitary organizations"; to supplement article 6 with paragraph six of the following content: "participation in councils of public points of protection of public order (hereinafter – councils of public points)."; to supplement the Law with chapter 41 of the following content:

"CHAPTER 41

COUNCILS OF PUBLIC POINTS

### Article 231. Formation of councils of public points

Councils of public points are an organizational form of combining the efforts of voluntary squads, organizations and citizens in activities on protection of public order and prevention of offenses, as well as their interaction with subjects of prevention of offenses defined by legislative acts of the Republic of Belarus.

Councils of public points are formed from among citizens recommended by subjects of prevention of offenses, with the consent of these citizens, including from among officials of subjects of prevention of offenses, and carry out their activities under the general guidance of the respective local executive and administrative bodies.

Decisions on the establishment and abolition of councils of public points, approval of their personnel composition are adopted by the respective local executive and administrative bodies. The procedure for establishment and abolition of councils of public points, their interaction with subjects of prevention of offenses, as well as measures to encourage citizens and public associations participating in activities on protection of public order and prevention of offenses, are determined by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus.

The permanent location of councils of public points are public points – special premises determined by the respective local executive and administrative bodies.

### Article 232. Powers of Public Point Councils

Councils of public points: study the state of public order in the territory assigned to the public point council, provide assistance to subjects of crime prevention in their activities on prevention and suppression of offenses; coordinate the work of organizations represented in the public point council, facilitate the establishment of permanent interaction and exchange of experience in prevention and suppression of offenses between these organizations and subjects of crime prevention located in the territory assigned to the public point council; organize duty shifts of members of the public point council at the public point, hear their reports on the performance of duties to participate in the protection of public order; organize discussions on strengthening public order, as well as the behavior of citizens brought to administrative or criminal liability, at their meetings, at meetings of citizens at their place of residence or place of stay, study or work with submission of appropriate recommendations to subjects of crime prevention; develop and submit to subjects of crime prevention recommendations on issues of prevention and suppression of offenses in the territory assigned to the public point council; petition the subjects specified in part one of Article 22 of this Law for the encouragement of citizens actively participating in activities on prevention and suppression of offenses.

### Article 233. Support for the Activities of Public Point Councils

Assistance in the functioning of public point councils is provided by subjects of crime prevention. Necessary conditions are created at public points for the work of employees of internal affairs bodies, emergency situations bodies and subdivisions, border service bodies of the Republic of Belarus, officials of other subjects of crime prevention.

Internal affairs bodies, emergency situations bodies and subdivisions, border service bodies of the Republic of Belarus provide public point councils with instructional and methodological manuals and provide them with information necessary for their activities in the volume determined by the heads of these bodies and subdivisions or their deputies.

Provision of public point councils with necessary premises, means of communication, furniture, other material and technical means, payment for public utilities, communication services, operational expenses of the said points is carried out at the expense of funds from the corresponding local budgets and other sources not prohibited by the legislation of the Republic of Belarus.».

### Article 38. Recognition as Invalid of a Law and a Separate Provision of a Law

Recognize as invalid:

The Law of the Republic of Belarus of November 10, 2008 "On the Fundamentals of Activities on Crime Prevention" (National Register of Legal Acts of the Republic of Belarus, 2008, No. 277, 2/1549); Article 18 of the Law of the Republic of Belarus of December 28, 2009 "On Amendments and Additions to Certain Laws of the Republic of Belarus" (National Register of Legal Acts of the Republic of Belarus, 2010, No. 5, 2/1630).

### Article 39. Implementation of the Provisions of this Law

The Council of Ministers of the Republic of Belarus shall within three months: bring the decisions of the Government of the Republic of Belarus into compliance with this Law; ensure that republican bodies of state administration subordinate to the Government of the Republic of Belarus bring their normative legal acts into compliance with this Law; take other measures necessary for the implementation of the provisions of this Law.

### Article 40. Entry into Force of this Law

This Law enters into force in the following order: Articles 1–38 – three months after the official publication of this Law; other provisions – after the official publication of this Law.
