Original caption and preamble

Act of 9 July 2014, containing rules on municipal support in the field of self-reliance, participation, sheltered living and relief (Social Support Act 2015)

We Willem-Alexander, by the grace of God, King of the Netherlands, Prince of Orange-Nassau, etc. etc. etc.

To all who shall see or hear these read, greeting! do make known:

Whereas We have taken into consideration that citizens bear their own responsibility for the way in which they organise their lives and participate in social life, and that citizens may be expected to assist each other in this to the best of their ability; that it is also desirable to establish new rules to bring the rights and obligations of the citizen into better balance with each other; that citizens who are insufficiently self-reliant, either by themselves or together with persons in their immediate environment, or are insufficiently able to participate, must be able to rely on support organised by the government; that the support of the self-reliance and the participation of persons with a disability, chronic psychological or psychosocial problems, must be aimed at enabling citizens to remain in their own living environment for as long as possible; that it is reasonable to place the government responsibility for supporting self-reliance and participation as close as possible to the citizen; that it is therefore desirable to establish new rules on the municipal responsibility for the support of persons with a disability and persons with psychological or psychosocial problems; that it is furthermore desirable to ensure good accessibility of provisions, services and spaces for people with a disability;

So it is that We, having heard the Advisory Division of the Council of State, and in consultation with the States General, have approved and decreed, as We hereby approve and decree:

### Artikel 1.1.2

For the purposes of this act and the provisions based thereon, the following shall be equated with: a. spouse: registered partner; b. married: registered as partners.

For the purposes of this act and the decrees adopted for its implementation: a. an unmarried adult who shares a joint household with another unmarried adult shall also be regarded as married or as a spouse, unless it concerns a blood relative in the first degree; b. a person who lives permanently separated from the person to whom he is married shall also be regarded as unmarried.

A joint household exists if two persons have their main residence in the same dwelling and they demonstrate that they care for each other by making a contribution to the costs of the household or otherwise. A joint household shall in any case be deemed to exist if the persons concerned have their main residence in the same dwelling and: a. they have been married to each other or have previously been equated therewith for the purposes of this act, b. a child has been born from their relationship or one has acknowledged a child of the other, c. they have mutually obligated themselves to contribute to the household pursuant to a valid cohabitation contract, or d. they are regarded on the basis of a registration as a joint household that corresponds in nature and purport to the joint household referred to in the third paragraph.

An order in council shall determine which registrations, and for which period, shall be taken into account for the application of the fourth paragraph, part d.

Rules may be laid down by order in council with respect to what is understood by demonstrating that one cares for another, as referred to in the third paragraph.

### § 2. General provisions

### Artikel 1.2.1

A resident of the Netherlands shall be eligible in accordance with the provisions of this act for a customised provision, consisting of: a. support for his self-reliance and participation, to be provided by the executive board of the municipality of which he is a resident, to the extent that, due to a disability, chronic psychological or psychosocial problems, he is not sufficiently self-reliant or able to participate through his own efforts, with usual help, with informal care or with help from other persons in his social network, b. sheltered living, to be provided by the executive board of the municipality to which he applies, to the extent that, due to psychological or psychosocial problems, he is unable to maintain himself in society through his own efforts, with usual help, with informal care or with help from other persons in his social network, or c. shelter, to be provided by the executive board of the municipality to which he applies, if he has left the home situation, whether or not in connection with risks to his safety as a result of domestic violence, and is unable to maintain himself in society through his own efforts, with usual help, with informal care or with help from other persons in his social network.

### Artikel 1.2.2

A foreign national shall only be eligible for the provision of a customised provision if he is lawfully resident within the meaning of Article 8, parts a to e and l, of the Aliens Act 2000

A foreign national shall, in derogation from the first paragraph, not be eligible for a customised provision consisting of shelter in the cases referred to in Article 24, second paragraph, of

Directive 2004/38/EC

. The first sentence shall not apply to shelter in connection with risks to the safety of the person concerned as a result of domestic violence.

In derogation from the first or second paragraph, it may be determined that in cases to be specified by or pursuant to order in council, if necessary in derogation from Article 10 of the Aliens Act 2000

, categories of foreign nationals not lawfully residing in the Netherlands to be indicated by or pursuant to that order, shall be wholly or partially eligible for customised provisions to be indicated by that order. Eligibility for a customised provision does not give a foreign national a right to lawful residence.

The order referred to in the third paragraph may determine that the executive board shall ensure the provision of the provisions designated by that order.

## Hoofdstuk 2. Maatschappelijke ondersteuning

### § 1. Opdracht gemeente

### Artikel 2.1.1

The municipal authority shall ensure social support.

The municipal authority shall ensure the quality and continuity of the provisions.

### Artikel 2.1.2

The municipal council periodically adopts a plan concerning the policy to be pursued by the municipal executive regarding social support.

The plan describes the policy intentions regarding decisions to be taken or actions to be performed by the executive board that are aimed at: a. promoting social cohesion, the accessibility of facilities, services and spaces for people with disabilities, promoting safety and liveability in the municipality, as well as preventing and combating domestic violence; b. enabling the various categories of informal carers and volunteers to perform their tasks as informal carer or volunteer as much as possible; c. determining at an early stage whether residents require social support; d. preventing residents from becoming dependent on social support; e. providing general facilities to residents who require social support; f. providing customised facilities to support self-reliance and participation to residents of the municipality who are unable or insufficiently able to do so on their own, with usual help, with informal care or with help from other persons in their social network; g. providing customised facilities to persons who are unable to maintain themselves in society on their own and who require sheltered housing or shelter in connection with psychological or psychosocial problems or because they have left the home situation, whether or not in connection with risks to their safety as a result of domestic violence; h. implementing the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities concluded in New York on 13 December 2006 (Treaty Series 2007, 169).

The plan is aimed at ensuring that: a. clients can remain in their own living environment for as long as possible; b. clients who live in sheltered housing or receive shelter have a safe living environment and, if possible, are again able to maintain themselves in society on their own. The plan gives special attention to: a. service provision that is as integrated as possible in the areas of social support, public health, prevention, care, youth care, education, welfare, housing, work and income; b. cooperation with healthcare insurers and healthcare providers as referred to in the with a view to service provision that is as integrated as possible; c. choice options between providers for those to whom a customised facility is provided, taking into account the religious denomination, philosophy of life and cultural background of clients, in particular for small target groups; d. the accessibility of facilities, services and spaces for people with disabilities; e. the manner in which continuity of assistance is guaranteed, in particular with regard to the person who, upon reaching a certain age, can no longer receive youth care as referred to in the

Youth Act; f. possibilities to enable people to remain in their own living environment for as long as possible through the deployment of guidance, including day care; g. the manner in which residents are informed about the persons who can act as representative of a client who cannot be deemed capable of a reasonable assessment of his interests in this regard.

The plan indicates the manner in which the municipality

The plan indicates which results the municipal executive wishes to achieve in the period covered by the plan, which criteria are used to measure how these results have been achieved and which performance indicators are used with regard to providers.

### Artikel 2.1.3

The municipal council adopts by ordinance the rules necessary for the implementation of the plan referred to in Article 2.1.2 and the decisions to be taken or actions to be performed by the executive board for its implementation.

The ordinance shall in any case determine: a. the manner in which and the criteria on which it is determined whether a client qualifies for a customised facility for self-reliance, participation, sheltered housing or shelter; b. the manner in which the amount of a personal budget is determined, whereby it applies that the amount must be adequate; c. which requirements are imposed on the quality of facilities, including requirements relating to the expertise of professionals; d. with regard to which facilities a scheme for handling clients' complaints is required; e. with regard to which facilities a scheme for client participation in proposed decisions of the provider that are of importance to users is required.

The ordinance shall determine the manner in which residents, including in any case clients or their representatives, are involved in the implementation of this Act, whereby in any case the manner in which they: a. are given the opportunity to make proposals for policy; b. are enabled at an early stage to provide solicited and unsolicited advice in decision-making on ordinances and policy proposals; c. are provided with support to enable them to fulfil their role effectively; d. can participate in periodic consultation; e. can submit topics for the agenda of this consultation; f. are provided with the information required for adequate participation in the consultation. The ordinance shall lay down rules for combating wrongful receipt of a customised facility or a personal budget, as well as abuse or improper use of the Act.

### Artikel 2.1.4

By ordinance it may be determined that a client owes a contribution to the costs: a. for the use of a general provision, not being client support; b. for a customized provision or a personal budget.

In the ordinance, the amount of the contribution for the different types of provisions may be determined differently and it may be determined that: a. for persons belonging to groups described therein, a discount indicated therein on the contribution for a general provision applies; b. the contribution for a customized provision or a personal budget: is owed as long as the client makes use of the customized provision or during the period for which the personal budget is provided, and is dependent on the income and assets of the client and his or her spouse.

The total of the contributions for a customized provision or a personal budget does not exceed the cost price. In the ordinance it is determined in what manner the cost price is calculated.

By or pursuant to general administrative order, rules are established with regard to the contributions for a customized provision or a personal budget, including rules on: a. the amount, b. the manner in which the income and assets are taken into account in the determination, c. the period within which the owed contribution must be paid, d. the manner of collection, and e. the exceptional grounds for owing a contribution.

The proposal for a general administrative order as referred to in the fourth paragraph, which relates to the assets referred to in the second paragraph, is not made earlier than four weeks after the draft has been submitted to both chambers of the States General. If one of the chambers of the States General decides not to agree with the draft, no proposal is made and a new draft may not be submitted to both chambers of the States General earlier than six weeks after the decision of that chamber of the States General.

The contribution for a customized provision or a personal budget, with the exception of that for shelter, is determined and collected for the municipality by the CAK.

In the ordinance it is determined by which body the contribution for a customized provision or a personal budget for shelter is determined and collected. The executive board ensures that the CAK is notified of the contributions determined by the said body, insofar as they do not relate to persons who have left the home situation in connection with risks to their safety as a result of domestic violence.

### § 3. Maatwerkvoorzieningen

### Artikel 2.3.1

The executive board ensures that a customized provision is provided to persons who are eligible for it.

### Article 2.3.2

If a notification of a need for social support is made to the college, the college shall conduct an investigation in consultation with the person by or on whose behalf the notification was made and, where possible, with the informal carer or informal carers or their representative, as soon as possible, but no later than six weeks, in accordance with the second to eighth paragraphs. The college shall confirm receipt of the notification. Before the investigation commences, the client may hand over to the college a personal plan in which they describe the circumstances referred to in the fourth paragraph, parts a to g, and indicate which social support they consider most appropriate. The college shall inform the client of this possibility and allow them seven days after the notification referred to in the first paragraph to hand over the plan.

The college shall inform the client and their informal carer before the investigation of the possibility of using free client support.

The college shall investigate: a. the needs, personal characteristics and preferences of the client; b. the possibilities to improve their self-reliance or their participation or to provide for their need for sheltered accommodation or shelter through their own efforts or with customary assistance; c. the possibilities to achieve improvement of their self-reliance or their participation or to provide for their need for sheltered accommodation or shelter through informal care or assistance from other persons in their social network; d. the need for measures to support the client's informal carer; e. the possibilities to achieve improvement of their self-reliance or their participation through the use of a general facility or by performing socially useful activities, or the possibilities to provide for their need for sheltered accommodation or shelter through the use of a general facility; f. the possibilities to achieve optimally coordinated service provision with a view to the need for improvement of their self-reliance, their participation or for sheltered accommodation or shelter, through cooperation with health insurers and healthcare providers and parties in the field of public health, youth care, education, welfare, housing, work and income; g. what contributions to the costs the client will owe with application of the provisions pursuant to article 2.1.4

, .

If the client has handed over to the college a personal plan as referred to in the second paragraph, the college shall include that plan in the investigation referred to in the fourth paragraph, parts a to g.

During the investigation, the client or their representative shall be informed of the options to choose the provision of a personal budget. The client or their representative shall be informed in comprehensible terms of the consequences of that choice.

The client or their representative shall provide the college with the data and documents necessary for the investigation and which they can reasonably obtain.

The college shall provide the client or their representative with a written representation of the results of the investigation.

An application as referred to in article 2.3.5 cannot be made until the investigation has been conducted, unless the investigation has not been conducted within the period mentioned in the first paragraph.

### Article 2.3.4

### Article 2.3.5

The college shall decide on an application: a. from a resident of the municipality for a tailored facility for the benefit of self-reliance and participation; b. from a resident of the Netherlands for a tailored facility for the benefit of shelter and sheltered accommodation.

The college shall issue the decision within two weeks of receipt of the application.
