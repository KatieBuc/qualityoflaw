# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  
1. Region is the City of Tual.  
2. Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and Co-Administration with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.  
3. Regional Government is the regional head as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
4. Mayor is the Mayor of Tual.  
5. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution as an element of the Regional Government administration.  
6. Administration is all actions carried out by government and non-government institutions which include prevention, protection and recovery for victims of violence against women and children.  
7. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, and is not yet married including a child still in the womb.  
8. Woman is every person who is physically born as a woman.  
9. Victim is a woman and or child who experiences suffering and or hardship either directly or indirectly as a result of an act of violence.  
10. Prevention is all efforts carried out by government and non-government institutions aimed at providing a sense of security to women and children.  
11. Child protection is all activities carried out to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
12. Protection of women is all activities carried out to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
13. Suffering is any act that results in or may result in suffering whether psychological, physical, sexual, economic, social to the victim.  
14. Physical Violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting or causing death.  
15. Psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feeling helpless, and or severe psychological suffering to women and or children.  
16. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.  
17. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim, when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.  
18. Companion is a person or institution that has the ability to accompany victims to provide counseling, therapy, legal services and advocacy for strengthening victim recovery.  
19. Women and child protection institution is an institution providing services to victims of violence, based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical services (Including Medicolegal), psycho-social and legal services.  
20. Safe house is a temporary residence used to provide protection for women and children victims in accordance with the standard operating procedures or abbreviated as SOP that are determined.  
21. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and or other community organizations.  
22. Integrated Service Center hereinafter referred to as PPT is a unified unit that carries out integrated service functions for women and children victims of violence, based in hospitals, health centers, P2TP2A, Safe Houses, Child Social Protection Houses (RPSA), Trauma Center Protection Houses (RPTC), Women's Social Protection Houses (RPSW).  
23. Integrated Service Center for Women and Child Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is an integrated service unit established by the Regional Government to provide comprehensive and quality services to women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 1

The administration of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out based on Pancasila, the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, as well as the principles and basic rights as regulated in the convention on women and children which include:  
a. Respect for human rights;  
b. Non-discrimination;  
c. The best interests of women and or children;  
d. The right to life, survival, and development;  
e. Respect for the opinions of women and children;  
f. Gender justice and equality; and  
g. Protection of victims.

#### Article 2

The purpose of administering protection for women and children victims of violence is to provide prevention, protection and services to women and children from acts of violence.

#### Article 3

The activities for preventing the occurrence of violence as referred to in article 3 include:  
a. Socialization;  
b. Dissemination; and  
c. Training;

#### Article 4

The activities for protection and services to victims as referred to in Article 3 include:  
a. Complaints;  
b. Health Services;  
c. Social Rehabilitation Services;  
d. Legal Aid Services; and  
e. Repatriation and Social Reintegration Services.

# CHAPTER II

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Victims have the right to:  
a. have their dignity and honor respected as human beings;  
b. receive protection from family, community and regional government;  
c. receive information;  
d. optimal services;  
e. continuous services up to the rehabilitation stage;  
f. confidential handling;  
g. psychological assistance and legal aid; and  
h. make their own decisions;

# CHAPTER III

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 6

1. Protection of Women and Children is the obligation and responsibility of the Regional Government, community, parents and family;  
2. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in article 6 paragraph (1) include:  
a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;  
b. establishing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
c. conducting cooperation in the administration of Women and Children from acts of violence;  
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
e. allocating budget for the administration of protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
f. allocating budget for the administration of protection of women and children from acts of violence according to regional financial capacity and;  
g. fostering and supervising the administration of Protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
3. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government is obliged to provide and administer services in the form of:  
a. establishing and facilitating the administration of integrated service institutions for victims by involving relevant community elements;  
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims.

#### Article 7

The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 paragraph (1) include:  
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;  
b. providing protection to victims;  
c. providing emergency assistance;  
d. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to the competent authorities; and  
e. participating in the handling of victims of violence.

#### Article 8

The obligations of parents and/or family as referred to in Article 6 paragraph (1) include:  
a. preventing all forms of violence and protecting women and children as family members;  
b. providing support for women and children victims of violence who are part of family members;

# CHAPTER IV

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional

#### Article 9

1. In the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence which includes prevention and mitigation activities, an Integrated Service Center (PPT) shall be established.  
2. The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of elements of the Regional Government and elements originating from related institutions outside the Regional Government.  
3. Further provisions regarding the PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be further regulated by Mayor Regulation.  
4. The PPT as referred to in Paragraph (1), in implementing protection for women and children victims of violence may coordinate with institutions and or organizations in Tual City.  
5. In the Implementation of Protection for women and children victims of violence, it may be established at the sub-district, urban village and village levels.

#### Article 10

1. In addition to establishing the PPT as referred to in Article 9, to support the implementation of empowerment and protection for women and children victims of violence, the regional government may establish P2TP2A.  
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of elements of the Police, Prosecutor's Office, Court, Health Office, Social Office, Ministry of Religious Affairs, Office of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, NGOs, Child Observers.  
3. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), is a functional work unit having the duty to organize integrated services for women and children victims of violence.  
4. In carrying out the main duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (3), P2TP2A may coordinate and become a working partner of the PPT.  
5.  Further provisions regarding P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), paragraph (3), and paragraph (4), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Two

Handling and Mechanism

#### Article 11

1. The Implementation of Protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner in medical handling, legal protection, medicolegal, psychological and economic, the implementation of which may be carried out through partnerships with social community institutions engaged in the field of protection of women and children.  
2. The implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1), must take into account religious norms as well as the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.  
3. Principles of service and assistance:  
a. fast;  
b. safe;  
c. empathy;  
d. guarantee of confidentiality;  
e. easily accessible; and  
f. free of charge.  
4. The mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures.

# CHAPTER V

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE AND PROTECTION

#### Article 12

1. Forms of violence against women and children include physical, psychological, sexual, neglect, exploitation and other violence.  
2. Forms of physical violence are any acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage of a fetus in the womb and or cause death.  
3. Forms of sexual violence are sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes or certain purposes.  
4. Forms of psychological violence are acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness.  
5. Forms of violence of neglect are intentional or unintentional acts that result in the non-fulfillment of basic needs.  
(6) Forms of violence of exploitation are forms of violence including acts of exploiting children economically or sexually to benefit oneself or others, acts with or without the consent of the victim but not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully, transferring or transplanting organs and or body tissues or utilizing or the ability of a person to obtain benefits.

#### Article 13

1. Every woman and child who becomes a victim of violence must receive protection in accordance with applicable provisions.  
2. Protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out for residents of Tual City both in Tual City and outside Tual City.

#### Article 14

1. Forms of protection for women and children victims of violence are in the form of medical protection, legal, medicolegal (forensic medicine), psychological, repatriation and social reintegration.  
2. Forms of medical protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1). In the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.  
3. Forms of legal protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), in the form of services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.  
4. Forms of medicolegal protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of medical services for the purposes of evidence in the field of law.  
5. Forms of psychological protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), in the form of assistance in the context of recovery of the traumatic condition of victims including the provision of Safe Houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving.  
6. Forms of Repatriation protection as referred to in paragraph (1) are efforts to return women and children victims of violence from abroad to the debarkation point/entry point, or from the receiving area to the area of origin.  
7. Forms of Social Reintegration protection as referred to in paragraph (1) are efforts to reunite women and children victims of violence with family, substitute family.

# CHAPTER VI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 15

1. The Government in implementing protection for women and children victims of violence. May involve community participation.  
2. Participation as referred to in paragraph (1), may be carried out by individuals, groups or social community organizations.

#### Article 16

1. Social community organizations that participate in the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in article 15 paragraph (2), shall be given guidance by the regional government through regional apparatus organizations related to the duties of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.  
2. Organizational guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), may be in the form of social guidance, skills guidance and operational technical guidance.

# CHAPTER VII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

## Part One

Guidance

#### Article 17

1. The Regional Government is obligated to conduct guidance on the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.  
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:  
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;  
b. technical guidance and training;  
c. provision of facilities; and  
d. monitoring and evaluation.

## Part Two

Supervision

#### Article 18

1. The regional government is obligated to conduct supervision of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.  
2. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted with the principles of:  
a. professional;  
b. transparent; and  
c. accountable.

# CHAPTER VIII

## SOURCES OF FUNDS AND REPORTING

#### Article 19

1. Sources of funds for the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence carried out by regional apparatus of Tual City are from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of Tual City.  
2. Sources of funds as referred to in paragraph (1), may also come from other lawful and non-binding sources.  
3. Sources of funds for implementation from other lawful sources as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be supervised in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
4. PPT is obligated to report the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence periodically to the Mayor of Tual in accordance with applicable regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 20

PPT that already exist namely P2TP2A and Yante Fangnan Yanad (Child-Loving Family) at the time this Regional Regulation comes into effect shall continue to carry out their duties until the formation of PPT based on this Regional Regulation.

# CHAPTER X

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 21

This Regional Regulation comes into effect on the date of promulgation.

##### I. GENERAL

The implementation of protection for victims of violence is carried out based on the principles of humanity, integration and justice. Therefore every person must receive their right to protection for a sense of security and freedom from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity which is contrary to what is aspired to in the Preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, namely the creation of a just and civilized humanity. In principle, the strategic target of the implementation of protection is to maintain the sustainability of protection through integrated, comprehensive services and a safe and comfortable shelter that is conducive for victims of violence. These strategic targets are pursued through interrelated stages, both planning, protection, services and financing, supervision and sanctions.  

Therefore, a legal umbrella in the form of a Regional Regulation is needed that is capable of accommodating all issues related to the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence, capable of providing integrated services holistically and comprehensively, and firmly giving a mandate to institutions to coordinate policies and supervision, and a mandate to institutions to provide services. In addition, regional regulations can also open the involvement of non-governmental institutions and the community to play a broad role.  

Based on several considerations above, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence.
