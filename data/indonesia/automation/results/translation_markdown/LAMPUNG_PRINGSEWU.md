BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

THE REGENT OF PRINGSEWU,

a. that every citizen has human rights that must be guaranteed by the state to obtain a sense of security, be protected and be free from all forms of violence.
b. that women and children are vulnerable groups, therefore they need to receive protection and services from the state that can provide prevention, protection and services to victims of violence;

c. that the regional government together with the community is obliged to implement prevention, protection and recovery efforts for children and women victims of violence for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

1. Region is Pringsewu Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Pringsewu.  
4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution as an element of the Regional Government administration.  
5. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of Pringsewu Regency.  
6. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is a regional apparatus in Pringsewu Regency.  
7. Woman is an adult human being of female gender.  
8. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child in the womb.  
9. Child victim of violence is a child who receives harsh treatment either physically, psychologically, economically, socially, sexually, and other losses resulting from state policy, acts of violence and or threats of violence within the scope of the household and community.  
10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.  
11. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim.  
12. Violence against women is any act of violence against women that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or in private life.1
13. Violence against children is any action that results in misery or suffering to children physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom.  
14. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causes death.  
15. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.  
16. Economic violence is any act that deliberately neglects family members in the form of not providing a proper living care or maintenance.  
17. Neglect is the act of neglecting women and or children within the scope of the household which according to the applicable law for the person concerned or due to agreement or contract he is obliged to provide living, care or maintenance to that person. Neglect also applies to any person who causes economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.  
18. Psychological violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, sense of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.  
19. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, court, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.  
20. Law enforcement is the action of apparatus authorized by the state to implement laws and regulations.  
21. Legal aid is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocates to carry out the process of accompanying witnesses and or victims of violence against women and children.  
22. Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
23. Implementation of protection is a series of activities aimed at preventing the occurrence of violence, providing protection and recovery services and social reintegration, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation carried out by the Integrated Service Center.  
24. Prevention is a direct effort made by the Regional Government to prevent acts of violence against women and children.  
25. Complaint is a notification either in writing or orally by an interested party to an authorized institution, in which the person concerned suffers losses due to the actions of others.  
26. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.  
27. Assistant is a person or institution that has the expertise to assist victims in conducting counseling therapy advocacy for strengthening and self-recovery of victims of violence.  
28. Integrated Service is a series of activities to provide protection for women and children victims of violence carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as a unified implementation, prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal aid for victims of gender-based violence and children.  
29. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for victims of violence in the region.  
30. Victim Recovery is all efforts to strengthen victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, whether physically, psychologically, socially, economically or sexually.  
31. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for strengthening victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems they face.  
32. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing the community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, family substitutes who can provide protection and fulfillment of the victim's needs.  
33. Safe House (shelter) is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with the determined operational standards.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

##### Article 2 

The implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out based on the principles of respect or recognition of equal human rights and dignity, non-discrimination, gender equality and justice as well as protection of the human rights of Women and Children and the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to provide protection and services that include aspects of prevention, service and assistance, reunification and empowerment.

#### Article 4 

The implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence aims to:  
a. prevent all forms of acts of violence including trafficking in persons;  
b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;  
c. protect, provide a sense of security for victims of acts of violence;  
d. provide services to victims of violence; and  
e. carry out comprehensive recovery for victims.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 5

The scope of protection for women and children victims of violence includes prevention efforts, services and empowerment of victims of violence in the region.

# CHAPTER IV

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 6

Forms of violence include:  
a. physical violence;  
b. psychological violence;  
c. sexual violence;  
d. neglect;  
e. exploitation; and/or  
f. other violence.

#### Article 7

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or cause death.

#### Article 8

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 6 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 9

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 6 letter c is caused by:  
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;  
b. forced sexual intercourse;  
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or;  
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 10

Neglect as referred to in Article 6 letter d is caused by:  
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or manage as appropriate committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
c. acts that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or;  
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 11

Exploitation as referred to in Article 6 letter e is caused by:  
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  
b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or  
c. any form of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

#### Article 12

Other violence as referred to in Article 6 letter f is caused by:  
a. threats of violence include: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and  
b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

##### CHAPTER V

##### RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 13

Every victim has the right to receive:  
a. protection;  
b. information;  
c. optimal services;  
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;  
e. confidential handling;  
f. psychological and legal assistance; and  
g. guarantees of rights related to the victim's status as a family member and community member.

#### Article 14

In addition to the rights as referred to in Article 13, child victims of violence also receive special rights including:  
a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;  
b. the right to basic services; 1c. the right to equal protection;  
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and/or  
e. the right to obtain freedom;  
f. the right of victims and families to obtain facilities in the judicial process.

# CHAPTER VI

## DUTIES AND OBLIGATIONS OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 15

1. The Regional Government has the duty to carry out prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against Women and Children, by:  
a. collecting data and information about Women and Children Victims of Violence;  
b. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;  
c. providing education on anti-violence values against children and women; and
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for Women and Children victims of violence.  
2. The Regional Government is obliged to carry out prevention, handling and recovery efforts for victims of violence against Women and Children, provide and organize services for victims in the form of:  
a. providing and facilitating the establishment of integrated services for victims by involving institutions and community elements;  
b. encouraging public awareness about the importance of protection for victims; and  c. empowering communities for the prevention of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER VII

## INSTITUTIONS ORGANIZING PROTECTION FOR VICTIMS

#### Article 16

1. The Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT) as the Organizing, Prevention and Protection Institution for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the region.  
2. PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of elements:  
a. health;  
b. psychologists;  
c. law enforcement;  
d. social and empowerment;  
e. education and religion;  
f. non-governmental organizations; and  
g. private sector.  
3. Further provisions regarding PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

## PREVENTION OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 17

1. The Government in an effort to prevent acts of violence against women and children carries out empowerment of families, parents, communities by providing information, guidance and or counseling.  
2. In addition to empowerment and support as referred to in paragraph (1), the regional government carries out efforts as follows:  
a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;  
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;  
c. striving to create employment opportunities for women;  
d. building community participation and concern for prevention and from acts of violence against women and children;  
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;  
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and or care about women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 18

1. Prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in article 16, is carried out by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the field of:  
a. social;  
b. health;  
c. education;  
d. manpower;  
e. women's empowerment and child protection;  
f. mental;  
g. security and order;  
2. Prevention of acts of violence by SKPD as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated and continuous manner based on existing regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## IMPLEMENTATION, FORMS OF PROTECTION AND SERVICES

#### Article 19

1. The implementation of protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the PPT framework.
2. PPT in handling medical, legal, medicolegal, psychological and economic protection which in its implementation may establish partnerships with Social Community Institutions engaged in the protection of Women and Children.
3. The implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) must take into account religious norms, customs and the rights and obligations of parents/guardians, husbands and/or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.
4. The mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) which are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 20

1. Forms of protection provided to victims, witnesses and reporters organized by PPT are in the form of:
a. medical;
b. legal;
c. medicolegal (Forensic Medicine);
d. psychological; ore. economic.
2. The form of medical protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, is in the form of treatment and recovery from physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics.
3. The form of legal protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, is in the form of services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.
4. The form of medicolegal protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, is in the form of medical services for the purposes of evidence in the legal field.
5. The form of psychological protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, is in the form of assistance in order to restore traumatic conditions including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving.
6. The form of economic protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1), is in the form of services for skills and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

# CHAPTER X

## REPORTING

#### Article 21

1. PPT reports prevention, handling and protection activities for women and children victims of violence to the SKPD in charge of women's empowerment and child protection.
2. The Head of SKPD in charge of women's empowerment reports prevention, handling and protection activities for women and children victims of violence to the Regent.
3. The Regent reports the protection of women and children victims of violence to the Governor.
4. Further provisions regarding procedures and mechanisms for reporting as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (3) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER XI

## CONTROL, GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 22

1. The Regent carries out control, guidance, and supervision of the implementation of protection for victims of violence against women and children.
2. Control, guidance, and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by the SKPD in charge of women's empowerment and child protection.

# CHAPTER XII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 23

1. The community participates in the implementation of protection for victims.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:
a. forming family partners at the village/sub-district level by the community;
b. forming women and child protection units within community organizations;
c. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
d. providing first aid to victims; ande. reporting to the relevant agency if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

# CHAPTER XIII

## FUNDING

#### Article 24

1. The regional government is obliged to allocate a budget to the integrated service center (PPT) in carrying out the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.
2. The budget allocation as referred to in paragraph (1) is adjusted to the regional financial capacity and is implemented in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XIV

## SANCTION PROVISIONS

#### Article 25

1. Officials appointed to implement integrated protection, services and recovery who do not carry out their obligations and responsibilities shall be subject to actions and or sanctions in accordance with the provisions of applicable Laws and Regulations;
2. PPT managers who in carrying out the tasks of protection, services and recovery for victims of violence, violate the principles of implementing protection, services and recovery may be subject to sanctions in accordance with PPT internal mechanisms and sanctions in accordance with the provisions of applicable Laws and Regulations.

# CHAPTER XV

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 26

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

So that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Pringsewu Regency.

##### I. GENERAL

One of the materials of the Amendment to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia is the guarantee of human rights and constitutional rights of citizens. The amendment can be interpreted as a manifestation of awareness of the importance of protecting human rights and constitutional rights of citizens, which grew and became the spirit of reform. Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.

Every citizen has human rights that must be guaranteed by the state to obtain a sense of security, be protected and be free from all forms of violence. Women and children are vulnerable groups, so they need to receive protection from the state that can provide prevention and protection for victims of violence.

The regional government together with the community is obliged to implement prevention, protection and recovery efforts for children and women victims of violence for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity.

So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, so institutional and regulatory support is needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for women and children victims of violence in Pringsewu Regency.

#### Article 2

What is meant by "respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity" is a series of actions to respect, appreciate and guarantee the fulfillment of victims' rights in order to uphold human dignity.

What is meant by "non-discrimination" is not making distinctions for any reason or in any way, whether concerning religion and beliefs, ethnicity, race, gender, skin color, language, and politics.

What is meant by "gender equality and justice" is a process to be fair to both men and women in obtaining opportunities and their rights as human beings so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and security activities as well as equality in enjoying the results of development.

What is meant by "protection of women's and children's human rights" is providing human rights in the form of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

What is meant by "best interests of victims" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies must be the primary consideration.
