# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  
1. Region is Konawe Kepulauan Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Government of Konawe Kepulauan Regency.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Konawe Kepulauan.  
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Konawe Kepulauan Regency.  
5. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, -4- social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  
6. Woman is a person who has female genitalia, can experience menstruation, pregnancy, childbirth, breastfeeding and includes persons who are legally recognized as Women.  
7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child in the womb.  
8. Violence is any act against a person that results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.  
9. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.  
10. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.  
11. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, serious injury or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or fainting and/or causes death.  
12. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feeling of powerlessness or severe psychological suffering to a person.  
13. Sexual violence is any act in the form of forced sexual intercourse, sexual abuse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural and/or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  
14. Exploitation is an action with or without the victim's consent which includes but is not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or -5- body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits.  
15. Victim is a woman and child who experiences physical suffering, psychological suffering, sexual suffering, either directly or indirectly resulting from acts of violence.  
16. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.  
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.  
18. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.  
19. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by Professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.  
20. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the self-empowerment of victims of violence to resolve the problems they face.  
21. Repatriation is an effort to return women and child victims of violence to the family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.  
22. Rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological, and social conditions so that they can carry out their roles properly both in the family and in society.  
23. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with the family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.  
24. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organizations that provide assistance.  
25. Integrated Service Center hereinafter referred to as PPT is a generic term that can be used by various institutions that provide services for victims of violence such as Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, Safe House, Child Social Protection House, Trauma Center Protection House, Women's Social Protection House, Shelter, etc.  
26. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing services for handling reports/complaints, health services, Social Rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance, as well as Repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Child Victims of Violence. 27. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for relevant agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and child victims of violence.  
28. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to provide protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

# CHAPTER III

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 3

Forms of violence against women and children are:  
a. physical violence;  
b. psychological violence;  
c. sexual violence;  
d. neglect; and  
e. exploitation.

#### Article 4

Physical violence as referred to in Article 3 letter a is caused by acts that cause pain, injury, serious injury or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or cause death.

#### Article 5

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 3 letter b is caused by acts that cause fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feeling of powerlessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 6

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 3 letter c is caused by:  
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;  
b. forced sexual intercourse;  
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or  
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 7

Neglect as referred to in Article 3 letter d is caused by:  
a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for care;  
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after children as they should be, committed by parents, guardians or any other party responsible for their care;  
c. acts that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or  
d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 8

Exploitation as referred to in Article 3 letter e is caused by:  
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  
b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/or  
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

# CHAPTER IV

## RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 10

Every victim has the right to obtain:  
a. protection from individuals, groups and institutions both government and non-government. b. access and information about the existence of complaint centers, PPT, and all matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and involvement in the decision-making process related to assistance and case development.  
c. services that include medical, extensive medicolegal, psychosocial and legal.  
d. handling that does not stop at physical and psychological healing, but until the victim can resume life in society including the restoration of their good name.  f. confidential handling which includes guarantees for the victim that their identity will not be disseminated, medical care and legal handling.  
g. assistance provided by psychologists to victims suffering from trauma/other mental problems to restore the victim's mental condition.  
h. legal aid assistance provided by persons and/or legal aid institutions to victims at every level of examination and during the legal process.  
i. guarantees of rights related to the victim's status

# CHAPTER V

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 10

Obligations and responsibilities in the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence are the shared responsibility of:  
a. regional government;  
b. society;  
c. family and parents.

#### Article 11

1. The Regional Government is obliged and responsible for providing Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence.  
2. The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children victims of violence adopted by the government;  
b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence;  
c. Collecting data and information about women and children victims of violence;  
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;  
e. establishing an Integrated Service Center;  
f. allocating budget in the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;  
g. Fostering, supervising and evaluating the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.  

#### Article 12

1. The obligations and responsibilities of society as referred to in Article 10 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation.  
2. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:  
a. preventing acts of Violence against victims;  
b. establishing complaint posts at the sub-district/village level;  
c. establishing women and child protection units within community organizations;  
d. independently conducting socialization of women's rights and children's rights;  
e. providing first aid to victims; and  
f. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.  
4. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 13

The obligations of family and parents as referred to in Article 10 letter c, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as part of family members.

# CHAPTER VI

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutions

#### Article 14

1. In order to provide services and protection to women and children from violence, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center as a service center for women and children victims of violence.  
2. The Integrated Service Center as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of elements of the Office of Population Control Family Planning Women's Protection and Child Protection, Health Office, Education and Culture Office, Social Office, Population Administration and Civil Registration Office, Community and Village Empowerment Office, Civil Service Police Unit, Regional General Hospital, Police Resort, Non-Governmental Organizations, and Higher Education Institutions.  
3. The Integrated Service Center as referred to in paragraph (2), is established by Regent's Decree.  
4. Further provisions regarding the organizational structure of the Integrated Service Center are regulated by Regent's Regulation.

#### Article 15

1. In providing Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence, the Integrated Service Center is obliged to:  
a. provide services as quickly as possible and free of charge to Victims;  
b. implement Protection and fulfillment of victims' rights to Medical Rehabilitation, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, social reintegration and legal assistance;  
c. cooperate with certain institutions in providing translators and volunteer assistants needed for Victims;  
d. network with government or private hospitals for treatment and health recovery of Victims and cooperate with witness and Victim protection institutions, social protection houses or trauma centers owned by the government, community, or other institutions for the recovery of Victims' health;  
e. provide convenience, comfort, and safety for Victims;  
f. maintain confidentiality of Victims;  
g. provide economic empowerment;  
h. provide fulfillment of legal assistance for Victims;  
i. in the event that integrated service implementation is carried out in a networked manner, the Integrated Service Center remains responsible for the entire referral process of services required by Victims.  
2. The provision of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with Minimum Service Standards.

## Part Two

Minimum Service Standards

#### Article 16

Minimum Service Standards for the Field of Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence, include services for:  
a. handling complaints/reports of Victims of Violence against Women and Children;  
b. health services for Women and Children Victims of Violence;  
c. law enforcement and legal assistance for Women and Children Victims of Violence;  
d. repatriation for Women and Children Victims of Violence;  
e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medicolegal for Women and Children Victims of Violence;  
f. psychological services for Women and Children Victims of Violence;

#### Article 17

Minimum Service Standards as referred to in Article 16 have performance indicators including:  
a. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers in integrated service units;  
b. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at Community Health Centers and Hospitals;  
c. coverage of Social Rehabilitation services provided by trained Social Rehabilitation officers for Women and Children Victims of Violence in integrated service units;  
d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for Women and Children Victims of Violence in integrated service units;  
e. coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of Violence against Women and Children;  
f. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive legal assistance services;  
g. coverage of Repatriation services for Women and Children Victims of Violence; and  
h. coverage of social reintegration services for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

## Part Three

Forms and Mechanisms of Prevention

#### Article 18

Integrated Service CenterForms of prevention of violence against women and children carried out by the Integrated Service Center, may be implemented through:  
a. socialization activities of laws and regulations to the community related to the protection of the rights of women and children; and  
b. training of Integrated Service Center members related to the implementation of their duties in preventing violence against children and women.

#### Article 19

1. The Integrated Service Center in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with the Sub-district Integrated Service Center, and competent parties in making efforts to prevent violence against women and children.  
2. Further provisions regarding the mechanism for implementing prevention duties as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Four

Forms and Mechanisms of Services

#### Article 20

1. Forms of services provided to women and children victims of violence, are as follows:  
a. complaint services;  
b. health services;  
c. legal assistance;  
d. repatriation;  
e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medicolegal;  
f. psychological services; and  
g. economic empowerment.  
2. Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in accordance with minimum service standards established by the government and implemented by regional apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:  
a. social affairs;  
b. health;  
c. women's empowerment and child protection; and  
d. mental and spiritual.  
3. In carrying out duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (2), the regional government cooperates with government agencies, provincial government, other district/city governments, communities, families and parents.  
4. Further provisions regarding procedures for services, and handling of women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 21

1. In carrying out service duties for women and children victims of violence, the Integrated Service Center coordinates with the Sub-district Integrated Service Center.  
2. The mechanism for implementing duties as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be organized according to Standard Operating Procedures which are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Five

Assistance

#### Article 22

1. Assistance is carried out by persons and/or institutions that cooperate with the Integrated Service Center.  
2. The mechanism for assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) which are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Six

Principles of Services and Assistance

#### Article 23

The provision of services and assistance to victims, is carried out with the principles of:  
a. fast, safe, and empathetic;  
b. guarantee of confidentiality;  
c. easily accessible; and  
d. free of charge.

# CHAPTER VII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 24

1. The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.  
2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:  
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;  
b. technical guidance and training;  
c. provision of facilities;  
d. monitoring; and  
e. evaluation.  
3. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out with the principles of:  
a. professional;  
b. transparent; and  
c. accountable.

#### Article 25

Guidance and supervision as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), are carried out in order to realize the objectives of implementing protection for women and children victims of violence in accordance with minimum service standards established by laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## REPORTING

#### Article 26

1. The Integrated Service Center reports the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence to the Regent.  
2. Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be submitted in writing, including:  
a. administration;  
b. finance;  
c. services; and  
d. performance.  
3. Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be carried out at least once every 3 (three) months.

# CHAPTER IX

## FUNDING

#### Article 27

Funds for the implementation of protection of women and children victims of violence, are sourced from:  
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and  
b. other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 28

1. The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or non-governmental organizations and universities that implement protection for women and children victims of violence.  
2. Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be in accordance with regional financial capacity and implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

# CHAPTER X

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF KONAWE KEPULAUAN REGENCY

NUMBER 7 YEAR CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Every citizen has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and has the right to obtain security and freedom from all forms of violence. Violence is any act against a person that results in suffering or pain physically, sexually, psychologically, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.

Women and children are groups vulnerable to experiencing violence. All forms of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and crimes against human dignity, therefore legal protection is needed to protect their dignity and to guarantee their right to life in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

This view is based on Article 28G paragraph (1) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia which states that "Every person has the right to protection of self, family, honor, dignity, and property under their control, and has the right to security and protection from the threat of fear to do or not do something which is a human right".

The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement efforts to protect women and children victims of violence through implementing protection policies for women and children victims of violence applied by the government, establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence, establishing PPT, ensuring -2- the implementation of service facilities for victims, seeking effectiveness and efficiency for the victim recovery process, and seeking the creation of cooperation and coordination in victim recovery efforts.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Konawe Kepulauan Regency so as to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of services, assistance, guidance, supervision, reporting and funding for women and children victims of violence in Konawe Kepulauan Regency.
