# IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

REGENT OF NORTH LOMBOK

PROVINCE OF WEST NUSA TENGGARA

# REGIONAL REGULATION OF NORTH LOMBOK REGENCY

NUMBER 1 OF 2017

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD.

REGENT OF NORTH LOMBOK,

Considering:
 
 
 
 
a. that to realize the constitutional rights of every citizen in accordance with the principle of equality before the law, the Regional Government needs to strive to guarantee the protection of human rights for Women and Children;
b. that Women and Children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience Violence, so they need to receive optimal Protection;
c. that Protection as referred to in letter b, is included in mandatory affairs which are the duties, authorities and responsibilities of the Regional Government and its prevention must involve all parties;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b, and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation and Protection of Women and Children.

In View of:

1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1999 Number 165, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3886);
3. Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 109, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4235) as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 297, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5606);
4. Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419);
5. Law Number 26 of 2008 concerning the Establishment of North Lombok Regency in the Province of West Nusa Tenggara (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2008 Number 99, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4872);
6. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);
7. Government Regulation Number 4 of 2006 concerning the Implementation and Cooperation for the Recovery of Victims of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2006 Number 15, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5121);

With Mutual Approval

REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF NORTH LOMBOK REGENCY

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Part One

Definitions

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Region is North Lombok Regency;
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration;
3. Regent is the Regent of North Lombok;
4. Regional Apparatus Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is the Regional Apparatus Work Unit within the Government of North Lombok Regency;
5. Implementation of Protection is all actions and service efforts to guarantee and protect the rights of Women and Children organized by the Regional Government and the Community;
6. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a Child who is still in the womb;
7. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by Professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery;
8. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by Assistants during the service process;
9. Assistant is a person or representative from an Institution who has the expertise to provide Assistance/social worker who has professional competence in their field;
10. Institution is a Community non-governmental organization (NGO) that provides Assistance;
11. Supervision is a systematic monitoring process of the entire Implementation of Protection of legal aid for Children and Women;
12. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations;
13. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the annual financial plan of the Region established by Regional Regulation;
14. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is a service for empowerment and Protection of Women and Children established by the Government that is Community-based;
15. Violence is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully;
16. Women victims of Violence are Women who experience actions that result in misery or suffering physically, sexually, economically or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty arbitrarily, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life;
17. Child victims of Violence are Children who experience actions that result in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically or neglect that threatens life, body and degrades human dignity;
18. Children in emergency situations referred to are Children who become refugees, Children victims of riots and unrest, Children victims of disasters and Children in conflict situations;
19. Children in disaster situations are natural disasters, fire disasters and unrest;
20. Complaint handling is a series of actions carried out by integrated service providers to follow up on reports of acts of Violence against Women and Children submitted by victims, families or the Community;
21. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can perform their social functions normally;
22. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or Community that can provide Protection and fulfillment of needs for victims;
23. Integrated service unit or abbreviated as UPT is a unified unit that performs the function of integrated services for Women and Children victims of Violence;
24. Safe house is a temporary shelter that aims to provide Protection and a sense of security to victims of Violence;
25. Child Protection Institution or abbreviated as LPA is an Independent Institution in the field of Child Protection and fulfillment of Children's rights that is Community-based;
26. Integrated Service Center of North Lombok Regency or abbreviated as PPT KLU is a form of integrated service delivery based on community health centers to handle victims of Violence against Women and Children which includes medical, medicolegal, and psychosocial services.

## Part Two

Principles and Objectives

#### Article 2

1. Protection of Women and Children shall be carried out with due regard to religion, customs, social culture of the Community by prioritizing the basic principles of Women's rights and Children's rights.  
2. The basic principles of Protection of Women's and Children's rights as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. non-discrimination;  
b. the best interests of Women and Children;  
c. the right to life, survival, and development;  
d. respect for the views of the Child;  
e. legal certainty (Protection of victims' rights);  
f. local wisdom;  
g. justice and gender equality;  
h. transparency;  
i. accountability; and  
j. sustainability.

#### Article 3

The Implementation of Protection of Women and Children aims to:  
a. provide guidance to the Regency Government in planning policies and strategies for the Protection of Women and Children;  
b. prevent all forms of Violence against Women and Children and violations of Women's and Children's rights;  
c. protect and provide services to Women and Child Victims of Violence;  
d. strengthen the protective environment for Women and Children from all forms of Violence, exploitation, maltreatment and neglect;  
e. enhance empowerment of Women and Children; and.  
f. increase the role of government institutions, business entities, media and/or NGOs in the Region.

# CHAPTER II

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

General

#### Article 5

The Regional Government, Community, family, and parents have obligations and responsibilities for the efforts to Implement Protection of Women and Children.

## Part Two

Obligations and Responsibilities of the Regional Government

#### Article 6

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to:  
a. respect and guarantee the human rights of every Woman and Child without distinction as to ethnicity, religion, race, class, sex, ethnicity, culture, and language, legal status of the Child, birth order of the Child, and physical and/or mental condition;  
b. provide support for facilities and infrastructure in the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children, including providing breastfeeding facilities for Children, playgrounds, recreational facilities, shelters, and establishing integrated service centers for the Protection of Women and Children;  
c. supervise the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;  
d. ensure the availability of a safe and comfortable environment for the optimal growth and development of Children;  
e. allocate budget to Institutions that have programs in the field of Protection of Women and Children;  
f. coordinate programs, budgets and services that ensure ease of service to victims;  
g. formulate guidelines for the implementation of Protection of Women and Children;  
h. provide services for Women and Child Victims of Violence;  
i. monitor, supervise and evaluate the Implementation of Protection and Assistance for Women and Children;  
j. prevent, reduce risks, and carry out handling of all forms of Violence, exploitation, maltreatment, Children in conflict with the law and Children in emergency situations;  
k. strengthen family and Community-based child care;  
l. increase the participation of Children in policy-making that affects them, including in family life, education and the Community; and  
m. increase Community participation in the Protection of Women and Children including prevention, risk reduction and handling.  
2. The provision of facilities and infrastructure as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, is for the fulfillment of service standards;  
3. The provision of facilities and infrastructure by the Regional Government shall be carried out in stages adjusted to the financial capacity of the Region.

## Part Three

Obligations and Responsibilities of the Community

#### Article 7

The Community has obligations and responsibilities for the Protection of Women and Children through Community participation activities in the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children in the form of:  
a. providing information and/or reporting any occurrence of vulnerability risks and Violence that comes to their knowledge;  
b. facilitating deliberation and consensus to achieve restorative justice for Children in conflict with the law through informal channels and carried out at the village/sub-district government level and institutional authorities;  
c. identifying/early detection and initial handling of Children at risk;  
d. mediation and guidance for families experiencing domestic Violence, domestic conflict, and divorce;  
e. preparation of families and the Community in receiving Children who are victims of maltreatment and/or Children at risk; and  
f. the process of repatriation, social Rehabilitation, and social Reintegration.

## Part Four

Obligations and Responsibilities of Family and Parents

#### Article 8

1. family and parents have obligations and responsibilities toward Children in the form of:  
a. caring for, maintaining, educating, and protecting Children;  
b. preventing early marriage;  
c. ensuring the continuity of the Child's education according to the Child's ability, talent and interest; and  
d. reporting every birth of a Child to the authorized agency for birth registration.  
2. In the event that parents are absent or their whereabouts are unknown or for any reason, cannot carry out their obligations and responsibilities, then the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) may be transferred to the family, which shall be carried out in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER III

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

General

#### Article 9

Every Woman and Child has basic rights as a human being that must be protected, promoted, upheld and fulfilled in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 10

Every Woman has the right to:  
a. live, maintain life and improve their standard of living;  
b. live peacefully, safely, peacefully, happily, prosperously physically and spiritually;  
c. enjoy a good and healthy living environment;  
d. live in a family within a lawful marriage bond based on the provisions of laws and regulations;  
e. obtain work according to ability, conditions and fair and just wages;  
f. specifically for Women with disabilities, the right to obtain facilities and special treatment in public services;  
g. play an active role in politics and government in accordance with their potential and capabilities;  
h. obtain legal information and services;  
i. obtain services to increase their income; and  
j. obtain other rights in accordance with their human dignity and based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 11

Every Child has the right to:  
a. obtain education and teaching according to age level, physical and mental condition, intelligence and interests and talents;  
b. live, grow, develop and participate reasonably in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive Protection from acts of Violence, exploitation, discrimination and neglect;  
c. have their needs fulfilled by the family;  
d. receive religious guidance;  
e. obtain their identity; and  
f. obtain other rights in accordance with their human dignity and based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 12

The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to facilitate efforts to Protect, promote, uphold and fulfill the rights of Women and Children according to their authority based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two

Implementation of Protection of Women and Children

#### Article 13

The Implementation of Protection of Women and Children includes:  
a. prevention and Protection;  
b. Handling, recovery of victims of Violence;  
c. Rehabilitation, Reintegration and empowerment;  
d. law enforcement services and legal aid;  
e. facilitation of juvenile justice; and  
f. cultivating a gender-just social system.

### Paragraf 1

Prevention and Protection

#### Pasal 14

1. Related SKPD responsible for the Protection of Women and Children shall organize prevention and Protection in the form of:  
a. dissemination of information;  
b. developing data centers;  
c. organizing education on the prevention of Violence against Women and Children;  
d. developing child-friendly school models;  
e. encouraging increased participation of Children in preventing Violence against Children as well as socialization and promotion of Children's rights through the establishment of Children's Forums/Children's Councils.  
f. preparing a code of ethics for educators and orphanage officers as well as parties involved in handling Children;  
g. providing guidelines or SOPs on handling Violence against Children;  
h. preparing guidelines for the implementation of recovery and social Reintegration for child victims of Violence;  
i. preparing guidelines for the role of the Community in providing recovery and Reintegration services;  
j. involving the participation of the Community in recovery and Reintegration services for child victims of Violence;  
k. raising awareness about safe sex, the dangers of cigarettes, alcoholic beverages, narcotics and psychotropic abuse, child marriage, pornography, pornographic acts and HIV/AIDS to families, the Community and Children;  
l. increasing the capacity of families and Child care Institutions regarding Child Protection and child care skills.  m. increasing the capacity of all Child Protection services;  
n. guarantee of continuity of character-based education in accordance with the 12 (twelve) year compulsory education program;  
o. fulfillment of the right to legal identity for Children and Women including birth certificates and marriage certificates;  
p. guarantee of obtaining standard health services;  
q. guarantee of the right of Children to receive exclusive breastfeeding with the obligation of public spaces to provide breastfeeding corners;  
r. guarantee of Protection and fulfillment of Children's rights from the womb and Women's rights;  
s. fulfillment of Children's needs according to the age of the Child;  
t. Assistance and reproductive and psychological health services for adolescent Children and Women;  
u. development of Marriage Age Maturation Programs;  
v. strengthening the function of Institutions or non-governmental organizations engaged in Child empowerment; and  
w. encouraging and facilitating the development of Child-Friendly Regencies.  
2. Implementation of prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) may cooperate with Educational Institutions, Religious Institutions, Media, Business Entities and/or NGOs.

#### Pasal 15

1. Everyone must protect Women and Children from the threat of danger of Violence against Women and Children.  
2. Every person or Institution is prohibited from falsifying data/documents that result in Violence against Women and Children and/or non-fulfillment of Children's rights.

### Paragraf 2

Handling, Recovery of Victims of Violence

#### Pasal 16

1. Related SKPD responsible for social affairs, health, and Protection of Women and Children, shall organize Handling and Recovery in the form of:  
a. providing Assistance in legal processes;  
b. conducting mediation and/or dispute resolution;  
c. accompanying referrals, psychological and medical recovery;  
d. accompanying the legal identity process for Women and Children; and  
e. conducting counseling.  
2. Implementation of Handling and Recovery as referred to in paragraph (1) may cooperate with the Police, Hospitals, Integrated Service Centers, Legal Aid Institutions, Business Entities and/or NGOs.

### Paragraf 3

Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment

#### Pasal 17

1. Related SKPD responsible for social affairs and Protection of Women and Children shall organize Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment in the form of:  
a. Assistance for victims of Women and Children;  
b. provision of safe houses;  
c. healing or recovery;  
d. return to family and Community;  
e. facilitating the development of interests and talents for Women and Children;  
f. developing economic enterprises for adult Women;  
g. developing creative spaces for Children; and  
h. conducting Supervision, monitoring and evaluation of the Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment process.  
2. Implementation of Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) letters a through d may cooperate with educational institutions, Rehabilitation centers, safe houses, private institutions and/or NGOs.  
3. The mechanism for Organizing Protection against Violence against Women and Children shall be organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) and Service Standards (SP) which will be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

### Paragraf 4

Legal Aid

#### Pasal 18

1. Legal aid services are intended to provide Assistance and Protection to Women and Children victims of Violence with a gender-responsive approach so that victims and perpetrators obtain justice and certainty of their legal rights.  
2. Types of legal aid services include:  
a. litigation legal aid; and  
b. non-litigation legal aid.  
3. The Regional Government shall organize legal aid in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
4. In providing legal aid as referred to in paragraph (3), the Regional Government cooperates with advocate organizations in the Region.

### Paragraf 5

Handling of Children in Conflict with the Law

#### Pasal 19

1. The Regional Government plays a role in facilitating the handling of Children in conflict with the law.  
2. handling of Children in conflict with the law as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. capacity building of government apparatus, social workers and the Community;  
b. increased coordination with law enforcement officials;  
c. provision of supporting facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of the juvenile criminal justice system;  
d. development of effective methods through information support, facilities and positive activities as well as taking immediate action on gaps that encourage Children and/or groups of Children to commit legal violations;  
e. provision and delivery of legal and psychosocial assistance services in realizing restorative justice and diversion processes for Children in conflict with the law;  
f. encouraging paradigm shifts in the Community towards Children in conflict with the law; and  
g. developing diversion mechanisms for Children in conflict with the law in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
3. Related SKPD responsible for social affairs and education shall organize social Rehabilitation and education for Children in conflict with the law in the form of:  
a. provision of safe houses;  
b. psychosocial Rehabilitation;  
c. spiritual mental Rehabilitation;  
d. fulfillment of the right to education; and  
e. social Reintegration.

### Paragraf 6

Cultivating a Gender-Just Social System

#### Pasal 20

1. Cultivating a gender-just social system includes:  
a. gender-based planning, budgeting and development systems;  
b. gender-based human resource capacity building; and  
c. gender-based political rights.  
2. The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in the form of gender mainstreaming in every policy and Community life.

#### Pasal 21

Further provisions regarding the Organization of Protection of Women and Children shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

# BAB IV

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Bagian Kesatu

General

#### Pasal 22

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect Women and Children from acts of Violence that occur in the form of:  
a. physical Violence;  
b. sexual Violence;  
c. economic Violence;  
d. social Violence;  
e. psychological Violence;  
f. domestic neglect;  
g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and h. threats of certain actions.

#### Article 23

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of Violence as referred to in Article 22 shall undertake efforts to:  
a. prevent the continuation of criminal acts;  
b. provide Protection to victims of acts of Violence;  
c. provide emergency assistance; and/or  
d. assist in the process of submitting an application for the determination of Protection.

## Part Two

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 24

1. Regional Government shall prevent Violence against Women and Children by:  
a. socializing laws and regulations;  
b. providing counseling/guidance;  
c. providing education on the dangers of domestic Violence;  
d. conducting seminars/workshops or the like;  
e. establishing work networks in efforts to prevent Violence;  
f. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of Violence prevention based on partnership patterns;  
g. establishing a Violence prevention system, mapping of locations or areas prone to the occurrence of Violence; and h. increasing public awareness in behaving in accordance with religious norms and morality.  
2. In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be implemented by:  
a. family and/or closest relatives;  
b. the Community;  
c. educational institutions;  
d. health institutions;  
e. social community institutions; and  
f. business sector and other institutions.

#### Article 25

1. Regional Government is obliged to prepare prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan.  
2. Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

## Part Three

Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 26

1. Regional Government shall implement Integrated Services for Women and Children victims of acts of Violence through activities:  
a. complaint services;  
b. health services;  
c. counseling;  
d. spiritual guidance;  
e. social Rehabilitation services;  
f. legal aid and Assistance services; and/or  
g. repatriation and Reintegration.  
2. Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:  
a. special service rooms within the police force;  
b. expert and professional personnel;  
c. service centers and safe houses; and/or  
d. other facilities and infrastructure necessary for victim recovery.  
3. Implementation of Services for victims of acts of Violence shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A and other social institutions.  
4. P2TP2A and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner.  
5. Integrated Services for victims of acts of Violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (4) shall be implemented in accordance with Standard Operating Procedures.

#### Article 27

Procedures and procedures for the Protection of Women and Children victims of Violence shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

STRENGTHENING AND DEVELOPMENT OF INSTITUTIONS PROVIDING PROTECTION SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 28

In the context of Protection of Women and Children, the following shall be established in the Region:  
a. Integrated Service Center, in the form of Integrated Service Center for Women Empowerment and Child Protection (P2TP2A);  
b. Child Protection Institution (LPA); and  
c. other institutions according to needs and/or provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 29

1. P2TP2A as referred to in Article 28 letter a functions as a coordinative institution among Regional Government, law enforcement apparatus, service institutions, educational institutions, Legal Aid Institutions, Individuals, Private Sector and/or NGOs, which have capacity in the Protection of Women and Children.  
2. The duties of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. providing Services, including:  
1\. preparing and developing information related to Women and Children and coordinating throughout the Regency and reporting to the Province/Province of West Nusa Tenggara;  
2\. developing a central database obtained from the Region regarding the condition of Violence against Women and Children;  
3\. receiving complaints both in writing and orally;  
4\. providing Services, mediation and Assistance to victims of Violence against Women and Children;  
5\. conducting education/training; and  
6\. coordinating with related institutions/individuals both in other Regencies/Cities, Provincial Government in the context of providing Protection to Women and Children.  
b. providing Protection to victims of acts of Violence, reporters and witnesses.  
3. Appointment of P2TP2A management as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by Regent Decree.  
4. P2TP2A shall be under the coordination of the SKPD or work unit handling women empowerment and Child Protection.

#### Article 30

1. In order to increase the effectiveness of the Implementation of Child Protection, the Regent shall establish LPA as referred to in Article 28 letter b which is independent in nature.  
2. LPA as referred to in paragraph (1) shall have the duties to:  
a. conduct Supervision of the implementation of Protection and fulfillment of Children's rights;  
b. provide input and proposals in the formulation of policies regarding the Implementation of Child Protection;  
c. collect data and information regarding Child Protection;  
d. receive and conduct examination of public complaints regarding violations of Children's rights;  
e. conduct mediation on disputes regarding violations of Children's rights;  
f. conduct cooperation with community institutions, Government and NGOs in the form of work networks, for the fulfillment and Protection of Children's rights; and  
g. conduct handling activities for cases of violations and neglect of Children's rights.

# CHAPTER VI

## PARTICIPATION

## Part One

Community Participation

#### Article 31

1. The Community may participate in the Protection of Women and Children.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out in the form of:  
a. assisting the Regional Government in the form of Supervision of Protection of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children;  
b. reporting to the competent authorities if they know of Women and Children who experience Violence; and  
c. participating in the process of prevention, recovery, Rehabilitation, Reintegration and Empowerment of victims.

## Part Two

Child Participation

#### Article 32

1. Every Child has the right to participate in expressing opinions, ideas, thoughts and others and to be involved in decision-making processes concerning their lives either directly or indirectly, which is implemented with the consent and will of all Children based on awareness and understanding, in accordance with age and level of maturity of thinking.  
2. Child Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out through Child Forums/Child Councils starting from the Village, Sub-district, and Regency levels.  
3. Child Forums/Child Councils as referred to in paragraph (2) represent Children, both representation of Children's geographical domicile, Children's socio-cultural groups, Children with special needs and Children's educational backgrounds.  
4. Establishment of Child Forums/Child Councils as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be facilitated by the Regional Government.  
5. Establishment of Child Forums/Child Councils as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be determined by Regent Decree.

#### Article 33

Funding sources for Child Forums/Child Councils may come from:  
a. donations from the Community/Business Entities that are non-binding in nature;  
b. assistance from the Regional Government; and/or  
c. other funding sources in accordance with laws and regulations.

#### Article 34

1. In every formulation of policies related to Children, the Regional Government shall consider and accommodate the opinions of Children conveyed through the Children's Forum/Children's Council.  
2. The Regional Government may include representatives of the Children's Forum/Children's Council in the Regional Development Planning Deliberation.

# CHAPTER VII

2. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: organizing technical guidance and training; and providing facilities for the fulfillment of Service Standards.  
3. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted to realize the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children in accordance with Service Standards implemented by P2TP2A and Institutions that implement services for the fulfillment and Protection of Women and Children.

#### Article 36

1. Supervision as referred to in Article 35 paragraph (1) includes: conducting monitoring and evaluation of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children; and receiving and following up on reports.

# CHAPTER VIII

## REPORTING

#### Article 37

1. The SKPD that carries out the duties and functions in the field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection shall submit a report to the Regent regarding the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children in the Region.  
2. The report as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be submitted at least 1 (one) time per year.

# CHAPTER IX

## FINANCING

#### Article 38

Financing for the implementation of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children shall be charged to the APBD and other lawful and non-binding sources of funds.

Administrative Sanctions

1. 2. The mechanism for imposing sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide Protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. That Women and Children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience Violence and therefore need to receive Protection. Violence against Women and Children constitutes a violation of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed without discrimination.  

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, Violence against Women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the Child.  

The existence of Women and Child Victims of Violence has not yet received adequate Services, therefore minimum Services for victims of Violence are required. In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in North Lombok Regency so as to avoid Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to carry out the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children in the form of a Regional Regulation. This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Child Victims of Violence, particularly in terms of Services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and Supervision.
