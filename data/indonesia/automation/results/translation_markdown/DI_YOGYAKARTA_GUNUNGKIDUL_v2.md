Considering:  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
a. that the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence has been stipulated by Regional Regulation of Gunungkidul Regency Number 25 of 2012;  
b. that in accordance with social developments and in order to optimize the protection of women and children victims of violence, Regional Regulation Number 25 of 2012 needs to be amended and adjusted;  
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a and letter b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children from Violence;  

In View of:  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;  
2. Law Number 15 of 1950 concerning the Establishment of Regency Regions within the Special Region of Jogjakarta (State Gazette of 1950 Number 44);  
3. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 224, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended, most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2015 (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);

Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 109, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3143) as last amended by Law Number 17 of 2016 (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2016 Number 237, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5946);

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:  
1. Women are persons of female gender.  
2. Children are persons who have not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including children still in the womb.  
3. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in direct lineage upward or downward up to the third degree.  
4. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.  
5. Violence against Women is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.  
6. Violence against Children is any act that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, exploitation, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty, and other violence.  
7. Victims are women and children who experience misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.  
8. Child Victims of Violence are children who experience violence and children in conflict with the law.  
9. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  
10. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
11. Special Protection is protection given to children in emergency situations, children in conflict with the law, children from minority and isolated groups, children who are exploited economically and/or sexually, children who are trafficked, children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse (drugs), children victims of kidnapping, sale, trafficking, children victims of violence both physical and/or mental, children with disabilities, and children victims of maltreatment and neglect.  
12. Community is individuals, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.  
13. Social Rehabilitation is services aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.  
14. Services are immediate activities and actions taken to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing of the impending, ongoing or completed occurrence of violence against women and children carried out by professionals in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.  
15. Counseling for Victims is the provision of assistance by an expert or trained person in such a way as to increase psychological understanding and ability in solving the problems faced.  
16. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and or spiritual guidance, for the self-strengthening of victims of violence to resolve the problems faced.  
17. Technical Implementation Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is a technical implementation unit of the agency established by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.  
18. Technical Implementation Unit of the Women and Child Protection Agency hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a technical implementation unit of the agency established by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.  
19. Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner and established at the regency, Kapanewon/sub-district, and Kalurahan/village levels.  
20. Safe House is a temporary residence or shelter for victims, provided to provide protection during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers of perpetrators.  
21. Government Agency is a collective term covering work units/organizational units of ministries/departments, Non-Departmental Government Institutions, secretariats of state high institutions, and other government agencies, both central and regional, including State-Owned Enterprises, State-Owned Legal Entities, and Business Entities.  
22. Region is Gunungkidul Regency.
23. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration.  
24. Regent is the Regent of Gunungkidul.  
25. The Office of Women's Empowerment, Child Protection, and Family Planning, Community and Village Empowerment of Gunungkidul Regency hereinafter referred to as the Office, is the implementing element of government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 2

The protection of women and children aims to:  
a. ensure the fulfillment of the rights of women and children so that they can participate optimally in society in accordance with human dignity and dignity;  
b. provide protection and provide a sense of security to women and children from all forms of violence and discrimination;  
c. prevent and eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children including trafficking in persons;  
d. provide services to women and children victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses;  
e. restore, strengthen the physical and mental condition of women and children victims of violence; and  
f. increase awareness and community participation in preventing violence against women and children.

# CHAPTER II

## AUTHORITY OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 3

1. The Regent has the authority to provide protection to women and children from violence.  
(2) The authority as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:a. prevention of violence;  
b. services for victims of violence; and  
c. strengthening and institutional development.

#### Article 4

1. In exercising the authority as referred to in Article 3, the Regent establishes programs and activities for the protection of women and children in the Regional Action Plan.  
2. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (1) shall at least contain:  
a. programs and activities for the protection of women and children from violence;  
b. programs and activities for regional action to prevent all forms of violence and discrimination against women and children;  
c. programs and activities for action to improve and enhance protection services for women and children from violence; and  
d. programs and activities for action to empower women from violence.  
3. Provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER III

## PREVENTION OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Types of Violence

#### Article 5

Types of violence include:  
a. physical violence;  
b. psychological violence;  
c. sexual violence;  
d. neglect;  
e. exploitation; and/orf. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is an act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c includes:  
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;  
b. forced sexual intercourse;  
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or  
d. forced sexual intercourse with another person for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d includes:  
a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after children as they should be, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or  
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is:  
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  
b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues;  
c. utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/or  
d. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f namely:  
a. threats of violence include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and  
b. coercion, includes a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

## Part Two

Prevention Efforts

#### Article 12

Prevention of violence as referred to in Article 3 paragraph (2) letter a is carried out by:  
a. Regional Government;  
b. family;  
c. Community;  
d. mass media; and  
e. business sector.

#### Article 13

1. Violence prevention efforts carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 12 letter a include:  
a. socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection of women and children;  
b. education about the dangers of domestic violence;  
c. mapping of violence-prone areas;  
d. compilation of data and information about women and children vulnerable to violence; and/or  
e. counseling/mental and spiritual guidance.  
3. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are coordinated by the Agency.

#### Article 14

1. Families and Communities carry out violence prevention efforts independently according to their needs and capabilities.  
2. The Regional Government provides support and facilitation for violence prevention efforts carried out by the community.

# CHAPTER IV

PROVISION OF SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Rights of Victims of Violence

#### Article 15

Women and children victims of violence are entitled to receive:  
a. Protection;  
b. information;  
c. excellent service;  
d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation and social reintegration stage;  
e. confidential handling;  
f. psychological and legal assistance; and  
g. guarantee of rights related to status as family members and community members.

#### Article 16

In addition to receiving rights as referred to in Article 15, child victims of violence also receive special rights in the form of:  
a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth, and development;  
b. the right to basic services;  
c. the right to equal protection;  
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and  
e. the right to obtain freedom in accordance with the law.

#### Article 17

1. In the event that victims of violence require continuous handling, they have the right to stay in a Safe House or alternative residence.  
2. Continuous handling as referred to in paragraph (1) is handling at the Social Rehabilitation stage carried out by individuals, groups or Government and non-Government institutions.  
3. Safe House as referred to in paragraph (1) provides a temporary safe place and/or shelter for victims of violence until their rights are restored.

#### Article 18

1. The Regional Government provides a Safe House in the Region.  
2. The operation and/or management of the Safe House as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Agency.

#### Article 19

1. In the event that the Regional Government has not yet provided a Safe House as referred to in Article 18 paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with third parties that have a Safe House.  
2. Cooperation with third parties as referred to in paragraph (1) is further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Handling of Victims of Violence

#### Article 20

1. Handling provided to women and children victims of violence in the form of services, includes:  
a. complaints;  
b. health;  
c. legal aid;  
d. Social Rehabilitation;  
e. medicolegal;  
f. psychological; and  
g. assistance.  
2. Services as referred to in paragraph (1) are provided with:   
a. fast and accurate;  
b. safe and comfortable;  
c. a sense of empathy;  
d. non-discrimination;  
e. easily accessible;  
f. free of charge; and  
g. guaranteed confidentiality

#### Article 21

Complaint services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter a include:  
a. initial identification or recording of victims; and  
b. consent for action to be taken.

#### Article 22

1. Health Services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter b include:  
a. first aid to victims;  
b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics; and  
c. referral to health facilities in accordance with professional standards;  
2. In the event that women and children victims of violence require treatment, health workers are obliged to provide recovery services and health Social Rehabilitation.  
3. The provision of recovery services and health Social Rehabilitation as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 23

1. Legal services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter c to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by:  
a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;  
b. accompanying victims at the reporting, investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and assisting victims to fully describe the violence they have experienced; and  
c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 24

1. Social Rehabilitation Services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter d, among others:  
a. motivation and psychosocial diagnosis;  
b. care and nurturing;  
c. vocational training and entrepreneurship development;  
d. spiritual mental guidance;  
e. physical guidance;  
f. social guidance and psychosocial counseling;  
g. accessibility services;  
h. social assistance and aid;  
i. resocialization guidance;  
j. follow-up guidance; and/or  
k. referral.  
2. Forms of vocational training and entrepreneurship development as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, among others:  
a. skills training;  
b. field work practice;  
c. apprenticeship;  
d. entrepreneurial skills training;  
e. facilitation of joint business group formation;  
f. business capital in the form of business support tools; and/or  
g. business implementation assistance.

#### Article 25

1. Medicolegal services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter e constitute medical services to provide optimal professional assistance in utilizing medical science for the purposes of law enforcement and justice.  
2. Medicolegal services as referred to in paragraph (1) among others:  
a. visum et repertum; and  
b. visum et psikiatrikum.

#### Article 26

1. Psychological services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter f constitute an action taken to restore the psyche of women and children as victims of violence.  
2. Psychological services as referred to in paragraph (1) among others:  
a. conducting psychological evaluations for women and child victims of violence referred by the police using forensic interviews with various techniques;  
b. conducting individual psychological counseling for women and child victims of violence with psychological disorders related to the violence they have experienced;  
c. implementing family psychological counseling for women and child victims of violence;  
d. conducting individual psychotherapy for women and child victims of violence with psychological disorders related to the violence they have experienced;  
e. conducting group psychotherapy for women and child victims of violence with psychological disorders related to the violence they have experienced;  
f. providing psychological assistance for children in conflict with the law; and  
g. providing testimony as a witness of fact in all levels of the legal process (Official Report of Examination, case review and trial)
3. In addition to psychological services as referred to in paragraph (1), psychological services may be in the form of spiritual guidance.  
4. Spiritual counselors as referred to in paragraph (3) provide services to women and child victims of violence in the form of providing counseling to strengthen and provide a sense of security.

#### Article 27

1. Assistance services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter g are carried out by individuals or institutions that have expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.  
2. Assistance services as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;  
b. accompanying victims during the medicolegal process;  
c. accompanying victims during the mediation, examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;  
d. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;  
e. maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including coverage by the mass media;  
f. coordinating with other companions; and/or  
g. providing continuous handling up to the Social Rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 28

Provisions regarding the handling of victims of violence as referred to in Article 20 are further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

### UPT PPA

#### Article 29

1. The Regional Government may establish UPT PPA to provide services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, require special protection, and other problems in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.  
2. UPT PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) is positioned under and is responsible to the Agency.

#### Article 30

1. UPT PPA in carrying out its duties as referred to in Article 29 performs service functions among others:  
a. public complaints;  
b. victim outreach;  
c. case management;  
d. temporary shelter;  
e. mediation; and/or  
f. victim assistance.  
2. UPT PPA provides services as referred to in paragraph (1) based on minimum service standards that have been established by laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER V

## INSTITUTIONAL STRENGTHENING AND DEVELOPMENT

## Part One

Institutional Strengthening

#### Article 31

1. In implementing violence prevention and services to women and child victims of violence, the Agency may cooperate with related regional apparatus/institutions.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in the form of FPKK.  
3. Related regional apparatus/institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) among others regional apparatus/institutions whose duties and functions are in the fields of:  
a. education;  
b. employment;  
c. health;  
d. social affairs;  
e. women's empowerment and child protection;  
f. mental and spiritual;  
g. peace and order;  
h. population and civil registration;  
i. law; and  
j. cooperatives and MSMEs.

## Part Two

Institutional Development

#### Article 32

1. The Regional Government optimizes and facilitates FPKK in prevention and services to women and child victims of violence.  
2. FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. Regency FPKK;  
b. Kapanewon FPKK; and  
c. Kalurahan FPKK.

#### Article 33

1. The Regent establishes the Regency FPKK.  
2. The establishment of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Regent Decree.

#### Article 34

The Regency FPKK as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (1) letter a has duties among others:  
a. coordinating and synchronizing efforts to provide protection to women and children from violence carried out by members;  
b. building, maintaining, and developing networks and referral systems;  
c. communicating, socializing, and educating the community to actively participate in the protection of women and children from violence;  
d. conducting monitoring and evaluation;  
e. coordinating the implementation of handling services for women and child victims of violence at the Regency level;  
f. collecting and disseminating data and information related to the provision of protection to women and children from violence;  
g. conducting supervision, guidance, and assistance to Kapanewon FPKK and/or Kalurahan FPKK;  
h. receiving referrals from Kapanewon FPKK and/or Kalurahan FPKK; and/or  
i. reporting implementation to the Regent periodically.

#### Article 35

Elements of the Regency FPKK as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter a include:  
a. Government Agencies; and  
b. community institutions.

#### Article 36

1. Government Agencies as referred to in Article 35 letter a shall at minimum include Government Agencies which among others have duties and functions in the fields of:  
a. social affairs;  
b. handling of women and children;  
c. health;  
d. education;  
e. youth and sports;  
f. religion;  
g. population;  
h. manpower; and/or  
i. industry and trade.  
2. Community institutions as referred to in Article 35 letter b are grouped based on their field of activity, among others:  
a. health field;  
b. psychology field;  
c. legal field;  
d. social field; and/or  
e. economic field.

#### Article 37

1. The Panewu shall establish the FPKK Kapanewon.  
2. The establishment of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Decree of the Panewu.

#### Article 38

The FPKK Kapanewon as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter b has duties among others:  
a. implementing and coordinating the handling of women and children victims of violence within the scope of Kapanewon;  
b. receiving referrals from FPKK Kalurahan; and/or  
c. conducting documentation of case handling in accordance with the mechanisms and service standards that have been established.

#### Article 39

The FPKK Kapanewon as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter b shall at minimum consist of elements of:  
a. health;  
b. education;  
c. law;  
d. psychosocial;  
e. community;  
f. spirituality;  
g. children's forum; and/or  
h. empowerment.

#### Article 40

1. The FPKK Kalurahan shall be established by the Lurah.  
2. The establishment of FPKK Kalurahan as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Decree of the Lurah.  
3. In the event that the FPKK Kalurahan as referred to in paragraph (1) has not yet been established, the FPKK Kabupaten and/or FPKK Kapanewon may provide facilitation and assistance.

#### Article 41

The FPKK Kalurahan as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter c shall at minimum consist of elements of:  
a. health;  
b. education;  
c. law;  
d. community leaders;  
e. religious leaders;  
f. children's forum/youth organization;  
g. Kalurahan officials; and/or  
h. other related organizations/institutions.

#### Article 42

The FPKK Kalurahan as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter c has duties among others:    
a. preparing programs and activities in efforts to prevent and handle violence against women and children within the scope of Kalurahan; and/or  
b. referring cases and coordinating with FPKK Kapanewon or FPKK Kabupaten in the event that FPKK Kalurahan experiences obstacles related to case handling.

# CHAPTER VI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 43

1. The community may participate in the implementation of protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) among others:  
a. establishing family partners at the kalurahan level;  
b. establishing community institutions whose field of work is in the field of protection of women and children;  
c. conducting socialization of the rights of women and children independently;  
d. conducting prevention efforts if finding indications of violence against women and children in the community;  
e. providing first aid to women and children victims of violence; and  
f. reporting to the competent authority if violence against women and children occurs in their environment.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, community institutions, educational institutions, religious institutions, and the business sector.

#### Article 44

1. The Regional Government shall involve the business sector and the private sector to participate in the protection of women and children.  
2. Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:  
a. socialization to employees in the business environment regarding the rights of women and children;  
b. socialization to employees in the business environment regarding the dangers of acts of violence against women and children;  
c. providing assistance in the implementation of protection of women and children as a form of corporate social responsibility.

# CHAPTER VII

## FUNDING

#### Article 45

In order to support the implementation of protection for women and children victims of acts of violence, funding is required which may be sourced from:  
a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBD);  
b. Kalurahan Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBKal); and  
c. other lawful and non-binding sources in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 46

1. The Regent shall conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by the Agency.  
3. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted in order to realize the objectives of implementing protection of women and children victims of violence in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
4. Guidance and supervision shall be implemented at least (two) times a year.

# CHAPTER IX

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, the provisions of laws and regulations governing the Establishment, Organizational Structure, Duties, Functions, and Work Procedures by Technical Implementers of Protection of Women and Children are declared to remain in force insofar as they are not contrary to the provisions in this Regional Regulation or are not specifically regulated in this Regional Regulation.  
2. At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force, the FPKK that has been established is declared to remain in force insofar as it is not contrary to the provisions in this Regional Regulation or is not specifically regulated in this Regional Regulation.

#### Article 48

At the time this Regional Regulation comes into force:  
a. Regional Regulation of Gunungkidul Regency Number 25 of 2012 concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence (Regional Gazette of Gunungkidul Regency Year 2012 Number 25); and

are revoked and declared invalid.

#### Article 49

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

##### I. GENERAL

That women and children as vulnerable groups in family and community life often experience problems that violate human rights, that protection of women and children is a mandatory concurrent affair which is the authority, obligation, and responsibility of the Regional Government, so that regulation is needed as a basis for implementing comprehensive and integrated protection of women and children in a Regional Regulation.  

In this regard, protection efforts in the Region are needed in the form of institutional support and regulations that can guarantee its implementation. This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts specifically in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children from violence in Gunungkidul Regency.
