# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Gorontalo Regency.
2. Regent is the Regent of Gorontalo.
3. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous Region.
4. Regional Governance is the administration of government affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.
6. Woman is a person who is female.
7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.
8. Violence against women is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.
9. Violence against children is any act that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.
10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly.
11. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, court, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.
12. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
13. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.
14. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to accompany victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
15. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the Region to provide services in the context of empowerment of women and children victims of violence.
16. Safe House is a shelter for victims, during the accompaniment process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and danger from perpetrators.
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
18. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, foster care, and within that household.
19. Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner.

#### Article 2

Protection of Victims is based on:
a. respect and fulfillment of the rights of victims;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. the best interests of victims; and
e. empowerment.

#### Article 3

Protection of Victims aims to:
a. prevent violence against women and children;
b. provide services to Victims; and
c. empower women victims of violence.

#### Article 4

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes efforts to prevent, provide services and empower victims of violence in the Region.

# CHAPTER II

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence include:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. exploitation; and/or
f. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:
a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
c. acts of neglecting people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. acts with or without the victim's consent that include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:
a. threats of violence which include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and
b. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER III

## VICTIM'S RIGHTS

#### Article 12

Every Victim obtains the right:  
a. to have their dignity and worth as a human being respected;  
b. health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;  
c. to determine their own decisions;  
d. to obtain information;  
e. confidentiality of their identity;  
f. compensation;  
g. social rehabilitation;  
h. complaint handling; 
i. to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or  
j. assistance.

#### Article 13

Child victims of violence in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 12, also obtain special rights, consisting of:  
a. respect for survival, growth and development;  
b. basic services;  
c. equal protection;  
d. freedom from various stigma; and/or  
e. obtaining freedom.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

Regional Government

#### Article 14

1. The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to implement efforts to protect women and children victims of violence through:  
a. policy determination;  
b. preparation of program and activity planning;  
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure; and  
e. allocating budget;  
f. establishment of a Children's forum, and  
g. establishment of a Task Force.  
2. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government organizes services for victims in the form of:  
a. facilitating the establishment of P2TP2A;  
b. facilitating P2TP2A facilities and infrastructure according to the Region's capacity; and  
c. facilitating the Task Force as a network forum for handling Victims.

#### Article 15

The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to:  
a. supervise the implementation of services to victims, with minimum service standards;  and  
b. provide budget for victim protection through the APBD and/or other lawful Regional financial sources.

## Part Two

Community and Family

#### Article 16

The community and family have obligations and responsibilities which include:  
a. preventing the occurrence of violence against women and children;  
b. reporting if violence occurs;  
c. protecting victims; and  
d. providing emergency assistance.

# CHAPTER V

## P2TP2A

#### Article 17

1. P2TP2A is established by the Regional Government and/or community institutions.  
2. The organizational structure of P2TP2A established by the regional government as referred to in paragraph (1), is established according to needs.

#### Article 18

1. The Gorontalo Regency Government provides a Safe House as a form of service and protection for victims of violence through P2TP2A.  
2. The safe house referred to in article 1 (one) is equipped with facilities and infrastructure.

#### Article 19

1. The organizational structure of P2TP2A established by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) consists of:  
a. chairman or other equivalent designation;  
b. general vice chairman;  
c. secretary;  
d. treasurer;  
e. executive chairman; and  
f. executive vice chairman.  
2. The establishment, structure, and duties of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) are stipulated by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 20

1. P2TP2A established by the Regional Government must have at least three counselors.  
2. Counselors as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. counselor in the field of law;  
b. counselor in the field of health; and  
c. counselor in the field of psychology;  
d. counselor in other fields.  
3. The implementation of counseling carried out by counselors can be implemented in a networked manner as follows:  
a. counseling in the field of law involving the Police, Prosecutor's Office, District Court, Religious Court, Regional Government Legal Division, Legal Advocacy Institutions and Community Organizations;  
b. counseling in the field of health involving the Health Office, Regional General Hospital and other health services;  
c. counseling in the field of psychology involving psychological assistants and/or psychologists.

# CHAPTER VI

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Prevention

#### Article 21

1. Efforts to prevent violence against women and children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the regional apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.  
2. Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:  
a. forming a working network in efforts to prevent violence;  
b. the working network referred to in point 1 (one) is:  
1. task force on violence against women and children;  
2. Bele lomongoditi moli;  
3. Children's Forum;  
4. Berlian (Bersama Lindungi Anak); and  
5. Suami sayang istri;  
c. forming a violence prevention system;  
d. conducting socialization about laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence; and  
e. providing critical education about the rights of women and children for the community.   
3. The establishment of Institutions and Procedures for implementing the duties of the working network as referred to in paragraph (2) will be regulated by Regent's Regulation.

#### Article 22

In addition to the Regional Government, prevention efforts as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (1) are carried out by:  
a. family and/or closest relatives;  
b. community;  
c. educational institutions; and  
d. Non-Governmental Organizations.

# CHAPTER VII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 39

1. The community participates in the implementation of protection of Victims.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of:

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF GORONTALO REGENCY

NUMBER 4 OF 2018 CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN AGAINST ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### I. GENERAL  

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.  

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Gorontalo Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.  

So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee their implementation.  

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in Gorontalo Regency.
