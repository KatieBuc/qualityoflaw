# IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

GOVERNMENT OF SUMENEP REGENCY

NUMBER 7 OF 2011

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF SUMENEP

Considering:


a. that the number of violence against women and children in Sumenep Regency is still high while services and protection for women and children in Sumenep Regency have not been carried out optimally;
b. that in the framework of implementing regional autonomy, the regulation and service to the community, especially for women and children victims of violence, is the duty and function of the regency government because violence against women and children is a violation of Human Rights;
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a and b, it is necessary to establish the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Regional Regulation of Sumenep Regency.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  
1. Regional Government is the Government of Sumenep Regency;  
2. Regent is the Regent of Sumenep;  
3. Regional Apparatus is an organization/institution within the Regional Government that is responsible to the Regional Head in the context of government administration consisting of the Regional Secretariat, Regional Agencies and Regional Technical Institutions, Sub-districts and Civil Service Police Units according to regional needs;  
4. Implementation is a form of implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence;  
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb;  
6. Violence is any unlawful act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim;  
7. Violence against Women is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life;  
8. Violence against children is any act that results in or may result in discrimination, exploitation, whether economic or sexual, neglect, cruelty, abuse, injustice and other mistreatment;  
9. Physical Violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causes death;  
10. Sexual Violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes;  
11. Economic violence is any act that neglects family members in the form of not providing proper livelihood, care or maintenance;  
12. Psychological Violence is any act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person;  
13. Victim is a woman or child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence;  
14. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecution, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred;  
15. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination;  
16. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a service center that has the duties and functions of coordinating with related agencies in the formulation of technical policies and implementation in the field of prevention and eradication as well as elimination of acts of violence against children and women, implementation of prevention and eradication of acts of violence against women and children in accordance with applicable laws and regulations as well as implementation of policies and prevention of problems in the field;  
17. Social Community Institution is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and/or other community organizations;  
18. Population is Indonesian citizens and foreigners who enter legally and reside in the territory of Indonesia.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out based on respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity, non-discrimination, gender equality and justice as well as protection of the human rights of women and children and the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

The implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to provide protection and services to women against gender-based violence and the best interests of children that occur in the household or public.

# CHAPTER III

## INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND

## CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 4

1. The Regent through the Regional Apparatus establishes a community service work unit that functions as P2TP2A.  
2. The Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1) in implementing the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence may coordinate with other Regional Apparatus.

# CHAPTER IV

## HANDLING AND MECHANISM

#### Article 5

1. The implementation of Protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the P2TP2A framework.  
2. P2TP2A in handling medical, legal, medicolegal, psychological and economic protection in its implementation may partner with social community institutions engaged in the protection of women and children.  
3. The implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) must take into account religious norms as well as the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands/other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.  
4. The mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE AND PROTECTION

#### Article 6

1. Forms of violence against Women and Children include physical, sexual, economic and psychological.  
2. Forms of physical violence are any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causes death.  
3. Forms of sexual violence are sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  
4. Forms of economic violence are neglect of family members in the form of not providing proper livelihood, care or maintenance.  
5. Forms of psychological violence are acts that cause fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 7

1. Every woman and child who becomes a victim of violence must receive protection in accordance with applicable provisions.  
2. Protection of women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out for residents of Sumenep Regency whether committed in Sumenep or outside Sumenep.  
3. Protection of women and children victims of violence in addition to being carried out for residents of Sumenep as referred to in paragraph (2), also for non-residents of Sumenep committed within the territory of Sumenep Regency.

#### Article 8

1. Forms of protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence include medical, legal, medicolegal (forensic medicine), economic and psychological protection.  
2. The form of medical protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel free of charge.  
3. The form of legal protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.  
4. The form of medicolegal protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field.  
5. The form of economic protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of services for skills and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.  
6. The form of psychological protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of assistance in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in resolving problems.

# CHAPTER IX

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 13

Matters not yet regulated in this Regional Regulation insofar as concerning its implementation shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.
