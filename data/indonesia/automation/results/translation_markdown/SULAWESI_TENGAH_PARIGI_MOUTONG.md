# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:
1. Region is the Regency Region of Parigi Moutong.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of Parigi Moutong.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child who is still in the womb.
6. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and organizations and/or community organizations.
7. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in direct lineage upward or downward up to the third degree.
8. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty;
9. Violence against Women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
10. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child
11. Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of Violence.
12. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, court, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that Violence against Women will or has occurred;
13. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from Violence and discrimination.
14. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
15. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the Victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that Violence against the Victim will, is or has occurred.
16. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to accompany Victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of Violence Victims.
17. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for strengthening Violence Victims to resolve the problems they face.
18. Repatriation is an effort to return Women and Child Victims of Violence to the family, or community that can provide protection and fulfill their needs.
19. Counseling is a process of providing objective and complete information, carried out systematically with interpersonal communication guidance, counseling techniques and mastery of clinical knowledge aimed at helping a person recognize their current condition, the problems being faced, and determine solutions or efforts to overcome these problems.
20. Rehabilitation is the recovery of Victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
21. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
22. Restitution is the payment of compensation charged to the perpetrator based on a court decision that has permanent legal force for material and/or immaterial losses suffered by the victim or their heirs.
23. Technical Implementation Unit of the Women and Child Protection Service, hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is the Technical Implementation Unit of the Women and Child Protection Service of Parigi Moutong Regency.
24. Integrated Service Center for Women and Child Empowerment, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service provider institution for Victims established by the Regional Government.
25. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM are performance benchmarks for integrated service units in providing complaint handling services, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal assistance as well as repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Child Victims of Violence.
26. Social Welfare Implementation Institution hereinafter referred to as LPKS is an institution or social service place that carries out social welfare administration for Children in conflict with the law, established by the ministry of social affairs.
27. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to provide protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

# CHAPTER II

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 2

Women and child victims of violence receive rights, including:
a. the right to be respected for their dignity as human beings;
b. the right to recovery;
c. the right to determine their own decisions;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidentiality;
f. the right to social rehabilitation;
g. the right to complaint handling that is fast, appropriate, comfortable and according to needs;
h. the right of Victims and their families to obtain facilities in the judicial process;
1. the right to Assistance;
J. the right to security; and
k. the right to restitution.

#### Article 3

Child victims of violence in addition to receiving rights as referred to in Article 4, also receive special rights, including:
a. the right to survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic population services;
c. the right to equal protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and
e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER III

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 4

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of violence that occur in public or private life in the form of:
a. physical violence;
b. sexual violence;
c. economic violence;
d. social violence;
e. psychological violence;
f. domestic neglect;
g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and
h. threats of certain actions.

#### Article 5

Every person who hears, sees or knows of the occurrence of Violence as referred to in Article 4 shall make efforts to:
a. prevent the continuation of criminal acts;
b. provide protection to victims;
c. provide emergency assistance; and
d. assist in the complaint process and submission of applications for protection orders.

## Part Two

Prevention of Violence

#### Article 6

The Regional Government shall prevent acts of violence against women by:  
a. socializing laws and regulations;  
b. empowering and raising awareness among families, parents and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling;  
c. making efforts to increase the quantity and quality of education, both formal and non-formal and informal;  
d. making efforts to build community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of women and children from violence in an integrated community-based manner;  
e. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities, religious institutions/organizations and various non-governmental organizations in efforts to prevent violence against women and children;  
f. establishing a violence prevention system, mapping locations or areas prone to violence;  
g. raising public awareness that respects women and protects children;  
h. establishing UPTD PPA in every Sub-district, and Women and Children Protection Service Posts at the Village and Sub-district levels; and  
i. establishing a women and children protection task force.  
2. In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be implemented by:a. family and/or closest relatives;  
b. community;  
c. vertical agencies;  
d. law enforcement apparatus:e. educational institutions;  
f. health institutions;  
g. religious institutions/organizations;  
h. community social institutions;  
1. business sector; and  
j. other institutions.  
3. Prevention of acts of violence against women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus having the main duties and functions in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 7

1. The Regional Government is obliged to prepare prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 6 in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan.  
2. The programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

## Part Three

Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 8

1. The Regional Government shall implement integrated services for victims of acts of violence, through activities:  
a. complaint services;  
b. health services;  
c. counseling;  
d. psychological services;  
e. spiritual guidance;  
f. social rehabilitation services;  
g. legal assistance and support services;  
h. safe house services; andy ori. repatriation and social reintegration.  
2. In the event that the Regional Government does not yet have counseling personnel as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, it may request assistance from social workers, volunteer companions, and/or spiritual counselors.  
3. Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:  
a. special service rooms within the police force;  
b. expert and professional personnel;  
c. service centers and Safe Houses; and /orod. other facilities and infrastructure necessary for the recovery of victims including perpetrators.  
4. The provision of services to victims of acts of violence shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with other social institutions.  
5. Other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (4) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner.  
6. Integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (5) shall be implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures.  
7. Provisions regarding standard operating procedures as referred to in paragraph (6) shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 9

1. Complaint services as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (1) letter a shall be implemented to receive reports of acts of violence.  
2. Reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may originate from:  
a. victims;  
b. referrals; orc. outreach.

#### Article 10

1. Health services as referred to in Article 8 paragraph - (1) letter b, shall be implemented using health facilities owned by the Regional Government or other referral hospitals by providing medical treatment and health recovery services to victims.  
2. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be performed by health personnel to victims in accordance with professional standards, standard operating procedures and the medical needs of victims.  
3. Health services as referred to in paragraph (1), through efforts to:  
a. take anamnesis from victims;  
b. examine victims;  
c. provide first aid;  
d. restore health both physical and psychological, make efforts to prevent disease and other health disorders;  
e. provide counseling;  
f. refer to more adequate health service facilities if necessary, may refer to other parties with a multidisciplinary approach, including health services, medicolegal, psychosocial support and legal assistance; and/org. provide information to the police.  
4. In addition to the efforts as referred to in paragraph (3) for certain cases, health personnel may provide reproductive health services in accordance with medical needs.  
5. In providing services as referred to in paragraphs (3) and (4), health personnel must create medical records in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations,  
6. For every medical action to be performed by health personnel, there must be informed consent for medical action from the victim or their family in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
7. For investigation purposes, authorized health personnel must prepare a visum et repertum and/or visum et repertum psichiatricum. or prepare a medical certificate.  
8. Health personnel are entitled to legal protection.

#### Article 11

Legal assistance and protection services as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (1) letter g include:  
a. providing protection in a Safe House;  
b. providing legal information to victims;  
c. providing support for victims as witnesses from the investigation process to the verdict;  
d. providing special legal protection for child victims of acts of violence may be carried out by appointing guardianship in accordance with laws and regulations.

#### Article 12

1. Repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (1) letter g shall be implemented with the following principles:  
a. voluntary;  
b. safe and dignified; and  
c. respect for rights.  
2. In the event that the Regional Government is not yet able to implement repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in paragraph (1), it may request assistance from relevant parties.  
3. Social services to provide social welfare for children in conflict with the law shall be carried out by the Regional Government together with LPKS and other social institutions.  
4. LPKS and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) are rehabilitation and temporary shelter facilities for children in conflict with the law before repatriation and social reintegration are implemented.

# ELUCIDATION OF REGIONAL REGULATION OF PARIGI MOUTONG REGENCY

NUMBER 15 OF 2018 CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of Violence against Women and Children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in Parigi Moutong Regency so that they are protected from Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the form of regional regulations. So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, so institutional support and regulations are needed that can guarantee its implementation. This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of Women and Children Victims of Violence in Parigi Moutong Regency.

What is meant by "social violence" is an act by a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life to a person or may cause physical damage or damage to another person's property.

Letter e
What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

a. an act that results in the non-fulfillment of;

e. an act that results in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.
