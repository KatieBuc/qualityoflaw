NUMBER 2 OF 2022

THE REGENT OF BIREUEN,

Considering:  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

a. that women as noble and dignified beings need to be empowered in their capabilities and have the right to receive protection in accordance with Islamic Sharia;  
b. that based on Article 231 of Law Number 11 of 2006 concerning the Governance of Aceh, it states that the Regency Government is obligated to promote and protect the rights of women and children and to undertake dignified empowerment efforts;  
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a and letter b, it is necessary to establish a Qanun concerning the Protection of Women;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Qanun, what is meant by:

1. Region is Bireuen Regency.  
2. Regency is Bireuen Regency which is part of the provincial region of Aceh led by a Regent.  
3. Regency Government is the Government of Bireuen Regency as the administrator of affairs carried out by the regency government and the Regional House of Representatives of Bireuen Regency in accordance with their respective functions and authorities.  
4. Regency Government is an element of the administration of the Regency Government consisting of the Regent and regency apparatus.  
5. Regent is the Head of the Government of Bireuen Regency who is elected through a democratic process conducted based on the principles of direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair in accordance with laws and regulations.  
6. Regional House of Representatives of the Regency hereinafter abbreviated as DPRK is an element of the administration of the Government of Bireuen Regency whose members are elected through general elections in accordance with laws and regulations.  
7. Woman is a person (human being) who has reached the age of 15 years who has menstruated, is pregnant, gives birth and breastfeeds.  
8. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving just and equal relations with men.  
9. Victim is a woman who experiences threats of violence and or violence inside and or outside the household and or becomes an object of trafficking/exploitation, and or discrimination in all its forms and aspects.  
10. Threat of Violence is any act of non-physical abuse using or not using means unlawfully that can cause danger to the body, life, freedom and human rights of women.  
11. Violence is any act against a person, especially women, which results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully, whether inside or outside the household.  
12. Discrimination Against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status on the basis of equality between men and women.  
13. Women's Rights are all rights possessed by every woman which are part of human rights.  
14. Special Protection is protection provided to women and children in emergency situations, women and children in conflict with the law, women and children from minority and isolated groups, women and children who are exploited economically and/or sexually, women and children who are trafficked, women and children who are victims of kidnapping, sale, trafficking, women and children victims of violence both physical and/or mental.  
15. Community is individuals, families, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.  
16. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.  
17. Labor Force is every person who is capable of performing work to produce goods and/or services either to meet their own needs or for the community.  
18. Worker/Laborer is every person who works by receiving wages or remuneration in other forms.  
19. Person with Disability is every person who experiences physical, intellectual, mental, and/or sensory limitations in the long term who in interacting with the environment may experience obstacles and difficulties to participate fully and effectively with other citizens based on equal rights.  
20. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will, is or has occurred.  
21. Rehabilitation is a series of actions carried out by authorized parties in their field to restore the physical, mental and psychosocial condition of women to their original condition.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of women is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect for the values of Islamic sharia and Acehnese customs that are responsive to women;  
b. legal certainty;  
c. public interest;  
d. respect for human rights;  
e. justice and gender equality;  
f. non-discrimination;  
g. professionalism, accountability and transparency; and
h. protection and fulfillment of victims' rights.

#### Article 3

Protection of women has the objectives to:

a. strengthen regional policy and budget support in order to optimize the implementation of women's protection;  
b. clarify the commitment of policy makers both at the regency, sub-district and gampong levels in the framework of increasing efforts and guarantees of protection for women;  
c. improve coordination, integration, synchronization, and synergy between SKPK with all stakeholders including religious organizations, professional organizations, academics, the private sector and the wider community in efforts to improve the quality of women's protection;  
d. build public knowledge and awareness to support efforts to protect women;  
e. increase the institutional capacity and human resources of UPTD PPA;  
f. provide guarantees to women to be able to fulfill their rights as human beings in all aspects of life;  
g. provide a sense of security to women in all aspects of life; and
h. reduce the rate of violence against women.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 4

The scope of regulation in this Qanun includes:

a. principles and objectives;  
b. women's rights;  
c. protection of women;  
d. obligations and responsibilities of the Regency Government;  
e. support of gampong government;  
f. strategies and mechanisms for implementing women's protection;  
g. community participation; and
h. awards.

# CHAPTER IV

## WOMEN'S RIGHTS

## Part One

General

#### Article 5

1. Every woman has the right to:  
a. live and maintain her life, and improve the level of welfare in her life;  
b. practice religious teachings in accordance with Islamic sharia;  
c. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;  
d. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;  
e. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, form a family and continue offspring;  
f. obtain fair and equal treatment in carrying out her roles and functions both in family life, society and participate fully in every process of making, determining and implementing development policies;  
g. obtain education and teaching at all types, levels, and pathways of education, without any obstacles and pressure in order to improve her skills and quality of life;  
h. obtain information and knowledge for women about health, especially reproductive health comprehensively; and i. obtain special protection in carrying out her work or profession against matters that threaten safety and/or health related to reproductive function.  
2. Every woman in her capacity as a wife, while in a marriage bond has the same rights and responsibilities as her husband over all matters relating to her married life, relations with her children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.  
3. In addition to the rights as referred to in paragraph (2), women as wives have the right to receive material and spiritual support from their husbands.  
4. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities from her former husband over all matters relating to children, taking into account the best interests of the child.

## Part Two

Rights of Women with Disabilities

#### Article 6

Every woman with disabilities has the right to obtain:  
a. education at all units, pathways, types, and levels of education;  
b. work and decent livelihood in accordance with the type and degree of disability, education, and ability;  

c. equal treatment to play a role in development and enjoy its results;  
d. accessibility in the context of independence;  
e. rehabilitation, social assistance, and maintenance of social welfare standards;  
f. equal rights to develop talents, abilities, and social life; and
g. other rights as regulated in laws and regulations.

## Part Three

Rights of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 7

Every woman victim of violence has the right to obtain:

a. respect for dignity and worth as a human being;  
b. health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;  
c. determine her own decision in handling the violence case she experienced;  
d. obtain information;  
e. obtain guarantee of confidentiality;  
f. social rehabilitation;  
g. complaint handling;  
h. obtain facilitation in the judicial process;  
   i. assistance; and
j. obtain empowerment after her case is resolved.

## Part Four

Special Treatment

#### Article 8

1. Every woman who is in a condition of disability, old age, and/or pregnancy, has the right to obtain facilities and special treatment related to access to public services.  
2. Every elderly woman, person with physical disability and/or mental disability, has the right to obtain care, education, training, and special assistance at the expense of the Regency Government to ensure a decent life in accordance with human dignity.  
3. Every woman who is entangled with narcotics either as a perpetrator or victim has the right to receive fair treatment both in the law enforcement process and the rehabilitation (recovery) process.

# CHAPTER V

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

Implementation of Protection of Women

#### Article 9

Implementation of protection of women is provided to:

a. women as housewives;  
b. women heads of household;  
c. women domestic workers;  
d. women workers/laborers;  
e. women with disabilities;  
f. women homeless;  
g. women former residents of correctional institutions;  
h. women victims of violence;  
i. women victims of conflict; and
j. elderly women.

### Paragraph 1

Women as Housewives

#### Article 10

Implementation of protection for women as housewives as referred to in Article 9 letter a, in the following matters:  
a. material and spiritual support;  
b. the head of the family provides special protection to housewives during pregnancy; and
c. housewives who have given birth must receive protection from the head of the family to reduce the burden as a housewife.

#### Article 11

Housewives who work outside the home must receive protection from the head of the family in the form of giving permission and being able to provide domestic workers.

### Paragraph 2

Women Heads of Household

#### Article 12

1. Women heads of household as referred to in Article 9 letter b, are widows whose husbands have died and as a result of divorce must receive protection.  
2. Implementation of protection for women heads of household as referred to in paragraph (1) in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, negative stigma and economic empowerment for women heads of household.  
3. Women as heads of household as referred to in paragraph (2) receive protection rights over:  
a. discriminatory actions and efforts to weaken the function of women as economic supporters of the family;  
b. strengthening capacity and recognition as heads of household in their environment; and
c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.  
4. Implementation of protection for women as heads of household whose husbands have died as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. women as heads of household obtain 1/8 (one eighth) of the total remaining assets of their husbands if they have children; and b. women as heads of household obtain 1/4 (one quarter) of the total remaining assets of their husbands if they do not have children.  
5. Implementation of protection for women as heads of household as a result of divorce as referred to in paragraph (1) during the iddah period, the former husband is responsible for providing residence and support.

### Paragraph 3

Women Domestic Workers

#### Article 13

1. Implementation of protection for women domestic workers as referred to in Article 9 letter c, in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, cruel treatment, inhumane, degrading human dignity, and exploitation in employment relationships.  
2. Women domestic workers as referred to in paragraph (1) must receive protection from employers in the form of:  
a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;  
b. health and social security; and
c. guarantee not to discriminate, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation.

### Paragraph 4

Women Workers/Laborers

#### Article 14

1. Implementation of protection for women workers/laborers as referred to in Article 9 letter d, to provide protection from economic exploitation and cruel, inhumane treatment and seek proper appreciation for work.  
2. Women workers/laborers as referred to in paragraph (1) must receive protection from employers in the form of:  
a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;  
b. health and social security; and
c. guarantee not to discriminate, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation.

### Paragraph 5

Women with Disabilities

#### Article 15

1. The implementation of protection for women with disabilities as referred to in Article 9 letter e, in order to provide protection and prevention, against forms of discrimination including freedom from cruel, inhuman treatment, degrading human dignity, and freedom from exploitation.  
2. Women with disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection over:  
a.guarantee of continuity and personal self-development;  
b.accessibility of information, legal aid, mobility, social services; and c. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation for the improvement of their function and capacity.

### Paragraph 6

Homeless Women

#### Article 16

1. The implementation of protection for homeless women as referred to in Article 9 letter f, in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.  
2. Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection over:  
a. decent housing;  
b. health and social security;  
c. population administration registration;  
d. opportunities to obtain education and skills to improve economic status; and
e. access to information and legal consultation services.

### Paragraph 7

Women Former Inmates of Correctional Institutions

#### Article 17

1. The implementation of protection for women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in Article 9 letter g, in order to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting to life in society, so that they can carry out life normally.  
2. Women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection over:  
a. social rehabilitation and reintegration; and
b. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

### Paragraph 8

Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 18

1. The implementation of protection for women victims of violence as referred to in Article 9 letter h, in order to provide legal protection and/or health recovery.  
2. Protection for women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. legal aid;  
b. assistance after the occurrence of acts of violence;  
c. social rehabilitation and reintegration; and
d. health services.

#### Article 19

1. Every victim as referred to in Article 18 has the right to receive protection, information, integrated services, continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage and confidential handling from individuals, groups or institutions both the Regency Government and the community.  
2. In the event of violence, every victim has the right to receive health services and assistance, both psychologically and legally and to receive guarantees of their rights related to their status as women.

### Paragraph 9

Women Victims of Conflict

#### Article 20

The implementation of protection for women victims of conflict as referred to in Article 9 letter i, through:  
a. providing a sense of security;  
b. fulfillment of basic needs of women victims of conflict;  
c. eliminating trauma; and
d. providing services needed for victims.

### Paragraph 10

Elderly Women

#### Article 21

1. The implementation of protection for elderly women as referred to in Article 9 letter j, in order to carry out their social functions and play an active role naturally in their social life.  
2. Protection of elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) is prioritized for protection from their children and heirs.  
3. If elderly women do not receive services from their children and heirs and/or do not have children and other heirs as referred to in paragraph (2), then the protection of elderly women becomes the responsibility of the Regency Government.  
4. Elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection over:  
a. basic health services to maintain and improve health status including gerontological/geriatric services,  
b. use of facilities and accessibility of public facilities and infrastructure; and c. government administration services.

# CHAPTER VII

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## OF THE REGENCY GOVERNMENT

## Part One

General

#### Article 22

1. The Regency Government has obligations and responsibilities for the protection of women.  
2. Efforts to protect women as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;  
b.determination of implementation guidelines;  
c. provision of services; and
d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.  
3. Protection of women as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with laws and regulations.

#### Article 23

1. In implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and empowerment of women victims, the Regency Government may cooperate with the community/community institutions.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 24

The Regent in implementing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women by making efforts to:

a. coordinate the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women among regency apparatus;  
b. cooperate with Regencies/Cities within one Province, and cooperation between Regencies/Cities in other Provinces in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
d. strengthen institutional capacity for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women;  
e. facilitate services for the protection of women;  
f. provide services for the protection of women;  
g. implement affirmative action for the protection of women; and
h. prepare a data system for the protection of women.

#### Article 25

1. The Regency Government has obligations and responsibilities to carry out efforts to prevent violence against women in the form of:  
a. collecting data and information about victims of violence and laws and regulations;  
b. conducting education on anti-violence values against women;  
c. socializing laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for victims of violence;  
d. fostering support from religious leaders and traditional leaders to build community awareness of the importance of protecting women;  
e. monitoring and evaluating the implementation of protection for victims of violence; and
f. preparing reports on the implementation of protection for women victims of violence.  
2. To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence against women, the Regency Government is obliged to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:  
a. establishing institutions and facilitating the implementation of integrated services for victims by involving community elements; and b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims.  
3. To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women, the Regency Government makes efforts as follows:  
a. conducting socialization regarding regulations and policies concerning violence against women for gampong apparatus, traditional leaders and religious leaders;  
b. providing counseling to immediately report to local authorities if violence against women occurs;  
c. forming a network in efforts to prevent violence; and d. other efforts deemed strategic to prevent the occurrence of violence against women.

#### Article 26

1. The district government is obligated to provide social assistance for women experiencing social problems and disaster victims. (2) The district government must provide the right to social security for poor women and women with limited abilities

## Part Two

Women's Protection Institution

#### Article 27

1. The District Government establishes a women's protection institution.  
2. The women's protection institution as referred to in paragraph (1) also handles protection of children.  
3. The women's protection institution as referred to in paragraph (2) is implemented by establishing UPTD PPA.  
4. The District Government must guarantee the availability of adequate facilities and infrastructure, human resources, and budget to realize the operations of UPTD PPA.  
5. Further provisions regarding UPTD PPA are regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

## GAMPONG GOVERNMENT SUPPORT

#### Article 28

1. The Gampong Government supports women's protection efforts by:  
a. issuing gampong-level regulations on women's protection;  
b. socializing regulations related to women's empowerment and protection to the community;  
c. encouraging the realization of a gampong environment that is friendly to women;  
d. building community awareness including customary leaders and religious leaders at the gampong level to care about issues of violence against women;  
e. allocating budget to support women's empowerment and protection programs; and
f. building a system for handling violence against women at the gampong level.  
2. The efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e, above can be developed according to the financial capacity of the gampong, problems and needs of women.

# CHAPTER IX

## STRATEGY AND MECHANISM FOR WOMEN'S PROTECTION

## Part One

General

#### Article 29

1. The Regent integrates policies, programs, and activities for women's protection into district development planning.  
2. The integration of policies, programs, and activities for women's protection must be incorporated into planning and budget documents which include the Regional Long-Term Development Plan (RPJPD), Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD), District Work Unit Strategic Plan (Renstra-SKPK), District Government Work Plan (RKPK), and District Work Unit Work Plan and Budget (RKA-SKPK).  
3. The District Work Unit whose duties and functions handle women's empowerment affairs facilitates the integration of policies, programs, and activities for women's protection into the preparation of planning documents; in paragraph (2).  
4. In carrying out the duties and paragraph (3), the work unit that handles and budget as contained in its functions as referred to in coordinates with the Agency (BAPPEDA).

## Part Two
Strategy

#### Article 30

(1) Women's protection is implemented by integrating Gender Mainstreaming (PUG) and disaggregated databases, gender data and violence data in an integrated manner based on applicable laws and regulations.  

(2) The District Work Unit that handles women's empowerment and protection coordinates with other Regional Apparatus Organizations in order to realize the implementation of women's protection in an integrated manner.  

(3) Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2), is carried out within the framework of strengthening synergy in policy formulation, program and activity support, as well as monitoring and evaluation.

## Part Three

Mechanism

#### Article 31

The mechanism for implementing women's protection is carried out through stages:

a. policy formulation of program and activity planning;  
b. budgeting of programs and activities;  
c. implementation of programs and activities; and
d. monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

#### Article 32

1. Policy formulation of program and activity planning as referred to in Article 31 letter a, is implemented through:  
a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of disaggregated data;  
b. coordination and synchronization of program and activity planning preparation with related SKPK; and
c. determination of programs and activities in planning documents.  
2. Policy formulation of program and activity planning is further regulated in service standards and standard operating procedures.  
3. Budgeting of programs and activities as referred to in Article 31 letter b, is implemented through general budget policies incorporated in APBK.  
4. Implementation of programs and activities as referred to in Article 31 letter c, is implemented through:  
a. implementation of programs and activities contained in SKPK budget documents; and
b. coordination and synchronization of program and activity implementation with related SKPK.  
5. Implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in Article 31 letter d, is implemented through:  
a. periodic monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of programs and activities that have been carried out; and
b. making periodic reports annually submitted to the Regent.

# CHAPTER VIII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 33

1. The community can participate in women's protection.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) can be carried out by individuals and/or organizations.  
3. Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) can be carried out by:  
a. providing input and suggestions to the District Government related to policies, programs and activities for women's protection;  
b. monitoring District Government policies related to women's protection;  
c. initiating or proposing new policies that need to be established by the District Government in efforts to optimize women's protection;  
d. assisting gampong apparatus in carrying out women's protection efforts as regulated in this Qanun;  
e. conducting socialization about the elimination of violence against women;  
f. providing information and/or reporting violence against women to law enforcement institutions or authorized parties; and
g. monitoring the performance of law enforcement officials in handling cases of violence against women.

#### Article 34

To guarantee the best service for women victims of violence, the community whether individually, groups of people or institutions can coordinate with UPTD PPA.

#### Article 35

1. The District Government encourages the business world to participate in protecting women from acts of violence.  
2. Such participation is carried out both internally and externally.  
3. Internal participation as referred to in paragraph (2) can be in the form of:  
a. socialization to employees within the company environment regarding the dangers of acts of violence against women;  
b. building an internal women's system to handle issues of violence against women; and
c. providing social assistance as a form of corporate social responsibility.  
4. External participation as referred to in paragraph (2) can be in the form of:  
a. providing special opportunities for women victims of violence to work in the company;  
b. creating special education and training for women victims of violence;  
c. educational assistance for women victims of violence who have been withdrawn from education; and
d. fostering and increasing the economic independence of women victims of violence.

# CHAPTER IX

## AWARDS

#### Article 36

1. The Regency Government grants awards to State Apparatus who are deemed to have commitment and succeed in carrying out the task of protecting women.  
2. The Regency Government grants awards to Community Organizations, Private Sector and Individuals who are deemed to have made innovations and succeed in supporting efforts to protect women.  
3. Awards are also granted to Gampong Governments who are deemed to have commitment and succeed in supporting efforts to protect women.  
4. Further regulation regarding the granting of awards as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER X

## FINANCING

#### Article 37

All costs for the implementation of policy planning of programs and activities related to the protection of women are sourced from:  
a. Regency Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
b. Other sources that are legal and non-binding.

# CHAPTER XI

## GUIDANCE, MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 38

1. The Regent through the Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment affairs conducts guidance and supervision over the protection of women.  
2. The form of guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) is as follows:  
a. providing socialization to the community and business sector regarding policies on the protection of women;  
b. providing books, leaflets, brochures regarding the protection of women, and other related issues and disseminating them to the community;  
c. facilitating capacity building of institutions organizing the protection of women;  
d. facilitating forums for women's participation, women's organizations and organizations for persons with disabilities;  
e. facilitating rehabilitation and reintegration services for women victims of disasters and victims of violence;  
f. coordinating the participation of the business sector and other institutions, including international assistance for the protection of women; and g. granting awards to the community and business sector both individually and institutionally who have made efforts to protect women.

#### Article 39

1. To ensure synergy, continuity, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women, the Regency Government conducts monitoring.  
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women.  
3. Monitoring is conducted periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of regional apparatus implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women.  
4. Monitoring is conducted from planning to implementation of policies, programs and activities.

#### Article 40

1. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women is conducted at the end of each fiscal year.  
2. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women are used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women for the following year.  
3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XII

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 41
Implementing regulations of this Qanun in the form of Regent Regulations shall be stipulated no later than six (6) months from the promulgation of this Qanun.

#### Article 42

This Qanun shall come into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Qanun is ordered by placing it in the Gazette of Bireuen Regency.

##### I. GENERAL

1. That Islamic Sharia grants a high and noble position to women. Women have the same position as men as servants of Allah and dignified creatures. In the Qur'an and the Hadith of the Prophet Muhammad SAW, no teachings and activities have ever been found that lead to discrimination and exploitation of women. In fact, one of the missions of the Prophet Muhammad SAW in this world is to elevate the dignity and honor of women from a very low and despicable state.  
2. That Law Number 44 of 1999 concerning the Implementation of Special Autonomy for Aceh and Law Number 11 of 2006 concerning the Governance of Aceh have provided a foundation for the empowerment and protection of women which is an important activity because the condition of women so far has been quite concerning in terms of education, economy, health, labor, law and even threats of violence, exploitation, discrimination, pornography and pornographic acts which are often targeted at women.  
3. The obligation of the Bireuen Regency Government feels the need to have a Bireuen Regency Qanun on the Empowerment and Protection of Women, it is expected that every woman can be empowered and receive protection without discrimination and also the Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) which already exists in Bireuen Regency has a Juridical Foundation as one of the organizers of empowerment and protection of women in Bireuen Regency.
