BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF CENTRAL MALUKU,

Considering : a. that as citizens, women and children have the right to obtain protection from any act of violence committed against them which may cause victims of physical, psychological, sexual violence, or domestic neglect;

b. that protection for women and children victims of violence is an effort to provide a sense of security and freedom from all acts of violence guaranteed by the State;

c. that the government including the Regional Government, families, social organizations and or other community organizations have a responsibility to prevent acts of violence and protect women and children victims of violence in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations;

d. that the Regional Regulation on the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence requires substantial changes related to institutional matters to be adjusted to the latest regulations at the National level, and has not comprehensively regulated the efforts to implement protection that ensures the fulfillment of victims' rights to truth, justice, recovery and guarantee of non-recurrence of cases, so that a new Regional Regulation is needed that can guarantee its implementation.

e. that based on the considerations referred to in letter a, letter b, letter c and letter d, it is necessary to stipulate a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Central Maluku Regency.

2. Regional Government is the Regent along with related Regional Apparatus Organizations as elements of Regional Government administration.

3. Regent is the Regent of Central Maluku.

4. Regional People's Representative Council, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of Central Maluku Regency as an element of Regional Government administration.

5. Agency is the Agency that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

6. Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA, is an institution established by the provincial or regency/city regional government to provide services to women and children experiencing criminal and civil problems.

7. Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to provide prevention, protection, handling and fulfillment of the rights of women and children victims of violence by providing complaint services, medical services, social rehabilitation, legal aid, as well as social reintegration and repatriation.

8. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.

9. Woman is a female who has reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years or more.

10. Violence against women (and children) is any act against a person, especially women (and children), that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or economic loss.

11. Victim of violence is a woman and/or child who experiences suffering, whether physical, psychological, sexual, and/or economic loss as a result of an act of violence.

12. Prevention is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women and/or children carried out by the government, family, social institutions or other community institutions, police, prosecutors or other parties to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and/or children.

13. 16 Days of Activism Against Violence Against Women (and Children) Campaign is a campaign to encourage efforts to eliminate violence against women (and children) worldwide, which is carried out annually for 16 days from November 25 to December 10.

14. Protection of women and children is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by the government, family, social institutions or other community institutions, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties, whether temporary or based on court decisions or rulings, in connection with acts of violence against women and children.

15. Spiritual guidance services are recovery carried out through spiritual, faith and religious approaches.

16. Complaint services are a series of actions carried out by the UPT PPA organizer to follow up on reports of acts of violence against victims of violence.

17. Health rehabilitation services are the recovery of victims of violence from the health disorders they suffer.

18. Medicolegal services are medical services and operational services in the field of medicine and law, including visum examinations.

19. Social Rehabilitation Services are services aimed at restoring and developing the abilities of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.

20. Legal Aid Services are legal services provided by legal aid providers free of charge to legal aid recipients.

21. Repatriation services are efforts to return victims of violence to their area of origin or to family, substitute family or community.

22. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims of violence with family, substitute family and community who can provide protection and fulfill the needs of victims.

23. Recovery is medical/psychiatric and psychological action towards victims of violence as an effort to restore the physical and mental condition of victims to their original state.

24. Companion is a person trusted by the victim to accompany them during the criminal justice process.

25. Paralegal Assistance is legal assistance by a companion who does not have qualifications as a legal practitioner.

26. Health facilities are hospitals, community health centers, auxiliary community health centers, medical clinics, village health posts, places of practice for midwives/nurses/medical assistants/doctors.

27. Regional Action Plan is the foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of violence.

28. Safe House is temporary housing provided to provide protection to victims in accordance with predetermined operational standards.

29. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

30. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband wife and child, or father and child, or mother and child, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward or sideways up to the third degree.

31. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or step mother, or adoptive father and/or adoptive mother.

Article 2

Principles

(1) The protection of women and children is based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia as well as the principles and basic rights as regulated in the Convention on Women and Children, including:

a. respect for human rights;
b. non-discrimination;
c. the best interests of women and/or children;
d. the right to life, survival and development;
e. respect for the opinions of women and children; f. gender equality and justice; and
g. protection of victims;
(2) Legal Certainty and Local Wisdom.

Article 3

Objectives

The objectives of protection for women and children victims of violence are:

a. to ensure the fulfillment of the rights of women and/or children in accordance with applicable provisions;
b. to prevent and protect women and/or children from acts of violence;
c. to encourage the community to carry out its obligations in the prevention and protection of women and children in accordance with applicable laws and regulations; and
d. to restore victims from the consequences of violence experienced.

Article 4

Scope

The scope of implementation of protection for women and children Victims of Violence includes:

a. institutional implementation of protection for women and children;
b. duties and responsibilities for prevention, protection and recovery;
c. forms and mechanisms of prevention; and
d. provision of medical services, medicolegal services, special examination rooms, legal aid, expert personnel, psychological recovery, safe houses, social companions, clergy, customary institutional support, repatriation, social reintegration, economic empowerment and other services needed by women and children victims of violence to support the fulfillment of the rights of women and children victims of violence.

CHAPTER II

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 5

Victims have the right to receive:

a. protection from family, Regional Government, police, prosecutors, courts, legal aid institutions, social institutions or other parties either temporarily or based on the establishment of a protection order from the court;
b. special handling related to the safety and confidentiality of victims;
c. physical health, mental and psychological services according to the suffering experienced by the victim;
d. assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations;
e. spiritual guidance services; and
f. guarantee of continuation of special education for children who are victims of violence.

Article 6

In the event of an act of violence, every victim has the right to receive both psychological and legal assistance and to receive guarantees of their rights related to their status as a wife, mother, child, family member or as a member of society.

CHAPTER III

DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

PREVENTION, PROTECTION AND RECOVERY Article 7

Prevention, protection and recovery of women and children victims of violence are duties and responsibilities covering:

a. regional government;
b. the community, social organizations and/or other community organizations in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations; and
c. family, and/or parents.

Article 8

(1) The duties and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 7 letter a, include:

a. implementing protection policies for women and children from acts of violence established by the government;
b. establishing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
c. carrying out cooperation in the provision of protection for women and children victims of violence;
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence to relevant agencies both vertical and regional;
e. allocating budget for the protection of women and children victims of violence according to the Regional financial capacity; and
f. fostering and supervising the provision of protection for women and children from acts of violence.
(2) In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in a Regional action plan.
(3) The Regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (2) is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.
(4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 9

The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 7 letter b, include:

a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing protection to victims;
c. providing emergency assistance;
d. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to the competent authorities; and
e. participating in the handling of victims of violence.

Article 10

The obligations and responsibilities of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 7 letter c include:

a. preventing all forms of violence and protecting women and children as family members.
b. providing support for women and children victims of violence who are part of their family members.

CHAPTER IV

PROVISION OF PROTECTION

Part One

Institution

Article 11

(1) To coordinate the duties and responsibilities as referred to in Article 7, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Unit.

(2) The Integrated Service Unit as referred to in paragraph (1) is P2TP2A.

(3) P2TP2A as UPT PPA in the Region may take the form of a Regional Coordinator or other designation established by the Regent.

Article 12

(1) P2TP2A is located in the capital of Central Maluku Regency with a working area covering the entire area of Central Maluku Regency.

(2) P2TP2A can encourage the availability of women and children service centers at the sub-district level and/or Negeri and Administrative Negeri.

Article 13

(1) The objectives of establishing P2TP2A are:

a. facilitating the needs of women and children victims of violence in fulfilling the rights of victims, namely the right to truth, justice, recovery and guarantee of non-recurrence of cases; and
b. realizing welfare, justice and Gender equality in various fields of women's and children's lives comprehensively.
(2) The main duties of P2TP2A are:
a. conducting prevention efforts against violence towards women and children;
b. conducting handling efforts for women and children victims of violence; and
c. conducting rehabilitation efforts including recovery, empowerment, repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence.
(3) The functions of P2TP2A are:
a. as a data and information center on women and children;
b. as a service center for women and children victims of violence; and
c. as a women and children empowerment center.

Article 14

The position of P2TP2A is under the Regional Government and is responsible to the Regent.

Article 15

(1) P2TP2A consists of at least a Chairman/Head, a Vice Chairman/Vice Head, a Secretary, a Treasurer and fields in accordance with applicable Laws and Regulations.

(2) Membership of P2TP2A consists of at least representatives of relevant regional apparatus organizations within the scope of Regional Government, Police, Prosecutors, Courts, Non-Governmental Organizations, Community Leaders, Religious Leaders and Legal Aid Institutions.

(3) To be appointed as a member of the P2TP2A management must meet the requirements as regulated in applicable Laws and Regulations.

(4) The term of office of the P2TP2A management is 3 (three) years from the date of inauguration, and can subsequently be reappointed for one subsequent period.

Article 16

P2TP2A is obligated to:

a. provide services and handling as quickly as possible to victims;
b. provide ease and comfort of services for victims;
c. provide services free of charge for victims; d. maintain the safety and confidentiality of victims; and
e. strive to achieve justice and legal certainty for victims.

Part Two

Prevention

Article 17

Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children can be implemented through:

a. socialization of Laws and Regulations to the community related to the protection of women and children periodically;
b. 16 Days of Anti-Violence against women and children campaign every year;
c. integrating the issue of violence against women and children into the education curriculum in schools;
d. implementation of referral mechanisms in the handling and recovery of victims of violence against women and children; and
e. coordination between institutions that have main duties and functions related to the prevention and handling of violence against women and children.

Article 18

In addition to prevention efforts as referred to in Article 17, the Regional Government conducts awareness efforts for the community, families and parents through:

a. increasing the number and quality of education both formal, non-formal and informal;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income improvement and social services; and
c. building community participation and concern to implement prevention and towards women and children from acts of violence.

Article 19

(1) Prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 17 and Article 18, is carried out by regional apparatus organizations according to their duties and functions in the fields of:

a. women's empowerment and child protection; b. health;
c. mental and spiritual;
d. social;
e. education;
f. employment; and
g. peace and order.
(2) Prevention of acts of violence by regional apparatus work units as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented based on the Regional Action Plan.

Part Three

Services for Victims

Article 20

(1) Services for victims are organized in cooperation between related institutions/agencies, including

a. legal services in the form of legal assistance and paralegal assistance;

b. medical services;

c. medicolegal services;

d. psychological services;

e. rehabilitation services;

f. social reintegration and repatriation services; and g. support services for economic independence.

(2) Coordination of services for victims as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by P2TP2A.

Article 21

(1) In addition to the forms of services as referred to in Article 20, the Regional Government through P2TP2A is obliged to provide temporary protection services for victims including, but not limited to:

a. initial rescue of victims from acts of violence; and b. medical emergency treatment for victims.
(2) To provide protection as referred to in paragraph (1), P2TP2A may cooperate with security apparatus, Negeri and Administrative Negeri Government, kelurahan, customary leaders and or community groups that provide assistance services for women and children victims of violence

Article 22

(1) The Regent establishes minimum Service standards provided to women and children victims of violence.

(2) In addition to the minimum Service standards as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes special Minimum Service Standards for victims with Disabilities.

Article 23

(1) In addition to services for victims as referred to in Article 20, Article 21, Article 22, the Regional Government is obliged to provide assistance services for victims.

(2) Assistance services for victims may be implemented in cooperation with persons or institutions providing services for victims.

(3) The mechanism of assistance as referred to in paragraph (2), is implemented according to standard operating procedures established by Regent Regulation.

Part Four

Empowerment of Victims of Violence

Article 24

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to empower women and children victims of violence.

(2) Empowerment of victims as referred to in paragraph (1), by involving P2TP2A.

(3) In empowering women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government prepares empowerment programs in the economic field, which include:

a. arranging the necessary needs for entrepreneurship training for women victims of violence to improve business knowledge, attitudes and skills;
b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;
c. providing assistance in developing economic businesses;
d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large enterprises, as well as financial institutions to develop businesses of women victims of violence;
e. endeavoring to provide capital for women victims of violence; and
f. expanding access to information and promoting products of women victims of violence.
(4) In empowering women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may establish cooperation with job training institutions organized by the private sector.

CHAPTER V

COMPLAINT AND HANDLING PROCEDURES

Article 25

(1) Complaints of victims of violence to P2TP2A may be submitted by victims, families, community members, police officers, volunteer assistants, or social workers.

(2) Leaders or officers at P2TP2A are obliged to serve victims based on the provisions as referred to in Article 21.

(3) Leaders or officers of P2TP2A immediately handle victims in accordance with established procedures.

(4) Leaders or officers of P2TP2A within a maximum of 24 (twenty-four) hours from receiving the victim's complaint are obliged to report it to the nearest police officer.

Article 26

In the event that victims are outside their area of origin, P2TP2A is obliged to immediately report to the Regent, so that the Regent coordinates with the Regent/Mayor of the victim's area of origin to take actions or protective measures and repatriation of victims to their area of origin.

Article 27

(1) In handling victims, P2TP2A is obliged to network with government or private hospitals for treatment and health recovery.

(2) If necessary, P2TP2A may also network with social protection houses or trauma centers owned by the government or community, or safe houses owned by the government or community, or other social service institutions.

Article 28

Further provisions regarding complaint procedures and handling of victims of violence are regulated further in the P2TP2A Standard Operating Procedures.

CHAPTER VI

COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

Part One

Cooperation

Article 29

(1) The Regional Government may conduct cooperation in order to achieve the objectives of organizing protection for women and children victims of violence.

(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted with:

a. Government;
b. Provincial government;
c. Other district/city governments; and
d. Non-governmental institutions.
(3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1), includes: a. Exchange of data and information;
b. Rehabilitation of victims of Violence;
c. Repatriation and social reintegration; and
d. Provision of evidence and witnesses and followed up in accordance with Laws and Regulations.
(4) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), is conducted in the form of Joint Agreement.

Part Two

Partnership

Article 30

(1) The Regional Government forms partnerships with the business world in organizing protection for women and children.

(2) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1), are conducted through:

a. notification of job opportunity information for women victims of violence;
b. education and training for women and children victims of violence;
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of violence; and
d. fostering and improving the economic independence of women victims of violence.
(3) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) are implemented in the form of joint agreements.

CHAPTER VII

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 31

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to provide guidance on organizing protection for women and children victims of violence including technical guidance and training.

(2) The Regional Government is obliged to conduct supervision and evaluation of organizing protection for women and children victims of violence.

(3) Supervision and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2), is conducted with professional, transparent, accountable principles.

(4) In addition to supervision conducted by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (2), the community may conduct supervision of organizing protection for women and children victims of violence.

(5) Supervision conducted by the community as referred to in paragraph (4) by conveying aspirations to the Regent or DPRD.

CHAPTER VIII

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 32

(1) The community shall participate in the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of:

a. establishing protection units or complaint posts for women and child victims of violence within community organizations and/or within Negeri communities, Administrative Negeri/kelurahan
b. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
c. preventing the occurrence of violence;
d. providing first aid to victims; and
e. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
(3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, community social institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector and mass media.

CHAPTER IX

REPORTING

Article 33

(1) P2TP2A shall report the implementation of protection for women and child victims of violence to the Regent through the relevant Agency.

(2) Reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be submitted in writing, covering administration, finance, services and performance.

(3) Submission of written reports as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out at least every 1 (one) month.

CHAPTER X

FINANCING

Article 34

(1) The Regional Government shall provide an expenditure budget every year through the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget as well as other legal and non-binding sources of income to finance the implementation of prevention, protection and recovery tasks for women and child victims of violence.

(2) The budget for prevention, protection and recovery activities for women and child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be attached to the relevant Agency and managed by P2TP2A in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Article 35

(1) The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or civil society organizations that implement protection for women and child victims of violence.

(2) Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be provided in accordance with the Regional Financial capacity and implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

CHAPTER XI

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 36

Upon the enactment of this Regional Regulation, Regional Regulation of Central Maluku Regency Number 12 of 2014 concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Child Victims of Violence in Central Maluku Regency is declared null and void.

Article 37

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Central Maluku Regency.

I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide protection to every citizen in accordance with the Preamble to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. Women and children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience violence and therefore need to receive protection. Violence against women and children is a violation of human rights, therefore victims need to be protected in their dignity and honor and their right to life guaranteed without discrimination.

Violence against women and children is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or hardship for women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in physical, mental, sexual, psychological suffering or hardship, including neglect and mistreatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the child.

The presence of women and child victims of violence has not received adequate services, therefore comprehensive implementation of Protection for Women and Children is needed.

In 2014, the Regional Government and DPRD established Regional Regulation Number 12 concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children. However, the aforementioned PERDA needs to be amended in relation to institutional arrangements to be adjusted to the latest regulations at the National level. In addition, the PERDA has not comprehensively regulated the implementation of protection efforts that guarantee the fulfillment of victims' rights to truth, justice, recovery and guarantees of non-recurrence of cases, therefore a new regional regulation is needed that can guarantee its implementation.

This new Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children, particularly in terms of institutional arrangements, forms and mechanisms of prevention, prevention of acts of violence, assistance mechanisms, services for victims of violence and empowerment of victims of violence.

II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

Article 1

Every year, this activity takes place from November 25, which is the International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women (and Children), until December 10, which is International Human Rights Day. The selection of this time period is to symbolically link violence against women (and Children) with human rights, and to emphasize that violence against women (and Children) is one form of human rights violation.

Article 2

Letter a What is meant by "respect for human rights" is the appreciation and treatment of Women and Children in accordance with the rights they possess as part of human rights.

Letter b What is meant by "non-discrimination" is any action and deed carried out without distinction based on economic, social, cultural, political background, gender, age, religion, race and so forth.

Letter c What is meant by "best interests of women and children" is all actions concerning Women and/or children undertaken by the Government, Society, Legislative Body, and Judicial Body wherein the best interests of women and/or children must receive primary consideration.

Letter d What is meant by "right to life, survival, and development" is the most fundamental right for Women and/or Children protected by the state, government, society, family and parents.

Letter e What is meant by "respect for the opinions of women and children" is respect for the rights of women and/or children to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially regarding matters that affect their lives.

Letter f What is meant by "gender equality and justice" is respect for the position of women including girls that is equal and equivalent to other members of society, fairly without discriminating against them in all aspects of life.

Letter g What is meant by "victim protection" is action taken to prevent and protect women and/or children who experience acts of violence both physically and psychologically.

Article 21

Paragraph 1

Point a

What is meant by "Initial rescue of victims from acts of violence" is that victims have the right to rescue actions when acts of violence occur if the victim's safety is threatened. This rescue action must be carried out by village/sub-district officials at the victim's place of residence or at the location where the act of violence occurred. Village/sub-district officials must immediately coordinate with the nearest security officers.

Point b

What is meant by "Medical emergency handling for victims" is when the victim suffers sufficiently severe injuries, village/sub-district officials or medical personnel or security officers or family/community members who are aware of the occurrence of the act of violence must bring the victim to the nearest service facility to provide emergency assistance before the victim is taken to the hospital for further treatment.
