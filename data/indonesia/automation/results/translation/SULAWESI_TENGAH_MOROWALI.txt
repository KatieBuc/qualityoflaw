CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article I

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 years, including a child in the womb.
2. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
3. Protection is all service actions to fulfill rights, as well as guarantee and protect the rights of victims of violence. 4. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
5. Protection of women is an effort aimed at protecting women and their rights in order to obtain a sense of security from violence.
6. Violence is any act that results in or causes suffering and pain whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threat of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom arbitrarily, whether occurring in public or in private life.
7. Physical violence is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or unconsciousness and/or causes death.
8. Psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness or severe psychological suffering to a person.
9. Victim of violence, hereinafter referred to as victim, is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by individuals.
10. Regional Technical Service Unit for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as UP/[D PPA, is a regional technical implementing unit, established by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection and other problems. ll.Coordination Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FKPKK, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked and cooperative manner.
12. Region is Morowali Regency.
13. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent in the implementation of Government affairs which are the authority of the Region.
14. Regional Government is the Government of Morowali Regency. 15. Regional Governance is the administration of government affairs by the Government and DPRD according to the principles of autonomy and co-administration based on the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
16. Regent is the Regent of Morowali.
17. Office of Village Community Empowerment, Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, hereinafter abbreviated as Dinas PMDP3A, is the regional apparatus of Morowali Regency that implements government affairs in the field of women and children.

Article 2

The scope of regulation for the Protection of Women and Children from violence includes:

a. forms of violence;
b. rights of Women and Children from violence;
c. obligations and responsibilities;
d. prevention;
e. services for victims of Violence;
f. empowerment;
g. cooperation;
h. guidance and Supervision;
i. funding; and
j. criminal provisions.

CHAPTER II

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 3

Forms of Violence include:

a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence; and/or
d. domestic neglect.

Article 4

Physical violence as referred to in Article 3 letter a results in: a. the victim experiencing pain or serious injury;

b. death of the victim; and

c. causing illness or obstruction in carrying out work, position or livelihood or daily activities.

Article 5

Psychological violence as referred to results in:

a. Fear;
b. loss of self-confidence;
c. loss of ability to act; d. feelings of helplessness; and/or
e. severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 6

in Article a letter b

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 3 letter c includes: a. acts of sexual harassment;

b. coercion of unnatural or unwanted sexual relations; c. coercion of sexual relations with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes; and

d. sexual violence through social media.

Article 7

Domestic neglect as referred to in Article 3 letter d is caused by:

a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of the Child's reasonable needs, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or properly care for the child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
c. acts that neglect a person within their household scope, whereas according to law they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Article 8

Women and Children who are victims of acts of Violence receive the following rights:

a. the right to have their dignity and worth as human beings respected; b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
c. the right to determine their own decision;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidentiality of their identity;
f. the right to restitution;
g. the right to social rehabilitation;
h. the right to complaint handling;
i. the right to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and/or j. the right to assistance.

Article 9

(1) In the event that victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 8 require ongoing treatment, they are entitled to stay in a safe house or alternative residence.

(2) Ongoing treatment as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes treatment at the rehabilitation stage carried out by individuals, groups or Government and non-Government institutions.

Article 1O

(1) Safe houses as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) provide temporary shelter and/or accommodation for victims of Violence until their rights are restored.

(2) The operation and/or management of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the PMDP3A Office.

(3) For security and at the request of victims of Violence, the manager or organizer of the safe house as referred to in paragraph (2) may place victims of Violence in certain confidential locations.

{4) Further provisions regarding the organizers of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated in the Regent's Regulation.

Article 1 I

Children as victims of acts in addition to receiving the rights

of Violence as referred to in Article 8, also receive special rights, as follows:

a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development; b. the right to basic services;
c. the right to equal protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and/or
e. the right to obtain freedom.

CHAPTER IV

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One

Regional Government

Article 12

(1) The Regional Government has the obligations:

a. to implement policies for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence established by the government;
b. to establish policies, programs, and activities for the protection of Women and Children from acts of violence; and
c. to cooperate with law enforcement officials, government officials, as well as educational, health, hospital institutions, and various Non-Governmental Organizations that care for Women and Children in the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.
(2) The Regional Government is responsible:
a. to provide support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence; and b. to allocate budget for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence according to the Regional financial capacity. (3) In the case of the implementation of Regional Government obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and the implementation of Regional Government responsibilities as referred to in paragraph l2\, the Regional Government formulates a Regional Action Plan for victim protection. (4) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3) constitutes part of the Medium Term Development Plan.

Article 13

The Regent in carrying out obligations as referred to in Article 12 delegates to the PMDP3A Office and/or related Regional Apparatus in accordance with their authority.

Part Two

Community and Family

Article 14

(1) The community and family have the following obligations and responsibilities:

a. to prevent the occurrence of Violence against Women and Children; b. to provide information and/or report acts of Violence against Women and Children to law enforcement or authorized parties;
c. to participate in the assistance and handling of victims of acts of Violence;
d. to protect victims; and
e. to provide emergency assistance.
(2) Forms of community and family participation are carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of statutory regulations.

CHAPTER V

PREVENTION

Article 15

(1) Efforts to prevent Violence against Women and Children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the PMDP3A Office;

(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by:

a. building networks and collaboration with law enforcement officials, government officials, as well as educational, health, hospital institutions, various Non-Governmental Organizations that care for Women and Children;
b. building community and family participation and awareness regarding the prevention and protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;
c. establishing a violence prevention system that provides a complete and easily accessible information system;
d. conducting socialization regarding statutory regulations related to the protection of Women and Children who are victims of Violence;
e. providing critical education about the rights of Women and Children as victims of Violence to the community; and
f. opening complaint posts for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

Article 16

(1) Prevention of acts of Violence against Women and Children as referred to in Article 15, is carried out by the PMDP3A Office and Regional Apparatus that administer affairs in the fields of: a. education;

b. manpower;

c. health;

d. social;

e. women's empowerment and child protection;

f. mental and spiritual;

g. tranquility and order; and

h. communication and information.

(2) Prevention of acts of Violence by the PMDP3A Office and related Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out in an integrated and sustainable manner.

f. rehabilitation.

b. employment;
c. health;
d. social;
e. Women's empowerment and Child protection;
f. mental and spiritual; and

a. identification or initial recording of victims; and

I. GENERAL

Protection from violence that threatens the safety and health of women and children must be provided from an early age. Protection is necessary to prevent violence and physical, mental and sexual exploitation. For example, women and children who are victims of abuse, neglect, expulsion and maltreatment by parents or other persons. For women and children experiencing such problems the government must provide Safe Houses and adequate budget, the role of various parties to help handle violence problems faced by children and women including social community organizations, volunteers, social workers, medical personnel, advocates and religious figures.

The condition of children's deterioration along with their rights and the condition of violence against children and women that frequently occurs in the region should be a common concern for all stakeholders, not least the regional government, to play a role in preventing and addressing such deterioration and violence.

For the regional government of Morowali Regency in facing the universe of conditions above, various actions are taken to fulfill children's rights and to prevent and assist in handling acts of violence against children and women which are endeavored by establishing a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children From Acts of Violence.
