PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

MAYOR OF BANJAR

WEST JAVA PROVINCE

REGIONAL REGULATION OF BANJAR CITY

NUMBER 10 OF 2017

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

MAYOR OF BANJAR,

Considering:

a. that every woman and child has the right to survival, growth, and proper development and has the right to protection from violence and discrimination as mandated in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
b. that acts of violence against women and children in Banjar City have been increasing from year to year so that it becomes the responsibility of the Regional Government of Banjar City to guarantee the protection, fulfillment, and respect for the rights of women and children;
c. that Banjar City does not yet have a regional regulation governing the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence so that a comprehensive legal basis is needed in order to fulfill the rights of women and children;
d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

In View of:

1. Article 18 Paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 109, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4235) as amended several times, most recently by Government Regulation in Lieu of Law Number 1 of 2016 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2016 Number 99, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5882);
3. Law Number 27 of 2002 concerning the Establishment of Banjar City in West Java Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 130, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4246);
4. Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419);
5. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);

With the Mutual Approval of

THE REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF BANJAR CITY

and

MAYOR OF BANJAR

HAS DECIDED:

To Establish:

REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE.

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. City Region is the Banjar City Region.
2. City Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Banjar.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government that is the authority of the region.
6. Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
7. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
8. Woman is a person who is female by sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.
9. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child who is still in the womb. 10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence that occurs within the Regional territory.
11. Violence is any act against women and children that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.
12. Women's empowerment is the strengthening of women victims of violence to be able to work and work independently after being recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
13. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural circumstances of society.
14. Social rehabilitation is recovery from disturbances to physical, psychological and social conditions in order to be able to carry out their role again properly both in the family and in society.
15. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with their families and communities.
16. Assistance is an activity to provide counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims carried out by a person or representative of an institution that has expertise.
17. Prevention is an effort by the government together with families and communities as well as the private sector and/or business world to prevent the occurrence of violence through socialization, education, advocacy and promotion activities.
18. Handling is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing, and knowing that violence against victims will, is, and/or has occurred.
19. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy, and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
20. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
21. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is a technical implementing unit of the Regional Apparatus that carries out technical operational activities and/or technical support activities for integrated services for women and children victims of violence.
22. Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK is a coordination forum for the prevention, handling and empowerment of victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner and is formed at the Regional, sub-district, and village levels.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES, PRINCIPLES, AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of women and children victims of violence is implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect and recognition of rights; and
b. equal human dignity.

Article 3

Protection of women and children victims of violence is implemented based on the principles of:
a. gender responsive;
b. non-discrimination;
c. equal and respectful relationships;
d. maintaining privacy and confidentiality;
e. providing a sense of security and comfort;
f. respecting individual differences;
g. non-judgmental;
h. respecting the choices and decisions of victims;
i. sensitive to the background and condition of victims;
j. use of language that is appropriate and understood by victims;
k. fast and simple;
l. empathy; and
m. the best interests of the victim.

Article 4

1. The objectives of protection for women victims of violence are directed to:
a. create conditions that support the development of the potential of women victims of violence;
b. strengthen the potential already possessed by women victims of violence; and
c. improve the work skills of women victims of violence.
2. The objectives of protection for child victims of violence are directed to:
a. provide social rehabilitation for child victims of violence;
b. reunite child victims of violence with their family and/or environment; and
c. improve the empowerment of child victims of violence.

CHAPTER III

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Part One

Stages

Article 5

The stages of protection for women victims of violence include:
a. prevention of violence against women;
b. protection services for women victims of violence; and
c. empowerment of women victims of violence.

Part Two

Implementation

Paragraph 1

Prevention of Violence Against Women

Article 6

1. Prevention of violence against women as referred to in Article 5 letter a is carried out in an integrated manner coordinated by the Regional Apparatus responsible for women's empowerment and child protection.
2. Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by involving:
a. family;
b. community;
c. private sector;
d. community institutions;
e. social institutions; and
f. educational institutions.

Article 7

Prevention of violence against women as referred to in Article 6 is implemented among others by:
a. forming a network in efforts to prevent violence against women;
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, private sector and non-governmental organizations; and
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of women.

Paragraph 2

Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence

Article 8

1. Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b are provided to women residents of the Region and/or women who experience violence with the location of the incident in the Region.
2. The implementation of Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out with the principles of:
a. fast;
b. safe;
c. sense of empathy;
d. non-discrimination;
e. easily accessible; and
f. guaranteed confidentiality.

Article 9

1. Forms of Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 8 include:
a. legal aid services;
b. health services;
c. social rehabilitation services;
d. repatriation and social reintegration services;
e. complaint, consultation, and counseling services; and/or
f. assistance services.
2. In addition to Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), Services are carried out by receiving and sending case referrals from/or to other service units in a networked manner.
3. The implementation of Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Apparatus responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
4. Further provisions regarding the form and procedures for Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (3) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

Paragraph 3

Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

Article 10

1. Empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is implemented through efforts including:
a. job training; and
b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups.
2. The implementation of empowerment of women victims of violence is carried out with the principles of:
a. equality and justice of rights;
b. responsibility;
c. efficiency and effectiveness; and
d. respect and enforcement of human rights.
3. The implementation of empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by relevant Regional Apparatus in accordance with their main duties and functions.

CHAPTER IV

PROTECTION OF CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Part One

Stages

Article 11

The stages of protection for child victims of violence include:
a. prevention of violence against children;
b. handling of child victims of violence; and
c. rehabilitation of child victims of violence.

Part Two

Implementation

Paragraph 1

Prevention of Violence against Children

Article 12

1. Prevention of Violence against Children as referred to in Article 11 letter a is implemented by:
a. forming a network in efforts to Prevent Violence against Children;
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of Violence Prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, private sector and Non-Governmental Organizations; and
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the Protection of Child Victims of Violence.
2. Prevention of Violence against Children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

Article 13

Efforts to prevent Violence against Children as referred to in Article 12 are carried out by involving:
a. parents and/or family;
b. community;
c. educational institutions;
d. child social welfare institutions; and/or
e. other institutions engaged in child protection care.

Paragraph 2

Handling of Child Victims of Violence

Article 14

1. Handling of child victims of violence as referred to in Article 11 letter b is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
2. In handling child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with:
a. vertical agencies;
b. provincial government;
c. other regency/city governments;
d. village government;
e. non-governmental organizations;
f. private parties;
g. community; and/or
h. family.

Paragraph 3

Rehabilitation of Child Victims of Violence

Article 15

1. Rehabilitation Services for Child Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 11 letter c are carried out in the form of:
a. legal aid services;
b. health rehabilitation services;
c. social rehabilitation services; and/or.
d. repatriation and social reintegration services.
2. In the Rehabilitation services for Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the City Regional Government may cooperate with:
a. vertical agencies;
b. provincial government;
c. other regency/city governments;
d. village government;
e. non-governmental organizations;
f. private parties;
g. community; and/or
h. family.
3. Further provisions regarding the form and procedures for rehabilitation services for Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in a Mayor Regulation.

CHAPTER V

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 16

1. Every Victim has the right to:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as a human being;
b. the right to health and psychological recovery;
c. the right to determine their own decisions;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidentiality of identity;
f. the right to compensation from the perpetrator;
g. the right to Social Rehabilitation;
h. the right to complaint handling;
i. the right to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and/or
j. the right to assistance.
2. In addition to the rights as referred to in paragraph (1), Child Victims of Violence have the right to:
a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;
b. the right to basic services;
c. the right to equal protection;
d. the right to be free from various stigma; and/or
e. the right to obtain freedom.

CHAPTER VI

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

Article 17

1. The City Regional Government implements efforts to prevent violence against Women and Children in the form of:
a. collecting data and organizing an information system for the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence;
b. conducting education on anti-violence values towards Women and Children; and
c. disseminating laws and regulations related to Women's Empowerment and Protection of Child Victims of Violence.
2. The City Regional Government is obligated and responsible to provide and organize services for Victims in the form of:
a. establishing and organizing technical service institutions for Victims by involving community elements; and
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims of violence.

CHAPTER VII

INSTITUTIONS

Part One

General

Article 18

Technical service institutions for Victims are implemented by P2TP2A and FPKK coordinated by the Agency that administers Women's Empowerment and Child Protection affairs.

Part Two

P2TP2A

Article 19

1. The provision of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.

Article 20

1. P2TP2A provides services and temporary protection in the form of safe houses.
2. In the event that P2TP2A does not yet have a safe house as referred to in paragraph (1), victims are referred to other integrated service centers that have safe houses.

Article 21

1. P2TP2A consists of several counseling fields.
2. The counseling fields as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of at least:
a. law;
b. health;
c. spiritual; and
d. psychology.
4. The implementation of counseling is carried out by counselors and may be implemented in a networked manner by involving related parties.

Article 22

1. Forms of services to Victims organized by P2TP2A include:
a. medical services;
b. medico-legal services;
c. psychosocial services;
d. legal services;
e. social reintegration services; and
f. economic independence services.
2. Provisions regarding the mechanism of services to victims organized by P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

Part Three

FPKK

Article 23

1. In order to protect and guarantee the rights of women and children from acts of violence, FPKK is established.
2. FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:
a. City Regional FPKK;
b. Sub-district FPKK; and
c. Village FPKK.
3. Provisions regarding the procedures for establishment, organizational structure, and elements of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

Article 24

The duties and functions of FPKK as referred to in Article 23 consist of:
a. coordinating and synchronizing prevention, services, and empowerment of women and child victims of violence;
b. maintaining and developing FPKK in prevention, services, and empowerment of victims in a networked manner and referral system;
c. conducting education on non-discrimination values towards women and children;
d. disseminating laws and regulations related to the implementation of Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence;
e. collecting, compiling, and presenting reports on data of victims of violence; and
f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER VIII

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 25

1. The community may participate in the protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through:
a. provision of safe houses and/or shelters;
b. establishment and management of child orphanages;
c. establishment of child rehabilitation centers;
d. active supervision of violence against women and children and reporting to the competent authorities;
e. providing first aid to victims;
f. reporting the occurrence of violence against women and children and providing protection;
g. forming family partners at the village level by the community; and
h. other forms of community participation related to the implementation of women's protection and child protection.

Article 26

1. In addition to P2TP2A as referred to in Article 20, the community may form forums or groups at the sub-district and village levels that function as P2TP2A networks in the framework of implementing efforts for the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence.
2. The formation of forums or groups in villages as referred to in paragraph (1) may be facilitated by the Village Government.

CHAPTER IX

MONITORING, EVALUATION, AND REPORTING

Article 27

Monitoring, Evaluation, and Reporting of the protection of women and child victims of violence is coordinated by the Regional Agency in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

Article 28

1. The Regional Agency in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection conducts monitoring, evaluation, and reporting of the results of activities for the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence on a regular basis.
2. Reporting of activity results as referred to in paragraph (1) is submitted to the Mayor through the Regional Agency in charge of regional development planning.
3. The implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) involves the forum for handling victims of violence against women and children in a tiered manner.

CHAPTER X

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 29

1. Guidance and supervision of the implementation of Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence is carried out by the Regional Agency in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. coordination;
b. guidance;
c. education and training; and
d. monitoring and evaluation.
3. Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a covers aspects related to planning and implementation.
4. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b covers aspects related to planning, implementation, procedures, financing, quality, control and supervision.
5. Education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is carried out in accordance with laws and regulations.
6. Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d is carried out regularly every 3 (three) months.

CHAPTER XI

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 30

This Regional Regulation comes into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Banjar City.

I. GENERAL

Violence against women and children has shown an increasing trend. Economic factors, social environment and technological developments have a major influence on the increase in such violence.

This condition requires the Regional Government to take concrete steps through program activities as an effort to prevent and protect women and children from violence. One of the efforts to prevent violence against women is carried out through women's empowerment and optimization of previously possessed potential, so that it is expected to be able to improve and develop themselves to play a role and be protected from potential violence. This condition will strengthen the form of appreciation and respect for women while maintaining their role and nature as women to play a role in educating and supporting the welfare and happiness of their families. Efforts to prevent violence against children are the obligation of parents, families, communities and the private sector.

Community service is the frontline when there is potential violence against children. The mechanism for preventing violence and protecting child victims of violence involves multiple sectors coordinated by the Regional Government. The said efforts are carried out with the direction of providing social rehabilitation for child victims of violence, reuniting child victims of violence with their families and/or environment, and increasing the empowerment of child victims of violence.

In connection with this matter, to realize the provision of protection efforts for women and child victims of violence as well as for legal certainty in the implementation of women's empowerment and protection of child victims of violence in the Regional area, it is necessary to regulate it in a Regional Regulation.
