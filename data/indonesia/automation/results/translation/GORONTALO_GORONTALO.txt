CHAPTER II

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

Forms of violence include:

a. physical violence,
b. psychological violence,
Cc. sexual violence,
d. neglect,
e. exploitation, and/or 1 other violence.

Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a au injury, wound or disability on a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering in a person.

Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:

a. acts in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse,
Cc. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:

a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care,
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as appropriate carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care,
Cc. acts of neglecting people within the scope of their household, even though according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person, and/or, d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:

a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others, acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial, and /or all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:

a. threats of violence which include any act unlawfully in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict the fundamental freedom of a person, and coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

Il. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Gorontalo Regency so as to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and child victims of violence in the form of regulations in the Region.

So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and child victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, thus institutional and regulatory support is needed to ensure its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for women and child victims of violence in Gorontalo Regency.
