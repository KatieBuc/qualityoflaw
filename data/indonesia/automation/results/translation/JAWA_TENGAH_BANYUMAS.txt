CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms mean:

1. Region is Banyumas Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.
3. Regent is the Regent of Banyumas.
4. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is a regional apparatus assigned with certain main duties and authorities in the region.
5. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child still in the womb.
6. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
7. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, as well as receive protection from violence and discrimination.
8. Protection Implementation is a series of activities aimed at preventing violence, providing protection and services for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children, conducting coordination and cooperation, and increasing community participation carried out by the Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Violence Against Children.
9. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment based on sex and aimed at reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights, resulting in but not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
10. Violence Against Children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment committed against children, resulting in but not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
11. Victim of Gender-Based Violence is a person who, because of their sex, experiences physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the household and community.
12. Child Victim of Violence is a child who receives harsh treatment both physically, psychologically, economically, socially, sexually, and other losses resulting from state policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence within the household and community.
13. Victim Recovery is all efforts to empower victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to be more empowered, both physically, psychologically, socially, economically and sexually.
14. Companion is a social worker who has professional competence in their field.
15. Accompaniment is any action in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the purpose of empowering victims of gender-based violence and violence against children to resolve the problems they face.
16. Social Reintegration is the process of preparing victims and communities that support the reunification of victims into a family or family substitute environment that can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.
17. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children in accordance with established operational standards.
18. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Violence Against Children, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT PKBGA, is a special service center for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children conducted cross-functionally and cross-sectorally as a unified implementation so that victims receive maximum integrated and comprehensive services.
19. Sub-District Level Complaint Service Institution, hereinafter abbreviated as LPPTK, is a special service center for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children at the sub-district level so that victims receive maximum integrated and comprehensive services.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES, OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

Article 2

The Principles of Protection Implementation for Victims of Gender-Based Violence and Violence Against Children are:

a. non-discrimination;
b. best interests of victims;
c. gender justice and equality;
d. victim protection;
e. maternal survival;
f. survival, growth, and development of children; g. respect for the opinion of children;
h. transparency;
i. integration;
j. not blaming victims;
k. empowerment;
1. victim confidentiality;
m. decision-making in the hands of victims.

Article 3

The Objectives of Victim Protection are to:

a. prevent all forms of gender-based violence and violence against children that occur within the household and/or community;T
b. provide protection in the form of complaint services, health services, social rehabilitation services, legal aid and enforcement services, as well as repatriation and social reintegration services; c. seek to increase community participation.

Article 4

The scope of protection for Victims includes prevention, service and empowerment efforts for victims of violence.

CHAPTER III

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

The forms of violence are:

a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. exploitation; and/or
f. other violence.

Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or cause death.

Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:

a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;T
c. forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:

a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child as they should, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
c. acts that neglect a person within their household; and/or
d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that victims are under the control of that person.

Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is caused by:

a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others; b. acts with or without the victim's consent that include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or abilities of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits;

T

c. any form of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:

a. threat of violence includes every unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or bodily movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrain the fundamental freedom of a person; and
b. coercion, includes a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

CHAPTER IV

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Part One

Regional Government

Article 12

(1) The Regional Government is obliged and responsible to implement efforts to protect victims of violence in the form of: a. preventing the occurrence of all forms of gender-based violence and violence against children;

b. providing protection by providing complaint services, health services, social rehabilitation services, legal assistance and law enforcement services, repatriation and social reintegration services, seeking to increase community participation, as well as monitoring and reporting;

c. encouraging and increasing community participation; d. conducting cooperation with service providers in efforts to prevent, protect and recover victims of violence. (2) To carry out the obligations as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government has the authority to:

a. formulate policies and programs on the elimination of gender-based violence and violence against children;
b. establish an integrated service center;
c. establish complaint service institutions at the sub-district level; T
d. facilitate the implementation of integrated service centers and activities of complaint service institutions at the sub-district level; e. provide facilities and infrastructure;
f. increase the capacity of service provider institutions;
g. conduct coordination and cooperation in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children; h. encourage and increase community participation; i. conduct monitoring and evaluation.
(3) In the implementation of obligations, responsibilities and authorities, the Regent may delegate to the relevant SKPD.
(4) The relevant SKPD that receives delegation from the Regent as referred to in paragraph (3) in its implementation shall prepare a Regional Action Plan for victim protection.

Part Two

Community, Family and Parents

Article 13

In efforts to provide protection for victims of violence, the Community, Family and Parents have the obligation and responsibility: a. to prevent violence against women and children; b. to report if violence occurs;

c. to protect victims; and

d. to provide emergency assistance.

Part Three

LPPTK

Article 28

(1) LPPTK as referred to in Article 14 letter b, shall be established by the Regional Government in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

(2) The establishment of LPPTK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall consist of the following members:

a. elements of the Sub-District Government;
b. sub-district police elements;
c. health service institutions;
d. community leaders;
e. religious leaders;
f. elements of community organizations.

Article 29

LPPTK as referred to in Article 28, shall have the following tasks: a. receive complaints on gender-based and child violence cases; b. provide initial consultation to victims of gender-based and child violence;

c. conduct crisis intervention for victims of gender-based and child violence;

d. provide referral for handling gender-based and child violence cases to PPT PKBGA.

Article 30

In carrying out the tasks as referred to in Article 29, LPPTK shall be obliged to:

a. coordinate and consult with PPT PKBGA; b. document and report every gender-based and child violence case handled to PPT PKBGA.

Article 31

LPPTK shall be established by a Sub-District Head Decree.

Part Four

Rights of Violence Victims

Article 32

(1) Every victim of gender-based and child violence shall have the right to: a. obtain protection for personal safety, family and property, and be free from threats related to information that will be, is being, or has been provided; b. participate in the process of selecting and determining the form of protection and security support;

c. be free from incriminating questions;

d. obtain information regarding case developments and court decisions;

e. obtain complaint services, health services, social rehabilitation services, legal assistance and enforcement services, as well as repatriation and social reintegration services.

(2) The rights of victims as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Part Five

Cooperation

Article 33

(1) In organizing protection for victims of gender-based and child violence, the Regional Government may cooperate with the Government, Provincial Government, other Regency/City Governments and other institutions. -yv

(3) Cooperation between the Regional Government and the Regency/City shall include coordination, advocacy and referral.

(4) The implementation of cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

b. providing protection for victims;

c. providing emergency assistance;T 17

I. GENERAL

The right to a decent life, dignity and honor of women and children should receive protection in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination and the possibility of acts of violence.

The policy on protection services for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children has been normatively regulated in Regulation of the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection of the Republic of Indonesia Number 01 of 2010 concerning Minimum Service Standards for Integrated Services for women and children victims of violence.

Concrete steps that can be taken by the Regional Government so that women and children are protected from acts of violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, can be carried out by implementing one legal rule set forth in the form of a regional regulation. - The formulation of norms regarding the protection of women and children victims of violence in the form of a regional regulation needs to be carried out, the enactment of the regional regulation is in order to reinforce institutional support.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims of violence, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence, community participation, implementation mechanisms, guidance, financing and supervision.

II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

Article 1

Sufficiently clear

Article 2

Point a

What is meant by "non-discrimination" is protection for all victims of gender-based violence and violence against children without distinction of ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.

Point b

What is meant by "best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning the victim carried out by the government, society, and the judiciary, the best interests of the victim must be a primary consideration.

Point c

What is meant by "gender justice" is fair treatment given to women and men.

What is meant by "gender equality" is a condition and position that describes a harmonious, aligned, and balanced relationship between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in access, participation, control and benefits in the implementation of development and enjoying the results of development in family life, as well as in society, nation and state.

Point d

What is meant by "victim protection" is providing a sense of security to the victim carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

Point e

What is meant by "maternal survival" is ensuring that a mother does not experience death that occurs during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum, whether caused by physical or non-physical conditions.

Point f

What is meant by "growth and development" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.

What is meant by "survival" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity and the right to enjoy the highest attainable health status.

Point g

What is meant by "respect for the views of the child" is respect for children's rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.

Point h

What is meant by "openness" is that in the provision of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children is transparent among service providers.

Point i

What is meant by "integration" is that in the provision of protection for victims of gender-based violence and violence against children is implemented by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal assistance, psychologists, clergy, social workers, police.

Point j

What is meant by "not blaming the victim" is an attitude and treatment of not blaming the victim for the occurrence of violence they experienced.

Point k

What is meant by "empowerment" is that every effort provided must be able to strengthen the victim, both physically, psychologically, socially and economically.

Point l

What is meant by "victim confidentiality" is every action taken to ensure the victim is in a safe condition from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychology of the victim.

Point m

What is meant by "decision-making in the hands of the victim" is the victim's right to determine the best choice in resolving their problem.
