CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is the East Halmahera Regency Region.
2. Regional Government is the East Halmahera Regency Regional Government.
3. Regent is the East Halmahera Regent.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and DPRD in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Technical Regional Apparatus is a Regional Apparatus whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the affairs of Women's Empowerment and/or Child Protection.
6. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
7. Woman is a human being of female sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.
8. Women's Empowerment is any effort to enhance physical, mental spiritual, social capacity, knowledge, and skills so that women are prepared to be utilized according to their respective capabilities.
9. Fulfillment of Child Rights is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and obtain protection from violence and discrimination.
10. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological, and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of a person's liberty.

11 . Victim is a woman and/or child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.

12. Protection is all preventive, service and empowerment measures to protect and guarantee the rights of women and children from acts of violence.

13. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.

14. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will, is, or has occurred.

15. Empowerment is the strengthening of women victims of violence to be able to work and work independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.

16. Health Rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of victims including physical, psychological and social health so that victims can carry out their roles properly and normally both in the family and in society.

1 7. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction to be able to carry out social functions normally.

18. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of violence from the regional area to their area of origin.

19. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family or community that can provide protection and fulfill the needs of victims.

20. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2PTP2A, is a Regional Technical Implementation Unit (UPTDD) of the Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children integrated in the technical Regional Apparatus of East Halmahera Regency.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of Women and Children is carried out based on the principles of:

a. respect for and protection of Human Rights;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. best interests of the Child;
e. the right to life, survival, and development; f. empowerment; and
g. legal certainty.

Article 3

This Regional Regulation aims to:

a. ensure the fulfillment of the rights of women and children;
b. maintain the integrity of the household in order to realize a harmonious family;
c. prevent violence against women and children;
d. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children; e. provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence;
f. provide protection to reporters and witnesses; and g. empower women victims of violence.

Part Third

Forms of Violence

Article 27

Forms of violence include:

a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. exploitation; and/ a tau
f. other violence.

Article 28

Physical violence as referred to in Article 27 letter a is caused by actions that result in pain, injury, Iuka or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, pmgsan and/or cause death.

Article 29

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 27 letter b is caused by actions that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 30

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 27 letter c is caused by:

a. actions in the form of sexual harassment;
b. forced sexual intercourse;
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or specific purposes.

Article 31

Neglect as referred to in Article 27 letter d is caused by:

a. actions that result in the unfulfillment of children's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, carried out by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
b. actions of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as appropriate, carried out by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;
c. actions of neglecting people within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
d. actions that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Article 32

Exploitation as referred to in Article 27 letter e is caused by:

a. actions that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
b. actions with or without the victim's consent including prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or obscenity activities.

Article 33

Other violence as referred to in Article 27 letter f is caused by:

a. threat of violence includes : any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's inherent freedom; and
b. coercion, includes : a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

Part Fourth

e. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence; and
f. providing critical education on the rights of women and children for the community.

a. family and/or closest relatives;
b. community;
c. educational institutions; and

CHAPTER IV

INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN (P2TP2A)

Article 44

(1) The Regional Government in providing protection for women and children from acts of violence, shall establish P2TP2A as a regional technical implementing unit (UPTDD) integrated into the Technical Regional Apparatus.

(2) The provision of protection and services to women and children victims of violence shall be carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A. (3) The provision of services to women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

a. providing fast and free services to victims; b. providing protection and fulfillment of victims' rights to health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration and legal assistance;
c. cooperating with certain institutions in providing interpreters and volunteer companions needed for victims; d. networking with government or private hospitals for the treatment and health recovery of victims and cooperating with witness and victim protection institutions, social protection houses or trauma centers owned by the government, community or other institutions for the health recovery of victims;
e. providing ease, comfort and safety for victims; f. maintaining the confidentiality of victims; and
g. providing fulfillment of legal assistance for victims.
(4) Further provisions regarding the establishment, procedures and mechanisms for the operation of P2TP2A shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

CHAPTER V

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 45

Every woman and child victim of violence shall receive the following rights:

a. right to complaint handling;
b. right to have their dignity respected as human beings; c. right to obtain protection from family, community, regional government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court;
d. right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by victims;
e. right to determine their own decisions;
f. right to special handling related to victim confidentiality;
g. right to obtain information;
h. right to confidentiality of identity;
i. right to social rehabilitation;
j. right to accompaniment by social workers and legal assistance at every level of the examination process in accordance with laws and regulations; and k. right to spiritual guidance services.

Article 46

Women and children victims of violence in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 45, shall also obtain special rights as follows: a. right to survival;

b. right to grow and develop;

c. right to obtain equal protection; and

d. right to participate in community life.

CHAPTER VI

PROHIBITIONS

Article 47

(1) Every business operation whose activities may disturb the growth and development of children shall be prohibited from accepting child visitors. (2) Business operations as referred to in paragraph (1) include: a. discotheques; b. nightclubs; c. bars; d. adult karaoke; e. pubs; f. massage parlors; g. steam bath/sauna parlors; and h. other similar business fields.

Article 48

Every operator of hotels, motels, inns, tourism guesthouses and similar business activities shall be prohibited from renting rooms to children without being accompanied by parents or adult family members or supervising teachers/persons in charge in the context of carrying out school activities or other activities.

Article 49

(1) Every business operator of internet services, play stations and other types of children's games based on information and communication technology is required to manage and design child-friendly places.

(2) Every business operator as referred to in paragraph (1) must: a arrange rooms/booths with sufficient lighting and not enclosed; b not accept children wearing uniforms and/or during school hours; c locally block sites containing elements of pornography and pornographic acts; and d limit visiting hours and supervise the use of existing facilities.

CHAPTER VII

MONITORING AND EVALUATION

Article 50

(1) To ensure synergy, balance and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, activities, protection of Women and children, the Government conducts monitoring.

(2) Monitoring as referred to in paragraph ( 1) is carried out by the technical Regional Apparatus and P2TP2A.

(3) Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of Women and children.

(4) Monitoring is carried out periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of Regional Apparatus implementing policies, activities for the protection of Women and children victims of violence.

(,5i) Monitoring is carried out starting from planning up to implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children every year.

Article 51

(1) Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Protection of Women and children victims of violence is carried out at the end of each Budget year or when needed as required.

(2) The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of Women and children victims of violence are used as input material for the preparation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of Women and children victims of violence for the following year.

(3) Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

CHAPTER VIII

FINANCING

Article 52

Funding for the implementation of Protection of Women and children, is sourced from:

a. Regional revenue and expenditure budget; and/or
b. Other legitimate and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER IX

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

Article 53

(1) The Regent conducts guidance and supervision over the implementation of this regional regulation.

(2) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph ( 1) include providing implementation instructions, guidance, supervision, monitoring and evaluation of the Implementation of Protection of Women and Children.

CHAPTER X

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 54

(1) In the implementation of Protection of Women and children, the community may:

a. establish family partners at the Sub-district/Village level by the community;
b. conduct socialization of women's and children's rights independently; c. provide first aid to victims; and
d. report to the competent authority if violence against victims occurs in their environment.
(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector and/or mass media.

CHAPTER XI

ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS

Article 55

(1) Every person or business entity that violates the provisions as referred to in Article 47, Article 48, Article 49 shall be subject to administrative sanctions in the form of: a Written warning; b License suspension; c Administrative fine; and d License revocation.

(2) Written warning as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is given 3 (three) times with a grace period between one warning and another of at least 1 (one) month.

(3) License suspension as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is imposed if a written warning has been given as referred to in paragraph (2).

(4) License suspension as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b may be withdrawn if the person or business entity committing the violation pays an administrative fine of Rp. 30,000,000,- (thirty million rupiah).

(5) If within the period specified in the license suspension, the administrative fine is not paid, then the license will be revoked.

CHAPTER XII

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 56

No later than 1 (one) year, calculated from the effective date of this Regional Regulation, the Implementing Regulations of this Regional Regulation must have been established.

Article 57

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, order the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of East Halmahera Regency.

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore it is necessary to protect their dignity and guarantee their right to life in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in East Halmahera so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations. So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, so institutional support and regulations that can guarantee its implementation are needed. This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in East Halmahera.
