CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is East Kutai Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the regional government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region of East Kutai Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of East Kutai.
4. Office is the Office of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection of East Kutai Regency.
5. Village is a legal community unit that has territorial boundaries authorized to regulate and manage government affairs, the interests of the local community based on community initiative, rights of origin and/or traditional rights recognized and respected in the government system of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia.
6. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral lines up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of the woman.
7. Protection . . .

REGENT OF EAST KUTAI

EAST KALIMANTAN PROVINCE

7. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.

8. Service is all efforts and actions to fulfill the rights of victims including complaint services, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance.

9. Violence Against Women is any act that violates, hinders, eliminates enjoyment, and disregards the human rights of women.

10. Women Victims of Violence are women who experience acts of violence and discrimination whose rights are not fulfilled.

11. Health Rehabilitation is the recovery of Women Victims of Violence from health disorders they suffer.

12. Social Rehabilitation is all efforts to restore and develop the abilities of Women Victims of Violence so they can carry out their social roles and functions both in family life and/or society.

13. Repatriation is an effort to return Women Victims of Violence to their family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.

14. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Women Victims of Violence with their family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.

15. Legal Assistance ...

REGENT OF EAST KUTAI

15. Legal Assistance is legal services provided by legal companions and advocates to provide support to witnesses and/or Women Victims of Violence.

16. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a functional work unit that provides integrated services for Women Victims of Violence.

17.Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is an integrated service unit established by the Regional Government to provide Services to Women Victims of Violence and children comprehensively including complaint services, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance.

18. Minimum Service Standard hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for UPTD PPA services in providing complaint services, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and legal assistance for Women Victims of Violence and children.

19. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to provide protection for Women Victims of Violence in accordance with established standards.

CHAPTER II

RIGHTS OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

Every female victim of violence has the right:

a. to be respected for her dignity and worth as a human being;
b. to obtain protection from family, community, Regional Government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on the determination of a protection order from the court;
c. to recovery of physical, psychological and sexual health in accordance with the suffering experienced by Female Victims of Violence;
d. to special handling related to the confidentiality of Female Victims of Violence;
e. to assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
f. to spiritual guidance services; and
g. to determine her own decisions.

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

1. GENERAL

Protection of women is important because women constitute a vulnerable or subordinated group, particularly in connection with the many cases of violence against women who become victims.

Furthermore, in the National Action Plan for the Elimination of Violence against Women of 1999, it is stated that women as mothers of the nation and children as successors of the nation are creations of Almighty God, and therefore must be protected and their honour, dignity and self-worth preserved in a reasonable and proportional manner, both legally, economically, politically, socially and culturally without distinction of ethnicity, religion, race and class.

Although efforts have been made to protect women and there are government policies through laws and regulations that have firmly formulated measures for the protection of women, including efforts to protect women victims of violence, in reality the intensity or number of cases of violence against women in East Kutai Regency is still high while services and protection for women in East Kutai Regency have not been optimally implemented. Empirically in East Kutai Regency, an important factor that has influenced the process of prevention and protection of women so far is that the existing laws and regulations do not explicitly regulate the procedures and mechanisms for prevention and protection.
