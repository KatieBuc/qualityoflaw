I II

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by: 1. Region is Palangka Raya City.

2. Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region.

3. Mayor is the Mayor of Palangka Raya.

4. Regional Apparatus hereinafter abbreviated as PD is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and Regional House of Representatives in administering government affairs which are the authority of the Region.

5. Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing security in fulfilling their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.

6. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

7. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.

8. Woman is a person of female sex.

9. Child is a person who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old and has not married, including an unborn child.

10. Community is individuals, community groups, including parents and families.

11. Prevention is all efforts to eliminate all risks and adverse impacts of violence against women and children.

I 11

12. Child-Friendly City hereinafter abbreviated as KLA is a Community that is conducive to developing all social psychological potentials of a person optimally.

13. Child Obligations are everything that must be carried out by children according to the function and role of children.

14. Forum for Handling Victims of Violence Against Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as FPK2PA is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is tiered at the city and sub-district levels.

15. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is an integrated service center in efforts to empower women in various fields of development, as well as protect women and children from various types of discrimination, and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons established by the regional government or community-based.

16. Child-Friendly Village hereinafter abbreviated as KRA is the development of a village area that unites the commitment and resources of the village government involving the community and business community located in the village in order to promote, protect, fulfill and respect children's rights, which are planned consciously and sustainably.

Article 2

Protection of Women and Children is organized based on the principles: a. respect, fulfillment, protection, advancement, and enforcement of rights; and b. legal certainty.

Article 3

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is implemented based on the principles: a. responsive to rights;

b. non-discrimination;

c. maintaining confidentiality;

d. empowerment; and

e. participation.

I 11

Article 4

The objectives of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence include: a. ensuring the fulfillment of rights;

b. providing justice and equality of rights; c. providing protection and security; and d. conducting empowerment.

Article 5

The scope of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence includes: a. protection of Women; b. protection of Children;

c. coordination and cooperation;

d. responsibilities of Regional Government;

e. community participation;

f. guidance and supervision;

g. evaluation; h. reporting; and

1. funding.

CHAPTER ll

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

Part One

General

Article 6

Protection of Women includes:

a. services;
b. empowerment;
c. prevention; and d. monitoring.

Part Two

Services

Article 7

(1) The forms of services for Protection of Women as referred to in Article 6 letter a include: a. legal assistance;

b. health services;

c. social rehabilitation services; d. complaint, consultation and counseling services; e. social reintegration services; and

f. pre-marriage guidance services.

I 11

(2) Pre-marriage guidance services as referred to in paragraph (1) letter f, are implemented for every woman who will enter into marriage.

(3) Guidance is conducted as referred to in paragraph (2) to provide understanding and knowledge regarding family resilience, health, psychology, religion, social and culture.

(4) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (3) is implemented by the Regional Apparatus that administers affairs in the field of Women Empowerment and Child Protection.

(5) In implementing guidance as referred to in paragraph (4) the Regional Apparatus may involve: a. relevant agencies;

b. religious leaders; and

c. traditional leaders.

(6) Further provisions regarding minimum service standards for protection of Women are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

Part Three

Empowerment

Article 8

(1) The forms of empowerment for Protection of Women as referred to in Article 6 letter b include: a. job training; and

b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups.

(2) Implementation of empowerment for Protection of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Apparatus in accordance with its main duties and functions.

(3) Further provisions regarding procedures for empowerment of Protection of Women are regulated in Mayor Regulation.

Part Four

Prevention

Article 9

(1) Prevention of violations of Women's rights as referred to in Article 6 letter c is implemented by: a. establishing work networks;

I II

b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, private sector, non-governmental organizations; and

c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to Protection of Women. d. prevention of child marriage at child age. (2) Further provisions regarding prevention of violations of Women's rights are regulated in Mayor Regulation.

Part Five

Monitoring

Article 10

(1) Monitoring is implemented in the form of observation, identification, and recording of the implementation of Protection of Women.

(2) Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted by the Protection of Women and Children coordination team through coordination forum activities.

(3) Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) produces data and information regarding the implementation of fulfillment of Women's rights.

(4) The monitoring results as referred to in paragraph (3) constitute material for the Mayor to conduct evaluation.

Part Four

Prevention

Article 14

(1) Prevention of violations of Children's rights is implemented by the Regional Government and the community. (2) Prevention of violations of Children's rights implemented by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by: a. establishing work networks;

(3) Prevention of violations of Children's rights implemented by the community as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by:

a. increasing Children's productivity; b. developing Children's self-potential;
c. preventing child marriage;

(5) Further provisions regarding prevention of violations of Children's rights as referred to in paragraph (3), are regulated in Mayor Regulation.

CHAPTER VI

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Part One

Obligations and Responsibilities of Parents and/or Family

Article 28

Parents are obliged and responsible to: a. raise, care for, educate, manage and protect family members;

b. develop family members in accordance with their abilities, talents, and interests; and c. prevent marriage at the age of Children.

Article 29

Enhancement of family participation in the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence is carried out through: a. prevention of marriage at the age of Children;

b. bringing Women closer to health services; and

c. strengthening family resilience.

Part Two

Community Involvement

Article 30

(1) The community is involved in the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence.

(2) Community involvement as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of participation through prevention, services, empowerment, monitoring, supervision and fulfillment of the rights of Women and/or Children.

f. establishing Women and Child Protection units within community organizations and educational institutions;

g. establishment and management of orphanages;

h. establishment of Child rehabilitation facilities; provision of shelters and/or social facilities;
