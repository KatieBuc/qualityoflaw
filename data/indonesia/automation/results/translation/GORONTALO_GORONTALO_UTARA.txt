BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF NORTH GORONTALO

Considering: a. that in order to protect the constitutional rights of women and children in accordance with the mandate of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, the Government of North Gorontalo Regency is responsible for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.

b. that North Gorontalo Regency, which has the Philosophy of Custom Based on Sharia, Sharia Based on the Book of Allah, upholds Religious Values and Customs as well as noble character, the Government of North Gorontalo Regency is morally fully responsible for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

c. that every citizen has the right to be free from torture or treatment that degrades dignity, self-esteem and human dignity and has the right to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence

d. that the increase in violent treatment of women and children in North Gorontalo Regency is an act that degrades human dignity, so that the role of the Government of North Gorontalo Regency is required so that women and children are protected from acts of violence

e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b, and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms are defined as:

1. Region is North Gorontalo Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Regent is the Regent of North Gorontalo.
4. Regional People's Representative Council, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the regional head and DPRD in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
6. Forum for the Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FP2ATK, is a forum established based on this Regional Regulation whose membership consists of relevant agencies and communities concerned with protection from acts of violence.
7. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb. 8. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and community organizations. 9. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband, wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
10. Every person is an individual or corporation. 11. Violence is any act that results in suffering or hardship physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.
12. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or hardship to women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.
13. Violence against children is any act that results in or may result in suffering to children physically, sexually, economically, socially and psychologically.
14. Victim is a woman and child who experiences suffering and/or hardship either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
15. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will occur, is occurring or has occurred.
16. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
17. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
18. Cooperation is an activity or effort undertaken by several people to achieve common goals. 19. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against victims will occur, is occurring or has occurred.
20. Companion is a social worker who has professional competence in their field.

21 . Recovery of victims is all efforts to strengthen victims of domestic violence to become more empowered, both physically and psychologically.

22. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A, is an institution providing integrated services to victims established by the Regional Government.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

The Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of Victims of Acts of Violence;
b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. for victims of acts of violence; and
e. legal certainty.

Article 3

The Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence aims to:

a. prevent all forms of Violence against Women and Children;
b. protect Women and provide services to Women and Children Victims of Acts of Violence; and c. empower Women and Children Victims of Acts of Violence.

CHAPTER III

SCOPE

Article 4

The Scope of this Regional Regulation consists of:

a. protection of women; and
b. protection of children.

Article 5

The protection of women as referred to in Article 4 letter a includes:

a. prevention of acts of violence;
b. integrated services for victims of Acts of violence; c. empowerment of victims of Acts of violence.

Article 6

The protection of children as referred to in Article 4 letter b includes:

a. protection of children who become victims of acts of violence; and b. special protection of children.

CHAPTER IV

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

Part One

General

Article 7

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of violence that occur in public or private life in the form of:

a. physical violence;
b. sexual violence;
c. economic violence;
d. social violence;
e. psychological violence;
f. domestic neglect;
g. coercion or deprivation of freedom; and
h. threats of certain actions.

Article 8

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence as referred to in Article 7 is obliged to make efforts to:

a. prevent the continuation of criminal acts;
b. provide protection to victims of acts of violence; c. provide emergency assistance; and/or
d. assist in the process of submitting applications for the determination of protection Part Two Prevention of Acts of Violence

Article 9

(1) The Regional Government carries out prevention as referred to in Article 5 letter a by:

a. socializing laws and regulations; b. providing counseling/guidance;
c. providing education on the dangers of domestic violence;
d. conducting seminars/workshops or the like;
e. establishing work networks in violence prevention efforts; f. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
g. establishing violence prevention systems, mapping locations or areas prone to violence; and
h. increasing public awareness in behaving in accordance with religious norms.
(2) In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be carried out by:
a. family and/or closest relatives;
b. the community;
c. educational institutions;
d. health institutions;
e. social community institutions; and
f. the business world and other institutions.

Article 10

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to prepare Prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan;

(2) Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

Article 11

(1) Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment implements prevention as referred to in Article 9;

(2) Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1) in carrying out prevention may cooperate with vertical Agencies.

Part Three

Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 12

(1) The Regional Government implements integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b through activities:

a. complaint services;
b. health services;
c. counseling;
d. spiritual guidance;
e. social rehabilitation services;
f. legal aid and assistance services; and/or g. repatriation and reintegration.
(2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:
a. special service rooms in police lines;
b. expert and professional personnel;
c. service centers and safe houses; and/or
d. other facilities and infrastructure needed for victim recovery.
(3) The provision of services to victims of acts of violence is carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A and other social institutions;
(4) P2TP2A and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner;
(5) Integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (4) are implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures;
(6) Provisions regarding Standard Operating Procedures as referred to in paragraph (5) are regulated by Governor Regulation.

Part Four

Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 13

(1) Empowerment of victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c carried out by the Regional Government in the form of:

a. job training;
b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and c. capital assistance.
(2) Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.

CHAPTER V

CHILD PROTECTION

Part One

Protection of Children Who Become

Victims of Acts of Violence

Article 14

(1) Protection of children who become victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 6 letter a is the responsibility of the Regional Government;

(2) Protection of child victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by:

a. formulating prevention policies, risk reduction, victim handling and child data and information systems; b. organizing maintenance, care, and social rehabilitation of neglected children, both inside and outside institutions;
c. providing shelters, maintenance and care for neglected children; and organizing special protection for children.

Article 15

(1) Provisions regarding prevention and services for acts of violence against women as referred to in Articles 7 through 13 apply mutatis mutandis to the protection of children from acts of violence;

(2) In addition to prevention as referred to in paragraph (1), also by increasing family capacity in child protection and child care skills.

Part Two

Special Protection of Children

Article 16

(l) Special protection of children as referred to in Article 6 letter b is provided to:

a. Children in emergency situations;
b. Children in conflict with the law;
c. Children from minority and isolated groups;
d. Children who are exploited economically and/or sexually; e. Children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse;
f. Children who are victims of pornography;
g. Children with HIV/AIDS;
h. Child victims of abduction, sale, and/or trafficking; i. Child victims of physical and/or psychological violence; j. Child victims of sexual crimes;
k. Child victims of terrorism networks;
1. Children with Disabilities;
m. Child victims of abuse and neglect;
n. Children with deviant social behavior; and
o. Children who are victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their Parents' condition.
(2) The Regional Government in implementing handling of special protection of children as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI

COOPERATION AND COORDINATION

OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Part One

General

Article 17

(1) The Regional Government conducts cooperation and coordination in order to ensure the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence;

(2) Cooperation and coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by Regional Apparatus that administer governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Part Two

Institutional Framework for Protection of Women and Children

Article 18

(1) The Regional Government in conducting cooperation and coordination on Protection of Women and children from acts of violence, forms FP2ATK;

(2) FP2ATK membership consists of elements:

a. regional government;
b. vertical agencies;
c. educational institutions; and
d. community organizations concerned with the protection of women and children from acts of violence.
(3) The Organizational Structure of FP2ATK as in paragraph (2) consists of:
a. policy formulation working group;
b. socialization working group;
c. service working group; and
d. data, information, monitoring and evaluation working group. (4) The formation and term of office of FP2ATK members as referred to in paragraph (1) are stipulated by Regent Decree.

Article 19

(1) FP2ATK has the duties:

a. providing input in policy formulation; b. coordinating with Regional Apparatus and other social institutions;
c. socializing laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
d. collecting and developing data and information related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. conducting review, monitoring, evaluation, and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; and
f. providing reports, suggestions, input, and considerations to the Regent.

Article 20

FP2ATK as referred to in Article 18 paragraph (2) is assisted by the Secretariat at the SKPD that handles women's empowerment and child protection affairs.

Article 21

Further provisions regarding the mechanism and working procedures of FP2ATK are regulated by Regent Regulation.

Article 22

(1) The Regional Government in order to serve victims of acts of violence, forms P2TP2A;

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) has the duties: a. providing services, including:

1. receiving complaints/reports of violence against women and/or children, consultation, and counseling;
2. receiving and sending case referrals from Regency/City Regional Government or institutions formed by Regency/City Regional Government;
3. providing legal assistance;
4. health;
5. social rehabilitation;
6. legal services; and
7. repatriation and social reintegration.
b. providing protection to victims of acts of violence, reporters and witnesses.
(3) The appointment of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) is stipulated by Regent Decree.
(4) Regional Apparatus provides guidance to P2TP2A in implementing the duties as referred to in paragraph (2).

Cooperation

Article 23

(1) In certain cases the Regional Government may cooperate with:

a. other regional governments;
b. police;
c. advocates;
d. health workers;
e. social workers;
f. volunteer companions;
g. spiritual counselors;
h. other law enforcers, to assist victims in the court hearing process;
i. national commission on violence against women; j . Indonesian child protection commission or Child Protection Institution; and/or
k. certain parties desired for the benefit of victims. (2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of: a. socialization;
b. provision of legal aid;
c. health services;
d. victim assistance;
e. counseling;
f. social rehabilitation;
g. spiritual guidance; and/or
h. repatriation and social reintegration.

Part Four

Coordination

Article 24

(1) The Regent coordinates the protection of women and children from acts of violence with:

a. government;
b. Regency/City regional government;
c. vertical agencies;
d. educational institutions; and
e. community institutions.
(2) Coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: a. religious affairs;
b. health affairs;
c. education affairs;
d. social affairs;
e. legal affairs;
f. labor affairs; and
g. other affairs.

CHAPTER VII

COMMUNITY AND BUSINESS PARTICIPATION

Article 25

(1) Every person may participate in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence;

(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by:

a. reporting acts of violence against women and/or children;
b. providing first aid to victims of Acts of Violence;
c. independently socializing the rights of women and children; and/or
d. establishing independent community social institutions.

Article 26

In the event that community social institutions implement protection of women and children from acts of violence, they must coordinate with FP2ATK.

Article 27

(1) The Regional Government encourages the business sector to participate in the protection of women and children from acts of violence; (2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of: a. socialization to employees within the company regarding the dangers of acts of violence against women and children; and b. assistance as a form of corporate social responsibility.

CHAPTER IX

Article 28

(1) The Regional Government prepares and develops an information and reporting system for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;

I . GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute a violation of human rights so they need to be protected in their dignity and honor and their right to life must be guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. In order to prevent and address violence against women and children in North Gorontalo Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades their degree and human dignity, it is necessary to provide protection to women and children victims of violence in the form of regulations in the Region. So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protective measures in the Region, so institutional and regulatory support is needed to ensure its implementation. The Regional Regulation regulates protective measures for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in North Gorontalo Regency

Letter a

"Physical violence" means any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes death

Letter b

"Sexual violence" means any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

Letter c

"Economic violence" means an act that restricts a person from working inside or outside the home to earn money and goods, including allowing a person who works to be exploited, while someone does not fulfill the economic needs of the family.

Letter d

"Social violence" means an act by a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life to a person or may cause physical damage or damage to another person's property

Letter e

"Psychological violence" means an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Letter f

"Household Neglect" means: a. an act that results in the failure to fulfill

b. the needs of a child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;

c. an act of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as appropriate; d. an act of neglecting a person within the household, whereas according to the law applicable to him or her or because of an agreement or contract he or she is obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person;

e. an act that results in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Sufficiently clear.

Letter h

"Threat of a certain action" means any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, pictures, symbols, or body movements, with or without the use of means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom.
