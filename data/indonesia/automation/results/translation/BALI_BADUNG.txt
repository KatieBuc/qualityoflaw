b. that Women and Children are vulnerable groups who tend to experience Violence which constitutes acts violating human rights and need to receive legal Protection;

c. that the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence in Badung Regency needs to be supported by institutional and regulatory frameworks so as to guarantee its implementation;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Badung Regency.
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the Regional Government organizer.
3. Regent is the Regent of Badung.
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Badung Regency.
5. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to Victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
6. Woman is an adult human of female sex and a person recognized by law as a Woman.
7. Child is someone who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old, including a Child still in the womb.
8. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without using means against physical and psychological that poses danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.
9. Violence against Women is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or hardship for Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
10. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child.
11. Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences suffering and/or hardship as a result of Violence and/or threat of Violence.
12. Victim of Violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts and acts of Violence committed by either state apparatus or by the state or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.
13. Health Rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of Victims covering physical, psychological, and sexual health so that Victims can perform their role again properly and naturally both in the family and in society.
14. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can perform their social function naturally.
15. Repatriation is an effort to return Victims of Violence from the Regional territory to the Region of origin.
16. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide Protection and fulfillment of needs for Victims.
17. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a functional work unit that organizes integrated services for witnesses and/or Victims of acts of Violence.
18. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to carry out the functions and main tasks of integrated services for Women and Children Victims of Violence, covering complaint services, Health Rehabilitation, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, Social Reintegration, and Legal Aid.
19. Minimum Service Standard hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing services for handling reports/complaints, health services, Social Rehabilitation, enforcement and legal aid, as well as Repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of Victims; b. justice and gender equality;
c. non-discrimination;
d. the best interests of Victims; and
e. legal certainty.

Article 3

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to:

a. prevent all forms of Violence against Women and Children;
b. protect Women and provide services to Women and Children Victims of Violence; and
c. empower Women and Children Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

Article 4

Every Woman and Child Victim of Violence has the right: a. to have their worth and dignity as human beings respected;

b. to receive Protection from family, community, Regional Government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order determination from the court;

c. to physical, psychological and sexual health recovery according to the suffering experienced by Victims of Violence;

d. to special handling related to the confidentiality of Victims;

e. to assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;

f. to spiritual guidance services; and

g. to determine their own decisions.

CHAPTER IV

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Article 5

(1) The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to provide Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence.

(2) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) include:

a. implementing policies for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence based on laws and regulations;
b. establishing policies, programs and activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
c. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
d. allocating budget in the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
e. fostering and supervising the implementation of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

Article 6

(1) The community has the obligation and responsibility to provide Protection to Women and Children Victims of Violence.

(2) The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out in the form of:

a. preventing the occurrence of acts of Violence against Women and Children;
b. providing Protection to Victims;
c. providing emergency assistance;
d. providing information and/or reporting acts of Violence against women and Children to the competent authorities; and
e. participating in the handling of Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER V

INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

Article 7

(1) In carrying out Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region, the Regent may establish PPT.

(2) The establishment and development of PPT shall be adjusted to the development of priority needs, and the financial capacity of the Region as well as the institutional and personnel capacity existing in the Region.

(3) PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may accommodate institutions/work units that provide services for Violence Victims such as P2TP2A, Safe Houses, Children's Social Protection Houses, Trauma Center Protection Houses, Women's Social Protection Houses, Shelters and other service activities in accordance with the prevailing laws and regulations.

(4) Forms of services carried out by PPT include: a. complaints;

b. Health rehabilitation;

c. Social rehabilitation;

d. legal assistance;

e. repatriation; and

f. social reintegration.

(5) In providing Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence, PPT is obligated to:

a. provide services as quickly as possible and free of charge to Victims;
b. carry out Protection and fulfillment of victims' rights to Health Rehabilitation, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, social reintegration and legal assistance;
c. cooperate with certain institutions in providing interpreters and accompanying volunteers needed for Victims;
d. establish networks with government or private hospitals for treatment and health recovery of Victims and cooperate with Witness and Victim Protection agencies, social Protection houses or trauma centers owned by the government, community, or other institutions for the health recovery of Victims;
e. provide ease, comfort, and safety for Victims;
f. maintain the confidentiality of Victims;
g. provide fulfillment of legal assistance for Victims;
h. in the event that the implementation of integrated services is carried out in a networked manner, PPT remains responsible for the entire referral process of services needed by Victims.
(6) The provision of integrated services as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be implemented in accordance with SPM.

Article 8

(1) Community Institutions may establish PPT.

(2) In establishing PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), it shall be guided by the provisions of the prevailing laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VI

MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

Article 9

SPM in the Field of Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence includes services for:

a. handling complaints/reports of Violence Victims against Women and Children;
b. health services for Women and Children Victims of Violence; c. Social rehabilitation for Women and Children Victims of Violence;
d. law enforcement and legal assistance for Women and Children Victims of Violence; and
e. repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

Article 10

SPM as referred to in Article 9 has performance indicators including:

a. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers within the integrated service unit;
b. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive health services by trained health workers at Community Health Centers and Hospitals;
c. coverage of Social Rehabilitation services provided by trained Social Rehabilitation officers for Women and Children Victims of Violence within the integrated service unit;
d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for Women and Children Victims of Violence within the integrated service unit;
e. coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of Violence against Women and Children;
f. coverage of Women and Children Victims of Violence who receive legal assistance services;
g. coverage of Repatriation services for Women and Children Victims of Violence; and
h. coverage of social reintegration services for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER VII

MONITORING AND EVALUATION

Article 11

(1) To ensure synergy, sustainability, and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, the Regional Government shall conduct monitoring.

(2) Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is to determine developments and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region.

(3) Monitoring shall be conducted periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of Regional Work Units that implement policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

(4) Monitoring shall be conducted from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence for the current year.

Article 12

(1) Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children shall be conducted at the end of each fiscal year or if necessary as needed.

(2) The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children shall be used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women and Children for the following year.

(3) Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER VIII

REPORTING

Article 13

(1) The Regent is responsible for making reports on the implementation of SPM in the Field of Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region to the Governor with copies submitted to the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection and the Minister of Home Affairs.

(2) Reporting of implementation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted annually.

CHAPTER IX

FUNDING

Article 14

Funding for the implementation of Protection for Women and Children from acts of Violence shall be sourced from:

a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and
b. other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

CHAPTER X

DEVELOPMENT AND SUPERVISION

Article 15

(1) The Regent shall conduct development and supervision of the implementation of SPM in the Field of Integrated Services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region.

(2) Development as referred to in paragraph (1) includes providing implementation instructions, guidance, supervision, monitoring and evaluation of service implementation for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

CHAPTER XI

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

Article 16

(1) In carrying out Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence, the community may:

a. form family partners at the sub-district/village level by the community;
b. conduct socialization of the rights of Women and Children independently; c. provide first aid to Victims; and
d. report to the competent authority if Violence against Victims occurs in their environment.
(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

CHAPTER XII

TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Article 17

In the event that PPT as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (1) has not been established, the existing P2TP2A shall continue to carry out the tasks and functions of integrated services for Women and Children Victims of Violence in the Region.

CHAPTER XIII

CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article 18

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of its promulgation. So that everyone may know it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Badung Regency.

I. GENERAL

The State has an obligation to provide Protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. Women and Children are among vulnerable groups who tend to experience Violence and therefore need to receive Protection. Violence against Women and Children constitutes a violation of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life must be guaranteed without discrimination.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological aspects that causes danger to life, body or causes the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, Violence against Women is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in suffering or hardship to Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against Children is any act against Children that results in suffering or hardship physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of Children. The existence of Women and Children Victims of Violence has not yet received adequate services, therefore minimum services are needed for Victims of Violence.

In order to prevent and overcome Violence against Women and Children in Badung Regency so that they are protected from Violence, threats of Violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to carry out Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for Women and Children Victims of Violence, particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, financing, guidance and supervision of Women and Children Victims of Violence in Badung Regency.
