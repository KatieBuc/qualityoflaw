REGENT OF TOLITOLI,

Considering : a. that violence against women and children constitutes a crime against human rights thus it becomes the obligation of the state to protect from all forms of violence and discrimination;

b. that to fulfill the rights of women and children as well as part of the regional government's efforts to protect women and children from exploitation, neglect and discrimination;

c. that to provide direction, foundation and legal certainty in the protection of women and children, regional regulation is required;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

In View of : 1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a Child who is still in the womb.
- 2. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
- 3. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in a direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
- 4. Violence is any act that results in the occurrence of misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully;
- 5. Violence against Women is any act that results in or may result in the misery or suffering of Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in the public domain or in private life.
- 6. Violence against Children is any act against a Child that results in the occurrence of misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the Child.
- 7. Victim is a Woman and Child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of Violence.
- 8. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, court, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that Violence against Women will or has occurred;
- 9. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, as well as receive protection from Violence and discrimination.
- 10. Special Protection is a form of protection received by a Child in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger the self and life in their growth and development.
- 11. Service is action taken as soon as possible to Victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that Violence against Victims will, is or has occurred.
- 12. Companion is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to accompany Victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of Violence Victims.
- 13. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the empowerment of Violence Victims to resolve the problems faced.
- 14. Repatriation is an effort to return Women and Children Victims of Violence to the family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.
- 15. Counseling is a process of providing objective and complete information, carried out systematically with interpersonal communication guidance, guidance techniques and mastery of clinical knowledge aimed at helping a person recognize their current condition, the problem being faced, and determine a way out or effort to overcome the problem.
- 16. Rehabilitation is the recovery of Victims from psychosocial disorders and restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in the community.
- 17. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite Victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
- 18. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an integrated service provider institution for Victims established by the Regional Government.
- 19. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a benchmark for the service performance of integrated service units in providing complaint handling services, health services, social rehabilitation, law enforcement and legal aid as well as repatriation and social reintegration for Women and Children Victims of Violence.
- 20. Social Welfare Implementation Institution hereinafter abbreviated as LPKS is an institution or place of social service that carries out the implementation of social welfare for Children in conflict with the law, which is established by the ministry of social affairs.
- 21. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to provide protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
- 22. Region is Tolitoli Regency.
- 23. Regent is the Regent of Tolitoli.
- 24. Regional Government is the Regent of Tolitoli as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
- 25. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent of Tolitoli and the Regional House of Representatives in the Implementation of Governmental Affairs that are the authority of the Regency.
- 26. Regional House of Representatives of Tolitoli Regency hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.

#### Article 2

(1) The scope of this Regional Regulation includes:

- a. the rights of women and children victims of violence;
- b. Protection of Women victims of acts of violence;
- c. child protection;
- d. P2TP2A;
- e. cooperation, obligations and responsibilities;
- f. information and reporting systems;
- g. guidance and supervision; and
- h. financing.
  - (2) Protection of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, includes:
- a. prevention of acts of Violence;
- b. integrated services for Victims of acts of Violence; and
- c. empowerment of Victims of acts of Violence.
  - (3) Child Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, includes:
- a. Protection of Children who are Victims of acts of Violence; and b. Special Protection for children.

# CHAPTER II

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE Article 3

Women and Children Victims of acts of Violence receive rights, including: a. the right to be respected for their dignity as human beings; b. the right to recovery;

c. the right to determine their own decisions;

d. the right to obtain information;

e. the right to confidentiality;

f. the right to social Rehabilitation;

g. the right to complaint handling that is fast, appropriate, comfortable and according to needs;

h. the right of victims and their families to obtain convenience in the judicial process;

i. the right to assistance;

j. the right to security; and

k. the right to restitution.

#### Article 4

Children Victims of acts of Violence in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 3, also receive special rights, including: a. the right to survival, growth and development; b. the right to basic population services;

c. the right to equal protection;

d. the right to be free from various stigma; and

e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER III

PROTECTION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 5

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of Violence occurring in public or private life in the form of:

- a. physical violence;
- b. sexual violence;
- c. economic violence;
- d. social violence;
- e. psychological violence;
- f. domestic neglect;
- g. coercion or deprivation of freedom; and
- h. threat of certain actions.

#### Article 6

Every person who hears, sees or knows of the occurrence of Violence as referred to in Article 5 shall make efforts to: a. prevent the continuation of the criminal act;

b. provide protection to victims;

c. provide emergency assistance; and

d. assist in the complaint process and submission of applications for protection orders.

## Part Two

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 7

(1) The Regional Government conducts Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in Article 2 paragraph (2) letter a by:

- a. socializing laws and regulations;
- b. conducting empowerment and awareness to families, parents and communities by providing information, guidance and/or counseling;
- c. conducting efforts to increase the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
- d. conducting efforts to build community participation and concern to carry out prevention and protection of Women and Children from Violence;
- e. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities, religious institutions/organizations and various non-governmental organizations in efforts to prevent Violence against Women and Children;
- f. establishing a Violence prevention system, mapping locations or areas prone to Violence;
- g. increasing community awareness that respects Women and protects Children;
- h. establishing P2TP2A at the Regency level and Service Posts for the Protection of Women and Children at the District, Village and Sub-District levels; and
- i. establishing a task force for the protection of Women and Children.
  - (2) In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be carried out by:
- a. Family and/or closest relatives;
- b. Community;
- c. vertical agencies;
- d. law enforcement apparatus;
- e. educational institutions;
- f. health institutions;
- g. religious institutions/organizations;
- h. social community institutions; and
- i. business world and other institutions.
  - (3) Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus having the principal tasks and functions in the field of Women's empowerment and Child protection.

#### Article 8

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to prepare prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 7 in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan.

(2) The programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

## Part Three

Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 9

(1) The Regional Government implements integrated services for Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 2 paragraph (2) letter b through activities:

- a. complaint services;
- b. health services;
- c. Counseling;
- d. psychological services;
- e. spiritual guidance;
- f. social rehabilitation services;
- g. legal aid and Assistance services;
- h. safe house services; and/or
- i. repatriation and social reintegration.
  - (2) In the event that the Regional Government does not yet have counseling personnel as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, it may request assistance from social workers, volunteer Assistants, and/or spiritual advisors.
  - (3) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:
- a. special service rooms in police lines;
- b. expert and professional personnel;
- c. service centers and Safe Houses; and/or
- d. other facilities and infrastructure needed for the recovery of victims including perpetrators.
  - (4) The provision of services to Victims of acts of Violence shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A with other social institutions.
  - (5) P2TP2A and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (4) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner.
  - (6) Integrated Services for Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), paragraph (3), paragraph (4) and paragraph (5) shall be implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures.

#### Article 10

(1) Complaint services as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) letter a are implemented to receive reports of acts of Violence.

(2) Reports as referred to in paragraph (1) may originate from: a. victims;

b. referrals; or

c. outreach.

#### Article 11

(1) Health services as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) letter b, shall be implemented using health facilities owned by the Regional Government or other referral hospitals by providing treatment services and health recovery for Victims.

(2) Health services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out by health personnel for Victims in accordance with professional standards, standard operating procedures and the medical needs of Victims.

(3) Health services as referred to in paragraph (1), through efforts to:

- a. conduct anamnesis for Victims;
- b. conduct examination of Victims;
- c. provide first aid;
- d. restore health both physically and psychologically, carry out efforts to prevent disease and other health disorders;
- e. counseling;
- f. refer to more adequate health service facilities if necessary may refer to other parties with a multidisciplinary approach, including health services, medicolegal, psychosocial Assistance and legal aid; and/or
- g. provide information to the police.
  - (4) In addition to the efforts as referred to in paragraph (3) for certain cases, health personnel may provide reproductive health services in accordance with medical needs.
  - (5) In providing services as referred to in paragraph (3) and paragraph (4), health personnel must make medical records in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
  - (6) For every medical action to be performed by health personnel there must be medical action consent from the Victim or their family in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
  - (7) For investigation purposes, authorized health personnel must make a visum et repertum and/or visum et repertum psychiatricum or make a medical certificate.
  - (8) Health personnel have the right to receive legal protection.

#### Article 12

Services for legal assistance and protection as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) letter g include:

- a. provide protection in Safe Houses;
- b. provide legal information to Victims;
- c. provide Assistance for Victims as witnesses from the investigation process to the verdict;
- d. provide special legal protection for Child Victims of acts of Violence which may be carried out by appointing guardianship in accordance with laws and regulations.

#### Article 13

(1) Repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) letter i shall be implemented with the following principles: a. voluntary;

b. safe and dignified;

c. respect for rights.

(2) In the event that the regional government is not yet able to implement Repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in paragraph (1), it may request assistance from relevant parties.

(3) Social services to provide social welfare for Children in conflict with the law shall be carried out by the Regional Government together with LPKS and other social institutions.

(4) LPKS and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) are rehabilitation and temporary shelter places for Children in conflict with the law before repatriation and social reintegration are carried out.

## Part Four

Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 14

(1) Empowerment of Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 2 paragraph (2) letter c carried out by the Regional Government in the form of:

- a. job training;
- b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and c. capital assistance.
  - (2) Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.

# CHAPTER IV

CHILD PROTECTION

## Part One

Protection of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

### Paragraph 1

Stages of Protection of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 15

Stages of protection for Child Victims of acts of Violence, include: a. prevention of acts of Violence against Children;

b. handling of Child Victims of acts of Violence; and

c. rehabilitation of Child Victims of acts of Violence.

### Paragraph 2

Prevention of Violence Against Children

#### Article 16

(1) Prevention of acts of Violence against Children as referred to in Article 15 letter a shall be implemented by:

- a. forming a working network in efforts to prevent Violence against Children;
- b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of Violence prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, private sector and Non-Governmental Organizations; and
- c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations relating to the protection of Child Victims of Violence.
  - (2) Prevention of acts of Violence against Children as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus having the main duties and functions in the field of Women's empowerment and Child protection.

#### Article 17

Prevention of acts of Violence against Children in addition to being carried out by regional apparatus as referred to in Article 16 paragraph (2), also involves:

- a. parents and/or family;
- b. community;
- c. educational institutions;
- d. Child social welfare institutions; and/or
- e. other institutions engaged in the field of caring for Child protection.

### Paragraph 3

Handling of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 18

Handling of Child Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 15 letter b shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

### Paragraph 4

Rehabilitation of Child Victims of Acts of Violence

#### Article 19

(1) Rehabilitation of Child Victims of acts of Violence as referred to in Article 15 letter c shall be carried out in the form of:

- a. legal aid services;
- b. health rehabilitation services;
- c. social rehabilitation services; and/or
- d. repatriation and social reintegration services.
  - (2) In rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with:
- a. vertical agencies;
- b. provincial government;
- c. other regency/city governments; village government;
- d. non-governmental organizations;
- e. private parties;
- f. community; and/or
- g. family.

## Part Two

Special Protection of Children

#### Article 20

(1) Special protection of Children as referred to in Article 2 paragraph (3) letter b shall be provided to:

- a. Child Victims of physical, psychological, sexual Violence and neglect; b. Children in conflict with the law;
- c. Children from Minority and Isolated Groups;
- d. Children who are economically and/or sexually exploited; e. Children who are Victims of Pornography;
- f. Child Victims of Sexual Crimes;
- g. Children who are Victims of Abuse of Narcotics, Alcohol, Psychotropics and Other Addictive Substances;
- h. Children with HIV/AIDS;
- i. Children with Disabilities;
- j. Child Victims of abuse and neglect;
- k. Children with Deviant Social Behavior;
- l. Child Victims of Terrorism Networks;
- m. Child Victims of Kidnapping, Sale and/or Trafficking; and n. Children who are Victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their parents' condition.
  - (2) The Regional Government in implementing the handling of special protection of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two

Obligations and Responsibilities

#### Article 25

The obligation and responsibility to provide protection to Women and Children from acts of Violence is the joint responsibility of the Regional Government and the Community.

#### Article 26

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 25, include:

- a. establishing, implementing policies, programs, and conducting collaborative activities in the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;
- b. facilitating the establishment of institutions for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence and providing support for facilities and infrastructure;
- c. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence according to regional financial capacity;
- d. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;
- e. providing protection services for Women and Children Victims of acts of Violence;
- f. encouraging and increasing community participation; and g. appointing parents and/or substitute family caregivers as a protection measure for Children who are Victims of acts of Violence.

# CHAPTER VII

COMMUNITY AND BUSINESS PARTICIPATION

#### Article 27

(1) Every person may participate in providing protection to Women and Children from acts of Violence.

(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by:

- a. preventing and stopping the occurrence of acts of Violence; b. providing protection to Victims;
- c. providing first aid to Victims of acts of Violence; d. reporting acts of Violence against Women and/or Children; e. independently socializing the rights of Women and Children; f. strengthening community social institutions in handling acts of Violence against Women and Children; and/or g. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to acts of Violence against Women and Children.

#### Article 28

(1) The Regional Government encourages the business sector to participate in the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of: a. socialization to employees within the company regarding the dangers of acts of Violence against Women and Children; and b. assistance as a form of corporate social responsibility.

# CHAPTER VIII

INFORMATION AND REPORTING SYSTEM

#### Article 29

(1) The Regional Government prepares and develops an information and reporting system for the protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

(2) The information and reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 30

(1) The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of P2TP2A.

(2) Guidance and supervision are carried out by the Regional Apparatus which is assigned and responsible in the field of Women's empowerment and Child protection.

(3) The results of the implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (2) must be reported to the Regional Government.

# CHAPTER X

FUNDING

#### Article 31

Funding for the Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence may be sourced from:

- a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and/or
- b. other lawful non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 32

(1) The Regional Government may provide funding assistance to community organizations, social organizations, or non-governmental organizations that implement protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.

(2) Funding assistance as referred to in paragraph (1) is in accordance with regional financial capacity and implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

I. GENERAL

The Preamble to the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia states that the State has the obligation to provide protection to every citizen.

#### article 28

G paragraph (1) of the Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia of 1945 explicitly states that: every person has the right to protection of their person, family, honor, dignity, and property under their control, as well as the right to a sense of security and protection from the threat of fear to do or not do something which is a basic right. Violence is a manifestation of human emotional behavior, rather than rational behavior.

In the context of protection for victims of crime, there are preventive and repressive efforts undertaken, both by the community and the government (through its law enforcement apparatus), such as providing protection or supervision from various threats that may endanger the victim's life, providing adequate medical assistance, and legal assistance. Protection means safeguarding something from dangerous things, that something can be interests or objects or goods. In addition, protection also contains the meaning of safeguarding provided by someone to weaker people. Thus, legal protection can be interpreted as all government efforts to ensure legal certainty to provide protection to its citizens so that their rights as citizens are not violated, and those who violate them may be subject to sanctions in accordance with applicable regulations.

#### Article 5

Point a

What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or causes death.

Point b

What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial and or certain purposes.

Point c

What is meant by "economic violence" is an act that restricts humans from working inside or outside the home to generate money and goods, including allowing humans who work to be exploited, while someone does not fulfill the economic needs of the family.

Point d

What is meant by "social violence" is as an act of a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life to a person or may cause physical damage or damage to another person's property.

Point e

What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Point f

What is meant by "Household Neglect" is: a. an act that results in the non-fulfillment of; b. the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;

c. deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should be; d. an act of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person;

e. an act that results in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Point g

Sufficiently clear.

Point h

What is meant by "threat of certain action" is any unlawful act in the form of words, writing, images, symbols, or body gestures, either with or without using means that create fear or restrict the fundamental freedom of a person.

What is meant by "visum et repertum or visum et repertum psychiatricum" is a written statement made by a doctor in forensic medicine at the request of the authorized investigator regarding the results of a medical examination of humans, whether alive or dead or parts or suspected parts of the human body, based on their knowledge and under oath, for the purposes of pro justitia.
