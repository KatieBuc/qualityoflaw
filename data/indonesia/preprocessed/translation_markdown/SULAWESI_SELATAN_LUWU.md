# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Government is the Central Government.
- 2. Governor is the Governor of South Sulawesi Province.
- 3. Region is Luwu Regency.
- 4. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
- 5. Regent is the Regent of Luwu.
- 6. Gender is a concept that refers to roles and responsibilities as women and as men that are created and internalized in families, society and the culture of the community in which we live, including expectations, attitudes, characteristics, behavior of how to be a man and how to be a woman. or
- 7. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, resulting in language, political beliefs, reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or human rights and basic freedoms in the use of rights life both individually and collectively in the fields of politics, economy, law, social, culture and other aspects of life. 8. Women's empowerment is any effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social, knowledge and skills abilities so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective capabilities.
- 9. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, temporarily or courts or other parties either based on a court order.
- 10. Violence is any act unlawfully, with or without using means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom-
- 11. Disability is an Individual who has physical or mental limitations.
- 12. Regional Cooperation is an agreement between a governor and a governor or a governor and a regent/mayor or between a regent/mayor and another regent/mayor, and or a governor, regent/mayor with a third party, made in writing and giving rise to rights and obligations.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of Women is implemented based on the principles of:

- a. respect for human rights;
- b. justice and gender equality, c. non-discrimination; and
- d. protection of victims.

#### Article 3

The implementation of Protection of Women aims to:

- a. increase the participation of women both individually and in groups as potential and resources in the implementation of social community life;
- b. prevent all forms of Discrimination;
- c. empower women both individually and in groups and communities experiencing gender issues so that they are able to meet their needs independently;
- d. prevent and handle risks from the vulnerability of women so that their survival can be fulfilled in accordance with minimum basic needs;
- e. reduce the gap in opportunities between women and men in achieving development; and
- f. reduce acts of Violence against women.

# CHAPTER III

SCOPE

#### Article 4

The implementation of protection of women from acts of violence includes:

- a. prevention;
- b. handling; c. empowerment.

#### Article 5

(1) Protection of women from violence includes protection of women in households, in communities, in workplaces and in conflict and disaster areas.

(2) Protection of women from violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes women with disabilities, elderly women.

# CHAPTER IV

HUMAN RIGHTS AND FUNDAMENTAL FREEDOMS

#### Article 6

Human Rights and fundamental human freedoms consist of:

- a. The right to life;
- b. The right to form a family and procreate;
- c. The right to self-development;
- d. The right to obtain justice;
- e. The right to personal freedom;
- f. The right to security;
- g. The right to welfare; and
- h. The right to participate in government.

#### Article 7

(1) Every Woman has the right to:

- a. live peacefully, safely, serenely, happily, prosperous physically and spiritually;
- b. live in a family within the bonds of lawful marriage; c. maintain life and improve her standard of living; d. have work she likes and is also entitled to fair employment conditions;
- e. obtain education and instruction in all types, levels and paths of education in accordance with the specified requirements; a healthy and clean environment;
- g. receive fair work wages in accordance with her work that is comparable and commensurate with human dignity;
- h. elect and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting according to applicable laws and regulations; special protection in the implementation of her work or profession against matters that may threaten her safety and/or health in relation to women's reproductive function; and
- i. receive special protection
- j. special rights inherent to women due to their reproductive function are guaranteed and protected by law.
  - (2) Women who are adults and or are married have the right to perform legal acts themselves unless otherwise determined by laws and regulations and other social norms.
  - (3) A lawful marriage can only take place based on the free will of the woman and the man in accordance with laws and regulations.
  - (4) A wife during the marriage bond has the same rights and responsibilities as her husband over all matters relating to their marital life, relations with their children and ownership rights as well as management of joint assets.
  - (5) After the dissolution of her marriage, a woman has the same rights and responsibilities as her former husband over all matters relating to their children, with regard to the best interests of the child.
  - (6) After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has the same rights as her former husband over all matters relating to joint assets without diminishing the rights of the child, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
  - (7) Every woman who has a Disability, is elderly, and is pregnant has the right to obtain facilities and special treatment.
  - (8) Every elderly woman, with physical and/or mental disabilities, has the right to obtain care, education, training and special assistance at the expense of the regional government to ensure a decent life in accordance with human dignity.

# CHAPTER V

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One Prevention

#### Article 8

The target of prevention as referred to in Article 4 letter a is every woman.

#### Article 9

(1) Prevention as referred to in Article 4 letter a, includes: a. collecting data and information about women victims of violence and laws and regulations; b. conducting education about values toward women; anti-violence

c. socializing laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women victims of violence;

d. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for women victims by involving community elements;

e. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for women victims of violence; 1. Increasing women's awareness of the importance of complying with ethics, religious norms and cultural norms in behavior.

(2) Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regency Regional Government, police institutions, prosecutor's institutions, judicial institutions, non-governmental organizations, and the community in accordance with their duties, functions, and responsibilities.

## 7. Part Two Handling

#### Article 10

The target of handling as referred to in Article 4 letter b is every woman.

#### Article 11

(1) The forms of services for women victims of violence that are provided include:

- a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at recovering the physical condition of the victim carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;
- b. medicolegal services are a form of medical service for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;
- c. psychosocial services are services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of the victim, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have confidence, strength, and independence in solving their problems;
- d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process;
- e. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.
  - (2) The service mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented according to standard operating procedures which will be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 12

The implementation of integrated services for women victims of violence is conducted free of charge, quickly, safely, empathetically, non-discriminatory, easily accessible, and with confidentiality guarantees.

#### Article 13

Integrated service providers are obligated to provide services in accordance with the service principles as referred to in Article 12.

#### Article 21

(1) The provision and facilitation of Protection services for women as referred to in Article 20 letter c are in the form of:

- a. handling of complaints/reports;
- b. legal assistance;
- c. health rehabilitation;
- d. social rehabilitation;
- e. social reintegration; or f. repatriation assistance.
  - (2) The provision and facilitation of Protection services for women as referred to in paragraph (1) are given to women:
- a. victims of violence;
- b. victims of human trafficking;
- c. elderly who are not independent;
- d. with Disabilities;
- e. victims of sexual exploitation; f. victims of disasters and those in conflict-prone areas; and
- g. workers/employees.

# CHAPTER VIII

INSTITUTIONS, GUIDANCE, AND SUPERVISION

## Part One Institutions

#### Article 23

(1) Handling as referred to in Article 11 is carried out in an integrated manner by related SKPDs and/or service institutions that handle the protection of women from acts of violence.

(2) The implementation of integrated services is coordinated by BPPKB or another name which is further regulated by Regent Regulation.

(3) The implementation of integrated services is in accordance with laws and regulations.

## Part Two Guidance

#### Article 24

(1) The regional government is obligated to conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for women victims of violence.

(2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:

- a. Guidelines and fulfillment standards;
- b. Technical guidance and training;
- c. Provision of facilities;
- d. Monitoring,
- e. Evaluation.
  - (3) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2), is intended to realize the protection of women from acts of violence in accordance with minimum service standards implemented by SKPD and the community.
  - (4) SKPD as referred to in paragraph (3) is the Regional Work Unit within the Regional Government of Luwu Regency that is related to the protection of women against acts of violence.

# CHAPTER X

MONITORING, EVALUATION AND REPORTING

#### Article 27

(1) To ensure synergy, continuity and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Empowerment and Protection of women, the Regional Government conducts monitoring.

(2) Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to identify developments and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Empowerment and Protection of women in the Region.

(3) Monitoring is carried out periodically from planning through to policies, programs and activities for the Empowerment and Protection of women for the current year.

(4) Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Empowerment and Protection of women is carried out at the end of each fiscal year.

(5) The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the Empowerment and Protection of women are used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs and activities for the following year.

(6) The Regent submits a report on the implementation of integrated services for the Protection of women victims of violence to the Governor with a copy submitted to the minister in charge of women's empowerment and child protection affairs, and the minister in charge of home affairs.

(7) The Regent submits a report on the implementation of the formation and development of task forces, forums, working groups or other institutions in an integrated manner to the minister in charge of home affairs with a copy to the minister in charge of women's empowerment and child protection affairs.

(8) The implementation of reporting as referred to in paragraph (6) and paragraph (7) is carried out annually and/or when necessary.

# CHAPTER XI

PROHIBITION AND SANCTIONS

#### Article 28

Any person who intentionally commits violence against women shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with statutory regulations.
