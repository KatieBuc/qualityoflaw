IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Region is Mataram City.
- 2. Regional Government is the Mayor and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
- 3. Mayor is the Mayor of Mataram.
- 4. Regional People's Representative Council, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of Mataram City as an element of regional government administration.
- 5. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the regional apparatus work unit of Mataram City.
- 6. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as UKPD, is the regional apparatus work unit of Mataram City.
- 7. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child in the womb.
- 8. Woman is an adult human of female sex and a person recognized by law as a woman. 9. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children. 10. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center. 11. Violence is any act that results in or causes suffering and distress whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threat of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.
- 12. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or unconsciousness and/or causes death.
- 13. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, sense of disbelief or severe psychological suffering to a person.
- 14. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, coercion of sexual relations, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.
- 15. Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence.
- 16. Victim of acts of violence is an individual or group of people who suffer physically, mentally or emotionally, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
- 17. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
- 18. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.
- 19. Assistant is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.
- 20. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of acts of violence to the family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.
- 21. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in the community.
- 22. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
- 23. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organization (NGO) that provides assistance.
- 24. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is an institution providing services to victims of violence, based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psychosocial and legal services.
- 25. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the regional government to provide services in the context of empowerment of women and children victims of acts of violence.
- 26. Regional Action Plan is the foundation and guidance for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in the context of implementing activities for the prevention and protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.
- 27. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.
- 28. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations. 29. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and a collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.
- 30. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence is carried out based on the principles of:

- a. respect for the rights of victims;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination; and
- d. best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

(1) The purpose of implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence is to:

- a. prevent acts of violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;
- b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children:
- c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
- d. provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses; and
- e. facilitate and mediate domestic disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household.
  - (2) The purpose of implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), includes aspects of:
- a. prevention;
- b. service and assistance;
- c. reunification; and
- d. empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

Every victim has the right to receive:

- a. protection;
- b. information;
- c. optimal service;
- d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage; e. confidential handling;
- f. psychological and legal assistance; and g. guarantees of rights related to status as a family member and as a member of society.

#### Article 5

Child victims of acts of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 4, also receive special rights, as follows:

- a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development;
- b. the right to basic services;
- c. the right to equal protection;
- d. the right to be free from various stigma; and
- e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER IV

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 6

Obligations and responsibilities in implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence are the shared responsibility of:

- a. regional government;
- b. community;
- c. family; and
- d. parents.

#### Article 7

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of local government as referred to in Article 6 letter a, include:

- a. implementing protection policies for women and children from acts of violence established by the government;
- b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence; c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence; d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence;
- e. allocating budget for the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity; and
- f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence.
  - (2) In order to implement the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the local government shall establish action programs and activities for the protection of women and children in 1 (one) Regional Action Plan as the basis for SKPD and UKPD in implementing protection for women and children from acts of violence.
  - (3) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), constitutes part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD).
  - (4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 8

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 6 letter b, shall be implemented in the form of community participation.

(2) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

- a. preventing acts of violence against women and children;
- b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
- c. participating in providing assistance and/or handling victims of acts of violence.
  - (3) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 9

The obligations of the family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 letters c and d, who are legally fully responsible for preventing all forms of violence and protecting women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER V

DUTIES AND AUTHORITIES OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 10

(1) The Local Government has the duty to implement efforts to prevent violence against women and children, in the form of:

- a. collecting data and information on women and children victims of acts of violence, and laws and regulations;
- b. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence;
- c. conducting education on anti-violence values towards women and children; and
- d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence.
  - (2) The Local Government in anticipating acts of violence against women and children, is authorized to provide and implement services for victims in the form of:
- a. providing and facilitating the establishment of integrated services for victims of acts of violence involving institutions and community elements;
- b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims; and
- c. conducting community empowerment for the prevention of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER VI

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional

#### Article 11

(1) In order to provide services and protection to women and children from acts of violence, the local government shall establish PPT as an integrated service center for women and children victims of acts of violence.

(2) PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of elements: Health Office, Youth and Sports Education Office, Social Manpower and Transmigration Office, Cooperatives Industry and Trade Office, Population and Civil Registration Office, Women's Empowerment and Family Planning Agency, Community Empowerment Agency, Civil Service Police Unit, Mataram City Regional General Hospital, Mataram City Police Resort, Non-Governmental Organizations, and Universities.

(3) PPT as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be established by Mayor Decree.

(4) PPT may receive case referrals from Sub-districts or Community Health Centers.

(5) Further provisions regarding PPT as referred to in paragraphs (3) and (4), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 12

(1) In addition to establishing PPT as referred to in Article 11, to support the implementation of empowerment for women victims of acts of violence, the local government may establish P2TP2A.

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of elements: Youth and Sports Education Office, Social Manpower and Transmigration Office, Cooperatives Industry and Trade Office, Population and Civil Registration Office, Women's Empowerment and Family Planning Agency, Community Empowerment Agency, Civil Service Police Unit, Mataram City Regional General Hospital, Mataram City Police Resort, Non-Governmental Organizations, and Universities.

(3) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1), has the main duties and functions as an institution for empowering women victims of acts of violence, namely by providing training to improve capabilities, skills, and independence.

(4) In implementing the main duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (3), P2TP2A may coordinate and become a working partner of PPT.

(5) Further provisions regarding P2TP2A as referred to in paragraphs (1), (2), (3), and (4), shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation

## Part Three

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 17

(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), the regional government undertakes efforts as follows:

- a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;

- c. opening employment opportunities for women;
- d. building community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence;
- e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
- f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women and children; and
- g. opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

- b. pre employment training; and

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

I. GENERAL

The State has the obligation to provide a sense of security to its citizens from threats and actions that can disturb and damage psychological, physical, sexual and economic security. This is philosophically stated in the preamble of the 1945 Constitution, that the purpose of establishing the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia is to protect the entire Nation and Homeland of Indonesia.

Since the Indonesian Government has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women), it must comply with these provisions. Human Rights state that the enforcement and fulfillment of Human Rights is the responsibility of the State, especially the Government.

Through Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection, the government recognizes the existence of children's rights. The inherent rights of children include basic rights as human beings, namely the Right to Life, the Right to Grow and Develop, the Right to Protection and the Right to Participation.

To fulfill the Right to Life, children need sufficient, healthy and nutritious food in adequate quantities, as well as optimal access to health care. Protection from violence that threatens their safety and health must be provided from an early age. Protection is necessary to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. To fulfill the right to grow and develop, children need space to play, exercise, and education that is appropriate to their physical and psychological development. In fulfilling children's rights, every government administrator, society and parents must understand and care about children's rights. The existence of legal instruments and legal apparatus that defend children's interests is necessary for this protection effort. Legislation that is principled on defending the best interests of the child is required to realize protection that is legal in nature.

Furthermore, it is not uncommon to find cases of women and children who experience problems of physical, psychological, sexual and social violence. For example, women and children who are victims of abuse, neglect, eviction and maltreatment (abuse) by parents or other people. For women and children who experience such problems, the government must provide a Safe House (children protection home) or Integrated Service Center (PPT) for women and children who are victims of acts of violence. A Safe House or PPT is a safe and friendly place for women and children. The PPT is equipped with companions from educators, doctors, psychologists, ulama/clergy and is networked with lawyers for women and children as well as the RPK Unit at the Police.

The fate of women and children who are victims of acts of violence must be given attention by the government. Many cases of acts of violence against women and children are often caused by factors that develop in society, such as low levels of economy, education, and environments in the industrial sector. Therefore, victims of acts of violence like this need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights.

For the empowerment of victims of acts of violence, regional governments can establish an Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) as a center for empowering women victims of acts of violence which specifically has the main tasks and functions for the empowerment of women and children who are victims of acts of violence.

From the above framework, the Government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, economically and socially to prevent, suppress, reduce, and eliminate all forms of violence against women and children. From these various efforts, a legal framework will be realized in the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence.

II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a What is meant by "respect for the rights of victims" is a series of actions to respect, appreciate and ensure the fulfillment of the rights of victims.

Letter b What is meant by "justice and gender equality" is a process to be fair to men and women and equal conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and national security activities and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

Letter c What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims by not making distinctions based on age, sex, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d What is meant by "The best interests of victims" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, must be the primary consideration.

#### Article 3

Paragraph (1)

Sufficiently clear.

Paragraph (2)

Letter a

What is meant by the "prevention" aspect is a protection strategy effort through:

- a. Primary prevention, all people, families, communities and the state in efforts to increase the ability of knowledge, understanding and ensure that violence against women and children does not occur, includes socialization of policies, adequate services, supportive workplace policies, as well as life skill training for women and children. What is meant by life skill training includes conflict resolution without violence, stress management skills, resource management, effective decision making, effective interpersonal communication, guidance on the psychosocial development of women and children.
- b. Secondary prevention, aimed at community groups with high risk in efforts to increase skills, including training and victim services to ensure that violence against women and children does not occur in the next generation. Activities carried out here include conducting home visits for parents who have just had children to conduct self-assessment whether they are at risk of committing violence against children in the future.
- c. Tertiary prevention, intended to increase parenting abilities that ensure that violence against women and children is not repeated, what is done here is integrated services for women and children who are victims of violence, through counseling, stress management training.

Letter b What is meant by the "service and assistance" aspect is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals and assistants in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

Letter c What is meant by the "reunification" aspect is an effort to return and restore the physical and psychological condition of victims which then reunites them with the victim's family and community.

Letter d What is meant by the "empowerment" aspect is a process of increasing the role of society to participate in the implementation of protection for women and children who are victims of acts of violence which aims to gain access and participation in the process of protection of women and children broadly.

#### Article 4

Letter a What is meant by "obtaining protection" is obtaining protection from individuals, groups and institutions both governmental and non-governmental.

Letter b What is meant by "obtaining information" is access and information about the existence of complaint centers, PPT, and all matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and involvement in decision-making processes related to assistance and case development.

Letter c What is meant by "optimal services" is services that include medical, extensive medicolegal, psychosocial and legal. Letter d

What is meant by "continuous handling until the rehabilitation stage" is handling that does not stop until physical and psychological recovery, but until the victim can resume life in society including the restoration of their good name.

Letter e What is meant by "confidential handling" is an effort to guarantee certainty for victims that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disseminated.

Letter f What is meant by "receiving psychological assistance" is assistance provided by psychologists to victims suffering from trauma/other mental problems to restore the victim's mental condition. Meanwhile, legal assistance is assistance provided by persons and/or legal aid institutions to victims at every level of examination and during the legal process.

Letter g What is meant by "guarantee of rights related to victim status" is an effort to provide certainty and protection for victims as family members and members of society.
