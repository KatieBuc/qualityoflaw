# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is implemented based on the principles of:

- a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of Victims of Acts of Violence;
- b. justice and gender equality;
  - c. non-discrimination;
- d. the best interests of Victims of acts of violence; and
- e. Principles of the right to life, survival and development,

#### Article 3

The objectives of protecting women and children from acts of violence are to: a.prevent violence against women and children including trafficking in persons; b.eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;

c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children; d.provide services and Empowerment to women and children victims of violence, reporters and witnesses; e. facilitate and mediate domestic disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household; and

f. Facilitate the recovery of the rights, dignity and personal values of victims.

# CHAPTER III

SCOPE

#### Article 4

(1) The Scope of this Regional Regulation consists of a. protection of women; and b. protection of children.

(2) Protection of women as referred to in Article 4 letter a includes: a. prevention of acts of violence;

b. integrated services for victims of acts of violence; and c. empowerment of victims of Acts of violence. (3) Protection of children as referred to in Article 4 letter b includes:

- a. protection of children who are victims of acts of violence; and b. special protection of children.

# CHAPTER IV

IMPLEMENTATION

OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 5

The Regional Government is responsible for efforts to protect women from acts of violence that occur in public or in private life in the form of a. physical violence; b.sexual violence;

c. economic violence; d.social violence;

e. psychological violence;

f. domestic neglect;

g. coercion or deprivation of liberty; and h.threats of certain actions.

#### Article 6

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence as referred to in Article 5 is obligated to make efforts to: a.prevent the continuation of criminal acts; b.provide protection to victims of acts of violence; c. provide emergency assistance; and/or d.provide assistance in the process of submitting an application for the determination of protection.

## Part Two

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 7

(1) The Regional Government carries out prevention as referred to in article 6 letter a by way of: a. socializing laws and regulations; b. providing counseling/guidance; c. providing education on the dangers of domestic violence; d. conducting seminars/workshops or the like; e. forming work networks in efforts to prevent violence; f. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns; g. establishing a violence prevention system, mapping locations or areas prone to violence; and h.increasing public awareness in behaving in accordance with religious norms and customs

(2) In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be carried out by: a.Religious institutions; b.family and/or closest relatives;

c. customary leaders; d.customary institutions;

e. community;

f. educational institutions;

g. health institutions; h.social community institutions; and

i. business world and other institutions.

#### Article 8

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to prepare prevention programs or activities as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (1) in the Regional Work Plan and Regional Strategic Plan;

(2) Programs or activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are to support the realization of the Regional Long-Term Development Plan and Regional Medium-Term Development Plan.

#### Article 9

(1) The Regional Apparatus Organization that conducts governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment shall implement prevention as referred to in Article 8; (2) The Regional Apparatus Organization as referred to in paragraph (1) in carrying out prevention may cooperate with vertical agencies.

## Part Three

Integrated Services for Victims of Acts of Violence Article 10

(1) The Regional Government shall implement integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b through the following activities: a. complaint services;

b. health services;

c. counseling;

d. spiritual guidance;

e. social rehabilitation services;

f. legal aid and assistance services; and/or g. repatriation and reintegration. (2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of: a. special service rooms;

b. expert and professional personnel;

c. service centers and safe houses; and/or d. other facilities and infrastructure necessary for victim recovery.

(3) The provision of services to victims of acts of violence shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government together with P2TP2A and other social institutions;

(4) P2TP2A and other social institutions as referred to in paragraph (3) may receive and send case referrals from other service units in a networked manner;

(5) Integrated services for victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) through (4) shall be implemented in accordance with standard operating procedures; (6) Provisions regarding Standard Operating Procedures as referred to in paragraph (5) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Four

Empowerment of Victims of Acts of Violence Article 11

(1) Empowerment of victims of acts of violence as referred to in article 5 letter c carried out by the Regional Government shall be in the form of: a. job training;

b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and c. capital assistance.

(2) Empowerment as referred to in paragraph 1 shall be carried out by the Regional Apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.

# CHAPTER V

CHILD PROTECTION

## Part One

Protection of Child Victims

of Acts of Violence

#### Article 12

(1) Protection of children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in article 5 letter a shall be the responsibility of the Regional Government. (2) Protection of child victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by: a. formulating policies on prevention, reduction of vulnerable risks, handling of victims and child data and information systems; b. organizing the maintenance, care, and social rehabilitation of neglected children, both inside and outside institutions;

c. providing shelter, maintenance and care for neglected children; and d. organizing special protection for children.

#### Article 13

(1) Provisions regarding the prevention and service of acts of violence for the protection of women as referred to in Articles 7 through 12 shall apply mutatis mutandis to the protection of children from acts of violence;

(2) In addition to carrying out prevention as referred to in paragraph (1), this shall also be done by increasing family capacity in child protection and parenting skills.

## Part Two

Special Protection for Children

#### Article 14

Special protection for children shall be provided to:

(1)

a. Children in emergency situations;

b. Children in conflict with the law;

c. Children from minority and isolated groups;

d. Children who are exploited economically and/or sexually; e. Children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse; f. Children who are victims of pornography; g. Children with HIV/AIDS;

h. Child victims of kidnapping, sale, and/or trafficking; i. Child victims of physical and/or psychological violence; j. Child victims of sexual crimes; k. Child victims of terrorist networks; l. Children with Disabilities;

m. Child victims of abuse and neglect; n. Children with deviant social behavior; and o. Children who are victims of stigmatization from labeling related to the condition of their Parents. (2) The Regional Government in carrying out the handling of cases as referred to in paragraph (1) shall comply with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part One General

#### Article 15

(1) The Regional Government shall carry out cooperation and coordination for the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence.

(2) Cooperation and coordination as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by the Regional Apparatus Organization that conducts governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

## Part Two

Institutional Framework for the Protection of Women and Children

#### Article 16

(1) The Regional Government in conducting cooperation and coordination for the Protection of Women and children from acts of violence, shall establish a Forum for the Care of Women and Children from Acts of Violence (FP2ATK);

(2) FP2ATK membership shall consist of elements from:

- a. regional government;
- b. vertical agencies;
- c. educational institutions;
- d. health institutions;
- e. social institutions;
- f. manpower institutions; and
- g. community organizations concerned with the protection of women and children from acts of violence.
  - (3) The Organizational Structure of FP2ATK as referred to in paragraph (2) shall consist of: a. policy formulation working group; b. socialization working group; c. service working group; and
- d. data, information, monitoring and evaluation working group. (4) The establishment and term of office of FP2ATK members as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent Decree.

#### Article 17

FP2ATK shall have the following duties:

- a. provide input in policy formulation; b. coordinate with Regional Apparatus Organizations and other social institutions;
- c. socialize laws and regulations relating to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
- d. collect and develop data and information related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence;
- e. conduct review, monitoring, evaluation and supervision of the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence; and
- f. provide reports, suggestions, input and considerations to the Regent.

#### Article 18

FP2ATK as referred to in article 16 paragraph (2) shall be assisted by the Secretariat at the Regional Apparatus Organization that handles affairs of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 19

Further provisions regarding the mechanism and working procedures of FP2ATK shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 20

(1) The Regional Government in order to provide services to victims of acts of violence, shall establish P2TP2A. (2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall have the following duties:

a. provide services, including:

- 1. receive complaints/reports of violence against women and/or children, consultation, and counseling; 2. receive and send case referrals from the Regency/City Regional Government or institutions established by the Regency/City Regional Government; 3. provide legal assistance;

4. health services;

5. social rehabilitation;

6. legal services; and

7. repatriation and social reintegration.

b. provide protection to victims of acts of violence, reporters and witnesses. (3) The appointment of P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent Decree. (4) Regional Apparatus shall provide guidance to P2TP2A in carrying out duties as referred to in paragraph (2).

L GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination. In order to prevent and address violence against women and children in North Lampung Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades their human dignity, it is necessary to provide protection to women and child victims of violence in the form of Regional Regulation to regulate protective measures, thus requiring institutional support and regulations that can guarantee its implementation, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and child victims of violence in North Lampung Regency

What is meant by "physical violence" is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or defects on a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death. Letter b.

What is meant by "sexual violence" is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or specific purposes.

What is meant by "economic violence" is an act that restricts humans from working inside or outside the home to produce money and goods, including allowing humans who work to be exploited, while someone does not fulfill the economic needs of the family.

What is meant by "social violence" is an act of a person or group of people that causes injury or loss of life of a person or can cause physical damage or damage to other people's property

Letter e.

What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feeling helpless and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

What is meant by "Domestic Neglect" is:

- 1. actions that result in the failure to meet the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social;
- 2. deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of children as they should be; 3. actions that neglect people within the household scope, even though according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide life, care, or maintenance to that person; 4. actions that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

Letter g.

What is meant by "worker neglect" is an act of any person or legal entity that results in improper treatment of female workers even though according to the law applicable to them or because of an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide adequate protection from lawful actions against workers such as haphazard recruitment, unclear shelter and workplace, arbitrary business owners.
