REGIONAL REGULATION OF CENTRAL LOMBOK REGENCY NUMBER 22 OF 2024

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD THE REGENT OF CENTRAL LOMBOK,

Considering Recalling that women and children are God's creatures and assets of the nation who must be protected, respected, and have their human rights fulfilled by the State, law and government to obtain true welfare as stated in Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia: that women and children need protection from various forms of violence, exploitation, child marriage, and neglect, there is a need for strategic efforts from the Regional Government and other parties who are obliged to provide protection for women and children, that Regional Regulation of Central Lombok Regency Number 3 of 2009 concerning Protection of Children and Women Victims of Violence is no longer in accordance with the development of laws and policies on the protection of women and children established by the government and provincial government so it needs to be replaced, that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children.

# CHAPTER III

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

#### Article 9

The implementation of Protection of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

- a. efforts to improve family quality,
- b. efforts to fulfill the special needs of women, c. efforts to prevent and Protect Women victims of Violence, Exploitation, and Discrimination in a fast, integrated, and comprehensive manner, and
- d. efforts to Protect Women in disaster situations.

#### Article 10

Efforts to improve family quality as referred to in Article 9 letter a, are implemented through:

- a. improving child quality through the provision of access to information, education, counseling, and services regarding child care, nurturing, Protection, and development,
- b. improving adolescent quality through the provision of access to information, education, counseling, and services regarding family life,
  - c. improving the functioning, role, and duties of the family, d. empowering vulnerable families through Protection and assistance and/or facilitation to develop themselves to be equal with other families,

- f. increasing access and opportunities to receive information and family economic resources: and g. developing innovative methods through assistance and/or more effective facilitation for underprivileged families, and developing programs and activities in efforts to reduce poverty rates for underprivileged families and women who serve as heads of families.

#### Article 11

#### Article 12

- a. prevention of women from becoming victims of Violence, Exploitation, and Discrimination:
- b. handling of women victims of Violence, Exploitation, and Discrimination, and
- c. Empowerment of Women victims of Violence, Exploitation, and Discrimination.

#### Article 13

Efforts to Protect Women in disaster situations as referred to in Article 9 letter d, are implemented through:

- a. health services, and
- b. provision of women-friendly facilities at disaster evacuation sites.

Regional Regulation of Central Lombok Regency number 3 year 2009 concerning Protection of child and women victims of violence has been in force for approximately 14 years. During the period of its validity there have been many changes in policies and laws and regulations in the field of Protection of children and women whether taken by the central government, provincial government or Central Lombok regency government. Therefore at the regency regional regulation level adjustments need to be made so that the Central Lombok regency government has a legal basis in implementing policies and programs in the field of Protection of women and children.

The field of regional governance has undergone changes with the revocation of Law Number 32 Year 2004 concerning Regional Governance by Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Governance. Based on article 12 of Law Number 23 year 2014 concerning Regional Governance that the affairs of women's empowerment and child protection constitute Mandatory Government Affairs that are not related to Basic Services. In the appendix to Law Number 23 year 2014 concerning Regional Governance is regulated in more detail the division of regency government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection. The field of women includes authorities: a). Prevention of violence against women involving parties within the scope of regency/city Region. b). Provision of services for women victims of violence requiring coordination at regency/city Region level. c. Strengthening and development of institutions providing Protection services for women at regency/city Region level. Field. Women's Quality of Life includes, Institutionalization of PUG at government institutions at regency/city Region level. b.
