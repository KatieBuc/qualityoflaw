REGIONAL REGULATION OF KUBU RAYA REGENCY NUMBER 9 YEAR 2016

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY THE REGENT OF KUBU RAYA,

Considering Whereas

a. that violence against women constitutes a violation of human rights so that women need to be protected in their dignity and honor and guaranteed their right to life in accordance with their nature and disposition;

b. that to overcome violence against women in Kubu Raya Regency, prevention efforts and optimization of overcoming violence against women through policies to ensure legal protection for women are needed;

c. that to provide legal protection for women, the regulation of protection of women from acts of violence needs to be regulated in a Regional Regulation;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women From Acts of Violence;

1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia Year 1945;

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Region is Kubu Raya Regency.
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government Administrator who leads the Implementation of Government Affairs which are the Authority of the Autonomous Region.
- 3. Regent is the Regent of Kubu Raya.
- 4. Regional Work Unit hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD is an Organization or institution in the regional government that is responsible to the Regent.
- 5. Women are human beings of female gender starting from infants, adolescents, adults up to the elderly.
- 6. Women with Disabilities are women who have personal limitations, which can be physical, cognitive, mental, sensory, emotional, developmental or some combination of these.
- 7. Elderly Women are women who are 60 years of age and above.
- 8. Violence is any act that results in or can result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
- 9. Violence against women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering to women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically including certain threats, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
- 10. Victim is a woman who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence and life vulnerability.
- 11. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the regional government, community, and/or other parties who know or hear that violence against victims will or has occurred.
- 12. Service/handling is action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and/or knowing that violence against women will, is or has occurred.
- 13. Assistance is a person or association from an institution that has expertise in providing assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and/or advocacy.
- 14. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is an institution or functional work unit that provides integrated services to victims of violence.
- 15. Safe House is a place of residence for victims during the assistance process for the security and comfort of victims from threats and danger.
- 16. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a performance benchmark for integrated service units in providing 5 (five) types of services for women and children victims of violence, namely (1) Complaint Handling, (2) Health Services, (3) Social Rehabilitation, (4) Law Enforcement and Legal Assistance, and (5) Repatriation and Social Reintegration.

1 7. Complaint Handling is a series of actions taken by integrated service providers to follow up reports of acts of violence against women submitted by victims, families and the community.

18. Health Services are efforts that include promotive, curative and rehabilitative aspects.

19. Social Rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the capabilities of a person who experiences social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions normally.

20. Law Enforcement is the action of officials authorized by the State to implement laws and regulations.

21. Legal Assistance is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocacy to carry out the process of assisting witnesses and/or victims of violence against women that is gender sensitive.

22. Repatriation is the effort to return women victims of violence from abroad to the debarkation/entry point, or from the receiving area to the area of origin.

23. Social Reintegration is the effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family or community that can provide protection and fulfillment for victims.

24. Integrated Service Unit hereinafter abbreviated as UPT is a unit that performs integrated service functions for women victims of violence.

25. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in one straight line upwards or downwards.

26. Household is husband, wife and children, people who have family relations due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care and guardianship and/or domestic workers who reside in the household.

#### Article 2

Protection of Women from acts of violence is based on: a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of victims;

b. justice and gender equality;

c. non-discrimination; and

d. the best interests of the victim.

#### Article 3

Protection of women from acts of violence aims to:

- a. prevent violence against women;
- b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women; c. protect and provide a sense of security for women; d. facilitate and mediate domestic disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household; e. provide services and/or handling to victims; and f. empower women victims of violence and vulnerable groups.

# CHAPTER II

SCOPE OF PROTECTION

#### Article 4

The scope of protection of women from acts of violence includes: a. prevention;

b. services and/or handling; and

c. empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

PREVENTION, SERVICES AND/OR HANDLING

AND EMPOWERMENT

## Part One

Prevention

#### Article 5

(1) Prevention efforts as referred to in Article 4 letter a shall be carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the SKPD responsible for Women's Empowerment.

(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by means of:

- a. establishing a working network;
- b. conducting coordination, integration, and synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
- c. establishing a violence prevention system;
- d. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of women victims of violence, including raising awareness among families and communities;
- e. providing education for women victims of violence including access to employment opportunities; and
- f. through complete and easily accessible communication, information and education media.
  - (3) In addition to prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), prevention efforts shall also be carried out by families and/or closest relatives, communities, educational institutions and customary institutions.

(2) PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from and/or to other service units in a networked manner.

(3) Further provisions regarding the development, mechanism, and working relationship of integrated PPT networks as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER III

VIOLENCE

#### Article 13

Violence against women consists of:

- a. physical violence;
- b. psychological violence;
- c. sexual violence;
- d. neglect;
- e. exploitation; and/or
- f. other violence.

#### Article 14

Physical violence as referred to in Article 13 letter a includes acts that result in pain, wounds, injury or disability to the body, miscarriage, unconsciousness, and/or death.

#### Article 15

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 13 letter b includes acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness, and/or severe psychological suffering.

#### Article 16

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 13 letter c includes: a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;

b. coercion of sexual relations in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or

c. coercion of sexual relations with others for commercial purposes and/or specific purposes.

#### Article 17

Neglect as referred to in Article 13 letter d includes: a. acts that neglect women within their household; and/or

b. acts that result in economic dependency.

#### Article 18

Exploitation as referred to in Article 13 letter e includes: a. acts that exploit women economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others.

b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the energy or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.

c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of victims for profit, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

#### Article 19

Other violence as referred to in Article 13 letter f includes: a. any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without the use of means that create fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom.

b. a situation in which a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

CHAPTER IV

RIGHTS OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 20

Every woman victim of violence has the right to:

- a. have her dignity and honor respected as a human being;
- b. receive physical and psychological health recovery from the suffering experienced;
- c. determine her own decisions;
- d. receive information;
- e. have her identity kept confidential;
- f. receive compensation;
- g. receive social rehabilitation;
- h. receive spiritual guidance;
  - 1. receive complaint handling;
    - J. receive facilitation in the judicial process;
- k. receive assistance; and
  - I. guarantee of rights related to status as a family member and community member.

# CHAPTER VI

RESPONSIBILITIES OF GOVERNMENT AND INSTITUTIONS

## Part One

Regional Government

#### Article 21

(1) The Regional Government is obligated and responsible for implementing victim protection efforts by means of:

- a. establishing PPT;
- b. formulating policies;
- c. preparing plans and implementing programs and activities; d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure; and
- e. supervising the provision of services to victims with minimum service standards.
  - (2) In implementing the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall prepare a Regional Action Plan for Women's Protection.
  - (3) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 22

The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (1) shall be coordinated by the SKPD responsible for women's empowerment issues.

## Part Two

Community and Family

#### Article 23

Communities and families have obligations and responsibilities to: a. prevent the occurrence of violence against women;

b. report when violence occurs;

c. protect victims; and

d. provide emergency assistance.

## Part Three

Coordination of Women's Protection

#### Article 24

(1) Services and/or handling for victims shall be conducted in a networked manner.

(2) The network as referred to in paragraph (1) aims to: a. coordinate and synchronize the handling of ICS services;

b. develop networks and referral systems; and

c. collect, compile and present reports on violence.

(3) The network as referred to in paragraph (1) carries out its duties and functions in accordance with the forms of services as referred to in Article 7.

# CHAPTER VI

INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER

#### Article 25

(1) In providing services and temporary protection in the form of safe houses (shelters) for victims, an ICS needs to be established.

(2) The ICS may be established by vertical agencies in the Region, Regional Government, and/or community institutions.

(3) The ICS established by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of elements of the Regional Police, RWUs that oversee Health, Education, Social Affairs, Community Empowerment, Women's Empowerment and Child Protection as well as Community Social Institutions that have the function of providing protection to women from acts of violence conducted in a networked manner.

(4) In the event that the ICS does not have a safe house (shelter) as referred to in paragraph (1), the victim shall be referred to an ICS that has a safe house.

(5) The ICS as referred to in paragraph (2) consists of several counseling fields including:

- a. legal field;
- b. health field;
- c. spiritual field; and
- d. psychology field.
  - (6) The ICS as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree.

# CHAPTER VII

INFORMATION AND REPORTING SYSTEM

#### Article 26

(1) The RWU that oversees Women's Empowerment shall implement the information system and submit reports on the implementation of women's protection to the Regent.

(2) Further provisions regarding the information and reporting system as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

FINANCING

#### Article 27

Financing for women's protection activities conducted by the Regional Government shall be sourced from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget and/or other legitimate sources in accordance with laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 28

(1) The community shall participate in the implementation of women's protection.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of: a. establishing family partners at the sub-district/village level by the community;

b. establishing women's protection units in community organizations;

c. conducting socialization of women's rights independently;

d. conducting preventive measures if indications of violence against women are found in the community;

e. providing first aid to victims; and

f. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against women occurs in their environment.

(3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, community social institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and/or mass media.

# CHAPTER X

DEVELOPMENT AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 29

(1) The Regional Government shall conduct development and supervision of the implementation of women's protection.

(2) Development and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall include:

- a. coordination;
- b. guidance;
- c. education and training; and
- d. monitoring and evaluation.
  - (3) Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a shall cover planning and implementation.
  - (4) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b shall cover planning, implementation, procedures, financing, quality, control, and supervision.
  - (5) Education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.
  - (6) Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d shall be conducted based on laws and regulations periodically every 6 (six) months.

# CHAPTER XI

CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 30

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by its placement in the Regional Gazette of Kubu Raya Regency.

I. GENERAL.

Acts of violence against women constitute violations of human rights therefore, their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

In order to prevent and address violence against women in Kubu Raya Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades their degree and human dignity, it is necessary to establish a regional regulation on the protection of women.

Women's protection constitutes all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention.

Existing laws and regulations or those related to the protection of women victims of violence have not yet regulated efforts to protect women in the regions, therefore institutional support and regulations at the regional level are needed to guarantee its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates efforts to protect women particularly in the form of prevention, services/handling, and empowerment of women in Kubu Raya Regency.

#### Article 2

Letter a

What is meant by "respect for and fulfillment of victims' rights" is the guarantee of the fulfillment of victims' basic rights.

Letter b

What is meant by "justice" is a state where every person, both men and women, are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to gain access and welfare.

Letter c

Letter d
