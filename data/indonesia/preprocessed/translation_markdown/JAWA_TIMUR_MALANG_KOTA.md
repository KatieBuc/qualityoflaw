# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by: 1. Region is Malang City.

2. Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.

3. Mayor is the Mayor of Malang.

4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the regional head and DPRD in organizing Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.

5. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including an unborn child.

6. Woman is an adult human of female gender and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.

7. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent acts of violence against women and children.

8. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties, whether temporary.

9. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.

10. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage or fainting and/or causes death.

11. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, distrust or severe psychological suffering to a person.

12. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual relations, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

13. Victim is a woman or child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.

14. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations. 15. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and a collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.

16. Parent is a biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother. 17. Service is an immediate activity and action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence. 18. Assistance is any action in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the self-strengthening of victims of violence to resolve the problems they face.

19. Repatriation is an effort to return women and children victims of violence to their family, foster family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.

20. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in the community.

21. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with their family, community, institution, or other social environment that can provide protection.

22. Institution is an agency/service/body/office within the scope of regional government and/or non-governmental organization that provides assistance.

23. Integrated Service Center hereinafter referred to as PPT is a generic term that can be used by various institutions that provide services for victims of violence such as the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, Safe House, Child Social Protection House, Trauma Center Protection House, Women's Social Protection House, Shelter House, etc.

24. Regional Action Plan is the foundation and guideline for related agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out prevention and protection activities for women and children victims of violence.

25. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out based on the principles of:

- a. respect for the rights of victims;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination; and
- d. the best interests of the victim.

#### Article 3

(1) The objectives of implementing protection for women and children victims of violence are to:

- a. prevent violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;
- b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
- c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
- d. provide services to women and children victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses; and
- e. facilitate and mediate domestic disputes to realize the integrity of a harmonious and prosperous household.
  - (2) The objectives of implementing protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include aspects of:
- a. prevention;
- b. service and assistance;
- c. reunification; and
- d. empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 4

Forms of violence against women and children may include:

- a. physical violence;
- b. psychological violence;
- c. sexual violence;
- d. neglect; and
- e. exploitation.

#### Article 5

Physical violence as referred to in Article 4 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

#### Article 6

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 4 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 7

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 4 letter c is caused by:

- a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
- b. forced sexual relations;
- c. forced sexual relations that are unnatural or unwanted; and/or
- d. forced sexual relations with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 8

Neglect as referred to in Article 4 letter d is caused by:

- a. acts that result in the failure to properly fulfill the needs of a child, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for care;
- b. acts of intentionally neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a child properly committed by parents, guardians or any other party responsible for their care;
- c. acts that neglect a person within their household, when according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide a livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
- d. acts that result in economic dependence by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 9

Exploitation as referred to in Article 4 letter e is caused by:

- a. actions that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
- b. actions with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the energy or capability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial; and/or
- c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

# CHAPTER IV

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 10

Every victim has the right to receive:

- a. protection;
- b. information;
- c. optimal services;
- d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage; e. confidential handling;
- f. psychological and legal assistance; and g. guarantees of rights related to status as a family member and community member.

#### Article 11

Child victims of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 10, also receive special rights, as follows:

- a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth and development; b. the right to basic services;
- c. the right to equal protection;
- d. the right to be free from various stigmas; and
- e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER V

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 12

Obligations and responsibilities in implementing protection for women and children victims of violence are a shared responsibility: a. regional government;

b. community;

c. family; and

d. parents.

#### Article 13

(1) The Regional Government implements protection efforts for women and children victims of violence through: a. implementing protection policies for women and children victims of violence applied by the government;

b. establishing policies, programs, and protection activities for women and children victims of violence;

c. establishment of PPT;

d. ensuring the implementation of service facilitation to victims;

e. pursuing effectiveness and efficiency for the victim recovery process; and

f. pursuing the creation of cooperation and coordination in victim recovery efforts.

(2) In order to implement the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, the regional government establishes programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in 1 (one) Regional Action Plan as a basis for regional apparatus in implementing protection for women and children victims of violence.

(3) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD).

(4) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is further regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 14

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 12 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation.

(2) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

- a. establishing family partners at the sub-district level; b. establishing women and children protection units within community organizations;
- c. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
- d. providing first aid to victims; and e. reporting to the competent authorities if violence against victims occurs in their environment. (3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, private sector, and mass media.
  - (4) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 15

The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 12 letters c and d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER VI

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Establishment of PPT

#### Article 16

(1) In order to provide services and protection to women and children from violence, the Regional Government establishes PPT as an integrated service center for women and children victims of violence.

(2) PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of elements of the Health Office, Youth and Sports Education Office, Social Office, Cooperative Industry and Trade Office, Population and Civil Registration Office, Women's Empowerment and Family Planning Agency, Community Empowerment Agency, Civil Service Police Unit, Regional General Hospital, City Police Resort, Non-Governmental Organizations, and Universities.

(3) PPT as referred to in paragraph (2), is established by decree of the Mayor.

(4) The implementation of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by PPT.

(5) PPT as referred to in paragraph (4) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a network.

(6) PPT provides services and temporary protection in the form of a safe house.

(7) In the event that PPT does not yet have a safe house, victims of violence are referred to PPT that has a safe house. (8) PPT consists of several counseling fields.

(9) Counseling fields as referred to in paragraph (8) consist of at least:

- a. legal field;
- b. health field;
- c. spiritual field; and
- d. psychology field.

(10)Further provisions regarding the organizational structure of PPT are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 19

Forms of services to victims organized by PPT include:

- a. Medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical wounds that aim to restore the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;
- b. Medicolegal services are a form of medical service for the purpose of evidence in the legal field; c. Psychosocial services are services provided in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have a sense of confidence, strength and independence, in solving their problems; d. Legal services to assist victims in undergoing legal processes, and;
- e. Economic independence services in the form of skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

(2) The mechanism for implementing duties as referred to in paragraph (1), is organized according to Standard Operating Procedures which are further regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Three

Prevention by Regional Apparatus

#### Article 21

(1) To prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children, the government carries out empowerment and awareness-raising to families, parents and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.

(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness-raising as referred to in paragraph (1), the government undertakes efforts as follows:

- a. increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;
- b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;
- c. opening employment opportunities for women; d. building community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of women and children from violence;
- e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
- f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women and children; and
- g. opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children from violence.

#### Article 22

(1) Prevention of the occurrence of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 21, is carried out by regional apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:

- a. social;
- b. health;
- c. education;
- d. manpower;
- e. women's empowerment and child protection; f. mental and spiritual; and
- g. peace and order.
  - (2) Prevention of violence by regional apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Action Plan.

## Part Four

Mechanism for Accompaniment by Persons and/or Institutions in Cooperation with PPT

#### Article 23

(1) Accompaniment is carried out by persons and/or institutions in cooperation with PPT.

(2) The accompaniment mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1), is organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) which are further regulated by Mayor's Regulation.

## Part Five

Principles of Service and Accompaniment

#### Article 24

The provision of services and accompaniment to victims, is carried out with the following principles:

- a. fast, safe, and empathetic;
- b. guarantee of confidentiality;
- c. easily accessible; and
- d. no fees charged.

## Part Six

Services

#### Article 25

(1) Forms of services provided to women and children victims of violence, are as follows:

- a. complaint services;
- b. health services;
- c. legal aid;
- d. repatriation;
- e. rehabilitation, social reintegration, and medicolegal; f. identification services; and
- g. psychological services.
  - (2) Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are carried out in accordance with minimum service standards established by the government and carried out by regional apparatus whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
- a. social;
- b. health;
- c. women's empowerment and child protection; and
- d. mental and spiritual.
  - (3) In carrying out the duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (2), the regional government cooperates with government agencies, provincial government, other regency/city governments, the community, families and parents.
  - (4) Further provisions regarding the procedures for services and handling of women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3), are regulated by Mayor's Regulation.

SOURCES OF FUNDS

#### Article 29

I. GENERAL

Violence against women and children constitutes a violation of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. The fate of women and children victims of violence must be given attention by the regional government. The numerous cases of violence against women and children are often caused by factors developing within society, for example low economic levels, education, environments located in industrial sectors. Therefore, victims of such violence need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights.

For the empowerment of victims of violence, the regional government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT) which is a generic term that can be used by various institutions providing services for victims of violence such as the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A), Safe House, Social Protection House for Children (RPSA), Trauma Center Protection House (RPTC), Social Protection House for Women (RPSW), Shelter House, etc.

Which specifically has the main duties and functions for the empowerment of women and children victims of violence. The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to carry out efforts to protect women and children victims of violence through: implementing protection policies for women and children victims of violence adopted by the government, establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence, establishment of PPT, ensuring the implementation of service convenience for victims, seeking effectiveness and efficiency for the victim recovery process, and seeking to create cooperation and coordination in victim recovery efforts.

#### Article 3

Paragraph (1)

Sufficiently clear.

Paragraph (2)

Point a

What is meant by the "prevention" aspect is a protection strategy effort through:

- a. Primary prevention, all people, families, society and the state in efforts to improve the ability of knowledge, understanding and prevent violence against women and children from occurring, including policy socialization, adequate services, supportive workplace policies, and life skill training for women and children.

What is meant by life skill training includes conflict resolution without violence, stress management skills, resource management, making effective decisions, effective interpersonal communication, guidance on psychosocial development of women and children.

b. Secondary prevention, aimed at high-risk community groups in efforts to improve skills, including training and victim services to prevent violence against women and children from occurring in the next generation. Activities carried out here include home visits for parents who have just had children to conduct self assessment of whether they are at risk of committing violence against children in the future.

c. Tertiary prevention, intended to improve parenting abilities that prevent violence against women and children from recurring, what is done here is integrated services for women and children who are victims of violence, through counseling and stress management training.

Point b

What is meant by the "service and assistance" aspect is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals and assistants according to their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

Point c

What is meant by the "reunification" aspect is an effort to return and restore the physical and psychological condition of victims which then reunites them with the victim's family and community.

Point d

What is meant by the "empowerment" aspect is a process to increase the role of the community to participate in the administration of protection for women and children victims of violence which aims to gain access and participation in the process of protection of women and children broadly.
