# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this regional regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Regional Government is the Mayor and regional apparatus as elements of the organizer of the Regional Government of South Tangerang City.
- 2. Mayor is the Mayor of South Tangerang.
- 3. Implementation is the form of implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.
- 4. Woman is a person who has female genitals, can experience menstruation, pregnancy, childbirth, breastfeeding and includes a person who has obtained legal status as a Woman.
- 5. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb.
- 6. Violence is any act that results in or can result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim.
- 7. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in public or private life.
- 8. Physical violence is any act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability on a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or causes death.
- 9. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.
- 10. Economic violence is any act that neglects family members in the form of not providing a proper living, care or maintenance.
- 11. Psychological violence is any act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and or severe psychological suffering to a person.
- 12. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
- 13. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear of or has occurred violence against women.
- 14. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
- 15. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment hereinafter referred to as P2TP2A is an institution that provides services and protection for women and children, especially those who have problems due to acts of violence, discrimination, human trafficking and other social problems in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psycho-social, public facility services and legal protection and assistance.
- 16. Community Social Institution is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and or other Community Organizations.
- 17. Residents are Citizens of South Tangerang and foreigners who enter legally and reside in the area of South Tangerang City. 18. Service is an action that must be immediately taken to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing of, during or after the occurrence of violence against the victim.
- 19. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy in order to provide strengthening and recovery of victims of violence as stipulated in a decision of an authorized official.
- 20. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy and spiritual guidance for self-empowerment of victims of violence, especially victims of domestic violence, to resolve the problems they face.
- 21. Integrated Service Center hereinafter referred to as PPT is a unified unit that carries out integrated service functions for women and children victims of violence, which is based on hospitals, Community Health Centers, P2TP2A and other similar institutions that are managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medico-legal), psycho-social and legal services.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence is carried out based on respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity, non-discrimination, gender equality and justice as well as protection of women's and children's human rights and the best interests of victims.

#### Article 3

The Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to provide prevention, protection, and services to women and children against gender-based violence and the best interests of children that occur in households or in public.

#### Article 4

The activities for the prevention of violence as referred to in Article 3, include:

- a. Socialization;
- b. Dissemination; and
- c. Training.

#### Article 5

The activities for the protection and services to victims as referred to in Article 3, include:

- a. Health services;
- b. Victim assistance;
- c. Counseling;
- d. Spiritual guidance;
- e. Resocialization; and
- f. Empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 6

Every woman and child victim of violence has the right to: a. Protection;

b. Obtain information;

c. Optimal service;

d. Continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage;

e. Confidential handling;

f. Psychological and legal assistance;

g. Guarantee of rights related to status as a family member as well as a member of society.

# CHAPTER IV

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 7

(1) The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to carry out efforts to prevent and overcome violence against women and children.

(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented in the form of:

- a. Collecting data and information about women and children victims of violence;
- b. Conducting education on anti-violence values against women and children;
- c. Conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence;
- d. Training for PPT members;
- e. Conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.
  - (3) To anticipate acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government is obliged to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
- a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving relevant community elements; b. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for victims.

#### Article 8

In carrying out its obligations and responsibilities as referred to in Article 7, the Regional Government must pay attention to the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands, wives or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

# CHAPTER V

INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK FOR THE PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 9

(1) In the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence which includes prevention and mitigation activities, an Integrated Service Center (PPT) is established.

(2) The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), consists of Regional Government elements and elements from related institutions outside the Regional Government

(3) Further provisions regarding the PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be further regulated by Mayor Regulation.

(4) The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), in implementing the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence may coordinate with institutions and or organizations in South Tangerang City and other regions.

(5) In the Implementation of Protection for women and children victims of violence which includes prevention and mitigation activities, an Integrated Service Post may be established at the sub-district and village levels.

# CHAPTER VI

HANDLING AND MECHANISM

#### Article 10

(1) The implementation of Protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner in medical treatment, legal protection, medicolegal, psychological and economic which can be implemented through partnerships with social community institutions engaged in the protection of women and children.

(2) The implementation of protection for victims as referred to in paragraph (1), must take into account religious norms as well as the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for the victim.

(3) Principles of Service and Assistance:

- a. Fast, safe and empathetic;
- b. Guarantee of confidentiality;
- c. Easily accessible;
- d. No charges imposed.
  - (4) The mechanism for handling protection as referred to in paragraph (1), is stipulated by Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

FORMS OF VIOLENCE AND PROTECTION

#### Article 11

(1) Forms of violence against Women and Children include physical, sexual, economic and psychological.

(2) Forms of physical violence include any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disabilities to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and or causes death.

(3) Forms of sexual violence include sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner, forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or specific purposes.

(4) Forms of economic violence include neglect of family members in the form of not providing proper livelihood, care or maintenance.

(5) Forms of psychological violence include acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feeling helpless and or severe psychological suffering on a person.

#### Article 12

(1) Every woman and child who becomes a victim of violence must receive protection in accordance with applicable provisions.

(2) Protection for women and children victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out for residents of South Tangerang City whether committed in South Tangerang City or outside South Tangerang City.

(3) Protection for women and children victims of violence as an initial action is also carried out for non-residents of South Tangerang City where the incident occurred in the South Tangerang City area, followed by coordination with the region of origin of the resident.

#### Article 13

(1) Forms of protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence include medical, legal, medicolegal (forensic medicine), economic and psychological protection.

(2) Forms of medical protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the victim's physical condition carried out by medical and paramedical personnel.

(3) Forms of legal protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include services and assistance to help victims undergo legal and judicial processes.

(4) Forms of medicolegal protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include medical services for the purposes of evidence in the legal field.

(5) Forms of psychological protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include assistance in order to restore the victim's traumatic condition including provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide psychological support so that victims have self-confidence, strength and independence in problem solving.

(6) Forms of economic protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), include services for skills and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.

# CHAPTER VIII

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 14

(1) The Regional Government in implementing the protection of Women and Children Victims of violence, may involve community participation. (2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), may be carried out by individuals, groups or social community organizations.

#### Article 15

(1) Social community organizations that participate in the implementation of protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 14 paragraph (2), are provided guidance by the Regional Government through Regional Work Units related to the duties of protecting and empowering women and children.

(2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), may include social guidance, skills guidance and operational technical guidance.

# CHAPTER IX

FUNDING SOURCES AND REPORTING

#### Article 16

(1) The source of funding for the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence carried out by the regional apparatus of South Tangerang City is from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of South Tangerang City.

(2) The source of funding as referred to in paragraph (1), may also come from other legal and non-binding sources.

(3) The source of funding for implementation from other legal sources as referred to in paragraph (2), is supervised in accordance with statutory regulations.

(4) The PPT is obligated to report the implementation of protection activities for women and children victims of violence periodically to the Mayor in accordance with applicable regulations.

# CHAPTER X

SANCTIONS PROVISIONS

#### Article 17

(1) Institutions or bodies established by the Mayor to carry out service duties that violate the principles of service as set forth in Article 10 paragraph (3), may be subject to administrative sanctions.

(2) Perpetrators of acts of violence shall be subject to criminal sanctions in accordance with applicable Law.

(3) Any person who intentionally does not provide protection to women and children resulting in the occurrence of violence, allows violence to occur, does not report and does not provide protection to victims, shall be subject to sanctions as stipulated in applicable statutory regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

C L O S I N G

#### Article 18

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of its stipulation. In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of South Tangerang City.

I. GENERAL

Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. The State takes the view that all forms of violence, especially domestic violence, constitute a violation of human rights and a crime against human dignity as well as a form of discrimination.

This view of the State is based on Article 28 of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, together with its amendments. Article 28G paragraph (1) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia stipulates that "Every person shall have the right to protection of his/her person, family, honor, dignity, and property under his/her control, and shall have the right to feel secure and to protection from the threat of fear to do or not to do something which is a human right". Article 28H paragraph (2) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia stipulates that "Every person shall have the right to receive facilities and special treatment to obtain the same opportunities and benefits in order to achieve equality and justice".

Current developments show that acts of physical, psychological, sexual violence and domestic neglect do in fact occur, necessitating adequate legal instruments to eliminate violence, particularly domestic violence. Legal reform that favors vulnerable or subordinated groups, particularly women and children, has become essential in connection with the large number of cases of violence, especially domestic violence; this also occurs in South Tangerang City, in 2010 violence cases reported to the Tangerang Police numbered 143 cases, at the South Jakarta Metro Police 56 cases were recorded, Tigaraksa Police

Tangerang Regency 23 cases and in 2011 there were 70 cases recorded at the South Jakarta Metro Police and 28 cases at the Tigaraksa Police Tangerang Regency, this does not yet reflect the actual conditions in society, because cases of violence especially those occurring within the household are still considered shameful in the view of society and also the courage of the community to report incidents still needs to be improved, therefore a regional regulation on the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence needs to be owned by South Tangerang City.

The emergence of cases of violence against children and women is often caused by factors that develop in society, such as low economic levels, education, social environment and others. Therefore, victims of such violence need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights. From the above framework, the Regional Government is responsible for taking actions both legally, politically, economically and socially to suppress, prevent, reduce and eliminate all forms of violence against children and women.

In carrying out guidance, development and protection of women and children, the role of the community is needed, whether through institutions for the protection of women and children, religious institutions, non-governmental organizations, community organizations, social organizations, business sectors, mass media, or educational institutions.

This Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children from violence is closely related to several other prevailing laws and regulations, among others, Law Number 1 of 1946 concerning the Criminal Code and its Amendments, Law Number 8 of 1981 concerning the Criminal Procedure Code, Law Number 1 of 1974 concerning Marriage, Law Number 7 of 1984 concerning Ratification of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women, Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human

Rights and most importantly Law number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence and Law number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.

This Regional Regulation, in addition to regulating matters of prevention and protection as well as recovery of victims of violence, also regulates matters of obligations for law enforcement officials, health workers, social workers, volunteer assistants, or spiritual counselors to protect victims so that they are more sensitive and responsive. To carry out violence prevention, Agencies or Services that have duties and responsibilities related to the field of women's empowerment and child protection carry out preventive actions, among others, organizing communication, information, and education on violence prevention, protection of women and children.

Based on this thinking, it is time to establish a Regional Regulation in South Tangerang City on the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, particularly Domestic Violence, which is regulated in a comprehensive, clear, and firm manner to protect and side with victims, as well as to provide education and awareness to the community and officials that all acts of violence constitute crimes against human dignity.

#### Article 1

Sufficiently clear.

#### Article 2

The rights in this provision are intended to guarantee their lives in accordance with human dignity, enhance self-confidence, and the ability to participate in community, national, and state life. What is meant by discriminatory treatment is, for example, treatment that discriminates based on ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status, birth order of children, and physical and/or mental condition.

What is meant by "gender equality" is a condition in which women and men enjoy equal status and have the same conditions to fully realize human rights and their potential for integrity and survival proportionally.

#### Article 3

What is meant by prevention is all activities carried out to prevent, reduce and even eliminate acts of violence among others by organizing communication, information and education. What is meant by services and protection for women and children victims of gender-based violence is that in all service and protection actions, gender equality is taken into account. What is meant by the best interests of the child is that in all actions concerning children carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies, and judicial bodies, the best interests of the child must be the primary consideration.

#### Article 4

Point a

Socialization means a process of instilling or transferring habits or values and rules from one generation to another in a group or society regarding the prevention of violence.

Point b

Dissemination means an activity directed at target groups or individuals so that they obtain information, raise awareness, receive, and ultimately utilize such information.

Point c

Training means a series of activities designed to enhance the skills, knowledge, experience, or attitude changes of an individual. Training concerns the acquisition of specific skills or knowledge. Socialization, dissemination and training activities on violence prevention may be conducted by relevant Regional Apparatus Work Units, relevant Agencies, individuals, groups or Social Community Organizations.

#### Article 5

Point a

Health services means efforts that include promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative aspects.

Point b

Receiving psychological assistance means assistance provided by a psychologist to victims suffering from trauma/other mental problems to restore the victim's mental condition. Meanwhile, legal assistance means assistance efforts provided by persons and/or legal aid institutions to victims at every level of examination and throughout the legal process.

Point c

Counseling means the provision of assistance by an expert or trained person in such a way that the victim's understanding and psychological capacity increases in solving the problems faced.

Point d spiritual guidance means counseling provided by a spiritual advisor.

Point e

Resocialization means the victim can resume life in society including the restoration of their good name.

Point f

Empowerment means making something become empowered or have power or have strength.

#### Article 6

Point a

Protection means the victim receives protection from family, police, prosecution, court, advocates, social institutions or other parties, either temporarily or based on the determination of a protection order from the court.

Point b

Receiving information means the right of victims to obtain information relating to prevention, complaints, medical services and protection. Point c

Optimal service means service that can be provided to victims in accordance with the capability of the service provider.

Point d

Continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage means handling provided until the victim's recovery in society.

Point e

Confidential handling means efforts to guarantee certainty for victims that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disclosed.

Point f

Psychological and legal assistance means the right of victims to obtain psychological and legal assistance at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

Point g

Guarantees of rights related to status as a family member or community member.
