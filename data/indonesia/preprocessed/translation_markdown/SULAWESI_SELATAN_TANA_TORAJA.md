# REGENT OF TANA TORAJA

REGENT OF TANA TORAJA

SOUTH SULAWESI PROVINCE

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF TANA TORAJA,

Considering : a. that the State of the Republic of Indonesia is a State that upholds the dignity and worth of every human being, guarantees the rights and security of women and children, and freedom from all forms of violence, mistreatment, exploitation, trafficking and neglect; that women as mothers of the nation and children as the shoots and future generation of the nation are creatures created by Almighty God and therefore must be protected and their honor, dignity and self-worth safeguarded appropriately and proportionally, both legally, economically, politically, socially and culturally, without distinction of ethnicity, religion, race and class; that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, and letter b, it is necessary to stipulate a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children;

In View of : 1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the State of the Republic of Indonesia;

Law Number 29 of 1959 concerning the Establishment of Level II Regions in Sulawesi (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1959 Number 74, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 1822);

Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 1999 Number 165, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3886);

Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 109, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4235) as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 concerning Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 297, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5606);

Law Number 13 of 2003 concerning Manpower (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2003 Number 39, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4279);

Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419);

Law Number 12 of 2011 concerning the Formulation of Legislation (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2011 Number 82, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5234);

Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended several times, most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);

Law Number 8 of 2016 concerning Persons with Disabilities (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2016 Number 69, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5871);

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

Region is Tana Toraja Regency.

Regent is the Regent of Tana Toraja.

Regional Government is the administration of governmental affairs by the regional government and regional house of representatives in accordance with the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.

Regional Government is the regional head as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region.

Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional representative institution which is positioned as an element of the Regional Government administration.

Sub-district is a territorial part of the regency area led by a sub-district head.

Lembang is another term for village which is a territorial part of the regency area led by the Head of Lembang; Lembang Government is the term for village government in Tana Toraja Regency.

Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife and their children, or father and children or mother and children or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward laterally to the third degree.

Parents are biological father and/or mother or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother.

Guardian is a person or body that in reality exercises custody as a parent over a child. Woman is a human being who can menstruate, become pregnant, give birth and breastfeed.

Child is a person who has not reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a baby still in the womb. Abandoned child is a child whose needs are not properly fulfilled, whether physical, mental, spiritual, or social. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court decisions.

Protection of women is all efforts made to ensure the fulfillment of women's rights as a form of respect for human rights.

Empowerment of women is any effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social capacity, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective capabilities.

Protection of children is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, and develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

Child with Disability is a Child who has physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory limitations in the long term which in interaction with the environment and community attitudes can encounter obstacles that make it difficult to participate fully and effectively based on equal rights.

Child with Excellence is a Child who has extraordinary intelligence or has potential and/or special talents not limited to intellectual abilities, but also in other fields.

Adopted Child is a Child whose rights are transferred from the environment of the authority of the Family of Parents, legal Guardian, or other person responsible for the care, education, and raising of that Child into the environment of the adoptive Parents' Family based on a court decision or decree.

Foster Child is a Child who is cared for by a person or institution to be provided guidance, maintenance, care, education, and health because the Child's Parents or one of the Parents is unable to guarantee the Child's normal growth and development.

Custody is the power of Parents to care for, educate, maintain, nurture, protect, and develop the Child in accordance with the religion professed and in accordance with the Child's abilities, talents, and interests.

Rights of the Child are part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and fulfilled by Parents, Family, community, state, government, and regional government.

Companion is a social worker who has professional competence in their field.

Violence is any act that is unlawful, which results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of a person's freedom.

Violence against women is any act based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering to women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.

Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the child.

Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is a collaborative network that organizes integrated social service functions.

Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is a collaborative network that organizes integrated service functions for the protection of women and children.

Community Social Institution is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and/or other community organizations. Family Welfare Consultation Institution, hereinafter abbreviated as LK3, is an integrated social service unit that implements family psychosocial problem management to realize family resilience.

Regional Child Protection Commission, hereinafter abbreviated as KPAD, is an integrated social service unit that implements child protection services to realize the fulfillment of children's rights.

Gender mainstreaming is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of national development policy programs.

Discrimination is any restriction, harassment, or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, language, political belief, which results in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural fields, and other aspects of life.

Safe House (Shelter) is a shelter for women and children including families and witnesses of violence victims.

# CHAPTER II

SCOPE

#### Article 2

The Scope of this Regional Regulation consists of: implementation of women's protection; and implementation of child protection; obligations and responsibilities; institutions; child-friendly regency; special development planning deliberation for women and children.

# CHAPTER III

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

The implementation of women's protection is based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia covering: respect for human rights; justice and gender equality; non-discrimination; victim protection; the best interests of women; respect for women's opinions; legal certainty; and empowerment of women.

#### Article 4

The implementation of child protection is based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia as well as the basic principles of the convention on the rights of the child including: non-discrimination; the best interests of the child; the right to life, survival, and development; and respect for the views of the child.

#### Article 5

The objectives of implementing women's protection are: to ensure the fulfillment of women's rights; to protect and provide a sense of security for women; to maintain household integrity in order to realize a harmonious and prosperous family; to ensure the fulfillment of women's rights; to provide services to women; to provide protection to reporters and witnesses; and to empower women victims of violence.

#### Article 6

The objectives of implementing child protection are: to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights; to protect and provide a sense of security for children; to maintain household integrity in order to realize a harmonious and prosperous family; to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights; to realize qualified, virtuous, and prosperous children; to provide services to child victims of violence; and to provide protection to reporters and witnesses.

# CHAPTER IV

IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S PROTECTION

## Part One

Women's Rights

#### Article 7

The rights of women are as follows: to live, maintain life and improve their standard of living; to live peacefully, safely, in peace, happiness, prosperity physically and spiritually; to enjoy a good and healthy living environment; to live in a family and continue offspring within the bond of lawful marriage based on the provisions of laws and regulations; to obtain employment in accordance with ability, conditions and fair and just wages; specifically for women with disabilities, the right to obtain facilities and special treatment in public services; to play an active role in the field of politics and government in accordance with the potential and capabilities they possess; to obtain information and legal services; to obtain services to increase their income; and to obtain other rights in accordance with their human dignity and based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two

Women's Empowerment

#### Article 8

In order to improve the position, role, and quality of women, as well as efforts to realize equality and gender justice in family, community, national, and state life, women must be empowered.

Women's empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) is directed to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so as to be able to play a role and participate in activities: economy; socio-culture; politics; and law.

#### Article 9

The implementation of women's empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2) letter a, is carried out through: providing skills and job training; facilitating the formation of productive economic business groups; facilitating the strengthening and development of productive economic business groups; facilitating capital assistance; and facilitating the development of marketing networks.

#### Article 10

Women's empowerment in the socio-cultural field as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2) letter b, is carried out through: increasing knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of education in stages according to potential to improve social status; increasing awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health; increasing awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and

d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and the development of science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 11

The implementation of women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2) letter c, includes: involving women in decision-making at various levels; providing opportunities for women to hold public office; participation in general elections; and self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.

The implementation of women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 12

The implementation of women's empowerment in the legal field as referred to in Article 8 paragraph (2) letter d, includes: increasing awareness and knowledge in the legal field through communication, information and education services; and facilitating access and legal consultation services.

The implementation of women's empowerment in the legal field as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 13

The Regional Government is obliged and responsible for efforts to empower women.

Efforts to empower women as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 14

(1) To implement women's empowerment as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1), the Regional Government organizes gender mainstreaming including: establishing the implementation of gender mainstreaming; coordinating, facilitating, and mediating gender mainstreaming; facilitating institutional strengthening and developing gender mainstreaming mechanisms in government institutions, women's study centers, research and development institutions, non-governmental institutions; implementing gender mainstreaming related to the fields of development, education, health, economics, law, human rights and politics, environment and socio-culture; improving the quality of life of women related to the fields of development, education, health, economics, law, human rights and politics, environment and socio-culture; integrating efforts to improve the quality of life of women related to the fields of development, education, health, economics, law, human rights and politics, environment and socio-culture; coordinating the implementation of improving the quality of life of women related to the fields of development, education, health, economics, law, human rights and politics, environment and socio-culture; organizing the protection of women, especially protection for victims of violence, women workers, elderly women, and women with disabilities, in conflict areas and disaster-affected areas; facilitating the integration of women's protection, especially for victims of violence, women workers, elderly women and women with disabilities, in conflict areas and disaster-affected areas; coordinating the protection of women, especially for victims of violence, women workers, elderly women and women with disabilities; facilitating the strengthening and development of networks of community institutions or organizations and the business sector for the implementation of gender mainstreaming; developing gender-based information systems; and providing and organizing integrated or non-integrated services for women victims of violence, women workers, elderly women, and women with disabilities.

(2) The forms of women's empowerment and gender mainstreaming mechanisms as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented in accordance with laws and regulations.

## Part Three

Prevention of Violence and Participation in Prevention

### Paragraph 1

Prevention of Violence

#### Article 15

Efforts to prevent violence against women are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the agency having the duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented through the following means: establishing work networks in efforts to prevent violence; carrying out coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership work patterns between Regional Government institutions and the community; building an integrated security system in areas prone to cases of violence against women; establishing complaint and service units regarding violence against women at the sub-district, village and/or Lembang levels; and awareness campaigns, socialization and education to the community about the protection and empowerment of women.

### Paragraph 2

Participation in Prevention

#### Article 16

Participation in prevention efforts as referred to in Article 15, the Regional Government must involve: family; community; and women's advocacy organizations.

## Part Four

Protection of Women

#### Article 17

Protection of women includes: protection of the personal safety of victims or witnesses; physical and psychological threats; confidentiality of the identity of victims and witnesses; provision of testimony during legal proceedings regulated through protection mechanisms.

Protection of women victims also includes the provision of safe houses as temporary shelters to obtain a sense of safety during judicial proceedings and/or outside judicial proceedings.

The provision of protection and safe houses is carried out by the Regional Government.

Further provisions on the mechanism for protection of women as referred to in paragraph (2), and the provision of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (3), shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

PROVISION OF CHILD PROTECTION

## Part One

Rights and Obligations of Children

#### Article 18

The rights of children are as follows: the right to survival, growth, development and participation appropriately in accordance with human dignity and to receive protection from violence and discrimination; the right to a name as personal identity and citizenship status; the right to worship according to their religion, think, and express themselves in accordance with their level of intelligence and age, under parental guidance; the right to know their parents, be raised, and be cared for by their own parents; the right to obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual, and social needs; the right to obtain education and instruction in the framework of developing their personality and intelligence level in accordance with their interests and talents; specifically for children with disabilities, also have the right to obtain special education, while children who have excellence are also entitled to receive special education; the right to express and have their opinions heard, receive, seek, and provide information in accordance with their level of intelligence and age for their self-development in accordance with the values of decency and propriety; the right to rest and utilize free time, associate with peers, play, recreate, and create in accordance with their interests, talents, and intelligence level for self-development; children with disabilities have the right to obtain rehabilitation, social assistance and maintenance of social welfare standards; the right to be cared for by their own parents, unless there are reasons and/or valid legal rules showing that the separation is in the best interests of the child and is a last consideration; the right to meet directly and have regular personal relations with both parents; the right to obtain care, maintenance, education and protection for the growth and development process from both parents in accordance with their abilities, talents, and interests; the right to obtain living expenses from both parents; the right to obtain protection from abuse in political activities, involvement in armed conflicts, involvement in social unrest, involvement in events containing elements of violence and involvement in warfare.

#### Article 19

Every child has the right to obtain protection from being the target of persecution, torture, or imposition of inhuman punishment.

Every child has the right to obtain freedom in accordance with law.

Every child has the right to be protected from arrest, detention, and/or criminal acts of child imprisonment shall only be carried out if in accordance with applicable law and can only be carried out as a last resort.

#### Article 20

Children who are detained due to legal cases, have the right to: receive humane treatment and their placement separated from adults; obtain legal aid or other assistance effectively at every stage of applicable legal efforts; and defend themselves and obtain justice before an objective and impartial juvenile court in closed hearings to the public.

#### Article 21

Every child who becomes a perpetrator or victim of sexual violence or who is in conflict with the law has the right to confidentiality.

#### Article 22

In the event that for any reason their parents cannot guarantee the growth and development of the child, resulting in the child becoming neglected, then the child has the right to be cared for or adopted as a foster child or adopted child by another person or institution in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 23

Every child is obliged to: respect parents, guardians, and teachers; love family, community, and care for friends; love the homeland, nation and state; perform worship in accordance with the teachings of their religion; and practice noble ethics and morals.

## Part Two

Prevention

#### Article 24

Efforts to prevent violence against children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the agency having the duties and functions of coordination in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented through the following means: establishing work networks in efforts to prevent violence; carrying out coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership work patterns between regional government institutions and the community; building an integrated security system in areas prone to cases of violence against children; incorporating local content material on children's rights and the elimination of violence against children as teaching materials in teaching and learning activities in early childhood education institutions up to the level of Senior High School; establishing complaint and service units regarding violence against children at the sub-district, village and/or lembang levels, as well as schools; awareness campaigns, socialization and education to the community about child protection.

## Part Three

Child Protection

#### Article 25

Every child receives protection from violent treatment, exploitation, trafficking, discrimination, mistreatment and neglect.

Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is elaborated in various fields of life, among others: in the field of health; in the field of religion; in the field of education; in the field of social affairs; and special protection.

#### Article 26

Protection in the field of health as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter a, is carried out by the government through the provision of health facilities and endeavoring to provide comprehensive health services for children, so that every child obtains the optimal degree of health from the womb.

Comprehensive health services as referred to in paragraph (1) include promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, both for basic health services and referrals.

Comprehensive health service efforts as referred to in paragraph (2) are implemented by the Regional Government free of charge for families who are unable to afford it.

#### Article 27

Parents and family provide protection to maintain the health of children starting from examination of child growth development since in the womb.

In the event that parents and family are unable to carry out the protection as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall fulfill it.

The Regional Government provides assistance and free services for underprivileged communities as referred to in paragraph (2), and its implementation is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 28

The Regional Government provides protection to children from attempts to transplant body organs for the purposes of other parties.

The Regional Government, parents and family protect children from acts of: removal of body organs and/or body tissues of children without regard to the health of the child; buying and selling of organs and/or body tissues of children; and health research that uses children as research objects without the permission of their parents.

#### Article 29

Every child receives protection in the field of religion as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter b, to worship according to their religion.

Protection of children in embracing their religion as referred to in paragraph (1) includes fostering, guidance, and practice of good religious teachings for children.

#### Article 30

Every child receives protection in the field of education as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter c, to obtain education.

The Regional Government, parents, and family must provide the widest possible opportunity to children to obtain education from an early age.

The Regional Government provides adequate facilities and infrastructure to support the teaching and learning process for children.

Non-governmental institutions may provide teaching and learning facilities and infrastructure for children, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 31

Education as referred to in Article 30 paragraph (1), is intended and directed for: development of personality abilities, mental, talents, and physical of children as optimally as possible; development of respect for parents, and fellow human beings; development of understanding of customary and cultural values in the place where the child grows and develops; preparation of children for a responsible life; and development of respect and love for the nation and state.

#### Article 32

The Regional Government provides protection for children with disabilities, to get opportunities in obtaining special education.

Children who have excellence are given the opportunity to receive special education.

#### Article 33

The Regional Government provides education costs and/or free assistance or special services for children from underprivileged families, neglected children, and children in remote areas.

#### Article 34

Protection in the field of education as referred to in Article 30 paragraph (1), applies both outside the school environment and within the school environment.

Protection outside the school environment is to safeguard and keep children away from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination.

Protection in the school environment is protection from acts of violence that can be committed either by school administrators, by teachers, or by their own friends.

#### Article 35

Special services as referred to in Article 33, in addition to assistance in the form of money, school equipment, may also be in the form of procurement of inter-school buses whose procurement is in accordance with the regional financial capacity.

#### Article 36

Protection of children in the Social field as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter d, includes maintenance and care of neglected children.

Maintenance and care as referred to in paragraph (1), include: fulfillment of basic needs consisting of clothing, food, shelter, education, health, security guarantees, freedom to worship according to their religion and equal treatment with other children to achieve social integration as optimally as possible; and fulfillment of special needs for children with disabilities.

Maintenance of neglected children as referred to in paragraph (1), may also be carried out by individuals and non-governmental institutions or community institutions.

Government institutions, and/or individuals, and/atu community institutions in providing services as referred to in paragraph (2), may cooperate with various related service units.

Supervision of the implementation of maintenance and care as referred to in paragraph (1), is carried out by regional apparatus organizations (OPD) that administer social sector affairs in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 37

Special protection as referred to in Article 25 paragraph (2) letter e, is given specifically to children namely: children in emergency situations; children in conflict with the law; children from minority and isolated groups; children exploited economically and/or sexually; child victims of trafficking; children who are victims of abuse of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic substances, and other addictive substances (napza); child victims of abduction; child victims of violence both physical and mental; and children with disabilities

#### Article 38

Children in emergency situations as referred to in Article 37 letter a, consist of: children who become refugees; child victims of riots; child victims of natural disasters; and children in armed conflict situations.

Special protection for children in emergency situations as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 39

The regional government and community provide special protection for children in conflict with the law as referred to in Article 34 letter b, which includes children in conflict with the law and child victims of criminal acts.

Special protection for children in conflict with the law as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented through: treatment of children humanely in accordance with the dignity and respect for the rights of children; provision of special child companion officers; provision of special facilities and infrastructure; imposition of appropriate sanctions for the best interests of the child; continuous monitoring and recording of the development of children in conflict with the law; provision of guarantees to maintain relations with parents or family; and protection from disclosure of identity through mass media and to avoid labeling.

(3) Special protection for children who are victims of criminal acts as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented through: child rehabilitation efforts; protection efforts from disclosure of identity through mass media and to avoid labeling; provision of safety guarantees for victim witnesses; provision of accessibility to obtain information regarding case developments.

#### Article 40

Special protection for children from minority and isolated groups as referred to in Article 37 letter c, is carried out through the provision of facilities and infrastructure to be able to enjoy their own culture, acknowledge and practice their own religious teachings, and use their own language.

#### Article 41

The Regional Government provides special protection for children who are exploited economically and/or sexually as referred to in Article 37 letter d and child victims of violence both physical and mental as referred to in Article 37 letter h.

Special protection for exploited children and child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through: socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of children who are exploited economically and/or sexually; monitoring, reporting, and imposition of sanctions; and involvement of various organizations, Regional Government, companies, labor unions, social institutions and the community.

#### Article 42

Special protection for children who are involved in and become victims of abuse of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropics, and other addictive substances (drugs) as referred to in Article 37 letter f, is carried out through supervision, prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation efforts by the Regional Government and the community.

#### Article 43

The Regional Government provides special protection for child victims of trafficking as referred to in Article 37 letter e and child victims of abduction as referred to in Article 37 letter g which is carried out through efforts: supervision; protection; prevention; treatment; and rehabilitation.

#### Article 44

The Regional Government provides special protection for children with disabilities as referred to in Article 37 letter i, through efforts: humane treatment of children in accordance with dignity and honor, as well as respect for children's rights; fulfillment of special needs; and obtaining the same treatment as other children to achieve social integration as optimally as possible.

# CHAPTER VI

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

General

#### Article 45

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence and discrimination are joint responsibilities:

Regional Government;

Lembang Government;

Community; and

Parents and Family.

## Part Two

Regional Government

#### Article 46

The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 45 letter a, include: establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence as established by the government; cooperating with other parties in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence; allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence according to regional financial capacity; fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; providing recovery services and social reintegration; encouraging and increasing community participation; and providing human resources in the implementation of protection of women and child victims of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination.

In the event that a child becomes neglected as referred to in Article 22, the Regional Government is responsible and obligated to take over and provide services to neglected children as referred to in Article 36 paragraph (2) letter a.

Community institutions, and/or individuals may submit an application to the court to become guardian and/or foster parent for neglected children.

#### Article 47

In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in Article 46 paragraph (1), the Regent establishes programs and action activities for the protection of women and children in the regional action plan as a basis for regional apparatus in implementing the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination.

The regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (1), is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD). Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

Lembang Government

#### Article 48

The obligations and responsibilities of the Lembang Government as referred to in Article 45 letter b, include: implementing policies for the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination as established by the Regional Government; cooperating with other parties in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination according to lembang financial capacity; fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; and encouraging and increasing community participation.

## Part Four

Community

#### Article 49

The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 45 letter c, are carried out by participating in the protection of women and children victims of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination, both individually and in groups.

The role of the community as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, women and children protection institutions, social welfare institutions, community organizations, educational institutions, mass media, and the business community.

The role of the community in the implementation of protection of women and children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by: providing information through socialization and education regarding the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; providing input in the formulation of policies related to the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; reporting to the authorities if violations of acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination against women and children occur; playing an active role in the rehabilitation and social reintegration process for women and children victims of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; monitoring and supervising the Protection of Women and Children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; providing facilities and infrastructure in order to support the protection of women and children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; playing an active role in eliminating negative labeling of women and children victims of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination; and providing space for women and children to participate and express their opinions.

## Part Five

Parents and Family

#### Article 50

The obligations and responsibilities of parents and family as referred to in Article 45 letter d, are to safeguard and prevent the occurrence of all forms of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination both against women and against children.

The obligations and responsibilities of parents towards children as referred to in paragraph (1), are to: care for, maintain, educate, and protect children; develop children according to their abilities, talents, and interests; prevent marriages at child age; and provide character education and instillation of moral values in children.

Guardians and/or foster parents who have obtained determination as referred to in Article 46 paragraph (3), must be able to fulfill the provisions as referred to in article 36 paragraph (2) letter a.

Guardians and/or foster parents appointed as referred to in paragraph (3) must have the same religion as the Child.

Guardians and/or foster parents as referred to in paragraph (3) are responsible for the child and must manage the property of the child concerned for the best interests of the child.

Guardians and/or foster parents as referred to in paragraph (3), may represent the child to perform legal acts, both inside and outside the court for the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER VII

INSTITUTIONS

#### Article 51

In the context of protection of women and children, the Regional Government may establish PPT. The establishment and development of PPT is adjusted to priority needs, regional financial capacity as well as institutional and personnel capacity available in the region.

PPT as referred to in paragraph (1), may take the form of:

P2TP2A;

LK3;

KPAD; and other institutions as needed and in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 52

P2TP2A as referred to in Article 51 paragraph (3) letter a, is positioned at the regional level and sub-district level. P2TP2A is under the coordination of OPD or other units that handle women's empowerment and child protection. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) does not have the authority to formulate policies on women's empowerment and child protection.

P2TP2A is a form of integrated service unit, which functions as:

- a. information center for women and children;
- b. service center for women and children victims of violence; and empowerment center for women and children.

#### Article 53

The establishment of P2TP2A is stipulated by Regent's Decree for the regional level and sub-district head's decree for the sub-district level.

Membership of P2TP2A may come from elements of the Regional Government, professional organizations, academics, community leaders and other elements as needed.

Further provisions regarding P2TP2A are regulated in Regent's Regulation.

#### Article 54

In the context of handling psychosocial problems of victims, recovery of psychosocial conditions of victims and strengthening family resilience, LK3 may be established in the region.

Procedures for establishment, main tasks and functions as well as service mechanisms of LK3 are carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 55

In the context of providing protection and fulfillment of children's rights, KPAD may be established in the region.

Procedures for establishment, main tasks and functions as well as service mechanisms of KPAD as referred to in paragraph (1), are carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

CHILD-FRIENDLY REGENCY

#### Article 56

To ensure the fulfillment of children's rights to live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and to receive protection from violence and discrimination, real integrated efforts are needed between the regional government, community and business sector through the development of KLA.

Further provisions regarding the development of KLA as referred to in paragraph (1), are regulated by regional regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

SPECIAL DEVELOPMENT PLANNING DELIBERATION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 57

Special development planning deliberation for women and children is a form of annual meeting or forum specifically held for women and children to explore various ideas and inputs for the fulfillment of women's and children's rights in regional development program activities.

Special development planning deliberation for women and children is conducted before the implementation of development planning deliberation.

Further provisions regarding the implementation mechanism of special development planning deliberation for women and children as referred to in paragraph (1), are regulated by Regent's Regulation.

# CHAPTER X

FINANCING

#### Article 58

Costs for the implementation of protection of women and children are sourced from:

Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget;

Lembang Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and

Other legitimate and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

PROHIBITIONS

#### Article 59

Every business operation whose activities may disrupt child development is prohibited from receiving child visitors.

Business operations as referred to in paragraph (1), include among others: night clubs including discotheques, bars and pubs; adult karaoke; massage parlors; steam bath/sauna establishments; and other similar business fields.

#### Article 60

Every operator of hotels, motels, inns, tourist guesthouse businesses and similar business activities is prohibited from renting rooms to children without being accompanied by parents or adult family members or accompanying teachers/persons in charge in the context of carrying out school activities or other activities.

#### Article 61

Every operator of internet service businesses, play stations and types of children's games based on other information and communication technology is required to manage and design child-friendly places.

Every business operator as referred to in paragraph (1) must: arrange rooms/booths with adequate lighting and not closed; not accept children wearing uniforms and/or during school hours; locally block sites containing elements of pornography and pornographic acts; and limit visiting hours and supervise the use of existing facilities.

# CHAPTER XII

ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS

#### Article 62

Every person or business entity that violates the provisions as referred to in Article 59, Article 60 and Article 61, shall be subject to administrative sanctions in the form of: written warning; and revocation of permit.

Written warning as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a, is given 3 (three) times with a grace period between one warning and another warning of a maximum of 1 (one) month.

If after the third warning is not heeded, permit revocation will be carried out.

# CHAPTER XIII

CRIMINAL SANCTIONS

#### Article 63

Every person or institution that commits acts resulting in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, coercion, or deprivation of liberty against women and children resulting in violations of this regional regulation, shall be subject to criminal sanctions in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XIV

CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 64

Regent's Regulation as implementing regulation of this Regional Regulation must be stipulated no later than 1 (one) year from the promulgation of this Regional Regulation.

#### Article 65

This regional regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.

In order that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Tana Toraja Regency.

ELUCIDATION

OF

GENERAL

Women and children are creations of God who possess dignity and human rights that need to be respected and valued.

Women are pillars of the nation and children are the buds of the next generation of the nation's struggle. That children, who will later bear great responsibility as the nation's next generation, need to be given the widest possible opportunity to grow and develop optimally, both physically, mentally, spiritually and socially, so that children are of noble character.

Women and children have a strategic role in determining the success of development programs in all aspects of state life. However, due to the still low understanding of the dignity of women and children, they are vulnerable to becoming victims of violence and discrimination both within the family and in their environment.

Various acts of violence against women and children continue to increase from time to time. If there is no awareness to provide serious attention and handling, it will destroy the life of households, society, the nation and the state.

Considering this, it is time that a regulation is needed in the form of a regional regulation on the Protection of Women and Children that is able to answer while providing legal certainty for the Protection of Women and Children from acts of violence and discrimination.

As a concrete step in efforts to provide Protection for Women and Children in Tana Toraja Regency, through the initiative of the DPRD, a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children was drafted as an implementation of various laws and regulations, including Law Number 39 of 1999 on Human Rights, Law Number 23 of 2002 on Child Protection, Law Number 23 of 2004 on the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 35 of 2014 on Amendments to Law Number 23 of 2002 on Child Protection, Law Number 8 of 2016 on Persons with Disabilities and Regulation of the Minister of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection Number 6 of 2015 on the System for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

This Regional Regulation is intended to give responsibility to the Regional Government starting from the prevention of acts of violence to the handling of victims of violence.

In this regional regulation, the rights of Women and Children, Women's Empowerment, Prevention, Protection of Women and Children are regulated. Also regulated are special handling of neglected children, the responsibilities of regional government, the responsibilities of Lembang government, up to the Participation of the Community, parents and Family in the prevention, protection and handling of Women and Children.

This regional regulation also regulates financing sourced from the APBD, APBL and other sources that are not binding, which is expected to ensure the implementation of protection for Women and Children from acts of violence, exploitation, trafficking and discrimination.
