REGIONAL REGULATION OF CIREBON REGENCY

NUMBER 1 YEAR 2018

CONCERNING

PROTECTION, EMPOWERMENT OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF CIREBON,

Considering : a. that children are a mandate and gift of Almighty God in whom are inherent dignity and worth as complete human beings, and who are the next generation of the nation's ideals and struggles, and therefore need to receive protection and the widest possible opportunity for survival, growth and development in a proper manner;

b. that in connection with the fact that there are still many women and children who need to receive protection from various forms of violence, maltreatment, exploitation, and neglect, it is deemed necessary to have strategic efforts from the Regional Government and other parties who are obliged to provide protection to the said women and children;

c. that discrimination and violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and crimes against humanity, and therefore in the context of fulfilling the constitutional rights of women and children as referred to in letter b, the Regional Government may carry out coordinated functions in an integrated manner towards the implementation of its duties, authorities, and responsibilities;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning Protection, Empowerment of Women and Children.

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Region is Cirebon Regency;
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus of Cirebon Regency as elements of the administration of Cirebon Regency government; 3. Regional House of Representatives of Cirebon Regency, hereinafter referred to as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution as an element of regional government administration;
- 4. Regent is the Regent of Cirebon Regency;
- 5. Regional Apparatus Work Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as SKPD, is the Apparatus of Cirebon Regency;
- 6. Discrimination is any differentiation, harassment or exclusion, whether direct or indirect, based on human differences on the basis of religion, tribe, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs which results in the reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms in life, whether individual or collective, in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life;
- 7. Violence is any unlawful act with or without the use of means against the physical and psychological which causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom;
- 8. Violence against women is any act based on gender differences which results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, economic or social exploitation, including threats of certain acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty arbitrarily, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life; 9. Violence against children is any act against a child which results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, economic or social exploitation, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens the integrity of the body and demeans the dignity of the child;
- 10. Domestic violence is any act against a woman and child which results in misery or suffering physically, sexually, psychologically, economically and/or household neglect including threats to commit acts of coercion or deprivation of liberty unlawfully within the household scope;
- 11. Human Trafficking is the act of recruiting, transporting, harboring, sending, transferring, or receiving a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, fraud, deception and abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payments or benefits, so as to obtain the consent of a person having control over another person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in a person being exploited;
- 12. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child still in the womb;
- 13. Child marriage is a marriage conducted through civil law, religion or custom with or without registration or official consent where one or both partners are children under the age of 18 years;
- 14. Woman is a person (human) who has a vagina, can menstruate, become pregnant, give birth to children, and breastfeed;
- 15. Victim of violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights (HAM) violations, or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals, including victims are their heirs;
- 16. Protection is a series of fulfillment of the rights of women and children victims of violence and discrimination starting from prevention efforts, handling and empowerment;
- 17. Prevention is a direct effort undertaken by the Regional Government to prevent acts of violence against women and children and Indirectly;
- 18. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an Integrated Service Unit established by the Regional Government to provide services to women and children victims of violence comprehensively including information services, assistance and legal aid, counseling services, medical services and safe houses through referrals;
- 19. Task Force for the Prevention and Handling of Criminal Acts of Human Trafficking, hereinafter referred to as the Task Force, is a coordinating institution tasked with coordinating efforts to prevent and handle criminal acts of human trafficking in Cirebon Regency;
- 20. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother;
- 21. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral lines up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child;
- 22. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.
- 23. Child-Friendly Regency is a Regency that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of commitment and resources of government, community, business and media that is planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights and special protection of children.
- 24. Private Sector is Non-Governmental Institutions, Business World and Mass Media.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection and empowerment of women and children is organized based on the principles of:

- a. Humanity;
- b. Justice and gender equality;
- c. Shelter;
- d. Best interests of the victim;
- e. Maternal survival;
- f. Child survival and development;
- g. Non-discrimination;
- h. Victim confidentiality;
- i. Anti-violence;
- j. Victim empowerment;
- k. Holistic.

#### Article 3

The objectives of protection and empowerment of women and children are to: a. Fulfill the rights of children;

b. Prevent acts of violence against women and children including human trafficking;

c. Eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children:

- d. Protect and provide a sense of security for women and children; e. Provide services to women and children victims of violence;
- f. Protect children from child marriage and mediate household disputes to realize harmonious and prosperous household integrity.

# CHAPTER III

CHILD-FRIENDLY REGENCY

#### Article 4

(1) In the framework of implementing the Convention on the Rights of the Child and various laws and regulations and policies related to the fulfillment of children's rights and special protection of children, the Regional Government, private sector and community institutions make efforts to realize a Child-Friendly Regency.

(2) To realize a Child-Friendly Regency as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government, private sector and Community Institutions pay attention to children's rights related to the right to life, development, protection and participation.

(3) The Regional Government, Private Sector and Community Institutions in realizing a Child-Friendly Regency are incorporated in the Child-Friendly Regency Task Force.

(4) The Child-Friendly Regency Task Force is responsible for the fulfillment of children's rights related to:

- a. Civil rights and freedoms;
- b. Family environment and alternative care;
- c. Basic health and welfare;
- d. Education, use of leisure time and cultural arts activities; e. Special protection of children.

# CHAPTER IV

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE Article 5

The rights of victims and their families to obtain Ease in the service process consist of/include:

- a. Right to health recovery and right to psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
- b. Right to legal assistance;
- c. Right to obtain information;
- d. Right to social recovery;
- e. Right to restitution (trafficking, rape);
- f. Right to spiritual services;
- g. Right to reintegration;
- h. Right to shelter services;
- i. Right to a companion.

#### Article 6

Child victims of acts of violence, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 5, also obtain special rights, as follows;

a. Right to respect and full utilization for survival, growth and development;

b. Right to basic services;

c. Right to equal protection;

d. Right to be free from various stigma; and

e. Right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER V

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 7

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection and Empowerment for women and children are the shared responsibility of:

- a. Government/Regional Government and Government Institutions;
- b. Private Sector and Community Institutions;
- c. Society;
- d. Family and/or Parents.

#### Article 8

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 7 letter a, include:

- a. Establishing policies for protection and empowerment of women and children;
- b. Implementing policies, protection, and empowerment of women and children from acts of violence established by the government;
- c. Conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children;
- d. Providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children;
- e. Allocating budget for the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children, in accordance with regional financial capacity;
- f. Fostering and supervising the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children; and
- g. Realizing a child-friendly regency.
  - (2) In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes programs and action activities for the protection and Empowerment of women and children in the Regional Action Plan as a basis for SKPD in implementing the protection and Empowerment of women and children.
  - (3) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), is part of the Regional Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMD). (4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 9

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the Private Sector and society as referred to in Article 7 letter b, are implemented in the form of community participation and jointly.

(2) The forms of participation of the private sector and society as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

- a. Preventing acts of violence against women and children; b. Providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcers or authorized parties; and
- c. Participating in handling victims of acts of violence.
  - (3) The forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), are implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 10

The obligations of family and/or parents as referred to in Article 7 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER VI

ROLE OF GOVERNMENT, PRIVATE SECTOR AND COMMUNITY INSTITUTIONS Article 11

(1) To prevent acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government conducts empowerment and awareness-raising for families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling and realizing a Child-Friendly Regency.

(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness-raising as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government makes efforts as follows: a. Increasing the quantity and quality of education both formal and non-formal and informal;

b. Providing accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;

c. Providing employment opportunities for women;

d. Building community participation and concern for the prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence;

e. Building and providing an information system that is easily accessible; f. Building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that are engaged in and/or care about women and children;

g. Optimizing the integrated service center for women and children empowerment (P2TP2A) or by another name as a post for the protection of women and children from acts of violence; and

h. Opening community-based complaint posts for the protection of women and children.

#### Article 12

(1) The prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 11, is implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:

- a. Social affairs;
- b. Health;
- c. Education;
- d. Manpower;
- e. Women's empowerment and child protection;
- f. Mental and spiritual; and
- g. peace and order.
  - (2) The prevention of acts of violence by SKPD as referred to in paragraph (1), is implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Action Plan.

# CHAPTER VII

SERVICES FOR VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 13

(1) The forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence, are as follows:

- a. Complaint services;
- b. Health services;
- c. Legal services;
- d. Repatriation;
- e. Recovery, social reintegration, and medicolegal;
- f. Identification services;
- g. Psychological services; and
- h. Shelter.
  - (2) The forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented in accordance with minimum service standards established by the Government and implemented by SKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
- a. Social affairs;
- b. Economy;
- c. Health;
- d. Women's empowerment and child protection; and
- e. Mental and spiritual.
  - (3) In the implementation of duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government cooperates with Government Agencies, other Regional Governments, and the community.
  - (4) Further provisions regarding the procedures for handling services for women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), are regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 14

(1) The Regional Government is obliged to provide complaint services for the protection and empowerment of women and children.

(2) The Regional Government and the community or social service institutions may establish recovery houses or safe houses.

#### Article 15

Further provisions regarding complaint services and social services as referred to in Article 14 are regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 16

(1) In the context of providing protection services to women and children from acts of violence, the Regional Government establishes the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) or by another name as an integrated service center for the protection of women and children from acts of violence including victims of acts of violence.

(2) The Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) or by another name as referred to in paragraph (1), functions as a leading sector of the integrated service center in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.

(3) Further provisions regarding the organization and work procedures of the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) or by another name as referred to in paragraph (1), are established by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 17

In addition to establishing the Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment (P2TP2A) or by another designation as referred to in Article 16, in order to support the implementation of protection for women and children from acts of violence, the Regent shall establish:

- a. Task force on human trafficking;
- b. Task force on child marriage prevention;
- c. Regional action committee on the elimination of the worst forms of child labor;
- d. Task force on child-friendly regency or by another designation.

#### Article 18

(1) The task forces and regional action committee as referred to in Article 17 constitute a coordinative body tasked with coordinating the prevention and handling of human trafficking.

(2) Further provisions regarding the establishment, duties, and functions of the task forces as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be stipulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 19

(1) In order to achieve the objectives of protecting women and children, cooperation may be conducted with:

- a. The central government;
- b. Provincial government/other provinces;
- c. Regency/Municipal government;
- d. Non-governmental institutions; and
- e. The community.
  - (2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall include:
- a. Exchange of data and information;
- b. Recovery of victims of violence;
- c. Repatriation and social reintegration;
- d. Provision of evidence and witnesses; and
- e. Followed up in accordance with applicable law.
  - (3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) shall be formulated in the form of a Joint Agreement.

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 20

(1) The Regional Government shall establish partnerships with the business sector in the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

(2) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out through: a. Notification of employment opportunity information for women victims of violence;

b. Education and training for women victims of violence;

c. Educational assistance for women and children victims of violence who have been withdrawn from their education; and

d. Fostering and enhancing economic independence of women victims of violence.

(3) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2), shall be formulated in the form of an agreement.

# CHAPTER X

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 21

(1) The Regional Government shall be obligated to conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection, empowerment of women and children. (2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1), shall include: a. Guidelines and fulfillment standards;

b. Technical guidance and training;

c. Provision of facilities;

d. Monitoring; and

e. Evaluation.

(3) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be in order to realize the objectives of protection, empowerment of women and children in accordance with minimum service standards implemented by SKPD and the community.

(4) Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be conducted with professional, transparent, and accountable principles.

#### Article 22

The community may conduct supervision of the implementation of protection and empowerment of women and children organized by the Regional Government through the mechanism of conveying aspirations to the Regent or to the DPRD.

# CHAPTER XI

FINANCING

#### Article 23

Financing for the implementation of protection, empowerment of women and children shall be sourced from:

- a. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBN);
- b. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBD); and
- c. Other legitimate sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 24

(1) The Regional Government may provide financial assistance to community organizations, social organizations or non-governmental organizations that implement protection, empowerment of women and children from discrimination and acts of violence.

(2) Financial assistance as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be in accordance with the regional financial capacity, and shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations in the field of regional finance.

# CHAPTER XII

CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 25

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation. In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placing it in the Regional Gazette of Cirebon Regency.
