# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

- 1. Region is Belitung Regency.
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
- 3. Regent is the Regent of Belitung.
- 4. Regional Apparatus Organization hereinafter abbreviated as OPD is the Regional Apparatus of Belitung Regency.
- 5. Regional Apparatus Working Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as UKPD is the Regional Apparatus Working Unit of Belitung Regency.
- 6. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
- 7. Woman is a human being of female sex and a person recognized by law as a woman. 8. Violence is any act that results in or may result in suffering or misery whether physical, sexual, economic, psychological to the victim.
- 9. Victim is a woman and child who experiences suffering and/or misery either directly or indirectly as a result of violence and/or for and on behalf of another party who is their dependent.
- 10. Protection is all prevention, service and empowerment actions to protect and guarantee the rights of women and children from acts of violence.
- 11. Prevention is activities carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children. 12. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing of, during, or after the occurrence of violence against the victim.
- 13. Empowerment is strengthening women victims of violence to be able to endeavor and work independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
- 14. Companion is a person who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
- 15. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by a companion to women and children.
- 16. Counseling is the provision of assistance by an expert/trained person in such a way that the psychological understanding and ability of the victim and/or perpetrator increases in solving the problems faced.
- 17. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a functional work unit that provides integrated services to victims of violence.
- 18. Forum for Protection of Victims of Violence hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK is a coordination forum for prevention, handling and empowerment of victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner and is established at the regency, sub-district and village/kelurahan levels.
- 19. Standard Operating Procedures hereinafter abbreviated as SOP is the standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from complaints/identification, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, legal services up to repatriation and social reintegration of witnesses and/or victims.
- 20. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children.
- 21. Household is family members and relatives including grandchildren, nieces/nephews, siblings, grandparents, cousins and non-relatives including servants and drivers who live and eat from 1 (one) kitchen and reside in 1 (one) house.

# CHAPTER II

SCOPE

#### Article 2

The scope of protection of women and children from acts of violence, is as follows:

- a. principles and objectives;
- b. forms of violence;
- c. rights of women and child victims of violence;
- d. obligations and responsibilities;
- e. prevention of acts of violence;
- f. special protection of children;
- g. services and empowerment of victims of acts of violence; h. institutions;
- i. cooperation and partnership;
- j. reporting;
- k. guidance and supervision; and
- l. financing.

# CHAPTER III

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

Protection of women and children from acts of violence and discrimination is implemented based on the principles of:

- a. respect for human rights;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination;
- d. victim protection;
- e. empowerment; and
- f. integration.

#### Article 4

The objectives of protecting women and children from violence and discrimination are:

- a. to prevent acts of violence and discrimination against women and children including trafficking in persons; b. to eliminate all forms of violence, discrimination, and exploitation against women and children;
- c. to protect women and children from acts of violence and discrimination in obtaining their constitutionally legitimate rights;
- d. to protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
- e. to provide services and protection to women and children victims of violence and discrimination, reporters, and witnesses; and
- f. to facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize harmonious and prosperous household integrity.

# CHAPTER IV

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence against women and children include: a. physical violence;

b. psychological violence;

c. sexual violence;

d. neglect;

e. coercion/deprivation of freedom;

f. exploitation;

g. threats of certain acts; and/or

h. other violence.

# CHAPTER V

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE Article 6

Women and children victims of violence shall receive the following rights:

- a. the right to have their dignity and worth as human beings respected; b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by victims;
- c. the right to determine their own decisions;
- d. the right to obtain information;
- e. the right to confidentiality;
- f. the right to compensation;
- g. the right to social rehabilitation;
- h. the right to complaint handling;
- i. the right of victims and their families to receive facilitation in the judicial process; and
- j. the right to assistance.

#### Article 7

Child victims of violence, in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 6, shall also receive the following special rights:

- a. the right to respect and full utilization for survival, growth, and development;
- b. the right to basic services;
- c. the right to equal protection;
- d. the right to be free from various stigma; and
- e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER VI

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 8

The obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from violence are the joint responsibility of:

- a. the regional government;
- b. the community;
- c. the family; and
- d. parents.

#### Article 9

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the Regional Government as referred to in Article 8 letter a, include: a. implementing policies for the protection of women and children from violence established by the government;

b. establishing policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and children from violence; c. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection of women and children from violence; d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection of women and children from violence; e. allocating budget for the implementation of protection of women and children from violence in accordance with regional financial capacity; and

f. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection of women and children from violence.

(2) In order to carry out the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent shall establish programs and activities for the protection of women and children in a Regional Action Plan as the basis for OPD and

UKPD in implementing the protection of women and children from violence.

(3) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be incorporated in the strategic plan of OPD.

(4) Further provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 10

(1) The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 8 letter b, shall be carried out in the form of community participation.

(2) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:

- a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
- b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or competent authorities; and
- c. participating in the handling of victims of violence. (3) Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 11

The obligations of the family and/or parents as referred to in Article 8 letters c and d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

#### Article 12

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence against women and children is obligated to make efforts in accordance with their capacity to: a. prevent and stop the ongoing acts of violence;

b. provide protection to victims;

d. provide emergency assistance; and/or

e. assist in the process of submitting applications for protection orders.

# CHAPTER VII

PREVENTION OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 13

(1) To prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government shall conduct empowerment and awareness-raising to families, parents and the community by providing information, guidance, and/or counseling.

(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness-raising as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government shall undertake the following efforts:

- a. increasing the number and quality of educational units both formal and non-formal;
- b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement, and social services;
- c. opening employment opportunities for women;
- d. building community participation and concern for the prevention and protection of women and children from violence;
- e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
- f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement officials, government officials, universities, and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care about women and children; and
- g. opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children from violence.

#### Article 14

(1) Prevention of the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 13, shall be implemented by OPD and UKPD whose duties and functions are in the fields of:

- a. social affairs;
- b. health;
- c. education;
- d. manpower;
- e. women's empowerment and child protection; f. youth and sports;
- g. population and civil registration;
- h. mental and spiritual affairs; and
- i. public order and peace.
  - (2) Prevention of violence by OPD and UKPD as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in an integrated and sustainable manner based on the Regional Action Plan.
  - (3) In addition to the Regional Government, prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) must be carried out by:
- a. family and/or closest relatives;
- b. the community;
- c. educational institutions;
- d. health institutions;
- e. social community institutions; and
- f. the business sector and other institutions.

# CHAPTER VIII

SPECIAL PROTECTION FOR CHILDREN

#### Article 15

(1) Special protection for children is provided to:

- a. children in emergency situations;
- b. children in conflict with the law;
- c. children from minority and isolated groups;
- d. children who are exploited economically and/or sexually; e. children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse;
- f. children who are victims of pornography;
- g. children with HIV/AIDS;
- h. child victims of kidnapping, sale, and/or trafficking;
- i. child victims of physical and/or psychological violence;
- j. child victims of sexual crimes;
- k. child victims of terrorism networks;
- l. children with disabilities;
- m. child victims of abuse and neglect; n. children with deviant social behavior; and
- o. children who are victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their parents' condition.
  - (2) The Regional Government in implementing the handling of special protection for children as referred to in paragraph (1) in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

SERVICES AND EMPOWERMENT OF VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE Part One

Service Principles

#### Article 16

The provision of services to victims is implemented with the principles of:

- a. fast;
- b. safe and comfortable;
- c. empathy;
- d. non-discrimination;
- e. easily accessible;
- f. free of charge; and
- g. guaranteed confidentiality.

## Part Two

Forms of Services

#### Article 17

(1) Forms of services provided to women and children victims of acts of violence are as follows: a. complaint services;

b. health services;

c. legal aid;

d. rehabilitation and social reintegration;

e. repatriation;

f. identification services; and

g. psychological services.

(2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) must be supported by facilities in the form of:

- a. special service rooms within the police force;
- b. expert and professional personnel;
- c. service centers and safe houses; and/or
- d. other facilities and infrastructure necessary for victim recovery.
  - (3) Forms of services as referred to in paragraph (1), are implemented in accordance with minimum service standards established by the government and implemented by related OPDs whose duties and functions are in the fields of:
- a. social affairs;
- b. health;
- c. women's empowerment and child protection; and d. mental and spiritual.
  - (4) In carrying out the duties and functions as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government cooperates with government agencies, other regional governments, non-governmental organizations, and social institutions.
  - (5) Further provisions regarding the procedures for handling services for women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 18

The Regional Government is obligated to provide complaint services for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

#### Article 19

The Regional Government and the community or social service institutions may establish recovery houses or safe houses.

#### Article 20

Further provisions regarding Complaint Services and Social Services as referred to in Article 17 and Article 18, shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

Empowerment of Women Victims of Acts of Violence Article 21

(1) Empowerment of women victims of acts of violence may be carried out by the Regional Government in the form of: a. job training;

b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups; and/or

c. capital assistance.

(2) Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the OPD that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection, and government affairs in the field of Social Affairs in accordance with its duties and functions.

I. GENERAL

Women and children are a matter of serious concern, considering their vulnerable position to become victims of violence both from family and their environment. Various acts of violence against women and children both within the household and outside the household will destroy the life of the family, society, government, nation, and State, therefore the Government of Belitung Regency is obligated to provide protection to women and children from acts of violence which is an implementation of several laws and regulations, including Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 21 of 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Crime of Trafficking in Persons, Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014 concerning the Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.

Protection for women and children from acts of violence is one of the obligations of the Regional Government as regulated in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government. On that basis, the Government of Belitung Regency has made various efforts to protect the rights of women and children from acts of violence, but has not been able to provide protection to women and children from various acts of violence, among others, due to the still low understanding of the community regarding the rights of women and children, handling that has not been well coordinated, implementation that has not been sustainable, and so on.

Therefore, a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of the Rights of Women and Children from Acts of Violence is needed to provide legal certainty in the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

The Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of the Rights of Women and Children from Acts of Violence gives responsibility to the Regional Government ranging from prevention of acts of violence to handling of victims of acts of violence. In its implementation, the Regional Government cooperates with government agencies, other regional governments and the community. In addition, adequate funding support from the Government, Regional Government as well as the participation of the business sector and the community, is expected to eliminate violence against women and children. Through this Regional Regulation it is expected to further implement the rights of children and women and their protection, so that it can be implemented more comprehensively, integrated and continuously by government elements, families and the community as well as other institutions related to the efforts to protect children and women.

#### Article 4

Letter a What is meant by human trafficking is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, abduction, confinement, falsification of documents or identity, fraud, abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain the consent of the person holding control or such other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or causing a person to be exploited.

Letter b What is meant by exploitation is an act with or without the consent of the victim which includes prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or practices similar to slavery, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical sexual reproductive organs or unlawfully transferring/utilizing labor or transplanting organs and or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits.

Letter c Sufficiently clear. Letter d Sufficiently clear. Letter e What is meant by witness is a person who sees, hears and experiences an incident of violence and human rights violations.

Letter f Mediation is conducted for domestic violence cases that fall into the category of complaint offenses as regulated in Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence.

Letter b

Psychological violence is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering in a person.

Letter c

Exploitation is caused by:

- 1) acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
- 2) acts that with or without the consent of the victim include among others prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
- 3) all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse.

Letter g Threats of certain actions constitute a form of intimidation to do or not do what is requested by the perpetrator with consequences that are not good for the victim.

Letter h Other violence is caused among others by every unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear.
