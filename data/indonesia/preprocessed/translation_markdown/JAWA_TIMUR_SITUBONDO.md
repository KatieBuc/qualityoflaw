I. GENERAL

The implementation of protection for victims of violence is conducted based on the principles of humanity, integration and justice. Therefore, every person must receive their right to protection for a sense of security and freedom from torture or n treatment that degrades human dignity which is contrary to what is aspired to in the Preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, namely the creation of just and civilized humanity. To achieve integrated and comprehensive services for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence, an integrated form of service from various related institutions is required, including medical services, medicolegal, psychosocial, ' legal assistance, assistance, economic independence (training). These services are carried out evenly across all levels of society so that every citizen has the right to obtain equal opportunities to receive security and legal protection.

In principle, the strategic objective of implementing protection n is to maintain the sustainability of protection through integrated, comprehensive services and a safe and comfortable shelter that is conducive for victims of violence. These strategic objectives are pursued through interrelated stages, including planning, protection, services and financing, supervision and sanctions.

There are four important phenomena that need to be observed in relation

, to the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence. First, is violence against women and children which is increasing in number from year to year with the mode of violence tending to be more sadistic (perpetrators who slit throats, pour hot water, throw into wells, to breaking some body parts and so on). Second, the many cases of violence against Domestic Workers (PRT) which are dominated by women that have occurred recently require attention from all parties, especially the existence of legal protection, regulations and policies that protect them. Third, the lack of facilities and

\ld t a

,t a infrastructure for safe and comfortable shelters that are conducive for victims of violence. These three phenomena indicate the increasing complexity of violent behavior whose victims are dominated by women and children, both those who become domestic workers and others. Fourth, children are a trust and gift from God Almighty, in them is inherent dignity and worth as complete human beings. Children are the next generation of the nation's ideals who have a strategic role, and have special characteristics and traits that are expected to ensure the continuity of the existence of the nation and state in the future. Children need to get the widest possible opportunity to grow and develop optimally, both physically, mentally, cognitively, spiritually and socially.

Because of its nature, the growth and development of children must be carried out in an environment that protects them from all dangers in the form of optimal care. n

Therefore, a legal umbrella in the form of a Regional Regulation is needed that is capable of accommodating all issues related to the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence, capable of providing integrated services in a holistic and comprehensive manner, and firmly giving a mandate to institutions to coordinate policies and supervision, and a mandate to institutions to provide services. In addition, Regional Regulations can also open the involvement of non-governmental institutions and the community to play a broad role.

The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence in Situbondo Regency is carried out based on Situbondo Regent Regulation Number 6 of 2006 concerning the Implementation of n Protection for Victims of Acts of Violence against Women and Children and Situbondo Regent Decree No. 188/ 237 1P.00 L.2 /2OO7 concerning the Integrated Service Center (PPT) for Victims of Violence against Women and Children in Situbondo Regency. As the phenomena mentioned above, Regent Regulation Number 6 of 2006 has not been able to become a comprehensive legal umbrella in providing protection for women and children victims of violence. With the existence of this Regional Regulation, it is hoped that it can anticipate and overcome the conditions of violence that occur in Situbondo Regency, so that it can provide protection and services to victims of violence in an integrated and comprehensive manner.

#### Article 2

Letter aWhat is meant by "the principle of respect for human rights" is that the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is to ensure the fulfillment of the rights of victims of violence as a manifestation of respect for human rights.

Letter b What is meant by "the principle of gender equality and justice" is a process of equality and conditions for men and women to obtain equal opportunities and their rights as human beings, so that they are able to play a role and

Letter c What is meant by "the principle of protection of victims' rights" is the protection of the personal self, family, honor, dignity, and property under their control, a sense of security and protection from threats of fear, freedom from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity.

Letter e What is meant by "the principle of the best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.
