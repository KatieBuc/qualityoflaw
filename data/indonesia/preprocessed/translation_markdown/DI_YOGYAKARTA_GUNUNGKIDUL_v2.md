REGIONAL REGULATION OF GUNUNGKIDUL REGENCY NUMBER 1 OF 2020

CONCERNING

THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD THE REGENT OF GUNUNGKIDUL,

Considering : a. that the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence has been stipulated by Regional Regulation of Gunungkidul Regency Number 25 of 2012;

b. that in accordance with social developments and in order to optimize the protection of women and children victims of violence, Regional Regulation Number 25 of 2012 needs to be amended and adjusted;

c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a and b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children from Violence;

In View of : 1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

2. Law Number 15 of 1950 concerning the Establishment of Regencies within the Special Region of Yogyakarta (State Gazette of 1950 Number 44);

3. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 224, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587) as amended, most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2015 (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);

4. Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 109, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 3143) as most recently amended by Law Number 17 of 2016 (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2016 Number 237, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5946);

5. Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2004 Number 95, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4419);

With Joint Approval of

THE REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF GUNUNGKIDUL REGENCY

and

THE REGENT OF GUNUNGKIDUL

HAS DECIDED:

To Establish : A REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM VIOLENCE.

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean: 1. Women are persons of female gender.

2. Children are persons who have not yet reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including children still in the womb.

3. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct ascending or descending line up to the third degree.

4. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering, whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.

5. Violence against Women is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.

6. Violence against Children is any act that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, exploitation, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully, and other violence.

7. Victims are women and children who experience misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.

8. Child Victims of Violence are children who experience violence and children in conflict with the law.

9. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court decisions.

10. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

11. Special Protection is protection provided to children in emergency situations, children in conflict with the law, children from minority and isolated groups, children who are economically and/or sexually exploited, children who are trafficked, children who are victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic, and other addictive substance abuse (drugs), children who are victims of kidnapping, sale, trafficking, children who are victims of physical and/or mental violence, children with disabilities, and children who are victims of mistreatment and neglect.

12. Society is individuals, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.

13. Social Rehabilitation is services aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can perform social functions properly.

14. Services are immediate activities and actions taken to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing of the impending, ongoing or having occurred violence against women and children carried out by professionals in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.

15. Counseling for Victims is the provision of assistance by an expert or a person who is trained in such a way as to increase understanding and psychological ability in solving the problems faced.

16. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and/or spiritual guidance, for the empowerment of victims of violence to resolve the problems they face.

17. Technical Implementation Unit, hereinafter abbreviated as UPT, is a technical implementation unit of the agency formed by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.

18. Technical Implementation Unit of the Agency for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA, is a technical implementation unit of the agency formed by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.

19. Forum for the Protection of Violence Victims, hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner and is formed at the regency, Kapanewon/sub-district, and Kalurahan/village levels.

20. Safe House is a temporary residence or shelter for victims, provided to provide protection during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers from perpetrators.

21. Government Agency is a collective term covering work units/organizational units of ministries/departments, Non-Departmental Government Institutions, secretariats of high state institutions, and other government agencies, both central and regional, including State-Owned Enterprises, State-Owned Legal Entities, and Business Entities.

22. Region is Gunungkidul Regency. 8

23. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration.

24. Regent is the Regent of Gunungkidul.

25. Office of Women's Empowerment, Child Protection, and Family Planning, Community and Village Empowerment of Gunungkidul Regency, hereinafter referred to as the Office, is the implementing element of government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

#### Article 2

The protection of women and children aims to: a. ensure the fulfillment of the rights of women and children so they can participate optimally in society in accordance with human dignity;

b. provide protection and a sense of security to women and children from all forms of violence and discrimination;

c. prevent and eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children including trafficking in persons;

d. provide services to women and children who are victims of violence, reporters, and witnesses; e. restore and strengthen the physical and mental condition of women and children who are victims of violence; and

f. increase awareness and community participation in the prevention of violence against women and children.

# CHAPTER II

REGIONAL GOVERNMENT AUTHORITY

#### Article 3

(1) The Regent has the authority to provide protection to women and children from violence. (2) The authority as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

- a. prevention of violence;
- b. services for victims of violence; and c. institutional strengthening and development.

#### Article 4

(1) In exercising the authority as referred to in Article 3, the Regent establishes programs and activities for the protection of women and children in the Regional Action Plan.

(2) The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (1) shall contain at least:

- a. programs and action activities for the protection of women and children from violence;
- b. programs and regional action activities to prevent all forms of violence and discrimination against women and children;
- c. programs and action activities for the improvement and enhancement of services for the protection of women and children from violence; and
- d. programs and action activities for the empowerment of women from violence.
  - (3) Provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be further regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER III

PREVENTION OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Types of Violence

#### Article 5

Types of violence include:

- a. physical violence;
- b. psychological violence;
- c. sexual violence;
- d. neglect;
- e. exploitation; and/or
- f. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is an act that results in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes death.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c includes:

- a. acts in the form of sexual harassment: b. forced sexual intercourse;
- c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
- d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d includes:

- a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
- b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
- c. acts of abandoning a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
- d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is:

- a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
- b. acts with or without the victim's consent which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues;
- c. utilizing a person's labor or ability by another party to gain benefits, both material and immaterial; and/or
- d. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to gain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecent acts.

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f namely:

- a. threats of violence include any unlawful act in the form of words, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and
- b. coercion, includes a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

## Part Two

Prevention Efforts

#### Article 12

Prevention of violence as referred to in Article 3 paragraph (2) letter a is carried out by: a. Regional Government;

b. families; and

c. Community;

d. mass media; and

e. business sector.

#### Article 13

(1) Violence prevention efforts carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 12 letter a include:

- a. socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection of women and children;
- b. education about the dangers of domestic violence;
- c. mapping of areas prone to violence;
- d. compilation of data and information about women and children vulnerable to violence; and/or
- e. mental and spiritual counseling/guidance. (2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are coordinated by the Agency.

#### Article 14

(1) Families and Communities carry out violence prevention efforts independently according to their needs and capabilities.

(2) The Regional Government provides support and facilitation for violence prevention efforts carried out by the community.

# CHAPTER IV

PROVISION OF SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

Part One

Rights of Victims of Violence

#### Article 15

Women and children who are victims of violence are entitled to receive:

- a. Protection;
- b. information;
- c. excellent service;
- d. continuous handling up to the stage of rehabilitation and social reintegration;
- e. confidential handling;
- f. psychological and legal assistance; and g. guarantees of rights related to their status as family members and community members.

#### Article 16

In addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 15, child victims of violence also receive special rights in the form of:

- a. the right to respect and full use for survival, growth, and development;
- b. the right to basic services;
- c. the right to equal protection;
- d. the right to be free from various stigma; and
- e. the right to obtain freedom in accordance with the law.

#### Article 17

(1) In the event that victims of violence require continuous handling, they have the right to reside in a Safe House or alternative residence.

(2) Continuous handling as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes handling at the Social Rehabilitation stage carried out by individuals, groups or Government and non-Government institutions.

(3) The Safe House as referred to in paragraph (1) provides a place and/or temporary safe shelter for victims of violence until their rights are restored.

#### Article 18

(1) The Regional Government provides a Safe House in the Region.

(2) The operation and/or management of the Safe House as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Agency.

#### Article 19

(1) In the event the Regional Government has not yet provided a Safe House as referred to in Article 18 paragraph (1), the Regional Government may cooperate with third parties that own a Safe House.

(2) Cooperation with third parties as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Handling of Violence Victims

#### Article 20

(1) Handling provided to women and children victims of violence in the form of services, among others:

- a. complaints;
- b. health;
- c. legal aid;
- d. Social Rehabilitation;
- e. medicolegal;
- f. psychological; and
- g. assistance.
  - (2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be organized with:
- a. prompt and appropriate;
- b. safe and comfortable;
- c. sense of empathy;
- d. non-discrimination;
- e. easily accessible;
- f. free of charge; and
- g. guaranteed confidentiality

#### Article 21

Complaint services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter a include:

- a. identification or initial recording of victims; and b. consent for action to be taken.

#### Article 22

(1) Health Services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter b include:

- a. first aid to victims;
- b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel; and
- c. referral to health facilities in accordance with professional standards;
  - (2) In the event women and children victims of violence require treatment, health personnel are obligated to provide health recovery and Social Rehabilitation services.
  - (3) The provision of health recovery and Social Rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 23

(1) Legal services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter c to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by way of: a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding victims' rights and the judicial process;

b. accompanying victims at the reporting, investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and assisting victims to fully describe the violence they experienced; and

c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer assistants, and social workers so that the judicial process runs as it should.

#### Article 24

(1) Social Rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter d, among others:

- a. motivation and psychosocial diagnosis;
- b. care and nurturing;
- c. vocational training and entrepreneurship development;
- d. mental spiritual guidance;
- e. physical guidance;
- f. social guidance and psychosocial counseling; g. accessibility services;
- h. social assistance and aid;
- i. resocialization guidance;
- j. advanced guidance; and/or
- k. referral.
  - (2) Forms of vocational training and entrepreneurship development as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c, among others:
- a. skills training;
- b. field work practice;
- c. apprenticeship;
- d. entrepreneurial skills training;
- e. facilitation of forming joint business groups; f. business capital in the form of business support tools; and/or
- g. business implementation assistance.

#### Article 25

(1) Medicolegal services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter e constitute medical services to provide optimal professional assistance in utilizing medical science for the purposes of law enforcement and justice.

(2) Medicolegal services as referred to in paragraph (1) among others:

- a. visum et repertum; and
- b. visum et psikiatrikum.

#### Article 26

(1) Psychological services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter f constitute an action taken to restore the psyche of women and children as victims of acts of violence.

(2) Psychological services as referred to in paragraph (1) among others:

- a. conducting psychological evaluations for women and children victims of violence referred by police using forensic interviews with various techniques;
- b. conducting individual psychological counseling for women and children victims of violence with psychological disorders related to the violence they experienced;
- c. implementing family psychological counseling for women and children victims of violence;
- d. conducting individual psychotherapy for women and children victims of violence with psychological disorders related to the violence they experienced;
- e. conducting group psychotherapy for women and children victims of violence with psychological disorders related to the violence they experienced;
- f. providing psychological assistance for children in conflict with the law; and
- g. providing testimony as a factual witness in all levels of the legal process (Official Report of Examination, case review and trial)
  - (3) In addition to psychological services as referred to in paragraph (1), psychological services may be in the form of spiritual guidance.
  - (4) Spiritual counselors as referred to in paragraph (3) provide services to women and children victims of violence in the form of providing counseling to strengthen and provide a sense of security.

#### Article 27

(1) Assistance services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (1) letter g are carried out by individuals or institutions that have expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of violence victims.

(2) Assistance services as referred to in paragraph (1) include:

- a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;
- b. accompanying victims during the medicolegal process; c. accompanying victims during the mediation process, examination at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;
- d. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecutor's Office and Court;
- e. protecting the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media;
- f. coordinating with other assistants; and/or
- g. providing continuous handling up to the Social Rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 28

Provisions regarding the handling of violence victims as referred to in Article 20 shall be further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

UPT PPA

#### Article 29

(1) The Regional Government may establish UPT PPA to provide services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, require special protection, and other problems in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

(2) UPT PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) is positioned under and responsible to the Service.

#### Article 30

(1) UPT PPA in carrying out its duties as referred to in Article 29 performs service functions among others: a. public complaints;

b. victim outreach;

c. case management;

d. temporary shelter;

e. mediation; and/or

f. victim assistance.

(2) UPT PPA provides services as referred to in paragraph (1) based on minimum service standards established by laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER V

INSTITUTIONAL STRENGTHENING AND DEVELOPMENT Part One

Institutional Strengthening

#### Article 31

(1) In implementing violence prevention and services to women and children victims of violence, the Service may cooperate with regional apparatus/related institutions.

(2) Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in the form of FPKK.

(3) Regional apparatus/related institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) among others are regional apparatus/institutions whose tasks and functions are in the fields of:

- a. education;
- b. manpower;
- c. health;
- d. social affairs;
- e. women's empowerment and child protection;
- f. mental and spiritual;
- g. public tranquility and order.
- h. population and civil registration;
- i. law; and
- j. cooperatives and MSMEs.

## Part Two

Institutional Development

#### Article 32

(1) The Regional Government shall optimize and facilitate FPKK in the prevention and provision of services to women and children victims of violence.

(2) FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: a. Regency FPKK;

b. Kapanewon FPKK; and

c. Kalurahan FPKK.

#### Article 33

(1) The Regent shall establish the Regency FPKK.

(2) The establishment of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by Regent's Decree.

#### Article 34

The Regency FPKK as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (1) letter a has the tasks including: a. coordinating and synchronizing efforts to organize protection for women and children from violence carried out by members; b. building, maintaining, and developing networks and referral systems;

c. communicating, socializing, and educating the community to play an active role in protecting women and children from violence;

d. conducting monitoring and evaluation;

e. coordinating the implementation of services for handling women and children victims of violence at the Regency level;

f. collecting and disseminating data and information related to the organization of protection for women and children from violence;

g. conducting supervision, guidance, and assistance to Kapanewon FPKK and/or Kalurahan FPKK;

h. receiving referrals from Kapanewon FPKK and/or Kalurahan FPKK; and/or

i. reporting implementation to the Regent periodically.

#### Article 35

The elements of Regency FPKK as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter a include:

- a. Government Agencies; and
- b. community institutions.

#### Article 36

(1) Government Agencies as referred to in Article 35 letter a at a minimum include Government Agencies that have tasks and functions in the fields of:

- a. social affairs;
- b. women and children affairs;
- c. health;
- d. education;
- e. youth and sports;
- f. religion;
- g. population;
- h. manpower; and/or
- i. industry and trade.
  - (2) Community institutions as referred to in Article 35 letter b are grouped based on their fields of activity, including:
- a. health field;
- b. psychology field;
- c. legal field;
- d. social field; and/or
- e. economic field.

#### Article 37

(1) The Panewu shall establish the Kapanewon FPKK. (2) The establishment of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by Panewu's Decree.

#### Article 38

The Kapanewon FPKK as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter b has the tasks including: a. implementing and coordinating the handling of women and children victims of violence within the Kapanewon scope;

b. receiving referrals from Kalurahan FPKK; and/or

c. documenting case handling in accordance with established mechanisms and service standards.

#### Article 39

The Kapanewon FPKK as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter b consists of at least the following elements: a. health;

b. education;

c. law;

d. psychosocial;

e. Community;

f. spirituality;

g. children's forum; and/or

h. empowerment.

#### Article 40

(1) The Kalurahan FPKK shall be established by the Lurah. (2) The establishment of Kalurahan FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined by Lurah's Decree.

(3) In the event that the Kalurahan FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) has not been established, the Regency FPKK and/or Kapanewon FPKK may provide facilitation and assistance.

#### Article 41

The Kalurahan FPKK as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter c consists of at least the following elements: a. health;

b. education;

c. law;

d. community leaders;

e. religious leaders;

f. children's forum/youth organization;

g. Kalurahan officials; and/or

h. other related organizations/institutions.

#### Article 42

The Kalurahan FPKK as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (2) letter c has the tasks including:

- a. formulating programs and activities in efforts to prevent and handle violence against women and children within the Kalurahan scope; and/or
- b. referring cases and coordinating with Kapanewon FPKK or Regency FPKK in the event that Kalurahan FPKK experiences obstacles related to case handling.

# CHAPTER VI

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 43

(1) The community may participate in the organization of protection for women and children victims of violence.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

- a. forming family partners at the kalurahan level; b. forming community institutions whose field of work is in the protection of women and children;
- c. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
- d. conducting prevention efforts if finding indications of violence against women and children in the community;
- e. providing first aid to women and children victims of violence; and
- f. reporting to the competent authority if violence against women and children occurs in their environment.
  - (3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out by individuals, community institutions, educational institutions, religious institutions, and the business sector.

#### Article 44

(1) The Regional Government shall involve the business sector and private parties to participate in the protection of women and children.

(2) Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may include:

- a. socialization to employees in the business environment regarding the rights of women and children;
- b. socialization to employees in the business environment regarding the dangers of violence against women and children;
- c. providing assistance in the organization of protection for women and children as a form of corporate social responsibility.

# CHAPTER VII

FUNDING

#### Article 45

In order to support the organization of protection for women and children victims of violence, funding is required which may be sourced from:

- a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBD); b. Kalurahan Revenue and Expenditure Budget (APBKal); and
- c. other lawful and non-binding sources in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 46

(1) The Regent shall conduct guidance and supervision of the organization of protection for women and children victims of violence.

(2) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by the Agency.

(3) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in order to realize the objectives of organizing protection for women and children victims of violence in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

(4) Guidance and supervision shall be implemented at least (two) times a year.

# CHAPTER IX

CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 47

(1) Upon the entry into force of this Regional Regulation, the provisions of laws and regulations governing the Establishment, Organizational Structure, Duties, Functions, and Work Procedures by the Technical Implementers of Women's and Children's Protection shall remain in effect insofar as they are not inconsistent with the provisions of this Regional Regulation or are not specifically regulated in this Regional Regulation.

(2) Upon the entry into force of this Regional Regulation, FPKK that have been established shall remain in effect insofar as they are not inconsistent with the provisions of this Regional Regulation or are not specifically regulated in this Regional Regulation.

#### Article 48

Upon the entry into force of this Regional Regulation: a. Regional Regulation of Gunungkidul Regency Number 25 of 2012 concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence (Regional Gazette of Gunungkidul Regency of 2012 Number 25); and

b. Regent Regulation of Gunungkidul Number 48 of 2019 concerning Organization of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence (Regional News of Gunungkidul Regency of 2019 Number 48); are revoked and declared invalid.

#### Article 49

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date it is promulgated.

In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placement in the Regional Gazette of Gunungkidul Regency.

That women and children as vulnerable groups in family and community life often experience problems that violate human rights, that the protection of women and children is a mandatory concurrent affair that constitutes the authority, obligation, and responsibility of the Regional Government, thus requiring regulation as a basis for the implementation of comprehensive and integrated protection for women and children in a Regional Regulation.

In this regard, protection efforts in the Region are needed in the form of institutional and regulatory support that can guarantee its implementation. This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for women and children from violence in Gunungkidul Regency.
