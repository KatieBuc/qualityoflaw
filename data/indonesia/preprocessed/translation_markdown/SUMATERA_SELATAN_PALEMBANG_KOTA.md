REGARDING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

MAYOR OF PALEMBANG,

Considering: a. that every citizen has the right to obtain protection and guarantee of their human rights, and has the right to receive fair treatment to obtain equal opportunities and benefits in achieving life welfare;

b. that the laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children in regulating the efforts of women and children to be free from acts of violence in the region require regulations that can ensure their implementation;

c. that all forms of acts of violence against citizens, especially women and children, constitute a form of violation of human rights, therefore the state, government, society, and families are obliged and responsible for the protection of women and children from acts of violence;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence;

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

- 1. City is the City of Palembang.
- 2. City Government is the Government of the City of Palembang.
- 3. Mayor is the Mayor of Palembang.
- 4. Regional Apparatus is the Regional Apparatus within the City Government.
- 5. Office of Women Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment is the Office of Women Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment of the City of Palembang.
- 6. Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA, is a Technical Implementation Unit established by the government to provide services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.
- 7. Women are all women who are both inside and outside the household.
- 8. Children are persons who have not yet reached the age of 18 years, including children still in the womb.
- 9. Act of Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, or psychological to the victim.
- 10. Victim is a woman and/or child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of an act of violence.
- 11. Reporter is a person, family, or community who sees and hears that an act of violence against a woman has occurred or any woman who becomes a victim of an act of violence by another person.
- 12. Protection of Women and Children Facing the Law is a form of service that must be implemented by law enforcement officials or security officials to provide a sense of security, both physical and psychological, to victims or witnesses from threats, disturbances, terror, and violence from any party given at the stage of investigation, inquiry, prosecution, and during examination at court hearings.
- 27. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband, wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct lineage upward or downward up to the third degree.
- 28. Family Resilience is the dynamic condition of a family that has tenacity and resilience and contains physical material and spiritual mental capabilities to live independently and develop themselves and their families to live harmoniously in improving physical and spiritual welfare.
- 29. Community is a group of citizens who inhabit an official administrative area of government (at the level of Neighborhood Association, Community Association, Village and District) and cooperate in life for a sufficiently long time and comply with existing rules.
- 30. Religious Leaders consist of Ustadz/Ustadzah, Ulama, Priests, Pastors, Monks, Pedanda/Pandita/Sulinggih and other religious leaders who have gender sensitivity/awareness.
- 31. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy in order to provide strengthening and recovery to victims of violence as determined in a decision of the authorized official.

#### Article 2

Protection of women and children from acts of violence is implemented based on the principles of:

- a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of victims;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. local wisdom;
- d. non-discrimination;
- e. best interests of women and children;
- f. active participation; and
- g. restoration.

#### Article 3

The regulation of protection of women and children from acts of violence aims to:

- a. prevent violence against women and children;
- b. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children;
- c. provide services to women and children who are victims of acts of violence; and
- d. carry out empowerment of women victims of violence.

#### Article 4

The scope of regulation of protection of women and children from acts of violence includes:

- a. forms of acts of violence;
- b. rights and obligations;
- c. protection of women and children;
- d. guidance and supervision; and
- e. funding.

# CHAPTER II

FORMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of acts of violence include:

- a. physical violence;
- b. psychological violence;
- c. sexual violence;
- d. neglect; and
- e. exploitation.

#### Article 6

(1) The form of physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is that physical violence is any act that results in pain according to medical experts, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

(2) The form of psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is that psychological violence is an act that results in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, a sense of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

(3) The form of sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is that sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, coercion of sexual relations, in an unnatural manner or not desired by one party for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

(4) The form of neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is that neglect is an act of releasing and/or abandoning the responsibility of care, upbringing, education and livelihood towards any person who is a family member.

(5) The form of exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter e is that exploitation is utilizing all forms of potential whether physical, psychological and intelligence in women or children for economic gain or personal pleasure of the perpetrator.

# CHAPTER III

RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS

## Part One

Rights of Women and Children

#### Article 7

Women and children from acts of violence shall receive the following rights:

- a. the right to have their dignity respected as human beings; b. the right to recovery of health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by victims;
- c. the right to determine their own decisions;
- d. the right to obtain information;
- e. the right to confidentiality of identity;
- f. the right to restitution;
- g. the right to social rehabilitation;
- h. the right to complaint handling;
- i. the right of children to education;
- j. the right to obtain facilities in the judicial process; and
- k. the right to assistance.

## Part Two

Obligations of the Government, Community and Family

### Paragraph 1

City Government

#### Article 8

(1) In efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence, the City Government is obligated to:

- a. guarantee the rights of women and children without discrimination in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations; b. establish and implement policy programs and activities for the protection of the rights of women and children; c. facilitate the formation of community organizations concerned with women and children;
- d. cooperate with the community and/or community organizations and the private sector in the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
- e. carry out rehabilitation and empowerment of women and children who are victims of acts of violence; f. provide special protection to women and children who are victims of various acts of violence; and
- g. take preventive measures in order to prevent violations of the rights of women and children. (2) The responsibility in the effort to protect women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by regional apparatus in accordance with their duties and functions.
  - (3) Further provisions regarding the obligations of the City Government as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

### Paragraph 2

Community

#### Article 9

(1) Participate in prevention and provide support for the development of prevention programs for violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence.

(2) Provide information and/or report violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence to the authorities.

(3) Supervise the law enforcement process for cases of violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence.

(4) Small group units in society consisting of religious scholars, intellectuals, customary community units are responsible for protecting and preventing violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence.

(5) Community Participation referred to in paragraph (1) means that community responsibility is based on laws and regulations and local wisdom values of the local community.

### Paragraph 3

Family

#### Article 10

The family in efforts to protect the rights of women and children is responsible for:

- a. protecting women and children from conflicts and violence in the household that degrade the dignity of women and children;
- b. providing equal opportunities for women and children to develop themselves in the fields of education, social, economic, political and cultural; and
- c. preventing violations of the rights of women and children in the household.

# CHAPTER IV

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

Prevention

#### Article 11

( 1) In order to prevent violations of the rights of women and children, the City Government:

- a. conducts socialization regarding the prevention of violence against women and children by optimizing the role of community units, community leaders, religious leaders, social organizations and mass media;
- b. provides guidelines on handling acts of violence against women and children;
- c. develops an integrated database and information system on acts of violence against women and children;
- d. strengthens the function of social organizations engaged in protection and advocacy for women and children; and
- e. provides public facilities that are comfortable and safe for women and children.
  - (2) The responsibility of the City Government in efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph ( 1) is carried out by regional apparatus for women and children in accordance with their duties and functions.
  - (3) Further provisions regarding planning for the prevention of violations of the rights of women and children are integrated by the City Government into regional development planning.

#### Article 12

In order to prevent violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence, society: . . . a. plays an active role in monitoring various conditions occurring in their environment that may cause violations of the rights of women and children;

b. strengthens the role of social organizations, religious organizations and the family environment; and

c. prevents unregistered marriages and child marriages.

#### Article 13

In order to prevent violations of the rights of women and children from acts of violence, the family: a. protects women and children from various violations of rights in the household;

b. encourages the formation of a harmonious, educative, independent and fully responsible family; and

c. serves as a place for initial counseling and mediation for any family member suspected of violations of the rights of women and children.

## Part Two

Handling

#### Article 14

( 1) The implementation of handling women and children victims of acts of violence includes:

- a. handling complaints of victims of violence;
- b. health services; and
- c. law enforcement and legal assistance.
  - (2) The implementation of handling women and children victims of acts of violence as referred to in paragraph ( 1) is carried out through integrated services.
  - (3) Integrated services as referred to in paragraph (2) for women and children victims of acts of violence are carried out with the principles of:
- a. responsive;
- b. non-discrimination;
- c. equal and respectful relationships;
- d. maintaining confidentiality;
- e. providing a sense of security and comfort;
- f. respecting individual differences;
- g. non-judgmental;
- h. respecting the choices and decisions of victims;
  - 1. sensitive to the background and condition of victims; J. using language that is appropriate and understood by victims;
- k. fast and simple;
  - 1. empathy;
- m. fulfillment of the rights of women and children; and
- n. legal certainty.

#### Article 15

Handling of women and children victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 14 paragraph (1) letter a is carried out by:

- a. hospitals;
- b. public health centers;
- c. Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children; d. Safe houses;
- e. Women Crisis Centre;
- f. Marriage Guidance, Development and Preservation Board; g. Police;
- h. Prosecutor's Office;
- i. Courts;
- j. Ministry of Law and Human Rights;
- k. Legal Aid Institutions; and
  - 1. Other Institutions.

#### Article 16

(1) Complaints of acts of violence against women and children can be made by reporters consisting of victims, families, communities and/or social organizations.

(2) Reporters as referred to in paragraph (1) may make complaints:

- a. directly or through direct findings;
- b. through telephone or the like; and
- c. through letter.
  - (3) In addition to handling complaints submitted by reporters, officers at service institutions are also authorized to handle women and children victims of acts of violence obtained through other information.
  - (4) Further provisions regarding the procedures for complaint handling services are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 17

( 1) Health services in handling women and children victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 14 paragraph (1) letter b for women and children victims of acts of violence are carried out comprehensively, accessible 24 hours, quality and carried out with maximum health service standards.

(2) Women and children victims of acts of violence have the right to health services, including:

- a. medical services;
- b. medico-legal services; and
- c. psychosocial counseling.
  - (3) Medical services as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a, consist of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel.
  - (4) Medico-legal services as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b, constitute a form of medical service for the purposes of evidence in the field of law.
  - (2) Social rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
- a. counseling services;
- b. provision of safe houses;
- c. spiritual guidance; and
- d. economic recovery.
  - (3) The provision of services as referred to in paragraph (2), is organized by:
- a. UPT PPA is tasked with carrying out technical operational activities in its working area in providing services for women and children victims of acts of violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems;
- b. the position of UPT PPA is under and responsible to the Head of the Office that administers government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection at the city level; and
- c. religious agencies.

#### Article 21

( 1) Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (2) letter a aim to:

- a. assist victims in recognizing their problems and finding effective ways to overcome them themselves;
- b. empower victims to determine their future; c. provide moral support for victims in facing the legal process they are undergoing; and
- d. make victims feel accepted in the community environment. (2) Counseling services are carried out by gender-responsive social rehabilitation officers, consisting of:
- a. counselors who have certificates;
- b. psychologists; and
- c. psychiatrists.
  - (3) Counseling services are carried out in a place that guarantees a sense of security, comfort and confidentiality of information from victims. (4) Stages of counseling services include:
- a. initial counseling;
- b. follow-up counseling;
- c. case discussion; and
- d. field visits.

#### Article 22

( 1) For social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (2) letter b, the City Government may provide a safe house for women and victims of acts of violence in the form of intimidation by perpetrators or other parties.

(2) The location of the safe house as referred to in paragraph (1) must be kept confidential.

(3) Criteria for women and children victims of acts of violence who require a safe house include:
