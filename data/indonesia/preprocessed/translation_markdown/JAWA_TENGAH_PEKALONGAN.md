# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Region is Pekalongan Regency.
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
- 3. Agency for Community, Women and Family Planning Empowerment hereinafter referred to as BPMPKB is the Agency for Community, Women and Family Planning Empowerment of Pekalongan Regency as the institution that formulates and implements regional policies that are specific in the field of community, women and family planning empowerment. jdih.pekalongankab.go.id
- 4. Child is a person under the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb.
- 5. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
- 6. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and fulfilling the rights of victims carried out by family, integrated services, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecution, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
- 7. Protection against Gender is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecution, courts, regional government, or other parties who know or hear that violence against men or women will or has occurred either temporarily or based on court determination.
- 8. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
- 9. Gender-Based Violence is any form of restriction, exclusion, differentiation and all forms of treatment carried out on the basis of gender and aimed at reducing, eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or use of human rights, the consequences of which include and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological and economic violence.
- 10. Violence against children is any form of restriction, differentiation, exclusion and all forms of treatment carried out against children, the consequences of which include and are not limited to physical, sexual, psychological, and economic violence.
- 11. Victims of gender-based violence are persons who due to their gender experience physical, psychological, economic, social, sexual suffering, and other losses resulting from State policies, acts of violence and/or threats of violence in the scope of household and community.
- 12. Integrated Services is a series of activities to provide protection for victims of gender-based violence and children carried out jointly by relevant agencies or institutions as one unified implementation of prevention efforts, health services, psychosocial rehabilitation, repatriation, social reintegration, and jdih.pekalongankab.go.id legal aid for victims of gender-based violence and children.
- 13. Companion is a person or institution and/or body that has expertise in accompanying victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
- 14. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT is a unified unit that provides integrated services for women and child victims of violence.
- 15. Medical Services are facilities that provide clinical services in the field of diagnostics, and/or inpatient care for victims.
- 16. Social rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial condition disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning both in the family and in the community.
- 17. Legal Aid is the provision of legal assistance to victims seeking justice who are unable and face difficulties in the legal field outside or before the court without compensation.
- 18. Repatriation is the act of returning victims to their region of origin or country of origin while prioritizing protection services and fulfillment of their needs.
- 19. Social Reintegration is the reunification of victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
- 20. Safe House is a shelter for victims during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and danger from perpetrators.
- 21. Service Standards are benchmarks used as guidelines for service delivery and reference for assessing service quality as obligations and promises of organizers to the community in the context of quality, appropriate, easy, affordable and measurable services.
- 22. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and their children, or mother and their children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.
- 23. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, nursing, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household. jdih.pekalongankab.go.id
- 24. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget hereinafter abbreviated as APBD is the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of Pekalongan Regency.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of victims of gender-based violence and children is based on Pancasila and grounded in the 1945 Constitution, as well as basic principles that include:

- a. non-discrimination;
- b. best interests of victims;
- c. justice and gender equality;
- d. protection of victims;
- e. maternal survival;
- f. child survival, growth, and development; g. respect for the child's opinion;
- h. transparency;
- i. integration;
- j. not blaming victims;
- k. empowering;
- l. victim confidentiality;
- m. decision-making in the hands of victims.

#### Article 3

The purpose of implementing protection for victims of gender-based violence is to provide services and protection to men and women victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence that occurs within the household and/or outside the household.

# CHAPTER III

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 4

(1) The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to implement prevention efforts against the occurrence of violence in the form of:

- a. providing data and information about gender and child victims of violence in accordance with laws and regulations; b. conducting education on anti-gender-based violence values and violence against children; and c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection against gender-based violence and child victims of violence. jdih.pekalongankab.go.id
  - (2) To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government is obligated to provide and organize services for victims in the form of: a. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for victims by involving elements of SKPD, Agencies, Institutions, Community Organizations, Community;
- b. providing facilities and infrastructure;
- c. increasing the capacity of service provider institutions; d. conducting coordination and cooperation in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children;
- e. conducting monitoring and evaluation.
- f. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for victims.
  - (3) The Regional Government in carrying out obligations as referred to in paragraph (1) and (2) must consider the rights and obligations of parents, guardians, husbands or other persons who are legally responsible for victims.

# CHAPTER IV

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

(1) Forms of Gender-Based Violence include a. Physical Violence;

b. Psychological Violence;

c. Sexual Violence;

(2) Forms of Violence against Children include a. Physical Violence;

b. Psychological Violence;

c. Sexual Violence;

d. Neglect;

e. Exploitation;

f. Maltreatment

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) point a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or cause death.

#### Article 7

Psychological Violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) point b is caused by acts that result in jdih.pekalongankab.go.id fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to the victim.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (1) point c is caused by:

- a. acts in the form of sexual harassment both physical and psychological;
- b. forced sexual intercourse;
- c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
- d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) point d is caused by:

- a. acts that result in the unmet needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
- b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after children as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
- c. acts of abandoning persons within the household scope, whereas according to the applicable law or due to an agreement or contract, one is obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
- d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting from working decently inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) point e is caused by:

- a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;
- b. acts with or without the victim's consent that include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissue or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
- c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities or obscenity.

#### Article 11

Maltreatment as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) point f is caused by:

- a. all acts or actions whether intentional or unintentional committed by others that make an individual sick or disturbed in their feelings, or receive unpleasant feelings that make a person sad, disappointed, annoyed, angry and afraid.
- b. all sexual violations committed or permitted to be committed against young persons by adults or others who are legally responsible for them, including touching a child for sexual gratification or forcing a child to touch an adult, sexual intercourse, exposing sexual activities to children, pornography or allowing children to engage in sexual intercourse inappropriate for their development.

# CHAPTER V

VICTIMS' RIGHTS

#### Article 12

Every victim of gender-based violence and children receives the following rights:

- a. right to have their dignity and honor respected as a human being; b. right to make their own decisions;
- c. right to obtain information;
- d. right to confidentiality of their identity;
- e. right to complaint handling;
- f. right to health and psychological recovery from suffering experienced by the victim;
- g. right to receive facilitation in the judicial process; h. right to receive repatriation and social reintegration efforts for victims of gender-based violence and children. i. right to social rehabilitation.

## Part One

Institutional Framework

#### Article 13

In carrying out the obligation to protect victims of gender-based violence and children, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center (PPT)

## Part Two

Integrated Service Center

#### Article 14

(1) The PPT as referred to in Article 13 is established by the Regional Government by involving Regional Work Units, institutions, agencies and community organizations that have competence in providing protection to victims of gender-based violence and children.

(2) The PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) is a functional work unit that has the main tasks and functions of providing protection to victims of gender-based violence and child victims of violence in the form of seeking prevention, recovery, and social reintegration, providing legal protection, conducting coordination and, seeking increased community participation, as well as monitoring and reporting

#### Article 15

(1) The organizational structure of the PPT as referred to in Article 14 consists of:

- a. Chairperson;
- b. Vice Chairperson;
- c. Secretary; and
- d. Members.
  - (2) The divisions in the organizational structure of the PPT at minimum include:
- a. complaint service division;
- b. health service division;
- c. legal aid and law enforcement service division; d. social rehabilitation service division; and
- e. repatriation and social reintegration division.

#### Article 16

The complaint service division as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) point a has the task of:

- a. conducting interviews and observation of the victim's condition; 10 jdih.pekalongankab.go.id
- b. making recommendations for follow-up services;
- c. conducting coordination and referral to services and certain parties and related parties according to the victim's needs; and d. administering the complaint process.

#### Article 17

The health service division as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) point b has the task of:

- a. conducting examination, treatment, and follow-up care of the victim
- b. conducting coordination of health and medicolegal rehabilitation implementation;
- c. conducting medicolegal examination including collection of evidence on the victim and preparation of visum et repertum; d. conducting supporting and laboratory examinations on evidence;
- e. conducting consultation with specialist doctors or making referrals; and
- f. preparing case reports.

#### Article 18

The legal aid service division as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) point c has the task of:

- a. providing legal aid to victims of violence both litigation and non-litigation.
- b. preparing reports on the progress of legal handling.

#### Article 19

The social rehabilitation service division as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) point d has the task of:

- a. providing psychological assistance and social counseling by psychologists, counselors and social workers;
- b. providing spiritual guidance by spiritual counselors; c. providing assistance during the case handling process; and
- d. implementation of social rehabilitation is conducted at "shelter/safe house".

#### Article 20

The field of repatriation and social reintegration as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) letter e has the duties:

- a. coordinating and facilitating the repatriation process of victims and/or facilitating repatriation referrals to the Provincial PPT; jdih.pekalongankab.go.id
- b. facilitating the reintegration process of violence victims to their families, guardians, substitute families, communities, educational institutions or social institutions;
- c. preparing progress reports on the repatriation assistance and social rehabilitation process; and
- c. conducting monitoring for at least three months after the victim is returned to their family, guardian, substitute family, or community.

#### Article 21

(1) The procedures and mechanisms for the establishment of PPT are regulated by Regent Regulation.

(2) PPT Service Standards are regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

EMPOWERMENT OF VICTIMS

#### Article 22

Empowerment of Gender and Children Victims of Violence is implemented in the form of:

- a. Empowerment of gender-based violence victims includes job training, productive economic enterprises and joint business groups with capital assistance.
- b. Empowerment of child victims of violence who are not in school is ensuring child victims of violence receive the right to continue education, and/or receive job training with capital assistance.

# CHAPTER VIII

#### Article 23

(1) The Regional Government conducts guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection for gender-based violence and child victims.

(2) The implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out on a daily basis by BPMPKB.

# CHAPTER IX

FUNDING

#### Article 24

Funding for activities related to the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children conducted by the Regional Government is sourced from APBD, Provincial APBD and/or APBN as well as other lawful sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations. jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

# CHAPTER X

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 25

(1) The community participates in the implementation of protection for victims.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:

- a. providing protection for victims;
- b. providing emergency assistance;
- c. providing advocacy to victims and or the community regarding the handling of gender-based violence and child cases;
- d. assisting in the process of submitting applications for protection orders;
- e. conveying information to the authorized authorities related to gender-based violence and child cases;
- f. organizing strengthening of community groups in handling gender-based violence and children; and
- g. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to gender-based violence and children.

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore it is necessary to protect their dignity and honor and guarantee their right to life in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

In order to prevent and address gender-based violence and violence against children in Pekalongan Regency so as to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades the degree and dignity of humanity, it is necessary to provide protection for gender and child victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation

So far, laws and regulations governing the protection of gender and child victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts at the regional level, thus institutional support and regulations are needed to ensure its implementation.

This Regional Regulation governs protection efforts for victims, particularly with regard to prevention, services and empowerment for gender and child victims of violence in Pekalongan Regency.

#### Article 2

Letter a

"Non-discrimination" means protection for all victims of gender-based violence and children without distinction as to ethnicity, religion, race, class, gender, ethnicity, culture and language, legal status and physical or mental condition.

Letter b

"Best interests of the victim" means all actions concerning victims undertaken by the government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, whereby the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Letter c

"Gender justice" means fair treatment given to women and men. "Gender equality" means a condition and position that reflects a harmonious, balanced relationship between men and women in obtaining opportunities/chances in accessing, participating, controlling and benefiting in the implementation of development as well as enjoying the results of development in family life, as well as in community, nation and state life. jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

Letter d

"Victim protection" means providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family members, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.

Letter e

"Maternal survival" means ensuring that a mother does not experience death that occurs during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum, whether caused by physical or non-physical conditions

Letter f.

"Growth and development" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to education, the right to play, the right to create and recreate.

"Survival" of children is as stated in the principles of the Convention on the Rights of the Child, including the right to identity and the right to enjoy the highest attainable standard of health.

Letter g.

"Respect for the views of the child" means respect for the rights of children to participate and express their views in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives.

Letter h

"Openness" means that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children, it is transparent among integrated service providers.

Letter i

"Integration" means that in the implementation of protection for victims of gender-based violence and children, it is carried out by building coordination among service providers, including medical services, legal counselors, psychologists, religious counselors, social workers, police.

Letter j

"Not blaming the victim" means an attitude and treatment of not blaming the victim for the occurrence of violence experienced by them.

Letter k

"Empowering" means that every effort provided must be able to strengthen the victim, whether physically, psychologically, socially or economically. jdih.pekalongankab.go.id

Letter l

"Victim confidentiality" means any action taken to ensure the victim is in a safe condition from threats or other actions that threaten the life and psychological well-being of the victim.

Letter m

"Decision-making in the hands of the victim" means the victim's right to determine the best choice in resolving their problem.
