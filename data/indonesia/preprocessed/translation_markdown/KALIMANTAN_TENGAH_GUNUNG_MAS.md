# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Region is Gunung Mas Regency.
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration.
- 3. Regent is the Regent of Gunung Mas.

4 . Regional Apparatus is the auxiliary element of the Regent and Regional House of Representatives in the administration of governmental affairs that are the authority of the Region.

5. Protection of Women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.

6. Protection of Children is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from Violence and discrimination.

7. Women is a person who is female. 8 . Children is a person who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old, including children who are still in the womb.

9 . Society is individuals, community groups, including parents and families therein.

10. Prevention is all efforts to eliminate all risks and adverse impacts of violence against Women and Children.

11. Violence is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.

12. Forum for Handling Victims of Violence Against Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as FPK2PA, is a coordinating forum for handling victims of Violence against Women and Children whose implementation is tiered at the regency and district levels.

13. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service center in efforts to empower Women in various fields of development, as well as protect Women and Children from various types of discrimination and acts of Violence, including trafficking in persons established by the government or community-based.

14. Child-Friendly Village/Sub-District, hereinafter abbreviated as K/DRA, is the development of a village/sub-district area that unites the commitment and resources of the village/sub-district government involving the community and business world located in the village/sub-district in order to promote, protect, fulfill and respect children's rights, which are planned consciously and sustainably.

Article 2

Protection of Women and Children is organized based on the principles of: a. respect, fulfillment, protection, advancement, and enforcement of rights; and

b. legal certainty.

Article 3

Protection of Women and Children is implemented based on the principles of: a. responsive to rights;

b. non-discrimination;

c. maintaining confidentiality;

d. empowerment; and

e. participation.

Article 4

The objectives of Protection of Women and Children include:

- a. ensuring the fulfillment of rights;
- b. providing justice and equality of rights;
- c. providing protection and a sense of security; and
- d. conducting empowerment.

Article 5

The scope of Protection of Women and Children includes:

- a. Protection of Women;
- b. Protection of Children;
- c. coordination and cooperation;
- d. responsibilities of the Regional Government;
- e. community participation;
- f. guidance and supervision;
- g. evaluation;
- h. reporting; and
- i. funding.

Community Involvement

#### Article30

(1) The Community shall be involved in the Protection of Women and Children. (2) Community involvement as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of participation through Prevention, services, empowerment, monitoring, supervision and fulfillment of the rights of Women and/or Children.

# CHAPTER VIII

#### Article 32

(1) Evaluation of Women and Children Protection shall be carried out based on documents of service implementation, empowerment, Prevention, fulfillment and monitoring results of the implementation of Women and Children Protection.

I. GENERAL

Children are the future of the nation and the next generation of the nation's ideals, so that every Child has the right to survival, to grow and develop, to participate and has the right to protection from acts of Violence and discrimination as well as civil rights and freedoms.

Likewise Women who play the role of mothers who give birth to healthy, strong, resilient, virtuous, potential and quality children. Through the role of Women in the family, Society and the state will be born resilient generations who will continue the ideals of the nation. The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, particularly Article 27 paragraph (1) which states that "All citizens have equal standing in law and government and are obliged to uphold the law and government without any exception". Although the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia has guaranteed equal standing for every citizen, until now Women and Children still experience various obstacles to obtaining their rights in almost all fields.

On the other hand, Women have special needs compared to men, so in accordance with their nature, in certain conditions the Regional Government must be able to guarantee that their rights are fulfilled according to those needs. Whereas children, they are the mandate and grace of God Almighty, in whom is inherent dignity and worth as a complete human being. Considering the position and expectations for children as the potential and future of the nation, children deserve attention from all parties so that children can grow and develop normally and are protected from wrong treatment and actions, Violence, discrimination that will damage the child's development both physically, mentally and socially. For that reason children need to get the widest possible opportunity to be able to grow and develop optimally, both physically, mentally, socially and with noble character.

Starting from this matter, a regulation is needed that becomes a guideline for all parties, especially the Regional Government in guaranteeing the implementation of Protection of Women and Children in Gunung Mas Regency in accordance with the authority possessed as contained in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government, as well as other laws and regulations that regulate the Protection of Women and Children.
