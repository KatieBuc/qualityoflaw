# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Part One

Definitions

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:

1. Region is Gunungkidul Regency.

2. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration.

3. Regent is the Regent of Gunungkidul.

4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative body as an element of regional government administration.

5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.

6. Woman is a person who is female.

7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering, whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.

8. Violence against Women is any action that results in or may result in misery or suffering to women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.

9. Violence against Children is any action that results in or may result in suffering to children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.

10.Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring in the Special Region of Yogyakarta.

11.Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutors, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.

12. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

13. Service is action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against women and children will, is, or has occurred.

14. Behavior Change Counseling is psychological counseling provided to male perpetrators of domestic violence to help stop violence and become a person who can respect their partner, so as to establish a better relationship with their partner and children.

15. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is a functional work unit that provides integrated services for victims of violence.

16. Networked Integrated Service Center for Women and Child Empowerment, hereinafter referred to as Networked P2TP2A, is an Integrated Service Center in Gunungkidul Regency that provides networked integrated services for women and children victims of violence, established by the Decree of the Regent of Gunungkidul.

17. Forum for Handling Victims of Violence against Women and Children, abbreviated as FPK2PA, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children which is organized in a networked manner and established at the regency and sub-district levels.

18. Safe House is a shelter for victims during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers of perpetrators.

19. Standard Operating Procedure, hereinafter abbreviated as SOP, is the standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from complaints/identification, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, legal services, up to repatriation and social reintegration of witnesses and/or victims.

20. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and their children, or mother and their children, or blood relatives in direct ascending or descending line up to the third degree.

21. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, nursing, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and residing in that household.

22. Gender Mainstreaming in the Region is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of policies, programs, and development activities in the region.

23. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles, functions, and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.

24. Gender Equality is equal conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and national security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.

25. Gender Justice is a process to be fair to men and women.

26. Disaggregated data is data disaggregated by sex and status and conditions of women and men in all areas of development including health, education, economy and employment, the political field and decision-making, the legal and socio-cultural fields and violence.

27. Gender Responsive Budgeting Planning is a series of approaches to integrate gender perspectives in the planning and budgeting process, gender responsive planning is planning to achieve gender equality and justice, which is carried out through integrating experiences, aspirations, needs, potentials, and solving the problems of women and men.

28.Institution for Supervision and Service for Women and Children in Villages or Sub-districts or by Other Names is an institution established by the village community that functions to serve victims of violence with members consisting of local community leaders.

## Part Two

Principles, Objectives, and Scope

#### Article 2

The principles of Victim protection are:

- a. respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination;
- d. best interests of victims; and
- e. empowerment.

#### Article 3

The purpose of protection for women and children victims of violence is to provide guidance for protection and services for the best interests of women and children victims of violence in the domestic sphere and/or public sphere.

#### Article 4

The scope of protection for Victims includes prevention efforts, services to victims of violence, rehabilitation, and empowerment of victims of violence in the Region.

# CHAPTER II

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence include:

- a. physical violence;
- b. psychological violence;
- c. sexual violence;
- d. neglect;
- e. exploitation; and/or
- f. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:

- a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
- b. forced sexual intercourse;
- c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
- d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:

- a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of a child properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
- b. acts of intentionally neglecting to maintain, care for, or manage a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
- c. acts that neglect a person within the scope of one's household, whereas according to the applicable law or due to agreement or contract one is obligated to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
- d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter (e) is caused by:

- a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intent to benefit oneself or others;
- b. acts with or without the victim's consent that include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the energy or abilities of a person by other parties to obtain benefits either material or immaterial; and/or
- c. any form of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:

- a. threats of violence include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without the use of means that cause fear or restrain a person's fundamental freedom; and
- b. coercion, includes a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER III

INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER

#### Article 12

(1) The PPT is established by the Regional Government, Vertical Agencies in the Region, or community institutions.

(2) The Regional Government establishes the Networked P2TP2A as an integrated service center by Regent's Decree. (3) The Networked P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (2) is a functional work unit that has the duties and functions of providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.

(4) In carrying out the duties as referred to in paragraph (3), the Networked P2TP2A may provide temporary services and protection in the form of a safe house.

(5) In the event that the Networked P2TP2A does not have a safe house (shelter) as referred to in paragraph (4), then victims of violence are referred to a PPT that has a safe house.

#### Article 13

(1) The organizational structure of the Networked P2TP2A as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (2) consists of:

- a. General Chairperson;
- b. Executive Chairperson overseeing fields; c. Secretary; and
- d. Treasurer.
  - (2) The fields as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, at a minimum include:
- a. complaint service field;
- b. health service field;
- c. social rehabilitation service field;
- d. repatriation and social reintegration field; and
- e. legal aid service field.

#### Article 14

The General Chairperson as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1) letter a has the duties:

- a. coordinating the formulation of policies, strategies, programs, and activities as well as the necessary steps in the implementation of the Networked P2TP2A;
- b. conducting supervision and guidance on the implementation of protection and handling of victims of violence;
- c. implementing policies established by the Regent; and d. being responsible for the entire process of implementing protection and handling of violence against victims of violence.

#### Article 15

The Executive Chairperson as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1) letter b has the duties:

- a. coordinating the duties and functions of each service field;
- b. controlling the implementation of protection programs and handling of victims of violence;
- c. conducting coordination and cooperation with related external parties;
- d. collecting and utilizing resources effectively and efficiently for protection activities and handling of victims of violence; and
- e. providing periodic reporting to the Regent specifically for victims of trafficking in persons crimes, then a copy of the report is also provided to the task force for prevention and handling of trafficking in persons crimes.

#### Article 16

The Secretary as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1) letter c has the duties:

- a. assisting in carrying out the duties of the General Chairperson; b. assisting in preparing coordination activities and follow-up of protection, handling of victims of violence;
- c. assisting in preparing the work program plan of the central task force;
- d. providing administrative services in the cooperation of the Central Task Force with Ministries/Agencies and community institutions that are members of the Central Task Force;
- e. organizing service activities for the collection, processing, and presentation of data as well as preparation of secretariat activity reports; and
- f. fostering and implementing cooperative relations with relevant Ministries/Agencies and Community Institutions in the implementation of prevention and handling of trafficking in persons crimes.

#### Article 17

The Treasurer as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1) letter d has the duties:

- a. assisting the General Chairperson in managing the finances of the Networked P2TP2A;
- b. doing everything related to financial receipts and expenditures; and
- c. preparing financial reports submitted to the Executive Chairperson and General Chairperson.

#### Article 18

The complaint service field as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter a has the duties:

- a. conducting interviews and observation of the victim's condition; b. making recommendations for follow-up services;
- c. conducting coordination and referral to follow-up services and related parties; and
- d. administering the complaint process.

#### Article 19

The health service field as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter b has the duties:

- a. conducting examination, treatment, and follow-up care of victims;
- b. coordinating the implementation of health rehabilitation and medico-legal;
- c. conducting medico-legal examination including collection of evidence from victims and preparation of visum et repertum; d. conducting supporting and laboratory examination of evidence;
- e. conducting consultation with specialist doctors or making referrals; and
- f. preparing case reports.

#### Article 20

The social rehabilitation service field as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter c has the duties:

- a. providing assistance during the case handling process; and
- b. conducting counseling for victims and/or perpetrators of violence.

#### Article 21

The repatriation and social reintegration field as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter d has the duties:

- a. coordinating with related agencies for victim repatriation;
- b. preparing progress reports on the repatriation assistance process and social rehabilitation; and
- c. conducting monitoring for at least three months after victims are returned to their families.

#### Article 22

The legal aid service field as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter e has the duties:

- a. accompanying every legal service process; and b. preparing progress reports on legal handling.

#### Article 23

The organizational structure of the PPT established by Vertical Agencies in the Region and community institutions, is formed according to the needs of each agency.

#### Article 24

(1) The Networked P2TP2A must have at least three counselors.

(2) The counselors as referred to in paragraph (1) include: a. counselor in the field of law;

b. counselor in the field of health; and

c. counselor in the field of psychology.

(2) The implementation of counseling carried out by the Networked P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) includes providing behavioral change counseling.

#### Article 25

(1) In addition to establishing the Networked P2TP2A, the Regional Government shall establish a Forum for Handling Victims of Violence against Women and Children (FPK2PA).

(2) The FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of the district FPK2PA and sub-district FPK2PA.

(3) The Organizational Structure of the FPK2PA consists of:

- a. Patron;
- b. Advisor;
- c. Steering Board;
- d. Chairperson;
- e. Vice Chairperson;
- f. Secretary;
- g. Field Coordinator;
- h. Members; and
- i. Secretariat Staff.
  - (4) The establishment of the District FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree. (5) The establishment of the Sub-district FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be stipulated by Sub-district Head's Decree. (6) The duties and functions of the FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) are:
- a. coordinating and synchronizing the handling of PPT services;
- b. maintaining and developing networks and referral systems; and
- c. collecting, compiling, and presenting violence reports.
  - (7) The FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.
  - (8) The members of the FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (3) letter h consist of Regional Work Units that have duties and functions in the fields of social affairs, handling of women and children, health, education, youth and sports, religion, population, employment, and/or industry and trade, as well as Vertical Institutions and community institutions grouped in the following fields:
- a. health field;
- b. psychology field;
- c. legal field;
- d. social field; and
- e. economic field.

# CHAPTER IV

PREVENTION EFFORTS AGAINST VIOLENCE

TOWARDS WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

Prevention Efforts Against Violence towards Women Article 26

(1) Every person has an obligation to prevent violence against women.

(2) The Regional Government in efforts to prevent violence against women shall implement gender mainstreaming to realize gender equality and justice in the fields of education, economy, health, law, politics, religion, and socio-culture.

(3) Gender mainstreaming as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out based on disaggregated data as insight-opening data in analyzing gender-responsive budgeting planning.

(4) Further provisions regarding Gender Mainstreaming and Gender-Responsive Budgeting Planning shall be regulated in a Regent's Regulation.

## Part Two

Prevention Efforts Against Violence Towards Children

#### Article 27

(1) The Regional Government and the community shall carry out prevention of violence against children.

(2) Prevention of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes the fulfillment of children's basic rights according to their needs.

(3) Further regulation regarding the fulfillment of children's basic rights shall be regulated in a Regent's Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

SERVICE EFFORTS FOR WOMEN

AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Service Principles

#### Article 28

Principles of service delivery to victims: a. fast;

b. safe and comfortable;

c. empathetic;

d. non-discriminatory;

e. easily accessible;

f. free of charge; and

g. confidentiality guaranteed.

#### Article 29

In the implementation of integrated services, every victim shall receive the following rights:

- a. the right to have their dignity and worth as a human being respected;
- b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
- c. the right to make their own decisions;
- d. the right to obtain information;
- e. the right to confidentiality of their identity;
- f. the right to social rehabilitation;
- g. the right to complaint handling;
- h. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or
- i. the right to assistance.

## Part Two

Service Efforts for Women Victims of Violence Article 30

(1) The Regional Government shall provide services to women victims of violence.

(2) Services carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted by the Networked P2TP2A, in accordance with the type of service needed.

## Part Three

Service Efforts for Child Victims of Violence Article 31

(1) The Regional Government shall provide services to child victims of violence.

(2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by the Networked P2TP2A, according to the type of service needed.

# CHAPTER VI

EMPOWERMENT EFFORTS FOR

WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Empowerment Efforts for Women Victims of Violence Article 32

(1) The Regional Government shall implement empowerment for women victims of violence.

(2) Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by the Networked P2TP2A and may cooperate with all parties.

## Part Two

Empowerment Efforts for Child Victims of Violence.

#### Article 33

(1) The Regional Government shall provide empowerment to child victims of violence.

(2) Empowerment as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by the Networked P2TP2A and may cooperate with all parties.

# CHAPTER VII

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

#### Article 34

The Implementation of Protection shall be carried out in an integrated and coordinated manner within the framework of the Networked P2TP2A and FPK2PA as referred to in Article 25.

# CHAPTER VIII

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 35

(1) Every person shall participate in providing protection to women and children victims of violence. (2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of:

- a. establishing women and children protection institutions in villages whose tasks are to carry out prevention, services, and supervision;
- b. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights; c. providing assistance to victims;
- d. reporting and referring to the authorized agency/institution in the Sub-district if violence against victims occurs in their environment; and
- e. The authorized Agency/Institution in the Sub-district, when necessary, may refer to other Agencies/institutions.

# CHAPTER IX

FUNDING

#### Article 36

Funding for protection activities for victims carried out by the Regional Government shall be charged to the APBD, and/or other legal sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION.

#### Article 37

(1) The Regent shall conduct guidance and supervision on the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence, through the Regional Work Unit that has duties and functions in the field of women's and children's empowerment.

(2) Guidance and Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 38

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation. In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by its placement in the Regional Gazette of Gunungkidul Regency.

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and character without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Gunungkidul Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection to women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

Until now, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection efforts at the Regional level, so that institutional support and regulations that can guarantee their implementation are needed. This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in Gunungkidul Regency.

Letter d

What is meant by "the best interests of victims" is all the best actions concerning victims carried out by the Government, community, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, so the best interests of victims must be the primary consideration.

Letter e

What is meant by "empowerment" is the strengthening of victims of violence to be able to work and earn a living independently after they have recovered and been provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
