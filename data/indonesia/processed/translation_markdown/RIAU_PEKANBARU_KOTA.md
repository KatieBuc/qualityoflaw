CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

1. Region is Pekanbaru City.

2. Regional Government is the Pekanbaru City Government.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Pekanbaru.

4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of Pekanbaru City.
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and DPRD in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.

6. Office of Women Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment of Pekanbaru City or other designation hereinafter referred to as the Office is the Regional Apparatus that carries out the duties and functions of the affairs of

Women Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment.

7. Head of the Office of Women Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment of Pekanbaru City or other designation hereinafter referred to as the Head of Office is the Head of Apparatus whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the affairs of Women Empowerment, Child Protection and Community Empowerment.

8. Community is individuals, Families, Groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.
9. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their Children, or father and his Children, or mother and her Children, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward up to the third degree.

10. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother.

11. Every Person is an individual or corporation, whether in the form of a legal entity or not a legal entity.
12. Protection is all preventive actions, services and empowerment to protect and guarantee the rights of Women and Children from acts of violence.

13. Gender is a concept that refers to the difference in roles and responsibilities of men and Women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
14. Gender and Child Data System is the institutionalization of the administration of gender and Child data consisting of regulatory components, institutions, and mechanisms in the Region in the framework of planning, implementation, evaluation, and reporting of results of policies/programs/activities of development that are gender responsive and child sensitive.

15. Disaggregated Data is data selected by sex and age.

16. Gender responsive is consistent and systematic attention to the differences between Women and Men in society accompanied by efforts to eliminate structural and cultural barriers in achieving gender equality.
17. Gender sensitivity or gender awareness is the ability to identify gaps in power relations between Women and Men in the family and in the community, the impact of gender-based division of labor on Women and Men, and the experiences, problems, needs, interests and aspirations of Women and Men are different.

18. Gender Mainstreaming in the region hereinafter referred to as PUG is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of policies, programs, and development activities in the region.

19. Integrated Service Center hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is a unit that organizes integrated services for victims of violence and is integrated with PPT that organizes integrated services for witnesses and/or victims of criminal acts of trafficking in persons.

20. Gender Equality hereinafter referred to as KG is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between Women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

21. Women's Empowerment is any effort to improve physical, spiritual mental, social, knowledge, and skills so that Women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.

22. Women are humans of female sex and persons recognized by law as Women.

23. Empowerment of victims of violence is the strengthening of Women victims of violence to be able to work and work independently Vv after they have recovered and been provided with health and social rehabilitation services.

24. Criminal Acts of Trafficking in Persons or hereinafter referred to as TPPO is any act or series of acts that meet the elements of criminal acts determined in Law Number 21 Year 2007 Concerning the Eradication of Criminal Acts of Trafficking in Persons.

25. Health rehabilitation is an effort to restore the condition of victims covering physical, psychological and social health so that victims can carry out their roles again properly and reasonably in the family and in society.

26. Social rehabilitation is a service aimed at restoring and developing the abilities of a person who experiences social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions again properly.

27. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of violence from the regional area where the violence occurred back to the area of origin concerned.

28. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with the family, substitute family or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
29. Integrated Service Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPT PPA is a Technical Implementation Unit established by the Regional Government in providing services for Women and Children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.
30. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a Child who is still in the womb.

31. Fulfillment of Children's Rights hereinafter abbreviated as PHA is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and

Hy participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

32. Children with Disabilities are Children who have physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory limitations for a long period of time who in interacting with the environment and community attitudes may encounter obstacles that make it difficult to participate fully and effectively based on equal rights.

33. Children with Excellence are Children who have commendable character, extraordinary intelligence or have potential and/or special talents not limited to intellectual abilities, but also in other fields.

34. Children's Rights are part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and fulfilled by Parents, Family, community, state, government, and regional government.
35. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent acts of violence against Women and Children.
36. Safe House is a temporary residence or new residence designed under strict supervision and guarding for 24 (twenty-four) hours that is kept confidential in accordance with standards based on applicable provisions intended for Women and Children victims of acts of violence.

37. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence will, is or has occurred to victims.
38. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of security against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their development.

39. Violence is any act against a Child that results in suffering or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty unlawfully.

40. Victim is a Woman and/or Child who experiences suffering and/or suffering that occurs in the area of Pekanbaru City.

41. People with HIV/AIDS hereinafter referred to as ODHA are people who are already infected with HIV both at the stage where there are no symptoms and those who already have symptoms.

42. Standard Operating Procedure hereinafter abbreviated as SOP is a series of written instructions that are standardized regarding various processes of government administration, how and when it must be done, where and by whom it is done.

43. Child Friendly City hereinafter abbreviated as KLA is a city that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of commitment and resources of the Government, Regional Government, community and business world that is planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of Children's rights.

# CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

Rights of Women

#### Article 10

Every Woman has the following rights:

- a. b. f. to live in prosperous conditions physically and mentally; to live in a family in a lawful marriage bond in accordance with the teachings of the religion adhered to; to obtain education and teaching in all types, levels and pathways of education; to obtain protection from acts of discrimination; to obtain protection from economic and sexual exploitation; to obtain protection from physical, psychological, sexual violence, and neglect; to obtain decent work based on ability, interest and talent while upholding all matters related to her humanity; to obtain access in advancing the business undertaken in order to create adequate income improvement; to obtain work leave while receiving wages/income in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations; to obtain special protection in the performance of work or profession against matters that may threaten safety and/or health in relation to Women's reproductive function, k. to obtain legal information and services in accordance with her needs; to enjoy a good and healthy environment in accordance with standards and provisions of laws and regulations; to own and manage joint property in a lawful marriage in accordance with laws and regulations; and to have other rights attached in accordance with her human dignity based on the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two

Rights of Children

#### Article 11

Every Child has the following rights:

- a. the right to live, grow and develop naturally in accordance with human dignity, to participate in all fields of life,; to obtain protection from acts of discrimination; to obtain protection from economic and sexual exploitation; to obtain protection from physical, psychological, sexual violence and neglect; population identity and citizenship status; f. to obtain a birth certificate; g. to embrace a religion and worship according to the teachings of the religion h. and beliefs with the assistance of parental/guardian guidance; not to be forced to change religion by foster parents, adoptive parents and by anyone; to be recognized and know their parents, to be raised and cared for by their own parents; k. to obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual, and social needs; to obtain education and teaching in accordance with interests and talents; to obtain care, education, training, self-improvement and ability to participate in community and state life for Children with physical, mental disabilities and special needs; to express and be heard their opinion; to rest and utilize leisure time for self-development outside school hours; to obtain other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Three

Special Rights for Persons with Disabilities

#### Article 12

Women and Children with disabilities, have the right to: obtain various facilities in carrying out various public services; a. and obtain additional facilities and infrastructure in b. conducting activities in public spaces.

# CHAPTER IX

GENDER AND CHILD DATA SYSTEM

#### Article 187

The Region organizes the Gender and Child Data System in the context of implementing PUG and integrating Child rights into selected data as an insight opener, as well as input for gender analysis and fulfillment of Child rights.

#### Article 188

Organization of the Gender Data System is intended as a reference for the Region in providing and utilizing selected data for planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, evaluation and reporting of development policies/programs/activities that are gender-responsive and child-friendly.

#### Article 189

Organization of the Gender and Child Data System aims to:

- a. strengthen and promote institutions (regulations, institutions, mechanisms) of data systems disaggregated by sex and general conditions in the Region, which are reliable, can be presented quickly, accurate, comprehensive, and up-to-date;
- b. build or strengthen coordination mechanisms between Regions, in the implementation of collection and processing of selected data; and
- c. increase the availability and utilization of selected data for planning, implementation, monitoring, evaluation, and reporting of results of development policies/programs/activities ty that are gender-responsive and child-friendly in the Region.

#### Article 190

In organizing the Gender and Child Data System as referred to in Article 187, the Region may establish or develop a forum/working group for selected data.

#### Article 191

The forum/working group for selected data as referred to in Article 190, has tasks including:

- a. coordinating data management units, research units, planning units, and reporting units internally and externally in organizing the gender and Child data system; encouraging implementing units/work units to collect and utilize selected data and gender and Child analysis in formulating planning, implementation, evaluation and reporting of gender-responsive and child-friendly development activities; encouraging data management units, research units, reporting units to document and disseminate selected data into publications and database systems that are updated routinely; conducting monitoring and evaluation of the organization of the gender and Child data system at least (one) time in (one) year; and reporting the results of the implementation of the organization of the gender and Child data system to the Mayor and the DPRD Institution

#### Article 192

Implementation of the Gender and Child Data System covers all aspects of the following activities:

- a. collection;
- b. processing;
- c. analysis; and
- d. presentation of selected data.

#### Article 193

For the interests of effectiveness of organizing the Gender and Child Data System in the Region, the Regional Government engages and involves the following institutions:

- a. academic institutions;
- b. Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs); and
- c. related community groups.

#### Article 194

The gender data system as referred to in Article 187 can be utilized and accessed widely by the public.
