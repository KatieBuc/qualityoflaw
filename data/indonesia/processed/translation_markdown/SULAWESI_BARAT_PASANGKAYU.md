# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Region is Pasangkayu Regency.
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government organizer who leads the implementation of government affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region.
- 3. Regent is the Regent of Pasangkayu.
- 4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regent and DPRD in the administration of Government Affairs which are the authority of the Region.
- 5. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person who is recognized by law as a Woman.
- 6. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 years of age, including a Child in the womb.
- 7. Protection is all service actions to fulfill rights, as well as guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence.

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU

8. Protection of Women is an effort aimed at protecting Women and their rights in order to obtain a sense of security from acts of violence.

9. Protection of Children is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate, optimally in accordance with human dignity and honor, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

10. Violence is any act that results in or causes misery and suffering whether physical, sexual, psychological including neglect, threat of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or in private life.

11. Victim of acts of violence hereinafter referred to as victim is an individual or group of people who experience physical, mental or emotional suffering, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of human rights violations, or criminal acts and acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.

12. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.

13. Regional Action Plan is the foundation and guideline for related agencies and the community, in the context of implementing activities for the provision of protection to Women and Children Victims of acts of Violence.

14. Safe House is temporary housing, provided to give protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

# CHAPTER II

OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE

#### Article 2

The objectives of the protection of Women and Children are to:

# CHAPTER III

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 4

Forms of violence against Women and Children are:

- a. Physical violence;
- b. Psychological violence;
- c. Sexual violence;
- d. neglect; and/or
- e. exploitation.

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU

#### Article 5

Physical violence as referred to in Article 4 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causing death.

#### Article 6

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 4 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, sense of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 7

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 4 letter c is in the form of:

- a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
- b. coercion of sexual relations; and/or
- c. coercion of sexual relations with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 8

Neglect as referred to in Article 4 letter d is caused by:

- a. acts that result in the unfulfillment of the needs of Children in a reasonable manner, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for care;
- b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after Children as they should be, committed by parents, guardians or other parties responsible for their care;
- c. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
- d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting from working JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU appropriately inside or outside the home so that the Victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 9

Exploitation as referred to in Article 4 letter e is caused by:

- a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intent to benefit oneself or others;
- b. acts with or without the consent of the Victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or Services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
- c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the Victim to obtain benefits not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

# CHAPTER IV

RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

Rights of Women

#### Article 10

(1) The Regional Government must protect the rights of Women in the Region.

(2) The rights of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of the right to:

- a. basic needs;
- b. comfort and security;
- c. maximum health Services in facing acts of Violence;
- d. accompaniment services, legal assistance and spiritual guidance in facing acts of Violence; and e. confidentiality regarding the problems faced.

## Part Two

Rights of Children

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU

#### Article 11

(1) The Regional Government must protect the rights of Children in the Region. (2) The rights of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of the right to:

- a. live, grow, develop and participate properly in accordance with human dignity and receive guidance and Protection from Violence and discrimination;
- b. worship according to their religion, think and express themselves according to their level of intelligence and age under parental guidance;
- c. a birth certificate obtained and/or procured by their parents in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations;
- d. receive protection from family, community and Regional Government against discriminatory treatment and exploitation both economically and socially, neglect, cruelty, violence, abuse, injustice and other mistreatment;
- e. obtain formal or non-formal education again for Children who have dropped out of school;
- f. obtain health Services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual and social needs; g. obtain education and teaching according to their abilities, interests and talents; and
- h. obtain other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

DUTIES AND AUTHORITIES

## Part One

Duties

### Paragraph 1

Duties of Regional Government in Protection of Women

#### Article 12

(1) The Regional Government must carry out Protection of Women in the Region.

(2) Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by formulating strategic plans

(2) Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in the form of activities:

- a. Regional Action Plan;

- d. cooperation with institutions that have activities in preventing acts of Violence against Women.

#### Article 14

The Regional Government must assist in handling acts of Violence against Women.

### Paragraph 2

Duties of Regional Government in Protection of Children

#### Article 15

(1) The Regional Government must carry out Protection of Children in the Region.

(2) Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by formulating strategic plans for Protection of Children in the long term, medium term, and short term.

(3) Strategic formulation as referred to in paragraph (2) must be integrated with the Regional development planning documents.

(4) The Regional Government in formulating strategic plans

as referred to in paragraph (3) may involve the community, social institutions and/or other institutions that conduct activities related to Protection of Children.

#### Article 16

The Regional Government provides guidance and counseling for Children to embrace religion in accordance with the religion and beliefs of their parents.

#### Article 17

(1) The Regional Government must provide comprehensive health facilities for Children.

(2) Comprehensive health facilities as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of community health centers and hospitals.

(3) Comprehensive health as referred to in paragraph (1) includes promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, both for basic health Services and referrals.

(4) Comprehensive health as referred to in paragraph (3) is provided free of charge for families who are unable to afford it or poor families.

(5) The Regional Government carries out activities to support the provision of breast milk to infants.

#### Article 18

(1) The Regional Government must provide basic and secondary education for Children.

(2) In providing education as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government provides educational cost assistance or free education for students from poor families and/or neglected Children.

#### Article 19

(1) The Regional Government must accommodate and/or care for Children who are neglected by their parents and/or family.

(2) Accommodation and/or care for Children as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out through cooperation with institutions, social foundations, or Orphanages.

#### Article 20

(1) The Regional Government must provide special Protection for Child Victims of riots or Victims of natural disasters. (2) Special protection as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of fulfilling the needs for:

- a. basic needs consisting of food, clothing, housing, education, health, learning and recreation, security guarantees, and equal treatment; and
- b. special needs for Children with disabilities and Children experiencing psychosocial disorders.

#### Article 21

(1) The Regional Government must carry out prevention of attempts and acts of violence against Children.

#### Article 22

## Part Two

Authorities

#### Article 23

(1) The Regional Government has the authority to:

- a. accommodate Victims of acts of Violence against Women and Children; and
- b. accommodate and/or care for neglected Children. (2) The Regional Government in exercising its authority as referred to in paragraph (1) may cooperate with related parties.

JDIH KABUPATEN PASANGKAYU

#### Article 24

(1) Related parties as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) are:

- a. social community organizations, institutions or orphanages that have activities related to the protection, care, accommodation, guidance, empowerment of Children and/or Women;
- b. social workers or volunteers;
- c. advocates; and
- d. religious counselors.
  - (2) Cooperation as referred to in Article 23 paragraph (2) is carried out in the form of a cooperation agreement.

# CHAPTER VI

HANDLING ACTS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 25

(1) The Regional Government shall prepare medical personnel at community health centers or at hospitals that specifically handle Victims of Violence against Women and Children.

(2) Medical personnel as referred to in paragraph (1) shall provide complete health services to Victims of Violence against Women and Children.

#### Article 26

(1) The Regional Government shall assist in providing special rooms for Victims of Violence against Women and Children.

(2) The special rooms as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be located at the Resort Police and at every Sector Police in the Region.

#### Article 27

(1) Social workers or volunteer personnel as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter b shall be assigned to accompany Victims in facing the process of resolving acts of violence.

(2) In carrying out the duties as referred to in paragraph (1), social workers or volunteer personnel shall conduct activities: a. counseling or guidance to strengthen and provide a sense of security for Victims;

JDIH PASANGKAYU REGENCY

b. providing information regarding the rights of Victims to obtain Protection from the police and the issuance of Protection orders from the court;

c. escorting Victims to Safe Houses or alternative residences; and

d. conducting integrated coordination in providing services to Victims with agencies or related parties needed by Victims.

#### Article 28

(1) The Regional Government shall prepare Safe Houses. (2) Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be used for temporary handling of Victims of Violence against Women and Children.

(3) Safe Houses as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be provided in every Sub-district.

#### Article 29

(1) Advocates as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter c shall assist in providing legal services for Victims or provide legal consultation in the form of information regarding the rights of Victims and the judicial process;

(2) Advocates shall accompany and/or represent Victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court proceedings.

(3) Information on violence from Victims that is personal in nature shall be kept confidential by advocates.

(4) Advocates may coordinate with fellow law enforcers, volunteer companions, and social workers so that the judicial process runs as it should.

#### Article 30

Religious leaders as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) letter d shall be assigned to:

- a. provide spiritual guidance or strengthening of faith and piety to Victims; and
- b. provide explanations regarding the rights and obligations of Victims.

I. GENERAL

Protection from violence that threatens the safety of Women and Children must be provided from an early stage. Protection is necessary to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. For example, Women and Children become Victims of persecution, neglect, eviction and ill-treatment by parents or others. For Women and Children experiencing such problems, the Regional Government must provide Safe Houses and adequate budgets.

Likewise, the role of various parties to help address the problem of violence faced by Women and Children must be in synergy with the programs and activities of the Regional Government. Social community organizations, volunteers, social workers, medical personnel, advocates and religious leaders must also be mobilized and be able to participate in providing and enhancing a sense of security for Women and Children.

For the Regional Government of Pasangkayu Regency, the concrete step to create a legal umbrella in order to provide and enhance a sense of security and prevent acts of violence against Women and Children, is by establishing a Regional Regulation on Protection for Children and Women. Furthermore, the implementation of protection for Women and Children shall be carried out based on the principles of: justice; respect for the rights of Victims; gender justice and equality; non-discrimination; and the best interests of Victims.
