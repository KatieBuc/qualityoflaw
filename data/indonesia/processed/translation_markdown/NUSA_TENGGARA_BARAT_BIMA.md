# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms are defined as:

- 1. Region is Bima Regency.
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent as the element of regional government administration that leads the implementation of government affairs.
- 3. Regent is the Regent of Bima.
- 4. Regional House of Representatives of Bima Regency, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution that functions as an element of regional government administration.
- 5. Regional Apparatus is the supporting element for the Regent and DPRD in administering government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
- 6. Child is a person who has not yet reached 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child still in the womb.
- 7. Rights of the child are part of human rights that must be guaranteed, protected, and fulfilled by parents, family, community, government, regional government, and the state.
- 8. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
- 9. Special Protection is a form of protection received by Children in certain situations and conditions to obtain a guarantee of safety against threats that endanger themselves and their lives in their growth and development.
- 10. Women's Empowerment is a structured effort to realize gender equality in terms of access, participation, control, and benefits in development and control of resources in order to improve the quality of life and enhance the role of women.
- 11. Violence is any act that results in misery and/or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty.
- 12. Regional Children's Forum, hereinafter referred to as Forda, is a forum for Child participation formed with the aim of facilitating children's involvement in decision-making processes and enjoying changes concerning their lives either directly or indirectly, which is implemented with the consent and willingness of all children based on awareness and understanding, in accordance with age and level of thinking maturity.
- 13. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for Women's Empowerment, Child Protection, Population Control and Family Planning, hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD P3AP2KB, is a regional technical implementation unit formed by the regional government to provide services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.
- 14. Gender mainstreaming, hereinafter abbreviated as PUG, is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of national development policies and programs.
- 15. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
- 16. Gender Responsive Planning and Budgeting, hereinafter abbreviated as PPRG, is a planning and budgeting instrument designed to accommodate different needs and difficulties as women and as men with the aim of realizing fair planning and budgeting in order to achieve Gender Equality in development.
- 17. Gender Responsive Budget, hereinafter abbreviated as ARG, is a budget that is responsive to the needs of women and men with the aim of realizing gender equality and justice
- 18. Gender equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so as to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, national defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.
- 19. Child-Friendly Regency, hereinafter abbreviated as KLA, is a regency that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of government, community and business commitments and resources that are planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights.
- 20. Child Rights Mainstreaming, hereinafter abbreviated as PUHA, is a strategy for implementing child protection by integrating children's rights in legislation, policies, programs, activities and budgets starting from the planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring and evaluation stages with the principle of the best interests of the child.
- 21. Child Care is an effort to meet the needs for affection, attachment, safety, and welfare that is permanent and sustainable for the best interests of the Child.
- 22. Gender Mainstreaming Working Group, hereinafter referred to as Pokja PUG, is a consultation forum for implementers and drivers of PUG from various agencies or institutions in the region.
- 23. Gender Mainstreaming Focal Point, hereinafter referred to as Focal Point PUG, is a Regional Apparatus official who has the ability to carry out PUG in their respective work units.
- 24. Regional Medium-Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMD, is a regional planning document for a period of 5 (five) years and refers to the National Medium-Term Development Plan.
- 25. Regional Government Work Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RKPD, is a regional planning document for a period of 1 (one) year. 26. Regional Apparatus Work Plan, hereinafter referred to as Renja Perangkat Daerah, is a work planning document for Regional Apparatus for a period of 1 (one) year.
- 27. Regional Action Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RAD, is the Regional Action Plan in implementing the protection of women and children.

#### Article 2

The implementation of women's empowerment and child protection is carried out based on the principles of:

- a. respect for human rights;
- b. gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination;
- d. victim protection;
- e. best interests of the child;
- f. survival and development of the child;
- g. child participation; and
- h. benefit.

#### Article 3

The implementation of women's empowerment and child protection aims to:

- a. realize opportunities in access, participation, control, and benefits in development and control of resources in the Region; b. prevent and address violence, exploitation, and discriminatory treatment against women and children;
- c. improve the quality and role of women and children; and d. provide data for planning women's empowerment and the fulfillment and protection of children's rights.

# CHAPTER II

AUTHORITY

#### Article 4

(1) The implementation of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in the Region is carried out by the Regional Government.

(2) In implementing the Women's Empowerment and Child Protection, the Regent has the authority to:

- a. implement policies on Women's Empowerment and Child Protection at the regency level;
- b. provide human resources with gender and child rights perspectives, facilities, infrastructure and infrastructure that are friendly, safe, and comfortable for women and Children;
- c. conduct coordination and cooperation with stakeholders that are cross-sectoral in the Region; and
- d. conduct technical guidance and supervision in the Region. (3) The Regent in exercising their authority as referred to in paragraph (2), may assign the Regional Secretary or Regional Apparatus that administers government affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and Regional Child Protection.

#### Article 5

(1) Regional Apparatus that administers governmental affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in providing services for women and children experiencing problems of violence, discrimination, special protection, and other technical problems is implemented by UPTD P3AP2KB.

(2) UPTD P3AP2KB as referred to in paragraph (1) performs service functions:

- a. public complaints;
- b. victim outreach;
- c. case management;
- d. temporary shelter;
- e. mediation; and
- f. victim assistance.
  - (3) In the implementation of service functions as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b, a task force for women's empowerment and child protection may be formed, facilitated by the Regional Apparatus responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
  - (4) Service functions provided by UPTD P3AP2KB as referred to in paragraph (2) refer to service standards that have been established in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

IMPLEMENTATION

Planning

#### Article 6

Planning for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection is integrated into the RPJMD, RKPD, Regional Apparatus Work Plan, and RAD.

## Part Two

Quality of Life for Women

#### Article 7

The implementation of improving the Quality of Life for women is carried out through:

- a. institutionalization of PUG in Regional Government institutions;
- b. Women's Empowerment in the fields of politics, law, social, and economics in community organizations at the Regional level; and
- c. strengthening and development of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the Regional level.

#### Article 8

Institutionalization of PUG in government institutions as referred to in Article 7 letter a is carried out through the implementation of gender-responsive policies, programs, and activities at the regional level.

#### Article 9

Women's Empowerment in the fields of politics, law, social, and economics in community organizations as referred to in Article 7 letter b is carried out through:

- a. improving the quality and quantity of women in the fields of politics, law, social, and economics in community organizations at the regional level; and
- b. involving women in decision-making in the fields of politics, law, social, and economics in community organizations at the regional level.

#### Article 10

Strengthening and development of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions as referred to in Article 7 letter c is carried out through: a. capacity building of resources of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the regional level;

b. assistance in the implementation of Women's Empowerment services at the regional level;

c. development of Communication, Information, and Education on Women's Empowerment at the regional level;

d. strengthening networks among Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the regional level;

e. facilitation of standardization implementation of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the regional level; and

f. development of Women's Empowerment service provider institutions at the regional level.

## Part Three

Protection of Women

#### Article 11

The implementation of women's protection is carried out through: a. prevention of violence against women involving parties in the Region;

b. provision of services for women victims of violence requiring coordination among Regional Apparatus, Vertical Agencies, and other institutions in the Region; and

c. strengthening and development of women's protection service provider institutions in the Region.

#### Article 12

Prevention of violence against women as referred to in Article 11 letter a is carried out through:

- a. implementation of policies, programs and activities for preventing violence against women involving parties within the regional scope; b. provision of education on preventing violence against women in the region; c. enhancing the participation of the community, business sector, and media in preventing violence against women in the region; and
- d. creation and/or utilization of Citizen Information System applications in submitting reports of violence cases against women and children.

#### Article 13

Further provisions regarding the creation and/or utilization of applications as referred to in Article 12 letter d are regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 14

Provision of services for women victims of violence as referred to in Article 11 letter b is carried out through:

- b. provision of public complaint services for women victims of violence in the region;
- c. provision of outreach services for women victims of violence in the region;
- d. provision of case management services for women victims of violence at the district regional level;
- e. provision of temporary shelter services for women victims of violence in the region;
- f. provision of mediation services for women victims of violence in the region; g. provision of assistance services for women victims of violence in the region;
- h. provision of service facilities and infrastructure for women victims of violence in the region;
- i. provision of information facilities and infrastructure available in public spaces; and j. developing a responsive emergency system for occurrences of violence against women and children through applications.

#### Article 15

Strengthening and development of women's protection service provider institutions as referred to in Article 11 letter c is carried out through: a. capacity building of service provider sources for handling and protecting the rights of women victims of violence in the region;

b. provision of specific needs for women in emergency situations and special conditions in the region; and

c. development of Communication, Information, and Education on the protection of women's rights in the region.

# CHAPTER IV

GENDER MAINSTREAMING

#### Article 29

(1) The implementation of Women's Empowerment is carried out through PUG.

(2) The implementation of PUG as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by integrating Gender in the process of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of policies, programs, and development activities of the Regional Government that produces ARG.

(3) Gender-perspective policies, programs, and development activities of the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (2) are included in the RPJMD, Strategic Plan (Renstra) of Regional Apparatus, and Renja SKPD.

(4) The Regent conducts periodic evaluation of PUG implementation every year according to their authority to determine the achievements of PUG implementation.

#### Article 31

(1) In an effort to accelerate the implementation of PUG, institutional strengthening of PUG is carried out by establishing a PUG Working Group and PUG Focal Point. #### Article 32

# CHAPTER V

CHILD-FRIENDLY REGENCY

#### Article 33

(1) The Regional Government may organize CFC for the fulfillment of Children's rights and special protection of Children.

(2) The organization of CFC as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be the responsibility of the Regent.

(3) The Regent shall appoint the Regional Apparatus that administers affairs of women's empowerment and Child Protection as the coordinator of CFC organization.

#### Article 34

The organization of CFC as referred to in Article 33 paragraph (1) shall be implemented in 5 (five) stages, namely:

- a. CFC planning;
- b. pre-CFC;
- c. CFC Regional Action Plan;
- d. CFC implementation; and
- e. CFC evaluation.

#### Article 35

(1) At the CFC planning stage, the Regional Government shall realize its commitment by conducting a CFC declaration, forming a CFC Task Force, and preparing a CFC profile.

(2) The regulation regarding the formation of the CFC Task Force as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decision. (3) The preparation of the CFC profile as referred to in paragraph (1) shall contain data and information reflecting the conditions of CFC indicator implementation in the Region and its measures.

#### Article 36

(1) At the Pre-CFC stage, the Regional Government shall conduct a self-assessment of the CFC profile to determine the Regional CFC status before commencing CFC organization.

(2) The self-assessment as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be based on the achievement of 24 (twenty-four) CFC Indicators included in the institutional aspect and 5 (five) CFC clusters that have been established, namely: a. institutional;

b. civil rights and freedoms cluster;

c. family environment and alternative care cluster;

d. basic health and welfare cluster;

e. education, leisure time utilization, and cultural activities cluster; and

f. special protection cluster.

(3) The self-assessment as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted by referring to the guidelines in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 37

(1) In organizing CFC, the Regional Government shall integrate policies, programs, and development activities in the RAP.

(2) The preparation of the RAP as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 38

The CFC implementation stage shall be carried out based on the RAP that has been prepared by optimizing the role of the CFC Task Force and conducting monitoring.

#### Article 39

The Regent shall conduct periodic evaluation of CFC organization every year in accordance with his/her authority to determine the achievement of CFC organization.

#### Article 40

Further provisions regarding the organization of CFC shall be regulated by Regent's Regulation.

I. GENERAL

The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia mandates Regional Governments to regulate and manage their own regions in order to realize public welfare through improved services, empowerment and community participation. One of the authorities of regional governments is to make regional policies with the aim of improving the welfare of the people in their regions.

In addition, the 1945 Constitution has also mandated in Article 28D paragraph (1) that every person has the right to recognition, guarantee, protection and fair legal certainty as well as equal treatment before the law. Article 28B Paragraph 2 also states that every child has the right to survival, growth and development as well as the right to protection from violence and discrimination. This shows that girls should be protected by the state, especially from violence because their rights are already regulated in the constitution.

Therefore, it is necessary to make efforts to prevent and eradicate all forms of discrimination and violence against women and children, especially for regional areas. The Region is one of the most authorized parties in carrying out women's empowerment and child protection by conducting and formulating regulations to provide guarantees for women's empowerment and child protection.

One form of such regulation is Regional Regulation. This regulation is expected to be able to encourage efforts to improve the physical and mental conditions of women and child victims of violence.

#### Article 1

#### Article 2

Letter a

What is meant by "respect for human rights" is as a basic form of human rights to be free from fear, threats, and violence in accordance with related policies such as the ratification of CEDAW and the Law on Protection of Human Rights as described above becomes the basis for drafting.

Letter b

What is meant by "the principle of gender equality" is that the implementation of gender equality must realize the fulfillment of human rights, living needs, and different aspirations between women and men, girls and boys, the elderly, disability groups, as well as vulnerable and marginalized groups.

Letter c

What is meant by "the principle of non-discrimination" is not distinguishing ethnic group, race, religion, gender, language, political beliefs, national origin, economic status, physical or psychological condition of the child, or other factors.

Letter d

What is meant by "the principle of victim protection" is prioritizing the safety of victims from all forms of violence.

Letter e

What is meant by "the principle of the best interests of the child" is that in all actions concerning children carried out by the government, regional government, community, legislative body, and judiciary, the best interests of the child must be based as the primary consideration.

Letter f

What is meant by "the principle of child survival and development" is the most fundamental human right for children protected by the state, government, regional government, community, family, and parents.

Letter g

What is meant by "the principle of child participation" is respect for children's rights to participate and express their opinions in decision-making, especially if it concerns matters that affect their lives

Letter h

What is meant by "the principle of benefit" is that the implementation of women's empowerment and child protection must provide equal benefits for women and men.

#### Article 3

What is meant by "Access" is the identification of whether development policies/programs have provided fair space and opportunities for women and men.

What is meant by "Participation" is the identification of whether development policies/programs fairly involve women and men in voicing needs, constraints including in decision-making.

What is meant by "Control" is the identification of whether policies/programs provide equal opportunities for mastery to women and men to control development resources. What is meant by "Benefits" is the identification of whether policies/programs provide fair benefits for women and men.
