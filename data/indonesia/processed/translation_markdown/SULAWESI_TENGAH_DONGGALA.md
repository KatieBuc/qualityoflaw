#### Article 2

The objectives of protection of women and children are to:

- a. provide and increase a sense of security for women and children: b. help prevent acts of violence against women and children; c. help prevent exploitation activities against women and children d. provide services to women and children in facing problems.

b. Duties and authorities

f. Supervision;

g. Community participation.

# CHAPTER III

FULFILLMENT OF THE RIGHTS OF CHILDREN AND WOMEN

## Part One

Rights of Children

#### Article 5

In the implementation of child protection in the region, children have the right: a. to live, grow and develop and participate in a reasonable manner in accordance with human dignity and dignity and receive guidance and protection from violence, negligence and discrimination;

b. to a valid birth certificate obtained and/or facilitated by their parents in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations; c. to worship according to their religion, think and express themselves in accordance with their level of intelligence and age under the guidance of parents;

d. to know, be raised and cared for by their own parents; e. in the event that for some reason parents cannot guarantee the growth and development of the child or the child is in a neglected state, the child has the right to be cared for or adopted as a foster child or adopted child by others in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations; f. to obtain health services and social security in accordance with their physical, mental, spiritual and social needs; g. to obtain education and teaching according to their abilities, interests and talents: h. to express opinions and have their opinions heard; a. to rest and utilize time to develop from and their abilities: j. to receive protection from family, community and Regional Government against discriminatory treatment and exploitation whether economically or socially, neglect, cruelty, violence, mistreatment, injustice and other improper treatment;

k. to regain formal or non-formal education for children who drop out of school:

- l. to receive exclusive breast milk (Mother's Milk) as a priority; m. to receive guidance
- n. to obtain other rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

I. GENERAL

The state through the government and regional governments currently places very great attention on the existence of children. Based on their existence as the nation's next generation who are qualified and dignified, the government and regional governments have the obligation to recognize the existence of children's rights in their humanity. The inherent human rights of children include basic rights as human beings, namely the Right to Life, the Right to Growth and Development, the Right to Protection and the Right to Participation. To fulfill the Right to life, children need food in sufficient quantities, healthy and nutritious, as well as optimal access to health. In supporting the realization of children to grow and develop, children need various adequate educational facilities and infrastructure, and development of talents and interests. In order to realize the right to protection and the right to participation, children need serious attention from various parties, both the government, regional governments and the community.

Specifically regarding efforts to protect against violence that threatens the safety and health of children and women must be provided from an early age. Protection is needed to prevent violence and physical, mental and sexual exploitation. For example, women and children victims of abuse, neglect, expulsion and mistreatment by parents or other people. For women and children experiencing these problems, the government must provide Safe Houses and adequate budgets, the role of various parties to help address the violence faced by women and children including social community organizations, volunteers, social workers, medical personnel, advocates and religious leaders.

The condition of children's adversity along with their rights and the condition of violence against women and children that often occurs in the region should be a common concern for all stakeholders including regional governments to play a role in preventing and overcoming such adversity and violence.

For the regional government of Donggala Regency in facing the universe of conditions above, various actions are taken to fulfill children's rights and prevent and assist in handling acts of violence against women and children which are endeavored by establishing a Regional Regulation on Protection for Women and Children.
