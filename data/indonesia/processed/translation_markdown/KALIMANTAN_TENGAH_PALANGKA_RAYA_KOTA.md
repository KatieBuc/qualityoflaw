NUMBER 1 OF 2021

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

a. that every citizen has the right to survival, growth, and proper development and to obtain consistent and systematic security as a form of Protection for Women and Children as mandated in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
b. that Regional Government, family, groups, social organizations and/or other community organizations have responsibility for the implementation of fulfillment, protection, respect, promotion and prevention of rights in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;
c. that to provide direction, legal basis and legal certainty in the Protection of Women and Children, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children;

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:
1. Region is Palangka Raya City.
2. Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
3. Mayor is the Mayor of Palangka Raya.
4. Regional Apparatus hereinafter abbreviated as PD is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of government affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
6. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
7. Violence is any act that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion, or deprivation of liberty by law.
8. Woman is a person who is female.
9. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years who has not married, including a child who is still in the womb.
10. Community is an individual, community group, including parents and family.
11. Prevention is all efforts to eliminate all risks and adverse impacts of violence against women and children.
12. Child-Friendly City hereinafter abbreviated as KLA is a conducive community in developing all social psychological potential of a person optimally.
13. Child's obligation is everything that must be carried out by the child according to the function and role of the child.
14. Forum for Handling Victims of Violence Against Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as FPK2PA is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is tiered at the city and sub-district levels.
15. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A is an integrated service center in efforts to empower women in various fields of development, as well as protection of women and children from various types of discrimination, and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons established by the regional government or community-based.
16. Child-Friendly Village hereinafter abbreviated as KRA is the development of a village area that unites the commitment and resources of the village government involving the community and the business world located in the village in order to promote, protect, fulfill and respect children's rights, which are planned consciously and sustainably.

#### Article 2

Protection of Women and Children shall be conducted based on the principles of:
a. respect, fulfillment, protection, advancement, and enforcement of rights; and
b. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence shall be implemented based on the principles of:
a. responsive to rights;
b. non-discrimination;
c. maintaining confidentiality;
d. empowerment; and
e. participation.

#### Article 4

The objectives of Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence include:
a. ensuring the fulfillment of rights;
b. providing justice and equality of rights;
c. providing protection and security; and
d. conducting empowerment.

#### Article 5

The scope of Protection of Women and Children Victims of violence includes:
a. protection of Women;
b. protection of Children;
c. coordination and cooperation;
d. responsibility of Regional Government;
e. community participation;
f. guidance and supervision;
g. evaluation;
h. reporting; and
i. funding.

# CHAPTER II

## PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 6

Protection of Women includes:
a. services;
b. empowerment;
c. prevention; and
d. monitoring.

## Part Two

Services

#### Article 7

1. Forms of services for Protection of Women as referred to in Article 6 letter a include:
a. legal aid;
b. health services;
c. social rehabilitation services;
d. complaint, consultation and counseling services;
e. social reintegration services; and
f. pre-marital guidance services.
2. Pre-marital guidance services as referred to in paragraph (1) letter f, shall be implemented by every woman who will enter into marriage.
3. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be conducted to provide understanding and knowledge regarding family resilience, health, psychology, religion, social and culture.
4. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be implemented by the Regional Apparatus that administers affairs in the field of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
5. In implementing guidance as referred to in paragraph (4) the Regional Apparatus may involve:
a. relevant agencies;
b. religious leaders; and
c. customary leaders.
6. Further provisions regarding minimum service standards for protection of Women shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Three

Empowerment

#### Article 8

1. Forms of empowerment for Protection of Women as referred to in Article 6 letter b include:
a. job training; and
b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups.
2. Implementation of empowerment for Protection of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by the Regional Apparatus in accordance with its main duties and functions.
3. Further provisions regarding procedures for empowerment of Protection of Women shall be regulated in Mayor Regulation.

## Part Four

Prevention

#### Article 9

1. Prevention of violations against the rights of Women as referred to in Article 6 letter c shall be implemented by:
a. forming work networks;
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, private sector, non-governmental organizations; and
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to Protection of Women.
d. prevention of child marriage at child age.
2. Further provisions regarding prevention of violations against the rights of Women shall be regulated in Mayor Regulation.

## Part Five

Monitoring

#### Article 10

1. Monitoring shall be implemented in the form of observation, identification, and recording of the implementation of Protection of Women.
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted by the coordination team for Protection of Women and Children through coordination forum activities.
3. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) shall produce data and information about the implementation of fulfillment of Women's rights.
4. The results of monitoring as referred to in paragraph (3) shall constitute material for the Mayor to conduct evaluation.

##### CHAPTER III

##### PROTECTION OF CHILDREN

## Part One

General

#### Article 11

Protection of Children includes:
a. services;
b. fulfillment of Children's rights;
c. prevention; and
d. monitoring.

I 11 - 9.

## Part Two

Services

#### Article 12

1. Forms of services for Protection of Children as referred to in Article 11 letter a include:
a. legal aid;
b. social rehabilitation services; and
c. social reintegration services.
2. Further provisions regarding minimum service standards for services of Protection of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Mayor Regulation

## Part Three

Fulfillment of Children's Rights

#### Article 13

1. Fulfillment of Children's Rights as referred to in Article 11 letter b includes:
a. guarantee of civil rights and freedoms;
b. guarantee of family environment and alternative care;
c. guarantee of basic health and welfare;
d. education, leisure time, culture and recreation; and.
e. special protection.
2. Special protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e, shall be conducted for:
a. children in emergency situations;
b. children in conflict with the law;
c. children from minority and isolated groups;
d. children who are exploited economically and/or sexually;
e. children who become victims of narcotics, alcohol, psychotropic and other addictive substance abuse;
f. children who become victims of pornography;
g. children with HIV/AIDS;
h. children victims of kidnapping, sale and/or trafficking;
i. children victims of physical and/or psychological violence;
j. children victims of sexual crimes;
k. children victims of terrorism networks;
l. children with disabilities;
m. children victims of abuse and neglect;
n. children with deviant social behavior; and
o. children who become victims of stigmatization from labeling related to their parents' condition.
3. Fulfillment of Children's Rights as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in order to realize a Child-Friendly City.
4. Fulfillment of Children's Rights as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

## Part Four

Prevention

#### Article 14

1. Prevention of violations against the rights of Children shall be implemented by the Regional Government and the community.
2. Prevention of violations against the rights of Children implemented by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by:
a. forming work networks;
b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, private sector, non-governmental organizations; and
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to Protection of Children.
3. Prevention of violations against the rights of Children implemented by the community as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by:
a. increasing the productivity of Children;
b. developing the potential of Children;
c. preventing child marriage;
d. reducing maternal and infant mortality rates; and
e. preventing the occurrence of domestic violence.
4. Formation of work networks as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a, includes work partners of the Regional Government in the implementation of prevention of rights violations in the form of family partners.
5. Further provisions regarding prevention of violations against the rights of Children as referred to in paragraph (3), shall be regulated in Mayor Regulation.

## Part Five

Monitoring Article 15 The provisions regarding monitoring as referred to in Article 10 shall apply mutatis mutandis to the implementation of monitoring of the fulfillment of Children's rights and special protection of Children.

### CHAPTER IV

### COORDINATION AND COOPERATION

#### Article 16

1. The implementation of coordination and cooperation for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence aims to:  
a. enhance efforts to fulfill the rights of Women and Children; 
b. improve synergistic and harmonious working relationships; and  
c. obtain data and information in the implementation of Protection of Women and Children.  
2. In order to achieve the objectives as referred to in paragraph (J) the Regional Government cooperates with:  
a. other Regional Governments;  
b. related agencies;  
c. non-governmental institutions;  
d. universities; and
e. non-governmental organizations.  
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) includes:  
a. exchange of data and information;  
b. socialization or legal counseling lectures;  
c. rehabilitation of victims of violence; and  
d. repatriation and social reintegration.

##### CHAPTER V

##### RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

## Part One

General

#### Article 17

1.  The Regional Government is responsible for the protection of the rights of Women and Children.  
2.  The responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. education;  
b. health;  
c. politics;  
d. law;  
e. economy;  
f. culture; and  
g. social.  
3. The responsibilities of the Regional Government for the protection of the rights of Women as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by:  
a. formulation of gender-responsive Regional policies;  
b. institutionalization of gender mainstreaming within the Regional Government;  
c. empowerment of Women in the fields of politics, law, economy, culture and social affairs in city-level community organizations;  
d. strengthening and development of service provider institutions for Women's empowerment at the city level;  
e. implementing efforts to prevent violence against Women.  
4. The responsibilities of the Regional Government for the protection of the rights of Children as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by:  
a. formulation of policies, programs, and Regional development activities that are child rights-oriented which are set forth in Regional development planning documents;  
b. formulation of norms, standard procedures and criteria for the assessment of the fulfillment and protection of children's rights which refer to the norms, standards, procedures and criteria established by the government;  
c. implementation of child-friendly cities carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;  
d. facilitation of the implementation of Regional Children's forums;  
e. enhancement of community participation in the implementation and protection of children's rights;  
f. monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of programs for the fulfillment and Protection of Children;  
g. guidance of Child Protection institutions;  
h. providing special protection for Children; and  
i. implementing efforts to prevent violence against Children.

#### Article 18

1. The Regional Government in carrying out the responsibility of institutionalizing Gender Mainstreaming as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (3) letter b for the Protection of Women and Children must organize an information system for Women and Children data.  
2. The Women's data information system as referred to in paragraph (1) is an inseparable part of the Children's data information system.  
3. The Women and Children information system as referred to in paragraph (2) is further regulated by Mayor Regulation.  
4. Gender mainstreaming as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 19

1. In order to ensure the effectiveness of the implementation of the Protection of Women and Children, the Regional Government must conduct cross-sectoral coordination with related institutions.  
2. In the implementation of coordination for the Protection of Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (1) the Mayor establishes a team for the Protection of Women and Children.
3. In the coordination forum activities as referred to in paragraph (2) the coordination team for the Protection of Women and Children has the duties of:  
a. preparing the necessary materials related to the implementation of the Protection of Women and Children; and
b. conveying the results, obstacles, and solutions undertaken to overcome obstacles in the implementation of the Protection of Women and Children.  
4. The coordination forum as referred to in paragraph (3) is conducted periodically, at least 2 (two) times in 1 (one) year.

## Part Two

Prevention of Violence Against Women and Children

#### Article 20

1. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to implement efforts to prevent violence against Women and Children as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (3) letter e and paragraph (4) letter i carried out in the form of:  
a. collecting data and organizing an information system for the Protection of Women and Children victims of violence;  
b. conducting education on anti-violence values against Women and Children; and  
c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the empowerment of Women and the Protection of Children victims of violence.  
2. The Regional Government has the obligation and responsibility to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:  
a. establishing and organizing technical service institutions for victims by involving community elements; and  
b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims of violence.

## Part Three

Institutional

#### Article 21

1. The technical service institution as referred to in Article 20 paragraph (2) letter a is implemented by P2TP2A and FPK2PA.  
2. In the implementation of the Protection of Women and Children it is implemented by the Regional Technical Implementation Unit.  
3. In the case of the implementation of technical services as referred to in paragraph (1) the Regional Government may establish a task force.  
4.  The task force as referred to in paragraph (3) may also be institutionally strengthened through cooperation and coordination with:  
a. KLA task force;  
b. KRA task force;  
c. schools and/or educational institutions;  
d. Children's forum;  
e. children's centers;  
f. community organizations;  
g. Women's organizations; and  
h. other institutions concerned with the fulfillment of rights and the Protection of Children.  
5. The task force as referred to in paragraph (4) is a coordinative institution at the city, sub-district and village levels that coordinates policies, programs and activities to realize sub-districts, child-friendly villages.  
6. Further provisions regarding technical services as referred to in paragraph (3) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

### Paragraph 1

#### P2TP2A

#### Article 22 

1. The implementation of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.  
2. P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in the form of functional relationship patterns among components directed to achieve common goals.

#### Article 23

1. P2TP2A provides services and temporary protection in the form of a safe house.  
2. In the event that P2TP2A does not yet have a safe house, then victims of violence are referred to other integrated service centers that have safe houses.

#### Article 24

1. P2TP2A consists of several counseling fields.  
2. The counseling fields as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of:  
a. law;  
b. health;  
c. spiritual; and
d. psychology.  
3. The implementation of counseling is carried out by counselors and may be implemented by involving related parties.

#### Article 25

The forms of services to victims organized by P2TP2A include:   
a. medical services;  
b. medicolegal services;  
c. psychosocial services;  
d. legal services;  
e. social reintegration services; and  
f. economic independence services.

#### Article 26

Further provisions regarding P2TP2A as referred to in Article 22 paragraph (1) are regulated in the Mayor Regulation.

### Paragraph 2

#### FPK2PA

#### Article 27

1. In order to protect and guarantee the rights of Women and Children from acts of violence, FPK2PA is established.  
2. FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of:  
a. Regional FPK2PA;  
b. sub-district FPK2PA; and  
c. village FPK2PA.  
3. The establishment of Regional FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a is determined by the Mayor.  
4. The establishment of sub-district FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b is determined by the sub-district head.  
5. The establishment of Village FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is determined by the village head.
6. Further provisions regarding FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated in a Mayoral Regulation.

# CHAPTER VI

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

## Part One

Obligations and Responsibilities of Parents and/or Family

#### Article 28

Parents are obliged and responsible to:  
a. care for, nurture, educate, manage and protect family members;  
b. develop family members in accordance with their abilities, talents, and interests; and  c. prevent marriage at the age of Children.

#### Article 29

Increasing the role of families in the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence is carried out through:  
a. prevention of marriage at the age of Children;  
b. bringing Women closer to health services; and  
c. strengthening family resilience.

## Part Two

Community Involvement

#### Article 30

1. The community is involved in the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence.  
2. Community involvement as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of participation through prevention, services, empowerment, monitoring, supervision and fulfillment of the rights of Women and/or Children.
3.  The role of the community in the implementation of Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by:  
a. providing communication and information through socialization and education regarding the rights of Women and Children;  
b. providing suggestions and input in the formulation of policies related to the Protection of Women and Children;  
c. reporting to the competent authorities if violations of the rights of Women and Children occur;  
d. playing an active role in the rehabilitation and social reintegration process for Women and Children;  
e. forming family partners at the neighborhood association/community association or village level by the community;  
f. forming Women and Child Protection units within community organizations and educational institutions;  
g. establishment and management of orphanages;  
h. establishment of Child rehabilitation centers;  
i. provision of shelters and/or social homes;  
j. job skills training; and  
k. providing first aid when violations of the rights of Women and Children occur.
4. Further provisions regarding community participation are regulated by Mayoral Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 31

1. The Mayor is obliged to conduct guidance and supervision of the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence.  
2. The implementation of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Agency that administers affairs in the field of empowerment of Women and Protection of Child Victims of Violence.  
3.  Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. coordination;  
b. guidance;  
c. education and training;  
d. monitoring and evaluation; and  
e. reporting.  
4. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) to realize the objectives of Protection of Women and Children in accordance with standard operating procedures and criteria implemented by the Regional Agency that administers affairs in the field of empowerment of Women and Child Protection.  
5. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out with professional and transparent principles.  
6. In carrying out guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Agency that administers affairs in the field of empowerment of Women and Child Protection is obliged to report regularly to the Mayor.  
7. In the case of guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (2), the Regional Agency that administers affairs in the field of empowerment of Women and Child Protection may cooperate and coordinate with other Regional Agencies, the community, other agencies and/or organizational institutions.

# CHAPTER VIII

## EVALUATION

#### Article 32

1. Evaluation of the Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence is carried out based on documents of service implementation, empowerment, prevention, fulfillment and monitoring results of the implementation of Protection of Women and Children.  
2. The implementation of evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Agency that administers affairs in the field of empowerment of Women and Child Protection.  
3. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) includes process, impact and results evaluation.  
4. The results of evaluation as referred to in paragraph (3) constitute material for the Mayor to prepare reports.  
5. Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (4) is carried out at least 1 (one) time in 1 (one) year.  
6. Further provisions regarding evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayoral Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

## REPORTING

#### Article 33

1. Reporting as referred to in Article 32 paragraph (5) serves as consideration material for the Mayor in making policy decisions on the implementation of Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence.  
2. Reports on the implementation of Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out 1 (one) time in 1 (one) year or at any time if necessary.

##### CHAPTER X

##### FUNDING

#### Article 34

Funding for the implementation of Protection of Women and Child Victims of Violence is sourced from:  
a. regional revenue and expenditure budget; and  
b. other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.
