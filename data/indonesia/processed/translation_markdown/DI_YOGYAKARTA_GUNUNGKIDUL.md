# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

## Part One

Definitions

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms shall mean:  

1. Region is Gunungkidul Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of regional government administration.  
3. Regent is the Regent of Gunungkidul.  
4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution as an element of regional government administration.  
5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including a child still in the womb.  
6. Woman is a person of female gender.  
7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering, whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.  
8. Violence against Women is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.  
9. Violence against Children is any act that results in or may result in suffering of children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.  
10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring in the Special Region of Yogyakarta.  
11. Protection of Women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, court, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will or has occurred.  
12. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and receive protection from violence and discrimination.  
13. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against women and children will, is or has occurred.  
14. Behavior Change Counseling is psychological counseling provided to male perpetrators of domestic violence to help stop violence and become a person who can respect their partner, so as to establish a better relationship with their partner and children.  
15. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter abbreviated as PPT, is a functional work unit that provides integrated services for victims of violence.  
16. Networked Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter referred to as Networked P2TP2A, is an Integrated Service Center in Gunungkidul Regency that provides networked integrated services for women and children victims of violence, established by Decree of the Regent of Gunungkidul.  
17. Forum for Handling Victims of Violence against Women and Children, abbreviated as FPK2PA, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is networked and established at the regency and sub-district levels.  
18. Safe House is a shelter for victims during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and danger from perpetrators.  
19. Standard Operating Procedure, hereinafter abbreviated as SOP, is standard steps that must be taken in protecting victims starting from complaints/identification, health rehabilitation, social rehabilitation, legal services, to repatriation and social reintegration of witnesses and/or victims.  
20. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.  
21. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care, and representation, and/or domestic workers and reside in that household.  
22. Gender Mainstreaming in the Region is a strategy built to integrate gender into an integral dimension of planning, formulation, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of policies, programs, and development activities in the region.  
23. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles, functions, and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural circumstances of society.  
24. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings so that they are able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural activities, national defense and security, and equality in enjoying the results of development.  
25. Gender Justice is a process to be fair to men and women.  
26. Disaggregated data is data disaggregated by sex and status and condition of women and men in all fields of development including health, education, economy and employment, politics and decision-making, law and socio-culture and violence.  
27. Gender Responsive Planning and Budgeting is a series of approaches to integrate gender perspectives in the planning and budgeting process, gender responsive planning is planning to achieve gender equality and justice, carried out through integrating the experiences, aspirations, needs, potential, and problem solving of women and men.  
28. Women and Children Supervision and Service Institution in Villages or Sub-Districts or by Other Names is an institution established by the village community that functions to serve victims of violence with members consisting of local community leaders.

## Part Two

Principles, Objectives, and Scope

#### Article 2

The principles of Victim protection are:  
a. respect and fulfillment of victims' rights;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination;  
d. best interests of victims; and  
e. empowerment.

#### Article 3

The purpose of protection for women and children victims of violence is to provide a reference for protection and services for the best interests of women and children victims of violence in the domestic sphere and/or public sphere.

#### Article 4

The scope of protection for Victims includes prevention efforts, services to victims of violence, rehabilitation, and empowerment of victims of violence in the Region.

# CHAPTER II

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 5

Forms of violence include:  
a. physical violence;  
b. psychological violence;  
c. sexual violence;  
d. neglect;  
e. exploitation; and/orf. other violence.

#### Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or causing death.

#### Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

#### Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:  
a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;  
b. forced sexual intercourse;  
c. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or  
d. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes.

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:  
a. acts that result in the failure to fulfill the needs of children properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after children as they should be, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
c. acts that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or  
d. acts that result in economic dependency by limiting and/or prohibiting proper work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in Article 5 letter (e) is caused by:  
a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;  
b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits whether material or immaterial; and/or  
c. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or sexual abuse

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:  
a. threats of violence include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and  
b. coercion, includes a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER III

## INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER

#### Article 12

1. PPT is established by the Regional Government, Vertical Agencies in the Region, or community institutions.  
2. The Regional Government establishes a Networked P2TP2A as an integrated service center by Regent's Decree. (3) The Networked P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (2) is a functional work unit that has the duty and function of providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.  
4. In carrying out the duties as referred to in paragraph (3), the Networked P2TP2A may provide services and temporary protection in the form of a safe house.  
5. In the event that the Networked P2TP2A does not have a safe house (shelter) as referred to in paragraph (4), then victims of violence are referred to a PPT that has a safe house.

#### Article 13

1. The organizational structure of the Networked P2TP2A as referred to in Article 12 paragraph (2) consists of:  
a. General Chairperson;  
b. Executive Chairperson who oversees divisions;  
c. Secretary; and  
d. Treasurer.  
2. The divisions as referred to in paragraph (1) letter d, at a minimum include:  
a. complaints service division;  
b. health service division;  
c. social rehabilitation service division;  
d. repatriation and social reintegration division; and  
e. legal aid service division.

#### Article 14

The General Chairperson as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1) letter a has the duties of:  
a. coordinating the formulation of policies, strategies, programs, and activities as well as necessary measures in the implementation of the Networked P2TP2A;  
b. conducting supervision and guidance on the implementation of protection and handling of victims of violence;  
c. implementing policies established by the Regent; and  
d. being responsible for the entire process of implementing protection and handling of violence against victims of violence.

#### Article 15

The executive chairperson as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1) letter b has the duties of:  
a. coordinating the duties and functions of each service division;  
b. controlling the implementation of protection and handling programs for victims of violence;  
c. conducting coordination and cooperation with related external parties;  
d. collecting and utilizing resources effectively and efficiently for protection and handling activities for victims of violence; and  
e. providing periodic reports to the Regent, specifically for victims of trafficking in persons crimes, a copy of the report is also given to the task force for prevention and handling of trafficking in persons crimes.

#### Article 16

The Secretary as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1) letter c has the duties of:  
a. assisting in the implementation of the duties of the General Chairperson;  
b. assisting in preparing coordination activities and follow-up on protection, handling of victims of violence;  
c. assisting in preparing work program plans for the central task force;  
d. providing administrative services in the cooperation of the central Task Force with Ministries/Agencies and Community Institutions that are members of the Central Task Force;  
e. organizing service activities for the collection, processing, and presentation of data as well as preparation of secretariat activity reports; and  
f. fostering and implementing working cooperation with related Ministries/Agencies and Community Institutions in the implementation of prevention and handling of trafficking in persons crimes.

#### Article 17

The Treasurer as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (1) letter d has the duties of:  
a. assisting the general chairperson in managing the finances of the Networked P2TP2A;  
b. doing everything related to financial receipts and expenditures; and  
c. preparing financial reports submitted to the Executive Chairperson and General Chairperson.

#### Article 18

The complaints service division as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter a has the duties of:  
a. conducting interviews and observation of the victim's condition;  
b. making recommendations for follow-up services;  
c. conducting coordination and referrals to follow-up services and related parties; and  
d. conducting administration of the complaint process.

#### Article 19

The health service division as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter b has the duties of:  
a. conducting examination, treatment, and follow-up care for victims;  
b. conducting coordination of health rehabilitation implementation and medico-legal;  
c. conducting medico-legal examination including collection of evidence from victims and preparation of visum et repertum;  
d. conducting supporting examinations and laboratory tests on evidence;  
e. conducting consultation with specialist doctors or making referrals; and  
f. preparing case reports.

#### Article 20

The social rehabilitation service division as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter c has the duties of:  
a. providing assistance during the case handling process; and  
b. conducting counseling for victims and/or perpetrators of violence.

#### Article 21

The repatriation and social reintegration division as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter d has the duties of:  
a. conducting coordination with related agencies for victim repatriation;  
b. preparing progress reports on the assistance process for repatriation and social rehabilitation; and  
c. conducting monitoring for at least three months after the victim is returned to their family.

#### Article 22

The legal aid service division as referred to in Article 13 paragraph (2) letter e has the duties of:  
a. accompanying every legal service process; and  
b. preparing progress reports on legal handling.

#### Article 23

The organizational structure of PPT formed by Vertical Agencies in the Regions and community institutions, shall be formed in accordance with the needs of each agency.

#### Article 24

1. Networked P2TP2A shall have at least three counselors.
2. Counselors as referred to in paragraph (1) include:
a. counselor in the field of law;
b. counselor in the field of health; and
c. counselor in the field of psychology.
2. The implementation of counseling conducted by Networked P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) includes providing behavioral change counseling.

#### Article 25

1. In addition to forming Networked P2TP2A, the Regional Government shall form a Forum for Handling Victims of Violence against Women and Children (FPK2PA).
2. FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of district FPK2PA and sub-district FPK2PA.
3. The Organizational Structure of FPK2PA consists of:
a. Patron;
b. Advisor;
c. Supervisor;
d. Chairperson;
e. Vice Chairperson;
f. Secretary;
g. Field Coordinator;
h. Members; and
i. Secretariat Staff.
4. The establishment of District FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be stipulated by Regent's Decree.
5. The establishment of Sub-district FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be stipulated by Sub-district Head's Decree.
6. The Duties and Functions of FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) are:
a. coordinating and synchronizing the handling of PPT services;
b. maintaining and developing networks and referral systems; and
c. collecting, compiling, and presenting reports on violence.
7. FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (2) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.
8. Members of FPK2PA as referred to in paragraph (3) letter h consist of Regional Work Units that have duties and functions in the fields of social affairs, handling of women and children, health, education, youth and sports, religion, population, manpower, and/or industry and trade, as well as Vertical Agencies and community institutions grouped in the following fields:
a. health field;
b. psychology field;
c. law field;
d. social field; and
e. economic field.

# CHAPTER IV

## EFFORTS TO PREVENT VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

Efforts to Prevent Violence against Women

#### Article 26

1. Every person is obliged to prevent the occurrence of violence against women.
2. The Regional Government in efforts to prevent violence against women shall implement gender mainstreaming to realize gender equality and justice in the fields of education, economy, health, law, politics, religion, and socio-culture.
3. Gender mainstreaming as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be conducted based on disaggregated data as eye-opening data in analyzing gender-responsive budgeting planning.
4. Further provisions regarding Gender Mainstreaming and Gender-Responsive Budgeting Planning shall be regulated in a Regent's Regulation.

## Part Two

Efforts to Prevent Violence Against Children

#### Article 27

1. The Regional Government and the community shall conduct prevention of violence against children.
2. Prevention of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes the fulfillment of children's basic rights in accordance with their needs.
3. Further regulation regarding the fulfillment of children's basic rights shall be regulated in a Regent's Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

## EFFORTS TO PROVIDE SERVICES TO WOMEN

## AND CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Service Principles

#### Article 28

Principles of service delivery to victims:
a. fast;
b. safe and comfortable;
c. sense of empathy;
d. non-discrimination;
e. easily accessible;
f. not charged; and
g. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 29

In the implementation of integrated services, every victim shall receive the following rights:
a. the right to have their dignity respected as a human being;
b. the right to health and psychological recovery from the suffering experienced by the victim;
c. the right to determine their own decisions;
d. the right to obtain information;
e. the right to confidentiality of identity;
f. the right to social rehabilitation;
g. the right to complaint handling;
h. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or
i. the right to assistance.

## Part Two

Efforts to Provide Services to Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 30

1. The Regional Government shall provide services to women victims of violence.
2. Services conducted by the Regional Government as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted by Networked P2TP2A, in accordance with the type of service required.

## Part Three

Efforts to Provide Services to Child Victims of Violence

#### Article 31

1. The Regional Government shall provide services to child victims of violence.
2. Services as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented by Networked P2TP2A, in accordance with the type of service required.

# CHAPTER VII

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

#### Article 34

Implementation of Protection shall be conducted in an integrated and integrated manner within the framework of Networked P2TP2A and FPK2PA as referred to in Article 25.

# CHAPTER VIII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 35

1. Every person shall participate in providing protection to women and child victims of violence.
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:
a. forming women and child protection institutions in villages whose duties are to conduct prevention, services, and supervision;
b. conducting socialization of women's and children's rights;
c. providing assistance to victims;
d. reporting and referring to authorized agencies/institutions in the Sub-district if violence against victims occurs in their environment; and
e. Authorized Agencies/Institutions in the Sub-district, if necessary, may refer to other Agencies/institutions.

CONCERNING PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

##### I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights and therefore their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Gunungkidul Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades their degree and human dignity, it is necessary to provide protection to women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

To date, laws and regulations governing the protection of women and children victims of violence have not yet regulated protection measures at the Regional level, therefore institutional and regulatory support is needed to ensure its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection measures for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of women and children victims of violence in Gunungkidul Regency.

#### Article 2

Point a  
What is meant by "respect and fulfillment of victims' rights" is the guarantee of fulfillment of victims' basic rights.

Point b  
What is meant by "justice" is a condition where every person, both men and women, are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to gain access and welfare;

What is meant by "gender equality" is equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors.

Point c  
What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment toward victims without making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Point d  
What is meant by "the best interests of the victim" is all best actions concerning victims carried out by the Government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, therefore the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.

Point e  
What is meant by "empowerment" is the strengthening of violence victims to be able to work and earn a living independently after they have been restored and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
