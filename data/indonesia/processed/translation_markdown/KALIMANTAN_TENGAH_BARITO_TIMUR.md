# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

(1) The implementation of protection of women and children is carried out based on the principles of:

- a. respect for the rights of women and children;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination in the empowerment of women and children;
- d. the best interests of women and children; and
- e. restoration of social and economic rights for women and children who experience acts of violence or neglect.
  - (2) The objectives of implementing protection of women and children include:
- a. preventing acts of violence or neglect against women and children including trafficking in persons;
- b. eliminating all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;
- c. protecting and providing security for women and children; d. providing services to women and children victims of acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses; facilitating and mediating e. domestic disputes to realize harmonious and prosperous household integrity; and seeking compensation/compensation f. for losses suffered by victims from perpetrators of violence.

# CHAPTER III

SCOPE

Article 3

Protection of women and children in the region includes prevention, services, and empowerment of victims of physical violence, psychological violence, sexual violence, exploitation, trafficking in persons, and domestic neglect including child deviant behavior.

(2)

The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence is a joint obligation and responsibility between the Regional Government, community, family, and parents.

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN DAII ANAIS

The role of women is very important because they are the mothers of the nation who are the pillars of the family to build the future of human resources in various fields including family economic empowerment, as well as children who are the nation's generation who will play a role in filling development in the future.

Children and Women must receive protection from violence, exploitation, human trafficking and neglect by responsible parties. Protection is intended to create a peaceful and prosperous atmosphere for all levels of society in fostering life and togetherness within the regional area.

Violence cannot be avoided just like that, but must be prevented as early as possible from spreading to a level that is detrimental to all parties. Protection efforts are prioritized on prevention efforts and minimizing incidents.

Acts of violence against Children and Women constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination. So far, the laws and regulations governing the protection of Children and Women victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region so that institutional and regulatory support is needed to ensure their implementation. Regional Regulations regulate protection efforts for victims, especially in terms of prevention, services and empowerment of Children and Women victims of violence in Kotabaru Regency. As one of the protection efforts for Children and Women, it is carried out through guidance, training and assistance to victims and families of violence victims. Economic empowerment is also carried out to prevent women as victims, which can be done by growing and developing micro and small businesses in accordance with local potential. To develop micro and small businesses, it is necessary to increase the capacity and capability of women through assistance and guidance so that women can develop their businesses.

What is meant by "sexual violence" is any action in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual relations, forced unnatural or unwanted sexual relations, forcing sexual relations with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

What is meant by "exploitation" includes: actions that exploit children economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others; actions with or without the consent of the victim which b. include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or services, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully removing or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or utilizing the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain benefits both material and immaterial.

c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.

What is meant by "trafficking in persons" is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud, abuse of power or position of vulnerability, debt bondage or giving payments or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person who has control over that other person, whether conducted within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.
