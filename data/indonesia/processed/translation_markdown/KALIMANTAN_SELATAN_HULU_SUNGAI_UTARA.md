BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

THE REGENT OF HULU SUNGAI UTARA,

Considering : a. that in the framework of protecting the rights of women and children as mandated by the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, improving the quality of life of women and children and combating all forms of violence against women and children, there is a need for concern from all parties both the community and relevant government institutions and law enforcement officials;

b. that there is a tendency of increasing victims and cases of violence against women and children, which have not been handled optimally so that integrated services are needed by relevant agencies and community institutions/organizations to provide protection for women and children victims of violence;

c. that in an effort to facilitate women and children to improve skills and independence and obtain consultation services and problem solving experienced by women and children, an integrated service center for the empowerment of women and children is needed;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, and letter c, it is necessary to establish

a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection

of Women and Children;

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Region is the Regency of Hulu Sungai Utara.
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of the administration of the regional government of Hulu Sungai Utara Regency.
- 3. Regent is the Regent of Hulu Sungai Utara.
- 4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is the regional people's representative institution of Hulu Sungai Utara Regency.
- 5. Child is a person who has not reached 18 years of age, including those in the womb.
- 6. Woman is a person of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
- 7. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
- 8. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.
- 9. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, or psychological to the victim.
- 10. Physical violence is any act that causes pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, unconsciousness and/or causes death.
- 11. Psychological violence is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feeling of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

12. Sexual violence is any act in the form of sexual harassment, forced sexual intercourse, whether unnatural or unwanted with another person for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.

13. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.

14. Victim of acts of violence is an individual or group of people who experience suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of violations of human rights (HAM), or criminal acts and acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or Regional Government apparatus or by individuals.

15. Service is an action that must be immediately taken to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing about, during or after the occurrence of violence against the victim.

16. Assistance is activities and actions carried out by assistants during the service process.

17. Assistant is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to assist victims in conducting counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of violence victims.

18. Repatriation is an effort to return victims of acts of violence to their families, substitute families, or communities that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.

19. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and the restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in the community.

20. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with their families, communities, institutions or other social environments that can provide protection.

21. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of the Regional Government, and/or Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) that provide assistance.

22. Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is a functional work unit located in the sub-district and regency to provide integrated services for witnesses and/or victims of acts of violence in the framework of empowerment of women and children victims of acts of violence.

23. Family Information and Consultation Center (PIK) is an institution located in the village/kelurahan that provides consultation services and social guidance to individuals, family members, communities facing disruptions to their social functions.

24. Implementing officer or functional officer is a health worker, psychologist, psychiatrist, social worker, legal aid worker who comes from civil servants within their respective offices or special personnel employed at P2TP2A and PIK Keluarga.

25. Special Service Room, hereinafter abbreviated as RPK, is a safe and comfortable room specifically designated for witnesses and/or victims of criminal acts including suspects of criminal acts consisting of women and children who should be treated or require special treatment, and whose cases are being handled at the Police office.

26. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for related offices, vertical agencies, and the community in carrying out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.

27. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with specified operational standards.

28. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.

29. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, and mother and children.

30. Household is family members and relatives (grandchildren, nieces/nephews, older siblings, younger siblings, grandfathers, grandmothers, cousins and so on) and non-relatives (housekeepers, drivers and so on), who live and eat from one kitchen and reside in one house.

31. Parent is the biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or adoptive mother.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The principles of implementing the protection of women and children are: a. respect for the rights of women and children;

b. justice and gender equality;

c. non-discrimination;

d. the best interests of women and children.

#### Article 3

(1) The objectives of implementing the protection of women and children are to: a. prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children; b. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women and children;

c. protect and provide a sense of security for women and children; d. provide services to women and children, reporters, and witnesses;

Perda Kab. HSU Tahun 2016 Nomor 3 ttg Perlindungan Perempuan dan Anak

e. empower women for the fulfillment of the rights of women and children; and

f. facilitate and mediate household disputes to realize a harmonious and prosperous household integrity.

(2) The objectives of implementing the protection of women and children as referred to in paragraph (1), include aspects of:

- a. prevention;
- b. service and assistance;
- c. reunification;
- d. empowerment.

# CHAPTER III

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 4

Every victim has the right to obtain:

- a. protection;
- b. information;
- c. optimal service;
- d. continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage; e. confidential handling;
- f. psychological and legal assistance; and g. guarantees of rights related to status as a family member and community member.

#### Article 5

Children, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 4, also obtain special rights, as follows:

- a. the right to respect and full use for the continuation of life, growth, development and participation;
- b. the right to basic services;
- c. the right to equal protection;
- d. the right to be free from various stigmas; and
- e. the right to obtain freedom.

b. the community;

# CHAPTER V

DUTIES AND AUTHORITIES OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 10

(1) The Regional Government is tasked with carrying out efforts to prevent violence against women and children, in the form of:

- a. collecting data and information on women and children victims of acts of violence, and laws and regulations;
- b. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children;
- c. conducting education on anti-violence values toward women and children; and
- d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children.
  - (2) The Regional Government in anticipating acts of violence against women and children, is authorized to provide and organize services for victims in the form of:
- a. providing and facilitating the establishment of integrated services for victims of acts of violence by involving institutions and community elements;
- b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for victims; and
- c. conducting community empowerment for the prevention of acts of violence.

## Part Two

Form and Mechanism of Services

#### Article 12

The form of prevention of acts of violence against women and children carried out by P2TP2A and Family PIK, may be implemented through:

- a. socialization activities of laws and regulations to the community related to the protection of women's and children's rights; and
- b. training of P2TP2A and Family PIK members related to the implementation of their duties in conducting prevention of acts of violence against women and children.

(1) Regency P2TP2A in carrying out its duties and functions may coordinate with District P2TP2A, and competent parties in conducting efforts to prevent acts of violence against women and children.

#### Article 14

Forms of services for victims organized by P2TP2A include:

- a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel;
- b. medicolegal services constitute a form of medical service for the purposes of evidence in the legal field;
- c. psychosocial services constitute services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have confidence, strength and independence in resolving their problems;
- d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing judicial processes;
- e. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and may provide economic access so that victims can be independent.

## Part Three

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 16

(1) To prevent acts of violence against women and children, the Regional Government conducts empowerment and awareness to families, parents, and the community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.

(2) In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regional Government undertakes the following efforts:

- a. increasing the quantity and quality of education, both formal and non-formal and informal;
- b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services;
- c. opening employment opportunities for women;
- d. building community participation and concern to implement prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence;
- e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
- f. implementing curfews for children (under 18 years) leaving the house at night no later than 11.00 pm, if beyond that time must be accompanied by parents or family;
- g. restricting children from accessing the internet at internet cafes (warnet) during school hours;
- h. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, universities and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women and children; and
- i. opening complaint posts for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

I. GENERAL

The state has the obligation to provide security to its citizens from threats and actions that can disturb and damage psychological, physical, sexual and economic security. This is philosophically stated in the preamble of the 1945 Constitution, that the purpose of the establishment of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia is to protect the entire Indonesian nation and all Indonesian territory.

Because the Government of Indonesia has signed the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR 1948) and ratified CEDAW (Law Number 7 Year 1984 concerning the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women), it is obliged to fulfill these provisions. Human Rights state that the enforcement and fulfillment of Human Rights is the responsibility of the State, especially the Government.

Through Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 Year 2014, the Government recognizes the existence of children's rights. The inherent human rights of children include basic rights as human beings, namely the Right to Life, Right to Growth and Development, Right to Protection and Right to Participation.

To fulfill the Right to Life, children need sufficient, healthy and nutritious food, as well as optimal access to health care. Protection from violence that threatens their safety and health must be provided from an early age. Protection is needed to prevent physical, mental and sexual violence and exploitation. To fulfill the right to growth and development, children need space to play, exercise, and education that is appropriate to their physical and psychological development. In fulfilling children's rights, every government administrator, society and parents are obliged to understand and care about children's rights. The existence of legal instruments and legal apparatus that defend children's interests is needed for this protection effort. Legislation that is principled on defending the best interest of the child is needed to realize protection that is legal in nature.

Furthermore, it is not uncommon to find cases of women and children experiencing problems of physical, psychological, sexual and social violence. For example, women and children who are victims of abuse, neglect, expulsion and maltreatment (abuse) by parents or other people. For women and children experiencing these problems, the government must provide a Safe House (children protection home) or Integrated Service Center (PPT) for women and children victims of acts of

Regional Regulation of HSU Regency Year 2016 Number 3 concerning Protection of Women and Children violence. A Safe House or PPT is a safe place and friendly in nature for women and children. The PPT is equipped with assistants from educators, doctors, psychologists, religious leaders/clergy and is networked with lawyers for women and children as well as RPK Units at the Police.

The fate of women and children victims of violence must be given attention by the government. The many cases of violence against women and children are often caused by factors that develop in society, such as low economic levels, education, and environments in the industrial sector. Therefore, victims of violence like this need to receive protection in accordance with the principles of justice, truth, legal certainty, equality and upholding human rights.

For the empowerment of victims of violence, the Regional Government can establish an Integrated Service Center as a service center in the context of empowering women victims of violence which specifically has the main tasks and functions for empowerment of women and children victims of violence.

From the above framework, the Regional Government is responsible for taking legal, political, economic and social actions to prevent, suppress, reduce, and eliminate all forms of violence against women and children. From these various efforts, a legal framework will be realized in the implementation of protection for women and children.

#### Article 1

Sufficiently Clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a

What is meant by "respect for the rights of victims" is a series of actions to respect, appreciate and ensure the fulfillment of the rights of victims.

Letter b

What is meant by "justice and gender equality" is a process to be fair to men and women and equal conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, national defense and security activities and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

Letter c

What is meant by "non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims by not making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter d

What is meant by "The best interests of victims" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, must be the primary consideration.

#### Article 3

Paragraph (1)

Sufficiently clear.

Paragraph (2)

Letter a

What is meant by the "prevention" aspect is strategic protection efforts through:

- a. primary prevention, for all people, families, communities and the state in efforts to increase knowledge capacity, understanding and to ensure that violence against women and children does not occur, including policy socialization, adequate services, supportive workplace policies, and life skill training for women and children. What is meant by life skill training includes conflict resolution without violence, stress management skills, resource management, making effective decisions, effective interpersonal communication, guidance on the psychosocial development of women and children.
- b. secondary prevention, aimed at high-risk community groups in efforts to increase skills, including training and victim services to ensure that violence against women and children does not occur in the next generation. Activities carried out here include conducting home visits for parents who have just had children to conduct self assessment of whether they are at risk of committing violence against their children in the future.
- c. tertiary prevention, intended to increase parenting capacity that ensures violence against women and children does not recur, what is done here is integrated services for women and children who are victims of violence, through counseling, stress management training.

Letter b

What is meant by the "service and assistance" aspect is immediate activities and actions carried out by professionals and assistants in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.

Letter c

What is meant by the "reunification" aspect is an effort to restore and rehabilitate the physical and mental condition of victims and then reunite them with the victim's family and community.

Letter d

What is meant by the "empowerment" aspect is a process of increasing the role of the community to participate in the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence which aims to obtain access and participation in the protection process for women and children broadly.

#### Article 4

Letter a

What is meant by "obtaining protection" is obtaining protection from individuals, groups and institutions both governmental and non-governmental.

Letter b

What is meant by "obtaining information" is access and information about the existence of complaint centers, P2TP2A, and all matters related to the fulfillment of their rights and involvement in decision-making processes related to assistance and case developments.

Letter c

What is meant by "optimal services" is services that include medical, extensive medicolegal, psychosocial and legal.

Letter d

What is meant by "continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage" is handling that does not stop at physical and psychological healing, but until the victim can resume life in society including the restoration of their good name.

Letter e

What is meant by "confidential handling" is an effort to guarantee certainty for victims that their identity, medical treatment and legal handling will not be disseminated.

Letter f

What is meant by "obtaining psychological assistance" is assistance provided by psychologists to victims suffering from trauma/other mental problems to restore the victim's mental condition. Meanwhile, legal assistance is assistance provided by individuals and/or legal aid institutions to victims at every level of examination and during the legal process.

Letter g

What is meant by "guarantee of rights related to victim status" is an effort to provide certainty and protection for victims as family members and members of society.
