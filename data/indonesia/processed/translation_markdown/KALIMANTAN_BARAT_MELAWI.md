# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The Implementation of Protection for Women victims of violence is based on: a. respect for and fulfillment of victims' rights;

b. justice and gender equality;

c. non-discrimination;

d. best interests of the victim; and

e. empowerment

#### Article 3

The Implementation of Protection for Women victims of violence aims to: a. prevent violence against women;

b. provide services/handling to victims; and

c. empower women victims of violence and vulnerable groups;

d. eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against women.

# CHAPTER III

SCOPE OF PROTECTION

#### Article 4

The Scope of Implementation of Protection for Women victims of violence includes:

- a. prevention;
- b. service/handling;
- c. empowerment;

Prevention

#### Article 5

(1) Efforts to prevent violence against women are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by the SKPD in charge of Women's Empowerment;

(2) Prevention Efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:

a. forming work networks;

b. coordinating, integrating, and synchronizing violence prevention based on partnership patterns;

c. establishing a violence prevention system;

d. socializing statutory regulations related to the protection of women victims of violence;

#### Article 6

In addition to prevention efforts carried out by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 5, prevention efforts are also carried out by families and/or closest relatives, communities, educational institutions and Customary Institutions.

## Part Two

Service/Handling

#### Article 7

(1) The Implementation of Service/Handling for victims is carried out in an integrated manner by PPT.

(2) PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from and/or to other service units in a networked manner.

(3) Further provisions regarding the development, mechanism, and working relationships of the integrated PPT network as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S PROTECTION FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

Acts of violence against women constitute a violation of human rights, therefore, their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women in Melawi Regency so that they are protected from violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades the dignity and honor of humanity, it is necessary to establish a regional regulation on women's protection.

Women's protection is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention.

The existing laws and regulations or those related to the protection of women victims of violence have not yet regulated the efforts to protect women in the regions, so institutional and regulatory support at the regional level is needed to ensure its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates the efforts to protect women especially in the form of prevention, service/handling, and empowerment of women in Melawi Regency.
