# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  
1. Region is the Regency of West Southeast Maluku;  
2. Regional Government is the Regent and Regional Apparatus as elements of regional government administration;  
3. Regent is the Regent of West Southeast Maluku;  
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter referred to as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of West Southeast Maluku Regency;  
5. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb;  
6. Woman is every person who is physically born as a woman;  
7. Victim is a woman and child who experiences acts of violence and/or threats of violence that include physical, sexual, psychological, economic, and/or social violence, in the family, community and/or society, in conflict situations, post-conflict and the state;  
8. Implementation is all actions that include protection, services, and recovery for victims of violence;  
9. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by the regional government, family, advocates, social institutions, community, police, prosecutors, courts or other parties;  
10. Child Protection is all activities carried out to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human rights and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination;  
11. Women's Protection is all activities carried out to guarantee and protect women and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human rights and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination;  
12. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim, when seeing, hearing and knowing of, during or after the occurrence of violence against the victim;  
13. Companion is a person or institution that has the ability to accompany victims to provide counseling, therapy, legal services and advocacy, for the purpose of strengthening victim recovery;  
14. Women and Child Protection Institution is an institution providing services to victims of violence, based in hospitals, managed jointly in the form of medical services (including medicolegal), psycho-social and legal services;  
15. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection for women and children victims in accordance with the standard operating procedures or abbreviated as SOP determined;  
16. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and or other community organizations;  
17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, and mother and her children;  
18. Household is family members and relatives such as grandchildren, nieces/nephews, older siblings, younger siblings, grandfathers, grandmothers, husbands, wives, cousins and so on and non-relatives such as domestic workers, drivers and so on who live and reside in one house.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out based on the principles of:  
a. respect for human rights;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. protection of victims' rights;  
d. non-discrimination;  
e. the best interests of the victim.

#### Article 3

The purpose of implementing protection for women and children victims of violence is to provide protection and services to women and children victims of violence based on gender and the best interests of children that occur in the household and/or public.

# CHAPTER III

## VICTIMS' RIGHTS

#### Article 4

Every victim of violence has the right to obtain:  
a. protection and assistance;  
b. legal aid;  
c. health services according to medical needs;  
d. spiritual services;  
e. psychosocial services;  
f. information;  
g. identification services;  
h. social rehabilitation;  
i. repatriation; and  
j. social reintegration.

#### Article 5

1. In the event that victims of violence as referred to in article 4 require ongoing treatment, they have the right to stay in a safe house or alternative residence;  
2. Ongoing treatment as referred to in paragraph (1) is treatment at the rehabilitation stage carried out by individuals, groups or government and non-government institutions.

#### Article 6

1. Safe house as referred to in article 5 paragraph (1) provides a temporary safe place and/or shelter for victims of violence until their rights are restored;  
2. The implementation and/or management of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by KPPKB;  
3. For the safety and at the request of victims of violence, the manager or operator of the safe house as referred to in paragraph (2) may place victims of violence in a certain place that is kept confidential;  
4. Provisions regarding the implementation of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be further regulated by Regent regulation.

# CHAPTER IV

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

#### Article 7

The implementation of protection for women and children is a joint obligation and responsibility of:  
a. Regional Government;  
b. Individuals;  
c. Family;  
d. Parents/Guardians;  
e. Community;  
f. Social Organizations; and or  
g. Other Community Organizations in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 8

The obligations and responsibilities of the regional government as referred to in article 7 letter a include:  
a. establishing and implementing policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and children victims of violence;  
b. conducting cooperation in the implementation of protection for women and children from victims of violence;  
c. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of protection for women and children from victims of violence;  
d. allocating budget for the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence in accordance with regional financial capacity; and  
e. fostering and supervising the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 9

1. The obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 7 letter e, are implemented in the form of community participation including:  
a. caring for, maintaining, educating, and protecting children;  
b. developing children according to their abilities, talents and interests;  
c. preventing marriage at child age;  
d. preventing acts of violence against women and children;  
e. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children victims of violence to law enforcement or authorized parties; and  
f. participating in handling victims of acts of violence.  
2. In the event that parents are absent, or their whereabouts are unknown, or due to a reason, cannot carry out their obligations and responsibilities, then the obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), may be transferred to the head of the family, which is carried out in accordance with the provisions of applicable Laws and Regulations;  
3. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2) are carried out responsibly in accordance with Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 10

The obligations of the family and/or parents as referred to in Article 7 letter c and letter d, who legally have full responsibility to prevent all forms of acts of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER V

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutions

#### Article 11

1. The implementation of protection for victims is carried out in an integrated manner within the framework of P2TP2A;  
2. Provisions regarding P2TP2A will be further regulated in a Regent Regulation.

## Part Two

Form and Mechanism of Services

#### Article 12

Forms of services for victims organized by P2TP2A include:  
a. medical services in the form of treatment and recovery of physical condition carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;  
b. medicolegal services which are a form of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;  
c. first psychosocial assistance to victims carried out to identify the occurrence of violence and trauma experienced by victims;  
d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process;  
e. assistance to victims of violence carried out by competent persons or institutions that have the expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and have cooperated with P2TP2A; and  
f. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.  
2. The service mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1), is organized according to Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) and is further regulated by Regent's regulation.

## Part Three

Service Principles

#### Article 13

The provision of services to victims is carried out free of charge, quickly, safely, with empathy, non-discriminatory, easily accessible and with a guarantee of confidentiality.

#### Article 14

P2TP2A management is obliged to provide services in accordance with the service principles as referred to in Article 12.

# CHAPTER VI

## ASSISTANCE

#### Article 15

Assistance is carried out by persons or institutions that have the expertise to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence and have cooperated with P2TP2A.

# CHAPTER VII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 16

1. The community participates in the protection of children and women victims of violence individually or in groups.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, child protection institutions, social welfare institutions, community organizations, educational institutions, mass media, and the business sector.  
3. Community participation in the provision of protection for children and women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by:  
a. Providing information through socialization and education;  
b. Providing input in the formulation of policies related to the protection of children and women victims of violence;  
c. Reporting to the authorities if there is a violation of the rights of children and women victims of violence;  
d. Playing an active role in the rehabilitation and social reintegration process for children and women victims of violence;  
e. Conducting monitoring, supervision, and being responsible for the provision of protection for children and women victims of violence;  
f. Playing an active role by eliminating negative labeling of children and women victims of violence; and  
g. Providing space for children and women victims of violence to be able to participate and express their opinions.  
4. The role of community organizations and educational institutions as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out by taking the necessary steps in accordance with their respective duties, functions, and authorities to assist in the provision of services for children and women victims of violence.  
5. The role of mass media as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out through the dissemination of information and educational materials that are beneficial from the social, cultural, educational, religious, and health aspects of children and women victims of violence by taking into account the interests of children and women victims of violence.  
6. The role of the business sector as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out through:  
a. Company policies that are child and women victims of violence perspective;  
b. Products intended for children and women victims of violence must be safe for children and women victims of violence;  
c. Contributing to the fulfillment of the rights of children and women victims of violence through corporate social responsibility.

# CHAPTER VIII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 17

1. The Regional Government is obliged to conduct guidance and supervision of the provision of protection for women and children victims of violence;  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) include:  
a. Recovery guidelines and standards;  
b. Technical guidance and training;  
c. Provision of facilities;  
d. Monitoring; and  
e. Evaluation.  
3. Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) is in order to realize the objectives of protection for women and children victims of violence in accordance with minimum service standards;  
4. Supervision of the implementation of this Regional Regulation is carried out by the Regional Government and/or the community in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 18

The community may supervise the provision of protection for women and children victims of violence organized by the Regional Government with a mechanism for conveying aspirations to the Regent or to the DPRD.

# CHAPTER VIII

## FUNDING

#### Article 19

1. Financing for the provision of protection for women and children victims of violence is charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget of West Southeast Maluku Regency;  
2. Budget management for the provision of protection for victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

## CHAPTER IX

## PROHIBITIONS

#### Article 20

1. Every person is prohibited from:  
a. treating children and women in a discriminatory manner that results in children and women experiencing losses, both material and moral, thereby hindering their social function; or  
b. treating children and women with disabilities in a discriminatory manner.  
2. Every person is prohibited from placing, allowing, involving, ordering the involvement of children and women in situations and treatment of abuse and neglect;  
3. Every person is prohibited from placing, allowing, committing, ordering to commit, or participating in committing violence against children and women;  
4. Every person is prohibited from committing violence or threat of violence to force children and women to have sexual intercourse with him or others;  
5. Every person is prohibited from committing violence or threat of violence, forcing, committing fraud, committing a series of lies, or persuading children and women to commit or allow obscene acts to be committed;  
6. Every person is prohibited from placing, allowing, committing, ordering to commit, or participating in committing kidnapping, sale and/or trafficking of children and women;  
7. Every person is prohibited from obstructing children and women from enjoying their own culture, acknowledging and practicing their religious teachings and/or using their own language without neglecting access to community and cultural development;  
8. Every person is prohibited from recruiting or exploiting children and women for military and/or other interests and leaving children and women without protection of life;  
9. Every person is prohibited from placing, allowing, committing, ordering to commit, or participating in committing economic and/or sexual exploitation of children and women;  
10. Every person is prohibited from intentionally placing, allowing, involving, ordering the involvement of children and women in the abuse, as well as production and distribution of narcotics and/or psychotropic substances;  
11. Every person is prohibited from intentionally placing, allowing, involving, ordering the involvement of children and women in the abuse, as well as production and distribution of alcohol and other addictive substances.

# CHAPTER XI

## TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 21

The safe house as referred to in article 6 must be established no later than 1 (one) year after this Regional Regulation is promulgated.

#### I. GENERAL

Various acts of violence against both women and children within the household as well as outside the household continue to increase from time to time. This condition, if without serious handling, will destroy the life of households, society, nation and state. Therefore, the Government of West Southeast Maluku Regency is obliged to provide protection to women and children victims of violence which is an implementation of various laws and regulations, including Law Number 39 Year 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 21 Year 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Crime of Trafficking in Persons, Law Number 23 Year 2002 concerning Child Protection.

Protection of women and children from acts of violence is the obligation of the Regional Government as regulated in article 22 letter a, Law Number 23 Year 2004 concerning Regional Government which states "that in carrying out regional autonomy has the obligation to protect the community, maintain unity, integrity and national harmony as well as the integrity of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia".

On that basis, the Government of West Southeast Maluku Regency has taken steps as an effort to provide protection to women and children victims of violence, but has not been able to provide maximum and adequate protection against acts of violence that occur in the social life of the community, especially to women and children, among others caused by the still low understanding of the community regarding the rights of women and children, handling has not been well coordinated, its implementation has not been sustainable and so on. Therefore, a regulation in the form of a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence is needed to answer while providing legal certainty in the protection of women and children victims from acts of violence that occur.

The existence of a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence, gives responsibility to the Regional Government starting from the prevention of acts of violence to the handling of victims of violence. In its implementation, the Regional Government cooperates with government agencies and the community. In addition, adequate funding support from the Regional Government as well as community participation, it is hoped that violence against women and children can be reduced and even in time will be eradicated from the face of this beloved West Southeast Maluku earth.

#### II. ARTICLE BY ARTICLE

#### Article 1

Sufficiently Clear.

#### Article 2

Letter a
What is meant by "the principle of respect for human rights" is that the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is to ensure the fulfillment of the rights of victims of violence as a manifestation of respect for human rights.

Letter b
What is meant by "the principle of non-discrimination" is all actions and deeds carried out without distinguishing based on economic, social, cultural, political background, gender, age, religion, race and so on.

What is meant by "the principle of justice and gender equality" is a process of equality and conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and their rights as human beings, so as to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural activities, national defense and security and equality in enjoying the results of such development.

Letter c
What is meant by "the principle of victim protection" is the protection of self, family, honor, dignity and property under one's control, a sense of security and protection from threats of fear, freedom from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity.

Letter d
What is meant by "the principle of non-discrimination" is an attitude and treatment towards victims without making distinctions based on age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups.

Letter e
What is meant by "the principle of the best interests of the Victim" is all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, society, legislative bodies and judicial bodies, where the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration.
