# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation what is meant by:

- 1. Region is Penajam Paser Utara Regency.
  - 2. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the organizer of regional government who leads the implementation of governmental affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region of Penajam Paser Utara Regency.
  - 3. Regent is the Regent of Penajam Paser Utara.
  - 4. Village is a legal community unit that has territorial boundaries which has the authority to regulate and manage governmental affairs, the interests of the local community based on community initiative, rights of origin and/or traditional rights that are recognized and respected in the governmental system of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia.
  - 5. Family is a person who has blood relations in a direct line upward or downward and collateral lines up to the third degree, or who has marital relations, or a person who is the dependent of a woman.
  - 6. Service is immediate activities and actions carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
  - 7. Protection is all service actions to guarantee and protect the rights of victims of acts of violence organized by the Integrated Service Center.
  - 8. Prevention is all actions or efforts made to eliminate various factors that cause acts of violence against women.
  - 9. Woman is an adult human of female sex and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.
  - 10. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against the physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or causes the deprivation of a person's freedom.
  - 11. Violence Against Women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in suffering or hardship for Women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of liberty arbitrarily, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.
  - 12. Victim is a Woman who experiences acts of violence.
  - 13. Health Rehabilitation is the recovery of a Victim of trafficking in persons from the health disorders suffered.
  - 14. Social Rehabilitation is services aimed at restoring and developing the ability of a person experiencing social dysfunction so that they can carry out their social functions properly.
  - 15. Repatriation is an effort to return Women Victims of Violence to the Family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of their needs.
  - 16. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with the Family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for Victims.
  - 17. Legal Aid is legal services provided by legal assistants and advocates to carry out the process of accompanying sanctions and/or Victims of Violence Against Women and children that are gender sensitive.
  - 18. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a regional technical implementation unit established by the regional government in providing services for women and children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.
  - 19. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to provide protection to Victims in accordance with predetermined standards.

#### Article 2

The Protection of Women Victims aims to: a. protect Women Victims of Violence;

b. provide Services to Women Victims of acts of Violence; and c. empower Women Victims of Violence;

d. increase the capacity of women victims of violence in the empowerment of women through related Regional Apparatus Organizations, the Private Sector, the Business World and others.

# CHAPTER II

VICTIMS' RIGHTS

#### Article 3

Victims have the right to obtain:

- a. Protection from:
  - 1. Family;
  - 2. police;
  - 3. prosecutors;
  - 4. courts;
  - 5. advocates; and
  - 6. social institutions, or other parties either temporarily or based on a protection order from the court.
- b. Health services in accordance with medical needs;
- c. special handling related to the confidentiality of Victims; d. assistance by social workers and Legal Aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of legislation;
- e. spiritual guidance Services; and
- f. Victims have the right to determine their own decisions regarding which choices will be implemented.

# CHAPTER III

AUTHORITY AND RESPONSIBILITY

#### Article 4

(1) The Regional Government has the authority and is responsible for the Protection of Women Victims of Violence.

(2) The authority as referred to in paragraph (1) includes: a. service activities for Women Victims of Violence; b. monitoring, strengthening of victims of Violence; and

c. capacity building of apparatus and other stakeholders.

# CHAPTER IV

INSTITUTIONS

#### Article 5

(1) The Regent establishes UPTD PPA.

(2) Further provisions regarding the procedures for the establishment of UPTD PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 6

(1) UPID PPA services include:

- a. providing services and handling as quickly as possible to Victims;
- b. providing ease, comfort, safety, and free of charge for Victims;
- c. maintaining confidentiality of Victims; and
- d. ensuring justice and legal certainty for Victims.
  - (2) Further provisions regarding the procedures for UPID PPA Services as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 7

The implementation of UPTD PPA requires:

- a. adequate supporting facilities and infrastructure;
- b. implementing officers; and
- c. functional officers.

#### Article 8

(1) UPID PPA is tasked with carrying out technical operational activities in its working area in providing services for women and children who experience problems of violence, discrimination, special protection, and other problems.

(2) UPID PPA in carrying out its duties as referred to in paragraph ( 1 ) performs service functions:

- a. public complaints;
- b. victim outreach;
- c. case management;
- d. temporary shelter;
- e. mediation; and
- f. victim assistance.
  - (3) UPTD PPA in performing the functions as referred to in paragraph (21 is conducted for basic services in 1 (one) Regency.

# CHAPTER V

PREVENTION AND HANDLING

#### Article 9

(l) The Regional Government is obligated to conduct Prevention of Violence against Women quickly, in an integrated and coordinated manner.

(2) The conduct of Prevention of acts of Violence against Women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through the fields of:

- a. Education;
- b. Public facilities and infrastructure;
- c. Government and institutional governance
- d. Economy and employment;
- e. Social welfare;
- f. Culture;
- g. Information technology;
- h. Religion; and
- i. Family

#### Article 10

Handling of women victims of violence is by providing services in an integrated and coordinated manner including:

- a. coverage of Women Victims of Violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers in integrated service units; b. coverage of Women Victims of Violence who receive health services by trained health workers at puskesmas and hospitals: c. coverage of Social Rehabilitation services provided by trained Social Rehabilitation officers for Women Victims of Violence in integrated service units:
- d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for Women Victims of Violence in integrated service units:
- e. coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of Violence Against Women; f. coverage of Women Victims of Violence who receive Legal Aid services;
- g. coverage of Repatriation services for Women Victims of Violence; and h. coverage of Social Reintegration services for Women Victims of Violence.

# CHAPTER VI

SOCIAL PROTECTION HOUSE

#### Article 1 1

(1) The Regional Government is obligated to establish a social protection house and/or Safe House or other designation.

(2) The social protection house and/or Safe House as referred to in paragraph (i) ensures the implementation of Health Rehabilitation Services, Social Rehabilitation, Repatriation, and Social Reintegration.

(3) For the conduct of Services as referred to in paragraph (21, the Regional Government may utilize social protection houses and/or Safe Houses owned by the community or other social service institutions.

MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 13

(2) Monitoring is carried out starting from planning to the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women Victims of Violence for the current year.

#### Article 14

(2) The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women are used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs, and activities for the Protection of Women for the following year.

I. GENERAL

Violence against Women is one form of act that violates human rights. As is known, the human rights of Women have been guaranteed and regulated in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia and the Law on Human Rights. Therefore, protection of Women's rights from violence should be respected, upheld and enhanced in human dignity without discrimination, welfare and justice for Women.

Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom. Furthermore, Violence against women is any act based on gender difference that results in or is likely to result in suffering or distress of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or deprivation of freedom arbitrarily, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in suffering or distress physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the child. The existence of women victims of violence has not received adequate services so that minimum services for victims of violence are needed.

In order to prevent and address violence against Women in Penajam Paser Utara Regency to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women victims of violence in the form of a Regional Regulation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for women victims of violence specifically in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of women victims of violence in Penajam Paser Utara Regency.
