# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Region is the City of Madiun.
- 2. Regional Government is the Government of Madiun City.
- 3. Mayor is the Mayor of Madiun.
- 4. Agency for Community Empowerment, Family Planning, and Food Security, hereinafter abbreviated as BPMKB dan KP, is the Agency for Community Empowerment, Family Planning, and Food Security of Madiun City.
- 5. Appointed Official is the Head of the Agency for Community Empowerment, Family Planning, and Food Security of Madiun City.
- 6. Child is a person who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years of age, including a child still in the womb.
- 7. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
- 8. Protection of Women is all efforts carried out by family, government and others aimed at women to ensure the fulfillment of women's rights as a form of respect for human rights.
- 9. Protection of children is all activities to ensure and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and protection from violence and discrimination.
- 10. Violence is any unlawful act with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.
- 11. Violence against women is any act based on gender difference that results or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually, or psychologically including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public domain or in private life.
- 12. Violence against children is any act against children that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and maltreatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of children.
- 13. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
- 14. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence.
- 15. Service is an action that must be immediately carried out for victims when seeing, hearing and knowing of, during or after the occurrence of violence against victims.
- 16. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has expertise in accompanying victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
- 17. Repatriation is the effort to return women and children victims of violence from within/outside the country to the debarkation/entry point, or from the receiving region to the region of origin.
- 18. Social reintegration is the effort to reunite victims with family, substitute family, or community that can provide protection and fulfillment of needs for victims.
- 19. Empowerment is the strengthening of victims of violence to be able to make efforts and work independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
- 20. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection for victims in accordance with the specified operational standards.
- 21. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations. 22. Integrated Service Center for Acts of Violence Against Women and Children hereinafter referred to as PPT is a form of government and non-government network that organizes community-based integrated services handling victims of violence against women and children which includes legal assistance, medical, and psychosocial services.
- 23. Minimum Service Standards hereinafter abbreviated as SPM is a benchmark for service performance of integrated service units in providing services for handling reports/complaints, health services, social rehabilitation, enforcement and legal assistance, as well as repatriation and social reintegration for women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is carried out based on the principles of:

- a. respect for human rights;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination;
- d. victim protection;
- e. best interests of victims; and
- f. empowerment.

#### Article 3

(1) The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence aims to:

- a. prevent all forms of violence;
- b. protect victims of violence;
- c. take action against perpetrators of violence; and
- d. maintain the integrity of harmonious and prosperous households.
  - (2) Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of:
- a. physical violence;
- b. psychological violence;
- c. sexual violence;
- d. economic neglect; and
- e. restriction of movement.

# CHAPTER III

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 4

(1) Everyone is prohibited from allowing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.

(2) Everyone is obliged to report the occurrence of violence against women and children to the authorized agency.

(3) Protection of women and children victims of violence is carried out by:

- a. Regional Government;
- b. related agencies along with other social institutions; c. family and/or closest relatives;
- d. community; and/or
- e. educational institutions.
  - (4) Protection efforts for women and children in the event of violence against women and children as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by:
- a. forming work networks in efforts to prevent violence based on partnership patterns;
- b. providing repressive protection in the form of compensation and curative in the form of medical assistance, psycho-social rehabilitation; and
- c. conducting education and socialization about laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence.
  - (5) Further provisions regarding protection efforts for women and children as referred to in paragraph (4) are regulated by Mayoral Regulation.

# CHAPTER IV

RESPONSIBILITY

#### Article 5

The implementation of services for women and children victims of violence is a shared responsibility of: a. Regional Government;

b. community;

c. family; and/or

d. parents.

#### Article 6

(1) The Regional Government implements efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence against women and children, by:

- a. collecting data and information about women and children victims of violence;
- b. conducting education on anti-violence values toward women and children;
- c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence; and
- d. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence.
  - (2) To anticipate the occurrence of violence, the Regional Government is obligated to provide and implement services for women and children victims of violence by:
- a. establishing and facilitating the operation of integrated service institutions for victims involving community elements;
- b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for women and children victims of violence;
- c. conducting continuous handling up to the rehabilitation and social reintegration stage.
  - (3) The Regional Government in implementing efforts to prevent and anticipate violence against women and children as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) must pay attention to the rights and obligations of the community, family and/or parents who are legally responsible for the victim.

#### Article 7

The responsibility of the community as referred to in Article 6 paragraph (3) is implemented in the form of: a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children; and

b. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcers or authorized parties.

#### Article 8

The family and/or parents as referred to in Article 6 paragraph (3) have full responsibility to prevent all forms of violence and protect women and children as family members.

# CHAPTER V

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 9

Every woman and child victim of violence has the right to: a. receive protection from family, police, prosecutor's office, court, advocates, social institutions, community, Regional Government and/or other parties either temporarily or based on protection orders from the court;

b. health and psychological services according to medical needs;

c. special handling related to the confidentiality of victims;

d. assistance by social workers and legal aid at every level of the examination process in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations;

e. spiritual guidance services;

f. the right to information on case developments;

g. the right to safe house (shelter) services;

h. the right to restitution;

i. the right to psychosocial services/recovery; and j. the right to social reintegration.

#### Article 10

(1) In the event that women and children victims of violence as referred to in Article 9 require continuous handling, they have the right to stay in a safe house or alternative residence.

(2) Continuous handling as referred to in paragraph (1) constitutes handling at the rehabilitation stage conducted either by individuals, groups or Government and non-

Government institutions.

#### Article 11

(1) The operation and/or management of safe houses is carried out by BPMKB and KP.

(2) For security purposes and/or at the request of victims of violence, the manager or operator of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (1) must place victims of violence in a specific location that is kept confidential.

(3) Further provisions regarding the operation of safe houses as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VI

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Institutional

#### Article 12

(1) The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is conducted in an integrated manner within the PPT framework.

(2) Further provisions regarding PPT as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Two

Form and Service Mechanism

#### Article 13

(1) The forms of protection services for women and children victims of violence implemented by PPT include:

- a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims conducted by medical and paramedical personnel;
- b. medico-legal services constitute a form of medical service for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;
- c. psycho-social services constitute services provided by companions for the recovery of the victim's traumatic condition, including the provision of space and victim's house to protect victims from threats and intimidation to victims;
- d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process;
- e. economic self-sufficiency services in the form of services for skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.
  - (2) Further provisions regarding the service mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented according to Standard Operating Procedures regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Three

Service Principles

#### Article 14

The implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence is conducted free of charge, quickly, safely, empathetically, non-discriminatory, easily accessible and with a guarantee of confidentiality.

#### Article 15

PPT managers implement services in accordance with the service principles as referred to in Article 14.

# CHAPTER VII

MINIMUM SERVICE STANDARDS

#### Article 16

MSS for Integrated Services for women and children victims of violence, includes:

- a. handling of complaints/reports;
- b. health services;
- c. social rehabilitation;
- d. law enforcement and legal aid; and
- e. repatriation and social reintegration.

#### Article 17

(1) MSS as referred to in Article 16 has performance indicators including:

- a. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive complaint handling by trained officers in the integrated service unit;
- b. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive health services by trained health personnel at community health centers and hospitals;
- c. coverage of social rehabilitation services provided by trained social rehabilitation officers for women and children victims of violence within PPT;
- d. coverage of spiritual guidance services provided by trained spiritual guidance officers for women and children victims of violence within the integrated service unit;
- e. coverage of law enforcement from the investigation level to court decisions on cases of violence against women and children;
- f. coverage of women and children victims of violence who receive legal aid;
- g. coverage of repatriation services for women and children victims of violence; and
- h. coverage of social reintegration services for women and children victims of violence.
  - (2) Further provisions regarding MSS as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

EMPOWERMENT

## Part One

Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence Article 18

Implementation steps for empowerment of women victims of violence include:

- a. job training;
- b. productive economic enterprises and/or joint business groups; and
- c. capital assistance.

#### Article 19

Implementation steps for empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in Article 18 are carried out by:

- a. procuring the needs required for entrepreneurship training for women victims of violence, in order to increase business knowledge, attitudes and skills;
- b. facilitating the implementation of various job training and skills training;
- c. conducting assistance in developing productive economic enterprises;
- d. exploring cooperation with small, medium and large enterprises as well as financial institutions to develop businesses of women victims of violence;
- e. seeking to provide capital for women victims of violence; and
- f. expanding information access and promoting the results of productive economic enterprises of women victims of violence.

## Part Two

Empowerment of Children Victims of Violence

#### Article 20

Implementation Steps for the Empowerment of child victims of violence include efforts to increase understanding for children not to commit violence.

#### Article 21

Implementation steps for the empowerment of child victims of violence as referred to in Article 20 are carried out by way of:

- a. skills training in accordance with the child's interests; and b. fulfillment of education both formal and non-formal.

# CHAPTER IX

MONITORING AND EVALUATION

#### Article 22

(1) The Regional Government conducts monitoring to ensure the continuity and effectiveness of integrated steps in the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence.

(2) Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by BPMKB, and KP.

(3) Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out periodically through coordination and direct monitoring of regional apparatus work units that implement policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence.

(4) Monitoring is carried out from planning to implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence every year.

#### Article 23

(1) Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs, and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence is carried out at the end of each fiscal year or when necessary according to needs.

(2) The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence are used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs and activities for the protection of women and child victims of violence for the following year.

(3) Evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

REPORTING

#### Article 24

The Mayor through the appointed Official reports the implementation of MSS in the field of integrated services for women and child victims of violence every year in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XI

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 25

(1) The Mayor through the appointed Official conducts guidance and supervision over the implementation of MSS in the field of integrated services for women and child victims of violence in the Region.

(2) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

- a. provision of implementation guidelines;
- b. coaching;
- c. supervision; and/or
- d. monitoring, and evaluation of implementation for women and child victims of violence.

# CHAPTER XII

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 26

(1) In the provision of protection for women and child victims of violence, the community may:

- a. form family partners at the Sub-district level by the community;
- b. conduct socialization of women's and children's rights independently;
- c. provide first aid to victims; and
- d. report to the competent authority if violence against women and children occurs in their environment.
  - (2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by:
- a. individuals;
- b. social community institutions;
- c. non-governmental organizations;
- d. educational institutions;
- e. religious institutions;
- f. private sector; and/or
- g. mass media.

# CHAPTER XIII

FUNDING

#### Article 27

Funding for the provision of protection for Women and child victims of violence is sourced from: a. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and/or b. other legal and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER XIV

INVESTIGATION

#### Article 28

(1) Certain Civil Servant Officials within the Regional Government are granted special authority as Investigators to conduct investigations of criminal offenses violating this Regional Regulation, as referred to in the applicable Criminal Procedure Code.

(2) Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) are certain civil servant officials within the Regional Government appointed by the authorized official in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

(3) The authority of Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) is:

- a. receive, seek, collect, and examine information or reports concerning criminal offenses so that such information or reports become complete and clear;
- b. examine, seek, and collect information regarding individuals or Entities about the truth of acts committed in connection with criminal offenses;
- c. request information and evidence from individuals or Entities in connection with criminal offenses;
- d. examine books, records, and other documents concerning criminal offenses;
- e. conduct searches to obtain evidence of bookkeeping, recording and other documents, as well as seize such evidence;
- f. request assistance from experts in the framework of carrying out the duty of investigating criminal offenses;
- g. order to stop and/or prohibit a person from leaving a room or place when an examination is in progress and examine the identity of the person and/or documents carried as referred to in letter e;
- h. photograph a person related to a criminal offense;
- i. summon a person to be heard for their testimony and examined as a suspect or witness;
- j. terminate the investigation; and/or
- k. take other actions necessary for the smooth investigation of criminal offenses according to law that can be accounted for.
  - (4) Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) notify the commencement of the investigation and submit the results of their investigation to the Public Prosecutor through the Investigator Official of the State Police of the Republic of Indonesia, in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Law.

# CHAPTER XV

(2) In the event that the act as referred to in paragraph (1) is committed by a Civil Servant, it is supplemented with employment sanctions in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

(3) The criminal offense as referred to in paragraph (1) is a violation.

(4) Violations of the provisions as referred to in Article 4 paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) are subject to sanctions in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

I. GENERAL

That every citizen has the right to live free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, as well as to obtain a sense of security and freedom from all forms of violence, violence against women and children constitutes a violation of human rights and therefore needs to receive protection so that their right to life is guaranteed in accordance with their honor and dignity without discrimination.

The Regional Government has the obligation to provide protection to every citizen in accordance with the preamble of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia. Violence against women is any action based on gender differences that results or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life. Violence against children is any act against a child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, socially, psychologically, including neglect or maltreatment that threatens the integrity of the body and degrades the dignity of the child. The existence of women and children victims of violence have not received adequate services, so minimum services are needed for victims of violence.

This Regional Regulation on the Implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence is based on Pancasila, and founded on the 1945 Constitution, and refers to many Laws, especially those regulating the protection of women and children victims of violence.

Among them are Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection as amended by Law Number 35 of 2014, Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence and other related laws and regulations.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children victims of violence in Madiun City so that they are protected from threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and worth, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of regional regulations.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for women and children victims of violence particularly in terms of services, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, funding, guidance and supervision of women and children victims of violence in Madiun City.

#### Article 2

Sufficiently clear letter b

What is meant by the principle of "justice" is a state where every person, both men and women, are treated equally and obtain equal opportunities to get opportunities (access), as well as welfare.

What is meant by the principle of "gender equality" is the equality of rights, opportunities, benefits and decision-making between women and men including in entering employment opportunities both in the formal and informal sectors. letter c

What is meant by the principle of "non-discrimination" is the attitude and treatment of women and children by not making distinctions on the basis of gender, race, ethnicity, religion and between groups. letter d

What is meant by the principle of "victim protection" is the protection of personal self, family, honor, dignity, and property under their authority, sense of security and protection from threats of fear, freedom from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity. letter e

What is meant by the principle of "the best interests of the victim" is that all actions concerning victims carried out by the government, community, legislative body and judicial body, the best interests of the victim must be the primary consideration letter f

What is meant by "empowerment" is the strengthening of victims of violence to be able to strive and work independently after they are recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.

#### Article 3

paragraph (1)

Sufficiently clear paragraph (2) letter a

What is meant by "physical violence" is an act that causes pain, illness or serious injury such as slapping, hitting, spitting on, pulling hair/grabbing by the hair, kicking, burning with hot objects, hitting/injuring with weapons and or causing disability to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and or causing death. letter b

What is meant by "psychological violence" is an act that causes fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feeling of helplessness, and/or severe psychological suffering to a person. letter c

What is meant by "sexual violence" includes but is not limited to:

- a. coercion of sexual relations carried out against persons residing within the household scope and/or coercion of sexual relations against someone within their household scope with other persons, for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes;
- b. by force or threat of force compelling a woman who is not his wife to have sexual intercourse with him;
- c. by force or threat of force compelling someone to commit or allow obscene acts to be committed;
- d. deliberately using force or threat of force to compel a child to have sexual intercourse;
- e. deliberately using force or threat of force, coercion, deception, a series of lies or persuading a child to commit or allow obscene acts to be committed. letter d What is meant by "economic neglect" is neglecting the economic needs of a wife or child or family members or relatives such as not providing for the wife's livelihood, even spending the wife's money, not providing or withholding the wages of domestic workers or drivers and including acts of restricting and/or prohibiting family members from working properly. letter e What is meant by "restriction of freedom of movement" is restricting the freedom of movement of a wife or child in developing their potential both for economic and social purposes such as prohibiting them from gathering and socializing in society as well as restricting the freedom of movement of relatives such as preventing domestic workers or drivers from communicating or relating with family.
