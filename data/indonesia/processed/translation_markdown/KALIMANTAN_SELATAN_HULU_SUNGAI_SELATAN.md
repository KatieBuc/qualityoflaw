# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:  
1. Region is Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency.  
2. Regional Government is the Regional Head as an element of the Regional Government organizer who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.  
3. Regional Head hereinafter referred to as Regent is the Regent of Hulu Sungai Selatan.  
4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government organizer.  
5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regional Head and DPRD in organizing government affairs that are the authority of the region.  
6. Regional Long-Term Development Plan hereinafter abbreviated as RPJPD is a regional planning document for a period of 20 (twenty) years.  
7. Regional Medium-Term Development Plan hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMD is a regional planning document for a period of 5 (five) years.  
8. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.  
9. Violence is any act unlawfully with or without the use of means against physical and verbal that causes danger to life, body and/or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom.  
10. Violence Against Women is any action based on gender differences that results in or may result in misery or suffering of women physically, sexually or psychologically, including threats of certain actions, coercion or arbitrary deprivation of freedom, whether occurring in the public sphere or in private life.  
11. Violence Against Children is any act against a Child that results in misery or suffering physically, mentally, sexually, psychologically, including neglect and ill-treatment that threatens bodily integrity and degrades the dignity of the Child.  
12. Discrimination is any distinction, harassment or exclusion that is direct or indirect based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political beliefs that results in reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or use of human rights and basic freedoms in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.  
13. Trafficking in Persons is the act of recruitment, transportation, harboring, sending, transfer, or receipt of a person by threat of violence, use of violence, kidnapping, confinement, forgery, fraud and abuse of power or vulnerable position, debt bondage or giving payment or benefits, so as to obtain consent from the person holding control over that other person, whether carried out within the country or between countries, for the purpose of exploitation or resulting in the person being exploited.  
14. Child is a person who has not reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years and has not married including a Child still in the womb.  
15. Victim of Acts of Violence is a woman and Child who experiences suffering whether physical, mental or emotional, economic loss, or experiences neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights.  
16. Prevention is a direct effort carried out by the Regional Government and/or the Community to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and Children.  
17. Regional Technical Implementation Unit for the Protection of Women and Children hereinafter abbreviated as UPTD PPA is a regional technical implementation unit established by the Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency Government in providing services for women and Children who experience violence, discrimination, special protection and other problems.  
18. Safe House is a temporary residence used to provide protection to victims in accordance with determined operational standards.  
19. Social Reintegration is a process of preparing the Community and victims that supports the reunification of victims into the family environment, school environment, work environment and Community environment that can provide protection and fulfillment of victims' needs.  
20. Victim Recovery hereinafter Recovery is services provided in the form of strengthening and developing the ability of a person who experiences acts of violence against women and Children to be more empowered physically, psychologically, sexually, economically and socially.  
21. Assistance is all actions in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy and spiritual guidance, for the purpose of self-strengthening of women and Children from acts of violence to resolve the problems faced.  
22. Family is the smallest unit in the Community consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their Child, or father and his Child, or mother and her Child, or blood relatives in a straight line upward or downward to the third degree.  
23. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.  
24. Community is individuals, families, groups, and social organizations and/or community organizations.

#### Article 2

Protection of women and children from acts of violence is organized based on the principles of:  
a. humanity;  
b. justice and gender equality;  
c. non-discrimination;  
d. order and legal certainty;  
e. transparency; and  
f. protection.

#### Article 3

The purpose of protection of women and children from acts of violence, is to:  
a. prevent acts of violence against women and children including trafficking in persons;  
b. eliminate all forms of acts of violence and exploitation against women and children;  
c. protect, provide a sense of security for women and children;  
d. provide services to women and children from acts of violence, reporters, and witnesses; and  
e. empower women and children victims of acts of violence to be more empowered physically, psychologically, socially, and economically.

#### Article 4

The scope of regulation of protection of women and children from acts of violence includes:  
a. organization of protection of women and children from acts of violence;  
b. obligations and responsibilities;  
c. forms of violence;  
d. rights of women and children from acts of violence;  
e. institutions;  
f. cooperation and partnerships;  
g. guidance and supervision;  
h. awards; and  
i. funding;

# CHAPTER II

## ORGANIZATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## AND CHILDREN FROM ACTS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

General

#### Article 5

1. The Regional Government organizes protection of women and children from acts of violence in the Region.  
2. The organization of protection of women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented by the Regional Apparatus that organizes government affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.  
3. The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) involves stakeholders in the Region.  
4. The implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence in the region is guided by the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 6

The Regional Government in organizing the Protection of Women and Children from Acts of Violence as referred to in Article 5 carries out:  
a. prevention of acts of violence against women and children;  
b. legal protection;  
c. Recovery;  
d. coordination; and  
e. encouraging Community participation.

## Part Two

Prevention of Acts of Violence

#### Article 7

1. Regional Government conducts prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 6 letter a through empowerment and awareness to Families, Parents, and the Community by providing information, guidance and/or counseling.
2. In addition to empowerment and awareness as referred to in paragraph (1), it shall be implemented by means of:
a. providing material on prevention of acts of violence against women and children in both formal and informal education;
b. opening accessibility to obtain education, training, funding, income enhancement and social services in accordance with applicable provisions;
c. opening employment opportunities for women;
d. building community participation and concern towards prevention and protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. building and providing a complete and easily accessible information system;
f. building networks and cooperation with law enforcement apparatus, government apparatus, educational institutions, and various non-governmental organizations that work and/or care for women and children; and
g. opening an integrated service system for protection of women and children from acts of violence in every sub-district.

#### Article 8

1. Prevention of acts of violence against women and children as referred to in Article 7, shall be implemented by Regional Apparatus that administer governmental affairs in the fields of:
a. social affairs;
b. health;
c. education;
d. manpower;
e. population administration and civil registration;f. women's empowerment, protection of women and children;
g. cooperatives and Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises; and
h. public tranquility, public order, and community protection
2. Prevention of acts of violence by Regional Apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1), shall be implemented in an integrated and continuous manner based on RPJPD and RPJMD.

## Part Three

Legal Protection

#### Article 9

Regional Government conducts legal protection as referred to in Article 6 letter b, including:
a. providing protection in Safe Houses;
b. providing legal information to victims;
c. providing assistance for victims as witnesses from the investigation process until verdict; and
d. providing special legal protection for child victims of acts of violence which may be conducted through appointment of guardianship in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Four

Recovery

#### Article 10

Regional Government conducts recovery as referred to in Article 6 letter c, including:
a. providing physical recovery at health service institutions;
b. providing medicolegal services;
c. assisting victim repatriation;
d. providing temporary protection in Safe Houses;
e. providing psychosocial recovery and assistance;
f. providing spiritual guidance services; and/or
g. conducting preparation of family, school, work and community environment, as well as economic empowerment.

## Part Five

Coordination

#### Article 11

Regional Government conducts coordination as referred to in Article 6 letter d, including:
a. conducting coordination and cooperation in handling violence cases with integrated service institutions; and/or
b. conducting coordination and cooperation in handling violence cases with integrated services between Regions.

## Part Six

Community Participation

#### Article 12

Regional Government encourages community participation as referred to in Article 6 letter e, by means of:
a. fostering community concern towards cases of acts of violence against women and children;
b. encouraging the community to actively participate in providing information and reporting acts of violence against women and children;
c. fostering local wisdom in handling violence cases;
d. organizing strengthening of community groups in handling acts of violence against women and children;
e. disseminating information about laws and regulations related to acts of violence against women and children.

# CHAPTER III

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

General

#### Article 13

Obligations and responsibilities in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence are the joint responsibility of:
a. Regional Government; and
b. Community.

## Part Two

Regional Government

#### Article 14

1. Obligations and responsibilities of Regional Government as referred to in Article 13 letter a, include:
a. establishing, implementing policies, programs, and conducting cooperation activities in the administration of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
b. facilitating the establishment of institutional protection of women and children from acts of violence as well as providing support for facilities and infrastructure;
c. allocating budget for the administration of protection of women and children from acts of violence according to regional financial capacity;
d. fostering and supervising the administration of protection of women and children from acts of violence;
e. providing protection services for women and child victims of acts of violence;
f. encouraging and increasing community participation; and
g. appointing parents and/or substitute family caregivers as a protection measure for children who become victims of acts of violence.
2. In order to implement obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1), the Regent establishes programs and activities for protection of women and children in the Regional Action Plan as a basis for Regional Apparatus in implementing protection of women and children from acts of violence.
3. Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2), constitutes part of RPJMD.
4. Further provisions regarding Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Three

Community

#### Article 15

1. obligations and responsibilities of the community as referred to in Article 13 letter b, shall be administered in the form of community participation.
2. forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (1), include:
a. preventing the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children;
b. providing protection to women and children from acts of violence;
c. providing information and/or reporting acts of violence against women and children to law enforcement or authorized parties; and
d. participating in handling victims of acts of violence.
3. Forms of community participation as referred to in paragraph (2), shall be implemented responsibly in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

#### Article 16

Every person who hears, sees, or knows of the occurrence of violence against women and children is obligated to:
a. prevent and stop the continuation of acts of violence;
b. provide protection to victims;
c. provide emergency assistance; and/or
d. assist in the process of submitting applications for protection orders.

# CHAPTER IV

## FORMS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 17

Forms of violence against women and children include:
a. physical violence;
b. psychological violence;
c. sexual violence;
d. neglect;
e. maltreatment;
f. exploitation; and/or
g. other violence.

# CHAPTER V

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILD VICTIMS OF ACTS OF VIOLENCE

#### Article 18

Women and child victims of acts of violence receive the following rights:
a. right to have their dignity respected as human beings;
b. right to recovery;
c. right to determine their own decisions;
d. right to obtain information;
e. right to confidentiality;
f. right to social rehabilitation;
g. right to complaint handling in a fast, appropriate, comfortable and needs-based manner;
h. right of victims and their families to obtain facilitation in the judicial process;
i. right to assistance; and
j. right to security and protection.

#### Article 19

Child victims of acts of violence, in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 18, also obtain special rights as follows:  
a. right to survival, growth and development;  
b. right to basic population services;  
c. right to equal protection;  
d. right to be free from various stigma; and  
e. right to obtain freedom.

#### Article 20

The rights of children who are victims of acts of violence as referred to in Article 18 and Article 19 experienced by children with special needs shall be handled by the Regional Government and the community by referring to service standards for children with special needs.

#### Article 21

The fulfillment of rights as referred to in Article 18, Article 19 and Article 20 shall be guided by norms, standards, procedures, and criteria established by the government in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

## INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENTS

#### Article 22

1. The Regional Government shall establish UPTD PPA in providing protection to women and children from acts of violence.  
2. UPTD PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be established at the Regency level.  
3. Further provisions regarding the organization and working procedures of UPTD PPA as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

## COOPERATION AND PARTNERSHIP

## Part One

Cooperation

#### Article 23

1. The Regional Government shall develop cooperation patterns for the protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
2. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in order to achieve the objectives of protecting women and children from acts of violence in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
3. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted between the Regional Government and:  
a. Central Government;  
b. Provincial Government;  
c. other Regional Governments;  
d. Higher Education Institutions;  
e. Social and Religious Institutions;  
f. Non-Governmental Organizations; and/or  
g. Media.  
4. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) shall include:  
a. exchange of data and information;  
b. rehabilitation of victims of acts of violence;  
c. repatriation and social reintegration; and  
d. provision of evidence and witnesses.  
5. Cooperation as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

## Part Two

Partnership

#### Article 24

1. The Regional Government shall organize partnerships with the business sector in the protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
2. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in order to achieve the objectives of protecting women and children from acts of violence in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
3. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be conducted through:  
a. notification of employment opportunity information for women victims of acts of violence;  
b. education and training for women victims of acts of violence;  
c. educational assistance for women and children victims of acts of violence who have been withdrawn from their education; and  
d. fostering and increasing the economic independence of women victims of acts of violence.  
4. Partnership as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VIII

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 25

1. The Regional Government shall conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence.  
2. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) shall include:  
a. guidelines and fulfillment standards;  
b. technical guidance and training;  
c. coordination;  
d. monitoring;  
e. evaluation; and  
f. reporting.

#### Article 26

The community may conduct supervision of the implementation of protection of women and children from acts of violence organized by the Regional Government through a mechanism of conveying aspirations to the Regent or to the DPRD.

#### Article 27

Further provisions regarding the procedures for implementing guidance and supervision as referred to in Article 24 and Article 25 shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER IX

## AWARDS

#### Article 28

1. The Regional Government shall grant awards to every person or entity that participates in efforts to protect women and children from acts of violence in the Region.  
2. Awards as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be granted in the form of:  
a. certificate/plaque; and  
b. Regional incentive.  
3. Incentives as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b may be in the form of:  
a. fiscal; and  
b. non-fiscal.  
4. Fiscal incentives as referred to in paragraph (3) letter a may be in the form of:  
a. reduction or exemption of regional taxes; and/or  
b. reduction of regional levies.  
5. Non-fiscal incentives as referred to in paragraph (3) letter b may be in the form of regional licensing facilities in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
6. The procedures and forms of granting incentives as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be guided by the provisions of laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTIONS

#### Article 29

1. Every person who does not fulfill their obligations as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2) and Article 16 shall be subject to administrative sanctions.  
2. Administrative sanctions for persons as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of:  
a. oral warning;  
b. written warning; and/or  
c. administrative fine.  
3. Administrative sanctions for persons as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of:  
a. written warning;  
b. temporary suspension of activities;  
c. permanent cessation of activities;  
d. temporary revocation of permit;  
e. permanent revocation of permit; and/or  
f. administrative fine.  
4. Administrative fines as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c and paragraph (3) letter f shall constitute Regional revenue.  
5. Further provisions regarding the procedures for imposing administrative sanctions shall be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER XI

##### I. GENERAL

Women and Children have become a serious concern of the international community, considering their vulnerable position as victims of Violence from their families and environment. Various acts of Violence against both women and Children within the household and outside the household continue to increase from time to time. This condition, if without serious handling, will destroy the life of households, Society, nation and state. Therefore, the Government of Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency is obliged to provide Protection to women and Children victims of acts of Violence which is an implementation of various laws and regulations, including Law Number 39 of 1999 concerning Human Rights, Law Number 23 of 2004 concerning the Elimination of Domestic Violence, Law Number 21 of 2007 concerning the Eradication of the Crime of Trafficking in Persons, Law Number 35 of 2014 concerning Amendments to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection.

Protection of women and Children from acts of Violence is the obligation of the Regional Government in the context of regional autonomy, which is manifested in the form of Regional Regulations as stipulated in Article 236 paragraph (1), Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government.

On that basis, the Regional Government of Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency has taken steps as an effort to provide Protection to women and Children, but has not been able to provide maximum and adequate Protection against acts of Violence that occur in the social life of Society, especially to women and Children, among others caused by the still low understanding of Society regarding the rights of women and Children, handling has not been well coordinated, its implementation has not been sustainable, and so on. Therefore, a regulation in the form of a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children is needed to answer while providing legal certainty in the Protection of women and Children victims from acts of violence that occur.

The existence of a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children, gives responsibility to the Regional Government starting from Prevention of acts of violence to handling victims of acts of violence. In its implementation the Regional Government cooperates with government agencies, other regional governments and Society. In addition, adequate funding support both from the government, Regional Government and the participation of the business community and Society, it is hoped that violence against women and Children can be reduced and even in time will be eliminated from Hulu Sungai Selatan Regency.

#### Article 17

Point a  
Physical violence is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or disability to a person's body, miscarriage, loss of consciousness and/or cause death.  

Point b  
Psychological violence is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.  

Point c  
Sexual violence is caused by:  
1. acts in the form of sexual harassment;  
2. forced sexual intercourse;  
3. forced sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
4. forced sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and/or certain purposes.  

Point d  
Neglect is caused by:  
1. acts that result in the unfulfillment of a Child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by Parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;  
2. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or look after a Child as appropriate, committed by Parents, guardians, or other parties responsible for their care;  
3. acts of neglecting a person within the scope of one's household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they must provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
4. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting decent work inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.  

Point e  
Maltreatment is maltreatment of a Child's physical and emotional state including neglecting their education and health as well as sexual abuse resulting from erroneous human behavior toward the Child.  

Point f  
Exploitation is caused by:  
1. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others; 2) acts with or without the victim's consent including prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, exploitation of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or ability of a person by another party to obtain material or immaterial benefits; and/or
3. all forms of exploitation of sexual organs or other body organs of the victim to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all activities of prostitution or indecency.  

Point g  
Other violence is caused by:  
1. threats of violence include: any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's fundamental freedom; and
2. coercion, includes: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something contrary to their own will.
