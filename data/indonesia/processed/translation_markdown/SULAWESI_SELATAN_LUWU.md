# CHAPTER I

# GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Government is the Central Government.
2. Governor is the Governor of South Sulawesi Province.
3. Region is Luwu Regency.
4. Regional Government is the Regent as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
5. Regent is the Regent of Luwu.
6. Gender is a concept that refers to roles and responsibilities as women and as men that are created and internalized in families, communities and the culture of the society in which we live, including expectations, attitudes, characteristics, behavior of how to be a man and how to be a woman.
7. Discrimination is any restriction, harassment or exclusion that is directly or indirectly based on human differentiation on the basis of religion, ethnicity, race, ethnic group, group, class, social status, economic status, gender, resulting in language, political beliefs, reduction, deviation or elimination of recognition, implementation or human rights and fundamental freedoms in the use of rights in life both individually and collectively in the political, economic, legal, social, cultural and other aspects of life.
8. Women's empowerment is any effort to improve physical, mental spiritual, social, knowledge and skills capacity so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective abilities.
9. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to women carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, temporarily or courts or other parties either based on court decisions.
10. Violence is any act unlawfully, with or without the use of means against physical and psychological that causes danger to life, body or results in the deprivation of a person's freedom-
11. Disability is an Individual who has physical or mental limitations.
12. Regional Cooperation is an agreement between a governor and a governor or a governor and a regent/mayor or between a regent/mayor and another regent/mayor, and or a governor, regent/mayor with a third party, which is made in writing and creates rights and obligations.

# CHAPTER II

## PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of Women is implemented based on the principles of:
a. respect for human rights;
b. justice and gender equality,
c. non-discrimination; and
d. protection of victims.

#### Article 3

The implementation of Protection of Women aims to:
a. increase the participation of women both individually and in groups as potential and resources in the implementation of social community life;
b. prevent all forms of Discrimination;
c. empower women both individually and in groups and communities experiencing gender issues so that they are able to meet their needs independently;
d. prevent and address risks from women's vulnerability so that their survival can be fulfilled in accordance with minimum basic needs;
e. reduce the gap in opportunities between women and men in achieving development; and
f. reduce acts of Violence against women.

# CHAPTER III

## SCOPE

#### Article 4

The implementation of protection of women from acts of violence includes:
a. prevention;
b. handling;
c. empowerment.

#### Article 5

1. Protection of women from violence includes protection of women in the household, in the community, in the workplace and in conflict and disaster areas.
2. Protection of women from violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes women with disabilities, elderly women.

# CHAPTER IV

## HUMAN RIGHTS AND FUNDAMENTAL HUMAN FREEDOMS

#### Article 6

Human Rights and fundamental human freedoms, consist of:  
a. Right to life;  
b. Right to form a family and procreate;  
c. Right to self-development;  
d. Right to obtain justice;  
e. Right to personal freedom,  
f. Right to security;  
g. Right to welfare; and  
h. Right to participate in government.

#### Article 7

1. Every Woman has the right to:  
a. live peacefully, safely, in peace, happily, prosperously physically and spiritually,b. live in a family within the bonds of a lawful marriage;  
c. maintain life and improve her standard of living;  
d. have work that she likes and is also entitled to fair employment conditions;  
e. obtain education and teaching in all types, levels and pathways of education in accordance with the specified requirements;  
f. a healthy and clean environment;  
g. receive fair wages in accordance with her work that is comparable and commensurate with human dignity;  
h. elect and/or be elected in general elections, regional head elections, village head elections and/or other political office elections based on equal rights through direct, general, free, secret, honest and fair voting according to applicable laws and regulations;  
i. receive special protection in the performance of work or profession against matters that may threaten safety and/or health in connection with women's reproductive function; and  
j. special rights inherent to women due to their reproductive function are guaranteed and protected by law.  
2. Women who are adults and or are married have the right to perform legal acts independently unless otherwise determined by laws and regulations and other social norms.  
3. A lawful marriage can only take place upon the free will of the woman and the man in accordance with laws and regulations.  
4. A wife during the marriage bond has equal rights and responsibilities with her husband over all matters relating to their married life, relations with their children and ownership rights and management of joint property.  
5. After the dissolution of her marriage a woman has equal rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to their children, taking into account the best interests of the child.  
6. After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has equal rights with her former husband over all matters relating to joint property without reducing the rights of the child, in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.  
7. Every woman who is in a condition of Disability, elderly, and pregnant is entitled to receive facilities and special treatment.  
8. Every elderly woman, with physical and/or mental disability, is entitled to receive care, education, training and special assistance at the expense of the regional government to ensure a decent life in accordance with human dignity.

# CHAPTER V

## IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One Prevention

#### Article 8

The target of prevention as referred to in Article 4 letter a is every woman.

#### Article 9

1. Prevention as referred to in Article 4 letter a, includes:  
a. collecting data and information about women victims of violence and laws and regulations;  
b. conducting education on anti-violence values against women;  
c. socializing laws and regulations related to the implementation of protection for women victims of violence;  
d. establishing and facilitating the implementation of integrated service institutions for women victims by involving community elements;  
e. encouraging public awareness of the importance of protection for women victims of violence; 1. Increasing women's awareness of the importance of complying with ethics, religious norms and cultural norms in behavior.  
2. Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regency Regional Government, police institutions, prosecutor institutions, judicial institutions, non-governmental organizations, and the community in accordance with their duties, functions, and responsibilities.

## Part Two Handling

#### Article 10

The target of handling as referred to in Article 4 letter b is every woman.

#### Article 11

1. Forms of services for women victims of violence that are provided include:  
a. medical services, in the form of treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical personnel and paramedics;  
b. medicolegal services are forms of medical services for evidentiary purposes in the legal field;  
c. psychosocial services are services provided by companions in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims, including the provision of safe houses to protect victims from various threats and intimidation for victims and provide social support so that victims have confidence, strength, and independence in resolving their problems;  
d. legal services to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process;  
e. economic independence services in the form of services for skills training and providing economic access so that victims can be independent.  
2. The service mechanism as referred to in paragraph (1) is organized according to standard operating procedures that will be regulated in a Regent Regulation.

#### Article 12

The implementation of integrated services for women victims of violence is carried out free of charge, quickly, safely, with empathy, non-discrimination, easily accessible, and with guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 13

Integrated service providers are obliged to provide services in accordance with the service principles as referred to in Article 12.

# CHAPTER VII

## COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 22

1. The community may participate in the implementation of Empowerment and Protection of women.  
2. Participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, groups or social community organizations.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of:  
a. Responses and input in policy formulation;  
b. cooperation; and/or  
c. complaints/reports.  
4. The procedures for community participation as referred to in paragraph (3) are further regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER VIII

## INSTITUTIONS, GUIDANCE, AND SUPERVISION

## Part One Institutions

#### Article 23

1. Handling as referred to in Article 11 is carried out in an integrated manner by related SKPD and/or service institutions that handle the protection of women from acts of violence.  
2. The implementation of integrated services is coordinated by BPPKB or another name which is further regulated by Regent Regulation.  

3. The implementation of integrated services is in accordance with laws and regulations.

## Part Two Development

#### Article 24

1. Regional governments are obligated to conduct development and supervision of the implementation of protection for women victims of violence.  
2. Development as referred to in paragraph (1), includes:  
a. Guidelines and fulfillment standards;  
b. Technical development and training;  
c. Provision of facilities;  
d. Monitoring;  
e. Evaluation.  
3. Development as referred to in paragraph (2), is intended to realize the protection of women from acts of violence in accordance with minimum service standards implemented by SKPD and the community.  
4. SKPD as referred to in paragraph (3) is the Regional Work Unit within the Regional Government of Luwu Regency that is related to the protection of women against acts of violence.

## Part Three 

Supervision

#### Article 24

1. Regional governments are obligated to conduct supervision of the implementation of protection for women from acts of violence.  
2. Supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out with professional, transparent and accountable principles.  

Community

#### Article 25

may conduct supervision of the implementation of protection for women from acts of violence organized by the regional government with a mechanism for conveying aspirations to the Regent or DPRD.

# CHAPTER IX FUNDING

#### Article 26

1. Funding activities for the implementation of policies, programs and Women's Empowerment and Protection in the Region are sourced from the regional revenue and expenditure budget.  
2. In the event that the Region receives funding assistance for the implementation of policies, programs and activities for Women's Empowerment and Protection from the Government, it must be implemented in accordance with laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

## MONITORING, EVALUATION AND REPORTING

#### Article 27

1. To ensure synergy, continuity and effectiveness of integrated measures in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for Women's Empowerment and Protection, the Regional Government conducts monitoring.  
2. Monitoring as referred to in paragraph (1) is intended to determine the progress and obstacles in the implementation of policies, programs and activities for Women's Empowerment and Protection in the Region.  
3. Monitoring is conducted periodically starting from planning up to the policies, programs and activities for Women's Empowerment and Protection for the current year.  
4. Evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for Women's Empowerment and Protection is conducted at the end of each fiscal year.  
5. The results of the evaluation of the implementation of policies, programs and activities for Women's Empowerment and Protection are used as input material for the formulation of policies, programs and activities for the following year.  
6. The Regent submits a report on the implementation of integrated services for the Protection of women victims of violence to the Governor with a copy submitted to the minister in charge of women's empowerment and child protection affairs, and the minister in charge of home affairs.  
7. The Regent submits a report on the implementation of the establishment and development of task forces, forums, working groups or other institutions in an integrated manner to the minister in charge of home affairs with a copy to the minister in charge of women's empowerment and child protection affairs.  
8. The implementation of reporting as referred to in paragraph (6) and paragraph (7) is conducted annually and/or when necessary.

# CHAPTER XI

## PROHIBITIONS AND SANCTIONS

#### Article 28

Any person who intentionally commits violence against women shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with laws and regulations.
