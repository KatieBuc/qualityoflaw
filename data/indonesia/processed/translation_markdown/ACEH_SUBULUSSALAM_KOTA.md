Considering : a. that women have a strategic role in development so that improving the quality of life of women needs to receive recognition in accordance with local wisdom and the guidance of Islamic Sharia; that

b. that based on the mandate of Qanun Number 6 of 2009 concerning Women's Empowerment and Protection Articles 4 and 5 which regulate the scope of empowerment and protection, the protection of women is the authority of the City Regency Government; c. that in reality, the quality of life of women is still low and vulnerable to threats of violence, persecution, torture, domestic violence, trafficking in women, exploitation and discrimination against women which need to be immediately stopped by providing protection in accordance with human dignity;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, letter c, it is necessary to establish a Qanun of Subulussalam City concerning Women's Empowerment;

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Qanun, the following terms mean: 1. City is Subulussalam City.

2. City Government is the Mayor as an element of the administration of Regional Government who leads the implementation of governmental affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region.

3. Mayor is the Mayor of Subulussalam.

4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the regional head and DPRK in the administration of Government Affairs which are the authority of the Region.

5. Revenue and Expenditure Budget of Subulussalam City, hereinafter referred to as the City Revenue and Expenditure Budget abbreviated as APBK, is the annual financial plan of the City Government which is discussed and mutually approved by the Mayor and DPRK and established by Qanun.

6. City Medium-Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMK, is a planning document for a period of 5 (five) years.

7. Woman is an adult human being of female gender and a person who is legally recognized as a woman.

8. Woman with disability is someone who has not been able to accommodate with the surrounding environment due to having disorders, activity limitations resulting in participation restrictions.

H

9. Women's rights are all rights possessed by every woman which are human rights. 10. Empowerment is an effort to increase the capacity of women to obtain access and control over all resources in all aspects of life.

11. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security and guarantees for women's rights in all aspects of life.

12. Victim is a woman who experiences threats of violence and or violence inside and or outside the household and or becomes an object of trafficking/exploitation, and or discrimination in all its forms and aspects.

13. Violence is any act against a person, especially women, which results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually and/or domestic neglect including threats to commit acts, coercion, or unlawful deprivation of liberty, whether within or outside the scope of the household.

14. Discrimination is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of gender, which has the effect or purpose of reducing or eliminating the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or other fields by women, regardless of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women.

15. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations. 16. Community Participation is community involvement in the process of identifying problems and potentials that exist in the community, selection and decision-making regarding alternative solutions to address problems, implementation of efforts to overcome problems, involvement in the process of evaluating changes that occur.

17. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a direct line upward or downward and in a collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.

18. Public sphere is in an area or region of society or women who play a role outside the home.

19. Service is an activity and immediate action carried out by Professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.

20. Assistance is any action in the form of counseling, psychological therapy, advocacy, and spiritual guidance, for the self-strengthening of victims of violence to resolve the problems they face.

21. Institution is an agency/office/body/office within the scope of local government and/or non-governmental organizations that provide assistance.

22. Integrated Service Center, hereinafter referred to as PPT, is a generic designation that can be used by various institutions that provide services for victims of violence such as Integrated Service Center for Women's and Children's Empowerment, Safe House, Child Social Protection House, Trauma Center Protection House, Women's Social Protection House, Shelter, etc.

23. UPTD PPA is a technical and integrated service unit in handling women and children victims of violence.

#### Article 2

Women's empowerment is implemented based on the principles of:

a. respect for the values of Islamic sharia and Acehnese

custom;

b. legal certainty;

c. public interest;

d. orderly administration of government;

e. respect for human rights;

f. justice and gender equality as well as inclusion; g. professionalism, accountability and transparency; h. non-discrimination; and i. protection and fulfillment of victims' rights.

#### Article 3

Women's empowerment has the objectives of:

- a. recognizing the equality of rights legally so as to increase women's participation in the stages of development;
- b. improving the quality of life of women so that they are able to play a balanced role with men in various relevant aspects of life; providing guarantees to women to be able to fulfill their rights as human beings in all aspects of life;
- d. providing a sense of security to women in all aspects of life; and
- e. becoming a guideline for the Government of Subulussalam City in carrying out empower-
- f. ment and protection of women.

#### Article 4

(1) The empowerment and protection of women activities are carried out and coordinated by the Regional Working Unit whose scope of duties and responsibilities is in the field of women's empowerment and protection.

(2) Coordination as referred to in is carried out together with agencies, offices, paragraph (1) institutions

H education, business world and community organizations related to the empowerment and protection of women.

#### Article 5

(1) For the implementation of women's empowerment and protection, planning and supervision, budget is provided in the Revenue and Expenditure Budget of Subulussalam City (APBK).

(2) All budget for women's empowerment and protection is coordinated by the Regional Working Unit whose scope of duties and responsibilities is in the field of women's empowerment and protection.

#### Article 6

Women's empowerment is based on two pillars consisting of:

- a. Protection
- b. Empowerment

# CHAPTER II

WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 7

(1) Every woman has basic rights as a human being that must be protected, promoted, upheld and fulfilled in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations.

(2) Matters related to reproduction and nature do not prevent women from obtaining their rights in women's empowerment and protection.

#### Article 8

Women's rights as referred to in article 7 paragraph (1) include:

- a. Women obtain equal rights and obligations regarding life in marriage and family, both as parents to their children and as husband-wife partners, and there must be no coercion in marriage.
- b. Women obtain the right to education and teaching in all types, levels, and pathways of education, without any obstacles or pressure in order to improve their skills and quality of life. c. Women obtain the right to work and positions in all institutions, both government and non-government institutions, and at all levels for the purpose of increasing income and family welfare. d. Women obtain the right to special protection in the implementation of their work or profession, both in the public and domestic spheres, against matters that threaten their safety and/or health regarding reproductive functions.
- e. Women receive access to optimal and quality health information and services.

#### Article 9

(1) Women have the right to occupy political positions, both in the executive and legislative environments proportionally.

(2) Women have the right to carry out various political activities in accordance with laws and regulations.

(3) Women have the right to be actively involved in social community organizations, culture and customs for the development of themselves and their intellectual abilities.

(4) Women have the right to be nominated as legislative members by national political parties or local political parties in accordance with their capabilities.

#### Article 10

(1) Women have the right to participate fully in every process of planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation in the stages of development.

(2) To fulfill the provisions as referred to in paragraph (1), women have the right to receive capacity building.

# CHAPTER III

PROTECTION

## Part 1

Purpose and Objectives

#### Article 11

(1) Protection is intended as all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.

(2) Protection as referred to in paragraph (1) includes providing guarantees in the fulfillment of basic rights and improving access and quality of various services needed by women in the form of:

- a. Providing complaint/report handling services for victims of violence against women;
- b. Providing special services for women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, minority women, poor women, minority women, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters;
- c. Providing health services for women, women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, minority women, poor women, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters from the basic level to advanced referrals.;
- d. Providing social rehabilitation for women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, minority women, poor women, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters;
- e. Conducting law enforcement and legal assistance for women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, minority women, poor women, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters;
- f. Conducting repatriation and social reintegration by building social safety networks starting at the gampong, sub-district and city levels;
- g. Providing fulfillment of basic needs and specific needs of women;
- h. Opening information and access to all forms of protection against actions that harm women, including the use of information technology, social media;
- i. Providing protection in the implementation of work or profession against matters that can threaten the safety and or health regarding women's reproductive functions;
- j. Providing protection for women to practice their religious sharia and binding rights in the implementation of sharia; and
- k. In the implementation of their religious sharia activities as referred to in paragraph k is for women who embrace Islam and other religions.

## Part 2

Responsibility of the City Government

(1) The City Government

#### Article 12

is obliged to provide support for the development of service institutions for women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, minority women, poor women, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters, both those held by the government and private sector. (2) The City Government is obliged to:

- a. Establishment of Integrated Crisis Center (PKT) and/or Integrated Service Center (PPT) at Regional General Hospitals, Police Hospitals, Private Hospitals for the provision of services for women with disabilities, 咋 women with HIV/AIDS, women victims of violence and women victims of conflict and natural disasters; b. Establishment of Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment or UPTD PPA as a Technical Implementation Unit under the City Office of Women Empowerment, Child Protection and Family Planning; and
- c. Establishing a Shelter and Trauma Center (RPTC) as a facility to provide protection and assist women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters in psychosocial and traumatic recovery.

#### Article 13

The City Government is obliged to prepare guidelines for the implementation of protection for women, women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters not limited to:

- a. Preparation of service guidelines in Hospitals and other service units;
- b. Preparation of referral guidelines for both government and private sector;
- c. Preparation of prevention and handling guidelines at the basic service level; and
- d. Preparation of Guidelines for the Development of Community Health Centers Capable of Managing cases of violence against women

#### Article 14

(1) Every service provider institution is required to conduct disaggregated data collection on women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters who are handled in accordance with the established documentation format.

(2) The case handling mechanism can be through non-litigation and litigation channels

(3) Non-litigation channel as referred to in paragraph (2) is an effort to resolve cases of violence against women outside the court channel.

(4) Litigation channel as referred to in paragraph (2) is an effort to resolve cases of violence against women using the court channel.

#### Article 15

(1) Non-litigation pathways in handling cases of violence against women can be carried out through restorative justice mechanisms and customary court mechanisms.

(2) Gampong officials and other government officials are obligated to involve women in the process of resolving violence cases through non-litigation.

#### Article 16

(1) The city government must provide information and access to all forms of protection against actions that harm women.

(2) Forms of protection as referred to in paragraph (1) include fulfillment of basic rights, security, consultation and legal aid, psychosocial assistance, social reintegration and other necessary actions. (3) The City Government must provide free legal aid to women, women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, poor women, minority women, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters who are unable to afford it and face legal problems.

(4) Legal aid as referred to in paragraph (3) must be provided in accordance with human dignity.

#### Article 17

(1) The City Government is responsible for providing guarantees to accompanying officers to obtain access to information and assistance to women victims for the legal process they undergo.

(2) Guarantees as referred to in paragraph (1) are provided through a Mayoral Decree for Women Victim Assistance Workers.

# CHAPTER IV

EMPOWERMENT

## Part 1

Purpose and Objectives

(1) Empowerment

#### Article 18

of women, women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, minority women, poor women, victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters is intended to create conditions that enable women's potential to develop and endeavor economically, politically, socially and culturally as well as have a strategic role in decision-making.

(2) Empowerment of women, women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, minority women, poor women, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters is adapted to capacity building programs through: a. Strengthening self-reliance, namely efforts to improve knowledge, skills and work skills to more easily obtain employment;

b. The City Government conducts guidance for small industries and household industries managed by women;

c. Productive Economic Enterprises, namely efforts to cultivate entrepreneurial spirit and skills; d. Opening access to capital, marketing, networking and maintaining local production results at both national and international levels;

e. The Government guarantees and protects licenses for local products as referred to in paragraph (d) from piracy; imitation and other actions that harm local products in accordance with laws and regulations; and

f. Increasing knowledge expertise and leadership so as to provide opportunities for women to be actively involved in strategic positions at the gampong, sub-district and city levels.

#### Article 19

(1) In order to improve the quality of life of women, the City Government is responsible for:

- a. Respecting and guaranteeing women's rights without discrimination in accordance with human dignity;
- b. Implementing gender mainstreaming Establishing a gender mainstreaming working group;
- d. Increasing women's empowerment in roles, quality and capacity in the fields of:
  - 1. Religion;
  - 2. politics;
  - 3. law;
  - 4. social; and economy
- e. Strengthening and developing institutions providing women's empowerment services in the region.
  - (2) The City Government's responsibility in efforts to improve the quality of life of women as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Government which administers governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

## Part 2

Government Obligations Towards Women

#### Article 20

(1) The City Government must improve the quality of life of women starting from the gampong, sub-district and city levels. (2) The City Government must provide equal opportunities to women and men in improving their quality of life proportionally.

(3) The city government must endeavor to establish educational and training institutions or productive skills institutions for women as preparation for entering the workforce or creating independent businesses.

(4) The city government is obligated to realize the level of social welfare for women.

(5) The city government must provide facilities and infrastructure to accelerate the improvement of social welfare for women.

(6) Social welfare as referred to in paragraph (1) is the fulfillment of needs for clothing, food, shelter, education, health, employment and environmental peace/tranquility.

#### Article 21

(1) The city government provides adequate employment opportunities based on the level of education and skills possessed by women, women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, minority women, poor women, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters.

(2) The city government coordinates with agencies/institutions/business entities and employment providers to ensure partiality towards women in employment opportunities.

(3) Employment information and job seeker selection are conducted in an accountable, fair, transparent and inclusive manner. (4) The City Government may take temporary special measures by prioritizing women with disabilities, women with HIV/AIDS, minority women, poor women, women victims of violence, women victims of conflict and natural disasters.

(5) Women as referred to in paragraph (4) in obtaining employment according to their knowledge or skills by developing policy mechanisms for private companies and government as well as state-owned enterprises.

# CHAPTER VI

MONITORING, EVALUATION AND REPORTING

#### Article 25

(1) The Mayor conducts monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of women's empowerment and protection. (2) Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) aims to identify developments and obstacles in the implementation of women's empowerment and protection.

(3) Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is conducted at each SKPK and in a tiered manner among levels of government.

(4) Monitoring and evaluation are conducted before the preparation of programs or activities for the following year. (5) Implementation of monitoring and evaluation is carried out in cooperation with sub-district and gampong governments as well as civil society organizations.

(6) Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 26

(1) The Mayor is responsible for making annual reports on the implementation of women's empowerment and protection.

(2) Reporting on the implementation of women's empowerment and protection is carried out in stages starting from the kampong level.

(3) The Keuchik submits periodic reports to the Camat every three months.

(4) The Camat submits periodic reports to the Mayor through the head of Bappeda every six months.

(5) The Head of City Bappeda submits reports to the Mayor periodically every six months including reporting on the City PUG Working Group.

(6) City BAPPEDA also conducts a general evaluation of the implementation of women's empowerment and protection based on RPJMK and SKPK Renja.

(7) The Mayor submits reports on the implementation of women's empowerment and protection periodically to the Governor of Aceh every year.

(8) Reports are submitted to the Governor of Aceh with copies to the DPRK

(9) Reports as referred to in paragraph (1) are also copied to the State Minister for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection and the Minister of Home Affairs.

#### Article 27

(1) The Mayor may establish a special team to conduct monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of women's empowerment and protection.

(2) The establishment of the monitoring and evaluation team as referred to in paragraph (1) may be carried out through cooperation with Universities, Women's/Gender Study Centers or Non-Governmental Organizations.

(3) The Monitoring and Evaluation Team shall be under the coordination of or led by City BAPPEDA with members consisting of the Head of the Office of Women's Empowerment, Child Protection and Family Planning as vice chairman, one representative from each sub-district and civil society groups.

(4) The Monitoring and Evaluation Team in carrying out its duties and responsibilities shall be supported by City Bappeda.

#### Article 28

The Monitoring and Evaluation Team as stipulated in Article 27 has the following duties: conducting monitoring and evaluation of the process and results of annual planning;

b. providing input to SKPK in all matters related to Gender Mainstreaming;

c. providing input and suggestions regarding gender-responsive work programs;

d. ensuring the involvement of at least 30% women in every development process starting from planning, implementation and reporting;

e. supporting the integration of Musrembang proposals related to women's empowerment and protection into the SKPK Work Plan;

f. evaluating SKPK programs that are gender-based;

g. ensuring women receive benefits from development implementation;

h. preparing annual reports to the Mayor/Deputy

Mayor; and

i. providing recommendations for gender-perspective planning, budgeting and development implementation in the next stage.

#### Article 29

that

(1) The City Government provides a mechanism (channel) for public complaints about the achievements of women's empowerment and protection as well as the level of public service satisfaction.

(2) The facilities and infrastructure as well as the mechanism for public complaints are further regulated by Mayor Regulation.

# CHAPTER VII

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 30

(1) Every member of the community has the right to obtain opportunities to participate in women's empowerment and protection.

(2) Every member of the community has the right to obtain or provide information on violations of women's rights.

(3) The community has the right to convey suggestions and opinions in efforts for women's empowerment and protection.

# CHAPTER VIII

FUNDING

#### Article 31

(1) Funding for women's empowerment and protection in the City is sourced from the City Revenue and Expenditure Budget.

(2) Funding for women's empowerment and protection in the City may also be sourced from the Aceh Revenue and Expenditure Budget, State Revenue and Expenditure Budget.

(3) Funding for women's empowerment and protection in the City may be sourced from religious funds collected through Baitul Mal and other lawful sources.

(4) The use of religious funds as referred to in paragraph (3) is further regulated through Mayor regulations.

#### Article 32

(1) In the preparation of APBK and APBG, costs must be allocated for the benefit of enhancing women's empowerment and protection.

(2) Special autonomy funds that are part of the City must be partially allocated to finance activities as referred to in this qanun.

# CHAPTER IX

OTHER PROVISIONS

#### Article 33

To accelerate efforts for women's empowerment and protection, the Subulussalam City Government must implement special policies.

# CHAPTER X

CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 34

Matters not yet regulated in this qanun shall be further regulated by Mayor Regulation, after obtaining consideration from the Ulama Consultative Council (MPU).

#### Article 35

This qanun shall come into force on the date of promulgation.
