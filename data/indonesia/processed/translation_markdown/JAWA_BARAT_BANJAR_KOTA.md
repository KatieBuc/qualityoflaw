# MAYOR OF BANJAR

MAYOR OF BANJAR

WEST JAVA PROVINCE

LOCAL REGULATION OF BANJAR CITY

NUMBER 10 OF 2017

CONCERNING

PROTECTION OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

MAYOR OF BANJAR,

Considering : a. that every woman and child has the right to survival, to grow, and to develop properly and has the right to protection from violence and discrimination as mandated in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

b. that acts of violence against women and children in Banjar City have been increasing from year to year, making it the responsibility of the Regional Government of Banjar City to guarantee the protection, fulfillment, and respect for the rights of women and children;

c. that Banjar City does not yet have a local regulation governing the implementation of protection for women and children victims of violence, thus requiring a comprehensive legal basis in order to fulfill the rights of women and children;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in points a, b and c, it is necessary to enact a Local Regulation concerning Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;

In View of : 1. Article 18 Paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

2. Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 109, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4235) as amended several times, most recently by Government Regulation in Lieu of Law Number 1 of 2016 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2002 concerning Child Protection (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2016 Number 99, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5882);

3. Law Number 27 of 2002 concerning the Establishment of Banjar City in West Java Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2002 Number 130, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 4246);

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Local Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. City Region is the Region of Banjar City.
- 2. City Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of the Regional Government organizer who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous region.
- 3. Mayor is the Mayor of Banjar.
- 4. Regional House of Representatives, hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD, is a regional people's representative institution positioned as an element of Regional Government administration.
- 5. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Mayor and Regional House of Representatives in administering government that is the authority of the region.
- 6. Protection of women is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by giving consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
- 7. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
- 8. Woman is a person who is female by sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman.
- 9. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years, including a child still in the womb. 10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences misery and/or suffering either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring in the Region.
- 11. Violence is any act against women and children that results in misery or suffering physically, psychologically, sexually, and/or neglect, including threats to commit acts, coercion or deprivation of freedom unlawfully.
- 12. Women's empowerment is the strengthening of women victims of violence to be able to endeavor and work independently after being recovered and provided with health and social rehabilitation services.
- 13. Gender is a concept that refers to the roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural circumstances of society.
- 14. Social rehabilitation is recovery from interference with physical, psychological and social conditions so as to be able to perform their role again properly both in the family and in society.
- 15. Social reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with their families and communities.
- 16. Assistance is an activity to provide counseling, therapy and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims carried out by a person or representative from an institution that has expertise.
- 17. Prevention is the effort of the government together with families and communities as well as the private sector and/or business world to prevent the occurrence of violence through socialization, education, advocacy and promotion activities.
- 18. Handling is an action taken as soon as possible to victims when seeing, hearing, and knowing that violence against victims will occur, is occurring and/or has occurred.
- 19. Service is an activity and immediate action carried out by professionals according to their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy, and advocacy for the strengthening and recovery of victims of violence.
- 20. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in a direct line upward or downward to the third degree.
- 21. Integrated Service Center for the Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is a technical implementing unit of the Regional Apparatus that carries out technical operational activities and/or technical supporting activities for integrated services for women and children victims of violence.
- 22. Violence Victim Protection Forum, hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK, is a coordination forum for the prevention, handling and empowerment of women and child victims of violence whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner and is formed at the Regional, sub-district, and village levels.

# CHAPTER II

PRINCIPLES, FOUNDATION, AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 2

Protection of women and children victims of violence is implemented based on the principles of:

- a. respect and recognition of rights; and
- b. equal human dignity.

#### Article 3

Protection of women and children victims of violence is implemented based on the following principles:

- a. gender responsive;
- b. non-discrimination;
- c. equal and respectful relationships;
- d. maintaining privacy and confidentiality;
- e. providing a sense of safety and comfort;
- f. respecting individual differences;
- g. non-judgmental;
- h. respecting the choices and decisions of victims;
- i. sensitive to the background and conditions of victims; j. using appropriate language that is understood by victims; k. fast and simple;
- l. empathy; and
- m. best interests of victims.

#### Article 4

(1) The objectives of protection for women victims of violence are directed to:

- a. create conditions that support the development of the potential of women victims of violence;
- b. strengthen the potential already possessed by women victims of violence; and
- c. improve the work skills of women victims of violence.
  - (2) The objectives of protection for child victims of violence are directed to:
- a. provide social rehabilitation for child victims of violence;
- b. reunite child victims of violence with family and/or environment; and
- c. improve the empowerment of child victims of violence.

# CHAPTER III

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Stages

#### Article 5

The stages of protection for women victims of violence include: a. prevention of violence against women;

b. protection services for women victims of violence; and c. empowerment of women victims of violence.

## Part Two

Implementation

### Paragraph 1

Prevention of Violence Against Women

#### Article 6

(1) Prevention of violence against women as referred to in Article 5 letter a is carried out in an integrated manner coordinated by the Regional Apparatus in charge of women's empowerment and child protection.

(2) Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by involving:

- a. family;
- b. community;
- c. private sector;
- d. community institutions;
- e. social institutions; and
- f. educational institutions.

#### Article 7

Prevention of violence against women as referred to in Article 6 is implemented among others by: a. forming work networks in efforts to prevent violence against women;

b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, private sector and non-governmental organizations; and

c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of women.

### Paragraph 2

Protection Services for Women

Victims of Violence

#### Article 8

(1) Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b are provided to women residents of the Region and/or women who experience violence with the location of the incident in the Region.

(2) The implementation of Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out with the principles of:

- a. fast;
- b. safe;
- c. empathy;
- d. non-discrimination;
- e. easily accessible; and
- f. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 9

(1) The forms of Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 8 include:

- a. legal aid services;
- b. health services;
- c. social rehabilitation services;
- d. repatriation and social reintegration services;
- e. complaint, consultation, and counseling services; and/or f. assistance services.
  - (2) In addition to Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), Services are carried out by receiving and sending case referrals from/or to other service units in a network.
  - (3) The implementation of Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Apparatus in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.
  - (4) Further provisions regarding the form and procedures of Protection Services for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraphs (1) to (3) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

### Paragraph 3

Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 10

(1) Empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is implemented through efforts among others:

- a. job training; and
- b. productive economic enterprises and joint business groups. (2) The implementation of empowerment of women victims of violence is carried out with the principles of:
- a. equality and justice of rights;
- b. responsibility;
- c. effectiveness and efficiency; and
- d. respect and enforcement of human rights. (3) The implementation of empowerment of women victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the relevant Regional Apparatus in accordance with its main tasks and functions.

# CHAPTER IV

PROTECTION OF CHILD VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE

## Part One

Stages

#### Article 11

The stages of protection for child victims of violence include: a. prevention of violence against children;

b. handling of child victims of violence; and

c. rehabilitation of child victims of violence.

## Part Two

Implementation

### Paragraph 1

Prevention of Violence against Children

#### Article 12

(1) Prevention of Violence against Children as referred to in Article 11 letter a is implemented by:

- a. forming work networks in efforts to Prevent Violence against Children;
- b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of Violence Prevention based on partnership patterns with the community, private sector and Non-Governmental Organizations; and
- c. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the Protection of Child Victims of Violence.
  - (2) Prevention of Violence against Children as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out in an integrated manner and coordinated by the Regional Apparatus in charge of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

#### Article 13

Efforts to prevent Violence against Children as referred to in Article 12 are carried out by involving: a. parents and/or family;

b. community;

c. educational institutions;

d. child social welfare institutions; and/or

e. other institutions engaged in child protection care.

Handling of Child Victims of Violence

- b. provincial government;
- c. other regency/city governments;
- d. village government;
- e. non-governmental organizations;
- f. private parties;
- g. community; and/or

(2) In the Rehabilitation services for Child Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1), the City Regional Government may cooperate with:

- a. vertical agencies;
- b. provincial government;
- c. other regency/city governments;

- e. non-governmental organizations;

# CHAPTER V

VICTIMS' RIGHTS

#### Article 16

(1) Every Victim has the right to:

- a. the right to have their dignity and worth as a human being respected;
- b. the right to health and psychological recovery;
- c. the right to determine their own decisions;
- d. the right to obtain information;
- e. the right to confidentiality of their identity;
- f. the right to compensation from the perpetrator;
- g. the right to Social Rehabilitation;
- h. the right to complaint handling;
- i. the right to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or
- j. the right to assistance.
  - (2) In addition to the rights as referred to in paragraph (1), Child Victims of Violence have the right to:
- a. the right to respect for survival, growth and development;
- b. the right to basic services;
- c. the right to equal protection;
- d. the right to be free from various stigmas; and/or
- e. the right to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER VI

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

OF THE REGIONAL GOVERNMENT

#### Article 17

(1) The City Regional Government implements efforts to prevent the occurrence of violence against Women and Children in the form of:

- a. collecting data and organizing an information system for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence;
- b. conducting education on anti-violence values toward Women and Children; and
- c. socializing laws and regulations related to the Empowerment of Women and Protection of Child Victims of Violence.
  - (2) The City Regional Government is obligated and responsible to provide and organize services for Victims in the form of:
- a. establishing and organizing technical service institutions for Victims involving community elements; and
- b. encouraging community awareness of the importance of protection for Victims of violence.

# CHAPTER VII

INSTITUTIONS

## Part One

General

#### Article 18

Technical service institutions for Victims are implemented by P2TP2A and FPKK coordinated by the Agency that administers the affairs of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

## Part Two

P2TP2A

#### Article 19

(1) The provision of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.

#### Article 20

(1) P2TP2A provides temporary services and protection in the form of safe houses.

(2) In the event that P2TP2A does not yet have a safe house as referred to in paragraph (1), victims are referred to other integrated service centers that have safe houses.

#### Article 21

(1) P2TP2A consists of several counseling fields.

(2) The counseling fields as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of at least:

- a. law;
- b. health;
- c. spiritual; and
- d. psychology.
  - (3) The implementation of counseling is carried out by counselors and may be carried out in a networked manner involving related parties.

#### Article 22

(1) Forms of services to Victims organized by P2TP2A include:

- a. medical services;
- b. medico-legal services;
- c. psychosocial services;
- d. legal services;
- e. social reintegration services; and
- f. economic independence services.
  - (2) Provisions regarding the mechanism of services to victims organized by P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

## Part Three

FPKK

#### Article 23

(1) In order to protect and guarantee the rights of women and children from acts of violence, FPKK is established.

(2) FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of: a. City Regional FPKK;

b. Sub-district FPKK; and

c. Village FPKK.

(3) Provisions regarding the procedures for establishment, organizational structure, and elements of FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) are regulated by Mayor Regulation.

#### Article 24

The duties and functions of FPKK as referred to in Article 23 consist of:

- a. coordinating and synchronizing prevention, services, and empowerment for women and children victims of violence;
- b. maintaining and developing FPKK in the prevention, services, and empowerment of victims in a networked manner and referral system;
- c. conducting education on non-discrimination values toward women and children;
- d. socializing laws and regulations related to the implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence;
- e. collecting, compiling, and presenting data reports on victims of violence; and
- f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of Protection for Women and Children Victims of Violence.

# CHAPTER VIII

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 25

(1) The community may participate in the protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence. (2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out through:

- a. provision of safe houses and/or shelters; b. establishment and management of children's orphanages;
- c. establishment of child rehabilitation facilities;
- d. active supervision of violence against women and children and reporting to the competent authorities;
- e. providing first aid to victims; f. reporting the occurrence of violence against women and children and providing protection;
- g. forming family partners at the village level by the community; and
- h. other forms of community participation related to the implementation of women's protection and child protection.

#### Article 26

(1) In addition to P2TP2A as referred to in Article 20, the community may form forums or groups at the sub-district and village levels that function as a network of P2TP2A in the framework of implementing efforts for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence.

(2) The formation of forums or groups in villages as referred to in paragraph (1) may be facilitated by the Village Government.

# CHAPTER IX

MONITORING, EVALUATION, AND REPORTING

#### Article 27

Monitoring, Evaluation, and Reporting of the Protection of women and children victims of violence are coordinated by the Regional Apparatus responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

#### Article 28

(1) The Regional Apparatus responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection conducts monitoring, evaluation, and reporting of the results of activities for the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence on a regular basis.

(2) Reporting of activity results as referred to in paragraph (1) is submitted to the Mayor through the Regional Apparatus responsible for regional development planning.

(3) The implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in paragraph (1) involves the forum for handling victims of violence against women and children in a tiered manner.

# CHAPTER X

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 29

(1) Guidance and supervision of the implementation of the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence are carried out by the Regional Apparatus responsible for Women's Empowerment and Child Protection.

(2) Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) include:

- a. coordination;
- b. guidance;
- c. education and training; and
- d. monitoring and evaluation.
  - (3) Coordination as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a covers aspects related to planning and implementation.
  - (4) Guidance as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b covers aspects related to planning, implementation, procedures, funding, quality, control and supervision.
  - (5) Education and training as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c are carried out in accordance with laws and regulations. (6) Monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (2) letter d are carried out regularly every 3 (three) months.

CLOSING PROVISIONS

I. GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children have shown an increasing trend. Economic factors, social environment and technological development have a major influence on the increase in such acts of violence.

This condition requires the Regional Government to take concrete steps through activity programs as an effort to prevent and protect women and children from acts of violence. One of the efforts to prevent violence against women is carried out through women's empowerment and optimization of previously possessed potential, so that they are expected to be able to improve and develop themselves to play a role and be protected from potential acts of violence. This condition will strengthen the form of appreciation and respect for women while maintaining their role and nature as women to play a role in educating and supporting the welfare and happiness of their families. Efforts to prevent violence against children are the obligation of parents, families, communities and the private sector.

Community services are the frontline when potential violence against children occurs. The mechanism of violence prevention efforts and protection of child victims of violence involves multiple sectors coordinated by the Regional Government. These efforts are carried out with the aim of providing social rehabilitation for child victims of violence, reuniting child victims of violence with their families and/or environment, and increasing the empowerment of child victims of violence.

In connection with this matter, to realize the provision of protection efforts for women and children victims of violence as well as for legal certainty in the implementation of women's empowerment and protection of child victims of violence in the Regional area, it is necessary to regulate it in a Regional Regulation.
