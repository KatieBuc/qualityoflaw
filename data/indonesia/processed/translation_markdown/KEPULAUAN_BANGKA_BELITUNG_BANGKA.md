CONCERNING

THE IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION BY THE GRACE OF GOD ALMIGHTY

REGENT OF BANGKA,

Considering : a. that women's empowerment is carried out so that women can actualize their potential optimally to participate in development in accordance with their nature and capacity;

b. that women as national assets who play a role in the process of continuation and preparation of quality generations, need to be guaranteed their rights and protection from acts of violence and discrimination in order to build society, nation and state;

c. that based on the provisions of Article 12 paragraph (2) letter b of Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government, it is mandated that women's empowerment is a mandatory affair carried out by the Regency Regional Government;

d. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b, and c, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Implementation of Women's Empowerment and Protection;

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:

- 1. Regency is the Regency of Bangka.
- 2. Regional Government is the Regent as the element of Regional Government administration that leads the implementation of Government affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region.
- 3. Regent is the Regent of Bangka.
- 4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the Regional House of Representatives of the Regency of Bangka.
- 5. Regional Secretary is the Regional Secretary of the Regency of Bangka. 6. Regional Apparatus is the auxiliary element of the Regent and Regional House of Representatives in the administration of Government affairs which are the authority of the Region.
- 7. Human Rights are a set of rights inherent to the nature and existence of human beings as creatures of the Almighty God and are His gifts that must be respected, upheld and protected by the state, law, Government, and every person for the honor and protection of human dignity.
- 8. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social capabilities, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective capabilities.
- 9. Women's Protection is every effort aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
- 10. Discrimination Against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex which has the effect or purpose of impairing or nullifying the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women.
- 11. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
- 12. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, socio-cultural, defense and security activities and equality in enjoying development outcomes.
- 13. Community is individuals, families, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.
- 14. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, husband-wife and their children, or father and his children, or mother and her children.
- 15. Labor is every person who is capable of performing work to produce goods and/or services either to meet their own needs or for the community.
- 16. Worker/Laborer is every person who works by receiving wages or remuneration in other forms.
- 17. Elderly Women are persons who have reached 60 years of age and above.
- 18. Women with Disabilities are women who experience physical and/or mental obstacles that disrupt their activities and social interactions normally.
- 19. Homeless Women are women who do not have a permanent residence and for various reasons must live under bridges, public parks, roadsides or various other public facilities to sleep and carry out daily life.
- 20. Domestic Women Workers are persons who work for a person or several persons in a household to perform household work by receiving wages.
- 21. Women Heads of Households are women who, for various reasons, are left by their husbands, and finally act or contribute and function to carry out their roles and responsibilities as heads of households.
- 22. Women Former Inmates of Correctional Institutions are women who have served sentences in correctional institutions. 23. Women Victims of Disasters are women who suffer or die as a result of natural and social disasters.
- 24. Women Commercial Sex Workers are women who work as sex workers by their own will and/or are prostituted. 25. Women Victims of Violence are women who experience physical, mental suffering and/or economic losses caused by a criminal act.

#### Article 2

The scope of this Regional Regulation covers:

- a. women's rights;
- b. the obligations and responsibilities of the Regency Regional Government; c. women's empowerment;
- d. women's protection;
- e. strategies for women's empowerment and protection;
- f. implementation of women's protection;
- g. mechanisms for organizing women's empowerment and protection; h. financing; and
- i. guidance and supervision.

#### Article 3

Women's Empowerment and Protection is implemented based on the principles of: a. respect for human rights;

b. justice and gender equality;

c. non-discrimination; and

d. the best interests of women.

#### Article 4

The purpose of this Regional Regulation is:

- a. to empower women both individually and in groups and the wider community to be able to live more independently; and b. to prevent and address risks of violence and vulnerability of women so that their survival can be fulfilled in accordance with the principles of equality and justice.

# CHAPTER II

WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 5

(1) Every woman has the right to:

- a. live and maintain life and improve her standard of living;
- b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue her offspring;
- c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life to become a human being who is faithful, pious, responsible, of noble character, happy, and prosperous in accordance with human rights;
- d. obtain justice, a sense of security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;
- e. be involved in every stage of the development process;
- f. be free from slavery/servitude and threats;
- g. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;
- h. obtain welfare and a decent life;
- i. participate in politics;
- j. perform legal acts; and
- k. freely choose a partner in her marriage.
  - (2) Every woman during marriage has the same rights and responsibilities as her husband in all matters relating to her marital life, relationships with her children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with laws and regulations.
  - (3) After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities with her former husband in all matters relating to children with due regard to the best interests of the child.

# CHAPTER III

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE REGENCY REGIONAL GOVERNMENT Article 6

(1) The Regency Regional Government is obligated and responsible for women's empowerment and protection.

(2) Efforts for women's empowerment and protection as referred to in paragraph (1) include:

- a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;
- b. determination of implementation guidelines;
- c. provision of services; and
- d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.
  - (3) Efforts for women's empowerment and protection as referred to in paragraph (2) are carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER IV

WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 7

Women's empowerment is directed to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of: a. economy;

b. socio-culture;

c. politics; and

d. law.

#### Article 8

Women's empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 7 letter a is implemented through:

- a. provision of skills and job training;
- b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups; c. facilitation of the strengthening and development of productive economic business groups; d. facilitation and capital assistance; and
- e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 9

Women's empowerment in the social and cultural field as referred to in Article 7 letter b is implemented through:

- a. enhancement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;
- b. enhancement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome; c. health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts primarily in the field of reproductive health; d. enhancement of awareness and knowledge about family planning; e. independence, health and prosperity including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and
- f. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and develop science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 10

(1) Women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in Article 7 letter c includes:

- a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;
- b. provision of opportunities for women to hold public office;
- c. participation in general elections; and
- d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.
  - (2) Women's empowerment in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 11

(1) Women's empowerment in the legal field as referred to in Article 7 letter d includes:

- a. enhancement of awareness and knowledge in the legal field through communication, information and education services; and
- b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.
  - (2) Women's empowerment in the legal field as referred to in paragraph (1) is implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER V

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

#### Article 12

(1) Protection of women includes:

- a. social protection;
- b. economic protection; and
- c. legal protection.
  - (2) Social protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is to ensure the survival of women in the form of social protection in order to ensure the fulfillment of decent living needs.
  - (3) Economic protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is to prevent and address the risk of vulnerability resulting from shocks and social vulnerability of individuals, families, groups and/or communities for the survival of women.
  - (4) Legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c is implemented in terms of providing protection to women who become witnesses and victims of acts of violence and discrimination.

# CHAPTER VI

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 13

Implementation of protection of women is provided to:

- a. women workers/laborers;
- b. elderly women;
- c. women with disabilities;
- d. homeless women;
- e. women domestic workers;
- f. women heads of household;
- g. women former inmates of correctional institutions; h. women disaster victims;
- i. women commercial sex workers; and
- j. women victims of violence.

## Part Two

Women Workers/Laborers

#### Article 14

(1) Implementation of protection for women workers/laborers as referred to in Article 13 letter a is to provide protection from acts of economic exploitation and cruel, inhumane treatment and to seek proper appreciation for their work.

(2) Women workers/laborers as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection for:

- a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;
- b. health and social security;
- c. access to information and legal consultation services;
- d. opportunities to acquire knowledge and skills to improve status; and
- e. witnesses and victims.

## Part Three

Elderly Women

#### Article 15

(1) Implementation of protection for elderly women as referred to in Article 13 letter b is in order to extend life expectancy so they can perform their social functions and play an active role naturally in their social life.

(2) Elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection rights for:

- a. basic health services to maintain and improve health status including psychological services and diseases related to the elderly (gerontological/geriatric);;
- b. use of facilities and accessibility of public facilities and infrastructure; and
- c. government administrative services.

## Part Four

Women with Disabilities

#### Article 16

(1) Implementation of protection for women with disabilities as referred to in Article 13 letter c is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination including freedom from cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and freedom from exploitation.

(2) Women with disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection rights for:

- a. guarantee of continuity and personal self-development; b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, sports, arts and culture as well as utilization of information and communication technology;
- c. accessibility of information, legal aid, mobility, social services; and d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

## Part Five

Homeless Women

#### Article 17

(1) Implementation of protection for homeless women as referred to in Article 13 letter d is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.

(2) Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection rights for:

- a. decent housing;
- b. health and social security;
- c. population administrative registration;
- d. opportunities to acquire skills to improve economic status; and
- e. access to information and legal consultation services.

## Part Six

Women Domestic Workers

#### Article 18

(1) Implementation of Protection for women domestic workers as referred to in Article 13 letter e is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation in employment relationships.

(2) Women domestic workers as referred to in paragraph (1)receive protection for:

- a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;
- b. health and social security;
- c. access to information and legal consultation services; and
- d. opportunities to acquire knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Seven

Women Heads of Household

#### Article 19

(1) Implementation of Protection for women heads of household as referred to in Article 13 letter f is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, negative stigma and economic empowerment for women heads of household.

(2) Women heads of household as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection rights for:

- a. acts of discrimination and efforts to weaken the function of women as the economic support of the family;
- b. strengthening of capacity and recognition as head of household in their environment; and
- c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.

## Part Eight

Women Former Inmates of Correctional Institutions Article 20

(1) The Implementation of Protection for women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in Article 13 letter g is intended to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting to life in society, so that they can carry out life normally.

(2) Women former inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:

- a. rehabilitation and social reintegration; and
- b. opportunities to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Nine

Women Victims of Disaster

#### Article 21

(1) The Implementation of Protection for women victims of disaster as referred to in Article 13 letter h is intended to provide protection during disasters and post-disaster.

(2) Women victims of disaster as referred to in paragraph (1) shall receive protection for:

- a. access to proper housing;
- b. health services;
- c. information access services and legal consultation; and
- d. post-disaster recovery.

## Part Ten

Women Commercial Sex Workers

#### Article 22

(1) The Implementation of Protection for women commercial sex workers as referred to in Article 13 letter i is intended to provide health protection and improvement of life skills as capital to improve social status.

(2) Protection for women commercial sex workers as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

- a. providing life skills capabilities;
- b. facilitating reproductive health services; and
- c. preventing human trafficking.

## Part Eleven

Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 23

(1) The Implementation of Protection for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 13 letter j is intended to provide legal protection and/or trauma and health recovery.

(2) Protection for Women Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

- a. legal aid;
- b. assistance following acts of violence;
- c. rehabilitation and social reintegration; and
- d. trauma and health services.

# CHAPTER VII

STRATEGY FOR WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND PROTECTION Article 24

(1) Women's empowerment and protection shall be implemented by integrating gender mainstreaming and gender-disaggregated data in an integrated manner based on applicable laws and regulations.

(2) In women's empowerment and protection, a Working Group and Integrated Services shall be established as determined by Regent's Decree. (3) Women's empowerment and protection shall be coordinated by the Agency in charge of Women's Empowerment.

# CHAPTER VIII

MECHANISM FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT AND

PROTECTION

#### Article 25

The mechanism for implementing women's empowerment and protection shall be carried out through the following stages:

- a. formulation of policy for planning programs and activities; b. budgeting of programs and activities;
- c. implementation of programs and activities; and
- d. monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

#### Article 26

(1) The formulation of policy for planning programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter a shall be implemented through:

- a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of data; b. coordination and synchronization of planning of programs and activities with related Regional Apparatus; and
- c. determination of programs and activities in planning documents. (2) The formulation of policy for planning programs and activities shall be further regulated in minimum service standards and standard operating procedures.
  - (3) The formulation of policy for planning programs and activities for the implementation of women's empowerment and protection shall be coordinated by the Regional Apparatus in charge of Regency development planning.
  - (4) The budgeting of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter b shall be implemented through general budget policy as set forth in the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget.
  - (5) The implementation of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter c shall be implemented through:
- a. implementation of programs and activities as set forth in the budget documents of Regional Apparatus; and
- b. coordination and synchronization of implementation of programs and activities with related Regional Apparatus.
  - (6) The implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in Article 25 letter d shall be implemented through:
- a. conducting periodic monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of programs and activities that have been carried out; and b. preparing periodic reports.

# CHAPTER IX

FINANCING

#### Article 27

All costs for the implementation of policy for planning programs and activities related to Women's Empowerment and Protection shall be sourced from: a. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget;

b. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and

c. Other lawful and non-binding sources in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER X

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 28

(1) The Regent shall conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of women's empowerment and protection.

(2) The form of guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) is as follows:

- a. providing socialization to the public and business world regarding; b. policies on women's empowerment and protection;
- c. providing books, leaflets, brochures regarding women's empowerment and protection, and other related issues and disseminating them to the public;
- d. facilitating capacity building of institutions implementing women's empowerment and protection;
- e. facilitating forums for women's participation and women's organizations; f. facilitating rehabilitation and reintegration services for women victims of disaster and victims of violence;
- g. coordinating the participation of the business world and other institutions, including international assistance for women's empowerment and protection; and
- h. providing awards to the public and business world both individually and institutionally who have made efforts for women's empowerment and protection.
  - (3) The form of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of monitoring and evaluation activities on women's empowerment and protection.

# CHAPTER XI

CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation. In order that everyone may know of it, the promulgation of this Regional Regulation is ordered by placement in the Regional Gazette of Bangka Regency.
