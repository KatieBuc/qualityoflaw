# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms are defined as:

- 1. Region is Semarang Regency.
- 2. Regional Government is the administration of government affairs by the Regional Government and Regional House of Representatives based on the principles of autonomy and assistance tasks with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
- 3. Regional Government is the Regent and regional apparatus as elements of the Regional Government administration.
- 4. Semarang Regency Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is a regional people's representative institution as an element of regional government administration.
- 5. Regent is the Regent of Semarang.
- 6. Regional Apparatus Work Unit in charge hereinafter referred to as SKPD in charge is a Regional Apparatus Work Unit that has main duties and functions in accordance with the relevant field.
- 7. Human Rights are a set of rights inherent in the nature and existence of human beings as creatures of Almighty God and are His gift that must be respected, upheld and protected by the state, law, Government, and every person for the sake of honor and protection of human dignity and worth.
- 8. Women's Empowerment is every effort to improve physical, mental, spiritual, social capacity, knowledge, and skills so that women are ready to be utilized according to their respective capacities.
- 9. Women's Protection is all efforts aimed at protecting women and providing a sense of security in the fulfillment of their rights by providing consistent and systematic attention aimed at achieving gender equality.
- 10. Discrimination Against Women is any distinction, exclusion or restriction made on the basis of sex which has the effect or purpose of impairing or nullifying the recognition, enjoyment or exercise of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural, civil or any other field by women, regardless of their marital status, on the basis of equality between men and women.
- 11. Gender is a concept that refers to the differentiation of roles and responsibilities of men and women that occur as a result of and can be changed by the social and cultural conditions of society.
- 12. Gender Equality is the equality of conditions for men and women to obtain opportunities and rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in political, economic, social, cultural, defense and security activities, and equality in enjoying the results of development.
- 13. Community is individuals, families, groups and social organizations and/or community organizations.
- 14. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, husband-wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children.
- 15. Labor Force is every person capable of performing work to produce goods and/or services either to meet their own needs or for society.
- 16. Worker / Laborer is every person who works by receiving wages or compensation in other forms.

# CHAPTER II

SCOPE

#### Article 2

The scope of this Regional Regulation includes

a. principles and objectives;

b. women's rights;

c. obligations and responsibilities of regional government;

d. women's empowerment;

e. women's protection;

f. implementation of women's protection;

g. strategy for women's empowerment and protection;

h. mechanism for organizing women's empowerment and protection; i. financing;

j. guidance and supervision;

k. transitional provisions; and

l. closing provisions.

# CHAPTER III

PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

#### Article 3

Women's Empowerment and Protection is implemented based on the following principles:

- a. respect for human rights;
- b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination; and
- d. best interests of women.

#### Article 4

The objectives of this Regional Regulation are:

- a. to empower women both individually and in groups and society at large so that they are able to live more independently; and b. to prevent and address the risks of violence and vulnerability of women so that their survival can be fulfilled in accordance with the principles of equality and justice.

# CHAPTER IV

WOMEN'S RIGHTS

#### Article 5

(1) Every woman has the right to:

- a. live and maintain life and improve her standard of living;
- b. fulfill her basic needs to grow and develop properly, have a family and continue her lineage;
- c. develop her personality, to obtain education, and improve her quality of life in order to become a person who is faithful, devout, responsible, of noble character, happy, and prosperous in accordance with human rights;
- d. obtain justice, security, and freedom to express opinions without discrimination;
- e. be involved in every stage of the development process;
- f. be free from slavery or enslavement and threats;
- g. obtain protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property rights;
- h. obtain welfare and a decent life;
- i. participate in politics;
- j. perform legal acts; and
- k. freely choose a partner in her marriage.
  - (2) Every wife during the marriage bond has the same rights and responsibilities as her husband over all matters relating to their married life, relations with their children, and ownership and management of joint property in accordance with the prevailing laws and regulations.
  - (3) After the dissolution of marriage, a woman has rights and responsibilities with her former husband over all matters relating to the children with due regard to the best interests of the children.

# CHAPTER V

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF REGIONAL GOVERNMENT Article 6

(1) The Regional Government is obligated and responsible for the empowerment and protection of women.

(2) Efforts for women's empowerment and protection as referred to in paragraph (1) include:

- a. determination of policies, programs, and activities;
- b. determination of implementation guidelines;
- c. provision of services; and
- d. coordination of policies, programs and activities.
  - (3) Efforts for women's empowerment and protection as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be carried out in an integrated manner in accordance with the prevailing laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VI

WOMEN'S EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 7

Women's empowerment is directed towards obtaining opportunities and rights as human beings in order to be able to play a role and participate in the fields of:

- a. economy;
- b. social culture;
- c. politics; and
- d. law.

#### Article 8

Women's empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 7 letter a is implemented through:

- a. provision of skills and job training;
- b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic enterprise groups; c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic enterprise groups; d. facilitation and capital assistance; and
- e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 9

Women's empowerment in the socio-cultural field as referred to in Article 7 letter b is implemented through:

- a. improvement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;
- b. raising awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health;
- c. raising awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and
- d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and develop science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of women.

#### Article 10

(1) Empowerment of women in the political field as referred to in Article 7 letter c includes:

- a. involvement of women in decision-making at various levels;
- b. providing opportunities for women to hold public positions;
- c. participation in general elections; and
- d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.
  - (2) Empowerment of women in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 11

(1) Empowerment of women in the legal field as referred to in Article 7 letter d includes:

- a. increasing awareness and knowledge in the legal field through communication, information and education services;
- b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.
  - (2) Empowerment of women in the legal field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

#### Article 12

(1) Protection of women includes:

- a. social protection;
- b. economic protection;
- c. legal protection.
  - (2) Social protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter a is to ensure the survival of women in the form of social protection in order to ensure the fulfillment of decent living needs.
  - (3) Economic protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter b is to prevent and address the risk of vulnerability resulting from shocks and social vulnerability of a person, family, group and/or community for the survival of women.
  - (4) Legal protection as referred to in paragraph (1) letter c is implemented in terms of providing protection to women who become witnesses and victims of acts of violence and discrimination.

# CHAPTER VIII

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION OF WOMEN

## Part One

General

#### Article 13

Implementation of protection of women is provided to:

- a. women workers/laborers;
- b. elderly women;
- c. women with disabilities;
- d. homeless women;
- e. women domestic workers;
- f. women heads of household;
- g. former women inmates of correctional institutions; h. women disaster victims;
- i. women commercial sex workers; and
- j. women victims of violence.

## Part Two

Women Workers/Laborers

#### Article 14

(1) Implementation of protection for women workers/laborers as referred to in Article 13 letter a is to provide protection from acts of economic exploitation and cruel, inhumane treatment and to seek proper appreciation for work.

(2) Women workers/laborers as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection on:

- a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;
- b. health and social security;
- c. access to information and legal consultation services;
- d. opportunity to obtain knowledge and skills to improve status; and
- e. witnesses and victims.

## Part Three

Elderly Women

#### Article 15

(1) Implementation of protection for elderly women as referred to in Article 13 letter b is in order to extend life expectancy so as to be able to carry out their social functions and play an active role naturally in their social life.

(2) Elderly women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection on:

- a. basic health services to maintain and improve health status including gerontological/geriatric services;
- b. use of facilities and accessibility of public facilities and infrastructure; and c. government administrative services.

## Part Four

Women with Disabilities

#### Article 16

(1) Implementation of protection for women with disabilities as referred to in Article 13 letter c is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination including being free from cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and being free from exploitation.

(2) Women with disabilities as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection on:

- a. guarantee of continuity and personal self-development; b. participation in the fields of education, health, employment, politics, sports, arts and culture as well as the utilization of information and communication technology; c. accessibility of information, legal aid, mobility, social services; and d. early intervention efforts including treatment and rehabilitation to improve their function and capacity.

## Part Five

Homeless Women

#### Article 17

(1) Implementation of protection for homeless women as referred to in Article 13 letter d is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of violence caused by the absence of shelter or permanent residence.

(2) Homeless women as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection on:

- a. decent housing;
- b. health and social security;
- c. population administration registration;
- d. opportunity to obtain skills to improve economic status; and
- e. access to information and legal consultation services.

## Part Six

Women Domestic Workers

#### Article 18

(1) Implementation of Protection for women domestic workers as referred to in Article 13 letter e is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, cruel, inhumane treatment, degrading human dignity, and exploitation in employment relationships.

(2) Women domestic workers as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection on:

- a. recognition of rights, wages and decent working conditions;
- b. health and social security;
- c. access to information and legal consultation services; and
- d. opportunity to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Seven

Women Heads of Household

#### Article 19

(1) Implementation of Protection for women heads of household as referred to in Article 13 letter f is in order to provide protection and prevention against forms of discrimination, negative stigma and economic empowerment for women heads of household.

(2) Women heads of household as referred to in paragraph (1) receive the right to protection on:

- a. acts of discrimination and efforts to weaken the function of women as the economic support of the family;
- b. strengthening capacity and recognition as head of household in their environment; and
- c. accessibility to economic and financial institutions.

## Part Eight

Former Women Inmates

of Correctional Institutions

#### Article 20

(1) Implementation of Protection for former women inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in Article 13 letter g is in order to provide protection from obstacles to adjusting to community life, so as to be able to carry out life normally.

(2) Former women inmates of Correctional Institutions as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection on:

- a. social rehabilitation and reintegration; and
- b. opportunity to obtain knowledge and skills to improve economic status.

## Part Nine

Women Disaster Victims

#### Article 21

(1) Implementation of Protection for women disaster victims as referred to in Article 13 letter h is in order to provide protection during disasters and post-disaster.

(2) Women disaster victims as referred to in paragraph (1) receive protection on:

- a. access to decent housing;
- b. health services;
- c. information access and legal consultation services; and
- d. post-disaster recovery.

## Part Ten

Women Commercial Sex Workers

#### Article 22

(1) Implementation of Protection for female commercial sex workers as referred to in Article 13 letter i in order to provide health protection and improvement of life skills capabilities as capital to improve social status.

(2) Protection for female commercial sex workers as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

- a. providing life skills capabilities;
- b. facilitating reproductive health services; and
- c. preventing the occurrence of human trafficking.

## Part Eleven

Female Victims of Violence

#### Article 23

(1) Implementation of Protection for Female Victims of Violence as referred to in Article 13 letter j in order to provide legal protection and/or health recovery.

(2) Protection for Female Victims of Violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

- a. legal assistance;
- b. assistance after the occurrence of acts of violence;
- c. rehabilitation and social reintegration; and
- d. health services.

# CHAPTER IX

STRATEGIES FOR EMPOWERMENT AND

PROTECTION OF WOMEN

#### Article 24

(1) Empowerment and protection of women is implemented by integrating gender mainstreaming and gender-disaggregated data in an integrated manner based on applicable laws and regulations.

(2) In the empowerment and protection of women, a Working Group and Integrated Services shall be established as stipulated by Regent's Decree.

(3) Empowerment and protection of women is coordinated by the Agency handling Women's Empowerment.

# CHAPTER X

MECHANISM FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF EMPOWERMENT

AND PROTECTION OF WOMEN

#### Article 25

The mechanism for implementation of empowerment and protection of women is carried out through stages:

- a. formulation of policy for planning programs and activities; b. budgeting of programs and activities;
- c. implementation of programs and activities; and
- d. monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

#### Article 26

(1) Formulation of policy for planning programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter a is implemented through:

- a. mapping, collection, processing and presentation of data; b. coordination and synchronization of formulation of planning of programs and activities with related SKPD; and
- c. stipulation of programs and activities in planning documents.
  - (2) Formulation of policy for planning programs and activities is further regulated in minimum service standards and standard operating procedures.
  - (3) Formulation of policy for planning programs and activities for implementation of empowerment and protection of women is coordinated by the SKPD in charge of regional development planning.
  - (4) Budgeting of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter b is implemented through general budget policy as set forth in the APBD.
  - (5) Implementation of programs and activities as referred to in Article 25 letter c is implemented through:
- a. implementation of programs and activities as set forth in the SKPD budget document; and
- b. coordination and synchronization of implementation of programs and activities with related SKPD.
  - (6) Implementation of monitoring, evaluation and reporting as referred to in Article 25 letter d is implemented through:
- a. conducting monitoring and evaluation periodically on the implementation of programs and activities that have been implemented; and
- b. making reports periodically.

# CHAPTER XI

FINANCING

#### Article 27

All costs of Implementation of policy for planning programs and activities related to Empowerment and Protection of Women are sourced from: a. State Revenue and Expenditure Budget;

b. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget; and

c. Other sources that are legal and non-binding

# CHAPTER XII

GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 28

(1) The Regent or SKPD in charge of women's empowerment conducts guidance and supervision over empowerment and protection of women.

(2) The form of guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) is as follows:

- a. providing socialization to the community and business sector regarding policies on empowerment and protection of women;
- b. providing books, leaflets, brochures regarding empowerment and protection of women, and other related issues as well as disseminating them to the community;
- c. facilitation of capacity building of institutions implementing empowerment and protection of women;
- d. facilitation of forums for women's participation and women's organizations; e. facilitation of rehabilitation and reintegration services for female victims of disasters and victims of violence;
- f. coordinating the role and participation of the business sector and other institutions, including international assistance for empowerment and protection of women; and
- g. providing awards to the community and business sector both individually and institutionally that have carried out efforts for empowerment and protection of women.
  - (3) The form of supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) may be in the form of monitoring and evaluation activities over empowerment and protection of women.

# CHAPTER XIII

TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 29

At the time of the enactment of this Regional Regulation, all provisions of regional laws and regulations related to the Implementation of Empowerment and Protection of Women that have existed thus far, shall remain in force insofar as they are not in conflict with the provisions in this Regional Regulation.

I. GENERAL

The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia mandates the authority of regional governments to regulate and manage their own governmental affairs directed towards accelerating the realization of public welfare through improved services, empowerment and community participation. The authority of Regional Government is to make regional policies to provide services, increase participation, initiative, and community empowerment aimed at improving people's welfare.

The implementation of regional autonomy must always be oriented towards improving community welfare by always paying attention to the interests and aspirations that grow in the community. Therefore, governmental affairs relating to basic services such as basic education, health, fulfillment of minimum living needs, basic environmental infrastructure must be fulfilled.

Human rights are fundamental rights that are inherently attached to human beings by nature, are universal and enduring, so they must be protected, respected, maintained, and must not be ignored, reduced, or seized by anyone. In the context of Enforcement of Human Rights (HAM) to guarantee justice for every individual, efforts are made to prevent all forms of unbalanced treatment or that are considered detrimental to other parties, whether between individuals, between groups of individuals, or between individuals and between government and society, including the process of marginalization of women that makes women vulnerable to acts of violence, discrimination and exploitation in both domestic and public domains.

Women have the same and equal rights as men, however in some discriminatory conditions, the government is obliged to implement affirmative action so that the rights of women and women in vulnerable groups need to receive protection so that they do not experience violence and can live decent lives in accordance with the principles of humanity, equality and justice.

Women's empowerment and women's protection are implemented in order to realize gender equality and justice. To carry out these affairs, efforts are needed to increase the capacity of women's resources and strengthen gender mainstreaming institutions, through gender mainstreaming strategies and gender-disaggregated data.

The Government of Semarang Regency, as the party that has the authority to regulate and protect the citizens of Semarang Regency, deems it necessary to continuously undertake and formulate regulations in order to provide guarantees for women's empowerment and women's protection in Semarang Regency.

This Regional Regulation is expected to encourage efforts to improve the physical and mental conditions of women in fulfilling their rights and life needs as part of human rights in various fields of development, increase the commitment and effectiveness of regional governments in implementing policies, programs and activities and encourage the institutional capacity of government and society that improves the quality of life of women.

Female commercial sex workers are women who work as sex workers of their own volition and/or are prostituted. The improvement of life skills for commercial sex workers is intended to provide additional knowledge through training and skills in accordance with their talents and abilities so that they can live more independently and obtain decent work to improve their standard of living and change their social status so as not to be commercial sex workers because such work is not legalized.

The protection of commercial sex workers in this Regional Regulation is not intended to legalize the existence of commercial sex workers, but to provide protection to the said commercial sex workers in various sectors.
