BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

MAYOR OF GORONTALO,

Considering : a. that Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia guarantee the right of every citizen to be free from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and to obtain security and freedom from all forms of violence;

b. that all forms of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights so that their dignity and honor must be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and destiny without discrimination;

c. that women's empowerment and child protection are concurrent governmental affairs delegated to the regions to become the basis for implementing regional autonomy and become mandatory governmental affairs not related to basic services as referred to in Law Number 23 Year 2014 concerning Regional Government;

d. that in order to provide regulatory strengthening, direction, and foundation in the protection of women and children who are victims of violence, regulation is required relating to the Protection of Women and Children Who Are Victims of Violence;

e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letter a, letter b, letter c, letter d, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation concerning the Protection of Women and Children Who Are Victims of Violence;

# CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, the following terms mean:

- 1. Region is the City of Gorontalo.
- 2. Mayor is the Regional Head.
- 3. Regional Government is the Mayor as an element of the Regional Government administrator who leads the implementation of government affairs that are the authority of the autonomous Region.
- 4. Regional Governance is the administration of government affairs by the Regional Government and the Regional House of Representatives according to the principles of autonomy and co-administration with the principle of the widest possible autonomy within the system and principles of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia as referred to in the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia.
- 5. Woman is a person of female sex.
- 6. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 years, including a child still in the womb.
- 7. Violence is any act that results in or may result in suffering or hardship whether physical, sexual, economic, social, and psychological to the victim.
- 8. Violence against women is any act that results in or may result in suffering or hardship to women physically, sexually, economically, socially, psychologically, including threats of certain acts, coercion or deprivation of liberty, whether occurring in public or private life.
- 9. Violence against children is any act that results in or may result in suffering to children physically, psychologically, sexually, neglect, exploitation, and other violence.
- 10. Victim is a woman and child who experiences suffering and or hardship either directly or indirectly as a result of violence occurring within the territory of the City of Gorontalo.
- 11. Protection of women is all activities aimed at providing a sense of security carried out by the police, prosecutor's office, courts, social institutions, or other parties who know or hear that violence against women will occur, is occurring or has occurred.
- 12. Child protection is all activities to guarantee and protect children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and worth and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
- 13. Service is an action taken as soon as possible to the victim when seeing, hearing and knowing that violence against the victim will occur, is occurring or has occurred.
- 14. Companion is a person or representative from an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance to victims to conduct counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
- 15. Integrated Service Center for Women and Children Empowerment, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service unit established by the Region to provide services in the context of empowerment of women and children who are victims of violence.
- 16. Safe House is a shelter for victims during the assistance process, for the safety and comfort of victims from threats and dangers posed by perpetrators.
- 17. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and children, or mother and children, or blood relatives in direct line upward or downward up to the third degree.
- 18. Household is husband, wife, and children, people who have family relationships due to blood relations, marriage, breastfeeding, care, and guardianship, and/or domestic workers who reside in the household.
- 19. Forum for the Protection of Victims of Violence, hereinafter abbreviated as FPKK, is a coordination forum for handling victims of violence against women and children whose implementation is carried out in a networked manner.

Article 2

Protection of Victims is based on:

- a. respect for and fulfillment of the rights of victims; b. justice and gender equality;
- c. non-discrimination;
- d. the best interests of victims; and
- e. empowerment.

Article 3

Protection of Victims aims to:

- a. prevent violence against women and children;
- b. provide services to Victims; and
- c. empower women victims of violence.

Article 4

The scope of protection of Victims includes efforts to prevent, provide services and empower victims of violence in the City of Gorontalo.

CHAPTER 11

FORMS OF VIOLENCE

Article 5

Forms of violence include:

- a. physical violence;
- b. psychological violence;
- c. sexual violence;
- d. neglect;
- e. exploitation; and/or
- f. other violence.

Article 6

Physical violence as referred to in Article 5 letter a is caused by acts that result in pain, injury, wounds or defects to a person's body, miscarriage, fainting and/or cause death.

Article 7

Psychological violence as referred to in Article 5 letter b is caused by acts that result in fear, loss of self-confidence, loss of ability to act, feelings of helplessness and/or severe psychological suffering to a person.

Article 8

Sexual violence as referred to in Article 5 letter c is caused by:

- a. acts in the form of sexual harassment;
- b. coercion of sexual intercourse;
- c. coercion of sexual intercourse in an unnatural or unwanted manner; and/or
- d. coercion of sexual intercourse with others for commercial purposes and or certain purposes

#### Article 9

Neglect as referred to in Article 5 letter d is caused by:

- a. acts that result in the non-fulfillment of a child's needs properly, whether physical, mental, spiritual or social, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
- b. acts of deliberately neglecting to maintain, care for, or take care of a child as appropriate, committed by parents, guardians, or any other party responsible for their care;
  - 0. acts that neglect a person within the scope of their household, whereas according to the law applicable to them or due to an agreement or contract they are obliged to provide livelihood, care, or maintenance to that person; and/or
- d. acts that result in economic dependence by restricting and/or prohibiting working decently inside or outside the home so that the victim is under the control of that person.

#### Article 10

Exploitation as referred to in.

#### Article 5 letter e is caused by:

a. acts that exploit economically or sexually with the intention of benefiting oneself or others;

b. acts with or without the consent of the victim which include but are not limited to prostitution, forced labor or service, slavery or similar practices, oppression, extortion, utilization of physical, sexual, reproductive organs, or unlawfully transferring or transplanting organs and/or body tissues or exploiting the labor or abilities of a person by other parties to obtain benefits either material or immaterial; and/or

c. all forms of utilization of sexual organs or other body organs of victims to obtain benefits, including but not limited to all prostitution or indecency activities.

#### Article 11

Other violence as referred to in Article 5 letter f is caused by:

- a. threats of violence which include any unlawful act in the form of speech, writing, images, symbols, or body movements, whether with or without using means that cause fear or restrict a person's natural freedom; and
- b. coercion, including: a situation where a person/victim is ordered to do something in such a way that the person does something that is contrary to their own will.

# CHAPTER 111

RIGHTS OF VICTIMS

#### Article 12

Every Victim shall receive the right:

- a. to be respected for their dignity and honor as human beings;
- b. health and psychological recovery from suffering experienced by the victim;
- c. to determine their own decisions;
- d. to obtain information;
- e. confidentiality of their identity;
- f. compensation;
- g. social rehabilitation;
- h. complaint handling; i. to obtain facilitation in the judicial process; and/or j. assistance.

#### Article 13

Child victims of violence in addition to receiving the rights as referred to in Article 12, also receive special rights, consisting of:

- a. respect for survival, growth and development; b. basic services;
- e. equal protection;
- d. freedom from various stigma; and/or
- e. to obtain freedom.

# CHAPTER IV

OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

Regional Government

#### Article 14

(1) The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to implement efforts to protect women and children victims of violence through:

- a. policy establishment;
- b. preparation of program and activity planning;
- e. establishment of P2TP2A;
- d. providing facilities and infrastructure support;
- e. allocating budget; and
- f. establishment of FPKK.
  - (2) To anticipate the occurrence of acts of violence, the Regional Government provides services for victims in the form of:
- a. facilitating the establishment of P2TP2A;
- b. facilitating facilities and infrastructure of P2TP2A according to capacity; and c. facilitating FPKK as a network forum for victim handling.

#### Article 15

The Regional Government is obligated and responsible to:

- a. supervise the implementation of services to victims, with minimum service standards; and
- b. provide funds for victim protection through the APBD and/or other lawful regional financial sources.

## Part Two

Community and Family

#### Article 16

The community and family have obligations and responsibilities which include:

- a. preventing the occurrence of violence against women and children;
- b. reporting if violence occurs;
- e. protecting victims; and
- d. providing emergency assistance.

# CHAPTER V

INTEGRATED SERVICE CENTER FOR THE EMPOWERMENT OF

WOMEN AND CHILDREN

#### Article 17

(1) P2TP2A is established by the Regional Government and/or community institutions.

(2) The organizational structure of P2TP2A established by institutions is formed according to needs.

#### Article 18

(1) P2TP2A provides services and temporary protection in the form of safe houses for victims of violence.

(2) In the event that P2TP2A does not have a safe house as referred to in paragraph (1), victims of violence are referred to P2TP2A that has a safe house.

#### Article 19

(1) The organizational structure of P2TP2A established by the Regional Government as referred to in Article 17 paragraph (1) consists of:

- a. general chairperson or other equivalent designation;
- b. deputy general chairperson;
- e. executive chairperson;
- d. deputy executive chairperson;
- e. secretary;
- e. treasurer; and
- d. executive chairperson overseeing fields.
  - (2) Further provisions regarding the structure and duties of P2TP2A established by the Region as referred to in paragraph (1) are stipulated by decision of the Mayor.

#### Article 20

(1) P2TP2A established by the Regional Government must have at least three counselors.

(2) Counselors as referred to in paragraph (1) include: a. counselor in the field of law;

b. counselor in the field of health; and

e. counselor in the field of psychology.

(3) The implementation of counseling carried out by counselors can be implemented in a networked manner as follows:

- a. counseling in the field of law by involving the police, prosecution, district court, religious court, legal advocacy institutions and Non-Governmental Organizations;
- b. counseling in the field of health by involving the Health Office, Regional General Hospital and other health services;
- c. counseling in the field of psychology by involving psychological assistants and/or psychologists.

# CHAPTER VI

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

Prevention

#### Article 21

(1) Violence prevention efforts against women and children are carried out in an integrated manner by the Regional Government coordinated by regional apparatus that implements governmental affairs in the field of women's empowerment and child protection.

(2) Prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are implemented by:

- a. forming work networks in violence prevention efforts;
- b. conducting coordination, integration, synchronization of violence prevention based on partnership patterns;
- c. forming a violence prevention system;
- d. conducting socialization regarding laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children victims of violence; and
- e. providing critical education on the rights of women and children to the community.

#### Article 22

In addition to the Regional Government, prevention efforts as referred to in Article 21 paragraph (1) are carried out by:

- a. family and/or closest relatives;
- b. community;
- c. educational institutions; and
- d. Non-Governmental Organizations.

## Part Two

Services

#### Article 23

(1) The implementation of services to victims is carried out in an integrated manner by P2TP2A.

(2) P2TP2A as referred to in paragraph (1) may receive and send case referrals from or to other service units in a networked manner.

#### Article 24

The implementation of services to victims is carried out with: a. speed;

b. safety and comfort;

c. sense of empathy;

d. non-discrimination;

e. easy accessibility;

f. no charges; and

g. guaranteed confidentiality.

#### Article 25

Forms of services to victims include:

- a. complaint, consultation, and counseling services;
- b. assistance services;
- c. health services;
- d. social rehabilitation services;
- e. legal services; and
- f. repatriation and social reintegration services.

#### Article 26

Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Article 25 letter a include:

- a. identification or initial recording of victims; and
- b. consent to take action.

#### Article 27

Assistance services as referred to in Article 25 letter b include:

- a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;
- b. accompanying victims during the medicolegal process;
- c. accompanying victims during the examination process at the Police, Prosecution and court;
- d. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the Police, Prosecution and Court;
- e. maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties with no interest, including reporting by mass media;
- f. conducting coordination with other assistants; and g. providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 28

Health services as referred to in Article 25 letter e include:

- a. first aid to victims;
- b. care and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel; and
- e. referral to health services.

#### Article 29

(1) Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 25 letter d constitute services provided by assistants in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims.

(2) Social rehabilitation services as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:

- a. providing spiritual guidance to victims; and b. psychological recovery of victims.

#### Article 30

(1) Legal services as referred to in Article 25 letter e are in order to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process. (2) Legal services as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:

- a. providing legal consultation that includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;
- b. accompanying victims at the level of investigation, prosecution, and examination in court hearings and assisting victims to fully describe the violence they experienced;
- c. conducting coordination with fellow law enforcers, companion volunteers, and social workers so that the judicial process runs as it should.

#### Article 31

(1) Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in Article 25 letter f aim to return victims to their families and social environment.

(2) Repatriation and social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by the Regional Government in coordination with: a. the Regency/City Government within one region of Gorontalo Province; and

b. related government and non-government agencies and institutions.

## Part Three

Empowerment

### Paragraph 1

Empowerment of Women Victims of Violence

#### Article 32

Empowerment of women victims of violence includes: a. job training;

b. productive economic enterprises and/or joint business groups; and c. capital assistance.

#### Article 33

Job training as referred to in Article 32 letter a includes: a. skills training;

b. field work practice; and

c. apprenticeship.

#### Article 34

Productive economic enterprises and/or joint business groups as referred to in Article 32 letter b include:

- a. entrepreneurship skills training;
- b. facilitation of the formation of joint business groups; and
- c. assistance in business implementation.

#### Article 35

Capital assistance as referred to in Article 32 letter c includes:

- a. assistance with work facilities and infrastructure; and
- b. facilitation of working capital assistance.

### Paragraph 2

Fulfillment of the Rights of Child Victims of Violence

#### Article 36

(1) The Regional Government, P2TP2A, and the community are obliged to fulfill the rights of child victims of violence.

(2) The form of fulfillment of the rights of child victims of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) includes the fulfillment of children's basic rights in accordance with their needs.

## Part Four

Coordination of Victim Protection

#### Article 37

(1) In an effort to protect and provide service handling for victims, the Regional Government establishes FPKK.

(2) FPKK as referred to in paragraph (1) consists of: a. Regional FPKK;

b. Sub-district FPKK; and

c. Village FPKK.

(3) The establishment of Regional FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter a is determined by a decree of the Mayor.

(4) The establishment of Sub-district FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter b is determined by a decree of the Sub-district Head.

(5) The establishment of Village FPKK as referred to in paragraph (2) letter c is determined by a decree of the Village Head.

(6) Further provisions regarding the organizational structure and elements of FPKK are regulated by Mayoral Regulation.

#### Article 38

The duties and functions of FPKK as referred to in Article 37 paragraph (1) are:

- a. coordinating and synchronizing prevention, services, and empowerment for victims of violence against women and children;
- b. maintaining and developing FPKK in the prevention, services, and empowerment of victims of violence in a network and referral system;
- c. conducting education on non-discrimination values toward women and children;
- d. conducting socialization of laws and regulations related to the provision of protection for women and children victims of violence;
- e. collecting, compiling, and presenting data reports on victims of violence; and
- f. conducting monitoring and evaluation of the provision of protection for women and children victims of violence.

# CHAPTER VII

COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 39

(1) The community participates in the provision of protection for victims.

(2) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is in the form of: a. establishing family partners at the village level by the community;

b. establishing women and children protection units within community organizations;

c. conducting socialization of women and children's rights independently; d. providing first aid to victims; and

e. reporting to the authorized agency if violence against victims occurs in their environment.

(3) Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by individuals, social community institutions, non-governmental organizations, educational institutions, religious institutions, the private sector, and mass media.

I . GENERAL

Acts of violence against women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore their dignity and honor need to be protected and their right to life guaranteed in accordance with their nature and essence without discrimination.

In order to prevent and overcome violence against women and children in Gorontalo City so as to avoid violence, threats of violence, torture or treatment that degrades human dignity and honor, it is necessary to provide protection for women and children victims of violence in the form of Regional regulations.

So far, laws and regulations that regulate the protection of women and children victims of violence have not regulated protection efforts in the Region, therefore institutional and regulatory support is needed to ensure its implementation.

This Regional Regulation regulates protection efforts for victims, particularly in terms of prevention, services and empowerment for women and children victims of violence in Gorontalo City.
