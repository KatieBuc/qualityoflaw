# IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

REGENT OF MUSI RAWAS UTARA

SOUTH SUMATRA PROVINCE

# REGIONAL REGULATION OF MUSI RAWAS UTARA REGENCY

NUMBER 9 OF 2019

CONCERNING

IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN

BY THE GRACE OF ALMIGHTY GOD

REGENT OF MUSI RAWAS UTARA.

Considering:
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
a. that all forms of violence, torture or treatment that degrades the degree, dignity of women and children constitute violations of human rights, therefore it is necessary to protect their honor and dignity and guarantee their right to life in accordance with their nature and disposition without discrimination;
b. that violence, torture or treatment that degrades the degree and dignity of women and children continues to increase so that protection efforts must be carried out;
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a and b, it is necessary to establish a Regional Regulation on the Protection of Women and Children;

In view of:
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1. Article 18 paragraph (6) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;
2. Law Number 16 of 2013 concerning the Establishment of Musi Rawas Utara Regency in South Sumatra Province (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2013 Number 112, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5429);
3. Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2014 Number 244, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5587), as amended several times most recently by Law Number 9 of 2015 concerning the Second Amendment to Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia of 2015 Number 58, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia Number 5679);

With Mutual Approval

REGIONAL HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF MUSI RAWAS UTARA REGENCY

and

REGENT OF MUSI RAWAS UTARA

HAS DECIDED:

To establish:

REGIONAL REGULATION CONCERNING IMPLEMENTATION OF PROTECTION FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN.

# CHAPTER I

## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by:
1. Regency is Musi Rawas Utara Regency.
2. Regency Government is the Government of Musi Rawas Utara Regency.
3. Regent is the Regent of Musi Rawas Utara.
4. Regional Apparatus is an auxiliary element of the Regional Head and the Regional House of Representatives in the administration of Government Affairs that are the authority of the Region.
5. Office of Village Community Empowerment and Protection of Women and Children, hereinafter referred to as the Office, is the Regional Apparatus that administers the affairs of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection in Musi Rawas Utara Regency.
6. Woman is a person who has female genitalia who can menstruate and become pregnant or has obtained legal status as a woman.
7. Child is a person who is not yet 18 (eighteen) years old, including a child who is still in the womb.
8. Protection is all efforts aimed at providing a sense of security to victims carried out by family, advocates, social institutions, police, prosecutors, courts, or other parties either temporarily or based on court determination.
9. Child Protection is all activities to guarantee and protect Children and their rights so that they can live, grow, develop, and participate optimally in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.
10. Family is the smallest unit in society consisting of husband and wife, or husband and wife and their children, or father and his children, and mother and her children and among people who are their dependents.
11. Parents are biological father and/or mother, or stepfather and/or stepmother, or adoptive father and/or mother.
12. Guardian is a person or body who in fact exercises parental authority as Parents over a Child.
13. Child with Disability is a Child who has physical, mental, intellectual, or sensory limitations for a long period of time who in interacting with the environment and community attitudes may encounter obstacles that make it difficult to participate fully and effectively based on equal rights.
14. Child with Excellence is a Child who has extraordinary intelligence or has potential and/or special talents not limited to intellectual abilities, but also in other fields.
15. Violence is any act that results in or may result in misery or suffering whether physical, sexual, economic, social, psychological to the victim.
16. Child-Friendly Regency is a Regency that has a child rights-based development system through the integration of government, community and business commitments and resources, which are planned comprehensively and sustainably in policies, programs and activities to ensure the fulfillment of children's rights and protection.
17. Regional Medium-Term Development Plan, hereinafter abbreviated as RPJMD, is a Regional planning document for a period of 5 (five) years starting from inauguration until the end of the Regent's term of office.
18. Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget, hereinafter abbreviated as APBD, is the Annual Financial Plan of the Regional Government established by Regional Regulation.
19. Regional Action Plan is a document containing policies, programs and activities in order to realize objectives in accordance with regional authority.
20. Integrated Service Center for Women's Empowerment and Children, hereinafter abbreviated as P2TP2A, is an integrated service center in efforts to empower women in various fields of development, as well as protection of women and children from various types of discrimination and acts of violence, including trafficking in persons, established by the government or community-based.
21. Person with Disability is every person who experiences physical, intellectual, mental, and/or sensory limitations for a long period of time who in interacting with the environment may experience obstacles and difficulties to participate fully and effectively with other citizens based on equal rights.

#### Article 2

The implementation of Protection for Women and Children is based on the principles of:
a. respect and recognition of equal human rights and dignity;
b. non-discrimination;
c. gender equality and justice;d. protection of the human rights of Women and Children;
e. appreciation of the Child's views;
f. best interests of victims of violence; and
g. legal certainty.

#### Article 3

This Regional Regulation aims to:
a. serve as a guideline in planning and implementing policies for the Protection of Women and Children;
b. prevent violations of the rights of Women and Children;
c. realize integrated, comprehensive and sustainable efforts for the Protection of Women and Children;
d. prevent decisions and policies that violate and do not support efforts for the Protection of Women and Children; and
e. increase the role of the Regency Government, community, and other community institutions in efforts to Protect Women and Children.

#### Article 4

The scope of this Regional Regulation is as follows:
a. rights of Women and Children;
b. obligations and responsibilities;
c. implementation of Protection for Women and Children;
d. guidance and supervision; and
e. financing;

# CHAPTER II

## RIGHTS OF WOMEN AND CHILDREN

## Part One

Rights of Women

#### Article 5

1. Every Woman has the right to:  
a. embrace a religion and worship according to her religion and belief;  
b. obtain education, benefit from science and technology, arts and culture, in order to improve her quality of life;  
c. live and maintain life and her livelihood;  
d. form a family and continue descendants through legal marriage;  
e. develop herself and advance herself;  
f. work and receive fair and proper remuneration and treatment in employment relations;  
g. obtain equal opportunities in law and government;  
h. freedom of association, assembly and expression of opinion;  
i. protection of her person, family, honor, dignity and property under her control;  
j. security and protection from threats of fear to do or not do something;  
k. freedom from torture or treatment that degrades human dignity;  
l. live in physical and spiritual prosperity, reside and obtain a good and healthy living environment;  
m. obtain health services;  
n. communicate and obtain information to develop her personal and social environment;  
2. Every Woman has the right to obtain empowerment in the economic, socio-cultural, political and legal fields in order to develop herself and advance herself as referred to in paragraph (1) letter e.

#### Article 6

Women's Empowerment in the economic field as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) may be implemented through:  
a. provision of skills and job training;  
b. facilitation of the formation of productive economic business groups;  
c. facilitation of strengthening and development of productive economic business groups;  
d. facilitation and capital assistance; and  
e. facilitation of marketing network development.

#### Article 7

Women's Empowerment in the socio-cultural field as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) may be implemented through:  
a. improvement of knowledge, attitudes and skills to encourage the fulfillment of tiered education in accordance with potential to improve social status;  
b. improvement of awareness, willingness and ability to overcome health problems through quality promotive, preventive, curative, and rehabilitative efforts, especially in the field of reproductive health;  
c. improvement of awareness and knowledge about independent, healthy and prosperous family planning including access to consultation services and marriage registration; and  
d. facilitation and efforts to preserve customs and develop science, technology, arts and culture for the advancement of Women.

#### Article 8

1. Women's Empowerment in the political field as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) may be implemented through:  
a. involvement of Women in decision-making at various levels;  
b. provision of opportunities for Women to hold public office;  
c. participation in general elections; and  
d. self-development through organizations to associate, assemble and express opinions.  
2. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the political field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 9

1. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the legal field as referred to in Article 5 paragraph (2) may be implemented through:  
a. improvement of awareness and knowledge in the legal field through communication, information and education services; and  
b. facilitation of access and legal consultation services.  
2. The implementation of Women's Empowerment in the legal field as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be carried out in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

## Part Two Children's Rights

#### Article 10

Every Child has the right to live, grow, develop, and participate reasonably in accordance with human dignity and dignity, and receive protection from violence and discrimination.

#### Article 11

1. Every Child has the right to a name as personal identity and citizenship status.  
2. Personal identity and citizenship status as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 12

Every Child has the right to worship according to his/her religion, think, and express himself/herself in accordance with his/her level of intelligence and age under the guidance of Parents or Guardian.

#### Article 13

1. Every Child has the right to know his/her parents, be raised, and be cared for by his/her own Parents.  
2. In the event that for any reason the Parents cannot care for or guarantee the growth and development of the Child or the Child is in a state of neglect, then the Child has the right to be cared for or adopted as a foster child or adopted child by another person in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 14

Every Child who is in the care of Parents, Guardian, or other party responsible for care as referred to in Article 13 has the right to receive Protection from treatment of:  
a. discrimination;  
b. exploitation, both economic and sexual;  
c. neglect;  
d. cruelty/violence/abuse;  
e. injustice; and  
f. other mistreatment.

#### Article 15

1. Every Child has the right to be cared for by his/her own Parents, unless there are valid reasons and/or legal rules showing that the separation is in the best interests of the Child and is a last resort.  
2. In the event of separation as referred to in paragraph (1), the Child still has the right to:  
a. meet directly and maintain personal relations regularly with both Parents;  
b. obtain care, maintenance, education and Protection for the growth and development process from both Parents in accordance with abilities, talents, and interests;  
c. obtain living expenses from both Parents; and  
d. obtain other Children's rights.

#### Article 16

Every Child has the right to obtain health services and social security in accordance with physical, mental, spiritual, and social needs.

#### Article 17

1. Every Child has the right to obtain education and teaching in the context of developing his/her personality and level of intelligence in accordance with interests and talents.  
2. Every Child has the right to receive protection in educational units from sexual crimes and Violence committed by educators, education personnel, fellow students, and/or other parties.

#### Article 18

Every Child has the right to express and be heard his/her opinion, receive, seek, and provide information in accordance with his/her level of intelligence and age for his/her self-development in accordance with the values of decency and propriety.

#### Article 19

Every Child has the right to rest and utilize leisure time, socialize with Children of the same age, play, recreate, and create in accordance with interests, talents, and level of intelligence for self-development.

#### Article 20

Every Child has the right to obtain Protection from:  
a. abuse in political activities;  
b. involvement in armed conflict;  
c. involvement in social unrest;  
d. involvement in events containing elements of Violence and discrimination;  
e. involvement in warfare; and  
f. sexual crimes.

#### Article 21

1. Every Child has the right to obtain protection from being the target of persecution, torture, or imposition of inhuman punishment.  
2. Every Child has the right to obtain freedom in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.  
3. Arrest, detention, or criminal imprisonment of a Child shall only be carried out as a last resort in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

#### Article 22

Every Child who becomes a perpetrator or victim of a criminal act has the right to:  
a. receive humane treatment and placement separated from adults;  
b. obtain legal assistance or other assistance effectively at every stage of applicable legal efforts; and  
c. defend himself/herself and obtain justice before an objective and impartial Children's court in a closed session to the public.

#### Article 23

Every Child who becomes a victim or perpetrator of sexual violence and discrimination or who is in conflict with the law has the right to confidentiality.

#### Article 24

Every Child with Disabilities in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 10 through Article 23, has the right to obtain:  
a. special education;  
b. rehabilitation;  
c. social assistance; and  
d. maintenance of social welfare standards.

#### Article 25

Every Child who has excellence in addition to obtaining the rights as referred to in Article 10 through Article 23, has the right to obtain special education.

# CHAPTER III

## OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

## Part One

Regency Government

#### Article 26

1. The Regency Government is obliged and responsible to:  
a. organize the Protection of Women and Children;  
b. undertake prevention efforts against acts of violence toward Women and Children; and  
c. guarantee the fulfillment of the rights of Women and Children as referred to in Article 5 through Article 25.  
2. The obligations and responsibilities as referred to in paragraph (1) may be implemented through:  
a. policies on the Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence established by the central government;  
b. establishing programs and activities for the Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence;  
c. conducting cooperation in the organization of the Protection of Women and Children;  
d. providing support for facilities and infrastructure for the implementation of the Protection of Women and Children;  
e. allocating budget for the organization of the Protection of Women and Children in accordance with the financial capacity of the Regency;  
f. fostering and supervising the organization of the Protection of Women and Children;  
g. establishing an integrated service center; and  
h. realizing a Child-Friendly Regency.  
3. The Regency Government as referred to in paragraph (1), establishes programs and action activities for the Protection of Women and Children in 1 (one) Regional Action Plan as the basis for the Agency to implement the Protection of Women and Children from acts of Violence.  
4. The Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3), constitutes part of the RPJMD.  
5. Provisions regarding the Regional Action Plan as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

## Part Two Parents and Family

#### Article 27

Parents and Family are obliged and responsible to:  
a. care for, maintain, and educate Children;  
b. develop Children in accordance with their abilities, talents, and interests;  
c. prevent marriage at Child age;  
d. provide character education and instillation of moral values to Children.  
e. prevent violations of rights against Women and Children; and  
f. protect Women and Children from acts of violence.

## Part Three Community

#### Article 28

1. The Community is obliged and responsible to participate in organizing the Protection of Women and Children.  
2. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be implemented through:  
a. forming family partners at the sub-district and village level;  
b. forming units for the Protection of Women and Children within community organizations;  
e. conducting socialization and education about the rights of Women and Children independently;  
d. preventing violations of the rights of Women and Children;  
e. providing first aid to Women and Children who become victims of acts of violence; and  
f. providing information or reporting to the competent authority if acts of Violence against Women and Children occur in their environment.  
3. Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may be implemented by individuals or groups, academics, observers of Women and Children, community-based integrated child protection, community organizations/community institutions/religious institutions/other legitimate institutions.

# CHAPTER IV

### ORGANIZATION OF PROTECTION

## Part One

General

#### Article 29

1. The Regency Government in organizing the Protection of Women and Children is coordinated by the Agency.  
2. The Regency Government in organizing the Protection of Women and Children as referred to in paragraph (1) may establish P2TP2A.  
3. Further provisions regarding P2TP2A shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

#### Article 30

The organization of the Protection of Women and Children is implemented through efforts to:  
a. prevent;  
b. reduce vulnerability risks;  
c. handle; and  
d. rehabilitate.

## Part Two

Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence

#### Article 31

The organization of the Protection of Women and Children Victims of Violence is implemented through:  
a. complaint, consultation, and counseling services;  
b. assistance services;  
c. medical rehabilitation services;  
d. social rehabilitation services;  
e. legal services; and  
f. social reintegration services.

#### Article 32

Complaint, consultation, and counseling services as referred to in Article 31 letter a include:  
a. identification or initial recording of victims;  
b. case identification; and  
c. consent to take action.

#### Article 33

Assistance services as referred to in Article 31 letter b include:  
a. accompanying victims during the examination and health recovery process;  
b. accompanying victims during the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;  
c. monitoring the interests and rights of victims in the examination process at the police, prosecutor's office and court;  
d. maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of victims from all parties who have no interest, including reporting by mass media;  
e. providing a sense of security to victims; and  
f. providing continuous handling up to the rehabilitation stage.

#### Article 34

Medical rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 31 letter c include:  
a. first aid to victims;  
b. treatment and recovery of physical injuries aimed at restoring the physical condition of victims carried out by medical and paramedical personnel; and  
c. referral to health services.

#### Article 35

1. Social rehabilitation services as referred to in Article 31 letter d constitute services provided in order to restore the traumatic condition of victims.  
2. The form of services as referred to in paragraph (1) is through social support.  
3. Social support as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out through:  
a. spiritual guidance to victims; and  
b. psychological recovery of victims.

#### Article 36

Legal services as referred to in Article 31 letter e are to assist victims in undergoing the judicial process by:  
a. providing legal consultation which includes information regarding the rights of victims and the judicial process;  
b. accompanying victims at the investigation, prosecution, and examination levels in court hearings and helping victims to fully describe the violence they experienced; and  
c. coordinating with fellow law enforcers, volunteer assistants, and social workers so that the judicial process proceeds as it should.

#### Article 37

1. Social reintegration services as referred to in Article 31 letter f aim to return victims to their families and social environment.  
2. Social reintegration services as referred to in paragraph (1) are carried out by the Regency Government by coordinating with:  
a. related agencies and institutions both government and non-government; and  
b. community institutions and/or local community leaders.

## Part Three Child-Friendly Regency

#### Article 38

1. The Regency Government in order to realize a Child-Friendly Regency as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (2) letter h refers to the policy on the development of a Child-Friendly Regency that has been established.  
2. The policy on the development of a Child-Friendly Regency as referred to in paragraph (1) contains:  
a. the concept of a Child-Friendly Regency;  
b. children's rights; and  
c. the approach to developing a Child-Friendly Regency.  
3. Further provisions regarding the Child-Friendly Regency shall be regulated by Regent Regulation.

# CHAPTER V

## GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 39

1. The Regent shall conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of Protection of Women and Children.  
2. The Regent in conducting guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (1) may delegate to Regional Apparatus.  
3. Guidance and supervision as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be coordinated with the Agency.

#### Article 40

1. Supervision as referred to in Article 39 shall be implemented through monitoring and evaluation of:  
a. policies/programs for Protection of Women and Children; and  
b. implementation of activities for Protection of Women and Children.  
2. The results of monitoring and evaluation as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be submitted periodically to the Regent.

# CHAPTER VI

## FINANCING

#### Article 41

Financing for the implementation of Protection of Women and Children shall be sourced from:  
a. Regional Budget; and/or  
b. other lawful and non-binding sources in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

# CHAPTER VII

## PROVISIONS ON INVESTIGATION

#### Article 42

1. Certain Civil Servant Officials within the Regency Government who are given special authority as Investigators to conduct investigations of criminal acts as regulated in the provisions of Laws and Regulations.  
2. Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) are certain Civil Servants within the Regency Government who are appointed by authorized officials in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.  
3. The authority of Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:  
a. receiving reports or complaints from a person regarding the existence of a criminal act;  
b. taking first action at that time at the scene and conducting examination;  
c. ordering a suspect to stop and examining the suspect's identity card;  
d. seizing objects or documents;  
e. taking fingerprints and photographing a person;  
f. summoning persons to be heard and examined as suspects or witnesses;  
g. bringing in experts needed in connection with the examination of the case;  
h. terminating the investigation after receiving instructions from the Investigator that there is insufficient evidence or the incident does not constitute a criminal act and subsequently through the investigator notifying this matter to the Public Prosecutor, the suspect or their family;  
i. conducting other actions according to law that can be accounted for.  
4. Investigators as referred to in paragraph (1) shall notify the commencement of the investigation and submit the results of their investigation to the Public Prosecutor through the Investigator Official of the Police of the Republic of Indonesia, in accordance with the provisions regulated in the Criminal Procedure Code.

# CHAPTER VIII

## CRIMINAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 43

Any person who deliberately allows violence against women and children to occur and/or does not report the occurrence of violence against women and children shall be subject to sanctions in accordance with the provisions of Laws and Regulations.

# CHAPTER IX

## CLOSING PROVISIONS

#### Article 44

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation.  

In order that everyone may know of it, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by placing it in the Regional Gazette of North Musi Rawas Regency.
