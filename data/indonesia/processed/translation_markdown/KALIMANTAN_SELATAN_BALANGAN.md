# CHAPTER I GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 1

In this Regional Regulation, what is meant by :

- 1. Region is the Region of Balangan Regency.
- 2. Regional Government is the head of region as an element of the Regional Government administration who leads the implementation of governmental affairs which are the authority of the autonomous region.
- 3. Head of Region hereinafter referred to as Regent is the Regent of Balangan Regency.
- 4. Regional House of Representatives hereinafter abbreviated as DPRD is the regional people's representative institution of Balangan Regency which is positioned as an element of the Regional Government administration.
- 5. Agency or Board is an Agency or Board whose scope of duties and responsibilities includes the field of protection and empowerment of women and children in the region. 6. Woman is an adult human being of female sex and a person who is recognized by law as a woman. 7. Child is a person who has not yet reached the age of 18 (eighteen) years including a child who is still in the womb. 8. Community is individuals, families, groups, social organizations and/or community organizations.
- 9. Family is a person who has a blood relationship in a straight line upward or downward and collateral line up to the third degree, or who has a marital relationship, or a person who is a dependent of a woman and/or child.
- 10. Parent is biological father and/or mother, or step father and/or mother, or adoptive father and/or mother. 11. Protection of women and children is an effort to prevent and handle all forms of acts of violence including neglect and trafficking in persons that violate the human rights of women and children, as well as to provide basic and specific service needs of women and children in the conditions and situations they experience. 12. Prevention is an activity carried out to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children.
- 13. Service is an activity and immediate action carried out by professional personnel in accordance with their respective professions in the form of counseling, therapy and advocacy for the empowerment and recovery of victims of violence.
- 14. Empowerment of women and children is an effort to strengthen human rights, improve quality of life and increase participation of women and children in their social interactions.
- 15. Victim of acts of violence is an individual or group of people who suffer physically, mentally or emotionally, economic loss, or experience neglect, reduction or deprivation of their basic rights, as a result of violations of human rights (HAM), or criminal acts as well as acts of violence committed either by state apparatus or by the state or regional government apparatus or by individuals.
- 16. Assistance is an activity and action carried out by an assistant during the service process.
- 17. Assistant is a person or representative of an institution who has the expertise to provide assistance.
- 18. Rehabilitation is the recovery of victims from psychosocial disorders and restoration of normal social functioning, both in the family and in society.
- 19. Social Reintegration is an effort to reunite victims with family, community, institutions, or other social environments that can provide protection.
- 20. Safe House is a temporary residence, provided to give protection to victims in accordance with predetermined standards. 21. Regional Action Plan is a foundation and guideline for relevant agencies, vertical agencies, and the community, in order to carry out activities for the implementation of prevention and protection of women and children victims of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER II PRINCIPLES AND OBJECTIVES

Article 2

(1) Protection of women and children from acts of violence shall be conducted based on the principles of: a. respect for human rights; b. non-discrimination; and c. protection of victims.

(2) Protection of women and children from acts of violence aims to protect, respect, guarantee the human rights of women and children, and pursue the best interests of women and children.

# CHAPTER III SCOPE

Article 3

(1) Every woman and child has the right to obtain protection from acts of violence.

(2) Acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (1) consist of acts of:

- a. physical violence;
- b. sexual violence;
- c. psychological violence; d. intimidation;
- e. forced eviction;
- f. deprivation of liberty; g. neglect; and
- h. exploitation and human trafficking.

(3) Protection of women and children from acts of violence is the joint responsibility of the Regional Government, society, family, and parents. (4) Protection of women from acts of violence as referred to in paragraph (2) includes: a. prevention of acts of violence against women and children; b. services for women and children victims of acts of violence; and

c. empowerment of women and children victims of acts of violence.

# CHAPTER IV PREVENTION

#### Article 4

(1) The Regional Government, Society, Family, and Parents are obliged to prevent the occurrence of acts of violence against women and children to avoid the impact of situations and events.

(2) Prevention as referred to in paragraph (1) is also carried out against: a. acts of neglect; and b. indications or activities that contain potential for the occurrence of human trafficking.

(3) Prevention efforts are carried out by taking into account conditions, situations, problems and forms of handling that are appropriate and targeted.

#### Article 5

(1) The Regional Government in accordance with its authority carries out early prevention efforts.

(2) Early prevention efforts as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:

- a. conducting data collection and assessment of conditions, fulfillment of rights and various aspects related to women and children in social life in society;
- b. mapping areas prone to acts of violence based on data collection and assessment as referred to in point a and providing solutions in the form/manner capable of minimizing vulnerability;
- c. increasing public legal awareness through the role of community institutions/customary institutions, inter-religious communication forums to provide protection for women and children;
- d. enhancing the role of mass media in providing dissemination and information on the protection of women and children;
- e. regular counseling or socialization of laws and regulations related to the protection of women and children;
- f. opening channels for public complaints, and coordination channels from the village/sub-district, sub-district and Regional Government levels;
- g. immediate handling of public reports.

# CHAPTER V SERVICES

#### Article 6

(1) The Regional Government provides services to women and children victims of acts of violence and neglect.

(2) Services as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:

- a. complaints; b. health rehabilitation;
- c. social rehabilitation; d. social reintegration; and e. legal assistance and accompaniment.

#### Article 7

(1) Women and children victims of acts of violence are placed in safe houses or places whose whereabouts are kept confidential and receive basic fulfillment in accordance with their rights.

(2) Child victims of acts of violence who are placed in safe houses are provided with care according to their needs.

# CHAPTER VI EMPOWERMENT

#### Article 8

(1) The Regional Government in accordance with its authority empowers women and children victims of acts of violence and neglect in the form of improving resilience and improving economic enterprises.

(2) Improving resilience as referred to in paragraph (1)

is by providing guidance and accompaniment for mental spiritual strengthening in the form of religious knowledge.

(3) Improving economic enterprises as referred to in paragraph (1)

is by providing: a. work skills training; b. productive economic enterprise training and joint business groups;

c. accompaniment in the development of productive economic enterprises; d. capital assistance; and e. ease of access to information and marketing promotion of productive economic production results.

Article 9

Child victims of violence whose parental custody rights are revoked, their education becomes the responsibility of the Regional Government or foster parents who obtain custody rights from a court decision.

# CHAPTER VII INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK

Article 10

(1) In order to provide services to women and children victims of acts of violence, neglect and human trafficking, the Regional Government establishes an Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A).

(2) The Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A)

is positioned as an independent institution.

#### Article 11

(1) The Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A)

has the authority to organize the protection of women and children. (2) The duty of the Regional Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) is to carry out protection of women and children in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the applicable laws and regulations.

#### Article 12

The membership of the Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A) consists of elements of relevant agencies, vertical agencies, law enforcement, community organizations, non-governmental organizations, researchers, academicians and religious, customary, community leaders and activists for the protection of women and children in the region.

#### Article 13

(1) The Regent establishes the Management of the Integrated Service Center

for the Empowerment of Women and Children (P2TP2A).

(2) The establishment of the Management as referred to in paragraph (1)

is stipulated by Regent's Decree including the secretariat forum for the management and provision of supporting facilities for the implementation of activities.

Article 14

(1) The Management of the Integrated Service Center for the Empowerment of Women and

Children (P2TP2A) consists of leadership elements and members stipulated by Regent's Decree.

(2) The leadership elements include:

- a. chairperson;
- b. secretary; and c. treasurer.

(3) The membership elements are divided into the form of:

- a. services field
- b. information field;
- c. accompaniment field;
- d. education and training field.

Article 15

(1) The Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children in carrying out its functions builds a working network with: a. Police; b. Prosecutor's Office;

c. Courts;

d. Witness and Victim Protection Agency; e. Hospitals/Community Health Centers;

f. Other institutions that have similar objectives.

(2) In addition to building a working network as referred to in paragraph (1),

may cooperate with: a. Government;

b. Provincial Government;

c. Other Regency/City Governments; and d. Non-Governmental Organizations.

e. International organizations whose existence is recognized.

(3) Cooperation as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) is set forth in the form of joint agreements.

#### Article 16

(1) The Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children establishes partnerships with the business community for support in organizing protection for women and children in the Regional area.

(2) Partnerships as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of:

- a. information and opening of employment opportunities; b. provision of education and job training; c. provision of educational assistance for women and children victims of acts of violence; and d. provision of capital assistance for independent enterprises.

#### Article 17

The Regent as the person responsible for the protection and empowerment of regional women and children has the authority to make changes to the management or dismissal of members if the existing management or members do not carry out their roles and functions.

# CHAPTER VIII COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION

#### Article 18

(1)The community may participate in providing protection and empowerment to women and children victims of violence, neglect and human trafficking.

(2)Community participation as referred to in paragraph (1) may take the form of:

- a. providing information to authorized officials or institutions;
- b. assisting in the rescue of women and children;
- c. providing basic and or specific assistance for women and children;
- d. establishing family partners or small units in the village/sub-district environment that actively work together to protect the environment of women and children from acts of violence;
- e. independently disseminating the rights of women and children;
- f. providing legal assistance and accompaniment.

(3)Community participation in providing protection and empowerment of women and children shall be carried out responsibly in accordance with the provisions of statutory regulations.

# CHAPTER IX GUIDANCE AND SUPERVISION

#### Article 19

(1)The Regent shall conduct guidance and supervision of the implementation of regional protection and empowerment of women and children.

(2)The community is authorized to actively supervise the implementation of regional protection and empowerment of women and children.

(3)The authority of the community as referred to in paragraph (2) includes:

- a. providing input; b. submitting questions and obtaining answers from responsible regional government officials;
- c. requesting realization and concrete activities from those assigned duties and authority;
- d. other matters that are constructive and provide solutions.

# CHAPTER X REGIONAL ACTION PLAN

#### Article 20

(1) The Regent may establish a regional action plan related to the protection and empowerment of women and children.

(2) The regional action plan as referred to in paragraph (1) is manifested in the joint commitment of all elements in the region to implement the protection and empowerment of women and children and to eliminate all forms of violence, neglect and human trafficking.

# CHAPTER XI FUNDING

#### Article 21

(1)Funding for the Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children shall be charged to the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget.

Promulgated in Paringin on January 20, 2016

(2) In addition to funds from the Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budget, funding may come from assistance or donations from third parties that are not binding.

(3)Technically and for the operation of the Center for Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children, it shall be placed in the budgetary post of the Agency determined by the Regent.

(4)The allocation of funds by the Center for Protection and Empowerment

of Women and Children shall be based on the work program that has been established.

(5)Accountability for the use of funds for the implementation of protection of women and children shall be in accordance with the provisions of applicable statutory regulations.

# CHAPTER XIII CLOSING PROVISIONS

Article22

This Regional Regulation shall come into force on the date of promulgation. So that everyone may be informed thereof, orders the promulgation of this Regional Regulation by its placement in the Regional Gazette of Balangan Regency.

I. GENERAL

Violence against women and children is one form of act that violates human rights, because the right to protection from violence of a woman that has been guaranteed by the 1945 Constitution and the Law on Human Rights has been violated by the perpetrator of violence, which should be respected and upheld to enhance human dignity, welfare and justice for that woman. The impact of such violence causes women or children to suffer physically, psychologically, mentally, sexually, so that they need to receive protection and health rehabilitation services, social rehabilitation, repatriation and legal assistance carried out in an integrated manner by victim service providers established by the Government, Regional Government and the community.

Protection and empowerment of women and children currently become the focus in development. Between women and children are usually made into one unit in the scope of their regulation so that it is not focused and results in ambiguity between women and children. Women can be adults or children, while children can be female or male. In the division of authority as determined in Law Number 23 of 2014 concerning Regional Government, it has been focused on the scope of each form of handling, namely women and children are not made into a unit. In that context, in the regions related to women and children, regional regulations must be present regarding: a). Fulfillment of Children's Rights and Special Protection of Children, b). Gender Mainstreaming, c). Protection of Witnesses/Victims and Prevention of Trafficking in Persons, and d). Protection and Empowerment of Women and Children From Acts of Violence. These four regional regulations are a package that must be regulated by the Regional Government in accordance with its authority.
